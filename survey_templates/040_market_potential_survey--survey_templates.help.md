# Market Potential Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about market potential, including company details, user experience, and product feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your company's information.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your company or situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Market potential survey** (1): Enter a brief description of your company's market potential.
* **Category** (2): Select the category that best describes your company.
* **Sub category** (3): Select the sub category that best describes your company further.
* **What is the name of your company?** (4): Enter the name of your company.
* **Product Name** (5): Enter the name of the product you are using/representing.
* **Customer Name** (6): Enter the name of the customer associated with this company.
* **Contact Phone** (7): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (8): Enter the email of the contact person. (Format: `example@company.com`)
* **Company Name** (9): Enter the name of your company again.
* **Company Website** (10): Enter the website of your company.
* **Industry** (11): Enter the industry your company belongs to.
* **sub question 1** (12): Enter any additional information about your company.
* **What is your job role?** (13): Enter your job role in the company.
* **sub question 2** (14): Enter any additional information about your job role.
* **sub question 3** (15): Enter any additional information about your job role.
* **sub question 4** (16): Enter any additional information about your company.
* **What is your level of satisfaction with your current job?** (17): Select your level of satisfaction from Very High, High, Medium, Low, or Very Low.
* **Which of the following features would you like to see added to our product?** (18): Select all applicable features from Feature 1, Feature 2, Feature 3, Feature 4, Feature 5.
* **How many months have you been using our product?** (19): Enter the number of months you have been using the product.
* **How often do you use our product?** (20): Select how often you use the product from Very Often, Often, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **What do you like most about our product?** (21): Enter what you like most about our product.
* **What do you like least about our product?** (22): Enter what you like least about our product.
* **What feature(s) do you find most helpful?** (23): Select all applicable features from Feature 1, Feature 2, Feature 3, Feature 4, Feature 5.
* **What do you think is the primary reason why you use our product?** (24): Enter the reason why you use our product.
* **Do you have any other comments about our product?** (25): Enter any additional comments about the product.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* You can skip any questions that do not apply to your company or situation.
* If you have any other comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Do you have any other comments about our product?" field.
