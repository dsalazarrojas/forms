# Health Anxiety Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information on users' health anxiety, including their awareness and perceptions on certain health-related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
- Fill in the required fields, as indicated by the required label.
- Answer all questions in the space provided, and do not leave any sections blank.
- If you are unsure about a particular question, refer to this guide for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, not required): This section introduces the purpose of the survey and provides a brief context for the following questions.

### Page 2: Worries
* **Worries** (`Worries`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check all that apply to indicate your level of worry or concern regarding various health-related aspects.

### Page 3: Coping Strategies
* **Coping Strategies** (`Coping Strategies`, `text`, not required): Describe any strategies or methods you have used to manage or alleviate your health anxiety.

### Page 4: Experiences
* **Experiences** (`Experiences`, `date`, not required): Record any significant experiences or events related to your health anxiety, including dates if applicable.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions in a truthful and honest manner.
- If you are experiencing distress or concern, please consider reaching out to a healthcare professional for support.
