# drone_surveillance_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Drone Surveillance Equipment Inspection Form is designed to collect and document information about the condition and status of drones in use by our organization. This form is used for routine inspections to ensure that all drones are in a safe and functional state for operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct drone model from the "drone_model" field.
2. Enter the serial number of the drone in the "serial_number" field.
3. Enter the date of the inspection in the "inspection_date" field.
4. Select the time of the inspection in the "time" field.
5. Assess the condition of the drone using the "condition" field.
6. Enter any remarks or observations in the "remarks" field.
7. Enter the name of the person responsible for the drone in the "assigned_to" field.
8. Select the person to whom the drone is currently assigned, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **drone_model**: This field allows you to select the model of the drone being inspected. The available options are:
	+ DJI
	+ Rotorway
	+ Parrot
* **serial_number**: This field is used to enter the unique serial number of the drone.
* **inspection_date**: This field is used to record the date of the inspection. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time**: This field is used to record the time of the inspection. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **condition**: This field allows you to assess the condition of the drone. You can select one or more of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **remarks**: This field is used to enter any additional remarks or observations about the drone's condition.
* **assigned_to**: This field is used to enter the name of the person currently assigned to the drone.
* **remarks_2**: This field is used to select the person to whom the drone is currently assigned. You can choose one of the following options:
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Jack
