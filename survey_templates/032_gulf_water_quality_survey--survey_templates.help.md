# Gulf Water Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gulf Water Quality Survey is designed to collect data on water quality in the Gulf area. This form is intended for observers to report their observations, providing valuable information for better understanding and management of water resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of water body you observed (Gulf, River, Lake, Reservoir, or Other).
2. Indicate the location where you observed the water condition.
3. Choose the parameters you were measuring or observing (pH, Temperature, Dissolved Oxygen, Salinity, or Other).
4. Enter the date and time of your observation.
5. Provide your name and any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Water Body** (`water_body`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of water body where you observed the water condition (e.g., Gulf, River, Lake, or Reservoir).
* **Water Location** (`water_location`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the specific location where you observed the water condition.
* **Water Quality Parameter** (`water_quality_parameter`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the parameters you were measuring or observing (e.g., pH, Temperature, or Dissolved Oxygen).
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your observation.
* **Observation Time** (`observation_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your observation.
* **Observer** (`observer`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name as the person making the observation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments you have about the water condition.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when selecting the water body and parameters observed.
* Use the correct format for date and time (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for date and HH:MM for time).
* If you have any additional information or concerns, please use the comments field.
