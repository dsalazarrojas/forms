# Pregnancy And Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about pregnancy and development-related experiences and outcomes. It includes fields for reporting pregnancy status, prenatal care habits, and various aspects of pregnancy, birth, and infant care. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each section as prompted. The form consists of a series of questions that cover different aspects of pregnancy and development. Please answer each question carefully and accurately. If you're unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Pregnancy Status** (Pregnant, Not Pregnant, Miscarriage, Other)
Indicate your current or past pregnancy status.

### **Prenatal Care Habits** (Select all that apply)
Choose all the prenatal care habits that apply to you.

*   Yes, received regular prenatal check-ups and care
*   Yes, but did not receive regular prenatal check-ups and care
*   No, did not receive any prenatal care
*   Other, please describe

### **Pregnancy Experiences** (Date)
Report the dates of your pregnancy.

### **Baby Status** (Boy, Girl, Twins, Other)
Indicate the baby's status.

### **Birth Date** (Date)
Enter the birth date.

### **Prenatal Test Results** (Positive, Negative, Not Done)
Report the results of your prenatal tests.

### **Ultrasound Result** (Normal, Abnormal)
Indicate the ultrasound results.

### **Birth Weight** (Number)
Enter the birth weight.

### **Gestational Age** (Number)
Enter the gestational age.

### **Pregnancy Length** (Number)
Enter the length of your pregnancy.

### **Baby Gender** (Boy, Girl)
Indicate the baby's gender.

### **Birth Weight Grams** (Number)
Enter the birth weight in grams.

### **Delivery Method** (Vaginal, C-section, Other)
Choose the delivery method.

### **Baby Age** (Number)
Enter the baby's age.

### **Baby Height** (Number)
Enter the baby's height.

### **Mother** (Text)
Enter your name as the mother.

### **Father** (Text)
Enter your partner's name as the father.

### **Hospitalization Days** (Number)
Enter the length of hospitalization.

### **Medical Condition** (Text)
Describe any medical condition you experienced during pregnancy and delivery.

### **Hospitalization Cost** (Number)
Enter the cost of hospitalization.

### **Insurance Provider** (Text)
Enter the insurance provider.

### **Insurance Cost** (Number)
Enter the insurance cost.

### **Hospital Type** (Private, Public, Other)
Choose the type of hospital where you gave birth.

### **Hospital Name** (Text)
Enter the name of the hospital where you gave birth.

### **Comments** (Text)
Enter any additional comments or remarks about your pregnancy and development experiences. 
Please provide a clear and descriptive account of your experiences and outcomes.
