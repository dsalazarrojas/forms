# Community Outreach Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Outreach Program Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the outcomes and effectiveness of community outreach programs. This form helps program managers, coordinators, or evaluators gather information about the program's duration, budget, target audience, and outcomes achieved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the program being evaluated.
2. Select the program duration (one-time event, 1-4 weeks, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, or over 1 year).
3. Enter the start and end dates of the evaluation period.
4. List the primary goals of the program.
5. Select whether the program's goals were fully, mostly, partially, or not achieved.
6. Choose the level of target audience reached (yes, exceeded target, yes, met target, partially, or no).
7. Enter the total number of participants.
8. Enter the budget allocated and actual amount spent.
9. Choose the budget status (under budget, on budget, or over budget).
10. Select whether the resources were more than adequate, adequate, somewhat adequate, or inadequate.
11. Enter the main outcomes achieved by the program.
12. List the methods used to measure the outcomes.
13. Describe the challenges encountered during the program.
14. List the key lessons learned from the program.
15. Provide recommendations for future programs.
16. Choose whether the program should be expanded, maintained, modified, or discontinued.
17. Enter the evaluator's name.
18. Enter the date of evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Title**: Enter the title of the program being evaluated.
* **Program Duration**: Select the duration of the program from the given options.
* **Evaluation Period**: Enter the start and end dates of the evaluation period.
* **Primary Program Goals**: List the main objectives of the program.
* **Were Program Goals Achieved**: Select whether the program's goals were fully, mostly, partially, or not achieved.
* **Target Audience Reached**: Choose the level of target audience reached.
* **Total Number of Participants**: Enter the total number of participants.
* **Budget Allocated**: Enter the budget allocated for the program.
* **Actual Amount Spent**: Enter the actual amount spent on the program.
* **Budget Status**: Choose the budget status.
* **Were Resources Adequate**: Select whether the resources were more than adequate, adequate, somewhat adequate, or inadequate.
* **Program Outcomes**: List the main results achieved by the program.
* **How Were Outcomes Measured**: Select the methods used to measure the outcomes.
* **Challenges Encountered**: Describe the obstacles encountered during the program.
* **Lessons Learned**: List the key takeaways from the program.
* **Recommendations for Future Programs**: Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Should This Program Continue**: Choose whether the program should be expanded, maintained, modified, or discontinued.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the evaluator.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date of evaluation.
