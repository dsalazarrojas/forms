# Gameplay Strategy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from players about their gameplay strategy and performance in a game. It is intended for players who have played the game and would like to offer insights on their playing style, strengths, and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have played the game and are providing genuine feedback.
2. Review the options for each field carefully and select the ones that best describe your experience.
3. Complete all required fields, if any.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the fields that allow text responses.
5. Submit your feedback by clicking the submit button.
