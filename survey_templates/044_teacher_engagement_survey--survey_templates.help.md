# Teacher Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Teacher Engagement Survey" form is a tool to gauge the level of engagement and satisfaction among teachers in the school.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your teacher ID.
4. Enter your email address.
5. If required by your school, enter your school ID.
6. Answer the questions about your engagement level with the school.
7. Select the frequency of your meetings with the school administration.
8. Choose your preferred length of meetings.
9. Indicate the level of attendance at your meetings.
10. Evaluate your collaboration and communication levels with school administration.
11. Rate your overall satisfaction with school administration.
12. Express your level of happiness with school administration (very happy, sometimes, rarely, always).
13. Select your level of satisfaction with school administration (very satisfied, satisfied, dissatisfied, unhappy, very unhappy).
14. Enter any other comments about your engagement and satisfaction with school administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, required): Enter your teacher ID, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you back.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, optional): Enter your school ID, if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, select_one, optional): Rate your engagement level with the school (High, Medium, Low).
* **Frequency of Meeting** (`frequency_meeting`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you meet with the school administration (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Length of Meeting** (`meeting_length`, select_one, optional): Choose the length of your meetings (15-30, 30-60, 60-90, 90-120, Less than 15, More than 120).
* **Meeting Attendance** (`meeting_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your meeting attendance (Always, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
* **Collaboration Level** (`collaboration_level`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your collaboration level with school administration (High, Medium, Low).
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your communication level with school administration (High, Medium, Low).
* **Teacher Satisfaction** (`teacher_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your satisfaction with school administration (High, Medium, Low).
* **Very Happy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_very`, select_one, optional): Express your level of happiness with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Very happy, Extremely unhappy).
* **Very Often Happy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_very_often`, select_one, optional): Express your level of happiness with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Very often, Extremely often).
* **Sometimes Happy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_sometimes`, select_one, optional): Express your level of happiness with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Sometimes, Extremely often).
* **Rarely Happy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_rarely`, select_one, optional): Express your level of happiness with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Rarely, Extremely often).
* **Always Happy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_always`, select_one, optional): Express your level of happiness with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Always, Extremely often).
* **Very Satisfied with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_satisfied`, select_one, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Very satisfied, Extremely unsatisfied).
* **Satisfied with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_very_satisfied`, select_one, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Satisfied, Extremely unsatisfied).
* **Dissatisfied with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_dissatisfied`, select_one, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Dissatisfied, Extremely unsatisfied).
* **Unhappy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_unhappy`, select_one, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Unhappy, Extremely unhappy).
* **Very Unhappy with School Administration** (`teacher_satisfaction_very_unhappy`, select_one, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with school administration (High, Medium, Low, Very unhappy, Extremely unhappy).
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other comments or thoughts about your engagement and satisfaction with school administration.
