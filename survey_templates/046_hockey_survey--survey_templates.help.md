# Hockey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hockey Survey is designed to collect information about hockey players' experiences, preferences, and habits related to training. This information will help improve the quality and effectiveness of hockey training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Ensure you complete all questions, as they are all optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_experience_level_with_hockey**: Please select your level of experience in hockey.
* **how_many_times_per_week_do_you_train** (`training_frequency`): This question asks about the frequency of your training sessions per week.
* **what_time_of_year_do_you_start_training** (`training_time_of_year`): Please select the time of year you start training.
* **what_equipments_do_you_prefer** (`equipment_preferences`): Select the equipment you prefer for training.
* **what_are_your_feedback_points** (`feedback_points`): This is a text field for any additional feedback or comments.
* **in_what_environment_do_you_prefer_training** (`training_environment`): Select the environment you prefer for training.
* **what_is_your_training_intensity** (`training_intensity`): Please select your preferred training intensity.
* **what_are_your_training_goals** (`training_goals`): Select the goals you have for training.
* **do_you_prefer_training_with_a_coach** (`training_coach`): Select if you prefer training with a coach.
* **in_which_training_facility_do_you_prefer** (`training_facility`): Select the training facility you prefer.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_coaching** (`feedback_coaching`): Select how you feel about coaching.
* **how_many_days_off_per_week_do_you_tend_to_have** (`training_frequency_habits`): This question asks about your typical days off per week.
* **what_is_your_training_habits** (`training_habits`): Please select your training habits.
* **what_do_you_like_to_improve_in_your_training_environment** (`feedback_environment`): Select the areas you'd like to improve in your training environment.
* **how_intense_do_you_like_your_training** (`training_intensity_habits`): Select your preferred intensity level for training.
* **how_many_days_per_week_do_you_train** (`training_frequency_habits_2`): This question asks about your weekly training frequency.
* **do_you_prefer_training_with_a_coach** (`training_coach_2`): Select if you prefer training with a coach.
* **in_which_training_facility_do_you_prefer** (`training_facility_2`): Select the training facility you prefer.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_training_with_a_coach** (`feedback_coach_2`): Select how you feel about training with a coach.
* **how_many_times_do_you_train_per_week** (`training_frequency_habits_3`): This question asks about your weekly training frequency.
* **do_you_prefer_training_with_coach** (`training_coach_3`): Select if you prefer training with a coach.
* **what_do_you_prefer_in_your_training_facility** (`feedback_facility`): Select the areas you'd like to improve in your training facility.
* **how_intense_do_you_like_your_training** (`training_intensity_habits_3`): Select your preferred intensity level for training.
* **how_do_you_feel_about_your_training_facility** (`feedback_facility_2`): Select how you feel about your training facility.

Please note that all questions are optional, and you can choose any of the available options for each question. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the text field provided at the end of the form.
