# Social Dynamics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Dynamics Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the social dynamics within a team. It is a self-assessment tool that allows team members or team leaders to gather information about how the team interacts, communicates, and resolves conflicts. This information can be used to identify areas for improvement and develop strategies to enhance team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have access to the team's dynamics and communication styles.
2.  Answer each question honestly, based on your observations and experiences within the team.
3.  For select-type questions, choose the option that best describes your team's dynamics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Group Communication** (`group_communication`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended to gather information about the team's communication style within the group. Please provide a brief description of your team's communication style.
*   **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the roles played by team members in the team's dynamics. Choose all the roles that apply to your team, such as Leader, Supporter, Neutral, or Competitor.
*   **Conflict Patterns** (`conflict_patterns`, `text`, required/optional): This field is designed to explore the patterns of conflict within the team. Please provide a brief description of how conflicts arise and are resolved in your team.
*   **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field assesses the team's performance in terms of excellence, good, fair, or poor. Choose the option that best describes your team's performance.
*   **Team Conflicts** (`team_conflicts`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended to gather information about the conflicts within the team. Please provide a brief description of how conflicts affect your team's dynamics.
*   **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, `text`, required/optional): This field explores the team's overall dynamics, including how members interact, communicate, and work together. Please provide a brief description of your team's dynamics.
*   **Communication Styles** (`communication_styles`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the communication styles employed by team members, such as Direct, Indirect, Formal, or Informal. Choose all the styles that apply to your team.
*   **Team Roles** (`team_roles`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is designed to assess the team's roles, such as Diverging, Conforming, Compromising, or Conceding. Choose the option that best describes your team's roles.
*   **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended to explore how conflicts are resolved within the team. Please provide a brief description of your team's conflict resolution approach.
*   **Decision Making Process** (`decision_making_process`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field assesses the team's decision-making process, such as Autocratic, Participative, or Consensus. Choose the option that best describes your team's decision-making process.
*   **Group Size** (`group_size`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended to gather information about the team size and its impact on the team's dynamics. Please provide a brief description of your team size and its effects.
*   **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about the team's overall satisfaction levels, such as High or Low. Choose all the options that apply to your team's satisfaction levels.
*   **Team Satisfaction Reasons** (`team_satisfaction_reasons`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field explores the reasons behind the team's satisfaction levels, such as Good team spirit, Clear goals, Effective communication, Positive attitude, or Good team work. Choose all the reasons that apply to your team's satisfaction levels.
