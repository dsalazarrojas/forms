# Sincerity And Heart Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sincerity And Heart Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions on relationships, coaching, or personal development, focusing on emotional intelligence, sincerity, and authenticity. This survey aims to understand your perceptions and experiences in these areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the survey template options and select the most suitable one for your needs.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, providing numerical values for "emotional_openness" and select options for the other fields.
3. For "sincere_conversation" and "heartfelt_comments", provide detailed and sincere feedback.
4. Select your feedback on the survey itself in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **survey_template (Select One)**
*   survey_template: A selection of pre-made survey templates or types. Choose the one that best fits your needs.

### 2. **emotional_openness (Number)**
*   emotional_openness: Rate your emotional openness on a scale ( likely 1-10). This will help us gauge your emotional intelligence.

### 3. **authentic_relationships (Select Multiple)**
*   authentic_relationships: Select all that apply, indicating the authenticity in your relationships. (Yes/No)

### 4. **sincere_conversation (Text)**
*   sincere_conversation: Describe a recent conversation where you felt fully understood and accepted in your relationship.

### 5. **heartfelt_comments (Note)**
*   heartfelt_comments: Share any heartfelt comments or thoughts about your relationships or coaching experience.

### 6. **feedback (Select One)**
*   feedback: Rate the survey overall. Is it helpful? Was it well-structured? 

### 7. **coaching_goals (Select Multiple)**
*   coaching_goals: Indicate the goals you want to achieve through coaching or relationship building. (Yes/No)

### 8. **coaching_approach (Select One)**
*   coaching_approach: Select the coaching approach you think is most effective for you or your relationships. (Yes/No)

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Sincerity And Heart Survey effectively and provide valuable insights into your relationships and coaching experiences.
