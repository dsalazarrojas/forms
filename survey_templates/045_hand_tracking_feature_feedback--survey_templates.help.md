# hand_tracking_feature_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hand_tracking_feature_feedback form is used to collect feedback on the hand-tracking feature from team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form name**: This field is not required but provides a way for the team to track which form is being used to provide feedback.
2. Provide **rating 1**: Rate the feature's effectiveness on a scale (we assume this to be a 1-5 rating system).
3. Provide **rating 2**: Rate the feature's usability on a scale (we assume this to be a 1-5 rating system).
4. Enter **Comments**: Share any comments or suggestions you have about the feature.
5. Select **Assigned to**: Choose the team member who the feature was assigned to.
6. Enter **Email**: Enter your email address (we will not be collecting or storing any email addresses, but it's a required field for our internal tracking and reporting purposes).
7. Enter **Phone number**: This field is not required but provides a way for us to contact you if we need more information.
8. Select a **Date**: Choose the date when you last used the feature (this will help us track feedback over time).
9. Select a **Time**: Choose the time when you last used the feature (this will help us track feedback over time).
10. Enter **Notes**: Share any additional notes or comments about the feature.
11. (Optional) Enter **Form name 2**: If you need to provide feedback on a different form, enter its name here.
12. Select **Team member 1**: Choose the team member who the feature was assigned to (this is a second field to ensure we have multiple points of contact).
13. Select **Team member 2**: Choose the team member who the feature was assigned to (this is a second field to ensure we have multiple points of contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form name**: Enter the name of the form you are providing feedback on, if any.
* **Rating 1**: Rate the feature's effectiveness on a scale (assumed to be 1-5).
* **Rating 2**: Rate the feature's usability on a scale (assumed to be 1-5).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the feature.
* **Assigned to**: Choose the team member who the feature was assigned to.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for our internal tracking and reporting purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for more information.
* **Date**: Select the date when you last used the feature.
* **Time**: Select the time when you last used the feature.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the feature.
* **Form name 2**: (Optional) Enter the name of another form you are providing feedback on.
* **Team member 1**: Choose the team member who the feature was assigned to.
* **Team member 2**: Choose the team member who the feature was assigned to.

## Tips
Please ensure that all team members complete this form thoughtfully, providing accurate and detailed feedback on the hand-tracking feature.
