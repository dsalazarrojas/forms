# research_grant_proposal_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The research grant proposal intake form is for researchers to submit their proposals for research grants. This form helps us collect and evaluate project details, funding requirements, and team information for each proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read the form carefully**: Review the form to understand what information is required and why.
2. **Fill out each section carefully**: Complete each section with accurate and relevant information about your project.
3. **Submit your proposal**: Once you have filled out the form, submit it for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your research project.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including name, email, and institution affiliation.
- **Funding Requirements** (`funding_requirements`, text, required: false): Describe the funding requirements for your project, including budget breakdown and funding sources.
- **Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`, text, required: false): Outline the main objectives and goals of your research project.
- **Prophets** (`research_objectives_prophets`/`research_team_prophets`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether your project has any prophetic elements. 
  - If you select "Yes", describe the prophetic elements.
- **Research Team** (`research_team`, text, required: false): List the names and affiliations of your research team members.
- **Prophets** (`research_team_prophets`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether your research team has any prophetic elements. 
  - If you select "Yes", describe the prophetic elements.
- **Budget Details** (`budget_details`, number, required: false): Provide the detailed budget breakdown for your project.
- **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the project duration in months.
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the current status of your project. 
  - Select from "Active", "Inactive", or "Archived".
- **Funding Status** (`funding_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the current funding status of your project. 
  - Select from "Funded", "Unfunded", or "In Progress".
