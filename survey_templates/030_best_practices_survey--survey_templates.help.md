# Best Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist employees in completing the Best Practices Survey, which aims to gather feedback and insights on various aspects of the organization's processes and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Select the most accurate option for multiple-choice questions.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official employee record.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, text, required): Choose the department you belong to within the organization.
* **Tenure in Organization** (`employee_tenure`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your time with the organization.
* **Are you aware of the current Best Practices for your role?** (`best_practices_awareness`, select_one, required): Choose your level of awareness regarding the best practices for your role.
* **How often do you refer to the official process documentation?** (`documentation_referral_freq`, select_one, required): Select how frequently you refer to the documentation for guidance.
* **Quality of Current Process Documentation** (`documentation_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity and accuracy of the current process documentation.
* **How easy is it to implement these best practices?** (`implementation_ease_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of implementation of the best practices in your day-to-day work.
* **What is the biggest barrier to following best practices?** (`adoption_barrier_desc`, text, required): Describe any challenges or obstacles that prevent you from following the best practices.
* **Do you feel supported by management in adopting these processes?** (`management_support_status`, select_one, required): Choose how supported you feel regarding the best practices.
* **Which specific process is most in need of an update?** (`process_update_target`, text, required): Identify a process that requires improvement or updating.
* **Suggestion for improvement in that process** (`improvement_suggestion_text`, text, required): Describe the changes or improvements you would suggest for the identified process.
* **Describe a practice used by your team that should be shared** (`successful_team_practice_desc`, text, optional): Share a practice or approach that your team has found effective.
* **Have you received adequate training on best practices?** (`training_adequacy_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have received sufficient training on best practices.
* **What training format do you prefer for learning new processes?** (`preferred_training_format`, select_one, required): Select the type of training you find most effective.
* **Any other feedback regarding organizational processes** (`additional_survey_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional insights or suggestions you may have.
* **Date of Survey** (`survey_completion_date_stamp`, date, required): Enter the date you complete this survey.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If unsure about a question, provide the best answer based on your experience and understanding.
* Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask if you need clarification on any question.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help improve the organization's processes and practices.
