# Fish Farming Business Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your fish farming business, including its operational details, production methods, and management practices. It will help us understand your business needs and requirements, allowing us to provide you with more effective support and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, as this will help us understand your business better.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1 (first_page)**: Please provide a brief description of your fish farming business.
* **Business Name (business_name)**: Enter the name of your business, as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Business Address (business_address)**: Provide your business's physical address.
* **Business Phone (business_phone)**: Enter your business's contact phone number.
* **Business Email (business_email)**: Enter your business's email address.
* **Aquaculture Information (aquaculture_info)**: Describe the type of fish farming operation, such as freshwater or saltwater.
* **Farm Production Information (farm_production_info)**: Provide details about your farm's production capacity and processes.
* **Production Methods (production_methods)**: Select the method(s) your farm uses, such as Semi-Aquatic or Aquaculture. (Required)
* **Farm Production Methods (farm_production_methods)**: Select the method(s) used by your farm, such as Semi-Aquatic or Aquaculture. (Required)
* **Production System (production_system)**: Select any system(s) used in your farm, such as Aquaculture or Semi-Aquatic. (Optional)
* **Farm Production System (farm_production_system)**: Select any system(s) used in your farm, such as Aquaculture or Semi-Aquatic. (Optional)
* **Feed (feed)**: Describe the feed used by your farm.
* **Feed Type (feed_type)**: Select the type(s) of feed used by your farm, such as Grain-based feed or Algae-based feed. (Optional)
* **Biosecurity (biosecurity)**: Select whether your farm has biosecurity measures in place. (Optional)
* **Farm Biosecurity (farm_biosecurity)**: Select whether your farm has biosecurity measures in place. (Optional)
* **Disease Management (disease_management)**: Select whether your farm has disease management measures in place. (Optional)
* **Farm Disease Management (farm_disease_management)**: Select whether your farm has disease management measures in place. (Optional)
* **Disease Control (disease_control)**: Select whether your farm has disease control measures in place. (Optional)
* **Farm Disease Control (farm_disease_control)**: Select whether your farm has disease control measures in place. (Optional)
* **Water Quality (water_quality)**: Select the water quality of your farm, such as Good or Fair. (Optional)
* **Farm Water Quality (farm_water_quality)**: Select the water quality of your farm, such as Good or Fair. (Optional)
* **Farm Size (farm_size)**: Enter the size of your farm in numbers.
* **Farm Size Range (farm_size_range)**: Provide a description of your farm size.
* **Farm Location (farm_location)**: Enter your farm's location.

**Tips**: Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, and if you have any doubts or concerns, contact us for assistance.
