# Education Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Level Survey is designed to collect information about your level of education and related fields. This data will help us understand your background and experiences, which can inform decisions or policies related to education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the relevant sections that apply to you.
2. Please select your current level of education from the options provided.
3. If you have completed education other than the ones listed, please fill in the "Other" fields.
4. For each question, please provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Level** (`education_level_survey_page_1`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your current level of education.
* **What is your current level of education** (what_is_your_current_level_of_education, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the levels that apply to your current education (e.g., Bachelor, Master, PhD, Other).
* **What is your degree level** (what_is_your_degree_level, select_one, optional): Select the highest level of education you have completed (e.g., High School, Bachelor, Master, PhD).
* **What is your field of study** (what_is_your_field_of_study, text, optional): If your field of study is not listed, please fill in the "Other" field.
* **What is your level of English** (what_is_your_level_of_english, select_one, optional): Please select your level of English proficiency (e.g., Fluent, Basic, Average, Below Average).
* **What is your level of math** (what_is_your_level_of_math, select_one, optional): Please select your level of math proficiency (e.g., Fluent, Basic, Average, Below Average).
* **Education Background Other** (education_background_other, text, optional): If you have education background other than the ones listed, please fill in this field.
* **Years of Education** (years_of_education, number, optional): Please enter the number of years you have completed in your education.
* **Level of English Other** (level_of_english_other, text, optional): If you have a different level of English proficiency than listed, please fill in this field.
* **Level of Math Other** (level_of_math_other, text, optional): If you have a different level of math proficiency than listed, please fill in this field.
* **Education Background Other Detail** (education_background_other_detail, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about your education background.
* **Years of Education Other** (years_of_education_other, text, optional): If you have completed years of education other than the ones listed, please enter the number of years.
* **Level of English Other Detail** (level_of_english_other_detail, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about your level of English proficiency.
* **Level of Math Other Detail** (level_of_math_other_detail, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about your level of math proficiency.
