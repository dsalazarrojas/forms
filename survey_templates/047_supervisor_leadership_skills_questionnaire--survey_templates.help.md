# Supervisor Leadership Skills Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess a supervisor's leadership skills in various areas, such as coaching, communication, decision making, emotional intelligence, leadership, problem solving, and resilience. It will help supervisors self-evaluate their strengths and weaknesses in leadership and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please start by reading the introduction page for background and context.
- Select the relevant leadership skills that you possess or have used in your supervisory role.
- Rate your proficiency level for each selected skill using the rating scale provided.
- Provide examples or descriptions to support your ratings, if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)** (Note, Optional)
    This page provides background information and instructions for completing the questionnaire.
* **Supervisor Leadership Skills** (2) (Select One, Required)
    Please choose one leadership skill from the list that you feel best describes your role as a supervisor. The options provided are:
        - Coaching and Development
        - Communication
        - Decision Making
        - Emotional Intelligence
        - Leadership
        - Problem Solving
        - Resilience
        - Time Management
* **Supervisor Leadership Skills Rating** (3) (Number, Required)
    Rate your proficiency level for the selected leadership skill using the scale provided.
* **Coaching** (4) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Please select all the coaching styles that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Coaching for Results
        - Developmental Coaching
        - Situational Coaching
* **Communication** (5) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Select all the communication skills that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Communication Skills
        - Conflict Resolution
        - Interpersonal Relationships
* **Decision Making** (6) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Choose all the decision making skills that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Critical Thinking
        - Decision Making
        - Problem Analysis
* **Emotional Intelligence** (7) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Select all the emotional intelligence skills that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Emotional Awareness
        - Self-Awareness
        - Social Awareness
* **Leadership** (8) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Select all the leadership skills that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Change Management
        - Leadership
        - Strategic Management
* **Problem Solving** (9) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Select all the problem solving skills that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Analytical Thinking
        - Problem Analysis
        - Situational Problem Solving
* **Resilience** (10) (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Please select all the resilience skills that you have used in your supervisory role:
        - Adaptability
        - Flexibility
        - Resilience

## Tips
- Be honest when rating your proficiency levels.
- Use the provided examples or descriptions to support your ratings.
- You can use the feedback from this questionnaire to identify areas for improvement and develop a plan to enhance your leadership skills.
- If you are unsure about any of the skills or options, please ask your HR representative for clarification.
- Please note that this form is for your personal development and feedback purposes only.
