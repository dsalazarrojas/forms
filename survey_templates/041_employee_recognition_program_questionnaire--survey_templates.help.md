# Employee Recognition Program Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Recognition Program Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your preferences and experiences with recognition programs in the company. Your input will help us improve and develop our recognition program to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Select all that apply for the questions marked "select multiple" (e.g., "How do you currently recognize your employees?", "Which recognition programs do you participate in?").
* Choose one option for the questions marked "select one" (e.g., "What type of recognition do you prefer?", "How soon do you want to receive recognition?").
* Enter your answer in the text fields (e.g., "How often do you receive recognition?", "Comments").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Intro**: A brief introduction to get you started.
* **How do you currently recognize your employees?**: Please select all the methods you currently use to recognize your employees.
* *Manager Role*: Please enter your job role in the company.
* *How important is recognition to you?*: Please rate on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is not important and 10 is very important.
* *How often do you receive recognition?*: Please enter the frequency of recognition you receive, for example, "daily", "weekly", "monthly".
* *What type of recognition do you prefer?*: Please select the type of recognition that resonates with you.
* *How soon do you want to receive recognition?*: Please choose the timing of recognition, for example, "immediately", "soon after", "at the end of the month".
* *How often would you like to receive recognition?*: Please enter the frequency of recognition you would like to receive.
* *Recognition Type2*: A duplicate of the previous question for consistency and redundancy (not actually necessary, but following the pattern).
* *Recognition Value2*: Please rate the importance of recognition on a scale of 0-10.
* *Recognition Timing2*: Please choose the timing of recognition.
* *Recognition Frequency3*: A duplicate of the "How often do you receive recognition?" question for redundancy (not necessary).
* *What recognition programs do you participate in?*: Please select all the recognition programs you currently participate in.
* *Recognition Amount2*: Please rate the amount of recognition you receive, for example, "a lot", "a little", "none".
* *Which recognition programs do you like?*: Please select all the recognition programs you prefer.
* *What type of recognition do you like?*: Please select the type of recognition you prefer.
* *Recognition Amount3*: Another duplicate question not really necessary, but following the pattern.
* *Recognition Timing3*: Please choose the timing of recognition.
* *Recognition Frequency4*: Another duplicate question not really necessary.
* *Recognition Type4*: Another duplicate question not really necessary.
* *Recognition Amount4*: Another duplicate question not really necessary.
* *Comments*: Please enter any additional comments or feedback about recognition in the company.
