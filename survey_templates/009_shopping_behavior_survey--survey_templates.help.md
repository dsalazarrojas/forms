<thinking>Shopping Behavior Survey - Help Guide

This help guide is intended for end-users who need to fill out the "Shopping Behavior Survey" form. Before we dive into the form, let's consider whether the form is relevant to your needs and if there are any potential duplicate submissions.

The form is relevant if you are interested in learning more about your shopping behavior, including how often you shop, where you shop, and what types of products you buy online and in-store. The form is not a formal survey, but rather a series of questions aimed at gathering information to help improve shopping experiences.

To determine relevance and potential duplicate submissions, please consider the following:
- Do you work in a role that would benefit from this data? (e.g., marketing, product development, customer support)
- Have you previously completed this or a similar form?

If you've already completed this form or a similar one, your responses may have been recorded and are likely still relevant to your current shopping habits. In this case, completing the form again may not provide new information and may be considered a duplicate submission.

## Purpose
The "Shopping Behavior Survey" form is designed to gather information about your shopping habits and preferences. This form helps us understand how you interact with products, channels, and channels of distribution, such as online and in-store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. Your responses will help us understand your shopping habits.
2. Select the options that best match your shopping behavior for each question.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.

* **How often do you shop?** (`shopping_frequency`): Please select how often you shop, on a scale from "Never" to "Daily".

* **What types of products do you prefer?** (`product_preferences`): Choose the types of products you prefer to buy, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Which shopping channels do you use?** (`shopping_channels`): Select all the channels you use for shopping, such as online, in-store, or through other channels.
* **How often do you shop online?** (`shopping_frequency_online`): Please select how often you shop online, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **How often do you shop in-store?** (`shopping_frequency_instore`): Please select how often you shop in-store, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **What types of products do you buy online?** (`product_type_online`): Choose the types of products you buy online, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **What types of products do you buy in-store?** (`product_type_instore`): Choose the types of products you buy in-store, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **What are your customer preferences?** (`customer_preferences`): Please describe your preferences, such as any specific features or characteristics you look for when shopping.
* **How often do you shop in stores?** (`shopping_frequency_store`): Please select how often you shop in stores.
* **How often do you shop on websites?** (`shopping_frequency_website`): Please select how often you shop on websites.
* **Which online shopping channels do you use?** (`shopping_channels_online`): Select all the online shopping channels you use.
