# RNA Seq Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for RNA Seq Analysis from various labs, including lab details, contact information, and analysis specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the specific workflow stage of the RNA Seq Analysis in the "rna-seq-analysis-workflow" field.
2.  Enter the name of the lab performing the analysis in the "lab-name" field.
3.  Enter a valid contact email address in the "contact-email" field.
4.  Enter a valid contact phone number in the "contact-phone" field.
5.  Select multiple workflow stages in the "workflow-stage2" field.
6.  Enter the name of the contact person in the "contact-person" field.
7.  Enter the role of the contact person in the "contact-role" field.
8.  Optionally select whether the contact person is part of a specific department in the "contact-department" field.
9.  Enter the date of the analysis in the "date" field.
10. Enter the time of the analysis in the "time" field.
11. Optionally select multiple time zones in the "time-zones" field.
12. Optionally select multiple date zones in the "date-zones" field.
13. Enter the contact email address of the person to be copied on the feedback in the "contact-email-cc" field.
14. Optionally enter the contact phone number of the person to be copied on the feedback in the "contact-phone-cc" field.
15. Enter the name of the person to be copied on the feedback in the "contact-name" field.
16. Enter the role of the person to be copied on the feedback in the "contact-role-cc" field.
17. Optionally select whether the person to be copied on the feedback is part of a specific department in the "contact-department-cc" field.
17. Enter any additional notes about the form in the "notes" field.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields.
*   If you need to provide additional feedback or comments, use the "feedback" and "comments" fields.
*   If you need to provide any additional notes or suggestions, use the "notes" and "suggestions" fields.
*   Use the "contact-email-cc", "contact-phone-cc", and "contact-name" fields to specify who to copy on the feedback.
*   Be aware of the mandatory fields, as they must be completed for the form to be submitted correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
