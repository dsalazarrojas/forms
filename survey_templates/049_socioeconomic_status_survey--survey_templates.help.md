# socioeconomc_status_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the household's socioeconomic status, which is essential for analysis and research purposes. The data collected will help us understand the demographics and characteristics of our target population.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have read and understood the purpose of this form and the instructions provided.
2. Answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked as required).
4. You can select multiple options for select_multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Income** (household_income, number, required): Enter your household's monthly income.
* **Education Level** (education_level, select_one, required): Select the highest education level achieved by the household head or a member of the household.
	+ Less than High School
	+ High School Diploma
	+ Postgraduate Degree
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Type** (household_type, select_multiple, required): Select the types of household members.
	+ Single Parent
	+ Single Couple
	+ Multi-parent
	+ Couple
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Family Size** (family_size, number, optional): Enter the number of household members.
* **Employment Status** (employment_status, select_one, required): Select the employment status of the household head or a member of the household.
	+ Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Language** (household_language, select_one, required): Select the language spoken by the household.
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ Mandarin
	+ Arabic
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Age** (household_age, number, required): Enter the age of the household head or a member of the household.
* **Household Occupation** (household_occupation, select_multiple, required): Select the occupations of the household members.
	+ Blue Collar
	+ White Collar
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Location** (household_location, select_one, required): Select the location of the household.
	+ City
	+ Suburbs
	+ Rural
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Income** (household_income, number, required): Enter your household's monthly income again for verification purposes.
