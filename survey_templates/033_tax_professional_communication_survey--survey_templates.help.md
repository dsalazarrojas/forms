# Tax Professional Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Professional Communication Survey is designed to gather feedback from taxpayers about their experiences with tax professionals. This survey helps firms understand how to improve their communication style and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your experience with the tax professional you have worked with.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Quality**: Select the level of communication quality you experienced with the tax professional. This field allows you to rate the clarity and effectiveness of the professional's communication.
	+ Good: The tax professional communicated clearly and effectively.
	+ Poor: The tax professional had difficulty communicating with you.
* **Firm Name**: Enter the name of the firm you worked with.
* **Responsiveness**: Select the level of responsiveness you experienced with the tax professional.
	+ Value1: The tax professional responded promptly to your inquiries.
	+ Value2: The tax professional responded, but not always in a timely manner.
	+ Value3: The tax professional did not respond.
* **Firm Email**: Enter the email address of the firm you worked with.
* **Firm Phone**: Enter the phone number of the firm you worked with.
* **Clarity**: Select the level of clarity you experienced with the tax professional.
	+ Value1: The tax professional's communication was clear and easy to understand.
	+ Value2: The tax professional's communication was mostly clear but had some confusion.
	+ Value3: The tax professional's communication was unclear.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the tax professional.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or context that you think might be helpful for the firm.
* **Recommendation**: Select whether you would recommend the tax professional to others.
	+ Yes: Yes, I would recommend them.
	+ No: No, I would not recommend them.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the tax professional on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Survey Completed**: Date you completed this survey.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required. Please only submit your honest feedback if you have had a recent experience with a tax professional.
