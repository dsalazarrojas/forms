# Natural Overlay Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Overlay Feedback Form is designed to gather information from users about their experiences with a specific application or device, focusing on natural overlay feedback. This form aims to collect data on the device used, feedback type, issues encountered (if any), and suggestions for improvement, as well as provide a space for detailed comments and screenshots for further reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the device used (Android, iOS, or Other) in the "Device Used" field.
2. Choose the type of feedback you wish to provide (Positive, Negative, or both) in the "Feedback Type" field. You can select one or multiple options.
3. If you have encountered any issues, select the relevant categories (Product, UX, or Support) in the "Issue Encountered" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
4. In the "Issue Description" field, provide a brief description of the issue you encountered.
5. If you have suggestions for improvement, write them down in the "Suggestions" field.
6. Describe the natural overlay in the "Natural Overlay" field.
7. In the "Issue Screenshots" field, attach screenshots related to the issue (if any) and provide a brief description to help us better understand the issue.
8. In the "Feedback" field, provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Device Used** (`device_used`): Select the device you used to access the application or interact with the natural overlay.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`): Indicate the type of feedback you wish to provide (Positive, Negative, or both).
* **Issue Encountered** (`issue_encountered`): If you encountered any issues, choose the relevant categories (Product, UX, or Support) to help us better understand the problem.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): Provide a brief description of the issue you encountered.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): Write down any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Natural Overlay** (`natural_overlay`): Describe the natural overlay you experienced.
* **Issue Screenshots** (`issue_screenshots`): Attach screenshots related to the issue (if any) and provide a brief description to help us better understand the problem.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing the issue and providing feedback.
* If you have screenshots related to the issue, please attach them to help us better understand the problem.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask and we will be happy to clarify.
