# technology_usage_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on technology usage from users, which will help us better understand how technology is being used within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the technologies that apply to you from the "Technology Used" field.
2. Enter the number of hours you use each selected technology per week in the "Hours Used Per Week" field.
3. Rate the technology from 1-5 in the "Rating" field.
4. Fill out your "Email" and "Phone" fields with your contact information.
5. Enter the date and time you started using the technology in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Fill out the "Additional Comments" field if you have any other information about your technology usage.
7. Fill out the "Notes" field if you have any other comments or information about your technology usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (Select Multiple): Select all the technologies that you use, such as "Yes" or "No" for each technology.
* **Technology Used** (Select One): Select one technology that you use, such as "Mobile", "Laptop", or "Desktop".
* **Hours Used Per Week** (Number): Enter the number of hours you use the selected technology per week.
* **Rating** (Number): Rate the technology from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date you started using the technology.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time you started using the technology.
* **Additional Comments** (Note): Fill out this field if you have any additional comments or information about your technology usage.
* **Notes** (Note): Fill out this field if you have any other comments or information about your technology usage.
