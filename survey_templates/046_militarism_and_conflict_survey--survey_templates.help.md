# Militarism And Conflict Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on how individuals perceive militarism and their experiences with conflict. It's intended to collect information on personal views, conflict resolution approaches, and opinions on peace efforts. The survey will help us better understand the complexities of militarism and its impact on individuals and society.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form's questions and instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to share them in the "Comments" field.
