# Medical Procedure Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to simulate a medical procedure, gathering information about the patient and the doctor performing the procedure. It is intended for educational or training purposes, and should not be used to collect actual patient data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer as accurately as possible.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice for your simulation.
3. Fill out all fields truthfully, but do not enter real patient information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Please enter your simulated patient's information as you would in a real-world scenario.
* **What type of procedure do you want to simulate?**: Choose the type of procedure you want to simulate (e.g., surgery, medical check-up, etc.).
* **What medical condition does the patient have?**: Select all relevant medical conditions for the simulated patient.
* **What medical procedure are you performing?**: Choose the medical procedure you are simulating (e.g., surgery, check-up, etc.).
* **Location of procedure**: Select the location where the procedure will take place (e.g., hospital, clinic, etc.).
* **Age of patient**: Enter the simulated patient's age.
* **Years of experience as a surgeon**: Enter your own years of experience as a surgeon.
* **Gender of patient**: Select the gender of the simulated patient.
* **Gender of surgeon**: Select your own gender.
* **Hospital name**: Enter the simulated hospital name.
* **Doctor's name**: Enter your simulated doctor's name.
* **Date of procedure**: Enter the date of the simulated procedure.
* **Time of procedure**: Enter the time of the simulated procedure.
* **Doctor's specialty**: Select the simulated doctor's specialty.
* **Patient status**: Select the simulated patient's status (Active or Inactive).
* **Location details**: Enter any relevant location details for the simulated procedure.
* **Height of patient**: Enter the simulated patient's height.
* **Weight of patient**: Enter the simulated patient's weight.
* **Doctor's note**: Enter any relevant notes from the simulated doctor.
* **Nurse's note**: Enter any relevant notes from the simulated nurse.
* **Email of patient**: Enter the simulated patient's email address.
* **Phone of patient**: Enter the simulated patient's phone number.
* **Doctor's license**: Enter your simulated doctor's license number.
* **Patient condition details**: Enter any relevant details about the simulated patient's condition.
* **Address of hospital**: Enter the simulated hospital's address.
* **Doctor's note**: Enter any relevant notes from the simulated doctor.
* **Nurse's note**: Enter any relevant notes from the simulated nurse.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Do not enter real patient information.
* Review each field carefully before submitting.
