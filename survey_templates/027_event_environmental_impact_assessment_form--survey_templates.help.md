# Event Environmental Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Environmental Impact Assessment Form is designed to collect essential information about an event, including event specifics, sustainability efforts, and performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant page for the information you are entering (Event Information, Event Details, Sustainability Efforts, or Performance Metrics).
2. Type or enter the information requested in the corresponding field. For each field, be as clear and concise as possible.
3. Use the "Submit" button to save your entries and move to the next page or complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Information (Event Information)** (`event_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the event, including its name, date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Event Details (Event Details)** (`event_details`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the event, including its goals, objectives, and any notable features or attractions.
* **Sustainability Efforts (Sustainability Efforts)** (`sustainability_efforts`, `text`, optional): Describe the event's sustainability efforts, such as recycling programs, energy-efficient practices, or eco-friendly materials used.
* **Performance Metrics (Performance Metrics)** (`performance_metrics`, `text`, optional): Report performance metrics related to the event, including attendance, carbon footprint, or waste management data.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when answering each question, as it will help in assessing the environmental impact of the event.
- If you are unsure about a field, feel free to contact the form creators for clarification.
- Remember that the information collected will be used to identify areas of improvement for future events.
