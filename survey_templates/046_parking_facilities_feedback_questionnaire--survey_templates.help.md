# parking_facilities_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on parking facilities to help improve and evaluate their quality and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each section below, providing your honest and concrete feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parking Facilities Rating
* **Parking Facilities Rating** (`parking_facilities_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the parking facilities from 1-10, where 1 is Bad and 10 is Good.
### Parking Facilities Satisfaction
* **Parking Facilities Satisfaction** (`parking_facilities_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the parking facilities from 1-10, where 1 is Bad and 10 is Good.
### Parking Facilities Services
* **Parking Facilities Services** (`parking_facilities_services`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the parking facilities services as Good, Fair, or Bad.
### Parking Facilities Facility Condition
* **Parking Facilities Facility Condition** (`parking_facilities_facility_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Assess the condition of the parking facilities. Choose one or more of the options Good, Fair, or Bad.
### Parking Facilities Parking Options
* **Parking Facilities Parking Options** (`parking_facilities_parking_options`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Evaluate the parking options provided at the facilities. Choose one or more of the options Good, Fair, or Bad.
### Parking Facilities Accessibility
* **Parking Facilities Accessibility** (`parking_facilities_accessibility`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the parking facilities accessibility as Good, Fair, or Bad.
### Parking Facilities Facility Ease
* **Parking Facilities Facility Ease** (`parking_facilities_facility_easiness`, `number`, required: false): Rate how easy or difficult it was to navigate through the parking facilities from 1-10, where 1 is Very Difficult and 10 is Very Easy.
### Parking Facilities Parking Options Ease
* **Parking Facilities Parking Options Ease** (`parking_facilities_parking_options_easiness`, `number`, required: false): Rate how easy or difficult it was to find parking options within the facilities from 1-10, where 1 is Very Difficult and 10 is Very Easy.
### Parking Facilities Accessibility Ease
* **Parking Facilities Accessibility Ease** (`parking_facilities_accessibility_easiness`, `number`, required: false): Rate how easy or difficult it was to access the parking facilities from 1-10, where 1 is Very Difficult and 10 is Very Easy.
### Parking Facilities Satisfaction Why
* **Parking Facilities Satisfaction Why** (`parking_facilities_satisfaction_why`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Provide reasons why you are satisfied or not satisfied with the parking facilities. Choose one or more of the options Good, Fair, or Bad.
### Parking Facilities Satisfaction Why
* **Parking Facilities Satisfaction Why** (`parking_facilities_satisfaction_why_text`, `text`, required: false): Provide more detailed reasons why you are satisfied or not satisfied with the parking facilities.
### Parking Facilities Additional Comments
* **Parking Facilities Additional Comments** (`parking_facilities_additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Leave any additional comments or suggestions about the parking facilities.
