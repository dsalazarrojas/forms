# Mental Health Research Blog Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from mental health researchers to facilitate the publication of their work on our blog.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Participant Information section with your basic details.
2. In the Experience Description section, provide a brief description of your research experience.
3. Choose the Mental Health Topic that best describes your publication.
4. Select how long your research experience lasted.
5. Choose the methods of contact you have (phone, email, social media).
6. Choose the type of publication you are submitting (online, offline).
7. Choose how often you publish (daily, weekly, etc.).
8. If you select "Other" in the above option, provide details in the Publication Frequency Other field.
9. Enter the Location of your publication (e.g. online platform, academic journal).
10. Select the date of publication.
11. Choose the type of publication (e.g. one-time, periodic).
12. Provide details on what you plan to publish (in the Publication Agenda field).
13. Sign the Publication Consent section to confirm your agreement.
14. Confirm your publication consent status.
15. Sign the Publication Consent Agreement section to confirm your understanding of the consent terms.
15. Sign the Participant Signature section to confirm your participant consent.
16. Sign the Researcher Signature section to confirm your researcher consent.
16. Enter the date and time of submission.
17. Enter the date and time you agreed to publish.
18. Confirm the publication consent status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (1): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This section is for you to provide your basic details, including name, title, and contact information. Please fill in your participant information accurately and truthfully.

* **Experience Description** (2): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This section is for you to provide a brief description of your research experience. Be as specific as possible so we can understand your background.

* **Mental Health Topic** (3): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This is to help us categorize your publication under the relevant mental health topic. Please choose the best option that applies to your work.

* **Experience Duration** (4): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This is to help us understand how long your research experience lasted. Choose the option that best reflects your situation.

* **Contact Information** (5): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This section is for you to provide the methods of contact you have, such as phone, email, or social media.

* **Type of Publication** (6): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This is to choose the type of publication you are submitting, whether it's online or offline.

* **Publication Frequency** (7): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This is to choose how often you publish, whether daily, weekly, etc. If you select "Other", please provide details in the next field.

* **Publication Frequency Other** (8): <em>Required: No</em>
  * If you chose "Other" in the above field, please provide details on how often you publish here.

* **Location** (10): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the location of your publication, e.g. online platform, academic journal.

* **Publication Date** (11): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please select the date of publication.

* **Type of Publication Frequency** (12): <em>Required: No</em>
  * This is to choose the type of publication, e.g. one-time, periodic.

* **Publication Agenda** (13): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please provide details on what you plan to publish.

* **Publication Consent** (14): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please sign this section to confirm your consent to publication.

* **Publication Consent Agreement** (15): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please sign this section to confirm your understanding of the consent terms.

* **Participant Signature** (16): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please sign this section to confirm your participant consent.

* **Researcher Signature** (17): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please sign this section to confirm your researcher consent.

* **Date** (18): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the date of submission.

* **Time** (19): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the time of submission.

* **Participant Agreed Date** (20): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the date you agreed to publish.

* **Participant Agreed Time** (21): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the time you agreed to publish.

* **Researcher Agreed Date** (22): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the date you agreed to publish.

* **Researcher Agreed Time** (23): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please enter the time you agreed to publish.

* **Publication Consent Status** (24): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please confirm your publication consent status.

* **Participant Consent Status** (25): <em>Required: No</em>
  * Please confirm your participant consent status.
