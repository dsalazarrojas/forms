# Employee Leave Management Software Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Employee Leave Management Software Review Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Read through the introduction and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
*   Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
*   Select the type of software you are using from the "Software Type" dropdown menu.
*   Select all the leave types that your company uses with the software from the "Leave Types" dropdown menu. You can select multiple types.
*   Enter your feedback on the adoption of the software in the "Feedback on Adoption" field.
*   Rate your overall satisfaction with the software in the "Feedback on Satisfaction" field.
*   Select the improvement priority for the software from the "Improvement Priorities" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Company Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
*   **Software Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of software you are using.
*   **Leave Types** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the leave types your company uses with the software.
*   **Feedback on Adoption** (number, optional): Rate your feedback on the software adoption (e.g. 1-5 scale).
*   **Feedback on Satisfaction** (number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the software (e.g. 1-5 scale).
*   **Improvement Priorities** (select_one, optional): Select the improvement priority for the software (e.g. usability, features, support).

## Tips
*   Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
*   You can select multiple leave types for the "Leave Types" field.
*   Use the feedback on adoption and satisfaction fields to rate your overall experience with the software.
*   Select the improvement priority that best reflects the area you think needs improvement the most.
