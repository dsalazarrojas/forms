# social_work_introduction_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The social work survey template is used to gather information about clients, their support needs, and the services they require. This form is designed to help social workers understand the client's situation, identify their priorities, and determine the best course of action for their support and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information, including their preferred contact method and frequency.
2. Select the support services they need, such as emotional, financial, or practical support.
3. Choose the services they require and the delivery method for those services.
4. Determine the follow-up schedule and method for their ongoing support.
5. Add any additional notes and comments about the client's culture and service fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This section is used to collect basic information about the client.
* **Support Needs**: Indicate whether the client has support needs, with options for emotional, financial, or practical support.
* **Preferred Contact**: Select the preferred method of contact, such as Phone, Email, or Other.
* **Contact Frequency**: Choose the frequency of contact, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Client Support**: Select the type of support the client requires, with options for Emotional, Financial, Practical, or Spiritual.
* **Support Services**: Indicate the number of support services the client requires.
* **Client Goals**: Choose the client's goals, with options for Improve Daily Functioning, Build Support Network, or Address Mental Health.
* **Client History**: Provide a brief history of the client's situation.
* **Client Relationships**: Select the types of relationships the client has, such as Spouse, Family, or Friends.
* **Service Requests**: Indicate the number of support services the client requires.
* **Assessment Date**: Record the date of the assessment.
* **Social Worker Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the client's situation.
* **Social Worker Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for the client's support and care.
* **Client Feedback**: Allow the client to provide feedback about their experience.
* **Client Significance**: Record any information about the client's cultural background.
* **Service Delivery**: Select the method of service delivery, with options for In-Office, Off-Site, or Telephonic.
* **Follow-up Scheduling**: Indicate whether follow-up scheduling is needed.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Choose the frequency of follow-up, with options for Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Client Culture**: Provide any additional information about the client's culture.
* **Client Culture Additional**: Allow for any additional information about the client's culture.
* **Service Fees**: Record the service fees for the client.
* **Follow-up Method**: Choose the method of follow-up, with options for Phone Call, Email, or In-Person.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Outcome**: Record the outcome of the follow-up.
* **Client Significance (Additional)**: Allow for any additional information about the client's cultural background.
* **Service Fees (Additional)**: Record any additional service fees.
