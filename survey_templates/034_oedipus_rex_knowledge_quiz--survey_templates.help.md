# Oedipus Rex Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of the classic Greek tragedy, Oedipus Rex. The form is divided into several sections that cover various aspects of the play, including character knowledge, plot knowledge, and theme knowledge. Your goal is to fill in the form with your responses to the questions and submit them for review.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by navigating to the "Quiz Start" page.
2. On each subsequent page, read the hint carefully and select the most appropriate answer to each question.
3. For questions that require multiple answers, choose all applicable options.
4. For questions that require a single answer, select the most accurate response from the options provided.
5. When you have completed all the questions, click the "Submission" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Start** (`page 1`, `text`, not required): This page is the introduction to the quiz, and you can type in any information you'd like to get started.
* **Character Knowledge** (`character_knowledge`, `text`, required): This question is asking about the basic knowledge of characters in the play, specifically who the main characters are. Please type in your answer, considering the characters Oedipus, Jocasta, Laius, and Creon.
* **Plot Knowledge** (`plot_knowledge`, `text`, required): This question is asking about the central theme of the play, which is related to Oedipus' story. Please type in your answer, considering the plot's events and their consequences.
* **Main Characters** (`main_characters`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the main characters from the options provided. This will help us understand your knowledge of the play's characters.
* **Main Characters Answer** (`main_characters_answer`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all applicable main characters that you are familiar with. This will help us evaluate your knowledge of the play's characters.
* **Plot Outcome** (`plot_outcome`, `select_one`, required): Select the tragic end of the play from the options provided. This will help us understand your understanding of the play's events.
* **Theme Knowledge** (`theme_knowledge`, `text`, required): This question is asking about the main theme of the play, which is related to Oedipus' story. Please type in your answer, considering the play's message and significance.
* **Theme Knowledge Answer** (`theme_knowledge_answer`, `select_one`, required): Select the most accurate theme from the options provided.
* **Reading Group** (`reading_group`, `text`, not required): This question is asking about the social context in which you have encountered the play. Please type in any relevant information about your reading group.
* **Reading Group Answer** (`reading_group_answer`, `select_one`, not required): Select how you encountered the play from the options provided.
* **Class** (`class`, `text`, not required): This question is asking about the class in which you have studied the play. Please type in any relevant information about your class.
* **Class Answer** (`class_answer`, `text`, not required): This question is asking for your class's response.
* **Tutor** (`tutor`, `text`, not required): This question is asking about your tutor's response.
* **Tutor Answer** (`tutor_answer`, `text`, not required): This question is asking for your tutor's answer.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `note`, not required): This is the final page where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your answers.
* **Submission Answer** (`submission_answer`, `text`, not required): This is where you can type in any additional comments about your submission.
