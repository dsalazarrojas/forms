# Business Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information on the impact of business disruptions on organizations. It is designed to gauge the severity of these disruptions and the measures taken to recover. This survey will help us understand the effects on operations, finance, employees, and customers, as well as the resilience measures taken and future concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct option for each question based on your experience.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in text fields (e.g., employee_impact, supplier_challenges, customer_satisfaction, resilience_measures, future_concerns, support_needed, additional_comments).
3. Ensure all required fields (marked with `required: true`) are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (`respondent_name`, text, required: true):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Your Role (`respondent_role`, text, required: true):** Describe your position or involvement within the organization.
* **Email Address (`respondent_email`, email, required: false):** Enter your company email address if you have one.
* **Business Size (`business_size`, select_one, required: false):** Select the number of employees in your organization.
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-50
	+ 51-200
	+ 201-500
	+ 500+
* **Industry Sector (`industry_sector`, text, required: false):** Describe the sector or industry your business operates in.
* **Primary Operations Location (`operations_location`, text, required: false):** Specify the geographic area where your operations primarily take place.
* **Impact on Operations (`operational_impact`, select_one, required: true):** Assess the severity of the impact on operations.
	+ Severe disruption
	+ Significant impact
	+ Moderate impact
	+ Minor impact
	+ No impact
* **Financial Impact (`financial_impact`, select_one, required: true):** Evaluate the severity of the financial impact.
	+ Severe loss
	+ Significant loss
	+ Moderate loss
	+ Minor loss
	+ Positive impact
* **Revenue Change Percentage (`revenue_change`, number, required: false):** If applicable, enter the percentage change in revenue.
* **Impact on Employees (`employee_impact`, select_one, required: true):** Describe the effect on employees.
	+ Many lost jobs
	+ Some job losses
	+ Reduced hours
	+ Remote work transition
	+ No impact
* **Supply Chain Disruption (`supply_disruption`, select_one, required: true):** Assess the severity of supply chain disruption.
	+ Complete shutdown
	+ Severe constraints
	+ Moderate constraints
	+ Minor constraints
	+ No disruption
* **Key Supplier Challenges (`supplier_challenges`, text, required: false):** List the challenges faced with suppliers.
* **Customer Demand Changes (`customer_demand`, select_one, required: true):** Evaluate the change in customer demand.
	+ Severe decline
	+ Moderate decline
	+ Slight decline
	+ Stable
	+ Increased demand
* **Customer Satisfaction Impact (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false):** Assess the change in customer satisfaction.
	+ Significantly decreased
	+ Decreased
	+ Unchanged
	+ Improved
	+ Significantly improved
* **Expected Recovery Timeline (`recovery_timeline`, select_one, required: false):** Estimate the timeframe for recovery.
	+ Within 1 month
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ Over 1 year
	+ Uncertain
* **Resilience Measures Taken (`resilience_measures`, text, required: false):** Describe the steps taken to build resilience.
* **Future Concerns or Vulnerabilities (`future_concerns`, text, required: false):** Identify the future concerns or vulnerabilities.
* **Support Needed (`support_needed`, text, required: false):** Specify the type of assistance or support that would be helpful.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, required: false):** Include any other information you think is relevant to your situation.
