# Sports Facility User Satisfaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your overall satisfaction with the sports facility you visited.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To ensure accuracy, please complete the form based on your most recent visit to the sports facility.
2. If you have visited the facility multiple times, your answers should reflect your overall impression.
3. Enter the correct facility name as it is known internally.
4. Indicate your satisfaction with the overall facility in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
5. Share any suggestions or comments in the "Suggestions" field.
6. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address and/or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the sports facility you visited (e.g. "Local Gym").
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility (e.g. "Indoor", "Outdoor").
* **Facility Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the facility from 1-5, where 1 means poor and 5 means excellent.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the facility from 1-5, where 1 means very unsatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions or comments you have regarding your visit.
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or comments about your visit.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Visit Date**: Provide the date of your visit to the sports facility.
* **Facility Type**: This field is not currently being used, but we appreciate any feedback you provide.

Note: The fields "Facility Type" and "Phone" are not required, as they do not currently have any impact on the overall evaluation process.
