# User Experience (UX) Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Experience (UX) Assessment Form is designed to collect information from users about their experience with a product or service. This form will help us understand your satisfaction level, impressions of our product design, and other relevant aspects of your interaction with our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your answer from the multiple-choice questions.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience with the product or service in the text fields.
3. Answer the numerical questions with a value between 1 and 100.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Experience** (`user_experience`): Choose how would you describe your overall experience with our product or service?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Product Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name of the product or service you are assessing.
* **User Name** (`user_name`): Please enter your name so we can associate your feedback with your account.
* **Experience Description** (`experience_description`): Describe your experience with the product or service in a few sentences.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`): Choose how satisfied you are with our product or service.
	+ Select a value between 1 and 100.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): If you have any suggestions or feedback, please let us know.
* **Product Design** (`product_design`): Choose how good is our product or service design?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`): Choose how satisfied you are with our service.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Product Features** (`product_features`): Choose which features of our product or service you have used.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **User Improvement** (`user_improvement`): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know.
* **Improvement Status** (`improvement_status`): Choose the status of your suggestion.
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Improvement Details** (`improvement_details`): Describe your suggestion for improvement.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): If you have any additional feedback, please let us know.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Choose how would you rate our product or service?
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating Value** (`rating_value`): Enter a value between 1 and 100 to rate our product or service.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`): Choose the scale for your rating.
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Product Name (2)** (`product_name_2`): Enter the name of the second product or service you are assessing.
* **Experience Description (2)** (`experience_description_2`): Describe your experience with the second product or service.
* **User Name (2)** (`user_name_2`): Please enter your name so we can associate your feedback with your account.
* **Rating (2)** (`rating_2`): Choose how would you rate our second product or service?
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Rating Value (2)** (`rating_value_2`): Enter a value between 1 and 100 to rate our second product or service.
* **Product Name (3)** (`product_name_3`): Enter the name of the third product or service you are assessing.
* **Experience Description (3)** (`experience_description_3`): Describe your experience with the third product or service.
* **User Name (3)** (`user_name_3`): Please enter your name so we can associate your feedback with your account.
* **Product Features (2)** (`product_features_2`): Choose which features of our second product or service you have used.
	+ Yes
	+ No
