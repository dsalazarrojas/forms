# Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey form. The form is used to collect data on customer satisfaction, visit frequency, services offered, and recommendations for the nail salon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing each field and understand its purpose.
2. For each field, select the most accurate answer based on your experience at the nail salon.
3. Fill in all the required fields (marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ Required: Yes
