# Audio Editing Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Editing Feedback Survey is a tool to gather opinions and insights from clients on the quality of audio editing services received. This form helps identify areas of improvement, track progress, and foster a relationship with clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Select the most accurate option for each question based on your experience.
3.  Provide a detailed answer for open-ended questions (e.g., "What specific areas could be improved?" and "Any additional feedback for the editor?").
4.  Use the "Submit" button to submit your completed survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name\***: Please enter the name of the client you received the service from.
*   **Project Title or Episode Name\***: Enter the title of the project or episode you received the service for.
*   **Date Project Received\***: Enter the date you received the project or episode.
*   **Clarity and Balance of Sound\***: Rate how clear you can hear all elements in the audio.
	+   5 - Perfect: The audio is crystal clear and well-balanced.
	+   4 - Very Good: The audio is mostly clear, but with some minor issues.
	+   3 - Average: The audio is somewhat clear, but with noticeable issues.
	+   2 - Poor: The audio is not clear, with significant issues.
	+   1 - Unacceptable: The audio is completely unworkable.
*   **Background Noise Reduction\***: Rate how effective the noise cleanup was.
	+   Extremely Effective: The noise was almost completely removed.
	+   Effective: The noise was significantly reduced.
	+   Neutral: The noise was not much changed.
	+   Ineffective: The noise was not changed.
*   **Smoothness of Transitions and Edits\***: Rate how natural the cuts and edits feel.
	+   Seamless: The cuts and edits feel like a natural flow.
	+   Mostly Smooth: The cuts and edits feel mostly natural, but with some issues.
	+   Noticeable Edits: The cuts and edits feel somewhat unnatural.
	+   Abrupt/Jarring: The cuts and edits feel very unnatural.
*   **Background Music and FX Levels\***: Rate how well-balanced the background music and FX levels are with the dialogue.
	+   Well Balanced: The music and FX levels are well-balanced with the dialogue.
	+   Music too loud: The music and FX are too loud for the dialogue.
	+   Voice too loud: The dialogue is too loud for the music and FX.
	+   Needs more FX: The music and FX levels need improvement.
*   **Timeliness of Delivery\***: Rate how timely the service was.
	+   Early: The project was delivered before the deadline.
	+   On Time: The project was delivered on time.
	+   Slightly Late: The project was delivered a bit late.
	+   Very Late: The project was delivered much later than expected.
*   **Communication throughout the process\***: Rate how responsive the editor was.
	+   Excellent: The editor was very responsive and communicative.
	+   Good: The editor was generally responsive and communicative.
	+   Fair: The editor was somewhat responsive and communicative.
	+   Poor: The editor was not very responsive and communicative.
*   **Would you use our editing services again?\***: Rate your likelihood of using the service again.
	+   Definitely: I would definitely use the service again.
	+   Likely: I would probably use the service again.
	+   Unlikely: I would not use the service again.
*   **Any additional feedback for the editor?\***: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
*   **What specific areas could be improved?\***: Please provide specific areas for improvement for the service.
