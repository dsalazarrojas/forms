# Business Support Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Support Satisfaction Poll is a form designed to gather feedback on the support services provided by the company to business owners. The goal is to assess the overall satisfaction with the support process, its accessibility, quality, and timeliness, as well as the expertise level of support staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please select the relevant answers based on your experience with the support services.
2.  Fill in the required details such as your business name and date of the poll.
3.  Select the options for each question based on your satisfaction level with the support services received.
4.  For the 'Satisfaction with Support' question, please select your overall satisfaction level with the support services received.
5.  For any question that does not apply to your experience, please select 'N/A' or 'Not Applicable'.
6.  Provide any additional suggestions for improvement in the 'Suggestions for Improvement' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Owner Name** (`business_owner_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, text, optional): Select your industry or business sector (if applicable).
* **Poll Date** (`poll_date`, date, required): Select the date of the poll.
* **Have You Used Support Services** (`support_usage`, select_one, required): Did you receive support? Select 'Yes, Recently', 'Yes, Previously', or 'False'.
* **Type of Support Received** (`support_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all types of support services you received (e.g., Consulting Services, Financial Advice, etc.).
* **Accessibility of Support Services** (`support_accessibility`, select_one, required): Select how easy it was to access support (e.g., Very Easy, Easy, Adequate, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **Quality of Support Services** (`support_quality`, select_one, required): Select the quality of the service received (e.g., Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Response Time and Timeliness** (`response_timeliness`, select_one, required): Select how quickly support was provided (e.g., Very Timely, Timely, Acceptable, Slow, Very Slow).
* **Expertise Level of Support Staff** (`expertise_level`, select_one, required): Select the expertise level of the support staff (e.g., Highly Knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Adequate, Limited Knowledge, Lacking).
* **Problem Resolution** (`problem_resolution`, select_one, required): Select how much issue was resolved (e.g., Fully Resolved, Mostly Resolved, Partially Resolved, Not Resolved).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the support services received (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Impact on Business** (`business_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of support on your business (e.g., Significant Positive Impact, Positive Impact, Some Impact, Limited Impact, No Impact).
* **Likelihood to Use Again** (`likelihood_future_use`, select_one, required): Select the likelihood to use support again (e.g., Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Select whether you would recommend the support services to others (e.g., Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, Strongly Oppose).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Support Needs** (`additional_support_needs`, text, optional): Provide any additional support needs you have.

Note: If any question does not apply to your experience, please select 'N/A' or 'Not Applicable'.
