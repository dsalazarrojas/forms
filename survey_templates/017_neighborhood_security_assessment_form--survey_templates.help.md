# Neighborhood Security Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neighborhood Security Assessment Form is designed to collect information about neighborhood safety and security concerns from residents. It will help us understand the community's perception of safety and inform our efforts to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer the questions according to your genuine experiences and concerns about your neighborhood's safety.
3. For questions that ask you to choose from multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Use the free text field to provide any additional comments or information that you think are relevant to your answers.
5. When finished, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Neighborhood Safety Rating
*   **Neighborhood Safety Rating** (`neighborhood_safety_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall safety feeling about your neighborhood. Use a number between 1 (very unsafe) and 3 (very safe).

### 2. Crime Rate
*   **Crime Rate** (`crime_rate`, `number`, required): How often do you experience crime in your neighborhood? Use a number between 0 (never) and 3 (often).

### 3. Crime Type
*   **Crime Type** (`crime_type`, `select_one`, required): What type of crime do you experience in your neighborhood? Select one of the following: Murder, Violent Crime, Property Crime, Other.

### 4. Crime Frequency
*   **Crime Frequency** (`crime_frequency`, `number`, required): How often do you experience the crime type you selected in your neighborhood? Use a number between 0 (never) and 3 (often).

### 5. Crime Location
*   **Crime Location** (`crime_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Where do you experience the crime type you selected in your neighborhood? Select all that apply: Home, Work, School, Other.

### 6. Witness Statement
*   **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, `note`, required): Please describe what you have witnessed or experienced in your neighborhood. This can be a brief summary or a more detailed account, whichever you prefer.
