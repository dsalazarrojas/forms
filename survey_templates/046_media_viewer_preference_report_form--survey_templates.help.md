## media_viewer_preference_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from media viewers to report their preferences and feedback, helping us better understand their needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the following information:

1. **Viewer Type**: Select the type of viewer you are, from the options provided.
2. **Age Group**: Select all that apply from the list of age groups.
3. **Viewing Frequency**: Enter the number of times you have viewed our media content in the past month.
4. **Satisfaction**: Enter the amount of time you spend watching our media content on a given day. (Note: This field is optional.)
5. **Favorite Feature**: Select your favorite feature from the options provided. (Note: This field is optional.)
6. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about our media content.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
8. **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
9. **Other Contact Info**: Enter any other contact information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Viewer Type** (Viewer Type 1, Viewer Type 2, Viewer Type 3): Select the type of viewer you are, such as a casual viewer or a frequent viewer.
* **Age Group** (Age Group 1, Age Group 2, Age Group 3, Age Group 4, Age Group 5, Age Group 6): Select all that apply from the list of age groups.
* **Viewing Frequency** (Number): Enter the number of times you have viewed our media content in the past month.
* **Satisfaction** (Time): Enter the amount of time you spend watching our media content on a given day. (This field is optional.)
* **Favorite Feature** (Favorite Feature 1, Favorite Feature 2, Favorite Feature 3): Select your favorite feature from the options provided. (This field is optional.)
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about our media content.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Other Contact Info**: Enter any other contact information you would like to provide.
