# HOA Community Improvement Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOA Community Improvement Voting Form is used to gather input from residents on proposed community improvement projects. Please use this form to provide your opinions and suggestions on the projects listed below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the project name and description.
2. Select your vote for each project (Yes, No, or Vote for another project).
3. Provide any additional comments you'd like to share about the project.
4. Enter your name and email address (if you'd like to receive updates on the project).
5. Enter your contact phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the project you are voting on.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the proposed project.
* **Improvement Priority** (`improvement_priority`, select_one, required: false): Select Yes if you support the project, or No if you do not.
* **Vote For Project** (`vote_for_project`, select_multiple, required: true): Select Yes if you support the project, or No if you do not. Select one or multiple options.
* **Vote Against Project** (`vote_against_project`, select_multiple, required: false): Select Yes if you do not support the project, or No if you do not.
* **Vote Not Project** (`vote_not_project`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is not necessary and can be ignored.
* **Resident Comments** (`resident_comments`, note, required: false): Share any additional comments you have about the project.
* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we know who submitted the feedback.
* **Resident Email** (`resident_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates on the project.
* **Resident Phone** (`resident_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive a follow-up call from the HOA staff.

## Tips
- Be honest and respectful in your feedback. Your comments will be reviewed by the HOA board and may influence their decisions.
- Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it to ensure it is clear and concise.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the HOA staff or board.
