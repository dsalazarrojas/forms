# team_productivity_barriers_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to collect information about potential barriers to team productivity. It is meant to gather data on incidents that have hindered team performance and provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the team name in the designated field.
2. Enter the department in which the team operates.
3. Provide your manager's name, if applicable.
4. Describe the barrier to productivity that you encountered.
5. Give feedback on the barrier and its impact on the team.
6. Suggest ways to improve the situation.
7. Enter the date of the incident.
8. Enter the time of the incident, if applicable.
9. Enter your email address so that your feedback can be contacted for further discussion.
10. Provide any additional notes on the incident.
11. Leave feedback for your manager on the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Name** (team_name, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
- **Department** (department, text, required): Enter the department your team belongs to.
- **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager, if applicable.
- **Barrier** (barrier, text, required): Describe the specific barrier to productivity you encountered.
- **Feedback** (feedback, text, required): Provide feedback on the barrier and its impact on the team.
- **Suggestion** (suggestion, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the situation.
- **Date** (date, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
- **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident, if applicable.
- **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address so that your feedback can be contacted for further discussion.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
- **Notes** (notes, note, required): Provide any additional notes on the incident.
- **Manager Feedback** (manager_feedback, text, required): Leave feedback for your manager on the incident.
