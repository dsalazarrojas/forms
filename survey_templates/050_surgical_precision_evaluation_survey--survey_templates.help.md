# Surgical Precision Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data from surgeons and medical professionals for evaluating the precision and quality of surgical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into two main sections: **First Page**, **Patient Information**, **Procedure Information**, and **Questions**. 

1. **First Page**: This page is a brief introduction to the form and is not intended to be completed by users.
2. **Patient Information**: Please fill in the patient's information, including name, email address, and any other relevant details.
3. **Procedure Information**: Select the specific surgical procedure that was performed, and any other relevant details.
4. **Questions**: Answer each question in the following format:
	* For multiple-choice questions (select_one, select_multiple): Choose one or more relevant options from the list.
	* For text questions (text, number): Enter a brief answer in the provided space.
	* For date and time questions: Enter the date and time in the format MM/DD/YY HH:MM AM/PM.
	* For email questions: Enter the patient's email address.
