# Invasive Plant Removal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is designed to collect data on invasive plant removal activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date the survey was completed in the "Date Survey" field.
2. Enter the name of the location where the removal activity took place in the "Location Name" field.
3. Provide any additional notes about the location in the "Location Notes" field.
4. Choose the method used for removal in the "Removal Method" field (e.g. Invasive species removal, Non-invasive species removal, or Other - specify).
5. If "Other - Specify" was selected, enter a brief description of the method used in the "Other - Specify" field.
6. Enter the names of the species removed in the "Species Removed 1", "Species Removed 2", etc. fields. 
7. Enter the size of the area that was surveyed in hectares in the "Area Surveyed" field.
8. Enter the number of hours spent on the removal activity in the "Removal Effort Hours" field.
9. Enter the number of persons that worked on the removal activity in the "Removal Effort Persons" field.
10. Enter any outcomes from the removal activity in the "Removal Outcomes" field.
11. Provide any additional notes about the survey in the "Survey Notes" field.
12. Check the box if photos were attached to the survey in the "Photos Attached" field.
13. If photos were attached, enter a brief description of the photos in the "Other Photos - Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, text, optional): Enter any information that will be displayed at the top of the survey.
* **Date Survey** (`date_survey`, date, optional): Enter the date the survey was completed.
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the location where the removal activity took place.
* **Location Notes** (`location_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the location.
* **Removal Method** (`removal_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the method used for removal (e.g. Invasive species removal, Non-invasive species removal, or Other - specify).
* **Other - Specify** (`removal_method_other`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the removal method if "Other - Specify" was selected.
* **Species Removed 1** (`species_removed_1`, text, optional): Enter the name of the first species removed.
* **Species Removed 2** (`species_removed_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second species removed.
* **Area Surveyed** (`area_surveyed`, number, optional): Enter the size of the area that was surveyed in hectares.
* **Removal Effort Hours** (`removal_effort_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours spent on the removal activity.
* **Removal Effort Persons** (`removal_effort_persons`, number, optional): Enter the number of persons that worked on the removal activity.
* **Removal Outcomes** (`removal_outcomes`, text, optional): Enter any outcomes from the removal activity.
* **Survey Notes** (`survey_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the survey.
* **Photos Attached** (`photos_attached`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box if photos were attached to the survey.
* **Other Photos** (`other_photos`, text, optional): Enter the file name or a brief description of the photos taken.
* **Other Photos - Details** (`other_photos_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the photos taken.
