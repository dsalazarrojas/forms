# Bridal Party Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from bridal party members about their role, preferences, and availability. It helps us understand how to best support and accommodate your needs during the wedding party events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with accurate and honest information about yourself and your availability.
2. Make sure to review all the fields carefully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bridesmaid or Attendant Name** (`attendant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Bride's Name** (`bride_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the bride.
* **Your Role in the Bridal Party** (`role_in_party`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the bridal party (Maid of honor, Bridesmaid, Groomsman, Usher, Other attendant).
* **Your Relationship to the Bride** (`relationship_to_bride`, select_one, required): Choose how you know the bride (Best friend, Close friend, Sister or family, College friend, Coworker, Other).
* **Dress Size** (`dress_size`, select_one, optional): Select your typical dress size (XS, S, M, L, XL, 1X, 2X, 3X+).
* **Dress Style Preference** (`dress_style_preference`, text, optional): Enter your preferred dress style.
* **Dress Budget Comfort Level** (`dress_budget`, select_one, optional): Select your dress budget comfort level (Under 100, 100 - 200, 200 - 300, 300 - 400, 400+).
* **Shoe Size** (`shoe_size`, text, optional): Enter your shoe size.
* **Shoe Style Preference** (`shoe_preference`, text, optional): Enter your preferred shoe style.
* **Hair and Makeup Preference** (`hair_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose if you want hair and makeup done (Yes, with professional services, Maybe, depends on cost, No, I will do my own, Not applicable).
* **Preferred Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_multiple, optional): Select any color preferences (Blush and gold, Navy and silver, Burgundy and champagne, Green and copper, Pastels, Bold and vibrant, Neutral tones).
* **Accessibility or Mobility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Enter any special accommodations.
* **Number of Guests You Can Invite** (`guest_list`, select_one, optional): Choose how many plus-ones you can invite (Plus 0, Plus 1, Plus 2, Plus 3, Plus family, Other).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions or allergies (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut allergy, Other allergy, None).
* **Events You Will Attend** (`event_participation`, select_multiple, required): Choose which events you will attend (Bridal shower, Bachelorette party, Rehearsal dinner, Wedding day, Post-wedding brunch, All events).
* **Willing to Help With** (`event_responsibilities`, select_multiple, optional): Choose what you are willing to help with (Planning events, Hosting events, Decorating, Running errands, Day-of coordination, Speech or toasts, Other).
* **Budget Concerns** (`budget_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any financial constraints.
* **Availability Concerns** (`availability_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any conflicts with work, school, or other commitments.
* **Special Requests or Questions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any other information or requests.
