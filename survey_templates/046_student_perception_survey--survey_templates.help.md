# Student Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect students' perceptions of their learning environment and experiences in a course. The goal is to gather information about students' satisfaction with various aspects of their courses, including teacher support, course materials, classroom environment, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the options that best describe your experiences and perceptions.
2. Enter your answers in the spaces provided.
3. Click "Submit" to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Template** (`survey_template`, `Note`, Not required): This is the title of your course. Please enter the name of your course.
* **Student Experience** (`student_experience`, `Text`, Not required): Describe your experiences in this course. Please provide a brief statement about your overall experience in this course.
* **Student Learning** (`student_learning`, `Text`, Not required): Describe your understanding and engagement with course material. Please provide a brief statement about your learning experience.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, `Select One`, Not required): Select "Yes" if you feel you have received sufficient support from your teacher.
* **Teacher Climate** (`teacher_climate`, `Select Multiple`, Not required): Select all the aspects that describe your experience with your teacher.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, `Text`, Not required): Describe the materials provided in the course. Please provide a brief statement about the materials.
* **Course Organization** (`course_organization`, `Text`, Not required): Describe the organization of the course material. Please provide a brief statement about the course structure.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, `Text`, Not required): Provide any feedback about the course or teacher.
* **Classroom Environment** (`class_size`, `Select One`, Not required): Select "Yes" if you feel the classroom environment is suitable for learning.
* **Teacher Technology** (`teacher_technology`, `Select Multiple`, Not required): Select all the aspects that describe your experience with your teacher's technology use.
* **Course Content** (`course_content`, `Select One`, Not required): Select "Yes" if you feel the course content is relevant to your needs.
* **Course Relevance** (`course_relevance`, `Select One`, Not required): Select "Yes" if you feel the course is relevant to your needs.
* **Teacher Flexibility** (`teacher_flexibility`, `Select Multiple`, Not required): Select all the aspects that describe your experience with your teacher's flexibility.
* **Course Structure** (`course_structure`, `Select Multiple`, Not required): Select all the aspects that describe your experience with course structure.
* **Teacher Help** (`teacher_help`, `Select One`, Not required): Select "Yes" if you feel you receive sufficient help from your teacher.
* **Class Size** (`class_size`, `Select One`, Not required): Select "Yes" if you feel the class size is suitable for learning.
* **Instructor Help** (`instructor_help`, `Select Multiple`, Not required): Select all the aspects that describe your experience with your instructor's help.
* **Teacher Help Flexibility** (`teacher_help_flexibility`, `Select Multiple`, Not required): Select all the aspects that describe your experience with your teacher's help flexibility.
* **Teacher Availability** (`teacher_availability`, `Time`, Not required): Enter your available times for your teacher.
* **Teacher Availability Flexible** (`teacher_availability_flexible`, `Time`, Not required): Enter your flexible available times for your teacher.
* **Teacher Availability Unflexible** (`teacher_availability_unflexible`, `Time`, Not required): Enter your unflexible available times for your teacher.
* **Student Availability** (`student_availability`, `Time`, Not required): Enter your available times for your studies.
* **Student Availability Flexible** (`student_availability_flexible`, `Time`, Not required): Enter your flexible available times for your studies.
* **Student Availability Unflexible** (`student_availability_unflexible`, `Time`, Not required): Enter your unflexible available times for your studies.
* **Student Perception** (`student_perception`, `Number`, Not required): Rate your perception of your learning experience on a scale of 1-5.
