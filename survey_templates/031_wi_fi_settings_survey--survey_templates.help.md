# Wi-Fi Settings Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your current Wi-Fi settings and experiences. This will help us better understand your needs and provide more suitable Wi-Fi services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current Wi-Fi network provider from the list provided.
2. Estimate the number of devices connected to your Wi-Fi network.
3. Choose your preferred Wi-Fi speed from the given options.
4. Select how often you use your Wi-Fi for streaming.
5. Check the box if you have any issues with your current Wi-Fi connection.
6. Count the devices you typically use on your Wi-Fi network at home.
7. Estimate your monthly data usage.
8. Check the box if you use a router or access point for your Wi-Fi.
9. Select your current internet plan speed.
10. Estimate the number of users connected to your Wi-Fi network.
11. Check the boxes for any Wi-Fi-related issues you experience at home or work.
12. Check the box if you plan to increase your Wi-Fi speed in the next 6 months.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current Wi-Fi network provider?** (`current_provider`, `select_one`, optional): This question helps us understand your current network provider. Please select the one that matches your current service provider.
* **How many devices are connected to your Wi-Fi network?** (`devices_connected`, `number`, optional): This question is to help us understand the number of devices connected to your network. Please count the devices you currently have connected.
* **What is your preferred Wi-Fi speed?** (`preferred_speed`, `select_one`, optional): This question helps us understand your required speed for a better online experience. Please select the speed that suits your needs.
* **How often do you use your Wi-Fi for streaming?** (`streaming_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This question is to understand your streaming habits. Please select how often you stream your videos and music.
* **Do you have any issues with your current Wi-Fi connection?** (`connection_issues`, `checkbox`, optional): Check the box if you have any issues with your connection.
* **How many devices do you typically use on your Wi-Fi network at home?** (`devices_at_home`, `number`, optional): This question helps us understand how many devices you use on your network. Please count the devices you typically use.
* **How much data do you use per month on your Wi-Fi network?** (`monthly_data`, `number`, optional): This question is to help us understand your monthly data usage. Please estimate your data usage.
* **Do you use a router or access point for your Wi-Fi?** (`router_or_access_point`, `checkbox`, optional): Check the box if you use a router.
* **What is your current internet plan speed?** (`internet_plan_speed`, `select_one`, optional): This question is to help us understand your current plan speed. Please select the correct speed.
* **How many users are connected to your Wi-Fi network?** (`users_at_network`, `number`, optional): This question is to help us understand the number of users connected to your network. Please estimate the number of users.
* **Do you have any Wi-Fi-related issues at home or work?** (`wifi_issues_at_home_work`, `checkbox`, optional): Check the box if you experience any issues.
* **Do you plan to increase your Wi-Fi speed in the next 6 months?** (`plan_to_increase_speed`, `checkbox`, optional): Check the box if you plan to upgrade your speed.
