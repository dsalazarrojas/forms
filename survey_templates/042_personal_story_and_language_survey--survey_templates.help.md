# Personal Story And Language Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Story And Language Survey is designed to collect information about your personal background, language skills, and experiences with multilingualism. This form will help us better understand your language abilities and backgrounds, which will inform educational and research initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Enter any additional information that you think is relevant to your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Multilingual Background**: Please indicate if you have a background in more than one language.
* **Language Use**: Select all the languages you use in your daily life.
* **Personal Narratives**: Please write a personal experience that reflects your background and language use.
* **Demographics**: Please select your ethnicity from the options provided.
* **Contact Info**: Select the method of contact that best suits you.
* **Language Skills**: Select all the language skills you possess.
* **Language Abilities**: Indicate your proficiency level in listening, reading, speaking, and writing in languages other than your native language.
* **Multilingual Language Use**: Please describe how you use languages other than your native language in your daily life.
* **Language Ability Score**: Please indicate your self-assessed proficiency level in languages other than your native language.
* **Education Level**: Please select your educational level.
* **Language Usage**: Please describe how you use languages other than your native language in your daily life.
* **Multilingual Family**: Indicate if you have family members who speak languages other than your native language.
* **Language Skills (continued)**: Please select all the additional language skills you possess, beyond reading, speaking, writing, and listening.
* **Language Abilities (continued)**: Please indicate your self-assessed proficiency level in each language you possess.
* **Language Usage (continued)**: Please describe how you use languages other than your native language in your daily life.
