# Passenger Safety Perception Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect passenger feedback on their safety experience during a trip with a driver.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the answer for each question based on your personal experience with the driver.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as this will help us understand your safety concerns and improve driver performance.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all required for a complete assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has the driver been involved in any incidents** (`safety_concern`, `select_one`): If yes, please select 'Yes' from the dropdown menu.
* **If yes, what was the location** (`incident_location`, `select_multiple`): Select all locations that apply to the incident(s) that occurred.
* **What type of incident occurred** (`incident_type`, `select_one`): Select the type of incident that occurred, if applicable.
* **Date of incident** (`incident_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the incident in the required format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Name of driver** (`driver_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the driver who was involved in the incident.
* **Name of passenger** (`passenger_name`, `text`): Enter your name as the passenger.
* **Safety rating** (`safety_rating`, `number`): Rate your overall safety experience with the driver on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `note`): Enter any additional comments or observations you have about your safety experience with the driver.
* **How do you feel about your overall safety** (`general_safety_feeling`, `select_one`): Select 'Yes' if you feel your safety was good, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Are there any areas for improvement** (`safety_improvement`, `select_multiple`): Select all areas where you think there is room for improvement.
* **How would you rate the driver's performance** (`driver_performance`, `select_one`): Rate the driver's performance as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **How would you rate the driver's attitude** (`driver_attitude`, `select_one`): Rate the driver's attitude as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`): Select 'Yes' if you had a positive experience, and 'No' otherwise.
