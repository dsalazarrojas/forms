# Participant Mid Point Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from participants on their experience with the program, focusing on their likes, dislikes, and perceptions of the program's quality and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. Fill in the blanks with your thoughts and opinions.
3. Make sure to complete all the required fields, if any.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Survey Form** (`participant_survey_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a general information field where you can write any thoughts, comments, or feedback about the program.
* **What did you like most about the program** (`participant_survey_question_1`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe the aspect of the program that you enjoyed the most.
* **What did you like least about the program** (`participant_survey_question_2`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the aspect of the program that you found least enjoyable or problematic.
* **What was the most helpful resource or activity during the program** (`participant_survey_question_3`, text, optional):
	+ Identify the most useful or valuable resource or activity you encountered during the program.
* **Were there any challenges or barriers to your participation in the program** (`participant_survey_question_4`, text, optional):
	+ If you experienced any difficulties or challenges, describe them here.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the program** (`participant_survey_question_5`, number, optional):
	+ Answer on a scale (e.g., 1-5) to indicate your overall impression of the program's quality.
* **How likely are you to recommend the program to a friend or colleague** (`participant_survey_question_6`, number, optional):
	+ Answer on a scale (e.g., 1-5) to indicate how likely you are to recommend the program.
* **How would you rate the program in terms of meeting its objectives** (`participant_survey_question_7`, number, optional):
	+ Answer on a scale (e.g., 1-5) to gauge your perception of the program's effectiveness in meeting its goals.
* **What could the program do in the future to improve** (`participant_survey_question_8`, text, optional):
	+ Suggest any ideas or improvements for the program's future development.

## Tips

- Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this feedback will help us better understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to write them down, even if they don't fit in the provided fields.
