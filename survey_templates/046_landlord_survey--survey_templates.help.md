# Landlord Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from landlords to help us better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First name (First name)** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
   Please enter your first name in the field provided.
2. **Last name (Last name)** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
   Enter your last name in this field.
3. **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required: false)
   Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
4. **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`contact_number`, text, required: false)
   Enter your contact number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXXX-XXX-XXXX` if you are not from the United States.
5. **Move-in date (Move-in date)** (`move_in_date`, date, required: false)
   Select the date you moved into your current accommodation using the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter the first name of the person filling out the form.
* **Last name**: Enter the last name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person filling out the form in the format `username@domain.com`.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the person filling out the form in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Move-in date**: Select the date you moved into your current accommodation using the calendar provided.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are experiencing any issues while filling out the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not submit it again.
* If you are not a landlord, please do not fill out this form.
