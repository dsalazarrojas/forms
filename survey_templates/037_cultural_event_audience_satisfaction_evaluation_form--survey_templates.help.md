# Cultural Event Audience Satisfaction Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from event attendees to help us improve future cultural events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your honest feedback about your recent cultural event attendance.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3. For select multiple questions (e.g., Will Recommend, Will Return), select all that apply.
4. For select one questions (e.g., Assigned to, Event Type), select only one option that best describes your experience.
5. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Please enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Date Attended**: Enter the date you attended the event.
* **Time Attended**: Enter the time you attended the event.
* **Performance Rating**: Rate the performance of the event on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations or suggestions you have for future events.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for future events).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Assigned to**: Choose who you were assigned to attend the event with (if applicable). If not assigned, select "Other".
* **Other Assigned to**: If you selected "Other", please enter the name(s) of the person(s) you attended with.
* **Pages Visited**: Enter the number of pages or areas you visited during the event.
* **Time Spent**: Enter the amount of time you spent at the event.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the event on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Will Recommend**: Indicate if you will recommend the event to friends or not.
* **Why Didn't Recommend**: If you selected "No" for "Will Recommend", explain why.
* **Why Recommended**: If you selected "Yes" for "Will Recommend", enter why you recommended the event.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you attended (e.g., Art, Music, Performance).
* **Other Event Type**: If you selected "Other" for "Event Type", enter the type of event you attended.
* **Event Frequency**: Indicate how often you attend events like this one.
* **Other Event Frequency**: If you selected "Other" for "Event Frequency", enter how often you attend events like this one.
* **Will Return**: Indicate if you will return to this event or not.
* **Will Recommend to Friend**: Indicate if you will recommend this event to a friend or not.
* **Will Recommend to Family**: Indicate if you will recommend this event to your family or not.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
