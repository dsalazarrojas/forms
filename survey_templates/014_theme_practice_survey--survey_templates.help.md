# Theme Practice Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the Theme Practice Survey form, which is a comprehensive template for creating and customizing surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a theme from the options provided in the "Survey Theme" field.
2. Select the practice options that apply to your survey in the "Practice" field.
3. Enter a survey title and description in the "Survey Title" and "Survey Description" fields respectively.
4. Select the category for your survey in the "Survey Category" field.
5. Customize your survey questions and themes as needed in the "Survey Questions" and "Survey Theme" fields.
6. Enter any additional details such as email, phone number, and date/time as needed in the corresponding fields.
