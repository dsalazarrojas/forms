# Architectural Influence Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This quiz is designed to gauge your knowledge and interest in architectural design, covering topics such as sustainability, historic preservation, and accessibility. The quiz is composed of five questions about your understanding of architectural design principles and your personal interests in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, which includes your name, email address, and organization or institution (if applicable).
2. Answer the five questions about your knowledge of architectural design principles, choosing from the provided options.
3. Indicate how much architectural design has influenced you, choosing from the given options.
4. Select all the design topics that interest you the most.
5. If you are interested in architecture as a career, choose the appropriate option.
6. Finally, indicate the difficulty level of the quiz.
7. You can add any additional comments or questions in the last field.
