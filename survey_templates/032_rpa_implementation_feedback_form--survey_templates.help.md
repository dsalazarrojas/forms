RPA Implementation Feedback Form - Help Guide
=====================================

### Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from end users about their experience with the Robotic Process Automation (RPA) implementation in our organization. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the quality of our services.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability, and click "Submit" to send your feedback to our team. If you need assistance or clarification on any of the questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
#### 1. **RPA Implementation Feedback** (`rpa_implementation_feedback_form`, Select One, Required)
This field asks about your level of satisfaction with the RPA implementation. Choose from the following options:
* Very Satisfied
* Somewhat Satisfied
* Somewhat Dissatisfied
* Very Dissatisfied

#### 2. **Overall Experience Rating** (`rpa_implementation_feedback_form_rating`, Number, Required)
Please rate your overall experience with the RPA implementation on a scale from 1 to 10.

#### 3. **Percentage of Automated Tasks** (`rpa_implementation_automated_tasks`, Number, Required)
Please enter the percentage of tasks that were automated during this implementation.

#### 4. **Current Level of Satisfaction** (`rpa_implementation_satisfaction`, Select One, Required)
This field asks about your current level of satisfaction with the RPA implementation. Choose from the following options:
* Very Satisfied
* Somewhat Satisfied
* Somewhat Dissatisfied
* Very Dissatisfied

#### 5. **Area for Improvement** (`rpa_implementation_improvement_area`, Select Multiple, Required)
Please select one or more areas where you would like to see improvement in the RPA implementation. Choose from the following options:
* Task Management
* Process Efficiency
* Communication
* Training
* Other (please specify)

#### 6. **Comments** (`rpa_implementation_comments`, Note, Optional)
If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them here.

#### 7. **Feedback Email** (`rpa_implementation_feedback_form_email`, Email, Optional)
Please enter your email address if you would like to receive a follow-up on your feedback.

#### 8. **Feedback Phone** (`rpa_implementation_feedback_form_phone`, Text, Optional)
If you would prefer to receive a call from our team to discuss your feedback, please enter your phone number here.

Note: The optional fields (email and phone) are only required if you choose to use them. If not, simply leave them blank.
