# Vacation Questioner - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather basic information about users' vacation plans, including whether they have a plan, their current state of mind about taking a vacation, and some basic contact and category information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions as they appear on each page.
2.  Fill in the relevant information for each question, making sure to choose the correct option for select type fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Already thinking about your vacations?** (`vacation_plan`, `text`, optional): This is a simple text field where you can type a few sentences about your current thoughts about taking a vacation. This is an optional field.
*   **Got that plan in your head?** (`got_a_plan`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question has two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Please choose which one best describes your current state of mind about taking a vacation.
*   **Help yourself with this survey!** (`get_going`, `select_one`, optional): This question also has two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. Again, choose the one that best describes your current state of mind about this survey.
*   **Get going** (`vacation_details`, `email`, optional): This is an email input field, but please note that it is not for the intended recipient of the email, but rather the internal contact information of the person taking the survey.
*   **Duration** (`vacation_duration`, `number`, optional): Please enter the number of days or weeks you plan to take for your vacation. This will help us to determine the duration of your absence from work.
*   **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, optional): Please enter your expected return date from your vacation.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): This is a text field for your phone number. Please enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): This is another text field for your address. This will help us to know where you are staying during your vacation.
*   **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for categorizing your form submission. Please choose one of the two options: 'Survey Templates' or 'Quote Forms'.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): This is a text field for any additional description about your survey submission.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): This field is likely for tracking which tool is being used to complete the survey.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
*   If a field is marked as optional, you are not required to fill it, but it's still helpful for us to know this information.
*   Please enter accurate and complete information to ensure that your form submission is processed correctly.
