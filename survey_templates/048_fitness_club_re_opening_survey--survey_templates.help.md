# Fitness Club Re Opening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from fitness clubs about their re-opening plans, including the services offered, membership types, and operational details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select all services you will be offering at your fitness club on the first page.
2. Choose the type of membership you will be offering on the second page.
3. Enter the date your fitness club has been closed on the third page.
4. Enter the re-opening date of your fitness club on the fourth page.
5. Select the method you will use to communicate the re-opening with your customers on the fifth page.
6. Choose whether or not you will have a grand opening event on the sixth page.
7. Enter the method you will use to handle membership payments on the seventh page.
8. Enter the membership fee on the eighth page.
9. Decide on whether or not you will have a free trial period on the ninth page.
10. Choose your approach to handling customer feedback on the tenth page.
11. Confirm if you have a fitness director or manager on the eleventh page.
12. Decide if you have a waiting list for membership on the twelfth page.
13. Choose the duration of the waiting list on the thirteenth page.
14. Enter the duration of the waiting list on the fourteenth page.
15. Choose whether or not you will have staff on site on the fifteenth page.
16. Enter the number of people working at the club on weekdays on the sixteenth page.
17. Enter the number of people working at the club on weekends on the seventeenth page.
18. Enter the time staff work on weekdays on the eighteenth page.
19. Enter the time staff work on weekends on the nineteenth page.
20. Confirm if your club is open on weekdays on the twentieth page.
21. Confirm if your club is open on weekends on the twenty-first page.
22. Decide if your club is open on holidays on the twenty-second page.
23. Decide if your club is open during special events on the twenty-third page.
24. Enter any other information about your club on the twenty-fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What services do you want to offer** (page 1, select_multiple): Choose all services you will be offering at your fitness club, such as Gym, Group Fitness Classes, Personal Training, Nutrition Counseling, and Spas.
* **What type of membership do you offer** (page 2, select_one): Select the type of membership you will be offering, such as Monthly, Annually, Quarterly, or One-Time Pass.
* **How long has your fitness club been closed** (page 3, date): Enter the date your fitness club has been closed.
* **What is the re-opening date for your fitness club** (page 4, date): Enter the date your fitness club will re-open.
* **How will you communicate the opening with your customers** (page 5, select_one): Select the method you will use to communicate the re-opening with your customers, such as Social media, News letter, Phone call, Email, Other, or None.
* **Will you have a grand opening event** (page 6, select_one): Choose whether or not you will have a grand opening event.
* **How will you handle membership payments** (page 7, text): Enter the method you will use to handle membership payments.
* **What is the membership fee** (page 8, number): Enter the membership fee.
* **Is there a free trial period available** (page 9, select_one): Decide if you will have a free trial period available.
* **How will you handle customer feedback** (page 10, select_one): Choose your approach to handling customer feedback.
* **Is there a fitness director or manager** (page 11, select_one): Confirm if you have a fitness director or manager.
* **Is there a waiting list for membership** (page 12, select_one): Decide if you have a waiting list for membership.
* **Is there a waiting list duration** (page 13, select_one): Choose the duration of the waiting list.
* **Waiting list duration** (page 14, number): Enter the duration of the waiting list.
* **Will you have any staff on site** (page 15, select_one): Choose whether or not you will have staff on site.
* **How many people work at the club on weekdays** (page 16, text): Enter the number of people working at the club on weekdays.
* **How many people work at the club on weekends** (page 17, text): Enter the number of people working at the club on weekends.
* **What time do they work on weekdays** (page 18, time): Enter the time staff work on weekdays.
* **What time do they work on weekends** (page 19, time): Enter the time staff work on weekends.
* **Is your club open on weekdays** (page 20, select_one): Confirm if your club is open on weekdays.
* **Is your club open on weekends** (page 21, select_one): Confirm if your club is open on weekends.
* **Is your club open on holidays** (page 22, select_one): Decide if your club is open on holidays.
* **Is your club open during special events** (page 23, select_one): Decide if your club is open during special events.
* **Any other information** (page 24, text): Enter any other information about your club.
