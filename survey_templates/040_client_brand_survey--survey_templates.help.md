# Client Brand Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client feedback on the company's brand perception, personality, and services. Your input will help us understand how we're doing and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your relationship with us from the options provided in the "How would you describe your relationship with us?" field.
2. Choose where you first learned about our brand from the options in the "How did you first learn about our brand?" field.
3. For the "Brand Personality Rating" field, select up to three adjectives that best describe our brand personality.
4. Provide a numerical rating for the perceived quality and value of our services in the "Perceived Quality" and "Value for Money" fields.
5. Evaluate our visual identity by selecting how appealing you find it in the "Visual Identity Rating" field.
6. Compare our brand to competitors in the "Compared to Competitors" field.
7. Share your emotional connection to our brand by selecting the corresponding option in the "Emotional Connection" field.
8. Describe the single most important factor that influenced your choice of our brand in the "Primary Reason for Choosing" field.
9. Rate how well we deliver on our brand promises in the "Brand Promise Delivered" field.
10. If you'd like, provide suggestions on areas we could improve in the "Improvement Areas" field.
11. Tell us how likely you are to recommend our brand to a colleague in the "Recommend NPS" field.
12. If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
13. Finally, please enter the date of this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you describe your relationship with us?** (`client_type`, required): This field helps us understand your level of engagement with our brand.
* **How did you first learn about our brand?** (`brand_awareness_source`, required): This field provides insight into where our brand's message resonates.
* **Brand Personality Rating** (`brand_personality_rating`, required): Select up to three adjectives that best describe our brand personality.
* **Brand Attributes** (`perception_header`, optional): You can add any comments or notes about our brand attributes.
* **Perceived Quality** (`quality_perception`, required): Rate the quality of our services from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Superior).
* **Value for Money** (`value_perception`, required): Rate the value of our services from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Superior).
* **Visual Identity Rating** (`visual_identity_rating`, required): Evaluate how appealing our visual identity is.
* **Compared to Competitors** (`competition_comparison`, required): Compare our brand with others in the market.
* **Emotional Connection** (`emotional_connection`, required): Describe the strength of your emotional bond with our brand.
* **Primary Reason for Choosing** (`primary_reason_for_choosing`, required): Explain the single most important factor that influenced your choice of our brand.
* **Brand Promise Delivered** (`brand_promise_delivered`, required): Rate how well we keep our promises.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, optional): Suggest areas where we could improve our brand perception.
* **Recommend NPS** (`recommend_nps`, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend our brand to a colleague.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Date** (`survey_date`, optional): The date you completed this survey.
* **Thank you for your valuable insight** (`survey_footer`, optional): A note to express appreciation for your input.
