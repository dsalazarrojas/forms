# Conveyancing Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property transaction for conveyancing purposes. It is used by solicitors and other professionals to ensure a smooth and legally sound process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name.
2. Enter your Primary Email Address.
3. Provide your Contact Phone Number.
4. Complete the Property Details section.
5. Specify the Property Type.
6. Choose the correct Tenure.
7. If applicable, provide the Years Remaining on Lease.
8. Complete the Transaction Details section.
9. Specify the Transaction Type.
10. If applicable, provide the Purchase Price, Target Completion Date, and other relevant details.
11. Complete the Mortgage Information section.
12. If applicable, provide the Mortgage Lender Name, Mortgage Amount, and other relevant details.
13. Complete the Legal Details section.
14. If applicable, provide the Other Solicitor Name and other relevant details.
15. Complete the Planning and Building section.
16. If applicable, provide the Alterations Details, Planning Permission, and other relevant details.
17. Complete the Boundaries and Disputes section.
18. Specify if there are any Boundary Disputes, Neighbor Disputes, and Easements or Rights of Way.
19. Complete the Utilities and Services section.
20. If applicable, specify the Water Supply, Drainage, and Heating System.
21. Complete the Occupiers section.
22. If applicable, specify who currently occupies the property and if anyone else will be living at the property.
23. Complete the Additional Information section.
24. If applicable, provide any Special Conditions and Additional Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of property (e.g., Detached House, Semi-Detached House, etc.).
* **Tenure** (`tenure`, select_one, required): Choose the type of tenure (e.g., Freehold, Leasehold, etc.).
* **Years Remaining on Lease** (`lease_remaining`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years remaining on the lease.
* **Transaction Type** (`transaction_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of transaction (e.g., Purchase, Sale, etc.).
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the agreed purchase price.
* **Target Completion Date** (`completion_date_target`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the target completion date.
* **Is this a chain-free transaction** (`chain_free`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if this is a chain-free transaction.
* **Will you need a mortgage** (`mortgage_required`, select_one, required): Choose yes or no if you will need a mortgage.
* **Mortgage Lender Name** (`mortgage_lender`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the mortgage lender.
* **Mortgage Amount** (`mortgage_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the amount borrowed.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the deposit amount.
* **Have you received a mortgage offer** (`mortgage_offer_received`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if you have received a mortgage offer.
* **Other Solicitor Name** (`other_solicitor_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the other solicitor.
* **Is there an existing mortgage on the property** (`existing_mortgage`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if there is an existing mortgage on the property.
* **Alterations Made** (`alterations_made`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if any alterations have been made to the property.
* **Alterations Details** (`alterations_details`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any alterations made to the property.
* **Planning Permission Obtained** (`planning_permission_obtained`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if planning permission was obtained.
* **Building Regulations Approval** (`building_regulations_approval`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if building regulations approval was obtained.
* **Boundary Disputes** (`boundary_disputes`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if there are any boundary disputes.
* **Neighbor Disputes** (`neighbor_disputes`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if there are any neighbor disputes.
* **Easements or Rights of Way** (`easements_rights_of_way`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if you are aware of any easements or rights of way.
* **Water Supply** (`water_supply`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of water supply (e.g., Mains Water, Private Supply, etc.).
* **Drainage** (`drainage`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of drainage (e.g., Mains Drainage, Septic Tank, etc.).
* **Heating System** (`heating_system`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of heating system (e.g., Gas Central Heating, Oil Central Heating, etc.).
* **Who currently occupies the property** (`current_occupiers`, select_one, optional): Choose who currently occupies the property.
* **Anyone else living at the property** (`anyone_else_living`, select_one, optional): Choose yes or no if anyone else will be living at the property.
* **Names of Other Occupiers** (`occupier_names`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the names of other occupiers.
* **Any Special Conditions** (`special_conditions`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any special conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any additional comments.
