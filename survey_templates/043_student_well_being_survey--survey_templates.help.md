# Student Well Being Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is intended to help students complete the Student Well Being Survey form. This form is designed to gather information about your well being, relationships, goals, and support systems. Your responses will help create a comprehensive picture of your current state and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading the introduction page, which provides context and background information about the survey.
2. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more of the options that apply to you.
4. For text fields, provide a brief explanation of your thoughts, feelings, or experiences.
5. Complete each page of the form, and once finished, submit the form.
