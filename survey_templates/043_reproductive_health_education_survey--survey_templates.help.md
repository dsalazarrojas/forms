# reproductive_health_education_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reproductive health education survey is designed to collect participant information for education purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field. You can fill out the form in sections, one page at a time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, text, optional): This is a free text field where you can enter any information you'd like. You can write a short message or a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant, as they would like to be referred to.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the participant's age. This will help us understand their demographic information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth. This will help us understand their age range.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select the highest level of education you have completed. Choose from the options: High School, Bachelor's Degree, or Master's Degree.
* **Reproductive Health Concerns** (`reproductive_health_concerns`, text, optional): If you have any concerns about your reproductive health, enter them here. This is an optional field, but it will help us provide more relevant education and support.

## Tips
Please fill out each field carefully, as the more information you provide, the more we can help you with your reproductive health education. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to ask!
