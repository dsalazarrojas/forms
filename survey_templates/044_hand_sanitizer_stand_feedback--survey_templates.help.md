# Hand Sanitizer Stand Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with hand sanitizer stands. It will help us understand how often and where users use hand sanitizer stands, and what they think about their overall satisfaction with them. The goal is to improve our hand sanitizer stands' performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your workplace and location where you last used the hand sanitizer stand.
2. Tell us about how often you use hand sanitizer stands.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the hand sanitizer stands, using a numerical value from 1 to 5.
4. Share any ideas or suggestions you have for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workplace** (`workplace`, text, optional): Please tell us about the name of your workplace, school, or other location where you last used the hand sanitizer stand. This will help us understand the context of your use.
* **location** (`location`, text, optional): Describe the location within your workplace where you last used the hand sanitizer stand, such as a specific office, classroom, or area. This will help us identify specific use cases.
* **frequency_of_use** (`frequency_of_use`, text, optional): How often do you use hand sanitizer stands? You can answer this with a general description, such as "daily", "several times a week", "rarely", etc.
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with hand sanitizer stands, using a numerical value from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **improvement_suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions on how we can improve hand sanitizer stands, please share them with us.
