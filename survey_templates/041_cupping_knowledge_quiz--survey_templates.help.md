# Cupping Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cupping Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and experience with cupping, a traditional form of Chinese medicine. Please answer the questions accurately and concisely to help us better understand your understanding of cupping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, providing the most accurate information about your cupping experience and practice habits.
2. Review each field's instructions and options carefully before selecting an answer.
3. If you're unsure about a question, refer to the field's explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cupping knowledge quiz** (Page 1, Introduction): This field is a brief introduction to the form.
* **Cupping technique** (Page 2, Type: number): Please select the number that best represents your level of experience with cupping technique. Note: This field is not required.
* **Contrainications** (Page 3, Type: select_multiple): Select any contraindications that apply to you, such as hypersensitivity to certain medications or pregnancy. 
* **Benefits** (Page 3, Type: select_one): Select the benefit(s) you've experienced with cupping, such as relaxation, pain relief, or improved sleep quality.
* **Risks** (Page 4, Type: text): Describe any risks or potential side effects you've experienced with cupping.
* **Contrainications risks** (Page 4, Type: number): Please enter the number that best represents the level of contraindications risks you've experienced. 
* **Other contraindications risks** (Page 4, Type: text): If you've experienced contraindications risks not listed, describe them in this field.
* **Medical condition** (Page 5, Type: select_multiple): Select any medical conditions that apply to you, such as diabetes or arthritis.
* **Other medical condition** (Page 5, Type: text): If you have another medical condition not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Cupping certification** (Page 6, Type: select_one): Select your level of cupping certification, if any.
* **Other cupping certification** (Page 6, Type: text): If you have another certification not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Cupping course** (Page 6, Type: select_multiple): Select any cupping courses you've completed.
* **Other cupping course** (Page 6, Type: text): If you've completed another course not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Years of practice** (Page 7, Type: number): Please enter the number that best represents your years of practice with cupping.
* **How often do you practice cupping** (Page 7, Type: select_multiple): Select how often you practice cupping, such as daily, weekly, or rarely.
* **Cupping frequency** (Page 7, Type: select_multiple): Select the frequency of your cupping practice, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Other cupping frequency** (Page 7, Type: text): If you practice cupping at another frequency not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Cupping duration** (Page 8, Type: select_multiple): Select the duration of your cupping practice, such as 5-15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, or 30 minutes or more.
* **Other cupping duration** (Page 8, Type: text): If you practice cupping at another duration not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Cupping area** (Page 8, Type: select_multiple): Select the areas you've applied cupping to, such as back, legs, or other (please specify).
* **Other cupping area** (Page 8, Type: text): If you've applied cupping to another area not listed, describe it in this field.
* **How long do you practice cupping** (Page 9, Type: select_multiple): Select the length of time you've practiced cupping, such as less than a year, 1-2 years, or more than 2 years.
* **Medical condition** (Page 9, Type: text): If you have any medical condition that may affect your practice, describe it in this field.
* **Medical condition other** (Page 9, Type: text): If you have another medical condition not listed, describe it in this field.
