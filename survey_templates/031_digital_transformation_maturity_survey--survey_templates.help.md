# Digital Transformation Maturity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Transformation Maturity Survey is a tool to help organizations assess their current state of digital transformation, including their capabilities, processes, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your organization's current state and goals.
2. For select_one, select_multiple, and number fields, choose the most relevant option that best describes your organization.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or example.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed.
