# Quiz On Exclusion And Discrimination - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect responses about exclusion and discrimination experiences. It consists of 12 questions, including a note field for conclusion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide honest and accurate responses to each question.
3. If a question does not apply to you, feel free to skip it or mark it as "Not Applicable" (if available).
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, **required: false**): This is a note field where you can read the introduction and understand the context of the form.
* **Have you ever experienced or witnessed exclusion** (`question1`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is asking about your personal experiences or observations of exclusion. Be honest about your encounters.
* **Have you ever experienced or witnessed discrimination** (`question2`, `text`, **required: false`): Similar to the previous question, this one is about your personal experiences or observations of discrimination.
* **Have you ever felt excluded** (`question3`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is about your feelings of being excluded. Reflect on whether you've ever felt left out or ostracized.
* **Have you ever witnessed discrimination** (`question4`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is asking about your observations of others being discriminated against.
* **Have you ever experienced being discriminated against** (`question5`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is about your personal experiences of being discriminated against.
* **Have you ever witnessed someone being discriminated against** (`question6`, `text`, **required: false`): Similar to the previous question, this one is about observing others being discriminated against.
* **Have you ever witnessed exclusion** (`question7`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is about observing exclusion happening to others.
* **Have you ever experienced exclusion** (`question8`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is about your personal experiences of being excluded.
* **Have you ever experienced discrimination** (`question9`, `text`, **required: false`): This question is about your experiences of being discriminated against.
* **Have you ever witnessed discrimination** (`question10`, `text`, **required: false`): This is another question about observing discrimination happening to others.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `note`, **required: false`): This is a note field for you to add any final thoughts or comments about your experiences.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your best recollection.
* If you have additional comments or thoughts after completing the form, feel free to add them in the conclusion field.
* Remember to review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
