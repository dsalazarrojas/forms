# High School Student Climate Change Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Climate Change Awareness Survey aims to gather information from high school students on their awareness and perceptions of climate change. This survey is designed to understand how students think about climate change, what they know about it, and how they believe their schools can address it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly, and make sure to answer all questions that apply to you. You can choose one or more options for multiple-choice questions. If you are unsure about a question, please select "Not applicable" or "Not likely at all" as a response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): This is a brief description of the survey and its purpose.
* **Do you believe climate change is happening?** (Select One: Very concerned, Somewhat concerned, Not concerned at all): This question asks about your level of concern regarding climate change. Please select one of the provided options that best describe your feelings.
* **What are some of the main reasons why climate change is happening?** (Select Multiple: Carbon emissions, Deforestation, Burning fossil fuels): This question asks about your understanding of the causes of climate change. Choose one or more of the provided options that you believe are contributing factors.
* **On a scale of 1-10 how concerned are you about climate change?** (Number): This question asks about your level of concern, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **What do you think is the most important action we should take to address climate change?** (Select One: Reduce carbon emissions, Invest in renewable energy, Protect and restore natural habitats): This question asks about your opinion on the most effective action to address climate change. Choose one of the provided options.
* **What do you think is the biggest obstacle to addressing climate change?** (Select Multiple: Lack of awareness, Economic costs, Government policies): This question asks about the challenges you think hinder climate change efforts. Choose one or more of the provided options.
* **How do you think climate change will affect your community in the next 10 years?** (Select One: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely at all): This question asks about your perception of the impact of climate change on your community. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Do you think climate change will affect your daily life in the next 10 years?** (Select One: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely at all): This question asks about your personal experience and expectations of climate change's impact on your daily life. Select one of the provided options.
* **What do you think is the biggest opportunity for schools to address climate change?** (Select Multiple: Educate about climate change, Implement sustainable practices, Support climate change research and development): This question asks about the ways schools can contribute to climate change efforts. Choose one or more of the provided options.
* **How can schools best educate students about climate change?** (Select Multiple: Classroom instruction, Field trips, Online resources): This question asks about the best practices for teaching climate change. Choose one or more of the provided options.
