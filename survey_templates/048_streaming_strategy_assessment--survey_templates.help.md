# Streaming Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you assess your streaming habits and identify areas for improvement. It's designed to help streamers evaluate their performance and habits, and potentially help streaming platforms or organizations optimize their streaming experience for their users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your creator information, including your name and other relevant details.
2. Answer the questions about your streaming habits, such as how often you stream and what time of day you typically stream.
3. Choose the challenges you face while streaming, such as difficulty finding an audience or equipment failure.
4. Select your content planning method, whether it's scripted, improvised, or a mix of both.
5. Indicate what helps you improve your streaming performance, like better equipment or coaching.
6. Choose the criteria you use to evaluate your performance, such as engagement metrics or viewer retention.
7. Select your streaming habits that influence your performance, such as regular streaming or consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Information** (1, **name: creator_info**, optional)
	+ This field is for providing your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a creator.
* **What are your streaming goals?** (2, **name: streaming_goals**, optional)
	+ Answer this question to describe your objectives for streaming, such as growing your audience or improving engagement.
* **Frequency of Streaming** (3, **name: frequency_of_streaming, optional)
	+ Select how often you stream:
		- **Streaming 1-2 times a week**
		- **Streaming 3-4 times a week**
		- **Streaming 5 or more times a week**
* **Time of Streaming** (4, **name: streaming_time, optional)
	+ Choose the time of day you typically stream.
* **Challenges Faced** (5, **name: challenges_faced, optional)
	+ Select all that apply:
		- **Difficulty finding an audience**
		- **Equipment failure**
		- **Lack of focus**
		- **Personal issues**
* **Content Planning** (6, **name: content_planning, optional)
	+ Choose how you plan your content:
		- **Scripted content**
		- **Improvisation**
		- **A mix of both**
* **Performance Improvement** (7, **name: performance_improvement, optional)
	+ Select all that apply:
		- **Better equipment**
		- **More practice**
		- **Coaching/feedback**
		- **Other (please specify)**
* **Evaluation Criteria** (8, **name: evaluation_criteria, optional)
	+ Select all that apply:
		- **Engagement metrics**
		- **Viewer retention**
		- **Audience feedback**
		- **Other (please specify)**
* **Streaming Habits** (9, **name: streaming_habits, optional)
	+ Select all that apply:
		- **Regular streaming**
		- **Consistency**
		- **Time of day**
		- **Other (please specify)**

## Tips
- Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about a question, feel free to choose "Other (please specify)" for more details.
- This form is meant to be a helpful tool for self-reflection and improvement, so don't worry about being perfect – just be sincere!
