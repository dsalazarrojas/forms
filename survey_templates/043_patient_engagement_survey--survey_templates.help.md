# Patient Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Engagement Survey is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience and engagement with our healthcare organization. This survey aims to understand patients' perceptions, satisfaction, and any additional comments they may have. Please note that this survey is anonymous and all responses will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each section.
2. For each question, provide your honest and thoughtful response.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by *) if applicable.
4. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Demographics** (1, *optional*):
	+ This section is intended for you to provide some basic information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, or other demographic details.
	+ Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Patient Engagement** (2, *optional*):
	+ This section is about your level of engagement with our healthcare organization, such as how well you feel you have been informed, supported, or involved in your care.
	+ Please rate your level of engagement based on your personal experience.
* **Satisfaction** (3, *optional*):
	+ This section is designed to gauge your satisfaction with our healthcare services, staff, and facilities.
	+ Please rate your satisfaction based on your personal experience.
* **Experience** (4, *optional*):
	+ This section is an open-ended question allowing you to describe any positive or negative experiences you've had with our healthcare organization.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (5, *optional*):
	+ If you have any further feedback, suggestions, or comments that didn't fit within the previous sections, please use this space to express them.
	+ This section is entirely optional, but your input is valuable to us.
* **Patient ID** (6, *optional*):
	+ This section is intended for you to provide your patient ID number, if you have one.
	+ Please enter your ID number if you know it, otherwise, you can skip this field.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can skip any section if you're not comfortable providing the information. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
