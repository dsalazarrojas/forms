# Survey Participation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects contact information and demographic data from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. You will be asked for the following information:
* First name
* Last name
* Email
* Phone number
* Date of birth
* Survey ID
* Survey name

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **participant_last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address. Make sure to use a valid email address.
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **participant_date_of_birth**: Select your date of birth.
* **participant_survey_id**: Enter your survey ID if you have one.
* **participant_survey_name**: Enter the name of the survey you are participating in.

## Tips
* Be accurate with the information provided.
* Do not submit duplicate entries.
* Ensure that all required fields have correct information. 
* If you need help, contact your survey administrator.
