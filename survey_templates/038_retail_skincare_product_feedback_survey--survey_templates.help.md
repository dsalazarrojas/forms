# Retail Skincare Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on their experience with a specific retail skincare product. This will help the company understand customer satisfaction, product performance, and sales channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Provide information about the product you purchased, including the product ID if you know it.
3. Answer the questions about your customer information and satisfaction with the product.
4. Share your thoughts on product recommendations and improvements.
5. Let us know about your purchase experience, including the sales channel you used and the purchase date.
6. If you've shared your experience on social media, please select all the platforms you've used.
7. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
8. Finally, enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you about any further updates or changes to the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Info**: Please provide a brief description of the product you purchased, including the product name and code. This will help us understand your purchase experience.
* **Customer Info**: Please share your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself as a customer. This will help us understand your perspective as a customer.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product, using the number scale provided. A higher score indicates a more positive experience.
* **Recommendations**: Share any thoughts or ideas you have for improving or adding features to the product. This is your opportunity to suggest how we can better serve you and our other customers.
* **Sales Channels**: Select all the sales channels where you've seen or interacted with our product, including Amazon, Other, and any other online platforms. This will help us understand how you discover and purchase our product.
* **Sales Channels Other**: If you selected "Other" as your sales channel, please provide a brief description of where you saw or purchased the product.
* **Purchase Value**: Please provide the amount you spent on the product, excluding any taxes or shipping costs. This will help us understand the value you place on our product.
* **Purchase Date**: Share the date you made the purchase, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Product ID**: If you know your product ID, please share it with us. This is optional but helps us track product performance.
* **Social Media**: Select all the social media platforms where you've shared your experience with our product. This will help us understand your online behavior and interactions.
* **Social Media Other**: If you've shared on a social media platform not listed, please specify it in this field.
* **Contact Us**: Enter your email address if you'd like to stay up-to-date on any changes or updates to the product.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our product. This is your chance to share your thoughts and suggestions.
