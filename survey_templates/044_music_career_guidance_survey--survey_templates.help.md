# Music Career Guidance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Career Guidance Survey is designed to gather information about your musical background and career goals to provide you with personalized guidance on your music career path. This survey will help you make informed decisions about your future in the music industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your First name, Last name, Email, and Age.
2. If you have any musical instrument of choice, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Instrument" field.
3. Choose your preferred music style(s) from the list in the "Music style" field.
4. If you have any music education, select "Yes" or "No" for each relevant option.
5. Indicate your career goal(s) by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each relevant option.
6. If you have any other career goals, type them in the "Other" field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your survey response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* Label: First name
* Type: Text input field
* Description: Enter your first name to help us better understand who you are.

### Last name
* Label: Last name
* Type: Text input field
* Description: Enter your last name to help us better understand who you are.

### Email
* Label: Email
* Type: Email input field
* Description: Enter your email address to receive guidance and updates about your music career path.

### Instrument
* Label: Instrument
* Type: Select one option
* Description: Select "Yes" if you play any instrument, and "No" if you do not.

### Music style
* Label: Music style
* Type: Select multiple options
* Description: Choose the music style that best represents your musical taste or preference. You can select multiple options if you have a diverse style.

### Music education
* Label: Music education
* Type: Select multiple options
* Description: Indicate if you have had any music education, such as classes, lessons, or diplomas. You can select multiple options if you have multiple types of education.

### Career goal
* Label: Career goal
* Type: Select one option
* Description: Indicate your primary career goal, such as becoming a performer, composer, or producer.

### Other
* Label: Other
* Type: Text input field
* Description: If you have any other career goals, type them in this field.

### Age
* Label: Age
* Type: Number input field
* Description: Enter your age to help us better understand your demographic information.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure you receive relevant guidance.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* You can select multiple music styles if you have a diverse style.
* Do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification about any field.
* You will receive guidance and updates about your music career path after completing this form.
