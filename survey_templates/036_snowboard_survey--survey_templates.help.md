# snowboard_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The snowboard_survey form is designed to gather information from users on their experience and opinions. It consists of several sections, including a welcome page, introduction, feedback, satisfaction, trends, recommendations, product development, and future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the snowboard_survey form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by filling out the welcome page.
2. Move on to the introduction page, where you can provide more details about your experience.
3. On the feedback page, share any thoughts or comments about your experience.
4. On the satisfaction page, select one of the three satisfaction options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
5. For the trends page, enter your thoughts and ideas about future developments.
6. On the recommendations page, provide suggestions for future improvements.
7. On the product development page, enter any ideas for product enhancements.
8. Finally, on the future events page, share any events you're interested in or think are worth attending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Welcome
* **Welcome** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This is the first page of the form. Please introduce yourself and provide any information relevant to the survey.

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe your experience in more detail. This is your chance to share the story of your encounter with the form.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share your thoughts or comments about your experience. This is an opportunity to provide constructive feedback for future improvements.

### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select one of the three satisfaction options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied. This will help us understand your level of satisfaction with the form.

### Trends
* **Trends** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your thoughts about future trends or developments. This will help us shape the product roadmap.

### Recommendations
* **Recommendations** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Suggest improvements or features you'd like to see in the future. Your input is valuable to us.

### Product Development
* **Product Development** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Share any ideas you have for product enhancements. We appreciate your creative input.

### Future Events
* **Future Events** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Let us know if you're interested in or suggest any future events. This helps us plan and execute future events effectively.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* The form is designed to be completed within a reasonable time frame.
* Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation.
