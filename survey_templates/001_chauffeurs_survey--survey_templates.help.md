# Chauffeurs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather honest feedback from Chauffeurs on various aspects of their role, including their experience, job satisfaction, and challenges. Your input will help us understand the profession's strengths and weaknesses, allowing us to make informed decisions to improve the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the most accurate answer to each question based on your experience. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional comments or feedback" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **How many years of experience do you have as a chauffeur?** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, true)
Select the number of years you have been working as a chauffeur from the provided options.
### 2. **Primary service type you provide** (`primary_service_type`, `select_one`, true)
Choose the type of service you primarily offer as a chauffeur from the given options.
### 3. **Your employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, true)
Select your current employment status from the options provided.
### 4. **Overall job satisfaction rating** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, true)
Rate your overall satisfaction with your job from 1 (Very Unsatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
### 5. **How would you rate your work-life balance?** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, true)
Evaluate your current work-life balance from Poor to Excellent.
### 6. **Is your compensation adequate for the work you do?** (`compensation_adequacy`, `select_one`, true)
Assess whether your compensation meets your expectations from Significantly below expectations to Exceeds expectations.
### 7. **How would you rate the condition of your work vehicle?** (`vehicle_condition`, `select_one`, true)
Evaluate the condition of your vehicle from Poor to Excellent.
### 8. **Level of schedule flexibility** (`schedule_flexibility`, `select_one`, true)
Choose the level of flexibility you have in your schedule from Very inflexible to Very flexible.
### 9. **Access to training and development opportunities** (`training_opportunities`, `select_one`, true)
Select the level of access you have to training and development opportunities from None available to Excellent.
### 10. **How supported do you feel by management?** (`management_support`, `select_one`, true)
Rate how supported you feel by your management from Very unsupported to Very supported.
### 11. **Client interactions are generally** (`client_interactions`, `select_one`, true)
Assess the nature of your interactions with clients from Very negative to Very positive.
### 12. **Do you have safety concerns in your role?** (`safety_concerns`, `select_one`, true)
Indicate the level of safety concerns you have from Major concerns to No concerns.
### 13. **Satisfaction with technology and dispatch systems** (`technology_satisfaction`, `select_one`, true)
Rate your satisfaction with the technology and dispatch systems from Very unsatisfied to Very satisfied.
### 14. **Do you see long-term career prospects in this role?** (`future_prospects`, `select_one`, true)
Select the level of long-term career prospects you see from No prospects to Excellent prospects.
### 15. **What are your biggest challenges as a chauffeur?** (`challenges`, `select_multiple`, false)
Choose all that apply from the list of challenges provided.
### 16. **What improvements would most benefit your role?** (`improvements_needed`, `text`, false)
Enter your suggestions for improvements that would benefit your role.
### 17. **Would you recommend the chauffeur profession to others?** (`recommend_industry`, `select_one`, true)
Select whether you would definitely, probably, or probably not recommend the profession to others.
### 18. **Additional comments or feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, false)
Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience as a chauffeur.
