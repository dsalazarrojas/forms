# Health and Wellbeing Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your overall well-being across various aspects of your mental, physical, and social health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question with the options provided. Select one or multiple answers depending on the question type (select_one or select_multiple).
2. Be honest when answering, as your responses will help you identify areas that need improvement.
3. Complete the form in one sitting, or save your progress and come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you practice mindfulness** (`wellbeing_quiz_questions`, select_multiple, optional): Answer how often you practice mindfulness, such as meditation, deep breathing, or other relaxation techniques.
* **How would you rate your mental health** (`mental_health`, number, optional): Rate your mental health on a scale (not specified in the form, so please answer based on your own understanding).
* **How would you rate your physical health** (`physical_health`, number, optional): Rate your physical health on a scale (not specified in the form, so please answer based on your own understanding).
* **How would you rate your relationships** (`relationships`, select_one, optional): Rate how strong your relationships are, with options being Good or Fair.
* **Sleep quality and duration** (`sleep_health`, select_one, optional): Answer how good your sleep quality and duration are, with options being Good or Poor.
* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional): Answer whether you have a good work-life balance, with options being Yes or No.
* **Nutrition and diet** (`nutrition`, select_one, optional): Answer whether you have a healthy nutrition and diet, with options being Yes or No.
* **How often are you physically active** (`physical_activity`, select_one, optional): Answer how often you engage in physical activities, such as exercise, walking, or other activities, with options being Yes or No.
* **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, select_one, optional): Answer whether you have good stress management, with options being Yes or No.
* **How much time do you spend on self-care** (`self_care`, select_one, optional): Answer how much time you spend on self-care activities, with options being Yes or No.
* **How often do you feel connected to others** (`social_connections`, select_one, optional): Answer how often you feel connected to others, with options being Yes or No.
* **How often do you take care of yourself** (`self_maintenance`, select_one, optional): Answer how often you practice self-maintenance, with options being Yes or No.

## Tips

* Be honest and take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about an answer, choose the option that best fits your situation.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your administrator or help desk.
