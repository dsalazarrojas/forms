# Electrical Services Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with electrical services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company name to represent the business that received the service.
2. Enter your name and contact information, including email address and phone number.
3. Choose the date when the electrical service was provided.
4. Indicate whether the service provided was of the type you requested.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the service overall, with options ranging from 1 to 5.
6. Select the rating for the quality of the service, with options for Good, Fair, or Poor.
7. Rate the performance of the technician, with options for Good, Fair, or Poor.
8. Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name (company_name)**: Enter the name of the company that provided you with electrical services.
* **Contact name (contact_name)**: Enter your name as the person completing this survey.
* **Contact email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address to ensure that you can be reached for follow-up.
* **Service date (service_date)**: Enter the date the electrical service was provided.
* **Type of service (service_type)**: Indicate whether the service provided was the type of service you requested.
* **Satisfaction rating (satisfaction_rating)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, with options ranging from 1 to 5.
* **Rating of service quality (rating_service_quality)**: Select the rating for the quality of the service, with options for Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Rating of technician (rating_technician)**: Rate the performance of the technician, with options for Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments (comments)**: Provide any comments or feedback you may have about the service.
* **Contact phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your phone number to ensure that you can be reached for follow-up.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the support team.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
