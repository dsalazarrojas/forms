# Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey is a customer satisfaction survey used to measure customer loyalty and satisfaction. It helps us understand your thoughts and feelings about our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your contact information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (if you'd like us to follow up with you)
	* Phone (optional, but helps us get in touch with you faster)
2. Answer the Net Promoter Score questions:
	* How likely are you to recommend our product or service? (Scale: 0-10)
	* Will you recommend our product or service? (Select one of the options)
	* Will you recommend our business? (Select one of the options)
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the open-ended questions:
	* What other comments do you have about your experience?
	* Do you have any other thoughts or suggestions for us?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name.
* **Email** (email): Your email address, so we can follow up with you (if you'd like).
* **Phone** (text): Your phone number (optional, but helps us get in touch with you faster).
* **How Likely to Recommend** (number): On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend our product or service?
* **Will You Recommend** (select_one): Will you recommend our product or service? (Select one of the options: Very unlikely, Not very likely, Somewhat likely, Neither likely nor unlikely, Somewhat likely, Very likely, Definitely likely)
* **Will You Recommend Our Business** (select_one): Will you recommend our business? (Select one of the options: Very unlikely, Not very likely, Somewhat likely, Neither likely nor unlikely, Somewhat likely, Very likely, Definitely likely)
* **Additional Feedback/Comments** (text): Any other comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us.
* **Open-Ended Question 1** (text): What other comments do you have about your experience?
* **Open-Ended Question 2** (text): Do you have any other thoughts or suggestions for us?
