# Long Service Award Recipient Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from employees who have received a long service award, to help us better understand their experiences and feelings towards the award process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with options, select all that apply.
3. For questions with text fields, provide a brief description or comment.
4. Make sure to submit the form after completing all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long have you been with the company?** (`1`, `number`, required): This question asks for the number of years you have been working for the company.
* **What award you received?** (`2`, `text`, required): Please type in the name or description of the award you received.
* **How satisfied are you with your award?** (`3`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the award process, on a scale of 1-5.
* **What was the most memorable part of your award?** (`4`, `text`, required): Describe the most significant or memorable part of your award experience.
* **How did you celebrate your award?** (`5`, `text`, optional): Share how you celebrated or commemorated your award.
* **Has your award influenced you to stay with the company?** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the award influenced your decision to continue working with the company.
* **Would you like to be recognized again in the future?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply for which type of recognition you would like to receive in the future.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future long service awards?** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide any ideas or suggestions for improving the award process.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future long service award winners?** (`9`, `text`, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions for future award winners.
* **Are you happy to be part of this company?** (`10`, `text`, optional): Share your feelings about being part of the company.
* **Do you feel valued and appreciated?** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you feel valued and appreciated by the company.
* **What would you like to see in the future?** (`12`, `text`, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions for improving the company or award process.
* **Are you happy with the way your award was communicated?** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you were happy with the way your award was communicated.
* **How long would you like to work at this company?** (`14`, `number`, required): Provide the number of years you would like to continue working for the company.
* **What would make you feel more valued and appreciated?** (`15`, `text`, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions on how to improve how you feel valued and appreciated.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`16`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your award or company experience.
* **Is there anything else you would like to add?** (`17`, `text`, optional): Share any further thoughts or suggestions.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the award process?** (`18`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the award process, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Would you like to be recognized for any other milestone(s) in your time at the company?** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for which type of recognition you would like to receive in the future.
* **Would you like to be recognized for any other milestone(s) in the future?** (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for which type of recognition you would like to receive in the future.
* **Do you have any other suggestions or comments about the award process?** (`21`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving the award process.
* **Have you been recognized as a long service award winner previously?** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you have been recognized as a long service award winner before.
* **Would you like to see a video or photo with your award?** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you would like to have a video or photo with your award.
* **Have you taken advantage of any other employee benefits?** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply for which employee benefits you have used.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`25`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your award or company experience.
