# manifestation_integration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the current state of a specific manifestation's integration with a practitioner. It helps the team to assess the progress, identify challenges, and provide necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions below, and make sure to submit your responses before closing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Integration Goals (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of your current integration goals.
* **Current Manifestation Progress (Page 1)**: Describe the progress made so far in integrating the manifestation with the practitioner.
* **Support Needs (Page 1)**: Select all the support needs that apply to your current integration. You may choose 'Yes' for each support need that you require.
* **Challenges (Page 2)**: Describe any challenges you are currently facing with the integration.
* **Integration Progress (Page 2)**: Describe the progress made so far in integrating the manifestation with the practitioner.
* **Integration Practitioner Relationship (Page 2)**: Select the nature of your relationship with the integration practitioner. You may choose 'Yes' if you have a direct relationship or 'No' if you do not.
* **Comments (Page 2)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the integration.
* **Is Survey Urgent (Page 3)**: Select 'Yes' if the integration has urgent needs that require immediate attention.
* **Integration Survey Comments (Page 3)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the integration.
* **Support Needs Urgent (Page 3)**: Select all the support needs that are urgent for the integration. You may choose 'Yes' for each support need that you require.
* **Previous Integration Experiences (Page 4)**: Describe any previous experiences you have had with integrating similar manifestations with practitioners.
* **Previous Manifestation Experiences (Page 4)**: Describe any previous experiences you have had with manifestations similar to this one.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
- Be specific about the challenges you are facing and the support needs you require.
- Provide as much detail as possible in the comments and additional feedback fields.
