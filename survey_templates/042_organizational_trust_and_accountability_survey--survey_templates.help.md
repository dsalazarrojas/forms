# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions carefully, as your input is crucial for understanding the organizational climate.
2. Be as honest as possible with your answers.
3. Do not skip any questions, as they are all important.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trust**:
	+ Label: Trust
	+ Type: Select One
	+ This question asks if you have trust in your organization and leadership. Please select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No".
* **trust_1, trust_2, trust_3, trust_4**:
	+ Label: trust_1, trust_2, trust_3, trust_4
	+ Type: Number
	+ These questions are about your perception of trust in different areas within the organization. Please enter a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means "completely disagree" and 10 means "completely agree".
* **accountability_1, accountability_2, accountability_3, accountability_4**:
	+ Label: accountability_1, accountability_2, accountability_3, accountability_4
	+ Type: Number
	+ These questions are about your perception of accountability within the organization. Please enter a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means "completely disagree" and 10 means "completely agree".
* **transparency_1, transparency_2, transparency_3, transparency_4**:
	+ Label: transparency_1, transparency_2, transparency_3, transparency_4
	+ Type: Number
	+ These questions are about your perception of transparency within the organization. Please enter a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means "completely disagree" and 10 means "completely agree".
