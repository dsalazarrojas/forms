# Group Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and scores on a group's performance. It will guide the user through a series of questions and fields designed to elicit their honest evaluation of their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your name and title if required.
- Read the introduction, which is intended to provide background and context for the form.
- Answer each question/field according to your genuine experience and understanding of the group's performance.
- If required, select your submission option from the "submit" field (Manager's Review or Submit).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, `required: false`): This section is a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Group Name** (`group_name`, `text`, `required: false`): This field is used to input the name of the group being evaluated.
* **Feedback Score 1** (`feedback_score_1`, `number`, `required: false`): Please rate your level of satisfaction with the group's performance from 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Feedback Score 2** (`feedback_score_2`, `number`, `required: false`): Similar to Feedback Score 1, please rate the group's performance from 1-5, considering any specific areas of improvement.
* **Feedback Score 3** (`feedback_score_3`, `number`, `required: false`): Rate the group's performance again, this time for its overall effectiveness.
* **Feedback Score 4** (`feedback_score_4`, `number`, `required: false`): Another opportunity to evaluate the group's performance.
* **Feedback Score 5** (`feedback_score_5`, `number`, `required: false`): Final feedback on the group's performance.
* **Group Feedback** (`group_feedback`, `text`, `required: false`): Provide any comments or feedback you may have for the group.
* **Manager Feedback** (`manager_feedback`, `text`, `required: false`): Leave any comments or feedback for the manager of the group.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select "Manager's Review" to send the completed form for evaluation or "Submit" to finalize your response and save your feedback.
