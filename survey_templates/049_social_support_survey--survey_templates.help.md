# Social Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Support Survey is a form designed to collect information about an individual's social interactions and support systems. The survey is intended to gather data for research or analysis purposes, such as understanding social support networks and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and select the response that best describes your experience or feelings.
2. Fill out each question completely and accurately.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main
**Main** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your social interactions.

### Interaction with Family
**Interaction with Family** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options that best describes your interactions with your family:
* Strongly agree
* Somewhat agree
* Neutral
* Somewhat disagree
* Strongly disagree

### Interaction with Friends
**Interaction with Friends** (`3`, `text`, optional): Provide your thoughts or feelings about your interactions with friends.

### Interaction with Neighbours
**Interaction with Neighbours** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply of the following options that best describe your interactions with your neighbours:
* Strongly agree
* Somewhat agree
* Neutral
* Somewhat disagree
* Strongly disagree

### Interaction with Colleagues
**Interaction with Colleagues** (`5`, `text`, optional): Write your thoughts or feelings about your interactions with colleagues.

### Interaction with Children
**Interaction with Children** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options that best describes your interactions with your children:
* Strongly agree
* Somewhat agree
* Neutral
* Somewhat disagree
* Strongly disagree

### Interaction with Partners
**Interaction with Partners** (`7`, `text`, optional): Provide your thoughts or feelings about your interactions with your partners.

### Interaction with Peers
**Interaction with Peers** (`8`, `text`, optional): Write your thoughts or feelings about your interactions with your peers.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, try to answer based on your best recollection.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for assistance.
