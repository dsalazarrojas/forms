# Social Media Post Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about social media posts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Email address.
2. Provide your Feedback on the social media post.
3. Select a Rating for the post (1-5).
4. Choose the Social Media Platform where you interacted with the post (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
5. Optionally, add a Date and Time if relevant to your feedback.
6. If needed, provide additional Notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field to collect your name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: A field to collect your email address for contact purposes.
* **Feedback**: A text field to provide your thoughts and opinions about the social media post.
* **Rating**: A dropdown menu to select a rating for the post (1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest).
* **Social Media Platform**: A menu to select the platform where you interacted with the post (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
* **Date**: A field to optionally add a date related to your feedback.
* **Time**: A field to optionally add a time related to your feedback.
* **Note**: A text area to provide additional notes about your experience, if needed.
