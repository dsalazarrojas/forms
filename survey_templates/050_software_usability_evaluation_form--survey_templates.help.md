# Software Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for gathering feedback on the usability of software tools and platforms. It helps users provide detailed information about their experiences with the software, including how they use it, what they like and dislike, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a note page, where you can provide any additional context or information about your experience with the software.
2. Fill in your **user info**: Enter your name, email, or other identifying information so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up questions.
3. Answer **yes or no** questions about whether you **used software used to perform the task** and what specific areas of the software you **used to perform the task**.
4. Answer **yes or no** questions about **how long you used the software** and how frequently you use it.
5. Select all the **areas where you experienced issues**.
6. Provide any **additional comments** about your experience with the software.
7. Rate the **usability of the software** from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent and 1 is poor.
8. Provide a brief **rating feedback** about your experience with the software.
9. Enter any **contact information** so we can follow up with you about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Software Used**: This field asks if you used the software to perform a specific task. Select "Yes" if you did, and "No" if you didn't.
* **Used What**: This field asks what specific areas of the software you used. Select all that apply.
* **Used For**: This field asks if you used the software for a specific purpose. Select "Yes" if you did, and "No" if you didn't.
* **How Long**: This field asks how long you used the software. You can enter a numeric value (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Frequency**: This field asks how frequently you use the software. Select "Yes" if you do, and "No" if you don't.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the software.
* **Rating**: This field asks you to rate the usability of the software. Choose a score from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent and 1 is poor.
* **Overall Rating**: This field asks for an overall rating of the software. Choose a score from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent and 1 is poor.
* **Rating Feedback**: This field asks for a brief feedback about your rating. Be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for any contact information, such as email or phone number, so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Suggest Improvement**: This field is for any specific suggestions or ideas you have for improving the software.

Note: Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers, and feel free to add any additional information or comments that you think will be helpful in understanding your experience with the software.
