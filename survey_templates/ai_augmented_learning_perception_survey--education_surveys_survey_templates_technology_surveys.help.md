# AI Augmented Learning Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather your opinions and experiences regarding the use of AI in educational settings. Your responses will help us better understand your perceptions of AI's effectiveness, benefits, and challenges in enhancing learning outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Please select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
3. For text fields, provide detailed and specific responses.
4. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name
**Full Name** (`text`, required): Enter your first and last name.

### 2. Email Address
**Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address. We'll never share your email with others.

### 3. Institution Role
**What is your role in education?** (`select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your role:
	* Student
	* Teacher
	* Administrator
	* Parent
	* Other

### 4. Age Group
**Age Group** (`select_one`, required): Select your age group:
	* Under 18
	* 18-24
	* 25-34
	* 35-44
	* 45-54
	* 55+

### 5. Date of Survey Completion
**Date of survey completion** (`date`, required): Enter the date you're completing this survey.

### 6. Experience with AI in Learning
**How much experience do you have with AI in educational settings?** (`select_one`, required): Select your level of experience:
	* No experience
	* Limited experience
	* Moderate experience
	* Extensive experience
	* Expert level

### 7. AI Tools Used
**Which AI learning tools have you used?** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable AI tools:
	* ChatGPT
	* Khan Academy AI
	* Duolingo
	* Grammarly
	* Wolfram Alpha
	* Other

### 8. Hours Per Week with AI-Assisted Learning
**Approximately how many hours per week do you engage with AI-assisted learning?** (`integer`, optional): Enter the approximate number of hours you spend with AI-assisted learning per week.

### 9. Effectiveness of AI in Learning
**How effective do you believe AI is in enhancing learning outcomes?** (`integer`, required): Rate AI's effectiveness on a scale of 1-10.

### 10. Satisfaction with AI in Learning
**How satisfied are you with your experience using AI in learning?** (`select_one`, required): Select your level of satisfaction:
	* Very satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very dissatisfied

### 11. Concerns about AI in Learning
**What concerns do you have about AI in learning?** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable concerns:
	* Academic integrity
	* Over-dependence on technology
	* Privacy issues
	* Reduced human interaction
	* Bias in AI systems
	* Lack of regulation
	* None

### 12. Benefits of AI-Assisted Learning
**What benefits do you see in AI-assisted learning?** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable benefits:
	* Personalized learning
	* Immediate feedback
	* Accessibility improvements
	* Efficiency gains
	* Enhanced engagement
	* Better assessment tools
	* Other

### 13. Future Usage Likelihood
**How likely are you to continue using AI tools in your learning journey?** (`integer`, required): Rate your likelihood on a scale of 1-10.

### 14. Recommend AI Learning Tools to Others
**Would you recommend AI learning tools to others?** (`select_one`, required): Select your willingness to recommend:
	* Definitely yes
	* Probably yes
	* Neutral
	* Probably no
	* Definitely no

### 15. Aspects of AI Experience
**Which aspects apply to your experience with AI in learning?** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable aspects:
	* Easy to use
	* Time-saving
	* Improved understanding
	* Confusing at times
	* Lacks human touch
	* Well-designed interface

### 16. Overall Rating of AI's Impact
**Overall rating of AI's impact on your learning (1-10)** (`integer`, required): Rate AI's impact on a scale of 1-10.

### 17. Additional Feedback
**What would you improve or change about current AI learning tools?** (`text`, optional): Provide specific feedback.

### 18. Additional Comments
**Additional comments or suggestions** (`text`, optional): Share any other thoughts about AI in education.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your insights and experiences with us!
