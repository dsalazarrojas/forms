# Agent Information Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about agency agents, including their name, joining date, official email, phone number, language proficiency, primary area of expertise, relevant certifications, current work schedule, previous industry experience, software tools mastered, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read through the form carefully** before starting to complete it.
2. **Fill out all required fields**, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. **Use the hints provided** to help guide you through the form and ensure you provide accurate information.
4. **Save your work** as you go along, in case you need to stop and come back to the form later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Joining Date (id: 3)**: Enter the date you joined the agency or started working with us.
* **Official Email (id: 4)**: Enter your official email address that is associated with your agency profile.
* **Primary Phone Number (id: 5)**: Enter your primary phone number that can be reached at for official purposes.
* **Language Proficiency (id: 7)**: Select all languages you are proficient in conducting business in.
* **Primary Area of Expertise (id: 8)**: Select your primary area of expertise, such as Residential Sales or Commercial Leasing.
* **Relevant Certifications (id: 9)**: Enter any relevant certifications or qualifications you hold, such as CFA, CPA, or REALTOR.
* **Current Work Schedule (id: 10)**: Select your current work schedule, such as Full-time, Part-time, or Contract/Freelance.
* **Previous Industry Experience (id: 11)**: Summarize your previous industry experience and how it relates to your current role.
* **Software Tools Mastered (id: 12)**: Select all software tools you are proficient in, such as Salesforce CRM or HubSpot.
* **Emergency Contact Information (id: 13)**: Enter the name, relation, and phone number of an emergency contact person.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* Please double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes to your form, please save your work and contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
