# April Fools Day Office Prank Ideas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect prank ideas suitable for an office environment for April Fools Day, along with their characteristics such as type, effort required, and estimated cost. By filling out this form, you'll help us create a comprehensive list of prank ideas for office use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details on the "About You" page.
2. In the "Prank Ideas" page, type a funny and safe prank idea that you enjoy or have seen before.
3. In the "Share Your Favorite Prank Idea" section, describe a funny and safe prank idea that you enjoy or have seen before.
4. Choose the type of prank (e.g., Technology Based, Office Supplies, etc.) that the prank idea you shared fits into.
5. Indicate the effort required to set up and execute the prank.
6. Estimate the cost of the prank.
7. Decide who would enjoy this prank in your office.
8. Describe any safety considerations.
9. Rate how funny this prank idea is.
