# School Enrollment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Enrollment Survey is designed to collect information about students and their families to help educational institutions tailor their services and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure all required fields are filled out first.
2. Complete all the fields in the form, using the options provided for select_one, select_multiple, and select_one options.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Info** (`family_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your family.
* **Student Background** (`student_background`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your student.
* **School Info** (`school_info`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **How many people live in your household?** (`family_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Household income?** (`family_income`, number, optional): Enter your household income.
* **What is the highest level of education your parent(s) or guardian(s) have achieved?** (`family_education`, select_one, required): Choose the highest level of education your parent(s) or guardian(s) have achieved from the options provided.
* **What is your current grade level?** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current grade level from the options provided.
* **Date of birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone number** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **School id** (`school_id`, number, optional): Enter your school id.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Other (please specify)** (`student_grade_level_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your grade level.
* **Other info** (`student_other_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **What is the main language spoken at home?** (`family_language`, select_one, required): Choose the main language spoken at home from the options provided.
* **Other (please specify)** (`family_language_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional language spoken at home.
* **How many languages spoken at home?** (`family_language_spoken`, number, optional): Enter the number of languages spoken at home.
* **Achievements** (`student_achievements`, note, optional): Enter any notable achievements or accomplishments.
* **What are the student’s favorite hobbies?** (`student_hobbies`, text, optional): Enter the student's favorite hobbies.
* **Family members' hobbies** (`family_hobbies`, text, optional): Enter the hobbies of family members.
* **Other (please specify)** (`family_education_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your family's education.
* **Highest level of education** (`family_education_level`, select_one, required): Choose the highest level of education from the options provided.
* **What is your parents' or guardian's employment status?** (`family_employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the employment status of your parents or guardian from the options provided.
* **What field of education do you think will be the best for you or your family member?** (`family_education_field`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more fields of education that you think will be the best for you or your family member.
* **Which of these languages do you speak?** (`student_language_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the languages you speak.
* **Which level of education do you expect to achieve?** (`student_education_level`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more levels of education that you expect to achieve.
* **Other (please specify)** (`family_education_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your family's education.
