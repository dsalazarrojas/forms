# product survey form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Survey Form" is designed to gather opinions and feedback about products or services. This form aims to collect information about your products or services' performance, effectiveness, and quality compared to competitors, as well as your overall experience and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select all applicable options for each multiple-choice question.
3. Provide clear and concise text responses for open-ended questions.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your product/service.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can include them in the "Satisfaction" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Survey Form Page 1 (Required: No)**: This is the introductory page to the form.
* **Using Product/Services (Required: No)**: Please describe how you are using our product or service.
* **Competitors (Select Multiple, Required: No)**: Indicate how much more or worse our product or services are compared to competitors.
	+ How much more effective are your products/services compared to competitors
	+ How much worse are your products/services compared to competitors
	+ Are your products/services more expensive compared to competitors
	+ Are your products/services less expensive compared to competitors
* **Satisfaction And Goals (Required: No)**: Describe your satisfaction with our product or service and your goals for using it.
* **How Long Have You Been Using (Required: No)**: Please select the date you started using our product or service.
* **Competitors Providers (Select One, Required: No)**: Please select the provider (e.g., Google, Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, or Others) that you consider as our main competitor.
* **Overall Experience (Select Multiple, Required: No)**: Indicate your overall experience with our product or service.
	+ Very Positive
	+ Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
	+ Very Negative
* **Satisfaction (Required: No)**: Please provide a clear and concise description of your satisfaction with our product or service.
* **Satisfaction (Required: No)**: This is a repeat of the previous question, please provide another clear and concise description of your satisfaction with our product or service.
* **Prospective Services 4 (Select Multiple, Required: No)**: Please indicate your satisfaction with our prospective services.
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Bad
	+ Very Bad
* **Product Quality (Required: No)**: Please provide a clear and concise description of the quality of our product or service.
* **Quality of Product (Required: No)**: This is a repeat of the previous question, please provide another clear and concise description of the quality of our product or service.
* **Overall Quality (Select Multiple, Required: No)**: Indicate your overall quality experience with our product or service.
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Bad
	+ Very Bad

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the "Satisfaction" fields.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your product/service.
* This form is designed to gather information and help us improve our product or service.
