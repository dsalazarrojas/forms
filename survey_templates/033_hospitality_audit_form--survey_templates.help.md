# Hospitality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Audit Form is designed to collect feedback and data from guests on their experiences at the hotel. This form helps hotel staff to identify areas of improvement and maintain high standards of service and cleanliness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the hotel name where the guest was visited.
2. Enter the date of the visit.
3. Enter the employee name of the person who interacted with the guest.
4. Rate the guest's satisfaction with their overall experience.
5. Rate the cleanliness of the hotel.
6. Rate the quality of food served.
7. Rate the service provided by hotel staff.
8. Enter any comments or feedback about their stay.
9. Enter the hotel's email address.
10. Enter the hotel's phone number.
11. Enter the hotel's website.
12. Enter the employee title of the person who interacted with the guest.
13. Enter the employee name of the person who interacted with the guest (if different from step 3).
14. Enter the hotel's department (e.g., front desk, housekeeping).
15. Enter the employee email address.
16. Enter the employee phone number.
17. Enter the hotel's physical address.
18. Enter the city where the hotel is located.
19. Enter the state where the hotel is located.
20. Enter the zip code where the hotel is located.
21. Enter the country where the hotel is located.
22. Enter the region or area where the hotel is located.
23. Enter the time zone of the hotel.
24. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Name (1)**: Enter the name of the hotel where the guest was visited.
* **Date Visited (2)**: Enter the date of the visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Name (3)**: Enter the name of the employee who interacted with the guest.
* **Guest Satisfaction (4)**: Rate the guest's overall satisfaction with their stay using the scale: 1 = Very Dissatisfied, 2 = Dissatisfied, 3 = Satisfied, 4 = Satisfied, 5 = Very Satisfied.
* **Cleanliness Rating (5)**: Rate the cleanliness of the hotel using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Food Rating (6)**: Rate the quality of food served using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Service Rating (7)**: Rate the service provided by hotel staff using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Overall Rating (8)**: Rate the overall experience using a number scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comments (9)**: Enter any additional feedback or comments about their stay.
* **Hotel Email (10)**: Enter the hotel's email address.
* **Hotel Phone (11)**: Enter the hotel's phone number.
* **Hotel Website (12)**: Enter the hotel's website.
* **Employee Title (13)**: Enter the title of the employee who interacted with the guest.
* **Employee Name 2 (14)**: Enter the name of the employee who interacted with the guest (if different from step 3).
* **Department (15)**: Enter the department the employee belongs to (e.g., front desk, housekeeping).
* **Employee Email (16)**: Enter the email address of the employee who interacted with the guest.
* **Employee Phone (17)**: Enter the phone number of the employee who interacted with the guest.
* **Hotel Address (18)**: Enter the physical address of the hotel.
* **City (19)**: Enter the city where the hotel is located.
* **State (20)**: Enter the state where the hotel is located.
* **Zip (21)**: Enter the zip code of the hotel.
* **Country (22)**: Enter the country where the hotel is located.
* **Region (23)**: Enter the region or area where the hotel is located.
* **Time Zone (24)**: Enter the time zone of the hotel.
* **Submitted By (25)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

Note: Fields with no specific instructions can be completed as per hotel requirements or policies.
