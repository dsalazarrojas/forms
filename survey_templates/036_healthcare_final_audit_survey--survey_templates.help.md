# Healthcare Final Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather ratings and recommendations from healthcare professionals for the healthcare services provided. It's designed for internal quality improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Each field is optional, except when marked as "required" in the form definition.
3. Use the ratings for 1 to 5 to rate the level of satisfaction or quality of the healthcare services provided.
4. Use the text fields to provide specific recommendations or follow-up recommendations for improvements.
5. Ensure that the ratings and recommendations are relevant to the specific healthcare audit being conducted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

	+ This field is the main title of the form.
	+ Please enter a brief description of the healthcare final audit survey.
* **rating 1** (`rating_1`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Rate the quality of the service provided from 1 to 5.
	+ 1 = Poor, 2 = Fair, 3 = Average, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent.
