# Safety Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Culture Survey is a tool used to gather information about your perceptions and experiences regarding safety in the workplace. Your input will help us understand how we can improve safety practices and create a better working environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question based on your personal experience.
3. For questions with answer choices, select one or more of the options that best describe your response.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "question 7" text field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This page is meant to be filled out by all employees.
* *1. **question 1**: This question is asking about your level of agreement with the statement: "I feel comfortable reporting incidents or near misses."*
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel comfortable reporting incidents or near misses.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not feel comfortable reporting incidents or near misses.
* *2. **question 2 answer 1**: This question is asking about the frequency of reporting incidents or near misses. Select all that apply.*
	+ 'Yes' if you have reported incidents or near misses in the past.
	+ 'No' if you have not reported incidents or near misses in the past.
* *3. **question 3**: This question is asking about your perception of the importance of safety in your workplace. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you believe safety is very important.
	+ 'No' if you do not think safety is very important.
* *3. **question 3 answer 1**: This question is asking about the level of support you receive when reporting incidents or near misses. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you feel fully supported when reporting incidents or near misses.
	+ 'No' if you do not feel fully supported when reporting incidents or near misses.
* *4. **question 4**: This question is asking about your level of agreement with the statement: "I feel that my suggestions for improvement are taken seriously."*
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel that your suggestions are taken seriously.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not feel that your suggestions are taken seriously.
* *4. **question 4 answer 1**: This question is asking about the frequency of suggestions for improvement. Select all that apply.*
	+ 'Yes' if you have made suggestions for improvement.
	+ 'No' if you have not made suggestions for improvement.
* *5. **question 5**: This question is asking about your perception of the safety culture within your team/department. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you believe your team/department has a positive safety culture.
	+ 'No' if you do not believe your team/department has a positive safety culture.
* *5. **question 5 answer 1**: This question is asking about the frequency of safety meetings. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you attend regular safety meetings.
	+ 'No' if you do not attend regular safety meetings.
* *6. **question 6**: This question is asking about your perception of the availability of safety resources. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you feel that safety resources are readily available.
	+ 'No' if you do not feel that safety resources are readily available.
* *6. **question 6 answer 1**: This question is asking about the quality of safety resources. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you feel that safety resources are high-quality.
	+ 'No' if you do not feel that safety resources are high-quality.
* *7. **question 7**: This question is asking for any additional comments or concerns you may have about safety in your workplace. Please use this field to share any relevant information.*
* *8. **question 8 answer 1**: This question is asking about your level of agreement with the statement: "I feel that my employer takes safety seriously."*
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel that your employer takes safety seriously.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not feel that your employer takes safety seriously.
* *9. **question 9**: This question is asking about your perception of the safety training provided. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you feel that the safety training is comprehensive.
	+ 'No' if you do not feel that the safety training is comprehensive.
* *9. **question 9 answer 1**: This question is asking about the frequency of safety training. Select the most accurate response.*
	+ 'Yes' if you attend regular safety training sessions.
	+ 'No' if you do not attend regular safety training sessions.
* *10. **question 10**: This question is asking about your level of agreement with the statement: "I feel that I have a good understanding of my role in maintaining a safe workplace."*
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel that you have a good understanding of your role.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not feel that you have a good understanding of your role.
