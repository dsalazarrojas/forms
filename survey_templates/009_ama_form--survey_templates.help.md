# AMA Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AMA Form is designed to collect your questions, concerns, and interests in a structured and organized manner. It helps the respondent provide detailed and accurate information about their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Provide your Name**: Enter your name in the field below. This is optional, but it helps the respondent understand your context and provide a more personalized response.
2. **Enter your Email**: If you would like to receive follow-up responses or updates, provide your email address here. This is also optional.
3. **Select a Question Category**: Choose the category that best describes the topic of your question or concern.
4. **Ask Your Question**: Enter your question or concern in the text field below.
5. **Indicate Question Priority**: Select the level of importance you assign to your question or concern.
6. **Request Anonymity**: Check the box if you would like to submit your question anonymously.
7. **Request Follow-up**: Select if you would like to receive a follow-up response to your question.
8. **Provide Additional Context**: If you have any background information that might help answer your question, enter it here.
9. **Preferred Response Format**: Choose how you would like to receive the answer to your question.
10. **Desired Response Deadline**: Select the date by which you would like to receive the answer to your question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to provide context for the respondent.
- **Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive follow-up responses or updates, enter your email address here.
- **Question Category** (`question_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the topic of your question or concern.
- **Your Question** (`question_text`, text, required): Enter your question or concern here.
- **Question Priority** (`question_priority`, select_one, optional): Select the level of importance you assign to your question or concern.
- **Submit Anonymously** (`anonymous_question`, select_one, optional): Check this box if you would like to submit your question anonymously.
- **Follow-up Requested** (`follow_up_requested`, select_one, optional): Select if you would like to receive a follow-up response to your question.
- **Additional Context** (`question_context`, text, optional): If you have any background information that might help answer your question, enter it here.
- **Preferred Response Format** (`preferred_response_format`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive the answer to your question.
- **Desired Response Deadline** (`response_deadline`, date, optional): Select the date by which you would like to receive the answer to your question.
