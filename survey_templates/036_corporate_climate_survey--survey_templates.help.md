# Corporate Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Climate Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees about their perception and experience working for the company. This information will help management understand the climate of the company, identify areas of improvement, and develop strategies to enhance employee satisfaction and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the correct page to complete, each page is designed to collect specific information.
- For each field, carefully read the label and explanation to ensure accuracy.
- Enter your honest and genuine answers for each question.
- Review and verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Survey** (`company_survey`, text, required/optional): This page is meant to gather information about your experience with the company. Please provide your honest feedback about the company's culture, values, and policies.
* **Climate Survey** (`climate_survey`, text, required/optional): This page is designed to understand your perception of the company's climate. Please share your thoughts on the overall atmosphere and work environment of the company.
* **Employee Experience** (`employee_experience`, text, required/optional): This page is meant to gather feedback about your work experience and satisfaction with the company's policies, management, and benefits.

## Tips
- Be honest and genuine in your answers.
- Provide specific examples and details whenever possible.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the "Employee Experience" page.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
