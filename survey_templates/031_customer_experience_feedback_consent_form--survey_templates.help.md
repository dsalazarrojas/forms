# Customer Experience Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the company. It includes questions about their satisfaction with the product or service, quality of support, timeliness of responses, and overall experience with the company. Additionally, it asks for their basic demographics and contact information, as well as any additional comments or suggestions they may have. The form also includes a section for them to express their willingness to provide feedback and provide their final comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, including your name, email address, and any additional comments or suggestions.
3. Select the options that best represent your experience with the company.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction and Consent** (`Introduction and Consent`, text, optional): This section asks for your consent to share your feedback with the company. Please briefly introduce the purpose of this form and confirm that you are willing to provide feedback.
* **Demographics** (`Demographics`, text, optional): Please provide your basic demographics, such as your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* **Experience with the Company** (`Experience with the Company`, select_multiple, required): Please select all options that represent your experience with the company, such as "Positive", "Negative", "Mixed", etc.
* **Satisfaction with the Product or Service** (`Satisfaction with the Product or Service`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about our product or service. Be as specific as possible about your experiences and what we can improve.
* **Quality of the Product or Service** (`Quality of the Product or Service`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best represents your experience with the quality of our product or service.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Overall Experience with the Company** (`Overall Experience with the Company`, text, optional): Share your overall experience with the company.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`Suggestions for Improvement`, text, optional): Please suggest ways we can improve our service.
* **Additional Comments 1** (`Additional Comments 1`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Willingness to Provide Feedback** (`Willingness to Provide Feedback`, radio, required): Select the option that best represents your willingness to provide feedback.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information for follow-up.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`Additional Comments 2`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Satisfaction with the Company** (`Satisfaction with the Company`, select_one, required): Please select the option that best represents your satisfaction with the company.
* **Quality of Support** (`Quality of Support`, select_one, required): Select the option that best represents your experience with our support.
* **Timeliness of Responses** (`Timeliness of Responses`, select_one, required): Select the option that best represents your experience with our response time.
* **Experience with Communication** (`Experience with Communication`, select_one, required): Select the option that best represents your experience with our communication.
* **Experience with the Website or Platform** (`Experience with the Website or Platform`, select_one, required): Select the option that best represents your experience with our website or platform.
* **Final Comments** (`Final Comments`, text, optional): Please provide any final thoughts or comments.
