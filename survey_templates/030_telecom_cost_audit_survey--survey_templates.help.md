# telecom_cost_audit_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telecom cost audit survey is a tool used to gather information about a company's telecom expenses and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, indicated by a star (*) on the form.
3. You can skip optional fields if you do not have the necessary information.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Telecom Spend** (1): Enter your company's current telecom spend, if any.
* **Service Feedback** (2): Choose "Yes" if you are satisfied with the services received, "No" if you are not.
* **Comments** (3): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your telecom services.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Date** (6): Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **Time** (7): Enter the time you completed the survey.
* **Category** (8): Select all applicable categories that apply to your telecom services (e.g., IT, Finance, Procurement).
* **Vendor Name** (9): Enter the name of your telecom vendor.
* **Service Provider** (10): Enter the name of the service provider you received.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* You can skip any pages or fields that are not applicable to your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the telecom department.
* This form is intended to be a quick and easy way to gather information; please try to keep your answers concise.
