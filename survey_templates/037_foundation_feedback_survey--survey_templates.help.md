# Foundation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Foundation Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the Foundation program. This feedback will help us understand your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions, which will be instrumental in improving the program for future users.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  Ensure that you complete all required fields (marked with \*).
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.
4.  Submit your feedback, and we will review and act upon it accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (\*: This field is not required): A brief welcome message to get you started.
* **Name** (\*: This field is not required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback report.
* **Email** (\*: This field is not required): Enter your email address for follow-up or contact purposes.
* **Feedback** (\*: This field is not required): Share your thoughts about your experience with the Foundation program.
* **Suggestion** (\*: This field is not required): Offer any suggestions you have for improving the Foundation program.
* **Program Participation** (\*: This field is not required): Select one of the following options:
	+ True: You participated in the program.
	+ False: You did not participate in the program.
	+ Not Applicable: This option is for users who are not applicable to the program.
* **Program Satisfaction** (\*: This field is not required): Rate your satisfaction with the program (1-10).
* **Likely to Participate** (\*: This field is not required): Select all that apply:
	+ True: You are likely to participate in the program.
	+ False: You are not likely to participate in the program.
	+ Not Applicable: This option is for users who are not applicable to the program.
* **Why** (\*: This field is not required): Share the reasons why you are likely or unlikely to participate in the program.
* **Contact Info** (\*: This field is not required): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Time Spent** (\*: This field is not required): Enter the approximate amount of time you spent on the program.
* **Date Spent** (\*: This field is not required): Enter the date(s) you participated in the program.
* **Frequency** (\*: This field is not required): Enter any additional comments about your frequency of participation.
* **Other Comments** (\*: This field is not required): Share any other thoughts or comments about your experience with the Foundation program.
