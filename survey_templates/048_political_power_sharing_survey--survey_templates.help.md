# Political Power Sharing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers your opinions on various aspects of political power sharing, including conflict resolution, models, community involvement, decision-making processes, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the page with the corresponding section you want to respond to.
2.  Read the explanation for each field carefully and answer accordingly.
3.  Fill in the fields as instructed for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction** (`survey_start`, `note`, optional):
    This page provides a brief overview of the survey. Please read it to understand the context and purpose of the survey.
*   **How do you think conflicts can be resolved effectively** (`conflict_resolution`, `select_multiple`, optional):
    This field asks about your opinion on how conflicts can be resolved effectively. Select all the options that you think are effective ways to resolve conflicts.
*   **What do you think about power sharing models** (`power_sharing_models`, `select_one`, optional):
    This field asks about your opinion on power sharing models. Choose one option that best reflects your thoughts on power sharing models.
*   **What is the role of community participation** (`community_involvement`, `select_one`, optional):
    This field asks about your opinion on the role of community participation in power sharing. Choose one option that best reflects your thoughts on community involvement.
*   **How do you prefer your decision-making process** (`decision_making_process`, `select_multiple`, optional):
    This field asks about your preferred decision-making process. Select all the options that you prefer for your decision-making process.
*   **Provide details about power-sharing models** (`power_sharing_models_details`, `text`, optional):
    This field is for providing additional details about power sharing models. Please type in your thoughts on this topic.
*   **What is your preferred conflict resolution process** (`conflict_resolution_process`, `select_one`, optional):
    This field asks about your preferred conflict resolution process. Choose one option that best reflects your thoughts on conflict resolution.
*   **What do you think about civic leadership** (`civic_leadership`, `select_multiple`, optional):
    This field asks about your opinion on civic leadership. Select all the options that you think are important for civic leadership.
*   **Role of remediation in conflict resolution** (`role_of_remediation`, `select_multiple`, optional):
    This field asks about your opinion on the role of remediation in conflict resolution. Select all the options that you think are relevant for conflict resolution.
*   **Political culture and power sharing** (`political_culture`, `select_one`, optional):
    This field asks about your opinion on political culture and power sharing. Choose one option that best reflects your thoughts on this topic.
