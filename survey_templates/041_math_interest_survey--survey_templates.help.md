# Math Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' interests, preferences, and experiences related to mathematics. The data collected will be used to identify areas of interest within the math department and to improve teaching methods and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them accordingly.
2. Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple questions.
3. Enter your answer in the text fields.
4. Fill out the required fields.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please provide a brief overview of your background and experience with mathematics.
* **How do you feel about math?**: Choose how you feel about mathematics, whether you enjoy it or not.
* **Why do you like math?** (Optional): If you like math, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Favorite Math Topic**: Select your favorite math topic from the provided options.
* **Favorite Subject**: Select your favorite subject from the list (Math, English, Science, History, Geography, Arts).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Math Survey Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the math survey.
* **Favorite Book**: Select your favorite book from the provided options.
* **Time Spent Reading**: Enter the amount of time you spent reading math-related materials.
* **Rating of the Math Survey**: Rate the survey on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Favorite Math Resource**: Select your favorite math resource from the provided options.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Favorite Website**: Select your favorite website from the provided options.
* **Favorite Math App**: Select your favorite math app from the provided options.
* **Rating of Teachers**: Rate your teachers on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Favorite Tool Used**: Select your favorite tool used for math-related activities from the provided options.
* **Rating of Resources**: Rate the resources on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Favorite Educational Video**: Select your favorite educational video from the provided options.
