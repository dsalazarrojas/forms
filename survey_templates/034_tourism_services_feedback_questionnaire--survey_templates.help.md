# Tourism Services Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tourism Services Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with tourism services, helping service providers improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary information for each field, and click "Submit" when finished.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Name** (`tour_name`, text, false): Please enter the name of the tour you visited.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, false): Enter the date you visited the tour.
* **Tour Rating** (`tour_rating`, number, false): Rate your tour experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Tour Comments** (`tour_comments`, text, false): Share your thoughts and experiences about the tour.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, false): Choose the type of service provider you interacted with (Hotel, Guide, Tour Agency, etc.).
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, number, false): Rate the service provided from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Service Comments** (`service_comments`, text, false): Share your thoughts and experiences about the service.
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, select_one, false): Rate the quality of staff service (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, false): If you want to mention a specific staff member, enter their name here.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, false): Share any comments about the staff service.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, false): Share your overall feedback about the service provider.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, false): Choose the category of the service provider (Hotel, Tour Agency, Service, etc.).
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, select_multiple, false): Select the sub categories related to the service provider (Beach, Mountain, City, etc.).
* **Sub Sub Category** (`sub_sub_category`, select_multiple, false): Select the sub sub categories related to the service provider (Yes, No, etc.).
* **Sub Category 2** (`sub_category2`, select_multiple, false): Select the second sub category related to the service provider (Yes, No, etc.).
* **Sub Sub Category 2** (`sub_sub_category2`, select_multiple, false): Select the sub sub category 2 related to the service provider (Yes, No, etc.).
* **Sub Category 3** (`sub_category3`, select_multiple, false): Select the third sub category related to the service provider (Yes, No, etc.).
* **Sub Sub Category 3** (`sub_sub_category3`, select_multiple, false): Select the sub sub category 3 related to the service provider (Yes, No, etc.). 

Please note that the required fields are not marked as required, as per the original form design. However, it is essential to fill in all the fields as accurately and completely as possible to ensure that your feedback is comprehensive and helpful.
