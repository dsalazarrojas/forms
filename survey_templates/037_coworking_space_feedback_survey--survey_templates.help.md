# Coworking Space Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from coworkers about our space. Your input will help us improve our services and make it more suitable to our customers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the fields as follows:
- Page 1: Coworking Suggestions
  - This page is for any general suggestions or ideas you may have about our coworking space.
  - Please type in your suggestions in the text field provided.
- Page 2: Rating and Feedback
  - This page has several fields for rating and providing feedback about our space.
  - Please select one of the options for "How would you rate our space?" and answer the following questions:
    - "Which of our spaces do you frequent the most?" - Select one of the options provided.
    - "How familiar are you with our pricing model?" - Select one of the options provided.
    - "Do you think our pricing is fair?" - Select one or more of the options provided.
    - "Are there any amenities you would like to see us add or improve?" - Type in your suggestions in the text field provided.
    - "Have you used any of our on-site services?" - Select one or more of the options provided.
    - "Do you have any other feedback or suggestions?" - Type in your comments in the text field provided.
- Page 3: Additional Feedback
  - This page is for any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
  - Please rate how likely you are to recommend our coworking space to a friend, select "Yes" or "No".
  - We also have space for any additional comments or feedback.
  - You may also be asked to provide your contact information in case we need to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coworking Suggestions** (`coworking_suggestion`, text, optional): This is a text field for any general suggestions or ideas you may have about our coworking space.
* **How would you rate our space?** (`rating`, select_one, optional): This field has a dropdown menu with two options: "Good" and "Fair".
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional comments about our space.

* **How familiar are you with our pricing model?** (`pricing_familiarity`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you think our pricing is fair?** (`price_suggestion`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Are there any amenities you would like to see us add or improve?** (`amenity_suggestion`, text, optional): Type in your suggestions in the text field provided.
* **Have you used any of our on-site services?** (`on_site_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you have any other feedback or suggestions?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Type in your comments in the text field provided.
* **How likely are you to recommend our coworking space to a friend?** (`coworking_rating2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is a field for your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is a field for your phone number.

* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This is a field for the current time.
