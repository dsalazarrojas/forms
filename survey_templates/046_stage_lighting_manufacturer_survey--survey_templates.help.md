# Stage Lighting Manufacturer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from stage lighting manufacturers to understand their product offerings, market focus, challenges, and current trends in the industry. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey and provide us with your valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the survey instructions carefully.
2. Fill in your name and title of your company (if you'd like to provide it).
3. Choose the category that best describes your products (stage lights or other).
4. Provide a brief description of your product capacity.
5. List a few examples of your products (you can list multiple lines if needed).
6. Identify your target markets.
7. Share any challenges you're currently facing.
8. Share any current trends or observations in the stage lighting industry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **stage_lighting_manufacturer_survey** (`stage_lighting_manufacturer_survey`, text, optional): This is the title of your company. Please enter the name of your organization, such as "Acme Stage Lighting, Inc." or "Stage Lighting Co."
* **category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes your products. We're interested in understanding if you manufacture stage lights or other types of products.
* **capacity** (`capacity`, text, optional): Describe the capacity of your company, such as "small," "medium," or "large."
* **products** (`products`, text, optional): List a few examples of your products, such as "LED stage lights," "conventional stage lights," or "special effects systems."
* **markets** (`markets`, text, optional): Identify the markets where you sell your products, such as "theater," "dance," or "event."
* **challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Share any challenges you're facing as a stage lighting manufacturer, such as "competition from cheaper imports," or "innovation in technology."
* **trends** (`trends`, text, optional): Share any observations or trends you've noticed in the stage lighting industry, such as "increased use of LED lighting," or "integration with smart home systems."
