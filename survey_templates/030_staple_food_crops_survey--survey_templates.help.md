# Staple Food Crops Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects data on staple food crops, including production practices, market access, and access to support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant answers to each question and submit at the end. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your staple crop?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_1`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select the staple crop that you grow or produce the most. This could be maize, rice, sorghum, beans, groundnuts, cowpea, or millet.
* **How many acres of land do you cultivate?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_2`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the number of acres of land that you use for staple crop production.
* **What is your farm's production system?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_3`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select the production system that you use for staple crop production, such as rainfed, irrigated, or mixed.
* **How much of this land do you use for staple crops?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_4`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the percentage of land that you use for staple crop production.
* **What is your main market for staple crops?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_5`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select the main market where you sell or distribute your staple crops, such as formal or informal.
* **Have you ever received any agricultural extension services?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_6`, select_multiple, Optional)
  Please select all the services that you have received from extension services, such as training, inputs, or advice.
* **How much support do you need for your staple crops?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_7`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select the level of support that you need for staple crop production, such as high, medium, or low.
* **Do you have access to credit services for your staple crops?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_8`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have access to credit services for staple crop production.
* **How many family members are involved in staple crop production?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_9`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the number of family members who are involved in staple crop production.
* **What is your staple crop production season start date?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_10`, date, Optional)
  Please enter the date when your staple crop production season starts.
* **What is your staple crop production season end date?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_11`, date, Optional)
  Please enter the date when your staple crop production season ends.
* **Do you have a staple crop production permit?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_12`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have a staple crop production permit.
* **How many staple crop production permits do you have?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_13`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the number of staple crop production permits that you have.
* **Do you have staple crop insurance?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_14`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have staple crop insurance.
* **How many staple crop insurance policies do you have?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_15`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the number of staple crop insurance policies that you have.
* **What is your staple crop production value?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_16`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the total value of your staple crop production.
* **Do you have a staple crop production schedule?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_17`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have a staple crop production schedule.
* **How many staple crops do you have a schedule for?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_18`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the number of staple crops that you have a schedule for.
* **Do you have staple crop production costs?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_19`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have staple crop production costs.
* **How much staple crop production costs do you have?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_20`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the amount of staple crop production costs that you have.
* **Do you have staple crop production revenue?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_21`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have staple crop production revenue.
* **How much staple crop production revenue do you have?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_22`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the amount of staple crop production revenue that you have.
* **Do you have staple crop production profit?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_23`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have staple crop production profit.
* **How much staple crop production profit do you have?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_24`, number, Optional)
  Please enter the amount of staple crop production profit that you have.
* **Do you have a staple crop production record?** (`staple_food_crops_survey_25`, select_one, Optional)
  Please select whether you have a staple crop production record.
