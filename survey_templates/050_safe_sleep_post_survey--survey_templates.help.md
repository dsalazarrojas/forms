# Safe Sleep Post Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your child's safe sleep practices and environment. The survey is intended to help us understand your child's sleep habits and environment, which will aid in providing better guidance and support for your child's safety and health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Select the appropriate response for each question.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page1`, note, required: false): This is an introduction page that provides context for the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Sleep Practices** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page2`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the safe sleep practices that apply to your child's current sleep environment.
	+ `True`: The child sleeps on their back.
	+ `False`: The child sleeps on their side or stomach.
	+ `Unsure`: You are unsure about the child's sleep position.
* **Safe Sleep Environment** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page3`, text, required: false): Please describe the safe sleep environment for your child.
* **Supervising Adult** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page4`, text, required: false): Please identify the adult who supervises your child while sleeping.
* **Safe Sleep Place** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page5`, select_one, required: false): Please select the safe sleep place for your child.
	+ `Crib`
	+ `Bassinet`
	+ `Mat`
	+ `Other`
* **Sleeping Position** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page6`, select_one, required: false): Please select the position your child sleeps in.
	+ `Back`
	+ `Side`
	+ `Stomach`
	+ `Other`
* **Bedtime Routine** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page7`, text, required: false): Please describe the bedtime routine for your child.
* **Sleep Scheduling** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page8`, text, required: false): Please describe the sleep scheduling for your child.
* **Sleep Environment** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page9`, text, required: false): Please describe the sleep environment for your child.
* **Sleep Environment Quality** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page10`, text, required: false): Please rate the quality of your child's sleep environment (e.g., 1-10).
* **Sleep Environment Score** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page11`, number, required: false): Please enter a score for the sleep environment (e.g., 1-10).
* **Sleep Practices Score** (`safe_sleep_post_survey_page12`, number, required: false): Please enter a score for your child's sleep practices (e.g., 1-10).

## Tips
* Ensure that all questions are answered accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Other" or describe your answer in the "Other" field.
* If you need help or clarification about any question, please contact us.
