# School Lunch Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's school lunch experience. The survey aims to gather feedback on how frequently children eat school meals, their overall satisfaction with the school's meal provision, and any dietary restrictions or allergies that affect their meal options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Name** field with your child's name.
2. Select the name of your child's **School**.
3. Choose how frequently you get your child to eat school meals from the options **Regularly**, **Occasionally**, or **Rarely** in the **How often do you get your child to eat school meals?** field.
4. Choose your child's **age group** from the options **Elementary school student**, **Middle school student**, **High school student**, or **Other (please specify)**.
5. If applicable, provide any **Dietary restrictions or allergies** that affect your child's meal options.
6. Select how often you choose to send your child to school on weekends or holidays from the options **Regularly**, **Occasionally**, or **Rarely** in the **How often do you choose to send your child to school on weekends or holidays?** field.
7. Rate your **meal satisfaction** with the school's meal provision using the number scale.
8. If you have any **other comments or suggestions** for the school, please provide them in the text field.
9. If you have any **feedback on the school's lunch menu**, please provide it in the text field.
9. Finally, rate your **overall satisfaction** with the school's meal provision using the number scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Lunch Survey Information**: This is the title of the survey.
* **Name**: Enter your child's name.
* **School**: Enter your child's school name.
* **How often do you get your child to eat school meals?**: Select how frequently you get your child to eat school meals. You can choose from **Regularly**, **Occasionally**, or **Rarely**.
* **What is your child's age group?**: Choose your child's age group. You can select from **Elementary school student**, **Middle school student**, **High school student**, or **Other (please specify)**.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions or allergies that affect your child's meal options?** (Optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies that affect your child's meal options, provide them here.
* **How often do you choose to send your child to school on weekends or holidays?**: Select how often you choose to send your child to school on weekends or holidays. You can choose from **Regularly**, **Occasionally**, or **Rarely**.
* **How satisfied are you with the school's meal provision?** (Rating scale): Rate your meal satisfaction with the school's meal provision using the number scale.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions for the school?** (Text field): If you have any other comments or suggestions for the school, provide them here.
* **Do you have any feedback on the school's lunch menu?** (Text field): If you have any feedback on the school's lunch menu, provide it here.
* **How satisfied are you with the school's meal provision overall?** (Rating scale): Rate your overall satisfaction with the school's meal provision using the number scale.
