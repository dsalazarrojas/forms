# School Virtual Learning Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Virtual Learning Parent Survey aims to gather your feedback and insights on the remote learning environment provided by the school. Your input will help the school to better understand your needs and concerns, ensuring that we can provide the best support for your child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read each question carefully and answer it according to your true feelings.
2. Fill in all the required fields marked with asterisk (\*).
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, select the option that best reflects your answer.
4. If you have any further comments or feedback, please use the comments field.
5. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Start
* **Start** (`survey_start`, select_one, required): Please choose "Yes" if you have started using the remote learning environment or "No" if you haven't.

### Parent Name
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or as you would like to be addressed.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that the school can use to contact you for any updates or concerns.

### What is one thing you wish you had done differently in the remote learning environment?
* **What is one thing you wish you had done differently in the remote learning environment?** (`feedback_1`, text, required): Share one thing that you feel would have improved your experience in the remote learning environment.

### What was the most helpful resource or support provided to you during remote learning?
* **What was the most helpful resource or support provided to you during remote learning?** (`feedback_2`, text, required): Share the most helpful resource or support that you received during remote learning.

### What support do you feel you need to be successful in this remote learning environment?
* **What support do you feel you need to be successful in this remote learning environment?** (`feedback_3`, text, required): Share the support that you feel you need to be successful in the remote learning environment.

### How would you rate the overall quality of support provided to you in remote learning?
* **How would you rate the overall quality of support provided to you in remote learning?** (`rating_1`, select_one, required): Choose your answer from the following options: 
  \* Poor 
  \* Fair 
  \* Good 
  \* Very Good 
  \* Excellent

### How would you rate the effectiveness of the remote learning platform?
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of the remote learning platform?** (`rating_2`, select_one, required): Choose your answer from the following options: 
  \* Poor 
  \* Fair 
  \* Good 
  \* Very Good 
  \* Excellent

### What method of communication is your preferred method of contact from your school?
* **What method of communication is your preferred method of contact from your school?** (`communication_1`, select_multiple, required): Choose the options that you prefer to contact you:
  \* Phone Call 
  \* Email 
  \* Text message 
  \* Video call

### Are you able to receive emails sent from your school?
* **Are you able to receive emails sent from your school?** (`communication_2`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you can receive emails sent from your school, 'False' otherwise.

### Do you have any other comments or feedback you would like to share?
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback you would like to share?** (`comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or feedback that you feel are important for the school to know.
