# electric_vehicle_owners_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your electric vehicle habits, preferences, and behaviors. Your input will help us better understand electric vehicle adoption trends, charging habits, and driving patterns among electric vehicle owners. This information will be used to improve services and products related to electric vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer accordingly
* Choose the options that best fit your habits or preferences
* If you have any issues or need help, feel free to contact us

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What type of vehicle do you own?** (vehicle_type, select_one, required: false)
Choose the type of vehicle you own from the list below:
• Car
• Truck
• Bike
• Other (please specify in the comments)
### **What is the year of your vehicle?** (vehicle_year, number, required: false)
Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.
### **Does your vehicle have a battery size of 40kWh or larger?** (has_large_battery, select_one, required: false)
Select 'True' if your vehicle has a battery size of 40kWh or larger, 'False' otherwise.
### **How often do you charge your vehicle?** (charging_frequency, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose how often you charge your vehicle:
• True
• False
• Daily
• Weekly
• Monthly
### **What is your driving range?** (driving_range, select_one, required: false)

• 100-200mi

• 300+mi
### **How much does your vehicle weigh?** (vehicle_weight, select_one, required: false)

• 0-1t

### **How often do you drive in a week?** (driving_frequency, select_multiple, required: false)

### **How many miles do you drive per week?** (driving_miles_per_week, number, required: false)

### **How many miles do you drive per month?** (driving_miles_per_month, number, required: false)
Enter the number of miles you drive per month.
### **How many miles do you drive per year?** (driving_miles_per_year, number, required: false)
Enter the number of miles you drive per year.
