# identify_your_core_emotional_pain - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals identify their core emotional pain areas and gather information to better understand their emotional state.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You may select multiple options for the emotional pain areas that apply to you.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to this survey**: This is a introductory text that welcomes you to the form.
* **Emotional pain area 1**: Select one of the options (Area 1a, Area 1b, Area 1c) that best describes your emotional pain area 1. You may choose one or more options.
* **Emotional pain area 2**: Select multiple options (Area 2a, Area 2b, Area 2c) that best describe your emotional pain area 2. You may choose none, one, or more options.
* **Emotional pain area 3**: Select one option (Yes, No) that best describes your emotional pain area 3.
* **Date of occurrence**: Please enter the date when you first experienced this pain (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of day**: Please enter the time of day when you experienced this pain (in 24-hour format).
* **Emotional intensity**: Rate the intensity of your emotional pain on a scale from 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Emotional frequency**: Rate the frequency of your emotional pain on a scale from 1-10, where 1 is rare and 10 is frequent.
* **Emotional duration**: Rate the duration of your emotional pain on a scale from 1-10, where 1 is short-term and 10 is long-term.
* **Emotional support systems**: Select multiple options (Option 1, Option 2) that you feel would help you feel better.
* **Additional comments**: Please provide any additional comments or explanations that you feel would be helpful in understanding your emotional pain.
* **Contact information**: Please provide any contact information (e.g., email, phone number) if you would like to be contacted for further support or guidance.
