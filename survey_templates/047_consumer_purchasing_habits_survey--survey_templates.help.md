# Consumer Purchasing Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Purchasing Habits Survey aims to collect data on consumers' shopping behaviors and preferences to better understand their purchasing habits and decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please select all questions that apply** and provide accurate answers.
2. **Use only the options provided** for select-one and select-multiple questions.
3. **For text fields**, provide clear and concise descriptions of your shopping habits and behaviors.
