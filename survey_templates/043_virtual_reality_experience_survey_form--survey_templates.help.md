# virtual_reality_experience_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual reality experience survey form is a tool for collecting feedback from users about their experience with a virtual reality program. It is designed to gauge user satisfaction and identify areas of improvement for the program's development and enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply answer the questions presented to you. You can skip any questions that you do not feel comfortable answering or do not have an opinion on. The form is completely optional, and your responses will be used to improve the virtual reality program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome to the Virtual Reality Experience Survey** (`page: 1`): This is an introductory page that welcomes you to the survey. It is a one-time page and cannot be skipped.
- **What do you think of the Virtual Reality Program?** (`page: 2`): Select all options that apply to describe your thoughts on the Virtual Reality Program:
    - Yes
    - No
- **How would you rate your experience with this program?** (`page: 3`): Choose a number to rate your experience with the program on a scale of 1-5.
- **How comfortable did you feel during the program?** (`page: 4`): Choose a number to rate your comfort level on a scale of 1-5.
- **Would you recommend the Virtual Reality Program?** (`page: 5`): Select one option to indicate if you would recommend the program:
    - Yes
    - No
- **Do you have any comments?** (`page: 6`): Type in any comments or thoughts you have about the program.
- **How can we reach you?** (`page: 7`): Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you, if desired.
