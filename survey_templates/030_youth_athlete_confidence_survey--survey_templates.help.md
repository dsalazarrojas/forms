# Youth Athlete Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help coaches and administrators gain insight into the confidence levels of youth athletes. By completing this survey, you will be asked a series of questions that will help us understand your self-assessed confidence across different aspects and timeframes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For the "question five" field, select all applicable options that apply to you.
3. For the "time_of_year" field, select the current time of year (e.g., "Spring", "Fall", etc.).
4. For the "additional_comments" field, enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
