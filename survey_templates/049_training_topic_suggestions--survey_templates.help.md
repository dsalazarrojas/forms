# Training Topic Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather suggestions for training topics from users. It's intended for users to input their preferred format for the training and list specific topics or subject areas they're interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Training Topic Suggestions Form" link to access the form.
2. Select the preferred format for the training: video, in-person, or online.
3. List specific topics or subject areas you're interested in.
4. Enter your email address (optional).
5. Click on "Submit" to submit your suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Training Topic Suggestions Form (Page 1)
* **Training Topic Suggestions Form** (`training_topic_suggestions_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you can input your suggestions for training topics.
* **What is the preferred format for the training?** (`preferred_formats`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the format you prefer for the training (video, in-person, or online).
* **Training Ideas** (`training_ideas`, text, required/optional): List specific topics or subject areas you're interested in.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, required/optional): Enter your interests related to the training topic.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): This is a submit button, it doesn't ask for any information, just a formality.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (optional).

### Tips
* Make sure to only input information that is accurate and relevant.
* If you're having trouble completing a field, try to ask for help.
* If you're looking for more information about training topics, check our website or contact us.
