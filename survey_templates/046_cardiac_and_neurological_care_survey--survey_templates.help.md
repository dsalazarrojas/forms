# Cardiac And Neurological Care Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality of care provided by the cardiac and neurological care services. It will help us understand how you experienced our services, identify areas of strength and weakness, and make informed decisions to improve our care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For select one questions, choose the option that best represents your opinion.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Please fill out the form once per visit.
