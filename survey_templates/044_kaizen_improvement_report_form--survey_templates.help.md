# Kaizen Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kaizen Improvement Report Form is used to collect and track suggestions for small, continuous improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field. This is required to associate your improvement suggestion with your identity.
2. Select one or more Suggestion Type(s) from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Workflow", "Product", and "Service".
3. In the "Suggestion" field, provide a clear and concise description of the improvement suggestion.
4. Choose the Category that best describes the improvement suggestion from the dropdown menu: "Other", "Workflow", or "Product".
5. Select the Impact of the improvement suggestion from the dropdown menu: "Low", "Medium", and "High".
6. Enter the benefit of the improvement in the "Benefit" field.
7. Check if resources are required for the improvement suggestion. If yes, select "True" from the dropdown menu.
8. If any comments are needed, please provide them in the "Comments" fields.
9. Finally, submit the form to complete the kaizen improvement report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your Employee ID to associate your improvement suggestion with your identity.
* **Suggestion Type** (`suggestion_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more Suggestion Type(s) from the dropdown menu.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the improvement suggestion.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the Category that best describes the improvement suggestion from the dropdown menu.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_multiple, required): Select the Impact of the improvement suggestion from the dropdown menu.
* **Benefit** (`benefit`, text, required): Enter the benefit of the improvement.
* **Resources** (`resources`, select_one, optional): Check if resources are required for the improvement suggestion.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If any comments are needed, please provide them.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): If any comments are needed from the submitter, please provide them.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, text, optional): If any comments are needed from the approver, please provide them.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Approved On** (`approved_on`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the improvement suggestion from the dropdown menu.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Created On** (`created_on`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Modified By** (`modified_by`, text, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Modified On** (`modified_on`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in by the system.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select the Priority of the improvement suggestion from the dropdown menu.
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, optional): Enter any additional tags or keywords related to the improvement suggestion.

Note: Fields with a type of "date" are automatically filled in by the system and do not require user input.
