The Newsletter Updates Form is designed to collect information from users for newsletter updates. This form helps us gather valuable insights into your preferences and interests, enabling us to provide more relevant and engaging updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user input in the designated field.
2. Provide your email address so we can stay in touch.
3. Enter your first name for our records.
4. Enter your last name for our records.
5. Select the type of email you'd like to receive (Business or Personal).
6. Indicate how frequently you'd like to receive newsletters (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
7. If applicable, enter your date of birth for age-based newsletter targeting.
8. Choose a time of day you'd prefer to receive our newsletters.
9. Leave any additional comments or notes in the provided field.
