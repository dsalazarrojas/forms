# Patient Empowerment In Digital Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from patients about their experiences with digital health tools and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Complete the form in its entirety, as indicated by the required fields.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "Patient Comments" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Demographics**
 * **Patient Demographics** (`patient_demographics`, text, optional): This section asks for basic information about you, the patient. Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Health Tool Experience** (`health_tool_questions`, text, optional): This section asks about your experience with digital health tools and services. Please share your thoughts and feelings about your experiences.
* **Care Experience** (`care_experience_questions`, text, optional): This section asks about your experience with healthcare services. Please share your thoughts and feelings about your experiences.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, text, optional): This section asks about your experience with this survey. Please share your thoughts and feelings about completing this form.
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, text, optional): This section is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with digital health tools and services.
* **Healthcare Team** (`healthcare_team_questions`, text, optional): This section asks about your experience with the healthcare team. Please share your thoughts and feelings about your interactions with them.
