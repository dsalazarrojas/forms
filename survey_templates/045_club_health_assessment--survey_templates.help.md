# Club Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Health Assessment is a tool used to evaluate the health of a club from the perspective of its members. The form is designed to gather feedback and insights from members on various aspects of club life, including member information, engagement, retention, communication, inclusion, leadership, and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions about your membership status and level of participation on page 1 of the form.
2.  Rate your sense of belonging, how you feel valued as a member, and the opportunities for involvement and leadership in the club on page 3 of the form.
3.  Evaluate the quality of club communication, how informed you are about club activities, and how your voice is heard in the club on page 5 of the form.
4.  Rate the club's inclusiveness, diversity representation, and safety and respectfulness on page 7 of the form.
5.  Assess the leadership effectiveness, the club's clear goals and direction, and the main concerns and improvement areas on page 9 of the form.
6.  Share your main concerns about the club's health and provide suggestions for improvement on the last page of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name** (2, text, optional): This is your name as a member of the club.
* **How Long Have You Been a Member?** (3, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate how long you've been a member of the club, with options ranging from less than 1 semester to 4+ years.
* **Level of Participation** (4, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your level of participation in the club, with options ranging from very active - officer or leadership to minimal - rarely attend.
* **Sense of Belonging in Club** (6, number, required): Rate your sense of belonging in the club, with options ranging from 1 (no belonging) to 5 (strong belonging).
* **Feel Valued as a Member** (7, number, required): Rate how valued you feel as a member, with options ranging from 1 (not valued) to 5 (highly valued).
* **Opportunities for Involvement and Leadership** (8, number, required): Rate the opportunities for involvement and leadership in the club, with options ranging from 1 (limited) to 5 (abundant).
* **Likelihood to Remain a Member Next Year** (9, number, required): Rate your likelihood to remain a member next year, with options ranging from 1 (unlikely) to 5 (very likely).
* **Quality of Club Communication** (11, number, required): Rate the quality of club communication, with options ranging from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Kept Informed About Club Activities** (12, number, required): Rate how informed you are about club activities, with options ranging from 1 (not informed) to 5 (very informed).
* **Feel Your Voice is Heard** (13, number, required): Rate how you feel your voice is heard, with options ranging from 1 (not heard) to 5 (fully heard).
* **Club is Inclusive and Welcoming** (15, number, required): Rate how inclusive and welcoming you feel the club is, with options ranging from 1 (not inclusive) to 5 (very inclusive).
* **Diversity Well-Represented** (16, number, required): Rate the diversity representation in the club, with options ranging from 1 (not diverse) to 5 (very diverse).
* **Safe and Respectful Environment** (17, number, required): Rate how safe and respectful you feel in the club, with options ranging from 1 (not safe) to 5 (very safe).
* **Leadership Effectiveness** (19, number, required): Rate the leadership effectiveness, with options ranging from 1 (ineffective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Clear Goals and Direction** (20, number, required): Rate the club's clear goals and direction, with options ranging from 1 (not clear) to 5 (very clear).
* **Main Concerns About Club Health** (22, text, optional): Share your main concerns about the club's health.
* **Most Important Improvements Needed** (23, text, optional): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Suggestions or Comments** (24, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

*   Be honest and sincere in your answers.
*   Take as much time as needed to complete the form.
*   If you're unsure about any questions, you can always refer to the provided options.
*   Your feedback is appreciated and will help us make the club a better place for everyone.
