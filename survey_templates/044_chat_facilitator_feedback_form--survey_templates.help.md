# Chat Facilitator Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from chat facilitators, aiming to assess their performance and identify areas for improvement. The purpose of this form is to help facilitators grow and provide better experiences for their participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Section 1 - Your Information**: This section collects your basic information as a facilitator, including your name and email address (if you wish to receive follow-up communication).
2. Fill out your **Name** and **Email Address** (if you want to receive follow-up emails).
3. **Section 2 - Chat Event Details**: Provide information about the chat event you facilitated, including the **Name** and **Date** of the event.
4. **Section 3 - Facilitation Quality**: Rate the clarity of the facilitator's communication, their engagement with participants, and their responsiveness to questions.
5. **Section 4 - Overall Experience**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the facilitator and their performance, as well as their knowledge and professionalism.
6. **Section 5 - Feedback and Suggestions**: Share your strengths, areas for improvement, and any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address to receive follow-up communications.
* **Chat Event Attended** (`event_attended`, text, required): Enter the name of the chat event.
* **Date of Chat Event** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the chat event.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the chat facilitator.
* **Clarity of Communication** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the facilitator's communication. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Facilitator Engagement** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Rate the facilitator's engagement with participants. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Responsiveness** (`responsiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the facilitator's responsiveness to questions. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Pacing of Chat Discussion** (`facilitation_pacing`, select_one, optional): Rate the pacing of the chat discussion. Options are: Too Fast, Just Right, Too Slow.
* **How Well Topic Was Handled** (`topic_handling`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the discussion on the topic. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Inclusion of All Participants** (`participant_inclusion`, select_one, optional): Rate the inclusion of all participants in the discussion. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Facilitator Professionalism** (`professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the facilitator's professionalism and conduct. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the facilitator. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Facilitator Knowledge Demonstrated** (`knowledge_demonstrated`, select_one, optional): Rate the facilitator's knowledge of the topic. Options are: Expert, Very Knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Adequate, Lacking.
* **Facilitator Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): Share the strengths of the facilitator.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvements`, text, optional): Share areas where the facilitator could improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments.
