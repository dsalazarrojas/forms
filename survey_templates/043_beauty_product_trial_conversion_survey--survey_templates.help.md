# Beauty Product Trial Conversion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Product Trial Conversion Survey is designed to collect feedback from trial participants on their experience with a beauty product trial program. This survey aims to gather information on the effectiveness of the trial program and the product, as well as to identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide accurate feedback.
2. Select the most suitable option from the provided choices.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief but detailed answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trial Program Participated In** (Required)
	+ This question asks about the specific trial program you participated in.
* **When Did You Receive the Trial Product** (Required)
	+ Please enter the date you received the trial product.
* **What Is Your Current Status** (Required)
	+ Select your current status in the trial program (Still in Trial Period, Completed Trial, Converted to Purchase, Not Yet Decided).
* **Trial Product Quality Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the quality of the trial product (Very Poor, Poor, Average, Good, Excellent).
* **Product Effectiveness Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the effectiveness of the product (Very Ineffective, Ineffective, Neutral, Effective, Very Effective).
* **Value for Money Perception** (Required)
	+ Rate the value for money perception of the product (Poor Value, Fair Value, Good Value, Great Value, Excellent Value).
* **How Does It Compare to Competitors** (Required)
	+ Compare the product to its competitors (Much Worse, Worse, About the Same, Better, Much Better).
* **How Long Did You Use the Trial Product** (Required)
	+ Select the duration you used the trial product (Never Opened, Less than 1 Week, 1-2 Weeks, 2-4 Weeks, Full Trial Period).
* **Was Sample Size Sufficient to Test** (Required)
	+ Answer if the sample size was sufficient to test the product (Yes, No).
* **Trial Program Convenience Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the convenience of the trial program (Very Inconvenient, Inconvenient, Neutral, Convenient, Very Convenient).
* **Have You Converted to Full-Size Purchase** (Required)
	+ Select your conversion status (Yes - Already Purchased, Yes - Planning to Purchase, No - Not Planning to Purchase, Undecided).
* **Factors Influencing Purchase Decision** (Required)
	+ Provide a brief description of the factors influencing your purchase decision.
* **Any Barriers to Purchase** (Optional)
	+ If applicable, describe any barriers to purchase.
* **Did Trial Help You Discover New Products** (Required)
	+ Answer if the trial helped you discover new products (Yes, No).
* **Likelihood to Participate in Future Trials** (Required)
	+ Select your likelihood to participate in future trials (Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, Very Likely).
* **Would You Recommend This Trial Program** (Required)
	+ Select your recommendation for the trial program (Definitely Not, Probably Not, Unsure, Probably Yes, Definitely Yes).
* **How Could the Trial Program Be Improved** (Optional)
	+ Provide suggestions on how to improve the trial program.
* **Importance of Conversion Incentives** (Required)
	+ Rate the importance of conversion incentives (Not Important, Somewhat Important, Very Important, Critical).

By following these steps and answering the questions honestly, you will be providing valuable feedback to help improve the beauty product trial program. Thank you for participating!
