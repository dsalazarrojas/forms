# Contact Moment Observation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Moment Observation Log Form is designed to record observations of customer interactions, specifically for quality coaching purposes. This form will help agents identify areas for improvement and provide coaching to enhance their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form after each customer interaction, using the information collected from the observation.
2. Enter your name as the "Observer name" in the second field.
3. Enter the date of the interaction in the "Observation date" field.
4. Select the method of contact used (e.g., phone, email, chat, or in-person) from the options in the "Contact method observed" field.
5. Rate the outcome of the interaction from the options in the "Outcome of interaction" field.
6. Answer "True", "Partial", or "False" to indicate whether the agent followed procedures in the "Adherence to script" field.
7. Rate the agent's empathy from 1 to 5 in the "Empathy rating" field.
8. Rate the agent's professionalism from 1 to 5 in the "Professionalism rating" field.
9. Enter any coaching recommendations for the agent in the "Coaching recommendations" field (optional).
10. Add any additional notes about the interaction in the "Additional notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Contact Moment Observation Log --**: This is a note field to provide a brief summary of the interaction.
* **Observer name**: Please enter your name as the person recording the observation.
* **Observation date**: Enter the date of the interaction.
* **Contact method observed**: Select the method used to contact the customer (e.g., phone, email, chat, or in-person).
* **Outcome of interaction**: Rate the outcome of the interaction from the options provided.
* **Adherence to script**: Answer "True", "Partial", or "False" to indicate whether the agent followed procedures.
* **Empathy rating**: Rate the agent's empathy from 1 to 5.
* **Professionalism rating**: Rate the agent's professionalism from 1 to 5.
* **Coaching recommendations**: Enter any coaching suggestions for the agent (optional).
* **Additional notes**: Enter any other observations about the interaction (optional).
