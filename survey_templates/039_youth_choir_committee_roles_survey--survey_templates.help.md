# Youth Choir Committee Roles Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the roles and experiences of individuals who will be part of the youth choir committee. The information collected will help the committee to make informed decisions when selecting members for specific roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" page.
2. Choose your role preference (Youth or Adult) in the "Role Preferences" page.
3. If you have any relevant experience, please describe it in the "Experience" page.
4. If you have any experience related to the church, please describe it in the "Church Experience" page.
5. If you have any musical experience, please describe it in the "Musical Experience" page.
6. If you have any relevant leadership experience with youth, please describe it in the "Youth Leadership" page.
7. Lastly, if you are interested in being part of a specific committee, please provide the name of the role in the "Role Committee" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: (`contact_details`, text, optional)
    Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Role Preferences**: (`role_preferences`, select_one, required)
    Choose between being part of the "Youth" or "Adult" role within the committee.
* **Experience**: (`experience`, text, optional)
    Provide any relevant experience you have, including work, school, or other relevant activities.
* **Church Experience**: (`church_experience`, text, optional)
    If you have any experience related to the church, please describe it here.
* **Musical Experience**: (`musical_experience`, text, optional)
    If you have any musical experience, please describe it here.
* **Youth Leadership**: (`youth_leadership`, text, optional)
    If you have any experience leading youth, please describe it here.
* **Role Committee**: (`role_committee`, text, optional)
    If you are interested in being part of a specific committee, please provide the name of the role you are interested in.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses to help the committee make informed decisions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
