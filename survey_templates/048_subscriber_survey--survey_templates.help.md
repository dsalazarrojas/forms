# Subscriber Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from subscribers about their experiences with our website. It's a simple survey with multiple sections to gather information about their likes and dislikes, contact details, and rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions carefully and answer them accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Fill in your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Rate your overall experience with our website.
5. Submit your feedback when you're done.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you can provide your email and phone number. Please enter your email address as it appears on your email account, and your phone number as a series of digits. This information will help us reach out to you for follow-up questions or to provide updates about our website.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your email account. This will help us contact you with any relevant information or updates about our website.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number as a series of digits. This will help us contact you with any urgent matters or updates about our website.
* **What do you like about our website?** (`select_page_1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the options that apply to you from the list. This will help us understand what you enjoy about our website.

* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you visited our website or when you last interacted with us.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you visited our website or when you last interacted with us.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with our website from 1-6, where 1 is the lowest and 6 is the highest.
