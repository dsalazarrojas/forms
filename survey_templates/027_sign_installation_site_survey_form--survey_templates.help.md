# Sign Installation Site Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sign Installation Site Survey Form is a crucial tool for collecting and documenting information about sign installation sites. It helps ensure that all relevant details are gathered, stored, and easily accessible for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter site details, including the site address and description.
2. Provide contact information, including the contact person and contact details.
3. Add project details, images, documents, and videos related to the site (if applicable).
4. Set the site's status (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
5. Add any additional comments about the site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Site Details**
 * **site_details**: A text field for entering site address and other relevant information.
### 2. **Contact** 
* **contact**: A text field for entering contact person's name and information.
### 3. **Contact Info**
* **contact_info**: A text field for entering additional contact information (email, phone number, etc.).
### 4. **Contact Person**
* **contact_person**: A text field for entering the contact person's name and title.
### 5. **Project Details**
* **project_details**: A text field for entering project-specific details, such as project number, client, etc.
### 6. **Site Address**
* **site_address**: A text field for entering the site's physical address.
### 7. **Site Description**
* **site_description**: A text field for entering a brief description of the site.
### 8. **Site Notes**
* **site_notes**: A note field for entering additional information about the site (not required).
### 9. **Site Images**
* **site_images**: A text field for uploading site-specific images.
### 10. **Site Documents**
* **site_documents**: A text field for uploading site-specific documents.
### 11. **Site Video**
* **site_video**: A text field for uploading a video related to the site.
### 12. **Site Audio**
* **site_audio**: A text field for uploading audio related to the site.
### 13. **Date Installed**
* **site_date_installed**: A date field for entering the installation date of the site.
### 14. **Time Installed**
* **site_time_installed**: A time field for entering the installation time of the site.
### 15. **Site Status**
* **site_status**: A select field for selecting the site's status (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
### 16. **Comments**
* **site_comments**: A note field for adding any additional comments about the site (not required).
