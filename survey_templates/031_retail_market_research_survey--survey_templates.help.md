# Retail Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Market Research Survey is a form designed to collect data on customer behavior, preferences, and experiences with our retail store. This form helps us better understand our customers' needs and improve our services accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the field labeled "name".
2. Provide your email address in the field labeled "email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
4. Select the store you visited from the list in the field labeled "Store Visited".
5. Choose all the stores you looked at from the list in the field labeled "Store Looked At".
6. Select the type of purchase you made from the list in the field labeled "Purchase Made".
7. Choose your level of satisfaction from the list in the field labeled "Satisfaction".
8. Enter the location of the store you visited in the field labeled "Location".
9. Select the date you visited the store in the field labeled "Date of Visit".
10. Select the time you spent at the store in the field labeled "Time of Visit".
11. Provide any visit suggestions for the store in the field labeled "Visit Suggestions".
12. Share any comments you may have about your experience in the field labeled "Comments".
13. If applicable, enter the name of the employee who assisted you in the field labeled "Employee Name".
14. Enter the employee's phone number in the field labeled "Employee Phone".
15. Enter the employee's email in the field labeled "Employee Email".
16. Enter your customer ID in the field labeled "Customer ID".
17. Enter the store ID in the field labeled "Store ID".
18. Enter the store location in the field labeled "Store Location".
19. Select your customer type from the list in the field labeled "Customer Type".
20. Enter your age in the field labeled "Age".
21. Enter your income in the field labeled "Income".
22. Select your gender from the list in the field labeled "Gender".
23. Enter your occupation in the field labeled "Occupation".
24. Describe your relationship to the store in the field labeled "Relationship".
