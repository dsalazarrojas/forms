# support_customer_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and select all that apply to your support experience.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Fill in your contact information (Customer Name, Email, and Phone) if you want to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback Survey** (`Customer Feedback Survey`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This is the first question, where you can select all that apply to your support experience.
* **How was your support experience?** (`question_1`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience from 1-5.
* **Did our support team answer all your questions?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required: true): If you were not satisfied with the support you received, please select 'No' and provide feedback in the comments section.
* **What type of support did you receive?** (`question_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all types of support that apply to your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend our product or service?** (`question_4`, `number`, required: false): Rate your likelihood to recommend our product or service from 1-5.
* **Do you have any other comments about your support experience?** (`question_5`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please fill in your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please fill in your phone number.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other comments or feedback about our customer service, please provide them here.
* **Received Support** (`received_support`, `number`, required: false): This field is not currently being used.
