# Energy Audit Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Audit Check In Form is a tool for energy auditors to report their presence and gather information about a visit to a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out the required information about the property address.
*   Enter the date and time of your visit using the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields.
*   Provide contact information for the person you met during the visit, including their name, phone number, and email address.
*   Add any additional comments or notes about the visit in the "Comments" field.
*   Finally, specify the building area and type of the visited property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the exact address of the visited property.
*   **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visits`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of your visit using the standard time format (HH:MM).
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person you met during the visit.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person you met during the visit.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person you met during the visit.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
*   **Building Area** (`building_area`, text, required): Enter the area or section of the building that you visited.
*   **Building Type** (`building_type`, text, required): Indicate the type of building you visited (e.g., commercial, residential, etc.).

## Tips
*   Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you need to report multiple visits for the same property, you can resubmit this form multiple times.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your energy audit team for assistance.
