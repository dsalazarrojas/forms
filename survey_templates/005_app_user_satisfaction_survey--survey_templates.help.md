# App User Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The App User Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with the app. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance your overall satisfaction with the app.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Choose how you would describe yourself in relation to the app.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the app.
4. Evaluate the features you value the most and those you would like to see improved or removed.
5. Provide feedback on the app's speed and reliability.
6. Recommend whether or not you would suggest the app to others.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`name`, text, required): This is the name of the user. It is essential to provide your real name, as this will help us understand who is providing the feedback.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the email address of the user. It is used to contact you with updates and to send any additional information we need.
* **How would you describe yourself?** (`user_type`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options provided to describe your relationship with the app (e.g., Regular daily user, Weekly user, Occasional user).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the app by selecting one of the options provided.
* **How satisfied are you with our app?** (`satisfaction_score`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the app on a scale of 1-10.
* **Which features do you value the most?** (`most_valued_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all the features that you value the most from the provided options.
* **What features do you like the least?** (`least_liked_features`, text, optional): Provide feedback on features that you do not like or would like to see improved.
* **What new features would improve your experience?** (`feature_requests`, text, optional): Suggest new features that would enhance your experience with the app.
* **How would you rate the app's speed?** (`app_speed`, select_one, required): Evaluate the app's speed by selecting one of the provided options.
* **How reliable is the app?** (`reliability`, select_one, required): Rate the app's reliability by selecting one of the provided options.
* **Would you recommend this app to others?** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not you would recommend the app to others.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the app.

Note: This guide is intended to help you complete the form accurately and provide valuable feedback to the development team. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
