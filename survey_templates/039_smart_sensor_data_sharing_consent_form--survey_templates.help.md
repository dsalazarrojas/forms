# Smart Sensor Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about how you want to share your sensor data. This includes selecting the type of data to be shared, the reason for sharing, and the method of sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of data you want to share:
	* Choose from the options provided in the "Data Collection" field.
2. Choose the reason you want to share your data:
	* Select from the options provided in the "Data Sharing" field.
3. Choose the method of sharing your data:
	* Select from the options provided in the "Sharing Method" field.
4. Provide your email and phone number (optional):
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Data Collection**: Select how your sensor data was collected (e.g. at home, at work, in public areas, in vehicles, or other).
* **Data Sharing**: Choose who you want to share your data with (e.g. other departments within our organization, external partners, external vendors, government agencies, or other).
* **Sharing Reason**: Enter a brief description of why you want to share your data (optional).
* **Data Usage**: Choose how you want your data to be used (e.g. for research, business development, marketing, or other).
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are giving consent to share your data (optional).
* **Sensor Model**: Select the type of sensor model used to collect your data (e.g. Model 1, Model 2, or other).
* **User Data**: Choose what type of data you are sharing (e.g. personal data, non-personal data).
* **Sharing Method**: Select how you want to receive updates or notifications about your shared data (e.g. email, phone call, SMS, or other).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Form Footer 1-14**: These fields are not required and serve as a placeholder for future use.
