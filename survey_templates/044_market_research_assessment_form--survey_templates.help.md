# market_research_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess market research and gather information about your customers' needs and pain points. It is meant to be used to identify areas for improvement and opportunities to better serve your customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Survey Questions" page to input any questions or topics you'd like to cover in your market research.
2.  On the "Customer Pain Points" page, describe the specific problems or difficulties your customers are currently facing.
3.  In the "Market Research Assessment" page, provide your analysis and findings from the previous pages.
4.  For "Target Audiences" page, list any particular groups or demographics you'd like to focus on during your market research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (page 1, required: false)
	+ Input any specific questions or topics you'd like to cover in your market research.
* **Customer Pain Points** (page 2, required: false)
	+ Describe the specific problems or difficulties your customers are currently facing.
* **Market Research Assessment** (page 3, required: false)
	+ Summarize your analysis and findings from the previous pages.
* **Target Audiences** (page 4, required: false)
	+ List any particular groups or demographics you'd like to focus on during your market research.

## Tips

* Be specific and concrete when answering the survey questions and describing customer pain points.
* Use this form as a starting point and feel free to add or remove pages as needed.
* Remember to tailor your market research to the specific needs and goals of your organization. 

Note: This help guide is intended to be a starting point, and the form's purpose and layout can be adjusted based on your specific needs and goals.
