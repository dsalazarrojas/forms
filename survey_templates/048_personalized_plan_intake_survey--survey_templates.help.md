# Personalized Plan Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from you to create a personalized plan tailored to your needs. By providing your background and context, you will help us understand your goals and aspirations, allowing us to craft a customized plan just for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply answer the following questions in each section. You can type as much or as little as you need to provide context and information. Your answers will help us create a personalized plan to achieve your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **intro-page**: This is an introductory page where you can provide any additional context or background information about yourself.
* **section-1-label**: Please provide information about your current situation or goals that you'd like to achieve.
* **section-2-label**: Describe your aspirations or expectations for this plan.
* **section-3-label**: Share any relevant details or experiences that might be helpful in creating your personalized plan.
* **section-4-label**: Provide any additional context or information that might be useful in understanding your needs.
* **section-5-label**: Outline your goals or objectives for this plan.
* **section-6-label**: Share your expectations or requirements for this plan to be successful.
* **section-7-label**: Provide any relevant feedback or suggestions you have for the plan.
* **section-8-label**: Share any challenges or obstacles you anticipate during the plan's implementation.
* **section-9-label**: Outline any additional resources or support you might need to complete the plan.
* **section-10-label**: Provide a final review or any last-minute thoughts about the plan.
* **section-11-label**: Is there anything else you'd like to add or discuss about this plan?

## Tips
* Please be as thorough and detailed as possible in your answers to ensure we create the best possible plan for you.
* You can type as much or as little as you need to provide context and information.
* If you're unsure about what to provide in any section, feel free to skip or move on to the next one.
