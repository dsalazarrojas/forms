# Customer Feedback Survey Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers on their experience with our service or product. We use this feedback to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name.
2. Describe your experience with our service or product.
3. Share any suggestions you may have for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback Survey Approval Form** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Survey Questions** (`customer_survey_questions`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our service or product.
* **Customer Survey Comments** (`customer_survey_comments`, `note`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your experience.
* **Customer Survey Suggestions** (`customer_survey_suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* You can submit this form only once in 30 days.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
