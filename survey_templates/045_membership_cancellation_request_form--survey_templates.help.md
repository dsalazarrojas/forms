# Membership Cancellation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Cancellation Request Form is used to submit a formal request to cancel a membership. This form ensures that your cancellation request is processed in a timely and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (page 1, field 1) which is required for identity verification.
2. Enter your **Email** (page 1, field 2) which is required to verify your account.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** (page 1, field 3) which is optional but recommended for contact purposes.
4. Select your **Membership Type** (page 1, field 4) from the dropdown menu to indicate why you are cancelling.
5. Enter your **Reason for Cancellation** (page 1, field 5) which is a free-form text field for you to provide a brief explanation.
6. Enter your **Cancellation Date** (page 1, field 6) which is optional but recommended for accurate cancellation processing.
7. Select **Confirm Cancellation** (page 1, field 7) to confirm your intention to cancel.
8. Enter any additional **Comments** (page 1, field 8) regarding your cancellation.
9. Provide a **Signature** (page 1, field 9) which is optional but recommended for confirmation purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your membership documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with your membership account.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes, or leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership you hold, e.g., 'Yes' for active membership or 'No' for inactive membership.
* **Reason for Cancellation**: Provide a brief explanation for cancelling your membership. This field is free-form, so enter as much or as little information as necessary.
* **Cancellation Date**: Enter the date you would like your membership to be cancelled from. If left blank, it will be processed as of the current date.
* **Confirm Cancellation**: Select 'yes' to confirm your intention to cancel your membership.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information regarding your cancellation.
* **Signature**: Provide a signature to confirm that you are submitting this cancellation request.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that you have a valid reason for cancelling your membership before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our membership support team.
* Submission of this form will be processed in a timely manner.
