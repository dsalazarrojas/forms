# Music Influence On Youth Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect survey responses about music influence on youth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with your name, age, and any other relevant information.
2. In the Music Habits section, describe your music listening habits, such as how often you listen to music and your favorite music genres.
3. In the Music Influence section, rate how much different music styles or genres influence your life, such as how they affect your mood or behavior.
4. In the Listening Frequency section, specify how often you listen to music, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
5. In the Music Sources section, list your music sources, such as radio, YouTube, or social media.
6. In the Music Preferences section, describe your music preferences, such as your favorite artists or genres.
7. In the Liked/Disliked Music section, list your favorite and least favorite music.
8. In the Survey Complete section, confirm that you have completed the survey.
