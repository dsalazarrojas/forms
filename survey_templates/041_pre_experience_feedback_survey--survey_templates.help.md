# Pre Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Experience Feedback Survey is designed to collect valuable information from participants about their expectations, background, and communication preferences before they start a program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "What are your expectations" field (Field 1).
2. In the "About your background" field (Field 2), share a little about yourself, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
3. In the "About the program or activity" field (Field 3), tell us about the program or activity you are about to start.
4. Select your preferred method of receiving updates (Field 4).
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Field 5).
6. Enter your time zone (Field 6).
7. Select your start time (Field 7).
8. Choose your end time (Field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are your expectations** (1, text): Describe your expectations and what you are hoping to get out of the program or activity.
* **About your background** (2, text): Share a little about yourself, including any relevant experience or qualifications that might be helpful for us to know.
* **About the program or activity** (3, text): Tell us about the program or activity you are about to start.
* **How do you want to receive updates** (4, select_one): Choose how you would like to receive updates, such as by Email, SMS, or In-app notifications.
* **How would you like to be contacted** (5, select_multiple): Select the methods you would like to be contacted, such as by Phone, Email, or In-app messages.
* **Time Zone** (6, number): Enter your time zone so we can tailor the experience to your local time.
* **Start Time** (7, time): Select your start time for the program or activity.
* **End Time** (8, time): Select your end time for the program or activity.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* This form will only be filled out once before the participant starts the program or activity. 

Please note that the form will only be visible and accessible to participants who are about to start the program or activity.
