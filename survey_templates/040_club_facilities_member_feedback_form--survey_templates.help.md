# Club Facilities Member Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members on their experience with the club facilities. The information gathered will help the club to understand what areas need improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the facilities and services provided.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by filling out your member information on Page 1.
*   Answer the questions about your usage habits and satisfaction with the facilities on Page 2.
*   Rate the cleanliness, equipment maintenance, and safety of the facilities on Page 3.
*   Choose the amenities you currently use on Page 4.
*   Provide suggestions for improvement on Page 5.
*   Finally, rate your overall satisfaction and likelihood to recommend the club on Page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Member Information**
    (`Member Name`, Text, Optional)
    This is where you can enter your name so we can contact you for follow-up.
*   **Member ID Number** (`Member ID Number`, Text, Optional)
    If you have a member ID number, please enter it here. This is an optional field.
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, Email, Optional)
    Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up purposes.
*   **How long have you been a member?** (`Membership Duration`, Select One, True)
    Select one of the options to indicate how long you have been a member of the club.
*   **How often do you use the facilities?** (`Visit Frequency`, Select One, True)
    Choose how often you visit the facilities by selecting one of the options.
*   **Cleanliness of Facilities** (`Cleanliness`, Number, True)
    Rate the cleanliness of the facilities from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Equipment Maintenance and Repair** (`Maintenance`, Number, True)
    Rate the maintenance and repair of equipment from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Safety and Security** (`Safety`, Number, True)
    Rate the safety and security of the facilities from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Temperature Control and Comfort** (`Temperature Control`, Number, True)
    Rate the temperature control and comfort of the facilities from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Lighting Quality** (`Lighting`, Number, True)
    Rate the lighting quality of the facilities from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Restroom Facilities** (`Restroom Facilities`, Number, False)
    Rate the restrooms from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Amenities Currently Used** (`Amenities`, Select Multiple, False)
    Choose any of the amenities you currently use.
*   **Satisfaction with Current Amenities** (`Amenities Satisfaction`, Number, False)
    Rate your satisfaction with the current amenities from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`Improvements`, Note, False)
    Provide any suggestions or concerns you have about the facilities.
*   **Biggest Concern About Facilities** (`Biggest Concern`, Text, False)
    If you have any concerns, please enter them here.
*   **What Improvements Would You Like?** (`Desired Improvements`, Text, False)
    Suggest any improvements you would like to see.
*   **Most Wanted New Amenity or Service** (`New Amenity`, Select One, False)
    Choose one of the options for a new amenity or service you would like to see.
*   **Overall Satisfaction with Facilities** (`Overall Satisfaction`, Number, True)
    Rate your overall satisfaction with the facilities from 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
*   **Likelihood to Recommend Club** (`Likelihood to Recommend`, Number, True)
    Rate how likely you are to recommend the club from 1 (unlikely) to 5 (very likely).
*   **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`Additional Comments`, Text, False)
    Any additional comments or suggestions you have about the facilities.
