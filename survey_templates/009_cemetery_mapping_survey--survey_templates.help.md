# Cemetery Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cemetery Mapping Survey form is designed to collect standardized data on cemetery plots, monuments, and occupancy records. It helps to improve the overall quality of the cemetery's records and reduce errors during the survey process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the surveyor information section with your details, including your name and the survey date and time.
2. Move on to the plot data collection section, where you will identify the section number, row number, and plot number of the plot being surveyed.
3. In the monument details section, select the type of monument and assess its condition using the provided options.
4. For the occupancy records section, record the names on the monument and the dates of burial.
5. Finally, add any additional survey notes or observations in the additional survey notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surveyor Name** (`surveyor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the surveyor conducting the survey.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Record the date when the survey was conducted.
* **Section Number** (`section_number`, text, required): Identify the section being surveyed.
* **Row Number** (`row_number`, text, required): Specify the row within the section.
* **Plot Number** (`plot_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the individual plot.
* **Current Plot Status** (`plot_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the plot (Occupied, Reserved, Vacant, or Maintenance Needed).
* **Monument Type** (`monument_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of monument present (Upright Headstone, Flat Marker, Bench, Statue, or None).
* **Monument Condition** (`monument_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the state of the marker (Good, Needs Cleaning, Damaged, or Fallen / Leaning).
* **Enclosure Type** (`enclosure_type`, select_one, required): Determine if there are any surrounding boundaries (None, Curbing, Fencing, or Hedge).
* **Names on Monument** (`names_on_monument`, text, optional): Record the names as they appear on the stone.
* **Earliest Burial Date Found** (`earliest_burial_date`, date, optional): Record the earliest recorded burial date.
* **Latest Burial Date Found** (`latest_burial_date`, date, optional): Record the latest recorded burial date.
* **Survey Observations** (`general_observations`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply (Overgrown, Sinking, Readable Text, Unreadable Text, or Possible Mapping Error).
* **General Comments or Findings** (`general_survey_comments`, text, optional): Add any other information or notes of note.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form maintainer at form@gic.mx.
