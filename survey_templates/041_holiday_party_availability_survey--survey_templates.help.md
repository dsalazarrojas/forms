# Holiday Party Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your availability and preferences for a holiday party. Please answer the questions to help us plan the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time of the holiday gathering (page 2). 
2. Select your preferred start and end time (page 3).
3. Choose how much time you are available to dedicate to the party (page 4).
4. Select your preferred time and date options for attending the party (pages 5-8).
5. Specify any constraints you may have for attending the party, such as availability or time constraints (pages 9-12).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about your constraints (pages 13-15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Party Availability Survey** (page 1): Enter a brief description of your party (optional).
* **Date of Holiday Gathering** (page 2): Select the date of the holiday gathering.
* **Start Time** (page 3): Choose the start time of the party.
* **End Time** (page 4): Choose the end time of the party.
* **Duration** (page 5): Choose how much time you are available to dedicate to the party.
* **Preferred Time** (page 6): Select your preferred time for attending the party.
* **Preferred Duration** (page 7): Select your preferred duration for attending the party.
* **Will Attend** (page 8): Select whether you will attend the party and any constraints you have.
* **Constraints** (page 9): Select any constraints you have for attending the party, such as availability or time constraints.
* **Availability** (page 10): Select your availability for the party.
* **Constraints Date** (page 11): If you have any date constraints, select them here.
* **Constraints Time** (page 12): If you have any time constraints, select them here.
* **Will Attend Constraints** (page 13): Select whether you will attend the party with any constraints.
* **Will Attend Constraints Date** (page 14): If you have any date constraints for attending the party, select them here.
* **Will Attend Constraints Time** (page 15): If you have any time constraints for attending the party, select them here.
* **Constraints Duration** (page 16): Select any duration constraints you have for attending the party.
* **Constraints Note** (page 17): Enter any additional notes about your constraints.
* **Constraints Note Date** (page 18): Enter any additional notes about your date constraints.
* **Constraints Note Time** (page 19): Enter any additional notes about your time constraints.
* **Constraints Note Duration** (page 20): Enter any additional notes about your duration constraints.
