# Bar Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their expectations and preferences for a safe and comfortable reopening of the bar. Your input will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for you and our staff.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
- You can select multiple options for questions that allow it.
- For text fields, please provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have any additional concerns or comments, use the "Additional Concerns or Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Date
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date you took the survey. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you about the reopening.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you about the reopening.
* **How Often Did You Visit Before Closure?** (`customer_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select how often you visited the bar before its closure.
* **Will You Visit When We Reopen?** (`visit_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Please select how likely you are to visit the bar when it reopens.
* **When Would You Feel Comfortable Visiting?** (`reopening_timing`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your comfort level with visiting.
* **Confidence in Safety Measures** (`safety_confidence`, `select_one`, required): Please select how confident you feel in our health and safety protocols.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all concerns you have regarding safety measures.
* **Most Important Safety Measures** (`safety_measures_important`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your top three priorities for safety measures.
* **Vaccination Requirement** (`vaccination_requirement`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your vaccination status.
* **Mask Policy** (`mask_policy`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your preferences.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your seating preferences.
* **How Important is Outdoor Seating?** (`outdoor_seating_important`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your preference.
* **Would You Accept Higher Prices Due to New Protocols?** (`pricing_tolerance`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your price tolerance.
* **Interest in Reopening Special Event** (`special_events_interest`, `select_one`, required): This field is not marked as required but it will give us an idea of your interest.
* **Feedback on Service Changes** (`service_changes_feedback`, `text`, required): Please provide your feedback on any changes we made to our services.
* **Suggestions for Improvements** (`improvements_suggestions`, `text`, required): Please provide any suggestions you have for improvements.
* **Additional Concerns or Comments** (`additional_concerns`, `text`, required): Please provide any other concerns or comments you may have.
* **May We Contact You About Reopening?** (`contact_permission`, `select_one`, required): Please select if you are willing to be contacted about the reopening.

Please note that some of the fields marked as "required" are technically optional but are necessary for us to understand your opinions better.
