# Internet Connectivity Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your internet connectivity experience. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your needs and improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Next" to proceed to the next page.
2. Answer each question according to your experience with your internet service provider.
3. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it (e.g. multiple devices used).
4. If a question has a "number" option type, you can type a numerical value (e.g. speed in Mbps).
5. Submit your completed form.
