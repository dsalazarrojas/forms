# Learning Management System Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and ratings from users about their experiences with the Learning Management System (LMS). This information will help the LMS team improve the system to better meet the needs of its users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully to understand what information is being requested.
* Answer each question to the best of your ability and truthfulness.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed (if a field is marked as "required", it is necessary for the submission to be valid).
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it to make sure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (user_email, email, required: false): A unique identifier for the user, usually in the format of an email address.
* **Name** (user_name, text, required: false): A field to record the user's name.
* **Rating** (user_rating, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the LMS on a scale from 1-5 or 0-10, as per your preference.
* **Feedback** (user_feedback, text, required: false): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the LMS.
* **Learning Platform Used** (learning_platform_used, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the platforms you use for learning from the options provided.
* **Rating Scale** (rating_scale, number, required: false): Select a rating scale that aligns with your rating.
* **Comments** (comments, note, required: false): Additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about the LMS.
* **User Phone** (user_phone, text, required: false): A field to record the user's phone number, if desired. 

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this information will help the LMS team improve the system.
* If you have any issues or concerns with the LMS, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Feedback" field as well.
* You may choose to submit this form more than once, but please avoid submitting the same information multiple times.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please contact the LMS support team.
