# Gym Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and preferences from gym users, helping the gym to understand their overall experience, social media preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Choose your preferred social media platform (if you have one).
3. Enter your comment about the gym.
4. Select the date and time of your last visit.
5. Choose how often you visit the gym (if you want to share this information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required): This is the main section where you can write about your overall experience at the gym. Please provide a brief but thorough description of your experience.

### 2. Social Media Preference
* **Social Media Preference** (`social_media_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose which social media platform you prefer to leave feedback on. You can select one of the options: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or YouTube.

### 3. Comment Preference
* **Comment Preference** (`comment_preference`, text, required): This is where you can enter your comments about the gym. Please be as detailed as possible and provide constructive feedback.

### 4. Leave Comment
* **Leave Comment** (`comment`, text, required): This is similar to the "Comment Preference" field, but it's a separate field for you to enter your comments about the gym. Please use this field if you want to provide more detailed comments.

### 5. Social Media Choice
* **Social Media Choice** (`social_media_choice`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which social media platforms you have. You can select one or more of the options: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, or YouTube.

### 6. Decision Factor
* **Decision Factor** (`decision_factor`, select_one, optional): Choose why you would stay with this gym or leave it. You can select one of the options: Stay with this gym, Leave this gym, or No preference.

### 7. Comment Count
* **How many times have you been to this gym?** (`comment_count`, number, required): Enter the number of times you have visited the gym.

### 8. Last Visit Date
* **Last Visit Date** (`last_visit_date`, date, required): Select the date of your last visit to the gym.

### 9. Last Visit Time
* **Last Visit Time** (`last_visit_time`, time, required): Select the time of your last visit to the gym.

### 10. Frequency of Visit
* **How often do you visit this gym?** (`frequency_visit`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you visit the gym. You can select one or more of the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
