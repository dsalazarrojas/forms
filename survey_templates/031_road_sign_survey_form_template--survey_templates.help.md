# Road Sign Survey Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about road signs. Please fill out the form accurately and completely for each road sign.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "condition" field with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate the current condition of the road sign.
2. In the "visibility" field, select all applicable options to indicate the visibility of the road sign.
3. In the "compliance" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the road sign complies with regulations.
4. Enter a number in the "position" field to indicate the current position of the road sign.
5. Enter a number in the "size" field to indicate the current size of the road sign.
6. In the "color" field, select all applicable options to indicate the color of the road sign.
7. In the "material" field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the material used for the road sign.
8. Use the "note" fields to add any additional notes or comments about the road sign.
9. If necessary, enter your email address and phone number in the "email" and "phone" fields.
10. Enter the name of the person assigned to the road sign in the "assigned_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **condition**: Select "Yes" if the road sign is in good condition. Select "No" otherwise.
* **visibility**: Select all applicable options to indicate how visible the road sign is.
* **compliance**: Select "Yes" if the road sign complies with regulations. Select "No" otherwise.
* **position**: Enter the current position of the road sign.
* **size**: Enter the current size of the road sign.
* **color**: Select all applicable options to indicate the color of the road sign.
* **material**: Select "Yes" if the road sign is made of a specific material. Select "No" otherwise.
* **note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the road sign.
* **email**: Enter your email address if you need to contact you for further information.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number if you need to be contacted for further information.
* **assigned_to**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the road sign.
* **note_2** to **note_14**: Add any additional notes or comments about the road sign.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
