# Viral Video Creators Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to help us understand the demographics and behaviors of viral video creators. Your responses will help us better understand the types of creators we're targeting, their monetization strategies, and how we can best support their growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Select your response from the provided options for each question.
3. Fill in any text fields, such as "Influencer" or "55 or older".
4. Ensure you've answered all questions before proceeding to the next section.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of video creator are you?** (`section_1`): Choose one of the four options to describe your type of video content creation (Influencer, Content Creators, Vloggers, or Other).
* **Which of the following best describes you?** (`form_control_1`): Select one of the five options to indicate your experience level in creating videos (Yes, I've been making videos for 1 year or less, Yes, I've been making videos for 1-2 years, Yes, I've been making videos for 2-5 years, Yes, I've been making videos for 5 years or more, or I've done a little bit of video creation, but not for report).
* **What type of video do you usually create?** (`form_control_2`): Select multiple options that apply to your video content (Vlogging, Gaming, Educational, Entertainment, or Other).
* **How do you typically monetize your videos?** (`section_2`): Choose one or more of the three options to describe how you earn money from your content (Sponsorships, Merchandise, or Affiliate marketing).
* **What do you usually monetize your videos through?** (`section_2_sub_1` to `section_2_sub_3`): Choose one or more of the three options to describe the specific channels you use for monetization (Brands, Advertisements, Affiliate marketing, T-shirts, Hats, Posters, or Other).
* **How do you currently use social media?** (`section_3`): Select multiple options that apply to your social media usage (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, or Other).
* **How many followers do you have on social media?** (`section_4`): Choose one of the five options to describe your social media follower count (Less than 1000, 1000-10k, 10k-50k, 50k-100k, or 100k+).
* **How much money do you make from your videos?** (`section_5`): Select one of the five options to describe your current income from video content (Less than $100, $100-1000, $1000-10000, $10000-50000, or $50000+).
* **What is your age range?** (`section_6`): Select one option to describe your age group (18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, or 55 or older).
* **How did you hear about our quiz?** (`form_control_2`): Select multiple options that apply to how you discovered this quiz (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google, or Other).
* **How did you find out about this quiz?** (`form_control_3`): Choose one of the three options to describe how you learned about this quiz (Friends, Social Media, or Online Advertising).
* **Would you like to get a free 1-hour consultation on growing your YouTube channel?** (`form_control_4` and `form_control_5`): Select one option to express your interest in receiving the consultation (Yes or No).

Please note that all questions are optional, and you can skip any questions that don't apply to you. Answering this form will help us better understand your needs and provide you with more relevant support.
