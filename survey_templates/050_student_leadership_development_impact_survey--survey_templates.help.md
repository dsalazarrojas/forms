# Student Leadership Development Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about the impact of leadership development programs on students in your school. Your input will help us understand the effectiveness of these programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your school program from the list of options.
2. Enter the year you are currently in.
3. Enter your name and other relevant information.
4. Indicate if you participated in the school survey.
5. If you have any other information to share, please use the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Programs**: Select your school's leadership development program (e.g., High School, Junior College, etc.).
* **Survey Year**: Enter the current academic year you are in (e.g., 2022-23).
* **Student Name**: Enter your name.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are participating in (e.g., Leadership Development Program).
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **School Survey Date**: Enter the date of the school's survey (if applicable).
* **EMail**: Enter your email address (if you are comfortable sharing it).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you are comfortable sharing it).
* **Program Coordinator**: Enter the name of the program coordinator (if applicable).
* **School Admin**: Enter the name of the school administrator (if applicable).
* **Other Info**: If you have any other information to share about your leadership development experience, please use this field.
* **School Survey**: Indicate if you participated in the school survey (Yes/No).
