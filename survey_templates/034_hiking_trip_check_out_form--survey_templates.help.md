# Hiking Trip Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a hiker's trip, including their name, contact information, and trip details. It helps park staff and administrators track hiker activity, ensure safety, and make informed decisions for future planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the "name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "email_address" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
* Indicate if you used any special gear during your trip in the "gear_used" field.
* Choose whether you completed the trip from the options in the "trip_details" field.
* Enter the date you completed the trip in the "check_out_date" field.
* Select the time of day when you completed the trip in the "check_out_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the hiker database.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **trip_details** (`trip_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from "Yes" to indicate that the trip details are complete, or "No" to indicate they are not.
* **gear_used** (`gear_used`, select_one, required/optional): Choose "Yes" if you used any special gear during the trip, or "No" otherwise.
* **check_out_date** (`check_out_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the trip.
* **check_out_time** (`check_out_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of day when you completed the trip.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your data is up-to-date and reliable.
* If you have any trouble filling out the form, please don't hesitate to contact park staff for assistance.
* If you have any suggestions or comments about the form, please provide them to help us improve the hiker database.
