# Cybersecurity Threat Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Threat Report Form is a tool used to collect information about potential or actual threats to your organization's cybersecurity. This form helps gather essential details about the nature and frequency of threats, their impact, and the methods used to resolve them. It also captures information on how threats are reported and resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have access to all necessary information.
2. Select the appropriate threat type(s) from the dropdown menu, or answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if a threat exists.
3. Choose the severity level of the threat from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the frequency of the threat from the dropdown menu.
5. Provide a brief description of the threat.
6. Describe the impact of the threat.
7. Select the category of the threat.
8. Enter the date the report was submitted.
9. Select the status of the threat.
10. Select the status update of the threat.
11. Enter additional notes or comments about the threat.
12. Enter any suggestions for resolving the threat.
13. Enter the date the threat was resolved.
14. Describe the method used to resolve the threat.
15. Enter the email of the person who reported the threat.
16. Enter the name of the person who reported the threat.
17. Enter the phone number of the person who reported the threat.
18. Enter a unique threat ID.
19. Enter a threat ID number.
20. Enter the date the threat was reported.
21. Enter the time the threat was reported.
22. Enter the time zone where the threat was reported.
23. Enter the location where the threat was reported.
24. Enter the source of the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **threat_type** (`threat_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of threat, or answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if a threat exists.
* **threat_severity** (`threat_severity`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the severity level of the threat.
* **threat_frequency** (`threat_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of the threat.
* **threat_description** (`threat_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the threat.
* **threat_impact** (`threat_impact`, `text`, required: false): Describe the impact of the threat.
* **threat_category** (`threat_category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the category of the threat.
* **reported_by** (`reported_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email of the person who reported the threat.
* **report_date** (`report_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **threat_status** (`threat_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the threat.
* **threat_status_update** (`threat_status_update`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status update of the threat.
* **threat_resolution** (`threat_resolution`, `text`, required: false): Enter additional notes or comments about the threat.
* **threat_suggestions** (`threat_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Enter any suggestions for resolving the threat.
* **threat_resolution_date** (`threat_resolution_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the threat was resolved.
* **threat_resolution_method** (`threat_resolution_method`, `text`, required: false): Describe the method used to resolve the threat.
* **reported_by_email** (`reported_by_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the person who reported the threat.
* **report_by_name** (`report_by_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who reported the threat.
* **report_by_phone** (`report_by_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who reported the threat.
* **threat_id** (`threat_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique threat ID.
* **threat_id_number** (`threat_id_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter a threat ID number.
* **threat_reported_date** (`threat_reported_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the threat was reported.
* **threat_reported_time** (`threat_reported_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the threat was reported.
* **threat_reported_time_zone** (`threat_reported_time_zone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the time zone where the threat was reported.
* **threat_reported_location** (`threat_reported_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the threat was reported.
* **report_source** (`report_source`, `text`, required: false): Enter the source of the report.

This guide will help users complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.
