The following is a user-facing help guide for the "Store Management Practice Feedback Poll" form.

# Store Management Practice Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about the store management practice. The form is designed to collect general feedback, management satisfaction, management support, clarity of store management, effectiveness of store management, compliance of store management, and fairness of store management. This feedback will help in understanding the areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide honest and sincere feedback about your experience with the store management practice.
3. Make sure to complete all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Feedback
* **General Feedback** (`general_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any general feedback about the store management practice.

* **Management Satisfaction** (`management_satisfaction`, text, required: false): This field is for rating your satisfaction with the store management practice.

* **Management Support** (`management_support`, text, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on the level of support provided by the store management team.

* **Clarity of Store Management** (`management_clarity`, text, required: false): This field is for assessing how clear the store management practice is.

* **Effectiveness of Store Management** (`management_effectiveness`, text, required: false): This field is for evaluating the effectiveness of the store management practice.

* **Compliance of Store Management** (`management_compliance`, text, required: false): This field is for assessing the compliance of the store management practice.

* **Fairness of Store Management** (`management_fairness`, text, required: false): This field is for evaluating the fairness of the store management practice.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- Complete all the required fields (if any).
- Take care to provide clear and concise answers.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them.
