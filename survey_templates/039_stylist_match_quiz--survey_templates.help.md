# Stylist Match Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stylist Match Quiz is a tool to help you find the best stylist for your needs. Please answer a few questions to get personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Client Preferences" page to provide information about your style preferences.
2. In the "List of Stylists" section, select all stylists that match your preferences.
3. On the "Match Stylist" page, choose one stylist that best fits your style.
4. In the "Stylist Details" section, enter the name and picture of your chosen stylist (if available).
5. Finally, provide a brief bio of the stylist you have chosen.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, `note`, required: false): Please describe your personal style preferences, such as hair type, color, and texture. This information will help us narrow down the best stylists for you.
- **List of Stylists** (`styst_list`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all stylists that match your preferences from the list. You can choose multiple stylists if you have varying preferences.
- **Match Stylist** (`match_stylist`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one stylist that best fits your style from the selected list.
- **Stylist Name** (`styal_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the stylist you have chosen.
- **Stylist Picture** (`styal_picture`, `text`, required: false): Add a picture of your chosen stylist (if available).
- **Stylist Bio** (`styal_bio`, `text`, required: false): Write a brief bio of the stylist you have chosen.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing your style preferences.
- You can select multiple stylists if you have varying preferences.
- If you're unsure about a stylist's name or picture, you can leave it blank.
- Our system will provide personalized recommendations based on your selections.
