# Embossed Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our embossed products. Your feedback will help us to understand customer satisfaction and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief title for the product that you are providing feedback on.
2. **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the product that you are providing feedback on.
3. **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of product you are providing feedback on, either "Embossed" or "Non-Embossed".
4. **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, not required): Rate your experience with the product on a scale of 1-10.
5. **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, not required): Enter the price of the product.
6. **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the product that you are providing feedback on.
7. **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
8. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
9. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Provide any additional feedback you may have on your experience with the product.
10. **Submitted Time** (`submitted`, time, not required): This field is automatically populated with the current time when you submit the form.
