# Childcare Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Childcare Exit Survey is a tool used to collect information from parents or guardians about their experience with our program. It helps us understand your reasons for leaving, satisfaction levels, and areas of improvement. The collected data will be used to enhance our services and ensure we provide the best possible care for children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the provided fields with your information.
2. Select the answers for the rating questions (e.g. "Overall Satisfaction", "Quality of Care", etc.) from the corresponding dropdown menus.
3. Type your responses for the text fields (e.g. "Reason for Leaving", "Best Aspect of Program", etc.).
4. Click the "Submit" button to finish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date** (1, `survey_date`, required): The date you are submitting this survey.
* **Parent/Guardian First Name** (2, `parent_guardian_first_name`, required): Legal first name of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
* **Parent/Guardian Last Name** (3, `parent_guardian_last_name`, required): Legal last name of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (4, `parent_guardian_email`, required): Primary email address for contact.
* **Child First Name** (5, `child_first_name`, required): Legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (6, `child_last_name`, required): Legal last name of the child.
* **Child Age** (7, `child_age`, required): Current age of the child in years.
* **Enrollment Start Date** (8, `enrollment_start_date`, required): Date when the child started at this program.
* **Enrollment End Date** (9, `enrollment_end_date`, required): Date when the child's last day was.
* **Reason for Leaving** (10, `reason_for_leaving`, required): Select the main reason for leaving.
* **Reason for Leaving Details** (11, `reason_for_leaving_details`, required): Provide more information about the reason for leaving.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (12, `overall_satisfaction`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction level (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Quality of Care** (13, `quality_of_care`, required): Rate the quality of care provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Staff Professionalism** (14, `staff_professionalism`, required): Rate the staff professionalism (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Staff Caring and Nurturing** (15, `staff_caring`, required): Rate how caring the staff was (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Communication Quality** (16, `communication_quality`, required): Rate the communication with staff (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Facility Cleanliness** (17, `facility_cleanliness`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the facility (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Safety Measures** (18, `safety_measures`, required): Rate the safety measures in place (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Curriculum Quality** (19, `curriculum_quality`, required): Rate the curriculum and activities (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Value for Cost** (20, `value_for_cost`, required): Rate the value for the cost (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value).
* **Child Development Progress** (21, `child_development_progress`, required): Rate the observed child development progress (Significant Progress, Moderate Progress, Some Progress, Minimal Progress, No Progress Observed).
* **Child Happiness** (22, `child_happiness`, required): Rate the happiness level of the child (Very Happy, Happy, Neutral, Unhappy, Very Unhappy).
* **Would Recommend** (23, `would_recommend`, required): Would you recommend this program to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Neutral, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Best Aspect of Program** (24, `best_aspect`, required): What was the best aspect of this program.
* **Areas for Improvement** (25, `areas_for_improvement`, required): What could be improved about this program.
* **Additional Comments** (26, `additional_comments`, not required): Any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
