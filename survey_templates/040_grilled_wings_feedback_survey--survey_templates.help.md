# Grilled Wings Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grilled Wings Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on our grilled wings. Your feedback will help us to understand what we're doing well and what we need to improve on to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Tell us how long it took to receive your order in the "How long did it take to receive your order?" field.
3. Rate the taste, presentation, service, and price of your order using the corresponding fields.
4. Choose whether or not you would recommend us based on your experience.
5. Rate the quality of the food and beverages you received.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (text, required): Please enter your name.
* **How long did it take to receive your order?** (time, required): Enter the time it took for you to receive your order.
* **How would you rate the taste?** (number, required): Rate the taste from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How would you rate the presentation?** (number, required): Rate the presentation from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How would you rate the service?** (number, required): Rate the service from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How would you rate the price?** (number, required): Rate the price from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **What would you recommend to us?** (select multiple, required): Choose whether or not you would recommend us to others.
* **How would you rate the quality of the food?** (select multiple, required): Rate the quality of the food from Good, Bad, or Not Applicable.
* **Food Quality 2** (select multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the food from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Not Applicable.
* **How would you rate the quality of the beverage?** (select multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the beverage from Good, Bad, or Not Applicable.
* **Additional comments?** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Rating Beverage 2** (select multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the beverage from Good, Fair, or Not Applicable.
* **Rating Beverage 3** (select multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the beverage from Good, Fair, or Not Applicable.
* **Rating Beverage 4** (select multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the beverage from Good, Fair, or Not Applicable.
* **Rating Beverage 5** (select multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the beverage from Good, Fair, or Not Applicable.
