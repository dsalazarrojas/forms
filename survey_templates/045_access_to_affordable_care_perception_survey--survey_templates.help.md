# Access To Affordable Care Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information on participants' perceptions and experiences with accessing affordable care in their community. The goal is to identify areas for improvement and provide insights on how local organizations can better serve their community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your abilities.
2. Select the correct answer options for each question.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Please enter your 5-digit postal code.
* **Your Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the options provided:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-44
	+ 45-64
	+ 65+
* **What is your current health insurance status?** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Private Insurance
	+ Medicare or Medicaid
	+ Uninsured
	+ Other
* **How would you rate the affordability of healthcare in your community?** (`affordability_rating`, select_one, required):
	+ Very Affordable
	+ Affordable
	+ Neutral
	+ Unaffordable
	+ Very Unaffordable
* **In the past year - have you skipped medical care due to cost?** (`cost_as_barrier`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - Multiple times
	+ Yes - Once
	+ No
* **Which of these costs are most difficult for you?** (`financial_barriers`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Co-pays
	+ Prescription Drugs
	+ Monthly Premiums
	+ Deductibles
	+ Emergency Care
* **How long does it take to travel to your primary doctor?** (`travel_time`, select_one, required):
	+ Less than 15 mins
	+ 15-30 mins
	+ 30-60 mins
	+ Over 1 hour
* **How easy is it to get an appointment when you are sick?** (`wait_time_for_appt`, select_one, required):
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **What are the biggest barriers to care in your area?** (`community_barriers`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Lack of Transportation
	+ Language Barriers
	+ Limited Clinic Hours
	+ No Specialists Nearby
	+ High Costs
* **What healthcare services are missing in your community?** (`needed_services`, text, optional):
	+ Please describe any services you feel are lacking in your community.
* **How can local organizations improve access to affordable care?** (`suggestions_for_access`, text, optional):
	+ Please share any suggestions you have.
* **Would you like to receive information about local low-cost clinics?** (`follow_up_interest`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes
	+ No

Please note that this form should be filled out by one person at a time. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
