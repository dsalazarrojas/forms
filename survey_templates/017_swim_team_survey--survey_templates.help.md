# Swim Team Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect information from swimmers and their coaches about their swim team experience. We want to understand your thoughts and achievements in the team, so please take a few minutes to provide us with your feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you're providing accurate information.
2. Answer every question to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for guidance from your coach or team manager.
4. Please make sure to fill out this form only once per swim meet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete ID** (`athlete_id`, Number, Optional): Enter your unique athlete ID assigned by the team.
* **Swimmer Name** (`swimmer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as a swimmer.
* **Swimmer ID** (`swimmer_id`, Text, Optional): Enter your swimmer ID if you have one.
* **Swimmer Email** (`swimmer_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Swimmer Address** (`swimmer_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your home address, if you want to be contacted via mail.
* **Swimmer Phone** (`swimmer_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for any emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`swimmer_birthdate`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your swim team or school.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your swim coach.
* **Swim Type** (`swim_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of swim you're participating in (butterfly, individual, medley).
* **Swim Distance** (`swim_distance`, Select One, Optional): Select the distance you're swimming (200m, 400m, 600m, 800m, 1000m, 1600m, 1800m).
* **Swim Stroke** (`swim_stroke`, Select One, Optional): Select the stroke you're using (front crawl, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, freestyle).
* **Swim Event** (`swim_event`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the event you're participating in (100m, 200m, 400m, 800m, 1500m).
* **Swim Meet** (`swim_meet`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of swim meet you're participating in (qualifying, invitational, championship).
* **Swim Meet Date** (`swim_meet_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the swim meet.
* **Swim Meet Location** (`swim_meet_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the swim meet.
* **Swim Meet Time** (`swim_meet_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the swim meet.
* **Swim Meet Details** (`swim_meet_details`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of swim meet (warmup, heat, final).
* **Swim Meet Result** (`swim_meet_result`, Select One, Optional): Select the result of the swim meet (won, lost).
* **Swim Meet Notes** (`swim_meet_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the swim meet.
* **Swim Meet Score** (`swim_meet_score`, Number, Optional): Enter your score in the swim meet.
* **Swim Meet Rank** (`swim_meet_rank`, Select One, Optional): Select your rank in the swim meet (1st, 2nd, 3rd).
* **Swim Meet Medal** (`swim_meet_medal`, Select One, Optional): Select the medal you received in the swim meet (gold, silver, bronze).
* **Swim Meet Ribbon** (`swim_meet_ribbon`, Select One, Optional): Select whether you received a ribbon in the swim meet (ribbon, no ribbon).
