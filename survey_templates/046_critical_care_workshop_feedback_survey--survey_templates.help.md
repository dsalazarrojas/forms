# Critical Care Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from workshop participants to help improve future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form by following these steps:
1. Review each page of the form, and select the correct answer that best matches your feedback on the workshop.
2. Be as detailed and honest as possible in your answers, as they are anonymous and will be treated confidentially.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the free-text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the workshop**: This field is a free-text area where you can provide a brief description of your overall experience of the workshop.
* **Overall rating**: Choose an answer that best reflects your overall rating of the workshop (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Workshop content**: Provide any feedback about the quality, relevance, and effectiveness of the workshop materials and content.
* **Quality of instructor**: Select one or more answers that best describe the quality of instruction and guidance provided by the workshop instructor (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Workshop organizers**: Provide any feedback or comments about the organizers of the workshop.
* **Feedback comments**: This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback you have about the workshop.
* **Email**: If you want to be contacted for any follow-up discussion or for being entered into a draw, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Additional comments**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have about the workshop.
