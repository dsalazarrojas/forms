# Art Exhibition Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from visitors on their experience at an art exhibition. This feedback helps us understand what we do well and what we can improve on to provide a better experience for future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2.  Choose the date you visited the exhibition.
3.  Enter the name of the exhibition you visited.
4.  Answer the questions on overall satisfaction, quality of artwork displayed, quality of exhibition curation, and other aspects of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you visited the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the exhibition you visited.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Exhibition** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the exhibition.
* **Quality of Artwork Displayed** (`exhibition_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the artwork displayed.
* **Quality of Exhibition Curation** (`exhibition_curation`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the exhibition curation.
* **Venue Quality** (`venue_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the venue.
* **Layout and Flow of Exhibition** (`layout_flow`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ease of navigation of the exhibition.
* **Clarity of Signage and Information** (`signage_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of signage and information.
* **Usefulness of Artwork Descriptions** (`artwork_descriptions`, `select_one`, required): Rate the usefulness of artwork descriptions.
* **Understanding of Exhibition Theme** (`theme_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding of the exhibition theme.
* **Friendliness of Staff** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the friendliness of the staff if you interacted with them. (Note: this field is optional)
* **Knowledge of Staff** (`staff_knowledge`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the knowledge of the staff about the artwork and exhibition if you interacted with them. (Note: this field is optional)
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select areas you think we could improve for future exhibitions.
* **Suggestions for Future Exhibitions** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Suggest improvements or ideas you have for future exhibitions.
* **Would You Recommend This Exhibition** (`recommend`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would recommend this exhibition to others.
* **Likelihood of Returning for Future Exhibitions** (`return_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how likely you are to return for future exhibitions.
