# Ancient Gaul Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge of Ancient Gaul history, culture, and geography. It consists of multiple sections, each focused on a different aspect of Gaulish civilization. The quiz aims to assess your understanding of key figures, battles, languages, and events that shaped Gaul during the Roman conquest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Ancient Gaul Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading each question carefully and selecting the correct answer from the provided options.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions, which are marked as "true" in the options list.
3. For questions with "Select all that apply" options, choose all the relevant answers.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form, as there is no option to save and resume later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, required):** Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant in the quiz.
* **Tribes and Regions (`section_tribes_regions`, note, not required):** This section provides a brief introduction to the quiz, highlighting the importance of understanding the tribes and regions of Ancient Gaul.
* **Who was the major tribal leader who united Gaul against Julius Caesar? (`major_tribal_leader_against_caesar`, select_one, required):** Choose the correct name from the options provided, such as Vercingetorix.
* **Ancient Gaul was primarily located in which modern-day country? (`primary_region_of_gaul`, select_one, required):** Select the correct country from the options provided, for example, Modern-day France.
* **Which of these were types of Gaulish fortifications? (`gaulish_fortification_types`, select_multiple, required):** Choose all the correct types of fortifications from the options, such as Oppidum.
* **To which language family did the Gauls belong? (`main_language_family`, select_one, required):** Select the correct language family from the options provided, for example, Celtic.
* **Which decisive battle effectively ended Gaulish independence? (`battle_that_ended_gaulish_independence`, select_one, required):** Choose the correct battle from the options provided, such as Alesia.
* **In what year did Caesar begin the Gallic Wars (BC)? (`year_caesar_began_gallic_wars_bc`, number, required):** Enter the correct year in numerical format.
* **Describe one major impact of Romanization on Gaul. (`impact_of_romanization_on_gaul`, text, required):** Type a brief description of one significant effect of Romanization on Gaul.
* **Are you interested in taking more ancient history quizzes? (`interested_in_more_history_quizzes`, select_one, not required):** Indicate your interest in taking more history quizzes.
