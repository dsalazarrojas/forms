# Professional Board Election Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Board Election Voting Form is used to gauge the opinions of members or employees on key decisions of a professional board. It is a crucial tool for organizations to understand the views of their constituents and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Header**: This is the header section of the form, which provides a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
2. **Candidate List**: Select all the candidates that you agree with the action or decision.
3. **Vote For**: Choose the specific candidate you want to vote for.
4. **Candidate Details**: Provide details about the candidate you have selected.
5. **Vote Confirmed**: Confirm that you have voted or confirm that you have not voted.
6. **Vote Confirmed By**: Confirm who has confirmed your vote.
7. **Vote Confirmed Date**: Confirm the date of your vote.
8. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the questions, click the submit button to submit your vote.
