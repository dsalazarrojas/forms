# AI Agent Chat Interaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have interacted with our AI chat agents. Your responses will help us improve the performance and usability of our chat agents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address to help us contact you if necessary.
2. Choose how often you use our AI chat agents (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or First time).
3. Select the date and time you interacted with our chat agent.
4. Describe what you wanted to accomplish during this interaction.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the chat agent's performance (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
6. Choose the rating (1-10) for the overall quality of the interaction.
7. Select all the features you found valuable (Easy to use, Fast responses, Helpful suggestions, Accurate information, Clear explanations, Friendly tone).
8. Select all the areas where you think the chat agent could improve (Relevance of responses, Response speed, Accuracy of facts, Clarity of explanation, Handling complex requests, Tone and style).
9. Decide whether you would recommend our AI chat agent to others (Definitely, Probably, Not sure, Probably not, or False).
10. Agree or disagree with being contacted for follow-up on your feedback.
10. If you have any additional comments or examples, please provide them in the text box below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Organization (optional)**: If you're representing a company or team, enter their name.
* **How often do you use AI chat agents?**: Select the option that best matches your typical usage frequency.
* **Date of this interaction**: Enter the date you interacted with our chat agent.
* **Time of interaction**: Enter the local time of the interaction (optional).
* **What did you want to accomplish in this chat?**: Describe what you wanted to achieve during this interaction.
* **How satisfied are you with the AI chat agent?**: Rate your satisfaction with the interaction (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
* **Overall rating (1-10)**: Rate the quality of the interaction (1-10).
* **Best aspects of the interaction**: Select all the features you found valuable (Easy to use, Fast responses, Helpful suggestions, Accurate information, Clear explanations, Friendly tone).
* **Areas to improve**: Select all the features that need improvement (Relevance of responses, Response speed, Accuracy of facts, Clarity of explanation, Handling complex requests, Tone and style).
* **Would you recommend this AI chat agent to others?**: Decide whether you would recommend our chat agent to others.
* **May we contact you to follow up about your feedback?**: Agree or disagree with being contacted for follow-up.
* **Additional comments or examples**: Provide any additional comments or examples about your interaction with our chat agent.
