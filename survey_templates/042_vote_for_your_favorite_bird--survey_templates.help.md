# Vote For Your Favorite Bird - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vote For Your Favorite Bird" form is a survey designed to gather information about users' favorite bird species. This form is used to gather data that will help us understand user preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button when you have filled out all the required fields.
2. Select your favorite bird species from the list provided.
3. Fill out your first and last name.
4. Enter your email address (if you provide it).
5. Choose your preferred contact method from the list provided.
6. If you have a different contact preference, enter it in the "Other" field.
7. Select your time of day.
8. Enter your date of birth (if you choose to provide it).
9. Enter your age.
10. If your favorite bird is not on the list, enter it in the "Favorite Bird Other" field.
11. Choose how you heard about us from the list provided (you can select multiple options).
12. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can enter it in the "Comments" field.
13. Finally, agree to our terms of service by selecting "True" from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Favorite Bird**: Select your favorite bird from the list. You can choose one of the available options.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is not required.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This field is not required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted?**: Select how you would like to be contacted from the options provided (Phone, Email, Mail, In Person).
* **Other (if above)**: If you selected one of the above options, but prefer to be contacted in a different way, enter the method you prefer.
* **Time of Day**: Select the time of day that best suits you (e.g., 12:00, 16:00, 20:00).
* **Time Of Day Other**: If none of the above options are correct, enter your time of day preference.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth. This field is not required.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth. This field is not required.
* **Age**: Enter your age. This field is not required.
* **Favorite Bird Other**: If your favorite bird is not on the list, enter it here.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Select how you heard about us from the options provided (Social Media, Google, Event, Other, Word of Mouth). You can select multiple options.
* **How Did You Hear About Us Other**: If none of the above options are correct, enter how you heard about us.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Agree to our terms of service**: Select "True" to agree to our terms of service. This field is required.
