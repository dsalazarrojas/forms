# Holiday Market Shopping Budget Planner - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help holiday market shoppers plan and track their shopping budgets. It collects information about the shopper, their shopping list, and post-event feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Shopper Details** section by providing your name and email address.
2. Create a list of items you plan to purchase by filling out the **Shopping List** section.
3. Add each item to the list by clicking on the "Add Item" button and filling out the **Item**, **Description**, **Quantity**, and **Cost** fields.
4. Select all items on the list that apply to you by clicking on the "Select All" button.
5. Calculate the subtotal and total by filling out the **Subtotal** and **Total** fields.
6. Provide post-event feedback by filling out the **Post-Event Feedback** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shopper Details** (`title`): Fill out this section with your name and email address.
* **Name** (`shopper_details_name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`shopper_details_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`shopper_details_phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Shopping List** (`shopping_list`): Create a list of items you plan to purchase.
* **Item** (`shopping_list_item`): Enter the name of the item you want to add to the list.
* **Description** (`shopping_list_item_description`): Enter a brief description of the item.
* **Quantity** (`shopping_list_item_quantity`): Enter the quantity of the item you want to purchase.
* **Cost** (`shopping_list_item_cost`): Enter the cost of the item.
* **Items** (`shopping_list_items`): Select all items on the list that apply to you.
* **Subtotal** (`shopping_list_subtotals`): Calculate the subtotal of all selected items.
* **Total** (`shopping_list_total`): Calculate the total of all selected items.
* **Post-Event Feedback** (`post_event_feedback`): Provide feedback about your shopping experience.
* **Rating** (`post_event_feedback_rating`): Rate your experience (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor, Very poor).
* **Comments** (`post_event_feedback_comments`): Enter any comments about your experience.
* **Recommended** (`post_event_feedback_recommended`): Let us know if you would recommend this shopping experience to others.
