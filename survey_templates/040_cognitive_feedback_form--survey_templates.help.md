# Cognitive Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a cognitive session, such as a training, assessment, coaching, or workshop. The form captures their overall satisfaction, experience, and perception of the session, as well as their feedback on the facilitator's performance and the quality of materials provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct answers for each question based on your experience and perception of the session.
3. Take your time to answer each question carefully, as your feedback is valuable for improving future sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your official email address.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the cognitive session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of cognitive session you participated in:
	+ Training
	+ Assessment
	+ Coaching
	+ Workshop
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Overall Satisfaction with Session** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate how relevant the material was to your needs on a scale from 1 (not relevant) to 5 (highly relevant).
* **Clarity of Content** (`content_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the material was on a scale from 1 (very unclear) to 5 (very clear).
* **Pace of Session** (`pace_appropriateness`, select_one, required): Rate how well-paced the session was on a scale from 1 (too slow) to 5 (too fast).
* **Level of Engagement** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Rate how engaged you were during the session on a scale from 1 (not engaged) to 5 (highly engaged).
* **Knowledge or Skills Gained** (`knowledge_gain`, select_one, required): Rate what you learned from the session on a scale from 1 (nothing learned) to 5 (substantial learning).
* **Ability to Apply What Was Learned** (`practical_application`, select_one, required): Rate how easily you can apply what you learned on a scale from 1 (not applicable) to 5 (highly applicable).
* **Improvement in Confidence** (`confidence_improvement`, select_one, required): Rate how much your confidence improved on a scale from 1 (decreased) to 5 (significant improvement).
* **Facilitator Knowledge and Expertise** (`facilitator_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the facilitator's knowledge and expertise on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Communication and Teaching Skills** (`facilitator_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the facilitator's communication and teaching skills on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Support and Responsiveness** (`facilitator_support`, select_one, required): Rate how helpful the facilitator was on a scale from 1 (not supportive) to 5 (very supportive).
* **Quality of Materials Provided** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the materials provided on a scale from 1 (poor quality) to 5 (excellent quality).
* **Usefulness of Materials** (`materials_usefulness`, select_one, required): Rate how useful you found the materials on a scale from 1 (not useful) to 5 (highly useful).
* **Strengths of the Session** (`session_strengths`, text, optional): Provide any positive comments about the session.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Provide any areas for improvement for future sessions.
* **Topics for Future Sessions** (`future_sessions`, text, optional): Provide any topics you would like to explore in future sessions.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
* Your feedback is valuable for improving future sessions.
