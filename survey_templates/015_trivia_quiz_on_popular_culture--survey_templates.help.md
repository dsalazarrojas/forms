# trivia_quiz_on_popular_culture - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form "trivia_quiz_on_popular_culture" is designed to gather information about users' familiarity with popular culture, including movies, music, and trends. This will help in creating a trivia quiz that can be used for educational or entertainment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Go through each page, and answer the questions and fill in the fields as prompted.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For numerical fields, enter a whole number value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> (intro_text, text, optional): A brief introduction to the trivia quiz.
* This is a text field where you can provide any information you want to share with the users.
### <label> (movies, select_multiple, optional): What are your favorite movies?
* Select all the movies that you enjoy watching.
### <label> (music, text, optional): What type of music do you enjoy?
* Enter your favorite type of music.
### <label> (trends, number, optional): What current trend are you currently following?
* Enter the current trend you are interested in.
### <label> (movie_genres, select_one, optional): What genre of movie do you like most?
* Select your favorite movie genre.
### <label> (music_genres, select_one, optional): What type of music do you enjoy most?
* Select your favorite type of music.
### <label> (answer, select_one, optional): What is your favorite answer?
* Select your favorite answer from the options A, B, C, or D.
### <label> (submit, date, optional): What is your preferred submission date?
* Enter the date you prefer to submit your answers.
### <label> (confirm, select_multiple, optional): Do you confirm your answers?
* Select all that apply.
### <label> (rating, number, optional): How would you rate your favorite?
* Enter a whole number value for your favorite rating.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can provide your honest answer.
* The form is designed to be fun and engaging, so feel free to participate and enjoy!
