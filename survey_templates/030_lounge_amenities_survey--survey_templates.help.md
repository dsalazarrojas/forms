# lounge_amenities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on the lounge's amenities, services, and overall experience. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and optimize the lounge to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the relevant information for each question. You may choose from the options provided, or add your own comments in the free-text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Home Lounge Info** (Select One)
This question asks about the availability of home lounge facilities.
- **Yes**: The lounge has the facilities listed. 
- **No**: The lounge does not have the facilities listed.

### 2. **Available Services** (Select Multiple)
Choose the services available at the lounge that you have used.
- **Yes**: Select one or multiple services that apply to you.
- (Example: food and beverage services, laundry facilities, etc.)

### 3. **Food and Beverage Services** (Select Multiple)
Choose the food and beverage services available at the lounge that you have used.
- **Yes**: Select one or multiple services that apply to you.
- (Example: snacks, meals, beverages, etc.)

### 4. **Amenities** (Select Multiple)
Choose the amenities available at the lounge that you have used.
- **Yes**: Select one or multiple services that apply to you.
- (Example: Wi-Fi, TV, etc.)

### 5. **Overall Experience** (Select One)
Rate your overall experience at the lounge.
- **Yes**: You had a positive experience.
- **No**: You had a negative experience.

### 6. **Rating** (Number)
Rate your satisfaction with the lounge's facilities and services on a scale of 1-5.
- 1: Very dissatisfied
- 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
- 3: Neutral
- 4: Somewhat satisfied
- 5: Very satisfied

### 7. **Feedback** (Text)
Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience at the lounge.

### 8. **Email** (Email)
Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from us.

### 9. **Phone** (Text)
Enter your phone number to receive calls and messages from us. (Optional)

### 10. **Other Feedback** (Note)
Share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience at the lounge.
