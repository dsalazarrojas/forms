# Flagship Store Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback from customers who have visited a flagship store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (1, text, optional): Fill in your name.
* **Email** (2, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Visit Date** (3, date, optional): Select the date of your visit to the store.
* **Store Location** (4, text, optional): Type in the store location where you visited.
* **Rating** (5, number, optional): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 for your experience.
* **Comments** (6, text, optional): Optionally, provide any additional comments about your visit.
* **Store Association** (7, text, optional): If you visited a specific store, type in its name.
