# Economic Transformations Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Economic Transformations Assessment form is designed to collect data on various aspects of economic transformations in different sectors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into multiple pages, each with its own set of questions. Complete each page according to the instructions provided.
2. For text fields, please provide a brief description or answer in the space provided.
3. For selection fields (e.g. select_one, select_multiple), choose the option that best reflects your situation.
4. Ensure you answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
5. You may optionally provide additional comments or comments at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Intro
* **Intro** (`intro`, note, not required): This is a brief introduction page with no specific questions. Please take a moment to read through the page and familiarize yourself with the form's purpose and instructions.

### Digitalization
* **How do you perceive digitalization in your sector?** (`digitalization`, text, required): Describe your understanding of digitalization in your sector.
* **What is the impact of digitalization on your sector?** (`digitalization_impact`, text, required): Explain the effects digitalization has had or will have on your sector.

### Globalization
* **How do you perceive globalization in your sector?** (`globalization`, text, required): Describe your understanding of globalization in your sector.
* **What is the impact of globalization on your sector?** (`globalization_impact`, text, required): Explain the effects globalization has had or will have on your sector.

### Green Economy
* **How do you perceive the green economy in your sector?** (`green_economy`, text, required): Describe your understanding of the green economy in your sector.
* **What is the impact of the green economy on your sector?** (`green_economy_impact`, text, required): Explain the effects the green economy has had or will have on your sector.

### Sector Selection
* **Sector** (`sector`, select_one, required): Select the sector you are working in or represent.
* **Sector list** (`sector_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant sectors you are working in or represent.

### Organization and Contact Information
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Provide the name of the organization you represent or are associated with.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role within the organization.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select the country you are representing.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person to contact.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your work phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information not covered by other questions.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.

### Scale and Time Spent
* **Scale** (`scale`, select_one, required): Select whether you are operating on a scale.
* **Scale list** (`scale_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant scales you are operating on.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Enter the time spent on economic transformations.

### Confidence
* **Confidence** (`confidence`, number, optional): Rate your confidence in the accuracy of the provided information.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the importance of economic transformations in your sector.

### Sector Identification
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry you are working in or represent.
* **Subsector** (`subsector`, select_one, required): Select the subsector you are working in or represent.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Provide the name of the organization you represent or are associated with.
