# Textual Properties Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Textual Properties Assessment Form is designed to collect information on the properties of text samples, including names, dates, and evaluation data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Select the date of the evaluation in the "evaluation_date" field.
4. Enter the time of the evaluation in the "evaluation_time" field.
5. Choose the assessment level for the text sample in the "assessment_level" field.
6. Select the method used for the assessment in the "assessment_method" field.
7. Provide feedback on the text sample in the "feedback" field.
8. Enter any additional evaluation comments in the "evaluation" field.
9. Summarize your overall assessment of the text sample in the "assessment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **evaluation_date**: Select the date of the evaluation.
* **evaluation_time**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **assessment_level**: Choose the level of the assessment (e.g., good, bad, average).
* **assessment_method**: Select the method used for the assessment (e.g., subjective, objective).
* **feedback**: Provide any feedback on the text sample.
* **evaluation**: Enter any additional evaluation comments.
