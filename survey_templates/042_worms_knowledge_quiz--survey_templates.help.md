# Worms Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worms Knowledge Quiz is a survey designed to gauge a user's knowledge about worms. It consists of three sections: an introduction, a quiz, and a submission section.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This section is optional, and you can skip it if you don't feel like providing an answer. However, it's a good opportunity to provide some background information about your familiarity with worms.
2. **Quiz**: Choose the correct answer from the options provided for each question. You can select only one option per question.
3. **Submission**: Enter your name and any additional comments you'd like to make about your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (form_1, text, optional): This is the first section of the form where you can provide some background information about your familiarity with worms. If you're not sure what to write, you can leave it blank.
* **Quiz** (form_2, select_one, required): This section consists of multiple-choice questions about worms. Choose the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
* **Submission** (form_3, text, optional): This is the final section where you can enter your name and any additional comments about your answer. If you're not sure what to write, you can leave it blank.

## Tips
* Be honest about your knowledge of worms. The more accurate your answers, the more helpful the results will be.
* Take your time to read each question carefully and choose the correct answer.
