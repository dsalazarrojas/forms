# private_jet_grooming_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from private jet customers about their experience with the airline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the cabin cleanliness.
2. Provide feedback on the quality of grooming services provided.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
4. If you have any comments or concerns, use the notes section to provide more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **cabin_cleanliness** (`cabin_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Rate the cleanliness of the cabin. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for your answer.
* **grooming_services** (grooming_services, text, required): Provide feedback on the quality of grooming services provided.
* **overall_satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. Choose from 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.
* **comments** (comments, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, use this section to provide more details about your experience.
* **contact_info** (contact_info, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you for follow-up communication.
* **aviation_team** (aviation_team, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you interacted with the aviation team during your flight.
* **date_and_time** (date_and_time, date, required): Provide the date and time of your flight.
* **time_zone** (time_zone, select_one, required): Indicate your time zone.
* **flight_number** (flight_number, select_one, required): Enter the flight number.
* **cabin_type** (cabin_type, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of cabin used for your flight.
* **aircraft_model** (aircraft_model, select_one, required): Select the model of the aircraft used.
* **flight_number_alt** (flight_number_alt, select_one, optional): If this is your second flight with us, enter the flight number for this flight.
