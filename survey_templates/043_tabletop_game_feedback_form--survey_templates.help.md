# tabletop_game_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The table-top_game_feedback_form is used to collect feedback on tabletop game experience. This form will help game developers understand what to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form fields with your honest feedback.
2. Be as specific as possible about what you liked and disliked about the game.
3. Provide suggestions for improvements, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Title** (`game_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the game you played.
* **Game Version** (`game_version`, text, not required): Enter the version of the game you played.
* **Rating** (`player_rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall experience with the game, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-5).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share your thoughts and opinions about the game.
* **Recommended Improvements** (`recommended_improvements`, text, not required): Suggest any improvements you think the game needs.

## Tips
* Please be constructive and specific with your feedback.
* Try to be as descriptive as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* Remember to save your work before submitting the form.
