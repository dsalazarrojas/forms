# Landscape Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our landscape services. We value your input and would like to hear about your overall satisfaction, thoughts on company performance, and any areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly to help us understand your experience.
2. Type your response in the text box provided for each question.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) if present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience (\*)**: This is the first question that asks about your overall experience with our landscape services. Please type in a brief description of your experience.
* **Company Performance (\*)**: This question is about your thoughts on our company's performance. Be as specific as possible and provide examples or details if applicable.
* **Customer Experience**: This question is about your interaction with our company staff or representatives. Were you treated well? Did you have any issues that were not addressed?
* **What We Do Well**: This is an opportunity for you to tell us what we do well and why it was a positive experience for you.
* **Improve What We Do**: If there's anything we can improve on, please let us know. Be specific and provide suggestions or ideas.
* **Customer Information**: If you have any additional information about your experience, such as the name of the staff member you interacted with, please include it here.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other thoughts or comments that don't fit in the other fields, please include them here.
* **Contact Phone**: This is a text field for you to input your phone number. Please enter the phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
