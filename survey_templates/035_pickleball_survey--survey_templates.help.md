# Pickleball Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about your playing habits and preferences in pickleball. This will help us better understand how you play and what we can do to improve our services for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read each question carefully and answer to the best of your ability.
2. You can choose to fill in all or some of the questions, as they are all optional.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask a staff member for clarification.
4. Once you've completed the form, please click the submit button to submit your answers.
