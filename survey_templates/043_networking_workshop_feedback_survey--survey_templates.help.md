This form is a survey designed to collect feedback from participants of a networking workshop. The purpose of this form is to gather opinions and insights from attendees on various aspects of the workshop, including the session topics, speakers, networking opportunities, and overall experience.

The form consists of 7 fields, each asking a different question or requesting feedback. The questions cover the following areas:

*   Session topics covered: This question is asking for a multiple-choice answer on the quality of the session topics.
*   Speakers: This question is asking for a single-choice answer on the quality of the speakers.
*   Networking: This question is asking for multiple-choice answers on the level of networking activity.
*   Takeaways: This question is asking for free-text answers on the biggest takeaways from the workshop.
*   Suggestions: This question is asking for free-text answers on suggestions for future sessions.
*   Comments: This question is asking for free-text answers on any other comments or thoughts.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through each question carefully to understand what is being asked.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select all the options that apply to you.
3.  For single-choice questions, select only one option that best describes your opinion.
4.  For free-text questions, type in your answers in the text box provided.
5.  Make sure to complete all the questions, as indicated by the required/optional labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How was the networking workshop?** (`intro`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks for a general feedback on your experience of the networking workshop.
*   **Session topics covered** (`session`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the session topics that you felt were covered well or poorly. For example, you can select "Networking 101", "Building relationships", "Career growth", or None of the above.
*   **How were the speakers?** (`speaker`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one option that best describes your experience with the speakers, such as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **How would you rate your level of networking?** (`networking`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the options that describe your level of networking activity, such as "Extremely active", "Somewhat active", "Not very active", or None of the above.
*   **What are your biggest takeaways?** (`takeaways`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a list or a brief description of the biggest takeaways or key points you gained from the workshop.
*   **Do you have any suggestions for future sessions?** (`suggestions`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any suggestions or ideas you have for future sessions or workshops.
*   **Any other comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any other comments or thoughts you have about the workshop.
