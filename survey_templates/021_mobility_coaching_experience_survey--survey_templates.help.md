# Mobility Coaching Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with mobility coaching sessions. It is a tool to help us understand your perspective and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Select your response from the available options for each question.
3. Don't worry if you don't know the answer to a question, you can always leave it blank or provide additional information in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mobility Coaching Experience Survey** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is the introduction page, providing an overview of the form and its purpose.
* **How would you rate the quality of your mobility coaching sessions?** (`session_quality`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to rate the quality of your sessions, from "Very Good" to "Poor".
* **What level of satisfaction do you feel after our sessions?** (`client_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to rate your level of satisfaction, from "Very High" to "Very Low".
* **What is your current sentiment towards mobility coaching?** (`client_sentiment`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to describe your current sentiment, from "Very Positive" to "Very Negative".
* **How can we improve our sessions?** (`session_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Choose as many or as few of the options as you like to suggest ways we can improve our sessions.
* **How frequently do you think you will attend our sessions?** (`session_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to predict your frequency of attendance, from "Daily" to "Rarely".
* **Do you have any additional feedback?** (`feedback`, note, optional): If you have any thoughts or suggestions that didn't fit into the previous questions, use this field to provide them.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates from us.
* **Are you happy to receive email updates?** (`email_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you'd like to receive email updates, or "No" if you'd prefer not to.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive calls from us.
* **Are you happy to receive phone calls?** (`phone_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you'd like to receive phone calls, or "No" if you'd prefer not to.
* **Other Contact** (`other_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options to describe how you'd like to be contacted, including "Email", "Phone", or "Other".
