# COVID 19 Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess and document the COVID-19 safety protocols and procedures of an organization or team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided fields and determine the correct answers based on your organization's current state of COVID-19 safety protocols and procedures.
2. Answer each field accurately and honestly, providing detailed explanations when necessary.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
4. Once completed, review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name_val`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or team being assessed.
* **Department or Team Reviewed** (`department_team_text`, text, required): Identify the department or team being assessed.
* **Workplace Safety Officer** (`safety_officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the safety officer responsible for the assessment.
* **Date of Review** (`review_date_val`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Physical Distancing Protocol Score** (`social_dist_score`, number, required): Rate your organization's adherence to physical distancing protocols from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Mask Mandate Compliance** (`mask_compliance_cat`, select_one, required): Choose the level of compliance with mask mandates in your organization:
	+ Full Compliance: All employees and visitors wear masks correctly at all times.
	+ Partial Compliance: Some employees or visitors may not be wearing masks.
	+ Non-Compliant: No one is wearing masks.
	+ N/A: Not Applicable (if not applicable due to special circumstances).
* **Sanitation Station Availability** (`sanitation_station_yn`, select_one, required): Report the availability of hand sanitizer or handwashing stations:
	+ Adequate: Hand sanitizer or handwashing stations are available and easily accessible.
	+ Need more: Hand sanitizer or handwashing stations are present but not easily accessible.
	+ Inadequate: No hand sanitizer or handwashing stations are available.
* **Staff Training Completion** (`training_completion_yn`, select_one, required): Indicate whether all staff have received COVID-19 safety training:
	+ True: All staff have completed the required training.
	+ False: Some or no staff have not completed the required training.
	+ In Progress: Training is ongoing or incomplete.
* **Health Screening at Entry** (`entry_screening_yn`, select_one, required): Report the availability of health screenings for arrivals:
	+ True: Temperature checks or symptom questionnaires are conducted for all arrivals.
	+ False: No health screenings are conducted for all arrivals.
	+ Sometimes: Health screenings are conducted sporadically or only for certain groups.
* **Ventilation Improvement Status** (`ventilation_status_cat`, select_one, required): Report the status of ventilation improvements:
	+ Completed: Improvements have been implemented.
	+ Scheduled: Improvements are planned but not yet implemented.
	+ Not Planned: No improvements are planned.
	+ N/A: Not Applicable (if not applicable due to special circumstances).
* **Identified Safety Gaps** (`safety_gaps_desc`, text, optional): Describe any areas where safety protocols are failing (if applicable).
* **Action Plan for Gaps** (`action_plan_text`, text, optional): Outline steps to address identified safety gaps (if applicable).
* **I Certify this Assessment** (`assessment_auth_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm that the assessment is accurate and will be shared with management:
	+ True: The assessment is accurate and will be shared.
	+ False: The assessment is not accurate or will not be shared.

## Tips

* Ensure that assessments are performed regularly to identify and address any changes or issues.
* Consult with colleagues, superiors, or relevant experts if unsure about any aspect of the assessment.
* Document all assessments and actions taken to address identified gaps.
* Review and update this assessment as necessary to reflect changes in your organization's safety protocols and procedures.
