# Student Tutoring Program Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students on the Student Tutoring Program, allowing the program to evaluate its effectiveness and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Program Name: This is where you will enter the name of the program you are evaluating.
2. Write Your Overall Thoughts: Respond to the question "What did you think of the program overall?" in the space provided.
3. Suggest Improvements: If there's anything you'd like to see changed or improved about the program, share your thoughts in the answer to "What would you like to see improved in the program?".
4. Availability: Select your availability for the next 30 days, if applicable, in the "What is your availability for the next 30 days?" field.
5. Rate Our Quality: Evaluate the quality of the program overall by selecting a rating in the "How would you rate the quality of the program overall?" field.
6. Contact Information: If you have any questions, concerns, or would like to provide feedback, fill in the contact information fields: Program Contact, Program E-mail, Program Phone, Program Location, and Program Hours.
7. Personal Information: If you'd like to provide your personal contact information, fill in the fields: Student Name, Student E-mail, Student Phone, and Student Comments.
8. Staff Feedback: If you'd like to provide feedback on the staff, fill in the fields: Staff Name, Staff E-mail, Staff Phone, and Staff Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (text): Enter the name of the program you are evaluating.
* **What did you think of the program overall?** (text): Share your thoughts about the program, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **What would you like to see improved in the program?** (text): Suggest improvements or changes to the program.
* **What is your availability for the next 30 days?** (date): Select your availability for the next 30 days, if applicable.
* **What is your availability for the next 30 days?** (time): Select your availability for the next 30 days, if applicable.
* **Program Contact** (text): Provide the contact information for the program.
* **Program E-mail** (email): Enter the email address of the program.
* **Program Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the program.
* **Program Location** (text): Enter the location of the program.
* **Program Hours** (text): Enter the program hours.
* **Program Comments** (note): Add any additional comments about the program.
* **Program Comments** (note): Add any additional comments about the program.
* **Student Name** (text): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student E-mail** (email): Enter your email address as a student.
* **Student Phone** (text): Enter your phone number as a student.
* **Student Comments** (note): Add any additional comments as a student.
* **Staff Name** (text): Enter the name of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Staff E-mail** (email): Enter the email address of the staff member.
* **Staff Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the staff member.
* **Staff Comments** (note): Add any additional comments about the staff member.
* **Staff Location** (text): Enter the location of the staff member.
