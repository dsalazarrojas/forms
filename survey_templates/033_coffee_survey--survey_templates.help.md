# Coffee Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from coffee shop customers about their preferences, habits, and opinions on coffee. The goal is to understand their behavior, tastes, and values to help coffee shops improve their services and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your customer ID or name (optional).
2. Indicate how often you visit coffee shops (page 2).
3. Choose your favorite coffee drink (page 3).
4. Select your preferred roast level, origin, milk preference, and sweetness level (pages 4-6).
5. Rate your coffee knowledge level and willingness to try new coffee (page 7).
6. Choose how much you spend per visit and your monthly coffee spending (pages 8-9).
7. Select all factors that influence your shop choice (page 10).
8. Indicate your interest in specialty coffee and concern about sustainability (pages 11-12).
9. Leave any additional feedback or comments (pages 13-14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID or Name (page 2)**: If you have a customer ID or name that you'd like to provide, enter it here (optional).
* **How often do you visit coffee shops (page 2)**: Choose how frequently you visit coffee shops. This will help us understand your habits.
* **Favorite Coffee Drink (page 3)**: Select your go-to coffee drink from the list.
* **Preferred Roast Level (page 4)**: Choose your preferred roast level for your coffee. Do you like it light, medium, dark, or extra dark?
* **Preferred Coffee Origin (page 4)**: Select where you'd like your coffee to come from. Do you prefer single-origin, blend, local, organic, or no preference?
* **Milk Preference (page 5)**: Choose how you take your milk. Do you prefer regular milk, skim milk, soy milk, almond milk, oat milk, coconut milk, or no milk?
* **Sweetness Preference (page 5)**: Rate your desired level of sugar in your coffee. Do you like it with no sugar, light sugar, normal sugar, extra sugar, or with syrup?
* **Coffee Knowledge Level (page 6)**: Rate your level of coffee knowledge. Are you a casual drinker, coffee enthusiast, coffee connoisseur, or a professional in the industry?
* **Willingness to Try New Coffee (page 7)**: Indicate how often you're willing to try new coffee. Rate from 1 (never) to 5 (always).
* **Typical Spending per Visit (page 8)**: Choose how much you usually spend per visit.
* **Monthly Coffee Spending (page 9)**: Approximate how much you spend on coffee in a month.
* **Most Important Factors in Shop Selection (page 10)**: Select all factors that influence your shop choice. Choose from coffee quality, price, location, atmosphere, staff friendliness, speed of service, variety of options, and sustainability.
* **Interest in Specialty Coffee (page 11)**: Indicate how interested you are in specialty coffee.
* **Concern about Coffee Sustainability (page 12)**: Rate your concern about the sustainability of coffee.
* **Suggestions for Coffee Shops (page 13)**: Leave any suggestions for coffee shops to improve.
* **Additional Comments (page 14)**: Share any other feedback or comments you'd like to share.
