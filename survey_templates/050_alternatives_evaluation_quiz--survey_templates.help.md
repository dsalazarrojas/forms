# Alternatives Evaluation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alternatives Evaluation Quiz is a tool used to gather data and insights about the evaluation process of alternatives. This form helps to structure and document a systematic evaluation process to aid decision-making. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question in relation to your current or proposed alternatives evaluation.

### Step 1: Enter your contact information
* Evaluator Name: Enter your full name.
* Email Address: Enter your contact email address.

### Step 2: Evaluation Details
* Project/Decision Name: Enter the name of the project or decision being evaluated.
* Evaluation Date: Enter the date when the evaluation was performed.
* Evaluation Purpose: Select the reason why you are evaluating, e.g., Vendor selection, Software selection, Equipment purchase, Service provider selection, Process improvement, Strategic planning, Budget allocation, or Other.
* Number of Alternatives: Enter the number of alternatives being evaluated.

### Step 3: Enter Alternative Details
* Alternative 1 Name: Enter the name of the first alternative.
* Alternative 1 Cost: Enter the estimated cost of the first alternative (optional).
* Alternative 1 Overall Score: Rate the first alternative from 1-10.
* Alternative 1 Pros: Enter the advantages of the first alternative.
* Alternative 1 Cons: Enter the disadvantages of the first alternative.
* Alternative 2 Name: Enter the name of the second alternative.
* Alternative 2 Cost: Enter the estimated cost of the second alternative (optional).
* Alternative 2 Overall Score: Rate the second alternative from 1-10.
* Alternative 2 Pros: Enter the advantages of the second alternative.
* Alternative 2 Cons: Enter the disadvantages of the second alternative.
* Alternative 3 Name: Enter the name of the third alternative (optional).
* Alternative 3 Cost: Enter the estimated cost of the third alternative (optional).
* Alternative 3 Overall Score: Rate the third alternative from 1-10 (optional).
* Alternative 3 Pros: Enter the advantages of the third alternative (optional).
* Alternative 3 Cons: Enter the disadvantages of the third alternative (optional).

### Step 4: Evaluation Criteria
* Evaluation Criteria: Select the factors that are important for the evaluation, e.g., Cost, Quality, Timeline, Features/functionality, Vendor reputation, Support/maintenance, Scalability, Ease of use, Security, Compliance.
* Cost Importance: Rate the importance of cost from 1-5.
* Quality Importance: Rate the importance of quality from 1-5.
* Timeline Importance: Rate the importance of the timeline from 1-5.

### Step 5: Finalize Your Recommendation
* Preferred Alternative: Choose the alternative that you recommend.
* Recommendation Rationale: Enter a brief explanation for your choice.
* Confidence Level: Rate your confidence level from 1-5.
* Next Steps: Enter the recommended next steps.

## Tips
- Be as concrete and objective as possible when evaluating each alternative.
- Use a systematic approach to evaluate each alternative.
- Consider multiple perspectives when evaluating alternatives.
- Document all your evaluation criteria and weights.
- Keep your recommendation clear and concise.
- Review and refine your evaluation process as needed.
- Consider sharing your evaluation with others.

Note: The form requires some fields to be optional, as indicated by the "required: false" property in the YAML. You can complete the form without filling them if they are not applicable to your situation.
