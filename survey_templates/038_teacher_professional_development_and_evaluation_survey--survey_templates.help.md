# Teacher Professional Development And Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers evaluate and improve their professional development and training sessions. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the effectiveness and areas of improvement of the training session, as well as to suggest potential areas of development for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `session_title` field with the title of the current training session.
2. Enter the duration of the session in the `training_session_duration` field.
3. Select the evaluation of the session from the `evaluation` field.
4. Identify areas of improvement from the `improvement_areas` field.
5. Provide suggestions for improvement in the `session_improvement_plan` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **session_title (Text)**: Enter the title of the training session.
* **training_session_duration (Number)**: Enter the duration of the training session in hours.
* **evaluation (Select Multiple)**: Select how you evaluate the session:
	+ Session was very good
	+ Session was good
	+ Session was fair
	+ Session was poor
	+ Session was very poor

	+ Communication skills
	+ Time management skills
