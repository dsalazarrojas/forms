# Town Hall Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a town hall meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely.
- Provide clear answers to each question.
- Be honest in providing your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Details** (text): Provide a brief description of the meeting, including its title, purpose, or any notable moments.
- **Participant Name** (text): Type your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Meeting Attended** (select one): Indicate how many meetings you attended (1, 2, 3, or 4).
- **Feedback** (text): Share your thoughts, comments, or concerns about the meeting.
