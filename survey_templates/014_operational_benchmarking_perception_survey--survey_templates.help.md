# operational_benchmarking_perception_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Operational Benchmarking Perception Survey is a form used to assess the level of familiarity, effectiveness, benefits, and challenges related to benchmarking in the organization. This survey aims to provide insights into the current state of benchmarking practices within the organization and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable options for each question from the provided options.
2. For questions with multiple options (e.g., benchmarking_familiarity), select all options that apply to you.
3. Fill in the text field for comments, if you have any additional insights or thoughts.
4. Enter numerical values for benchmarking_effectiveness and benchmarking_benefit, if applicable.
5. For benchmarking_challenge, select a time duration (e.g., hours, days, weeks, months, years) and enter the number of hours spent on benchmarking activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Benchmarking Familiarity** (`benchmarking_familiarity`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question assesses your level of familiarity with benchmarking practices within the organization.
	+ Select all options that apply to you:
		- Option 1: I am very familiar with benchmarking practices within the organization.
		- Option 2: I have some knowledge of benchmarking practices within the organization.
		- Option 3: I am not familiar with benchmarking practices within the organization.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This question measures how effective you perceive benchmarking to be in achieving your goals.
	+ Select one option that best applies to you:
		- Option 1: Very effective.
		- Option 2: Somewhat effective.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question identifies the benefits you perceive from benchmarking practices within the organization.
	+ Select all options that apply to you:
		- Option 1: Improved decision-making.
		- Option 2: Increased efficiency.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This question highlights the challenges you face when implementing benchmarking practices within the organization.
	+ Select all options that apply to you:
		- Option 1: Limited resources.
		- Option 2: Lack of standardization.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have any additional insights or thoughts about benchmarking practices within the organization, enter them here.
* **Benchmarking Effectiveness** (`benchmarking_effectiveness`, number, required: false):
	+ This question measures your perception of the effectiveness of benchmarking practices within the organization on a scale of 1-10.
* **Benchmarking Challenge** (`benchmarking_challenge`, time, required: false):
	+ If you have spent time on benchmarking activities, select the duration of time spent and enter the number of hours.
* **Benchmarking Benefit** (`benchmarking_benefit`, number, required: false):
	+ This question measures your perception of the benefits of benchmarking practices within the organization on a scale of 1-10.

## Tips
* Answer questions honestly, and select all options that apply to your current perception of benchmarking practices within the organization.
* Take your time to complete the form, and feel free to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
* Please submit your completed survey form to help us better understand the state of benchmarking practices within the organization.
