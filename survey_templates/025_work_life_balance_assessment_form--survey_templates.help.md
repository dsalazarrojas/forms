# Work Life Balance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Work Life Balance Assessment Form" is designed to evaluate your current work-life balance. This form helps you assess how you allocate your time and energy between work and personal life activities. It's meant to help individuals identify areas of improvement to achieve a better balance between professional and personal well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of questions, which are designed to assess different aspects of your work-life balance.
2. For each question, select the response that best fits your current situation.
3. Ensure you fill out all the questions on the form, but note that some fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Work-Life Balance Questionnaire** (`work_life_balance_questionnaire`, text, optional)
This first question is a free-text field where you can provide any thoughts or comments about your current work-life balance.

### **2. Current Work-Life Balance Level** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the three options to indicate your current level of work-life balance:
	* High: You feel your work and personal life are well balanced.
	* Medium: You're somewhere in between, with room for improvement.
	* Low: You feel your work and personal life are out of balance.

### **3. Time Spent at Work** (`time_spent_on_work`, number, optional)
Enter the approximate number of hours you spend working per week.

### **4. Time Spent on Leisure** (`time_spent_on_leisure`, number, optional)
Enter the approximate number of hours you spend on leisure activities per week.

### **5. Stress Level** (`stress_level`, select_one, optional)
Select one of the three options to indicate your current stress level:
	* High: You often feel stressed or overwhelmed.
	* Medium: You sometimes feel stressed, but it's manageable.
	* Low: You rarely feel stressed.

### **6. Multiple Dimensions of Balance** (`work_life_balance_3`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the dimensions of balance that you currently achieve or would like to achieve:
	* High: You're highly skilled in this area.
	* Medium: You're somewhat skilled in this area.
	* Low: You're not skilled in this area.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when answering each question.
* Take your time to think about each question before selecting an answer.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to choose the option that best fits your current situation.
