# TV Show Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Show Design Survey form is a collection of fields designed to gather key information about TV shows. This form is used for various applications, including content development, marketing, and analytics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by ensuring you have the required fields filled out or have a clear idea of the topic you are providing input for.
2. Begin filling out the form with the provided fields, starting from "TV show design suggestions" (Page 1).
3. Move on to the other fields, such as target audience, genre, and main characters.
4. For select fields, use the available options to select the most relevant information.
5. Ensure you fill out all the required fields or mark them as irrelevant if you cannot provide information.
6. Finally, review your submission to ensure accuracy and relevance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **TV Show Design Suggestions** (`1`, `text`, `not required`)
*   A field for users to submit any TV show design suggestions or ideas that they feel would be interesting or worth exploring.

### 2. **Target Audience** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `not required`)
*   Select the target audience for the TV show, such as adults, children, or a specific age group.

### 3. **Genre** (`3`, `select_one`, `not required`)
*   Choose the genre of the TV show, such as action, comedy, drama, or animation.

### 4. **Main Characters** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `not required`)
*   Select the main characters in the TV show, including their names and descriptions.

### 5. **Episode Length** (`5`, `number`, `not required`)
*   Enter the approximate length of each episode in minutes.

### 6. **Target Platforms** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `not required`)
*   Select the platforms where the TV show will be released, such as Netflix, Amazon, or YouTube.

### 7. **Key Art Style** (`7`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Briefly describe the key art style of the TV show, including any unique or attention-grabbing features.

### 8. **Episode Frequency** (`8`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Provide an idea of the episode frequency, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or a one-time release.

### 9. **Release Windows** (`9`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Describe the release windows for the TV show, including any time-sensitive or specific release dates.

### 10. **Episode Distribution** (`10`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Explain the episode distribution plan for the TV show, including how the episodes will be released and when.

### 11. **Production Countries** (`11`, `text`, `not required`)
*   List the production countries for the TV show.

### 12. **Cast/Crew** (`12`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Provide a list of the main cast and crew members for the TV show.

### 13. **Key Art Images** (`13`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Upload any key art images for the TV show.

### 14. **Key Art Videos** (`14`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Provide any key art videos for the TV show.

### 15. **Episode Summary** (`15`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Write a brief summary of each episode.

### 16. **Episode Script** (`16`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Provide a script for each episode.

### 17. **Season Duration** (`17`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Enter the duration of each season.

### 18. **Season Episodes** (`18`, `number`, `not required`)
*   Enter the number of episodes for each season.

### 19. **Season Summary** (`19`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Write a brief summary of each season.

### 20. **Production Budget** (`20`, `number`, `not required`)
*   Enter the production budget for the TV show.

### 21. **Production Company** (`21`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Provide the name of the production company.

### 22. **Production Team** (`22`, `text`, `not required`)
*   List the main production team members.

### 23. **Release Date** (`23`, `date`, `not required`)
*   Enter the release date for the TV show.

### 24. **Release Time** (`24`, `time`, `not required`)
*   Enter the release time for the TV show.

### 25. **Created By** (`25`, `text`, `not required`)
*   Provide the name and contact information of the TV show creator.



This form is designed for users to gather key information about TV shows. Each field is a crucial piece of data that can be used in various applications, including content development, marketing, and analytics. Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, ensuring you have considered the available options for select fields.
