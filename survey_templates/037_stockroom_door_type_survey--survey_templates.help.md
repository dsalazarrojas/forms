# Stockroom Door Type Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about stockroom doors, including whether they are used, their condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully before starting the survey.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. For select one fields, choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your answer.
4. For the "Comments" field, provide any additional information or details about the stockroom door.
5. Fill in the "Assigned Date" and "Assigned Time" fields with the date and time you are completing the form.
6. Select the "Assigned User" field to indicate who is responsible for completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (1): Enter a brief description or comment about the stockroom door.
* **Stockroom Door Type** (2): Indicate whether the stockroom door is used or not.
* **Condition** (3): Describe the condition of the stockroom door.
* **Frequency of Use** (4): Answer "Yes" if the stockroom door is regularly used, "No" otherwise.
* **Performance Impact** (5): Answer "Yes" if the stockroom door has a significant impact on performance, "No" otherwise.
* **Performance Measures** (6): Answer "Yes" if you use performance measures for the stockroom door, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments** (7): Provide any additional comments or details about the stockroom door.
* **Assigned Date** (8): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Assigned Time** (9): Enter the time you are completing this form.
* **Assigned User** (10): Indicate who is responsible for completing this form.
