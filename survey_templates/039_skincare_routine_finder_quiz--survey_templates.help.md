# Skincare Routine Finder Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide personalized skincare recommendations based on the user's skin type, concerns, and habits. Please answer the questions honestly to receive the most accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the most accurate answer for your situation.
2. Fill out all the required fields (marked as *required*).
3. You can select multiple answers for questions that allow it (e.g., skin concerns).
4. If you have no answer for a particular field, you can leave it blank (e.g., "I don't use any products").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Welcome to the Skincare Routine Finder Quiz!** (`intro_page`, note, not required)
This is the introduction page, and you can skip it if you've already read it.

### **What is your skin type?** (`skin_type`, select_one, not required)
Please select the option that best describes your skin type:

* Normal: If you have balanced skin with no specific issues.
* Dry: If you have dry, tight, or flaky skin.
* Oily: If you have shiny or greasy skin.
* Combination: If you have a mix of normal and oily skin.
* Sensitive: If you have skin that easily irritates or reacts to products.

### **What are your skin concerns?** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple, not required)
Please choose the concerns that apply to you. Select multiple answers if necessary:

* Acne: If you have acne or acne scars.
* Aging: If you have fine lines, wrinkles, or age spots.
* Hyper-pigmentation: If you have dark spots or uneven skin tone.
* Irritation: If you experience irritation or redness.
* Redness: If you have red or inflamed skin.

### **What are your product preferences?** (`product_preferences`, text, not required)
Please enter any products you currently use or are interested in using (e.g., moisturizers, serums, face washes).

### **What is your age?** (`age`, number, not required)
Please enter your age.

### **At what time of day do you do your skincare routine?** (`time_of_day`, time, not required)
Please enter the time of day you perform your skincare routine (e.g., morning, evening).

### **How often do you perform your skincare routine?** (`routine_frequency`, select_one, not required)
Please select the frequency of your routine:

* Morning and Night: If you do your routine twice a day.
* Morning: If you do your routine only in the morning.
* Night: If you do your routine only at night.
* Other: If you have a custom routine.

### **Which products do you currently use?** (`products_used`, text, not required)
Please enter any products you currently use (e.g., face wash, moisturizer, sunscreen).

### **What products would you be interested in?** (`recommended_products`, text, not required)
Please enter any products you're interested in trying or learning more about.

### **How often do you perform your skincare routine?** (`routine_frequency`, select_one, not required)
Please select the frequency of your routine (e.g., how often you apply products).

Note: You can ask for more help or guidance if you have any questions or concerns about your answers.
