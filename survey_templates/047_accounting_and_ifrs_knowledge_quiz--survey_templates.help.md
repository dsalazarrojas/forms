# Accounting And IFRS Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your knowledge of accounting and IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards) standards. It will help you identify areas where you need to improve your understanding of these important financial standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant information, including your full name and email address.
2. Answer the questions regarding general accounting concepts, such as the fundamental accounting equation and accrual basis of accounting.
3. Identify the correct method of depreciation and the classification of current assets.
4. Answer the IFRS-specific questions, which cover topics such as the definition of fair value, permitted inventory valuation methods, and the key difference between IFRS and US GAAP.
5. Select your confidence level in your IFRS knowledge and indicate if you would be interested in further training on the subject.
