# Graphics Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Graphics Configuration Survey form to help you provide valuable feedback on your graphics experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer every question truthfully, as your feedback will directly contribute to improving graphics-related features.
2. Choose from the given options for each question, and select "No Change" when it's the best fit for your situation.
3. For the "satisfaction" question, rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What's your GPU usage like? (`GPU usage`, `select_one`, required)
* Answer this question with one of the provided options: Low, High, Medium, or Unlimited. This will help us understand how you're utilizing your GPU.
### What do you need from your display? (`display_needs`, `select_multiple`, required)
* Choose all that apply from Higher Resolution, More Frames Per Second (FPS), Better Graphics Quality, or No Change. This will help us understand your display preferences.
### On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with your current graphics experience? (`satisfaction`, `number`, required)
* Rate your satisfaction level from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied). This will help us gauge your overall satisfaction with the current graphics experience.
### Do you belong to any gaming communities online? (`gaming_community`, `select_one`, optional)
* Answer with either "Yes" or "No". This will help us understand your online gaming habits.
### Any additional comments? (`comments`, `note`, optional)
* Share any extra comments or thoughts you have about your graphics experience. This is your chance to provide more specific feedback.
