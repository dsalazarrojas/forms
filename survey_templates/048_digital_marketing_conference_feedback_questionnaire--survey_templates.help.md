# Digital Marketing Conference Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Marketing Conference Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback and opinions from attendees of the conference. Its purpose is to gather information on various aspects of the conference to help improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee information on the first page, including your name, title, and email address.
2. On the second page, rate your experience of the conference on a scale of 1-5.
3. Provide feedback and comments on the sessions you attended.
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Select any opinions that resonate with you from the list provided.
6. Submit your completed form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Information**: This page is to collect your basic information, including name, title, and email address. Please fill in your details accurately.
* **Conference Experience**: This field is to rate your overall experience of the conference on a scale of 1-5.
* **Session Rating**: Rate each session you attended on a scale of 1-5, providing feedback on how useful you found each session.
* **Session Comments**: Provide detailed feedback on the sessions you attended, including what you liked and disliked about them.
* **General Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the conference.
* **Attendee Opinions**: Select any opinions that resonate with you from the list provided.
* **Attendee Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you may have for improving future conferences.
* **Attendee Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about the conference.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
