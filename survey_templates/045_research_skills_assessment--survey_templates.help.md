# Research Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the research skills and capabilities of a research assistant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions truthfully.
2. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
3. Enter any additional comments or questions in the notes or comments fields.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Survey Template**
* **Survey Template** (`survey_template`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the research topic or area you are working on.

### 2. **Research Areas**
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check all the areas of research that apply to your research project. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 3. **Research Methodology**
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`, `select_one`, required): Select the research methodology you are using. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### 4. **Research Skills**
* **Research Skills** (`research_skills`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your research skills or experience.

### 5. **Date**
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you completed this form.

### 6. **Time**
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you completed this form.

### 7. **Notes**
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about this form.

### 8. **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### 9. **Phone**
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### 10. **Research Capabilities**
* **Research Capabilities** (`research_capabilities`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check all the research capabilities that apply to your research project. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### 11. **Comments**
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about this form.

### 12. **Output File**
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the file name or URL of the output file related to your research project.

Tips: 

* Be accurate and honest in your answers.
* Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification.
