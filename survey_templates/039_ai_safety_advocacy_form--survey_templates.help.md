# AI Safety Advocacy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users complete the AI Safety Advocacy Form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the type of information you want to provide, which will be displayed as separate sections. These sections are: Personal Information, Advocacy & Interests, and Engagement Preferences.
2. In the Personal Information section, enter your full name, email address, and country/region where you live.
3. In the Advocacy & Interests section, select all the areas of AI safety that you are interested in (Technical Alignment, Governance and Policy, Ethics and Fairness, Robustness and Interpretability, Biosecurity and AI, and Long-term Risk Mitigation).
4. In the Expertise section, choose your current level of expertise in AI safety (Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert).
5. In the Contributions section, select all the ways you can contribute (Research & Analysis, Community Organizing, Policy & Advocacy, Financial Contributions, Event Support, and Content Creation/Translation).
6. In the Engagement Preferences section, select your preferred communication channels (Email, Slack/Discord, WhatsApp/Telegram, and Monthly Newsletter).
7. If you'd like, you can provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in AI safety advocacy.
8. Finally, choose how you'd like to receive advocacy updates and news (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address. This will be used as the primary contact point.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for events or meetings. This is optional.
* **Country/Region (5)**: Enter the country or region where you reside.
* **Areas of AI Safety Interest (7)**: Select all areas of AI safety that you are interested in.
* **Current Level of Expertise (8)**: Choose your level of expertise in AI safety.
* **Professional Background (9)**: Enter your professional background or current organization, if applicable.
* **Contribution Types (10)**: Select all the ways you'd like to contribute to AI safety.
* **Availability for Meetings/Events (11)**: Select your availability for meetings and events.
* **Preferred Communication Channels (13)**: Select all the communication channels you'd like to use.
* **Interest Reason (14)**: Share a brief explanation of why you are interested in AI safety advocacy. This is optional.
* **Referral Source (15)**: If someone referred you, select how you heard about us.
* **Consent to Updates (16)**: Agree to receive updates and news about AI safety advocacy.
* **Note:**
  - This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes.
  - Be accurate and honest with your responses.
  - If you have any concerns or need clarification, contact [insert contact information].
