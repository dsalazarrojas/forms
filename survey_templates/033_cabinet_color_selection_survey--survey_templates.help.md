# Cabinet Color Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their cabinet color preferences, kitchen design style, and other related aspects. The data collected will be used for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your participant information in the "I am a..." field.
2. Choose your preferred kitchen design style from the list provided.
3. Select your preferred cabinet color for the primary cabinet from the list.
4. If you are interested in a two-tone kitchen, select your preferred accent color from the list.
5. If you have chosen a two-tone kitchen, provide your desired accent color in the text field.
6. Choose your preferred cabinet finish from the list.
7. Select your preferred hardware finish from the list.
8. Choose the desired countertop material to pair with your cabinet colors.
9. Indicate where you get your design inspiration from.
10. Determine how important it is for the color to remain trendy for more than 10 years.
11. Consider whether you would be willing to use a bold color in your kitchen.
12. Provide your email to receive the 2026 Trend Report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I am a...** (`participant_type`, required): This field is used to identify your role or profession. Select your title from the provided options.
* **Preferred Kitchen Design Style** (`design_style`, required): This field asks about your preferred kitchen design style. Choose the style that best fits your vision.
* **Preferred Primary Cabinet Color** (`primary_cabinet_color`, required): This field is used to determine your primary cabinet color preference. Select the color that you most like.
* **Interest in a Two-Tone Kitchen** (`secondary_accent_color`, required): This field asks about your interest in having a two-tone kitchen. Select how interested you are in this design approach.
* **If two-tone, what accent color would you choose** (`accent_color_choice`, optional): If you have chosen a two-tone kitchen, provide your desired accent color in this field.
* **Preferred Cabinet Finish** (`finish_type`, required): This field is used to determine your preferred cabinet finish. Choose the finish that you prefer.
* **Preferred Hardware Finish** (`hardware_finish`, required): This field asks about your preferred hardware finish. Select the finish that you prefer.
* **Desired Countertop Material** (`countertop_pairing`, required): This field is used to determine the desired countertop material to pair with your cabinet colors. Choose the material that you prefer.
* **Where do you get your design inspiration** (`influence_source`, optional): This field asks about where you get your design inspiration from. Select all the sources that apply.
* **How important is it that the color remains trendy for 10 plus years** (`color_longevity`, required): This field is used to determine how important it is for the color to remain trendy for more than 10 years. Choose the option that best fits your perspective.
* **Would you consider a "bold" color for your kitchen** (`bold_color_willingness`, required): This field asks about your willingness to use a bold color in your kitchen. Choose the option that best fits your vision.
* **Share your email to receive our 2026 Trend Report** (`email_for_results`, optional): This field is used to receive the 2026 Trend Report. Enter your email address to get the report.

Note: This form is a one-time use form and not a recurring one. If you have already completed the survey, you will not be able to access it again.
