# Healthcare Climate Impact Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare organizations understand the climate change impacts they face and identify the top priorities for climate action within their organization. It will help in assessing the current state of climate change awareness and action within the organization, and provide insights for developing effective climate action plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer the questions as they relate to your organization.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the most relevant option that best describes your answer.
3. If a question allows for a free-text response, provide a clear and concise answer that best explains your thoughts.
4. For questions that ask for a list of options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is climate change?** (`title`, `text`, required): This question asks you to provide a brief description of what climate change means to your organization.
- **What are the observed impacts of climate change on our organization?** (`climate_change_impacts`, `text`, required): Please describe the observed impacts of climate change on your organization, such as changes in weather patterns, increased frequency of extreme events, or other relevant effects.
- **Are there any other impacts that are important to note?** (`climate_change_impacts_other`, `select_multiple`, required): This question allows you to select all other impacts that are important to your organization, such as economic, social, or cultural effects.
- **Other (please specify)** (`climate_change_impacts_other`, `text`, required): If there are other impacts not listed, please provide a brief description of these impacts.
- **What are the top priorities for climate action in our organization?** (`climate_action_priorities`, `select_one`, required): Select the top priority for climate action in your organization.
- **Other (please specify)** (`climate_action_priority_other`, `text`, required): If none of the options are suitable, please provide a brief description of your priority.
- **What does resilience mean in our organization?** (`climate_action_resilience`, `text`, required): This question asks you to describe how your organization views resilience in the context of climate change.
- **What impact does this action have on our organization?** (`climate_action_impact`, `text`, required): Please describe the impact of the selected action on your organization.
- **What are the key implementation steps for this action?** (`climate_action_implementation`, `text`, required): This question asks you to outline the key steps your organization plans to take to implement the selected action.
- **Do you have an implementation plan for this action?** (`climate_action_implementation_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your organization's current situation regarding implementation plans.
- **Other (please specify)** (`climate_action_implementation_other`, `text`, required): If none of the options are suitable, please provide a brief description of your current situation regarding implementation plans.

## Tips

* Be specific and clear in your answers to help us understand your organization's climate change impacts and actions.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* This survey is designed to help your organization develop effective climate action plans. Your honest and thoughtful responses will help make this possible.
