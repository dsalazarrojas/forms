# Worker Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Information Survey is designed to collect personal and professional details about employees for internal record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
4. Select the date you were hired in the "Date Hired" field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required/optional): This field is for your employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (text, required/optional): This field is for your employee's job title or role in the company.
* **Department** (text, required/optional): This field is for your employee's department or team.
* **Date Hired** (date, required/optional): This field is for the date your employee was hired, in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Employee ID** (number, required/optional): This field is for your employee's unique ID number.
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): This field is for your employee's contact phone number.
