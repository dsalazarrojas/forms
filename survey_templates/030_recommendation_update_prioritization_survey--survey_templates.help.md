# Recommendation Update Prioritization Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Recommendation Update Prioritization Survey is used to collect information on the status of recommendations, update score and priority, and stakeholders involved. This form helps to prioritize and manage recommendations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review the form carefully to avoid duplication of efforts.
3. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Update Prioritization Survey** (`recommendation_update_prioritization_survey`): A text field where you can enter a brief description of the update prioritization survey.
* **Recommendation Title** (`recommendation_title`): Enter the title of the recommendation.
* **Recommendation Description** (`recommendation_description`): Describe the recommendation in detail.
* **Update Score** (`update_score`): Select the update score for the recommendation.
* **Update Priority** (`update_priority`): Select the update priority for the recommendation.
* **Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`): Select all stakeholders involved in the recommendation.
* **Selected Stakeholders** (`selected_stakeholders`): Select the stakeholders that are actually involved in the recommendation.
* **Selected Stakeholders Count** (`selected_stakeholders_count`): Enter the count of selected stakeholders.
* **Total Stakeholders** (`total_stakeholders`): Enter the total count of stakeholders.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments related to the recommendation.
* **Update Status** (`update_status`): Select the update status of the recommendation.
* **Recommendation Status** (`recommendation_status`): Select the status of the recommendation.
* **Update Comments** (`update_comments`): Enter any comments related to the update.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Enter the user who created the recommendation.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Enter the user who updated the recommendation.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): Select the date and time when the recommendation was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Select the date and time when the recommendation was last updated.
