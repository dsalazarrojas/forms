# wedding_venue_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their experience with a wedding venue, including details about the venue and event, check-out status, rental items, comments, contact information, return dates and times, return status, and form submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Venue Details** field with the name and address of the venue.
2. Select the type of event you are attending (e.g., **Wedding**, **Birthday**, or **Company Party**).
3. Check the **Check Out Status** field to confirm if you have checked out the venue.
4. Select the rental items you are using (e.g., **Chairs**, **Tables**, or **Linens**).
5. Add any comments or feedback about your experience at the venue.
6. Provide your **Contact Info** (name, email, phone number) so we can reach out to you.
7. If you have already returned the rented items, fill in the **Return Date** and **Return Time** fields.
8. Confirm the **Return Status** of the items (e.g., **Returned** or **Not Returned**).
9. Fill in the **Comments Returned** field with any additional feedback about the return process.
10. Confirm if this form has been submitted (e.g., **True** or **False**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Venue Details**: The name and address of the venue you are visiting.
* **Event Info**: The type of event you are attending (e.g., **Wedding**, **Birthday**, or **Company Party**).
* **Check Out Status**: Confirm if you have checked out the venue.
* **Rental Items**: Select the items you are renting (e.g., **Chairs**, **Tables**, or **Linens**).
* **Comments**: Add any additional feedback or comments about your experience at the venue.
* **Contact Info**: Your name, email, and phone number.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Return Date**: The date you returned the rented items (if applicable).
* **Return Time**: The time you returned the rented items (if applicable).
* **Return Status**: Confirm if you have returned the rented items (e.g., **Returned** or **Not Returned**).
* **Comments Returned**: Add any additional feedback about the return process.
* **Contact Person**: The person you would like to contact about your experience.
* **Contact Number**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Comments 2**: Add any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Form Submitted**: Confirm if this form has been submitted (e.g., **True** or **False**).

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields to submit the form correctly.
* If you have already returned the rented items, please fill in the corresponding fields (Return Date, Return Time, and Return Status).
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please add them in the Comments field.
