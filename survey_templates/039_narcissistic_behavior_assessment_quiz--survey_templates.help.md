# Narcissistic Behavior Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This assessment is designed to help you understand your level of narcissistic behavior. Narcissistic behavior refers to a pattern of grandiosity, lack of empathy, and a tendency to exploit or manipulate others for personal gain. This form will ask you to rate your level of agreement with certain statements related to narcissistic behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with numerical scales (e.g., 1-10), select the number that best represents your level of agreement.
3. For the "Submit" question, select one of the provided options to express your satisfaction with the response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **grandiosity** (Scale 1, Required): This question asks you to rate how often you engage in behaviors that make you feel superior or more important than others.
* **need_for_sympathy** (Scale 2, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you seek sympathy or attention from others.
* **authority** (Scale 3, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel like you deserve to be in charge or in positions of power.
* **preoccupation_with_success** (Scale 4, Required): This question asks you to rate how often you focus on achieving success or being successful.
* **self_admiration** (Scale 5, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you admire or praise yourself.
* **reaction_to_criticism** (Scale 6, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you react negatively to criticism or feedback.
* **attention-seeking** (Scale 7, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you engage in behaviors that draw attention to yourself.
* **manipulativeness** (Scale 8, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you use manipulation or emotional coercion to get what you want.
* **entitlement** (Scale 9, Required): This question asks you to rate how often you believe you are entitled to special treatment or privileges.
* **sense_of_emptiness** (Scale 10, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel empty or unfulfilled inside.
* **lack_of_confidence** (Scale 11, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you lack confidence or feel uncertain about your abilities.
* **self_centeredness** (Scale 12, Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel like you are the center of attention.
* **exaggerated_expectations** (Scale 13, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you have unrealistic expectations for yourself or others.
* **reaction_to_failure** (Scale 14, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you react negatively to failure or setbacks.
* **exploitativeness** (Scale 15, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you engage in behaviors that exploit or manipulate others for personal gain.
* **lack_of_empathy** (Scale 16, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you have difficulty understanding or relating to others' feelings or needs.
* **enviousness** (Scale 17, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel envious or resentful towards others.
* **envy** (Scale 18, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel envious or resentful towards others' successes or possessions.
* **arrogance** (Scale 19, Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel superior to others or believe you are better than everyone else.
* **sense_of_achievement** (Scale 20, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you feel a sense of accomplishment or pride in your achievements.
* **grandiosity_with_dangerous_act** (Scale 21, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you engage in behaviors that are reckless or impulsive.
* **lack_of_emotion** (Scale 22, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you have difficulty experiencing or expressing emotions.
* **lack_of_self_control** (Scale 23, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you struggle with managing your impulses or emotions.
* **Submit** (Scale 24, Required): Select one of the provided options to express your satisfaction with the response.
* **grandiosity_with_dangerous_act** (Scale 25, Not Required): This question asks you to rate how often you engage in behaviors that are reckless or impulsive.
 
Note: The questions are based on the Hirschberg Narcissism Scale, a widely used measure of narcissistic behavior.
