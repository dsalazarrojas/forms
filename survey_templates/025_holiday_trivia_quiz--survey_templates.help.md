# Holiday Trivia Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Holiday Trivia Quiz form is a fun and interactive way to test your knowledge about various holidays and celebrations. By completing this form, you'll be presented with questions about different holidays, and you'll have the chance to showcase your knowledge and learn something new.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Holiday Trivia Quiz form, follow these steps:

* Review the questions carefully and choose the correct answers for each one.
* Make sure to select all applicable options for questions that allow multiple answers.
* For questions with a select_one type, choose only one answer.
* For questions with a select_multiple type, choose multiple answers if applicable.
* You can skip questions if you are not familiar with the holiday or the answer.
* Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.
