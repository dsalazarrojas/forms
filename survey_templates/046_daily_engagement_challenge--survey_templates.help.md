# Daily Engagement Challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on participant engagement and reflection on a challenge or program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
2. Enter the participant's reflection date in the "participant_reflection_date" field.
3. Provide a brief text description in the "participant_reflection_text" field.
4. Select the participant's current status in the "participant_reflection_status" field.
5. Enter the challenge start and end dates in the "challenge_start_date" and "challenge_end_date" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the number of participants in the "participant_count" field.
7. Provide any additional comments in the "coach_reflection_note" field (if applicable).
8. Enter the program name, location, and facilitators' names in the "program_name", "program_location", and "program_facilitators" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Engagement Challenge Form** (`daily_engagement_challenge_form`): This is the main form field where you can enter your response.
* **Participant Reflection Date** (`participant_reflection_date`): Enter the date of your participant's reflection.
* **Participant Reflection Text** (`participant_reflection_text`): Provide a brief text description of your participant's reflection.
* **Participant Reflection Status** (`participant_reflection_status`): Select your participant's current status (Active or Inactive).
* **Participant Reflection Status Note** (`participant_reflection_status_note`): Provide any additional notes on your participant's status (if applicable).
* **Challenge Start Date** (`challenge_start_date`): Enter the start date of the challenge.
* **Challenge End Date** (`challenge_end_date`): Enter the end date of the challenge.
* **Coach Reflection Note** (`coach_reflection_note`): Provide any additional comments on your participant's engagement (if applicable).
* **Participant Count** (`participant_count`): Enter the number of participants in the challenge.
* **Challenge Duration** (`challenge_duration`): Provide the duration of the challenge (optional).
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the program.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`): Enter the location of the program.
* **Program Facilitators** (`program_facilitators`): Enter the names of the facilitators of the program.
* **Participant Demographics** (`participant_demographics`): Provide any additional demographics information on your participant (if applicable).
* **Participant Contact Info** (`participant_contact_info`): Provide any additional contact information on your participant (if applicable).
* **Participant Organizers** (`participant_organizers`): Enter the names of the program organizers.
* **Participant Supervisors** (`participant_supervisors`): Enter the names of the supervisors.
* **Participant Facilitators** (`participant_facilitators`): Enter the names of the facilitators.
* **Participant Supports** (`participant_supports`): Provide any additional supports information on your participant (if applicable).
* **Participant Partners** (`participant_partners`): Enter the names of the participant's partners.
* **Participant Reflection Photo** (`participant_reflection_photo`): Provide any additional photo information on your participant's reflection (if applicable).
* **Participant Reflection Audio** (`participant_reflection_audio`): Provide any additional audio information on your participant's reflection (if applicable).
* **Participant Reflection Video** (`participant_reflection_video`): Provide any additional video information on your participant's reflection (if applicable).
* **Participant Reflection Other** (`participant_reflection_other`): Provide any additional information on your participant's reflection (if applicable).
* **Participant Reflection Audio Other** (`participant_reflection_audio_other`): Provide any additional audio information on your participant's reflection (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the appropriate fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or facilitator for guidance.
