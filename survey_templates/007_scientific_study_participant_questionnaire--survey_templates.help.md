# scientific_study_participant_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect demographic and socioeconomic data from participants in a scientific study.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please contact the study administrator for guidance.
4. Once you have completed the form, click on the submit button to send it to the study administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_screening_details** (Text, required: False): Please provide a brief description of how you were screened for the study.
* **participant_consent** (Note, required: False): Please indicate if you have given consent to participate in the study.
* **screening_status** (Select One, required: False): Please select the status of your screening (Active or Inactive).
* **demographic_details** (Text, required: False): Please provide any additional demographic details about yourself.
* **participant_age_group** (Select One, required: False): Please select your age group (Yes or No).
* **participant_gender** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your gender (Yes or No).
* **participant_education** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your education level (Yes or No).
* **participant_language** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your language (Yes or No).
* **participant_marital_status** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your marital status (Active or Inactive).
* **participant_ethnicity** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your ethnicity (Yes or No).
* **participant_residence_area** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your residence area (Yes or No).
* **participant_employment_status** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **participant_relationship_status** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your relationship status (Active or Inactive).
* **participant_income_group** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your income group (Yes or No).
* **participant_income_source** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your income source (Yes or No).
* **participant_family_income** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your family income (Yes or No).
* **participant_employment_type** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your employment type (Yes or No).
* **participant_education_level** (Select Multiple, required: False): Please select your education level (High or Low).
* **participant_employment_years** (Number, required: False): Please enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **participant_employment_years_education** (Number, required: False): Please enter the number of years you have been employed and educated.
* **participant_years_living_with_partner** (Number, required: False): Please enter the number of years you have been living with your partner.
* **participant_children_count** (Number, required: False): Please enter the number of children you have.
* **participant_children_age** (Number, required: False): Please enter the age of your children.
* **participant_income** (Number, required: False): Please enter your monthly income.
* **participant_comments** (Note, required: False): Please provide any additional comments you would like to make about your participation in the study.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact the study administrator for guidance.
* Please ensure that all information is kept confidential and used solely for research purposes.
