# Childhood Trauma Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childhood Trauma Survey is a series of questions designed to collect information on your experiences and perceptions of childhood trauma. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability. Your participation is crucial in helping us better understand the impact of childhood trauma on individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions on each page (indicated by `--`). You may take breaks if needed.
2. For select_one questions, choose one answer that best describes your response.
3. For select_multiple questions, select all that apply.
4. For select_one questions with numerical options, rate your response using the numbers provided.
5. For select_multiple questions with numerical options, select all that apply.
6. You may leave additional comments or concerns in the text field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent Information** (`Respondent ID Number`, `text`, required/optional): This is your unique ID number for confidentiality purposes only. You are free to provide any name or alias you are comfortable with.
- **Respondent ID Number** (`Respondent ID Number`, `text`, required): This is a required field and will help us track your responses.
- **Age Group** (`18-25`, `select_one`, required): Please select the age group you identify with.
- **Gender Identity** (`Male`, `Female`, `Non-binary`, `Other`, `Prefer not to say`, select_one, required/optional): This is a self-identification question.
- **Trauma Exposure Overview** (`-- Trauma Exposure Overview --`, `note`, required/optional): This is a space for you to provide any relevant information on your trauma experiences.
- **Have you experienced trauma during childhood** (`Yes`, `No`, `Unsure`, select_one, required): Please answer this question honestly.
- **Type of trauma experienced** (`Physical abuse`, `Emotional abuse`, `Neglect`, `Sexual abuse`, `Domestic violence`, `Loss or grief`, `Accident or injury`, `Natural disaster`, `Community violence`, `Medical trauma`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply.
- **Approximate duration of trauma** (`Single incident`, `Few weeks`, `Few months`, `1-2 years`, `Multiple years`, select_one, required/optional): This is a question to help us understand the duration of your trauma.
- **About the Trauma** (`-- About the Trauma --`, `note`, required/optional): This is a space for you to provide any additional information on your trauma experiences.
- **Relationship of the perpetrator to you** (`Family member`, `Teacher or authority figure`, `Peer or friend`, `Stranger`, `Not applicable`, select_one, required/optional): Please answer this question carefully.
- **Was the trauma reported to authorities** (`Yes, it was reported`, `No, it was not reported`, `Unsure`, select_one, required/optional): This is a question to help us understand if the trauma was reported.
- **Impact on Life** (`-- Impact on Life --`, `note`, required/optional): This is a space for you to provide any information on how the trauma has affected your life.
- **Emotional impact of childhood trauma** (`1 (no impact)`, `2 (mild impact)`, `3 (moderate impact)`, `4 (significant impact)`, `5 (severe impact)`, number, required): Please rate your emotional impact.
- **Current symptoms experienced** (`Anxiety`, `Depression`, `Nightmares`, `Flashbacks`, `Difficulty concentrating`, `Sleep problems`, `Trust issues`, `Low self-esteem`, `Avoidance behaviors`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply.
- **Impact on daily functioning** (`1 (no impact)`, `2 (mild impact)`, `3 (moderate impact)`, `4 (significant impact)`, `5 (severe impact)`, number, required): Please rate your impact on daily functioning.
- **Impact on personal relationships** (`1 (no impact)`, `2 (mild impact)`, `3 (moderate impact)`, `4 (significant impact)`, `5 (severe impact)`, number, required): Please rate your impact on personal relationships.
- **Recovery and Support** (`-- Recovery and Support --`, `note`, required/optional): This is a space for you to provide any additional information on your recovery and support processes.
- **Have you sought professional help** (`Yes, currently`, `Yes, in the past`, `No`, select_one, required): Please answer this question honestly.
- **Types of treatment received** (`Therapy or counseling`, `Psychiatric medication`, `Support groups`, `Hospitalization`, `Other`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all that apply.
- **Level of recovery or healing** (`1 (no progress)`, `2 (mild progress)`, `3 (moderate progress)`, `4 (significant progress)`, `5 (significant progress)`, number, required): Please rate your level of recovery or healing.
- **Do you have supportive relationships** (`Yes`, `No`, `Somewhat`, select_one, required/optional): Please answer this question honestly.
- **Additional comments or concerns** (`-- Additional comments or concerns --`, `text`, required/optional): This is a space for you to provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability. If you need help or support, please contact us at any time.
