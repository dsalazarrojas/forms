# explore_your_family_heritage - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you explore and understand your family's past, including their origins, events, and relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by sharing your family history on page 1.
2. Move on to page 2 to select whether or not your family has a tree structure.
3. On page 3, choose your ancestral origins from the options provided.
4. If you have family events you'd like to share, move on to page 4 and 5.
5. On page 4, select the type of event you'd like to report (Yes/No).
6. If you reported an event, provide the date on page 5.
7. If you reported an event, provide the time on page 6.
8. Move on to page 7 to describe the location of the event.
9. On page 8, provide a description of the event.
10. On page 9, select which family members you have a relationship with (Yes/No).
11. If you have a relationship with a family member, provide their name on page 10.
12. Finally, on page 11, describe your relationship with the family member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **share_your_family_history** (Page 1): A field to share your family's overall history.
* **family_tree** (Page 2): Select whether or not your family has a tree structure.
* **ancestral_origins** (Page 3): Choose your ancestral origins from the options provided.
* **family_events** (Page 4): Select whether or not you'd like to report a family event.
* **date_of_event** (Page 5): If you reported an event, provide the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **time_of_event** (Page 6): If you reported an event, provide the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 03:45 PM).
* **location_of_event** (Page 7): Describe the location of the event.
* **event_description** (Page 8): Provide a detailed description of the event.
* **family_members** (Page 9): Select which family members you have a relationship with.
* **first_name** (Page 10): Provide the first name of the family member you selected.
* **last_name** (Page 11): Provide the last name of the family member you selected.
* **relationship_to_you** (Page 12): Describe your relationship with the family member.
