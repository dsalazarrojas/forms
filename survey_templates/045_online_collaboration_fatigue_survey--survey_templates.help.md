# Online Collaboration Fatigue Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and perceptions of virtual meetings. It aims to identify potential areas for improvement in virtual meeting organization, duration, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answer that best reflects your experiences with virtual meetings. Please answer each question honestly, and do not feel obligated to complete the entire form if you have participated in no virtual meetings in the past week.
2. The form consists of 10 questions, each with a specific question or statement related to virtual meetings.
3. Use the provided options to choose the best answer for each question.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Meeting Fatigue Questions**: This question is not actually part of the form, but rather a placeholder for the section that follows.
* **How often do you have virtual meetings?** (`virtual_meeting_frequency`, select_one, not required): How many virtual meetings you have participated in recently. Choose an option that reflects your typical frequency of participating in virtual meetings.
	+ Daily: You have participated in at least one virtual meeting per day.
	+ Several times a week: You have participated in 2-3 virtual meetings per week.
	+ Several times a month: You have participated in 1-2 virtual meetings per month.
	+ Rarely: You have not participated in any virtual meetings recently.
* **What is the length of your virtual meetings in minutes?** (`meeting_duration`, number, not required): Please enter the average length of your virtual meetings in minutes.
* **How would you rate the quality of your virtual meetings?** (`meeting_quality`, select_one, not required): Rate the quality of your virtual meetings on a scale from High to Low.
	+ High: You generally find virtual meetings to be engaging and productive.
	+ Medium: You find virtual meetings to be somewhat engaging and productive, but with some room for improvement.
	+ Low: You find virtual meetings to be less engaging and less productive.
* **How often do you experience collaboration fatigue during virtual meetings?** (`collaboration_fatigue`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all that apply to the frequency with which you experience collaboration fatigue during virtual meetings.
	+ Frequently: You often feel overwhelmed or fatigued during virtual meetings.
	+ Sometimes: You occasionally feel overwhelmed or fatigued during virtual meetings.
	+ Rarely: You rarely feel overwhelmed or fatigued during virtual meetings.
* **How does virtual meeting fatigue impact your productivity?** (`productivity_impact`, text, not required): Describe how virtual meeting fatigue affects your productivity in a few sentences.
* **How does collaboration fatigue impact your satisfaction level during virtual meetings?** (`satisfaction_level`, text, not required): Describe how collaboration fatigue affects your satisfaction level during virtual meetings.
* **How often do you submit virtual meeting minutes?** (`submission_frequency`, select_one, not required): Choose the frequency with which you submit virtual meeting minutes.
	+ Always: You submit virtual meeting minutes regularly.
	+ Most of the time: You submit virtual meeting minutes most of the time.
	+ Sometimes: You submit virtual meeting minutes occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You rarely submit virtual meeting minutes.
* **How do you submit virtual meeting minutes?** (`submission_method`, select_one, not required): Choose the method you use to submit virtual meeting minutes.
	+ Email: You submit virtual meeting minutes via email.
	+ Cloud-based platform: You use a cloud-based platform to submit virtual meeting minutes.
	+ Collaboration software: You use collaboration software to submit virtual meeting minutes.
	+ Other: Specify another method in the comments.
* **How satisfied are you with virtual meetings?** (`collaboration_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Rate your level of satisfaction with virtual meetings.
	+ Very satisfied: You are generally very satisfied with virtual meetings.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat satisfied with virtual meetings.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about virtual meetings.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat dissatisfied with virtual meetings.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with virtual meetings.
