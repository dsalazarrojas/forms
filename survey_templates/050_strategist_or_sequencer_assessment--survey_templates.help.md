# Strategist Or Sequencer Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the characteristics and preferences of an individual, likely a project manager or strategist, to help with resource allocation or team composition decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide the name of your company in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Describe your current job in the "Job Description" field.
6. Select your job status (High, Medium, or Low) in the "Job Status" field.
7. Rate your job satisfaction level (Very High, High, Medium, Low) in the "Job Satisfaction Level" field.
8. Answer the questions about your planning style (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not At All Important) in the "Planning Style 1" and "Planning Style 2" fields.
9. Choose your problem-solving style (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, Not At All Effective) in the "Problem Solving Style 1" and "Problem Solving Style 2" fields.
10. Select the time-saving options (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not At All Important) in the "Time Saving" field.
11. Choose the importance of your job (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not At All Important) in the "Job Importance" field.
12. Rate the urgency of your job (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not At All Important) in the "Job Urgency" field.
13. Select the frequency of your job (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not At All Important) in the "Job Frequency" field.
14. Enter the time required to complete your job in the "Time Required" field (in HH:MM format).
15. Enter the start and end times of your job in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields (in 24-hour format).
16. Choose the date you completed this assessment (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
17. Enter any comments about your current job or role.
18. Rate your job satisfaction (Very High, High, Medium, Low).
19. Enter the name of the person who submitted this assessment.
