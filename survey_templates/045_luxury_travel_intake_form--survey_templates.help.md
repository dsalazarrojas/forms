# Luxury Travel Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from clients about their luxury travel preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the general information section with your basic details.
2. Select your travel preferences from the options provided.
3. Enter your travel dates and time.
4. Provide your contact information.
5. Enter your travel agency details, including their contact information and notes.
6. Enter your client information and notes.
7. Select your travel type, length, and currency.
8. Enter the travel agency's rate.
