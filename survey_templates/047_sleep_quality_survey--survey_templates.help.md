# sleep_quality_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your sleep habits, patterns, and quality. It will help collect data on your sleep behaviors, including your sleep schedules, wake-up times, and self-assessed sleep quality. The information collected will be used to analyze and understand individual sleep patterns and potential risks or benefits associated with sleep habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as prompted.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
3. Review and adjust your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**: This field is for capturing any additional comments or information you'd like to add to your answers. It's an optional field.
* **Sleep Patterns**: Please describe your typical sleep patterns, including your bedtime and wake-up times, and any unusual sleep-related behaviors.
* **Sleep Quality (Sleep Quality)**: Describe your self-assessed sleep quality, including how well-rested, refreshed, and alert you feel after waking up.
* **Sleep Habits (Sleep Habits)**: Share information about your daily routines that may influence your sleep quality, such as exercise, diet, or relaxation techniques.
* **Sleep Patterns Frequency (Sleep Patterns Frequency)**: Specify how often you usually follow your typical sleep patterns.
* **Wake-up Time**: Provide your usual wake-up time, including AM and PM hours.
* **Wake-up Time Duration (Wake-up Time Duration)**: Describe how long you usually sleep after waking up.
* **Sleep Quality Scale (Sleep Quality Scale)**: Choose a score that best represents your self-assessed sleep quality.
* **Sleep Quality Score (Sleep Quality Score)**: Rate your sleep quality on a scale.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: This field is for providing more insight or context about your sleep habits.
* **Sleep Quality Risk Factors (Sleep Quality Risk Factors)**: Identify any factors that you think may affect your sleep quality.
* **Sleep Quality Intervention (Sleep Quality Intervention)**: Describe any actions you take or plan to take to improve your sleep quality.
* **Sleep Quality Goals (Sleep Quality Goals)**: Set goals for improving your sleep quality.
* **Sleep Quality Outcomes (Sleep Quality Outcomes)**: Share any positive or negative outcomes you've experienced due to changes in your sleep habits.
