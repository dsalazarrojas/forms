# tv_ad_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on viewer demographics, ad characteristics, and viewer feedback to conduct research on television advertisements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately based on your knowledge or research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is this survey for** (Note, id: 1)
* A short description of the survey's purpose and goal.

### 2. **Viewers demographics** (id: 2)
* This field is a free-text field for describing viewer demographics.

### 3. **Ad characteristics** (id: 3)
* Select all characteristics that apply to the ad:
	+ Length
	+ Music
	+ Color

### 4. **Ad effects** (id: 4)
* Select the effect the ad had:
	+ Appealing
	+ Repulsive
	+ Neutral

### 5. **Ad location** (id: 5)
* Select where the ad was placed:
	+ Top
	+ Middle
	+ Bottom

### 6. **Ad time** (id: 6)
* Enter the time the ad was shown.

### 7. **Ad repetition** (id: 7)
* Enter the number of times the ad was shown.

### 8. **TV channel** (id: 8)
* Select the TV channel where the ad was seen:
	+ ABC
	+ CBS
	+ NBC

### 9. **TV show** (id: 9)
* Enter the name of the TV show.

### 10. **Ad rating** (id: 10)
* Enter the ad's rating.

### 11. **Viewer feedback** (id: 11)
* Select the viewer's feedback on the ad:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate in your answers to provide valuable insights for the research.
* If you are unsure of an answer, try to provide the best effort based on your knowledge or research.
