# Parent Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Engagement Survey is designed to gauge the level of engagement of parents or guardians with their child's school. This survey will help educators and administrators understand how parents interact with the school, their level of involvement, and areas where they may need more support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Parent Engagement Survey, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each question indicating your level of engagement with the school.
2. For questions with a "select_multiple" type, select all that apply.
3. For questions with a "text" type, provide a brief explanation or answer in the space provided.
4. For questions with a "time" type, indicate the frequency or duration of your engagement with the school.
5. Be accurate and honest when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Involvement** (`parent_engagement_survey.1`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks if you have been involved in school events, activities, or meetings.
* **Teacher Support** (`parent_engagement_survey.2`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks if you feel supported by your child's teacher or teachers.
* **Parent-School Communication** (`parent_engagement_survey.3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks about your level of communication with the school, including emails, phone calls, or meetings.
* **Educational Goals** (`parent_engagement_survey.4`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your goals for your child's education.
* **Survey Length** (`parent_engagement_survey.5`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks about how long you take to complete the survey.
* **School Climate** (`parent_engagement_survey.6`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks about your perception of the school's climate (1-10).
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_engagement_survey.7`, `note`, required/optional): This question asks for any additional comments or concerns you have about your level of involvement with the school.
* **Parent Attendance** (`parent_engagement_survey.8`, `number`, required/optional): This question asks about your frequency of attending school events or activities.
* **Parent Participation** (`parent_engagement_survey.9`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks if you participate in school events or activities.
* **Educational Background** (`parent_engagement_survey.10`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks about your educational background.
* **Parent Involvement Frequency** (`parent_engagement_survey.11`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your frequency of involvement with your child's education.
* **Survey Length** (`parent_engagement_survey.12`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks about how long you take to complete the survey.
* **Survey Frequency** (`parent_engagement_survey.13`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks about how often you participate in surveys like this.
* **Parent Communication** (`parent_engagement_survey.14`, `time`, required/optional): This question asks about your frequency of communication with the school.
* **Climate** (`parent_engagement_survey.15`, `text`, required/optional): This question asks about your perception of the school's climate.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to answer each question thoroughly.
* If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to your child's teacher or school administrator.
* If you need help filling out the form, don't hesitate to ask.
