# Environmental Lifestyle Choices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to understand your habits and behaviors towards sustainable living practices. The information collected will help to improve the understanding of sustainable living practices and provide insights for educational and research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the introduction and instructions carefully before starting the survey.
2. Review each section and question carefully, and answer them based on your actual experiences and preferences.
3. Select the relevant options for each question.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.
