# DIY Beauty Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DIY Beauty Practice Survey is designed to gather information about users' at-home beauty routines, habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and opinions.
3. Fill out the form in one sitting, or save your progress and come back later.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **What is your age group?** (`title`, `number`, optional): This question helps us understand your demographic characteristics.
* **How many days per week do you practice beauty routines?** (`beauty_practice_days`, `number`, optional): This question gauges your frequency of practice.
* **Which of the following frequencies do you practice beauty routines?** (`routine_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options that best fits your frequency of practice.
### Page 2: Habits and Preferences
* **Which beauty products do you use?** (`products_used`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the products you use for your beauty routines.
* **What motivates you to practice beauty routines?** (`practice_motivations`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reasons that drive your practice.
* **What are some common challenges you face with at-home beauty routines?** (`challenges`, `select_multiple`, optional): Identify the challenges you commonly face.
* **On a scale from 1 to 10, how satisfied are you with your current at-home beauty routine?** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with your current routine.
### Page 3: Products and Interests
* **What's your favorite beauty product?** (`favorite_beauty_product`, `text`, optional): Describe your favorite beauty product.
* **What type of beauty product is your favorite?** (`favorite_beauty_product_category`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the category of your favorite product.
* **What are your interests in the beauty industry?** (`beauty_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the areas you're interested in.
### Page 4: Frequency and Satisfaction
* **How often do you change your at-home beauty routine?** (`practice_frequency_change`, `select_one`, optional): Describe your frequency of change.
* **On a scale from 1 to 10, how satisfied are you with the frequency of your at-home beauty practice?** (`practice_frequency_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the frequency.
* **How often do you practice at-home beauty routines?** (`practice_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options that best fits your frequency.
* **What's your favorite beauty brand?** (`favorite_beauty_brand`, `text`, optional): Describe your favorite beauty brand.
### Page 5: Influences and Motivations
* **On a scale from 1 to 10, how satisfied are you with your at-home beauty practice overall?** (`practice_satisfaction_level`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction.
* **What drives your at-home beauty practice?** (`practice_mission`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reasons that drive your practice.
* **How aware are you of your at-home beauty practices' impact on your skin health?** (`practice_awareness`, `number`, optional): Rate your awareness of skin health impact.
* **Who influences your at-home beauty practice?** (`practice_influence`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the influencer types.
* **How often are you influenced by online beauty content?** (`practice_influence`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options that best fits your online influence.
### Page 6: Final Questions
* **What motivates your at-home beauty practice?** (`practice_motivation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the motivations that drive your practice.
* **How often do you change your at-home beauty routine?** (`practice_frequency_change`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the options that best fits your frequency change.
* **On a scale from 1 to 10, how satisfied are you with the impact of your at-home beauty practice on your mood?** (`practice_mood_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the impact on your mood.
* **What's your favorite beauty channel?** (`favorite_beauty_channel`, `text`, optional): Describe your favorite beauty channel.

Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
