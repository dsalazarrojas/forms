# needs_assessments_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your department's needs assessment. This includes answering questions about the main goals and objectives of your department, potential risks or barriers, expected outcomes, and other important information. This data will help us better understand your department's needs and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the relevant options for each question. If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are the main goals and objectives of your organization?** (`needs_assessment_questions`, `text`, required): This question is asking for a brief description of the primary goals and objectives of your department. Please provide a clear and concise answer that summarizes your department's main objectives.
* **What are some potential risks or barriers that you see as challenges?** (`needs_assessment_questions2`, `text`, required): This question is asking for any potential obstacles or difficulties that your department might face. Be as specific as possible and provide examples or details to support your answer.
* **What are the expected outcomes or benefits from this project?** (`needs_assessment_questions3`, `text`, required): This question is asking for the positive results or advantages that your department hopes to achieve from this project. Clearly outline what you expect to gain or accomplish.
* **What are some other important information that we should know about?** (`needs_assessment_questions4`, `text`, required): This question is asking for any additional details or context that might be helpful for us to understand your department's needs. Be as thorough as possible.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and title as the primary contact person for your department.
