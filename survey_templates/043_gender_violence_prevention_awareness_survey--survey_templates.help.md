# Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey is a tool designed to gather data and raise awareness about gender violence prevention. This survey aims to collect information on the experiences and perceptions of individuals regarding gender violence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the survey.
2. Each question is divided into sections, and you can navigate between them using the navigation buttons.
3. Read each question carefully and provide honest and accurate responses.
4. You can skip optional questions if you do not wish to provide the information.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, click on the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **What Is Gender**: Please select the gender that you identify with.
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Have You Experienced Violence**: Please select all types of violence that you have experienced. You can select multiple options.
	+ Physical
	+ Emotional
	+ Verbal
	+ Sexual
* **Location**: Enter the location where you have experienced violence.
* **Date**: Enter the date when you experienced violence.
* **Time**: Enter the time of day when you experienced violence.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Can You Consent**: Please select whether you can consent to participate in this survey.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Comments**: If you have any comments or feedback, please enter them here (optional).

## Tips
* Please provide honest and accurate responses.
* You can skip optional questions if you do not wish to provide the information.
* If you experience any difficulties or have questions, please contact your survey administrator.
