# Participant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on various aspects of a project, workshop, or event. The form includes questions and fields to gather detailed comments and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the field labeled "Participant Name".
2. Provide your **Participant Email** address in the field labeled "Participant Email" (optional).
3. Answer **Feedback Question 1**, **Feedback Question 2**, **Feedback Question 3**, and **Feedback Question 4** with your thoughts and opinions.
4. Share any **Additional Feedback** you may have.
5. Rate your overall experience by selecting a **Participant Rating** from 1-5.
6. Select your level of agreement with the statement "This project/event was a great experience" by choosing **Participant Recommend** from the options provided.
7. Choose whether you **Will Recommend** this project/event to others by selecting one or more options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or to receive further information.
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions on the project's relevance to your goals and objectives.
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional): Describe how the project or event met or did not meet your expectations.
* **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question_3`, text, optional): Provide any feedback on the project's organization and management.
* **Feedback Question 4** (`feedback_question_4`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback on other aspects of the project.
* **Participant Comment** (`participant_comment`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Participant Recommend** (`participant_recommend`, select_one, optional): Select your level of agreement with the statement "This project/event was a great experience".
* **Participant Will Recommend** (`participant_will_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you will recommend this project/event to others, selecting one or more options.
