# Retail Inventory Management Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate and assess the effectiveness of the retail inventory management practices across different stores. It will help identify strengths and weaknesses, which can be used to improve the overall management and reduce losses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for each question based on your store's practices.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Enter the date when you completed the form to track the progress.
4. Repeat the form for multiple stores if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_location**: Select all locations where you have stores (Yes/No)
  This field is asking about the different locations of stores you have. 
  Please select all locations that apply.
* **store_name**: Enter the name of your store.
  This field is asking you to enter the name of your store. 
  Please enter the exact name as it appears on the store's signage or documentation.
* **inventory_practices**: Select if your store implements inventory management practices (Yes/No)
  This field is asking about your store's inventory practices. 
  Please select 'Yes' if your store has implemented inventory practices.
* **inventory_control**: Select the level of inventory control your store has (Yes/No)
  This field is asking about the level of inventory control your store has.
