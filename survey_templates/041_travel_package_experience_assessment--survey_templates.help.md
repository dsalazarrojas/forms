# Travel Package Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment on a travel package, which is a collection of travel services and amenities that can be customized to suit a traveler's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all the required fields. Some fields are marked as optional, but please fill them in if you have any information or opinions to provide.
2. Select the relevant answers for each field based on your experience with the travel package.
3. Provide detailed answers in the text fields, such as "Rating Reason" and "Travel Agent Comment".
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the package you are providing feedback on.
* **Package Rating** (2, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to rate your experience with the package: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Rating Reason** (3, text, required): Provide a reason for your rating.
* **Overall Rating** (4, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the package on a numerical scale.
* **How satisfied are you with the package?** (5, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your level of satisfaction with the package: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **What could be improved?** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to what could be improved with the package: Quality of the package, Quality of the service, Quality of the experience, Timing of the package, or Other.
* **Travel Agency** (7, text, required): Enter the name of the travel agency that provided the package.
* **How satisfied are you with the travel agency?** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your level of satisfaction with the travel agency: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Travel Agent** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the travel agent who assisted you with the package.
* **How satisfied are you with the travel agent?** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your level of satisfaction with the travel agent: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Rate the travel agent** (11, number, required): Rate your experience with the travel agent on a numerical scale.
* **Price** (12, text, required): Enter your experience with the price of the package.
* **Satisfaction with the price** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your level of satisfaction with the price: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Comparability of prices** (14, text, required): Enter your experience with the price comparability of the package.
* **What would make you book this package again?** (15, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply: True, Maybe, or False.
* **Would you recommend this package?** (16, select_one, required): Select one of the available options: True, False, or Maybe.
* **Why would you or wouldn't recommend this package?** (17, text, required): Enter your reason for recommending or not recommending the package.
* **What type of traveler are you?** (18, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your type of traveler: Adventure Traveler, Family Traveler, Food and Wine Traveler, Luxury Traveler, Budget Traveler, or Other.
* **How often do you travel?** (19, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to your travel frequency: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Please provide detailed answers in the text fields.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Other" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form. Your feedback is valuable to us!
