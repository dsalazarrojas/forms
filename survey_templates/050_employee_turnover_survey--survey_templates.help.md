# employee_turnover_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Employee Turnover Survey form effectively and accurately. Please read through the guide to familiarize yourself with the form's purpose, fields, and instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by carefully reading the guide to understand the form's purpose and the questions being asked.
- Ensure you have all necessary information and authorization to complete the form.
- Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (1, number, not required): 
  Enter the ID number of the employee who left the company.
- **Supervisor Name** (2, text, not required): 
  Enter the name of the supervisor or manager who was directly responsible for the employee's departure.
- **Department** (3, text, not required): 
  Enter the department of the employee who left the company.
- **Reason for Leaving** (4, text, not required): 
  Briefly describe the reason why the employee left the company.
- **Job Performance** (5, select_one, not required): 
  Select one of the options provided to rate the employee's job performance.
  Options: 
  - Option 1: Excellent
  - Option 2: Average
  - Option 3: Poor
- **Job Satisfaction** (6, select_multiple, not required): 
  Select all that apply to rate the employee's job satisfaction.
  Options: 
  - Option 1: Excellent
  - Option 2: Average
  - Option 3: Poor
- **Manager Relationship** (7, text, not required): 
  Describe the relationship between the employee and their manager.
- **Communication** (8, number, not required): 
  Rate the quality of communication between the employee and their team/manager.
- **Job Security** (9, number, not required): 
  Rate the level of job security the employee felt while working with the company.
- **Work-life Balance** (10, number, not required): 
  Rate the level of work-life balance the employee had while working with the company.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or seek help from the relevant department.
- Remember that your feedback is essential to us, and it will contribute to our growth and improvement.
