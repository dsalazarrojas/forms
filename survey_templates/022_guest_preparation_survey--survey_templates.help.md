# Guest Preparation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form, "Guest Preparation Survey," is used to collect essential information about guests visiting your establishment. This includes details about their arrival and departure dates, preferred accommodations, special requests, and contact information. The goal is to gather all necessary details to provide a comfortable stay for the guests and to ensure a smooth interaction between the staff and the guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and fill in the requested information for each section.
2.  Enter your name and contact information if required.
3.  Select your preferred arrival date and time.
4.  Choose your preferred accommodations.
5.  List any special requests you may have.
6.  Enter your contact information (phone number or email) so that we can contact you if needed.
7.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Guest Arrival Details

*   **Guest Arrival Details** (`guest_preparation_survey_1`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for the details about your arrival, such as your name, date, or any other relevant information that you think we might find helpful.
*   **Guest Preferences** (`guest_preparation_survey_2`, `text`, required: false): In this field, list any personal preferences or requirements you may have during your stay. For example, you might prefer a particular room location or have any food allergies.
*   **Additional Information** (`guest_preparation_survey_4`, `text`, required: false): Provide any further information that you think will help us better understand and accommodate your needs.
*   **Guest Contact Info** (`guest_preparation_survey_5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact details so that we can reach you if we need anything.
*   **Guest Comments** (`guest_preparation_survey_6`, `text`, required: false): In this field, you can enter any comments or notes you have about your stay.

### Guest Accommodations

*   **Guest Accommodations** (`guest_preparation_survey_3`, `text`, required: false): Here, select your accommodation preferences, such as room type or location.
*   **Guest Room Preference** (`guest_preparation_survey_10`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred type of room.
*   **Guest Room Preference - 2** (`guest_preparation_survey_18`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred room for your next stay.

### Guest Arrival

*   **Guest Arrival Date** (`guest_preparation_survey_7`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected date of your arrival.
*   **Guest Arrival Time** (`guest_preparation_survey_8`, `time`, required: false): Enter the expected time of your arrival.
*   **Guest Arrival Date - 2** (`guest_preparation_survey_15`, `date`, required: false): If you have any other planned arrival dates.
*   **Guest Arrival Time - 2** (`guest_preparation_survey_16`, `time`, required: false): Enter the expected time of your arrival for this date.
*   **Guest Arrival Date - 3** (`guest_preparation_survey_23`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected date of your arrival.

### Guest Stay

*   **Guest Stay Duration - 2** (`guest_preparation_survey_17`, `number`, required: false): Enter the expected length of your stay for this visit.
*   **Guest Stay Duration - 3** (`guest_preparation_survey_25`, `number`, required: false): Enter the expected length of your stay for the next visit.

### Special Requests
