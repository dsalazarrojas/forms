# Community Digital Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Community Digital Access Survey is a tool used by our organization to assess and improve digital access in our community. Your feedback and information will help us better understand the needs and challenges faced by our residents in terms of digital access, device availability, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form completely and accurately. All marked fields are required.
* Please be honest about your current situation and needs.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Resident** (`resident_full_name_das`, `text`, required): This is your name as it appears on your ID or identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`resident_email_das`, `email`, required): We'll use this to send you follow-up information and survey results. Please ensure it's accurate.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`resident_phone_das`, `text`, required): This is the phone number we can use to contact you about your survey response.
* **Residential Address** (`resident_address_das`, `text`, required): Please provide your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Digital Access and Device Availability** (`section_digital_access_assessment_das`, `note`, required): This section helps us understand your current digital access and device availability. Please answer the following questions truthfully.
*   * **Reliable Internet Access** (`internet_access_availability_das`, `select_one`, required): Do you have:
	+ Yes, reliable access
	+ Somewhat reliable access
	+ No, unreliable access
	+ No internet access at all
*   * **Available Devices** (`device_access_availability_das`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all devices you have access to:
	+ Laptop/Desktop Computer
	+ Smartphone/Tablet
	+ Internet-Enabled Gaming Console
	+ Digital Home Assistant
	+ Other (please specify)
*   * **Primary Device for Internet Access** (`primary_device_for_internet_das`, `select_one`, required): Which device do you primarily use for internet access?
	+ Laptop/Desktop Computer
	+ Smartphone/Tablet
	+ Internet-Enabled Gaming Console
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Digital Skills and Training Feedback** (`section_digital_skills_feedback_das`, `note`, required): We want to know about your digital skills and training needs.
*   * **Digital Skill Level** (`digital_skill_level_das`, `number`, required): Rate your overall comfort and skill with digital technology:
	+ 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High)
*   * **Digital Training Interests** (`digital_training_interest_das`, `select_multiple`, required): Which digital training topics are you most interested in?
	+ Basic Computer/Internet Skills
	+ Online Safety and Privacy
	+ Digital Literacy and Navigation
	+ Coding/Programming Skills
	+ Digital Content Creation
	+ Specialized Software Training
*   * **Digital Resources and Support** (`digital_resource_availability_das`, `select_one`, required): Do you have:
	+ Yes, adequate resources
	+ Somewhat adequate resources
	+ No, inadequate resources
*   * **Improvement Ideas for Digital Access** (`improvement_ideas_digital_access_das`, `text`, required): Share your ideas for improving digital access in our community.
* **Digital Signature** (`resident_signature_das`, `text`, required): Please type your full name as it appears on your ID or identification documents.
* **Date of Survey Submission** (`submission_date_final_das`, `date`, required): Please indicate the date you're submitting this survey.
* **Newsletter Opt-in** (`newsletter_opt_in_das`, `select_one`, required): Would you like to receive our community center's newsletter?
	+ Yes, sign me up!
	+ No, thank you
*   * **Referral Source** (`referral_source_das`, `select_one`, optional): How did you hear about this digital access survey?
	+ Email
	+ Social Media
	+ Website
	+ Friend/Colleague
	+ Poster/Flyer
*   * **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_full_name_das`, `text`, required): Provide the name of someone not living with you as an emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Take your time, and answer the questions carefully.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
* Please keep the information confidential and not share it with others.
