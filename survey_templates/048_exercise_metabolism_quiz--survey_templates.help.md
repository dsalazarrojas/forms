# Exercise Metabolism Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Exercise Metabolism Quiz form. The quiz is intended to gather information about your exercise habits and how they affect your metabolism.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each one honestly.
2. Type your responses into the corresponding text fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, although this form does not have any required fields marked as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Metabolism Quiz 1**: Please describe your typical exercise routine.
  (This is the first text field where you can provide information about your exercise habits.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 2**: What types of exercise do you usually do?
  (This field is for selecting or describing the types of exercise you typically engage in.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 3**: How often do you exercise in a week?
  (This field is for reporting how frequently you exercise in a week.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 4**: What is the intensity of your exercise routine?
  (This field is for describing the intensity of your exercise habits.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 5**: How many minutes do you usually spend exercising per session?
  (This field is for reporting the duration of your exercise sessions.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 6**: What is your typical exercise pace?
  (This field is for describing the tempo or speed at which you exercise.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 7**: Do you take regular breaks during exercise?
  (This field is a yes/no or true/false question about taking breaks.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 8**: Describe your exercise frequency on weekdays.
  (This is another text field for describing your exercise habits by frequency, possibly related to the days of the week.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 9**: Describe your exercise frequency on weekends.
  (This field is similar to the previous one, but for the weekends.)
- **Metabolism Quiz 10**: Do you have any medical conditions or concerns that may impact your exercise routine?
  (This field is for providing any health-related information that might be relevant.)

## Tips
- Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately to ensure the form collects reliable data.
- If you're unsure about any question, you can always consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
