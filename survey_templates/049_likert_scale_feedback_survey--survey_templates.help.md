# Likert Scale Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a particular topic or service. Your input will help us understand your opinions and sentiments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and answer "yes" or "no" to the options provided. You can answer all or some of the questions, depending on your experience. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **likert_scale1** (yes/no): This question asks about a specific aspect of your experience. Please answer "yes" if this applies to you, and "no" otherwise.
* **likert_scale2** (yes/no): This question is similar to the previous one, and you should answer "yes" if it applies to you, and "no" otherwise.
* **likert_scale3** (yes/no): This question is another opportunity for you to provide feedback about your experience. Answer "yes" if it applies to you, and "no" otherwise.
* ... and so on, until **likert_scale25** (yes/no).
* **likert_scale26** (yes/no): This is the final question. Please answer "yes" if it applies to you, and "no" otherwise.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions that apply to you.
* If you are unsure about a question, feel free to skip it.
* Your input is valuable to us, and it will only take a few minutes to complete.
