# Webhook Delivery Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the suitability of a webhook delivery platform based on several criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin with the **Introduction** page, which provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. Move on to the **Vendor Comparison Criteria** page, where you will select the criteria that matters most to you when evaluating a webhook delivery platform. Choose one or multiple options from the list provided.
3. On the **Satisfaction Scoring** page, rate your satisfaction level with the platform using a numerical score.
4. On the **Decision-Ready Assessments** page, select all the criteria that you are currently using or plan to use with the platform.
5. In the **Additional Comments** section, add any additional information or thoughts you have about the platform.
6. Enter the **Webhook URL** of the platform you are evaluating.
7. Provide your **Contact Info** in the email format.
8. Finally, select the date you would like to submit your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Vendor Comparison Criteria** (Page 2): This section allows you to select one or multiple criteria that are important to you when evaluating a webhook delivery platform. Please choose one or more options from the list provided.
	+ **Criteria 1**: This option evaluates the user experience offered by the platform.
	+ **Criteria 2**: This option assesses the platform's reliability and uptime.
	+ **Criteria 3**: This option considers the platform's support and documentation quality.
* **Satisfaction Scoring** (Page 3): Rate your satisfaction level with the platform using a numerical score out of 10.
* **Decision-Ready Assessments** (Page 4): Select all the criteria that you are currently using or plan to use with the platform.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5): Enter any additional information or comments about the platform.
* **Webhook URL** (Page 6): Enter the Webhook URL of the platform you are evaluating.
* **Contact Info** (Page 7): Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Submission Date** (Page 8): Select the date when you would like to submit your evaluation.
* **Submission Time** (Page 9): Choose the time when you would like to submit your evaluation.
