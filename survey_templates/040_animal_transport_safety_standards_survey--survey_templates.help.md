# Animal Transport Safety Standards Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your views on animal transport safety standards, including current practices, challenges, and potential improvements. Your input will help identify gaps and areas for improvement in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For select_multiple and select_one questions, choose all that apply or select the most relevant option.
3. For text fields, provide brief but clear answers.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (3)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
