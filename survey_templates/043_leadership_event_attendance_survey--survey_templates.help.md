# Leadership Event Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from attendees on the leadership event, allowing you to evaluate its effectiveness and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the "first_name" field. This is the name you would like to be referred to in your feedback.
2. Enter your **last name** in the "last_name" field. This is your surname.
3. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field. This will be used to contact you for any further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
4. Select your **session participation level** for each session attended from the dropdown menu in the "session_participation" field.
5. Rate your **overall experience** of the event from the dropdown menu in the "overall_rating" field.
6. Enter any **feedback** you have about the event in the text area. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts and opinions.
7. If you have any **suggestions** for improving future events, enter them in the text area.
8. Select any **next steps** you would like to take as a result of attending the event from the dropdown menu in the "next_steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name here.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name here.
* **email**: Enter your email address here. This will be used for follow-up contact or to send you additional information.
* **session_participation**: Select how engaging you found each session.
	+ Highly Engaging: The session was highly engaging and informative.
	+ Moderately Engaging: The session was moderately engaging and somewhat informative.
	+ Not Engaging: The session was not engaging and not very informative.
* **overall_rating**: Rate your overall experience of the event.
	+ Excellent: The event was excellent and met your expectations.
	+ Good: The event was good, but not excellent.
	+ Fair: The event was fair and could be improved.
	+ Poor: The event was poor and did not meet your expectations.
* **feedback**: Enter any feedback you have about the event here.
* **suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improving future events, enter them here.
* **next_steps**: Select any next steps you would like to take as a result of attending the event.
	+ Follow-up meeting: You would like to have a follow-up meeting to discuss further.
	+ Follow-up phone call: You would like to have a follow-up phone call to discuss further.
	+ Additional resources: You would like to receive additional resources related to the event.
