# Visual Problem Solving Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather opinions and attitudes towards visual problem solving, a process that involves identifying and addressing visual problems. The goal is to understand how individuals approach and utilize visual problem solving techniques in their daily lives, professions, and relationships. Your responses will help us better understand the nuances of visual problem solving and its impact on personal and professional outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
*   For each question, select the answer that best represents your opinion or attitude.
*   You can choose more than one option for questions that allow multiple selections.
*   If you have any comments or thoughts related to a question, feel free to provide them in the corresponding text field.
