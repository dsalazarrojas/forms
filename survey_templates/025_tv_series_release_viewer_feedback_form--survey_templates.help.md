# TV Series Release Viewer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Series Release Viewer Feedback Form is designed to collect viewer information and feedback on each episode of a TV series. This feedback is crucial for content creators and production teams to understand their audience's preferences and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name.
2. Provide a detailed feedback on the episode(s) you've watched.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Confirm if you've watched the episode.
5. Select the episodes you've watched.
6. Confirm if you've watched the series.
7. Choose the type of feedback (Positive or Negative).
8. Rate the episode on a scale of 1-10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (whats-your-name)** (Required):
	+ Enter your name to be associated with your feedback.
* **Feedback (feedback)** (Required):
	+ Provide detailed feedback on the episode(s) you've watched.
* **Email (email)** (Required):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Release Date (release-date)** (Required):
	+ Choose the release date of the episode you're providing feedback on.
* **Watched (watched)** (Required):
	+ Confirm if you've watched the episode.
* **Episodes Watched (episodes-watched)** (Required):
	+ Select the episodes you've watched.
* **Series Watched (series-watched)** (Required):
	+ Confirm if you've watched the series.
* **Feedback Type (feedback-type)** (Required):
	+ Choose the type of feedback (Positive or Negative).
* **Rating (rating)** (Required):
	+ Rate the episode on a scale of 1-10.

## Tips
- Please be honest and specific with your feedback.
- Fill out this form for each episode separately.
- Make sure to provide contact information so we can get back to you.
- Rate the episode based on your actual experience.
