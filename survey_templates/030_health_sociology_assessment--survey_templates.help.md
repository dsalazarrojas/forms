# Health Sociology Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Sociology Assessment form is designed to gather information on your health and well-being from various perspectives. It consists of several sections that cover different aspects of your life, including health behaviors, self-rated health, social factors, family support, community health, education, health care, health outcomes, and mental health. This form is used to help healthcare professionals understand your needs and provide you with the best possible care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each section thoroughly and honestly.
2. For each section, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask yourself what you would want to know if you were in the healthcare professional's position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Behaviors** (`health_behaviors`, `note`, required: false`): This section is a free-text field where you can describe your daily habits and behaviors that affect your health. Please explain any regular activities, exercises, or routines that impact your well-being.
* **Self-Rated Health** (`self_rated_health`, `text`, required: false`): Here, you rate your overall health status. Choose the option that best represents your current health situation.
* **Social Factors** (`social_factors`, `select_multiple`, required: false`): Select all the options that apply to your social situation. These factors can include family, friends, or work-related issues that affect your health.
* **Family Support** (`family_support`, `select_one`, required: false`): Choose the option that best describes your family's support for your health.
* **Community Health** (`community_health`, `number`, required: false`): Enter the number of community activities you participate in regularly.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required: false`): In this field, please describe any educational programs or workshops you've attended related to your health.
* **Health Care** (`health_care`, `select_multiple`, required: false`): Select all the options that apply to your current health care situation.
* **Health Outcomes** (`health_outcomes`, `date`, required: false`): If applicable, enter a specific date that indicates a positive or negative health change in your life.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, `time`, required: false`): In this field, please provide a rough estimate of the time you spend on relaxing activities.
