# traveling_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about travel preferences and details for planning trips, booking flights, or creating travel itineraries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your travel preferences and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`traveling_preferences_survey`, `text`, Optional): This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled for your convenience.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, Optional): Please select the date of your trip or the period of time you are planning to travel.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, Optional): Choose the start time of your trip.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, Optional): Choose the end time of your trip.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the type of travel you are planning (e.g., leisure, business, or study).
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose the mode of transportation you prefer (e.g., car, plane, or train).
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the destination of your trip.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, `number`, Optional): Enter the frequency of your travels (e.g., once a month, weekly, or yearly).
* **Travel Budget** (`travel_budget`, `number`, Optional): Enter your travel budget.
* **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the currency of your travel budget.
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose the documents you will need for travel (e.g., passport, ID card, or credit card).
* **Travel Instrument** (`travel_instrument`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the travel instrument you will use (e.g., car, plane, or train).
* **Travel Vehicle** (`travel_vehicle`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the vehicle you will use (e.g., car, bus, or taxi).
* **Preferred Travel Time** (`travel_time`, `time`, Optional): Enter your preferred travel time.
* **Travel Mode of Transportation** (`travel_mode_of_transportation`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the mode of transportation you prefer.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the travel document you will need (e.g., visa, passport, or ID card).
* **Travel Frequency Other** (`travel_frequency_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify the frequency of your travel.
* **Travel Documents Other** (`travel_documents_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify the travel documents you will need.
* **Travel Instrument Other** (`travel_instrument_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify the travel instrument you will use.
* **Travel Vehicle Other** (`travel_vehicle_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify the vehicle you will use.
* **Travel Mode of Transportation Other** (`travel_mode_of_transportation_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify the mode of transportation you will use.
* **Travel Document Other** (`travel_document_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify the travel document you will need.
* **Travel Budget Other** (`travel_budget_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify your travel budget.
* **Travel Time Other** (`travel_time_other`, `text`, Optional): If other, please specify your preferred travel time.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your travel preferences.
