# Personality Research Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic and personality trait data from respondents to facilitate further research and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully, ensuring you understand each question.
2. Select the appropriate answer or enter the required information for each section.
3. Complete all required fields, marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 1** (Page 2): Select whether you "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether certain personality traits apply to you.
* **Section 2** (Page 3): Select all that apply from the list, as the hint suggests.
* **Section 3** (Page 4): Select your "Yes" or "No" answers for the various personality traits.
* **Section 4** (Page 5): Select all that apply from the list.
* **Section 5** (Page 6): Enter the year and month of your birthday in the format YYYY-MM.
* **Section 6** (Page 7): Enter a valid email address.
* **Section 7** (Page 8): Enter a valid phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Section 8** (Page 9): This is a placeholder section.
* **Section 9** (Page 10): Enter a brief description of your current mood.
* **Section 10** (Page 11): Enter a numerical answer for this question.
