# Brake Inspector Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brake Inspector Qualification Survey is designed to gather information about a brake inspector's qualifications, experience, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. For select-one questions, choose the option that best represents your level of expertise or experience.
3. For select-multiple questions, choose all options that apply.
4. For text fields, enter your full name, email address, and professional reference information accurately.
5. For numerical fields, enter the total number of years of experience and years of brake-specific experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, **optional**): Enter your internal employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Highest Educational Degree** (`highest_degree`, select_one, **required**): Choose the highest level of education you have completed.
* **Automotive Training or Schooling** (`automotive_schooling`, number, **optional**): Enter the years of formal automotive training you have received.
* **Brake Certification Status** (`brake_certification`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose all brake-related certifications you hold.
* **Total Years of Experience** (`total_experience`, number, **required**): Enter the total number of years you have worked in the automotive industry.
* **Years Specifically in Brake Work** (`brake_specific_years`, number, **required**): Enter the number of years you have worked specifically on brake systems.
* **Brake Inspection Experience** (`inspection_experience`, number, **required**): Enter the number of years you have conducted brake inspections.
* **Hydraulic Systems Knowledge** (`hydraulic_knowledge`, select_one, **required**): Choose your level of expertise in hydraulic systems.
* **Diagnostic and Testing Skills** (`diagnostic_skills`, select_one, **required**): Choose your level of expertise in diagnostic and testing skills.
* **Safety Compliance Knowledge** (`safety_knowledge`, select_one, **required**): Choose your level of expertise in safety compliance.
* **Proficiency with Testing Equipment** (`equipment_proficiency`, select_one, **required**): Choose your level of expertise with testing equipment.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, consult with a supervisor or colleague.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the relevant authorities.
