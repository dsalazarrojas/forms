# Creative Play Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting creative play ideas for programs or activities. These ideas can be related to various fields such as art, music, drama, storytelling, games, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:

	* **submission_date**: Enter the date of submission.
2. Optionally, provide more details:

	* **activity category**: Choose a category for the creative play idea (e.g., Art, Music, Drama, etc.).
	* **activity idea description**: Provide a detailed description of the creative play idea.

	* **submission author**: Select whether the author of the submission is required (Yes/No).
