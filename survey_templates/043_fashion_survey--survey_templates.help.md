# Fashion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist users in understanding the purpose and usage of the Fashion Survey form.

The Fashion Survey form is designed to collect information from customers about their fashion preferences and experiences. It is used to gather feedback and opinions on various aspects of fashion, including customer feedback, product ratings, price ranges, and other relevant information.

### How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, simply follow the instructions below for each field.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, and use the options provided for select fields.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fashion Survey** (`fashion_survey`, text, optional): A text field where customers can provide their thoughts on fashion.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): A text field where customers can share their feedback on their fashion experiences.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): A numeric field where customers can rate their fashion products from 1 to 10.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, number, optional): A numeric field where customers can select their preferred price range for fashion products.
* **Fashion Category** (`fashion_category`, select one, optional): A select one field where customers can choose their preferred fashion category.
* **Select One Field** (`select_one_field`, select one, optional): A select one field where customers can choose one option.
* **Select Multiple Field** (`select_multiple_field`, select multiple, optional): A select multiple field where customers can choose multiple options.
* **Date Field** (`date_field`, date, optional): A date field where customers can input a date.
* **Time Field** (`time_field`, time, optional): A time field where customers can input a time.
* **Note Field** (`note_field`, note, optional): A note field where customers can provide additional notes or comments.
* **Email Field** (`email_field`, email, optional): An email field where customers can input their email address.
* **Phone Number Field** (`phone_number_field`, text, optional): A text field where customers can input their phone number.
* **Fashion Survey Comments** (`fashion_survey_comments`, text, optional): A text field where customers can provide additional comments on fashion surveys.

### Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, if applicable.
* Use the options provided for select fields to ensure accuracy.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

Please note that some fields may not be required for every user, and some fields may have specific instructions or guidelines. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
