# Voice Message Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users on their voice message experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You may skip fields that are not relevant to your experience. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your voice message experience.
* **How often do you listen to voice messages?** (`frequency`, `number`, optional): This field is to understand how often you listen to voice messages.
* **How satisfied are you with the current voice message experience?** (`satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your current voice message experience.
* **Which of the following platforms do you most frequently use for listening to voice messages?** (`favorite_platforms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the platforms you use most often for listening to voice messages. Options are Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa, and Other.
* **What are your favorite features in a voice message?** (`favorite_features`, `text`, optional): Describe your favorite features in a voice message.
* **Other platforms used for voice messages** (`other_platforms`, `text`, optional): If you use other platforms not listed, describe them here.
* **How often do you receive voice messages?** (`message_frequency`, `number`, optional): This field is to understand how often you receive voice messages.
* **How often do you find yourself satisfied with voice messages?** (`satisfaction_frequency`, `number`, optional): This field is to understand how often you find yourself satisfied with voice messages.
* **How satisfied are you with your current voice message experience?** (`message_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your current voice message experience.
* **Would you recommend our voice message service to a friend?** (`recommended`, `number`, optional): Indicate if you would recommend our service to a friend.
* **Any comments?** (`user_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to describe your team or organization.
* **Team members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the team members that are involved in your organization. Options are Product Manager, UX Designer, Researcher, Product Designer, Product Owner, and Other.
* **Product** (`product`, `text`, optional): Describe your product.
* **Product type** (`product_type`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of your product.
* **Product features** (`product_features`, `text`, optional): Describe the features of your product.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter your product name.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter the URL of your product.
* **Product image URL** (`product_image_url`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter the URL of your product image.
* **Product description** (`product_description`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to describe your product in more detail.
* **Product price** (`product_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of your product.
* **Product rating** (`product_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your product.
* **User Input 2** (`user_input_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide additional comments or feedback.
* **Team members 2** (`team_members_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the team members that are involved in your organization. Options are Product Manager, UX Designer, Researcher, Product Designer, Product Owner, and Other.
* **Product type 2** (`product_type_2`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of your product.
* **Product features 2** (`product_features_2`, `text`, optional): Describe the features of your product.
