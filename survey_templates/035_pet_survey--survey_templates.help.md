# Pet Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about pet owners' habits, experiences, and care for their pets. It is used to gather insights into pet care and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you have from the options provided.
2. If your pet is a cat or dog, answer the questions on the next page. If you have another type of pet, select "Other" and enter a brief description.
3. Answer each question honestly, and choose all that apply to each multi-select question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the type of pet you have?**: Select the type of pet you have, such as Cat or Dog. If you have another type of pet, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **How old is your pet?**: Enter the age of your pet in years.
* **How many pets do you have?**: Select how many pets you have, up to 3 options.
* **What is your pet's name?**: Enter your pet's name.
* **Is your pet spayed or neutered?**: Select whether your pet is spayed or neutered.
* **How many times a week do you walk your pet?**: Enter the number of times you walk your pet per week.
* **How many hours a day do you spend with your pet?**: Enter the number of hours you spend with your pet per day.
* **What is your pet's weight?**: Enter your pet's weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **What is your pet's age in days?**: Enter your pet's age in days.
* **What date did you bring your pet home?**: Enter the date you brought your pet home.
* **What time did you bring your pet home?**: Enter the time you brought your pet home.
* **How much time does your pet spend on a typical day?**: Select how much time your pet spends on a typical day, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **What is your pet's breed?**: Select the breed of your pet, up to 3 options.
* **What is your pet's sex?**: Select the sex of your pet, such as Male or Female.
* **Does your pet have any medical conditions?**: Select whether your pet has any medical conditions.
* **How much exercise does your pet get?**: Select how much exercise your pet gets, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **How often do you take your pet to the vet?**: Select how often you take your pet to the vet.
* **What is your pet's vaccination status?**: Select the vaccination status of your pet, up to 3 options.
* **How much money do you spend on pet supplies per month?**: Enter the amount you spend on pet supplies per month.
* **How many times a month do you take your pet to the vet?**: Enter the number of times you take your pet to the vet per month.
* **How much time do you spend with your pet on a typical day?**: Enter the number of hours you spend with your pet per day.
* **How many people live with your pet?**: Select how many people live with your pet, up to 3 options.
* **Do you have any other pets?**: Select whether you have any other pets.
* **How many years do you have with your pet?**: Enter the number of years you have with your pet.
* **Why did you get your pet?**: Enter a brief description of why you got your pet.
