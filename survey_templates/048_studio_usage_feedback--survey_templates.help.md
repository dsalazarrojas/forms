# Studio Usage Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Studio Usage Feedback form is designed to collect information about your experiences and satisfaction with the studio. Your feedback will help us understand your needs and preferences, enabling us to make necessary adjustments and improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the date of your most recent session with the studio.
2. Enter the start and end times of your session.
3. Indicate the duration of your session.
4. Rate your overall experience using the provided options.
5. Provide details about the facilities you used during your session, including any areas for improvement.
6. Describe any equipment you used during your session and suggest ways to enhance it.
7. Offer comments or suggestions for improving facilities at the studio.
8. Comment on any equipment that you used, including suggestions for improvement.
9. Rate your overall experience using the provided options.
10. Share any final comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your most recent session with the studio.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): Indicate the duration of your session.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the studio using the provided options.
	+ Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, text, optional): Describe any facilities you used during your session, including any areas for improvement.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, optional): Describe any equipment you used during your session, including suggestions for improvement.
* **Facilities Improvement** (`facilities_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving facilities at the studio.
* **Equipment Improvement** (`equipment_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements for the equipment used during your session.
* **Facilities Comment** (`facilities_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about facilities.
* **Equipment Comment** (`equipment_comment`, text, optional): Offer any comments or suggestions related to the equipment used during your session.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the studio, using the provided options.
	+ Please select the option that best represents your experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any final comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Session Comment** (`session_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your session.

Note: Please complete the form as thoroughly as possible, providing honest and constructive feedback. Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort.
