# Customer Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their interactions with our customer service team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your level of satisfaction with your recent customer service interaction.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer service.
3. Enter the date and time of your recent contact with our customer service team.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Interaction** (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not at all satisfied): Select the level of satisfaction you experienced during your recent interaction with our customer service team.
- **Customer Service Rating** (optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer service team on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
- **Time of Contact** (MM/DD/YY): Enter the date and time of your recent contact with our customer service team in MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 02/20/2023).
- **Customer Service Survey Response** (optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our customer service team.

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- If you have had multiple interactions with our customer service team, please fill out this form for each interaction separately.
- If you have any technical issues while filling out this form, please contact our support team for assistance.
