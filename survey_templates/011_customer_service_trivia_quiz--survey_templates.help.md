## Purpose
The customer service trivia quiz is designed to test your knowledge on various aspects of customer service. It is not a timed test, and you can complete it at your own pace. The goal is to gauge your understanding of customer service concepts and principles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply read each question carefully and select the most appropriate option from the available choices. Some questions may require multiple selections. Ensure that you have sufficient knowledge of customer service concepts before proceeding.
