# p2p_crypto_trading_feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have engaged in peer-to-peer (P2P) crypto trading activities. The purpose of this form is to gather information about their experience, including their overall satisfaction with the process, perceived speed and ease of use, level of trust and support received, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Make sure you have participated in a P2P crypto trade and that your email address matches the one used for the trade.
2. Scroll through the form and select the corresponding answer for each field.
3. Fill out all fields marked as required.
4. Optionally, you can add additional comments or recommendations in the 'comments' and 'recommendations' fields.
5. Enter your email address in the 'contact_us' fields to validate your identity.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trade_id** (`trade_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the trade you participated in. This will help us associate your feedback with your actual trade.
* **overall_rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5 stars. The options are:
	+ 1: Very bad experience
	+ 2: Bad experience
	+ 3: Neutral experience
	+ 4: Good experience
	+ 5: Very good experience
* **rating_comments** (`rating_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your overall rating.
* **trade_speed** (`trade_speed`, number, optional): Rate the speed of the trade from 1-5, with 1 being very slow and 5 being very fast.
* **ease_of_use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, optional): Rate how easy or challenging you found the trade process to be. The options are:
	+ fast
	+ easy
	+ challenging
* **trustworthiness** (`trustworthiness`, select_multiple, optional): Rate how much you felt trusted throughout the trade. The options are:
	+ high
	+ medium
	+ low
* **support** (`support`, select_multiple, required): Rate the level of support you received during the trade. The options are:
	+ good
	+ bad
	+ neutral
* **communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, required): Rate the quality of communication you received during the trade. The options are:
	+ good
	+ bad
	+ neutral
* **security** (`security`, select_one, required): Rate the level of security you felt during the trade. The options are:
	+ good
	+ bad
	+ challenging
* **support_team** (`support_team`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of support team you interacted with. The options are:
	+ good
	+ bad
	+ neutral
* **experience** (`experience`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **recommendations** (`recommendations`, note, optional): Add any recommendations for future improvements.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **contact_us** (`contact_us`, email, optional): Enter your email address to validate your identity.
* **contact_us_email** (`contact_us_email`, email, optional): This field is a duplicate of 'contact_us' and should not be filled out.
