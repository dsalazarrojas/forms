# Robotics Component Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us evaluate various robotics components based on their performance and characteristics. By answering the questions in this survey, you will provide valuable insights into the strengths and weaknesses of each component, which will help us to better understand the robotics ecosystem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. Each page is designed to cover a specific aspect of the component, and you may skip pages that are not relevant to your specific component. Please answer the questions based on your experience and knowledge of the component.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Component Type** (component_type, select_one, required): This section asks you to select the type of component you are evaluating. Please choose the option that best describes your component.
* **Select Subcomponent** (subcomponent, select_multiple, required): This section asks you to select the subcomponents that are relevant to your evaluation. Please choose all options that apply.
* **Evaluation Type** (evaluation_type, select_one, required): This section asks you to select the type of evaluation you are conducting. Please choose the option that best describes your evaluation.
* **Performance Rating** (performance_rating, number, required): This section asks you to rate the performance of your component on a scale of 0-10.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your component.
* **Test Results** (test_results, text, optional): This section is for you to share any test results or data related to your component.
* **Component Name** (component_name, text, required): This section asks you to provide the name of your component.
