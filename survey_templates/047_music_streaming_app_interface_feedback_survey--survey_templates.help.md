# Music Streaming App Interface Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback about your experience with our music streaming app. Your input will help us improve the app's user interface and make it more enjoyable for you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer the questions carefully and honestly.
* For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the best option that best describes your opinion.
* For text questions, provide a brief description of your answer.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the corresponding text fields.
* Email and phone number fields are optional, but if you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, Note, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or information that you would like to share about your experience with the app.
* **Category** (2, Select One, optional): Choose the category that best describes the main menu: Main Menu, Search, or Profile.
* **Subcategory** (4, Text, optional): If none of the categories above fit your experience, please describe the subcategory here.
* **Subcategory Other** (5, Text, optional): If you have a subcategory that is not listed, please describe it here.
* **What do you think of our main menu?** (6, Select Multiple, optional): Rate our main menu as Easy to use, Confusing, or Unnecessary feature.
* **Other (please specify)** (7, Text, optional): If none of the above options fit your experience, please describe it here.
* **How would you rate our search function?** (8, Select One, optional): Rate our search function as Very good, Not good at all, Somewhat, or Neither.
* **Other (not applicable)** (9, Text, optional): If our search function doesn't apply to your experience, please describe why it's not applicable.
* **How would you rate our profile page?** (10, Select One, optional): Rate our profile page as Very good, Not good at all, Somewhat, or Neither.
* **Other (please specify)** (11, Text, optional): If our profile page doesn't fit your experience, please describe it here.
* **Do you have any comments?** (12, Text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you may have about the app.
* **Email** (13, Email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (14, Text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your phone number.
* **Rating** (15, Number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the app on a scale of 1-5.
* **Category 2** (16, Text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please describe them here.
* **Subcategory 2** (17, Text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please describe them here.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (18, Text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the app.
* **Other (please specify)** (19, Text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please describe them here.
