# Maintenance Expectation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gauge the expectations and satisfaction of customers regarding maintenance services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction carefully.
2. Fill out the "Name" field with your name.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact No" field.
5. Choose your expectation of maintenance services from the "Maintenance Expectation" dropdown menu.
6. Select all that apply for your satisfaction level with the services from the "Satisfaction" dropdown menu.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field (optional).
8. Sign and date the form in the "Signature" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the survey.
* **Name** (<required>): Enter your name.
* **Email** (<required>): Enter your email address.
* **Contact No** (<required>): Enter your contact number.
* **Maintenance Expectation** (<required>): Choose your expectation of maintenance services from the dropdown menu.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Satisfaction** (<required>): Select all that apply for your satisfaction level with the services from the dropdown menu.
	+ Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Comments** (<optional>): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Signature** (<optional>): Sign and date the form in this field.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and carefully.
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
* Sign the form if you have filled out all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
