# Bank Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on customer satisfaction with the bank's services, products, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by selecting the appropriate age group for yourself.
2. Indicate if you are the primary account holder or not.
3. Choose all banking products you currently have with us.
4. Rate your satisfaction with our online, mobile, and branch banking services.
5. Rate your satisfaction with communication and clarity of statements and notifications.
6. Tell us if you find our bank services to be innovative or not.
7. Express your likelihood to continue banking with us for the next 2 years.
8. Indicate if you have considered switching to another bank recently.
9. Select the area where you believe we need the most improvement.
10. Provide any additional feedback on your experience.
11. Finally, rate how likely you are to recommend our bank to friends or family (Net Promoter Score).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group (id: 2)**: This question helps us understand your demographic profile.
	+ Select the age group that best represents you.
* **Are you the primary account holder? (id: 3)**: This question helps us know who is making the decisions for the accounts.
	+ Select 'True' if you are the primary account holder.
* **Current Accounts and Services (id: 5)**: This question helps us understand what banking services you use.
	+ Select all products you currently have with us.
* **Online / Desktop Banking Portal (id: 7)**: Rate your satisfaction with our online banking services.
	+ Select your level of satisfaction.
* **Mobile App Functionality (id: 8)**: Rate your satisfaction with our mobile app.
	+ Select your level of satisfaction.
* **Overall Branch Experience (id: 9)**: Rate your overall experience at our branches.
	+ Select your level of satisfaction.
* **Communication Clarity (id: 10)**: This question helps us understand how clear our communications are.
	+ Select your level of satisfaction with our statements and notifications.
* **Do you consider our bank to be trustworthy? (id: 12)**: This question helps us understand your perception of our trustworthiness.
	+ Select your level of agreement.
* **How innovative do you find our services? (id: 13)**: This question helps us understand how innovative we are compared to others.
	+ Select your level of agreement.
* **Likelihood to Continue Banking with us (id: 14)**: This question helps us understand your commitment to our bank.
	+ Select your level of likelihood.
* **Have you considered switching to another bank? (id: 15)**: This question helps us understand if you are happy with our bank.
	+ Select 'True' if you have considered switching.
* **Area for Improvement (id: 16)**: This question helps us understand where we need to improve.
	+ Select the area where you believe we need the most improvement.
* **Open-ended Feedback (id: 17)**: This question allows you to share any additional thoughts or suggestions.
* **Net Promoter Score (id: 18)**: This question helps us understand how likely you are to recommend us to friends or family.
	+ Select your score between 0-10.
