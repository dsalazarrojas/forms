# Digital Banking Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about your experience with our digital banking service. Your input will help us understand what works well and what can be improved to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest and detailed feedback.
2. For fields with options, select the best response that fits your experience.
3. If you have comments, use the "comments" field to elaborate on your answers.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, review your responses and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select multiple, not required): Choose from 1 to 5 to rate your satisfaction with various aspects of our service. Select all that apply.
* **Common Issues** (`common_issues`, text, not required): Describe any recurring problems you've encountered while using our service.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggest_improvements`, text, not required): Share your ideas on how we can improve our service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or insights about your experience.
* **Language Spoken at Home** (`first_language`, select one, not required): Select the language you most commonly speak or use at home.
* **Language Spoken at Last Interaction** (`last_language`, select one, not required): Select the language you were interacting with us in at the time of your last contact.
* **Date of Last Interaction** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your last interaction with our service.
* **Time of Last Interaction** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of your last interaction with our service.
* **Submit Your Feedback** (`submit`, select one, not required): Select "Submit" to submit your feedback, or "Cancel" to exit without submitting.
* **Confirm Your Submission** (`confirm_submit`, select one, not required): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your feedback by selecting "Yes", or "No".
* **Confirm Your Feedback** (`confirm`, select multiple, not required): Confirm that you've submitted feedback about the following: <list "rating", "common_issues", "suggest_improvements", "comments">.
