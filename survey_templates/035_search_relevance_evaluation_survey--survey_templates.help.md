# Search Relevance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the search results of a search engine or a search algorithm, specifically in the context of assessing their relevance and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully and based on your most recent interaction with the search engine or algorithm.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your experience with the search results.
3. If you have any comments or further insights, use the "What did you think about the search results?" field.
4. For fields with a numerical scale (e.g., "How clear were the search results?" or "How satisfied are you with the search results?"), use the scale to indicate your level of agreement or satisfaction.
5. If you're unsure or have no preference, select "Not at All Important" for questions about importance and select the middle value of the scale for numerical questions.

* **Introduction**: This is a brief text field where you can provide a general overview of your experience with the search engine or algorithm.

* **How important is it to you that the search results are relevant?**: Select how important it is to you that the search results are relevant to your search.
