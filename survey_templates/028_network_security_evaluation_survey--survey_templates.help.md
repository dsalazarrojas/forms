# Network Security Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Network Security Evaluation Survey is designed to assess an organization's current network security posture. It is a self-assessment tool to help you identify areas of improvement and prioritize security measures to ensure the safety and integrity of your organization's network.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly based on your organization's current state.
2. Select one or multiple options for questions that ask for multiple-choice responses.
3. Ensure you save your progress in case you need to stop and resume later.
4. When finished, review and confirm your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is the current state of your organization's network segmentation?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_1`): This question assesses how well your organization's network is segmented into isolated areas to prevent or limit lateral movement of threats. Choose from the options below:
	+ Segmented and secure
	+ Partially segmented, but with some gaps
	+ Not segmented at all
* **2. How do you rate the level of risk management in your organization?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_2`): This question evaluates how effectively you manage potential risks to your organization's network and resources. Choose from the options below:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **3. What is the current state of your organization's security governance practices?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_3`): This question looks into the maturity of your organization's security governance practices. Choose from the options below:
	+ Mature and well-established
	+ Developing but not yet mature
	+ Basic or absent
* **4. What is your current experience with the following security measures?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_4`): This question checks on your familiarity with various security measures. Select all that apply:
	+ Network segmentation
	+ Access control and authentication
	+ Encryption and data protection
* **5. Are you using a formal risk assessment process?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_5`): This question verifies if your organization has a formal process for assessing risks. Choose from the options below:
	+ True
	+ False
* **6. How often do you perform regular security audits or assessments?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_6`): This question assesses the frequency of your organization's security audits and assessments. Choose from the options below:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly
* **7. What is your current level of awareness about common network threats?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_7`): This question measures your organization's awareness of potential network threats. Choose from the options below:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **8. How many people in your organization have access to sensitive data?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_8`): This question checks how many staff have access to sensitive data. Choose one or multiple from the options below:
	+ 1-5 people
	+ 6-10 people
	+ More than 10 people
* **9. Have you ever experienced a security breach or incident?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_9`): This question inquires if your organization has encountered a security incident. Choose from the options below:
	+ True
	+ False
* **10. How quickly do you respond to security incidents?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_10`): This question assesses your organization's response time to security incidents. Choose from the options below:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 hour
	+ Within 1 day
	+ Within 1 week
* **11. What is your current budget for security measures?** (`network_security_evaluation_page_11`): This question inquires into your organization's budget for security measures. Choose one or multiple from the options below:
	+ < $10,000
	+ $10,000-$50,000
	+ $50,000-$100,000
