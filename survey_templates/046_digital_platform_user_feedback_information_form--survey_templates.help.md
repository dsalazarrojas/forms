# Digital Platform User Feedback Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and ratings on a digital platform. We appreciate your input and use it to improve the platform for a better experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your feedback and comments on the "Feedback" field.
2. Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1 and 5 in the "Rating" field.
3. If you have a recommendation for the platform, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Recommendation" field.
4. Select your frequency of using the platform in the "Frequency of use" field (Daily, Weekly, or Rarely).
5. If you have any comments on your frequency of use, please provide them in the "Frequency of use comments" field.
6. Select the platform features you use from the list in the "Platform features" field (User profiles, Messaging, Customization options, Search, Notifications).
7. Rate your overall experience by selecting a number between 1 and 5 in the "Overall rating" field.
8. Provide any comments on your overall rating in the "Overall rating comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, required): Please enter your thoughts and comments about your experience with the digital platform.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Recommendation** (`Recommendation`, select_one, required): If you would recommend the platform, select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Category** (`Category`, text, optional): Enter a category related to your feedback (e.g., "User interface", "Features", etc.).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to contact you for follow-up or additional information.
* **Platform name** (`Platform name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the digital platform you are providing feedback on.
* **Feedback comments** (`Feedback comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your feedback.
* **Frequency of use** (`Frequency of use`, select_one, optional): Select how often you use the platform (Daily, Weekly, or Rarely).
* **Frequency of use comments** (`Frequency of use comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or details about your frequency of use.
* **Platform features** (`Platform features`, select_multiple, optional): Select the features you use from the list.
* **Overall rating** (`Overall rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Overall rating comments** (`Overall rating comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments on your overall rating.

Note: Some fields are not required, but filling them out can provide more specific and valuable feedback.
