# 2017 2018 Media Center Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2017 2018 Media Center Survey is designed to gather feedback and responses from users to better understand their experience, preferences, and satisfaction with the Media Center.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Full name** field, which is required for survey submission.
2. Enter your **Email address**, which is also required for survey submission.
3. Select the **Date of survey completion**, which will automatically be filled in using the current date.
4. Choose your **Age group** from the provided options.
5. Rate your **satisfaction** with the Media Center, selecting one of the provided options.
6. Choose your **Overall rating** on a scale of 1-10, where 1 represents the worst and 10 represents the best.
7. Select all the **Aspects that applied** to your experience with the Media Center (optional).
8. Choose your **Primary use** of the Media Center from the provided options.
9. Select how often you **Use the Media Center**.
10. Choose the **Device you primarily use** to access the Media Center.
11. Select all the **Media types accessed** (optional).
12. Rate the **Search functionality**, **Navigation experience**, and **Content quality** on a scale of 1-10.
13. Select the level of **Technical issues** you experienced (optional).
14. Rate the **Customer support** (optional).
15. Rate how likely you are to **Recommend the Media Center**.
16. Provide any **Open feedback** or suggestions for improvement.
17. List any **Additional features you would like to see**.
18. Add any **Additional comments or notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for survey submission.
* **Date of survey completion** (`date`, `date`, required): This will automatically be filled in using the current date.
* **Age group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your age group from the provided options.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the Media Center, selecting one of the provided options.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate your overall experience with the Media Center on a scale of 1-10.
* **Aspects that applied** (`experience_aspects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the aspects that applied to your experience with the Media Center.
* **Primary use** (`primary_use`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary use of the Media Center from the provided options.
* **Use the Media Center** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you use the Media Center.
* **Device used** (`device_used`, `select_one`, required): Choose the device you primarily use to access the Media Center.
* **Media types accessed** (`media_types_accessed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the media types you access.
* **Search functionality** (`search_functionality_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the search functionality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Navigation experience** (`navigation_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the navigation experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Content quality** (`content_quality_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the quality of content on a scale of 1-10.
* **Technical issues** (`technical_issues`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of technical issues you experienced.
* **Customer support** (`customer_support_rating`, `integer`, optional): Rate the customer support (optional).
* **Recommend Media Center** (`recommend_likelihood`, `integer`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend the Media Center.
* **Open feedback** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any open feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional features** (`additional_features_desired`, `text`, optional): List any additional features you would like to see.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
