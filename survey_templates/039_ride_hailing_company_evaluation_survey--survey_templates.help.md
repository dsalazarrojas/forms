# ride-hailing-company-evaluation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with our ride-hailing service. Your input will help us improve our service and better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter your email address in the designated field.
4. Submit your completed form.
