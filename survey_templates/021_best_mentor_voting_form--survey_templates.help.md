# Best Mentor Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Best Mentor Voting Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on the quality of mentoring provided by an individual. This form is used to assess and improve the organization's mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your voter information, including your full name, role, and contact email address.
2. Select the mentor you are voting for from the options provided.
3. Rate the mentor's guidance and support, accessibility and availability, knowledge sharing, encouragement of personal and professional growth, and professionalism and ethical conduct using the provided rating scale (1-5).
4. Describe specific situations where the mentor went above and beyond in their support and guidance.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the mentor's performance.
6. Finally, provide the date of your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 2. Your Full Name (required)
- **Your Full Name**: Your full name is required to be filled in this field.

### 3. Your Role (required)
- **Your Role**: Select the one that best describes your relationship to the mentor.

### 4. Your Contact Email (required)
- **Your Contact Email**: Your contact email address is required for internal verification purposes.

### 5. Mentor Name (required)
- **Mentor Name**: The name of the individual you are voting for.

### 6. Mentor's Department or Team (optional)
- **Mentor's Department or Team**: The department or team where the mentor works.

### 7. Guidance and Support Provided (required)
- **Guidance and Support Provided**: Rate how well the mentor provided guidance and support (1-5).

### 8. Accessibility and Availability (required)
- **Accessibility and Availability**: Rate how easy it is to schedule time with the mentor (1-5).

### 9. Knowledge Sharing (required)
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Rate the mentor's willingness to share knowledge and expertise (1-5).

### 10. Encouragement of Personal and Professional Growth (required)
- **Encouragement of Personal and Professional Growth**: Rate the mentor's encouragement of personal and professional growth (1-5).

### 11. Professionalism and Ethical Conduct (required)
- **Professionalism and Ethical Conduct**: Rate the mentor's professionalism and ethical conduct (1-5).

### 12. Describe how this mentor has helped you or others achieve goals (required)
- **Describe how this mentor has helped you or others achieve goals**: Be as specific as possible with your description.

### 13. Describe a time they went above and beyond (required)
- **Describe a time they went above and beyond**: Provide a specific situation that highlights the mentor's mentorship.

### 14. One word that describes this mentor (required)
- **One word that describes this mentor**: Use a single adjective to describe the mentor.

### 15. Additional Comments or Feedback (optional)
- **Any other comments or feedback**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback on the mentor's performance.

### 16. Date of Vote (required)
- **Date of Vote**: The date on which you are submitting your vote.

Please note: This form is for feedback and rating purposes only, and the information provided will be used to assess and improve the mentorship program.
