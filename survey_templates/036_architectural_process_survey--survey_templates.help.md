# Architectural Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Process Survey is designed to gather feedback on the architectural process, including satisfaction with planning, design, and implementation stages. This feedback will help inform future project management decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name (optional)
* Select your role in the project (required)
* Enter the name of the project you participated in (required)
* Answer questions about your satisfaction with the planning stage (required)
* Answer questions about your satisfaction with the design stage (required)
* Answer questions about your satisfaction with the implementation stage (required)
* Answer questions about your experience with communication during the design stage (required)
* Answer questions about how well revisions were handled during the design stage (optional)
* Answer questions about your satisfaction with the implementation stage (required)
* Answer questions about your experience with project timeline and budget adherence (required)
* Identify any main challenges you faced during the project (optional)
* Suggest areas for improvement (optional)
* Let us know if you would recommend this process to others (required)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`text`, Optional): Please enter your name, if you choose to do so.
* **Your Role in the Project** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your role in the project from the list: Project owner or client, Architect, Project manager, Contractor, Consultant, Other stakeholder.
* **Project Name** (`text`, Required): Please enter the name of the project you participated in.
* **Satisfaction with Planning Stage** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your level of satisfaction with the planning stage from the list: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **Clarity of Project Goals during Planning** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your level of satisfaction with the clarity of project goals during the planning stage from the list: Not clear at all, Somewhat unclear, Neutral, Mostly clear, Very clear.
* **Satisfaction with Design Stage** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your level of satisfaction with the design stage from the list: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **Quality of Communication during Design** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your level of satisfaction with the quality of communication during the design stage from the list: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Were Revisions Handled Well?** (`select_one`, Optional): Please select whether revisions were handled well from the list: Not well, Somewhat, Neutral, Mostly well, Very well.
* **Satisfaction with Implementation Stage** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your level of satisfaction with the implementation stage from the list: Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **Project Timeline Adherence** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your experience with project timeline adherence from the list: Significantly behind, Behind schedule, On schedule, Ahead of schedule.
* **Budget Management** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your experience with budget management from the list: Significantly over, Over budget, On budget, Under budget.
* **Main Challenges Encountered** (`text`, Optional): Please describe any main challenges you faced during the project.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`text`, Optional): Please suggest any areas for improvement.
* **Would You Recommend this Process to Others?** (`select_one`, Required): Please select whether you would recommend this process to others from the list: Definitely not, Probably not, Maybe, Probably yes, Definitely yes.
