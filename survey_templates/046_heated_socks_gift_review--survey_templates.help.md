# Heated Socks Gift Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback about a heated socks gift. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction with the gift and provide suggestions for future product improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Heated Socks Gift Review" form from the platform.
2. Review the form fields and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Gift Information
**Gift Information** (`Gift Information`, `text`, required): This is the first page of the form where you can start filling in the required information. Please provide any details about the gift you received, such as the gift's condition, content, and any notable features.

### Recipient Details
**Recipient Details** (`Recipient Details`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of person who received the gift from the options provided. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.

### Gift Details
**Gift Details** (`Gift Details`, `text`, required): Provide more specific information about the gift you received, such as its size, color, or any other features that might be relevant to your experience.

### Were you Glad with the Gift
**Were you Glad with the Gift** (`Were you Glad with the Gift`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with the gift. Please select all that apply from the options provided.

### What would you improve?
**What would you improve?** (`What would you improve?`, `note`, required): This is a free text field where you can suggest improvements or suggestions for the gift.

### Gift Suggestions
**Gift Suggestions** (`Gift Suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or ideas about the gift, please type them in this field. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have anything to add.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve our products and services.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact our support team.
