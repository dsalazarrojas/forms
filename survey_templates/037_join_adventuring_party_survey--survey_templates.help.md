# Join Adventuring Party Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users who want to join an adventuring party. It will help us to tailor the adventure experience to their preferences and ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for us to understand your preferences and tailor the adventure experience.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, based on your preferences.
3. Enter your player name if you would like to be identified during the adventure.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information** (player_details): Please enter your player information, such as your character class, experience, and role in the party.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address, this will be used to contact you about your adventure experience.
* **Adventure Experience** (experience): Choose your level of adventure experience (1-100).
* **Party Preferences** (party_preferences): Describe your party preferences, such as any specific roles or features you would like to play.
* **Adventure Type** (adventure_type): Choose the type of adventure you would like to participate in (e.g. Adventure Story or Adventure Map).
* **Difficulty Level** (difficulty_level): Select the difficulty level of the adventure you prefer (Easy, Medium, or Hard).
* **Party Size** (party_size): Choose the number of players you would like to join with.
* **Adventure Length** (adventure_length): Select the length of the adventure you would like to participate in (Short, Medium, or Long).
* **Character Class** (character_class): Choose your character class for the adventure (Wizard, Warrior, or Rogue).
* **Role in Party** (role): Select your role within the party (Leader or Member).
* **Experience Points** (experience_2): Enter the number of experience points you have earned during previous adventures.
* **Adventure Length 2** (adventure_length_2): Select the length of the adventure you would like to participate in (Short, Medium, or Long).
* **Player Name** (player_name): Enter your player name, if you would like to be identified during the adventure.
* **Adventure Settings** (adventure_settings): Enter any additional adventure settings or preferences you have (optional).
* **Experience Points 2** (experience_2): Enter the number of experience points you have earned during previous adventures (optional).

Note: All fields with an asterisk (\*) are required to be filled out.
