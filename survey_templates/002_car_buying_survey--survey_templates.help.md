# Car Buying Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Buying Survey is designed to gather information about your car buying preferences, habits, and expectations. This survey will help us understand your needs and behaviors when purchasing a vehicle, enabling us to provide better services and products tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly, and select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
2. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experience.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to explain or provide additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" section.
4. Once you've completed the survey, click the submit button to send your responses for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your name** (Page 1, `respondent_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No

- **Your email address** (Page 1, `respondent_email`): Enter your email address that we can use to follow up with you after the survey.
  * Type: Email field
  * Required: No

- **How often do you purchase a vehicle?** (Page 2, `purchase_frequency`): Choose how often you buy a vehicle, such as Every 2-3 years, Every 3-5 years, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: Yes

- **What is your primary reason for purchasing a car?** (Page 2, `primary_purchase_driver`): Choose the main reason why you buy a car, such as Replacement, Upgrade, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: Yes

- **How do you typically research vehicles?** (Page 3, `research_method`): Select all methods you use to research vehicles, such as Online Reviews, Dealer Websites, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question with multiple selections
  * Required: No

- **How comfortable are you shopping for cars online?** (Page 3, `online_shopping_comfort`): Rate your comfort level when shopping for cars online, such as Very Comfortable, Comfortable, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: Yes

- **How flexible is your budget?** (Page 4, `budget_flexibility`): Choose how flexible you are with your budget, such as Very Flexible, Fixed within 5%, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Typical consideration period before purchase** (Page 4, `consideration_timeframe`): Select the typical time frame you take before making a purchase, such as Less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Dealer interaction preference** (Page 5, `dealer_interaction_preference`): Choose your preference for interacting with dealers, such as In-Person Only, Mostly In-Person, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **How much does financing affect your purchase decision?** (Page 5, `financing_influence`): Rate the importance of financing in your decision-making process, such as Critically Important, Not Important, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: Yes

- **Are you loyal to a specific car brand?** (Page 5, `brand_loyalty`): Choose your level of brand loyalty, such as True, Very Loyal, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Environmental impact - how important?** (Page 6, `environmental_concern`): Rate the importance of environmental impact in your decision-making process, such as Very Important, Not Important, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Do safety ratings influence your purchase?** (Page 6, `safety_rating_influence`): Choose how often safety ratings influence your purchase, such as Always, Rarely, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **How important are modern technology features?** (Page 7, `technology_features_importance`): Rate the importance of modern technology features, such as Must Have, Not Important, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **How satisfied were you with your previous vehicle?** (Page 7, `previous_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction level with your previous vehicle, such as Very Satisfied, N/A, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Would you recommend this car to others?** (Page 8, `recommendation_likelihood`): Choose whether you would recommend this car to others, such as Definitely, Probably Not, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Price vs Quality - what matters more?** (Page 8, `price_vs_quality_priority`): Choose what matters more to you, such as Price is Priority, Quality is Priority, etc.
  * Type: Multiple-choice question
  * Required: No

- **Any additional feedback or suggestions?** (Page 9, `additional_feedback`): Use this space to provide any comments, thoughts, or suggestions about your car buying experience.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
