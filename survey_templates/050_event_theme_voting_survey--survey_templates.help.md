# Event Theme Voting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your preferences for event themes. You will be asked to choose a theme and provide a brief description of what it means to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred event theme by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the theme you've chosen in the text fields below each theme option.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your response.
