# Event Theme Voting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your preferences for event themes. You will be asked to choose a theme and provide a brief description of what it means to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred event theme by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the theme you've chosen in the text fields below each theme option.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Theme 1** (`theme_choices`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more themes that interest you the most.
* **Theme 1 description** (`theme_description_1`, text, required): Provide a brief description of what Theme 1 means to you.
* **Theme 2** (`theme_choices`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more themes that interest you the most.
* **Theme 2 description** (`theme_description_2`, text, required): Provide a brief description of what Theme 2 means to you.
* **Theme 3** (`theme_choices`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more themes that interest you the most.
* **Theme 3 description** (`theme_description_3`, text, required): Provide a brief description of what Theme 3 means to you.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, date, optional): Note: This field is not actually a date field but a submit button. The form will automatically submit your responses when you click it. However, this field is required to be present for the form to work properly.
