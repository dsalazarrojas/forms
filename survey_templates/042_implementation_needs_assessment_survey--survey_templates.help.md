# Implementation Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help project teams assess their current implementation needs, identify potential challenges, and gather information about the project's scope, goals, and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review the project details and ensure that you have access to the necessary information.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering questions that require text responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Enter a brief description of your project, including its title, scope, and objectives.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`): Indicate if your project faces any challenges, such as technical difficulties or resource limitations.
* **Resources** (`resources`): Select if you have sufficient resources to complete the project, such as personnel, budget, and materials.
* **Timelines** (`timelines`): Enter the project's start and end dates, or estimated completion date if it is not yet known.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Choose the current status of your project, either Active or Inactive.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`): Enter the name of the project's consultant or team member responsible for the project's management.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Enter the name of the organization or company responsible for the project's implementation.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact information for the project's point of contact.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments that are relevant to the project's implementation.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments that are relevant to the project's management.
* **Resource Plan** (`resource_plan`): Enter a description of your project's resource plan, including personnel, budget, and materials.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`): Enter the name of the project's manager or team leader.
* **Implementation Team** (`implementation_team`): Enter the names of the team members involved in the project's implementation.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`): Enter a description of your project's scope, including its objectives and deliverables.
* **Project Goals** (`project_goals`): Enter the project's goals and objectives.
* **Project Objectives** (`project_objectives`): Enter the project's specific objectives and outcomes.
* **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`): Enter the project's expected outcomes.
* **Project Success Criteria** (`project_success_criteria`): Enter the criteria for measuring the project's success.
* **Project Success Indicators** (`project_success_indicators`): Enter the indicators for measuring the project's success.
* **Project Risks** (`project_risks`): Enter any potential risks or challenges that the project may face.
* **Project Constraints** (`project_constraints`): Enter any constraints or limitations that affect the project's implementation.
* **Project Scope Items** (`project_scope_items`): Enter the items included in the project's scope.
* **Project Scope Outcomes** (`project_scope_outcomes`): Enter the outcomes expected from the project's scope.
