# Academic Conference Participation Barriers Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to understand the barriers that individuals face when participating in academic conferences. The goal is to gather information on the obstacles that prevent or discourage people from attending conferences, which can inform efforts to improve conference accessibility and inclusivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best fits your situation.
4. For text boxes, provide a brief but clear answer.
5. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, use the "Any additional comments" text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Academic Conference Participation Barriers Survey --** (`survey_intro`, `note`, optional): This is an introduction to the survey, which explains its purpose and importance.
* **Full name (optional)** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Please provide your full name, which will help us better understand your response.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the email address associated with your account, which we may use to contact you for further information.
* **Current academic position** (`academic_position`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current academic position from the list.
* **Primary field of study or research** (`field_of_study`, `text`, required): Describe your primary field of study or research.
* **Country of residence** (`country_of_residence`, `text`, required): Specify the country where you reside.
* **How many academic conferences have you attended in the last 3 years** (`conferences_attended`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of conferences you have attended in the last 3 years.
* **-- Barriers to Participation --** (`barriers_section`, `note`, optional): This is an introduction to the barriers questions, which will help you think about what obstacles you face.
* **Have financial constraints prevented you from attending a conference** (`financial_barriers`, `select_one`, required): Choose how financial constraints have affected your conference participation.
* **Which financial barriers have you experienced** (`specific_financial_barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all the financial barriers that apply to you.
* **Which institutional barriers have you experienced** (`institutional_barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all the institutional barriers that apply to you.
* **Which personal barriers have you faced** (`personal_barriers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all the personal barriers that apply to you.
* **Has the availability of virtual conferences reduced your barriers** (`virtual_conferences`, `select_one`, required): Choose how the availability of virtual conferences has affected your participation.
* **What conference format do you prefer** (`preferred_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred conference format.
* **What support would most help you attend conferences** (`support_needed`, `text`, optional): Describe the support you think would be most helpful for conference participation.
* **Any additional comments about conference participation barriers** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional thoughts or comments about conference participation barriers.
