# PCJ Evaluation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PCJ Evaluation Feedback Form is used to collect specific feedback from users on the PCJ evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Review the form and ensure you have completed the PCJ evaluation process.
* Answer each question in the provided fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the PCJ evaluation process on a scale from 1-5.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the evaluation process, also on a scale from 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide specific feedback on what we did well during the PCJ evaluation process.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Suggest areas we could improve on.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, `note`, required: false): This is a placeholder field for any additional notes or comments.
