# Home Renovation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their home renovation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all required fields marked with a red asterisk (*) as they are essential to providing a complete response.
2. For the "overall_experience" question, select one of the options provided.
3. In the "renovation_services" field, select all the services you received during your renovation project.
4. In the "communication" field, provide a brief note about your experience with communication.
5. For the "project_manager_name", "project_manager_email", and "project_manager_phone" fields, provide the name, email, and phone number of your project manager.
6. In the "project_manager_comments" field, provide any additional comments about your project manager.
7. If required, select a rating for your project manager's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Renovation Feedback** (`1`): Describe your overall experience with the home renovation project.
* **Overall Experience** (`2`): How would you rate your overall experience with this project? (Select one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4)
* **Renovation Services** (`3`): Select all the services you received during your renovation project. (Select all that apply.)
* **Communication** (`4`): Briefly describe your experience with communication during the project.
* **Project Manager Name** (`6`): Enter the name of your project manager.
* **Project Manager Email** (`7`): Enter the email of your project manager.
* **Project Manager Phone** (`8`): Enter the phone number of your project manager.
* **Project Manager Comments** (`9`): Add any additional comments about your project manager.
* **Project Manager Rating** (`11`): Select a rating for your project manager's performance. (If not required, you can leave this field blank.) 
* **Project Manager Rating Scale** (`12`): Select the scale for your project manager's performance.
