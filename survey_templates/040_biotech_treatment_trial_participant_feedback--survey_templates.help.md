# Biotech Treatment Trial Participant Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in the Biotech Treatment Trial. The information gathered will help us better understand your experiences, challenges, and satisfaction with the treatment and the trial process. Your participation is crucial in helping us improve the treatment and make it more effective for all participants. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
2. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask and we will do our best to address them.
3. For multiple choice questions, please select the option that best describes your experience.
4. For open-ended questions (e.g., side effects description), please type in your answers in the space provided.
5. If you're unsure of an answer, it's okay to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter your personal details.
* **Participant ID**: Your unique study identification number.
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, Prefer Not to Say, Other).
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number.
* **Trial Information**: Briefly describe your experience in the trial.
* **Trial Name**: Name of the clinical trial.
* **Trial ID**: Trial identification number.
* **Treatment Arm**: Select your assigned treatment group.
* **Enrollment Date**: Date you joined the trial.
* **Treatment Duration**: Select how long you have been in treatment (Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, More than 1 year).
* **Current Visit Number**: Number of this visit.
* **Feedback Date**: Date of this feedback.
* **Treatment Experience**: Briefly describe your experiences with the treatment.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the treatment (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Treatment Effectiveness**: Select how effective you feel the treatment is (Very Effective, Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, Not Effective At All, Too Early to Tell).
* **Symptom Improvement**: Select how much improvement you have felt in your symptoms (Significant Improvement, Moderate Improvement, Slight Improvement, No Change, Slight Worsening, Moderate Worsening, Significant Worsening).
* **Quality of Life**: Select how your quality of life has changed (Much Better, Somewhat Better, No Change, Somewhat Worse, Much Worse).
* **Side Effects**: If you have experienced any side effects, select all that apply.
* **Side Effects Severity**: Select the severity of the side effects (Mild - Tolerable, Moderate - Uncomfortable, Severe - Difficult to Tolerate, Very Severe - Debilitating, Not Applicable).
* **Side Effects Management**: Select how well side effects are managed (Very Well Managed, Well Managed, Adequately Managed, Poorly Managed, Not Applicable).
* **Side Effects Description**: Briefly describe your side effects.
* **Treatment Administration**: Briefly describe your experience receiving the treatment.
* **Administration Method**: Select how the treatment is administered (Intravenous Infusion, Injection, Oral Medication, Topical Application, Inhalation, Other).
* **Administration Comfort**: Select how comfortable you are with the treatment administration (Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, Very Uncomfortable).
* **Visit Experience**: Briefly describe your experience with the clinic visits.
* **Wait Time**: Select how long you typically wait (Less than 15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 1-2 hours, More than 2 hours).
* **Staff Courtesy**: Select how you would rate the staff's courtesy (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Facility Cleanliness**: Select how you rate the facility's cleanliness (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Communication Quality**: Select how well you feel the staff communicates (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Information Clarity**: Select how clear the information is provided (Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, Very Unclear).
* **Concerns Addressed**: Select how well your concerns are addressed (Always, Usually, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
* **Treatment Burden**: Briefly describe how burdensome participation is.
* **Time Burden**: Select how time-consuming participation is (Not Burdensome, Slightly Burdensome, Moderately Burdensome, Very Burdensome, Extremely Burdensome).
* **Travel Burden**: Select how difficult travel is (Not Difficult, Slightly Difficult, Moderately Difficult, Very Difficult, Extremely Difficult).
* **Financial Burden**: Select how much a financial impact there is (No Financial Burden, Minimal Burden, Moderate Burden, Significant Burden).
* **Daily Life Impact**: Select how participation affects your daily life (No Impact, Minimal Impact, Moderate Impact, Significant Impact, Major Impact).
* **Continuation Willingness**: Select if you would continue participation (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Recommendation**: Select if you would recommend this trial (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Positive Aspects**: Briefly describe what has been positive.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Briefly describe areas that could be improved.
* **Questions or Concerns**: If you have any questions or concerns, type them here.
* **Contact Preference**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone Call, Email, Text Message, Mail, Any Method).
* **Follow-up Permission**: Select if you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Yes - Please Contact Me, Yes - Only If Necessary, No - Do Not Contact).
* **Feedback Confidentiality**: Select if you understand that your feedback is confidential (I Understand My Feedback Is Confidential, I Agree to Provide Honest Feedback).

Note to participants: Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and it will help us to better understand your experiences and improve the treatment and the trial process. Please answer each question accurately and honestly, and if you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask. 

This guide is for reference only and should be used in conjunction with any other instructions or guidance provided by the study team. 

Thank you for your participation.
