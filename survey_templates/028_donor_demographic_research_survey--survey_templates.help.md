## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and contact details from donors, as well as their answers to survey questions, to aid organizations and researchers in their studies or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Demographic Information page.
	* This page includes the following fields:
		+ <Demographic Information>
	* Fill out the demographic information fields, which are: *your name*, *your email*, and *other contact details*. This information helps us understand your background.
2. Move on to the Contact Details page.
	* Fill out the required contact fields with your *name*, *email*, and *phone number*. This ensures we can get in touch with you.
3. Proceed to the Survey Questions page.
	* Answer the survey questions truthfully and concisely. This will provide valuable insights for our research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Demographic Information
* **Name** (text, required): Your full name.

* **Other Contact Details** (text, optional): Any other contact information you'd like to share with us.

### Contact Details
* **Name** (text, required): Your name, as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email** (text, required): Your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Your phone number for further communication.

### Survey Questions
* **Survey Questions** (text, optional): Answer our survey questions truthfully and to the best of your ability. This will help us gain valuable insights for our research.

### Survey Answers
* **Survey Answers** (text, optional): This is where you provide detailed answers to the survey questions. Please answer concisely and truthfully.
