# Baal Veer Series Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baal Veer Series Quiz is designed to gauge the audience's knowledge and enthusiasm for the Baal Veer series. It assesses participants' level of fandom, knowledge of the show's key events, and overall impression of the series. The data collected will help us to better understand our audience's engagement and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your name and email address in the first two fields:**
	* Enter your first and last name in the **Your Name** field.
	* Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
2. **Select your age group:**
	* Choose a category that best describes your age group from the dropdown menu.
3. **Rate your level of fandom:**
	* Select how much of a Baal Veer fan you are from the options provided.
4. **Answer the quiz questions:**
	* For each question, choose the correct answer from the options provided.
5. **Rate the series (optional):**
	* If you'd like to share your opinion, rate the series from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
6. **Share your favorite moment (optional):**
	* Describe your favorite moment from the series.
7. **Recommend the series (optional):**
	* Choose if you'd recommend the series to others.
8. **Leave any other feedback (optional):**
	* Share any additional thoughts or comments about the series.

- **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Your name is the name you'd like to be known by.

- **Question 6 - Which Network Aired Baal Veer?** (`question_6`, select_one, required): Select the correct network from the options provided.
- **Question 7 - What Is Your Favorite Episode?** (`question_7`, text, optional): Describe your favorite episode.
