# Workshop Participation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about workshop participation, including general details, workshop specifics, and feedback/ratings from session participants. This data will help us understand how the workshop was received and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your General Information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide information about the workshop, such as date attended, location, and duration.
3. Share your experience and rating of the workshop (on a scale of 1-5).
4. Offer comments about your experience.
5. Identify the session participants, their count, gender, and age range.
6. Provide information about the event you attended, including comments and whether the event organizer was present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Field 1)
**General Information** (`text`, required: false)
Enter a brief description of yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Workshop Details (Field 2)
**Workshop Details** (`text`, required: false)
Enter details about the workshop, such as date attended, location, and duration.

### Session Information (Field 3)
**Session Information** (`text`, required: false)
Enter information about the session, such as session type and session leader.

### Rating (Field 4)
**Rating** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".

### Rating Comment (Field 5)
**Rating Comment** (`text`, required: false)
Enter a brief comment about your rating.

### Session Rating (Field 6)
**Session Rating** (`number`, required: false)
Rate your experience with the session on a scale of 1-5.

### Session Comments (Field 7)
**Session Comments** (`text`, required: false)
Enter comments about your experience with the session.

### Next Steps (Field 8)
**Next Steps** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate what you plan to do next as a result of attending the workshop, such as "Improve" or "Enhance".

### Next Steps Reason (Field 9)
**Next Steps Reason** (`text`, required: false)
Explain why you plan to take the next steps you've indicated.

### Participant Info (Field 10)
**Participant Info** (`text`, required: false)
Enter a brief description of yourself, including any relevant information about your role or affiliation.

### Participant Comments (Field 11)
**Participant Comments** (`text`, required: false)
Enter comments about your participant experience.

### Session Participants (Field 12)
**Session Participants** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate which participants attended the session, including the number and gender of each.

### Session Participants Count (Field 13)
**Session Participants Count** (`number`, required: false)
Enter the count of participants in the session.

### Session Participants Gender (Field 14)
**Session Participants Gender** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate the gender of each participant.

### Session Participants Age (Field 15)
**Session Participants Age** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate the age range of each participant.

### Event Info (Field 16)
**Event Info** (`text`, required: false)
Enter details about the event you attended.

### Event Comments (Field 17)
**Event Comments** (`text`, required: false)
Enter comments about the event.

### Event Organizer (Field 18)
**Event Organizer** (`text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the event organizer.

### Event Organizer Info (Field 19)
**Event Organizer Info** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Indicate whether the event organizer was present or not.

### Event Organizer Comments (Field 20)
**Event Organizer Comments** (`text`, required: false)
Enter comments about the event organizer.

### Workshop Info (Field 21)
**Workshop Info** (`text`, required: false)
Enter details about the workshop, such as date and location.

### Workshop Comments (Field 22)
**Workshop Comments** (`text`, required: false)
Enter comments about the workshop.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* Be as detailed and honest in your responses as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
