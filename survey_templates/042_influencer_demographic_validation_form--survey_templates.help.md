# influencer_demographic_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information from influencers for validation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field, paying attention to the hint text for additional guidance.
3. Double-check that you have not already been previously validated to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Demographics (text)**: A short description of the person filling out the form.
* **Name (text)**: Enter your full name.
* **Location (text)**: Enter your city or location.
* **Gender (select_one)**: Select your gender (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say).
* **Age (number)**: Enter your age.
* **Occupation (text)**: Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Income (text)**: Enter your income level or range.
* **Social Media (select_multiple)**: Select all the social media platforms you use (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, Other).
* **Email (email)**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Website (text)**: Enter your website or blog URL.
* **Timezone (time)**: Select your time zone.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email Opt-out (select_one)**: Indicate whether you would like to opt-out of emails.
* **Phone Opt-out (select_one)**: Indicate whether you would like to opt-out of phone calls.
* **Email Frequency (select_one)**: Select how often you would like to receive emails (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Never).
* **Email Format (select_one)**: Select the format of your email address (Business, Personal).
* **Email Provider (select_one)**: Select your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, AOL, Other).
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter additional contact information.
* **Contact Info 2 (text)**: Enter more contact information.
* **Social Media 1 (select_multiple)**: Select your first social media platform.
* **Social Media 2 (select_multiple)**: Select your second social media platform.
* **Social Media 3 (select_multiple)**: Select your third social media platform.
* **Social Media 4 (select_multiple)**: Select your fourth social media platform.
* **Email Frequency 2 (select_one)**: Select how often you would like to receive emails from this platform.
* **Email Format 2 (select_one)**: Select the format of your email address for this platform.
* **Email Provider 2 (select_one)**: Select your email provider for this platform.
* **Contact Info 3 (text)**: Enter more contact information.
* **Contact Info 4 (text)**: Enter even more contact information.
* **Social Media 4 (select_multiple)**: Select your fourth social media platform.
* **Social Media 5/6 (select_multiple)**: Select your additional social media platforms.
* **Email Frequency 3 (select_one)**: Select how often you would like to receive emails from this platform.
* **Email Format 3 (select_one)**: Select the format of your email address for this platform.
* **Email Provider 3 (select_one)**: Select your email provider for this platform.
* **Contact Info 5 (text)**: Enter even more contact information.
* **Contact Info 6 (text)**: Enter even more contact information.
