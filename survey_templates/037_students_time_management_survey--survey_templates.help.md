# Students Time Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect data on students' daily, weekly, and monthly time management habits. Your input will help us understand how you spend your time and identify areas where we can provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number.
2. Answer the questions about your daily, weekly, and monthly time management habits, including what you typically do during these periods.
3. Indicate your study habits, including any time-saving strategies.
4. Estimate the time you waste on activities and the time you save.
5. Finally, describe your academic commitment, including any challenges you face.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your official university email address.
- **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Study Area** (5, text, required): Describe the area where you usually study.
- **Daily Time Management** (6, time, required): Estimate how you spend your time on a typical day.
- **Weekly Time Management** (7, time, required): Estimate how you spend your time on a typical week.
- **Monthly Time Management** (8, time, required): Estimate how you spend your time on a typical month.
- **Study Habits** (9, text, required): Describe your study habits, including any routines or strategies you use.
- **Time Wasted** (10, text, optional): Identify any activities that you consider time-wasting.
- **Time Saved** (11, text, optional): Describe any time-saving strategies you use.
- **Academic Commitment** (12, text, required): Describe your commitment to your academic responsibilities, including any challenges you face.
