# Volunteer Engagement Challenge Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Engagement Challenge Poll is a form used to collect information from users about their volunteer engagement experiences, including their name, email, organization, experience, and feedback.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email in the **Email** field.
3. Provide information about your organization in the **Organization** field.
4. Share your experience in the **Experience** field.
5. If you have any suggestions, you can type them in the **Suggestions** field.
6. Add any feedback you'd like to share in the **Feedback** field.
7. Select the tool that was assigned to you in the **Assigned Tool** field.
8. Upload your output file in the **Output File** field.
9. Enter your form ID in the **Form ID** field.
10. Select your category in the **Category** field.
11. Type a description of your experience in the **Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)**: This is where you enter your name.
* **Email (2)**: Please enter your email address.
* **Organization (4)**: Describe your organization, such as your university or company.
* **Experience (5)**: Share your experience with us, e.g., how long you've been volunteering, what projects you've worked on.
* **Suggestions (6)**: If you have any suggestions or ideas, type them here.
* **Feedback (7)**: Add any feedback you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool (8)**: Choose the tool that was assigned to you.
* **Output File (9)**: Upload your output file here.
* **Form ID (10)**: Enter your form ID, which can be found in the URL.
* **Category (11)**: Select the category that best describes your experience.
* **Description (12)**: Provide a brief description of your experience.
