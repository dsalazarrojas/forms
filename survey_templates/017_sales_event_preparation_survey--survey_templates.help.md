# Sales Event Preparation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to help assess the preparation status of sales events. It is intended for sales teams to provide information on the readiness of their events, allowing for better planning and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information for the sales event you are preparing.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge and experience with the event.
3. Answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
