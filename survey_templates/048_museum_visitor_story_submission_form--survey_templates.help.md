## Museum Visitor Story Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Museum Visitor Story Submission Form is a tool for museum visitors to share their experiences, stories, and feedback about their visit. This form will help the museum team understand the visitors' perspectives, allowing us to improve our services and make your next visit even better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details (Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Share your story about your visit, including any interesting or remarkable experiences.
3. If you have attached a photo during your visit, select it in the provided field.
4. Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Information (form_section_1)**: This is the first section of the form where you can enter your basic contact details.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to in our records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Your Story (story)**: Share your story about your visit, including any interesting moments or experiences you had.
* **Have you attached a photo? (photo)**: If you have attached a photo during your visit, select the correct checkbox.
* **Select a photo (upload_photo)**: If you have a photo, select it from the provided list.
* **Feedback (feedback)**: If you have any suggestions or comments about your visit, share them here.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling out all the fields will help us better understand your experience and provide a more comprehensive report for our future improvements.
