# Product Designer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to apply for a Product Designer position at our company. Fill out this form to showcase your skills and experience in product design, and we'll review your application for potential future opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to all questions.
2. **Use a valid email address**. This will be used as your login information if you are selected for an interview.
3. **Enter a valid phone number**. We may contact you to discuss your application.
4. **Provide a link to your portfolio**, if applicable. This will help us get a better understanding of your work.
5. **Attach relevant work samples**, if possible. This will showcase your design skills and experience.
6. **Include your educational background**. This will help us assess your formal education and training.
7. **Link to your educational institutions**, if applicable. This will give us a clear understanding of your academic background.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (<name>)**: This is the first step to getting in touch with us. Please provide your name so we can personalize our response to you.
* **Email (<email>)**: Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached. This is how we will communicate with you about your application.
* **Phone (<phone>)**: Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you to discuss your application.
* **Portfolio Link (<portfolio_link>)**: If you have a website or platform showcasing your work, please provide the link here. This will give us a better understanding of your design skills and experience.
* **Portfolio Attachments (<portfolio_attachments>)**: If you have files or links to specific work samples, please attach them here. This will showcase your design skills and experience in a more concrete way.
* **Experience Description (<experience_description>)**: Describe your work experience, highlighting your role, responsibilities, and accomplishments.
* **Experience Company (<experience_company>)**: If you've worked with a company before, please provide the name here.
* **Experience Length (<experience_length>)**: If you've worked with any company, please indicate the length of your experience there.
* **Experience Title (<experience_title>)**: If you've worked with a company, please provide your job title there.
* **Experience Type (<experience_type>)**: Select the type of experience you had (e.g., Design Assistant, Designer, Freelancer, Other).
* **Work Sample (<work_sample>)**: Attach relevant work samples that showcase your design skills and experience.
* **Education (<education>)**: Describe your formal education, highlighting your relevant courses and training.
* **Education URL (<education_url>)**: If you have a website or platform showcasing your educational background, please provide the link here.

## Tips
* **Double-check your answers** for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* **Use clear and concise language** to help us get a better understanding of your application.
* **Only submit one application per person**, even if you have multiple applications to share.
* **Attach only relevant work samples** and documents to ensure our review process is efficient.
