## Beauty Service Environment Comfort Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the comfort and ambiance of our beauty service environment. It will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement to provide a more enjoyable and relaxing atmosphere for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question honestly, selecting the best response that reflects your experience. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk (*)).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the overall ambiance of the salon?** (overall_ambiance_rating): Rate your overall experience of the ambiance of the salon, using the 1-5 scale.
* **Was the lighting comfortable for your service?** (lighting_level): Select the option that best describes your experience with the lighting during your visit:
	+ Too Bright
	+ Just Right
	+ Too Dim
* **Was the temperature in the salon comfortable?** (temperature_comfort): Select the option that best describes your experience with the temperature during your visit:
	+ Too Warm
	+ Comfortable
	+ Too Cold
* **Was the background music at an appropriate volume?** (music_volume): Select the option that best describes your experience with the music during your visit:
	+ Too Loud
	+ Appropriate
	+ Too Quiet
	+ No Music
* **Was your service station clean and tidy?** (station_cleanliness): Select the option that best describes your experience with the cleanliness of your service station:
	+ Yes, impeccable
	+ Mostly clean
	+ Needs improvement
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the restrooms?** (restroom_cleanliness): Select the option that best describes your experience with the cleanliness of the restrooms:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Did not use
* **Did you observe proper tool sanitization practices?** (tool_sanitization_perception): Select the option that best describes your experience with the tool sanitization:
	+ True
	+ I believe so
	+ False
	+ I did not notice
* **How comfortable was the seating during your service?** (seating_comfort): Select the option that best describes your experience with the seating:
	+ Very Comfortable
	+ Somewhat Comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Uncomfortable
* **How would you rate the waiting area comfort?** (waiting_area_comfort): Rate your experience with the waiting area comfort, using the 1-5 scale.
* **Were you offered a beverage and was it satisfactory?** (beverage_offering_satisfaction): Select the option that best describes your experience with the beverage offering:
	+ Yes, very satisfied
	+ Yes, but could be better
	+ No, I was not offered
	+ N/A
* **How friendly was the staff during your visit?** (staff_friendliness): Rate your experience with the staff friendliness, using the 1-5 scale.
* **Did the staff present a professional appearance?** (professional_appearance): Select the option that best describes your experience with the staff appearance:
	+ True
	+ Somewhat
	+ False
* **Any suggestions to make our environment more comfortable?** (suggestion_for_environment): Share any suggestions or comments you have to improve our environment.

Note: This form requires answers to all fields marked with an asterisk (*). Make sure to fill out all required fields to provide a complete and honest feedback.
