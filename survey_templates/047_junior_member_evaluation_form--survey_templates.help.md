# Junior Member Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Junior Member Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and performance metrics from junior members in the organization. This form is used to evaluate their performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide suggestions for development goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. If a field is not applicable, please mark it as "No" (for select_multiple fields) or enter a brief explanation (for text fields).
3. Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers whenever possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Junior Member Evaluation** (1): This field is a text field where you can provide a brief evaluation of the junior member's performance and behavior.
* **Performance Metrics** (2): Please select all the performance metrics that apply to the junior member's performance:
	+ Yes: Indicates that the junior member has met the performance metric.
	+ No: Indicates that the junior member has not met the performance metric.
* **Development Goals** (3): Please enter specific development goals or areas of improvement for the junior member.
* **Strengths and Areas for Improvement** (4): This field is where you can describe the junior member's strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Junior Member Feedback** (5): Please provide any additional feedback or comments about the junior member's performance, behavior, or suggestions for improvement.
* **Manager Feedback** (6): This is where the manager can provide feedback or comments about the junior member.

## Tips
- Be objective and honest in your evaluation.
- Focus on providing specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
- If you're unsure about a field, please reach out to your supervisor or mentor for guidance.
