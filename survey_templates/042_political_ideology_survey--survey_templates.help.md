# Political Ideology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Ideology Survey is a tool used to measure the political views, party affiliations, and interests of individuals. It is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of your political standpoint.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the best description of your political ideology from the options provided (page 1).
2. Choose the parties you are affiliated with or interested in (page 1).
3. Provide a brief description of your political viewpoint (page 2).
4. Select the area of politics that interests you the most (page 3).
5. Choose the issues that matter most to you in politics (page 3).
6. Select whether you are male, female, or both in terms of the survey (page 4).
7. Enter the date you completed this survey (page 4).
8. Select the time of day you completed this survey (page 4).
9. Provide any additional comments or notes about your political views (page 5).
10. Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (page 6).
11. Enter your phone number for contact purposes (page 6).
12. Provide any additional comments or notes (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Political Ideology** (`political_ideology_survey`, select_one, page 1): Select the best description of your political ideology. This could be Conservative, Liberal, Moderate, or Progressive.
* **Political Party Affiliation** (`political_party_affiliation`, select_multiple, page 1): Choose the parties you are affiliated with or interested in. You can select multiple options.
* **Political Viewpoint** (`political_viewpoint`, text, page 2): Provide a brief description of your political viewpoint. Please keep this short and to the point.
* **Political Party Identifier** (`political_party_identifier`, select_one, page 2): Select the party you identify with the most.
* **Political Interest** (`political_interest_in_ideology`, select_multiple, page 3): Choose the area of politics that interests you the most. Select all that apply.
* **Political Party Interests** (`political_party_interests`, select_multiple, page 3): Choose the issues that matter most to you in politics. Select all that apply.
* **Political Surveyed** (`political_surveyed`, select_one, page 4): Select whether you are male, female, or both in terms of the survey.
* **Political Survey Date** (`political_survey_date`, date, page 4): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Political Survey Time** (`political_survey_time`, time, page 4): Select the time of day you completed this survey.
* **Political Survey Note** (`political_survey_note`, note, page 5): Provide any additional comments or notes about your political views.
* **Political Survey Email** (`political_survey_email`, email, page 6): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Political Survey Phone** (`political_survey_phone`, text, page 6): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Political Survey Note 2** (`political_survey_note_2`, note, page 7): Provide any additional comments or notes.
