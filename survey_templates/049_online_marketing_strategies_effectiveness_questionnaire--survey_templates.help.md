## Online Marketing Strategies Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the effectiveness of various online marketing strategies in your organization. It aims to gather information on the channels and roles involved, as well as challenges faced and strategies used. This will help us better understand how you use online marketing, what works best for you, and what challenges you face. Please ensure that this is the first time you're completing this form.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read through each question carefully and answer as accurately as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the questionnaire. You can provide any additional context or information that you feel is necessary.
* **Marketing channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks you to select all the marketing channels you use. Please choose from:
	+ Digital (e.g., websites, social media, online ads)
	+ Social media (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram)
	+ Email (e.g., newsletters, promotional emails)
	+ Print (e.g., flyers, brochures)
	+ Offline (e.g., events, trade shows)
* **How often do you use marketing channels?** (`marketing_channels_frequency`, `number`, required: true): Here, you'll be asked to provide a numerical answer indicating how often you use each selected marketing channel.
* **What is the most persuasive marketing channel?** (`marketing_channels_persuasion`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the marketing channel that is most persuasive to you.
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks you to select the roles involved in your online marketing strategy. Choose from:
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Product
	+ Customer service
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Industries** (`industries`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the industries you operate in. You can choose more than one industry.
* **Challenges you face using online marketing** (`challenges`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to share, provide any challenges you face when using online marketing.
* **What's the most effective online marketing strategy?** (`effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the online marketing strategy that you find most effective.
* **Comparison of online marketing strategies** (`comparison`, `note`, required: false): This is a space to compare and contrast the effectiveness of different online marketing strategies. Feel free to share your thoughts and experiences.
