# Ocean Plastic Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' awareness and behavior related to ocean plastic pollution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions on each page according to your experiences and habits.
2.  Select the correct options for each question to provide us with accurate information.
3.  You may choose to answer all or some of the questions; there are no required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input**: 
    - **User Input** (optional): You can write any additional information or comments about your experiences or concerns related to ocean plastic pollution.
- **Questions**: 
    - **How often do you go to the beach?** (select one):
        - Beach (If you regularly visit the beach)
        - Never (If you never visit the beach)
        - Occasionally (If you occasionally visit the beach)
        - Frequently (If you frequently visit the beach)
    - **Have you ever picked up trash on the beach?** (select one):
        - True (If you have picked up trash on the beach)
        - False (If you haven't picked up trash on the beach)
    - **Do you use reusable bags for shopping?** (select one):
        - True (If you always use reusable bags)
        - False (If you don't use reusable bags)
    - **Do you avoid buying products with single-use plastic?** (select one):
        - True (If you always avoid buying products with single-use plastic)
        - False (If you don't avoid buying products with single-use plastic)
    - **Do you carry a refillable water bottle?** (select one):
        - True (If you always carry a refillable water bottle)
        - False (If you don't carry a refillable water bottle)
    - **Do you participate in beach cleanups?** (select one):
        - True (If you participate in beach cleanups)
        - False (If you don't participate in beach cleanups)
    - **Do you support organizations working to reduce plastic pollution?** (select one):
        - True (If you support such organizations)
        - False (If you don't support such organizations)
    - **Do you think you'll change your behavior to reduce plastic pollution in the next 6 months?** (select one):
        - True (If you plan to change your behavior)
        - False (If you don't plan to change your behavior)
    - **Do you think you'll tell others about ocean plastic pollution?** (select one):
        - True (If you plan to share information about ocean plastic pollution)
        - False (If you don't plan to share information about ocean plastic pollution)
