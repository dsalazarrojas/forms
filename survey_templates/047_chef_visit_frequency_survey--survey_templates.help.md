# Chef Visit Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chef Visit Frequency Survey is designed to collect feedback from visitors to a chef-led culinary experience. This survey aims to understand your visit frequency, preferences, and satisfaction with the food and chef interaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page and fill in the required fields.
2. Please answer each question carefully, as your feedback will help us improve our services.
3. If a question is not applicable to your experience, feel free to note it in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Visitor Context --**: This section provides a brief context for your visit.
* **Guest Name (Optional)**: If you wish to share your name, please type it here. This information will help us understand your feedback better.
* **How often do you visit for a chef-led experience?** (`Weekly`, `Bi-Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Once every few months`, or `First-time visitor`): Select the frequency of your visits to help us understand your behavior.
* **What is the primary reason for your visit?** (`Special Occasion`, `Culinary Learning`, `Business Dining`, `Casual Interest`, or `Recommended by Friend`): Choose the reason that best describes your visit.
* **-- Chef Experience --**: This section provides a brief context for your experience with the chef.
* **Satisfaction with Chef Interaction**: (`Very Satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Unsatisfied`): Rate your satisfaction with the chef's interaction and explanation of dishes.
* **Overall Quality of Food**: (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Average`, `Poor`): Rate the quality of food you experienced.
* **Satisfaction with Menu Variety**: (`Highly Satisfied`, `Satisfied`, `Needs More Variety`): Rate your satisfaction with the menu variety.
* **Which dish stood out to you?**: Type the name of the dish that you enjoyed the most.
* **-- Future Planning --**: This section provides a brief context for your future plans.
* **What other dishes or cuisines would you like to see?**: If you have any suggestions for new dishes or cuisines, please type them here.
* **How likely are you to return for another visit?** (`Very Likely`, `Likely`, `Unlikely`): Rate your likelihood of returning for another visit.
* **Preferred time for chef visits**: (`Lunch`, `Early Dinner`, `Late Dinner`, `Weekend Brunch`): Select the time that suits you best for chef visits.
* **Any other comments for our hospitality team?**: If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please type them here.
* **Date of Visit Surveyed**: This date will be automatically populated with the current date.

Note: Please ensure you are completing this form honestly and accurately to help us improve our services. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
