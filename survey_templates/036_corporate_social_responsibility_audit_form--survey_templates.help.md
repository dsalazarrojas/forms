**Corporate Social Responsibility Audit Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Audit Form is designed to evaluate your company's commitment to social responsibility. This form helps you assess your company's progress in areas such as CSR initiatives, strategies, and stakeholder engagement. It's a tool for your company to reflect on its social and environmental impact and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in one of the following sections: 
    *   Page 1: Introduction 
    *   Page 2: Company Information 
    *   Page 3: CSR Initiatives 
    *   Page 4: CSR Strategies 
    *   Page 5: Stakeholder Engagement

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction

*   **Introduction** (<label>): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read this page to understand the context of the form.
*   **company** (<label>): This field is not applicable.

### Page 2: Company Information

*   **company** (<label>): Select the current state of your company's CSR initiatives, whether you have implemented them.
*   **initiative name** (<label>): Enter the name of your CSR initiative.
*   **initiative description** (<label>): Briefly describe your CSR initiative.
*   **initiative status** (<label>): Choose the status of your CSR initiative, such as whether it is ongoing, pending, or cancelled.
*   **initiative owners** (<label>): Select all the individuals who are involved with your CSR initiative.
*   **csr strategies** (<label>): Choose the strategies your company uses to implement CSR initiatives.
*   **strategy description** (<label>): Describe the CSR strategy you've chosen.
*   **strategy owners** (<label>): Select all the individuals who are involved with your CSR strategy.
*   **stakeholders** (<label>): Select if you have engaged with stakeholders in your CSR initiatives.
*   **stakeholder name** (<label>): Enter the name of the stakeholder you have engaged.
*   **stakeholder role** (<label>): Indicate whether the stakeholder is involved in CSR decisions.
*   **stakeholder feedback** (<label>): Enter feedback from the stakeholder.
*   **stakeholder feedback owners** (<label>): Select all the individuals who are involved with stakeholder feedback.
