# Post Appointment Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers after their interactions with the service. It allows us to identify areas for improvement and ensure that our services meet our customers' expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill out all questions marked as "required."
3. For questions that offer multiple options, select only one answer.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, use the "Additional Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the purpose of your visit?** (question_1, text, required)
This question is meant to understand the reason for your visit. Please provide a brief description of why you visited the service.
* **Was the staff friendly?** (question_2, select_one, required)
This question helps us understand how our staff treated you during your visit. Please select one of the provided options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the overall service experience?** (question_3, select_multiple, required)
This question is to gauge your overall satisfaction with the service. Select all applicable options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Would you recommend this service to others?** (question_4, select_one, required)
Please let us know if you would recommend our service to others. Select one of the provided options: Yes or No.
* **What can we do to improve?** (question_5, note, false)
If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our service, please type your thoughts here.
* **Additional Comments** (question_6, note, false)
If you have any additional comments or feedback that are not covered in other questions, please add them here.
* **Additional Comments** (question_7, note, false)
This is another opportunity to add any additional comments or feedback.
