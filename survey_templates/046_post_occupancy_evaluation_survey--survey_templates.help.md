# post_occupancy_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from occupants on their experience in the building or space after a certain period of time. Your input will help us understand how well the space meets your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or questions about a particular question, please feel free to ask. Each question is designed to elicit specific information about your experience in the space, and your input is invaluable in helping us improve the building.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`note`, required: false)
  This is a short introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **How Comfortable Do You Feel In Your Space?** (`number`, required: false)
  This question asks how comfortable you feel while in the building or space. Please rate your level of comfort on a scale, for example, 1 (not comfortable at all) to 5 (very comfortable).
* **How Satisfied Are You With Your Office Space?** (`number`, required: false)
  This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the space. Please rate your level of satisfaction on a scale, for example, 1 (not satisfied at all) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How Satisfied Are You With The Building's Functionality?** (`select_one`, required: false)
  This question asks about your satisfaction with the building's systems, such as lighting, air quality, temperature, noise levels, water quality, and safety. Please choose one of the options provided that best describes your feelings.
  Options: 
    - Building Systems
    - Lighting
    - Air Quality
    - Temperature
    - Noise Levels
    - Water Quality
    - Safety
    - Other
