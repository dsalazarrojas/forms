# Pro Social Bonding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your social behavior and preferences, helping us better understand your social network and group dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the "required" label).
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best describes your situation.
4. For fields with multiple options, select as many as apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age (2)**: Please enter your age.
* **sex (3)**: Select the option that best describes your sex.
* **education (4)**: This is a multiple-choice field; select all the types of education that apply to you.
* **Marital Status (5)**: Choose the option that best describes your marital status.
* **Employment Status (6)**: Select the option that best describes your employment status.
* **Group Size (8)**: Choose the option that best describes the size of your social group.
* **Frequency Participate (9)**: Select how often you participate in groups or social events.
* **Group Type (10)**: This is a multiple-choice field; select all the types of groups that apply to you.
* **Frequency Attend (11)**: Select how often you attend events or gatherings with groups.
* **Group Dedication (12)**: Choose the level of dedication you have for your social groups.
* **Frequency Contribute (13)**: Select how often you contribute to groups or causes.
* **Pro Social Behavior (14)**: This is a multiple-choice field; select all the options that apply to you.
* **Social Media (15)**: Choose all the social media platforms you use.
* **Email (16)**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone (17)**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Address (18)**: Enter your address (if you have one).
* **Comments (19)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submission 1-6 (21-26)**: Select one of the options for each of these fields (if applicable).
 
This form is designed to be thorough and helpful, so feel free to ask if you have any questions or concerns!
