# staff_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about staff satisfaction, which is essential for managers and HR teams to understand the sentiment and needs of their team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. For each question, select a rating or enter your answer according to the type of question.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers.
3. For questions with multiple select options, choose all that apply.
4. For text and date fields, provide clear and concise information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your job?** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): This question assesses your overall satisfaction with your job. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **How satisfied are you with your current work environment?** (`work_satisfaction`, number, required): This question measures your satisfaction with your work environment. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **How satisfied are you with your team?** (`team_satisfaction`, number, required): This question evaluates your satisfaction with your team. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **How satisfied are you with the balance between work and personal life?** (`work_life_balance`, number, required): This question gauges your satisfaction with the balance between work and personal life. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **How secure do you feel about your job?** (`job_security`, number, required): This question assesses your job security. Rate your security on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very insecure" and 5 being "very secure".
* **How satisfied are you with communication within the company?** (`communication`, number, required): This question measures your satisfaction with communication within the company. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **What feedback do you have for the management?** (`feedback`, text, required): This question allows you to provide feedback to the management. Please provide your thoughts and suggestions.
* **What is your contact information?** (`contact_info`, text, required): This question is to collect your contact information. Please enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Who is your manager?** (`manager`, text, required): This question is to identify your manager's name.
* **What department do you belong to?** (`department`, select_multiple, required): This question is to select your department. Choose all that apply.
* **How satisfied are you with your job performance?** (`job_performance`, number, required): This question evaluates your job performance. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required): This question is the same as the first question, overall job satisfaction. Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "very dissatisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **What is the date you submitted this survey?** (`date_submitted`, date, required): This question is to collect the date you submitted this survey. Please enter the date in the correct format.
* **What department do you belong to?** (`department`, select_multiple, required): This question is to select all departments that apply. Choose all that apply.

# Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your manager or HR representative.
