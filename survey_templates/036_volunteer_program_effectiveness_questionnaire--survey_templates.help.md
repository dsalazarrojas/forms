# Volunteer Program Effectiveness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Program Effectiveness Questionnaire is a tool used to evaluate and improve volunteer programs within an organization. It helps assess the program's quality, impact, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your evaluation of the volunteer program.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question, as indicated.
3. Ensure you answer every question marked as "required" in the form.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Program Quality** (id: 1, select_multiple, required): Assess the overall quality of the volunteer program. Choose one or multiple options that best describe your evaluation.
	+ Good: The program meets the expected standards.
	+ Poor: The program falls short of expected standards.
* **Program Impact** (id: 2, select_one, required): Evaluate the program's impact on the organization and its stakeholders.
	+ Option 1: Significant impact on the organization and stakeholders.
	+ Option 2: Moderate impact on the organization and stakeholders.
	+ Option 3: Some impact on the organization and stakeholders.
	+ Option 4: Limited impact on the organization and stakeholders.
	+ Option 5: No impact on the organization and stakeholders.
* **Program Participants** (id: 3, select_multiple, required): Determine the number of participants in the program.
	+ Yes: The program has active participants.
	+ No: There are no active participants.
* **Program Outcomes** (id: 4, select_multiple, required): Identify the outcomes achieved through the program.
	+ Yes: The program achieved its intended outcomes.
	+ No: The program did not achieve its intended outcomes.
* **Program Satisfaction** (id: 5, select_multiple, optional): Evaluate the satisfaction of participants with the program.
