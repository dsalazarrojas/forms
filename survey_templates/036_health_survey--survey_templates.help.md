# health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health survey form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history, symptoms, and treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant medical history and symptoms sections to answer the questions.
2. Enter the patient's information, including their name, contact details, and medical conditions.
3. Provide details about the patient's medication, allergies, and medical procedures.
4. Enter the patient's family history and doctor's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
5. Rate the doctor's services and provide any additional comments or recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: A text field to enter the patient's medical history.
* **Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms that apply to the patient.
* **Patient Info**: Select the patient's name from the options provided.
* **Doctor Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient's condition.
* **Date of Last Contact**: Enter the date of the patient's last contact with the doctor.
* **Time of Last Contact**: Enter the time of the last contact with the doctor.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select the medical conditions that apply to the patient.
* **Medication**: Select all the medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies**: Select all the allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Procedures**: Select all the medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Family History**: Enter the patient's family medical history.
* **Doctor Specialties**: Select the doctor's specialties.
* **Doctor**: Enter the doctor's name.
* **Phone**: Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the doctor's address.
* **Email**: Enter the doctor's email.
* **License**: Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Degree**: Select the doctor's degree.
* **Years of Service**: Enter the doctor's years of service.
* **Office Hours**: Enter the doctor's office hours.
* **Languages**: Select the languages spoken by the doctor.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the doctor.
* **Rating**: Rate the doctor's services.
* **Recommendation**: Enter any recommendations for the doctor.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments about the doctor.

**Tips**

* Please answer all the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, consult with the doctor or healthcare professional.
* This form is for the benefit of the patient's health and well-being. Please fill it out carefully to provide the best possible outcome.
