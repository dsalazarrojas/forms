# Skincare Product Refill Program Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from individuals interested in the Skincare Product Refill Program. We want to understand how you found out about our program, what type of products you're interested in, and how often you reorder our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the guidelines below and fill out each field accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's In Your Refill Program?* (Program Name):** This is a text field where you can provide the name of your refill program. Please enter a brief name that reflects your program's identity.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select one or more methods through which you heard about our program:
	+ Referral: A friend or family member recommended our program.
	+ Social Media: You found out about us through social media platforms.
	+ Online Search: You searched for us online.
	+ Friends: Your friends recommended our program.
* **What Product Type Do You Want?** (Select One): This field allows you to select the type of product you're interested in:
	+ Moisturizers: Products that moisturize and hydrate your skin.
	+ Exfoliants: Products that help remove dead skin cells.
	+ Serums: Concentrated liquid or gel-like products for specific skin concerns.
	+ Eye Cream: Products for the skin around your eyes.
	+ Other: If your preferred product type is not listed, select "Other" and provide more details.
* **How Often Do You Reorder?** (Select One): This field allows you to select how often you reorder our products:
	+ Daily: You reorder our products daily.
	+ Weekly: You reorder our products weekly.
	+ Bi-Weekly: You reorder our products every two weeks.
	+ Monthly: You reorder our products monthly.
	+ Never: You have not reorded our products yet.
* **How Would You Like To Contact Us?** (Select One): This field allows you to select your preferred contact method:
	+ Phone: Call us at our phone number.
	+ Email: Send us an email.
	+ Text: Send us a text message.
	+ In-App: Contact us through our app.
* **Do You Have Any Other Feedback?** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about our program.
* **What Products Are You Most Interested In?** (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select multiple products you're interested in:
	+ Moisturizers: Products that moisturize and hydrate your skin.
	+ Exfoliants: Products that help remove dead skin cells.
	+ Serums: Concentrated liquid or gel-like products for specific skin concerns.
	+ Eye Cream: Products for the skin around your eyes.
* **Do You Have Any Comments?** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about our program.
* **Email:** This field is where you can enter your email address. Please ensure you enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
