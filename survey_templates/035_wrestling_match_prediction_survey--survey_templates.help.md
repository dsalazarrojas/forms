# Wrestling Match Prediction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wrestling Match Prediction Survey is designed to collect user opinions on a wrestling match. The survey asks users to select the best options for various parameters of the match, such as the confidence level of each wrestler and the match result.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the first wrestler (Wrestler One) from the options provided.
* For each wrestler, select your confidence level (High, Medium, or Low) from the options.
* If you want to provide a reason for your confidence level, you can type it in the text box next to the confidence level.
* Select the expected match result from the options provided.
* Select the second wrestler (Wrestler Two) from the options provided.
* If you want to submit your prediction, select the "Submit" option. If you need to submit your prediction by other means, select "Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Wrestler One**: Select the first wrestler participating in the match. The options are numbered 1 and 2.
* **Score One's Confidence**: Select your confidence level in the outcome of the first wrestler. The options are High, Medium, and Low.
* **Score Two's Confidence**: Select your confidence level in the outcome of the second wrestler. The options are High, Medium, and Low.
* **Reason One**: Provide a reason for your confidence level of the first wrestler. Type your answer in the text box.
* **Reason Two**: Provide a reason for your confidence level of the second wrestler. Type your answer in the text box.
* **Reason Three**: Provide a reason for your confidence level of the second wrestler. Type your answer in the text box. (This field is optional, but if you want to provide an additional reason, you can use this field.)
* **Reason Four**: Provide a reason for your confidence level of the second wrestler. Type your answer in the text box. (This field is optional, but if you want to provide an additional reason, you can use this field.)
* **Reason Five**: Provide a reason for your confidence level of the second wrestler. Type your answer in the text box. (This field is optional, but if you want to provide an additional reason, you can use this field.)
* **Match Result**: Select your expected outcome of the match. The options are Option 1 and Option 2.
* **Select Wrestler Two**: Select the second wrestler participating in the match. The options are numbered 1 and 2.
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to submit your prediction. If you need to submit by other means, select "Other".
* **Submit Button**: This is a duplicate of the "Submit" field, but in a different format.
