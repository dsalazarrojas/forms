# Urban Area Dietary Habits Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the dietary habits of urban areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select all that apply for each question.
3. Enter numerical answers for questions that require numbers.
4. Answer all questions, but do not worry if you don't know the answer to a particular question.
5. If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Urban Area Dietary Habits Survey Form** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form, please enter your urban area name.
* **What is your daily food intake?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all the food categories that apply to your daily intake:
	+ Fruit
	+ Vegetables
	+ Meat
* **How much do you eat?** (`3`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of times you eat per day.
* **What is your preferred method of transportation?** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Please select the method of transportation you use most frequently:
	+ Bus
	+ Bike
	+ Walk
* **How do you spend most of your free time?** (`5`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of how you usually spend your free time.
* **Do you have access to clean drinking water?** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How do you usually get your food?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all that apply:
	+ Restaurant
	+ Market
	+ Home cooking
* **How much time do you spend cooking?** (`8`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of hours spent cooking per day.
* **Do you have access to a refrigerator?** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your income level?** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all that apply:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Do you have a car?** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How many hours do you have free time?** (`12`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of hours you have free time per day.
* **Do you exercise regularly?** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your age?** (`14`, `number`, required): Please enter your age.
* **Do you work at home?** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How many hours do you watch TV daily?** (`16`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of hours you watch TV per day.
* **How many people live with you?** (`17`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of people living with you.
* **Do you have a car?** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ True
	+ False

Please answer this form carefully and honestly, and let us know if you have any questions or need assistance.
