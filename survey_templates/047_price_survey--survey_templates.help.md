# Price Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect price information from customers or users for research or data collection purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering a title for your price survey in the `form_title` field. This will help us understand the context of your survey.
2.  Enter the name of the product you are surveying in the `product_name` field.
3.  Provide a brief description of your product in the `form_description` field. This will help us understand more about the product you are surveying.
4.  Enter the price of your product in the `product_price` field.
5.  Enter the price of another product or service you are surveying in the `price_0` field. If this is not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
6.  Enter the price of another product or service you are surveying in the `price_1` field. If this is not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
7.  Enter the name of the business or company associated with your product in the `business_name` field.
8.  If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the `form_notes` field.
9.  Enter your email address in the `form_email` field. This will help us contact you if we need further information.
10.  Enter your phone number in the `form_phone` field.
11.  Finally, click the "form_submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **form_title** (text, required): Enter a title for your survey.
*   **product_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the product you are surveying.
*   **form_description** (note, required): Enter a brief description of your product.
*   **product_price** (number, required): Enter the price of your product.
*   **price_0** (number, optional): Enter the price of another product or service (if applicable).
*   **price_1** (number, optional): Enter the price of another product or service (if applicable).
*   **business_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the business or company associated with your product.
*   **form_notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
*   **form_email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **form_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **form_submit** (text, required): Click to submit your survey.
