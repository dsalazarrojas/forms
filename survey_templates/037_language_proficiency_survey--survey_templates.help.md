# language_proficiency_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The language proficiency survey is a form designed to assess an individual's proficiency in languages spoken at home. This survey aims to gather information about an individual's native language, reading and writing skills, listening and speaking skills, language level, experience with other languages, and more. The data collected will help inform language learning programs and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the native language from the provided options.
2. Answer the "read_write_skills" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" for all languages.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "listen_speak_skills" for all languages.
4. Answer "language_id" by selecting "Yes" or "No" for all languages.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for "speaker_listeners" and "listeners_talkers" for all languages.
6. Choose a language level (High or Low) for all languages.
7. Answer "other_languages" by selecting "Yes" or "No" for all languages.
8. For "years_of_experience", type the number of years of experience with the language (if applicable).
9. Answer "last_language_class" by selecting "Yes" or "No" for all languages.
10. For "native_language_other" to "language_notes", provide any additional information about each section in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **native_language** (`native_language`, `text`, required): Enter your native language (the language you speak at home).
* **read_write_skills** (`read_write_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for your reading and writing skills.
* **listen_speak_skills** (`listen_speak_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for your listening and speaking skills.
* **language_id** (`language_id`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for your language ID.
* **speaker_listeners** (`speaker_listeners`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for your speaker listeners.
* **listeners_talkers** (`listeners_talkers`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for your listeners talkers.
* **language_level** (`language_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose a language level (High or Low) for all languages.
* **other_languages** (`other_languages`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for any other languages.
* **years_of_experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Type the number of years of experience with the language (if applicable).
* **last_language_class** (`last_language_class`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for all languages for your last language class.
* **native_language_other** (`native_language_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your native language.
* **read_write_skills_other** (`read_write_skills_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your reading and writing skills.
* **listen_speak_skills_other** (`listen_speak_skills_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your listening and speaking skills.
* **language_id_other** (`language_id_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your language ID.
* **speaker_listeners_other** (`speaker_listeners_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your speaker listeners.
* **listeners_talkers_other** (`listeners_talkers_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your listeners talkers.
* **language_level_other** (`language_level_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your language level.
* **other_languages_other** (`other_languages_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your other languages.
* **years_of_experience_other** (`years_of_experience_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your years of experience.
* **last_language_class_other** (`last_language_class_other`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about your last language class.
* **native_language_notes** (`native_language_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your native language.
* **read_write_notes** (`read_write_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your reading and writing skills.
* **listen_speak_notes** (`listen_speak_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your listening and speaking skills.
* **language_notes** (`language_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your language.
