# Community Development Initiative Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from community residents on their development priorities, issues, and satisfaction. This information will help us understand your needs and preferences, which will be taken into account when planning community development initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2. Please provide your honest and accurate feedback.
3. Take the required fields (indicated by *) as these are crucial for our development planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Resident** (`resident_full_name_pol`, text, required*): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`resident_email_pol`, email, required*): Enter your email address for follow-up and poll results delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`resident_phone_pol`, text, required*): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Residential Address** (`resident_address_pol`, text, required*): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip.
* **Which area in our community needs the most development attention** (`priority_development_area_pol`, select_one, required*): Select the area that needs the most attention.
	+ Options: Housing and Real Estate, Public Transportation, Parks and Recreation, Commercial/Business Zones, Education and Schools, Public Safety and Infrastructure
* **What are the biggest development issues you see** (`top_development_issues_pol`, select_multiple, required*): Select all the issues that apply.
	+ Options: Affordable Housing Lack, Poor Public Transit, Outdated Infrastructure, Lack of Green Spaces, Declining Business District, Unsafe Neighborhood Conditions
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with our community's development** (`overall_community_satisfaction_pol`, number, required*): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
* **Rate your satisfaction with recent development initiatives** (`development_initiative_satisfaction_pol`, number, required*): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
* **What are your ideas for future development initiatives** (`future_development_ideas_pol`, text, required*): Share your ideas for future development initiatives.
* **Would you be willing to support a development initiative** (`development_support_availability_pol`, select_one, required*): Select one of the three options: Yes, sign me up!, No, thank you, Maybe, more info please.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)** (`resident_signature_pol`, text, required*): Enter your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date of Poll Submission** (`submission_date_final_pol`, date, required*): Enter the date and time of this form submission.
* **Would you like to receive the community center's newsletter** (`newsletter_opt_in_pol`, select_one, required*): Select one of the two options: Yes, sign me up!, No, thank you.
* **How did you hear about this development poll** (`referral_source_pol`, select_one, required*): Select one of the five options: Email, Social Media, Website, Friend/Colleague, Poster/Flyer.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_full_name_pol`, text, required*): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_pol`, text, required*): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
