# Building Emergency Response Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the emergency response readiness of building occupants by gathering information on various aspects of their knowledge and behaviors related to emergency evacuation procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, department, and job title in the respective fields.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Report the date of the survey in the "Survey Date" field.
4. Answer the questions about your knowledge and experiences related to emergency evacuation procedures in the "Building Name" field.
5. For each question, select the answer that best describes your situation.
6. In the "Suggestions for Improvement" field, feel free to provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving the emergency response systems.
7. For the "Additional Training Needed" field, select the areas where you feel you need more training.
8. Finally, rate the building's overall preparedness in the "Overall Preparedness Rating" field and provide any additional comments if needed.

	+ Required: false

	+ Type: text
	+ Required: true

	+ Type: text

## Tips
- Answer honestly and carefully to provide accurate results.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your supervisor or emergency response team for clarification.
- Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Suggestions for Improvement" and "Additional Comments" fields.
- Rate the building's overall preparedness honestly, considering your experiences and observations.
