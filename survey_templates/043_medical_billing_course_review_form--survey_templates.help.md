# Medical Billing Course Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Billing Course Review Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from course attendees, allowing us to assess the course's quality and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information: First Name, Last Name, Email, Session Date, and Session Time.
2. If applicable, select your medical specialty and whether you attended a session or not (Training Location).
3. Provide your feedback and ratings for the course and instructor in the text fields (Course Feedback and Instructor Rating).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is a required field to contact you about your feedback.
* **Medical Specialty**: If you have a medical specialty, please select from the available options (General Practice, Surgery, Cardiology, etc.). If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Training Location**: Indicate whether you attended a session at a training location.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of your session in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-06-01).
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of your session in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
* **Course Feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments you have about the course. This helps us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the course.
* **Instructor Rating**: Rate your instructor's performance with a brief comment or description. This will help us improve future instructor training and course content.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the Course Feedback field.
- For the Instructor Rating field, be as brief and specific as possible to provide useful feedback.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
