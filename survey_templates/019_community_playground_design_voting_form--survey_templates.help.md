# Community Playground Design Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the community's preferences and opinions on the design and features of a playground in our community. The input will help inform the design process and ensure that the final product meets the community's needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and phone number to confirm your identity and contact information.
2. Select your preferred playground location from the dropdown menu.
3. Indicate your preferred theme, primary age group focus, and equipment preferences.
4. Choose your preferred surface material and accessibility features.
5. Select your preferred shade structures, seating, and additional features.
6. Prioritize the budget and maintenance aspects of the project.
7. Indicate your preferred completion timeline.
8. If you are interested, indicate your willingness to volunteer or help with fundraising.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to confirm your identity.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the city or town where you live.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the state or province where you live.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of your location, ensuring it is within the community.
* **Playground Name or Location** (`playground_name`, `select_one`, required): Select the specific playground location you are interested in, or suggest a new location.
* **Preferred Theme** (`preferred_theme`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred theme from the provided options.
* **Primary Age Group Focus** (`age_group_focus`, `select_one`, required): Select the age group you are most interested in.
* **Equipment Preferences** (`equipment_votes`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to 5 equipment types you prefer.
* **Preferred Surface Material** (`surface_material`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred surface material from the provided options.
* **Accessibility Features** (`accessibility`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all accessibility features you are interested in.
* **Shade Structures** (`shade_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred shade structure.
* **Seating Preferences** (`seating`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all seating types you prefer.
* **Additional Features** (`additional_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all additional features you are interested in.
* **Budget Priority** (`budget_priority`, `select_one`, required): Indicate what matters most to you in terms of budget.
* **Maintenance Priority** (`maintenance`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred maintenance level.
* **Completion Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred completion timeline.
* **Would You Like to Volunteer?** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): If you are interested, indicate your willingness to volunteer with the project.
* **Would You Like to Help with Fundraising?** (`fundraising_interest`, `select_one`, optional): If you are interested, indicate your willingness to help with fundraising.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
Please answer each question honestly and accurately. Your input will help shape the final playground design.
