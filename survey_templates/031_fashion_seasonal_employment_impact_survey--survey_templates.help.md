# fashion_seasonal_employment_impact_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on the impact of seasonal employment on your organization, including seasonal hiring needs, operational impact, and business impact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select or enter your answers for each page's fields.
2. Ensure that you have selected or entered information for all required fields.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Hiring Needs** (`seasonal_hiring_needs`, text, required): This field asks about your organization's seasonal hiring needs. Please provide a brief description of your hiring needs during peak and non-peak seasons.
* **Operational Impact** (`operational_impact`, text, required): This field asks about the operational impact of seasonal employment on your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description of how seasonal employment affects your operations.
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, text, required): This field asks about the business impact of seasonal employment on your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description of how seasonal employment affects your business.
* **Peak Season** (`peak_season`, select_one, required): This field asks about the peak season for your organization. Please select one of the provided options: Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter.
* **Employment Needs** (`employment_needs`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the type of employment needed during the peak season. Please select all applicable options from: Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal.
* **Hiring Start Date** (`hiring_start_date`, date, optional): This field asks about the start date of hiring for the peak season. If you don't have a specific start date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Hiring End Date** (`hiring_end_date`, date, optional): This field asks about the end date of hiring for the peak season. If you don't have a specific end date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Operational Start Date** (`operational_start_date`, date, optional): This field asks about the start date of operational activities for the peak season. If you don't have a specific start date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Operational End Date** (`operational_end_date`, date, optional): This field asks about the end date of operational activities for the peak season. If you don't have a specific end date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Seasonality Factor** (`seasonality_factor`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the seasonality factor for your organization. Please select one of the provided options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Seasonality Factor Description** (`seasonality_factor_description`, text, optional): This field asks for a description of the seasonality factor you selected in the previous field.
* **Peak Seasonal Impact** (`peak_seasonal_impact`, text, optional): This field asks about the peak seasonal impact on your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **Non-Peak Seasonal Impact** (`non_peak_seasonal_impact`, text, optional): This field asks about the non-peak seasonal impact on your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **Operational Impact Factor** (`operational_impact_factor`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the operational impact factor for your organization. Please select one of the provided options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Operational Impact Factor Description** (`operational_impact_factor_description`, text, optional): This field asks for a description of the operational impact factor you selected in the previous field.
* **Seasonal Impact Factor** (`seasonal_impact_factor`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the seasonal impact factor for your organization. Please select one of the provided options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Seasonal Impact Factor Description** (`seasonal_impact_factor_description`, text, optional): This field asks for a description of the seasonal impact factor you selected in the previous field.
* **Business Impact Factor** (`business_impact_factor`, select_one, optional): This field asks about the business impact factor for your organization. Please select one of the provided options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Business Impact Factor Description** (`business_impact_factor_description`, text, optional): This field asks for a description of the business impact factor you selected in the previous field.
* **Operational Strategy** (`operational_strategy`, text, optional): This field asks about the operational strategy for your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **Operational Risks** (`operational_risks`, text, optional): This field asks about the operational risks for your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **Operational Challenges** (`operational_challenges`, text, optional): This field asks about the operational challenges for your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description.
* **Seasonal Hiring Needs Rationale** (`seasonal_hiring_needs_rationale`, text, optional): This field asks for a rationale for your seasonal hiring needs.
* **Operational Hiring Needs Rationale** (`operational_hiring_needs_rationale`, text, optional): This field asks for a rationale for your operational hiring needs.
* **Business Risks** (`business_risks`, text, optional): This field asks about the business risks for your organization. Please provide a clear and concise description.
