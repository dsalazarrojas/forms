# Chronic Pain Management Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Pain Management Survey Form is designed to collect information about a patient's chronic pain, its management, and overall well-being. Its purpose is to help healthcare providers assess a patient's current pain status, identify potential challenges in pain management, and determine the effectiveness of current treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, carefully follow the instructions for each section. Ensure you answer all required fields, and when in doubt, consult your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient ID (ID 2)**: Enter your unique patient ID number.
*   **Survey Date (ID 3)**: Enter the date you're completing this form.
*   **Primary Type of Pain (ID 5)**: Choose the primary type of pain you experience: Musculoskeletal, Neuropathic, Inflammatory, Visceral, Mixed, or Other (if not listed, specify the type in the text field).
*   **Pain Location (ID 6)**: Describe the location of your pain.
*   **Pain Level Today (ID 8)**: Rate your current pain level from 0 to 10.
*   **Average Pain This Week (ID 9)**: Rate the average pain level you've experienced this week from 0 to 10.
*   **Worst Pain This Week (ID 10)**: Rate the worst pain level you experienced this week from 0 to 10.
*   **Best Pain This Week (ID 11)**: Rate the best pain level you experienced this week from 0 to 10.
*   **Ability to Engage in Physical Activity (ID 13)**: Choose how your pain affects your ability to engage in physical activity: No limitation, Mild limitation, Moderate limitation, Severe limitation, Unable to participate.
*   **Current Work Capacity (ID 14)**: Choose how your current work capacity is affected by your pain: Full-time work, Part-time work, Limited light work, Unable to work, On disability.
*   **Sleep Quality (ID 15)**: Choose how your sleep quality is affected by your pain: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very poor.
*   **Participation in Social Activities (ID 16)**: Choose how your pain affects your participation in social activities: Very active, Moderately active, Somewhat limited, Very limited, Unable to participate.
*   **Current Treatments (ID 18)**: Select all treatments you're currently undergoing (Medication, Physical therapy, Psychological therapy, Injections, Surgery, Acupuncture, Massage, Exercise program, Mind-body techniques).
*   **How Well Are You Following Prescribed Medications (ID 19)**: Choose how well you're following prescribed medications: Excellent compliance, Good compliance, Fair compliance, Poor compliance, Not taking medications.
*   **How Effective is Your Current Treatment Plan (ID 21)**: Choose how effective your current treatment plan is: Very effective, Moderately effective, Slightly effective, Not effective, Worsening.
*   **Overall Satisfaction with Pain Management (ID 22)**: Choose how satisfied you are with your pain management: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied, Very unsatisfied.
*   **Biggest Challenge with Pain Management (ID 24)**: Describe the biggest challenge you face with pain management.
*   **Improvements Desired for Pain Management (ID 25)**: Describe what improvements would help your pain management.

**Tips**: When completing this form, refer to your healthcare provider if you're unsure about any questions. Answer all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
