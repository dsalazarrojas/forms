# Contact Lens Comfort Study Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register interest in a contact lens comfort study and collect contact information for potential participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Ensure you have provided all required information (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name \***: Enter your full name as you would like it to be used for study contact. This will help us get in touch with you regarding the study.
* **Email address \***: Provide an active email address where you can be reached for study communications. This will be used for scheduling and study updates.
* **Phone number**: If you have an optional phone number, please enter it so we can contact you more easily for scheduling purposes.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years. This will help us assess your eligibility for the study.
* **Currently using contact lenses**: Check 'True' if you regularly wear contact lenses.
* **Current lens type**: Select your current type of contact lens (Soft, RGP, or Hybrid) if you regularly wear contact lenses.
* **Typical daily wear hours**: Enter the average hours per day you wear contact lenses if you wear them daily.
* **Experience of comfort issues**: Describe any discomfort or symptoms you have experienced while wearing contact lenses.
* **Availability for study sessions**: Enter the days or times you are typically available for study sessions.
* **Consent to be contacted about the study**: Check 'I consent' if you are willing to be contacted about the study. This will help us reach out to you with study updates and schedule sessions.
* **Additional notes**: Enter any other information that might be relevant to your participation in the study.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Ensure you have provided all required information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
