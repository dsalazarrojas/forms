# Employee Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather honest feedback from employees about their experience and satisfaction with their workplace. It is a tool for managers and HR teams to understand employee needs and improve work environments and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select all options that apply from the drop-down menus.
3. Provide clear and concise comments in the text fields.
4. Make sure to review and submit your survey responses before moving on to the next page.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields at the end of the survey.
