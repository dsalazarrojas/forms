# Human Rights Literacy Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the level of literacy in human rights among end users. It is a self-assessment tool that evaluates the level of understanding of human rights concepts, principles, and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and take a moment to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. For each question, select the response that best represents your understanding or experience.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this form is designed to help you evaluate your level of understanding.
4. Use the comments section to provide any additional information or context that may be relevant to your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This field provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Read it carefully to understand the context and scope of the survey.
* **Questions** (`questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Answer the following questions with the level of understanding or experience you have. You can select multiple answers if they apply to you.
	+ **Yes**: You have a good understanding of this concept.
	+ **No**: You do not have a good understanding of this concept.
* **Awareness** (`awareness`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the level of awareness you have regarding human rights.
	+ **High**: You have a good understanding of human rights concepts, principles, and practices.
	+ **Medium**: You have a general understanding of human rights concepts, but may need more information or training.
	+ **Low**: You have a limited understanding of human rights concepts, and may need further education or training.
* **Attitudes** (`attitudes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the attitudes you hold towards human rights.
	+ **Yes**: You have positive attitudes towards human rights.
	+ **No**: You do not have positive attitudes towards human rights.
* **Engagement** (`engagement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the level of engagement you have with human rights.
	+ **Yes**: You are actively engaged with human rights initiatives or activities.
	+ **No**: You are not actively engaged with human rights initiatives or activities.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `note`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any final thoughts or comments on your understanding of human rights.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Use this section to provide any additional information or context that may be relevant to your responses.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide your demographic information, such as name, job title, or department.
