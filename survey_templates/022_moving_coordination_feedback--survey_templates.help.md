# Moving Coordination Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a moving company on the coordination aspect of the move.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose one of the options for "Moving Company" to rate your experience with the moving company.
2. Enter a numerical value in the "Moving Coordination" field to rate your experience on this aspect of the move.
3. In the "Communication" field, enter any comments or feedback about how the moving company communicated with you throughout the process.
4. Select one of the options for "Overall Satisfaction" to rate your overall experience with the moving company.
5. If you have any additional comments about your move, please enter them in the "Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Moving Company** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate the overall quality of the moving company's service.
* **Moving Coordination** (`number`): Enter a numerical value to rate how well the moving company coordinated the move.
* **Communication** (`text`): Enter any comments about how the moving company communicated with you throughout the process.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Very Satisfied`, `Somewhat Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat Dissatisfied`, `Very Dissatisfied`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the moving company's service.
* **Comment** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your move.
