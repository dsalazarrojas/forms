> # Eyelash Extension Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyelash Extension Knowledge Quiz is a series of questions designed to evaluate your knowledge of eyelash extension practices. It is intended to help you identify areas for improvement and ensure you are up-to-date with industry standards.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, simply answer each question in the space provided.
- Each question is a separate page, so please click "Next" or "Submit" to move on to the next one.
- Fill out as much of the form as you can, but it is not necessary to answer every question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What is Eyelash Extension?** (`what-is-eyelash-extension`, `text`, required)
This question asks for a brief description of what eyelash extension is.

### 2. **Lash Artist Background** (`lash_artist_background`, `text`, required)
This question is asking about your background or experience in the eyelash industry.

### 3. **Lash Extension Methods** (`lash_extension_methods`, `text`, required)
This question inquires about different methods or techniques used in eyelash extensions.

### 4. **Common Complications** (`common_complications`, `text`, required)
This question is about potential complications that may occur during or after eyelash extensions.

### 5. **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, `text`, required)
This question is asking about what steps you would take in case of an emergency related to eyelash extensions.

### 6. **Lash Safety and Hygiene** (`lash_saftey_and_hygiene`, `text`, required)
This question is about the safety and hygiene protocols you follow during eyelash extension procedures.

### 7. **Common Materials and Supplies** (`common_materials_and_supplies`, `text`, optional)
This question is about the materials and supplies you use during eyelash extension procedures.

### 8. **Sanitation and Sterilization** (`sanitation_and_sterilization`, `text`, optional)
This question is about your procedures for maintaining a clean and sterile environment.

### 9. **Lash Application and Adhering** (`lash_application_and_adhering`, `text`, required)
This question is about your methods for applying and adhering eyelash extensions.

### 10. **Final Checklist** (`final_checklist`, `text`, required)
This question is asking you to review and confirm your understanding of the previous questions.

### 11. **Common Materials and Supplies** (hidden field, `text`, required)
This question is about materials and supplies that can be used during eyelash extensions. 

Note: This field can be ignored as it is not necessary for the proper completion of the form.
