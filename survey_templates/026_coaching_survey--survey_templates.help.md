# Coaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Survey is designed to gather feedback from respondents about their coaching experience, focusing on the coach's performance, areas for improvement, and overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information as a respondent:
	* Enter your name in the "Your Name" field (Field 2).
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (Field 3).
	* Select the name of the organization or club you're affiliated with in the "Club or Organization" field (Field 4).
	* Choose your role in the "Your Role" field (Field 5).
2. Select the coach you're evaluating:
	* Enter the coach's name in the "Coach Name" field (Field 7).
	* Specify the coach's position in the "Coach Position" field (Field 8).
	* Indicate how long you've worked with the coach in the "How long have you worked with this coach?" field (Field 9).
3. Evaluate the coach's performance:
	* Rate the overall coaching effectiveness in the "Overall Coaching Effectiveness" field (Field 11).
	* Rate the coach's performance in various aspects, such as:
		+ Athlete development and improvement (Field 12).
		+ Team performance and results (Field 13).
		+ Technical knowledge and expertise (Field 14).
		+ Communication with athletes (Field 15).
		+ Ability to motivate (Field 16).
4. Identify the coach's strengths and areas for improvement:
	* In the "Key Strengths of This Coach" field (Field 18), describe what the coach does well.
	* In the "Areas for Improvement" field (Field 20), suggest areas the coach can improve.
5. Provide recommendations and additional comments:
	* In the "Recommendations" field (Field 21), provide suggestions for improvement.
	* In the "Additional Comments" field (Field 24), add any other feedback or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (Field 2): Enter your name as the respondent.
* **Email Address** (Field 3): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Club or Organization** (Field 4): Select the name of the organization or club you're affiliated with.
* **Your Role** (Field 5): Choose your role, e.g., athlete, coach, or staff member.
* **Coach Name** (Field 7): Enter the coach's name you're evaluating.
* **Coach Position** (Field 8): Specify the coach's position or title.
* **How long have you worked with this coach?** (Field 9): Select the duration of your collaboration with the coach.
* **Overall Coaching Effectiveness** (Field 11): Rate the coach's overall effectiveness.
* **Overall Coaching Effectiveness Options:**
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Athlete Development and Improvement** (Field 12): Rate the coach's ability to develop and improve athletes.
* **Team Performance and Results** (Field 13): Rate the coach's team performance and results.
* **Technical Knowledge and Expertise** (Field 14): Rate the coach's technical knowledge and expertise.
* **Communication with Athletes** (Field 15): Rate the coach's communication with athletes.
* **Ability to Motivate** (Field 16): Rate the coach's ability to motivate athletes.
* **Key Strengths of This Coach** (Field 18): Describe the coach's key strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Field 20): Suggest areas the coach can improve.
* **Recommendations** (Field 21): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (Field 24): Add any other feedback or thoughts.
* **Retain Coach** (Field 23): Rate whether the coach should be retained.
* **Retain Coach Options:**
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Neutral
	+ Probably no
	+ Definitely no
