# Long Term Medication Safety Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Long Term Medication Safety Evaluation Form is used to assess the safety of long-term medication use. This form helps healthcare providers collect information on various aspects of medication usage and identify potential issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and understanding of your medication use.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Be thorough in your responses, as this information is crucial for your healthcare provider to assess your medication safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (required):
	+ This is an introductory page that asks you to briefly describe your current medication use.
* **Medication Side Effects** (text, required):
	+ Describe any side effects you experience while taking your medication.
* **Adherence Patterns** (select_one, not required):
	+ Select one option that best describes your adherence pattern:
		+ Option 1: Always take medication as prescribed
		+ Option 2: Missed one or more doses
		+ Option 3: Stopped taking medication due to side effects
* **Overall Experience** (select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all options that describe your overall experience with the medication:
		+ Option 1: Improved symptoms
		+ Option 2: No change
		+ Option 3: Worse symptoms
* **Side Effects Frequency** (date, required):
	+ Enter the date when you first started experiencing side effects.
* **Side Effects Severity** (select_one, not required):
	+ Select one option that describes the severity of side effects:
		+ Option 1: Mild
		+ Option 2: Moderate
		+ Option 3: Severe
* **Medication Safety Concerns** (select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all options that describe your medication safety concerns:
		+ Option 1: Drowsy or sleepy
		+ Option 2: Dizziness or lightheadedness
		+ Option 3: Difficulty breathing
* **Adherence Adverse Events** (text, required):
	+ Describe any adverse events you have experienced while adhering to your medication regimen.
* **Adherence Adherence** (number, required):
	+ Enter the number of times you have taken your medication as prescribed.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (select_one, not required):
	+ Select one option that describes your overall satisfaction with the medication:
		+ Option 1: Very satisfied
		+ Option 2: Slightly satisfied
		+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Follow-up** (select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all options that describe your follow-up needs:
		+ Option 1: Regular check-ups
		+ Option 2: Contact me if concerns arise
* **Medication Dosage** (text, required):
	+ Enter your medication dosage.
* **Medication Frequency** (text, required):
	+ Enter your medication frequency.
* **Medication Duration** (select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all options that describe your medication duration:
		+ Option 1: Short-term (less than 1 month)
		+ Option 2: Medium-term (1-3 months)
		+ Option 3: Long-term (more than 3 months)
* **Medication Name** (text, required):
	+ Enter the name of your medication.
* **Dosage Form** (select_one, not required):
	+ Select one option that describes your medication dosage form:
		+ Option 1: Tablets or capsules
		+ Option 2: Liquid or injection
* **Medication Route** (select_one, not required):
	+ Select one option that describes your medication route:
		+ Option 1: Oral
		+ Option 2: Injection or IV
* **Additional Comments** (text, not required):
	+ Enter any additional comments or concerns you have about your medication.
* **Contact Info** (text, required):
	+ Enter your contact information so that your healthcare provider can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (text, required):
	+ Enter any follow-up comments or concerns you have.
* **Contact** (text, required):
	+ Enter your contact information so that your healthcare provider can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **Email** (email, required):
	+ Enter your email address so that your healthcare provider can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **Fax** (text, required):
	+ Enter your fax number so that your healthcare provider can reach out to you for follow-up.
* **Address** (text, required):
	+ Enter your address so that your healthcare provider can reach out to you for follow-up.
