# Pedestrian Safety Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pedestrian Safety Survey is designed to collect information on pedestrian safety incidents to identify potential risks and improve overall safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field carefully and accurately.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant option that best describes the situation.
3. If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or select "Other" and provide a detailed description in the corresponding text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`1`, text, required): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Street Name** (`2`, text, required): Enter the name of the street where the incident occurred.
* **Description of incident** (`3`, text, required): Briefly describe the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Location** (`4`, text, required): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Age of Pedestrian** (`6`, number, required): Enter the age of the pedestrian involved in the incident.
* **Gender of Pedestrian** (`7`, select_one, required): Select the gender of the pedestrian involved in the incident.
* **Injured** (`8`, select_one, required): Indicate if the pedestrian was injured or not.
* **Injured severity level** (`9`, select_multiple, required): Select the severity of the injury.
* **Police response time** (`10`, time, required): Enter the time it took for the police to respond.
* **Vehicle involved** (`11`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Cause of incident** (`12`, select_multiple, required): Select the cause of the incident.
* **Cause of incident - Other** (`13`, text, required): If the cause of the incident is not listed, enter a detailed description.
* **Comment** (`14`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Safety Risk** (`15`, number, required): Enter a numerical value for the safety risk assessment.
* **Vehicle speed** (`16`, number, required): Enter the speed of the vehicle involved.
* **Vehicle distance** (`17`, number, required): Enter the distance the vehicle was traveling.
* **Vehicle type** (`18`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle involved.
* **Pedestrian crossing** (`19`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the pedestrian was crossing the street.
* **Pedestrian crossing location** (`20`, text, required): Enter the location of the pedestrian crossing.
* **Pedestrian crossing type** (`21`, select_one, required): Select the type of pedestrian crossing.
* **Other pedestrian crossing** (`22`, text, required): If the pedestrian crossing type is not listed, enter a detailed description.
* **Pedestrian speed** (`23`, number, required): Enter the speed of the pedestrian.
* **Pedestrian visibility** (`24`, number, required): Enter the pedestrian's visibility assessment.
* **Pedestrian visibility time** (`25`, time, required): Enter the time the pedestrian had to see the vehicle.
