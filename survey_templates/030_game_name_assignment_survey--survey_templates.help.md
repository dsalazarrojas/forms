# Game Name Assignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Name Assignment Survey is a form used to assign a name to a game. It is designed to help game designers provide accurate and relevant information about the game's title, preferred genre, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in the required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the preferred genre from the dropdown list.
4. Enter any additional ideas or details about the game title in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Title (game_title)**: This field is used to enter the proposed game title. Please enter a concise and descriptive title that accurately reflects the game's theme and content.
* **Preferred Genre (preferred_genre)**: This field is used to select the game's genre. Choose from the available options: Action, Adventure, Puzzle, or Role Playing.
* **Additional Ideas or Details (name_ideas)**: This text field is available for you to enter any additional ideas or details that may be relevant to the game title. Feel free to provide any relevant context or information that will help the game designer make an informed decision.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the game title accurately reflects the game's theme and content.
* Be concise and descriptive in your title entry.
* Select a genre that best represents the game's style and gameplay.
* Use this opportunity to provide any additional context or information that may be helpful to the game designer.
