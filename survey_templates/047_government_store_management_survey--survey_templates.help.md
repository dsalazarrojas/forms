# Government Store Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about the performance, operations, and challenges of government stores. It will help store managers provide feedback on their store's management, including store location, store manager, store hours, store operations, challenges, improvement areas, manager responses, and performance indicators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question accurately and truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. If a question does not apply to your store, you may leave it blank.
3. Please provide clear and concise answers in the required format for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your store location.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, text, optional): Please enter your name and title (e.g., Store Manager, Assistant Manager).
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, time, optional): Please enter your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-18:00).
* **Store Operations** (`store_operations`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Store Operations
	+ Store Hours
	+ Store Location
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Please describe any challenges your store is facing.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Please identify areas where you would like to see improvement.
* **Manager Responses** (`manager_responses`, text, optional): Please provide any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Performance Indicators** (`performance_indicators`, number, optional): Please enter a numerical value to indicate your store's performance (e.g., sales, customer satisfaction).
