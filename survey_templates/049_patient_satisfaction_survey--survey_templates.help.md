# Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Satisfaction Survey form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from patients about their experiences with a healthcare provider or clinic. This form helps healthcare providers understand patient satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.
2. Fill out the form sections, including Name, Phone, Email, Patient Satisfaction, Rating, Feedback, and any other required/optional fields.
3. Select the correct options for Patient ID, Survey ID, and other select_multiple fields.
4. Choose the correct rating for Rating and select options for Category, Subcategory, and other select_multiple fields.
5. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (id: 2): Enter your name as it appears on your identification. This is not required for form submission.
* **Phone** (id: 3): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This is not required for form submission.
* **E mail** (id: 4): Enter your email address for communication purposes. This is not required for form submission.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (id: 5): Provide your level of satisfaction on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Rating** (id: 6): Select one of the following options: Excellent, Very Good, Average, Poor. This helps us understand your experience.
* **Feedback** (id: 7): Share any comments or suggestions about your experience. This is not required for form submission.
* **Submit** (id: 8): Choose to submit or cancel your form submission.
* **Date** (id: 9): Select the date you submitted this form.
* **Time** (id: 10): Select the time you submitted this form.
* **Note** (id: 11): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Category** (id: 12): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is not clearly defined in the form structure, but it may be for categorizing or tracking purposes.
* **Subcategory** (id: 13): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is similar to Category and may be for further categorization.
* **Patient ID** (id: 14): Enter your unique patient ID number. This is not required for form submission.
* **Survey ID** (id: 15): Enter a unique survey ID number. This is not required for form submission.
* **Created At** (id: 16): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is for tracking when the form was created.
* **Updated At** (id: 17): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is for tracking when the form was updated.
* **Deleted At** (id: 18): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is for tracking when the form was deleted.
* **Patient Rating** (id: 19): Enter a rating for your experience, similar to the Rating field.
* **Survey Rating** (id: 20): Enter a rating for the survey itself, similar to the Rating field.
* **Patient Feedback** (id: 21): Share any feedback about your experience. This is not required for form submission.
* **Submitted** (id: 22): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is for tracking whether the form was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (id: 23): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is for tracking when the form was submitted.
* **Survey Status** (id: 24): Select one or more of the following options: Active, Inactive. This field is for tracking the survey status.
* **Created By** (id: 25): Select one or more of the following options: Yes, No. This field is for tracking who created the form.
