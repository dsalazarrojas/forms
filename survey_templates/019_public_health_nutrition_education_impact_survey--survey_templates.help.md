# Public Health Nutrition Education Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the impact of public health nutrition education sessions. It gathers information on the outcomes and effectiveness of these sessions to help improve the quality and effectiveness of future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information for each question. If you need help or clarification, please do not hesitate to reach out to the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education Session Details** (`education_session_details`): This is the main section where you can provide a brief description of the education session.
* **Title** (`survey_title`): Enter a brief title for the session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Outcomes** (`education_outcomes`): Select one or more outcomes of the education session that you experienced (e.g., Better Nutrition Knowledge, Improved Food Choices, Increased Food Security, Better Health Outcomes, or Other).
* **Other Outcomes** (`education_outcomes_others`): If you selected "Other" as an outcome, please provide a brief explanation of what you achieved.
* **Pre-Session Knowledge** (`pre_session_knowledge`): Estimate the level of knowledge you had before the session (e.g., 0-1, 2-3, 4-5).
* **Post-Session Knowledge** (`post_session_knowledge`): Estimate the level of knowledge you gained after the session (e.g., 0-1, 2-3, 4-5).
* **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`): Estimate the number of people who attended the session (e.g., 1-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21-50, More than 50).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`): Select the level of education you have (e.g., High School, Bachelor, Master, Doctoral).
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the program you are associated with.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`): Enter the date of the education session.
* **Time Spent Learning** (`time_spent_learning`): Estimate the time spent learning from the session (e.g., 30-60 minutes, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours).
* **Education Facilitator** (`education_facilitator`): Select the facilitator of the education session (e.g., Jimmy, John, Jane, Jane Doe).
* **Other Education Facilitator** (`education_facilitator_others`): If you selected "Other" as an education facilitator, please provide the name.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`): Select the length of the session (e.g., 30-60, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours).
* **Education Location** (`education_location`): Select the location of the education session (e.g., Urban, Rural, Community Center).
* **Education Notes** (`education_notes`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`): Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the survey.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`): Enter any comments or suggestions for future sessions.
* **Follow-up E-mail** (`follow_up_email`): Enter the email address of the follow-up contact person.
* **Follow-up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`): Enter the phone number of the follow-up contact person.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for the follow-up.
