# Corporate Social Responsibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Corporate Social Responsibility Survey is designed to measure the awareness, perception, and attitudes of employees towards corporate social responsibility (CSR) practices within the organization. The survey aims to gather information on various CSR initiatives, their effectiveness, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**:
	* This page provides a brief overview of the survey's purpose and instructions on how to complete it.
2. **Demographic Information**:
	* Select your demographic information (Option 1 or Option 2) from the dropdown menu. This information will help us understand the diversity of our respondents.
3. **Questions**:
	* Select all that apply from the available options for each question. You can choose one option for each question.
4. **Subsequent questions**:
	* Follow the same selection process as in step 3 for the remaining questions.
