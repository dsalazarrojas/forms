# fire_safety_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by authorized personnel to provide feedback on fire safety concerns, drills, and training within the company's internal fire safety procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name and last name to identify yourself.
* Enter your email address to contact you, if needed.
* Provide the department responsible for this issue.
* Offer your feedback and comments about the fire safety procedures and equipment.
* Indicate if you have received fire safety training.
* Indicate if you attended a fire safety drill.
* Enter the date and time of the incident or concern.
* Specify the name of the person assigned to investigate or address the issue.
* Specify the name of the person or team responsible for submitting this feedback.
* Enter the date when you submitted this feedback.
* Optionally, add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Department** (4): Identify the department responsible for this issue (e.g., Operations, HR, etc.).
* **Feedback** (5): Provide specific and detailed feedback on fire safety concerns, procedures, or equipment.
* **Safety Concerns** (6): If you have additional safety concerns, list them here.
* **Fire Safety Training** (7): Check 'Yes' if you have received fire safety training.
* **Fire Safety Drill** (8): Check 'Yes' for each fire safety drill you attended.
* **Date** (9): Enter the date of the incident or concern.
* **Time** (10): Enter the time of the incident or concern.
* **Assigned To** (11): Specify the name of the person assigned to investigate or address this issue.
* **Assigned By** (12): Specify the name of the person or team that assigned you this issue.
* **Submitted By** (13): Specify your name for submission purposes.
* **Submitted Date** (14): Enter the date when you submitted this feedback.
* **Note** (15): Add any additional comments or notes about this issue.
