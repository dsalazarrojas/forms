# Car Of The Year Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and input from users on their favorite car of the year. This information will help us understand what users value most in a vehicle and identify trends in the automotive industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name, email address, and select your top choice for the car of the year category.
2. Rate the design and aesthetics, performance, value for money, safety features, innovation, and your reason for choosing the vehicle.
3. Briefly describe why you chose your top pick.
4. If you're an enthusiast, select your level of expertise.
5. Indicate whether you'd like to participate in future automotive surveys.
6. Agree to the terms of the voting.
7. Enter a name for confirmation and any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Full Name** (Required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address to confirm your vote.
* **Voting Category** (Required): Select the category for your top choice (e.g., Best Compact Car).
* **Your Nominee for Car of the Year** (Required): Enter the make and model of the vehicle you've chosen.
* **Nominee Vehicle Year** (Required): Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Rate the Design and Aesthetics** (Required): Scale of 1 to 10, with 10 being Exceptional.
* **Rate the Performance and Driving Experience** (Required): Scale of 1 to 10, with 10 being Exceptional.
* **Rate the Value for Money** (Required): Scale of 1 to 10, with 10 being Exceptional.
* **Rate the Safety Features** (Required): Scale of 1 to 10, with 10 being Exceptional.
* **Rate the Innovation and Technology** (Required): Scale of 1 to 10, with 10 being Exceptional.
* **Why Did You Choose This Vehicle?** (Required): Briefly describe your decision-making process.
* **Your Automotive Enthusiast Level** (Required): Select how you'd describe yourself (e.g., Casual Driver).
* **Would You Like to Participate in Future Automotive Surveys?** (Required): Indicate whether you'd like to participate.
* **I Acknowledge the Voting Terms** (Required): Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your vote.
* **Type Your Name for Voting Confirmation** (Required): Enter your name for confirmation.
* **Any Additional Comments or Requests** (Optional): Provide more details for your vote.
