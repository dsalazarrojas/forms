# Video Reflection Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Reflection Feedback Form is designed to gather information about the effectiveness of a video reflection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Video Title**: Enter the title of the video you are providing feedback on.
2. **Name**: Enter your name, which will be used to associate your feedback with your identity.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if necessary.
4. **Video Rating**: Rate the video using a scale from 1 to 5.
5. **Reflection**: Provide a brief reflection on the video, including your thoughts and impressions.
6. **Rating Comment**: Provide any additional comments related to your rating of the video.
7. **Watched Video**: If you have watched the video, select the duration you watched it.
8. **Watched Video Comment**: Provide any comments or feedback on the video itself.
9. **Video Reflection**:
	* **Satisfied**: Choose how satisfied you are with the video, using the options Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
	* **Improved**: Choose how much the video improved your understanding or skills, using the options Much Improved, A Little Improved, or Not Improved.
	* **Likely to Improve**: Choose how likely you are to improve the video, using the options Content, Engagement, or Both.
10. **Video Reflection Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the video.
