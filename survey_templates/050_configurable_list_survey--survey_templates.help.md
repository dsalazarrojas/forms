# Configurable List Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from users on various topics, allowing for a more detailed and comprehensive understanding of their opinions, experiences, and needs. It's a flexible tool for gathering information on different categories, making it suitable for various use cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to the questions based on your experiences and opinions.
3. Make sure to fill out all the fields before submitting the form.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting, ensuring accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the survey, which should be a brief and descriptive name that reflects the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, not required): This field is for your name, which will be used to identify you as the respondent.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, not required): This is your contact email address, which may be used for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, not required): This is your contact phone number, which may be used for follow-up communication.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, not required): This field is for your organization or institution, which will be used to identify your affiliation.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, not required): This field is for your department within the organization or institution.
* **Survey Type** (`survey_type`, `select_one`, required): This field asks about the type of survey you're participating in, which helps to categorize your responses.
* **Survey Purpose** (`survey_purpose`, `text`, required): This field is for providing a brief description of why you're conducting the survey.
* **Category 1 Name** (`category_1_name`, `text`, required): This field is for listing the first category you're evaluating.
* **Category 1 Items** (`category_1_items`, `text`, not required): This field is for listing items related to the first category.
* **Category 1 Rating** (`category_1_rating`, `select_one`, not required): This field asks for your rating of the importance of the first category.
* **Category 1 Comments** (`category_1_comments`, `text`, not required): This field is for providing additional notes or comments about the first category.
* **Category 2 Name** (`category_2_name`, `text`, not required): This field is for listing the second category you're evaluating.
