# Gaming Income Opportunities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about gaming income opportunities. It's intended for gamers to provide details about their income streams, such as gaming channels, sponsorships, and other sources of income.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the `Gamer Info` section.
2. Select all relevant income streams from the `Income Streams` section.
3. If you have specific income amounts, enter them in the `Income Level` section. Note that this field is optional and not required.
4. Provide a brief description of your motivation for creating this survey in the `Motivation` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Gamer Info (Text, required: false)
Giver your name and any other identifying information as the primary gamer.

* **Name**: Enter your name as the primary gamer.
* **Other Info**: Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your gamer tag or handle.

### Income Streams (Select Multiple, required: false)
Select all income streams that apply to you. You can choose from:
* **Gaming**: Income from gaming-related activities, such as game sales, esports, or streaming.
* **YouTube**: Income from YouTube videos or live streams.
* **Twitch**: Income from Twitch streaming.
* **Patreon**: Income from Patreon supporters or patrons.
* **Sponsorships**: Income from sponsorships or brand deals.
* **Affiliate**: Income from affiliate marketing or promotion.
* **Other**: Select this option if none of the above apply.

### Income Level (Number, required: false)
If you want to provide specific income amounts, enter them here. This is optional and only applicable if you have a clear, numerical income level to report.

### Motivation (Text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of why you're participating in this survey. For example, you might write "I'm interested in learning more about gaming income opportunities" or "I'd like to see how I compare to other gamers."

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
- Select only income streams that apply to you.
- Keep your answers brief and clear.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
