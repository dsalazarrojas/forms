# Beer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your beer consumption habits, preferences, and opinions. Your responses will help us better understand the market and improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, select the most accurate option that reflects your opinion or behavior.
3. Use the available options for select fields (e.g. "Select one" or "Select multiple").
4. Enter dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy for the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Your Beer Habits --**
  * **How often do you enjoy a beer?**: This question asks about your frequency of beer consumption. Choose the option that best represents your habits.
  * **What are your favorite beer styles?**: Select up to 3 beer styles that you enjoy the most.
* **-- Brewery Experience --**
  * **What is the most important factor when choosing a beer?**: Choose the factor that influences your decision the most.
* **How important is brewery atmosphere to your experience?**: Rate the importance of brewery atmosphere to you, using the 1 to 5 scale.
* **Rate the average service quality in taprooms you visit**: Rate the service quality in taprooms you have visited, using the 1 to 5 scale.
* **-- Pricing and Value --**
  * **What is an acceptable price for a premium craft 6-pack?**: Choose the price range that you consider acceptable.
  * **Do you feel craft beer provides good value compared to mass-market beer?**: Choose your opinion on the value of craft beer.
* **-- Future Interests --**
  * **How interested are you in Non-Alcoholic (NA) craft beer?**: Choose your level of interest in NA craft beer.
  * **Which seasonal flavors do you look forward to most?**: Select up to 3 seasonal flavors you enjoy the most.
* **Any advice for brewers to improve their products?**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have for brewers.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which you completed this survey.

# Tips
* Please note that some fields are required to complete the survey.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
