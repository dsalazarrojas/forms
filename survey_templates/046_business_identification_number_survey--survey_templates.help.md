## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Legal Name** (`business_legal_name`, text, required): Enter the registered business name.
* **DBA Name (if different)** (`dba_name`, text, optional): Enter the Doing Business As name if different from the registered business name.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the business structure from the options provided (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).
* **Principal Place of Business** (`principal_address`, text, required): Enter the main business address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province where the business is located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code for the business location.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where the business is located.
* **EIN (Employer Identification Number)** (`ein_number`, text, optional): Enter the Federal tax ID number.
* **EIN Issued Date** (`ein_issued_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the EIN was issued.
* **State Tax ID** (`state_tax_id`, text, optional): Enter the state-specific tax ID number.
* **DUNs Number** (`duns_number`, text, optional): Enter the DUNs number.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license`, text, optional): Enter the local or state license number.
* **State Registration/Certificate Number** (`state_registration_number`, text, optional): Enter the state registration number.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the business was registered.
* **SBA Approval Number (if applicable)** (`sba_number`, text, optional): Enter the SBA certified minority business approval number.
* **Industry Classification Code** (`industry_classification`, text, optional): Enter the NAICS or SIC code.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any other IDs or details that may be needed.
