# Retail Chain Sale Event Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Chain Sale Event Voting Form is designed to gather feedback from customers and employees on the success and organization of retail chain sale events. The data collected will help the store management team identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer(s) that apply to the event you attended.
2. Provide detailed answers for the open-ended questions (question2, question4, question7, question8, and question9).
3. Choose the manager who oversaw the event.
4. If you wish to provide more information or feedback, you can include your contact details in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: This field is a multiple-choice question that asks if you attended the event. Select one or more answers that apply to your experience.
  * **Yes**: You attended the event.
  * **No**: You did not attend the event.

* **What was the event name?**: Please provide a brief description of the event you attended.
* **How many stores were involved?**: Enter the number of stores that participated in the event.
* **Was the event well-organized?**: This question has three options:
  * **Well-organized**: The event was well-planned and executed.
  * **Not well-organized**: The event was poorly planned or executed.
  * **Neutral**: You are neutral or have no opinion on the event's organization.
* **What did you learn from the event?**: Please provide a brief description of what you learned or gained from the event.
* **Will you attend another event like this?**: Answer this question with one of the following options:
  * **True**: You will likely attend another event like this.
  * **False**: You will not attend another event like this.
  * **Maybe**: You are unsure or have not decided.
* **Which store had the best sales display?**: Select one or more stores that you felt had the best sales display during the event.
  * **Yes**: The selected store had a great sales display.
  * **No**: The selected store did not have a great sales display.
* **What would you like to see improved in future events?**: Provide a suggestion or recommendation for how the events can be improved.
* **What was your favorite sale?**: Please provide a brief description of your favorite sale or purchase made during the event.
* **How many sales did you make during the event?**: Enter the number of sales you made during the event.
* **Event Rating**: This is a note field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information, including email and phone number, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Store Location**: Enter the location of the store you visited or attended the event.
* **Store Manager**: Select the manager who oversaw the event.
