The UFF MAD Team Survey is a tool used to gauge team member participation, understanding, and engagement. This form is designed to be a quick and easy way to gather valuable feedback from team members, helping the team leader or administrator to identify areas for improvement and optimize the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully and select the most accurate response for each field.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Use the available options for each field to provide as much detail as possible.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

* **Overview Attended** (select_one): Select whether you have attended the meeting, event, or training session in question.
  + `I have attended`: You have attended the meeting.
  + `I have not attended`: You have not attended the meeting.
  + `Maybe`: You are not sure or had an alternate opinion about attending.
