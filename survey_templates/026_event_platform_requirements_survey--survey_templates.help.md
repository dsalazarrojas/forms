# Event Platform Requirements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your event planning needs and preferences. Your input will help us understand what features and services we should prioritize to improve our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that apply to your event planning process.
2. Fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Click submit to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Platforms Used**: Select all the platforms you use for event planning:
	+ Yes (if you have used them)
	+ No (if you have not used them)
	+ Other (if you have used other platforms not listed)
* **Event Priorities**: Choose all the priorities that are relevant to your event planning:
	+ Yes (if it is a priority)
	+ No (if it is not a priority)
	+ Other (if it is a different priority)
* **Event Timeframe**: Enter the start and end dates of your event (mm/dd/yy to mm/dd/yy).
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of your event (hh:mm).
* **Event Teams Involved**: Select all the teams involved in your event:
	+ Yes (if they are involved)
	+ No (if they are not involved)
	+ Other (if other teams are involved)
* **Expected to Use**: Choose all the platforms you expect to use for your event:
	+ Yes (if you expect to use them)
	+ No (if you do not expect to use them)
	+ Other (if you expect to use other platforms not listed)
* **Teams Used**: Select the teams you actually use for your event:
	+ Yes (if you use them)
	+ No (if you do not use them)
* **Teams**: Select all the teams that are involved in your event:
	+ Yes (if they are involved)
	+ No (if they are not involved)
* **Expectations**: Enter any additional expectations or comments about your event planning process.
* **Priorities 2**: Choose all the priorities that are relevant to your event planning:
	+ Yes (if it is a priority)
	+ No (if it is not a priority)
	+ Other (if it is a different priority)
* **Timeframe 2**: Enter the start and end dates of your event (mm/dd/yy to mm/dd/yy).
* **Duration 2**: Enter the duration of your event (hh:mm).
