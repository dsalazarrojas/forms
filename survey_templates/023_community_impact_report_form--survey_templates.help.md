# Community Impact Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Impact Report Form is designed to collect information on the progress and outcomes of community programs during a specific reporting period. This data will be used to evaluate and improve these initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the reporting period covered by this report using the options provided in the "Reporting Period" field.
- Enter the full name of your organization in the "Full Organization Name" field.
- Enter your name as the person submitting the report in the "Name of Person Submitting Report" field.
- Enter the total number of unique individuals reached across all programs in this period in the "Total Unique Individuals Reached" field.
- Provide a list of key achievements and milestones met in the "List Key Achievements and Milestones" field.
- Describe any major challenges or obstacles encountered in the "Major Challenges and Obstacles Encountered" field.
- Summarize community feedback received in the "Community Feedback Highlights" field.
- Enter specific data points or Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) tracked in the "Impact Measurement Results" field.
- Offer suggestions for future adjustments and improvements in the "Future Recommendations and Adjustments" field.
- Finally, select the "Date of Submission" field to record the date this report is being submitted.
- Indicate if a follow-up meeting is required in the "Does this report require a follow-up meeting?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporting Period**: Select the period this report covers. This could be any of the following options: Quarter 1 (Jan-Mar), Quarter 2 (Apr-Jun), Quarter 3 (Jul-Sep), Quarter 4 (Oct-Dec), or Annual Report.
* **Full Organization Name**: Enter the full name of your organization as it is registered or known to the community.
* **Name of Person Submitting Report**: Please enter your name to ensure accountability and tracking of who submitted this report.
* **Total Unique Individuals Reached**: Enter the total number of unique individuals reached across all programs in this reporting period.
* **List Key Achievements and Milestones**: Provide a brief but comprehensive list of significant goals and milestones met during this reporting period.
* **Major Challenges and Obstacles Encountered**: Describe any significant challenges or obstacles that hindered your progress or impact.
* **Community Feedback Highlights**: Summarize the key points from community feedback received during this period.
* **Impact Measurement Results**: Enter specific data points or KPIs that measured the impact of your programs.
* **Future Recommendations and Adjustments**: Based on your current results, suggest changes or improvements that should be made for the next reporting period.
* **Date of Submission**: Record the date this report is being submitted.
* **Does this report require a follow-up meeting?**: Indicate if a follow-up meeting is necessary to discuss the report's findings or progress.
