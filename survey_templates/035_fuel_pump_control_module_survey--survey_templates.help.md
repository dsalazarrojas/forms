# Fuel Pump Control Module Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Pump Control Module Survey is used to collect information about the condition, installation, and maintenance of fuel pumps in vehicles. This survey will help users assess the performance and lifespan of their fuel pumps and make informed decisions about maintenance and replacement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are authorized to complete this survey.
2. Verify that the information provided is accurate and consistent with available documentation and site visits.
3. Select the relevant options for each field from the provided lists.
4. Enter any relevant comments or details about the fuel pump.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fuel Pump Control Module** (`fuel_pump_control_module_survey`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the fuel pump control module.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the fuel pump (e.g., Fuel Pump Control Module, Fuel Pump, Fuel System).
* **Make/Model** (`make_model`, text, required): Enter the make and model of the vehicle equipped with the fuel pump.
* **Year of Production** (`year_of_production`, number, required): Enter the year the fuel pump was produced.
* **Year Installed** (`year_installed`, number, required): Enter the year the fuel pump was installed.
* **Fuel Pump Size** (`fuel_pump_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the fuel pump (e.g., 1/2 Inch, 3/4 Inch, 1 Inch, 1+ Inch).
* **Fuel Pump Type** (`fuel_pump_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of fuel pump (e.g., Electric, Hydraulic, Pneumatic).
* **Fuel Pump Condition** (`fuel_pump_condition`, select_one, required): Select the condition of the fuel pump (e.g., New, Used, Rebuilt).
* **Installation Type** (`installation_type`, text, required): Enter the type of fuel pump installation (e.g., Standard, High-Pressure).
* **Installed Location** (`installed_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the fuel pump was installed.
* **Installed Date** (`installed_date`, date, required): Enter the date the fuel pump was installed.
* **Installed Time** (`installed_time`, time, required): Enter the time the fuel pump was installed.
* **Fuel Pump Pressure Rating** (`fuel_pump_pressure_rating`, number, required): Enter the pressure rating of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Flow Rate** (`fuel_pump_flow_rate`, number, required): Enter the flow rate of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Wattage Rating** (`fuel_pump_wattage_rating`, number, required): Enter the wattage rating of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Vibration Rating** (`fuel_pump_vibration_rating`, number, required): Enter the vibration rating of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Leakage Rating** (`fuel_pump_leakage_rating`, number, required): Enter the leakage rating of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Temperature Rating** (`fuel_pump_temperature_rating`, number, required): Enter the temperature rating of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Maintenance** (`fuel_pump_maintenance`, text, required): Enter any maintenance history or notes about the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Failure Reason** (`fuel_pump_failure_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for any failures or malfunctions of the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Replacement Part** (`fuel_pump_replacement_part`, text, required): Enter the replacement part used for the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Replacement Cost** (`fuel_pump_replacement_cost`, text, required): Enter the cost of replacing the fuel pump.
* **Fuel Pump Replacement Date** (`fuel_pump_replacement_date`, date, required): Enter the date the fuel pump was replaced.
* **Fuel Pump Replacement Time** (`fuel_pump_replacement_time`, time, required): Enter the time the fuel pump was replaced.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the fuel pump.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* Verify that the information provided is consistent with available documentation and site visits.
* Do not duplicate entries or surveys without proper documentation and communication.
* Complete this survey for each fuel pump in your vehicle.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or maintenance team.
