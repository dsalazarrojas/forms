# Smartphone Consumer Behavior Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data about the smartphone usage behavior of consumers to help identify patterns and trends in the data. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. For multiple choice questions, select only one option from the provided choices.
3. For numeric questions, enter a whole number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, text, not required): This is the initial question, it will introduce you to the form. Please read it carefully and proceed to the next question.
* **Do you use a smartphone?** (`2`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Yes' if you use a smartphone, otherwise answer 'No'.
* **What phone model you use?** (`3`, select_multiple, required): Please select the phone model(s) you use.
* **How often do you use your phone?** (`4`, number, required): Please enter the number of hours you use your phone per day.
* **How do you use your screen?** (`5`, select_multiple, required): Please select the ways you use your screen.
* **What is the size of your screen?** (`6`, select_one, required): Please select the size of your screen.
* **What apps do you use?** (`7`, select_multiple, required): Please select the apps you use.
* **What accessories do you use with your phone?** (`8`, select_multiple, required): Please select the accessories you use.
* **Do you think your phone is expensive?** (`9`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Yes' if you think your phone is expensive, otherwise answer 'No'.
* **What is your battery life like?** (`10`, select_multiple, required): Please select the battery life you experience with your phone.
* **What is the quality of your screen?** (`11`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Good' if you think your screen quality is good, otherwise answer 'Poor'.
* **How much memory does your phone have?** (`12`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Yes' if you think your phone has enough memory, otherwise answer 'No'.
* **How much storage does your phone have?** (`13`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Yes' if you think your phone has enough storage, otherwise answer 'No'.
* **How do you charge your phone?** (`14`, select_multiple, required): Please select the ways you charge your phone.
* **What color is your phone?** (`15`, select_multiple, required): Please select the color of your phone.
* **What is your screen resolution?** (`16`, select_one, required): Please select the resolution of your screen.
* **What is the size of your screen?** (`17`, select_one, required): Please select the size of your screen.
* **Which operating system is your phone using?** (`18`, select_multiple, required): Please select the operating system of your phone.
* **What is your phone camera like?** (`19`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Yes' if you think your camera is good, otherwise answer 'No'.
* **How much capacity does your battery have?** (`20`, number, required): Please enter the battery capacity of your phone.
* **How much does your phone weigh?** (`21`, number, required): Please enter the weight of your phone.
* **How long is your phone?** (`22`, number, required): Please enter the length of your phone.
* **How wide is your phone?** (`23`, number, required): Please enter the width of your phone.
* **How tall is your phone?** (`24`, number, required): Please enter the height of your phone.
* **Note** (`25`, note, not required): This is a note field where you can add any additional information about your phone.

## Tips
Please answer each question accurately and honestly to the best of your knowledge. If you're unsure about a particular question, please don't hesitate to ask. We appreciate your participation in this form and thank you for your time.
