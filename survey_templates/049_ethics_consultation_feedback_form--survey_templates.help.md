# Ethics Consultation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Consultation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers or clients about their experience with ethics consultations. This information will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name (if applicable).
3. Enter your Email (we'll use this to contact you if needed).
4. Provide your Phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
5. Share your Feedback about the ethics consultation.
6. Rate your experience using the Rating field (from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent).
7. Rate another aspect of your experience using the Another Rating field (from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name (if you have one).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about the ethics consultation. Be as detailed as you'd like, but keep it concise.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the ethics consultation on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Another Rating** (`rating_2`, number, optional): Rate another aspect of your experience with the ethics consultation on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent).

## Tips
- Be honest and constructive with your feedback. Your input is valuable to us!
- You can rate different aspects of your experience, like the consultation process, communication, or outcome.
