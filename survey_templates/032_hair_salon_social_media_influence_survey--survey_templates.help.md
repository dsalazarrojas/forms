# Hair Salon Social Media Influence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to understand the effectiveness of our social media marketing efforts and how they influence our customers' behavior and decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select all the social media platforms that you use to interact with us.
3. Choose how you discovered our salon (e.g., social media, word of mouth, etc.).
4. Rate your experience with our social media marketing efforts.
5. Answer the questions about follow-up and customer satisfaction.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Salon Social Media Influence Survey** (`survey_title`, text, required: false): This is the title of the survey.
* **Salon Name** (`salon_name`, text, required: false): Please type in the name of the salon you visited or plan to visit.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the social media platforms you use to interact with us (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
* **Social Media Goals** (`social_media_goals`, select_one, required: false): Choose which social media goals you feel we have achieved or not (e.g., to increase awareness, to engage, etc.).
* **Current Platforms** (`current_platforms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the social media platforms you currently use.
* **Platforms Used** (`platforms_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the social media platforms you have used to interact with us.
* **Discovery Channel** (`discovery_channel`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you discovered our salon (e.g., social media, word of mouth, etc.).
* **How Discovered** (`how_discovered`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the ways you discovered our salon.
* **Social Media Influence** (`social_media_influence`, number, required: false): This question is not applicable. You can skip it.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide your website.
* **Thank You Message** (`thank_you`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to leave a message of appreciation.
* **Survey Completed Date** (`survey_completed_date`, date, required: false): This is the date you completed the survey.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Message** (`follow_up_message`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide a follow-up message.
* **Follow-up Frequency Other** (`follow_up_frequency_other`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide a custom follow-up frequency.
* **Survey Influenced** (`survey_influenced`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether our social media efforts influenced your behavior or decisions.
* **Survey Influenced Reason** (`survey_influenced_reason`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the reasons why our social media efforts influenced your behavior or decisions.
* **Survey Satisfied** (`survey_satisfied`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with our social media marketing efforts.
* **Survey Satisfied Reason** (`survey_satisfied_reason`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the reasons why you are satisfied or not satisfied with our social media marketing efforts.
* **Survey Recommend** (`survey_recommend`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you would recommend our social media marketing efforts.
* **Survey Recommend Reason** (`survey_recommend_reason`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the reasons why you would or would not recommend our social media marketing efforts.
* **Survey Completed** (`survey_completed`, select_one, required: false): Rate whether you completed this survey.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false): This question is a duplicate of the previous question. You can skip it.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide additional comments or suggestions.
