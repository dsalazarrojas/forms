Urban Design Public Input Submission Form - Help Guide
=====================================================

## Purpose

The Urban Design Public Input Submission Form is a platform for stakeholders to provide feedback and input on urban design projects. It is used to gather information on various aspects of a project, including its name, location, status, and comments from submitters and respondents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Street Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Select the **Street Type** from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief **Description** of the project.
4. Fill out the **Submitter Information** fields:
	* **Submitter Name**
	* **Submitter Email**
	* **Submitter Phone**
5. Provide the **Project Information**:
	* **Project Name**
	* **Project Description**
	* **Project Location**
	* **Project Status**
6. Answer the **Submitter Survey** questions:
	* Has Submitter Completed Survey
	* Responds to Other
	* Other Comments
	* Respondent
	* Comments
	* Additional Comments
	* Suggestions
	* Submitter Survey Comments 2
	* Submitter Survey Additional Comments 2
	* Surveyed
	* Respondent Count
	* Submitter Survey Comments 3

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Name** (`street_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the street where the project is located.
* **Street Type** (`street_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of street (e.g. Urban, Boulevard, Parkway).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the submission.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide additional comments from the submitter.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the project (e.g. Active, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Has Submitter Completed Survey** (`submitter_survey_completed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if the submitter has completed the survey.
* **Responds to Other** (`submitter_survey_respondent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if the submitter responds to other.
* **Other Comments** (`submitter_survey_completed_other`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional comments on the survey.
* **Respondent** (`submitter_survey_respondent_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the respondent.
* **Comments** (`submitter_survey_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide comments on the survey.
* **Additional Comments** (`submitter_survey_additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional comments on the survey.
* **Suggestions** (`submitter_survey_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Provide suggestions on the survey.
* **Submitter Survey Comments 2** (`submitter_survey_comments_2`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional comments on the survey.
* **Submitter Survey Additional Comments 2** (`submitter_survey_additional_comments_2`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional comments on the survey.
* **Surveyed** (`submitter_survey_surveyed`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if the survey has been completed.
* **Respondent Count** (`submitter_survey_respondent_count`, `text`, required: false): Enter the count of respondents.
* **Submitter Survey Comments 3** (`submitter_survey_comments_3`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional comments on the survey.

Note: This help guide is not intended to be an exhaustive explanation of the form fields, but rather a concise overview of the form's purpose and functionality.
