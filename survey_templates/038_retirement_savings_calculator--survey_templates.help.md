# Retirement Savings Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retirement Savings Calculator is a financial planning tool designed to help you estimate your retirement savings needs. It will guide you through a series of questions to determine your target wealth, retirement goals, and other relevant factors to provide a personalized estimate of your total savings required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the "Max" questions to determine the maximum amount of savings required to achieve your highest possible returns.
2. Answer the "Min" questions to determine the minimum amount of savings required to avoid risk or financial insecurity.
3. Review your answers and adjust as needed to reflect your personal preferences and goals.
4. Click the "Calculate" button to generate your estimated total savings requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Contributions** (`monthly_contributions`, `text`, optional): Enter the amount you plan to contribute monthly to your retirement savings.
* **Monthly Rate of Return** (`monthly_rate_of_return`, `number`, optional): Enter the expected rate of return on your investments.
* **Target Wealth** (`target_wealth`, `select_one`, optional): Select your target wealth goal (Retirement, Other).
* **Target Age** (`target_age`, `time`, optional): Enter your target retirement age.
* **Inflation Rate** (`inflation_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the expected inflation rate.
* **Years to Retirement** (`years_to_retirement`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years until your retirement.
* **Monthly Savings Requirement** (`monthly_savings_requirement`, `select_one`, optional): Select your maximum monthly savings requirement ($1,000, $2,000, $3,000, $4,000, $5,000, $6,000, $7,000, $8,000, $9,000, $10,000).
* **Inflation Adjusted Monthly Savings Requirement** (`inflation_adjusted_monthly_savings_requirement`, `number`, optional): Enter the inflation adjusted monthly savings requirement.
* **Monthly Savings** (`monthly_savings`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you plan to save monthly.
* **Total Savings** (`total_savings`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your maximum total savings goal ($10,000, $20,000, $30,000, $40,000, $50,000, $60,000, $70,000, $80,000, $90,000, $100,000).
* **Savings Frequency** (`savings_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you plan to save monthly or not.
* **Retirement Goal** (`retirement_goal`, `select_one`, optional): Select your retirement goal (Maximize wealth, Minimize risk, Maximize returns, Other).
* **Max Inflation Rate** (`max_inflation_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the maximum expected inflation rate.
* **Max Inflation Adjusted Monthly Savings Requirement** (`max_inflation_adjusted_monthly_savings_requirement`, `number`, optional): Enter the maximum inflation adjusted monthly savings requirement.
* **Max Monthly Savings** (`max_monthly_savings`, `number`, optional): Enter the maximum amount you plan to save monthly.
* **Max Total Savings** (`max_total_savings`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your maximum total savings goal ($10,000, $20,000, $30,000, $40,000, $50,000, $60,000, $70,000, $80,000, $90,000, $100,000).
* **Max Savings Frequency** (`max_savings_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you plan to save monthly or not.
* **Retirement Goal Max** (`retirement_goal_max`, `select_one`, optional): Select your maximum retirement goal (Maximize wealth, Minimize risk, Maximize returns, Other).
* **Min Inflation Rate** (`min_inflation_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the minimum expected inflation rate.
* **Min Inflation Adjusted Monthly Savings Requirement** (`min_inflation_adjusted_monthly_savings_requirement`, `number`, optional): Enter the minimum inflation adjusted monthly savings requirement.
* **Min Monthly Savings** (`min_monthly_savings`, `number`, optional): Enter the minimum amount you plan to save monthly.
* **Min Total Savings** (`min_total_savings`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your minimum total savings goal ($10,000, $20,000, $30,000, $40,000, $50,000, $60,000, $70,000, $80,000, $90,000, $100,000).
* **Min Savings Frequency** (`min_savings_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you plan to save monthly or not.
* **Retirement Goal Min** (`retirement_goal_min`, `select_one`, optional): Select your minimum retirement goal (Maximize wealth, Minimize risk, Maximize returns, Other).

## Tips
* Make sure to answer the "Max" questions to determine the maximum amount of savings required to achieve your highest possible returns.
* Make sure to answer the "Min" questions to determine the minimum amount of savings required to avoid risk or financial insecurity.
* Review your answers carefully before clicking the "Calculate" button to ensure accuracy.
* Adjust your answers as needed to reflect your personal preferences and goals.
* Consider consulting a financial advisor for personalized advice before making significant financial decisions.
