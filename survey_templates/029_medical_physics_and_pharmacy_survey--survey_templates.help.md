# medical_physics_and_pharmacy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the "Medical Physics and Pharmacy Survey" form. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the current state of collaboration between medical physics and pharmacy professionals. It aims to collect data on their experiences, challenges, and future visions for this field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide background information in the "background information" field (Page 1).
2. Select your current position (Page 2).
3. Choose specific areas of interest (Page 3, select one option).
4. Describe the challenges facing the field (Page 4).
5. Select multiple key topics related to your experience (Page 5, select multiple options).
6. Choose the type of collaboration you have (Page 6, select one option).
7. Describe recent events in your experience (Page 7).
8. Select the state of your collaboration (Page 8, select one option).
9. Provide a future vision for the field (Page 9).
9. Share any suggestions (Page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - Background Information
* **Background Information (text)**: This is the first page where you will provide some background information about yourself or your team.

### Page 2 - Current Position
* **Current Position (text)**: This field is where you will describe your current position or role in the medical physics and pharmacy field. This could be your job title, department, or institution.

### Page 3 - Specific Areas of Interest
* **Specific Areas of Interest (select one)**: Choose one specific area of interest from the list:
	+ Anesthesia and Critical Care
	+ Clinical Trials
	+ Pharmacology
	+ Radiology
	+ Research

### Page 4 - Challenges Facing
* **Challenges Facing the Field (text)**: Describe the challenges you have faced or are currently facing in the medical physics and pharmacy field.

### Page 5 - Key Topics
* **Key Topics (select multiple)**: Select all the key topics that are relevant to your experience:
	+ Pharmacology
	+ Radiology
	+ Research
	+ Regulatory
	+ Safety
	+ Science

### Page 6 - Collaboration
* **Collaboration (select one)**: Choose the type of collaboration you have:
	+ Interdisciplinary
	+ Interprofessional
	+ Multidisciplinary
	+ University-Industry Partnership

### Page 7 - Recent Events
* **Recent Events (text)**: Describe any recent events or experiences you have had in the medical physics and pharmacy field.

### Page 8 - Collaboration State
* **Collaboration State (select one)**: Select the state of your collaboration:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Page 9 - Future Vision
* **Future Vision (text)**: Provide a future vision for the medical physics and pharmacy field.

### Page 10 - Suggestions
* **Suggestions (text)**: Share any suggestions for improvement or innovation in the field.
