# Mortality Themes Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mortality Themes Assessment form is designed to collect information about an individual's perceptions and emotions related to mortality. It is used to identify themes that an individual has experienced or is likely to experience, based on their answers to various questions in each theme. This form is likely used in research or clinical settings to help professionals provide better care or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the relevant theme section, indicating your thoughts and feelings about mortality.
2. If you have any personal thoughts or concerns about mortality, you can type them in the "Summary" fields.
3. When you have finished answering all questions, click on the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

* **Mortality Themes Assessment 1 (Theme 1)**: This section assesses your thoughts and feelings about mortality. Answer questions related to what you think mortality is and what concerns you have about it.
* **Mortality Themes Assessment 2 (Theme 2)**: This section is similar to the first one, but focuses on a different aspect of mortality.
* **Mortality Themes Assessment 3 (Theme 3)**: This section assesses your thoughts and feelings about mortality as well, but with a different focus.
* **Email Summary**: If you would like to receive a summary of your responses via email, enter your email address here.
* **Phone Summary**: If you would like to receive a summary of your responses via phone call, enter your phone number here.
* **Summary by Email**: Enter a summary of your responses here, and you will receive a summary via email.
* **Summary**: This is an optional field where you can type a summary of your responses.

* **Theme 6**: This section is similar to the previous ones, but with a different focus.
