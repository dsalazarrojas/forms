# TMJ Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your awareness and experiences related to TMJ issues. Your participation will help us better understand users' knowledge and perceptions about this condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select your answers from the options provided for each question.
3. Enter your email address and phone number if you wish to receive a copy of your results.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Inputs** (user_inputs): Enter your responses to the questions asked in the form.
- **Quiz Questions** (quiz_questions): Select all that apply from the list of questions to answer how you relate to them. For example, "Sometimes", "Often", or "Never".
- **Multiple Choice for Quiz 2** (quiz_questions_2): Select all that apply from the list of options for Question 2.
- **Date Completed** (date_completed): Enter the date on which you completed this form.
- **Time Completed** (time_completed): Enter the time on which you completed this form.
- **Comments** (comments): Provide any additional comments or information about your experiences with TMJ issues.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address if you wish to receive a copy of your results.
- **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive a follow-up call.
- **Submit** (submit): Click this button to submit your answers.
- **Back** (back): Click this button to return to the previous page without submitting your answers.
