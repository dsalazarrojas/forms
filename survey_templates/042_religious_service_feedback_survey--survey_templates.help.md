# religious_service_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from religious service attendees to improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name
2. Enter your Email Address
3. For each question, select the answer that best matches your response:
    * How often do you attend our religious service?
        + 'Yes'
        + 'No'
    * How satisfied are you with our service?
        + 1 - Very dissatisfied
        + 2 - Somewhat dissatisfied
        + 3 - Neutral
        + 4 - Satisfied
        + 5 - Very Satisfied
4. If applicable, type in your favorite hymn or song.
5. Type in any comments or suggestions you have for improving our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **How often do you attend our religious service?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how often you attend our religious service.
* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (Select One, Required): Select the level of satisfaction you experience with our service.
* **What is your favorite hymn or song?** (Text, Optional): Type in the name of your favorite hymn or song.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments or suggestions you have for improving our service.
* **What could be improved about our service?** (Text, Optional): Type in any areas of improvement you think we could implement.
 
## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* Answer all required fields.
