# AI Services Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the services provided by our AI. The feedback will help us understand how our services performed and identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide detailed information about your experience with our AI services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the AI service you used from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide the date you used the service.
3. Rate the quality of the AI output, processing speed, and ease of use of the interface.
4. Describe how relevant the results were to your request.
5. Select your overall satisfaction with the service (1-5).
6. Choose whether the service met your expectations.
7. Indicate how likely you are to recommend our AI services to a colleague.
8. Select all the positive and improvement aspects of the service.
9. Describe any specific issues you encountered.
10. Suggest new features or capabilities you would like to see.
11. Provide your email address if you would like a response to your feedback.
12. Let us know if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Which AI service did you use?**: Select the AI service you used from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand which service you are providing feedback about.
* **Date of Service Usage**: Enter the date you used the service. This will help us track your experience over time.
* **Quality of AI output**: Rate the technical quality of the result. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Speed of processing/response**: Rate the speed of the processing/response. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Ease of use of the interface**: Rate the ease of use of the interface. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Relevance of results to your request**: Rate how well the AI understood your request. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Overall satisfaction**: Select your overall satisfaction with the service (1-5). This will help us understand your general opinion.
* **Did the service meet your expectations?**: Choose whether the service met your expectations. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend our AI services to a colleague?**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend our AI services to a colleague. Select the option that best describes your opinion.
* **What did you like most?**: Select all the positive aspects of the service that you liked most.
* **What needs improvement?**: Select all the areas that need improvement.
* **What specific issues did you encounter?**: Describe any specific issues you encountered while using the service.
* **New features or capabilities?**: Suggest new features or capabilities you would like to see in our services.
* **Email Address**: If you would like a response to your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **May we contact you regarding this feedback?**: Let us know if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Please describe any specific issues you encountered**: Provide detailed information about any specific issues you encountered.
* **What new features or capabilities would you like to see?**: Suggest new features or capabilities you would like to see in our services.
* **Email Address**: If you would like a response to your feedback, please enter your email address. (This is a duplicate of the previous field, but with a different label.)
* **May we contact you regarding this feedback?**: Let us know if you would like to be contacted about your feedback. (This is also a duplicate of the previous field, but with a different label.)

**Tips**
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your experience and providing feedback.
* If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.
* If you have any specific questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your input and thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback.
