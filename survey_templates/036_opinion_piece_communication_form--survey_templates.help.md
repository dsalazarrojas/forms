To ensure this form is completed correctly and accurately, please read the following help guide.

## Purpose
The Opinion Piece Communication Form is designed to collect feedback, comments, and other opinions from individuals, organizations, or communities. This form helps to gather information about a specific opinion piece, such as an article, story, or other form of written content, and provides a structured way to communicate your thoughts and insights.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
2. **Choose the correct category**: Select the correct category for your opinion piece, such as "Opinion Piece", "Article", or "Story".
3. **Enter your name and email**: Provide your real name and a valid email address.
4. **Add any comments or thoughts**: Share your thoughts and insights about the opinion piece, if desired.
5. **Submit the form**: Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to the intended recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, required/true): Choose the category for your opinion piece. This helps the recipient understand the type of content they are responding to.
* **Writer Name** (`Writer Name`, required/true, type: `text`): Enter your real name as you want it to be displayed on the opinion piece.
* **Email** (`Email`, required/true): Enter a valid email address associated with your name.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, optional/true): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the opinion piece. This is an optional field, but helps to provide more context and insight.
* **Submitter Category** (`Submitter Category`, required/true): Select the category for the person submitting the opinion piece. This helps the recipient understand who is submitting the content.
* **Writer Email** (`Writer Email`, required/true): Enter the email address associated with the writer of the opinion piece.
* **Date** (`Date`, required/true): Enter the date when the opinion piece was submitted.
* **Time** (`Time`, required/true): Enter the time when the opinion piece was submitted.
* **Submitter** (`Submitter`, required/true): Enter your name as you want it to be displayed for the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, required/true): Enter a valid email address for the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`Submitter Comments`, optional/true): Share any additional comments or context about the submitter, if desired.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional/true): Enter a phone number for the submitter, if desired.
* **Note** (`Note`, optional/true): Add any additional information or context, if desired.
* **Submitter Email 2** (`Submitter Email 2`, required/true): Enter another valid email address for the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone 2** (`Submitter Phone 2`, optional/true): Enter another phone number for the submitter, if desired.
* **Submitter Category 2** (`Submitter Category 2`, required/true): Select another category for the submitter.

Please note that some fields are marked as required, and you must fill them out for the form to be submitted correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
