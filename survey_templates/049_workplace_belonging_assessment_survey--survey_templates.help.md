# Workplace Belonging Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Belonging Assessment Survey is a tool designed to help employees feel connected and engaged in their workplace. It assesses their level of belonging in various aspects of their job and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best describe your experience. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Survey** (`team_survey`): This question asks about your feelings of belonging and inclusion in your team. Choose 'Yes' if you feel like you belong and are included in your team's activities, or 'No' if you don't feel like you belong or are included.
* **Connection** (`connection_survey`): This question asks about your level of connection with your team members. Choose 'Yes' if you feel connected to your team members, or 'No' if you don't feel connected.
* **Inclusion** (`inclusion_survey`): This question asks about how included you feel in your work environment. Choose 'Yes' if you feel included, or 'No' if you don't feel included.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`): This question asks about your level of job satisfaction. Choose 'Satisfied' if you're satisfied with your job, or 'Dissatisfied' if you're not satisfied.
* **Work Satisfaction** (`work_satisfaction`): This question asks about your level of work satisfaction. Choose 'Satisfied' if you're satisfied with your work, or 'Dissatisfied' if you're not satisfied.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`): This question asks about your access to communication channels. Choose 'Yes' if you have access to communication channels, or 'No' if you don't have access.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`): This question asks about your feelings towards your team leadership. Choose 'Yes' if you trust and respect your team leadership, or 'No' if you don't.
* **Manager Survey** (`manager_survey`): This question asks about your feelings towards your manager. Choose 'Yes' if you trust and respect your manager, or 'No' if you don't.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): This question allows you to provide any additional feedback you'd like to share with your supervisor or HR representative.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about your workplace experience.
