# Marine Organisms Characteristics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on marine organisms, including their habitat, species, size, and location. The information collected will help in understanding and analyzing the characteristics of marine organisms.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by selecting the "What is a marine organism" page (page 1).
* Answer the first question and provide a brief description of the marine organism.
* Proceed to the next pages and answer the questions accordingly.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as required).
* Once completed, review your answers to ensure they are accurate.
* If you need to save and come back later, you can do so by clicking the "Save" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is a Marine Organism** (`1`, `text`, `false`)
	+ This is the first question on the form. Please provide a brief description of the marine organism you are observing or collecting.
* **What habitat does it live in** (`2`, `text`, `false`)
	+ Describe the habitat where the marine organism lives, such as coral reef, open ocean, or estuary.
* **Which species does it belong to** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
	+ Select all applicable species that the marine organism belongs to (e.g., fish, shellfish, plankton).
* **What color is it** (`4`, `select_one`, `true`)
	+ Select the color of the marine organism (e.g., blue, green, brown).
* **What shape is it** (`5`, `select_one`, `true`)
	+ Select the shape of the marine organism (e.g., spherical, linear, irregular).
* **What size is it** (`6`, `number`, `true`)
	+ Provide the size of the marine organism in units (e.g., centimeters, millimeters).
* **Where does it live** (`7`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
	+ Select all applicable locations where the marine organism lives (e.g., ocean, lake, river).
* **Date collected** (`8`, `date`, `false`)
	+ If you collected the marine organism, provide the date of collection.
* **Time collected** (`9`, `time`, `false`)
	+ If you collected the marine organism, provide the time of collection.
* **Notes** (`10`, `note`, `false`)
	+ Add any additional notes about the marine organism.
* **Who collected** (`11`, `text`, `false`)
	+ Provide the name of the person who collected the marine organism.
* **Email** (`12`, `email`, `false`)
	+ Provide the email of the person who collected the marine organism.
* **Phone** (`13`, `text`, `false`)
	+ Provide the phone number of the person who collected the marine organism.
* **Research Team** (`14`, `text`, `false`)
	+ Provide the name of the research team that collected the marine organism.

Note: This guide is for general understanding and may not cover all possible edge cases or exceptions. If you are unsure about a particular field, it's always best to refer to the field's type and required status.
