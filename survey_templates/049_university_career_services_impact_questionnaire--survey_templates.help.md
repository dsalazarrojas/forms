# University Career Services Impact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from students and alumni on the impact of career services provided by the university. It helps the university assess the effectiveness of its career services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, make sure you have a clear understanding of the questions and fields. The form is designed to be completed by students and alumni who have used or are interested in using the university's career services. Follow these steps:

1. Fill out your title: Enter your name and title as you would like it to appear on the report.
2. Enter your information: Provide your student ID, email, and phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
3. Answer the usage question: Select how frequently you use the university's career services.
4. Rate your satisfaction: Select how satisfied you are with the career services.
5. Select your outcomes: Choose all the outcomes that you have achieved as a result of using the career services.
6. Provide any additional comments or data: If you have any additional comments or data that would be helpful in understanding the impact of career services.
7. Choose a follow-up date and time if you want to be contacted for further follow-up on your feedback.
8. Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **University Career Services Impact Questionnaire** (form_title, text, required/optional): This is the title of the report that will be used to identify your submission.
  - *Please enter your name and title as you would like it to appear on the report.*
- **Student/Alumni Information** (student_alumni_info, text, required/optional): This is your personal information.
  - *Please enter your student ID, email, and phone number so that we can contact you if needed.*
- **Usage of Career Services** (usage_services, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about how frequently you use the university's career services.
  - *Choose how frequently you use the career services: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.*
- **Satisfaction with Career Services** (satisfaction_services, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the career services.
  - *Choose how satisfied you are with the career services: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Unsatisfied, or Very Unsatisfied.*
- **Outcomes of Career Services** (usage_outcomes, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the outcomes you have achieved as a result of using the career services.
  - *Choose all the outcomes you have achieved: Graduate School, Job Placement, Internship/Co-op, Research, Extracurricular, or Other.*
- **Data Collection** (data_collection, number, required/optional): This is a numerical field to gather additional comments or data.
  - *Please provide any additional comments or data that would be helpful in understanding the impact of career services.*
- **Follow-up** (follow_up, date, required/optional): This is a date field to schedule follow-up contact with you.
  - *Choose a follow-up date and time if you would like to be contacted for further follow-up on your feedback.*
- **Email** (email, email, required/optional): This is your email address.
  - *Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.*
- **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): This is your phone number.
  - *Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.*
