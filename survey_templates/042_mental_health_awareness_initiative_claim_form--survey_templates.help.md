# mental_health_awareness_initiative_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals who are seeking support or sponsorship for mental health initiatives. This form helps to understand their needs, goals, and requirements for mental health initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Provide the initiative date and description.
3. Choose the claimant support type (supporter, non-supporter, or both).
4. Enter the initiative sponsorship details, including the amount and currency.
5. Provide your claimant status and any additional notes or comments.
6. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your submission.
