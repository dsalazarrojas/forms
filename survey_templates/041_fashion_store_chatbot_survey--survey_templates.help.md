# Fashion Store Chatbot Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our fashion store chatbot. Your feedback and preferences will help us improve the chatbot's performance and provide a better customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and provide your honest responses.
2. Answer each field according to your thoughts and feelings.
3. Please note that some fields are free-text, allowing you to provide as much detail as you'd like.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Preferences** (Text) 
  - Please tell us about your general preferences when interacting with our fashion store chatbot.
  - You can mention anything that comes to mind about your experiences, likes, and dislikes.
* **Budget Preferences** (Text)
  - Share with us your budget or spending habits. This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Feedback** (Text)
  - Provide any comments or suggestions about your experience with the chatbot.
  - Think about how we can improve and what we do well.
* **Customer Preferences Questions** (Text)
  - Answer any questions you have about our store or chatbot.
  - Ask us anything that comes to mind.
* **Budget** (Text)
  - Describe your budget or spending habits when interacting with our fashion store chatbot.
* **Product Preferences** (Text)
  - Share your likes, dislikes, and preferences when it comes to our products.
* **Customer Info** (Text)
  - Provide demographic information about yourself (age, location, etc.).
  - This will help us better understand our customer base.
* **Store Info** (Text)
  - Share any information about our store or services that you find interesting or valuable.
* **Purchase Preferences** (Text)
  - Describe your purchase behavior or preferences when interacting with our fashion store chatbot.
* **Chatbot Feedback** (Text)
  - Provide any suggestions or comments about the chatbot's performance or usability.
  - Think about how we can improve and what we do well.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* Your responses will help us improve the chatbot and provide a better customer experience.
