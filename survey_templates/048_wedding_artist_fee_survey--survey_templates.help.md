# Wedding Artist Fee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Artist Fee Survey is a form designed to collect information from artists participating in wedding events. The form aims to gather details about their fees, services offered, and contact information. This information will help event organizers and wedding planners make informed decisions about hiring artists for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Wedding Artist Fee Survey, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the form.
2. Review each field carefully and provide the required information accurately.
3. Fill out the fields on each page according to your knowledge and experience.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next page.
5. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist Name
* **Artist Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or contact information.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: Yes

### Base Formation
* **Base Formation**: Enter any base fee or minimum required for your services.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

### Travel Preferences
* **Travel Preferences**: Select all methods you prefer or are willing to use for traveling to events, such as driving or flying.
* **Type**: select_multiple
* **Required**: No

### Fee Range 1
* **Fee Range 1**: Enter your minimum fee for an event (e.g., the lowest amount you accept for a service).
* **Type**: number
* **Required**: Yes

### Fee Range 2
* **Fee Range 2**: Enter your maximum fee for an event (e.g., the highest amount you accept for a service).
* **Type**: number
* **Required**: Yes

### Travel Cost 1
* **Travel Cost 1**: Enter the cost for traveling to events, such as fuel or transportation.
* **Type**: number
* **Required**: Yes

### Travel Cost 2
* **Travel Cost 2**: Enter the cost for returning from events, such as fuel or transportation.
* **Type**: number
* **Required**: Yes

### Booking Priorities
* **Booking Priorities**: Select your top priorities when booking events, such as being budget-friendly, convenience, or time-sensitive.
* **Type**: select_multiple
* **Required**: No

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information or notes that may be useful for the event organizer or planner.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Type**: email
* **Required**: Yes

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your official phone number.
* **Type**: number
* **Required**: No

### Service Type
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you offer, such as photography, hair and makeup, or videography.
* **Type**: select_one
* **Required**: Yes

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to help the event organizers and planners make informed decisions about booking you for their events.
