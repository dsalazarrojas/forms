# Holiday Event Recovery Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience at a recent holiday event. The information collected will help the organization refine their event management strategy and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the purpose and questions carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and honest feedback.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully, as they will help the organization understand your experience.
3. Use the provided options for questions with multiple-choice options, such as the "overall_event_satisfaction" question.
4. For open-ended questions like "what_we_can_improve" and "what_improve_on", provide specific and concrete suggestions.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Event Recovery Survey Form** (`holiday_event_recovery_survey_form`, text, optional): This is a brief description of the event, which can be left blank if it's not necessary.
* **Attendee Feedback** (`attendee_feedback`, text, required): Share your honest feedback about your experience at the event.
* **Overall Event Satisfaction** (`overall_event_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you were with the event, from "Not Satisfied" to "Extremely Satisfied".
* **What We Can Improve** (`what_we_can_improve`, text, optional): Suggest specific areas where the event can be improved.
* **Would Attend Again** (`would_attend_again`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how likely you are to attend a similar event in the future, from "Not Likely" to "Extremely Likely".
* **What to Improve On** (`what_improve_on`, text, required): Share specific things you think the event can improve on, such as logistics, content, or communication.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact, if you'd like to be reached out for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact, if you'd like to be reached out for further discussion.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a confirmation button to submit your completed survey.
