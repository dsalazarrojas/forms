# New Year's Business Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year's Business Goals Survey is a tool for businesses to reflect on their objectives and set targets for the upcoming year. It's meant to be completed by business owners or managers to assess their company's progress and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the current year**: In the "New Year" field, enter the current year. This will serve as a reference point for your goals and objectives.

2. **Identify your company objectives**: Choose one or more of the options in the "What are your company objectives" field, based on what you want to achieve with your business this year. You can select one or multiple options, as this field is not required.

3. **Describe your business aspirations**: In the "What are your business aspirations" field, enter a brief description of what you hope to achieve or become as a business. This is an opportunity to share your vision or dreams for your company.

4. **Identify your target market**: In the "Who is your primary target market" field, select the options that best describe your target audience. This will help you tailor your strategies and resources to effectively reach and serve your customers.

5. **Set a financial goal**: In the "What is your financial goal" field, enter a numerical value that represents your target revenue or financial objective for the year. This will help you track progress and stay on track.

6. **Outline your marketing strategy**: In the "What is your marketing strategy" field, enter a brief description of how you plan to promote and grow your business. This will help you stay focused on your marketing efforts and make informed decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Year**: <input type="text"> Enter the current year as a reference point for your goals and objectives.
- **What are your company objectives**: <input type="select"> Choose one or more options for what you want to achieve with your business this year.
  - **Improve customer satisfaction**: Select this option if you aim to increase customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  - **Increase sales**: Select this option if you hope to grow your sales and revenue this year.
  - **Enhance customer experience**: Select this option if you want to provide better services or products to your customers.
- **What are your business aspirations**: <input type="text"> Enter a brief description of what you hope to achieve or become as a business.
- **Who is your primary target market**: <input type="select_multiple"> Select one or more options that best describe your target audience.
  - **Local customers**: Select this option if your business primarily serves customers in your local area.
  - **National customers**: Select this option if your business serves customers across the country.
  - **International customers**: Select this option if your business operates globally.
- **What is your financial goal**: <input type="number"> Enter a numerical value that represents your target revenue or financial objective for the year.
- **What is your marketing strategy**: <input type="text"> Enter a brief description of how you plan to promote and grow your business.
