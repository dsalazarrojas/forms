# Marketing Mix (4Ps) Survey Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketing Mix (4Ps) Survey Questionnaire is designed to gather information about your preferences and behavior when it comes to a product. This form will ask questions about your demographic information, product satisfaction, price perception, and visit frequency to help us understand how to improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best describes how often you visit the store.
2. Choose the status of your relationship with this brand.
3. Select your age and income level.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the product using the date you last visited or purchased it.
5. Choose your gender.
6. Enter your estimated delivery time for the product.
7. Rate the price of the product using a number scale.
8. Choose your occupation.
9. Choose your education level.
10. Select your marital status.
11. Enter a brief description of how frequently you visit the store.
12. Enter your estimated income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you visit this store**: This field asks how often you visit the store. Please select the option that best describes your visit frequency. For example, if you visit the store once a week, select "Store A" as your answer.
* **What is your relationship status with this brand?**: This field asks about your relationship status with the brand. Please select one of the options provided.
* **How satisfied are you with this product?**: This field asks about your satisfaction with the product. Please select a date when you last visited or purchased the product.
* **What is your age?**: This field asks for your age.
* **How do you rate the price of this product?**: This field asks you to rate the price of the product using a number scale.
* **How long do you think it takes to get your product?**: This field asks about your estimated delivery time for the product.
* **What is your gender?**: This field asks about your gender.
* **How do you rate the service of this store?**: This field is a text field for you to describe your experience with the store's service.
* **What is your income?**: This field asks for your income.
* **What is your occupation?**: This field asks about your occupation.
* **What is your education level?**: This field asks about your education level.
* **What is your marital status?**: This field asks about your marital status.
* **Do you visit this store frequently?**: This field asks for a brief description of how frequently you visit the store.
