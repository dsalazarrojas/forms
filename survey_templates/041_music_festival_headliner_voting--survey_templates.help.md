# Music Festival Headliner Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Festival Headliner Voting form is designed to gather opinions from attendees on their favorite headliner and overall festival experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your favorite headliner from the options provided.
2. Rate your overall music festival experience by selecting one of the given options.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions for future music festivals, please include them in the space provided.
4. If you have any contact information, please provide your email address and/or phone number.
5. Let us know what interests you about the music festival, such as the headliners, food, or activities.
6. Finally, indicate how often you attend music festivals in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Headliner Voting Form** (`music_festival_headliner_voting_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you will select your favorite headliner from the options provided.
* **Who is your favorite headliner?** (`headliner_selection`, select_one, required): Please select the number of your favorite headliner. The options are: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
* **How would you rate the music festival experience?** (`music_festival_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall music festival experience from 1 to 5.
* **Who would you recommend as headliner?** (`headliner_recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a favorite headliner that you would like to see at future music festivals, please select them.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about future music festivals.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to help us contact you if we need more information.
* **What interests you about the music festival?** (`music_festival_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select what interests you about music festivals, such as headliners, food, or activities.
* **How often do you attend music festivals?** (`music_festival_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you attend music festivals, such as once a year or multiple times a year.
