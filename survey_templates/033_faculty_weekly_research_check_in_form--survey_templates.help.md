# Faculty Weekly Research Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from faculty members about their weekly research progress, allowing the research administrators to track and monitor the status of ongoing research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the relevant research area that aligns with your project.
4. Enter the title of your research project.
5. Choose the current research stage from the dropdown menu.
6. If any obstacles have been encountered during the research, describe them in the "Obstacles" field.
7. Select the department where your research is taking place.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
9. If necessary, select the date you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name here.
* **Research Area** (3): Select the area of research that best aligns with your project.
* **Research Project** (4): Enter the title of your research project.
* **Research Stage** (5): Choose the current stage of your research from the options provided.
* **Obstacles** (6): Describe any obstacles that have been encountered during your research.
* **Department** (7): Select the department where your research is taking place.
* **Comments** (8): Add any additional comments or feedback about your research.
* **Date Submitted** (9): Enter the date you are submitting this form (not required).

## Tips
- Please make sure to submit this form for the correct week and that you have not already submitted it for the current week.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the research administrators for clarification.
- This form is only required for faculty members with active research projects.
- Please be as concise as possible when filling out this form, but provide enough detail to help the research administrators track and monitor your research progress.
