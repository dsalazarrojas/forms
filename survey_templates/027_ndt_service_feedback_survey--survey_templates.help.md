# NDT Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) service provided. Please answer the questions to help us improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with as much information as possible.
2. Select the options that apply to your job from the drop-down menus.
3. Rate the technician's performance using the number scales.
4. Enter any comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Details** (Job Details, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe the job you've done, including the service provided and any other relevant details.
* **Service Provided** (Service Provided, select_one, required/optional): Please select the service provided from the options (NDT Service or Other Service). If the service is not listed, you can choose "Other Service" and provide a brief description in the Job Details field.
* **Date of Testing** (Date of Testing, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the testing.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the testing.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the testing.
* **Testing Duration** (Testing Duration, number, required/optional): Enter the total duration of the testing (in minutes).
* **Testing Type** (Testing Type, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of testing from the options (Mechanical, Electrical, Pneumatic).
* **Testing Area** (Testing Area, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the area where the testing was done from the options (Area 1, Area 2, Area 3).
* **Technician Name** (Technician Name, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the technician who performed the testing.
* **Technician Rating** (Technician Rating, number, required/optional): Rate the technician's performance using the number scale.
* **Technician Comment** (Technician Comment, text, required/optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the technician's performance.
* **Customer Rating** (Customer Rating, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service using the number scale.
* **Customer Comment** (Customer Comment, text, required/optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the service.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your overall satisfaction with the service from the options (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).

Please note that this form is not required to be completed in one session. You can save your progress and come back later to finish it.
