# observer_feedback_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from observers who attended an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form one page at a time.
2. On the "Feedback" page, answer the questions about what you enjoyed and didn't enjoy about the event. 
3. On the "Contact" page, provide any additional comments or information about yourself. 
4. On the "Additional Comments" page, add any other comments you'd like to share. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`observer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`observer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`observer_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **What did you enjoy the most about this event?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required): Describe what you enjoyed the most about the event.
* **What did you least enjoy about this event?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required): Describe what you enjoyed the least about the event.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any other comments or information you'd like to share.
