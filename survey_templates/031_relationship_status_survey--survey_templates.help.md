# Relationship Status Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your current relationship status and your partner's relationship status. Please provide accurate and honest answers to help us better understand your relationship circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current relationship status from the options provided on the first page.
2. If you have a partner, fill out their details on the second page. 
3. Answer the remaining questions about how you met, your partner's relationship status, and how long you've been in your current relationship. 
4. Indicate your relationship satisfaction level and your partner's relationship satisfaction level (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Your Current Relationship Status** (Required): Choose your current relationship status from the options Active or Inactive.
* **Your Partner's Name** (Optional): If you have a partner, please enter their name.
* **Your Partner's Age** (Optional): If you have a partner, please enter their age.
* **Your Partner's Occupation** (Optional): If you have a partner, please enter their occupation.
* **How Did You Meet** (Optional): Select Yes or No to indicate whether you met your partner through mutual friends, online, etc.
* **Your Partner's Current Relationship Status** (Required): Choose your partner's current relationship status from the options Active or Inactive.
* **How Long Have You Been In Your Current Relationship** (Optional): If you have been in a relationship, please select the date you've been in the relationship.
* **Relationship Satisfaction Level** (Optional): Select your relationship satisfaction level, whether you're Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Your Partner's Relationship Satisfaction Level** (Optional): If you have a partner, select their relationship satisfaction level, Satisfied or Dissatisfied. 

## Tips
Please answer all required fields truthfully. If you are unsure about any question, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
