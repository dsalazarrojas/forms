# Program Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Evaluation Checklist is a tool used by program administrators and evaluators to gather feedback and evaluate the effectiveness and engagement level of a program. It helps to identify areas of improvement and inform decisions about program adjustments and enhancements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and respond to each question honestly.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly, but do not feel obligated to answer every question.
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best reflects your opinion or experience.
4. For note questions, provide a brief but detailed response.
5. For number questions, enter a numerical value as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Evaluation Checklist** (`program_evaluation_checklist_form_section_1`, text, not required): This is the introduction section of the form, providing an overview of the program's purpose and goals.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, select_one, not required): This section asks for your feedback about the program's effectiveness and relevance from a participant's perspective.
* **Stakeholder Feedback** (`stakeholder_feedback`, select_one, not required): This section asks for feedback from a stakeholder's perspective, providing insight into the program's overall effectiveness and relevance.
* **Program Evaluation Checklist - Program Effectiveness** (`program_evaluation_checklist_program_effectiveness`, select_one, not required): This question assesses the overall effectiveness of the program.
* **Program Evaluation Checklist - Participant Engagement** (`program_evaluation_checklist_participant_engagement`, select_multiple, not required): This section asks for feedback on the level of engagement from participants, with options for High, Medium, and Low.
* **Program Evaluation Checklist - Stakeholder Engagement** (`program_evaluation_checklist_stakeholder_engagement`, select_one, not required): This question assesses the level of engagement from stakeholders.
* **Program Evaluation Checklist - Recommendations** (`program_evaluation_checklist_recommendations`, note, not required): This is a note section where you can provide suggestions and recommendations for program improvement.
* **Program Evaluation Checklist - Evaluation Frequency** (`program_evaluation_checklist_evaluation_frequency`, number, not required): This question asks for the frequency of evaluations and feedback provided.
