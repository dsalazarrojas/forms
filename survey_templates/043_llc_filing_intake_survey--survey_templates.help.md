# llc_filing_intake_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to the filing of a Limited Liability Company (LLC). The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently for the filing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Company Name and Phone Number of the LLC.
2. Provide the Registered Address and Principal Place of Business for the LLC.
3. Select the Principal Business Type (Single Member LLC, Multi Member LLC, Series LLC, or Series LLC with Series LLC Articles).
4. Enter the Number of Owners for the LLC.
5. Provide the Date of Inception and any additional notes regarding the Date of Inception.
6. Enter the Principal Place and Goals of Business for the LLC.
7. Enter information about the Authorized Person and their title.
8. Provide the Principal Business ID and Registered Agent information if applicable.
9. Enter the Registered Agent's name, title, address, phone number, and email address.
