# Animal Healing Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Animal Healing Frequency Survey" form is designed to gather information about your healing practices, experience, and work with animals. This survey aims to understand your frequency of conducting healing sessions, the types of animals you work with, and the benefits you commonly observe in your practice.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in your practitioner name and email address (if applicable).
3. Select your practitioner type, years of practice, and certification status.
4. Choose the animals you work with.
5. Select how often you conduct healing sessions, the average number of sessions per week, and the typical session duration.
6. Indicate the conditions you commonly address in your practice.
7. Choose how often animals typically receive treatment from you.
8. Select the average number of sessions an animal receives from you.
9. Rate the overall effectiveness of your practice.
10. Choose the benefits you commonly observe in your practice.
11. (Optional) Share any additional comments or success stories.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practitioner Name**: Your name as a healing practitioner.
* **Email Address** (optional): Your contact email for any follow-up questions or collaboration opportunities.
* **Practitioner Type**: Select the type of healing modality you practice (e.g., Reiki, Acupuncture, Massage therapy, etc.).
* **Years Practiced**: Choose how long you have been practicing healing (1-20+ years).
* **Certification Status**: Indicate your certification status (e.g., certified by a recognized body, trained but not certified, etc.).
* **Animals Worked With**: Select all the animals you commonly work with (e.g., dogs, cats, horses, etc.).
* **Session Frequency**: Choose how often you conduct healing sessions (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Average Sessions Per Week**: Select the average number of healing sessions you conduct per week.
* **Session Duration**: Select the typical length of a healing session (15-60 minutes or more).
* **Conditions Treated**: Choose the conditions you commonly address in your practice (e.g., pain management, anxiety, etc.).
* **Treatment Frequency**: Select how often animals receive treatment from you (e.g., single session, weekly, etc.).
* **Average Sessions Per Animal**: Select the average number of sessions an animal receives from you.
* **Overall Effectiveness**: Rate the overall effectiveness of your practice (e.g., very effective, effective, etc.).
* **Benefits Observed**: Select the benefits you commonly observe in your practice (e.g., reduced pain, improved behavior, etc.).
* **Client Feedback**: (Optional) Choose how you collect client feedback (e.g., verbal, written, etc.).
* **Veterinary Collaboration**: Indicate if you collaborate with veterinarians (e.g., regular, occasional, etc.).
* **Challenges Faced**: (Optional) Share any difficulties or challenges you face in your practice.
* **Success Stories**: (Optional) Share a success story or anecdote about your practice.
* **Research Interest**: (Optional) Indicate if you're interested in participating in research studies.
* **Additional Comments**: (Optional) Provide any additional information about your practice.
