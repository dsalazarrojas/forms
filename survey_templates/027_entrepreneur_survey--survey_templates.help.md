# entrepreneur_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from entrepreneurs about their businesses, including demographics, industry, and financials. The survey aims to gather data to help entrepreneurs and stakeholders better understand the business landscape and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information about your business.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the options provided for select_one and select_multiple questions to choose the most relevant response.
4. If you have additional information that doesn't fit within the provided options, use the "additional_info" field to provide more context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Entrepreneur Survey** (`introduction`): This is an introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): Provide the name of your business.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`): Enter the location of your business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`): Select the type of business you have (e.g., option 1, option 2, option 3).
* **Startup Stage** (`startup_stage`): Choose the stage of your startup (e.g., option 1, option 2).
* **Customer Base** (`customer_base`): Select all relevant customer bases that apply to your business (e.g., option 1, option 2, option 3).
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`): Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Funding** (`funding`): Enter the funding level of your business.
* **Industry** (`industry`): Provide a brief description of your industry.
* **Revenue** (`revenue`): Enter your business's revenue.
* **Growth Rate** (`growth_rate`): Enter your business's growth rate.
* **Website** (`website`): Provide the URL of your website.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`): Enter your social media presence (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn).
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`): Describe your marketing channels (e.g., email, social media, paid advertising).
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`): Describe your target audience.
* **Financials** (`financials`): Provide any additional financial information about your business.
* **Competition** (`competition`): Describe your competitors.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`): List any challenges your business faces.
* **Business Partners** (`business_partners`): List any business partners you have.
* **Exit Strategy** (`exit_strategy`): Describe your exit strategy (if applicable).
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`): Choose the stage of your business (e.g., option 1, option 2, option 3).
* **Business Model** (`business_model`): Describe your business model.
* **Market Segment** (`market_segment`): Describe your market segment.
* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`): Describe your customer segment.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Provide any additional information about your business.

## Tips
Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate and reliable results. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
