# Earpiece Usage Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earpiece Usage Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with earpieces. This form will help the company providing the earpiece to improve their products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Rate the earpiece by choosing a number between 1 and 5.
3. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the earpiece and the support provided.
4. Select the type of product you were using the earpiece with (headphones, earbuds, or other).
5. Choose how often you use the earpiece (rarely, daily, weekly, or monthly).
6. Select the type of device you were using the earpiece with (smartphone, tablet, desktop, or other).
7. Answer questions about your purchase decision, usage duration, and purchase experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Earpiece Usage Feedback Form** (`form_heading`, `text`, required/optional): A brief introduction to the form.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **How would you rate this earpiece?** (`rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the earpiece on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Common Issues** (`common_issues`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any issues with the earpiece, please describe them.
* **Does this form influence your product purchasing decision?** (`product_purchase_decision`, `select_one`, required/optional): Yes, no, or maybe.
* **How satisfied are you with this earpiece?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, or not satisfied.
* **How do you rate the support from the provider?** (`support`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the support from the provider on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Any additional comments about the earpiece.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Any additional comments about your experience with the earpiece.
* **What type of product were you using this earpiece with?** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Headphones, earbuds, or other.
* **What type of device were you using this earpiece with?** (`device_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Smartphone, tablet, desktop, or other.
* **How many earpieces do you have?** (`how_many_earpieces`, `number`, required/optional): The number of earpieces you have.
* **How much did you pay for each earpiece?** (`purchase_price`, `number`, required/optional): The price you paid for each earpiece.
* **Date of purchase** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date you purchased the earpiece.
* **How long have you used this earpiece?** (`duration_usage`, `time`, required/optional): The duration of your usage of the earpiece.
* **How often do you use this earpiece?** (`frequency_usage`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rarely, daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Where did you purchase this earpiece?** (`purchase_location`, `text`, required/optional): The location where you bought the earpiece.
* **Who provided this earpiece?** (`product_provider`, `text`, required/optional): The company that provided the earpiece.
* **What do you like most/least about this earpiece?** (`comments_on_earpiece`, `text`, required/optional): Your thoughts about the earpiece.
* **What do you like most/least about purchasing this earpiece?** (`comments_on_purchase`, `text`, required/optional): Your thoughts about the purchase process.
* **What do you like most/least about the support from the provider?** (`comments_on_support`, `text`, required/optional): Your thoughts about the support provided.
* **What do you like most/least about the purchase process?** (`comments_on_purchase_process`, `text`, required/optional): Your thoughts about the purchase process.
* **What do you like most/least about the return policy?** (`comments_on_return_policy`, `text`, required/optional): Your thoughts about the return policy.
* **What do you like most/least about shipping?** (`comments_on_shipping`, `text`, required/optional): Your thoughts about the shipping process.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have already submitted feedback for the same product or have already purchased the earpiece, you will be directed to a "Thank you for your feedback" page.
* If you have any issues with the earpiece, please describe them in the "Common Issues" field.
