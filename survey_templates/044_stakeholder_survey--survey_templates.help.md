# Stakeholder Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Stakeholder Survey is designed to gather information from key stakeholders about their experiences and perceptions related to the project. This form will help us understand their opinions, suggestions, and concerns, which will inform our decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Fill in the **Stakeholder Survey** text field to provide a brief introduction or description of the project or issue you are addressing.
3. For the **Improvement Areas** field, select all areas where you have suggestions or concerns. You can choose multiple options.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, if you wish to be contacted for further clarification or updates.
5. If you have a contact number, enter it in the **Contact Number** field. This is optional but recommended.
6. Finally, enter your **Date of Birth** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stakeholder Survey** (text, optional): This text field is where you can provide a brief introduction or description of the project or issue you are addressing. Please keep it concise and relevant.
* **Improvement Areas** (select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple areas where you have suggestions or concerns. Check all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further clarification or updates.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): If you have a contact number, enter it in this field. This is optional but recommended for ease of contact.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth if applicable. If not applicable, leave blank.

## Tips

- Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can in the **Stakeholder Survey** text field.
- For the **Improvement Areas** field, select all areas that apply to your concerns or suggestions.
- Ensure your email address is correctly formatted (e.g., [name]@[domain]).
- If you have any issues or concerns not addressed in this form, please provide additional information in the **Improvement Areas** field.
