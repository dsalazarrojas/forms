# Fundraising Event Success Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the success of a fundraising event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name** (1, text): Enter the name of the event.
*   **Event Date** (2, date): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Location** (3, text): Enter the location of the event.
*   **Event Organizer** (4, text): Enter the name of the event organizer.
*   **Event Type** (5, select_one): Select the type of event (Event, Fundraiser, or Campaign).
*   **Team Member** (6, text): Enter your team member's name.
*   **Contact Info** (7, text): Enter your contact information.
*   **Event Satisfaction** (8, select_multiple): Choose how satisfied you were with the event (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Unsatisfied, or Very Unsatisfied).
*   **Event Improvement** (9, text): Enter areas for improvement.
*   **Team Improvement** (10, text): Enter areas for team improvement.
*   **General Improvement** (11, text): Enter general improvement.
*   **Event Lessons Learned** (12, text): Enter lessons learned.
*   **Event Comments** (13, text): Enter event comments.
*   **Event Recommendation** (14, text): Enter recommendations for future events.
*   **Follow-up** (15, select_one): Determine if follow-up is required (True or False).
*   **Follow-up Info** (16, text): Enter follow-up information.
*   **Follow-up Method** (17, text): Enter follow-up method.
*   **Follow-up Frequency** (18, text): Enter follow-up frequency.
*   **Follow-up Timeframe** (19, text): Enter follow-up timeframe.
*   **Follow-up Comments** (20, text): Enter follow-up comments.
*   **Follow-up Recommendation** (21, text): Enter follow-up recommendation.
*   **Follow-up Lessons Learned** (22, text): Enter lessons learned from follow-up.
*   **Follow-up Improvement** (23, text): Enter follow-up improvement areas.
*   **Follow-up Next Action** (24, text): Enter follow-up next action.
*   **Follow-up Next Steps** (25, text): Enter follow-up next steps.
