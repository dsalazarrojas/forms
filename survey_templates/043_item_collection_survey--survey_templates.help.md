# item_collection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is used to collect item information, such as its name, category, description, price, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **item_name** field with the name of the item.
2. Select the **item_category** from the provided options.
3. Enter a brief description of the item in the **item_description** field.
4. Enter the price of the item in the **item_price** field.
5. Select the **item_status** from the provided options (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
6. Enter the reason for the **item_status** in the **item_status_reason** field.
7. Select the **item_image_url** from the provided options.
8. Enter the **item_weight** of the item.
9. Enter the **item_length**, **item_width**, and **item_height** dimensions of the item.
10. Enter the **item_depth** of the item.
11. Enter the **item_color** of the item.
12. Enter the **item_size** of the item.
13. Enter the **item_material** of the item.
14. Select the **item_weight_class** from the provided options (Class A, Class B, or Class C).
15. Enter the **item_type** of the item.
16. Enter the **item_brand** of the item.
17. Enter the **item_model** of the item.
18. Enter the **item_code** of the item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **item_name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the item.
* **item_category** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select the category of the item from the provided options.
* **item_description** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the item.
* **item_price** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the price of the item.
* **item_date** (Date, Required/Optional): This field is not used in this form.
* **item_time** (Time, Required/Optional): This field is not used in this form.
* **item_note** (Note, Required/Optional): This field is not used in this form.
* **email** (Email, Required/Optional): This field is not used in this form.
* **phone** (Text, Required/Optional): This field is not used in this form.
* **item_status** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select the status of the item from the provided options (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **item_status_reason** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the reason for the **item_status**.
* **item_image_url** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select the image URL of the item from the provided options.
* **item_weight** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the weight of the item.
* **item_length** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the length of the item.
* **item_width** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the width of the item.
* **item_height** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the height of the item.
* **item_depth** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the depth of the item.
* **item_color** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the color of the item.
* **item_size** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the size of the item.
* **item_material** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the material of the item.
* **item_weight_class** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select the weight class of the item from the provided options (Class A, Class B, or Class C).
* **item_type** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the type of the item.
* **item_brand** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the brand of the item.
* **item_model** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the model of the item.
* **item_code** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the code of the item.
