# Consultant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultant Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from clients who have worked with the consultancy. This form helps us identify areas of strength and improvement, ensuring we continue to deliver high-quality services that meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Organization Name, the name of the project, and the Primary Consultant(s) Involved.
2. Provide your overall satisfaction with the consulting delivery, including ratings for scope fulfillment, deliverable quality, and demonstration of subject matter expertise.
3. Share your experience with the consultant's communication performance, including responsiveness, clarity, and coordination.
4. Identify areas for improvement and suggest recommendations for future services.
5. Indicate your likelihood to hire the consultancy again and refer a colleague.
6. Add any final comments or concerns.
7. Select how you want your feedback used (e.g., as a testimonial, anonymously, or internally).
8. Date the form when you complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **Consulting Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you worked on with the consultancy.
* **Primary Consultant(s) Involved**: Enter the name(s) of the consultant(s) working on your project.
* **Project Completion Date**: Enter the date the project was completed.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Consulting Delivery (1-10)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the consultancy's services.
* **To what extent did we fulfill the original project scope? (1-5)**: Rate how well the consultancy met your project scope.
* **Satisfaction with the Quality of Final Deliverables (1-5)**: Rate your satisfaction with the quality of the deliverables.
* **Demonstration of Subject Matter Expertise (1-5)**: Rate how well the consultant demonstrated their expertise.
* **Responsiveness and Promptness of Communication (1-5)**: Rate how responsive and prompt the consultant was in communication.
* **Clarity and Professionalism of Advice/Guidance (1-5)**: Rate how clear and professional the consultant's advice was.
* **Effectiveness in Coordinating with Client Team (1-5)**: Rate how well the consultant coordinated with your team.
* **What was the strongest aspect of our consulting engagement?**: Share the strongest part of your experience with the consultancy.
* **One area where we could improve our consulting services**: Suggest areas where the consultancy can improve.
* **How likely are you to hire our consultancy again? (1-5)**: Rate your likelihood to hire the consultancy again.
* **How likely are you to refer us to a professional colleague? (1-5)**: Rate your likelihood to refer the consultancy to a colleague.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any final comments or concerns.
* **May we use your feedback as a professional testimonial?**: Select how you want your feedback used.
* **Date of Feedback Completion**: Date the form when you complete it.
* **Your Name and Title**: Enter your name and title.
