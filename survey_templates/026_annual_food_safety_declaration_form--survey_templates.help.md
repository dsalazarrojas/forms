This form is designed to help food businesses declare their food safety measures and procedures. The purpose of the form is to gather information about a business's ability to maintain a safe food environment. This includes data about the business's name, contact information, staff training, food safety certification, and other relevant measures to ensure food safety. 

The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of food safety. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately.

### Annual Food Safety Declaration Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The form is used to collect information about a food business's food safety measures and procedures.

#### How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your business's name, contact person's name, job title, email address, and contact phone number.
2. Select the current year for which you are making the declaration.
3. Choose the type of food business you operate (e.g., restaurant, cafe, food truck, etc.).
4. Enter the total number of employees handling food.
5. Confirm if you have current food safety certification.
6. If yes, select the type of certification you hold.
7. Indicate the percentage of staff trained in food safety.
8. Confirm if you have a HACCP plan in place.
9. Confirm if you monitor food temperatures regularly, occasionally, or not at all.
10. Indicate if you have comprehensive allergen management procedures.
11. Confirm if you verify all or some of your suppliers.
12. Confirm if you have a pest control program in place.
13. Enter the date of your last health inspection (if applicable).
14. Select the result of your last health inspection (if applicable).
15. Confirm if any violations were corrected.
16. Indicate if you have had any foodborne illness incidents this year.
17. If yes, describe the details of the incidents.
18. Confirm if you have documented recall procedures.
19. Confirm if you make a declaration that the information provided is accurate.
20. Finally, sign and date the declaration.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (1)**: Enter your business's official name as it appears on your business cards or licenses. 
* **Contact Person Name (2)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for food safety within your business. 
* **Position Title (3)**: Enter the job title of the contact person. 
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter the contact email address of your business. 
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter the phone number where your business can be reached. 
* **Business Address (6)**: Enter the full address of your food facility. 
* **Declaration Year (7)**: Select the year for which you are making the declaration. 
* **Business Type (8)**: Choose the type of food business you operate (e.g., restaurant, cafe, food truck, etc.). 
* **Employee Count (9)**: Enter the total number of employees handling food. 
* **Food Safety Certification (10)**: Confirm if you have current food safety certification. 
* **Certification Type (11)**: If yes, select the type of certification you hold. 
* **Staff Trained (12)**: Indicate the percentage of staff trained in food safety. 
* **HACCP Plan (13)**: Confirm if you have a HACCP plan in place. 
* **Temperature Monitoring (14)**: Confirm if you monitor food temperatures regularly, occasionally, or not at all. 
* **Allergen Management (15)**: Indicate if you have comprehensive allergen management procedures. 
* **Supplier Verification (16)**: Confirm if you verify all or some of your suppliers. 
* **Pest Control (17)**: Confirm if you have a pest control program in place. 
* **Last Inspection Date (18)**: Enter the date of your last health inspection (if applicable). 
* **Last Inspection Result (19)**: Select the result of your last health inspection (if applicable). 
* **Violations Corrected (20)**: Confirm if any violations were corrected. 
* **Foodborne Illness Incidents (21)**: Indicate if you have had any foodborne illness incidents this year. 
* **Incident Details (22)**: If yes, describe the details of the incidents. 
* **Recall Procedures (23)**: Confirm if you have documented recall procedures. 
* **Declaration Statement (24)**: Confirm if you make a declaration that the information provided is accurate. 
* **Signature Date (25)**: Sign and date the declaration. 
* **Additional Comments (26)**: Enter any other information you would like to include. 

Note: This form is an essential part of maintaining a safe food environment. By completing this form, you are declaring that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
