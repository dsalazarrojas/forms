# Development And Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is a self-reporting tool for team members to share their preferences and habits related to collaboration, communication, and feedback. Your input will help us better understand what works best for you and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
* Fill out the survey honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* Please select all the options that apply to your situation.
* If there are additional options or answers not listed, please use the provided "Other" field to provide more information.
