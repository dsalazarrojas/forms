# Service Rate Card Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Rate Card Evaluation form is used to gather feedback from clients on their experience with the service and provide an overall rating of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and make sure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Complete each field according to your experience with the service.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Rate Card Evaluation Page 1** (`service_rate_card_evaluation_page_1`, note, optional): This page is an introduction to the form and provides a brief overview of the service.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, optional): This field asks for your feedback on your experience with the service.
* **Service Rate Card** (`service_rate_card`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to choose one of the following options for the service's overall quality:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Service Rate Card 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6** (`service_rate_card_1`, `service_rate_card_2`, `service_rate_card_3`, `service_rate_card_4`, `service_rate_card_5`, `service_rate_card_6`, select_multiple, optional): These fields ask you to choose one or more of the following options for specific aspects of the service:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): This field asks you to enter a numerical rating for the service (e.g. 1-5).

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the client feedback field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the service team.
