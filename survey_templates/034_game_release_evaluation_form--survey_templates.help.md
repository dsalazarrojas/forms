# Game Release Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a game release. It will help us understand how users interact with the game, what they like or dislike, and what they think about the game's features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as accurately as possible.
2. For select fields, choose one option that best represents your experience or preference.
3. You can submit feedback about any game release only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Version**: Enter the version of the game you played.
	+ This field is used to track changes between different game releases.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the game on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the worst and 5 is the best.
	+ This field helps us understand how much you enjoyed or disliked the game.
* **Rating**: Rate the game on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the worst and 5 is the best.
	+ This field helps us understand how much you like or dislike the game.
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or suggestions about the game.
	+ This field is an open space for you to share your thoughts about the game.
* **Game Type**: Choose the type of game you played (e.g., Game, Sports, Puzzle, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand the type of game you interacted with.
* **Platform**: Choose the platform where you played the game (e.g., PC, Console, Mobile, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand where you played the game.
* **How Much Time Did You Play?**: Enter the amount of time you spent playing the game.
	+ This field helps us understand how long you played the game.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
	+ This field is for your contact information, but feel free to fill it out only if you want.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
	+ This field is for your contact information, but feel free to fill it out only if you want.
* **What Are Your Next Steps with This Game?**: Describe your next steps with the game (optional).
	+ This field is an open space for you to share your plans or intentions.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the game (optional).
	+ This field is an open space for you to share your thoughts about the game.
* **Player Type**: Choose your player type (e.g., Casual, Hardcore, Beginner, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand your level of experience or skill.
* **How Long Have You Been Playing?**: Enter the number of years you've been playing games (optional).
	+ This field helps us understand how long you've been playing games.
* **How Often Do You Play?**: Enter how often you play games (on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is rarely and 5 is very often).
	+ This field helps us understand how frequently you play games.
* **How Long Do You Play?**: Enter the duration of your gaming session (optional).
	+ This field helps us understand how long you play games.
* **Favorite Game Genre**: Choose your favorite game genre (e.g., Action/Adventure, Strategy, Puzzle, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand what type of games you like.
* **Least Favorite Game Genre**: Choose your least favorite game genre (e.g., Action/Adventure, Strategy, Puzzle, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand what type of games you dislike.
* **Favorite Game Platform**: Choose your favorite game platform (e.g., PC, Console, Mobile, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand where you prefer to play games.
* **Least Favorite Game Platform**: Choose your least favorite game platform (e.g., PC, Console, Mobile, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand where you dislike playing games.
* **Player Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the game on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
	+ This field helps us understand how satisfied you are with the game.
* **Why Dissatisfied**: Choose the reason why you were dissatisfied with the game (e.g., Gameplay, Story, Graphics, etc.).
	+ This field helps us understand what went wrong with the game.
* **Game Rating 2**: Rate the game on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the worst and 5 is the best.
	+ This field is similar to the **Rating** field, but with an additional rating.
* **How Often Do You Play?**: Enter how often you play games on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is rarely and 5 is very often.
	+ This field is similar to the **How Often Do You Play?** field, but with an additional rating.
* **Favorite Game Genre 2**: Choose your favorite game genre (e.g., Game, Sports, Puzzle, etc.).
	+ This field is similar to the **Favorite Game Genre** field, but with an additional rating.
* **Game Type 2**: Choose the type of game you played (e.g., Game, Sports, Puzzle, etc.).
	+ This field is similar to the **Game Type** field, but with an additional rating.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and honestly.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the **Additional Comments** field.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
