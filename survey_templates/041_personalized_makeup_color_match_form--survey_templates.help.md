# Personalized Makeup Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to match a user's makeup color with their personal characteristics, providing tailored suggestions for makeup products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your skin tone from the options provided.
2. Choose your face shape, considering the shape of your face.
3. Select your hair color.
4. Choose your eye color.
5. Select your complexion, considering your skin undertones.
6. Pick your lip shape.
7. Choose your skin type.
8. Select your preferred makeup color.
9. (Optional) If you have a second skin tone, select it.
10. (Optional) If you have a second face shape, select it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skin Tone** (`skin_tone`, select_one, required): Select the best description of your skin tone from the options provided.
* **Face Shape** (`face_shape`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the face shape that best describes you. This will help the system understand your facial structure.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select your natural hair color, even if you dye or color it.
* **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color of your eyes.
* **Complexion** (`complexion`, select_one, optional): Choose the best description of your skin undertones (fair, medium, or dark).
* **Lip Shape** (`lip_shape`, select_multiple, optional): Pick the shape of your lips.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your skin type (dry, oily, or combination).
* **Preferred Makeup Color** (`preferred_makeup_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select your favorite makeup color or color palette.
* **Skin Tone 2** (`skin_tone_2`, select_one, optional): If you have a second skin tone, select it.
* **Face Shape 2** (`face_shape_2`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a second face shape, select it.
