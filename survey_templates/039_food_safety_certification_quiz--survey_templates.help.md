# Food Safety Certification Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an individual's or an organization's knowledge and adherence to food safety best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions according to the instructions provided. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food Safety Practices** (Required): This field is for you to describe your food safety practices and procedures in the workplace.
- **Food Safety Certification** (Required): Please select either "Certified" or "Non-Certified" to indicate your current certification status.
- **Food Safety Training** (Optional): This field is for you to describe any additional training you have received on food safety.
- **Food Safety Procedures** (Required): Select the best practices you follow for food safety procedures.
- **Food Safety Policies** (Optional): This field is for you to describe your food safety policies and procedures in the workplace.
- **Food Safety Standards** (Required): Enter the current time you have spent on food safety training.
- **Food Safety Certification Process** (Required): Please select either "Manual" or "Automated" to indicate how your certification was obtained.
- **Food Safety Certification Level** (Required): Select the level of your certification (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
- **Date** (Required): Enter the date of your last food safety certification.
- **Time** (Required): Enter the time you spent on your last food safety certification.
- **Email** (Required): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
- **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
- **Emergency Contact** (Required): Select whether you have an emergency contact person or not.
- **Certification Validity** (Optional): Select whether your certification is valid or not.
- **Certification Status** (Required): Select whether your certification is Active or Inactive.
- **Additional Comments** (Optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information.
- **Certification Expiration** (Required): Enter the date of your certification expiration.
- **Certification Status Reason** (Optional): This field is for you to describe why your certification status is Incomplete.
- **Certification Renewal** (Optional): Select whether you are due for certification renewal.
- **Renewal Date** (Optional): If you selected "True" for certification renewal, enter the date when you are due to renew.
- **Certification Level** (Required): Select the level of your certification (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
- **Training Hours** (Required): Enter the number of hours you have completed for your food safety training.
- **Training Location** (Required): Select where you received your food safety training (Online or In-person).
- **Emergency Contact Name** (Required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (Required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Note** (Optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your emergency contact person.
