# Pool Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users after a visit to a pool, providing a snapshot of their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the optional fields (comments, additional comments, and suggestions), you can provide any information you think is relevant or helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Safety** (`pool_safety_rating`, number, **required**): Rate your experience with the pool's safety features, such as lifeguards, emergency equipment, and safety guidelines.
* **Staff Service** (`staff_service_rating`, number, **required**): Evaluate the friendliness and helpfulness of the pool staff, including their responses to your questions and concerns.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, **required**): Assess the overall cleanliness of the pool area, including the condition of the facilities, equipment, and restrooms.
* **Facilities** (`facilities_rating`, number, **required**): Rate the quality and availability of pool facilities, such as pool amenities, changing rooms, and locker rooms.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, **required**): Provide a general rating of your experience at the pool, taking into account all aspects of your visit.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, number, **required**): Rate the friendliness and welcoming attitude of the pool staff, including their approachability and willingness to help.
* **Facilities Quality** (`facilities_quality`, number, **required**): Evaluate the overall quality and comfort of the pool facilities, including amenities and amenities availability.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, number, **required**): Determine whether you believe the pool offers good value for money, considering your experience and expectations.
* **Will Revisit** (`will_revisit`, number, **required**): Indicate whether you would consider visiting the pool again based on your current experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or suggestions that you think might be helpful for the pool management.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Offer any other information that you think is relevant to your experience or feedback.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, **optional**): Suggest any improvements or changes that you think would enhance the pool experience.
