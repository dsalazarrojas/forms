# Research Survey On Thesis Topics - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from researchers about their preferences and interests related to thesis topics. It is intended to collect data that will be used for internal research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form and ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2.  For fields with multiple options (e.g., "Thesis Topics", "institution"), select all options that apply to you.
3.  Complete each field according to your preference or attitude towards the topic.
4.  If you have any comments or additional information about the topics or institutions, use the "comments" field.
5.  Once you've completed all fields, review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Thesis Topics** (`Thesis Topics`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about your attitudes towards certain topics related to thesis research. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **preferred_educational_institution** (`preferred_educational_institution`, select_one, required: false): This field is asking about your preferred educational institution. Select the institution that best suits your preferences.
* **comments** (`research_survey_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any comments or additional information about the topics or institutions, use this field to provide them.
* **thesis_preferences** (`research_survey_on_thesis_topics_2`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is asking about your preferences related to thesis topics. Select all options that apply to you.
* **researcher_id** (`researcher_id`, select_one, required: false): This field is asking about your researcher ID. Select "Yes" if you have a researcher ID.
* **preferred_institution** (`preferred_institution`, select_one, required: false): This field is asking about your preferred institution. Select the institution that best suits your preferences.
* **institution** (`research_survey_on_thesis_topics_3`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is asking about your institution. Select all options that apply to you.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and honest when providing your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your research support team.
* This form is designed to collect data for internal research purposes only.
