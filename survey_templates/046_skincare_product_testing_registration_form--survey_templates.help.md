# Skincare Product Testing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your preferences, experiences, and interests related to skincare products. The data collected will help us understand what you like and dislike about skincare products, which can inform product development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your registration form with your contact information (email and phone number) if you haven't already done so.
2. Answer the multiple-choice questions about your skincare product testing preferences, experiences, and interests. Select all that apply.
3. Fill in your date of birth and other demographic information.
4. Provide feedback about your skincare product testing preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): Fill in your name and any other relevant details if you want to register.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your email address and phone number if you want to get in touch with us.
* **Skincare Product Testing Preferences** (`skincare_product_testing_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your skincare product testing preferences.
	+ If "Yes" is selected, you have experience or are interested in testing skincare products.
* **Testing Experience** (`testing_experience`, text, optional): Fill in any details about your testing experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select one, optional): Select how you would like to provide feedback (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Fill in your date of birth.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select one, optional): Select your skin type (dry, oily, or combination).
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your skin concerns.
* **Skincare Products** (`skincare_products`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your interest in skincare products.
* **Skincare Product Testing Frequency** (`skincare_product_testing_frequency`, text, optional): Fill in how often you test skincare products.
* **Skin Sensitivities** (`skin_sensitivities`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your skin sensitivities.
* **Product Testing Preferences** (`product_testing_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your product testing preferences.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, select one, optional): Select whether you have completed the survey (completed, pending, or cancelled).
* **Skincare Product Testing Interests** (`skincare_product_testing_interests`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your interests in skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing History** (`skincare_product_testing_history`, select one, optional): Select whether you have testing history (Yes or No).
* **Skincare Product Testing Pain Points** (`skincare_product_testing_pain_points`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your pain points related to skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing Liking** (`skincare_product_testing_liking`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your liking scale for skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing Liking Scale** (`skincare_product_testing_liking_scale`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your liking scale for skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing Pain Points Reasons** (`skincare_product_testing_pain_points_reasons`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your reasons for pain points related to skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing Pain Points Frequency** (`skincare_product_testing_pain_points_frequency`, text, optional): Fill in how often you experience pain points related to skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing Pain Points Severity** (`skincare_product_testing_pain_points_severity`, text, optional): Fill in the severity of your pain points related to skincare product testing.
* **Skincare Product Testing Liking Reasons** (`skincare_product_testing_liking_reasons`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your reasons for liking related to skincare product testing.

## Tips

* Select all that apply for select multiple fields.
* Enter accurate information for demographic fields.
* Select the correct options for your preferences and experiences.
* If you have any feedback, provide it in the text field.
* If you have any pain points, please describe them in the corresponding text field.
