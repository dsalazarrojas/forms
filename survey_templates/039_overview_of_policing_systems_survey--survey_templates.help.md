# Overview Of Policing Systems Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on various policing systems and their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate answer based on your personal experience or knowledge.
2. Select "Yes" for answers that indicate a positive response to the question.
3. Select "No" for answers that indicate a negative response to the question.
4. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
5. Answer all questions, even if you're unsure or don't have an opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Region specific policing models** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about your familiarity with or experience of region-specific policing models, i.e., policing approaches that are tailored to a particular region or community.
- **Public trust** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about whether the public has trust in the policing system in your region or community.
- **Reform priorities** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about whether reform priorities are being met in your region or community.
- **Policing satisfaction** (Number):
    * This question is asking you to rate your satisfaction with the policing system in your region or community on a scale. For example, 1-10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
- **Reform policing satisfaction** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about your satisfaction with the reform of the policing system in your region or community.
- **Public engagement** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about public engagement in the policing system in your region or community, such as whether people are actively participating in policing decisions or events.
- **Trust in policing** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about whether you trust the police in your region or community.
- **Police satisfaction** (Select Multiple):
    * This question is asking about your satisfaction with the police in your region or community.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective in your answers.
* Answer based on your personal experience and knowledge.
* If you're unsure or don't have an opinion, it's okay to mark "No" or select an option that reflects that.
* If you have feedback or suggestions, feel free to include them in the space provided at the end of the form.
