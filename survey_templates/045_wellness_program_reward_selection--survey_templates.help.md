# Wellness Program Reward Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you select a reward for your participation in a wellness program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by selecting one or more options from the "Select Reward" page.
- On the "Delivery Details" page, please provide the date for when you would like to receive your reward.
- On the "Participant Feedback" page, provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select Reward** (`reward_selection`, select_multiple): Choose one or more of the available rewards. You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) and clicking on each option.
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, date): Enter the date when you would like to receive your reward. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the program.

## Tips
- Try to provide as much information as possible for each field, but you can skip any fields you are unsure about.
- Make sure to select one or more rewards that you are actually interested in, as this will ensure you receive a reward that you will enjoy.
- If you have any issues with the form or would like to ask questions, contact your wellness program administrator.
