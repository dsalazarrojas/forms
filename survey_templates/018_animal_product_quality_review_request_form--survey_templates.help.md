# Animal Product Quality Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from the requester for the review of an animal product. The form helps in ensuring that all necessary details are collected to conduct a thorough review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Your name and the name of your organization.
2. The name of the animal product to be reviewed.
3. The reason for the review request (e.g., Quality Check, Customer Complaint, Regulatory Compliance).
4. The key performance indicators (KPIs) for the product quality review (e.g., Nutritional Value, Purity).
5. Describe the sampling methodology used to collect the samples.
6. Select a desired completion date for the review.
7. Indicate whether technical specifications have been provided.
8. Provide additional technical review notes (optional).
9. Enter the email address for receiving the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the person requesting the review.
* **Organization or Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the organization or company making the review request.
* **Product Name to be Reviewed** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the product to be reviewed.
* **Reason for Review Request** (Select One, Required): Select one of the provided options to specify the reason for the review request:
	+ Routine Quality Check
	+ Investigation of Customer Complaint
	+ Regulatory Compliance Requirement
	+ Evaluation of New Supplier
	+ Product Re-certification
* **Key Performance Indicators to be Tested** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all that apply from the list of provided options:
	+ Nutritional Value and Composition
	+ Purity and Absence of Contaminants
	+ Shelf Life and Stability
	+ Palatability and Acceptance
	+ Structural Integrity
* **Sampling Method Employed** (Text, Required): Describe the method used to collect the samples.
* **Desired Review Completion Date** (Date, Required): Select a date by which you would like the review to be completed.
* **Have Technical Specifications Been Provided?** (Select One, Required): Indicate whether technical specifications have been provided:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Included in this form
* **Additional Technical Review Notes** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information or notes for the review.
* **Report Delivery Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address to receive the report.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the selected options for the reason for review request and key performance indicators.
* Be specific when describing the sampling methodology.
* Ensure the desired completion date is realistic and achievable.
* If technical specifications are not provided, please indicate so.
* Use a valid email address for report delivery.
