# Ethanol Effects Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ethanol Effects Assessment form is a survey aimed at understanding the effects of ethanol on users. The form collects information on various aspects of ethanol usage, including frequency and amount used, to help analyze and assess its effects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out the form completely and accurately, following the guidelines below for each field.
2. Answer each question truthfully, as your responses will contribute to a better understanding of ethanol's effects.
3. Review and submit your responses to finalize the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Response** (`user_response`, text, required: false): This field is an open-ended text box for any additional comments or responses you may have regarding the use of ethanol. You can use this field to share any thoughts or experiences you believe may be relevant to the assessment.
* **Frequency Used** (`frequency_used`, text, required: false): Enter your response regarding the frequency of your ethanol usage. For example, you may type "every day" or "rarely."
* 
* **Frequency** (`frequency_used_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the options below that best describe your frequency of using ethanol:
 + Yes
* **Never** (`never`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have never used ethanol or no longer use it. 
* **Less than once a week** (`less_than_once_a_week`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you use ethanol less than once a week.
* **About once a week** (`about_once_a_week`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you use ethanol about once a week.
* **Almost every day** (`almost_every_day`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you use ethanol almost every day.
* **How much used** (`how_much_used`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of ethanol you've used, for example, 1, 2, or 5 units.
* **Units used** (`units_used`, text, required: false): Enter any additional units you've used with ethanol, for instance, "pounds" or "ounces."
* **Amount moderately used** (`amount_moderately_used`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Good' if you've used ethanol moderately.
* **Amount frequently used** (`amount_frequently_used`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have used ethanol frequently.
* **Amount very frequently used** (`amount_very_frequently_used`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you use ethanol very frequently.
* **Other chemicals** (`other_chemicals`, text, required: false): Provide any other chemicals that you have also used in addition to ethanol, with a brief explanation.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Use the provided options for select fields, but feel free to add any additional comments or explanations in the `User Response` field.
* If you have used other chemicals in addition to ethanol, provide a clear and concise description of their use.
* Review and submit your responses carefully to ensure they accurately represent your experiences with ethanol.
