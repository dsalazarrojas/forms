# Landing Page Review Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Landing Page Review Checklist" form is used to assess a landing page's effectiveness in meeting specific criteria. This form guides users through a series of questions to evaluate the title, headings, and call-to-action on the landing page, helping them identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select all the applicable options for each field by ticking the boxes next to them.
- Ensure you complete all fields that are marked as required.
- You can skip fields that are not marked as required but are optional.
- For select fields, choose an option from the list provided.
- For text fields, enter your response in the designated space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **landing_page_review_checklist_1** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the landing page's title is clear and concise, or "No" otherwise.
* **is_the_title_clear** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the title is clear and concise or why it's not.
* **is_the_title_focused** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the title is focused on the main topic or why it's not.
* **is_the_headline_effective** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the headline is effective in grabbing the user's attention or why it's not.
* **is_the_headline_compelling** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the headline is compelling in persuading the user to take action or why it's not.
* **is_the_subheading_focused** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the subheading is focused on the main topic or why it's not.
* **is_the_description_effective** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the description is effective in explaining the offer or why it's not.
* **is_the_description_compelling** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of why the description is compelling in persuading the user to take action or why it's not.
* **is_the_call_to_action_effective** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the call-to-action is clear and direct, or "No" otherwise.
* **is_the_cta_compelling** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the call-to-action is compelling and persuasive, or "No" otherwise.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- Use concrete examples from the landing page to support your evaluations.
- Focus on providing actionable feedback that can be used to improve the landing page's effectiveness.
