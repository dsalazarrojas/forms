# custom_product_sampling_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "custom_product_sampling_request_form" is used to collect information from business contacts for custom product sampling requests. This form helps our team gather necessary details to fulfill the requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter contact information**: Fill in the required fields (business contact, contact email, and business phone).
2. Provide additional contact information (product name, product description, quantity requested, and shipping details) as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Contact**: Enter the contact name, company name, or representative's name who is responsible for the request. This helps us identify the person making the request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can communicate with the requestor. This is used for internal purposes to discuss the request.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are requesting, including any relevant product code or part number. Be as specific as possible to ensure we can fulfill the request correctly.
* **Product Description**: Provide a brief description of the product you are requesting. This helps our team understand the product's features and requirements.
* **Quantity Requested**: Enter the number of units you are requesting. If this is a one-time request, you can simply enter 1.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where the product is to be shipped, including the city and state.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city name where the product is to be shipped.
* **Shipping ZIP**: Enter the ZIP code where the product is to be shipped.
* **Product Request Message**: This optional field is for any additional information or comments related to your request. Feel free to enter any specifics you'd like to ensure we can fulfill your request correctly.
* **Business Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you for further communication. This is optional but can be helpful for us to reach out with updates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling this form for a valid reason and not duplicating a request already made.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us at [contact email or phone number].
