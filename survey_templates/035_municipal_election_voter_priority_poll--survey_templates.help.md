# municipal_election_voter_priority_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and priorities from voters in a municipal election. It is meant to understand the concerns and sentiments of residents in the context of the election.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide honest answers to the questions.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. You can save your progress and come back later to complete the form, if needed.
4. Once you've finished, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Priorities** (`resident_priorities`, `text`, optional): This field is meant for you to express your top priorities as a resident. Be as specific as possible about what is most important to you.
* **Resident Feedback** (`resident_feedback`, `text`, optional): Here, you can share any feedback you have about your experience as a resident. This can be positive or negative comments, suggestions, or observations.
* **Voter Sentiment** (`voter_sentiment`, `text`, optional): Describe your overall sentiment towards the municipal election. Are you satisfied, indifferent, or concerned about the current state of your community?
* **Resident Parks Priority** (`resident_parks`, `text`, optional): Rank the importance of parks and green spaces in your community. If parks are a priority, please specify why.
* **Resident Community** (`resident_community`, `text`, optional): Share any thoughts about what you believe is essential for a strong sense of community in your area.
* **Voter Demographics** (`voter_demographics`, `text`, optional): If you're comfortable providing demographic information, please do so to help us better understand our audience.
* **Resident Education** (`resident_education`, `text`, optional): Suggest any educational initiatives or programs you believe would benefit the community.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific in your answers.
* If unsure about any field, try to provide the best-effort answer you can.
* You can ask for help if you need clarification on any field or question.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide valuable feedback!
