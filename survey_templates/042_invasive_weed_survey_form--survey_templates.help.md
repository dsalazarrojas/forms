This form is designed to collect information about invasive weed sightings. The purpose of this form is to gather data about the locations, species, and status of invasive weeds. The form is intended for surveyors to report their findings in a structured and consistent manner. By filling out this form, surveyors can help us better understand the distribution and characteristics of invasive weeds in the area.

## Purpose

The Invasive Weed Survey Form is used to collect data about invasive weed sightings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Survey Location**: Enter the location where the invasive weed was spotted. This could be a specific spot, a general area, or an entire region.
2.  **Sighting Date**: Enter the date when the invasive weed was spotted.
3.  **Sighting Time**: Enter the time when the invasive weed was spotted.
4.  **Species ID**: Enter the ID of the invasive weed species. If you're unsure, you can refer to the species list provided.
5.  **Common Name**: Enter the common name of the invasive weed species.
6.  **Scientific Name**: Enter the scientific name of the invasive weed species.
7.  **Sighting Latitude**: Enter the latitude of the invasive weed sighting location.
8.  **Sighting Longitude**: Enter the longitude of the invasive weed sighting location.
9.  **Sighting Elevation**: Enter the elevation of the invasive weed sighting location.
10. **Sighting Description**: Provide a description of the invasive weed sighting, including any notable features or observations.
11. **Photo or Drawing**: You may upload a photo or drawing of the invasive weed. This will help us better understand the species.
12.  **Follow Up Status**: Select the current status of the follow-up on this sighting:
    *   **Active**: The follow-up is active and requires further investigation.
    *   **Resolved**: The issue has been resolved.
    *   **Not Needed**: This sighting does not require further follow-up.
13.  **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the last follow-up on this sighting.
14.  **Follow Up Comment**: Add any comments or notes about the follow-up on this sighting.
15.  **Follow Up Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person you contacted about the sighting.
16.  **Follow Up Priority**: Select the priority of the follow-up:
    *   **High**: This sighting requires urgent attention.
    *   **Medium**: This sighting requires standard attention.
    *   **Low**: This sighting requires minimal attention.
17.  **Follow Up Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the follow-up on this sighting.
