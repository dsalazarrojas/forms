# Festival Stallholder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from festival vendors about their overall satisfaction with the festival experience and their willingness to participate in future festivals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are a registered vendor for the festival and that you have attended the event.
2. If you have already completed this survey in a previous festival season, clarify in the comments field.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. Select "Undecided" for the future participation question if you are unsure.
5. Provide genuine feedback and input your responses concisely.
6. If a field is not required, feel free to skip it without affecting your overall feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your festival experience?**
	+ This question helps us understand how well we met your expectations.
	+ Please rate your satisfaction from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 10 (very satisfied).
* **What are your comments about this festival?**
	+ Share your thoughts, suggestions, or any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Are you interested in participating in future festivals?**
	+ Select "True" if you are definitely interested, "False" if you are not, or "Undecided" if you are unsure.
* **How could we improve the festival to serve you better?**
	+ Share your ideas and suggestions on how we can improve the festival to better meet your needs.

## Tips
* Please provide genuine feedback and input your responses accurately and concisely.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Undecided" for the future participation question.
* If a field is not required, feel free to skip it without affecting your overall feedback.
