# Cafe Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your thoughts and preferences as we prepare to reopen our cafe after a temporary closure. Your responses will help us tailor our services to better meet your needs and ensure a comfortable and enjoyable experience for everyone. Please be as honest as possible, and thank you for participating!

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with your answers, and submit it. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or that you do not feel comfortable answering. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often did you visit us before our temporary closure** (`customer_familiarity`): This question helps us understand how much you value our cafe as a regular destination. Your answer will help us plan our reopening events and promotions.
* **How comfortable do you feel about dine-in service** (`comfort_level_dine_in`): We want to ensure that you feel safe and comfortable in our cafe. Please respond with a number from 1 (Not Comfortable) to 5 (Very Comfortable) to help us gauge our dine-in service quality.
* **Which safety measures are most important to you** (`safety_measures_importance`): Select up to 3 safety measures that you value the most. This will help us prioritize our safety protocols.
* **Which menu items have you missed the most** (`menu_must_haves`): We'd love to hear what menu items you've missed the most. This helps us decide on our reopening menu offerings.
* **Would you accept a limited menu for the first few weeks** (`limited_menu_acceptance`): We may need to introduce a limited menu to ensure quality and efficiency. Please respond with your acceptance of this measure.
* **Would you use a mobile app to order and pay ahead** (`mobile_ordering_interest`): We are exploring mobile ordering and payment options. Let us know if this interests you.
* **Interest in Curbside Pickup options** (`curbside_pickup_interest`): We'd like to know if you'd be interested in our Curbside Pickup service.
* **Preferred Opening Hours** (`service_hours_pref`): Select the time slots that you'd prefer for our opening hours. This will help us plan our operating schedule.
* **Would you like to be notified of our exact reopening date** (`subscribe_alerts`): Please let us know if you'd like to be updated on our reopening date.
* **Any other words of encouragement or suggestions** (`reopening_comments`): This is your chance to share any additional feedback or suggestions for our reopening.
* **Email Address for notifications** (`contact_email`): If you'd like to receive updates on our reopening, please provide your email address. This is optional but helpful for us to keep in touch.
* **Any other menu items you would like to see in future** (`reopening_comments`): We're constantly updating our menu. If you have any preferences, now is the time to share them.
* **Exact reopening date** (`survey_completion_date`): Please enter today's date to confirm that the form has been completed.
