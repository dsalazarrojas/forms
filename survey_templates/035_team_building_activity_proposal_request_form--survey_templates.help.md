# Team Building Activity Proposal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Activity Proposal Request Form is a tool for submitting proposals for team building activities to the management for approval. The form helps to centralize and streamline the proposal submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including company name, event type, budget, date, number of attendees, details, contact person email, and team leader and event manager approval.
2. Optionally, provide additional information such as location, department, and uploaded files.
3. Review and submit the form to send it to the management for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. company (Required)
Enter the name of the company for which the team building activity is proposed.

### 3. event_type (Required)
Select whether the proposal is for a "Yes" or "No" event type.

### 4. budget (Required)
Enter the budget for the proposed team building activity.

### 5. date (Required)
Enter the date for the proposed team building activity.

### 6. location (Optional)
Enter any additional location information for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 7. attendees (Required)
Enter the number of attendees expected for the proposed team building activity.

### 8. details (Required)
Provide a detailed description of the proposed team building activity.

### 9. contact_person (Optional)
Enter the contact person email for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 10. contact_info (Optional)
Enter any additional contact information for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 11. department (Optional)
Enter the department for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 12. team_leader (Required)
Select whether the team leader approved this proposal ("Yes" or "No").

### 13. event_manager (Required)
Select whether the event manager approved this proposal ("Yes" or "No").

### 14. manager_approval (Optional)
Select whether the manager approved this proposal ("Yes" or "No"). This field is not required.

### 15. team_approval (Optional)
Select whether the team approved this proposal ("Yes" or "No"). This field is not required.

### 16. comments (Optional)
Enter any comments for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 17. file_upload (Optional)
Upload any additional files related to the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 18. file_url (Optional)
Enter the URL of any uploaded files related to the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 19. file_type (Optional)
Select whether the uploaded files are "Yes" or "No". This field is not required.

### 20. uploaded_files (Optional)
Select whether the uploaded files are "Yes" or "No". This field is not required.

### 21. report (Optional)
Enter any report or notes for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 22. attachment (Optional)
Enter any additional attachment information for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 23. submission_report_attachment (Optional)
Enter any submission report attachment information for the proposed team building activity. This field is not required.

### 24. submission_comment (Optional)
Enter any comments for the proposed submission. This field is not required.

### 25. submission_status (Optional)
Select the submission status as "Active" or "Inactive". This field is not required.

Note that all fields marked as "Optional" are not required and can be skipped.
