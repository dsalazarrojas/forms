# Child And Youth Referee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child and Youth Referee Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from child and youth referees on their experiences and perceptions of their refereeing roles. The survey aims to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for the development of refereeing programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Provide detailed responses for text fields, such as the biggest challenge and most positive aspect of refereeing questions.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Youth Referee Survey --** (Page 1)
	+ This is the introduction page, providing a brief overview of the survey.
* **Name** (Page 2)
	+ Enter your name as it is spelled.
* **Age** (Page 3)
	+ Enter your age in years.
* **Sport you Referee** (Page 4)
	+ Select the sport you referee from the list.
* **League or organization** (Page 5)
	+ Enter the name of the league or organization you are associated with.
* **How long have you been refereeing?** (Page 6)
	+ Select how long you have been refereeing from the provided options.
* **Overall satisfaction with refereeing** (Page 7)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with refereeing from 1 (Very unsatisfied) to 5 (Very satisfied).
* **Quality of referee training provided** (Page 8)
	+ Select the quality of training provided from the provided options.
* **Support and mentoring from experienced referees** (Page 9)
	+ Select the level of support and mentoring you receive from experienced referees.
* **Confidence in knowledge of game rules** (Page 10)
	+ Select your level of confidence in your knowledge of game rules.
* **Ability to manage games and players** (Page 11)
	+ Select your ability to manage games and players from the provided options.
* **How players treat you during games** (Page 12)
	+ Select how players treat you during games from the provided options.
* **How coaches treat you during games** (Page 13)
	+ Select how coaches treat you during games from the provided options.
* **How parents treat you during games** (Page 14)
	+ Select how parents treat you during games from the provided options.
* **Biggest challenge as a referee** (Page 15)
	+ Describe the biggest challenge you face as a referee.
* **Most positive aspect of refereeing** (Page 16)
	+ Describe the most positive aspect of refereeing for you.
* **Improvements needed for youth referees** (Page 17)
	+ Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Will you continue refereeing next season?** (Page 18)
	+ Select whether you will continue refereeing next season from the provided options.
* **Additional feedback or comments** (Page 19)
	+ Provide any additional feedback or comments about your refereeing experience.
