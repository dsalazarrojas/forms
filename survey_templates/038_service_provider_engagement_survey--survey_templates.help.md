# Service Provider Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Provider Engagement Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with a service provider. It helps service providers understand their strengths and weaknesses, making them better equipped to enhance customer experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, please select the relevant ratings based on your experience with the service provider.
2.  For the text fields, provide as much detail as possible, but keep your answers concise.
3.  If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Service Provider Rating** (Provider Rating Options): Rate the service provider's overall performance on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
*   **Overall Performance Rating** (Vendor Performance Rating Options): Rate the service provider's overall performance on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
*   **Communication Quality Rating** (Communication Quality Rating Options): Rate the service provider's communication quality on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
*   **Timeliness Rating** (Timeliness Rating Options): Rate the service provider's timeliness on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction Options): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale from Very Unsatisfied to Very Satisfied.
*   **Recommendation or Comment** (Text Field): If you have a recommendation or comment about the service, please share it here.
*   **Contact Information** (Email Field): If you'd like to be contacted by the service provider with further questions or for more information, please provide your email address. Please note that this information is optional.
*   **What did you like or dislike about the service?** (Text Field): Share any positive or negative experiences with the service.
*   **Additional Comments** (Text Field): If you have any additional comments or questions about the service, please share them here.
*   **Provider Rating Options** (Select One): Select the best rating for the service provider based on your experience.
*   **Vendor Performance Rating Options** (Select One): Select the best rating for the service provider's performance based on your experience.
*   **Communication Quality Rating Options** (Select One): Select the best rating for the service provider's communication quality based on your experience.
*   **Timeliness Rating Options** (Select One): Select the best rating for the service provider's timeliness based on your experience.
*   **Overall Satisfaction Options** (Select One): Select the best rating for your overall satisfaction with the service based on your experience.
