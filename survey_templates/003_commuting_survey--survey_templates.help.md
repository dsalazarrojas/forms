# Commuting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commuting Survey form is used to gather information about your commuting habits and preferences for geographic planning and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your home zip code in the "What is your home zip code?" field.
2. Enter your work or destination zip code in the "What is your work or destination zip code?" field.
3. Select your primary mode of transportation that covers the longest distance of your trip in the "Primary Mode of Transportation" field.
4. If applicable, select any secondary mode of transportation used during the same trip in the "Secondary Mode of Transportation" field.
5. Choose the primary reason for your mode of transport choice from the options provided in the "Why do you choose this mode of transport?" field.
6. Estimate your monthly commute cost in the "Estimated Monthly Commute Cost" field.
7. Indicate how much you consider environmental impact when making your travel choices in the "How much does environmental impact influence your travel choices?" field.
8. Choose how often you use public transit in the "How often do you use public transit?" field.
9. If you drive, rate your satisfaction with parking availability at your destination in the "How satisfied are you with parking availability at your destination?" field.
10. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the "Any additional feedback for transportation planners?" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your home zip code?** (`home_zip_code`, text, required): Enter your home zip code to help us understand your geographic location.
* **What is your work or destination zip code?** (`work_zip_code`, text, required): Enter your work or destination zip code to help us understand your commute destination.
* **Primary Mode of Transportation** (`primary_travel_mode`, select_one, required): Select your primary mode of transportation that covers the longest distance of your trip.
* **Secondary Mode of Transportation** (`secondary_travel_mode`, select_one, optional): Select any secondary mode of transportation used during the same trip, if applicable.
* **Why do you choose this mode of transport?** (`reason_for_mode_choice`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for your mode of transport choice from the provided options.
* **Estimated Monthly Commute Cost** (`monthly_commute_cost`, number, required): Estimate your monthly commute cost, including fuel, parking, transit passes, or maintenance.
* **How much does environmental impact influence your travel choices?** (`environmental_impact_awareness`, select_one, required): Indicate how much you consider environmental impact when making your travel choices.
* **How often do you use public transit?** (`transit_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you use public transit per month.
* **How satisfied are you with parking availability at your destination?** (`parking_availability_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with parking availability at your destination, only if you drive.
* **Any additional feedback for transportation planners?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for new routes, bike lanes, or transit timing.

Note: This guide is intended for users and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
