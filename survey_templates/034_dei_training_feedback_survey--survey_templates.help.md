# DEI Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DEI Training Feedback Survey is a tool to gather feedback from attendees of DEI training sessions. This survey helps us understand the effectiveness, value, and overall experience of the training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the on-screen instructions and fill out each section with your feedback and ratings. Be honest and candid in your responses, and review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Training Feedback (Text)**: This is a free-form text field where you can provide general comments about the training session.
*   **How effective was the training? (Select One)**: Rate the effectiveness of the training session by selecting one of the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
*   **How satisfied were you with this session? (Select Multiple)**: Rate your satisfaction level with the session by selecting one or more of the following options:
    + Yes
    + No
*   **How valuable was the training to you? (Number)**: Rate the value you gained from the training session on a scale.
*   **Suggestions for future sessions (Text)**: Provide suggestions and ideas for future DEI training sessions.
*   **Additional feedback (Text)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the training session.
