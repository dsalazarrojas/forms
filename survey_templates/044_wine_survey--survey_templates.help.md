# Wine Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your wine preferences, habits, and experiences. Your responses will help us understand your tastes and behaviors related to wine consumption, purchasing, and appreciation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly to provide accurate information about your wine preferences and habits.
2. Select the options that best match your answers for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or details in the "Any additional comments about wine?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the wine survey** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a welcome message to get you started.
* **What type of wine do you enjoy?** (`wine_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the types of wine you enjoy, such as Red, White, Rosé, Sparkling, or Dessert.
* **How often do you drink wine?** (`wine_consumption_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you drink wine, such as Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **What is your favorite type of wine?** (`favorite_wine_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your favorite type of wine from the list.
* **What price range do you usually pay for a bottle of wine?** (`price_range`, `number`, required): Enter the price range you usually pay for a bottle of wine.
* **Which of the following wines do you like?** (`wine_consumption_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the wines you like from the list.
* **Is there a wine not listed above that you prefer?** (`wine_consumption_preferences_other`, `text`, optional): If there's a wine not listed above that you prefer, describe it in this field.
* **Where do you usually purchase wine?** (`wine_purchase_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose where you usually buy wine, such as Winery, Liquor store, Online store, or Grocery store.
* **How often do you participate in wine tastings?** (`wine_tasting_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you participate in wine tastings, such as Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Are you a member of a wine club?** (`wine_club_membership`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you're a member of a wine club, True or False.
* **How often do you attend wine club events?** (`wine_club_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you attend wine club events, such as Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **How often do you visit a winery?** (`winery_visits`, `select_one`, optional): Select how often you visit a winery, such as Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Why do you drink wine?** (`wine_consumption_purpose`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose why you drink wine, such as Social, Special occasions, Relaxation, Food pairing, or Other.
* **What is your average monthly wine purchase amount?** (`wine_purchase_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the average amount of money you spend on wine per month.
* **How often do you drink wine?** (`wine_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you drink wine, such as Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Do you prefer a strong or weak wine taste?** (`wine_taste_preferences`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you prefer a strong or weak wine taste.
* **Which wine color do you prefer?** (`wine_color_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferred wine color, such as Red, White, Rosé, Sparkling, or All.
* **Which types of food pair well with wine?** (`food_pairing_wine`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose which types of food you think pair well with wine, such as Pizza, Pasta, Salad, Cheese plate, or Dessert.
* **What time of day do you usually drink wine?** (`wine_consumption_time`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the time of day you usually drink wine, such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime.
* **What do you think of wine quality?** (`wine_quality`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your opinion on wine quality, such as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Terrible.
* **What do you usually pay for a bottle of wine?** (`wine_price`, `select_one`, required): Select the price range you usually pay for a bottle of wine.
* **How do you usually drink wine?** (`wine_taste`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you usually drink wine, such as Slowly or Quickly.
* **Why do you purchase wine?** (`wine_purchase_reason`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose why you buy wine, such as Gift, Self-consumption, Business, Special occasion, or Other.
* **What is your favorite type of wine taste?** (`favorite_wine_taste`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite type of wine taste, such as Acidity, Fruity, Earthy, Spicy, or Floral.
* **Any additional comments about wine?** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about wine.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* Your responses will help us improve our understanding of wine preferences and habits.
