# Wedding Survey Form Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a wedding survey form designed for wedding planners or event organizers to collect information about wedding participants. The form helps gather essential details about the number of attendees and the number of guests each participant will bring to the wedding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the wedding participant.
2. Answer the question "Will they attend?" with either "Yes" or "No".
3. If they will attend, answer the questions about their personal belongings and additional wedding-related details.
4. Enter the number of prophets (if any).
5. Select the number of guests they can attend.
6. Enter the date they can get into the venue.
7. Select the method of entry (Yes or No) and whether they can bring any items to the event.
8. Answer the question about what they will bring (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Participant** (Required, text): Enter the name of the wedding participant.
* **Will they attend?** (Optional, select_one): Select "Yes" if the participant will attend, otherwise select "No".
* **Do they have their own prophets?** (Optional, select_multiple): If they have their own prophets, select all applicable options.
* **Prophets Count** (Optional, number): Enter the number of prophets, if any.
* **How many guests can attend?** (Optional, number): Enter the number of guests this participant can attend.
* **How many guests can attend?** (Optional, number): This field seems to be a duplicate. Please ignore or delete this field for consistency.
* **How will they get in?** (Optional, date): Enter the date they can get into the venue.
* **Can they get in?** (Optional, select_multiple): Select "Yes" if they can get into the venue, otherwise select "No".
* **Will they bring?** (Optional, select_one): Select "Yes" if they will bring any items to the event, otherwise select "No".
