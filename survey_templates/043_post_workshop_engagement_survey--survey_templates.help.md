# Post Workshop Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the feedback and suggestions from participants, trainers, and event organizers after a workshop or training session. The survey aims to understand the participant's satisfaction, perceived key takeaways, and improvement ideas for future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer that best describes your experience and opinions.
2. Provide a clear and concise comment or idea for each question that requires text input.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is valuable for improving future workshops.
