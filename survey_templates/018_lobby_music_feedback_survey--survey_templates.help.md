# รับบ Lobby Music Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the lobby music. Please provide your honest opinions to help us improve our music selection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Rate our lobby music, background music, and entertainment music on a scale from 1 to 10.
3. Select your favorite music genre and the type of music you would like to hear in the lobby.
4. Choose how you rate our music preference.
5. If you have any comments, please type them in the comments field.
6. If you are willing, please provide your contact information so we can follow up with you for further feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **How do you rate our lobby music?** (rating_lobby_music, number, optional): Rate our lobby music on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How do you rate our background music?** (rating_background_music, number, optional): Rate our background music on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How do you rate our entertainment music?** (rating_entertainment_music, number, optional): Rate our entertainment music on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **What is your favorite music genre?** (favorite_music_genre, select_one, required): Select one of the following music genres: Jazz, Pop, Classical, Rock, Hip-Hop, Country, R&B, Electronic.
* **Music volume** (music_volume, number, optional): Please rate the volume of our music on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **What type of music would you like to hear in the lobby?** (music_type, select_multiple, optional): Select all types of music you would like to hear in the lobby. You can select multiple options.
* **How do you rate our music preference?** (music_preference, select_one, required): Choose one of the following music preferences: Too quiet, Just Right, Too loud.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the text area.
* **May we contact you for further feedback?** (consent_to_contact, text, required): If you are willing, please provide your email address so we can follow up with you for further feedback.
* **Contact Information** (contact_information, note, optional): If you are willing to provide it, please enter your contact information so we can follow up with you for further feedback.
