# Press Comment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Press Comment Request Form is a simple form designed to collect information from users to facilitate communication with the press. It includes fields for first name, last name, email, phone number, comments or feedback, date, and time. The form is meant to be completed for official purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Enter any comments or feedback in the "Press Comment" field.
6.  Select your availability for the press conference by choosing a date in the "Date" field.
7.  Select your preferred time for the press conference by choosing a time in the "Time" field.
8.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
    (`first_name`, text, required) 
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
    (`last_name`, text, required)
*   **Press Comment**: Enter any comments or feedback.
    (`press_comment`, text, required)
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
    (`email`, email, required)
*   **Date**: Select the date you are available for the press conference.
    (`date`, date, required)
*   **Time**: Select the time you are available for the press conference.
    (`time`, time, required)
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
    (`phone`, text, required)
*   **Submit**: Click this button to submit your information.
    (`submit_button`, text, not required)
*   **Error Name**: This field is not applicable. Do not complete this field.
    (`error_name`, text, not required)
