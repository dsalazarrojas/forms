# Cultural Awareness Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Awareness Training Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of cultural awareness training programs and assess the participant's experience, satisfaction, and perception of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and honest responses.
2. For the optional fields, provide your honest opinions and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Training Program** (`program_name`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have attended a cultural awareness training program, or 'No' if you have not attended such a program.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training program you participated in.
* **Hours of Training** (`training_hours`, number, optional): If applicable, report the total number of hours spent in the training program.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, select_one, optional): Assess the level of training you received, with options: High, Low.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_comments`, note, optional): Share any thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Provide your numerical evaluation of the training program, on a scale (to be determined).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional): Write a short evaluation of the training program.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, select_one, optional): Report your overall experience with the training program, with options: Yes, No.
* **Program Effectiveness** (`program_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Assess the effectiveness of the program, with options: Yes, No.
* **Recommend to Others** (`recommendation`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would recommend the training program to others, with options: Yes, No.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Report your level of satisfaction with the training program, with options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **Training Area** (`training_area`, select_one, optional): Indicate the area of training, with options: Yes, No.

Note: The evaluation score and comments sections are optional and should only be filled if you are able to provide a numerical score and written feedback, respectively.
