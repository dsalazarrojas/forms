# Bird Personality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bird Personality Assessment is a 20-question survey designed to help you discover your unique personality type, represented by a bird species that reflects your traits and characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Information**:
	* Enter your first name, last name, and email address (required fields).
	* Choose your age group from the dropdown menu (required).
2. Read the **Assessment Instructions** carefully and answer the 20 questions that follow.
3. Select the best answer for each question, taking into account how you typically behave in different situations.
4. Once you've finished, submit your answers to generate your results.
