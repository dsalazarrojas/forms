# Sales Funnel Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Funnel Checklist Form is designed to help you evaluate the effectiveness of your sales process and identify areas for improvement. It consists of multiple sections, each covering a different stage in the sales funnel. By completing this form, you can gauge your performance in each stage and make data-driven decisions to optimize your sales strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding answer for each stage of the sales process. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose the option that best describes your current situation. For number fields, enter a numerical value. Make sure to answer each question carefully, as your responses will help you create a comprehensive picture of your sales process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are currently generating leads through various means such as advertising, trade shows, or social media. Choose all applicable options.
* **Lead Qualifying** (`lead_qualifying`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have established a clear process for qualifying leads and 'No' if you have not.
* **Lead Follow-up** (`lead_follow_up`, select_multiple, required): Check the methods you are using for follow-up with leads, including phone calls, emails, or meetings.
* **Lead Conversions** (`lead_conversions`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you have converted a lead into a sale and 'No' if not.
* **Conversion Rates** (`conversion_rates`, number, required): Enter the conversion rate as a numerical value (e.g., 20%).
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select 'Satisfied' if customers are generally satisfied with your sales process and 'Dissatisfied' if not.
* **Sales Process** (`sales_process`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the methods you use to engage with customers, such as product demos, presentations, or meetings.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required): Choose the primary source of your leads, e.g., 'Yes' for online advertising.
* **Sales Stage** (`sales_stage`, select_one, required): Select the current stage of the sales process, e.g., 'Yes' for initial contact.
* **Conversion Stage** (`conversion_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the stage at which the lead converted into a sale, e.g., 'Yes' for final stages.
* **Lead Value** (`lead_value`, select_multiple, required): Determine the value of your leads based on their potential revenue or other factors.
* **Sales Value** (`sales_value`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the value of your sales strategy, e.g., based on revenue generated.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, required): Check the frequency and methods used for follow-up, including phone calls, emails, or meetings.
* **Lead Qualifying Value** (`lead_qualifying_value`, select_multiple, required): Assess the value of lead qualification, e.g., based on the number of qualified leads.
* **Conversion Values** (`conversion_values`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the factors contributing to conversion, e.g., product features, price, or customer service.
* **Customer Interaction** (`customer_interaction`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if customers are actively engaged throughout the sales process and 'No' if not.
* **Product Value** (`product_value`, select_multiple, required): Assess the value of your product or service, e.g., based on customer satisfaction or revenue generated.
* **Lead Stage** (`lead_stage`, select_one, required): Select the current stage of the lead in the sales process.
* **Conversion** (`conversion`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if a lead converted into a sale and 'No' if not.
* **Sales Stage Values** (`sales_stage_values`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the factors that influence sales stage, e.g., customer needs or product features.
* **Customer Satisfaction Score** (`customer_satisfaction_score`, number, required): Enter a numerical value to gauge customer satisfaction.
* **Sales Funnel Value** (`sales_funnel_value`, select_multiple, required): Determine the value of the sales funnel, e.g., based on conversion rates or customer satisfaction.
* **Follow-up Values** (`follow_up_values`, select_multiple, required): Assess the value of follow-up activities, e.g., based on customer engagement or revenue generated.
* **Lead Source Values** (`lead_source_values`, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the value of lead sources, e.g., based on conversion rates or customer acquisition costs.
* **Lead Value Score** (`lead_value_score`, number, required): Enter a numerical value to gauge lead value.
