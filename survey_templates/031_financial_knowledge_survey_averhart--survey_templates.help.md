# financial_knowledge_survey_averhart - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help financial experts or professionals assess their own financial knowledge, goals, and experience with financial institutions. It will help them identify areas where they need improvement and provide valuable insights into their own skills and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by reading the introduction carefully to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
* Select your level of knowledge about personal finance from the options provided.
* Describe your short-term financial goals for the next 6-12 months.
* Choose the financial institutions you are familiar with.
* Select all the financial products or services you have used in the past 6-12 months.
* Choose how you currently manage your debt.
* Answer whether you have ever experienced a financial crisis or emergency.
* Rate your level of knowledge about investment and saving strategies (optional).
* Rate how well our institution meets your financial needs (optional).
* Rate your satisfaction with your current financial situation (optional).
* Select the status of your submission.
* Choose the date and time of your submission.
* Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
* Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Level of Knowledge** (id: 2): This field asks you to select your level of knowledge about personal finance. Please choose from the options: "I am highly knowledgeable", "I have some knowledge", or "I have little knowledge".
* **Your Short-Term Financial Goals** (id: 3): Please describe your financial goals for the next 6-12 months. This will help us understand your priorities and aspirations.
* **Financial Institution Familiarity** (id: 4): Choose the financial institutions you are familiar with. Select all that apply.
* **Financial Products and Services Used** (id: 5): Select all the financial products or services you have used in the past 6-12 months.
* **Debt Management** (id: 6): Choose how you currently manage your debt.
* **Financial Crisis or Emergency** (id: 7): Answer whether you have ever experienced a financial crisis or emergency.
* **Knowledge about Investments and Saving Strategies** (id: 8): Rate your level of knowledge about investment and saving strategies. Please use the number scale provided.
* **Institutional Meeting Financial Needs** (id: 9): Rate how well our institution meets your financial needs.
* **Current Financial Situation** (id: 10): Rate your satisfaction with your current financial situation.
* **Submission Status** (id: 11): Select the status of your submission.
* **Submission Date** (id: 12): Please select the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (id: 13): Select the time of your submission.
* **Any Additional Comments** (id: 14): Please provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Your Email** (id: 15): Please enter your email address.
* **Your Phone Number** (id: 16): Please enter your phone number.
* **Any Notes** (id: 17): Please provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Email 2** (id: 18): Please enter your email address (optional).
