# # Breakfast Program Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the breakfast program provided by your institution. Your input will help improve the quality and effectiveness of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and email address (optional).
2.  Choose your participant type (Student, Teacher, Staff Member, Parent, Administrator, or Other).
3.  Select your level of participation in the program.
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
5.  Rate the quality of food served.
6.  Rate the variety of food options.
7.  Indicate whether the food is served at proper temperature.
8.  Choose if portions are adequate.
9.  Rate the menu variety.
10.  Choose your favorite breakfast items.
11.  Indicate any least favorite items.
12.  Rate the service speed.
13.  Rate the friendliness of staff.
14.  Rate the cleanliness of the dining area.
15.  Indicate whether your dietary needs are met.
16.  Rate the impact of the program on your day.
17.  Suggest any improvements for the program.
18.  Provide any additional comments or feedback.
19.  Indicate whether you would recommend the program to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
