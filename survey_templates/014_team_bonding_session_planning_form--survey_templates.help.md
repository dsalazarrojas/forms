# Team Bonding Session Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Bonding Session Planning Form is designed to gather essential information from team members to plan an engaging and effective team bonding session. This form helps organizers understand individual preferences for the session details, allowing them to tailor the event to suit the team's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page to understand the different sections and the options provided for each field.
2. Provide your preferences for the session details, date, time, activities, budget, team size, and session type.
3. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any concerns or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Bonding Session Details**: This section is an open note field for you to provide any additional information about the session details.
* **Date Preferences**:
	+ **Yes**: You prefer to have the session on a specific date.
	+ **No**: You are flexible with the session date.
* **Time Preferences**:
	+ **Yes**: You prefer a specific time for the session.
	+ **No**: You are flexible with the session time.
* **Activity Preferences**:
	+ **Yes**: You prefer a particular activity or type of activity for the session.
	+ **No**: You don't have a specific preference or would like to leave it up to the team leaders.
* **Venue Preferences**:
	+ **Yes**: You prefer a specific venue or location for the session.
