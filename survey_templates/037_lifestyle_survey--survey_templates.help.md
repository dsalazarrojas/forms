# Lifestyle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This lifestyle survey aims to collect information about your personal characteristics, habits, and preferences. The survey is divided into several sections, each covering different aspects of your lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences or characteristics.
3. Enter any additional information that does not fit into the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): This section is not applicable for this survey as there are no questions to answer.
* **Hobbies** (`hobbies`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your hobbies or interests that you enjoy doing in your free time. You can choose more than one option.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_one, required): Choose one interest that you are most passionate about.
* **Favorite Food** (`favorite_food`, select_one, required): Select your favorite type of food.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your work experiences or industries you have worked in.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your education that you would like to share.
* **Personality Type** (`personality_type`, select_one, required): Choose one personality type that best describes you.
* **Favorite Travel** (`favorite_travel`, select_one, required): Select your favorite type of travel.
* **Favorite Sport** (`favorite_sport`, select_one, required): Select your favorite sport.
* **Work Style** (`work_style`, select_one, required): Choose one work style that best describes your work approach.
* **Family Status** (`family_status`, select_one, optional): Select your family status.
* **Favorite Occupation** (`favorite_occupation`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your favorite occupation that you would like to share.
* **Hobbies Other** (`hobbies_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional hobbies that you would like to share that are not listed above.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Favorite Social Media** (`favorite_social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your favorite social media platforms.
* **Personality Trait** (`personality_trait`, select_one, required): Choose one personality trait that best describes you.
* **Interests Other** (`interests_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional interests that you would like to share that are not listed above.
* **Education Other** (`education_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your education that you would like to share.
* **Work Experience Other** (`work_experience_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional work experiences that you would like to share.
* **Favorite Food Other** (`favorite_food_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional favorite foods that you would like to share.
* **Travel Experience Other** (`travel_experience_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional travel experiences that you would like to share.
* **Hobby Other** (`hobby_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional hobbies that you would like to share.
* **Occupation Other** (`occupation_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional occupation that you would like to share.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select all your education levels.
* **Personality Type Other** (`personality_type_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your personality type that you would like to share.

**Tips**
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Select all options that apply to you.
* Enter any additional information that you would like to share.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, choose the one that best fits your description.
