# Caregiver Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Caregiver Intake Form is designed to collect demographic and professional information from caregivers to match their qualifications with caregiving positions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out your personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
*   Proceed to fill out your professional details such as your education level, certifications, work experience, and the type of employment desired.
*   Select your preferred facility type and the types of care experience you have.
*   Provide information on your skills, language spoken, and preferred shift.
*   Indicate your availability to start and provide professional references.
*   Consent to the background check and acknowledge your understanding of the health screening process.
*   Confirm your reliable transportation method.
*   Finally, provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Caregiver Name**: Your full name as it should be spelled correctly.
*   **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
*   **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth for age verification.
*   **Position or Role Applying For**: The job title or type of caregiving role you are applying for.
*   **Employment Type**: Select your desired type of employment (full-time, part-time, temporary, etc.).
*   **Facility Type**: Where you would prefer to work.
*   **Education Level**: The highest level of education you have achieved.
*   **Nursing or Healthcare Education**: Any certifications, licenses, or training related to nursing or healthcare.
*   **Certifications or Licenses**: List any certifications or licenses you hold, such as CNA, LPN, RN, etc.
*   **Certification Expiration Dates**: The expiration dates of your certifications.
*   **Work Experience**: The total years of healthcare work experience you have.
*   **Previous Healthcare Employers**: List any previous healthcare employers or facilities.
*   **Types of Care Experience**: Select any care experience you have, such as elderly care, disability care, etc.
*   **Key Skills and Strengths**: A list of your key skills or strengths as a caregiver.
*   **Languages Spoken**: List any languages you speak fluently.
*   **Shift Preference**: Your preferred shift schedule.
*   **Availability Start Date**: The date when you are available to start.
*   **Professional References**: Names and contact information of 2-3 professional references.
*   **Consent to Background Check**: Confirm that you consent to the background check.
*   **Health Screening Acknowledgment**: Understand the health screening process.
*   **Reliable Transportation**: Confirm your reliable transportation method.
*   **Additional Information**: Any other information that you think would be helpful for your application.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you are unsure of any question, do not hesitate to reach out.
*   Please attach any supporting documents or evidence to back up your claims.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
