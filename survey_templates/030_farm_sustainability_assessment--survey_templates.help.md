# Farm Sustainability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Sustainability Assessment is designed to collect information about farm sustainability practices and challenges from farmers and farm operators. It is used to assess the level of sustainability and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the relevant information for each field and follow the instructions provided. Make sure to check all that apply to ensure that you answer all questions accurately.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Information** (`farm_sustainability_assessment`): This field is used to determine the level of information you have about your farm. Select from No Information, Some information, Full information.
* **Farm name** (`farm_name`): Enter the name of your farm.
* **Farm location** (`farm_location`): This field is used to select the county where your farm is located. Select all that apply from Agricultural Land, Forestry, Urban, Wildlife Preserve.
* **Farm operation** (`farm_operation`): Select the type of farm you have from Crop, Livestock, Forestry, Mixed (Both crop and livestock).
* **Farm type** (`farm_type`): This field is used to select all types of farms you have. Select all that apply from Conventional, Organic, Biodynamic, Biodynamic and organic.
* **Farm size** (`farm_size`): Enter the number of acres farmed.
* **Farm production** (`farm_production`): This field is used to select all products you produce. Select all that apply from Grains, Dairy, Poultry, Nuts.
* **Farm challenges** (`farm_challenge`): Enter some of the key challenges you face in your farming operation.
* **Farm goal** (`farm_goal`): Enter your goals for the farm.
* **Farm sustainability practice** (`farm_sustainability_practice`): Describe the sustainable practices you are using.
* **Farm sustainability level** (`farm_sustainability_level`): Rate the sustainability level of the practices you are using.
* **Farm sustainability progress** (`farm_sustainability_progress`): Describe the progress you have made toward implementing these sustainable practices.
* **Farm certification** (`farm_certification`): This field is used to select all farm certifications you have. Select all that apply from USDA Organic, Non-GMO, Biodynamic, Certified Humane.
* **Farm resources** (`farm_resources`): Enter any resources you are using.
* **Farm support** (`farm_support`): Enter any support you receive.
* **Farm partners** (`farm_partners`): Enter any partnerships you have.
* **Farm partnership** (`farm_partnership`): Enter any partnership information.
* **Farm financials** (`farm_financial`): Enter your farm's financial information.
* **Farm insurance** (`farm_insurance`): Enter any insurance you have.
* **Farm legal** (`farm_legal`): Enter any legal information.
* **Farm environment** (`farm_environment`): Enter any environmental information.
* **Farm health and safety** (`farm_health_and_safety`): Enter any health and safety information.
* **Farm energy and water** (`farm_energy_and_water`): Enter any energy and water information.
* **Farm waste and recycling** (`farm_waste_and_recycling`): Enter any waste and recycling information.
* **Farm safety and security** (`farm_safety_and_security`): Enter any safety and security information.

Note: The form fields are designed to be flexible, and some fields are optional. Make sure to complete all fields that apply to you.
