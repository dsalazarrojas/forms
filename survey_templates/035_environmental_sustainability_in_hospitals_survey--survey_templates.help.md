# Environmental Sustainability In Hospitals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Sustainability In Hospitals Survey is designed to collect data on a hospital's environmental sustainability practices and performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please proceed page by page. Each page will ask you to provide information or answer questions related to environmental sustainability in hospitals.

* **Introduction** (`intro_1`): This is a note section that provides a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type_1`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: This hospital has implemented environmental sustainability initiatives.
	+ No: This hospital has not implemented environmental sustainability initiatives.
	+ Other: Please specify other (provide additional information in the text field).
* **Green Initiatives** (`green_initiatives_1`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes: This hospital has implemented green initiatives such as recycling programs, energy-efficient equipment, etc.
	+ No: This hospital does not have green initiatives.

* **Challenges** (`challenges_1`): Select one of the following options:

	+ Other: Please specify other (provide additional information in the text field).
* **Ideas** (`ideas_1`): Please provide your suggestions or ideas for environmental sustainability initiatives.
* **Questions** (`questions_1`): Select one of the following options:

	+ Other: Please specify other (provide additional information in the text field).
