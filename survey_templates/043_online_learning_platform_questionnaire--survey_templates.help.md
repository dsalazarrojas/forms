## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your interest in taking part in an online learning platform. It will help us understand your preferences and reasons for potentially enrolling in courses, as well as gather your feedback and ratings for the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Read each question carefully and select your answers as required.
* Fill in all the fields, if you have any additional information or comments.
