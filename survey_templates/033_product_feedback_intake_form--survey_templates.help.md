# Product Feedback Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback and ratings from users to help us improve our products and services. Please provide your honest feedback and rating to help us better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by an *).
2. Provide a brief description of your feedback in the "Description" field.
3. Select your rating and satisfaction level from the relevant fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to add them in the "Open Text Feedback" field.
5. If you have any further questions or need help, use the "Open Select Feedback" field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you if needed.
7. Provide any additional product information as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Feedback Form** (`product_feedback_form`, text, not required): A short description of your feedback.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, not required): The ID number of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): A brief description of your experience with the product.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Your rating of the product (1-5 stars).
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, not required): Select the rating scale you think best reflects your experience.
*	*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Any additional comments or feedback.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate your level of satisfaction with our product.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate if you would recommend our product.
* **Open Text Feedback** (`open_text_feedback`, text, not required): Any additional comments or feedback.
* **Open Select Feedback** (`open_select_feedback`, select_one, not required): Select any additional feedback or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): The name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Category** (`category`, text, not required): The category of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, text, not required): The subcategory of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, text, not required): The version of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating Date** (`rating_date`, date, not required): The date and time you rated the product.
* **Rating Time** (`rating_time`, time, not required): The time you rated the product.
* **Rating Note** (`rating_note`, note, not required): Any additional notes or comments about your rating.
* **Email Subject** (`email_subject`, select_one, not required): Select the reason for your email.
* **Email Message** (`email_message`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or messages.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, not required): The image of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Image Caption** (`product_image_caption`, text, not required): A caption for the product image.
* **Product Video** (`product_video`, text, not required): The video of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Video Caption** (`product_video_caption`, text, not required): A caption for the product video.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate with your feedback.
* If you have any questions or need help, use the "Open Select Feedback" field.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Open Text Feedback" field.
* If you have any further questions or need help, contact us at [your email or support contact].
