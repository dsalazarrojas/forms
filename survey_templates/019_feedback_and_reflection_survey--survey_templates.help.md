# feedback_and_reflection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and reflection from users after an event, aiming to improve future events and assess the effectiveness of the event in achieving its goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and provide your honest response.
2.  Answer each question based on your genuine experience at the event.
3.  You can select multiple options for some questions, such as rating and multiple choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
* **Event Type**:
  *   Label: Event Type
  *   Type: Select One
  *   Explanation: Choose the type of event you attended (Yes, No).
* **Rating**: 
  *   Label: Rating
  *   Type: Select Multiple
  *   Explanation: Rate the event based on your experience (Good, Fair).
* **What was the purpose of the event?**: 
  *   Label: What was the purpose of the event?
  *   Type: Text
  *   Explanation: Provide a clear and specific answer about the event's purpose.
* **What did you learn from the event?**: 
  *   Label: What did you learn from the event?
  *   Type: Text
  *   Explanation: Describe what new knowledge or skills you acquired during the event.
* **What did you like most about the event?**: 
  *   Label: What did you like most about the event?
  *   Type: Text
  *   Explanation: Share your favorite or most valuable part of the event.
* **What didn't you like about the event?**: 
  *   Label: What didn't you like about the event?
  *   Type: Text
  *   Explanation: Share any criticisms, dislikes, or areas for improvement.
* **Goals**:
  *   Label: Goals
  *   Type: Select Multiple
  *   Explanation: Indicate if the event met your personal or professional goals (Yes, No).
*   **Any recommendations for the next event?**: 
  *   Label: Any recommendations for the next event?
  *   Type: Select Multiple
  *   Explanation: Suggest improvements or changes for future events.
* **Feedback**: 
  *   Label: Feedback
  *   Type: Select Multiple
  *   Explanation: Provide any general feedback or comments about the event (Yes, No).

## Tips

*   Be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses.
*   Focus on providing constructive feedback that can help improve future events.
*   If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or advice.
