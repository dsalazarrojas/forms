# sports_club_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and habits related to food and beverages at the sports club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your food preferences from the list of options.
2. Choose how often you visit the sports club.
3. Select your preferred beverage from the list of options.
4. If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve the club, type them in the Ideas to Improve field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Selection** (`food_selection`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the food options that you prefer.
	+ Select as many options as apply to you.
* **How often do you visit?** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Please choose the frequency of your visits to the sports club.
	+ Select one option that best describes your visit frequency.
* **Preferred Beverage** (`preferred_beverage`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred beverage from the list of options.
	+ Choose one option that you like best.
* **Ideas to Improve** (`improve_facility`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve the sports club, type them in this field.
	+ This is an open field for you to provide any ideas or suggestions.
