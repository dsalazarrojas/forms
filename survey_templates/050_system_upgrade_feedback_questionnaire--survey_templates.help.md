# System Upgrade Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback questionnaire designed to gather information from users about their experience with the system upgrade. We want to understand how well the new features are working, what users think could be improved, and how the upgrade has affected their productivity and satisfaction with the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you have not used the new features, please do not fill out this form.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help us improve the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the main reason you're taking the System Upgrade Feedback Questionnaire?** (Page 1, Question 1)
	+ This is a free-text question where you can explain why you are taking the survey. Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can.
* **What was your experience with the system before the upgrade?** (Page 1, Question 2)
	+ Describe your experience with the system before the upgrade. This will help us understand what we improved and what we need to work on.
* **What features did you use for the first time after the upgrade?** (Page 2, Question 3)
	+ Check all the features that you used for the first time after the upgrade. This will help us know what features need more work and what features are working well.
* **How satisfied are you with the new features?** (Page 2, Question 4)
	+ Choose one of the three options: "New features are great!", "Neutral", or "Not satisfied". This will help us gauge your satisfaction level with the new features.
* **What could be improved in the new features?** (Page 3, Question 5)
	+ Provide any feedback you have about the new features. This could be a suggestion, a complaint, or just an idea for improvement.
* **Comments** (Page 4, Question 6)
	+ If you want to add any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **What is your email address?** (Page 5, Question 7)
	+ Please enter your email address so we can contact you with the results of the survey and follow up if needed.
* **What is your phone number?** (Page 6, Question 8)
	+ If you want to be contacted by phone for further follow up, please enter your phone number.
* **What is your name?** (Page 7, Question 9)
	+ Please enter your name so we can associate it with your feedback.
* **What is your company name?** (Page 8, Question 10)
	+ If you are filling out this form on behalf of your company, please enter your company name.

# Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your answers.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
* If you want to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* If you want to be contacted by email, please provide your email address.
