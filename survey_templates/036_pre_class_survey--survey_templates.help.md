# pre_class_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience in a class. It aims to identify areas of improvement and help instructors refine their teaching practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being collected.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. For each question, select the most accurate response that reflects your experience in the class.
4. If you have additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, `note`, required): This section is a space for you to provide any general feedback about your experience in the class. Please be as detailed as possible and include any notable events or incidents that stood out to you.
* **Expectations** (`expectations`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the options that best describe your pre-course expectations and what was actually met during the course. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Lessons** (`lessons`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes what you learned from this course.
* **Knowledge Gaps** (`knowledge_gaps`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the options that best describe the knowledge gaps you identified during the course. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Classroom Experience** (`classroom_experience`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here. This section is not required, but it's a great opportunity to provide more detailed feedback.
