# Homeostasis Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on areas where the organization may need more work on homeostasis. Homeostasis is a concept describing the balance and regulation of internal and external factors that influence an organization's performance and well-being. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and prioritize our efforts to maintain a healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form and its relevance to the organization.
2. Answer question one by selecting one of the options (A, B, C, or D) that best describes the area where the organization needs more work on homeostasis.
3. Answer question two by providing your thoughts and ideas in the text field. This is an opportunity to share your insights and experiences.
4. Answer question three by selecting multiple options (A, B, C, or D) that reflect the areas where the organization needs more work on homeostasis.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Homeostasis Instruction**: This field is for any additional information or clarification on the questions or form process. Please use it to ask questions or provide more context.
* **question one**: Choose one of the options (A, B, C, or D) that best describes the area where the organization needs more work on homeostasis.
* **question two**: Provide your thoughts and ideas on the aspects of homeostasis that require improvement. This is a free-text field where you can type as much or as little as you like.
* **question three**: Select multiple options (A, B, C, or D) that reflect the areas where the organization needs more work on homeostasis. You can select as many or as few as you like.
* **Submit**: This is the button to submit your completed form. Once clicked, your feedback will be sent to the relevant stakeholders for review and consideration.
