# Human Learning Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Learning Assessment Quiz form is designed to assess the learning abilities of individuals or groups through a series of questions. This form helps in identifying areas of improvement and understanding of specific topics or skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine the type of quiz you want to conduct by selecting the "Quiz Type" option. This helps us tailor the quiz to your specific needs.
2.  Choose the type of questions you want to include in your quiz by selecting the "Question Type" option.
3.  Select the questions from our question bank to create your quiz.
4.  Set a score for your quiz, if applicable.
5.  Choose how much time is allowed for the quiz, if time is a factor.
6.  Select individuals to receive the quiz results via email by selecting "Email To" and "Email Cc" options.
7.  Decide on phone recipients for your quiz results by selecting "Phone To" and "Phone Cc" options.
8.  Add any additional comments or notes regarding your quiz in the "Note" field.
9.  Choose whether to submit or save the quiz for later by selecting "Submit" or "Save and Submit" respectively.
10.  Select whether to cancel your quiz by choosing "Cancel" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quiz Type (`1`, `select_one`, required/optional):** Choose the type of quiz you want to conduct, such as a general assessment or a test. This selection will influence the type of questions and content that will be displayed.
* **Question Type (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional):** Select the type of questions you want to include, such as true or false or multiple choice.
* **Question Bank (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):** Select the questions from our pre-curated question bank that you'd like to include in your quiz.
* **Question (`4`, `text`, required/optional):** If you'd like to add a custom question, enter it here.
* **Score (`5`, `number`, required/optional):** Set a score for your quiz, if applicable. This will help in assessing the learner's performance.
* **Time Allowed (`6`, `time`, required/optional):** Choose how much time you'd like to allow for the learner to complete the quiz.
* **Email To (`7`, `email`, required/optional):** Select individuals to receive the quiz results via email. Enter their email addresses separated by commas.
* **Email Cc (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):** Select individuals to receive a copy of the quiz results via email. Enter their email addresses separated by commas.
* **Phone To (`9`, `text`, required/optional):** Select individuals to receive the quiz results via phone. Enter their phone numbers separated by commas.
* **Phone Cc (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):** Select individuals to receive a copy of the quiz results via phone. Enter their phone numbers separated by commas.
* **Note (`11`, `note`, required/optional):** Enter any comments or notes regarding your quiz.
* **Submit (`12`, `select_one`, required/optional):** Click "Submit" to complete and submit your quiz.
* **Cancel (`13`, `select_one`, required/optional):** Click "Cancel" to stop and abandon your quiz.
* **Save and Submit (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional):** Click "Save and Submit" to save your quiz for later and submit it.
