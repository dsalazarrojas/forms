## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Site Name or Location** in the field provided. This is the name or description of the location where the aquifer is being assessed.
2. Choose the **Survey Date** when the assessment was conducted.
3. Enter the **Surveyor Name** who conducted the assessment.
4. If available, provide the **GPS Coordinates** of the site in the format of latitude and longitude (e.g., 37.7749° N, 122.4192° W).
5. For **Well Depth (feet)**, enter the depth of the aquifer below the surface.
6. For **Water Level (feet below surface)**, enter the current water level of the aquifer below the surface.
7. Assess the **Water Quality** as:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
8. Check if there are **Signs of Contamination**:
	* None Observed
	* Possible Contamination
	* Definite Contamination
9. If applicable, enter the **Salinity Level Assessment** to describe the salt content of the water.
10. If applicable, enter the **Flow Rate or Yield (gallons per minute)** to describe the rate at which water flows from the aquifer.
11. Enter any **Additional Observations** about the aquifer.
12. Provide **Monitoring Recommendations** for future assessments or maintenance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name or Location**: Enter the name or description of the location where the aquifer is being assessed.
* **Survey Date**: Choose the date when the assessment was conducted.
* **Surveyor Name**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the assessment.
* **GPS Coordinates (if applicable)**: If available, enter the location coordinates in the format of latitude and longitude (e.g., 37.7749° N, 122.4192° W).
* **Aquifer Data Collection**: This field is not used in this form, as it is a note type.
* **Well Depth (feet)**: Enter the depth of the aquifer below the surface.
* **Water Level (feet below surface)**: Enter the current water level of the aquifer below the surface.
* **Water Temperature (Fahrenheit)**: This field is not used in this form, as it is not required.
* **Water Quality Assessment**: Assess the quality of the water as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Signs of Contamination**: Check if there are:
	+ None Observed
	+ Possible Contamination
	+ Definite Contamination
* **Salinity Level Assessment**: If applicable, enter the description of the salt content of the water.
* **Flow Rate or Yield (gallons per minute)**: If applicable, enter the rate at which water flows from the aquifer.
* **Additional Observations**: Enter any other important details or notes about the aquifer.
* **Monitoring Recommendations**: Provide suggestions for future assessments or maintenance.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible with the information provided.
* Use specific units and formatting for measurements (e.g., feet, gallons per minute, etc.).
* If unsure about any field, consult with the surveyor or expert in the field.
