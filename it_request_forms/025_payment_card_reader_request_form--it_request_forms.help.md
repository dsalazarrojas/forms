# Payment Card Reader Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a payment card reader to support the payment card reader program in your location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of reader you need (e.g., Payment Card Reader, Mobile Payment Reader, or Contactless Payment Reader).
2. Choose the specific reader type (e.g., Card Swipe Reader, Contactless Card Reader, or Card Insertion Reader) from the list of options.
3. Select your location (e.g., New York, London, or Chicago).
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your payment card reader.
5. Provide the date and time when the issue occurred.
6. Enter the device ID of the payment card reader.
7. Select the status of your request (e.g., Pending, In Progress, or Resolved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of reader do you have?** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1`): This field allows you to select the type of payment card reader you have (e.g., Payment Card Reader, Mobile Payment Reader, or Contactless Payment Reader).
* **Reader Type** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_reader_type`): This field allows you to choose the specific type of reader you are using (e.g., Card Swipe Reader, Contactless Card Reader, or Card Insertion Reader).
* **Location** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_location`): This field allows you to select your location (e.g., New York, London, or Chicago).
* **Issue Description** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_issue_description`): This field is for you to describe the issue you are experiencing with your payment card reader.
* **Issue Date** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_issue_date`): This field is for you to enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_issue_time`): This field is for you to enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Device ID** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_device_id`): This field is for you to enter the device ID of the payment card reader.
* **Status** (`payment_card_reader_request_form_page_1_status`): This field allows you to select the status of your request (e.g., Pending, In Progress, or Resolved).

## Tips
* Only authorized personnel should use this form to submit requests for payment card readers.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed quickly and efficiently.
* If you are unsure about the status of your request, contact your authorized personnel for assistance.
