# Dictionary Lookup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dictionary Lookup Request Form is a tool for users to request dictionary lookups, allowing them to search for specific terms or words in a dictionary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Dictionary Name" field with the name of the dictionary you would like to search in.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Request Type" field to indicate the type of request you are making.
3. Provide a brief description of your request in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the specific term or word you are searching for in the "Request Term" field.
5. Select the language of the term you are searching for in the "Language" field.
6. Fill in the "Definition" field with the expected outcome or result of the lookup.
7. If necessary, provide additional information or follow-up details in the "Follow-up" field.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dictionary Name** (`dictionary_name`, required): Enter the name of the dictionary you would like to search in.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, required): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a standard lookup, or "No" if you have a specific reason why the standard lookup cannot be fulfilled.
* **Description** (`request_description`, required): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Request Term** (`request_term`, required): Enter the specific term or word you are searching for.
* **Language** (`language`, required): Select the language of the term you are searching for.
* **Definition** (`definition`, required): Enter the expected outcome or result of the lookup.
* **Follow-up** (`request_follow_up`, optional): Provide additional information or follow-up details if necessary.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required): Click this button to send your request.
