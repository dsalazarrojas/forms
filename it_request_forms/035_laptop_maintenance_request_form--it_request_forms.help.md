# Laptop Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laptop Maintenance Request Form is a tool for users to submit information about their laptop maintenance requests. This form ensures that all relevant details are captured accurately, including priority and technician assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Click on the form link to access the form.
2.  Fill out the required fields, marked as "Required" in the legend.
3.  Provide a valid date for the "Request Date", "Scheduled Start Date", and "Scheduled End Date" fields.
4.  Enter your name in the "User Name" field for proper tracking and assignment.
5.  Input your laptop's model and serial number in the "Laptop Model" and "Laptop Serial Number" fields for proper identification.
6.  Describe the issue in the "Issue Description" field to help technicians understand the problem.
7.  Select the priority of the issue by choosing one of the three options (Low, Medium, High) in the "Issue Priority" field. This will determine how urgently the maintenance request is processed.
8.  For the "Status" field, select one or both of the options "Active" and "Inactive" depending on the request's status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date on which you would like the maintenance request to be processed.
* **User Name** (2, text, required): Fill in your name for proper tracking and assignment to a technician.
* **Laptop Model** (3, text, required): Enter your laptop's model for identification purposes.
* **Laptop Serial Number** (4, text, required): Enter your laptop's serial number for proper identification.
* **Issue Description** (5, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing with your laptop.
* **Issue Priority** (6, select_one, required): Select one of the three options (Low, Medium, High) to determine the urgency of the maintenance request.
* **Scheduled Start Date** (7, date, optional): Enter the date on which you would like the maintenance request to start.
* **Scheduled End Date** (8, date, optional): Enter the date on which you would like the maintenance request to end.
* **Assigned Technician** (9, select_one, required): Select a technician from the provided options to be assigned to the maintenance request.
* **Status** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select one or both of the options "Active" and "Inactive" to indicate the status of the maintenance request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure proper processing of your maintenance request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or a technician for assistance.
* Maintenance requests will be processed based on the selected priority level.
* Please note that this form is intended for internal use only; do not share with external parties.
