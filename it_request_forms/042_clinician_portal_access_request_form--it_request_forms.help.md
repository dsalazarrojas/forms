# Clinician Portal Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The clinician portal access request form is used to request access to the clinician portal and related systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Ensure you have a valid reason for requesting access.
* Select the correct type of access you are requesting (e.g. New Access, Additional Access, Access Modification, Access Renewal, Temporary Access, or Access Termination).
* Choose the specific systems or applications you are requesting access to (e.g. Electronic Health Records, Patient Portal, Scheduling System, etc.).
* Select the level of access you require (e.g. Read Only, Read and Write, Full Access, Administrative, or Custom).
* Choose the patient population you would like access to (e.g. All Patients, My Patients Only, Department Patients, Specific Panel, or Other).
* Provide a brief justification for your access request.
* Select the start date you would like your access to begin.
* Select the end date of your access (if temporary).
* Choose the urgency of your request (e.g. Routine, Urgent, or Critical).
* Confirm that you have completed the required training and certifications (HIPAA and Security Awareness).
* Confirm that you have obtained the necessary approvals from your manager and the IT department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date**: The date you are requesting access to begin.
* **First Name**: Your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Your full last name.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee ID number (if applicable).
* **Work Email**: Your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your work phone number.
* **Department**: The department you work in (e.g. Emergency, ICU, Medical-Surgical, etc.).
* **Your Role**: Your position or job title (e.g. Physician, Nurse Practitioner, etc.).
* **Manager or Supervisor Name**: The name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Email**: The email address of your manager or supervisor.
* **Type of Access Requested**: The type of access you are requesting (e.g. New Access, Additional Access, Access Modification, etc.).
* **Systems or Applications**: Select all the systems or applications you would like access to (e.g. Electronic Health Records, Patient Portal, Scheduling System, etc.).
* **Access Level Needed**: The level of access you require (e.g. Read Only, Read and Write, Full Access, etc.).
* **Patient Population Access Needed**: The patient population you would like access to (e.g. All Patients, My Patients Only, Department Patients, etc.).
* **Business Justification for Access**: A brief explanation of why you need this access.
* **Access Start Date Needed**: The date you would like your access to begin.
* **Access End Date**: The date your access will end (if temporary).
* **Request Urgency**: The level of urgency of your request (e.g. Routine, Urgent, Critical).
* **HIPAA Training Completed**: A confirmation that you have completed the required HIPAA training.
* **Security Training Completed**: A confirmation that you have completed the required Security Awareness training.
* **Confidentiality Agreement Signed**: A confirmation that you have signed the confidentiality agreement.
* **Have You Had Portal Access Before**: A confirmation of whether you have had portal access before.
* **Additional Notes**: Any other relevant information about your request.
* **Requester Signature**: Your full name.
* **Date**: The date you sign this form.
* **Manager Approval**: A confirmation of your manager's approval.
* **Manager Signature**: Your manager's full name.
* **IT Approval**: A confirmation of the IT department's approval.
* **IT Signature**: The IT department's full name.
