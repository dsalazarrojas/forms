# AdBook Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit support requests for various issues related to AdBook. It helps our support team understand your concerns and provide timely resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name, company/organization name, and contact email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the category of your issue (e.g., Account Access / Password, Campaign Management, etc.).
3. Indicate the urgency level of your issue (e.g., Low, Medium, High, or Emergency).
4. Provide a brief subject/issue title and a detailed description of the problem.
5. If applicable, include the related campaign ID/name and your operating system/browser version.
6. If you have screenshots to share, select "True". 
7. Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or In-App Chat).
8. Finally, provide a date for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Company / Organization** (`company_org`, `text`, required): Enter your company or organization name if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Support Category** (`issue_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes your issue (e.g., Account Access / Password, Campaign Management, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency level of your issue (e.g., Low - Question / General Inquiry, Medium - Workflow Impeded, etc.).
* **Brief Subject / Issue Title** (`subject_line`, `text`, required): Provide a concise title describing your issue.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (`detailed_description`, `text`, required): Describe the issue in detail, including steps to reproduce if it's a bug.
* **Related Campaign ID / Name** (`ad_campaign_id`, `text`, optional): If your issue is related to a specific campaign, enter the campaign ID or name.
* **Operating System and Browser** (`os_browser`, `text`, optional): Mention your operating system and browser version (e.g., Windows 11 - Chrome).
* **Related Previous Ticket Number** (`previous_tickets`, `text`, optional): If this is a follow-up issue, enter the previous ticket number.
* **Do you have screenshots to provide?** (`screenshot_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you have screenshots to share.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In-App Chat).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_timestamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
