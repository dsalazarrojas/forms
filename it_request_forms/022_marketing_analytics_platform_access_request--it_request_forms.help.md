# Marketing Analytics Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to request access to the marketing analytics platform. To use this form, you must be a valid user and have a legitimate reason for requesting access.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Name and Email address accurately and completely.
2. Select your Department from the options provided.
3. Choose a Request Start Date and Request End Date for the access period.
4. Enter a brief Request Reason to explain why you need access to the platform.
5. Select one of the Approved By options (John, Jane, or Joe) to indicate who will be approving your request.
6. Choose the Request Status (Pending, Approved, or Denied) and select all applicable statuses.
7. You can leave comments for the approver in the Comments field if necessary.
8. Finally, click Submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your account.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of options to help us understand your role and team.
* **Request Start Date**: Choose the start date for the requested access period. This should be the earliest date you need access from.
* **Request End Date**: Choose the end date for the requested access period. This should be the latest date you need access until.
* **Request Reason**: Briefly explain why you need access to the platform. Be specific about your goals and objectives.
* **Approved By**: Select who will be approving your request from the options provided. This will help us understand who to notify about your request.
* **Request Status**: Select all statuses that apply to your request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied). This will help us track your request's progress.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or information for the approver in this field.
* **Comments (continued)**: If you need to add more comments, continue typing here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting access to the platform.
* Double-check the information provided before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
* Be specific and clear when requesting access for a specific time period.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the approver directly.
