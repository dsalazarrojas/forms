# Digital Learning Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Learning Resource Request Form is used to submit new requests for digital learning resources. This form helps to gather essential information to evaluate and process the request internally.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information.
2. Review the form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Request** (<id: 1>): A brief description of the new request.
* **Your Name** (<id: 2>): Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID or as you would like to be known.
* **Email Address** (<id: 3>): Enter your official email address associated with your work or university account.
* **Department** (<id: 4>): Select the department or area you belong to.
* **Description** (<id: 5>): Provide a detailed description of the digital learning resource you are requesting.
* **Tool** (<id: 6>): Indicate whether the resource is a tool or not.
* **Priority** (<id: 7>): Select whether the resource is of high or low priority.
* **Date** (<id: 8>): Enter the date you are requesting the resource for.
* **Time** (<id: 9>): Specify the time you are expecting the resource to be available.
* **Submitter** (<id: 10>): Enter your official name as it appears on your official ID or as you would like to be known.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the fields correctly and accurately.
* Review your submission before clicking the submit button to ensure everything is complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the internal support team for clarification.
* Please note that incomplete or inaccurate submissions may delay the processing of your request.
