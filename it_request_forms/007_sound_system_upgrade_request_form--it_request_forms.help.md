# Sound System Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound System Upgrade Request Form is used to submit a request to upgrade or replace your sound system.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the following information:
	+ Select the type of audio equipment you would like to upgrade (e.g., Speaker, Microphone, Headphones).
	+ Indicate the current status of your audio equipment (e.g., Available, Outdated, Broken).
	+ Provide a brief explanation for upgrading your audio equipment.
	+ If you have a budget in mind, please specify it.
	+ Fill out your contact information (name, email, and/or phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audio Equipment** (select_one):
    - This field allows you to choose the type of audio equipment you would like to upgrade. 
* **Current Status** (select_multiple):
    - This field helps us understand your audio equipment's current state. Select all that apply.
* **Reason for Upgrading** (text):
    - This is a brief explanation for why you are requesting an upgrade.
* **Budget** (number):
    - If you have a specific budget in mind, please provide it here.
* **Contact Information** (text):
    - Please provide your name, email address, or phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Email** (email):
    - This is your email address where we can reach out to you for further discussion or updates.
* **Phone Number** (text):
    - This is your phone number where we can contact you for further information.
* **Submitter** (text):
    - Please enter your name so we can identify you as the submitter of this request.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form to help us better understand your needs.
- If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- We will review your request and be in touch with you via the contact information you provided.
