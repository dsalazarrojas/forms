# Referral Service Login Assistance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Service Login Assistance Request form is designed to help individuals resolve issues with their referral service logins. If you are experiencing difficulties with accessing your referral account, such as forgotten passwords or account lockouts, this form is intended to guide you through the process of resolving your issue.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Describe your issue with the referral platform in the "Describe your issue with the referral platform" field.
2. Select the referral platform you are experiencing difficulties with (e.g., Google Referral Platform, Microsoft Referral Platform, or Other).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the date and time of when the issue occurred in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. If necessary, provide any additional information about the issue in the "Additional Information" field.
7. Add any comments about the issue in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Describe your issue with the referral platform**: Enter a brief description of the problem you are experiencing with your referral account, such as "I forgot my password" or "I am locked out of my account".
* **Referral Platform**: Select the type of referral platform you are experiencing difficulties with, such as Google Referral Platform, Microsoft Referral Platform, or Other.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to communicate with you regarding the status of your issue.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number, which will be used to contact you if further information is required to resolve the issue.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional information about the issue, please provide it here.
* **Note**: Add any comments or thoughts about the issue, which will be helpful for our team to better understand the problem.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue to ensure that our team can provide the best assistance.
* If you are experiencing recurring issues, please provide as much information as possible about the issues you have experienced.
* If you have any additional information or concerns that you would like to share with our team, please use the "Additional Information" field to provide it.
