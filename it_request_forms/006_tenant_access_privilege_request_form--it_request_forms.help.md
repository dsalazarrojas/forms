# Tenant Access Privilege Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access privileges for a tenant. It's intended to be used when a user wants to access a tenant's information or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select one requester**: Choose the type of person requesting access (Tenant, Owner, Manager, or Admin).
2. Fill in the **Access Privilege**, **Request Date**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, **Privilege Level**, **Request Status**, **Comments**, **Requester Name**, **Tenant Name**, **Reason**, and **Notes** fields as necessary.
3. Select the **Access Privilege** and **Privilege Level** options from the dropdown menus.
4. **Submit the form** to send the request to the approver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select one requester**: This field allows you to choose the type of person requesting access (Tenant, Owner, Manager, or Admin).
* **Access Privilege**: Choose the level of access you need (Full Access, Limited Access, or View Only).
* **Request Date**: Enter the date when you want to start requesting access.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you want to start using the requested access.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you want to stop using the requested access.
* **Privilege Level**: Select the level of privilege you're requesting (Full Access, Limited Access, or View Only).
* **Request Status**: Choose the status of your request (New, Approved, Rejected, or Completed).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your request.
* **Requester Name**: Enter your name for reference.
* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant you're requesting access for.
* **Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for your request.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the person who will approve your request.
* **Approver Email**: Enter the email of the person who will approve your request.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will approve your request.
* **Approver Notes**: Add any additional notes for the approver.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name and email for reference.
* **Approver**: Enter the name of the person who will approve your request.
