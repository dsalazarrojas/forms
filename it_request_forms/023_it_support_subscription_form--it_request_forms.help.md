# it_support_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_support_subscription_form is designed to collect general information and support requests from users. It helps us understand your needs and preferences, and enables us to provide you with the best possible support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your general information.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of your support request in the "Support Request" field.
3. Choose the type of request you are submitting (e.g., "True" or "False").
4. (Optional) Select multiple categories for your request if applicable.
5. Specify your preferred date and time for assistance.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Support Request (2)** (`text`, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of your support request. Please include any necessary details about your issue or inquiry.
* **Contact Information (3)** (`text`, optional): Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you about your support request.
* **Request Type (4)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of request you are submitting (e.g., "True" or "False"). This helps us understand the nature of your request.
* **Selected Multiple (5)** (`select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, select multiple categories for your request. This helps us tailor our support to your specific needs.
* **Date (6)** (`date`, optional): Specify your preferred date for assistance.
* **Time (7)** (`time`, optional): Specify your preferred time for assistance.
* **Note (8)** (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (10)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Clearly and concisely describe your support request in the "Support Request" field.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact us through other means (e.g., phone, email) for assistance.
