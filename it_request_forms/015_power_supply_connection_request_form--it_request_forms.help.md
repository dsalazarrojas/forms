# Power Supply Connection Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Power Supply Connection Request Form is used to collect information from users about their power supply connection requests. Please fill out this form to submit your request and a member of our team will review and process it.

## How To Complete This Form

### Please select a connection ID from the drop-down menu.
1. Click on the "Connection ID" field and select the corresponding ID from the list.
2. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
4. Enter the location of the power supply connection in the "Connection Location" field.
5. Select the type of power supply from the "Power Supply Type" field.
6. Choose the date and time for the power connection.
7. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter any comments you have for the submitter in the "Submitter Comments" field.
9. Enter the name of the approver in the "Approver Name" field.
10. Enter any comments for the approver in the "Approver Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Connection ID**: Select one of the available connection IDs from the drop-down menu.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Connection Location**: Enter the location of the power supply connection.
* **Power Supply Type**: Select the type of power supply you need (AC, DC, or DC/AC).
* **Power Connection Date**: Enter the date for the power connection.
* **Power Connection Time**: Enter the time for the power connection.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the request.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any comments you have for the submitter.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the person approving the request.
* **Approver Comments**: Enter any comments for the approver.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
