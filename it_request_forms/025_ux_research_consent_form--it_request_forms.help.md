# UX Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users regarding their participation in UX research, including the expected duration and frequency of their involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the information being asked.
2. Fill out the form with your information, making sure to provide accurate details about your participation.
3. Submit the form to confirm your interest and details of participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: A brief text box where you can provide any necessary information about your participation in the UX research.
 
### Participant Information
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Please enter your name as it should be recorded for research purposes.
 
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Please enter your email address as it will be used for communication purposes.
 
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: If you have a phone number where you can be reached, please enter it here. This is not required.
 
* **Participant Agreement (research_participant_agree)**: Please confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of your participation in the research.
 
* **Reason for Participation (research_participant_reason)**: If there is a reason you're interested in participating, please explain it briefly here. This is not required but can be helpful for the research team.
 
### Research Details
* **Expected Participation Duration (research_participant_duration)**: Estimated length of time you are willing to participate.
 
* **Expected Start Date (research_participant_start_date)**: When you would like to start participating.
 
* **Expected End Date (research_participant_end_date)**: When you would like to end participating.
 
* **Expected Participation Frequency (research_participant_frequency)**: How often you are willing to participate per week.
 
Note that some fields are optional and only required for the research team to better understand your participation. Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible, and do not hesitate to contact the research team if you have any questions.
