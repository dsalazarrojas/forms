# Document Scanner App Download Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Scanner App Download Request form is used to collect information from users requesting a download of the Document Scanner App. This form helps our team to fulfill the download request efficiently and prevent duplication of requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide any additional comments you'd like to include with your request. Please make sure to enter your information accurately and clearly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **document_scanner_app_download_request_form**
* This field is the main title of the form. You don't need to fill this field, as it's a read-only field.
* Please ignore this field and proceed to the next sections.

### 2. **user_info**
* Please enter a brief description of your request. This can include any information that might be helpful for our team to process your request.

### 3. **user_email**
* Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your download request.
* Please make sure to enter a valid email address.

### 4. **user_phone**
* This field is optional, but you can enter your phone number if you'd like us to call you back.
* Please note that we might not be able to call you back due to time zone or other technical limitations.

### 5. **app_type**
* Select the type of app you're requesting (Windows, Mac, Web App, or Mobile). If you're unsure which type is correct, please contact our support team.
* This field is required to ensure that we deliver the correct app version for your device.

### 6. **company**
* This field is optional, but please fill it in if you're requesting the app for company use.
* This will help our team to prioritize your request and ensure that we have the right information to process it efficiently.

### 7. **team**
* This field is optional, and please fill it in if you're requesting the app for a team or department.
* This will help us to understand the scale of your request and assign the right resources to it.

### 8. **request_date**
* Please select the date you're requesting the app for. This will help us to prioritize your request and ensure that we deliver it within the required timeframe.
* Please note that this date is not a deadline, and we'll do our best to deliver the app as soon as possible.

### 9. **comments**
* If you have any additional comments or information to provide with your request, please enter them here.
* This will help our team to better understand your requirements and provide you with a more accurate solution.
* This field is optional but recommended for complex or specific requests.
