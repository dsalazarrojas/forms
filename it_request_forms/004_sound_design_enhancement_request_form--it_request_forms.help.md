# Sound Design Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Design Enhancement Request Form is a tool used to gather information for sound design enhancement requests. It is designed to help us understand your needs and requirements for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your brief description of the sound design enhancement request in the "Sound Design Brief" field.
2. Indicate your references for further information or context, selecting all relevant options in the "References" field.
3. Choose the priority level of your request, selecting one of the three options: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
4. Select the audio format required for your project, choosing one of the three options: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
5. Provide more details about the audio you are targeting with this enhancement request in the "Target Audio" field.
6. Set a deadline for the completion of the requested enhancement, using the calendar date picker.
7. If applicable, specify a budget for the project in the "Budget" field.
8. Fill in your contact information, including any relevant details that would help us get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_submission** (text, required: false): This field is where you can briefly describe your sound design enhancement request. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of what you are looking for.
* **sound_design_brief** (text, required: false): In this field, please provide a more detailed description of your sound design enhancement request. Try to be as thorough as possible, including any relevant information or context that might help us understand your needs.
* **references** (select_multiple, required: false): Please select all relevant references that provide more information or context for your sound design enhancement request. This could be links, documents, or any other type of resource that might be helpful for us to understand your needs.
* **priority** (select_one, required: false): Choose the priority level of your sound design enhancement request, selecting one of the three options: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3". This will help us understand the urgency and importance of your request.
* **audio_format** (select_one, required: false): Select the audio format required for your project, choosing one of the three options: "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **target_audio** (text, required: false): In this field, provide more details about the audio you are targeting with this enhancement request. This could be a specific type of audio, a file name, or any other relevant information.
* **deadline** (date, required: false): Set a deadline for the completion of the requested enhancement, using the calendar date picker. If there is no specific deadline, please leave this field blank.
* **budget** (number, required: false): If applicable, specify a budget for the project in this field. Please note that this is an optional field and not required for all requests.
* **contact_info** (text, required: false): Fill in your contact information, including any relevant details that would help us get in touch with you. This could be your email, phone number, or any other contact method.
