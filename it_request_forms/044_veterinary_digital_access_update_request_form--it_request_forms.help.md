# Veterinary Digital Access Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the access levels of existing digital access to sensitive information within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the staff member whose access level you wish to update.
2. Choose the new role for the staff member.
3. Select the new access level for the staff member.
4. Provide a brief reason for the change.
5. Enter the staff member's current access level.
6. Enter the staff member's new access level.
7. Enter the submitter's name.
8. Enter the submitter's email address.
9. Enter the submitter's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, select_one, required/optional): Select the staff member whose access level you wish to update.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the new role for the staff member.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable levels for the new access level.
* **Reason For Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason for the change.
* **Current Access Level** (`current_access_level`, select_one, required/optional): Enter the staff member's current access level.
* **New Access Level** (`new_access_level`, select_one, required/optional): Enter the staff member's new access level.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure you are only updating the access levels of staff members who are eligible for changes.
* If the reason for the change is not clearly described, it may be denied.
* If a staff member's access level is changed, they will be informed of the change.
