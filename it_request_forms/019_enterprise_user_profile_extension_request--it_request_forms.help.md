# Enterprise User Profile Extension Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension of an existing user profile. The user profile extension is necessary when an employee's current profile does not accurately reflect their new role, position, or job title.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with your new details and submit it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of the changes you want to make to your user profile.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Select "Approve" to confirm your request, "Reject" to deny the request, or "Review" to request more information before making a decision.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person who will be approving your request.
