# Contact Support Team - Help Guide
## Purpose
The contact support team form is used to submit requests for help and support from our IT team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Requester name** (`Requester name`): Enter the full name of the person requesting support.
	* **Requester email** (`Requester email`): Enter the email address where you can be reached for updates.
	* **Issue Summary** (`Issue summary`): Briefly describe the problem you're experiencing.
	* **Issue severity** (`Issue severity`): Choose how critical the issue is:
		+ Low
		+ Medium
		+ High
		+ Critical
2. Optionally, add any additional information or context that will help us quickly resolve your issue:
	* **Department** (`Department`): Identify the department or team responsible for the issue.
	* **Affected Systems/Applications** (`Affected systems`): List any systems or applications affected.
	* **Steps to Reproduce** (`Steps to reproduce`): If your issue is reproducible, please provide step-by-step instructions.
	* **Attach logs or screenshots** (`Attach logs or screenshots via email`): For large files or sensitive information, attach them via email instead of uploading directly.
3. If you're open to alternative contact methods, specify them:
	* **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred contact method`): Choose how you'd like our support team to communicate with you:
		+ Email
		+ Phone
4. If you have an urgent issue, provide a phone number:
	* **Contact Phone** (`Contact phone`): Enter a phone number for emergency contact, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- IT Support Request --** (`header`): This is the header for the form. It's not a field to fill in, but rather a title to indicate the purpose of the form.
* **Requester name** (`Requester name`): Enter the full name of the person submitting the request.
* **Requester email** (`Requester email`): Enter the email address where you can be reached for updates.
* **Department** (`Department`): If you're unsure which department is responsible for your issue, enter the department here.
* **Issue summary** (`Issue summary`): Briefly describe the problem you're experiencing. Be as detailed as possible so we can quickly understand the issue.
* **Issue severity** (`Issue severity`): Choose the level of criticalness of the issue:
	+ Low: Not critical, but would like some guidance.
	+ Medium: Critical, but not blocking business operations.
	+ High: Critical, affecting business operations.
	+ Critical: Emergency, affecting critical business operations.
* **Affected systems** (`Affected systems`): List any systems or applications affected by the issue.
* **Steps to reproduce** (`Steps to reproduce`): If your issue is reproducible, provide step-by-step instructions so we can easily replicate the issue.
* **Attach logs or screenshots** (`Attach logs or screenshots via email`): For large files or sensitive information, use email for attachment instead of uploading directly.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred contact method`): Choose how you'd like our support team to communicate with you:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`): Enter a phone number for emergency contact, if needed.
* **Additional information** (`Additional information`): Provide any other relevant information that will help us quickly resolve your issue.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the form to help us quickly understand your issue.
* If you're unsure which department is responsible for your issue, don't worry! We'll direct your request to the right team.
* If you have large files or sensitive information to share, use email for attachment.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, feel free to ask!
