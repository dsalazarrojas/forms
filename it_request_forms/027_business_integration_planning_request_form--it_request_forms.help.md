# Business Integration Planning Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to plan and prioritize business integration requests. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your request is properly reviewed and scheduled.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page to navigate through the form sections.
- Fill out each field completely, including text boxes and select options.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **-- Integration Planning Request --** (Page 1: Header):
	* This page is a header section and a placeholder.
	* Please proceed to the next page to start completing the form.

- **Requester name** (Page 2):
	* Please enter your name or the name of the person requesting integration support.
	* This field is required.

- **Department** (Page 2):
	* If applicable, enter the department or team related to your request.
	* This field is not required but can help us better understand your context.

- **Contact email** (Page 2):
	* Enter your contact email address for scheduling and any further clarifications.
	* This field is required.

- **Systems involved** (Page 2):
	* List the systems that require integration in this text box.
	* This field is required.

- **Strategic goals** (Page 2):
	* Briefly describe the high-level goals for the integration.
	* This field is not required.

- **Expected timeline** (Page 2):
	* Estimate the expected timeline for the integration in weeks or months.
	* This field is not required.

- **Budget approval** (Page 3):
	* Select 'True' if budget approval is already obtained for this work.
	* Select 'False' otherwise.
	* This field is not required.

- **Priority level** (Page 3):
	* Select the urgency level for this request:
		+ Low: Less urgent request.
		+ Medium: Medium priority request.
		+ High: High priority request.
	* This field is required.

- **Additional notes** (Page 3):
	* Enter any other relevant information or comments about your request.
	* This field is not required.

## Tips
- Double-check your form submission before clicking the submit button to ensure all required fields are completed.
- Be concise and clear in your descriptions to help us better understand your request.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact the support team after submitting the form.
