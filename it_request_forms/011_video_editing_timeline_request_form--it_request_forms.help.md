# video_editing_timeline_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and organize project details for video editing tasks. It includes fields for project descriptions, deadlines, files, deadline dates, times, and notes. This form is intended to streamline project management and video editing processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information about your project before filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the "Project Details" field with a brief description of your project.
3.  In the "Deadlines" field, list the important deadlines associated with your project.
4.  In the "Files" field, attach any relevant files related to your project.
5.  Enter the deadline date in the "Deadline Date" field.
6.  Enter the deadline time in the "Deadline Time" field.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments in the "Deadline Note" field.
8.  Provide any project-specific notes in the "Project Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (Project Details)**: A text field for you to describe your project in a few words.
* **Deadlines (Deadlines)**: A text field for you to list the important deadlines associated with your project.
* **Files (Files)**: A text field for you to attach relevant files related to your project.
* **Deadline Date (Deadline Date)**: A date field for you to enter the deadline date of your project.
* **Deadline Time (Deadline Time)**: A time field for you to enter the deadline time of your project.
* **Deadline Note (Deadline Note)**: A note field for you to add any additional notes or comments about your deadline.
* **Project Note (Project Note)**: A note field for you to add any project-specific notes.
*   **Note:** This form is a general-purpose project management tool and may not cover all your specific needs. Be sure to check with your project manager or team lead if you need to add any custom fields or have any specific requirements.
*   **Note:** If you need to edit your submission, please contact the form administrator for assistance. They will be happy to help you with any updates or changes.
