# Industrial Communication Setup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect details about industrial communication setup requests. Fill out this form to inform us about your specific needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing accurate information.
2. Fill in the fields with the required information for your request.
3. Submit the form once all fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is your request about

* **What is your request about** (`industrial_communication_setup_request_form_1`, text, optional): Briefly describe the nature of your industrial communication setup request.

### 2. Location

* **location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location related to your industrial communication setup request.

### 3. Equipment ID

* **equipment id** (`equipment_id`, number, optional): Enter the equipment ID number relevant to your request.

### 4. Equipment Type

* **equipment type** (`equipment_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of equipment related to your industrial communication setup request.

### 5. Communication Method

* **communication method** (`communication_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method you would like to use for communication. Options are:
	+ Fiber Optic
	+ Wired Ethernet
	+ Wireless

### 6. Connection Type

* **connection type** (`connection_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the connection type you would like to use. Options are:
	+ Wired
	+ Wireless

### 7. Date of Request

* **date of request** (`date_of_request`, date, optional): Enter the date of your request in the format YYYY-MM-dd.

### 8. Time of Request

* **time of request** (`time_of_request`, time, optional): Enter the time of your request in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".

### 9. Notes

* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding your industrial communication setup request.

### 10. Email

* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### 11. Phone

* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Tips

- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential issues with processing your request.
- Double-check for any formatting or syntax errors before submitting the form.
