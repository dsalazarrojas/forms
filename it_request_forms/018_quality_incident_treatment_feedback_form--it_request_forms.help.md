# Quality Incident Treatment Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for end users to provide feedback on the quality incident that was treated. Please ensure that you are providing feedback for a specific incident by checking that you have not already submitted feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct **incident_type** for the incident that was treated. This will help the quality team to understand the severity of the incident.
2. Enter the correct **incident_date** when the incident occurred.
3. If relevant, select the correct **incident_details** about the incident that happened.
4. Provide the details of the treatment that was applied to the incident.
5. Give your overall **feedback** about the treatment of the incident.
6. Select the correct **quality_incident** to ensure that your feedback is directed at the right incident.
7. Choose the person the incident was **assigned_to** for proper follow-up.
8. If you have any additional comments about the incident, enter them in the **comments** field.
9. Attach any relevant files or documentation that support your feedback in the **attachment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of incident that occurred, such as Critical, Major, or Minor.
* **incident_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **incident_details** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional details about the incident that are not captured in the other fields.
* **treatment** (Note, Optional): Describe the actions taken or treatments applied to resolve the incident.
* **feedback** (Note, Optional): Give your overall feedback about the treatment of the incident.
* **quality_incident** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the quality incident that you are providing feedback on. This ensures that your feedback is directed at the correct incident.
* **assigned_to** (Select One, Optional): Select the person the incident was assigned to for proper follow-up.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **attachment** (Text, Optional): Attach any relevant files or documentation that support your feedback.
