# Tech Conference AV Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from presenters about their audio-visual (AV) equipment requirements for the Tech Conference. Please fill out this form to ensure that we can provide the necessary equipment and support for your presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided all the necessary information.
3. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presenter Details** (`presenter_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, title, and any other relevant contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or requests.
* **Presenter** (`presenter`, text, optional): Enter your name as it will be displayed on the conference schedule and materials.
* **Equipment Request** (`equipment_request`, select multiple, optional): Check all the equipment you require for your presentation, such as microphones, projectors, or laptops.
* **Conference Setup** (`conference_setup`, select one, required): Choose the type of conference setup that best suits your needs, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the equipment you require, such as specific brand or model numbers.
* **Technical Support** (`technical_support`, select multiple, optional): Check if you need technical support during your presentation, such as AV technician or IT staff assistance.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the event, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or requests.
* **Presenter AV Requirements** (`presenter_av_requirements`, time, optional): Provide a specific time for your presentation and/or equipment setup.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise noted. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
