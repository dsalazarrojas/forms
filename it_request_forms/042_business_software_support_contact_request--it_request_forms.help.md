# Business Software Support Contact Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather detailed information from users to efficiently assist with their business software support requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name, email address, and contact number (if you want to be contacted by phone).
2. Select the correct license type you have for the software.
3. Identify the type of issue you are experiencing, its category, and its priority level.
4. Describe the issue in detail, including any error messages or steps to reproduce the problem.
5. If necessary, provide information about your environment, such as your operating system and browser used.
6. If you're experiencing issues with specific modules or features, list them.
7. Choose how you prefer to be contacted (email, phone, video call, or chat).
8. Attach any relevant documentation or screenshots if possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Your contact number, if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Your organization's name.
* **Product or Software Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Identify which product or software you are contacting about.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, options: Free, Starter, Professional, Enterprise, Trial, Other, optional): Select the type of license you have for the software.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, options: Technical Bug, Feature Request, Account Issue, Billing Question, Password Reset, Integration Help, Performance Issue, Other, required): Choose the type of problem you are experiencing.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, options: Low, Medium, High, Critical, required): Indicate the urgency of the issue.
* **Detailed Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the problem in as much detail as possible, including any error messages or steps to reproduce the issue.
* **Error Message or Code** (`error_message`, text, optional): If you've encountered any error messages, provide them here.
* **Steps to Reproduce the Issue** (`steps_to_reproduce`, text, optional): Describe how to reproduce the issue, if possible.
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`, text, optional): Provide information about your operating system.
* **Browser Used** (`browser_used`, text, optional): Identify which browser you are using.
* **Affected Modules or Features** (`affected_modules`, text, optional): List which parts of the software are affected.
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, text, optional): Explain how the issue affects your operations.
* **Number of Users Affected** (`number_of_users_affected`, number, optional): If possible, provide the number of people affected.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, options: Email, Phone, Video Call, Chat, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted.
* **Attachments Available** (`attachments_available`, select_one, options: True, False, optional): Decide if you have documentation or screenshots to share.
