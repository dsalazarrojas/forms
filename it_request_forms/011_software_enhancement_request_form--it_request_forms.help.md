# Software Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Enhancement Request Form is a tool for users to submit suggestions and ideas for improving the software. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences to deliver better software experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a software enhancement request, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required and optional fields on each page.
2. For each field, make sure to provide clear and specific information to help us understand your request.
3. Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Software Enhancement Request Form** (text, required): Enter a brief summary of your enhancement request.
- **Customer Information** (text, optional): Provide any relevant customer contact information or details.
- **Details** (text, optional): Describe the enhancement you are requesting in more detail.
- **Product Information** (text, optional): Specify the product you are requesting the enhancement for.
- **Product Information 2** (text, optional): Provide additional product information if needed.
- **Request Type** (select multiple, optional): Indicate the type of request you are making (e.g., 'Yes' for a new feature, 'No' for a bug report).
- **Request Description** (text, optional): Describe the specific issue or feature you are requesting.
- **Priority** (select one, optional): Indicate the priority level of your request (e.g., 'Yes' for high priority, 'No' for low priority).
- **Status** (select one, optional): Indicate the current status of your request (e.g., 'Active' for a pending request, 'Inactive' for a closed request).
- **Resolution** (text, optional): Provide any resolution or solution you have found for the issue, if applicable).

## Tips
- Be specific and clear in your requests to help us understand and address your needs efficiently.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
- We appreciate your input and will review each request carefully to ensure we deliver the best possible software experience for you.
