# Physical Access Security Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Access Security Request Form is used to request physical access to a facility or location within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information, including your name and ID number.
2. Provide facility access information, including the location and reason for access.
3. Select the access level required for your access request (High or Low).
4. Choose your approver's name and email address.
5. Provide your approver's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (Employee ID Number, Employee Name, Employee Title): 
  Enter your employee ID number, name, and title to identify yourself.
  
- **Facility Access Information** (Facility Name, Access Reason): 
  Enter the facility name and reason for access to provide context for your approver.
  
- **Reason for Access** (Reason for Access): 
  Enter a clear and concise reason for your access request.
  This field is required to ensure that your request is properly reviewed and approved.
  
- **Access Start Time** (Start Time): 
  Enter the start time of your requested access period.
  If you are unsure of the start time, leave it blank.
  
- **End Time** (End Time): 
  Enter the end time of your requested access period.
  If you are unsure of the end time, leave it blank.
  
- **Duration** (Duration): 
  Enter the duration of your requested access period in hours.
  This field is optional and may not be applicable to your request.
  
- **Location** (Facility Location): 
  Enter the location within the facility where you will be accessing.
  This field is required to ensure that your approver knows where to find you.
  
- **Access Level** (Access Level): 
  Select High or Low level of access required for your request.
  Choose the level that best fits your needs.
  
- **Comments** (Comments): 
  Enter any additional comments or information about your access request.
  This field is optional.
  
- **Approver Name** (Approver's Name): 
  Select your approver's name from the list of designated approvers.
  Your approver will review and approve your request.
  
- **Approver Email** (Approver's Email): 
  Enter your approver's email address.
  This field is required for your approver to contact you.
  
- **Approver Phone** (Approver's Phone): 
  Enter your approver's phone number.

- **Approver Comments** (Approver's Comments):

- **Approver Date** (Approver's Date):

  This field is optional and may not be applicable.

  This field is optional and may not be applicable.
  
- **Approver Signature** (Approver's Signature):
