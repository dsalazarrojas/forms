# restaurant_pos_installation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant POS installation form is designed to collect all necessary information for installing a restaurant POS system. This form serves as a comprehensive guide for the installer or a template for the restaurant owner to fill out before the installation process begins.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first and last name in the first_name and last_name fields.
2.  Enter the company name and address in the company and company\_address fields.
3.  Add your contact information in the contact\_phone, contact\_email, and contact\_email2 fields.
4.  Provide restaurant information in the restaurant\_name and location fields.
5.  Select the POS system being installed from the options in the pos\_system field.
6.  Schedule the installation date and time in the installation\_date and installation\_time fields.
7.  List the equipment to be installed in the equipment\_list, equipment\_list2, and equipment\_list3 fields.
8.  Enter additional notes in the restaurant\_pos\_installation\_form\_note and restaurant\_pos\_installation\_form\_note2 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or restaurant.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the company or restaurant.
*   **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the restaurant.
*   **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the restaurant.
*   **Date** (`installation_date`, `date`, required): Schedule the installation date.
*   **Time** (`installation_time`, `time`, required): Schedule the installation time.
*   **POS System** (`pos_system`, `select_one`, required): Select the POS system being installed.
*   **Restaurant Hours** (`restaurant_hours`, `text`, required): Enter the restaurant's operational hours.
*   **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, `text`, required): List the equipment to be installed.
*   **Additional Equipment** (`additional_equipment`, `text`, required): List any additional equipment needed.
*   **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location for the installation.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone2`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number for the contact person.
*   **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email2`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email for the contact person.
*   **Company 2** (`company2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or restaurant for the contact person.
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address for the contact person.
*   **Equipment List 2** (`equipment_list2`, `text`, required): List any additional equipment needed for the contact person.
*   **Notes** (`restaurant_pos_installation_form_note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
*   **Notes 2** (`restaurant_pos_installation_form_note2`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
*   **Equipment List 3** (`equipment_list3`, `text`, required): List any additional equipment needed.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all information before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the installation team for assistance.
