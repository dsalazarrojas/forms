# Chat Message Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chat Message Submission Form is a tool for end-users to submit messages or inquires related to technical support, billing, or other issues related to products or services. This form is designed to gather essential information to ensure a timely and effective response from the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your sender's information (Your Name, Email Address, and optional Phone Number).
2. Select the type of message you are submitting (Type of Message, Priority Level, and Product or Service Referenced).
3. Enter the detailed message or inquiry (Message Content).
4. Specify how you would like to be contacted (Preferred Contact Method, Best Time to Contact).
5. Optionally, attach any relevant screenshots or files.
6. Finally, confirm your submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (sender_name)**: Enter your full name as the sender of this message.
* **Email Address (sender_email)**: Enter your email address for response communication.
* **Phone Number (sender_phone)**: Enter your phone number for optional contact.
* **Customer ID (customer_id)**: If you have a customer ID, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Type of Message (message_type)**: Select the type of message you are submitting (Technical Support, Billing Inquiry, Feature Request, Bug Report, General Question, Complaint, or Other).
* **Subject (subject)**: Enter a brief subject line for your message.
* **Message Content (message_content)**: Enter a detailed message or inquiry.
* **Priority Level (priority)**: Select the urgency level of your message (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
* **Product or Service Referenced (product_service)**: If the message is related to a specific product or service, enter the name here.
* **Error Message or Code (error_message)**: If applicable, enter any error message or code related to your inquiry.
* **Steps Taken (steps_taken)**: Describe any steps you have already taken to resolve the issue.
* **Attach Screenshot or File (screenshot_attached)**: Indicate if you have attached any screenshots or files with your message.
* **Preferred Contact Method (preferred_contact)**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Chat, or No Preference).
* **Best Time to Contact (best_time_contact)**: Enter your preferred time for the support team to contact you.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: The current submission date will be automatically populated.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields to facilitate efficient processing of your message.
* Attach any relevant screenshots or files to support your inquiry.
* Choose your preferred contact method and best time for the support team to contact you.
* If you have already taken any steps to resolve the issue, please describe them in the "Steps Taken" field.
* The submission date will be automatically populated based on the current date.
