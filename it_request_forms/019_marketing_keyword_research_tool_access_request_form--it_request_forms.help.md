# Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Request Form is a tool used to request access to the marketing keyword research tool for projects. This form helps to manage access to the tool and ensure that team members have the necessary permissions to use it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Provide all the required information.
* Review the fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your team lead or marketing director for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Keyword Request**: This is where you can request access to the marketing keyword research tool.
  * Enter a brief description of the project and what you need access for.
* **Team Members**: Select all the team members who need access to the tool. This can include Team Lead, Senior Marketing Manager, Marketing Manager, Content Writer, SEO Specialist, IT Specialist, or Other.
* **Access Start Date**: Enter the date when you need access to the tool to start.
* **Access End Date**: Enter the date when you no longer need access to the tool.
* **Reason for Request**: Explain why you're requesting access to the tool.
* **Approved By**: Select who will approve your request. This can be Marketing Director, Team Lead, Senior Marketing Manager, Marketing Manager, IT Manager, or Other.
* **Approved Date**: This is the date when your request will be approved.
* **Access Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your access request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Department**: Select which department you belong to. This can be Sales Team, Marketing Team, IT Team, HR Team, or Other.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description**: Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Requestor Name**: Enter your name.
* **Requestor Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Requestor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Approved By Name**: Enter the name of the person who approved your request.
* **Approved By Email**: Enter the email address of the person who approved your request.
* **Approved By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who approved your request.
* **Marketing Keyword Research Tool Version**: Enter the version of the marketing keyword research tool you're using.
* **Marketing Keyword Research Tool Version Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the tool version.
* **Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Type**: Select the type of access you need. This can be One-time, Reoccurring, or Unlimited.
* **Marketing Keyword Research Tool Request Frequency**: Select how often you plan to use the tool. This can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your access request.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided form fields and is intended to be a general explanation. The actual fields and their explanations may vary based on the specific requirements and context of your organization. It's recommended to review the form carefully and consult with your team lead or marketing director if you're unsure about any field.
