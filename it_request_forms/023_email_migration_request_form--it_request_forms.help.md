# email_migration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email_migration_request_form is a request form for customers seeking email migration services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information and the information of the person requesting the migration.
2. Enter the email address that requires migration.
3. If applicable, choose the migration services you require.
4. If you have a service provider, choose "Yes" to indicate their involvement.
5. Optionally, add any additional comments or questions in the "Notes" field.
6. Review your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Customer Info** (customer_info, text, not required)
Enter your basic information so we can get in touch with you if necessary.

### 2. **Email Requester** (email_requester, text, required)
Enter the name or ID of the person requesting the email migration.

### 3. **Email Info** (email_info, text, required)
Enter the email address that requires migration.

### 4. **Migration Date** (migration_date, date, not required)
Optional field to provide the specific date for the email migration.

### 5. **Migration Time** (migration_time, time, not required)
Optional field to provide the specific time for the email migration.

### 6. **Notes** (notes, text, not required)
Use this field to add any additional comments or questions related to the email migration request.

### 7. **Email Migration Services** (email_migration_services, select_multiple, required)
Choose the services you require for the email migration:
	* 'Yes'
	* 'No'

### 8. **Service Provider** (service_provider, select_one, required)
Choose whether or not you have a service provider:
	* 'Yes'
	* 'No'

### 9. **Customer Service Email** (customer_service_email, email, not required)
Optional field to enter the customer service email address for the service provider.

### 10. **Service Request** (service_request, note, not required)
Use this field to add any additional requests or notes related to the email migration.
