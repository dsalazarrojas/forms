# CRM Support Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRM Support Ticket Form is a tool for submitting issues or problems that require attention from the support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the ticket information section, including the Ticket ID, Date of Submission, and Issue Title.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select the issue category and priority level of the problem.
4. Describe the issue in detail, including any steps to reproduce the issue if applicable.
5. Optionally, provide environment information, such as the affected module or system and browser type.
6. Choose your preferred method of contact for follow-up questions or updates.
7. Indicate whether you are available to be followed up by the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ticket ID** (`ticket_id`): This is an auto-assigned number that is used to identify your ticket.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you are submitting the ticket.
* **Time of Submission** (`submission_time`): Enter the time you are submitting the ticket. This field is not required, but it's helpful for time zone and scheduling purposes.
* **Your Name** (`submitter_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Your Email Address** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your issue.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Are you a customer or representative?** (`submitter_type`): Select whether you are a customer or a representative.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`): Choose the category of the issue, such as Technical Issue, Billing Question, or Feature Request.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`): Select the priority level of the issue, such as Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Issue Title** (`issue_title`): Enter a brief summary of the issue.
* **Detailed Description** (`issue_description`): Describe the issue in detail, including any steps to reproduce the issue if applicable.
* **Steps to Reproduce** (`steps_to_reproduce`): If applicable, provide a step-by-step guide on how to reproduce the issue.
* **Affected Module or System** (`affected_module`): If applicable, enter the affected module or system.
* **Browser Type** (`browser_type`): If applicable, enter the browser type.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or updates.
* **May we follow up with you about this issue?** (`follow_up_ok`): Indicate whether you are available for the support team to follow up with you about this issue.
