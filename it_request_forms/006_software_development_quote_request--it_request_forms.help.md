# Software Development Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Development Quote Request form is designed to gather essential information from clients or customers seeking quote requests for software development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided below.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to provide the best quote for your project.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional): A brief description of your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of project you are seeking development services for (Web Development, Mobile Development, Enterprise Software Development).
* **Software Development** (`software_development`, select_multiple, optional): Check Yes if your project requires software development services (Yes, No).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location of your project (Remote, Local, Hybrid).
* **Development Stack** (`development_stack`, select_one, optional): Choose the development stack for your project (Full-Stack, Frontend, Backend, Database).
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for your project.
* **Estimated Budget** (`estimated_budget`, number, optional): Enter the estimated budget for your project.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your project (In Progress, Completed, Not Started).
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who the project is assigned to (Chatjimmy, John Doe).
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the assigned date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of your project.

Please note that each field is marked as optional, unless otherwise stated in the form instructions. However, providing accurate and comprehensive information will help us provide the best quote for your project.
