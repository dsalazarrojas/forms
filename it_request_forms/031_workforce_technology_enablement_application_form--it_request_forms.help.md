# Workforce Technology Enablement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Technology Enablement Application Form is used to request access to specific tools and resources within the organization. This form is designed to collect relevant information from the requester, the manager, and other relevant stakeholders to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections to understand what information is required.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Requester Name" and "Requester Email" fields, as these are essential for contacting you.
3. In the "Manager Info" section, enter your manager's name, title, and email address to ensure we can contact them about your request.
4. In the "Justification" section, clearly explain why you need access to the requested tool or resource.
5. In the "Policy Acknowledgment" field, indicate your understanding of the organization's policies and procedures.
6. In the "Requested Tool" field, select the specific tool or resource you are requesting access to.
7. In the "Manager Email" and "Manager Phone" fields, enter your manager's contact information.
8. Enter your manager's title in the "Manager Title" field.
9. In the "Department" field, enter the department you belong to or the team you are part of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text): Please provide a brief description of the requested tool or resource and its purpose.
* **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, text): Enter your manager's name, title, and email address to ensure we can contact them about your request.
* **Justification** (`justification`, text): Clearly explain why you need access to the requested tool or resource.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, select): Acknowledge that you understand the organization's policies and procedures related to the requested tool or resource.
* **Requested Tool** (`requested_tool`, select_multiple): Select the specific tool or resource you are requesting access to.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email): Enter your manager's email address to ensure we can contact them about your request.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Enter your manager's phone number for additional contact.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text): Enter your manager's title to ensure we have the correct information.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text): Enter your name, as we need to contact you about your request.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Department** (`department`, text): Enter the department you belong to or the team you are part of.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help or have questions, contact our support team for assistance.
* Be clear and concise in your justification for the requested tool or resource.
* Ensure your manager's contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
