This form is designed to collect information about the resource requests of CMMC Level 2 certification. The purpose of this form is to help identify potential resources needed for CMMC Level 2 implementation, including consulting services, assessment support, implementation guidance, documentation review, training, tool/software licenses, and other specific requirements. The form will be reviewed for relevance and uniqueness to ensure that the resources are being requested appropriately, preventing duplicate requests and ensuring that the right resources are being allocated to support the CMMC Level 2 implementation.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for resources to support CMMC Level 2 implementation in your organization. It will help us to understand your specific needs and allocate the right resources to support your implementation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information and needs.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your needs and requirements.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
