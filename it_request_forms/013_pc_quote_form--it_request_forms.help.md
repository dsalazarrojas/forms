# PC Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PC Quote Form is designed to collect information from customers about their quote requests. This form is used to gather details such as customer information, quote details, and other relevant information to process the quote request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to save your quote request.
2. Fill out the customer information section with your details.
3. Provide your quote details, including any necessary notes or comments.
4. Fill out your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
5. Choose a payment method and terms that apply to your quote request.
6. Select any relevant quote terms and conditions.
7. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
8. Provide your customer address and any additional notes if necessary.
9. Enter the quote date and time if applicable.
10. Select who the quote is assigned to and the status of the quote.
11. Fill out the quote number and any other necessary details.
12. Indicate whether you would like to receive customer notes or sales notes.
13. Enter any due date and time for the quote.
14. Select your customer company and sales person if applicable.
15. Calculate the total quote price, including any discounts.
16. Choose a quote discount type if applicable.
17. Enter the sub-total of your quote before any discounts.
