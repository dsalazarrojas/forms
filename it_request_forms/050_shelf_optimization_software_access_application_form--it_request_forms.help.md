# Shelf Optimization Software Access Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Shelf Optimization Software. It collects personal and work-related information from users to determine their eligibility for software access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter the name of your team or department in the **Team Name** field.
6. Select the Software you are requesting access to from the **Software Name** dropdown list.
7. Enter a clear and concise reason for your request in the **Reason for Request** field.
8. Select the department(s) you belong to from the **Department** multi-select field.
9. Enter the name of your manager in the **Manager Name** field.
10. Finally, choose a date for your request in the **Request Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number or a number where you can be reached.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team or department.
* **Software Name** (`software_name`, select_one, optional): Select the software you are requesting access to from the list provided.
*   *Software A*: Select this option for software A.
*   *Software B*: Select this option for software B.
*   *Software C*: Select this option for software C.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise reason for your request.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department(s) you belong to from the list provided.
*   *IT*: Select this option for the IT department.
*   *Finance*: Select this option for the Finance department.
*   *Marketing*: Select this option for the Marketing department.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your request.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will increase the efficiency of the software access request process.
