# Employee Equipment Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to submit requests for new or replacement equipment. The form helps employees provide essential details about the equipment they need, including the type of equipment, reason for the request, and other relevant information. The completed form will be reviewed and processed by the relevant department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the category of the request (New Hire or Replacement).
* Choose the type of equipment needed (Laptop, Mobile Phone, or Tablet).
* Select the project or department related to the equipment request (if applicable).
* Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* Select the department where the equipment will be used.
* Specify the name of the equipment needed.
* Enter the quantity of the equipment required.
* Provide a brief reason for the request (optional).
* Enter the name of your manager (if applicable).
* Choose the person who approved the request (if applicable).
* Enter the date the equipment was ordered.
* Enter the date the equipment was delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire/Replacement** (`new_hire`): 
  Select "New Hire" if you are a new employee and need equipment for your role. Select "Replacement" if you are replacing existing equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_name`): Enter the name of the equipment you are requesting, e.g., Laptop, Mobile Phone, or Tablet.
* **Project** (`project`): Select the project or department where the equipment will be used, e.g., Marketing Campaign, Sales Training, or Product Launch.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your email address associated with your employee account.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department where the equipment will be used.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Enter the name of the specific equipment you are requesting.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the number of equipment pieces needed.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Provide a brief reason for the equipment request, if applicable.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of your manager if the request requires approval.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`): Select the person who approved the equipment request.
* **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`): Enter the date the equipment was ordered.
* **Date Delivered** (`date_delivered`): Enter the date the equipment was delivered.
