# Device Rollover Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a device rollover request for an IT asset. It helps to track the process of transferring ownership and maintenance responsibilities of a device from one employee to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information as the submitter.
2. Enter the employee ID of the employee who currently owns the device.
3. Enter the reason for the rollover request.
4. Provide details about the new device that will take the place of the old one.
5. Specify the model of the new device.
6. Enter the asset number of the device being transferred.
7. Enter the date the device was purchased.
8. Select the current status of the device.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about the device.
10. Enter your contact information as the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the employee ID of the employee who currently owns the device.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the reason for the rollover request.
* **New Device Details** (`new_device_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about the new device that will take the place of the old one.
* **Device Model** (`new_device_model`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the model of the new device from the available options.
* **Asset Number** (`asset_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the asset number of the device being transferred.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the device was purchased.
* **Current Status** (`current_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the device from the available options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the device.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as the submitter.

## Tips

* Double-check all the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all necessary information is provided for a smooth and error-free transfer process.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the IT department for assistance.
