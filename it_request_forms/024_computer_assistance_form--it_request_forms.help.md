# Computer Assistance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Computer Assistance Form is designed to gather information about computer-related issues from end-users, enabling the support team to provide efficient assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

1. In the **Issue Summary** field, provide a brief summary of the problem you are experiencing.
2. In the **Requester name** field, enter your full name.
3. In the **Contact email** field, enter your contact email address.
4. In the **Device type** field, select the type of device you are using (e.g., Laptop, Desktop, Tablet, Mobile, or Other).
5. If you know the **Operating system** version, please enter it in the corresponding field.
6. In the **Issue description** field, provide a detailed description of the problem and its symptoms.
7. Select the **Urgency** level of your issue (Low, Medium, or High).
8. Choose your **Preferred contact method** (Email, Phone, or Remote session).
9. In the **Location** field, indicate the location where you can be reached for support (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Issue Summary (Issue Summary)**: A brief summary of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Requester name (Requester name)**: Your full name.
* **Contact email (Contact email)**: Your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Device type (Device type)**: The type of device you are using (e.g., Laptop, Desktop, Tablet, Mobile, or Other).
* **Operating system (Operating system)**: The operating system and version of your device (if known).
* **Issue description (Issue description)**: A detailed description of the problem and its symptoms.
* **Urgency (Urgency)**: The level of urgency you require for support (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Preferred contact method (Preferred contact method)**: The method by which you would prefer the technician to contact you (Email, Phone, or Remote session).
* **Location (Location)**: The location where you can be reached for support (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field to enable the support team to assist you efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask the support team for clarification.
* The support team will respond to your issue based on the information provided in this form.
