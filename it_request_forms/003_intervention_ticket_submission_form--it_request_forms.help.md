# Intervention Ticket Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to submit a ticket for assistance or support from the technical team for a specific problem.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the relevant information for the problem you are experiencing.
2.  Fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
3.  If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter a brief description of the problem you are experiencing.
*   **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the type of ticket you are submitting. The options are: Hardware, Software, and Other.
*   **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the problem in more detail, providing any relevant information or context.
*   **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select one or more locations where the problem exists (e.g. Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name.
*   **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date you plan to visit the technical team (if applicable).
*   **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time you plan to visit the technical team (if applicable).
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any relevant contact information for follow-up.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
*   **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.
*   **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any comments or feedback for the technical team.
*   **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name.
*   **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
*   **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your email address again (to confirm).
*   **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your company name.
*   **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your job title.
*   **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your department name.
*   **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your location.
*   **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments or notes about yourself.
*   **Submitter Request** (`submitter_request`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter a specific request for the technical team.
*   **Submitter Category** (`submitter_category`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any relevant category for the submission.
*   **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional comments for the technical team.
*   **Submitter Employees** (`submitter_employees`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the number of employees affected by the problem.
*   **Submitter Date of Visit** (`submitter_date_of_visit`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date you plan to visit the technical team (if applicable).

Note: This form is not intended to be used for any other purpose than submitting a ticket for assistance or support from the technical team.
