# new_employee_equipment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_employee_equipment_form" is used to collect information from new employees regarding their equipment preferences and requests. This form helps the HR department to understand the equipment needs of new employees and facilitate the process of providing necessary equipment for work tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details in the "Employee Details" section.
2. Indicate your equipment requests in the "Equipment Requests" section.
3. Choose your preferred equipment in the "Preferred Equipment" section.
4. Select your preferred equipment type in the "Preferred Equipment Type" section.
5. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your equipment preferences in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Fill in your employee ID or name to identify yourself as the new employee completing this form.
* **Equipment Requests** (`equipment_requests`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have equipment requests, and optionally add a list of specific equipment you need.
* **Preferred Equipment** (`preferred_equipment`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have preferred equipment, and optionally add a list of specific equipment you prefer.
* **Preferred Equipment Type** (`preferred_equipment_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a preferred equipment type, and optionally select a specific equipment type you prefer.
* **Preferred Equipment Subtype** (`preferred_equipment_subtype`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a preferred equipment subtype, and optionally add a list of specific subtypes you prefer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your equipment preferences.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Tips:
* Make sure to be specific about your equipment requests and preferences to ensure accurate processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
* This form is intended for new employees only. If you are an existing employee, please use the "existing_employee_equipment_form" instead.
