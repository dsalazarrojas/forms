# Add On Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request permission for add-ons or extensions to be installed on various levels of scope within your organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address as requested.
2. Provide accurate information about the add-on or extension you are requesting, including its name, publisher, version, and the specific features you need.
3. Explain the business justification for your request, including the impact on system security.
4. Specify the installation scope and the level of urgency.
5. Estimate the duration of use.
6. Confirm that you have reviewed the vendor's privacy policy.
7. Indicate whether you have obtained supervisor approval.
8. Date and sign the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Requester Name** (`Requester Name`, `Text`, Required)

Enter your full name as the requester.

### 2. **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `Email`, Required)

Enter your corporate email address.

### 3. **Department** (`Department`, `Text`, Required)

Enter the department or division you belong to.

### 4. **Position/Title** (`Position/Title`, `Text`, Required)

Enter your job title.

### 5. **Name of Add-On/Extension** (`Name of Add-On/Extension`, `Text`, Required)

Enter the name of the add-on or extension you are requesting.

### 6. **Publisher/Developer** (`Publisher/Developer`, `Text`, Required)

Enter the name of the publisher or developer of the add-on.

### 7. **Version Number** (`Version Number`, `Text`, Optional)

Enter the version number of the add-on you are requesting (if applicable).

### 8. **Browser Type** (`Browser Type`, `Select One`, Required)

Select the browser the add-on is designed to work with.

### 9. **Business Justification** (`Business Justification`, `Text`, Required)

Explain why you need the add-on for your work and its potential impact on system security.

### 10. **Security Impact Assessment** (`Security Impact Assessment`, `Text`, Optional)

Describe how the add-on may affect system security (if applicable).

### 11. **Specific Functionality Needed** (`Specific Functionality Needed`, `Text`, Required)

List the specific features of the add-on you require.

### 12. **Alternative Solutions Tried** (`Alternative Solutions Tried`, `Text`, Optional)

List any alternative solutions you have tried before requesting this add-on.

### 13. **Installation Scope** (`Installation Scope`, `Select One`, Required)

Specify where the add-on should be installed (e.g., your workstation, department computers, organization-wide, or specific project machines).

### 14. **Urgency Level** (`Urgency Level`, `Select One`, Required)

Select the level of urgency for your request (Low, Medium, High, Critical).

### 15. **Estimated Duration of Use** (`Estimated Duration of Use`, `Select One`, Required)

Estimate the duration you expect to use the add-on.

### 16. **Data Access Required** (`Data Access Required`, `Text`, Optional)

List any data types that the add-on requires access to.

### 17. **Compliance Review Needed** (`Compliance Review Needed`, `Select One`, Required)

Indicate whether the add-on requires compliance review.

### 18. **Vendor Privacy Policy Review** (`Vendor Privacy Policy Review`, `Select One`, Optional)

Confirm that you have reviewed the vendor's privacy policy.

### 19. **Approval Requested** (`Approval Requested`, `Select One`, Required)

Indicate whether you request approval for the add-on.

### 20. **Supervisor Approval** (`Supervisor Approval`, `Select One`, Required)

Confirm whether your supervisor has approved this request.

### 21. **Request Date** (`Request Date`, `Date`, Required)

Enter the date of this request.

### 22. **Requester Signature** (`Requester Signature`, `Text`, Required)

Provide an electronic signature to confirm your request.

## Tips

- Be accurate and thorough in your answers.
- Review each field carefully before submitting.
- Ensure you have not submitted similar requests in the past.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or relevant department head for guidance.
