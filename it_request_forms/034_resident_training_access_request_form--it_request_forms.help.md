# Resident Training Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Training Access Request Form is used to request access to a training program. To complete this form, please provide the following information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the program you are requesting access to in the **Program Name** field.
2. Select the **Training Date** you plan to attend.
3. Provide the **Supervisor Name** who approved your access request.
4. Choose the **Purpose of Access** for your training.
5. Describe the **Program Details**, including any relevant information about the program.
6. Enter your **Resident Name**, as it appears on your training ID badge.
7. Enter your **Email** address, so we can contact you with the status of your request.
8. Enter your **Phone** number, so we can contact you in case of any issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the training program you are requesting access to. This is a required field.
* **Training Date**: Select the date on which you plan to attend the training.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor, as they must approve your access request.
* **Purpose of Access**: Choose the option that best describes why you are requesting access to this training.
	+ option 1: General Training
	+ option 2: Specialized Training
	+ option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Program Details**: Describe the program in detail, including any relevant information you think is necessary for the training coordinator to consider your request.
* **Resident Name**: Enter your resident name as it appears on your training ID badge.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with the status of your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you with any issues.
* **Note**: All fields are optional, except for **Program Name** and **Resident Name**. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
