# Applicant Portal Login Assistance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Applicant Portal Login Assistance Request" form is a support request form designed to help applicants who are experiencing issues with logging in to the applicant portal. This form provides a structured way for applicants to describe their problem and for IT support staff to provide timely and effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address associated with your portal account.
2. If you have a contact phone number, please enter it for follow-up support.
3. Select the type of issue you are experiencing.
4. If you know your portal username, please enter it.
5. Choose your preferred contact method for follow-up support.
6. If you have a screenshot related to your issue, please provide the name of the file.
7. Describe the issue you are experiencing in brief.
8. Indicate the urgency of your request.
9. (Optional) If you have internal notes for the support staff, please provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name (full name)**: Enter the full name of the person submitting the request.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address associated with your portal account.
* **Phone number (Optional)**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up support.
* **Issue type**: Select the type of issue you are experiencing (Cannot log in, Password reset, Account locked, or Other).
* **Username (Optional)**: If you know your portal username, enter it here.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email or Phone).
* **Screenshot filename (Optional)**: If you have a screenshot related to your issue, enter the name of the file here.
* **Problem description**: Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Urgency**: Choose how urgent you consider your request to be (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Internal notes**: (Optional) Provide any internal notes for the support staff to use in handling your request.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue you are experiencing.
* If you are unsure about the issue type, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* If you have a screenshot, please ensure the file is in a format that can be opened by the support staff.
* Our support team will respond to your request as soon as possible, based on the information provided.
