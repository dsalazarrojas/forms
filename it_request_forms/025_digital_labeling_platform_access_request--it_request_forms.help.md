# Digital Labeling Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Digital Labeling Platform for your project. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information about yourself and your project.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out your details as the requester in the "Requester Details" section.
- In the "Project Description" field, please provide a brief description of your project, including any relevant details about the project's goals and objectives.
- In the "Terms Consent" section, select "I understand and agree" to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the platform.
- In the "Project Details" field, please provide any additional details about your project that are not already included in the "Project Description" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Requested By" field, which will be used as your username for the platform.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Details**: This section is where you will enter your name and contact information. Please provide your full name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Project Description**: This field is where you will provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Terms Consent**: This section is where you will confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the platform. Please select "I understand and agree" to acknowledge your agreement.
* **Project Details**: This field is where you can provide any additional details about your project that are not already included in the "Project Description" field.
* **Requested By**: This field is where you will enter your email address, which will be used as your username for the platform.
## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your contact person for clarification.
- You can submit this form multiple times, but please note that duplicate requests may cause confusion and delays in project processing.
