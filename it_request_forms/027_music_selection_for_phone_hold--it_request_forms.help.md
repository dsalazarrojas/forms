# Music Selection For Phone Hold - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is intended for customers or clients to provide information about their preferences for phone holds, including music selection and call back options.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form as follows:

1. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' in response to 'Music Preference'.
2. If you have selected 'Yes', you can specify a call back time and time zone for the music to be played.
3. If you select 'No', please provide a reason in the 'Additional Info' field.
4. Provide your email address in the 'Email' field if you want to be contacted back.
5. If you have selected to be called back, provide a phone number in the 'Phone Number' field.
6. Specify the duration of music to be played in the 'Music Duration' field if you want to have music played during the call.
7. Confirm your call back preferences in the 'Call Back Confirmation' fields.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Preference** (`Music Preference`, `select_one`, required): Please choose 'Yes' if you want music to be played during the call, or 'No' if you do not want music to be played. 
* **Call Back** (`Call Back`, `time`, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for music preference, specify the time when you want to be called back. 
* **Call Back Number** (`Call Back Number`, `text`, required): If you selected to be called back, enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Call Back Time** (`Call Back Time`, `date`, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for music preference, specify the time when you want to be called back.
* **Call Back Time Zone** (`Call Back Time Zone`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for music preference, specify the time zone for the call back time.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `text`, optional): If you selected 'No' for music preference, provide a reason for not wanting music to be played.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address if you want to be contacted back.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number where you can be reached if you selected to be called back.
* **Music Duration** (`Music Duration`, `number`, optional): If you selected 'Yes' for music preference, specify the duration of music to be played during the call.
* **Call Back Confirmation** (`Call Back Confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm whether you want to be called back or not.
* **Call Back Confirmation 2** (`Call Back Confirmation 2`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your call back preferences.
* **Call Back Confirmation 3** (`Call Back Confirmation 3`, `text`, optional): Confirm any additional call back preferences or information.
