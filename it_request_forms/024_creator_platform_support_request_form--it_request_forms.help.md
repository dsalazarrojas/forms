# creator_platform_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The creator_platform_support_request_form is used to submit a support request to the creator platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose a category that best describes the nature of your request.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue you are encountering.
3. Specify the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, High).
4. Select the tool or team member you would like to be assigned to resolve the issue.
5. Enter your follow-up email address for further communication.
6. Enter your follow-up phone number for further communication.
7. Set a follow-up time for the support team to contact you.
8. Provide any additional follow-up notes or comments.
9. Optionally, upload any relevant files or attachments.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.
