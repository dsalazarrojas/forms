# Annual Equipment Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Equipment Review Form is a tool used by the IT department to collect data on the current status of company equipment. This form helps to identify which equipment requires replacement, maintenance, or disposal, and informs decisions on resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the required information for each field.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Full Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer conducting this equipment review.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Department**: Select the department the equipment belongs to. This will help us to organize and report on equipment by department.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of this equipment review.
* **Review Year**: Select the year this equipment review is for. This will help us to report on long-term trends.
* **Equipment Category**: Select the type of equipment being reviewed. This will help us to group similar equipment together.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the asset identification number for the equipment being reviewed.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name or description of the equipment.
* **Manufacturer**: Enter the equipment manufacturer if known.
* **Model Number**: Enter the equipment model number if known.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the equipment serial number if known.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date the equipment was purchased if known.
* **Purchase Cost**: Enter the original purchase price of the equipment.
* **Current Value**: Enter the estimated current value of the equipment.
* **Equipment Condition**: Select the current physical condition of the equipment.
* **Operational Status**: Select whether the equipment is fully operational or not.
* **Usage Frequency**: Select how often the equipment is used.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select the current maintenance status of the equipment.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the last maintenance if known.
* **Next Maintenance Due**: Enter the date of the next maintenance due if known.
* **Issues Found**: Select whether any issues were found with the equipment.
* **Issue Details**: Enter any problems or issues found with the equipment.
* **Replacement Needed**: Select whether the equipment needs replacement.
* **Replacement Justification**: Enter the reason for needing replacement if applicable.
* **Estimated Replacement Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of replacing the equipment.
* **Disposal Required**: Select whether the equipment requires disposal.
* **Recommendations**: Enter any suggested actions or recommendations for the equipment.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other relevant information about the equipment.
