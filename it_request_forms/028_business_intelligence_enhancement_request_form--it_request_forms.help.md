# Business Intelligence Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture requests for Business Intelligence enhancements, allowing the BI team to deliver efficient and effective solutions to stakeholders. It helps us understand the specific needs and goals of the requester, enabling us to prioritize and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required fields.
2. Be concise and clear in your descriptions, ensuring the BI team understands your needs.
3. Review and proofread your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name (`requester_name`, `text`, required)**: This field is for you to enter your name, which will help the BI team contact you for follow-up discussions.
* **Contact email (`contact_email`, `email`, required)**: Provide your email address for the BI team to reach out to you for further information or clarification.
* **Current toolset (`current_toolset`, `text`, optional)**: If you have any existing Business Intelligence tools or platforms in use, please list them here. This will help the BI team understand your current context.
* **Enhancement summary (`enhancement_summary`, `text`, required)**: Describe the specific Business Intelligence enhancement or feature you are requesting. This should be a concise summary of your needs.
* **Desired outcome (`desired_outcome`, `text`, required)**: Explain what a successful BI enhancement would look like. This will help the BI team understand the impact and benefits of the enhancement.
* **Priority (`priority`, `select_one`, required)**: Select the priority level of your request (Low, Medium, or High) to help the BI team schedule and prioritize their work.
* **Estimated effort hours (`estimated_effort_hours`, `number`, optional)**: If you have a rough estimate of the effort required to deliver the enhancement, please provide it. This will help the BI team plan and resource their work.
* **Data sources affected (`data_sources_affected`, `text`, optional)**: List the major data sources involved in the enhancement request. This will help the BI team understand the scope and complexity of the request.
* **Approval required (`approval_required`, `select_one`, optional)**: If this enhancement requires executive approval, please select 'True'. This will ensure that the necessary approvals are obtained before proceeding with the enhancement.

Note: Please ensure that you have the necessary approvals before submitting this form, if required.
