# Account ID Lookup Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Account ID Lookup Request form is used to gather information from requesters to identify and verify account IDs. This form helps our team in locating and resolving issues related to account IDs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Requester Information" section.
2. Provide your full name in the "Account Holder Name" field.
3. If known, enter the account holder's email address and phone number.
4. Select the reason for the account ID lookup from the dropdown menu.
5. Indicate the system or platform to search for the account.
6. Enter any additional identifying information that might help locate the account.
7. Choose the urgency level for the request.
8. Add any additional notes for our team to reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name so our team knows who is making the request.
* **Email Address** (email, required): If possible, enter the account holder's email address to aid in verification.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the account holder's phone number to help our team locate the account.
* **Department** (select_one, required): Select the department related to the account request.
* **Reason for Account ID Lookup** (select_one, required): Choose the reason for the account ID lookup from the list.
* **Account Holder Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of the person or organization whose account ID is being looked up.
* **Account Holder Email Address (if known)** (email, optional): Enter the account holder's email address if available.
* **Account Holder Phone Number (if known)** (text, optional): Enter the account holder's phone number if available.
* **System or Platform** (select_one, required): Select the system or platform where the account is located.
* **Additional Identifying Information** (text, optional): Provide any other details that might help our team locate the account.
* **Urgency Level** (select_one, required): Choose the urgency level for the request.
* **Additional Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information that our team should be aware of.

## Tips
- Ensure that you're submitting a new request, and not a duplicate.
- Double-check your contact information and the account holder's details for accuracy.
- Choose the most relevant reason and urgency level for your request.
- Provide any additional information that can help our team locate and resolve the issue efficiently.
