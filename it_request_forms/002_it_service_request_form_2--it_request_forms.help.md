# it_service_request_form_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request internal support services from IT department. It will help in efficiently routing requests from customers, allowing the IT department to provide swift assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form with the required information to submit a service request.
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are a customer who has contacted us before.
- Select the category relevant to your service request.
- Briefly describe the problem you are experiencing.
- Enter your contact email address.
- Enter your contact phone number.
- Add any further explanation or context about your service request (optional).
- Add any comments or additional information about your service request (optional).
- If your service request does not fit into the above categories, please provide a brief description in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_contact** (customer_contact, select_multiple, Optional): This field helps us understand whether you have previously contacted us or not. Select 'Yes' if you are a repeat customer.
- **select_category** (select_one, Optional): This field allows you to select the category that best describes your service request.
  - Select "Option 1"
  - Select "Option 2"
  - Select "Option 3"
- **problem_description** (text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the problem you are experiencing.
- **contact_email** (email, Optional): Enter your contact email address so we can get back to you.
- **contact_phone** (text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **further_explanation** (note, Optional): Add any additional context or information about your service request.
- **comments** (note, Optional): Add any comments or additional information about your service request.
- **other** (text, Optional): If your service request does not fit into the above categories, please provide a brief description.
