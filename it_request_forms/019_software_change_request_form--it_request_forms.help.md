# Software Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by internal stakeholders to submit software change requests for technical support or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Software Change Request` field with a clear and concise description of the change required.
2. Provide employee details in the `Employee Details` field, including your name and job title.
3. Enter your company details in the `Company Details` field.
4. Describe the software update request in the `Software Update Request` field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the `Comments` field.
6. Click the `Submit` button to submit the form.
7. Enter your email address in the `Confirm Email` field to receive a confirmation of your submission.
8. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the `Confirm Phone` field to receive a follow-up call from the technical support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Software Change Request** (`software_change_request_form`, text, required): Describe the software change request in a clear and concise manner. This is the main field for describing the change.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and job title in this field. This helps the technical support team understand who submitted the request.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Enter your company name and any other relevant details in this field.
* **Software Update Request** (`software_update_request`, text, optional): Describe the software update request in detail.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the software change request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form and initiate the software change request process.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your submission.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive a follow-up call from the technical support team.
