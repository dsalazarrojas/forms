# Legacy Contact Setup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legacy Contact Setup Request Form is used to collect information about the person who will be handling an account's legacy contact. This form helps us set up a legacy contact for an account to ensure that important information and tasks are communicated to the right person in the event of an account's status change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** (page 1) with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email** (page 1) to make sure we can contact you about your request.
3. Enter your **Phone** (page 1) number so we can reach out to you if needed.
4. Provide your **Trusted Contact's Name** (page 2) if there's someone you trust to handle the account's legacy contact.
5. Add any relevant **Account Details** (page 2) for the person handling the account's legacy contact.
6. Finally, enter any **Comments** (page 2) about the request, such as any specific circumstances or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name (required, page 1)
* **User Name**: This is your full name, as this will be used to identify you for this request.

### Email (required, page 1)
* **Email**: This is your email address, which we'll use to contact you about your request.

### Phone (optional, page 1)
* **Phone**: If you prefer, you can enter your phone number so we can contact you by phone if needed.

### Trusted Contact (optional, page 2)
* **Trusted Contact**: If there's someone you trust to handle the account's legacy contact, enter their name.

### Account Details (optional, page 2)
* **Account Details**: Add any relevant information for the person handling the account's legacy contact, such as any special circumstances or instructions.

### Comments (optional, page 2)
* **Comments**: Add any comments or information you'd like to include with this request, such as a reason for the request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out as much information as possible.
- If you have a specific reason for this request, please include it in the **Comments** field.
- If you've filled out this form by mistake, please let your administrator know so we can correct your request.
