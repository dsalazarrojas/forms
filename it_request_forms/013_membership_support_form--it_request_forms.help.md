# Membership Support Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Support Form is a platform for users to seek assistance with their membership concerns. This form collects information about the user's membership inquiries, making it easier for the support team to address their issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the appropriate option for "prophets" (id: 4).
2. Fill in the text field "membership_prophet" (id: 5) with a specific answer.
3. In the "membership_prophet_2" field (id: 6), select multiple options from the available choices.
4. Provide a detailed description of your membership request in the "membership_support_request_details" field (id: 2).
5. Continue with "membership_prophet_3" (id: 7), which is a text field for additional information.
6. If applicable, select "membership_prophet_8" (id: 12) to indicate whether you're a member.
7. Fill in "membership_prophet_10" (id: 14) with a date if necessary.
8. Finally, confirm whether you're a member in the "membership_prophet_15" field (id: 18), and optionally select multiple options if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets** (id: 4) (`select_one`, required: false): Select a specific option to categorize your membership inquiry.
* **membership_support_request_details** (id: 2) (`text`, required: false): Describe the specific reason for your membership support request.
* **membership_support_request_details_2** (id: 3) (`text`, required: false): Provide further details about your membership support request.
* **membership_prophet** (id: 5) (`select_one`, required: false): Fill in a specific answer.
* **membership_prophet_2** (id: 6) (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options for your membership inquiry.
* **membership_prophet_3** (id: 7) (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your membership request.
* **membership_prophet_8** (id: 12) (`select_one`, required: false): Select an option to indicate whether you are a member.
* **membership_prophet_10** (id: 14) (`date`, required: false): Enter a date if needed.
* **membership_prophet_15** (id: 18) (`select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm whether you are a member.
* **membership_prophet_17** (id: 21) (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information.
