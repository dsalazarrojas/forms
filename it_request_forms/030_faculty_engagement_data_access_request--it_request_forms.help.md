# Faculty Engagement Data Access Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by faculty and staff to request access to specific data and systems for research and engagement purposes. Please note that each request is reviewed and approved on a case-by-case basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  In the "Request Purpose" field, describe the reason for your request.
2.  In the "Request Duration" field, specify the expected length of time you will need access to the data or system.
3.  In the "Policy Acknowledgment" field, select the policy that best applies to your request.
4.  In the "Start Date" field, enter the date you need to start accessing the data or system.
5.  In the "End Date" field, enter the date you expect to complete your work with the data or system.
6.  In the "Contact Person" field, enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your request.
7.  In the "Department" field, enter the name of the department you are associated with.
8.  In the "Faculty" field, enter the names of the faculty members involved in your request.
9.  In the "Staff" field, enter the names of the staff members involved in your request.
10. In the "Student" field, enter the names of the students involved in your request.

* Make sure to provide sufficient information to justify your request.
* Be clear about the expected length of time you will need access to the data or system.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct policy for your request.
* Double-check that you have entered the correct contact information and department name.
