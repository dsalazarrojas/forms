# robotics_maintenance_scheduling_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule maintenance for robotics equipment. It gathers essential information to ensure that the maintenance is performed correctly and on time.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief description of the robotics maintenance request in the **form_title** field.
2.  Specify whether the robotics equipment is from a factory or a lab in the **factory_robotics** field.
3.  Enter the robot's serial number in the **robot_serial_number** field.
4.  Choose the type of maintenance required from the options in the **maintenance_type** field.
5.  Select a date for the maintenance in the **maintenance_date** field.
6.  Specify the start time of the maintenance in the **start_time** field.
7.  Specify the end time of the maintenance in the **end_time** field.
8.  Enter any facilities related to the maintenance in the **facilities** field.
9.  Mention any lab-related robotics in the **lab_robotics** field.
10.  Provide any additional notes or comments about the submission in the **submit_button** field.
11.  Enter the name of the facility where the maintenance will take place in the **facility_name** field.
12.  Specify the location of the robotics equipment in the **equipment_location** field.
13.  Mention the robot support team responsible for the maintenance in the **robot_support_team** field.
14.  Enter the name of the support contact person in the **support_contact_name** field.
15.  Enter the email of the support contact person in the **support_email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (text, optional): A brief description of the robotics maintenance request.
* **factory_robotics** (text, optional): Specify whether the robotics equipment is from a factory or a lab.
* **robot_serial_number** (text, optional): Enter the robot's serial number for easy identification.
* **maintenance_type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of maintenance required.
*   - **Yes**: Indicate that maintenance is needed.
*   - **No**: Indicate that no maintenance is needed.
* **maintenance_date** (date, optional): Select a date for the maintenance.
* **start_time** (time, optional): Specify the start time of the maintenance.
* **end_time** (time, optional): Specify the end time of the maintenance.
* **facilities** (text, optional): Enter any facilities related to the maintenance.
* **lab_robotics** (text, optional): Mention any lab-related robotics.
* **submit_button** (text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the submission.
* **facility_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the facility where the maintenance will take place.
* **equipment_location** (text, optional): Specify the location of the robotics equipment.
* **robot_support_team** (text, optional): Mention the robot support team responsible for the maintenance.
* **support_contact_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the support contact person.
* **support_email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the support contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your maintenance request is processed correctly.
* Review your submission for any mistakes or inaccuracies before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the support team for assistance.
