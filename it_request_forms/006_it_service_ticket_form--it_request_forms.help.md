# it_service_ticket_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_service_ticket_form is a form used for submitting requests related to IT services. It's a crucial tool for submitting and tracking IT service tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the it_service_ticket_form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Submitter Name** field with your name.
2. Select the **Ticket Type** field to indicate whether the issue is related to IT services or not.
3. Provide a brief description of the issue in the **Description** field.
4. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **E-mail** field.
6. Select the **Assigned Tool** field to choose which tool will be used to resolve the issue.
7. Choose the **Category** field to categorize the issue.
8. Select the **Status** field to update the status of the issue.
9. Add any additional comments in the **Comments** field.
10. Choose the **Submitted By** field to select who submitted the issue.
11. Fill in the **Submitted At** field with the date and time the issue was submitted.
12. If necessary, update the issue status in the **Updated At** field.
13. If the issue is resolved, fill in the **Resolved At** field with the date and time it was resolved.
14. Select the **Resolved By** field to indicate who resolved the issue.
15. Choose the **Priority** field to indicate the priority of the issue.
16. Select the **Tags** field to add any relevant tags to the issue.
17. Enter any additional notes about the issue in the **Notes** field.
18. Fill in the **Submitter Name** field with your name (not required, but recommended).
19. Enter your email address in the **Submitter Email** field (not required, but recommended).
20. Enter your phone number in the **Submitter Phone** field (not required, but recommended).
21. Add any additional comments about the issue in the **Submitter Comments** field (not required, but recommended).
22. Select the **Submitter Category** field to categorize the issue submitter.
23. Enter the name of your company in the **Submitter Company** field (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submitter Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the submitter of the issue.
- **Ticket Type** (select_one, required): Select whether the issue is related to IT services or not.
- **Description** (text, required): Briefly describe the issue you're experiencing.
- **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number.
- **E-mail** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Date** (date, required): Enter the date the issue was submitted.
- **Time** (time, required): Enter the time the issue was submitted.
- **Assigned Tool** (select_multiple, required): Choose the tool that will be used to resolve the issue.
- **Category** (select_multiple, required): Categorize the issue.
- **Status** (select_one, required): Update the status of the issue.
- **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the issue.
- **Submitted By** (select_multiple, required): Select who submitted the issue.
- **Submitted At** (date, required): Enter the date and time the issue was submitted.
- **Updated At** (date, optional): If necessary, update the issue status.
- **Resolved At** (date, optional): If the issue is resolved, enter the date and time it was resolved.
- **Resolved By** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate who resolved the issue.
- **Priority** (select_one, optional): Indicate the priority of the issue.
- **Tags** (select_multiple, optional): Add any relevant tags to the issue.
- **Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the issue.
- **Submitter Name** (text, optional): Enter your name (recommended).
- **Submitter Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address (recommended).
- **Submitter Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (recommended).
- **Submitter Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments (recommended).
- **Submitter Category** (select_multiple, optional): Categorize yourself as the submitter.
- **Submitter Company** (text, optional): Enter the name of your company (not required).
