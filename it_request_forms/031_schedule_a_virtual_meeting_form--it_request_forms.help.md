# Schedule A Virtual Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Schedule A Virtual Meeting Form" is a tool used to gather information about virtual meetings. It is intended for meeting planners and organizers to capture the details of meetings, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and that meetings run smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Schedule A Virtual Meeting Form" button to access the form.
2. Fill out the form by entering the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`Meeting Details`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`Meeting Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the virtual meeting. 
* **Meeting Time** (`Meeting Time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the virtual meeting.
* **Attendees** (`Attendees`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether or not attendees are expected.
* **Meeting Location** (`Meeting Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Durations** (`Meeting Durations`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Agenda Items** (`Meeting Agenda Items`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether or not an agenda will be part of the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (`Meeting Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional meeting notes.
* **Meeting Requester** (`Meeting Requester`, `select_one`, required/optional): Identify the meeting requestor.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the meeting requestor.
* **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Identify the department related to the meeting.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the meeting requestor.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the meeting requestor.
* **Title** (`Title`, `select_one`, required/optional): Identify the job title of the meeting requestor.
* **Job Position** (`Job Position`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the meeting requestor.
* **Meeting Location Address** (`Meeting Location Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the virtual meeting location.
* **Meeting Location Building** (`Meeting Location Building`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the building where the virtual meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Location Room** (`Meeting Location Room`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room where the virtual meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Location Notes** (`Meeting Location Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes about the virtual meeting location.
* **Meeting Equipment** (`Meeting Equipment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate the equipment needed for the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Equipment Notes** (`Meeting Equipment Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any notes about the meeting equipment.
* **Meeting Presentation Materials** (`Meeting Presentation Materials`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether or not presentation materials are required.
* **Presentation Notes** (`Presentation Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional presentation notes.
* **Additional Meeting Notes** (`Additional Meeting Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the virtual meeting.
* **Meeting Approval** (`Meeting Approval`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether or not the meeting has been approved.

Please note that some fields might be marked as 'required/optional', indicating that they need to be filled out for the form to be valid or are additional information that can be provided for better meeting planning.
