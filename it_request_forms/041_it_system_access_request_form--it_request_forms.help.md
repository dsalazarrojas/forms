# IT System Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to IT systems, processes, or services from the IT department. It helps to ensure that all requests are properly documented and reviewed in a centralized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as the requester.
2. Select the IT System you are requesting access to.
3. Choose your desired Access Level (High, Low, or Medium).
4. Enter the expected duration of access (optional).
5. Provide the Request Date (optional).
6. Enter your Employee ID.
7. Enter your Employee Name.
8. Enter your Department.
9. Enter your contact email.
10. Enter your contact phone number.
11. Enter the name of the IT Contact Person (if applicable).
12. Add any additional Request Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name and title.
* **IT System** (2, select_one, required/optional): Choose the IT system you are requesting access to. Available options are: IT Security and Compliance, IT Security, and IT Compliance.
* **Access Level** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your desired access level. Options are: High, Low, and Medium.
* **Access Duration** (4, number, required/optional): Enter the expected duration of access (in days).
* **Request Date** (5, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are requesting access to the IT system (optional, but recommended).
* **Employee ID** (6, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Department** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Contact E-mail** (9, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (10, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **IT Contact Person** (11, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the IT contact person handling your request.
* **Request Comments** (12, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information to support your request.
