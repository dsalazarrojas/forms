# energy_meter_reconfiguration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The energy meter reconfiguration request form is used to submit a request for reconfiguration or replacement of energy meters. This form helps gather essential information required for a smooth and efficient reconfiguration or replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your details:**
	* Fill in the required fields with your correct name, email, and phone number.
	* Ensure that your contact information is accurate so that we can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
2. **Select the meter type:**
	* Choose the type of energy meter that requires reconfiguration or replacement from the list provided.
	* Select only one meter type per request.
3. **Specify the location:**
	* Choose the location where the meter is installed from the list provided.
	* Select multiple locations if applicable.
4. **Provide meter details:**
	* Enter the serial number of the meter that requires reconfiguration or replacement.
5. **Specify reconfiguration or replacement request:**
	* Choose the type of request you are making, such as changing the configuration or replacing the meter entirely.
6. **Attach supporting documentation (if required):**
	* If you have any relevant documentation, such as invoices or receipts, please attach them to support your request.
7. **Submit meter readings:**
	* Enter the meter readings for the specific meter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
- **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Meter Type** (`meter_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of energy meter that requires reconfiguration or replacement.
- **Location** (`meter_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the location where the meter is installed.
- **Meter Serial Number** (`meter_serial_number`, `text`, required): Enter the serial number of the meter.
- **Reconfiguration Request Type** (`reconfiguration_request_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of request you are making.
- **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, `note`, required): Attach any relevant documentation to support your request.
- **Meter Readings** (`meter_readings`, `time`, required): Enter the meter readings for the specific meter.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have attached all required supporting documentation to facilitate the reconfiguration or replacement process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Submitting duplicate requests will lead to delays and potential rejections. Please ensure that you have read and understood the purpose of this form before proceeding.
