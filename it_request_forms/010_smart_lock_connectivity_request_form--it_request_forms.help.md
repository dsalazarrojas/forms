# Smart Lock Connectivity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lock Connectivity Request Form is a tool used to report issues with smart locks. Fill out this form to request assistance with your smart lock problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide as much detail as possible about your issue.
3. Select the relevant options for the "Smart Lock" field.
4. Fill in the "Additional Info" field with any further information that might be helpful for our team to assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Smart Lock** (`smartlock_form`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your smart lock.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name and any contact phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, `text`, optional): Specify the status of your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, optional): Select who you would like to be assigned to your issue.
	+ Options: Jimmy, Jane, John, Other
* **Contact People** (`contact_people`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any people you would like to be contacted regarding your issue.
	+ Options: Jane, Other, John
* **Smart Lock Type** (`smartlock_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify the type of smart lock you're experiencing issues with.
	+ Options: Bluetooth, Zigbee, Other
* **Smart Lock Location** (`smartlock_location`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your issue started.
* **Smart Lock Status** (`smartlock_status`, `select_one`, optional): Specify the status of your smart lock.
	+ Options: Online, Offline, Other
* **Smart Lock Description** (`smartlock_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of your smart lock.
* **Request Cause** (`request_cause`, `text`, optional): Specify the cause of your request.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for our team to assist you.
* **Smart Lock Connection** (`smartlock_connection`, `text`, optional): Enter any information about your smart lock's connection.
* **Smart Lock Serial Number** (`smartlock_serial_num`, `text`, optional): Enter your smart lock's serial number.
* **Smart Lock Manufacturer** (`smartlock_manufacturer`, `text`, optional): Enter your smart lock's manufacturer.
* **Smart Lock Model Number** (`smartlock_model_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your smart lock's model number.
* **Smart Lock Software Version** (`smartlock_software_ver`, `text`, optional): Enter your smart lock's software version.
* **Smart Lock Firmware Version** (`smartlock_firmware_ver`, `text`, optional): Enter your smart lock's firmware version.

Please be as thorough as possible when filling out this form, and we'll do our best to assist you with your smart lock issues.
