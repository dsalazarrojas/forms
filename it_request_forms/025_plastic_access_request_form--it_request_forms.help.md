# Plastic Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plastic Access Request Form is used to request access to plastic materials for your department or team. Fill out this form to submit your request and provide necessary information for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please provide your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
- Enter your Phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
- Select your Department and Team to ensure your request is directed to the right people.
- Choose the Location where you plan to use the plastic materials.
- Select the Date and Time you expect to use the materials.
- Provide a Reason for your request.
- Confirm Manager Approvals and provide your Manager's Email for validation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full first name as you would like it to appear in the request form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full last name as you would like it to appear in the request form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your Phone number so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the department that you represent or are requesting for.
* **Team** (`team`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the team you are a part of or the team requesting for.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the location where you plan to use the plastic materials.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `required`): Select the date you plan to use the plastic materials.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `required`): Select the time you plan to use the plastic materials.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `note`, `required`): Provide a brief explanation for your request.
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm if your Manager has approved your request.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your Manager's Email address for validation.
