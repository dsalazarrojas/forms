# Device Pick Up Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a device pick up at a specific location and time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Email.
5. Enter the Location where you would like to pick up the device.
6. Enter any additional Device Information (e.g., device type, serial number).
7. If you have an appointment time, enter it in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`<first_name>`, text, required): Enter the first name of the person picking up the device.
* **Last Name** (`<last_name>`, text, required): Enter the last name of the person picking up the device.
* **Phone** (`<phone>`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Email** (`<email>`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Location** (`<location>`, text, optional): Enter the location where you would like to pick up the device.
* **Device Info** (`<device_info>`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the device, such as device type or serial number.
* **Appointment Time** (`<appointment_time>`, time, optional): Enter the time you wish to pick up the device, in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used to contact you.
* If you have an appointment time, please use the format `HH:MM AM/PM` to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any additional information about the device, please enter it in the field "Device Info".
