# it_support_business_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for business registration for IT support purposes. It allows businesses to provide their contact information, services they require, and any additional comments or notes they may have. The form is designed to help our team understand their needs and provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill in your **Contact Info** (Page 1).
	* Enter your business name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
3. Provide your **Name** (Page 1).
	* Enter the name of the person completing the form.
4. Enter your **Address** (Page 1).
	* Provide your business address.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** (Page 1).
	* Enter the phone number of your business.
6. Enter your **Email** (Page 1).
	* Enter the email address of your business.
7. Select the **Services** you require (Page 1).
	* Choose the services you need our IT support team to help with, such as "IT Support", "Network", or "Server".
8. Enter your **Message** (Page 2).
	* Provide any additional comments or information about your business and what you need our support with.
9. Add **Notes** (Page 2).
	* Provide any additional notes or comments about your business and your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**:
	+ **Name** (required): Enter your business name.
	+ **Address**: Enter your business address.
	+ **Phone Number** (required): Enter the phone number of your business.
	+ **Email** (required): Enter the email address of your business.
* **Name** (optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Address** (optional):
	+ Enter your business address.
* **Phone Number** (optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of your business.
* **Email** (optional):
	+ Enter the email address of your business.
* **Services**:
	+ Select the services you need our IT support team to help with.
* **Message**:
	+ Provide any additional comments or information about your business and what you need our support with.
* **Notes**:
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments about your business and your request.
* **Date**:
	+ Enter the date of your request.
* **Time**:
	+ Enter the time of your request.
* **Password**:
	+ Enter your password (note: this field is not recommended for security purposes, but it's included in the form for completeness).
* **Services**:
	+ Select the services you require (IT Support, Network, Server).
