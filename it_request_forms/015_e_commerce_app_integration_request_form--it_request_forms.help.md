# e_commerce_app_integration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about e-commerce app integrations, specifically for API, Custom, and SDK integrations. It's essential to complete this form accurately to provide the necessary details for our team to review and process the integration requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, making sure to answer all the required fields.
2. Start with the **App Details** section, where you can provide general information about your app, such as its title and description.
3. In the **Use Cases** section, explain how your app will be used within our platform and what benefits it will bring to our users.
4. In the **Integration Details** section, select the type of integration you're requesting (API, Custom, or SDK) and provide further details about your integration request.
5. Please fill out the **Contact Details** section with your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
6. In the **Team** section, provide information about the team members involved in the integration process.
7. Fill out the **Assigned Tool** section with the tool(s) you're using for the integration.
8. In the **Comments** section, add any additional comments or questions you have about the integration.
9. Fill out the **Notes** section with any other relevant information about the integration.
10. Select the **Integration Status** of your request from the options provided.
11. In the **Next Steps** section, choose the next steps we should take regarding the integration.
12. In the **Integration Issues** section, select any issues you're experiencing with the integration.
13. In the **Integration Type** section, select the type of integration you're requesting (API, Custom, or SDK).
14. In the **Integration Scope** section, provide any additional information about the integration scope.
15. In the **App Version** section, provide the version of your app.
