# Payroll Portal Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to request access to the Payroll Portal. Please note that access is restricted to one employee per department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Review the form's purpose and ensure you meet the eligibility criteria.
- Select the department you belong to.
- Choose your current job function.
- Type in your manager's name.
- Enter your reason for requesting access to the Payroll Portal.
- Fill in your employee ID.
- Enter your employee name.
- Enter your email address.
- Type in your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Request**: This question helps us understand whether you are requesting access for yourself or someone else.
	+ This field is a dropdown menu with two options: Yes or No.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to to ensure you are requesting access for the correct department.
	+ This field allows you to choose one or more departments that you belong to.
* **Job Function**: Enter your current job function to help us understand your role in the department.
	+ This field allows you to select one of the provided options.
* **Manager**: Enter the name of your manager to facilitate verification of your request.
	+ This field is for entering a text value.
* **Reason for Request**: Explain the reason you are requesting access to the Payroll Portal.
	+ This field is for entering a text value.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as per your employee record.
	+ This field is for entering a numeric value.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name as per your employee record.
	+ This field is for entering a text value.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you regarding your request.
	+ This field is for entering an email value.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number to reach out to you about your request.
	+ This field is for entering a text value.

## Tips
- You are not allowed to submit this form if you don't belong to any department.
- Please ensure that the details you enter are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have submitted this form before, it will be considered as a duplicate and not processed.
