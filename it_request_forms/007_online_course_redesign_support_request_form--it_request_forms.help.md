# Online Course Redesign Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Redesign Support Request Form is designed to collect information from institutions regarding their online course redesign needs. This form helps the support team understand the requirements and provide effective support for the redesign process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in all the required fields (marked as required) with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Provide a clear and concise description of your course redesign needs in the "Course Redesign Request" field.
3.  Make sure to include your institution's name and contact information, such as email and phone number.
4.  Select the relevant options for your institution's location and course status.
5.  Optionally, you can provide additional redesign suggestions or comments in the "Redesign Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Course Redesign Request** (`course_redesign_request`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your institution's course redesign needs. This field should include specific details about the changes you would like to see in your course.
*   **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your institution's name to help us identify and track your requests.
*   **Institution Email** (`institution_email`, `email`, required): Enter your institution's email address so we can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Provide your name and title within the institution for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Fill in your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
*   **Institution Location** (`institution_location`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if your institution's location is relevant to the course redesign process.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the courses you would like to see redesigned.
*   **Course Section** (`course_section`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the course sections you would like to see redesigned.
*   **Course Type** (`course_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the course types you would like to see redesigned.
*   **Course Status** (`course_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the status of the courses you would like to see redesigned (Active or Inactive).
*   **Redesign Goal** (`redesign_goal`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide a specific goal or objective for the course redesign.
*   **Redesign Suggestions** (`redesign_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Add any additional suggestions or comments about the course redesign process.
*   **Course Tagging** (`course_tagging`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the course tags you would like to see applied.
*   **Comments** (`course_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the course redesign process.
