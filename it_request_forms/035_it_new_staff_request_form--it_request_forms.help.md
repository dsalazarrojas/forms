# IT New Staff Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit new staff requests for internal hiring or changes in job titles. It is used by employees to provide information about the new staff member, including their name, job title, contact information, and location details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the first name of the new staff member in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the new staff member in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter the job title of the new staff member in the "Job title" field.
4. Enter the email address of the new staff member in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the new staff member in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter the date of birth of the new staff member in the "Date of birth" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the address of the new staff member in the "Address" field.
8. Enter the city of the new staff member in the "City" field.
9. Select the state of the new staff member's location in the "State" field.
10. Enter the zip code of the new staff member's location in the "Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: <i>(First name)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the first name of the new staff member.
- **Last name**: <i>(Last name)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the last name of the new staff member.
- **Job title**: <i>(Job title)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the job title of the new staff member.
- **Email**: <i>(Email)</i> (<i>email</i>, required): Enter the email address of the new staff member.
- **Phone**: <i>(Phone)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the phone number of the new staff member.
- **Date of birth**: <i>(Date of birth)</i> (<i>date</i>, optional): Enter the date of birth of the new staff member (if applicable).
- **Address**: <i>(Address)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the address of the new staff member.
- **City**: <i>(City)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the city of the new staff member's location.
- **State**: <i>(State)</i> (<i>select_one</i>, required): Select the state of the new staff member's location.
- **Zip**: <i>(Zip)</i> (<i>text</i>, required): Enter the zip code of the new staff member's location.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the entered information for correctness before submission.
- If the new staff member's date of birth is not known or applicable, leave this field blank.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the IT department for assistance.
