# Request Form For Visuals Display - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users request a visual display for various purposes such as presentations, meetings, or training sessions. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your visual display request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request Form For Visuals Display** field with your request title.
2. Choose a **Visual Display Category** from the dropdown list, e.g., "Yes" for presentations or "No" for meetings.
3. Select the **Assigned Tool** that you would like to use for your visual display, or choose "No" if you don't have a preference.
4. Provide your **Assigned By** contact information (email) so that we can reach out to you for further details.
5. Add a **Description** of your visual display request to help us understand your needs better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form For Visuals Display** (`title`, `text`, required): A brief title for your request.
* **Visual Display Category** (`visual_display_request_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category of your visual display request, e.g., "Yes" for presentations or "No" for meetings.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the tool you would like to use for your visual display, or choose "No" if you don't have a preference.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, `email`, required/optional): Provide your email contact information so that we can reach out to you for further details.
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, required/optional): Add a description of your visual display request to help us understand your needs better.
