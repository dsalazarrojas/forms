# Digital Wallet Access Recovery Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Wallet Access Recovery Request form is used to request access to the digital wallet, a secure system that requires authorization and permission. This form should only be used by authorized personnel who need access to the digital wallet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a description of the reason for your access request in the "Description" field.
3. Attach any relevant files or documents that support your request in the "Files" field.
4. Specify the follow-up details in the "Follow Up" field.
5. Choose the follow-up date and time in the "Follow Up Date" and "Follow Up Time" fields.
6. Select the support team member to whom your request will be assigned in the "Assigned To" field.
7. Choose the status of your request in the "Status" field.
8. Select the follow-up status of your request in the "Follow Up Status" field.
9. If necessary, provide additional comments for the manager in the "Manager Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Description** (Description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the reason for your access request.
* **Files** (Files, text, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents that support your request.
* **Follow Up** (Follow Up, text, optional): Specify the follow-up details, such as any specific dates or times.
* **Follow Up Date** (Follow Up Date, date, optional): Choose the date for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Time** (Follow Up Time, time, optional): Choose the time for follow-up.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, required): Select the support team member to whom your request will be assigned.
* **Team Lead** (Team Lead, select_multiple, optional): Select additional team leads involved in your request.
* **Manager Comments** (Manager Comments, text, optional): Provide additional comments for the manager.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, required): Choose the status of your request.
* **Due Date** (Due Date, date, optional): Choose the due date for your request.
* **Follow Up Status** (Follow Up Status, select_one, required): Choose the follow-up status of your request.
* **Created At** (Created At, date, read-only): This field shows the date and time this form was created.

# Tips

* Only authorized personnel should use this form to request access to the digital wallet.
* If you have previously submitted a digital wallet access request, do not submit this form again.
* Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Attach any relevant files or documents to support your request.
* Choose the correct status and follow-up status for your request.
