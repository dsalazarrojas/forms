# electronic_repair_and_ticketing_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electronic_repair_and_ticketing_form_template is used to record and manage electronic repair and ticketing requests. This form captures essential information about the repair shop, customer details, and order-related data, allowing for efficient tracking and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the name of the repair shop where the electronic device was repaired.
2.  Enter the customer's name.
3.  Enter the customer's phone number.
4.  Enter the customer's email address.
5.  Enter the date of the order.
6.  Enter a brief description of the order.
7.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the order is a new or repeat order.
8.  Select the status of the order as Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Repair shop**: Enter the name of the repair shop where the electronic device was repaired.
*   **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
*   **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.
*   **Order Description**: Enter a brief description of the order.
*   **Order Type**: Select 'Yes' to indicate a new order or 'No' for a repeat order.
*   **Order Status**: Select 'Active' if the order is ongoing or 'Inactive' if the order has been completed.
