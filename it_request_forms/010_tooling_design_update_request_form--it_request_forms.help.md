# Tooling Design Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tooling Design Update Request Form is used to submit and track updates or changes to tooling designs. This form is intended to provide all necessary information for the reviewing team to evaluate and approve the changes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible about the design change request.
2. Select the affected areas where the design change will have an impact.
3. Provide a brief description of the design change and its potential impact on manufacturing and engineering processes.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, so that the reviewing team can get in touch with you for further questions or clarification.
5. If applicable, select the team member or manager who will be responsible for reviewing and approving the design change.
6. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the team lead or manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tooling Design Update Request** (`tooling_design_update_request_form_page_1`): Enter a brief description of the design change request.
* **Design Change Reason** (`design_change_reason`): Provide a detailed reason for the design change request.
* **Affected Areas** (`affected_areas`): Select the areas where the design change will have an impact (e.g., Machine 1, Machine 2, Machine 3).
* **Requested Changes** (`requested_changes`): Enter a detailed description of the design change.
* **Manufacturing Impact** (`manufacturing_impact`): Describe the impact on manufacturing processes.
* **Engineering Impact** (`engineering_impact`): Describe the impact on engineering processes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the team member or manager who will review and approve the design change.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the design change.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the design change.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`): Enter the name and contact information of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`): Enter the email address of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (`reporting_manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of your reporting manager.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`): Enter the name and contact information of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`): Enter the email address of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`): Enter the phone number of the team lead.
* **Manager** (`manager`): Enter the name and contact information of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter the email address of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of your manager.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the reviewing team.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your submission date and time to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any additional comments or notes about the design change, use the comments field to enter them.
* If you have any other team members or managers involved in the review process, enter their contact information accordingly.
