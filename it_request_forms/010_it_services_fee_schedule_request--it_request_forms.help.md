# it_services_fee_schedule_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_services_fee_schedule_request" form is used to request IT services from an organization or company. It helps gather information about the service requested, contact details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the relevant fields.
2. Provide a brief description of the service you are requesting.
3. Select the service type from the options provided.
4. Choose the preferred day and time for the service.
5. Select the duration of the service required.
6. Add any additional notes about your request.
7. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IT Provider** (`it_provider`): Enter the name of the IT provider you are working with.
* **Service Name** (`service_name`): Describe the service you are requesting.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`): Provide a brief description of the service.
* **Service Price** (`service_price`): Enter the price for the service (if applicable).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service you are requesting from the provided options.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Service Time** (`service_time`): Select the preferred time of the service.
* **Service Day** (`service_day`): Select the day of the service.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`): Select the duration of the service required.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes about your request.
* **IT Services Fee Schedule Request Form** (`form_title`): This is the title of the form, not a field to fill out. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are requesting a service that is not listed in the service type options, please contact the IT department for assistance.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the IT department.
