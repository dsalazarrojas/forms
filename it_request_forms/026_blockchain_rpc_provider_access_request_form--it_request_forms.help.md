# Blockchain RPC Provider Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to blockchain RPC services. Please fill out this form to provide necessary information for your access request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Requestor Name**: Enter your full name.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
	* **Organization**: Enter your company or team name.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your role.
2. Select your blockchain network and access type:
	* **Blockchain Network**: Choose the network you need access to (e.g., Ethereum Mainnet, Ethereum Testnet, etc.).
	* **Access Type**: Select the type of access you need (e.g., Read Only, Read and Write, etc.).
	* **Use Case**: Choose how you plan to use the service (e.g., Application Development, Testing and QA, etc.).
3. Provide project details:
	* **Project Name**: Enter your project name.
	* **Project Description**: Give a brief description of your project.
	* **Expected Daily Requests**: Estimate the number of RPC calls you expect per day.
4. Select your access duration and rate limits:
	* **Access Start Date**: Choose when you need access to start.
	* **Access Duration**: Select how long you need access for (e.g., Temporary - 1 Week, Long Term - 1 Year, etc.).
	* **Rate Limit Requirements**: Decide if you need increased limits or unlimited access.
5. Confirm your requirements and agreements:
	* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your approving manager.
	* **Security Training Completed**: Confirm if you have completed the required security training.
	* **Compliance Agreement**: Agree to the terms of service.
6. Add any additional notes:
	* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other relevant information that may be helpful for your request.
