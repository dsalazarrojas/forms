# Team Workspace Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Workspace Setup Form is a setup form used to collect information from teams to better understand their needs and preferences for team workspaces. This form helps IT teams to configure the workspace settings based on the team's requirements and needs. The form is used to determine team leads, team members, workspace setup details, collaboration needs, access requests, data centralization, and standardization of access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team leads for your team. You can choose multiple team leads if applicable.
2. Indicate whether your team has team employees or not.
3. Provide details about your team's workspace setup.
4. Indicate your team's collaboration needs.
5. Select whether you have access requests or not.
6. Indicate whether you need to centralize your data or not.
7. Choose whether you want to standardize access or not.
8. Add any additional IT notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Leads**: Select all team leads that will be in charge of your team. This will help IT teams understand who will be responsible for different aspects of team workspaces.
* **Team Employees**: Indicate whether your team has employees or not. This will help IT teams configure the workspace settings for team members.
* **Workspace Setup Details**: Enter the details about your team's workspace setup. This will help IT teams understand your team's unique needs and preferences.
* **Collaboration Needs**: Indicate your team's collaboration needs. This will help IT teams understand how your team works together.
* **Access Requests**: Select whether you have access requests or not. This will help IT teams configure access settings for team members.
* **Centralize Data**: Indicate whether you need to centralize your data or not. This will help IT teams understand your data needs.
* **Standardize Access**: Choose whether you want to standardize access or not. This will help IT teams configure access settings for team members.
* **IT Notes**: Add any additional notes for IT teams to understand your team's needs and requirements.
