# Wallpaper Request For Browser Extension - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Wallpaper Request for Browser Extension form. The form is designed to collect feedback and requests from users, enabling the development team to improve the extension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your feedback, request, or bug report.
2. Choose the relevant options for Browser Type, Operating System, and Request Type.
3. Provide a clear and concise Description of your feedback, request, or bug report.
4. If necessary, attach a Screenshot or other relevant Attachment.
5. Enter your Email and Phone Number for contact purposes.
6. Enter your Address and Zip Code for location information.
7. Choose the Submit Date when you submit your feedback.
8. Click on the Submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: A text field where you can enter your feedback, request, or bug report.
* **Browser Info**: A text field for any specific browser-related information.
* **Contact Info**: A text field for your contact information.
* **Browser Type**: Select the type of browser you are using (Firefox, Chrome, Opera).
* **Browser Version**: Enter the version of your browser.
* **Operating System**: Choose the operating system you are using (Windows, Linux, macOS).
* **Request Type**: Select the type of request you are submitting (Bug report, Feature request, Feedback).
* **Request Priority**: Choose the priority level of your request (Low, Medium, High).
* **Description**: A text field for a clear and concise description of your feedback, request, or bug report.
* **Screenshot**: A text field for attaching a screenshot related to your feedback, request, or bug report.
* **Attachment**: A text field for attaching any other relevant files or documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for location information.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for location information.
* **Submit Date**: Choose the date when you submit your feedback.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields to avoid missing out on submitting your feedback.
* Be clear and concise in your description and provide any relevant screenshots or attachments.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* If you have any additional information to share, please do not hesitate to attach it.
* Please do not submit duplicate feedback.
