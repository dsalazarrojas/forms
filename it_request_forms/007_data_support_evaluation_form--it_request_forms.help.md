# Data Support Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your experience with our data support team, identify areas for improvement, and make necessary adjustments to better serve you and other customers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
- If you encounter any issues or have trouble with a field, let us know so we can assist you.
- Make sure to submit the form only once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Data Support Interaction (1)**: Describe your interaction with our data support team. This can include any questions, issues, or requests you had during your interaction.
- **Satisfaction Level (2)**: Choose how satisfied you are with the support you received. Options include Very High, High, Neutral, Low, and Very Low.
- **Improvement Areas (3)**: Select all areas where you think we could improve our support. You can choose one or multiple options: Communication, Timeliness, Quality of Help, Overall Experience, or Other.
- **Additional Feedback (4)**: If there's anything else you'd like to share about your experience, please provide it here. This is an opportunity to give us more detailed feedback about your interaction with our team.
- **Contact Information (5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up or clarification if needed.
- **Phone Number (6)**: If you prefer to be contacted over the phone, please enter your phone number here. This field is optional.
- **Date of Interaction (7)**: Enter the date of your interaction with our data support team. This helps us track and reference your feedback over time.
- **Time of Interaction (8)**: Enter the time of your interaction with our data support team. This helps us understand the timing of your feedback.
- **Support Channel (9)**: Choose how you initially interacted with our team. Options include Phone, Email, Chat, In Person, or Other.
- **Follow Up (10)**: Select a date for a follow-up interaction, if necessary. This helps us schedule follow-up conversations or provide you with updates on your feedback.

## Tips
- Please answer each field carefully and accurately to help us provide the best support experience for you and other customers.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, don't hesitate to contact us.
