# social_media_account_linking_support_request - Help Guide
## Purpose

The social media account linking support request form is a tool to report issues related to social media accounts connected to our system. This form helps us understand the problem and provide the necessary assistance to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of social media account you are experiencing issues with from the "Account Type" dropdown menu.
2.  Choose the current status of your account from the "Account Status" dropdown menu.
3.  Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing with your social media account in the "Issue Description" field.
4.  Attach any relevant images, videos, or audio recordings to help us better understand the problem in the "Issue Image", "Issue Video", and "Issue Audio" fields.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about the issue in the "Issue Notes" field.
6.  Provide your email address or phone number in the "Issue Email" and "Issue Phone" fields so we can contact you for further assistance.
7.  Select the date and time when the issue occurred or started occurring in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields.
8.  Specify the location where the issue occurred in the "Issue Location" field.
9.  Choose how you would like to be supported, whether by a human agent or an automated bot, in the "Issue Support Agent" field.
10.  If possible, share your screen or describe the issue in the "Issue Screenshare" field for additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Account Type**: Select the type of social media account you are experiencing issues with.
* **Account Status**: Choose the current status of your account.
* **Issue Description**: Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Issue Image**: Attach any relevant images to help us better understand the problem.
* **Issue Video**: Attach any relevant videos to help us better understand the problem.
* **Issue Audio**: Attach any relevant audio recordings to help us better understand the problem.
* **Issue Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the issue.
* **Issue Email**: Provide your email address so we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Issue Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Issue Date**: Select the date when the issue occurred or started occurring.
* **Issue Time**: Select the time when the issue occurred or started occurring.
* **Issue Location**: Specify the location where the issue occurred.
* **Issue Support Agent**: Choose how you would like to be supported.
* **Issue Screenshare**: If possible, share your screen or describe the issue for additional context.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue you are experiencing.
* Be as clear and concise in your descriptions.
* Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask your internal support team or supervisor for guidance.
