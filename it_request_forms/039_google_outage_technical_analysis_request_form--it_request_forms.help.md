# Google Outage Technical Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request analysis of a Google outage that has impacted your business. It helps us to understand the scope and effect of the outage and to identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the title of the outage in the "Google Outage Technical Analysis Request Form" field. This title should be unique and descriptive of the incident.
2.  Indicate the business impact of the outage by selecting a level of severity from the "Business Impact" dropdown menu.
3.  Describe the circumstances surrounding the outage in the "Description" field.
4.  Enter the name of the business impacted by the outage in the "Business Name" field.
5.  Enter the location of the business in the "Business Location" field.
6.  Select the start time of the outage in the "Start Time" field.
7.  Select the end time of the outage in the "End Time" field.
8.  Enter the duration of the outage in the "Duration" field.
9.  Indicate whether the start and end times are AM/PM and in the correct timezone.
10. Enter the email address of the business contact person in the "Business Email" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the business contact person in the "Business Phone" field.
12. Enter the name of the technical contact person in the "Tech Contact" field.
13. Enter any additional notes or comments about the outage in the "Notes" field.
14. Enter your name in the "Submitter" field.
15. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
16. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
17. Enter the name of your manager in the "Manager Name" field.
18. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
19. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
20. Select the type of business from the "Business Type" list.
21. Select the submitter type from the "Submitter Type" list.
22. Select the sector of the business from the "Business Sector" list.
23. Select the type of location of the business from the "Business Location Type" list.
24. Select the type of manager from the "Manager Type" list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Google Outage Technical Analysis Request Form** (1, text, required): Enter a title for the outage incident.
* **Business Impact** (2, select_one, required): Select the level of impact the outage had on your business.
* **Description** (3, text, required): Describe the circumstances surrounding the outage.
* **Business Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of the business impacted.
* **Business Location** (5, text, required): Enter the location of the business.
* **Start Time** (6, date, required): Select the start time of the outage.
* **End Time** (7, date, required): Select the end time of the outage.
* **Duration** (8, number, required): Enter the duration of the outage.
* **Start Time AM/PM** (9, text, required): Indicate whether the start time is AM or PM.
* **Start Time Timezone** (10, text, required): Enter the timezone of the start time.
* **End Time AM/PM** (11, text, required): Indicate whether the end time is AM or PM.
* **End Time Timezone** (12, text, required): Enter the timezone of the end time.
* **Business Email** (13, email, required): Enter the email address of the business contact person.
* **Business Phone** (14, text, required): Enter the phone number of the business contact person.
* **Tech Contact** (15, text, required): Enter the name of the technical contact person.
* **Notes** (16, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the outage.
* **Submitter** (17, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (18, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (19, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Manager Name** (20, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (21, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (22, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (23, text, required): Enter the department of the submitter.
* **Business Type** (24, text, required): Enter the type of business.
* **Business Type List** (25, select_multiple, required): Select the type of business from the list.
* **Submitter Type** (26, text, required): Enter the type of submitter.
* **Submitter Type List** (27, select_multiple, required): Select the type of submitter from the list.
* **Business Sector** (28, text, required): Enter the sector of the business.
* **Business Sector List** (29, select_multiple, required): Select the sector of the business from the list.
* **Business Location Type** (30, text, required): Enter the type of location of the business.
* **Business Location Type List** (31, select_multiple, required): Select the type of location of the business from the list.
* **Manager Type** (32, text, required): Enter the type of manager.
* **Manager Type List** (33, select_multiple, required): Select the type of manager from the list.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the "Notes" field.
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, please contact technical support.
