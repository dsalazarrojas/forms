# Workstation Privacy Shield Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workstation Privacy Shield Enrollment Form is used to collect information for employees who request privacy shields for their workstations. This form is a crucial part of the internal process to ensure that sensitive data is protected and individual privacy is maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Review and correct any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester** (`1`, text, required): Enter the name of the person requesting the privacy shield.
* **Request Location** (`2`, text, required): Describe the location where the requestor will be working or accessing the shielded data. 
* **Requester Name** (`3`, text, required): Enter the name of the requestor.
* **Requester Email** (`4`, email, required): Enter the email address of the requestor.
* **Requester Phone** (`5`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the requestor.
* **Requester Department** (`6`, text, required): Enter the department the requestor belongs to.
* **Requester Job Title** (`7`, text, required): Enter the job title of the requestor.
* **Workstation Description** (`8`, text, required): Describe the workstation that requires the privacy shield.
* **Workstation Location** (`9`, text, required): Describe the location of the workstation.
* **Privacy Shield Request Reason** (`10`, select_one, required): Select the reason for the request for a privacy shield (Shielded, Unshielded, Confidential).
* **Shielded Area** (`11`, select_multiple, required): Select areas where the shielded data will be accessed (Data Center, Breakroom, Conference Room).
* **Ongoing Workplace Needs** (`12`, text, optional): Describe any ongoing needs for the workspace.
* **Manager Name** (`13`, text, required): Enter the name of the requestor's manager.
* **Manager Email** (`14`, email, required): Enter the email address of the requestor's manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`15`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the requestor's manager.
* **Manager Department** (`16`, text, optional): Enter the department of the requestor's manager.
* **Manager Job Title** (`17`, text, required): Enter the job title of the requestor's manager.
* **Approved Manager Signature** (`18`, note, optional): This field is for the approved manager to sign.
* **Approved Manager Name** (`19`, text, required): Enter the name of the approved manager.
* **Approved Manager Title** (`20`, text, required): Enter the title of the approved manager.
* **Approved Date** (`21`, date, required): Enter the date of approval.
* **Approved Time** (`22`, time, required): Enter the time of approval.
* **Comments** (`23`, text, optional): Add any comments for the approval process.
* **Status** (`24`, select_one, required): Select the status of the request (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submitted By** (`25`, text, required): Enter the user who submitted the form.

**Tips**:
- Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Review and correct any errors before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals in place before submitting the form.
- Duplicate submissions will be removed to avoid confusion and ensure that only the latest version of the form is processed.
