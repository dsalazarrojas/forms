# efinsa_software_access_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to the efinsa software. Fill out the form with your application details, user information, and access requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. On the "Application details" page, provide a brief description of your application.
3. On the "User information" page, enter your name and select your role in the organization.
4. On the "Access duration" page, choose the duration of access you require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application details** (`application_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your application.
- **User name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select your role in the organization, such as "Manager", "Developer", or "Auditor".
- **Access duration** (`access_duration`, time, optional): Choose the duration of access you require, such as "Short-term" or "Long-term".
