# Internet Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for internet service requests, allowing our team to efficiently address your technical issues and resolve them in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a complete and accurate service request, follow these steps:
1. Choose the category that best describes your issue. If your issue doesn't fit into the provided category, select "Other".
2. Provide a brief description of your issue.
3. Select the type of internet service you are requesting.
4. Add any additional comments or information to help our team better understand your request.
5. If you have any further questions or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Select multiple, required: false): Choose the category that best describes your issue, or select "Other" if it doesn't fit into the provided category.
* **Description** (Text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your internet service issue or problem. The more details you give, the faster we can assist you.
* **Service Requested** (Select one, required: false): Let us know the type of internet service you are requesting help with: Dialup, Broadband, or Other.
* **Assigned To** (Select one, required: false): Please choose the team member or department responsible for handling your request, or choose from the pre-assigned options provided.
* **Date** (Date, required: false): The date will default to the current date, but you can manually enter it if you want to record a request for a specific date.
* **Comments** (Text, required: false): Add any additional information that can help our team understand and resolve your issue.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, required: false): Provide any further details or questions you have about your request.
