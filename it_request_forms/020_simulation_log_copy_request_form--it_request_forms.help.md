# Simulation Log Copy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simulation Log Copy Request Form is designed to collect information from users who want to request access to a simulation log. This form helps to gather essential details to fulfill the user's request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the log date in the "Log Date" field. This field is not required, but providing the correct date will help us to locate the specific log you are requesting.
2. Select the log type from the options provided in the "Log Type" field. This field is also not required but will help us to narrow down the search results.
3. Enter the log ID in the "Log ID" field. This field is also not required but will aid in identifying the specific log.
4. Provide a brief description of the data you are requesting in the "Description" field. This field is not required, but it will help us to understand your needs better.
5. If you are representing an organization or a company, please enter your company's name or affiliation in the "Requester" field. This field is not required but will help us to identify your affiliation.
6. Enter your reason for requesting the data in the "Request Reason" field. This field is not required but will aid in understanding your request better.
7. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact E-mail" field. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you regarding your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Log Date** (date): Enter the date you are requesting access to the simulation log.
* **Log Type** (text): Select the type of log you are requesting (e.g., simulation type, date range, etc.).
* **Log ID** (text): Enter the specific ID of the log you are requesting.
* **Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the data you are requesting.
* **Requester** (text): If you are representing an organization or company, enter your company's name or affiliation.
* **Request Reason** (text): Enter your reason for requesting the data.
* **Contact E-mail** (email): Enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you regarding your request.
