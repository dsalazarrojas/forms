# it_equipment_upgrade_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_equipment_upgrade_request_form" is a form for submitting requests for IT equipment upgrades or new equipment purchases. This form helps the IT department to quickly and accurately gather information about the user's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of request you are making. You can choose from "Hardware Upgrade", "Software Upgrade", or "Other" if your request does not fit into the other categories.
2.  Select the type(s) of equipment you are requesting. You can choose one or multiple options from "Computer", "Printer", "Monitor", "Headset", "Keyboard", "Mouse", or "Other".
3.  Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting. This will help the IT department understand your needs and preferences.
4.  Choose the priority level of your request. You can select from "High", "Medium", or "Low" to indicate how urgent your request is.
5.  Enter your contact email address. This is how the IT department will communicate with you about your request.
6.  Enter your contact phone number. This will allow the IT department to reach out to you if they need more information or have questions.
7.  Choose the user who will be assigned to work on your request. You can select from "ChatJimmy" or "Other".
8.  Provide any additional comments you would like to add to your request. This is an optional field and can be used to provide more information about your request.
9.  Choose how often you would like to receive upgrades for the selected equipment. You can select from "One Time" or "Recurring".
10.  Select the IT equipment tag that best describes the equipment you are requesting. You can choose from "Tag 1", "Tag 2", or "Tag 3".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Type** (`Request Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of request you are making, such as "Hardware Upgrade" or "Software Upgrade".
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of equipment you are requesting, such as "Computer" or "Printer".
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Priority** (`Priority`, select_one, required): Choose the priority level of your request, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address. This is how the IT department will communicate with you about your request.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number. This will allow the IT department to reach out to you if they need more information or have questions.
* **Assigned User** (`Assigned User`, select_one, required): Choose the user who will be assigned to work on your request, such as "ChatJimmy" or "Other".
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Upgrade Frequency** (`Upgrade Frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive upgrades for the selected equipment, such as "One Time" or "Recurring".
* **IT Equipment Tag** (`IT Equipment Tag`, select_one, required): Select the IT equipment tag that best describes the equipment you are requesting, such as "Tag 1", "Tag 2", or "Tag 3".
