# Stt Supervisor Confirmation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from supervisors or managers about their work, which will be used for administrative purposes such as tracking, reporting, or auditing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out **volunteer_confirmation** on the first page. Enter a brief confirmation statement about your supervisory or management role.
2. On the second page, provide the **work_details** in the text area. Describe the details of your work, including any relevant information that supports your role or responsibilities.
3. Finally, on the third page, enter your **confirm_submit** statement, which is a general confirmation to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **volunteer_confirmation** (Page 1): A brief confirmation statement about your supervisory or management role.
  - This field is optional, but it's essential to provide accurate and relevant information about your position.
  - Please keep your response concise and focused on your role.
- **work_details** (Page 2): Enter the details of your work, including any relevant information that supports your role or responsibilities.
  - This field is also optional, but it's crucial for administrative purposes.
  - Use clear and concise language to describe your work.
- **confirm_submit** (Page 3): A general confirmation statement to submit the form.
  - This field is a required confirmation to ensure the form is submitted correctly and accurately.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure the information is reliable and useful for administrative purposes.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the form administrators for clarification.
- The form is designed to be completed by supervisors or managers, so please ensure you're filling it out for the correct purpose.
