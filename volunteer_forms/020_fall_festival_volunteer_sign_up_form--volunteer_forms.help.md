# Fall Festival Volunteer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Festival Volunteer Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering at the Fall Festival event. This form helps us to organize and coordinate volunteer shifts and roles for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your name, contact information (email and phone number), and availability for the volunteer shift (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
2. Select the roles you are interested in volunteering for (e.g., 'Yes' for Yes, 'No' for No).
3. Provide your special skills or additional comments about your volunteer experience.
4. Submit the form to sign up for the Fall Festival Volunteer Program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fall Festival Volunteer Sign-Up**: This is the first page of the form where you can select the volunteer shift you are available for. Please choose from Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Select the shift that you are available to volunteer for. This will help us assign you to a specific shift.
* **Volunteer Role**: Indicate if you are interested in volunteering for any of the available roles. Please select 'Yes' for any role you are interested in and 'No' for any role you are not interested in.
* **Volunteer Availability**: Enter your availability date to ensure you can volunteer on the day of the festival.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you with any further questions or updates.
* **Volunteer Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address so we can keep you updated on event details and volunteer schedules.
* **Volunteer Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or government ID.
* **Special Skills**: If you have any special skills or qualifications that might be helpful for the festival, please describe them here.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions about volunteering, please enter them here.
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as the person who will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer Address**: Enter the address where you will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer City**: Enter the city where you will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer State**: Enter the state where you will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer Zip**: Enter the zip code of the address where you will be volunteering.
* **Volunteer Form 17**: This field is currently not described in the provided YAML, so please ignore it.
* **Volunteer Form 18**: This field is intended for selecting other volunteer forms or roles, but the options are not clearly defined in the YAML. Please assume it's a selection for other volunteer roles or forms.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select if you are a volunteer or staff member. Please assume it's a selection for the type of volunteer (e.g., festival staff, volunteer leader).
* **Volunteer Team Member**: Indicate if you are part of a volunteer team. Please assume it's a selection for team membership.
* **Volunteer Form 21**: This field is currently not described in the YAML, so please ignore it.
* **Volunteer Team**: Enter the name of the team you will be part of.
* **Volunteer Form 23**: This field is currently not described in the YAML, so please ignore it.
* **Volunteer Position**: Enter your title or position within the volunteer team.
* **Volunteer Status**: Enter your volunteer status (e.g., active, inactive, pending).

Note: The fields marked as "Volunteer Form X" (e.g., 17, 21, 23) are not clearly described in the YAML, so their labels and explanations are assumed based on the available information. Please review and update the YAML if these fields require more specific descriptions.
