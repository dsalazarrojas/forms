# Volunteer Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Volunteer Check In Form is used to record the attendance and arrival/departure times of volunteers for events or activities. This form is intended for volunteer check-in and check-out processes, ensuring accurate record-keeping and efficient management of volunteer schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the `event_info` field with the name or description of the event you are volunteering for.
2. Indicate your attendance status in the `attendance_info` field by selecting "Yes" if you are attending the event or "No" if you are not.
3. Enter your time of arrival in the `time_arrived` field using the time format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
4. Enter your time of departure in the `time_departing` field using the same time format (e.g., 17:15 for 5:15 PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the name or description of the event you are volunteering for.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_info`, select multiple, required/optional: optional): Indicate whether you are attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Arrival Time** (`time_arrived`, time, required/optional: optional): Enter your time of arrival using the time format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Departure Time** (`time_departing`, time, required/optional: optional): Enter your time of departure using the same time format (e.g., 17:15 for 5:15 PM).

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately record your arrival and departure times to ensure efficient scheduling and record-keeping.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, feel free to ask your supervisor or event organizer for clarification.
* Double-check your entries for errors before submitting the form.
