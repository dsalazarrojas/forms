# Hospice Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential volunteers who want to help out at hospices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the Personal Info section.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Share your relevant volunteer experience in the Volunteer Experience section.
4. List any medical conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer in the Medical Conditions section.
5. Describe your level of commitment to volunteering in the Volunteer Commitment section.
6. Specify your availability to volunteer in the Availability section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Personal Info</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, required/optional): 
  This section is for your personal details such as name, address, email, and phone number. Please fill in your name, address, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.
* **Contact Info** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Contact Info</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, required/optional): 
  This section is for your contact information such as email, phone number, and preferred contact method. Please fill in your email, phone number, and preferred contact method to make it easier for us to reach out to you.
* **Volunteer Experience** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Volunteer Experience</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, required/optional): 
  This section is for you to share any relevant volunteer experience you have. Please describe your previous volunteer work, including the organization, location, and duration of your volunteer work.
* **Medical Conditions** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Medical Conditions</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, required/optional): 
  This section is for you to list any medical conditions that may affect your ability to volunteer. Please be honest about any medical conditions that may impact your ability to perform volunteer tasks.
* **Volunteer Commitment** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Volunteer Commitment</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, required/optional): 
  This section is for you to describe your level of commitment to volunteering. Please answer questions such as how many hours a week you can dedicate to volunteering, and any specific tasks you are willing to perform.
* **Availability** (<span style="font-weight: bold;">Availability</span>, <span style="font-weight: bold;">text</span>, required/optional): 
  This section is for you to specify your availability to volunteer. Please answer questions such as which days and hours you are available to volunteer, and any specific dates or periods you are unavailable.
