# volunteer_opportunity_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer_opportunity_inquiry_form is used to provide information to potential volunteers about available opportunities for helping out in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the contact information section (pages 1-4) with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have the skills required for the opportunity.
3. In the skills section (page 5), select the skills you have from the dropdown list.
4. Optionally, provide a brief description of the volunteer opportunity you are inquiring about in the last text field (page 6).
5. Choose whether or not you are affiliated with an organization (page 7).
6. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Opportunity** (page 1):
	+ A brief description of the volunteer opportunity you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Name** (page 2):
	+ Fill in your name as the person inquiring about the volunteer opportunity.
* **Contact Email** (page 3):
	+ Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (page 4):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can easily contact you.
* **Skills** (page 5):
	+ Select from the list of skills you have, to help us match you with the right opportunity.
* **Volunteer Opportunity** (page 6):
	+ If you have specific skills or interests, describe them briefly.
* **Organization Affiliation** (page 7):
	+ Check "Yes" if you represent an organization that would like to offer an opportunity; "No" otherwise.
* **Volunteer Opportunity** (page 8):
	+ Enter the name of the volunteer opportunity you would like to inquire about.
	+ Note: this field is the same as page 1, for consistency and easy completion.
