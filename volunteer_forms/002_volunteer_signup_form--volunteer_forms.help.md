# Volunteer Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Signup Form is a tool used to collect information from volunteers who wish to participate in projects, tasks, or events. This form will be used to gather necessary details to assign volunteers to suitable projects or tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name and Last Name fields with your full name.
2. Provide your Contact Phone number in the Contact Phone field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Select your Participant Type (project, task, or event) from the options provided.
5. Answer Yes or No for Project and Task participation.
6. Enter the Start Date and End Date of your availability.
7. Select your Timezone from the options provided.
8. Choose your Status (active, inactive, or pending).
9. Add any additional Comments if needed.
10. Select your Participant ID from the options provided.
11. Choose your Participant Reference (internal, external, or none).
12. Add any Notes for the organization's reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required): Choose project, task, or event as your participant type.
* **Project** (`project`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes or No for project participation.
* **Task** (`task`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes or No for task participation.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your availability.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your availability.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select your time of availability.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_multiple, required): Select your timezone.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Choose active, inactive, or pending as your status.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, optional): Enter your participant ID.
* **Participant Reference** (`participant_ref`, select_one, optional): Choose internal, external, or none as your participant reference.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes for the organization's reference.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select Yes or No for assigned tool participation.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your participant name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your participant email.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your participant phone number.
* **Email Verification** (`participant_email_verified`, select_one, optional): Choose True or False for email verification.
* **Phone Verification** (`participant_phone_verified`, select_one, optional): Choose True or False for phone verification.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, optional): Choose active, inactive, or pending as your participant status.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Choose Active or Inactive as your project status.
* **Task Status** (`task_status`, select_one, optional): Choose Active or Inactive as your task status.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organization for assistance.
* Please fill out this form only once for each participant.
