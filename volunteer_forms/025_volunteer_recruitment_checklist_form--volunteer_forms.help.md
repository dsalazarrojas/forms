# volunteer_recruitment_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps the organization understand your qualifications, skills, and suitability for volunteer opportunities. Please answer each question carefully and honestly to ensure that we can place you in a role that suits your skills and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, follow the instructions on each page carefully. Each page will guide you through the different sections of the form.
2. Make sure to select all applicable answers for each section, as they are crucial for the organization to understand your qualifications and suitability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Volunteer Checklist** (`volunteer_checklist`, select_multiple, required: false)
	* Select all the volunteer opportunities that interest you and that you feel you would be a good fit for. This will help the organization understand your preferences and availability.
### 2. **Essential Tasks** (`essential_tasks`, select_one, required: true)
	* Select all the essential tasks that you are capable of performing. These tasks are mandatory for the organization and are essential for the volunteer's role.
### 3. **Qualifications** (`qualification_requirements`, select_one, required: true)
	* Choose the qualifications that you possess, such as language skills, experience, or certifications. This will help the organization understand your skills and abilities.
### 4. **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, note, required: true)
	* Provide a brief description of your volunteer experience, skills, or any other relevant information that you think would be helpful for the organization to know.
### 5. **Volunteer Requirements** (`volunteer_requirements`, select_multiple, required: true)
	* Indicate whether you have any physical, language, or technical requirements that may impact your ability to perform volunteer duties. This will help the organization understand any potential challenges you may face and ensure they can accommodate your needs.
