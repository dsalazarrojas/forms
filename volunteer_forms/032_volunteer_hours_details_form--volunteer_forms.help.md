# Volunteer Hours Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about volunteer hours worked by volunteers. It is used to track and record volunteer time spent on various activities or projects. This form is typically used by volunteers to report their hours, and by administrators to review and verify the information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your volunteer name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
2. Fill in the "Hours Spent" field with the total number of hours worked on a specific activity or project.
3. Enter the number of "Hours Left" remaining to be worked on the current project.
4. Enter the total "Volunteer Hours" worked on all projects, if applicable.
5. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a volunteer.
- **Hours Spent** (`hours_spent`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of hours you spent working on the current activity or project.
- **Hours Left** (`hours_left`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you have remaining to spend on the current project.
- **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, not required): Enter the total number of hours you have worked on all projects, if you wish to report your overall volunteer hours.
- **Volunteer Hours Form** (`volunteer_hours_form`, `text`, not required): This field is not currently in use.
- **Volunteer Hours Details** (`volunteer_hours_details_form`, `text`, not required): This field is not currently in use.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately report your volunteer hours to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact your administrator for assistance.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure accurate information is entered.
