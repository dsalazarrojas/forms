# Food Security Seminar Volunteer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help Food Security Seminar organizers collect volunteer information for their upcoming events. The form asks for details such as contact information, volunteer availability, and other relevant details to facilitate communication and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the options that apply to your volunteer availability.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
3. Add any additional comments or notes about your volunteer status.
4. Select all the options that apply to your volunteer status.
5. Enter your volunteer hours and days.
6. Select all the options that apply to your volunteer status.
