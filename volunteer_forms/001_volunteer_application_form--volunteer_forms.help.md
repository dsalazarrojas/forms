# Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for individuals to submit their information to volunteer for various tasks and activities. This form helps us understand your availability, contact information, and other relevant details to ensure a smooth and effective volunteering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Review and submit your application once you have completed all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **volunteer_application**: This is the main text field where you can introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in volunteering.
- **contact_information**: Please provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
- **volunteer_hours**: This field is to record the number of hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
- **volunteer_shifts**: Please select "Yes" if you are available to work shifts, and "No" otherwise.
- **volunteer_dates**: Choose a date when you are available to volunteer.
- **volunteer_notes**: Use this field to provide any additional information about your availability or any relevant details.
- **volunteer_email**: Please provide your email address so we can contact you for further information.
- **volunteer_phone**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergencies.
- **volunteer_message**: Use this field to communicate any other information or ask questions.
- **volunteer_form_submission**: Please select "Yes" if you are submitting this form for the purpose of volunteering, and "No" otherwise.

Note: If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please contact us at [insert contact information].
