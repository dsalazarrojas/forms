# equuvation_volunteer_appeal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equuvation Volunteer Appeal form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in becoming volunteers. The form collects personal details, volunteer skills, and interests to help us understand the applicant's fit for our volunteer opportunities. Please note that the information provided will be reviewed for relevance and non-duplication before being considered for volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the volunteer information carefully and ensure you have all necessary details prepared.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Submit the form to initiate the volunteer application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Appeal**: This field allows you to briefly describe your interest in volunteering with us. Please provide a short statement explaining why you want to volunteer with us.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like to be known as a volunteer.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your volunteer application.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Indicate the number of hours you are available to volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Skills**: Select all the volunteer skills you possess, such as Biking, Hiking, Coding, or Teaching.
* **Volunteer Interests**: Select all the volunteer interests that align with the skills you possess, such as Biking, Hiking, Coding, or Teaching.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Please provide any relevant volunteer experience you have had in the past. This can include previous volunteer work, internships, or any other relevant experience.
* **Volunteer Availability**: Indicate your availability to volunteer, including specific dates and times.
* **Volunteer References**: Please provide names and contact information for two professional references who can vouch for your volunteer experience and skills.
* **Volunteer Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to share, please include them here.
* **I Agree**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the volunteer terms and conditions.
* **I Agree Date**: Please indicate the date you agree to the volunteer terms and conditions.
* **Signature**: Please sign this form by typing your name to confirm your application.
