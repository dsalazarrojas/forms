# Covid 19 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who wish to contribute their time and resources to help with COVID-19 efforts. By filling out this form, you'll be adding your name to our list of volunteers and will be contacted by our team to schedule volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your personal information.
2. Select your medical practice from the list.
3. Choose your volunteer role(s).
4. Provide your date and time of birth.
5. Note any relevant information about your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Medical Practice**: Select the medical practice where you work or are affiliated with. This will help us schedule volunteer opportunities relevant to your field of expertise.
* **Volunteer Role**: Choose one or more roles that you're willing to take on during your volunteer work. This will help us tailor our volunteer opportunities to your skills and interests.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Note**: If you have any special skills or availability, please note it here. This will help us tailor our volunteer opportunities to your capabilities.
* **Date Available**: Enter the date you are available to start volunteering. This will help us schedule volunteer opportunities that fit your availability.
