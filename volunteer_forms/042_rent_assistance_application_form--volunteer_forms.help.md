# rent_assistance_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rent assistance application form is designed to collect essential information from applicants for rent assistance programs. This form will aid in processing and assessing the application for eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to enter your correct and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your active and accessible email address.

## Tips

- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you need help, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- If you have any questions, please reach out to us for further clarification.
