# Volunteer Coordinator Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from Volunteer Coordinators, a crucial role in our organization's volunteer programs. It allows us to understand their role, organization, and contact details, enabling us to communicate effectively and provide them with the necessary resources and support. Please fill out this form to help us better assist you in your volunteer coordination efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to enter it in the correct format (e.g., [name]@organization.com).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the name of your volunteer program in the "Volunteer Program Name" field.
6. Enter the Email address of your Volunteer Program Manager in the "Volunteer Program Manager Email" field.
7. Enter the Phone number of your Volunteer Program Manager in the "Volunteer Program Manager Phone" field.
8. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization Name" field.
9. Provide the website of your organization in the "Organization Website" field.
10. Enter your title in the "Volunteer Coordinator Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your Email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@organization.com).
* **Phone** (4): Enter your Phone number.
* **Volunteer Program Name** (5): Enter the name of your volunteer program.
* **Volunteer Program Manager Email** (6): Enter the Email address of your Volunteer Program Manager.
* **Volunteer Program Manager Phone** (7): Enter the Phone number of your Volunteer Program Manager.
* **Organization Name** (8): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Website** (9): Enter the website of your organization.
* **Volunteer Coordinator Title** (10): Enter your title.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely, as this information will be used to communicate with you and your organization.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
