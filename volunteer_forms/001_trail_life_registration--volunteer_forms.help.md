# Trail Life Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trail Life Registration form is used to collect contact and personal information from individuals and organizations that want to register themselves or their family members/youths for the life registration service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
2. Enter information about your family members/youths on the "Family Info" page.
3. Fill out the "Youth Info" page with information about any young individuals that need to be registered.
4. Provide leadership information on the "Leadership Info" page.
5. Enter your contact details on the page "Contact Details".
6. List your emergency contacts on the "Emergency Contact" page.
7. Report any medical history on the "Medical History" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details. This information will be used to contact you about your registration.
* **Family Info** (family_info, text, required/optional): Enter information about your family members that you want to register. 
* **Youth Info** (youth_info, text, required/optional): Enter information about any young individuals you want to register.
* **Leadership Info** (leadership_info, text, required/optional): Enter information about the leaders of your organization or community.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, text, required/optional): Enter the contact details of a family member or friend to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical History** (medical_history, text, required/optional): Enter any medical history or conditions that may affect your life registration.
