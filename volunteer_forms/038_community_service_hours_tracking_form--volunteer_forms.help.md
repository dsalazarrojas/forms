# Community Service Hours Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to track the community service hours of volunteers, ensuring accurate recording and verification of hours worked.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Student or Volunteer ID).
2. Choose the correct type of service you provided (e.g., Community Outreach, Environmental, Education).
3. Describe your service experience (Service Description).
4. Record the hours you worked (Hours Log).
5. Verify the information with your supervisor (Verified by Supervisor).
6. Sign and date the form (Supervisor Signature and Verification Date).
7. Add any additional comments or entries (Additional Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (Volunteer Information): Your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Student or Volunteer ID).
* **First Name** (First Name): Your first name. (Required)
* **Last Name** (Last Name): Your last name. (Required)
* **Email Address** (Email Address): Your email address. (Required)
* **Student or Volunteer ID** (Student or Volunteer ID): If applicable, please enter your student ID or volunteer ID. (Not required)
* **School or Organization** (School or Organization): The school or organization you are associated with. (Not required)
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level): If you are a student, select your grade level. (Not required)
* **Service Organization Information** (Service Organization Information): Where you volunteered. (Not required)
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name): The name of the organization you served. (Required)
* **Organization Address** (Organization Address): The address of the organization. (Required)
* **Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Name): The name of your supervisor. (Required)
* **Supervisor Email** (Supervisor Email): The email address of your supervisor. (Required)
* **Supervisor Phone** (Supervisor Phone): The phone number of your supervisor. (Not required)
* **Type of Service** (Type of Service): The type of service you provided. (Required)
* **Service Description** (Service Description): A description of your service experience. (Required)
* **Hours Log** (Hours Log): Track your hours worked. (Not required)
* **Date of Service** (Date of Service): The date you served. (Required)
* **Start Time** (Start Time): The time you started serving. (Required)
* **End Time** (End Time): The time you ended serving. (Required)
* **Break Duration** (Break Duration): In minutes, the duration of your break. (Not required)
* **Total Hours** (Total Hours): Calculated automatically. (Required)
* **Cumulative Total Hours** (Cumulative Total Hours): The running total hours. (Required)
* **Service Reflection** (Service Reflection): What did you learn from your service experience. (Not required)
* **Skills Used** (Skills Used): Select all the skills you used during your service. (Not required)
* **Verification** (Verification): Verification from your supervisor. (Not required)
* **Verified by Supervisor** (Verified by Supervisor): Confirmation from your supervisor. (Required)
* **Supervisor Signature** (Supervisor Signature): Type your supervisor's full name. (Not required)
* **Verification Date** (Verification Date): The date your supervisor verified. (Not required)
* **Do You Have Additional Entries** (Do You Have Additional Entries): If you have more entries, select 'Yes - Add More'. (Not required)
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments): Optional comments. (Not required)

## Tips

* Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid errors.
* Verify your information with your supervisor before submitting.
* Keep the form organized and easy to read.
* Use a clear and concise language when describing your service experience.
* Proofread your information before submitting.
* Keep a record of your service hours for your own reference. 

Note: This guide is a general help guide and might not cover all possible use cases. Please refer to your supervisor or the organization's guidelines for more specific instructions.
