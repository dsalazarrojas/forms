# volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer opportunity enrollment form is designed to gather information from users to enable them to participate in volunteer opportunities. This form helps users to provide their details for a specific opportunity, and it is essential to fill it out accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter the name of the volunteer opportunity you wish to enroll in.
2.  Provide your contact name and email address so that the organization can contact you.
3.  Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
4.  Select the type of volunteer opportunity you want to be part of.
5.  Choose the start and end dates of your involvement in the opportunity.
6.  Specify the time you can commit for volunteering, using the time format.
7.  Indicate your availability for shifts, such as morning, afternoon, evening, or weekend.
8.  Add any additional comments about your involvement.
9.  If you have been assigned a unique volunteer form ID, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the name of the volunteer opportunity?** (`volunteer_opportunity_name`): Enter the name of the volunteer opportunity you want to enroll in. This should match the opportunity you see in the relevant opportunity listing.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name so that the organization can contact you about the opportunity.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so that the organization can communicate with you about the opportunity.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
- **Type of Volunteer** (`volunteer_type`): Choose the type of volunteer opportunity you want to be part of, as described in the dropdown options.
- **Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`): Enter the date when you plan to start your involvement in the volunteer opportunity.
- **End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`): Enter the date when you plan to end your involvement in the volunteer opportunity.
- **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`): Enter the time you can commit to volunteering in the format specified (e.g., 08:00 for 8 am).
- **Shift** (`volunteer_shift`): Choose the shift you are available for, such as morning, afternoon, evening, or weekend.
- **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about your involvement in the opportunity.
- **Volunteer Form ID** (`volunteer_form_id`): If you have been assigned a unique volunteer form ID, enter it here to ensure accurate tracking.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information you provide to ensure it is accurate.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals to fill out this form.
- Review the relevant opportunity details for any specific requirements or instructions.
