# Voice Voluntary Organization For Initiative Citizen Empowerment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about volunteers and their programs for initiative citizen empowerment. It aims to gather details about the volunteer, their contact information, and the organization or program they are leading.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your volunteer opportunity or program.
2. Enter a brief description of your program or organization.
3. Provide your personal details, including name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the date and time of the program or organization.
5. Describe the purpose and target group of your program.
6. Specify the duration and budget of your program.
7. Mention the number of volunteers involved.
8. Enter contact information for the volunteer leader.

* **Form Id** (`form_id`, number, required: false): Enter a unique ID for this form, which helps us track your submission.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category for your volunteer opportunity or program. Options include:
	+ Volunteer Forms
	+ Community Forms
	+ Other Forms
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description about this form.

* **Email of Volunteer** (`email_of_volunteer`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.

* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
