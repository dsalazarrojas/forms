# Habitat Build Volunteer Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Habitat Build Volunteer Time Off Form is a tool for volunteers to report their time off work hours with Habitat. The form is used to ensure that volunteers and Habitat staff accurately track volunteer hours and manage volunteer schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the volunteer's name.
2. Enter the volunteer's phone number.
3. Enter the volunteer's email address.
4. Enter the volunteer's organization affiliation (if applicable).
5. Enter the name of the organization the volunteer is working with.
6. Enter the start and end times of the shift.
7. Enter the number of hours the volunteer is requesting to take off.
8. Enter the number of hours the volunteer has already completed.
9. Enter the date the volunteer was offered to take time off.
10. Enter any additional notes about the volunteer's time off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`Volunteer Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the volunteer.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`Volunteer Phone`, text, required): Enter the volunteer's phone number.
* **Volunteer Email** (`Volunteer Email`, email, required): Enter the volunteer's email address.
* **Volunteer Organization** (`Volunteer Organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the volunteer's organization (if applicable).
* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization the volunteer is working with (if applicable).
* **Shift Start Time** (`Shift Start Time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the volunteer's shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`Shift End Time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the volunteer's shift.
* **Volunteer Hours Requested** (`Volunteer Hours Requested`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the volunteer is requesting to take off.
* **Volunteer Hours Completed** (`Volunteer Hours Completed`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the volunteer has already completed.
* **Date Offered** (`Date Offered`, date, required): Enter the date the volunteer was offered to take time off.
* **Volunteer Notes** (`Volunteer Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the volunteer's time off.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields to ensure that your volunteer hours are recorded correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor for guidance.
* This form is used to record volunteer time-off hours only, not regular hours worked.
