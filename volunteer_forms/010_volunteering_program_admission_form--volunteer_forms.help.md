# Volunteering Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteering Program Admission Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in joining the volunteering program. This includes basic contact details and skills/availability information to ensure a smooth and suitable fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Indicate your availability to volunteer in the "Availability" field.
6. List any relevant skills you have in the "Skills" field.
7. For "Skills List," select one or more of the options that best describe your skills.
8. If applicable, describe your volunteer experience in the "Volunteer Experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Availability** (text, required/optional): Indicate your availability to volunteer.
* **Skills** (text, required/optional): List any relevant skills you have.
* **Skills List** (select multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the options that best describe your skills.
* **Volunteer Experience** (text, required/optional): If applicable, describe your volunteer experience.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* Be specific when listing your skills.
* Select all applicable skills that you have from the "Skills List" options.
* If you have any volunteer experience, describe it concisely but clearly.
