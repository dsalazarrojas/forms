# life_church_service_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a volunteer's service, capturing essential information such as their first name, last name, email, phone number, service date and time, service type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in the "First Name" field with your name as requested by the form.
2. Next, fill in the "Last Name" field with your surname.
3. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Fill in the "Date" field with the date you plan to provide service.
6. Select the time you will be providing service in the "Time" field.
7. Check the "Service Type" field to indicate the type of service you will be offering.
8. Fill in any additional information in the "Service Details" field, if necessary.
9. Select your volunteer status in the "Volunteer Status" field.
10. Enter your volunteer role in the "Volunteer Role" field.
11. Check if you are a member of a volunteer group in the "Volunteer Group" field.
12. Choose your volunteer shift in the "Volunteer Shift" field.
13. Select the status of the service as active or inactive in the "Service Status" field.
14. Check if you are assigned to a user in the "Assigned User" field.
15. Check if you are assigned to a group in the "Assigned Group" field.
16. Enter any additional service notes in the "Service Note" field, if necessary.
17. Fill in the "Assigned Date" field with the date you were assigned for the service.
18. Select the time you were assigned for the service in the "Assigned Time" field.
19. Enter your name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
20. Enter the name of the user assigned to you in the "Assigned User Name" field.
21. Enter the name of the group assigned to you in the "Assigned Group Name" field.
22. Fill in the location where you will be providing service in the "Service Location" field.
23. Enter any additional information about the service location in the "Service Location Note" field, if necessary.
24. Enter the location ID of the service location in the "Service Location ID" field.
