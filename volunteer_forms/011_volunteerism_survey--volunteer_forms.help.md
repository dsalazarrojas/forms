# Volunteerism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteerism Survey" form is designed to collect information from individuals who have volunteered for an organization. This data will be used to better understand their experiences and improve future volunteer opportunities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your information and experiences as a volunteer.
2. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Survey** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is the main question being asked. Please provide a brief description of your experience as a volunteer.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization** (`3`, `text`, required: false): If you are willing to share, please provide the name of the organization you volunteered with.
* **What type of experience?** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the options that best describes your experience as a volunteer:
	+ Option 1: [Insert option 1 description]
	+ Option 2: [Insert option 2 description]
	+ Option 3: [Insert option 3 description]
* **Date of last experience** (`5`, `date`, required: false): If you are able to, please provide the date of your last volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer duration** (`6`, `number`, required: false): If you are able to, please estimate the duration of your volunteer experience in hours.
* **Reason for leaving** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you are no longer volunteering, please provide a brief explanation for why you left.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false): If you are willing to share, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If you are willing to share, please enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`10`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
