# Volunteer Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about volunteer nominees for recognition and appreciation. It is used to gather details about the volunteer's service, achievements, and qualifications. The form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel to nominate deserving volunteers for recognition and awards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the relevant information for the volunteer being nominated.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator (2)**: Enter the email address of the person nominating the volunteer.
* **Title (3)**: Enter a brief title or description of the volunteer's role or achievement.
* **Department (4)**: Indicate whether the volunteer has worked in the department(s) you are nominating them for. Select all applicable departments from the list.
* **Start Date (5)**: Enter the date the volunteer started serving.
* **End Date (6)**: Enter the date the volunteer ended their service (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Name (7)**: Enter the full name of the volunteer being nominated.
* **Volunteer Position (8)**: Enter a brief description of the volunteer's role or position.
* **Volunteer Hours (9)**: Enter the number of hours the volunteer has served.
* **Comments (10)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the volunteer's service.
* **Recommendations (11)**: Enter any recommendations or testimonials from others about the volunteer.
* **Awards (12)**: Indicate whether the volunteer has received any awards or recognition.
* **Photo (13)**: Upload a photo of the volunteer (optional).
* **File (14)**: Attach any relevant files or documents supporting the nomination (optional).
* **Other Information (15)**: Enter any other relevant information about the volunteer.
* **Signature (16)**: Enter your signature as the nominator.
* **Agree (17)**: Indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the nomination.
* **Additional Comments (18)**: Enter any additional comments or questions about the nomination (optional).

Note: Required fields are marked as such in the form. Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely to ensure a successful nomination.
