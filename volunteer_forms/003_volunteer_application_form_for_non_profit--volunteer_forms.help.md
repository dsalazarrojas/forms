# Volunteer Application Form For Non Profit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Application Form For Non Profit is a tool used by non-profit organizations to collect information from potential volunteers, donors, or other applicants. This form helps the organization to understand who is interested in contributing to their cause and what type of involvement they are looking for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Choose your application type by selecting one or more options in the "Application Type" field.
6.  Choose the volunteer shift you are interested in by selecting a number in the "Volunteer Shift" field.
7.  Enter your availability in the "Availability" field using a 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00-16:00).
8.  Enter the date you are applying in the "Application Date" field.
9.  Optionally, enter any additional comments about your application in the "Comments" field.
10. Choose the non-profit organization you are interested in by selecting one or more options in the "Non Profit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Application Type**: Choose how you would like to contribute to the non-profit:
	+ Volunteer: You are interested in volunteering for the non-profit.
	+ Donor: You are interested in donating to the non-profit.
	+ Other: Choose this option if none of the above apply.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Select the shift you are available to volunteer for (if applicable).
* **Availability**: Enter the time you are available to volunteer using a 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00-16:00).
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you are applying to volunteer.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your application.
* **Non Profit**: Choose the non-profit organization you are interested in:
	+ American Red Cross: Select this option if you are interested in the American Red Cross.
	+ ASPCA: Select this option if you are interested in the ASPCA.
	+ YMCA: Select this option if you are interested in the YMCA.
