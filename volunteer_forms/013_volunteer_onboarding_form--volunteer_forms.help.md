# Volunteer Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Volunteer Onboarding Form is designed to gather essential information from new volunteers, helping them to get started with their role and responsibilities within the team. This form is used to categorize volunteers into different roles and types, providing them with a clear understanding of the tools and resources available to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Select your volunteer type (Volunteer or Non-Volunteer) and role(s).
3. Enter your availability dates and time slots.
4. Choose the tool assigned to you (chatjimmy or chatbot).
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Volunteer
* **New Volunteer (Page 1)**: This is the first page of the form, where you can enter your name and other contact details.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can reach out to you with important updates and information.

### Volunteer Info
* **Volunteer Info (Page 3)**: This is a text field where you can add any additional information about yourself or your role.

### Volunteer Type
* **Volunteer Type (Page 4)**: Select your role as either Volunteer or Non-Volunteer, depending on your level of involvement with the team.

### Volunteer Role
* **Volunteer Role (Page 5)**: Select one or more roles that you'd like to take on within the team. This will help us understand your responsibilities and how you can contribute.

### Email
* **Email (Page 6)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with important updates and information.

### Phone
* **Phone (Page 7)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you for urgent matters.

### Availability
* **Availability (Page 8)**: Enter your availability dates for volunteer work. This helps us schedule your shifts and ensure you're available to contribute.

### Availability Time
* **Availability Time (Page 9)**: Enter your availability time slots. This helps us understand when you're available to work.

### Notes
* **Notes (Page 10)**: Add any additional comments or information about your role or availability.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool (Page 11)**: Choose the tool that you'll be working with (chatjimmy or chatbot). This will provide you with access to the necessary tools and resources for your role.

### Submit
* **Submit (Page 12)**: Once you've completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send your application.
