# Volunteer Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off from volunteer duties for a specific period. The form captures essential details such as the start and end dates of the leave, the reason for the request, and the status of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "start_date" field with the date you plan to start your time off.
2. Fill in the "end_date" field with the date you plan to end your time off.
3. Provide a brief explanation for your request in the "volunteer_reason" field.
4. If applicable, select the "volunteer_status" field with the current status of your request (e.g., Approved, Denied, or Pending).
5. Finally, fill in your manager's name in the "volunteer_manager_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **start_date** (Text, Required): Enter the date you plan to start your time off in the format MM/dd/YY.
- **end_date** (Text, Not Required): Enter the date you plan to end your time off in the format MM/dd/YY.
- **volunteer_reason** (Text, Not Required): Provide a brief explanation for your request for time off.
- **volunteer_status** (Select Multiple, Not Required): Select the current status of your request (e.g., Approved, Denied, or Pending).
- **manager_name** (Text, Not Required): Enter your manager's name who is responsible for approving or denying your request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields accurately to ensure your time off is properly recorded.
- If you are unsure about the status of your request, check with your manager or the person you reported to before selecting an option.
- The volunteer status should reflect the current situation of your request.
