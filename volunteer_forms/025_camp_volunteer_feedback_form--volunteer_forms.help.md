# Camp Volunteer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Volunteer Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improvement while volunteering at our camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field's label and hint for guidance on what to fill in.
2. Fill in your name and email address accurately.
3. Select your volunteer role and the session(s) you volunteered for.
4. Choose how many hours you volunteered and rate your overall experience.
5. Rate the quality of staff support, training, and other aspects of your experience.
6. Describe your favorite and most challenging parts of volunteering.
7. Identify any skills you gained or improved.
8. Choose whether you would volunteer again and recommend our camp to others.
9. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
10. Rate communication from camp leadership and feel appreciated.
11. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Volunteer Role**: Select the role you took on during your volunteering session.
* **Session You Volunteered For**: Select the session for which you are providing feedback.
* **Total Volunteer Hours**: Choose the approximate hours you volunteered.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience as a volunteer (5 - Excellent, 4 - Very Good, 3 - Good, 2 - Fair, 1 - Poor).
* **Support from Camp Staff**: Rate staff support (5 - Excellent, 4 - Very Good, 3 - Good, 2 - Fair, 1 - Poor).
* **Quality of Volunteer Training**: Rate the quality of the training provided (5 - Excellent, 4 - Very Good, 3 - Good, 2 - Fair, 1 - Poor, or Did not receive training).
* **Clarity of Your Role and Responsibilities**: Rate how clear your role and responsibilities were (Very clear, Mostly clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Very unclear).
* **Resources and Materials Provided**: Rate the adequacy of resources and materials provided (More than adequate, Adequate, Somewhat adequate, Inadequate, Very inadequate).
* **Experience Interacting with Campers**: Rate your experience interacting with campers (Extremely rewarding, Very rewarding, Rewarding, Neutral, Challenging, Very challenging).
* **Workload Manageability**: Rate how manageable your workload was (Very manageable, Manageable, Somewhat manageable, Overwhelming at times, Too overwhelming).
* **Schedule Flexibility**: Rate the flexibility of your schedule (Very flexible, Somewhat flexible, Neutral, Inflexible, Very inflexible).
* **Favorite Part of Volunteering**: Describe your favorite part of volunteering.
* **Most Challenging Part of Volunteering**: Describe the most challenging part of your experience.
* **Skills Gained or Improved**: Select all the skills you gained or improved as a volunteer.
* **Would You Volunteer Again**: Indicate whether you would volunteer again (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Maybe, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Recommend to Others**: Indicate whether you would recommend our camp to others (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Maybe, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Improvements Suggested**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* **Additional Training Needed**: Provide any additional training that would be helpful.
* **Communication from Camp Leadership**: Rate communication from camp leadership (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Feel Appreciated**: Rate how you felt appreciated as a volunteer (Very appreciated, Appreciated, Somewhat appreciated, Neutral, Not appreciated).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
