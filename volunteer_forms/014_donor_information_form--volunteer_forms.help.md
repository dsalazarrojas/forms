# Donor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Information Form is designed to collect accurate and up-to-date information from donors, including their contact details, donation preferences, and donation history. This information is essential for efficient communication and tailor-made donation campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: full name, email, donor type, donation frequency, and gift frequency.
2. Provide accurate contact information (street, city, state, and zip) if you have it.
3. Select the correct donor type (individual, business, or organization).
4. Choose your preferred donation frequency (once, regularly, or sporadically).
5. Choose your preferred gift frequency (once, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, or as needed).
6. Fill out the optional fields: phone number, street, city, state, and zip.
7. Provide any additional notes for the donor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (donor_contact_info): Enter your name and contact details, if available.
* **Full Name** (donor_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (donor_email): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (donor_phone): Enter your phone number for efficient communication.
* **Street** (donor_street): Enter your street address.
* **City** (donor_city): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (donor_state): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip** (donor_zip): Enter your zip code.
* **Donor Type** (donor_donor_type): Select whether you are an individual, business, or organization.
* **Donation Frequency** (donor_donation_frequency): Choose how frequently you make donations (once, regularly, or sporadically).
* **Gift Frequency** (donor_gift_frequency): Choose how frequently you make gifts (once, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, or as needed).
* **First Donation Date** (donor_first_donation_date): If you have a record, enter the date of your first donation.
* **Last Donation Date** (donor_last_donation_date): If you have a record, enter the date of your last donation.
* **Total Donated** (donor_total_donated): Enter the total amount donated.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or notes for the donor.
