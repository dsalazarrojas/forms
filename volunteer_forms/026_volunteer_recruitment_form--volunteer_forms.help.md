# Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with our organization. This information will be used to match them with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Provide a valid **Email Address** that we can use to contact you.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
4. Select the days you are available to volunteer.
5. Choose your available time slots (morning, afternoon, or evening).
6. Select your preferred **Volunteer Role** (Volunteer, Organizer, or Both).
7. If you have any volunteer experience, please describe it in the **Volunteer Experience** field.
8. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name and phone number.
9. Optional: Provide your **Availability** if you have a specific date in mind you can be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A required field where you enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name**: A required field where you enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email**: A required field where you enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: A required field where you enter your phone number so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Days Available**: Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer (Monday to Sunday).
* **Available Time**: Choose the time slots you are available to volunteer (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Volunteer Role**: Select your preferred role (Volunteer, Organizer, or Both).
* **Volunteer Experience**: Optional field for describing any volunteer experience you have.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Optional field for providing the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Optional field for providing your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Availability**: Optional field for specifying a date you are available to volunteer.
