# Giveaway Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to register individuals who are interested in participating in a giveaway event. The form collects essential information about the registrant, including their name, contact details, event preferences, and other relevant data. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, providing you with the necessary information to complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Full Name** field (Field 1).
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field (Field 2).
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field (Field 3).
4. Select your preferred contact method in the **Contact Method** field (Field 4).
5. Indicate your gender in the **Gender** field (Field 5).
6. Enter the date of the event you are interested in attending in the **Event Date** field (Field 6).
7. Enter the time of the event you are interested in attending in the **Event Time** field (Field 7).
8. Provide any additional information about yourself or your interests in the **Additional Info** field (Field 8). (Optional)
9. Select your area of interest (if applicable) in the **Interest** field (Field 9).
10. If you have a referral, please enter their name or contact information in the **Referral** field (Field 10). (Optional)
11. If you have volunteered previously, please report the number of hours you have volunteered in the **Volunteer Hours** field (Field 11). (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Field 1)**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **Email (Field 2)**: Enter your valid email address. This will be used to contact you regarding the giveaway event.
* **Phone Number (Field 3)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for the event.
* **Contact Method (Field 4)**: Select how you would like to be contacted for the event, such as email, phone, or in-person.
* **Gender (Field 5)**: Select your gender from the options provided. This information will help us tailor the event experience to your needs.
* **Event Date (Field 6)**: Enter the date of the event you are interested in attending.
* **Event Time (Field 7)**: Enter the time of the event you are interested in attending. Use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Additional Info (Field 8)**: Provide any relevant information about yourself that may help us tailor the event experience.
* **Interest (Field 9)**: If applicable, select your area of interest for the event.
* **Referral (Field 10)**: If you were referred to the event by someone, please enter their name or contact information.
* **Volunteer Hours (Field 11)**: If you have volunteered in the past, report the number of hours you have volunteered. This will help us verify your eligibility for the giveaway event.

## Tips

* Please double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time for the event you are interested in attending.
