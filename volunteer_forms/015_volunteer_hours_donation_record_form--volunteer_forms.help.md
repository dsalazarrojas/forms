# Volunteer Hours Donation Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track volunteer hours donated to an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the volunteer who donated the hours.
2. Enter the date and time you donated the hours.
3. Enter the number of hours you donated.
4. Enter the name of the organization you were working with.
5. Enter a brief description of your role and any relevant details about your volunteer work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the volunteer who donated the hours.
* **Date Donated** (`date_donated`, date, required): Enter the date and time you donated the hours. Please use the format `MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Time Donated** (`time_donated`, time, required): Enter the time you started and ended your volunteer work. Please use the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you donated.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you were working with.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your role and any relevant details about your volunteer work.
* **Hours Donated Description** (`hours_donated_description`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your volunteer work.

## Tips
* Make sure to only submit a single entry for each volunteer's hours donated.
* Be as detailed as possible with your entry to help the organization track and verify your volunteer hours.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organization for clarification.
