# Volunteer Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Recommendation Form is used to collect information about a volunteer's qualifications, work habits, and other relevant details from recommenders. This form helps assess the volunteer's character and reliability.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the volunteer's information, such as their title.
2. Answer the questions about your relationship with the volunteer, including whether you have a direct working relationship.
3. Provide details about the volunteer's work habits, such as their start and end dates, and hours worked per week.
4. Sign the form to confirm your recommendation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer (Volunteer)**: Enter the volunteer's title.
* **Recommender (Recommender)**: Choose whether you have a direct or indirect relationship with the volunteer.
* **Recommender Name (Recommender Name)**: Enter your name as the recommender.
* **Recommender Email (Recommender Email)**: Enter your email address as the recommender.
* **Recommender Phone (Recommender Phone)**: Enter your phone number as the recommender.
* **Recommender Position (Recommender Position)**: Enter your position or title as the recommender.
* **Recommender Organization (Recommender Organization)**: Enter your organization or institution as the recommender.
* **Recommender Relationship (Recommender Relationship)**: Choose whether you have a direct or indirect relationship with the volunteer.
* **Recommender Start Date (Recommender Start Date)**: Enter the volunteer's start date.
* **Recommender End Date (Recommender End Date)**: Enter the volunteer's end date.
* **Recommender Hours Worked (Recommender Hours Worked)**: Enter the volunteer's hours worked per week.
* **Recommender Hours per Week (Recommender Hours per Week)**: Enter the volunteer's average hours worked per week.
* **Recommender Additional Comments (Recommender Additional Comments)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the volunteer.
* **Recommender Signature (Recommender Signature)**: Sign the form to confirm your recommendation.
* **Recommender Date (Recommender Date)**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Recommender Time (Recommender Time)**: Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Agree (Agree)**: Choose whether you agree with the volunteer's qualifications.
