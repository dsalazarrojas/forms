# Volunteer Animal Rescue Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Animal Rescue Application Form is a tool used by animal rescue organizations to collect information from potential volunteers. This form helps the organization assess the applicant's suitability for volunteering and ensures that they have the necessary contact details, availability, and experience required for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Volunteer Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Volunteer Contact Info" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Volunteer Address" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Volunteer Email" field.
5. Select your availability in the "Volunteer Availability" field. Please use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD) and time format (HH:MM).
6. For the "Volunteer Availability" field again, select your availability for the second time to confirm.
7. Choose your relevant experience from the "Volunteer Experience" field. Select one or more options that apply to you.
8. Select any references you have provided from the "Volunteer References" field. Select one or more options that apply to you.
9. Finally, provide any additional information in the "Volunteer Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name** (Text, required): Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Volunteer Contact Info** (Text, required): Enter your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Volunteer Address** (Text, required): Enter your home address or any other address where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Availability** (Date, required): Select a date when you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Availability** (Time, required): Select a time when you are available to volunteer, matching the date selected above.
* **Volunteer Experience** (Select One, required): Choose one of the following options that apply to you:
	+ Option 1: I have previous experience in animal care and handling.
	+ Option 2: I have previous experience in customer service.
	+ Option 3: I have previous experience in other relevant areas.
* **Volunteer References** (Select Multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Option 1: I have a professional reference from an animal shelter or rescue organization.
	+ Option 2: I have a personal reference from a friend or family member.
	+ Option 3: I do not have a reference.
* **Volunteer Additional Info** (Note, required): Provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to your application.
