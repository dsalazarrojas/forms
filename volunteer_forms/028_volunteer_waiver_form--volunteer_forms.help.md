# Volunteer Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from volunteers, including their personal details, emergency contact information, and terms of volunteering. This information is vital for ensuring the well-being and safety of both the volunteer and the individuals they are assisting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your current address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. For your emergency contact, fill in their name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
7. Specify your relationship with the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
8. Enter the contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
9. Enter the contact's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
10. If you have any additional information about your emergency contact, please provide it in the "Emergency Contact Note" field.
11. If you are volunteering, select the start date of your volunteer role in the "Volunteer Start Date" field.
12. If applicable, select the end date of your volunteer role in the "Volunteer End Date" field.
13. Confirm your terms and conditions of volunteering by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
14. Finally, select your volunteer role in the "Volunteer Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "month/day/year".
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required): Enter the contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, `date`, required): If you are volunteering, select the start date of your volunteer role.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, select the end date of your volunteer role.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your terms and conditions of volunteering by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, `text`, optional): Select your volunteer role.
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you have signed the waiver by selecting "Yes" or "No".
