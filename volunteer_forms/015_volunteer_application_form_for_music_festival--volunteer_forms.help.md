# Volunteer Application Form For Music Festival - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering at the music festival. By completing this form, you will be able to provide your contact details, emergency contact information, shift preferences, and application details, which will be reviewed by the festival organizers to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Fill in the required fields with your information.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details for each field.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. Please provide your real name and a valid phone number and email address so that we can get in touch with you.

### Emergency Contact Details
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is used to collect information about your emergency contact. Please provide the name, phone number, and email address of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.

### Shift and Availability
* **Shift and Availability** (`shift_and_availability`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is used to collect information about your shift preferences. Please provide the times you are available to work and any specific shifts or tasks you are interested in.

### Application Details
* **Application Details** (`application_details`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is used to collect more information about your application. Please provide any additional details that will help us understand your skills, experience, and qualifications.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is used to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of volunteering. Please read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact details and emergency contact information for accuracy.
* Be specific about your shift preferences and availability.
* Provide clear and concise application details to help us understand your qualifications.
* Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting your application.
