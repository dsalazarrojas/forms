# Fundraiser Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details and payment information for a fundraiser. It is intended for individuals or groups to purchase items or services from a fundraiser event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the **Order Details** page, enter your name and any other relevant information about your order.
2. On the **Order Items** page, select the items you would like to purchase.
3. On the **Order Total** page, enter the total cost of your order.
4. On the **Payment Method** page, select your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about your order.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, true): Select one or more items you would like to purchase from the available options.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, true): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, true): Select your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).

## Tips
- Make sure to select only the items you want to purchase.
- Enter the correct total cost of your order.
- Ensure you select a valid payment method.
- Review your order carefully before submitting the form.
