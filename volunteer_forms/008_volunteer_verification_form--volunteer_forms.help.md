# volunteer_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer verification form is used to collect and validate the personal and professional information of volunteers in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the designated fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your Address in the Address field.
5. Specify your City in the City field.
6. Enter your Zip code in the Zip field.
7. Enter your Date Of Birth in the Date Of Birth field.
8. Select the number of Volunteer Hours you have completed in the Volunteer Hours field.
9. Choose your Volunteer Experience from the options provided in the Volunteer Experience field.
10. Select the Tool that you are most familiar with or have used in the past in the Assigned Tool field.
11. If you are not familiar with any of the listed Tools, please enter the name of the Tool you are most familiar with in the Assigned Tool Other field.
12. Select your current Volunteer Status from the options provided in the Volunteer Status field.
13. Add any additional Notes about your volunteer experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that you use for communication with the program.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your physical address where you reside.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Zip** (number, required): Enter the zip code of your residence.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year).
* **Volunteer Hours** (number, optional): Enter the total number of hours you have volunteered with the program (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Volunteer Experience** (select_multiple, required): Choose from the options provided the areas where you have volunteered with the program.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required): Select the tool that you are most familiar with or have used in the past.
* **Assigned Tool Other** (text, optional): If you are not familiar with any of the listed Tools, enter the name of the Tool you are most familiar with.
* **Volunteer Status** (select_one, required): Select your current volunteer status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience.
