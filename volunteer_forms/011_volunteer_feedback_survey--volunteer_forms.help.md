# Volunteer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from volunteers who have participated in events managed by the organization. The feedback will be used to refine the event management process, identify areas for improvement, and enhance the overall volunteer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the introduction and understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Select the volunteer event you would like to provide feedback on from the "Which volunteer event" dropdown menu.
3. Provide your feedback in the "Feedback" text field.
4. Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Rate this volunteer event" field.
5. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" text field.
6. If you didn't select an event from the dropdown menu, specify the event in the "Other" text field.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact info" text field, including your name and any other relevant details.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, this will help us to contact you for further clarification if needed.
9. Finally, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, not required): This field is for you to enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Please provide your honest feedback about your experience at the event. This will help us to understand what we did well and what we could improve.
* **Rate this volunteer event** (`rating`, number, not required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Which volunteer event** (`volunteer_event`, select_one, not required): Select the event you would like to provide feedback on from the dropdown menu. If the event is not listed, you can specify it in the "Other" field.
* **Other** (`volunteer_event_other`, text, not required): If the event is not listed in the dropdown menu, enter the name of the event you would like to provide feedback on.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need further clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for further clarification.
