# Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA" form is designed to help the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) record and manage information about fundraisers organized by the school. This form helps to track and promote fundraising events, providing a streamlined process for school staff and PTA members.

## How To Complete This Form

To get started, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the **Fundraiser Title** (Field 1) with a brief and descriptive title of the fundraiser event.
* Enter a **Page Title** (Field 2) that captures the essence of the fundraiser event.
* Select a **Form Category** (Field 3) to classify the fundraiser as either "Volunteer Forms" or "Other" (e.g., "Volunteer Forms" for events that involve volunteers, "Other" for events that don't).
* Provide a **Form Description** (Field 4) that explains the details of the fundraiser event.
* Choose the **Share Methods** (Field 5) you want to use to share the fundraiser information, such as text, email, or social media.
* Optionally, share additional information about **Page Share** (Field 6) where you can provide more details about how you'd like to share the fundraiser event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Fundraiser Title (Fundraiser Title)**: Enter a brief and descriptive title for the fundraiser event.
* **2. Page Title (Page Title)**: Enter a short title that captures the essence of the fundraiser event.
* **3. Form Category (Form Category)**: Choose between "Volunteer Forms" or "Other" to classify the fundraiser event.
*   * "Volunteer Forms" for events that involve volunteers, 
     * "Other" for events that don't involve volunteers.
* **4. Form Description (Form Description)**: Enter a detailed description of the fundraiser event.
* **5. Share Methods (Share Methods)**: Choose one or more methods to share the fundraiser information, such as text, email, or social media.
* **6. Page Share (Page Share)**: Enter additional information about how you'd like to share the fundraiser event, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to keep your responses brief and clear, but also detailed enough to communicate the essence of the fundraiser event.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authority in the school for guidance.
* Remember to review and update the form regularly to ensure accuracy and consistency across all fundraiser events.
