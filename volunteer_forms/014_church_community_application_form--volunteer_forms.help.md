# Church Community Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Community Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in becoming a part of the church community, including volunteers for various ministries and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full legal name in the "Applicant name" field.
2.  Enter your email address for application updates and communication.
3.  Provide your phone number for best contact.
4.  Enter your address, if applicable (this is not required but helpful for us).
5.  Select one or more areas of interest from the list (Youth ministry, Outreach, Worship team, Hospitality, and Administration).
6.  Mention your typical availability for volunteering (weekday evenings, weekends, or other).
7.  Briefly describe your previous experience, if applicable.
8.  If you have a reference, enter their name.
9.  Confirm your consent for a background check for certain roles.
10.  Add any additional comments you think are helpful for the team to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant name** (Required): Enter your full legal name.
*   **Email address** (Required): Enter the email address we can use for application updates and communication.
*   **Phone number** (Required): Enter your best contact phone number.
*   **Address** (Optional): Enter your city and postal code, if applicable.
*   **Areas of interest** (Required, Select Multiple): Choose one or more of the options:
    *   Youth ministry
    *   Outreach
    *   Worship team
    *   Hospitality
    *   Administration
*   **Typical availability** (Required): Enter your typical weekday and weekend availability.
*   **Previous experience** (Optional): Briefly describe your previous experience, if applicable.
*   **Reference name** (Optional): If you have a reference, enter their name.
*   **Consent to background check** (Required, Select One): Confirm your consent for a background check for certain roles.
*   **Additional comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments or information you think the team should know.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Choose your areas of interest carefully, as this will help us place you in the right role.
*   Be honest about your previous experience and availability to help us match you with the right opportunities.
*   If you have a reference, please make sure to ask their permission before sharing their name.
*   Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
