# Clothing Donation Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Clothing Donation Valuation Form" is designed for donors to provide information about clothing items they are donating to an organization. This form helps to accurately evaluate the value of the items, which is crucial for tax purposes and inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the donor information section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the type of clothing item being donated (e.g., shirt, pants, dress, etc.).
3. Provide details about the item, such as brand, color, size, and condition.
4. Describe any damage or missing parts (e.g., stains, holes, missing buttons, etc.).
5. Indicate whether the item was washed before donation.
6. Estimate the original purchase price and current value of the item.
7. Enter the quantity of the item being donated.
8. Calculate the total donation value for all items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name**: Enter your full name as the donor.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Donation Date**: Enter the date the item was donated.
* **Type of Clothing Item**: Select the type of clothing item being donated (e.g., shirt, pants, dress, etc.).
* **Brand Name**: If known, enter the brand name of the item.
* **Color**: Enter the color of the item (e.g., blue, red, black, etc.).
* **Size**: Enter the size of the item (e.g., 32, XL, M, etc.).
* **Gender Category**: Choose the gender category of the item (e.g., Mens, Womens, Childrens, Unisex).
* **Overall Condition**: Choose the overall condition of the item (e.g., like new, very good, good, fair, poor).
* **Stains, Holes, or Visible Damage?**: Indicate if there are any stains, holes, or visible damage on the item.
* **Describe Any Damage**: If there is any damage, describe it in detail.
* **Missing Parts?**: Indicate if any parts (e.g., buttons, zippers) are missing.
* **What is Missing?**: If parts are missing, describe what is missing.
* **Was Item Washed Before Donation?**: Indicate if the item was washed before donation.
* **Original Purchase Price**: Estimate the original purchase price of the item (in USD).
* **Estimated Current Value**: Estimate the current value of the item based on its condition and brand (in USD).
* **Quantity of This Item**: Enter the quantity of the item being donated.
* **Total Donation Value**: Calculate the total donation value for all items (in USD).
* **Special Notes**: Add any special notes or unique features about the item.
