# military_missions_volunteer_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers participating in military missions or events. Please fill it out accurately and honestly to ensure that your information is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. If a field is marked as "required", it is essential to fill it out for the form to be considered complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mission Information**: This field is for providing a brief description of the mission or event you will be participating in. Please enter the specific details of the mission or event in this field.
* **Name**: This field is for your name, which will be used to identify you as a volunteer. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address, which will be used for communication purposes. Please double-check the accuracy of your email address.
* **Confirm Email**: This field is for confirming your email address. Please enter the same email address you entered in the "Email" field.
* **Phone**: This field is for your contact phone number. Please enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Mission Date**: This field is for the date of the mission or event. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD" when filling out this field.
* **Mission Time**: This field is for the time of the mission or event. Please use the format "HH:MM AM/PM" when filling out this field.
* **Mission Location**: This field is for the location of the mission or event. Please enter the specific location of the event.
* **Mission Description**: This field is for providing a brief description of the mission or event. Please enter the specific details of the mission or event in this field.
* **Volunteer Information**: This field is for providing any additional information about yourself as a volunteer. Please enter any relevant details about your volunteer role or responsibilities.
* **Military ID Number**: This field is for entering your military ID number. Please enter the correct ID number if applicable.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or issues, do not hesitate to contact the organizers for assistance.
