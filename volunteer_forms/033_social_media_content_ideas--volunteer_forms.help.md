# Social Media Content Ideas - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Social Media Content Ideas" form is designed to collect and organize social media content ideas across various platforms and categories.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Category**: Select the category that best fits your content idea. Choose from "Volunteer Forms" or "Other Forms".
2. Fill in the **Description** field with a brief summary of your content idea.
3. **Social Media Platforms**: Select the platforms where your content will be published. Choose one or multiple options: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, or any other platform of your choice.
4. **Audience**: Specify the target audience for your content. This could be a specific group of people, a demographics, or any other relevant detail.
5. **Content Type**: Select the type of content you have in mind: Video, Text Post, or Image.
6. **Content**: Fill in the field with the main content or idea you want to share.
7. **Tags**: Add any relevant tags to help categorize and filter your content.
8. **Published By**: If the content has been published, mention who published it.
9. **Published At**: If the content has been published, indicate when it was published.
10. **Updated By**: If the content has been updated, mention who updated it.
11. **Updated At**: If the content has been updated, indicate when it was updated.
12. **Created At**: Mention when the content was created.
13. **Created By**: Indicate who created the content.
14. **Updated At 2**: Mention any additional update dates for the content.
15. **Tags 2**: Add any additional tags for the content.
16. **Created By 2**: Indicate who created the additional content.
17. **Created By 3**: Indicate who created any additional content.
18. **Social Media Link**: Provide a link to the social media post or content.
19. **URL**: Enter the URL of the content or post.
20. **URL Title**: Add a title for the URL.
21. **URL Title (English)**: Add a title for the URL in English.
22. **URL Title (Italian)**: Add a title for the URL in Italian.
23. **URL Description**: Provide a brief description for the URL.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can fill out as much or as little information as you need.
