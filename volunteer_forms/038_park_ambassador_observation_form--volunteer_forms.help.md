# park_ambassador_observation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect observations from volunteers on park conditions, helping to identify areas that require maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct observation status for your report.
2. Choose the correct photo type for your observation.
3. Enter the date and time of your observation.
4. Add any comments or notes you want to include with your report.
5. Upload your photos to the designated photo path.
6. Enter any other relevant details about your observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Observation Status**: Choose the status of your observation. Options include:
	+ Park Volunteer: This status indicates that the area is in good condition and no maintenance is required.
	+ Maintenance: This status indicates that maintenance is needed for the area.
	+ Maintenance - Needed: This status indicates that maintenance is needed, but not urgently.
	+ Not Applicable: This status indicates that the area does not apply to your observation.
* **Photo Type**: Choose the type of photo taken for your observation. Options include:
	+ Park Bench
	+ Park Bench - Alternative
	+ Park Picnic Table
	+ Park Picnic Table Alternative
* **Observation Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of your observation.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes you want to include with your report.
* **Photo Path**: Enter the path to the photo you have uploaded to the server.
* **Latitude and Longitude**: Enter the latitude and longitude coordinates of your observation.
* **Photo URL**: Enter the URL of your observation photo.
* **Observation Notes**: Enter any additional notes or details about your observation.
* **Photo Taken**: Enter the date and time the photo was taken.
* **Park Location**: Enter the location of your observation.
* **Observation Date**: Enter the date of your observation.
* **Observation Time**: Enter the time of your observation.
* **Location Description**: Enter a description of the location of your observation.
* **Photo File**: Enter the path to the photo file you have uploaded.
* **Observation Area**: Enter the area of your observation.
* **Submission Status**: Choose the status of your submission. Options include:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending Review
	+ Rejected
