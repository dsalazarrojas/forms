# Volunteer Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather more details about volunteer agreements, allowing teams to have a better understanding of the volunteer's status and commitments. It's an essential tool for volunteer management, ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the volunteer's name and any other required information.
3. Select the start and end dates of the agreement.
4. Indicate whether the volunteer is active or inactive.
5. Choose any relevant roles the volunteer is assigned to.
6. Specify the volunteer's status.
7. Enter the volunteer's contact information.
8. Provide an email address for the volunteer (if available).
9. Add any additional notes about the volunteer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization name** (Text, Optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **volunteer name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the volunteer.
* **start date** (Date, Optional): Select the date when the volunteer agreement started.
* **end date** (Date, Optional): Select the date when the volunteer agreement ends.
* **role** (Select, Optional): Indicate if the volunteer is in a role or not.
* **Volunteer Role** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose any relevant roles the volunteer is assigned to.
* **status** (Select, Optional): Choose the volunteer's status: Active or Inactive.
* **contact info** (Text, Optional): Enter the volunteer's contact information.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the volunteer's email address.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional information about the volunteer.
