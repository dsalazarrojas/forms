# GoCardless Volunteer Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with GoCardless. Please complete the form with your personal details and contact information to sign up as a volunteer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you have access to.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Provide your Address in the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name, e.g., John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name, e.g., Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address, e.g., johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD), e.g., 1990-01-01.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address, e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and valid information for each field.
* Use a valid email address that you have access to.
* Use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for the "Date Of Birth" field.
* Enter your phone number with the correct format, e.g., 123-456-7890.
