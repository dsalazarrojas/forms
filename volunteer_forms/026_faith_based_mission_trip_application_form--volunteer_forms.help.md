# faith_based_mission_trip_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faith-Based Mission Trip Application Form is designed to gather information from volunteers participating in faith-based missions. This form helps us understand the participant's background, trip details, contact information, payment details, and other relevant aspects that are essential for a successful mission trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information on Page 1, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal information.
2. On Page 2, describe the trip details, such as the destination, dates, and any special requests or requirements.
3. On Page 3, enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
4. On Page 4, provide payment details, such as your preferred payment method and any additional instructions.
5. Review and agree to the Participant Agreement on Page 5, which outlines the terms and conditions of the trip.
6. If applicable, provide medical clearance and any necessary medical information on Page 6.
7. On Page 7, list any skills or experience you have that are relevant to the mission trip.
8. Finally, provide up to three participant references on Page 8, including their name, organization, and contact information.

* **Participant Information** (Page 1):
	+ <name> (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ <type> (Text): Please enter your name in the format as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ <required/optional> (Optional): Not required for this field.
