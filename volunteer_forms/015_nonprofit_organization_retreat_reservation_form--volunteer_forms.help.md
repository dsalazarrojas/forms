# Nonprofit Organization Retreat Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for Nonprofit Organization retreat participants to provide information about themselves and their participation in the retreat. The form ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date information to plan and prepare for the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Add any additional notes about yourself in the "Participant Notes" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Participant Address" field.
6. Select your city from the drop-down list in the "Participant City" field.
7. Enter your state in the "Participant State" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "Participant Zip" field.
9. Select your country from the drop-down list in the "Participant Country" field.
10. Enter your date of birth in the "Participant Date of Birth" field.
11. Select your preferred time of participation in the "Participant Time of Participation" field.
12. Enter the dates you will be participating in the retreat in the "Participant Retreat Start Date" and "Participant Retreat End Date" fields.
13. Select your participation type in the "Participant Retreat Type" field.
14. Choose your attendance options in the "Participant Attendance" field.
15. Add any additional information about your participation in the "Participant Retreat Participation" field.
16. If you have any other information to share, add it in the "Participant Retreat Participation Other" field.
17. Add any additional details about your participation in the "Participant Retreat Participation Other Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your active email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, required): Select your country of origin.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Participant Time of Participation** (`participant_time_of_participation`, time, required): Select your preferred time of participation in the retreat.
* **Participant Retreat Type** (`participant_retreat_type`, select_one, required): Select your type of participation in the retreat.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, select_multiple, required): Choose your attendance options for the retreat.
* **Participant Retreat Participation** (`participant_retreat_participation`, text, required): Add any information about your participation in the retreat.
* **Participant Retreat Participation Other** (`participant_retreat_participation_other`, text, required): If you have any other information to share, add it here.
* **Participant Retreat Participation Other Details** (`participant_retreat_participation_other_details`, text, required): Add any additional details about your participation.
* **Participant Retreat Participation Other Details Other** (`participant_retreat_participation_other_details_other`, text, required): If you need to share more details, add them here.
* **Participant Retreat Participation Other Details Other Details** (`participant_retreat_participation_other_details_other_details`, text, required): Add any other additional details.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
