# Volunteer Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register as volunteers for our organization. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can effectively manage volunteer roles and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Indicate the type of role you're interested in volunteering for (e.g., General, Administrative, Events, Fundraising, or Other).
3.  Specify how often you'd like to volunteer (e.g., One-time, Ongoing, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
4.  Select how many hours you can commit per week.
5.  Fill out your emergency contact information, including their name, relationship, phone number, email address, address, and country.
