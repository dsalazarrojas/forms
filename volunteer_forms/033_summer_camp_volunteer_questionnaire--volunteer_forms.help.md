# Summer Camp Volunteer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your availability and preferences as a volunteer for the summer camp event. Please complete this form to provide your details and help us make the best use of your volunteer time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, as it will help us to better match you with available volunteer opportunities.
2. Review the form before submitting to ensure you have included all required fields and accurate information.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the summer camp staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name (Volunteer Name)** (`Volunteer Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be listed in our records.
* **Volunteer Email (Volunteer Email)** (`Volunteer Email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Phone (Volunteer Phone)** (`Volunteer Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Preferred Contact Method (Volunteer Preferred Contact Method)** (`Volunteer Preferred Contact Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, Text Message, or In Person.
* **Volunteer Preferred Language (Volunteer Preferred Language)** (`Volunteer Preferred Language`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, or Other).
* **Volunteer Available Time (Volunteer Available Time)** (`Volunteer Available Time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time when you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Available Days (Volunteer Available Days)** (`Volunteer Available Days`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all days you are available to volunteer (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Volunteer Experience (Volunteer Experience)** (`Volunteer Experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your experience with summer camps (Summer Camps, Camp Counselor, Group Leader, or Other).
* **Volunteer Vehicles (Volunteer Vehicles)** (`Volunteer Vehicles`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all vehicles you own or have access to (Car, Truck, Bike, or Other).
* **Volunteer Medical Conditions (Volunteer Medical Conditions)** (`Volunteer Medical Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all medical conditions you have (None, Allergies, Medical Condition 1, Medical Condition 2).
* **Volunteer Medical Emergencies (Volunteer Medical Emergencies)** (`Volunteer Medical Emergencies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all medical emergencies you can handle (First Aid, CPR, EpiPen, or Other).
* **Volunteer Emergency Contacts (Volunteer Emergency Contacts)** (`Volunteer Emergency Contacts`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please enter at least one emergency contact (None, Name1, Name2, Name3).
