# Volunteer Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Certificate Form is designed to collect information for volunteer certificates, allowing volunteers or organizations to track and verify volunteer hours, activities, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to be completed, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. Enter your volunteer information, including your name and any other relevant details.
3. Enter the organization's information, including their name and logo.
4. Fill out the volunteer hours and description fields, providing accurate information about your volunteer hours.
5. Select the certificate status and provide a description if necessary.
6. Add your signature and the organization's signature.
7. Complete the date fields, including the start date and print date.
8. Enter the total hours and description.
9. Indicate whether the certificate has been printed or not.
10. Enter the certificate version and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization you volunteered with.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you started volunteering.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the total hours you have volunteered.
* **Volunteer Hours Description**: Provide a description of your volunteer activities.
* **Organization Logo**: Enter the logo of the organization you volunteered with (if available).
* **Certificate ID**: Enter a unique ID for your certificate (if required).
* **Certificate Status**: Select the status of your certificate (Active or Inactive).
* **Certificate Status Description**: Provide a description of your certificate status (if necessary).
* **Volunteer Signature**: Add your signature to confirm your information.
* **Organization Signature**: Add the organization's signature to confirm their information.
* **Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Total Hours**: Enter the total hours you have volunteered (if required).
* **Total Hours Description**: Provide a description of your total hours (if necessary).
* **Print Date**: Enter the date the certificate was printed (if necessary).
* **Certificate Printed**: Indicate whether the certificate has been printed or not.
* **Certificate Version**: Enter the version of your certificate.
* **Volunteer ID**: Enter a unique ID for your volunteer account (if required).
* **Organization ID**: Enter a unique ID for the organization's account (if required).
* **Signature**: Add any additional signature required for the form.
* **Organization Signature 2**: Add any additional organization signature required for the form.
* **Certificate Number**: Enter a unique number for your certificate (if required).
* **Certificate Number 2**: Enter an additional unique number for your certificate (if required).
