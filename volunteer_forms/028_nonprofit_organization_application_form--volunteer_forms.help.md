# Nonprofit Organization Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from nonprofit organizations to evaluate their application for partnership with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out the "Contact Information" section with your organization's name, address, and contact person's name and email.
3. Write a brief statement of your organization's mission in the "Mission Statement" field.
4. Provide a brief description of your nonprofit organization in the "Nonprofit Organization Details" field.
5. List the details of your employees, including their names, roles, and emails, in the "Employee Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Your organization's name, address, and contact person's name and email.
* **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`): A brief statement of your organization's mission.
* **Nonprofit Organization Details** (`nonprofit_organization_details`): A brief description of your nonprofit organization.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`): A list of details about your employees, including their names, roles, and emails.
