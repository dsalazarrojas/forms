# food_parcel_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for food parcels, a type of aid provided to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are completing it for a valid request.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" below).
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (1, number, **not required**): A unique identifier for the form submission.
* **Form Title** (2, text, **not required**): The title of your request, briefly describing the purpose of the food parcel.
* **Request Date** (3, date, **not required**): The date you are requesting the food parcel for.
* **Request Time** (4, time, **not required**): The time of day you are requesting the food parcel for.
* **Request Location** (5, text, **not required**): The location where you would like to receive the food parcel.
* **Food Type** (6, select multiple, **required**): Select all the types of food you are requesting (Food, Drink, Dessert, or Other).
* **Request Description** (7, text, **required**): Provide a detailed description of your request, including any specific needs or preferences.
* **Phone Number** (8, text, **not required**): Your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Email** (9, email, **required**): Your contact email address for further communication.
* **Note** (10, note, **not required**): Any additional comments or information about your request.
