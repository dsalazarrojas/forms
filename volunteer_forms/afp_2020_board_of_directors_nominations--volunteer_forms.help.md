# AFP 2020 Board Of Directors Nominations - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AFP 2020 Board Of Directors Nominations form is a tool used to nominate individuals for positions on the board of directors of a certain organization. The form collects information from both the nominee and the nominator, providing a comprehensive overview of the nominee's qualifications, experience, and availability for the position. This information will be reviewed by the nomination committee to determine the best candidates for the board positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to nominate someone. Check with your organization's leadership or review the eligibility criteria before starting.
2. Select the position you are nominating the individual for from the options provided.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the nominee.
4. Provide a brief statement of the nominee's qualifications and contributions.
5. If you have any conflicts of interest, disclose them accurately and in detail.
6. Select the time commitments the nominee can accommodate for board duties.
7. If applicable, list two professional references with their name, relationship, and contact information.
8. Sign your name as the nominator, indicating consent to the nomination and role expectations.
9. Fill out all other fields as necessary and provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter the nominee's full legal name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the nominee's preferred contact email (work or personal).
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, required): Enter the best number to reach the nominee.
* **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter the nominee's street address, city, state or province, and postal code.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the nominee's date of birth.
* **Current city** (`current_city`, text, optional): Enter the city and state or province of the nominee's current residence.
* **Current employer/organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nominee's current employer or organization, and their role.
* **Position nominated for** (`position_nominated`, select one, required): Select the board position the nominee is being considered for.
* **Years as an AFP member** (`years_of_membership`, integer, optional): Enter the number of years the nominee has been an AFP member.
* **Relevant leadership experience** (`leadership_experience`, select multiple, optional): Check all experiences that apply to the nominee, such as board service, committee chair, fundraising lead, program management, volunteer coordination, strategic planning, or other.
* **Statement of qualification** (`statement_of_qualification`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the nominee's qualifications and contributions (recommended 150-300 words).
* **Conflict of interest declaration** (`conflict_of_interest`, select one, required): Indicate whether any potential or actual conflicts of interest exist for the nominee.
* **Conflict details** (`conflict_details`, text, optional): If conflicts of interest exist, describe them in detail.
* **Availability for board duties** (`availability`, select multiple, required): Select all time commitments the nominee can accommodate for board duties.
* **Professional references** (`professional_references`, text, optional): List two professional references with their name, relationship, and contact information.
* **Nominee signature** (`nominee_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the nominator, indicating consent to the nomination and role expectations.
* **Nominator name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator email address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email for follow-up.
* **Relationship of nominator to nominee** (`nominator_relationship`, select one, required): Select how the nominator knows the nominee.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Fill out the current date.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information for the nomination committee.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are accurately and completely filled out.
* Be honest and transparent when declaring conflicts of interest.
* Provide a comprehensive and detailed statement of qualification.
* List professional references with accurate and complete information.
* Review the nomination carefully before submitting.
* Submit the nomination before the deadline.
