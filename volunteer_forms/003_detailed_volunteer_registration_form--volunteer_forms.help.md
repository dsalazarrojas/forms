# Detailed Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Detailed Volunteer Registration Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from potential volunteers who are interested in volunteering with our non-profit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select all the volunteer areas of interest from the "Volunteer Areas of Interest" field.
6.  Choose how often you can volunteer from the "How Often Do You Volunteer?" field.
7.  Specify how many volunteer shifts you are able to commit to per month in the "Volunteer Shifts" field.
8.  Enter the date you would like to start volunteering in the "What Date Would You Like to Start Volunteering?" field.
9.  If you are already volunteering elsewhere, enter the date you plan to start volunteering with us in the "If you are already volunteering elsewhere, what date do you plan to start volunteering with us?" field.
10.  Specify the amount of time you are able to commit per volunteer shift in the "How much time are you able to commit per volunteer shift?" field.
11.  Choose your non-profit affiliation from the "Non Profit Affiliation" field.
12.  Enter the name of your non-profit organization in the "Non Profit Organization Name" field.
13.  Enter your non-profit position in the "Non-profit Position" field.
14.  Choose how you would like to submit your volunteer application from the "Volunteer Application Submission" field.
15.  Finally, select your volunteer status from the "Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Required: False)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Required: False)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Required: False)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Required: False)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Areas of Interest (Required: False)**: Select all areas where you are interested in volunteering. Options: Volunteer and Event Planning, Event Promotion, Community Outreach, Volunteer Management, Data Entry, Social Media Management, Database Management, Other.
* **How Often Do You Volunteer? (Required: False)**: Choose how often you can volunteer from Once, Ongoing, 1-3 days, 1-2 weeks, More.
* **Volunteer Shifts (Required: False)**: Specify how many shifts you are able to commit to per month.
* **What Date Would You Like to Start Volunteering? (Required: False)**: Enter the date you would like to start volunteering with us.
* **If you are already volunteering elsewhere, what date do you plan to start volunteering with us? (Required: False)**: Enter the date you plan to start volunteering with us if you are already volunteering elsewhere.
* **How much time are you able to commit per volunteer shift? (Required: False)**: Specify the amount of time you are able to commit per volunteer shift.
* **Non Profit Affiliation (Required: False)**: Choose your non-profit affiliation from Non-profit Employee, Non-profit Volunteer, Not a Non-profit Employee or Volunteer.
* **Non Profit Organization Name (Required: False)**: Enter the name of your non-profit organization.
* **Non-profit Position (Required: False)**: Enter your non-profit position.
* **Volunteer Application Submission (Required: False)**: Choose how you would like to submit your volunteer application from Online Application Submission, Offline Application Submission, Both.
* **Status (Required: False)**: Select your volunteer status from Active, Inactive, Pending.

Note: The required field status is not applicable here as this form is designed for non-mandatory completion. However, it's worth noting that some fields are marked as "Required: False", meaning they are not mandatory to fill.
