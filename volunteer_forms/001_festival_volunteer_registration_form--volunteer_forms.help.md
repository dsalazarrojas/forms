# Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register volunteers for the festival. It collects information about your personal details, availability, and special talents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
5. Select your **Available Slots** for the festival in the fifth field.
6. Select your **Interest Areas** for the festival in the sixth field.
7. If you have any **Special Talents**, enter them in the seventh field.
8. Choose the **Event Date** for which you are available to volunteer in the eighth field.
9. Select the **Event Time** for which you are available to volunteer in the ninth field.
10. Enter the **Event Duration** for which you are available to volunteer in the tenth field.
11. Select your **Volunteer Role** for the festival in the eleventh field.
12. Enter your **Availability** for the festival in the twelfth field.
13. Enter your **Availability 2** for the festival in the thirteenth field.
14. Enter your **Availability 3** for the festival in the fourteenth field.
15. Enter your **Availability 4** for the festival in the fifteenth field.
16. If you have any **Special Skills**, enter them in the sixteenth field.
17. Enter your **Emergency Contact Name** in the seventeenth field.
18. Enter your **Emergency Contact Phone Number** in the eighteenth field.
19. Select your **Emergency Contact Relationship** in the nineteenth field.
20. Enter your **Emergency Contact Address** in the twentieth field.
21. Enter your **Emergency Contact Email** in the twenty-first field.
22. Enter a message in the twenty-second field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Available Slots 1** (5, select_multiple, optional): Select any available time slots for the festival.
* **Available Slots 2** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional available time slots for the festival.
* **Interest Areas 1** (7, select_one, optional): Select one or more interest areas for the festival.
* **Interest Areas 2** (8, select_one, optional): Select one or more interest areas for the festival.
* **Special Talents** (9, text, optional): Enter any special talents you may have.
* **Event Date** (10, date, required): Select the date of the festival.
* **Event Time** (11, time, required): Select the time of the festival.
* **Event Duration** (12, number, required): Enter the duration of the festival in hours.
* **Volunteer Role** (13, select_one, optional): Select your volunteer role for the festival.
* **Availability** (14, text, optional): Enter your availability for the festival.
* **Availability 2** (15, text, optional): Enter your additional availability for the festival.
* **Availability 3** (16, text, optional): Enter your further availability for the festival.
* **Availability 4** (17, text, optional): Enter your final availability for the festival.
* **Special Skills** (18, text, optional): Enter any special skills you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (19, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (20, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (21, select_one, optional): Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (22, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (23, email, required): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Message** (24, text, optional): Enter a message for your emergency contact.
