# Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect detailed information about a volunteer's funds request, ensuring accurate processing and management of these requests across different institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. **Volunteer Forms**: Select "Yes" if the request is from a volunteer who wants to request funds; select "No" otherwise.
2. **Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting funds.
3. **Institution Id**: Enter the unique identifier of the institution associated with this request.
4. **Amount Requested**: Enter the amount of funds the volunteer is requesting.
5. **Reason For Request**: Provide a clear and concise description of why the volunteer is requesting funds.
6. **Expected Use**: Explain how the requested funds will be used.
7. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person (if applicable).
8. **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution associated with this request.
9. **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable).
10. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
11. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about the request.
12. **Date Requested**: Enter the date when the funds were requested.
