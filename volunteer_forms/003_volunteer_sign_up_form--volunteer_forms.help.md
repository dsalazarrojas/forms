# volunteer_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer sign-up form is used to collect information from volunteers for various shifts and dates. This form helps the volunteer coordinator to match volunteers with available shifts and schedule volunteer tasks efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your volunteer type (e.g., Yes or No).
2. Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
3. Specify your availability for shifts by selecting a date for the shift you are available to work.
4. Choose whether you are available to volunteer on the specified shift date (Yes or No).
5. Select whether you are available to work a volunteer shift (Yes or No).
6. Enter the number of volunteer hours you are available to work.
7. Add any additional comments about your volunteer preferences or availability.
8. If you are available to volunteer at a specific location, select Yes.
9. Confirm that you have submitted your volunteer form by selecting Yes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Type**: Select your volunteer type (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Shift Time**: Enter your shift date in date format (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the date you are available to end volunteering.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Shift Date**: Select a date for the shift you are available to work.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Select whether you are available to work a volunteer shift (Yes or No).
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about your volunteer preferences or availability.
* **Volunteer Location**: Select whether you are available to volunteer at a specific location (Yes or No).
* **Volunteer Form Submitted**: Confirm that you have submitted your volunteer form (Yes or No).
