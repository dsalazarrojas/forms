# Firewise Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document fire safety activities. It helps track and manage information about fire safety events, including start and end dates and times, locations, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date and time of the activity in the "Start Date" and "Start Time" fields.
2. Enter the end date and time of the activity in the "End Date" and "End Time" fields.
3. Enter the total hours spent on the activity in the "Total Hours" field.
4. Enter any total expenses or costs associated with the activity in the "Total Expenses" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the activity in the "Description" field.
6. Select a category for the activity from the dropdown menu in the "Category" field.
7. Enter any locations relevant to the activity in the "Location" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about the activity in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
12. If applicable, enter the person assigned to the activity in the "Assigned" field.
13. If applicable, enter the person assigned to in the "Assigned To" field.
14. Enter the form ID number in the "Form ID" field (only if provided by the administrator).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date (1)**: Enter the start date of the activity.
* **End Date (2)**: Enter the end date of the activity.
* **Start Time (3)**: Enter the start time of the activity.
* **End Time (4)**: Enter the end time of the activity.
* **Total Hours (5)**: Enter the total number of hours spent on the activity.
* **Total Expenses (6)**: Enter any total expenses or costs associated with the activity.
* **Description (7)**: Provide a brief description of the activity.
* **Category (8)**: Select a category for the activity from the dropdown menu.
* **Location (9)**: Enter any relevant locations for the activity.
* **Notes (10)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the activity.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Name (13)**: Enter your name.
* **Assigned (14)**: If applicable, enter the person assigned to the activity.
* **Assigned To (15)**: If applicable, enter the person assigned to in the "Assigned To" field.
* **Form ID (16)**: Enter the form ID number (only if provided by the administrator).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are completing this form for a valid fire safety activity.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or administrator for assistance.
