# Confidential Volunteer Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have worked with or know the volunteer being referenced. The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the volunteer's skills, behavior, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have worked with or know the volunteer being referenced.
2. Answer each field accurately and truthfully, based on your experiences and observations.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the most appropriate option that reflects your assessment.
4. For text fields, please provide a brief but specific description.
5. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly mentioned as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number that we can use to reach you.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Relationship to Volunteer**: Choose how you know the volunteer (e.g. Former Supervisor, Current Supervisor, Colleague, etc.).
* **Volunteer Full Name**: Enter the full name of the volunteer being referenced.
* **Volunteer Role**: Enter the specific role or position the volunteer held while volunteering.
* **Volunteer Period**: Enter the dates they volunteered.
* **Reference Purpose**: Briefly describe the reason for this reference (e.g. to evaluate their suitability for a specific role).
* **Overall Assessment**: Choose an overall assessment of the volunteer's performance (e.g. Outstanding, Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **Reliability**: Choose how dependable you consider the volunteer to be (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Punctuality**: Choose how well the volunteer performed in terms of punctuality (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Attitude**: Choose how well the volunteer demonstrated a positive attitude (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Interpersonal Skills**: Choose how well the volunteer interacted with others (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Communication Skills**: Choose how well the volunteer demonstrated communication skills (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Adaptability**: Choose how well the volunteer adapted to situations (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Commitment Level**: Choose how committed the volunteer was to the project (e.g. Excellent, Very Good, Good, etc.).
* **Skills Observed**: Choose any specific skills you observed the volunteer demonstrating (e.g. Leadership, Teamwork, etc.).
* **Key Strengths**: Briefly describe any key strengths you observed.
* **Areas for Growth**: Briefly describe any areas where the volunteer may need improvement.
* **Notable Contributions**: Describe any notable contributions the volunteer made.
* **Handles Stress Well**: Choose how well the volunteer handled stress and pressure.
* **Works Well Independently**: Choose how well the volunteer worked independently.
* **Suitable for Role**: Choose how well-suited the volunteer is for the role.
* **Any Concerns**: Describe any concerns you may have about the volunteer's performance.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the volunteer.
* **Confidentiality Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand the reference is confidential.
* **Reference Confirmation**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date**: Date and sign this form to confirm your submission.
