# cross-border-volunteer-waiver-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cross-border-volunteer-waiver-form is designed to collect essential information from volunteers traveling across international borders. It helps the organization host manage and provide a safe experience for the volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2.  Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3.  Provide your current address in the field labeled "Address".
4.  Enter your birth date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY in the field labeled "Birth Date".
5.  If you have an emergency contact person, provide their name in the field labeled "Emergency Contact".
6.  Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the field labeled "Emergency Contact Phone".
7.  Enter the emergency contact's email address in the field labeled "Emergency Contact Email".
8.  Select whether you have an emergency contact person by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the field labeled "Emergency Authorization".
9.  Enter your role in the field labeled "Role" (select "Yes" or "No").
10. List the activities you will be participating in at the border in the field labeled "Border Activities" (select multiple options).
11.  Indicate if you have emergency authorization by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the field labeled "Emergency Authorization".
12.  If you have travel plans, enter the travel date in the format MM/DD/YYYY in the field labeled "Travel Date".
13.  Enter the travel time in the format HH:MM in the field labeled "Travel Time".
14.  Describe your travel duration in the field labeled "Travel Duration".
15.  List the travel destination(s) in the field labeled "Travel Destination".
16.  Specify the purposes of your travel in the field labeled "Travel Purposes".
17.  Enter the mode of transportation you will be using for travel (e.g., plane, bus, car) in the field labeled "Transportation".
18.  If applicable, specify the transportation provider (e.g., airline, bus company) in the field labeled "Transportation Provider".
19.  Indicate whether you will be using a specific transportation method (e.g., yes, no) in the field labeled "Transportation Method".
20.  Enter the date and time of your return in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM in the field labeled "Return Date" and "Return Time".
21.  Provide a brief description of the border activities you will be participating in in the field labeled "Description of Border Activities".
22.  If you have emergency authorization, explain the reason for it in the field labeled "Reason for Emergency Authorization".
23.  If you are authorized, state your relationship to the authorized person in the field labeled "Relationship to Authorized Person".
24.  Finally, enter the name of the signer of the emergency authorization in the field labeled "Signer Name".


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, text, required: false): Please enter your First Name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, text, required: false): Enter your Last Name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Address** (`3`, text, required: false): Your current address, which is where you can be reached during your stay.
* **Birth Date** (`4`, date, required: false): Enter your birth date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/28/1999).
* **Emergency Contact** (`5`, text, required: false): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`6`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`7`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Authorization** (`8`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have emergency authorization by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Role** (`9`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if this is your role or "No" if not.
* **Border Activities** (`10`, select_multiple, required: false): List the activities you will be participating in at the border.
* **Emergency Authorization** (`11`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have emergency authorization by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Travel Date** (`12`, date, required: false): Enter your travel date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 02/28/2023).
* **Travel Time** (`13`, time, required: false): Enter your travel time in the format of HH:MM (e.g., 14:00).
* **Travel Duration** (`14`, text, required: false): Describe your travel duration.
* **Travel Destination(s)** (`15`, text, required: false): List the destinations you plan to visit.
* **Travel Purposes** (`16`, text, required: false): Specify the purpose of your travel.
* **Transportation** (`17`, text, required: false): Enter the mode of transportation you will use (e.g., plane, bus, car).
* **Transportation Provider** (`18`, text, required: false): If applicable, specify the transportation provider (e.g., airline, bus company).
* **Transportation Method** (`19`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether you will use a specific transportation method (e.g., yes, no).
* **Return Date** (`20`, date, required: false): Enter your return date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 03/01/2024).
* **Return Time** (`21`, time, required: false): Enter your return time in the format of HH:MM (e.g., 15:00).
* **Description of Border Activities** (`22`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the border activities you will be participating in.
* **Reason for Emergency Authorization** (`23`, text, required: false): If you have emergency authorization, explain the reason for it.
* **Relationship to Authorized Person** (`24`, text, required: false): State your relationship to the authorized person.
* **Signer Name** (`25`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the signer of the emergency authorization.
