# Community Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to volunteer with the organization. The form will be used to match potential volunteers with opportunities that fit their skills, availability, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, as it will be used to determine which volunteer opportunities you are eligible for.
2. Be honest and provide all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant full name
* **Applicant full name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Applicant email
* **Applicant email** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly for communication about volunteer opportunities.

### Phone number
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your preferred contact number, which will be used for communication and emergencies.

### Street address
* **Street address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code to ensure accurate contact and delivery.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): List the days and times you are available to volunteer, including specific dates and time slots if possible.

### Relevant skills and experience
* **Relevant skills and experience** (`skills_and_experience`, text, optional): List any skills, training, or experience that may match the volunteer roles we offer.

### Emergency contact name and phone
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide a contact name and phone number in case we need to reach out to you during emergencies or for urgent matters.

### Areas of interest
* **Areas of interest** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the program areas you are interested in, such as Youth programs, Environmental, Events, Fundraising, or Administration.

### Consent for background check
* **Consent for background check** (`consenting_background_check`, select_one, required): If required for certain roles, please choose 'True' to consent for a background check, or 'False' otherwise.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure you are matched with suitable volunteer opportunities.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors or omissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
