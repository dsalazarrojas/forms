# Volunteer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer request form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers to facilitate the coordination and management of volunteer work. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth volunteer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting "Volunteer Request" as the form type.
2. Enter your name and other contact information in the required fields.
3. Select your volunteer type (Human or Non-Human) and choose your volunteer role (Administrative, Clerical, Technical, or Other).
4. Specify your volunteer hours and availability.
5. Select the dates you are available to volunteer.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Provide a valid zip code for your location.
8. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Read and agree to the volunteer agreement by checking the "Volunteer Agreement" field.
10. Sign and date your agreement in the "Volunteer Signature" and "Volunteer Signature Date" fields.
11. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Volunteer Request** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your volunteer request.
- **Volunteer Interest** (`2`, `text`, required): Share any specific interests or skills you have that would be beneficial for the volunteer role.
- **Service Opportunities** (`3`, `text`, required): Describe the type of services you are interested in providing (e.g., administrative, technical, etc.).
- **Volunteer Info** (`4`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about yourself, such as your background or experience.
- **Volunteer Sign Up** (`5`, `text`, required): Confirm your interest in volunteering.
- **Service Details** (`6`, `text`, required): Specify any specific details about the services you can provide.
- **Volunteer Commitment** (`7`, `text`, required): Describe your level of commitment to the volunteer role.
- **Volunteer Availability** (`8`, `text`, required): List the dates and times you are available to volunteer.
- **Additional Comments** (`9`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.
- **Volunteer Agreement** (`10`, `text`, required): Check the box to agree to the volunteer agreement.
- **Confirm Volunteer** (`11`, `text`, required): Confirm your interest in volunteering.
- **Volunteer Status** (`12`, `text`, required): Check the box to confirm your volunteer status.
- **Volunteer Type** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Human" or "Non-Human" as your volunteer type.
- **Volunteer Role** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the following volunteer roles:
  * Administrative
  * Clerical
  * Technical
  * Other
- **Volunteer Hours** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer per week.
- **Volunteer Dates** (`16`, `date`, required): Select the dates you are available to volunteer.
- **Volunteer Time** (`17`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available to volunteer (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
- **Volunteer Email** (`18`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Volunteer Phone** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Volunteer Address** (`20`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
- **Volunteer Zip** (`21`, `number`, required): Enter your zip code.
- **Volunteer Agreed** (`22`, `text`, required): Check the box to confirm you have read and agreed to the volunteer agreement.
- **Volunteer Signature** (`23`, `note`, required): Sign your name as the volunteer.
- **Volunteer Signature Date** (`24`, `date`, required): Date your signature.
- **Volunteer Notes** (`25`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about your volunteer experience.
