# Student Volunteer Hour Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Volunteer Hour Log Form is a tool used to record the time spent by students on volunteer activities. This form is intended to track student volunteer hours for academic purposes, such as fulfilling volunteer hour requirements for academic programs, or for reporting volunteer activities to external organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number.
2. Enter the name of the volunteer organization or event.
3. Enter the number of hours spent on the volunteer activity.
4. Select the date of the volunteer activity.
5. Select the time spent on the volunteer activity.
6. Choose the type of volunteer work done (Volunteer, Community Service, Other, or Academic).
7. Provide any additional comments about the volunteer activity.
8. Enter your name as the student submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to record the student's ID number. Enter the student's unique identifier.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to record the name of the volunteer organization or event.
* **Hours Donated** (`hours_donated`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to record the number of hours spent on the volunteer activity. Please enter a whole number.
* **Date Donated** (`date_donated`, `date`, required: false): This field is used to select the date of the volunteer activity.
* **Time Donated** (`time_donated`, `time`, required: false): This field is used to select the time spent on the volunteer activity. Enter the time in the format HH:MM (e.g., 01:30).
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to choose the type of volunteer work done (Volunteer, Community Service, Other, or Academic).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional comments about the volunteer activity.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to record your name as the student submitting the form.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise noted, but filling them out will help ensure accurate and complete reporting.
