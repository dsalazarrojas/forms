# Nonprofit Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a nonprofit organization's activities. It asks for the participant's name, waiver date, waiver acknowledgement, organization abbreviation, and contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter the date you signed this waiver in the "waiver_date" field.
3. Check the box to indicate your acknowledgement of the waiver terms. You can select either "Yes" or "No" from the "participant_signature" field.
4. If applicable, enter the abbreviation of the organization you represent in the "organization_abbreviation" field.
5. Finally, provide your contact information in the "participant_contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name (<participant_name>)**: Please enter your name in this field.
- **Waiver Date (<waiver_date>)**: Enter the date on which you signed this waiver.
- **Waiver Acknowledgement (<participant_signature>)**: Please confirm that you understand and agree with the waiver terms by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
- **Organization Abbreviation (<organization_abbreviation>)**: If you are representing an organization, please enter its abbreviation here. 
- **Participant Contact Information (<participant_contact_info>)**: Please enter your contact information if you have any questions or concerns. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organization for clarification.
- Make sure to sign and date your waiver in the appropriate fields to ensure its validity.
- This form should be completed on time, please submit it before participating in the activity. 

Note: The form's fields and purpose can be adjusted based on the actual requirements of the nonprofit organization, this help guide is based on the provided YAML schema.
