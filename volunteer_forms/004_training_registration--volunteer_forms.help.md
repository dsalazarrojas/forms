# Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Registration" form is designed to collect and manage training data, including their provider, type, start and end dates, location, capacity, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the provider name in the "Provider Name" field.
2.  Select the type of training in the "Training Type" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the start and end dates of the training in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields respectively.
4.  Enter the capacity of the training in the "Capacity" field.
5.  Select the status of the training in the "Training Status" field.
6.  Enter any additional training hours in the "Training Hours" field.
7.  Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
8.  Enter the name of the training provider in the "Training Provider" field.
9.  Enter the location of the training in the "Training Location" field.
10.  Select the time of the training in the "Training Time" field.
11.  Enter the date of the training in the "Training Date" field.
12.  Enter any additional training notes in the "Training Notes" field.
13.  Enter the email of the volunteer in the "Volunteer Email" field.
14.  Enter the phone number of the volunteer in the "Volunteer Phone" field.
15.  Enter the name of the volunteer in the "Volunteer Name" field.
16.  Enter the name of the training manager in the "Training Manager" field.
17.  Enter the name of the event manager in the "Event Manager" field.
18.  Enter the address of the training in the "Training Address" field.
19.  Enter the email of the manager in the "Manager Email" field.
20.  Enter the phone number of the manager in the "Manager Phone" field.
21.  Enter the name of the manager in the "Manager Name" field.
22.  Select the status update of the training in the "Training Status Update" field.
23.  Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the training provider.
*   **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of training (Corporate, Nonprofit, or Event Management).
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the training.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the training.
*   **Capacity** (`capacity`, number, required): Enter the capacity of the training.
*   **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the training (Active, In Progress, or Completed).
*   **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Enter any additional training hours.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
*   **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training provider.
*   **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training.
*   **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the training.
*   **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training.
*   **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional training notes.
*   **Volunteer Email** (`volunteer_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the volunteer.
*   **Training Manager** (`training_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training manager.
*   **Event Manager** (`event_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event manager.
*   **Training Address** (`training_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the training.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the manager.
*   **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the manager.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the manager.
*   **Training Status Update** (`training_status_update`, select_one, required): Select the status update of the training (Active, In Progress, or Completed).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
*   Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact the training team.
