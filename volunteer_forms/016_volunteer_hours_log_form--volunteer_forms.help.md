# Volunteer Hours Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Hours Log Form is used to record and track volunteer hours worked by volunteers for their respective organizations. This form is essential for maintaining accurate volunteer hour records, which can be used for reimbursement claims, grant reporting, and other administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form to ensure you understand each section and that it applies to your volunteer work.
2.  Enter the total number of hours worked on the **Volunteer Hours Log Form Hours** field.
3.  Indicate whether you volunteered with an organization or not on the **Volunteer Hours Log Form Organizations** field.
4.  Select the date of your volunteer work on the **Volunteer Hours Log Form Date** field.
5.  Enter the time you spent volunteering on the **Volunteer Hours Log Form Time** field.
