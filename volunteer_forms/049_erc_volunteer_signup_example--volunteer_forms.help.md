# Erc Volunteer Signup Example - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Erc Volunteer Signup Example form is a tool for individuals to sign up to volunteer their time and skills to help with various tasks. It is intended to be a straightforward and organized way to collect necessary information from volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Use the provided options to select your preferred volunteer role and time slot.
4. Enter your name, email address, and phone number correctly.
5. Provide any additional information or comments in the space provided.
6. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Welcome to the Erc Volunteer Signup Example
- **Welcome to the Erc Volunteer Signup Example** (note): This page is an introduction to the volunteer program and its purpose.

### Page 2: What would you like to help with?
- **What would you like to help with?** (select_one): Select one of the options provided:
	+ Volunteer Coordinator
	+ Volunteer Manager
	+ Other
This field is asking you to indicate what type of volunteer role you would like to take on. Please choose the option that best reflects your skills and interests.

### Page 3: What is your name?
- **What is your name?** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).

### Page 4: What is your email?
- **What is your email** (email): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.

### Page 5: What is your phone number?
- **What is your phone number** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for volunteer-related matters.

### Page 6: What is your preferred time slot?
- **What is your preferred time slot?** (time): Select a time slot that works best for you to volunteer.

### Page 7: Additional information
- **Additional information** (note): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience or skills.

### Page 8: Confirm
- **Confirm** (note): Review your answers and confirm your volunteer sign-up.
This is the final page to review and confirm your volunteer sign-up. Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
