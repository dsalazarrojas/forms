# Artist Info Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information about you as an artist or band to showcase your profile, performance capabilities, and availability for events. It helps event organizers and potential clients discover and contact you for performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your artist or band name.
2. Enter your primary contact email address.
3. Enter your phone number for easy communication.
4. Choose your primary genre and secondary genres (if applicable).
5. Write a brief 150-word description of your art or performance.
6. Choose your performance type (e.g., live music, DJ set, spoken word, etc.).
7. Indicate the typical duration of your performances.
8. List any necessary equipment or technical requirements.
9. Select your availability for performances (days, times, etc.).
10. Specify how much advance notice you need for performances.
11. Share your past performance experience and references.
12. Indicate your willingness to be interviewed.
13. List any media files or samples you can provide.
14. Confirm your agreement to the artist registration terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name under which you want to be credited as an artist or band.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, required): Provide your primary contact email address for communication and potential booking.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for efficient communication with event organizers.
* **Website or Social Media URL** (`artist_website`, `text`, optional): Share a link to your online presence (e.g., website, social media).
* **Primary Genre** (`genre`, `text`, required): Choose your primary genre of music or art (e.g., rock, pop, painting, etc.).
* **Secondary Genres** (`secondary_genres`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any additional genres you work with (e.g., jazz, hip-hop, etc.).
* **Artist Biography** (`artist_bio`, `text`, required): Write a 100-150 word description of your art or performance style.
* **Type of Performance Offered** (`performance_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the types of performances you offer (e.g., live music, DJ set, spoken word, comedy, etc.).
* **Typical Set Duration** (`set_duration`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the average duration of your performances (e.g., 15-30 minutes, 1-2 hours, etc.).
* **Equipment or Technical Needs** (`equipment_required`, `text`, optional): List any special equipment or technical requirements for your performances.
* **Availability for Performances** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the days and times you are available for performances.
* **Advance Notice Needed** (`advance_notice`, `select_one`, optional): Specify how much advance notice you need for performances (e.g., same day, 1 week, 1 month, etc.).
* **Previous Performance Experience** (`previous_performances`, `text`, optional): Share your past performance experience and notable events.
* **References or Testimonials** (`references`, `text`, optional): Provide contacts who can vouch for your work and experience.
* **Will to Be Interviewed** (`interview_willing`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to be interviewed.
* **Interview Topics You Can Discuss** (`interview_topics`, `text`, optional): List topics you are comfortable discussing during interviews.
* **Media Files or Samples** (`media_files`, `text`, optional): Offer any media files or samples of your work.
* **I Agree to This Artist Registration** (`agree_registration`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions of this artist registration form.
