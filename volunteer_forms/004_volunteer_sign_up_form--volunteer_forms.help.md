# Volunteer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic information from volunteers who want to sign up for an event. It's designed to be easy to use and understand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Sign Up" field with your name.
2. Provide the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
4. Type in the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
5. Fill in your name as the volunteer in the "Volunteer Name" field.
6. Enter your organization's name (if applicable) in the "Organization" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign Up** (text, required): Enter your name to sign up as a volunteer.
* **Event Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event you want to volunteer for.
* **Event Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event you want to volunteer for.
* **Event Location** (text, required): Enter the location of the event you want to volunteer for.
* **Volunteer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the volunteer.
* **Organization** (text, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the event.
