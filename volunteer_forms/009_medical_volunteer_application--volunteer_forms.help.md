# medical_volunteer_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in volunteering for medical-related roles. It collects contact information and availability to help assess your suitability for a volunteer role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6.  Enter the name of your city in the "City" field.
7.  Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
8.  Select your relevant volunteer role from the "Volunteer Role" field.
9.  Choose your preferred shift from the "Shift" field.
10.  If applicable, enter your medical license number in the "Medical License" field.
11.  If applicable, describe your emergency services experience in the "Emergency Services" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Medical Doctor
	+ Emergency Responder
	+ Other
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred shift:
	+ 8am-4pm
	+ 4pm-12pm
	+ Other
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, required/optional): If you have a medical license, enter the number here.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, text, required/optional): Describe your experience with emergency services, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Be honest when selecting your volunteer role.
- If you have a medical license, please ensure that it's current and valid.
- Describe your experience with emergency services, if applicable.
- You can choose one or more volunteer roles.
