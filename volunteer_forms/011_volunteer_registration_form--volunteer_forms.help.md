# Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers about their availability and role for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and contact information (fields 3 and 4).
2. Indicate if you can participate in the event (field 6).
3. Select your volunteer role (field 7).
4. Choose your available time slots for volunteering (fields 8, 9, and 10).
5. Input any additional notes or comments about your volunteering experience (field 11).
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (field 3): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (field 4): Enter your email address.
* **Organization** (field 2): Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.
* **Event** (field 6): Select 'Yes' if you can participate in the event.
* **Volunteer Role** (field 7): Select your volunteer role.
* **Volunteer Shift** (field 8): Choose your available time slots for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Start Time** (field 9): Enter your volunteer start time.
* **Volunteer End Time** (field 10): Enter your volunteer end time.
* **Volunteer Notes** (field 11): Input any additional information about your volunteering experience.
* **Submit** (field 12): Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your form.
