# Charity Event Volunteer Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights from volunteers who are offboarding after participating in a charity event. This form helps us understand your experience, gather your suggestions, and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Volunteer Full Name**, which is your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, which we will use to communicate with you after your offboarding.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**, which we will use to contact you if needed.
4. Select the **Event Name** where you volunteered, to help us keep track of our events.
5. Choose the date of the event when you participated.
6. Indicate your **Volunteer Role**, which describes the responsibilities you had during the event.
7. Enter the total number of **Hours Volunteered**, to help us measure our volunteer efforts.
8. Rate your **Satisfaction with Volunteer Experience** and **Clarity of Volunteer Role and Expectations** to help us understand how we can improve.
9. Rate **How Well Was the Event Organized** and **Support and Training Provided** to help us understand how to better support our volunteers.
10. Let us know if you feel **Connected to the Organization Mission**, and if you would **Volunteer Again** in the future.
11. Provide any **Additional Comments** or suggestions you may have for future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Full Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`<email>`, email, required): Enter your personal or work email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`<phone>`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
* **Event Name** (`<event_name>`, text, required): Choose the name of the event where you volunteered.
* **Event Date** (`<event_date>`, date, required): Enter the date of the event when you participated.
* **Volunteer Role** (`<volunteer_role>`, text, required): Describe your volunteer role and responsibilities during the event.
* **Total Hours Volunteered** (`<hours_volunteered>`, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you spent volunteering.
* **Satisfaction with Volunteer Experience** (`<experience_satisfaction>`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with your volunteer experience.
* **Clarity of Volunteer Role and Expectations** (`<role_clarity>`, select_one, required): Rate how clear your volunteer role and expectations were.
* **How Well Was the Event Organized** (`<event_organization>`, select_one, required): Rate how well the event was organized and managed.
* **Support and Training Provided** (`<volunteer_support>`, select_one, optional): Rate the support and training you received during your volunteer experience.
* **Connected to the Organization Mission** (`<mission_alignment>`, select_one, required): Rate how connected you feel to the organization's mission.
* **Would You Volunteer Again** (`<volunteer_again>`, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to volunteer again in the future.
* **Suggestions for Future Volunteer Experiences** (`<improvement_suggestions>`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or feedback for improving future volunteer experiences.
* **Offboarding Date** (`<offboarding_date>`, date, required): Enter the date of this form.
* **Additional Comments** (`<additional_comments>`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
