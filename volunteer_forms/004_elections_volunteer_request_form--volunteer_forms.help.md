## Elections Volunteer Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elections Volunteer Request Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering for elections. This form collects personal details, availability, and preferences for volunteer work to help us better understand your interest and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
2. Choose the correct options for your availability and preferences.
3. Make sure to save your progress if you need to leave and come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (<code>first_name</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your first name as you would like to be known.
* **Last Name** (<code>last_name</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your last name as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (<code>email</code>, <type>email</type>): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with further information.
* **Contact Number** (<code>contact_number</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you with further information.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (<code>volunteer_start_date</code>, <type>date</type>): Select the date you are interested in starting to volunteer.
* **How will they help?** (<code>how_help</code>, <type>text</type>): Briefly describe how you plan to contribute to the elections volunteer team.
* **Which days work best?** (<code>best_days</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>): Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
	+ Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
* **Volunteer Hours** (<code>volunteer_hours</code>, <type>number</type>): Enter the number of hours you are willing to volunteer per session.
* **Volunteer Duration** (<code>volunteer_duration</code>, <type>select_one</type>): Select the duration of your volunteer period.
	+ Options: Half-day, Full-day, Multi-day
* **Volunteer Hours Details** (<code>volunteer_hours_details</code>, <type>text</type>): Provide additional information about your volunteer hours, such as specific shifts or requirements.
* **Volunteer Location** (<code>volunteer_location</code>, <type>text</type>): Enter the location where you are willing to volunteer, such as the polling station.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any delays in processing your request.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the "Volunteer Hours Details" field.
* We will be in contact with you shortly after submitting your form to provide further information.
