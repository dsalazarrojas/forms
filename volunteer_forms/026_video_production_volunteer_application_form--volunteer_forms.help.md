# video_production_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are interested in volunteering for video production projects. It helps us to understand their availability, experience, skills, and preferences to match them with suitable projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Select your availability for the project.
3. Share your relevant volunteer experience, skills, and interests.
4. Choose your preferred role in the project (editor, director, or producer).
5. Specify the duration of your availability in hours.
6. Select your preferred tool or equipment (chatjimmy or other).
7. Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Application**: A brief description of your interest in volunteering for video production projects.
* **Project Details**: A short description of the project you are interested in or willing to work on.
* **Volunteer Experience**: List your relevant volunteer experience, including the project name, duration, and a brief description of your role.
* **Availability**: Choose your availability for the project (Option 1: Immediately, Option 2: Within 2 weeks, Option 3: Within 4 weeks).
* **Email**: Provide your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number so that we can reach you.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are available to start the project.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are available to start the project.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose your preferred tool or equipment (chatjimmy or other).
* **Project Location**: Provide the location of the project (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose your volunteer type (volunteer or staff).
* **Project Duration**: Enter the duration of your availability in hours.
* **Availability Start**: Enter the start date of your availability.
* **Availability End**: Enter the end date of your availability.
* **Assigned Role**: Choose your preferred role in the project (editor, director, or producer).
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are willing to commit to the project.
* **Volunteer Skills**: List your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Volunteer Interest**: List any specific interests or areas you would like to focus on.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.
