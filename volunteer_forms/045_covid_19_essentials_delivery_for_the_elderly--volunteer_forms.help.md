This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking to provide essential services to elderly individuals in need during the COVID-19 pandemic. The form helps identify the individual's requirements, such as delivering groceries, medications, or household supplies, and ensures that the delivery process is tailored to their specific needs. 

To prevent duplicate requests, the form will check for existing requests that match the provided information. 

Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

# COVID 19 Essentials Delivery For The Elderly - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for essential delivery services to elderly individuals in need during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with accurate information about the individual in need.
2. Be as specific as possible when selecting the type and amount of assistance needed.
3. Provide a preferred delivery date and time to ensure timely service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Elderly Person Name** (`elderly_person_name`): Enter the full name of the individual who needs the assistance.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter the age of the individual (optional but helpful for understanding their needs).
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the primary contact phone number for this request.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the individual or their representative (optional).
* **Street Address** (`street_address`): Enter the delivery address for the elderly person.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where the delivery will be made.
* **State/Province** (`state`): Enter the state or province where the delivery will be made.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the zip code for the delivery address.
* **Reason for Request** (`request_reason`): Choose one or more reasons why the individual needs assistance due to COVID-19.
* **Type of Assistance Needed** (`assistance_needed`): Select all types of assistance that apply to the individual's needs.
* **Specific Items or Groceries Needed** (`specific_items`): List any specific items or groceries required for the individual.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences** (`dietary_restrictions`): List any dietary restrictions or preferences for the individual.
* **List of Medications Needed** (`medication_list`): If medication pickup is needed, list the medications required.
* **Pharmacy Name** (`pharmacy_name`): If medications are being picked up, enter the name of the pharmacy.
* **Budget for Essentials** (`budget_available`): Enter the budget available for purchases (if applicable).
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (`preferred_delivery_time`): Choose a preferred delivery time to suit the individual's schedule.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date`): Enter a preferred delivery date for the essential items.
* **Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`): Provide instructions on how to access the building or home where delivery will be made.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this request.
* **Contact Person Phone** (`contact_person_phone`): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person (optional).
* **Any Health or Safety Concerns?** (`health_concerns`): List any health or safety concerns that might affect the delivery process.
* **COVID-19 Exposure Risk Level** (`covid_exposure_risk`): Choose the level of COVID-19 exposure risk for the individual.
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (`special_instructions`): Enter any additional information or special requests for this delivery.
