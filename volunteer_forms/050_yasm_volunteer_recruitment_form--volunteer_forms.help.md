# YASM Volunteer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The YASM Volunteer Recruitment Form is designed to collect essential information about volunteers to help match them with suitable opportunities and optimize our operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you have provided all required fields.
3. If a field is optional, you may choose to include your answer or leave it blank if not applicable.
4. Double-check that all your information is correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title or occupation, enter it here.
* **Skills**: Choose all relevant skills you possess that would be beneficial to YASM activities. You may select multiple options.
* **Availability**: Select your availability date for the volunteer opportunity. If you are unsure, please leave blank.
* **Preferred Shift**: Choose your preferred shift for the volunteer opportunity.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you wish to begin your volunteer work.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you wish to end your volunteer work.
* **Shift Duration**: Enter the shift duration you are available for.
* **Location**: If applicable, enter the location where you would like to volunteer.
* **Shift**: Enter the shift schedule you are available for.
* **Availability Time**: Enter the time you are available to volunteer.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a specific tool assigned to you, enter it here.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your availability or preferences.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions about the volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer ID**: Enter a unique identifier for you as a volunteer (if you have one).
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as a volunteer (if you have already registered as a volunteer).
* **Volunteer Name**: Note: This field is not actually required, as it is a duplicate field. Please disregard.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not necessary for the majority of volunteers and can be left blank.
* **Notes**: Use this space to add any additional comments or information about your availability or preferences.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions about the volunteer opportunity.
* **Location**: If you are unsure about your location, please leave blank.
* **Shift**: This field is not necessary for most volunteers and can be left blank.
* **Availability Time**: This field is not necessary for most volunteers and can be left blank.
* **Shift Duration**: This field is not necessary for most volunteers and can be left blank.
