# non_profit_reimbursement_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about non-profit reimbursement requests, allowing you to track and process requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as required or as needed, based on the field labels.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate information for all required fields.
3. Select the correct expense type from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the request.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or your organization.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or your organization.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date when the service was provided.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the service provided.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the service provided.
* **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense that this request is for (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Upload File**: Upload a file supporting your request (if required).
* **Upload File 2**: Upload any additional supporting files (if required).

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information to ensure efficient processing of your request.
* Use the correct expense type to ensure that your request is properly categorized.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
