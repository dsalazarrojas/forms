# Volunteer Orientation Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about volunteer attendance at an orientation session. It helps us understand who attended, their details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Click submit once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Date <small>(<type: date, optional)</small>
• Session Date: Enter the date of the session you attended.

### Session Time <small>(<type: time, optional)</small>
• Session Time: Enter the time of the session you attended.

### Volunteer Name <small>(<type: text, optional)</small>
• Volunteer Name: Enter your name as a volunteer.

### Participant Type <small>(<type: select_multiple, optional)</small>
• Participant Type: Select all the participant types that apply to you.

### Contact Method <small>(<type: select_one, optional)</small>
• Contact Method: Select the best method to contact you.

### Comments <small>(<type: note, optional)</small>
• Comments: Enter any comments or feedback about the session.

### Attended Session <small>(<type: select_one, optional)</small>
• Attended Session: Select the session you attended.

### Session Location <small>(<type: text, optional)</small>
• Session Location: Enter the location of the session.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information to help us better understand volunteer engagement and improve future sessions.
- Fill in all required fields to ensure we receive your details correctly.
- If you have any comments or feedback, please use the comments field to provide them.
