# Volunteer Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers about their availability and skills to help match them with volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name and contact information (Name, Email, and Phone Number) to help us get in touch with you.
2. Choose a preferred time for volunteering, considering your availability.
3. Select a volunteer role that suits your interests and skills.
4. List any relevant skills you have that can be useful for volunteering.
5. Specify the number of volunteer hours you are available to give each week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `time`, required): Choose a time that best represents your availability for volunteering.
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, `time`, optional): Select a specific time that you would prefer to volunteer, if different from your availability.
* **Volunteer Role** (`Volunteer Role`, `text`, optional): Choose a role that you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `text`, optional): List any relevant skills you have that can be useful for volunteering.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`Volunteer Hours`, `number`, required): Specify the number of volunteer hours you are available to give each week.

## Tips
* Be honest about your availability and skills to ensure you are matched with suitable volunteer opportunities.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
* We appreciate your commitment to volunteering and look forward to working with you!
