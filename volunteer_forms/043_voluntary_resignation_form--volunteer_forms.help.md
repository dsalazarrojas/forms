# Voluntary Resignation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's voluntary resignation. It captures essential information to ensure a smooth transition of responsibilities and official recording of the employee's departure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your employee information.
2. Provide your email address for communication and updates.
3. Enter the date of your last day of work.
4. Specify the reason for your resignation.
5. Confirm the date of your resignation.
6. Sign the form to acknowledge and agree to the terms.
7. Sign the form again to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address for communication and updates.
* **Date Of Resignation** (`date_of_resignation`, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to leave the company.
* **Reason For Resigning** (`reason_for_resigning`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your willingness to provide more details.
* **Last Day Of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, required): Enter the date of your last day of work.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your resignation.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department you will be leaving.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): The manager should sign to acknowledge and agree to the terms of your resignation.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): You should sign to confirm your understanding and agreement with the terms of your resignation.
* **Date Of Resignation Confirmation** (`date_of_resignation_confirmation`, date, required): Confirm the date of your resignation with the manager.
