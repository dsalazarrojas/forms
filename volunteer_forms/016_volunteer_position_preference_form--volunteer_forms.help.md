# Volunteer Position Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Position Preference Form is a self-reporting tool used to determine your volunteer position preference. This form will help us understand your availability and skills to better match you with volunteer opportunities that suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Volunteer Preference Form" page.
2. Answer the questions according to your volunteer position preference.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Volunteer Preference Form** (`volunteer_position_preference_form`, text, not required)
The first section is the main title of the form. It's a text field where you can provide your preference for a volunteer position. You can type anything in here, but it's not required to submit a position.

### 2. **Skills** (`volunteer_skills`, select multiple, not required)
Check all the skills that you have or are proficient in. This will help us understand your strengths and find matching volunteer opportunities. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No

### 3. **Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, date, not required)
Indicate when you are available to volunteer. Please select a date or dates from the calendar.

### 4. **Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, text, not required)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This will help us get in touch with you about volunteer opportunities that match your preferences.

### Tips
Please make sure to fill in all fields accurately and provide as much detail as possible. This will help us create a more precise match for you. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
