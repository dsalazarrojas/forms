# New Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for registration purposes. Please fill out the form accurately to help us understand your availability and preferences for volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details such as name, email, phone, and address.
2. Select your country of origin.
3. Choose your state and shift preferences.
4. Indicate your availability and preferred shifts.
5. Select your languages, skills, and interests.
6. Provide your education and experience details.
7. Confirm your volunteer agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Volunteer Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Volunteer Address**: Enter your current address for reference purposes.
* **Volunteer Country**: Select your country of origin from the provided options.
* **Volunteer State**: Select all the states you are available to volunteer in. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Date**: Select your preferred volunteer date.
* **Volunteer Time**: Indicate your preferred volunteer time. (Note: This field is not required for registration)
* **Volunteer Note**: This is an optional field for any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Volunteer Additional Info**: Enter any additional details about your volunteer experience or background.
* **Volunteer Skills**: Select all the skills you possess. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Interests**: Select all the interests you have. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Languages**: Select all the languages you are proficient in. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Indicate if you have volunteering experience. (Note: This field is not required for registration)
* **Volunteer Education**: Indicate your level of education. (Note: This field is not required for registration)
* **Volunteer Availability**: Select your availability for volunteering. This is a required field.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Select all the shifts you are available to volunteer during. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Flexible Time**: Select all the flexible time slots you are available for volunteering. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select all the volunteer types you are interested in. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Indicate if you are available to volunteer for a certain number of hours. (Note: This field is not required for registration)
* **Shift Hours**: Indicate if you are available to volunteer during specific shift hours. (Note: This field is not required for registration)
* **Volunteer Termination**: Indicate if you have any termination agreements or restrictions. (Note: This field is not required for registration)
* **Volunteer Reference**: Enter a reference or any relevant details about your volunteering experience or background.
* **Volunteer Agreement**: Agree to the volunteer terms and conditions.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to ensure a successful registration process. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
