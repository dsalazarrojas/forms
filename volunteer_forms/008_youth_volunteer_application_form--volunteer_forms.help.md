# Youth Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from youth applicants who want to volunteer for an event or organization. This form helps to gather essential details about the applicant, their availability, and preferred volunteer shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in the required information in each field accurately and honestly.
* If a field is not required, feel free to skip it if it's not applicable to you.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

### 1. User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your basic details such as your name and any other relevant information about yourself.

### 2. Volunteer Info
* **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the volunteer role.

### 3. Volunteer Shifts
* **Volunteer Shifts** (`volunteer_shifts`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more volunteer shifts that you are available to work. The options are 8:00 AM - 9:00 AM, 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM, 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM. Please choose the shifts that fit your schedule.

### 4. Available Dates
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more dates when you are available to volunteer. The options are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. Please choose the dates that fit your availability.
