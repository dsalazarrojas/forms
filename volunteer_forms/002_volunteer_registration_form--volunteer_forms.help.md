# volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer_registration_form is a form used to register volunteers for events. It gathers essential information from volunteers, including name, contact, event preferences, and availability, to efficiently allocate them to events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Select your preferred Event Type from the Event Type dropdown list.
5. Select all the Volunteer Groups you would like to join.
6. Select the Volunteer Project you would like to join.
7. Enter the Start Date of your availability in the Start Date field.
8. Enter the End Date of your availability in the End Date field.
9. Enter the Time of your availability in the Time field.
10. Enter the Location of your preferred work in the Location field.
11. Select your preferred Shift.
12. Enter any additional Notes (if any).
12. Enter any additional Notes (if any) in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Event Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of event you're interested in participating in.
* **Volunteer Group** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the volunteer groups you'd like to join.
* **Volunteer Project** (Select One, Required): Choose the project you'd like to work on.
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you'd like to start volunteering.
* **End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you'd like to end volunteering.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time you're available to volunteer.
* **Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where you'd like to volunteer.
* **Shift** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred shift.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): Enter any assigned tools or equipment.
* **Event ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the event ID (if you have one).
