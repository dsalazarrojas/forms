# Voluntary Statement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to gather clear and concise statements or feedback from volunteers or beneficiaries. The form's main objective is to elicit relevant information and sentiments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the introduction page and take a moment to consider your statement or feedback.
2.  Provide your name in the designated field.
3.  Offer your statement or details in the "Statement Details" field. Please keep it concise and clear.
4.  Select your preferred method of contact, if applicable, from the dropdown options in "Contact Preferences".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Please type your full name or the name you wish to be associated with your statement.
*   **Statement Details** (`statement_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your statement or details as briefly and clearly as possible. Be as specific as you can about your thoughts or feedback.
*   **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or none) if we need further clarification or follow-up information.
