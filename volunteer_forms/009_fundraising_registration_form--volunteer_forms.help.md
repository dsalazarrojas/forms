# Fundraising Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for volunteers to register their information and goals for participating in fundraising activities. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that you can participate in our fundraising events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the **Volunteer Information** field.
2. Specify your **fundraising goal** (if applicable).
3. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
5. Select your **donation_type** (multiple options are allowed).
6. Enter the **amount** of your donation.
7. Choose the **date_of_donation** and **time_of_donation** for your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details to help us understand your volunteer needs.
* **fundraising_goal**: Describe your fundraising goal, such as the specific project or activity you're supporting.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any questions or updates.
* **donation_type**: Choose all the types of donations you're willing to make (e.g., monetary, material, expertise).
* **amount**: Enter the exact amount of your donation.
* **date_of_donation**: Choose the date you plan to make your donation.
* **time_of_donation**: Choose the time of your donation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- Review and update your information as needed to reflect any changes in your volunteer status or fundraising goals.
