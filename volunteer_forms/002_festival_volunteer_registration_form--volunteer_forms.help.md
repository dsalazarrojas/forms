## Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Festival Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers interested in participating in the festival. This form helps us to understand your availability, areas of interest, and special talents, and ensure that we have the right volunteers for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that we will use this email to communicate with you about your volunteer opportunities.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Indicate your available slots by selecting the options that apply to you.
6. Select the areas of interest that you would like to volunteer for.
7. If you have any special talents or skills that you think would be valuable to the festival, please enter them in the "Special Talents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.).
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Available Slots**: Select the available slots where you can volunteer. These are the times and dates of the festival that you are available to work.
* **Areas of Interest**: Select the areas of the festival that you would like to volunteer for. This will help us match you with the right role.
* **Special Talents**: If you have any special skills or talents that would be valuable to the festival, such as photography, social media management, etc., please enter them here.
