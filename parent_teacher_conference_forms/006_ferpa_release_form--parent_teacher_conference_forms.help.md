# Ferpa Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for the Ferpa Release Form. It allows parents or guardians to provide their personal contact information and consent for their child's participation in various activities, likely for school purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and address in the **Parent Name** and **Parent Address** fields, respectively.
2. Enter the name of your child's school in the **School Name** field.
3. Once you've filled out the form, review it carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
4. Submit the form once you're satisfied with your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Address** (text, required): Enter your home address as a parent or guardian.
* **School Name** (text, required): Enter your child's school name.

## Tips
- Double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your child or school staff for guidance.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review this guide if you need further clarification on any field or the form's purpose.
