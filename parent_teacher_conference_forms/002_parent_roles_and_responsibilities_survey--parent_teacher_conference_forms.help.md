# Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents about their roles and responsibilities in their child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required fields (indicated by *) to provide accurate and complete information.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help educators and administrators understand your involvement and support.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it, but please be aware that providing additional information can be beneficial for a more comprehensive understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the parent/guardian of the child.
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, text, required): Please provide your contact information, such as your address, phone number, or email address.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so that we can communicate with you more effectively.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Please provide your phone number so that we can contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **Parent Note** (`parent_note`, note, required): Please provide any additional notes or comments that you think are relevant to your child's education.
* **Parent Skills and Knowledge** (`parent_skills_and_knowledge`, text, optional): Please list any special skills or knowledge that you possess that can support your child's education.
* **Parent Knowledge** (`parent_knowledge`, text, optional): Please list any areas of knowledge that you are familiar with or have expertise in that can benefit your child's education.
* **Parent Skills** (`parent_skills`, text, optional): Please list any skills that you possess that can support your child's education.
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, optional): Please list any concerns that you may have about your child's education or well-being.
* **Parent Ideas** (`parent_ideas`, text, optional): Please share any ideas that you have for improving your child's education or school community.
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_involvement`, text, optional): Please describe your level of involvement in your child's education, including volunteering, attending school events, or participating in decisions.
* **Parent Supports** (`parent_supports`, text, optional): Please list any ways that you can support your child's education, such as helping with homework or attending parent-teacher conferences.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, text, optional): Please share any additional comments or feedback that you have about your child's education or school experience.

Please remember to review your answers carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
