# teacher_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "teacher_request_form" is used to collect information and schedule meetings between teachers and parents of students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student details, including the student's grade level.
2. Select the subject area you would like to discuss with the parent.
3. Choose a date and time for the parent-teacher conference.
4. Add a message to the teacher or parent with any necessary details.
5. Select the student's name from the available options.
6. Provide your name and any relevant school information.
7. Enter your email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name, grade level, or any other relevant details.
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_one, required: false): Choose the subject area you would like to discuss with the parent (e.g., math, English, science, history, art, music, or other).
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, required: false): Select a date and time for the parent-teacher conference.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Add any additional details or requests to the teacher or parent.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the student's name from the available options.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, required: false): Provide any information about the parent or guardian.
* **Teacher Details** (`teacher_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and title (e.g., teacher, assistant teacher).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication.

## Tips
* Please be concise and clear in your message to ensure the parent understands the purpose of the meeting.
* Use the student's name to ensure accurate identification.
* Double-check the date and time for the meeting to avoid conflicts.
