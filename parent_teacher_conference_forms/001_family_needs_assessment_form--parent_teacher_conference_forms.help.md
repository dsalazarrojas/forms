# Family Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Needs Assessment Form is a tool for parents or guardians to share their needs and concerns with school administrators and teachers. It helps the school support and address the needs of families, ensuring they receive the help and services they require.

## How To Complete This Form

*   **Read the form carefully**: Before starting, make sure you have enough time to answer all the questions.
*   **Gather necessary information**: Collect all relevant details you want to share with the school, such as any challenges you're facing or services your child requires.
*   **Answer each question honestly**: Provide accurate and clear responses to each question.
*   **Save and submit**: Complete all fields and save your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent Information** (`parent_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any relevant details about your family.
    *   Provide your name as a parent or guardian.
    *   You can add any additional details about your family that might be helpful for the school to know.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
*   **Parent-Teacher Relationship Status** (`parent_relationship_status`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your child, such as "biological father/mother" or "step-parent".
*   **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, text, optional): Share your contact information with the school.
*   **Parent Request** (`parent_request`, text, optional): Specify any specific requests or concerns you have for the school.
*   **Parent Additional Comments** (`parent_additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you have.
*   **Child Information** (`children_information`, text, optional): Enter your child's name and any relevant details.
*   **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name.
*   **Child Relationship Status** (`child_relationship_status`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your child, such as "biological parent" or "adoptive parent."
*   **Child Contact** (`child_contact`, text, optional): Share your child's contact information with the school.
*   **Child Request** (`child_request`, text, optional): Specify any specific requests or concerns you have for your child.
*   **Child Additional Comments** (`child_additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you have for your child.
*   **Parent Counseling Service** (`parent_counseling_service`, text, optional): If you would like to receive counseling services, select "Yes".
*   **Requests From Service Provider** (`requests_service_provider`, select multiple, optional): Select if you would like to request any additional services from a service provider.
*   **Other Counseling Service** (`other_counseling_service`, text, optional): If you have any other counseling services in mind, specify them here.
*   **Other Counseling Service Details** (`other_counseling_service_details`, text, optional): Share any additional details about the other counseling services you need.

## Tips

*   If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to contact the school.
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and clearly.
*   The school will review your submission and address your concerns and requests as needed. 

Please note that this form will be used to better understand your family's situation and provide necessary support.
