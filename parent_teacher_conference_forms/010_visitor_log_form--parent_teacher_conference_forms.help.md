# Visitor Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Log Form is a tool for recording and tracking information about visitors who enter or interact with a facility or organization. The form is designed to collect essential details such as contact information, purpose of visit, and time spent on site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Visitor Log Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Select your Role, which could be a student, staff, or any other relevant designation.
4. Indicate your Department or area of affiliation.
5. Enter the Building Name or location where you are visiting.
6. Record the Date and Time of your visit.
7. Enter your Phone number for emergency contact purposes.
8. Write any additional Notes about your visit.
9. Indicate the number of Visitors in your group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents or ID.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents or ID.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): Select your designated role while visiting the facility, e.g., Student, Staff, or Guest.
- **Department/Office** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department or office you are visiting.
- **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the building where you are visiting.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Record the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Record the time of your visit in 12-hour format, e.g., 01:00 PM.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about your visit that you would like to be recorded.
- **Visitor Count** (`visitor_count`, number, required: false): Indicate the number of visitors in your group.
