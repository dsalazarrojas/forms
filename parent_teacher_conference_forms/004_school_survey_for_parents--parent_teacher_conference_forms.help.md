# School Survey For Parents - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents about their child's interests, education level, and other relevant details. The goal is to help teachers and administrators better understand the needs and abilities of students, which can inform instruction and support provided to them. This form is intended for parents who want to provide accurate and helpful information about their child's development and learning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Begin by reading the form's purpose and instructions carefully.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked with a `*`.
3. Choose the correct options from the select boxes (e.g., education level, attendance, parent participation).
4. Enter your email address and parent name in the respective text fields.
5. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as a parent/guardian.
* **Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Education Level** (`parent_education_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of education (High School, Some College, College, Some Postgraduate).
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required): Enter your child's current grade level.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, required): Choose your child's typical school attendance (Regular, Irregular, Unavailable).
* **Parent Participation** (`parent_participation`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you participate in your child's education (High, Medium, Low).
* **Parent Occupation** (`parent_occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation (if you choose to share this information).
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select any interests your child enjoys (Music, Art, Sports, Reading).
* **Student Hobbies** (`student_hobbies`, select_multiple, optional): Select any hobbies your child enjoys (Reading, Playing Sports, Playing Music, Hiking).
* **Student Homeschooled** (`student_homeschooled`, select_one, optional): Select if your child is homeschooled (False, True).
* **Student Languages** (`student_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select any languages your child speaks (English, Mandarin, Spanish, French).
* **Other Language** (`student_languages_other`, text, optional): If the above language options do not apply, enter a different language here.
* **Other Interests** (`student_interests_other`, text, optional): If the above interest options do not apply, enter a different interest here.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, feel free to ask your school or teacher for support.
* Remember that all information provided will be used to support your child's education and well-being.
