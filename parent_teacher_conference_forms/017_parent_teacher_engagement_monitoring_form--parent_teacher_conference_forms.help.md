# Parent Teacher Engagement Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and evaluate parent-teacher engagement in schools. It gathers data on key aspects of this engagement, including parent involvement, teacher support, communication methods, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and scope of each section.
2. Complete each section according to your knowledge and experiences.
3. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
4. If you are unsure about a particular question, please refer to your supervisor or relevant school official.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Involvement Fields**: This section asks about the level of parent involvement in your child's education.
	+ **Parent Involvement Fields** (`parent_involvement_fields`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the level of parent involvement in your child's education. Please provide a brief description of your experiences or observations regarding parent participation in school activities, parental support, or other relevant aspects.
* **Teacher Support Fields**: This section asks about the level of teacher support you receive.
	+ **Teacher Support Fields** (`teacher_support_fields`, number, required): This field is used to rate the level of support you receive from your child's teacher. Please rate your experiences according to the scale provided, where 1 indicates a low level of support and 5 indicates a high level of support.
* **Parent Feedback Fields**: This section asks about your feedback on parent-teacher engagement.
	+ **Parent Feedback Fields** (`parent_feedback_fields`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about your opinions on the following:
		- Yes: Do you feel engaged with your child's teacher?
		- No: Do you feel disengaged with your child's teacher?
* **School Climate Fields**: This section asks about the overall school climate.
	+ **School Climate Fields** (`school_climate_fields`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the overall school climate or atmosphere. Please provide a brief description of your experiences or observations about the school environment.
* **Communication Fields**: This section asks about your preferred communication methods.
	+ **Communication Fields** (`communication_fields`, select_one, required): This field asks about your preferred method of communication with your child's teacher. Please select one of the following options:
		- Phone
		- Email
		- Text
* **Teacher Response Fields**: This section asks for any additional comments or feedback from the teacher.
	+ **Teacher Response Fields** (`teacher_response_fields`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **School Improvement Fields**: This section asks about potential areas for school improvement.
	+ **School Improvement Fields** (`school_improvement_fields`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about potential areas for school improvement. Please select all that apply:
		- Yes: Are there any areas that need improvement?
		- No: Is the school performing well?
* **Next Steps Fields**: This section asks about any next steps or actions.
	+ **Next Steps Fields** (`next_steps_fields`, text, optional): This field is used to describe any next steps or actions that the teacher or school may take based on your feedback.
* **Follow Up Fields**: This section asks about follow-up activities.
	+ **Follow Up Fields** (`follow_up_fields`, select_one, required): This field asks about follow-up activities. Please select one of the following options:
		- Yes: Will you be following up with you about this?
		- No: Will not be following up with you about this.
