# Before And After School Care Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Before And After School Care Information Form is used to gather essential information from parents/guardians about their child's care needs before and after school. This form helps care providers understand the child's requirements, preferences, and any special needs or allergies they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's first name in the "Student First Name" field.
2. Enter the student's last name in the "Student Last Name" field.
3. Select the student's date of birth from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the name of the school the student attends.
5. Select the grade level of the student.
6. Check the boxes to indicate if the student needs before-school or after-school care.
7. Enter the start date of the care program.
8. Select the usual pick-up time (if applicable).
9. Enter the names and relationships of authorized persons allowed to pick up the student.
10. If applicable, enter any special needs or allergies the student has.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student First Name (Student First Name)**: Enter the first name of the student.
* **Student Last Name (Student Last Name)**: Enter the last name of the student.
* **Date of Birth (Student Date of Birth)**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **School Name (School Name)**: Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **Grade Level (Grade Level)**: Select the grade level of the student from the options provided.
* **Needs Before School Care (Needs Before School Care)**: Check if the student needs before-school care.
* **Needs After School Care (Needs After School Care)**: Check if the student needs after-school care.
* **Care Program Start Date (Care Program Start Date)**: Enter the start date of the care program.
* **Usual Pick-up Time (Usual Pick-up Time)**: If applicable, select the usual pick-up time for the student.
* **Authorized Persons for Pick-up (Authorized Persons for Pick-up)**: Enter the names and relationships of authorized persons allowed to pick up the student.
* **Special Needs or Allergies (Special Needs or Allergies)**: If applicable, enter any special needs or allergies the student has.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter the primary contact phone number of the parent/guardian.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter the email address of the parent/guardian.
* **Home Address (Home Address)**: Enter the home address of the parent/guardian.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed accurately to ensure the care provider has the necessary information to provide quality care to the child.
