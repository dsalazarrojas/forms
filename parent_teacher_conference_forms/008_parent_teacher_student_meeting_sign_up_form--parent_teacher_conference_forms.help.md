# Parent Teacher Student Meeting Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Teacher Student Meeting Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from parents and teachers about meeting schedules with students.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Parent Info**: Enter the parent's information in this text field.
2. **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name.
3. **Parent Email**: Enter the parent's email address.
4. **Teacher Info**: Enter the teacher's information in this text field.
5. **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
6. **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
7. **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
8. **Meeting Date**: Select the date of the meeting.
9. **Meeting Time**: Select the time of the meeting.
10. **Meeting Duration**: Choose the duration of the meeting from 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
11. **Meeting Room**: Select the meeting room (A, B, or C).
12. **Meeting Date Range**: Select the date range of the meeting.
13. **Meeting Start Time**: Select the start time of the meeting.
14. **Meeting End Time**: Select the end time of the meeting.
15. **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the meeting.
16. **Meeting Note**: Enter any additional notes about the meeting.
17. **Meeting Status**: Select the status of the meeting (In Progress, Completed, or Rescheduled).
18. **Parent Message**: Enter any message from the parent.
