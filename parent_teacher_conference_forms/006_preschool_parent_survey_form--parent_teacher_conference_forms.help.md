# Preschool Parent Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Parent Survey Form is a tool designed to gather feedback and information from parents about their experiences at our preschool. It aims to help us understand how we can better support our students and families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your child's **school name** on the first page.
2. Provide your **name**, **email**, and **phone number**.
3. Answer the **rating** question by selecting one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
4. If you have any **comments** or **questions** about our preschool, please provide them in the text boxes provided.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions that you'd like to share via **uploads** or **audio**, you can do so in the text boxes provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school-name**: The name of the preschool your child attends.
* **name**: Your name, so we can address you personally.
* **email**: Your email address, in case we need to contact you for more information.
* **phone**: Your phone number, in case we need to reach out to you by phone.
* **rating**: How do you rate your experience with our preschool? Choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* **comments**: Any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience.
* **questions**: Do you have any questions or concerns that you'd like to bring up?
* **uploads**: If you have any additional comments or questions that you'd like to share via upload, please do so here.
* **comments-audio**: If you have comments or questions that you'd prefer to share via audio, please record and upload them.
* **questions-audio**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via audio, please record and upload them.
* **comments-email**: If you have comments or questions that you'd like to share via email, please provide them here.
* **questions-email**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via email, please provide them here.
* **comments-phone**: If you have comments or questions that you'd like to share via phone, please type them in here.
* **questions-phone**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via phone, please type them in here.
* **rating-uploads**: If you have any additional comments or questions about our rating, please do so here.
* **rating-audio**: If you have comments or questions about our rating, please record and upload them.
* **rating-email**: If you have questions or concerns about our rating, please provide them here.
* **questions-uploads-uploads**: If you have any additional questions or concerns, please do so here.
* **questions-audio-uploads**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via audio, please record and upload them.
* **questions-audio-audio**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via audio, please record and upload them.
* **questions-email-uploads**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via email, please provide them here.
* **questions-phone-uploads**: If you have questions or concerns that you'd like to express via phone, please type them in here.

Note: The fields with **_uploads** in their names are optional and can be used to share additional comments, questions, or concerns via upload, email, or audio.
