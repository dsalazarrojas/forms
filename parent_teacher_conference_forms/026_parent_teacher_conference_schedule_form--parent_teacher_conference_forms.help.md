# parent-teacher-conference-schedule-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule and collect information for parent-teacher conferences. It helps teachers to efficiently schedule conferences with parents, ensuring that both parties are well-prepared and on the same page. This form gathers necessary details such as the conference date, start and end times, parent and teacher names, and conference duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready, including the parent's and teacher's availability and any relevant notes.
2. Select the date of the conference from the provided options or enter a custom date.
3. Enter the start and end times of the conference.
4. Enter the names of the parent and teacher involved in the conference.
5. Specify the duration of the conference.
6. Add any additional notes or comments, if needed.
7. Ensure that the email address is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What date** (conference_date, date, required: false): This is the date of the conference. Select a date from the provided options or enter a custom date.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, required: false): Enter the time the conference will begin.
* **End Time** (end_time, time, required: false): Enter the time the conference will end.
* **Parent Name** (parent_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian involved in the conference.
* **Teacher Name** (teacher_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher involved in the conference.
* **Conference Duration** (conference_duration, number, required: false): Specify the duration of the conference, in minutes or hours.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for the conference, if needed.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian, to be used for communication regarding the conference.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information you enter for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant parties.
- This form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your needs, so feel free to adjust the information as necessary.
- If you need to reschedule or cancel a conference, use the form to communicate this change effectively.
