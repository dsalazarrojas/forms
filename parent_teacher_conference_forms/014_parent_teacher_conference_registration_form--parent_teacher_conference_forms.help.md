# Parent Teacher Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from parents and teachers for parent-teacher conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name, email, home phone number, and parent relationship to the student.
* Select your school year and confirm your attendance.
* Choose a conference date and time that suits you.
* Enter your conference location.
* Provide your student's name and grade level.
* Enter your student's birth date.
* Write a brief message to the teacher.
* Sign the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name as a parent or guardian.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name as a parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address for communication.
* **Home Phone** (`home_phone`, `text`, required): Your home phone number.
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, `text`, required): Your relationship to the student (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `select_one`, required): Choose your school year from the given options.
* **Conference Date** (`conference_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for the conference.
* **Conference Time** (`conference_time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for the conference.
* **Conference Location** (`conference_location`, `text`, required): The location of the conference.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): The student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, required): Choose the student's grade level.
* **Student Birth Date** (`student_birth_date`, `date`, required): The student's birth date.
* **Parent Message** (`parent_message`, `text`, required): A brief message to the teacher.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this form to confirm your participation.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this form to confirm the student's attendance.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): The teacher's name.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required): The teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required): The teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Title** (`teacher_title`, `text`, required): The teacher's title or position.
* **Teacher School** (`teacher_school`, `text`, required): The school where the teacher works.
* **Teacher Subject** (`teacher_subject`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the teacher's subject or subjects.
* **Teacher Note** (`teacher_note`, `note`, required): A brief note for the teacher.
* **Parent Attendance** (`parent_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your attendance.
* **Parent Note** (`parent_note`, `note`, required): A brief note for the parent.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): The student's ID number.
