# Parent-Teacher Conference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents and teachers about their experiences during parent-teacher conferences. By completing this form, you will help your school gather valuable insights to improve their parent-teacher conference process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. You can skip any fields that are not required.
4. Once you have finished filling out the form, click the submit button to send it to your school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Experience** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can describe your experience during the parent-teacher conference. Please provide any details you feel are relevant to your experience.
* **Communication** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide feedback about the communication during the parent-teacher conference. Please be honest about your experiences, but be respectful.
* **Parent Reception** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field has three options:
		- Parent Reception 1
		- Parent Reception 2
		- Parent Reception 3
	+ Please select the option that best describes your reception during the parent-teacher conference.
* **Teacher Reception** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field has three options:
		- Teacher Reception 1
		- Teacher Reception 2
		- Teacher Reception 3
	+ Please select the option that best describes your teacher's reception during the parent-teacher conference.
* **Future Conferences** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see implemented for future parent-teacher conferences.
* **Conference Communication** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide feedback about the communication during the parent-teacher conference. Please be as specific as possible about what worked well and what didn't.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the parent-teacher conference.
