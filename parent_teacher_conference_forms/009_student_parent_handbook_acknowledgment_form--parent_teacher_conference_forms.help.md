# Student Parent Handbook Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain acknowledgment from parents or guardians about the content of the Student Parent Handbook. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter your name (parent/guardian) in the **Parent Name** field.
3. Enter your contact information in the **Parent Contact Info** field.
4. Select the **Parent Signature** field with the option that accurately represents your understanding of the handbook.
5. If applicable, select the **Student Signature** field with the option that accurately represents the student's understanding of the handbook.
6. Enter the current date in the **Date** field.
7. Enter any additional information about the teacher in the **Teacher Info** field.
8. Enter the teacher's name in the **Teacher Name** field.
9. If you have attached the Student/Parent Handbook, make sure to fill in the **Handbook Attach** field with the relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: The student's name as recorded in the school's records.
* **Parent Name**: Your name as a parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Contact Info**: Your contact information, including any relevant phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Parent Signature**: Select one of the two options: I have read and understand the Student Parent Handbook or I have not read and do not understand the Student Parent Handbook.
* **Student Signature**: If applicable, select one of the two options: I have read and understand the Student Parent Handbook or I have not read and do not understand the Student Parent Handbook.
* **Date**: The current date.
* **Teacher Info**: Any additional information about the teacher that is relevant to the student's educational process.
* **Teacher Name**: The teacher's name as recorded in the school's records.
* **Handbook Attach**: If you have attached the Student/Parent Handbook, enter the relevant information such as the title of the document and its date attached.
