# Parenting Strategies Implementation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parenting Strategies Implementation Guide is a form designed to gather information from parents and caregivers about their involvement and participation in the Parent-Teacher conference. The form aims to document and analyze the effectiveness of various parenting strategies and approaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: 
    - First name
    - Last name
    - Email address
    - Phone number
    - Date of birth
    - Address
    - City
    - State
    - Zip code
2. Categorize your involvement and approach:
    - Select the category that best describes your involvement (Parent-Teacher, School, or Community)
    - Choose the approach you will be taking or recommend for the conference (Positive Behavioral Support or Crisis Intervention)
3. Note your goals and observations:
    - Write down your goals for your child (optional)
    - Add any notes from the parent or caregiver
4. Confirm your conference details:
    - Enter the conference date
    - Enter the conference time
5. Indicate your attendance:
    - Select whether you will be attending the conference

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure communication and follow-up.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for communication and scheduling.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for location and logistics.
* **City**: Enter the city for location and logistics.
* **State**: Enter the state for location and logistics.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for location and logistics.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your involvement:
	+ Parent-Teacher: Direct communication between parents and teachers.
	+ School: Involvement in school activities or events.
	+ Community: Involvement in community events or programs.
* **Category (other)**: Select other categories of involvement if none of the above options match your case.
* **Approach**: Choose the approach you will be taking or recommend during the conference:
	+ Positive Behavioral Support: Focusing on positive behavior and support strategies.
	+ Crisis Intervention: Focusing on addressing crisis situations.
* **Other Approach**: Select other approaches if none of the above options match your case.
* **Goal**: Enter your goals for your child (optional).
* **Notes (Parent)**: Add any notes from the parent or caregiver.
* **Notes (Caregiver)**: Add any notes from the caregiver.
* **Conference Date**: Enter the conference date.
* **Conference Time**: Enter the conference time.
* **Parent Attendance**: Select whether you will be attending the conference:
	+ True: Confirm your attendance.
	+ False: Decline your attendance.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to select the correct category and approach to accurately reflect your involvement and strategies.
* Ensure the conference details are correct to avoid scheduling conflicts.
