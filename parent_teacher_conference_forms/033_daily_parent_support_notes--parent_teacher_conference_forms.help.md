# Daily Parent Support Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication between parents and teachers during parent-teacher conferences. It helps to document the discussion, ensure that both parties are on the same page, and provide a record of any action plans or concerns that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer the questions in the spaces provided, using the fields below to guide your responses.
3. Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Details** (`conference_details`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the conference, including the date and any relevant context.
* **Parent Notes** (`parent_notes`, text, required: false): Write down any notes or concerns that the parent would like to share with the teacher.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, text, required: false): Add any notes or comments from the teacher's perspective.
* **Action Plans** (`action_plans`, text, required: false): Outline any action plans or next steps that were discussed during the conference.
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, required: false): Record any concerns or questions the parent would like to raise with the teacher.
* **Conference Date** (`conference_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the parent-teacher conference.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required: false): A space for the parent to sign and date their agreement with the action plans.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, note, required: false): A space for the teacher to sign and date their agreement with the action plans.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Remember to proofread and edit your responses before submitting the form.
