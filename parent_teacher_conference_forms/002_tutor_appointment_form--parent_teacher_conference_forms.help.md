# Tutor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Appointment Form is designed to help schedule and manage tutor sessions. It gathers necessary information from both the tutor and the student to ensure a smooth and productive session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, provide the schedule for the tutor sessions.
2. On the second page, enter the tutor's details.
3. On the third page, enter the student's information.
4. On the fourth page, select the tutor(s) for the session.
5. On the fifth page, confirm the appointment details.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Tutors Sessions** (`schedule_tutors_sessions`, text, optional): This field is where you provide the schedule for the tutor sessions. Enter the dates, times, and duration of the sessions.
* **Tutor Details** (`tutor_details`, text, optional): This field is used to enter information about the tutor, such as their name, title, and qualifications.
* **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): This field is used to enter information about the student, such as their name, grade level, and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Select Tutors** (`select_tutors`, select_one, required): Select the tutor(s) for the session from the list of available options.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, note, required): This field is used to confirm the appointment details, such as the date, time, and tutor selected.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form and save the session information.
* **Save Draft** (`save_draft`, text, optional): Click this button to save a draft of the form and continue later.
* **Publish** (`publish`, text, optional): Click this button to publish the form and make it live.
* **Cancel Draft** (`cancel_draft`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel the draft and start over.

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional" or "required", which means you can skip them if not necessary or fill them out accordingly.
