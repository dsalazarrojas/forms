# Prenatal Education Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for prenatal education, helping us understand their expected date, contact details, and preferences for a smooth and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the expected mothers' name in the "expected mothers" field.
2. Enter the expected date of delivery in the "expected date" field.
3. Choose a preferred date for the prenatal class in the "prenatal class date" field.
4. Add your name and email address in the "contact name" and "email" fields, respectively.
5. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Confirm the date and time for your next appointment in the "confirm date" and "confirm time" fields.
7. Add any additional comments or remarks in the "notes" field.
8. Select your preferred tool from the "assigned tool" dropdown menu.
9. Briefly describe your category and interests in the "category" field.
10. Provide a description of your needs and preferences in the "description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expected Mothers**: Enter the names of all the expected mothers, separated by commas.
* **Expected Date**: Enter the expected date of delivery in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Prenatal Class Date**: Choose a preferred date for the prenatal class.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Confirm Date**: Enter the date for your next appointment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Confirm Time**: Enter the time for your next appointment in 24-hour format.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or remarks about your preferences or needs.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select your preferred tool from the available options: chatjimmy, tool2, or tool3.
* **Category**: Briefly describe your category and interests.
* **Description**: Provide a description of your needs and preferences.
