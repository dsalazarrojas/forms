# Kindergarten Parent Teacher Meeting Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and record parent-teacher meetings for kindergarten students. It helps teachers gather information from parents and provide updates on a child's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Parent Information section with your child's details.
2. Select the meeting time that suits you best from the Meeting Preferences section.
3. Choose the meeting date from the calendar.
4. Enter the preferred time for the meeting in the Preferred Time section.
5. Enter your contact number in case the teacher needs to reach you.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Parent Notes section.
7. Enter any meeting notes or updates from the Teacher Notes section (if applicable).
8. Finally, enter your name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information (1)**: Enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Meeting Preferences (2)**: Select a suitable meeting time for you (Morning, Afternoon, or Other).
* **Meeting Date (3)**: Choose a date for the meeting from the calendar. Be sure to pick a date that works for you and the teacher.
* **Meeting Time (4)**: Enter the specific time you'd like to meet (8:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, or 11:00 AM).
* **Preferred Time (5)**: Select your preferred meeting time from the list (8:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, or 11:00 AM).
* **Contact Number (6)**: Enter your contact number so the teacher can reach you.
* **Parent Notes (7)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your child.
* **Meeting Notes (8)**: Enter any meeting notes or updates from the teacher.
* **Teacher Notes (9)**: Enter any relevant information from the teacher.
* **Submitter (10)**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.

## Tips
- Be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
- Choose a meeting date and time that works for both you and the teacher.
- Use the Parent Notes section to share any important details about your child.
- The Teacher Notes section is for the teacher to provide updates about your child's progress.
