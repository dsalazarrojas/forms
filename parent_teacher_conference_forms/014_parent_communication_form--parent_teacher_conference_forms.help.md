# Parent Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help facilitate effective communication between parents and teachers during parent-teacher conferences. It is intended to gather information about the child's progress, concerns, and expectations for future conferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Provide the title of the conference topic in the "Conference Topic" field.
3.  Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4.  Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
5.  Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
6.  Provide any additional comments or notes about the conference in the "Comments" and "Teacher Conference Notes" fields.
7.  Select one of the provided options in the "Parent Conference Notes" field to indicate your feedback and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Conference Topic
*   **Conference Topic** (text, required): Specify the topic that will be discussed during the conference.
*   **Conference Topic** (text, required): This field is intended to provide a clear focus for the conference.

*   **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter the parent's name here.

*   **Parent Email** (email, required): Enter the parent's email address here.

*   **Parent Phone** (text, required): Enter the parent's phone number here.

*   **Teacher Name** (text, optional): This field is optional and intended to provide additional details from the teacher who will conduct the conference.

*   **Date of Conference** (date, optional): Choose a date for the conference.

*   **Time of Conference** (time, optional): Choose the time for the conference.

*   **Comments** (note, required): This field is intended to provide a space for any comments or notes about the conference.

*   **Teacher Conference Notes** (select_multiple, optional): This field is optional but allows the teacher to take notes during the conference.

*   **Parent Conference Notes** (select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate your feedback and expectations.
