# Customer Tip Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Tip Feedback Survey" form is designed to gather information about your experience with a particular service or business. It includes questions about your tip frequency, satisfaction, and feedback about the service staff. Your input will help us improve our service and ensure that you have an excellent experience with us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. For fields with hints, use the information provided for guidance.
4. If you have any additional comments, use the "Comments" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you usually tip when dining** (`customer_tip_feedback`): This is the first question in the form. It asks about your general habit of tipping when dining out. Please select the option that best describes your behavior.
* **When dining** (`dining_frequency`): This question is about how often you dine out. Choose the option that best suits your dining frequency.
* **Tip amount** (`tip_amount`): On a $100 bill, how much do you usually leave as a tip? Enter a numerical value.
* **Tip type** (`tip_type`): This field is about the type of tip you usually leave. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **How often do you tip** (`tip_frequency`): This is similar to the first question, but more specific to your tipping frequency.
* **How satisfied do you feel** (`satisfaction_score`): Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 0-100.
* **Service level** (`service_level`): This question is about the quality of service you received. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Service attitude** (`service_attitude`): This field is about the attitude of the service staff. Choose the option that best describes their behavior towards you.
* **Staff attitude** (`staff_attitude`): This question is similar to the previous one, but more specific to the staff's attitude towards you.
* **Tip Frequency 2** (`tip_frequency_2`): This is the second question asking about your tipping frequency.
* **Satisfaction Score 2** (`satisfaction_score_2`): This is the second question asking about your satisfaction.
* **Tip Amount 2** (`tip_amount_2`): This question is similar to the first one, but more specific to your tipping amount on a $100 bill.
* **Tip Type 2** (`tip_type_2`): This field is asking about the type of tip you usually leave. You can enter your answer in the text field.
* **Comments** (`comments`): If you have any additional comments or feedback, use this field to share them.
* **Email address** (`email_address`): This is your email address, which we may use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): This is your phone number, which we may use to contact you if needed.
* **Name** (`name`): This is your name.
* **Email** (`email`): This is your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This is your phone number.
* **Staff name** (`staff_name`): This is the name of the staff member who served you.
* **Staff email** (`staff_email`): This is the email address of the staff member who served you.
* **Staff phone** (`staff_phone`): This is the phone number of the staff member who served you.
* **Staff title** (`staff_title`): This is the title of the staff member who served you.
* **Date** (`date`): This is the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`time`): This is the time of your visit.

Note: Some fields have hints to guide you, but if you have any doubts, please refer to the explanation provided below each field. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this survey.
