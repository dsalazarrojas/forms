# Online Store Checkout Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Store Checkout Feedback Form" is a tool for customers to provide their experience and feedback on the checkout process. This form helps us understand what you liked and disliked about your shopping experience, enabling us to make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Select how satisfied you were with the overall shopping experience.
2. Describe your experience during the checkout process.
3. Rate your experience out of five stars.
4. Specify which part of the process you had issues with.
5. Share any additional comments or suggestions.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. (Optional) Add any comments about your experience.
9. Provide your customer number.
10. Provide your name, which will be used to identify you as a customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you were with the overall shopping experience. Options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Poor
* **What was your experience like at checkout?**: Describe your experience during the checkout process.
* **How would you rate your experience?**: Rate your experience out of five stars.
* **Which part of the checkout process did you have the most issues with?**: Choose one of the options to specify which part of the process you had issues with:
	+ Navigation
	+ Payment
	+ Shipping
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Any additional feedback?**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for us.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: (Optional) Add any comments about your experience.
* **Customer number**: Provide your customer number.
* **Customer name**: Provide your name, which will be used to identify you as a customer.
* **Form title**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Form category**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Assigned tool**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submitted by user**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submitted by user ID**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submitted by user email**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submitted by user phone**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submission status**: Choose how your submission is status:
	+ Pending
	+ Completed
	+ In Progress
* **Submission comments**: Add any comments about your submission.
* **Submission date**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submission time**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
* **Submitted by**: This field is automatically filled in and should not be edited.
