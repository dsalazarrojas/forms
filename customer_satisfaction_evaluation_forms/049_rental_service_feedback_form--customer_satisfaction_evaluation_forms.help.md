# Rental Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their recent rental experience. Your feedback will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, which are marked as **Required**.
2. Provide your feedback in the **Feedback** field.
3. Select your overall **Overall Rating** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your **Email** if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
5. If you have a **Phone** number you would like to provide, you can enter it here (optional).
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience in the **Additional Note** field (optional).
7. Enter your **Name** so we can address you personally.
8. Select whether you **Category** of feedback is **Yes** or **No** (optional).
9. In the **Comment** field, you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience (optional).
10. For **Rating 5**, **Rating 4**, **Rating 3**, **Rating 2**, and **Rating 1**, select the qualities you experienced during your rental experience (optional).
11. For **Category 5**, **Category 4**, select one of the options that best describes your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (Field 1): This is the main field where you can provide your thoughts and feedback about your rental experience. This field is required.
* **Overall Rating** (Field 2): Select your overall rating for your rental experience from the dropdown menu. This field is required.
* **Email** (Field 3): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address here (optional).
* **Phone** (Field 4): If you would like to provide your phone number, you can enter it here (optional).
* **Additional Note** (Field 5): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience (optional).
* **Name** (Field 6): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Category** (Field 7): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether you have had a similar experience with us before (optional).
* **Comment** (Field 8): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience (optional).
* **Rating 5** (Field 10), **Rating 4** (Field 12), **Rating 3** (Field 13), **Rating 2** (Field 14), and **Rating 1** (Field 15): Select the qualities you experienced during your rental experience.
* **Category 5** (Field 15), **Category 4** (Field 16): Select one of the options that best describes your experience.

Note: The fields with a star (*) are optional, but if you have any feedback about those aspects of your experience, please feel free to fill them out.
