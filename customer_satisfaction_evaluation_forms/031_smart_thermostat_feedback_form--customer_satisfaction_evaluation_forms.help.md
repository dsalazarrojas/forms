# Smart Thermostat Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the Smart Thermostat system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Smart Thermostat Systems" field and type in the name of your smart thermostat system.
2. Choose your overall satisfaction level by selecting one of the options.
3. Select the temperature control level by choosing one of the options.
4. Provide any smart suggestions you have for the system in the text field.
5. Rate the system's reliability by selecting one of the options.
6. Choose one of the customer service options, if applicable.
7. Provide any additional comments in the text field.
8. Rate the smart thermostat system by providing a number between 1 and 10.
9. Suggest any improvements to the system in the text field.
10. Rate the features of the smart thermostat system by selecting multiple options.
11. Rate the features of the smart thermostat system by providing a number between 1 and 10.
12. Choose one of the options for the smart thermostat system area.
13. Rate the smart thermostat system area by providing a number between 1 and 10.
14. Suggest any areas of improvement for the smart thermostat system in the text field.
15. Rate the suggest area of the smart thermostat system by providing a number between 1 and 10.
16. Choose one of the options for the smart thermostat suggest area.
17. Suggest any improvements to the smart thermostat system area in the text field.
18. Rate the suggest area of the smart thermostat system by providing a number between 1 and 10.
19. Suggest any improvements to the suggest area of the smart thermostat system in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart Thermostat Systems** (`1`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide the name of your smart thermostat system.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`2`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the system.
* **Temperature Control** (`3`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to rate the temperature control level of your system.
* **Smart Suggestions** (`4`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide any suggestions you have for the system.
* **System Reliability** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to rate the system's reliability.
* **Customer Service** (`6`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to rate the customer service you received.
* **Additional Comments** (`7`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments about your experience with the system.
* **Smart Thermostat Rating** (`8`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the system overall, on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Smart Thermostat Improvement** (`9`, `text`, required): This field is used to suggest any improvements to the system.
* **Smart Thermostat Features** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to rate the features of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat Features Rating** (`11`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the features of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat System** (`12`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to rate the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat System Rating** (`13`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat Price** (`14`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the price of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat Suggestions** (`15`, `text`, required): This field is used to suggest any improvements to the system.
* **Smart Thermostat Recommends** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to rate the smart thermostat system's recommendable features.
* **Smart Thermostat Improvement Area** (`17`, `text`, required): This field is used to suggest any areas of improvement for the system.
* **Smart Thermostat System Area** (`18`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to rate the area of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat Improvement Area Rating** (`19`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the improvement area of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat System Area** (`20`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to rate the area of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat System Area Rating** (`21`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the area of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat Suggest Area** (`22`, `text`, required): This field is used to suggest any areas of improvement for the system.
* **Smart Thermostat Suggest Area Rating** (`23`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the suggest area of the smart thermostat system.
* **Smart Thermostat System Suggest Area** (`24`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to suggest any improvements to the suggest area of the system.
* **Smart Thermostat Suggest Area Rating** (`25`, `number`, required): This field is used to rate the suggest area of the smart thermostat system.

Note: This help guide is provided as a general explanation of the form and its fields. Please refer to the actual form for specific field options and instructions.
