# customer_support_satisfaction_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your feedback on your experience with our customer support service. This will help us understand how we can improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field below and provide your feedback accordingly.
2. Select your rating and feedback for each question.
3. Provide your name and contact information so we can reach out to you for further communication.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the submit button.

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): Please read this introduction to understand what this form is for and how it will help us improve our service.
* **Rate** (`Rate`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer support service. Select one of the following options: Good, Fair, or Poor.

* **Satisfaction with Resolution** (`How would you rate your satisfaction with the resolution of the issue`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with how we resolved your issue on a scale of 1-5.
