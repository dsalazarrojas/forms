# Customer Onboarding Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Onboarding Feedback Request Form" is a tool used to gather feedback from customers about their experience during the onboarding process. It helps us to identify areas of improvement and refine our onboarding process to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Customer Onboarding Feedback" field with your thoughts and experiences about our onboarding process.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date of your onboarding in the "Onboarding Date" field.
6. Choose the time of your onboarding in the "Onboarding Time" field.
7. Rate your overall experience in the "Rating" field.
8. Provide any additional comments about your onboarding experience in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Onboarding Feedback (<span style="color: blue;">Customer Onboarding Feedback</span>)
*   **Customer Onboarding Feedback** (`customer_onboarding_feedback`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide feedback about your experience during the onboarding process. Please be specific about what happened, what worked well, and what didn't.
### Name (<span style="color: blue;">Name</span>)
*   **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
### Email (<span style="color: blue;">Email</span>)
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
### Phone (<span style="color: blue;">Phone</span>)
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you if needed.
### Onboarding Date (<span style="color: blue;">Onboarding Date</span>)
*   **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your onboarding.
### Onboarding Time (<span style="color: blue;">Onboarding Time</span>)
*   **Onboarding Time** (`onboarding_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your onboarding.
### Rating (<span style="color: blue;">Rating</span>)
*   **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### Comments (<span style="color: blue;">Comments</span>)
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your onboarding experience.
