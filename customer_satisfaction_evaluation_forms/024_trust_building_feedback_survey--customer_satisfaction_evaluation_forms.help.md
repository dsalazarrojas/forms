# Trust Building Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trust Building Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our service delivery process. This form is used to identify areas of improvement and build trust with customers, ensuring a positive relationship and a higher level of customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your company name (if applicable).
3. Describe your relationship with our service (e.g., customer, client, partner, etc.).
4. Rate your satisfaction with a number from 1-5 (for rating, quality, and time management fields).
5. Answer the open-ended questions (comments and feedback) and rate your trust level with us (Trust Building field).
6. Choose the frequency and channel of follow-up actions (Follow up, Follow up Frequency, and Follow up Channel fields).
7. Select the type of follow-up actions you received or would like to receive (Follow up Type field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, required): If you have a company affiliation, please enter it.
*   **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with our service (e.g., customer, client, partner, etc.).
*   **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with our service on a scale from 1-5.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
*   **Service Delivery** (`service_delivery`, text, required): Describe your experience with our service delivery process.
*   **Communication** (`communication`, text, required): Rate your satisfaction with our communication (1-5).
*   **Quality** (`quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of our service (1-5).
*   **Time Management** (`time_management`, number, optional): Rate the time management of our service (1-5).
*   **Trust Building** (`trust_building`, select_one, true): Select your level of trust in our service (Very High, High, Neutral, Low).
*   **Overall Feeling** (`overall_feeling`, select_multiple, false): Rate your overall feeling towards our service (Very Good, Good, Bad, Very Bad).
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, false): Provide any feedback or suggestions about our service.
*   **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select_one, false): Rate the frequency of follow-up actions (High, Medium, Low, None).
*   **Follow up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_multiple, false): Select the frequency of follow-up actions (Often, Rarely, Never).
*   **Follow up Channel** (`follow_up_channel`, select_multiple, false): Choose the channel for follow-up actions (Phone, Email, Mail, Social Media).
*   **Follow up Type** (`follow_up_type`, select_multiple, false): Select the type of follow-up actions you received or would like to receive (Call, Message, Letter).

Note: Please fill out this form carefully and honestly, as it will help us improve our service and build a stronger relationship with you.
