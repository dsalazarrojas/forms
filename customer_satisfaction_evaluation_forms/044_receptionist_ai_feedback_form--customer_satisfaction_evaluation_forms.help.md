# Receptionist AI Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receptionist AI Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience with our receptionist services. This form helps us understand how well we met your expectations and what we can improve to better serve you in the future. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us provide you with even better service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the requested information for each field. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have anything to share. The form is divided into sections with a specific focus, so feel free to answer the questions that apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Overall Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required: false)

*This field is a rating of your overall satisfaction with our receptionist service. Please select a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.*

### **Receptionist Service** (`receptionist_service`, text, required: false)

*Please describe the quality of the receptionist service you received. If you had a positive experience, be as specific as possible about what made it great. If you had a negative experience, let us know what we could do differently next time.*

### **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required: false)

*Choose one of the options below to rate how well our receptionist communicated with you.*
* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### **Friendliness of Service** (`friendliness_service`, select_multiple, required: false)

*Choose all that apply to indicate how friendly you found our receptionist to be. You can select multiple answers if that's what fits your experience.*
* Very Friendly
* Friendly
* Neutral
* Unfriendly

### **Receptionist Response Time** (`receptionist_response_time`, select_one, required: false)

*Choose the speed of our receptionist's response to your queries.*
* Fast
* Medium
* Slow

### **Professionalism** (`receptionist_professionalism`, select_one, required: false)

*Rate the professionalism of our receptionist's behavior.*
* Very Professional
* Somewhat Professional
* Not Professional

### **Receptionist Knowledge** (`receptionist_knowledge`, select_one, required: false)

*Rate the receptionist's knowledge of their role and services.*
* Extremely Knowledgeable
* Somewhat Knowledgeable
* Not Knowledgeable

### **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, select_multiple, required: false)

*Choose all that apply to indicate how well our receptionist communicated with you.*
* Very Good
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### **Improvement Requests** (`improvement_requests`, text, required: false)

*If there's anything we can do better, please let us know what you would suggest!*

### **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false)

*Any other comments or suggestions you'd like to share about your experience with our receptionist?*
