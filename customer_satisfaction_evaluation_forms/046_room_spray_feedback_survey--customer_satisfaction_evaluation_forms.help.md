# room_spray_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about their experience with our room spray products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the "User Input" fields, as they pertain to your experience with our room sprays.
2. Choose your preferred scents from the "Scent Preferences" field.
3. Select your assessment of the "Product Performance" field, based on your experience with the product.
4. Enter your satisfaction level with the product, using the numerical scale.
5. If you have any additional comments about your experience, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address and phone number, if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
7. Fill in the "Date of Survey" and "Time of Survey" fields, to help us track the timing of your feedback.
8. Add any additional notes about your experience in the "Note" field.
9. Assign your feedback to a specific person, if you have a specific contact for your feedback.
10. Select a "Form ID" to associate your feedback with a specific record.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for the remaining "User Input" fields, as they pertain to your experience with our room sprays.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have used our room sprays before. This field helps us understand your baseline experience with our product.
* **Scent Preferences (2)**: Choose your preferred scents from the available options. This field helps us understand your preferences for fragrances.
* **Product Performance (3)**: Select your assessment of the product's performance, based on your experience. This field helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our product.
* **Satisfaction Level (4)**: Enter your satisfaction level with the product, using the numerical scale. This field helps us understand your overall satisfaction with our product.
* **Additional Comments (5)**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience. This field is optional, but helps us to understand your thoughts and feelings.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for further discussion. This field helps us to contact you for more information.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted for further discussion. This field is optional, but helps us to contact you quickly.
* **Date of Survey (8)**: Fill in the date you completed this survey. This field helps us track the timing of your feedback.
* **Time of Survey (9)**: Fill in the time you completed this survey. This field helps us track the timing of your feedback.
* **Note (10)**: Add any additional notes about your experience. This field is optional, but helps us to understand your thoughts and feelings.
* **Assigned To (11)**: Select the person you would like your feedback to be assigned to, if you have a specific contact for your feedback.
* **Form ID (12)**: Select a "Form ID" to associate your feedback with a specific record.
* **User Input 2-14 (13-15)**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the remaining "User Input" fields, as they pertain to your experience with our room sprays. These fields help us to understand various aspects of your experience, such as ease of use, price, and availability.
