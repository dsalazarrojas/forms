# Recommendation Strength Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the recommendation strength of a customer's experience at your organization. The goal is to collect feedback on the touchpoints where customers feel a strong connection with your brand, product, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your genuine experience with our organization.
2. Select the correct answers for each question.
3. Ensure you answer every question, even if you're unsure about a particular touchpoint.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro`, note, optional): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before answering the questions.
* **Touchpoints** (`touchpoints`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about the touchpoints where you felt a strong connection with our organization. You can select multiple options if applicable.
	+ 'Yes' indicates a touchpoint where you felt a strong connection.
	+ 'No' indicates a touchpoint where you did not feel a strong connection.
* **question 1** (`question1`, select_one, optional): This question asks about a specific touchpoint related to our organization.
	+ 'Yes' indicates you strongly agree with the question.
	+ 'No' indicates you do not agree with the question.
* **answer 1** (`answer1`, number, optional): This question asks for a specific numeric answer.
* **question 2** (`question2`, select_one, optional): This question asks about another specific touchpoint related to our organization.
	+ 'Yes' indicates you strongly agree with the question.
	+ 'No' indicates you do not agree with the question.
* **answer 2** (`answer2`, number, optional): This question asks for another specific numeric answer.
* **question 3** (`question3`, note, optional): This is a question that does not have any options. Please provide a brief note about this question.
* **question 4** (`question4`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about multiple specific touchpoints related to our organization.
	+ 'Yes' indicates you felt a strong connection at a touchpoint.
	+ 'No' indicates you did not feel a strong connection at a touchpoint.
* **answer 4** (`answer4`, select_one, optional): This question asks for a specific answer for the selected touchpoints.
* **question 5** (`question5`, time, optional): This question asks for a specific time-related answer.
* **answer 5** (`answer5`, note, optional): This is a question that does not have any options. Please provide a brief note about this question.
* **question 6** (`question6`, select_one, optional): This question asks about another specific touchpoint related to our organization.
	+ 'Yes' indicates you strongly agree with the question.
	+ 'No' indicates you do not agree with the question.
* **answer 6** (`answer6`, text, optional): This question asks for a specific text-based answer.

## Tips
- Please answer every question honestly based on your genuine experience.
- If you are unsure about a question, try your best to provide an answer.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text field at the end of the form.
- This form is meant to be a subjective and personal experience-based assessment.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
