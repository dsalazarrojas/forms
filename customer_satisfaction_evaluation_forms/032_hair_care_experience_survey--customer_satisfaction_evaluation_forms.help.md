# Hair Care Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on their experience with hair care services. It's essential to provide honest and detailed responses to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply from the options provided for each question.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially for open-ended questions.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hair_care_experience_survey**: 
  • Please select your overall experience with hair care services. Options: Yes or No.
* **hair_care_service**:
  • Please rate your experience with the hair care service, including cutting, styling, and other services. Options: Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **hair_care_price**:
  • Please provide your honest opinion on the price of the hair care service.
* **hair_care_product_quality**:
  • Describe the quality of the hair care products used during your visit.
* **hair_care_services_value**:
  • Please select all that apply for the services offered by the hair care service that you value. Options: Yes or No.
* **hair_care_experience**:
  • Please rate your overall experience with the hair care service. Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or Option 6.
* **hair_care_willingness**:
  • Please provide your willingness to return to the hair care service for future services.
* **hair_care_recommend**:
  • Please select all that apply for how likely you are to recommend the hair care service. Options: Yes or No.
* **contact_details**:
  • Please select all that apply for how you would like to be contacted by the hair care service. Options: Yes or No.

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your responses.
* Select all that apply for each question.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the open-ended questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
