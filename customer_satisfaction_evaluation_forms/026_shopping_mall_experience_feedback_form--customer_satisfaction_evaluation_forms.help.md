# shopping_mall_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect customer feedback on their shopping mall experience. It consists of multiple pages with various fields that need to be filled out in order to provide detailed and accurate feedback. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "page_1" section, where you will be asked to evaluate the helpfulness of store staff. Answer the question "Were store staff helpful?" and select the level of their helpfulness.
2. Proceed to the "page_2" section, which contains a text field for any additional comments you'd like to share.
3. Go to "page_3" to indicate how you found the overall clarity of store information and select the corresponding options. If you have more comments, you can add them in the text field provided.
4. Answer "How helpful were the store staff?" in the "store_staff_helpfulness_level" field and select one of the options from the dropdown list.
5. Provide your contact details, including email and phone numbers if you'd like to be contacted for further communication.
6. Finally, select the "submit" button to complete the form and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shopping_mall_visit** (Page 1, Optional)
	+ This field is not applicable to this guide.
* **were_store_staff_helpful** (Page 1, Text)
	+ Answer this question to rate the helpfulness of store staff during your visit.
* **how_helpful_were_store_staff** (Page 1, Select)
	+ Select one of the options provided to indicate how helpful you found the store staff.
* **how_clearly_was_store_clarity** (Page 2, Select Multiple)
	+ Choose one or more options that describe how clearly you found store information during your visit.
* **product_info** (Page 2, Note)
	+ Add any additional comments or observations about product information you encountered during your visit.
* **overall_satisfaction** (Page 3, Select)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with your shopping mall experience.
* **comments** (Page 2, Text)
	+ Share any additional comments you'd like to make about your visit.
* **email** (Page 4, Email)
	+ Provide your email address to allow further communication about your feedback.
* **phone** (Page 4, Text)
	+ Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **name** (Page 4, Text)
	+ Provide your name for identification purposes.
* **submit** (Page 4, Text)
	+ Click this button to submit your completed form.
* **additional_comments** (Page 5, Note)
	+ Add any additional comments or information not covered in previous fields.
* **customer_info** (Page 5, Text)
	+ Enter any relevant customer information.
* **contact_us** (Page 5, Select)
	+ Select one of the options to choose how you'd like to be contacted.
* **follow_up** (Page 6, Select Multiple)
	+ Choose how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up about your feedback.

Note: Please fill out all the required fields to ensure that your feedback is submitted successfully.
