# Daily Life Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Life Feedback Survey is a short and straightforward form designed to gather your thoughts and experiences about your daily life. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand your needs and concerns, enabling us to improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, as your feedback will help us tailor our services and products to better suit your needs.
2. Make sure to complete the form only once a day. If you have already filled it out recently, do not fill it out again, as it may be unnecessary.
3. If you experience any issues or concerns that are not addressed in the current form, please contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required: false):
  This question measures how satisfied you are with your current life situation. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
- **Feedback** (`feedback_question`, text, required: false):
  Share with us any specific thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about your daily life. This is an open-ended field for you to express yourself freely. Please be as detailed as you can, and don't hesitate to mention any positive or negative experiences you've had recently.
- **Daily Life** (`daily_life`, number, required: false):
  This question is about your daily life, on a scale of 1-10. Please rate how you feel about your daily life.
