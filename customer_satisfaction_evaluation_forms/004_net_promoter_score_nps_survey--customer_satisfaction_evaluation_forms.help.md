# Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey is a tool for measuring customer satisfaction and loyalty. It's used to gauge how likely customers are to recommend your product or service, and to identify areas where improvement is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, choose the response that best matches your experience with our product or service.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text box at the end of the form to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall satisfaction with our product or service?** (`intro_question`, `text`, `required`): This question asks for your honest assessment of your overall satisfaction with our product or service. Please answer this question in a few sentences, and be as specific as possible.
* **How likely are you to recommend our product or service to a friend or colleague?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, `required`): This question asks you how likely you are to recommend our product or service to someone you know. Choose the response that best matches your intention:
	+ Not at all likely
	+ Not likely
	+ Likely
	+ Very likely
	+ Certain
* **How likely are you to purchase from us again?** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, `required`): This question asks you how likely you are to buy from us again in the future. Choose all the responses that apply:
	+ Not at all likely
	+ Not likely
	+ Likely
	+ Very likely
* **What is your main reason for not recommending our product or service?** (`question_3`, `text`, `optional`): If you have a reason why you're not recommending our product or service, please type it in here. This will help us understand what we can improve.
* **Would you like to provide any additional comments or feedback?** (`question_4`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any thoughts or suggestions, please share them with us in the text box. This is a chance to tell us anything else you'd like to tell us.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our product or service?** (`question_5`, `date`, `required`): This question asks for a numerical rating of your overall satisfaction. Please choose a date (represented as a numerical value in the format YYYY-MM-DD) to reflect your assessment.
