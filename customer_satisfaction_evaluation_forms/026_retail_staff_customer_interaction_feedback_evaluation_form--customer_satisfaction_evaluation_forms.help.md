# Retail Staff Customer Interaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their interactions with retail staff, allowing us to understand their experiences and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your interaction with the retail staff.
2. Select the relevant options for "customer_satisfaction" and "customer_rating" fields according to your experience.
3. Choose the correct options for "retail_staff_member_details" and "store_details" fields based on your interaction.
4. Provide the date and time of your interaction in the "date_of_interaction" and "time_of_interaction" fields.
5. Fill in the "store_name" and "store_facebook" fields if applicable.
