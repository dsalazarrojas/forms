# Customer Advocacy Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights from customers about their experiences and satisfaction with our company. By completing this form, you will be providing valuable information that will help us improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
*   Select the options that best match your thoughts and experiences.
*   If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What do you think about our customer service?** (`customer_satisfaction_question`, text, required: false)
    This question asks for your subjective opinion about our customer service.
*   **Please rate our customer service** (`customer_satisfaction_score`, number, required: false)
    This question asks you to rate our customer service on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Have you been helped by our Customer Success team?** (`have_been_helped_by_our_customer_success_team`, select_one, required: false)
