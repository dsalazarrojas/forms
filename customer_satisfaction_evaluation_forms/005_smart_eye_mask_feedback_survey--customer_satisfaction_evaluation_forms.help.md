# Smart Eye Mask Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the Smart Eye Mask product, including what they like and dislike about it, how the company has performed, and their overall satisfaction ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's questions and instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best match your experience or feelings about the Smart Eye Mask product.
4. Submit the form when you have finished answering all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets** (1, text, optional): This is an introductory text field where you can provide any comments or remarks about the Smart Eye Mask product or your experience with it. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **what_do_you_like** (2, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that you like about the Smart Eye Mask product. You can choose multiple options from the list.
* **how_did_we_do** (3, select_one, optional): Choose how you think the company has performed in relation to your expectations. You can select one option from the list.
* **what_do_you_dislike** (4, text, optional): This is another text field where you can express any complaints or suggestions about the Smart Eye Mask product or your experience with it. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **comments** (5, text, optional): Use this text field to provide any additional comments or remarks about the Smart Eye Mask product or your experience with it. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **rating1** (6, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the Smart Eye Mask product on a scale of 1-5. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **rating2** (7, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the Smart Eye Mask product's ease of use on a scale of 1-5. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **rating3** (8, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the Smart Eye Mask product's performance on a scale of 1-5. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **rating4** (9, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the overall experience of using the Smart Eye Mask product on a scale of 1-5. This field is optional and not required to answer.
* **rating5** (10, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the value for money of the Smart Eye Mask product on a scale of 1-5. This field is optional and not required to answer.
