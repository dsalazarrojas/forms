# customer_experience_enhancement_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer experience enhancement feedback form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of our service, product, and overall experience. This feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance our customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or more options for the "customer_experience" section, depending on your feelings about our customer service:
	* Terrible
	* Bad
	* Good
	* Very Good
	* Excellent
2. For the "service_quality" section, rate the quality of our service on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
3. In the "product_quality" section, select one option that best describes the quality of our product or service:
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Very Good
	* Excellent
4. In the "feedback" section, provide your thoughts on what we can improve. This is an optional but highly valued section, as it gives us valuable insights into your concerns.
5. Enter your email address in the "Your email" field to allow us to follow up on your feedback, if needed.
6. Enter your name in the "Your name" field for identification purposes.
7. You can add additional comments in the "Additional comments" section.
7. Finally, click "Submit" to send your feedback. You can also click "Cancel" to exit the form without submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about our customer service?** (`customer_experience`, select_multiple): This question asks you to rate your overall experience with our customer service. Select one or more options that best describe your feelings.
* **How would you rate the quality of our service?** (`service_quality`, number): This question asks you to rate the quality of our service on a scale of 1-5. A higher score indicates a better experience.
* **How would you rate the quality of our product or service?** (`product_quality`, select_one): This question asks you to rate the quality of our product or service. Select one option that best describes your experience.
* **What can we improve?** (`feedback`, text): This question asks you to provide your thoughts on what we can improve. This is an optional but highly valued section, as it gives us valuable insights into your concerns.
* **Your email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address to allow us to follow up on your feedback, if needed.
* **Your name** (`name`, text): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Additional comments** (`message`, note): Add any additional comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text): Click this button to send your feedback.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text): Click this button to exit the form without submitting.
