# Customer Experience Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers about their experience at our business. It helps us understand areas of improvement and identify what we do well.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in your overall experience and satisfaction ratings.
3. Provide your contact information if you'd like to be contacted.
4. Share your feedback and suggestions for improvement.
5. Answer any follow-up questions or rating questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience at our business?** (id: 1, text)
  This is a free text field where you can share your thoughts about your overall experience at our business.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with our services?** (id: 2, select_one)
  Choose a rating from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied) to express your level of satisfaction.
* **How would you rate our customer service?** (id: 3, number)
  Rate our customer service from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (id: 4, email)
  If you'd like to be contacted, provide your email address. This field is optional.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (id: 5, text)
  Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted. This field is optional.

  Choose the time of your visit.
