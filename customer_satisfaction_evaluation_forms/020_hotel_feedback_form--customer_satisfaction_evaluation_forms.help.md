# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Feedback Form is a tool for guests to provide their opinion and feedback on their stay at our hotel. It helps us to understand their satisfaction and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit your feedback. The form has the following pages:

1. **Hotel Feedback Form** (Page 1): This is the title of the form.
2. **Guest Name** (Page 2): Please enter your name to let us know who you are.
3. **Email** (Page 3): Enter your email address to contact you in case we need to follow up on your feedback.
4. **Guest Feedback** (Page 4): Share your thoughts about your stay, including what you liked and disliked.
5. **Rating** (Page 5): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
6. **Additional Comments** (Page 6): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please add them here.
7. **Submit** (Page 7): Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Feedback Form**: This is the title of the form. It is not required to fill out, but it helps us to identify the form's purpose.
* **Guest Name**: Please enter your name to let us know who you are. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need to follow up on your feedback. (Required)
* **Guest Feedback**: Share your thoughts about your stay, including what you liked and disliked. (Required)
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. (Required)
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments or suggestions, please add them here. (Required)
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us. (Required)
