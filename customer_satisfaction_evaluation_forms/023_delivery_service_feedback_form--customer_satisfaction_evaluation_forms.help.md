# Delivery Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their delivery experiences. This will help us understand what we are doing well and what we can improve on to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out the required fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Service Feedback**: This field allows you to provide a brief description of your delivery experience.
* **Service Rating**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates the quality of our service. Please rate the service as follows:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Delivery Driver Name**: This field is for you to provide the name of the delivery driver who handled your delivery.
* **Feedback**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Date of Delivery**: This field is for you to select the date of your delivery. Please select the date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Time of Delivery**: This field is for you to select the time of your delivery. Please select the time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 AM).
* **Delivery Service Rating**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates the quality of our delivery service. Please rate the service as follows:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Delivery Time**: This field is for you to enter the estimated time it took for our delivery service to arrive.
* **Delivery Date**: This field is for you to select the date of your delivery. Please select the date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Customer Service Rating**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates the quality of our customer service. Please rate the service as follows:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates your overall satisfaction with our delivery service. Please rate your satisfaction as follows:
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Service Quality**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates the quality of our service. Please rate the service as follows:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Service Compatibility**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates the compatibility of our service with your needs. Please rate the service as follows:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Good
	+ 5: Excellent
* **Will Use Again**: This field is a scale from 1-5 that rates if you will use our delivery service again. Please rate as follows:
	+ 1: Very unlikely
	+ 2: Unlikely
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Likely
	+ 5: Very likely
* **Recommendations**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions for our delivery service.
* **Contact Us**: This field is for you to enter your contact information (email or phone number) in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Phone Number**: This field is for you to enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is for you to select the tool used during your delivery. Please select "ChatJimmy" if the assigned tool was ChatJimmy, or "Other" if it was something else.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Recommendations" field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us through the "Contact Us" field.
