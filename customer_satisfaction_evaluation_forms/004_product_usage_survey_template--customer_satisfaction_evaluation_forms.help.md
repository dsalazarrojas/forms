# product usage survey template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on your experience with our product. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction with the product and guide us in making improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, use the "additional comments" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This page provides a brief overview of the form.
* **Product usage** (Page 2): Please tell us about your experience with our product, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Rating** (Page 3): Rate your overall satisfaction with our product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Contact info** (Page 4): Select how you would like to be contacted: by phone, email, or both.
* **Additional comments** (Page 5): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.
* **Date** (Page 6): Please enter the date you started using our product.
* **Time** (Page 7): Please enter the time you last used our product.
* **Product usage survey template category** (Page 8): This field seems to be asking for information that is already covered by other fields. However, to follow the form's structure, please select all categories that apply: product usage survey templates, customer satisfaction evaluation forms, or other.
* **Product usage survey template description** (Page 9): This field seems to be redundant. However, please use it to provide any additional information about your experience with our product.

Note: The fields on page eight and nine might not be as relevant as other fields, but to follow the form's structure, we have included them in the help guide.
