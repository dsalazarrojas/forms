# Comprehensive Record Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our services. The collected data will help us improve our services to better meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the "Service received" field with the specific service you used.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the service by selecting one of the options provided in the "Satisfaction level" field.
3. Rate the ease of access to the service in the "Ease of access" field.
4. Rate the helpfulness of staff in resolving your issue in the "Staff helpfulness" field.
5. Indicate whether your issue was resolved to your satisfaction in the "Issue resolved" field.
6. Rate your likelihood to recommend our services to others in the "Likelihood to recommend" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service received**: The specific service you used that you are providing feedback on.
- **Satisfaction level**: Rate your satisfaction with the service by selecting one of the following options:
  - Very satisfied: You were extremely pleased with the service.
  - Satisfied: You were generally pleased with the service.
  - Neutral: You were neither satisfied nor dissatisfied with the service.
  - Dissatisfied: You were generally dissatisfied with the service.
  - Very dissatisfied: You were extremely displeased with the service.
- **Ease of access**: Rate the ease of access to the service you used:
  - Very easy: The service was very easy to access.
  - Easy: The service was relatively easy to access.
  - Moderate: The service was moderately difficult to access.
  - Difficult: The service was difficult to access.
- **Staff helpfulness**: Rate the helpfulness of staff in resolving your issue:
  - Excellent: The staff was extremely helpful.
  - Good: The staff was generally helpful.
  - Fair: The staff was neutral.
  - Poor: The staff was not very helpful.
- **Issue resolved**: Indicate whether your issue was resolved to your satisfaction:
  - True: Your issue was completely resolved to your satisfaction.
  - Partially: Your issue was partially resolved to your satisfaction.
  - False: Your issue was not resolved to your satisfaction.
- **Likelihood to recommend**: Rate your likelihood of recommending our services to others:
  - Definitely: You would definitely recommend our services to others.
  - Probably: You would probably recommend our services to others.
  - Maybe: You are unsure if you would recommend our services to others.
  - Unlikely: You would not recommend our services to others.
- **Respondent name**: This is an optional field for providing your name, but if left blank, your feedback will be anonymous.
- **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our services.
