> # Gym Facility Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Facility Feedback Form is designed to gather information from customers about their experience at our gym facility. This form helps us understand your opinions and suggestions on various aspects of our gym, such as cleanliness, customer service, and overall satisfaction. By providing feedback, you are helping us to improve and better serve our members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the page with the question you want to answer.
2. Read the question carefully and choose the best option that applies to your experience.
3. Fill in the required information, such as your email and phone number (if applicable).
4. Add any additional comments you may have about your experience.
5. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
6. Submit the form to share your feedback with us.
