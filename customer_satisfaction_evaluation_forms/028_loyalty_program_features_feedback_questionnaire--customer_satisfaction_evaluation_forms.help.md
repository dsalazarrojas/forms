# Loyalty Program Features Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather your feedback on our loyalty program features and overall experience. Your input will help us understand how we're doing and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
* Fill out the form by providing your feedback on the various aspects of our loyalty program.
* Answer every question, even if you don't have an answer for a particular field.
* If you have more feedback to provide, you can use the "Do you have any other feedback?" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate our program fulfillment? (Page 1)**: This question is about how well we've met your expectations in terms of program delivery. Please rate from 1 (not at all fulfilled) to 10 (fully fulfilled).
* **Reward redemptions (Page 1)**: Please describe your experience with redeeming rewards from our program.
* **Loyalty program value (Page 1)**: How valuable do you find our loyalty program? Choose one of the options to indicate how you feel about it.
* **Program features (Page 1)**: Which of the following features do you think are most important for our loyalty program? Choose one or more options.
* **Feedback on features (Page 1)**: Which aspects of our program have you been satisfied or dissatisfied with? Choose one or more options.
* **Overall experience (Page 1)**: How would you rate your overall experience with our loyalty program? Rate from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **Customer service (Page 2)**: How would you rate our customer service? Choose one of the options.
* **Email (Page 2)**: Please provide your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback).
* **Phone (Page 2)**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **How satisfied are you with our loyalty program? (Page 3)**: How satisfied are you with our loyalty program? Choose one of the options.
* **What improvements do you have for the loyalty program? (Page 3)**: If you have any suggestions for improvements, please describe them here.
* **Do you have any other feedback? (Page 3)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
