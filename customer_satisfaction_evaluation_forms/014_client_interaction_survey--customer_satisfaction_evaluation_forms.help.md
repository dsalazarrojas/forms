# Client Interaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Interaction Survey is a tool used to evaluate the quality of service provided by our organization. By providing feedback through this survey, you will help us to identify areas of improvement and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely to provide thorough feedback.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Any Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, Optional): This field is for your name, if you wish to be identified in the feedback.
* **Date of Service or Interaction** (`service_date`, date, Required): Please provide the date of your recent interaction with our staff member.
* **Name of Staff Member you interacted with** (`staff_member`, text, Optional): If you recall the name of the staff member you interacted with, please provide it here.
* **Satisfaction Ratings** (`section_ratings`, note, Optional): This section is for rating your overall satisfaction with our service. Please read the hints for the specific ratings below.
* **How would you rate the professionalism of our staff?** (`professionalism_rating`, select_one, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the professionalism of our staff member, using the scale: 1 - Poor, 2 - Average, 3 - Good, 4 - Excellent, 5 - Extremely Excellent.
* **How clearly did we communicate with you?** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the communication, using the scale: 1 - Not Clear, 2 - Average, 3 - Good, 4 - Very Clear, 5 - Extremely Clear.
* **Was your issue or request resolved effectively?** (`resolution_status`, select_one, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the resolution of your issue or request, using the scale: True, Partially, or False.
* **How satisfied were you with the speed of our response?** (`response_speed`, select_one, Optional): Rate your satisfaction with the response speed, using the scale: 1 - Dissatisfied, 2 - Average, 3 - Satisfied, 4 - Very Satisfied, 5 - Extremely Satisfied.
* **Feedback** (`section_feedback`, note, Optional): This section is for providing any additional feedback or comments you may have.
* **What did we do well during this interaction?** (`strengths`, text, Optional): Share with us what we did well during the interaction.
* **What could we have done better?** (`improvements`, text, Optional): Share with us what we could have done better during the interaction.
* **Would you recommend our services to others?** (`referral_likelihood`, select_one, Optional): Rate your willingness to recommend our services to others, using the scale: True, False, Maybe.
* **Any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback you may have.
