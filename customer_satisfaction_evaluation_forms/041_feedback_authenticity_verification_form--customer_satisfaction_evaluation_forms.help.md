# Feedback Authenticity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feedback Authenticity Verification Form is used to gather feedback from customers and verify its authenticity. This form helps us understand customer satisfaction and identify any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose one of the available options for "Customer Satisfaction Evaluation".
2. If necessary, provide feedback in the "Feedback" text box.
3. Select "Active" or "Inactive" for "Verification Status".
4. Choose a date and time for "Verification Date".
5. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" text box.
6. Enter your email address for verification purposes.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Enter the customer's name.
9. Enter the customer's contact information.
10. Enter the customer's feedback.
11. Enter your evaluation of the customer's satisfaction.
12. Choose a rating for the customer's satisfaction.
13. Choose whether the customer's feedback is authentic ("Yes" or "No").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction Evaluation**: This field is used to evaluate the customer's satisfaction with your product or service. Choose one of the options (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
* **Feedback**: If you have additional comments or concerns about the customer's feedback, enter them here.
* **Authenticity Verification**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the customer's feedback is authentic.
* **Verification Status**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the verification status of the customer's feedback.
* **Verification Date**: Enter the date when you verified the customer's feedback.
* **Verification Time**: Enter the time when you verified the customer's feedback.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the customer's feedback.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Contact Info**: Enter the customer's contact information.
* **Customer Feedback**: Enter the customer's feedback.
* **Customer Satisfaction Evaluation**: Enter a numerical value (1-5) indicating the customer's satisfaction.
* **Rating**: Enter a numerical value (1-5) indicating the customer's satisfaction rating.
* **Customer Feedback Authenticity**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the customer's feedback is authentic.

## Tips
* Please complete this form carefully and honestly to ensure accurate information.
* Avoid duplicating effort by completing this form only once for each customer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or customer service team.
* This form is used to gather feedback and satisfaction evaluations from customers, so be sure to review the customer's feedback carefully and consider their concerns.
