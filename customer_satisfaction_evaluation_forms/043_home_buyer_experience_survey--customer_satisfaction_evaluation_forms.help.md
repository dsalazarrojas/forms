# Home Buyer Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experiences with our real estate services. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most appropriate answer from the available options.
3. You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Buyer Experience**:
	+ This field is where you can provide a brief summary of your overall experience with our real estate services.
* **What was the most important factor for you when searching for a home?**:
	+ Select the factor that was most important to you when searching for a home. Our options include:
		- Lifestyle
		- Location
		- Budget
		- Size
		- Amenities
		- Other (please specify)
* **On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied were you with the overall home buying experience?**:
	+ This question is self-explanatory. Rate your satisfaction with our services on a scale of 1-5.
* **What was the most frustrating part of your experience?**:
	+ This field is for you to provide a detailed description of the most frustrating part of your experience with our services.
* **How did you find the communication with our team?**:
	+ Select the level of satisfaction you have with the communication from our team. Our options include:
		- Exceptional
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **What could we do to improve?**:
	+ This field is for you to provide additional comments or suggestions on how we can improve our services.
* **Have you recommended our real estate services to anyone?**:
	+ Select whether you have recommended our services to others.
* **How did you find the overall value of the service?**:
	+ This question is self-explanatory. Rate the value you received from our services on a scale.
* **What would make you more likely to recommend our service in the future?**:
	+ This field is for you to provide additional comments or suggestions on what would make you more likely to recommend our services.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions for us?**:
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for our services.
* **How did you find the overall experience?**:
	+ Select the level of satisfaction you have with the overall experience. Our options include:
		- Exceptional
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
