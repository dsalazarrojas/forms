# Point Of Sale Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Point Of Sale Feedback Form is a tool used to gather customer feedback after a purchase or interaction with our store. This form helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please complete it thoroughly and honestly. You will be asked to provide your feedback in the following steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Feedback** (text field)
2. Rate your overall experience with our store using the **Overall Rating** (number field)
3. If applicable, provide the **Store Name** and **Product Name** (text fields)
4. Share any additional **Comments** you have about your visit
5. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** (text fields) for contact information (optional)
6. Select the time of day you visited our store using the **Time** (time field) and the date of your visit using the **Date** (date field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback** (required): Please share your thoughts and impressions about your experience with our store. This will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve.
* **Overall Rating** (required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Store Name** (optional): If you visited a specific store, please let us know its name. This helps us track feedback for each location.
* **Product Name** (optional): If you purchased a product, please let us know its name. This helps us understand which products need improvement.
* **Comments** (optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your visit.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to offer assistance.
* **Time** (required): Select the time of day you visited our store.
* **Date** (required): Select the date of your visit.
