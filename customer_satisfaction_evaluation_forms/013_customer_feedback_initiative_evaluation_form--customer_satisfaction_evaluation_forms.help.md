# Customer Feedback Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Feedback Initiative Evaluation Form is a tool for customers to provide their feedback and opinions on a specific initiative or project. This form helps us understand your experience and gather information about areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on **Page 1: Customer Information**.
2. Answer the questions on **Page 2: Feedback Questions**.
3. Provide any additional comments or observations on **Page 3: Additional Comments**.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Customer Information

* **Customer Information** (`<name>`, `text`, not required): This is the space where you can provide your name, which will help us identify you as a participant in this initiative.
* **Customer Information** (`<name>`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or identification information, you can provide it here.

### Page 2: Feedback Questions

* **Feedback Questions** (`<text>`, `text`, not required): This is the space where you can provide your thoughts and opinions on the initiative. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.

### Page 3: Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments** (`<text>`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding the initiative, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback. Your input is valuable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can modify this form to suit your needs or create new forms based on this template. Just give credit if you use it.
