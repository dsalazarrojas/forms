# real_estate_agent_service_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with real estate agents. The feedback collected will help us evaluate the performance of the agents and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your actual experience with the real estate agent.
2. Select one of the options provided for each question, or provide a response in the text field if the question requires a longer answer.
3. Fill out all questions, even if it takes some time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Contact (1)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Was the agent's first communication with you in-person or over the phone? Answer "Yes" if the agent came to visit or called you.
* **Communication (2)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: How did the agent communicate with you? (e.g., email, phone, text, in-person)
* **Follow-up (3)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Did the agent follow up with you after the first contact? Answer "Yes" if they did.
* **Professionalism (4)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Was the agent's behavior professional during your interactions? (e.g., friendly, attentive, knowledgeable)
* **Availability (5)** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Answer: How available was the agent to address your needs? (Scale: 1-10)
* **Response Time (6)** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Answer: How quickly did the agent respond to your inquiries? (Scale: 1-10)
* **Rating (7)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Overall, how would you rate your experience with the agent? (Good/Fair)
* **Comments (8)** (`note`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with the agent?
* **Email (9)** (`email`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What was the agent's email address, if any?
* **Phone (10)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What was the agent's phone number, if any?
* **Follow-up Contacted (11)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Did the agent follow up with you after the first contact? Answer "Yes" if they did.
* **Follow-up Time (12)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Were the follow-ups from the agent timely? Answer "Yes" if they were.
* **Follow-up Frequency (13)** (`number`, required: false)
	+ Answer: How frequent were the follow-ups from the agent? (Scale: 1-10)
* **Overall Experience (14)** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What were the positive and negative aspects of your experience with the agent? (Select all that apply)
* **First Contact Type (15)** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What type of contact was the first contact with the agent? (Select all that apply) (e.g., phone call, email, in-person meeting)
* **Follow-up Type (16)** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What type of follow-up did the agent use? (Select all that apply) (e.g., phone call, email, text)
* **Communication Method (17)** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Answer: How did the agent communicate with you? (Select all that apply) (e.g., phone, email, text)
* **Contact Person (18)** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Answer: With whom did you first contact the agent? (Select all that apply) (e.g., real estate company, realtor)
* **Follow-up Person (19)** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Who followed up with you after the first contact? (Select all that apply) (e.g., real estate company, realtor)
* **Follow-up Time (20)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: Were the follow-ups from the agent timely? Answer "Yes" if they were.
* **Follow-up Frequency (21)** (`select_one`, required: false)
	+ Answer: How frequent were the follow-ups from the agent? (Yes/No)
* **Contact Person Name (22)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What was the name of the contact person, if any?
* **Follow-up Person Name (23)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What was the name of the follow-up person, if any?
* **Communication Person (24)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What was the name of the person who communicated with you, if any?
* **Communication Person Name (25)** (`text`, required: false)
	+ Answer: What was the name of the communication person, if any? 

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to give us a complete picture of your experience.
* You can provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your experience with the agent.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [support email or phone number].
