# Cable Bundle Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a customer satisfaction evaluation form designed to collect feedback on the cable and internet services provided by the company. It will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best reflects your experience.
3. For required fields, make sure to provide a response to move on to the next page.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, review your answers and submit the form.
