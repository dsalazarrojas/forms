# Happiness Mastery Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a program or service, specifically regarding their happiness and mastery. The goal is to help us understand what we do well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Happiness Mastery Feedback Survey** title, which should already be filled in as a default.
2. Enter your **Customer ID**, if you have one.
3. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction** on a scale of 1 to 5.
4. Choose the **Options** that best describe your **Overall Satisfaction**.
5. Provide your **Feedback** about your experience.
6. Select the **Program Participants** who you interacted with during the program.
7. Choose the areas where you think we could **Improve**.
8. Suggest any **Recommendations** for our service.
9. Leave any **Additional Comments** about your experience.
10. Provide your **Email** and/or **Phone Number** for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Happiness Mastery Feedback Survey**: This is the title of the form. Please leave this field as is, as it is already filled in.
* **Customer ID**: Enter your unique customer ID, if you have one. This helps us identify you in our records.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose the option that best describes your overall satisfaction:
	+ Option 1: Very Dissatisfied
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Option 3: Neutral
	+ Option 4: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Option 5: Very Satisfied
* **Feedback**: Provide a brief description of your experience with our service. Be as specific as possible to help us understand what went well and what didn't.
* **Program Participants**: Select all the people you interacted with during the program. This helps us understand who was involved in your experience.
* **Improvement Areas**: Choose the areas where you think we could improve. You can select all that apply.
* **Recommendations**: Suggest any improvements or changes that you think we should make to our service.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact, if you prefer.

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help us better understand your experience and provide the best possible service.
