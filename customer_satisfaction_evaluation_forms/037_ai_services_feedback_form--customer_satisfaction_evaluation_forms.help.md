# AI Services Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with AI services. Please take a few minutes to provide your honest feedback to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the AI service you used from the "AI service used" dropdown menu.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service by selecting a number from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest) in the "Overall satisfaction" field.
3. Rate the reliability of the service by selecting a number from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest) in the "Reliability of the service" field.
4. Rate the value for money of the service by selecting a number from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest) in the "Value for money" field.
5. If you encountered any critical issues, describe them in the "Major issues encountered" field.
6. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions for improvement" field.
7. Indicate whether you would recommend the service to others by selecting "True", "Maybe", or "False" in the "Would you recommend this service to others" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Details**: This is an optional field where you can provide additional context about the service you used. You can type in any relevant information you think is helpful for us to know.
* **Customer Name**: This is an optional field for you to provide your name.
* **Company**: This is an optional field for you to provide your company information.
* **AI Service Used**: Select the AI service you used from the dropdown menu.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service by selecting a number from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any specific ideas or feature requests you have for improving the service.
