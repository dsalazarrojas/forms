# Painter And Decorator Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painter And Decorator Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have received a service from our painter and decorator services. This form will help us understand your experience and satisfaction with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses, especially in the text fields.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, contact us directly for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name is the first part of your name, which will be used to address you in our response.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address you used to contact us. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.

### Phone number
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, number, not required): This is your contact number. If you provide it, we can contact you back if we need more information or clarification.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Please rate your experience with us from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).

### Service Quality
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of service you received from us:
	+ Interior
	+ Exterior
	+ Both

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): If you have any comments or suggestions about your experience, please share them here. This will help us understand your experience better.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your feedback form. Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve our services.
