# Client Feedback Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client feedback and monitoring information to help us understand your experiences with our services. We value your input and use it to improve our service quality, communication, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your details:
	* First Name: Enter your legal first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your legal last name.
	* Email Address: Enter your primary contact email.
	* Phone Number: Enter your best contact number. (Optional)
2. Select the monitoring period for this feedback:
	* Monitoring Period: Choose how long you've been monitoring our services. This could be "This Week", "This Month", "This Quarter", "This Year", or "Specific Date Range".
3. Indicate how often you use our services:
	* How Often Do You Use Our Services?: Choose "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually", or "As Needed".
4. Evaluate your overall satisfaction:
	* Overall Satisfaction This Period: Select "Very Satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", or "Very Dissatisfied".
5. Compare your satisfaction with previous periods:
	* How Has Your Satisfaction Changed?: Choose "Much Better", "Somewhat Better", "About the Same", "Somewhat Worse", "Much Worse", or "First Time Responding".
6. Rate our service quality:
	* Service Quality This Period: Select "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
7. Rate our communication:
	* Communication Rating: Select "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
8. Rate our responsiveness:
	* Responsiveness Rating: Select "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
9. Rate our reliability:
	* Reliability Rating: Select "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", "Poor".
10. Rate the value for money:
	* Value for Money Rating: Select "Excellent Value", "Good Value", "Fair Value", "Poor Value", or "Very Poor Value".
11. Report any issues encountered:
	* Did You Encounter Any Issues?: Select "No Issues", "Minor Issues", "Moderate Issues", "Significant Issues", or "Major Issues".
	* Describe the Issues: Enter a description if you experienced any issues.
12. Indicate issue resolution:
	* Were Issues Resolved Satisfactorily?: Select "Yes, Completely", "Yes, Mostly", "Partially", "False", or "Not Applicable".
13. Share positive experiences:
	* Positive Experiences This Period: Enter any positive experiences.
14. Provide improvement suggestions:
	* Suggestions for Improvement: Enter any suggestions for improvement.
15. Indicate additional services needed:
	* Additional Services You Might Need: Enter any additional services you may need in the future.
16. Plan to continue our relationship:
	* Do You Plan to Continue Our Relationship?: Select "Definitely Yes", "Probably Yes", "Undecided", "Probably No", or "Definitely No".
17. Rate your willingness to recommend:
	* How Likely Are You to Recommend Us?: Select "Very Likely", "Likely", "Neutral", "Unlikely", or "Very Unlikely".
18. Enter any additional comments:
	* Additional Comments: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
19. Set the monitoring form date:
	* Monitoring Form Date: Enter the date this form was completed.

This form is designed to be completed by clients who have used our services in the selected monitoring period. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our services.
