# Customer Feedback Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer feedback tracker is a form designed to collect feedback from customers to improve our services or products. It helps us understand their experiences and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name to help us identify you as a customer.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you in case of any follow-up questions.
3. Rate your experience from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
4. Share your feedback about your experience, and don't hesitate to suggest any improvements.
5. Choose a category for your feedback.
6. Add any additional comment you'd like to provide.
7. If you'd like to be contacted, please select your preferred method (Phone, Email, Chat, or In-app).
8. Enter the time you'd like to be contacted (optional).
9. Provide the date and time you're providing the feedback (optional).
10. Indicate whether you're a Returning, New, or Loyal customer.
11. Add any additional department you'd like to contact (optional).
12. If you've been contacted by a specific person, please provide their name (optional).
13. Specify where you obtained the feedback (optional).
14. Indicate what type of customer you are (optional, multiple select).
15. Select a customer segment (optional).
16. Choose the type of feedback you're providing (optional).
17. Select the channel through which you were contacted (optional).
18. Select another customer segment (optional).
19. Choose another customer segment (optional).
