# E Bike Customer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers who have purchased an E-Bike. The goal is to collect information on customer satisfaction, which will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is designed to be simple and easy to complete. Please follow the steps below:

### 1. Name
* <label>Name</label> (text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Email
* <label>Email</label> (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Type:** Email
* **Hint:** Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Rating
* <label>Rating</label> (number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Number
* **Hint:** Rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Bad and 5 is Very Good.

### 4. E-Bike Purchased
* <label>E-Bike Purchased</label> (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the E-Bike model you purchased.
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Hint:** Select the E-Bike model that matches your purchase.

### 5. Purchase Date
* <label>Purchase Date</label> (date, required/optional): Enter the date you purchased your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Date
* **Hint:** Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.

### 6. Purchase Time
* <label>Purchase Time</label> (time, optional/optional): Enter the time you purchased your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Time
* **Hint:** Enter the time in the format HH:MM.

### 7. Satisfaction
* <label>Satisfaction</label> (select_one, required/optional): Select your level of satisfaction with your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Select One
* **Hint:** Select Very Good, Good, Neutral, Bad, or Very Bad.

### 8. E-Bike Used
* <label>E-Bike Used</label> (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you have used your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Hint:** Select True if you have used your E-Bike, False otherwise.

### 9. Recommended
* <label>Recommended</label> (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you would recommend your E-Bike to others.
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Hint:** Select True if you would recommend your E-Bike, False otherwise.

### 10. Comments
* <label>Comments</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter any comments or suggestions about your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter any additional information about your experience with your E-Bike.

### 11. E-Bike Model
* <label>E-Bike Model</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter the model of your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter the model number or name of your E-Bike.

### 12. E-Bike Color
* <label>E-Bike Color</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter the color of your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter the color of your E-Bike.

### 13. Customer Service
* <label>Customer Service</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter any comments about our customer service.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter any feedback about our customer service team.

### 14. E-Bike Condition
* <label>E-Bike Condition</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter the condition of your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter the condition of your E-Bike, e.g., new, used, etc.

### 15. Purchase Channel
* <label>Purchase Channel</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter the channel through which you purchased your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter the store or website where you purchased your E-Bike.

### 16. Purchase Price
* <label>Purchase Price</label> (number, optional/optional): Enter the price you paid for your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Number
* **Hint:** Enter the price you paid for your E-Bike, if applicable.

### 17. Overall Satisfaction
* <label>Overall Satisfaction</label> (select_one, required/optional): Select your overall level of satisfaction with your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Select One
* **Hint:** Select Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

### 18. Will Recommend
* <label>Will Recommend</label> (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you would recommend your E-Bike to others.
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Hint:** Select True if you would recommend your E-Bike, False otherwise.

### 19. Will Purchase
* <label>Will Purchase</label> (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you would consider purchasing an E-Bike again.
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Hint:** Select True if you would consider purchasing an E-Bike again, False otherwise.

### 20. Will Recommend E-Bike
* <label>Will Recommend E-Bike</label> (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you would recommend the E-Bike to others.
* **Type:** Select Multiple
* **Hint:** Select True if you would recommend the E-Bike, False otherwise.

### 21. Rating Suggestion
* <label>Rating Suggestion</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter any suggestions for improving your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter any ideas for improving our E-Bikes.

### 22. Suggestions
* <label>Suggestions</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions about your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter any further ideas or feedback about your E-Bike.

### 23. Contact Number
* <label>Contact Number</label> (text, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter your phone number, if applicable.

### 24. Email
* <label>Email</label> (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Type:** Email
* **Hint:** Enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents.

### 25. Comments
* <label>Comments</label> (text, optional/optional): Enter any final comments or feedback about your E-Bike.
* **Type:** Text
* **Hint:** Enter any additional thoughts or ideas about your E-Bike experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Enter accurate and honest information about your experience with your E-Bike.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your email address].
