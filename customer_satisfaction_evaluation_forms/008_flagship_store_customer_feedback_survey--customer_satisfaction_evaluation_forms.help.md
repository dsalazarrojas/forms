The Flagship Store Customer Feedback Survey form is designed to collect valuable insights from customers about their experience at the Flagship Store. This form aims to gather information on customer satisfaction, feedback, and other relevant details that can help us improve our services. To avoid duplication, we'll be using the data collected from this form to track and analyze customer feedback over time.

The form consists of 8 sections, each designed to capture a specific aspect of the customer's experience. Please ensure you complete the form accurately and honestly to provide us with the most helpful feedback.

## Purpose
This form is for customers to share their thoughts and feelings about their experience at the Flagship Store. The data collected will be used to enhance our services, customer satisfaction, and overall store experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Customer Information** (page 1) with your basic contact details.
2.  On page 2, provide your **Contact Information**, including your email address, which is required for us to get back to you.
3.  On page 3, record the **Feedback Details** of your visit, which includes the date you visited us.
4.  Choose your **Rating** (page 4) based on your overall experience at the Flagship Store.
5.  Select your level of **Satisfaction** (page 5) with our store, from "Very Satisfied" to "Not Satisfied."
6.  In **Comments** (page 6), add any additional thoughts or comments about your visit.
7.  On page 7, select the **Visit Date** of your visit to the Flagship Store.
8.  On the final page, choose your **Visit Time** when you visited our store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (page 1) (optional)
    *   Label: Customer Information
    *   Type: Text
    *   This field is meant for customers to provide basic contact details.
*   **Contact Information** (page 2) (required)
    *   Label: Contact Information
    *   Type: Email
    *   This field is required to allow us to contact you back.
*   **Feedback Details** (page 3) (required)
    *   Label: Feedback Details
    *   Type: Date
    *   Please enter the date you visited the Flagship Store.
*   **Rating** (page 4) (required)
    *   Label: Rating
    *   Type: Number
    *   Select a number from 1 to 5 to rate your experience at the Flagship Store.
*   **Satisfaction** (page 5) (required)
    *   Label: Satisfaction
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Choose your level of satisfaction with our store from "Very Satisfied," "Somewhat Satisfied," or "Not Satisfied."
*   **Comments** (page 6) (optional)
    *   Label: Comments
    *   Type: Note
    *   Add any additional comments about your visit.
*   **Visit Date** (page 7) (required)
    *   Label: Visit Date
    *   Type: Date
    *   Select the date of your visit to the Flagship Store.
*   **Visit Time** (page 8) (required)
    *   Label: Visit Time
    *   Type: Time
    *   Choose the time of your visit to the Flagship Store.

**Tips**

*   Please fill in the form accurately and honestly.
*   Ensure you select a valid email address in the **Contact Information** field.
