# Chat Interaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chat Interaction Survey is designed to collect feedback on customers' experiences with the chat support system. The form gathers information on customer satisfaction, agent performance, and overall experience during the chat interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including your name, email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up communication), and date of the chat interaction.
2. Choose the type of issue you addressed during the chat from the provided options.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the resolution and the overall chat experience.
4. Assess the chat agent's friendliness, knowledge, responsiveness, and professionalism.
5. Rate the ease of using the chat interface and the speed of the chat responses.
6. Provide feedback on your overall chat experience and if you would use the chat support again in the future.
7. Suggest improvements for future development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up communication.
* **Date of Chat Interaction**: Choose the date of the chat interaction from the calendar.
* **Name of Chat Agent**: If you remember the agent's name, enter it here.
* **Type of Issue Addressed**: Choose the type of issue you addressed during the chat from the provided options.
* **Was Your Issue Resolved**: Rate your satisfaction with the resolution of the issue.
* **Satisfaction with Resolution**: Rate your satisfaction with the resolution.
* **Agent Friendliness**: Rate the chat agent's friendliness.
* **Agent Knowledge and Competence**: Rate the chat agent's knowledge and competence.
* **Agent Responsiveness**: Rate the chat agent's responsiveness.
* **Agent Professionalism**: Rate the chat agent's professionalism (Note: This field is optional).
* **Ease of Using Chat Interface**: Rate the ease of using the chat interface.
* **Speed of Chat Response**: Rate the speed of the chat responses.
* **Overall Chat Experience**: Rate your overall chat experience.
* **Would You Use Chat Support Again**: Indicate if you would use the chat support again in the future.
* **Would You Recommend Chat Support to Others**: Indicate if you would recommend the chat support to others.
* **What Went Well**: Describe the positive aspects of the chat interaction.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide suggestions for future development.

Note: Please answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our chat support system.
