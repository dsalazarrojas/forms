# Customer Success Story Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Success Story Questionnaire is designed to collect information about customers who have had a positive experience with our company. This information is used to showcase customer success stories and case studies that can be used for marketing and sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the customer success story is genuine and representative of our company's capabilities.
2. Only include stories from customers who have had a positive experience with our company.
3. Do not submit duplicate stories.
4. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
5. Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Success Story ID** (`customer_success_story_id`, number, required: false): This field is used to track and identify the customer success story.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company that the customer has had a positive experience with.
* **Customer Success Story** (`customer_success_story`, text, required: false): Describe the customer's success story in as much detail as possible.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who had the positive experience.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the job title of the customer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a contact email for the customer or their representative.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter a contact phone number for the customer or their representative.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the start date of the customer's project or engagement with our company.

## Tips
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
* If you're sharing a story about a project or engagement with our company, be sure to include relevant details that demonstrate our company's capabilities and value.
* Your submission will be reviewed and verified before being published.
* By submitting this form, you grant our company permission to use your customer success story in marketing and sales materials.
