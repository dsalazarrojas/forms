# support_customer_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after a support interaction. The goal of this form is to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and any issues they encountered during the interaction. This feedback will help us to improve our support services and better understand our customers' needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into multiple pages. Complete each page carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For each field, please select or type the information that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Customer Satisfaction
* **Customer Satisfaction (Customer Satisfaction, number, required)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the support you received, on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 2: Rating Score
* **Rating Score (Rating Score, number, required)**: Provide a rating score from 1 to 10, indicating your overall satisfaction with the support interaction.

### Page 3: Feedback
* **Feedback (Feedback, note, required)**: Share any additional comments or details about your experience. This could include what went well, what didn't go well, or anything else you'd like to share with us.

### Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our support services.
