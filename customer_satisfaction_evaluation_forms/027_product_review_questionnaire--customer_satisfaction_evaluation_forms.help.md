# Product Review Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Review Questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback and reviews from customers about a product. This feedback is used to improve the product and services provided by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill out the Customer Survey section with a brief description of your experience with the product.
3. In the Product Info section, provide any relevant information about the product that you used or reviewed.
4. In the Rating Scale section, select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the product.
5. In the Review Text section, provide a detailed review of your experience with the product.
6. In the Email section, enter your email address if you would like to be contacted with any follow-up questions or to receive a response to your review.
7. In the Phone section, enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
8. In the Date section, enter the date you used or reviewed the product.
9. In the Time section, enter the time you used or reviewed the product.
10. In the Additional Notes section, provide any additional comments or observations about your experience with the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Survey** (`customer_survey`, text, optional): A brief description of your experience with the product. This section is a good place to start by describing your overall impression of the product.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the product that you used or reviewed. This could include details about the features, functionality, or any issues you encountered.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): A rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the product. A higher rating indicates a higher level of satisfaction.
* **Review Text** (`review_text`, text, optional): A detailed review of your experience with the product. This section is a good place to provide specific examples or details about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted with any follow-up questions or to receive a response to your review.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you used or reviewed the product.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you used or reviewed the product.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about your experience with the product.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Keep your responses brief and to the point.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about any of the fields, feel free to ask.
* This form is not intended to be used for any commercial or promotional purposes.
