# Customer Service Responsiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on customer service responsiveness from customers, helping us evaluate the quality of our customer service and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Select the rating that best describes your experience with our customer service representative.
2.  Choose the time taken by our representative to respond to your issue.
3.  Rate your satisfaction with our customer service on a scale of 1 to 5.
4.  Provide any reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with our customer service.
5.  Answer any additional questions about your experience with our customer service.
