# public_service_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Public Service Feedback Form** is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with public services. This information is used to identify areas of improvement and enhance the quality of service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions and provide any requested information in the spaces provided.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Service Feedback** (Required): This field is where you can provide your feedback on what we did well. Please provide your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
* **Public Service Feedback** (Required): This field is where you can provide your feedback on what we could improve.
* **Contact Info** (Optional): You can provide your contact information (email address) in this field if you wish.
* **Service Area**: This is a selection field where you can indicate the area(s) of public service you experienced (Area 1, Area 2, Area 3). Please select all areas that apply.
* **Contact Us** (Required): This field is a selection field where you can choose the best way to contact us (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3). Please select the option that best suits your needs.
* **Feedback** (Required): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Service Rating** (Optional): This field is for you to rate your service experience on a scale from 1-10.
* **Public Service Time** (Optional): This field is for you to provide the time of day you received the service (HH:MM format).
* **Public Service Feedback Rating** (Optional): This field is where you can rate the quality of the public service experience (Scale from 1-10).
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): This field is for any additional comments you may have.
* **Public Service Satisfaction** (Optional): This field is for you to rate how satisfied you are with the service (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3). Please select the option that best describes your satisfaction.
