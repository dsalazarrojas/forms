# Support Interaction Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Interaction Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback and evaluations from customers after an interaction with a support team member. This information helps us understand our customers' satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each field according to your experience.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue that was discussed during the support interaction.
3. Choose the channel through which the support interaction took place (e.g., Mobile, Email, Phone, Chat, Video, or Other).
4. Indicate whether the issue was resolved or is still in progress.
5. Select one or more emotions that describe how you felt during the interaction (e.g., Angry, Neutral, Happy).
6. Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall customer satisfaction.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback you have for the support team.
8. Enter your name (if you feel comfortable sharing it).
9. Enter your email address (if you feel comfortable sharing it).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`1`, `number`, optional): This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the support interaction. Please enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the issue that was discussed during the support interaction. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand the problem.
* **Interaction Channel** (`interaction_channel`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the channel through which the support interaction took place (e.g., Mobile, Email, Phone, Chat, Video, or Other).
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the issue was resolved, is still in progress, or not applicable.
* **Customer Feeling** (`customer_feeling`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more emotions that describe how you felt during the interaction (e.g., Angry, Neutral, Happy).
* **Customer Satisfaction Score** (`customer_satisfaction_score`, `number`, optional): Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate your overall customer satisfaction.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have for the support team.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have for the support team.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name if you feel comfortable sharing it.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you feel comfortable sharing it.
