# scanner_usage_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from scanner users, allowing us to improve our product and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form page by page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to complete the form only once for each scanner used.
3. If you have already provided feedback for the same scanner, please do not resubmit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Name (Page 1)
* **Enter user name**: This is the name of the person filling out the form. Enter your name so we can associate your feedback with you.
### Scanner Type (Page 1)
* **Scanner type**: Choose one or more of the available scanner types:
	+ **Flatbed scanner**
	+ **Handheld scanner**
	+ **Standalone scanner**
### Feedback (Page 2)
* **Feedback**: This is a text field where you can provide any feedback or comments about the scanner's functionality, usability, or any issues you've experienced while using it.
### Rating (Page 2)
* **Rating**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with the scanner on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
### Comments (Page 3)
* **Comments**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the scanner.
### Contact (Page 3)
* **Contact**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback.
### Phone (Page 3)
* **Phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
### Assigned To (Page 4)
* **Assigned to**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the scanner, such as your supervisor or team lead.

Please note that the form has 4 pages, and each page should be filled out one at a time. The required fields are marked as such, and you should only fill out the optional fields if you have additional information to provide.
