# Order History Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their order history. It will help us understand their satisfaction, order details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with your purchase** (`customer_satisfaction`):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with your purchase on a scale of Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **What was the order date?** (`order_details`):
	+ Enter the date of the order in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What was the total cost of the order?** (`order_total`):
	+ Enter the total cost of the order as a number.
* **What items were purchased?** (`order_items`):
	+ Provide a brief description of the items purchased.
* **Would you purchase from us again?** (`rating`):
	+ Choose True or False to indicate if you would purchase from us again.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Provide any additional comments about your experience with the order.
* **What category does this order belong to?** (`category`):
	+ Select all categories that apply to the order.
* **On a scale of 1 to 10** (`rating_value`):
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your satisfaction.
* **Why did you rate it that way?** (`rating_comment`):
	+ Provide a brief explanation for your rating.
* **How can we improve?** (`contact_us`):
	+ Provide any suggestions for how we can improve.
* **What is your order number?** (`order_number`):
	+ Enter the order number provided by us.
* **When did you review the order?** (`review_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the review.
* **When did you purchase the order?** (`purchase_date`):
	+ Enter the date of purchase.
* **What was the total cost of the order?** (`total_cost`):
	+ Enter the total cost of the order as a number.
* **How did you purchase the order?** (`purchase_method`):
	+ Select how you purchased the order, such as Online, In Store, or Other.
* **How did you pay for the order?** (`payment_method`):
	+ Select all methods of payment used, such as Cash, Credit Card, PayPal, or Other.
* **What is the status of the order?** (`order_status`):
	+ Select all status of the order, such as Active, Completed, Cancelled, or On Hold.
* **What shipping method did you use?** (`shipping_method`):
	+ Select all shipping methods used, such as Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Pickup.
* **Have you made any returns?** (`return_policy`):
	+ Choose True or False to indicate if you made any returns.
* **If yes, why did you return it?** (`return_reason`):
	+ Provide a brief explanation for why you returned the item.
* **How did you return it?** (`return_method`):
	+ Select all methods of return, such as Mail, In Store, or Other.
* **In which country did you purchase the item?** (`purchase_country`):
	+ Select the country of purchase.
* **In which country was the item shipped?** (`shipping_country`):
	+ Select the country of shipment.
* **In which country did you purchase the item?** (`purchase_date_country`):
	+ Select the country of purchase.
* **In which country was the item shipped?** (`shipping_date_country`):
	+ Select the country of shipment.
