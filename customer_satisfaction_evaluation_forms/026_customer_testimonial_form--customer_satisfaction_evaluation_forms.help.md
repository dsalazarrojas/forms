# Customer Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Testimonial Form is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from customers to improve our business services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose how satisfied you were with our business from the available options.
2. If you are not satisfied, please provide a brief description of your testimonial.
3. Provide your name and email address for contact purposes.
4. Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for us.
5. If you have not recommended our business to a friend or family member, please state the reason why.
6. Suggest areas for improvement.
7. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Testimonial Form** (`customer_testimonial_form`, text, not required): This field is the main section of the form where you can type your testimonial.
* **How satisfied were you?** (`customer_testimonial_form_rating`, select_one, not required): Select how satisfied you were with our business from the available options:
	+ Not at all satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`testimonial`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our business.
* **Photo** (`testimonial_photo`, text, not required): If you'd like to share a photo, please upload it.
* **Have you recommended our business to a friend or family member?** (`recommended_your_business`, select_one, not required): Choose 'True' if you have recommended our business, 'False' otherwise, or 'Unanswered' if you haven't made up your mind yet.
* **Why didn't you recommend our business?** (`why_didnt_recommend`, text, not required): If you haven't recommended our business, please state the reason why.
* **How can we improve?** (`how_did_we_improve`, text, not required): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`testimonial_comments`, text, not required): Add any additional comments or feedback.
