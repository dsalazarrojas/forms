# Aftersales Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with our aftersales services, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments to ensure our services meet and exceed customer expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide us with your feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer name and order/invoice number as references.
2. Select the date of the service received.
3. Choose the type of service you received (e.g., product repair, warranty exchange, technical support).
4. Rate the speed of service resolution (very fast, fast, average, slow, very slow).
5. Rate the technical expertise of the staff who assisted you (highly knowledgeable, competent, needs improvement, poor).
6. Rate the professionalism and attitude of the staff member (excellent, good, fair, poor).
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience (5 - extremely satisfied, 4 - satisfied, 3 - neutral, 2 - dissatisfied, 1 - extremely dissatisfied).
8. Indicate whether your issue was fully resolved (yes, partially resolved, no, still pending).
9. Provide suggestions on how we can improve (optional).
9. Lastly, rate how likely you are to recommend our services to others (definitely, probably, not sure, probably not).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (required):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Invoice or Order Number** (required):
	+ Enter the number of your invoice or order to help us locate your service history.
* **Date of Service** (required):
	+ Select the date you received the service.
* **Type of service received** (required):
	+ Choose the type of service you received (e.g., product repair, warranty exchange, technical support).
* **Speed of service resolution** (required):
	+ Choose how fast your issue was addressed (very fast, fast, average, slow, very slow).
* **Technical expertise of the staff** (required):
	+ Rate the staff member's knowledge and skill in resolving your issue (highly knowledgeable, competent, needs improvement, poor).
* **Professionalism and attitude of the representative** (required):
	+ Choose how professional and friendly you found the staff member to be (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Overall satisfaction** (required):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience (5 - extremely satisfied, 4 - satisfied, 3 - neutral, 2 - dissatisfied, 1 - extremely dissatisfied).
* **Was your issue fully resolved?** (required):
	+ Choose whether your issue was fully resolved or still pending.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (optional):
	+ Share any ideas you have on how we can improve our services.
* **Would you recommend our services to others?** (required):
	+ Choose how likely you are to recommend our services to others (definitely, probably, not sure, probably not).
