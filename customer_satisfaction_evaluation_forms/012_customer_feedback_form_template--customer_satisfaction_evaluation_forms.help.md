# Customer Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer feedback, which is essential for us to understand their satisfaction and experiences with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2. Select the type of feedback you have for us.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide a message with your feedback.
5. Rate your overall experience.
6. Provide any additional comments you have.
7. Select any relevant customer information.
8. (Optional) Attach any supporting documents related to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`page 1`, **required**): Enter your name and contact details accurately, so we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.
* **Feedback Type (Feedback Type)** (`page 2`, **required**): Choose the type of feedback you're providing, such as positive, negative, or suggestions.
* **Email (Email)** (`page 3`, **required**): Enter your official email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`page 4`, **optional**): If you're willing, provide your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Message (Message)** (`page 5`, **required**): Type your feedback in this text box. This will give us an idea of your experience and any suggestions you may have.
* **Rating (Rating)** (`page 6`, **required**): Rate your overall experience with us, using the provided options.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`page 7`, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them here.
* **Customer Information (Customer Information)** (`page 8`, **required**): Select any relevant customer information that applies to your feedback.
* **Attachment (Attachment)** (`page 9`, **optional**): Attach any supporting documents or files related to your feedback.
