# holiday_promotions_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on holiday promotions. It is meant to help us understand what our customers liked and disliked about our holiday offers, and how we can improve them in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and honestly, as your feedback is crucial for us to understand your needs and preferences.
2.  Provide your email address so we can get back to you with updates and improvements.
3.  If you have any suggestions or ideas, use the "what would you like to see improved next year" field to share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, required/optional)
This field is where you can enter your name. Please type it in correctly so we can address you properly in our response.

### 2. What did you like most about holiday promotion (`feedback_question_1`, text, required/optional)
Please write down what you enjoyed most about our holiday promotion. This will help us understand what we did well and what we can build upon.

### 3. What did you dislike most about holiday promotion (`feedback_question_2`, text, required/optional)
This field is where you can express any concerns or dislikes you had about our holiday promotion. Be as specific as possible so we can address your issues effectively.

### 4. What would you like to see improved next year (`what_improved`, text, required/optional)
If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve our holiday promotion next year, please share them with us here.

### 5. Email (`customer_email`, email, required/optional)
Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and improvements. We value your feedback and want to get back to you with results.

## Tips
*   Please answer all fields completely and honestly.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
*   We appreciate your time and feedback, and we will use it to improve our services. 

Note: This guide is designed to provide clear and concise explanations of each field, making it easy for customers to fill out the form and provide their feedback. By following these steps, customers can effectively provide their opinions and help us improve our holiday promotions.
