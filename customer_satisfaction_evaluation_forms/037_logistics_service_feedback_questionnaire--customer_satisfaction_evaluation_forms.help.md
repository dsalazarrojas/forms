# logistics_service_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about the logistics service provided by the company, aiming to measure their satisfaction and provide valuable insights into the company's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Ensure that all mandatory fields are filled out.
3. Provide your feedback in the relevant fields, such as customer_satisfaction, logistics_service, rating, select_multiple, date, time, email, and phone.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to provide them in the note field.
5. Submit the form to complete the feedback process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Satisfaction
* **Customer Satisfaction (customer_satisfaction)**: This field asks for your overall satisfaction with the logistics service. Please rate your satisfaction level from 1 to 100, where 1 is the lowest and 100 is the highest.

### 2. Logistics Service
* **Logistics Service (logistics_service)**: This field is for you to describe your experience with the logistics service. Please provide a brief explanation of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects.

### 3. Rating
* **Rating (rating)**: This field asks for your rating of the logistics service, with a minimum value of 1 and a maximum value of 5. Please select the rating that best reflects your experience.

### 4. Select Multiple
* **Select Multiple (select_multiple)**: This field allows you to choose one or more options that best describe your experience. Please select all the options that apply to you.

### 5. Date
* **Date (date)**: This field asks for the date of the logistics service. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 6. Time
* **Time (time)**: This field asks for the time of the logistics service. Please enter the time in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### 7. Email
* **Email (email)**: This field asks for your email address. Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you if needed.

### 8. Phone
* **Phone (phone)**: This field asks for your phone number. Please enter your phone number.

### 9. Note
* **Note (note)**: This field is for any additional comments or concerns you may have about the logistics service. Please use it to provide any extra information that will help us understand your experience better.
