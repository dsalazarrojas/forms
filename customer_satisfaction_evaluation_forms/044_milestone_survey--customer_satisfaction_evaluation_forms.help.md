# Milestone Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Milestone Survey form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from customers after a milestone event or project completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill in the required fields, as indicated by the "required" field label.
3. For fields with multiple options, choose one or more of the provided options.
4. Fill in any additional comments or notes in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false)
* This field asks for your rating of the customer satisfaction with the milestone event or project completion. Choose one or more of the provided options: Very good, Good, Neutral.

### 2. **Milestone survey date** (`milestone_survey_date`, date, required: false)
* This field is for the date of the milestone event or project completion.

### 3. **Milestone survey time** (`milestone_survey_time`, time, required: false)
* This field is for the time of the milestone event or project completion.

### 4. **Milestone survey note** (`milestone_survey_note`, note, required: false)
* This field is for any additional comments or notes you'd like to provide about the milestone event or project completion.

### 5. **Customer satisfaction evaluation** (`customer_satisfaction_evaluation`, text, required: false)
* This field is for a more detailed evaluation of the customer satisfaction. Please provide a brief written comment.

### 6. **Minimalist survey** (`minimalist_survey`, select_one, required: false)
* This field asks for your rating of the minimalist design of the milestone event or project completion. Choose one of the provided options: Very good, Good, Neutral.

### 7. **Milestone survey phone** (`milestone_survey_phone`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your phone number, in case we need to contact you for further information or follow-up.

### 8. **Milestone survey email** (`milestone_survey_email`, email, required: false)
* This field is for your email address, in case we need to contact you for further information or follow-up.

### 9. **Milestone survey address** (`milestone_survey_address`, text, required: false)
* This field is for your physical address, in case we need to contact you for further information or follow-up.

### 10. **Milestone survey city** (`milestone_survey_city`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the city of the customer's location, in case we need to verify or follow-up on any issues.

### 11. **Milestone survey country** (`milestone_survey_country`, select_one, required: false)
* This field asks for the country where you reside or the location of the milestone event. Choose one of the provided options: Thailand, USA, Australia.

Please fill in all the required fields and any additional comments or notes you'd like to provide. If you have any further questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
