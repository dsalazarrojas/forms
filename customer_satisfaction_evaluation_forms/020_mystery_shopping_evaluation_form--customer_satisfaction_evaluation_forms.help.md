# Mystery_Shopping_Evaluation_Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Mystery_Shopping_Evaluation_Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences at a store, evaluating various aspects of their visit, including service quality, communication, resolution, and overall experience. This form helps identify areas of improvement and provides a basis for customer service evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information such as store details (store name, address, phone number, email, and website).
2. Rate your overall experience and customer service, if applicable.
3. Provide feedback on the date and time of your visit, mystery shopper information, and any comments.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your feedback.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you have any feedback or comments, please provide them in the "Customer_Feedback" field.
* If you are a mystery shopper, please sign and date the form to confirm your identity.
