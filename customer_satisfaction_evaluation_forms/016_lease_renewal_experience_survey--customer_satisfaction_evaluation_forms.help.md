# Lease Renewal Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a property's lease renewal experience, including communication channels, lease length, lease terms, and other relevant details. This form is intended for use by property managers to gather feedback from customers and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and select the relevant options from the dropdown menus or enter your responses in the text fields.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your supervisor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the property manager who handled the lease renewal process.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels that were used to communicate with the customer during the lease renewal process. Possible options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Lease Length** (`lease_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the lease, in months or years.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the terms that were presented to the customer during the lease renewal process. Possible options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Renewal Process** (`renewal_process`, text, optional): Describe the renewal process that was followed.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_one, optional): Rate the customer's experience with the renewal process. Possible options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Lease Renewal Process** (`lease_renewal_process`, date, optional): Record the date of the lease renewal process.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the renewal.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the lease end.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the lease renewal. Possible options are:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the lease renewal experience.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the property manager who handled the lease renewal process, not by the customer directly.
