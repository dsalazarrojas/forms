# Mix2X User Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mix2X User Feedback Survey is designed to collect your thoughts and experiences about our product or service. Your feedback is crucial to help us understand how we can improve and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety, including questions that are not marked as optional.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "feedback" field to provide more information.

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): A brief introduction to your experience with Mix2X.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, number, optional): Please rate your overall experience with Mix2X on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_one, required): Which of the following best describes your experience with our product? (Select one of the following options: option 2, option 3, option 4)
* **Product Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Which of the following best describes your satisfaction with our product? (Select multiple options: option 1, option 2, option 3, option 4)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide more information in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address will help us contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number will help us contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, date, optional): Please provide the date you first used our product or service.
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, time, optional): Please provide the time you first used our product or service.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, number, optional): Please rate your experience with Mix2X on a scale of 1-5.
