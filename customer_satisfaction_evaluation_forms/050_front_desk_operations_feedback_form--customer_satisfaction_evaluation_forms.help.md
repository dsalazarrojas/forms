# Front Desk Operations Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our front desk services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your experience with our front desk services. Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, note, optional): Please provide a short note about your overall satisfaction with our services.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, optional): Please select the quality of service you received:
	+ Good
	+ Bad
	+ Average
* **Staff Attentiveness** (`staff_attentiveness`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the attentiveness of our staff:
	+ Very Attentive
	+ Somewhat Attentive
	+ Not Attentive
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Please select the communication level of our staff:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Bad
	+ Very Bad
* **Check in/Check out** (`check_in_check_out`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the ease of check in and check out:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Somewhat Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the cleanliness of our facilities:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_one, optional): Please select the quality of our facilities:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your overall satisfaction with our services:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Please share any improvement suggestions you have for our front desk services.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience with our front desk services.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.

Note: Please do not enter any unnecessary or sensitive information in the Contact Info and Contact Number fields.
