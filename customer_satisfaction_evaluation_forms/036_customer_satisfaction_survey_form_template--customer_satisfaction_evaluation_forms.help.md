# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Survey Form Template is designed to gather valuable feedback from customers to improve their overall experience with your product or service. This form is intended to be a one-stop-shop for understanding customer satisfaction across different aspects, providing actionable insights to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a rating for overall satisfaction from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
2. Rate your experience with the product or service (number).
3. Share your thoughts on customer satisfaction (text).
4. Choose the level of quality you experienced with the product or service (Very Good, Good, Average).
5. Select the level of customer service you received (Very Good, Good, Average).
6. Provide any additional feedback (note).
7. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
8. Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
9. Leave any additional comments about your experience.
10. Rate your satisfaction with the product or service again (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Satisfaction Survey Form Template** (`select_one`): This is the first question to ask customers about their overall satisfaction. Choose the option that best describes your current experience.
* **Overall Experience** (`number`): Rate your experience with the product or service on a scale. This helps to understand the level of satisfaction with specific aspects.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`text`): Share your thoughts on customer satisfaction. This is an open-ended question where you can express your opinions and feelings about your experience.
* **Product/Service Quality** (`number`): Rate the quality of the product or service. Choose the option that best fits your experience.
* **Customer Service** (`select_multiple`): Indicate the level of customer service you received. Choose as many options that apply.
* **Feedback** (`note`): Provide any comments or suggestions you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (`text`): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Comments** (`text`): Leave any additional comments about your experience.
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating** (`select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the product or service again. This is another opportunity to express your feelings about your experience.
* **Product Satisfaction** (`text`): Share your thoughts on product satisfaction. This is an open-ended question where you can express your opinions and feelings about your experience.
* **Customer Service Rating** (`text`): Rate your satisfaction with the customer service you received. This helps to understand the level of satisfaction with specific aspects.

Note: The fields marked as `required` but not `type: select_one` or `type: text` may be skipped if not applicable.
