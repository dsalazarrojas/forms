# Device Upgrade Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers after a device upgrade. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that future upgrades meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the question "overall_satisfaction" with your level of satisfaction with the device upgrade.
2. Provide a rating of the device upgrade in the "rating" field.
3. If you have any reason for upgrading your device, type it in the "reason_for_upgrade" field.
4. If you have any recommended improvements, type them in the "recommended_improvements" field.
5. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **overall_satisfaction (Very Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Satisfied, Very Satisfied)**: Please choose the option that best reflects your overall satisfaction with the device upgrade.
* **rating (number)**: Rate the device upgrade from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **reason_for_upgrade (text)**: Type a brief description of why you upgraded your device. For example, "I upgraded for new features", "I upgraded for improved performance", etc.
* **recommended_improvements (text)**: Type any suggestions you have for improvements to the device or its upgrade process.
* **comments (text)**: Type any additional comments or thoughts you have about the device upgrade.
