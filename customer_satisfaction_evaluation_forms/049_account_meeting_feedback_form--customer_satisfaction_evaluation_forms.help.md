# Account Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the account meeting, focusing on the account manager's performance, meeting materials, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information:
	* Client Name: Your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
	* Client Company: Name of your company.
2. Meeting details:
	* Meeting Date: Date of the meeting, in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/22/2024).
3. Meeting evaluation:
	* Meeting Topic: Briefly describe the main topic of the meeting.
	* Account Manager Name: Name of the account manager who led the meeting.
4. Select your responses for the following questions:
	* Meeting Format: How was the meeting conducted? (Select one: In-person, Video conference, Phone call, Hybrid).
	* Overall Satisfaction: How satisfied were you with the meeting overall? (Select one: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
	* Meeting Objectives Met: Were the meeting objectives clearly defined and met? (Select one: Completely Met, Mostly Met, Partially Met, Not Met, Unclear Objectives).
	* Agenda Followed: Did the account manager follow the meeting agenda? (Select one: Strictly Followed, Mostly Followed, Somewhat Followed, Did Not Follow, No Agenda Provided).
	* Preparation Quality: How well prepared was the account manager? (Select one: Exceptionally Well Prepared, Well Prepared, Adequately Prepared, Poorly Prepared, Not Prepared).
	* Communication Effectiveness: How effectively did the account manager communicate? (Select one: Very Effective, Effective, Moderately Effective, Minimally Effective, Ineffective).
	* Responsiveness to Questions: How responsive was the account manager to your questions and concerns? (Select one: Very Responsive, Responsive, Moderately Responsive, Slightly Responsive, Not Responsive).
	* Meeting Materials Quality: How would you rate the quality of materials presented during the meeting? (Select one: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, No Materials Provided).
	* Time Management: Did the account manager manage the meeting time effectively? (Select one: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
	* Follow Up Commitments: Did the account manager follow up on commitments made during the meeting? (Select one: Yes, Completely, Yes, Mostly, Partially, No, Not Yet, No Follow Up Promised).
	* Value Added Perception: Did you feel the meeting added value to your business relationship? (Select one: Significant Value, Moderate Value, Little Value, No Value, Negative Value).
	* Likelihood to Recommend: How likely are you to recommend this account manager to others? (Select one: Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions:
	* Improvement Suggestions: Suggest ways to improve future meetings.
	* Positive Aspects: What aspects of the meeting did you find most valuable?
	* Additional Comments: Any other comments about the meeting or account manager.
