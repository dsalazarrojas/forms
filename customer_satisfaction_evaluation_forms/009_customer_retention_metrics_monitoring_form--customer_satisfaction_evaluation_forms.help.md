# Customer Retention Metrics Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Retention Metrics Monitoring Form is a crucial tool for measuring customer satisfaction and retention levels. It is used to gather valuable feedback and insights that help us understand how well we are meeting your needs and expectations. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to ensure you continue to have a positive experience with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the number of months you've been a customer with us in the "How many months do you have been a customer with us?" field.
2. Choose your overall satisfaction level with our services from the "What is your overall satisfaction level with our services?" dropdown menu.
3. Select all that apply from the "What are your customer retention rates?" dropdown menu.
4. Rate your customer satisfaction level using the number input field "What is your customer satisfaction rating?".
5. If you'd like to add any additional feedback, please use the "Can you please provide any additional feedback on customer satisfaction and retention?" text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How many months do you have been a customer with us?** (`customer_retention_period_1`, select_one, required)
  Select the number of months you've been a customer with us to help us understand your duration with our company.
* **What is your overall satisfaction level with our services?** (`customer_satisfaction_level_1`, select_one, required)
  Choose this option to rate your overall satisfaction level with our services, on a scale from 1 to 4.
* **What are your customer retention rates?** (`customer_retention_rates_1`, select_multiple, required)
  Select all that apply to help us understand your customer retention rates, such as "High", "Medium", "Low", or "N/A".
* **What is your customer satisfaction rating?** (`customer_satisfaction_1`, number, required)
  Rate your customer satisfaction level using a number input field, where 1 is the lowest and 4 is the highest.
* **Can you please provide any additional feedback on customer satisfaction and retention?** (`feedback`, note, optional)
  Use this text area to provide any additional feedback or comments about your customer satisfaction and retention experience with us.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible with your feedback to help us better understand your needs.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* Your input will help us make data-driven decisions to continuously improve our services and ensure your positive experience.
