# User Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer feedback on their experience with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide your feedback and opinions in the relevant fields.
3. Select your rating for our service.
4. Optionally, provide additional comments if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required*): Please enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required*): Please enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required*): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our service.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required*): Rate your level of satisfaction with our service, using the scale from Very Good to Very Poor.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required*): Choose how you would rate our service, using the options Very Good, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **Submitted Via** (`submitted_via`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the device or platform you used to submit this form, using the options Web, Mobile, or Other.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required*): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form, if possible.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you were assigned a specific tool or agent during your interaction with our service, please mention it here.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): This field is not used in the form functionality, but is a system-generated field for internal purposes.

Note: The asterisk (*) indicates a required field.
