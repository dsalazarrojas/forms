# salon_client_post_appointment_satisfaction_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data from clients after a service has been provided at the salon. The collected data will be used to evaluate and improve the overall experience and service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, such as Client ID, Date of Service, Time of Service, and Client Signature.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the overall service quality and with the service staff member.
3. Provide feedback and comments about the service and service staff.
4. Indicate whether you would recommend the salon to your friends and family.
5. Sign and date the form to finalize your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client ID (ID: 1, type: number, required: true)**: Please enter your unique client ID, which can be found on your appointment card or receipt.
* **Salutation (ID: 2, type: select_one, required: false)**: Select one of the following options: Mr., Mrs., Dr., or Ms.
* **First Name (ID: 3, type: text, required: true)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (ID: 4, type: text, required: true)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (ID: 5, type: email, required: true)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (ID: 6, type: text, required: true)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Service (ID: 7, type: date, required: true)**: Enter the date you received the service.
* **Time of Service (ID: 8, type: time, required: true)**: Enter the time you received the service.
* **Service Type (ID: 9, type: select_multiple, required: false)**: Select one or more of the following options: Haircut, Color, Style.
* **How would you rate our service quality? (ID: 10, type: select_one, required: true)**: Rate your satisfaction with the overall service quality by selecting one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Please provide feedback for each service (ID: 11, type: text, required: true)**: Provide detailed feedback for each service you received.
* **Overall Satisfaction (ID: 12, type: number, required: true)**: Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Staff (ID: 13, type: select_one, required: true)**: Select the service staff member who provided your service.
* **How satisfied are you with your service staff? (ID: 14, type: select_one, required: true)**: Rate your satisfaction with the service staff member by selecting one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Please provide feedback about your service staff (ID: 15, type: text, required: true)**: Provide feedback about the service staff member.
* **Would you recommend our salon to your friends and family? (ID: 16, type: select_one, required: true)**: Indicate whether you would recommend the salon to your friends and family by selecting one of the following options: Yes, No, Undecided.
* **Why or why not? (ID: 17, type: text, required: false)**: Provide a reason for recommending or not recommending the salon.
* **Additional Feedback (ID: 18, type: text, required: false)**: Provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Comments (ID: 19, type: text, required: false)**: Leave any comments or suggestions.
* **Client Signature (ID: 20, type: note, required: true)**: Sign and date the form to finalize your feedback.
* **Date Signed (ID: 21, type: date, required: false)**: Date and time the form was signed.
* **Time Signed (ID: 22, type: time, required: false)**: Time the form was signed.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
