# Daily Returns Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Returns Tracking Survey form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have gone through the return process. This form is used to assess the efficiency and effectiveness of our return process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out the form for a return that has already been processed.
2. Answer the questions as they relate to your actual return experience.
3. Answer each question in as much detail as possible, so we can get a clear understanding of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Return Experience**: Please describe your experience with returning your item, including any notable events or interactions with our staff.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date on which you returned your item.
* **Product Returned**: Describe the product you returned.
* **Reason for Return**: Explain why you were returning your item.
* **Customer Feedback**: Share any comments, suggestions, or concerns you had during the return process.
* **Return Process**: Describe how you found the return process to be (e.g. easy, difficult, unclear).
* **Communication with Customer**: Rate how clear and responsive our communication was during the return process (e.g. 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent).
* **Resolution of Return**: Describe how your return was resolved (e.g. refund, replacement, store credit).
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the return process (e.g. 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied).
* **Communication with Return Team**: Any additional comments or feedback you have for the return team.

Tips:

* Be honest and detailed in your answers, as your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our return process.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the "Customer Feedback" field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns about the return process, please contact our return team.
