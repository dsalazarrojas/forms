# Extension Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from customers to improve our extension's usability and functionality. Your input will help us understand how you're using the extension and what we can do to make it more convenient for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, fill in your thoughts and ratings in the provided fields.
2. For each field, follow the instructions below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Page (Text)**: This is the main survey question. Please provide your thoughts on how you use our extension. You can type as much or as little as you need to explain your experience.
* **Usage Questions (Text)**: This field is not actively used in this form. You can safely skip it.
* **Feedback (Text)**: Share any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about our extension. We value your input and would love to hear your thoughts!
* **Rating (Select One)**: Choose one of the provided ratings (Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, Very Bad) to give us an understanding of your overall satisfaction with our extension.
* **Email (Email)**: If you'd like to receive updates or be contacted for further questions, please provide your email address. We don't share our customers' contact information with anyone.
* **Phone (Text)**: This field is not actively used in this form. You can safely skip it.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible in your answers.
- If you have any suggestions or complaints, please let us know.
- Thank you for taking the time to help us improve our extension!
