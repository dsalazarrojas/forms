# Number Input And Range Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect customer feedback on their experience with the service, including how many times they've used it, their satisfaction level, price expectation, and other related metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **What is your name** (`form_1`, text, optional): Enter your name to provide a personal touch to your feedback.
2. **How many times have you used our service** (`form_2`, number, optional): Enter a number indicating how many times you've used our service.
3. **What is your satisfaction with our service** (`form_3`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best represents your satisfaction level:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
	* Option 4
4. **What is your price expectation** (`form_4`, number, optional): Enter a number indicating your expected price range for our service.
5. **When did you last use our service** (`form_5`, date, optional): Select a date indicating when you last used our service (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
6. **How much did you use our service** (`form_6`, number, optional): Enter a number indicating how much you used our service (format: %).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name**: A text field where you can provide your name for feedback purposes.
* **How many times have you used our service**: A number field where you can indicate how many times you've used our service.
* **What is your satisfaction with our service**: A select_one field with four options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, and Option 4. Choose the one that best represents your satisfaction level.
* **What is your price expectation**: A number field where you can enter your expected price range for our service.
* **When did you last use our service**: A date field where you can select a date indicating when you last used our service (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **How much did you use our service**: A number field where you can indicate how much you used our service (format: %).

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* You can leave any field blank if it's not applicable to you.
* If you have any feedback or comments, please feel free to include them in the comments section (not shown in this guide).
