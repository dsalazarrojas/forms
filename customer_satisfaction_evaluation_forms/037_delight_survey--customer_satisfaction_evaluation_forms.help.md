# Delight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delight Survey is designed to collect feedback and measure customer satisfaction on various aspects of their experience. It helps identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for business development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. Select the correct answers to the rating and multiple-choice questions.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **how_was_it* (`1`, `number`): This question asks you to rate how satisfied you were with the overall experience on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 6 (very satisfied).
- **customer-service** (`2`, `text`): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our customer service.
- **prophets-satisfaction** (`3`, `select_multiple`): Select all the satisfaction levels that apply to your experience. Options: Satisfaction level 1, Satisfaction level 2, Satisfaction level 3, Satisfaction level 4, Satisfaction level 5, Satisfaction level 6.
- **customer-satisfaction* (`4`, `number`): Similar to "how_was_it", this question also asks for your satisfaction level but on a different scale. Please answer on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 6 (very satisfied).
- **feedback** (`5`, `select_one`): Choose the best description of your feedback. Options: Positive, Negative, Neutral.
- **contact-info** (`6`, `note`): You can include any additional comments or contact information here. Please provide relevant details about your experience and any questions or concerns.
- **email** (`7`, `email`): Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you regarding your feedback. This is optional.
- **phone** (`8`, `text`): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone. This is optional.
- **date-visited** (`9`, `date`): Enter the date and time you visited our establishment. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **time-visited** (`10`, `time`): Enter the time you visited, using the 24-hour clock format.
- **rating** (`11`, `select_multiple`): Select all the services or employees that contributed to your experience, from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
- **comments** (`12`, `text`): You can add any additional comments about your experience here.
- **employee-name** (`13`, `select_one`): If you had a specific employee who contributed to your experience, select their name from the options.
