# Color Street Product Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your honest feedback on your experience with Color Street products. Your input will help us understand how to improve and better serve you and our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, the date of purchase, and the specific product you bought.
2. Provide your overall satisfaction rating and answer questions about the ease of application, wear duration, color accuracy, and product quality.
3. Rate the value for money and indicate your likelihood to repurchase.
4. Share your favorite feature of the product and any areas for improvement.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (1): Enter your name for follow-up purposes (optional).
* **Purchase Date** (2): Provide the date you purchased the product.
* **Product Purchased** (3): Specify the Color Street product you bought.
* **Shade Name** (4): Enter the name of the nail strip shade you applied.
* **-- Satisfaction Ratings --** (5): This section is a note to indicate that the following questions pertain to your satisfaction with the product.
	+ **Overall Satisfaction** (6): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
	+ **Ease of Application Rating** (7): Rate how easy it was to apply the nail strips (Very Easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **-- Product Quality --** (10): This section is a note to indicate that the following questions pertain to the product quality.
	+ **Quality Rating** (11): Rate the overall quality of the product (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
	+ **Value for Money** (12): Rate the value for money (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value).
* **Recommendation and Repurchase** (13, 14):
	+ **Would You Recommend** (13): Indicate if you would recommend Color Street to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
	+ **Likelihood to Repurchase** (14): Rate how likely you are to buy from Color Street again (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Additional Comments and Features** (15, 16, 17):
	+ **Best Feature** (15): Share what you liked most about the product.
	+ **Areas for Improvement** (16): Suggest any areas where we can improve.
	+ **Additional Comments** (17): Provide any other feedback or suggestions you have.

Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! Your input is invaluable to us.
