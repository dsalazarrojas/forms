# delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our delivery service. It will help us understand areas of improvement and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. You can submit your feedback at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the quality of the service you received** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_page`, text, optional): Please describe your experience with our delivery service, including any issues or areas of improvement you've encountered.
* **How would you rate our service** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_rating`, select, optional): Rate your experience with our service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What was your experience with the delivery** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_comments`, text, optional): Share any details about your delivery experience, such as the date, time, and location of the delivery.
* **How can we improve our service** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest ways we can improve our service to better meet your needs.
* **Which category best describes your experience** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_category`, select, optional): Select the category that best represents your experience with our delivery service (courier, retail, or logistics).
* **What is your email** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **What is your phone number** (`delivery_service_satisfaction_feedback_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Please answer all fields that apply to your experience with our delivery service.
- If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to describe them in the "What was your experience with the delivery" field.
- If you have suggestions for improvement, share them in the "How can we improve our service" field.
- If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email and/or phone number.
