# e_commerce_assistance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_assistance_survey form is designed to collect customer feedback on their experience with e-commerce services. This form gathers information on specific aspects of the e-commerce platform, including user input, email, phone number, rating, and other optional fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user input in the designated field.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Phone field.
4. Select your rating from the list of options in the Rating field.
5. If applicable, enter the date and time of your interaction with the e-commerce platform.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field (optional).
7. If you have feedback on the e-commerce platform, enter it in the E-commerce field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your personal experience or feedback about the e-commerce platform.
* **Email** (`email_input`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further assistance.
* **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to allow us to contact you more easily.
* **Rating** (`rating_input`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to rate your overall experience with the e-commerce platform.
* **Date** (`date_input`, date, optional): If you can remember the date of your interaction with the e-commerce platform, enter it.
* **Time** (`time_input`, time, optional): If you can recall the time of your interaction with the e-commerce platform, enter it.
* **Note** (`note_input`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **E-commerce** (`e_commerce_input`, text, optional): If you have any feedback or suggestions on the e-commerce platform, enter it here.
