# Website Delivery Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Website Delivery Feedback Survey Form" is designed to gather feedback on your delivery experience with our website. This form will help us understand how we can improve our delivery process and ensure that it meets your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide useful feedback, please answer each question to the best of your ability. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback" field. If you have any contact information, such as email or phone number, please use the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Experience** (`delivery_experience`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience with our delivery process.
- **Delivery Speed** (`delivery_speed`, number, optional): Rate your delivery speed on a scale of 1-5.
- **Delivery Speed Scale** (`delivery_speed_scale`, select_one, optional): Select a delivery speed scale from 1 (slowest) to 5 (fastest).
- **Punctuality** (`delivery_punctuality`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from 1 (mostly on time), 2 (sometimes late), 3 (mostly late).
- **Quality** (`delivery_quality`, text, optional): Describe the quality of our delivery process.
- **Cost** (`delivery_cost`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with our delivery cost.
- **Customer Support** (`customer_support`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with our customer support, from Good to Poor.
- **Communication with Staff** (`communication_with_staff`, text, optional): Describe how well we communicated with you about your delivery.
- **Time** (`delivery_time`, date, optional): Enter the date of your delivery.
- **Date** (`delivery_date`, time, optional): Enter the time of your delivery.
- **Order ID** (`order_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of possible Order IDs.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your delivery experience.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
- **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about our delivery process.
