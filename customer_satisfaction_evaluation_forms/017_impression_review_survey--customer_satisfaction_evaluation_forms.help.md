# Impression Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on your impression of our services or products. Your responses will help us understand your thoughts and feelings, allowing us to make improvements and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
2. If a question doesn't apply to your experience, simply skip it.
3. Take the time to add any additional comments or concerns that you'd like to share with us.
4. Review each section carefully and make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Impression** (`first_page`, `note`, required): This question is an open-ended field where you can share your initial impression of our services or products.
* **Product/Service/Event** (`product_service_event`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of product or service you interacted with (product, service, event, or onboarding).
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall impression of our services or products on a scale of 1-5.
* **Positive Impressions** (`positive_impressions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the list of positive adjectives that describe your impression (friendly, helpful, impressive, well-designed, well-managed, well-executed, well-communicated, well-informed, efficient, effective, satisfying, positive).
* **Negative Impressions** (`negative_impressions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the list of negative adjectives that describe your impression (unhelpful, unimpressive, poorly-designed, poorly-managed, poorly-executed, poorly-communicated, confusing, uninformed, inefficient, unproductive, negative).
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, `note`, required): This question is an open-ended field where you can share your priorities for improvement, if any.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Share any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the list of contact details you'd like to share with us (phone, email, address, contact).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Share the name and title of the contact person you interacted with.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Share the name of the company you interacted with.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Share the department name of the contact person you interacted with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Share the job title of the contact person you interacted with.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Share the name of the company you represent.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, required: false): Share the phone number of the company you represent.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, `text`, required: false): Share the email address of the company you represent.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, required: false): Share the website of the company you represent.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required: false): Share the address of the company you represent.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `note`, required): Share any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with us.
* **First Impression 2** (`first_impression_2`, `note`, required): This question is an open-ended field where you can share your second impression of our services or products.
* **Overall Impression 2** (`overall_impression_2`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall impression of our services or products on a scale of 1-5.
* **Positive Impressions 2** (`positive_impressions_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the list of positive adjectives that describe your impression (friendly, helpful, impressive, well-designed, well-managed, well-executed, well-communicated, well-informed, efficient, effective, satisfying, positive).
* **Negative Impressions 2** (`negative_impressions_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply from the list of negative adjectives that describe your impression (unhelpful, unimpressive, poorly-designed, poorly-managed, poorly-executed, poorly-communicated, confusing, uninformed, inefficient, unproductive, negative).
* **Improvement Priorities 2** (`improvement_priorities_2`, `note`, required): This question is an open-ended field where you can share your priorities for improvement, if any.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `note`, required): Share any additional comments or concerns you'd like to share with us.

Note that some fields are not required, but it's essential to answer all questions to provide valuable feedback. You can always add any additional comments or concerns in the comments field.
