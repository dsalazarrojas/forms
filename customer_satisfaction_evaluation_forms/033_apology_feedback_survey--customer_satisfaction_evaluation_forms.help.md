# Apology Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our apology process. We want to hear about your experience and use this information to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Answer each question with the option that best describes your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the fields marked as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date**: Please fill in the date you completed this survey.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Enter your name.
* **Customer ID or Account Number** (`customer_id`): If applicable, enter your customer ID or account number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Incident** (`issue_date`): Enter the date of the incident that prompted the apology.
* **Type of Issue Experienced** (`issue_type`): Select the type of issue you experienced:
	+ Product quality
	+ Service failure
	+ Communication issue
	+ Billing or payment problem
	+ Delivery delay
	+ Customer service mistake
	+ Other
* **Severity of Issue** (`issue_severity`): Select the severity of the issue:
	+ Minor inconvenience
	+ Moderate impact
	+ Significant impact
	+ Severe impact
* **Did You Receive an Apology?** (`apology_received`): Select how you received the apology:
	+ Yes, in person
	+ Yes, by phone
	+ Yes, by email
	+ Yes, other method
	+ No, did not receive apology
* **How Timely Was the Apology?** (`apology_timeliness`): Select how timely the apology was:
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 24 hours
	+ Within 3-5 days
	+ Within a week
	+ Delayed
* **How Sincere Did the Apology Feel?** (`apology_sincerity`): Select how sincere the apology felt:
	+ Very sincere
	+ Sincere
	+ Somewhat sincere
	+ Not sincere
	+ No apology given
* **Was the Apology Adequate?** (`apology_adequacy`): Select how adequate the apology was:
	+ More than adequate
	+ Adequate
	+ Barely adequate
	+ Inadequate
	+ No apology given
* **Was a Resolution or Compensation Offered?** (`resolution_offered`): Select if a resolution or compensation was offered:
	+ Yes, fair solution
	+ Yes, generous solution
	+ Yes, but insufficient
	+ No resolution offered
* **Did You Accept the Resolution?** (`resolution_accepted`): Select if you accepted the resolution:
	+ Yes, satisfied
	+ Yes, but reluctantly
	+ No, did not accept
	+ N/A - no resolution offered
* **What Action Was Taken to Prevent Recurrence?** (`action_taken`): If applicable, enter any actions taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.
* **Has This Apology Restored Your Trust?** (`trust_restoration`): Select how much trust has been restored:
	+ Yes, fully restored
	+ Partially restored
	+ Somewhat restored
	+ Not restored
* **Will You Continue Doing Business with Us?** (`future_business`): Select your likelihood to continue doing business with us:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably not
	+ False
* **Likelihood to Recommend Us to Others?** (`likelihood_recommend`): Select your likelihood to recommend us to others:
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **Feedback on Our Communication?** (`communication_feedback`): If applicable, enter any feedback on our communication.
* **Suggestions for Improvement?** (`improvement_suggestions`): If applicable, enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Resolution?** (`overall_satisfaction`): Select your overall satisfaction with the resolution:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
