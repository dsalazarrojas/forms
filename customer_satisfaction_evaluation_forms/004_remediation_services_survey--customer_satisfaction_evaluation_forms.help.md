# Remediation Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remediation Services Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from customers who have received remediation services from the company. The goal of this form is to understand the customer's experience and satisfaction with the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating questions to indicate your level of satisfaction with the services provided.
2. Select the services you received during your experience.
3. Enter the date and time of the service provided (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
5. Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive follow-up correspondence from us).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Rating
* **How Would You Rate Our Services** (`service_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services provided on a scale of 1 to 5.

### Satisfaction Level
* **How Satisfied With Your Experience** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to indicate your level of satisfaction:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

### Services Received
* **What Services Were Provided** (`service_provided`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services you received during your experience:
	+ Remediation
	+ Consulting
	+ Support

### Services Requested
* **What Services Did You Request** (`service_request`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services you requested:
	+ Remediation
	+ Consulting
	+ Support

### Date of Service
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Enter the date you received the service (if applicable).

### Time of Service
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): Enter the time you received the service (if applicable).

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up correspondence from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up calls from us.

Note: Please ensure you have completed the required fields before submitting the form.
