# Hot And Cold Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hot And Cold Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your overall feelings and suggestions regarding our services, products, or interactions. This form collects both immediate and reflective feedback to help us better understand your experiences and make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. For fields with score questions (e.g., "Immediate Feelings Score" and "Reflective Feelings Score"), select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Strongly Disagree" and 5 is "Strongly Agree".
3. In text fields, provide a brief description of your thoughts or experiences.
4. Choose a category that best describes the feedback you're providing (e.g., Customer Service, Product, or Other).
5. For the "Follow Up Question" section, select how often you'd like us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Immediate Feelings**: Describe your current feelings or impressions about our services, products, or interactions.
* **Immediate Feelings Score**: Indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with your current feelings.
* **Immediate Feelings Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your immediate feelings.
* **Reflective Feelings**: Share your reflections or thoughts about our services, products, or interactions over time.
* **Reflective Feelings Score**: Indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with your reflective feelings.
* **Reflective Feelings Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your reflective feelings.
* **Immediate Suggestions**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have for improvement or next steps.
* **Immediate Suggestions Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your immediate suggestions.
* **Cold Suggestions**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have for long-term improvements or next steps.
* **Cold Suggestions Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your cold suggestions.
* **Category**: Choose a category that best describes the feedback you're providing (e.g., Customer Service, Product, or Other).
* **Category Comments**: Provide additional information or context about the category you've chosen.
* **Follow Up Question**: Indicate how often you'd like us to follow up with you.
* **Follow Up Question Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your follow-up preferences.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to help us contact you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Email Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your contact information.
* **Phone Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your contact information.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any further thoughts or concerns.
* **Category Other**: Enter any additional category not listed above.
* **Category Other Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your chosen category.
* **Follow Up Question Other**: Indicate how often you'd like us to follow up with you if "Other" category is chosen.
* **Follow Up Question Other Comments**: Provide additional information or context about your follow-up preferences.
