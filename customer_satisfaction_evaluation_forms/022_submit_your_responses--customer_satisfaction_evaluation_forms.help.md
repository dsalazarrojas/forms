# Submit Your Responses - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer satisfaction evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our product or service. This form will help us understand your satisfaction level and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your level of satisfaction with our product or service from the "Customer Satisfaction Page" dropdown menu.
2. Indicate whether you would like to contact us for further assistance or questions by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Contact Us" field.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Provide your contact number, email address, or website (if applicable) in the "Contact Number", "Email Address", and "Website" fields.
5. Indicate which social media platforms you are present on (if any) in the "Social Media Platforms" field.
6. Select the date and time of your experience in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Finally, confirm whether you would like to submit your responses by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Satisfaction Page** (`Customer Satisfaction 3`, `Select one`, required/false): This field asks for your overall satisfaction level with our product or service. Please select "Satisfied" if you are generally pleased with your experience, or "Dissatisfied" if you have some concerns.
* **Contact Us** (`Contact Us`, `Select one`, required/false): If you would like to contact us for further assistance or questions, please select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `Text`, required/false): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them here.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `Number`, required/false): Please provide your contact number if you would like us to follow up with you.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `Email`, required/false): If you have an email address where we can reach you, please provide it.
* **Website** (`Website`, `Text`, required/false): If you have a website where we can find more information about you or your business, please provide it.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`Social Media Platforms`, `Select multiple`, required/false): Please indicate which social media platforms you are present on, such as Twitter, Facebook, or LinkedIn.
* **Date** (`Date`, `Date`, required/false): Please select the date of your experience with us.
* **Time** (`Time`, `Time`, required/false): Please select the time of your experience with us.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `Select one`, required/false): Finally, please confirm whether you would like to submit your responses.
