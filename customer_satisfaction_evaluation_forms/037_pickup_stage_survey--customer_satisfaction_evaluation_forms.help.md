# Pickup Stage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pickup Stage Survey" form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences during the pickup stage of a delivery or service process. This feedback will help our team understand how we can improve our services to provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct pickup location from the options provided.
2. Choose whether the time taken was reasonable or not.
3. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 0-10.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
5. Enter your name (optional).
6. Enter your email address (optional).
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
