# Luxury Customer Experience Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and assess the luxury customer experience, helping us identify areas of improvement and refinement. It's essential to provide honest and constructive insights from our customers to enhance their experience with us.
## How To Complete This Form
Please carefully review each section and fill out the form step-by-step:
1. Select all the options that best describe your experience with our services.
2. Enter your actual address or location (e.g., city, state, or country) to help us understand your context.
3. Rate your experience with our services on a scale if prompted.
4. Share any feedback or comments about your experience with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Standards** (`service_standards`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  + Select all the options that best describe your experience with our services.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: true)
  + Enter your actual address or location (e.g., city, state, or country).
* **Service Scores** (`service_scores`, `text`, required: false)
  + Rate your experience with our services on a scale if prompted.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false)
  + Share any feedback or comments about your experience with us.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed in your responses to help us understand your experience.
- Use your best judgment when selecting options or providing feedback.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask or skip it if not applicable.
- Your input is invaluable, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve.
