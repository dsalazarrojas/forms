# Menu Sequence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from customers about their experience with a menu sequence, likely in a restaurant or food establishment. The form collects feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions on each page, starting from page 1.
2. For each question, select the relevant answer from the options provided.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the text field labeled "comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **menu_sequence_survey** (Note): This field is for general feedback and comments.
* **preferred_sequence** (Select Multiple, Yes/No): Please select "Yes" if this is your preferred menu sequence, and "No" otherwise.
* **menu_item** (Select One, Multiple Options): Select one of the provided menu items that you prefer.
* **rating** (Missing options): Unfortunately, this field is missing options. Please provide your feedback to us, and we will do our best to provide a clear rating scale.
* **comments** (Note): Please enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **customer_satisfaction** (Number): This field is intended for numerical feedback, but the purpose is unclear. Please provide feedback, and we will do our best to understand your concerns.
* **customer_satisfaction_rating** (Select One, Satisfied/Dissatisfied): Please select whether you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your experience.
* **customer_comments** (Note): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
