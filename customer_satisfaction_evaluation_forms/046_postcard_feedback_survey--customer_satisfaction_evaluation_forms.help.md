# Postcard Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers after they have received a postcard or a similar communication from our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Customer Name (if you'd like to provide it).
2. Share your thoughts and opinions in the Feedback field.
3. If you have a personal email address, provide it so we can contact you about your feedback (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name if you'd like to provide it. This will help us know who you are and make it easier for us to contact you if needed.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about our postcard or communication. Be as honest as you can, and let us know what we did well or could improve on.
* **Customer Email**: If you'd like to hear from us about your feedback, provide your email address (optional, but helpful for us to reach out with a response).
  Tips:
  * You can skip this step if you're not comfortable sharing your email address.
  * If you do, ensure it's a personal email address we can use to contact you.
