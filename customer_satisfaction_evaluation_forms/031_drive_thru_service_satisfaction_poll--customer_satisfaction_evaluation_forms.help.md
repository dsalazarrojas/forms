# DriveThruServiceSatisfactionPoll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience at a DriveThru service. It aims to evaluate the speed, accuracy, and overall satisfaction of the service. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select one of the options for each question to rate your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Any comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome**: This section is just an introduction to the form.
- **How would you rate our service speed?**: This question asks you to rate how fast our service was. Please select one of the options:
  - Very fast
  - Fast
  - Average
  - Slow
  - Very slow
- **How accurate were our orders?**: This question asks you to rate how accurate our orders were. Please select one of the options:
  - Very accurate
  - Mostly accurate
  - Somewhat accurate
  - Not very accurate
  - Not at all accurate
- **How would you rate our staff's service?**: This question asks you to rate how well our staff served you. Please select one of the options:
  - Very good
  - Good
  - Average
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Cleanliness of the store**: This question asks you to rate how clean the store was. Please select one of the options:
  - Very clean
  - Mostly clean
  - Somewhat clean
  - Not very clean
  - Not at all clean
- **How would you rate your satisfaction with your order?**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your order. Please select one of the options:
  - Very satisfied
  - Satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied
- **Any comments?**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in here.
- **Phone**: Please enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for follow-up).
- **Email**: Please enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up).
- **Employee name**: If you know the name of the staff member who served you, please enter it here.
