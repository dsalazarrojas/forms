# Client Retention Strategy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Retention Strategy Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a client's retention strategy across several key areas, helping businesses identify areas for improvement and implement targeted measures to enhance client loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start with the first page and follow the instructions for each section.
2.  Enter the assessment date to record the date you are completing the form.
3.  Enter the client account name as per the company's official name.
4.  Provide the name of the account manager responsible for the client.
5.  Answer each question truthfully based on your current client relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date**: <label> (1, date, required)
    Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Client Account Name**: <label> (2, text, required)
    Enter the official name of the client account.
* **Account Manager Name**: <label> (3, text, required)
    Enter the name of the account manager handling the client.
* **Is There a Defined Retention Strategy?**: <label> (4, select_one, required)
    Check if there's a comprehensive, partial, or no formal strategy in place.
* **Level of Proactive Client Engagement**: <label> (5, select_one, required)
    Indicate how proactively you engage the client.
* **Communication Frequency Target**: <label> (6, select_one, optional)
    If applicable, enter the desired communication frequency.
* **Loyalty or Incentive Program**: <label> (7, select_one, optional)
    Check if there's an active, planned, or no program.
* **Level of Service Personalization**: <label> (8, select_one, required)
    Indicate how customized the service is for this client.
* **Are Customer Success Metrics Tracked?**: <label> (9, select_one, required)
    Confirm if comprehensive, some, or no metrics are tracked.
* **Value Realization Demonstration**: <label> (10, select_one, optional)
    Check if value realization is consistently, occasionally, rarely, or not demonstrated to the client.
* **Churn Risk Level**: <label> (11, select_one, required)
    Assess the level of risk for client churn.
* **Expansion Opportunities**: <label> (12, select_one, optional)
    If applicable, enter multiple, some, limited, or no opportunities.
* **Barriers to Expansion or Retention**: <label> (13, text, optional)
    Provide any barriers or reasons preventing client growth.
* **Retention Action Plan**: <label> (14, text, required)
    Describe specific steps to strengthen client retention.
* **Internal Stakeholder Alignment**: <label> (15, select_one, optional)
    Check if stakeholders are strongly, somewhat, or not aligned on the retention strategy.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers to get a true assessment.
* Use the form as a starting point for identifying areas of improvement.
* Consider sharing the results with your team to align on a retention strategy.
* Use the assessment as a guide to create a comprehensive client retention plan.
