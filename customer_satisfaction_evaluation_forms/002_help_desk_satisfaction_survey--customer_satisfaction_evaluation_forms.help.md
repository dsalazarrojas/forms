# help_desk_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on their experience with our help desk service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of each question.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all applicable fields.
3. Provide detailed answers to each question, as they will help us understand your experience better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Support** (`Customer Support`, Text, Optional): Please describe the level of support provided by our team.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, Text, Optional): Describe any issues or challenges you faced with communication from our team.
* **Resolution** (`Resolution`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if the issue was resolved to your satisfaction. You can select 'Yes' if the issue was resolved, or 'No' if it was not.
* **Response Time** (`Response Time`, Time, Optional): Estimate the time it took for our team to respond to your issue.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`Staff Helpfulness`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if our staff were helpful in resolving your issue. You can select 'Yes' if they were helpful, or 'No' if they were not.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, Note, Optional): Write a brief note about your overall satisfaction with the service provided.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, Text, Optional): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you have about our service.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any comments or additional information that may help us improve our service.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing answers to each question.
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please share them in the "Feedback" and "Comments" fields.
* If you have already submitted feedback on the same issue, your new feedback will not be recorded.
* If you need help with filling out this form, feel free to contact our support team.
