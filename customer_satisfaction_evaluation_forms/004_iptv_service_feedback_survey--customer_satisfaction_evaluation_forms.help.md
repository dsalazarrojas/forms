# iptv_service_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your honest feedback about your experience with our IPTV service. Your input will help us improve and refine our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly based on your actual experience with our IPTV service.
2. For each question, choose the most accurate option from the available choices.
3. Provide detailed answers for open-ended questions such as "Improvement Ideas" and "Additional Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **IPTV Service Feedback Survey** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form.
* **IPTV Provider** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have an IPTV provider. This question is not required, but it helps us understand who provides your IPTV service.
* **Channel** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you use a channel, "No" otherwise.
* **Channel Quality** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of our channels on a scale of 1-5. This will help us understand how our channel offerings meet your expectations.
* **Video Content** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate what type of video content you are interested in or currently watching. This can include sports, movies, TV shows, documentaries, etc.
* **Service Availability** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you experience any issues with our service availability, "No" otherwise.
* **Overall Experience** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our IPTV service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`8`, `text`, required): Share your ideas on how we can improve our service. This is an important question, as your input will help us make informed decisions for future development.
* **Would you recommend our IPTV service to a friend or family member?** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you would recommend our service to someone else.
* **Additional Comments** (`10`, `text`, required): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about our IPTV service.
* **Assigned Tool** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is not relevant for most customers. Please do not answer it.
* **Submitted By** (`12`, `text`, required: false): This question is not relevant for most customers. Please do not answer it.
* **Submitted On** (`13`, `date`, required: false): This question is not relevant for most customers. Please do not answer it.
