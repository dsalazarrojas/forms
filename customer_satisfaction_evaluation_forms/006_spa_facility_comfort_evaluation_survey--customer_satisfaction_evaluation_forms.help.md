# Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the comfort level of our spa facility. We are interested in understanding your experience and opinions about our facility's conditions. Please answer the questions honestly, and your feedback will help us to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question as accurately as possible based on your experience during your visit.
2. If a question is not applicable to your visit, please indicate that you did not experience it.
3. Fill in all required fields.
4. You can save your progress and return to the form later using the 'Save and Continue Later' button.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Introduction** (`1`, note, required: false): 
This is a brief introduction to explain what the survey is about and what you can expect. Please read it carefully before starting the survey.

**Spa Facility Condition** (`2`, text, required: true): 
Please describe the overall condition of our spa facility during your visit. For example, was it clean, well-maintained, and comfortable?

**Temperature Control** (`3`, select_one, required: false): 
Do you have the ability to control the temperature in the areas you visited? (Yes/No)

**Ambiance** (`4`, select_multiple, required: false): 
Which areas of our spa facility's ambiance did you find most comfortable? (Please select all that apply):
Yes/No, for: relaxation area, treatment areas, reception area, etc.

**Humidity Level** (`5`, number, required: false): 
What was the level of humidity in the spa facility during your visit? On a scale from 1 (very low) to 10 (very high).

**Lighting** (`6`, number, required: false): 
How would you rate the lighting in the spa facility during your visit? On a scale from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).

**Noise Level** (`7`, number, required: false): 
How would you rate the noise level in the spa facility during your visit? On a scale from 1 (very high) to 10 (very low).

**Clarity of Water** (`8`, number, required: false): 
How clear was the water in the treatment areas? On a scale from 1 (very cloudy) to 10 (very clear).

**Air Quality** (`9`, number, required: false): 
How would you rate the air quality in the spa facility during your visit? On a scale from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).

Note: Please answer these questions based on your overall experience during your visit, not just the specific treatment or service you received. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our services.
