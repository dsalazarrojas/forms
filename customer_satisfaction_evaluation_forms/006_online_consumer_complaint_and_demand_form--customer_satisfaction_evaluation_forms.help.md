# online-consumer-complaint-and-demand-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback, complaints, and demands from customers, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide a clear and concise description of your complaint or demand in the "Customer Complaint and Demand" field.
2. Select the services you require from the "What Services Do You Need" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the type of complaint you're experiencing from the "What Type Of Complaint" dropdown menu. You can select multiple types if applicable.
4. In the "Complaint Detail" field, provide a detailed description of your complaint or demand.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for us to get in touch with you regarding your issue.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, so we can contact you for further clarification or to provide updates on your issue.
7. Select the date of the incident or issue in the "Date" field.
8. Choose the time of the incident or issue in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Complaint and Demand (Customer Complaint and Demand, text, required):** Provide a clear and concise description of your complaint or demand.
* **What Services Do You Need (What Services Do You Need, select_one, optional):** Select the services you require from our company to resolve your issue.
* **What Type Of Complaint (What Type Of Complaint, select_multiple, optional):** Choose the type of complaint you're experiencing. You can select multiple types if applicable.
* **Complaint Detail (Complaint Detail, text, optional):** Provide a detailed description of your complaint or demand.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, optional):** Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you regarding your issue.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional):** Enter your phone number for us to contact you for further clarification or to provide updates.
* **Date (Date, date, optional):** Select the date of the incident or issue.
* **Time (Time, time, optional):** Choose the time of the incident or issue.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your complaint or demand is clear and concise.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue.
* If you need to provide further information or clarification, don't hesitate to contact our support team.
* We'll do our best to resolve your issue as soon as possible.
