# SBI Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers regarding their visit to our branch. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers in the future.

<thinking>
Please note that this form is intended to capture your honest feedback. Your responses will be kept confidential and used to improve our branch operations. If you have visited our branch recently, we encourage you to participate and provide your feedback.

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Please respond to each question based on your recent visit to our branch.
2. For questions with multiple possible responses (e.g., Overall Satisfaction), choose one answer that best describes your experience.
3. If a question does not apply to your visit, please write "Not Applicable" or "N/A" in the text field.
4. Make sure to enter your contact information (Email and Contact Number) if you would like to be contacted by us for follow-up questions or to provide additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What was the purpose of your visit?**
*   This section is a brief description of the reason for your visit.

### 2. **Branch Name**
*   This is the name of the branch you visited.

### 3. **Branch Location**
*   This is the location of the branch you visited.

### 4. **Visit Date**
*   Please enter the date of your visit to our branch.

### 5. **Visit Time**
*   This is the time of your visit to our branch.

### 6. **How was the staff interaction?**
*   Describe your experience with the staff, including any positive or negative interactions.

### 7. **How attentive were the staff?**
*   Rate the attentiveness of the staff during your visit.

### 8. **Facilities and Ambiance**
*   Describe the facilities and ambiance of our branch, including any positive or negative aspects.

### 9. **Services**
*   Describe the services you received during your visit, including any positive or negative aspects.

### 10. **Overall Satisfaction**
*   Choose one of the options that best describes your overall satisfaction with your visit.

### 11. **Do you have any feedback?**
*   Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for us.

### 12. **Email**
*   If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your email address.

### 13. **Phone**
*   If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your contact number.

### 14. **Employee Name**
*   If you remember the name of the staff member you interacted with, please enter it here.

### 15. **Department**
*   If you know the department you visited, please enter it here.

### 16. **Shift**
*   If you know the shift your visited, please enter it here.

### 17. **Contact Number**
*   If you would like to be contacted by us, please enter your contact number.
