# end_user_satisfaction_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end-users, evaluating their satisfaction with a service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Choose your rating for the overall experience.
2. Describe your satisfaction with the service provided (Service Quality).
3. Share your thoughts on the customer service you received.
4. Provide your feedback on the communication aspect of the service.
5. Share your experience with the resolution process.
6. Evaluate the value for money aspect of the service.
7. Choose whether you would recommend this service to others (Recommend).
8. If you have any additional comments, please enter them below (Comments).
9. Enter your email address for further contact (Email).
10. Provide your contact number for follow-up (Contact Number).
11. Select any additional feedback you'd like to provide (Feedback).
12. Provide a numerical rating for the service (Rating).
13. Enter the date you completed this form (Date).
14. Select your preferred time of interaction (Time).
15. Choose the person assigned to you for this service (Assigned).
16. Enter the time you started working on this form (Submitted).
17. If applicable, enter the end date of the service (End Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall** (1, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Quality** (2, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the service provided.
* **Customer Service** (3, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on the customer service you received.
* **Communication** (4, text, required: false): Provide your feedback on the communication aspect of the service.
* **Resolution** (5, text, required: false): Share your experience with the resolution process.
* **Value For Money** (6, text, required: false): Evaluate the value for money aspect of the service.
* **Recommend** (7, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you would recommend this service to others.
* **Comments** (8, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments, please enter them below.
* **Email** (9, email, required: false): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **Contact Number** (10, text, required: false): Provide your contact number for follow-up.
* **Feedback** (11, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional feedback you'd like to provide.
* **Rating** (12, number, required: false): Provide a numerical rating for the service.
* **Date** (13, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (14, time, required: false): Select your preferred time of interaction.
* **Assigned** (15, select_one, required: true): Choose the person assigned to you for this service.
* **Submitted** (16, time, required: false): Enter the time you started working on this form.
* **End Date** (17, time, required: false): If applicable, enter the end date of the service.
