# product_review_submission_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on products you have purchased or used. Your input will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields according to your preference.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Product Details" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
4. Rate the product from 1 to 5 in the "Product Rating" field.
5. Optionally, provide additional feedback in the "Feedback Text" field.
6. Enter a valid email address in the "E-mail" field (this will help us contact you).
7. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Click "Submit" when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product you are reviewing, including its name, version, or any other relevant details.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional): Rate the product's performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the product's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, select_one, required): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to send you updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to call you.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional, not actually a form input but just a label!): Click to submit your feedback.
