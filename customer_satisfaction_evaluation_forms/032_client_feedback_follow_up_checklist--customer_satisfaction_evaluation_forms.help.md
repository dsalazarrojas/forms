**Client Feedback Follow Up Checklist - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients after an interaction or issue resolution, helping you assess their satisfaction with the resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with a star (*), with accurate and specific information about your interaction or issue resolution.
2. For optional fields, provide any additional information you feel is relevant or necessary.
3. Select the appropriate options for each field, taking into account your honest assessment of the interaction or issue resolution.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it, ensuring all required fields are completed and all answers are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Enter your full legal first name.
- **Last Name** (2): Enter your full legal last name.
- **Email Address** (3): Enter your primary contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone. This field is not required for completion.
- **Date of Interaction** (5): Enter the date when the interaction or issue resolution occurred.
- **Type of Interaction** (6): Select the type of contact you had with us, such as a phone call, email exchange, in-person meeting, or video conference.
- **Staff Member Who Assisted** (7): If applicable, enter the staff member who assisted you with your issue or interaction.
- **Was Your Issue Resolved?** (8): Select whether your issue was completely, partially, or not at all resolved.
- **Satisfaction With Resolution** (9): Rate your satisfaction with the resolution, using the scale provided.
- **Response Time Rating** (10): Rate your experience with our response time, using the provided scale.
- **Staff Professionalism Rating** (11): Rate the professionalism displayed by the staff member assisting you, using the scale provided.
- **Staff Knowledge Rating** (12): Rate the staff member's knowledge about your issue, using the scale provided.
- **Communication Clarity Rating** (13): Rate the clearness of communication from the staff member, using the provided scale.
- **Is Follow-Up Needed?** (14): Mark 'True' if additional action is required or 'False' if not.
- **Type of Follow-Up Needed** (15): If applicable, enter the type of follow-up needed.
- **Expected Follow-Up Timeline** (16): Select when you would like us to follow up, if applicable.
- **Additional Feedback** (17): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your interaction or issue resolution.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (18): Enter any suggestions on how we can improve our services or interactions.
- **Overall Experience Rating** (19): Rate your overall experience with our services, using the provided scale.
- **How Likely Are You to Contact Us Again?** (20): Rate your likelihood of contacting us again in the future, using the provided scale.
- **Checklist Completion Date** (21): Enter the date you completed this form.
