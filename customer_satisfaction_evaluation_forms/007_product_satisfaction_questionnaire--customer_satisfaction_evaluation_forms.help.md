# Product Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer satisfaction feedback on a product, its performance, and overall satisfaction experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Product Satisfaction Questionnaire (Page 1)**:
	* This is the first page of the questionnaire. You will see the following questions:
		- **Product category**: Please choose one or more categories that best describe your product.
		- **Product rating**: Please rate the product using a scale from 1 to 5.
		- **Product satisfaction rating**: Choose a rating for product satisfaction from 1 to 4.
		- **Product satisfaction level**: Choose a level of satisfaction from 1 to 4.
		- **Product satisfaction description**: Please provide a brief description of your satisfaction experience with the product.
	* Fill out the form with your answers, and proceed to the next page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product category**: Please select one or more categories that best describe your product. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
	+ Options: Computer, Phone, Other
* **Product rating**: Please rate the product using a scale from 1 to 5. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the product.
	+ A higher rating indicates higher satisfaction.
* **Product satisfaction rating**: Choose a rating for product satisfaction from 1 to 4. This will help us understand your level of satisfaction with the product.
	+ 1 - Poor, 2 - Fair, 3 - Good, 4 - Excellent
* **Product satisfaction level**: Choose a level of satisfaction from 1 to 4. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the product.
	+ 1 - Unsatisfied, 2 - Neutral, 3 - Satisfied, 4 - Very Satisfied
* **Product satisfaction description**: Please provide a brief description of your satisfaction experience with the product.
* **Product name**: Please enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product description**: Please enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product weight**: Please enter the weight of the product.
* **Product width**: Please enter the width of the product.
* **Product height**: Please enter the height of the product.
* **Product length**: Please enter the length of the product.
* **Product color**: Please enter the color of the product.
* **Product material**: Please enter the material of the product.
* **Contact method**: Please choose your preferred contact method:
	+ Email, Phone, Other
* **Contact number**: Please enter your contact number.
* **Contact email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Comments**: Please enter any additional comments about your experience with the product.
* **Satisfaction level**: Please enter your overall satisfaction level with the product.
* **Date**: Please enter the date of your experience with the product.
* **Time**: Please enter the time of your experience with the product.
* **Note**: Please provide any additional notes about your experience with the product.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
