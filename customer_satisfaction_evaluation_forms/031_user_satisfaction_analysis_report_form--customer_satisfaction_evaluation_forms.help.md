# user_satisfaction_analysis_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The user satisfaction analysis report form is used to gather information about user satisfaction with a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the issue you're experiencing. Please choose one of the options from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue or concern you have with your account in the Emacs Users Comments field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. This is how we will contact you to discuss your feedback.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field. This will help us to contact you in case we need to discuss your feedback further.
5. Select the category for this issue from the Category 2 dropdown menu.
6. Provide any additional comments you would like to share in the Comments field.
7. Finally, click the Submit button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category**: Select the category of the issue you're experiencing.
  - (Select one of: "Yes", "No")
- **Emacs Users Comments**: Please provide a brief description of the issue or concern you have with your account.
  - Type: Text field
  - Required: Yes
- **Email**: Enter your email address. This is how we will contact you to discuss your feedback.
  - (Format: email, e.g. `example@example.com`)
  - Required: Yes
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will help us to contact you in case we need to discuss your feedback further.
  - Type: Text field
  - Required: Yes
- **Category 2**: Select the category for this issue from the dropdown menu.
  - (Select one of: "Yes", "No")
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments you would like to share in this issue.
  - Type: Note field
  - Required: Yes
- **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
  - Type: Text field
  - Required: Yes

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* Avoid using profanity or abusive language.
* If you have multiple concerns, it's best to submit one form per issue.
* Our team will review your feedback and respond to you as soon as possible.
