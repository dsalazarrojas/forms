# Urban Convenience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from customers about their recent experience at your store. It will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and submit it. Each field is described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Category)**: Select the category this form belongs to, either "Customer Satisfaction Evaluation Forms" or "Feedback Forms".
* **Product/Service (Product/Service)**: Choose from a list of options whether the product/service is related to "Food", "Non-Food", "Services", or other.
* **Product/Service Category (Product/Service Category)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the product/service has a subcategory.
* **Sub-Category (Sub-Category)**: Enter the subcategory related to your product/service.
* **Store Name (Store Name)**: Enter the name of the store where you made the purchase.
* **Store Location (Store Location)**: Enter the location of the store.
* **Product Price (Product Price)**: Enter the price of the product/service.
* **Purchase Date (Purchase Date)**: Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Purchase Time (Purchase Time)**: Enter the time of the purchase.
* **Store Hours (Store Hours)**: Describe the store hours of the store.
* **Product Weight (Product Weight)**: Enter the weight of the product.
* **Product Length (Product Length)**: Enter the length of the product.
* **Product Width (Product Width)**: Enter the width of the product.
* **Product Height (Product Height)**: Enter the height of the product.
* **E-mail (E-mail)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Describe your feedback about your experience.
* **Overall Rating (Overall Rating)**: Select from the options "Urban Convenience", "Excellent", "Good", "Poor", "Bad" to rate your overall experience.
* **Product Name (Product Name)**: Enter the name of the product.

Please note that some fields are not required, but it's still helpful if you can provide as much information as possible. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time.
