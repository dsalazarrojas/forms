# Status Reacquisition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our service. Your input is valuable in helping us understand how we can improve and better serve you.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number fields.
2. Choose a Date and Time for when you experienced our service.
3. In the Note field, describe your experience with our service. This can include any details or comments you'd like to share with us.
4. Select your level of Satisfaction with our service from the options provided.
5. If you've reacquired any of the options listed, please select them. If none of the options apply, type "Other (please specify)" and add your own description.
6. Choose how you rate the quality of our service from the options provided.
7. Select how you rate our communication from the options provided.
8. Select how you rate your overall experience with our service.
9. Rate our service with a numerical rating out of 5.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.
11. Let us know if you'd like to be contacted again and with whom you'd like to be contacted.
12. Select how you rate your customer type.
13. Select how you rate the quality of our service.
14. Select how you rate our communication.
15. Select how you rate your overall experience with our service.
16. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.
17. Select your level of customer satisfaction with our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date you experienced our service.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time you experienced our service.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Describe your experience with our service.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with our service.
* **Reacquired** (`reacquired`, select_multiple, optional): If you've reacquired any of the options listed, select them.
* **Other (please specify)** (`other_reacquired`, text, optional): If none of the previous options apply, type in your own description.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Customer type** (`customer_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you categorize your customer type.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of our service.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate our communication.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience with our service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate our service with a numerical rating.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you'd like to be contacted again and with whom you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact** (`contact`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted.
* **Comments 2** (`comments2`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your customer satisfaction with our service.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled in order to submit the form.
