# Favorite Notification Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and ratings from customers or users regarding their favorite notifications or services. It helps us to understand what you like or dislike about the notifications and services we provide.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best describes your experience with the favorite notification.
2. If you have any feedback about the notification, please type it in the "feedback" field.
3. If you have any suggestions on how to improve the service, please type it in the "suggestion" field.
4. Choose the category that best describes the service you received.
5. Provide your contact information if you want us to follow up with you.
6. Select the date and time you received the notification.
7. If you have any note or comments about the service, please type it in the "note" field.
8. If you want to provide your email address, please do so in the "email" field.
9. Choose the option that best describes your favorite aspect of the notification.
10. If you have any comments about the service, please type them in the "comments" field.
11. Rate your experience with a number (e.g., 1-5).
12. If you want us to follow up with you, select the option that best describes your preference.
13. Finally, click submit to send us your feedback.

* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, select_multiple, required): Select the option that best describes your experience with the favorite notification.
