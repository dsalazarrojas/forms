# User Interaction Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Interaction Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers after significant interactions with our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for "Feedback Form" to indicate whether you have had a positive or negative interaction.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product or service using the "Customer Satisfaction Rating" dropdown.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, type them in the "Additional Comments" text field.
4. If you would like us to follow up with you, provide your email address.
5. If you would like to be contacted by phone, provide your phone number.
6. If you have any further comments, type them in the "Comments" text field.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
8. If you have any final notes or comments, type them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form**: Select "Yes" if you had a positive interaction with our product or service, or "No" if you did not.
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our product or service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Additional Comments**: Type any additional comments or concerns you may have about your interaction.
* **Email**: If you would like us to follow up with you, type in your email address.
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, type in your phone number.
* **Comments**: Type any further comments or concerns you may have.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback to us.
* **Note**: Type any final notes or comments about your interaction.
