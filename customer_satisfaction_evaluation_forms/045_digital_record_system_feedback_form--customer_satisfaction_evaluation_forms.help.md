# Digital Record System Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our digital record system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter a title for the feedback you are providing.
2.  Provide a brief description of your feedback.
3.  Specify what you want to tell us about your experience.
4.  Rate your experience with our system.
5.  If you have any suggestions for improvement, provide them.
6.  Enter a valid email address to be contacted for follow-up questions or further assistance.
7.  Enter your phone number for additional contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Record System Feedback Form minimalist** (form_title): This is the title of your feedback. You can enter a brief title that summarizes your experience with our digital record system.
* **Description** (form_description): This field is for you to provide a brief description of your experience with our digital record system. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **What do you want to tell us about?** (form_feedback): This field is for you to specify what you want to tell us about your experience. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Rate your experience** (form_rating): This field is for you to rate your experience with our digital record system. Please select a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (form_suggestions): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in this field. This will help us understand what we can do better.
* **Email** (form_email): This is the field for you to enter a valid email address. We will use this to contact you if we need to follow up on your feedback or answer any questions you may have.
* **Phone** (form_phone): This is the field for you to enter your phone number. We will use this number to contact you if we need to follow up on your feedback or answer any questions you may have.
* **Submit** (form_submit): This is the final step. Please click on this button to submit your feedback. Your feedback will be reviewed and acted upon by our team.
