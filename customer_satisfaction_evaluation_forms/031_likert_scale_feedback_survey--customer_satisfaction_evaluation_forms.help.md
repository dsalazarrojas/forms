# Likert Scale Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Likert Scale Feedback Survey designed to collect end-user feedback on our service. It is used to measure customer satisfaction and gather suggestions for improvement. This survey is intended to be a brief and concise way to gather your thoughts and opinions about our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the survey introduction to understand the purpose and context.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, using the options provided.
3. Make sure to submit your feedback before leaving the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Introduction (1)**: This is a short description of the survey and its purpose. Please take a moment to read it and understand what we are asking.
* **How would you rate our service overall? (2)**: Rate our service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is Very Dissatisfied and 5 is Very Satisfied.
* **Do you have any suggestions or feedback for us? (3)**: Share any thoughts or comments you have about our service. This is your chance to provide constructive feedback to help us improve.
* **How satisfied are you with the quality of our product/service? (4)**: Choose one of the options that best describes your level of satisfaction.
* **How likely are you to recommend our product/service to a friend or colleague? (5)**: Select one of the options that best describes your willingness to recommend.
* **How would you rate the overall value of our product/service? (6)**: Choose one of the options that best describes your perception of the value.
* **How likely are you to use our product/service again? (7)**: Select one of the options that best describes your likelihood of using it again.
* **How would you rate the speed of our service? (8)**: Choose one of the options that best describes your satisfaction with the speed.
* **Submit (9)**: This is the final step to complete the survey. Select the option to submit your feedback.
* **Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback (10)**: This is a message of appreciation for taking the time to participate in the survey.

Tips:

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Take your time to think before making a selection.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
