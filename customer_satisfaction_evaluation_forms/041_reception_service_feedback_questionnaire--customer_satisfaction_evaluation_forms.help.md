# Reception Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experience at our reception service. This will help us understand areas of improvement and provide better service to our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Be honest about your experience and feelings.
3. Fill out the form completely, but only answer the questions that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Experience (General Experience)**: Use numbers to rate your general experience from 1 to 10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is very good.
* **Staff Interaction (Staff Interaction)**: Select the option that best describes your interaction with the reception staff.
* **Service Quality (Service Quality)**: Select all the options that best describe your experience with the service.
* **Reception Staff Performance (Reception Staff Performance)**: Rate your experience with the reception staff using numbers from 1 to 10, where 1 is very poor and 10 is very good.
* **Service Speed (Service Speed)**: Select the option that best describes how quickly you received the service.
* **Reception Area (Reception Area)**: Describe the reception area where you received the service.
* **Received Help (Received Help)**: Select the option that best describes the help you received.
* **Receptionist Name (Receptionist Name)**: Enter the name of the receptionist who helped you.
* **Received Help From (Received Help From)**: Enter the name or department of the person or team that helped you (if applicable).
* **Received Help From Others (Received Help From Others)**: Select all the options that best describe the help you received from others.
* **Suggestion (Suggestion)**: Provide any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Share any additional feedback you have about your experience.
