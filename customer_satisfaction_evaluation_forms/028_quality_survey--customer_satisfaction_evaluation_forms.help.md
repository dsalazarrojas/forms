# Quality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this quality survey is to gather information about your experience with our product or service. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we use this survey to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best fit your experience with our product or service.
2. Type your answer in the text field, if applicable.
3. Choose the corresponding rating for the numerical fields.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks how satisfied you are with your overall experience. Choose from the options "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Please type in the name of the product or service you are evaluating.
* **Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the product or service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the service you received on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, required: false): This field asks if you received good customer service. Choose from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required: false): Rate the price of the product or service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, time, required: false): This field is not currently used. Please disregard.
* **Purchase Experience** (`purchase_experience`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts about your purchase experience. 

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional, but your input is still valuable to us.
