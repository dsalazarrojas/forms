# Account Setup Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Account Setup Feedback Survey" is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with setting up their account. This form helps us understand how easy or difficult it was to set up the account, how long it took, and any issues you encountered. Your input will help us improve the setup process for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide us with your feedback, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely, answering all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. For any issues you encountered, select all that apply.
4. If you have any additional feedback, feel free to provide it in the "Any Other Feedback" section.
5. Once you've completed the form, click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Optional)** (`full_name`, text, required: false): This is an optional field where you can provide your full name if you'd like.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: true): Please enter the email address you used to create your account.
* **Account Type Created** (`account_type`, select_one, required: true): Select the type of account you created: Personal, Business, Enterprise, Trial, or Other.
* **Approximate Date You Set Up Your Account** (`setup_date`, date, required: false): If you can remember, please enter the approximate date you set up your account.
* **How Easy Was the Account Setup Process** (`overall_ease`, select_one, required: true): Rate the ease of use of the account setup process: Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, or Very difficult.
* **How Long Did the Setup Take** (`setup_time`, select_one, required: true): Select the time it took you to set up your account: Less than 5 minutes, 5-10 minutes, 10-20 minutes, 20-30 minutes, or More than 30 minutes.
* **Were the Setup Instructions Clear** (`instructions_clarity`, select_one, required: true): Rate the clarity of the setup instructions: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, or Very unclear.
* **Did You Encounter Any Issues During Setup** (`issues_encountered`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all issues you encountered during setup, such as Verification email not received, Confusing form fields, Technical error or crash, Payment processing issue, Unclear terminology, or No issues encountered.
* **Did You Need to Contact Support During Setup** (`support_needed`, select_one, required: true): If you needed to contact support during setup, rate the resolution time: Yes, and the issue was resolved quickly, Yes, but it took a while to resolve, Yes, but the issue was not resolved, or No, I did not need support.
* **How Would You Rate the Welcome or Onboarding Experience** (`welcome_experience`, select_one, required: true): Rate the welcome or onboarding experience: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Were Key Features Easy to Find After Setup** (`feature_discovery`, select_one, required: true): Select how easy it was to find key features after setup: Yes, very easy, Somewhat easy, Difficult to find, or I have not explored yet.
* **Would You Recommend This Service to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required: true): Select if you would recommend this service to others: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably not, or Definitely not.
* **What Could We Improve About the Setup Process** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, provide them in this text field.
* **Any Other Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, required: false): If you have any other feedback or comments, feel free to provide them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide your genuine feedback, as it will help us improve the account setup process.
* If you're unsure about any question, please answer based on your overall experience.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
