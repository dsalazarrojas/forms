# Retail Product Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer feedback and satisfaction data about a specific retail product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Ensure that all fields with a asterisk \* are filled out.
3. Navigate through each page until completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, \*): Rate the product you are evaluating, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-5).
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text): Enter the name of the product you are evaluating.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, \*): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, select_one, \*): Select how satisfied you are with the product.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the product.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, \*): Enter your contact information.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, \*): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, \*): Enter your email address. Please make sure it matches the one entered in "Contact Info".
* **Contact Email Confirmation** (`contact_email_confirmation`, email, \*): Re-enter your email address for verification.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): Enter the store location where you purchased the product, if applicable.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_multiple, \*): Select whether or not you have purchased this type of product before.
* **Product Type 2** (`product_type_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not you have purchased this type of product before. If you are not sure, enter "No".
* **Product Type 3** (`product_type_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not you have purchased this type of product before. If you are not sure, enter "No".
* **Product Type 4** (`product_type_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether or not you have purchased this type of product before. If you are not sure, enter "No".
* **Store Info** (`store_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the store where you purchased the product.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the store name where you purchased the product.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the store address where you purchased the product.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): Enter the store phone number where you purchased the product.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, email, optional): Enter the store email address where you purchased the product.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the product.
