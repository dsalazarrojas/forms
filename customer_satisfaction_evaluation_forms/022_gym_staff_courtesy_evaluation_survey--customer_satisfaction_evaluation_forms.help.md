# gym_staff_courtesy_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on your interaction with the staff of the gym, including their quality of service, punctuality, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly based on your genuine experience with the staff.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Submit your feedback at the end of the form.
