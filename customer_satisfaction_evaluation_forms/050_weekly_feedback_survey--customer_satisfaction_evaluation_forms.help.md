# weekly_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers regarding their overall experience with our company on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply click on the "submit" button at the end of the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Answer the rating questions with a number between 1 and 5.
4. In the "Additional feedback" field, you can write any extra comments or suggestions you have.
5. If you would like to provide contact details, you can add them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Feedback Survey Title**: This field is for you to add a brief description of what you are rating (e.g. "Service from Sales team" or "Support team performance").
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with our company, on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **Rate your experience with our services**: Rate your experience with our services, on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **Ease of Use**: Rate how easy it was to use our services, on a scale of 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy).
* **Quality of our services**: Rate the quality of our services, on a scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
* **Likelihood of Recommendation**: Rate how likely you are to recommend us to others, on a scale of 1 (not likely at all) to 5 (very likely).
* **Additional Feedback**: This field is for any extra comments or suggestions you have about your experience with our company.
* **Contact Details**: If you would like to be contacted back regarding your feedback, please provide your contact details (email, phone number, address, city, state, country, company, job, department, floor, room).
* **Contact Details Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Contact Details Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Contact Details Address**: Please enter your address.
* **Contact Details Zip**: Please enter your zip code.
* **Contact Details State**: Rate if your contact details include your state.
* **Contact Details Country**: Rate if your contact details include your country.
* **Contact Details Company**: Rate if your contact details include your company.
* **Contact Details Job**: Rate if your contact details include your job title.
* **Contact Details Position**: Rate if your contact details include your position.
* **Contact Details Department**: Rate if your contact details include your department.
* **Contact Details Floor**: Rate if your contact details include your floor number.
* **Contact Details Room**: Rate if your contact details include your room number.
* **Contact Details City**: Rate if your contact details include your city.

Note: If you have already submitted feedback, please do not resubmit. This form should only be completed by customers who have interacted with our company in the past week.
