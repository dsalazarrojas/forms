# CCTV Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on our CCTV customer satisfaction survey. We use this feedback to identify areas of strength and weakness in our service and installation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Customer Name
2. Enter your Email Address
3. If you have a phone number, enter it
4. Tell us about your Installation Date
5. If applicable, mention the Service Technician's name
6. Rate your Overall Satisfaction with our service
7. Rate the quality of the Installation
8. Rate the behavior and communication of the Technician
9. Tell us how your CCTV system is functioning
10. Rate your satisfaction with our Response Time
11. Evaluate the Value for Money of our service
12. Select any issues you have encountered
13. Tell us how likely you are to recommend us to others
14. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions
15. Indicate if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): This will be used for follow-up if needed. Ensure it is a valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): This is your best contact number in case we need to reach out.
- **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, Date, Required): Please enter the date when your CCTV system was installed.
- **Service Technician Name** (`service_technician`, Text, Optional): If you remember the name of the technician that installed your system, please enter it here.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with our service? Select one of the options from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
- **Installation Quality** (`installation_quality`, Select One, Required): Rate the quality of the installation work done by our team.
- **Technician Professionalism** (`technician_professionalism`, Select One, Required): Rate the behavior and communication of the technician that installed your system.
- **System Functionality** (`system_functionality`, Select One, Required): Is your CCTV system working as expected? Select from Exceeds Expectations to Not Working Properly.
- **Response Time Satisfaction** (`response_time`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with our response time?
- **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, Select One, Required): Do you feel you received good value for money from our service? Select from Excellent Value to Very Poor Value.
- **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`, Select Multiple, Required): Select any issues you have encountered with your system. 
- **Would You Recommend** (`recommend_to_others`, Select One, Required): How likely are you to recommend us to friends or colleagues? Select from Very Likely to Very Unlikely.
- **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, Text, Optional): Please provide any other comments or suggestions you may have.
- **May We Contact You** (`follow_up_permission`, Select One, Required): Allow us to contact you for follow-up or select that we may not contact you.
