# Sliding Door Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather customer feedback on the sliding door product. The form asks customers to share their thoughts on the product's quality, installation experience, functionality, satisfaction, and whether they would recommend the product to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Sliding Door Functionality" field, please type a brief description of your experience with the product.
3. For the "Sliding Door Satisfaction" field, please select a number rating from 1 to 5, with 1 being "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 being "Very Satisfied".
4. For the "Would You Recommend This Sliding Door?" field, please select "True" if you would recommend the product, and "False" otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sliding Door Product Quality** (`title`, `text`, optional): Please describe your perception of the product's quality.
* **Sliding Door Installation Experience** (`title`, `text`, optional): Share your experience with the installation process.
* **Sliding Door Functionality** (`title`, `text`, optional): Describe your experience with the product's functionality.
* **Sliding Door Satisfaction** (`title`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the product, with 1 being "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 being "Very Satisfied".
* **Would You Recommend This Sliding Door?** (`title`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you would recommend the product or not.
