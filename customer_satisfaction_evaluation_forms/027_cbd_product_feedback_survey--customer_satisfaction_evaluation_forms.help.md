# CBD Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Customer Satisfaction Evaluation Form designed to gather feedback on our CBD products, helping us to identify areas for improvement and ensure the quality and effectiveness of our products meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please select the product you purchased, the source of your purchase, and answer the subsequent questions honestly. You may also add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which product did you purchase?**
	+ This question asks you to select the specific CBD product you purchased from us.
	+ Please choose the correct product name that best represents the product you bought.
* **Where did you purchase this product?**
	+ This question asks you to select the source of your purchase (e.g., Online Store, Retail Location, etc.).
	+ Please choose the location where you bought the product.
* **What was your primary reason for using this CBD product?**
	+ This question seeks to understand the main purpose for which you used the product (e.g., pain relief, anxiety management, etc.).
	+ Please select the reason that best describes your usage experience.
* **How often do you use this product?**
	+ This question inquires about your usage frequency (e.g., daily, a few times a week, etc.).
	+ Select the frequency that accurately represents your usage of the product.
* **How effective was the product for your needs?**
	+ This question assesses the product's effectiveness for your intended purpose.
	+ Rate the product's effectiveness on the scale provided.
* **What dosage or amount do you typically use?**
	+ This question asks for the specific amount or dosage of the product you usually consume.
	+ Please enter the quantity in text format (e.g., 1 dropper, 2 gummies, etc.).
* **Did you experience any side effects?**
	+ This question seeks to understand if you encountered any adverse reactions or effects while using the product.
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Rate the taste or texture of the product**
	+ This question asks for your opinion on the product's taste or texture.
	+ Evaluate the product's taste or texture using the provided scale (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Are you satisfied with the product packaging?**
	+ This question investigates your satisfaction with the packaging of the product.
	+ Select your level of satisfaction using the provided scale (Yes, very satisfied, Yes, satisfied, No, needs improvement).
* **Do you feel the product is worth the price?**
	+ This question assesses the perceived value of the product in relation to its cost.
	+ Rate the product's value on the scale provided (Yes, great value, Fair price, No, too expensive).
* **How likely are you to purchase this product again?**
	+ This question gauges your intent to repurchase the product.
	+ Select the option that best reflects your likelihood of purchasing the product again.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience?**
	+ This question provides an opportunity to add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the product.
	+ Please use this space to express your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Would you like to join our VIP list for exclusive discounts?**
	+ This question invites you to join our loyalty program for special offers and promotions.
	+ Select 'True' to join the list, or 'False' to decline.

## Tips
To ensure accurate and helpful feedback, please answer these questions honestly. Your responses will help us improve our products and services to better meet your needs.
