# Online Shopping Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on your experience with our online shopping platform. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance your shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select one page at a time to fill out.
2. For each page, answer the questions as honestly as possible.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required): This question asks about your experience with our communication. Please select "Very good", "Good", or "Bad" to indicate your satisfaction.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience with our customer service team. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Return Policy** (`return_policy`, text, optional): Describe your experience with our return policy. Please include any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, number, optional): Evaluate the quality of the products you purchased from us.
* **Shopping Experience** (`shopping_experience`, number, optional): Rate your experience with shopping on our platform.
* **Website Usability** (`website_usability`, number, optional): Evaluate the usability of our website.

Note: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.
