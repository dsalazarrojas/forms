# Mobile App Navigation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile App Navigation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on your experiences with our mobile app's navigation. Your input will help us understand what's working well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a helpful review, please answer the questions honestly, and try to be as specific as possible. You can rate the questions on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is 'Not satisfied' and 5 is 'Very satisfied'. You can also select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate your overall experience on our mobile app?** (`app_experience`, Number, Required): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the app's navigation. Please rate your experience from 1 to 5.
* **Which features of our mobile app are you using most frequently?** (`app_usage`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the features you use regularly to help us understand your usage patterns.
* **What do you think is missing from our mobile app navigation?** (`feedback_missing_features`, Text, Optional): If you think there's something we're missing, let us know what it is. Be as specific as possible.
* **How would you rate the ease of navigation on our mobile app?** (`navigation_ease`, Number, Required): Rate the ease of navigating our app from 1 to 5.
* **Are there any issues or bugs that have caused you inconvenience while using our mobile app?** (`app_issues`, Select One, Optional): If you've experienced any issues, let us know what they were. This helps us identify and fix problems.
* **How would you rate the speed of our mobile app navigation?** (`app_speed`, Number, Required): Rate the speed of navigating our app from 1 to 5.
* **How often do you use our mobile app on a daily basis?** (`daily_usage`, Number, Required): Help us understand your daily usage patterns.
* **Which of the following features would you like to see added to our mobile app?** (`app_feature_additions`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the features you'd like to see added.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with our mobile app's search function?** (`search_satisfaction`, Number, Required): Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5.
* **How often do you use our mobile app's search function?** (`search_usage`, Number, Required): Help us understand your search usage patterns.
* **Are there any features that you would like to see removed from our mobile app?** (`removed_features`, Select One, Optional): If there's a feature you'd like to see gone, let us know.
* **How would you rate the overall design of our mobile app's navigation?** (`design_rating`, Number, Required): Rate the design from 1 to 5.
