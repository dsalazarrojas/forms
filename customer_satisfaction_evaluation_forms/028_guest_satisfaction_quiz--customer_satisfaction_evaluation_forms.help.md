# Guest Satisfaction Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Guest Satisfaction Quiz" form is used to collect feedback from guests about their experience at a venue. It is used to gather information that will help us improve our service and provide better experiences for our guests in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information about your experience.
2. For "Value Added Services" and "Value for Money" fields, select one of the options from the dropdown menu.
3. You can enter any additional information in the "Contact Info" and "Phone No" fields if you'd like us to follow up with you.
4. Once you've completed all the fields, click submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Experience** (`guest_experience`, text, not required): Describe your overall experience at the venue.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, not required): Rate the quality of the service you received.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, text, not required): Tell us about the quality of the food you had during your visit.
* **Value Added Services** (`value_added_services`, select multiple, not required): Select from the list:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Okay
	+ Not Okay
	+ Not Applicable
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select one, not required): Select from the list:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Okay
	+ Not Okay
	+ Not Applicable
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, note, not required): Enter your overall satisfaction with your experience.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you.
* **Phone No** (`phone_no`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to follow up with you.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Use the dropdown menus for "Value Added Services" and "Value for Money" fields.
* You can enter any additional information in the "Contact Info" and "Phone No" fields if you'd like us to follow up with you.

Note: This form is not required, and users can skip it if they choose.
