# Internet Connectivity Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet Connectivity Survey Form is designed to collect information about customers' experiences with internet connectivity, including speed, stability, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill out the fields as needed. The form is divided into sections, each with its own set of fields. You may need to scroll down to access all the fields in each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
- **General Information (1)**: Enter any relevant information about your internet connection, such as your service provider or connection type.

### Internet Speed (Page 2)
- **Internet Speed (2)**: Estimate your average internet speed in Mbps (MegaBytes per second). This will help us gauge your connection's performance.

### Network Stability (Page 3)
- **Network Stability (3)**: Describe your experience with network stability. Do you experience frequent disconnections or slow loading times?

### Customer Service (Page 4)
- **Customer Service (4)**: Please rate how satisfied you are with our customer service, including responsiveness and helpfulness.

### Internet Coverage (Page 5)
- **Internet Coverage (5)**: Estimate your coverage area, or how widespread your internet signal can reach.

### Device Usage (Page 6)
- **Device Usage (6)**: Report how much time you spend on devices connected to your internet, including hours per day or total hours per month.

### Satisfaction (Page 7)
- **Satisfaction (7)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our internet services. Are you satisfied or dissatisfied with your experience?

### Comments (Page 8)
- **Comments (8)**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about our internet services. We value your feedback and want to hear your thoughts!
