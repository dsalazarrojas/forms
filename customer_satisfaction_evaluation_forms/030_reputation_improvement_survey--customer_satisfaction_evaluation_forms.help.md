# Reputation Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the customer's satisfaction with our service. We value your input and want to understand what we're doing well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the corresponding information. Most fields are optional, but if you're unsure what to put in a particular field, please don't hesitate to contact us. Please follow these steps:

1. **Answer "yes" or "no" to questions about your experience with our service** (Field 2: select_one).
2. **Select all that apply to describe your experience** (Field 3: select_multiple).
3. **Enter the date of your visit or interaction with our service** (Field 4: date).
4. **Enter the time of your visit or interaction with our service** (Field 5: time).
5. **Write any additional comments or notes about your experience** (Field 6: note).
6. **Provide your email address** (Field 7: email).
7. **Enter your phone number** (Field 8: phone).
8. **Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-10** (Field 9: customer_satisfaction_evaluation_form_1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction Evaluation Form** (Field 1: customer_satisfaction_evaluation_form): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Select One** (Field 2: select_one): Choose "Yes" if you had a positive experience with our service, and "No" if you had a negative experience.
* **Select Multiple** (Field 3: select_multiple): Select all that apply to describe your experience with our service. For example, if you had a good experience with our service, select "Yes" for every option.
* **Date** (Field 4: date): Enter the date of your visit or interaction with our service. This will help us track your feedback over time.
* **Time** (Field 5: time): Enter the time of your visit or interaction with our service. This will help us better understand your experience.
* **Note** (Field 6: note): Write any additional comments or notes about your experience. This is a great place to provide more details about your visit or interaction.
* **Email** (Field 7: email): This is where you can provide your email address. We'll use this to contact you for further feedback or to follow up on your experience.
* **Phone** (Field 8: phone): This is where you can enter your phone number. We'll use this to contact you if we need more information or to follow up on your experience.
* **Customer Satisfaction Evaluation Form 1** (Field 9: customer_satisfaction_evaluation_form_1): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-10.
