# Accuracy Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Accuracy Assessment Survey" form is designed to collect feedback from customers on the accuracy of a particular product or service. This form helps the development team to identify areas for improvement and make necessary changes to enhance the accuracy of the product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product or service you are evaluating from the "Product or Service Name" field.
2. Rate the precision of the product or service using the "Precision Rating" field. Select 1 for "Not Precise" and 5 for "Highly Precise".
3. Evaluate the reliability of the product or service using the "Reliability Rating" field. Select "Unreliable", "Somewhat Reliable", or "Very Reliable".
4. If you have encountered any errors, select all that apply from the "Areas Needing Improvement" field.
5. Choose the frequency of errors you have found using the "Frequency of Errors Found" field. Select "Never", "Rarely", "Occasionally", or "Frequently".
6. If you have recently experienced an accuracy issue, describe it in the "Description of Recent Accuracy Issue" field.
7. If an error was reported, rate your satisfaction with the resolution using the "Resolution Satisfaction" field. Select "Dissatisfied", "Neutral", or "Satisfied".
8. Provide any suggestions for quality assurance using the "Suggestions for Quality Assurance" field.
9. Rate your overall confidence in the product or service using the "Overall Confidence Score" field. Choose a score between 1 and 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product or Service Name** (`product_or_service_name`, text, required): This field is where you select the product or service you are evaluating.
* **Precision Rating** (`precision_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the precision of the product or service. Select 1 for "Not Precise" and 5 for "Highly Precise".
* **Reliability Rating** (`reliability_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the reliability of the product or service. Select "Unreliable", "Somewhat Reliable", or "Very Reliable".
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (`areas_needing_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): If you have encountered any errors, select all that apply.
* **Frequency of Errors Found** (`frequency_of_errors_found`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of errors you have found. Select "Never", "Rarely", "Occasionally", or "Frequently".
* **Description of Recent Accuracy Issue** (`description_of_recent_accuracy_issue`, text, optional): If you have recently experienced an accuracy issue, describe it here.
* **Resolution Satisfaction** (`resolution_satisfaction`, select_one, required): If an error was reported, rate your satisfaction with the resolution. Select "Dissatisfied", "Neutral", or "Satisfied".
* **Suggestions for Quality Assurance** (`suggestions_for_quality_assurance`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for quality assurance.
* **Overall Confidence Score** (`overall_confidence_score`, number, required): Rate your overall confidence in the product or service. Choose a score between 1 and 10.
* **-- Thank you for contributing to our quality improvement --** (`survey_footer`, note, optional): This is a note field to acknowledge your contribution to the quality improvement process.
