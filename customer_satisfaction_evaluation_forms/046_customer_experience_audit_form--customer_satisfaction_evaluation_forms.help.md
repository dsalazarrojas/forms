# customer_experience_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Experience Audit Form" is a tool designed to measure the customer's overall satisfaction with a company's interaction. It is meant to be completed by customers who have experienced our services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Experience Form** (`Customer Experience Form`, text, required: false): A general description of your experience with our company.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`Customer Satisfaction`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 indicating how satisfied you are with our service or product.
* **Customer Service Quality** (`Customer Service Quality`, text, required: false): Description of how well we met your needs during the interaction.
* **Customer Journey** (`Customer Journey`, text, required: false): Thoughts on the overall journey through our company.
* **Customer Feedback** (`Customer Feedback`, text, required: false): Additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Customer Experience Score** (`Customer Experience Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for the overall experience with our company.
* **Customer Satisfaction Score** (`Customer Satisfaction Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for customer satisfaction.
* **Customer Service Score** (`Customer Service Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for customer service quality.
* **Customer Journey Score** (`Customer Journey Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for the overall journey through our company.
* **Customer Feedback Comments** (`Customer Feedback Comments`, text, required: false): Additional comments or suggestions.
* **Customer Service Comment** (`Customer Service Comment`, text, required: false): Thoughts on how to improve our service.
* **Customer Satisfaction Comment** (`Customer Satisfaction Comment`, text, required: false): Thoughts on how to improve customer satisfaction.
* **Customer Experience Score** (`Customer Experience Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for customer experience.
* **Customer Satisfaction Score** (`Customer Satisfaction Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for customer satisfaction.
* **Customer Service Score** (`Customer Service Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for customer service.
* **Customer Journey Score** (`Customer Journey Score`, number, required: false): Score from 1-5 for customer journey.
