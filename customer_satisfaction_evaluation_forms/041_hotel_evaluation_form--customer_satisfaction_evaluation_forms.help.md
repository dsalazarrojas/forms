# Hotel Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their stay at the hotel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hotel_check_in** (Date, Required): Please enter the date you checked in to the hotel.
* **check_out** (Date, Required): Please enter the date you checked out of the hotel.
* **overall_experience** (Number, Required): Rate your overall experience at the hotel from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **room_quality** (Text, Optional): Describe the quality of your room, including cleanliness, comfort, and any other notable features.
* **cleanliness** (Number, Optional): Rate the cleanliness of your room from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **amenities** (Select One, Optional): Choose which amenities you used during your stay, such as Air Conditioner, Bath Tub/Shower, Free Breakfast, Free Parking, Free Wi-Fi, In-room coffee machine, or Mini Bar.
* **food_quality** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose which meals you had during your stay, such as Breakfast, Dinner, Lunch, or Room Service.
* **staff_service** (Number, Optional): Rate the service provided by hotel staff from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **hotel_atmosphere** (Text, Optional): Describe the atmosphere of the hotel, including the ambiance, decor, and overall feel.
* **recommend** (Number, Optional): Rate how likely you are to recommend the hotel to others from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **rating** (Number, Optional): Rate your overall experience at the hotel from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

Remember to be honest and accurate when filling out this form, as your feedback is crucial to helping us improve our services.
