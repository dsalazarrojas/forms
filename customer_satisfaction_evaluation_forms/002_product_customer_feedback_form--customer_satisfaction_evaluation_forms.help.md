# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their experiences with a product. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Personal Information
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive any responses or updates regarding your feedback.

### Step 4: Provide Product Information
1. **Product ID**: Select the relevant product ID from the dropdown list.

### Step 5: Share Your Feedback
1. **Feedback**: Please share your thoughts about the product, including any praise, criticism, or suggestions for improvement.
2. **Rating 1**: Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the product.
3. **Rating 2**: Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the product.

### Step 6: Optional Details
- **Date**: If relevant, enter the date you experienced the product or service.
- **Time**: If relevant, enter the time you experienced the product or service.
- **Comment**: Any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive any responses or updates regarding your feedback.
* **Contact Number (text)**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **Product ID (select_one)**: Select the relevant product ID from the dropdown list.
* **Feedback (text)**: Share your thoughts about the product, including any praise, criticism, or suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating 1 (select_multiple)**: Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the product.
* **Rating 2 (select_multiple)**: Select one or more ratings that best describe your experience with the product.
* **Date (date)**: If relevant, enter the date you experienced the product or service.
* **Time (time)**: If relevant, enter the time you experienced the product or service.
* **Comment (text)**: Any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback to help us better understand your experience.
- If you have any issues or questions about your feedback, please contact us and we will be happy to assist.
