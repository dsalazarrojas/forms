# customer_retention_strategy_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Retention Strategy Evaluation Form" is designed to gather data on your company's customer satisfaction and retention strategies. This form helps evaluate the effectiveness of your existing strategies and identify areas for improvement. It is typically used by customer-facing teams to assess customer satisfaction, retention rates, and strategies employed to retain customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information for each field.
2. For select multiple options, select all applicable strategies from the dropdown menu.
3. Review and submit the form to evaluate your customer retention strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your company's name for reference and identification.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, Text, Optional): Describe the job function of the person completing this form.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name for accountability and reference.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Type of department or team responsible for customer interactions.
* **Retention Period** (`retention_period`, Number, Optional): The length of time a customer retains your service or product.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, Number, Optional): Measure of customer satisfaction on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Strategy Effectiveness** (`strategy_effectiveness`, Number, Optional): Measure of effectiveness of customer retention strategies (e.g., 1-10).
* **Retention Rate** (`retention_rate`, Number, Optional): Rate at which customers remain with your company.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, Number, Optional): How often customers provide feedback (e.g., 1-10).
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, Number, Optional): Overall customer satisfaction level (e.g., 1-10).
* **Customer Retention Strategy** (`customer_retention_strategy`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable customer retention strategies from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please be specific and accurate when filling out the form to ensure meaningful data for evaluation.
* Review the help guide and form instructions for clarity and completeness.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
