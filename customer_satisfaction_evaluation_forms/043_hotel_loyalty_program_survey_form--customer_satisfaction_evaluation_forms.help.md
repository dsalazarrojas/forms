# Hotel Loyalty Program Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Loyalty Program Survey Form is a tool used to collect feedback from customers about their stay experience, satisfaction, and loyalty program. This form helps the hotel management to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the relevant sections. You can select all that apply for multiple-choice questions and provide a yes/no answer for single-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Customer Satisfactions**
* **Customer Satisfactions** (`Note`, Optional): Please share your overall satisfaction with our hotel.
  - This is an open-ended note field where you can write down your thoughts and opinions about your stay experience.

### **Stay Experience**
* **Stay Experience** (`Select Multiple`, Not Required): Select all that apply.
  - Select one or more of the options to rate your overall stay experience:
    - Overall Satisfaction
    - Service Quality
    - Cleanliness
    - Food Quality

### **Staff Interactions**
* **Staff Interactions** (`Select One`, Not Required): Please select an option.
  - Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No' based on your experience with hotel staff:
    - 'Yes'
    - 'No'

### **Loyalty Program Member**
* **Loyalty Program Member** (`Select One`, Not Required): Please select an option.
  - Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No' based on whether you are a member of our loyalty program:
    - 'Yes'
    - 'No'

### **Communication**
* **Communication** (`Select Multiple`, Not Required): Select all that apply.
  - Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No' for each of the options:
    - 'Yes'
    - 'No'

### **Satisfaction With Hotel**
* **Satisfaction With Hotel** (`Select Multiple`, Not Required): Select all that apply.
  - Select one or more of the options to rate your satisfaction with our hotel:
    - Satisfied
    - Dissatisfied

### **Satisfaction With Hotel Loyalty Program**
* **Satisfaction With Hotel Loyalty Program** (`Select Multiple`, Not Required): Select all that apply.
  - Select one or more of the options to rate your satisfaction with our hotel loyalty program:
    - Satisfied
    - Dissatisfied

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them in the **Customer Satisfactions** field.
* If you are a member of our loyalty program, please let us know so we can provide you with better services.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time in completing this form.
