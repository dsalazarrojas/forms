# Migration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migration Feedback Form is designed to collect customer feedback on their migration experience. It's essential to understand that this form is intended for customers who have gone through a migration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure it's relevant to your migration experience.
2. Complete all required fields, which are:
	* Customer Satisfaction Rating
	* Migration Date
	* Migration Type
	* Migration Status
3. Answer the optional fields as much as possible, as they will help us improve our migration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Satisfaction Rating** (`customer_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required)
Rate your satisfaction with the migration experience on a scale of 1-5 stars.

### 2. **Customer Satisfaction Comment** (`customer_satisfaction_comment`, text, optional)
Share your thoughts on why you rated your satisfaction as you did.

### 3. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your migration experience.

### 4. **Attached File** (`attached_file`, text, optional)
Attach any relevant files that can help us better understand your migration experience.

### 5. **Migration Date** (`migration_date`, date, required)
Select the date of your migration.

### 6. **Migration Time** (`migration_time`, time, optional)
Select the time of your migration.

### 7. **Migration Type** (`migration_type`, select_one, required)
Choose the type of migration you experienced (Internal, External, or Hybrid).

### 8. **Migration Status** (`migration_status`, select_one, required)
Choose the status of your migration (Pending, In Progress, or Completed).

### 9. **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required)
Select all that apply to describe your overall satisfaction with the migration experience.

### 10. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional)
Provide any additional feedback or suggestions on how we can improve our migration process.

### 11. **Submitted By** (`submitted`, text, optional)
Enter your name or identifier.

### 12. **Migrated By** (`migrated_by`, text, optional)
Enter the name or identifier of the person or team responsible for your migration.

### 13. **Migration Notes** (`migration_notes`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments on your migration experience.

### 14. **Attached Files** (`attached_files`, text, optional)
Attach any relevant files to support your feedback.

### 15. **Customer Satisfaction Rating 2** (`customer_satisfaction_rating_2`, select_one, required)
Rate your satisfaction with the migration experience on a scale of 1-5 stars.

### 16. **Customer Satisfaction Comment 2** (`customer_satisfaction_comment_2`, text, optional)
Share your thoughts on why you rated your satisfaction as you did for the second time.

### 17. **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional)
Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your migration experience.

### 18. **Attached File 2** (`attached_file_2`, text, optional)
Attach any relevant files that can help us better understand your migration experience.
