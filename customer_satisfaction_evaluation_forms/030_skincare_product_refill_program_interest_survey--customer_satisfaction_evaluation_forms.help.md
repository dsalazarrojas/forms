# Skincare Product Refill Program Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on the Skincare Product Refill Program. It includes questions about your satisfaction with the refills, your interest in refilling, and your purchase frequency and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding answer for each question. You may select multiple options for some questions. Once you've completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction**: This question asks how satisfied you are with your refills. Please select from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Product Refill Interest**: How often would you like to refill your products? Please select from Refill Regularly to Never.
* **Product Type**: Which type of product are you interested in refilling? Please select from Skincare, Haircare, Both, or Other.
* **Purchase Frequency**: How often do you purchase refills? Please select from Daily to Never.
* **Refill Purchased**: Have you already purchased refills from us? Yes or No.
* **Purchase Frequency of Refills**: How often do you purchase refills? Please select from Every 1-2 weeks to Never.
* **Communication Preferences**: How would you like to communicate with us? Please select from Email, Phone, SMS, In App, or Other.
* **Product Interest**: Which type of product interests you the most? Please select from Skincare, Haircare, Both, or Other.
* **How Satisfied Are You With Your Refills?**: Please select from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **How Do You Like Refills?**: Please select the reasons you like or dislike refills from a list of options.
* **Product Satisfaction**: This question asks which type of product you are satisfied or dissatisfied with. Please select from Facial, Body, Both, or Other.
* **How Can We Improve?**: What areas of our services need improvement? Please select from Quality of Refills, Price, Communication, Product, Delivery Time, or Other.
* **Why Can We Improve?**: Please select the reasons for dissatisfaction with our services.
* **Can We Improve?**: What areas of our services can be improved? Please select from Quality of Refills, Price, Communication, Product, Delivery Time, or Other.
* **Would Recommend**: Would you recommend our refills to others? Yes or No.
* **Communication Preferences 2**: How would you like to communicate with us again? Please select from Email, Phone, SMS, In App, or Other.
* **Product Refill Interest 2**: How often would you like to refill your products? Please select from Refill Regularly to Never.
* **Product Type 2**: Which type of product are you interested in refilling? Please select from Skincare, Haircare, Both, or Other.
* **Purchase Frequency (2)**: How often do you purchase refills? Please select from Daily to Never.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* You may select multiple options for some questions.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the space provided for that.
