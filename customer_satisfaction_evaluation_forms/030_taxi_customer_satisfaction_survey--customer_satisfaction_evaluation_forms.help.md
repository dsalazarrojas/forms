# Taxi Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taxi Customer Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the taxi service. The purpose of this survey is to understand how customers perceive the service, identify areas of improvement, and gather suggestions for better customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. For fields with options, choose the response that best describes your experience.
3. For open-ended fields, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience (\*)**: This field asks about your overall impression of the taxi service.
* **Fare (\*)**: Please enter the amount you paid for the taxi ride.
* **Service Quality (\*)**: Select the option that best describes the service quality of your taxi ride. You can choose from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Helpfulness of Driver (\*)**: This field is for you to describe the helpfulness of your driver during the ride.
* **Overall Satisfaction (\*)**: Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the taxi service. You can choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Improvement Areas**: This field is for you to suggest any areas where you think the service can be improved.
* **Recommend Taxis (\*)**: Check if you would recommend this taxi service to others.
* **Contact Method (\*)**: Select the method you would prefer to contact us (Phone, Email, or Text Messaging).
* **Contact Number**: If you select Contact Method as Phone or Text Messaging, please enter your contact number.
* **Contact Message**: If you select Contact Method as Email, please provide a brief message.
* **Comments (\*)**: Choose all that apply from the options to describe your experience. You can choose from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Rating (\*)**: Provide a numerical rating for your experience.
* **Rating Reason (\*)**: Select the reason for your rating. You can choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
