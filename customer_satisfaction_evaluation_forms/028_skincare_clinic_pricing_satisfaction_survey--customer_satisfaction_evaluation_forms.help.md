# Skincare Clinic Pricing Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Clinic Pricing Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on their pricing experience. This information helps us improve our services and pricing strategy to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate answers for each question.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "Comments" field to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pricing (1)**: Enter the price range you are interested in.
* **Satisfaction (2)**: Select your level of satisfaction with the price range you entered.
	+ 1: Very Dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ 5: Very Satisfied
* **Would you recommend our clinic to a friend? (3)**: Select all that apply:
	+ 1: Yes, I would recommend our clinic to a friend
	+ 2: No, but maybe in the future
	+ 3: No, I would not recommend our clinic
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you and keep you informed about our services.
* **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Category (7)**: Select a category that best represents your interest in our clinic:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
* **Subcategory (8)**: Select all that apply:
	+ X
	+ Y
	+ Z
* **Price Range (9)**: Select all price ranges that interest you:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
	+ D
	+ E
* **Price (10)**: Enter the price you are interested in or currently paying.
* **Service (11)**: Select the service you are interested in:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
* **Duration (12)**: Select the duration of the service:
	+ X
	+ Y
	+ Z
* **Comments (4)**: Any additional comments or feedback about your experience?
* **Recommendation (3)**: Note: This field is already covered by "Would you recommend our clinic to a friend?" and is not included to avoid duplication.
