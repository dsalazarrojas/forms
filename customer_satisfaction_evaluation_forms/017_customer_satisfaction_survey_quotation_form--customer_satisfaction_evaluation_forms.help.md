# customer_satisfaction_survey_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer satisfaction feedback on our service, aiming to understand customer preferences and experiences to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Be honest and provide specific examples or experiences when answering questions like "Example of where you experienced an issue with our service".
3. If a question is not relevant to your experience, you can skip it or answer "Not Applicable" to indicate this.
4. Make sure to save your progress and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely are you to recommend our service?** (`how_likely_recommmend`, text, required: false): This question helps us understand how likely you are to recommend our service to others. Please answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "Not likely at all" and 10 is "Very likely".
* **Reason for your last interaction with our service** (`reason_last_interaction`, text, required: false): This question allows us to understand why you last interacted with our service. Please provide a brief description of your experience.
* **On a scale of 0-10, how satisfied are you with our service?** (`satisfaction_level`, number, required: false): This question measures your level of satisfaction with our service. Please answer on a scale of 0-10, where 0 means "Very dissatisfied" and 10 means "Very satisfied".
* **What aspects of our service do you enjoy the most?** (`favorite_aspects`, text, required: false): This question helps us understand which aspects of our service you enjoy the most. Please provide a list or a brief description.
* **Example of where you experienced an issue with our service** (`issue_example`, text, required: false): This question allows us to understand where you experienced an issue with our service. Please provide a specific example or description.
* **How likely are you to return to our service in the future?** (`likely_return`, select_one, required: true): This question helps us understand if you plan to return to our service. Please choose from the options:
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Not likely
	+ Not at all likely
* **What do you think we could do to improve our service?** (`improvement_points`, text, required: false): This question allows us to understand areas where we can improve our service. Please provide specific suggestions or ideas.
* **Do you have any comments about your experience with our service?** (`comments`, note, required: false): This question is an open-ended space for you to share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Why did you contact us initially?** (`reason_initial_contact`, text, required: false): This question helps us understand why you initially contacted us. Please provide a brief description.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, number, required: false): This question measures your overall experience with our service. Please answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Very bad" and 5 means "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the quality of our communication?** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required: true): This question helps us understand the quality of our communication. Please choose from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How quickly were you able to get help when you needed it?** (`quick_support`, select_one, required: true): This question measures the speed of our support. Please choose from the options:
	+ Instantly
	+ Within a few minutes
	+ Within a few hours
	+ Not quickly enough

## Tips
* Please answer all required questions to provide a complete and honest feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback!
