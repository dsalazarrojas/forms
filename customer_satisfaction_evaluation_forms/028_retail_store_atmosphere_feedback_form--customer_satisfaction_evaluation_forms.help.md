# retail_store_atmosphere_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate and improve the atmosphere of our retail store. It will help us assess the quality of our store environment, including the lighting, music, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding option for each field to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Atmosphere** (`store_atmosphere`, select_one, not required): Rate the overall atmosphere of our store. Choose from Very Good, Somewhat Good, Neutral, Somewhat Bad, or Very Bad.
* **Lighting** (`lighting`, number, not required): Rate the lighting in our store, with a value between 1 and 10.
* **Music** (`music`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all music options that apply:
	+ Too Loud
	+ Too Quiet
	+ Just Right
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, not required): Rate the temperature in our store, with a value between 1 and 10.
* **Store Staff** (`store_staff`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all staff options that apply:
	+ Very Friendly
	+ Somewhat Friendly
	+ Somewhat Unfriendly
	+ Very Unfriendly
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, note, not required): Share your overall experience in the store. This can include any comments or suggestions you have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about our store.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please enter your phone number.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, not required): Please enter the address of the store you are providing feedback for.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the store you are providing feedback for.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, time, not required): Please enter the store hours of the store you are providing feedback for.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate with your feedback, as it will help us improve our store atmosphere.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Comments" field.
