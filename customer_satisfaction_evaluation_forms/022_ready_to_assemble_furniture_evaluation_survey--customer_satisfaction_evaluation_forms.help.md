# Ready To Assemble Furniture Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on the quality and assembly experience of our furniture products. The survey aims to understand your satisfaction with our products and service, as well as identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant answers to each question based on your experience with our product. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Assembly Issue** (`product_assembly_issue`, `select_one`, required): Check 'Yes' if you experienced any issues with assembling the product, 'No' if you did not experience any issues, or 'Not sure' if you're unsure.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the quality issues you experienced with the product (e.g. 'Good', 'Poor').
* **Assembly Instructions** (`assembly_instructions`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the instructions that helped or hindered your assembly experience (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
* **Improve Assembly Instructions** (`instructions_improvement`, `select_one`, required): Check if you think our assembly instructions could be improved.
* **Issue Escalation** (`issue_escalation`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the issues that were escalated to our customer service team (e.g. 'Yes', 'No').
* **Describe the Issue** (`issue_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you experienced.
* **Solution to the Issue** (`issue_solution`, `text`, required): Describe the solution you would like to see for the issue you experienced.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with our product (e.g. 'Very satisfied', 'Satisfied', 'Neutral', 'Dissatisfied', 'Very dissatisfied').
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to contact you back.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Customer Reference** (`customer_reference`, `text`, required): Enter your customer reference number.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, required): Enter the order ID number.
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, `date`, required): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, `text`, required): Enter the order number.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product.
* **Customer Service Rating** (`customer_service_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with our customer service (e.g. '5', '4', '3', '2', '1').
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with our product (e.g. '5', '4', '3', '2', '1').
* **Assembly Time** (`assembly_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time it took to assemble the product.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with the product (e.g. 'Very satisfied', 'Satisfied', 'Neutral', 'Dissatisfied', 'Very dissatisfied').
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Follow-up Questions** (`follow_up_question`, `text`, optional): Answer any follow-up questions or comments you have.
* **Additional Comments** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

Note: All required fields are marked with a red asterisk (\*) and must be filled in to complete the form.
