# Pet Grooming Service Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our pet grooming service. Your feedback is crucial in helping us to improve our services and make your visits with us more enjoyable for you and your pet.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (name, email, and contact method).
2. Select the services your pet has visited us for.
3. Rate your experience with our pet grooming service (staff professionalism, friendliness, attentiveness, and overall satisfaction).
4. Indicate the price you paid for the services.
5. Specify the service location and time of visit.
6. Select the staff member who served you.
7. Add any additional comments or special requests.
8. Confirm your submission to receive a confirmation email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (1): Please provide your name, email, and contact method (phone, email, or social media) to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Grooming Services** (3): Select all the services your pet received during the visit (bathing, nail trimming, hair styling, ear and eye cleaning, nail grouting, furminating, manicure, pampering).
* **Grooming Services Frequency** (4): Indicate how often you visit us for these services (never, rarely, sometimes, often, almost always).
* **Staff Professionalism** (6): Rate the professionalism of the staff who served you (unprofessional, neutral, professional).
* **Staff Friendliness** (7): Rate the friendliness of the staff who served you (unfriendly, neutral, friendly).
* **Staff Attentiveness** (11): Rate the attentiveness of the staff who served you (poor, fair, good, excellent).
* **Staff Knowledge** (10): Rate the knowledge of the staff who served you (poor, fair, good, excellent).
* **Price** (12): Enter the amount you paid for the services.
* **Service Location** (13): Select the location where you visited us for the service (local pet shop, veterinarian's office, pet hotel).
* **Service Time** (14): Enter the time you spent in our service.
* **Staff Experience** (15): Select the experience level of the staff who served you (less than 1 year, 1-2 years, more than 2 years).
* **Additional Services** (16): Add any additional services that were not listed in the previous question.
* **Special Requests** (17): Enter any special requests you made during the visit.
* **Contact Method** (18): Select the method we used to contact you (phone, email, social media).
* **Email** (19): Enter your email address.
* **Website** (20): Enter our website URL.
* **Review** (21): Enter a review or feedback about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (22): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date of Visit** (23): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Staff** (24): Enter the name of the staff member who served you.
* **Submission Confirmation** (25): Confirm your submission to receive a confirmation email.
