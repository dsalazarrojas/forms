# Customer Churn Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Churn Survey is designed to gather information about customers who have stopped using our product or service. This survey aims to understand the reasons behind customer churn and gather feedback on potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that best fit your experience with our product or service. You may choose multiple reasons for churn and likelihood to recommend or reactivate. Please provide honest and accurate answers to help us better serve you and our customers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Churn Survey Form** (`customer_churn_survey_form_data`, text, optional): This is the main form where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our product or service.
* **Customer Churn Reasons Why Didn't Choose** (`customer_churn_reasons_why_didnt_choose`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the reasons that contributed to your decision to stop using our product or service. You may choose from the following options:
	+ We moved to a competitor
	+ Product quality issue
	+ Service quality issue
	+ Pricing issue
	+ No need for product
	+ Not enough information
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Customer Churn Reasons Why Left** (`customer_churn_reasons_why_left`, select multiple, optional): Please select all the reasons that contributed to your decision to stop using our product or service. You may choose from the following options:
	+ Pricing
	+ Product quality
	+ Service quality
	+ Communication issue
	+ No need for product
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Customer Churn Survey Likely to Reactivate** (`customer_churn_survey_likely_to_reactivate`, select multiple, optional): Please indicate how likely you are to re-engage with our product or service. You may choose from the following options:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Not Very Likely
	+ Not Likely
* **Customer Churn Survey Likely to Recommend** (`customer_churn_survey_likely_to_recommend`, select multiple, optional): Please indicate how likely you are to recommend our product or service to others. You may choose from the following options:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Not Very Likely
	+ Not Likely

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the form to help us improve our product or service.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please feel free to include them in the main form field.
* If you have already completed a similar survey, please do not duplicate your feedback.
