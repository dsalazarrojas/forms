# retail_product_delivery_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a retail product, specifically regarding delivery experience, product quality, and other related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with the product and delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was your delivery experience** (`delivery_experience`, `note`, required: false): This field is for you to describe your overall experience with the delivery process. Please provide any comments or details about your experience in this field.
* **What was the quality of the product** (`product_quality`, `number`, required: false): This field is to rate the quality of the product you received. Please select the number that best describes your level of satisfaction with the product's quality.
* **How satisfied are you with the product** (`product_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for you to rate your level of satisfaction with the product. Please select one of the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **How fast was the delivery speed** (`delivery_speed`, `time`, required: false): This field is to rate the speed of the delivery. Please select the option that best describes your experience with the delivery speed.
* **How clear were our communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for you to rate the clarity of our communication. Please select one or more of the options: Very Clear, Somewhat Clear, Neutral, Somewhat Unclear, or Not Clear.
* **How reasonable was the price** (`product_price`, `number`, required: false): This field is for you to rate the reasonableness of the product price. Please select the number that best describes your level of satisfaction with the price.
* **How fast was the delivery** (`delivery_punctuality`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is to rate the punctuality of the delivery. Please select one of the options: On Time, Late, or Not on Time.
* **How reliable is the product** (`product_reliability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for you to rate the reliability of the product. Please select one or more of the options: Very Reliable, Somewhat Reliable, Neutral, Somewhat Unreliable, or Not Reliable.
* **How often did we contact you** (`communication_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is to rate the frequency of our communication with you. Please select one or more of the options: Very Often, Somewhat Often, Neutral, Somewhat Not Often, or Not Often.
* **What was the quality of the product after using it** (`product_quality_complaints`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for you to rate the quality of the product after using it. Please select one or more of the options: No Complaints, Some Complaints, or Many Complaints.
* **How fast was the delivery after using the product** (`delivery_speed_complaints`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is to rate the speed of the delivery after using the product. Please select one of the options: On Time, Late, or Not on Time.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to use the "How was your delivery experience" field to provide more details.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in completing this form.
