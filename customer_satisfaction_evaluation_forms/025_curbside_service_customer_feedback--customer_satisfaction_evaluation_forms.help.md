# Curbside Service Customer Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Curbside Service Customer Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from customers after they've interacted with our curbside service team. This form helps us understand their experience, identify areas for improvement, and measure our service quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Curbside Service Customer Feedback**: This is the form's title and header.
2. **Rating**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 to express your satisfaction with our curbside service.
* If you're satisfied with the service, select a higher rating. For example, 5 stars for an excellent experience and 1 star for a poor experience.
3. **Comments**: Share any thoughts, comments, or concerns you have about your recent curbside service interaction.
* Be as detailed as possible, but keep your comment concise.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates about your feedback or if we need to contact you about your response.
* This is not required, but it helps us get in touch with you if we need more information.
5. **Phone Number**: If you have a preferred phone number where we can reach you, please enter it here.
* This is not required, but it's helpful for follow-up questions or concerns.
6. **Contact Permission**: Select whether you'd like to be contacted by us after submitting your feedback.
* If you select "True," we'll be able to contact you to follow up on your feedback.
7. **Curbside Visit Details**: If you have any additional comments or details about your curbside visit, please write them here.
* This is an optional field, but it helps us understand more about your experience.
8. **Signature**: You can sign your name here, but it's optional.
* This is for your reference and won't be used for follow-up communication.
9. **Staff**: If you remember the staff member's name, you can enter it here.
* This is optional but helps us identify the staff member's name.
10. **Staff Rating**: Rate the staff member's performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
* If they did an excellent job, select 5 stars, and if they did poorly, select 1 star.
11. **Staff Name**: Enter the staff member's name if you remember it.
* This is optional but helps us identify the staff member.
12. **Curbside Visit Date**: If you know the date of your curbside visit, please enter it here.
* This is optional but helps us track visit dates.
13. **Time**: If you know the time of your curbside visit, please enter it here.
* This is optional but helps us understand the timing of your visit.
14. **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our curbside service on a scale of 1 to 5.
* If you're very satisfied, select 5 stars, and if you're not satisfied, select 1 star.
15. **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or feedback, please share them here.
* This is optional but helpful for us to understand your experience.
16. **Customer Feedback**: Share your overall feedback about your curbside service experience.
* Be as detailed as possible, but keep your comment concise.
17. **Service Level**: Select how you perceived the service level during your visit:
* High: The service was prompt and efficient.
* Medium: The service was average.
* Low: The service was slow or below expectations.
18. **Communication Level**: Rate the communication level during your visit:
* Good: The staff communicated clearly and effectively.
* Fair: The staff's communication was average.
* Poor: The staff's communication was unclear or unhelpful.
19. **Curbside Service Team**: Select whether you'd like to be contacted by our curbside service team again:
* True: Yes, we'd like to be contacted again.
* False: No, we're not interested in being contacted again.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to help us understand your experience.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know in the "Comments" field.
- We value your feedback and appreciate your time in completing this form.

By following these instructions, you'll be able to provide us with valuable feedback and help us improve our curbside service. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
