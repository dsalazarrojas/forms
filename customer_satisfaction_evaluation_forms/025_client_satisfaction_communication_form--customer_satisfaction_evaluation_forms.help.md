# Client Satisfaction Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from clients on their experience with the services provided, to help improve communication and services.
## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form in a few minutes by following these steps:
1. Start by providing your name and contact email in the **Client Name** and **Contact Email** fields respectively.
2. Select the **Primary Communication Channels** used to contact you.
3. Answer the questions about the adequacy, clarity, and frequency of communications.
4. Rate the **Professionalism, Personalization, and Completeness of Information** provided.
5. Provide feedback on **Expectation Setting, Proactive Updates, and Areas for Improvement**.
6. Optionally, you can provide additional **Positive Feedback** and **Areas for Improvement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Your name as the client.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Primary Communication Channels Used** (`communication_channels_used`, select_multiple, required): This is where you can select the methods used to contact you, e.g. email, phone, video calls, or in-person meetings.
* **Was Communication Frequency Adequate?** (`communication_frequency_adequacy`, select_one, required): Choose an option to indicate if communications were too frequent, just right, not frequent enough, or inconsistent.
* **Clarity of Communications** (`communication_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of communications, from very clear to very unclear.
* **Our Responsiveness to Your Communications** (`communication_responsiveness`, select_one, required): Choose an option to indicate how quickly we responded, from immediate to no response.
* **Professionalism of Communications** (`professional_tone`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of communications, from excellent to very poor.
* **Personalization of Communications** (`personalization_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate if communications felt highly personalized, well personalized, generic but appropriate, impersonal, or not personalized at all.
* **Completeness of Information Provided** (`information_completeness`, select_one, required): Rate the completeness of information provided, from all information provided to insufficient information.
* **Expectation Setting** (`expectation_setting`, select_one, required): Rate the expectation setting, from excellent to not set.
* **Proactive Updates on Status or Changes** (`proactive_updates`, select_one, optional): Choose an option to indicate if we proactively updated you on status or changes, from consistently proactive to no proactive updates.
* **Would You Like to Adjust Communication Preferences?** (`communication_preferences`, text, optional): Optionally, you can suggest changes to communication preferences, e.g., less frequent emails or more updates.
* **Areas for Improvement in Communication** (`areas_improvement`, text, optional): Provide suggestions on areas where communication could be improved.
* **What Worked Well in Our Communications?** (`positive_feedback`, text, optional): Share what worked well in our communications.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
