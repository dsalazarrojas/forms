# Financial Service Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a financial service. The information collected will help us understand your needs and preferences, and improve our service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. If you have an email address, enter it in the Email field. This is optional.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field, if you prefer.
4. Answer the question "What service did you visit?" to describe the specific service you interacted with.
5. Provide a detailed description of "What type of interaction did you have with our staff?" in the text field.
6. Answer questions 3-8 with a rating system, where you select one of the options to rate your experience. For example, "How would you rate the quality of service?"
7. If applicable, answer "Was your service issue resolved?" and select all applicable options.
8. Enter the Date and Time of your visit in the Date and Time fields.
9. If you have any additional comments, suggestions or feedback, please provide them in the "What suggestions do you have for service improvement?" field.
10. Answer questions 9-14 with a rating system, where you select one of the options to rate your experience.
11. Finally, answer the question "What is your age?" and "What is your employment status?" and "What is your annual household income?" and "How many people are in your household?" and "How many people are in your household that are 65 or older?".
