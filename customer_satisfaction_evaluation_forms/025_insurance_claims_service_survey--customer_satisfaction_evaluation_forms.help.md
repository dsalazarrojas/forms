# Insurance Claims Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the insurance claims service provided to customers. It helps the company identify areas of improvement and provide data-driven insights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer the questions according to your experience with the insurance claims service.
2. Select the relevant options for each question based on your feedback.
3. Provide additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" field if you have any comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of the claim you submitted.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the service provided. Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Delays** (`delays`, select_multiple, required/optional): Report any delays you experienced during the claims process. Options: No Delays, Delays, but minor, Significant Delays.
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the level of communication you received from the insurance company. Options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate how frequently you were communicated with during the process. Options: Frequent, Occasional, Never.
* **Resolution Level** (`resolution_level`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the level of resolution provided for your claim. Options: High, Low.
* **Resolution Speed** (`resolution_speed`, select_multiple, required/optional): Report how fast or slow the resolution process was. Options: Fast, Slow.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the service.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate your preferred contact method. Options: Phone, Email, In person.
* **Additional Contact Method** (`contact_method_additional`, text, required/optional): If you have any other preferred contact method, please specify.
* **Submit** (`submission`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your feedback.
