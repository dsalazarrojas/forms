# Clothing Brand Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect your feedback on your experience with our clothing brand. Your input will help us improve our products and services to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can use the "Any additional feedback or comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name. If you want to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This field is for your email address, which we may use to contact you for future surveys and updates.
* **-- Your Recent Purchase --** (`section_purchase`, note, optional): This section is about your last purchase from us. Please describe your recent purchase in a few words.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): This field is for the date when you made your last purchase. Please select the correct date from the calendar.
* **Where did you purchase** (`purchase_location`, select_one, required): This field asks about the location where you bought the item. Please select one of the options: Online store, Physical retail store, Pop-up shop, Third-party retailer, or Social media.
* **Type of Item Purchased** (`item_type`, select_one, required): This field asks about the type of item you purchased. Please select one of the options: Shirt or Blouse, Pants or Jeans, Dress, Jacket or Coat, Accessory, Shoes, Activewear, or Other.
* **-- Product Quality --** (`section_product`, note, optional): This section is about the quality of the product. Please rate the quality of the product you purchased.
* **How would you rate the product quality** (`product_quality`, select_one, required): This field asks about your rating of the product quality. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very poor.
* **How satisfied are you with the fit** (`fit_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This field asks about your satisfaction with the fit of the product. Please select one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate the material quality** (`material_quality`, select_one, required): This field asks about your rating of the material quality. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very poor.
* **How durable do you expect this item to be** (`durability_expectation`, select_one, required): This field asks about your expectation of the item's durability. Please select one of the options: Very durable, Somewhat durable, Average durability, Not very durable, or Likely to wear out quickly.
* **Do you feel the price was good value** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): This field asks about your perception of the price. Please select one of the options: Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Overpriced, or Very overpriced.
* **-- Shopping Experience --** (`section_experience`, note, optional): This section is about your overall shopping experience. Please rate your experience with our online store or app.
* **How easy was it to find what you needed** (`ease_of_shopping`, select_one, required): This field asks about your ease of finding what you needed. Please select one of the options: Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, or Very difficult.
* **Website or app experience was user-friendly** (`website_app_experience`, select_one, optional): This field asks about your experience with our website or app. Please select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **Checkout process was smooth and secure** (`checkout_process`, select_one, required): This field asks about your experience with the checkout process. Please select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **Shipping speed met your expectations** (`shipping_speed`, select_one, required): This field asks about the speed of your shipping. Please select one of the options: Much faster than expected, Faster than expected, As expected, Slower than expected, or Much slower than expected.
* **How would you rate the packaging quality** (`packaging_quality`, select_one, required): This field asks about the quality of the packaging. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very poor.
* **-- Brand and Loyalty --** (`section_brand`, note, optional): This section is about your thoughts on our brand.
* **Overall impression of the brand** (`brand_perception`, select_one, required): This field asks about your overall impression of the brand. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very poor.
* **Would you recommend us to others** (`recommend_brand`, select_one, required): This field asks if you would recommend our brand to others. Please select one of the options: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Maybe, Probably not, or Definitely not.
* **Likelihood of purchasing again** (`repeat_purchase`, select_one, required): This field asks about your likelihood of purchasing from us again. Please select one of the options: Very likely, Likely, Uncertain, Unlikely, or Very unlikely.
* **What do you like most about our brand** (`favorite_aspects`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select what you like most about our brand. You can select multiple options.
* **What could we improve on** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): This field is for any suggestions you have on how we can improve.
* **Any additional feedback or comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you have.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any question, please choose the option that best describes your experience.
* You can skip any questions that don't apply to you.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Any additional feedback or comments" field.
