# customer_support_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Support Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with our customer support service. This form helps us understand how we can improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your demographic information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide contact information:
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* E-mail: Enter your email address.
3. Rate your experience:
	* Rating: Choose a rating for your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
4. Select a category and subcategory for your feedback:
	* Category: Choose a category for your feedback (Customer Service, Product, Other).
	* Subcategory: Select one or more subcategories that apply to your feedback (Support Quality, Communication, Helpfulness).
5. Date and time of interaction:
	* Date: Enter the date of your interaction with our customer support service.
	* Time: Enter the time of your interaction with our customer support service.
6. Identify the tool used and the support agent:
	* Assigned Tool: Enter the tool used to resolve your issue.
	* Assigned User: Enter the name of the support agent who helped you.
7. Rate the service quality:
	* Customer Support Service: Choose a rating for the overall quality of our service (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
8. Rate your satisfaction:
	* Customer Satisfaction: Choose how satisfied you are with the service you received (Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied).
9. Provide additional comments:
	* Customer Service Improvement: Enter any suggestions for improvement for our service.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback:
	* Additional Comments: Enter any other feedback or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address.
* **Rating**: Choose a rating for your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Category**: Choose a category for your feedback (Customer Service, Product, Other).
* **Subcategory**: Select one or more subcategories that apply to your feedback (Support Quality, Communication, Helpfulness).
* **Date**: Enter the date of your interaction with our customer support service.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your interaction with our customer support service.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool used to resolve your issue.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the support agent who helped you.
* **Customer Support Service**: Choose a rating for the overall quality of our service (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Customer Service Quality**: Choose how you rate the quality of our service (Good, Very Good, Excellent).
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with the service you received (Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied).
* **Customer Service Improvement**: Enter any suggestions for improvement for our service.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other feedback or comments you have.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Output File**: Enter any file name you want to associate with the form.
* **Output File**: Enter any file name you want to associate with the form.
