# feedback_collection_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with our company.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information, which includes your Company Name, Participant Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Date of Visit, Time of Visit, and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the preferred method of communication to respond to your feedback.
3. Select the channel through which feedback will be collected, which could be via web, mailchimp, or other.
4. Optionally, you can provide your Participant ID and Employee ID for identification purposes.
5. Finally, type your feedback in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the company you are contacting.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name or the name of the participant providing feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note, Required): This is a text box where you can type your feedback, suggestions, or complaints.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where we can contact you to discuss your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you to discuss your feedback.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your visit to our office or location.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, Time, Required): Enter the time of your visit to our office or location.
* **Communication Method** (`communication_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to receive a response to your feedback, which can be phone, email, or in person.
* **Feedback Collection Channel** (`feedback_collection_channel`, Select One, Required): Choose the channel through which feedback will be collected, which can be web, mailchimp, or other.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, Text, Optional): This is an optional field for identification purposes.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Text, Optional): This is an optional field for identification purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can respond to your feedback efficiently.
* Be specific when providing feedback, as it will help us to understand your concerns better.
* If you have any additional information or attachments that support your feedback, please attach them to the email or provide them in person.

Note: The provided YAML contains a mix of fields for contact information, feedback collection method, and other optional fields. The form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward, with each field having a clear label and description.
