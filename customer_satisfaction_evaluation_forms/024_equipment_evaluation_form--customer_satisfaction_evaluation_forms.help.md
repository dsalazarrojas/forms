# equipment_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_evaluation_form is used to gather customer feedback on the performance, reliability, and overall satisfaction of equipment or services provided. This form helps in identifying areas of improvement and ensuring that the equipment meets the customer's expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the equipment or item being evaluated.
2.  Select the type of equipment or service being evaluated.
3.  Provide a rating (1-5) for the performance of the equipment based on its functionality and quality.
4.  Provide a rating (1-5) for the reliability of the equipment based on its durability and consistency.
5.  Provide a rating (1-5) for the value for money rating of the equipment, considering its price and quality.
6.  Provide a rating (1-5) for the ease of use of the equipment, considering how easy or difficult it is to operate.
7.  Provide an overall rating (1-5) of the equipment based on your satisfaction.
8.  Add any comments or feedback about the equipment or service.
9.  Select the person assigned to evaluate the equipment, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item name** (`item_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the equipment or item being evaluated.
* **Equipment type** (`equipment_type`, Text, Optional): Select the type of equipment or service being evaluated.
* **Performance rating** (`performance_rating`, Number, Optional): Provide a rating (1-5) for the performance of the equipment based on its functionality and quality.
* **Reliability rating** (`reliability_rating`, Number, Optional): Provide a rating (1-5) for the reliability of the equipment based on its durability and consistency.
* **Value for money rating** (`value_rating`, Number, Optional): Provide a rating (1-5) for the value for money rating of the equipment, considering its price and quality.
* **Ease of use** (`ease_of_use`, Number, Optional): Provide a rating (1-5) for the ease of use of the equipment, considering how easy or difficult it is to operate.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, Number, Optional): Provide an overall rating (1-5) of the equipment based on your satisfaction.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any comments or feedback about the equipment or service.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, Select One, Optional): Select the person assigned to evaluate the equipment, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough while completing the form to provide valuable feedback.
* Select the correct type of equipment or service being evaluated from the options provided.
* Use the ratings scale consistently, for example, if you rate the performance as 4, then also rate the reliability and value for money as 4.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please be specific and provide examples to support your ratings.
