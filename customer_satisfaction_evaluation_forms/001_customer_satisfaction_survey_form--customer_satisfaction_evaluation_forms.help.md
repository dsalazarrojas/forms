# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on our service. Your feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select an answer for each question on the form. You can choose from the provided options or type in your answers if a text field is available.
2. Fill in any required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide as much information as you can in the optional fields, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Any additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate our service?** (`Very Satisfied`, `Somewhat Satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat Dissatisfied`, `Very Dissatisfied`): This question helps us understand how satisfied you are with our service. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend us?** (`Likely`, `Neutral`, `Unlikely`): This question helps us gauge the likelihood of you recommending our service to others.
* **What is your name**: This is a text field where you can provide your name for our records.
* **Email address**: This is an email field where you can provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone number**: This is a text field where you can provide your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **How can we contact you?** (`Email`, `Phone`, `Phone and Email`): Please select the contact method(s) that you prefer.
* **Any additional comments**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Would you like to be contacted for a follow-up?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question helps us determine if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up on your feedback.
