# telecom_customer_service_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

The telecom_customer_service_feedback form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their interactions with our telecom service. This includes information on their satisfaction levels, the channel used to contact us, and any comments or suggestions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. If a field is not applicable or not relevant to your experience, select "Other" for interaction channel or mark it as not applicable.
3. Use the provided fields to give as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Please enter your name to help us associate your feedback with your account.
* **Interaction Channel** (`interaction_channel`, Select One, Optional): If you used our phone service, select "Phone". If you used our email service, select "Email". If you used another channel, select "Other".
* **Date of Interaction** (`interaction_date`, Date, Optional): If you're able to recall the date of your interaction with our service, please enter it.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts and comments about your experience with our telecom service.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, Number, Optional): Use a number from 1-10 to indicate your level of satisfaction with our service. 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Service Improvement** (`service_improvement`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our service, please enter them here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Optional): If you'd like to provide your contact number, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): If you'd like to provide your email address, please enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your experience, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about a field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in completing this form.
