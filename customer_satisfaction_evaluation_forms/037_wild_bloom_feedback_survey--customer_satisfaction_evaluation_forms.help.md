# Wild Bloom Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Wild Bloom Feedback Survey, a tool that helps us understand your experience with our products and services. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve, and we appreciate your time and input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Wild Bloom Feedback Survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your answers for each question. You can choose from the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
2. Provide your feedback in the text box at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, not required): This question asks about your general satisfaction with our products and services. Please select a number from 1 (Not satisfied) to 5 (Very satisfied).
* **What was your experience with our products?** (`product_experience`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all that apply from the list of options to describe your experience with our products.
	+ 1 - Not satisfied
	+ 2 - Somewhat satisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Satisfied
	+ 5 - Very satisfied
* **How do you rate the quality of our products?** (`product_quality`, select_one, not required): Select the option that best represents your assessment of our product quality.
	+ 1 - Very poor
	+ 2 - Somewhat poor
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **How do you rate our customer service?** (`customer_service`, select_one, not required): Select the option that best represents your assessment of our customer service.
	+ 1 - Very poor
	+ 2 - Somewhat poor
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **What do you think is the optimal price for our products?** (`price`, number, not required): Enter the price you believe is most suitable for our products. Note: This question is not a rating or assessment but rather an open-ended question.
* **Do you have any additional feedback?** (`feedback`, text, not required): If you have any thoughts or suggestions about our products or services, please share them here.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all that apply from the list of options for how you would like to be contacted in the future.
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ SMS/Text Message
	+ In-app Message
