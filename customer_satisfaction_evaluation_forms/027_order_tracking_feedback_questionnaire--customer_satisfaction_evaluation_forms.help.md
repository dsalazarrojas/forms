# Order Tracking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Order Tracking Feedback Questionnaire" is designed to collect information about your experience with our order tracking process. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond accordingly.
2. Answer all required questions (marked as "required: true" in the form).
3. You can skip optional questions (marked as "required: false") if you choose.
4. Enter your responses in the space provided for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How was your overall experience?
* **How was your overall experience?** (`customer_experience`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with our order tracking process.

### What was the product you ordered?
* **What was the product you ordered?** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter the specific product or item you ordered from us.

### How did we track your order?
* **How did we track your order?** (`order_tracking`, text, required): Describe how we communicated with you about the status of your order.

### How would you rate our communication?
* **How would you rate our communication?** (`communication`, select_one, required): Rate our communication as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### What was your order status update frequency?
* **What was your order status update frequency?** (`order_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of order status updates you received from us. Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never.

### Did we provide sufficient tracking information?
* **Did we provide sufficient tracking information?** (`feedback_on_tracking`, select_one, required): Answer with True or False, indicating whether you felt we provided enough tracking information.

### How effective was the tracking?
* **How effective was the tracking?** (`tracking_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of our tracking as Very, Somewhat, Not Very, or Not At All.

### Any additional comments?
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further remarks or observations about your experience, feel free to add them here.

Tips:
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.
* If you need help with any of the fields, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us and will help us improve our services.
