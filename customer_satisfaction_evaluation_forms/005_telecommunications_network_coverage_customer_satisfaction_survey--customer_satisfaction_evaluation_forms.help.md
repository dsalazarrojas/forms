# telecommunictions_network_coverage_customer_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist users in completing the telecom network coverage customer satisfaction survey form. The form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the telecom network coverage provided by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined below:

1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, taking into account your experience with the telecom network coverage.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best reflects your experience.
3. For text fields, provide your response in the space provided.
4. For email and phone number fields, enter your email address and phone number respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Telecom Network Coverage** (`telecom_network_coverage`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the telecom network coverage.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best reflects your experience with the service quality:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Network Coverage** (`network_coverage`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options that best reflect your experience with the network coverage:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **Coverage Area** (`coverage_area`, number, optional): Enter the area of coverage you are referring to.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the customer service.
* **Communication Issues** (`communication_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options that best reflect the communication issues you have encountered:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
* **Billing Issues** (`billing_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the options that best reflect the billing issues you have encountered:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best reflects your overall experience:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Customer Support** (`customer_support`, select_one, optional): Choose the option that best reflects your experience with customer support:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
