# All In One Package Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a package purchase. It will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, and **Purchase Date** fields. These are all required fields.
2. Select the **Package Purchased** field from the dropdown menu to indicate which package you purchased.
3. Choose your **Package Value** from the dropdown menu to indicate the price range of the package.
4. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction**, **Value for Money**, **Package Contents Rating**, **Quality Rating**, **Delivery/Setup Rating**, and **Customer Service Rating** using the corresponding dropdown menus.
5. Answer the **Ease of Purchase** question by selecting how easy or difficult it was to buy the package.
6. Select your level of agreement with the statement "Did it meet your expectations?" in the **Met Expectations** dropdown menu.
7. Indicate whether you would **Recommend** this package to others.
8. Answer the **Would Purchase Again** question by selecting whether you would buy this package again.
9. If you have any specific **Best Feature** that you enjoyed about the package, feel free to type it in.
10. If you have any **Areas for Improvement**, you can type in your suggestions.
11. If you think there are any **Missing Features** that you would add, type them in.
12. Compare the package to its competitors by selecting how it compares in the **Comparison to Competitors** dropdown menu.
13. Indicate how often you **Use** the package in the **Usage Frequency** dropdown menu.
14. If you encountered any **Issues**, select all that apply.
15. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments** about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: This is your contact number, optional but recommended.
* **Purchase Date**: When did you purchase the package?
* **Package Purchased**: Select the package you purchased from the dropdown menu.
* **Package Value**: Select the price range of the package from the dropdown menu.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the package.
* **Value for Money**: Rate the value you received for the price you paid.
* **Package Contents Rating**: Rate the package contents and how well they met your expectations.
* **Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of the package or service.
* **Delivery/Setup Rating**: Rate the delivery or setup process.
* **Customer Service Rating**: Rate the support you received from our customer service team.
* **Ease of Purchase**: How easy or difficult was it to buy the package?
* **Met Expectations**: Did the package meet your expectations?
* **Would Recommend**: Would you recommend this package to others?
* **Would Purchase Again**: Would you buy this package again?
* **Best Feature**: If you could pick one feature of the package, what would it be?
* **Areas for Improvement**: If there are any areas we can improve, please suggest them.
* **Missing Features**: If there are any features you would add, type them in.
* **Comparison to Competitors**: How does this package compare to its competitors?
* **Usage Frequency**: How often do you use the package?
* **Issues Encountered**: Select all that apply if you encountered any issues.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or comments about your experience.
