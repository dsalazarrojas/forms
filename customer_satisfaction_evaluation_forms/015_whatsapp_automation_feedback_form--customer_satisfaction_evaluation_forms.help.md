# WhatsApp Automation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with WhatsApp automation features and tools. Your input will help us improve and refine our product to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and concisely.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields.
4. Don't forget to select the tool you used to access this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about our WhatsApp automation features?** (`feedback_1`, `text`, required: false): Please share your overall impression of our WhatsApp automation features. Write down your thoughts, likes, and dislikes.
* **How can we improve our features?** (`feedback_2`, `text`, required: false): This is your chance to suggest ways we can improve our features. Be specific and detailed in your suggestions.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our features. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What tool did you use to submit this form?** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the tool you used to access this form. This helps us understand which tools are working well and where we need to improve.
* **What did you like about the tool?** (`feedback_3`, `text`, required: false): Share the features or aspects of the tool that you enjoyed or found useful.
* **How can we improve the tool?** (`feedback_4`, `text`, required: false): Suggest ways we can improve the tool, focusing on specific features or aspects that need enhancement.
* **How can we improve this form?** (`submission`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback on the form itself, such as its layout, fields, or overall design.
* **Do you have any other feedback?** (`feedback_5`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have that didn't fit into the previous questions.
