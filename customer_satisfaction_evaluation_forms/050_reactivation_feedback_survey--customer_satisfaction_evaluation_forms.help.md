# Reactivation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reactivation Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers who have reactivated their accounts. This survey aims to understand the customer's satisfaction with the reactivation process, their experience with our support team, and their overall perception of our services. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us identify areas for improvement and providing a better experience for you and our future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form according to the instructions provided.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Submit the form to share your feedback with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Company** (2): The name of the company you are representing.
* **Job Title** (3): Your job title as it appears on your account.
* **Department** (4): The department you belong to as it appears on your account.
* **Location** (5): The location associated with your account.
* **Contact** (6): Enter your email address or other contact method as it appears on your account. This is a required field.
* **Reactivation Survey Important** (7): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Reactivation Survey Easy to Fill** (8): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Reactivation Survey Helpful** (9): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments Important** (10): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (11): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (12): Enter any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
* **Customer Support Experience** (13): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Overall Experience** (14): Enter your rating on a scale of 1-5.
* **Time to Fill Out Survey** (15): Enter the time it took you to complete the survey.
* **How Much Time Spent on Survey** (16): Enter the time spent on the survey.
* **Would Recommend** (17): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Preferences** (18): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Communication Channels** (19): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **Reactivation Method** (20): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Email** (21): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (22): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Comments** (23): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Job Title Other** (24): Enter any other job title you'd like to use.
* **Reactivation Survey Important** (25): Select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
