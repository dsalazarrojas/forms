# Guest Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from guests about their experience at our location. We value your opinion and would like to hear about your visit, what we did well and what we could improve on.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.
2. Choose your answers carefully, as they will help us understand your experience better.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide specific details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction_form`, note): This section is a place for you to express your overall satisfaction with our service. Please write a short message to let us know how we did.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_form`, select_multiple): Select all the ratings that apply to your experience:
	+ Rating: Very Good
	+ Rating: Good
	+ Rating: Neutral
	+ Rating: Bad
	+ Rating: Very Bad
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text): Be specific and provide details about how we could improve your experience.
* **How Did You Feel During Your Visit** (`how_did_you_feel_during_your_visit`, select_one):
	+ Very Happy
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Unsatisfied
	+ Very Unsatisfied
* **What Were The Most Important Factors For You** (`what_were_the_most_important_factors_for_you`, select_multiple): Select all the factors that were most important for you:
	+ Cleanliness
	+ Friendliness
	+ Price
	+ Food Quality
	+ Service Quality
* **How Did You Get To Our Location** (`how_did_you_get_to_our_location`, select_one):
	+ Public Transport
	+ Walk
	+ Ride-hailing
	+ Drove
	+ Other
* **How Did You Leave Our Location** (`how_did_you_leave_our_location`, select_one):
	+ Public Transport
	+ Walk
	+ Ride-hailing
	+ Drove
	+ Other
* **Do You Recommend Our Location To Others** (`do_you_recommend_our_location_to_others`, select_one):
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Would You Like To Receive Promotions** (`would_you_like_to_receive_promotions`, select_one):
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Email** (`email`, email): Please enter your email address if you would like to receive updates and promotions from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): If you would like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
