# Tech Client Feedback Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tech Client Feedback Inquiry Form is designed to collect feedback from clients after a visit or interaction with our technical team. This form will help us identify areas for improvement and ensure that our services meet the highest standards of quality and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have had a recent interaction with our technical team.
2. Choose one of the options for "Feedback Type" that best describes your experience.
3. Choose one of the options for "Product/Service" that best describes the service you received.
4. If you have any additional feedback, please type it in the "Feedback" field.
5. If applicable, indicate your preferred follow-up request.
6. Enter your contact email address.
7. If you have any further comments or observations, please note them in the "Comment" field.
8. Finally, if applicable, enter the date and time of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (Select "Yes" or "No"): This field asks if you have had a recent interaction with our technical team. Please answer honestly.
* **Feedback Type** (Select One): This field asks for the type of feedback you want to provide. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Product/Service** (Select One): This field asks for the service you received from our team. Choose the option that best describes the service.
* **Feedback** (Text): If you have any additional feedback, please type it in this field.
* **Follow-up Request** (Select One): If you have a follow-up request, please select one of the options.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your contact email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Comment** (Text): If you have any additional comments or observations, please type them in this field.
* **Date of Visit** (Date): If applicable, enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (Time): If applicable, enter the time of your visit.
* **Note** (Note): If you have any additional comments or observations, please note them in this field.

Note: Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us improve our services.
