# HR Leadership Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The HR Leadership Roundtable Registration Form is designed to collect information from HR leaders for the HR Leadership Roundtable event. This form helps the organizers plan and tailor the event according to the participants' interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2.  Provide your Job Title and the name of your Company.
3.  Select your Topic of Interest from the provided options (Leadership, HR, Communication, or Management).
4.  Choose your preferred timing for the roundtable, selecting one or more options from Morning, Afternoon, or Both.
5.  If you have any additional Notes or comments, you can write them in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### Last Name

*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### Email

*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your professional email address.

### Job Title

*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current job title in your organization.

### Company

*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.

### Topic of Interest

*   **Topic of Interest** (`topic_of_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the topic that interests you the most from Leadership, HR, Communication, or Management.

### Preferences

*   **Preferences** (`preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferred timing for the roundtable, selecting one or more options from Morning, Afternoon, or Both.

### Notes

*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or details you would like to share with the organizers.
