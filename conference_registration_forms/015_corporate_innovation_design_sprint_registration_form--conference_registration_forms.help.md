# Corporate Innovation Design Sprint Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Innovation Design Sprint Registration Form is designed to capture information from teams and participants who wish to attend a corporate innovation design sprint. This form is used to ensure that all relevant details are collected for the successful execution of the design sprint.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

- Click on the "Registration Form" page.
- Fill out the registration form fields with your team and participant details.
- Select relevant options for participant skills, interests, education, and employment.
- Enter the company details and contact information.
- Fill out additional fields such as team size, participant count, participant type, team lead information, and participant status.
- Click "Submit" to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: 
  - This field is used to fill out the registration information. Please provide your team name and details here.
  **Team Details**: 
  - Please enter the team details here, including team members' names and roles.
  **Participant Details**: 
  - Provide details about each team member, including name, role, and title.
  **Company Details**: 
  - Enter your company name, address, and other relevant information.
  **Participant Skills**: 
  - Select the relevant skills that each team member possesses.
  **Participant Interests**: 
  - Choose the interests of each team member that are relevant to the design sprint.
  **Participant Education**: 
  - Enter the education details of each team member.
  **Participant Experience**: 
  - Enter the experience details of each team member, including numbers of years of experience.
  **Participant Employment**: 
  - Select the employment status of each team member.
  **Team Leadership**: 
  - Choose the team leadership type.
  **Company Name**: 
  - Enter your company name.
  **Company Address**: 
  - Enter your company address.
  **Contact Name**: 
  - Enter the name of the contact person.
  **Contact Email**: 
  - Enter the contact email address.
  **Contact Phone**: 
  - Enter the contact phone number.
  **Notes**: 
  - Enter any additional comments or notes about your team or participant.
  **Team Size**: 
  - Enter the size of the team.
  **Participant Count**: 
  - Enter the number of participants.
  **Participant Type**: 
  - Select the type of participants.
  **Team Lead**: 
  - Select the team lead type.
  **Team Lead Title**: 
  - Enter the team lead title.
  **Participant Title**: 
  - Enter the participant title.
  **Participant Role**: 
  - Enter the participant role.
  **Participant Status**: 
  - Select the status of the participant.
  **Participant Status Date**: 
  - Enter the participant status date.
  **Participation Status**: 
  - Select the participation status of the team member.
  - **Team Lead**: 
  - Select the team lead type.
  - **Participation Status Date**: 
  - Enter the participation status date.

**Tips**

- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to ensure a successful registration process.
- Double-check all details before submitting the form.
- In case of any doubts or issues, please contact the relevant contact person.
