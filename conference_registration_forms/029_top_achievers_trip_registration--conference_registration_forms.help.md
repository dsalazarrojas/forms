# Top Achievers Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for conference participants to register their trip details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's details in the **Participant Details** section.
2. Select the preferred travel class in the **Trip Preferences** section.
3. Choose the accommodation type in the **Accommodation** section.
4. Fill in the flight details in the **Flight Details** section.
5. Select the travel document type in the **Travel Document** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email.
* **Participant Phone Number**: Enter the participant's phone number.

### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email.
* **Participant Phone Number**: Enter the participant's phone number.

### Trip Preferences
* **Travel Class**: Select the preferred travel class (Economy, Business, First Class).

### Accommodation
* **Accommodation Type**: Choose the accommodation type (Hotel, Hosted, Self Catering).

### Travel Details
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the departure date.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the arrival date.

### Travel Document
* **Travel Document Type**: Select the travel document type.
* **Travel Document Attachment**: Attach any relevant travel documents.

### Flight Preferences
* **Flight Class**: Select the flight class (Economy, Business, First Class).

### Flight Details
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number.
* **Flight Time**: Enter the flight time.
* **Flight Air Port**: Enter the flight airport.

### Travel Dates
* **Departure Date**: Enter the departure date.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the arrival date.

### Flight Class
* **Flight Class**: Select the flight class (Economy, Business, First Class).

### Flight Air Port
* **Flight Air Port**: Enter the flight airport.

### Travel Document Attachment
* **Travel Document Attachment**: Attach any relevant travel documents.

### Flight Type
* **Flight Type**: Enter the flight type.

### Departure
* **Departure Date**: Enter the departure date.

### Travel Class
* **Travel Class**: Select the travel class.

### Accommodation Class
* **Accommodation Class**: Choose the accommodation class.

### Travel Document Note
* **Travel Document Note**: Enter any relevant travel documents notes.

### Submit
