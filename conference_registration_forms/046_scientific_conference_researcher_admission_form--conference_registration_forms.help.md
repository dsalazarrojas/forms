# Scientific Conference Researcher Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from researchers interested in presenting their work at a scientific conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal and contact information in the General Information section.
2. Provide details about your presentation in the Presenter Information section.
3. Submit your form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the event where you will be presenting.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your professional email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, false): Enter your company or institution's name.
* **Are you a presenter?** (`presenter`, select_one, false): Select one of the options to indicate whether you are a presenter or not.
* **Paper ID** (`paper_id`, number, false): Enter the ID of your paper.
* **Paper Title** (`paper_title`, text, false): Enter a brief title of your paper.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, select_one, false): Select one of the options for the session name.
* **Paper Status** (`paper_status`, select_multiple, false): Select one or more of the options for the paper status.
* **Paper Status Hint** (`paper_status_hint`, note, false): This is a hint for paper status.
* **Presenter Request** (`presenter_request`, select_one, false): Select one of the options for presenter request.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, false): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the conference.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, false): Enter the area of your research.
* **Presenter Info** (`presenter_info`, text, false): Provide additional information about yourself as a presenter.
* **Paper Submission Status** (`paper_submission_status`, select_one, false): Select one of the options for paper submission status.
* **Paper Submission Date** (`paper_submission_date`, date, false): Enter the date of paper submission.
* **Presenter Request Hint** (`presenter_request_hint`, note, false): This is a hint for presenter request.
* **Paper ID** (`paper_id`, text, false): Enter the ID of your paper again.
* **Paper Info** (`paper_info`, text, false): Provide additional information about your paper.
* **Paper Abstract** (`paper_abstract`, text, false): Enter a brief summary of your paper.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, false): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, text, false): Enter the ID of the event.
* **Researcher Info** (`researcher_info`, text, false): Provide additional information about yourself as a researcher.
* **Paper Submission Info** (`paper_submission_info`, text, false): Provide additional information about your paper submission.
* **Conference ID** (`conference_id`, text, false): Enter the ID of the conference.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, false): Provide additional information about the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers.
