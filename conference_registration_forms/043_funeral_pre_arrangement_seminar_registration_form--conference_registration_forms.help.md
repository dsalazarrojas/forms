# Funeral Pre Arrangement Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funeral Pre Arrangement Seminar Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who are registering for the seminar. This form aims to understand the registrant's demographics and relationship with the individual being registered for, and to confirm the accuracy of their contact and address details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for registration purposes.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and seminar updates.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for seminar organizers to contact you.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your full address for seminar organizers to send information and materials.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside for seminar organizers to tailor the experience.
- **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code for seminar organizers to identify your location.
- **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Address** (`confirm_address`, text, optional): Confirm your address to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm City** (`confirm_city`, text, optional): Confirm your city of residence to ensure accuracy.
- **Confirm Zip** (`confirm_zip`, number, optional): Confirm your zip code to ensure accuracy.
- **Name Of Attendant** (`name_of_attendant`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual you are registering for.
- **Relation to Attendant** (`relation_to_attendant`, select_one, optional): Choose how you relate to the individual you are registering for (e.g., Spouse, Child, Parent, Other).
- **Will Become Attendant** (`will_become_attendant`, select_one, optional): Answer whether you will become an attendant in the future (True or False).
