# Beauty Expo Media Credential Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Expo Media Credential Application form is used to gather information from media representatives to ensure they have the necessary credentials and arrangements in place for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal and professional information in the Applicant Information section.
2. Provide details about your media outlet and coverage plans in the Organization Information and Media Coverage Details sections.
3. Confirm your credentials and parking arrangements in the Credential Verification and Technical Requirements sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Applicant Information --

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): This is a required field where you must enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Title and Position** (`Title and Position`, `text`, required): Enter your job title and position at your media outlet.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address associated with your media outlet.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.

### -- Organization Information --

* **Organization/Media Outlet Name** (`Organization/Media Outlet Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your media outlet.
* **Organization Type** (`Organization Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of media outlet you represent (Print Publication, Online Publication, Broadcast, Blog, Social Media, Trade Media, or Other).
* **Circulation or Audience Reach** (`Circulation or Audience Reach`, `text`, required): Enter the number of readers or viewers your media outlet reaches per month or year.
* **Organization Headquarters Location** (`Organization Headquarters Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your media outlet's headquarters.

### -- Media Coverage Details --

* **Coverage Focus Areas** (`Coverage Focus Areas`, `text`, required): Enter the areas you plan to cover during the event (Beauty, fashion, lifestyle, health, etc.).
* **Previous Expo or Event Coverage** (`Previous Expo or Event Coverage`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have covered the Beauty Expo or a similar event in the past.
* **Describe Previous Coverage Experience** (`Describe Previous Coverage Experience`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your previous experience covering the Beauty Expo or similar events.
* **Type of Coverage Planned** (`Type of Coverage Planned`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of coverage you plan to do during the event (Written Articles, Photography, Video, Social Media, Live Streaming, or Interviews).
* **Story Ideas or Angles** (`Story Ideas or Angles`, `text`, required): Enter the story ideas or angles you plan to cover during the event.

### -- Credential Verification --

* **Valid Press Credentials on File** (`Valid Press Credentials on File`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have valid press credentials on file.
* **Press Credential Number** (`Press Credential Number`, `text`, optional): Enter your press credential number if applicable.
* **Credential Expiration Date** (`Credential Expiration Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the expiration date of your press credentials if applicable.

### -- Social Media Presence --

* **Instagram Handle** (`Instagram Handle`, `text`, optional): Enter your Instagram handle if applicable.
* **Instagram Follower Count** (`Instagram Follower Count`, `number`, optional): Enter your Instagram follower count if applicable.
* **Twitter/X Handle** (`Twitter/X Handle`, `text`, optional): Enter your Twitter/X handle if applicable.
* **Twitter/X Follower Count** (`Twitter/X Follower Count`, `number`, optional): Enter your Twitter/X follower count if applicable.
* **YouTube Channel** (`YouTube Channel`, `text`, optional): Enter your YouTube channel if applicable.
* **YouTube Subscriber Count** (`YouTube Subscriber Count`, `number`, optional): Enter your YouTube subscriber count if applicable.

### -- Technical Requirements --

* **Parking Required** (`Parking Required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need parking arrangements for the event.
* **Press Kit Needed** (`Press Kit Needed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need press kit materials for the event.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`Special Accommodations Needed`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of any special accommodations you need.
