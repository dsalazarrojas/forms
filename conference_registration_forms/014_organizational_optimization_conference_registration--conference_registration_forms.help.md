# Organizational Optimization Conference Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to register and provide details for the Organizational Optimization Conference. It collects information on your session preferences, contact details, and specific requirements you may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Registration Form to provide your basic details.
2. On the Session Preferences form, select the sessions you are interested in attending.
3. On the Session Selection form, choose specific sessions you want to attend.
4. On the Sessions form, select all sessions that apply to you.
5. Enter your Contact Email address so we can reach you.
6. Enter your Contact Phone number so we can reach you.
7. If you have any dietary requirements, provide them in the Dietary Requirements form.
8. Add any additional notes in the Notes form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (1)**: Provide your basic details here.
* **Session Preferences (2)**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending. This will help us understand your interests and plan accordingly.
* **Session (3)**: Choose specific sessions you want to attend from the list provided.
* **Sessions (4)**: Select all sessions that apply to you. This will help us understand your interests and plan accordingly.
* **Contact Email (5)**: Enter your email address so we can reach you with conference updates and information.
* **Contact Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you with last-minute updates and information.
* **Dietary Requirements (7)**: If you have any dietary requirements, please specify them here. This will help us arrange for your meals and accommodations.
* **Notes (8)**: Add any additional notes or comments you have. This is an open field for any other information you want to share with us.
