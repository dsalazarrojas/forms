# Retail Payment Tech Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for the Retail Payment Tech Event. It is intended for attendees who have been properly invited to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your event name in the "Event name" field.
2. Enter your start and end dates of attendance in the "Start date" and "End date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your payment method from the dropdown menu in the "Payment method" field.
7. Enter the amount you intend to pay in the "Payment amount" field.
8. Enter any additional details about your registration in the "Additional details" field.
9. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes, I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions" in the "Agreement to terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event name** (Event name, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Start date** (Start date, date, required): Enter the date on which you will start attending the event.
* **End date** (End date, date, required): Enter the date on which you will end attending the event.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment method** (Payment method, select multiple, optional): Select the method by which you will be making payment for the event.
	+ Bank transfer: Transfer money from your bank account.
	+ Card: Pay by credit card.
	+ Cash: Pay in cash.
* **Payment amount** (Payment amount, number, optional): Enter the amount you intend to pay for the event.
* **Payment reference** (Payment reference, text, optional): Enter any payment reference.
* **Event preferences** (Event preferences, select one, optional): Select whether you will attend the event or not.
	+ Yes, I will attend the event
	+ No, I will not attend the event
* **Additional details** (Additional details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your registration.
* **Agreement** (Agreement, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Terms and conditions** (Terms and conditions, text, required): This is where you can find the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Agreement to terms and conditions** (Agreement to terms and conditions, select one, required): Select "Yes, I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions" to confirm your agreement.
* **Contact person** (Contact person, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact number** (Contact number, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Company** (Company, text, optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **Position** (Position, text, optional): Enter your position in the company.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter the department of your company.
* **Job title** (Job title, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Company name** (Company name, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Team size** (Team size, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Team lead** (Team lead, text, optional): Enter the name of the team lead.
* **Team email** (Team email, email, optional): Enter the email of your team.
* **Team website** (Team website, text, optional): Enter the website of your team.
* **Team address** (Team address, text, optional): Enter the address of your team.
* **Team city** (Team city, text, optional): Enter the city of your team.
* **Team state** (Team state, text, optional): Enter the state of your team.
* **Team zip** (Team zip, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your team.
* **Team country** (Team country, text, optional): Enter the country of your team.
* **Team note** (Team note, text, optional): Enter any note about your team.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only complete this form if you have been properly invited to the event.
* Double-check the accuracy of your input before submission.
* Be clear and concise in your answers.
