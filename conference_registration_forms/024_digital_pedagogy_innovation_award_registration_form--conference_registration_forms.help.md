# Digital Pedagogy Innovation Award Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering your innovative pedagogical practice for the Digital Pedagogy Innovation Award. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the submitter information section, which includes your name and email address.
2. In the main category section, select the relevant category for your innovative pedagogical practice.
3. In the practice description section, provide a clear and concise description of your innovative pedagogical practice.
4. In the submission sections, answer each question in as much detail as possible, providing any relevant supporting materials or resources.
5. Finally, review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name? (`submitter_info`):** Enter your name to associate with your submission.
* **What is your email address? (`submitter_email`):** Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Which category does your innovative pedagogical practice belong to (`main_category`):** Select the main category for your practice from the given options.
* **Please describe your innovative pedagogical practice (`award_title`):** Provide a clear and concise description of your practice.
* **What inspired you to submit this innovative pedagogical practice (`submission_1`):** Explain what motivated you to share your practice with the community.
* **How did you implement your innovative pedagogical practice in your teaching (`submission_2`):** Describe how you have applied your practice in your classroom or teaching environment.
* **Which submitter organisation? (`submitter_organisation`):** Enter the name of the organisation you represent.
* **What is the name of your submission? (`submission_3`):** Provide a brief title for your submission.
* **Which subcategory does your innovative pedagogical practice belong to (`subcategory`):** Select the relevant subcategory for your practice.
* **Can you provide more details about your innovative pedagogical practice? (`submission_4`):** Expand on your practice, providing any relevant information or resources.
* **How will your innovative pedagogical practice benefit the students? (`benefits`):** Explain the potential benefits of your practice for students.
* **Is this a team or individual submission? (`submission_5`):** Indicate whether this is a team or individual effort.
* **What challenges did you face when implementing your innovative pedagogical practice? (`submission_6`):** Share any obstacles or difficulties you encountered.
* **How do you intend to measure the impact of your innovative pedagogical practice? (`submission_7`):** Describe your methods for evaluating the effectiveness of your practice.
* **Are you willing to share any supporting materials or resources about your innovative pedagogical practice? (`submission_8`):** Provide any relevant resources or materials that can support your submission.
* **What do you think will be the potential benefits and limitations of your innovative pedagogical practice for the students? (`submission_9`):** Reflect on the potential outcomes and limitations of your practice for students.
