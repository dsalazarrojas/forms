# Fireside Chat Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fireside Chat Registration form is designed to collect information from users for a fireside chat conference. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fireside Chat Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your User Info, including any relevant details that will help us get to know you better.
2. Next, select your talk interests by choosing from the available options (AI/ML, Data Science, Computer Vision).
3. Indicate your engagement preferences by selecting one or more of the available options (Email, Phone, In-Person).
4. If you have any specific interest in a topic, provide more details in the Topic Interest field.
5. If you are registering as a speaker, fill in the Speaker Details field with your relevant information.
6. If you are an attendee, fill in the Attendee Details field with your information.
7. Provide your email address in the Email field. This will be used to contact you with conference updates and information.
8. If you have a phone number, enter it in the Phone field.
9. Confirm your email address by selecting one of the options (Yes, No).
10. Finally, read and acknowledge the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information that will help us get to know you better.
* **Talk Interests** (`talk_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from the available options (AI/ML, Data Science, Computer Vision) to indicate your areas of interest.
* **Engagement Preferences** (`engagement_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Select one or more of the available options (Email, Phone, In-Person) to indicate how you would like to engage with the conference.
* **Topic Interest** (`topic_interest`, number, required: false): Enter a number that corresponds to your level of interest in a specific topic.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required: false): Select if you are registering as a Speaker or Attendee.
* **Topic Interest Details** (`topic_interests_details`, text, required: false): Provide more details about your topic interest.
* **Speaker Details** (`speaker_details`, note, required: false): If registering as a Speaker, provide your speaker bio or other relevant information.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false): If registering as an Attendee, provide your attendee bio or other relevant information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive conference updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your email address by selecting one of the options (Yes, No).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: false): Read and acknowledge the Terms and Conditions to complete the form.

## Tips
Remember to fill in all fields accurately and thoroughly, as this information will help us tailor the conference to your needs and preferences. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
