# professional_development_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a professional development workshop. It collects the necessary information for the workshop organizers to communicate with participants and facilitate a smooth workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the registration number (if required for your workshop).
*   Provide your first and last name.
*   Enter your email address to confirm that it's accurate.
*   Enter your phone number (if required for your workshop).
*   Select the type of workshop you are interested in attending.
*   Choose a date and time for the workshop (if available).
*   Select a tool for communication (e.g., chatjimmy, Google Forms, or Paper Trail).
*   Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
*   Confirm that your email address is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Number** (1, text): Enter your registration number if required for your workshop.
*   **First Name** (2, text): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (3, text): Enter your last name.
*   **Email Address** (4, email): Enter your email address to confirm that it's accurate.
*   **Phone Number** (5, text): Enter your phone number (if required for your workshop).
*   **Workshop Type** (6, select_one): Select the type of workshop you are interested in attending.
*   **Date** (7, date): Select a date for the workshop (if available).
*   **Time** (8, time): Select a time for the workshop (if available).
*   **Registration Date** (9, date): Enter the date you registered for the workshop (optional).
*   **Additional Comments** (10, note): Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
*   **Confirm Email** (11, select_one): Confirm that your email address is accurate.
*   **Assigned Tool** (12, text): Enter the tool assigned for communication.
*   **Confirm Tool** (13, select_one): Confirm the tool you selected for communication.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Double-check your selection for the workshop type, date, and time.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, use the additional comments field to provide more information.
*   Confirm that your email address is correct to avoid any communication issues.
