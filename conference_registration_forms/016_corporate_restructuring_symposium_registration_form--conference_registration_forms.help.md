# Corporate Restructuring Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Restructuring Symposium Registration Form is an online registration form designed for attendees to register for the symposium. This form is intended for individuals affiliated with a company or organization interested in corporate restructuring strategies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure this is a valid email address you can be contacted at.
4. Provide the name of your company in the "Company" field.
5. Select your role in the "Role" field. This will help us understand your perspective on corporate restructuring.
6. Enter your department in the "Department" field (if applicable).
7. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field (if applicable).
8. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the URL of your company in the "Company URL" field (if applicable).
10. Enter your company's address in the "Company Address" field (if applicable).
11. Describe your job function in the "Job Function" field (if applicable).
12. Indicate your company's sector in the "Business Sector" field (if applicable).
13. Enter the number of employees in your company in the "Number of Employees" field (if applicable).
14. Enter your company's revenue in the "Company Revenue" field (if applicable).
15. Indicate your industry in the "Industry" field. This will help us understand your area of expertise.
16. Select the type of company you represent in the "Company Type" field. This will help us understand your company's structure.
17. Enter your time zone in the "Time Zone" field (if applicable).
18. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field (if applicable).
19. Select the time of the event in the "Event Time" field (if applicable).
20. Enter the registration fee in the "Registration Fee" field (if applicable).
21. Make any special requests in the "Special Requests" field (if applicable).
22. Enter any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field (if applicable).
23. Confirm the terms of agreement in the "Agreement" field (if applicable).
24. Select the contact person from the list (if applicable).
25. Confirm your acceptance of the terms in the "Terms Accepted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address you can be contacted at.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role within your company (CEO, Manager, Employee).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your department within the company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your job title within the company.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your contact number.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the URL of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the address of your company.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): If applicable, describe your job function within the company.
* **Business Sector** (`business_sector`, text, optional): If applicable, indicate your company's sector.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Company Revenue** (`company_revenue`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your company's revenue.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your company's industry (Technology, Finance, Retail).
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Select your company type (Public, Private, Non-Profit).
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, optional): If applicable, enter your time zone.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If applicable, select the time of the event.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the registration fee.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If applicable, make any special requests.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any comments or feedback.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, optional): If applicable, confirm the terms of agreement.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_multiple, required): Select the contact person from the list (John Doe, Jane Doe, Bob Smith).
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm your acceptance of the terms.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form to avoid incomplete submissions.
* Double-check the fields you fill in for accuracy.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out fields that allow text input.
* If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the organizers of the symposium for clarification.
