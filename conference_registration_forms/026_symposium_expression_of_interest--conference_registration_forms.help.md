# symposium_expression_of_interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The symposium_expression_of_interest form is designed to collect information from participants about their interest in attending a symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: Enter your details, including any required or specified information.
- **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
- **Topic Of Interest**: Select the topic that best aligns with your goals and expectations from the symposium.
  *This field allows you to express your interest in a specific topic. The options are likely to be related to the theme or focus of the symposium.*
- **Conference Interest**: Choose the conferences you are interested in attending. 
  *This field allows you to specify the conferences you are interested in. The options are likely to be specific conferences or events.*
- **Participant Type**: Select your role or position at the symposium. 
  *This field indicates your affiliation or purpose of attending the symposium.*
- **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
  *This field requires your email address, which will likely be used as your primary contact information.*
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  *This field requires your phone number, which will be used for further communication.*
- **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information relevant to your participation in the symposium.
  *This field is for any extra information that you feel is necessary to include.*
- **Submission Date**: Select the date you intend to submit your application.
  *This field is likely to be the date when you plan to submit your completed form.*
- **Submission Time**: Select the time you intend to submit your application.
  *This field is likely to be the time when you plan to submit your completed form.*
- **Participant Details 1**, **Participant Details 2**: Enter any additional details as required or specified.
  *These fields are for any extra participant information you may need to provide, as per specified requirements.*
