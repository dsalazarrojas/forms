# Virtual Conference Participant Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Virtual Conference Participant Consent Form is a survey designed to collect information from participants attending a virtual conference. It aims to gather essential details about each participant, including their consent to participate, experience with virtual events, and any concerns or comments they may have. Completing this form will help us understand your preferences and needs, ensuring that our virtual conference is a valuable and enjoyable experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out this form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2.  For questions with multiple options, select only one response that best describes your situation.
3.  If you have any concerns or comments, use the text fields to express them clearly.
4.  Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, text, required): This field is for you to indicate whether you will participate in the virtual conference. Please select "I will participate" if you plan to join us.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Here, please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one, to help us reach you with conference updates and information.
*   **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): If you wish to share your address, please do so here.
*   **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number, if you have one, to ensure we can contact you in case of any last-minute updates.
*   **Parent Email** (`Parent Email`, text, optional): If you're attending with a parent or guardian, please provide their email address here.
*   **Will you participate?** (`participant_conference_participation`, select_one, required): Select "I will participate" if you plan to join the virtual conference.
*   **Virtual Event Experience** (`participant_virtual_conference_experience`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your experience with virtual events:
    *   I have extensive experience with virtual events
    *   I have some experience with virtual events
    *   I have little experience with virtual events
    *   I have no experience with virtual events
*   **Concerns** (`participant_conference_concerns`, text, optional): Share any concerns or questions you have about the virtual conference.
*   **Additional Comments** (`participant_conference_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them here.
*   **Participant Comments** (`Participant Comments`, text, optional): This is a space for any comments or feedback about the virtual conference.
*   **Parent Comments** (`Parent Comments`, text, optional): If you're attending with a parent or guardian, please share their comments here.
*   **Will you participate?** (`participant_conference_participation_2`, text, optional): This is a duplicate question for validation purposes. Please ignore it and do not answer it.
