# Accessibility In Care Design Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from participants interested in attending the "Accessibility In Care Design Workshop." This form helps to ensure that each participant has a smooth and inclusive experience during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, professional title, organization, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and professional background.
3. Select your workshop session choice (morning, afternoon, both, or virtual attendance).
4. Specify any accessibility needs you may have.
5. Choose which session you would like to attend (morning or afternoon).
6. Indicate your dietary restrictions if any.
7. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
8. Specify your primary objectives for attending the workshop.
9. Answer questions about your previous training or experience with accessibility.
10. Select topics you are most interested in learning about.
11. Describe the outcomes you hope to achieve from the workshop.
12. Specify the source of funding for your attendance.
13. Make any special requests you may have.
14. Provide consent for recordings if the workshop is filmed.
15. Share any additional comments or information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID. 
* **Professional Title**: Your job title or role in your organization. 
* **Organization**: Name of your company, institution, or organization. 
* **Email Address**: Your professional email address. 
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number. 
* **Professional Background**: Your area of expertise or work experience. 
* **Workshop Session Choice**: Select which session you would like to attend (morning, afternoon, both, or virtual). 
* **Accessibility Needs**: Specify any special accommodations or requirements you may need during the workshop. 
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies you have. 
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of your emergency contact person. 
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of your emergency contact person. 
* **Workshop Objectives**: Specify the primary reasons for attending the workshop. 
* **Previous Training**: Indicate your level of training or experience with accessibility. 
* **Specific Topics of Interest**: Select the topics you are most interested in learning about during the workshop. 
* **Expected Outcomes**: Describe the outcomes you hope to achieve from the workshop. 
* **Funding Source**: Indicate the source of funding for your attendance. 
* **Special Requests**: Make any special requests you may have for the workshop. 
* **Consent to Recordings**: Specify if you consent to being recorded if the workshop is filmed. 
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional information you would like to share about your experience or preferences. 

# Tips

* Ensure that you complete the form accurately and carefully to ensure that your needs are met.
* If you have any special requests, please make sure to note them clearly.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
