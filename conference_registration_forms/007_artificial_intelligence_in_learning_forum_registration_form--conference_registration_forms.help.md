# Artificial Intelligence In Learning Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees to the Artificial Intelligence In Learning Forum. It collects information about the attendees, their interests, and their needs, to provide the best experience for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as **Required**.
2. Select all the options that apply to your situation.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, Required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (text, Optional): If you want to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Organization or Institution** (text, Required): If you are affiliated with an organization or institution, enter the name.
* **Job Title or Role** (text, Required): Enter your job title or role in your organization.
* **Attendee Type** (select_one, Required): Choose the category that best describes you:
	+ **Educator**: A teacher, professor, or academic.
	+ **Administrator**: A school administrator, department head, or similar role.
	+ **Researcher**: A researcher in AI or related fields.
	+ **Technology Provider**: A vendor, supplier, or technical expert.
	+ **Student**: A student in AI-related fields.
	+ **Industry Professional**: A professional working in AI-related fields.
* **Experience with AI in Education** (select_one, Required): Choose the level of your experience with AI in education:
	+ **No Experience**: None.
	+ **Beginner**: Basic understanding of AI concepts.
	+ **Intermediate**: Understanding of AI basics and some applications.
	+ **Advanced**: Significant experience and expertise in AI.
	+ **Expert**: Advanced understanding and application of AI in education.
* **Topics of Interest** (select_multiple, Optional): Choose all the topics you are interested in:
	+ **Machine Learning in Classrooms**
	+ **Natural Language Processing**
	+ **Personalized Learning**
	+ **Ethical AI**
	+ **AI Tools and Platforms**
	+ **Research and Innovation**
* **Registration Date** (date, Required): Choose the date you will be attending the event.
* **Ticket Type** (select_one, Required): Choose the type of ticket that best suits your needs:
	+ **Full Conference Pass**: Full access to all conference events.
	+ **Single Day Pass**: Access to specific days of the event.
	+ **Virtual Attendance**: Participate remotely.
	+ **Student Rate**: Discounted rate for students.
	+ **Academic Rate**: Discounted rate for academics or students.
* **Dietary Requirements** (select_multiple, Optional): If you have dietary restrictions, choose the options that apply:
	+ **Vegetarian**
	+ **Vegan**
	+ **Gluten-Free**
	+ **Kosher**
	+ **Halal**
	+ **None** (no dietary restrictions)
* **Accessibility Requirements** (text, Optional): If you have any special accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Interested in Networking Activities** (select_one, Optional): Choose if you are interested in networking activities:
	+ **Yes**
	+ **No**
* **Opt-in to Follow-up Communications** (select_one, Optional): Choose if you want to receive follow-up communications:
	+ **Yes**
	+ **No**
* **Additional Comments** (text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.

Please fill out the form accurately, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
