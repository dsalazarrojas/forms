# Virtual Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Conference Registration Form is used to collect registration information for attendees, speakers, exhibitors, staff, and students for a virtual conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in all the required fields, marked as such.
2.  Select the correct attendee type from the options provided.
3.  Enter your conference name and date if applicable.
4.  Enter your registration fee if applicable.
5.  Verify your contact information is accurate.
6.  Review the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
7.  Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **contact_number** (Text): Enter your contact number.
* **type** (Select One):
  *   **Attendee**: If you're attending the conference as a participant.
  *   **Speaker**: If you're presenting at the conference.
  *   **Exhibitor**: If you're exhibiting at the conference.
  *   **Staff**: If you're a staff member of the conference.
  *   **Student**: If you're a student attending the conference.
* **conference** (Text): Enter the name of the conference you're attending.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date of the conference.
* **registration_fees** (Number): Enter the registration fee (if applicable).
* **code** (Text): Enter a registration code (if applicable).
* **submit** (Text): This is a submit button to submit your registration.
* **confirm** (Text): This is a confirmation field to confirm your submission.
* **error** (Note): This field is for error messages.
* **terms** (Note): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **policy** (Note): Review and agree to the privacy policy.
* **link_to_conference** (Text): Enter the URL of the conference.
* **label_to_conference** (Text): Enter a label for the conference URL.
* **link_to_terms** (Text): Enter the URL of the terms and conditions.
* **label_to_terms** (Text): Enter a label for the terms and conditions URL.
* **link_to_privacy** (Text): Enter the URL of the privacy policy.
* **label_to_privacy** (Text): Enter a label for the privacy policy URL.
* **user** (Text): Enter your user ID.
* **email** (Text): Enter your email address.
* **ip** (Text): Enter your IP address.
* **browser** (Text): Enter your browser type.
* **os** (Text): Enter your operating system.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please ensure that required fields are correctly filled in before submitting to avoid errors.
