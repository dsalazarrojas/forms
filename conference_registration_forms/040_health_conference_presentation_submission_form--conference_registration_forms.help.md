# Health Conference Presentation Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for presenters to submit their presentation information for the health conference. It allows us to gather essential details about the speaker and the session they will present.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options from the available choices for the session type, presentation type, and submit options.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Information (1)**: Fill in this field with a brief description of your session.
* **Presenter Information (2)**: Provide your name, affiliation, phone number, email, title, position, and organizational information.
* **Presentation Type (3)**: Choose the type of presentation you will be giving, such as Presentation, Panel, or Symposium.
* **Abstract (4)**: Summarize your presentation in about 250 words or less.
* **Presenter Bio (5)**: Briefly describe your background and qualifications in about 100 words or less.
* **Speaker Experience (6)**: List your relevant experience in the field, especially in presentations or talks.
* **Session Title (7)**: Enter the title of your session.
* **Session Description (8)**: Summarize your session in about 250 words or less.
* **Session Date (16)**: Choose a date for your session.
* **Session Time (17)**: Choose a time for your session.
* **Presentation Length (18)**: Select the length of your presentation, in minutes.
* **Session Type (19)**: Choose the type of session you will be presenting, such as Plenary, Concurrent, or Poster.
* **Presenter Affiliation (20)**: Enter your affiliation or institution.
* **Presenter Affiliation 2 (21)**: Enter any other affiliations or institutions.
* **Presenter Affiliation 3 (22)**: Enter any other affiliations or institutions.
* **Submit (9)**: Review your information and submit the form. 

Note: Please double-check your information before submitting, as it will be used to create the schedule and other conference materials.
