# Supply Chain Digitalization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to provide information about themselves and their companies for the Supply Chain Digitalization Conference. This information will be used to tailor the conference content and organization to your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your role in the supply chain:
	* Choose the option that best describes your job function within the supply chain.
2. Fill in your company details:
	* Enter your company's name.
	* Enter your company's address.
3. Provide contact information:
	* Enter your email address.
	* Enter your contact number.
4. Describe your company:
	* Choose the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large).
	* Describe any current projects you are working on.
	* Enter your company's website (if available).
5. Social Media Links:
	* Enter your company's Facebook page (if available).
	* Enter your company's Twitter page (if available).
	* Enter your company's LinkedIn page (if available).
	* Enter your company's Instagram page (if available).
	* Enter your company's YouTube page (if available).
6. Registration preferences:
	* Select the type of registration (Program, Workshop, Consultation).
	* Choose the date you would like to register for.
7. Provide more information about your company:
	* Choose the type of company you work for (Private, Public, Non-Profit).
	* Enter your industry.
	* Enter your location.
	* Enter your country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in the supply chain**: This field is to select your job function within the supply chain.
	+ Options: Procurement Specialist, Supply Chain Manager, Logistics Coordinator, Other
* **Company Name**: This field is to enter your company's name.
* **Company Address**: This field is to enter your company's address.
* **Email Address**: This field is to enter your email address.
	+ Format: [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com)
* **Contact Number**: This field is to enter your contact number.
* **Company Size**: This field is to select the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Current Projects**: This field is to describe any current projects you are working on.
* **Company Website**: This field is to enter your company's website (if available).
* **Company Facebook**: This field is to enter your company's Facebook page (if available).
* **Company Twitter**: This field is to enter your company's Twitter page (if available).
* **Company LinkedIn**: This field is to enter your company's LinkedIn page (if available).
* **Company Instagram**: This field is to enter your company's Instagram page (if available).
* **Company YouTube**: This field is to enter your company's YouTube page (if available).
* **What are your Supply Chain Digitalization goals**: This field is to describe your goals related to supply chain digitalization.
* **Preferred Language**: This field is to select the language you prefer to communicate in.
* **Time Zone**: This field is to select the time zone you are in.
* **Type of registration**: This field is to select the type of registration (Program, Workshop, Consultation).
* **Registration Date**: This field is to choose the date you would like to register for.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is to provide any additional comments you have.
* **Type of Company**: This field is to select the type of company you work for (Private, Public, Non-Profit).
* **Industry**: This field is to enter your industry.
* **Location**: This field is to enter your location.
* **Country**: This field is to enter your country.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Some fields have specific formats or options that need to be selected.
* If you are unsure about any information, please refer to your company's supply chain department or your supervisor for clarification.
