# Cultural Conference Panel Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Conference Panel Booking Form is designed to gather essential details from attendees, including their selections and accessibility needs, to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the cultural conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your details in the "attendee_details" field.
2. Select the panels you'd like to attend in the "panel_selections" field.
3. If you have any accessibility needs, please select the option that best describes your requirements in the "accessibility_needs" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Select the date and time of the conference you will be attending in the "date" and "time" fields respectively.
7. If you have any special instructions or requests, please enter them in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details**: (<page_1_name>, <type>, required): Enter your name and any other details as requested.
* **Panel Selections**: (<page_2_name>, select_multiple, required): Select the panels you would like to attend.
* **Accessibility Needs**: (<page_3_name>, select_one, required): If you have any accessibility needs, please select the option that best describes your requirements.
* **Email**: (<page_4_name>, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (<page_5_name>, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: (<page_6_name>, date, optional): Select the date of the conference you will be attending.
* **Time**: (<page_7_name>, time, optional): Select the time of the conference you will be attending.
* **Note**: (<page_8_name>, note, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests, please enter them here.
