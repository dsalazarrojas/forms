# Urban Land Use Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Land Use Conference Registration Form is used to collect essential information from conference attendees. This information helps us understand who is attending the conference, their role, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your category (Speaker, Delegate, Organizer, or Sponsor).
3. Select the sessions you are interested in attending.
4. Provide your date and time of attendance.
5. Write a brief bio to help us understand your background and interests.
6. Add any additional notes if you need to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This is where you provide your name, address, and other contact details.

* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address will be used for conference communication and updates.

* **Contact Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number that we may use to contact you.

### Role Selection
* **Which category do you belong to?** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose your role at the conference: Speaker, Delegate, Organizer, or Sponsor.

### Session Selection
* **Which sessions are you interested in attending?** (`sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sessions that you would like to attend. You can choose more than one session.

### Attendance Information
* **Date of Attendance** (`date`, date, optional): Provide the date(s) you plan to attend the conference.
* **Time of Attendance** (`time`, time, optional): Provide the time(s) you plan to arrive and leave the conference.

### Bio and Notes
* **Brief Bio** (`bio`, text, optional): Write a brief bio to help us understand your background and interests.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.
