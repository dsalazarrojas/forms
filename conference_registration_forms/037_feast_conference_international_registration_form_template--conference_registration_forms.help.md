# Feast Conference International Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Feast Conference International Registration Form Template is used to collect information from attendees for the conference. It includes fields for basic contact information, preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Add your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your Date of Birth from the Date Of Birth field.
6. Choose your preferred role at the conference (Attend, Speak, or Organize) in the What do you want to do at the conference? field.
7. Select all the options that apply to your interests (Worship, Learn, Network, or Socialize) in the What are your interests? field.
8. If you have any specific Time constraints, please enter them in the Time field.
9. Add any Notes about your registration in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **What do you want to do at the conference?** (`select_one_1`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your role at the conference (Attend, Speak, or Organize).
	+ This field is required for registration.
* **What are your interests?** (`select_multiple_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your interests (Worship, Learn, Network, or Socialize).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): If you have any specific time constraints, enter them here.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or details about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): This field is not used in the registration process.

Tips:

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact the conference organizers.
* You can only submit this form once per person.
