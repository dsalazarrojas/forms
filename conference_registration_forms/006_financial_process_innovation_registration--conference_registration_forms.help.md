# Financial Process Innovation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees or participants to a financial process innovation conference or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  On the "Project details" page, provide a brief description of the project you are registering for.
2.  On the "Contact information" page, enter your full contact details, including your name and any necessary contact information.
3.  On the "Registration info" page, enter any additional registration information as requested.
4.  On the "Submit and preview" page, review your entries before finalizing your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project details
*   **Project details** (`project_details`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the project you are registering for.

### Contact information
*   **Contact information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your full contact details, including your name and any necessary contact information.

### Registration info
*   **Registration info** (`registration`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional registration information as requested.

### Submit and preview
*   **Submit and preview** (`submit_and_preview`, `text`, optional): Review and finalize your registration information before submission.
 

 ## Tips
*   This form is meant for basic registration purposes. If you have any complex or specialized needs, please consider adding more fields or sections as necessary.
*   Be accurate with your contact information for proper communication with the event organizers.
*   Review your entries carefully before finalizing your registration to avoid errors.
