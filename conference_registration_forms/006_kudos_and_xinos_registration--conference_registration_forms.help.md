# Kudos-And-Xinos-Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for an event. You will need to provide information about yourself, your preferences, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Add any additional comments or notes in the "note" field.
5. Select one option from the "select-one" field.
6. Select multiple options from the "select-multiple" field.
7. Choose a date and time if relevant.
8. Enter a decimal value (e.g., a price or measurement).
9. Enter any additional text information in the "text-field" field.
10. Enter an integer value.
11. Add any additional comments or notes in the "note 2" field.
12. Enter your email address again in the "email 2" field.
13. Provide your phone number again in the "phone 2" field.
14. Select one option from the "select-one 2" field.
