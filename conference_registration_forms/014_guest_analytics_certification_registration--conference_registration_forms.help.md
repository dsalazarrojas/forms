# Guest Analytics Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Analytics Certification Registration form is used to collect information from users for the Guest Analytics Certification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. (Note: This field is optional.)
4. Select your desired certification program in the "Certification Program" field.
5. In the "Motivations" field, briefly describe why you are interested in registering for the certification program.
6. Select "Agree" to indicate your consent to the terms and conditions.
7. Enter the name of the program you are registering for in the "Program Name" field.
8. Select the start date for your certification program in the "Start Date" field.
9. Select the end date for your certification program in the "End Date" field.
10. Enter the duration of your certification program in the "Duration" field.
11. Choose your preferred location for the certification program in the "Location" field.
12. Choose your time zone in the "Time Zone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the guest analytics team.
* **Certification Program** (Select One, Required): Choose the certification program you are interested in:
  * Guest Analytics Certification - Intermediate
  * Guest Analytics Certification - Advanced
  * Guest Analytics Certification - Expert
* **Motivations** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe why you want to register for the certification program.
* **Consent** (Select One, Required): Select "Agree" to indicate your consent to the terms and conditions.
* **Program Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the program you are registering for.
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): Select the start date of your certification program.
* **End Date** (Date, Required): Select the end date of your certification program.
* **Duration** (Number, Required): Enter the duration of your certification program.
* **Location** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred location for the certification program:
  * Online
  * Offline
* **Time Zone** (Select One, Required): Choose your time zone:
  * EST
  * PST
  * CDT
  * EDT
  * CET
  * CEST
  * GMT
