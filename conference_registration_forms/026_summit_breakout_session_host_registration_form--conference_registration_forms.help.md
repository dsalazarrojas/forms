# Summit Breakout Session Host Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who want to host or lead breakout sessions during the summit. Please provide accurate and detailed information to ensure your session is successfully included in the summit program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the General Info section, which includes the title of your session.
2. Move on to the Contact Person section, where you'll provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the session.
3. In the Session Info section, provide details about your session, including the session date and time, capacity, description, and tags.
4. The Breakout Sessions section is optional and should be filled out only if you're hosting multiple sessions.
5. The Host Info section collects information about the organization hosting the session, contact person, address, phone number, and email.
6. The Speaker Info section should be filled out if you're presenting, speaking, or hosting a session with a speaker.
7. Finally, select the type of session you're hosting (Panel, Workshop, or Presentation).
