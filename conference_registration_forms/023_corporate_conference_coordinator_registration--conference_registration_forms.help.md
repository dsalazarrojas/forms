# Corporate Conference Coordinator Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Conference Coordinator Registration form is intended for Corporate Conference Coordinators to provide information about the conferences they are involved with. This form is not meant for public use, as it's primarily used for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Form (page 1) with your conference details.
2. In the Contact Form (page 2), provide your contact information.
3. In the Conference Details page (page 3), enter the conference's specific information.
4. In the Coordinator Info page (page 4), enter your details as a coordinator for the conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (page 1)**
	+ **Registration Form**: A form to enter conference details. Please fill out this form with the following information:
		- Conference Name
		- Conference Date
		- Conference Location
* **Contact Form (page 2)**
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as a contact person.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Conference Details (page 3)**
	+ **Conference Name**: Enter the conference's name.
	+ **Conference Date**: Enter the conference's date.
	+ **Conference Location**: Enter the conference's location.
* **Coordinator Info (page 4)**
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as a conference coordinator.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address as a conference coordinator.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number as a conference coordinator.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact form@gic.mx.
