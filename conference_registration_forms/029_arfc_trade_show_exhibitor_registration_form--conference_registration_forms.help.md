# ARFC Trade Show Exhibitor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ARFC Trade Show Exhibitor Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from exhibitors who want to participate in the ARFC Trade Show. Completing this form ensures that the exhibitor provides all the required details for a successful trade show experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your company's legal name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the primary contact person in the "Primary Contact Name" field.
3. Provide the email address for trade show communications in the "Primary Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number for trade show communications in the "Primary Contact Phone" field.
5. Select the type of booth you want in the "Booth Type" field (Standard 10x10, Premium 10x20, Custom Booth, Shared Booth, Virtual Booth).
6. If you have any preferences for booth location, enter them in the "Booth Location Preference" field (optional).
7. Enter the industry your company operates in the "Company Industry" field.
8. Provide a brief description of what you offer in the "Products/Services Offered" field.
9. Enter the number of staff members who will attend the trade show in the "Number of Staff Attending" field.
10. If applicable, enter the URL of your company website in the "Company Website" field.
11. List the marketing materials you will bring to the trade show in the "Marketing Materials to Bring" field.
12. If applicable, enter any special requirements you have for your booth in the "Special Requirements" field.
13. Provide shipping information for materials to the venue in the "Shipping/Delivery Information" field (optional).
14. Select the method you will use to pay for the booth in the "Payment Method" field (Credit Card, Check, Wire Transfer, Purchase Order).
15. Confirm that you have read and understood the cancellation policy in the "Cancellation Policy Acknowledged" field.
