# Software Conference Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request leave from a software conference. It is intended for employees or attendees to inform the organizers or coordinators of their absence during the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Leave Request" field.
2. Enter the start date of your absence in the "Start Date" field.
3. Enter the end date of your absence in the "End Date" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Leave Request (`leave_request_form`): 
• This field is for your name. Please enter your name as it appears on official records.

### Start Date (`start_date`):
• This field is used to specify the start date of your leave. Enter the date you will be leaving the conference.

### End Date (`end_date`):
• This field is used to specify the end date of your leave. Enter the date you will be returning to the conference.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in your name and dates accurately to ensure your leave request is processed correctly.
- If you have any supporting documentation or reasons for your leave, please attach them to the form.
- Submit the form to the conference organizers or coordinators to complete the leave request process.
