# Women's Health Tech Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for the Women's Health Tech Forum. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can tailor the event experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your session preferences from the options provided.
2. Enter your email address, phone number, and other required contact information.
3. Provide your gender, age, and occupation information.
4. Enter your company/organization and job title information.
5. Select your industry from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Preferences (Select Multiple)** (`session_preferences`, Select Multiple): Choose the sessions you wish to attend during the forum. Select as many or as few as you like.
* **Email** (`email`, Text): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about event updates and details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text): Provide your phone number so we can reach you about event logistics.
* **Gender** (`gender`, Text): Please select your gender to help us understand the demographics of our attendees.
* **Age** (`age`, Number): Enter your age to help us tailor the event content and experience.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, Text): Describe your occupation or job title to help us understand your area of work.
* **Company/Organization** (`company_organizati`, Text): Enter your company name or organization you represent.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text): Provide your job title to help us understand your role within your organization.
* **Industry** (`industry`, Text): Select the industry you work in to help us understand the sector you represent.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all sessions you wish to attend.
* Enter accurate and complete contact information.
* Provide honest and accurate information about your occupation, job title, and industry.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please contact us using the provided email address.
