# Financial Forecasting Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Forecasting Seminar Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees to ensure a smooth and productive experience for all participants. This form gathers details about attendees, their session preferences, and financial information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Attendee Details" page (Page 1), which requires information such as title, name, and other personal details about the attendee.
2. Move on to the "Session Choices" page (Page 2), where you can select which sessions you would like to attend by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each session.
3. Provide your expectations for the seminar in the "Expectations" page (Page 3), where you can write any additional comments or questions you may have.
4. If you have a preferred training provider, you can select it in the "Training Provider" page (Page 4). If not, leave it blank.
5. Add your organization name and any other relevant information in the "Organization" page (Page 5).
6. In the "Attendee Info" page (Page 6), add any other attendee details, such as company or department.
7. Finally, on the "Contact Preferences" page (Page 7), select your contact preferences, such as "Yes" or "No" for newsletter subscriptions and other communications.
8. For each of the three sessions (Session 1, Session 2, and Session 3), select which one you would like to attend by choosing "Yes" or "No" on the "Session" page (Page 8-10).
9. At the end, provide your financial information, such as your budget for the seminar, in the "Financial Info" page (Page 11), and submit the form.
