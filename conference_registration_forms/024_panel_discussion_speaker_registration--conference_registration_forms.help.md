# Panel Discussion Speaker Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for speakers to register themselves for a panel discussion event, providing organizers with an essential overview of their background and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Speaker Name, Speaker Brief Bio, and Speaker Experience.
2. Select your relevant Topic(s) from the provided options.
3. If applicable, upload a Speaker Image to help us identify you.
4. Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to appear on the speaker list.
* **Speaker Topic** (`speaker_topic`, text, optional): Briefly describe the topic you wish to cover during the panel discussion.
* **Speaker Brief Bio** (`speaker_brief_bio`, text, optional): Write a concise 1-2 paragraph bio about yourself.
* **Speaker Headline** (`speaker_headline`, text, optional): Choose a headline that best describes your profession or expertise.
* **Select Topics** (`panel_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant topics you have experience in.
* **Speaker Experience** (`speaker_experience`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your relevant experience.
* **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image`, text, optional): Upload an image to help us identify you.
* **Speaker Headline** (this is a duplicate field) (this field is not necessary, but if someone wants to enter it again, they can) 

Note: We recommend not to enter the duplicate field.
