# Factory Data Analytics Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for registering attendees for the Factory Data Analytics Conference. Please provide the necessary information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by *).
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your registration carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, optional): Enter your personal information, such as name and title.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, optional): Provide any relevant event-specific information.
* **Consent** (`consent`, required): Select all applicable options to agree to the terms and conditions.
*   * Option 1
*   * Option 2

* **Preferences** (`preferences`, optional): Enter any preferred settings or configurations.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Output File** (`outputfile`, optional): If applicable, upload any relevant files or reports.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, optional): List any tools or equipment assigned to you for the conference.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your registration before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your conference organizer.
