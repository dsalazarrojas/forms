# industry-conference-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for an industry conference. It aims to gather essential details about attendees, including their preferences, contact information, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your attendee information (id: 1)
2. Share your contact details (id: 2)
3. Indicate your session preferences (id: 3, 7, 22, 23)
4. Enter any dietary restrictions (id: 5)
5. Provide accessibility information (id: 6)
6. Confirm your session preferences (id: 8, 23)
7. Add any additional comments or notes (id: 9, 10, 24)
8. Enter payment details (id: 16, 17)
9. Confirm your payment method (id: 18)
10. Confirm your registration details (id: 25)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Info (id: 1)**: Enter your name and any other identifying information required by the conference.
* **Contact Info (id: 2)**: Provide your email address and phone number to ensure you receive important conference updates.
* **Session Preference (id: 3)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in attending sessions.
* **Registration Info (id: 4)**: Enter any additional details about your registration.
* **Dietary Restrictions (id: 5)**: Specify any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Info (id: 6)**: Provide any additional accessibility information you need to know about the conference.
* **Session Preferences Select One (id: 7)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your session preferences.
* **Session Preferences Select Multiple (id: 8)**: Select multiple session preferences.
* **Dietary Restriction Notes (id: 9)**: Enter any additional notes about your dietary restrictions.
* **Accessibility Info Notes (id: 10)**: Enter any additional notes about your accessibility needs.
* **Email (id: 11)**: Provide your email address for conference updates.
* **Phone (id: 12)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth (id: 13)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time (id: 14)**: Select a time for the conference.
* **Registration Date (id: 15)**: Enter the date of your registration.
* **Payment (id: 16)**: Enter your payment details.
* **Payment Method (id: 17)**: Select your payment method.
* **Payment Instructions (id: 18)**: Enter any additional payment instructions.
* **Additional Info (id: 19)**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Terms and Conditions (id: 20)**: Review and agree to the conference's terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy (id: 21)**: Review and agree to the conference's privacy policy.
* **Session Preferences Select One 2 (id: 22)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your session preferences.
* **Session Preferences Select Multiple 2 (id: 23)**: Select multiple session preferences.
* **Attendee Comments (id: 24)**: Enter any comments or notes about your registration.
* **Confirmation (id: 25)**: Review and confirm your registration details.
