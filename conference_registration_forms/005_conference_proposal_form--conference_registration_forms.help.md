# Conference Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for organizations and individuals to submit proposals for conferences. The goal is to provide detailed information about their proposal, including title, description, expected audience, and budget, to help event organizers review and evaluate their potential participation in the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Conference Proposal Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "Required" below.
2. Provide a brief description of your proposal, including the title, expected audience size, and preferred dates for the session.
3. If applicable, include an estimated budget or sponsorship request.
4. Attach any supporting materials, such as links or attachments, to enhance your proposal.
5. Accept the Agreement to Event Terms, confirming your proposal submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (Organization name, text, Required): Enter the name of your company or institution submitting the proposal.
* **Primary contact person** (Primary contact person, text, Required): Identify the person responsible for proposal communication.
* **Contact email address** (Contact email address, email, Required): Provide the email address for follow-up communication.
* **Proposal title** (Proposal title, text, Required): Enter a short descriptive title for your proposal.
* **Proposal description** (Proposal description, text, Required): Write a 300-500 word description of your proposal, outlining goals and scope.
* **Expected audience size** (Expected audience size, number, Optional): Estimate the number of attendees for your session.
* **Preferred dates** (Preferred dates, text, Optional): List one or two preferred dates for the session.
* **Budget or sponsorship requested** (Budget or sponsorship requested, number, Optional): Provide an estimated budget if applicable.
* **Supporting materials** (Supporting materials, text, Optional): Attach links or documents to enhance your proposal.
* **Agreement to Event Terms** (Agreement to Event Terms, select_one, Required): Confirm your acceptance of the event terms by selecting "Agree".

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Keep the proposal description within the 300-500 word limit.
* Attach relevant supporting materials to enhance your proposal.
* Double-check your contact email address for accuracy.
* Read and accept the Agreement to Event Terms carefully before submission.
