# Labor Law Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor Law Seminar Registration Form is used to collect information from attendees who are interested in attending the Labor Law Seminar. This form helps the seminar's organizers to track the attendees' details, including their name, contact information, and seminar preferences. The information collected on this form will be used to finalize the seminar schedule and ensure that attendees can receive updates and reminders about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your name (First Name and Last Name) to identify yourself.
2.  Provide your email address so that you can receive updates and reminders about the seminar.
3.  Enter your phone number so that the seminar organizers can contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
4.  Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" if you plan to attend the seminar.
5.  If you are unable to attend, provide a brief reason in the "If not attending, why not?" field.
6.  If you have any special needs for the seminar (e.g., Accommodation, Food, Transportation), select the relevant options.
7.  Enter your registration date and time so that the seminar organizers can track your registration.
8.  Add any additional notes or comments that you would like to share with the seminar organizers.
9.  Enter the name of your association, if applicable.
10. Enter your role or position within your organization.
11. Select your registration status (Pending, Confirmed, or No).
12. If your registration status is "Other", provide a brief explanation.
13. Enter the email address and phone number of the attendee who is registering (if not yourself).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, First Name, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (text, Last Name, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (email, Email, required): Enter your email address so that you can receive updates and reminders about the seminar.
*   **Phone** (text, Phone, required): Enter your phone number so that the seminar organizers can contact you if necessary.
*   **Will you attend the seminar?** (select\_one, Will you attend the seminar?, required): Select "Yes" if you plan to attend the seminar.
*   **If not attending, why not?** (text, If not attending, why not?, required): If you are unable to attend, provide a brief reason.
*   **Attendee needs** (select\_multiple, Attendee needs, required): Select any special needs you may have for the seminar (e.g., Accommodation, Food, Transportation).
*   **Registration Date** (date, Registration Date, required): Enter the date you registered for the seminar.
*   **Registration Time** (time, Registration Time, required): Enter the time you registered for the seminar.
*   **Notes** (note, Notes, required): Add any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with the seminar organizers.
*   **Association Name** (text, Association Name, required): Enter the name of your association, if applicable.
*   **Role** (text, Role, required): Enter your role or position within your organization.
*   **Registration Status** (select\_one, Registration Status, required): Select your registration status (Pending, Confirmed, or No).
*   **If other, please specify** (text, If other, please specify, required): If your registration status is "Other", provide a brief explanation.
*   **Attendee Email** (email, Attendee Email, required): Enter the email address of the attendee who is registering (if not yourself).
*   **Attendee Phone** (text, Attendee Phone, required): Enter the phone number of the attendee who is registering (if not yourself).

Note:
This help guide is designed to be a step-by-step guide on how to fill out the Labor Law Seminar Registration Form. Please review each field carefully, and ensure you fill out the form with the most accurate and up-to-date information.
