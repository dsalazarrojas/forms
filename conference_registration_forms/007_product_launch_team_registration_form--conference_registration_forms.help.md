# Product Launch Team Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Product Launch Team Registration Form is a tool used to register team members for a product launch event. It helps gather information about team members' roles, skills, and availability. This information is essential for the smooth coordination of team communication and logistics during the launch event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have the information or if it's not relevant to your registration.
4. For select options, choose the relevant option from the available choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Launch Team Registration** (`id: 1`, `type: note`, required: false):
    This field is a note that confirms you are filling out the product launch team registration form.
* **role** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, required: false):
    Choose "Yes" if you are a member of the product launch team, "No" otherwise.
* **skills** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false):
    Choose all the skills that apply to you from the provided options.
* **availability** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, required: false):
    Enter your availability for the product launch event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Product Launch Team Registration** (`id: 5`, `type: note`, required: false):
    This field is a note that confirms you are filling out the product launch team registration form.
* **email** (`id: 6`, `type: email`, required: false):
    Enter your official email address.
* **phone** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false):
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **team lead** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, required: false):
    If you're a team lead, choose "Yes" to confirm. If not, choose "No".
* **product manager** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, required: false):
    If you're a product manager, choose "Yes" to confirm. If not, choose "No".

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the product launch team for guidance.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
