# seminar_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seminar registration form is designed to collect attendee information and details for seminars or conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the seminar registration form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "attendee_information" section.
2. Provide your details in the "seminar_details" section, which is a required field.
3. Read and acknowledge the information in the "registration_info" section, and provide your response.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, type them in the "attendee_questions" section.
5. If you have made a payment, provide the information in the "payment_info" section.
6. Select your status as an attendee in the "seminar_attendee" section.
7. Finally, provide payment details in the "payment_details" section, which is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **attendee_information** (Required: No): This field captures your name and contact information. Please fill out this section with your full name and any other relevant details.
* **seminar_details** (Required: Yes): This field asks about your interest in attending the seminar. Please provide a brief description of your interest or any specific topics you would like to learn about.
* **registration_info** (Required: Yes): This field explains the terms and conditions of your registration. Please read this carefully and select "Yes" to acknowledge that you understand.
* **attendee_questions** (Required: No): If you have any questions or concerns about the seminar, type them in this field.
* **payment_info** (Required: No): If you have made a payment, provide the payment details in this field.
* **seminar_attendee** (Required: No): This field asks about your status as an attendee. Select "Yes" if you are attending, otherwise select "No".
* **payment_details** (Required: Yes): This field requires you to provide payment details. Please fill out this section with the payment information.
* **seminar_attendee** (Required: No): This field is similar to the previous one, but it's not required. Please select your status as an attendee in this field as well.
