# Speaker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Registration Form is designed to collect speaker information for a conference or event. This form is intended for accepted speakers and helps organizers gather essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have not already registered as a speaker for this event.
2.  Fill in your name in the **speaker_name** field.
3.  Provide a brief bio in the **speaker_bio** field.
4.  Enter your topic in the **topic** field.
5.  Enter your email address in the **speaker_email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **speaker_name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents or your business card.
* **speaker_bio** (text, optional): Write a brief description of yourself, including your professional background, interests, or any relevant experience.
* **topic** (text, optional): Enter your presentation topic, including any relevant keywords or tags.
* **speaker_email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted about your speaker details and event logistics.

## Tips
* Please double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure you can receive important event communications.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, feel free to include them in the **speaker_bio** field.
