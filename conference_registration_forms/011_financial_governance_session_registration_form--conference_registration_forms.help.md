# Financial Governance Session Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Governance Session Registration Form is a tool used to register sessions for financial governance events. It is used to collect essential information about the session, including its name, date, time, and organizer's contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the financial governance session in the **Financial Governance Session Name** field.
2. Enter the date of the session in the **Session Date** field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter the time of the session in the **Session Time** field in the format `HH:MM`.
4. Enter the name and contact details of the session organizer in the **Financial Governance Session Organizer** field.
5. Enter the email address of the session organizer in the **Financial Governance Session Organizer Email** field.
6. Enter the phone number of the session organizer in the **Financial Governance Session Organizer Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Governance Session Name (financial_governance_session_name)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the financial governance session. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide a clear and concise name for the session.
* **Session Date (session_date)** (`date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the session in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide the correct date for the session.
* **Session Time (session_time)** (`time`, `required: false`): Enter the time of the session in the format `HH:MM`. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide the correct time for the session.
* **Financial Governance Session Organizer (financial_governance_session_organizer)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the name and contact information of the session organizer.
* **Financial Governance Session Organizer Email (financial_governance_session_organizer_email)** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter the email address of the session organizer.
* **Financial Governance Session Organizer Phone (financial_governance_session_organizer_phone)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the session organizer.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact the session organizer for assistance.
