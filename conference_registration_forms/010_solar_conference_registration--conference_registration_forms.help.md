# Solar Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Solar Conference Registration form is designed for attendees to register for the conference. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and efficient conference experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, phone number, and address as required.
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the conference and in conference communications.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted for conference updates, announcements, and other important information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current address for conference organization and communication purposes.
