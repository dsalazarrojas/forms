# Research Collaboration Matchmaking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Collaboration Matchmaking Registration form is designed to facilitate the research collaboration matchmaking process between researchers. This form helps to match researchers with similar interests and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose your research area of interest from the dropdown menu.
3. Briefly describe your research interests, expertise, and current research projects.
4. Provide details about your current and past research grants, if applicable.
5. Submit the form to be matched with potential collaborators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research University** (`research_university`): Please enter the name of the university where you are affiliated with.
* **Research Interest** (`research_interest`): Briefly describe your research interests and areas of focus.
* **Collaboration Preferences** (`collaboration_preferences`): Describe your collaboration preferences, such as working style, research methodology, and communication style.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`): Select the area of research that you specialize in from the dropdown menu.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`): Enter the name of the institution where you work or study.
* **Research Email** (`research_email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for collaboration inquiries.
* **Research Phone** (`research_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for collaboration inquiries.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Affiliation Name** (`affiliation_name`): Enter the name of your institution or company.
* **Position** (`position`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Research Group** (`research_group`): Enter the name of your research group or department.
* **Research Description** (`research_description`): Briefly describe your research background and interests.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`): Enter any specific research interests or topics you want to collaborate on.
* **Research Expertise** (`research_expertise`): Enter any special skills or expertise you have that could benefit collaboration.
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`): Enter any areas of research you are specialized in.
* **Research Topics** (`research_topics`): Enter any specific research topics you are interested in collaborating on.
* **Research Keywords** (`research_keywords`): Enter any relevant keywords related to your research.
* **Research Publications** (`research_publications`): Enter any notable research publications or papers you have authored.
* **Research Grant ID** (`research_grant_id`): Enter the ID of your current research grant.
* **Research Grant Title** (`research_grant_title`): Enter the title of your current research grant.
* **Research Grant Amount** (`research_grant_amount`): Enter the amount of your current research grant.
* **Research Grant Start Date** (`research_grant_start_date`): Enter the start date of your current research grant.
* **Research Grant End Date** (`research_grant_end_date`): Enter the end date of your current research grant.

## Tips

* Please fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be specific when describing your research interests and expertise.
* Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for potential collaborators to know.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact email].
