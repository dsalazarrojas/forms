# Standby Participant Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standby Participant Registration form is used for registering attendees who can potentially join a conference if there are available spots. It is used to gather necessary information for registration and confirmation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Session ID, which is required for registration purposes.
2. Fill out your personal details:
   - Enter your name in the "name" field.
   - Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your contact preference for communication:
   - Select how you would like to be contacted by the conference organizers (Phone call, Text message, Email, Mail, or Other).
4. If necessary, describe any special requirements in the "special_requirements" field.
5. Add any comments you'd like to share with the conference organizers.
6. Enter your Session ID (if you have one) in the "session_id_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session ID**: A unique identifier for your conference session.
- **name**: Your full name as you would like to be known by the conference organizers.
- **Email**: The email address we will use for communication with you.
- **Phone**: A valid phone number we can use to contact you.
- **Contact Preference**: How you would like to be contacted by the conference organizers. 
  - Phone call: We will call you on your registered phone number.
  - Text message: We will send you a text message on your registered phone number.
  - Email: We will contact you via email.
  - Mail: We will send you mail at your registered address.
  - Other: Describe your preferred contact method in the comments field.
- **Special Requirements**: If you have any special requirements (e.g. accessibility needs), describe them here.
- **Comments**: Any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
- **Session ID** (again): This is a duplicate field for Session ID to ensure accuracy and to provide a backup in case the first field is invalid.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Be as accurate and complete as possible in your responses.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the conference organizers for guidance.
