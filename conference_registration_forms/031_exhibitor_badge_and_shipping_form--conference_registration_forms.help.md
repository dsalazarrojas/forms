# Exhibitor Badge And Shipping Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect exhibitor shipping and badge information. It is used to ensure that you are correctly registered to receive your exhibitor badge and have a clear understanding of your shipping details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and completely. Make sure to fill out each field as requested.
2. Review your information to ensure it is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Badge Information** (`exhibitor_badge_info`, text, optional): This field is optional and is used to provide additional information about your badge.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): This field is optional and is used to provide additional information about your shipping.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive your shipping. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Please enter your shipping address in the format "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Please enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, select_one, required): Choose the state of your shipping address. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, required): Please enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, select_one, required): Choose the country of your shipping address. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Shipping Email** (`shipping_email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address for shipping purposes.
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, optional): This field is optional and is used to provide a phone number for shipping purposes.
* **Additional Shipping Information** (`shipping_note`, note, optional): This field is optional and is used to provide any additional shipping information.
