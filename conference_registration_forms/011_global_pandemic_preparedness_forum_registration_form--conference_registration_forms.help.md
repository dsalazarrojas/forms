# Global Pandemic Preparedness Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals registering for the Global Pandemic Preparedness Forum. The purpose of this form is to gather contact information, medical conditions, and logistical details of attendees to help prepare for and respond to global health crises.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Form Information** field with your name and any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
2. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact** field.
3. In the **Emergency Contact Type** field, select the relation between you and your emergency contact person.
4. Choose the specific relationship between you and your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
5. Indicate whether you have any medical conditions in the **Medical Condition** field.
6. Provide any additional information about your medical condition in the **Medical Condition Information** field.
7. Enter more details about your medical condition in the **Medical Condition Details** field.
8. Enter information about your travel arrangements in the **Travel Arrangements** field.
9. Provide details about your accommodation in the **Accommodation Information** field.
10. Choose the type of accommodation you will use in the **Accommodation Type** field.
11. Enter information about your transportation in the **Transportation** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information (ID: 1)**: This field is for you to enter your name and any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact (ID: 2)**: This field is for you to enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Type (ID: 3)**: This field is for you to select the relation between you and your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (ID: 4)**: This field is for you to choose the specific relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition (ID: 5)**: This field is for you to indicate whether you have any medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition Information (ID: 6)**: This field is for you to provide any additional information about your medical condition.
* **Medical Condition Details (ID: 7)**: This field is for you to enter more details about your medical condition.
* **Travel Arrangements (ID: 8)**: This field is for you to enter information about your travel plans.
* **Accommodation Information (ID: 9)**: This field is for you to enter details about your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Type (ID: 10)**: This field is for you to choose the type of accommodation you will use.
* **Transportation (ID: 11)**: This field is for you to enter information about your transportation.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any medical conditions, please provide additional information in the **Medical Condition Information** and **Medical Condition Details** fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when entering your travel arrangements and accommodation information.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the **Form Information** field.
