# HR Data Governance Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Data Governance Forum Registration Form is used to register participants for the HR Data Governance Forum. This form is intended for HR professionals and individuals interested in learning more about data governance in the human resources context.

## How To Complete This Form

### To begin, select "Submit" to start the registration process. You will be directed to the first page of the form.
### Fill out the first page with your personal information, including your first name, last name, and email address. Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
### Move on to the next page and select your session interest (HR, IT, or other).
### On the following page, you will be asked to agree to the terms and conditions, privacy policy, and both terms and privacy policy. Ensure you carefully review these before making your selection.
### Finally, enter your contact information, including your address and time zone.
### Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Your first name will be used to identify you at the forum. Please enter your full first name.
* **Last name**: Your last name will be used to identify you at the forum. Please enter your full last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you use for work or personal correspondence. This will be used to communicate with you about the forum.
* **Company**: If you represent a company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Role**: If you have a specific role within your company, please enter it here.
* **Job title**: If you have a specific job title, please enter it here.
* **Department**: If you have a specific department within your company, please enter it here.
* **Job function**: If you have a specific job function, please enter it here.
* **Session interest**: Select one or multiple session interests that you are interested in. You may select HR, IT, or other.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to provide, please enter them here.
* **Agreement**: Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" to proceed.
* **Contact**: Enter your name and contact information, including your address and time zone.
* **Agree Privacy**: Select "Agree to the privacy policy" to proceed.
* **Agree Terms Privacy**: Select "Agree to both terms and conditions" to proceed.
* **Comments 2**: If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to provide, please enter them here.
* **Email Agreement**: This field is not required, and you do not need to enter your email address.
* **Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the forum.
* **Time**: Enter the time you plan to attend the forum.
* **Timezone**: Select your time zone (UTC, EST, EST5, CST, CST5, PST, PST5).
* **Agree**: Select "Agree to the terms and conditions" to proceed.
* **Agree Privacy**: Select "Agree to the privacy policy" to proceed.

* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **State**: Enter your state of residence.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Comments 2**: If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to provide, please enter them here.
