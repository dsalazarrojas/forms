# Multi Family Housing Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees to the Multi Family Housing Summit. Please fill out this form to let us know your preferences and needs for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to choose one option for each field that applies to you.
- If a field is not applicable, please fill out the field with 'N/A' or a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your business email address.
- **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your business phone number.
- **Conference Dates** (`conference_dates`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more dates that you are planning to attend the summit. Please note that this will help us plan the event schedule.
- **Reason for Attending** (`conference_attending_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the reason why you are attending the summit.
- **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Please enter any additional information that you would like to share with us.
- **How did you hear about us?** (`conference_registration_form_8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you heard about the summit. This will help us understand our marketing channels.
- **How will you be attending?** (`conference_registration_form_9`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you will be attending the summit. This will help us plan for in-person attendees.
- **Which format will you be attending?** (`conference_registration_form_10`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the format you will be attending (in-person, hybrid, or online).
- **How many guests will be attending?** (`conference_registration_form_11`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter the number of guests that will be attending with you.
- **Job Title** (`conference_registration_form_12`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your job title. 

Note: This form is designed to be completed before the summit and any changes made after submission might not be possible or will be subject to extra fees. Please review carefully before submitting. 

Please make sure to check the summit website for more information about the summit and plan accordingly. 

Please let us know if you need help or have any questions.
