# Speaker Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Submission Form is designed to collect information from speakers who wish to participate in a conference. The form helps the conference organizers gather essential details about the speaker's profile, submission, and contact information. This form ensures that the speaker's submission is properly received and processed for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Select Conference Type**: Choose whether the conference will be held in-person, online, or hybrid.
2.  **Enter Conference Name**: Provide the name of the conference where you will be speaking.
3.  **Enter Speaker Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or profile.
4.  **Enter Contact E-mail**: Provide your professional email address.
5.  **Enter Speaker Bio**: Write a brief description of yourself and your qualifications.
6.  **Select Submission Type**: Choose the type of submission you are making, such as a Talk, Workshop, Panel, or Tutorial.
7.  **Enter Submission Title**: Provide a title for your submission.
8.  **Enter Submission Abstract**: Write a short abstract summarizing your submission.
9.  **Enter Submission Keywords**: Optional – provide keywords related to your submission.
10.  **Upload Speaker Image**: Optional – upload a suitable image for your speaker profile.
11.  **Enter Submitter Name**: Provide the name of the person submitting you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Conference Type**: Select the format of the conference you are applying to speak at. This field is required to ensure correct categorization of your submission.
*  **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference you are attending. This field helps the organizers verify your submission and ensure accurate promotion.
*  **Speaker Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or profile.
*  **Contact E-mail**: Enter your professional email address. This ensures that we can get in touch with you for further information or follow-up.
*  **Speaker Bio**: Write a brief description of yourself and your qualifications. This field helps your audience understand your background and expertise.
*  **Submission Type**: Choose the type of submission you are making, such as a Talk, Workshop, Panel, or Tutorial. This field categorizes your submission for the conference organizers.
*  **Submission Title**: Enter a title for your submission. This field helps your audience understand the content and relevance of your talk.
*  **Submission Abstract**: Write a short abstract summarizing your submission. This field provides a brief overview of your talk, workshop, or panel.
*  **Submission Keywords**: Optional – enter keywords related to your submission. This field helps the organizers filter and categorize your submission for the audience.
*  **Speaker Image**: Optional – upload a suitable image for your speaker profile. This helps your audience associate a face with your name.
*  **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting you. This ensures that your submission is properly attributed to the correct person.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed in full.
*   Proofread your submission before sending it for review.
*   If you have trouble uploading the speaker image, try adjusting the image dimensions to 200x200 pixels.
