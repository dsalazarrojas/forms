# Global People Analytics Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about attendees to the Global People Analytics Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Form (Page 1) with your name, job title, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your attendee details, including any segmentation information (Page 2).
3. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and job title (Page 3).
4. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form (Page 1)
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, `text`, `false`): This is the starting page of the form where you can enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your role or area of expertise.
* **Attendee Segmentation** (`attendee_segmentation`, `text`, `false`): If you have a specific segment or group affiliation, please enter it here.
* **Registration Manager** (`registration_manager`, `text`, `false`): If you are being registered by a manager, enter their name here.

### Attendee Details (Page 2)
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional details about yourself.
* **Attendee Segmentation** (`attendee_segmentation`, `text`, `false`): If you have a specific segment or group affiliation, please enter it here.
* **Registration Manager** (`registration_manager`, `text`, `false`): If you are being registered by a manager, enter their name here.

### Contact Information (Page 3)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional contact information, such as your company or organization.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `false`): Enter your job title.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns with the form, contact the registration team.
* The form is intended for conference registration only, so please do not use it for any other purpose.
