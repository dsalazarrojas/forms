# Educational Philosophy Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Educational Philosophy Forum. It collects essential information to manage participant registration, communication, and to ensure a smooth forum experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Describe your participant interests in the "participant_interests" field.
5. Read the Code of Conduct and indicate your agreement or disagreement in the "consent" field by selecting "Agree" or "Disagree".
6. Provide any additional contact information in the "contact_info" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<id: 1>)** (`first_name`, **text**, required: no): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name (<id: 2>)** (`last_name`, **text**, required: no): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email (<id: 3>)** (`email`, **email**, required: no): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Interests (<id: 4>)** (`participant_interests`, **text**, required: no): Describe your participant interests.
* **Consent (<id: 5>)** (`consent`, **select_one**, required: no): Read the Code of Conduct and indicate your agreement or disagreement by selecting "Agree" or "Disagree".
* **Contact Info (<id: 6>)** (`contact_info`, **text**, required: no): Please provide any additional contact information, if necessary.
