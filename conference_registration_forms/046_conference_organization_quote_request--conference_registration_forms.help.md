# Conference Organization Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help conference organizers request a quote for conference services from our organization. Please fill out this form with as much detail as possible to enable us to provide an accurate quote for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the required information for each field.
2. For services needed, select all the services required for your conference.
3. For date-related information, please provide the most critical dates for your event.
4. For contact information, please provide the main contact person's name, title, and email address.
5. For additional requirements, provide any special needs or requirements you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Request Form**: This is a placeholder for a note, please do not fill this field.
* **Request Date**: Date of request, the date you submitted this form.
* **Request Reference**: Internal tracking number, not required but useful for us to reference your request.
* **Contact Information**: Main contact person's name, title, and email address.
* **Organization Name**: Name of the organization hosting the conference.
* **Conference Name**: Name of the conference/event.
* **Conference Type**: Category of the conference (e.g., Corporate Meeting, Academic Conference, etc.).
* **Expected Attendees**: Number of participants anticipated for the conference.
* **Event Dates**: Start and end dates of the conference.
* **Event Duration**: Number of days the conference will run.
* **Services Needed**: Select all the services required for your conference, including:
	+ Venue Rental
	+ Room Setup
	+ Audio Visual Equipment
	+ Lighting
	+ Stage Setup
	+ Backdrop and Signage
	+ Catering Services (Breakfast, Morning Coffee Break, Lunch, Afternoon Coffee Break, Dinner, Cocktail Reception)
	+ Technical Services (Live Streaming, Recording Services, Simultaneous Translation, Mobile App, Registration System, Badge Printing)
	+ Additional Services (Event Staff, Security, Photography, Videography, Entertainment, Transportation, Accommodation Booking)
* **Budget Information**: Estimated budget for the conference services.
* **Timeline**: When you need the quote, please select the most critical deadline for your event.
* **Additional Requirements**: Special needs or requirements you may have.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Source of information about our organization.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other information you would like to share with us.

Please provide accurate and complete information to enable us to provide you with the best possible quote for your conference services.
