# Healthcare Data Ethics Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the Healthcare Data Ethics Roundtable. The purpose of this form is to collect information about each participant, including their name, contact details, and preferences for accessibility and dietary requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3. Choose one or two topics you are interested in from the provided options.
4. If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them in the text field.
5. If you have other accessibility needs not listed, you can also provide a brief description.
6. Select any dietary requirements you may have, or choose "None" if you do not have any.
7. If you have any other dietary requirements not listed, you can provide a brief description in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter any phone number you would like us to have in case we need to contact you.
* **Topic of interest 1**: Choose one topic that you are interested in from the list.
* **Topic of interest 2**: Choose another topic that you are interested in from the list.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, describe them in this field.
* **Other accessibility needs**: If you have other accessibility needs not listed, provide a brief description here.
* **Dietary requirements**: Select any dietary requirements you may have, or choose "None" if you do not have any.
* **Other dietary requirements**: If you have other dietary requirements not listed, provide a brief description here.
