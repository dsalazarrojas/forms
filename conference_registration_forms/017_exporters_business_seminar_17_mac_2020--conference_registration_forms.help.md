# EXPORTERS BUSINESS SEMINAR (17 Mar 2020) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from attendees to register for the EXPORTERS BUSINESS SEMINAR on March 17, 2020.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the corresponding fields as follows:
	* **Name**: Your full name as it should be printed on your name tag.
	* **Email**: Your email address.
	* **Phone**: Your phone number.
	* **Attendance Dates**: The dates you will be attending the seminar.
	* **Attendance Time**: The time you will be attending the seminar.
	* **Company**: The company you represent.
	* **Role**: Your role in the company.
	* **Seminar Type**: The type of seminar you will be attending (please select from options, if provided).
	* **Payment Method**: The method you will be using to pay for the seminar.
	* **Notes**: Any additional information you'd like to provide about your registration.
	* **Organizers**: The names of the seminar organizers.

Please note that some fields are optional, but filling in all required fields will help us better serve you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form for Attending Seminar**: This is the main form for registering to attend the seminar.
* **Name**: Provide your full name as it should be printed on your name tag.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about the seminar.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you about the seminar.
* **Attendance Dates**: Select the dates you will be attending the seminar.
* **Attendance Time**: Select the time you will be attending the seminar.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Role**: Enter your role in the company.
* **Seminar Type**: Select the type of seminar you will be attending (if provided).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method you will be using to pay for the seminar.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your registration.
* **Organizers**: Please list the names of the seminar organizers.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields for a smooth registration process.
* If you are experiencing any issues with the form, please contact us at your email or phone number you provided.
