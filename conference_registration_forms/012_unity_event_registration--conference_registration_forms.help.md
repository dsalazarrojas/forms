# Unity Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unity Event Registration form is designed to collect information from users to help us better understand their needs and preferences for upcoming Unity events. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can provide you with the best experience possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Data**: Please fill in your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details that you'd like to share with us.
2. Select your **Session Interests** by choosing from the available options. These will help us tailor the event to your needs.
3. Let us know about any **Accessibility Needs** you may have by selecting the option that best fits your requirements.
4. Choose your **Session Preferences** for the event, if desired.
5. Enter any **Contact Details** that you'd like to share with us.
6. If you have any **Additional Comments** or questions, use this space to share them with us.
7. Provide **Event Details** if you'd like to specify any specific event-related information.
8. Finally, choose your **Session Preferences 2** by selecting the option that best fits your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Data**: This field is where you'll provide your basic contact information, such as name and email address.
* **Session Interests**: This field allows you to choose multiple session interests that align with your goals and objectives. Select all that apply to help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Accessibility Needs**: This field helps us understand any special accessibility requirements you may have, such as audio or visual impairments. Select the option that best describes your needs.
* **Session Preferences**: This field allows you to choose your preferred session style or format for the event.
* **Contact Details**: This field is an open-ended text area for you to provide any contact details you'd like to share with us.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any questions or comments about the event, use this field to share them with us.
* **Event Details**: This field is an open-ended text area for you to provide any additional event-related information.
* **Session Preferences 2**: This field is similar to **Session Preferences**, but allows you to choose another option that best fits your requirements.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to contact form@gic.mx.
