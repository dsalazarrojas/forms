# Additive Manufacturing Technology Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Additive Manufacturing Technology Fair. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter your company or institution in the "Organization" field.
3. Select your job title or role in the "Role or Title" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Choose the days you will attend in the "Attendance Days" field.
6. Select up to two workshops you would like to attend in the "Workshop Selection" field.
7. If you have any dietary requirements, please enter them in the "Dietary Requirements" field.
8. If you require accommodation assistance, select "True" in the "Accommodation Needed" field.
9. Finally, add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Information
* **Attendee name (Attendee Name, text, required)**: Please enter your full name for badge purposes.
### Organization
* **Organization (Organization, text, optional)**: Enter your company or institution name.
### Role or Title
* **Role or Title (Role, text, optional)**: Select your job title or role.
### Email Address
* **Email address (Email address, email, required)**: Enter your email address for confirmations and updates.
### Attendance Days
* **Attendance days (Attendance days, select_multiple, required)**: Select the days you will attend the fair.
### Workshop Selection
* **Workshop selection (Workshop selection, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose up to two workshops you would like to attend.
### Dietary Requirements
* **Dietary requirements (Dietary requirements, text, optional)**: If you have any dietary requirements, please enter them here.
### Accommodation Needed
* **Accommodation needed (Accommodation needed, select_one, optional)**: If you require accommodation assistance, select "True".
### Additional Notes
* **Additional notes (Additional notes, text, optional)**: Add any additional information for the organizers.

Note: This form is not exhaustive and may be subject to change. Please refer to the event organizers for any updates or changes.
