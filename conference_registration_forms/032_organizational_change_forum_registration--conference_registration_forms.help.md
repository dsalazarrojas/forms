# Organizational Change Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for conference registration. To register for a conference, you will need to provide your details and preferences for sessions you wish to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Provide your attendee details.
2. Provide session details for the sessions you wish to attend.
3. Specify your roles within the organization.
4. Add any additional notes if necessary.
5. Enter the date and time of your session.
6. Enter your phone number for reference.
7. Add any final notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details (Required)**: This field is used to capture your basic details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Session Details (Required)**: This field is used to capture the session details you wish to attend. Please provide the session name and any other relevant details.
* **Roles (Required)**: This field is used to specify your roles within the organization. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Notes (Optional)**: This field is used to add any additional information or notes you wish to share with the organizers.
* **Date (Required)**: This field is used to select the date of your session. Please select the date from the provided options.
* **Time (Required)**: This field is used to select the time of your session. Please select the time from the provided options.
* **Phone (Optional)**: This field is used to capture your phone number for reference.
* **Notes 2 (Optional)**: This field is used to add any final notes or comments you wish to share with the organizers.
