# NISE-Stim-Certification-Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the "NISE-Stim-Certification-Registration" form, which is designed for registering and managing certification information.

## How To Complete This Form
To register a certification, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and their corresponding sections, which include:
	* Training Provider Information
	* Applicant Information
	* Certification Details
	* Additional Notes and Contact Information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Training Provider Information
* **training_provider** (`training_provider`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the training provider.
* **training_institution** (`training_institution`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the training institution.
* **applicant_info** (`applicant_info`, text, required): Enter the applicant's information.
* **cert_info** (`cert_info`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the certification information.
* **cert_dates** (`cert_dates`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm the certification dates.
* **cert_location** (`cert_location`, text, required): Enter the certification location.
* **cert_cost** (`cert_cost`, number, required): Enter the certification cost.
* **cert_fee** (`cert_fee`, number, required): Enter the certification fee.
* **cert_price** (`cert_price`, number, required): Enter the certification price.
* **cert_discount** (`cert_discount`, number, required): Enter the certification discount.
* **cert_total** (`cert_total`, number, required): Enter the certification total.
* **cert_status** (`cert_status`, select_multiple, required): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to confirm the certification status.
* **cert_notes** (`cert_notes`, text): Enter any additional notes about the certification.
* **cert_url** (`cert_url`, text): Enter the certification URL.
* **cert_phone** (`cert_phone`, text): Enter the certification phone number.
* **cert_email** (`cert_email`, email): Enter the certification email.
* **cert_comments** (`cert_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments about the certification.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submitting to avoid any errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
