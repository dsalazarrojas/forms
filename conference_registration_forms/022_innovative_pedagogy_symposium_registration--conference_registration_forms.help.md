# Innovative Pedagogy Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees for the Innovative Pedagogy Symposium. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Attendee Details" section, which asks for your general information.
2. In the "Session Interests" section, select the sessions you are interested in attending.
3. In the "Accessibility Needs" section, let us know if you have any accessibility requirements.
4. Fill out the "Contact Details" section with your email and phone number.
5. In the "Notes" section, add any additional information or comments.
6. Finally, fill out the "Date" and "Time" sections if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (`text`, required/optional): Provide your name and any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Session Interests** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the sessions you are interested in attending by checking the corresponding boxes.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`select_one`, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
* **Contact Details** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates and information about the symposium.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you are able to attend the symposium.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you are able to attend the symposium.
* **Note 2** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
* **Email 2** (`email`, optional): Enter an alternate email address for you.
* **Phone 2** (`text`, optional): Enter an alternate phone number for you.
* **Date 2** (`date`, optional): Enter an alternate date you are able to attend the symposium.
* **Time 2** (`time`, optional): Enter an alternate time you are able to attend the symposium.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and comprehensively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the organizers.
* You can always contact us if you need any assistance or clarification on any of the fields.
