# PR Session Attendee Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PR Session Attendee Registration Form is used to collect information about participants attending a PR session. This includes their basic contact information, interests, and details about their workshop and training needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the "Participant info" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, ensuring it is a valid email address.
4. Select your interests from the list provided in the "Interests" field.
5. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Provide details about the workshop you are attending in the "Workshop Details" field.
7. Enter any additional training details in the "Training Details" field.
8. Provide any event details in the "Event Details" field.
9. Enter media briefing details in the "Media Briefing Details" field.
10. Add any participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering your participant information, such as your name or participant ID.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This field is for entering your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select your interests from the provided list.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for entering your contact number.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering details about the workshop you are attending.
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional training details.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering any event details.
* **Media Briefing Details** (`media_briefing_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering media briefing details.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): This field is for adding any participant notes.
