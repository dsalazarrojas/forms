# Digital Humanities Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who wish to register for the Digital Humanities Collaboration Conference. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your details and express your interests in participating in the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Conference Registration Form**: This is the starting point of the form. It serves as a title for the form and is not a field that requires input.
2. **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents or as you would like to be known at the conference.
3. **Email**: Provide your professional email address that we can use to communicate with you about the conference.
4. **Affiliation**: Enter your institution or organization where you are affiliated with.
5. **Research Interests**: Select all the research areas that interest you. You can choose one or multiple research areas from the list provided.
6. **Collaboration Preferences**: Choose all the collaboration preferences that suit you best.
7. **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of your current project or research focus.
