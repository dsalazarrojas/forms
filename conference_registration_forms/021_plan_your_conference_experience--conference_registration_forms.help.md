# Plan Your Conference Experience - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you tailor your conference experience to your preferences, schedule, and interests. By providing us with information about your conference plans, you'll be able to:
* Choose your session schedule
* Select your preferred workshops
* Provide additional information to help us tailor our support to your needs

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the sessions and workshops that interest you the most.
2. Choose your preferred schedule and workshop time slots.
3. Provide additional information to help us support you during the conference.
4. Review your selections to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Information** (page 1): This section asks for basic conference information.
	+ **Conference Information**: Please provide a brief description of the conference you are attending.
* **Session Schedule** (page 2): Select the sessions that interest you the most.
	+ **Session Schedule**: Choose from Morning Session, Afternoon Session, and Evening Session.
* **Workshop Schedule** (page 3): Select the workshops you want to attend.
	+ **Workshop Schedule**: Choose from Workshop 1, Workshop 2, and Workshop 3.
* **Breakout Sessions Schedule** (page 4): Select the breakout sessions you'd like to attend.
	+ **Breakout Sessions Schedule**: Choose from Breakout Session 1, Breakout Session 2, and Breakout Session 3.
* **Preferred Session Time** (page 5): Indicate your preferred session time.
	+ **Preferred Session Time**: Select from Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.
* **Preferred Workshop** (page 6): Choose the workshop you prefer.
	+ **Preferred Workshop**: Select from Workshop 1, Workshop 2, and Workshop 3.
* **Preferred Breakout Session** (page 7): Select your preferred breakout session.
	+ **Preferred Breakout Session**: Choose from Breakout Session 1, Breakout Session 2, and Breakout Session 3.
* **Sessions** (page 8): Select any additional session you'd like to attend.
	+ **Sessions**: Choose from Session 1, Session 2, and Session 3.
* **Breakout Sessions** (page 9): Select any additional breakout sessions you'd like to attend.
	+ **Breakout Sessions**: Choose from Breakout Session 1, Breakout Session 2, and Breakout Session 3.
* **Workshops** (page 10): Select any additional workshops you'd like to attend.
	+ **Workshops**: Choose from Workshop 1, Workshop 2, and Workshop 3.
* **Preferred Language** (page 11): Select your preferred language.
	+ **Preferred Language**: Choose from English or Spanish.

## Tips
* Please select only sessions and workshops that you are actually interested in.
* If you need to provide additional information, use the text field at the end of the form.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is accurate.
