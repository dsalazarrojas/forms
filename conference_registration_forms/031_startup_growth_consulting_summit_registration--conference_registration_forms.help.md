# startup_growth_consulting_summit_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Startup Growth Consulting Summit. Please fill out this form to provide your details and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your registration details (fields 1-2).
2. Select your interests for the summit (field 3).
3. Confirm your consent (field 4).
4. Provide your company information (field 5).
5. Specify your role at the company (field 6).
6. Indicate your industry association (field 7).
7. Enter your location (field 8).
7. Enter your job title (field 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (Registration Details, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your full name and any other relevant registration details.
* **Attendee Details** (Attendee Details, `number`, required/optional): This field is currently not clearly defined in the form YAML. Based on the field type, it seems to be an attendee count or a numerical identifier. Please clarify your intention behind this field if possible.
* **Interests** (Interests, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the topics or sessions you are interested in attending during the summit.
* **Consent** (Consent, `note`, required/optional): This field is likely for obtaining the attendee's consent for the summit. Please review the provided options carefully and make a selection accordingly.
* **Company** (Company, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Role** (Role, `text`, required/optional): Specify your role at the company or organization you represent.
* **Industry** (Industry, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether your industry is related to the summit theme.
* **Location** (Location, `text`, required/optional): Enter your location or city where you will be attending the summit.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or position.

Note: The fields marked as "required/optional" indicate that they are not necessarily required to fill out, but providing them can enhance the registration process or experience.
