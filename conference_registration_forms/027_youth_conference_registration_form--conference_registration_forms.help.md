# Youth Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Conference Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees for the purpose of registration and event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email Address in the email field labeled "Email Address".
4. Enter your Phone Number in the text field labeled "Phone Number".
5. Select your event date in the date field labeled "Event Date".
6. Choose your start time in the time field labeled "Start Time".
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the note field labeled "Note".
8. Select your attendee type from the dropdown menu labeled "Attendee Type".
9. Choose your additional attendees from the dropdown menu labeled "Additional Attendants".
10. Select your preferred date in the date field labeled "Date".
11. Choose your preferred time in the time field labeled "Time".
12. Enter your age in the number field labeled "Age".
13. Enter your height in the number field labeled "Height".
14. Select your preferred option from the dropdown menu labeled "Select Multiple".
15. Choose your preferred option from the dropdown menu labeled "Select One".
16. Enter your email address in the email field labeled "Email 2".
17. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "Phone 2".
18. Add any additional notes or comments in the note field labeled "Note 2".
19. Select your preferred option from the dropdown menu labeled "Select Multiple 3".
20. Choose your preferred option from the dropdown menu labeled "Select One 3".
21. Enter your decimal number in the number field labeled "Decimal".
22. Enter your integer in the number field labeled "Integer".
23. Select your preferred date in the date field labeled "Date 2".
24. Choose your preferred time in the time field labeled "Time 2".
25. Enter your email address in the email field labeled "Email 3".
