# Health Journalism Awareness Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for the Health Journalism Awareness Event. The form helps event organizers and administrators to manage the event attendees and their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Event Registration** page by providing your details.
2. On the **Attendee Details** page, provide your contact information and other personal details.
3. On the **Roles** page, select the roles you have for the event.
4. On the **Session Preferences** page, select the sessions you prefer.
5. On the **Media Organization**, **School**, and **Ngo** pages, select if you belong to any of these organizations or not.
6. On the **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address** pages, provide your contact details.
7. On the **Role**, **Session Preference**, **Role 2**, **Session Preference 2**, **Role 3**, **Session Preference 3**, and **Role 4** pages, select your roles and session preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration**: This is the first page of the form where you can provide your basic details.
	+ **Title**: This field is not present in the form, but it is likely used to enter the title of the event.
* **Attendee Details**: This page collects your contact information and personal details.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Roles**: Select the roles you have for the event.
	+ **Role 1**: Select yes if you have this role.
	+ **Role 2**: Select yes if you have this role.
* **Session Preferences**: Select the session preferences you prefer.
	+ **Session Preference 1**: Select yes if you prefer this session.
	+ **Session Preference 2**: Select yes if you prefer this session.
* **Media Organization**, **School**, and **Ngo**: Select if you belong to any of these organizations or not.
* **Role 3**, **Session Preference 3**, **Role 4**, and **Session Preference 4**: Select your roles and session preferences.
* **Role 5**, **Session Preference 5**, **Role 6**, and **Session Preference 6**: Select your roles and session preferences.
* **Role 7** and **Session Preference 7**: Select your roles and session preferences.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to select the correct roles and session preferences for yourself.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
