# Animal Welfare Tech Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the Animal Welfare Tech Demo session. It will help us tailor the event experience according to your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. You will need to provide your:
* Full name
* Organization or employer
* Job title or position
* Email address
* Phone number
* Technology categories of interest
* Preferred demo session time
* Number of additional team members attending (if any)
* Special accessibility requirements (if any)
* Information on how you heard about the demo

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_name_full`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Organization or Employer** (`organization_name_employer`, `text`, required): Please provide the name of your organization or employer.
* **Job Title or Position** (`job_title_position`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Technology Categories of Interest** (`tech_category_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the categories that interest you:
	+ Shelter Management Software
	+ Wildlife Tracking and GPS Devices
	+ Veterinary Diagnostic and Lab Tools
	+ AI for Animal Behavior Analysis
	+ Fundraising and Donor Engagement Tech
* **Demo Session Preference** (`session_preference_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred demo session time:
	+ Morning Session - 9am to 12pm
	+ Afternoon Session - 1pm to 4pm
	+ Full Day VIP Pass
* **Number of Additional Team Members Attending** (`team_attendee_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of team members attending with you. If you're attending alone, enter 0.
* **Special Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs_req`, `text`, optional): If you have any special accessibility needs or requirements, please let us know.
* **How did you hear about this demo?** (`lead_source_discovery`, `select_one`, required): Tell us how you heard about this demo:
	+ Email Newsletter
	+ Social Media
	+ Colleague Recommendation
	+ Official Website
	+ Industry Event
* **Registration Confirmation Note** (`registration_confirmation_note`, `note`, optional): This field will display a note to confirm your registration and provide further instructions.
