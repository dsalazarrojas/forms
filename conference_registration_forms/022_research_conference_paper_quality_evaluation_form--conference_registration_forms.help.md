# Research Conference Paper Quality Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Conference Paper Quality Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the quality of research papers submitted to conferences. This form helps evaluators assess the paper's content, relevance, and overall quality. It is typically used by conference organizers or reviewers to ensure that papers meet the required standards for presentation and publication.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the paper title in the `Paper Title` field.
2. Select the paper authors in the `Paper Authors` field.
3. Enter the paper scores in the `Paper Scores` field. The scores range from 1 to 5.
4. Select a range for the paper scores in the `Paper Score Range` field.
5. Enter the date and time of evaluation in the `Date` and `Time` fields.
6. Select the paper category in the `Paper Category` field.
7. Select the research area of the paper in the `Research Area` field.
8. Indicate whether the paper has been submitted for review in the `Paper Submission` field.
9. Enter any evaluation comments in the `Evaluation Comments` field.
10. Enter your name as the evaluator in the `Evaluator` field.
11. Set the review date and time in the `Review Date` and `Review Time` fields.
12. Select the current paper review status in the `Paper Review Status` field.
13. Enter any comments about the review status in the `Paper Review Status Comments` field.
14. Select the final paper status in the `Paper Status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Paper Title** (`Paper Title`, text, required): Enter the title of the research paper being evaluated.
* **Paper Authors** (`Paper Authors`, select multiple, required): Select all authors of the research paper.
* **Paper Scores** (`Paper Scores`, number, required): Enter a score for the paper's quality, ranging from 1 to 5.
* **Paper Scores Range** (`Paper Score Range`, text, required): Select a range for the paper's scores.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of evaluation.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of evaluation.
* **Paper Category** (`Paper Category`, select one, optional): Select the category of the paper.
* **Research Area** (`Research Area`, select one, required): Select the research area of the paper.
* **Paper Submission** (`Paper Submission`, select one, optional): Indicate whether the paper has been submitted for review.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`Evaluation Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the paper's evaluation.
* **Evaluator** (`Evaluator`, text, optional): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Review Date** (`Review Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of review.
* **Review Time** (`Review Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of review.
* **Paper Review Status** (`Paper Review Status`, select one, required): Select the current status of the paper review.
* **Paper Review Status Comments** (`Paper Review Status Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the review status.
* **Paper Status** (`Paper Status`, select one, required): Select the final status of the paper.
* **Paper Status Comments** (`Paper Status Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the paper's status.
* **Paper Score Range** (`Paper Score Range`, select one, optional): Select a range for the paper's scores.
* **Paper Category Hint** (`Paper Category Hint`, text, optional): Enter a hint about the paper's category.
* **Paper Submission Hint** (`Paper Submission Hint`, text, optional): Enter a hint about submission.
* **Paper Review Status Hint** (`Paper Review Status Hint`, text, optional): Enter a hint about the review status.
* **Paper Status Hint** (`Paper Status Hint`, text, optional): Enter a hint about the paper's status.
