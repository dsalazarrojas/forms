# HR Policy Modernization Roundtable Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the HR Policy Modernization Roundtable. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below with your information.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields (indicated by \*).
3. Click on "Submit" to submit your completed form for processing.
4. If you have already registered for the roundtable, please do not submit this form again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (5, text, required): Please enter your job title.
* **Session Preferences** (6, select_multiple, required): Select the sessions you are interested in attending. You may select multiple sessions.
* **Accessibility Needs** (7, select_one, required): If you have any accessibility needs, please select "True".
* **Dietary Restrictions** (8, select_multiple, required): If you have any dietary restrictions, please select the options that apply to you.
* **Additional Needs** (9, text, optional): If you have any additional needs that are not listed, please specify them here.
* **Submit** (10, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Cancel** (11, text, optional): Click this button to cancel your registration and return to the main menu.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are using a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* If you have any issues with your registration, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
