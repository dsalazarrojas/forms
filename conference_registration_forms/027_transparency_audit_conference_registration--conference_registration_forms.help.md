# Transparency Audit Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Transparency Audit Conference Registration" form is a self-service tool for attendees to provide their details and preferences for the conference. It helps the conference organizers to collect relevant information about the attendees and their expectations for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details in the "Attendee Details" page, including your name, organization, and position.
2.  Choose your preferred attendance mode (In-person, Remote, or Hybrid) in the "Attendance Mode" page.
3.  Select the sessions you are interested in attending by choosing the relevant options in the "Session Preferences" page.
4.  Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number) in the "Attendee Information" page.
5.  Add any additional comments about the sessions you have chosen in the "Session Comments" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Details**: This section is where you provide your personal details, including your name, organization, and position.
    *   **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
    *   **Attendee Organization**: Enter your current or previous organization where you are working.
    *   **Attendee Position**: Enter your current position or function.
*   **Attendance Mode**: Choose how you plan to attend the conference: In-person, Remote, or Hybrid.
*   **Session Preferences**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending by choosing the relevant options.
    *   **Session 1, Session 2, Session 3**: Select one or multiple sessions you are interested in attending.
*   **Attendee Email**: Enter your official email address.
*   **Attendee Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Attendee Organization**: This field will be filled by the user automatically based on their entry in the "Attendee Name" field.
*   **Attendee Position**: This field will be filled by the user automatically based on their entry in the "Attendee Name" field.
*   **Session Comments**: Add any comments or questions about the sessions you have chosen.
    *   This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments about the sessions you are attending.
