# Corporate Group Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register a corporate group for a conference. Please fill out the form with the required information to help us plan and prepare for the needs of your group.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your corporate group.
2. Ensure you provide accurate details about the number of attendees, group size, and other requirements.
3. Check the "Conference Registration Additional Requirements" field for any specific needs or requests your group may have.
4. Review and confirm your registration details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Registration Group Name** (`conference_registration_group_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your corporate group.
* **Conference Registration Company** (`conference_registration_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Conference Registration Job Title** (`conference_registration_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the primary contact person.
* **Conference Registration Job Function** (`conference_registration_job_function`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job function of the primary contact person.
* **Conference Registration Email** (`conference_registration_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Conference Registration Phone** (`conference_registration_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Conference Registration Start Date** (`conference_registration_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the conference.
* **Conference Registration End Date** (`conference_registration_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the conference.
* **Conference Registration Number of Attendees** (`conference_registration_number_of_attendees`, number, optional): Enter the number of attendees in your group.
* **Conference Registration Group Size** (`conference_registration_group_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of your group.
* **Conference Registration Number of Rooms Requested** (`conference_registration_number_of_rooms_requested`, number, optional): Enter the number of rooms required for your group.
* **Conference Registration Number of Breakout Sessions** (`conference_registration_number_of_breakout_sessions`, number, optional): Enter the number of breakout sessions required for your group.
* **Conference Registration Number of Hotel Rooms** (`conference_registration_number_of_hotel_rooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of hotel rooms required for your group.
* **Conference Registration Additional Requirements** (`conference_registration_additional_requirements`, note, optional): Enter any additional requirements or requests your group may have.
* **Conference Registration Additional Notes** (`conference_registration_additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for our reference.
* **Conference Registration is Paid** (`conference_registration_is_paid`, select_one, optional): Select 'Paid' if your registration is paid, 'Not Paid' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration is Free** (`conference_registration_is_free`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if your registration is free, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration is Meeting Planner** (`conference_registration_is_meeting_planner`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are a meeting planner, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration is Visa Required** (`conference_registration_is_visa_required`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if visa is required for your group, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration has Food and Beverages** (`conference_registration_has_food_and_beverages`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you require food and beverages, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration has Travel Reimbursement** (`conference_registration_has_travel_reimbursement`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if travel reimbursement is required, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration has Meeting Space** (`conference_registration_has_meeting_space`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you require meeting space, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration has Audio Visual Equipment** (`conference_registration_has_audio_visual_equipment`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you require audio visual equipment, 'False' otherwise.
* **Conference Registration has Catering** (`conference_registration_has_catering`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you require catering, 'False' otherwise.
