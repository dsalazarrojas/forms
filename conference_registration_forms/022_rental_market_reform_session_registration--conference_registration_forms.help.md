# Rental Market Reform Session Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the rental market reform session. It includes fields for providing session details, attendee information, and registration status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session details section with the required information.
2. Provide attendee details in the attendee details section.
3. Select the relevant schedules for the session in the session schedules section.
4. Choose your registration status.
5. Add any additional notes if necessary.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Provide your address for contact purposes.
8. Choose the session date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required): Fill in the title of the session.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the attendee.
* **Session Schedules** (`session_schedules`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more schedules for the session. Options are Housing Organization, Council, and Advocacy Group.
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, select_one, required): Choose your registration status, either Registered or Not Registered.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your registration.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter a valid address for contact purposes.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the session.
