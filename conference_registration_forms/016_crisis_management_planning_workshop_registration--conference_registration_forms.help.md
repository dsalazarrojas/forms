# Crisis Management Planning Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Crisis Management Planning Workshop Registration" form is designed to collect essential information from participants attending the workshop. This form will help organizers gather details about the attendees, their preferences, schedules, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please make sure you have all the necessary information and details before filling out the form.
2.  Start by selecting the correct options for "Participant Preferences 4" (ID 14) and "Workshop Schedules 2" (ID 16), which are required fields.
3. Next, fill out the other sections as needed, providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
4. Make sure to review and submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.
