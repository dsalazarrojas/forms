# Higher Ed Digital Strategy Network Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your information for the purpose of registering you as a member of the Higher Ed Digital Strategy Network. Your registration data will be used to facilitate communication and networking within the group.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the designated fields.
- Enter your Email address in the Email field.
- Enter your Organization (university or institution) in the Organization field.
- Type in your Job Title in the Job Title field.
- Select your University from the dropdown menu in the University field.
- For Interests, choose the options that best represent your areas of interest.
- Optionally, enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
- Confirm your Email address in the Confirm Email field.
- Optionally, confirm your Organization in the Confirm Organization field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required/Optional): Enter your institutional email address. This is how we will contact you.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your university or institutional name (e.g. University of California, Los Angeles, Example University).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your job title or position within your institution.
* **University** (`university`, Select One, Required/Optional): Select your university from the dropdown menu.
* **Interests** (`interests`, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Choose the options that best represent your areas of interest.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number (optional but recommended for better communication).
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, Email, Required/Optional): Re-enter your email address to verify accuracy.
* **Confirm Organization** (`confirm_organization`, Text, Required/Optional): Re-enter your Organization name (if you entered it earlier).

## Tips

- Ensure your Email and Confirm Email fields match exactly for accurate verification.
- If you selected "Other" in the University field, please specify the name of your university in the Organization field.
- Be precise with your Interests selection to help others with similar interests connect with you.
- Fill out the form only if you're genuinely interested in joining the network, and avoid duplicating your registration by checking the member list before submitting.
