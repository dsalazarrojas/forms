# HR Policy Optimization Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration details for the HR Policy Optimization Conference. This form is used to collect personal information, preferences, and agreement terms from attendees. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Attendee details**: Please fill out the "Attendee details" section with your personal information.
2. **Attendee preferences**: Select your preferred options for the conference.
3. **Consent**: Choose your consent option.
4. **Name**: Enter your name.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address.
6. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
7. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
8. **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth.
9. **Event Details**: Enter any additional event details.
10. **Session Preferences**: Select your preferred session options.
11. **Workshop Details**: Enter any workshop details.
12. **Workshop Preferences**: Choose your workshop preference.
13. **Conference Details**: Enter any conference details.
14. **Conference Preferences**: Select your preferred conference options.
15. **Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms of the conference.
16. **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of your agreement.
17. **Payment Terms**: Enter your payment terms.
18. **Payment Terms Date**: Enter the date of your payment terms.
19. **Payment Terms Agreement**: Choose your payment terms agreement.
