# Tax Optimization Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for conference registration, specifically for tax optimization conference attendees who want to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required information.
2. Select your interests for the event from the options provided.
3. Provide your email address and phone number, if you wish to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing your name and any other relevant information about yourself that you would like to share with the event organizers.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional details about your attendance to the event, such as your expectations or any special requests.
* **Event Interests** (`event_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the topics or themes that interest you the most about the tax optimization conference.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): This field is for providing any consent or agreement related to the event or your attendance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about the event or receive updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about the event or receive updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact the event organizers.
* Review and submit the form carefully before clicking the submit button.
