# global_hr_strategy_summit_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global HR Strategy Summit registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to enhance their experience and facilitate the planning and execution of the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Conference Registration Form** (Page 1):
	* Enter your details as requested.
2. Provide your **Attendee Details** (Page 2):
	* Include your name, email, job title, and other relevant details as required.
3. Indicate your **Session Interests** (Page 3):
	* Select the sessions you would like to attend.
4. Specify any **Special Requirements** (Page 4):
	* If you have any accessibility, dietary, or other special needs, please describe them here.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Registration Form**: A text field where you enter your information to complete your registration.
* **Attendee Details**: 
	+ This field is intended for providing details such as your name, email address, and job title to help us better understand your background and facilitate communication.
* **Session Interests**:
	+ Select the sessions you are interested in attending. This will help us plan the schedule and allocate resources efficiently.
* **Special Requirements**:
	+ If you have any special requirements, such as accessibility needs, dietary restrictions, or other specific needs, please describe them here. This will help us make necessary arrangements.
* **Conference Registration Form**: A text field for entering your conference registration details.
* **Attendee Details 2**: A duplicate of the **Attendee Details** field, likely a placeholder or a redundant field that can be removed or modified based on your conference needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
