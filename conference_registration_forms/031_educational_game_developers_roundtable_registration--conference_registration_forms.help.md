# Educational Game Developers Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Game Developers Roundtable Registration form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to facilitate networking, discussions, and communication among them. Please read this guide carefully to ensure you complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible, including Conference Name, Role, and Email.
2.  Select your role in the conference (Developer, Organizer, Speaker, Moderator, or Attendee) to help us understand your participation.
3.  If you wish to be assigned a specific tool for communication and collaboration, select the tool from the options provided.
4.  Indicate your interests and discussion topics to ensure we can tailor the conference schedule to your needs.
5.  Enter any relevant details about yourself that you are comfortable sharing.
6.  Review your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Conference Name (conference_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the conference you are registering for.
- **Role (role, select_one, required)**: Choose your role in the conference (Developer, Organizer, Speaker, Moderator, or Attendee).
- **Attendee Details (attendee_details, note, optional)**: Write any relevant details about yourself.
- **Session Interests (session_interests, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the topics that interest you for sessions or discussions.
- **Discussion Topics (discussion_topics, select_multiple, optional)**: Indicate which topics you would like to discuss.
- **Email (email, email, optional)**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone (phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for additional communication.
- **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool, select_one, optional)**: Choose a tool for collaboration from the provided options.
- **Output File (output_file, text, optional)**: Enter the output file if applicable.

**Tips:**

- Ensure the accuracy of your submission, as this information helps us tailor the conference schedule to your needs.
- Be prepared for follow-up questions or requests regarding the provided information.
- If you need help with completing the form, contact us for assistance.
