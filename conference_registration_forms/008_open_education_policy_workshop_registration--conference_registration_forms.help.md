# Open Education Policy Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant details for the Open Education Policy Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. Select the sessions you are interested in by choosing from the options under "Session Choices".
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Add any additional notes about your participation in the "Notes" field.
6. Select your role (Faculty Member or Student) in the "Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your institutional email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your participation or requests for accommodations.
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, select multiple, optional): Select multiple sessions that you are interested in attending. 
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, optional): Choose whether you agree or disagree with the workshop's terms and conditions.
* **Institution** (`participant_institution`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your institution's name.
* **Role** (`participant_role`, select one, optional): Choose your role in the institution (Faculty Member or Student).

## Tips

* Make sure to select only sessions that you are actually interested in, as this will help with planning and logistics.
* If you need assistance or have special requests, use the "Notes" field to provide more details.
