# HR Performance Metrics Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the HR Performance Metrics Summit. It helps us gather essential information about attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To register successfully, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on "Submit" to begin the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Details (`id: 1`)** (`conference_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the conference you are attending.
* **Name (`id: 2`)** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our event materials.
* **Email (`id: 3`)** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and event details.
* **Attendance Type (`id: 4`)** (`attendance_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose between "Full-Time," "Part-Time," or "Guest" to specify your attendance type.
* **Special Needs (`id: 5`)** (`special_needs`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special needs, please specify them here.
* **Phone (`id: 6`)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date (`id: 7`)** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date of your attendance.
* **Time (`id: 8`)** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of your attendance.
* **Note (`id: 9`)** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information about yourself.
* **Additional Info (`id: 10`)** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Add any further details about your experience or attendance.

## Tips

*   Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and relevant to the conference.
*   If you have any special needs, don't hesitate to let us know.
*   Our team will be in touch with you through the email address you provided.
*   We encourage you to choose the correct attendance type for your role in the summit.
