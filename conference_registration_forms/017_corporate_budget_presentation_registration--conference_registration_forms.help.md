# corporate_budget_presentation_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a corporate budget presentation. It's intended for use by companies or organizations to gather details about attendees and their session preferences for a meeting or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each text field with the requested information.
2. Enter your name in the "Form Data" field (Page 1).
3. Provide your details in the "Attendee Details" field (Page 2).
4. Make your session choices in the "Session Choices" field (Page 3).
5. Specify any special requirements in the "Special Requirements" field (Page 4).
6. Repeat steps 3-5 for any additional sessions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data (Page 1)** (`Form Data`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name for this presentation registration.
* **Attendee Details (Page 2)** (`Attendee Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your details for this registration.
* **Session Choices (Page 3)** (`Session Choices`, `text`, required/optional): Choose your preferred sessions for this presentation.
* **Special Requirements (Page 4)** (`Special Requirements`, `text`, required/optional): List any special requirements you might have for this presentation.
* **Session Choices 1 (Page 5)** (`Session Choices 1`, `text`, required/optional): Select your preferred session choices for this presentation.
* **Special Requirements 1 (Page 6)** (`Special Requirements 1`, `text`, required/optional): Specify any special requirements for this session.
* **Session Choices 2 (Page 7)** (`Session Choices 2`, `text`, required/optional): Make your session choices for this presentation.
* **Special Requirements 2 (Page 8)** (`Special Requirements 2`, `text`, required/optional): List any special requirements for this session.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter, especially if you're providing your name or details.
* Ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
