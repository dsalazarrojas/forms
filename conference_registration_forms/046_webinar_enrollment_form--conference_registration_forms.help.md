# Webinar Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Enrollment Form is designed to collect necessary information from users enrolling in a webinar. Please fill out this form to provide your details for the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
2. Enter your First Name in the "first name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your Email Address in the "email address" field. Ensure that it is a valid email address.
5. If you have a Phone Number, enter it in the "phone" field.
6. If you are representing a Company, enter it in the "company" field.
7. If you have a Job Title, enter it in the "job_title" field.
8. If you are located in a specific Department, enter it in the "department" field.
9. Enter your Location in the "location" field.
10. Select your Time Zone from the drop-down list.
11. If applicable, enter your Time Zone Offset in the "time_zone_offset" field.
12. Enter the Webinar Time in the "webinar_time" field.
13. Enter the Start Time of the webinar in the "start_time" field.
14. Enter the End Time of the webinar in the "end_time" field.
15. Enter the Date of the webinar in the "date" field.
16. Optionally, add any additional comments or information in the "webinar_enrollment_form_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address in this field.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company, enter it here.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title if applicable.
* **Department**: Enter your department if applicable.
* **Location**: Enter your location.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone from the drop-down list.
* **Time Zone Offset**: Enter your time zone offset if applicable.
* **Webinar Time**: Enter the time of the webinar.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the webinar.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the webinar.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the webinar.
* **Webinar Enrollment Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your enrollment.

Note: All fields without a red asterisk (\*) are optional, but filling out all fields will help us better understand your preferences and requirements.
