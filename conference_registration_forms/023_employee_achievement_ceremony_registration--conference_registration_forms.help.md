# Employee Achievement Ceremony Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Achievement Ceremony Registration form is used to collect RSVPs and other necessary information from employees who wish to attend a ceremony honoring their achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely.
* Select your preferred date and time for the ceremony.
* If you are bringing guests, indicate the number of guests and their names.
* If you have special requirements, such as accommodations or other needs, please specify.
* Enter a brief RSVP message if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RSVP**: Choose whether you will be attending the ceremony. Select one or more of the options provided (Yes or No).
* **Guests**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring. This will help event organizers arrange for their accommodations.
* **Guest Names**: List the names of your guests.
* **Relation**: If you are bringing guests, choose their relation to you (spouse, colleague, manager, or other).
* **RSVP Message**: Enter a brief message to let event organizers know any special details about your attendance.
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any special needs or requirements for the ceremony, select Yes.
* **RSVP Date**: Choose the date you plan to attend the ceremony.
* **RSVP Time**: Choose the time you plan to attend the ceremony.
