# Internal Leadership Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal leadership team members to register for the Internal Leadership Forum, a meeting where they can discuss and plan strategies for internal initiatives. The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for event planning and logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the primary contact method for communication.
2.  Provide your name in the "Name" field. This will help us identify you and ensure accurate registration.
3.  Select your session preferences from the available options: "Internal Leadership Team" and "Management" and "Leadership".
4.  If you have any additional comments or information, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" field. This is optional.
5.  Enter the event date in the "Event Date" field. This is the date on which the Internal Leadership Forum will take place.
6.  Choose the event start time in the "Event Start Time" field. This is the time when the Internal Leadership Forum will begin.
7.  Provide the address of the meeting location in the "Address" field. This is necessary for logistical purposes.
8.  If you have any parking details or specific requirements, you can mention them in the "Parking Details" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to ensure accurate registration and identification.
*   **Session Preferences** (`Session Preferences`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more options for your preferences, such as "Internal Leadership Team", "Management", or "Leadership".
*   **Session Presentation** (`Session Presentation`, `text`, not required): This field is not currently used; you can skip it.
*   **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the Internal Leadership Forum.
*   **Event Start Time** (`Event Start Time`, `time`, required): Choose the start time of the Internal Leadership Forum.
*   **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the meeting location.
*   **Parking Details** (`Parking Details`, `text`, not required): If you have any parking requirements or specifics, please mention them here.
*   **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or information, provide them here.
