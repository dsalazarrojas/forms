# Workplace Efficiency Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals who wish to participate in the Workplace Efficiency Forum. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details from both the individual and their parent or guardian, if applicable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select whether you are registering for yourself or for someone else.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Select all the session interests that apply to you.
4. Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name or the first name of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name or the last name of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization or the organization of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title or the job title of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the session interests that apply to you or the individual you are registering for. 
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your registration or the registration date of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your registration or the registration time of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your registration or the registration of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments about your registration or the registration of the individual you are registering for. 
* **First Name Parent** (`first_name_parent`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Last Name Parent** (`last_name_parent`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Email Parent** (`email_parent`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Phone Parent** (`phone_parent`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Organization Parent** (`organization_parent`, text, required/optional): Enter the organization of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Job Title Parent** (`job_title_parent`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Session Interests Parent** (`session_interests_parent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the session interests that apply to the individual you are registering for. 
* **Registration Date Parent** (`registration_date_parent`, date, required/optional): Enter the registration date of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Registration Time Parent** (`registration_time_parent`, time, required/optional): Enter the registration time of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Additional Info Parent** (`additional_info_parent`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the registration of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Comments Parent** (`comments_parent`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments about the registration of the individual you are registering for. 
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event. 
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event. 
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes about the event.
