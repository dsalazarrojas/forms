# Conference Keynote Topic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees on their preferences for keynote topics at conferences. By filling out this form, you can help us select engaging keynote topics that cater to your interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address (fields 2 and 3).
2. Select your job role (field 4) from the provided options.
3. Choose your industry (field 6) from the provided options.
4. Indicate your experience level (field 7) from the provided options.
5. Select your primary topic interest (field 9) from the provided options.
6. Choose up to two secondary topics of interest (field 10) from the provided options.
7. Provide up to three specific topic suggestions (fields 12, 13, and 14).
8. Select your preferred speaker type (field 16) from the provided options.
9. Indicate the importance of speaker diversity (field 17) from the provided options.
10. Select your preferred presentation format (field 19) from the provided options.
11. Choose your preferred session duration (field 20) from the provided options.
12. Select your preferred interaction level (field 21) from the provided options.
13. Choose up to three learning objectives (field 22) from the provided options.
14. Describe the current challenges you face (field 23).
15. Share your past keynote feedback (field 24).
16. Provide any additional suggestions (field 25).

## Tips

* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* Choose the options that best fit your preferences and interests.
* If you have any additional suggestions or ideas, don't hesitate to share them.
* The form is intended to be a collaborative process, so feel free to provide feedback and suggestions.
