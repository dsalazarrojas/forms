# Crop Innovation Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Crop Innovation Day Registration Form" is a form designed for attendees to register for the Crop Innovation Day event. This form helps us understand your interests and preferences for the sessions and tracks available during the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each session, depending on whether you are interested in attending that session.
2. Fill in your name, organization, university, and email address in the respective fields.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, which are marked as "Yes" or "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions, please enter them here.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant details about yourself as an attendee.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.

* **University** (`university`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your university or affiliation.
