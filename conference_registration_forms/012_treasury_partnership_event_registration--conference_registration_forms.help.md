# Treasury Partnership Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and stakeholders for a Treasury Partnership Event. It is used to gather session choices, attendee details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide event details such as session choices and attendee details.
2. Select up to 2 sessions of your choice.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number (optional).
4. Add any additional notes about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: (`session_details`, `text`, required: false) - Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Session Choices**: (`session_choices`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Select up to 2 sessions for the event.
* **Session Choices 2**: (`session_choices_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false) - Select up to 2 more sessions (optional).
* **Attendee Details**: (`attendee_details`, `text`, required: false) - Provide your name and any other relevant details.
* **Session Choice**: (`session_choic`, `select_one`, required: false) - Select a single session from the options.
* **Time**: (`time`, `time`, required: false) - Enter the time of your attendance (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
* **Date**: (`date`, `date`, required: false) - Enter the date of your attendance (e.g., 24/02/2024, etc.).
* **Notes**: (`notes`, `note`, required: false) - Add any additional information about your attendance.
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, required: false) - Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, `text`, required: false) - Enter your phone number.
* **Attendee Details 2**: (`attendee_details_2`, `text`, required: false) - Provide any additional details about yourself (optional).
