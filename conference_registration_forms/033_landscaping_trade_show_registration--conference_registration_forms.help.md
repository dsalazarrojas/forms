# Landscaping Trade Show Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register exhibitors and attendees for the Landscaping Trade Show. It captures essential information required for event management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Exhibitor Details" page to begin. This page asks for basic contact information for the exhibitor.
2. Fill out the "Exhibitor Info" page, which captures more specific details about the exhibitor.
3. Move to the "Attendee Details" page to register attendees.
4. Continue to the "Contact Person" page to add a contact person for the exhibitor.
5. Provide your phone number and email address on the "Phone" and "Email" pages.
6. Confirm your email address on the "Confirm Email" page.
7. Choose a date and time for the trade show on the "Date" and "Time" pages.
8. Select the booth number and company name on the "Booth Number" and "Company Name" pages.
9. Enter your website on the "Website" page.
10. Add any additional notes on the "Notes" page.
11. On the "Exhibitor Type" page, select one exhibitor type from the options provided.
12. On the "Select Multiple Exhibitor Services" page, select multiple services offered by the exhibitor.
13. Choose a venue on the "Select One Venue" page.
14. On the "Select Multiple Venues" page, select multiple venues for the exhibitor.
15. Confirm your venue selection on the "Confirm Venue" page.
16. On the "Date 2" page, choose a date for the trade show.
17. Select a time for the trade show on the "Time 2" page.
18. On the "Select Multiple Exhibitor Services 2" page, select multiple services offered by the exhibitor.
19. Add any additional notes on the "Notes" page.
