# Sustainability Summit Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Summit Lead Generation Form is designed to gather contact information and other relevant details from potential attendees and stakeholders for the Sustainability Summit. This form is intended to allow attendees to register for the summit and provide their contact information to be potentially followed up with by the summit organizers for more information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in this text field.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this text field. This field is required.
3. **Title**: Select your title from the list of options provided (e.g. Mr, Ms, Dr, etc.). This field is required.
4. **Job Title**: Enter your job title in this text field. This field is optional.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address in this text field. This field is required and must be in a valid email format.
6. **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this text field.
7. **Department**: Enter your department or area of work in this text field. This field is optional.
8. **Company URL**: Enter the URL of your company or organization in this text field. This field is optional.
9. **Job Function**: Enter a brief description of your job function in this text field. This field is optional.
10. **Industry**: Enter the industry you work in in this text field. This field is optional.
11. **Location**: Enter the location of your company or organization in this text field. This field is optional.
12. **Job Function Other**: If your job function is not listed, please enter a description of your job function in this text field. This field is optional.
13. **Company Slogan**: Enter a brief description of your company's slogan or tagline in this text field. This field is optional.
14. **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job description in this text field. This field is optional.
15. **Company Description**: Enter a brief description of your company or organization in this text field. This field is optional.
16. **Company Tagline**: Enter a brief description of your company's tagline in this text field. This field is optional.
17. **Company Mission**: Enter a brief description of your company's mission statement in this text field. This field is optional.
18. **Job Title Other**: If your job title is not listed, please enter a description of your job title in this text field. This field is optional.
19. **Company Vision**: Enter a brief description of your company's vision statement in this text field. This field is optional.
20. **Company Values**: Enter a brief description of your company's values statement in this text field. This field is optional.
21. **Company Mission Statement**: Enter a brief description of your company's mission statement in this text field. This field is optional.
22. **Company History**: Enter a brief description of your company's history in this text field. This field is optional.
23. **Job Description Other**: If you have a job description that is not listed, please enter it in this text field. This field is optional.
