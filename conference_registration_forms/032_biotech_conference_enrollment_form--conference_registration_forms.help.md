# Biotech Conference Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in registering for the biotech conference. It includes personal details, professional background, and conference preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill out your personal details, including your first name, last name, professional title, and email address.
2.  Next, enter your organization information, including the name and type of your organization.
3.  Select your preferred enrollment type and the days you plan to attend.
4.  Choose your preferred workshops and networking events.
5.  If applicable, provide information about your group registration and any special accommodations you may need.
6.  Select your payment method and provide any necessary payment information.
7.  Finally, acknowledge the terms and conditions of the conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your family name.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your company or institution name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of organization you represent (Biotechnology Company, Pharmaceutical Company, etc.).
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose the number of employees in your organization (1-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1000+).
* **Enrollment Type** (`enrollment_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your conference enrollment type (Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, Virtual Attendance, etc.).
* **Conference Days** (`conference_days`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose which days you plan to attend (Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Day 4, All Days).
* **Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose your preferred workshops (Gene Therapy Development, CRISPR Applications, etc.).
* **Networking Events** (`networking_events`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose which networking events you plan to attend (Welcome Reception, Poster Session, etc.).
* **Professional Background** (`professional_background`, `note`, `optional`): Briefly describe your professional background.
* **Area of Expertise** (`area_of_expertise`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your primary area of expertise (Drug Discovery, Clinical Development, etc.).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your years of experience in biotech or related field (Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, etc.).
* **Conference Goals** (`conference_goals`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Choose your conference goals (Learn About Latest Research, Network with Peers, etc.).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_one`, `required`): Select any dietary restrictions you have (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
* **Accommodation Needs** (`accommodation_needs`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any special accommodations you may need.
* **Hotel Booking Interest** (`hotel_booking`, `select_one`, `required`): Let us know if you need hotel accommodation (Yes, No, etc.).
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, `note`, `optional`): Enter your payment information (Credit Card, Purchase Order, Wire Transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your payment method (Credit Card, Purchase Order, etc.).
* **Promo Code** (`promo_code`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any applicable discount code.
* **Group Registration** (`group_registration`, `select_one`, `required`): Let us know if you are registering as a group (No, Yes, 2-5 Attendees, etc.).
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, `number`, `optional`): If registering as a group, enter the number of attendees.
* **Marketing Preferences** (`marketing_preferences`, `select_one`, `optional`): Let us know how you heard about this conference (Email Newsletter, Professional Association, etc.).
* **Continuing Education** (`continuing_education`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose if you need Continuing Education credits (Yes, No).
* **Areas of Special Interest** (`special_interests`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any areas of interest you have.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, `required`): Agree to the conference policies and terms.
