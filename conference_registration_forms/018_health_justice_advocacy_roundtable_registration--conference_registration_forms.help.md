# Health Justice Advocacy Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Health Justice Advocacy Roundtable. The purpose of the form is to collect information about you and your interests, so we can provide a comfortable and engaging experience for you and other participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on each page to access the fields you need to fill out.
*   Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
*   Review your submission before submitting the form.
*   Click the "Submit" button to send your registration information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
*   **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your identity, including your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share.

### Interests
*   **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the interests you wish to discuss or learn more about during the Roundtable. You can choose more than one option.

    *   Yes: You have an interest in this area and would like to discuss or learn more.
    *   No: You do not have an interest in this area at this time.

### Accessibility Needs
*   **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): If you require any accommodations or assistance to participate in the Roundtable, please select all that apply.
    *   Yes: You require accommodations or assistance to participate in the Roundtable.
    *   No: You do not require accommodations or assistance at this time.

### Accessibility Needs Description
*   **Accessibility Needs Description** (`accessibility_needs_description`, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" for any accessibility needs, please provide a brief description to help us better understand and accommodate your requirements.

### Emergency Contact
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.

### Relationship
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact, such as "spouse" or "friend".

### Emergency Contact Message
*   **Emergency Contact Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, text, optional): Leave any additional message for the emergency contact, such as your expected departure and return times.

### Terms and Conditions
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review the provided Terms and Conditions, which outline the Roundtable's expectations and guidelines for participation.
