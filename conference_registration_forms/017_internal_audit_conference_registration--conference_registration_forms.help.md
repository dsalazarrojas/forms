# Internal Audit Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internal audit conference registration form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to ensure a smooth and successful event. Please follow this guide to fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information (marked as required).
2. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.
3. Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (Required): Please fill out your name, title, and contact information (if available).
* **Session Preferences** (Optional): Select the sessions you are interested in attending. You can select multiple sessions by holding down the Ctrl key while clicking on the sessions.
* **Requirements** (Optional): If you have any specific requirements or requests, please fill this field with a brief description.
* **Contact Information** (Optional): Provide your contact information for follow-up communication.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and event updates.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Date** (Optional): Please enter the date of the event (if it's not already filled out).
* **Time** (Optional): Enter the time of the event (if it's not already filled out).
* **Note** (Optional): Write any additional notes about your registration or requirements.
* **Attendee Session Preferences** (Optional): Confirm your interest in attending sessions.
* **Session 1/2/3** (Optional): If you're interested in specific sessions, fill out the corresponding field with a brief description.
* **Required Session Preference** (Optional): Indicate if you have any required session preference.
* **Attendee Requirements** (Optional): If you have any special requirements, please describe them briefly.
* **Additional Requirements** (Optional): If you have any additional requirements, please list them out.
* **Special Requirements** (Optional): If you have any special requirements that don't fit in other fields, please describe them briefly.
* **Attendee Notes** (Optional): Write any notes about your registration or requirements.
* **Contact Person** (Optional): Provide the name of the contact person (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter the contact person's email address (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter the contact person's phone number (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Organization** (Optional): Enter the contact person's organization (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Title** (Optional): Enter the contact person's title (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Department** (Optional): Enter the contact person's department (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Address** (Optional): Enter the contact person's address (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact City** (Optional): Enter the contact person's city (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Country** (Optional): Enter the contact person's country (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Zip** (Optional): Enter the contact person's zip code (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact State** (Optional): Enter the contact person's state (if it's not already filled out).
* **Contact Notes** (Optional): Write any notes about the contact person (if it's not already filled out).
* **Submit** (Required): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Cancel** (Optional): Click this button to cancel your registration and remove your information from the system.
