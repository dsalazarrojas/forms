# Process Automation Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Automation Roundtable Registration form is used to collect information from attendees and participants for the Process Automation Roundtable conference.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the conference name.
2. Fill in your name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select one or more topics that you are interested in.
5. Choose your role at the event.
6. Provide any additional information in the Participation Consent field.
7. If you have a company, enter it in the Company field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Name**: conference_name (text, required: false) - Enter the name of the conference.
* **Name**: name (text, required: false) - This is your name.
* **Email**: email (email, required: false) - Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: phone (text, required: false) - Enter your phone number.
* **Topic Interests**: topic_interests (select_multiple, required: false) - Select one or more topics that you are interested in.
	+ Options: Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning
* **Role**: role (select_one, required: false) - Select your role at the event.
	+ Options: Attendee, Speaker, Organizer
* **Participation Consent**: participation_consent (text, required: false) - This is a text field for any additional information.
* **Company**: company (text, required: false) - This is your company name.
## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Answer all questions honestly.
