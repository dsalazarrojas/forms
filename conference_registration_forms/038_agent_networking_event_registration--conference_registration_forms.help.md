# Agent Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Agent Networking Event. Please fill out this form to provide your information and let us know about your networking goals and any special requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Full name" field with your name as it should appear on the badge.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for event confirmations and updates.
3. Select your role or title in the "Role or title" field (if applicable).
4. Choose your primary and secondary networking goals in the "Networking goals" field. This will help us understand your expectations from the event.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, choose them in the "Dietary requirements" field.
6. If you have any special requests or needs, provide them in the "Special requests" field.
7. Select your sessions of interest in the "Sessions of interest" field (if applicable).
8. Agree to the event terms and code of conduct in the "Agree to event terms and code of conduct" field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (`full_name`, **Required**): Enter your full name as it should appear on the badge.
- **Company or brokerage** (`company`, **Optional**): If you're representing a company or brokerage, enter it here.
- **Email** (`email`, **Required**): Enter your email address for event confirmations and updates.
- **Role or title** (`role`, **Optional**): Enter your job title or function.
- **Networking goals** (`networking_goals`, **Required**: Select at least one of the following options: **Find partners, Learn best practices, Recruit talent, Promote listings, Other**): Choose your primary and secondary networking goals.
- **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, **Optional**): Select any dietary restrictions you may have: **None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal, Kosher, Other**).
- **Special requests** (`special_requests`, **Optional**): If you have any special requests or needs, provide them here.
- **Sessions of interest** (`sessions_of_interest`, **Optional**): Select your sessions of interest from the options provided.
- **Agree to event terms and code of conduct** (`agree_to_terms`, **Required**): Select **Agree** to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the event terms and code of conduct.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper event organization and communication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your convenience.
