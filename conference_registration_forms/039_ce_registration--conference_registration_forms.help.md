# Continuing Education (CE) Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Continuing Education (CE) Event Registration. It is essential to ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to secure your spot for the event and receive your certificate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required for each field.
2. Enter your full name in the "Attendee Full Name" field.
3. Provide your professional email address in the "Professional Email Address" field, as this is where your certificate will be sent.
4. Enter your employer or organization name in the "Employer or Organization Name" field.
5. Select your professional designation from the options provided in the "Professional Designation" field.
6. Enter your state license number in the "State License Number" field, as this is required for CE credit reporting.
7. Select one breakout session for each time slot in the "Select Your Breakout Sessions" field.
8. Choose your dietary requirements for the provided lunch session in the "Dietary Requirements" field.
9. If you require any specific accessibility accommodations, enter the necessary information in the "Do you require any specific accessibility accommodations?" field.
10. Specify the number of CE credits you are seeking in the "How many CE credits are you seeking?" field.
11. Select the registration type that applies to you from the options provided in the "Registration Type" field.
12. Choose the payment method you prefer from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
13. Confirm that you agree to have your contact information shared with other attendees by selecting "True" in the "I agree to have my contact info shared with other attendees" field.
14. Confirm that you acknowledge the 7-day cancellation and refund policy by selecting "I Acknowledge" in the "I acknowledge the 7-day cancellation and refund policy" field.
15. Finally, submit the form by selecting the current date in the "Today's Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_name_ce`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Professional Email Address** (`attendee_email_ce`, email, required): This is where your certificate will be sent. Please ensure it is your correct professional email address.
* **Employer or Organization Name** (`employer_org_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your employer or organization where you are affiliated with.
* **Professional Designation** (`professional_designation_ce`, select_one, required): Choose your professional designation from the options provided.
* **State License Number** (`license_number_ce`, text, required): Enter your state license number, as this is required for CE credit reporting.
* **Select Your Breakout Sessions** (`sessions_selection_ce`, select_multiple, required): Choose one breakout session for each time slot.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements_ce`, select_one, required): Choose your dietary requirements for the provided lunch session.
* **Do you require any specific accessibility accommodations?** (`accessibility_needs_ce`, text, required): If you require any specific accessibility accommodations, please enter the necessary information.
* **How many CE credits are you seeking?** (`ce_credit_hours_requested`, number, required): Specify the number of CE credits you are seeking.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type_ce`, select_one, required): Choose the registration type that applies to you from the options provided.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_preference_ce`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method you prefer from the options provided.
* **I agree to have my contact info shared with other attendees** (`networking_consent_ce`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to have your contact information shared with other attendees.
* **I acknowledge the 7-day cancellation and refund policy** (`cancellation_policy_ce_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm that you acknowledge the 7-day cancellation and refund policy.
* **Today's Date** (`submission_timestamp_ce`, date, required): Select the current date.

Please review this guide carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information for the Continuing Education (CE) Event Registration form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers directly.
