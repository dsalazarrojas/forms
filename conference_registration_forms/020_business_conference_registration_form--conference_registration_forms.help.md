# Business Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Conference Registration Form is designed to collect attendee information for the conference. This form gathers essential details to organize and manage the conference efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee Information** (fields 2-5): Enter your first name, last name, job title, and company name.
2. Select your **Registration Type** (field 9): Choose the type of registration that suits your needs (Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
3. Choose your **Preferred Attendance Day(s)** (field 10): Select the days you plan to attend.
4. Specify **Dietary Restrictions** (field 11) if you have any dietary needs (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.).
5. Mention **Accessibility Requirements** (field 12) if you need any special accommodations.
6. Select your **Preferred Track** (field 14): Choose your primary interest area (Technology Innovation, Sales and Marketing, etc.).
7. Choose your **Workshop Selection** (field 15): Select the workshops you'd like to attend.
8. Indicate your **Networking Interest** (field 16): Choose how interested you are in networking events.
9. Fill in your **Registration Fee** (field 18): Enter the conference fee amount.
10. Select your **Payment Method** (field 19): Choose how you plan to pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
11. Provide your **Billing Address** (field 20): Enter your address for billing purposes.
12. Review and accept the **Terms and Conditions** (fields 21-22): Agree to the conference's terms and conditions.
13. Consent to the **Privacy Policy** (field 23): Agree to the conference's privacy policy.
14. Indicate your interest in **Marketing Communications** (field 24): Choose if you'd like to receive marketing communications.
