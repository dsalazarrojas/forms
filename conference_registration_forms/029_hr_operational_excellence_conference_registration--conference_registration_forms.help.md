# HR Operational Excellence Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Operational Excellence Conference Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees for the HR Operational Excellence Conference. This form is used to ensure that you provide the necessary details for event organizers to prepare for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Select your Participation Type by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
4. Indicate any Special Needs you may have in the text field.
5. Choose your Registration Type by selecting one option from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the Event Date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Select the Start Time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. Select the End Time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
9. If applicable, enter the Conference URL in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Title** (`conference_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the conference you are interested in attending.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participation Type** (`participation_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple options to indicate your participation type, such as HR Professional, HR Business Leader, or HR Business Partner.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, not required): If you have any special needs, please describe them in this text field.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of registration you are interested in, such as In Person, Remote, or Other.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of the event in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of the event in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Conference URL** (`conference_url`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the URL of the conference.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Use the 24-hour format for Start Time and End Time (HH:MM).
* Be specific and clear in your description of Special Needs.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
