# Conference Pre Networking Session RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help conference attendees RSVP for the pre-networking session. It will collect information about your preferences and needs for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. If you have a Conference Registration ID, enter it in the "Conference Registration ID" field. This is not required but can be helpful for organizers.
3. Enter your Email Address where you can be reached for session details.
4. Choose your Organization or Company from the provided options.
5. Select your Job Title from the provided options.
6. Select your Preferred Networking Session from the provided options.
7. Choose your Primary Networking Goal from the provided options.
8. Select your Industry Sector from the provided options.
9. Choose your Professional Experience Level from the provided options.
10. Briefly describe your Research or Professional Interests (if applicable).
11. Select the topics you would like to discuss in group discussions (if applicable).
12. If you have any dietary restrictions or requirements, select them from the provided options.
13. Specify any dietary notes or needs you may have.
14. If you need any accessibility accommodations, type them in the "Accessibility Requirements" field.
15. Indicate if you will bring business cards to exchange during networking.
16. Choose how you would like to be introduced during the session.
17. Select if you would like to receive the attendee contact list after the event.
18. Add any additional comments or requests in the "Additional Comments or Requests" field (if applicable).
19. Finally, confirm your attendance at the pre-networking session by selecting "Yes I Will Attend" from the "I confirm my attendance at the pre-networking session" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as registered for the conference.
* **Conference Registration ID**: Your conference registration confirmation number, if you have one (optional).
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can be reached for session details.
* **Organization or Company**: Select your current workplace from the provided options.
* **Job Title**: Choose your current position from the provided options.
* **Preferred Networking Session**: Select your preferred session time (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening Mixer).
* **Primary Networking Goal**: Choose your main reason for networking (e.g., Meet Potential Collaborators, Find Mentorship Opportunities, etc.).
* **Industry Sector**: Select your primary industry from the provided options.
* **Professional Experience Level**: Choose your career stage (Student, Early Career, Mid Career, Senior, or Executive).
* **Research or Professional Interests**: Briefly describe your key research or professional interests (if applicable).
* **Topics You Would Like to Discuss**: Select topics for group discussions (if applicable).
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary restrictions or requirements you may have (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
* **Dietary Notes**: Specify any specific dietary needs or requests you may have.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Type any accessibility accommodations you may need.
* **Will you bring business cards**: Indicate if you will bring physical business cards to exchange during networking.
* **How would you like to be introduced**: Choose how you would like to be introduced during the session.
* **Interest in Post-Event Follow Up**: Select if you would like to receive the attendee contact list after the event.
* **Additional Comments or Requests**: Add any other information you think is relevant or would like to share.
* **I confirm my attendance at the pre-networking session**: Confirm your commitment to attend the pre-networking session by selecting "Yes I Will Attend".
