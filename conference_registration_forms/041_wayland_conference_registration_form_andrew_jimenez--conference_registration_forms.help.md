# Wayland Conference Registration Form - Andrew Jimenez - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to register attendees for the Wayland Conference. The purpose of this form is to collect contact information and details about the attendees' role in the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the contact information section with your name and email address.
2. Select your contact method by choosing between "I Agree" or "I do not Agree".
3. Specify your full name in the required field.
4. Select your email address in the required field.
5. If you are attending on a specific date, please enter the date in the "Date" field.
6. If you are attending at a specific time, please enter the time in the "Time" field.
7. Finally, select your role in the conference by choosing between "Attendee", "Speaker", or "Sponsor".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This field is used to collect your contact name and email address. Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
  - **(Contact Information, text, required)**
* **Contact Method**: This field is a confirmation of your contact method. Please select whether you agree or disagree with the contact method used by the conference organizers. This is a required field.
  - **(Contact Method, select_one, required)**
* **Personal Info**: This field is used to collect your personal info, however, it seems it was left empty and might not be used in this specific form. Please leave it blank.
  - **(Personal Info, text, required)** 
* **Full Name**: This field is used to collect your full name, please enter it as it appears on your identification or business card.
  - **(Full Name, text, required)**
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address.
  - **(Email, email, required)**
* **Date**: If you are attending on a specific date, please enter it in this field. However, it seems it was left empty and might not be used in this specific form.
  - **(Date, date, required)**
* **Time**: If you are attending at a specific time, please enter it in this field. However, it seems it was left empty and might not be used in this specific form.
  - **(Time, time, required)**
* **Role**: This field is used to specify your role in the conference.
  - **(Role, select_one, required)**

## Tips
