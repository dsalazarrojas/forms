# Health Equity Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Equity Symposium Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees for event planning, organization, and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address, which will be used for communication and confirmation purposes.
3. Specify your institution affiliation.
4. Indicate your role at the institution.
5. Choose your session interests by selecting one or more options from the list.
6. If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
7. If you have dietary restrictions or preferences, select the applicable options and provide additional details in the "Other (please specify)" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address that we will use for communication and confirmation.
* **Institution (text)**: Specify the name of your institution or organization.
* **Role (text)**: Indicate your role at the institution (e.g., researcher, student, faculty member).
* **Session Interests (select multiple)**: Choose one or more sessions that interest you. These selections will help us tailor our event to your needs.
* **Accessibility Needs (select multiple)**: If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them here (e.g., wheelchair, walker, service dog).
* **Dietary Restrictions (select multiple)**: If you have dietary restrictions, please select them here (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
* **Other (please specify) (text)**: If you have any other dietary needs or requirements not listed, please specify them here and provide details.
* **Details (text)**: If your dietary restrictions are not listed, please provide additional information about your needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper event planning and communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
