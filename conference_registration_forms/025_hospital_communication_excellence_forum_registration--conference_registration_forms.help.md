# hospital_communication_excellence_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hospital Communication Excellence Forum Registration" form is used to collect information from attendees, speakers, or other stakeholders to facilitate their participation in the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Preferred attendance (in person or virtual)
2. (Optional) If you have any dietary needs (e.g. Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free), select them from the list.
3. If you have any accessibility needs (e.g. Wheelchair Accessible, Audio Descriptive Support, Visual Alternative Support), select them from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you for forum updates or information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.
* **Preferred Attendance**:
	+ **In Person**: If you prefer to attend the forum in person.
	+ **Virtual**: If you prefer to attend the forum virtually.
* **Dietary Needs** (Optional):
	+ **Vegetarian**: If you have a vegetarian diet.
	+ **Vegan**: If you have a vegan diet.
	+ **Gluten Free**: If you have a gluten-free diet.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Optional):
	+ **Wheelchair Accessible**: If you require wheelchair accessibility.
	+ **Audio Descriptive Support**: If you need audio descriptive support.
	+ **Visual Alternative Support**: If you need visual alternative support.
