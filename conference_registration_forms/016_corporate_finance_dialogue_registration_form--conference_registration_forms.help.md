# Corporate Finance Dialogue Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Corporate Finance Dialogue Registration Form" is designed to register participants for a corporate finance dialogue event. This form collects information about the participant's user details, interests, and contact information to ensure accurate representation and effective communication during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details, including your name, job title, department, and company.
2. Select the topics you are interested in, such as finance, investment, and risk management.
3. Indicate your consent for the roundtable, webinar, and expert discussion events.
4. Enter your contact details, including email, phone, company, and website.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
* **User Details** (User Details, text, required: false): Enter your name and job title to help us get to know you better.

### Topic Interests
* **Topic Interests** (Topic Interests, select multiple, required: false): Select the topics that interest you the most, such as finance, investment, and risk management.

### Consent Options
* **Roundtable Consent** (Roundtable Consent, select one, required: false): Indicate your consent for the roundtable event.
* **Webinar Interest** (Webinar Interest, select one, required: false): Indicate your interest in the webinar event.
* **Expert Discussion Consent** (Expert Discussion Consent, select one, required: false): Indicate your consent for the expert discussion event.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required: false): Enter your email address, phone number, company, and website to ensure we can contact you effectively.

### Additional Information
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (Company, text, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (Department, text, required: false): Enter your department.
* **Company Address** (Company Address, text, required: false): Enter your company address.
* **Company Website** (Company Website, text, required: false): Enter your company website.

### User Agreement
* **User Agreement** (User Agreement, text, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and honest when filling out your user details.
* Select the topics that genuinely interest you to ensure you are represented during the event.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions to secure your registration.
