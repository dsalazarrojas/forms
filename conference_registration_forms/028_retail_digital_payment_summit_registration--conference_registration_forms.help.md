# Retail Digital Payment Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Retail Digital Payment Summit. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" text field.
2. On the second page, select your participation status in the "Participation Type" select-one field.
3. If you have any special requirements, click on the "Special Requirements" note field to provide additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): This is the field where you can enter your details. Please provide your name, email, or any other relevant information you would like to share.
* **Participation Type** (`participation_type`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you will participate or not in the summit.
	+ Select "Yes" if you plan to participate in the summit.
	+ Select "No" if you do not plan to participate in the summit.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, note, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional requirements you have. For example, if you need any special accommodations, please list them here. 

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
