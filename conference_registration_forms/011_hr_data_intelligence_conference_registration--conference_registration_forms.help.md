# HR Data Intelligence Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees for the HR Data Intelligence Conference. The collected data will be used to manage registrations, track attendee preferences, and provide a seamless experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your main information, including your name and any relevant details.
2. Enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
3. Share your work information, including your job title, company, and department.
4. Specify your preferences, such as dietary requirements and any special requests.
5. Provide information about the conference, including the tracks and sessions you are interested in.
6. Indicate your session preferences, including the topics and speakers you are interested in.
7. Share your dietary preferences, including any dietary restrictions or requirements.
8. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name and relationship to you.
9. Answer any additional questions we may have.
