# Circular Manufacturing Economy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Circular Manufacturing Economy Registration form is designed to collect information from participants in the Circular Manufacturing Economy conference. This includes their personal and professional details, their experience with circular economy practices, and their goals and objectives for attending the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your organization name, job title, and organization type.
3. Select your experience level with circular economy practices.
4. Choose the topics that you are interested in.
5. If applicable, select your current circular economy practices, company size, and preferred workshop sessions.
6. Indicate your dietary restrictions.
7. Choose your attendance type (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is your given name as per your identification documents. Please enter it as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Last Name**: This is your surname as per your identification documents. Please enter it as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email Address**: This is your official email address associated with your professional identity. Please use your work email or a personal email that you use for professional communication.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number that the conference organizers can use to contact you.
* **Organization or Company Name**: This is the name of your organization or company. Please enter the official name as per your business registration documents.
* **Job Title**: This is your job title within your organization. Please enter it as it appears on your business cards or ID card.
* **Organization Type**: This is the type of your organization. Please select from the provided options.
* **Company Size**: This is the size of your company. Please select from the provided options.
* **Primary Manufacturing Sector**: This is your primary manufacturing sector. Please select from the provided options.
* **Annual Production Volume**: This is the volume of production of your company. Please select from the provided options.
* **Topics of Interest (Select all that apply)**: These are the topics you are interested in learning more about. Please select all that apply.
* **Current Circular Economy Practices (Select all that apply)**: These are the circular economy practices you are currently implementing or planning to implement. Please select all that apply.
* **Experience with Circular Economy Practices**: This is your level of experience with circular economy practices. Please select from the provided options.
* **What Do You Hope to Gain from This Conference**: This is the main goal you have for attending the conference. Please enter it in a concise manner.
* **Interested in Networking with Other Participants**: This is your level of interest in networking with other participants. Please select from the provided options.
* **Looking for Collaboration or Partnership Opportunities**: This is your interest in collaboration or partnership opportunities. Please select from the provided options.
* **Areas for Potential Collaboration**: This is the area where you are looking for collaboration or partnership opportunities. Please enter it in a concise manner.
* **Preferred Workshop Sessions (Select all that apply)**: These are the workshop sessions you prefer to attend. Please select all that apply.
* **Dietary Restrictions for Meals**: This is your dietary restriction for meals. Please enter it in a concise manner.
* **Attendance Type**: This is your preferred attendance type. Please select from the provided options.
