# Health Behavior Science Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Behavior Science Symposium Registration form is designed to help attendees provide their details and interests for the symposium. It is not required but highly recommended to complete this form before the symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Main Page section.
2. Then, proceed to the Attendee Details section and provide your details.
3. Next, move to the Session Interests section and select your interests.
4. Finally, provide any special needs you may have in the Special Needs section.
5. Review your form to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page
* **Main Page** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first section of the form. It is not required but it's a good idea to start by filling out this section.

### Attendee Details
* **Attendee Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your details here, including your name, email, and any other information you think is relevant.

### Session Interests
* **Session Interests** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your interests from the list provided. You can select multiple interests.

### Special Needs
* **Special Needs** (`4`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any special needs, such as wheelchair accessibility or dietary restrictions, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the symposium administration for clarification.
* Please review your form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
