# Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge Registration form is designed to collect information from teams participating in the "Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge". This form is used to gather details about the team, their project, and contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) below.
2. Select your team members and leads from the provided options.
3. Enter your project summary and team details in the designated text fields.
4. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Details** (Team Details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your team.
* **Project Summary** (Project Summary, text, optional): Describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, select multiple, optional): Select all team members from the options provided.
* **Team Leads** (Team Leads, select one, optional): Select a team lead from the options provided.
* **Contact Info (Name and Phone)** (Contact Info, text and phone, optional): Enter your name and phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a team lead and at least one team member.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the challenge organizers.
