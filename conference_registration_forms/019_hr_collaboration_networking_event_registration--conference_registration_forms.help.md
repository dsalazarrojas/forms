# HR Collaboration Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the HR Collaboration Networking Event. The form is intended to be quick and easy to fill out, and the information collected will be used to create a list of attendees that will be shared with other attendees and event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Details: Provide your name, any relevant job title, and any other identifying information that you would like to share with other attendees.
2. Share Event Details: Briefly describe what you are looking for at the HR Collaboration Networking Event. This will help other attendees understand your interests and preferences.
3. Select your Interests: Choose the categories that align with your interests and areas of expertise. This will help other attendees connect with you based on shared interests.
4. Enter Contact Information: Provide your email address and phone number, so that other attendees can reach out to you for follow-up communication.
5. Provide Consent: You will need to confirm that you are willing to have your contact information shared with other attendees.
6. Review and Confirm: Double-check that your information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (1, text, required: false): This field is for your personal details, such as your name and job title. Please enter the information you would like to share with other attendees.
* **Event Details** (2, text, required: false): Briefly describe what you are looking for at the HR Collaboration Networking Event. This will help other attendees understand your interests and preferences.
* **Interests** (3, select multiple, required: false): Choose the categories that align with your interests and areas of expertise. Select one or more from the options provided.
* **Contact Info** (4, text, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number, so that other attendees can reach out to you for follow-up communication.
* **Email** (6, email, required: false): Enter the email address that you would like to share with other attendees.
* **Phone** (7, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, so that other attendees can reach out to you for follow-up communication.
* **Confirm Email** (9, email, required: false): Double-check that your email address is correct by entering it again.
* **Confirm Phone** (10, text, required: false): Double-check that your phone number is correct by entering it again.
* **Confirm Notes** (11, note, required: false): Double-check that your notes are correct by re-entering them. This is to ensure that your contact information is up-to-date.
* **Consent** (5, text, required: false): Confirm that you are willing to have your contact information shared with other attendees.
