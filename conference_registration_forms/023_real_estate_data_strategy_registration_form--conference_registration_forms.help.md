# Real Estate Data Strategy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants for the Real Estate Data Strategy conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, paying attention to the required fields marked as `required` true.
2. Select the correct options for `Interested Topics`, `Participant Agreement`, and `Participant Other Agreement` fields.
3. Enter your contact information accurately in the `Contact Email`, `Contact Phone`, `Participant Name`, `Participant Title`, `Participant Company`, and `Participant Job Title` fields.
4. Enter your address details in the `Participant Address`, `Participant City`, `Participant State`, `Participant Postal`, and `Participant Country` fields.
5. Enter your language and timezone offset correctly in the `Participant Language` and `Participant Timezone Offset` fields.
6. Fill out any other fields as required or if you have additional comments or interests to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**
  • **Participant Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or ID.
  • **Participant Title** (`text`, `required`): Enter your title or position at your company.
  • **Participant Company** (`text`, `required`): Enter your company's name.
  • **Participant Job Title** (`text`, `required`): Enter your job title.
  • **Participant Address** (`text`, `required`): Enter your address for communication.
  • **Participant City** (`text`, `required`): Enter your city name.
  • **Participant State** (`text`, `required`): Enter your state or province name.
  • **Participant Postal** (`text`, `required`): Enter your postal or zip code.
  • **Participant Country** (`text`, `required`): Enter your country name.
  • **Participant Timezone** (`text`, `required`): Enter your timezone offset.
  • **Participant Language** (`text`, `required`): Enter your language.
  • **Participant Timezone Offset** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your timezone offset, if needed.
  • **Participant Dialect** (`text`, `optional`): This field might be used to specify any dialect or accents used.
  • **Participant Comments** (`text`, `optional`): Add any comments or notes about yourself.
  • **Participant Additional Comments** (`text`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or notes about yourself.
* **Contact Information**
  • **Contact Email** (`email`, `required`): Enter your contact email address.
  • **Contact Phone** (`text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Agreements**
  • **Participant Agreement** (`select_one`, `required`): Select one of the agreements provided.
  • **Participant Other Agreement** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any other agreement you have.
* **Registration Data**
  • **Interested Topics** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the topics you are interested in.
  • **Participant Interest** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any other participant's interests, if applicable.
* **Miscellaneous**
  • **Participant Comments** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any comments or notes about your participation in the conference.
  • **Participant Timezone Offset** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any timezone offset for your location.
