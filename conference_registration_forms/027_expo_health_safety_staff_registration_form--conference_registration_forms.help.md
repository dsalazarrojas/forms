# Expo Health & Safety Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering expo health and safety staff to ensure their information is documented for any emergencies or incidentals during the event. Please fill out this form to provide your details to our event staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the following fields with your details:
	* Your name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* Email: Enter your work email address.
	* Phone: Enter your work phone number.
	* Job title: Enter your job title or position within the expo team.
	* Team member of: If you're part of a team, please specify which team you belong to.
	* Emergency contact name: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
	* Emergency contact phone: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
	* Emergency contact email: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
2. Provide your shift details:
	* Shift start: Enter the start time of your shift.
	* Shift end: Enter the end time of your shift.
3. Specify your location:
	* Location: Enter the location where you will be working during your shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Enter your work email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
- **Job title**: Enter your job title or position within the expo team.
- **Team member of**: If you're part of a team, please specify which team you belong to.
- **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
- **Shift start**: Enter the start time of your shift, using the standard date format (DD-MM-YYYY).
- **Shift end**: Enter the end time of your shift, using the standard date format (DD-MM-YYYY).
- **Location**: Enter the location where you will be working during your shift.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the expo staff for assistance.
