# Expense Allocation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information and expense details from attendees of a conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Info section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. In the Expense Info section, provide details about your expenses, such as dates, amounts, and a brief description.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, fill out the Additional Notes section.
4. Choose your preferred contact method from the Contact Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, optional): This section is for filling out your name and any other relevant information.
* **Expense Info** (`Expense Info`, text, optional): Provide details about your expenses, such as dates, amounts, and a brief description.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): This is a submission button, not a field. It is required for submitting the form.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, fill out this section.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, select one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method from the options provided: Email, Phone, or Both.
