# Speaker Registration And Material Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Speaker Registration And Material Upload" form is a required form for speakers to register for a conference and upload necessary materials. This form helps the conference organizers gather essential information about the speaker and their session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the **Speaker Details** field with your name, title, and bio.
2. Upload your speaker photo and profile information in the **Speaker File Upload** field.
3. Provide details about your session in the **Session Details** field.
4. Confirm whether you will be providing session information in the **Speaker Session Info** field.
5. Fill out the rest of the fields with your personal and session information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Details** (`speaker_details`, text, required): Fill out your name, title, and bio in this field.
* **Speaker File Upload** (`speaker_file_upload`, text, optional): Upload your speaker photo and profile information here. This field is optional, but it will help the conference organizers get to know you better.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your session, including the topic, description, and any other relevant information.
* **Speaker Session Info** (`speaker_session_info`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether you will be providing session information or not. If you choose "Yes", you will be able to upload additional session materials.
* **Speaker Additional Info** (`speaker_additional_info`, note, optional): Fill out any additional information about yourself or your session.
* **Session Info** (`session_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Fill out the date of your session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Fill out the time of your session.
* **Speaker Notes** (`speaker_notes`, note, optional): Fill out any additional notes about yourself or your session.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, optional): Fill out your email address.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, optional): Fill out your phone number.
* **Speaker Address** (`speaker_address`, text, optional): Fill out your address.
* **Speaker City** (`speaker_city`, text, optional): Fill out your city.
* **Speaker Country** (`speaker_country`, text, optional): Fill out your country.
* **Speaker Postal Code** (`speaker_postal_code`, text, optional): Fill out your postal code.
* **Speaker File 2 Upload** (`speaker_file_2_upload`, text, optional): Upload additional file materials.
* **Speaker File 3 Upload** (`speaker_file_3_upload`, text, optional): Upload even more file materials.
* **Speaker File 4 Upload** is missing from the input schema, but we'll ignore that for now. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact the conference organizers for help.
* Review your form carefully before submission to ensure you've completed it correctly.
