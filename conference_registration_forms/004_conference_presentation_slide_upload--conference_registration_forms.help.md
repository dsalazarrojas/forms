# Conference Presentation Slide Upload - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Conference Presentation Slide Upload form is a crucial part of the conference registration process. It allows presenters to provide information about their presentation, ensuring a smooth and organized conference experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your personal details: 
    - **Presenter Full Name** (`presenter_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on conference materials.
    - **Email Address** (`email`): Provide your contact email address for any presentation-related communications.
    - **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number for any last-minute questions or issues.
    - **Organization or Institution** (`organization`): Specify your affiliation, if any.
2. Enter your presentation details: 
    - **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`): Enter the exact title of your presentation.
    - **Session Track** (`session_track`): Choose the track your presentation falls under.
    - **Presentation Date** (`presentation_date`): Select the scheduled date of your presentation.
    - **Presentation Time Slot** (`presentation_time`): Choose your assigned time slot.
3. Prepare your presentation: 
    - **Slide Deck Format** (`slide_format`): Select the format of your presentation (e.g., PowerPoint, PDF, Keynote).
    - **Slide Deck** (`slide_file`): Upload your presentation file, up to 50MB.
    - **Slide Deck Version** (`slide_version`): Confirm that this is the final version of your presentation.
4. Media and Technical Requirements: 
    - **Embedded Media** (`embedded_media`): If your presentation contains videos, audio, or animations, select the appropriate option.
    - **Slide Aspect Ratio** (`aspect_ratio`): Specify the aspect ratio of your slides.
    - **Fonts Embedded** (`font_embedding`): Confirm if fonts are embedded in your presentation.
5. Additional Requirements: 
    - **Special Technical Requirements** (`special_requirements`): If your presentation has any specific setup needs.
    - **Need Backup Copy on USB** (`backup_needed`): If you require a backup copy of your presentation.
6. Equipment and Recording: 
    - **Use Own Laptop** (`presenter_laptop`): Confirm if you will use your own laptop.
    - **Laptop Type** (`laptop_type`): Specify the type of laptop you will use (if applicable).
    - **Wireless Presentation Clicker Needed** (`wireless_clicker`): If you require a wireless presentation clicker.
    - **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`): Confirm your consent to being recorded for conference archives.
7. Final Confirmation: 
    - **Confirm Upload Complete and Accurate** (`upload_confirmation`): Verify that your submission is complete and accurate.
