# fiscal_policy_review_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in reviewing fiscal policies related to finance, development, or both.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by providing your User Details. This is the first page of the form.
2. On the second page, choose the topics that interest you from the provided options: Public Finance, International Public Finance, and Economic Development.
3. If you have any questions or comments, fill in the "Questions" field. This is optional.
4. Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
5. Finally, complete the form by filling in all required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Topic Interests**: Select all topics that interest you from the provided options. This will help us tailor the content and discussions for your needs.
* **Questions**: If you have any questions or comments, fill this field in. This is an open text field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you before and after the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you quickly and easily, if necessary.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to complete the form successfully.
* If you are interested in more than one topic, select all relevant options in the "Topic Interests" field.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, you can fill in the "Questions" field.
