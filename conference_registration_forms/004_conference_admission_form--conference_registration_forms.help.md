# Conference Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for a conference. It collects required information to process admission and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure all required fields (indicated by *) are completed.
2. Select your preferred admission ticket type.
3. Choose your registration category.
4. Provide your preferred payment method.
5. Check the terms and conditions box to acknowledge agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Full Name** (Attendee Full Name, text, *): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, *): Enter your email address where you will receive confirmation and updates.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (Mobile Phone Number, text, *): Enter your mobile phone number for communication.
* **Organization / Company Name** (Organization / Company Name, text, *): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Select Admission Ticket Type** (Select Admission Ticket Type, select_one, *): Choose the type of admission ticket you require:
	+ General Admission - Full Conference
	+ VIP / Executive Pass
	+ Single Day Pass - Day 1
	+ Single Day Pass - Day 2
	+ Student / Academic Rate
* **Registration Category** (Registration Category, select_one, *): Choose your registration category:
	+ Early Bird Registration
	+ Regular Registration
	+ Late / On-site Registration
* **Dietary Requirements or Restrictions** (Dietary Requirements or Restrictions, select_multiple, optional): Check any dietary restrictions you require:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten Free
	+ Nut Allergy
	+ Halal
	+ Kosher
* **Special Accessibility Requirements** (Special Accessibility Requirements, text, optional): Describe any special accessibility needs.
* **How did you hear about this conference?** (How did you hear about this conference?, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the conference:
	+ Email Newsletter
	+ Social Media
	+ Colleague Recommendation
	+ Industry Publication
	+ Search Engine
* **Number of Additional Attendees in Group** (Number of Additional Attendees in Group, number, *): Enter the number of additional attendees registering with you.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Preferred Payment Method, select_one, *): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Invoice Organization
* **I agree to the conference terms and conditions and cancellation policy** (I agree to the conference terms and conditions and cancellation policy, select_one, *): Check the box to acknowledge agreement with the conference terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to check the conference agenda for any relevant sessions or workshops related to your area of expertise.
* Review the conference terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please contact the conference organizers.
* This form must be completed in full before proceeding with payment.
