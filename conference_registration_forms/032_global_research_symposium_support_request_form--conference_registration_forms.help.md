# Global Research Symposium Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request support for the Global Research Symposium. Please fill it out carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your needs are adequately understood and met.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name for the event or research project** (`research_event_name`):
	* Enter the name of the research event, project, or symposium you are interested in attending.
2. **Select a date for the event** (`research_event_date`):
	* Enter the date of the research event you are interested in attending.
3. **Enter a brief description of the research topic** (`research_topic`):
	* Provide a short description of the research topic or area of study you are interested in.
4. **Provide your contact information** (`contact_person`):
	* Enter your name and title (e.g., "Dr.", "Prof.") to help the organizers get in touch with you.
5. **Enter your email address** (`email`):
	* Enter your email address so that the organizers can contact you directly.
6. **Enter your phone number** (`phone`):
	* Enter your phone number to ensure that you can be reached quickly and efficiently.
7. **Enter your address** (`address`):
	* Enter your address so that the organizers can send you any necessary information or materials.
8. **Enter any additional requests for support** (`research_symposium_support_request`):
	* Use the note field to describe any specific needs or requests you have for support. Be as detailed as possible.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your needs are met.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to contact the organizers directly.
* If you have already contacted the symposium organizers, do not fill out this form again.
