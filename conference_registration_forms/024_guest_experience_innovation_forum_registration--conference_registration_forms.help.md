# Guest Experience Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests who are interested in attending the Guest Experience Innovation Forum. The form helps us understand their interests and requirements, and provides a more personalized experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the second last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Select your interests for the sessions you're interested in attending. You can choose from the dropdown menu.
5. If you have any special requirements, enter them in the special requirements field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Session interests** (`session_interests`, select multiple, required: false): Select the session(s) you're interested in attending.
* **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, required: false): If you have any special requirements for your attendance (e.g. accessibility needs), please enter them in this field.

## Tips
* Please be specific when selecting session interests.
* If you have any questions or need assistance completing this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is for external use only, and is not intended for internal purposes.
