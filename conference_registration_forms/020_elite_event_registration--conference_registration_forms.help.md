# Elite Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elite Event Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees for an event. This form will help you to provide essential details about the event, allowing us to better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details, including attendee information and contact details.
2. Select the event preferences that apply to you.
3. Provide your payment method and event date/time information.
4. Add any additional attendees to your list.
5. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details (1)** (text, required: false): Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Event Preferences (2)** (text, required: false): Select any preferences that apply to you, such as VIP or General event categories.
* **Contact Info (3)** (text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including address for shipping and billing.
* **Consent (4)** (text, required: false): Please provide consent for the event.
* **Address (5)** (text, required: false): Enter your address for shipping and billing purposes.
* **Email (6)** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (7)** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date (8)** (date, required: false): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time (9)** (time, required: false): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Location (10)** (text, required: false): Enter the event location.
* **Event Type (11)** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of event you are registering for (e.g., VIP, High-End, General).
* **Event Status (12)** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of your event registration (e.g., Confirmed, Cancelled, Pending).
* **Payment Method (13)** (select_one, required: false): Select your payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Attendee List (14)** (text, required: false): Enter a list of attendees if you are registering for multiple people.
