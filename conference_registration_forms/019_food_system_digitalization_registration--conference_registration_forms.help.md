# Food System Digitalization Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Food System Digitalization Registration form is designed to gather information from interested individuals who want to take part in the conference. This form is used to register participants and collect their details, interests, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, and fill out the required information.
3. You can skip fields that are not mandatory, but providing all the necessary information will help us better understand your interests and needs.
4. Once you have filled out the form, click on the submit button to send your registration details to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registration Program

* **Registration Program** (`registration_program`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about your registration status for the conference. Select "Yes" if you are interested in registering for the conference, or "No" if you are not.

*   **Registration Program** (`registration_program`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select "Yes" if you are interested in registering for the conference, or "No" if you are not.

### Participant Interests

* **Participant Interests** (`registration_participant_interests`, text, optional): In this field, please list your interests and what you are looking for in the conference. This will help us tailor the experience to your needs.

*   **Participant Interests** (`registration_participant_interests`, text, optional)
    Type in a brief description of your interests, and what you are looking for in the conference.

### Participant Details

* **Participant Details** (`registration_participant_details`, select_one, optional): This field asks about your participant details, specifically if you have any specific needs or requirements. Select "Yes" if you have any specific needs, or "No" if you do not.

*   **Participant Details** (`registration_participant_details`, select_one, optional)
    Select "Yes" if you have any specific needs or requirements, or "No" if you do not.

### Participant Consent

* **Participant Consent** (`registration_participant_consent`, number, optional): This field asks for your consent to share your information with other participants and organizers. Please note that this is not a mandatory field, but providing your consent will allow us to share your details with others for the purpose of networking and conference organization.

*   **Participant Consent** (`registration_participant_consent`, number, optional)
    This field is optional, but if you consent, please type "1", and if you do not, type "0".
