# Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preventive Medicine Innovation Summit Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees, including their contact details, attendance status, and sharing preferences. This guide will walk you through the form fields and help you complete it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Summit Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Name** and **Contact Email** in the subsequent fields.
3. Select your **Attendance Type** (Attending or Not Attending).
4. Enter your **Contact Phone Number**.
5. Fill in your **Organization**, **Job Title**, and **City** and **Country** details.
6. Write a brief **Bio** to introduce yourself.
7. Choose who you would like to **Share Details With** (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
8. Decide who you would like to **Share Email With** (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
9. Select whether you would like to **Share Phone Number With** (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
10. Choose who you would like to **Share Bio With** (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
11. Select whether you would like to **Share Address With** (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
12. Decide if you would like to **Share Notes With** (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
13. Finally, confirm your registration by clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summit Name**: Enter the name of the summit you are attending.
* **Name**: Provide your full name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Attendance Type**: Select Attending or Not Attending to confirm your status.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your mobile or phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization**: Enter your company or organization's name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title within your organization.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you reside or work.
* **Bio**: Write a brief introduction about yourself (optional, but recommended).
* **Share Details With**: Choose who you would like to share your details with.
* **Share Email With**: Select who you would like to share your email with.
* **Share Phone Number With**: Choose who you would like to share your phone number with.
* **Share Bio With**: Choose who you would like to share your bio with.
* **Share Address With**: Select who you would like to share your address with.
* **Share Notes With**: Choose who you would like to share your notes with.
* **Share With**: Decide if you would like to share your registration information with others (Summit Organizers, All Attendees, or My Company).
* **Share Name With**: Choose who you would like to share your name with.
* **Share Organization With**: Select who you would like to share your organization's information with.
* **Share Role With**: Choose who you would like to share your job title with.
* **Share Job Title With**: Select who you would like to share your job title with.
* **Share Phone With**: Choose who you would like to share your phone number with.
* **Submit**: Confirm your registration by clicking this button.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your registration information (optional, but recommended).

Note: This guide is designed to be a comprehensive reference for end-users. Please review and update the guide as necessary to reflect any changes to the form's structure or content.
