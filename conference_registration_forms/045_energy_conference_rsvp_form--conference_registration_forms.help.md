# Energy Conference RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Conference RSVP Form is used to collect attendee details for the conference, allowing organizers to plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted. Start by entering your name and contact details in the "Attendee Details" section. Then, select your attendance status and accessibility needs in the "Attendance" and "Accessibility needs" sections. If you have specific dietary needs, select them in the "Dietary needs" section. Finally, choose the sessions you are interested in attending in the "Sessions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to help the organizers reach out to you.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact information such as email or phone number to help the organizers reach out to you.
* **Are you attending**: Select whether you will be attending the conference or not.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Select any accessibility needs you may have, such as wheelchair accessibility or accommodations for mobility impairments.
* **Dietary Needs**: Select any dietary restrictions or preferences you have, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Sessions**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Sessions of interest**: Select any sessions that you are particularly interested in or that you would like to prioritize attending.
* **Are you attending**: This field is redundant and is not recommended to be filled out. It is intended for the organizers to keep track of attendee status.
* **Accessibility needs**: Select any additional accessibility needs you may have, such as American Sign Language interpretation or closed captions.
* **Dietary needs**: Select any additional dietary needs you may have, such as gluten-free or other specific dietary requirements.
* **Sessions**: This field is intended to select the sessions you will actually be attending.
* **Sessions of interest**: This field is intended to select the sessions you are interested in or that you would like to attend.
