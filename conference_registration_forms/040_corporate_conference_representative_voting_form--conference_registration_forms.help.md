# Corporate Conference Representative Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for corporate conference representatives to provide feedback, vote on conference aspects, and validate their delegate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and role in the respective fields.
2. Provide feedback on your experience with the conference.
3. Select your vote on the given options.
4. If applicable, include your contact information and comments.
5. Verify if you are a delegate or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (required)**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Role (required)**: Specify your role at the conference (e.g., delegate, staff, etc.).
* **Company (optional)**: If you're representing a company, enter it here.
* **Feedback (required)**: Share your thoughts on the conference, what went well, and what didn't.
* **Vote (required)**: Choose one of the three options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Comments (optional)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about the conference.
* **Contact info (optional)**: Select any relevant contact information (e.g., Email, Phone, Social Media).
* **Post event follow-up (optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted after the event, select a date.
* **Post event follow-up time (optional)**: Choose a time for post-event follow-up.
* **Verified Delegate (required)**: Confirm if you are a delegate or not.
* **Comments2 (optional)**: Add any further comments or thoughts.
Please ensure you've filled out all required fields accurately.
