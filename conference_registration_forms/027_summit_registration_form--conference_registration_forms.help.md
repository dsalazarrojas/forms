# Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summit Registration Form is a crucial tool for gathering essential information from attendees, managers, and delegates for the summit. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary contact and affiliation information is collected correctly, facilitating proper communication and organization during the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as `required: true` in the form.
2. For non-required fields, fill in the information as needed, but be aware that leaving them blank may cause discrepancies in our records.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates during the summit.

### Name of Organization
* **Name of Organization** (`name_of_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization, if applicable.

### Name of Department
* **Name of Department** (`name_of_department`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your department, if applicable.

### Title of Position
* **Title of Position** (`title_of_position`, text, required: false): Enter your title or position within your organization.

### Name of Manager
* **Name of Manager** (`name_of_manager`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have a manager, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes'.

### Email of Manager
* **Email of Manager** (`email_of_manager`, email, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for having a manager, please enter their email address.

### Confirm Email
* **Confirm Email** (`email_2`, email, required: false): Re-enter your email address for confirmation purposes.

### Name of Designee
* **Name of Designee** (`name_of_designee`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have a designee, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes'.

### Title of Designee
* **Title of Designee** (`name_of_designee_title`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for having a designee, enter their title.

### Name of Delegate
* **Name of Delegate** (`name_of_delegate`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you have delegates, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes' for each delegate.

### Title of Delegate
* **Title of Delegate** (`name_of_delegate_title`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for having delegates, enter their titles.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter your date of birth.

### Time of Birth
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required: false): If applicable, enter your time of birth.

### Name of Attendee
* **Name of Attendee** (`name_of_attendee`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the attendee, if applicable.

### Title of Attendee
* **Title of Attendee** (`name_of_attendee_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the attendee, if applicable.

### Title of Manager
* **Title of Manager** (`name_of_manager_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of your manager, if applicable.

### Name Of Manager Email
* **Name Of Manager Email** (`name_of_manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of your manager, if applicable.

### Name Of Manager 2
* **Name Of Manager 2** (`name_of_manager_2`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have a second manager, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes'.

### Email Of Manager 2
* **Email Of Manager 2** (`email_of_manager_2`, email, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for having a second manager, enter their email address.

### Name of Designee 1
* **Name of Designee 1** (`name_of_designee_1`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have a first designee, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes'.

### Title of Designee 1
* **Title of Designee 1** (`name_of_designee_1_title`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for having a designee, enter their title.

### Name of Delegate 1
* **Name of Delegate 1** (`name_of_delegate_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you have a first delegate, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes'.

### Title of Delegate 1
* **Title of Delegate 1** (`name_of_delegate_1_title`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Yes' for having a delegate, enter their title.

### Name of Delegate 2
* **Name of Delegate 2** (`name_of_delegate_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you have a second delegate, and if so, confirm this selection by selecting 'Yes'.

Please note that some fields are optional but may cause discrepancies in our records if left blank. It is essential to fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure proper communication and organization during the summit. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
