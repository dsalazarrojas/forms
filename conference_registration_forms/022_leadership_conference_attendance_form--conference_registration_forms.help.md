# Leadership Conference Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Conference Attendance Form is designed to gather essential details about attendees to the leadership conference. This form helps us track and manage the attendance efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you about conference updates and information.
3. Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication or in case of last-minute conference changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required/optional): Your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required/optional): Your email address for conference updates and communication.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, required/optional): Your phone number for any necessary follow-up or last-minute conference changes.
