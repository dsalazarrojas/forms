# Speed Networking With Experts Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Speed Networking With Experts Registration form efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the registration process.
2. Fill in your details according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speed Networking With Experts Registration** (`speed_networking_with_experts_registration`, text, optional): This is the title of your registration submission. Feel free to fill it in with any information that describes your participation in the event.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to provide information about yourself:
	+ `attendee name`: Your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
	+ `attendee email`: Your professional email address for communication with event organizers.
	+ `attendee company`: Your workplace or current organization name.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Describe your expectations, interests, or areas of focus for the networking event. This will help organizers connect you with relevant experts.
* **Preferred Times** (`preferred_times`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred time slots for the networking event:
	+ `morning`
	+ `afternoon`
	+ `evening`

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling in your preferences and details.
* Make sure to review your submission before submitting to avoid errors.
* Reach out if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
* Review the event organizers' guidelines for any additional requirements or recommendations.
