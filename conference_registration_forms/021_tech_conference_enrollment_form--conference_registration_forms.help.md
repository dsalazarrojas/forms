# Tech Conference Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to enroll in the Tech Conference. It gathers information about their preferences and interest in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the conference in the "Conference Name" field.
2. Provide your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Indicate whether you are interested in attending by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Attendee Interest".
5. Choose which session(s) you are interested in attending by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Session Interest".
6. Select the date of the event from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
7. Choose the time of the event from the "Event Time" field.
8. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
9. Provide any additional preferences or comments about the event in the "Event Preferences" field.
10. Select your type of attendance (e.g. "Speaker", "Attendee", etc.) in the "Attendee Type" field.
11. Provide a message to the event organizers in the "Attendee Message" field.
12. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Enter the path to the output file in the "Output File" field.
14. Enter a unique ID for the form in the "Form ID" field.
15. Select the category of the form in the "Form Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the conference you are enrolling in.
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Interest** (select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you are interested in attending by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Session Interest** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose which session(s) you are interested in attending by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Event Date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (time, required: false): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Preferences** (text, required: false): Provide any additional preferences or comments about the event.
* **Attendee Type** (text, required: false): Select your type of attendance (e.g. "Speaker", "Attendee", etc.).
* **Attendee Message** (text, required: false): Provide a message to the event organizers.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Output File** (text, required: false): Enter the path to the output file.
* **Form ID** (number, required: false): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Form Category** (text, required: false): Select the category of the form.

Tips:
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your enrollment.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
