# seminar_convention_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the seminar convention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the third field.
4. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the list in the fourth field.
5. If you are attending with someone else, select the option and fill in their **Person Accompanying Attendee Name**, **Person Accompanying Attendee Phone**, and **Person Accompanying Attendee Email** fields.
6. Enter the **Attending Convention** date in the tenth field.
7. Enter the **Convention Fee** in the eleventh field.
8. Check if the **Registration Fee Paid** checkbox to confirm payment.
9. Add any **Attendee Notes** in the last field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required: False): Choose how you would like to pay for the convention. Options are:
  + Cash
  + Card
  + Other
* **Attending with** (Select Multiple, Required: False): If you are attending with someone else, select this option and fill in their information in the subsequent fields.
* **Person Accompanying Attendee Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter the name of the person you are attending with.
* **Person Accompanying Attendee Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter the phone number of the person you are attending with.
* **Person Accompanying Attendee Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter the email address of the person you are attending with.
* **Attending Convention** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date you will be attending the convention.
* **Convention Fee** (Number, Required: False): Enter the fee you are paying for the convention.
* **Fee Total** (Number, Required: False): This field is auto-calculated based on the convention fee and other factors.
* **Registration Fee Paid** (Select One, Required: False): Confirm if you have paid the registration fee.
* **Attendee Notes** (Note, Required: False): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
