## Inclusive Tourism Innovation Summit Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inclusive Tourism Innovation Summit Registration form is used to gather information from individuals interested in attending the summit. This form helps us understand your requirements and preferences to better tailor the experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with the required information, marked as `Required` below.
2. Choose your accessibility needs, session interests, and dietary preferences from the provided options.
3. Enter your contact number and email address.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation or requirements.
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`conference_registration_form`, text, Required)
  This field is the main registration form where you can provide your name and other basic information.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, Optional)
  This field is for any additional details you would like to share about yourself, such as your role or area of interest.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, Optional)
  Please indicate if you have any accessibility needs, such as wheelchair accessibility or sign language interpretation, so we can accommodate your requirements.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_one, Optional)
  Choose the sessions that interest you the most, so we can tailor the program to your preferences.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, select_multiple, Optional)
  Indicate your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc., so we can accommodate your dietary needs.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, Optional)
  Enter your contact number, which we will use to get in touch with you regarding the summit.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional)
  Enter your email address, which we will use to get in touch with you regarding the summit.
* **Note** (`note`, note, Optional)
  Add any additional notes or comments about your participation or requirements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting your accessibility needs and session interests.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the note field.
* We will review and respond to your submitted form as soon as possible.
