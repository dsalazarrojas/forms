# Beverage Export Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from beverage companies interested in participating in the Beverage Export Program. Its primary goal is to help you get started with your export journey and receive support from the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your company name and contact person's details.
2. Select your company's country of origin.
3. Provide your company's website and production capacity.
4. Choose the types of beverages you produce.
5. Describe your current export experience and status.
6. Indicate which countries you currently export to (if any).
7. Select the markets you aim to enter.
8. Choose your export budget range.
9. Decide on your export timeline.
10. Provide any additional comments about your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person Name** (2, required): Enter the full name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (3, required): Enter the job title of your primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (4, required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates from the program.
* **Phone Number** (5, required): Enter your phone number for program communication.
* **Company Address** (6, required): Enter your company's full business address.
* **Country of Origin** (7, required): Select where your company is based from the list.
* **Company Website** (8, required): Enter the URL of your company website.
* **Years in Business** (9, required): Select how long your company has been operating from the options.
* **Beverage Categories Produced** (10, required): Select the types of beverages your company produces.
* **Current Export Experience** (11, required): Select your current export status from the options.
* **Current Export Markets** (12, optional): If you currently export to any countries, enter them in the text field.
* **Target Export Markets** (13, required): Select the markets you aim to enter from the list.
* **Annual Production Capacity** (14, required): Select your production capacity from the options.
* **Certifications Held** (15, required): Select any relevant export-related certifications your company holds.
* **Program Goals** (16, required): Describe what you hope to achieve from this program in the text field.
* **Export Challenges** (17, required): Describe any export challenges you face in the text field.
* **Support Needed** (18, required): Select any type of support you need from the options.
* **Export Budget Range** (19, required): Select your export investment budget from the options.
* **Export Timeline** (20, required): Select when you plan to start exporting from the options.
* **Additional Comments** (21, optional): Provide any other information about your company that may be helpful.
