# sustainability_expo_opening_rsvp - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "sustainability_expo_opening_rsvp" form is used to collect information from attendees who are interested in attending the sustainability expo. The purpose of this form is to gather data on attendee availability, guest count, any special requests, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your attendance status by choosing "yes" or "no" in the "event_attendance" field.
2. Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you in the "guest_count" field.
3. If you have any special requests (e.g., dietary restrictions, accessibility needs), provide them in the "special_requests" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_attendance**: Choose "yes" if you plan to attend the sustainability expo, and "no" if you do not.
* **guest_count**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you. This information will help us estimate the total number of attendees and make necessary arrangements.
* **special_requests**: Provide any special requests you may have, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, so we can accommodate you properly.
* **contact_info**: Enter your email address or other contact information so we can get in touch with you about the event.
