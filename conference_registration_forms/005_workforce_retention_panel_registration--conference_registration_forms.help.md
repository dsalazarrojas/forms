# Workforce Retention Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Retention Panel Registration form is designed to collect information from participants to improve workforce retention and employee engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your participant details in the Participant Details section.
2. Select your consents for data sharing and usage in the Consents section.
3. Choose your preferred date and time of participation in the Date and Time section.
4. Input your email and phone number in the Contact Information section.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.
6. Finally, click on the Submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (1): 
  Input your name, expertise, and any other relevant details about yourself as a participant.
  
- **Consents** (3): 
  Select the options that apply to you regarding data sharing and usage.

- **Date and Time** (4): 
  Choose the date and time when you are available to participate in the Workforce Retention Panel.

- **Email** (5): 
  Input your email address that we can use to contact you.

- **Phone** (6): 
  Input your phone number that we can use to contact you.

- **Notes** (7): 
  Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.

- **Submit** (8): 
  Click on this button to submit your form.
