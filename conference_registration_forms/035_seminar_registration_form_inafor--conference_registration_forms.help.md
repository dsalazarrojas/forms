# Seminar Registration Form INAFOR - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Registration Form INAFOR is a general-purpose form used for collecting contact and identification details from individuals interested in attending a seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the required information for each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Make sure to enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
3.  Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5.  Enter a valid email address in the `email` field. Note that an email address is required to be valid for this field.
6.  Enter your address in the `address` field.
7.  Enter any additional address details in the `address_2` field.
8.  Enter your university name in the `university` field.
9.  Enter your department name in the `department` field.
10. Enter your position in the `position` field.
11. Enter your academic degree in the `academic_degree` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **seminar_registration_form_inafor** (`seminar_registration_form_inafor`, text, optional): 
Enter the title of the seminar registration form.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_first_name** (`first_name`, text, optional): 
Enter your first name.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_last_name** (`last_name`, text, optional): 
Enter your last name.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
Enter your phone number.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_email** (`email`, email, optional): 
Enter a valid email address.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_address** (`address`, text, optional): 
Enter your address.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_address_2** (`address_2`, text, optional): 
Enter any additional address details.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_university** (`university`, text, optional): 
Enter your university name.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_department** (`department`, text, optional): 
Enter your department name.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_position** (`position`, text, optional): 
Enter your position.

### **seminar_registration_form_inafor_academic_degree** (`academic_degree`, text, optional): 
Enter your academic degree.

# Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
- Double-check your information for errors before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your seminar organiser.
