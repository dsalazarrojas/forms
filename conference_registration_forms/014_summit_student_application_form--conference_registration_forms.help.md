# Summit Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to apply for a Summit, a significant event. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful Summit experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields.
3. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are attending.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your university.
* **Summits** (`summits`, select multiple, not required): Select all the Summits you are interested in attending.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Upload Docs** (`upload_docs`, text, required): Attach any relevant documents required by the Summit organizers.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, not required): Once you've filled out the form, click this button to complete your application.
