# Conference Gift Bag Refill Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Gift Bag Refill Request Form is used to request a refill of conference gift bags for an event. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the refill request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Gift Bag Refill Request" page to begin the process.
2.  Enter the "Request Date" in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Fill out the "Requester Name" field with your name.
4.  Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
5.  Select your department in the "Department" field (if applicable).
6.  Provide the event details in the "Conference Information" field (if applicable).
7.  Enter the "Conference Name" and "Conference Dates" fields.
8.  Select the "Conference Location" (if applicable).
9.  Enter the number of attendees expected for the event in the "Expected Attendees" field (if applicable).
10. Describe the "Bag Details" and "Items Requested" (if applicable).
11. Choose the "Bag Type", "Bag Color", and "Promotional Items" (if applicable).
12. Select any "Informational Materials" and "Snacks and Beverages" (if applicable).
13. Choose the "Tech Items" (if applicable).
14. Enter the number of gift bags needed in the "Quantity Needed" field.
15. Select the current stock level from "Empty Need Everything", "Low Need Refill", "Medium Partial Refill", or "Well Stocked Top Up".
16. Enter the "Delivery Location" where the gift bags should be sent.
17. Select the "Delivery Date Needed" when you need the gift bags.
18. Enter any "Special Instructions" or specific requirements for the gift bags.
19. Enter the "Budget Code" (if applicable).
20. If necessary, click "Approve" or select "Urgent Pre-Approved" in the "Approval Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gift Bag Refill Request** (`<request_header>`): This field is used to enter the title of the request. You should enter the request's purpose in this field. 
* **Request Date (`<request_date>`)**: Enter the date on which you're requesting the refill. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Requester Name (`<requester_name>`): Enter your name. Ensure it matches the one on your company ID.
* **Email Address (`<requester_email>`): Enter your company email address. This field is used for communication purposes.
* **Department (`<department>`): If you belong to a department, enter it here (if applicable).
* **Conference Information (`<conference_info>`): Enter any information regarding the event, such as location, dates, and other details (if applicable).
* **Conference Name (`<conference_name>`): Enter the name of the conference.
* **Conference Dates (`<conference_dates>`): Enter the date(s) of the conference.
* **Conference Location (`<conference_location>`): Enter the location of the conference.
* **Expected Attendees (`<expected_attendees>`): If this is a large event, estimate the number of expected attendees. This information helps organizers determine the quantity needed for the conference gift bags.
* **Bag Details (`<bag_details>`): Enter the specific specifications for the gift bags you wish to refill, for instance, size, style, and material.
* **Bag Type (`<bag_type>`): Select a suitable bag type from the following options: 
  - Standard Tote Bag
  - Premium Canvas Bag
  - Drawstring Backpack
  - Reusable Shopping Bag
  - Eco-Friendly Bag
  - Custom Branded Bag
* **Bag Color (`<bag_color>`): Select a preferred color for the gift bags from the following options: 
  - Black
  - Navy
  - Red
  - Blue
  - Green
  - White
  - Custom
* **Items Requested (`<items_requested>`): List any items you wish to include in the gift bags, such as promotional materials, informational materials, or snacks and beverages.
* **Promotional Items (`<promotional_items>`): Select any promotional materials you wish to include in the gift bags from the following options: 
  - Pens
  - Notebooks
  - Lanyards
  - USB Drives
  - Water Bottles
  - T-Shirts
  - Stickers
  - Magnets
* **Informational Materials (`<informational_materials>`): Select any informational materials you wish to include in the gift bags from the following options: 
  - Conference Program
  - Brochures
  - Flyers
  - Business Cards
  - Coupons
  - Maps
* **Snacks and Beverages (`<snacks_beverages>`): Select any edibles or beverages you wish to include in the gift bags from the following options: 
  - Candy
  - Mints
  - Granola Bars
  - Tea Bags
  - Coffee Packets
  - None
* **Tech Items (`<tech_items>`): Select any electronic or technical items you wish to include in the gift bags from the following options: 
  - Phone Chargers
  - Earbuds
  - Screen Cleaners
  - Cable Organizers
  - None
* **Quantity Needed (`<quantity_needed>`): Enter the number of gift bags you require to be refilled.
* **Current Stock Level (`<current_stock>`): Select the current stock level from the following options: 
  - Empty Need Everything
  - Low Need Refill
  - Medium Partial Refill
  - Well Stocked Top Up
* **Delivery Information (`<delivery_info>`): Enter any specific delivery information.
* **Delivery Location (`<delivery_location>`): Enter the address where the gift bags should be delivered.
* **Delivery Date Needed (`<delivery_date>`): Enter the date by which you need the gift bags delivered.
* **Special Instructions (`<special_instructions>`): Enter any specific requirements or requests for the gift bags.
* **Budget Code (`<budget_code>`): If the refill is related to a specific project or budget, enter the corresponding budget code.
* **Approval Status (`<approval_status>`): Enter your manager's approval status from the following options: 
  - Pending Approval
  - Approved
  - Urgent Pre-Approved

## Tips
- Be accurate and precise when filling out this form to avoid any delays or miscommunication.
- Check the current stock level to ensure that the refill request is justified.
- Double-check the "Delivery Date Needed" to ensure that the gift bags are delivered on time.
- Enter any "Special Instructions" or specific requirements to help the organizers process your request accurately.
