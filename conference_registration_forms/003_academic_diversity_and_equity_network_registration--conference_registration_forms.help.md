# Academic Diversity And Equity Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for the Academic Diversity and Equity Network, a platform that aims to promote diversity, equity, and inclusion in academia. The purpose of this form is to collect information about your personal and professional background, interests, and goals to facilitate meaningful connections and collaboration within the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Professional Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select your Institution or Organization in the "Institution or Organization" field.
5. Enter your current role or position in the "Role or Position" field.
6. (Optional) If you are part of a department or division, enter it in the "Department or Division" field.
7. Select all the areas of interest related to diversity and equity in the "Areas of Interest in Diversity and Equity" field.
8. (Optional) If you have experience in DEI work, enter the number of years in the "Years of Experience in DEI Work" field.
9. Describe your goals for joining the network in the "Goals for Joining the Network" field.
10. (Optional) If you are interested in collaborative research or initiatives, select the corresponding option in the "Collaboration Interests" field.
11. (Optional) If you have any accessibility needs, enter them in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
12. Choose your preferred method of communication in the "Preferred Communication Method" field.
13. Select the geographic region you primarily work in from the "Geographic Region" field.
14. (Optional) If you have been part of similar networks before, select the correct option in the "Previous DEI Network Membership" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address that the network can use for communications.
* **Institution or Organization**: Select the name of your university, college, or organization.
* **Role or Position**: Enter your current position or role.
* **Department or Division**: (Optional) If you are part of a department or division, enter it here.
* **Areas of Interest in Diversity and Equity**: Select all the areas of interest related to diversity and equity.
* **Years of Experience in DEI Work**: (Optional) If you have experience in DEI work, enter the number of years.
* **Goals for Joining the Network**: Describe why you want to join the network.
* **Collaboration Interests**: Select the option that best describes your interest in collaborative research or initiatives.
* **Accessibility Needs**: (Optional) If you have any accessibility requirements, enter them here.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose how you would like to receive network updates.
* **Geographic Region**: Select the region where you primarily work.
* **Previous DEI Network Membership**: (Optional) If you have been part of similar networks before, select the correct option.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are essential for completing the form. The other fields are optional but can help the network administrator tailor communications and opportunities according to your needs and interests.
