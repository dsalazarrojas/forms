# Eco Efficiency Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Efficiency Symposium Registration form is designed to collect information from users interested in attending the Eco Efficiency Symposium. This form helps us understand their interests and requirements for the event, which enables us to better plan and accommodate their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your interests and requirements by choosing the relevant options from the provided multiple-choice answers.
* Provide your contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
* If applicable, add any notes or additional information that you think is relevant to your registration.
* Optionally, select the date and time you are available to attend the symposium.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Interests 1 (<name>: session_interests_1)**: This field allows you to express your interest in specific sessions or topics related to the symposium.
  * Select one or more options that best fit your interests.
* **First Name (<name>: first_name)**: This field is for your first name.
  * Type in your first name.
* **Last Name (<name>: last_name)**: This field is for your last name.
  * Type in your last name.
* **Email (<name>: email)**: This field is for your email address.
  * Type in your email address.
* **Phone (<name>: phone_1)**: This field is for your phone number.
  * Type in your phone number.
* **Special Requirements 1 (<name>: special_requirements_1)**: This field allows you to express any special requirements you have, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions.
  * Select one or more options that best fit your requirements.
* **Special Requirements 2 (<name>: special_requirements_2)**: This field allows you to express any additional special requirements you have.
  * Select one or more options that best fit your requirements.
* **Session Interests 2 (<name>: session_interests_2)**: This field allows you to express your interest in specific sessions or topics related to the symposium.
  * Select one or more options that best fit your interests.
* **Note (<name>: note_1)**: This field is for any additional notes or information you think is relevant to your registration.
  * Type in any information you think is important.
* **Date (<name>: date)**: This field is for the date you are available to attend the symposium.
  * Select the date you are free.
* **Time 1 (<name>: time_1)**: This field is for the time you are available to attend the symposium.
  * Select the time you are free.
* **Time 2 (<name>: time_2)**: This field is for an additional time slot you are available to attend the symposium.
  * Select the time you are free.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to help us best plan and accommodate your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We will review your registration carefully and get back to you with further information about your application.
