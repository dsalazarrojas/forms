# Parent Teacher Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Teacher Conference Registration Form is used to collect information from parents and teachers to facilitate effective communication during parent-teacher conferences. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are documented and communicated to both parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please ensure you have a stable internet connection and a device with a compatible web browser.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information. If you need help with any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
3. Review and submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian. Ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Teacher's Name** (5, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the teacher associated with the student.
* **Teacher's Email** (6, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Teacher's Phone** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **Conference Date** (8, date, required): Select the date of the parent-teacher conference.
* **Conference Time** (9, time, required): Select the time of the parent-teacher conference.
* **School** (10, text, required/optional): Enter the school name where the conference will take place.
* **Classroom** (11, select_one, required): Select the classroom where the student is enrolled.
* **Parent's Note** (12, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns from the parent's perspective.
* **Teacher's Note** (13, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the teacher's perspective.
* **Teacher's Additional Info** (14, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information from the teacher.
* **Teacher's Additional Note** (15, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the teacher.
* **Additional Info** (16, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the conference.
* **Additional Comments** (17, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns from either party.
* **Student's Name** (18, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student's Grade** (19, text, required/optional): Enter the grade level of the student.
* **Student's Date of Birth** (20, date, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Student's Date of Birth Conflict** (21, time, required/optional): Enter any potential conflicts with the student's date of birth.
* **Teacher's Signature** (22, text, required/optional): Enter the teacher's signature.
* **Parent's Signature** (23, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's signature.
* **Additional Fields** (24, select_multiple, required): Select the additional fields to be included in the conference.
* **Additional Information** (25, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the conference.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
* This form is for your benefit to ensure a smooth and productive parent-teacher conference.
