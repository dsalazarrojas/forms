# green_retail_architecture_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The green retail architecture registration form is used for participants to provide their details, interests, and contact information for the Green Retail Architecture Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details page with your personal information.
2. Select one or more registration interests from the list provided.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This page is used to collect your personal information, such as name and other identifying details.
* **Registration Interests**: Please select one or more of the interests that align with your involvement in the Green Retail Architecture Conference. These interests include:
	+ Retail Design
	+ Sustainability
	+ Community Engagement
	+ Events
	+ Community Development
	+ Other (you can specify your own interest)
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, you can include them in the "Other" field under Registration Interests.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us at [contact information].
