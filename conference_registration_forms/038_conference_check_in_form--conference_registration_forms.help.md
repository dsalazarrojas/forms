# Conference Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Check In Form is used to capture essential information from attendees when they arrive at a conference. This form ensures that attendees can be easily checked in and registered, and that necessary details are recorded for the conference organizers. It's a mandatory form for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your attendee name as it appears on your registration materials.
2. Enter your registration ID as provided on your registration confirmation email or materials.
3. Select the time of your arrival.
4. Confirm if a badge was issued to you.
5. Choose your meal preference (if applicable).
6. Provide any accessibility needs.
7. Enter your contact phone number (if necessary).
8. Choose whether you consent to media release (if applicable).
9. Enter the name of the staff member who verified your check-in.
10. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your check-in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on your registration materials.
* **Registration ID** (`registration_id`, `text`, **required**): Enter the ID provided on your registration confirmation email or materials.
* **Check in time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, **required**): Select the time of your arrival at the conference.
* **Badge issued** (`badge_issued`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if a badge was issued to you.
* **Meal preference** (`meal_preference`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose your meal preference (if applicable). Options are Regular, Vegetarian, Vegan, and Gluten free.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, **optional**): Describe any accommodations required due to disability or special needs.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your contact phone number for urgent on-site communication.
* **Consent to media release** (`consent_media_release`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether you consent to photos and recordings being taken and shared.
* **Staff verifier name** (`staff_verifier`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the staff member who verifies your check-in.
* **Check in notes** (`check_in_notes`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about your check-in.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. This form is a critical part of the conference registration process, and your cooperation is appreciated.
