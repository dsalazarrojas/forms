# healthcare_workforce_inclusion_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect conference registration information from attendees of the healthcare workforce inclusion forum. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Conference Details" section with any relevant information about the conference.
2. Provide details about the attendee in the "Attendee Details" section.
3. Specify any accessibility needs in the "Accessibility Needs" section.
4. Confirm registration details in the "Confirm Registration" section.
5. Enter contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
6. Specify emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contacts" section.
7. Provide payment details in the "Payment Details" section.
8. Enter additional information in the "Additional Information" section.
9. Confirm email, phone number, date, time, timezone, city, address, state, and zip code in the "Confirm Email", "Confirm Phone", "Confirm Date", "Confirm Time", "Confirm Timezone", "Confirm City", "Confirm Address", "Confirm State", and "Confirm Zip" fields.
