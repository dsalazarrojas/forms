# industrial_robotics_showcase_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from exhibitors for the Industrial Robotics Showcase, including contact and registration details, participation type, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the exhibitor's information in the **Exhibitor Info** field. This is a general text field for any information the exhibitor would like to provide.
2. Fill in the **Contact Info** field with the exhibitor's contact details. This is a text field for any additional contact information.
3. In the **Registration Info** field, enter any relevant registration information for the exhibitor.
4. Select the exhibitor's **Participation Type** from the list provided. This will help us understand their interests and preferences.
5. Choose the exhibitor's **Interests** by selecting multiple options from the list. This will help us tailor our event to their needs and preferences.
6. Enter the exhibitor's **Email** address. This is an essential piece of information for communication and registration purposes.
7. Enter the exhibitor's **Phone** number. This is an optional field for additional contact information.
8. If applicable, enter the exhibitor's **Company** name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Info** (Text): A general text field for any information the exhibitor would like to provide.
* **Contact Info** (Text): A text field for any additional contact information.
* **Registration Info** (Text): A text field for any relevant registration information.
* **Participation Type** (Select Multiple): Select the exhibitor's participation type from the list provided.
* **Interests** (Select Multiple): Choose the exhibitor's interests by selecting multiple options from the list.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the exhibitor's email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the exhibitor's phone number. (Optional)
* **Company** (Text): If applicable, enter the exhibitor's company name.
