# COI Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COI Form is a crucial component of the conference registration process. By completing this form, you are confirming that you have no conflicts of interest that may impact your participation in the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete legal name in the "Full Name" field (page 1).
2. Enter your current position or role in the "Professional Title" field (page 1).
3. Provide the name of your employer or affiliated organization in the "Organization or Institution" field (page 1).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (page 1).
5. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (page 1).
6. Select the name of the conference you are attending or presenting at in the "Conference Name" field (page 1).
7. If you have a presentation topic, enter a brief description in the "Presentation Topic or Session" field (page 2).
8. If you have any financial interests in organizations related to the conference topic, select "Yes" and provide details in the "If yes, please describe the financial interest" field (page 2).
9. If you have a personal relationship with speakers or organizers, select "Yes" and provide details in the "If yes, please describe the relationship" field (page 3).
10. If you are employed by a competing organization, select "Yes" and provide details in the "If yes, please provide details" field (page 4).
11. If you hold any patents or intellectual property related to the conference topic, select "Yes" and provide details in the "If yes, please describe the intellectual property" field (page 5).
12. If you have received research funding from organizations related to the conference topic, select "Yes" and provide details in the "If yes, provide details about funding sources" field (page 6).
13. If you have any other conflicts of interest not covered above, select "Yes" and provide details in the "Please describe any other potential conflicts" field (page 7).
14. Finally, select "I agree" to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Professional Title** (1): Your current position or role, e.g., researcher, engineer, or professor.
* **Organization or Institution** (1): The name of your employer or affiliated organization.
* **Email Address** (1): Your email address, where you can be contacted for conference-related matters.
* **Phone Number** (1): Your phone number, where you can be contacted for conference-related matters (optional).
* **Conference Name** (1): The name of the conference you are attending or presenting at.
* **Presentation Topic or Session** (2): A brief description of your presentation topic or session (if applicable).
* **Do you have any financial interests in organizations related to this conference topic?** (2): Select "Yes" if you have any financial interests, such as stocks, consulting fees, or grants.
* **If yes, please describe the financial interest** (2): Provide details about your financial interests, including stocks, consulting fees, or grants.
* **Do you have a personal relationship with speakers or organizers?** (3): Select "Yes" if you have a close relationship with speakers or organizers, such as family, close friends, or professional partnerships.
* **If yes, please describe the relationship** (3): Specify the nature and duration of your relationship.
* **Are you or anyone closely associated with you employed by a competing organization?** (4): Select "Yes" if you or someone closely associated with you is employed by a competing organization.
* **If yes, please provide details** (4): Provide details about your employment or association with a competing organization.
* **Do you hold any patents or intellectual property related to conference topics?** (5): Select "Yes" if you hold any patents or intellectual property related to the conference topics.
* **If yes, please describe the intellectual property** (5): Provide details about your patents or intellectual property.
* **Have you received research funding from organizations related to this topic?** (6): Select "Yes" if you have received research funding from organizations related to the conference topic.
* **If yes, provide details about funding sources** (6): Provide details about your research funding sources.
* **To your knowledge, is there any other conflict of interest not covered above?** (7): Select "Yes" if you have any other conflicts of interest not covered above.
* **Please describe any other potential conflicts** (7): Provide details about any other conflicts of interest not covered above.
* **I certify that the information provided above is accurate and complete to the best of my knowledge** (8): Select "I agree" to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Submission Date** (9): Date of submission, which will be automatically filled in when you submit the form.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure a conflict-free environment.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, it's better to err on the side of caution and select "Not Sure" or "Not Applicable" when applicable.
* Remember to provide detailed descriptions for fields that require it to ensure clarity and context.
