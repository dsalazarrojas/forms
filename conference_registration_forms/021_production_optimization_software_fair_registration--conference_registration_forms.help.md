# Production Optimization Software Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to register for the Production Optimization Software Fair. It is intended to gather information about the attendees, their organizations, and their interests for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information:
	* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are interested in.
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Role**: Select your role.
	* **Interested In**: Select all the areas you are interested in (Production, Development, Design, Testing, or Other).
	* **Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
	* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are interested in. (Required)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. (Required)
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Role**: Select your role at the organization. (Required)
* **Interested In**: Select all the areas you are interested in (Production, Development, Design, Testing, or Other). (Required)
* **Interested In Other**: If you have selected "Other", enter a brief description of your interest. (Optional)
* **Agree to Terms**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions. (Required)
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review the terms and conditions before confirming. (Required)
* **Confirm Terms**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions. (Required)
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete your registration. (Required)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you select "Other" in the "Interested In" field, provide a brief description of your interest.
* Review the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the event organizers at form@gic.mx.
