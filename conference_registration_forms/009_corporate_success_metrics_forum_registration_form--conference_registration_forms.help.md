# Corporate Success Metrics Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration for the Corporate Success Metrics Forum. It is designed to collect information from attendees to facilitate event organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your conference name.
2. Enter your name.
3. Provide your contact email.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your preferred language.
6. Choose your company size.
7. Select your industry.
8. Enter your job title.
9. Select your attendee type.
10. Fill in any additional comments or preferences you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference you are attending.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred language from the list: English, Spanish, French.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to attend the event.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, note, required): Provide any comments or preferences related to the event.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you registered for the event.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select your company size from the list: Micro, Small, Medium, Large.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry from the list: Finance, Marketing, Technology.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): Enter your company website.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, select_one, required): Select your attendee type from the list: Employee, Client.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company address.
* **City-State-Region** (`city_state_region`, select_one, required): Select your city from the list: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter your company phone number.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
* **Attendee Comments** (`attendee_comments`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback related to your attendance.
* **Registration Preferences** (`registration_preferences`, note, required): Provide any preferences or requests related to your registration.
