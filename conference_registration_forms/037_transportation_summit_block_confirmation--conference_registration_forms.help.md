# Transportation Summit Block Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm a transportation block for a conference, providing essential information about a participant's attendance, block preferences, and group preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **conference** field with the conference name or title.
2. Enter your **name** in the space provided.
3. Provide your **email** address where you can be reached for any updates or information.
4. Enter your **phone number** for any emergency contact purposes.
5. Select the **block** that you have been assigned to.
6. Indicate your **participation** status: true if you will attend, false if you won't, or maybe if you're unsure.
7. If applicable, select your **group** preference: yes if you'd like to be grouped together, no if you'd rather not.
8. Add any additional **note** about your transportation needs or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **conference**: Enter the name of the conference you're attending.
* **name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and information.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number in case of any last-minute changes or updates.
* **block**: Select the transportation block assigned to you for the conference.
* **participation**: Indicate your expected participation status (true, false, or maybe).
* **group**: Select your group preference (yes or no).
* **note**: Add any additional information you'd like to share about your transportation needs or concerns.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the conference organizers.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
