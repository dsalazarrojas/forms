# National Online Forensics Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant and coach information for the National Online Forensics Competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Click on the "Start Submission" button to begin filling out the form.
- Fill out each field with the required information.
- Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a participant in the competition.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, text, required: false): Enter your school's name.
* **Event 1** (`event_id_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the event 1 option that you are participating in.
* **Event 2** (`event_id_2`, select_one, required: false): Select the event 2 option that you are participating in.
* **Event 3** (`event_id_3`, select_one, required: false): Select the event 3 option that you are participating in.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a coach.
* **Coach School** (`coach_school`, text, required: false): Enter your school's name as a coach.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as a coach.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as a coach.
* **Coach Experience** (`coach_experience`, note, required: false): Enter your coaching experience.

## Tips
- Please double-check the accuracy of your entries before submitting.
- Ensure that you have the correct permission or clearance from your school or organization to participate in this competition.
- If you need help, contact the competition organizers for support.
