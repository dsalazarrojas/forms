# Tech Conference Early Bird Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Conference Early Bird Membership Form is used to gather information from potential attendees who wish to take advantage of the conference early bird offer. Fill out this form to provide your details and determine your eligibility for the offer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you enter the correct information for your intended conference.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Title** (`conference_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the conference you are interested in.
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the conference you are interested in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the conference.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the conference.
* **Early Bird Price** (`early_bird_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the early bird price for the conference.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the payment amount for the conference.
* **Attendee First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Attendee Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your intended conference.
