# Conference Registration Form With Payment PayPal Standard - Help Guide

## Purpose
The conference registration form is used to collect attendee information and payment details for the conference. This form is required for all attendees and is used to confirm registration and payment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the designated fields.
2. Provide your phone number and company or organization affiliation.
3. Select your job title and attendee type.
4. Choose your registration option and select all applicable conference days.
5. Select your meal functions and dietary preferences.
6. If necessary, specify any dietary notes or restrictions.
7. Select your T-Shirt size (if applicable).
8. Enter your PayPal email address and confirm your payment details.
9. Indicate if an invoice is required.
10. If required, provide company billing information and specify accessibility requirements.
11. Enter your emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
12. Select your preference for marketing communications (if applicable).
13. Let us know how you heard about us (if applicable).
14. Review and agree to the terms and cancellation policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter the email address where you will receive confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company or Organization** (`text`, required): Enter your affiliation or company name.
* **Job Title** (`text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Attendee Type** (`select_one`, required): Select your category (Professional, Academic, Student, Government, Non-Profit, Retired).
* **Registration Option** (`select_one`, required): Choose your registration type (Full Conference - $495, Single Day - $195, Student Full - $150, Virtual Access - $99, Group Rate 5+ - $395 each).
* **Days Attending** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all the conference days you will be attending (Day 1 - Opening and Keynotes, Day 2 - Technical Sessions, Day 3 - Workshops, Day 4 - Closing).
* **Meal Functions** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the meal functions you would like to attend (Welcome Breakfast, Lunch Day 1, Lunch Day 2, Networking Dinner, Closing Brunch).
* **Dietary Preference** (`select_one`, required): Select your dietary preference (Standard, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Kosher, Halal).
* **Dietary Notes** (`text`, optional): Provide any dietary notes or restrictions if necessary.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`select_one`, optional): Select your T-Shirt size (Small, Medium, Large, X-Large, XX-Large, Prefer Not).
* **PayPal Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your PayPal email address for payment processing.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`note`, optional): You will be redirected to PayPal for payment confirmation.
* **Invoice Required** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if an invoice is required.
* **Invoice Details** (`text`, optional): If an invoice is required, enter your company billing information.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`text`, optional): If necessary, specify any accessibility requirements.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Receive Future Communications** (`select_one`, optional): Select your preference for marketing emails.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`select_one`, optional): Let us know how you heard about us (Email, Social Media, Colleague, Website, Advertisement, Other).
* **I Agree to Terms and Cancellation Policy** (`select_one`, required): Review and agree to the terms and cancellation policies.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any dietary notes or accessibility requirements, please specify in the designated fields.
