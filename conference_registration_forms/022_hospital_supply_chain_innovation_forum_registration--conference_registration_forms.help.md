## hospital_supply_chain_innovation_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospital supply chain innovation forum registration form is designed to collect information from attendees, including their registration details and attendee details. This form is intended to streamline the registration process for the conference, ensuring that attendees can easily provide their information and confirm their registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, "Registration Details".
	* Fill in your name and any other relevant information as prompted.
2. Proceed to the second page, "Attendee Details".
	* Enter your attendee information, including any necessary details.
3. Review your information and click "Submit" to submit your registration.
4. On the final page, "Confirm Registration", review your information again and confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will enter your basic registration information.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This is where you will enter the details about the attendee you are registering, such as their name, email, and phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false):
	+ Click this button to submit your registration form and review it for final confirmation.
* **Confirm Registration** (`confirm_registration`, text, required: false):
	+ This is the final page where you will confirm your registration information before it is submitted.
