# Compliance Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Networking Event Registration form is used to gather information from attendees for a networking event focused on regulatory compliance and risk management. This guide will walk you through the form sections and fields to help you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your contact information, including your full name, job title, organization name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your industry sector from the options provided.
3. Choose the sessions you are interested in attending.
4. If you have any dietary requirements, please select the relevant option.
5. Indicate whether this is your first time attending the event.
6. Specify how you heard about the event.
7. Check the box to consent to participate in networking activities and agree to follow event guidelines.
8. Choose how you would like to receive marketing communications.
9. Finally, add any additional comments or requests you have for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Job Title**: Your current position or role in your organization.
* **Organization Name**: The name of your company or organization you represent.
* **Email Address**: This will be used for event updates and confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Your best contact number for event updates.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the industry related to your work or interests.
* **Networking Interests**: Select the areas you are interested in connecting with others about.
* **Session Preferences**: Choose the sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Indicate any special dietary needs you have for the catering.
* **Is this your first time attending this event?**: Check "Yes" if this is your first time attending the event.
* **How did you hear about this event?**: Specify the source where you learned about the event.
* **Participation Consent**: Check "Yes" to consent to participate in networking activities and follow event guidelines.
* **Marketing Communications**: Choose how you would like to receive event updates.
* **Additional Comments or Requests**: Add any special requests or questions you have for the organizers.
