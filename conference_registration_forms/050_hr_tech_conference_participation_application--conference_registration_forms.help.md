# HR Tech Conference Participation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Tech Conference Participation Application is a form designed for HR professionals who are interested in participating in the HR Tech Conference. This form collects essential information about you and your company to help us tailor the conference experience to your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Attendee Details" section with your name and contact information.
2. Choose your preferred participation method (in person, virtual, or hybrid) from the "Participation Type" dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, describe any specific requirements you may have in the "Requirements" section.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**:
	* (1, attendee_details, text, required):
		+ Enter your name and contact information in this field. Please use your professional email address and phone number.
* **Participation Type**:
	* (2, participation_type, select_one, required):
		+ Choose how you would like to participate in the conference: in person, virtual, or hybrid.
* **Requirements**:
	* (3, requirements, note, optional):
		+ If you have any specific requirements or requests (e.g., accommodations, dietary restrictions), please describe them here.
* **Submit**:
	* (4, submit, text, required):
		+ Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
