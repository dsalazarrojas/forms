# Church Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for the church conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
*   Fill in all required fields (marked with \*).
*   Select the correct options for select fields.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name (<type>text, <required>true):** Enter your full name as on your conference badge.
* **Organization (<type>text, <required>false):** Enter your church or group name, if applicable.
* **Email address (<type>email, <required>true):** Enter your email address for registration confirmation.
*   This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone number (<type>text, <required>false):** Enter your phone number for urgent notices.
*   This will be used for important updates or reminders.
* **Ticket Type (<type>select_one, <required>true):** Choose the type of ticket you require.
*   Early bird, Regular, Student, or Speaker.
*   Early bird: For early registrants.
*   Regular: Standard registration.
*   Student: For students or youth.
*   Speaker: For speakers or presenters.
*   Choose the option that best describes your registration type.
* **Dietary Preferences (<type>text, <required>false):** Enter any food restrictions or preferences.
*   If you have any dietary needs, please specify here.
*   Leave blank if none.
* **Accommodation Required (<type>select_one, <required>false):** Check if you need assistance with lodging.
*   True or False.
*   If you need help with accommodation, select True.
*   If not, select False.
*   This will help us provide the right assistance.
* **Special Needs or Accessibility (<type>text, <required>false):** Enter any special needs or accessibility requirements.
*   If you have any special needs or requirements, please specify here.
*   Leave blank if none.
*   This will help us provide the right support.
* **Session Selection (<type>text, <required>false):** Enter your preferred breakout sessions or tracks.
*   Enter your preferred sessions or tracks.
*   Leave blank if none.
*   This will help us provide the right materials.
*   **Agree to Terms and Code of Conduct (<type>select_one, <required>true):** Confirm that you agree to the terms and code of conduct.
*   True or False.
*   This is a required field.
*   Please select True to confirm your agreement.
*   This will ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.
