# Ethical Data Usage In Academia Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for individuals from academia (university staff and students) to register and agree to the use of data according to their institution's policies and guidelines. Completing this form ensures you comply with your institution's data usage and handling policies, which are essential for maintaining data integrity and trust in research and academic activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Enter your details accurately and truthfully.
2. **Ensure all required fields are completed**: You will be prompted to enter your First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Institution, Role, Data policies (submission agreement), and other relevant details.
3. **Choose your institution and role**: Select your institution and role (Student, Researcher, or Faculty) from the provided options.
4. **Check the submission agreement**: Carefully review and agree or disagree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as per your identification documents. This will be your primary name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as per your identification documents. This will be your surname.
* **Email**: Enter your institutional email address or your personal email address (if it's not institutional).
* **Phone**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached, including any necessary international prefix (e.g., +1 for USA or +44 for the UK).
* **Institution**: Select your institution from the options provided. These are the universities you are associated with.
* **Role**: Choose your role within the selected institution from the options provided (Student, Researcher, Faculty).
* **Data policies**: This field is related to your institution's data handling policies. Please read and understand the policies before agreeing or disagreeing to them.
* **Ethical guidelines**: Enter any relevant notes about how you follow the ethical guidelines in your work or research.
* **Submission agreement**: Select your choice of submission, whether to agree or disagree to the institution's terms and conditions.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information or context that is relevant to your registration and data usage. This is an optional field.
 

# Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to ensure successful submission.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, contact your institution's support team for assistance.
* Review the submission agreement carefully before agreeing or disagreeing to it.
* If you have any questions about the form, refer to your institution's data handling policies or consult with your departmental or institutional authorities.
