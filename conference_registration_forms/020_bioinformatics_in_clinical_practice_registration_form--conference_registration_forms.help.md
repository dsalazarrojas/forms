# Bioinformatics In Clinical Practice Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration to the Bioinformatics In Clinical Practice conference. It is a comprehensive form that captures your personal, professional, and conference preferences information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your personal information in the **Attendee Information** page.
2. On the **Attendee Information** page, fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Professional Title**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
3. Proceed to the **Organization Information** page to fill in your **Organization Name**, **Organization Type**, **Department**, **Organization Address**, **City**, **State or Province**, **ZIP Code**, and **Country**.
4. Select your conference preferences in the **Conference Details** page, including the **Registration Type**, **Conference Days**, and **Workshop Selection**.
5. On the **Additional Information** page, provide your **Professional Background**, **Specialty Area**, **Years of Experience**, **Bioinformatics Experience Level**, **Dietary Requirements**, and **Accommodation Needs**.
6. In the **Payment Information** page, select your payment method and confirm your payment details.
7. Finally, fill in any **Promo Code** you may have, **How Did You Hear About This Conference**, and **Areas of Special Interest**.
8. Ensure that you agree to the conference **Terms Agreement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Information** (`id: 1`): Enter your personal details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Your given name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Your family name.
* **Professional Title** (`id: 4`): Your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`id: 5`): Your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 6`): Your contact number.
* **Organization Information** (`id: 7`): Enter your organization details, including name, type, address, and more.
* **Organization Name** (`id: 8`): Name of your institution or company.
* **Organization Type** (`id: 9`): Type of organization (e.g., Hospital or Medical Center, University or Academic Institution, etc.).
* **Department** (`id: 10`): Your department or division.
* **Organization Address** (`id: 11`): Complete street address of your organization.
* **City** (`id: 12`): City of your organization.
* **State or Province** (`id: 13`): State or province of your organization.
* **ZIP Code** (`id: 14`): Postal code of your organization.
* **Country** (`id: 15`): Country of your organization.
* **Conference Details** (`id: 16`): Select your conference preferences, including registration type, conference days, and workshop selection.
* **Registration Type** (`id: 17`): Type of registration (e.g., Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
* **Conference Days** (`id: 18`): Select the days you will attend.
* **Workshop Selection** (`id: 19`): Choose your preferred workshops.
* **Continuing Education** (`id: 20`): Select if you need CE credits.
* **Professional Background** (`id: 21`): Tell us about your background.
* **Specialty Area** (`id: 22`): Your area of specialization.
* **Years of Experience** (`id: 23`): In your field.
* **Bioinformatics Experience Level** (`id: 24`): Your experience with bioinformatics.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`id: 25`): Any dietary restrictions.
* **Accommodation Needs** (`id: 26`): Any special accommodations.
* **Payment Information** (`id: 27`): How you will pay for the conference.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 28`): Select payment type (e.g., Credit Card, Purchase Order, etc.).
* **Promo Code** (`id: 29`): Discount code if applicable.
* **How Did You Hear About This Conference** (`id: 30`): Help us improve outreach.
* **Areas of Special Interest** (`id: 31`): Topics you want to learn about.
* **Networking Interest** (`id: 32`): Interested in networking events.
* **Exhibitor Interest** (`id: 33`): Interested in visiting exhibitors.
* **Terms Agreement** (`id: 34`): Agree to conference policies.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submission to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* Follow the provided guidelines for payment and accommodation arrangements.
