# Corporate Event Pass Reservation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to reserve passes for corporate events. Please complete the form with your company's information and the details of the attendees you wish to reserve passes for.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of pass you wish to reserve.
2. Enter the attendee's information, including their name, email, phone number, and any additional notes.
3. Choose your company's name, address, and department.
4. Provide the event dates and times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information (Company Input)** (`Company Input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's information. This will help us understand which company is reserving passes.
* **Pass Type (Pass Type)** (`Pass Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of pass you wish to reserve. This will help us determine which type of pass to provide.
* **Attendee Information (Attendee Input)** (`Attendee Input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of the attendees you wish to reserve passes for.
* **Attendee Name (Name)** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the attendee's name.
* **Email (Email)** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the reservation.
* **Company Name (Company Name)** (`Company Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address (Company Address)** (`Company Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Department (Department)** (`Department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of the attendee within your company.
* **Role (Role)** (`Role`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the role of the attendee within your company.
* **Start Date (Start Date)** (`Start Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Start Time (Start Time)** (`Start Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Date (End Date)** (`End Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the event.
* **End Time (End Time)** (`End Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the event.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with pass reservations.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your company's event coordinator for clarification.
