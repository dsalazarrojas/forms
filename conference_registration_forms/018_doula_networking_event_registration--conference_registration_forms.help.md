# Doula Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for both attendee registration and event planning purposes. It gathers information about the event, such as details, date, time, and location, as well as attendee information, like name, email, and dietary restrictions. Event organizers can also use this form to provide their contact information and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Specify your phone number (if desired) in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter any event details you'd like to share in the "Event details" field (optional).
6.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are attending with someone (optional).
7.  Choose your dietary restrictions from the "Dietary restrictions" options (optional).
8.  Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment method" options (required).
9.  Specify the payment status and date (optional).
10.  Enter the event date in the "Event date" field (required).
11.  Select the start time in the "Start time" field (required).
12.  Specify the end time in the "End time" field (required).
13.  Enter the event duration in the "Duration" field (required).
14.  Enter the start location in the "Start location" field (required).
15.  Enter the start address in the "Start address" field (required).
16.  Enter the end location in the "End location" field (required).
17.  Enter the end address in the "End address" field (required).
18.  Provide the contact information of the event organizer in the "Organizer contact" field (required).
19.  Enter the phone number of the event organizer (optional).
20.  Enter the email of the event organizer (optional).
21.  Enter the website of the event organizer (optional).
22.  Enter any additional information you'd like to share in the "Additional info" field (optional).
23.  Leave a comment or note in the "Comments" field (optional).
24.  Enter any notes in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Event details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any details you'd like to share about the event.
*   **Attending with** (`attending_with`, select\_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are attending with someone, "No" otherwise.
*   **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select\_multiple, required/optional): Choose your dietary restrictions from the options.
*   **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select\_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method.
*   **Payment status** (`payment_status`, text, required/optional): Enter the payment status.
*   **Payment date** (`payment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the payment date.
*   **Event date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Start time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Select the start time of the event.
*   **End time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Select the end time of the event.
*   **Duration** (`duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
*   **Start location** (`start_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the event will start.
*   **Start address** (`start_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the start location.
*   **End location** (`end_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the event will end.
*   **End address** (`end_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the end location.
*   **Organizer contact** (`organizer_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the event organizer.
*   **Organizer phone** (`organizer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
*   **Organizer email** (`organizer_email`, text, required/optional): Enter the email of the event organizer.
*   **Organizer website** (`organizer_website`, text, required/optional): Enter the website of the event organizer.
*   **Additional info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the event.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Leave a comment about the event.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes about the event.

This help guide is designed to ensure that all fields in the "Doula Networking Event Registration" form are understood correctly. The fields have been explained step-by-step, and the purpose and how to complete the form have been outlined. 

Note that this guide is not exhaustive, and any further updates or modifications to the form might require updates to this help guide.
