# Employee Wellness Leadership Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees who wish to attend the Employee Wellness Leadership Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the required information in each section.
3. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form to register for the conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Conference Details (Page 1)
- **Conference Details** (`conference_details`, text, not required): Please enter a brief description of the conference you are registering for.

### Attendee Preferences (Page 1)
- **Attendee Preferences** (`attendee_preferences`, text, not required): If you have any special requests or preferences (e.g., dietary restrictions or accessibility needs), please let us know here.

### Session Choices (Page 1)
- **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, text, not required): List the sessions you would like to attend, or mention any topics of interest you would like to discuss during the conference.
