# Sustainability Summit Attendee Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Summit Attendee Registration form is used to collect essential information from attendees to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience at the summit. This form helps the organizers understand your preferences and needs, allowing us to provide better support and services during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., [name@domain.com](mailto:name@domain.com)).
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field provided.
4. Select your **Country** of residence from the dropdown list.
5. Type your **City** in the text field provided.
6. Enter your **Zip** code in the correct format (e.g., 10001).
7. Choose your preferred **Hiking** activity (if any) from the dropdown list.
8. Choose your preferred **Cycling** activity (if any) from the dropdown list.
9. Choose your preferred **Soccer** activity (if any) from the dropdown list.
10. Type in your preferred **Reading** activity or interest (if any).
11. Type in your preferred **Writing** activity or interest (if any).
12. Choose your dietary preferences:
	* **Vegan** (plant-based diet)
	* **Vegetarian** (diet that includes dairy products)
	* **Non-Vegetarian** (includes meat and animal products)
13. Choose your dietary preference for **Gluten-Free** products:
	* **Gluten-free** (no gluten)
	* **Not Gluten-free** (includes gluten)
14. Choose your dietary preference for **Dairy-Free** products:
	* **Dairy-free** (no dairy products)
	* **Not Dairy-free** (includes dairy products)
15. Choose your dietary preference for **Nut-Free** products:
	* **Nut-free** (no nuts)
	* **Not Nut-free** (includes nuts)
16. Choose your dietary preference for **Soy-Free** products:
	* **Soy-free** (no soy products)
	* **Not Soy-free** (includes soy products)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: This is the field to enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government ID.
* **Email**: This is the field to enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: This is the field to enter your phone number, which will be used for contacting you during the event.
* **Country**: This field is to select the country where you reside. This information helps us understand the attendees' global presence.
* **City**: This is the field to enter the city where you live.
* **Zip**: This is the field to enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Hiking**: This field is to select any hiking activities you plan to participate in during the summit.
* **Cycling**: This field is to select any cycling activities you plan to participate in during the summit.
* **Soccer**: This field is to select any soccer activities you plan to participate in during the summit.
* **Reading**: This is the field to enter any reading activities or interests you have.
* **Writing**: This is the field to enter any writing activities or interests you have.
* **Vegan**: This field is to select your dietary preference for plant-based food.
* **Gluten Free**: This field is to select your dietary preference for gluten-free products.
* **Dairy Free**: This field is to select your dietary preference for dairy-free products.
* **Nut Free**: This field is to select your dietary preference for nut-free products.
* **Soy Free**: This field is to select your dietary preference for soy-free products.

Note: Please fill out all the fields accurately to help us provide the best experience for you at the Sustainability Summit. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
