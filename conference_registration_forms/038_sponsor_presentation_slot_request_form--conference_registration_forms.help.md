# Sponsor Presentation Slot Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Presentation Slot Request Form is designed to gather essential details from potential sponsors about their presentation preferences in a conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the introduction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Title** (required): Enter the title of your presentation (up to 50 characters). This will be displayed during the presentation schedule.
* **Session Description** (required): Provide a short description of your presentation (up to 100 characters). This will give attendees an idea of what to expect from your presentation.
* **Speaker Name** (required): Enter your name or the name of the speaker presenting.
* **Presentation Type** (required): Choose the type of presentation you are offering, such as Presentation, Workshop, Panel Discussion, or Other.
* **Start Time** (required): Select your preferred start time for the presentation.
* **Duration** (required): Specify the length of your presentation in minutes.
* **Additional Information** (optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers, enter it here.
* **Attach Files** (optional): You can attach any files that support your presentation, such as handouts or supporting materials.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address so that organizers can contact you with further questions or instructions.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number so that organizers can reach you for any last-minute questions or updates.
* **Additional Comments** (optional): If you have any other comments or requests, enter them here.
