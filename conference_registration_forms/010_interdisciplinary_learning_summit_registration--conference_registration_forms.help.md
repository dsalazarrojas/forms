# Interdisciplinary Learning Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in attending the Interdisciplinary Learning Summit. The form is used to gather details about each attendee, including their session interests, accessibility needs, and payment information. This information will be used to create a participant list and to tailor the event experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the User Details section with your name and any other personal details you would like to share.
2.  Select your session interests from the list provided.
3.  Check if you have any accessibility needs or requirements.
4.  Enter your university details if you are affiliated with a university.
5.  Fill in your payment information if you have not already done so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`<name>`, `<type>`, `required/optional`): This section is for you to fill in your name and any other personal details you would like to share. You can enter as much or as little information as you feel comfortable.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, `select_multiple`, `required/optional`): Select one or multiple session interests that you are interested in. Please note that selecting all options will also result in the selection of all sessions.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, `required/optional`): Check if you have any accessibility needs or requirements. This will help us to provide an accessible event experience for you.
* **University Details** (`university_details`, `text`, `required/optional`): If you are affiliated with a university, please enter your university details here. This will help us to understand your institutional context and tailor the event experience to your needs.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, `required/optional`): If you have not already paid for your registration, please fill in your payment information here. Please note that payment information is required for attendance.
