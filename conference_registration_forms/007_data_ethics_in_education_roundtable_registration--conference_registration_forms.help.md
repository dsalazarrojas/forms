# Data Ethics In Education Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from attendees of the "Data Ethics In Education Roundtable" conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the email field.
3. Choose your affiliation (if applicable) from the text field.
4. Select your role within the conference (Chair, Member, or Guest) from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your interests in data ethics topics from the multiple-choice fields.
6. Answer the consent question with either "True" or "False".
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an institution or organization, enter it here.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role within the conference (Chair, Member, or Guest).
* **Topic Interest 1** (`topic_interest_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three topics you are interested in.
* **Topic Interest 2** (`topic_interest_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three topics you are interested in. (Note: This field is identical to the previous one. You can skip it if you have already selected the first topic interest field.)
* **Topic Interest 3** (`topic_interest_3`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three topics you are interested in. (Note: This field is identical to the first two. You can skip it if you have already selected the first two topic interest fields.)
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Answer this question with either "True" or "False".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and efficient registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Please note that by registering, you agree to share your information with the organizers of the "Data Ethics In Education Roundtable" conference.
