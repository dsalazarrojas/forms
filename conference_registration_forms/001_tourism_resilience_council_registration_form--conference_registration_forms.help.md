# Tourism Resilience Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees who are interested in participating in a conference hosted by the Tourism Resilience Council. The information collected will be used to create a personalized experience for each attendee, including tailored conference materials and program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Select your country of residence from the "Country of Residence" dropdown list.
* Select how you heard about this event from the "How did you hear about this event?" dropdown list.
* Select how much time you have for tourism activities from the "How much time do you have for tourism activities?" dropdown list.
* Select the type(s) of tourist experience you identify with from the "What type of tourist experience do you identify with?" dropdown list.
* Select your preferred mode of transportation from the "Do you have a preferred mode of transportation?" dropdown list.
* Select whether you prefer to stay in a city from the "Do you prefer to stay in a city?" dropdown list.
* Optionally, enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Country of Residence** (Select One, required): Select your country of residence.
* **How did you hear about this event?** (Select Multiple, required): Select one or more ways you heard about this event, such as Social Media, Word of Mouth, or Other.
* **How much time do you have for tourism activities?** (Select One, required): Select how much time you have for tourism activities, such as 1-2 days, 2-4 days, 5-7 days, or More than 7 days.
* **What type of tourist experience do you identify with?** (Select Multiple, required): Select one or more types of tourist experience you identify with, such as Nature-based, Culture-based, Adventure-based, Food-based, Relaxation-based, or Education-based.
* **Do you have a preferred mode of transportation?** (Select One, required): Select your preferred mode of transportation, such as By car, By train, By plane, By bus, By bike, or Walking.
* **Do you prefer to stay in a city?** (Select One, required): Select whether you prefer to stay in a city or not.
* **Comments** (Note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your participation in the conference.

Note: The information provided in this form will be used to create a personalized experience for each attendee, and it is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to get the most out of this event.
