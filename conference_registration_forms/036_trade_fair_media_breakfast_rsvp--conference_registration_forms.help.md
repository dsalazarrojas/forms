# Trade Fair Media Breakfast RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Fair Media Breakfast RSVP form is used to collect information from attendees, media professionals, and guests for the Trade Fair event. This form is used for communication and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Info section with your name and any additional information you would like to share.
2. Provide details about your guest(s) if they will be attending with you.
3. Specify your name and role (e.g., Trade Reporter, Trade Photographer, Guest).
4. Indicate your attendance plans (e.g., True, False, Maybe).
5. Select your dietary needs.
6. Check if you will be attending for media purposes.
7. Enter your contact information if you are a media professional.
8. Share any additional comments or information about yourself or your guest(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (General Info, text, false): A brief introduction about yourself and your guest(s).
* **Guest Details** (Guest Details, text, false): Details about your guest(s), including their name, role, and attendance plans.
* **Name** (Name, text, false): Your name.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, false): Your role at the event (e.g., Trade Reporter, Trade Photographer, Guest).
* **Attendance** (Attendance, select_multiple, false): Indicate if you will be attending (True), not attending (False), or unsure (Maybe).
* **Dietary Needs** (Dietary Needs, select_multiple, false): Select your dietary preferences (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, None).
* **Media Attendance** (Media Attendance, select_one, false): Check if you will be attending for media purposes (True or False).
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, false): Your contact information if you are a media professional.
* **Social Media** (Social Media, text, false): Share your social media handle.
* **Email** (Email, email, false): Your email address.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, false): Any additional comments or information about yourself or your guest(s).
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, false): Any additional information about yourself or your guest(s).
* **Media Contact** (Media Contact, text, false): If you are a media contact, please enter your information.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, false): The name of your media contact.
* **Contact Title** (Contact Title, text, false): The title of your media contact.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, false): The phone number of your media contact.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, false): The email address of your media contact.
* **Company** (Company, text, false): The name of your company.
* **Address** (Address, text, false): The address of your company.
* **City** (City, text, false): The city of your company.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, false): The zip code of your company.
* **State** (State, text, false): The state of your company.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, false): The phone number of your company.
* **Photo Credit** (Photo Credit, text, false): If you are providing photos, please enter your credit information.
* **Image URL** (Image URL, text, false): If you are uploading an image, please provide the URL.
