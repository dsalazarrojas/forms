# Worship Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from registrants for a worship conference. It covers various aspects of the registrant's details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Fill in your registrant's details, such as name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select your dietary preferences and meal options.
4. Provide your conference title and dates.
5. Specify your preferences for merchandise, such as t-shirts and hoodies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Registration**: This is the title of the form. Please enter a descriptive title for the conference.
* **Multiple Registrations**: Select 'Yes' if you are registering multiple people.
* **Single Registration**: Select 'Yes' if you are registering only one person.
* **Registrant Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be listed in the conference materials.
* **Registrant Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Registrant Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Registrant Address**: Enter your address for shipping and other purposes.
* **Registrant City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **Registrant State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Registrant ZIP**: Enter your zip code.
* **Registrant DOB**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Conference Title**: Enter the title of the conference.
* **Conference Start Date**: Enter the start date of the conference.
* **Conference End Date**: Enter the end date of the conference.
* **Registrant Diet**: Select 'Yes' if you have dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Registrant Meal Preference**: Select your preferred meal options.
* **Registrant Food Allergy**: If you have a food allergy, please enter it here.
* **Registrant Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
* **Registrant T-Shirt Size**: Select the size of t-shirt you prefer.
* **Registrant T-Shirt Color**: Select the color of t-shirt you prefer.
* **Registrant Hoodie Size**: Select the size of hoodie you prefer.
* **Registrant Hoodie Color**: Select the color of hoodie you prefer.
* **Conference Ending**: Enter a description of how you would like the conference to end.
* **Registrant Tees**: Please enter any t-shirt preferences or requests.
* **Registrant Shirts**: Please enter any shirt preferences or requests.
