# Lean Innovation Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information and specific details about attendees for the Lean Innovation Forum. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure you receive important updates and information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and company.
2. Provide your job title and job role.
3. Select your role at the event (CEO, Manager, or Team Member).
4. Enter your team size if you are attending with a team.
5. Fill in the name of the event you are registering for.
6. Select the date and time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address. This is how you will receive important updates about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, text, optional): Enter your job role or position.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select one of the following roles: CEO, Manager, or Team Member. This determines your level of access and involvement with the event.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of your team if you are attending with one.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event. If left blank, it will be assumed as the current date.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the event. If left blank, it will be assumed as 12:00 PM.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration or event participation.

## Tips

* Please fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are attending with a team, please ensure you enter the correct team size.
* You will receive important updates and notifications about the event after submitting this form.
* Please review your submitted information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
