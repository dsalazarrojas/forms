# Oncology Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oncology Conference Registration form is designed to collect the necessary information from attendees to facilitate their participation in the conference. This form helps the organizers with contact, medical, and session preferences details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin on the `main_page` where you will see a brief overview of the conference.
2. Click on the "Attendee Information" page to enter your personal details.
3. Fill in the required fields: `First Name`, `Last Name`, `Email`, and `Phone`.
4. Select your `Sessions of Interest` by choosing multiple options from the list.
5. Move on to the next page, "Session Preferences", and select your preferred sessions.
6. Provide your `Contact Information` including your contact name, number, and a brief description of your medical conditions.
7. Fill in the `Doctor Information` and `Emergency Contact` fields as needed.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Sessions of Interest** (`session_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple sessions you are interested in attending. Please note that this is a required field.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to be contacted about the conference.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Number** (`emergency_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in the conference.
- **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical information that may be required.
- **Medical Credentials** (`medical_credentials`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant medical credentials or documents.
- **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, `text`, optional): List any relevant medical specialties.
- **Session Preference 1** (`session_preference_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more session preferences from the list.
- **Session Preference 2** (`session_preference_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more session preferences from the list.
- **Session Preference 3** (`session_preference_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more session preferences from the list.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): If you need to provide a specific date, please enter it here.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): If you need to provide a specific time, please enter it here.
- **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, `text`, required): Review and submit your registration form.
