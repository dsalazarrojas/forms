# Event Innovation Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by event planners and attendees to provide information about the events they are interested in, their contact details, and any other relevant details. This form is essential for the smooth organization and coordination of the event, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered for a successful and engaging event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Event Planner Information**:

    * Fill in the fields as required and fill in all relevant information about the event.
    * Make sure to select the correct event date from the calendar or provide the event date manually.
2. **Provide Attendee Information**:

    * Enter the contact details of the attendees, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. **Indicate Session Interest**:

    * Select the sessions you are interested in by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
4. **Add Additional Notes (Optional)**:

    * You can add any additional comments or information in the 'Note' section.
5. **Submit the Form**:

    * Once you have filled in all the necessary information, click on the 'Submit' button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner Info (Page 1)**: This section captures the information about the event planner. Enter your name, and any relevant information about your event.
* **Attendee Info (Page 1)**: This section captures the contact details of the attendees, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Session Interested (Page 1)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the sessions you are interested in to help the event team with planning and coordination.
* **Presenter Info (Page 2)**: This section captures information about the presenter, such as their name and any other relevant details.
* **Event Details (Page 2)**: Enter any other details about the event that you feel is relevant.
* **Session Interested 2 (Page 2)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the sessions you are interested in.
* **Contact Info (Page 3)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Session Interested 3 (Page 3)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the sessions you are interested in.
* **Note (Page 4)**: Add any additional comments or information about the event.
* **Email (Page 4)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Page 4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date (Page 5)**: Select the event date from the calendar or enter it manually.
