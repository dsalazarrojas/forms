# Dialogue Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dialogue Series Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable experience at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Attendee name" field.
2. If you have an affiliation with an organization, enter the name in the "Organization affiliation" field. This is optional.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email address" field for registration confirmation purposes.
4. Select one or more sessions you are interested in attending from the "Sessions interested in attending" dropdown.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, mention them in the "Dietary restrictions" text field.
6. If you have any accessibility needs, describe them in the "Accessibility needs" text field.
7. Choose whether to consent to being recorded or photographed in the "Consent to be recorded or photographed" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Organization affiliation**: If you represent a company or group, enter the name here. This is optional.
* **Email address**: A valid email address is required for registration confirmation and future communication.
* **Sessions interested in attending**: Select one or more sessions that you are interested in attending.
* **Dietary restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them here.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you require any special accommodations or have specific accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Consent to be recorded or photographed**: Choose whether to consent to being recorded or photographed during the event for documentation purposes.

## Tips
Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form for an accurate and complete registration. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us at the address provided below.
