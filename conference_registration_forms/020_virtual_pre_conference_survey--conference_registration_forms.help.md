## Virtual Pre Conference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Pre Conference Survey is a form designed to gather information from attendees regarding their physical presence and availability to provide insights for conference organizers to plan a better experience. This form helps attendees indicate their attendance status, physical conditions, share their thoughts, and provide their contact information for further communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
3.  For "Attendance status", select one of the provided options that best describes your expected attendance.
4.  For "Physical conditions", select all the options that apply to you.
5.  In the "Thoughts" field, type your comments, feedback, or suggestions.
6.  For "Email" and "Phone number" fields, provide your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendance status**: Please select the option that best describes your expected attendance status. For example, if you plan to attend, select "Option 1". If you are unable to attend, select "Option 2" or "Option 3".
*   **Physical conditions**: Select all the options that apply to you, such as "Yes" for "Yes" if you have any of the listed conditions.
*   **Thoughts**: This is a text field where you can type your comments, feedback, or suggestions. Be as detailed as possible to help us know more about you.
*   **Email**: Type your email address to allow us to contact you in case of any updates or further information.
*   **Phone number**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at [contact information].
