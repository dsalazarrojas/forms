# Ingredient Transparency Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ingredient Transparency Summit Registration form is designed to collect information from potential attendees of the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all the necessary information to provide.
2. Complete each field as instructed.
3. Click on the "Submit" button when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Field 1: Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (`form_data`, text, optional): Please enter your name and any additional details you'd like to share about yourself.

### Field 2: Session 1
* **Session 1** (`session_preferences_1`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred session for this day.

### Field 3: Session 2
* **Session 2** (`session_preferences_2`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred session for this day.

### Field 4: Session 3
* **Session 3** (`session_preferences_3`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred session for this day.

### Field 5: Submit
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your registration form.

### Field 6: Error Message
* **None** (`error_message`, note, optional): This field is not editable and will display any error messages that occur during form submission.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submission.
* If you encounter an error message, review the form again and try resubmitting.
* If issues persist, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
