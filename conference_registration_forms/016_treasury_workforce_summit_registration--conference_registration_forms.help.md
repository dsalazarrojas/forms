# treasury_workforce_summit_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to register attendees for the Treasury Workforce Summit, a virtual event for employees to learn about new technologies and trends in the field of treasury management. The form ensures that attendees provide necessary information for the summit organizers and helps them prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name, Last Name, Email, Job Title, Department, Role, Location, Participation Type, Time, Date**: Fill out these fields with your personal and professional details, as required.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. If you have any special requests or comments, use the **Comments** field to note them.
4. If you have a preferred time for the event, use the **Time** field to specify it.
5. If you have any additional information about your availability, use the **Date** field to specify it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or current role in the organization.
* **Department**: Enter your department or team you belong to.
* **Role**: Enter your role or position in the organization.
* **Location**: Enter your location or work place.
* **Participation Type**: Select your preferred participation type (Full-time, Part-time, Remote).
* **Preferences**: Select all the timeslots you are available for (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, All day).
* **Comments**: Enter any special requests or comments about your participation.
* **Time**: Enter your preferred time for the summit.
* **Date**: Enter your preferred date for the summit.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter correct and up-to-date information to ensure you receive timely updates about the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organizers or event team.
* Review and double-check your entries before submission.
* If you need to cancel or modify your participation, contact the organizers or event team.
