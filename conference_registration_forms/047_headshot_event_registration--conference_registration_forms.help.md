# Headshot Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Headshot Event Registration form is used to collect information from attendees about their event attendance and preferences. This form is required for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Event Details section with your event information.
2. List your Attendee Details in the Attendee Details section.
3. Select your preferred Headshot Sessions from the Headshot Sessions section.
4. Indicate your photo preferences and photo release consent in the Photo Preferences and Photo Release sections.
5. Specify your promotional use preferences in the Promotional Use section.
6. Add any additional notes in the Notes section.
7. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Fill in the event information (event name, date, time, location, etc.).
* **Attendee Details** (`attendees_details`, text, required/optional): List the names and contact information of all attendees registering.
* **Headshot Sessions** (`headshot_sessions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the headshot sessions you would like to attend.
* **Photo Preferences** (`photo_preferences`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if you have any photo preferences (e.g. no photo, photo with a friend, etc.).
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required/optional): Agree to the photo release terms and conditions.
* **Promotional Use** (`promotional_use`, select_one, required/optional): Specify if you allow your photo to be used for promotional purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number, etc.).

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission from all attendees to collect their contact information.
* Please ensure you have read and understand the photo release terms and conditions.
