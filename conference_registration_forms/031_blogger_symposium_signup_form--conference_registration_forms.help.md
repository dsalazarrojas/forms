# Blogger Symposium Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for the annual blogger symposium. This form will ask for information such as name, email, phone number, blog name, blog URL, and more to ensure that you have a smooth and enjoyable experience at the symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the field label.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for the select fields (e.g., registration type, attendance days, and session tracks of interest).
3. Enter your full name as it should appear on the badge.
4. Provide a valid email address for confirmation and updates.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Enter your blog name and URL.
7. If applicable, enter your organization or company name.
8. Select your registration type (e.g., full conference pass, single day pass, virtual attendance, or workshop only).
9. Select all the days you plan to attend.
10. Choose your session tracks of interest.
11. Select your dietary requirements for catering purposes.
12. Choose whether you need accommodation assistance.
13. Select your t-shirt size for conference swag.
14. If presenting, provide your proposed topic.
15. If paying by credit card, please select credit card as your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blogger Symposium Registration** (id: 1, label: Blogger Symposium Registration, type: note): This is the header for the registration form.
* **Full Name** (id: 2, label: Full Name, type: text, required: true): Please enter your full name as it should appear on your badge.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, label: Email Address, type: email, required: true): Enter a valid email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, label: Phone Number, type: text, required: true): Enter your contact number.
* **Blog Name** (id: 5, label: Blog Name, type: text, required: true): Enter the name of your blog.
* **Blog URL** (id: 6, label: Blog URL, type: text, required: true): Enter the URL of your blog.
* **Organization or Company** (id: 7, label: Organization or Company, type: text, required: false): If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (id: 8, label: Job Title, type: text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Registration Type** (id: 9, label: Registration Type, type: select_one, required: true): Select your registration type (e.g., full conference pass, single day pass, virtual attendance, or workshop only).
* **Days Attending** (id: 10, label: Days Attending, type: select_multiple, required: true): Select all the days you plan to attend.
* **Session Tracks of Interest** (id: 11, label: Session Tracks of Interest, type: select_multiple, required: true): Choose the session tracks of interest to you.
* **Dietary Requirements** (id: 12, label: Dietary Requirements, type: select_one, required: true): Select your dietary requirements for catering purposes.
* **Accommodation Needed** (id: 13, label: Accommodation Needed, type: select_one, required: true): Choose whether you need accommodation assistance.
* **T-Shirt Size** (id: 14, label: T-Shirt Size, type: select_one, required: false): Select your t-shirt size for conference swag.
* **Networking Goals** (id: 15, label: Networking Goals, type: text, required: false): Enter your networking goals (e.g., seek collaborations or partnerships).
* **Interested in Presenting** (id: 16, label: Interested in Presenting, type: select_one, required: true): Choose whether you are interested in presenting.
* **Proposed Topic** (id: 17, label: Proposed Topic, type: text, required: false): If presenting, enter your proposed topic.
* **How Did You Hear About Symposium** (id: 18, label: How Did You Hear About Symposium, type: select_one, required: false): Select how you heard about the symposium.
