# exhibition_corner_space_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "exhibition_corner_space_booking_form" is a user-facing form designed to collect essential information for conference and exhibition-related bookings and submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your conference details in the first field.
2.  Enter your event details in the next field.
3.  Describe your exhibition details in the subsequent field.
4.  Fill out your contact information to help us get in touch with you.
5.  Specify the event dates for the exhibition.
6.  Enter the event times for the exhibition.
7.  Include your booth details for the exhibition.
8.  Submit your information for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Conference Details** (conference_details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your conference.
*   **Event Details** (event_details, text, optional): Describe your event.
*   **Exhibition Details** (exhibition_details, text, optional): Provide details about your exhibition.
*   **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we get in touch with you.
*   **Event Dates** (event_dates, date, optional): Enter the event dates in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Event Times** (event_times, time, optional): Enter the event start and end times in the format HH:MM.
*   **Booth Details** (booth_details, text, optional): Include any specific details about your booth.
*   **Submission** (submission, text, optional): This field is not used in the provided form data.
*   **Submission Success** (submission_success, text, optional): This field is not used in the provided form data.
*   **Submission Error** (submission_error, text, optional): This field is not used in the provided form data.

## Tips

*   Double-check your submission to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
*   If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us through your contact information.
