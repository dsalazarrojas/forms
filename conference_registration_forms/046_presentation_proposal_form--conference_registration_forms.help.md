# Presentation Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a presentation proposal for the conference. It gathers information about the presentation, speaker, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Make sure to select the correct presentation type (Presentation, Workshop, or Panel).
3. Choose the presentation format you are submitting (Video, Slides, or Handouts).
4. Add any additional information or files related to your presentation.
5. Enter your name and email for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Presentation Proposal Form**: This is the main title of your presentation proposal.
* **Speaker**: Enter the name of the speaker who will be presenting the proposal.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the speaker who will be presenting the proposal.
* **Title**: Enter the title of your presentation.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the presentation (if known).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the presentation (if known).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the presentation (if known).
* **Presentation Type**: Select the type of presentation you are submitting (Presentation, Workshop, or Panel).
* **Presentation Length**: Enter the estimated length of your presentation.
* **Presentation Format**: Select the format of your presentation (Video, Slides, or Handouts).
* **Presenter Experience**: Enter any relevant experience or credentials of the presenter.
* **Presentation File**: If you have any files related to your presentation, upload them here.
* **Presentation Link**: If you have a link to your presentation, enter it here.
* **Presentation Video**: If you have a video related to your presentation, enter it here.
* **Presentation Slides**: If you have slides related to your presentation, enter them here.
* **Presentation Audio**: If you have audio related to your presentation, enter it here.
* **Other**: If you have any other information related to your presentation, enter it here.
* **Other Info**: If you have any additional information about your presentation, enter it here.
* **Other Files**: If you have any other files related to your presentation, enter them here.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the submitter of the proposal.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email as the submitter of the proposal.
