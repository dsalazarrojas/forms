# Conference Session Chair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Session Chair Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential session chairs about their personal and professional details, preferences, and availability. This form helps us to manage the conference organization process and ensure a diverse and suitable selection of session chairs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter your professional title and institution or organization.
4. Select your area of expertise and preferred session types.
5. Indicate your availability for chairing sessions.
6. Provide your brief biography for the conference program.
7. Upload a photo of yourself.
8. Confirm your commitment to chair a session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Chair Full Name** (`chair_name`, text, required): Your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Professional Title** (`title`, text, required): Your professional title or position.
* **Institution or Organization** (`institution`, text, required): Your affiliation or institution.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Your department or specific unit within your institution.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State or Province** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code or ZIP code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select your areas of expertise.
* **Previous Chairing Experience** (`chairing_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have previous chairing experience.
* **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, text, optional): Describe your experience as a session chair.
* **Preferred Session Types** (`preferred_sessions`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of sessions you would like to chair.
* **Topic Preferences** (`topic_preferences`, text, optional): Specify the topics of interest you would like to chair.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to chair sessions.
* **Time Preferences** (`time_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred time slots for chairing sessions.
* **Preferred Session Length** (`session_length`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred session duration.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any special accommodations you require.
* **Brief Biography** (`biography`, text, required): Enter a brief description of yourself for the conference program.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload a link to your photo.
* **Travel Support Needed** (`travel_support`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need travel support.
* **Accommodation Needed** (`accommodation_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you require accommodation.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_one, required): Select your dietary requirements.
* **Dietary Notes** (`dietary_notes`, text, optional): Specify any dietary notes.
* **CV or Resume** (`cv_attached`, text, optional): Upload a link to your CV or resume.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): Enter the names and contacts of your professional references.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Commitment Confirmation** (`commitment_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment to chair a session.

Note: This guide is not exhaustive, please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
