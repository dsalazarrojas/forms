# Professional Learning Conference Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Learning Conference Application form is designed to gather information from attendees about their interests, preferences, and details for a professional learning conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
2. **School District**: Enter the district of the school.
3. **Session Interests**: Select all the sessions you are interested in attending by checking the corresponding boxes.
4. **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or additional information you'd like to share, enter it here.
5. **Attendee Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
6. **Session Interests Attendee**: Select all the sessions that you are interested in attending.
7. **Conference Details**: Enter any additional conference details or information.
8. **Sessions**: Select all the sessions that you are interested in attending.
9. **Attendee Details**: Select the type of conference you are attending (e.g., School District, Training Organization).
10. **School**: Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
11. **District**: Enter the district of the school.
12. **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name (School Name)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
* **School District (School District)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter the district of the school.
* **Session Interests (Session Interests)** (`select_multiple`, Not Required): List the conference sessions you are interested in. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Special Requests (Special Requests)** (`text`, Not Required): If you have any special requests or additional information you'd like to share, enter it here.
* **Attendee Details (Attendee Details)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Session Interests Attendee (Session Interests Attendee)** (`select_multiple`, Required): Select all the sessions that you are interested in attending.
* **Conference Details (Conference Details)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter any additional conference details or information.
* **Sessions (Sessions)** (`select_multiple`, Required): Select all the sessions that you are interested in attending.
* **Attendee Details (Attendee Details)** (`select_one`, Required): Select the type of conference you are attending (e.g., School District, Training Organization).
* **School (School)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
* **District (District)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter the district of the school.
* **Organization (Organization)** (`text`, Not Required): Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering your school and district information.
* Ensure you select the correct session interests and attendee type.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure everything is accurate and complete.
