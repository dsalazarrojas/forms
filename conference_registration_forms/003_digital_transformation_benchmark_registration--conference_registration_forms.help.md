# Digital Transformation Benchmark Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for organizations to register their interest in participating in the Digital Transformation Benchmark program. Please fill out the form to provide us with information about your organization and its goals for participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and provide accurate information about your organization.
2. Fill out the registration form completely, including all required fields.
3. Review and confirm your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**: This is the main section where you can enter any additional information about your organization's participation in the benchmark program.
* **Organization Name**: Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Digital Maturity Stage**: Choose the stage that best describes your organization's current digital maturity level: Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3.
* **Goals for Benchmark Program**: Please describe your goals for participating in the program.
* **Enrollment Agreement**: You must agree to participate in the program by selecting "I accept".
* **Goals for Benchmark**: Please describe your specific goals for the benchmark program.
* **Email**: Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Please enter your valid phone number.
* **Name**: Please enter your name.
* **Role**: Please enter your role within the organization.
* **Department**: Please enter the department you belong to within the organization.
* **Organization**: Please enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Person**: Please enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share about your organization.
* **Goals for Study**: Please describe your specific goals for the study.
