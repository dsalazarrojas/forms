# Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees and speakers for the Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference. This form will help us understand your interests, preferences, and consent for the conference, enabling us to provide a better experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your conference information in the `conference_info` field.
2. Provide your attendee details in the `attendee_details` field.
3. Share your preferences with us in the `preferences` field.
4. Check the `consents` field to indicate your consent for the conference.
5. Click on the `submit` button to send your registration details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **conference_info** (`conference_info`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Fill in your conference-related information, such as the type of conference you are interested in, the number of attendees you are registering, and any other relevant details.
* **attendee_details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Provide your attendee's details, including your name, role, and organization, to help us prepare for the conference.
* **preferences** (`preferences`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Share your preferences for the conference program, including any sessions, workshops, or networking activities you are interested in, and any special requests you may have.
* **consents** (`consents`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Check the relevant boxes to indicate your consent for the conference, including consent for photo and video recording, and any other relevant consents.
* **submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Click this button to send your registration details and complete the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to help us provide a better experience for you and other attendees.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
