# AI And Law Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The AI And Law Conference Registration form is designed for attendees to register for the conference. By filling out this form, you'll provide necessary information to receive a personalized badge and help the organizers prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee name**, which should be your full name as it should appear on your badge.
2. Enter your **Email address**, where you'll receive the registration confirmation.
3. Choose your **Ticket type** from the available options (Standard, Student, Speaker, or Sponsor).
4. Check the **Sessions or tracks of interest** you plan to attend.
5. (Optional) If you have any **Dietary restrictions**, please let us know for catering purposes.
6. (Optional) If you have any **Accessibility requirements**, provide the necessary information so we can accommodate your needs.
7. Enter your **Emergency contact phone number**, including the name of the contact person.
8. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as it should appear on your badge.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): Your affiliation or employer.
*   **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): The email address where you'll receive registration confirmation.
* **Ticket type** (`ticket_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your registration level (Standard, Student, Speaker, or Sponsor).
*   **Sessions or tracks of interest** (`sessions_interested`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the sessions you plan to attend.
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): Provide any dietary restrictions for catering purposes.
* **Accessibility requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, `text`, optional): Share any accessibility needs you have.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number and name.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your attendee name is accurate and consistent with other registration information.
* Double-check your email address to receive the confirmation correctly.
* Be specific when selecting your ticket type and sessions of interest.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, let us know to ensure a smooth experience.
