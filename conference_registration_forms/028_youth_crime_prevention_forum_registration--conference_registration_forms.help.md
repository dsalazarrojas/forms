# Youth Crime Prevention Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the Youth Crime Prevention Forum. The purpose of this form is to gather attendee registration details, including name, contact information, and interest in attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date for the **Event Date** that you would like to attend.
6. Indicate your **Attendance Interest** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Indicate your **Registration Interest** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
8. Enter any additional **Comment** about your registration in the "Comment" field.
9. Select the **Category** that best describes your interest in the event (e.g., "Yes" for Yes, "No" for No, or select "Other" to specify a different category).
10. If you select "Other" in the **Category** field, you will be prompted to provide more information in the **Category Other** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date**: Select a date for the event you would like to attend.
* **Attendance Interest**: Indicate your interest in attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Registration Interest**: Indicate your interest in registering for the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your interest in the event (e.g., "Yes" for Yes, "No" for No, or select "Other" to specify a different category).
* **Category Other**: If you select "Other" in the **Category** field, this field will appear to allow you to specify a different category.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.
