# Management Consulting Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Management Consulting Forum Registration Form is a tool used by attendees to register for the conference and provide essential information about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. If your company is relevant to your registration, enter it in the Company field.
6. Enter your current Job Title in the Job Title field.
7. Enter your current Department (if applicable) in the Department field.
8. Select your Job Status from the list:
	- Manager
	- Director
	- Team Member
	- CEO
9. Choose a date and time for your attendance in the Attendance Date and Attendance Time fields.
10. Select how you want to attend the forum (In-person or Online) in the Registration Type field.
11. Add any additional message or information in the Message field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, enter it here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your current department (if applicable).
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Enter your current role.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your job status from the list:
	+ Manager
	+ Director
	+ Team Member
	+ CEO
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you will attend the forum.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of your attendance.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required): Select how you want to attend the forum:
	+ In-person
	+ Online
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information or notes.
