# Retail Sustainability Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for retail businesses interested in collaborating with us on sustainability initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to include all required information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Partner Details (Partner Details)
- **Partner Details** (Partner Details, required: false): This field is intended to be a brief description of your company or store.
### Interests (Interests, select multiple, required: false): Select all the interests that apply to your business. You can choose one or more of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
### Goals (Goals, required: false): This field is for you to describe your company's or store's goals and objectives related to sustainability.
### Contact Information (Contact Information, required: false): This field is for you to provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
