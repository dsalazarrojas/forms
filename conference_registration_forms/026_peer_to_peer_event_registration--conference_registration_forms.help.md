# Peer To Peer Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer To Peer Event Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees for our Peer To Peer events. This form will help event organizers prepare for the event, ensuring that attendees have a fantastic experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the title of the event, ensuring it accurately represents the event you are registering for.
2.  Provide your attendee details, including any relevant background information.
3.  Indicate your role preference by selecting the relevant options (Yes/No).
4.  Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you about event updates and information.
5.  Provide your contact information, which will help event organizers get in touch with you.
6.  Select a date from the available options for the event, as this information will help event organizers plan and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Peer To Peer Event Registration**: This is the title of the event you are registering for. Enter the accurate and up-to-date title of the event.
* **Attendee Details**: Provide any additional background information about yourself that you believe is relevant to the event organizers.
* **Role Preference**: Choose your role preference for the event (Yes or No). This information will help event organizers plan accordingly.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so event organizers can contact you with event updates and information.
* **Contact**: Provide your contact information so event organizers can get in touch with you.
* **Date**: Select the date of the event, which will help event organizers plan and schedule the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the event title is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests for the event, please mention them in the attendee details section.
* Double-check your email address and contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any conflicts or changes in your schedule, please notify event organizers through the provided email address.
