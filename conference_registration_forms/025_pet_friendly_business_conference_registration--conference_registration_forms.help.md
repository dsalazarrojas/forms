# Pet Friendly Business Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register pets and their owners for the conference. To register your pet, please fill out this form completely and accurately. If you have already registered your pet, you will be prompted to review and update the information if any changes have been made.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Type of Pet" to indicate if you are bringing a pet.
2. If you are bringing a pet, fill out the pet's information on the following pages:
	* "Pet Name"
	* "Date of Birth"
	* "Owner Name"
	* "Owner Email"
	* "Owner Phone"
3. If you are not bringing a pet, you can skip to the next page.
4. Fill out your own information on the following pages:
	* "Attendee Name"
	* "Job Title"
	* "Organization"
	* "Email"
	* "Phone"
5. Select all the sessions you are interested in attending by checking the boxes next to each session title.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type of Pet**: Select "Yes" if you are bringing a pet along. If you are not, select "No".
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your pet's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the pet's owner (usually yourself).
* **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name, the person who is attending the conference.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Sessions**: Select all the sessions you are interested in attending by checking the boxes next to each session title.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your registration.

## Tips
* Please make sure to accurately and completely fill out all fields, as this will help us provide the best experience for you and your pet.
* If you have any comments or questions, feel free to include them in the "Comments" field.
* If you are unsure about anything, contact us for help.
