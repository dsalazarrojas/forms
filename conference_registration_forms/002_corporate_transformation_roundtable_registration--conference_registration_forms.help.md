# Corporate Transformation Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the registration process for the Corporate Transformation Roundtable. Please read this guide carefully to ensure you complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name and Last name fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your email address to receive updates and invitations.
3. Provide your phone number for future communication.
4. Select your topic interest(s) from the options below to indicate what you're interested in learning about:
	* Artificial intelligence
	* Blockchain
	* Internet of things
	* Machine learning
	* Other (please specify)
5. Confirm your consent by selecting "True" if you consent to receiving communications and updates from us.
6. Fill in your company name, job title, department, and role if applicable.
7. Optionally, provide any other information about your function or interests.
8. You will also be asked to specify your function or role for other related events and networking opportunities.
