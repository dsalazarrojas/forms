# HR Sustainability Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the HR Sustainability Forum. It will help us gather information about attendees and ensure that the necessary contact and demographic information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
2. Enter your First name in the text box provided.
3. Enter your Last name in the text box provided.
4. Enter your Email address in the text box provided.
5. Enter your Phone number in the text box provided.
6. Enter the name of the event you are attending in the text box provided.
7. Select your session interest(s) from the dropdown menu.
8. If necessary, provide your Event date and Event time.
9. Choose how many attendees you plan to bring with you (Attendance).
10. Provide any comments or additional information in the text box provided.
11. Enter your Organization in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Event name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Session Interest** (`session_interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your session interests. The options are: HR, Sustainability, and Conference.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): If necessary, provide the date of the event.
* **Event time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): If necessary, provide the time of the event.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `number`, required: false): Choose how many attendees you plan to bring with you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or additional information.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the event organizers.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permission to register for the forum if registering on behalf of someone else. 

Note: The explanations for each field are based on the provided YAML field labels and hints. If a field label is unclear or ambiguous, a best-effort explanation has been provided to ensure clarity and practicality.
