# Sponsor Booth Electricity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to manage the electricity requirements and installation details for sponsor booths at a conference/event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your sponsor booth details.
2. Provide your contact information (name, email, and phone number) to ensure we can reach you for any further information.
3. Specify your power requirements (power consumption and voltage requirement).
4. Choose your preferred installation schedule.
5. Select the service provider and type of electricity service you need.
6. Enter the total installation cost and electricity rate.
7. Provide any comments or additional information about your booth, service provider, and installation cost.
8. Confirm your approval and installation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Booth Electricity Request** (`sponsor_booth_electricity_request`): This is the title of the form, which describes the purpose of the form.
* **Installation Schedule** (`installation_schedule`): Choose a date and time for the installation of your electricity services.
* **Power Requirements** (`power_requirements`): Enter the amount of power your booth requires in watts.
* **Power Consumption** (`power_consumption`): Enter the amount of power your booth consumes in watts.
* **Total Booth Size** (`total_booth_size`): Enter the size of your booth in square meters or feet.
* **Number of Outlets** (`number_of_outlets`): Enter the number of outlets your booth requires.
* **Voltage Requirement** (`voltage_requirement`): Choose the voltage level required for your booth (110, 220, or 380).
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date of your approval for the electricity services.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`): Choose who approved your electricity services (Administrator, Manager, or Sponsor).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name to ensure we can reach you for any further information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address to ensure we can reach you for any further information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you for any further information.
* **Approved At** (`approved_at`): Enter the date when your approval was given.
* **Installation Status** (`installation_status`): Choose the status of your electricity services installation (Installed, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Comments Status** (`comments_status`): Choose the status of your comments (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Electricity Service Type** (`electricity_service_type`): Choose the type of electricity service you need (On-site Generator, Off-site Generator, or City Power).
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`): Choose your service provider (Service Provider 1, Service Provider 2, or Service Provider 3).
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`): Enter the date of your electricity services installation.
* **Comments Service Provider** (`comments_service_provider`): Provide any comments about your service provider.
* **Total Installation Cost** (`total_installation_cost`): Enter the total cost of your electricity services installation.
* **Comments Installation Cost** (`comments_installation_cost`): Provide any comments about your installation cost.
* **Electricity Rate** (`electricity_rate`): Enter the rate of your electricity services.
* **Comments Electricity Rate** (`comments_electricity_rate`): Provide any comments about your electricity rate.
* **Comments Other** (`comments_other`): Provide any other comments about your electricity services.
