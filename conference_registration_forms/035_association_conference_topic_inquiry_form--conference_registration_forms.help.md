# Association Conference Topic Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential speakers for the association's conference. It helps the conference organizers evaluate and schedule sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your inquiry date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Select the conference you are interested in presenting a session for.
3. Enter the year of the conference.
4. Provide your first name, last name, professional title, and organization.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Write a brief professional biography.
7. Describe your prior speaking experience.
8. Enter the title and description of your proposed session topic.
9. List the learning objectives of your session.
10. Specify the target audience for your session.
11. Choose the proposed session format (e.g., keynote, full-length session, etc.).
12. Select the preferred session duration.
13. If applicable, list any co-presenters.
14. Specify if you will provide any handouts or materials.
15. If you will provide materials, describe them briefly.
16. Indicate if you have presented at this conference before.
17. If you have presented at this conference before, specify the year you presented.
18. Describe how your topic aligns with the conference theme.
19. Enter the date you completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Inquiry Date
* **inquiry_date** (`inquiry_date`, `date`, required): The date you submit this form.

### Conference Name
* **conference_name** (`conference_name`, `text`, required): The conference you are interested in presenting a session for.

### Conference Year
* **conference_year** (`conference_year`, `number`, required): The year of the conference.

### Speaker First Name
* **speaker_first_name** (`speaker_first_name`, `text`, required): Your first name.

### Speaker Last Name
* **speaker_last_name** (`speaker_last_name`, `text`, required): Your last name.

### Professional Title
* **speaker_title** (`speaker_title`, `text`, required): Your professional title or position.

### Organization
* **speaker_organization** (`speaker_organization`, `text`, required): Your company or institution.

### Email Address
* **speaker_email** (`speaker_email`, `email`, required): Your contact email.

### Phone Number
* **speaker_phone** (`speaker_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.

### Speaker Bio
* **speaker_bio** (`speaker_bio`, `text`, required): A brief professional biography.

### Prior Speaking Experience
* **speaking_experience** (`speaking_experience`, `text`, required): A description of your prior conference presentations.

### Proposed Topic Title
* **topic_title** (`topic_title`, `text`, required): The title of your proposed session topic.

### Topic Description
* **topic_description** (`topic_description`, `text`, required): A detailed description of your session topic.

### Learning Objectives
* **topic_objectives** (`topic_objectives`, `text`, required): The learning objectives of your session.

### Target Audience
* **target_audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): The target audience for your session.

### Session Format
* **session_format** (`session_format`, `select_one`, required): The proposed session format (e.g., keynote, full-length session, etc.).

### Session Duration
* **session_duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required): The preferred session duration.

### Co-Presenters (if any)
* **co_presenters** (`co_presenters`, `text`, optional): Names of other speakers who will be co-presenting with you.

### Audio/Visual Requirements
* **av_requirements** (`av_requirements`, `text`, optional): Any audio or visual requirements for your session.

### Handouts or Materials Provided
* **materials_provided** (`materials_provided`, `select_one`, required): Whether you will provide any handouts or materials.

### Description of Materials
* **materials_description** (`materials_description`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the materials you will provide.

### Previous Conference Participation
* **previous_conference_participation** (`previous_conference_participation`, `select_one`, required): Whether you have presented at this conference before.

### Year of Previous Presentation
* **conference_attendance_year** (`conference_attendance_year`, `number`, optional): The year you presented at this conference before.

### Alignment with Conference Theme
* **topic_alignment** (`topic_alignment`, `text`, required): A description of how your topic aligns with the conference theme.

### Form Submission Date
* **inquiry_submission_date** (`inquiry_submission_date`, `date`, required): The date you completed this form.
