# Tax Strategy Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Strategy Conference Registration form is used to collect registration information and details from attendees for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and professional details.
2. Choose your preferred sessions.
3. Select your tax requirements and payment method.
4. Enter any additional attendee requirements or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your registration.
	+ Label: Registration Information
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Session Preferences**: Choose which sessions you would like to attend.
	+ Label: Session Preferences
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **What are your tax requirements**: Select your tax requirement type (Individual, Business, or Other).
	+ Label: What are your tax requirements
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.

	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No

	+ Label: Name

	+ Required: No

	+ Label: Additional Attendee Requirements
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
