# Financial Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering attendees for the Financial Symposium. It aims to collect relevant information and preferences from users to ensure a smooth and organized experience for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (denoted by asterisks).
2. Choose the sessions you would like to attend from the "session_choice" field.
3. Select your preferred payment method (if applicable).
4. Fill in your attendee's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Info** (attendee_info, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself that you would like to share with the symposium organizers.
- **Session Choice** (session_choice, select multiple, optional): Select all the sessions you would like to attend by choosing from the available options. You can choose one or multiple sessions.
- **Payment Method** (payment_method, select one, optional): If you have not paid for your registration yet, select your preferred payment method for processing. This field will only appear if the event organizers have specified this information.
- **Attendee Name** (attendee_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person attending the symposium. This is the name that will be displayed on the symposium materials and communications.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Review your session choices carefully to ensure you are selecting the correct sessions for your interests and schedule.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with your registration, contact the symposium organizers at form@gic.mx.
