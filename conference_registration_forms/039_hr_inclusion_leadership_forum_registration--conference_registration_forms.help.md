# hr_inclusion_leadership_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Inclusion Leadership Forum Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals attending the conference. This form aims to gather details on attendees' interests, preferences, and requirements for the sessions and event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to receive updates and communications from the organizers.
3. Indicate your session interests by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the available options.
4. If applicable, specify your accessibility needs to ensure a comfortable and inclusive experience.
5. Choose your preferred session topics by selecting "Yes" or "No" for the available options.
6. Confirm your role at the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Provide your contact number for easy communication.
8. If relevant, specify any additional session preferences or interests.
9. Enter your Job Title and Organization information for networking purposes.
10. Fill in your Company and Address details for official record-keeping.
11. Finally, verify your preferences and interests to ensure we have an accurate understanding of your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for updates and communication from the organizers.
- **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your interests among the available options. This helps us tailor the content and sessions to your needs.
- **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required: false): If you have any accessibility needs, please indicate them here to ensure a comfortable experience for you.
- **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred session topics to make the most of your time.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide your contact number for easy communication and follow-up.
- **Session Topics** (`session_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your interests among the session topics to help us create relevant content.
- **Role at Event** (`role_at_event`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your role at the event to help us understand your involvement.
- **Session Preferences 2** (`session_preferences_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Additional session preferences or interests.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter your Organization name for networking purposes.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title for professional networking.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your Company name for official record-keeping.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your Address for official purposes.
- **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required: false): Enter your secondary Address for official purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a complete registration.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Select only relevant session interests and preferences.
* Ensure you provide accurate information for official record-keeping.
* Review your preferences and interests to ensure they are correct.
* Contact us if you have any questions or need assistance.
