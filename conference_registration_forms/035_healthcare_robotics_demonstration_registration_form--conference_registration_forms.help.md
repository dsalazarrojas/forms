# Healthcare Robotics Demonstration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for conference attendees to register for the healthcare robotics demonstration. Please fill in the required fields to secure your spot.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or business card.
* **Attendee Email** (required): Enter your email address that you regularly check.
* **Attendee Phone** (optional): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up questions or last-minute updates.
* **Attendee Institution** (optional): If you're affiliated with an institution, enter its name.
* **Attendee Position** (optional): Enter your role or position within your institution.
* **Attendee Session Preferred** (optional): Select the sessions you're interested in attending, separated by commas (e.g., "Option 1, Option 2, Option 3").
* **Attendee Consent** (required): Confirm that you've read and understood the terms of the demonstration, including any data collection and usage policies.
* **Attendee Additional Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration or the demonstration.
* **Attendee Agreement** (required): Confirm that you've agreed to the terms of the demonstration, including data collection and usage policies.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting, as they will be used to tailor the demonstration experience to your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers for assistance.
