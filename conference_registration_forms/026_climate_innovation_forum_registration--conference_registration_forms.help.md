# Climate Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for the Climate Innovation Forum. The form helps the organizers to tailor the event to the attendees' needs and preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in all the required fields and select your preferences for the conference. You can complete the fields in any order, as the form structure is not strictly linear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect your name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Organization/Company** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect the name of your organization or company.
* **Registration Type** (`select_one`, required): This field is used to select the type of registration you are interested in (Individual, Corporate, Student, Early Bird, or VIP).
* **Attendance Format** (`select_one`, required): This field is used to select your preferred attendance format (In-person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Climate and Sustainability Experience** (`select_one`, required): This field is used to select your level of experience with climate and sustainability (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Industry** (`select_one`, optional): This field is used to select the industry you belong to (Technology, Energy, Agriculture, Transportation, Finance, Government, Nonprofit, or Education).
* **Job Title** (`text`, optional): This field is used to collect your job title or position within your organization.
* **Industry** (this field is duplicated, consider removing it or merging it with the previous field)
* **Conference Details** (`note`, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information about the conference you may want to share.
* **Preferred sessions to attend** (`select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to select all the sessions you are interested in attending (Keynote addresses, Breakout sessions, Workshops, Networking events, Exhibition hall, Panel discussions, or All sessions).
* **Topic interests** (`select_multiple`, required): This field is used to select all the topics you are interested in (Clean energy innovation, Carbon capture, Sustainable agriculture, Climate finance, Policy and regulation, Corporate sustainability, Climate justice, or Technology solutions).
* **Experience Level** (`note`, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information about your experience level.
* **Interested in speaking or presenting?** (`select_one`, optional): This field is used to select if you are interested in speaking or presenting at the conference.
* **Interested in one-on-one networking?** (`select_one`, optional): This field is used to select if you are interested in one-on-one networking at the conference.
* **Dietary requirements** (`text`, optional): This field is used to collect any dietary requirements you may have.
* **Accessibility needs** (`text`, optional): This field is used to collect any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Additional requests or information** (`text`, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information or requests you may have.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
- Select all the options that apply to you.
- Use the "Topic interests" and "Preferred sessions to attend" fields to help the organizers tailor the event to your needs.
- If you have any dietary requirements or accessibility needs, please make sure to fill in the corresponding fields.
- If you have any additional requests or information, please fill in the "Additional requests or information" field.
