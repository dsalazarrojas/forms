# Future Economy Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and companies for the Future Economy Event. The goal is to gather necessary details to organize and execute the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration information on the first page.
2. Enter the required details for your attendee, such as name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide your company's information, including name, position, and URL.
4. Select your country code and industry sector (if applicable).
5. Add any relevant notes or agreements.
6. Review and confirm your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information (Page 1)** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your registration information.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company.
* **Company Position** (`company_position`, text, required: false): Enter the position of the company representative.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the company.
* **Country Code** (`country_code`, select_one, required: false): Select the country code of the company's location.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the industry sector(s) of the company.
* **Attendee Notes** (`attendee_notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes about the attendee.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes about the event.
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, note, required: false): Add any notes about the company.
* **Attendee Agreement** (`attendee_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you agree with the attendee's terms and conditions.
* **Event Agreement** (`event_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you agree with the event's terms and conditions.
* **Company Agreement** (`company_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you agree with the company's terms and conditions.
* **Event Terms** (`event_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any terms or requirements for the event.
* **Company Terms** (`company_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any terms or requirements for the company.
* **Event Requirements** (`event_requirements`, text, required: false): Enter any requirements for the event.
* **Company Requirements** (`company_requirements`, text, required: false): Enter any requirements for the company.
* **Event Schedule** (`event_schedule`, time, required: false): Enter the schedule of the event.
* **Company Schedule** (`company_schedule`, time, required: false): Enter the schedule of the company.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter any assigned tool or equipment.

Note: Fields marked as required: false can be left blank if not applicable. However, please ensure that all required fields are filled for accurate and efficient processing.
