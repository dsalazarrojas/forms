# Healthy Snacking Expo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Snacking Expo Registration form is designed to collect attendee information and session interests for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee details, including your name and email address.
2. Select any dietary restrictions you may have (e.g., Gluten-free, Lactose-free, Nut-free).
3. Choose the sessions or topics that interest you the most (you can select multiple options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Details
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any conference materials or badges.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to send you updates and notifications about the conference.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please select the relevant options from the list. If you don't have any restrictions, you can choose "None".
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the sessions or topics that interest you the most by selecting the relevant options from the list. You can select multiple options.
