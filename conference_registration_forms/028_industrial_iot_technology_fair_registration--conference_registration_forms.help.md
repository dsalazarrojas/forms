# industrial_iot_technology_fair_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration details from individuals and organizations interested in attending the industrial IoT technology fair. This includes contact information, event specifics, and representative details if applicable.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select the relevant options for Industry Interests and How you heard about us.
3. Choose how you would like us to follow up with you.
4. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
5. Review the form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`2`, `text`, required): This is the field where you can enter your full name.
* **Company Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Please enter your company email address. This is required for verification purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Type in your company name.
* **Industry Interests** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all industries that interest you, including AI/Robotics, Data Analytics, Internet of Things, Automation, Energy Management, Cyber Security, and Other (please specify).
* **Other Interests (Optional)** (`7`, `text`, required): If your industry is not listed, you can specify other interests here.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select how you heard about the fair, such as Social Media, Word of Mouth, Online Search, Friend/Colleague, or Other (please specify).
* **How can we follow up with you?** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred method of follow up, which can be via Phone, Email, In Person, or Other (please specify).
* **Do you have any special requests or needs?** (`10`, `text`, required): If you have any specific requests or requirements, please mention them here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions by selecting "I accept the terms and conditions".
* **Consent** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent by selecting "Yes".
* **Any additional comments?** (`13`, `text`, required): If you have any further comments or concerns, please mention them here.
* **Number of Event Attendees** (`14`, `number`, required): Enter the number of attendees you will be bringing.
* **Event Date** (`15`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`16`, `time`, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type** (`19`, `text`, required): Type in the type of event you are attending.
* **Company/University Type** (`20`, `text`, required): Type in your company or university type.
* **Representative Name** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the representative.
* **Representative Email** (`22`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the representative.
* **Representative Phone** (`23`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the representative.
* **Number of Representatives** (`24`, `number`, required): Enter the number of representatives attending.
* **Additional Comments** (`25`, `text`, required): If you have any further comments, mention them here.

## Tips

- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any special requests or needs, mention them in the "Do you have any special requests or needs?" field.
- Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions and consent before submitting.
