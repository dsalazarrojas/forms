# Speaker Disclosure And Conflict Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from speakers for conference planning and organization purposes. It is essential that speakers fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential issues or conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill it out.
2.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are indicated by a red asterisk (\*) symbol. These fields are essential for speaker registration.
3.  For each field, provide accurate and complete information as required.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, please contact the conference organizers for clarification.
5.  Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the speaker's name.
* **Speaker Affiliation** (`speaker_affiliation`, text, required): This field is used to collect the speaker's affiliation or institution.
* **Speaker Institution** (`speaker_institution`, text, required): This field is used to collect the speaker's institution or organization name.
* **Financial Relationships** (`financial_relationships`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to collect information about any financial relationships the speaker may have with the conference or its sponsors. Select "Yes" if you have any financial relationships.
* **Conflict Of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to collect information about any conflicts of interest the speaker may have. Select "Yes" if you have any conflicts of interest.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, required): This field is used to collect a brief bio about the speaker.
* **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image`, text, required): This field is used to collect the speaker's image or photo.
* **Speaker Contact** (`speaker_contact`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the speaker's contact information, such as phone number and email.
* **Presentation Type** (`presentation_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect information about the type of presentation (e.g., keynote, breakout, etc.).
* **Presentation Topic** (`presentation_topic`, text, required): This field is used to collect the title or topic of the presentation.
* **Presentation Abstract** (`presentation_abstract`, text, required): This field is used to collect a brief abstract or description of the presentation.
* **Presentation Date and Time** (`presentation_date` and `presentation_time`, date and time, required): These fields are used to collect the date and time of the presentation.
* **Presentation Duration** (`presentation_duration`, number, optional): This field is used to collect the duration of the presentation in minutes.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect the speaker's phone number.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, required): This field is used to collect the speaker's email address.

Please note that some fields may have specific formatting requirements or have specific options available for selection. If you are unsure, please contact the conference organizers for clarification.
