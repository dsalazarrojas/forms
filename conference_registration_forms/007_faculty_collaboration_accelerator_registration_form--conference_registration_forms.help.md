# Faculty Collaboration Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from faculty members about their collaboration and meeting details. This information will be used to facilitate communication and collaboration among faculty members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided the correct information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)** (`General Info`, text, optional): Provide a brief introduction about your collaboration goals and expectations.
* **Collaboration Details (2)** (`Collaboration Details`, text, optional): Describe your collaboration project or meeting details.
* **Contact Info (3)** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Share your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Faculty Info (4)** (`Faculty Info`, text, optional): Provide more details about your faculty or organization.
* **Collaboration Programs (5)** (`Collaboration Programs`, multiple select, optional): Select the programs you are involved in.
* **Email (6)** (`Email`, text, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number (7)** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Faculty Program Manager (8)** (`Faculty Program Manager`, single select, optional): Select the manager of your faculty program.
* **Faculty Contact (9)** (`Faculty Contact`, text, optional): Share more contact information about your faculty.
* **Program Start Date (10)** (`Program Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date (11)** (`Program End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Meeting Location (12)** (`Meeting Location`, text, optional): Describe the location of your meeting.
* **Meeting Start Time (13)** (`Meeting Start Time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your meeting.
* **Meeting End Time (14)** (`Meeting End Time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (15)** (`Meeting Duration`, text, optional): Describe the duration of your meeting.
* **Total Participants (16)** (`Total Participants`, text, optional): Enter the total number of participants in your meeting.
* **Participant Info (17)** (`Participant Info`, text, optional): Share more information about the participants in your meeting.
* **Meeting Location Info (18)** (`Meeting Location Info`, text, optional): Describe more details about your meeting location.
* **Meeting Agenda (19)** (`Meeting Agenda`, text, optional): Share the agenda of your meeting.
* **Collaboration Details Info (20)** (`Collaboration Details Info`, text, optional): Provide more information about your collaboration project.
* **Faculty Collaboration Agreement (21)** (`Faculty Collaboration Agreement`, single select, optional): Select the agreement for your faculty collaboration.
* **Meeting Notes (22)** (`Meeting Notes`, text, optional): Share any additional meeting notes.
* **Meeting Summary (23)** (`Meeting Summary`, text, optional): Describe the summary of your meeting.
* **Next Meeting Date (24)** (`Next Meeting Date`, date, optional): Enter the next meeting date.
* **Next Meeting Time (25)** (`Next Meeting Time`, time, optional): Enter the next meeting time.
* **Meeting Frequency (26)** (`Meeting Frequency`, text, optional): Describe the frequency of your meetings.
* **Faculty Collaboration Level (27)** (`Faculty Collaboration Level`, text, optional): Describe the level of collaboration among faculty members.
* **Faculty Collaboration Status (28)** (`Faculty Collaboration Status`, text, optional): Provide the status of your faculty collaboration.
* **Faculty Collaboration Notes (29)** (`Faculty Collaboration Notes`, text, optional): Share any additional collaboration notes.
* **Submission (30)** (`Submission`, single select, optional): Select the submission option.

## Tips
* Double-check your form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to reach out.
* This form is designed to facilitate communication and collaboration among faculty members; please respect other users' information and boundaries.
