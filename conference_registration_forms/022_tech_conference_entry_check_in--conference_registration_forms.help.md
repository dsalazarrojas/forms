# Tech Conference Entry Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tech Conference Entry Check In" form is designed to collect information from conference attendees for check-in purposes. This form will help us track and manage the conference attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Conference Information" page with details about the conference you are attending. This includes the name of the conference and any other relevant information.
2. Move on to the "Attendee Information" page, where you can provide details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
3. Finally, provide your "Arrival Information" on the last page, including your planned arrival time and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Conference Information
* **Conference Information** (`<title>`, required): Enter the name of the conference you are attending.
* **Conference Information** (description, required): Provide any other relevant information about the conference.

### Attendee Information
* **Attendee Information** (`<name>`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the conference.
* **Attendee Information** (email, optional): Enter your email address for conference communication.

### Arrival Information
* **Arrival Information** (planned arrival time, optional): Let us know your expected arrival time at the conference.
* **Any additional information about your arrival** (text, optional): Provide any other relevant details about your arrival.
