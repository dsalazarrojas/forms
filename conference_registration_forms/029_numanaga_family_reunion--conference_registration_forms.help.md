# Numanaga Family Reunion - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Numanaga Family Reunion" form is for conference registration, gathering information from attendees to facilitate a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out each field as required or as you see fit.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Name**: The conference name you are attending. Enter the name of the conference you are interested in.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Family Name**: The name of your family. Enter the name of the family you are representing.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Email**: The email address of the person submitting the form. This will be used for communication purposes.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Optional
* **Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the form. This will be used for contact purposes.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
* **Checkin Date**: The date of check-in for the event. Enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Optional
* **Checkin Time**: The time of check-in for the event. Enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: Optional

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
