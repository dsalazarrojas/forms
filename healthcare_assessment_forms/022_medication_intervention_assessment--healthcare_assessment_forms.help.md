# Medication Intervention Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Intervention Assessment form is designed to collect information about a patient's current medication regimen and any potential changes or concerns related to their medication. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's medication status and identify potential issues that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's information in the fields below.
2. Answer the questions about the patient's current medication regimen.
3. If necessary, report any changes or concerns related to the patient's medication.
4. Provide any additional comments or follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.

### Medication Name
* **Medication Name** (`Medication Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medication the patient is currently taking.

### Dosage
* **Dosage** (`Dosage`, text, optional): Enter the dosage instructions for the patient's medication, including any specific instructions or frequency.

### Frequency
* **Frequency** (`Frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency at which the patient takes their medication.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient started taking their medication.

### Stop Date
* **Stop Date** (`Stop Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient stopped taking their medication.

### Medication Changes
* **Medication Changes** (`Medication Changes`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options: As needed, As prescribed, As directed, Other, None.

### Reason for Medication Changes
* **Reason for Medication Changes** (`Reason for Medication Changes`, text, optional): Report any reason for changes or concerns related to the patient's medication.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history or allergies the patient may have.

### Medication Allergies
* **Medication Allergies** (`Medication Allergies`, select_one, optional): Select from the options: Yes, No.

### Medication Side Effects
* **Medication Side Effects** (`Medication Side Effects`, text, optional): Report any side effects the patient has experienced with their current medication.

### Follow-Up Comments
* **Follow-Up Comments** (`Follow-Up Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or follow-up information for the healthcare professional.

### Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact the healthcare professional responsible for the patient's care.
