# Human Health And Family Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Health And Family Assessment form is designed to collect information on the health status and family history of an individual, as well as their lifestyle factors and consent status. This information is crucial for healthcare providers to make informed decisions about treatment planning and risk assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current health status from the options provided.
2. Choose your family history from the available options.
3. If you have any lifestyle factors that may impact your health, enter them in the text field.
4. Check the box to indicate your consent status.
5. Choose whether to submit or cancel the form.
6. If you are submitting the form, select the date and time of your current health status.
7. If necessary, enter any additional comments about your lifestyle factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health status** (`health_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your current health status from the options provided. This will help healthcare providers understand your overall health condition.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, select_one, required): Choose your family history from the options provided. This will help healthcare providers understand potential genetic or hereditary risks.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (`lifestyle_factors`, text, optional): If you have any lifestyle factors that may impact your health, enter them here. This can include habits such as smoking, diet, or exercise.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate your consent status. This is an important step in ensuring that you are aware of the information being collected.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to submit or cancel the form. If you are submitting the form, please select "Submit".
* **Health Status Date** (`health_status_date`, date, optional): If you are submitting the form, enter the date of your current health status.
* **Health Status Time** (`health_status_time`, time, optional): If you are submitting the form, enter the time of your current health status.
* **Lifestyle Factors Comments** (`lifestyle_factors_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments about your lifestyle factors, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields, as marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any of the options, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Take your time to carefully review the form before submitting.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact your healthcare provider for support.
