# health_data_collaboration_network_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from healthcare organizations, research organizations, and pharmaceutical companies for collaboration and data sharing in the healthcare sector. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your organization's name in the **organization_name** field.
2.  Select your organization type from the options in the **organization_type** field.
3.  Enter your organization's address in the **address** field (this is an optional field).
4.  Enter your contact number in the **contact_number** field. This is a required field.
5.  Select your health data interests from the options in the **Health Data Interests** field (this is a multiple choice field, select all that apply).
6.  Choose whether you have consent to share your health data in the **Consent** field.
7.  Enter any additional notes about your organization in the **Additional Notes** field (this is an optional field).
8.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field (this is a required field).
9.  (Optional) Upload health data related to your organization, and save it as an attachment or share a URL link for the file.
10.  Enter the date on which you granted consent to share your data in the **Consent Date** field.
11.  Enter the time on which you granted consent to share your data in the **Consent Time** field.
12.  Choose whether you want your organization's consent status to be **Yes** or **No** in the **Consent Status** field (this field is optional).
13.  Enter any consent-related notes in the **Consent Notes** field (this is an optional field).
14.  (Optional) Upload consent related documents, such as contracts or agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on all official documents.
*   **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent. Options include Healthcare Provider, Research Organization, and Pharmaceutical Company.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your organization's street address, including the city, state, and zip code.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your organization's contact number for further communication.
*   **Health Data Interests** (`Health Data Interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the health data interests that apply to your organization.
*   **Consent** (`Consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you have consent to share your health data.
*   **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your organization.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your organization's email address for further communication.
*   **Upload Health Data** (`Upload Health Data`, text, optional): (Optional) Upload health data related to your organization, and save it as an attachment or share a URL link for the file.
*   **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you granted consent to share your data.
*   **Consent Time** (`Consent Time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which you granted consent to share your data.
*   **Consent Status** (`Consent Status`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you want your organization's consent status to be **Yes** or **No**.
*   **Consent Notes** (`Consent Notes`, note, optional): Enter any consent-related notes.
*   **Upload Consent** (`Upload Consent`, text, optional): (Optional) Upload consent related documents, such as contracts or agreements.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your form.
*   **Back** (`Back`, text, optional): Use this button to go back to the previous page in case you want to change your input or need help with any field.
