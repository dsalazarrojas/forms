# Cryptocurrency Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cryptocurrency Eligibility Assessment is a form designed to evaluate an individual's suitability to use a cryptocurrency exchange. This assessment helps to ensure that users understand and comply with the regulatory requirements and risks associated with cryptocurrency trading. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and secure trading experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, taking into account your experience and knowledge regarding cryptocurrency trading.
2. Select the correct options for each question based on your understanding.
3. Fill in the text fields with your honest and accurate responses.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they reflect your genuine understanding and intentions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exchange Experience** (`exchange_experience`, text, optional): This question is meant to assess your experience in exchanging cryptocurrencies. Please provide a brief description of your exchange experience, including any relevant details about your trading activities.
* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, select_multiple, required): This question is designed to gauge your understanding of the risks associated with cryptocurrency trading. Please select all the risks you are aware of, such as market volatility, security threats, and regulatory changes.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, text, required): This question seeks to understand your investment experience in cryptocurrencies. Please describe your investment history, including any successful or unsuccessful trades.
* **Financial Knowledge** (`financial_knowledge`, text, required): This question aims to evaluate your understanding of financial concepts related to cryptocurrency trading, such as market analysis, risk management, and investment strategies.
* **Regulatory Compliance** (`regulatory_compliance`, text, optional): This question is meant to assess your knowledge of regulatory requirements and compliance in cryptocurrency trading. Please provide any relevant information about your understanding of regulations and compliance.
* **User Suitability** (`user_suitability`, select_one, required): This question determines whether you are suitable to use a cryptocurrency exchange based on your answers to the previous questions. Please select 'Yes' if you are suitable.
* **Advisor Relationship** (`advisor_relationship`, text, optional): This question is meant to assess whether you have an advisor or mentor guiding your cryptocurrency trading decisions. If you have an advisor, please provide their contact information.
* **Exchange Regulations** (`exchange_regulations`, text, optional): This question is designed to understand your knowledge of exchange regulations. Please provide any relevant information about your understanding of exchange regulations.
* **Legal Entity** (`legal_entity`, select_one, required): This question determines whether you are a legal entity (company, organization, etc.) or an individual. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Legal Entity Type** (`legal_entity_type`, select_one, optional): If you are a legal entity, please select the type of entity (e.g., company, organization, etc.).
* **Exchange Liquidity** (`exchange_liquidity`, number, optional): This question is meant to assess the liquidity of the exchange you plan to use. Please enter a numerical value indicating the exchange's liquidity.
* **Exchange Ownership** (`exchange_ownership`, select_one, optional): This question is designed to understand the ownership structure of the exchange you plan to use. Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, text, optional): This question is meant to assess your relationship with a financial institution. If you have a relationship with a financial institution, please provide their information.
* **Exchange Ownership Structure** (`exchange_ownership_structure`, select_one, optional): This question is meant to understand the ownership structure of the exchange you plan to use. Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Exchange Business Model** (`exchange_business_model`, text, optional): This question is designed to understand the business model of the exchange you plan to use. Please provide any relevant information about the exchange's business model.
* **Exchange Fees** (`exchange_fees`, number, optional): This question is meant to assess the fees associated with the exchange you plan to use. Please enter a numerical value indicating the fees.
* **Regulatory Environment** (`regulatory_environment`, select_multiple, optional): This question is designed to understand the regulatory environment of the exchange you plan to use. Please select all applicable options.
* **Exchange Compliance Program** (`exchange_compliance_program`, text, optional): This question is meant to assess the compliance program of the exchange you plan to use. Please provide any relevant information about the exchange's compliance program.
* **Exchange Security Measures** (`exchange_security_measures`, select_multiple, optional): This question is designed to understand the security measures of the exchange you plan to use. Please select all applicable options.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, note, optional): This question is meant to understand your agreement with the exchange regarding user terms and conditions. Please review and agree to the terms before proceeding.

Note: The questions that are not marked as "required" are optional, but please fill them out if you have relevant information to provide.
