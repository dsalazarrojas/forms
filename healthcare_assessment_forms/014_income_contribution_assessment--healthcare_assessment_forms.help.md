# Income Contribution Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Income Contribution Assessment form is designed to gather detailed information about an individual's income and financial situation. This information is likely used to determine eligibility for government programs or other forms of financial assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the following questions about your income and financial situation.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask your contact person for clarification.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Income** (`household_income`, number, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting your household's total income for the month/year.
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting your annual income. Please enter the total amount of your annual income.
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting your monthly income. Please enter the total amount of your monthly income.
* **Financial Support** (`financial_support`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting any financial assistance you receive from others, such as family members or government programs.
* **Other Income** (`other_income`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting any additional income you have, such as investments or self-employment income.
* **Expenses** (`expenses`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting your monthly expenses, such as rent, utilities, and food.
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for describing how often you receive income. Please choose from one of the options.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting where your income comes from (e.g., employment, investment, or benefits).
* **Financial Assistance** (`financial_assistance`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting any government programs or other forms of financial assistance you receive.
* **Income Verification** (`income_verification`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is for verifying your income. Please choose "Yes" if you can provide proof of income, or "No" if you cannot.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting your employment status. Please choose from one of the options.
* **Financial Assistance Source** (`financial_assistance_source`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting where your financial assistance comes from. Please choose from one of the options.
* **Annual Income Range** (`annual_income_range`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting the range of your annual income.
* **Monthly Income Range** (`monthly_income_range`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting the range of your monthly income.
* **Financial Support Type** (`financial_support_type`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for describing the type of financial support you receive.
* **Other Financial Support** (`other_financial_support`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting any other financial support you receive.
* **Income Frequency Description** (`income_frequency_description`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for describing how often you receive income.
* **Annual Income Frequency** (`annual_income_frequency`, time, required: false)
	+ This field is for reporting how often you receive income within a year.
* **Financial Support Type** (`financial_support_type`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for describing the type of financial support you receive.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask your contact person for clarification.
* Please fill out the form as accurately and honestly as possible to ensure that your information is up-to-date and secure.
