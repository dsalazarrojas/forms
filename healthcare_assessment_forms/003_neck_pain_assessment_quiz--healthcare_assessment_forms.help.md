# Neck Pain Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neck Pain Assessment Quiz is a form designed to collect information from patients about their neck pain experiences. This information will be used to identify potential causes of neck pain and develop a treatment plan tailored to the patient's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that apply to your situation.
3. For text fields, provide detailed information about your symptoms, medical history, and other relevant factors.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields. While some fields are optional, providing complete and accurate information will help healthcare professionals better understand your needs.
