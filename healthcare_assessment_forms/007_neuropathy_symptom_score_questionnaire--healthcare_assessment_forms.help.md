# Neuropathy Symptom Score Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neuropathy Symptom Score Questionnaire is designed to collect information on the severity of neuropathy symptoms. This form helps healthcare professionals assess and monitor the condition, enabling them to provide effective care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information as requested.
2. **Pain Score**: Please rate your pain level using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No pain
   * 1 = Mild pain
   * 2 = Moderate pain
   * 3 = Severe pain
3. **Numbness Score**: Rate your numbness level using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No numbness
   * 1 = Mild numbness
   * 2 = Moderate numbness
   * 3 = Complete numbness
4. **Tingling Score**: Rate your tingling sensations using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No tingling sensations
   * 1 = Mild tingling sensations
   * 2 = Moderate tingling sensations
   * 3 = Severe tingling sensations
5. **Burning Score**: Rate your burning sensations using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No burning sensations
   * 1 = Mild burning sensations
   * 2 = Moderate burning sensations
   * 3 = Severe burning sensations
6. **Weakness Score**: Rate your muscle weakness using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No weakness
   * 1 = Mild weakness
   * 2 = Moderate weakness
   * 3 = Severe weakness
7. **Electrical Shocks Score**: Rate your electrical shock sensations using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No electrical shocks
   * 1 = Mild electrical shocks
   * 2 = Moderate electrical shocks
   * 3 = Severe electrical shocks
8. **Fatigue Score**: Rate your fatigue level using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No fatigue
   * 1 = Mild fatigue
   * 2 = Moderate fatigue
   * 3 = Severe fatigue
9. **Heat/Cold Sensation Score**: Rate your heat/cold sensations using the following scale: 
   * 0 = No heat/cold sensations
   * 1 = Mild heat/cold sensations
   * 2 = Moderate heat/cold sensations
   * 3 = Severe heat/cold sensations
10. **Symptoms**: Choose all the symptoms you are experiencing: 
    * Numbness
    * Pain
    * Tingling
    * Burning
    * Weakness
    * Electrical Shocks
    * Fatigue
    * Heat/Cold Sensation

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section is for demographic information and contact details.
* **Pain Score**: This section assesses the severity of pain experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Numbness Score**: This section assesses the severity of numbness experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Tingling Score**: This section assesses the severity of tingling sensations experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Burning Score**: This section assesses the severity of burning sensations experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Weakness Score**: This section assesses the severity of muscle weakness experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Electrical Shocks Score**: This section assesses the severity of electrical shock sensations experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Fatigue Score**: This section assesses the severity of fatigue experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Heat/Cold Sensation Score**: This section assesses the severity of heat/cold sensations experienced due to neuropathy.
* **Symptoms**: This section allows you to choose all the symptoms you are experiencing due to neuropathy.
* **Numbness Score 2**, **Pain Score 2**, **Tingling Score 2**, **Burning Score 2**, **Weakness Score 2**, **Electrical Shocks Score 2**, **Fatigue Score 2**, and **Heat/Cold Sensation Score 2**: These sections are similar to their respective sections above, but are used to assess symptoms over time, such as changes or improvements in symptoms.
* **Symptoms**: This is a multiple-choice section where you can select all the symptoms you are experiencing due to neuropathy.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when rating your symptoms.
* If you experience any other symptoms not listed, please describe them in the comments section provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
