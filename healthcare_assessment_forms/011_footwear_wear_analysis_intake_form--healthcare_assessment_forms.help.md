# Footwear Wear Analysis Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Footwear Wear Analysis Intake Form is designed to gather information about a patient's foot health and footwear wear. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the condition of a patient's feet and shoes to provide personalized recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about your feet and shoes.
2. Report any pain or discomfort you're experiencing in your feet.
3. Select the areas of your feet where you have taken photos for further analysis.
4. Answer questions about your gait (walking style) and footwear usage.
5. Describe any shoe wear or conditions you've noticed.
6. Provide additional information about your footwear preferences.
7. Report any medical history or follow-up information.
8. Share any shoe fitting issues or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Please fill in your basic demographic information, such as your name, age, and contact details.
* **Pain Report**: Describe any pain or discomfort you're experiencing in your feet. This will help us understand your foot health and provide targeted guidance.
* **Photos**: Select all the areas of your feet where you'd like to share photos for further analysis. Please note that this is a multiple-choice question.
	+ Front Left Foot
	+ Back Left Foot
	+ Front Right Foot
	+ Back Right Foot
* **Gait Assessment**: Choose a number that best describes your walking style. 
* **Usage Information**: Indicate how often you wear your shoes, as this can affect wear and tear. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Occasional
	+ Rarely
* **Shoe Wear**: Describe any wear or conditions you've noticed on your shoes. This can include scuffing, heel wear, or other signs of excessive wear.
* **Other Information**: Share any additional information about your footwear or foot health that might be relevant to your condition.
* **Pain Level**: On a scale of 1-10, rate the level of pain you're experiencing in your feet.
* **Pain Duration**: Report how long you've been experiencing pain in your feet. Please note that this can affect the severity of your condition.
* **Footwear Preferences**: Indicate your preference for custom or stock shoes. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Custom
	+ Stock
	+ Other
* **Follow-Up Information**: Provide any additional information about your follow-up appointments or concerns.
* **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history that might impact your foot health.
* **Shoe Fitting Information**: Describe any issues or concerns you have with the fit of your shoes.
* **Shoe Wear (continued)**: Provide any additional comments or descriptions about your shoe wear or conditions. 

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when answering each question, as this will help us provide accurate and effective guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact our healthcare professionals for assistance.
* This form is for the purpose of analysis and will not be shared with any third parties without your consent.
