# Immunodeficiency Disorders Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients with immunodeficiency disorders for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email Address (required).
4. Provide your Phone Number.
5. Describe your medical condition.
6. Enter your Doctor's Name.
7. Enter your Doctor's Title.
8. Enter your Doctor's Clinic.
9. Enter your Date of Birth.
10. Enter your Doctor's Specialty.
11. Enter your Doctor's Degree.
12. Select all applicable Immunodeficiency Disorders (HIV, AIDS, Tuberculosis, Cancer) that you suffer from.
13. Describe your Symptoms.
14. Enter your Treatment Plan.
15. Enter the Follow Up Date and Time for your appointment.
16. Enter the Follow Up Location.
17. Describe your Medical History.
18. List any medications you are currently taking.
19. List any allergies you have.
20. Upload a medical image (if applicable).
21. Provide a Medical Report.
22. Enter any notes from your Doctor.
23. Enter any notes from you as a patient.
24. Finally, enter the Submission Date for the form.
