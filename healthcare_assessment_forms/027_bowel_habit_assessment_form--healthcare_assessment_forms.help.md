# Bowel Habit Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess your bowel habits, including your bowel movement frequency, stool consistency, straining during bowel movements, and any other symptoms you may be experiencing. It will help healthcare professionals understand your current bowel habits and identify any potential issues that may need medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your full legal name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the date you are taking this assessment.
4. Select your gender from the options provided.
5. Choose how often you have bowel movements from the options provided.
6. Indicate the most common consistency of your stool.
7. Answer questions about your straining during bowel movements, feeling of incomplete evacuation, bloating, and other symptoms.
8. List any medications you are currently taking.
9. Report any relevant medical conditions you have.
10. Provide information about your family history of bowel or digestive issues.
11. Answer questions about the duration of your symptoms.
12. Describe what seems to trigger your symptoms.
13. Indicate how much your symptoms affect your daily life.
14. If applicable, describe any previous treatments you have sought.
15. Lastly, state your goals for treatment.

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to provide a comprehensive assessment.
