# Minor Interaction Suitability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Interaction Suitability Assessment form is a tool used to assess the suitability of interactions between adults and minors. It helps healthcare professionals gather information about the interaction, including the date, time, location, risk level, and any safety precautions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Adult Minor Interaction to indicate whether the interaction was suitable.
2. Enter the date of the interaction in the Date Interaction Occurred field. Use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Enter the time of the interaction in the Time Interaction Occurred field. Use the 24-hour format.
4. Enter a brief description of the location of the interaction in the Location Interaction Occurred field.
5. Choose the risk level of the interaction from the options provided in the Risk Level field.
6. Enter any safety precautions taken during the interaction in the Safety Precautions Taken field.
7. Enter any notes or comments about the youth's response to the interaction in the Youth Response field.
8. Enter any notes or comments about the adult's response to the interaction in the Adult Response field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Adult Minor Interaction**: This field is used to indicate whether the interaction was suitable or not. Select 'Yes' for suitable and 'No' for unsuitable.
* **Date Interaction Occurred**: Enter the date of the interaction in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Interaction Occurred**: Enter the time of the interaction in the 24-hour format.
* **Location Interaction Occurred**: Enter a brief description of the location of the interaction.
* **Risk Level**: Choose the risk level of the interaction from the options provided. The options are:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ None
* **Safety Precautions Taken**: Enter any safety precautions taken during the interaction.
* **Youth Response**: Enter any notes or comments about the youth's response to the interaction.
* **Adult Response**: Enter any notes or comments about the adult's response to the interaction.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete each field thoroughly and accurately to ensure a thorough assessment.
* If a field is not applicable, enter 'N/A' or 'Not Applicable' to indicate that.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your organization's policies and procedures for guidance.
