# Population Density Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Population Density Assessment form is designed to assess the population density of a given area. This form is intended to help healthcare professionals or administrators evaluate the population density of a specific city or region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the city or region being assessed in the "City Name" field.
2. Enter the size of the area in square kilometers in the "Area Size (km2)" field.
3. Enter the population of the area in the "Population" field.
4. Enter the population density ratio of the area in the "Population Density Ratio" field.
5. Classify the population density of the area as 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Population Density Classification" field.
6. Provide any additional information or comments about the population density of the area in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the name of the person responsible for the assessment in the "Contact Person" field.
8. Enter the email of the person responsible for the assessment in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the assessment in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the name of the organization or institution associated with the assessment in the "Organization" field.
11. Enter the job title of the person responsible for the assessment in the "Job Title" field.
12. Enter the department of the person responsible for the assessment in the "Department" field.
13. Enter the address of the person responsible for the assessment in the "Address" field.
14. Enter the email of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
15. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
16. Enter the external phone number of the contact person in the "Ext. Phone" field.
17. Enter the external fax number of the contact person in the "Ext. Fax" field.
18. Enter the external email of the contact person in the "Ext. Email" field.
19. Enter the external address of the contact person in the "Ext. Address" field.
20. Enter the name of the external contact person in the "Ext. Contact Person" field.
21. Enter the department of the external contact person in the "Ext. Department" field.
22. Enter the job title of the external contact person in the "Ext. Job Title" field.
23. Enter the organization of the external contact person in the "Ext. Organization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **City Name** (`city_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the city or region being assessed.
* **Area Size (km2)** (`area_size_km2`, number, required): Enter the size of the area in square kilometers.
* **Population** (`population`, number, required): Enter the population of the area.
* **Population Density Ratio** (`population_density_ratio`, number, required): Enter the population density ratio of the area (number of people per square kilometer).
* **Density** (`density`, number, required): Enter the density of the area (number of people per square kilometer).
* **Population Density Classification** (`population_density_classification`, select_one, required): Classify the population density of the area as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the population density of the area.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the assessment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person responsible for the assessment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the assessment.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or institution associated with the assessment.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person responsible for the assessment.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department of the person responsible for the assessment.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person responsible for the assessment.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Ext. Phone** (`contact_ext_phone`, text, optional): Enter the external phone number of the contact person.
* **Ext. Fax** (`contact_ext_fax`, text, optional): Enter the external fax number of the contact person.
* **Ext. Email** (`contact_ext_email`, email, optional): Enter the external email of the contact person.
* **Ext. Address** (`contact_ext_address`, text, optional): Enter the external address of the contact person.
* **Ext. Contact Person** (`contact_ext_contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the external contact person.
* **Ext. Department** (`contact_ext_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the external contact person.
* **Ext. Job Title** (`contact_ext_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the external contact person.
* **Ext. Organization** (`contact_ext_organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization of the external contact person.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the person responsible for the assessment or the organization associated with the assessment.
- This form is intended to be used for assessment purposes only and should not be used for commercial purposes.
