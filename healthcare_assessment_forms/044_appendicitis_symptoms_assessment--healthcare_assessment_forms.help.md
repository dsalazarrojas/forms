# Appendicitis Symptoms Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess patients with suspected appendicitis. The form collects patient information, medical history, and physical exam findings to determine the likelihood of appendicitis and recommend further action.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Select the patient's gender.
3. Enter the assessment date and time.
4. Describe the patient's chief complaint (main reason for visit).
5. Assess the patient's pain and select the corresponding options.
6. Select the aggravating and relieving factors that affect the patient's pain.
7. Describe any associated symptoms (e.g., nausea, vomiting, etc.).
8. Select the patient's medical history, including previous episodes of appendicitis, abdominal surgeries, and current medications.
9. Enter the patient's physical exam findings, including rebound tenderness, guarding, McBurney point tenderness, and other relevant findings.
10. Determine the likelihood of appendicitis and select the corresponding option.
11. Select the recommended action and triage level.
12. Enter the healthcare provider's name and credentials.
13. Enter the assessment completion time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`patient_gender`): Select the patient's gender.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`): Describe the patient's main reason for visit.
* **Pain Assessment** (`pain_section`): Assess the patient's pain by selecting the corresponding options.
* **Pain Location** (`pain_location`): Select where the patient's pain is located.
* **Pain Onset** (`pain_onset`): Describe when the patient's pain started.
* **Pain Progression** (`pain_progression`): Select how the patient's pain has progressed.
* **Pain Severity** (`pain_severity`): Rate the patient's pain from 1 to 10.
* **Pain Character** (`pain_character`): Select the type of pain the patient is experiencing.
* **Pain Radiation** (`pain_radiation`): Determine if the pain radiates to other areas.
* **Aggravating Factors** (`aggravating_factors`): Select factors that make the patient's pain worse.
* **Relieving Factors** (`relieving_factors`): Select factors that make the patient's pain better.
* **Associated Symptoms** (`associated_symptoms_section`): Describe any associated symptoms (e.g., nausea, vomiting, etc.).
* **Nausea** (`nausea`): Determine if the patient has nausea.
* **Vomiting** (`vomiting`): Determine if the patient has been vomiting.
* **Vomit Description** (`vomitus_description`): Describe the patient's vomit (if applicable).
* **Appetite** (`appetite`): Determine the patient's appetite.
* **Fever** (`fever`): Determine if the patient has a fever.
* **Temperature Reading** (`temperature_reading`): Enter the patient's temperature reading (if measured).
* **Bowel Movement Changes** (`bowel_movements`): Determine if the patient has any bowel movement changes.
* **Urinary Symptoms** (`urinary_symptoms`): Determine if the patient has any urinary symptoms.
* **Abdominal Swelling** (`abdominal_swelling`): Determine if the patient has abdominal swelling.
* **Inability to Pass Gas** (`inability_to_pass_gas`): Determine if the patient can pass gas normally.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history_section`): Describe the patient's medical history, including previous episodes of appendicitis, abdominal surgeries, and current medications.
* **Previous Appendicitis** (`previous_appendicitis`): Determine if the patient has had appendicitis before.
* **Previous Abdominal Surgeries** (`previous_abdominal_surgeries`): Determine if the patient has had abdominal surgeries before.
* **Surgery Details** (`surgery_details`): Describe the patient's surgical history (if yes).
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`): Enter the patient's current medications.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`): Enter the patient's medication allergies.
* **Chronic Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`): Describe the patient's chronic medical conditions.
* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`): Determine if the patient could be pregnant.
* **Menstrual History** (`menstrual_history`): Enter the patient's last menstrual period (if applicable).
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs_section`): Enter the patient's vital signs (if measured).
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`): Enter the patient's blood pressure reading (if measured).
* **Heart Rate** (`heart_rate`): Enter the patient's heart rate (if measured).
* **Respiratory Rate** (`respiratory_rate`): Enter the patient's respiratory rate (if measured).
* **Oxygen Saturation** (`oxygen_saturation`): Enter the patient's oxygen saturation reading (if measured).
* **Physical Exam Findings** (`physical_exam_section`): Enter the patient's physical exam findings, including rebound tenderness, guarding, McBurney point tenderness, etc.
* **Rebound Tenderness** (`rebound_tenderness`): Determine if the patient has rebound tenderness.
* **Guarding** (`guarding`): Determine if the patient has guarding.
* **McBurney Point Tenderness** (`mcburney_point`): Determine if the patient has McBurney point tenderness.
* **Rovsing Sign** (`rovsign`): Determine if the patient has Rovsing sign.
* **Psoas Sign** (`psoas_sign`): Determine if the patient has Psoas sign.
* **Obturator Sign** (`obturator_sign`): Determine if the patient has Obturator sign.
* **Likelihood of Appendicitis** (`appendicitis_likelihood`): Determine the likelihood of appendicitis.
* **Differential Diagnosis** (`differential_diagnosis`): Describe other possible causes of the patient's symptoms.
* **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`): Select the recommended action for the patient.
* **Triage Level** (`triage_level`): Determine the patient's triage level.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Assessor Credentials** (`assessor_credentials`): Enter the healthcare provider's credentials.
* **Assessment Completion Time** (`assessment_complete_time`): Enter the time completed.

## Tips

* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Seek further guidance from a healthcare provider if unsure about any information.
* This form is for suspected cases of appendicitis only. If the patient's symptoms do not suggest appendicitis, refer the patient to the appropriate specialist.
