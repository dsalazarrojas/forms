# Safety Symbols Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Symbols Assessment form is designed to collect information on participants' perception of safety and risk levels regarding certain hazards or risks. The form helps to gather data on the level of confidence or uncertainty participants have regarding these hazards and their impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully to provide a clear understanding of your perception.
2. Use the provided dropdown menus to select your answers for the select_one type questions.
3. Enter numbers in the required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter any additional information about yourself.
* **hazard_icon_1** (`hazard_icon_1`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you perceive this hazard as having a high risk, or 'No' if you do not.
* **confidence_level_1** (`confidence_level_1`, number, required: false): Enter your confidence level regarding this hazard. Please note that the numbers represent the level of confidence you have in your assessment.
* **confidence_level_1_hazard_1** (`confidence_level_1_hazard_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the level of confidence you have regarding this hazard (High or Low).
* **question_1** (`question_1`, note, required: false): Enter any additional thoughts or questions you have about this hazard.
* **question_2** (`question_2`, note, required: false): Enter any additional thoughts or questions you have about this hazard.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Enter any additional feedback or comments you have about this hazard.
* **hazard_icon_2** (`hazard_icon_2`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you perceive this hazard as having a high risk, or 'No' if you do not.
* **confidence_level_2** (`confidence_level_2`, number, required: false): Enter your confidence level regarding this hazard.
* **confidence_level_2_hazard_1** (`confidence_level_2_hazard_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the level of confidence you have regarding this hazard (High or Low).

## Tips
* If you are unsure about a particular question, please select 'No' or provide a note with your thoughts.
* Please ensure that you are filling out the correct form and not duplicating your answers.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the project team for assistance.
