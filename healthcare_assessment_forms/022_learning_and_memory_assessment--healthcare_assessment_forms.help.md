# learning_and_memory_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your background, learning, and memory abilities. Please answer the questions truthfully to help us better understand your needs and provide you with the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to select all applicable options for each question.
3. Provide detailed descriptions for the self-rating questions.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, please write them down in the background questions section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background**: This is a note field where you can provide any relevant background information.
* **Self-ratings**: Rate your current cognitive status as "Good" or "Fair".
* **Consent**: Confirm that you have read and understood the instructions and that you are consenting to participate in the assessment. Choose "Yes" or "No".
* **What is your highest level of education completed?**: Select your highest level of education completed from the options: "None", "High School", "Some college", or "College".
* **Do you have any medical conditions that could impact your learning and memory?**: Select all conditions that apply from the options "True" or "False".
* **What are your current occupation or employment status?**: Select your current occupation or employment status from the options: "Employed", "Unemployed", "Retired", or "Other".
* **Cognitive status**: Rate your current cognitive status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Self-rating 1 description**: Provide a description of your self-rating 1.
* **Self-rating 2**: Rate your current cognitive status as "Good", "Fair", or leave blank.
* **Self-rating 2 description**: Provide a description of your self-rating 2.
* **Self-rating 3**: Rate your current cognitive status as "Good", "Fair", or leave blank.
* **Self-rating 3 description**: Provide a description of your self-rating 3.
* **Do you have any learning or memory concerns or issues you'd like to address?**: Write down any concerns or issues you'd like to address.
* **How do you think you might benefit from this assessment?**: Write down how you think you might benefit from this assessment.
* **Do you have any questions or concerns about the process?**: Write down any questions or concerns you have about the assessment process.
* **Are there any specific time constraints you have for this assessment?**: Write down any specific time constraints you have for the assessment.
* **Do you have any other questions or concerns about this assessment?**: Write down any other questions or concerns you have.
* **Do you have any other medical conditions that might impact your participation in this assessment?**: Write down any other medical conditions that might impact your participation.
* **Do you have any concerns about your privacy?**: Confirm that you have read and understood the instructions and that you are consenting to participate in the assessment.
* **Do you have a preferred time for the assessment?**: Select your preferred time for the assessment from the options "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening", or "Other".
* **Do you have a preferred location for the assessment?**: Select your preferred location for the assessment from the options "Clinic", "Home", or "Other".
* **How will you be traveling to the assessment?**: Select how you will be traveling to the assessment from the options "Drive", "Public transportation", or "Other".
* **Do you have any concerns about your internet connectivity?**: Confirm that you have no concerns about your internet connectivity.
* **Do you have any other concerns?**: Write down any other concerns you have.
