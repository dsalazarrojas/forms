# Substance Use Stages Of Change Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Substance Use Stages Of Change Assessment form is designed to assess the stages of change in individuals with substance use issues. This form is intended to collect data on the individual's progress in addressing their substance use problems.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each section.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Form Info**: This field is used to gather demographic information about the individual, such as their name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Substance Use Assessment**: This section is used to assess the individual's substance use behavior, including the types of substances used, frequency of use, and any other relevant information.
* **Readiness For Change**: This field is used to determine the individual's willingness to change their substance use behavior.
* **Stages Of Change**: This section is used to assess the individual's stage of change in addressing their substance use issues.
* **Readiness For Treatment**: This field is used to determine the individual's readiness to receive treatment for their substance use issues.
* **Treatment Planning**: This section is used to develop a plan for the individual's treatment.
* **Treatment Services**: This field is used to identify the treatment services the individual will receive.
* **Follow-up Counseling**: This section is used to schedule follow-up counseling sessions for the individual.
* **Follow-up Counseling Frequency**: This field is used to determine how often the individual will receive follow-up counseling.
* **Follow-up Counseling Duration**: This section is used to determine the length of each follow-up counseling session.
* **Treatment Outcome**: This field is used to assess the individual's progress in treatment.
* **Follow-up Counseling Notes**: This section is used to record notes from follow-up counseling sessions.
* **Form Completion**: This field is used to confirm that the form has been completed.

Note: The following sections are duplicates of the original form:
* **Intake Form Info 2**: This field is a duplicate of the original intake form information.
* **Treatment Goals 2**: This field is a duplicate of the original treatment goals.
* **Treatment Outcome 2**: This field is a duplicate of the original treatment outcome.
* **Follow-up Counseling Notes 2**: This field is a duplicate of the original follow-up counseling notes.

Please note that these duplicates are included to allow for the collection of additional information and to ensure that all relevant data is gathered.
