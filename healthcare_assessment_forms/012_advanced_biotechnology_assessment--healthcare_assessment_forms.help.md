> # Advanced Biotechnology Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Advanced Biotechnology Assessment is used to evaluate a candidate's expertise and skills in various areas of biotechnology. This form is designed for healthcare professionals to assess a candidate's qualifications and experiences in biotechnology.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by entering your information in the corresponding fields.
2. Each field is labeled with its corresponding name and label.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully and answer accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Professional Profile
**Professional Name** (`Professional Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
**Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Describe your current position in the field of biotechnology.
**Organization** (`Organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your current organization.

### Biotechnology Expertise
**Primary Specialty Area** (`Primary Specialty Area`, select_one, required): Choose your primary area of expertise in biotechnology from the options:
	* Pharmaceuticals
	* Agriculture
	* Environmental
	* Industrial
	* Medical Devices
	* Diagnostics
	* Genomics
	* Synthetic Biology
	* Other
**Specialty Areas of Expertise** (`Specialty Areas of Expertise`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of biotechnology where you have expertise:
	* Cell culture
	* Protein purification
	* Molecular cloning
	* PCR techniques
	* Bioinformatics
	* Fermentation
	* Downstream processing
	* Quality assurance
	* Regulatory affairs
**Laboratory Techniques Proficiency** (`Laboratory Techniques Proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency level in advanced laboratory techniques:
	* Beginner
	* Intermediate
	* Advanced
	* Expert

### Regulatory Compliance
**FDA Regulations Knowledge** (`FDA Regulations Knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose how familiar you are with FDA regulations for biotechnology products:
	* Very familiar
	* Moderately familiar
	* Slightly familiar
	* Not familiar
**GMP Compliance Experience** (`GMP Compliance Experience`, select_one, required): Select your experience with Good Manufacturing Practices:
	* Extensive experience
	* Some experience
	* Training only
	* No experience
**Intellectual Property Awareness** (`Intellectual Property Awareness`, select_one, required): Choose how familiar you are with IP issues in biotechnology:
	* Very familiar
	* Moderately familiar
	* Slightly familiar
	* Not familiar

### Research and Development
**R&D Project Leadership** (`R&D Project Leadership`, select_one, required): Select your experience with leading biotechnology research and development projects:
	* Yes, multiple projects
	* Yes, one project
	* Assisted on projects
	* No leadership experience
**Funding Acquisition Experience** (`Funding Acquisition Experience`, select_one, required): Choose how you have secured funding for biotechnology research:
	* Federal grants
	* Private investors
	* Corporate funding
	* Venture capital
	* No experience
	* Other
**Publication Record** (`Publication Record`, select_one, required): Select your number of peer-reviewed publications in biotechnology:
	* None
	* 1-5
	* 6-10
	* 11-20
	* More than 20

### Challenges and Opportunities
**Biggest Challenge** (`Biggest Challenge`, text, optional): Describe the biggest challenge you see facing biotechnology today.
**Emerging Opportunity** (`Emerging Opportunity`, text, optional): Describe the emerging opportunity that excites you most in biotechnology.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about a field, please ask your contact person for clarification.
- This form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals. If you are not a healthcare professional, please do not complete this form.
