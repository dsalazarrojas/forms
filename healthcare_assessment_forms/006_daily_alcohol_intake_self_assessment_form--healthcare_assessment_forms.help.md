# Daily Alcohol Intake Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand your current alcohol intake habits and potential health risks associated with your consumption.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your alcohol intake habits.
3. If you have any other information that may be relevant to your responses, please use the "Other" field to provide additional details.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Questionnaire**: Please provide a brief description of your alcohol intake habits.
* **Alcohol consumption today**: Please report the amount of alcohol consumed today.
* **Alcohol consumption yesterday**: Please report the amount of alcohol consumed yesterday.
* **How much drinking**: Please report the amount of alcohol consumed in the past week.
* **Drinking frequency**: Please select the frequency of your drinking habits:
	+ Beer
	+ Wine
	+ Spirits
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Drinking frequency other**: If your drinking frequency is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Reason for drinking**: Please select the reasons why you drink:
	+ To relax
	+ To socialize
	+ To celebrate
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Reason for drinking other**: If your reason for drinking is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Health risk factors**: Please select the level of health risk associated with your drinking habits:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Health risk factors other**: If your health risk factors are not listed above, please describe them in this field.
* **Blood pressure**: Please select the current status of your blood pressure:
	+ Hypertension
	+ Normal
	+ None
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Blood pressure other**: If your blood pressure is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Alcohol consumption frequency**: Please select the frequency of your alcohol consumption:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Alcohol consumption frequency other**: If your alcohol consumption frequency is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Have you always consumed alcohol?**: Please select the frequency of your alcohol consumption in the past:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
* **Have you always consumed alcohol other**: If your alcohol consumption frequency in the past is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Do you feel guilty about your consumption?**: Please select the frequency of your guilt feelings:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
* **Do you feel guilty about your consumption other**: If your guilt feelings are not listed above, please describe them in this field.
* **Do you feel guilty about your consumption frequency?**: Please select the frequency of your guilt feelings about your consumption:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Do you feel guilty about your consumption frequency other**: If your guilt feelings about your consumption frequency are not listed above, please describe them in this field.
* **Have you also consumed others?**: Please select the frequency of alcohol consumption by others:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Rarely
* **Have you also consumed others other**: If your alcohol consumption by others is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Have you also consumed others frequency**: Please select the frequency of alcohol consumption by others:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Have you also consumed others frequency other**: If your alcohol consumption by others is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
* **Have you also consumed others frequency other 2**: If your alcohol consumption by others frequency is not listed above, please describe it in this field.
