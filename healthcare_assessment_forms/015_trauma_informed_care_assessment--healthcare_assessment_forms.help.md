# Trauma Informed Care Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma Informed Care Assessment form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of trauma-informed care practices in a healthcare setting. This form helps assess the level of awareness, understanding, staff engagement, resources and support, policies and procedures, staff retention, and evaluation scale of the care practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and the expected responses.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, based on your knowledge and experience.
3. For each field, select the appropriate response option that best reflects your understanding and experience.
4. Enter any additional comments or details in the text fields as necessary.
5. Ensure that you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: This page is an introduction to the form and explains the purpose and importance of trauma-informed care practices.
* **Trauma Informed Care Practice (Page 2)**: Please select the level of priority for trauma-informed care practices in your organization (High Priority, Medium Priority, Low Priority).
* **Awareness and Understanding (Page 3)**: Please select how much awareness and understanding of trauma-informed care practices there is in your organization (High Priority, Medium Priority, Low Priority).
* **Staff Engagement (Page 4)**: Please select the level of engagement from staff in trauma-informed care practices (High, Medium, Low, None).
* **Resources and Support (Page 5)**: Please enter any additional comments or details about resources and support provided for trauma-informed care practices.
* **Policies and Procedures (Page 6)**: Please select the effectiveness of policies and procedures for trauma-informed care practices (Effective, Partially Effective, Not at All).
* **Staff Retention (Page 7)**: Please select the impact of trauma-informed care practices on staff retention (Increase, Maintain, Decrease).
* **Evaluation Scale (Page 8)**: Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to evaluate the effectiveness of trauma-informed care practices.
* **Review By (Page 9)**: Please enter any additional comments or details about who reviewed or evaluated the trauma-informed care practices.
* **Date Submitted (Page 10)**: Please enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Date Completed (Page 11)**: Please enter the date the form was completed.
* **Date Reviewed (Page 12)**: Please enter the date the trauma-informed care practices were reviewed.
* **Submitted By (Page 13)**: Please enter your name and any additional comments or details about who submitted the form.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the option that best reflects your understanding and experience.
* If you have any additional comments or details, please enter them in the text fields as necessary.
