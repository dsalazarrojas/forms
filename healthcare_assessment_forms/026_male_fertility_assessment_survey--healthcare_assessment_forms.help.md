# male_fertility_assessment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist users in completing the "male_fertility_assessment_survey" form, which is designed to collect information about male fertility. The purpose of this form is to gather data on various aspects of male reproductive health, including demographics, lifestyle, family history, medical history, reproductive system, medications, social lifestyle, reproductive lifestyle, male fertility questionnaire, reproductive health, and test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form sections and understand what each section is asking for.
2.  Answer each question in the relevant section, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields, if any.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
