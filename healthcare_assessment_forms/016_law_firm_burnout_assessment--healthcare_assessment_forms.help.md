# Law Firm Burnout Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Firm Burnout Assessment is designed to help law firms evaluate their burnout risk levels and identify areas for improvement. It assesses burnout risk factors, symptoms, and prevention strategies to support employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The assessment consists of multiple pages, each focusing on a specific aspect of burnout risk and management.
2.  Please answer all questions honestly, as your responses will help us understand your firm's burnout risk levels.
3.  For each question, select the most appropriate response from the provided options or type a brief answer in the text fields.
4.  You can skip questions that do not apply to your firm or your role.
5.  Take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete the assessment.
