# Kidney Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the kidney health of patients. It collects information on demographics, medical history, symptoms, medical tests, kidney function, and disease status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Name**, their **Contact Number**, and **Email** (if applicable).
2. Describe any **Symptoms** the patient is experiencing.
3. Provide details on the patient's **Medical History**.
4. List any **Lifestyle Details** that are relevant to the patient's health.
5. Describe any **Family History** that may be relevant to the patient's kidney health.
6. List any **Medical Conditions** the patient has or is suspected to have.
7. List any **Medications** the patient is currently taking.
8. Describe any **Allergies** the patient has.
9. Select any **Medical Tests** that have been performed or are planned.
10. Provide details on the **Medical Results** of any medical tests.
11. Indicate the **Kidney Disease Status**, choosing from the options: Healthy, Borderline Kidney Disease, Chronic Kidney Disease, End-Stage Kidney Disease, or Other (Specify).
12. If the kidney disease status is "Other (Specify)", provide details in the **Kidney Disease Status Other** field.
13. Select the patient's **Kidney Function**, choosing from the options: Good, Reduced, Abnormal, or Other (Specify).
14. If the kidney function is "Other (Specify)", provide details in the **Kidney Function Other** field.
15. Schedule a **Follow-up** appointment or assessment date in the date field.
16. Specify the time of the scheduled **Follow-up** appointment in the time field.

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.

* **Medical History**: Provide details on the patient's medical history.

* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions the patient has or is suspected to have.

* **Allergies**: Describe any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Tests**: Select any medical tests that have been performed or are planned.
* **Medical Results**: Provide details on the medical results of any tests.
* **Kidney Disease Status**: Indicate the patient's kidney disease status, choosing from the options: Healthy, Borderline Kidney Disease, Chronic Kidney Disease, End-Stage Kidney Disease, or Other (Specify).
* **Kidney Disease Status Other**: If the kidney disease status is "Other (Specify)", provide details on the disease status.
* **Kidney Function**: Select the patient's kidney function, choosing from the options: Good, Reduced, Abnormal, or Other (Specify).
* **Kidney Function Other**: If the kidney function is "Other (Specify)", provide details on the kidney function.
