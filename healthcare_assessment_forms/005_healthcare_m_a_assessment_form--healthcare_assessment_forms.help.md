## Healthcare M&A Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is for healthcare professionals who need to fill out the Healthcare M&A Assessment Form. This form is designed to help identify and assess potential risks and opportunities for mergers and acquisitions (M&A) in the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the acquisition target.
2. Answer all questions clearly and concisely, using the provided space to elaborate on your responses if necessary.
3. Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
