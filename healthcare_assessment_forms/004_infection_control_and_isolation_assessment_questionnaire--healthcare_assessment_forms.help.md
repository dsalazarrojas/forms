# Infection Control And Isolation Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control And Isolation Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used to assess an individual's risk of infection and isolation practices in a healthcare setting. This form will help healthcare professionals to identify patients who are at high risk of infection and isolation, assess the effectiveness of infection control practices, and identify potential areas for improvement in infection control measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the patient's identification details in the "Patient Identification" field.
2. Describe the patient's current infection control practice in the "Infection Control Practice" field.
3. Identify any risk factors that the patient has that may affect their risk of infection in the "Risk Factor" field.
4. Describe the patient's contact precautions in the "Contact Precautions" field.
5. Describe the mode of transmission that may affect the patient's risk of infection in the "Transmission" field.
6. Describe the measures taken to control infection in the "Infection Control Measures" field.
7. Describe the patient's current isolation status in the "Isolation" field.
8. Enter the review frequency for the patient's infection control and isolation practices in the "Review Frequency" field.
9. Describe the contact trace status for the patient in the "Contact Trace" field.
10. Enter the review status for the patient's infection control and isolation practices in the "Review Status" field.
11. Enter any comments or additional information about the patient's infection control and isolation practices in the "Review Comments" field.
12. Enter the contact trace status for the patient in the "Contact Trace Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the patient's identification details such as name, date of birth, and medical record number.
* **Infection Control Practice** (`infection_control_practice`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the patient's current infection control practice.
* **Risk Factor** (`risk_factor`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to identify any risk factors that the patient has that may affect their risk of infection.
* **Contact Precautions** (`contact_precautions`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the patient's contact precautions.
* **Transmission** (`transmission`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the mode of transmission that may affect the patient's risk of infection.
* **Infection Control Measures** (`infection_control_measures`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the measures taken to control infection.
* **Isolation** (`isolation`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the patient's current isolation status.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the review frequency for the patient's infection control and isolation practices.
* **Contact Trace** (`contact_trace`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe the contact trace status for the patient.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the review status for the patient's infection control and isolation practices.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any comments or additional information about the patient's infection control and isolation practices.
* **Contact Trace Status** (`contact_trace_status`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the contact trace status for the patient.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the patient's details are accurate and up-to-date.
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Please describe each field clearly and concisely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant healthcare professional.
* Please review and update the patient's data regularly to ensure that their risk of infection and isolation practices are properly assessed and improved.
