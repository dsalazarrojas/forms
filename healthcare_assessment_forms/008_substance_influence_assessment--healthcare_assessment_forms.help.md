# Substance Influence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the influence of a substance on an individual's behavior or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Incident Details**
	* Fill in the Incident Details section with a description of the incident where the substance is suspected to have played a role.
	* This could include information about the context, setting, and any other relevant circumstances surrounding the incident.
2. **Select Incident Date**
	* Choose the date of the incident from the calendar picker in the Incident Date field.
3. **Assess Substance Involvement**
	* From the Substance Involvement dropdown list, select all substances that are suspected to have contributed to the incident.
	* This could include Amphetamine, Caffeine, Cocaine, Heroin, LSD, Opiate, or THC.
4. **Enter Assessor Details**
	* In the Assessor Details section, provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and role.
	* This will help in understanding the context of the assessment.
5. **Document Observations and Notes**
	* In the Incident Observations section, provide a detailed account of what was observed during the incident.
	* In the Incident Notes section, provide any additional information that may be relevant to the assessment.
6. **Provide Dose Information**
	* If you have information about the amount or dosage of the substance involved, please enter it in the Substance Doses field.
7. **Assessor Rating**
	* Provide a rating of the substance's influence on the individual's behavior or actions, using a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
8. **Final Notes**
	* Use the Incident Notes field to provide any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: Provide a description of the incident where the substance is suspected to have played a role.
* **Incident Date**: Choose the date of the incident from the calendar picker.
* **Substance Involvement**: Select all substances that are suspected to have contributed to the incident.
* **Assessor Details**: Provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and role.
* **Substance Doses**: If available, enter the amount or dosage of the substance involved.
* **Incident Observations**: Provide a detailed account of what was observed during the incident.
* **Assessor Rating**: Provide a rating of the substance's influence on the individual's behavior or actions, using a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Incident Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the assessment.
