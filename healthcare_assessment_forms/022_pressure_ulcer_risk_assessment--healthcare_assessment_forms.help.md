# Pressure Ulcer Risk Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the risk of pressure ulcers in patients. It helps healthcare professionals identify patients at risk and implement preventive measures to reduce the likelihood of pressure ulcer development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the **Patient Info** section, including their ID number, name, and any other relevant details.
2. In the **Pressure Areas** section, list any areas on the patient's body where pressure ulcers are likely to develop or have developed.
3. Evaluate the patient's pain score by selecting the appropriate level of pain from the options provided in the **Pain Score** section.
4. Record any observations or relevant information about the patient's condition in the **Observations** section (optional).
5. Determine the patient's risk score by selecting the appropriate level of risk from the options provided in the **Risk Score** section.
6. Indicate whether the patient is at risk of developing pressure ulcers by answering the question in the **Pressure Ulcer Risk Assessment Form** section.
7. Identify any care targets or interventions necessary to mitigate the patient's risk in the **Care Targets** section (optional).
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition in the **Notes** section (optional).
9. Enter your name and title in the **Submitted By** section (optional).
10. Enter the date and time of submission in the **Date Submitted** section (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's ID number, name, and any other relevant details.
* **Pressure Areas**: List any areas on the patient's body where pressure ulcers are likely to develop or have developed.
* **Pain Score**: Evaluate the patient's pain score by selecting the appropriate level of pain from the options provided:
	+ 0-1
	+ 1-2
	+ 3
	+ 4-5
	+ 6-8
	+ 9-10
	+ > 10
* **Observations**: Record any observations or relevant information about the patient's condition (optional).
* **Risk Score**: Determine the patient's risk score by selecting the appropriate level of risk from the options provided:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
* **Pressure Ulcer Risk Assessment Form**: Indicate whether the patient is at risk of developing pressure ulcers by answering "Yes" or "No".
* **Care Targets**: Identify any care targets or interventions necessary to mitigate the patient's risk (optional).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name and title (optional).
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date and time of submission (optional).
