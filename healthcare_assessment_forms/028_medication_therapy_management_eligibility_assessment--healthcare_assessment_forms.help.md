# Medication Therapy Management Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Therapy Management Eligibility Assessment form is designed to help healthcare providers assess a patient's eligibility for MTM. This form gathers information about the patient's medication history, medical record, and other relevant details to support a collaborative and informed decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select One or More Medications**: Choose the medications the patient is currently taking or considering taking.
2. **Enter Patient Details**: Provide the patient's name, date of birth, medical record number, and address.
3. **Add Medical Record Notes**: Enter any relevant medical history or notes about the patient.
4. **Enter Medication List**: List the medications the patient is currently taking or considering taking.
5. **Enter Pharmacy Details**: Enter the name of the pharmacy the patient visits.
6. **Submitter's Information**: Enter your name, email, phone number, job title, organization, department, and date and time of submission.
7. **Review and Submit**: Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medication Therapy Management Eligibility Assessment Form**: This is the main form where you will enter the patient's medication history and other relevant details. (Required)
* **Patient Details**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, medical record number, and address. (Required)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name. (Required)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY). (Required)
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number. (Required)
* **Address**: Enter the patient's current address. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address. (Optional)
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who referred the patient. (Optional)
* **Medication List**: List the medications the patient is currently taking or considering taking. (Required)
* **Medical Record Notes**: Enter any relevant medical history or notes about the patient. (Optional)
* **Pharmacy Name**: Enter the name of the pharmacy the patient visits. (Optional)
* **Submitter's Name**: Enter your name as the individual submitting the form. (Required)
* **Submitter's E-mail**: Enter your email address as the individual submitting the form. (Required)
* **Submitter's Phone**: Enter your phone number as the individual submitting the form. (Required)
* **Submitter's Job Title**: Enter your job title as the individual submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Submitter's Organization**: Enter your organization as the individual submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Submitter's Department**: Enter your department as the individual submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Submitter's Date**: Enter the date and time of submission. (Required)
* **Submitter's Time**: Enter the time of submission. (Required)
* **Submitter's IP**: Enter your IP address as the individual submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Submitter's User Agent**: Enter your user agent as the individual submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Created At**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was created. (Required)
* **Updated At**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was last updated. (Required)

Note: Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access the patient's medical record before completing this form.
