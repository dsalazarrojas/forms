# Emotional Distance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Distance Assessment form is designed to evaluate the emotional connection between a patient and their caregivers or family members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields (First name, Last name, Email, Age, and Relationship) accurately and completely.
2. If the patient has a date of birth, please provide it, but it's not mandatory.
3. Select one or more options to describe the patient's emotional distance from their caregivers or family members (Emotional, Close, Medium, or Far).
4. Specify the relationship of the person being assessed (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Child, or Spouse).
5. Provide any additional comments about the patient's emotional distance or relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, **Required**): Enter the patient's first name accurately.
* **Last name** (2, text, **Required**): Enter the patient's last name accurately.
* **Email** (3, text, **Required**): Enter the patient's email address accurately.
* **Age** (4, number, **Required**): Enter the patient's age accurately.
* **Date of birth** (5, date, **Optional**): If available, provide the patient's date of birth.
* **Emotional distance** (6, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose one or more options that best describe the patient's emotional connection with their caregivers or family members:
	+ Emotional: Very close and loving.
	+ Close: Somewhat close but with some distance.
	+ Medium: Moderate emotional connection.
	+ Far: Somewhat distant.
* **Relationship** (7, select_one, **Required**): Choose the relationship of the person being assessed (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Child, or Spouse).
* **Comments** (8, text, **Optional**): Add any additional information about the patient's emotional distance or relationship.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or their caregivers.
* If you have completed the form previously, you can edit the existing information to update the patient's record.
