# Veterinary Infection Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Infection Assessment form is designed to help collect information about your pet's infection symptoms and medical history. It's a tool for veterinarians to gather the necessary details to provide an accurate diagnosis and treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the details about your pet, including their species, age, and weight.
2. List any symptoms your pet is experiencing, such as vomiting, diarrhea, or fever.
3. Indicate the suspected sites of infection, like skin, wound, or tumor.
4. Describe any treatments or medications your pet has received.
5. Enter the name and contact information of your veterinarian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Details (pet_details)**: Enter your pet's species, age, and weight to help us better understand their medical history.
* **Symptoms (symptoms)**: Select all symptoms your pet is experiencing from the list:
	+ Severe vomiting
	+ Severe diarrhea
	+ Fever
	+ Rapid breathing
	+ Lack of appetite
	+ Weight loss
	+ Pus at the injection site
	+ Redness or swelling
	+ Pus at the wound
* **Suspected Infection Sites (suspected_infection_sites)**: Select the suspected site of infection from the list:
	+ Skin
	+ Wound
	+ Tumor
	+ Joint
	+ Soft tissue
	+ Bone
	+ Other (specify)
	+ Nail
	+ Tooth
* **Treatments (treatments)**: Enter any treatments or medications your pet has received.
* **Veterinarian Name (veterinarian_name)**: Enter your veterinarian's name and contact information.
* **Contact Information (contact_information)**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you with any further questions or updates.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing your pet's symptoms and medical history.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your veterinarian before completing this form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided contact information.
