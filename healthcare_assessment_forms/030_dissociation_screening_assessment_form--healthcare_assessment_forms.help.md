# Dissociation Screening Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's dissociation experiences, severity, diagnosis, and other related symptoms. It's intended for healthcare professionals to gather information about a patient's mental health and provide a standardized assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
2. Select the correct responses based on your experiences or observations.
3. For questions with multiple-choice options, choose one or multiple answers that apply to your situation.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please consult with a healthcare professional or provide additional information in the "Other Symptoms" or "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Demographics
* **Demographics_label** (text): This field is intended for you to provide your contact information, such as your name, email, or phone number. Please enter your details accurately.

### Client Reported Experiences
* **client_reported_experiences_label** (select multiple): Please indicate if you have experienced any of the following:
	+ Yes, frequently
	+ Yes, sometimes
	+ No

### Trauma Experiences
* **trauma_experiences_label** (select multiple): Please indicate if you have experienced any of the following traumas:
	+ Yes, frequently
	+ Yes, sometimes
	+ No

### Dissociation Frequency
* **dissociation_frequency_label** (number): This field is for you to report how often you experience dissociation. Please enter a number that represents the frequency of your experiences (e.g., 1-10).

### Dissociation Severity
* **dissociation_severity_label** (select one): Please indicate the severity of your dissociation experiences:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Dissociation Diagnosis
* **dissociation_diagnosis_label** (select one): Please indicate if you have been diagnosed with dissociation:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Mental Health Professional
* **mental_health_pro_label** (select one): Please indicate if you have a mental health professional:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Other Symptoms
* **other_symptoms_label** (text): If you're experiencing any other symptoms not listed above, please describe them here.

### Notes
* **notes_label** (text): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them here.

### Other Information
* **other_info_label** (text): If you have any other information you'd like to share, please enter it here.
* **other_info2_label** (text): If you have any other information you'd like to share, please enter it here.
* **other_info3_label** (text): If you have any other information you'd like to share, please enter it here.

### Phone
* **phone_label** (text): If you have a phone number where we can contact you, please enter it here.

### Email
* **email_label** (email): If you have an email address where we can contact you, please enter it here.

### Date of Birth
* **date_of_birth_label** (date): Please enter your date of birth.

### Time
* **time_label** (time): If relevant, please enter your current time.

### Date
* **date_label** (date): If relevant, please enter your current date.

This form will help healthcare professionals assess your dissociation experiences and provide you with appropriate guidance and support. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to them.
