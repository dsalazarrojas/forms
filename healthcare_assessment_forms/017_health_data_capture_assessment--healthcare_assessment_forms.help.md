# Health Data Capture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Data Capture Assessment form is designed to capture essential health information from patients, enabling healthcare professionals to make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully to understand each section's purpose.
2. Answer every question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Fill out as many or as few fields as required by the healthcare professional or your specific situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Assessment Forms** (`healthcare_assessment_forms`, text, required/optional): This field allows you to enter additional information about the patient's health forms, such as previous assessments or medical records.
* **Collect health intake details** (`collect_health_intake_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide as much detail as possible about the patient's health status, including any relevant medical history or current symptoms.
* **Lifestyle insights** (`lifestyle_insights`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether the patient has a healthy lifestyle, such as regular exercise, balanced diet, or adequate sleep. You can select multiple options if applicable.
* **Collect lifestyle insights** (`collect_lifestyle_insights`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the patient's lifestyle habits that may impact their health.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): You can indicate that you have obtained the patient's consent for the assessment, which is 'Yes', 'No', or 'null' for unknown. 
* **Clinics** (`clinics`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the clinics that are relevant to the patient's care, which can be 'Primary', 'Secondary', or 'null' for unknown.
* **Wellness providers** (`wellness_providers`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the wellness providers that are relevant to the patient's care, which can be 'Primary', 'Secondary', or 'null' for unknown.
* **Care teams** (`care_teams`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the care teams that are relevant to the patient's care, which can be 'Primary', 'Secondary', or 'null' for unknown.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
- If you're unsure about any question or field, please consult the healthcare professional or seek guidance.
- This form is not intended to replace professional medical advice or treatment. If you have any concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
