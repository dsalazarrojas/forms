# Metamorphosis Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metamorphosis Assessment form is designed to collect information for a comprehensive evaluation or assessment in the healthcare sector. It aims to gather details about the current state, goals, and progress of a subject or patient, along with personal and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Current State" page.
2. Move on to the "Goals", "Obstacles", and "Progress" pages to provide information about the subject's or patient's goals, obstacles, and current progress.
3. In the "Transformation Goals" section, select the appropriate answers to indicate the goals and objectives of the assessment.
4. Enter the current progress in the "Current Progress" field.
5. Estimate the expected progress in the "Expected Progress" field.
6. Provide the relevant time and date for the assessment in the "Time" and "Date" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the subject's or patient's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the subject's or patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Finally, select the relevant answer to the "State" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current State** (Page 1, Note type): This field is a text area where you can provide a detailed description of the subject's or patient's current state. This could include their physical condition, medical history, or any other relevant information.
* **Goals** (Page 2, Text type): List the goals and objectives of the assessment.
* **Obstacles** (Page 3, Text type): List any obstacles or challenges that the subject or patient may be facing.
* **Progress** (Page 4, Number type): Enter the current progress of the subject or patient, on a scale of 0-100.
* **Transformation Goals** (Page 5, Select Multiple type): Select all the goals and objectives that apply to the assessment.
	+ 'Yes': Indicate that the goal is relevant and applicable.
	+ 'No': Indicate that the goal is not relevant or not applicable.
* **Current Progress** (Page 6, Number type): Enter the current progress of the subject or patient, on a scale of 0-100.
* **Expected Progress** (Page 7, Number type): Estimate the expected progress of the subject or patient, on a scale of 0-100.
* **Time** (Page 8, Time type): Enter the relevant time for the assessment, e.g., appointment time or treatment duration.
* **Date** (Page 9, Date type): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Email** (Page 10, Email type): Enter the subject's or patient's email address.
* **Phone** (Page 11, Text type): Enter the subject's or patient's phone number.
* **State** (Page 12, Select One type): Select the current state of the subject or patient, with the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ 'Yes': Indicate that the subject or patient is in the desired state.
	+ 'No': Indicate that the subject or patient is not in the desired state.
