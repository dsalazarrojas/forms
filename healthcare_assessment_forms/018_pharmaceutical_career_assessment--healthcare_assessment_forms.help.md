# Pharmaceutical Career Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your background, experience, and interests within the pharmaceutical industry. It will help us understand your current situation and identify potential areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields marked as "required" (indicated by a asterisk * in the label) in each section.
2. For multiple-choice fields (e.g., "Education"), select all relevant options that apply to you.
3. For text fields (e.g., "Main Information", "Work Experience", "Desired Location"), provide a brief answer or description in the space provided.
4. If you're unsure about a particular field, please ask for clarification or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information** (*): This field is required. Please provide a brief description of your current job title, company, and any relevant experience or certifications you have in the pharmaceutical industry.
* **Education** (*): This field is required. Select all the degrees you have earned or are currently pursuing in the pharmaceutical industry (e.g., PharmD, PhD, MS, BS, BA, Other).
* **Work Experience** (*): This field is required. Describe your relevant work experience in the pharmaceutical industry, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Skills** (*): This field is optional. Select any programming languages or skills you have experience with that are relevant to the pharmaceutical industry (e.g., Python, Java, C++).
* **Career Interests** (*): This field is optional. Select all areas of interest within the pharmaceutical industry that apply to you (e.g., Pharmaceutical Research, Clinical Trials, Medical Sales, Other).
* **Desired Location** (*): This field is required. Provide a brief description of your preferred location for your pharmaceutical career (e.g., city, state, country).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* The information you provide will be kept confidential and used solely for the purpose of this survey.
