# Correctional Mental Health Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the mental health status of individuals in correctional facilities.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the fields with the relevant information about the individual's mental health status.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary documentation and authorization to complete this form.
3. Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly and that the form is complete before submitting it.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Correctional Facility** (1): Please enter the name of the correctional facility where the individual is being held.
* **Date Last Mental Health Evaluation** (2): Please enter the date of the last mental health evaluation for this individual.
* **Mental Health Problems** (3): Please list any mental health problems that this individual has, such as depression, anxiety, etc.
* **Medication Use** (4): Please indicate whether this individual takes any medication for their mental health problems.
* **Mental Status** (5): Please select the current mental status of this individual, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Mental Status Score** (6): Please enter a score for the individual's mental status.
* **Risk Assessment** (7): Please indicate whether this individual is at risk for any mental health problems.
* **Treatment Plan** (8): Please outline the treatment plan for this individual's mental health problems.
* **Care Coordinator Notes** (9): Please enter any notes from the care coordinator for this individual.
* **Mental Health History** (10): Please enter any relevant mental health history for this individual.
* **Medical History** (11): Please enter any relevant medical history for this individual.
* **Mental Status History** (12): Please enter any history of mental status for this individual.
* **Mental Status Notes** (13): Please enter any notes about the individual's mental status.
* **Assessment Tool** (14): Please indicate whether this form has been used to assess this individual's mental health status.
* **Assessment Tool Score** (15): Please enter a score for the assessment tool.
* **Mental Health Goal** (16): Please enter a goal for this individual's mental health status.
* **Mental Health Goal Description** (17): Please enter a description of the goal for this individual's mental health status.
* **Mental Status Risk** (18): Please enter any risk factors for this individual's mental health status.
* **Corrections Mental Health** (19): Please enter any mental health issues related to the individual's corrections status.
* **Mental Status Outcome** (20): Please enter the outcome of the individual's mental health status.
* **Corrections Outcome** (21): Please enter the outcome of the individual's corrections status.
* **Mental Status Recommendation** (22): Please enter any recommendations for this individual's mental health status.
* **Corrections Recommendation** (23): Please enter any recommendations for the individual's corrections status.
* **Mental Status Conclusion** (24): Please enter any conclusion about the individual's mental health status.
* **Corrections Conclusion** (25): Please enter any conclusion about the individual's corrections status.
