# Joint Making Rules Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Making Rules Assessment form is designed to evaluate your confidence in making joints in woodworking, covering essential rules, techniques, and areas of confidence.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and the questions asked.
2. Consider your experience and confidence in each area covered by the form.
3. Answer the questions accordingly, using the options provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the most important rules when making joints** (`What are the most important rules when making joints`):
	+ This field is an open-ended text response where you can provide any relevant rules or guidelines you follow when making joints.
* **Wood awareness and preparation** (`Wood awareness and preparation`):
	+ Please select the level of your awareness and preparation in woodworking, with options ranging from 0 to 5.
* **Glue selection** (`Glue selection`):
	+ Choose all the types of glue you are familiar with and use in your joint-making process.
* **Glue application techniques** (`Glue application techniques`):
	+ Provide any relevant details or techniques you use when applying glue in your joint-making process.
* **Confidence in woodworking joints** (`Confidence in woodworking joints`):
	+ Select your level of confidence in making joints, ranging from Low to High.
* **Confidence in joints technique** (`Confidence in joints technique`):
	+ Choose your level of confidence in the technique involved in joint-making, ranging from Low to High.
* **Confidence in joint construction** (`Confidence in joint construction`):
	+ Select your level of confidence in the construction process involved in joint-making, ranging from Low to High.
* **Written explanations** (`Written explanations`):
	+ Provide any relevant written explanations for your answers, elaborating on your reasoning.
* **Email** (`Email`):
	+ Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up).
* **Phone** (`Phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up).

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this assessment is designed to evaluate your confidence and understanding.
* Use the options provided for each field, and do not hesitate to provide any additional information if needed.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please feel free to contact us for further clarification.
