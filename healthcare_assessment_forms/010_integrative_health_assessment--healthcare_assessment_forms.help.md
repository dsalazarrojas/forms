# Integrative Health Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information for integrative health assessments, which are designed to evaluate a patient's overall well-being and identify areas for improvement in their physical, emotional, and mental health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number and date of birth.
3. Select any medical conditions that apply to you.
4. Provide a brief note about your medical history.
5. Select up to three wellness goals that you are currently working towards.
6. Indicate whether you have health insurance and specify your provider.
7. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code.
8. Provide contact information for your emergency contact, including their relationship to you and their phone number and email address.
9. Enter the name, phone number, and contact information for your primary care provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select any medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Provide a brief note about your medical history.
* **Wellness Goals** (`wellness_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three wellness goals that you are currently working towards.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have health insurance.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Specify your health insurance provider.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Primary Care Provider** (`primary_care_provider`, text, optional): Enter your primary care provider's name.
* **Primary Phone** (`primary_phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary care provider's phone number.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's Name 2** (`doctor_name_2`, text, optional): Enter your second doctor's name.
* **Doctor's Phone 2** (`doctor_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your second doctor's phone number. 

## Tips
- Please provide as much information as possible to ensure accurate and comprehensive assessment.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
- This form is for your personal use and is not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment.
