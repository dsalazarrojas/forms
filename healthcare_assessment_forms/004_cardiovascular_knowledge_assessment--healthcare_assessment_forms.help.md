# Cardiovascular Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the cardiovascular knowledge of individuals, helping healthcare providers identify knowledge gaps and areas for further education. This assessment is a tool to gauge one's understanding of various heart health topics, including risk factors, symptoms, and treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`) for tracking purposes.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (`Email Address`) for receiving results and follow-up communications.
3. Select your **Role** (`Your Role`) - e.g., Patient, Healthcare Student, Healthcare Professional, or General Public.
4. Choose your **Age Group** (`Age Group`) - e.g., Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, etc.
5. Indicate your **Education Level** (`Education Level`) - e.g., Less than high school, High school diploma, Some college, etc.
6. Answer the following multiple-choice questions about cardiovascular health, selecting the best option from the provided choices.
7. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments or Questions** (`Additional Comments or Questions`) if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name for tracking and reference purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive the results and follow-up communications.
* **Your Role**: Select your role to help us tailor the content to your needs. This could be Patient, Healthcare Student, Healthcare Professional, General Public, Caregiver, or Other.
* **Age Group**: Choose your age category to help us gauge your cardiovascular knowledge based on demographics.
* **Education Level**: Select the highest level of education completed to help us assess your baseline knowledge.
* **Prior Heart Health Education**: Indicate if you have received formal education on heart health, and if so, whether it was comprehensive or basic.
* **What is considered a normal blood pressure reading?**: Select the correct answer from the provided options.
* **Which of the following is NOT a risk factor for heart disease?**: Choose the option that best answers this question.
* **How many minutes of moderate exercise is recommended for heart health?**: Select the correct duration from the options.
* **What does LDL cholesterol represent?**: Choose the correct answer from the options.
* **Which symptom is commonly associated with a heart attack?**: Select all that apply from the options.
* **What is atrial fibrillation?**: Choose the best description from the options.
* **Which diet is most recommended for heart health?**: Select the correct option.
* **True or False - Stress can contribute to heart disease?**: Choose True or False.
* **What is the primary function of the heart?**: Select the correct answer.
* **Which of the following can help prevent heart disease?**: Select all that apply from the options.
* **How confident do you feel about your heart health knowledge?**: Choose how confident you are before taking this assessment.
* **Which heart health topics would you like to learn more about?**: Select all that apply from the options.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- Be honest in your answers, as they will help us tailor educational materials to your needs.
- If you have any doubts or questions, use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field to provide more information.
