# Health Sociology Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Sociology Assessment is a form used to gather information about an individual's health behaviors and sociological factors that influence community health. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the relationships between an individual's health status and their social environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Health Sociology Assessment, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and select the most accurate response.
* Answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate results.
* Select multiple options where indicated.
