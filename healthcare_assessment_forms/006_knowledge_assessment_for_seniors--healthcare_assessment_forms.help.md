# Knowledge Assessment For Seniors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the knowledge of seniors on various topics related to healthy aging habits, including understanding healthy aging habits, confidence level, and specific questions about different aspects of healthy aging.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have filled out the required fields, such as the Senior Center ID, Name, and HealthCare Provider ID.
2. Select the correct answers for each question based on your understanding and knowledge.
3. Choose the correct option for the Confidence Level based on your actual confidence in the information provided.
4. Enter any notes or comments related to the assessment in the Notes field.
5. Choose the submission status and date for the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Senior Center** (`senior_center_id`): This is the ID of the senior center where the assessment took place.
* **Name** (`name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider_id`): This is the ID of the healthcare provider who conducted the assessment.
* **Wellness Program** (`wellness_program_id`): This field is currently not being used in the form.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): This is the date when the assessment took place.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`): This is the time when the assessment took place.
* **Understanding Of Healthy Aging Habits** (`understanding_of_healthy_aging_habits`): Please select 'Yes' if you have a good understanding of healthy aging habits, 'No' otherwise.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`): Please select the level of confidence you have in the information provided. Options are High, Low.
* **Question 1** (`question_1`): Please select 'Yes' if you have an understanding of how to maintain a healthy weight as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 2** (`question_2`): Please select 'Yes' if you know the importance of staying physically active as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 3** (`question_3`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the benefits of a balanced diet as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 4** (`question_4`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to manage stress as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the importance of getting enough sleep as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 6** (`question_6`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to manage chronic conditions as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the importance of staying mentally stimulated as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 8** (`question_8`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to maintain social connections as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the benefits of staying organized as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 10** (`question_10`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to manage finances as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 11** (`question_11`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the importance of staying safe as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 12** (`question_12`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to manage medication as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 13** (`question_13`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the benefits of staying connected with others as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 14** (`question_14`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to stay up to date with health information as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 15** (`question_15`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the importance of having a support system as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 16** (`question_16`): Please select 'Yes' if you know how to manage chronic conditions as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Question 17** (`question_17`): Please select 'Yes' if you understand the benefits of staying positive as you age, 'No' otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`): This is a text field where you can enter any additional comments or information related to the assessment.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Please select 'Active' if the submission is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This is the date when the submission was made.
