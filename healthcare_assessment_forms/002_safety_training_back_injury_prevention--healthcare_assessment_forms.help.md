# Safety Training: Back Injury Prevention - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess trainees' understanding of back injury prevention principles during a safety training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Record your attendance by entering the correct number in the "Training Attendance" field.
2. Select all the safe lifting principles you understand from the "Safe Lifting Principles" field. Make sure to choose all relevant options.
3. If you have any back injury concerns, describe them in the "Back Injury Concerns" field.
4. In the "How to Prevent Back Injuries" field, provide additional information on how to prevent back injuries.
5. Enter your comments and feedback in the "Trainee Comments" field.
6. If you have any comments or feedback for the trainer, enter them in the "Trainer Comments" field.
7. As the safety manager, you may add any comments or feedback in the "Safety Manager Comments" field.
8. Confirm the back injury reporting status by selecting "Reported", "Not reported", or "Not sure" from the "Back Injury Reporting Status" field.
9. Enter the date of the training session in the "Training Date" field.
10. Enter the time of the training session in the "Training Time" field.
11. Enter your name and role as the trainer in the "Trainer Name" and "Trainer Role" fields.
12. Enter your email address in the "Trainer Email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Trainer Phone" field.
14. Enter any notes or comments from the trainer in the "Trainer Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Attendance (1)**: Record your attendance by entering a number.
* **Safe Lifting Principles (2)**: Select all the safe lifting principles you understand.
	+ Proper lifting techniques
	+ Back safety principles
	+ Hazardous materials handling
* **Back Injury Concerns (3)**: Describe any back injury concerns you have.
* **How to Prevent Back Injuries (4)**: Provide information on how to prevent back injuries.
* **Trainee Comments (5)**: Enter any comments or feedback you have.
* **Trainer Comments (6)**: Enter any comments or feedback for the trainer.
* **Safety Manager Comments (7)**: Enter any comments or feedback as the safety manager.
* **Back Injury Reporting Status (8)**: Confirm the back injury reporting status by selecting "Reported", "Not reported", or "Not sure".
* **Training Date (9)**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time (10)**: Enter the time of the training session.
* **Trainer Name (11)**: Enter your name as the trainer.
* **Trainer Role (12)**: Enter your role as the trainer.
* **Trainer Email (13)**: Enter your email address as the trainer.
* **Trainer Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number as the trainer.
* **Trainer Notes (15)**: Enter any notes or comments from the trainer.
