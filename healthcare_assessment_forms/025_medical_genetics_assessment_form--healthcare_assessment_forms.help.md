# Medical Genetics Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Genetics Assessment Form is designed to gather comprehensive information about a patient's medical genetics assessment, which includes their medical history, family history, genetic conditions, and other relevant details. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's genetic risk factors and develop an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medications, or previous treatments in the **Patient History** section.
2.  Record the patient's family medical history, including any genetic conditions that may be affecting their relatives in the **Family History** section.
3.  Select the genetic conditions that the patient may be experiencing based on their family history and medical background in the **Genetic Conditions** section.
4.  Record the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical events or conditions in the **Medical History** section.
5.  Obtain the patient's consent for genetic testing and counseling in the **Consent** section.
6.  Record the genetic counselor's observations and recommendations in the **Genetic Counselor** section.
7.  Record the clinic's or healthcare provider's information in the **Clinic Information** section.
8.  Capture the patient's name, age, relationship to the patient, and gender in the **Patient Information** section.
9.  Select the genetic tests that may be relevant for the patient in the **Genetic Testing** section.
10.  Record the results of any genetic testing in the **Genetic Testing Result** section.
11.  Record the date of the genetic testing result in the **Genetic Result Date** section.
12.  Record the patient's medical record number, provider, and date in the **Medical Record Information** section.
13.  Record any additional notes or recommendations by the genetic counselor in the **Genetic Counselor Note** section.
14.  Confirm the patient's consent for genetic testing and counseling in the **Patient Consent** section.
15.  Record the date of the patient's consent in the **Patient Consent Date** section.
16.  Sign and date the genetic counselor's involvement in the process in the **Genetic Counselor Signature** section.
17.  Sign and date the clinic or healthcare provider's involvement in the process in the **Clinic Signature** section.
18.  Sign and date the patient's consent and understanding of the process in the **Patient Signature** section.
19.  Record the healthcare provider's review of the patient's medical record in the **Medical Record Review** section.
20.  Record any additional notes or recommendations by the clinic or healthcare provider in the **Clinic Note** section.
