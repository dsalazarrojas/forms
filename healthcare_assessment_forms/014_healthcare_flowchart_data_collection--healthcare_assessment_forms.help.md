# Healthcare Flowchart Data Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document patient information for healthcare assessments. It helps healthcare professionals to evaluate and manage patient care effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
3. Use the options provided for select_multiple and select_one fields to choose the most accurate answer.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the text fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Concerns** (1, text, required):
	+ This field is a free-text area where you can document any concerns or issues the patient is experiencing.
* **Symptom Checklist** (2, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select multiple symptoms from the provided options that the patient is experiencing.
* **Patient History** (3, text, required):
	+ This field is for documenting the patient's medical history, including any past medical conditions or treatments.
* **Contact Details** (4, text, required):
	+ This field is for recording contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses for the patient.
* **Patient Goals** (5, text, required):
	+ This field is for documenting the patient's goals and expectations for their care.
* **Care Workers Involved** (6, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select multiple healthcare workers involved in the patient's care.
* **Patient Outcome** (7, select_one, required):
	+ This field allows you to select the expected outcome or result of the patient's care.
* **Follow Up Plan** (8, text, required):
	+ This field is for documenting any follow-up plans or scheduled appointments.
* **Clinical Notes** (9, text, required):
	+ This field is for any additional clinical notes or comments.
* **Medication Allergies** (10, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select the patient's medication allergies.
* **Patient Preferences** (11, text, required):
	+ This field is for documenting the patient's preferences or values.
* **Healthcare Team** (12, text, required):
	+ This field is for recording the healthcare team members involved in the patient's care.
* **Patient Status** (13, text, required):
	+ This field is for documenting the patient's current status, such as "stable" or "discharged".
