# Functional Ability Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a patient's functional ability in various areas, including mobility, communication, cognitive function, personal care, and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer each question based on the patient's actual condition and ability. For select_one fields, choose the option that best describes the patient's situation. For select_multiple fields, choose all applicable options. For select_one fields with options like "Adequate", "Limited", or "Unlimited", choose the option that best reflects the patient's ability or condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Independence** (`2`, `select_one`, required): This field assesses the patient's ability to perform daily tasks, such as walking. Please choose the option that best describes the patient's current level of independence, based on their physical condition.
* **Mobility** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): This field evaluates the patient's mobility, including sitting, standing, and climbing stairs. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current level of mobility.
* **Communication** (`4`, `select_one`, required): This field assesses the patient's ability to communicate effectively, verbally. Please choose the option that best describes the patient's current level of communication ability.
* **Cognitive Function** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's cognitive function, including their alertness and orientation. Choose the option that best describes the patient's current level of cognitive ability.
* **Personal Care** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): This field assesses the patient's ability to perform self-care tasks, such as bathing and toileting. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current level of personal care ability.
* **Nutrition** (`7`, `select_one`, required): This field evaluates the patient's nutritional status, including their ability to access and consume food. Please choose the option that best describes the patient's current nutritional condition.
* **Continence** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): This field assesses the patient's continence, including their ability to control bladder and bowel movements. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current level of continence ability.
* **Social Interaction** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's ability to interact with others, including their ability to participate in social activities. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current level of social interaction ability.
* **Safety** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): This field assesses the patient's safety, including their risk of falling or harming themselves. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current level of safety ability.
* **Sleep** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's sleep quality or duration, including their ability to rest and relax. Please choose the option that best describes the patient's current sleep condition.
* **Bowels** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): This field assesses the patient's bowel function, including their ability to have regular bowel movements. Please choose the option that best describes the patient's current bowel condition.
* **Bladder** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's bladder function, including their ability to control their bladder. Please choose the option that best describes the patient's current bladder condition.
* **Continence Care** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field assesses the patient's continence care needs, including their ability to manage incontinence issues. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current continence care ability.
* **Bowels Care** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's bowel care needs, including their ability to manage bowel issues. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current bowel care ability.
* **Bladder Care** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field assesses the patient's bladder care needs, including their ability to manage bladder issues. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current bladder care ability.
* **Personal Care 2** (`17`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's personal care needs, including their ability to perform daily self-care tasks. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current personal care ability.
* **Continence Care 2** (`18`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field assesses the patient's continence care needs, including their ability to manage incontinence issues. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current continence care ability.
* **Bowels Care 2** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field evaluates the patient's bowel care needs, including their ability to manage bowel issues. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current bowel care ability.
* **Bladder Care 2** (`20`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field assesses the patient's bladder care needs, including their ability to manage bladder issues. Choose all applicable options that describe the patient's current bladder care ability.
* **Notes** (`21`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition or care needs. Please enter any relevant information here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have informed the patient about the purpose and use of their data before collecting it.
* Be thorough and accurate when completing each field, as this will help provide a comprehensive assessment of the patient's functional ability.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, please consult with the patient or healthcare team to ensure accuracy.
* This form should be completed with the patient's input, and the results can help inform care planning and decision-making.
