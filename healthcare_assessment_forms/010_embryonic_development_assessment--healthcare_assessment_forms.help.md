# Embryonic Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Embryonic Development Assessment form is designed to collect data on the embryonic development of a fetus or newborn, taking into account various factors related to the mother's health, demographics, and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the age of the embryo/fetus/newborn.
2. Select the assessment method used.
3. Choose the outcome(s) of the assessment.
4. Enter the gestational date.
5. Enter the maternal height, weight, and other relevant information.
6. Indicate the status of the placenta.
7. Select the options for the maternal health status, insurance, income frequency, income source, marital status, education, employment status, housing status, and other relevant fields.
8. Add any additional notes about the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Embryo Age** (`embryo_age`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the age of the embryo or fetus in weeks or months.
* **Assessment Method** (`assessment_method`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the method used to assess the embryonic development, e.g., ultrasound, MRI, or clinical examination.
* **Assessment Outcome** (`assessment_outcome`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the outcome(s) of the assessment, e.g., normal development, growth restriction, or other.
* **Gestational Date** (`gestational_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of gestation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Maternal Height** (`maternal_height`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal height in inches or centimeters.
* **Placenta Status** (`placenta_status`, `number`, required: `false`): Indicate the status of the placenta, e.g., normal, thickened, or other.
* **Maternal BMI** (`maternal_bmi`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal Body Mass Index (BMI).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: `false`): Add any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
* **Maternal Age** (`maternal_age`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal age in years.
* **Maternal Weight** (`maternal_weight`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Maternal Weight Unit** (`maternal_weight_unit`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the unit of measurement for maternal weight, e.g., pounds or kilograms.
* **Maternal Weight Category** (`maternal_weight_category`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the category of maternal weight.
* **Maternal Height** (`maternal_height`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal height in inches or centimeters.
* **Maternal Gravida** (`maternal_gravida`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal gravida (age in years at last birth).
* **Maternal Parities** (`maternal_parities`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of previous births.
* **Maternal Marital Status** (`maternal_marital_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the marital status of the mother.
* **Maternal Education** (`maternal_education`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the maternal education level.
* **Maternal Employment Status** (`maternal_employment_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the employment status of the mother.
* **Maternal Housing Status** (`maternal_housing_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the housing status of the mother.
* **Maternal Income** (`maternal_income`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the maternal income in dollars.
* **Maternal Income Source** (`maternal_income_source`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the source of the maternal income.
* **Maternal Income Frequency** (`maternal_income_frequency`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the frequency of the maternal income.
* **Maternal Health Status** (`maternal_health_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the health status of the mother.
* **Maternal Health Insurance** (`maternal_health_insurance`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the health insurance of the mother.
* **Maternal Cigarettes** (`maternal_cigarettes`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the maternal smoking status.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the administrator.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them to the notes field.
