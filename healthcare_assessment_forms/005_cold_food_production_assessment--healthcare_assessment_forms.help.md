# Cold Food Production Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the compliance status of a food production facility with regards to cold storage and food safety practices. It helps identify areas for improvement and ensures that the facility is operating according to established guidelines and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Facility Information**: Complete the first five fields with the relevant information about the facility, including the facility's name, address, the date of the assessment, the assessor's name, and their title.
2. **Cold Storage and Monitoring**: Select the type of food production facility and the types of cold storage equipment available on-site. Enter the current temperature ranges for refrigerators and freezers, and select the frequency at which temperatures are monitored and recorded.
3. **Hygiene Practices**: Select all the observed hygiene practices, such as hand washing stations, sanitizers, hair nets, gloves, protective clothing, and foot baths.
4. **Cleaning and Sanitation**: Select the frequency at which the cold storage is cleaned and sanitized.
5. **Pest Control**: Select the pest control methods in use, such as traps, bait stations, air curtains, sealed entry points, regular inspections, and professional services.
6. **Product Storage**: Select the method of product storage organization, such as by product type, temperature requirement, production date, expiration date, FIFO, or mixed storage.
7. **Cross-Contamination Prevention**: Select measures taken to prevent cross-contamination, such as separate storage areas, color-coded equipment, physical barriers, dedicated tools, staff training, and signage.
8. **Employee Training**: Select the status of employee food safety training, such as all staff certified, most staff certified, some staff certified, training in progress, or no formal training.
9. **HACCP Plan**: Select the status of the Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) plan, such as implemented and active, in development, planned, not required, or not aware.
10. **Backup Power**: Select the type of backup power system in place, such as a generator on-site, battery backup, or dual power supply.
11. **Temperature Alarm Systems**: Select all the alert systems for temperature deviations, such as audible alarms, visual alarms, SMS alerts, email alerts, phone call alerts, or none.
12. **Equipment Maintenance**: Select the frequency at which equipment is serviced.
13. **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the most recent health inspection.
14. **Inspection Result**: Select the outcome of the last inspection.
15. **Non-Compliance Issues**: List any known non-compliance issues.
16. **Corrective Actions**: List the corrective actions required to address issues.
17. **Overall Assessment**: Evaluate the overall facility assessment rating, such as excellent, good, satisfactory, needs improvement, or poor.
18. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: The name of the food production facility.
* **Facility Address**: The complete street address of the facility.
* **Assessment Date**: The date of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name**: The name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Title**: The job title or role of the assessor.
* **Facility Type**: The type of food production facility (e.g., commercial kitchen, food processing plant).
* **Cold Storage Types On-Site**: Select all the types of cold storage equipment available on-site (e.g., walk-in refrigerator, walk-in freezer, reach-in refrigerator, reach-in freezer, blast chiller, cold room, freezer tunnel).
* **Refrigerator Temperature Range**: Enter the current temperature setting in Fahrenheit for refrigerators.
* **Freezer Temperature Range**: Enter the current temperature setting in Fahrenheit for freezers.
* **Temperature Monitoring Frequency**: Select how often temperatures are monitored (e.g., continuous, every hour, every 2 hours, every 4 hours, twice daily, once daily, weekly).
* **Temperature Monitoring Method**: Select all the methods used to record temperatures (e.g., digital data loggers, analog thermometers, digital thermometers, infrared sensors, manual log sheets, automated alert system).
* **Hygiene Practices Observed**: Select all observed hygiene practices (e.g., hand washing stations, sanitizer dispensers, hair nets, gloves, protective clothing, foot baths).
* **Cleaning Schedule Frequency**: Select the frequency at which cold storage is cleaned (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, as needed).
* **Pest Control Measures**: Select all the pest control methods in use (e.g., traps, bait stations, air curtains, sealed entry points, regular inspections, professional service).
* **Product Storage Organization**: Select the method used to store products (e.g., by product type, temperature requirement, production date, expiration date, FIFO, mixed storage).
* **Cross-Contamination Prevention Measures**: Select all the measures taken to prevent cross-contamination (e.g., separate storage areas, color-coded equipment, physical barriers, dedicated tools, staff training, signage).
* **Employee Food Safety Training Status**: Select the status of employee food safety training.
* **HACCP Plan Status**: Select the status of the HACCP plan (e.g., implemented and active, in development, planned, not required, not aware).
* **Backup Power System**: Select the type of backup power system in place (e.g., generator on-site, battery backup, dual power supply).
* **Temperature Alarm Systems**: Select all the alert systems for temperature deviations (e.g., audible alarms, visual alarms, SMS alerts, email alerts, phone call alerts, none).
* **Equipment Maintenance Schedule**: Select the frequency at which equipment is serviced.
* **Last Health Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the most recent health inspection.
* **Last Inspection Result**: Select the outcome of the last inspection.
* **Current Non-Compliance Issues**: List any known non-compliance issues.
* **Corrective Actions Needed**: List the corrective actions required to address issues.
* **Overall Facility Assessment Rating**: Evaluate the overall facility assessment rating (e.g., excellent, good, satisfactory, needs improvement, poor).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or recommendations.
