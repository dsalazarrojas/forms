# Anaesthesia Patient Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anaesthesia Patient Record form is designed to document a patient's information, medical history, and intraoperative and post-operative data to ensure accurate and comprehensive care during and after surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. **Patient Weight in kg**: Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
4. **Pre-existing Conditions**: Select all pre-existing medical conditions that apply from the list.
5. **Details of Known Allergies**: Enter any known allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
6. **Previous Anaesthesia Complications**: Indicate if the patient has experienced any previous anaesthesia complications.
7. **Type of Anaesthesia Administered**: Select the type of anaesthesia administered to the patient.
8. **Anaesthetic Agents Administered**: Enter any anaesthetic agents used during the procedure, including dosages if possible.
9. **Vital Signs Monitoring Notes**: Enter any vital signs monitoring notes, if applicable.
10. **Initial Recovery Status**: Select the patient's initial recovery status.
11. **Post-Operative Care Instructions**: Enter any specific post-operative care instructions for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: Required to identify the patient accurately.
* **Date of Birth**: Required for anaesthesia planning and medical history.
* **Patient Weight in kg**: Required for accurate anaesthesia planning and dosing.
* **Pre-existing Conditions**: Optional but important for understanding the patient's medical history.
* **Details of Known Allergies**: Optional but crucial for safe anaesthesia administration.
* **Previous Anaesthesia Complications**: Required for identifying potential risks.
* **Type of Anaesthesia Administered**: Required for patient care and anaesthesia planning.
* **Anaesthetic Agents Administered**: Required for accurate record-keeping and patient care.
* **Vital Signs Monitoring Notes**: Optional but important for monitoring the patient's condition.
* **Initial Recovery Status**: Required for post-operative care and recovery planning.
* **Post-Operative Care Instructions**: Optional but important for guiding post-operative care.
