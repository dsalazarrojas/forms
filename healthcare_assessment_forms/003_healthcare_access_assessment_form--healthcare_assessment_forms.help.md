# healthcare_access_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare_access_assessment_form is a tool used to evaluate a patient's healthcare access, delivery, and support needs. It is essential to provide accurate and comprehensive information to aid in making informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the required information accurately and truthfully.
3. If a field is not relevant, please mark it as "N/A" (Not Applicable).
4. Use the provided options for select fields or date fields.
5. For text fields, provide a clear and concise description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false)
This field is used to capture the patient's basic information such as name, address, and contact details. Enter the patient's details as accurately and clearly as possible.

### **Access To Healthcare** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the options that apply to the patient's access to healthcare. This field will help identify any barriers or limitations to the patient's healthcare access.

### **Barriers To Access** (`3`, `text`, required: false)
Describe any barriers or obstacles that prevent the patient from accessing healthcare services. This could include physical, social, or economic challenges.

### **Healthcare Provider** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the healthcare provider that is currently treating the patient. This will help in providing continuity of care.

### **Patient Care Coordinator** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the patient care coordinator who is handling the patient's care. This will ensure that the care team is aware of the patient's needs.

### **Healthcare Delivery** (`6`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date and time when the healthcare delivery was provided to the patient.

### **Healthcare Access** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the methods that are used for healthcare access such as phone calls, emails, or online portals.

### **Patient Support** (`8`, `note`, required: false)
Provide any additional support or resources that are needed for the patient's care. This could include transportation, home care, or counseling.

### **Follow Up** (`9`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date and time for any follow-up appointments or care check-ups.

### **Healthcare Delivery Date** (`10`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date when the healthcare delivery was last provided to the patient.
