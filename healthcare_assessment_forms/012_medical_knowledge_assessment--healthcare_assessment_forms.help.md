# Medical Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to provide their medical knowledge and experience information for assessment. The data collected will be used to evaluate individual healthcare professionals' expertise and knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out each page with the required and optional information.
3. Review each field carefully and provide accurate answers.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (1, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **age** (2, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **medical knowledge** (3, text, optional): Briefly describe your medical knowledge and experience.
* **specialty** (4, select_one, optional): Please select your medical specialty. Options: Yes, No.
* **medical school** (5, text, optional): Enter the name of your medical school.
* **medical degree** (6, select_one, optional): Please select your medical degree level. Options: High, Low.
* **hospital department** (7, select_one, optional): Please select your hospital department. Options: Yes, No.
* **medical license** (8, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have a medical license. Options: Yes, No.
* **medical experience** (9, number, optional): Enter your medical experience in years.
* **medical professional registration** (10, select_one, optional): Please select whether you are registered as a medical professional. Options: Yes, No.
* **medical specialty** (11, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have a medical specialty. Options: Yes, No.
* **medical board** (12, select_one, optional): Please select whether you are registered on the medical board. Options: Yes, No.
* **medical license status** (13, select_one, optional): Please select the status of your medical license. Options: Active, Inactive.
* **medical degree level** (14, select_one, optional): Please select your medical degree level. Options: High, Low.
* **medical specialty board** (15, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have a medical specialty board. Options: Yes, No.
* **hospital** (16, text, optional): Enter the name of your hospital.
* **hospital name** (17, text, optional): Enter the name of your hospital.
* **department** (18, text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **physician** (19, select_one, optional): Please select whether you are a physician. Options: Yes, No.
* **physician name** (20, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **physician experience** (21, number, optional): Enter your physician experience in years.
* **physician license** (22, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have a physician license. Options: Yes, No.
* **physician board** (23, select_one, optional): Please select whether you are registered on the physician board. Options: Yes, No.
* **physician registration** (24, select_one, optional): Please select whether you are registered as a physician. Options: Yes, No.
* **submission** (25, note, optional): Please provide a brief description of your clinical knowledge submission. 

## Tips
* Ensure you answer each field accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact your healthcare supervisor.
