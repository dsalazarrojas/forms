# Dengue Medical Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dengue Medical Certificate Form is a tool for healthcare organizations to assess the severity of dengue fever cases. This form is designed to collect relevant information from patients, allowing healthcare professionals to evaluate and manage the condition effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    a.  Start by filling out your basic information: Patient Name, Date Of Symptoms, and Signature.
    b.  Next, select your Dengue Test Result (Positive, Negative, or Not Tested).
    c.  If tested, describe the duration of your fever in days.
    d.  If necessary, provide a description of your symptoms.
    e.  If you received treatment, describe the treatment you underwent.
    f.  Finally, choose the type of medical certificate you need (Sick Leave, School Absence, or Insurance).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (text): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport. This information is essential for accurate identification and record-keeping purposes.
*   **Date Of Symptoms** (date): Enter the date when your dengue fever symptoms first appeared.
*   **Dengue Test Result** (select one): 
    +  **Positive**: If your test result is positive, it means you have dengue fever.
    +  **Negative**: If your test result is negative, it means you do not have dengue fever.
    +  **Not Tested**: If you haven't undergone a dengue test, select this option.
*   **Fever Duration** (number): If you've undergone a test, enter the number of days your fever lasted.
*   **Symptoms Description** (text): Describe any symptoms you experienced, such as headaches, joint pain, or rash.
*   **Treatment Description** (text): If you received treatment, describe what you underwent (e.g., medication, hospital stay).
*   **Medical Certificate Type** (select one): 
    +  **Sick Leave**: If you need a medical certificate for work or school, choose this option.
    +  **School Absence**: If you need a certificate for school absence, choose this option.
    +  **Insurance**: If you need a certificate for insurance purposes, choose this option.
*   **Signature** (text): This field is for your signature, which confirms that the information you've provided is accurate.

## Tips

- Always ensure that you've filled out all required fields (Patient Name, Date Of Symptoms, Dengue Test Result, and Signature).
- Be honest when selecting your Dengue Test Result.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult a healthcare professional.
