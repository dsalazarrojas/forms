# Gender Identity Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals understand and assess their gender identity. It is intended to be completed by individuals themselves and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best fit your feelings and experiences. You can choose one or multiple options for each question, depending on your response.

* **Gender Spectrum**: Select the options that best describe how you identify your gender. You may choose one or multiple options.
	+ Male, Female, Non Binary, Agender, Androgenus, Androgyne, Bigender, Cis, Genderqueer, Gender Variant, Genderfluid, Gender Non Binary, Intersex, Two-Spirit, Trans Man, Trans Woman, Transgender, Pangender, Neutrois, Gender Critical, Third Gender, Gender Neutral, Genderless, Cis Man, Cis Woman, Other
* **Gender Label**: Select the option that best describes your gender label.
	+ Male, Female, Non Binary, Other
* **Sex At Birth**: Select the option that best describes your sex at birth.
	+ Male, Female, Other
* **Sex Assigned At Birth**: Select the option that best describes your sex as assigned at birth.
	+ Male, Female, Other
* **Sex Identity**: Select the option that best describes your sex identity.
	+ Male, Female, Other
* **Birth Date**: Enter your birth date (optional).
* **Age**: Enter your age (optional).
* **How Do You Identify**: Enter how you identify (optional).
* **Comfort Level**: Select your comfort level with your gender identity.
	+ High, Medium, Low
* **Gender Feeling**: Select your feelings about your gender.
	+ Happy, Sad, Angry, Neutral
* **Gender Identity Important**: Select how important your gender identity is to you.
	+ Very, Somewhat, Not Very
