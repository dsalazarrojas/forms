## Social Care Outreach Initiative Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Care Outreach Initiative Evaluation form is designed to gather data on social care outreach initiatives. This form helps assess the effectiveness of these initiatives, providing valuable insights for future program development and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the fields below, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **outreach_visit_details**: This field is for documenting the details of the outreach visit, including the date, time, and any notable events or discussions.
* **participant_information**: Please provide any relevant information about the participant, such as their name, contact information, or demographics.
* **feedback**: Share any feedback or observations from the participant, which can help inform future outreach efforts.
* **date_last_contact**: Record the date of the last contact with the participant, if applicable.
* **time_last_contact**: Record the time of the last contact with the participant, if applicable.
* **contact_method**: Select the method of contact used during the outreach visit, such as phone, email, or in-person.
* **notes**: This is a free-text field for any additional notes or comments about the participant or outreach effort.
