# Client Struggles Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Struggles Assessment form is designed to collect information about the client's struggles and challenges, helping to identify areas that require attention and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question in the designated space, taking into account the hints and options provided.
3. Be specific when describing your struggles and challenges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (2): Your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This field is used to identify you uniquely.
- **Age** (3): Your age.
  - This field helps us understand your age group.
- **Email Address** (4): A valid email address where we can contact you.
  - This field ensures we can reach out to you with any necessary information or follow-up.
- **Phone Number** (5): A phone number where we can contact you.
  - This field allows us to communicate with you effectively.
- **Assessment Date** (6): The date of this assessment.
  - This field helps track the date and time of your assessment.
- **Primary Concerns and Challenges** (7): Briefly describe your primary concerns and challenges.
  - This field helps us understand your main issues.
- **What is your biggest struggle or challenge right now?** (8): Describe your biggest struggle or challenge in as much detail as possible.
  - This field is used to identify your primary concern.
- **How long have you been dealing with this struggle?** (9): Select the duration of your struggle.
  - This field helps us understand the timeline of your concern.
- **How intense is this struggle? - 1 to 10** (10): Rank the intensity of your struggle, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
  - This field helps us evaluate the gravity of your situation.
- **How does this struggle impact your daily life?** (11): Describe the effects of your struggle on your daily life.
  - This field provides insight into how your life is affected by your concerns.
- **What other struggles or challenges are you facing?** (12): List any other areas where you are struggling or facing challenges.
  - This field helps us identify additional areas that require attention.
- **Which areas of your life are most affected?** (13): Select all areas affected by your struggles.
  - This field helps us understand where you need assistance.
- **What do you think is causing these struggles?** (15): Describe the root causes of your struggles.
  - This field helps us understand the underlying reasons for your concerns.
- **Other factors contributing to your struggles** (16): Describe any other contributing factors.
  - This field helps us identify potential contributors to your situation.
- **Have you tried to address these struggles before?** (17): Indicate if you have attempted to address these issues previously.
  - This field provides insight into your past efforts.
- **What approaches or solutions have you tried?** (18): Describe any approaches or solutions you have used to address your struggles.
  - This field helps us understand your past efforts.
- **If previous attempts were unsuccessful, why do you think?** (19): Provide reasons for the failure of your previous attempts.
  - This field helps us identify potential reasons for the lack of success.
- **What areas would you most like to improve?** (21): Rank your top 3 priorities for improvement.
  - This field helps us understand your focus areas.
- **What would an ideal resolution look like for you?** (22): Describe the best possible outcome for your situation.
  - This field helps us understand your desired outcome.
- **What timeframe would you like to see improvement?** (23): Select the desired timeframe for improvement.
  - This field helps us understand your desired pace for change.
- **What support or resources do you currently have?** (25): List any support or resources you currently have access to.
  - This field helps us understand your current resources.
- **What type of support or help would be most valuable?** (26): Select all types of support or help you would find valuable.
  - This field helps us understand your support needs.
- **What prevents you from getting the help you need?** (27): Describe any barriers to seeking help.
  - This field helps us identify potential obstacles.
- **How would you rate your physical health?** (29): Rate your physical health on a scale.
  - This field helps us understand your physical well-being.
- **How would you rate your mental health?** (30): Rate your mental health on a scale.
  - This field helps us understand your mental status.
