# Healthcare Patient Safety Protocol Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the safety protocol in place at a healthcare facility, focusing on the patient's concerns, safety practices, and facility survey. It will help healthcare professionals evaluate the effectiveness of the safety protocol and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the relevant department from the "Department" field (e.g., Emergency Department, Operating Room, etc.).
5. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field (if applicable).
6. Enter your medical license number in the "Medical License Number" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
8. Enter the date of admission in the "Date of Admission" field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
9. Enter the time of admission in the "Time of Admission" field (HH:MM AM/PM format).
10. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" field.
11. Select the date of assessment from the "Assessment Date" field.
12. Enter the assessment time in the "Assessment Time" field (HH:MM AM/PM format).
13. Enter the facility's name in the "Facility Name" field.
14. Enter the facility's address in the "Facility Address" field.
15. Enter the facility's contact number in the "Facility Contact Number" field.
16. Enter the facility's email address in the "Facility Email" field.
17. Describe any safety concerns you have in the "Safety Concerns" field.
18. Describe the safety practices you follow in the "Safety Practices" field.
19. Describe the safety protocol in place at the facility in the "Safety Protocol" field.
20. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Facility Survey" (e.g., do you have a safety survey in place?).
21. Describe any patient concerns you have in the "Patient Concerns" field.
22. Provide additional details about the patient's concerns in the "Patient Concerns Details" field.
23. Describe any recommendations you have for the facility in the "Facility Recommendations" field.
24. Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-up Date" field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
25. Enter the follow-up time in the "Follow-up Time" field (HH:MM AM/PM format).
