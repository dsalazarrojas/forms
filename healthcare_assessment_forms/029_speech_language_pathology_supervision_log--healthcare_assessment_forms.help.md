# Speech Language Pathology Supervision Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Speech Language Pathology (SLP) supervisors to document supervisory activities and interactions with their supervisees. It helps track supervisee details, session times, activities, feedback, and supervisor signature, ensuring effective supervision and progress monitoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Supervisee Details" to indicate whether you are supervising the individual.
2. Enter the supervisee's name in the "Supervisee Name" field.
3. Record the start and end times of the session using the "Session Start Time" and "Session End Time" fields.
4. Describe the activities performed during the session in the "Session Activities" field.
5. Provide feedback to the supervisee in the "Supervisee Feedback" field.
6. If applicable, indicate your signature in the "Supervisor Signature" field.
7. Enter the date of the session in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisee Details** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple, required: false): Check this box to indicate whether you are supervising the individual.
* **Supervisee Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the supervisee.
* **Session Start Time** (`time`, required: false): Record the start time of the session.
* **Session End Time** (`time`, required: false): Record the end time of the session.
* **Session Activities** (`text`, required: false): Describe the activities performed during the session.
* **Supervisee Feedback** (`text`, required: false): Provide feedback to the supervisee.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`Yes/No`, select_one, required: false): If applicable, indicate your signature to confirm supervision.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the session.
