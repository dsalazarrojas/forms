# online_diagnostic_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about a patient's online diagnostic evaluation. It's intended for use in a healthcare setting to collect data from patients or individuals who are undergoing online diagnostic evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **name** in the first field. This is a required field, so make sure to fill it in.
2. Enter your **phone number** in the second field. This field is also required.
3. Provide your **email address** in the third field. This field is required as well.
4. In the **medical_history** field, describe your medical history, including any past illnesses, allergies, or surgeries.
5. List any **medications** you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
6. Indicate your **employment status**, such as working or unemployed.
7. Fill in your **education level**, including any degrees or certifications you have earned.
8. Enter your **income** level, if applicable.
9. For the **anxiety** field, indicate if you are currently experiencing anxiety.
10. Similarly, for **depression**, indicate if you are currently experiencing depression.
11. Describe any **trauma** you have experienced, if applicable.
12. If there is a **family history** of medical issues, describe it in this field.
13. For **family_members_with_diagnosis**, list any immediate family members who have been diagnosed with a medical condition.
14. Indicate your **current_provider** if you have one.
15. List any **previous_providers** you have seen in the past.
16. For **alcohol_use**, describe your level of alcohol use, if applicable.
17. If you use **drugs**, describe your level of use, if applicable.
18. Finally, provide an **Emergency Contact** name and phone number for the healthcare provider to reach out to in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number where the healthcare provider can contact you.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **medical_history**: Describe any past illnesses, allergies, or surgeries you have had.
* **medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **employment_status**: Indicate your current employment status, such as working or unemployed.
* **education**: Enter your education level, including any degrees or certifications you have earned.
* **income**: Enter your income level, if applicable.
* **anxiety**: Indicate if you are currently experiencing anxiety.
* **depression**: Indicate if you are currently experiencing depression.
* **trauma**: Describe any trauma you have experienced, if applicable.
* **family_history**: Describe any family history of medical issues.
* **family_members_with_diagnosis**: List any immediate family members who have been diagnosed with a medical condition.
* **current_provider**: Indicate your current healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **previous_providers**: List any previous healthcare providers you have seen.
* **alcohol_use**: Describe your level of alcohol use, if applicable.
* **drug_use**: Describe your level of drug use, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide an Emergency Contact name and phone number, in case the healthcare provider needs to contact someone for you.
