# Sleep And Stress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals assess patients' sleep quality, stress levels, and use of relaxation techniques.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and understand the context.
2. Answer each question carefully, considering how you would rate your sleep quality, sleep duration, level of stress, and use of relaxation techniques.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction (Page 1)
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, optional): The introduction provides background information about the form and its purpose.

### How would you rate your sleep quality (Page 2)
* **How would you rate your sleep quality** (`sleep_quality`, number, optional): Rate your sleep quality on a numerical scale, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Sleep duration (hours) (Page 3)
* **Sleep duration (hours)** (`sleep_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you typically sleep each night.

### Level of stress (Page 4)
* **Level of stress** (`stress_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of stress you are experiencing from the options High, Low.

### Stress factors (Page 5)
* **Stress factors** (`stress_factors`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the options Yes, No. This question allows you to specify multiple stress factors affecting your sleep.

### What kind of relaxation tech do you use (Page 6)
* **What kind of relaxation tech do you use** (`relaxation_tech_used`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the options Yes, No. This question allows you to specify multiple relaxation techniques you use.

### Wake-up time (Page 7)
* **Wake-up time** (`wake_up_time`, time, optional): Enter your wake-up time in 24-hour format (e.g., 07:00).

### Morning feeling (Page 8)
* **Morning feeling** (`morning_feeling`, select_one, optional): Choose how you feel in the morning from the options Yes, No.

### Sleep stages (Page 9)
* **Sleep stages** (`sleep_stages`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the options Yes, No.
