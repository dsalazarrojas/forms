# Neuroprotection Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a patient's current neuroprotection status and identify potential risks or needs for further evaluation. This information will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about the patient's care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly. If a question does not apply to you, simply check the "No" or "Not Applicable" option.
2. If you have any symptoms or risk factors, select all that apply from the list provided.
3. If you have any notes or comments about your lifestyle that you'd like to share with your healthcare provider, use the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Symptoms
• **Symptoms** (Multiple Selection): Check all the boxes that apply to your current health status:
	+ Yes: I am experiencing the following symptoms.
	+ No: This box does not apply to me.
	+ (Example: Headaches, difficulty concentrating, memory loss)

### Risk Factors
• **Risk Factors** (Text Field): Describe any factors that may put your neuroprotection at risk, such as recent head trauma, medication use, or medical conditions. (Example: recent head trauma, high blood pressure)

### Lifestyle
• **Lifestyle** (Select One): How would you rate your overall lifestyle in terms of neuroprotection?
	+ Yes: I have a healthy lifestyle.
	+ No: I do not have a healthy lifestyle.

### Notes
• **Notes** (Text Field): Share any additional comments or questions you have about your neuroprotection status or concerns you have about your care.
