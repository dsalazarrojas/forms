# Health Assessment For Chronic Conditions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's chronic conditions, symptoms, and lifestyle. The data collected will be used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's health status and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Details** section, providing your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
2. Answer **Chronic Conditions** with "Yes" or "No", indicating whether you have any chronic conditions.
3. Select **Symptoms** that apply to you, choosing "Yes" or "No" for each symptom.
4. In the **Lifestyle** section, provide any additional information about your lifestyle that may be relevant to your health.
5. In the **Medical History** section, provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your healthcare professional to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Details** (patient_details, text, required): Please enter your name and date of birth to help our healthcare professionals understand your identity and provide personalized care.
  - **Note:** This information is required.
- **Chronic Conditions** (chronic_conditions, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have any chronic conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or cardiovascular disease.
- **Symptoms** (symptoms, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for each symptom that applies to you, such as fatigue, pain, or difficulty sleeping.
- **Lifestyle** (lifestyle, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your lifestyle that may be relevant to your health, such as your diet, exercise habits, or stress levels.
  - **Note:** This information is not required, but it can help our healthcare professionals understand you better.
- **Medical History** (medical_history, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your healthcare professional to know, such as previous surgeries or medications.
  - **Note:** This information is not required, but it can help our healthcare professionals provide more informed care.
