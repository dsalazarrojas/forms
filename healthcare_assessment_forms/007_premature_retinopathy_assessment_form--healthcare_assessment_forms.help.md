# Premature Retinopathy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premature Retinopathy Assessment Form is a healthcare-specific form designed to gather information on the severity of premature retinopathy in patients. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the condition's impact on the patient's vision and determine the best course of action for proper care and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's personal information (First Name, Last Name, Guardian Name, and Emergency Contact Name)
2. Select the patient's address from the available options
3. Choose the correct medical condition (No issue, Mild, Moderate, or Severe)
4. Select the treatment received or planned (None, Medication, Surgery, or Other)
5. Check the consent status for the assessment (True, False, or Unknown)
6. Provide a brief note on the consent explanation
7. Enter the patient's retina position and depth
8. Select the assessment result (No issue, Possible issue, or Issue)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter the patient's first name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter the patient's last name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Guardian Name** (Required): Enter the name of the patient's guardian or responsible adult.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Address** (Required): Enter the patient's address as it appears on their official documents.
* **Medical Condition** (Required): Select the correct medical condition from the available options (No issue, Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
* **Treatment** (Required): Select the treatment received or planned for the patient from the available options (None, Medication, Surgery, or Other).
* **Consent Given** (Required): Select the correct status of the patient's consent (True, False, or Unknown).
* **Consent Explained** (Required): Provide a brief note on the patient's consent explanation.
* **Retina Position** (Required): Enter the patient's retina position as it appears on medical documentation.
* **Retina Depth** (Required): Enter the patient's retina depth as it appears on medical documentation.
* **Assessment Result** (Required): Select the correct assessment result from the available options (No issue, Possible issue, or Issue).
