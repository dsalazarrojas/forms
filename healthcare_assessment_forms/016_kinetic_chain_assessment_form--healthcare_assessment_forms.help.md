# Kinetic Chain Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kinetic Chain Assessment Form is a tool used in a healthcare setting to collect information on a client's kinetic chain, which is a series of movements that connect the joints, muscles, and bones of the body. The form gathers data on the client's movements, observations, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Details (1)**: A text field for documenting the client's assessment details.
* **Client Information (2)**: A text field for collecting client information, such as name, address, and contact numbers.
* **Movement Observation (3)**: A note field for recording notable movements or observations.
* **Assessment Date (4)**: A date field for documenting the assessment date.
* **Assessment Time (5)**: A time field for documenting the assessment time.
* **Phone Number (6)**: A text field for collecting phone number information.
* **Email (7)**: A text field for collecting email information.
* **Select One (8)**: A select-one field for choosing one option from a list of options.
* **Select Multiple (9)**: A select-multiple field for choosing multiple options.
* **Decimal (10)**: A number field for collecting a decimal value.
* **Integer (11)**: A number field for collecting an integer value.
* **Text (12)**: A text field for collecting additional information.
* **Note (13)**: A note field for recording any additional comments or notes.
* **Date (14)**: A date field for documenting a specific date value.
* **Time (15)**: A time field for documenting a specific time value.
* **Email 2 (16)**: A text field for collecting email information.
