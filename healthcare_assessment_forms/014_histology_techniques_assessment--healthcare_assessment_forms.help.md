# Histology Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the histology skills and knowledge of laboratory technicians. It assesses their proficiency and experience in histology techniques, including lab background, equipment, methods, and proficiency level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and provide accurate information about your experience and skills.
2. Fill out the form completely, as missing information may affect the assessment.
3. Select the most accurate options for each field, and if unsure, select "More training needed" for skills that require improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lab Training Background** (`lab_training_background`, text, optional): Briefly describe your background and experience in histology training.
* **Lab Equipment** (`lab_equipment`, text, optional): List the equipment you are familiar with, such as microscopes, microtomes, and staining equipment.
* **Histology Methods** (`histology_methods`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Microtome**: Experience with a microtome, which is a device used for sectioning tissue samples.
	+ **Tissue sectioning**: Ability to section tissue samples accurately and consistently.
	+ **Embedding and staining**: Knowledge of embedding and staining techniques for histology.
	+ **Staining techniques**: Experience with various staining techniques, such as H&E (Hematoxylin and Eosin).
	+ **Imaging and visualization**: Knowledge of imaging and visualization techniques for histology, including microscopic examination.
* **Microscope Quality** (`microscope_quality`, select_one, optional):
	+ **New**: Familiarity with a recently purchased or installed microscope.
	+ **Old**: Familiarity with an older microscope.
	+ **Very old**: Familiarity with an extremely old microscope.
* **Sample Preparation** (`sample_preparation`, text, optional): Describe the preparation methods you are familiar with, including fixation, dehydration, and embedding techniques.
* **Staining Equipment** (`staining_equipment`, text, optional): List the staining equipment you are familiar with, such as H&E staining equipment.
* **Sectioning** (`sectioning`, number, optional): Enter the number of sections you have prepared in the past month.
* **Section Thickness** (`section_thickness`, number, optional): Enter the average thickness of sections you typically cut.
* **Histology Proficiency** (`histology_proficiency`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Expert**: High level of proficiency in histology techniques.
	+ **Intermediate**: Moderate level of proficiency in histology techniques.
	+ **Beginner**: Basic level of proficiency in histology techniques.
* **Histology Certification** (`histology_certification`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Hold a certification in histology.
	+ **No**: Do not hold a certification in histology.
* **Lab Management** (`lab_management`, text, optional): Describe your experience with lab management, including supervising and leading a team.
* **Lab Supervision** (`lab_supervision`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Supervise lab activities and oversee technician work.
	+ **No**: Do not supervise lab activities.
* **Histology Training Needs** (`histology_training_needs`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **More training needed**: Identify areas where you need additional training or guidance.
	+ **No need for more training**: Suggest areas where you feel you have sufficient knowledge and skills.

Note: This form is designed to be used for internal assessment and feedback, so please provide honest and accurate information about your skills and experience.
