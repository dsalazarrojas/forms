# Stool Consistency Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stool Consistency Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used to collect information about a patient's stool consistency and bowel movements. It is used to assess the patient's gastrointestinal health and identify potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Intake Page with any relevant information.
2. Answer the questions on the Bowel Frequency page.
3. Select the correct stool consistency on the Stool Consistency page.
4. Describe any symptoms you are experiencing on the Symptoms page.
5. Answer questions about any medical conditions, medications, or blood test results on the Medical Conditions, Medication, and Blood Test Results pages.
6. Enter any relevant medical history, procedures, or test results on the Medical History, Medical Procedure, and Medical Results pages.
7. Enter any follow-up information on the Follow-up Schedule and Follow-up Notes pages.
8. Add any additional comments or notes on the Additional Comments page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Page** (1, Intake Page, Optional): This page is for general information about the patient. Fill out any relevant details about the patient's medical history, current condition, or other relevant information.
* **Bowel Frequency** (2, Bowel Frequency, Optional): How often do you experience bowel movements in the past 24 hours? Select one of the options: Less than 2 times, 2-3 times, 4-6 times, More than 6 times.
* **Stool Consistency** (3, Stool Consistency, Optional): What is the consistency of your stool? Select one of the options: Normal, Loose, Pellet, Mucous, Undigested, Other.
* **Symptoms** (4, Symptoms, Optional): Please describe any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Medical Conditions** (5, Medical Conditions, Optional): Have you been diagnosed with any medical conditions related to bowel health? Select one of the options: Diarrhea, Constipation, Blood in stool, Mucous, Other.
* **Medication** (6, Medication, Optional): Are you taking any medication that may affect bowel health? Select one of the options: True, False, Unsure.
* **Follow-up** (7, Follow-up, Optional): Enter the date of your last follow-up appointment or check-up.
* **Time of Last Stool** (8, Time of Last Stool, Optional): Enter the date of your last bowel movement.
* **Time of Last Stool** (9, Time of Last Stool, Optional): Enter the time of your last bowel movement.
* **Time of Last Stool Note** (10, Time of Last Stool Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about your last bowel movement.
* **Stool Form Submissions** (11, Stool Form Submissions, Optional): How often do you submit stool forms in the past 30 days? Select one of the options: Less than 10, 10-20, 21-30, More than 30, None.
* **Bowel Movement Frequency** (12, Bowel Movement Frequency, Optional): How often do you experience bowel movements in the past 24 hours? Select one of the options: Less than 2 times, 2-3 times, 4-6 times, More than 6 times.
* **Blood Test Results** (13, Blood Test Results, Optional): What are the results of your blood test? Select one of the options: Positive, Negative, Pending, Other.
* **Blood Test Date** (14, Blood Test Date, Optional): Enter the date of your blood test.
* **Medication Name** (15, Medication Name, Optional): Enter the name of any medication you are taking.
* **Medication Dosage** (16, Medication Dosage, Optional): Enter the dosage of any medication you are taking.
* **Medical History** (17, Medical History, Optional): Enter any relevant medical history.
* **Medical Procedure** (18, Medical Procedure, Optional): Enter any relevant medical procedure information.
* **Medical Results** (19, Medical Results, Optional): Enter any relevant medical test results.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (20, Follow-up Schedule, Optional): Enter any follow-up schedule information.
* **Follow-up Notes** (21, Follow-up Notes, Optional): Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Medical Provider** (22, Medical Provider, Optional): Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Diagnosis** (23, Medical Diagnosis, Optional): Enter any medical diagnosis information.
* **Medical Notes** (24, Medical Notes, Optional): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (25, Follow-up Schedule, Optional): Enter any follow-up schedule information.
* **Additional Comments** (26, Additional Comments, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
