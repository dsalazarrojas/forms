# Population Health Analytics Project Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Population Health Analytics Project Evaluation Form is designed to collect project data and feedback from teams working on projects related to population health analytics. This form helps to evaluate and document project objectives, outcomes, challenges, and other relevant information to facilitate future reference and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out each section with accurate and clear information about your project.
3. For sections with multiple options, select the relevant option that best describes your project status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Objectives** (`project_objectives`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of your project's objectives, what you aimed to achieve.
* **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the outcomes or results of your project, including any significant achievements or milestones.
* **Project Challenges** (`project_challenges`, text, optional):
	+ List any challenges or difficulties you encountered during the project.
* **Future Improvements** (`future_improvements`, text, optional):
	+ Share any suggestions or ideas for improving the project or related processes.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional):
	+ List the names and roles of the people involved in the project.
* **Team Skills** (`team_skills`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the skills and expertise required for the project, including any specializations or areas of focus.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply from the available options: Active, Completed, In Progress.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, optional):
	+ Provide the name and contact information of your project lead.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Share any additional feedback or comments about the form or the project.

# Tips

* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
