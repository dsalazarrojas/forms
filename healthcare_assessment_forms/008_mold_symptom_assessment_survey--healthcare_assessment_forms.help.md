# Mold Symptom Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess symptoms associated with mold exposure. The goal is to gather information about the user's current health status, exposure concerns, and any symptoms they are experiencing. This data will help healthcare professionals understand the severity of mold exposure and guide further action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions in the Symptom Report page.
2. In the Exposure Concerns page, select all the concerns that apply.
3. In the Next Action Plan page, provide a brief description of any recommended actions or treatments for the user to consider.
4. In the Exposure Precautions page, report any precautions the user is taking to prevent further mold exposure.
5. In the Follow-up Status page, report the results of any follow-up actions or treatments.
6. In the Mold Symptoms page, select one or more symptoms that the user is currently experiencing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptom Report (Page 1)**:
	+ This section is where you will report any symptoms you are currently experiencing related to mold exposure.
	+ Please describe any discomfort, pain, or health issues you are experiencing.
* **Exposure Concerns (Page 2)**:
	+ If you have any allergies, asthma, or other health concerns related to mold, select them here.
	+ This will help us understand any potential risks or complications.
* **Follow-up (Page 3)**:
	+ If you have a follow-up appointment or treatment scheduled, enter the date here.
* **Exposure History (Page 4)**:
	+ Provide any information about your recent exposure to mold, including the location and duration of exposure.
* **Next Action Plan (Page 5)**:
	+ Suggest any steps or treatments you think would be helpful for managing your mold exposure symptoms.
* **Exposure Precautions (Page 6)**:
	+ Report any precautions you are taking to prevent further mold exposure.
* **Follow-up Status (Page 7)**:
	+ Update us on the status of any follow-up actions or treatments.
* **Mold Symptoms (Page 8)**:
	+ Select one or more symptoms you are currently experiencing related to mold exposure.
	+ These symptoms will help us understand the severity of your exposure.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any symptom or concern, please ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to support healthcare professionals in understanding your mold exposure symptoms. Your input will help us provide better care.
