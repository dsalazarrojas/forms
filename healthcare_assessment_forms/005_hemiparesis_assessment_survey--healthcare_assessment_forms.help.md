# hemiparesis_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hemiparesis_assessment_survey form is a tool used to collect assessments related to hemiparesis, a condition that affects the brain and nervous system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the internal reasoning block above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptom Severity** (`symptom_severity`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to select all the symptoms that apply to you, such as weakness, numbness, or loss of coordination.
* **Functional Impact** (`functional_impact`, text, required): Describe how your hemiparesis condition affects your daily life.
* **Mobility Needs** (`mobility_needs`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you require assistive devices or 'No' if you do not.
* **Pain Intensity** (`pain_intensity`, number, required): Enter a number between 0 and 10 to indicate your pain level.
* **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you experience emotional changes or 'No' if you do not.
* **Cognitive State** (`cognitive_state`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you experience cognitive changes or 'No' if you do not.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, number, required): Enter a number between 0 and 10 to indicate your sleep quality.
* **Social Interactions** (`social_interactions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the social interactions you have difficulty with, such as communicating or participating in social events.
* **Mobility Status** (`mobility_status`, select_one, required): Choose 'Active' if you are able to move freely or 'Inactive' if you are not.
* **Overall Functional Status** (`overall_functional_status`, select_one, required): Choose 'Active' if you are able to perform daily activities or 'Inactive' if you are not.
* **Mobility Needs Assistive Aids** (`mobility_needs_assistive_aid`, select_multiple, required): Select all the assistive aids you require for mobility, such as wheelchairs or walkers.
* **Emotional Support System** (`emotional_support_system`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you have a support system for your emotional well-being or 'No' if you do not.
