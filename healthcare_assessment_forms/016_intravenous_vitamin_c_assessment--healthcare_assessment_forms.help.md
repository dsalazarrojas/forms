# Intravenous Vitamin C Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather important information from patients requiring intravenous Vitamin C therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the therapy you are requesting, including any relevant health concerns.
2. Give us any relevant health details you have.
3. Choose a preferred appointment time that suits you best.
4. If you have any contact information, such as an email or phone number, please provide them.
5. Select a date and time for the appointment.
6. Add any additional patient notes for our team's reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Therapy Details** (iv_therapy_details): This field is where you can provide any details about the therapy you are requesting. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Patient Health Details** (patient_health_details): In this field, please provide any relevant health information you have. This will help us better understand your needs.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (preferred_time): Choose a time that suits you best for your appointment.
* **Doctor Name**: If you have a preferred doctor, you can mention their name here. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Appointment Date** (appointment_date): Select a date for your appointment. This will help us schedule your appointment accordingly.
* **Patient Email** (patient_email): If you have an email address, please provide it here. This will help us contact you with appointment reminders or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): If you have a phone number, please provide it here. This will help us contact you with appointment reminders or updates.
* **Appointment Time** (appointment_time): Select a time for your appointment. Please note that this may conflict with the preferred time you chose in the previous field.
* **Patient Notes** (patient_notes): If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please add it here. This will help us better understand your needs.
