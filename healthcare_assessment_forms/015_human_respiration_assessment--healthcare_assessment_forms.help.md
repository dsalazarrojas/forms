## Human Respiration Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Respiration Assessment form is designed to collect information from healthcare professionals and patients to assess the respiratory system's functionality and potential risks. This form helps in identifying symptoms, risk factors, and medical history related to the respiratory system, which can aid in providing appropriate care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the `User Name` field. This field is used to identify you as the person being assessed.
2.  Describe any symptoms you are experiencing in the `Symptoms` field. This can include coughing, shortness of breath, or any other breathing difficulties you may be experiencing.
3.  List any risk factors you are aware of in the `Risk Factors` field. This can include smoking, air pollution, or any other environmental factors that may affect your breathing.
4.  Provide a list of any prescribed medications you are currently taking in the `Medication` field.
5.  Describe your medical history, including any relevant illnesses or procedures in the `Medical History` field.
6.  List any allergies you have in the `Allergies` field.
7.  Enter the name of your primary care provider or emergency contact in the `Emergency Contact` field.
8.  Provide the phone number of your primary care provider or emergency contact in the `Contact Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name**: (`user_name`, text, required/optional: false)
    This field is used to identify you as the person being assessed. Please enter your full name as it appears on your medical record.
*   **Symptoms**: (`symptoms`, text, required/optional: false)
    Describe any breathing difficulties you are experiencing, such as coughing, shortness of breath, or any other symptoms that may be affecting your respiratory system.
*   **Risk Factors**: (`risk_factors`, text, required/optional: false)
    List any factors that may be affecting your breathing, such as smoking, air pollution, or exposure to chemicals.
*   **Medication**: (`medication`, text, required/optional: false)
    List any prescribed medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency of use.
*   **Medical History**: (`medical_history`, text, required/optional: false)
    Describe any relevant medical history, including illnesses, procedures, or previous injuries that may be affecting your respiratory system.
*   **Allergies**: (`allergies`, text, required/optional: false)
    List any allergies you have, including any medications or substances that trigger allergic reactions.
*   **Emergency Contact**: (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional: false)
    Enter the name of your primary care provider or emergency contact, in case of any unexpected medical emergencies.
*   **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional: false)
    Provide the phone number of your primary care provider or emergency contact, so that we can quickly contact them in case of an emergency.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your symptoms and medical history.
*   List any relevant medical documents or test results that may be useful for your healthcare provider.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask for help or clarification.
