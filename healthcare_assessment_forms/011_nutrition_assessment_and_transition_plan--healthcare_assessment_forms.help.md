# Nutrition Assessment And Transition Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nutrition Assessment And Transition Plan" form is designed to collect essential information about a client's dietary needs and preferences. This information will be used to develop a tailored nutrition plan that meets their specific requirements for optimal health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that the information provided is up-to-date and relevant.
2. For each field, provide a brief but thorough answer to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully and verify the information with the client before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_nutrition_intake**: This is a free-text field where clients can provide any relevant background information about their nutrition status, including any medical conditions, food intolerances, or allergies.
* **dietary_goals**: Select one or more options that best describe your short-term and long-term dietary goals. Options include:
	+ "Weight loss"
	+ "Weight gain"
	+ "Muscle gain"
	+ "Maintenance"
* **dietary_restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions you have, such as food intolerances or preferences, in this field.
* **food_intolerances**: Select any food intolerances you experience, such as:
	+ "Dairy"
	+ "Gluten"
	+ "Nuts"
	+ "Soy"
	+ "Fish"
	+ "Shellfish"
	+ "Eggs"
	+ "Crustaceans"
* **medical_conditions**: List any medical conditions you have or are currently managing, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **allergies**: List any allergies you have, including food allergies.
* **nutrition_goals**: Select one or more options that best describe your nutrition goals, including:
	+ "Weight loss"
	+ "Weight gain"
	+ "Muscle gain"
	+ "Maintenance"
* **consent_date**: Provide the date you have reviewed and consented to the form.
* **consent_time**: Provide the time you have reviewed and consented to the form.
* **consent_signature**: Sign and date your consent to the form.
* **client_signature**: Sign and date your consent as the client.
* **consent_time**: Provide the time you have reviewed and consented to the form.
* **client_signature**: Sign and date your consent as the client.

Note: The **client_nutrition_intake** field should be reviewed carefully for completeness and accuracy before proceeding. This information will be used as the foundation for your tailored nutrition plan.

Tips:
* Please ensure that the information collected is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before proceeding.
* Verify the information with the client before proceeding.
