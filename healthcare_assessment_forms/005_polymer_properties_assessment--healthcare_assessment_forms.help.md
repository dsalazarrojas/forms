# Polymer Properties Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document various properties of polymers, including physical and chemical information, for evaluation and assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the relevant fields with the requested information. The fields are divided into several sections, each with its own label and description. Please make sure to fill in each field accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Sample ID
* **Sample ID** (`sample_id`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the sample.

### 2. Sample Type
* **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of polymer (Polypropylene, Polyester, Polyamide).

### 3. Density
* **Density** (`density`, number, optional): Enter the density of the polymer.

### 4. Melting Point
* **Melting Point** (`melting_point`, number, optional): Enter the melting point of the polymer.

### 5. Hardness
* **Hardness** (`hardness`, number, optional): Enter the hardness of the polymer.

### 6. Tensile Strength
* **Tensile Strength** (`tensile_strength`, number, optional): Enter the tensile strength of the polymer.

### 7. Yield Strength
* **Yield Strength** (`yield_strength`, number, optional): Enter the yield strength of the polymer.

### 8. Chemical Behavior
* **Chemical Behavior** (`chemical_behavior`, select_one, optional): Select the chemical behavior of the polymer (Good, Fair, Poor).

### 9. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the polymer.

### 10. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.

### 11. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.

### 12. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.

### 13. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.

### 14. Physical Description
* **Physical Description** (`physical_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the physical properties of the polymer.

### 15. Mechanical Description
* **Mechanical Description** (`mechanical_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the mechanical properties of the polymer.

### 16. Chemical Description
* **Chemical Description** (`chemical_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the chemical properties of the polymer.

### 17. Remarks
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): Enter any additional remarks or notes.

### 18. Sample Weight
* **Sample Weight** (`sample_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the sample.

### 19. Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

### 20. Reference
* **Reference** (`reference`, text, optional): Enter any reference or citation for the assessment.

### 21. Author
* **Author** (`author`, text, optional): Enter the name of the author who assessed the polymer.

### 22. Version
* **Version** (`version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the assessment.

### 23. Revision Date
* **Revision Date** (`revision_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the revision.

### 24. Revised By
* **Revised By** (`revision_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who revised the assessment.

### 25. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes after the revision.

Please note that some fields are optional, and you should only fill in the information that is relevant to your assessment. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
