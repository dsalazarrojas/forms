# Healthcare Facility Emergency Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Healthcare Facility Emergency Assessment Form is used to collect information about a healthcare facility's emergency response capabilities in the event of an incident. This form helps the facility assess its readiness and potential vulnerabilities, enabling them to prepare for and respond to emergencies effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form sections**: Each section is designed to gather specific information about the facility's emergency response capabilities.
2. **Answer each question truthfully**: Provide accurate and detailed information about the facility's emergency response capabilities.
3. **Use the provided fields**: Each field is specifically designed to collect a particular type of information. For example, use the "select_one" field for single-choice questions and "text" fields for longer descriptions.
4. **Check the required fields**: Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
* **Emergency Contact**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Provide the name of the contact person responsible for emergency response.
* **Incident Type**: (<type>, select_one, required/true) - Select the type of incident (e.g., Medical Emergency, Fire, Other).
* **Incident Date**: (<date>, date, required/optional) - Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: (<time>, time, required/optional) - Enter the time of the incident.
* **Facility Condition**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Describe the incident conditions.
* **Urgent Needs**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - List the urgent needs during the incident.
* **Facility Capacity**: (<name>, number, required/optional) - Enter the facility's maximum capacity.
* **Patient Capacity**: (<name>, number, required/optional) - Enter the facility's patient capacity.
* **Medical Team Size**: (<name>, number, required/optional) - Enter the number of medical staff trained for emergency response.
* **Staff Training**: (<name>, select_multiple, required/true) - Select the medical staff training types (e.g., CPR/First Aid, Basic Life Support, Advanced Life Support).
* **Facility Equipment**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - List the available facility equipment during emergencies.
* **Incident Reporting**: (<date>, date, required/optional) - Enter the date and time of the incident reporting.
* **Reporting Person**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Provide the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Contact**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the contact information of the reporting person.
* **Incident Description**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Describe the incident in detail.
* **Incident Location**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the location of the incident.
* **Weather Conditions**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Describe the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Facility Location**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the facility's location.
* **Incident Injuries**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - List the injuries or damage to people, property, or environment.
* **Facility Phone**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Facility Email**: (<name>, email, required/optional) - Enter the facility's email address.
* **Facility Address**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the facility's address.
* **Facility Website**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter the facility's website.
* **Facility Notes**: (<name>, text, required/optional) - Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility.
