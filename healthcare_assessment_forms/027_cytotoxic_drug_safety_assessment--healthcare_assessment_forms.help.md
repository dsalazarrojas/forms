# cytotoxic_drug_safety_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the safety assessment of cytotoxic drugs. It's essential to ensure that this form is completed accurately and thoroughly to prevent errors and ensure patient well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Assessor field with the name and title of the healthcare professional who is completing the form.
2. Enter the Date of Occurrence field with the date the cytotoxic drug was administered or handled.
3. Select the correct Drug Type from the provided options.
4. Answer the Safety Measure question by selecting one of the options provided.
5. Answer the Drug Administered question by selecting one of the options provided.
6. Select the Incidents option that best describes the incidents associated with the cytotoxic drug.
7. Enter any additional information about the drug administration or handling in the Drag and Drop field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessor**: Enter the name and title of the healthcare professional who is completing this form.
* **Date of Occurrence**: Enter the date on which the cytotoxic drug was administered or handled.
* **Drug Type**: Select the type of cytotoxic drug that was administered or handled from the provided options.
* **Safety Measure**: Select one of the options to indicate the level of safety measure taken for the drug.
* **Drug Administered**: Select one of the options to indicate whether the drug was administered to the patient or not.
* **Incidents**: Select one or more options that best describe the incidents associated with the cytotoxic drug.
* **Drag and Drop**: Enter any additional information about the drug administration or handling in this field.
