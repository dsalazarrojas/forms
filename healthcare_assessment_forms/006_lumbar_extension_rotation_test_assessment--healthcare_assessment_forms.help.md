# Lumbar Extension Rotation Test Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lumbar Extension Rotation Test Assessment is a comprehensive evaluation tool used to assess the lumbar spine and surrounding muscles. This form is designed to gather information about a patient's clinical findings, diagnosis, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Details page with the relevant information about the patient.
2. On the Clinical Findings page, write down any clinical findings related to the patient's condition.
3. On the Intake Details page, enter any additional information about the patient's intake.
4. On the Clinical Finding Notes page, provide any notes about the clinical findings.
5. On the Clinical Finding Description page, provide a description of the clinical findings.
6. On the Clinical Finding Diagnosis page, enter the diagnosis related to the clinical findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details (patient_details)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant medical history.
* **Clinical Findings (clinical_finding)**: Describe any clinical findings related to the patient's condition, such as pain, numbness, tingling, or other symptoms.
* **Intake Details (intake_details)**: Enter any additional information about the patient's intake, such as the date and time of the assessment, and the patient's presenting complaint.
* **Clinical Finding Notes (clinical_finding_notes)**: Provide any additional notes about the clinical findings, such as the patient's response to treatment or any other relevant information.
* **Clinical Finding Description (clinical_finding_description)**: Provide a detailed description of the clinical findings, including any relevant medical terminology or jargon.
* **Clinical Finding Diagnosis (clinical_finding_diagnosis)**: Enter the diagnosis related to the clinical findings, taking into account any relevant medical information or research. 

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when providing information to ensure accurate assessment and diagnosis.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or healthcare professional for guidance.
* Remember to review and update the form regularly to ensure it accurately reflects the patient's condition and treatment progress. 

Note: This form is for educational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for actual medical practice. Consult a healthcare professional for proper assessment and diagnosis.
