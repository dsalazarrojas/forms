# Concentration And Energy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concentration And Energy Assessment form is a tool used by healthcare professionals to evaluate a patient's concentration and energy levels. This assessment helps identify areas of concern and provides a basis for recommendations or referrals for further treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's full name in the "Patient name" field.
2. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Assessment date" field.
3. Rate your sleep quality in the past week using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Select your typical daily energy levels from the options: High, Moderate, or Low.
5. Rate your ability to focus during tasks using the options: Strong, Adequate, or Poor.
6. If applicable, list any factors that affect your energy levels (e.g., medications, stress, diet, or other factors).
7. Provide any suggested interventions or referrals in the "Recommendations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Sleep quality** (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required): Rate your sleep quality in the past week. Select from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Daily energy levels** (`daily_energy_levels`, select_one, required): Choose your typical daily energy levels from High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Concentration ability** (`concentration_ability`, select_one, required): Rate your ability to focus during tasks. Select from Strong, Adequate, or Poor.
* **Factors affecting energy** (`factors_affecting_energy`, text, optional): If applicable, list any factors that affect your energy levels.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any suggested interventions or referrals.

## Tips
Remember to be honest and thorough in your ratings and input. This form is for your well-being and to help healthcare professionals understand your situation better.
