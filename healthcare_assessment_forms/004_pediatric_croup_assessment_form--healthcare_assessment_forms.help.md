# Pediatric Croup Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Croup Assessment Form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to quickly and accurately assess and diagnose croup in children. It helps gather vital information about the patient's condition, medical history, and treatment administered.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Provide a brief description of the chief complaint in the "Chief Complaint" field.
3. Record the patient's medical history, including any allergies or previous medical conditions, in the "Medical History" field.
4. Enter any relevant allergy information in the "Allergy Information" field.
5. Report any respiratory findings, such as a high-pitched cry or difficulty breathing, in the "Respiratory Findings" field.
6. List any medications administered during the visit in the "Medication List" field.
7. Record the treatment administered to the patient in the "Treatment Administered" field.
8. Note any follow-up information, including the follow-up status and date, in the "Follow-Up" section.
9. Select a diagnosis from the options provided in the "Diagnosis" field.
10. List any medications administered to the patient, if applicable, in the "Medications Administered" field.
11. If any other medications were administered, enter them in the "Other Medication Administered" field.
12. Record the follow-up status and date in the "Follow-Up Status" and "Follow-Up Date" fields, respectively.
13. Enter the physician's name and title in the "Physician Name" and "Physician Title" fields.
14. Record the location where the visit took place in the "Location" field.
15. Add any additional notes about the visit in the "Note" field.
16. Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email, in the "Phone Number" and "Email" fields, respectively.
17. Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable, in the "Medical Record Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1):** Enter the patient's name, age, and ID number, if applicable.
* **Chief Complaint (2):** Describe the patient's chief complaint, such as a high-pitched cry or difficulty breathing.
* **Medical History (3):** Record the patient's medical history, including any allergies or previous medical conditions.
* **Allergy Information (4):** Enter any relevant allergy information.
* **Respiratory Findings (5):** Report any respiratory findings, such as a high-pitched cry or difficulty breathing.
* **Medication List (6):** List any medications administered during the visit.
* **Treatment Administered (7):** Record the treatment administered to the patient.
* **Follow-Up (8):** Record the follow-up status and date.
* **Diagnosis (9):** Select a diagnosis from the options provided.
* **Medications Administered (10):** List any medications administered to the patient, if applicable.
* **Other Medication Administered (11):** If any other medications were administered, enter them here.
* **Follow-Up Status (12):** Select the follow-up status.
* **Follow-Up Date (13):** Record the follow-up date.
* **Physician Name (15):** Enter the physician's name.
* **Physician Title (16):** Enter the physician's title.
* **Location (17):** Record the location where the visit took place.
* **Note (18):** Add any additional notes about the visit.
* **Phone Number (19):** Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email (20):** Enter the patient's contact email.
* **Medical Record Number (21):** Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
* **Patient ID (22):** Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Patient Age (23):** Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient Gender (24):** Select the patient's gender.
* **Date Time (25):** Record the date and time of the visit.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure the patient receives proper treatment.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with a healthcare professional.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or the hospital administration.
