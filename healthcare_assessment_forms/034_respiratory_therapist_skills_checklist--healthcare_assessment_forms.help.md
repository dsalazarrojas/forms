# Respiratory Therapist Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and qualifications of a respiratory therapist. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the therapist's experience, licensure, core skills, equipment proficiency, hiring/staffing/training decisions, and other skills. This form is likely to be used in a healthcare setting to evaluate the qualifications of a respiratory therapist for a specific job or role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Therapist Experience" page and choose the relevant option that matches your experience level.
2. Choose your current licensure from the list provided.
3. Select your core skills from the list provided.
4. Select your equipment proficiency from the list provided.
5. Choose the relevant option for hiring/staffing/training decisions.
6. If you have any other relevant skills, please specify them in the "Other Skills" field.
7. Finally, add any comments you may have in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Therapist Experience** (`therapist_experience`, `note`, required: false): Select the level of experience you have as a respiratory therapist. This will help us understand your background and qualifications.
* **Licensure** (`licensure`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your current licensure from the list provided. This will help us understand your certification level.
* **Core Skills** (`core_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the core skills you have as a respiratory therapist. These skills include Cardiac Pulmonary Rehabilitation, Pulmonary Rehabilitation, Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation, and Ventilatory Care.
* **Equipment Proficiency** (`equipment_proficiency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the equipment you are proficient in. This will help us understand your ability to use equipment in a clinical setting.
* **Hiring/Staffing/Training** (`hiring_staffing_training`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the relevant option for your hiring, staffing, or training decisions. This will help us understand your decision-making process.
* **Other Skills** (`other_skills`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other relevant skills, please specify them here. This will help us understand any additional qualifications you have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments you may have about your qualifications or experience. This will help us understand any additional information about your skills and experience.
