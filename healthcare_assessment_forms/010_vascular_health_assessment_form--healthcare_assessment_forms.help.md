# Vascular Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's vascular health status, covering various aspects such as general information, symptoms, risk factors, medical history, medication usage, test results, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required information. You may choose to fill out all fields or skip the ones that are not marked as "required."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (General Information, text, required:false): Provide basic details about yourself, such as name, contact information, or any other relevant personal data.
* **Symptoms** (Symptoms, select_multiple, required:false):
	+ Select all the symptoms that apply to you:
		- High Blood Pressure
		- Fatigue
		- Chest Pain
		- Cough
		- Shortness of Breath
		- Nausea and Vomiting
		- Dizziness or Lightheadedness
		- Headache
* **Risk Factors** (Risk Factors, select_one, required:false): Check the risk factor that applies to you:
	+ Diabetes
	+ High Cholesterol
	+ Smoking or tobacco use
	+ Family History of Heart Disease
	+ High Blood Pressure
	+ Obesity
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required:false): Enter any relevant medical history information, such as past health conditions or allergies.
* **Medication** (Medication, select_multiple, required:false):
	+ Select all medications you are currently taking:
		- Aspirin
		- Amlodipine
		- Atorvastatin
		- Warfarin
* **Test Results** (Test Results, select_multiple, required:false):
	+ Select the results of any recent medical tests:
		- Normal
		- Abnormal
		- Inconclusive
* **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required:false):
	+ Give your consent to the assessment:

**Tips**
- Please ensure to provide accurate information to aid in the assessment process.
- If you experience any symptoms or concerns related to vascular health, seek immediate medical attention.
