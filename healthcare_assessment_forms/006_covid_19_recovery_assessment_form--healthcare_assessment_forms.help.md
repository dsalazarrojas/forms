# COVID 19 Recovery Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Recovery Assessment Form is designed to help healthcare providers assess your recovery status after a COVID-19 diagnosis. This form collects information on your medical history, current health status, and recovery progress to provide personalized care and improve patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of this assessment.
2. **Patient Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
3. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. **Medical Record Number**: If you have a medical record number, please enter it (optional).
5. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up communications.
6. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
7. **Initial COVID-19 Diagnosis Date**: Enter the date you were first diagnosed with COVID-19.
8. **Recovery Start Date**: Enter the date you started feeling better.
9. **Current Health Status**: Rate your overall health status from Excellent to Poor.
10. **Lingering Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are still experiencing, such as Fatigue or Tiredness, Difficulty Breathing or Shortness of Breath, etc.
11. **Symptom Severity**: Rate the severity of your lingering symptoms, from Not experiencing any symptoms to Very severe - unable to perform daily activities.
12. **Return to Work Status**: Report your current work status, from Returned to work full-time to Unable to work.
13. **Physical Activity Level**: Rate your current physical activity level compared to before COVID-19, from Same as before to Unable to be physically active.
14. **Breathing Exercises**: Indicate if you are doing breathing exercises or pulmonary rehabilitation.
15. **Mental Health Status**: Rate your mental health status from Excellent to Very poor - need immediate support.
16. **Mental Health Support**: Indicate if you are receiving mental health support, such as counseling or therapy.
17. **Sleep Quality**: Rate your sleep quality from Excellent to Poor.
18. **Appetite Status**: Rate your appetite from Normal to Significantly decreased.
19. **Follow-up Appointments**: Indicate if you have attended all recommended follow-up appointments.
20. **Medications**: Indicate if you are currently taking any medications for COVID-19 related conditions.
21. **Medication List** (optional): List any current medications, including dosages.
22. **Support System**: Indicate if you have adequate support at home.
23. **Additional Health Concerns**: Share any additional health concerns you may have.
24. **Primary Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter your primary healthcare provider's name.
25. **Patient Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns.
