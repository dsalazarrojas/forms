# Cervical Injection Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cervical Injection Assessment Form is used to record the details of cervical injection procedures, including assessment date, patient information, and procedure-related data. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and documented accurately for patient follow-up and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary patient information, including their date of birth, medical record number, and contact details.
2. Fill in the date of assessment, which should be the date the form is being completed.
3. Enter the patient's name and medical record number in the relevant fields.
4. Record the date of the cervical injection procedure.
5. Select the type of injection performed.
6. Specify the level of the cervical spine injected.
7. Indicate which side was injected.
8. Choose the type of imaging guidance used.
9. Confirm whether contrast was used during the procedure.
10. List any medications administered during the procedure.
11. Record the total dosage administered.
12. Rate the patient's pre-procedure pain level (0-10).
13. Assess the patient's tolerance of the procedure.
14. Check for any complications that occurred during the procedure.
15. Describe any complications that occurred.
16. Record the patient's post-procedure pain level (0-10), if applicable.
17. Confirm whether discharge instructions were provided.
18. Indicate whether a follow-up appointment is scheduled.
19. Record the date of the follow-up appointment, if applicable.
20. Add any additional clinical notes.
21. Sign the form as the physician who performed the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Date (1)**: Date the form is being completed.
* **Patient Name (2)**: Full name of the patient.
* **Patient Date of Birth (3)**: Date of birth of the patient.
* **Patient ID Number (4)**: Medical record number of the patient.
* **Procedure Date (5)**: Date the cervical injection procedure was performed.
* **Performing Physician (6)**: Name of the physician who performed the injection.
* **Injection Type (7)**: Type of cervical injection performed (e.g., Epidural steroid injection).
* **Injection Level (8)**: Level of the cervical spine injected (e.g., C2-C3).
* **Laterality (9)**: Side of the injection (e.g., Right, Left, Bilateral).
* **Imaging Guidance (10)**: Type of imaging guidance used (e.g., Fluoroscopy).
* **Contrast Used (11)**: Whether contrast was used during the procedure.
* **Medication Administered (12)**: List any medications injected during the procedure.
* **Dosage (13)**: Total dosage of medication administered.
* **Pre-Procedure Pain Level (14)**: Pain level before the procedure (0-10).
* **Procedure Tolerance (15)**: Assess the patient's tolerance of the procedure.
* **Complications (16)**: Check for any complications that occurred during the procedure.
* **Complication Details (17)**: Describe any complications that occurred.
* **Post-Procedure Pain Level (18)**: Pain level after the procedure (0-10), if applicable.
* **Discharge Instructions Given (19)**: Whether discharge instructions were provided to the patient.
* **Follow-up Scheduled (20)**: Whether a follow-up appointment is scheduled.
* **Follow-up Date (21)**: Date of the follow-up appointment, if applicable.
* **Assessment Notes (22)**: Additional clinical notes.
* **Physician Signature (23)**: Sign the form as the physician who performed the procedure.
* **Signature Date (24)**: Date of signing.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and update the form as necessary after each procedure.
* Use this form for future reference and follow-up with patients.
* Complete the form according to your institutional policies and guidelines.
