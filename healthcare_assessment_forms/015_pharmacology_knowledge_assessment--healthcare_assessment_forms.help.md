# Pharmacology Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge in pharmacology, a branch of science concerned with the study of drugs and their effects on living organisms. It is intended to evaluate your understanding of various pharmacological concepts, principles, and practices. By completing this form, you will be presented with a series of questions and required to respond with your answers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **form title** in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select the relevant **category** related to your assessment.
3. Provide your **full name** in the "Learner Name" field.
4. Enter your **email address** associated with your account in the "Learner Email" field.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Learner Phone" field.
6. Select the appropriate answer to each **Quiz Question** from the drop-down list.
7. Choose the correct answer to each question by selecting **Correct** or **Incorrect**.
8. Enter the **score** you earned based on your performance.
9. If you have any comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
10. Finally, confirm that you have completed the assessment by checking the box marked **Submitted**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a brief title of your assessment.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, required: true):
	+ Select the relevant category related to your assessment.
* **Learner Name** (`learner_name`, text, required: true):
	+ Enter your full name associated with your account.
* **Learner Email** (`learner_email`, email, required: true):
	+ Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Learner Phone** (`learner_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, select_multiple, required: true):
	+ Select the appropriate answer to each question from the drop-down list.
* **Quiz Responses** (`quiz_responses`, select_one, required: true):
	+ Choose the correct answer to each question by selecting **Correct** or **Incorrect**.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required: true):
	+ Enter the score you earned based on your performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any comments you have about the assessment.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Confirm that you have completed the assessment.
* **Submitted Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false):
	+ (Note: Not used in this form)
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false):
	+ (Note: Not used in this form)
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ (Note: Not used in this form)

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
* Take your time to read each question carefully before responding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the relevant authorities.
* Double-check your responses before submitting the assessment.
* Make sure to enter your correct email address to receive any updates about your assessment.
