## Chronic Care Model Redesign Application Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Chronic Care Model Redesign Application Form is a comprehensive guide that helps healthcare organizations apply for participation in the Chronic Care Model Redesign program. This program is designed to support healthcare providers in redesigning their care delivery systems to better serve patients with chronic conditions.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the first section.
2. Select your organization's type and size.
3. Specify the chronic conditions of focus for your organization.
4. Describe your current care model and redesign experience.
5. Outline your motivations for applying and primary goals.
6. Specify the expected outcomes and any potential barriers to implementation.
7. Detail your team's readiness and resources available for participation.
8. Select a suitable implementation timeline and any additional support needed.
9. Finally, confirm your commitment to participate in the program.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for application updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact regarding application.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your healthcare organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Hospital, Primary Care Clinic, etc.).
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required): Select the approximate number of providers in your organization.
* **Patient Population Served** (`patient_population`, select_one, required): Select the approximate number of patients your organization serves.
* **Chronic Conditions of Focus** (`chronic_conditions_focus`, select_multiple, required): Select all chronic conditions of focus for your organization (e.g., Diabetes, Hypertension, etc.).
* **Current Care Model** (`current_care_model`, select_one, required): Describe your current care model (e.g., Traditional fee-for-service, Patient-Centered Medical Home, etc.).
* **Previous Redesign Experience** (`redesign_experience`, select_one, required): Describe your previous redesign experience (e.g., No first time, Yes small projects, etc.).
* **Motivation for Application** (`motivation_for_application`, text, required): Explain why you want to participate in the program.
* **Primary Goals** (`primary_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the primary goals you hope to achieve through the program (e.g., Improve patient outcomes, Reduce costs, etc.).
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): Specify the specific outcomes you expect from the program.
* **Team Readiness** (`team_readiness`, select_one, required): Assess your team's readiness and motivation for the program (e.g., Very ready and motivated, Somewhat ready, etc.).
* **Leadership Support** (`leadership_support`, select_one, required): Describe the level of executive support for the program (e.g., Full executive commitment, Moderate support, etc.).
* **Resources Available** (`resources_available`, select_multiple, required): Select the resources your organization can commit to the program (e.g., Dedicated staff time, IT support, etc.).
* **Implementation Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select the planned implementation timeline for the program (e.g., Immediately, Within 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Potential Barriers** (`potential_barriers`, text, required): Identify any potential barriers to implementation.
* **Additional Support Needed** (`additional_support_needed`, text, optional): Specify any additional support needed for the program.
* **How Did You Hear About This Program** (`how_heard`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about the program (e.g., Professional organization, Colleague referral, etc.).
* **Commitment Agreement** (`agree_commitment`, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment to participate fully in the program.

### Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* Review each section carefully to ensure you provide all necessary information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators for assistance.
* Remember to sign the commitment agreement at the end of the form to confirm your participation.
