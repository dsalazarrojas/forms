# Heel Pain Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heel Pain Assessment Survey is a form designed to help healthcare professionals gather information from patients about their heel pain. This form aims to collect data that will aid in understanding the severity and frequency of the pain, its type, location, and any previous injuries that may be contributing to the pain. By completing this form, patients can help healthcare professionals develop a more comprehensive understanding of their condition and provide valuable insights for proper diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Heel Pain Assessment Survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Patient Info** field with your relevant personal information.
2. Enter your **Contact Info** in case we need to reach out to you for further discussion or follow-up.
3. Describe your **Heel Pain** in the provided field.
4. Indicate the type of pain you are experiencing by selecting the options provided in the **Pain Type** field.
5. Report the duration of your pain in the **Pain Duration** field.
6. Specify how often you experience pain in the **Pain Frequency** field.
7. Describe the trigger for your pain in the **Pain Trigger** field.
8. Indicate the location of your pain in the **Pain Location** field.
9. Provide a detailed description of your pain in the **Pain Description** field.
10. If you have experienced any previous injuries, please describe them in the **Previous Injuries** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: This field is for entering your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for your contact information, such as your phone number and email address, in case we need to contact you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Heel Pain**: Describe your heel pain in this field.
* **Pain Type**: Choose the type of pain you are experiencing from the provided options: Severe, Moderate, or Mild.
* **Pain Duration**: Report the duration of your pain in days, weeks, or months.
* **Pain Frequency**: Specify how often you experience pain, using the number of times per day or week.
* **Pain Trigger**: Describe the trigger or cause of your pain.
* **Pain Location**: Indicate the location of your pain, such as on the back of the heel, side of the heel, etc.
* **Pain Description**: Provide a detailed description of your pain, including any associated symptoms or sensations.
* **Previous Injuries**: If you have experienced any previous injuries, describe them in this field.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your pain and any previous injuries.
* Use the provided options for **Pain Type** and **Pain Location** to ensure accurate and consistent data.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us for guidance.
* Completing this form will help healthcare professionals better understand your condition and provide you with the necessary care and attention.
