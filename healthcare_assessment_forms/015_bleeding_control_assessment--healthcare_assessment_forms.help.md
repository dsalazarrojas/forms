# Bleeding Control Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bleeding Control Assessment form is designed to collect information about an incident involving bleeding, allowing responders to assess and document the situation efficiently. This form is used to record details about the incident, the patient's condition, and any interventions used to control the bleeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "incident_header" page.
2. Enter the Responder's name in the "Responder name" field.
3. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident date" field using the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
4. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident time" field using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
5. Enter the patient's age in years in the "Patient age" field.
6. Select the location where the bleeding occurred in the "Bleeding site" field.
7. Estimate the volume of blood loss in milliliters in the "Estimated blood loss ml" field.
8. Select all applicable interventions used to control the bleeding in the "Interventions used" field (e.g., direct pressure, tourniquet, wound packing, hemostatic dressing, or transport to hospital).
9. Enter any available patient vital signs (e.g., pulse, blood pressure) in the "Patient vitals notes" field.
10. Enter a brief summary of the patient's outcome at handover in the "Outcome" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Responder name** (`Responder name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person completing the assessment.
* **Incident date** (`Incident date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident using the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Incident time** (`Incident time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Patient age** (`Patient age`, `number`, optional): Enter the patient's age in whole years.
* **Bleeding site** (`Bleeding site`, `text`, required): Select the location where the bleeding occurred.
* **Estimated blood loss ml** (`Estimated blood loss ml`, `number`, optional): Estimate the volume of blood loss in milliliters.
* **Interventions used** (`Interventions used`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable interventions used to control the bleeding (e.g., direct pressure, tourniquet, wound packing, hemostatic dressing, transport to hospital).
* **Patient vitals notes** (`Patient vitals notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any available patient vital signs (e.g., pulse, blood pressure).
* **Outcome** (`Outcome`, `text`, required): Enter a brief summary of the patient's outcome at handover.

## Tips
* Always enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the assessment is comprehensive and effective.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or seek guidance from medical professionals.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure it is complete and accurate.
