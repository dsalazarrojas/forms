# manual-dexterity-assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manual Dexterity Assessment form is used to evaluate a patient's manual dexterity and overall functional ability. This form is typically completed by healthcare professionals to gather information about a patient's ability to perform tasks and assess their need for physical therapy or rehabilitation services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical history and current status to ensure that you are using the correct version of this form.
2. Answer each question on the form based on the patient's current condition.
3. Select the answer "Yes" if the patient can perform the task without difficulty, and "No" if they are unable to perform the task or experience difficulty.
4. For the "Observations" field, enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition or any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Task Performance (Select Multiple)**:
	+ This field asks you to indicate if the patient can perform specific tasks without difficulty. Select all the tasks that the patient can perform.
* **Task History (Text Field)**:
	+ This field is used to record any relevant medical history or previous experiences with similar tasks that may affect the patient's current condition.
* **Observations (Note Field)**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition, such as any limitations or areas of concern that need further attention.

## Tips

* Make sure to use the correct version of this form for each patient, based on their medical history and current status.
* Use the "Observations" field to document any important information that may not be captured in the other fields.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please refer to the patient's medical records for clarification.
