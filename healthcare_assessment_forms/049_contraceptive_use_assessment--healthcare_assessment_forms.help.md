# Contraceptive Use Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contraceptive Use Assessment form is designed to gather information on an individual's contraceptive use patterns, including their age group, current contraceptive status, and sources of information on contraception. This form helps healthcare professionals understand an individual's needs and preferences, enabling them to provide better care and advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select your age group from the dropdown menu.
2.  If you are currently using any form of contraception, select "True" in the "Are you currently using any form of contraception" field.
3.  If you are currently using contraception, select your primary method from the dropdown menu.
4.  If you have used contraception in the past, select how long you have used it from the dropdown menu.
5.  If you are not using contraception, select the primary reasons from the dropdown menu.
6.  Select the sources of information on contraception that you rely on.
7.  If you need additional counseling or information on specific methods, select the methods you are interested in.
8.  Rate your satisfaction with your access to contraceptive care.
9.  Finally, select the date of assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Age Group**: Please select your age group from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your demographic profile and relate it to your contraceptive use patterns.
*   **Are you Currently using any form of Contraception**: If you are using contraception, select "True." If not, select "False." This will help us understand your current contraceptive status.
*   **If YES, what is your primary method**: If you have selected "True" in the previous question, select your primary method of contraception from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your preferred method of contraception.
*   **How long have you used this method**: If you have selected a primary method, select how long you have used it from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your experience with different methods.
*   **Barriers and Motivations**: If you are not using contraception, select the primary reasons from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your motivations for not using contraception.
*   **Where do you get most of your information on contraception**: Select the sources of information on contraception that you rely on from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand how you stay informed on contraception.
*   **Would you like additional counseling or info on specific methods**: If you need additional information on specific methods, select the methods you are interested in from the dropdown menu. This will help us provide you with targeted information and counseling.
*   **How satisfied are you with your access to contraceptive care**: Rate your satisfaction with your access to contraceptive care using the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your experience with our services.
*   **Date of Assessment**: Finally, please select the date of assessment. This will help us track the completion of this form and provide you with a reference point for your progress.
