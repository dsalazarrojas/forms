# Adolescence And Puberty Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adolescence And Puberty Assessment form gathers information about a participant's personal details, puberty-related concerns or symptoms, and emotional wellbeing. This data is collected for clinicians or counselors to provide guidance on the participant's development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your full name.
2. Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Select your grade or age group from the options provided.
4. If you have any puberty-related concerns or symptoms, briefly describe them in the text field.
5. If applicable, select your menstruation status from the options provided.
6. Rate your overall emotional wellbeing in the last 2 weeks.
7. Select any body image concerns that apply to you.
8. Indicate if parental consent has been obtained.
9. If applicable, enter any additional notes for the clinician or counselor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Full name, text, required):
    Enter your full name, including any middle name or surname.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required):
    Use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-06-01) to enter your date of birth.
* **Grade or age group** (Grade or age group, select_one, required):
    Select one of the following options to indicate your school grade or age range:
    - Under 10
    - 10 to 12
    - 13 to 15
    - 16 to 18
* **Puberty-related concerns or symptoms** (Puberty related concerns or symptoms, text, required):
    Briefly describe any physical or emotional changes you are experiencing related to puberty.
* **Menstruation status** (Menstruation status, select_one, required):
    Select one of the following options if applicable:
    - Not yet started
    - Started and regular
    - Started and irregular
    - Prefer not to say
* **Emotional wellbeing rating** (Emotional wellbeing rating, select_one, required):
    Rate your overall emotional wellbeing in the last 2 weeks as follows:
    - Very low
    - Low
    - Moderate
    - High
    - Very high
* **Body image concerns** (Body image concerns, select_multiple, not required):
    Select any of the following concerns that apply:
    - Weight concerns
    - Skin changes
    - Height concerns
    - Hair changes
* **Parental consent** (Parental consent, select_one, required for participants under 18):
    Select 'True' if parental consent has been obtained.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, text, not required):
    Enter any additional information for the clinician or counselor.
* **Parental consent obtained** (Parental consent obtained, select_one, required):
    Select 'True' if parental consent has been obtained.
