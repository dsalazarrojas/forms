# Surgery Recovery Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgery Recovery Assessment form is designed to gather information about a patient's recovery from surgery, including their vital signs, pain level, and any complications they may be experiencing. This form helps healthcare professionals monitor and assess the patient's progress, ensuring timely and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the patient's information**: Enter the patient's name, follow-up date, and any other relevant details in the text field labeled "patient_update".
2. **Report your vital signs**: Indicate whether the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and temperature, are within normal range ("Yes" or "No") in the "vital_signs" field.
3. **Assess pain level**: If the patient is experiencing pain, select the number that best describes their current pain level in the "pain_level" field.
4. **Specify pain location**: If the patient is experiencing pain, describe the location of the pain in the "pain_location" field.
5. **List medications**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking in the "medication" field.
6. **Check for complications**: Select "Yes" or "No" for any complications the patient is experiencing, such as infection or allergic reaction, in the "complications" field.
7. **Create follow-up plan**: Write a brief plan for the patient's follow-up care in the "follow_up_plan" field.
8. **Share medical history**: Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant allergies or medical conditions, in the "medical_history" field.
9. **Describe surgical details**: Provide any additional information about the patient's surgery, including the type of procedure performed and any notes about the recovery process, in the "surgical_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Update** (<name>, <type>: text, required): Provide any relevant patient information, such as name, address, or insurance details.
* **Follow-up Date** (<name>, <type>: date, required): Enter the date of the patient's follow-up appointment.
* **Pain Level** (<name>, <type>: number, optional): Select a number from 0 (no pain) to 5 (severe pain) to describe the patient's current pain level.
* **Pain Location** (<name>, <type>: text, required): Describe the location of the patient's pain, such as back, leg, or abdomen.
* **Medications** (<name>, <type>: text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Vital Signs** (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the patient's vital signs are within normal range ("Yes" or "No").
* **Complications** (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" or "No" for any complications the patient is experiencing.
* **Follow-up Plan** (<name>, <type>: text, optional): Write a brief plan for the patient's follow-up care.
* **Medical History** (<name>, <type>: text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant allergies or medical conditions.
* **Surgical Details** (<name>, <type>: text, optional): Provide any additional information about the patient's surgery, including the type of procedure performed and any notes about the recovery process.
