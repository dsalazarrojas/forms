# Fitness Longevity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Longevity Assessment Form is designed to evaluate an individual's overall fitness, nutrition, sleep, stress management, mental health, and overall wellness. This form will help assess an individual's health and wellness status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to select only one answer for select_one fields and multiple answers for select_multiple fields.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) and skip the optional ones.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Demographic Information** (`2`, `text`, optional): Provide your name, age, and any other demographic information you'd like to share.
* **Fitness Level** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to describe your current fitness level:
	+ Option 1: Beginner
	+ Option 2: Intermediate
	+ Option 3: Advanced
* **Exercise Frequency** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ways you exercise:
	+ Option 1: Regularly
	+ Option 2: Occasionally
	+ Option 3: Rarely
* **Nutrition Habits** (`5`, `text`, required): Describe your typical diet and eating habits.
* **Sleep Quality** (`6`, `number`, required): Rate your sleep quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Stress Management** (`7`, `time`, optional): Describe how you manage your stress, e.g., meditation, exercise, or talking to friends.
* **Mental Health** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to describe your mental health:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Physical Activity** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ways you engage in physical activity:
	+ Option 1: Regularly
	+ Option 2: Occasionally
	+ Option 3: Rarely
* **Health Goals** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to describe your health goals:
	+ Option 1: Weight Loss
	+ Option 2: Muscle Gain
	+ Option 3: Endurance
* **Medical Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to describe your medical status:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Exercise Factors** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the factors that influence your exercise:
	+ Option 1: Motivation
	+ Option 2: Time
	+ Option 3: Energy
* **Mental Factors** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to describe your mental factors:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Social Support** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the ways you receive social support:
	+ Option 1: Family
	+ Option 2: Friends
	+ Option 3: Online Communities
* **Overall Wellness** (`15`, `text`, required): Describe your overall well-being.
* **Health Risks** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the health risks you are aware of:
	+ Option 1: High Blood Pressure
	+ Option 2: Diabetes
	+ Option 3: Mental Health Issues
* **Medical Conditions** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the medical conditions you have:
	+ Option 1: High Blood Pressure
	+ Option 2: Diabetes
	+ Option 3: Mental Health Issues
* **Sleep Duration** (`18`, `number`, required): Rate how many hours you sleep per night.
* **Sleep Quality** (`19`, `number`, required): Rate your sleep quality on a scale of 1-10.
* **Exercise Facility** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options to describe your exercise facility:
	+ Option 1: Home
	+ Option 2: Gym
	+ Option 3: Park
* **Nutrition Facility** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options to describe your nutrition facility:
	+ Option 1: Home
	+ Option 2: Restaurant
	+ Option 3: Store
* **Mental Facility** (`22`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options to describe your mental facility:
	+ Option 1: Home
	+ Option 2: Therapy
	+ Option 3: Support Group
* **Medical Facility** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options to describe your medical facility:
	+ Option 1: Doctor's Office
	+ Option 2: Hospital
	+ Option 3: Clinic
* **Overall Score** (`24`, `number`, required): Enter a final overall score based on your answers.
* **Submit** (`25`, `text`, required): Review your answers and submit the form.
