## # CT Imaging Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CT Imaging Knowledge Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of healthcare professionals in the field of CT imaging. This form covers various aspects of CT imaging, including CT basics, radiation safety, contrast agents, patient positioning, protocol selection, artifact identification, emergency procedures, quality control, and areas requiring improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and employee ID on the first page.
2. Select your department and role/position from the provided options.
3. Rate your experience level with CT imaging.
4. Choose your assessment date.
5. Answer the questions on the CT basics, radiation safety, contrast agents, patient positioning, protocol selection, artifact identification, emergency procedures, quality control, and areas requiring improvement.
6. Select your preferred training format for additional knowledge.
7. Provide any additional comments or information about your CT imaging knowledge.
