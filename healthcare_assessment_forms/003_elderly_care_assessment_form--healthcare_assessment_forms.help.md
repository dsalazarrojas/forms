# Elderly Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elderly Care Assessment Form is designed to collect information about elderly care recipients, aiming to assess their overall well-being and identify potential areas of concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free area with a pen or a keyboard.
2. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
3. Be honest and accurate when providing information about the patient.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing general information about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History (page 2)** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or injuries.
* **Current Medications (page 3)** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List the patient's current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergies (page 4)** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): Describe any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medical Conditions (page 5)** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any ongoing medical conditions the patient has.
* **Cognitive Status (page 6)** (`cognitive_status`, `text`, required: false): Evaluate the patient's cognitive status, including any memory loss, confusion, or difficulty with communication.

## Tips

* Please review and double-check your answers before submission.
* If the patient has difficulty answering a question, please seek clarification or help from a healthcare professional.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please contact a healthcare professional for guidance.
