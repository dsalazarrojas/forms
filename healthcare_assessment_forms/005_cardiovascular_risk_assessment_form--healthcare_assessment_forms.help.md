# Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Form is designed to assess an individual's cardiovascular risk factors and provide a comprehensive evaluation of their overall risk profile. This form is used for routine screenings, pre-employment physicals, insurance requirements, personal health monitoring, or physician recommendations.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name, date of birth, and other demographic information on page 1.
2. Answer the questions regarding your medical history, such as your ethnicity, gender, family history of premature cardiovascular disease, diabetes status, and existing medical conditions.
3. Provide information on your lifestyle factors, including your height, weight, blood pressure, smoking status, alcohol consumption, and physical activity level.
4. Answer the questions on your current symptoms, stress level, sleep quality, and other factors that may affect your cardiovascular health.
5. Indicate the purpose of this assessment, such as a routine screening or pre-employment physical.
6. If applicable, provide the name of your referring healthcare provider and any additional health information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Personal Information

* **Patient Full Name** (`Patient Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender at Birth** (`Gender at Birth`, select_one, required): Select your gender at birth, either Male or Female.
* **Ethnicity** (`Ethnicity`, select_one, optional): Select your ethnicity from the provided options.

### Page 2: Medical History

* **Height** (`Height`, text, required): Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight** (`Weight`, text, required): Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Systolic Blood Pressure** (`Systolic Blood Pressure`, number, required): Enter your top blood pressure number in mmHg.
* **Diastolic Blood Pressure** (`Diastolic Blood Pressure`, number, required): Enter your bottom blood pressure number in mmHg.
* **Currently Taking Blood Pressure Medication** (`Currently Taking Blood Pressure Medication`, select_one, required): Select whether you are currently taking blood pressure medication.
* **Smoking Status** (`Smoking Status`, select_one, required): Indicate your current smoking status, whether you are a current smoker, former smoker, or never smoked.
* **Diabetes Status** (`Diabetes Status`, select_one, required): Indicate your diabetes status, whether you have type 1, type 2, prediabetes, or no diabetes.

### Page 3: Lifestyle Factors

* **Family History of Premature Cardiovascular Disease** (`Family History of Premature Cardiovascular Disease`, select_one, required): Select whether you have a family history of premature cardiovascular disease.
* **Physical Activity Level** (`Physical Activity Level`, select_one, required): Indicate your average weekly physical activity level.
* **Alcohol Consumption** (`Alcohol Consumption`, select_one, required): Indicate your average weekly alcohol consumption.
* **Existing Medical Conditions** (`Existing Medical Conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any existing medical conditions you may have.
* **Current Symptoms** (`Current Symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Select any current cardiovascular symptoms you are experiencing.

### Page 4: Risk Factors

* **Total Cholesterol Level** (`Total Cholesterol Level`, number, optional): Enter your most recent total cholesterol reading in mg/dL.
* **LDL Cholesterol** (`LDL Cholesterol`, number, optional): Enter your most recent LDL cholesterol reading in mg/dL.
* **HDL Cholesterol** (`HDL Cholesterol`, number, optional): Enter your most recent HDL cholesterol reading in mg/dL.
* **Triglycerides** (`Triglycerides`, number, optional): Enter your most recent triglyceride reading in mg/dL.

### Page 5: Additional Information

* **Average Daily Stress Level** (`Average Daily Stress Level`, number, required): Rate your average daily stress level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Sleep Quality** (`Sleep Quality`, select_one, required): Indicate your average sleep quality in terms of hours per night.
* **Referring Healthcare Provider** (`Referring Healthcare Provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your referring healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **Additional Health Information** (`Additional Health Information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant health information.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and knowledge.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a thorough evaluation of your cardiovascular risk profile.
* This form is intended for routine screenings, pre-employment physicals, insurance requirements, personal health monitoring, or physician recommendations. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your healthcare provider or contact us for assistance.
