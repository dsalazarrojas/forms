# Snakebite Emergency Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Snakebite Emergency Assessment Form is designed to collect critical information from medical professionals and first responders to assess and treat snakebite emergencies. This form helps gather essential details about the patient, snake type, bite location, and other relevant factors to provide timely and effective medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's basic information such as name, age, and any relevant contact details.
2. **Bite Info**: Provide details about the snakebite, including the type of snake, bite location, and other relevant information.
3. **Medical History**: If applicable, describe any pre-existing medical conditions or previous medical interventions related to the snakebite.
4. **Snake Type**: Select the correct type of snake that caused the bite from the options provided.
5. **Bite Location**: Choose the location where the snakebite occurred.
6. **Bite Depth**: Indicate the depth of the snakebite, if known.
7. **Wound Status**: Select the current status of the wound, if applicable.
8. **Symptoms**: Select any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
9. **Medical Attention**: Identify any medical attention the patient requires.
10. **First Aid Provision**: Provide details about any first aid provided to the patient.
11. **Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the snakebite incident.
12. **Location**: Enter the location where the snakebite occurred.
13. **Medical Team**: Select the medical team members involved in treating the patient.
14. **Patient Info 1-2**: Enter any additional patient information.
15. **Medical History 1-2**: If applicable, describe any previous medical interventions related to the snakebite.
16. **Bite Info 1-2**: Provide details about the bite location and type for multiple bites.
17. **Medical Team 1-2**: Identify any additional medical team members involved.
18. **Symptoms 1-2**: Select any additional symptoms the patient is experiencing.
