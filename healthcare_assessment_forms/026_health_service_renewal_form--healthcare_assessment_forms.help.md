# Health Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Service Renewal Form is used to collect information about the patient's health service, including clinic name, preferred renewal period, start and end dates, patient name, phone number, email, and other relevant details. This form is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the patient's health service status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the clinic name where the patient received the health service.
2. Choose the preferred renewal period from the options provided.
3. Enter the start date of the service.
4. Enter the end date of the service.
5. Enter the patient's name.
6. Enter the patient's phone number.
7. Enter the patient's email address.
8. Add any relevant notes about the renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Service Renewal Form Data**: Enter a brief description of the health service being renewed. (Text field)
* **Clinic Name**: Enter the name of the clinic where the patient received the health service. (Text field)
* **Preferred Renewal Period**: Select the preferred renewal period for the health service. (Select one from the options: 3 months, 6 months, 12 months)
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the service. (Date field)
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the service. (Date field)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name. (Text field)
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number. (Text field)
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address. (Email field)
* **Renewal Notes**: Add any relevant notes about the renewal. (Text field)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth. (Date field)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare provider for guidance.
* Double-check the entered information for correctness before submitting the form.
