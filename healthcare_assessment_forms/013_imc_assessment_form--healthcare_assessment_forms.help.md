# IMC Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IMC Assessment Form is a tool used to collect measurements related to a person's health and wellness, specifically height, weight, age, body mass index (BMI), and waist circumference. This form is intended for use in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your height in centimeters (cm) in the **Height Measurement** field.
2. Enter your weight in kilograms (kg) in the **Weight Measurement** field.
3. Enter your age in years in the **Age** field.
4. Enter your body mass index (BMI) in the **Body Mass Index** field. Note: This field is for calculated BMI, please do not enter manually.
5. Enter your waist circumference in centimeters (cm) in the **Waist Circumference** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Height Measurement** (`height_measurement`, number, required: false): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight Measurement** (`weight_measurement`, number, required: false): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age in years.
* **Body Mass Index** (`body_mass_index`, text, required: false): This field is calculated based on your height and weight, please do not enter manually.
* **Waist Circumference** (`waist_circumference`, number, required: false): Enter your waist circumference in centimeters.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate measurements for height and weight to get an accurate BMI calculation.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to a healthcare professional for assistance.
