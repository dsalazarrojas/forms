# Hernia And Varicose Vein Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Hernia And Varicose Vein Assessment" form is designed to collect information about a patient's symptoms, medical history, and treatment options for varicose veins. This form is used to assess the severity of the symptoms, identify potential causes, and determine the most effective treatment plan for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, ensuring that the information provided is accurate and complete.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose before providing an answer.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your personal identification and any relevant information about yourself.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list:
	+ Pain
	+ Fatigue
	+ Redness
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Indicate where you are experiencing the symptoms:
	+ Left
	+ Right
	+ Both
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe your symptoms in more detail, if you feel it is necessary.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Please provide your consent for treatment by checking the box, indicating your understanding of the treatment options and risks involved.
* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, text, optional): Provide your medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe any underlying medical conditions you are experiencing.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable treatment options for your condition:
	+ Medication
	+ Surgery
	+ Other
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up appointment for further evaluation and treatment.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your condition.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): This is your unique patient ID number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): This is your medical record number.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure proper diagnosis and treatment of your condition.
