# Sepsis Assessment For Pregnant Women - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sepsis Assessment for Pregnant Women form is designed to collect information for the early identification and treatment of sepsis, a life-threatening condition that can occur during pregnancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Provide detailed and specific information about the patient's condition.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing background information about the patient, such as name, age, and medical history.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as fever, chills, or difficulty breathing.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, text, optional): List any risk factors that may increase the patient's likelihood of developing sepsis, such as recent surgery, severe illness, or recent travel.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Record the patient's vital signs, such as temperature, blood pressure, and heart rate.
* **Clinical Findings** (`clinical_findings`, text, optional): Describe any significant clinical findings, such as abnormal laboratory results or clinical examination results.
* **Maternal Information** (`maternal_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the mother's health, such as her age, weight, and medical history.
* **Pregnancy Information** (`pregnancy_info`, text, optional): Report details about the pregnancy, such as gestational age, fetal position, and any complications.
* **Fetal Information** (`fetal_info`, text, optional): Record any information about the fetus, such as fetal movement, fetal position, and any fetal complications.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with a medical professional.
* This form is for the use of healthcare professionals only.
