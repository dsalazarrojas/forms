# Medical Technology Accessibility Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the accessibility of medical technologies used in your healthcare facility. Please use this form to evaluate the accessibility of a specific medical technology, providing feedback on its usability, barriers to use, and rating of its overall performance. This information will help us to identify areas for improvement and ensure that our medical technologies are accessible to everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the **Medical Technology Accessibility Evaluation Form** title.
2. Enter your **Staff Name**.
3. Select whether the technology is **Yes** or **No** in the **Technology Type** field.
4. For each barrier you identify, check the corresponding **Assess Barriers** field. You can select multiple barriers.
5. If desired, provide feedback in the **Collect Staff Feedback** field.
6. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
7. Enter the contact email of the person evaluated.
8. Enter the contact phone number of the person evaluated.
9. Rate the technology's performance on a scale from 1 to 10 in the **Technology Rating** field.
10. Provide any additional comments about the technology in the **Technology Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Technology Accessibility Evaluation Form (1)**: This is the title of the form, which you will complete at the top of this page.
* **Staff Name (2)**: Please enter your name to identify yourself as the person completing this form.
* **Technology Type (3)**: Check **Yes** if the technology you are evaluating is a medical device or technology, or **No** if it is not. This will help us to categorize the feedback.
* **Assess Barriers (4)**: Identify any barriers you have encountered while using the technology by checking the corresponding boxes. You can select multiple barriers.
	+ **Yes** (Barriers present)
	+ **No** (No barriers present)
* **Collect Staff Feedback (5)**: If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement, please provide them in this field.
* **Date (6)**: Enter the date you completed the evaluation.
* **Time (7)**: Enter the time you completed the evaluation.
* **Email (8)**: Enter the contact email of the person evaluated.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter the contact phone number of the person evaluated.
* **Technology Rating (10)**: Rate the technology's performance on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Technology Comment (11)**: Provide any additional comments about the technology's performance.
