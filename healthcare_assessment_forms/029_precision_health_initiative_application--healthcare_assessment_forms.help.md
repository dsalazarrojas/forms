# precision_health_initiative_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Precision Health Initiative Application form is designed to collect participant information for a precision health initiative. This form helps gather details about the participant's interests, contact information, medical history, and consent for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the introduction, where you can provide a brief description of your background and experience with health initiatives.
2. Fill in your participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
3. Specify your interests in the "Participant Interests" section by selecting one or more options from the list. If you are not interested in any of the provided options, select "No" for each.
4. Provide your consent to participate in the program by selecting "Yes" in the "Consent" field.
5. Enter your contact details, including phone number, in the "Contact Details" and "Phone Number" fields.
6. If you have any medical history or conditions, provide details in the "Medical History" and "Medical Conditions" fields. If you do not have any medical conditions, select "No" for both.
7. If you have undergone any medical procedures, provide details in the "Medical Procedure Info" field.
8. Finally, review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief description of your background and experience with health initiatives.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes

* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details such as name and address.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes

* **Participant Interests**: Select one or more options to specify your interests.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No

* **Consent**: Provide your consent to participate in the program.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes

* **Contact Details**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes

* **Contact Information**: Enter your address and any other relevant contact information.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes

* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No

* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes

* **Emergency Contact**: Enter an emergency contact's name and phone number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No

* **Participant Interests**: Select one or more options to specify your interests.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No

* **Medical History**: Provide details about your medical history.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No

* **Medical Procedure Info**: Provide details about any medical procedures you have undergone.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No

* **Medical Conditions**: Select "Yes" if you have any medical conditions.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No

* **Medical Procedure Outcome**: Select "Yes" if you have undergone any medical procedures.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
