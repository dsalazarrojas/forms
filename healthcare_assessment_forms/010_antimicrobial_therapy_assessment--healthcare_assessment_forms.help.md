# Antimicrobial Therapy Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Antimicrobial Therapy Assessment form is a critical tool used to evaluate the appropriateness and effectiveness of antimicrobial therapy in patients. It helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's response to current antimicrobial therapy, identify potential issues, and provide recommendations for improvement. This form ensures that healthcare providers have accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's progress, allowing for timely adjustments to treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary patient data and medical records before starting the assessment.
2. Select the correct date for the assessment.
3. Enter the patient's medical record number.
4. Enter the patient's name (optional but recommended for reference).
5. Select the name of the clinical reviewer.
6. Choose the admission date or start date of therapy.
7. Select the site of infection from the given options.
8. Indicate whether a culture was obtained before antibiotics were started.
9. Indicate whether culture and sensitivity results are available.
10. If the organism has been identified, enter the known organism.
11. Select the susceptibility results for the current agent (if known).
12. Enter the current antimicrobial agent(s) being used, including the dose.
13. Enter the number of days of the current therapy.
14. Assess whether the indication for therapy is appropriate.
15. Determine whether the antimicrobial agent is appropriate for the infection site and organism.
16. Evaluate whether the dosage is suitable considering renal function.
17. Determine whether the dosing frequency is suitable.
18. Decide if the patient is a candidate for de-escalation (reducing the spectrum or switching to an oral formulation).
19. If de-escalation is recommended, provide a de-escalation recommendation (if applicable).
20. Identify any significant drug interactions.
21. Assess the patient's tolerance to therapy (adverse effects).
22. Evaluate the clinical response to therapy.
23. Enter the planned total duration of therapy.
24. Provide any stewardship recommendations (if applicable).
25. Decide on the need for a follow-up assessment and select the recommended frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment date**: Enter the date of the assessment to track progress and ensure timely follow-up.
* **Patient medical record number**: Enter the patient's unique identifier for accurate record keeping and tracking.
* **Patient name**: Enter the patient's name for reference and identification purposes (optional).
* **Clinical reviewer name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider conducting the assessment.
* **Admission date or therapy start**: Select the date the therapy was initiated or the patient was admitted.
* **Site of infection**: Choose the correct location of the infection from the provided options.
* **Was culture obtained before antibiotics started?**: Indicate whether a culture was taken before antibiotics were initiated.
* **Culture and sensitivity results available?**: Confirm whether culture and sensitivity results are available for the patient.
* **Organism identified**: If the organism has been identified, enter the known organism.
* **Susceptibility results for current agent**: If the results are available, select the susceptibility results for the current antimicrobial agent.
* **Current antimicrobial agent(s)**: Enter the name and dose of the antimicrobial agent(s) being used.
* **Days of current therapy**: Enter the number of days the therapy has been ongoing.
* **Is the indication for therapy appropriate?**: Assess whether the reason for administering therapy is suitable.
* **Is the antimicrobial agent appropriate for infection?**: Determine whether the antimicrobial agent is suitable for the infection site and organism.
* **Is dosage appropriate?**: Evaluate the current dosage considering the patient's renal function.
* **Is dosing frequency appropriate?**: Assess whether the dosing frequency is adequate.
* **Is the patient a candidate for de-escalation?**: Decide if the patient's condition is suitable for de-escalation.
* **De-escalation recommendation (if applicable)**: Provide a recommendation for de-escalation if necessary.
* **Are there significant drug interactions?**: Identify any substantial interactions between medications.
* **Is the patient tolerating therapy well?**: Evaluate the patient's tolerance to therapy, including any adverse effects.
* **Clinical response to therapy**: Assess the patient's clinical progress and response to therapy.
* **Planned total duration of therapy**: Enter the estimated total duration of therapy.
* **Stewardship recommendations (if applicable)**: Provide recommendations for improving therapy or reducing unnecessary antimicrobial use.
* **Follow-up assessment recommended?**: Determine the need for a follow-up assessment and select the recommended frequency.
