# Healthcare Staff Competency Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Healthcare Staff Competency Assessment Form is used to evaluate the skills and abilities of healthcare staff members in areas such as communication, leadership, and problem-solving.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant information for each field, and fill in the required details.
2. Choose the correct answers for each section based on your knowledge and experience.
3. Provide comments and ratings as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Unit** (`id: 3`): Select the healthcare unit where the assessment took place (e.g., Clinic, Hospital).
* **Staff Member** (`id: 4`): Enter the name of the staff member being assessed.
* **Competency Assessed** (`id: 5`): Choose the area of competency being assessed (e.g., Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving).
* **Assessment Type** (`id: 9`): Choose the type of assessment (e.g., Form, Survey, Quiz).
* **Competency Assessed 2** (`id: 13`): Choose the second area of competency being assessed (e.g., Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving).
* **Healthcare Unit**, **Assessment Type**, **Competency Assessed 2**, and **Submitted By** fields are required for this form. The other fields are optional.
 
## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate information in all fields.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY, HH:MM AM/PM).
* If you need help or have questions, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
