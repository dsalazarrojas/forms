# health_services_capacity_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "health_services_capacity_assessment" form is designed to collect information about the capacity and capabilities of a healthcare service. This form is used to assess the capacity of a healthcare service to provide adequate care and support to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide Patient Info**: Enter the patient's information, including any necessary details about their medical history and current condition.
2. **Enter Hospital Info**: Provide information about the healthcare service, including its address, contact details, and any relevant medical equipment or facilities available.
3. **Service Info**: Describe the services offered by the healthcare service, including any specialized care or treatment options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: <label> (patient_info, text, required/optional): This section is used to enter basic patient information, such as name, date of birth, and medical history.
* **Hospital Info**: <label> (hospital_info, text, required/optional): Enter information about the healthcare service, including its address, phone number, and any relevant medical equipment or facilities.
* **Service Info**: <label> (service_info, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the healthcare service, including any specialized care or treatment options available.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all necessary information about the patient and the healthcare service before completing this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the healthcare service directly for clarification.
* This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other forms and assessments to provide a comprehensive understanding of the healthcare service's capacity and capabilities.
