# Family Background Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Background Assessment form is used to collect information about a household's demographics and lifestyle. This form is used to gather data for assessment and planning purposes in healthcare settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided with each section.
3. If you are unsure about any field, seek clarification from the person or organization administering the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **household_member_1** (`household member 1`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the first household member.
* **household_member_2** (`household member 2`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the second household member.
* **household_income** (`household income`, number, required/optional): Enter the total household income for the year.
* **household_size** (`household size`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people living in the household.
* **family_relationships** (`family relationships`, select multiple, required/optional): Indicate any family relationships, such as spouse, child, or other relatives.
* **medical_history** (`medical history`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions that may be relevant to the household.
* **household_location** (`household location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the household resides.
* **contact_details** (`contact details`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the household.
* **family_vehicles** (`family vehicles`, text, required/optional): Enter any information about vehicles owned or used by the household.
* **household_income_source** (`household income source`, select one, required/optional): Indicate the source of the household income (e.g. employment, retirement, etc.).
* **family_vehicles_years** (`family vehicles years`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of the oldest vehicle.
* **family_vehicles_miles** (`family vehicles miles`, number, required/optional): Enter the total miles driven by all household vehicles in the past year.
* **family_vehicles_years_driving** (`family vehicles years driving`, number, required/optional): Enter the years the oldest vehicle has been in use.
* **family_vehicles_other** (`family vehicles other`, text, required/optional): Enter any other information about vehicles not listed.
* **family_vehicles_years_other** (`family vehicles years other`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of other vehicles not listed.
* **family_vehicles_miles_other** (`family vehicles miles other`, number, required/optional): Enter the total miles driven by other vehicles in the past year.
* **family_vehicles_maintenance** (`family vehicles maintenance`, number, required/optional): Enter the annual maintenance cost of all household vehicles.
* **family_vehicles_repair** (`family vehicles repair`, text, required/optional): Enter any recent repairs or maintenance issues with household vehicles.
* **family_vehicles_maintenance_cost** (`family vehicles maintenance cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the total maintenance cost of all household vehicles.
* **family_members** (`family members`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional family members not already listed.
* **family_members_years** (`family members years`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of additional family members.
* **family_members_males** (`family members males`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of males in the household.
* **family_members_females** (`family members females`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of females in the household.
* **family_members_children** (`family members children`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of children in the household.
* **family_members_children_years** (`family members children years`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of children in the household.
