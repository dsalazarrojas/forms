# Medico Legal Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Medico Legal Assessment Questionnaire is designed to collect comprehensive information from patients or individuals undergoing medical evaluation. It is a crucial tool for medical professionals to gather the necessary details to provide accurate and effective medical treatment and care.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information for medical evaluation.)
4. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your **Occupation** in the "Occupation" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
6. Enter your **Contact E-mail** in the "Contact E-mail" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
7. Enter your **Contact Phone** in the "Contact Phone" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
8. Enter the **Date of Medical Evaluation** in the "Date of Medical Evaluation" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Consent** in the "Consent" field.
10. Provide a brief description of your **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Medical Condition** in the "Medical Condition" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
12. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Medical Treatment** in the "Medical Treatment" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
13. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Medical Test** in the "Medical Test" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
14. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Medical Result** in the "Medical Result" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
15. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Medical Recommendation** in the "Medical Recommendation" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
16. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Medical Certification** in the "Medical Certification" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
17. Enter your **Medical Certification Number** in the "Medical Certification Number" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
18. Enter the **Medical Certification Issue Date** in the "Medical Certification Issue Date" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
19. Enter the **Medical Certification Expiration Date** in the "Medical Certification Expiration Date" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
20. Enter the name of the **Medical Issuer** in the "Medical Issuer" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
21. Enter a brief description of the **Medical Investigation** in the "Medical Investigation" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
22. Enter a brief description of the **Medical Evidence** in the "Medical Evidence" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
23. Enter a brief description of the **Medical Report** in the "Medical Report" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
24. Enter a brief description of the **Medical Document** in the "Medical Document" field. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide accurate information.)
25. Enter your **Medical Signature** in the "Medical Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Please enter your current address.
* **Occupation**: Please enter your current occupation.
* **Contact E-mail**: Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Medical Evaluation**: Please enter the date of your medical evaluation.
* **Consent**: Please select "Yes" if you consent to medical treatment, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical History**: Please provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Medical Condition**: Please select "Yes" if you have a medical condition, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Treatment**: Please select "Yes" if you have received medical treatment, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Test**: Please select "Yes" if you have undergone a medical test, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Result**: Please select "Yes" if you have received a medical result, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Recommendation**: Please select "Yes" if you have received a medical recommendation, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Certification**: Please select "Yes" if you have medical certification, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Certification Number**: Please enter your medical certification number.
* **Medical Certification Issue Date**: Please enter the date when your medical certification was issued.
* **Medical Certification Expiration Date**: Please enter the date when your medical certification expires.
* **Medical Issuer**: Please enter the name of the person who issued your medical certification.
* **Medical Investigation**: Please enter a brief description of the medical investigation.
* **Medical Evidence**: Please enter a brief description of the medical evidence.
* **Medical Report**: Please enter a brief description of the medical report.
* **Medical Document**: Please enter a brief description of the medical document.
* **Medical Signature**: Please sign your medical report with your signature.

## Tips
Please ensure that you complete all fields accurately and truthfully. The information collected will be used for medical evaluation and treatment purposes. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with a medical professional for guidance.
