# Metabolic Age Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Age Assessment form is designed to estimate a person's biological age based on their physical characteristics and lifestyle habits. It's intended for individuals who want to have a general understanding of their health status and make informed decisions about their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in numerical fields with accurate measurements.
3. Select the correct option for each select fields.
4. Leave any fields blank if you do not have the information or are unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Height** (`height`, Number, Not Required): Enter your height in centimeters (cm) to the nearest 0.5 cm.
- **Weight** (`weight`, Number, Not Required): Enter your weight in kilograms (kg) to the nearest 0.1 kg.
- **Age** (`age`, Number, Not Required): Enter your age in years.
- **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, Select One, Not Required): Select one of the following:
  - Sedentary (little to no exercise)
  - Lightly active (light exercise/sports 1-2 days/week)
  - Moderately active (light exercise/sports 3-5 days/week)
  - Very active (hard exercise/sports 6-7 days/week)
- **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, Select One, Not Required): Select one of the following:
  - Less than 7 hours
  - 7-8 hours
  - More than 8 hours
- **Smoke** (`smoke`, Select One, Not Required): Select one of the following:
  - False (do not smoke)
  - True (smoke)
- **Alcohol Consumption** (`alcohol_consumption`, Select One, Not Required): Select one of the following:
  - Light
  - Moderate
  - Heavy
- **Exercise Days** (`exercise_days`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select all the days you engage in light or hard exercise/sports:
  - Yes (Monday)
  - Tuesday
  - Wednesday
  - Thursday
  - Friday
  - Saturday
  - Sunday
  - Other
  (Note: If you do not exercise, select "No")
- **Metabolic Age** (`metabolic_age`, Number, Not Required): This field will be populated with your estimated metabolic age.
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, Note, Not Required): Enter any health recommendations or concerns you have after completing the form.
- **Estimated Body Mass Index (BMI)** (`estimated_body_mass_index`, Number, Not Required): This field will be populated with your estimated body mass index.
