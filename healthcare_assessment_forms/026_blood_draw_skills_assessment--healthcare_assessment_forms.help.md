# Blood Draw Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Blood Draw Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate the competency of healthcare professionals in performing blood draw procedures. This form helps assessors evaluate the skills and performance of candidates in various aspects of phlebotomy, including hand hygiene, PPE usage, patient identification, and other critical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before starting the assessment.
2. Begin by filling out the required information:
	* `Candidate Name`: Enter the name of the candidate undergoing the assessment.
	* `Assessment Date`: Enter the date of the assessment.
	* `Assessor Name`: Enter your name and credentials.
	* `Overall Competency`: Select the candidate's overall competency level (Competent, Needs Improvement, or Not Competent).
3. Evaluate the candidate's skills in each section and enter the corresponding score:
	* `Prior Blood Draw Training Completed`: Indicate whether the candidate has completed prior training in blood draw procedures.
	* `Hand Hygiene - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's hand hygiene score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `PPE Usage - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's PPE usage score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Patient Identification - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's patient identification score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Venipuncture Site Selection - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's venipuncture site selection score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Skin Preparation - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's skin preparation score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Needle Insertion Technique - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's needle insertion technique score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Tube Filling Sequence - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's tube filling sequence score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Specimen Handling - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's specimen handling score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Specimen Labeling - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's specimen labeling score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
	* `Safety Procedures - Score (1-5)`: Rate the candidate's safety procedures score from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
4. Provide any additional comments or recommendations for the candidate in the `Assessor Comments` field (optional).
5. If needed, indicate whether remedial training is required and schedule a `Reassessment Date` for the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name**: The name of the candidate undergoing the assessment.
* **Employee or ID Number**: The unique identifier or employee number of the candidate (if applicable).
* **Assessment Date**: The date of the assessment, when the candidate's skills were evaluated.
* **Assessor Name**: The name of the assessor performing the evaluation, along with their credentials.
* **Assessor Credentials**: Any additional information about the assessor's qualifications or expertise.
* **Prior Blood Draw Training Completed**: Indicate whether the candidate has completed prior training in blood draw procedures.
* **Hand Hygiene - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's hand hygiene from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **PPE Usage - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's PPE usage from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Patient Identification - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's patient identification from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Venipuncture Site Selection - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's venipuncture site selection from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Skin Preparation - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's skin preparation from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Needle Insertion Technique - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's needle insertion technique from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Tube Filling Sequence - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's tube filling sequence from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Specimen Handling - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's specimen handling from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Specimen Labeling - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's specimen labeling from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Safety Procedures - Score (1-5)**: Rate the candidate's safety procedures from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Overall Competency**: Select the candidate's overall competency level (Competent, Needs Improvement, or Not Competent).
* **Remedial Training Needed**: Indicate whether remedial training is required for the candidate.
* **Reassessment Date**: Schedule a reassessment date for the candidate (if needed).
* **Assessor Comments**: Provide any additional comments or recommendations for the candidate (optional).

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use the score ratings to provide specific and objective feedback to the candidate.
* Be thorough in your evaluation and provide constructive comments to help the candidate improve their skills.
* Follow established protocols and procedures for blood draw assessments to ensure accuracy and fairness.
