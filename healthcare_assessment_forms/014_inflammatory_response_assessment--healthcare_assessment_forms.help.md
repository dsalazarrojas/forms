# Inflammatory Response Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for patients undergoing medical assessment for inflammatory response.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide general information about the patient, including their contact details.
2. Report any symptoms and history of the patient.
3. List any health conditions the patient is experiencing.
4. Specify any medications the patient is taking.
5. Mention any allergies the patient has.
6. Enter the patient's medical record number.
7. Choose a contact preference for the next step in the assessment.
8. Provide an email address for communication.
9. Provide a phone number for communication.
10. Enter the patient's medical record number (again, for confirmation).
11. Report any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
12. List up to 8 symptoms the patient is experiencing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant general information about the patient.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required/optional): Provide the patient's contact information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Symptoms and History** (`Symptoms and History`, text, required/optional): Report any symptoms and history the patient is experiencing.
* **Health Conditions** (`Health Conditions`, text, required/optional): List any health conditions the patient is experiencing.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, required/optional): Specify any medications the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, required/optional): Mention any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Contact Preference** (`Contact Preference`, text, required/optional): Choose a contact preference for the next step in the assessment.
* **Email** (`Email`, text, required/optional): Provide an email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Provide a phone number for communication.
* **Medical Record Number (Confirmation)** (`Medical Record Number`, text, required/optional): Re-enter the patient's medical record number for confirmation.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, text, required/optional): Report any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Symptoms 1-8** (`Symptoms 1`, `Symptoms 2`, `Symptoms 3`, `Symptoms 4`, `Symptoms 5`, `Symptoms 6`, `Symptoms 7`, `Symptoms 8`, text, required/optional): List up to 8 symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Medical Condition 1-3** (`Medical Condition 1`, `Medical Condition 2`, `Medical Condition 3`, text, required/optional): List up to 3 medical conditions the patient is experiencing.

Tips:

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
* This form is for assessment purposes only and should not be used for any other purpose.
* The data collected through this form will be used for medical professionals to better understand and diagnose patients.
