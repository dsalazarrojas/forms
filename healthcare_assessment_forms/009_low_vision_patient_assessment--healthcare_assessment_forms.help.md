# Low Vision Patient Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Low Vision Patient Assessment form is designed to collect information on a patient's vision health and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your personal details such as your name, age, date of birth, medical record number, and contact information (phone, email, and address).
2. Describe your current vision level and any difficulties you experience with your vision.
3. If you have any medical conditions that affect your vision, select them from the list provided.
4. If you have any other eye conditions, please provide a brief description.
5. Indicate if you are expecting a follow-up and provide the date and time of the follow-up.
6. Enter the details of your follow-up appointment, including the provider, location, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: This field is used to capture the patient's name as per the patient record.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age in numbers.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medical Record Number**: This field is used to store the patient's medical record number.
* **Vision Level**: This field asks you to select the current level of your vision from the list provided.
* **Visual Difficulty**: This field is used to capture any difficulties you experience with your vision, such as blindness, perceiving colors, depth, textures, distance, or near vision.
* **Goal for Vision**: This field is used to capture your personal goal for vision improvement.
* **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions that affect your vision from the list provided.
* **Eye Condition**: This field is used to capture any other eye conditions you may have.
* **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your appointment in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address.
* **Note**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Follow-up**: Indicate if you are expecting a follow-up.
* **Appointment Scheduled**: Indicate if the follow-up appointment is scheduled.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up appointment in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Provider**: Enter the name of the follow-up provider.
* **Follow-up Location**: Enter the location of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Phone**: Enter the phone number of the follow-up provider.
* **Follow-up Email**: Enter the email of the follow-up provider.
* **Follow-up Address**: Enter the address of the follow-up location.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the dates and times you enter for accuracy.
* If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
