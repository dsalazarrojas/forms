# Cardiology ROI Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the Return on Investment (ROI) of cardiology departments in healthcare organizations. It collects data on various aspects of cardiology department operations, including organization information, departmental metrics, and investment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
2. Review each field carefully to guarantee the accuracy of the data collected.
3. Use the available options to select the most appropriate responses for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization (hospital or clinic) being assessed.
- **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of organization (e.g., Hospital, Outpatient Clinic, etc.).
- **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
- **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department being assessed (e.g., Cardiology, Cardiovascular Surgery, etc.).
- **Annual Cardiology Patient Volume** (`annual_patient_volume`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate number of cardiology patients treated by the department annually.
- **Current Technology Investment Level** (`current_technology_investment`, `select_one`, required): Select the current level of technology investment in the department (e.g., Under $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, etc.).
- **ROI Metrics Currently Tracked** (`roi_metrics_tracked`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all metrics currently tracked by the department (e.g., Patient outcomes, Readmission rates, etc.).
- **Current Patient Satisfaction Score** (`patient_satisfaction_score`, `text`, optional): Enter the current patient satisfaction score (percentage if available).
- **30-Day Readmission Rate** (`readmission_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the 30-day readmission rate (percentage for cardiac patients).
- **Average Length of Stay** (`average_length_of_stay`, `number`, optional): Enter the average length of stay (in days for cardiac patients).
- **Procedure Volume Trend** (`procedure_volume_trend`, `select_one`, required): Select the trend in procedure volume over the past 12 months (e.g., Increasing Significantly, Stable, etc.).
- **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of staff satisfaction (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
- **Quality Improvement Initiatives** (`quality_improvement_initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all quality improvement initiatives currently active (e.g., ACC Chest Pain MI Registry, etc.).
- **Identified Technology Gaps** (`technology_gaps`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all identified technology gaps (e.g., Electronic health records integration, etc.).
- **Budget Constraints for New Investments** (`budget_constraints`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of budget constraints for new investments (e.g., No constraints, Moderate constraints, etc.).
- **Expected ROI Time Frame** (`expected_roi_timeframe`, `select_one`, required): Select the expected ROI timeframe for new investments (e.g., Less than 6 months, 6-12 months, etc.).
- **Priority Investment Areas** (`priority_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top 3 priority areas for investment (e.g., Diagnostic equipment, etc.).
- **Competitive Position in Market** (`competitive_position`, `select_one`, required): Select the competitive position in the market (e.g., Market leader, Average, etc.).
- **Assessment Contact Name** (`assessment_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the assessment contact person.
- **Assessment Contact Title** (`assessment_contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of the assessment contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the assessment contact person.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
