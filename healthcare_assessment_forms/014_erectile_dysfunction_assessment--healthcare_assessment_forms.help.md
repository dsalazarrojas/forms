# Erectile Dysfunction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Erectile Dysfunction Assessment form is used to gather information about a patient's condition related to erectile dysfunction. This form is designed to be completed by the patient and will help healthcare professionals to evaluate and manage their condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. For free-text fields, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: This page is used to collect basic information about you, such as your name and contact details. Please fill in your name, address, and phone number accurately.

### Symptoms (Page 2)
* **Symptoms**: This question is about your experiences with erectile dysfunction symptoms. Please answer "Yes" if you have any of the following symptoms:
	+ Difficulty achieving or maintaining an erection
	+ Reduced erection quality or duration
	+ Difficulty with ejaculation or orgasm

### Medical History (Page 3)
* **Medical History**: Please list all medical conditions that have been diagnosed with you in the past 5 years. This will help us to understand your overall health status.

### Treatment Plan (Page 4)
* **Treatment Plan**: Describe your current treatment plan, including any medications or therapies you are taking.

### Medications (Page 5)
* **Medications**: This question is about your current medications. Please answer "Yes" if you are taking any of the following:
	+ Medications for erectile dysfunction
	+ Other medications that could be related to your condition

### Follow-up Appointments (Page 6)
* **Follow-up Appointments**: List any upcoming appointments or scheduled follow-ups.

### General Question (Page 7)
* **General Question**: This is an open-ended question for you to share any additional information about your condition or concerns. Please provide as much detail as possible.

### Contact Info (Page 8)
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
