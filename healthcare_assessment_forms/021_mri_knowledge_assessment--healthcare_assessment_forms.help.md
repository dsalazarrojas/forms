# MRI Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess staff knowledge and proficiency in performing Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and accurately. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of MRI procedures. Please answer all questions in the relevant sections, and do not leave any fields blank if they are required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is an MRI** (1, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your understanding of what an MRI is and how it works.
- **What are the safety risks of MRI** (2, text, optional): Please list the safety risks associated with MRI procedures.
- **What are the MRI procedure steps** (3, text, optional): Describe the steps involved in performing an MRI procedure.
- **MRI equipment usage** (4, text, optional): Explain how you use MRI equipment during a procedure.
- **What are the MRI indications** (5, text, optional): List the medical conditions that are indicated for MRI procedures.
- **What are the MRI contraindications** (6, text, optional): List the medical conditions that are contraindicated for MRI procedures.
- **What are the MRI safety procedures** (7, text, optional): Describe the safety procedures used during MRI procedures.
- **What are the MRI emergency procedures** (8, text, optional): Explain the emergency procedures used during MRI procedures.
- **How often do you have MRI staff training** (9, select_one, optional): Select how frequently you receive MRI staff training.
  - **Never**
  - **Occasionally**
  - **Frequently**
  - **Regularly**
- **How many MRI form submissions do you have in a week** (10, number, optional): Enter the number of MRI forms you submit per week.
- **How many minutes does it take to fill out an MRI form** (11, number, optional): Enter the time it takes to fill out an MRI form.
- **How many total MRI forms have you submitted** (12, number, optional): Enter the total number of MRI forms submitted.
- **Date of last MRI form submission** (13, date, optional): Enter the date of your last MRI form submission.
- **Time of last MRI form submission** (14, time, optional): Enter the time of your last MRI form submission.
- **Total MRI submissions** (15, number, optional): Enter the total number of MRI forms submitted.
- **How many minutes it takes to fill out an MRI form per week** (16, number, optional): Enter the average time it takes to fill out an MRI form per week.
- **How many MRI forms are submitted per week** (17, number, optional): Enter the average number of MRI forms submitted per week.
- **Total MRI forms submitted** (18, number, optional): Enter the total number of MRI forms submitted.
- **Number of staff** (19, number, optional): Enter the total number of staff members.
- **How much time does it take on average to fill out an MRI form per staff member** (20, number, optional): Enter the average time it takes to fill out an MRI form per staff member.
- **Total staff hours spent filling out MRI forms** (21, number, optional): Enter the total time spent by staff members filling out MRI forms.
- **Average MRI forms filled out per staff member** (22, number, optional): Enter the average number of MRI forms filled out by each staff member.
- **Total MRI forms submitted per staff member** (23, number, optional): Enter the total number of MRI forms submitted by each staff member.
- **Total staff hours spent filling out MRI forms per week** (24, number, optional): Enter the total time spent by staff members filling out MRI forms per week.
- **Total forms submitted per week** (25, number, optional): Enter the total number of forms submitted per week.
