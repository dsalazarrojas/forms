# Acupuncture SOAP Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acupuncture SOAP Notes Form is designed to record and document patient sessions, providing a structured format for practitioners to note observations, treatments, and progress.

## How to Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the session details: patient name, date of the session, and session/visit number.
2. In the subjective section, record the patient's report of symptoms and experience, including their reported pain level.
3. In the objective section, describe the practitioner's observations and diagnostic findings, including tongue diagnosis and pulse diagnosis.
4. In the assessment section, identify the TCM pattern and clinical impression.
5. Outline the clinical impression/progress toward goals.
6. Note any acupuncture points treated and any other modalities used during the session.
7. Provide a plan for the next session, including any herbal recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Practitioner Name** (`Practitioner Name`, required): Enter your name, as the practitioner performing the session.
* **Date of Session** (`Session Date`, required): Enter the date of the session using the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
* **Session / Visit Number** (`Session / Visit Number`, optional): Enter the session/visit number, if applicable.
* **Subjective** (`Client's Report of Symptoms and Experience`, optional): Record the patient's report of symptoms and experience.
* **Patient Reported Progress or New Concerns** (`Patient Reported Progress or New Concerns`, required): Record any new concerns or progress reported by the patient.
* **Subjective Pain Level (0-10)** (`Subjective Pain Level (0-10)`, required): Record the patient's reported pain level on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Objective** (`Practitioner's Observations and Diagnostic Findings`, optional): Describe the practitioner's observations and diagnostic findings, including tongue diagnosis and pulse diagnosis.
* **Tongue Diagnosis** (`Tongue Diagnosis`, required): Describe the color, shape, coating, etc. of the patient's tongue.
* **Pulse Diagnosis** (`Pulse Diagnosis`, required): Describe the rate, depth, quality, etc. of the patient's pulse.
* **Physical Observations** (`Physical Observations`, optional): Record any postural, skin color, range of motion, or palpation tenderness observed during the session.
* **Assessment** (`TCM Pattern and Clinical Impression`, optional): Identify the TCM pattern and clinical impression.
* **TCM Pattern / Diagnosis** (`TCM Pattern / Diagnosis`, required): Identify the TCM pattern and diagnosis.
* **Clinical Impression / Progress Toward Goals** (`Clinical Impression / Progress Toward Goals`, required): Record the clinical impression/progress toward goals.
* **Clinical Impression / Progress Toward Goals** (`Clinical Impression / Progress Toward Goals`, required, with options: `Significant Improvement`, `Moderate Improvement`, `Slight Improvement`, `No Change`, `Regression`): Select the clinical impression/progress toward goals from the options provided.
* **Acupuncture Points Treated** (`Acupuncture Points Treated`, required): List the acupuncture points treated during the session.
* **Needle Technique and Retension Time** (`Needle Technique and Retension Time`, optional): Record the needle technique and retention time used during the session.
* **Other Modalities Used** (`Other Modalities Used`, optional, with options: `Cupping`, `Moxa`, `Gua Sha`, `Tui Na`, `Electro-Acupuncture`): Select any other modalities used during the session.
* **Plan for Next Session / Herbal Recommendations** (`Plan for Next Session / Herbal Recommendations`, required): Record any plan for the next session, including any herbal recommendations.
* **Next Scheduled Appointment** (`Next Scheduled Appointment`, optional): Enter the date and time of the next scheduled appointment.
