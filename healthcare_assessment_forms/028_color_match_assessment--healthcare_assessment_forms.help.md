# Color Match Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Match Assessment form is designed to help healthcare professionals match a client's skin tone and natural hair and eye colors with suggested color palettes, providing a personalized approach to skincare and makeup recommendations. This form helps to identify a client's skin tone and hair color, allowing for tailored advice on how to enhance their appearance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's full name in the **Client name** field.
2. Select the **Assessment date** in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Choose the client's **Skin tone description** from the options: Warm, Cool, or Neutral.
4. If applicable, enter the client's **Hair color** and **Eye color**.
5. Provide a list of suggested **Recommended color palette** for the client.
6. If applicable, upload photos of the client for further assessment (select **Photos attached: True**).
7. Provide any **Additional recommendations** for skincare or makeup.
8. Consent to use the client's **photos and data** for the assessment (select **Consent to use images and data: True**).



## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name (text, required)**: Enter the full name of the client being assessed.
*  **Assessment date (date, required)**: Select the date of the assessment in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Skin tone description (select_one, required)**: Choose the description that best matches the client's skin tone: Warm, Cool, or Neutral.
* **Hair color (text, optional)**: If applicable, enter the client's natural or current hair color.
* **Eye color (text, optional)**: If applicable, enter the client's natural eye color.
* **Recommended color palette (text, required)**: Provide a list of suggested colors for the client.
* **Photos attached (select_one, optional)**: Indicate if photos of the client are attached for further assessment.
* **Additional recommendations (text, optional)**: Provide any additional styling or product suggestions for the client.
* **Consent to use images and data (select_one, required)**: Select to consent to the use of client's photos and data for the assessment.
