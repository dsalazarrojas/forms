# Neuro Agility Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neuro Agility Assessment Survey is designed to help you gain insights into your preferences and tendencies in various areas of your work and personal life. This information can be used to inform coaching, training, or development programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best fit your preferences and tendencies for each question. For most fields, you can select multiple options, so be sure to choose all that apply. If a field is not relevant to you, please select "No Preference" or "Not Applicable". For numeric fields (stress level, energy level), use numbers to indicate your level of agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neuro Agility Assessment Survey** (`survey_title`, text, optional): 
	+ This is a title field for your information only, please enter your name if you'd like. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Thinking Preferences** (`thinking_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Choose all that apply from the options provided, as they best fit your preferences for how you think. This could include preferences for clarity, speed, creativity, or other thinking styles.
* **Learning Preferences** (`learning_preferences`, select_one, optional): 
	+ Choose one option that best fits your learning style. This could include preference for visual, auditory, hands-on, or other learning styles.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_one, optional): 
	+ Choose one option that best fits your communication style. This could include preference for direct, indirect, formal, or informal communication styles.
* **Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Choose all that apply from the provided options, as they best fit how you approach solving problems. This could include preferences for structure, flexibility, or other approaches.
* **Feedback Preferences** (`feedback_preferences`, select_one, optional): 
	+ Choose one option that best fits your feedback style. This could include preference for positive, constructive, or direct feedback.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Choose all that apply from the provided options, as they best fit your approach to time management. This could include preferences for being punctual, adaptable, or other styles.
* **Work Preferences** (`work_preferences`, select_one, optional): 
	+ Choose one option that best fits your work style. This could include preference for autonomy, teamwork, or other work styles.
* **Stress Level** (`stress_level`, number, optional): 
	+ Use numbers to indicate your level of stress, with higher numbers indicating higher stress levels.
* **Energy Level** (`energy_level`, number, optional): 
	+ Use numbers to indicate your level of energy, with higher numbers indicating higher energy levels.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Choose all that apply from the provided options, as they best fit your motivations. This could include preferences for intrinsic, extrinsic, or other motivators.

Note: Please answer honestly and accurately based on your personal experiences.
