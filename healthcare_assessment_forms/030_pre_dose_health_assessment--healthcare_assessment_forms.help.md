# pre-dose-health-assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre-dose-health-assessment" form is used to collect information about a patient's health status before administering a medication or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information about the patient's health status.
2. For each field, select the correct option or enter the required information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks whether the patient has any allergies. Select "Yes" or "No" based on your assessment.
* **Patient Medications** (`patient_medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks whether the patient is currently taking any medications. Select "Yes" or "No" based on your assessment.
* **Recent Symptoms** (`recent_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks whether the patient is experiencing any recent symptoms. Select "Yes" or "No" based on your assessment.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks whether the patient has given their consent for the treatment. Select "Yes" or "No" based on your assessment.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for a description of the patient's current medical condition. Enter a brief description of the condition.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, `text`, required: false): This field asks for any relevant medical treatment information related to the patient's condition. Enter any relevant details.
* **Consent Text** (`consent_text`, `note`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or notes from the patient regarding their consent.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: false): This field asks for the date the patient gave their consent for the treatment.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have obtained the patient's consent before administering any treatment.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you have collected all necessary information.
* If the patient has any medical conditions or allergies, do not administer the treatment until their safety and well-being can be assured.
