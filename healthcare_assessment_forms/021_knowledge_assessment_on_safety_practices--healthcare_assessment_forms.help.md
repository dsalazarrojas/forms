# Knowledge Assessment On Safety Practices - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Knowledge Assessment On Safety Practices form is designed to evaluate employees' knowledge and confidence in various aspects of safety practices. This form helps identify potential safety concerns and areas that require additional training or support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your knowledge and confidence in each of the safety practices listed below.
2. Please answer each question as accurately as possible based on your current knowledge and practices.
3. Ensure that you have not completed this form recently or as part of another evaluation process to avoid duplication of effort.
