# Healthcare Professional Screener - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information about healthcare professionals working at your institution. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Ensure that you have permission from the healthcare professional to submit their data.
3. Answer each field according to the healthcare professional's actual status.
4. Review the data before submitting it for internal use only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Staff Screener** (<code>screener_form</code>, <type>text</type>, required>false): This field allows you to enter your name or title.
* **Roles** (<code>roles</code>, <type>select_one</type>, required:false): Select the roles of the healthcare professional. You can choose one role from the provided options, such as General Medicine, Emergency Medicine, or Surgical Medicine.
* **Medical Specialty** (<code>medical_specialty</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required:false): Choose the medical specialty of the healthcare professional. You can select multiple options, such as Family Medicine, Pediatrics, or Cardiology.
* **Medical License** (<code>medical_license</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required:false): Select the status of the healthcare professional's medical license. You can choose options such as Active, Lapsed, or Expired.
* **Work Experience** (<code>work_experience</code>, <type>number</type>, required:false): Enter the healthcare professional's work experience in years.
* **Certifications** (<code>certifications</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required:false): List any medical certifications held by the healthcare professional. Select multiple options, such as ACLS/BLS, BLS/ACLS, or PALS.
* **Medical Language** (<code>medical_language</code>, <type>select_one</type>, required:false): Select the language proficiency of the healthcare professional. You can choose one language from the options, such as English, Spanish, or Mandarin.
* **Medical Credentials** (<code>medical_credentials</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required:false): Choose the medical credentials of the healthcare professional. Select multiple options, such as Board Certified, Board Eligible, or Residency Completed.

## Tips

* Double-check that you have accurate information about the healthcare professional before submitting the form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you select the correct options.
* If unsure about any field, please ask the healthcare professional for clarification.
* Please do not submit duplicate entries for the same healthcare professional. 

This document will help healthcare professionals understand how to use this form correctly. Please review and share this guide with your staff.
