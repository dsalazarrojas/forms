# icu risk assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICU Risk Assessment Form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's risk level and condition. This form helps healthcare professionals quickly and accurately assess a patient's risk and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the ICD-10 code in the "ICD Code" field.
3. Enter the patient's risk score, which should be a numerical value.
4. Enter the date of the risk assessment.
5. Enter the name of the healthcare professional completing the form.
6. Enter the contact number (phone number or other contact information).
7. Enter any additional patient notes, if applicable.
8. Enter the patient's email address.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification card.
* **ICD Code** (`icd_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the ICD-10 code associated with the patient's condition, if applicable.
* **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value indicating the patient's risk level, with higher values indicating higher risk.
* **Risk Assessment Date** (`risk_assessment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **Healthcare Professional Name** (`healthcare_professional_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the healthcare professional completing the form.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number or other contact information.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's risk assessment.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter the patient's email address.
* **ICU Risk Assessment Form Notes** (`icu_risk_assessment_form_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's risk assessment.
* **ICU Risk Assessment Form Comments** (`icu_risk_assessment_form_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any final comments or observations about the patient's risk assessment.

## Tips
* This form is intended for use in the ICU setting.
* Ensure that you have the necessary training and experience to complete this form accurately.
* Review the patient's chart and medical records for more information before completing this form.
* Enter the patient's risk score and assessment date as accurately as possible.
* Use the patient notes field to add any additional information about the patient's condition.
* Use the comments fields to add any final observations or recommendations about the patient's risk assessment.
