# Social Media Engagement Metrics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Social Media Engagement Metrics Report. The report will summarize engagement metrics for various social media platforms, including Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the platforms for which you want to collect engagement metrics.
2. Choose the date range for which you want to collect metrics.
3. Fill in any other necessary fields, such as report title, description, and report type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`1`): Enter your name and any other relevant information. (optional)
* **Select Platform** (`2`): Choose the social media platforms for which you want to collect metrics. (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram)
	+ Select one or multiple platforms to collect metrics.
* **Engagement Metrics** (`3`): Enter the engagement metrics you want to collect for the selected platforms. (e.g., likes, comments, shares)
* **Engagement Date Range** (`4`): Enter the date range for which you want to collect metrics.
* **Date** (`5`): Enter the date for which you want to collect metrics. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)
* **Time** (`6`): Enter the time for which you want to collect metrics. (Format: HH:MM)
* **Select Multiple** (`7`): Choose the social media platforms for which you want to collect metrics. (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram)
	+ Select one or multiple platforms to collect metrics.
* **Engagement Metrics 2** (`8`): Enter the engagement metrics you want to collect for the selected platforms. (e.g., likes, comments, shares)
* **Engagement Date Range 2** (`9`): Enter the date range for which you want to collect metrics.
* **Date 2** (`10`): Enter the date for which you want to collect metrics. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)
* **Time 2** (`11`): Enter the time for which you want to collect metrics. (Format: HH:MM)
* **Email** (`12`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`13`): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`14`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **User Note** (`15`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the user.
* **Email Address** (`16`): Enter the email address of the user.
* **User Phone** (`17`): Enter the phone number of the user.
* **User Note 2** (`18`): Enter any additional notes or comments from the user.
* **Email 2** (`19`): Enter the email address of the user.
* **Phone 2** (`20`): Enter the phone number of the user.
* **Report Type** (`21`): Choose the type of report you want to generate. (Summary, Detail, Raw Data)
* **Report Title** (`22`): Enter the title of the report.
* **Report Description** (`23`): Enter a brief description of the report.
* **Report Date** (`24`): Enter the date for which the report is generated. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)
* **Submit** (`25`): Click this button to submit the form and generate the report.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the report is generated correctly.
