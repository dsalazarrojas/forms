# Social Media Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your social media presence and goals. Please fill out this form to ensure that your social media accounts are properly set up and aligned with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page and select the social media platforms you use.
2. Fill out the role and experience fields with a brief description of your work experience and role within the company.
3. Provide a short bio in the bio field.
4. Answer the questions regarding your social media goals, expected outcome, and target audience.
5. Choose the content strategy and type you prefer.
6. Select the content topics you'd like to create and distribute.
7. Determine your content scheduling frequency and distribution channels.
8. Indicate whether you'll use analytics tools and report your results frequently.
9. Choose the reporting channels and method you'll use.
10. Select the social media channels you'll be managing.
11. Describe the channels you'll use for engagement.
12. Define your engagement strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Platforms**: Select all the social media platforms you use, such as Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, or Instagram.
* **Role**: Briefly describe your role within the company.
* **Experience**: Describe your experience in the company.
* **Bio**: A short description of you and your background.
* **Social Media Goals**: Indicate if you're interested in achieving social media goals.
* **Job Description**: Briefly describe your job duties.
* **Expected Outcome**: Describe what you hope to achieve with social media.
* **Social Media Platforms Listed**: Indicate if you've listed all your social media platforms.
* **Target Audience**: Describe your target audience.
* **Content Strategy**: Choose a content strategy that suits your goals.
* **Content Type**: Describe the type of content you'd like to create.
* **Content Topics**: Select all the content topics you'd like to cover.
* **Content Scheduling**: Determine how frequently you'll post content.
* **Content Distribution**: Choose your content distribution channels.
* **Analytics Tool**: Indicate if you'll use analytics tools.
* **Reporting Frequency**: Determine how frequently you'll report your results.
* **Reporting Method**: Choose your reporting method.
* **Reporting Channels**: Select all the channels you'll use for reporting.
* **Social Media Channels**: Indicate if you'll manage all your social media channels.
* **Channels Description**: Describe the social media channels you'll use.
* **Channels Goals**: Indicate if you have social media goals for each channel.
* **Engagement Strategy**: Describe your engagement strategy.
* **Engagement Channels**: Choose the channels you'll use for engagement.
* **Channels Description**: Briefly describe the channels you'll use for engagement.
