# Social Media Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Audit Form is a data collection tool designed to gather information about your social media presence, engagement, and demographics. This form helps us understand your social media strengths and weaknesses, allowing us to provide valuable insights for marketing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the platforms where you have a social media presence by choosing the relevant options in the "Engagement Metrics" field.
2. For each selected platform, indicate whether you have a presence (Yes) or not (No).
3. Select the demographics that best describe your target audience or community in the "Demographics" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title:** Social Media Audit Form
* **Engagement Metrics** (id: 2, required): Please select the platforms where you have a social media presence. This field is required.
	+ Options: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn
* **Demographics** (id: 12, required): Select the demographics that best describe your target audience or community. This field is required.
	+ Options: Age, Location, Income, Gender
* **Engagement Metrics 2** (id: 7, required): Repeat the process for the second set of engagement metrics.
	+ Options: Yes, No
