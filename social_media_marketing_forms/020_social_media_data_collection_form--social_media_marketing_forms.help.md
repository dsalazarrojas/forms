# Social Media Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Data Collection Form is used to collect detailed information about a specific social media platform. This form helps to track and analyze social media data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant social media platform(s) you are dealing with.
2. Enter the URL for the social media platform.
3. Enter a comma-separated list of handles (e.g., usernames) associated with the account.
4. Enter the number of followers for the account.
5. Specify whether the account's purpose is for personal or professional use.
6. Select the type(s) of account (if applicable).
7. List all account owners separated by commas.
8. Enter any additional information about the account.
9. Provide your email address and phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Media Platform**: Select one or more from the options provided (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram).
* **Platform URL**: Enter the website URL for the social media platform.
* **Account Handles**: Enter a list of handles (usernames) separated by commas.
* **Follower Count**: Enter the number of followers for the account.
* **Account Purpose**: Select whether the account is for personal or professional use.
* **Account Type**: Select the type(s) of account (if applicable).
* **Account Owners**: List all account owners separated by commas.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any extra details about the account.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
* Use a comma to separate multiple handles in the Account Handles field.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
