# Social Media Post Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to gather feedback about your experience with a social media post. Your input will help us understand what you like and dislike about the post, and how we can improve our content creation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. You can submit your answers without leaving any fields blank, but it's essential to answer all questions.
3. If you want to share more about your answer, you can provide additional details in the text fields (e.g., social_media_question_3, social_media_question_15, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### social_media_post_feedback_survey_page_1

* **What was your experience with this social media post?** (social_media_question_1, select_one): Answer this question to describe your experience with the post. You can choose from three options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **What can be improved?** (social_media_question_2, text): Share your thoughts on what could be improved about the post.
* **Would you recommend this post to a friend?** (social_media_question_4, select_one): Tell us whether you would recommend the post to a friend, with two options: True or False.
* **Which social media platform do you use most often?** (social_media_question_5, select_multiple): Choose the social media platforms you use most frequently.
* **How important is social media to your daily life?** (social_media_question_6, number): Rate how important social media is to your daily life on a scale.
* **How many hours do you spend on social media per week?** (social_media_question_7, number): Report how many hours you spend on social media each week.
* **What time of day do you usually check your social media accounts?** (social_media_question_8, time): Tell us when you usually check your social media accounts during the day.
* **Email** (email_input, email): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (phone_input, text): Provide your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback (optional).
