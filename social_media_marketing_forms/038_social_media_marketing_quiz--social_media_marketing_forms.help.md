# Social media marketing quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a company's social media marketing strategy, goals, and practices. The information collected will help the company to better understand their current social media situation, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Provide specific and detailed responses whenever possible.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each multiple-choice question.
4. If a question does not apply to your situation, select "None" or "N/A" as a placeholder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you know about social media marketing?**: This question is intended to gauge your current understanding of social media marketing principles and practices.
* **Which social media platforms do you use?**: Please select all the platforms that your company uses for social media marketing.
* **What are your social media goals?**: This question is designed to help us understand what you aim to achieve through social media marketing.
* **Who is your social media target audience?**: Please select who your company's social media target audience is.
* **What is your content strategy?**: Select all the content strategies that your company uses.
* **Which channels do you use for content?**: Please select all the channels that your company uses for content distribution.
* **What is your engagement strategy?**: This question is intended to help us understand how you interact with your target audience.
* **How do you measure success on social media?**: Please select how you measure your social media success.
* **How do you allocate your budget?**: This question is designed to understand how you budget for social media marketing.
* **How much time do you dedicate to social media marketing?**: Please enter the number of hours you dedicate to social media marketing per week.
* **What is your social media budget?**: Enter your total social media budget.
* **Who is your social media manager?**: Please select who is responsible for social media marketing in your company.
* **Which automation tools do you use?**: Select all the social media automation tools that your company uses.
* **Do you use a content calendar?**: This question is intended to determine if you use a content calendar.
* **What are your analytics tools?**: Please select all the analytics tools that your company uses.
* **Do you use email marketing?**: This question is designed to determine if you use email marketing for social media.
* **What is your return on investment?**: Please enter your return on investment for social media marketing.
* **What time zone are you in?**: This question is intended to help us determine your location.
* **What time zone offset do you have?**: Please enter your time zone offset.
* **Date**: Please enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time**: This question is designed to help us determine your completion time.
* **Additional notes**: Please provide any additional comments or notes that will help us better understand your company's social media strategy.
