# Instagram Profile Analysis Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for analysis and potential outreach to users regarding their Instagram profile. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to facilitate a smooth analysis process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the "User Name" field. This is the name you use to identify yourself on Instagram.
2. Provide your **Email ID** in the "Email ID" field. This is the email address associated with your Instagram account.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This is the phone number associated with your account or the one you'd like to be contacted on.
4. Enter the **Profile URL** of your Instagram profile in the "Profile URL" field. This is the direct link to your profile, typically in the format of http://instagram.com/yourusername.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter the name you use to identify yourself on Instagram.
* **Email ID**: Enter the email address associated with your Instagram account.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number associated with your account or the one you'd like to be contacted on.
* **Profile URL**: Enter the direct link to your Instagram profile in the format of http://instagram.com/yourusername.

## Tips
- Ensure that the email address entered is associated with your Instagram account.
- If you are having trouble finding your Profile URL, try searching for your username on Instagram and copying the link from your browser's address bar.
