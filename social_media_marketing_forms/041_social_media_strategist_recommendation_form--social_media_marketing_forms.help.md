# Social Media Strategist Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Strategist Recommendation Form is designed to help identify potential social media marketing strategies based on user preferences and team characteristics. It collects data on social media channel preferences, team size, budget, communication frequency, team location, and client relationship to provide personalized recommendations for a social media strategist.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you have a social media marketing strategy in place. If you do, select "Yes" from the **social_media_channels** field. If not, select "No".
2. Indicate how many team members you have in your team from the **team_members** field.
3. Select your preferred social media channels from the **preferred_channels** field.
4. Answer how often you communicate with your team from the **communication_frequency** field.
5. Choose the size of your team from the **team_size** field.
6. Enter your budget for social media marketing efforts in the **budget** field.
7. Select your relationship with the client from the **client_relationship** field.
8. Indicate which communication channels you use to communicate with your team from the **communication_channels** field.
9. Choose your team's location from the **team_location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **social_media_channels** (Select multiple)
	+ **Yes**: You have a social media marketing strategy in place.
	+ **No**: You do not have a social media marketing strategy in place.
* **team_members** (Select multiple)
	+ **Yes**: Your team is larger than 1 person.
	+ **No**: Your team is 1 person or less.
* **preferred_channels** (Select one)
	+ **Yes**: You prefer to use a particular social media channel.
	+ **No**: You do not prefer to use a particular social media channel.
* **how_often_do_you_communicate_with_your_team** (Select one)
	+ **Yes**: You frequently communicate with your team.
	+ **No**: You do not frequently communicate with your team.
* **team_size** (Select one)
	+ **Yes**: Your team is larger than 2 people.
	+ **No**: Your team is 2 people or less.
* **budget** (Number)
	+ This field is used to enter your budget for social media marketing efforts.
* **client_relationship** (Select multiple)
	+ **Yes**: You have a strong relationship with the client.
	+ **No**: You do not have a strong relationship with the client.
* **communication_channels** (Select multiple)
	+ Indicate which communication channels you use to communicate with your team.
* **team_location** (Select one)
	+ **Yes**: Your team is located in a particular city or region.
	+ **No**: Your team is not located in a particular city or region.

## Tips
* Answer all fields truthfully to ensure accurate recommendations.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the options provided.
* This form is not intended to be filled out multiple times by the same user. If you need to fill out the form again, start from the beginning and answer truthfully.
