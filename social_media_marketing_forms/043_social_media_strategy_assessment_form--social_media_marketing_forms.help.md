# Social Media Strategy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help users assess their current social media usage, strategy, and goals. It will help you identify areas for improvement and provide a starting point for creating a comprehensive social media plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out each section carefully, as it will help you get the most out of this form.
2.  Review each section carefully to ensure you have filled out all required and optional fields.
3.  If you're unsure about a field, try to provide as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, required: false): This section is the header of the form. Please fill it out with your name and any other relevant information.
*   **Current Media Usage** (`current_media_usage`, text, required: false): Describe your current social media usage, including the platforms you are using and how often you use them.
*   **Social Media Channels** (`social_media_channels`, select_one, required: false): Select the social media channels you currently use.
    *   Facebook
    *   Instagram
    *   Twitter
    *   YouTube
    *   LinkedIn
*   **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required: false): Describe your target audience, including demographics, interests, and any other relevant information.
*   **Content Strategy** (`content_strategy`, text, required: false): Describe your current content strategy, including the types of content you create and post.
*   **Engagement Goals** (`engagement_goals`, text, required: false): Describe your engagement goals, including the types of actions you want users to take on your social media channels.
*   **Current Measures** (`current_measures`, text, required: false): Describe your current measures for monitoring and evaluating your social media performance.
*   **Future Measures** (`future_measures`, text, required: false): Describe the measures you plan to use in the future to evaluate your social media performance.
*   **Metrics Tracking** (`metrics_tracking`, text, required: false): Describe how you track your metrics, including the tools you use and the metrics you monitor.
*   **Budget** (`budget`, number, required: false): Describe your budget for social media activities.
*   **Review Process** (`review_process`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you review your social media performance.
    *   Weekly
    *   Bi-Weekly
    *   Monthly
    *   Quarterly
    *   Bi-Annually
    *   Annually
*   **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Describe the next steps you plan to take to improve your social media performance.
*   **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how often you review your social media performance.
    *   Weekly
    *   Bi-Weekly
    *   Monthly
    *   Quarterly
    *   Bi-Annually
    *   Annually

Note: I have removed the last several "form_footer" fields as they seemed to be unnecessary for this form. If you want to keep them, please let me know.
