# Social Media Engagement Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your social media engagement habits and preferences. Your responses will help us better understand how you interact with our social media platforms and content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the social media platform(s) you have used in the past 30 days.
2. Indicate the level of engagement you have with our social media platforms.
3. Choose your preferred audience type (demographic, psychographic, or behavioral).
4. Select the type(s) of interactions you prefer (comments, messages, videos, photos, or posts).
5. Choose how often you interact with our social media platforms.
6. Select how often you see our posts.
7. Choose the type of content you prefer (text, image, video, or link).
8. Select why you engage with our posts (interesting, informative, entertaining, etc.).
9. If desired, choose a time of day when you usually see our posts.
10. If desired, choose a time of day when you usually interact with our posts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facebook (platform_used)**: Select the social media platform(s) you have used in the past 30 days.
* **Engagement Level (engagement_level)**: Indicate the level of engagement you have with our social media platforms. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ (No option list provided, assume this field will be filled out based on user's actual engagement level)
* **Audience Preference (audience_preference)**: Choose your preferred audience type (demographic, psychographic, or behavioral).
	+ Demographic: Based on age, gender, location, etc.
	+ Psychographic: Based on interests, values, lifestyle, etc.
	+ Behavioral: Based on behavior patterns, habits, etc.
* **Preferred Interactions (interaction_preference)**: Select the type(s) of interactions you prefer (comments, messages, videos, photos, or posts).
	+ Comments: Commenting on posts
	+ Messages: Sending private messages
	+ Videos: Watching videos
	+ Photos: Viewing photos
	+ Posts: Posting content
* **Frequency of Use (frequency_of_use)**: Choose how often you interact with our social media platforms. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Interaction Frequency (interaction_frequency)**: Select how often you interact with our social media platforms. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 1 time
	+ 1-2 times
	+ 3-5 times
	+ More than 5 times
* **Post Type (post_type)**: Choose the type of content you prefer (text, image, video, or link). Please select one of the following options:
	+ Text
	+ Image
	+ Video
	+ Link
* **Engagement Reason (engagement_reason)**: Select why you engage with our posts. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Interesting
	+ Informative
	+ Entertaining
	+ Educational
	+ Instructive
* **Post Schedule (post_schedule)**: If desired, choose a time of day when you usually see our posts. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 14:00, etc.).
* **Interaction Schedule (interaction_schedule)**: If desired, choose a time of day when you usually interact with our posts. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 14:00, etc.).
