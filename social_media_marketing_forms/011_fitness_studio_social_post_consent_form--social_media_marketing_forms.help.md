# Fitness Studio Social Post Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from individuals who participate in social media posts by the Fitness Studio. It ensures that the individuals are aware of the Fitness Studio's terms and conditions and have given their consent to be featured in social media posts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each field explanation for any questions or doubts.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `dd/mm/yyyy`.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you give your consent to be featured in social media posts, 'False' otherwise.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all social media platforms where you would like to be featured, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the trainer who will be working with you.
* **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the trainer.
* **Studio Name** (`studio_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the studio where you will be working out.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the trainer.
* **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Created On** (`created_on`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each field explanation for any questions or doubts.
- Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the Fitness Studio for clarification. 

Note: The Fitness Studio will review and manage all submissions to ensure that they comply with their guidelines and regulations.
