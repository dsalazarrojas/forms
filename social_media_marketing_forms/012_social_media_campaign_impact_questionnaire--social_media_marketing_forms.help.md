# Social Media Campaign Impact Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback on the impact of a social media campaign. Your responses will help us better understand how our campaign reached and engaged with you, and will inform future campaign strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Answer each question truthfully and in good faith**. This will help us gather accurate information and improve our campaign.
- **Complete all required fields**. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
- **Select options from the provided lists**. These lists are available for each question and should be chosen based on your honest experience with the campaign.
- **Use the "Additional comments or feedback" field to provide any further information**. This is an optional field for additional thoughts or opinions that did not fit into any of the other questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you post on Social Media?**: Please select a number that represents how often you post on social media. This question helps us understand your current social media posting frequency.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Please select the way you first heard about us, as it may relate to the campaign you're being asked about. This will help us understand how our campaign reached you.
* **Which platforms did you encounter our campaign on?**: Please select all platforms where you encountered our campaign, as this will help us understand which platforms were effective.
* **How much impact did our campaign have on your life?**: Please select a number that represents your experience with the campaign, from 1 (very ineffective) to 6 (very effective).
* **How effective was our social media campaign?**: Please select a number that represents your opinion on the campaign, from 1 (very ineffective) to 6 (very effective).
* **What did you like about the campaign?**: Please describe anything you enjoyed or appreciated about the campaign. This will help us improve future campaigns.
* **What did you dislike about the campaign?**: Please describe any issues or concerns you encountered with the campaign, or anything you did not like. This will help us correct mistakes and improve future campaigns.
* **Will you participate in further research or surveys about social media campaigns?**: Please select whether you are willing or not willing to participate in further research or surveys. This is an optional question, and your choice will be respected.
* **Additional comments or feedback**: This field is for any additional thoughts or opinions about the campaign that did not fit into any of the other questions. Please use this space to provide a more thorough response.

## Tips

- **Take your time, and answer each question carefully**. This will ensure that we gather accurate and valuable information.
- **If unsure, select "Don't know" or "Unsure"**. This will let us know we need further clarification.
