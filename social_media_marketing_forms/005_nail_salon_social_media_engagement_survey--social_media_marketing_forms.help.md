# Nail Salon Social Media Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather data about your social media presence and engagement strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your salon's name in the "Salon Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is required for us to contact you.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Select one social media platform you use for your salon's online presence from the "Social Media" field.
5. Choose which type(s) of posts you create on your selected social media platform(s) from the "Post Type" field.
6. Rate your engagement score on a scale of 0-10 in the "Engagement Score" field.
7. Enter any additional comments in the "Form Footer" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Name (salon_name)**: Please enter the name of your nail salon.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (phone)**: This field is optional, but if you provide your phone number, we'll be able to contact you more easily.
* **Social Media (social_media)**: Select which social media platform(s) you use to promote your salon.
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Pinterest
* **Post Type (post_type)**: Choose the type(s) of content you create on your selected social media platform(s).
	+ Facebook post
	+ Instagram post
	+ Twitter post
	+ Email newsletter
	+ Text message
* **Engagement Score (engagement_score)**: Rate your engagement on a scale of 0-10.
* **Form Footer (form_footer)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your social media efforts.
