# Social Media Advertising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Social Media Advertising Form is a tool for planning and executing social media advertising campaigns. It guides users to provide essential details about their campaigns, ensuring accurate and efficient execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary information ready before filling out the form.
2. Select the "Social Media Advertising Form" option from the list of available forms.
3. Fill out the form with the required details, following the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, optional): Enter a unique name for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Target Location** (`target_location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the locations where you want to target your social media advertising.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Specify the start date for your social media advertising campaign.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Specify the end date for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, optional): Select the target audience for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Ad Budget** (`ad_budget`, text, optional): Enter the budget for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Ad Budget Currency** (`ad_budget_currency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the currency for your ad budget.
* **Marketing Channel** (`marketing_channel`, select_one, optional): Select the marketing channel for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Specify the start time for your social media advertising campaign.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Specify the end time for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Ad Schedule** (`ad_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the ad schedule for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Ad Budget Allocation** (`ad_budget_allocation`, select_multiple, optional): Allocate your ad budget for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the social media platforms for your campaign.
* **Target Gender** (`target_gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select the target gender for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Target Age** (`target_age`, select_multiple, optional): Select the target age for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Ad Frequency** (`ad_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the ad frequency for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Impressions** (`ad_impressions`, number, optional): Enter the number of impressions for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Clicks** (`ad_clicks`, number, optional): Enter the number of clicks for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Cost** (`ad_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Reach** (`ad_reach`, number, optional): Enter the reach for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Engagement** (`ad_engagement`, number, optional): Enter the engagement for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Conversion Rate** (`ad_conversion_rate`, number, optional): Enter the conversion rate for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Frequency Cap** (`ad_frequency_cap`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency cap for your social media advertising campaign.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned for your social media advertising campaign.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that you've covered all the required fields. This will ensure accurate and efficient execution of your social media advertising campaigns.
