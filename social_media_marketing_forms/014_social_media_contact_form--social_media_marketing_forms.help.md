# social_media_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social_media_contact_form is used to gather contact information and social media presence of the reportee for social media marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Your Information" section.
2. In the "Contact Form" section, enter your email address and phone number.
3. In the "Message" section, enter a brief message about your social media presence.
4. In the "Attach File" section, you can attach any relevant files.
5. In the "Agree" section, check the boxes to agree to the terms and conditions, policy, and privacy policy.
6. In the "Social Media" section, select your presence on various social media platforms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Information (page_1)**:
	+ **Name (reported_name)**: Enter your name.
	+ **Email (reported_email)**: Enter your email address. (Note: This field expects a valid email format.)
	+ **Phone (reported_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Message (reported_message)**: Enter a brief message.
	+ **Attach File (reported_attachment)**: You can attach any relevant files.
* **Contact Form (page_2)**:
	+ **Reporter Info (reporter_info)**: Enter a short information about yourself.
	+ **Reporter Pic (reporter_pic)**: You can upload a picture.
* **Agree**:
	+ **Agree to Terms (reported_agree)**: Check this box if you agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ **Agree to Policy (reported_agree_policy)**: Check this box if you agree to the policy.
	+ **Agree to Privacy (reported_agree_privacy)**: Check this box if you agree to the privacy policy.
* **Social Media**:
	+ **Facebook (reporter_facebook)**: Select your Facebook presence.
	+ **Instagram (reported_instagram)**: Select your Instagram presence.
	+ **Twitter (reported_twitter)**: Select your Twitter presence.
	+ **LinkedIn (reporter_linkedin)**: Select your LinkedIn presence.
	+ **YouTube (reported_youtube)**: Select your YouTube presence.
	+ **Website (reported_website)**: Enter your website URL.
* **Terms and Conditions**:
	+ **Agree to Terms 2 (reported_agree_terms_2)**: Check this box if you agree to terms 2.
	+ **Terms 2 Link (reporter_terms_2_link)**: Enter the link to terms 2.
	+ **Agree to Terms 3 (reported_agree_terms_3)**: Check this box if you agree to terms 3.
	+ **Terms 3 Link (reported_terms_3_link)**: Enter the link to terms 3.
