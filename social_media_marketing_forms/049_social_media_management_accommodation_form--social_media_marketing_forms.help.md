# Social Media Management Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for social media management purposes, including category selection, description, output file, assigned tool, form ID, and additional details such as pages, notes, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select the Category**: Choose the relevant category for your social media management purpose from the drop-down list. This will help the system understand the type of social media management you need assistance with.
2.  **Enter a Description**: Provide a brief description of the social media management task or requirement you're seeking help with.
3.  **Select or Enter the Output File Name**: If you have an output file already prepared, provide its name for quick reference. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
4.  **Assign a Tool**: Choose the tool assigned for the social media management task from the drop-down list. This will ensure the correct tool is applied for the task.
5.  **Enter a Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form, which will be used for reference and tracking purposes.
6.  **Enter Page Numbers**: Provide page numbers for the pages related to the social media management task.
7.  **Add Notes and Contact Information**: Enter any additional notes or contact information that may be helpful for the team to understand the task better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (social_media_management_accommodation_form)**: This is the title of the form, which will be used for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Category (social_media_marketing_forms)**: This field is for selecting the category of the social media management task. Please choose the most relevant option from the provided list.
* **Description (description)**: A brief description of the social media management task or requirement is required. This will help the team understand the purpose of the task.
* **Output File (output_file)**: If you have an output file already prepared, provide its name for quick reference. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Choose the tool assigned for the social media management task from the drop-down list. This ensures the correct tool is applied for the task.
* **Form ID (form_id)**: Enter a unique ID for the form, which is used for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Page Numbers (page_1 to page_8)**: Provide page numbers for the pages related to the social media management task.
* **Notes (note, note_2, note_3)**: Enter any additional notes or comments that may be helpful for the team to understand the task better.
* **Contact Information (email, phone)**: Provide your email address or phone number for the team to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Date and Time (date, time)**: Enter the date and time for the social media management task if it is required.
* **Extra Form Fields (social_media_management_accommodation_form_2 to social_media_management_accommodation_form_5)**: These fields are for additional, specific information related to the social media management task, such as extra notes or comments.

**Tips**
1.  Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
2.  Double-check the category and assigned tool selection to ensure the correct tool is applied to the task.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to the relevant team members for clarification or guidance.
