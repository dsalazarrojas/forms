# social_media_online_course_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media online course enrollment form is designed to collect user information and course preferences for online course enrollment. This form is used to streamline the process of enrolling users in social media online courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select the course(s) you are interested in enrolling for (Online Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Content Creation).
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Choose a date for your course enrollment (optional).
6. Add any additional notes or comments you would like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (text)**: This field is used to collect your name and other relevant user information.
* **Name (text)**: This field is used to enter your name.
* **Age (number)**: This field is used to enter your age.
* **Email (email)**: This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (text)**: This field is used to enter your phone number (optional).
* **Course Selection (select multiple)**: This field allows you to select the course(s) you are interested in enrolling for. Choose from the options: Online Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Content Creation.
* **Date (date)**: This field is used to choose a date for your course enrollment (optional).
* **Note (note)**: This field is used to add any additional notes or comments you would like to include.
