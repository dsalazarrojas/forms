# Social Media Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Photo Release Form is used to obtain permission from clients to share photos taken by the company on social media platforms for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport).
2. Enter your email address that you use to contact you.
3. Provide your phone number (optional, but recommended for easy contact).
4. Select the date of the photo release.
5. Specify the number of photos you are releasing for marketing use.
6. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you grant permission for the photo release.
7. If you choose "Yes", enter the date when the release expires.
8. Enter the name of the person responsible for contact (optional, but recommended for communication).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client's Name**: Please enter your full official name as it appears on your ID or passport. This will help us verify your identity.
*   **Client's Email**: Provide the email address you use to contact you. We'll use this to reach out with any updates or questions.
*   **Client's Phone (optional)**: Enter your phone number for easy communication, but it's not required.
*   **Date of Photo Release**: Select the date when you agree to release your photo for marketing purposes.
*   **Number of Photos**: Enter the number of photos you are releasing for marketing use.
*   **Photo Release Agreement**: Select "Yes" to grant permission for us to share your photos, or "No" if you don't agree. Please note that choosing "No" will prevent us from sharing your photos.
*   **Release Expiration Date (optional)**: If you choose to grant permission, enter the date when the release expires.
*   **Contact Person (optional)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contact, in case we need to reach out with any questions or updates.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
