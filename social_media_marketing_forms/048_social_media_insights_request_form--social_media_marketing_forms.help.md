# Social Media Insights Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Media Insights Request Form is designed to collect information about your company's social media marketing campaigns. This form is intended to help us understand your campaign's goals and objectives, and provide you with relevant insights and advice to improve your online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name and any relevant details about your organization.
2. Provide your contact information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Specify the start and end dates of your social media campaign.
4. Choose the social media channels you want to focus on (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, or YouTube).
5. Indicate if you need analytics for your campaign.
6. Briefly describe your company and its description on social media.
7. Optionally, upload your company logo.
8. Review and submit your form once you have filled out all the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to identify your company. Enter the name of your company as it appears on your website or social media profiles.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your social media profiles or official business correspondence.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your social media profiles or official business correspondence.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address associated with your social media profiles or official business correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of any questions or concerns.
* **Request Start Date** (`request_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the start date of your social media campaign, if applicable.
* **Request End Date** (`request_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the end date of your social media campaign, if applicable.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the URL of your company's website or social media profile.
* **Social Media Channels** (`social_media_channels`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the social media platforms you want to focus on (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, or YouTube).
* **Analytics Needed** (`analytics_needed`, number, optional):
	+ Select the number of analytics you need for your campaign.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your company and its services or products.
* **Company Logo** (`company_logo`, text, optional):
	+ Upload your company's logo to help us provide personalized insights.
