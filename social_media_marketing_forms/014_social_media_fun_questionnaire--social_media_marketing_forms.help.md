# Social Media Fun Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your social media habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the social media platforms you use. You can select multiple platforms.
2. Enter your age.
3. Indicate how often you use social media.
4. Choose your favorite social media platform.
5. Select how often you post on social media.
6. Choose the time you usually post on social media. (This field only appears if you select a specific time for step 5.)
7. Choose the type of content you usually post on social media.
8. Select the people you usually post for on social media.
9. Enter your social media posting goals.
10. Indicate how often you check your social media.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the social media platforms you use.
* **Age** (`age_range`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Social Media Usage** (`social_media_usage`, select_one, required: false): Indicate how often you use social media.
* **Favorite Social Media Platform** (`favorite_social_media`, select_one, required: false): Choose your favorite social media platform.
* **Social Media Posting Frequency** (`social_media_posting_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you post on social media.
* **Time** (`social_media_posting_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you usually post on social media.
* **Content Type** (`social_media_posting_content_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of content you usually post on social media.
* **Target Audience** (`social_media_target_audience`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the people you usually post for on social media.
* **Social Media Posting Goals** (`social_media_posting_goals`, text, required: false): Enter your social media posting goals.
* **Social Media Usage Frequency** (`social_media_usage_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Indicate how often you check your social media.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
Remember to answer honestly, and do not worry about your responses being right or wrong. This form is designed for research purposes only.
