# Social Media Age Verification Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Age Verification Petition Form is a simple, multi-page form designed to collect information from users to verify their age. This form is required for users who wish to engage in certain activities or access specific content on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Form Data" page.
   - This page contains basic information that we need from you to proceed with the verification process.
2. Fill out the "Name" field with your legal name.
   - Please use your real name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
   - We will use this address to communicate with you about your verification status.
4. Fill out the "Age" field with your age.
   - Please enter your age to confirm that you are 18 or older.
5. Select the option that best describes how you heard about this form in the "How did you hear about this form" field.
   - This helps us understand how our users are finding our platform.
6. Choose your country from the list in the "Country" field.
   - Please select your country of residence.
7. Choose your state from the list in the "State" field.
   - Please select your state of residence.
8. Fill out the "City" field with your city name.
   - Please enter the name of your city.
9. Fill out the "Zip" field with your zip code.
   - Please enter your zip code for further verification.
10. Select the option that best describes how you heard about this form in the "Why are you petitioning" field.
    - This helps us understand your reason for filling out this form.
11. Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree" field.
    - Please review and agree to our terms of service.
12. Upload a signature image in the "Signature Image" field.
    - Please upload a clear, recent photo of your signature.
13. Enter your user ID in the "User ID" field.
    - Please enter your user ID or account number.
14. Fill out the "IP Location" field with your IP address.
    - Please enter your IP address for further verification.
15. Select the "Agree" option to agree to our terms of service again.
    - Please review and agree to our terms of service.
