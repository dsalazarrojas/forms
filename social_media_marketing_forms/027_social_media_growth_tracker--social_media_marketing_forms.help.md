# Social Media Growth Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to track social media growth and engagement metrics for your social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the tracking period for which you want to track your social media growth and engagement metrics. You can choose from month, quarter, or year.
2. Choose the platforms you want to track. You can select multiple platforms, such as Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter.
3. Enter the metric names and values for each platform. For each platform, you can enter up to 5 metrics with their corresponding values.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Period** (`id: 1`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Choose the tracking period for which you want to track your social media growth and engagement metrics. You can select from month, quarter, or year.
* **Platform** (`id: 2`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose the platforms you want to track. You can select multiple platforms, such as Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter.
* **Engagement** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Choose the engagement metrics you want to track for each platform. You can select likes, comments, and shares.
* **Metric 1** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the first metric you want to track.
* **Metric 1 Value** (`id: 5`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the value of the first metric you want to track.
* **Metric 2** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the second metric you want to track.
* **Metric 2 Value** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the value of the second metric you want to track.
* ... (Repeat for **Metric 3** to **Metric 11**)

Note that each metric name and value pair should be filled in for the platform and tracking period selected.
