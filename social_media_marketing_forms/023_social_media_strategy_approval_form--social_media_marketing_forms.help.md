# Social Media Strategy Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for new social media campaigns from management. It's a standardized way to ensure that all necessary information is gathered and considered before launching a campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **social media campaign name** in the first field. This is the name of the campaign you're requesting approval for.
2. Provide a brief **campaign description** in the second field. This should include any relevant details about the campaign, such as the target audience and goals.
3. Specify the **target audience** in the third field. This will help management understand who the campaign is targeting.
4. Enter the **campaign start date** in the fourth field. This is the date you plan to start the campaign.
5. Enter the **campaign end date** in the fifth field. This is the date you plan to end the campaign.
6. Enter the **campaign budget** in the sixth field. This is the amount of money allocated for the campaign.
7. Make a **approval decision** in the final field. This will either be "True", "False", or "Maybe" indicating whether the campaign has been approved or rejected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **social media campaign name (1)**: The name of the campaign you're requesting approval for.
* **campaign description (2)**: A brief summary of the campaign's purpose and goals.
* **target audience (3)**: The group of people the campaign is targeting.
* **campaign start date (4)**: The date the campaign will begin.
* **campaign end date (5)**: The date the campaign will end.
* **campaign budget (6)**: The amount of money allocated for the campaign.
* **approval decision (7)**: A yes, no, or maybe decision on whether to approve the campaign.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields completely and accurately.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your team lead for clarification.
