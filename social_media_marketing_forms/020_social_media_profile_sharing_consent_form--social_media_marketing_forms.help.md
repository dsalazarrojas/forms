# social_media_profile_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent for sharing their social media profile information with others.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the social media profiles you want to share.
2. Choose the marketing campaigns you are participating in or wish to participate in.
3. Check the consent for sharing your profile information.
4. Select the scope of sharing (everyone, friends, or specific users).
5. Choose the frequency of sharing (once, often, daily, or multiple times a week).
6. Enter any additional sharing details if needed.
7. Select the tool assigned to you for sharing.
8. Enter a unique form ID (if required).
9. Optionally, enter the output file location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social media profiles** (`social_media_profiles`, text, required/optional): Select one or more social media profiles you are willing to share (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **Marketing campaigns** (`marketing_campaigns`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the marketing campaigns you are participating in or wish to participate in (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Other).
* **Sharing consent** (`sharing_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the boxes to agree to share your profile information, profile picture, posts, and messages with others.
* **Sharing scope** (`sharing_scope`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the scope of sharing, such as sharing with everyone, friends, or specific users.
* **Sharing frequency** (`sharing_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how often you are willing to share your information, such as once, often, daily, or multiple times a week.
* **Sharing details** (`sharing_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about your sharing preferences if needed.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to you for sharing (e.g., Chatjimmy, Other).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique form ID (if required).
* **Output file** (`output_file`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you want the output file to be saved.
