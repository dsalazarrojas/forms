# social_media_marketing_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information and establish permissions for social media marketing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
4. **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number.
5. **Company name**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
6. **Social media platforms**: Select all the platforms you are willing to use for marketing. (Note: This field is required.)
7. **Permissions to use images and testimonials**: Select which platforms you are willing to allow us to use your images and testimonials on. (Note: This field is required.)
8. **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification.
9. **Note**: Add any additional information or comments.
