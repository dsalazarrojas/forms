# Social Media Post Automation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Media Post Automation Form is designed to collect information from users about their social media post preferences. This form helps users schedule and automate social media posts across various platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn. By filling out this form, users can specify the post type, schedule the post time, and add image and text content for their social media posts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the desired social media platform for your post by selecting from the options on the "Post Type" field (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn).
2. Enter the date you want to schedule the post using the "Post Date" field.
3. Add the image you want to use for your post in the "Image Upload" field.
4. Enter the text for your post in the "Post Text" field.
5. Add any additional details for your post by providing a URL in the "Post Url" field.
6. Enter a title and description for your URL in the "Post Url Title" and "Post Url Description" fields.
7. Add any further image information in the "Post Image" and "Post Image Alt" fields.
8. Finally, enter a caption for your post in the "Post Image Caption" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Time** (id: 1, required: false): This field allows users to specify the time at which they want to schedule their social media post.
* **Post Type** (id: 2, required: false): Choose the social media platform for your post (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn).
* **Post Date** (id: 3, required: false): Enter the date you want to schedule the post.
* **Image Upload** (id: 4, required: false): Upload an image for your social media post.
* **Post Text** (id: 5, required: false): Enter the text for your social media post.
* **Post Url** (id: 6, required: false): Add any additional details for your post by providing a URL.
* **Post Url Title** (id: 7, required: false): Enter a title for your URL.
* **Post Url Description** (id: 8, required: false): Enter a description for your URL.
* **Post Image** (id: 9, required: false): Add any further image information for your post.
* **Post Image Alt** (id: 10, required: false): Enter alternative text for your image.
* **Post Image Caption** (id: 11, required: false): Enter a caption for your post.
