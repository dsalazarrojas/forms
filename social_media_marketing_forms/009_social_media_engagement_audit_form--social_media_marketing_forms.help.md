# social-media-engagement-audit-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social-media-engagement-audit-form is designed to collect information about social media accounts, including their type, URL, and engagement metrics. This form is intended for social media account managers or administrators to provide a comprehensive audit of their social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all social media accounts you manage from the list provided.
2. Choose the type of social media account you manage (Personal, Business, or Organization).
3. Enter the URL of the social media account.
4. Choose whether the social media account is Self-Managed or Agency-Managed.
5. (Optional) Enter the date when the social media account was created.
6. (Optional) Enter the date when the social media account was last checked.
7. Enter the total number of followers on the social media account.
8. Enter the age of the social media account in years.
9. Enter the frequency of posts on the social media account.
10. Enter the engagement rate of the social media account (e.g., likes, comments, shares).
11. Enter the total number of posts on the social media account.
12. Enter the total number of engagements on the social media account (e.g., likes, comments, shares).
13. Choose the status of the social media account (Active, Inactive, Suspended).
14. Provide any additional notes or comments about the social media account.
