# Social Media Brand Insights - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your social media presence and engagement strategies. It will help you identify areas for improvement and set realistic goals for growing your social media brand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the social media channels you currently use.
2. Choose the engagement channels you are actively using.
3. Choose your current brand status (High, Medium, Low, or Unknown).
4. Select areas where you feel we can improve on (e.g., Social presence, Engagement, Improvement, Audience, Message, Content, etc.).
5. Describe the strength of your social presence (Strong, Medium, Weak, or None).
6. Describe your improvement goals (write a brief description).
7. Describe your engagement goals (write a brief description).
8. Choose your current improvement status (High, Low, Medium, or None).
9. Write down your next steps for improving your social media presence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What social media channels do you use?** (`social_media_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select all the social media channels you currently use (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, etc.). Choose all that apply.
* **Which engagement channels do you use?** (`engagement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select all the engagement channels you are actively using (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, etc.). Choose all that apply.
* **What is your current brand status?** (`current_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to choose your current brand status (High, Medium, Low, or Unknown).
* **What areas can we improve on?** (`improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select areas where you feel we can improve on (e.g., Social presence, Engagement, Improvement, Audience, Message, Content, etc.). Choose all that apply.
* **How strong is your social presence?** (`social_presence`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to describe the strength of your social presence (Strong, Medium, Weak, or None).
* **What improvement goals do you have?** (`improvement_goals`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing your improvement goals (write a brief description).
* **What engagement goals do you have?** (`engagement_goals`, `text`, required: false): This field is for describing your engagement goals (write a brief description).
* **What is your current improvement status?** (`current_improvement_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to choose your current improvement status (High, Low, Medium, or None).
* **What are your next steps?** (`next_steps`, `note`, required: false): This field is for writing down your next steps for improving your social media presence. 

Note: This form is for collecting information and setting goals, not for actual improvement planning or execution. Please answer based on your current situation and goals.
