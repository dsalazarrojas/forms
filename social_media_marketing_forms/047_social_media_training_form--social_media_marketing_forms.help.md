# Social Media Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information, training needs, and requirements for social media training programs or courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your participant information**: Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. **Provide date**: Enter the date of the training session or course you're interested in.
3. **Specify training needs**: Describe your training needs and requirements in the "Training Needs" field. Be as specific as possible so that trainers can tailor their sessions to your needs.
4. **Choose your training level**: Select your level of experience in social media and training (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. **Select areas of interest**: Select the areas of social media you're interested in learning more about (Social Media, Content Creation, or Analytics).
6. **Confirm your agenda**: Let us know if you have an agenda or plan in place for the training session.
7. **Indicate objectives**: Confirm if you have specific objectives for the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Text)** (`participant_information`, Text, Optional): Enter your basic participant information.
* **Name (Text)** (`name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the training session or course you're interested in.
* **Training Needs (Note)** (`training_needs`, Note, Optional): Describe your training needs and requirements in as much detail as possible.
* **Participant Requirements (Text)** (`participant_requirements`, Text, Optional): Enter any specific requirements you have for the training session.
* **Training Needs Details (Note)** (`training_needs_details`, Note, Optional): Provide more details about your training needs.
* **Training Needs Level (Select)** (`training_needs_level`, Select, Optional, [Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced]): Select your level of experience in social media and training.
* **Training Needs Areas (Select Multiple)** (`training_needs_areas`, Select Multiple, Optional, [Social Media, Content Creation, Analytics]): Select the areas of social media you're interested in learning more about.
* **Has Agenda (Select)** (`has_agenda`, Select, Optional, [True, False]): Let us know if you have an agenda or plan in place for the training session.
* **Has Objectives (Select)** (`has_objectives`, Select, Optional, [True, False]): Confirm if you have specific objectives for the training session.

## Tips

* Be specific and detailed when describing your training needs and requirements.
* If you have any attachments or supporting files, please attach them to the "Training Needs Details" field.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to form@gic.mx.
