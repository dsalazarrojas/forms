# social_media_management_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and manage agreements related to social media management services between businesses and their representatives. It collects essential details such as business names, contact information, agreement terms, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Provide your name as the owner in the "Owner Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
4. Specify the start and end dates of the agreement in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
5. Select the social media platforms you'll be managing in the "Social Media Platforms" field.
6. Enter any additional details or agreements in the "Agreement Term" field.
7. Sign and date the agreement in the "Signature Date" field.
8. Confirm that you've read and agree to the terms in the "Signed" field.
9. Specify the status of the agreement in the "Agreement Status" field.
10. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name as the owner or representative of your business.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Start Date**: Specify the start date of the agreement.
* **End Date**: Specify the end date of the agreement.
* **Social Media Platforms**: Select the social media platforms you'll be managing (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
* **Agreement Term**: Enter any additional details or agreements specific to this management service.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the agreement in this field.
* **Signed**: Confirm that you've read and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Agreement Status**: Specify the current status of the agreement (e.g., Signed and Agreed, Not Signed, Under review, etc.).
* **Termination Date**: Specify the date of termination, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments relevant to the agreement.
* **Business Name 2**: Enter the name of the second business involved, if applicable.
* **Manager Name 2**: Enter the name of the second manager or representative, if applicable.
* **Contact Email 2**: Enter the email address of the second business or representative, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the second business or representative, if applicable.
* **Start Date 2**: Specify the start date of the second agreement, if applicable.
* **End Date 2**: Specify the end date of the second agreement, if applicable.
* **Agreement Term 2**: Enter any additional details or agreements specific to this second management service.
* **Manager Signature 2**: Date and sign the second agreement in this field.
* **Business Signature 2**: Date and sign the second agreement in this field.
