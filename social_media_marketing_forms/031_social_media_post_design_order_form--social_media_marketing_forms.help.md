# Social Media Post Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Post Design Order Form is designed to gather information from users for creating visually appealing and on-brand social media posts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the social media channel you wish to use for your post (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, or YouTube).
2. Choose the type of image or video you want to create (Image, Video, or Story).
3. Select the size of the image (Full Width, Square, or Portrait).
4. Pick a design style that fits your preferences (Minimalist, Creative, Artistic, or Classic).
5. Choose a color scheme that suits your taste (Colorful, Monochromatic, or Pastel).
6. Optionally, enter a text color and provide a description of the image to help us better understand your vision.
