# social_media_content_calendar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by the social media team to collect and organize content details for publication on social media channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of content you are publishing (e.g. Yes/No, Text, Image, Video, etc.).
2. Enter the date the content will be published.
3. Provide a brief description of the content.
4. Add any links or media related to the content.
