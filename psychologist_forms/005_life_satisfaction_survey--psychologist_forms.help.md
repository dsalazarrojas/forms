# Life Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Satisfaction Survey is a self-assessment tool used to measure an individual's general and specific life satisfaction levels. It is not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment. Please answer the questions honestly, and take your time to provide accurate responses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, rating your level of satisfaction or providing a written response as instructed.
2. Choose the correct response option for each question (if applicable).
3. Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
4. Take a moment to review your responses before submitting the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Happiness** (`general_happiness`, number, required): Rate your general happiness level on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Assess your current overall satisfaction level on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Emotional Satisfaction** (`emotional_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your emotional satisfaction level on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Satisfaction with Life** (`satisfaction_with_life`, text, not required): Provide a brief written response describing your satisfaction with life in general.
- **General Happiness Score** (`general_happiness_score`, number, required): A calculated score based on your general happiness rating.
- **Overall Satisfaction Score** (`overall_satisfaction_score`, number, required): A calculated score based on your overall satisfaction rating.
- **Emotional Satisfaction Score** (`emotional_satisfaction_score`, number, required): A calculated score based on your emotional satisfaction rating.
- **Overall Satisfaction with Life Score** (`overall_satisfaction_with_life_score`, text, not required): A calculated score based on your satisfaction with life rating.
- **Emotional Satisfaction with Life Score** (`emotional_satisfaction_with_life_score`, text, not required): A calculated score based on your emotional satisfaction with life rating.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional thoughts or comments about your survey experience.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly, and take your time to provide accurate responses.
* If a field has an option selection, choose the most suitable option based on your current feelings.
* Remember, this survey is for personal assessment and feedback. Do not use it for clinical diagnosis or treatment purposes.
