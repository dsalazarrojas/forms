# Psychometric Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychometric Test is a series of questions designed to assess various psychological and behavioral characteristics. It is intended to provide insights into a user's cognitive abilities, personality traits, and demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the option that best describes your response to each question.
3. Do not skip any questions.
4. Enter your email address if required.

* **test 1** (`test_1`, text, optional): This field is a free-response question where you can enter any text that describes your thoughts, feelings, or experiences related to the question.
* **test 2** (`test_2`, number, optional): This field is a numerical question where you can select a number that best describes your response.
* **test 3** (`test_3`, select_one, optional): Choose one option that best describes your response. The options are: Option A, Option B, and Option C.
* **test 4** (`test_4`, number, optional): This field is another numerical question where you can select a number that best describes your response.
* **test 5** (`test_5`, text, optional): This field is another free-response question where you can enter any text that describes your thoughts, feelings, or experiences related to the question.
* **test 6** (`test_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that best describe your response. The options are: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **test 7** (`test_7`, note, optional): This field is a text area where you can enter any additional comments or notes related to your response.
* **test 8** (`test_8`, email, optional): Enter your email address if required.
* **test 9** (`test_9`, text, optional): This field is another free-response question where you can enter any text that describes your thoughts, feelings, or experiences related to the question.
