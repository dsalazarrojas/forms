# mental_health_awareness_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their mental health experiences. It is intended for patients to rate their satisfaction with the mental health awareness provided by medical professionals.
## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`form_page_1`, `text`, required: false): This is a text input field where you can provide your general feedback about your mental health experience. Please feel free to write anything that you feel is important to share.
* **Medical Professional** (`medical_professional`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to determine if you received mental health services from a medical professional. Please select either "Yes" or "No".
* **question 1** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to determine which questions you were asked during your mental health assessment. Please select all the questions that apply.
* **question 1 answer** (`question_1_answer`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to rate your answer to question 1. Please select either "Yes" or "No".
* **question 2** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to determine which questions you were asked during your mental health assessment. Please select all the questions that apply.
* **question 2 answer** (`question_2_answer`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to rate your answer to question 2. Please select either "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): This field is used for any additional comments you might have about your mental health experience.
* **Medical Professional Rating** (`medical_professional_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate your satisfaction with the medical professional who provided your mental health services. Please select a number between 1 and 5.
* **Patient Rating** (`patient_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate your satisfaction with your mental health experience. Please select a number between 1 and 5.
* **Medical Professional Rating** (`medical_professional`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to determine if you received mental health services from a medical professional. Please select either "Yes" or "No".
