# Personality Research Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather demographic and research information from participants to help researchers better understand personality-related behaviors and trends. By completing this form, participants will be contributing to the development of more effective psychological theories and interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any questions, feel free to ask your researcher for clarification.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
4. If you select "Other" in the demographic information section, you will be given an opportunity to provide additional details.
5. Please enter your answers accurately and honestly to ensure reliable data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro (Page 1)** (`text`, Optional): A brief introduction about yourself or your research goals. Please keep it concise and within the character limit.
* **Demographic Information (Page 2)** (`select_one`, Required): Please select your gender from the options below:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
* **Personality Test (Page 3)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Indicate if you have any of the following personality traits:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Personality Scale (Page 4)** (`number`, Optional): Enter a number that represents your personality scale (not required, but please be honest).
* **Research Goals (Page 5)** (`text`, Optional): Describe your research goals or objectives. Please be clear and specific about your goals.
* **Research Area (Page 6)** (`select_one`, Optional): Please select the area of research you are interested in or conducting:
	+ Personality
	+ Cognitive
	+ Emotional
* **Research Focus (Page 7)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Indicate if you have a research focus on any of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Research Sample Size (Page 8)** (`number`, Optional): Enter the number of participants in your research sample (if applicable).
* **Research Duration (Page 9)** (`time`, Optional): Enter the duration of your research (e.g., days, weeks, months, etc.).
* **Research Status (Page 10)** (`select_one`, Optional): Please select your research status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Pending

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure reliable data.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your researcher.
* Thank you for participating in this research!
