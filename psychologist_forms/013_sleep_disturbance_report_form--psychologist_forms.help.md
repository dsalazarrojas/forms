# Sleep Disturbance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sleep Disturbance Report Form is used to collect data on a participant's sleep patterns, experiences, and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read this guide carefully**: Take a few minutes to review this guide before starting to fill out the form.
- **Only fill out once**: Each participant should fill out the form only once to avoid duplication.
- **Be accurate and honest**: Provide clear and truthful information to help identify potential sleep issues and inform interventions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
• **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, `optional`): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. You can read it once and then start filling out the form.

### Participant Information
• **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any relevant information about yourself as a participant, such as your name, age, or any other relevant context.

### Sleep Patterns
• **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, `number`, `optional`): Indicate your average hours of sleep per night on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is less than 5 hours and 10 is 8 or more hours.

### Sleep Durations
• **Sleep Durations** (`sleep_durations`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the average number of nights you've had difficulty sleeping in the past week (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).

### Sleep Quality
• **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose one of the following options to describe your sleep quality:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent

### Sleep Onset
• **Sleep Onset** (`sleep_onset`, `date`, `optional`): If you've noticed any changes in your sleep patterns, indicate the date you first started experiencing them.

### Bedtime
• **Bedtime** (`bedtime`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time you go to bed every night.

### Wake Up Time
• **Wake Up Time** (`wake_up_time`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time you wake up every morning.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the researcher or supervisor for guidance.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any issues with sleep, please consult a healthcare professional for further assistance.
