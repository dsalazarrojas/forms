# Professional Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Counseling Informed Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from clients before they begin therapy or counseling sessions. It helps establish a safe and successful therapeutic relationship by gathering client information, explaining the risks and limitations associated with therapy, and informing the client about confidentiality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each page and carefully review the information presented.
* Fill out your name, contact information, and emergency contact information in the designated fields.
* Indicate whether you have an emergency contact (Yes/No).
* Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* Review and agree to the risks and limitations associated with therapy.
* Review and acknowledge the confidentiality policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (1)**: Please enter your name, contact details, and any other information you would like to share with your therapist.
* **Informed Consent (2)**: Please read and agree to the terms of consent, which include understanding your rights and responsibilities in the counseling process.
* **Risks and Limitations (3)**: Please review the potential risks and limitations associated with therapy, including the importance of establishing a good doctor-patient relationship.
* **Confidentiality (4)**: Please review and acknowledge the confidentiality policy, which includes understanding how your information will be stored and shared.
* **Emergency Contact (5)**: Please indicate whether you have an emergency contact, and if so, provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact Details (6)**: Please enter your contact information, including your name and any other details you would like to share with your therapist.
* **First Name (7)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (8)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email (9)**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone (10)**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name (11)**: Please enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name (12)**: Please enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship (13)**: Please indicate the relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone (14)**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (15)**: Please enter the email address of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please take your time to review each page carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to discuss them with your therapist.
* Remember to review and sign the form to indicate your understanding and agreement to the terms of consent.
