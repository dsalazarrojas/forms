# object-relations-assessment-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The object-relations-assessment-survey is a tool designed to assess an individual's object relations, gathering information on their current relationships, patterns of relating to others, emotions, and thoughts.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your current relationships or experiences.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief description of your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This page provides a brief overview of the assessment.
### 2. Current Relationships
* **Current Relationships** (`current-relationships`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of relationship you currently have with others, such as:
	+ Parent
	+ Child
	+ Sibling
	+ Peer
	+ Friend
	+ Partner
	+ Family Member
	+ Other (please specify)
### 3. Relationship Patterns
* **Relationship Patterns** (`relationship_patterns`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the patterns of relating you have noticed in your relationships, such as:
	+ Positive Patterns
	+ Negative Patterns
	+ Ambiguous Patterns
	+ Conflicted Patterns
	+ Unclear Patterns
### 4. Emotions and Thoughts
* **Emotions and Thoughts** (`emotions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the emotions and thoughts you experience in your relationships, such as:
	+ Anxious
	+ Calm
	+ Happy
	+ Neutral
	+ Sad
	+ Stressed
	+ Worried
### 5. Emotions Intensity
* **Emotions Intensity** (`emotions_intensity`, number, required: false): If you feel comfortable, rate the intensity of your emotions on a scale from 0-10.
### 6. Thoughts and Feelings
* **Thoughts and Feelings** (`thoughts`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your thoughts and feelings related to your relationships.
### 7. Feelings and Thoughts (Time)
* **Feelings and Thoughts (Time)** (`feelings`, time, required: false): Report the amount of time you spend thinking about your relationships and emotions.
### 8. Relationship Patterns Frequency
* **Relationship Patterns Frequency** (`relationship_patterns_frequency`, number, required: false): If you feel comfortable, rate the frequency of your relationship patterns on a scale from 0-10.
