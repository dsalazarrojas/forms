# Character Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Character Profile Form is a tool for gathering and organizing information about fictional characters. It helps users create well-rounded and believable characters by collecting details about their personal, physical, and psychological aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Character Profile Form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field, especially for optional fields.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Name** (1): Enter the name of the character's creator or developer.
* **Project Title** (2): Enter the title of the project or story where this character will appear.
* **Character Full Name** (3): Enter the complete name of the character, including middle names.
* **Character Nickname or Alias** (4): Enter any alternative names or nicknames the character uses.
* **Character Age** (5): Enter the character's current age in the story.
* **Character Gender** (6): Select the gender identity of the character from the provided options.
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-binary
	+ Genderfluid
	+ Agender
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Character Occupation** (7): Enter the character's current job or role.
* **Physical Appearance Description** (8): Describe the character's physical appearance, including height, build, hair, eyes, and any distinguishing features.
* **Key Personality Traits** (9): Enter up to 5 key personality traits that describe the character, separated by commas.
* **Character Strengths** (10): Select the strengths or abilities that this character possesses, separated by commas.
	+ Intelligence
	+ Physical strength
	+ Emotional resilience
	+ Leadership
	+ Creativity
	+ Empathy
	+ Determination
	+ Loyalty
* **Character Weaknesses** (11): Select the areas of vulnerability or weaknesses that this character has, separated by commas.
	+ Impulsiveness
	+ Self-doubt
	+ Trust issues
	+ Fear of failure
	+ Stubbornness
	+ Perfectionism
	+ Social anxiety
	+ Anger issues
* **Backstory Summary** (12): Provide a brief summary of the character's key life events that shaped them.
* **Primary Motivation** (13): Describe what drives this character to act or make decisions.
* **Character Goals** (14): Enter the character's short-term and long-term objectives.
* **Character Fears** (15): Enter any phobias or deep fears the character may have.
	+ (Optional)
* **Key Relationships** (16): Enter the names and descriptions of important people in the character's life.
	+ (Optional)
* **Expected Character Arc** (17): Select how the character will change throughout the story.
	+ Positive growth
	+ Negative decline
	+ Redemption
	+ Corruption
	+ Self-discovery
	+ Minimal change
* **Dialogue Style** (18): Describe how the character speaks or communicates with others.
	+ (Optional)
* **Additional Notes** (19): Enter any other relevant details about the character.
	+ (Optional)
