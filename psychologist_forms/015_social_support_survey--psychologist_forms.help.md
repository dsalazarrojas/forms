# social_support_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social_support_survey form is designed to assess how well-supported an individual feels in their social environments. It helps to identify areas where people may be feeling unsupported and inform interventions to improve social support systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer the questions as honestly as possible. Select "Yes" for relationships you have in a particular area, "No" for no relationships, and "None" when you have no relationships in a particular area. For the satisfaction questions, select the number that best represents your level of satisfaction. You can also select "No" for relationships you do not have in a particular area.
