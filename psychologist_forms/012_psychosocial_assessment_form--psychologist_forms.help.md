# Psychosocial Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychosocial Assessment Form is used to gather comprehensive information about a patient's mental health history, emotional state, and support systems to provide a holistic understanding of their overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's basic demographic information, such as name, age, and contact details.
2. Review each section below, marking 'Yes' or 'No' according to the patient's status.
3. Select all applicable options in each section to provide a comprehensive picture of the patient's psychosocial status.
4. Complete all sections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, type: note, required: false): This field allows you to document the patient's mental health history, including any previous mental health conditions, treatments, or hospitalizations.
    - Enter a brief description of the patient's mental health history.

- **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, type: select_one, required: false): This field assesses the patient's current emotional state.
    - Select 'Yes' if the patient is currently experiencing any emotional distress or mental health concerns.

- **Family History** (`family_history`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field inquires about the patient's family history of mental health conditions.
    - Select all 'Yes' if any family members have a history of mental health conditions.

- **Social Support** (`social_support`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This section evaluates the patient's social support network.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient has a support system in place.

- **Coping Mechanisms** (`coping_mechanisms`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field assesses the patient's coping mechanisms.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient uses any of the listed coping mechanisms.

- **Patient Relationships** (`patient_relationships`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field examines the patient's relationships with others.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient has any relationships that may affect their mental health.

- **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, type: select_one, required: false): This field inquires about the patient's treatment plan.
    - Select 'Yes' if the patient has an established treatment plan.

- **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, type: select_one, required: false): This field explores the patient's personal goals.
    - Select 'Yes' if the patient has specific goals they are working towards.

- **Family Goals** (`family_goals`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field evaluates the family's goals and aspirations.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the family has goals that may impact the patient's mental health.

- **Family Relationships** (`family_relationships`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field assesses the patient's relationships with their family members.
    - Select all 'Yes' if any family relationships may be affecting the patient's mental health.

- **Support System** (`support_system`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field examines the patient's support system.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient has a support system in place.

- **Mental Health Services** (`mental_health_services`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field inquires about the patient's mental health services.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient has accessed mental health services.

- **Mental Health Treatment** (`mental_health_treatment`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field explores the patient's mental health treatment.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient has received mental health treatment.

- **Patient Caregiver Relationships** (`patient_caregiver_relationships`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field examines the patient's relationships with caregivers.
    - Select all 'Yes' if any relationships with caregivers may be affecting the patient's mental health.

- **Patient Support Systems** (`patient_support_systems`, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field evaluates the patient's support systems.
    - Select all 'Yes' if the patient has any support systems in place.

Note: All fields are optional, and the patient can choose not to answer if they wish. This form is designed to be completed by mental health professionals with the patient's consent.
