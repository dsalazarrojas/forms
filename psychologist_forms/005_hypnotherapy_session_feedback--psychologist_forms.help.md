# Hypnotherapy Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with a hypnotherapy session. It helps us understand how we can improve our services and better serve our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible, based on your experience with the session.
2. For each field, provide a clear and concise response that reflects your thoughts and feelings.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Session** (`feedback_session`, text, not required): Please describe your experience with the hypnotherapy session, including what you liked and disliked.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, not required): Please select one of the following options to rate your experience:
	+ Poor
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, not required): Please select a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest rating and 5 is the highest rating.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the session.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggest_improvements`, text, not required): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our services, please provide them here.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, email, not required): If you would like to get in touch with us, please enter your email address. We will get back to you as soon as possible.
* **Therapist's Name** (`therapist_name`, text, not required): Please provide the name of your therapist, if you can recall it.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any attachments or files to share, please contact us separately.
* We appreciate your input and look forward to serving you better in the future.
