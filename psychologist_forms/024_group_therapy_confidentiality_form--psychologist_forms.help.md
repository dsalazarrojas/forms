# Group Therapy Confidentiality Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group Therapy Confidentiality Form is designed to be completed by clients participating in group therapy sessions to understand and agree to the confidentiality terms of their treatment. This form helps establish a clear agreement between the client and the therapist on the level of confidentiality expected during sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please read this form carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
- Complete each field with accurate and truthful information.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your therapist for clarification.
- Once completed, please hand this form over to your therapist before starting the therapy sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required/optional: false):
	* This section is intended to capture basic contact and identifying information about the client. Please provide your full name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.

- **Privacy Terms** (`Privacy Terms`, text, required/optional: false):
	* In this section, please describe your understanding of what is confidential and what is not. You may also list specific information that you do not want shared with others or kept confidential.

- **Confidentiality Agreement** (`Confidentiality Agreement`, text, required/optional: false):
	* In this section, please confirm your agreement to keep all information discussed during group therapy sessions confidential. This includes respecting the confidentiality of other clients' information and not sharing any details about others' experiences or identities.

- **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required/optional: false):
	* If you need to provide contact information for an emergency contact, please enter their name, phone number, and email address (if available). This is for the therapist's use in case of an emergency or extreme circumstances.

- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional: false):
	* In this section, please provide any additional contact information that you think would be relevant for your therapist to have in case of emergencies or follow-up sessions. This may include your address, social media handles, or other relevant details.

## Tips
- Please remember that this form is not a substitute for a full consultation with your therapist. If you have any questions or concerns, always speak with your therapist directly.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you have any issues with confidentiality, discuss them with your therapist before starting the therapy sessions.
- Remember that confidentiality is a two-way agreement. You should not share any confidential information about others or other clients.
