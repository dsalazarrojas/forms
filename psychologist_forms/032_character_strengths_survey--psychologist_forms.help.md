# Character Strengths Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Character Strengths Survey" is designed to help individuals identify and evaluate their character strengths, exploring how often they are applied and their impact on daily functioning. This survey aims to understand the respondent's strengths, their frequency of use, and how they support daily tasks and development goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name (if you choose to provide it for anonymity).
2. Identify your primary strength in the "strength 1" field.
3. List your additional strengths in the "strength 2" and "strength 3" fields (if applicable).
4. Select how often you use these strengths in the "frequency of use" field. Choose from "Rarely", "Sometimes", "Often", or "Always".
5. Describe how these strengths impact your daily functioning in the "impact on functioning" field.
6. If relevant, mention your development goal related to your strengths in the "development goal" field.
7. Indicate the support you need to further develop your strengths in the "support needed" field.
8. Finally, enter the date of submission in the "survey date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Strengths Survey --** (`header`, no hint): This is the title of the survey.
* **respondent name** (`respondent_name`, optional): This is where you can enter your name if you wish to remain anonymous, but it is not required.
* **strength 1** (`strength_1`, required): Describe your primary strength in this field.
* **strength 2** (`strength_2`, required): If you have multiple strengths, list them here.
* **strength 3** (`strength_3`, optional): If you have more strengths, list them here.
* **frequency of use** (`frequency_of_use`, required): Select how often you apply your strengths.
* **impact on functioning** (`impact_on_functioning`, optional): Explain how your strengths affect your daily tasks and responsibilities.
* **development goal** (`development_goal`, optional): If relevant, mention a goal you aim to achieve related to your strengths.
* **support needed** (`support_needed`, optional): If needed, specify the support that would help you develop your strengths further.
* **survey date** (`survey_date`, optional): Enter the date of submission.
