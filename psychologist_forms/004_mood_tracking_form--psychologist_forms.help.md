# Mood Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mood Tracking Form is a tool designed to help individuals track their current mood and energy levels, as well as log their progress over time. The form is a simple and efficient way to gather information about a user's emotional state and identify patterns or trends.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Mood Tracking Form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction to understand the purpose and instructions of the form.
2.  Fill out the "Introduction" section with a brief description of your current situation or context.
3.  Select your current mood from the dropdown menu in the "How would you rate your current mood?" section.
4.  Select your current energy levels from the dropdown menu in the "How would you rate your current energy levels?" section.
5.  Select your current pain level from the dropdown menu in the "What is your current pain level?" section.
6.  Add any additional comments or insights about your current mood or energy levels in the "Notes" section.
7.  Record the date of each submission in the "Mood Log" section.
8.  Confirm your submission by reading the "Submission Confirmation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`text`, required: false): A text field where you can briefly describe your current situation or context. This will help you and us understand your current situation.
*   **How would you rate your current mood?** (`select_one`, required: false): A dropdown menu with four options: Happy, Sad, Angry, and Anxious. Select the option that best describes your current emotional state.
*   **How would you rate your current energy levels?** (`select_one`, required: false): A dropdown menu with three options: High, Low, and Medium. Select the option that best describes your current energy levels.
*   **What is your current pain level?** (`select_one`, required: false): A dropdown menu with three options: High, Low, and None. Select the option that best describes your current pain or distress levels.
*   **Notes** (`note`, required: false): A text field where you can provide additional comments or insights about your current mood or energy levels.
*   **Mood Log** (`date`, required: false): A date field where you can record the date of each submission.
*   **Submission Confirmation** (`note`, required: false): A text field where you can confirm your submission and receive a confirmation of your progress.

## Tips

*   Take a few minutes to carefully read and complete each section.
*   Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   Use the "Notes" section to provide additional context or insights about your current mood or energy levels.
*   Regularly review and update your "Mood Log" to track your progress over time.
