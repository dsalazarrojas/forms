# Narcissistic Behavior Assessment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Narcissistic Behavior Assessment Quiz is designed to help individuals evaluate their potential narcissistic tendencies. This form is intended to provide a general assessment, not a definitive evaluation or diagnosis. It is not a substitute for professional mental health advice or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. Each question is a statement about potential narcissistic tendencies. Choose the response that best fits your feelings or behaviors.
3. You can select only one response per question.
4. The form will automatically calculate your results based on your answers.
