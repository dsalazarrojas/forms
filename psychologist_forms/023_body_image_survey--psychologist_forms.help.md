# Body Image Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your perceptions and feelings about your body image. Your responses will help us create a safe and supportive environment for individuals to discuss and cope with body image concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Respondent Information**: Please provide your name and email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
   - <code>Respondent Name</code> (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
   - <code>Email Address</code> (email): Enter your valid email address.
   - <code>Age Group</code> (select): Choose your age group from the provided options.
2. Body Image Satisfaction:
   - <code>Overall Body Satisfaction</code> (select): Rate your overall satisfaction with your body (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Facial Appearance Satisfaction</code> (select): Rate your satisfaction with facial appearance (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Body Shape Satisfaction</code> (select): Rate your satisfaction with body shape (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Weight Satisfaction</code> (select): Rate your satisfaction with your weight (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
3. Social Appearance:
   - <code>Appearance in Casual Clothing</code> (select): Rate how you look in everyday clothes (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Appearance in Formal Clothing</code> (select): Rate how you look in formal wear (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Appearance in Swimwear</code> (select): Rate how you look in swimming attire (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Prefer Not to Answer).
   - <code>Satisfaction with Muscle Tone</code> (select): Rate satisfaction with muscle tone (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Satisfaction with Skin</code> (select): Rate satisfaction with skin (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Satisfaction with Hair</code> (select): Rate satisfaction with hair (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
   - <code>Satisfaction with Physical Fitness</code> (select): Rate overall satisfaction with physical fitness (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
4. Confidence and Social Impact:
   - <code>Does appearance affect confidence</code> (select): Rate how much appearance affects your confidence (Greatly, Somewhat, Neutral, Slightly, Not at All).
   - <code>Does appearance affect social interactions</code> (select): Rate how much appearance affects your social interactions (Greatly, Somewhat, Neutral, Slightly, Not at All).
5. Media and Social:
   - <code>Comparison to media images</code> (select): Rate how often you compare yourself to media images (Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
   - <code>Social media impact</code> (select): Rate the impact of social media on your body image (Greatly Negative, Somewhat Negative, Neutral, Somewhat Positive, Greatly Positive, No Impact).
6. Concerns and Impact:
   - <code>Areas of Greatest Concern</code> (select multiple): Select all that apply regarding areas where you have body image concerns.
   - <code>Impact on daily life</code> (select): Rate how much body image affects daily activities (Greatly Impacts, Somewhat Impacts, Neutral, Minimally Impacts, Does Not Impact).
7. Support:
   - <code>Professional help consideration</code> (select): Rate whether you've considered seeking professional support for body image concerns (Yes, currently receiving, Yes, have in the past, Considering, False, Unsure).
8. Additional Comments:
   - <code>Additional Comments</code> (text): If you'd like to share any additional thoughts or insights, please feel free to type them here.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and authentically.
- Take your time, and it's okay to skip questions that make you uncomfortable.
- If unsure, choose the best available option based on your feelings.
- You can also use the "Additional Comments" section to elaborate on your responses or provide any supporting information.
