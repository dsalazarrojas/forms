# Psychology Expert Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple, user-facing feedback form intended to solicit psychology expert feedback. It helps to gauge the satisfaction and experience of clients after a consultation session with a psychology expert.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page to understand the form's purpose.
2. Fill in the required information as prompted:
	* Enter your **name** to identify yourself.
	* Enter your **email address** to allow the expert to contact you (if needed).
	* Select a valid **date** for your appointment to provide context to the feedback.
	* Choose a corresponding **time** for your appointment.
3. Rate your experience with a numerical value in the **rating** field.
4. Answer the question "how would you rate our services" with a numerical value in the **question_1** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form's purpose.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to help the expert identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow the expert to contact you (if needed).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select a valid date for your appointment to provide context to the feedback.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Choose a corresponding time for your appointment.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience with a numerical value (e.g., 1-5).
* **How would you rate our services?** (`question_1`, number, optional): Answer this question with a numerical value indicating your satisfaction with the services provided.

## Tips
Please fill in the form accurately and honestly. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help improve our services.
