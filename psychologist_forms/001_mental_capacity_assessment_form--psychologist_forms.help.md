# Mental Capacity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Capacity Assessment Form is designed to assess the mental capacity of a patient. This form is used to evaluate a patient's ability to understand and communicate their needs and wishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
3. Answer the question "Capacity Status" with either "True" or "False" to indicate the patient's mental capacity.
4. Enter any relevant patient condition information in the "Patient Condition" field.
5. Enter the name and license number of the healthcare provider completing the form in the "Healthcare Provider Name" and "Healthcare Provider License" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Capacity Status** (`capacity_status`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if the patient has mental capacity, "False" otherwise.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, optional): Enter any relevant patient condition information.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the healthcare provider completing the form.
* **Healthcare Provider License** (`healthcare_provider_license`, text, required): Enter your license number as the healthcare provider completing the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have obtained the necessary consent from the patient before completing this form.
* Please be thorough in your assessment of the patient's mental capacity.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult with a colleague or seek additional guidance.
