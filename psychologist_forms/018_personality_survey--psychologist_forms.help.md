# Personality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess various personality traits, such as agreeableness, conscientiousness, extraversion, and others. It's meant to be a general tool for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the option that best fits your personality.
2. Answer all questions honestly, but keep in mind that this is not a diagnostic test and your answers will not be used for any specific purpose.
3. Select one option for each question unless otherwise indicated.
4. For questions with multiple options, choose all that apply.
5. Click "Submit" to complete and save your answers.
