# occupationalTherapyProgressNoteTemplate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The occupationalTherapyProgressNoteTemplate form is designed to provide a structured format for occupational therapists to document client progress, treatment plans, and other essential information. This form helps therapists track client progress, set and evaluate treatment goals, and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Client Info page (page 1), which requires basic client information such as name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
2. Proceed to the Treatment Plan page (page 2), where you will document the client's treatment plan, including goals, objectives, and interventions.
3. On the Progress Notes page (page 3), record the client's progress, including any relevant comments or observations.
4. Continue to the Discharge Info page (page 4), where you will document the client's discharge information, such as any medications or treatments they may be receiving.
5. On the Evaluation page (page 5), evaluate the client's progress and provide feedback.
6. The Client Goals page (page 6) is for recording the client's short-term and long-term goals.
7. On the Treatment Plan 2 page (page 7), document any additional treatment plans or interventions.
8. Move on to the Progress Assessment page (page 8), where you will assess the client's progress.
9. The Treatment Goals page (page 9) is for recording any additional treatment goals.
10. On the Interventions page (page 10), document any interventions or activities implemented.
11. Continue to the Evaluation 2 page (page 11), where you will evaluate the client's progress again.
12. On the Client Feedback page (page 12), record any feedback from the client.
13. Next, document the Progress Notes 2 page (page 13), with any additional progress notes.
14. On the Dischargeinfo 2 page (page 14), document any additional discharge information.
15. Move on to the Additional Info page (page 15), for any extra information not recorded elsewhere.
16. Finally, on the Review page (page 16), enter any review comments or ratings.
17. Enter the Reviewer's name, title, and department on the Reviewer page (page 17).
18. Enter the review date and time on the Time page (page 18).
19. Add a review comment on the Review Comments page (page 19).
20. Enter any additional review comments on the Additional Review page (page 20).
21. Enter the Reviewer's signature on the Reviewer Signature page (page 21).
22. Enter the Reviewer's title on the Reviewer Title page (page 22).
23. Enter the Reviewer's department on the Reviewer Department page (page 23).

## Tips
* Always ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form regularly to ensure that all necessary information is recorded.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a colleague or supervisor.
* Remember to document any changes or updates to the client's treatment plan.
* Reviewer, please ensure that you enter accurate and constructive review comments.
* Always sign and date the review, and enter your department's name.
