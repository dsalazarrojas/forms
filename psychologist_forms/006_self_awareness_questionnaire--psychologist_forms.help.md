# Self Awareness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Awareness Questionnaire is designed to help you develop a better understanding of yourself. By answering these questions, you will gain insight into your self-awareness level, emotions, values, goals, and overall self-awareness development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, considering your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.
2. For select_one questions, choose only one option that best describes you.
3. For select_multiple questions, choose as many options as apply to you.
4. Provide thorough answers in text fields, such as describing your feelings or behaviors.
5. Save your progress and come back to complete any remaining sections if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How would you rate your self-awareness level** (`self_awareness_questionnaire`, number, required/optional): This question helps you evaluate your self-awareness level on a scale. Choose a number that best describes how you see yourself.
- **How would you feel about your feelings** (`self_awareness_feelings`, text, required/optional): Describe how you feel about your emotions, e.g., accepting, suppressing, or acknowledging them.
- **Who is the person you are** (`self_awareness_person`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your personality type: introvert, extrovert, or mix.
- **How do you express your emotions** (`self_awareness_emotions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the emotions you commonly express, such as anger, fear, or joy.
- **How would you describe your thinking style** (`self_awareness_thinking`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes how you typically think, e.g., critical, optimistic, or pessimistic.
- **Describe a behavior that shows self-awareness** (`self_awareness_behaviors`, text, required/optional): Describe a behavior that demonstrates your self-awareness, such as recognizing your emotions or taking time to reflect.
- **What is important to you in life** (`self_awareness_values`, select_multiple, required): Select all the values that matter to you, such as love, respect, or honesty.
- **What are your short-term goals** (`self_awareness_goals`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the short-term goals that are relevant to you, such as achieving a promotion or mastering a new skill.
- **What is most important to you** (`self_awareness_values_important`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that matter most to you, such as love, respect, or honesty.
- **Why are your goals important** (`self_awareness_goals_important`, text, required/optional): Describe why your goals are important to you and how they contribute to your self-awareness.
- **What did you learn about yourself** (`self_awareness_conclusion`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the options that you learned about yourself, such as self-awareness or mindfulness.
- **What did you do to become more self-aware** (`self_awareness_reflection`, text, required/optional): Describe the actions you took to improve your self-awareness, such as practicing mindfulness or seeking feedback.

Note: This form is designed to be completed in a self-paced and individual manner. Please answer the questions honestly, and do not rush through the form. If you need help or clarification, refer to this guide or ask for assistance.
