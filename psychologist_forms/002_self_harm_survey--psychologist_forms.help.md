# Self Harm Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Harm Survey is a confidential and non-judgmental assessment tool designed to gather insights into how individuals who self-harm navigate the risks, emotions, and experiences related to their condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with the corresponding option that best reflects your experiences and feelings. You can select multiple options for each question. Please be honest and open in your responses, and don't worry about what others might think or say.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you ever hurt yourself on purpose (self_harm_experiences)**: This question asks about your personal experiences with self-harm behaviors in the last 2 months. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **If so, how often have you hurt yourself in the last 2 months (self_harm_experiences_details)**: This question is designed to help us understand the frequency of your self-harm behaviors in the last 2 months. Choose the option that best describes how often you have engaged in self-harm activities.

* **Have you ever sought help for self-harm (self_harm_treatment)**: This question is about your willingness to seek support for your self-harm behaviors. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **If so, what was the outcome (self_harm_treatment_details)**: This question helps us understand the effectiveness of your help-seeking behaviors. Choose the options that describe your experiences.
* **How often do you self-harm (self_harm_frequency)**: This question is designed to help us understand the frequency of your self-harm behaviors. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **How often do you think you will self-harm in the next 30 days (self_harm_frequency_details)**: This question is about your predictions and expectations for your self-harm behaviors in the future. Choose the option that best reflects your thoughts.
* **Do you feel like you might die from self-harm (self_harm_mortality)**: This question is about the severity of your self-harm behaviors and how often you think you might die as a result. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Why do you feel like you might die (self_harm_mortality_details)**: This question helps us understand the reasons behind your concerns about self-harm and mortality. Choose the options that best describe your thoughts.
* **How likely do you feel that self-harm is a risk to your life (self_harm_mortality_risk)**: This question is about the level of danger you associate with self-harm. Select all the options that apply to you.

* **What might stop you from self-harm (self_harm_prevention)**: This question is about the strategies you use to prevent self-harm behaviors. Select all the options that apply to you.
