# Psychological Reliability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather essential information about the client to assess their reliability. It includes questions on demographics, occupation, language proficiency, financial stability, emotional stability, coping mechanisms, emergency contact information, and informed consents.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2.  Make sure to answer all required fields.
3.  Review each section carefully to ensure you understand its relevance and importance.
4.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask your therapist or counselor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (2): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Primary Occupation** (3): Select the occupation that best describes your primary role. Options include:
    -   Student
    -   Athlete
    -   Artist
    -   Business Owner
*   **Primary Language** (4): Select your primary language proficiency. Options include:
    -   English
    -   Spanish
    -   French
*   **Primary Income** (5): Enter your current annual income. If you prefer not to disclose this information, you may leave this field blank.
*   **Emotional Stability Rating** (6): Rate your emotional stability on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very unstable and 10 being very stable.
*   **Coping Mechanisms Rating** (7): Rate your coping mechanisms on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very weak and 10 being very strong.
*   **Note** (8): Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the assessment.
*   **Email** (9): Enter your official email address. This will help us contact you in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact** (10): Select who should be contacted in case of an emergency. Options include:
    -   Self
    -   Family
    -   Friend
    -   Other
*   **Emergency Phone** (11): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Client Agreed To Terms** (12): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the assessment.
*   **Informed Consents** (13): Confirm that you have received and understood the informed consents.
*   **Emergency Phone Numbers** (14): Enter multiple phone numbers of your emergency contact, separated by commas.

Please remember to review and understand each section carefully to ensure accurate and thorough assessment. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult your therapist or counselor.
