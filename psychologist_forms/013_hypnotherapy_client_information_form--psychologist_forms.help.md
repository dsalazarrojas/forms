# Hypnotherapy Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from clients, including their name, contact details, medical condition, and date of birth, to assess their suitability for hypnotherapy sessions and create a record for future references.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): This field is where you can enter your full name for the purpose of this form.
* **Email** (email, required): Please enter your valid email address so we can contact you for further information or to schedule appointments.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Medical Condition** (text, optional): Describe your current medical condition or any health concerns you wish to address through hypnotherapy.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your date of birth in a standard date format (e.g., DD/MM/YY).
* **Therapist Notes** (text, optional): This is an optional field for the therapist to take notes during sessions. Please do not enter anything here unless requested by the therapist.
* **Email** (email, optional): This field is actually not required, but please enter a valid email address if you wish to be contacted for further information or to schedule appointments.

Note: The "Therapist Notes" field is intended for the therapist to take notes during sessions, and you should not fill it in unless explicitly instructed to do so.
