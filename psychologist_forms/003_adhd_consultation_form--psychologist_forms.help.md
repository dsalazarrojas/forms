# ADHD Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ADHD Consultation Form is a tool for patients to provide information about their ADHD symptoms, concerns, and background. This form helps the consultant (psychologist or therapist) to get a clear understanding of the patient's situation and prepare for the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully read the instructions and explanations for each field.
2. Answer all the required fields. If you're unsure about any field, please consult the consultant or seek guidance.
3. Be accurate and truthful when providing information about your ADHD symptoms, concerns, and background.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient name
* **Patient name**: This field is to provide your full name as recorded in official documents or as you would like to be referred to.
* **Type: Text**
* **Required: Yes**

### 2. Date of birth
* **Date of birth**: Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format to enter your date of birth.
* **Type: Date**
* **Required: Yes**

### 3. Presenting concerns and symptoms
* **Presenting concerns and symptoms**: In this field, describe your main ADHD symptoms and concerns in as much detail as possible. This will help the consultant understand your situation better.
* **Type: Text**
* **Required: Yes**

### 4. Duration of symptoms
* **Duration of symptoms**: If applicable, please estimate the approximate months or years you have been experiencing these symptoms.
* **Type: Text**
* **Required: No**

### 5. Areas of functional impairment
* **Areas of functional impairment**: Select one or more from the options:
	+ Academic performance
	+ Work performance
	+ Social relationships
	+ Home routines
* **Type: Select Multiple**
* **Required: Yes**

### 6. Prior assessments or diagnoses
* **Prior assessments or diagnoses**: If you have had any previous evaluations or diagnoses, please list them here.
* **Type: Text**
* **Required: No**

### 7. Current medications
* **Current medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including the dosage and the prescribing clinician's name.
* **Type: Text**
* **Required: No**

### 8. Emergency contact phone number
* **Emergency contact phone number**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Type: Text**
* **Required: Yes**

Please note that this form is a guide to help you understand the fields and their purpose. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the consultant for clarification.
