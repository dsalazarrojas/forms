# Color Personality Test - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Color Personality Test is a psychological assessment tool that aims to understand individual personality traits based on the colors people choose. By selecting two colors, you will receive insights into your dominant personality traits, introversion/ extroversion tendency, decision-making style, recommended roles or careers, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, if you wish (optional).
2. Select a date for your assessment (optional, but recommended for tracking progress).
3. Choose two colors from the provided options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange, Teal, and Grey.
4. Select your self-assessment of introversion or extroversion tendency.
5. Describe your general decision-making style.
6. Leave any additional comments or thoughts you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (Optional): This field is for your name, but you can leave it blank if you prefer.
* **Date** (Optional): This field is for tracking your progress, if you wish to do so.
* **Choose color 1** (Required): Select a color that you feel most connected to or identify with the most. This color will help us understand your emotional tone or emotional state.
* **Choose color 2** (Required): Select a second color that you feel represents you or your personality. This color will help us understand your rational or logical side.
* **Dominant personality traits** (Optional): Based on your color selections, we will generate a brief description of your dominant personality traits.
* **Introversion or extroversion tendency** (Optional): Select a self-assessment of whether you tend to be an introvert or extrovert.
* **Decision making style** (Optional): Describe your general approach to making decisions.
* **Recommended roles or careers** (Optional): Based on your test results, we will provide suggested roles or careers that might suit your personality.
* **Additional comments** (Optional): Share any thoughts, feelings, or insights you gained from this test.
* **Recommended roles or careers** (Optional, but this field will be populated with suggested roles/careers after the test): This field will show the suggested roles or careers based on your test results.
 
## Tips

* Be honest with your color selections, as they reflect your subconscious mind's preferences.
* Take your time to carefully select two colors that resonate with you.
* Don't overthink your decisions; just choose the colors that come up naturally.
* You can always revisit this form and retake the test to see how you grow or change over time.
