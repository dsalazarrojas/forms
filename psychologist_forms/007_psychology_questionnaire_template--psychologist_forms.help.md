# Psychology Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's mental health and well-being, which will help mental health professionals develop an appropriate treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, `text`, required=false): Enter the patient's basic information, such as their name and contact details.
2. **Questions List** (`Questions List`, `text`, required=false): Provide a list of questions that the patient has responded to previously or that you would like to ask them in the current session.
3. **Question 1** (`Question 1`, `select_one`, required=false): Choose one option from the provided list that best describes the patient's current state regarding the question.
4. **Question 2** (`Question 2`, `select_multiple`, required=false): Choose multiple options from the provided list that best describe the patient's current state regarding the question.
5. **Question 3** (`Question 3`, `text`, required=false): Enter any additional information or comments regarding the patient's current state.
6. **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required=false): Enter the date of the current session or assessment.
7. **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required=false): Enter the time of the current session or assessment.
8. **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required=false): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the patient's current state.
9. **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required=false): Enter the patient's email address.
10. **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required=false): Enter the patient's phone number.
