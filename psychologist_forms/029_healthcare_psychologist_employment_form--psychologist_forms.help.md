# Healthcare Psychologist Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Psychologist Employment Form is a tool used to collect vital information about healthcare psychologists applying for a position. The purpose of this form is to ensure that you have a better understanding of your qualifications, work experience, and other relevant details that may impact your employment opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details, including your name, email address, and contact number. Make sure to enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Provide your current address to ensure we can contact you for any further information or discussions.
3. In the "Experience" field, describe your previous work experience as a healthcare psychologist, highlighting your relevant skills and qualifications.
4. In the "Education" field, mention your highest level of education achieved, such as a Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.), Doctor of Medicine (M.D.), Master of Science (M.Sc.), or Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.).
5. If applicable, select the languages you are proficient in, such as English, Spanish, or French.
6. Choose your current work experience level, which can be 1-5 years, 5-10 years, or 10-15 years.
7. If you have a salary in mind, please enter it in the "Salary" field.
8. In the "Notice Period" field, enter the date you would like to provide notice if you are hired.
9. Enter the start date for your intended notice period in the "Notice Start Date" field.
10. If you have a specific reason for providing notice, please mention it in the "Notice Reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address where you can be reached for further discussions.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address**: Provide your current address to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Experience**: Describe your previous work experience as a healthcare psychologist, highlighting your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Education**: Mention your highest level of education achieved, such as Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.), Doctor of Medicine (M.D.), Master of Science (M.Sc.), or Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.).
* **Language**: If applicable, select the languages you are proficient in.
* **Availability**: Choose your current work experience level.
* **Salary**: If you have a specific salary in mind, please enter it here.
* **Notice Period**: Enter the date you would like to provide notice if you are hired.
* **Notice Start Date**: Enter the start date for your intended notice period.
* **Notice Reason**: If you have a specific reason for providing notice, please mention it here.
* **Work Experience**: Choose your current work experience level.
* **Notice End Date**: This field is not applicable for new hires, so please leave it blank.
* **Notice End Reason**: This field is not applicable for new hires, so please leave it blank.
