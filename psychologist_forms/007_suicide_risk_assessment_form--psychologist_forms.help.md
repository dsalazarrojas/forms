# suicide_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Suicidal Risk Assessment Form is designed to help mental health professionals and clinicians assess an individual's suicidal risk factors. This form is a tool to gather information about an individual's mental health, social support system, and history of suicidal thoughts and behaviors. Its goal is to help identify potential risk factors that may indicate a high level of suicidal risk.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and their relevance to assessing suicidal risk.
2. Please answer each question accurately and honestly to the best of your ability.
3. If you need help understanding a question or need clarification, feel free to ask your mental health professional for assistance.
4. Complete all pages, as each page contributes to the overall assessment of suicidal risk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Suicide Risk Assessment**
	+ **Suicide Risk Assessment (page_1)**: This is the first page of the form, and it asks for your basic information such as name and contact details.
* **Page 2: Risk Factors**
	+ **Risk Factors (page_2)**: This page asks about the individual's risk factors that may contribute to suicidal thoughts and behaviors, such as recent life events, trauma, or mental health conditions.
* **Page 3: Mental Health History**
	+ **Mental Health History (page_3)**: This page explores the individual's mental health history, including any previous mental health diagnoses, treatments, or hospitalizations.
* **Page 4: Social Support**
	+ **Social Support (page_4)**: This page asks about the individual's social support system, including friends, family, or support groups.
* **Page 5: Suicidal Thoughts**
	+ **Suicidal Thoughts (page_5)**: This page explores the individual's suicidal thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, including any previous attempts.
* **Page 6: Suicidal Feelings**
	+ **Suicidal Feelings (page_6)**: Similar to the previous page, this page asks about the individual's feelings of hopelessness, despair, or emotional pain.
* **Page 7: Suicidal Behaviors**
	+ **Suicidal Behaviors (page_7)**: This page asks about the individual's previous suicidal behaviors, including any attempts, plans, or preparations.
* **Page 8: Safety Plan**
	+ **Safety Plan (page_8)**: This page is designed to help the individual develop a safety plan to prevent future suicidal attempts.
* **Page 9: Suicidal Tendencies**
	+ **Suicidal Tendencies (page_9)**: This page explores the individual's tendencies or patterns of suicidal thoughts and behaviors.
* **Page 10: Mental Status**
	+ **Mental Status (page_10)**: This page asks about the individual's current mental state, including any current symptoms or concerns.
* **Page 11: Suicidal Plans**
	+ **Suicidal Plans (page_11)**: This page asks about any current or previous suicidal plans, including methods or intentions.
* **Page 12: Medical History**
	+ **Medical History (page_12)**: This page explores the individual's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or hospitalizations.
* **Page 13: Substance Use**
	+ **Substance Use (page_13)**: This page asks about the individual's substance use history, including any current or previous use of addictive substances.
* **Page 14: Mental Status 2**
	+ **Mental Status 2 (page_14)**: This page is similar to page 10, asking about the individual's current mental state.
* **Page 15: Suicidal Thoughts 2**
	+ **Suicidal Thoughts 2 (page_15)**: This page is similar to page 5, asking about the individual's suicidal thoughts and feelings.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully and honestly, as the information gathered will help identify potential risk factors.
* If you are experiencing suicidal thoughts or feelings, please seek help from a mental health professional immediately.
* This form is not a substitute for professional medical or therapeutic advice. If you are in crisis, contact emergency services or your mental health professional for help.
