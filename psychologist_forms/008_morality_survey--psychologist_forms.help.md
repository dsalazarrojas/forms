# Morality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect your thoughts and feelings on different moral situations. Your responses will help us better understand how people think and feel about these situations, which will aid in improving research and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read each question carefully and understand what's being asked.
2. Choose the best answer for each question based on your thoughts and feelings.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, but note that some questions are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (intro): This is a brief text area where you can provide any background information about your thoughts and feelings before answering the questions.
* **Feelings** (feelings): Please describe your general feelings about the situations described in the questions. This can be as simple as "positive", "negative", or "neutral".
* **opinion 1** (opinion1): Please choose one of the options that best represents your opinion on the first situation.
* **Moral 1** (moral1): Please choose which of the options apply to you for the first situation.
* **Feelings 2** (feelings2): Please describe your general feelings about the second situation.
* **opinion 2** (opinion2): Please choose one of the options that best represents your opinion on the second situation.
* **Moral 2** (moral2): Please choose which of the options apply to you for the second situation.
* **Introduction Note** (intro_note): This is an optional text area where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your answers.
* **Feelings Note** (feelings_note): This is an optional text area where you can provide any additional thoughts or feelings about your answers.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Take your time to read each question carefully before answering.
- If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on your general feelings or opinions.
- This form is voluntary and anonymous, so feel free to answer openly.
