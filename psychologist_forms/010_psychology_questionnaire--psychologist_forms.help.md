# Psychology Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Questionnaire is designed to collect patient information and psychological assessment data from a psychologist or mental health professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, based on your personal experience.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
4. Complete each field as instructed, and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (`patient_info`, `text`, optional):** Please enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant demographic information.
* **Symptoms (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional):** Check all the symptoms that the patient is currently experiencing, such as anxiety, depression, or sleep issues.
* **Diagnosis (`diagnosis`, `select_one`, optional):** Select the current diagnosis of the patient, such as depression or anxiety disorder.
* **Treatment (`treatment`, `select_multiple`, optional):** Check all the treatments or therapies the patient is currently receiving, such as medication or talk therapy.
* **Patient Data (`patient_data`, `text`, optional):** Please enter any additional information about the patient's medical history or relevant details.
* **Patient Notes (`patient_notes`, `text`, optional):** Enter any notes or comments about the patient's condition or progress.
* **Therapist Info (`therapist_info`, `text`, optional):** Enter your name and contact information as the therapist or mental health professional.
* **Patient Contact (`patient_contact`, `text`, optional):** Enter the patient's contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Practice Info (`practice_info`, `text`, optional):** Enter your practice's information, such as address or phone number.
* **Practice Contact (`practice_contact`, `text`, optional):** Enter the practice's contact information, such as email or website.
* **Practice Location (`practice_location`, `text`, optional):** Enter the location of your practice.
* **Practitioner Info (`practitioner_info`, `text`, optional):** Enter your name and contact information as the practitioner.
* **Patient Email (`patient_email`, `email`, optional):** Enter the patient's email address.
* **Practice Phone (`practice_phone`, `text`, optional):** Enter the practice's phone number.
* **Practice Address (`practice_address`, `text`, optional):** Enter the practice's address.
* **Next Appointment Status (`next_appointment_status`, `select_one`, optional):** Select the status of the patient's next appointment, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Submitted By (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, optional):** Select whether the form was submitted by the patient or by a healthcare professional.
* **Patient Status (`patient_status`, `date`, optional):** Enter the patient's current status, such as active or inactive.
* **Assessment Date (`assessment_date`, `date`, optional):** Enter the date of the patient's assessment or evaluation.
* **Next Appointment (`next_appointment`, `time`, optional):** Enter the time of the patient's next appointment.
* **Assessment Result (`assessment_result`, `text`, optional):** Enter the results of the patient's assessment or evaluation.
* **Notes (`notes`, `text`, optional):** Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's case.
* **Patient Consent (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, optional):** Select whether the patient has given consent for treatment or evaluation.
* **Follow-up Info (`follow_up_info`, `text`, optional):** Enter any follow-up information or next steps.
* **Practice Contact (`practice_contact`, `text`, optional):** Enter the practice's contact information, such as email or website.
* **Practice Location (`practice_location`, `text`, optional):** Enter the location of your practice.
