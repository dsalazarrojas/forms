# Beck Depression Inventory Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to help healthcare professionals assess a patient's symptoms of depression. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to get an accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please fill in your name as it appears on your identification.
2.  Select the date of your current assessment.
3.  Answer the questions based on how you have been feeling over the past two weeks.
4.  Choose the options that best describe your current feelings and experiences.
5.  For each question, select only one option that best suits your current state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date of your current assessment.
*   **Sadness - How sad have you been feeling** (`sadness`, `select_one`, Required): This question assesses how frequently you feel sad. Choose the option that best describes your current feelings:
    *   I do not feel sad

    *   I am sad all the time
    *   I am so sad that I cannot bear it
*   **Pessimism - About the future** (`pessimism`, `select_one`, Required): This question evaluates your feelings about the future. Choose the option that best describes your current perspective:
