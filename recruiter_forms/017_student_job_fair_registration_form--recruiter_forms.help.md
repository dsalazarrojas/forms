# Student Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Job Fair Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from recruiters for their job postings during student job fairs. This form helps event organizers and students to access and review job details, ensuring a successful and efficient fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Recruiter Name, which is the name of the representative from your company.
2. Fill in your Company Name, which is the name of your organization.
3. Provide your Contact Number, so students can get in touch with you.
4. Enter your Contact Email, which is used for communication.
5. Select the type of job you are offering (Part-time, Full-time, or Internship).
6. Describe the job details, including job requirements and responsibilities.
7. Fill in the event dates: Fair Start Date, Fair End Date, Fair Setup Date, and Fair Takedown Date.
8. Provide the Fair Location where the job fair will take place.
9. You can optionally add a link to your Company Website, a logo, and other job details such as Job Description, Job Requirements, Company Description, and Company Contact.
10. Select the job status (Open, Closed, or Pending).
11. Finally, select the job category (Technology, Marketing, Finance, Sales, Arts, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recruiter Name** (Required): Enter the name of the representative from your company.
* **Company Name** (Required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Number** (Required): Enter a contact number for students to get in touch with you.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter the email address used for communication with students.
* **Job Type** (Required): Select the type of job you are offering (Part-time, Full-time, or Internship).
	+ Part-time: A part-time job is usually less than full-time.
	+ Full-time: A full-time job is a permanent position with a standard workweek.
	+ Internship: A position for students or recent graduates to gain work experience.
* **Job Details** (Required): Describe the job, including its requirements and responsibilities.
* **Fair Start Date** (Required): Enter the starting date of the job fair.
* **Fair End Date** (Required): Enter the ending date of the job fair.
* **Fair Setup Date** (Required): Enter the setup date for the job fair.
* **Fair Takedown Date** (Required): Enter the takedown date for the job fair.
* **Fair Location** (Required): Enter the location of the job fair.
* **Company Website** (Optional): Add a link to your company website, if available.
* **Company Logo** (Optional): Upload a logo representing your company.
* **Job Description** (Optional): Add a job description for students to review.
* **Job Requirements** (Optional): Add job requirements for students to consider.
* **Company Description** (Optional): Add a description of your company.
* **Company Contact** (Optional): Add a contact person for your company.
* **Recruiter Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments from the recruiter.
* **Company Contact** (Optional): Add a contact person for your company.
* **Job Status** (Required): Select the status of the job (Open, Closed, or Pending).
	+ Open: The job is available for students to apply.
	+ Closed: The job is no longer accepting applications.
	+ Pending: The job is in a pending status, waiting for further instructions.
* **Job Type Description** (Optional): Add a description of the job type.
* **Company Type** (Optional): Select the type of company (Startup, Corporation, or Non-Profit).
	+ Startup: A startup company is an innovative venture.
	+ Corporation: A large corporation with established operations.
	+ Non-Profit: A non-profit organization with a social or community purpose.
* **Job Category** (Required): Select the category of the job (Technology, Marketing, Finance, Sales, Arts, or Other).
	+ Technology: A job related to technology, software development, or IT.
	+ Marketing: A job related to marketing, sales, or promotion.
	+ Finance: A job related to finance, accounting, or economics.
	+ Sales: A job related to sales, customer service, or business development.
	+ Arts: A job related to arts, design, or creativity.
	+ Other: Select "Other" for jobs that don't fit into the above categories.
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional comments or information about the job.

## Tips
1. Make sure to fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), to ensure a smooth and efficient submission process.
2. Use correct dates in the format of YYYY-MM-DD for dates and MM/DD/YYYY for time-sensitive dates.
3. Proofread your submission before saving to ensure accuracy and consistency.
4. If unsure about any field, refer to this guide for assistance.
