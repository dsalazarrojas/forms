# Hiring Procedure Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that the hiring process is followed consistently and effectively for each position. It helps to standardize the hiring process and prevent duplication of efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with a blank slate, and review the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Go through each page and carefully read the label and any hints provided.
3. Fill out each field based on the specific requirements and information for the job you are hiring for.
4. For the "Interview Questions" field, select all questions that apply to the job.
5. For fields that require numerical input, enter a number in the specified format (e.g., a dollar sign and a numerical value for salary range).
6. Submit the form once you have completed all required and non-required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Job Description (Page 1)
* **Job Description** (`<title>`): A brief summary of the job title and role. This field should be filled out with a concise and descriptive title of the position.
* **Description** (`<hint>`): A brief summary of the job's responsibilities and duties.

### 2. Job Requirements (Page 2)
* **Job Requirements** (`<title>`): A list of essential and nice-to-have requirements for the job. This field should be filled out with a list of key qualifications and skills required for the position.

### 3. Interview Questions (Page 3)
* **Interview Questions** (`<label>`): A list of questions that will be asked during the interview process. Select all questions that apply to the job in question.
* **Select Questions** (`<hint>`): This field should not be used as a hint, instead, review the options to determine which questions are relevant to the job.

### 4. Salary Range (Page 4)
* **Salary Range** (`<label>`): The salary range expected for the position. Enter a number in the format of `$X`, where `X` is the minimum and maximum salary.
