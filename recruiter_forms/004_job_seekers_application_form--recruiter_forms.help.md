# Job Seekers Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job seekers about their personal and professional details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Date of Birth in the respective fields.
2. Upload your Resume.
3. Select up to three skills you possess from the dropdown menu.
4. Describe your Work Experience, Education, and References in the text fields provided.
5. Fill in your Job Title, Job Description, Job Type, Salary, Location, Job Status, Job Category, Job Level, Job Type 2, Job Position, Job Employer, Job Salary, Job Description 2, and Job Status 2.
6. Select multiple Job Types 2 from the dropdown menu.
7. Review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume or CV.
* **Skills**: Select up to three skills you possess from the dropdown menu.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience.
* **Education**: Describe your educational background.
* **References**: Provide information about your references.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job you are applying for.
* **Salary**: Enter your expected salary range.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you are willing to work.
* **Job Status**: Enter your current job status.
* **Job Category**: Enter the category of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Level**: Enter the level of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Type 2**: Select multiple job types you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Position**: Enter your job position.
* **Job Employer**: Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
* **Job Salary**: Enter the salary range you are expecting.
* **Job Description 2**: Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Status 2**: Enter your current job status.
