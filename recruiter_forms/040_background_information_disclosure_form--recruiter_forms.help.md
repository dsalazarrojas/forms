# Background Information Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects background information from employees for employment screening purposes. Please ensure you understand the implications of providing inaccurate or incomplete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details as required in the form, including your name, date of birth, address, and contact information.
2. Select the correct reasons for the background check from the options provided.
3. If applicable, list your previous employers and education history.
4. Disclose any convictions or past employment issues.
5. Confirm that you authorize your employer to conduct a background check and release them from liability.
6. Sign and date the form, ensuring that you provide accurate and truthful information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, optional): If you have a company ID, enter it here. If not, leave blank.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number in XXX-XX-XXXX format.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your current residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your current residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your personal contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you are or will be working in.
* **Hire Date or Start Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter your date of hire or start date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Purpose of Background Check** (`background_check_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the reason for the background check from the options provided.
* **Background Checks Authorized** (`checks_authorized`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable background checks from the options provided.
* **Previous Employers** (`previous_employers`, text, required): List your previous employers for the last five years.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, optional): List any schools and degrees you have completed.
* **Professional Licenses or Certifications** (`professional_licenses`, text, optional): List any professional licenses or certifications you hold.
* **Criminal Conviction Disclosure** (`conviction_disclosure`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have been convicted of a crime.
* **Conviction Explanation** (`conviction_explanation`, text, optional): Provide additional information if you have been convicted of a crime.
* **Authorization Clause** (`authorization_clause`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I authorize' to allow the employer to conduct a background check.
* **Release Clause** (`release_clause`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, I release' to release the employer from liability.
* **Truthfulness Statement** (`truthfulness_statement`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes, certified' to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign your name digitally or in handwriting.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign the form in MM/DD/YYYY format.
