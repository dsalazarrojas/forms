# College Basketball Recruiting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Basketball Recruiting Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a college basketball player's profile, contact details, academic and athletic background, and other relevant attributes. This form helps us gather detailed data for recruitment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the player.
2. Ensure the player's contact details are up-to-date.
3. Be honest and accurate in providing the player's athletic and academic achievements.
4. Attach any supporting documents like certificates or achievements if applicable.
5. Make sure to save the form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player First Name** (`player_first_name`, text, required): Enter the player's first name.
* **Player Last Name** (`player_last_name`, text, required): Enter the player's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the player's date of birth in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Height - inches** (`height`, number, required): Enter the player's height in inches.
* **Weight - lbs** (`weight`, number, required): Enter the player's weight in pounds.
* **Primary Position** (`primary_position`, select_one, required): Select the player's primary position on the court.
* **Secondary Position** (`secondary_position`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the player's secondary position on the court. If not applicable, leave it empty.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address.
* **Player Phone Number** (`player_phone`, text, required): Enter the player's phone number.
* **High School or Club Name** (`high_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the current or recent school the player attended.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): Enter the year the player graduated from high school.
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, number, required): Enter the player's current GPA on a 4.0 scale.
* **Standardized Test Score (SAT or ACT)** (`standardized_test_score`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the player's standardized test score.
* **Scoring Average - points per game** (`points_per_game`, number, required): Enter the player's scoring average in points per game.
* **Rebounding Average - per game** (`rebounds_per_game`, number, required): Enter the player's rebounding average per game.
* **Assists Average - per game** (`assists_per_game`, number, required): Enter the player's assists average per game.
* **Field Goal Percentage** (`field_goal_percentage`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the player's field goal percentage.
* **Three-Point Shooting Percentage** (`three_point_percentage`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the player's three-point shooting percentage.
* **Free Throw Shooting Percentage** (`free_throw_percentage`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the player's free throw shooting percentage.
* **Awards and Recognition** (`awards_recognition`, text, optional): List any awards and recognition the player received.
* **Player Strengths and Skills** (`strengths`, text, required): Describe the player's best abilities and skills.
* **Areas for Development** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Identify areas where the player can improve.
* **Coach or Trainer Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player's current or recent coach.
* **Coach Email or Phone** (`coach_contact`, text, required): Enter the coach's email or phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Phone** (`parent_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the parent's contact phone number.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
