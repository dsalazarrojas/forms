# Background Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Background Check Request Form is used to request background checks for candidates who have been shortlisted or selected for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information as the requester.
3. Enter the candidate's name and position they are being considered for.
4. Select the reason for the background check and the authorized scope of the check.
5. Indicate the priority level of the request (if any).
6. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter the date of when you created the request.
8. Submit the form to initiate the background check process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requester Name (1)
* **Requester name**: <em>Your name and title, e.g., Hiring Manager</em> (This is the name of the person requesting the background check.)
* **Label**: Requester name
* **Hint**: This is the name of the person requesting the background check.
* **Type**: text
* **Optional**: No

### Candidate Name (2)
* **Candidate name**: <em>Full legal name of the candidate</em>
* **Label**: Candidate name
* **Hint**: This is the full legal name of the candidate.
* **Type**: text
* **Optional**: No

### Position (3)
* **Position**: <em>Job title of the position the candidate is being considered for</em>
* **Label**: Position to be filled
* **Hint**: This is the job title of the position the candidate is being considered for.
* **Type**: text
* **Optional**: No

### Reason for Background Check (4)
* **Reason for background check**: <em>Explain why you need the background check (e.g., employment screening, volunteer role, or licensing)</em>
* **Label**: Reason for background check
* **Hint**: Explain why you're requesting a background check.
* **Type**: text
* **Optional**: Yes

### Authorized Scope (5)
* **Authorized scope of check**: <em>Select all applicable options (e.g., Criminal record, Employment verification, Education verification, or Credit check)</em>
* **Label**: Authorized scope of check

### Priority Level (6)
* **Priority level**: <em>Select the urgency of the request (Normal, High, or Urgent)</em>

### Requester Email (7)

* **Label**: Requester email
* **Hint**: Enter your email address where you want the results to be sent.

### Requester Phone (8)

### Submission Date (9)
* **Submission date**: <em>Date the request was created</em>
* **Label**: Submission date
* **Hint**: This is the date the request was created.
