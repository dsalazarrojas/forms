# Career Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Career Fair, which is a platform for students, job seekers, employers, recruiters, and other stakeholders to connect with each other and explore opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your registration type from the options provided in the "Registration Type" field.
2. Enter the name of the career fair you wish to register for in the "Career Fair Name" field.
3. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
5. Enter a valid email address in the "Email Address" field. We will use this address to send you updates and confirmation.
6. Enter your phone number in the international format (including the country code) in the "Phone Number" field.
7. If you are an employer or volunteer, enter your organization name in the "Organization or Company Name" field.
8. If you are an employer or volunteer, enter your title or position in the "Your Position or Title" field.
9. If you are an employer, enter your primary contact person's name in the "Employer Contact Name" field.
10. If you are an employer, enter the primary contact person's email address in the "Employer Primary Contact Email" field.
11. If you are an employer, select the booth size you need from the options in the "Booth Space Needed" field.
12. If you are an employer, specify any special requirements for your booth in the "Special Booth Requirements" field.
13. If you are an employer, list the job titles and levels you are recruiting for in the "Positions You Are Recruiting For" field.
14. If you are an employer, list the areas you are hiring in in the "Specialties or Departments Hiring" field.
15. If you are a job seeker, select your experience level from the options in the "Experience Level" field.
16. If you are a job seeker, select your preferred type of employment from the options in the "Type of Employment Sought" field.
17. If you are a volunteer, specify your availability to volunteer in the "Volunteer Availability" field.
18. If you are a volunteer, list any relevant skills you can offer in the "Volunteer Skills or Interests" field.
19. If the event includes meals, specify any dietary restrictions in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
20. If you have any accessibility needs, select "Yes, please specify" and fill in the "Specific Accessibility Needs" field.
21. Finally, select how you heard about the Career Fair from the options in the "How Did You Hear About This Career Fair?" field.
22. Check the box in the "I Agree to the Career Fair Terms and Conditions" field to confirm you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Type**: Select whether you are registering as a candidate or employer.
*   **Career Fair Name**: Enter the name of the career fair you wish to register for.
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where we can send you updates and confirmation. We will use this address to communicate with you during the event.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in international format (including country code) so we can reach you.
*   **Organization or Company Name** (optional): If registering as employer or volunteer, enter your organization or company name.
*   **Your Position or Title** (optional): If registering as employer or volunteer, enter your title or position.
*   **Employer Contact Name** (optional): If registering as employer, enter your primary contact person's name.
*   **Employer Primary Contact Email** (optional): If registering as employer, enter the primary contact person's email address.
*   **Booth Space Needed** (optional): If registering as employer, select the booth size you need. Options are 6x6 feet, 10x10 feet, 10x20 feet, and custom size.
*   **Special Booth Requirements** (optional): If registering as employer, enter any special requirements for your booth.
*   **Positions You Are Recruiting For** (optional): If registering as employer, list the job titles and levels you are recruiting for.
*   **Specialties or Departments Hiring** (optional): If registering as employer, list the areas you are hiring in.
*   **How Many People Are You Recruiting?** (optional): If registering as employer, enter the number of people you are recruiting.
*   **Major or Field of Study** (optional): If registering as student or job seeker, enter your major or field of study.
*   **Expected Graduation Year** (optional): If registering as student, enter your expected graduation year.
*   **Industries of Interest** (optional): If registering as job seeker, select all the industries you are interested in.
*   **Experience Level** (optional): If registering as job seeker, select your experience level. Options are Entry-level, Internship, 1-3 years experience, 3-5 years experience, 5+ years experience, and Career Changer.
*   **Type of Employment Sought** (optional): If registering as job seeker, select your preferred type of employment. Options are Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship, Co-op, and Remote.
*   **Volunteer Availability** (optional): If registering as volunteer, specify your availability to volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Skills or Interests** (optional): If registering as volunteer, list any relevant skills or interests you can offer.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (optional): If the event includes meals, specify any dietary restrictions you have.
*   **Accessibility Needs** (optional): If you have any accessibility needs, select "Yes, please specify" and fill in the "Specific Accessibility Needs" field.
*   **Specific Accessibility Needs** (optional): If you have any accessibility needs, enter a specific description of your needs.
*   **How Did You Hear About This Career Fair?** (optional): Select how you heard about the Career Fair.
*   **I Agree to the Career Fair Terms and Conditions** (optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the Career Fair.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
