# Job Description Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Description Form is a critical tool used to collect and manage job-related information. It's used to gather details about job openings, which are essential for various business processes, including job postings, applicant tracking, and HR management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and each field's requirements.
2. Start by filling out the "Job Title" field, which should reflect the exact title of the job opening.
3. Provide a brief summary of the job in the "Job Summary" field.
4. Indicate whether the job is in a specific category by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Category" field.
5. Choose the type of job by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Type" field.
6. Enter the salary range for the job in the "Salary" field.
7. Describe the benefits offered to the job applicant in the "Benefits" field.
8. Specify the job location by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Location" field.
9. Type out the job description in the "Job Description" field.
10. List the required qualifications for the job in the "Job Qualifications" field.
11. Outline the job requirements in the "Job Requirements" field.
12. Set the job status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Job Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (text, not required): Enter the exact title of the job opening.
* **Job Summary** (text, not required): Provide a brief summary of the job.
* **Job Category** (select_one, not required): Indicate whether the job is in a specific category.
* **Job Type** (select_one, not required): Choose the type of job.
* **Salary** (number, not required): Enter the salary range for the job.
* **Benefits** (text, not required): Describe the benefits offered to the job applicant.
* **Job Location** (select_one, not required): Specify the job location.
* **Job Description** (text, not required): Type out the job description.
* **Job Qualifications** (text, not required): List the required qualifications for the job.
* **Job Requirements** (text, not required): Outline the job requirements.
* **Job Status** (select_one, not required): Set the job status to "Active" or "Inactive".

## Tips
- Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and each field's explanation for clarification.
- If you're an HR representative, please ensure that all necessary fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
