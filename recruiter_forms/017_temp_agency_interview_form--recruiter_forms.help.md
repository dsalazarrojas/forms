# Temp Agency Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temp Agency Interview Form is designed to collect and store information about job titles, candidates, and submissions. This form will help the agency track and manage all the data related to temp agency interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the job title of the candidate.
2. Provide the candidate's name, email, and contact number.
3. Select their availability for the interview.
4. Choose whether or not the candidate's role fits the position.
5. Enter any notes about the candidate.
6. Decide whether or not to submit the candidate for interview.
7. Select the submission confirmation status.
8. Enter the candidate's ID.
9. Provide the submission URL.
10. Enter the submission date.
11. Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
12. Choose the submission status.
13. Decide whether or not the candidate is assigned to someone.
