# Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Contract Form is a tool used for capturing essential information between an employer and an employee, outlining the terms of their employment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information as follows:
	* Employee Name: Please fill in the name of the employee.
	* Employee Email: Enter the email address of the employee.
	* Employee ID: Enter the ID number of the employee.
	* Employer Name: Fill in the name of the employer.
	* Employee Phone: Enter the phone number of the employee.
	* Employer Phone: Enter the phone number of the employer.
	* Contract Type: Select either Fixed Term or Indefinite from the dropdown options.
	* Contract Duration: Fill in the contract duration in text format.
	* Contract Agreement: Select the option that reflects the employee's agreement to the contract terms.
	* Contract Status: Choose the status of the contract from the dropdown options.
2. Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable:
	* Date Signed: Fill in the date the contract was signed.
	* Time Signed: Fill in the time the contract was signed.
	* Contract End Date: Fill in the date the contract is expected to end.
	* Contract End Time: Fill in the time the contract is expected to end.
	* Terminate Date: Fill in the date the contract was terminated.
	* Contract Reason: Fill in the reason for contract termination.
	* Contract Start Date: Fill in the date the contract started.
	* Contract Start Time: Fill in the time the contract started.
	* Contract End Reason: Fill in the reason for contract end.
