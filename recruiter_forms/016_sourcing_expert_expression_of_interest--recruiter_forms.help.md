# Sourcing Expert Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sourcing Expert Expression Of Interest" form is designed to collect information from experts interested in working with us as a recruiter. This form helps us assess your availability and suitability for potential job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter your current job title, which will help us understand your current role and responsibilities.
3. Choose your desired location for work.
4. Enter your email address to help us contact you for further discussions.
5. Provide your phone number to ensure we can reach you efficiently.
6. Enter your current job title to help us understand your background.
7. Select your current job function to provide us with a clear picture of your expertise.
8. Choose your desired job function to express your interest in specific areas.
9. Enter the industry you are currently working in or want to work in.
10. Enter the job title you are interested in.
11. Select the specific job function that interests you the most.
12. Choose the desired location for your new job.
13. Enter your expected salary range.
14. Provide your availability, including the date and time you can start.
15. Enter your work hours, in a 24-hour format.
16. Fill out a second set of contact information for backup purposes.
17. Enter the job title of your desired new role.
18. Select the job function that aligns with your desired new role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): This section is used to provide us with your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address. This information will help us reach out to you for further discussions.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Please enter your current job title, which will give us an idea of your current role and responsibilities.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Choose the location where you would prefer to work.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number to ensure we can reach you efficiently.
* **Current Job Title** (`job_title_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title to help us understand your background.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required/optional): Select your current job function to give us a clear picture of your expertise.
* **Desired Job Function** (`job_function_2`, text, required/optional): Choose your desired job function to express your interest in specific areas.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required/optional): Enter the industry you are currently working in or want to work in.
* **Job Title of Interest** (`job_title_3`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title you are interested in.
* **Job Function 3** (`job_function_3`, text, required/optional): Select the specific job function that interests you the most.
* **Job Function 4** (`job_function_4`, text, required/optional): Choose the job function that aligns with your desired new role.
* **Desired Location** (`location_2`, text, required/optional): Choose the location where you would prefer to work.
* **Salary Expectation** (`salary_expectation`, text, required/optional): Enter your expected salary range.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required/optional): Provide your availability, including the date and time you can start.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, required/optional): Enter your work hours in a 24-hour format.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_information_2`, text, required/optional): Fill out a second set of contact information for backup purposes.
* **Job Title 4** (`job_title_4`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of your desired new role.
* **Job Function 4** (`job_function_4`, text, required/optional): Select the job function that aligns with your desired new role.
