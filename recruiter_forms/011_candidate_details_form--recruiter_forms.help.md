# Candidate Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information from candidates about their background, experience, and other relevant details for job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to check the required fields marked as such.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`candidate_first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`candidate_last_name`, text, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Full Address** (`candidate_address`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`candidate_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State or Province** (`candidate_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`candidate_zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`candidate_country`, select_one, required): Select the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your birth date (only if required for the position).
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`linkedin_profile`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn page link (only if you want to share it).
* **Portfolio or Website URL** (`portfolio_website`, text, optional): Enter your personal website or portfolio link (only if you want to share it).
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, text, optional): Enter your current company name (only if applicable).
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current position (only if applicable).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years of experience in your field.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Select your highest education level.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): Enter the major or area of study.
* **University or Institution** (`university_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school (only if applicable).
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of graduation (only if applicable).
* **Key Skills** (`key_skills`, text, required): Enter your top skills relevant to the position.
* **Industry Experience** (`industry_experience`, select_multiple, required): Select the industries you have worked in.
* **Salary Expectation** (`salary_expectation`, text, optional): Enter the expected annual salary (only if applicable).
* **Availability to Start** (`availability`, select_one, required): Select when you can start.
* **Work Authorization Status** (`work_authorization`, select_one, required): Select your work authorization status.
* **Willing to Relocate** (`willing_to_relocate`, select_one, required): Select if you are willing to relocate.
* **Resume Attached** (`resume_attached`, select_one, required): Select if you have attached your resume.
* **Cover Letter Attached** (`cover_letter_attached`, select_one, optional): Select if you have attached a cover letter.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select the source of the job posting.
* **Referred By** (`referral_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who referred you (only if applicable).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date submitted.
