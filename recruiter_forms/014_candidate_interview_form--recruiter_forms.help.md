# Candidate Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Candidate Interview Form is a comprehensive tool used to document and evaluate a candidate's performance during the interview process. It helps recruiters to gather and track relevant information to make informed hiring decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to conduct the interview and gather the required information.
2.  Review the form carefully before proceeding.
3.  Fill in all required fields and provide clear and concise answers to each question.
4.  Use the form in conjunction with other assessment tools and techniques for a comprehensive evaluation.
5.  Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the assessment criteria or procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate First Name** (Required): Enter the candidate's given name.
    *   Hint: Given name
*   **Candidate Last Name** (Required): Enter the candidate's family name.
    *   Hint: Family name
*   **Candidate Email** (Required): Enter the candidate's contact email address.
    *   Hint: Contact email
*   **Candidate Phone** (Required): Enter the candidate's contact phone number.
    *   Hint: Contact number
*   **Position Applied For** (Required): Enter the job title the candidate is applying for.
    *   Hint: Job title
*   **Interview Date** (Required): Enter the scheduled interview date.
    *   Hint: Scheduled date
*   **Interview Time** (Required): Enter the scheduled interview time.
    *   Hint: Scheduled time
*   **Interview Format** (Required): Select the type of interview (Phone, Video call, In-person, Panel, Technical, Behavioral, Case study).
    *   Hint: Type of interview
*   **Interviewer Name** (Required): Enter the name of the person conducting the interview.
    *   Hint: Who conducted interview
*   **Interviewer Title** (Optional): Enter the title or role of the interviewer.
    *   Hint: Interviewer role
*   **Years of Experience** (Required): Enter the candidate's level of experience.
    *   Hint: Candidate experience level
*   **Education Level** (Required): Select the highest level of education achieved by the candidate (High school, Associate, Bachelor, Master, Doctorate, Other).
    *   Hint: Highest degree
*   **Technical Skills Rating** (Required): Rate the candidate's technical skills (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not assessed).
    *   Hint: Rate technical abilities
*   **Communication Skills Rating** (Required): Rate the candidate's communication skills (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    *   Hint: Rate communication
*   **Problem Solving Skills Rating** (Required): Rate the candidate's problem-solving skills (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not assessed).
    *   Hint: Rate problem solving
*   **Cultural Fit Rating** (Required): Rate the candidate's cultural fit (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
    *   Hint: Rate fit with company
*   **Leadership Potential** (Optional): Rate the candidate's leadership potential (High potential, Moderate potential, Low potential, Not assessed).
    *   Hint: Rate leadership qualities
*   **Key Strengths Observed** (Required): Enter the key strengths observed during the interview.
    *   Hint: Candidate strengths
*   **Areas for Improvement** (Optional): Enter areas where the candidate needs improvement.
    *   Hint: Areas needing development
*   **Salary Expectations** (Optional): Enter the candidate's salary expectations.
    *   Hint: Candidate expectations
*   **Availability to Start** (Required): Select the availability date for the candidate to start.
    *   Hint: When can candidate begin
*   **Relocation Required** (Optional): Enter if relocation is required for the candidate.
    *   Hint: Does candidate need to relocate
*   **Visa Sponsorship Needed** (Required): Check if visa sponsorship is required for the candidate.
    *   Hint: Work authorization
*   **Overall Recommendation** (Required): Select the overall recommendation for the candidate.
    *   Hint: Hiring recommendation
*   **Rationale for Recommendation** (Required): Enter the reasoning for the recommendation.
    *   Hint: Explain your decision
*   **Next Steps Recommended** (Required): Select the recommended next steps.
    *   Hint: What should happen next
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional): Enter any other notes or comments.
    *   Hint: Any other notes
*   **Form Completion Date** (Required): Enter the date the form was completed.
    *   Hint: Date filled

Please review this guide carefully and use it as a reference when completing the form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to your team or HR department.
