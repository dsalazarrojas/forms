# Football Recruiting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Recruiting Form is designed to collect information about football players, including contact details, athletic background, and other relevant data for recruitment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by entering your first name and last name in the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields.
2.  Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section, including your phone number and email address.
3.  Select your **School Attended** from the drop-down menu.
4.  Choose the **Positions Played** you are skilled in or have experience with.
5.  Enter your **Graduation Date** if applicable.
6.  Enter your **Height** and **Weight** for better evaluation.
7.  Select your **Athletic Background** in the drop-down menu to specify your level of experience.
8.  Answer the questions about your **High School** and **College/University** attendance in the corresponding fields.
9.  If applicable, indicate your experience with **Athletic Directors**, **Head Coach**, and **Assistant Coach**.
10. Confirm your **High School Attended** by selecting a yes or no option.
11. Answer questions regarding **Recruited** and **College/University Recruited** to provide more context about your recruitment experience.
12. Finally, confirm your **High School Recruited** and **College/University Recruited** experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
*   **School Attended**: Select the school you attended.
*   **Positions Played**: Choose the positions you have experience with or are skilled in.
*   **Graduation Date**: Enter your graduation date if applicable.
*   **Height**: Enter your height.
*   **Weight**: Enter your weight.
*   **Athletic Background**: Select your level of athletic background.
*   **High School**: Answer yes or no to indicate if you attended high school.
*   **College/University**: Answer yes or no to indicate if you attended college/university.
*   **Athletic Directors**: Answer yes or no to indicate your experience with athletic directors.
*   **Head Coach**: Answer yes or no to indicate your experience with head coaches.
*   **Assistant Coach**: Answer yes or no to indicate your experience with assistant coaches.
*   **High School Recruited**: Answer yes or no to indicate if you were recruited during high school.
*   **College/University Recruited**: Answer yes or no to indicate if you were recruited during college/university.
*   **Athletic Background 6-9**: Select your level of athletic background for specific fields.

Note: The fields marked as **None** can be selected if you do not have experience or want to indicate no experience.
