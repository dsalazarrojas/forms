# Part Time Job Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from part-time job seekers. It's used to gather details about their category, salary range, work availability, job type, location, duties, skills, education, experience, job status, contact information, availability date, preferred language, and job type.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by selecting the category that best describes the type of job you're looking for.
- Choose the salary range that you're willing to accept.
- Indicate how many hours you can work per week.
- Select the days of the week you're available to work.
- Choose the hours you work per day.
- Specify the type of job you prefer (part-time, full-time, internship, or temporary).
- Select the location where you'd like to work (home office, office, on-site, remotely, or anywhere).
- List the duties you can perform (administrative, data entry, customer service, data analysis, data entry and analysis, or other).
- Mention any other skills you have.
- Indicate your level of education.
- Specify your level of experience.
- Select your current job status (full-time, part-time, internship, temporary, retired, or other).
- Provide your contact information (email, phone number, or any other relevant details).
- If applicable, mention your availability date.
- Choose your preferred language.
- Select your job type (part-time, full-time, internship, temporary, or other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the type of job you're looking for (e.g., Accounting, Administrative Assistant, Advertising, etc.).
* **What type of pay can you accept** (`What type of pay can you accept`, select_multiple, required): Choose the salary range you're willing to accept (e.g., 1-10k, 10001-20k, 20001-30k, etc.).
* **How many hours can you work per week?** (`How many hours can you work per week?`, select_one, required): Indicate the number of hours you can work per week (e.g., 0-5, 10-15, 16-20, 21-25, etc.).
* **What days of the week do you work?** (`What days of the week do you work?`, select_multiple, required): Select the days of the week you're available to work (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **What hours do you work per day?** (`What hours do you work per day?`, select_one, required): Choose the hours you work per day (e.g., Less than 5, 5-10, 11-15, 16-20, etc.).
* **What type of job do you prefer?** (`What type of job do you prefer?`, select_one, required): Specify the type of job you prefer (e.g., part-time, full-time, internship, temporary).
* **Where do you prefer to work?** (`Where do you prefer to work?`, select_multiple, required): Select the location where you'd like to work (e.g., home office, office, on-site, remotely, anywhere).
* **What duties can you perform?** (`What duties can you perform?`, select_multiple, required): List the duties you can perform (e.g., administrative, data entry, customer service, data analysis, etc.).
* **What skills do you have?** (`What skills do you have?`, text, optional): Mention any additional skills you have.
* **Education** (`Education`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of education (e.g., some high school, high school graduate, bachelor's degree, etc.).
* **Experience** (`Experience`, select_one, required): Specify your level of experience (e.g., less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 4-6 years, etc.).
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current job status (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship, temporary, etc.).
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Provide your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
* **Availability Date** (`Availability Date`, date, optional): Mention your availability date.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred language (e.g., English, Spanish, French, Chinese, etc.).
* **Job Type 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8** (`Job Type 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job type (e.g., part-time, full-time, internship, temporary, etc.).
