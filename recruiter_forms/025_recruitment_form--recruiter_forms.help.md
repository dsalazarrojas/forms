# Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Form is designed to collect information about job openings and applicants. It is a tool for recruiters or hiring managers to input data related to job postings and applicant contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a job posting from the available options or create a new one.
2. Enter the required and optional information for the job posting, including job title, job type, job description, and job requirements.
3. Select the job status (active or inactive) and job category (yes or no).
4. Enter the recruiter contact information, including their email and phone number.
5. Enter the applicant contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
6. Select the application status (active or inactive) and add any additional notes.

- **Job Application** (1) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering a job application.
- **Applicant Details** (2) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering applicant details.
- **Contact Info** (3) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering contact information.
- **Job Posting** (4) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering a job posting.
- **Application Date** (5) (`date`, required: false): A date field for entering the application date.
- **Application Time** (6) (`time`, required: false): A time field for entering the application time.
- **Job Title** (7) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering a job title.
- **Job Type** (8) (`select_one`, required: false): A select field for selecting job type (yes or no).
- **Salary Range** (9) (`number`, required: false): A number field for entering the salary range.
- **Location** (10) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering a location.
- **Job Description** (11) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering a job description.
- **Job Requirements** (12) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering job requirements.
- **Job Status** (13) (`select_one`, required: false): A select field for selecting job status (active or inactive).
- **Job Category** (14) (`select_one`, required: false): A select field for selecting job category (yes or no).
- **Recruiter** (15) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering recruiter information.
- **Recruiter Email** (16) (`email`, required: false): An email field for entering recruiter email.
- **Recruiter Phone** (17) (`text`, required: false): A text field for entering recruiter phone number.
- **Application Notes** (18) (`note`, required: false): A note field for entering additional notes.
- **Job Application Status** (19) (`select_one`, required: false): A select field for selecting application status (active or inactive).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review and double-check your input before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above or contact the system administrator.
