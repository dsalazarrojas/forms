# Recruitment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Requisition Form is used to gather information about job openings, including job title, type, category, level, location, description, status, opening and closing dates, reporting line, contact person, contact email, contact phone, job posting URL, and job ID. This form helps recruiters and HR teams to create accurate and detailed job postings that meet the company's needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the correct information for the job posting you're creating.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Use the provided hints and labels as a guide for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (1): Enter the title of the job you are creating a posting for. This title should be clear and concise.
* **Job Type** (2): Check if the job is available or not. Select "Yes" if the job is available, "No" otherwise.
* **Job Category** (3): Select all applicable job categories for the job you are creating a posting for.
* **Job Level** (4): Choose the level of the job, either High or Low.
* **Job Location** (5): Enter the location where the job is based.
* **Job Description** (6): Provide a detailed description of the job, including responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Status** (7): Select the current status of the job, either Active or Inactive.
* **Job Opening Date** (8): Enter the date when the job was first opened.
* **Job Closing Date** (9): Enter the date when the job will be closed.
* **Reporting Line** (10): Enter the person or department responsible for the job.
* **Contact Person** (11): Enter the name of the person who can be contacted about the job.
* **Contact E-mail** (12): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (13): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Job Posting URL** (14): Enter the URL of the job posting, if applicable.
* **Job ID** (15): Enter a unique identifier for the job.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use the provided hints and labels as a guide for each field.
* If you have any doubts or questions, refer to your supervisor or HR team for clarification.
* The form is designed to be filled out by recruiters and HR teams to create accurate and detailed job postings that meet the company's needs and requirements.
