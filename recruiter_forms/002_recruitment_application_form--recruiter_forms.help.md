# Recruitment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Application Form is designed to collect information from job applicants. It is intended to be used by individuals interested in applying for a job opening, and the submitted information will be reviewed for consideration for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the job posting details on the next page.
2. Fill in your job applicant information.
3. Enter your contact information.
4. Describe your experience, education, and any relevant skills.
5. List your references (if applicable).
6. Upload any required documents (if applicable).
7. Click on the "Submit" button to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Applicant Info** (`job_applicant_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself as a job applicant.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, text, required): Enter the job posting ID or title of the job you're applying for.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required): List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): List at least two professional references with their names, job titles, company names, and contact information.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Describe any relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): List your relevant skills, including technical skills and soft skills.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required): Upload any required documents, such as a resume or cover letter.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit your application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Use the job posting ID or title to ensure you're applying for the correct position.
* Upload any required documents as specified in the job posting.
* Review your application before submitting to ensure it's complete and accurate.
