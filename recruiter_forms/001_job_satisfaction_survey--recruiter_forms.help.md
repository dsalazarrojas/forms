# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Satisfaction Survey is a tool designed to measure job satisfaction among employees. Its purpose is to gather information on their feelings and experiences related to their job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form questions and instructions carefully.
2. Answer each question according to your honest feelings and experiences.
3. Complete the form as honestly and accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Title** (Text Field, Optional)
Provide a title or heading for your survey.

### **Intro** (Text Field, Optional)
Provide an introduction or explanation for your survey, if necessary.

### **Introduction** (Note Field, Optional)
This is a note field where you can provide more detailed information about your survey.

Tips:
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
