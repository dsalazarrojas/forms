# request_for_hire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for job postings, allowing users to specify job title, job type, job location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Contact Person field with the name of the person responsible for the job posting.
2. Enter the contact number in the contact_number field.
3. Select the job title from the job_title field.
4. Select the job type from the job_type field.
5. Select the work type from the work_type field.
6. Enter the job start date in the job_start_date field.
7. Enter the job end date in the job_end_date field.
8. Enter a note in the Prophets field.
9. Enter the work hours in the work_hours field.
10. Enter the job location in the job_location field.
11. Enter the work location in the job_location field (Note: this field is not directly related to the job location).
12. Enter a description of the job in the job_description field.
13. Enter the job requirements in the job_requirements field.
14. Enter additional job requirements in the job_requirements2 field.
15. Enter a description of the job posting in the job_posting field.
16. Enter the job email in the job_email field.
17. Enter the job URL in the job_url field.
18. Enter the job salary in the job_salary field.
19. Enter the job salary range in the job_salary_range field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the job posting.
* **Contact Number** (`2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the contact number for the job posting.
* **Job Title** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the title of the job posting.
* **Job Type** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of job (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance).
* **Work Type** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of work (Remote, On-site, or Hybrid).
* **Job Start Date** (`6`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the job.
* **Job End Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the job.
* **Prophets** (`8`, `note`, required/optional): Enter a note for the job posting.
* **Work Hours** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the work hours for the job posting.
* **Job Location** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the job.
* **Work Location** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the work location (Note: this field is not directly related to the job location).
* **Job Description** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the job.
* **Job Requirements** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the requirements for the job.
* **Job Requirements 2** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter additional job requirements.
* **Job Posting** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the job posting.
* **Job Email** (`16`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email for the job posting.
* **Job URL** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL for the job posting.
* **Job Salary** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the salary for the job posting.
* **Job Salary Range** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the salary range for the job posting.
