# Carrier Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carrier Application Form is used to collect information from carriers for the purpose of evaluating their application to operate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out accurately and completely.
3. If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Legal Name
* **Company Legal Name** (`company_legal_name`): Type the company's name as registered with the Department of Transportation (DOT).
### Doing Business As (DBA)
* **Doing Business As (DBA)** (`doing_business_as`): If your company operates under a different name, enter that name here.
### DOT Number
* **DOT Number** (`dot_number`): Enter your Department of Transportation (DOT) identifier.
### MC Number
* **MC Number** (`mc_number`): Enter your Motor Carrier authority number.
### Primary Contact Name
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
### Contact Phone Number
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
### Contact Email Address
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
### Dispatch Phone Number
* **Dispatch Phone Number** (`dispatch_phone`): Enter the 24-7 phone number of your dispatch service.
### Dispatch Email Address
* **Dispatch Email Address** (`dispatch_email`): Enter the email address of your dispatch service.
### Equipment Types
* **Equipment Types** (`equipment_types`): Select all the types of equipment your company operates. This can include Dry Van, Flatbed, Reefer, Step Deck, Power Only, or Box Truck.
### Total Number of Trucks
* **Total Number of Trucks** (`number_of_trucks`): Enter the total number of active trucks in your fleet.
### Total Number of Trailers
* **Total Number of Trailers** (`number_of_trailers`): Enter the total number of active trailers in your fleet.
### Primary Service Areas and Regions
* **Primary Service Areas and Regions** (`service_areas`): Enter the regions where your company primarily operates.
### Current Safety Rating
* **Current Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`): Select your current safety rating as per the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA).
### Hazmat Certified
* **Hazmat Certified** (`hazmat_certified`): Indicate whether your company has hazmat certification.
### CARB Compliant
* **CARB Compliant** (`carb_compliant`): Indicate whether your company meets California Air Resources Board (CARB) requirements.
### Auto Liability Insurance Amount
* **Auto Liability Insurance Amount** (`liability_insurance_amount`): Enter the total liability insurance coverage amount for your vehicles.
### Cargo Insurance Amount
* **Cargo Insurance Amount** (`cargo_insurance_amount`): Enter the total cargo insurance coverage amount.
### I Certify the Information Provided is Accurate
* **I Certify the Information Provided is Accurate** (`application_certification`): Type your name as digital signature to certify the information provided is accurate.
