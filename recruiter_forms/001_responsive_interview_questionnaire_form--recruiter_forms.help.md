# Responsive Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants about their suitability for a position in the company. It is not a comprehensive assessment, but rather a way to get a general idea of the applicant's preferences, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. You can skip any field that is not relevant to your situation.
3. If a field is multiple-choice, select the answer that best fits your situation.
4. If a field is open-ended, provide a detailed answer that best describes your thoughts or experiences.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Please enter a title that best describes your application.
* **Do you like working with others?** (`question_one`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you enjoy working with others, 'False' if you don't, or 'Undecided' if you are neutral.
* **How many years of experience do you have in this field?** (`question_two`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in this field.
* **What is your availability for the next 3 months?** (`question_three`, date, optional): Enter your availability in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **What time do you usually work?** (`question_four`, time, optional): Enter the time you usually work in the format 'HH:MM'.
* **Do you want to work as a freelancer or have a full-time job?** (`question_five`, select_one, optional): Select 'Freelancer' or 'Full-time'.
* **Do you have any other questions for us?** (`question_six`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you have any questions, 'False' if you don't.
* **How would you rate your skills in the given field?** (`question_sixteen`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'High', 'Medium', or 'Low' to rate your skills.
* **What is the best time to contact you?** (`question_sixteen`, time, optional): Enter the time when you are available for contact in the format 'HH:MM'.
* **Would you like to receive a follow-up email?** (`question_seventeen`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you want to receive a follow-up email, 'False' if you don't.
* **Do you have any other questions for us?** (`question_eighteen`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you have any questions, 'False' if you don't.
* **How would you rate this form?** (`question_nineteen`, select_one, optional): Select 'Good' or 'Bad' to rate this form.
* **What is the purpose of this survey?** (`question_twenty`, text, optional): Enter the purpose of this survey.
* **How did you come to this survey?** (`question_twenty_one`, select_one, optional): Select 'Social media', 'Google search', 'Referral', or 'Other' to describe how you came to this survey.
* **Do you want to be contacted by our company?** (`question_twenty_two`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you want to be contacted, 'False' if you don't.
* **What do you think about our company?** (`question_twenty_three`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts about our company.
* **What do you think about our company?** (`question_twenty_four`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts about our company.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, note, optional): This field is automatically filled when you submit the form.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form truthfully.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for your convenience, so feel free to submit it when you are ready.
* If you are having trouble submitting the form, please contact us for assistance.
