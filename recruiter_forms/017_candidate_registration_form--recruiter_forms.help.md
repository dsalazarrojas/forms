# Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about you, the candidate, for our review and future communication. Please fill out the form to help us get to know you better and tailor opportunities that suit your skills and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your email address in the field labeled "Email Address". Please ensure it's your primary email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone Number". This will be used for communication.
5. Enter your street address in the field labeled "Street Address". Include your home address.
6. Enter the name of the city where you reside in the field labeled "City".
7. Enter the state or province where you live in the field labeled "State or Province".
8. Enter your ZIP or postal code in the field labeled "ZIP or Postal Code".
9. Select your country of residence in the field labeled "Country".
10. If applicable, fill out the fields labeled "Date of Birth", "Graduation Date", and "Years of Work Experience".
11. Select your preferred gender in the field labeled "Gender".
12. Choose the positions you are interested in (full-time, part-time, contract, etc.) in the field labeled "Positions You Are Interested In".
13. Select the job categories that interest you in the field labeled "Job Categories of Interest".
14. Choose the locations where you would be willing to work (downtown, north side, etc.) in the field labeled "Preferred Work Locations".
15. Choose your highest education level in the field labeled "Highest Education Level".
16. If you've graduated, fill out the field labeled "Field of Study".
17. If you've graduated, enter the date you graduated in the field labeled "Graduation Date".
18. Enter the number of years of work experience you have in the field labeled "Years of Work Experience".
19. List your key skills in the field labeled "Key Skills".
20. If you speak languages other than your native language, select them in the field labeled "Languages Spoken".
21. Choose your work authorization status (Yes - citizen, Yes - permanent resident, etc.) in the field labeled "Are You Authorized to Work".
22. If you have a preferred salary range, enter it in the field labeled "Salary Expectation".
23. Choose when you can start working in the field labeled "When Can You Start".
24. Choose whether you are willing to relocate in the field labeled "Willing to Relocate".
25. If you've attached your resume, select "True" in the field labeled "Resume Uploaded".
26. If you've attached a cover letter, select "True" in the field labeled "Cover Letter Uploaded".
27. Choose how you heard about us in the field labeled "How Did You Hear About Us".
28. Finally, select the date you registered with us in the field labeled "Registration Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`candidate_first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`candidate_last_name`, text, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Provide your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Street Address** (`candidate_address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`candidate_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`candidate_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you live.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`candidate_zip`, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`candidate_country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your birth date.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender identity.
* **Positions You Are Interested In** (`positions_interested`, select_multiple, required): Choose the positions you are interested in (full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Job Categories of Interest** (`job_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select the job categories that interest you.
* **Preferred Work Locations** (`available_locations`, select_multiple, required): Choose the locations where you would be willing to work.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Choose your highest education level.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your major or area of study.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you graduated.
* **Years of Work Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years of work experience.
* **Key Skills** (`key_skills`, text, required): List your key skills.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages`, select_multiple, optional): If you speak languages other than your native language, select them.
* **Are You Authorized to Work** (`work_authorization`, select_one, required): Choose your work authorization status (Yes - citizen, etc.).
* **Salary Expectation** (`salary_expectation`, text, optional): If you have a preferred salary range, enter it.
* **When Can You Start** (`availability_to_start`, select_one, required): Choose when you can start working.
* **Willing to Relocate** (`willing_to_relocate`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are willing to relocate.
* **Resume Uploaded** (`resume_uploaded`, select_one, required): Select whether you've attached your resume.
* **Cover Letter Uploaded** (`cover_letter_uploaded`, select_one, optional): Select whether you've attached a cover letter.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, select_one, required): Choose how you heard about us.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Finally, select the date you registered with us.
