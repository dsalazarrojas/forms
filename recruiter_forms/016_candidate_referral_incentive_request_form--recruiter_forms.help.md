# Candidate Referral Incentive Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for current employees to refer candidates for open positions within the company. By filling out this form, you can help us identify potentially qualified candidates and provide incentives for your referrals.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name, ID number, and email address in the required fields (fields 1-3).
2. Provide the referred candidate's name, email address, and phone number (fields 6-8).
3. Describe the candidate's qualifications and what makes them stand out (fields 14-15).
4. Select the department and job posting ID or reference number where you think the candidate would fit best (fields 5 and 11).
5. Confirm your relationship to the candidate and how long you've known them (fields 12-13).
6. Choose the type of incentive you are claiming and provide a brief description (fields 19 and 20).
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to the referral policy and terms (fields 21-22).
8. Finally, provide any additional comments or information about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel documents.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your company ID number.
* **Employee Email Address** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone Number** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Your Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Referred Candidate Full Name** (`referred_candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are referring.
* **Referred Candidate Email** (`referred_candidate_email`, email, required): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Referred Candidate Phone** (`referred_candidate_phone`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Referred Candidate LinkedIn Profile** (`referred_candidate_linkedin`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's LinkedIn URL.
* **Position Referred For** (`position_referred_for`, text, required): Enter the job title or position for which you are referring the candidate.
* **Job Posting ID or Reference** (`job_posting_id`, text, optional): Enter the job posting ID or reference number.
* **Your Relationship to Candidate** (`relationship_to_candidate`, select_one, required): Select how you know the candidate.
* **How Long Have You Known the Candidate** (`how_long_known`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your relationship to the candidate.
* **Why Is This Candidate Qualified** (`candidate_qualifications`, text, required): Describe the candidate's qualifications.
* **Candidate Key Strengths** (`candidate_strengths`, text, required): Describe what makes the candidate stand out.
* **Date of Referral** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this referral.
* **Has the Candidate Been Hired** (`candidate_hired`, select_one, required): Select the candidate's employment status.
* **Candidate Hire Date** (`candidate_hire_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the candidate was hired.
* **Type of Incentive Requested** (`incentive_type_requested`, select_one, required): Select the type of incentive you are claiming.
* **Incentive Amount Expected** (`incentive_amount`, text, optional): Enter the expected value of the incentive.
* **Have You Read the Referral Policy** (`policy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select that you have read and understand the policy.
* **Do You Agree to Policy Terms** (`policy_agreement`, select_one, required): Select that you agree to the policy terms.
* **Are You Eligible for Referral Bonus** (`eligibility_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are eligible for a referral bonus.
* **Have You Made Previous Referrals** (`previous_referrals`, select_one, optional): Select your previous referral history.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Request Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this referral.

Note: Some fields may be optional or have specific instructions for formatting or selection. Please review each field carefully before submitting your referral.
