# Recruitment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit job openings or positions that are being recruited for within the organization. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly, providing all required information to ensure a smooth and effective recruitment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out the form correctly, as indicated by the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct job title from the options provided.
3. Choose the correct job type (Full time, Part time, or Freelance).
4. Write a brief description of the job in the job description field.
5. Provide your contact information, including your name and contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit form**: This field is the title of the form, which should not be filled out. It is a placeholder for the form title.
* **Upload resume** (`resume_upload`): Upload your resume in PDF format. This will help us get a better understanding of your background and experience.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`): This is where you provide your name and contact number. Make sure to include your correct contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select the correct job title for the position you are submitting. This will help us understand the role and responsibilities of the position.
* **Location** (`location`): Provide the location where the position is based.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Choose the correct job type (Full time, Part time, or Freelance).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`): Write a brief description of the job, including the key responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`): This field is optional and can be used to provide additional information about the position.
* **Submitter info** (`submitter_info`): This is where you provide your name and contact number.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Provide your email address so we can contact you regarding the submission. 

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have already submitted a job opening with the same title within the past 3 months, do not submit the same form again.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise job description and requirements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
