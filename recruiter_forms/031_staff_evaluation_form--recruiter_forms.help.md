# Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Staff Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from staff members on their performance, skills, and areas of improvement to help managers or supervisors identify strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Staff Evaluation Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your staff member's information:
	* Enter the staff member's name in the **Staff Member Information** field.
	* Provide a brief description of the staff member's role or position.
2. Provide your evaluation:
	* Enter your evaluation criteria in the **Evaluation Criteria** field.
	* Describe your evaluation results in the **Evaluation Results** field.
3. Add any comments or suggestions:
	* Write any comments or suggestions for the staff member in the **Comments** field.
	* If applicable, provide recommendations for growth or areas of improvement in the **Recommendations** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Member Information**: Enter the staff member's name to associate the evaluation with the correct person. This field is optional.
* **Supervisor Information**: Enter your name and title as the supervisor conducting the evaluation. This field is optional.
* **Evaluation Criteria**: Describe the criteria or standards used to evaluate the staff member's performance.
* **Evaluation Results**: Enter the results of your evaluation, describing the staff member's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the staff member to improve their performance.
* **Recommendations**: If applicable, provide recommendations for the staff member's growth or areas of improvement.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and honestly.
* Keep the feedback constructive and specific.
* Use specific examples to support your evaluation results.
* Review and finalize the form before submitting it to the staff member.

Note: The date field is not required for most users, but it's a good practice to include it for record-keeping purposes.
