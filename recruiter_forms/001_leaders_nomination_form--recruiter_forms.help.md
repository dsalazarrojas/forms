# Leaders Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to nominate a colleague for their exceptional performance and potential leadership abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Select the nominee's position title from the list of available options.
3. Provide a brief description of the nominee's leadership qualities and achievements in the Nominee Brief field.
4. Provide a brief description of yourself and your relationship with the nominee in the Submitter Brief field.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the nominated leader's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the nominated leader's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the nominated leader's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the nominated leader's phone number.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, select_multiple, required): Select the nominee's position title from the list of available options: Assistant Manager, Assistant Director, Deputy Manager.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of why you are nominating this person for leadership recognition.
* **Nominee Brief** (`nominee_brief`, text, optional, 1500 char): Provide a 1500 character description of the nominee's leadership qualities and achievements.
* **Submitter Brief** (`submitter_brief`, text, optional, 500 char): Provide a 500 character description of your relationship with the nominee and why you are submitting this nomination.
