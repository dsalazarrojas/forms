# Hiring Manager Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Manager Satisfaction Survey is designed to gauge the satisfaction level of hiring managers with the recruitment process. It helps us understand your experiences, strengths, and areas for improvement, which we will use to refine our processes and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Enter your Job Title.
3. Choose your Department.
4. Rate your Overall Satisfaction with a number from 1 to 10.
5. Select your level of agreement with the statement "I feel that my communication with our recruitment team is good.".
6. Provide any Feedback on your experiences.
7. Offer any Suggestions on how we can improve.
8. Rate your level of satisfaction with your current team by selecting one of the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Text) (Required)
*   **First Name**: A text field to fill in your first name.

### Last Name (Text) (Optional)
*   **Last Name**: A text field to fill in your last name.

### Email (Email) (Optional)
*   **Email**: An email field to fill in your email address.

### Job Title (Text) (Optional)
*   **Job Title**: A text field to fill in your job title.

### Department (Text) (Optional)
*   **Department**: A text field to fill in your department name.

### Overall Satisfaction (Number) (Optional)
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: A rating system to rate your overall satisfaction with a number from 1 to 10.

### Communication (Select One) (Optional)
*   **Communication**: A dropdown menu to select your level of agreement with the statement "I feel that my communication with our recruitment team is good.".

### Feedback (Text) (Optional)
*   **Feedback**: A text field to provide any feedback on your experiences.

### Suggestions (Text) (Optional)
*   **Suggestions**: A text field to offer any suggestions on how we can improve.

### How Satisfied Are You with Your Current Team? (Select One) (Optional)
*   **How satisfied are you with your current team?**: A dropdown menu to rate your level of satisfaction with your current team.
