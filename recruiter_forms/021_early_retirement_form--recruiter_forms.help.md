# Early Retirement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Early Retirement Form is a tool used to gather essential information about an employee's intention to retire early. This form helps to clarify the employee's reasons for leaving, their future plans, and other relevant details. It is a crucial document that aids in maintaining an accurate and up-to-date record of employee information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Early Retirement Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Early Retire Plan** (`early_retirement_form_plan`, text, required: false): This field is for the employee to provide a brief description of their early retirement plan.
* **Reasons for Leaving** (`early_retirement_form_reasons`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the reasons that apply for leaving the company.
* **Future Plans** (`early_retirement_form_future_plans`, text, required: false): Describe your future plans, including any new career goals or pursuits.
* **Last Day Worked** (`early_retirement_form_last_day_worked`, date, required: false): The date of the last day you worked for this company.
* **Last Day Paid** (`early_retirement_form_last_day_paid`, date, required: false): The date of the last payment received from the company.
* **Last Day of Work** (`early_retirement_form_last_day_of_work`, date, required: false): The date when you last worked in the office.
* **Managers Name** (`early_retirement_form_managers_name`, text, required: false): The name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Managers Email** (`early_retirement_form_managers_email`, email, required: false): The email address of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Notice Period** (`early_retirement_form_manager_notice_period`, number, required: false): The number of weeks or days given for notice.
* **Notice Period Days** (`early_retirement_form_manager_notice_period_days`, number, required: false): The number of days given for notice.
* **Last Notice Period Date** (`early_retirement_form_last_notice_period_date`, date, required: false): The date of the last notice period.
* **Comments** (`early_retirement_form_comments`, text, required: false): Any additional comments or remarks.
* **Last Day Checked Out** (`early_retirement_form_last_day_checked_out`, date, required: false): The date when you last checked out of the office.
* **Last Leave Allowed** (`early_retirement_form_last_leave_allowed`, date, required: false): The date when leave was last allowed.
* **Date of Notice** (`early_retirement_form_date_of_notice`, date, required: false): The date when the notice was given.
* **Expected Date of Return** (`early_retirement_form_expected_date_of_return`, date, required: false): The expected date of your return to work.
* **Last Day Worked Locally** (`early_retirement_form_last_worked_locally`, date, required: false): The date when you last worked locally.
* **Last Notice Date** (`early_retirement_form_last_notice_date`, date, required: false): The date of the last notice.
* **Last Date of Absence** (`early_retirement_form_last_date_of_absence`, date, required: false): The date of your last absence.
* **Last Date of Return to Work** (`early_retirement_form_last_date_of_return_to_work`, date, required: false): The date of your last return to work.
* **Expected Date of Return to Work** (`early_retirement_form_expected_date_of_return_to_work`, date, required: false): The expected date of your return to work.
* **Last Date of Termination** (`early_retirement_form_last_date_of_termination`, date, required: false): The date of your last termination.
* **Last Date of Termination Date** (`early_retirement_form_last_date_of_termination_date`, date, required: false): The date of your last termination date.
* **Last Date of Termination Confirmed** (`early_retirement_form_last_date_of_termination_confirmed`, date, required: false): The date of your last termination confirmed date.

## Tips

* Fill in the form accurately and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
