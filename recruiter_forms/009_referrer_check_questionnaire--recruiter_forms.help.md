# Referrer Check Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity and qualifications of a person recommending a candidate for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact information and relationship with the candidate.
2. Provide a brief description of why you are recommending this candidate.
3. Answer any additional questions on the subsequent pages, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **page 1**: This is the first page of the form, where you will provide your contact information. Please fill out the form fields with your real name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **page 2**: On this page, you will describe your relationship with the candidate, such as "colleague", "friend", "family member", etc.
* **page 3**: Here, you will provide a brief explanation of why you are recommending this candidate. Try to be specific about the candidate's qualifications and skills that make them suitable for the position.
* **page 4**: This page is not applicable as the form only has 3 pages.
* **page 5**: This page is also not applicable.

Note: Please ensure you only fill out this form if you are a real person with a genuine reason for recommending a candidate.
