# Digital Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Digital Recruitment Form, which is designed to collect relevant information from job applicants for recruitment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your relevant information, paying attention to the required fields marked as such.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Select the appropriate options for each field, if applicable.
* Review your submission before clicking the "submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Education
* **Education** (`job_applicant_education`): Enter your educational background, including your level of education, institution, and relevant courses.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`job_applicant_skills`): Select the skills you possess from the list of options provided.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`job_applicant_experience`): Describe your relevant work experience in this field.

### Resume Upload
* **Resume Upload** (`job_applicant_resume`): Upload your resume or CV for our review.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`job_applicant_contact_info`): Enter your email address and any other relevant contact details.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`job_applicant_phone_number`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`job_applicant_availability`): Select your availability for an interview or job start date.

### Reference
* **Reference** (`job_applicant_reference`): Provide the name and contact information of a professional reference.

### Reference 1
* **Reference 1** (`job_applicant_reference_1`): Provide additional reference information, if necessary.

### Submission Status
* **Submission Status** (`submit`): Select the option that best describes your submission status.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission before clicking the "submit" button to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
