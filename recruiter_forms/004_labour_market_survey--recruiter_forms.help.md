# Labour Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labour Market Survey form is a tool used to collect data on market trends, consumer preferences, and other relevant information from various stakeholders in the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the sample size of your market survey.
2. Select the method you usually use to collect data.
3. Choose the frequency at which you conduct surveys.
4. Select the period of your survey.
5. Enter the time for your survey.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.
7. Enter your contact email.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Select the tool assigned to you for this survey.
10. Select the surveyor assigned to you.
11. Select the recruiter assigned to you.
12. Select the recruitment agency assigned to you.
13. Enter any comments or notes.
14. Select the category of the survey.
15. Select the subcategory of the survey.
16. Enter the location of the survey.
17. Select the status of the surveyor.
18. Select the role of the surveyor.
19. Select the experience of the surveyor.
20. Select the manager assigned to you.
21. Select the coordinator assigned to you.
22. Enter any additional notes for the surveyor.
23. Select who submitted the survey.
24. Enter any comments or notes.
