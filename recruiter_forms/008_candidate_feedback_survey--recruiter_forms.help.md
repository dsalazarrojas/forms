# Candidate Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from candidates about their interview experience with our company. This feedback will help us improve our interview process and better serve our future candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best match your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Submit your feedback to help us improve our interview process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Position Interviewed For** (`position_title`, `text`, required): The job title you were interviewing for.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, required): The date of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name (if known)** (`interviewer_name`, `text`, optional): If you can remember the name of the interviewer, please share it.
* **Type of Interview** (`interview_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of interview you had:
	+ Phone Screening
	+ Video Interview
	+ In-Person Interview
	+ Panel Interview
	+ Technical Interview
	+ Group Interview
	+ Other
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, `text`, optional): If you were interviewed in-person, please provide the location.
* **Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approximate length of the interview:
	+ Less than 15 minutes
	+ 15-30 minutes
	+ 30-45 minutes
	+ 45 minutes - 1 hour
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ More than 2 hours
* **How Comfortable Did You Feel During the Interview?** (`comfort_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your comfort level:
	+ Very Comfortable
	+ Comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Uncomfortable
	+ Very Uncomfortable
* **How Clear Was the Interviewer's Communication?** (`interviewer_communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of the interviewer's communication:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Adequate
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Did the Interviewer Listen Attentively to Your Answers?** (`listening_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select one:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How Relevant Were the Questions?** (`question_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the relevance of the questions:
	+ Very Relevant
	+ Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Not Relevant
	+ Irrelevant
* **Were the Questions Appropriately Difficult?** (`question_difficulty`, `select_one`, optional): Select one:
	+ Too Easy
	+ Appropriate
	+ Too Difficult
	+ Inconsistent Difficulty
* **Did the Questions Feel Fair and Unbiased?** (`question_fairness`, `select_one`, required): Select one:
	+ Very Fair
	+ Fair
	+ Somewhat Fair
	+ Unfair
* **Did the Interviewer Demonstrate Knowledge About the Company?** (`company_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Select one:
	+ Excellent Knowledge
	+ Good Knowledge
	+ Basic Knowledge
	+ Limited Knowledge
	+ No Knowledge
* **Did the Interviewer Clearly Explain Job Expectations?** (`job_expectations`, `select_one`, required): Select one:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ No Information Provided
* **What is Your Overall Impression of the Company Based on This Interview?** (`overall_impression`, `select_one`, required): Rate your impression:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Would You Be Interested in Continuing with This Position?** (`would_continue`, `select_one`, required): Select one:
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **What Could Be Improved About the Interview Process?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share your suggestions.
* **Any Other Comments or Feedback?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any other comments or feedback.
