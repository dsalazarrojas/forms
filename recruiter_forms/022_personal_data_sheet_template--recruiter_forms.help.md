# Personal Data Sheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal data from individuals for internal use within your organization. It is a generic template with a simple and clear structure, intended to provide a comprehensive understanding of the individual's personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review the form to understand the sections and fields.
2. Enter your personal details into the corresponding fields.
3. Submit the form when you have filled out all required fields (if any).
4. Review your submissions before saving or sharing with others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal name (id: 1)
* **Personal name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name, including first and last names.

### Email (id: 2)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.

### Address (id: 3)
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your mailing address for personal correspondence.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete submission.
* Review your submission carefully before saving or sharing with others to prevent errors.
* Use this template as a starting point and modify it according to your specific needs.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
