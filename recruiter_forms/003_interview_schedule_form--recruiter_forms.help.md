# Inteview Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inteview Schedule Form is a tool used to create and manage interview schedules. It helps users to collect and organize necessary information for interviews, ensuring that all parties involved are aware of the schedule and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Interview Location**: Enter the location where the interview will take place.
   - **Date of Interview**: Choose the date of the interview.
   - **Time of Interview**: Select the time of the interview.
   - **How would you like to be contacted**: Choose your preferred method of contact.
   - **Interviewer Name**: Enter the name of the interviewer.
   - **Confirm schedule**: Confirm that the schedule is correct.
2. Provide optional information:
   - **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or details about the interview.
   - **Interviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the interviewer.
   - **Interviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the interviewer.
   - **Interviewer Comments**: Add any comments from the interviewer.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Location** (`interview_schedule_form_interview_location`): Enter the location where the interview will take place.
* **Date of Interview** (`interview_schedule_form_date`): Choose the date of the interview.
* **Time of Interview** (`interview_schedule_form_time`): Select the time of the interview.
* **How would you like to be contacted** (`interview_schedule_form_preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone call, or Text message).
* **Interviewer Name** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer`): Enter the name of the interviewer.
* **Confirm schedule** (`interview_schedule_form_confirm_schedule`): Confirm that the schedule is correct.
* **Additional Comments** (`interview_schedule_form_additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or details about the interview.
* **Interviewer Email** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_email`): Enter the email address of the interviewer.
* **Interviewer Phone** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the interviewer.
* **Interviewer Comments** (`interview_schedule_form_interviewer_comments`): Add any comments from the interviewer.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the entered information before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the form to ensure that all parties involved understand the schedule and details.
