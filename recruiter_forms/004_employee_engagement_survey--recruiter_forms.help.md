# Employee Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their job satisfaction, departmental satisfaction, company satisfaction, and communication. The collected data can help HR or management identify areas for improvement and enhance the overall employee experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your employee name.
2. Enter your job title.
3. Specify your department.
4. Type your company name.
5. Answer the satisfaction questions on a scale from 1-10, indicating your level of satisfaction with your job, department, and company.
6. Provide your suggestions for improvement in the text field.
7. Enter your manager's name.
8. Type your manager's title (e.g., Manager, Director, etc.).
9. Enter your manager's email address.
10. Type your manager's phone number.
11. Answer the satisfaction question on communication.
12. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name**: Enter your employee name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **job_title**: Type your job title or position within the company.
* **department**: Specify your department or team you belong to.
* **company**: Type your company name.
* **question_1**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with your job. 1 is dissatisfied, 10 is satisfied.
* **question_2**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with your department. 1 is dissatisfied, 10 is satisfied.
* **question_3**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with your company. 1 is dissatisfied, 10 is satisfied.
* **question_4**: Provide suggestions for improvement, if any.
* **employee_suggestion**: Type any suggestions or comments for improvement.
* **manager_name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **manager_title**: Type your manager's title (e.g., Manager, Director, etc.).
* **manager_email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **manager_phone**: Type your manager's phone number.
* **question_5**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with the feedback process. 1 is dissatisfied, 10 is satisfied.
* **question_6**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your satisfaction with communication. 1 is dissatisfied, 10 is satisfied.
