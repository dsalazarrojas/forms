# Recruitment Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals who have gone through the recruitment process. Your input will help us improve the experience for future candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide a thorough feedback, please answer the following questions based on your recent experience:

1. Please rate your overall experience in the recruitment process, focusing on your most recent interview.
2. Describe your experience in as few words as possible, while being as specific as you can.
3. Rate the communication from the team, using a scale from 1 to 10.
4. Rate the interview process, from 1 to 10.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey**: This page serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **What was your experience like?**: Please describe your most recent experience in the recruitment process, focusing on specific events or interactions.
* **How would you rate our communication?**: Rate the communication from the team, using a scale from 1 to 10. A higher rating indicates better communication.
* **How would you rate the interview process?**: Rate the overall interview process, using a scale from 1 to 10. A higher rating indicates a more efficient and effective process.
* **Do you have any additional feedback?**: This is an opportunity to share any comments or concerns you have about the recruitment process. Your input is valuable, so please be as specific as possible.
