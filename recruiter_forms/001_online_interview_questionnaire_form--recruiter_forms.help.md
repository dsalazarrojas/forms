# online-interview-questionnaire-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for online interviews, such as screening or assessment interviews, where the recruiter or assessor needs to gather information from the candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date.
2. Select the relevant options for "select one" and "select multiple" questions.
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
4. Add any additional comments or notes.
5. Enter your score (if applicable).
6. Select additional options for "select one other" and "select multiple other" questions (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: This is a required field to identify the form for reference.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the current date in the format `dd/mm/yyyy`.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the current time in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
* **Select one** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more options from the dropdown list.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Score** (`score`, `number`, optional): Enter your score.
* **Select one other** (`select_one_other`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select multiple other** (`select_multiple_other`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more options from the dropdown list.
* **File** (`file`, `text`, optional): Upload a file.
* **URL** (`url`, `text`, optional): Enter a URL.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`, `text`, optional): Enter the date and time in the format `dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Date Range** (`date_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a date range in the format `start date - end date`.
* **Time Range** (`time_range`, `text`, optional): Enter a time range in the format `start time - end time`.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter a date in the format `dd/mm/yyyy`.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time in the format `hh:mm AM/PM`.
