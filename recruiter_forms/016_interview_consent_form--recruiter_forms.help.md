# Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of an interview or session, including consent from the interviewee, and to ensure that the interview is conducted fairly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Consent Form**: Provide a brief explanation of the interview, including the session details and the reason for the interview. This is where you should describe the interview to the interviewee and obtain their consent.
2. **Consent Date**: Enter the date on which the consent was obtained.
3. **Session Details**: Provide a brief description of the session, including the session start and end times.
4. **Session Location**: Enter the location where the session was conducted.
5. **Session Length**: Enter the length of the session.
6. **Session Notes**: Enter any notes from the session.
7. **Session Outcome**: Enter the outcome of the session.
8. **Consent Given**: Check "True" if consent was obtained from the interviewee, "False" if consent was not obtained, or "declined" if the interviewee declined to participate.
9. **Time of Consent**: Enter the time at which consent was obtained.
10. **Reason Declined**: If consent was declined, enter the reason for the decline.
11. **Consent Note**: Enter any additional notes about the consent process.
12. **Recruiter Signature**: Sign and date your signature to indicate that you have conducted the session and obtained consent from the interviewee.
13. **Interviewer Signature**: Sign and date your signature to indicate that you have participated in the session.
14. **Interviewee Signature**: Sign and date your signature to indicate that you have participated in the session and understand the details of the consent.
15. **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your signature.
16. **Session Conducted By**: Enter the name and role of the person who conducted the session.
17. **Recruiter Email**: Enter the contact email of the recruiter.
18. **Recruiter Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the recruiter.
19. **Interviewer Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the interviewer.
20. **Interviewee Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the interviewee.
21. **Session Conducted At**: Enter the date and time the session was conducted.
