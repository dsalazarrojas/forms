# Interview Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Assessment Form is a tool used by recruiters and hiring managers to assess candidates during the interview process. It collects information about the candidate's job title, personal details, and assessment results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct candidate and job application.
2. Complete each field according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the candidate's position.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the interview.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the interview.
* **Interviewer Comments** (`interviewer_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments from the interviewer.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter the score given to the candidate (1-10).
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Choose the candidate's rating (Excellent, Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Unsatisfactory).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of job (Full Time, Part Time, Remote, Contract, Freelance, Internship, Temporary).
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose the department (Sales, Marketing, HR, Admin, Operations, Engineering).
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the candidate is a team lead ('True', 'False', Maybe).
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, required): Choose whether the candidate has a manager ('True', 'False', Maybe).
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the candidate has a supervisor ('True', 'False', Maybe).
* **Direct Report** (`direct_report`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the candidate is a direct report ('True', 'False').
* **Performance Review** (`performance_review`, select_one, required): Choose whether a performance review is required ('True', 'False').
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the candidate participates in a training program ('True', 'False').
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the interview.
* **Interview Type** (`interview_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of interview (Face-to-Face, Phone, Video, Online).
* **Interview Format** (`interview_format`, select_multiple, required): Choose the format of the interview (One on One, Panel, Group, Panel with multiple candidates, One on One with multiple interviews, Group with multiple interviews).
* **Candidate Background Check** (`candidate_background_check`, select_one, required): Choose whether a background check is required ('True', 'False').
* **Candidate References** (`candidate_references`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the candidate's references are checked ('True', 'False').
