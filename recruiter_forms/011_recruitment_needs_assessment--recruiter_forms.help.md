# Recruitment Needs Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recruitment Needs Assessment form is designed to gather essential information from the business team about the recruitment needs of the organization. This form helps identify the key requirements for a specific role, including its budget allocation, project timelines, job title, and job description.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are providing accurate information about the role's requirements.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each field, but be concise and clear in your responses.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role Requirements** (`role_requirements`, text, required/optional: false): Please provide a brief description of the role's requirements, including its responsibilities and key performance indicators (KPIs). This will help us understand the scope of work for the role.
* **Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`, number, required/optional: false): Enter the estimated budget allocation for the role, which will help us plan and resource the recruitment process.
* **Project Timelines** (`project_timelines`, date, required/optional: false): Provide the project timelines, including the start and end dates, to help us schedule and plan the recruitment process.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the title of the role, which will help us create the job posting and attract the right candidates.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional: false): Write a detailed job description that outlines the role's responsibilities, requirements, and expectations. This will help us create an accurate job posting and attract the right candidates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide clear and concise information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from your team.
* This form is a critical input for our recruitment process, so please take the time to provide accurate and thoughtful responses.
