# Research Intern Interview Scorecard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the score given to research interns after conducting an interview. The score is based on the evaluator's assessment of the intern's performance and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the score for the research intern interview. Choose a number between 1 and 10, representing your evaluation of their performance.
2. Add any additional notes about the interview, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **research_intern_interview_scorecard_form** (`text`, **Optional**): Enter a brief description of the interview, such as the date and location.
  This field is used to provide context for the score.
- **research_intern_interview_scorecard_form_score** (`number`, **Optional**): Give a score between 1 and 10 for the research intern's performance during the interview.
  This score represents your evaluation of their abilities and performance.
- **research_intern_interview_scorecard_form_notes** (`note`, **Optional**): Provide any additional notes about the interview, if necessary.
  This field is for any extra information that may not be captured in the score.
- **research_intern_interview_scorecard_form_score_2** (`number`, **Optional**): Enter another score between 1 and 10 for the research intern's performance during a different aspect of the interview.
  This score provides an alternative perspective on their abilities and performance, allowing for a more nuanced evaluation.

## Tips
* Ensure that each form is unique and not duplicated for the same research intern.
* Use the score to provide a subjective evaluation of the intern's performance and abilities.
* Use the notes field to capture any additional information that may not be covered in the score.
* Complete the form for each research intern separately.
