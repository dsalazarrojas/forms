# employee_referral_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employee referral questionnaire is a form used by recruiters to gather information about an employee's job preferences and details. This form helps recruiters understand the employee's career aspirations and job expectations, making it easier for them to provide relevant job opportunities. The information gathered through this form will help us match employees with suitable job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in **Contact Details** and **Contact Details 2**, if necessary.
2. Select the job type, department, location, and title that best match your preferences in **Job Details**.
3. Provide a brief description of the job you are looking for in **Job Description**.
4. (Optional) If you are looking for a specific salary range, enter a minimum and maximum salary in **Job Salary**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you about job opportunities.
* **Contact Details 2** (`Contact Details 2`, text, optional):
	+ If you have a second contact method, such as a LinkedIn profile or a work email, you can enter it here.
* **Job Details** (`Job Details`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the job type, department, location, and title that best match your preferences.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, optional):
	+ Type a brief description of the job you are looking for, including any specific skills or qualifications you have.
* **Job Salary** (`Job Salary`, number, optional):
	+ If you have a minimum and maximum salary range in mind, enter it here to help us narrow down potential job matches.

## Tips
* Make sure to be specific when selecting job details, as this will help us match you with more relevant job opportunities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
* The more information you provide, the better we can tailor job opportunities to your preferences.
