# Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to submit their personal information, educational background, contact details, and references. The purpose of this form is to collect basic data from applicants for the employer's consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Personal Info** page with your name, email address, and any other relevant personal details.
2. On the **Educational Background** page, provide information about your educational background, including degrees, institutions, and relevant courses or certifications.
3. If you have any references or would like to provide additional contact information, fill out the **Contact Info** page. This is optional and only required if you have references to provide.
4. Finally, on the **Reference Info** page, provide information about your professional references, including their names, job titles, and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (`name`, `type`, required/optional): This is the first page where you will provide your personal information, including your name and email address. This is a required field.
  - **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your application.
  - **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached by the employer.
- **Educational Background** (`name`, `type`, required/optional): This page is where you will provide your educational history, including degrees, institutions, and relevant courses or certifications.
  - **Educational Background**: Briefly describe your educational background, including any relevant degrees, institutions, or courses.
- **Contact Info** (`name`, `type`, required/optional): This page is optional and is where you can provide additional contact information if you have references to provide.
  - **Contact Info**: If you have references, you can enter their names, job titles, and contact details here.
- **Reference Info** (`name`, `type`, required/optional): This is the final page where you will provide information about your professional references.
  - **Reference Info**: Enter the names, job titles, and contact details of your professional references.
