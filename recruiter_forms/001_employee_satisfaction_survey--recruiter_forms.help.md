# Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help employees complete the Employee Satisfaction Survey form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To start, please provide your basic information, including your First Name, Email, Job Title, Department, and Phone Number. Next, answer the satisfaction questions by rating your feelings on a 1-5 scale. You may also choose to provide additional comments or answers to the select questions about your team and role. Finally, confirm your answers by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (2): Enter your official work email address.
* **Job Title** (3): Enter your official job title as it appears on your work documents.
* **Department** (4): Enter the department you belong to.
* **How satisfied are you with your team?** (5): Rate your satisfaction on a 1-5 scale, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **How satisfied are you with your job?** (6): Rate your satisfaction on a 1-5 scale, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Do you enjoy coming to work?** (7): Select "True" if you enjoy coming to work and "False" if you do not.
* **Do you feel supported by your team?** (8): Select all that apply: "True", "False", or "Sometimes".
* **Do you have a clear understanding of your role?** (9): Select "True" or "False".
* **Do you have a clear understanding of the team's goals?** (10): Select "True" or "False".
* **Comments** (11): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
* **Date Completed** (12): Enter the date you completed the form (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time Completed** (13): Enter the time you completed the form (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Phone Number** (14): Enter your work phone number.
* **Email 2** (15): Enter your official work email address (if different from your email).
* **Job Title 2** (16): Enter your official job title (if different from your title).
* **Department 2** (17): Enter the department you belong to (if different from your department).
* **First Name 2** (18): Enter your first name (if different from your first name).
* **Last Name** (19): Enter your last name.
* **Comments (2)** (20): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
* **Date Received** (21): Enter the date the form was received (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time Received** (22): Enter the time the form was received (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Assigned Tool** (23): Enter the tool or system used to assign this form.
* **Form ID** (24): Enter the unique identifier for this form.
* **Output File** (25): This field is for internal use only, please leave it blank.
