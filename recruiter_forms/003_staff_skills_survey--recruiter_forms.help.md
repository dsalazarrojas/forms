# Staff Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Skills Survey is designed to help staff members express their interest levels in various tasks and sections within their job roles. This survey is meant to be a self-reporting tool for personal development and team building purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the tasks and sections that you are interested in or have experience with. For each section, you will be presented with a list of tasks and options to select from.
2.  For each section, you will have the option to select "Strongly interested", "Somewhat interested", or "Not at all" to indicate your level of interest. This will help you better understand your own strengths and weaknesses.
3.  Once you have selected all the sections and tasks that apply to you, submit the form. Your responses will not be evaluated or used for any formal purposes.
