# Talent Search Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Search Registration Form is designed to help us discover new talents and skills within our organization. This form collects personal and professional information from individuals who want to showcase their abilities and potentially be considered for future opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to allow us to contact you.
3. Enter your Phone Number for future communication.
4. Select your Date of Birth.
5. Choose your Talent Area of Interest from the provided options.
6. If you have any singing experience, select the relevant fields from the list.
7. If you have any additional information that you'd like to share, enter it in the "Additional Info" field.
8. If you have been assigned a specific tool for this talent search, enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.
9. If your tool is not listed, select "Other" and enter your tool in the "Other Tool" field.
10. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for future communication.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Talent Area of Interest** (select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of talent you are interested in (Singing, Music, Dance, Acting).
* **Singing Fields** (select_one, required): If you are a singer, select the type of singing you do (Classical, Musical, Pop, Opera).
* **Additional Info** (note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Assigned Tool** (note, optional): If you have been assigned a specific tool, enter it here.
* **Other** (note, optional): If your tool is not listed, select "Other" and enter your tool in the "Other Tool" field.
* **Other Tool** (text, optional): Enter your tool name if you selected "Other".
* **Submit** (text, required): Click to submit your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (note, optional): This field is for internal use only, please ignore.
* **Assigned Tool Other** (note, optional): This field is for internal use only, please ignore.
