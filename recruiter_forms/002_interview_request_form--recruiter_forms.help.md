# interview_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interview_request_form is a tool used to gather information from candidates during the interview process. This form helps recruiters and hiring managers to efficiently gather relevant details about the candidate, such as their preferred interview time and date, availability, and interview type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Candidate Information**: Enter the candidate's name (first and last name), email address, and phone number.
2. **Select Interviewers**: Choose the interviewers you would like to schedule with you. Select as many interviewers as necessary.
3. **Preferred Interview Time**: Choose a preferred interview time that suits you best.
4. **Preferred Interview Date**: Choose a preferred interview date that works for you.
5. **Availability**: Select your availability for the interview (e.g., Monday to Friday).
6. **Interview Type**: Choose the type of interview you prefer (e.g., phone, video, in-person, or other).
7. **Interview Details**: Add any additional information about the interview.
8. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional context or information that may be helpful for the interview process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`): Please fill in the candidate's first and last name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter the candidate's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter the candidate's last name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the candidate's email address. Please ensure it is accurate to prevent email bounce backs or lost communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Interviewers** (`interviewers`): Select as many interviewers as you would like to schedule with you. This will help us to allocate resources appropriately.
* **Preferred Interview Time** (`preferred_interview_time`): Choose a preferred interview time that suits you best. Please select from the available options or provide a custom time.
* **Preferred Interview Date** (`preferred_interview_date`): Choose a preferred interview date that works for you. Please select from the available options or provide a custom date.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Select your availability for the interview (e.g., Monday to Friday).
* **Interview Type** (`interview_type`): Choose the type of interview you prefer (e.g., phone, video, in-person, or other).
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`): Add any additional information about the interview, such as its purpose or expected duration.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`): Provide any additional context or information that may be helpful for the interview process.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the job title or position for which you are applying. 

Note: Please ensure to review and submit the form carefully, as this will help the recruiters and hiring managers to schedule the interview according to your preferred time and date.
