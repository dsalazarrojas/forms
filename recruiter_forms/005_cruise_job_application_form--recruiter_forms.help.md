# Cruise Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect the necessary information for users to apply for cruise-related job postings. This form is intended for candidates who are interested in working on cruise ships, either as crew members or for other roles. Please fill out this form carefully and completely to submit your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your valid Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached. This will be used as your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required: false): Enter your phone number, including the country code, for communication purposes.
