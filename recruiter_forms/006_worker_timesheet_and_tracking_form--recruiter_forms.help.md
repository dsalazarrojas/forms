# Worker Timesheet And Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Worker Timesheet And Tracking Form is used to track employee work hours, including start and end times, lunch breaks, and notes. This form helps to accurately record employee work hours, which can be used for payroll purposes or other administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a asterisk \* below). Enter your Employee ID, select your Start Time and End Time, and provide a valid Lunch Duration and Hours Worked.
2. Optionally, add any additional notes about your work hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID\*** (`employee_id`): \*Enter your unique Employee ID number, which helps the system identify you as the employee submitting this form.
* **Start Time\*** (`start_time`): Enter the time you started working in the format 12:00 AM (12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, etc.).
* **End Time\*** (`end_time`): Enter the time you finished working in the format 12:00 AM (12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, etc.).
* **Lunch Duration** (`lunch_duration`): Enter the duration of your lunch break in hours.
* **Hours Worked\*** (`hours_worked`): Enter the total number of hours you worked, including breaks and lunch.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or details about your work hours if needed.
