# Employee Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Appraisal Form is a tool used to evaluate and assess an employee's performance over a certain period of time. It helps supervisors or reviewers to identify areas of improvement and provide feedback to employees.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Employee Name** field with the name of the employee being evaluated.
2.  Select the **Date** of the evaluation in the corresponding field.
3.  Choose the overall **Rating** of the employee's performance.
4.  In the **Comments** field, provide any observations or feedback about the employee's performance.
5.  Select the **Date of next review** for the employee.
6.  Choose whether the employee needs **Recommended Training**.
7.  Choose whether the employee's performance has **Increased**, **Decreased**, or **No Change**.
8.  In the **Areas for Improvement** field, provide any areas where the employee needs to improve.
9.  Sign the form with your **Employee Signature**.
10. Sign the form with your **Reviewer Signature**.
11. Select the **Date of Signature** for both your signatures.
12. Choose whether the employee's performance has **Increased**, **Decreased**, or **No Change** in the **Employee Progress 2** field.
13. In the **Areas For Improvement 2** field, provide any areas where the employee needs to improve.
14. Choose whether the employee needs **Recommended Training 2**.
