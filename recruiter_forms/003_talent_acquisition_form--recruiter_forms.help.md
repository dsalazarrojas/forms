# Talent Acquisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Acquisition Form is used for gathering information from candidates or employees about job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Enter your job title and any other relevant details in the job title field.
3. Select the correct job status from the drop-down menu (e.g., Open, Closed, Pending).
4. Choose the correct person who submitted you for the job (e.g., HR, Manager, Recruiter, Team Member).
5. Enter the date and time you submitted your application.
6. Enter any additional comments or job descriptions if necessary.
7. Fill out any other required fields marked as true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **candidate_name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **job_title** (text, not required): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **resume** (text, not required): Attach or paste a resume or CV, if necessary.
* **job_application** (text, not required): Enter a brief job application.
* **work_experience** (note, not required): Enter any relevant work experience.
* **education** (text, not required): Enter your educational background.
* **languages** (text, not required): Enter any languages you are proficient in.
* **references** (text, not required): Enter any professional references.
* **name** (text, required): Re-enter your name for verification purposes.
* **company** (text, required): Enter the company you are applying to or associated with.
* **job_title_2** (text, required): Enter the job title for which you are applying.
* **contact_email** (email, not required): Enter an email address for contact purposes.
* **contact_phone** (text, not required): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **job_application_2** (text, not required): Enter a brief job application.
* **job_description** (note, not required): Enter a job description, if necessary.
* **job_status** (select_one, required): Select the correct job status (Open, Closed, Pending).
* **submitted_by** (select_one, required): Choose the person who submitted you for the job (HR, Manager, Recruiter, Team Member).
* **submitted_date** (date, required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **submitted_time** (time, not required): Enter the time you submitted your application.
* **submitted_by_2** (text, required): Re-enter your name for verification purposes.
