# Recruitment Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recruitment Checklist Form is used by recruiters and hiring managers to efficiently track and record key information for job openings. This form helps ensure that all essential details are captured during the recruitment process, streamlining communication and decision-making. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the job description and requirements for the vacant position.
2. Specify the job category and type to help in filtering and categorization.
3. Enter the job status to keep track of the current stage of the recruitment process.
4. Schedule interviews and select the relevant interviewers.
5. Make decisions on offering or rejecting the job.
6. Add any relevant notes for further reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Job Description** (Job Description, text, required/optional)
Enter a brief description of the job, including key responsibilities and requirements.

### 2. **Job Requirements** (Job Requirements, text, required/optional)
Specify the essential skills, qualifications, and experience needed for the job.

### 3. **Job Category** (Job Category, text, required/optional)
Select the category that best fits the job type, e.g., software development, marketing, etc.

### 4. **Job Type** (Job Type, text, required/optional)
Choose the job type from available options, e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, etc.

### 5. **Job Status** (Job Status, text, required/optional)
Select the current status of the job opening, e.g., open for applications, closed, etc.

### 6. **Interview Schedule** (Interview Schedule, date, required/optional)
Select the interview schedule date and time from the calendar.

### 7. **Interviewers** (Interviewers, select multiple, required/optional)
Choose the interviewers assigned to each interview session.

### 8. **Job Offer** (Job Offer, select one, required/optional)
Select whether a job offer has been made, yes or no.

### 9. **Job Rejection** (Job Rejection, select one, required/optional)
Select whether the job offer was rejected, yes or no.

### 10. **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional)
Add any additional information or comments related to the job.

This form helps recruiters and hiring managers keep track of the key aspects of the job opening, ensuring a smooth and efficient recruitment process.
