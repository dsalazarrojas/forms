## <thinking> This form is designed to capture interview notes from an interviewer or recruiter, providing a structured way to record the performance, experience, and suitability of candidates for a position. The form helps in maintaining a consistent and objective evaluation process. It can be used for both internal and external candidate interviews. The form is meant for internal use within an organization and is not intended to be used as a standalone assessment tool. The following guide will help you fill out the form correctly and effectively.

## Interview Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Notes Form is a tool used to gather information and impressions from interviewers to evaluate the performance and suitability of candidates for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form for each interview, using the provided fields to document your observations and notes.
- Use this form to evaluate the candidate's overall experience, strengths, and areas for improvement.
- The form can be used for both internal and external candidate interviews.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Interviewer Name (1)**: Type the name of the person conducting the interview.
* **Interviewer Notes (2)**: Write down any observations, comments, or notes you made during the interview.
* **Job Title (3)**: Record the job title of the position being interviewed for.
* **Candidate Name (4)**: Type the name of the candidate being interviewed.
* **Date (5)** and **Date (12)**: Record the date of the interview, using the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Interview Duration (6)**: Note the duration of the interview.
* **Overall Experience (7)**: Evaluate the candidate's overall experience in the field, using the provided options (Very Positive, Somewhat Positive, Neutral, Somewhat Negative, Very Negative).
* **Willingness (8)**: Rate the candidate's willingness to be recommended for the position, using the provided options (Very Positive, Somewhat Positive, Neutral, Somewhat Negative, Very Negative).
* **Willingness Reason (9)**: Provide a brief reason for your willingness rating.
* **Recommendation (10)**: Choose whether to Highly Recommend, Recommend, or Not Recommend the candidate for the position.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the candidate.
* **Date (12)**: Again, record the date of the interview, using the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
