# online_internship_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online internship application form is a tool used to gather information from potential candidates who are interested in internships within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the online internship application form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Applicant Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. Describe your education and work experience in the Education and Work Experience section.
4. List your relevant skills and certifications in the Skills and Certifications section.
5. Attach your resume in the Resume Upload section.
6. Select your skills from the Skills section.
7. Choose your availability in the Availability section.
8. Select your job preferences from the Job Preferences section.
9. Add any Additional Comments you have regarding your application.
10. Add any References in the References section.
11. Click the Submit button to complete and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (text, required: false)
	+ This section is for your personal details, including your name and other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (text, required: false)
	+ Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Education and Work Experience** (text, required: false)
	+ Describe your education and work experience, including relevant degrees, diplomas, and work history.
* **Skills and Certifications** (text, required: false)
	+ List your relevant skills and certifications that would be beneficial for the internship.
* **Resume Upload** (text, required: false)
	+ Attach a resume that showcases your experience and qualifications.
* **Skills** (select multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose your relevant skills from the list.
* **Availability** (date, required: false)
	+ Select your availability for the internship, including dates and times you are available.
* **Job Preferences** (select one, required: false)
	+ Choose your preferred job role or department within our organization.
* **Additional Comments** (note, required: false)
	+ Add any comments or questions you have regarding the internship opportunity.
* **References** (text, required: false)
	+ List any professional references who can vouch for your experience and qualifications.
* **Submit** (text, required: false)
	+ Click this button to complete and submit your application.
