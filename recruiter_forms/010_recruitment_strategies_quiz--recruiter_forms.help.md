# Recruitment Strategies Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your recruitment strategies and gain insights into your processes. It will ask you about your approach to new hire onboarding, training, and feedback. This information will help you identify areas for improvement and develop more effective recruitment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the best answer that applies to your recruitment process.
2. Answer the questions based on your current practices, not ideal or hypothetical scenarios.
3. If you're unsure about a question, select "Other" or describe your process in the "Other" field.
4. Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your current recruitment strategies.
