# Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exit Interview Form is designed to gather important information about an employee's reasons for leaving the organization. This form will help the organization understand the reasons behind the departure and identify areas for improvement. It will also help employees provide their feedback and comments on their job and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, simply type in your answers to each question. You can type as much or as little as you need to provide your feedback.
2.  Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3.  If you have any additional comments or information that you want to share, use the "comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any general information about yourself, such as your job title, department, or length of service.
*   **Reason for Departing** (`reason_for_departing`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to explain the reason why you are leaving the organization. Please be honest and specific about your reason.
*   **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, text, required/optional): In this field, you can share any concerns or feedback you have about your job performance during your time at the organization.
*   **Benefits and Termination** (`benefits_and_termination`, text, required/optional): Here, you can discuss any benefits or severance package you are entitled to or have received as part of your departure.
*   **Communication and Resources** (`communication_and_resources`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to share any insights or suggestions on how the organization can improve communication and resource allocation.
*   **Job Finding Process** (`job_finding_process`, text, required/optional): If applicable, describe your experience with the job finding process after leaving the organization.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Share your overall feedback and suggestions for improving the organization and work environment.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Finally, this is an open field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience working with the organization.
