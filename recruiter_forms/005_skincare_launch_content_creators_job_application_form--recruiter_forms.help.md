# Skincare Launch Content Creators Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for applicants to apply for the Skincare Launch Content Creators job opening. It collects essential information from potential candidates to help us understand their background, qualifications, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the format of `example@email.com` in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number without any special characters in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your relevant experience level in the "Experience" field.
6. Select one option from the "Select One" dropdown list.
7. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple 1" dropdown list.
8. Provide any additional information or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address in the format of `example@email.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number without any special characters.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required: false): Select your relevant experience level, which is a number indicating the amount of experience you have.
* **Select One** (`select_1`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Select Multiple 1** (`select_multiple_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options from the list:
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
