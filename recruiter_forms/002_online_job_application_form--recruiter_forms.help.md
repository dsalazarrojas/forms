# online_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online job application form is a tool used to collect information from individuals interested in potential hiring opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. If you are unable to attach a physical resume, please provide a brief description of your experience and qualifications.
3. Ensure that your contact information and job title are up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Info (Required)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): This is the field where you will enter your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.

### 2. Resume (Optional)
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, optional): If you have a physical resume, you can attach it here. If not, please provide a brief description of your experience and qualifications.

### 3. Job Title (Required)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required): Select the job title that best describes the position you are applying for. Please choose one of the options provided.

### 4. Job Description (Required)
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for, including relevant details about the role.

### 5. Contact Number (Required)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you for further discussion.

### 6. Email Address (Required)
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you electronically.

### 7. Education (Required)
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you have completed a degree or certification relevant to the job you are applying for. Please select all relevant options.

### 8. Skills (Optional)
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, optional): List any relevant skills you have that align with the job requirements.

### 9. Work Experience (Optional)
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your relevant work experience.

### 10. References (Optional)
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any relevant professional references.
