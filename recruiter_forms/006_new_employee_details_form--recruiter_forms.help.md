# new_employee_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_employee_details_form is a crucial tool for HR or administrative purposes to collect and track employee information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the new employee.
2. Enter the first name of the employee in the First Name field.
3. Enter the last name of the employee in the Last Name field.
4. Enter the employee's email address in the Email field.
5. Select the employee's position from the available options (Manager, Team Member, or Contractor).
6. Check one or more job status (New Hire, Onboarding, or Terminated).
7. Enter the employee's start date in the start_date field.
8. Enter the employee's end date in the end_date field (if applicable).
9. Enter the number of work hours the employee will be working (if applicable).
10. Enter the manager's name in the manager_name field.
11. Enter the manager's title in the manager_title field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first name of the employee.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name of the employee.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the employee's email address in the standard format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Position** (`position`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the employee's position (Manager, Team Member, or Contractor).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check one or more job status (New Hire, Onboarding, or Terminated).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the employee's start date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the employee's end date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (if applicable).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of work hours the employee will be working (if applicable).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the employee's manager.
