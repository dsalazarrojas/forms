# dream_job_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dream job survey form is a tool designed to gather information about your career aspirations, job status, and satisfaction. This data is used by our recruiters to better understand your preferences and provide more suitable job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each step on this page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your current job status** (`whats_your_current_job_status`, select_one, optional): Select from the options: Active or Inactive to indicate your current job status.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, optional): Enter a number to rate your satisfaction level with your current job. The higher the number, the more satisfied you are.
* **Job Satisfaction Level** (`job_satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from High, Medium, or Low to rate your job satisfaction level. You can select one or multiple options.
* **What makes your eyes light up** (`dream_job_survey_question1`, text, optional): Enter a short description of what makes you excited or motivated about your career or job.
* **What are your long-term goals** (`dream_job_survey_question2`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your long-term career aspirations or goals.
