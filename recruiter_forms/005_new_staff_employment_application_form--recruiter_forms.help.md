# New Staff Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Staff Employment Application Form is designed to facilitate the hiring process for businesses by collecting relevant job posting information.

## How To Complete This Form

*   This form is divided into several sections. Each section should be filled out completely for the form to be considered valid.
*   The form has multiple pages. You will need to fill out the form one page at a time.
*   Use the provided hints and guidelines for each section to ensure you provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Staff Employment Form** (`new_staff_employment_application_form`): A brief description of the job posting.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter a short summary of the job posting.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`): A title of the job posting.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter a brief and accurate title of the job posting.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`): The location of the job.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter the exact location of the job posting.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`): A description of the job posting.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter a few words describing the job.
*   **Employment Type** (`employment_type`): The type of employment for this job posting.
	+ Type: Select one option. Select from the options: Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
*   **Job Status** (`job_status`): The current status of the job posting.
	+ Type: Select one option. Select from the options: Open, Closed, or Filled.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Name of the contact person.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Email of the contact person.
	+ Type: Email field. Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Phone number of the contact person.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`): The type of job posting.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter a type of the job posting.
*   **Job Category** (`job_category`): The category of the job posting.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter a category of the job posting.
*   **Salary** (`salary`): The salary for this job posting.
	+ Type: Number field. Enter the salary for this job posting.
*   **Benefits** (`benefits`): Are benefits offered?
	+ Type: Select multiple options. Select if yes, and choose from the options: True or False.
*   **Benefits Details** (`benefits_details`): If yes, provide specific benefits.
	+ Type: Text field. Enter specific benefits offered.
