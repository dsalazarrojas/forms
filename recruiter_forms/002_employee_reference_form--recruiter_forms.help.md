# Employee Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect contact information and employment details from an employee's reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's contact details, including their title, first name, last name, and email address.
2. Specify the company they work at.
3. Provide information about their job title and how long they have been at the company.
4. Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_reference**: (employee_reference, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the employee's reference.
* **contact_details**: (contact_details, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's contact information, including their title, first name, and last name.
* **contact_title**: (contact_title, select_one, required/optional): Select the title of the employee's contact person.
* **contact_first_name**: (contact_first_name, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the employee's contact person.
* **contact_last_name**: (contact_last_name, select_one, required/optional): Select the last name of the employee's contact person.
* **contact_company**: (contact_company, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company where the employee works.
* **reference_title**: (reference_title, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the reference.
* **reference_first_name**: (reference_first_name, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the reference.
* **reference_last_name**: (reference_last_name, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the reference.
* **work_at**: (work_at, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company where the employee works.
* **years_at**: (years_at, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years the employee has been at the company.
* **position**: (position, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **report_to**: (report_to, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person the employee reports to.
* **contact_email**: (contact_email, email, required/optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **contact_phone**: (contact_phone, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **contact_mobile**: (contact_mobile, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's mobile number.
* **contact_extension**: (contact_extension, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **contact_job_title**: (contact_job_title, select_one, required/optional): Select the job title of the employee's contact person.
* **contact_job_start**: (contact_job_start, date, required/optional): Enter the date the employee started their job.
* **contact_job_end**: (contact_job_end, date, required/optional): Enter the date the employee's job ended.
* **contact_company**: (contact_company, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company where the employee works.
* **how_long_at**: (how_long_at, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years the employee has been at the company.
* **last_evaluated_by**: (last_evaluated_by, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who last evaluated the employee.
* **contact_note**: (contact_note, note, required/optional): Enter any additional contact notes.
* **confirm**: (confirm, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

Please note that some fields are required, so be sure to fill them out correctly to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
