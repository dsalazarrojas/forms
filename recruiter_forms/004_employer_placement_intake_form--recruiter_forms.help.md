# Employer Placement Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employers who are seeking to place candidates for available job openings. The information collected will help us better understand the job details and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the company that is posting the job.
2. Enter the job title of the position that is available.
3. Briefly describe the job in a few sentences.
4. Select the type of job this is (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract).
5. If the job type is not among the options, please enter the type in the text field.
6. Enter the URL where the job was posted.
7. Select the date the job was posted.
8. Select the time the job was posted.
9. Enter the name of your company.
10. Enter the contact person's name.
11. Enter the contact person's email address.
12. Enter the contact person's phone number.
13. Enter the contact person's phone extension (if applicable).
14. Enter the address line 1 of your company.
15. Enter the address line 2 of your company.
16. Enter the city of your company.
17. Enter the state of your company.
18. Enter the zip code of your company.
19. If you have a job ID, enter it. If not, enter the job ID in the text field.
20. If you have a job ID other than the one you entered above, enter it in the text field.
21. Select the status of the job (Open, Closed, or Pending).
22. If the job status is not among the options, enter it in the text field.
23. Select the source of the job posting (Social Media, Job Board, Website, or Referral).
24. If the job posting source is not among the options, enter it in the text field.
25. Select the reason for submitting the job (New Job, Job Update, Replacing Old Job, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your company.
- **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job position you are trying to fill.
- **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job in a few sentences.
- **Job Type**: Select the type of job this is (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract). If the job type is not among the options, enter it in the text field.
- **Job Type Other**: If you selected "Other" as the job type, enter the type of job in this field.
- **Job Posting URL**: Enter the URL where the job was posted.
- **Job Posting Date**: Select the date the job was posted.
- **Job Posting Time**: Select the time the job was posted.
- **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
- **Contact Phone Extension**: Enter the contact person's phone extension (if applicable).
- **Address Line 1**: Enter the address line 1 of your company.
- **Address Line 2**: Enter the address line 2 of your company.
- **City**: Enter the city of your company.
- **State**: Enter the state of your company.
- **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your company.
- **Job ID**: If you have a job ID, enter it. If not, enter the job ID in the text field.
- **Job ID Other**: If you have a job ID other than the one you entered above, enter it in this field.
- **Job Status**: Select the status of the job (Open, Closed, or Pending). If the job status is not among the options, enter it in the text field.
- **Job Status Other**: If you selected "Other" as the job status, enter the status of the job in this field.
- **Job Posting Source**: Select the source of the job posting (Social Media, Job Board, Website, or Referral). If the job posting source is not among the options, enter it in the text field.
- **Job Posting Source Other**: If you selected "Other" as the job posting source, enter the source of the job in this field.
- **Submission Reason**: Select the reason for submitting the job (New Job, Job Update, Replacing Old Job, or Other).
