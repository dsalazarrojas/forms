# Pre Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, called "Pre Screening Form," is used to gather preliminary information from job applicants before the screening or interviewing process. It aims to collect relevant details about the applicant's general information, job-specific details, and contact information. The form serves as a starting point for further evaluation and consideration in the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **General Info** in the "General Info" section. This includes any relevant details about yourself that are not specified below.
2.  Fill in your **Job Applicant Info**, which includes your name and other identifying details.
3.  Enter your **Contact Details**, which include your email address and phone number.
4.  Describe your job **Job Description** and how you believe you fit the position.
5.  Share your **Education Background** to demonstrate your relevant experience and qualifications.
6.  Provide a brief summary of your **Work Experience** to demonstrate your skills and expertise.
7.  List your **Skills and Certifications** that match the job requirements.
8.  If applicable, **Upload Documents** such as your resume, references, or other supporting materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info
**General Info** (text, required/optional): This is a free-text field for you to provide any relevant general information about yourself. This could include personal background, hobbies, or interests, but it's not required for this field.

### Job Applicant Info
**Job Applicant Info** (text, required): Enter your name, address, and other identifying details here.

### Contact Details
**Contact Details** (text, required): Please enter your email address and phone number.

### Job Description
**Job Description** (text, required): Describe the job you are applying for and how you believe you fit the position.

### Education Background
**Education Background** (text, optional): Share your educational background, including degrees, institutions, and relevant courses.

### Work Experience
**Work Experience** (text, optional): Briefly describe your past work experience and how it relates to the position.

### Skills and Certifications
**Skills and Certifications** (text, optional): List your relevant skills and certifications that match the job requirements.

### Upload Documents
**Upload Documents** (text, optional): Upload any supporting documents such as a resume, references, or other relevant materials.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
*   Be concise and clear in your responses, and provide as much detail as possible.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or team for assistance.
