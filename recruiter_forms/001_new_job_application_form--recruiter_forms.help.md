# New Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Job Application Form is a tool for internal use by the organization to collect and process job applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Details section with your personal and contact information.
2. Enter your work experience in reverse chronological order, starting from the most recent first.
3. Provide information about your education background.
4. Add any relevant skills or qualifications you have.
5. Upload any relevant documents, such as your resume or references.
6. Review and confirm the submission details.
7. Finally, confirm your submission by clicking the "Submit Application" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number in this section.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Work Experience**: Enter your previous work experience in reverse chronological order, starting from the most recent first.
* **Education Background**: Enter all relevant education information.
* **Skills Section**: Enter any relevant skills or qualifications you have.
* **References**: Enter any relevant references or contacts.
* **Submission**: Upload your resume and any other relevant documents.
* **Confirm Submission**: Review and confirm the submission details.
* **Confirm Email Address**: Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone Number**: Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Submit**: Confirm the submission.
* **Confirm Job Title**: Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Confirm Job Type**: Confirm the type of job you are applying for.
* **Confirm Job Status**: Confirm the status of your job application.
* **Confirm Job Location**: Enter the location where you will work.
* **Confirm Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Confirm Responsibilities**: Enter the responsibilities of the job.
* **Confirm Required Skills**: Enter any required skills for the job.
* **Confirm Required Qualifications**: Enter any required qualifications for the job.
* **Confirm Certifications**: Enter any certifications you have.
* **Confirm Languages**: Enter any languages you are proficient in.
* **Confirm Holidays**: Confirm the holidays or days off you require.
* **Confirm Shifts**: Confirm the shifts or schedule you are available for.
* **Confirm Wages**: Confirm your expected wages.
* **Confirm Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about your application.
* **Confirm Confirmation**: Enter a confirmation message or note.
* **Confirm Rejection**: Enter a rejection message or note.
* **Confirm Confirmation Message**: Enter a confirmation message or note about your application status.
* **Confirm Application Status**: Confirm the status of your application.
* **Confirm Application Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about your application status.
