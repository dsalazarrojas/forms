# recruiting_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recruiting_intake_form is designed to collect essential information about job openings and candidates for the hiring process. It helps the recruitment team to gather accurate and consistent data for making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields with the candidate's personal details.
2. Enter the Email address of the candidate or the contact person.
3. Specify the Job Title of the position opening.
4. Select the Job Type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract) that matches the job requirements.
5. Provide a brief Job Description of the position.
6. Enter the Years of Experience required for the position.
7. List the Job Requirements of the position.
8. Outline the Ideal Candidate Profile.
9. Specify the Available Work Schedule.
10. Enter any additional Recruiter Comments.
11. If applicable, select the Job Start and Job End Dates.
12. Choose the Job Status (In Progress, Active, or Completed).
13. If necessary, assign an existing Recruiter to the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the candidate's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the candidate's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the candidate or contact person.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the position.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Choose the job type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract) that matches the job requirements.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the position.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the minimum years of experience required for the position.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required): List the key responsibilities and expectations of the role.
* **Ideal Candidate Profile** (`ideal_candidate_profile`, text, required): Outline the characteristics and qualifications of the ideal candidate.
* **Available Work Schedule** (`available_work_schedule`, time, required): Specify the days and time frames when the candidate is available to work.
* **Recruiter Comments** (`recruiter_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes from the recruiter.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the job opening.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the job opening.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of the job (In Progress, Active, or Completed).
* **Assigned Recruiter** (`assigned_recruiter`, text, required): If necessary, assign an existing recruiter to the job.
