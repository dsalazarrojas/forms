# Carrier Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carrier Profile Form is a crucial part of the onboarding process for carrier companies. It collects essential information about the carrier's operational details, contact information, and certifications. This information enables us to properly classify and onboard the carrier company, ensuring a seamless integration with our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by ensuring you have all the necessary information about the carrier company, including its legal name, type, and establishment date.
2.  Fill in the **Carrier Company Name**, selecting the correct type of carrier company and year established.
3.  Provide the **Primary Contact Name**, **Contact Title**, and **Primary Phone Number**, along with the **Primary Email Address**.
4.  List the **Types of Vehicles Operated** (e.g., 18-Wheeler, Box Truck, etc.).
5.  Enter the **Number of Vehicles in Fleet**.
6.  Describe the **Geographic Service Areas** (regions or states served).
7.  Select the **Types of Cargo Transported** (e.g., General Merchandise, Hazardous Materials, etc.).
8.  If applicable, provide the **Federal Operating Authority Number** and **Motor Carrier (MC) Number**.
9.  Specify the **General Liability Insurance Limit** and confirm whether the carrier is **HazMat Certified**.
10. Finally, provide any **Additional Information or Special Capabilities** about the carrier company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Carrier Company Name**: Enter the legal business name of the carrier company.
*   **Type of Carrier Company**: Select the type of carrier company (e.g., Trucking Company, Freight Broker, etc.).
*   **Year Company Established**: Enter the year the carrier company was established.
*   **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the primary contact person's name for the carrier company.
*   **Contact Title**: Enter the primary contact person's title.
*   **Primary Phone Number**: Enter the primary contact person's phone number.
*   **Primary Email Address**: Enter the primary contact person's email address.
*   **Types of Vehicles Operated**: Select all the types of vehicles operated by the carrier company.
*   **Number of Vehicles in Fleet**: Enter the number of vehicles in the carrier company's fleet.
*   **Geographic Service Areas**: Describe the regions or states served by the carrier company.
*   **Types of Cargo Transported**: Select all the types of cargo transported by the carrier company.
*   **Federal Operating Authority Number**: If applicable, enter the Federal Operating Authority Number.
*   **Motor Carrier (MC) Number**: If applicable, enter the Motor Carrier (MC) Number.
*   **General Liability Insurance Limit**: Select the general liability insurance limit.
*   **HazMat Certified**: Confirm whether the carrier is HazMat certified.
*   **Additional Information or Special Capabilities**: Provide any additional information or special capabilities about the carrier company.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank or indicate "Not Applicable" to avoid any errors.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for clarification.
