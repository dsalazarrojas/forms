# Sales Employment Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential sales candidates to assess their suitability for a sales role. It is used to collect relevant data about the candidate's experience, skills, and preferences to determine their fit for a sales position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form carefully and accurately, providing truthful information about your experience, skills, and preferences.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, as they are designed to gather specific information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Role**: Select the sales role that best describes your current or desired role in a sales environment. Options are: Sales Representative, Account Manager, Business Development Officer, and others.
* **Years of Experience**: Provide the number of years of experience you have in sales or a related field. This will help us understand your level of expertise and maturity in the field.
* **Education**: Select all relevant education credentials that you have achieved, such as Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD degrees.
* **Job Satisfaction**: This field is optional and not required. If you have any comments or concerns about job satisfaction, please feel free to express them.
* **Availability**: Provide the date you are available to start work. This will help us understand your immediate availability and schedule.
* **Work Experience**: Select all relevant work experience you have in sales or related fields, such as Marketing, Sales, Engineering, and others.
* **Skills**: Select all relevant skills you possess, such as Communication, Teamwork, Problem-Solving, and others.
* **References**: Select all relevant references that can vouch for your work experience and skills, such as Personal, Professional, or Social media connections.
* **Salary Requirements**: Provide your expected salary range or requirements.
* **Benefits**: Select all relevant benefits you expect or require, such as Health, Vision, or Dental.
* **Work Arrangement**: Select the type of work arrangement you are looking for, such as Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
* **Work Place**: Select the type of work environment you prefer, such as Home office, On-site, or Hybrid.
* **Notice Period**: Provide the length of notice period you expect or are willing to give, in weeks or months.
* **Notice Period Duration**: Provide the duration of the notice period you expect or are willing to give, in weeks or months.
* **Termination Notice Period**: Provide the length of notice period for termination, in weeks or months.
* **Termination Notice Period Unit**: Select the unit of time for termination notice period, such as Weeks, Months, or Years.
* **Termination Notice Period Unit Short**: Select the abbreviated unit of time for termination notice period, such as s, months, or years.
* **Termination Notice Period Unit Short Suffix**: Select the suffix for termination notice period unit, such as s or Other.
* **Job Title**: Provide your expected or current job title.
* **Reporting Manager**: Select how you report to a manager, such as Directly or Indirectly.
* **Supervisor**: Indicate whether you have a supervisor or not.
* **Team Management**: Indicate whether you are able to manage a team or not.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience, skills, or preferences.
