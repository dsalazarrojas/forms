# talent_acquisition_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Acquisition Report Form is designed to gather information about the candidate's journey through the recruitment and hiring process. This form is used to document and track the progress of potential candidates, from initial contact to final hiring decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand its purpose and the information it collects.
2. Scroll through each page to access the different sections of the form.
3. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`Candidate Information`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing general information about the candidate. Please enter the candidate's name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
* **Recruitment Process** (`Recruitment Process`, text, required/optional): Describe the steps taken to recruit the candidate, including any advertising, initial screening, or other relevant processes.
* **Hiring Process** (`Hiring Process`, text, required/optional): Outline the steps taken to hire the candidate, including interviews, assessments, or other evaluation processes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If a section is not applicable, mark it as "N/A" or "Not Applicable".
* If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
