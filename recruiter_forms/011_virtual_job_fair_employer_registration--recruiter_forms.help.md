# Virtual Job Fair Employer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from employers about their virtual job fair event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information about your event.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the name of the event.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the job title of the position being offered.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the name of the company offering the job.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter a brief description of the job being offered.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, false): 
Select the type of job being offered from the available options.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the company's website URL.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the requirements for the job.
* **Number of Openings** (`number_of_openings`, number, false): 
This field is used to enter the number of open positions available for the job.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, false): 
This field is used to enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, false): 
This field is used to enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the location of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter the duration of the event.
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter a URL for the job listing.
* **Employer ID** (`employer_id`, text, false): 
This field is used to enter a unique identifier for the employer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, false): 
This field is used to enter any additional notes about the job or event.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the virtual job fair team.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* This form will only be reviewed once submitted.
* If you have already registered for a virtual job fair, you will not be able to register again.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the virtual job fair team for assistance.
