# Job Search Priority Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Search Priority Survey is a tool to help job seekers gather and share information about their job search priorities and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the job type that best describes your current job search (Page 1: General Information).
2. Choose the skills you possess that are your greatest strengths (Page 2: Skills).
3. Select your level of experience (Page 3: Experience).
4. Choose your educational background (Page 4: Education).
5. Select your work history (Page 5: Work History).
6. Choose the type of job you are looking for (Page 6: Job Preferences).
7. Select the skills you are looking for in a job (Page 7: Skills Required).
8. Select the salary range you are looking for (Page 8: Salary Range).
9. Choose the location where you would like to work (Page 9: Job Location).
10. Review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Page 1)
	+ This page is the introduction to the form and asks for basic contact information and job search status.
* **Job Type** (Page 2)
	+ Please select the job type that best describes your current job search: Full Time, Part Time, or Contract.
* **What Are Your Greatest Strengths?** (Page 3)
	+ Select the skills you possess that you consider your greatest strengths: Leadership, Communication, or Problem Solving.
* **What Experience Do You Have?** (Page 4)
	+ Choose your level of experience: 0-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **What's Your Educational Background?** (Page 5)
	+ Select your highest level of education: High School, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree, or Master's Degree.
* **What's Your Work History?** (Page 6)
	+ Select your work history: 0-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **What Kind of Job Are You Looking For?** (Page 7)
	+ Choose the type of job you are looking for: Full Time, Part Time, or Contract.
* **What Skills Are You Looking For?** (Page 8)
	+ Select the skills you are looking for in a job: Communication, Leadership, or Problem Solving.
* **What Salary Range Are You Looking For?** (Page 9)
	+ Choose the salary range you are looking for: 0-20k, 20-40k, 40-60k, 60-80k, or More than 80k.
* **What Location Are You Looking For?** (Page 10)
	+ Select the location where you would like to work: Remote, In Person, or Hybrid.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately to ensure that your job search priorities are reflected.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately represent your preferences.
* Use this form to tailor your job search to your specific needs and interests.
* This form is for personal use and should not be used to collect sensitive or personal data without the user's consent.
