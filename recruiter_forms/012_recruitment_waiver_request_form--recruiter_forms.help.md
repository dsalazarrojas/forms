# Recruitment Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Recruitment Waiver Request Form is used for handling exceptions and waivers in the recruitment process. This form helps gather necessary information for making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your title and name in the first two fields. These are your personal details for the waiver request.
2. Enter your email and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
3. If you have any exceptional circumstances or need to provide additional details, use the "Exception details" field.
4. If you need to support your waiver request, use the "Supporting details" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the decision made for your waiver request.
6. Indicate whether you have signed a waiver or not and when it was signed.
7. Confirm if the waiver has been accepted or not.
8. Enter the date and time when the waiver was accepted (if applicable).
9. Provide details about who accepted the waiver.
10. Add any additional comments or information in the "Waiver comments" field.
11. Use the "Recruiter comments" field for comments from the recruiter.
12. Enter any additional comments for the decision made.
13. Add any comments for the exception reasons.
14. If necessary, upload any supporting documents or signed documents.
15. Attach the waiver form to the request (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your title for the waiver request.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for the waiver request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Exception details** (`exception_details`, `note`, optional): If you have exceptional circumstances, use this field to provide details.
* **Supporting details** (`supporting_details`, `text`, optional): If you need to support your waiver request, use this field.
* **Decision date** (`decision_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the decision made for your waiver request.
* **Decision time** (`decision_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the decision made for your waiver request.
* **Waiver signed date** (`waiver_signed_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you signed the waiver (if applicable).
* **Waiver signed time** (`waiver_signed_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when you signed the waiver (if applicable).
* **Waiver accepted** (`waiver_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the waiver has been accepted or not.
* **Waiver accepted date** (`waiver_accepted_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the waiver was accepted (if applicable).
* **Waiver accepted time** (`waiver_accepted_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when the waiver was accepted (if applicable).
* **Waiver accepted by** (`waiver_accepted_by`, `text`, optional): Provide details about who accepted the waiver.
* **Waiver comments** (`waiver_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information for the waiver request.
* **Recruiter comments** (`recruiter_comments`, `note`, optional): Use this field for comments from the recruiter.
* **Decision comments** (`decision_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments for the decision made.
* **Exception comments** (`exception_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any comments for the exception reasons.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Upload any supporting documents or papers.
* **Waiver signed documents** (`waiver_signed_documents`, `text`, optional): Upload the signed waiver documents (if applicable).
* **Waiver form attached** (`waiver_form_attached`, `text`, optional): Attach the waiver form to the request (if applicable).

## Tips

* Always review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your recruiter or contact person for guidance.
