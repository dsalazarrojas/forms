# Employee Exit Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Exit Interview Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather feedback from departing employees to help us understand their reasons for leaving and their thoughts on company policies, culture, and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each section:

1. **Introduction**: Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you've read all the fields carefully.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, providing as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing.
3. Submit the form when you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reason_for_leaving**: This field is where you can explain your reason(s) for leaving the company. Please provide a brief explanation of your reason(s) for departure.
* **new_job_description**: Here, you can describe the job description of your new role, including any specific responsibilities and expectations.
* **new_company**: Please provide the name and/or website of your new company.
* **new_manager_description**: Describe your new manager's style and expectations, including any notable management philosophies or practices.
* **new_manager_rating**: Rate your new manager on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being Very High and 5 being Very Low, for the following attributes:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **new_work_life_balance**: Rate your experience with work-life balance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being Very High and 5 being Very Low, for the following:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **new_job_security**: Select the level of job security you experienced at your new company:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **new_job_satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your new job on a scale:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **new_culture**: If you have any specific thoughts on your new company's culture, please provide a brief description.
* **new_communication**: Describe your experiences with communication within your new company, including any notable successes or challenges.
* **new_morale**: If you have any thoughts on the overall morale at your new company, please provide a brief description.
* **new_recommendations**: If you have any recommendations for the company's future growth and development, please provide a brief description.
* **new_open_door**: If you have any other comments, suggestions, or concerns, please provide a brief description.

Note: The HR department will use this form to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions for future development and growth. Your input is invaluable in helping us create a better work environment for future employees.
