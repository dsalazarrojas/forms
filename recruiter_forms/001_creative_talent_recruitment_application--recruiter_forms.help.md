# Creative Talent Recruitment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your background, experience, and skills to evaluate your suitability for creative talent recruitment positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Describe your work experience, highlighting your achievements and responsibilities in each role.
3. Choose your level of education and any relevant skills you possess.
4. Provide your resume and/or portfolio link for us to review.
5. Fill out the contact information and any other relevant fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (Page 1):
	+ **Personal Details** (title: Personal Details)
		- **Personal Details**: This section is where you can enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
		- **Hint**: Please fill this section accurately to ensure we have the correct contact information.
* **Work Experience** (Page 2):
	+ **Work Experience**: Please describe your work experience, highlighting your achievements and responsibilities in each role.
		- **Hint**: Use bullet points or numbered lists to break up your work experience for easier reading.
* **Education** (Page 3):
	+ **Education**: Choose your level of education to help us evaluate your background.
		- **Hint**: Select the most relevant option that applies to you.
* **Skills** (Page 4):
	+ **Skills**: Choose the relevant skills you possess to enhance your application.
		- **Hint**: Only select skills that you are proficient in.
* **Resume/Portfolio** (Page 5):
	+ **Resume/Portfolio**: Attach a copy of your resume or provide a link to your online portfolio.
		- **Hint**: Make sure your resume or portfolio is up-to-date and accurately represents your experience and skills.
* **Contact Details** (Page 6):
	+ **Contact Details**: Enter your email, phone number, and address.
		- **Hint**: Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* **Other Fields** (Page 7-10):
	+ **Work Experience 2-6**: Repeat the **Work Experience** section for each additional work experience you have.
	+ **Resume Link**: Enter a link to your online resume or portfolio.
	+ **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address**: Repeat the **Contact Details** section for each additional contact method. 

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing information about your background, experience, and skills.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists to break up your text for easier reading.
* Attach a clear and up-to-date resume or provide a link to your online portfolio.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy. 

Note: The fields are not all compulsory, however, providing complete and accurate information will improve your chances of being contacted for further evaluation. 

Please take 10-15 minutes to complete this form, and we appreciate your honesty and accuracy in filling it out. 

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us. 

This form should take you no more than 10-15 minutes to complete.
