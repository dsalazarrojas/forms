# Virtual Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for candidates to register for virtual interviews with our company. It helps us to gather necessary information about you and your qualifications for the interview. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to increase your chances of getting selected for the interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, including your first and last name.
2. Enter a valid email address and phone number (if you have one).
3. Provide your job title and upload your resume (if applicable).
4. In the "Message" field, briefly introduce yourself and mention your availability for the interview.
5. Select the type of job you are interested in (e.g., software development, data science, etc.).
6. Choose your job level (junior, senior, lead, or manager).
7. Choose your job category (frontend, backend, fullstack, or devops).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have one. This is optional.
* **Job title**: Enter a brief description of your current or desired job title.
* **Resume upload**: Upload a copy of your resume, if applicable. This helps us to get a better understanding of your qualifications.
* **Message**: Briefly introduce yourself and mention your availability for the interview.
* **Job type**: Select the type of job you are interested in, such as software development or data science.
* **Job level**: Choose your job level, which indicates your experience level. Options include junior, senior, lead, or manager.
* **Job category**: Choose the category of job you are interested in, such as frontend, backend, or fullstack.
