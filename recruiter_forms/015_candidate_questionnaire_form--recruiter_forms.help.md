# Candidate Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates for the hiring process. It helps us understand your background, skills, and preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient recruitment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure the information provided is accurate and relevant.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as <required: true>.
3. Provide the requested information in the provided formats (e.g., date, number, text).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (`candidate_first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name, as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`candidate_last_name`, text, required): Enter your family name, as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.

### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.

### 5. Position of Interest
* **Position of Interest** (`position_interest`, text, required): Specify which role you're interested in applying for.

### 6. Current Employer
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your current company.

### 7. Current Job Title
* **Current Job Title** (`current_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your current position title.

### 8. Years of Relevant Experience
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years of experience you have in your field.

### 9. Highest Education Level
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Select the highest level of education you've achieved.

### 10. Relevant Certifications
* **Relevant Certifications** (`relevant_certifications`, text, optional): List any professional certifications you hold.

### 11. Key Responsibilities
* **Key Responsibilities in Current Role** (`key_responsibilities`, text, required): Describe the main duties you're currently performing.

### 12. Major Professional Achievements
* **Major Professional Achievements** (`major_achievements`, text, required): List your notable professional accomplishments.

### 13. Technical Skills
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, text, required): List the relevant technical skills you possess.

### 14. Software or Tools Proficiency
* **Software or Tools Proficiency** (`software_proficiency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the software or tools you're proficient in.

### 15. Languages Spoken
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, select_multiple, optional): Select the languages you can speak.

### 16. Do You Have Management Experience
* **Do You Have Management Experience** (`management_experience`, select_one, required): Specify if you have management experience.

### 17. Largest Team Size Managed
* **Largest Team Size Managed** (`team_size_managed`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of direct reports you've managed.

### 18. Work Arrangement Preference
* **Work Arrangement Preference** (`work_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred work arrangement.

### 19. Willingness to Travel
* **Willingness to Travel** (`travel_willingness`, select_one, required): Specify the maximum amount of travel you're willing to do.

### 20. Willing to Relocate
* **Willing to Relocate** (`relocation_willingness`, select_one, required): Specify if you're willing to move for this role.

### 21. Expected Salary Range
* **Expected Salary Range** (`salary_range`, text, optional): Enter your expected annual salary range.

### 22. Earliest Start Date
* **Earliest Start Date** (`start_date_availability`, select_one, required): Select the earliest date you can start.

### 23. Notice Period Required
* **Notice Period Required** (`notice_period`, select_one, required): Specify the notice period required from your current employer.

### 24. Why Are You Interested in This Position
* **Why Are You Interested in This Position** (`why_interested`, text, required): Explain why you're interested in this position.

### 25. What Are Your Career Goals
* **What Are Your Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required): Describe your professional objectives.

### 26. How Did You Hear About Us
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, select_one, required): Select the source of the job posting.

### 27. Referred By
* **Referred By** (`referred_by`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the employee who referred you.

### 28. Additional Information
* **Any Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details.

### 29. Questionnaire Date
* **Questionnaire Date** (`questionnaire_date`, date, required): Date and time completed.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully to avoid errors.
* Ensure you've answered all required fields (marked as <required: true>).
* For fields marked as optional, feel free to provide more details if you wish.
