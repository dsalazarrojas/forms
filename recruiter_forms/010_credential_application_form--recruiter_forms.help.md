# credential_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "credential_application_form" form is used to collect basic contact and professional information from individuals applying for certain types of credentials or positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "first name" field with your first name.
2. Enter your "last name" in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your "email" address in the format of your_email@domain.com.
4. Enter your "phone" number in the format of 123-456-7890.
5. Choose a "birth_date" from the calendar or format it as MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Fill in your "job_title" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (Text): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **last name** (Text): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address, following the standard format your_email@domain.com.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **birth_date** (Date): Choose your date of birth from the calendar, or enter it as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **job_title** (Text): If required, enter your job title, e.g., Software Engineer, Marketing Manager, etc.
