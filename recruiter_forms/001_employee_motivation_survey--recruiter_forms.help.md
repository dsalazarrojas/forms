# Employee Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us understand the current level of motivation among our internal employees. Your feedback will help us identify areas where we can make improvements to create a more positive and motivating work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please complete this form in a quiet and private setting, without any distractions or interruptions.
- Take your time to answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- You can submit the form at any time, and you don't need to complete it in one sitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your primary motivation for working for this company?** (question_1):
  A place to type in your main reason for working for our company.
* **Do you feel valued and appreciated by your manager?** (question_2):
  Please select how you feel about being valued and appreciated by your manager.
  - **Very satisfied**: You feel very valued and appreciated by your manager.
  - **Somewhat satisfied**: You feel somewhat valued and appreciated by your manager.
  - **Not satisfied**: You do not feel valued and appreciated by your manager.
* **Do you feel empowered to take ownership of your work?** (question_3):
  Please select how you feel about taking ownership of your work.
  - **Strongly agree**: You strongly feel empowered to take ownership of your work.
  - **Somewhat agree**: You somewhat feel empowered to take ownership of your work.
  - **Neither agree nor disagree**: You neither agree nor disagree with taking ownership of your work.
  - **Somewhat disagree**: You somewhat disagree with taking ownership of your work.
  - **Strongly disagree**: You strongly disagree with taking ownership of your work.
* **Do you feel motivated to come to work on time?** (question_4):
  Please select if you feel motivated to come to work on time.
  - **True**: You are motivated to come to work on time.
  - **False**: You are not motivated to come to work on time.
* **Do you feel motivated to work extra hours when needed?** (question_5):
  Please select if you feel motivated to work extra hours when needed.
  - **True**: You are motivated to work extra hours when needed.
  - **False**: You are not motivated to work extra hours when needed.
* **Do you feel motivated to meet new challenges?** (question_6):
  Please select if you feel motivated to meet new challenges.
  - **True**: You are motivated to meet new challenges.
  - **False**: You are not motivated to meet new challenges.
* **Do you feel motivated to take initiative on projects?** (question_7):
  Please select if you feel motivated to take initiative on projects.
  - **True**: You are motivated to take initiative on projects.
  - **False**: You are not motivated to take initiative on projects.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how motivated do you feel?** (question_8):
  Please select a number between 1 and 5 to express your level of motivation.
  - **1**: Not motivated at all.
  - **2**: A little motivated.
  - **3**: Moderately motivated.
  - **4**: Very motivated.
  - **5**: Extremely motivated.
* **Do you enjoy coming to work?** (question_9):
  A place to type in how you feel about coming to work.
