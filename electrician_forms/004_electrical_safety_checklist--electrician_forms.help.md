# Electrical Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Safety Checklist is a comprehensive tool designed to help users identify and address potential electrical hazards in a workplace or setting. It consists of five sections: General Safety, Hazards, Safety Equipment, Safety Procedures, and Recordkeeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Electrical Safety Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and select the options that apply to your specific situation.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. Record your answers in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Section 1 - General Safety**
	+ **Electrical Equipment** (Electrical Equipment, Select Multiple): This option refers to any electrical equipment, such as generators, motors, or appliances, that are used in the workplace or setting.
	+ **Electrical Circuits** (Electrical Circuits, Select Multiple): This option refers to the pathways through which electrical current flows, including power lines, wiring, and other electrical connections.
	+ **Electrical Wires** (Electrical Wires, Select Multiple): This option refers to the insulated cables or wires used to carry electrical current.
* **Section 2 - Hazards**
	+ **Electrical Shock Hazard** (Electrical Shock Hazard, Select Multiple): This option refers to any situation where electrical shock or electrocution can occur, such as wet or slippery surfaces.
	+ **Electrical Fire Hazard** (Electrical Fire Hazard, Select Multiple): This option refers to any situation where an electrical fire can occur, such as overloaded or overheating electrical equipment.
	+ **Electrical Fall Hazard** (Electrical Fall Hazard, Select Multiple): This option refers to any situation where falls can occur due to electrical equipment or wiring.
* **Section 3 - Safety Equipment**
	+ **Safety Switches** (Safety Switches, Select Multiple): This option refers to any safety switches or devices used to prevent electrical shock or other hazards.
	+ **Grounding Equipment** (Grounding Equipment, Select Multiple): This option refers to any equipment used to ground electrical systems to prevent electrical shock.
	+ **Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupters** (Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupters, Select Multiple): This option refers to any devices that can interrupt electrical current in the event of an arc fault.
* **Section 4 - Safety Procedures**
	+ **Lockout/Tagout Procedures** (Lockout/Tagout Procedures, Select Multiple): This option refers to any procedures used to lock out or tag out electrical systems to prevent accidental start-up or activation.
	+ **Electrical Safety Training** (Electrical Safety Training, Select Multiple): This option refers to any training provided to personnel on electrical safety procedures and best practices.
	+ **Electrical Safety Inspections** (Electrical Safety Inspections, Select Multiple): This option refers to any regular inspections of electrical systems and equipment to identify potential hazards.
* **Section 5 - Recordkeeping**
	+ **Safety Inspection Records** (Safety Inspection Records, Select Multiple): This option refers to any records kept of electrical safety inspections and results.
	+ **Hazard Reporting** (Hazard Reporting, Select Multiple): This option refers to any reporting of electrical hazards or incidents.
	+ **Safety Training Records** (Safety Training Records, Select Multiple): This option refers to any records kept of electrical safety training and completion.
