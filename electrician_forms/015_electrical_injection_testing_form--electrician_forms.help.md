# Electrical Injection Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Injection Testing Form is designed to collect data on electrical injection testing for equipment, providing a structured format for recording and reviewing test results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Test Date** field to enter the date of the electrical injection test.
2. Enter the **Equipment ID** to identify the specific piece of equipment being tested.
3. Enter the **Location** of the test, such as a specific room or facility.
4. Provide details on the **Parameters** measured during the test, such as voltage or current levels.
5. Record the **Results** of the test in the provided field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Date**: A date field where you can select or enter the date of the electrical injection test. 
* **Equipment ID**: An ID field where you can enter a unique identifier for the piece of equipment being tested. 
* **Location**: A text field where you can enter the location where the test was performed. 
* **Parameters**: A text field where you can provide details on the parameters measured during the test, such as voltage or current levels. 
* **Results**: A text field where you can record the results of the test, including any relevant observations or notes. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* Use a consistent format for recording results to ensure easy comparison and analysis.
