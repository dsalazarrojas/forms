# Power Socket Installation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Socket Installation Request Form is used to request the installation of a new power socket. This form is for electricians to gather the necessary information to schedule and complete the installation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Power Socket Installation Request Form" to start the process.
2. Fill in the job details, including the appointment time and date you prefer for the installation.
3. Enter the details of the electrician or installer assigned to complete the job, including their name, phone number, and email.
4. Specify the type of property for which the power socket is being installed (residential, commercial, or industrial).
5. Enter a brief description of the job.
6. Add any additional notes for the installer.
7. Select the status of the job (new, in progress, or done).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Details Form** (`power_socket_installation_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Appointment Time Form** (`appointment_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the appointment time that suits you best (8am - 12pm, 12pm - 4pm, 4pm - 8pm).
* **Electrician Details Form** (`electrician_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the electrician or installer assigned to complete the job.
* **Installer Details Form** (`installer_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the installer who will complete the job.
* **Installation Date Form** (`installation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the installation in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Installation Time Form** (`installation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for the installation in HH:MM format.
* **Installer Name Form** (`installer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the installer who will complete the job.
* **Installer Phone Form** (`installer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the installer.
* **Installer Email Form** (`installer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the installer.
* **Property Type Form** (`property_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of property for which the power socket is being installed (residential, commercial, industrial).
* **Job Description Form** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Installer Notes Form** (`installer_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes for the installer.
* **Job Status Form** (`job_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the job (new, in progress, or done).

**Tips**

* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Enter accurate and clear information to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the administrator or technical support team.
