# Electrical Upgrade Work Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electrical upgrade work consent form is a document that confirms a client's consent for electrical upgrade work to be performed at their site. This form is typically completed by the client or site owner to provide their consent and authorization for the work to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and contact details, if you are the client or site owner.
2. Select the type of electrical upgrade work you are requesting, whether it is for "Electrical Upgrade Work" or "Non Electrical Upgrade Work".
3. Provide a clear and legible signature to confirm your consent for the electrical upgrade work to be performed.
4. If you are unable to sign the form in person, you can provide a scanned or photographed copy of your signature.
5. If you are providing an electronic signature, please ensure it is a clear and legible image.
6. Enter the date you signed the form.
7. Enter the date of the electrical upgrade work, if known.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about the electrical upgrade work.
9. Enter the location where the electrical upgrade work will be performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **electrical_upgrade_details** (`electrical_upgrade_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the electrical upgrade work you are requesting.
* **work_scope** (`work_scope`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of electrical upgrade work you are requesting, whether it is for "Electrical Upgrade Work" or "Non Electrical Upgrade Work".
* **client_signature** (`client_signature`, note, required: false): This is a field for the client's signature, which must be clear and legible. If you are unable to sign in person, please provide a scanned or photographed copy of your signature.
* **client_signature_image** (`client_signature_image`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for uploading or providing an electronic signature image.
* **inspector_signature** (`inspector_signature`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the signature of the person who will be performing the electrical upgrade work, either "Electrical Upgrade Inspector" or "Electrical Upgrade Supervisor".
* **electrical_upgrade_type** (`electrical_upgrade_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of electrical upgrade work being performed, whether it is for "Electrical Upgrade Work" or "Non Electrical Upgrade Work".
* **client_signature_date** (`client_signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the electrical upgrade work.
* **electrical_upgrade_work_date** (`electrical_upgrade_work_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the electrical upgrade work, if known.
* **electrical_upgrade_work_cost** (`electrical_upgrade_work_cost`, text, required: false): Enter any estimated cost of the electrical upgrade work.
* **electrical_upgrade_work_location** (`electrical_upgrade_work_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the electrical upgrade work will be performed.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign the form in a clear and legible manner.
* If you are unable to sign the form in person, please provide a scanned or photographed copy of your signature.
* Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are providing an electronic signature, please ensure it is a clear and legible image.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
