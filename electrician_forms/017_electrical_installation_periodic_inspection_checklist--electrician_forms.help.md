# Electrical Installation Periodic Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Electrical Installation Periodic Inspection Checklist is used to record and track the state of electrical installations on site. This form is completed by the site personnel or electrical engineers to document any issues or discrepancies found during periodic inspections. The information collected will help in identifying any potential electrical safety risks and ensure that the electrical installations are functioning correctly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the correct site location from the options provided in the "Site Details" section.
2. Check the status of electrical systems on site by selecting the corresponding options in the "Electrical Systems" section.
3. Enter the number of circuit breakers and their capacity in the "Circuit Breakers" and "Circuit Breaker Capacity" fields respectively.
4. Provide the status of circuit breakers in the "Circuit Breaker Status" field.
5. Select the type of electrical panel boards installed on site from the "Electrical Panel Boards" field.
6. Check the status of transformers and generators in the "Transformer Details" and "Generator Status" fields.
7. Enter the capacity of the generators in the "Generator Capacity" field.
8. Select any other electrical equipment installed on site from the "Other Equipment" field.
9. Check the status of fire alarm systems, sprinkler systems, and fire suppression systems in the "Fire Alarm Systems", "Sprinkler Systems", and "Fire Suppression Systems" fields.
10. Select the status of fire hydrant systems and fire extinguishing systems in the "Fire Hydrant Systems" and "Fire Extinguishing Systems" fields.
11. Check the status of fire alarm systems in the "Fire Alarm Systems Status" field.
12. Select the status of fire suppression systems and fire hydrant systems in the "Fire Suppression Systems Status" and "Fire Hydrant Systems Status" fields.
13. Check the status of fire extinguishing systems in the "Fire Extinguishing Systems Status" field.
14. Select the status of site elevators from the "Site Elevators" field.
15. Select the status of site cranes from the "Site Crane" field.
16. Check the presence and location of fire stretches in the "Fire Stretches" and "Fire Stretch Location" fields.
17. Check the emergency exit path in the "Emergency Exit Path" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details** (`Site Details`, `select_one`, not required): Select the correct site location from the options provided.
* **Electrical Systems** (`Electrical Systems`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field is used to indicate the types of electrical systems installed on site.
  • **Electrical Distribution**: This option refers to the electrical distribution system, which is the system that brings power from the main electrical grid to the building.
  • **Electrical Panel Board**: This option refers to the electrical panel board, which is the main electrical distribution board in a building.
  • **Transformers**: This option refers to the transformer, which is an electrical device that changes voltage levels.
  • **Generator**: This option refers to the generator, which is a machine that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
  • **None**: If none of the above options apply.
* **Circuit Breakers** (`Circuit Breakers`, `number`, required): Enter the number of circuit breakers installed on site.
* **Circuit Breaker Capacity** (`Circuit Breaker Capacity`, `number`, required): Enter the capacity of circuit breakers in amps (A) or kilo-volt-amperes (KVA).
* **Circuit Breaker Status** (`Circuit Breaker Status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of circuit breakers from the options provided.
  • **Normal**: This option indicates that circuit breakers are functioning correctly.
  • **False**: This option indicates that circuit breakers are not functioning correctly.
  • **None**: If not applicable.
* **Electrical Panel Boards** (`Electrical Panel Boards`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the type of electrical panel boards installed on site.
  • **Main Panel**: This option refers to the main electrical panel board in the building.
  • **Sub-Panel**: This option refers to sub-panel boards which are used in conjunction with the main panel board.
  • **None**: If none of the above options apply.
* **Transformer Details** (`Transformer Details`, `select_one`, not required): Check if transformers are installed on site.
* **Generator Details** (`Generator Details`, `select_one`, not required): Check if generators are installed on site.
* **Generator Status** (`Generator Status`, `select_one`, not required): Check the status of generators from the options provided.
