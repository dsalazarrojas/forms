# Electrical Services Price List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients or customers who are requesting electrical services. This information is used to create a customized price list or quote for the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the electrical service you are requesting in the **Service Request** field.
2. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
3. Select the location type from the **Location** field. Choose Home, Commercial, or Industrial as applicable.
4. Enter the date you would like to schedule the service in the **Date** field.
5. Enter the time you would like to schedule the service in the **Time** field.
6. Indicate your preferred time window in the **Preferred Time Window** field.
7. Select the type of electrical service you require from the **Service Type** field. Choose Residential, Commercial, or Industrial as applicable.
8. Enter the price for the service in the **Service Price** field. This is where you will provide the cost of the service.
9. Select the currency of the service price from the **Service Price Currency** field. This is where you will select the currency of the service price (e.g., USD, CAD, etc.).
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the service in the **Service Notes** field.
11. Provide any special scheduling preferences or requirements in the **Schedule Preferences** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request**: A text field where you can describe your electrical service request.
* **Contact Information**: A text field for your contact details.
* **Location**: A select-one field where you can select the type of location: Home, Commercial, or Industrial.
* **Date**: A date field for scheduling the service.
* **Time**: A time field for scheduling the service.
* **Preferred Time Window**: A text field for your preferred time slot.
* **Service Type**: A select-one field for the type of electrical service required. Choose Residential, Commercial, or Industrial as applicable.
* **Service Price**: A number field for the price of the service.
* **Service Price Currency**: A text field for the currency of the service price (e.g., USD, CAD, etc.).
* **Service Notes**: A text field for any additional notes or comments.
* **Schedule Preferences**: A text field for any special scheduling requirements.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure accurate pricing and scheduling.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, be sure to include them in the **Schedule Preferences** field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for assistance.
