# Photovoltaic System Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and evaluate information about photovoltaic systems for assessment and evaluation purposes. It is essential to ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the Site Information section with the required details about the site where the photovoltaic system is installed.
2. Provide information about the Roof Information, including any relevant details about the roof's size, condition, and any other factors that might affect the system's performance.
3. In the Usage Information section, specify how the system will be used, such as for residential or commercial purposes.
4. For the Solar Panel Information section, select the correct type of panel that will be used in the system from the provided options.
5. In the System Size section, enter the total system size in kW.
6. In the Mounting Information section, select the mounting type for the system.
7. In the Panel Orientation section, specify the orientation of the panels.
8. In the Tilt Angle section, enter the tilt angle of the panels.
9. In the Panel Type section, select the type of panel used.
10. In the Panel Wattage section, enter the wattage of the panels.
11. In the System Rating section, enter the system rating in kW.
12. In the Inverter Rating section, enter the inverter rating (optional).
13. In the Mounting Type section, select the type of mounting used.
14. In the Tilt Angle section, enter the tilt angle of the panels.
15. In the System Type section, select the system type.
16. In the Installation Details section, provide any additional information about the installation.
17. In the Contact Information section, provide contact details.
18. In the Agreement and Liability Waivers section, select the option to agree or not to agree to the terms.
19. In the Comments section, provide any additional comments or notes.
20. In the Signatures section, sign and date the form.
21. In the Date section, enter the date of completion.
22. In the Time section, enter the time of completion.
23. In the Photovoltaic System Evaluation section, enter any evaluation or assessment of the system.
