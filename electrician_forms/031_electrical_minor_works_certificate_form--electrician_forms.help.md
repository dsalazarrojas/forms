# Electrical Minor Works Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and confirm minor electrical works. It's designed to provide details about the work, address, and the certifier's information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the basic details about the minor work on page 1.
2. On page 2, enter the job address, which is the location where the minor electrical work was done.
3. On page 3, enter the site address, which might be different from the job address if the work was done at a different site.
4. Enter the date and start and finish times of the job.
5. Fill in the name of the electrical certificate holder, who is the person or organization responsible for the work.
6. Enter the name of the certifier, who is the person or organization that certified the work as safe.
7. Enter the date the certificate was issued.
8. Add the certifier's signature to confirm that the work is safe.
9. Finally, describe the job that was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Minor work details (Page 1)** (`minor_work_details`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief description of the minor electrical work that was done.
* **Job address (Page 2)** (`job_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where the minor electrical work was done.
* **Site address (Page 3)** (`worksite_address`, text, required: false): This field might be the same as the job address or different depending on where the work was done.
* **Date of work (Page 4)** (`job_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the minor electrical work was done.
* **Start time (Page 4)** (`job_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the minor electrical work.
* **Finish time (Page 4)** (`job_finish_time`, time, required: false): Enter the finish time of the minor electrical work.
* **Electrical certificate holder (Page 5)** (`electrical_certificate_holder`, text, required: false): This is the name of the person or organization that owns the electrical certificate.
* **Certifier name (Page 5)** (`certifier_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the person or organization that certified the minor electrical work as safe.
* **Certificate date (Page 5)** (`certification_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date that the certificate was issued.
* **Certifier signature (Page 5)** (`certifier_signature`, text, required: false): Add the certifier's signature to confirm that the work is safe.
* **Job description (Page 6)** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of the minor electrical work that was done.
