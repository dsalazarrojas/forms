# Transfer Switch Neutral Connection Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transfer Switch Neutral Connection Inspection Form is designed to gather information about the connection of a transfer switch, which is a crucial component in electrical systems. This form helps electricians inspect and report on the connection's security, inspection outcome, rating, and any comments related to the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
3. Ensure that you select the correct option for the inspection outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Connection Details** (transfer_switch_neutral_connection_inspection_form_1): 
  Please describe the connection details, including the type of wire used and any other relevant information about the connection.
* **Neutral Connection Inspection Outcome** (transfer_switch_neutral_connection_inspection_form_2): 
  Please select one of the following options based on your inspection:
  - Connection is secure: The connection appears to be secure and in good condition.
  - Connection is not secure: The connection is not secure and poses a risk.
  - Unable to verify: You were unable to inspect the connection thoroughly due to safety concerns or other reasons.
* **Switch Rating** (transfer_switch_neutral_connection_inspection_form_3): 
  Please rate the switch's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Inspection Comments** (transfer_switch_neutral_connection_inspection_form_4): 
  Please provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection, including any issues or concerns you found during the inspection.
