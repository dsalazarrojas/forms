# Electrical Work Risk Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help electrical professionals and project managers identify and assess potential electrical hazards on a project site, ensuring a safer working environment for workers and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
2. For each field, enter your best judgment and expertise.
3. If a field is marked as "required", ensure you fill it in completely.
4. When filling in the "Project Supervisors" and "Project Reviewed By" fields, select all relevant individuals.
5. Complete the "Review Status" field by selecting "Approved" once you have reviewed and approved the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Introduction (1)**: Briefly describe the project, its scope, and any relevant background information.
* **Electrical Project Details (2)**: Enter relevant project details, such as equipment and electrical systems involved.
* **Electrical Hazards Identified (3)**: List and describe any electrical hazards identified on the project site.
* **Electrical Hazards Assessment (4)**: Select all relevant hazards from the list, using your best judgment and expertise.
* **Electrical Hazards Control (5)**: Describe any control measures taken or planned to mitigate electrical hazards.
* **Electrical Hazards Summary (6)**: Summarize the electrical hazards and control measures taken.
* **Project Location (7)**: Enter the location of the project site.
* **Project Start Date (8)**: Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date (9)**: Enter the end date of the project.
* **Responsible Person (10)**: Enter the person responsible for the project.
* **Form Submitted By (11)**: Enter the person submitting the form.
* **Project Supervisors (12)**: Select all relevant project supervisors.
* **Project Reviewed By (13)**: Select all relevant individuals who reviewed the assessment.
* **Review Notes (14)**: Enter any notes from the review process.
* **Additional Comments (15)**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Review Status (16)**: Select "Approved" once the assessment has been reviewed and approved.
* **Comments (17)**: Enter any comments from the review process.
* **Review Date (18)**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time (19)**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Reviewed By (20)**: Enter the person who reviewed the form.
* **Form Submitted (21)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Reviewed (22)**: Enter the date and time the form was reviewed.
* **Review Notes (23)**: Enter any additional review notes.
* **Hazards Risk Level (24)**: Enter the level of risk associated with electrical hazards on the project site.
* **Hazards Control Effectiveness (25)**: Enter the effectiveness of the control measures taken to mitigate electrical hazards.

**Tips**

* Ensure you have the necessary expertise and information to complete the form accurately.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes on the project site.
* Keep the form accessible and easily understandable for all stakeholders.
