# Receptacle Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receptacle Testing Survey is a form used to document the results of receptacle testing in facilities. It is essential to accurately record the testing process, any issues found, and outcomes to ensure electrical safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the receptacle location in the corresponding field.
2. Provide the receptacle identifier.
3. Enter the date and time of the test.
4. Select the test result (Yes or No).
5. Indicate if any issues were found during the testing process.
6. Record the name of the safety inspector who performed the test.
7. Provide the name of the facility manager responsible for the facility's electrical safety.
8. Enter the name of the electrician who performed the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Receptacle location** (`receptacle_location`, text, optional)
* This field is used to record the location of the receptacle within the facility.

### 2. **Receptacle identifier** (`receptacle_identifier`, text, optional)
* This field is used to identify the specific receptacle being tested.

### 3. **Test time** (`test_time`, date, optional)
* This field is used to record the date and time of the test.

### 4. **Test result** (`test_result`, select_one, optional)
* This field is used to indicate whether the test result is positive (Yes) or negative (No).

### 5. **Issues found** (`issues_found`, select_multiple, optional)
* This field is used to identify any safety concerns or issues detected during the testing process.

### 6. **Safety inspector** (`safety_inspector`, text, optional)
* This field is used to record the name of the individual who performed the safety inspection.

### 7. **Facility manager** (`facility_manager`, text, optional)
* This field is used to record the name of the facility manager responsible for maintaining electrical safety.

### 8. **Electrician** (`electrician`, text, optional)
* This field is used to record the name of the electrician who performed the test.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
* If any issues are found, ensure that you document them thoroughly in the 'Issues found' field.
* Review the form before submission to ensure that all required information is provided.
