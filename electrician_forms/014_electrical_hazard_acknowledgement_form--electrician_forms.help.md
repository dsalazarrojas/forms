# Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form is used to record acknowledgement and contact information from electrical workers who have been exposed to or have come into contact with electrical hazards while performing their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate response for whether you have come into contact with electrical hazards while performing your work.
2.  Enter the name of the contact person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
3.  Enter the date on which you came into contact with electrical hazards.
4.  Enter the facility ID number, if applicable.
5.  Enter the location where you were exposed to electrical hazards.
6.  Select the method of contact (e.g. phone, email, etc.).
7.  Enter the name of the contact person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
8.  Enter the address of the facility where you were exposed to electrical hazards.
9.  Enter the contact number of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
10.  Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the electrical hazard acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement** (`electrical_hazard_acknowledgement`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have come into contact with electrical hazards while performing your work.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which you came into contact with electrical hazards.
*   **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the facility ID number, if applicable.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you were exposed to electrical hazards.
*   **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the method of contact (e.g. phone, email, etc.).
*   **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the facility where you were exposed to electrical hazards.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the electrical hazard acknowledgement.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, as it is used for emergency response and follow-up purposes.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or facility administrator.
*   This form is designed to be used for electrical workers, but it may be used by others who have come into contact with electrical hazards.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact your facility administrator.
