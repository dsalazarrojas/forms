# Home Electrical Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about the electrical system and potential hazards, defects, and repairs during a home electrical inspection. It is intended to be filled out by the inspector and used for documentation and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form for every home or building inspection.
2. Use the same fields across all inspections to ensure consistent data collection.
3. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly to ensure accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electrical System** (`electrical_system`, text, required): This field is used to describe the electrical system of the inspected location.
* **Electrical Hazards** (`electrical_hazards`, select_multiple, required): Select any of the following electrical hazards found during the inspection:
	+ Faulty wiring
	+ Damaged electrical outlets
	+ Overheating electrical equipment
	+ Exposed wiring
	+ Leaking oil or gas leaks
	+ Other electrical hazards (please describe)
* **Electrical Defects** (`electrical_defects`, select_one, required): Select the primary electrical defect found during the inspection:
	+ Faulty or damaged wiring
	+ Defective circuit breakers or fuses
	+ Overheating or hot electrical equipment
	+ Leaking oil or gas leaks
	+ Other electrical defects (please describe)
* **Electrical Repairs** (`electrical_repairs`, select_multiple, required): Select any electrical repairs or alterations made during the inspection:
	+ Alterations or additions
	+ Repairs to electrical panel or circuit breakers
	+ Repairs to electrical wiring
	+ New electrical outlets or switches
	+ Other electrical repairs (please describe)
* **Electrical System Changes** (`electrical_system_changes`, text, required): Describe any changes made to the electrical system during the inspection.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, required): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, text, required): Summarize the results of the inspection.
* **Electrical Comments** (`electrical_comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the electrical inspection.
* **Electrical Signature** (`electrical_signature`, text, required): The inspector's signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Be as descriptive as possible when answering questions that allow for text responses.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, consult with a colleague or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is designed to be filled out by one person at a time, please do not duplicate efforts.
* Use standard date and time formats for date_of_inspection and time_of_inspection fields (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD, HH:MM:SS).
