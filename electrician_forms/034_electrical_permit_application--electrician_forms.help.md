# electrical_permit_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electrical permit application form is used to collect project information for electrical permitting purposes. This includes gathering details about the project's specifics, timeline, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Project Details" section with the necessary information about your project.
2.  In the "Timeline" section, provide the start and end dates of your project.
3.  In the "Contact Info" section, enter your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`Project Details`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This section is used to provide a brief description of your project.
* **Timeline** (`Timeline`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the start and end dates of your project. You can format the dates as `MM/DD/YY - MM/DD/YY`.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This section is for entering your contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
