# Electric Power Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Power Transfer Request Form is a document used to request and manage transfers of electric power services. It is a vital tool for electricians and service providers to manage these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, contact number, and email.
2. Select the service type (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, etc.) for the transfer.
3. Provide the details of the transfer, including the meter reading and any relevant notes.
4. If applicable, upload any attachments related to the transfer.
5. Choose the person to be assigned to the transfer.
6. Choose the status of the form completion.
7. Confirm that you have filled the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Electric Power Transfer Request** (Required): Enter a clear and concise title for the transfer request.
* **Transfer Details** (Optional): Provide a detailed description of the transfer, including any relevant notes.
* **Transfer Date** (Required): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Meter Reading** (Optional): Enter the current meter reading, if applicable.
* **Account Number** (Optional): Enter the account number, if applicable.
* **Transfer Type** (Optional): Select the type of transfer (e.g., New Connection, Transfer, Reconnect).
* **Customer Details** (Optional): Enter any relevant details about the customer.
* **Name** (Optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter the customer's email.
* **Service Type** (Optional): Select the type of service (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water).
* **Service Address** (Optional): Enter the address where the service will be provided.
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes about the transfer.
* **Assigned To** (Optional): Select the person to be assigned to the transfer.
* **Assigned Date** (Optional): Enter the date of assignment.
* **Completed Date** (Optional): Enter the date of completion.
* **Form Completion Status** (Optional): Select the status of form completion (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Incomplete).
* **Submitted By** (Required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments about the form submission.
* **Attachments** (Optional): Select whether to upload attachments or not.
* **Attachment File Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the attachment.
* **Attachment File Type** (Optional): Enter the type of attachment.
* **Attachment Description** (Optional): Enter a description of the attachment.
* **Attachment URL** (Optional): Enter the URL of the attachment.
* **Attachment File** (Optional): Enter the file of the attachment.
* **Submitter** (Optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (Optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Department** (Optional): Enter the department of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Job Title** (Optional): Enter the job title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Company** (Optional): Enter the company of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Notes** (Optional): Enter any notes from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Comment** (Optional): Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments from the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At** (Optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Form Completion Status** (Optional): Select the status of form completion (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Incomplete).
