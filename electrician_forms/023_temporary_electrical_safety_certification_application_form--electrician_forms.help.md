# Temporary Electrical Safety Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Electrical Safety Certification Application Form is used to verify the electrical safety of a project. This form ensures that electrical installations and systems meet the necessary safety standards, and is typically completed by project managers, site managers, or electrical supervisors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the project in the **Project Details** field, including the project name, location, and a summary of the electrical work involved.
2. In the **Electrical Plans** field, provide a detailed description of the electrical plans and diagrams for the project, including any relevant schematics, blueprints, or technical drawings.
3. In the **Approvals** field, list any relevant approvals or permits obtained for the project, including dates and details of the approvals.
4. In the **Notes** field, provide any additional information or comments about the project that may be relevant to electrical safety.
5. Finally, indicate whether the project submission is intended for **Submission** by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (text): A brief description of the project, including project name, location, and a summary of the electrical work involved.
* **Electrical Plans** (text): A detailed description of the electrical plans and diagrams for the project, including any relevant schematics, blueprints, or technical drawings.
* **Approvals** (text): A list of any relevant approvals or permits obtained for the project, including dates and details of the approvals.
* **Notes** (note): Any additional information or comments about the project that may be relevant to electrical safety.
* **Submission** (select_one): Indicate whether the project submission is intended for "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not submitting a duplicate request for the same project.
* Review the project's electrical plans and safety protocols before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with a project manager or electrical supervisor for guidance.
