# Power Line Clearance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Power Line Clearance Application Form is a document that customers use to provide necessary information to facilitate the clearance process for their power line worksite. This form is designed to ensure that all relevant details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the work type requires clearance.
2. Enter the customer's name as it appears on their official identification.
3. Enter the customer's company name as it appears on their official identification.
4. Enter the name of the contact person for the customer.
5. Enter the email address of the contact person.
6. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
7. Enter the address of the contact person.
8. Provide a detailed description of the type of work to be performed.
9. Enter the date the work is expected to start.
10. Enter the date the work is expected to be completed.
11. Enter the location where the work will be performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Type** (Yes, No): Indicates whether the work type requires clearance. If you select "Yes", the form will be reviewed and processed accordingly.
* **Customer Name** (Text): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their official identification. This will help us verify their identity.
* **Customer Company** (Text): Enter the customer's company name as it appears on their official identification. This will help us verify their affiliation.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person for the customer. This will help us communicate with them effectively.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person. This will be used for correspondence and updates.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person. This will be used for phone calls and updates.
* **Contact Address** (Text): Enter the address of the contact person. This will help us locate them for any follow-up meetings or inspections.
* **Customer Work Type Details** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the type of work to be performed. This will help us understand the scope of the project.
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the date the work is expected to start. This will help us schedule the clearance process accordingly.
* **Completion Date** (Date): Enter the date the work is expected to be completed. This will help us track the progress and completion of the project.
* **Work Location** (Text): Enter the location where the work will be performed. This will help us ensure that the correct site-specific regulations are followed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the customer information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any delays in the clearance process.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
