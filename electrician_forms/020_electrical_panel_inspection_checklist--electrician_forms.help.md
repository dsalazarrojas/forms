# electrical_panel_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electrical panel inspection checklist is used to evaluate the safety and rating of electrical panels in various settings, helping to ensure compliance with relevant standards and codes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of electrical panel being inspected (residential, commercial, or industrial).
2. Enter the panel's rating (if applicable).
3. Choose the panel's rating system (NEMA, IEC, or UL).
4. Enter any relevant panel accessories.
5. Note any specific details about the panel's location.
6. Enter any additional notes about the panel inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Panel Type** (`electrical_panel_inspection_1`, `select_one`): Select the type of electrical panel being inspected (residential, commercial, or industrial).
* **Panel Rating** (`electrical_panel_inspection_2`, `number`): Enter the panel's rating, if applicable.
* **Panel Rating System** (`electrical_panel_inspection_3`, `select_one`): Choose the panel's rating system (NEMA, IEC, or UL).
* **Panel Location** (`electrical_panel_inspection_4`, `text`): Note any specific details about the panel's location.
* **Panel Accessories** (`electrical_panel_inspection_5`, `text`): Enter any relevant panel accessories.
* **Panel Label** (`electrical_panel_inspection_6`, `text`): Enter any other relevant details about the panel.
* **Panel Rating Rating** (`electrical_panel_inspection_7`, `number`): Enter the panel's rating (if applicable).
* **Panel Rating System** (`electrical_panel_inspection_8`, `select_one`): Choose the panel's rating system (NEMA, IEC, or UL).
* **Panel Rating Level** (`electrical_panel_inspection_9`, `text`): Enter the panel's rating level (if applicable).
* **Panel Inspection Notes** (`electrical_panel_inspection_10`, `text`): Enter any additional notes about the panel inspection.
