# Electric Service Load Change Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a change in service load for electrical services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of service you are requesting to modify:
	* Electric
	* Gas
	* Water
2. Choose the location where the service is being modified:
	* Home
	* Business
	* Factory
	* Warehouse
3. Enter the address of the service location.
4. Provide a brief description of the change.
5. Upload any supporting documents (e.g., photos, invoices).
6. Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are requesting to modify (Electric, Gas, or Water).
- **Service Location**: Choose the location where the service is being modified (Home, Business, Factory, or Warehouse).
- **Service Address**: Enter the address where the service is being modified.
- **Description**: Provide a brief description of the change.
- **Documents**: Upload any supporting documents related to the change.
- **Upload Documents**: Upload any files or photos related to the change.
- **Submitter**: Enter your name.
- **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Submitter Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to help us process your request efficiently.
* If you have any supporting documents, upload them to help us understand your request.
* We will contact you to confirm your request and provide further instructions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
