# Extension Cord Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Extension Cord Safety Checklist" form is used to gather information on the status and safety of extension cords in a facility. This form is essential for inspecting and maintaining extension cords to prevent electrical hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page, which provides context and instructions for filling out the form.
2. Select the relevant inspector information from the list of options.
3. Choose the current status of the extension cord from the options provided.
4. Enter the length of the extension cord in the specified units (e.g., feet).
5. Select the type of extension cord from the options (e.g., power or non-power).
6. Evaluate the condition of the extension cord and select the corresponding option (e.g., good, damaged, or not inspected).
7. Identify any hazards associated with the extension cord and select all that apply.
8. Provide any additional notes about the extension cord.
9. Attach a photo of the extension cord (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Information** (`Inspector Information`, `select_one`): Select the person or team conducting the inspection from the list of options.
* **Cord Status** (`Cord Status`, `select_one`): Choose the current status of the extension cord from the options (e.g., good, hazardous, or not inspected).
* **Cord Length** (`Cord Length`, `number`): Enter the length of the extension cord in the specified units (e.g., feet).
* **Cord Type** (`Cord Type`, `select_multiple`): Select all types of extension cords used in the facility (e.g., power or non-power).
* **Cord Condition** (`Cord Condition`, `select_one`): Evaluate the condition of the extension cord and select the corresponding option (e.g., good, damaged, or not inspected).
* **Hazards** (`Hazards`, `select_multiple`): Identify any hazards associated with the extension cord and select all that apply.
* **Cord Notes** (`Cord Notes`, `text`): Provide any additional notes or observations about the extension cord.
* **Photo** (`Photo`, `text`): Attach a photo of the extension cord (if necessary).

## Tips

* Make sure to thoroughly inspect the extension cord and select the correct status and condition.
* Attach photos of any extension cords with significant damage or hazards.
* Keep the notes field concise and descriptive to ensure accurate information is recorded.
* Review and update this form regularly to ensure the safety of personnel and equipment in the facility.
