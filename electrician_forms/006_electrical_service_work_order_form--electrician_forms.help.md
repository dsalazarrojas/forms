# Electrical Service Work Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a new electrical service work order for the service company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Job Number** in the field provided.
2. Enter the **Customer Name** in the field provided.
3. Select the **Job Status** from the options provided.
4. Enter the **Service Technician** who will be performing the job.
5. Enter the **Customer Contact** information.
6. Enter a detailed description of the **Job Description**.
7. Enter the **Job Location** where the job will be performed.
8. Enter the **Customer Comments** if any.
9. Select the **Service Company** that will be performing the job.
10. Enter the **Job Date** and **Job Time** for the job.
11. Enter the **Assigned Tool** if applicable.
12. Enter any additional **Notes** about the job.
13. Enter the **Email** address of the customer.
14. Enter the **Phone** number of the customer.
15. Enter the **Service Request** if any.
16. Enter the **Customer Address** where the job will be performed.
17. Enter the **Service Technician's Signature** to confirm completion of the job.
18. Enter the **Job Status Signature** to confirm the job status.
19. Enter the **Service Company Signature** to confirm the job completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the job assigned by the company.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Job Status**: Select the current status of the job from the options provided.
* **Service Technician**: Enter the name of the service technician assigned to the job.
* **Customer Contact**: Enter the customer's contact information.
* **Job Description**: Enter a detailed description of the job to be performed.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where the job will be performed.
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the customer.
* **Service Company**: Select the service company that will be performing the job.
* **Job Date**: Enter the date of the job.
* **Job Time**: Enter the time of the job.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the job if applicable.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the job.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Service Request**: Enter any additional service request from the customer.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address where the job will be performed.
* **Service Technician's Signature**: Enter the signature of the service technician to confirm completion of the job.
* **Job Status Signature**: Enter the signature to confirm the job status.
* **Service Company Signature**: Enter the signature of the service company to confirm the job completion.
* **Service Company Signature**: This signature is required to confirm the job completion and is typically used for billing purposes.
* **Job Status Signature**: This signature is used to confirm the job status and is typically used for internal tracking purposes. 

Note: The **Job Status** field is optional and can be changed as the job progresses. The **Job Status Signature** field is not required but is a good practice to get a signature from the service technician to confirm the job status. 

Tips:
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor for assistance.
