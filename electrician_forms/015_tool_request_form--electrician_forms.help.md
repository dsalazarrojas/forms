# Tool Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tool Request Form is designed to allow Electricians to request specific tools for a job or project. This form captures necessary information to ensure that the right tools are delivered to the right Electrician at the right time.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your job ID number in the "Job ID" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Select the tool(s) you require for the job in the "Tool" field.
4. Provide any additional details about the job or tool requirements in the "Description" field.
5. Ensure that your email and phone number are up-to-date and accurate in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job ID** (Number, Required): Enter the job ID number assigned to you by the customer or project manager.
* **Name** (Text, Not Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Assigned Tool** (Multi-Select, Required): Select the tool that is assigned to you for the job. You can select multiple tools if necessary.
* **Description** (Text, Not Required): Provide any additional details about the job or tool requirements.
* **Tools** (Select One, Required): Select the tool(s) you require for the job. Be sure to select only the tool(s) that are necessary for the job.
* **Notes** (Text, Not Required): Provide any additional comments or notes about the job or tool requirements.
* **Date Required** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date when the tool is required.
* **Time Required** (Time, Not Required): Enter the time when the tool is required.
* **Email** (Email, Not Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a tool request for the same job or project.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
