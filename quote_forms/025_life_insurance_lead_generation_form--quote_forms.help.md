# Life Insurance Lead Generation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect lead information from potential customers interested in life insurance. Fill out the form to help us understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
2. In the "Policy Details" section, briefly describe the type of policy you are looking for.
3. Select your answer in the "Insurance Policies" section to indicate if you currently have any life insurance policies.
4. In the "Policy Coverage" section, select the policy type that best suits your needs.
5. Select the type of insurance coverage you need in the "Insurance Coverage" section.
6. For "Insurance Type", select the types of insurance you are interested in.
7. For "Policy Aged", enter the age of your policy.
8. Enter the policy premium in "Policy Premimum".
9. Enter the policy term in "Policy Term".
10. Enter the contact date you are looking for in "Contact Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Policy Details** (`policy_details`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the type of policy you are looking for.
* **Insurance Policies** (`insurance_policies`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the insurance policies you currently have.
* **Policy Coverage** (`policy_coverage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the policy type that best suits your needs, such as term life insurance or whole life insurance.
* **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of insurance coverage you need, such as single life insurance or multiple life insurance.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of insurance you are interested in, such as term life or whole life.
* **Policy Aged** (`policy_ages`, `number`, optional): Enter the age of your policy.
* **Policy Premimum** (`policy_premimum`, `number`, optional): Enter the policy premium you are looking for.
* **Policy Term** (`policy_term`, `number`, optional): Enter the policy term you are looking for.
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the contact date you are looking for.
* **Agent Info** (`agent_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information for the agent to follow up with you.
