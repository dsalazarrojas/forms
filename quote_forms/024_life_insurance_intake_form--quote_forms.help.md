# Life Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in obtaining a life insurance quote. It is intended to gather essential details about the customer and their insurance needs, which will be used to provide personalized quotes and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Scroll through the form pages to gather all the necessary information.
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the **Field-by-Field Explanation** section for clarification.
4.  Once you have filled out all the fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
* **customer_info** (`Customer Information`, `Text`, Not Required): Provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details as prompted.

### Insurance Information
* **insurance_info** (`Insurance Information`, `Text`, Not Required): If you have existing insurance policies, please provide details about them, such as policy type and effective dates.

### Quote Details
* **quote_details** (`Quote Details`, `Text`, Not Required): If you have any specific requirements or requests for your quote, please include them here.

### Contact
* **contact** (`Contact`, `Text`, Not Required): If you would like to be contacted by our representative, please provide your preferred contact method and details.
