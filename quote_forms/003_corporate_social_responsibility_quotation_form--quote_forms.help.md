# Corporate Social Responsibility Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a company's CSR quotation request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in sections, starting with the company information.
2. Select the relevant contact details and contact person.
3. Enter the quotation details, including date and time.
4. Add any additional note or comment.
5. Select the vendor and product information.
6. Enter the price and currency.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (1): Enter the name of the company requesting the quotation.
* **Contact** (2): Select the contact details that apply to this quotation request.
*   *Option 1*
*   *Option 2*
*   *Option 3*
* **Contact Person** (3): Enter the name of the contact person for this quotation request.
* **Contact E-Mail** (4): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (5): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Quote Details** (6): Enter a brief description of the quotation request.
* **Quote Date** (7): Enter the date when the quotation is required.
* **Quote Time** (8): Enter the time when the quotation is required.
* **Quote Note** (9): Enter any additional note or comment about this quotation request.
* **Vendor** (10): Enter the name of the vendor associated with this quotation request.
* **Product** (11): Enter the name of the product or service being quoted.
* **Price** (12): Enter the price of the product or service.
* **Currency** (13): Select the currency of the price.
*   *USD*
*   *EUR*
*   *JPY*
* **Terms Conditions** (14): Enter any terms and conditions that apply to this quotation request.
