# Responsive Share Your Story Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Share Your Story Form is designed to gather user feedback, stories, and experiences with the company. This form serves as an open channel for users to share their thoughts, concerns, and ideas, helping the company understand their needs and preferences better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Share Your Story" button to access the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) below each field.
3. Enter your First Name in the "FirstName" field.
4. Enter your Email Address in the "EmailAddress" field.
5. Choose your experience with the company using the "Experience" dropdown menu.
6. Share your feedback and comments in the "FeedbackComments" field.
7. Add any additional comments or information in the "AdditionalComments" field.
8. Enter your Contact Number in the "ContactNumber" field.
9. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your Website (if applicable) in the "Website" field.
11. Add any other information you would like to share in the "OtherInfo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName** (text): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **EmailAddress** (email): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you for further communication.
* **Experience** (select one): Rate your experience with our company as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **FeedbackComments** (text): Share your thoughts, feedback, and concerns with us.
* **AdditionalComments** (text): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **ContactNumber** (text): Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you for further communication.
* **Website** (text): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **OtherInfo** (text): Share any other relevant information about yourself or your experience with our company.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any attachments or files to share, you may attach them to this form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact our support team.
