# PC Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PC Quote Form is a quote request form used by sales employees to gather information from customers about their product needs and preferences. This form helps to generate quotes for customers for products such as computers, hardware, and software.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out your personal details:
	* Customer Name: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	* Customer Email: Enter your work or personal email address.
	* Customer Phone: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
	* Customer Company: Enter your company or organization name.
	* Customer Address: Enter your street address.
	* Customer City: Enter the city where you are located.
	* Customer State: Select your state from the dropdown list.
	* Customer Zip: Enter your zip code.
	* Customer Country: Select your country of origin.
2. Select the product you are interested in:
	* Product Type: Choose a product type from the dropdown list (Cable, Computer, Hardware, Software).
3. Enter your product details:
	* Product Description: Describe your product needs and preferences.
	* Product Quantity: Enter the number of products you would like to quote.
	* Product Cost: Enter the cost of the product.
4. Confirm your quote details:
	* Quote Terms: Select any terms you would like to include in the quote (Standard, Special, Custom).
	* Quote Total: Enter the total cost of the quote.
	* Quote Discount: Enter any discount percentage.
	* Quote Tax: Enter any tax amount.
	* Quote Subtotal: Enter the subtotal of the quote.
5. Confirm your signature and date:
	* Customer Signature: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the quote terms.
	* Employee Signature: Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the quote terms.
	* Date: Enter the date of the quote.
	* Time: Enter the time of the quote.
