# Disposable Gloves Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quotation for disposable gloves. Please fill out the form with your relevant information to help us provide a detailed quote for your disposable glove needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with your accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number for quick contact.
4. Enter the First Name and Last Name of the person requesting the quotation (if different from you).
5. Enter the date and time you would like to receive the quotation.
6. Enter your Company name (if applicable).
7. Enter your address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **First Name 2** (`first_name_2`, `text`, **optional**): If this is not you, enter the first name of the person requesting the quotation.
* **Last Name 2** (`last_name_2`, `text`, **optional**): If this is not you, enter the last name of the person requesting the quotation.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you would like to receive the quotation.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time you would like to receive the quotation.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your address (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are not sure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
