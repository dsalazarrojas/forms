# Online Shopping Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide information about their product interests, contact details, and quote requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product details section with information about the product you are interested in, including any relevant specifications or features.
2. Provide your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter your shipping details, including your address and any specific shipping instructions.
4. In the quote details section, provide any additional information you would like to include about your quote request.
5. Finally, select the quote status to indicate whether you would like to receive a quote, and if so, when you would like to receive it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product details**: This field is for you to provide information about the product you are interested in.
* **Contact details**: Provide your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Shipping details**: Enter your address and any specific shipping instructions, such as expedited shipping or delivery to a specific location.
* **Quote details**: Use this field to provide any additional information about your quote request, such as special requirements or deadlines.
* **Quote status**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you would like to receive a quote, and if so, select the date you would like to receive it from the available options. If you do not want to receive a quote, select "No".

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can efficiently process your quote request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
