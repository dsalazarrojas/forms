# profile_questionnaire_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users to create a comprehensive profile of their background and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your work email in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field in the format MM/DD/YY.
6. Enter the name of your current or past company in the **company** field.
7. Enter the website of your current or past company in the **company_website** field.
8. Enter your job title in the **job_title** field.
9. Describe your job description in the **job_description** field.
10. Enter the number of years of experience you have in the **years_of_experience** field.
11. Enter your current location in the **current_location** field.
12. Select your current location's country from the **current_location_country** dropdown menu.
13. Select your job level from the **job_level** dropdown menu.
14. Select one or multiple job roles that you have experience in from the **job_role** dropdown menu.
15. Select one or multiple industries that you have experience in from the **industry** dropdown menu.
16. Select one or multiple job functions that you have experience in from the **job_function** dropdown menu.
17. Enter any project details in the **project_details** field.
18. Select one or multiple business sectors that you have experience in from the **business_sector** dropdown menu.
19. Enter the number of hours you are available to work per day in the **available_work_hours** field.
20. Enter any additional notes about your work hours in the **available_work_hours_2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (3, Email, Required): Enter your work email.
* **Phone Number** (4, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Company** (6, Text, Required): Enter the name of your current or past company.
* **Company Website** (7, Text, Required): Enter the website of your current or past company.
* **Job Title** (8, Text, Required): Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (9, Text, Required): Describe your job description.
* **Years of Experience** (10, Number, Required): Enter the number of years of experience you have.
* **Current Location** (11, Text, Required): Enter your current location.
* **Current Location Country** (12, Select One, Required): Select your current location's country from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Level** (13, Select One, Required): Select your job level from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Role** (14, Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple job roles that you have experience in from the dropdown menu.
* **Industry** (15, Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple industries that you have experience in from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Function** (16, Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple job functions that you have experience in from the dropdown menu.
* **Project Details** (17, Text, Required): Enter any project details.
* **Business Sector** (18, Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple business sectors that you have experience in from the dropdown menu.
* **Available Work Hours** (19, Number, Required): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per day.
* **Available Work Hours 2** (20, Number, Required): Enter any additional notes about your work hours.
