# Aromatherapist Quote Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients seeking aromatherapy services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client's personal details, including their full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the client type (Individual, Corporate, Wellness center, Spa or salon, Medical facility, or Other).
3. Choose the type of service requested (Aromatherapy consultation, Essential oil blending, Aromatherapy massage, Wellness workshop, Aromatherapy training, Corporate event, or Custom service).
4. Select the service duration (30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, Half day, or Full day).
5. Indicate the number of people to be served.
6. Select the primary focus of the service (Relaxation and stress relief, Pain management, Energy and vitality, Sleep improvement, Immune support, Emotional balance, or Custom wellness goal).
7. If applicable, enter the location address.
8. Choose the preferred date and time for the service.
9. Select the preferred location (Aromatherapist location, Client location - at home, Client location - workplace, or Neutral location).
10. If applicable, choose the budget range and preferred payment method.
11. Add any additional information or notes about the client's preferences and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required): Select the client type (Individual, Corporate, Wellness center, Spa or salon, Medical facility, or Other).
* **Type of Service Requested** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service requested (Aromatherapy consultation, Essential oil blending, Aromatherapy massage, Wellness workshop, Aromatherapy training, Corporate event, or Custom service).
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, select_one, required): Select the service duration (30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, Half day, or Full day).
* **Number of People to be Served** (`number_of_people`, number, required): Indicate the number of people to be served.
* **Primary Focus of Service** (`service_focus`, select_one, required): Select the primary focus of the service (Relaxation and stress relief, Pain management, Energy and vitality, Sleep improvement, Immune support, Emotional balance, or Custom wellness goal).
* **Preferred Essential Oils or Scents** (`preferred_essential_oils`, text, optional): Enter any preferred essential oils or scents.
* **Allergies or Scent Sensitivities** (`allergies_sensitivities`, text, optional): Enter any allergies or scent sensitivities.
* **Special Requests or Requirements** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Add any special requests or requirements.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose the preferred date for the service.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose the preferred time for the service.
* **Service Location** (`location_preference`, select_one, required): Select the preferred location (Aromatherapist location, Client location - at home, Client location - workplace, or Neutral location).
* **Location Address if Applicable** (`service_location_address`, text, optional): Enter the location address if applicable.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select the budget range (Under 100, 100-250, 250-500, 500-1000, 1000+, or Flexible).
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred payment method (Cash, Credit card, Bank transfer, Check, or Other).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, text, optional): Indicate how the client heard about the aromatherapist.
* **Any Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or questions.
