# Corporate Video Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for corporate video services. It is intended for clients who are seeking a quote for a video production project. By submitting this form, you will be providing us with essential information about your project, which will enable us to create a detailed quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required" (indicated by a red asterisk *).
2. Enter your project name and provide a brief description of your project.
3. Enter your budget for the project.
4. Choose a submission type from the "Submission Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your contact information, including name, company, email, and phone number.
6. You may also add any additional notes or files relevant to your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: *project_name* (Required) - Enter the name of your project. This will help us understand the scope and objective of your project.
* **Budget**: *budget* (Required) - Enter your estimated budget for the project.
* **Timeline**: *timeline* (Required) - Enter the desired completion date for the project.
* **Name**: *name* (Required) - Enter your name.
* **Company**: *company* (Required) - Enter your company name.
* **Email**: *email* (Required) - Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: *phone* (Required) - Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Type**: *submission_type* (Required) - Choose from: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Notes**: *notes* (Optional) - Add any additional notes or information about your project.
* **Files**: *files* (Optional) - Attach any relevant files related to your project.
* **Message**: *message* (Optional) - Add any additional comments or questions.
* **Terms and Conditions**: *terms_and_conditions* (Optional) - Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
