## quote_acceptance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quote acceptance form is used to obtain a client's acceptance of a quote, ensuring that all required information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their contract or business documentation.
2. **Quote Date**: Select the date the quote was provided to the client.
3. **Acceptance Date**: Enter the date the client accepts the quote, which will be the date they sign and return the form.
4. **Client Address**: Enter the client's address as it appears on their contract or business documentation.
5. **Total**: Enter the total price of the quote as accepted by the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their contract or business documentation. For example, John Doe or Acme Inc.
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, required): Select the date the quote was provided to the client. This helps us track the timeline for the quote's acceptance and fulfillment.
* **Acceptance Date** (`acceptance_date`, date, required): Enter the date the client accepts the quote, which will be the date they sign and return the form. This ensures we have a record of when the acceptance was received.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the client's address as it appears on their contract or business documentation. For example, 123 Main St, Anytown, USA.
* **Total** (`quote_total`, number, required): Enter the total price of the quote as accepted by the client. This ensures accurate financial records and payment processing.
