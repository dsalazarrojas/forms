# Legal Compliance Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Compliance Quotation Form is used to collect information from clients about their legal compliance needs. This information is used to provide a quotation for the services they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. The form is divided into multiple pages, but you can fill out the fields in any order you prefer. There is no submit button, as the form will automatically be submitted when you are finished filling it out.
