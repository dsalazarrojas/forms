# Spa Service Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who are requesting a quote for spa services. It includes fields for name, email, phone number, service interests, estimated visit time, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. In the "Service Interests" field, select the services you are interested in.
6. In the "Service Interests 2" field, list any additional services you are interested in.
7. In the "Visit Details" field, select the date and time you would like to visit.
8. In the "Comments" field, add any additional comments about your visit.
9. In the "Services" field, select the services you require.
10. In the "Estimated Visit Time" field, enter the estimated time for your visit.
11. In the "Quote Type" field, select the type of quote you are requesting.
12. In the "Quote Value" field, enter the value of the quote.
13. In the "Quote Discount" field, enter the discount amount for the quote.
14. In the "Quote Total" field, enter the total value of the quote.
15. In the "Quote Status" field, select the status of the quote.
16. In the "Last Visit" field, enter the last visit date.
17. In the "Last Visited" field, enter the last visited date.
18. In the "Number of Visits" field, enter the number of visits.
19. In the "Last Visit Time" field, enter the last visit time.
20. In the "Last Visit Comments" field, enter any comments about the last visit.
21. In the "Next Visit" field, select the next visit date.
22. In the "Last Next Visit" field, enter the last next visit date.
23. In the "Visit Frequency" field, enter the visit frequency.
24. In the "Quote Notes" field, add any additional notes about the quote.
25. In the "Submitted By" field, enter the name of the person who submitted the quote.
