# repair_estimation_quote_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide an estimate of the cost and time required to complete a job or repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (Customer Info, text, required): Enter the customer's name, address, phone number, email address, city, state, zip code, and country.
* **Service Provider** (Service Provider, select_one, optional): Select the type of service or repair required.
* **Job Details** (Job Details, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job or repair.
* **Damage Info** (Damage Info, text, optional): Enter any information about the damage or issue with the job or repair.
* **Repair Info** (Repair Info, text, optional): Enter any information about the repair process or procedures.
* **Labor Costs** (Labor Costs, number, optional): Enter the estimated labor costs for the job or repair.
* **Part Costs** (Part Costs, number, optional): Enter the estimated part costs for the job or repair.
* **Labor Hours** (Labor Hours, number, optional): Enter the estimated labor hours required for the job or repair.
* **Labor Hours Unit** (Labor Hours Unit, text, optional): Select the unit of measure for the labor hours.
* **Equipment Cost** (Equipment Cost, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of any equipment needed for the job or repair.
* **Material Cost** (Material Cost, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of any materials needed for the job or repair.
* **Total Cost** (Total Cost, number, optional): Enter the total estimated cost for the job or repair.
* **Customer Comments** (Customer Comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Technician Name** (Technician Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the technician assigned to the job or repair.
* **Technician Phone** (Technician Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the technician assigned to the job or repair.
* **Technician Email** (Technician Email, text, optional): Enter the email address of the technician assigned to the job or repair.
* **Customer Address** (Customer Address, text, optional): Enter the address of the customer.
* **Customer City** (Customer City, text, optional): Enter the city of the customer.
* **Customer State** (Customer State, text, optional): Enter the state of the customer.
* **Customer Zip** (Customer Zip, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the customer.
* **Customer Country** (Customer Country, text, optional): Enter the country of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, text, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Job Type** (Job Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of job or repair.
* **Job Status** (Job Status, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the job or repair.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click this button to complete and submit the form.
* **Total Cost** (Total Cost, number, optional): Enter the total estimated cost for the job or repair.

Note: The fields that are marked as "required" must be filled out before submitting the form. The fields that are marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
