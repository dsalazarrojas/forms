# Repair Estimation Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Estimation Quote Form is designed to collect information from customers for a repair estimation quote. This form helps us gather the necessary details to provide an accurate quote for the requested repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Start on the first page, "Customer Details", and enter your contact information.
3. Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
4. On the second page, "Equipment Details", provide details about the equipment that needs repair.
5. If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them on the third page, "Additional Details".
6. Click "Submit" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details (repair_estimation_quote_form_page_1)
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you to confirm the quote.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can receive notifications and updates about your repair.

### Equipment Details (repair_estimation_quote_form_page_2)
* **Equipment Description**: Describe the equipment that needs repair, including its make, model, and any relevant serial numbers.
* **Equipment Condition**: Explain the current condition of the equipment, including any pre-existing issues or defects.

### Additional Details (repair_estimation_quote_form_page_3)
* **Additional Information or Comments**: Provide any additional details or comments about the repair, such as deadlines or specific requirements.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure we can provide the best possible quote.
