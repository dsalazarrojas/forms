# Home Renovation Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for homeowners who are looking to get a quote for their home renovation project. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read this help guide** to understand the fields and what information is required.
- **Fill out each field** with the requested information.
- **Submit the form** once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, optional): This is the title of your project. Please provide a brief description of the project, including the type of renovation you are looking for (e.g., kitchen renovation, bathroom renovation, etc.).
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): This is where you can provide us with your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): This is the address of the property where the renovation will take place. Please make sure to provide a valid address to ensure we can get in touch with you and visit your property for the quote.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Use plain text for all fields, no attachments or files.
- Our team will review your form submission and get in touch with you to discuss your project further.
