# Cylindrical Roller Bearing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a quote request form for cylindrical roller bearings. It helps our supplier understand your specific needs and preferences to provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "customer" section.
2. Specify your bearing requirements in the "bearing" section, including type, material, size, quality, and material.
3. Add any additional details or instructions in the "quote details" section.
4. Provide your contact information in the "contact" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **cylindrical_roller_bearing_quote_request_form**: This is the form title.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Please enter your company or name.
* **Bearing Info** (`bearing_info`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Describe the specific bearing requirements you need.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Specify any delivery instructions or requirements.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Add any additional details or instructions for your quote.
* **Contact Info** (`contact`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Add your signature.
* **Quote Number** (`quote_number`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Please enter your quote number.
* **Bearing Type** (`bearing_type`, `select_multiple`, `required`: `true`): Select the type of bearing you need. (Ball, Cylindrical, Taper, Spherical)
* **Material** (`material`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Choose the material for your bearing. (Steel, Brass, Stainless Steel, Copper)
* **Bearing Size** (`bearing_size`, `select_multiple`, `required`: `true`): Select the size of your bearing. (1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1)
* **Bearing Quality** (`bearing_quality`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Choose the quality of your bearing. (Standard, Premium, High-Grade)
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, `required`: `false`): Enter the quantity of bearings you need.
* **Unit Of Measurement** (`unit_of_measurement`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select the unit of measurement for your bearing. (mm, in, ft, in2)
* **Bearing Life** (`bearing_life`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Choose the life of your bearing. (Standard, Long, Extra Long, Unlimited)
* **Bearing Material** (`bearing_material`, `select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select the material of your bearing. (Steel, Brass, Stainless Steel, Copper)
* **Bearing Size Diameter** (`bearing_size_diameter`, `number`, `required`: `false`): Enter the diameter of your bearing size.
* **Bearing Width** (`bearing_width`, `number`, `required`: `false`): Enter the width of your bearing.
* **Bearing Thickness** (`bearing_thickness`, `number`, `required`: `false`): Enter the thickness of your bearing.
* **Bearing Weight** (`bearing_weight`, `number`, `required`: `false`): Enter the weight of your bearing.
* **Bearing Price** (`bearing_price`, `number`, `required`: `false`): Enter the price of your bearing.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, `required`: `false`): Enter the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, `required`: `false`): Enter the delivery time.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Enter your name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, `required`: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, `required`: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `date`, `required`: `false`): Add your signature.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact our supplier.
* We will get back to you with a quote as soon as possible.
