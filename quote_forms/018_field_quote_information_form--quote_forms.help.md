# Field Quote Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display quote-related information from customers. It is an essential tool for our team to provide accurate and personalized quotes to our customers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the field quote in the "Field Quote" field.
2. Provide the customer's first name and last name in the "Customer First Name" and "Customer Last Name" fields respectively.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
4. Provide the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Enter the service address where the quote is required.
6. Select the date of the service using the date picker in the "Service Date" field.
7. Enter any additional notes about the service or customer in the "Customer Note" field.
8. Optionally, select the time of the service using the time picker in the "Service Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Quote** (`field_quote`, text, **required**: false): This field is the title of the field quote, which will be used to identify and label the quote in our system.
* **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, **required**: false): This field is where you enter the customer's first name. Please enter the name as it is spelled correctly.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, **required**: false): This field is where you enter the customer's last name. Please enter the name as it is spelled correctly.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, **required**: false): This field is where you enter the customer's email address. Please ensure that this is a valid email address. A valid email address will be in the format of `example@domain.com`.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, **required**: false): This field is where you enter the customer's phone number.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, **required**: false): This field is where you enter the address where the service will be performed.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, **required**: false): This field is where you select the date of the service using the date picker. Please select the date that is most convenient for the customer.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, **required**: false): This field is where you enter any additional information about the customer or service that is necessary to complete the quote.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, **required**: false): This field is where you select the time of the service using the time picker. If this field is completed, please ensure that it is most convenient for the customer.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, **required**: false): This field is where you enter the location of the service. Please enter the address or a descriptive location.
