# Auditor Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Auditor Estimate Form is a crucial tool for our firm to understand your needs and preferences. It's designed to gather essential information about your company, including your industry, annual revenue, number of employees, and other relevant details. This information will help us determine the best audit services for your organization and provide you with a more accurate estimate of our fees and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required information, including your full name, email address, phone number, company name, company address, request date, and desired completion date.
2. Select the type of audit you need, such as financial statement audit, review, or internal audit.
3. Choose your company type, industry, and annual revenue to help us understand your business context.
4. Specify the number of employees in your organization and the fiscal year end date.
5. If applicable, provide the name of your previous auditor and reason for changing auditors.
6. Select any additional services you require, such as tax preparation or bookkeeping.
7. Estimate your expected budget range for the audit.
8. Finally, provide your electronic signature, confirming that you've reviewed and agreed upon the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): This is the name of the person requesting the audit, usually the company's owner or representative.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): This is the contact email address of the person requesting the audit.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): This is the contact phone number of the person requesting the audit.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): This is the name of the company or organization requiring the audit.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): This is the full address of the company or organization requiring the audit.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): This is the date when the audit request was made.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required): This is the type of company or organization (Corporation, LLC, Partnership, etc.).
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): This is the industry where your company operates (Financial Services, Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.).
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): This is an approximation of your company's annual revenue.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, select_one, required): This is an approximation of the number of employees in your organization.
* **Audit Type Needed** (`audit_type_needed`, select_one, required): This is the type of audit required (Financial Statement Audit, Review, Compilation, etc.).
* **Audit Purpose** (`audit_purpose`, select_one, required): This is the purpose of the audit (Regulatory requirement, Lender requirement, etc.).
* **Fiscal Year End** (`fiscal_year_end`, date, required): This is the date of your fiscal year end.
* **Desired Completion** (`desired_completion`, date, required): This is the date when you need the audit completed.
* **Prior Auditor** (`prior_auditor`, select_one, required): This indicates if you had a prior auditor and if so, whether it was from the same firm or not.
* **Prior Auditor Name** (`prior_auditor_name`, text, optional): If you had a prior auditor, provide their name.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): If you changed auditors, provide the reason for the change.
* **Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`, text, optional): Provide any special circumstances or information that might affect the audit.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional services you require, such as tax preparation or bookkeeping.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Estimate your expected budget range for the audit.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): This is how you heard about our firm.
* **Referred By** (`referred_by`, text, optional): If someone referred you, provide their name.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): This is your electronic signature, confirming that you've reviewed and agreed upon the information provided.

## Tips
- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If a field is not applicable to your situation, simply select "None" or leave it blank.
- If you have any special circumstances or requirements, please provide them in the "Special Circumstances" field.
- This form is an essential tool for our firm to understand your needs and preferences. Please take the time to fill it out accurately to ensure we can provide the best services for you.
