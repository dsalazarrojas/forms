# Trip Planning Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information for a trip planning quote or estimate. Please fill out the form with your trip details to receive a quote for your travel arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details such as traveller name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
3. Enter your travel dates.
4. Choose your travel type.
5. Specify your departure city and destination city.
6. Select your accommodation preferences, including hotels, hostels, and guesthouses.
7. Indicate if you've used any other travel agencies.
8. Enter additional travel dates and times if necessary.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveller Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you with your quote.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your trip.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your trip.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of travel you're planning (e.g., leisure, business, etc.).
* **Departure City** (`departure_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your departure.
* **Destination City** (`destination_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your destination.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you've used any travel agency for this trip.
