# Home Removals Quote Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Removals Quote Template is a form used to generate a quote for a home removals service. It collects information about the move, including address, contact details, move date and time, move type, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact details, including name and email.
2. Enter the address of the home that will be moved.
3. Select the type of move and any additional services required.
4. Enter the weight and dimensions of the move.
5. Select the payment method for the quote.
6. Calculate the quote total and discount based on the provided options.
7. Review and confirm the quote before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **home_address** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the home that will be moved.
* **contact_name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **move_date** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the move.
* **move_time** (`5`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the move.
* **move_type** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of move (Move, Relocation).
* **moving_service** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of moving service required (Local Move, Long Distance Move, International Move).
* **additional_services** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional services required for the move.
* **move_distance** (`11`, `number`, optional): Enter the distance of the move (if applicable).
* **move_weight** (`12`, `number`, optional): Enter the weight of the move (if applicable).
* **move_height** (`13`, `number`, optional): Enter the height of the move (if applicable).
* **move_length** (`14`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the move (if applicable).
* **move_width** (`15`, `number`, optional): Enter the width of the move (if applicable).
* **move_depth** (`16`, `number`, optional): Enter the depth of the move (if applicable).
* **move_weight_units** (`17`, `select_one`, optional): Select the units for the move weight (Kilograms, Pounds, Tons).
* **move_distance_units** (`18`, `select_one`, optional): Select the units for the move distance (Kilometers, Miles, Feet).
* **move_date_range** (`19`, `date`, optional): Enter the range of dates for the move (if applicable).
* **move_time_range** (`20`, `time`, optional): Enter the range of times for the move (if applicable).
* **pickup_date** (`21`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for the pickup (if applicable).
* **pickup_time** (`22`, `time`, optional): Enter the time for the pickup (if applicable).
* **quote_total** (`23`, `number`, required): The total quote for the move.
* **quote_discount** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Select the discount rate for the move (0.0, 0.05, 0.1).
* **payment_method** (`25`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the payment method for the quote (Cash, Credit, Debit).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate quote generation.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the relevant authorities or service provider.
* This form is a template and should not be used to collect sensitive or personal data without the necessary legal and regulatory approvals.
