## Freelance Service Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their service needs and generate a quote for freelance services. It is intended for clients to provide their details and receive a quote for the services they require. The form is a simple text-based form with five fields that gather essential information from the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Enter your client's details in the "Client Information" field.
3. Provide information about the service you need in the "Service Information" field.
4. Enter the details of the quote you require in the "Quote Details" field.
5. Enter the total cost of the quote in the "Total" field.
6. Specify the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Information, text, required: false):** This field is where you can enter your client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Service Information (Service Information, text, required: false):** Describe the service you need, including the specifics of what you require and any deadlines or constraints.
* **Quote Details (Quote Details, text, required: false):** Enter the details of the quote you require, including any specific requirements or special instructions.
* **Total (Total, text, required: false):** Enter the total cost of the quote you require.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms, text, required: false):** Specify the payment terms you require, including any applicable deposit, payment schedule, or any other payment details.
