# Hair Design Equipment Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the hair design equipment, its condition, and other relevant details to assist in providing quote requests for hair design equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the equipment type from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief description of the equipment, if applicable.
3. Indicate the condition of the equipment, selecting all that apply.
4. Enter the date the equipment was purchased, if known.
5. Enter the price of the equipment, if known.
6. Select whether the equipment is located on-site or off-site.
7. Select whether a technician is required to operate the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment type**: Select one of the options to indicate the type of hair design equipment being quoted.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Equipment condition**: Select all that apply for the condition of the equipment.
* **Purchase date**: Enter the date the equipment was purchased, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the equipment, if known.
* **Location**: Select whether the equipment is located on-site or off-site.
* **Technician**: Select whether a technician is required to operate the equipment.
