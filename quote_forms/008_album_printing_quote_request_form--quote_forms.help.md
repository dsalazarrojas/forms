# Album Printing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for quote requests from clients seeking album printing services. It's a crucial tool to help us better understand your printing needs and provide a quote accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your business information (Business Name, Contact Person, and Email Address)
2. Select the correct Album Type and Album Size for your needs
3. Choose the desired Cover Material and Page Type
4. Estimate the number of pages needed
5. Specify the Print Quantity and desired Turnaround Time
6. Select the Photo Finish and Color Correction Service needed
7. Decide on the Design Service (if needed)
8. Provide your Shipping Address
9. If applicable, specify a Budget Range and if you'd like to see a Sample Album
10. Add any Additional Requests or comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter your photography business name to ensure we have the correct address and contact information for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the full name of the primary contact person for this quote request.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive the quote and any updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for any questions or follow-up.
* **Album Type**: Select the type of album you need printed (e.g., Wedding Album, Portrait Album, etc.).
* **Album Size**: Choose the desired dimensions for your album (e.g., 8x8 inches, 10x10 inches, etc.).
* **Cover Material**: Select the preferred cover material for your album (e.g., Leather, Linen, Velvet, etc.).
* **Page Type**: Choose the type of pages you need for your album (e.g., Layflat, Standard, Thick board, etc.).
* **Number of Pages**: Estimate the number of pages needed for your album.
* **Print Quantity**: Specify how many copies of the album you need.
* **Photo Finish**: Select the finish for your prints (e.g., Glossy, Matte, Satin, etc.).
* **Color Correction Service**: Decide if you need color correction for all images or just selected ones.
* **Design Service**: Choose if you need layout design or if you'll provide your own layouts.
* **Turnaround Time**: Select the desired turnaround time for your album (e.g., Standard, Rush, Express, etc.).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you'd like to receive your printed albums.
* **Budget Range**: If applicable, specify your estimated budget per album.
* **Sample Needed**: Decide if you'd like to see a sample album before proceeding.
* **Additional Requests**: Add any other special requirements or questions you have.
