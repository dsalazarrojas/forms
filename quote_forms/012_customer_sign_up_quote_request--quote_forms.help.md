# Customer Sign Up & Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Sign Up & Quote Request form is used to collect information from customers for a quote request. This form is used internally to better understand customer needs and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer details, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your project, including services required and project needs.
3. Select the services and project needs that apply to your quote request.
4. Enter your contact details, including email and phone number.
5. Specify your quote request details, including quote amount, currency, tax rate, and discount.
6. Confirm your agreement to the quote terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (1)**: Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Project Information (2)**: Provide a brief description of your project to help us understand your needs.
* **Services (3)**: Select the services you require for your project (Accountant, Arbitrator, Consultant, Financial Advisor, Project Manager, Sales).
* **Project Needs (4)**: Select the project needs that apply to your quote request (Accounting, Business Development, Financial Planning, General Accounting, General Consulting, Tax Accounting).
* **Contact Details (5)**: Enter your email and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Quote Request (6)**: Enter the amount of your quote request.
* **Follow-up (7)**: Choose how often you'd like us to follow up with you (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company (10)**: Enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Address (11)**: Enter your business address.
* **Website (12)**: Enter your website address.
* **Notes (13)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your quote request.
* **Date (14)**: Enter the date you need the quote by.
* **Time (15)**: Enter the time you need the quote by.
* **File Upload (16)**: Upload any relevant files or documents related to your quote request.
* **Agreement (17)**: Confirm if you agree to our quote terms and conditions.
* **Quote Details (18)**: Enter any additional information about your quote request.
* **Quote Amount (19)**: Enter the amount of your quote.
* **Quote Currency (20)**: Enter the currency of your quote.
* **Quote Tax Rate (21)**: Enter the tax rate for your quote.
* **Quote Discount (22)**: Enter any discount for your quote.
* **Quote Terms (23)**: Enter any additional terms or conditions for your quote.
* **Quote Note (24)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your quote request.
* **Quote Terms Accepted (25)**: Confirm if you have read and accepted our quote terms and conditions.
