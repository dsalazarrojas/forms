# Hire Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hire Inquiry Form is a collection of fields that helps delivery services gather information from clients about their hiring needs. This form is designed to collect relevant details about the client's pickup address, delivery date and time, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the address where you would like the delivery to be picked up.
2. Choose a date for the delivery.
3. Select a time for the delivery.
4. Enter the name of the delivery driver.
5. Enter the name of the delivery vehicle.
6. Choose the type of delivery (Food, Package, Both, or Other).
7. Select the estimated time it will take for the delivery (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, or More than 4 hours).
8. (Optional) Enter your contact email for the client to reach out to you.
9. (Optional) Enter the phone number of the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the pickup address**: <h1>Enter the address where you would like the delivery to be picked up.</h1>
* **What is the delivery date**: <h1>Choose a date for the delivery.</h1>
* **What is the time of day**: <h1>Select a time for the delivery.</h1>
* **What is the name of the delivery driver**: <h1>Enter the name of the delivery driver.</h1>
* **What is the name of the delivery vehicle**: <h1>Enter the name of the delivery vehicle.</h1>
* **Which type of delivery is this for**: <h1>Choose Food, Package, Both, or Other.</h1>
	+ Select Food for food delivery.
	+ Select Package for package delivery.
	+ Select Both for both food and package delivery.
	+ Select Other for other types of delivery.
* **How far away is the delivery**: <h1>Choose the estimated time it will take for the delivery.</h1>
	+ Less than 1 hour for short distances.
	+ 1-2 hours for medium distances.
	+ 2-4 hours for long distances.
	+ More than 4 hours for very long distances.
* **What is the contact email of the client**: <h1>Enter your contact email for the client to reach out to you.</h1>
* **What is the phone number of the client**: <h1>Enter the phone number of the client.</h1>

# Tips

* Make sure to enter the correct pickup address, as this will affect the delivery process.
* Choose a delivery date and time that suits your needs.
* Select the correct type of delivery and estimated time for the delivery.
* Enter accurate contact information so the client can reach out to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the delivery service directly.
