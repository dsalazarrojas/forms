# Moving Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Form is designed to collect information related to moving, which can be used for tracking or managing relocation or inventory management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Moving Form, please follow these steps:
* Click on the form fields and enter the required information.
* For fields with options, select the relevant option from the dropdown list.
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields, as indicated by the * symbol in the label.
* When finished, click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (moving_form_first_page)**: Enter a brief description of the moving purpose or details.
* **New Home (moving_form_new_home)**: Enter the address of the new location.
* **City (moving_form_city_new)**: Enter the city name of the new location.
* **State (moving_form_state_new)**: Select the state of the new location from the dropdown list.
* **Zip (moving_form_zip_new)**: Enter the zip code of the new location.
* **Country (moving_form_new_country)**: Select the country of the new location from the dropdown list.
* **Old House (moving_form_old_house)**: Enter the address of the old location.
* **Old Street (moving_form_old_street)**: Enter the street name of the old location.
* **Old City (moving_form_old_city)**: Enter the city name of the old location.
* **Old State (moving_form_old_state)**: Select the state of the old location from the dropdown list.
* **Old Zip (moving_form_old_zip)**: Enter the zip code of the old location.
* **Old Country (moving_form_old_country)**: Select the country of the old location from the dropdown list.
* **Date (moving_form_date)**: Enter the moving date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time (moving_form_time)**: Enter the moving time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note (moving_form_note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email (moving_form_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (moving_form_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Info (moving_form_additional_info)**: Enter any additional information about the move.
* **Additional Notes (moving_form_additional_notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Agree (moving_form_agree)**: Select "True" if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit (moving_form_submit)**: Click to complete and submit the form.
