# Project Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Project Submission Form is designed for artists to submit their projects for review and consideration. This form gathers essential information about the project, including its details, location, and status, as well as contact information for the artist. Please note that this form is not a general contact form, and you should not use it to inquire about project status or other non-submission related queries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Project Submission Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Artist Project Submission** page (id: 1).
2. Fill in the **Project Idea** field (id: 2) with a brief description of your project.
3. In the **Supplies** field (id: 3), list any supplies you will need for your project.
4. Enter the **Project Location** (id: 4) where the project will take place.
5. Provide your **Artist Email** (id: 5) and **Artist Phone** (id: 6) for contact purposes.
6. Set the **Start Date** (id: 7) and **End Date** (id: 8) for your project.
7. Choose the **Project Status** (id: 9) from the dropdown menu (Pending, Active, or Complete).
8. Enter your **Artist Name** (id: 10).
9. Add a detailed **Project Description** (id: 11) to describe your project.
10. Attach an image of your project with the **Project Image** field (id: 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Project Submission** (id: 1) (required: false): This is the starting page of the form, where you can introduce your project.
* **Project Idea** (id: 2) (required: false): A brief description of your project, including its concept or idea.
* **Supplies** (id: 3) (required: false): List any necessary supplies for your project, such as materials or equipment.
* **Project Location** (id: 4) (required: false): The location where your project will take place.
* **Artist Email** (id: 5) (required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for project-related inquiries.
* **Artist Phone** (id: 6) (required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for project-related inquiries.
* **Start Date** (id: 7) (required: false): The date when your project starts.
* **End Date** (id: 8) (required: false): The date when your project ends.
* **Project Status** (id: 9) (required: false): Select the current status of your project (Pending, Active, or Complete).
* **Artist Name** (id: 10) (required: false): Enter your name as the artist responsible for the project.
* **Project Description** (id: 11) (required: false): A detailed description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Project Image** (id: 12) (required: false): Upload an image related to your project.
