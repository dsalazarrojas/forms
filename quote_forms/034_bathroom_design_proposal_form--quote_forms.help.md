# Bathroom Design Proposal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their bathroom design needs. It includes questions about their contact and project details, preferred design style, and budget range. The form is used to create a proposal for a bathroom design project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and contact information.
2. Enter your project address and phone number.
3. Select the type of renovation you are interested in, such as a full remodel or a cosmetic update.
4. Choose your preferred design style from the options provided.
5. Check any key features you would like to include in your project, such as a free-standing bathtub or heated flooring.
6. Specify your estimated budget range.
7. Describe any main issues you are experiencing with your current bathroom.
8. Select a start date for your project.
9. Provide any reference images of your current bathroom or design inspiration.
10. Choose a time slot for an on-site consultation, if desired.
11. Let us know how you heard about us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the project location.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Type of Renovation** (`renovation_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of renovation you are interested in, such as:
	+ Full Remodel (Gut and Replace)
	+ Cosmetic Update (Surface only)
	+ Expansion (New footprint)
	+ New Construction
* **Preferred Design Style** (`design_style_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred design style:
	+ Modern/Minimalist
	+ Traditional/Classic
	+ Industrial
	+ Farmhouse/Rustic
	+ Bohemian
	+ Mid-Century Modern
* **Key Features to Include** (`key_features_requested`, select_multiple, optional): Select all features you would like to include in your project, such as:
	+ Free-standing Bathtub
	+ Walk-in Shower with Glass Door
	+ Double Vanity
	+ Heated Flooring
	+ Smart Toilet/Bidet
	+ Recessed Lighting
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Choose your estimated budget range:
	+ $5,000 - $10,000
	+ $10,000 - $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $50,000
	+ $50,000+
* **What are the main issues with your current bathroom?** (`current_bathroom_issues`, text, required): Describe any main issues you are experiencing with your current bathroom, such as lack of storage or poor lighting.
* **When would you like to start?** (`anticipated_start_date`, date, required): Select a start date for your project.
* **-- Reference Images --** (`photo_note`, note, required): Email any inspiration images or current bathroom photos to `design@remodelers.com`.
* **Best time for an on-site consultation** (`best_time_for_consult`, select_one, optional): Choose a time slot for an on-site consultation:
	+ Weekday Mornings
	+ Weekday Afternoons
	+ Weekends
* **How did you hear about us?** (`hearing_source`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about us:
	+ Social Media
	+ Google Search
	+ Referral
	+ Local Advertisement
* **Today Date** (`proposal_date`, date, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled in order to complete the form, while the optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
