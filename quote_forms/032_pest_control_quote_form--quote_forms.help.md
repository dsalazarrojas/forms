# pest_control_quote_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide information about their pest control needs to the service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:
- Please fill out all required fields.
- You can leave optional fields empty if they are not relevant to your situation.
- Be as concise as possible when filling out the form, especially when providing descriptions or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page** (text, required: false): This is the first page of the form, and you can use it to enter any relevant information about your pest control needs or concerns.
  - **Customer Info** (text, required: false): Please enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant contact information so we can get in touch with you.
  - **Pest Control Services** (select multiple, required: false): Choose all the services you are interested in, such as "Yes" for each service that applies to you.
  - **Pest Description** (text, required: false): Describe the pest control issue you are experiencing, including the location and severity.
  - **Treatement Cost** (number, required: false): Estimate the cost of the treatment you are interested in, including any additional costs such as equipment rental or travel fees.
  - **Payment Info** (text, required: false): Provide any necessary payment information, such as method of payment or billing address.
  - **Payment Terms** (date, required: false): If you have any specific payment terms or deadlines, such as a due date for payment, please enter the date here.
  - **Notes** (text, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your pest control needs.

## Tips
- Please be as thorough as possible when filling out the form to ensure the service provider can prepare an accurate quote.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us after submitting the form.
- This form is for informational purposes only, and a quote will be provided based on your submitted information.
