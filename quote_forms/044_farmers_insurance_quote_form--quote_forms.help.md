## Farmers Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmers Insurance Quote Form is used to gather information about the user's insurance needs so that we can provide a quote for their insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your home address to help us understand your location.
2. Enter your name and date of birth to help us verify your identity.
3. Enter your phone number and email address to contact you.
4. Select the type of insurance coverage you need.
5. Enter your vehicle information, including year, make, and model.
6. Enter your vehicle's license plate number and state.
7. Select your vehicle type.
8. Enter your vehicle's mileage and insurance type.
9. Enter the year you built your home.
10. Enter your home's square footage and value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your home address where you live.
* **Name and Date of Birth** (`name_and_date_of_birth`, text, required): Enter your name and date of birth to help us verify your identity.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Coverage Type** (`coverage_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of insurance coverage you need.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter your vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's license plate number and state.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you own.
* **Vehicle Miles** (`vehicle_miles`, number, required): Enter your vehicle's mileage.
* **Vehicle Insurance Type** (`vehicle_insurance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of insurance for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Use Yearly** (`vehicle_use_yearly`, number, required): Enter the year you use your vehicle.
* **Home Zip** (`home_zip`, number, required): Enter your home zip code.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of home you live in.
* **Home Square Footage** (`home_square_footage`, text, required): Enter your home's square footage.
* **Year Built** (`home_year_built`, number, required): Enter the year your home was built.
* **Home Value** (`home_value`, text, required): Enter the value of your home.
* **Vehicle Value** (`vehicle_value`, text, required): Enter the value of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Cost** (`vehicle_cost`, text, required): Enter the cost of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Insurance Cost** (`vehicle_insurance_cost`, text, required): Enter the cost of your vehicle's insurance.
* **Total Insurance** (`total_insurance`, text, required): Enter the total insurance cost.
* **Monthly Premium** (`premium`, text, required): Enter the monthly premium for your insurance.
* **Quote** (`quote`, note, required): This is the quote for your insurance coverage.
 
## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible quote. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
