# Audio Equipment Supplier Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide the supplier with information about your audio equipment needs and requirements. This will help the supplier tailor their response to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Make sure to answer all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (*):** Company Name (`company_name`, `text`, `true`)
    Enter your company's name or organization's name.
* **Contact Full Name (*):** Name of primary contact person (`contact_full_name`, `text`, `true`)
    Enter the full name of the person who will be the primary contact for this inquiry.
* **Email Address (*):** Contact email for quote delivery (`contact_email`, `email`, `true`)
    Enter the email address to which you would like to receive quotes and updates.
* **Phone Number (*):** Contact phone number (`contact_phone`, `text`, `true`)
    Enter the phone number to contact you.
* **Company Address (*):** Full mailing address (`company_address`, `text`, `true`)
    Enter your company's full mailing address.
* **Inquiry Date (*):** Date of this quote request (`inquiry_date`, `date`, `true`)
    Enter the date you would like to receive a quote for the equipment.
* **Equipment Category (*):** Primary category of equipment needed (`equipment_category`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select the primary category of audio equipment you are interested in.
* **Specific Products:** List specific products or models of interest (`specific_products`, `text`, `false`)
    Enter any specific products or models of audio equipment you are interested in.
* **Quantity Needed (*):** Approximate quantity required (`quantity_needed`, `number`, `true`)
    Enter an approximate quantity of the equipment you need.
* **Budget Range (*):** Estimated budget for this purchase (`budget_range`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select the estimated budget range for the equipment you are interested in.
* **Intended Use (*):** What will the equipment be used for (`intended_use`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select the primary intended use of the equipment.
* **Timeline (*):** When do you need the equipment (`timeline`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select when you need the equipment.
* **Installation Required:** Do you need installation services (`installation_required`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select whether you need installation services for the equipment.
* **Training Required:** Do you need training on the equipment (`training_required`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select whether you need training on the equipment.
* **Warranty Preference:** Preferred warranty coverage (`warranty_preference`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select your preferred warranty coverage for the equipment.
* **Financing Interest:** Are you interested in financing options (`financing_interest`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select whether you are interested in financing options for the equipment.
* **Trade-In Equipment:** Do you have equipment to trade-in (`trade_in_equipment`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select whether you have equipment to trade-in.
* **Additional Requirements:** Any other specifications or requirements (`additional_requirements`, `text`, `false`)
    Enter any other specifications or requirements for the equipment.
* **Preferred Contact Method:** How should we contact you (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, `true`)
    Select the preferred contact method for communication.
* **Best Contact Time:** Best time to reach you (`best_contact_time`, `select_one`, `false`)
    Enter the best time to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
* Double-check the accuracy of your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional requirements or specifications, please enter them in the "Additional Requirements" field.
