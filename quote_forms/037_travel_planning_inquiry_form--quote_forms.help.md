# travel_planning_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This travel planning inquiry form is designed to collect essential details from users for travel planning purposes. It covers various fields related to travel planning, including destination, dates, travel service provider, number of travelers, special requests, preferred language, contact information, travel agency details, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the travel planning inquiry form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the destination you plan to visit.
2. Provide the dates of your travel.
3. Choose your preferred language for travel planning.
4. Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
5. If you are representing a travel agency, provide their details, including name, phone number, email, and website.
6. Add any special requests or notes related to your travel planning.
7. Select the type of travel you plan to undertake (e.g., adventure, business, leisure, or relaxation).
8. Choose the travel style that suits your preferences (e.g., beach, cultural, history, relaxation, or urban).
9. Enter the status of your travel planning.
10. Complete the form with the required fields marked as 'Required'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination** (`destination`, `text`, required): Enter the location you plan to visit.
* **Dates** (`dates`, `date`, required): Enter the start and end dates of your travel.
* **Travel Service Provider** (`travel_service_provider`, `text`, optional): If you are working with a travel service provider, provide their name.
* **Number of Travelers** (`number_of_travelers`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people traveling with you.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Add any specific requests or notes related to your travel planning.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred language for travel planning.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a travel agency, enter their name.
* **Travel Agency Phone** (`travel_agency_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Email** (`travel_agency_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Website** (`travel_agency_website`, `text`, optional): Enter the website of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Address** (`travel_agency_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the travel agency.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to your travel planning.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): If this form is assigned to a specific person, enter their name.
* **Status** (`status`, `text`, required): Select the status of your travel planning (e.g., in progress, completed, pending).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at_reported_at`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments related to your travel planning.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of travel you plan to undertake (e.g., adventure, business, leisure, or relaxation).
* **Travel Style** (`travel_style`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the travel style that suits your preferences (e.g., beach, cultural, history, relaxation, or urban).
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (e.g., destination, dates, preferred language, status).
* Choose the correct type and style of travel that suits your preferences.
* If you are representing a travel agency, provide their details accurately.
* Add any special requests or notes related to your travel planning.
* Complete the form with the required fields marked as 'Required'.
