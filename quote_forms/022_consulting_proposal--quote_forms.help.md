# Consulting Proposal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and present a consulting proposal to a client. It will guide the user to fill out a comprehensive proposal that outlines the scope, timeline, and costs associated with a proposed project, including terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a sequential manner, one page at a time.
2. Start with the proposal date and internal proposal number, followed by your company's information (name, address, contact email, and phone number).
3. Enter the client's company name, contact name, title, email, and phone number.
4. Provide a detailed description of the project, including the objectives, scope of work, deliverables, and timeline.
5. Specify the estimated cost and payment terms.
6. Review and sign the proposal as the consultant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Proposal Date** (`proposal_date`, date): The date when this proposal is submitted to the client.
* **2. Proposal Number** (`proposal_number`, text): A unique internal reference number for the proposal.
* **3. Your Company Name** (`consultant_company_name`, text): Name of your consulting firm.
* **4. Your Company Address** (`consultant_address`, text): Street address of your company.
* **5. City** (`consultant_city`, text): City where your company is located.
* **6. State or Province** (`consultant_state`, text): State or province where your company is located.
* **7. ZIP or Postal Code** (`consultant_zip`, text): Postal code of your company's address.
* **8. Contact Email** (`consultant_email`, email): Email address of your company's contact person.
* **9. Contact Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text): Phone number of your company's contact person.
* **10. Client Company Name** (`client_company_name`, text): Name of the client organization.
* **11. Client Contact Name** (`client_contact_name`, text): Primary contact person's name at the client organization.
* **12. Client Contact Title** (`client_title`, text): Job title of the client contact person.
* **13. Client Email** (`client_email`, email): Email address of the client contact person.
* **14. Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text): Phone number of the client contact person.
* **15. Client Address** (`client_address`, text): Street address of the client organization.
* **16. Project Title** (`project_title`, text): Name of the proposed project.
* **17. Project Description** (`project_description`, text): Detailed description of the project.
* **18. Company Background** (`company_background`, text): Brief background about your firm.
* **19. Project Objectives** (`project_objectives`, text): What this project will achieve.
* **20. Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`, text): Detailed description of the work to be performed.
* **21. Deliverables** (`deliverables`, text): List of expected deliverables.
* **22. Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date): Proposed start date of the project.
* **23. Project Completion Date** (`project_completion_date`, date): Expected completion date of the project.
* **24. Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, text): Key milestones and timeline of the project.
* **25. Hourly Rate** (`hourly_rate`, number): Rate per hour in dollars.
* **26. Estimated Hours** (`estimated_hours`, number): Total estimated hours for the project.
* **27. Fixed Project Fee** (`fixed_fee`, number): Total fixed fee if applicable.
* **28. Additional Costs** (`additional_costs`, number): Any additional expenses.
* **29. Total Project Cost** (`total_project_cost`, number): Total estimated cost of the project.
* **30. Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one): Select one of the following payment terms:
	+ 50% Deposit, 50% on Completion
	+ 30% Deposit, 40% Mid-Project, 30% on Completion
	+ Monthly Invoicing
	+ Net 15
	+ Net 30
	+ Net 45
	+ Upon Completion
* **31. Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, text): Detailed payment schedule.
* **32. Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text): Additional terms and conditions.
* **33. Proposal Validity Period** (`proposal_validity`, select_one): Select one of the following periods:
	+ 15 Days
	+ 30 Days
	+ 45 Days
	+ 60 Days
	+ 90 Days
* **34. Consultant Signature** (`consultant_signature`, text): Type full name as signature.
* **35. Consultant Signature Date** (`consultant_signature_date`, date): Date signed.
* **36. Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text): Type full name as signature.
* **37. Client Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, date): Date signed by client.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the proposal.
- Review the payment terms carefully and ensure they align with your company's policies.
- Clearly explain the project objectives, scope, and deliverables to the client.
- Attach any necessary attachments or supporting documents to the proposal.
- Make sure to sign the proposal and have the client sign as well.
