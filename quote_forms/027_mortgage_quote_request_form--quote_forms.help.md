# Mortgage Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a mortgage quote from a lender and provide necessary information for the quote process. It collects data from both the client and lender sides, ensuring a smooth and efficient quoting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category for your quote request (Quote Forms or Request Forms).
2. Enter a brief description of your request (optional).
3. Fill in your client data, including phone number and email.
4. Input the desired quote date and time.
5. Select the quote provider from the available options.
6. Choose the status of your quote (New, In Progress, or Done).
7. Enter the quote ID.
8. Set the quote date and time.
9. Enter the quote value (optional).
10. Enter any additional information or notes.
11. Upload any related documents (optional).
12. Enter the lender's message to the client.
13. Enter the client's message back to the lender.
14. Enter the lender's signature (optional).
15. Enter the client's signature (optional).
16. Select the created date and time.
