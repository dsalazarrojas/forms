# Corporate Vehicle Rental Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a quote request for corporate vehicle rental. It is designed to gather essential information from clients or customers to understand their rental needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on "Corporate Vehicle Rental Quote Request" to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
   - Type: Choose "Yes" if you require a vehicle rental or "No" if not.
   - Make and Model: Enter the make and model of the vehicle required.
   - Name: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
   - Email: Enter your email address for correspondence.
   - Phone: Enter your phone number for contact.
   - Start Date and End Date: Select the dates for the vehicle rental period.
   - Duration: Enter the number of days for the rental period.
   - Additional Info (optional): Provide any additional information for the quote request.
   - Upload File (optional): You can upload any supporting documents or files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type** (`yes/no`, select_one): This field helps determine whether you require a vehicle rental or not.
* **Make** (`vehicle_make`, text): Enter the make of the vehicle you want to rent.
* **Model** (`vehicle_model`, text): Enter the model of the vehicle you want to rent.
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter your email address for correspondence with us.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, date): Select the date when you plan to pick up the vehicle.
* **End Date** (`rental_end_date`, date): Select the date when you plan to return the vehicle.
* **Duration** (`rental_duration`, number): Enter the number of days for the rental period.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to understand your needs better.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text): You can upload any supporting documents or files that might be necessary for your quote request.

Note: Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that we can process your quote request properly.
