# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a dog-friendly meet and greet session. It's intended for users to input details about their pet, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable meet and greet experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your pet's name in the **pet_name** field.
2. Select your pet's type from the **pet_type** dropdown menu (e.g., dog, cat, other).
3. Enter the date of the meet and greet session in the **meet_date** field.
4. Enter the time of the meet and greet session in the **meet_time** field.
5. Provide your contact information in the **contact_info** field.
6. Select the size of your pet from the **dog_size** dropdown menu (e.g., small, medium, large).
7. Enter the number of people walking your pet in the **number_of_walkers** field.
8. Enter the number of pets in attendance in the **number_of_pets** field.
9. Enter the number of children attending in the **number_of_children** field.
10. Enter the number of guests attending in the **number_of_guests** field.
11. Provide your dog walker's information in the **dog_walker_info** field.
12. Enter the meet location in the **meet_location** field.
13. Enter the meet duration in the **meet_duration** field (in minutes).
14. Select any pet licenses required (e.g., dog license, cat license) from the **pet_license** dropdown menu.
15. Add any additional notes about your pet in the **additional_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name (Text)**: Enter your pet's name.
* **pet_type (Select One)**: Select the type of your pet (e.g., dog, cat, other).
* **meet_date (Date)**: Enter the date of the meet and greet session.
* **meet_time (Time)**: Enter the time of the meet and greet session.
* **contact_info (Text)**: Provide your contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **dog_size (Select One)**: Select the size of your pet (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **number_of_walkers (Number)**: Enter the number of people walking your pet.
* **number_of_pets (Number)**: Enter the number of pets attending the meet and greet session.
* **number_of_children (Number)**: Enter the number of children attending the meet and greet session.
* **number_of_guests (Number)**: Enter the number of guests attending the meet and greet session.
* **dog_walker_info (Text)**: Provide your dog walker's information (e.g., name, number).
* **meet_location (Text)**: Enter the location of the meet and greet session.
* **meet_duration (Number)**: Enter the duration of the meet and greet session in minutes.
* **pet_license (Select Multiple)**: Select any pet licenses required (e.g., dog license, cat license).
* **additional_notes (Note)**: Add any additional notes about your pet.
