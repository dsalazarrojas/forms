# Paper Supply Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential details for paper supply quote requests from customers or end-users. It gathers necessary information such as category selection, item specifics, and contact details to facilitate efficient processing and quotation generation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your paper supply quote request from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the item number, name, and other requested details in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the price range of the item.
4. Specify the quantity required.
5. Enter the unit cost for the item.
6. Optionally, you can include the total cost, delivery date, delivery time, contact name, email, and phone number for further reference.
7. Add any additional comments for the quote request, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Required):
	+ Select the correct category for your paper supply quote request.
* **Item Number** (Optional):
	+ Enter the item number, if available.
* **Item Name** (Required):
	+ Enter the name of the item you are inquiring about.
* **Price Range** (Required):
	+ Enter the price range for the item (e.g., 1-10, $10-20).
* **Quantity** (Required):
	+ Specify the quantity of the item you require.
* **Unit Cost** (Required):
	+ Enter the unit cost for the item.
* **Total Cost** (Optional):
	+ If known, enter the total cost for the quote.
* **Delivery Date** (Optional):
	+ Enter the preferred delivery date for the item.
* **Delivery Time** (Optional):
	+ Enter the preferred delivery time for the item.
* **Contact Name** (Required):
	+ Enter your name and affiliation (if applicable) for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Optional):
	+ Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (Optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Comments** (Optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes for the quote request.

Note: Fields marked as "Required" must be filled before submitting the form.
