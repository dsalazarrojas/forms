# Auto Repair Service Quote Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide essential information for an auto repair quote. It's essential to complete this form accurately, so we can provide the best possible service and estimate for your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2.  Enter your email address so we can contact you with the quote and updates.
3.  Provide your phone number for follow-up questions and confirmation.
4.  Enter your vehicle's make, model, and year accurately.
5.  Enter your vehicle's VIN (if known).
6.  Select all the services you require from the list provided.
7.  Describe any specific symptoms or requests you have for the vehicle.
8.  Choose your preferred service date.
9.  Select your preferred method for managing the service (drop-off, wait at the shop, or shuttle).
10. If you have a budget in mind, provide it to help us offer suitable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name
*   **Full Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. Email Address
*   **Email Address** (email): This will be used to contact you with the estimate and updates.

### 3. Phone Number
*   **Phone Number** (text): This is for follow-up questions and confirmation.

### 4. Vehicle Make, Model, and Year
*   **Vehicle Information** (text): Enter your vehicle's make, model, and year accurately.

### 5. VIN
*   **VIN** (text): Enter your vehicle's 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number if known.

### 6. Services Requested
*   **Services Requested** (select multiple): Select all the services you require from the list provided.

### 7. Specific Symptoms or Requests
*   **Specific Symptoms or Requests** (text): Describe any specific symptoms or requests you have for the vehicle.

### 8. Preferred Service Date
*   **Preferred Service Date** (date): Enter your requested date for the repair.

### 9. Drop-off or Wait Preference
*   **Drop-off or Wait Preference** (select one): Choose how you plan to manage the service (drop-off, wait at the shop, or shuttle).

### 10. Budget Range (Optional)
*   **Budget Range (Optional)** (text): If you have a budget in mind, provide it to help us offer suitable options.

Tips:

*   Be as accurate as possible when entering information for the vehicle and services required.
*   If you're unsure about any of the services, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Provide any additional information that may be helpful for a more accurate estimate.
