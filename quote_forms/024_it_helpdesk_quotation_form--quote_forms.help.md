# IT Helpdesk Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for IT helpdesk quotations. It will help the IT department gather the necessary details to provide a quote for the requested services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Phone** in the third field.
4. Select the **Service Type** from the options provided in the fourth field.
5. Describe the **Description** of the service you require in the fifth field.
6. Enter the **Price** you expect to pay for the service in the sixth field.
7. Provide a brief **Quote** in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who is requesting the quote.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer who is requesting the quote.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer who is requesting the quote.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of service you require from the options provided.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the service you require in detail.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price you expect to pay for the service.
* **Quote** (`quote`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief quote for the service you require.

## Tips

- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- Check that the form is completed for each service request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the IT department.
