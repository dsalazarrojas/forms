# Commercial Cleaning Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for creating a commercial cleaning quote for a business. It helps to collect essential information from the customer to provide an accurate and comprehensive quote for the services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the company name to provide us with a clear identification of the business.
* Enter the primary contact person's name and contact email address to ensure we can reach them for any questions or clarifications.
* Enter the contact phone number as the best number to reach the contact. Note that this field is not required, but it's highly recommended for efficient communication.
* Choose the type of premises that applies to the customer's location (e.g., Office, Retail, Industrial, Medical facility, or Educational facility).
* Enter the approximate square footage of the premises. Please provide whole numbers only.
* Select all the cleaning services that the customer requires (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Deep, Carpet, Window, and Disinfection services).
* Choose the earliest start date for the cleaning services.
* Select the desired service frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, or One time deep clean).
* Provide any special instructions or access notes that may impact the cleaning services (e.g., parking, gate codes, or hazards).
* Choose an estimate of the budget range to help us provide an accurate quote. This will help us understand the customer's expectations and tailor the quote accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (Company name, text, required): Enter the name of the business to identify it for our records.
* **Contact person name** (Contact person name, text, required): Enter the primary contact person's name for scheduling and questions.
* **Contact email address** (Contact email address, email, required): Enter the email address where the quote should be sent.
* **Contact phone number** (Contact phone number, text, optional): Enter the best number to reach the contact for any questions or clarifications.
* **Type of premises** (Type of premises, select_one, required): Choose the category that applies to the customer's location (e.g., Office, Retail, Industrial, Medical facility, or Educational facility).
* **Approximate square footage** (Approximate square footage, number, required): Enter the approximate square footage of the premises. Please provide whole numbers only.
* **Services required** (Services required, select_multiple, required): Select all the cleaning services that the customer requires (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Deep, Carpet, Window, and Disinfection services).
* **Preferred start date** (Preferred start date, date, optional): Enter the earliest date to begin the cleaning services.
* **Service frequency** (Service frequency, select_one, required): Choose the desired frequency for the cleaning services (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, or One time deep clean).
* **Special instructions or access notes** (Special instructions or access notes, text, optional): Provide any special instructions or access notes that may impact the cleaning services (e.g., parking, gate codes, or hazards).
* **Budget range estimate** (Budget range estimate, select_one, optional): Choose an estimate of the budget range to help us provide an accurate quote.
