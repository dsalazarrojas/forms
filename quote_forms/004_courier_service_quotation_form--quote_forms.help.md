# Courier Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Courier Service Quotation Form" is designed to capture information from clients about their courier service requests. This form helps in providing a clear and concise overview of the client's requirements and allows the courier service provider to offer a suitable price quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the service type from the available options (courier service, freight forwarding, express mail, or courier forwarding).
3. Provide a brief description of the service details in the "Service Details" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the service request.
5. Enter the duration of the service required.
6. Enter the price estimate for the service.
7. Enter the client's address for the courier service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of service required (courier service, freight forwarding, express mail, or courier forwarding).
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the service required.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the service request.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the service request.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the service required.
* **Price Estimate** (`price_estimate`, number, optional): Enter an estimate of the price for the service.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address for the courier service.
