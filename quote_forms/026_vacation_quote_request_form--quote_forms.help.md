# Vacation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their vacation preferences and needs, allowing your company to provide personalized quotes for their trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Details** (Page 1).
2. Enter your **Contact Details** (Page 2).
3. Share your **Vacation Preferences** (Page 3).
4. Select your **Room Preferences** (Page 4).
5. Specify your **Transportation Requests** (Page 5).
6. Choose your **Vacation Dates** (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details (Page 1)
* **Customer Details** (required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details to help us get in touch with you.

### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **Contact Details** (required): Enter your phone number and email address so we can reach you for further questions or updates.

### Vacation Preferences (Page 3)
* **Vacation Preferences** (required): Describe your ideal vacation destination, type, and length of stay.

### Room Preferences (Page 4)
* **Room Preferences** (optional): Select the type of accommodation you would like to stay in during your vacation.

### Transportation Requests (Page 5)
* **Transportation Requests** (required): Specify any transportation needs you may have, such as airport pickups or car rentals.

### Vacation Dates (Page 6)
* **Vacation Dates** (required): Choose the dates you would like to go on your vacation.
