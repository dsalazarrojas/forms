# Free Bid Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by the bid team to collect and review project details from clients before submitting their proposal. It helps gather essential information about the project's date, scope, estimated cost, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the project date (page 1).
2. Enter the start date of the project (page 2).
3. Describe the project in detail (page 3).
4. Outline the project's scope of work (page 4).
5. Estimate the project's cost (page 5).
6. Specify the payment terms (page 6).
7. Enter any additional project notes (page 7).
8. Outline the payment schedule (page 8).
9. Enter the client's address (page 9).
10. Provide payment details (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Date** (page 1): Enter the date of the project.
* **Start Date of Project** (page 2): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project Description** (page 3): Briefly describe the project.
* **Scope of Work** (page 4): Outline the project's scope of work.
* **Estimated Cost** (page 5): Estimate the project's cost.
* **Payment** (page 6): Specify the payment terms.
* **Project Completion Date** (page 7): Enter the project completion date.
* **Project Status** (page 8): Select the project status (Complete, In Progress, Not Started, or Pending).
* **Contractor Name** (page 9): Enter the contractor's name.
* **Bid Proposal Form** (page 10): Enter any relevant bid proposal form information.
* **Project Budget** (page 11): Estimate the project budget.
* **Payment Terms** (page 12): Specify the payment terms.
* **Project Notes** (page 13): Enter any additional project notes.
* **Payment Schedule** (page 14): Enter the payment schedule.
* **Client Address** (page 15): Enter the client's address.
* **Payment Details** (page 16): Provide payment details.

Note: All fields are optional except for Project Status, which is required.
