# online_order_form_without_payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit their service requests without requiring payment at the moment. It helps salon staff to gather necessary information and process orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Make sure to select a service from the "Service" field.
3. Select the correct payment type from the "Payment Type" field.
4. Optionally, you can provide additional notes for the customer and the staff member processing their order in the "Customer Note" and "Employee Note" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Name** (`salon_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the salon where you would like to receive the service.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to have the service performed.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your service request.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your home or business address where the service will be performed.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the service will be performed.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, select_one, required): Select the state where the service will be performed from the provided options.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where the service will be performed.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where the service will be performed.
* **Service** (`service`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of service you would like to receive from the provided options.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about the service you selected.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required): Select the payment type you would like to use from the provided options.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes for the staff member processing your order.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member who will be fulfilling your order.
* **Employee Note** (`employee_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes for the staff member fulfilling your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Customer State, Service, and Payment Type).
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
