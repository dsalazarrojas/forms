# seo-optimization-quotation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for SEO optimization purposes. It is used to gather data about a company's SEO services, website information, target audience, SEO approach, keywords, content creation, content promotion, analytics tracking, and budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Company Info** field with a brief description of your company.
2. Select one or more options from the **Seo Services** field to indicate the services you require.
3. Fill in the **Website Info** field with details about your website.
4. Select one or more options from the **Target Audience** field to indicate your target audience.
5. Choose one option from the **Seo Approach** field to indicate your SEO approach.
6. Enter a list of **Keywords** relevant to your business.
7. Select one or more options from the **Analytics Tracking** field to indicate your analytics tracking requirements.
8. Enter a value in the **Content Creation** field to indicate the amount of content you require.
9. Enter a value in the **Content Promotion** field to indicate the promotion strategy you require.
10. Select one option from the **Budget** field to indicate your budget.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Info** (Company Info, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Seo Services** (Seo Services, select_one, required/optional): Select one or more services you require, such as SEO audit, keyword research, or content optimization.
* **Website Info** (Website Info, text, required/optional): Enter details about your website, such as URL, domain, and content.
* **Target Audience** (Target Audience, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more target audience options, such as B2B, B2C, or international.
* **Seo Approach** (Seo Approach, select_one, required/optional): Choose one SEO approach, such as technical, creative, or strategic.
* **Keywords** (Keywords, text, required/optional): Enter a list of relevant keywords for your business.
* **Content Creation** (Content Creation, number, required/optional): Enter the number of pieces of content you require, such as articles, videos, or social media posts.
* **Content Promotion** (Content Promotion, number, required/optional): Enter the promotion strategy you require, such as email marketing, social media advertising, or paid advertising.
* **Analytics Tracking** (Analytics Tracking, select_one, required/optional): Select one analytics tracking option, such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, or Adobe Analytics.
* **Budget** (Budget, text, required/optional): Enter your budget for the project.
