# Home Renovation Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for home renovation quote requests. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information so we can get back to you.
2. Give us an overview of your project, including the area(s) of your home you'd like to renovate.
3. Let us know what kind of renovation you're looking to do (e.g. kitchen, bathroom, whole house).
4. Enter your estimated budget for the project.
5. Schedule the start and end dates of your project.
6. Let us know the current status of your project (if applicable).
7. Add any additional information that might be helpful to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Person**: *Required* - Please select the person we should contact in regards to your project.
  - Person 1
  - Person 2
  - Person 3
- **Project Type**: *Optional* - Select the type of renovation you're looking to do.
  - Kitchen Renovation
  - Bathroom Renovation
  - Whole House
- **House Details**: *Optional* - Provide a description of the area(s) of your home you're renovating.
- **Estimated Budget**: *Optional* - Enter the estimated budget for your project.
- **Start Date**: *Optional* - Schedule the start date of your project.
- **End Date**: *Optional* - Schedule the end date of your project.
- **Project Status**: *Optional* - Provide the current status of your project.
  - In Progress
  - Completed
  - Cancelled
- **Additional Info**: *Optional* - Add any additional information that might be helpful to us.
- **Contact Method**: *Optional* - Select how you'd like to be contacted.
  - Email
  - Phone
  - Text Message
- **Email**: *Optional* - Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: *Optional* - Enter your phone number.
- **Address**: *Optional* - Enter your address.
- **City**: *Optional* - Enter your city.
- **Zip Code**: *Optional* - Enter your zip code.
- **Country**: *Optional* - Enter your country.
- **State**: *Optional* - Enter your state.
- **Country Code**: *Optional* - Enter your country code.
- **Notes**: *Optional* - Add any notes about your project.
- **Form ID**: *Optional* - Enter our form ID.
- **Assigned Tool**: *Optional* - Enter the tool assigned to your project.
- **Submission Date**: *Optional* - Enter the date you submitted this form.
- **Created Date**: *Optional* - Enter the date this form was created.
- **Updated Date**: *Optional* - Enter the date this form was last updated.
