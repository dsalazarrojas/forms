# Free Zone Company Registration Cost Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help entrepreneurs and business owners estimate the cost of registering their company in a free zone. It collects information about the company and its preferences to provide a detailed quote or estimate for the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each category as it applies to your company.
2. Enter the Company Name, Zip Code, Phone number, and Email.
3. Choose the desired Date of Incorporation and Number of Shares.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the other categories as it applies to your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Free Zone Company Registration** (Free Zone Company Registration, text, no): This is the main inquiry section. Please describe your company in a few words.
* **Category** (Category, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if you have any specific category, otherwise 'No'.
* **State** (State, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if the company is registered in any state, otherwise 'No'.
* **Country** (Country, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if the company is registered in any country, otherwise 'No'.
* **Industry** (Industry, select_multiple, no): Select all relevant industries that apply to your company.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, no): Enter the full name of your company.
* **Company Type** (Company Type, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if the company is a type that is applicable for free zone registration, otherwise 'No'.
* **Address** (Address, text, no): Enter the physical address of your company office or residence.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, no): Enter the zip code of the company's physical address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, no): Enter the contact phone number of your company.
* **Email** (Email, email, no): Enter the contact email of your company.
* **Date of Incorporation** (Date of Incorporation, date, no): Choose the desired date of incorporation.
* **Number of Shares** (Number of Shares, number, no): Enter the number of shares your company has.
* **Authorized Capital** (Authorized Capital, number, no): Enter the authorized capital of your company.
* **Paid-Up Capital** (Paid-Up Capital, number, no): Enter the paid-up capital of your company.
* **Business Activity** (Business Activity, select_multiple, no): Select all relevant activities that apply to your company.
* **Tax Residency** (Tax Residency, select_multiple, no): Select 'Yes' if your company is tax resident in the country, otherwise 'No'.
* **Shareholding** (Shareholding, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if your company has shareholding, otherwise 'No'.
* **Free Zone Type** (Free Zone Type, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if the company is applicable for free zone, otherwise 'No'.
* **Company Status** (Company Status, select_one, no): Select 'Active' if the company is active, otherwise 'Inactive'.
* **Registration Type** (Registration Type, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if the company is registered, otherwise 'No'.
* **Free Zone** (Free Zone, select_one, no): Select 'Yes' if the company is registered in a free zone, otherwise 'No'.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to get an accurate quote.
* Please note that this form is for estimation purposes and may not reflect the actual registration cost.
* If you have any questions or doubts, please contact our support team for further assistance.
