# Dining Table Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect inquiries from customers interested in buying a dining table product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required information in each field. You can skip any field that is not relevant to your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`, text, optional)
Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name (`last_name`, text, optional)
Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (`email`, email, optional)
Enter a valid email address that we can use to follow up on your inquiry.

### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your primary phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Date Of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. This field is required for certain age verification checks, but it is still optional in this form.
