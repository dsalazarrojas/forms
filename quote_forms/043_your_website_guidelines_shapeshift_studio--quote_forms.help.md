# Your Website Guidelines Shapeshift Studio - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from users to create a website guidelines document for a project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your website's details.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your website's URL.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Fill out the project's details, including a brief description and the budget.
6. Select the priority of the project (Low, Medium, or High).
7. Fill out the deadline for the project.
8. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Website Details** (`website_details`, text, optional): Enter the name of your website.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
- **URL** (`url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your website.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the project.
- **Project Description** (`project_details_description`, text, optional): Enter a more detailed description of the project.
- **Budget** (`project_details_budget`, number, optional): Enter the project's budget.
- **Deadline** (`project_details_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the project's deadline.
- **Priority** (`project_details_priority`, select_one, optional): Select the project's priority (Low, Medium, or High).
