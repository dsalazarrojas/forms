# Client Database Software Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from potential clients to prepare an accurate quote for a client database software solution. Please complete the form with the required details to help us better understand your needs and provide a tailored quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your official business name in the `Company Name` field.
2. Enter the name of the person responsible for the project in the `Primary Contact Name` field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the `Contact Email` field.
4. Enter the best phone number to reach the contact person in the `Contact Phone` field (optional).
5. Select the approximate number of employees in your company in the `Company Size` field.
6. If you're currently using a database solution, describe it in the `Current Database Solution` field (optional).
7. Select the features that are important for your organization in the `Required Features` field. You can choose multiple options.
8. Select an approximate budget range for the initial implementation in the `Budget Range` field.
9. Choose a desired timeline for the implementation in the `Desired Timeline` field.
10. Add any additional information or comments in the `Additional Information or Comments` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your official business name.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Provide an email address for follow-up.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, optional): Enter the best phone number to reach the contact person.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, required): Select the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Current Database Solution** (`current_database_solution`, optional): If you're currently using a database solution, describe it here.
* **Required Features** (`required_features`, required): Choose the features that are essential for your organization. Select multiple options if applicable.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, required): Select an approximate budget range for the initial implementation.
* **Desired Timeline** (`desired_timeline`, required): Choose a desired timeline for the implementation.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your requirements.
