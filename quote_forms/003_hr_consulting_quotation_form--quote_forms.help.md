# HR Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document HR consulting quotations for clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide client information in the Client Information page.
2. Enter job details on the Job Details page.
3. Select the quotation status, date, time, and currency on the relevant pages.
4. Indicate whether the quotation terms are accepted on the Quotation Terms page.
5. Enter any additional notes on the Quotation Notes page.
6. Enter the client's email address on the Client Email page.
7. Enter the client's phone number on the Client Phone page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter your client's name and other relevant details.
* **Job Details**: Enter a brief description of the job specifics.
* **Quotation Status**: Select the status of the quotation, which can be either HR or Non-HR.
* **Quotation Date**: Select the date of the quotation, in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Quotation Time**: Select the time of the quotation, in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Quotation Total**: Enter the total cost of the quotation in the selected currency.
* **Quotation Currency**: Select the currency of the quotation, which can be USD, EUR, or GBP.
* **Quotation Terms Accepted**: Check the box if the client has accepted the quotation terms.
* **Quotation Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the quotation.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
