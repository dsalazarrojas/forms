# Production Machinery Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users regarding a quote request for production machinery. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide a detailed description of your request and gather relevant information for the quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
2. Provide a brief description of the quote request in the "Quote Request Details" section.
3. Select the date of the quote request in the "Request Date" section.
4. Choose who requested the quote in the "Requested By" section.
5. Select the contact person responsible for this quote request in the "Contact Person" section.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
8. Choose the type of machinery you are looking for in the "Machinery Type" section.
9. Select the quantity of machinery you need in the "Quantity" section.
10. Choose the price range of the machinery in the "Price Range" section.
11. Enter any additional information related to the machinery in the "Additional Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (1)**: Enter your name and contact details.
* **Quote Request Details (2)**: Provide a brief description of your quote request.
* **Request Date (3)**: Select the date of the quote request.
* **Requested By (4)**: Choose who requested the quote.
* **Contact Person (5)**: Select the contact person responsible for this quote request.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Machinery Type (8)**: Choose the type of machinery you are looking for.
* **Quantity (9)**: Select the quantity of machinery you need.
* **Price Range (10)**: Choose the price range of the machinery.
* **Additional Info (11)**: Enter any additional information related to the machinery.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the quote request is processed accurately.
- Check your information for any errors or inaccuracies before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the support team.
