# Foreign Exchange Options Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a quote request for foreign exchange options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your request details in the Request Details field.
2. Select whether you are interested in a 'Yes' or 'No' option using the Instrument field.
3. If you are interested in a specific option, enter its notional value in the Notional field.
4. If applicable, enter the strike value in the Strike field.
5. Provide the desired expiration date and time in the Expiration Date and Expiration Time fields, respectively.
6. Enter the price you are interested in for the option in the Price field.
7. Enter the quantity of the option you would like to request in the Quantity field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Details** (Required: false): Enter a brief description of your request.
- **Instrument** (Required: false): Select 'Yes' to indicate you are interested in a specific option. Select 'No' if you are not interested.
- **Notional** (Required: false): Enter the notional value of the option, if applicable.
- **Strike** (Required: false): Enter the strike value, if applicable.
- **Expiration Date** (Required: false): Enter the desired expiration date of the option.
- **Expiration Time** (Required: false): Enter the desired expiration time of the option.
- **Price** (Required: false): Enter the desired price of the option.
- **Quantity** (Required: false): Enter the desired quantity of the option.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all relevant fields for a more accurate quote request.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for guidance.
- The more specific your request, the better we can assist you in providing an accurate quote.
