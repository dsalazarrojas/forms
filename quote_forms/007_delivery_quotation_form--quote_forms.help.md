## Delivery Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Delivery Quotation Form is a tool used to generate a quotation for delivery purposes. This form is designed to collect information from users about the products they want to order, the quantity they need, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Delivery Quotation Form" title and any other fields as required or optional according to your preferences.
2. Select the products you want to include in the quotation by choosing from the options provided in the "products" field.
3. Enter the quantity of each selected product.
4. Set the price for each product if you have a specific value in mind.
5. Choose a delivery date and time according to your preferences.
6. Add any additional notes about the delivery if needed.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Add your company name if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Quotation Form** (`delivery_quotation_form`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for the quotation.
* **products** (`products`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the products you want to include in the quotation.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of each selected product.
* **price** (`price`, `number`, optional): Set the price for each product if you have a specific value in mind.
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for the delivery.
* **delivery_time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time for the delivery.
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the delivery.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the quotation can be generated correctly.
* Use the "products" field to select only the products you want to include in the quotation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask for assistance from the support team.
* This form is used for delivery purposes, please ensure that the information provided is accurate to avoid any errors.
* Please use a valid email address and phone number to ensure proper communication.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
