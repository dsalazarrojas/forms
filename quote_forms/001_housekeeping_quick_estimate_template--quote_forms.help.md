# Housekeeping Quick Estimate Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is used to quickly gather and estimate the services required for housekeeping tasks in a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information:

1. Select the housekeeping services needed from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the square footage of the property.
3. Check if a site visit is required and provide the date of the visit.
4. Fill in the client's name and contact information (phone number and email).
5. Add any additional notes about the service area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Housekeeping Service**: 
  • This is where you select the services you want to provide for the property. Choose from options like "Vacuuming, mopping, dusting" or "Other" for custom services.

  - **Square Footage**:
    * Enter the total square footage of the property to estimate the services needed.

  - **Site Visit Required**:
    * Check this box if a site visit is required for the services. This helps us plan and prepare for the visit.

  - **Site Visit Date**:
    * Provide the date of the site visit if required. This ensures we can schedule the visit accordingly.

  - **Client Name**:
    * Fill in the name of the client or property owner for our records.

  - **Phone Number**:
    * Enter the client's phone number for easy contact.

  - **Email**:
    * Add the client's email address for communication.

  - **Service Area**:
    * Describe the area where the services will be provided, such as "Living Room" or "Kitchen". This helps us understand the scope of the work.

Please fill in the form as accurately as possible to help us provide a quick and efficient quote for the housekeeping services.
