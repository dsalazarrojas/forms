# party_bus_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Bus Quote Request Form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in renting a party bus for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary details about your party, including the date, time, and duration of the event.
2. Enter the number of guests attending the party and the location where the party will take place.
3. If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Choose the type of party bus you are interested in renting (Bus, Party Bus, Limousine, or Limo).
5. Finally, provide some information about yourself, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you to confirm your quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Bus Details** (`party_bus_quote_details`): This is the main description of your party and is required for us to get an accurate quote.
	+ Please enter a brief description of your party, including any specific details that will help us determine the size and type of party bus you need.
* **Party Date** (`party_date`): Select the date of your party.
	+ This will help us determine the availability of party buses on that specific date.
* **Party Time** (`party_time`): Choose the start time of your party.
	+ Please keep in mind that party buses can be booked for specific time slots, so this selection will help us narrow down our options.
* **Party Duration** (`party_duration`): Enter the expected duration of your party.
	+ This will help us calculate the total rental time and ensure we have enough staff and resources available.
* **Party Location** (`party_location`): Enter the address of the party location.
	+ Make sure to include any details about the location that we might need to know, such as parking restrictions or security concerns.
* **Number of Guests** (`party_number_guests`): Enter the expected number of guests attending the party.
	+ This will help us determine the capacity of the party bus and ensure it's sufficient for your event.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to confirm your quote.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address so we can send you updates and confirmations about your quote and booking.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Party Message** (`party_message`): If you have any special requests or instructions for your party, please include them here.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`): If you have any other specific requests or instructions, please include them here.
* **Party Type** (`party_type`): Choose the type of party bus you are interested in renting (Bus, Party Bus, Limousine, or Limo).
	+ Please consider the size and capacity of the bus, as well as any specific features you need for your event.
* **Form Submission Type** (`form_submission_type`): Choose the type of submission you would like to make (Quote, Event, or Reservation).
	+ This will help us determine the next steps in the booking process.

Note: All fields are optional, but please provide as much information as possible to help us better serve you.
