# Moving Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from submitters for quotes or proposals related to moving services. It will help you provide accurate quotes and serve your customers better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide your details and information as requested. Fill out all required fields (if any), and don't forget to upload any relevant files. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach you back.
* **Moving Details** (`moving_details`, text, optional): Describe the moving details, including the type of move, size of the move, and any special requirements.
* **Upload Files** (`upload_files`, text, optional): If you have any relevant documents or files to attach, upload them here.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, text, optional): Please indicate your agreement to our terms of service. This will help us better serve you with your quote needs.
