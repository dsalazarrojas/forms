# piano_moving_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are requesting a quote for piano moving services. Please fill out the form with your details to get an estimate of the moving costs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
- Select the correct "quote_type" (Piano Move) to indicate that you are requesting a quote for a piano move.
- Enter the date you plan to move (move_date) in the required "date" format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Select the correct "move_type" (e.g., residential, commercial) to indicate the type of move you are planning.
- Enter the estimated distance of the move (move_distance) in the space provided.
- Enter the weight of the piano (piano_weight) in pounds.
- Enter any additional notes or special instructions in the "additional_notes" field.
- If you have any extra information about the move (extra_info), feel free to add it in the "extra_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Quote Type**: Select "Piano Move" to indicate that you are requesting a quote for a piano move.
* **Move Date**: Enter the date you plan to move in the required "date" format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Move Time**: Select the time of the move (optional).
* **Move Location**: Enter the address of the move location.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional notes or special instructions about the move.
* **Move Distance**: Enter the estimated distance of the move in miles or kilometers.
* **Piano Weight**: Enter the weight of the piano in pounds.
* **Estimate Required**: If you have any specific requirements or restrictions for the estimate, enter them here.
* **Estimated Movers**: If you have any specific requirements or restrictions for the movers, enter them here.
* **Total Cost**: This field will be populated automatically with the estimated moving costs.
* **Form Submitted**: This field will be populated automatically with the timestamp of the last time the form was submitted.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the form in the required "date" format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Quote Details**: Any additional information about the quote you'd like to add.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Additional Info**: Any extra information about the move.
* **Special Instructions**: Any special instructions or requirements for the move.
* **Extra Info**: Any extra information about the move.
* **Move Type**: Select the type of move (e.g., residential, commercial).
* **Estimated Cost**: This field will be populated automatically with the estimated moving costs.
* **Estimated Cost Range**: Enter the estimated cost range of the move.
* **Notes**: Any additional information about the move.

Please note that some fields may not be required for the quote, but it's always best to fill out as much information as possible to get an accurate estimate.
