# Location Scouting Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a location scouting quote request. It's essential for our team to understand your needs and requirements for location scouting services. Please fill out this form if you're interested in requesting a quote for location scouting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field. If you have multiple options for a field, choose the one that best describes your situation.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for each field, as this will help us better understand your needs.
3. Review each field carefully and ensure you've selected the correct options before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote Request Form** (`location_scouting_quote_request_page_1`, Select Multiple, required: false): This field is where you can confirm your interest in requesting a quote for location scouting services. Select "Yes" if you are interested in requesting a quote, and "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info_page_1`, Text, required: false): In this field, please provide a brief overview of the customer you're working with, including their name, contact information, and any relevant details.
* **Customer Contact Information** (`customer_contact_info_page_1`, Text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the customer, including the person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Location Scouting Information** (`location_scouting_info_page_1`, Select One, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you have specific location scouting requirements, or "No" if you don't.
* **Project Requirements** (`project_requirements_page_1`, Text, required: false): Describe the project requirements, including any specific needs or constraints you have.
* **Production Team** (`production_team_page_1`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have a production team in mind, or "No" if you don't.
* **Submit Quote** (`submit_quote_page_1`, Text, required: false): Enter a unique ID for the quote, which will be used to identify your quote in the system.
* **Quote Confirmation** (`submit_quote_confirmation_page_1`, Text, required: false): Enter a brief confirmation statement for your quote, which will help us understand your understanding of the quote.

Note: This form has been designed to be user-friendly and easy to fill out. However, if you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
