# Repair Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for customers to submit their repair requests, allowing our team to assess and provide quotes for the required services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Customer Info (Page 1)**: Please fill in the customer's name, phone number, and email to ensure we can contact them about their repair request.
2. **Repair Request (Page 1)**: Describe the issue you'd like to have repaired, and we'll do our best to provide a quote within 24 hours.
3. **Estimate Date (Page 2)**: Select the desired date for your repair, or leave blank if you'd like our team to suggest one.
4. **Repair Services (Page 3)**: Choose the services you'd like to have repaired, and we'll include them in the quote.
5. **Selected Services (Page 3)**: Select all the repair services you've chosen from the list above.
6. **Description (Page 4)**: Describe the issue in more detail, so our technicians can understand it better.
7. **Customer Signature (Page 4)**: Sign below to confirm your repair request and agreement to our quote.
8. **Customer Name (Page 5)**: Enter your full name to confirm your identity.
9. **Customer Address (Page 5)**: Enter your address for our convenience.
10. **City (Page 5)**: Enter your city for our reference.
11. **State (Page 5)**: Enter your state for our reference.
12. **Zip (Page 5)**: Enter your zip code for our reference.
13. **Total Cost (Page 6)**: This will display our quote for the selected services.
14. **Customer Notes (Page 6)**: Add any additional information about your repair request, if needed.
15. **Assigned Technician (Page 7)**: This will display the assigned technician's name and contact information.
16. **Assigned Date (Page 7)**: This will display the scheduled date for the repair.
17. **Completion Date (Page 7)**: This will display the estimated completion date for the repair.
18. **Completed Status (Page 7)**: This will display the completion status of the repair.
19. **Technician Signature (Page 7)**: Sign below to confirm completion of the repair.
20. **Repair Quote (Page 8)**: Review our quote for the selected services.
21. **Customer Comments (Page 8)**: Add any comments about your experience.
22. **Additional Info (Page 9)**: Add any additional information that might be useful for the repair.
