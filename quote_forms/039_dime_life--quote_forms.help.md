# Dime Life - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dime Life" form is designed to help you track your daily expenses, income, and financial habits. Fill out this form to get a clear picture of your financial situation and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on each page to begin filling out the form.
2. For each text field, type in your responses according to the question.
3. Make sure to fill out all fields on each page, as some questions may be optional (though it's a good practice to complete them for a more accurate picture).
4. When you finish filling out the form, review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
