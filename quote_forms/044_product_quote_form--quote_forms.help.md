# product_quote_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product quote form is designed to gather information about a product and the customer, in order to provide a quote for the product. This form serves as a front-end representation of a backend process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product quote form page 1 with the relevant information.
2. Enter your customer's name in the customer name field.
3. Enter the product name in the product name field.
4. Enter a description of the product in the product description field.
5. Enter the price of the product in the product price field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: This field is where you enter the name of the customer who is requesting a quote for the product.
* **Product Name**: This field is where you enter the name of the product being quoted.
* **Product Description**: This field is where you enter a brief description of the product being quoted.
* **Product Price**: This field is where you enter the price of the product being quoted.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field to ensure the quote is generated correctly.
* Review the generated quote carefully before submitting it to the customer.
* If you have any questions or issues with the quote, contact the relevant department for assistance.
