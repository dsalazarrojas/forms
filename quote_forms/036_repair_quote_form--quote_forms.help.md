# Repair Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Quote Form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in getting some kind of repair services. This form will be used to create a quote for the customer based on the information provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (Text): Please enter your company's name and address information.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for the repair.
* **Phone** (Number): Enter your phone number, including area code.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like to schedule the repair.
* **Repair Details** (Text): Provide a brief description of the repair needed.
* **Authorization** (Email): If applicable, please provide the email address of the person who authorized this repair.
* **Description** (Text): A short description of the repair.
* **Total Price** (Number): Enter the total cost of the repair.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One): Choose the payment terms that apply to this repair (e.g. "1" or "2").
* **Terms and Conditions** (Note): This field is for any additional terms and conditions related to the repair.
