# Photography Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients for generating quotes for photography services. It guides you through the process of collecting details about the client's company, services, prices, event details, and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing company details in the **Company Details** page.
2. In the **Services** page, choose the specific services you require from the options provided, which include Portrait and Event, Wedding, Family, Pet, Newborn, Baby, Event, Baby and Event, Wedding and Event, and Family and Event.
3. In the **Prices** page, enter your desired price range or specific price.
4. In the **Event Details** page, provide a brief description of the event or shoot.
5. In the **Client Details** page, enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
6. Finally, in the **Quote** page, you can enter your quote, which will be generated based on the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (`text`, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you will provide details about your company, such as your business name, address, and contact information.
* **Services** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the services you require from the options provided, including Portrait and Event, Wedding, Family, Pet, Newborn, Baby, Event, Baby and Event, Wedding and Event, and Family and Event.
* **Prices** (`text`, required: false): Enter your desired price range or specific price for the services.
* **Event Details** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event or shoot, including any necessary details about the location, duration, and other relevant information.
* **Client Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about the client.
* **Quote** (`text`, required: false): Enter your quote based on the information provided in the previous pages.
