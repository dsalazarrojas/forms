# Painting, Quote And Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in getting painting quotes or estimates for a specific project. It helps us understand their needs and preferences, which enables us to provide accurate quotes or estimates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Specify your preferred time for the project in the "preferred time" field.
5. Select the type of service you are interested in: "painting", "quote", or "estimation".
6. Provide a brief description of your project in the "project details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (`last name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **preferred time** (`preferred time`, time, optional): Specify your preferred time for the project.
* **service** (service, select_one, required): Select the type of service you are interested in: "painting", "quote", or "estimation".
* **project details** (`project details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
