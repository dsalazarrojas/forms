# Offer And Counteroffer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offer And Counteroffer Form is used to document the terms of an offer and a potential counteroffer in a negotiation. It helps to clarify the terms and any changes made to the original proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the **Negotiation terms** page, enter the original terms of the offer.
2. On the **Offer details** page, provide a brief description of the offer.
3. On the **Counteroffer details** page, provide a brief description of the counteroffer.
4. On the **Signature fields** page, choose whether the offer and counteroffer have signatures.
5. On the **Comments** page, enter any additional comments or information relevant to the negotiation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Negotiation terms** (`negotiation_terms`, text, required/optional): Enter the original terms of the offer, such as the price, payment terms, or other relevant details.
* **Offer details** (`offer_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the offer, including any relevant information about the proposed terms.
* **Counteroffer details** (`counteroffer_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the counteroffer made in response to the original offer.
* **Signature fields** (`signature_fields`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether the offer and counteroffer have signatures, such as electronic or physical signatures.
* **Comments** (`offer_and_counteroffer_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information relevant to the negotiation, such as context or clarifications.
