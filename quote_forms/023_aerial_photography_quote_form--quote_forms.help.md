# Aerial Photography Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for aerial photography projects. It ensures that we understand their project requirements, location, and intended usage of the photos, making it easier for us to provide accurate quotes and deliverables.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client information section with the required details.
2. Choose the project type that best describes your project.
3. Provide the shoot location address, which can be a full address or coordinates.
4. Select the required deliverables for your project.
5. Indicate the requested shoot date, taking into account weather conditions.
6. (Optional) Specify the shoot location's airspaces awareness, if it's near an airport or restricted zone.
7. Choose the intended usage of the photos.
8. Select the budget range for your project.
9. Add any additional details that might be useful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name / Company** (`client_name`, `text`, required): This field is where you can enter your name or your company's name as the client.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please provide your valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of project you're undertaking, such as Real Estate (Residential) or Event Coverage.
- **Shoot Location Address** (`location_address`, `text`, required): Provide the full address or coordinates of the shoot location.
- **Requested Shoot Date** (`requested_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you'd like to shoot the project, considering weather conditions.
- **Required Deliverables** (`deliverables`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the files you need from the available options, such as High Res Photos (JPEG/RAW) or 4K Video Footage.
- **Airspaces Awareness** (`airspace_check`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the location is near an airport or restricted zone.
- **Intended Usage** (`usage_rights`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you plan to use the photos, like Personal Use or Print Advertising.
- **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the budget range that suits your project.
- **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific shots list or creative direction for us to understand your project better.
- **-- Client Information --** (`client_info_header`, `note`, required): This section is a header for the client information section, just click and fill it in.
- **-- Project Details --** (`project_details_header`, `note`, required): This section is a header for the project details section, just click and fill it in.
