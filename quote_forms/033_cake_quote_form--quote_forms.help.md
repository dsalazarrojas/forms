# Cake Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cake Quote Form is designed to gather information from customers to provide personalized quotes for custom cake orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the event date.
3. Choose the event type and number of people to serve.
4. Select the cake shape, flavor, and frosting type.
5. Specify any dietary requirements.
6. Choose whether you need delivery and setup.
7. If delivery is required, enter the delivery address.
8. Choose the quote validity period.
9. Select when you need to make a decision.
10. Provide any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Company Name (if applicable)**: Enter your company name if you have one.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive the quote.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Quote Date**: Enter the date for the quote.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event (e.g. Wedding, Corporate event, Birthday).
* **Number of People to Serve**: Enter the approximate number of people you plan to serve.
* **Cake Shape**: Select the preferred shape of the cake.
* **Cake Flavor**: Select the preferred flavor of the cake.
* **Frosting Type**: Select the preferred type of frosting.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements (e.g. Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Vegan).
* **Delivery Required**: Choose whether you need delivery.
* **Delivery Address**: If delivery is required, enter the full address.
* **Setup Required**: Choose whether you need setup at the venue.
* **Quote Validity**: Choose how long the quote should be valid.
* **Decision Timeline**: Choose when you need to make a decision.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes.
