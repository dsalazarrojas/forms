# Environmental Policy Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for environmental policy quotations, ensuring accurate internal processing and customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant quote type from the options provided.
3. Enter the customer's email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Specify the quote date and time.
5. Enter the quote amount.
6. Provide a brief description of the quotation.
7. Optionally, add any additional notes about the quotation.
8. Specify the quote status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Request**: Enter the details of the quotation request.
* **Quote Type**: Select the type of quotation you're requesting (Yes, No, Other).
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address for contact.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact (optional).
* **Quote Date**: Enter the date of the quotation.
* **Quote Time**: Enter the time of the quotation.
* **Quote Amount**: Enter the total amount of the quotation.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address (optional).
* **Quote Description**: Enter a brief description of the quotation.
* **Quote Notes**: Add any additional notes about the quotation (optional).
* **Quote Status**: Specify the status of the quotation (Yes, No, Other).
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Company**: Enter the customer's company name.
* **Quote Date 2**: Enter the date for the second quotation (if applicable).
* **Quote Time 2**: Enter the time for the second quotation (if applicable).

## Tips
- Fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays or misunderstandings.
- Ensure you've selected the correct quote type and status.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant department for clarification.
- This form is not meant for any other purpose; ensure you're using it for the intended use only.
