# Home Theater Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for a home theater installation project. It will help you gather essential information from the customer to provide an accurate quote and estimate the installation time and cost of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the project type from the drop-down menu. This will help us determine the scope of the project.
2. Enter the customer's contact information, including their name, contact number, and email.
3. Provide additional project details, such as project location, project budget, and project date.
4. List the equipment to be installed and its price.
5. Choose the installation type (e.g., Home Automation, Smart Home, Theater Installation).
6. Enter the installation date and time.
7. Leave any additional comments or notes for the installer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are requesting a quote for (Audio Installation, Video Installation, or Lighting Installation). This will help us determine the scope of the project.
* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's contact information, including their name, contact number, and email. This will help us get in touch with the customer for further questions or to provide the quote.
* **Name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their contact information.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Project Details**: Enter any additional details about the project, such as its location and date.
* **Project Location**: Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Budget**: Enter the estimated budget for the project.
* **Project Date**: Enter the date of the project.
* **Project Time**: Enter the time of the project.
* **Additional Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes for the installer.
* **Customer Message**: Leave a message for the customer.
* **Installation Type**: Select the type of installation you are requesting (e.g., Home Automation, Smart Home, Theater Installation).
* **Equipment List**: List the equipment to be installed.
* **Equipment Price**: Enter the price of the equipment to be installed.
* **Total Price**: This field will be automatically calculated based on the equipment price and other factors.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date of the installation.
* **Installation Time**: Enter the time of the installation.
* **Installer Comments**: Leave any comments or notes for the installer.
* **Quote Approved**: Select whether the quote is approved or not (True, False, or Pending).
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date when the quote was approved.
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time when the quote was approved.
* **Approval Note**: Leave any additional notes for the approval.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when the quote was submitted.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time when the quote was submitted.
