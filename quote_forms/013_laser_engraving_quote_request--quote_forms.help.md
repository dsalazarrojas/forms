# Laser Engraving Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Laser Engraving Quote Request" form is designed to collect information from clients for quote requests related to laser engraving services. This form will help us gather necessary details to provide accurate pricing and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Details** section with a brief description of the project, including any relevant details.
2. Upload or describe the **Artwork Files** related to the project.
3. Specify the **Quantity** of engraving required.
4. Provide a **Deadline** for the project.
5. Include any specific **Engraver Information** or notes.
6. Add any **Special Requests** related to the project.
7. Include any additional **Comments** about the project.
8. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
9. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
10. Specify the **Assigned Tool** (not currently applicable).
11. Enter the **Quote Details** for your project.
12. Select a **Pricing** option from the provided choices.
13. Finally, indicate whether or not you are ready to **Submit** the quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (1, text, optional): A brief description of the project, including any relevant details.
* **Artwork Files** (2, text, optional): Upload or describe the artwork files related to the project. This can be a file URL or a description of the artwork.
* **Quantity** (3, number, optional): The number of items to be engraved.
* **Deadline** (4, date, optional): The expected completion date for the project.
* **Engraver Information** (5, text, optional): Any specific information about the engraver, such as their name or contact details.
* **Special Requests** (6, text, optional): Any special requests or requirements for the project.
* **Comments** (7, text, optional): Additional comments or notes about the project.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (10, text, optional): Not currently applicable, but left for future use.
* **Quote Details** (11, text, optional): A summary of your quote request.
* **Pricing** (12, select_one, optional): Select a pricing option from the provided choices.
* **Submit** (13, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you are ready to submit the quote request.

**Tips**

* Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure we can provide accurate pricing and services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review and follow up on your quote request.
