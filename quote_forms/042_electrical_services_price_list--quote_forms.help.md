# Electrical Services Price List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Services Price List form is a tool to gather relevant information for electrical services, including service requests, schedule preferences, contact details, service type, pricing inquiries, schedule start and end dates, service price, additional notes, and assigned contact. This information helps in creating an up-to-date price list for electrical services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the required information for each field, as indicated by the "required" label.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Requests**: Enter a brief description of the electrical service required (e.g., installation, repair, maintenance).
* **Schedule Preferences**: Select a date for the service to be performed.
* **Contact Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the person requesting the service.
* **Service Type**: Select one or more types of electrical services required (e.g., Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC).
* **Pricing Inquiries**: Enter any specific pricing questions or concerns you have.
* **Schedule Start Date**: Enter the start date for the scheduled service.
* **Schedule End Date**: Enter the end date for the scheduled service (optional).
* **Service Price**: Enter the price for the electrical service.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the service.
* **Assigned Contact**: Select the person responsible for the service (e.g., Jimmy, John, Jane).
