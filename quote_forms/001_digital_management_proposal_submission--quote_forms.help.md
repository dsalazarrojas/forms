# Digital Management Proposal Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you submit a digital management proposal to our team. It collects information about your identity, proposal details, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out your personal contact information, including your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide a Title for your proposal.
3. Optionally, add a brief Description of your proposal.
4. Choose whether you submitted the proposal "Yes" or "No" under "Submitted By".
5. Select a Submission Date and Submission Time for when you submitted the proposal.
6. You are required to fill out the form completely to ensure that we can track your proposal properly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): A text field to input your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): A text field to input your last name.
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, optional): A text field to input a title for your proposal.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): A text field to input your company name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): A field to input your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): A text field to input your phone number.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): A text field to input a brief description of your proposal.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): A field to select whether you submitted the proposal "Yes" or "No".
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/optional): A date field to select the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): A time field to select the time of submission.
