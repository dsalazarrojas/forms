# Holistic Therapy Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a holistic therapy quote. It helps therapists or wellness professionals provide an accurate quote for a session with a client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Enter the Session Details, Client Information, and follow-up information as required.
3. Select the Session Options, Session Type, and Services as needed.
4. Enter any additional Notes or comments.
5. Enter the Client's Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
6. Review and submit the form to generate the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details** (Text, Required): Enter the date, time, and location of the session.
- **Client Information** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any relevant details.
- **Session Options** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the options for the session, such as "Cancellation Policy" or "Payment Methods."
- **Pricing Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional pricing information, such as package deals or discounts.
- **Follow Up** (Text, Optional): Enter any follow-up information, such as appointment reminders or payment due dates.
- **Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Enter any contact information for the client, such as emergency contact or next of kin.
- **Session Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of session, such as "Individual" or "Group."
- **Services** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional services provided, such as workshops or packages.
- **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the client's email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the client's phone number for contact purposes.
- **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the client.
