# dental_treatment_quote_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental treatment quote approval form is used to create and manage quotes for dental treatments. This form captures all the necessary information for the quote, including patient details, treatment plans, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Quote" option to indicate whether this quote is for a new treatment (Yes) or not (No).
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. If applicable, enter the quote date in the "Quote Date" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether treatment plans are included in the quote.
5. Enter the total cost of the treatment in the "Total Cost" field.
6. Provide insurance details in the "Insurance Details" field (if applicable).
7. Enter any additional doctor notes in the "Doctor Notes" field (if applicable).
8. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient has approved the quote.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the office has approved the quote.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the insurance company has approved the quote.
11. Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the quote status.
12. Enter the patient's signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
13. Enter the office signature in the "Office Signature" field.
14. Enter the doctor's signature in the "Doctor Signature" field.
15. If applicable, enter the title of the person who approved the quote in the "Approved By Title" field.
16. Enter any billing details in the "Billing Details" field (if applicable).
17. If applicable, enter the approval date in the "Approval Date" field.
18. Enter the name of the insurance company (if applicable).
19. Enter the office address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote**: This field indicates whether this quote is for a new treatment (Yes) or not (No).
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their insurance card or identification.
* **Quote Date**: If the quote is applicable, enter the date of the quote.
* **Treatments Plans**: Select "Yes" if the quote includes treatment plans, otherwise select "No".
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the treatment.
* **Insurance Details**: If the patient has insurance, enter the insurance details here (e.g., policy number, provider).
* **Doctor Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the doctor regarding the treatment.
* **Patient Approval**: Select "Yes" if the patient has approved the quote, otherwise select "No".
* **Office Approval**: Select "Yes" if the office has approved the quote, otherwise select "No".
* **Insurance Approval**: Select "Yes" if the insurance company has approved the quote, otherwise select "No".
* **Billing Status**: Select "Active" if the quote is active, otherwise select "Inactive".
* **Quote Approval**: Select "Yes" if the quote is approved, otherwise select "No".
* **Payment Terms**: Select "Yes" if payment terms are included in the quote, otherwise select "No".
* **Approval Note**: Enter any additional notes regarding the approval of the quote.
* **Approved By**: If the quote is approved, enter the title of the person who approved it.
* **Approved Date**: If the quote is approved, enter the date of approval.
* **Quote Status**: Select "Active" if the quote is active, otherwise select "Inactive".
* **Patient Signature**: The patient must sign this field to confirm they have read and understood the quote.
* **Office Signature**: The office staff must sign this field to confirm they have reviewed and approved the quote.
* **Doctor Signature**: The doctor who provided the quote must sign this field to confirm they have provided the quote.
* **Approved By Title**: Enter the title of the person who approved the quote (if applicable).
* **Billing Details**: Enter any billing details (if applicable).
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date the quote was approved (if applicable).
* **Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the insurance company (if applicable).
* **Office Address**: Enter the office address (if applicable).
