# vasectomy_service_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vasectomy_service_quote_request" form is used to gather information from patients for a vasectomy service quote request. This form helps our organization understand the individual's needs and preferences for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your valid Email address so we can reach out to you.
3. Provide your Phone Number for us to contact you.
4. Enter your Birth Date to ensure that you are of the required age for the procedure.
5. Share your Medical History, if any, to help us understand any potential risks or complications.
6. Specify any Follow Up information, if desired.
7. Provide any Additional information about yourself or your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-15).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Share any existing medical conditions or allergies that may impact the procedure.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, required/optional): If you'd like to schedule a follow-up conversation or appointment, provide that information here.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, required/optional): If there's any additional information about yourself or your situation that you'd like to share with us, you can provide it in this field.

Note: The required fields are First Name, Email, and Phone Number. The rest of the fields are optional but helpful for us to provide a more accurate quote and ensure a smooth process.
