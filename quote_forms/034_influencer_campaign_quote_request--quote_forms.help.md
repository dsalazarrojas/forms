# Influencer Campaign Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Influencer Campaign Quote Request" form is a tool for influencers to submit their campaign quote requests. This form captures essential details about the campaign, brand, contact, quote, and review quote information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Campaign Details**: Provide a brief description of your campaign, including the details of your request.
2. **Brand Details**: Enter your brand's name and other relevant details to ensure we can effectively communicate with your team.
3. **Contact Details**: Enter your name and email address so we can contact you for further clarification.
4. **Campaign Quote**: Enter your quote or offer for the campaign.
5. **Review Quote**: Confirm the quote you've provided for the campaign.
6. **Review Quote Details**: Provide additional details about the quote you've provided.
7. **Confirm Review**: Confirm whether you accept or reject the quote.
8. **Campaign Terms**: (Optional) - If you'd like, you can provide additional campaign terms or conditions.
9. **Upload Documents**: You can upload relevant documents to support your quote or campaign.
10. **Confirm Terms**: (Optional) - Confirm whether you agree, disagree, or are undecided on the campaign terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, `text`, required): A brief description of your campaign.
* **Brand Details** (`brand_details`, `text`, required): Enter your brand's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name and email address so we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Campaign Quote** (`campaign_quote`, `text`, required): Enter your quote or offer for the campaign.
* **Review Quote** (`review_quote`, `text`, required): Confirm the quote you've provided for the campaign.
* **Review Quote Details** (`review_quote_details`, `text`, required): Provide additional details about the quote you've provided.
* **Confirm Review** (`confirm_review`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether you accept or reject the quote.
* **Campaign Terms** (`campaign_terms`, `text`, optional): If you'd like, you can provide additional campaign terms or conditions.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, `text`, optional): You can upload relevant documents to support your quote or campaign.
* **Confirm Terms** (`confirm_terms`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm whether you agree, disagree, or are undecided on the campaign terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
