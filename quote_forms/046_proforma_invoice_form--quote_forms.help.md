# Proforma Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a draft invoice for accounting and financial purposes. It helps track client information, purchased items, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the client information section with the client's name, address, and contact person.
2. Indicate if any items were purchased (select "Yes" or "No" in the "Purchased Items" field).
3. Enter the purchased items in the "Purchased Items Table" field.
4. Enter the unit price, quantity, and price for each purchased item in the corresponding fields.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for payment terms.
6. Fill out the subtotal, tax, and shipping fee fields.
7. Enter the total amount and delivery terms.
8. Finally, fill out the delivery date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proforma Invoice Details**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional details about the invoice.
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the invoice number for this transaction.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Address**: Enter the client's address.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person for the client.
* **Purchased Items**: Check if any items were purchased. If yes, fill out the next fields for each item.
* **Purchased Items Table**: Enter a list of items purchased, one per line.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per item.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each item.
* **Price**: Calculate the price for each item by multiplying the unit price by the quantity.
* **Purchased Items**: This field is not used as the items list is entered in the "Purchased Items Table" field.
* **Payment Terms**: Select "Yes" or "No" if payment terms were agreed upon.
* **Subtotal**: Enter the total cost of the purchased items.
* **Tax**: Enter any applicable tax.
* **Shipping Fee**: Enter any applicable shipping fee.
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount including tax and shipping fee.
* **Delivery Terms**: Enter any delivery terms.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of delivery.
