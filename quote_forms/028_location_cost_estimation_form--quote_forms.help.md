# Location Cost Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Location Cost Estimation Form is used by event planners to collect details about an upcoming event, enabling them to provide accurate estimates of costs. Complete this form to provide the necessary information about your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant pages: Each page represents a different aspect of your event details.
2. Enter your venue details: Describe your chosen venue (e.g., hotel, venue name, address).
3. Provide an estimated budget: Enter the maximum budget you are willing to spend on the event.
4. Select the event type: Choose from the list of options (Event, Meeting, Wedding, Conference, Other).
5. Enter event start and end dates: Provide the start and end dates of your event.
6. Enter event start and end times: Provide the start and end times of your event.
7. Enter the number of guests: Specify the number of people attending your event.
8. Add any additional event details: Describe any unique aspects of your event.
9. Request a quote: Enter any specific requests or requirements for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Venue Details (Venue Details)**: Enter the address and any other relevant details about the venue where your event will take place.
* **Estimated Budget (Estimated Budget)**: Provide the maximum budget you are willing to spend on your event.
* **Event Type (Event Type)**: Choose from the list of options to select the type of event (Event, Meeting, Wedding, Conference, Other).
* **Event Start Date (Event Start Date)**: Enter the date of your event start.
* **Event Start Time (Event Start Time)**: Enter the start time of your event.
* **Event End Date (Event End Date)**: Enter the date of your event end.
* **Location (Location)**: Enter the physical address of the venue where your event will take place.
* **Number of Guests (Number of Guests)**: Specify the number of people attending your event.
* **Event Details (Event Details)**: Add any unique or specific details about your event that the event planner should know.
* **Request for Quotation (Request for quotation)**: Enter any specific requests or requirements for your event quote (e.g., catering, decorations, etc.).
