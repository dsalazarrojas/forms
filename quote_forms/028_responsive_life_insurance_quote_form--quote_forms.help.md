# Responsive Life Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Life Insurance Quote Form is designed to collect essential details from individuals interested in obtaining a life insurance quote. This form is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the policyholder's information, including their type of life insurance, insurance amount, and personal details, to generate an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of life insurance that best suits your needs.
2.  Enter the life insurance amount you are interested in.
3.  Provide your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and address (if applicable).
4.  Indicate your smoking status and age.
5.  Enter your date of birth and employment status.
6.  Enter your income level.
7.  If applicable, provide any existing medical conditions that may affect your policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Life Insurance Type** (id: 1)
    (`Life Insurance`, `Term Life Insurance`, `Whole Life Insurance`): Choose the type of life insurance that you are interested in.
*   **Life Insurance Amount** (id: 2)
    (`$`, `number`): Enter the life insurance amount you are interested in.
*   **Policyholder Name** (id: 3)
    (`string`, `text`): Enter your name as the policyholder.
*   **Policyholder Email** (id: 4)
    (`string`, `email`): Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Policyholder Phone** (id: 5)
    (`string`, `text`): Enter your phone number for easy contact. (Note: This field is not required.)
*   **Policyholder Address** (id: 6)
    (`string`, `text`): Enter your address (if applicable).
*   **Smoking Status** (id: 7)
    (`Non-smoker`, `Smoker`): Indicate your smoking status.
*   **Policyholder Age** (id: 8)
    (`number`): Enter your age.
*   **Policyholder Date of Birth** (id: 9)
    (`date`): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Employment Status** (id: 10)
    (`Employed`, `Unemployed`, `Self-Employed`): Select your employment status.
*   **Income Level** (id: 11)
    (`number`): Enter your income level.
*   **Existing Medical Conditions** (id: 12)
    (`string`, `note`): If applicable, provide any existing medical conditions.
