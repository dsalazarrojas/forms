# Furniture Removals Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Removals Quote Form is used to create quotes for customers who require furniture removal services.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which include your address, removal details, removal date, start time, and form ID.
2. Select the specific removal services you require, such as Furniture Removals, Furniture Packing, and Furniture Disposal.
3. If you need extra services like Wash, Dry Cleaning, or Laundry, select these options as well.
4. Add your email address so that the quote can be sent to you.
5. Leave any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address where the removal services will take place. This will help us determine the exact location and provide a more accurate quote.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used to address the quote and for our records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Removal Details** (`removal_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the removal, such as specific furniture items or any special requirements.
* **Removal Date** (`removal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you would like the removal services to take place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time for the removal services.
* **Removal Services** (`removal_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific removal services you require, such as Furniture Removals, Furniture Packing, and Furniture Disposal.
* **Extra Services** (`extra_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any extra services you need, such as Wash, Dry Cleaning, or Laundry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can send you the quote.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or questions in the "Notes" field.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for the form, which will be used to identify the quote. This is required for our internal tracking purposes.
