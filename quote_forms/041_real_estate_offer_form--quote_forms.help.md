# real_estate_offer_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for capturing information related to a real estate offer, including buyer and property details, offer terms, and buyer and seller communications. It is used to create a comprehensive record of the offer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Buyer Info**: Enter the buyer's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the property information, including its price and any other relevant details.
3. Enter the offer terms, including the offer date and time.
4. Provide a buyer message and a seller message to facilitate communication between the parties.
5. Sign and date both the buyer signature and seller signature fields to confirm agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name**: The name of the buyer making the offer.
* **Buyer E-mail**: The email address of the buyer.
* **Buyer Phone**: The phone number of the buyer.
* **Buyer Message**: A message from the buyer to the seller.
* **Property Info**: Details about the property being offered, including its price.
* **Offer Date**: The date the offer is made.
* **Offer Time**: The time the offer is made.
* **Seller Message**: A message from the seller to the buyer.
* **Seller Signature**: The seller's signature confirming the offer.

Note: Some fields are optional, but they all contribute to a comprehensive record of the offer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled and information is correct.
* Use the form to facilitate clear communication between the buyer and seller.
* Store the form as a record of the offer for future reference.
* Update the form regularly as changes to the offer occur.
* Consider including attachments or documents related to the offer in the form, if necessary.
