# Vehicle Maintenance And Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Maintenance And Repair Request Form is designed to collect information about vehicle maintenance and repair needs from users. This form will help the maintenance team understand what services are required and when.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the vehicle details such as type, model, and year.
2.  Select the maintenance services required (e.g., vehicle cleaning, oil change, tire replacement, etc.).
3.  Choose a desired service date and time.
4.  Enter the location where the service will be performed.
5.  Fill in your contact information (name, email, and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Maintenance Request** (vehicle_maintenance_request, text, required): This is a short description of the maintenance or repair issue.
* **Vehicle Details** (vehicle_details, text, required): Enter the vehicle's type, model, and year.
* **Maintenance Services Required** (maintenance_services_required, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following maintenance services: Vehicle Cleaning, Oil Change, Tire Replacement, Battery Replacement, Brake Replacement, Transmission Replacement.
* **Service Date** (service_date, date, required): Enter the desired service date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Service Time** (service_time, time, required): Choose the desired service time (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Service Location** (service_location, text, required): Enter the location where the service will be performed.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the vehicle maintenance request.
* Make sure to select all applicable maintenance services.
* Choose a date and time that is convenient for you.
* Enter accurate contact information to ensure we can get back to you with questions or updates.
