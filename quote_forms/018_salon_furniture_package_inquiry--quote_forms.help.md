# Salon Furniture Package Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from salon owners or 
  managers regarding a package deal for their business. The form 
  facilitates communication between the salon and their suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing customer information, including details about 
  yourself and your business.
- Next, please provide supplier information, including the name and 
  contact details of the supplier you are inquiring about.
- After that, describe the package details, including the specific 
  products or services you are interested in.
- Finally, provide your contact information, including your name, 
  email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): This field is used to 
  collect your name, business name, and other relevant details about 
  yourself and your business.
- **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`): This field is used to 
  collect the name and contact details of the supplier you are 
  inquiring about.
- **Package Details** (`package_details`): This field is used to 
  describe the specific products or services you are interested in.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): This field is used to 
  collect your name, email, and phone number so that the supplier 
  can get in touch with you.
- **Confirm Information** (`confirm_info`): This field is likely used 
  to verify that the information you provided is accurate.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise when providing information in each field.
- Ensure that you provide accurate contact information so that the 
  supplier can get in touch with you.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for 
  clarification or support before submitting the form.
- If you have already submitted an inquiry, please do not submit 
  duplicate requests as this may cause unnecessary confusion.
