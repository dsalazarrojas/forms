# Subcontractor Service Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from subcontractors about their service offers on a platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your bidder's name, contact information, and email address from the dropdown menu. 
2. Choose the service type that you can provide (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, or Painting).
3. Estimate the time required for each service in hours.
4. Enter your availability and end date of the project.
5. Enter your start and end dates for the service.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bidder's Name** (`Bidder's Name`, `text`, required/optional): This field asks for the name of the bidder. 
* **Bidder's Contact** (`Bidder’s Contact`, `text`, required/optional): This field is meant to collect contact information for the bidder, such as a phone number or another communication channel. 
* **Bidder's Email** (`Bidder’s Email`, `text`, required/optional): This field asks for the email address of the bidder. 
* **Bidder’s Phone** (`Bidder’s Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is meant to collect the phone number of the bidder. 
* **Service Details** (`Service Details`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows the bidder to select the type of service they can provide (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, or Painting). 
* **Subcontractor Information** (`Subcontractor Information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to provide any additional information about themselves. 
* **Service Estimates** (`Service Estimates`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to provide an estimate of the service they can offer. 
* **Electrical Service Estimate** (`Electrical Service Estimate`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to estimate the time required for the Electrical service. 
* **Plumbing Service Estimate** (`Plumbing Service Estimate`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to estimate the time required for the Plumbing service. 
* **Carpentry Service Estimate** (`Carpentry Service Estimate`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to estimate the time required for the Carpentry service. 
* **Painting Service Estimate** (`Painting Service Estimate`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to estimate the time required for the Painting service. 
* **Timeline** (`Timeline`, `time`, required/optional): This field is for the bidder to enter a time range when they can start and end the project. 
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is to enter the start date of the project. 
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is to enter the end date of the project.
