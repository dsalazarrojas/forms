# Request For Pricing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to gather information from users to request pricing for a specific good or service. This information will be used to provide an accurate quote for the requested item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of product you are requesting a quote for (Goods or Services).
2. Enter the price range you are looking for (optional).
3. Fill in your contact details and the seller's contact information (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Goods/Services**: Select whether you are requesting pricing for a good or a service.
	+ This field is used to determine the type of product you are requesting a quote for.
	+ Please choose Goods if you are requesting a physical product, and choose Services if you are requesting a service.
* **Price**: Enter the specific price you are looking for.
	+ This field is used to gather the exact price you are looking for.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **Price Range**: Select the price range you are looking for (optional).
	+ This field is used to gather a general idea of the price range you are looking for.
	+ Please select one of the options provided (10-20, 20-30, 30-40).
* **Other (please specify)**: If you selected Goods/Services and the product is not listed, please specify its name.
	+ This field is used to provide more details about the product you are requesting a quote for.
	+ Please enter the name of the product.
* **Buyer/Seller**: Select whether you are the buyer or seller of the product.
	+ This field is used to determine who will be responsible for making the purchase.
	+ Please choose Buyer if you are the one making the purchase, and choose Seller if you are the one selling the product.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information (required).
	+ This field is used to gather your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
	+ Please enter your email address.
* **Seller Information**: Enter the seller's information (optional).
	+ This field is used to gather the seller's contact details.
	+ Please enter their email address.
* **Buyer Information**: Enter the buyer's information (optional).
	+ This field is used to gather the buyer's contact details.
	+ Please enter their email address.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.
	+ This field is used to gather any additional information you would like to share.
	+ Please enter any relevant details.

# Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will do our best to provide an accurate quote based on the information provided.
