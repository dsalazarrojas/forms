# installation_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The installation quote request form is designed to collect necessary information for installation quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary details about the job you're requesting a quote for, including job address, job type, and job description.
2. Provide your contact information, including your contact number and email.
3. Select the desired installation date and time.
4. Choose whether to follow up with the customer and who to assign for the quote.
5. Choose the payment terms and method for the quote.
6. Enter any additional customer message or follow-up note.
7. Confirm the submitted date for the quote request.
