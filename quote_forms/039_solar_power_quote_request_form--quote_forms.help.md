# Solar Power Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers interested in solar power installation. Please fill out the form completely and accurately so that we can provide a tailored quote for your system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Provide your contact information, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your address, including your street address, city, state, and postal code.
3. Select your preferred method of contact.
4. Enter your solar panel preferences.
5. Enter the system capacity, if applicable.
6. Select your preferred installation date and time.
7. Enter your roof area, if applicable.
8. Select your energy use.
9. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info**: Enter your first and last name to help us contact you.
* **address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **contact_method**: Select how you would like us to contact you (e.g. phone, email, or mail).
* **solar_panel_preferences**: Enter any specific preferences you have for your solar panel system.
* **system_capacity**: Enter your system capacity, if applicable.
* **installation_date**: Select your preferred installation date.
* **preferred_installation_time**: Select your preferred installation time.
* **roof_area**: Enter your roof area, if applicable.
* **energy_use**: Select your energy use (e.g. for your home, business, or other).
* **notes**: If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **system_type**: Select yes or no for system type.
* **system_type_2**: Select multiple system types, separated by commas.
* **financing**: Select yes or no for financing.
* **financing_2**: Select multiple financing options, separated by commas.
* **address_2**: Enter a secondary address, if applicable.
* **city**: Enter your city.
* **state**: Enter your state.
* **postal_code**: Enter your postal code.
* **installation_preferences**: Enter any specific installation preferences.
* **preferred_installation_date**: Select your preferred installation date.
* **preferred_installation_time**: Select your preferred installation time.
* **system_size**: Enter your system size, if applicable.
