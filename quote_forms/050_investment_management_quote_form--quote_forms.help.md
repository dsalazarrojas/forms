# Investment Management Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Management Quote Form is designed to collect information from clients and advisors about their investment inquiries and preferences. This form is intended to support investment management activities by gathering relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Provide contact details in the **Contact** field.
3. Describe the investment inquiries and preferences in the **Investment Inquiries** field.
4. Enter any relevant advisor details in the **Advisor Details** field.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the **Additional Comments** field.
6. Enter any relevant investment details in the **Investment Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (Client Name, text, optional):** Enter the name of the client.
* **Contact (Contact, text, optional):** Provide any relevant contact information for the client.
* **Investment Inquiries (Investment Inquiries, text, optional):** Describe the client's investment inquiries and preferences.
* **Advisor Details (Advisor Details, text, optional):** Enter any relevant advisor details.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional):** Add any additional comments or information about the client's inquiry.
* **Investment Details (Investment Details, text, optional):** Enter any relevant investment details.
