# Disneyland Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disneyland Quote Request Form is used to gather information about your trip plans to Disneyland. This information helps our team provide you with a personalized quote for your travel needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your trip details, such as the type of trip you're planning.
2. Let us know who will be traveling with you, such as Disney or other travel agents.
3. Choose the travel planner that best fits your needs.
4. Select the type of trip you'll be taking.
5. Enter the number of travelers, adults, children, and rooms needed.
6. If applicable, enter the check-in and check-out dates for your trip.
7. Review your information carefully and submit the form to receive your personalized quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your trip, including any specific requests or requirements.
* **Travel Agents** (`travel_agents`, select_multiple, required): Choose the travel agents who will be assisting you, such as Disney or other travel agents.
* **Travel Planners** (`travel_planners`, select_one, required): Select the travel planner that best fits your needs, such as Disneyland, Walt Disney World, or Disney Cruise.
* **Trip Type** (`trip_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of trip you'll be taking, such as Vacation, Business, or Pleasure.
* **Trip Length** (`trip_length`, number, optional): Enter the number of days for your trip (if applicable).
* **Check-in Date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Enter the check-in date for your trip (if applicable).
* **Check-out Date** (`checkout_date`, date, optional): Enter the check-out date for your trip (if applicable).
* **Number of Adults** (`num_adults`, number, optional): Enter the number of adults traveling with you.
* **Number of Children** (`num_children`, number, optional): Enter the number of children traveling with you.
* **Number of Rooms** (`num_rooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of rooms needed for your trip.
* **Number of Travelers** (`num_travelers`, number, optional): Enter the total number of people traveling with you.
