# Insurance Form Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to collect personal and demographic information, medical history, and vehicle details from individuals. It is intended for general data collection purposes, such as assessing eligibility for insurance coverage or data research.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each page carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
* Fill in the required fields with your personal details, medical history, and vehicle information.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal History** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a brief description of your personal history, including any relevant background information.
* **Family History** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your family medical history, including any relevant conditions or concerns.
* **Household History** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a brief overview of your household composition, including family members and their health conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): List any existing medical conditions or health concerns you may have.
* **Medication History** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Record your current medications and dosages, if applicable.
* **Employment Info** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Share your employment status, occupation, and work hours.
* **Education History** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your education background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Insurance Info** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any relevant insurance details, such as policy numbers or coverage types.
* **Medical Professional Info** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): If applicable, include any relevant medical professional information, such as doctors or medical institutions you have visited.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): List any emergency contacts, including names and phone numbers.
* **Address** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your current address for correspondence purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`12`, `date`, required: `false`): Provide your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (`13`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`14`, `email`, required: `false`): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Social Media Links** (`15`, `text`, required: `false`): If applicable, share your social media links or profiles.
* **Vehicle Info** (`16`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your vehicle type, make, and model.
* **Vehicle Miles** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): Record your total vehicle mileage.
* **Vehicle Make** (`18`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`19`, `text`, required: `false`): Record your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`20`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Color** (`21`, `text`, required: `false`): Record your vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle License** (`22`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`23`, `text`, required: `false`): Record your vehicle's registration details.
* **Driver License** (`24`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide your driver's license number or details.
* **Driver Experience** (`25`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your driving experience, including years and type of driving (e.g., commercial, personal).

## Tips

* Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
* Ensure you have permission from any family members or emergency contacts listed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support team.
