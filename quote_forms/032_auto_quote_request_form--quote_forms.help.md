# Auto Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to generate an auto quote request. It helps us understand your contact details, vehicle requirements, and personal information to provide a personalized quote for your auto insurance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide your vehicle's make and model, year, and estimated annual mileage.
4. Indicate your eligibility for student, military, and safe driver discounts.
5. Specify the number of drivers on your policy.
6. If applicable, mention any specific coverage requests or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (id: 2): *Enter your full name, as this will be the primary contact person.*
* **Email Address** (id: 3): *Enter your email address for quote delivery.*
* **Phone Number** (id: 4): *Enter your best contact number for quote delivery and follow-up.*
* **City and State** (id: 5): *Enter your primary location, where the vehicle is typically parked or driven.*
* **Vehicle Make and Model** (id: 7): *Enter your vehicle's make and model, e.g., 'Nissan Sentra' or 'Toyota Camry'.*
* **Vehicle Year** (id: 8): *Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.*
* **Estimated Annual Mileage** (id: 9): *Enter the estimated miles you drive in a year.*
* **Student Discount Eligible** (id: 11): *Check if you are a full-time student with good grades.*
* **Military Discount Eligible** (id: 12): *Check if you are an active duty or veteran military personnel.*
* **Safe Driver Program Interest** (id: 13): *Check if you are interested in using a telematics device for lower rates.*
* **Number of Drivers** (id: 14): *Enter the number of drivers on your policy.*
* **Specific Coverage Requests** (id: 15): *Mention any special coverage requests or additional information.*

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only, and a representative will review and contact you to confirm your quote details.
