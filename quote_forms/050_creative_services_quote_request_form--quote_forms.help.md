**Creative Services Quote Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from clients or users for quote requests related to creative services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information about yourself and your project.
2. Enter your contact email address and contact number for internal communication.
3. Specify your project details, including budget allocation, expected delivery date, estimated time, project description, and any relevant project files.
4. Select the project status and quote request options that best fit your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name and other relevant details about yourself.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of your client or company.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your contact email address for internal communication.
* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description**: Enter a detailed description of your project.
* **Budget Allocation**: Select the budget allocation that best fits your project needs.
* **Expected Delivery Date**: Select the expected delivery date for your project.
* **Estimated Time**: Enter the estimated time required for the project.
* **Project Files**: Upload any relevant project files or documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address.
* **Contact Message**: Enter any additional contact information or messages.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of your project.
* **Quote Request**: Select the type of quote request you need.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please fill in as much information as possible to help us better understand your needs.
