# Detailed Service Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to gather details about a service request. Please fill it out to provide the necessary information about your service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to focus on it, then fill out the required information.
- Make sure to complete all required fields (those marked as required: true).
- For fields with options, select the most accurate answer.
- If you need help with a field, hover over the label for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule An Appointment** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Service Description** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a clear and concise description of the service you need.
* **Service Type** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need immediate service or 'No' for non-urgent services.
* **Service Type List** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options if you need multiple services.
* **Additional Service Details** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any extra details about the service you need.
* **Additional Service Details List** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter multiple details if they are relevant to your service request.
* **Appointment Date** (`id: 7`, `type: date`, required: false): Choose a date for your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`id: 8`, `type: time`, required: false): Select a time for your scheduled appointment.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`id: 10`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Name** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your name as it would appear on any appointment-related documentation.
* **Phone** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`id: 13`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Note** (`id: 14`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes for the staff assigned to your service.
* **Comment** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your service request.
* **Assigned Staff** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you prefer a specific staff member be assigned to your service.
