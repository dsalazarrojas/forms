# Health Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Quote Form is a form designed to collect relevant information for quoting health insurance options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Job Title to provide personal and business information.
3. Enter your phone number and email address to allow us to contact you.
4. Select your state and country from the provided options.
5. Choose the type and coverage of health insurance you are interested in.
6. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Insurance Quote Form Name** (`health_insurance_quote_form_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide the name of the quote form.
* **Phone** (`health_insurance_quote_form_phone`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`health_insurance_quote_form_email`, email, required):
	+ This field is used to provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`health_insurance_quote_form_date`, date, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide a date related to your insurance quote.
* **Time** (`health_insurance_quote_form_time`, time, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide a time related to your insurance quote.
* **Note** (`health_insurance_quote_form_note`, note, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes related to your insurance quote.
* **First Name** (`health_insurance_quote_form_first_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (`health_insurance_quote_form_last_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide your last name.
* **Company** (`health_insurance_quote_form_company`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide the name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`health_insurance_quote_form_job_title`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide your job title.
* **Address** (`health_insurance_quote_form_address`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide your business address.
* **City** (`health_insurance_quote_form_city`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide your city.
* **State** (`health_insurance_quote_form_state`, select_one, required):
	+ This field is used to select your state.
* **Zip** (`health_insurance_quote_form_zip`, text, required):
	+ This field is used to provide your zip code.
* **Country** (`health_insurance_quote_form_country`, select_one, required):
	+ This field is used to select your country.
* **Type** (`health_insurance_quote_form_type`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field is used to select the type of health insurance you are interested in (Individual or Group).
* **Coverage** (`health_insurance_quote_form_coverage`, select_one, required):
	+ This field is used to select the coverage you are interested in (True or False).
