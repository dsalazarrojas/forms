# delivery_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for delivery quotations. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and efficient delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide the necessary details for your quotation request. You can fill out the form in sections, and each section has a clear label and description to guide you through the process.
2. Start by providing your Quotation Details, which includes the date and other relevant information about your request.
3. Next, enter your customer name and contact information, including your email address.
4. Select the product you're interested in from the provided options.
5. Enter the quantity of the product you require.
6. If you have a preferred delivery address, select it from the available options.
7. Provide your contact number so we can get back to you.
8. Finally, enter any additional message you'd like to provide about your quotation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quotation Details** (`Quotation Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your quotation request, including any relevant details about your product needs.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you'd like to receive your delivery.
* **Product** (`product`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the product you're interested in from the available options.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of the product you require.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your preferred delivery address from the available options.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can get back to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication and confirmation.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your quotation request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field to ensure a smooth delivery process.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
* We'll respond to your quotation request as soon as possible after receiving this form.
