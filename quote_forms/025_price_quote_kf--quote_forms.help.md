# price_quote_kf - Help Guide
## Purpose
The price_quote_kf form is designed to collect information for creating price quotes. It includes sections for quote details, contact information, and quote terms acceptance. This form helps to ensure accurate and clear communication between the sales team and the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Complete the "quote form" section with the relevant details about the quote.
2. Enter contact information, including the name and email address of the person to contact.
3. Select the acceptance terms for the quote from the provided options.
4. Accept or decline the terms that are deemed "important".
5. Add any additional comments or terms to the "quote terms" section.
6. Sign the form with your digital signature.
7. Enter the date and time of the quote in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote form (1)**: Enter a brief description of the quote, such as the product or service being quoted.
* **Quote details (2)**: Provide a detailed description of the quote, including any relevant notes or comments.
* **Contact info (3)**: Select the name of the person to contact from the provided options, or enter a custom name.
* **Contact email (4)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact phone (5)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Quote terms (6)**: Add any additional terms or conditions to the quote, such as payment terms or deadlines.
* **Quote terms acceptance (7)**: Select whether you agree to the quote terms.
* **Quote terms (8)**: Add any important terms or conditions to the quote, such as warranties or disclaimers.
* **Quote terms acceptance checkbox (9)**: Select whether you agree to the important terms, or select "no" if you don't accept them.
* **Quote terms important checkbox (10)**: Select whether you agree to the important terms, or select "no" if you don't accept them.
* **Signature (11)**: Add your digital signature to confirm you've read and agreed to the quote terms.
* **Quote date (12)**: Enter the date of the quote.
* **Quote time (13)**: Enter the time of the quote.
* **Quote price (14)**: Enter the price of the quote.

## Tips

- Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
- Review the quote terms carefully before accepting or declining them.
- Sign and date the quote to confirm your agreement.
