# Service Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Estimate Form is used to estimate costs and gather essential information for services offered by the company. It is completed by customers to provide details about their service requests, and employees use it for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name in the text field provided.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name in the text field provided.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address in the format example@example.com.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the text field provided.
5. **Service Type**: Select the service type you are interested in from the drop-down options.
6. **Estimate At**: Select the date you would like to estimate for in the calendar picker.
7. **Estimate For**: Select the purpose of the estimate from the drop-down options.
8. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the service you require.
9. **Services**: Select the services you are interested in from the drop-down options.
10. **Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of the service in numerical format.
11. **Tax Rate**: Select the tax rate from the drop-down options.
12. **Discount**: Enter the discount percentage (if any).
13. **Total**: The total cost will be calculated automatically based on the cost and tax rate.
14. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Service Type** (Service Type, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select the type of service you are interested in.
* **Estimate At** (Estimate At, date, required/optional): This field is used to select the date you would like to estimate for.
* **Estimate For** (Estimate For, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select the purpose of the estimate.
* **Description** (Description, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the service you require.
* **Services** (Services, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select the services you are interested in.
* **Cost** (Cost, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter the estimated cost of the service in numerical format.
* **Tax Rate** (Tax Rate, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select the tax rate.
* **Discount** (Discount, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter the discount percentage (if any).
* **Total** (Total, number, required/optional): This field is automatically calculated based on the cost and tax rate.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about the estimate.
