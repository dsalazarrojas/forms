# Printing Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Printing Quotation Request Form is designed to collect information from customers about their printing needs. It's essential to understand their requirements to provide an accurate quotation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information, and submit it for further processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Printing Quotation Request** (`printing_quotation_request_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the main title of the form.
  - Please enter a brief description of the printing request.
- **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name, email, or phone number so that we can get in touch with them about their printing job.
- **Paper Size** (`paper_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the paper you require for your printing job. Options include:
  - A3
  - A5
  - A4
  - A6
  - Please select the appropriate paper size for your job.
- **Paper Orientation** (`paper_orientation`, select_one, optional): Select the orientation of the paper you require. Options include:
  - Portrait
  - Landscape
  - Please choose the orientation that suits your printing needs.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of copies you require for your printing job.
- **Color** (`color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color scheme you require for your printing job. Options include:
  - Black and White
  - Color
  - Please select the color option that suits your needs.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or requirements for your printing job, please enter them here.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address so that we can send them the quotation and any further updates.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number so that we can contact them about their printing job.
- **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about the customer or the printing job, please enter it here.
