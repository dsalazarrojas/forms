3.  Enter the **Departure City** and **Destination City** in their respective text fields. This information is crucial for the agency to determine the flight route and calculate the quote.
4.  Choose a **Departure Date** and an optional **Return Date**, if applicable. Note that the return date will only be required for round trips.
5.  Enter the **Number of Passengers** for the flight. This will help determine the size of the aircraft needed and the quote accordingly.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Flight Details** (`-- Flight Details --`, `note`, required/optional): This section is a note section where you can provide any additional information about your travel requirements.
*   **Preferred Aircraft Type** (aircraft_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of aircraft you prefer for your trip. This could be Very Light Jet, Light Jet, Midsize Jet, Super Midsize Jet, Large Cabin Jet, Turbo Prop, or Helicopter.
*   **Trip Type** (trip_type, select_one, required): Select whether the trip is One Way, Round Trip, or Multi City. This will influence the quote based on the type of trip.
*   **Departure City** (departure_city, text, required): Enter the city or airport code for your departure location.
*   **Destination City** (destination_city, text, required): Enter the city or airport code for your destination.
*   **Departure Date** (departure_date, date, required): Select the date you wish to depart.
*   **Return Date** (return_date, date, optional): Only required for round trips. Select the date you wish to return if you have selected Round Trip as your trip type.
*   **Number of Passengers** (number_of_passengers, number, required): Enter the number of passengers traveling with you.
*   **Contact Information** (`-- Contact Information --`, `note`, required/optional): A note section where you can enter any additional contact information.
*   **Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as the contact person for the quote.
*   **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number for the agency to contact you.
*   **Additional Requirements or Comments** (additional_requirements, text, required/optional): You can add any special requests, such as catering, ground transportation, or specific amenities.

### Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy, especially when providing city or airport codes.

*   For round trips, be sure to select the return date to ensure an accurate quote.
*   If you have any special requirements or comments, please let the agency know in the **Additional Requirements or Comments** field.
