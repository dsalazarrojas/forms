# Wedding Organizing Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from clients who are planning a wedding and require services from a wedding planner or organizer. The form is meant to collect basic contact and wedding details, as well as specific services needed for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling out the "Couple Info" section with your name and any other relevant information about yourself and your partner.
2.  In the "Contact Details" section, provide your contact information, such as phone number and email.
3.  In the "Wedding Details" section, provide more specific information about your wedding, including the date and location.
4.  In the "Wedding Services" section, select the specific services you need for your wedding.
5.  In the "Vendors" section, select the specific vendors you require for your wedding.
6.  In the "Budget" section, provide a rough estimate of your budget for the wedding.
7.  In the "Payment Terms" section, discuss any specific payment terms or conditions you require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Couple Info** (`couple_info`, text, optional): This is where you can provide general information about yourself and your partner.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This is where you can provide your contact information, such as phone number and email.
*   **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, text, optional): This is where you can provide more specific information about your wedding, including the date and location.
*   **Wedding Services** (`wedding_services`, select_multiple, optional): This is where you can select the specific services you need for your wedding, such as ceremony and reception venue selection, wedding party coordination, and wedding day coordination.
*   **Vendors** (`vendor_list`, select_multiple, optional): This is where you can select the specific vendors you require for your wedding, such as venue, caterer, photographer, and wedding planner.
*   **Budget** (`budget`, text, optional): This is where you can provide a rough estimate of your budget for the wedding.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): This is where you can discuss any specific payment terms or conditions you require.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
*   If you have any specific questions or requests, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   This form is meant to be a starting point for the client to provide information and is not meant to be a comprehensive wedding planning tool.
*   We will use the information provided in this form to provide a quote for the services you require.
