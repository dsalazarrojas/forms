# Administrative Assistant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and submit quotes for administrative services. Please fill it out carefully to provide all required information for a comprehensive quote.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure it is correct.
- Submit the form to generate a quote for your services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client information
* **Client information** (`client_information`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note section for any additional information about the client. You can write any relevant details here, but it is not required for the quote.

### 2. Client name
* **Client name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the company or individual requesting the quote.

### 3. Contact email
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter the email address of the person who will receive the quote and any further communication.

### 4. Services required
* **Services required** (`services_required`, `text`, required: true): Describe the administrative services you are requesting. Be as detailed as possible for an accurate quote.

### 5. Estimated hours
* **Estimated hours per month** (`estimated_hours`, `number`, required: true): Approximate the number of hours you need per month for the services.

### 6. Hourly rate
* **Hourly rate USD** (`hourly_rate`, `number`, required: true): Enter your proposed billing rate per hour.

### 7. Monthly estimate
* **Monthly estimate USD** (`monthly_estimate`, `number`, required: false): This field is for the calculated estimate for the client's budget. Please fill it out if you have a calculated estimate.

### 8. Preferred start date
* **Preferred start date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a preferred start date for the service, please enter it here.

### 9. Special terms
* **Special terms or conditions** (`special_terms`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special terms or conditions that need to be included in the contract or Service Level Agreement (SLA).

### 10. Approval signature name
* **Approval signature name** (`approval_signature`, `text`, required: true): Type your name to approve the quote and confirm your understanding of the services and terms.
