# Digital Advertising Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request quotes for digital advertising services from our agency. Please provide accurate and detailed information to help us understand your needs and provide a comprehensive quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields (marked as required).
2. Be as detailed as possible in providing information about your campaign.
3. Submit the form to initiate the quote request process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Required, Text): This field is for your name and should be filled in with the name of the person requesting the quote.
* **Agency** (Required, Select One): Select 'Yes' if you are affiliated with an agency, 'No' otherwise.
* **Budget** (Required, Number): Enter the budget for your advertising campaign.
* **Campaign Type** (Required, Select Multiple): Select all applicable campaign types that apply to your request.
* **Start Date** (Required, Date): Enter the start date of your campaign.
* **End Date** (Required, Date): Enter the end date of your campaign.
* **Campaign Duration** (Required, Number): Enter the duration of your campaign.
* **Media Budget** (Optional, Number): Enter the media budget for your campaign.
* **Agency Location** (Optional, Text): Enter the location of the agency you are affiliated with.
* **Manager Name** (Optional, Text): Enter the name of the manager you are working with.
* **Contact Name** (Optional, Text): Enter the name of the person you would like to be the contact person.
* **Contact E-Mail** (Required, Email): Enter the email address of the person you would like to be the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional, Text): Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be the contact person.
* **Notes** (Optional, Note): Enter any additional notes or details about your campaign.
* **Contact person** (Optional, Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
