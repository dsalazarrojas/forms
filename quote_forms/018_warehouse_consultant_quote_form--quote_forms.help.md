# Warehouse Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Consultant Quote Form is designed to generate a quote for warehouse consulting services. It collects information about the services offered, costs involved, and terms of service to provide a comprehensive quote.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a brief description of the services you would like to be offered by the consultant.
2. Enter the costs involved in the consultation process.
3. Specify the terms of service, including any conditions, limitations, or requirements.
4. Review the quote and ensure it accurately reflects your needs.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote Form** (`quote_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the services you would like to be offered by the consultant.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, optional): Describe the specific services you require from the consultant.
* **Services Description** (`services_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the services offered.
* **Costs Involved** (`costs_involved`, text, optional): Enter the costs associated with the consultation process.
* **Terms of Service - Services** (`terms_services`, text, optional): Specify any conditions, limitations, or requirements for the services offered.
* **Terms of Service - Costs** (`terms_cost`, text, optional): Describe any conditions, limitations, or requirements for the costs involved.
* **Terms of Service - General** (`terms_general`, text, optional): Specify any general terms of service that apply to the quote.
* **Terms of Service - Payment** (`terms_payment`, text, optional): Describe the payment terms and conditions.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details that need to be included in the quote.
* **Quote Signature** (`quote_signature`, select one, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the quote is ready to be submitted, or 'No' if the quote needs further review.
* **Submit** (`quote_submit`, select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to submit the quote, or 'No' if you'd like to review or make further changes.
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the quote is valid for.
* **Quote Signature** (`quote_signature`, select one, optional): This field is redundant and should not be filled out, please do not fill it out.
* **Submit** (`quote_submit`, select multiple, optional): This field is redundant and should not be filled out, please do not fill it out.
 
# Note: Please review the form carefully and ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information to generate a comprehensive quote.
