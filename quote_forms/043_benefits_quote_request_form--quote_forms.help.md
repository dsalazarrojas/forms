# Benefits Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your benefits requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal details in the "Requester Information" section.
2. Select your company size, industry, and provide your email address and phone number.
3. In the "Benefits Inquiry Details" section, select the type(s) of benefits you are interested in and specify your coverage type needs.
4. Specify your budget range (if known) and any specific requirements or preferences.
5. Select a timeline for implementation and preferred contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it is registered.
* **Company Size**: Select the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Industry**: Enter your company's industry.
* **Email Address**: Enter your company's or individual's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your company's or individual's phone number.
* **Type of Benefits Interested In**: Select all relevant options for health, dental, and life insurance, as well as other types of benefits.
* **Coverage Type Needed**: Select the type of coverage you need, such as employee only or family coverage.
* **Current Benefit Coverage Status**: Select your current benefit coverage status, such as no current coverage or looking to switch.
* **Estimated Budget Range**: Select the estimated monthly cost per employee.
* **Specific Requirements or Preferences**: Enter any special needs or must-haves.
* **Implementation Timeline**: Select the time frame you need to implement the benefits.
* **Do You Have an Existing Broker**: Check if you have an existing broker.
* **Have You Requested Quotes Before**: Check if you have requested quotes before.
* **Additional Information or Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method, such as email or phone.
