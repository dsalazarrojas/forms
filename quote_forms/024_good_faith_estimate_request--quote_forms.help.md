# Good Faith Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a good faith estimate. You will be asked to provide information to support your claim or request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully before submitting your request.
2. Select the services requested (Yes or No) from the list.
3. Enter the estimated date and time (if applicable) for the services requested.
4. Confirm that you will receive receipts for the services (Yes or No).
5. Choose a confirmation date for receiving the receipts.
6. Select the method for receiving the receipts (Yes or No).
7. Enter any relevant comments about the receipts.
8. Provide your billing information (Yes or No) if applicable.
9. Enter your signature and name in the respective fields.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
12. Enter any additional notes.
13. Agree to the terms and conditions (Yes or No).
14. Agree to the privacy terms (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Requested**: Choose the services you are requesting (Yes or No) from the list. This will help us to better understand your needs.
* **Itemized Estimate**: Enter a detailed estimate of the services requested.
* **Estimated Date**: Enter the estimated date for the services requested (if applicable).
* **Estimated Time**: Enter the estimated time for the services requested (if applicable).
* **Confirm Receipts**: Confirm that you will receive receipts for the services requested (Yes or No).
* **Confirm Date**: Choose a date for receiving the receipts.
* **Confirm Method**: Select how you will receive the receipts (Yes or No).
* **Confirm Comments**: Enter any comments about receiving the receipts.
* **Billing Info**: Provide your billing information (Yes or No) if applicable.
* **Submitter Signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Submitter Agree to Terms**: Agree to the terms and conditions (Yes or No).
* **Submitter Agree to Privacy**: Agree to the privacy terms (Yes or No).
