# Graphic Configuration Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a graphic configuration estimate request from a submitter. It will help us understand the project requirements and feasibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address as the submitter.
2. Provide a brief description of the project in the Request Details section.
3. Select the design type, format, and other relevant details.
4. Enter estimated budget and deadline for the project.
5. Fill in any relevant details about your department and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Details
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project.

### Design Type
* **Design Type** (`design_type`, `text`, required: false): Select the type of design required (Graphic, Digital, Branding).

### Color Scheme
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, `text`, required: false): Enter the color scheme for the design.

### Design Format
* **Design Format** (`design_format`, `text`, required: false): Select the format of the design (Brochure, Print Ad, Packaging).

### Estimate Needs
* **Estimate Needs** (`estimate_needs`, `text`, required: false): Provide any specific needs or requirements for the design.

### Estimated Budget
* **Estimated Budget** (`estimated_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated budget for the project.

### Estimated Time
* **Estimated Time** (`estimated_time`, `text`, required: false): Enter the estimated time required for the project.

### Design Request Deadline
* **Design Request Deadline** (`design_request_deadline`, `date`, required: false): Enter the deadline for the design request.

### Review Date
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the review date for the design.

### Submitter
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the submitter.

### Submitter Email
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the submitter.

### Submitter Phone
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as the submitter.

### Agency or Team
* **Agency or Team** (`agency_or_team`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the submitter is an agency or team.

### Team Members
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, required: false): Enter the team members involved in the project.

### Project Manager
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, required: false): Enter the project manager's name and contact information.

### Client
* **Client** (`client`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name and contact information.

### Client Phone
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.

### Client Email
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the project.

### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of submission.

### Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the submission time.

### Design Type 1
* **Design Type 1** (`design_type_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the design type (Graphic, Digital, Branding).

### Design Type 2
* **Design Type 2** (`design_type_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional design types (Brochure, Print Ad, Packaging).

### Submitter Department
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department as the submitter.

### Submitter Title
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your title as the submitter.

Tips:
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about the project to ensure a proper estimate.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors or omissions.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the support team for assistance.
* This form will help us understand your project requirements and feasibility, and we will get back to you with a proper estimate.
