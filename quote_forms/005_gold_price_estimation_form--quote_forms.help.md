# Gold Price Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and analyze data to make an estimate of the price of gold items. The form gathers information such as item weight, type, size, and customer preferences to help make an educated estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of gold item by choosing the relevant option from the "Item Type" field.
2. Enter the weight of the gold item in the "Weight" field. If the item's weight is not known, please select the "Unknown" option.
3. If applicable, select the size of the gold item in the "Item Size" field.
4. Choose the type of metal used in the gold item from the "Item Metal Type" field.
5. Enter the carat weight of the gold item, if known.
6. Provide any relevant customer information in the "Customer Info" field.
7. Enter any notes or comments about the gold item in the "Customer Note" field.
8. If you would like to indicate whether the customer is making an inquiry, select "True" or "False" in the "Customer Inquiry" field.
9. If you would like to provide a customer estimate of the price, select one or more options from the "Customer Estimate" field.
10. Finally, provide any additional comments or information from the customer in the "Customer Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weight** (item_weight, number, optional): Please enter the weight of the gold item. If you are unsure of the weight, select "Unknown".
* **Item Type** (item_type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of gold item you are estimating.
* **Item Size** (item_size, number, optional): If applicable, enter the size of the gold item.
* **Item Metal Type** (item_metal_type, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of metal used in the gold item.
* **Item Carat Weight** (item_carat_weight, number, optional): Enter the carat weight of the gold item, if known.
* **Customer Info** (customer_info, text, optional): Provide any relevant customer information that may aid in the estimate.
* **Customer Note** (customer_note, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the gold item.
* **Customer Inquiry** (customer_inquiry, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the customer is making an inquiry about the gold item.
* **Customer Estimate** (customer_estimate, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more customer estimate options.
* **Customer Message** (customer_message, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information from the customer.
