# Cybersecurity Audit Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your project and request a quote for our cybersecurity audit services. Please fill out the form below to help us better understand your needs and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your project details in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (*): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (*): Enter your name and job title.
* **Email** (*): Enter your email address.
* **Project Type** (*):
	+ Select "Yes" if you are requesting a full cybersecurity audit.
	+ Select "No" if you are not requesting a full audit or if you have specific questions.
* **Project Description** (*): Briefly describe your project and any specific services you require.
* **Start Date** (*): Enter the date you plan to start your project.
* **End Date** (*): Enter the date you plan to complete your project.
* **Name** (*): Enter your name.
* **Phone** (*): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (*): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (*): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Service Needed** (*):
	+ Select "Yes" if you need our full cybersecurity audit service.
	+ Select "No" if you only need specific services or have questions.
* **Service Details** (*): Describe the specific services you need or have questions about.
* **Deadline** (*): Enter the deadline for your project.
