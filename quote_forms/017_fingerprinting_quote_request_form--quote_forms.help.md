# Fingerprinting Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fingerprinting Quote Request Form is designed to collect information from users for individual or group inquiries, allowing us to provide quotes for their specific needs. This form helps us understand their requirements and preferences, enabling us to offer tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select whether you are submitting a quote request for individual or group inquiries.
2. Choose your preferred schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
3. Provide detailed service details in the text field.
4. Select your preferred services from the list of options.
5. Select your preferred schedule for your quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Individual/Group Inquiries (1)**: Select whether you are submitting a quote request for individual or group inquiries. This will help us understand who the quote is for.
* **Schedule Preferences (3)**: Choose your preferred schedule for your quote request. Please select one of the three options: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Service Details (4)**: Enter a detailed description of the services you require. This will help us understand your specific needs.
* **Service Preferences (5)**: Select the services you are interested in. Please choose one or more of the available options.
* **Schedule (6)**: Select your preferred schedule for the quote request. This will help us ensure we can accommodate your needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to submit accurate and complete information to ensure we can provide a suitable quote.
* If you have any additional requests or requirements, please include them in the "Service Details" field.
* We appreciate your patience as we review and process your quote request.
