# Lawn Topdressing Quote Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for lawn topdressing services. Please fill out this form with your information to receive a quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You can leave any field blank if you are not sure or do not have the information required for that field. Please make sure to complete all fields for which you have information available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (`user_name`, text, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more job types you are interested in (e.g. Grass, Landscaping).
- **Job Area** (`job_area`, select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more areas you are interested in (e.g. Local, Regional).
- **Schedule** (`schedule_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the schedule type you are interested in (e.g. One-time, Ongoing, Recurring).
- **Estimated Job Size** (`estimated_job_size`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated size of the job.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the job.
- **Finish Date** (`finish_date`, date, Optional): Enter the finish date of the job.
- **Job Duration** (`job_duration`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the job duration.
- **Address** (`address`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of address you are referring to (e.g. Single, Duplex, Multi, etc.).
- **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, Optional): Select the method you prefer for follow-up (e.g. Phone, Email, In-person).
- **Follow-up Schedule** (`follow_up_schedule`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the schedule for follow-up (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Estimated Job Cost** (`estimated_job_cost`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated cost of the job.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the payment terms you prefer (e.g. Cash, Check, Credit Card, Online Banking).

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure you receive the best possible quote for your lawn topdressing needs. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
