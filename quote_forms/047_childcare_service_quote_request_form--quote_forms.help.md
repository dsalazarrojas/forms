# Childcare Service Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a quote for childcare services. It is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with the information requested.
2. Answer the select_one type questions by selecting one option from the dropdown list.
3. Answer the select_multiple type questions by selecting one or more options from the dropdown list.
4. Enter your preferred contact method and best contact time for communication.
5. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date): Enter the date when you are requesting this quote, so we can ensure that we can fulfill your requested care schedule.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_guardian_first_name`, text): Enter the first name of the parent or guardian filling out this form.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_guardian_last_name`, text): Enter the last name of the parent or guardian filling out this form.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email): Enter the primary email address for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text): Enter the primary phone number for contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text): Enter the full street address.
* **City** (`city`, text): Enter the city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text): Enter the state or province.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text): Enter the postal or zip code.
* **Service Type Requested** (`service_type_requested`, select_one): Select one of the available options: In-home daycare, Center-based care, Nanny services, Babysitting services, After-school care, Weekend care, Overnight care, Emergency care, Other.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number): Enter the number of children needing care.
* **Children Ages** (`children_ages`, text): List the ages of all children needing care.
* **Care Schedule Needed** (`care_schedule_needed`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Full-time (40+ hours per week), Part-time (20-40 hours per week), Half-time (10-20 hours per week), Occasional or as-needed, Weekend only, Overnight.
* **Days of Week** (`days_of_week`, select_multiple): Select one or more of the available days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
* **Hours Per Day** (`hours_per_day`, text): Enter the number of hours per day needed.
* **Start Date Needed** (`start_date_needed`, date): Enter the date when you need care to begin.
* **Duration of Care** (`duration_of_care`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Ongoing, indefinite, 6 months or more, 3 to 6 months, 1 to 3 months, Less than 1 month, One-time event.
* **Special Needs Care** (`special_needs_care`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Yes, specialized care needed, Yes, minor accommodations, 'False', Unsure.
* **Special Needs Details** (`special_needs_details`, text): Enter a description of the special needs or accommodations required.
* **Meal Services Needed** (`meal_services_needed`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Yes, all meals, Yes, lunch and snacks, Yes, snacks only, No, will provide all food.
* **Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Yes, to school or activities, Yes, from school or activities, Yes, both ways, No transportation needed.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_multiple): Select one or more of the available options: Homework help, Tutoring, Light housekeeping, Laundry, Pet care, None, Other.
* **Preferred Care Environment** (`preferred_care_environment`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Home-like setting, Center-based setting, Outdoor-focused setting, No preference.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Under $200, $200 to $400, $400 to $600, $600 to $800, $800 to $1000, Over $1000, Flexible based on services.
* **Subsidy or Assistance** (`subsidy_or_assistance`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Yes, currently receiving, Yes, plan to apply, 'False', Unsure.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Online search, Referral from friend or family, Social media, Community event, School referral, Other.
* **Additional Questions or Requirements** (`additional_questions_or_requirements`, text): Enter any additional questions or special requirements.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Email, Phone call, Text message, Any method.
* **Best Contact Time** (`best_contact_time`, select_one): Select one of the available options: Morning (8 AM to 12 PM), Afternoon (12 PM to 5 PM), Evening (5 PM to 8 PM), Any time.
