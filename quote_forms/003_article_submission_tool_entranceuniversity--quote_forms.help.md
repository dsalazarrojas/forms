# Article Submission Tool EntranceUniversity - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Article Submission Tool EntranceUniversity is a form designed for authors to submit articles to the University's publication platform. This form collects essential information about the author and the article, including the author's name, email, and affiliation, as well as the article's title, category, keywords, and summary. The form also asks for the article's document and the author's contact information, such as phone number and preferred contact method. The form ensures that authors provide accurate and consistent information, which is then used to process and publish their articles on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Author Full Name** in the text field. This is the name of the person submitting the article.
2. Enter your **Author Email Address** in the email field. This should be a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
3. If applicable, enter your **Author Affiliation or Organization** in the text field.
4. Enter the **Article Title** in the text field. This should be a clear and concise title of the article.
5. Select the **Article Category** from the dropdown menu. Choose the category that best describes the article's content.
6. Enter the **Keywords or Tags** for the article in the text field. Comma-separated list.
7. Enter a brief **Article Summary** in the text field. This should be a short overview of the article's content.
8. Upload your **Article Document** in the text field. Make sure it's a valid file type and size.
9. Enter the **Approximate Word Count** of the article. This will help us understand the scope of the article.
10. Check if the article is an **Original Work - Not Previously Published** in the dropdown menu. If yes, you should also select 'Yes'.
11. Select a valid **Submission Date** from the calendar picker. This is the date when you're submitting the article.
12. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** in the text field. This will help us get in touch with you for further inquiries.
13. Choose your **Preferred Contact Method** from the dropdown menu. Select 'Email', 'Phone', or 'Both' to let us know how you want to be contacted.
14. Enter your **Author Biography** in the text field. This is an optional field to tell us more about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Author Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Author Email Address**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
- **Author Affiliation or Organization**: Optional field to provide your affiliation or organization.
- **Article Title**: Enter the clear and concise title of your article.
- **Article Category**: Select the category that best describes your article's content.
- **Keywords or Tags**: Enter comma-separated keywords or tags related to your article.
- **Article Summary**: Enter a brief overview of your article's content.
- **Article Document**: Upload a valid file type and size of your article.
- **Approximate Word Count**: Enter the approximate word count of your article.
- **Original Work - Not Previously Published**: Check if the article is an original work not previously published. If yes, select 'Yes'.
- **Submission Date**: Select a valid submission date for your article.
- **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to be contacted for further inquiries.
- **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you want to be contacted, 'Email', 'Phone', or 'Both'.
- **Author Biography**: Enter a brief biography of yourself, this is an optional field.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are necessary for processing and publishing your article.
