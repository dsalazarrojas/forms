# Multiple Selection Product Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for product quote requests from customers. It is intended to be used for both sales and internal purposes, helping us better understand customer needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you're providing the correct information.
2. If you've selected "Yes" for "Product Quote Request", please proceed to the next step.
3. Select the relevant products you're interested in from the "Select Products" field.
4. Enter the desired price quote in the "Price Quote" field.
5. Fill in the customer information in the "Customer Information" field, which is a text area where you can provide additional details about your customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Quote Request** (`product_quote_request`, select_multiple, Optional)
	+ This field allows you to select if you're interested in making a product quote request or not.
* **Select Products** (`select_products`, select_one, Optional)
	+ Use this field to select the products you're interested in getting a quote for. You can choose multiple products if necessary.
* **Price Quote** (`price_quote`, number, Optional)
	+ Enter the desired price quote for the selected products.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, note, Optional)
	+ Enter any additional information about your customer in this field.
