# Business Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The business insurance quote form is designed to collect information from customers to provide an accurate and relevant insurance quote for their business.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the header section, which provides some general information about the quote request.
2.  In the `Business name` field, enter your business's legal or trading name.
3.  In the `Contact name` field, enter the name of the primary contact person for the quote.
4.  In the `Contact email` field, enter an email address where the quote can be sent.
5.  In the `Coverage needed` field, select all the types of coverage you require (e.g. Liability, Property, Cyber, etc.).
6.  If your business has a total insured value, enter it in the `Estimated value at risk` field.
7.  If you have multiple locations, enter the number of locations that need to be covered in the `Number of locations` field.
8.  In the `Prior claims` field, provide any details about prior claims in the last 5 years.
9.  Finally, in the `Preferred contact method` field, select the best way you prefer to be contacted (e.g. Email, Phone, In person).
10.  In the `Additional notes` field, provide any other relevant information that may be required for the quote.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Quote Request --**: This is a note to provide a general title for the quote request.
*   **Business name**: Enter your business's legal or trading name.
*   **Contact name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for the quote.
*   **Contact email**: Enter an email address where the quote can be sent.
*   **Coverage needed**: Select all the types of coverage you require (e.g. Liability, Property, Cyber, etc.).
*   **Estimated value at risk**: If your business has a total insured value, enter it here.
*   **Number of locations**: If you have multiple locations, enter the number of locations that need to be covered.
*   **Prior claims**: Briefly describe any prior claims in the last 5 years.
*   **Preferred contact method**: Select the best way you prefer to be contacted (e.g. Email, Phone, In person).
*   **Additional notes**: Provide any other information that may be required for the quote.
## Tips

*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
