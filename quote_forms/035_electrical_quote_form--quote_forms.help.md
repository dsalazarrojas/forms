# Electrical Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about customers and their job details for quote generation and other administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) if applicable.
2. Make sure to enter the correct information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (1)**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last name (2)**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address. This must be a valid email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date visited site (5)**: Enter the date you visited the site.
* **Site address (6)**: Enter the address of the site you visited.
* **Site contact (7)**: Select the site contact person from the list.
* **Site contact phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the site contact.
* **Site contact email (9)**: Enter the email address of the site contact.
* **Job description (10)**: Enter a brief description of the job to be done.
* **Job type (11)**: Select all applicable job types from the list.
* **Customer address (12)**: Enter the address of the customer (if different from the site address).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information in each field to ensure correct quote generation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your sales representative for assistance.
* If you are a site contact person, please make sure to update your contact information accurately.
