# Bathroom Furniture Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps customers submit their bathroom furniture quote requests, providing essential details for our team to provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful quote request, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Delivery Address** (including city and state/province).
2. Select the type of furniture you're looking for from the **Furniture Type** options.
3. Choose your **Material Preference** from the available options.
4. Enter the **Required Width (Inches)** and **Required Depth (Inches)** of the furniture, if applicable.
5. Select a **Countertop Material** if your chosen type of furniture has a countertop.
6. Indicate your **Preferred Color or Finish** for the furniture.
7. Enter the **Quantity** of furniture you need.
8. If you have any **Special Requirements or Customizations**, please provide details in this field.
9. Choose when you would like to receive the furniture by selecting the desired **Urgency Level**.
10. Finally, select the **Date of Inquiry** for the quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted regarding the quote.
* **Delivery Address**: Provide a detailed address where the furniture can be delivered, including city and state/province.
* **Furniture Type**: Select all that apply, as different types of furniture may have different options.
* **Material Preference**: Choose your preferred material type for the furniture.
* **Required Width (Inches)**: If applicable, enter the width in inches for the chosen furniture type.
* **Required Depth (Inches)**: If applicable, enter the depth in inches for the chosen furniture type.
* **Countertop Material**: If your chosen furniture type has a countertop, select the material type.
* **Preferred Color or Finish**: Enter your preferred color or finish for the furniture.
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of furniture you need.
* **Special Requirements or Customizations**: If you have any special requests, such as hinges or handle styles, enter them in this field.
* **Urgency Level**: Indicate when you would like to receive the furniture.
* **Date of Inquiry**: Select the date you are submitting the quote request.
