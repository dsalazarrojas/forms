# generalInquiryRequestForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general inquiries from customers, which can be reviewed and addressed by assigned users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Select the appropriate inquiry type from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide detailed information about the inquiry in the "Inquiry Details" field.
4. Specify your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Mail) in the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
5. Confirm that you are a customer (self, user, or agent) and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Inquiry Type** (Inquiry Type): Select the type of inquiry you are submitting, such as Phone, Email, or Mail.
* **Inquiry Details** (Inquiry Details): Provide a detailed description of the inquiry.
* **Customer Request** (Customer Request): Enter any additional information or requests you have.
* **Business Information** (Business Information): Enter any business-related information that may be relevant to the inquiry.
* **Sales Channel** (Sales Channel): Select the channel through which you would like to be contacted (Yes or No).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Customer Notes** (Customer Notes): Enter any notes or comments you have about the inquiry.
* **Business Notes** (Business Notes): Enter any business-related notes or comments.
* **Assigned User** (Assigned User): Select the user who should handle the inquiry (chatjimmy or chatjimmy2).
* **Submitted At** (Submitted At): The date and time the inquiry was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By): The user who submitted the inquiry (self, user, or agent).
* **Customer Confirmation** (Customer Confirmation): Confirm that you are a customer (Yes, No, or Unknown).
* **Business Response** (Business Response): Confirm that the business has responded (Yes, No, or Unknown).
* **Assigned Status** (Assigned Status): The current status of the inquiry (New, In Progress, or Resolved).
* **Resolved By** (Resolved By): The user who resolved the inquiry (chatjimmy or user or agent).
* **Resolved At** (Resolved At): The date and time the inquiry was resolved.
* **Submitted At Time** (Submitted At Time): The time the inquiry was submitted.
* **Submitted By Time** (Submitted By Time): The time the inquiry was submitted.
* **Assigned Status Time** (Assigned Status Time): The time the inquiry was assigned to a user.
* **Resolved Status Time** (Resolved Status Time): The time the inquiry was resolved.
* **Customer Response Time** (Customer Response Time): The time it took for the customer to respond to the inquiry.
* **Business Response Time** (Business Response Time): The time it took for the business to respond to the inquiry.
* **Response Status Time** (Response Status Time): The time the response was sent to the customer.
* **Resolution Time** (Resolution Time): The time it took to resolve the inquiry.
