# Business Internet Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Internet Quote Form is designed to collect business information from clients seeking internet services. It is used to gather relevant details for generating a quote for the requested internet service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Company name: Enter the name of your business.
2. Enter the Contact name: Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the installation and billing.
3. Enter the Contact email: Enter the email address of the contact person for communication purposes.
4. Enter the Service address: Specify the address where the internet service will be installed.
5. Select the Required speed: Choose the target bandwidth you need from the available options.
6. Choose the Contract length: Select the preferred contract term.
7. Enter the Number of users: Approximate the number of concurrent users.
8. Select Additional services: Check the optional services you require (Static IP, Managed WiFi, Network security).
9. Enter the Best time to contact: Specify your preferred contact window.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact name** (`name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the installation and billing.
* **Contact email** (`name`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for communication purposes.
* **Service address** (`name`, `text`, required): Specify the address where the internet service will be installed.
* **Required speed** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Select the target bandwidth you need from the available options (50 Mbps, 100 Mbps, 200 Mbps, 500 Mbps or more).
* **Contract length** (`name`, `select_one`, not required): Select the preferred contract term (Month to month, 12 months, 24 months).
* **Number of users** (`name`, `number`, not required): Approximate the number of concurrent users.
* **Additional services** (`name`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check the optional services you require (Static IP, Managed WiFi, Network security).
* **Best time to contact** (`name`, `text`, not required): Specify your preferred contact window.
