# house_painting_estimate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a house painting estimate. It is intended to gather details about the customer's job address, contact number, and painting services required. The form also asks for the estimate date, painter's name, and painting color. The estimate price and accepted status are included for finalization and confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Type the job address in the "Job Address" field.
3. Provide a description of the job in the "Job Description" field.
4. Enter the customer's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Select the estimate date using the calendar picker or manually enter it in the "Estimate Date" field.
6. Enter the painter's name in the "Painter Name" field.
7. Select the painting services required (Exterior, Interior, Trim, or Doors) in the "Painting Services" field. You can choose one or more options.
8. Enter the painting color in the "Painting Color" field.
9. If necessary, enter the painting size in the "Painting Size" field.
10. Enter the estimated price for the job in the "Estimate Price" field.
11. Select "True" or "False" to indicate if the estimate is accepted in the "Accepted Estimate" field.
12. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: This field is required. Enter the name of the customer.
* **Job Address**: This field is required. Enter the address of the job location.
* **Job Description**: This field is required. Provide a brief description of the job.
* **Contact Number**: This field is required. Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Estimate Date**: This field is required. Select or enter the estimate date for the job.
* **Painter Name**: This field is required. Enter the name of the painter.
* **Painting Services**: This field is not required. Select the services required (Exterior, Interior, Trim, or Doors).
* **Painting Color**: This field is required. Enter the color to be painted.
* **Painting Size**: This field is not required. If applicable, enter the size of the job.
* **Estimate Price**: This field is required. Enter the estimated price for the job.
* **Accepted Estimate**: This field is required. Select "True" if the estimate is accepted, "False" otherwise.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is not required. Add any additional comments or notes about the job.
