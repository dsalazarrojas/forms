# outdoor_security_camera_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information to generate a quote for an outdoor security camera installation or service. Please fill out the form with the required details to help us provide you with an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. **Fill out all required fields** marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer
* **Customer** (`customer`, `text`, required):
 Enter your name and/or contact information to allow us to contact you about the quote.

### Property Info
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, `text`, required):
 Describe the property where the outdoor security camera will be installed, including any relevant details about the location, building, or surroundings.

### Camera Info
* **Camera Info** (`camera_info`, `text`, required):
 Specify the type and number of cameras you require, including any additional features or specifications.

### Quote Info
* **Quote Info** (`quote_info`, `text`, required):
 Provide any special requirements or details about the quote, such as preferred installation dates or any specific services you need.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely for a correct quote.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
