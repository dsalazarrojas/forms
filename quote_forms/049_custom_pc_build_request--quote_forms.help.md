# custom_pc_build_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to request a custom PC build. It's intended for users who are interested in creating a personalized computer configuration according to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name (field 1).
2. Enter your Email (field 2).
3. Provide your Phone number (field 3).
4. Add any relevant Notes (field 4) about your custom PC build requirements.
5. Choose the desired CPU type (field 5).
6. Select the desired Motherboard type (field 6).
7. Choose the desired Graphics type (field 7).
8. Select the desired RAM amount (field 8).
9. Choose the desired Storage type (field 9).
10. Finally, enter any additional comments or instructions in the Submit field (field 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (1): Provide your name as it appears on your identification documents or how you would like to be addressed.
- **Email** (2): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your custom PC build.
- **Phone** (3): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
- **Notes** (4): Add any additional details or specifications you would like to have included in your custom PC build.
- **CPU** (5): Select the desired CPU type according to your needs and preferences. For example, if you want a specific model, select it here.
- **Motherboard** (6): Choose the desired Motherboard type, such as the number of ports, USB ports, and expansion options.
- **Graphics** (7): Select multiple Graphics types that you would like to have included in your custom PC build. For example, if you want NVIDIA and AMD graphics, select both here.
- **RAM** (8): Select the desired RAM amount according to your needs and preferences. For example, 8GB, 16GB, 32GB, or 64GB.
- **Storage** (9): Choose the desired Storage type, such as the capacity and type (e.g., SSD, HDD).
- **Submit** (10): Enter any additional comments or instructions for your custom PC build.
