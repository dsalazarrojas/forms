# Telecommunications Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telecommunications Quote Request form is designed to collect information from customers for quote generation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Contact Person" field.
2. Provide the location of your business or organization in the "Location" field.
3. Enter the street address of your location in the "Street Address" field.
4. Select the type of service you are requesting a quote for from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
5. Indicate how frequently the service will be used in the "Service Frequency" field (e.g., number of times per week).
6. Enter the start and end dates for the service in the "Service Start Date" and "Service End Date" fields, respectively.
7. Add any additional notes about the service in the "Service Notes" field.
8. Repeat steps 2-7 for any additional locations and services you are requesting quotes for.
