# Group Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a group insurance quote, providing essential information about the agent, customer, and quote details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Group Quote section with the relevant information.
2. Enter the Agent's and Customer's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose whether or not to provide Insurance Quotes for the customer.
4. Enter the Premium Amount and select the Coverage Type.
5. Add a Quote Date and Quote Time.
6. Add any Notes about the quote.
7. Select the person who submitted the quote and the person who created it.
8. Choose the Quote Status and enter any additional Quote Status Notes.
9. Finally, submit the form to create the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Quote**: Enter the relevant information about the group quote.
* **Insurance Agent Information**: Enter the name and contact information of the insurance agent.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the insurance agent.
* **Agent Email**: Enter the email address of the insurance agent.
* **Agent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the insurance agent.
* **Customer Information**: Enter any relevant information about the customer.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Insurance Quotes**: Choose whether or not to provide insurance quotes for the customer.
* **Premium Amount**: Enter the premium amount for the quote.
* **Coverage Type**: Select the type of coverage required.
* **Quote Date**: Enter the date when the quote was generated.
* **Quote Time**: Enter the time when the quote was generated.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the quote.
* **Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the quote.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time when the quote was submitted.
* **Submitted By Name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the quote.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted the quote.
* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the quote.
* **Submitted By Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the person who submitted the quote.
* **Quote Status**: Select the status of the quote.
* **Quote Status Notes**: Enter any notes about the quote status.
* **Created By**: Select the person who created the quote.
* **Created By Name**: Enter the name of the person who created the quote.
* **Created On**: Enter the date when the quote was created.
