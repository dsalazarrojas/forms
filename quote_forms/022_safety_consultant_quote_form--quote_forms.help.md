# Safety Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients who are seeking safety consulting services. It will help you gather details about the client, including their contact information, services required, and any additional comments or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
3. Describe the services you require from the consultant in the "Consultant Quote" field.
4. Choose the type of consultant services you need from the "Consultant Services" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the client's address in the "Client Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name, as this will be used to identify them in our records.
* **Client Email**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached. This will help us contact you with any questions or to provide updates on your quote.
* **Consultant Quote**: Describe the services you require from the consultant, including any specific details about the work you need them to perform. This will help us create a tailored quote for you.
* **Consultant Services**: Choose the type of services you need from the dropdown menu. These options include:
	+ **Safety Audit and Compliance**: A comprehensive review of your safety procedures and regulatory compliance.
	+ **Risk Assessment**: A thorough evaluation of potential hazards and risks in your workplace.
	+ **Training and Development**: Customized training programs to develop your staff's safety knowledge and skills.
	+ **Other**: Specify any other services you require from the consultant.
* **Client Address**: Enter the client's physical address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure we can create a proper quote for you.
- Use the "Consultant Quote" field to provide detailed information about the services you need.
- If you select "Other" in the "Consultant Services" dropdown menu, please specify the type of services you require in the "Consultant Quote" field.
