## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the customer's information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it for accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter the customer's email.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Street** (5, text, required): Enter the customer's street address.
* **City** (6, text, required): Enter the customer's city.
* **State** (7, text, required): Enter the customer's state.
* **Zip** (8, number, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Address** (9, text, required): Enter the customer's full address.
* **Property Type** (10, select_one, required): Select the type of property, such as House, Apartment, etc.
* **Square Feet** (11, number, required): Enter the square feet of the property.
* **Window Count** (12, number, required): Enter the number of windows in the property.
* **Window Type** (13, select_one, required): Select the type of windows in the property, such as Single Pane, Double Pane, etc.
* **Window Size** (14, select_one, required): Select the size of the windows, such as 1/12, 1/16, etc.
* **Window Location** (15, select_multiple, required): Select the location of the windows, such as North, South, etc.
* **Estimate Date** (16, date, required): Enter the desired estimate date.
* **Estimate Time** (17, time, required): Enter the desired estimate time.
* **Service Provider** (18, text, required): Enter the service provider.
* **Service Type** (19, select_one, required): Select the type of service, such as One-Time Service, Ongoing Service, etc.
* **Additional Services** (20, select_multiple, required): Select any additional services needed, such as Glass Cleaning, Frame Cleaning, etc.
* **Price** (21, number, required): Enter the price of the service.
* **Notes** (22, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned User** (23, text, required): Enter the assigned user for the service.
* **Assigned Date** (24, date, required): Enter the assigned date for the service.
* **Submitted Date** (25, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
