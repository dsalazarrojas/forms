# Cold Storage Facility Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to be used by clients and customers to request a quote from our cold storage facility. It will help us provide an accurate estimate for their storage needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Company name, Contact name, and Contact email address. These are the primary identifying details we need to get in touch with you for quote discussions.
2. Enter the Storage capacity needed in cubic meters. This will help us determine the size of storage you require.
3. Specify the Temperature requirements for your storage needs. This can be a range or a setpoint.
4. Select the services you require from our storage options, which are:
	* Short term storage
	* Long term storage
	* Inventory management
	* Distribution
5. If you have a preferred start date for your storage, enter it in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. If you have a budget estimate for your storage, enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name (Company name, text, required)**: Enter your company's name to identify your organization.
* **Contact name (Contact name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the primary contact for your organization.
* **Contact email (Contact email, email, required)**: Enter the email address of your primary contact.
* **Storage capacity needed (Storage capacity needed in cubic meters, number, required)**: Enter a numeric estimate of the storage capacity you require in cubic meters.
* **Temperature requirements (Temperature requirements, text, required)**: Enter a range or setpoint for the temperature requirements of your storage needs.
* **Required services (Required services, select_multiple, required)**: Select the services you require from our storage options.
* **Preferred start date (Preferred start date, date, optional)**: Enter your preferred start date for your storage in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Budget estimate (Budget estimate, number, optional)**: Enter your estimated budget for your storage needs.
* **Additional information (Additional information, text, optional)**: Enter any other details about your project.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to get an accurate quote.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We will review and respond to your quote request as soon as possible after we receive it.
