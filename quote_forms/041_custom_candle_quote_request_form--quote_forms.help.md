## custom_candle_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom candle quote request form is a tool for customers to submit their quote preferences for a custom candle order. This form collects customer information, quote type, size, color, shape, note, email, phone, shipping method, and quote total.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer information in the "Customer Information" field.
2.  Select the type of quote you are requesting: "Candle", "Votive", "Tea Light", "Pillar", or "Other".
3.  Choose the size of your quote using the number field.
4.  Select the color(s) of your quote from the available options.
5.  Specify the shape of your quote using the text field.
6.  Add any additional notes about your quote in the "Quote Note" field.
7.  Enter your email address to receive the quote.
8.  Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions.
9.  Choose your preferred shipping method from the available options.
10.  Enter the total cost of your quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`,  text, **optional**): Enter your name and any relevant customer information.
*   **Quote Type** (`Quote Type`,  select_one, **optional**): Choose the type of quote you are requesting.
*   **Quote Size** (`Quote Size`,  number, **optional**): Enter the size of your quote.
*   **Quote Color** (`Quote Color`,  select_multiple, **optional**): Select the color(s) of your quote from the available options.
*   **Quote Shape** (`Quote Shape`,  text, **optional**): Specify the shape of your quote.
*   **Quote Note** (`Quote Note`,  note, **optional**): Add any additional notes about your quote.
*   **Quote Email** (`Quote Email`,  email, **optional**): Enter your email address to receive the quote.
*   **Quote Phone** (`Quote Phone`,  text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions.
*   **Quote Shipping Method** (`Quote Shipping Method`,  select_multiple, **optional**): Choose your preferred shipping method from the available options.
*   **Quote Total** (`Quote Total`,  number, **optional**): Enter the total cost of your quote.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you are not filling out this form more than once for the same customer.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our customer service team.
*   The form will be reviewed and processed within a timely manner.
