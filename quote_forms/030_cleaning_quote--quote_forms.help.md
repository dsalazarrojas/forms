# Cleaning Quote - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Cleaning Quote" form is designed to help cleaning service providers gather essential details from customers about their cleaning needs. This form helps ensure that the service provides a precise quote based on the customer's preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Contact Information:** Enter your first and last name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. **Property Information:** Select the type of property and its size to help the cleaning service understand your requirements.
3. **Cleaning Frequency and Preferences:** Choose your preferred frequency of cleaning and the day and time you'd like the service to be performed.
4. **Areas to Clean:** Select the areas of the property that need cleaning.
5. **Additional Services:** If you need any extra services such as carpet cleaning or window cleaning, mark the corresponding boxes.
6. **Special Requirements:** If you have any allergies, pets, or special requests, provide this information in the "Special Requirements or Requests" field.
7. **Current Cleaning Service:** If you are already using another cleaning service, let us know.
8. **How Did You Hear About Us:** Share how you found out about our services.
9. **Quote Preferences:** Choose how you'd like to receive your quote.
10. **Service Timeline:** Select when you need the cleaning service to be performed.
11. **Budget Range:** Indicate your expected budget range for the cleaning service.
12. **Additional Comments:** Provide any additional information or questions you have about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name. A required field for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Your last name. A required field for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Your email address. A required field for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number. A required field for communication.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you own, e.g., residential - house, apartment, or commercial - office.
* **Property Size**: Choose the size of your property, e.g., small, medium, large, or extra large.
* **Property Size (Commercial)**: Select the number of rooms in your commercial property.
* **Number of Bedrooms**: Select the number of bedrooms in your residential property.
* **Number of Bathrooms**: Select the number of bathrooms in your residential property.
* **Cleaning Type**: Choose the type of cleaning you need, e.g., standard, deep, or one-time cleaning.
* **Cleaning Frequency**: Select how often you'd like to schedule the cleaning service.
* **Preferred Day of Week**: Choose the day of the week you'd like the cleaning service to be performed.
* **Preferred Time of Day**: Select the time of day you'd like the cleaning service to be performed.
* **Select Areas to Clean**: Choose the areas of the property that need cleaning, e.g., living room, kitchen, or bedrooms.
* **Additional Services**: Select any extra services you need, e.g., carpet cleaning or window cleaning.
* **Do You Have Pets**: Indicate if you have pets in the property.
* **Special Requirements or Requests**: Provide any special instructions or requests you have for the cleaning service.
* **Allergies or Chemical Sensitivities**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have that the cleaning service should be aware of.
* **Do You Currently Have a Cleaning Service**: Indicate if you are already using another cleaning service.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Share how you found out about our services.
* **How Should We Send Your Quote**: Choose how you'd like to receive your quote.
* **When Do You Need Service**: Select when you need the cleaning service to be performed.
* **Expected Budget Range**: Indicate your expected budget range for the cleaning service.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Provide any additional information or questions you have about the service.
