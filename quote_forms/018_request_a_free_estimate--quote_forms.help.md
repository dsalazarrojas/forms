## Request A Free Estimate - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Request A Free Estimate" form is designed to allow users to submit a quote request for a service or product. This form is intended for customers and sales teams to gather information about the user's project or needs, and to provide a quick and easy way to request a quote. By filling out this form, users can provide necessary details about their project, and our team will be able to respond with a custom quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field. This is where you can enter your name or company name.
2. Enter your **Company** in the "Company" field. This field is intended for your company name or organization.
3. Type your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This is where you can enter your phone number so we can contact you.
4. Provide a brief description of your **Quote Request** in the "Quote Request" field. This is where you can enter a short message or details about your project.
5. Finally, enter any additional **Estimate Details** in the "Estimate Details" field. This is where you can provide further information about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a brief description of your project or services needed.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name or company name here.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name or organization here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Type your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Estimate Details** (`estimate_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your project that you'd like to share with our team.
