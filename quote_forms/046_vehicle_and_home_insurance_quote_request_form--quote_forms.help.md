# Vehicle And Home Insurance Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers or agents to request quotes for vehicle and home insurance. Please fill out the form with your details to get a quote for your desired coverage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the relevant contact method (phone or email) and providing your contact number or email address.
2. Enter your contact name, vehicle year, make, model, and plate number.
3. Provide information about your vehicle, including its year purchased and miles driven annually.
4. Select the type of coverage you need for your vehicle (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
5. Select the type of coverage you need for your home (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
6. Provide additional coverage selections for both vehicle and home (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
7. Enter your home address, city, and state.
8. Enter your home postal code.
9. Provide your date of birth.
10. Enter the cost for vehicle and home coverage, if applicable.
11. Enter the year you purchased your vehicle.
12. List any previous vehicle owners.
13. Set up your home damage control (time-based).
14. Review and submit the form to request a quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the method you would like to use to contact you (phone or email).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, optional): Enter your phone number to contact you.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, optional): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, optional): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Plate** (`vehicle_plate`, `text`, optional): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your home address.
* **Home City** (`home_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where your home is located.
* **Home State** (`home_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where your home is located.
* **Home Postal Code** (`home_postal_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the postal code of your home.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Vehicle Coverage** (`vehicle_coverage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of coverage you need for your vehicle (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
* **Home Coverage** (`home_coverage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of coverage you need for your home (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
* **Vehicle Coverage Additional** (`vehicle_coverage_additional`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional coverage types for your vehicle (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
* **Home Coverage Additional** (`home_coverage_additional`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional coverage types for your home (liability, collision, or comprehensive).
* **Vehicle Year Purchased** (`vehicle_year_purchased`, `number`, optional): Enter the year you purchased your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Previous Owners** (`vehicle_previous_vehicle_owners`, `note`, optional): List any previous owners of your vehicle.
* **Home Damage Control** (`home_damage_control`, `time`, optional): Set up your home damage control period.
* **Vehicle Annual Miles Driven** (`vehicle_annual_miles_driven`, `number`, optional): Enter the annual miles driven on your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Coverage Cost** (`vehicle_coverage_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of vehicle coverage.
* **Home Coverage Cost** (`home_coverage_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of home coverage.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure you receive the correct quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review and respond to your quote request as soon as possible.
