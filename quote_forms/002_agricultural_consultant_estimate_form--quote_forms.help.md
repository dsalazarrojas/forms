# Agricultural Consultant Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information for agricultural project estimates. It's crucial to provide accurate and clear responses to each field to ensure a thorough and accurate estimate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for each section, following the guidance provided for each field.
2. Provide detailed explanations for each field to give a clear understanding of your project requirements.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the farm or client requesting the estimate. This is the primary identifier for your project.
### 2. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Identify the primary contact person for the project, who will be reached for project updates and clarifications.
### 3. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide the email address of the primary contact person for the project. This will be used for sending the estimate and other project communications.
### 4. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person for scheduling and project discussions. If not applicable, skip this field.
### 5. **Project Scope Summary** (`project_scope`, text, required): Describe the services requested and goals of the project in this field. Provide a clear and concise summary of what needs to be done.
### 6. **Estimated Duration in Days** (`estimated_duration_days`, number, optional): If known, enter the estimated duration in days for the project. If not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
### 7. **Materials Cost Estimate** (`materials_cost_estimate`, number, optional): Enter a monetary estimate for materials related to the project. If not applicable, skip this field.
### 8. **Labor Cost Estimate** (`labor_cost_estimate`, number, optional): Enter a monetary estimate for labor costs related to the project. If not applicable, skip this field.
### 9. **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any special considerations or constraints that may affect the project's execution. This is an opportunity to provide extra context.
### 10. **Acceptance Acknowledgement** (`acceptance_signature`, note, required): Confirm your acceptance of the estimate terms by signing this field.
