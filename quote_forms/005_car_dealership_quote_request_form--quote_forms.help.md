# Car Dealership Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential customers who are interested in getting a quote for a vehicle purchase or trade-in from a car dealership. By filling out this form, you'll provide the dealership with the necessary details to prepare an accurate quote for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately with your information.
2. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, or text message) for the dealership to contact you.
3. Select the vehicle you are interested in, including make, model, and trim.
4. Specify any desired features for the vehicle.
5. Enter your estimated budget for the vehicle.
6. If you plan to trade in a vehicle, enter its estimated value.
7. Select a preferred contact date for the dealership to contact you.
8. Add any additional comments or details that you'd like the dealership to include in the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached for correspondence.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for the dealership to contact you.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, required): Choose the best way for the dealership to contact you:
	+ Email: Receive a quote via email.
	+ Phone: Receive a quote via phone call.
	+ Text message: Receive a quote via text message.
* **Vehicle of interest** (`vehicle_interest`, required): Enter the make, model, and trim of the vehicle you are interested in.
* **Desired features** (`desired_features`, optional): Select any features you'd like the vehicle to have:
	+ Leather seats.
	+ Sunroof.
	+ Navigation.
	+ Driver assist package.
	+ Tow package.
* **Budget range** (`budget`, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the vehicle in local currency.
* **Trade in estimated value** (`trade_in_value`, optional): If you plan to trade in a vehicle, enter its estimated value.
* **Preferred contact date** (`preferred_contact_date`, optional): Select the date when you'd like the dealership to contact you.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any extra details or comments you'd like the dealership to include in the quote.
