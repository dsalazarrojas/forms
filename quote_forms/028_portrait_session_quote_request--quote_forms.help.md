# Portrait Session Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Portrait Session Quote Request" form is a tool for clients to submit their quote requests for a portrait session. This form helps us to gather essential information about their session needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "Client Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Describe the details of your session, including location and date, in the "Session Details" field.
4. Select the type of session you are interested in by choosing from the "Package Type" options.
5. Choose the locations where you'd like to take photos by selecting from the "Photo Locations" options.
6. Enter the date and time of your preferred session in the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields.
7. Enter your preferred quote budget in the "Quote Budget" field.
8. Select your preferred payment option from the "Payment Option" options.
9. If you have any additional information to share about your session, enter it in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (`name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name as the client who is requesting the quote.

### Email (`name`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.

### Phone (`name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### Session Details (`name`, `text`, required: false)
Provide details about the session such as location and date.

### Package Type (`name`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the type of session you are interested in:
	* Portrait Session
	* Newborn Session
	* Family Session

### Photo Locations (`name`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select the locations where you'd like to take photos:
	* Studio
	* Location (Client's home)
	* Location (Unknown)
	* Location (Studio/Outdoor)

### Session Date (`name`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of your preferred session.

### Session Time (`name`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the time of your preferred session.

### Quote Budget (`name`, `number`, required: false)
Enter your preferred quote budget.

### Payment Option (`name`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose your preferred payment option:
	* Bank Transfer
	* Cash on Delivery
	* Credit Card
	* Other

### Additional Info (`name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter any additional information about your session.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will get back to you with a quote and session details as soon as possible.
* We appreciate your interest in our portrait sessions!
