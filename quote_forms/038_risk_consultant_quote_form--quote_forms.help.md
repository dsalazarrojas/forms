# Risk Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients seeking risk consultant services. It helps in generating a quote for the risk consultant services by gathering relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing your risk consultant information, such as your name, specialization, and experience.
2. Select your client type (individual or corporate) and years of experience.
3. Provide any additional information about the client's requirements.
4. Write down any additional notes you'd like to mention.
5. Enter your risk consultant quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Consultant Name** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_risk_consultant`, text, required): Please provide your name as a risk consultant.
* **Risk Consultant** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_risk_consultant`, text, required): This is your title or name as a risk consultant.
* **Client Type** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_client_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your client type, either "individual" or "corporate".
* **Years of Experience** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_risk_consultant_experience`, number, required): Provide the number of years you have experience as a risk consultant.
* **Specialization** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_risk_consultant_specialization`, text, required): Enter your area of specialization as a risk consultant.
* **Client Requirements** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_client_requirements`, text, required): Describe the client's requirements in detail.
* **Note** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_risk_consultant_note`, note, required): Add any additional information or notes about the client.
* **Quote** (`risk_consultant_quote_form_risk_consultant_quote`, text, required): Enter your quote for the risk consultant services.

## Tips

- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
- Double-check your quote before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
