# Franchise Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Consulting Quotation Form is a tool for collecting information from potential clients seeking consulting services for their franchise. This form is used to gather relevant details that can help in quoting or estimating the costs associated with the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Specify the date and time you are available to discuss your franchise consulting needs.
5. Enter the location where you would like to meet or discuss your needs.
6. Select the type of service you require from the "Service" dropdown menu.
7. Provide a brief description of your needs or requirements.
8. Enter the name of the consultant you would like to work with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (form_data): This field is for you to enter your name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date** (date): Select the date you are available to discuss your consulting needs.
* **Time** (time): Choose the time you are available to meet or discuss your needs.
* **Location** (location): Enter the location where you would like to meet or discuss your needs.
* **Service** (service): Select the type of service you require from the list provided.
* **Description** (description): Provide a brief description of your needs or requirements.
* **Consultant Name** (consultant_name): Enter the name of the consultant you would like to work with.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately, as this information will be used to quote or estimate the costs associated with the services provided.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your point of contact for clarification.
- Please note that this form is for your convenience, and your data will be used solely for the purpose of providing consulting services.
