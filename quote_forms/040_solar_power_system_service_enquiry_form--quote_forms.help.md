# Solar Power System Service Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to inquire about our solar power system services. They can choose to enquire about installation, maintenance, or other related services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer name.
2. Enter your company name if applicable.
3. Provide your email address for any follow-up communication.
4. Enter your phone number for our team to contact you.
5. Select one or more of the enquiry types that match your interest:
	* Installation
	* Maintenance
	* Other (please specify)
6. Write a brief description of your enquiry to help us understand your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name**: <customer_name> (text, optional): Please fill in your name as it should appear in our records.
- **Company Name (if applicable)**: <company_name> (text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter the company name here.
- **Email**: <email> (email, optional): Enter your email address so our team can contact you for further information.
- **Phone Number**: <phone_number> (text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Enquiry Type**: <enquiry_type> (select multiple, required): Please choose one or more of the enquiry types that match your interest.
	+ Installation
	+ Maintenance
	+ Other
- **Description**: <description> (text, optional): Write a brief description of your enquiry to help us understand your needs.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we have enough information to process your enquiry.
- Our team will review your form submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
