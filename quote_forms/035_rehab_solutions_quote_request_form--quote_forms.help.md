# Rehab Solutions Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehab Solutions Quote Request Form is designed for individuals to request quotes from our team for various therapy services. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences so we can provide the best possible services for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of therapy you are interested in from the "Type of Therapy" dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, select any additional therapies you are interested in from the "Additional Therapies" dropdown menu. You can choose more than one option.
7. Add any relevant notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your quote request to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field. We will use this to contact you regarding your quote and any follow-up questions.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field. We will use this to contact you regarding your quote and any follow-up questions.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your therapy sessions.
* **Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule your therapy sessions.
* **Type of Therapy**: Select the type of therapy you are interested in, such as Rehabilitation, Physical Therapy, or Occupational Therapy.
* **Additional Therapies**: If you are interested in additional therapies, select them from this dropdown menu. You can choose more than one option.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your therapy needs or preferences.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your quote request to our team.
