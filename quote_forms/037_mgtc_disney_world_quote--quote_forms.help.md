# MGTC Disney World Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture visitor information for planning purposes, allowing staff to prepare for the number of guests and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with your first name.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with your last name.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Provide your Arrival Date.
5. Provide your Departure Date.
6. Enter any information about your Travel Companion (if applicable).
7. Select the type of travel you are undertaking in the Travel Type field.
8. Select the number of guests you are traveling with in the Number of Guests field.
