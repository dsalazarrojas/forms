# Wire Rods Price Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wire Rods Price Quote Request Form is a tool for customers to request a price quote for their specific product requirements. It gathers essential information to provide an accurate quote and facilitate the quote process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name". Please note that this is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name". This is also a required field.
3. Enter your **Company** in the field labeled "Company". This field is required and should be the name of your organization.
4. Enter the name of the **Product Name** you are requesting a quote for in the field labeled "Product Name". This is a required field.
5. Describe the **Product Specs** you require in the field labeled "Product Specs". This field is required and should be a detailed description of your product needs.
6. Select your desired **Delivery Date** in the date field labeled "Delivery Date". Please note that this field should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. Enter your **Delivery Address** in the field labeled "Delivery Address". This is a required field.
8. Enter your **Contact Number** in the field labeled "Contact Number". This field is required and should be a valid phone number.
9. Enter a valid **Email** in the field labeled "Email". This field is required and should be a valid email address.
10. Enter the **Quantity** of the product you require in the field labeled "Quantity". This is a required field.
11. Enter the **Unit Price** of the product you require in the field labeled "Unit Price". This is a required field.
12. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Delivery Terms**. Please note that this field is required.
13. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Payment Terms**. This field is also required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are requesting a quote for.
* **Product Specs**: Describe your product requirements in detail.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you desire your product to be delivered. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where your product should be delivered.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of product you require.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of the product.
* **Delivery Terms**: Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate your delivery requirements.
* **Payment Terms**: Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate your payment requirements.
