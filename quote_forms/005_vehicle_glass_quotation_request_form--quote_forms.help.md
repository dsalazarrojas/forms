# Vehicle Glass Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Glass Quotation Request Form is designed to collect information from vehicle owners to create a quotation for vehicle glass repairs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner Information**: Please enter the vehicle owner's name, address, and contact details.
* **Vehicle Details**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and color.
* **Vehicle Damage Description**: Describe the damage on the vehicle's glass. If you have photos, please upload them.
* **Vehicle Photos**: Upload photos of the vehicle's damage.
* **Repair Quote**: Enter a brief description of the repair quote.
* **Quotation Price**: Enter the quotation price.
* **Vehicle Insurance Information**: Enter the vehicle's insurance provider and claim number.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Make**: Select the vehicle's make from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Model**: Select the vehicle's model from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Year**: Select the vehicle's year from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Color**: Select the vehicle's color from the options provided.
* **Body Shop Address**: Enter the body shop's address.
* **Body Shop Phone Number**: Enter the body shop's phone number.
* **Body Shop Name**: Enter the body shop's name.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the vehicle type from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Location**: Enter the vehicle's current location.
* **Vehicle Damage Level**: Select the vehicle's damage level from the options provided.
* **Body Shop Email**: Enter the body shop's email.
* **Vehicle Damage Description**: Enter a detailed description of the vehicle's damage.
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider from the options provided.
* **Insurance Claim Number**: Select the insurance claim number from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Insurance Status**: Select the vehicle's insurance status from the options provided.
* **Quotation Status**: Select the quotation status from the options provided.
* **Quotation Date**: Select the quotation date from the options provided.
