# Commercial Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential business information for commercial insurance coverage purposes. It helps us understand your business operations, risks, and coverage needs to provide you with the best possible insurance solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
2. Use the options provided for select fields.
3. If you have any additional information, please provide it in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Review all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business's legal name as it appears on your business license or registration.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business's complete address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your business's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, select_one, required): Choose the legal structure of your business (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit).
* **Industry Classification** (`industry_classification`, select_one, required): Choose your primary industry (e.g., Retail, Office/Professional, Manufacturing, Warehouse, Restaurant, Hotel, Healthcare, Auto Service, Other).
* **Years Operating** (`years_operating`, select_one, required): Choose how long your business has been operating (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years).
* **Number of Locations** (`number_of_locations`, number, required): Enter the total number of business locations.
* **Building Ownership** (`building_ownership`, select_one, required): Choose how you own or lease the building (e.g., Own Building and Land, Own Building Lease Land, Lease or Rent, Condominium).
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, required): Enter the construction year of the building.
* **Construction Type** (`construction_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of building materials used (e.g., Wood Frame, Masonry, Steel Frame, Concrete, Mixed).
* **Total Square Footage** (`total_square_footage`, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the building.
* **Building Replacement Cost** (`building_replacement_cost`, number, required): Enter the estimated cost to rebuild the building.
* **Business Personal Property Value** (`business_personal_property`, number, required): Enter the value of business personal property (e.g., equipment, inventory).
* **Customer Visits** (`customer_visits`, select_one, required): Choose how frequently customers visit your location (e.g., Yes Frequently, Yes Occasionally, Rarely, False).
* **Products Sold** (`products_sold`, select_one, required): Choose if you sell products (e.g., True, False).
* **Services Provided** (`services_provided`, select_one, required): Choose if you provide services (e.g., True, False).
* **Do You Provide Professional Advice** (`professional_advice`, select_one, required): Choose if you provide consulting or advisory services (e.g., True, False).
* **Number of Business Vehicles** (`business_vehicles`, number, required): Enter the total number of owned or leased business vehicles.
* **Vehicle Types** (`vehicle_types`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of vehicles owned or leased (e.g., Cars, Trucks, Vans, Trailers, Heavy Equipment, None).
* **Total Number of Employees** (`total_employees`, number, required): Enter the total number of full and part-time employees.
* **Annual Payroll Amount** (`payroll_amount`, number, required): Enter the total annual payroll amount.
* **Prior Claims** (`prior_claims`, select_one, required): Choose if you have had prior insurance claims in the past 5 years (e.g., None, 1 claim, 2-3 claims, 4 or more).
* **Claims Description** (`claims_description`, text, required): Enter a description of any prior claims.
* **Desired Coverages** (`desired_coverages`, select_multiple, required): Choose the coverages you need (e.g., Building Coverage, Business Personal Property, Business Income, General Liability, Professional Liability, Workers Compensation, Commercial Auto, Cyber Liability, Equipment Breakdown, Flood, Earthquake).
* **Deductible Preference** (`deductible_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred deductible amount (e.g., $500, $1,000, $2,500, $5,000, $10,000).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your commercial insurance coverage needs.
