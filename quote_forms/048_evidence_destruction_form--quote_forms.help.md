# Evidence Destruction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Evidence Destruction Form is used to document the destruction of evidence, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded accurately and consistently. This form is used by authorized personnel to confirm that evidence has been destroyed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form purpose and ensure you are authorized to complete the form.
2. Select all relevant options for the "Destruction" field, indicating whether the evidence was destroyed or not.
3. Enter relevant information in the "Inventory" field, as applicable.
4. Enter the date and time of destruction in the "Destruction Date" and "Destruction Time" fields, respectively.
5. Provide any additional notes regarding the destruction process in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter the email and phone contact information for the individual responsible for the evidence destruction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Destruction Form** (`destruction_form`, select_multiple, optional)
  This field indicates whether the evidence was destroyed, and you can select all applicable options.
- **Inventory Form** (`inventory_form`, text, optional)
  This field is used to record any relevant information about the items being destroyed, such as a list of items or a description.
- **Destruction Date** (`destruction_date`, date, optional)
  Enter the date on which the evidence was destroyed.
- **Destruction Time** (`destruction_time`, time, optional)
  Enter the time of day when the evidence was destroyed.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional)
  Provide any additional notes about the destruction process, such as the method used or any relevant details.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
  Enter the email contact information for the individual responsible for the evidence destruction.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
  Enter the phone contact information for the individual responsible for the evidence destruction.
