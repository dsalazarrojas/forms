# Contact Form Black - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Form Black is designed to collect information from clients or customers who are requesting a quote for a project. The form is meant to gather essential details about the project, including the contact person's name, contact information, project description, and estimated budget. By providing this information, the quote team can better understand the project requirements and create an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Contact Form Black, follow these steps:

1. Read the introductory note at the top of the form, which explains the purpose of the form and what information is being requested.
2. Fill in your full name, which is a required field.
3. Provide your company name (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address, which will be used to deliver the quote.
5. Enter your phone number, which will be used as a contact number.
6. Choose your preferred time window for contact.
7. Briefly describe the project you are requesting a quote for in the project description field.
8. Provide an estimated budget for the project (if available).
9. Enter the target date for project completion.
10. If necessary, attach supporting documents via email, or use the provided note field to indicate that you will send them separately.
11. In the additional requests or comments field, provide any further details or requests you may have for the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card. This is a required field and will help the quote team address you correctly.
- **Company name** (name, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name here. If not, leave this field blank.
- **Email address** (email, email, required): Provide a valid email address to receive the quote and any follow-up communications.
- **Phone number** (name, text, optional): Enter your best phone number to reach you, in case we need to contact you directly.
- **Preferred contact time** (name, time, optional): Select your preferred time window for the quote team to contact you, taking into account your time zone.
- **Project description** (name, text, required): Briefly describe the project you are requesting a quote for, including any relevant details or requirements.
- **Estimated budget** (name, number, optional): If available, provide an estimated budget for the project. This will help the quote team create a more accurate quote.
- **Required deadline** (name, date, optional): Enter the target date for project completion, if applicable.
- **Attach supporting documents via email** (name, note, optional): If you need to attach supporting documents, such as contracts or specifications, you can do so via email, as indicated in the note field.
- **Additional requests or comments** (name, text, optional): In this field, you can provide any additional details or requests you may have for the quote, such as specific requirements or deadlines.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Full name, Email address, Project description) for the quote team to create an accurate quote.
- If you have any supporting documents, be sure to attach them to the email as indicated in the form.
- Use your preferred contact time window to ensure that the quote team can contact you at a time that suits you best.
