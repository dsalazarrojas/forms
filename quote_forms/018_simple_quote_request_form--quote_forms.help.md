# Simple Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to submit a quote request to our organization. Please fill out this form with your details, and we'll get back to you with a quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field. If you need any assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your quote request. This could be a specific service or product you're looking for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your name. This will help us get in touch with you and address your quote request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, please provide the name of the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number, so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for correct formatting.
* If you have any attachments or files to include with your quote request, please contact us separately.
