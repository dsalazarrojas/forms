# Animator Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your project requirements and preferences for an animation quote. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information to help us create the best possible animation for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Client Information" section to provide us with your contact and company information.
2. In the "Project Details" section, describe your project and its objectives. This is a crucial part of the quote process.
3. Choose your preferred animation style in the "Animation Style" section. Our team can work in various styles, including 2D, 3D, Stop-Motion, and more.
4. Select the video duration and resolution required for your project in the "Technical Specifications" section.
5. Choose the deliverables you need from the "Deliverables" section. This will help us understand what you expect from us.
6. Finally, provide us with a deadline and timeline flexibility in the "Timeline and Deadline" section. This will help us plan and deliver your project on time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a client or representative.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your business name or organization name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Use this to communicate with us about your project.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number for any quick questions or clarifications.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter your project title and description.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Describe your project in detail, including its objectives and scope.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`, text, optional): Describe your overall project vision and scope.
* **Preferred Animation Style** (`animation_style`, select_one, required): Choose from 2D Traditional Animation, 3D CGI Animation, Stop-Motion, Motion Graphics, Whiteboard Animation, Cel Animation, Explainer Video Style, or Hybrid Approach.
* **Video Duration** (`video_length`, select_one, required): Select from 15-30 seconds, 30 seconds - 1 minute, 1-2 minutes, 2-5 minutes, 5-10 minutes, 10-15 minutes, 15-20 minutes, or 20+ minutes.
* **Required Video Resolution** (`video_resolution`, select_one, required): Choose from HD 1080p (1920x1080), 4K (3840x2160), Mobile Optimized, Social Media Format, Broadcast Quality, or Multiple Formats.
* **Frame Rate Required** (`frame_rate`, select_one, optional): Select from 24 FPS, 30 FPS, 60 FPS, Multiple Frame Rates, or No Preference.
* **Deliverables** (`deliverable_items`, select_multiple, required): Select what you need from Final Animation File, Editing Project Files, Storyboards, Animatics, Rough Animations, Source Files, Multiple Revisions, and Music and Sound Design.
* **Number of Revisions Included** (`revisions_included`, select_one, optional): Choose from Unlimited Revisions, 5 Revision Rounds, 3 Revision Rounds, 1 Revision Round, or No Revisions.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, required): Enter the deadline you need for your project.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, select_one, optional): Select from Flexible - can adjust, Some flexibility, Fixed deadline - no flexibility, or Tight timeline - rush preferred.
* **Reference Materials or Inspiration** (`reference_materials`, text, optional): Provide links to similar work or inspiration for your project.
* **Video Resolution** (`video_resolution`, select_one, required): This field is required for correct video resolution selection.
* **Frame Rate Required** (`frame_rate`, select_one, optional): This field is optional but helps us understand your frame rate requirements.
* **Deliverables** (`deliverable_items`, select_multiple, required): This field is required for correct deliverable selection.
* **Number of Revisions Included** (`revisions_included`, select_one, optional): This field is optional but helps us understand your revision requirements.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, required): This field is required for correct deadline selection.
* **Timeline Flexibility** (`timeline_flexibility`, select_one, optional): This field is optional but helps us understand your timeline flexibility.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your project and requirements.
* Choose your preferred animation style, and we will do our best to match it.
* Make sure to select the correct video duration and resolution for your project.
* Choose your required deliverables carefully, as this will affect the final product you receive.
* Provide a realistic deadline and timeline flexibility for us to plan and deliver your project on time.
* If you have any reference materials or inspiration, please share them with us to help us understand your vision.
