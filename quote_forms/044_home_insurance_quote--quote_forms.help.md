# Home Insurance Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information for a home insurance quote. Please provide the necessary details to receive an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the type of home you own (House, Condo, or Townhouse).
2.  Enter your main address in the space provided.
3.  If you have an email address and/or a phone number, please provide them for communication purposes.
4.  Enter the value of your home.
5.  Select the type of coverage you need (Liability, Property, or Other).
6.  Choose the policy period (1-year or 5-year).
7.  Select the type of policy you need (Home or Auto).
8.  If applicable, please enter the date and time of birth of the policy holder.
9.  Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to add.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Main Address**: Enter your primary address where you live.
*   **type**: Choose the type of home you own (House, Condo, or Townhouse).
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
*   **Home Value**: Enter the value of your home.
*   **Coverage Type**: Select the type of coverage you need (Liability, Property, or Other).
*   **Policy Period**: Choose the policy period (1-year or 5-year).
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person (if applicable).
*   **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number (if applicable).
*   **Policy Type**: Select the type of policy you need (Home or Auto).
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the policy holder (if applicable).
*   **Time of Birth**: Enter the time of birth of the policy holder (if applicable).
*   **Note**: Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to add.
