# Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the effectiveness and outcomes of a mentorship program. It gathers feedback from participants, mentors, and supervisors to understand the program's strengths and weaknesses. This form is essential for program improvement, development, and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question and section.
2. Select the relevant answers to each question based on your experience and perceptions.
3. Provide honest and constructive feedback to help improve the program.
4. Ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Impact** (id: 1, number): This field asks you to rate the overall impact of the mentorship program. Please rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Participants** (id: 2, select_multiple): Please select all participants who have been involved in the mentorship program.
* **Outcomes** (id: 3, text): Please list the outcomes of the mentorship program, such as skills gained, knowledge acquired, or personal growth.
* **Evaluation Method** (id: 4, select_one): Please select how you evaluated the program's impact, such as self-assessment, peer evaluation, or supervisor evaluation.
* **Program Support** (id: 5, select_multiple): Please select the types of support provided by the program, such as financial, logistical, or technical.
* **Program Satisfaction** (id: 6, number): This field is not directly relevant to the evaluation form. Please consider it removed or rephrased for better clarity.
* **Program Improvements** (id: 7, text): Please provide suggestions for improvements to the mentorship program.
