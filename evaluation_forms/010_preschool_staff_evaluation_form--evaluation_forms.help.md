# Preschool Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Preschool Staff Evaluation Form is used to gather information about the performance, skills, and characteristics of preschool staff members. This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel to help identify areas of improvement and strengths for professional development and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to complete it.
2. Answer all fields that are applicable to the staff member being evaluated.
3. Use the options provided for each field to select the most accurate answer.
4. Complete the form in a clear and concise manner.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Performance (ID 1)**: Please describe the staff member's performance over the evaluation period. Provide specific examples and details about their strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Staff Skills (ID 2)**: Indicate whether the staff member possesses the necessary skills and qualifications for their role. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the options provided.
* **Staff Attendance (ID 3)**: Record the staff member's attendance record over the evaluation period. Enter the number of days they were present.
* **Staff Comments (ID 4)**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the staff member's performance, skills, or attendance.
* **Assigned Teacher (ID 5)**: Select the teacher who was primarily responsible for evaluating the staff member.
* **Staff Abilities (ID 6)**: Describe the staff member's strengths and areas for development in terms of their abilities and skills.
* **Staff Duties (ID 7)**: List the primary duties and responsibilities assigned to the staff member.
* **Evaluation Date (ID 8)**: Enter the date when this form was completed.
* **Form Completion Date (ID 9)**: Record the date when the form was completed by the evaluator.
* **Submitted By (ID 10)**: Select the evaluator who completed this form.
