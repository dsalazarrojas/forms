# Open Position Candidate Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the candidate's performance and experience in the interview process. It is intended for the hiring team to understand the candidate's strengths and areas of improvement. Please complete this form to provide detailed and constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Excellent", "Average" or "Poor" to indicate the overall feedback type of the candidate's performance.
2. Enter any additional feedback you have for the candidate.
3. (Optional) Provide your rating of the candidate's performance by entering a number between 0 and 5.
4. If you have any suggestions for the candidate, enter them here.
5. Select the interview process type (On-site, Online, or On-time) that the candidate went through.
6. Select how satisfied you were with the candidate's experience (Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, or Somewhat Satisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details**: Please provide a brief description of the candidate, including their name and any relevant background information.
* **Feedback Type**: Choose a brief description of the candidate's performance, whether it was Excellent, Average, or Poor.
* **Rating**: Optional, but if you're providing a rating, enter a number between 0 and 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the candidate's performance.
* **Feedback**: Enter a detailed description of your experience with the candidate, including strengths and areas of improvement.
* **Suggestion**: If you have any suggestions or recommendations for the candidate, enter them here.
* **Interview Process**: If the candidate went through a face-to-face interview, select "On-site". If the interview was conducted online, select "Online". If the interview was conducted on a different time zone, select "On-time".
* **Candidate Experience**: Choose how satisfied you were with the candidate's experience, whether it was Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, or Somewhat Satisfied.
