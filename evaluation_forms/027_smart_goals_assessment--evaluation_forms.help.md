# SMART Goals Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The SMART Goals Assessment form is designed to evaluate the clarity and effectiveness of individual or team goals. It helps in identifying goals that adhere to the SMART criteria: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a different aspect of the SMART criteria. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2.  For each question, select the answer that best describes your goal or objective.
3.  Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
4.  You can skip any optional fields if not applicable to your goals.
5.  Review your responses carefully and ensure they accurately reflect your goals and objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This section is a brief overview of your goal or objective. It's not necessary to fill it out, but it's a good place to start by giving a brief context to your goal.
* **Goal Definition** (2): Here, provide a detailed description of your goal. Be specific and clear about what you aim to achieve.
* **Specific** (3): Answer "True" if your goal is well-defined and easy to understand.
* **Measurable** (4): If you can quantify your progress towards your goal, select "True". Otherwise, select "False" or "Not applicable".
* **Achievable** (5): If you believe your goal is realistic and attainable, select "True". Otherwise, select "False" or "Not applicable".
* **Relevant** (6): If your goal aligns with your overall objectives and priorities, select "True". Otherwise, select "False" or "Not applicable".
* **Time-bound** (7): If you have a specific deadline or timeframe for completing your goal, select "True". Otherwise, select "False" or "Not applicable".
* **Goal Ownership** (8): If you have ownership or responsibility for this goal, enter the number of people or teams involved.
* **Goal Priority** (9): If you have multiple goals, select the priority level of this goal on a scale of 1-5.
* **Target Completion Time** (10): Enter the expected time by which you intend to complete your goal.
* **Review Frequency** (11): Describe the frequency of review or progress tracking for your goal.
* **Review Comments** (12): Add any additional comments or feedback on your goal's progress.
* **Goal Completion Status** (13): If your goal is completed, select "Completed". If it's in progress, select "In Progress". Otherwise, select "Not Started".
* **Revision Needed** (14): If your goal requires review or revision, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
* **Comments** (15): Add any additional comments or explanations about your goal.

## Tips

* Ensure your goals are clear, concise, and specific to achieve better results.
* Review and update your goals regularly to reflect progress and changes.
* Use the SMART criteria as a guide to evaluate and improve your goals.
* This form is a self-assessment tool; be honest and thorough in your responses.
