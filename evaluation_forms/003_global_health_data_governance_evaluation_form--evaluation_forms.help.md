# global_health_data_governance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global health data governance evaluation form is designed to assess and improve the practices of health sector stakeholders in managing their data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and respond to every question.
2. Answer each question according to your current practices and experiences.
3. Provide detailed explanations for your responses to the challenges section.
4. Summarize your thoughts and recommendations in the "Conclusion" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction (Page 1)
*   **Introduction** (text): This is a brief overview of the form's purpose and scope. Please provide a short introduction that explains your understanding of the form's objectives.
*   **Hint**: This section is not required, but it may help provide context for your introduction.

### 2. Data Quality (Page 2)
*   **Data Quality** (select multiple): Indicate whether your data management practices prioritize accuracy, completeness, and consistency. Choose all that apply.
*   **Yes**: Your data management practices prioritize accuracy, completeness, and consistency.
*   **No**: Your data management practices do not prioritize accuracy, completeness, and consistency.
*   **Not Applicable**: Not applicable to your context.

### 3. Stakeholder Involvement (Page 3)
*   **Stakeholder Involvement** (select one): Select the types of stakeholders involved in your data governance process.
*   **Government**: Your data governance practices involve government stakeholders.
*   **NGO**: Your data governance practices involve non-governmental organizations.
*   **Research Organization**: Your data governance practices involve research organizations.
*   **Other**: Your data governance practices involve other types of stakeholders.

### 4. Data Sharing (Page 4)
*   **Data Sharing** (select multiple): Indicate whether your data is shared internally or externally with others.
*   **Yes**: Your data is shared internally or externally with others.
*   **No**: Your data is not shared internally or externally with others.
*   **Not Applicable**: Not applicable to your context.

### 5. Challenges (Pages 5-9)

*   **Challenge 2** (text): Describe another potential challenge you face with your current data management practices.
*   **Challenge 3** (text): Describe another potential challenge you face with your current data management practices.
*   **Challenge 4** (text): Describe another potential challenge you face with your current data management practices.
*   **Challenge 5** (text): Describe another potential challenge you face with your current data management practices.

### 6. Conclusion (Page 10)
*   **Conclusion** (text): Summarize your thoughts and provide recommendations for future improvements to your data governance practices.
*   **Hint**: This section is meant for users to provide a final summary and recommendations for future improvements.
