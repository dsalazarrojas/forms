# global_health_data_governance_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global health data governance evaluation form is designed to assess and improve the practices of health sector stakeholders in managing their data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and respond to every question.
2. Answer each question according to your current practices and experiences.
3. Provide detailed explanations for your responses to the challenges section.
4. Summarize your thoughts and recommendations in the "Conclusion" section.
