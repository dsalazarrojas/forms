# Camp Volunteer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from volunteers who have participated in a camp or similar program. It is a tool for the program organizers to understand the experiences and opinions of volunteers, making necessary improvements and adjustments for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the "Volunteer Experience Feedback" section, which provides context and background information about the program.
2. Answer the questions in the "Detailed Program Ratings" section, rating the aspects of your experience as instructed.
3. In the "Comments and Suggestions" section, provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.
4. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Volunteer Name (Optional)** (`feedback_volunteer_name`, `text`, Optional)
This field is an optional text input where you can provide your name if you choose to do so. If you prefer not to, you can leave this field blank.

### 2. **Volunteer Role - Position** (`feedback_role_position`, `text`, Required)
Please enter your role or position within the program.

### 3. **Session(s) Worked** (`feedback_session_worked`, `select_multiple`, Required)
Select all the sessions you participated in during your volunteer work.

### 4. **Rate your overall volunteer experience** (`feedback_experience_rating`, `number`, Required)
Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).

### 5. **Detailed Program Ratings** (`ratings_section_volunteer`, `note`, Optional)
This section is a note field where you can provide any additional comments about your experience.

### 6. **Support and Guidance from Staff** (`rating_staff_support_vol`, `select_one`, Required)
Rate the support and guidance you received from staff during your experience.

### 7. **Effectiveness of Orientation - Training** (`rating_training_effectiveness_vol`, `select_one`, Required)
Rate the effectiveness of the orientation and training provided.

### 8. **Availability of Necessary Supplies - Resources** (`rating_resource_availability_vol`, `select_one`, Required)
Rate the availability of necessary supplies and resources during your experience.

### 9. **What was the most meaningful part of your experience?** (`most_meaningful_moment_vol`, `text`, Required)
Describe the most meaningful part of your experience.

### 10. **What was the most challenging part of your role?** (`most_challenging_part_vol`, `text`, Optional)
Describe the most challenging part of your experience, if any.

### 11. **Suggestions for Improving the Volunteer Program** (`suggestion_improvement_vol`, `text`, Optional)
Provide any suggestions or comments you have for improving the program.

### 12. **Would you like to volunteer with us again?** (`return_next_year_vol`, `select_one`, Required)
Indicate your willingness to volunteer with us again.

### 13. **Estimated Total Volunteer Hours Served** (`volunteer_hours_verification`, `number`, Optional)
Estimate the total hours you served as a volunteer.

### 14. **Date Submitted** (`feedback_submission_date_vol`, `date`, Required)
Enter the date you are submitting this feedback form.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Please provide any feedback or comments you think will help improve the program.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas, please do not hesitate to share them.
