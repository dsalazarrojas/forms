# Work Order Close Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Work Order Close Out Form is used for closing out a work order by gathering necessary information and approvals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields to understand what each one represents.
2.  Enter the correct values for each field as required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Work Order Close Out**: This field is not a field, but rather the form title.
*   **Hours Worked**: Enter the total number of hours worked on the project.
*   **Materials Used**: Select whether materials were used during the project. Options are "Yes" or "No".
*   **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the project. 
*   **Job Status**: Select the status of the job. Options are "Active" or "Inactive".
*   **Job Number**: Enter the project's job number.
*   **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
*   **Job Comments**: Enter any relevant comments about the project.
*   **Hours Approved**: Enter the approved hours worked on the project.
*   **Labor Cost**: Enter the labor cost of the project.
*   **Material Cost**: Enter the material cost of the project.
*   **Total Cost Approve**: Enter the total approved cost of the project.
*   **Labor Cost Approve**: Enter the approved labor cost of the project.
*   **Material Cost Approve**: Enter the approved material cost of the project.
*   **Close Out Status**: Select the close out status. Options are "Active" or "Inactive".

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Review all fields before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
*   If you're unsure about any field, consult with the project manager or relevant personnel.
