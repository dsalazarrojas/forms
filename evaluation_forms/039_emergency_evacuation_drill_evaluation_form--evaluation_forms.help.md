# emergency_evacuation_drill_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of emergency evacuation drills. It asks participants and drill leaders to provide feedback on various aspects of the drill, including participants' safety, equipment, and overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each question.
3. Provide detailed comments in the text fields where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This is a brief introduction to the form. Read it carefully and proceed to the next section.
* **Drill Participants** (Select Multiple): Check all the participants who attended the drill. (Yes/No)
* **Drill Leaders** (Select Multiple): Check all the drill leaders who conducted the drill. (Yes/No)
* **Emergency Evacuation Procedure** (Text): Describe the emergency evacuation procedure used during the drill.
* **Drill Effectiveness** (Select One): Rate the effectiveness of the drill. (Highly Effective/Somewhat Effective/Not Effective)
* **Participant Safety** (Select One): Rate the safety of participants during the drill. (Highly Safe/Somewhat Safe/Not Safe)
* **Drill Participant Comments** (Text): Provide any comments you have about your experience during the drill.
* **Drill Effectiveness Comments** (Text): Provide any comments you have about the effectiveness of the drill.
* **Emergency Evacuation Equipment** (Select Multiple): Check all the equipment used during the drill. (Yes/No)
* **Equipment Maintenance** (Select Multiple): Check if the equipment used was well-maintained. (Yes/No)
* **Drill Facilities** (Select Multiple): Check all the facilities used during the drill. (Yes/No)
* **Drill Facilities Equipment** (Select Multiple): Check all the equipment used in the facilities. (Yes/No)
* **Drill Participant Emotions** (Select One): Rate your emotions during the drill. (Positive/Negative/Neutral)
* **Drill Leaders Emotions** (Select One): Rate the emotions of the drill leaders during the drill. (Positive/Negative/Neutral)
* **Drill Safety Benefits** (Select One): Rate the safety benefits of the drill. (Highly Valuable/Somewhat Valuable/Not Valuable)
* **Drill Emergency Procedures** (Select Multiple): Check all the emergency procedures used during the drill. (Yes/No)
* **Drill Procedure Changes** (Select One): Check if the drill procedure was changed during the drill. (True/False)
* **Drill Participant Suggestion** (Text): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the drill.
* **Drill Leaders Suggestion** (Text): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the drill.
* **Overall Drill Effectiveness** (Select One): Rate the overall effectiveness of the drill. (Highly Effective/Somewhat Effective/Not Effective)
* **Overall Emergency Response** (Select One): Rate the overall emergency response of the drill. (Highly Effective/Somewhat Effective/Not Effective)
* **Participants Emotions** (Select Multiple): Check all the emotions experienced by the participants during the drill. (Yes/No)
* **Participant Comments** (Text): Provide any comments you have about your experience during the drill.
* **Drill Leaders Comments** (Text): Provide any comments you have about the drill leaders' performance during the drill.
* **Overall Suggestions** (Text): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the drill.
