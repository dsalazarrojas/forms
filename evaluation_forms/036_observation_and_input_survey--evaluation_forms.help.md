# ประกObservation And Input Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from observers on their observations and inputs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your observer name in the "Observer Name" field.
2. Select the date of the observation in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the observation in the "Time" field.
4. Enter the location of the observation in the "Location" field.
5. Choose the type of observation (Observation or Input) in the "Observation Type" field.
6. Select the ratings for the observation in the "Ratings" field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Upload any relevant files in the "File Uploads" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer Name**: Enter your name as the observer.
* **Date**: Select the date of the observation using the date picker.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the observation using the time picker.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the observation took place.
* **Observation Type**: Choose between "Observation" and "Input" to indicate the type of observation.
* **Ratings**: Select one or more of the ratings: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about the observation.
* **File Uploads**: Upload any relevant files that support the observation.
