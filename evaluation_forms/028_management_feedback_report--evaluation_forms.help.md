# Management Feedback Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Management Feedback Report form is designed to collect feedback from staff members about their manager's performance. This feedback will help managers understand their strengths and weaknesses, enabling them to improve their management style and better support their team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Management Feedback Report form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the report.
2. Provide your manager's information, including their name and email address.
3. Describe your manager's behavior using the drop-down menu (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Choose the areas where you feel your manager has strengths or weaknesses (Communication, Teamwork, Time Management, Productivity).
5. Enter any comments you have about your manager.
6. Enter your name and submit the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the report is being submitted.
* **Manager's Information** (`manager_info`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name and email address.
* **Manager Behavior** (`manager_behavior`, select_one, optional): Describe your manager's behavior, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Staff Motivation** (`staff_motivation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how motivated you feel your staff is, selecting from Highly Motivated, Somewhat Motivated, Low Motivated, or Very Low Motivated.
* **Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, select_multiple, optional): Select from Communication, Teamwork, Time Management, or Productivity where you have concerns about your manager's performance.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your manager.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager's Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Report Submitted By** (`report_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Report Comments** (`report_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the report.
