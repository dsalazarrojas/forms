# Safety Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about workplace safety incidents, including their reporting, handling, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in one session, providing accurate and complete information for each question.
2. Answer all questions relevant to the incident you are reporting.
3. Select the correct options for each question based on your experience or knowledge of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Perception Survey** (`title`, required): This is the title of the form, and you will not need to fill it out as it's a system-generated field.
* **Safety Perception Survey** (`survey_page`, required): This is the main page of the survey where you will answer questions related to workplace safety.
* **Questions** (`questions`, required): Select all the options that apply to the incident you are reporting.
* **How safe do you feel at work** (`safety_feeling`, required): Rate your perception of safety at work on a scale (not explicitly defined in the form, so assume it's a subjective measurement).
* **Have you reported a safety incident** (`incident_reported`, required): If the incident was reported, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Have measures been taken to address safety concerns** (`safety_measures`, required): If measures were taken to address concerns, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **How did the incident get addressed** (`incident_response`, required): Select how the incident was handled.
* **Have you received safety training** (`safety_training`, required): If you received training, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Are procedures in place** (`safety_procedure`, required): If procedures are in place, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
* **Who was it reported to** (`incident_reported_to`, required): Select all who the incident was reported to.
* **Who reported it** (`reported_by`, required): Select all who reported the incident.
* **Where did the incident occur** (`incident_location`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **What time did the incident occur** (`incident_time`, optional): Enter the exact time of the incident.
* **Describe the incident** (`incident_description`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Date of the incident** (`incident_date`, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **List of safety concerns** (`safety_concerns`, optional): List safety concerns related to the incident.
* **Measures taken to address concerns** (`safety_measures_taken`, optional): Describe measures taken to address concerns.
* **Follow up action** (`incident_follow_up`, optional): Describe the follow-up action taken after the incident.
* **Incident status** (`incident_status`, required): Select the status of the incident (Active, Resolved, Closed).
* **Who reported the incident** (`incident_reported_by`, required): Select who reported the incident.
* **Incident affected** (`incident_affected`, required): Select if others were affected by the incident.
* **Who** (`incident_who_reported`, required): Select who reported the incident.
* **Where** (`incident_where`, required): Select where the incident occurred.
* **Why** (`incident_why`, optional): Provide a description of why the incident happened.
* **What** (`incident_what`, optional): Describe the incident.
* **When** (`incident_when`, optional): Provide the time of the incident.
