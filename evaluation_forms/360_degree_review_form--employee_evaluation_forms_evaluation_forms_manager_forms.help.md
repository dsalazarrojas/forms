# 360 Degree Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360 Degree Review Form is a tool used to collect feedback from reviewers who have worked with or observed the performance of the reviewee. This form provides a comprehensive evaluation of the reviewee's overall performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Reviewee Information**: Provide the full name, job title, and department/team of the person being reviewed.
2. **Your Relationship**: Select the role that best describes your relationship to the reviewee, such as Manager, Peer/Colleague, Direct Report, or Self-review.
3. **Assessment**: Provide an overall performance assessment, rating of 1 to 5, and select specific options for communication, teamwork, and leadership.
4. **Strengths**: Choose up to 5 items that best describe the reviewee's strengths.
5. **Growth Areas**: Identify areas where the reviewee may need improvement.
6. **Support**: Determine if the reviewee receives adequate support for growth.
7. **Notable Accomplishments**: List 2-3 notable achievements or contributions from the review period.
8. **Suggestions**: Provide specific and constructive suggestions for improvement or development.
9. **Additional Comments**: Enter any other observations or context that support this review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewee full name**: Enter the reviewee's first and last name.
* **Reviewee job title**: Enter the reviewee's job title.
* **Department or team**: Enter the reviewee's department or team.
* **Date of hire**: Enter the reviewee's date of hire (optional).
* **Years with organization**: Enter the number of years the reviewee has been with the organization.
* **Your relationship to the reviewee**: Choose the role that best describes your relationship to the reviewee.
* **Overall performance assessment**: Select the overall performance assessment from the options provided.
* **Communication rating**: Rate the reviewee's communication from 1 to 10.
* **Teamwork and collaboration rating**: Rate the reviewee's teamwork and collaboration from 1 to 10.
* **Leadership/initiative rating**: Rate the reviewee's leadership and initiative from 1 to 10 (optional).
* **Key strengths**: Choose up to 5 items that best describe the reviewee's strengths.
* **Areas for development**: Choose up to 5 items that best describe areas where the reviewee may need improvement.
* **Support for growth**: Select whether the reviewee receives adequate support for growth.
* **Notable accomplishments**: List 2-3 notable achievements or contributions from the review period.
* **Suggestions for improvement**: Enter specific and constructive suggestions for improvement or development.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any other observations or context that support this review.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples to support your assessment.
* Keep the feedback focused on the reviewee's performance and not on personal opinions or biases.
* Review this form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* This form should not be used for personal or informal purposes.
