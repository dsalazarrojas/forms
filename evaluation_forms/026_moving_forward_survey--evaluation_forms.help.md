# Moving Forward Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Forward Survey is designed to help you reflect on your past and future goals, achievements, and obstacles. This form will gather information on your experiences, aspirations, and challenges, which will aid us in providing you with personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the introduction page (page 1), where you will find a brief welcome message.
2. Review each section carefully and complete the required fields.
3. Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions, such as "past_year_achievements" and "forward_looking_achievements".
4. Enter your answers in the text fields, such as "past_year_obstacle" and "next_year_goal".
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, optional): A brief welcome message to guide you through the survey.

### Page 2: Past Year
* **past_year** (`past_year`, number, optional): Enter the year you are currently in or the last year you achieved your goals. This field helps us understand your timeline.

### Page 3: Forward Looking
* **Forward Looking** (`forward_looking`, text, optional): Describe your aspirations, hopes, and expectations for the upcoming year.

### Page 4: Past Year Achievements
* **Past Year Achievements** (`past_year_achievements`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
This field asks you to reflect on your accomplishments and achievements from the past year.

### Page 5: Forward Looking Achievements
* **Forward Looking Achievements** (`forward_looking_achievements`, select one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
This field helps us understand your goals and aspirations for the next year.

### Page 6: Next Year Goal
* **Next Year Goal** (`next_year_goal`, date, optional): Enter your desired date for your next goal or milestone.

### Page 7: Next Year Obstacle
* **Next Year Obstacle** (`next_year_obstacle`, text, optional): Describe any challenges or obstacles you may face in the upcoming year.

### Page 8: Next Year Support
* **Next Year Support** (`next_year_support`, text, optional): Describe the resources or support you need to overcome challenges and achieve your goals.

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Use the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
* If you need more space, use the text fields to elaborate on your answers.
* Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
