# protective_gloves_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the effectiveness and comfort of protective gloves. This form is designed to help us understand how well the gloves protected you and how comfortable you found them to be.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the page you wish to complete (there is only one page in this form).
2. Fill in the required fields with your honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Form (first_page)** (`text`, required: false): This is a free-text field where you can provide any comments or feedback you have on the gloves. Please keep your answer concise and to the point.
* **Glove Effectiveness (glove_effectiveness)** (`number`, required: false): This field is for rating how well the gloves protected you, on a scale of 0 to 10. Please rate based on your experience.
* **Comfort (comfort)** (`text`, required: false): This field is for rating how comfortable you found the gloves to be, on a scale of 0 to 10. Please rate based on your experience.
