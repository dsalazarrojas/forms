# dental_employee_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to the evaluation of dental employees. It's intended to provide a systematic way to gather data on their skills, strengths, and areas for improvement. The form is likely used by managers or supervisors to assess employee performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you understand what's required of you.
2. Fill out the "General Info" page with the employee's basic information (Name, ID number, etc.).
3. On the "Employee Info" page, provide any additional details about the employee.
4. For each section (e.g., "Dental Clinic Info," "Evaluation"), follow the instructions provided and fill out the relevant fields.
5. Use the "Evaluation Scale" fields to provide ratings or feedback on the employee's performance.
6. For "Recommended Treatment" fields, select "Yes" or "No" depending on whether the employee requires additional treatment or support.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
