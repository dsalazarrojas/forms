# Health Insurance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Evaluation Survey is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from policyholders about their health insurance plans. This form aims to gather information about the policyholders' overall satisfaction with their plans, including their experiences with the claims process and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide policyholder information (optional): If you wish to provide your name, email, or phone number, please enter it in the "Policyholder Information" field.
2. Enter health plan information (optional): If you wish to provide details about your health plan, such as plan name or ID, please enter it in the "Health Plan Information" field.
3. Evaluate your health plan: Answer the "Overall Evaluation of Health Plan" question by selecting a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with your plan.
4. Describe your claims process experience: Answer the "What do you think of your health plan's claims process?" question by providing a written evaluation of your experiences with the claims process.
5. Evaluate customer service: Answer the "What do you think of your health plan's customer service?" question by providing a written evaluation of your experiences with the customer service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policyholder Information** (Policyholder Information, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your basic details, such as name, email, or phone number. If you don't wish to provide this information, you can leave this field blank.
* **Health Plan Information** (Health Plan Information, text, optional): This field is where you can enter details about your health plan, such as plan name or ID. If you don't have this information, you can leave this field blank.
* **Overall Evaluation of Health Plan** (Overall Evaluation of Health Plan, number, required): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your health plan on a scale of 1-5.
* **What do you think of your health plan's claims process?** (What do you think of your health plan's claims process?, text, required): Please provide a written evaluation of your experiences with the claims process.
* **What do you think of your health plan's customer service?** (What do you think of your health plan's customer service?, number, required): Please provide a written evaluation of your experiences with the customer service.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when evaluating your health plan's claims process and customer service.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us.
