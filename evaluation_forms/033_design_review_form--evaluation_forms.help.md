# Design Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment on a design project, helping project stakeholders to identify areas of improvement and suggest recommendations for future developments.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
2.  Ensure that each section is completed correctly, as indicated in the field explanations below.
3.  If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank or mark it as "N/A" to avoid any confusion.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form, as corrections are not allowed after submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: This is the name of the project being reviewed. Please make sure to fill this out with the correct project name to avoid any confusion.
* **Reviewer Name**: This is your name as the reviewer. Please ensure that you fill out your correct name, as it will be linked to your feedback.
* **Date Reviewed**: This is the date you reviewed the project. Use the date format MM/DD/YY to avoid any errors.
* **Time Reviewed**: This is the time you reviewed the project. Use the 24-hour format HH:MM to avoid any confusion.
* **Evaluation Score**: Please rate the project's design quality using the provided scale. The evaluation score will help in identifying areas of improvement.
*   **Evaluation Score Scale**: This is a scale of evaluation scores where you can choose from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
*   **Evaluation Comments**: Provide a written feedback on the project's design, including positive and negative aspects.
*   **Review Notes**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or observations you may have about the project.
*   **Design Review Form**: This field is intended to be completed by the design team to describe the project's current design status. Please ensure this is filled out if you are a design team member.
*   **Reviewer Recommendations**: This is where you can provide specific recommendations for the project. Please be concrete and focused on the areas of improvement.
