# Granny Home Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Granny Home Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and opinions from users regarding the construction and use of granny flats. This form will help us understand your experience with building and using a granny flat, including its quality, investment, and installation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. Select the most relevant option from the drop-down menus for select fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by *.
4. Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the granny flat** (`granny_flat`, select_one, *): This question asks if the granny flat you built is currently occupied. Please select "Yes" if it is, or "No" if it's not.
* **Use of granny flat** (`granny_flat_use`, text): Describe how you use the granny flat, e.g., as a rental, for family members, or for storage.
* **How much money you spent on the granny flat** (`granny_flat_investment`, number): Enter the total amount you spent on the granny flat, including all construction and installation costs.
* **Return on investment** (`granny_flat_return`, number): Rate the return on investment of the granny flat, on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **How difficult was the installation** (`granny_flat_installation`, select_one): Rate the difficulty of the installation process, on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is very easy and 5 is extremely difficult.
* **How much planning was required** (`granny_flat_investment_planning`, select_one): Rate the amount of planning required for the installation, on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is little planning and 5 is extensive planning.
* **Will you consider building a granny flat in the future?** (`granny_flat_future`, select_one): Let us know if you would consider building another granny flat in the future, by selecting "Yes" or "No".
