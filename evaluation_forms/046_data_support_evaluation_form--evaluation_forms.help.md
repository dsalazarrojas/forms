# Data Support Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their interactions with the data support team, helping us understand their experience, satisfaction, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in a clear and concise manner.
2. Select the relevant options that apply to your experience.
3. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled in to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the nature of your interaction with our data support team?** (`interaction`, text, required: false): This field asks you to describe your interaction with the data support team, such as phone call, email, or chat. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your interaction?** (`satisfaction`, number, required: false): This question is meant to gauge your level of satisfaction with the service provided. Select a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your satisfaction.
* **What areas for improvement would you suggest we address?** (`improvement_area`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose one or more areas from the list, such as "Data Support Knowledge" or "Response Time", that you think the data support team should improve.
* **Do you have any other suggestions or ideas?** (`suggestion`, select_multiple, required: true): If you have any additional suggestions or ideas, select the "Yes" option and provide more details in the "Feedback" field.
* **Would you be willing to have a follow-up conversation to discuss your interaction in more detail?** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false): If you're willing to discuss your interaction further, select "Yes" and we'll be in touch.
* **Do you have any other feedback about our data support team?** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This field is an open-ended question for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about your interaction with the data support team.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): If you'd like to provide your name or a nickname for your feedback, feel free to do so.
