# Nursing Preceptor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on a preceptor's performance, communication skills, and ability to provide a comfortable learning environment for new nurses.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully, providing feedback on each field.
- Use the available options to select the most accurate response for each field.
- If you have any comments or suggestions that do not fit into the provided options, please use the "Comments and Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Clinical Performance
* **Clinical Performance** (`clinical_performance`, `number`, required/optional): Assess the preceptor's performance in providing clinical guidance and support to new nurses. Select one of the following options:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the preceptor's communication skills, including their ability to articulate information effectively. Select one of the following options:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Preceptor Support
* **Preceptor Support** (`preceptor_support`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the areas where the preceptor provided strong support to new nurses:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Professionalism
* **Professionalism** (`preceptor_professionalism`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the preceptor's professionalism, including their respect for others, commitment to excellence, and adherence to policies and procedures. Select one of the following options:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Mastery of Materials
* **Mastery of Materials** (`preceptor_mastery_of_materials`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the areas where the preceptor demonstrated a strong mastery of materials:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Organizational Skills
* **Organizational Skills** (`preceptor_organizational_skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the areas where the preceptor demonstrated strong organizational skills:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### New Nurse Comfort Level
* **New Nurse Comfort Level** (`new_nurse_comfort_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the preceptor's ability to create a comfortable learning environment for new nurses. Select one of the following options:
	+ High
	+ Low

### New Nurse Learning Experience
* **New Nurse Learning Experience** (`new_nurse_learning_experience`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate the preceptor's effectiveness in creating a positive learning experience for new nurses. Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement

### Comments and Suggestions
* **Comments and Suggestions** (`comments_and_suggestions`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the preceptor.

### Final Comments
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any final comments or suggestions you have for the preceptor.

### Preceptor Name
* **Preceptor Name** (`preceptor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the preceptor's name.

### New Nurse Name
* **New Nurse Name** (`new_nurse_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the new nurse's name.

## Tips
- Be specific and concise in your feedback.
- Focus on the preceptor's strengths and areas for improvement.
- Provide concrete examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
- Keep your comments respectful and professional.
