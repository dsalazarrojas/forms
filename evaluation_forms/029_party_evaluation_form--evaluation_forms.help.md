# Party Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and ratings from users about their experience at a party. Your input will help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your rating for each field based on your experience at the party.
2. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the comments field.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement, select one of the options in the feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Party Quality** (Page 1, id: 1): Rate the overall quality of the party from 1 to 5.
* **Music Quality** (Page 1, id: 2): Rate the quality of the music at the party from 1 to 5.
* **Food Quality** (Page 1, id: 3): Rate the quality of the food at the party from 1 to 5.
* **Atmosphere Quality** (Page 1, id: 4): Rate the quality of the atmosphere at the party from 1 to 5.
* **Service Quality** (Page 1, id: 5): Rate the quality of the service at the party from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 1, id: 6): Rate your overall satisfaction with the party from 1 to 5.
* **Party Organizer** (Page 1, id: 7): Select the rating for the party organizer's performance from 1 to 5.
* **Event Coordinator** (Page 1, id: 8): Select all the event coordinators who contributed to the party (optional).
* **Comments** (Page 1, id: 9): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the party.
* **Feedback** (Page 1, id: 10): Let us know if you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
