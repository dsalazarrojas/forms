# Journal Club Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Journal Club Evaluation Form is a comprehensive tool for evaluating papers and collecting feedback on their quality and relevance. It's designed to help assess the paper's readability, clarity, author contribution, and other essential factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the form and the paper you want to evaluate.
2. Select the relevant options for each section, using the available choices (e.g., yes, no, etc.).
3. Provide a brief explanation for each field as necessary.
4. Submit your evaluation and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Paper Readability** (1) (`paper_readability`, text, required: false): This field is used to rate the paper's readability, i.e., how easy it is to understand and read.
* **Paper Clarity** (2) (`paper_clarity`, text, required: false): This field is used to rate the paper's clarity, i.e., how well the author has organized their ideas.
* **Paper Organisation** (3) (`paper_organisation`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the paper's organization is effective or not.
* **Author Contribution** (4) (`author_contribution`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply regarding the author's contribution to the paper.
* **Relevance** (5) (`relevance`, number, required: false): Provide a numerical rating for the paper's relevance.
* **Scientific Quality** (6) (`scientific_quality`, text, required: false): This field is used to rate the paper's scientific quality, i.e., how well the research is conducted.
* **Peer Review** (7) (`peer_review`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide feedback on the peer review process.
* **Overall Score** (8) (`overall_score`, select_one, required: false): Choose the overall score for the paper.
* **Comments** (9) (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the paper.
* **Evaluation** (10) (`evaluation`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply regarding the evaluation of the paper.
* **E-mail** (11) (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your e-mail address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (12) (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date-Time** (13) (`date_time`, date, required: false): Provide the date and time you completed the evaluation.
