# vehicle-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle-evaluation-form is a collection of fields that gather information about a vehicle's characteristics, owner's information, and evaluation status. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle evaluation in the "Vehicle Evaluation" field (field 1).
2. Select the vehicle's condition (field 2).
3. Enter the vehicle's year (field 3) and mileage (field 4).
4. Choose the vehicle's price range (field 5) and color (field 6).
5. Select the vehicle's fuel type (field 7).
6. Enter the vehicle's owner's name (field 8), email (field 10), phone number (field 11), and comment (field 12).
7. Rate the vehicle's performance (field 13) and enter the purchase amount (field 14).
8. Evaluate the vehicle's purchaser reputation (field 15).
9. Choose the vehicle type (field 16), make (field 17), model (field 18), plate number (field 19), and features (field 20).
10. Enter any comments about the vehicle (field 21).
11. Select the vehicle's rating system (field 22), status (field 23), ownership status (field 24), and purchase status (field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Evaluation (field 1)**: Enter a brief evaluation of the vehicle's condition, performance, and overall quality.
* **Vehicle Condition (field 2)**: Select the vehicle's condition, such as Engine, Transmission, or Interior.
* **Vehicle Year (field 3)**: Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Mileage (field 4)**: Enter the vehicle's mileage.
* **Vehicle Price (field 5)**: Choose the vehicle's price range, such as under 50k, 50k-100k, or over 100k.
* **Vehicle Color (field 6)**: Select the vehicle's color, such as Red, Blue, or Green.
* **Vehicle Fuel Type (field 7)**: Choose the vehicle's fuel type, such as Petrol, Diesel, or Hybrid.
* **Vehicle Owner (field 8)**: Enter the vehicle's owner's name.
* **Purchaser Name (field 9)**: Enter the name of the person purchasing the vehicle.
* **Purchaser Email (field 10)**: Enter the purchaser's email address.
* **Purchaser Phone (field 11)**: Enter the purchaser's phone number.
* **Purchaser Comment (field 12)**: Enter any comments about the purchaser.
* **Vehicle Rating (field 13)**: Rate the vehicle's performance on a scale.
* **Purchase Amount (field 14)**: Enter the amount paid for the vehicle.
* **Purchaser Reputation (field 15)**: Evaluate the purchaser's reputation, such as Excellent, Very Good, or Good.
* **Vehicle Type (field 16)**: Choose the type of vehicle, such as Car, Van, or Truck.
* **Vehicle Make (field 17)**: Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model (field 18)**: Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Plate Number (field 19)**: Enter the vehicle's plate number.
* **Vehicle Features (field 20)**: Enter any notable features of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Comments (field 21)**: Enter any comments about the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Rating System (field 22)**: Choose the vehicle's rating system, such as 1-5 or 1-3.
* **Vehicle Status (field 23)**: Choose the vehicle's status, such as New, Used, or Old.
* **Vehicle Ownership Status (field 24)**: Choose the vehicle's ownership status, such as Private or Public.
* **Vehicle Purchase Status (field 25)**: Choose the vehicle's purchase status, such as Sold, Unsold, or Unknown.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have a legitimate interest in the vehicle being evaluated.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
