# Historical Position Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to provide an assessment of an individual's historical position, evaluating their performance and work history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the role of the individual being assessed.
2. Enter the individual's responsibilities during this period.
3. Select the dates of the performance period.
4. Evaluate the individual's performance on a scale of Good or Fair.
5. Provide any comments or additional information.
6. Decide if the individual's historical position assessment form has been uploaded.
7. Evaluate the performance evaluation form on a scale of Good or Fair.
8. Input the performance score.
9. Evaluate the individual's historical position on a scale of Good or Fair.
10. Decide if the individual's historical position assessment form scale has been used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Enter the role of the individual being assessed.
* **Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, `text`, required): List the individual's responsibilities during this performance period.
* **Dates** (`dates`, `date`, required): Select the dates of the performance period.
* **Performance Ratings** (`performance_ratings`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the performance ratings on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, `select_multiple`, optional): Decide if the individual's historical position assessment form has been uploaded.
* **Historical Position Assessment Form Comments** (`historical_position_assessment_form_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information on the historical position assessment form.
* **Historical Position Assessment Form Attachments** (`historical_position_assessment_form_attachments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Decide if the individual's historical position assessment form attachments have been uploaded.
* **Historical Position Evaluation Form** (`historical_position_evaluation_form_rating`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the performance evaluation form on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Historical Position Evaluation Score** (`historical_position_evaluation_rating_score`, `number`, required): Input the performance evaluation score.
* **Performance Evaluation Form** (`performance_evaluation_form_rating`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the performance evaluation form on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Historical Position Score** (`historical_position_score`, `number`, required): Evaluate the individual's historical position on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Historical Position Scale** (`historical_position_scale`, `select_one`, required): Decide if the individual's historical position assessment form scale has been used.
* **Historical Position Scale Score** (`historical_position_scale_score`, `text`, optional): Enter the score for the historical position scale.
* **Historical Position Evaluation Score** (`historical_position_evaluation_score`, `number`, required): Evaluate the individual's historical position on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, required): Input the evaluation score.
* **Historical Position Rating Scale** (`historical_position_rating_scale`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the individual's historical position on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Historical Position Rating** (`historical_position_scale_rating`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the individual's historical position on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Evaluation Form Rating System** (`evaluation_form_rating_system`, `text`, optional): Enter the evaluation form rating system.
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`, `text`, optional): Enter the performance score.
* **Historical Position Form Comment** (`historical_position_form_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments on the historical position form.

Note: Some fields are optional and should only be filled if necessary.
