# Visitas De Prospeco 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Visitas De Prospeco 2" form is designed to gather information about store visits, including details about the visited store, the customer, and the visit itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
2. Enter the necessary information in each field, taking care to enter the correct type of information (e.g., email for email fields, date or time for date/time fields, etc.).
3. Review your entries carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loja** (`loja_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store that was visited.
* **Loja E-mail** (`loja_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the store (if available).
* **Loja Telefone** (`loja_telefone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the store (if available).
* **Nome do Parceiro** (`parceiro_nome`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer or partner.
* **Parceiro E-mail** (`parceiro_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer or partner (if available).
* **Parceiro Telefone** (`parceiro_telefone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer or partner (if available).
* **Parceiro Endereço** (`parceiro_endereco`, text, optional): Enter the address of the customer or partner (if available).
* **Parceiro Obervações** (`parceiro_obs`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the customer or partner.
* **Ano da Visita** (`visita_ano`, text, optional): Enter the year of the visit.
* **Mês da Visita** (`visita_mes`, text, optional): Enter the month of the visit.
* **Dia da Visita** (`visita_dia`, text, optional): Enter the day of the visit.
* **Hora da Visita** (`visita_hora`, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Obervações da Visita** (`visita_obs`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Lojas Visitas** (`visita_lojas`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes to indicate whether or not each store was visited.
* **Parceiros Visitas** (`parceiro_lojas`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes to indicate whether or not each partner was visited.
* **Data da Visita** (`visita_data`, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Hora da Visita** (`visita_hora`, time, optional): Enter the hour of the visit.
* **Hora do Parceiro** (`parceiro_hora`, time, optional): Enter the hour of the partner.
* **Data do Parceiro** (`parceiro_data`, date, optional): Enter the date of the partner.
* **Ano da Visita** (`visita_ano`, number, optional): Enter the year of the visit.
* **Ano do Parceiro** (`parceiro_ano`, number, optional): Enter the year of the partner.
* **Montante da Visita** (`visita_montante`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the visit.
* **Montante do Parceiro** (`parceiro_montante`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the partner.
* **Satisfação da Visita** (`visita_satisfa_o`, select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate whether or not the visit was satisfactory.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your supervisor or support team.
