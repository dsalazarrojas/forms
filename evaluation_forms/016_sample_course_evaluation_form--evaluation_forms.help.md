# Sample Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the "Sample Course" from students, providing a structured way to express opinions on its content, relevance, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form completely, providing as much information as possible.
2. Ensure that you provide your true and honest feedback.
3. If you've already evaluated this course, please do not fill out the form multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the course you're evaluating.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Instructor Title** (`course_instructor`, select_one, optional): Select the title of the course instructor (Teacher, TA, Professor, Other).
* **Instructor Title** (`course_instructor_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the instructor (e.g. Mr., Mrs., Dr., etc.).
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, optional): Use a number (1-5) to rate your overall satisfaction with the course content.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and suggestions about the course.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you evaluated the course.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you spent evaluating the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.

Please note that all fields are optional, but filling out the form completely will provide the most valuable feedback.
