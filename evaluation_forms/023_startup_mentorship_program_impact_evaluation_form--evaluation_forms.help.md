# Startup Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the impact of the startup mentorship program on participating startups. It helps assess the program's effectiveness in achieving its goals and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Review the available options for select fields, and choose the one that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a detailed description of your experience.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the purpose of the startup?** (`startup_mission`, text, required): Please describe the main objective of the startup. This will help us understand the startup's goals and how the program has impacted them.
* **How has the program impacted the startup's mission?** (`mission_alignment`, text, required): Share specific examples of how the program has influenced the startup's mission.
* **What positive outcomes have the startup achieved through the program?** (`mentorship_outcomes`, select_multiple, required): Choose the positive outcomes achieved by the startup, such as "Positive", "Negative", or "Neutral". 
* **How has the program's impact on the startup been evaluated?** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of the program's impact evaluation, which can be "Quarterly", "Bi-Annually", or "Annually".
* **What outcomes have the program evaluations led to?** (`evaluation_outcomes`, text, required): Describe the outcomes of the program evaluations, such as changes in the startup's mission or growth.
* **What is the status of the form submission?** (`form_submission`, text, optional): If applicable, provide information about the form submission status.
* **Has the startup received support through the program?** (`startup_support`, select_one, required): Respond with "True" or "False", indicating whether the startup received support.
* **How much funding has the startup received from the program?** (`startup_support_funding`, number, required): Provide the exact amount of funding received.
* **Has the program's support impacted the startup's mission?** (`startup_evaluation_support`, select_one, required): Respond with "True" or "False", indicating whether the program's support had an impact on the startup's mission.
* **Form Submission Status Other** (`form_submission_status_other`, text, optional): If not applicable, describe the status of the form submission.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are complete and consistent.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the program administrators.
