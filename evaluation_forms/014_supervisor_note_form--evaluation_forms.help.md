# Supervisor Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and record supervisor observations and notes on specific dates and times regarding an individual's behavior.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
2. Select the relevant date and time fields to record the specific incident or observation.
3. Enter your supervisor's note in the designated fields.
