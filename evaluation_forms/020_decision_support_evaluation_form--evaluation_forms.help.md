# decision_support_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The decision support evaluation form is designed to collect evaluations about the decision support process. Evaluators use this form to provide feedback on the support process used, the level of support provided, and their overall experience with the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your evaluation. You can skip fields that don't apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Decision Support Process Used**
	+ (`decision_support_process_used`, text, optional): Describe the decision support process used in your evaluation.
* **Description**
	+ (`decision_process_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your evaluation experience.
* **Decision Support Level**
	+ (`decision_support_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of decision support you received, where:
		- High: High level of decision support.
		- Medium: Medium level of decision support.
		- Low: Low level of decision support.
* **Decision Support Impact**
	+ (`decision_support_impact`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the impact of the decision support you received, where:
		- High: High impact.
		- Medium: Medium impact.
		- Low: Low impact.
* **Decision Making Process**
	+ (`decision_making_process`, text, optional): Describe the decision making process used in your evaluation.
* **Stakeholders Involved**
	+ (`stakeholders_involved`, text, optional): List the stakeholders involved in the decision making process.
* **Decision Outcome**
	+ (`decision_outcome`, time, optional): Report the time it took to make the decision.
* **Evaluation Form**
	+ (`evaluation_form`, note, optional): Leave a note about your evaluation experience.
* **Evaluation Outcome**
	+ (`evaluation_outcome`, select_one, optional): Report your evaluation outcome, where:
		- Very Good: The evaluation experience was very good.
		- Good: The evaluation experience was good.
		- Fair: The evaluation experience was fair.
		- Poor: The evaluation experience was poor.
* **Recommendations for Improvement**
	+ (`recommendations_for_improvement`, text, optional): Provide recommendations for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**
	+ (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave additional comments about your evaluation experience.
* **Evaluation Form Comments**
	+ (`evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional): Leave comments about the evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Process Time**
	+ (`evaluation_process_time`, number, optional): Report the time it took to complete the evaluation process.
* **Evaluation Process Rating**
	+ (`evaluation_process_rating`, number, optional): Report a rating for the evaluation process, from 1 to 10.
* **Evaluation Form Version**
	+ (`evaluation_form_version`, text, optional): Report the version of the evaluation form used.

## Tips
- Be as thorough as possible when filling out the form.
- If a field doesn't apply to your experience, you can skip it.
- If you have any comments or recommendations, please be specific and clear. 

Please note that this form is meant for one-time evaluations, so it's not designed to be filled out multiple times by the same user.
