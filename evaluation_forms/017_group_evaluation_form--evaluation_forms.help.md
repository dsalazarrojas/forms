# group_evaluate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "group_evaluate_form" is a form used to gather input from users on their group's performance, allowing for a thorough review of the group's overall standing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Group Name" field with the name of your group.
2. In the "Evaluate Manager" field, select "Yes" or choose the name of the manager of your group.
3. Enter the "Evaluation Date" in the provided date field.
4. Select the "Evaluate Time" in the provided time field.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes about your evaluation in the "Evaluate Note" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Evaluate Email" field.
7. Add any additional phone number information in the "Evaluate Phone" field.
8. If you have any further comments, enter them in the "Evaluate Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Group Name** (`form_group_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your group.
- **Evaluate Manager** (`evaluate_manager`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are evaluating the manager, or choose the name of the manager of your group.
- **Evaluate Manager** (`evaluate_manager`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is a duplicate of "Evaluate Manager" field, intended for future expansion. 
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your evaluation.
- **Evaluate Time** (`evaluate_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of your evaluation.
- **Evaluate Note** (`evaluate_note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your evaluation.
- **Evaluate Email** (`form_evaluate_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Evaluate Phone** (`evaluate_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any phone number information.
- **Evaluate Note** (`evaluate_note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your evaluation.
