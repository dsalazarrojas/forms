# Virtual Reality Therapy Research Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of virtual reality (VR) therapy in achieving specific user goals and outcomes. It will help us understand how VR therapy has helped you, the user, achieve your goals and what improvements we can make to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
3. For open-ended questions, feel free to write as much or as little as you like.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or guidance from the administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Note): A space for you to provide any background information about your session.
* **User Demographics** (Text): Your demographic details such as age, occupation, etc. (optional)
* **User Experience** (Number): Rate your overall experience with VR therapy, on a scale of 1-10.
* **User Goals** (Text): Describe your goals for this session, e.g., reducing anxiety, improving focus, etc.
* **User Motivations** (Text): Explain what motivated you to try VR therapy, e.g., personal interest, medical condition, etc.
* **Virtual Reality Exposure** (Select Multiple):
	+ VR: You have used VR before.
	+ non-vr: You have not used VR before.
	+ other: Other (please specify)
* **Virtual Reality Experience** (Text): Describe your experience with VR, including any previous sessions.
* **Session Goals** (Text): Describe your goals for this session, e.g., relaxation, learning, etc.
* **Session Outcomes** (Text): Describe the outcomes you would like to achieve from this session, e.g., reduced stress, improved focus, etc.
* **Session Notes** (Text): Add any additional information about your session that you feel is relevant.
* **Evaluation** (Text): How did you feel about the session? Would you recommend it to others?
* **Suggestions** (Text): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them here.
* **Next Steps** (Text): What do you plan to do next, e.g., continue with VR, seek other therapies, etc.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Any other comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Session Rating** (Number): Rate the session on a scale of 1-10.
* **Total Time** (Number): Estimate the total time you spent on the session.
* **Virtual Reality System Use** (Number): Rate how much you used the VR system during the session, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Session Duration** (Number): Estimate the total duration of the session.
* **User Involvement** (Text): Describe your level of engagement during the session, e.g., active, passive, etc.
* **System Usefulness** (Text): Describe how useful you found the VR system in achieving your goals.
* **User Satisfaction** (Text): Rate your satisfaction with the session, e.g., very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, etc.
* **System Usability** (Text): Describe your experience with the VR system's usability, e.g., easy, difficult, etc.
* **System Feasibility** (Text): Describe your experience with the feasibility of the VR system, e.g., easy, difficult, etc.
* **System Fidelity** (Text): Describe your experience with the VR system's fidelity, e.g., realistic, not realistic, etc.
* **System Fidelity 2** (Text): Describe your experience with the VR system's fidelity, e.g., realistic, not realistic, etc.

By completing this form, you'll help us understand how VR therapy has impacted you and how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.
