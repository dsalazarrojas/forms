# Church Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Church Event Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees about their experiences with church events. This form helps us understand what we do well and what we can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. This will help us understand your experience and provide valuable feedback.
2. **Answer each question honestly**. Your responses will be kept confidential.
3. **Be specific**. Try to provide concrete reasons or examples when answering questions.
4. **Submit the form** once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Event** (`evaluated_event_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Your Role at the Event** (`role_at_event`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Attendee
	+ Volunteer
	+ Speaker/Presenter
	+ Organizer
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate how relevant you found the content or program of the event. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Highly Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Relevant
* **Venue and Facilities Rating** (`venue_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the venue and facilities. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Hospitality and Food Rating** (`hospitality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospitality and food. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ N/A
* **Event Organization** (`organization_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well-organized you found the event. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Well Organized
	+ Organized
	+ Average
	+ Poorly Organized
* **Spiritual Impact** (`spiritual_impact`, select_one, required): Rate the spiritual impact of the event. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Significant Impact
	+ Moderate Impact
	+ Minimal Impact
	+ No Impact
* **Favorite Part of the Event** (`favorite_part`, text, optional): If you have a favorite part of the event, please describe it.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): If there is anything we could have done better, please provide feedback.
* **Future Attendance** (`future_attendance`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would attend a similar event in the future. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Definitely
	+ Probably
	+ Not Sure
	+ False
* **Referral Likelihood** (`referral_likelihood`, select_one, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend our events to others. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Unlikely
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback, please provide them.
* **Your Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name_optional`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your name, please do so.
