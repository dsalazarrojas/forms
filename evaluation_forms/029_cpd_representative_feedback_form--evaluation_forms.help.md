# CPD Representative Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CPD Representative Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users on their interactions with representatives. The form is used to assess the representative's performance, identify areas for improvement, and provide suggestions for future interactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Date of Feedback**: Enter the date you provided feedback.
2. **Representative Name**: Enter the name of the representative you interacted with.
3. **Your Name**: Enter your name as the person providing feedback.
4. **Department**: Enter the department of the representative you interacted with (optional).
5. **Date of Interaction**: Enter the date of your interaction with the representative.
6. **Type of Interaction**: Choose the type of interaction you had with the representative (e.g., In-person meeting, Phone call, Email communication, Virtual meeting, Training session, Other).
7. **Professionalism**: Rate the representative's professionalism (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. **Knowledge**: Rate the representative's knowledge of the subject matter (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
9. **Communication**: Rate the representative's communication skills (Clarity and effectiveness, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
10. **Responsiveness**: Rate the representative's responsiveness to questions (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
11. **Helpfulness**: Rate the representative's helpfulness in addressing needs (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
12. **Time Management**: Rate the representative's respect for your time (Punctuality and efficiency, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
13. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the interaction (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
14. **Representative Strengths**: Provide specific strengths of the representative (optional).
15. **Areas for Improvement**: Suggest specific areas where the representative can improve (optional).
16. **Recommendations for Future Interactions**: Provide any recommendations for future interactions with the representative (optional).
17. **Would You Recommend This Representative**: Indicate whether you would recommend this representative in the future (Yes, strongly, True, Maybe, False, No, strongly not).
18. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your interaction with the representative (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Feedback**: This field is used to record the date you provided feedback. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Representative Name**: This field is used to record the name of the representative you interacted with. Please enter their full name as it appears on their business card or ID badge.
* **Your Name**: This field is used to record your name as the person providing feedback. Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
* **Department**: This field is used to record the department of the representative you interacted with. If the department is not relevant, you can leave this field blank.
* **Date of Interaction**: This field is used to record the date of your interaction with the representative. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Type of Interaction**: This field allows you to select the type of interaction you had with the representative (e.g., In-person meeting, Phone call, Email communication, Virtual meeting, Training session, Other).
* **Professionalism**: Rate the representative's professionalism on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Knowledge**: Rate the representative's knowledge of the subject matter on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Communication**: Rate the representative's communication skills on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Responsiveness**: Rate the representative's responsiveness to questions on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Helpfulness**: Rate the representative's helpfulness in addressing needs on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Time Management**: Rate the representative's respect for your time on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the interaction on a scale of Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Representative Strengths**: This field is used to provide specific strengths of the representative. Please enter their strengths or accomplishments.
* **Areas for Improvement**: This field is used to suggest specific areas where the representative can improve. Please enter their weaknesses or areas for growth.
