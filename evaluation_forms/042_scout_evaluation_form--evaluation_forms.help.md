# Scout Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scout Evaluation Form is a tool for collecting evaluations from scouts on a player's performance in a game or match.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your scout ID and/or your role in the evaluation process (e.g., coach, assistant coach, etc.) if required.
2. Fill out the form by answering the questions as per the instructions and guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Details
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, optional): This section is for entering the player's name or the player's ID number if you have one.
### Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`rating_overall`, number, optional): Rate the player's overall performance on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Positional Rating
* **Positional Rating** (`rating_position`, number, optional): If the player is assigned to a specific position, rate their performance on that position on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Positional Skills
* **Positional Skills** (`positional_skills`, text, optional): Describe the player's skills and abilities in the specific position they played.
### Athleticism
* **Athleticism** (`athleticism`, number, optional): Rate the player's athleticism on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Ball Control
* **Ball Control** (`ballcontrol`, number, optional): Rate the player's ball control on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Decision Making
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, number, optional): Rate the player's decision making on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Leadership
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, number, optional): Rate the player's leadership on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Communication
* **Communication** (`communication`, number, optional): Rate the player's communication on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Team Play
* **Team Play** (`teamplay`, number, optional): Rate the player's team play on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Sportsmanship
* **Sportsmanship** (`sportsmanship`, number, optional): Rate the player's sportsmanship on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Coachability
* **Coachability** (`coachability`, number, optional): Rate the player's coachability on a scale of 1 to 5.
### Scout Comments
* **Scout Comments** (`scout_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the player.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your feedback.
* Consider the player's performance in the context of their position and the game or match being evaluated.
* Use the scales to rate the player's performance based on your evaluation.
* Do not hesitate to provide constructive feedback.
