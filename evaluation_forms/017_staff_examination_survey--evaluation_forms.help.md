# Staff Examination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Examination Survey is a form used by managers to evaluate staff performance, identify strengths, and areas for improvement. This form is part of the HR process to help managers provide regular feedback and guidance to their staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the staff member being evaluated.
2. Provide a brief description of the staff member's performance.
3. Choose the staff member's employee performance level (e.g., excellent, good, fair).
4. Select the staff member's strengths.
5. Identify areas for improvement.
6. Choose a recommendation for the staff member's promotion (if applicable).
7. Select the staff member's level of responsibility.
8. Enter any additional comments from the manager.
9. Enter any comments from HR.
