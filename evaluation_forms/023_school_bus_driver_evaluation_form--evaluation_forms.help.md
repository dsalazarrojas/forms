# School Bus Driver Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Bus Driver Evaluation Form is used to assess the performance of school bus drivers in a specific period of time, evaluating their safety, management, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the "Bus Number" field to enter the bus's identification number.
2. Enter the "Evaluation Date" to specify the date of the evaluation.
3. Enter the "Driver Name" and "Driver ID" to identify the driver being evaluated.
4. Enter the "Supervisor Name" and "Supervisor ID" to identify the person completing the evaluation.
5. Rate the driver's performance using the "Performance Score" field, which can be entered as a number between 1 (lowest) and 5 (highest).
6. Provide any additional comments about the driver's performance in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7. Select the "Evaluation Form Completion Date" to mark the date the evaluation was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bus Number** (`Bus Number`, number, required): Enter the bus's identification number to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, date, required): Specify the date of the evaluation to ensure accurate records.
* **Driver Name** (`Driver Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the driver being evaluated.
* **Driver ID** (`Driver ID`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the driver being evaluated.
* **Supervisor Name** (`Supervisor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing the evaluation.
* **Supervisor ID** (`Supervisor ID`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the person completing the evaluation.
* **Performance Score** (`Performance Score`, number, required): Rate the driver's performance using a number between 1 (lowest) and 5 (highest).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`Evaluation Comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about the driver's performance.
* **Evaluation Form Completion Date** (`Evaluation Form Completion Date`, date, required): Select the date the evaluation was completed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
* If you are unsure about any field or the evaluation process, please consult with your supervisor or a designated authority for guidance.
