# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Feedback Form is a tool to collect guest feedback about their experience at our hotel. It helps us identify areas of improvement for our staff to enhance the quality of service and customer satisfaction. Please take a moment to provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1**: Fill in your information and feedback. You can submit the form by clicking on the "Submit" button.
- **Step 2**: Answer each question according to your experience. Please be as specific as possible in your answers.
- **Step 3**: Click "Submit" to send your feedback to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Your name will help us get back to you for further clarification if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address will help us contact you if we need more information about your feedback.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This will help us contact you if we need more clarification about your feedback.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple room types that you've experienced during your stay.
	+ One star: Below average stay
	+ Two stars: Average stay
	+ Three stars: Good stay
	+ Four stars: Excellent stay
	+ Five stars: Outstanding stay
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, optional): How would you rate our service?
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_one, optional): How would you rate the cleanliness of our hotel?
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Facilities** (`facilities`, select_multiple, optional): What facilities did you use during your stay? Choose one or multiple options.
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): How would you rate our hotel's location?
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Price** (`price`, select_one, optional): How would you rate the price of our services?
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your stay.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date of your stay. This will help us track your feedback with your stay.
