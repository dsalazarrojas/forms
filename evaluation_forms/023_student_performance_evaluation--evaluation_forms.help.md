This form is designed to collect information on a student's performance, specifically evaluating their level of excellence, good, or poor performance. The purpose of this form is to provide an easy way to record and track student progress in a subject, allowing for quick reference and evaluation. To prevent duplication, only one evaluation per student per subject should be submitted.

The form is composed of several fields:

*   Student name
*   Teacher name
*   Subject
*   Performance level (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
*   Date of evaluation
*   Time of evaluation
*   Notes (optional)
*   Email (for communication purposes)

Each field is explained below for better understanding and completion:

## Student Performance Evaluation - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the student's name in the `student_name` field. This is the name of the student being evaluated.
*   Enter the teacher's name in the `teacher_name` field.
*   Select the subject the student was evaluated on in the `subject` field.
*   Choose the performance level of the student (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) in the `performance_level` field.
*   Select the date of the evaluation in the `date` field (in the correct format: YYYY-MM-DD).
*   Enter the time of the evaluation in the `time` field (HH:MM).
*   Leave the `note` field empty or add any relevant notes.
*   Enter the email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student name** (`student_name`, text, **required**): This field is used to fill in the student's name.
*   **Teacher name** (`teacher_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person evaluating the student.
*   **Subject** (`subject`, text, **required**): Fill in the name of the subject the student was evaluated on.
*   **Performance level** (`performance_level`, select_one, **optional**): Select one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the evaluation (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Time** (`time`, time, **required**): Fill in the time of the evaluation in the format HH:MM.
*   **Notes** (`note`, note, **optional**): Leave empty or add any relevant notes for the evaluation.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter the email address for communication purposes.

Please note that all fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill in all fields for a complete evaluation.
