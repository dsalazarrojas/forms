# end_of_season_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review the performance of a season and collect feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have access to the information being requested and that the season has indeed ended.
2. Select the relevant seasonal performance details from the "Performance Measures" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the campaign goals that were achieved during the season.
4. Provide your overall rating of the season's performance.
5. Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
6. Select the date when the season ended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Performance**: A text area for you to provide any notes about the season's performance.
* **Feedback**: A text field for you to provide any feedback you may have about the season.
* **Suggestions**: A text field for you to provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Performance Measures**: A dropdown menu where you can select the performance measures achieved during the season. Please choose all that apply.
	+ Performance Measures 1
	+ Performance Measures 2
	+ Performance Measures 3
* **Campaign Goals**: A dropdown menu where you can select the campaign goals achieved during the season. Please choose all that apply.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Overall Rating**: A field to rate the performance of the season on a scale. Please note that this is optional.
* **Comments**: A text field for you to provide any additional comments about the season.
* **Date Completed**: A field to select the date when the season ended. Please ensure that you choose the correct date.
