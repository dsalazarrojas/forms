# School Leadership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Leadership Evaluation Form is a tool designed to evaluate the performance and qualities of a school leader. This form helps identify areas of strength and weakness in their leadership, providing valuable feedback for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your personal observations, experiences, and knowledge of the school leader.
2. For select-one questions, choose the best answer that best describes your assessment of the school leader's performance.
3. For select-multiple questions, select all the answers that apply to your assessment of the school leader's performance.
4. If a question is not applicable, please indicate "No" or "N/A" accordingly.
5. Double-check your answers to ensure you have provided accurate and honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Leadership Performance
* **School Leadership Performance** (`school_leadership_performance`, `select_one`, `No`):
This question asks if the school leader is performing well. Please choose "Yes" if you believe the school leader is performing well, and "No" otherwise.

### Leader Characteristics
* **Leader Characteristics** (`leader_characteristics`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the characteristics of the school leader, such as whether they are supportive, collaborative, or decisive. Please select all the characteristics that you believe apply to the school leader.

### Leader Behaviors
* **Leader Behaviors** (`leader_behaviors`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the behaviors of the school leader, such as whether they are approachable, transparent, or fair. Please select all the behaviors that you believe apply to the school leader.

### School Environment
* **School Environment** (`school_environment`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the school environment, such as whether it is safe, supportive, or inclusive. Please select all the characteristics that you believe apply to the school environment.

### Feedback Frequency
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `number`, `N/A`): 
This question asks how often you receive feedback from the school leader. Please enter a number to indicate the frequency of feedback you receive.

### Feedback Sources
* **Feedback Sources** (`feedback_sources`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the sources of feedback from the school leader. Please select all the sources that you receive feedback from, such as parents, teachers, students, or administrators.

### Overall Leadership Quality
* **Overall Leadership Quality** (`overall_leadership_quality`, `select_one`, `Good`): 
This question asks for your overall assessment of the school leader's quality. Please choose "Good" or "Poor" based on your assessment.

### Leader Support
* **Leader Support** (`leader_support`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the support provided by the school leader. Please select all the types of support that you believe the school leader provides, such as emotional, technical, or financial support.

### School Leadership Evaluation
* **School Leadership Evaluation** (`school_leadership_evaluation`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the evaluation process of the school leader. Please select all the methods that you believe are used to evaluate the school leader's performance.

### Feedback Channels
* **Feedback Channels** (`feedback_channels`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the channels used for feedback from the school leader. Please select all the channels that you believe are used, such as emails, meetings, or one-on-one conversations.

### Leader Communication
* **Leader Communication** (`leader_communication`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the communication style of the school leader. Please select all the characteristics that you believe apply to the school leader's communication style, such as clear, concise, or frequent.

### Leader Develops People
* **Leader Develops People** (`leader_develops_people`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the school leader's ability to develop their team members. Please select all the characteristics that you believe apply to the school leader's ability to develop their team members.

### Leader Leadership Style
* **Leader Leadership Style** (`leader_leadership_style`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the school leader's leadership style. Please select all the characteristics that you believe apply to the school leader's style, such as transformational, servant, or laissez-faire.

### Leader Leadership Quality
* **Leader Leadership Quality** (`leader_leadership_quality`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks for your assessment of the school leader's leadership quality. Please choose "Good" or "Poor" based on your assessment.

### School Leadership Development
* **School Leadership Development** (`school_leadership_development`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the development of the school leader. Please select all the areas that you believe are developed, such as vision, mission, or goals.

### Leader Leadership Skills
* **Leader Leadership Skills** (`leader_leadership_skills`, `select_multiple`): 
This question asks about the leadership skills of the school leader. Please select all the skills that you believe apply to the school leader, such as strategic, problem-solving, or communication skills.
