# Customer Support Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Support Audit Form is designed to help us better understand how our customer support process is performing and where we can improve. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions on each page to provide us with valuable feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page, answering the questions or selecting options that apply to your experience. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (Page 1)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_1, text, optional):
	+ This page is a general overview of your support experience. Feel free to share as much or as little as you'd like, and provide any comments or feedback you have.
* **Second Page (Page 2)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_2, time, required):
	+ Please provide the length of time you spent on your last support interaction. This will help us understand how long our support process is taking.
* **Third Page (Page 3)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_3, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your experience. This will help us understand the types of issues or challenges you faced during your last support interaction.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Fourth Page (Page 4)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_4, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one option to indicate whether or not you were able to resolve your issue.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Fifth Page (Page 5)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_5, date, required):
	+ Please provide the date of your last support interaction.
* **Sixth Page (Page 6)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_6, email, required):
	+ Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you about your issue.
* **Seventh Page (Page 7)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_7, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Eighth Page (Page 8)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_8, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one option to indicate whether or not you were satisfied with the support process.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Ninth Page (Page 9)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_9, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one option to indicate whether or not you would recommend our support process.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Tenth Page (Page 10)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_10, number, required):
	+ Please provide the number of issues you experienced.
* **Eleventh Page (Page 11)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_11, number, required):
	+ Please provide the number of issues you experienced.
* **Twelfth Page (Page 12)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_12, text, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Thirteenth Page (Page 13)** (customer_support_audit_form_page_13, email, optional):
	+ Please provide your email address if you would like us to follow up with you about your issue.
