# Operation Night Vision Nutrition Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operation Night Vision Nutrition Questionnaire is designed to collect information about an individual's health status, habits, and medical history to provide a personalized nutrition plan for optimal eye health.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  Ensure that all required fields (those marked as `required: true`) have been filled out.
3.  Select the appropriate options for each field based on your personal experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your purpose of visiting this health center?** (`introduction`, text, not required)
	+ This field is meant to be a brief description of why you're visiting the health center. Please provide your reason for your visit, such as for a check-up or a specific medical concern.
* **Height** (`height`, number, not required)
	+ This is where you'll enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, not required)
	+ This is where you'll enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Age** (`age`, number, not required)
	+ Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, not required)
	+ Select one of the following: Male, Female, Other.
* **Height-unit** (`height_unit`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select the unit of measurement for your height: feet, inches.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, not required)
	+ Describe your occupation or any relevant work-related information.
* **Diet** (`diet`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all that apply: vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, omnivore.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all that apply: gluten, dairy, nuts.
* **Medical conditions** (`medical conditions`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **Eating habits** (`eating habits`, text, not required)
	+ Describe your eating habits, including any specific diets or restrictions.
* **Smoking** (`smoking`, select_one, not required)
	+ Select one of the following: True, False.
* **Exercise** (`exercise`, select_multiple, not required)
	+ Select all that apply: True, False.
* **Blood pressure** (`blood_pressure`, number, not required)
	+ If applicable, enter your blood pressure reading.
* **Blood glucose** (`blood_glucose`, number, not required)
	+ If applicable, enter your blood glucose reading.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible, as this information will be used to provide personalized nutrition recommendations.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you're unsure about any field or question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
