# Army Mental Health Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Army Mental Health Evaluation Form is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of a soldier's mental health status, including demographic details, reasons for evaluation, primary concerns, symptoms, and overall functioning. This form helps clinicians and healthcare providers make informed decisions about treatment and intervention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and honest information about your mental health status.
2. Select the correct answers for each question, taking into account the definitions and descriptions provided.
3. Be sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Rank**: Select your current rank in the Army.
* **Service Number or ID**: Enter your service number or ID as assigned by the Army.
* **Unit or Command**: Specify the unit or command you are currently serving in.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date of the evaluation, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the clinician or healthcare provider conducting the evaluation.
* **Reason for Evaluation**: Select the reason for this mental health evaluation from the options provided.
	+ Routine screening: A routine evaluation as part of a regular check-up.
	+ Command referral: An evaluation initiated by your command or supervisor.
	+ Self-referred: You or someone on your behalf requested this evaluation.
	+ Medical evaluation: A medical evaluation for a specific medical reason.
	+ Crisis intervention: An urgent evaluation due to a crisis or emergency situation.
	+ Fitness for duty: An evaluation to determine fitness for duty.
	+ Other: Specify a reason not listed above.
* **Primary Mental Health Concern**: Select the primary mental health concern from the options provided.
	+ PTSD symptoms: Signs or symptoms of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD).
	+ Depression: Symptoms of depression, such as low mood or loss of interest in activities.
	+ Anxiety disorder: Symptoms of an anxiety disorder, such as panic attacks or excessive worry.
	+ Substance use: Use of substances, such as alcohol or illicit drugs.
	+ Adjustment disorder: Adjustment difficulties or emotional distress related to a specific event or situation.
	+ Sleep disorder: Difficulty sleeping or maintaining a normal sleep pattern.
	+ Relationship conflict: Conflicts or difficulties with relationships.
	+ Work stress: Stress related to work or job duties.
	+ Other: Specify a concern not listed above.
* **Duration of Symptoms**: Select the duration of your symptoms from the options provided.
	+ Less than 1 month: Symptoms have been present for less than a month.
	+ 1-3 months: Symptoms have been present for between 1-3 months.
	+ 3-6 months: Symptoms have been present for between 3-6 months.
	+ 6-12 months: Symptoms have been present for between 6-12 months.
	+ More than 1 year: Symptoms have been present for more than a year.
* **Current Mood Status**: Select your current mood status from the options provided.
	+ Stable: You are feeling relatively stable and calm.
	+ Slightly depressed: You are feeling slightly depressed or down.
	+ Moderately depressed: You are feeling moderately depressed or sad.
	+ Severely depressed: You are feeling severely depressed or hopeless.
	+ Elevated or manic: You are feeling overly excited or agitated.
* **Anxiety Level**: Select your anxiety level from the options provided.
	+ None: You are not experiencing anxiety.
	+ Mild: You are experiencing mild anxiety.
	+ Moderate: You are experiencing moderate anxiety.
	+ Severe: You are experiencing severe anxiety.
	+ Severe with panic: You are experiencing severe anxiety with panic attacks.
* **Sleep Quality and Patterns**: Select your sleep quality and patterns from the options provided.
	+ Normal: You are experiencing normal sleep quality and patterns.
	+ Slightly disturbed: You are experiencing slightly disturbed sleep.
	+ Moderately disturbed: You are experiencing moderately disturbed sleep.
	+ Severely disturbed: You are experiencing severely disturbed sleep.
	+ Insomnia: You are experiencing difficulty falling or staying asleep.
* **Cognitive Function**: Select your current cognitive function from the options provided.
	+ Normal: You are experiencing normal cognitive function.
	+ Slightly impaired: You are experiencing slightly impaired cognitive function.
	+ Moderately impaired: You are experiencing moderately impaired cognitive function.
	+ Severely impaired: You are experiencing severely impaired cognitive function.
* **Suicidal Ideation**: Select your current suicidal ideation status from the options provided.
	+ None: You are not experiencing suicidal ideation.
	+ Passive ideation: You are experiencing passive suicidal ideation.
	+ Active ideation: You are experiencing active suicidal ideation.
	+ Plan in place: You have a plan to harm yourself.
	+ Active intent with plan: You have a plan and intend to harm yourself.
* **Alcohol Use**: Select your alcohol use frequency from the options provided.
	+ None: You do not use alcohol.
	+ Occasional: You use alcohol occasionally.
	+ Regular: You use alcohol regularly.
	+ Frequent: You use alcohol frequently.
	+ Daily: You use alcohol daily.
* **Other Substance Use**: Select your other substance use or abuse status from the options provided.
	+ None: You do not use substances other than alcohol.
	+ Occasional use: You use substances occasionally.
	+ Regular use: You use substances regularly.
	+ Abuse pattern: You have an abuse pattern with substances.
	+ Dependency: You are experiencing substance dependency.
* **Current Occupational Functioning**: Select your current occupational functioning from the options provided.
	+ Normal: You are experiencing normal occupational functioning.
	+ Slightly impaired: You are experiencing slightly impaired occupational functioning.
	+ Moderately impaired: You are experiencing moderately impaired occupational functioning.
	+ Severely impaired: You are experiencing severely impaired occupational functioning.
	+ Unable to function: You are unable to function in your occupational role.
* **Social and Interpersonal Functioning**: Select your social and interpersonal functioning from the options provided.
	+ Good: You are experiencing good social and interpersonal functioning.
	+ Fair: You are experiencing fair social and interpersonal functioning.
	+ Poor: You are experiencing poor social and interpersonal functioning.
	+ Very poor: You are experiencing very poor social and interpersonal functioning.
* **Recommended Treatment or Intervention**: Select the recommended treatment or intervention from the options provided.
	+ Individual counseling: One-on-one counseling sessions.
	+ Group therapy: Group counseling sessions.
	+ Medication management: Medication management for mental health issues.
	+ Hospitalization: Inpatient treatment in a hospital setting.
	+ Community resources: Community resources, such as support groups or therapy sessions.
	+ Career counseling: Career counseling or coaching to improve job performance.
	+ No treatment needed: You do not require mental health treatment or intervention.
* **Fitness for Duty**: Select your fitness for duty status from the options provided.
	+ Fit for duty: You are fit for duty.
	+ Fit with restrictions: You are fit for duty with certain restrictions.
	+ Unfit for duty: You are unfit for duty.
	+ Pending evaluation: Your fitness for duty status is pending evaluation.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any question or section, ask your clinician or healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed in a confidential and respectful environment.
* If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact a crisis hotline or seek immediate assistance from a healthcare provider.
