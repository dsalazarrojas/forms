# Healthcare Infrastructure Assessment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect evaluations from healthcare providers on their facilities' quality and safety. It is used to help in evaluating the strengths and weaknesses of the healthcare infrastructure.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form header with a brief description of your facility.
- Select the provider information and facility information.
- Evaluate the quality of care, staff, and overall experience.
- Provide a comment on the evaluation.
- Choose the patient flow, overall rating, and submission date and time.
- Fill out the reviewer's information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Information** (`provider_info`): Enter the provider's name and contact information.
* **Facility Information** (`facility_info`): Enter the facility's name and address.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`): Rate the quality of care on a scale from Excellent to Bad.
* **Patient Care** (`patient_care`): Select multiple options for the quality of patient care, such as Quality of Care, Communication, Responsiveness to Needs, and Staff Competence.
* **Staff Information** (`staff_info`): Determine if staff members are competent and responsive.
* **Facility Safety** (`facility_safety`): Rate the facility's safety.
* **Patient Flow** (`patient_flow`): Rate the patient flow from Good to Bad.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`): Enter a numeric score for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comment** (`evaluation_comment`): Provide a comment on the evaluation.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`): Rate the overall experience from Excellent to Bad.
* **Evaluation Summary** (`evaluation_summary`): Summarize the evaluation.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`): Provide any recommendations for improvement.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`): Add any additional comments.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Record the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`): Record the time of submission.
* **Submission By** (`submission_by`): Record the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`): Record the reviewer's name.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Record the date of review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`): Record the time of review.
* **Review By** (`review_by`): Record the reviewer's name.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Record the reviewer's name.
* **Review Version** (`review_version`): Record the review version number.
