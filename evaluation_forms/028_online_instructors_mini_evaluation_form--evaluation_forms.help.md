# Online Instructors Mini Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Instructors Mini Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on the performance of online course instructors. This feedback helps to identify strengths and weaknesses of instructors and aids in the improvement of their teaching methods and communication skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name (if you're an instructor) or your contact information (if you're evaluating an instructor).
2. Enter the ID number of the course that you're evaluating.
3. Rate the instructor's performance on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4. Provide any additional comments about the instructor's communication and engagement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Instructors Name**: Enter the name of the instructor you're evaluating. If you're the instructor yourself, enter your own name.
* **Course Id**: Enter the ID number of the course associated with the instructor.
* **Rating**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 to express your opinion of the instructor's performance.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the instructor's communication and engagement. Be specific about what worked well and what could be improved.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is optional and not relevant for most users. If you're required to enter a date, please use the format mm/dd/yyyy. 

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Keep your comments brief and to the point.
* If you're an instructor, take your feedback seriously and use it to improve your teaching skills.
* If you're the evaluator, your feedback is valuable to helping instructors grow and improve.
