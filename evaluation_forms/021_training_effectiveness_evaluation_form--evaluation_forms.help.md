# Training Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness of a training session that you have attended. It is meant to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the content and session facilitators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have attended a training session and have some level of experience with the content being presented.
2. Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
3. Fill out the form completely and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_id** (`participant_id`, `number`, optional): Enter your participant ID, which is likely to be a unique identifier assigned to you during the registration process.
* **training_session_id** (`training_session_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the session ID, which is likely to be a unique code assigned to the training session you attended.
* **session_date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **session_time** (`session_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the training session you attended.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the participant of the training session.
* **session_title** (`session_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the training session you attended.
* **overall_impact** (`overall_impact`, `number`, optional): Rate the overall impact of the session on your knowledge, skills, or attitude. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **content_effectiveness** (`content_effectiveness`, `number`, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the session content in relation to your learning goals. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **session_materials** (`session_materials`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the session materials provided.
* **session_organizer** (`session_organizer`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the person who organized the training session.
* **session_facilitator** (`session_facilitator`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the person(s) who facilitated the training session.
* **session_location** (`session_location`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the location where the training session was held.
* **session_type** (`session_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of the training session (Virtual, Physical, or Hybrid).
* **session_length** (`session_length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the training session in minutes.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the session facilitator. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **rating_2** (`rating_2`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the session content. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **overall_rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, optional): Enter the overall rating of the session, between 1 and 5.
* **recommendation** (`recommendation`, `text`, optional): Enter your recommendation for the session.
* **recommendation_2** (`recommendation_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional recommendations or suggestions.
* **session_satisfaction** (`session_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training program. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **training_value** (`training_value`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the value you gained from the session. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **overall_value** (`overall_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the overall value you gained from the training session.
* **participant_feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional feedback or suggestions.

Note: Some fields are optional, but filling out all fields will give the most accurate and comprehensive feedback.
