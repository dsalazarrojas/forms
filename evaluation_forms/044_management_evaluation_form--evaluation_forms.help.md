The Management Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and information from managers about their team members' performance and management style. This form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness in management, and can be used as a tool for professional development and growth.

Before starting, please ensure that you are filling out the correct form. This form has multiple pages, but all the pages are part of the same form, so please complete all the pages carefully.

## Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## How To Complete This Form

The form consists of multiple pages. Each page has one or more form fields. Please complete each page carefully and thoroughly. You can use the hints provided for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Management Evaluation Form 1** (text, not required): This field is a free text field for you to provide any comments or feedback about the management style of the team member.
* **2. Management Evaluation Form 2** (select multiple, not required): Please select all the answers that apply to your evaluation. This field is meant for checking specific behaviors or practices.
	+ Select all the answers that you think are applicable to your management style.
* **3. Management Evaluation Form 3** (number, not required): This field is meant for rating the team member's management performance. Please provide a number between 0 and 100 to rate the performance.
* **4. Management Evaluation Form 4** (time, not required): This field is not currently in use. Please ignore it for now.
* **5. Management Evaluation Form 5** (select one, not required): Please select one of the two options: 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is meant for checking a specific behavior or practice.
* **6. Management Evaluation Form 6** (note, not required): This field is a free text field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **7. Management Evaluation Form 7** (number, not required): This field is not currently in use. Please ignore it for now.
* **8. Management Evaluation Form 8** (select multiple, not required): Please select all the answers that apply to your evaluation. This field is meant for checking specific behaviors or practices.
	+ Select all the answers that you think are applicable to your management style.
* **9. Management Evaluation Form 9** (text, not required): This field is a free text field for you to provide any comments or feedback about the team member.
