# SAS Observation And Coaching Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SAS Observation And Coaching Form is a tool used to record and analyze observations and coaching interactions between management and sales team members. This form is designed to gather information about the performance of the sales team member, provide feedback, and set goals and frequencies for coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Observation field with a brief description of the observation or coaching interaction.
2. Select the type of observation or coaching interaction from the options provided in the Observation Type field.
3. Enter a numerical value for the Sales Performance field, indicating the level of performance observed or achieved.
4. If applicable, select the Coaching Goal from the options provided, or provide an alternative goal in the Coaching Goal (Other) field.
5. Enter the Progress Tracking value to monitor progress towards the coaching goals.
6. Enter the Coaching Frequency value to indicate the frequency of coaching sessions.
7. Provide the date for the next Coaching Session.
8. Finally, enter any additional Coaching Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observation (1)**: A brief description of the observation or coaching interaction.
* **Coaching (2)**: A brief description of the coaching interaction.
* **Feedback (3)**: Not applicable as this field is not required.
* **Observation Type (4)**: Select the type of observation or coaching interaction (Observation or Coaching).
* **Sales Performance (5)**: A numerical value indicating the level of sales performance observed or achieved (e.g., 1-10).
* **Coaching Goal (6)**: Select a goal for coaching from the options provided, or provide an alternative goal in the Coaching Goal (Other) field.
* **Coaching Goal (Other) (7)**: If the Coaching Goal selected is not one of the provided options, enter an alternative goal.
* **Progress Tracking (8)**: A numerical value indicating progress towards the coaching goals.
* **Coaching Frequency (9)**: A numerical value indicating the frequency of coaching sessions.
* **Next Coaching Session (10)**: Date for the next coaching session.
* **Coaching Notes (11)**: Additional comments or notes from the coaching interaction.
