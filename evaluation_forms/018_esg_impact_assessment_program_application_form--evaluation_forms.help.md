# ESG Impact Assessment Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in the ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) Impact Assessment Program. The goal is to gather relevant details about applicants and their projects or initiatives related to the ESG program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you've answered all required fields.
2. Fill in each field accurately and completely.
3. Attach any necessary documents or supporting materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): This section is an opportunity to provide a brief introduction or overview of your project or initiative. Please describe your application in a clear and concise manner.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, optional): Here, you can provide additional details about the ESG program you are applying for. This may include information about your goals, objectives, or expected outcomes.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): In this section, please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us reach out to you with any follow-up questions or requests.
* **Application Attachments** (`application_attachments`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documents, such as resumes, project proposals, or certificates, please attach them here. This will help us review your application more efficiently.

## Tips
* Ensure that your attachments are in a suitable format and are not overly large.
* Please proofread your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
