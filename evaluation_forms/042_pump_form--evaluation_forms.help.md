# Pump Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pump Form is a general-purpose form used for collecting various types of information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. For select-one and select_multiple fields, choose the relevant options from the list.
4. Use the date and time fields to enter dates and times as required.
5. For note fields, enter a brief description or note as necessary.
6. For email fields, enter a valid email address.
7. Review your entire form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form 1** (`pump_form_1`, text, optional): Enter any relevant text information.
* **Form 2** (`pump_form_2`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value.
* **Form 3** (`pump_form_3`, number, optional): Enter another numerical value.
* **Form 4** (`pump_form_4`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the available options.
* **Form 5** (`pump_form_5`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Form 6** (`pump_form_6`, date, optional): Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Form 7** (`pump_form_7`, time, optional): Enter a time in the format 12:00 AM/12:00 PM.
* **Form 8** (`pump_form_8`, note, optional): Enter a brief note or description.
* **Form 9** (`pump_form_9`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Form 10** (`pump_form_10`, text, optional): Enter additional text information.
* **Form 11** (`pump_form_11`, text, optional): Enter more text information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* If a field is marked as required, ensure you fill it in before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Double-check your entire form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* Use the available options in select-one and select_multiple fields to make your selection.
