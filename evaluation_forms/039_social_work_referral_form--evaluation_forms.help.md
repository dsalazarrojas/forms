# social_work_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The social work referral form is designed to collect and manage information for social work referrals in educational settings. It helps gather essential details about students, referrals, and their progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* Student's name and ID
	* Referral reason
	* Referral date
	* Referral time (optional)
	* Student's grade level (optional)
	* Referral status (Active, Inactive, or Archived)
	* Whether a tool has been assigned to the student (Yes or No)
	* Whether a teacher is assigned to the student (Yes or No)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student being referred.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher who is being referred.
* **Teacher ID**: Enter the ID of the teacher who is being referred. (Optional)
* **Referral Reason**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the referral.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral. Format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Referral Time**: Enter the time of the referral if applicable. (Optional)
* **Student Grade Level**: Enter the student's current grade level. (Optional)
* **Referral Status**: Select the status of the referral: Active, Inactive, or Archived.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select whether a tool has been assigned to the student: Yes or No.
* **Assigned Teacher**: Select whether a teacher has been assigned to the student: Yes or No.
