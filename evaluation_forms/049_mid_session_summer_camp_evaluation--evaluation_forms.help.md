# Mid Session Summer Camp Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mid Session Summer Camp Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback from campers and their families about the quality of our summer camp program. This evaluation is crucial in helping us make data-driven decisions to improve our camp's offerings and services. Your feedback will help us identify areas of strength and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mid Session Summer Camp Evaluation form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page with the corresponding question you want to answer.
2. Read the question carefully and provide an honest assessment.
3. Choose your rating or provide feedback accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Attendant Feedback (1)** (`summer_camp_attendant_feedback`, note, optional)
  This field is used to collect any feedback you'd like to share about your experience with our camp attendants. Please feel free to express any thoughts or suggestions you have.

* **Program Rating (2)** (`program_rating`, number, optional)
  This is a 1-5 rating system to evaluate the quality of our summer camp program. Please rate the program on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).

* **Instructor Rating (3)** (`instructor_rating`, number, optional)
  This field allows you to rate your experience with our instructors. Please rate them on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).

* **Staff Rating (4)** (`staff_rating`, number, optional)
  Use this field to rate our camp staff, including counselors, directors, and other staff members. Please rate them on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).

* **Overall Rating (5)** (`overall_rating`, number, optional)
  This field is a final evaluation of your overall experience at our summer camp. Please rate us on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).

## Tips
- Please answer questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Ratings are based on your personal experience and opinions.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to share them in the field provided.

Note: This form will only ask you to fill out the pages relevant to your experience at our summer camp.
