# 5K Run Registration Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the 5K run event. It collects information necessary for race management and communication with participants, including contact details, emergency contacts, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant full name** (Registrant full name, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on race materials.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter your contact email address where you will receive confirmation and updates about the event.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone, phone, required): Enter your contact phone number, preferred mobile for race day updates.
* **Age on race day** (Age on race day, integer, required): Enter your age on the day of the event.
* **Shirt size selection** (Shirt size selection, select_one, required): Choose the size of the shirt you wish to wear during the event.
* **Team name (if registering as part of a team)** (Team name (if registering as part of a team), text, optional): If you are registering with a team, enter the team's name here. If not, leave blank.
* **Preferred bib pickup date** (Preferred bib pickup date, date, optional): Choose from the available bib pickup dates.
* **Payment method** (Payment method, select_one, required): Select the method used to pay for registration (Credit card, Bank transfer, Cash at pickup, Sponsorship).
* **Emergency contact and phone** (Emergency contact and phone, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number, using the format "Name — Phone".
* **Photo/video consent** (I consent to photos/videos taken during the event, select_one, optional): Choose to grant or deny permission for photos and videos taken during the event.
* **Important medical information** (Important medical information, text, optional): Share any important medical information or conditions that the organizers should be aware of.
* **Additional requests or comments** (Additional requests or comments, text, optional): Enter any additional requests or comments, such as accessibility needs or dietary preferences for post-race.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
