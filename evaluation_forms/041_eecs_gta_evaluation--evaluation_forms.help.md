# eecs-gta-evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EECS-GTA Evaluation Form is a tool for evaluating the performance of GTAs (Graduate Teaching Assistants) in the Electrical Engineering and Computer Science department. This form is used to assess the quality of teaching and support provided by GTAs, allowing for feedback and improvement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully.
2. For each section, provide detailed and honest feedback about your experience with the GTA.
3. Use the available fields to provide specific examples and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **gtaperformance**: 
* **gtaperformance** (Text): This field is for your overall evaluation of the GTA's performance. Please provide a brief description of your experience.

### 2. **gtaperformance2**: 
* **gtaperformance2** (Text): This field is for any additional comments or observations about the GTA's performance.

### 3. **gtaevaluation**: 
* **gtaevaluation** (Text): This field is for providing feedback on the GTA's evaluation criteria. Be specific about what you think was good or not-so-good about their teaching.

### 4. **gtaevaluation2**: 
* **gtaevaluation2** (Text): This field is similar to the previous one, for further evaluation comments.

### 5. **gtaevaluation3**: 
* **gtaevaluation3** (Text): This field provides an additional space for comments on the GTA's evaluation.

### 6. **gtafeedback**: 
* **gtafeedback** (Text): Use this field to provide general feedback on your experience with the GTA.

### 7. **rehire**: 
* **rehire** (Select One): This field is for your opinion on whether you would rehire the GTA. Please select from the available options:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree

### 8. **gtafeedback2**: 
* **gtafeedback2** (Text): This field is for additional comments on your experience with the GTA.

### 9. **evaluationcriteria**: 
* **evaluationcriteria** (Text): Provide specific feedback on what criteria you think is important for the GTA to improve on.

### 10. **gtap10**: 
* **gtap10** (Time): This field is not currently in use. Please disregard.

### 11. **gtaevaluation1**: 
* **gtaevaluation1** (Text): This field is for comments on your evaluation of the GTA's teaching.

### 12. **eecs-gta-evaluation-12**: 
* **eecs-gta-evaluation-12** (Select Multiple): Select any of the available options that apply to your experience:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree

### 13. **gtaevaluation2**: 
* **gtaevaluation2** (Text): This field is similar to previous fields, for any final comments.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your comments.
* Provide specific examples to illustrate your points.
* Keep your comments professional and respectful.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields. Some fields may have unclear labels or missing hints, which are explained best-effort in the corresponding sections.
