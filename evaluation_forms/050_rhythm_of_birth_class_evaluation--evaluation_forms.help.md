# Rhythm Of Birth Class Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rhythm of Birth Class Evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience in the class. This form helps instructors and administrators understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing them to improve the class content and structure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions relevant to your experience in the class.
2. Make sure to provide detailed and thoughtful responses to each question.
3. Indicate the required fields by answering "Yes" or providing a relevant answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Evaluation** (`class_evaluation`, text, optional): Please provide a brief evaluation of the class, including your overall impression and any comments or suggestions.
* **Class Learned** (`class_learnt`, text, required): Describe what you have learned from the class. Be specific about the skills or knowledge you gained.
* **Class Mentor Evaluation** (`class_mentor_evaluation`, text, optional): Provide feedback about the class mentor or instructor. This will help us understand their effectiveness in guiding you through the class.
* **Class Learned Total** (`class_learnt_total`, number, required): Report the total number of new skills or knowledge you gained from the class.
* **Class Mission Achievements** (`class_mission_achievements`, select_one, required): Check off the missions you accomplished during the class:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Class Mission Achievements Notes** (`class_mission_achievements_notes`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your mission achievements, if any.
* **Class Evaluated** (`class_evaluated`, select_multiple, required): Answer the following questions with the corresponding number:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
* **Class Evaluation Date** (`class_evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the evaluation.
* **Class Evaluation Time** (`class_evaluation_time`, time, optional): Report the time you spent on the class evaluation.
* **Class Evaluation Notes** (`class_evaluation_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the class.
* **Class Evaluation Learnt** (`class_evaluation_learnt`, text, optional): Describe what you have learned from the class evaluation process.
* **Class Evaluated Learnt** (`class_evaluated_learnt`, select_one, required): Answer the following question:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Class Evaluation Time Of Day** (`class_evaluation_time_of_day`, time, optional): Report the time of day you spent on the class evaluation.
* **Class Evaluation Time Of Day Of Week** (`class_evaluation_time_of_day_of_week`, time, optional): Report the time of day and day of week you spent on the class evaluation.
* **Class Evaluation Time Of Day Of Week Day** (`class_evaluation_time_of_day_of_week_day`, text, optional): Provide additional details about the time of day and day of week you spent on the class evaluation.
* **Class Evaluation Mission Achievements Date** (`class_evaluation_mission_achievements_date`, date, optional): Report the date you accomplished the mission achievements.
* **Class Evaluation Mission Achievements Time** (`class_evaluation_mission_achievements_time`, time, optional): Report the time you spent on mission achievements.
* **Class Evaluation Mission Achievements Notes** (`class_evaluation_mission_achievements_notes`, text, optional): Provide additional details about your mission achievements.
* **Class Evaluation Mission Achievements Total** (`class_evaluation_mission_achievements_total`, number, optional): Report the total number of mission achievements.
* **Class Mission Achievements** (`class_mission_achievements`, select_one, required): Answer the following question:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Class Mission Achievements Total Notes** (`class_mission_achievements_total_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the mission achievements.
