# cybersecurity_awareness_training_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cybersecurity awareness training feedback evaluation form is designed to collect user feedback on the effectiveness of the cybersecurity awareness training program. This form will help us understand how users perceive the training and provide valuable insights to improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter a title that summarizes your feedback or comments about the training.
2. Select the category of the form (evaluation or feedback).
3. Provide a description of your experience or comments about the training.
4. Enter your rating (1-5) to indicate your level of agreement or satisfaction with the training.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Leave any additional comments or feedback.
8. Enter the date and time of submission.
9. You can also provide information about the tool used for the form (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cybersecurity Awareness Training Feedback Evaluation Form**: This is the main title of the form.
* **Category**: Choose between "Evaluation Forms" or "Feedback Forms" to indicate the type of form you're filling out.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your experience or comments about the training.
* **User**: Enter your name or username (not actually a field in the form, but for explanation purposes).
* **Rating**: Rate your experience (1-5) to indicate your level of satisfaction or agreement.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion (if needed).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the exact time of your submission.
* **Form Submission ID**: This is an internal field that is automatically generated by the system.
* **Form ID**: This is an internal field that is automatically generated by the system.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool used for the form (if applicable).
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form (not actually a field in the form, but for explanation purposes).
* **Form Created At**: This is the date the form was created.
* **Form Updated At**: This is the date the form was last updated.
* **Form Completed At**: This is the date the form was completed.
* **Form Submission Count**: This is the total number of times the form has been submitted.

Please make sure to complete all the required fields and take a few minutes to provide thoughtful feedback to help us improve the cybersecurity awareness training program.
