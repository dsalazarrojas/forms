**Resident Patient Evaluation Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Resident Patient Evaluation Form is designed to gather critical information about a patient's medical history, allergies, test results, and other essential details. This form will help medical professionals evaluate the patient's overall condition and provide a comprehensive evaluation for better treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each patient's information, ensuring that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that the information is up-to-date and relevant.
3. Use the options provided for each field to help guide your input.
