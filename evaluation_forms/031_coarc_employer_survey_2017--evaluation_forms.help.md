# Coarc Employer Survey 2017 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coarc Employer Survey 2017 is a form designed to gather feedback from employers about their respiratory therapy graduates. This survey aims to understand their opinions and experiences with their graduates, providing valuable insights for the Coarc organization to improve its programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Respondent Information**: Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and your organization or facility.
2. Enter the name of the graduate you are evaluating.
3. Choose the duration of their employment.
4. Select their current employment status.
5. Evaluate their technical skills, clinical judgment, safety awareness, patient care quality, and overall preparedness for the role.
6. Rate their communication skills, teamwork, and commitment to professional development.
7. Provide any additional comments or observations about the graduate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, **required**): This is your full name, as the supervisor of the graduate.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Please provide your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your organization or facility name.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose one of the options:
	+ Hospital
	+ Clinic
	+ Rehabilitation Center
	+ Home Care
	+ Other
* **Graduate Name** (`graduate_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the graduate you are evaluating.
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose one of the options:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months - 1 year
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose one of the options:
	+ Actively employed
	+ Recently terminated
	+ On leave
	+ Other
* **Technical Skills and Knowledge** (`technical_skills`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their technical skills and knowledge:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Clinical Judgment** (`clinical_judgment`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their clinical judgment:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Safety Awareness and Compliance** (`safety_awareness`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their safety awareness and compliance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Patient Care Quality** (`patient_care`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their patient care quality:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Communication Skills** (`communication`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their communication skills:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Teamwork and Collaboration** (`teamwork`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their ability to work with others:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Commitment to Professional Development** (`professional_development`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their commitment to professional development:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Inadequate
* **Overall Preparedness for the Role** (`overall_preparedness`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate their overall preparedness for the role:
	+ Excellent

	+ Inadequate
* **Additional Comments or Observations** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or observations about the graduate.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your ratings and comments.
* Please provide specific examples to support your answers.
* If you have any additional feedback or concerns, use the "Additional Comments or Observations" field to provide more information.
