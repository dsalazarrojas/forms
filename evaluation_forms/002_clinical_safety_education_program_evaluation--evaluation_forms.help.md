# Clinical Safety Education Program Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the clinical safety education program you attended. Your input will help us improve future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your Participant Name, Role in Healthcare, and Program Date Attended.
2. Evaluate the program content by answering the questions in the "Program Content Evaluation" section.
3. Rate the instructor's performance in the "Instructor Evaluation" section.
4. Provide feedback on the program delivery in the "Program Delivery" section.
5. Share any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.

* **Program Content Evaluation** (`section_content`, `note`, `required`): Please rate the quality of the program content.

	+ `The material was relevant to my work`: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
	+ `The program covered the topic in appropriate depth`: Too basic, Just right, Too advanced, or Mixed depth.
* **Instructor Evaluation** (`section_instructor`, `note`, `required`): Please rate the instructor's performance.
