# Recording Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of a recorded audio or video file. It helps to evaluate the overall quality of the recording, including its sound, clarity, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant quality categories from the dropdown menus, rating the sound, clarity, background noise, music quality, voice quality, recording equipment, and file format.
2. If you have additional comments, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" text field.
3. If you have a file associated with the recording, you can upload it or provide the file URL.
4. Fill in the remaining fields with relevant information about the recording, such as recording date, time, location, and equipment used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audio or Video Quality** (`recording_quality_assessment_1`): Select the overall quality of the recording from the dropdown menu.
* **Sound Quality** (`sound_quality`): Rate the sound quality of the recording, considering factors such as volume, frequency, and clarity.
* **Clarity** (`clarity`): Evaluate the clarity of the recording, taking into account the presence of background noise, hiss, or other distortions.
* **Background Noise** (`background_noise`): Assess the background noise level during the recording.
* **Music Quality** (`music_quality`): Rate the music quality, including the performance, tone, and overall musicality.
* **Voice Quality** (`voice_quality`): Evaluate the voice quality, considering factors such as tone, pitch, and articulation.
* **Recording Equipment** (`recording_equipment`): Describe the recording equipment used, such as microphones, cameras, or other devices.
* **Recording Conditions** (`recording_conditions`): Rate the recording conditions, taking into account factors such as lighting, acoustics, and environmental factors.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the recording.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`): Upload a file associated with the recording, if available.
* **File URL** (`file_url`): Provide the URL of the file associated with the recording, if not uploading.
* **File Size** (`file_size`): Enter the size of the file in megabytes.
* **File Duration** (`file_duration`): Enter the duration of the recording.
* **File Format** (`file_format`): Select the format of the file from the dropdown menu.
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`): Enter the date of the recording.
* **Recording Time** (`recording_time`): Enter the time of the recording.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`): Describe the location where the recording was made.
* **Recording Device** (`recording_device`): Describe the device used to make the recording.
* **Recording Environment** (`recording_environment`): Describe the environment where the recording was made.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the recording.
* **Recording Conditions 2** (`recording_conditions_2`): Rate the recording conditions, taking into account factors such as lighting, acoustics, and environmental factors.
* **Recording Equipment 2** (`recording_equipment_2`): Describe the recording equipment used, such as microphones, cameras, or other devices.
* **Recording Location 2** (`recording_location_2`): Describe the location where the recording was made.
* **Recording Device 2** (`recording_device_2`): Describe the device used to make the recording.
* **Recording Environment 2** (`recording_environment_2`): Describe the environment where the recording was made.

Note: This form is designed to be used for assessing the quality of a recorded audio or video file. Please answer the questions truthfully and provide any additional comments or observations that may be helpful in evaluating the recording's quality.
