# AI Maturity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Maturity Assessment Form is designed to help assess the current state of AI capabilities and practices within an organization. It is intended to be filled out by an assessor, who will evaluate the current state of AI capabilities and practices within a specific business unit or department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Assessor name, Business unit or department, and all the maturity rating fields (4-9).
2. Rate each of the maturity fields (4-9) according to the level of implementation and effectiveness in your unit or department.
3. Provide a short summary and improvement priorities in the Summary of maturity assessment field (10) if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessor name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing this assessment.
* **Business unit or department** (3, text, required): Identify the business unit or department being assessed.
* **Strategy and governance maturity** (4, number, required): Rate the level of implementation and effectiveness of AI strategy and governance within your unit or department, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Data and infrastructure maturity** (5, number, required): Rate the level of implementation and effectiveness of AI data and infrastructure within your unit or department, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Capabilities and skills maturity** (6, number, required): Rate the level of implementation and effectiveness of AI capabilities and skills within your unit or department, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Processes and practices maturity** (7, number, required): Rate the level of implementation and effectiveness of AI processes and practices within your unit or department, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Risk and compliance controls maturity** (8, number, required): Rate the level of implementation and effectiveness of AI risk and compliance controls within your unit or department, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **AI technology and tools maturity** (9, number, required): Rate the level of implementation and effectiveness of AI technology and tools within your unit or department, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Summary of maturity assessment** (10, text, optional): Provide a short summary and improvement priorities for your unit or department.
