# Hospital Patient Discharge Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the hospital's patient discharge process. It aims to collect feedback, ratings, or other information to improve the process and ensure it meets the patient's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Start by reviewing the two pages**: The form has two pages: "Patient Discharge Process" and "Discharge Time".
- **Complete the "Patient Discharge Process" page**: This numerical field requires you to rate the patient's discharge process. Please select a number from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest) to indicate your assessment of the discharge process.
- **Complete the "Discharge Time" page**: This text field is for providing additional comments or notes about the time of discharge. Please type in any relevant details, such as any difficulties or concerns you experienced during the discharge process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Discharge Process (1):** A numerical field where you rate the patient's discharge process, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest). Please provide a score that reflects your assessment of the process's efficiency, effectiveness, and overall quality.
* **Discharge Time:** A text field for providing additional comments or notes about the time of discharge. Please type in any relevant details about your experience during the discharge process.
* **Nurse Name:** Not applicable for this form.
