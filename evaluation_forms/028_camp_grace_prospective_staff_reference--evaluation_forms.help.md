# Camp Grace Prospective Staff Reference - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect references from individuals who can vouch for prospective staff members of Camp Grace. It gathers information about the candidate's background, strengths, and areas for growth, which will help the organization make informed decisions about their suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the applicant you are providing a reference for in the **Name of Applicant** field.
2. Provide your full name and occupation in the **Your Full Name (Reference)** and **Your Occupation - Position** fields, respectively.
3. Rate the applicant's integrity and honesty, work ethic, ability to work in a team, emotional stability, and maturity using the provided scales.
4. Share your reservations, if any, about the applicant working with children in the **Suitability for Working with Children** field.
5. Describe the applicant's primary strengths and potential areas for growth.
6. Confirm whether you would hire or rehire the applicant, and if you would be available for further information.
7. Provide your contact phone number (optional).
8. Sign the form electronically with your full name, confirming the accuracy and truthfulness of the information provided.
9. Submit the date of reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Applicant** (`candidate_name_ref`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are providing a reference for.
* **Your Full Name (Reference)** (`reference_name_full`, text, required): Provide your full name as a reference.
* **Your Occupation - Position** (`reference_occupation`, text, required): Enter your occupation and position.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (`reference_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the applicant (e.g., former supervisor, teacher, or pastor).
* **How long have you known the applicant?** (`length_of_acquaintance`, text, required): Describe how long you have known the applicant.
* **Integrity and Honesty** (`rating_integrity_honesty`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's integrity and honesty using the provided scales.
* **Work Ethic and Reliability** (`rating_work_ethic`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's work ethic and reliability using the provided scales.
* **Ability to Work in a Team** (`rating_teamwork`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's ability to work in a team using the provided scales.
* **Emotional Stability and Maturity** (`rating_emotional_stability`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's emotional stability and maturity using the provided scales.
* **Suitability for Working with Children** (`working_with_children_suitability`, select_one, required): Share your reservations about the applicant working with children.
* **Details of Reservations (If any)** (`reservation_details_ref`, text, required): Describe any reservations you have about the applicant.
* **Primary Strengths of the Applicant** (`primary_strengths_applicant`, text, required): Share what makes the applicant a good fit for Camp Grace.
* **Potential Areas for Growth** (`areas_of_growth_applicant`, text, optional): Describe challenges the applicant might face.
* **Would you hire or rehire this person?** (`would_hire_rehire`, select_one, required): Confirm your willingness to hire or rehire the applicant.
* **May we contact you for further information?** (`reference_contact_followup`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you would be available for further information.
* **Your Contact Phone Number** (`reference_phone_num`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number (optional).
* **Electronic Signature (Full Name)** (`reference_signature_digital`, text, required): Sign the form electronically with your full name, confirming the accuracy and truthfulness of the information provided.
* **Date of Reference** (`reference_date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date of reference.
