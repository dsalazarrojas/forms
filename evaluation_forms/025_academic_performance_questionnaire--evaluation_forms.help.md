# Academic Performance Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Performance Questionnaire is a form used to evaluate a student's academic performance in a course. It helps instructors and teaching assistants provide a detailed assessment of the student's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by ensuring you have the necessary information to complete the form.
2. Review the form sections carefully and complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Be as specific and thorough as possible when providing feedback and suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, required): Enter the full name of the student being evaluated.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, required): Enter the official student identification number.
* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, required): Enter the name of the course being evaluated.
* **Instructor Name** (`Instructor Name`, required): Enter the name of the course instructor.
* **Academic Term** (`Academic Term`, required): Enter the semester, quarter, or term of the course.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`Overall Performance Rating`, required): Rate the student's overall academic performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Attendance Regularity Rating** (`Attendance Regularity Rating`, required): Rate the student's attendance and punctuality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Assignment Completion Rating** (`Assignment Completion Rating`, required): Rate the student's completion of assignments on a scale of 1-5.
* **Quality of Work Rating** (`Quality of Work Rating`, required): Rate the quality of the student's work on a scale of 1-5.
* **Participation and Engagement Rating** (`Participation and Engagement Rating`, required): Rate the student's classroom participation and engagement on a scale of 1-5.
* **Critical Thinking Rating** (`Critical Thinking Rating`, required): Rate the student's critical thinking abilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Collaboration Skills Rating** (`Collaboration Skills Rating`, required): Rate the student's ability to work with others on a scale of 1-5.
* **Improvement Over Time Rating** (`Improvement Over Time Rating`, required): Rate the student's improvement throughout the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **Key Strengths Identified** (`Key Strengths Identified`, optional): What are the student's primary academic strengths?
* **Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for Improvement`, optional): What areas should the student focus on improving?
* **Recommendations for Future** (`Recommendations for Future`, optional): What recommendations do you have for the student's continued academic success?
