# employee_benefits_program_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and data on the current benefits provided by the company to its employees. It is an evaluation form to help identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions on what changes to make to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information. If a field has a label of "Yes" or "No" as an option, select the correct answer. If a field is a text box, provide a brief answer or description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Benefits Program** (`page 1`, `text`, optional): This section is a general question asking about the benefits program. Please provide any comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Health Insurance** (`page 1`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks whether you have access to health insurance through your employer. Select "Yes" if you do, "No" if you don't.
* **Dental Care** (`page 1`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks whether you have access to dental care through your employer. You can select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Vision Benefits** (`page 1`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is similar to dental care, asking whether you have access to vision benefits.
* **Medical Benefits** (`page 1`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks whether you have access to medical benefits through your employer. Select "Yes" if you do, "No" if you don't.
* **Flexible Work Schedules** (`page 2`, `text`, optional): This question is about flexible work schedules, such as telecommuting or flexible hours. Please provide any feedback or comments you have about this benefit.
* **Telecommuting Options** (`page 2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is similar to flexible work schedules, asking about specific telecommuting options you may or may not have access to.
* **Employee Assistance Program** (`page 2`, `text`, optional): This question asks for feedback or comments about the employee assistance program, if it exists.
* **Benefits Education** (`page 2`, `text`, optional): This question is for feedback or comments about education provided on benefits, if any.
* **Feedback** (`page 3`, `text`, optional): This is the final question, where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the benefits program.
