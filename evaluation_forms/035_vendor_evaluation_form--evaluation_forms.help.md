# Vendor Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback and ratings from users about their interactions with a vendor. This information helps us understand the vendor's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the name of the vendor you are evaluating.
2. Rate the vendor's performance (optional).
3. Provide your overall satisfaction with the vendor (optional).
4. Rate the vendor's product quality (optional).
5. Rate the vendor's price satisfaction (optional).
6. Rate the vendor's communication (optional).
7. Rate the vendor's customer service (optional).
8. Rate the vendor's reliability (optional).
9. Provide your recommendation for the vendor (optional).
10. Choose how you contacted the vendor (optional).
11. Select all methods you used to contact the vendor (optional).
12. Enter the date of the evaluation (optional).
13. Enter the time of the evaluation (optional).
14. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience with the vendor (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor you are evaluating.
* **Rating**: Rate the vendor's performance on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Provide your overall satisfaction with the vendor on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Product Quality**: Rate the vendor's product quality on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Price Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the vendor's price on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Communication**: Rate the vendor's communication on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Customer Service**: Rate the vendor's customer service on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Vendor Reliability**: Rate the vendor's reliability on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Vendor Recommendation**: Provide your recommendation for the vendor (optional).
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you contacted the vendor (optional).
* **Contact Method 2**: Select all methods you used to contact the vendor (optional).
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation (optional).
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation (optional).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience with the vendor (optional).

## Tips

* Please complete the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Make sure to rate each field only once, even if you have multiple interactions with the vendor.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Notes" field to provide more context.
