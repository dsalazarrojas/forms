# Youth Ministry Internship Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Ministry Internship Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the experience and performance of interns participating in the Youth Ministry program. This form helps assess their skills, knowledge, and attitude acquired during the internship. It is essential to fill this form accurately to provide valuable feedback and insights for both the interns and the program administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the intern's name in the "Intern Name" field.
2. Select the date of evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
3. Choose the correct period of evaluation from the dropdown options in the "Evaluation Period" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intern Name (intern_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the intern being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date (evaluation_date, date, optional)**: Select the date on which you are conducting the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period (evaluation_period, number, optional)**: Choose the period of evaluation from the dropdown options. Please note that this field is not explicitly defined in the YAML, so it is assumed to be a dropdown with options like "1 week", "2 weeks", "3 weeks", "4 weeks", and so on.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the form accurately and honestly to provide constructive feedback.
- Evaluate the intern's performance based on their work, attitude, and behavior during the internship.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrators for clarification.
- Please submit the completed form to ensure timely evaluation and feedback for the interns.
