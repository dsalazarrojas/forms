# Executive Director Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Director Evaluation Form is used to assess the performance of the Executive Director. This form provides a structured way to collect feedback and ratings from the Department Head or other relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the Employee Name, which is the name of the Executive Director being evaluated.
3. Select the Evaluation Date from the calendar picker.
4. Choose who completed the evaluation (e.g., Department Head, Assistant to the Executive Director, etc.).
5. Provide feedback for the Executive Director.
6. Rate the Executive Director's performance from 1 to 5.
7. Provide feedback details for the rating.
8. If applicable, enter the Department Name, Department Head Name, Department Head Title, and Department Head Email and Phone Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **required**): This field is for entering the name of the Executive Director being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, **optional**): This field is for selecting the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluation Form Completed By** (`evaluation_form_completed_by`, select_one, **required**): This field is for choosing who completed the evaluation (e.g., Department Head, Assistant to the Executive Director, etc.).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **optional**): This field is for providing any additional feedback or comments for the Executive Director.
* **Rating 1 to 5** (`rating_1_to_5`, number, **required**): This field is for rating the Executive Director's performance from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback Details 1 to 5** (`feedback_details_1_to_5`, text, **optional**): This field is for providing details about the feedback given for the rating.
* **Evaluation Form Completed** (`evaluation_form_completed`, note, **optional**): This field is for providing any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, **optional**): This field is for entering the name of the department the Executive Director is leading.
* **Department Head Name** (`department_head_name`, text, **optional**): This field is for entering the name of the Department Head.
* **Department Head Title** (`department_head_title`, text, **optional**): This field is for entering the title of the Department Head.
* **Department Head Email** (`department_head_email`, email, **optional**): This field is for entering the email of the Department Head.
* **Department Head Phone** (`department_head_phone`, text, **optional**): This field is for entering the phone number of the Department Head.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback and ratings.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant stakeholders or the Executive Director.
