# Supervisor And Consultant Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisor and Consultant Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from the supervisor and consultant teams. It helps to evaluate the effectiveness and performance of the consultant and provides a way for supervisors to share their comments and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name as the supervisor.
2. Enter the consultant's name.
3. Select the evaluation date.
4. Answer the evaluation questions, providing specific examples to support your answers.
5. Choose the evaluation type (Formal, Informal, or Ad-Hoc).
6. Rate the consultant's performance and overall satisfaction.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
8. Select any areas of improvement or suggestions for the consultant.
9. Rate the overall rating of the evaluation form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Name** (Required)
	+ Enter your name as the supervisor completing this form.
* **Consultant Name** (Required)
	+ Enter the name of the consultant being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (Required)
	+ Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Supervisor Comments** (Required)
	+ Provide specific comments and feedback about the consultant's performance.
* **Consultant Comments** (Required)
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback from the consultant.
* **Evaluation Type** (Required)
	+ Select the type of evaluation (Formal, Informal, or Ad-Hoc).
* **Supervisor Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the consultant's performance on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Consultant Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the consultant's performance on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Required)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the consultant's performance.
* **Recommendation** (Optional)
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Evaluation Form Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the overall rating of the evaluation form.
* **Overall Rating** (Required)
	+ Rate the overall performance of the consultant.
* **Submission** (Optional)
	+ Select all areas of improvement or suggestions for the consultant.
* **Submission 2** (Optional)
	+ Select all areas of improvement or suggestions for the consultant.

Note: The form is divided into two pages. Please make sure to fill out both pages completely and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
