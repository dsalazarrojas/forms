# legal_case_outcome_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the outcome of a legal case after it has been resolved. This helps in identifying areas of improvement and ensuring that best practices are followed for future cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Case ID of the legal case being evaluated.
2. Select the type of case it was (Case Management, Litigation, Mediation, or Arbitration).
3. Choose the outcome of the case (Positive, Negative, Neutral, or Unsatisfactory).
4. Enter the case value or rating.
5. Rate the client's attitude (Very Positive, Positive, Negative, or Unsatisfactory).
6. Rate the lawyer's attitude (Very Positive, Positive, Negative, or Unsatisfactory).
7. Rate the court procedure (Very Efficient, Efficient, Inefficient, or Not Applicable).
8. Rate the court fees (Very High, High, Low, or Not Applicable).
9. Rate the lawyer fees (Very High, High, Low, or Not Applicable).
10. Write a brief description of your overall experience with the case.
11. Add any additional comments about the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case ID** (1, number, required): This field is used to enter the unique identifier of the case being evaluated.
* **Case Type** (2, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of case it was (Case Management, Litigation, Mediation, or Arbitration).
* **Outcome** (3, select_one, required): This field is used to rate the outcome of the case (Positive, Negative, Neutral, or Unsatisfactory).
* **Case Value** (4, number, required): This field is used to enter the case value or rating.
* **Client Attitude** (5, select_one, required): This field is used to rate the client's attitude (Very Positive, Positive, Negative, or Unsatisfactory).
* **Lawyer Attitude** (6, select_one, required): This field is used to rate the lawyer's attitude (Very Positive, Positive, Negative, or Unsatisfactory).
* **Court Procedure** (7, select_one, required): This field is used to rate the court procedure (Very Efficient, Efficient, Inefficient, or Not Applicable).
* **Court Fees** (8, select_one, required): This field is used to rate the court fees (Very High, High, Low, or Not Applicable).
* **Lawyer Fees** (9, select_one, required): This field is used to rate the lawyer fees (Very High, High, Low, or Not Applicable).
* **Overall Experience** (10, text, required): This field is used to write a brief description of your overall experience with the case.
* **Comments** (11, text, optional): This field is used to add any additional comments about the case.
