# Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from trainees about their experience in a medical simulation training session. It helps evaluate the effectiveness and quality of the training and provides insights to improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question or section according to your experience and thoughts about the session.
3. Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
4. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Evaluation Form

* **Evaluation Form** (optional): This is a free-form text field where you can provide any comments or thoughts about the session. Please be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise and relevant.

### Page 2: Session Details

* **Session Details** (optional): This is another free-form text field where you can provide any additional information about the session, such as what you learned, what was unclear, or what you enjoyed.

### Page 3: Evaluation Criteria

* **Critical Thinking, Creativity, Communication, Problem-Solving** (select multiple): Choose the areas where you felt you were adequately trained or struggled. You can select as many or as few options as you like.

### Page 4: Evaluation Scores

* **Evaluation Scores** (optional): In this text field, you can provide any specific scores or feedback for each of the evaluation criteria listed above.

### Page 5: Overall Rating

* **Overall Rating** (number): Provide a number from 1 to 10 to rate the overall quality and effectiveness of the session.

### Page 6: Session Evaluation

* **Session Evaluation** (note): This is a free-form text field where you can provide any final thoughts, suggestions, or feedback about the session.
