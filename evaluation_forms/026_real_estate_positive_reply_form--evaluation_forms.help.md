# real_estate_positive_reply_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and ratings from clients who have had a positive experience with a real estate agent. The information collected will help agents identify what they did well and how to improve their service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Provide your feedback about your experience with the real estate agent.
2. Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Dissatisfied".
3. Describe what the real estate agent did well.
4. Suggest areas of improvement for the agent.
5. Provide your contact details so the agent can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Feedback
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with the real estate agent.
### 2. How would you rate your experience?
* **How would you rate your experience?** (`rating`, select_one, required: false): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Dissatisfied".
### 3. What did we do well?
* **What did we do well?** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Describe what the real estate agent did well during your interaction.
### 4. What could we improve on?
* **What could we improve on?** (`improvement_points`, text, required: false): Suggest areas where the real estate agent could improve their service.
### 5. How can we contact you?
* **How can we contact you?** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide your email address, phone number, or other contact information so the agent can get back to you.
### 6. Real Estate Agent Name
* **Real Estate Agent Name** (`real_estate_agent_name`, text, required: false): If you'd like to provide the name of the real estate agent you interacted with, please do so.
### 7. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so the agent can get back to you.
### 8. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so the agent can get back to you.
### 9. Date and Time
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of your interaction with the real estate agent.

## Tips

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* The more detailed you are in your feedback, the more helpful it will be to the agent.
* Your feedback will help improve the service provided by the real estate agent and their team.
