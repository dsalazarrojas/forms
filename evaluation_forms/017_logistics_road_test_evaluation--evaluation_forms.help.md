# Logistics Road Test Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Logistics Road Test Evaluation form is used to document and evaluate the performance of a truck or vehicle during a road test. The form collects data on individual tests, including scores and comments, to provide an overview of the test process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the name of each test.
2.  Enter the score for each test.
3.  Select the date and time of the road test.
4.  Sign and date your signature as the driver.
5.  Sign and date your signature as the examiner.
6.  Sign and date your signature as the evaluator.
7.  Add any additional comments about the road test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test 1 Name** (`test_1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the first test.
* **Test 1 Score** (`test_1_score`, `number`, required): Enter the score for the first test.
* **Test 2 Name** (`test_2_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the second test.
* **Test 2 Score** (`test_2_score`, `number`, required): Enter the score for the second test.
* **Test 3 Name** (`test_3_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the third test.
* **Test 3 Score** (`test_3_score`, `number`, required): Enter the score for the third test.
* **Test 4 Name** (`test_4_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the fourth test.
* **Test 4 Score** (`test_4_score`, `number`, required): Enter the score for the fourth test.
* **Road Test Date** (`road_test_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the road test.
* **Road Test Time** (`road_test_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the road test.
* **Driver Signature** (`driver_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your signature as the driver.
* **Examiner Signature** (`examiner_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your signature as the examiner.
* **Evaluator Signature** (`evaluator_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your signature as the evaluator.
* **Road Test Comments** (`road_test_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about the road test.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be specific and accurate when entering test names, scores, and comments.
* Ensure that all signatures are dated.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for evaluation.
