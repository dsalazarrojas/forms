# Evaluation Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses seeking an evaluation service, primarily for quality, safety, and security assessments. It helps us understand your needs and preferences for the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your business name and contact information in the **Business Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Describe the service you are requesting in the **Description of Service Request** field.
5. Select your preferred time of day for the evaluation from the **Preferred Time of Day** field.
6. Choose your preferred date and time for the evaluation in the **Preferred Date and Time** field.
7. Specify the tools to be used during the evaluation in the **Assigned Tool** field.
8. Select the category of the evaluation from the **Category** field.
9. Choose multiple locations for the evaluation from the **Select Multiple Locations** field.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes in the **Additional Notes** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name and contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Description of Service Request** (`description`, `text`, required): Describe the service you are requesting.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred time of day for the evaluation (08:00 AM - 10:00 AM, 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 02:00 PM, 02:00 PM - 04:00 PM, 04:00 PM - 06:00 PM, 06:00 PM - 08:00 PM, 08:00 PM - 10:00 PM).
* **Preferred Date and Time** (`date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred date and time for the evaluation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Specify the tool(s) to be used during the evaluation.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the evaluation (Quality, Safety, Security).
* **Select Multiple Locations** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple locations for the evaluation (Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Repeat Category** (`repeat_category`, `text`, optional): Enter another category for evaluation.
* **More Repeat Category** (`more_repeat_category`, `text`, optional): Enter yet another category for evaluation.
* **Another Repeat Category** (`another_repeat_category`, `text`, optional): Enter another category for evaluation.
* **Last Repeat Category** (`last_repeat_category`, `text`, optional): Enter the last category for evaluation.
