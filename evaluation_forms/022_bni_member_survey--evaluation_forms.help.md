# BNI Member Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BNI Member Survey is designed to gather feedback from members on their overall experience within the chapter. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to help the chapter's leadership make informed decisions and improve the overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For select_one questions, choose the option that best represents your response.
3. For text-based questions, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. If a question doesn't apply to you, select the relevant option (e.g., "Not applicable," "Not yet," or "None").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Type in your email address. Ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Chapter Name** (`chapter_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the chapter you belong to.
* **How Long Have You Been a Member?** (`membership_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how long you've been a member from the provided options.
* **How Many Meetings Have You Attended This Quarter?** (`meeting_attendance`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of meetings you've attended this quarter.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the chapter, from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **Quality of Today's Meeting** (`meeting_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you rate the quality of the recent meeting you attended.
* **If Visitors Attended, How Were They Treated?** (`visitor_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Select how visitors were treated during the meeting.
* **Have You Received Quality Referrals?** (`referrals_received`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you've received quality referrals.
* **Have You Given Referrals to Others?** (`referrals_given`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you've given referrals to others.
* **Value of Networking Opportunities** (`networking_value`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the value of networking opportunities within the chapter.
* **Do You Feel the Chapter Has Good Leadership?** (`leadership_felt`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you feel the chapter has good leadership.
* **What Improvements Would You Suggest?** (`improvements_suggested`, `text`, optional): Share any improvements you'd like to suggest for the chapter.
* **Would You Recommend BNI to Others?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you'd recommend BNI to others.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
