# Logo Presentation Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the presentation and various aspects of a logo. It's designed to be completed by individuals with expertise in design, marketing, or other relevant fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the logo and answer the questions in the form honestly and objectively.
2. For each question, choose the most suitable answer from the available options.
3. Provide any additional comments or remarks in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Presentation** (form_1): Rate the overall presentation of the logo, considering its visual appeal, balance, and coherence.
* **Color Scheme Effectiveness** (form_2): Choose the best description of the color scheme's effectiveness in communicating the brand's values and message.
	+ Logo Design: The color scheme effectively conveys the logo's message and is visually appealing.
	+ Brand Guidelines: The color scheme is consistent with the brand's guidelines but not necessarily effective in conveying the message.
	+ Design Style: The color scheme fits the design style of the overall brand identity.
	+ Overall Impression: Neutral, does not stand out.
* **Composition** (form_3): Describe the composition of the logo, considering the arrangement of elements and visual balance.
* **Typography Effectiveness** (form_4): Rate the effectiveness of the typography in communicating the brand's message and values.
* **Overall Impression** (form_5): Describe your overall impression of the logo.
* **Category** (form_6): Choose the category the logo belongs to.
* **Subcategory** (form_7): Select all subcategories that apply to the logo.
* **Additional Comments** (form_8): Provide any additional comments or feedback for improvement.
* **Rating** (form_9): Rate the logo on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comment** (form_10): Provide a written comment to accompany your rating.
* **Logo Type** (form_11): Choose the type of logo the logo belongs to.
	+ Icon: The logo is an icon.
	+ Wordmark: The logo is a wordmark.
	+ Lettermark: The logo is a lettermark.
	+ Emblem: The logo is an emblem.
* **Logo Size** (form_12): Rate the size of the logo in relation to the overall design.

Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant person or team for guidance.
