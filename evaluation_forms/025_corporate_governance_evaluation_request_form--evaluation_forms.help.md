# Corporate Governance Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Governance Evaluation Request Form is designed to collect necessary information for a review request from the corporate governance team. This includes details about the request, the person or group being reviewed, and the desired timeline and frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `form_title` field with a brief description of the review request.
2. Select the date you would like to make the request in the `request_date` field.
3. Indicate whether the request requires review in the `review_requests` field (Yes/No).
4. Choose the preferred timeline for the review in the `preferred_timeline` field.
5. Set the desired review deadlines in the `review_deadlines` field.
6. Select the frequency of reviews in the `review_frequency` field.
7. Choose the type of review in the `review_type` field.
8. Indicate the current status of the review in the `review_status` field.
9. Add any additional comments about the review in the `review_comments` field.
10. Fill in the `board_name` and `team_name` fields with the relevant department information (if applicable).
11. Enter the department and title of the person or group being reviewed (`reviewee_department` and `reviewee_title`).
12. Enter the department and title of the person submitting the request (`submitter_department` and `submitter_title`).
13. Provide contact information (name, email, and phone number).
14. Enter the name and department of the individual or group being reviewed (`reviewee` and `reviewee_department`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A brief description of the review request.
  * (`form_title`, text, required)
* **Request Date**: The date you would like to make the request.
  * (`request_date`, date, required)
* **Review Requests**: Indicate whether the request requires review (Yes/No).
  * (`review_requests`, select_multiple, required)
* **Preferred Timeline**: Choose the preferred timeline for the review.
  * (`preferred_timeline`, select_one, required)
* **Review Deadlines**: Set the desired review deadlines.
  * (`review_deadlines`, date, required)
* **Review Frequency**: Choose the frequency of reviews.
  * (`review_frequency`, select_one, required)
* **Review Type**: Choose the type of review.
  * (`review_type`, select_multiple, required)
* **Review Status**: Indicate the current status of the review.
  * (`review_status`, select_one, required)
* **Review Comments**: Add any additional comments about the review.
  * (`review_comments`, text, required)
* **Board Name**: The name of the department submitting the request.
  * (`board_name`, text, optional)
* **Team Name**: The name of the team submitting the request.
  * (`team_name`, text, optional)
* **Department**: The department of the person or group being reviewed.
  * (`reviewee_department`, text, optional)
* **Role**: The title of the individual or group being reviewed.
  * (`reviewee_title`, text, required)
* **Submitter Department**: The department of the person submitting the request.
  * (`submitter_department`, text, optional)
* **Submitter Title**: The title of the person submitting the request.
  * (`submitter_title`, text, optional)
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the request.
  * (`submitter`, text, optional)
* **Contact Information**: Provide contact information (name, email, and phone number).
  * (`contact`, text, required)
* **Email**: The email address of the person submitting the request.
  * (`email`, email, required)
* **Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the request.
  * (`phone`, text, required)
* **Reviewee Name**: The name of the individual or group being reviewed.
  * (`reviewee`, text, required)
* **Contact**: The department and name of the individual or group being reviewed.
  * (`reviewee_department`, text, optional)
