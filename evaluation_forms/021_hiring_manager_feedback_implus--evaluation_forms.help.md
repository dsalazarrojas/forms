# Hiring Manager Feedback Implus - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Manager Feedback Implus form is designed to gather feedback from hiring managers on the performance of their team members. It helps to identify areas of improvement and areas of excellence, enabling data-driven decision-making and personnel development. This form should be completed by hiring managers to provide their honest and constructive feedback on their team members' performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with your personal information, including:
   - **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
   - **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
   - **Email**: Enter your official email address.
   - **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
   - **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the team's performance. Use the scale provided in the **Rating** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Your official email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position in the company.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the team's performance using the scale provided.
