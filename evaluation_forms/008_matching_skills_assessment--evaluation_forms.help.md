# Matching Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Matching Skills Assessment form is a tool to help you evaluate your skills in specific areas. It's a self-assessment tool, not a test, and it's not scored. The main purpose is to help you identify areas where you might need further development or improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page, one at a time. Start by selecting the most relevant option on page 1, and then proceed to page 2, where you will enter your skills assessment in the provided text field. On page 3, you will have the opportunity to provide additional information to support your skills assessment. Finally, on page 4, you will be asked about your available time for training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **matching_skills_1** (select_one, required: false): Select the most relevant option that describes your skills level. This field is meant to give us an idea of your general skills level, so please choose the option that best represents your current abilities.
* **skills_assessment** (text, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your skills in this area. Be as specific as possible, and try to use a standard set of skills-based language that we can easily understand. This will help us tailor feedback and recommendations to your needs.
* **matching_skills_4** (text, required: false): This field is meant to provide more information about your skills in the selected area. Please be as detailed as possible and provide any relevant context or examples.
* **matching_skills_5** (time, required: false): Indicate how many hours you are available to dedicate to training and development in this area.
