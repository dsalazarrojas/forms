# program_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data from program participants to evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Answer each question in the provided fields.
3. For the "Form" field, select the corresponding form type (Form A, B, or C) that best describes your experience with the program.
4. Enter the date and time you participated in the program.
5. Provide your comments and feedback in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Evaluation Survey (page 1)**: This is the first page of the survey, where you will find general questions to provide your feedback.
* **What was your role at the survey** (`participant_feedback`): Please describe your role or position at the time of the survey.
* **Program Evaluation Form (Select)** (`evaluation_form`): Select the form type (Form A, Form B, or Form C) that best describes your experience with the program.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you participated in the program.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you participated in the program.
* **What was your role at the survey** (`participant_feedback_first`): Repeat the description of your role or position at the survey.
* **What were your comments** (`participant_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the program.
* **Program Evaluation Survey (page 2)** (`program_evaluation_survey_second_page`): This is the second page of the survey, where you can continue providing feedback and information.
