# Role Model Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Model Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the leadership qualities of a specific role model.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the role model's behavior and context to ensure you can provide informed feedback.
2. Choose one option that best describes the role model's behavior for each of the Leadership Quality questions (fields 3-8).
3. Provide any additional comments about the role model's behavior in the Comments field (field 9).
4. Submit your assessment to provide valuable feedback.
