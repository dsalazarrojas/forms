# Syllable Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Syllable Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback on the sound quality and recording conditions of a syllable.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Syllable Rating: Rate the syllable's sound quality on a scale of 1-5.
	* Syllable Count: Enter the total number of syllables in the recording.
	* Recording Time: Enter the duration of the recording in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
	* Date Submitted: Enter the date you submitted this evaluation.
	* Time Submitted: Enter the time you submitted this evaluation.
	* Recording Device: Select the device used to record the syllable (e.g. Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop).
	* Evaluator Name: Enter your name as the evaluator.
2. Optional information:
	* Evaluator Comments: Add any additional comments or notes about the syllable.
	* Evaluator Title: Enter your title or position (e.g. Researcher, Engineer, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Syllable Evaluation Form**: This is the form's title, providing context for the evaluation.
* **Syllable Rating**: Rate the syllable's sound quality on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Poor sound quality.
	+ 2: Fair sound quality.
	+ 3: Good sound quality.
	+ 4: Excellent sound quality.
	+ 5: Perfect sound quality.
* **Syllable Count**: Enter the total number of syllables in the recording.
* **Recording Time**: Enter the duration of the recording in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Recording Device**: Select the device used to record the syllable (e.g. Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop).
* **Evaluator Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the syllable.
* **Evaluator Rating**: Rate your own evaluation on a scale of 1-5 (optional).
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted this evaluation.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time you submitted this evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Title**: Enter your title or position (e.g. Researcher, Engineer, etc.).
