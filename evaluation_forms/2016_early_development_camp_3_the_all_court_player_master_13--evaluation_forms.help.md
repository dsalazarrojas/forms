# 2016 Early Development Camp 3 The All Court Player Master 13 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and abilities of a tennis player, specifically for the 2016 Early Development Camp 3 tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player's personal information, including their full name, date of birth, and age.
2. Evaluate their technical skills, such as forehand, backhand, serve, volley, overhead, and footwork.
3. Assess their physical fitness, including their agility, endurance level, strength, flexibility, and cardiovascular endurance.
4. Evaluate their mental game, including their mental toughness, ability to maintain focus, tactical awareness, and ability to follow coach recommendations.
5. Provide a signature from the evaluator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the player's full legal name.
* **Date of birth** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the player's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Age** (`3`, `integer`, required): Enter the player's age.
* **Gender** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's gender (Male or Female).
* **Dominant hand** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's dominant hand (Right-handed or Left-handed).
* **Current playing level** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's current playing level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Tournament Level).
* **Coach name** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the player's coach's name.
* **Forehand technique rating (1-10)** (`8`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's forehand technique rating, with 1 being Needs significant work and 10 being Excellent.
* **Backhand technique rating (1-10)** (`9`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's backhand technique rating, with 1 being Needs significant work and 10 being Excellent.
* **Serve technique rating (1-10)** (`10`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's serve technique rating, with 1 being Needs significant work and 10 being Excellent.
* **Volley technique rating (1-10)** (`11`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's volley technique rating, with 1 being Needs significant work and 10 being Excellent.
* **Overhead/Smash technique rating (1-10)** (`12`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's overhead/smash technique rating, with 1 being Needs significant work and 10 being Excellent.
* **Footwork and movement rating (1-10)** (`13`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's footwork and movement rating, with 1 being Needs significant work and 10 being Excellent.
* **Court coverage ability** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's court coverage ability (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Ability to use different grips (select all that apply)** (`15`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the grips the player can use, including Eastern Forehand, Semi-Western Forehand, Western Forehand, Eastern Backhand, Continental, or Semi-Western Backhand.
* **Agility score (1-10)** (`16`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's agility score, with 1 being Needs improvement and 10 being Excellent.
* **Cardiovascular endurance level** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's cardiovascular endurance level (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Overall strength rating (1-10)** (`18`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's overall strength rating, with 1 being Needs improvement and 10 being Excellent.
* **Flexibility rating (1-10)** (`19`, `integer`, required): Evaluate the player's flexibility rating, with 1 being Needs improvement and 10 being Excellent.
* **Mental toughness level** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's mental toughness level (Low, Moderate, High, or Very High).
* **Ability to maintain focus during play** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's ability to maintain focus during play (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Tactical awareness and court positioning** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Select the player's tactical awareness and court positioning (Needs Work, Developing, Good, Strong, or Excellent).
* **Coach recommendations for improvement** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter any coach recommendations for the player's improvement.
* **Recommended date for next evaluation** (`24`, `date`, optional): Enter the recommended date for the next evaluation.
* **Evaluator signature** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the evaluator's signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the provided rating scales to evaluate the player's skills and abilities.
* Be honest and objective when assessing the player's performance.
* Use the recommended date for the next evaluation as a guide.
* Sign and date the form after completion.
