# Cultural Awareness Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after they have attended a cultural awareness training session.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer each question in as much detail as possible.
- Fill in the program name and session name where the training took place.
- Select the evaluation time when you completed the session.
- Rate the overall quality of the session using the provided rating scale.
- Optionally, provide any comments about the session.
- Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you with further questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional):
    - This field is where you enter the name of the program you attended.
    - Please fill in the name of the program as it appears on the promotional materials or as you know it.
- **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional):
    - This field is where you enter the name of the session you attended.
    - Please fill in the name of the session as it appears on the session materials.
- **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional):
    - This field is where you select the time when you completed the session.
    - Please select the time when you finished the session.
- **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional):
    - This field is where you rate the overall quality of the session using the rating scale provided.
    - Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, required):
    - This field is where you select the rating scale for your overall evaluation.
    - Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional):
    - This field is where you can provide any comments or feedback about the session.
    - Please be as detailed as possible, but keep your comments concise.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional):
    - This field is where you enter your email address.
    - We will use this to contact you with further questions or updates.
    - Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional):
    - This field is where you enter your phone number.
    - We will use this to contact you if we need further information.
    - Please ensure that your phone number is accurate and up-to-date.
