# Picnic Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Picnic Evaluation Form is used to collect data from picnic events, which will help in evaluating their effectiveness and identifying areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the relevant information for the picnic event you are evaluating.
2. Enter your name as the evaluator.
3. Describe the picnic event location.
4. Provide a brief description of the picnic event.
5. Enter the date and time the picnic was completed.
6. Rate the overall quality of the picnic event.
7. Select any areas that could be improved.
8. Enter any additional comments about the picnic event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Picnic Event Id** (`picnic_event_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the picnic event.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person evaluating the picnic event.
* **Picnic Location** (`picnic_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the picnic event.
* **Picnic Description** (`picnic_description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the picnic event.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the picnic event was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time the picnic event was completed.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall quality of the picnic event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_of_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas that could be improved for the picnic event. Options include Poor, Good, Fair, and Excellent.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the picnic event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in only the necessary information for the picnic event.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your picnic event guidelines for clarification.
* Remember to provide accurate and honest feedback to help improve future picnic events.
