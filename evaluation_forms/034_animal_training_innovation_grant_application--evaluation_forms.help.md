# Animal Training Innovation Grant Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate your innovation project for an animal training grant. Please fill in the required information to provide a clear and detailed application. Your application will be reviewed based on the information provided, so please ensure that you answer all the required fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form in sections: **Organization Information**, **Project Information**, **Budget**, **Team**, and **Evaluation Criteria**.
2. Make sure to provide all the required information in each section.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information, especially in the **Project Description**, **Project Goals**, **Expected Outcomes and Impact**, and **Evaluation Plan** fields.
4. Attach relevant documents as per the **Supporting Documents** section if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: Provide the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type**: Choose the type of organization you represent.
	+ Animal Shelter
	+ Rescue Organization
	+ Training Facility
	+ Research Institution
	+ Nonprofit
	+ Private Trainer
	+ Other
* **Tax ID or Registration Number**: Enter your organization's tax ID or registration number.
* **Organization Address**: Enter your organization's street address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your organization is located.
* **State**: Enter the state or province where your organization is located.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code or postal code of your organization.
* **Organization Phone**: Enter your organization's contact phone number.
* **Organization Website**: Enter the URL of your organization's website (optional).
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Title**: Enter a title for your project.
* **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Focus of Innovation**: Choose the area of innovation for your project.
	+ New Training Methods
	+ Technology Application
	+ Behavioral Science
	+ Animal Welfare
	+ Accessibility
	+ Conservation
	+ Other
* **Target Animal Types**: Choose the types of animals that will benefit from your project.
	+ Dogs
	+ Cats
	+ Horses
	+ Wildlife
	+ Exotic Animals
	+ Multiple Species
* **Specific Project Goals**: Enter the specific goals of your project.
* **Expected Outcomes and Impact**: Enter the expected outcomes and impact of your project.
* **Project Timeline**: Enter the start date and estimated duration of your project.
* **Total Project Budget**: Enter the total estimated cost of your project.
* **Grant Request Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting from the grant.
* **Other Funding Sources**: Enter any other sources of funding for your project (optional).
* **Budget Breakdown**: Enter how you plan to allocate the funds.
* **Project Lead Name**: Enter the name of the person leading the project.
* **Project Lead Qualifications**: Enter the education and experience of the project lead.
* **Key Team Members**: Enter the names and roles of key team members.
* **Success Metrics**: Enter how you will measure the success of your project.
* **Evaluation Plan**: Enter how you will evaluate the results of your project.
* **Sustainability Plan**: Enter how your project will continue long-term (optional).
* **Have Professional References Available**: Check if you have professional references available (yes/no).
* **Agree to Progress Reporting**: Check if you agree to grant reporting requirements (yes/no).
* **Additional Information or Comments**: Enter any other relevant information or comments.

## Tips

1. Make sure to answer all the required fields.
2. Be clear and concise in your answers.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the **Project Description**, **Project Goals**, **Expected Outcomes and Impact**, and **Evaluation Plan** fields.
4. Attach relevant documents to support your application if necessary.
