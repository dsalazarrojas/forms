# Educational Sector Indicator Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Sector Indicator Evaluation form is designed to collect data about the performance, quality, or progress of the educational sector. This data will be used to assess the strengths and weaknesses of the sector and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the relevant information
Review the form sections and questions carefully to ensure you are answering the right questions for your context. For each question, select the most accurate and up-to-date information available.

### Step 2: Answer the questions
Fill out the form with the selected information, being as detailed and specific as possible. If you need to attach any supporting documents or evidence, please do so at the end of the form.
