# New Year's Marketing Campaign Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the performance of your New Year's marketing campaign. Please complete it thoroughly to help us understand what worked well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer the questions as accurately and completely as possible.
2.  For any field, if the answer is not applicable, please select "Not Applicable" and provide a brief explanation.
3.  Ensure to fill out the form only once per campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Objective**: A brief description of the objective of this year's New Year's marketing campaign. This will help us understand what was aimed to be achieved.
* **Budget**: Enter the total budget allocated for this campaign.
* **Results**: Select one of the options provided:
	+ **Success**: If the campaign achieved its objectives.
	+ **Failure**: If the campaign did not achieve its objectives.
* **Marketing Channel**: Select all the marketing channels you used for this campaign:
	+ **Email**
	+ **Social Media**
	+ **Print**
	+ **Television**
* **Target Audience**: Enter the number of people you targeted with this campaign.
* **Evaluation Method**: Select all the methods you used to evaluate the campaign:
	+ **Survey**
	+ **Focus Group**
	+ **Poll**
	+ **Review**
* **Time Period**: Enter the period during which the campaign took place.
* **Time Spent**: Enter the total time spent on this campaign.

## Tips
1.  Please provide as much detail as possible in each field.
2.  Selecting "Not Applicable" in any field should be accompanied by a brief explanation.
3.  Fill out the form thoroughly to ensure a comprehensive evaluation.
