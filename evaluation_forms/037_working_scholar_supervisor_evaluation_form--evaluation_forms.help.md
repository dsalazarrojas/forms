# Working Scholar Supervisor Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Working Scholar Supervisor Evaluation Form is designed to assess the scholar's progress and provide constructive feedback for improvement. This form helps to evaluate the scholar's performance, achievements, and areas for growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the evaluation.
2. **Scholar Name**: Enter the name of the scholar being evaluated.
3. **Supervisor Name**: Enter your name as the supervisor.
4. **Evaluation Date**: Select the date of the evaluation.
5. **Evaluation Form Section 1-16**: Answer each section with either "Yes" or "No" based on the scholar's performance. Some sections are optional.
6. **Evaluation Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback for the scholar.
7. **Evaluation Recommendations**: Offer suggestions or recommendations for the scholar's improvement.
8. **Evaluation Conclusion**: Summarize your evaluation and conclusion.
9. **Evaluation Signature**: Sign and date the evaluation to confirm completion.
