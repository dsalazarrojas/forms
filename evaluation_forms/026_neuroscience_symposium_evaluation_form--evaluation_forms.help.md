# Neuroscience Symposium Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neuroscience Symposium Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from participants on various aspects of the event, including session titles, presenter names, evaluation, date, time, feedback, email address, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal information:
	* `participant_name`: Please enter your name as it appears on your conference badge or registration.
	* `email_address`: Please enter your email address (e.g., example@example.com).
	* `phone`: Please enter your phone number (optional).
2. Evaluate the sessions:
	* `session_title`: Please select the session title(s) you found most informative or relevant to your field of work.
	* `presenter_name`: Please list the presenter(s) name(s) of the selected session(s).
	* `evaluation`: Please provide a brief evaluation of the session(s) (e.g., informative, well-organized, etc.).
3. Provide feedback:
	* `date`: Please indicate the date of the symposium.
	* `time`: Please indicate the time of the symposium.
4. Provide any additional feedback:
	* `feedback`: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about the symposium.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title**: This is the title of this form, which is used to identify the symposium evaluation form.
* **participant_name**: This field is used to capture your name as it appears on your conference badge or registration.
* **session_title**: This field is used to select the session title(s) you found most informative or relevant to your field of work.
* **presenter_name**: This field is used to list the presenter(s) name(s) of the selected session(s).
* **evaluation**: This field is used to provide a brief evaluation of the session(s) (e.g., informative, well-organized, etc.).
* **date**: This field is used to capture the date of the symposium.
* **time**: This field is used to capture the time of the symposium.
* **feedback**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the symposium.
* **email_address**: This field is used to capture your email address (e.g., example@example.com).
* **phone**: This field is used to capture your phone number (optional).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when evaluating the sessions and providing feedback.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the `feedback` field.
* Thank you for taking the time to evaluate the symposium! Your feedback is invaluable to us.
