# Webhook Delivery Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Webhook Delivery Platform Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our Webhook Delivery Platform. It will help us to identify areas of improvement and measure the overall satisfaction of our customers. Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to provide us with valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please enter the name of your company in the `Company Name` field.
2.  Choose the size of your company from the `Company Size` dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the number of members in your team in the `Team Members` field.
4.  Rate our platform's reliability, scalability, security, and cost in the corresponding fields.
5.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform and select whether you would recommend it to others.
6.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the `Additional Comments` field.
7.  Indicate whether you have chatted with our sales team, support team, or both.
8.  Select whether you have received follow-up messages from us.
9.  Rate your satisfaction with the follow-up messages and overall platform experience.
10. Enter your rating for the platform and provide any comments about your experience.
11. Enter your contact information (email) for us to get in touch with you.
12. Finally, indicate whether you would like to receive follow-up messages from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Please enter the name of your company. This will help us to associate your feedback with your company's account.
* **Company Size** (3, select_one, required): Choose the size of your company from the dropdown menu. This will help us to understand the scope of your operations and tailor our services accordingly.
* **Team Members** (4, number, required): Please enter the number of members in your team. This will help us to assess the scale of your operations and provide more effective support.
* **Platform Rating** (5, number, required): Rate our platform's reliability on a scale of 1-5. This will help us to gauge the stability and dependability of our platform.
* **Platform Satisfaction** (6, select_one, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with the platform from the dropdown menu. This will help us to understand your overall experience with our platform.
* **Ease of Use** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select the ease of use of our platform. This will help us to identify areas where we can improve the user interface and experience.
* **Communication** (8, select_one, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with our communication from the dropdown menu. This will help us to assess the quality of our customer support and communication.
* **Reliability** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select the reliability of our platform from the dropdown menu. This will help us to evaluate the stability and dependability of our platform.
* **Scalability** (10, select_one, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with the platform's scalability from the dropdown menu. This will help us to understand the platform's ability to adapt to growing demands.
* **Security** (11, select_one, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with the platform's security from the dropdown menu. This will help us to ensure the safety and integrity of your data.
* **Cost** (12, number, required): Enter your rating for the cost of our platform. This will help us to balance our pricing with your expectations.
* **Recommendation** (13, text, optional): Please provide any comments or recommendations for our platform. This will help us to improve our services and tailor our offering to your needs.
* **Feedback** (14, text, required): Please provide any feedback about your experience with our platform. This will help us to identify areas of improvement and measure customer satisfaction.
* **Contact Info** (15, email, required): Please enter your contact information (email) so we can get in touch with you for follow-up discussions.
* **Additional Comments** (16, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about our platform.
* **Chat with Sales** (17, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have chatted with our sales team.
* **Chat with Support** (18, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have chatted with our support team.
* **Chat with Both** (19, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have chatted with both our sales and support teams.
* **Follow-up Messages** (20, select_one, required): Choose whether you received follow-up messages from us.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (21, select_one, required): Choose your overall satisfaction with the platform from the dropdown menu. This will help us to measure customer experience.
* **Rating** (22, number, required): Enter your rating for the platform. This will help us to evaluate the platform's quality and performance.
* **Comments** (23, text, required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Follow-up Messages Sent** (25, select_multiple, required): Select whether you received follow-up messages from us.
* **Form Report** (24, note, optional): Please read and acknowledge the form report. This will help us to confirm your understanding of the form's purpose and usage.

Note: This guide is intended to provide a clear understanding of each field's purpose and how to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
