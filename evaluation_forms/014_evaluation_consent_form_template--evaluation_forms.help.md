# Evaluation Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is used to collect evaluation information from participants, including their details, evaluation type, and agreement to participate in the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information on page 1: Participant Info.
	* Enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
2. Choose the type of evaluation you are participating in on page 2: Evaluation Type.
	* Select one of the provided options: Evaluation of your performance or Evaluation of your abilities.
3. Read and agree to the participant agreement on page 3: Participant Agreement.
	* This is a required field and must be filled in.
4. Read and agree to the evaluation agreement on page 4: Evaluation Agreement.
	* This is a required field and must be filled in.
5. Add any additional comments or observations on page 5: Note (optional).
6. (Optional) Enter the date of submission on page 6: Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Info (Page 1)
* **Participant Info (text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
### Evaluation Info (Page 2)
* This field is used to enter additional evaluation information.
### Participant Agreement (Page 3)
* **Participant Agreement (text)**: Read and agree to the participant agreement.
* This is a required field to ensure you understand your role and responsibilities as a participant.
### Evaluation Agreement (Page 4)
* **Evaluation Agreement (text)**: Read and agree to the evaluation agreement.
* This is a required field to ensure you understand the evaluation process and your role in it.
### Note (Page 5)
* **Note (text)**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
* This field is optional and can be used to provide more information about your evaluation.
### Submission (Page 6)
* This field is not currently used.
### Evaluation Type (Page 2)
* **Evaluation Type (select_one)**: Choose one of the provided options: Evaluation of your performance or Evaluation of your abilities.
* This field is required and must be selected.
### Date (Page 6)
* This field is not currently used.

# Tips
* Please fill in the form completely and accurately to ensure that your evaluation information is recorded correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the evaluation process, contact your evaluation administrator.
* Review and agree to both participant and evaluation agreements before proceeding with the evaluation.
* Enter any additional comments or observations if needed to provide a more complete evaluation.
