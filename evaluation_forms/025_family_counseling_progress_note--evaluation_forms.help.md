# Family Counseling Progress Note - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Counseling Progress Note is a form used to document the progress of family members in the counseling process. It is designed to track the effectiveness of the treatment plan and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing an overview of the client's relationship to the family, including the name(s) of the family members involved and their respective roles within the family.
2.  Answer the following questions to assess the client's progress and response to treatment:
   *   family_counseling_progress_question3: Has the client shown improvement
   *   family_counseling_progress_question4: Family Counseling Progress Question4
