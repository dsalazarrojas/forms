# pipeline_program_student_reflection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide students in reflecting on their experience in a pipeline program, helping them identify what they learned, set goals for future progress, and solidify their experience in their own mind.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form to ensure you understand the questions and fields that are being asked.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you can.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions or fields, feel free to ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the program started.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the program ended.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter a brief name of the program you participated in.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the program you attended.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required: false): Check the box next to 'Yes' if the program was of type 'Yes', and 'No' otherwise.
* **Program Outcome** (`program_outcome`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the boxes next to 'Yes', 'No', or other options that apply to indicate the outcome of your program.
* **Other Program Outcome** (`program_outcome_other`, text, required: false): If you selected an option other than 'Yes' or 'No' for Program Outcome, enter a brief description of what you mean by that option.
* **Start Date Month** (`program_start_date_month`, select_one, required: false): Check the box next to 'Yes' if your program started in the month you select, and 'No' otherwise.
* **End Date Month** (`program_end_date_month`, select_one, required: false): Check the box next to 'Yes' if your program ended in the month you select, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Goals** (`goals`, note, required: false): Write a brief description of your goals entering the program, including what you hoped to achieve and what motivated you to participate.
* **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, note, required: false): Write a brief description of what you learned during the program, including any takeaways or insights that will help you in your future endeavors.

Note: Fields marked as `required: false` are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
