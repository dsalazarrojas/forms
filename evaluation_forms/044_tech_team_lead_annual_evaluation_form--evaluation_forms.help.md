# Tech Team Lead Annual Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of a Tech Team Lead annually.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the evaluation cycle you are evaluating.
2. Fill in the overall performance section with a numerical rating.
3. Choose the relevant areas of focus.
4. Select your team's contribution to the organization.
5. Assess your communication skills.
6. Choose the collaboration level.
7. Evaluate your problem-solving skills.
8. Choose how often you receive feedback.
9. Select areas for improvement.
10. Add any additional comments.
11. Choose how often you give feedback.
12. Select areas for improvement.
13. Add your final comments.
14. Choose the evaluation form type.
15. Select a rating scale for your overall performance.
16. Select areas for improvement.
17. Choose a rating scale for your performance.
18. Select the overall rating.
19. Choose the evaluation cycle.
20. Enter the date submitted.
21. Enter the date evaluated.
22. Select who submitted the form.
23. Select who the form is submitted to.
24. Select the reviewer's name.
25. Select the overall rating.
26. Enter the date evaluated.
