# Job Offer Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate job offers from employers, providing a structured way to assess their pros and cons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the job offer details carefully.
2. Fill out the form with relevant information for each field.
3. Answer questions about the offer, such as its job title, location, salary, and employment type.
4. Choose options for select_one and select_multiple fields, like job type, employment type, job status, job recommendation, and company rating.
5. Enter dates for the job start and end dates.
6. Rate the job and provide reasons for your satisfaction.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the offer.
8. Finally, review your completed form for accuracy and completeness.
