# Self Evaluation Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you evaluate your own performance and progress in your role. It will guide you through a series of questions to help you identify your strengths, weaknesses, areas for improvement, and development plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing your name and a brief description of your self-evaluation form.
2. Answer the rating questions with one of the following options:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
3. Select all areas that apply for your performance level:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
4. Answer the open-ended questions with specific examples and thoughts.
5. Submit your completed form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This is the field where you enter your name.
* **self_evaluation_form_description** (`self_evaluation_form_description`, text, optional): This is an optional field for you to add any additional comments or details about your self-evaluation form.
* **rating_scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your overall performance on a scale of 1-4 (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **performance_level** (`performance_level`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas that apply for your performance level (High, Medium, Low).
* **strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): List your strengths and areas where you excel.
* **weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, text, optional): List any areas where you struggle or need improvement.
* **self_reflection** (`self_reflection`, text, optional): Add any additional thoughts or comments about your performance and progress.
* **areas_for_improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Identify specific areas where you need to improve.
* **development_plan** (`development_plan`, text, optional): Create a plan to address your areas for improvement and growth.
* **date_submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated and should be left blank.
* **time_submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): This field is automatically populated and should be left blank.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who submitted the form (Manager, HR, Employee).
* **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your performance and progress.

Note that some fields may not be required, but they are still important for providing additional context and insights to your self-evaluation.
