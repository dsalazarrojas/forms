# Sellers Product Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from sellers about their products. It is intended to be used by sellers to provide more information about their products, allowing customers or evaluators to make more informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and detailed information about your product and your experience as a seller.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you answer all required and optional questions.
3. Save your responses to the form to submit for review and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information (text)**: This field is for providing general information about yourself as a seller, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Product Information (Page 1)
* **Product Information (text)**: In this field, describe your product, including its features, benefits, and any unique selling points. Be as detailed as possible to help evaluators understand your product.

### Evaluation Ratings (Page 1)
* **Evaluation Ratings (text)**: In this field, provide a rating for your product, using the provided options to guide your response. This will help evaluators understand your product's performance and quality.

### Seller Feedback (Page 1)
* **Seller Feedback (text)**: Share any feedback or comments about your experience as a seller, including successes, challenges, and areas for improvement. This will help evaluators understand your approach to selling and customer service.

### Additional Questions (Page 2)
* **Additional Questions (text)**: This field is for any additional questions or concerns that you have about your product or experience as a seller. Be as detailed as possible to help evaluators understand your product and your approach.

## Tips
- Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
- Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
- Review and update your responses regularly to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- Contact the form administrators if you have any questions or concerns about the form or its use.
