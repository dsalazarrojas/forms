# Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from students on their course experiences. It helps instructors and course administrators evaluate the course's effectiveness, content, and overall quality. This feedback is essential to make improvements and ensure that courses meet the needs of students and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best fit your experience with each question.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Enter your email address to ensure that your feedback is received correctly.
4. Submit the form to send your feedback to the course administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: This is a required field that collects your student ID number. Please enter your student ID as displayed on your student ID card or your student account.
* **Course ID**: This is a required field that asks you to select whether you found the course relevant to your studies. Please select "Yes" if you found the course relevant, and "No" otherwise.
* **Evaluation Date**: This is a required field that records the date you took the course. Please select the date when you completed the course.
* **Overall Experience**: This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the course. Please select a number between 1 (Poor) and 5 (Good).
* **Course Quality**: This field asks you to rate the quality of the course content. Please select "Good" or "Poor".
* **Instructor Effectiveness**: This field asks you to rate how effective the instructor was in teaching the course. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Course Materials**: This field asks you to select whether you found the course materials sufficient. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Instructor Clarity**: This field asks you to rate how clear the instructor was in explaining the course content. Please select a number between 1 (Poor) and 5 (Good).
* **Instructor Response Time**: This field asks you to rate how quickly the instructor responded to your questions. Please select a time value (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Course Content**: This field asks you to select whether you found the course content relevant. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Course Organization**: This field asks you to select whether you found the course well-organized. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Course Support**: This field asks you to select whether you received sufficient support from the instructor or course staff. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Course Inclusivity**: This field asks you to select whether you felt included in the course. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Course Relevance**: This field asks you to select whether you found the course relevant to your studies. Please select "Yes" or "No".
* **First Name**: This is a required field that collects your first name.
* **Last Name**: This is a required field that collects your last name.
* **Email**: This is a required field that collects your email address.
* **Department**: This is a required field that collects the department offering the course.
* **Course Title**: This is a required field that collects the title of the course you took.
* **Course Number**: This is a required field that collects the course number.
* **Instructor Title**: This is a required field that collects the title of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Instructor First Name**: This is a required field that collects the first name of the instructor.
* **Instructor Last Name**: This is a required field that collects the last name of the instructor.
* **Instructor Email**: This is a required field that collects the email address of the instructor.

Please take your time to complete this form and provide honest feedback. Your input will help us improve the courses and make them more effective for future students.
