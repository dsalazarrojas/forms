# Lexicographer Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the skills of an individual in areas such as language, phone, and other relevant fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Name" field with your name as it should appear on the form.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. On "Page 2", briefly introduce yourself and describe your background and expertise.
5. Select your "Skills" level as either "Yes" or "No" in the "Skills" field.
6. Choose all the languages you have skills in the "Language Skills" field (select multiple options if applicable).
7. On "Page 3", provide any additional information that might be relevant for further assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name (Text, Optional)
Enter your name as it should appear on the form.

### Email (Email, Optional)
Enter your email address.

### Phone (Text, Optional)
Enter your phone number.

### Page 2 (Text, Optional)
Provide a brief introduction and description of your background and expertise.

### Skills (Select One, Optional)
Select "Yes" to indicate you have skills, "No" otherwise.

### Language Skills (Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose all the languages you have skills in. Select multiple options if applicable.

### Page 3 (Text, Optional)
Provide any additional information that might be relevant for further assessment.
