# Youth Sports Tryout Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Sports Tryout Evaluation form is designed to collect information and feedback from coaches and evaluators during the youth sports tryout process. This form helps to gather relevant details about the player's performance, coach's suggestions, and other essential information.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the relevant information for each section and filling in the necessary details.
2.  Begin with the general information (Page 1) - This page contains a text field where you can provide any general comments or observations about the tryout.
3.  Move on to the player information page (Page 2), where you will be able to enter specific details about the player.
4.  On the player performance page (Page 3), you will be selecting whether the player performed 'Yes' or 'No' for each criteria such as 'Yes' for 'Was the player well-prepared for the tryout?'
5.  Next, you will be able to provide the coach's suggestion for the player on the coach_suggestion field.
6.  On the coach comment page (Page 5), you will be able to enter any additional comments the coach wants to make about the player.
7.  On the scoring page (Page 6), you can score the player's performance using the 'score' field.
8.  Please note the date and time of the tryout on the date field.
9.  Enter any additional comments or notes you have about the player in the note field.
10.  Enter your email address on the email field so that we can contact you for any further follow up.
11.  Enter the player's contact information on the phone field.
12.  Finally, confirm whether the evaluation is complete on the evaluation_complete field by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **General Info (Page 1)**: *This field is for any general comments or observations about the tryout.*
*  **Player Info (Page 2)**: *This field is for entering specific details about the player.*
*  **Player Performance (Page 3)**: *Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each performance criteria.*
*  **Coach Suggestion (Page 4)**: *Enter any suggestions or comments the coach has for the player.*
*  **Coach Comment (Page 5)**: *This field is for any additional comments the coach wants to make about the player.*
*  **Score (Page 6)**: *This field is used to score the player's performance.*
*  **Date (Page 7)**: *Please note the date and time of the tryout.*
*  **Note (Page 8)**: *This field is for any additional comments or notes you have about the player.*
*  **Email (Page 9)**: *Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further follow up.*
*  **Phone (Page 10)**: *Enter the player's contact information.*
*  **Evaluation Complete (Page 11)**: *Confirm whether the evaluation is complete by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.*
