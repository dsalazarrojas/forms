# running_course_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The running_course_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the difficulty level of a running course and their overall satisfaction with the experience. This form helps us understand what worked well and what areas need improvement for future courses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
2. **Enter your Participant Name**: This is the name of the person who took the course.
3. **Enter your Email (if available)**: If you provided your email during course registration, please enter it here.
4. **Rate your overall satisfaction**: On a scale of 1-5, rate how satisfied you are with the course overall.
5. **Assess the difficulty level**: Rate how challenging you found the course on a scale of 1-5.
6. **Share your feedback**: Describe any positive or negative experiences you had during the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, required: false): Your name as the course participant.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): The email you used to register for the course (if applicable).
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **difficulty_level** (`difficulty_level`, number, required: false): Rate the difficulty level of the course, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share any feedback or comments you have about the course.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve future courses.
* If you have any attachments or supporting materials, please attach them to your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us directly.
