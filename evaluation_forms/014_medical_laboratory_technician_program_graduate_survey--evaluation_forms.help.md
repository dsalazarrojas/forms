# Medical Laboratory Technician Program Graduate Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from medical laboratory technician graduates, allowing us to evaluate the effectiveness of our program and identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts and experiences to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions and fill in the fields as instructed. You will be asked to provide your overall experience in the program, your satisfaction with the program's quality, your favorite and least favorite courses, your ratings of the program's curriculum, and your suggestions for improvement. Additionally, you will be asked to provide demographic information and describe your career goals and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience in the program. This section is an opportunity to share your honest feedback on the program's quality and relevance to your career goals.
* **Program Quality** (`program_quality`, number, required): Rate the program's quality on a scale of 1-5. A rating of 1 indicates a very poor quality, while a rating of 5 indicates an excellent quality.
* **Favorite Courses** (`favorite_courses`, text, required): List the courses or activities you enjoyed the most in the program. This section is an opportunity to highlight the programs that had a significant impact on your learning and career development.
* **Challenging Courses** (`challenging_courses`, text, required): List the courses or activities you found most challenging in the program. This section is an opportunity to identify areas where you struggled or needed more support.
* **Curriculum Rating** (`curriculum_rating`, number, required): Rate the program's curriculum on a scale of 1-5. A rating of 1 indicates a very poor curriculum, while a rating of 5 indicates an excellent curriculum.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Provide any suggestions or comments you have for improving the program. This section is an opportunity to offer constructive feedback and help us improve the program.
* **Career Preparedness** (`career_preparedness`, text, required): Please provide an honest assessment of how the program prepared you for your career as a medical laboratory technician. This section is an opportunity to evaluate the program's effectiveness in preparing you for your future career.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, note, required): Please provide your demographic information, such as age, gender, and location. This information will help us better understand the diversity of our graduate population.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, required): Describe your short-term and long-term career goals as a medical laboratory technician. This section is an opportunity to outline your aspirations and goals for your future career.
* **Resources and Support** (`resources_support`, text, required): List any resources or services that were particularly helpful or valuable during your time in the program. This section is an opportunity to highlight any support systems that aided your learning and career development.
* **Favorite Activities** (`favorite_activities`, text, optional): Describe the activities or projects that you enjoyed the most in the program. This section is an opportunity to reflect on your learning experiences and highlight any particularly memorable moments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any further comments or suggestions for program improvement. This section is an opportunity to offer additional feedback and help us improve the program.
