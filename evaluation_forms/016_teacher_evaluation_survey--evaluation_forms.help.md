# Teacher Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Evaluation Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from students about their experiences with a teacher. This feedback helps to assess the teacher's performance, teaching skills, and overall ability to communicate effectively with students.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to answer the questions carefully and honestly.
- Select the relevant option(s) that best describe your experience with the teacher.
- You can skip any question that does not apply to your situation.
- Make sure to provide specific examples or details when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Feedback (Note)**:
	+ Label: general_feedback
	+ Description: Share your overall impression of the teacher's performance.
* **Teacher Performance (Text)**:
	+ Label: teacher_performance
	+ Description: Describe the teacher's performance in the classroom, including their ability to engage students, manage the class, and overall teaching style.
* **Teaching Skills (Select Multiple)**:
	+ Label: teaching_skills
	+ Description: Rate the teacher's skills in the following areas: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor. Select all that apply.
	+ Example: If you want to say the teacher is "Excellent" in teaching skills, but "Fair" in communication skills, select both options.
* **Communication Skills (Text)**:
	+ Label: communication_skills
	+ Description: Describe how effectively the teacher communicates with students, including their ability to explain complex concepts, provide clear instructions, and create a positive classroom environment.
