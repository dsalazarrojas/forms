# Bachelor Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect evaluations of bachelor's programs to help us better understand their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, providing the best evaluation based on your experience.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name**: Please enter the name of the student whose program you are evaluating (optional).
* **Program name**: Enter the name of the degree program being evaluated (required). This will help us associate your evaluation with the specific program.
* **Evaluation period**: Describe the period during which you were evaluating the program (required). For example, "2025-2026 academic year".
* **Curriculum relevance rating**: Rate the relevance of the curriculum to the field or industry (required). This scale is from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Teaching quality**: Evaluate the quality of instruction provided by the program (required). Choose from Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **Academic support services**: Rate the availability and usefulness of academic support services (optional). Choose from Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **Resources and facilities**: Rate the quality of resources and facilities provided by the program (optional). Choose from Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **Career services effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of career services provided by the program (optional). Choose from Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent.
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (required). This scale is from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have (optional).

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your evaluation.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
- Your feedback is invaluable and will help us improve the program. Thank you for participating!
