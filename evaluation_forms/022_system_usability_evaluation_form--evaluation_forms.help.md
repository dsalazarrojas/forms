# System Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The System Usability Evaluation Form is designed to collect user feedback on the usability of our system. This form is intended to help us understand how well our system meets the needs and expectations of its users. Your input will help us improve the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Respond to the questions honestly and concisely.
2. Take about 10-15 minutes to complete the form.
3. Please provide as much detail as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How did you hear about us? (page_1)
* **How did you hear about us?** (`page_1`, text, optional): This question is meant to help us understand how you initially learned about our system. Please provide the source of your knowledge (e.g., friend, social media, online search).

### What was your first impression? (page_2)
* **What was your first impression?** (`page_2`, text, optional): Share your initial thoughts and feelings when you first interacted with our system. Try to be as detailed as possible, as this will help us understand how to improve the user experience.

### Can you explain what you were trying to accomplish? (page_3)
* **Can you explain what you were trying to accomplish?** (`page_3`, text, optional): Describe your goals or tasks when you used our system. This will help us understand how our system meets (or misses) your needs.

### Was it easy to find what you needed? (page_4)
* **Was it easy to find what you needed?** (`page_4`, number, optional): On a scale of 1-5, rate how easy or difficult it was to find the information or features you were looking for. A higher score indicates that it was easier to find what you needed.

### How would you rate the overall usability experience? (page_5)
* **How would you rate the overall usability experience?** (`page_5`, time, optional): Estimate the time it took you to complete your task or achieve your goal. This will help us understand the efficiency of our system.

### Can you give us some feedback? (page_6)
* **Can you give us some feedback?** (`page_6`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
	+ Feedback 1
	+ Feedback 2
	+ Feedback 3

### Can we contact you? (page_7)
* **Can we contact you?** (`page_7`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide additional feedback or discuss your experience further, please share your contact information.

### What is your contact information? (page_8)
* **What is your contact information?** (`page_8`, email, optional): If you're willing, please provide your email address so we can reach out to follow up on your feedback.

### Did you have any difficulty with our website? (page_9)
* **Did you have any difficulty with our website?** (`page_9`, text, optional): If you encountered any issues or difficulties while using our system, please describe them in detail so we can improve the user experience.
