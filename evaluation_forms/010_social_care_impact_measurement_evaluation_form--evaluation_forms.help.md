# Social Care Impact Measurement Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Care Impact Measurement Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and satisfaction of social care services provided to users. Your input is invaluable in helping us understand the impact of these services and make improvements for better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully, as some fields are optional but all contribute to providing a comprehensive understanding of your experience.
2. Provide accurate and honest information in each field, as this will help us tailor our services to better meet your needs.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service User Information**: Please provide a brief description of the service you interacted with. This will help us understand your context.
* **Service User (Email)**: If applicable, please enter your email address associated with the service. This will help us identify and follow up with you if necessary.
* **Service Provider**: Select the type of service provider that served you (Social Care Agency, Nonprofit, or Other).
* **Service Provider Name**: Provide the name of the service provider, if applicable.
* **Service Provider Phone**: If you have the service provider's phone number, please enter it. This will help us reach them if needed.
* **Service User Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Reasons for Satisfaction**: Choose all reasons that apply to why you're satisfied with the service.
* **Detailed Feedback**: Share any specific feedback, compliments, or concerns you have about the service. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve!

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any additional questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Your participation in this evaluation form will greatly contribute to the improvement of our social care services.

This guide should help users understand the form's purpose, complete it accurately, and provide valuable feedback to improve social care services.
