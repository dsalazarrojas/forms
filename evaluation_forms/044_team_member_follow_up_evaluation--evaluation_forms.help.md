# Team Member Follow Up Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and performance data on team members after a regular review.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For rating fields (e.g., Strengths, Performance Rating), select the relevant option that best describes the team member's abilities.
3. For text fields (e.g., Comments, Next Steps), enter your observations and notes in a clear and concise manner.
4. Date fields (e.g., Date of Review, Date of Last Review) should be entered in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Time fields (e.g., Time of Review) should be entered in the format HH:MM:SS.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Follow-Up Evaluation** (Page 1): Provide a brief overview of the team member's performance and any notable events that occurred during the review period.
* **Performance Feedback** (Page 2): Share your feedback on the team member's performance, including any areas of strength and areas for improvement.
* **Strengths** (Page 3): Select the strengths you have observed in the team member's performance, such as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Next Steps** (Page 3): Describe the actions the team member should take to improve their performance and grow professionally.
* **Comments** (Page 3): Enter any additional comments or observations about the team member's performance.
* **Date of Review** (Page 4): Enter the date of the regular review, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Review** (Page 4): Enter the time of the regular review, in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Email** (Page 4): Enter the team member's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Page 4): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **Team Member Follow-Up Evaluation** (Page 5): Provide a brief summary of the team member's performance and any next steps for growth and development.
* **Performance Rating** (Page 5): Rate the team member's performance as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Overall Rating** (Page 5): Rate the team member's overall performance as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Team Leadership Skills** (Page 5): Rate the team member's leadership skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Communication Skills** (Page 5): Rate the team member's communication skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Problem Solving Skills** (Page 5): Rate the team member's problem-solving skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Collaboration Skills** (Page 5): Rate the team member's collaboration skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Adaptability Skills** (Page 5): Rate the team member's adaptability skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Initiative** (Page 5): Rate the team member's initiative as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Feedback** (Page 6): Enter any feedback you have for the team member.
* **Action Plan** (Page 6): Create an action plan for the team member to improve their performance.
* **Follow-Up Plan** (Page 6): Create a follow-up plan to check on the team member's progress.
* **Conducted By** (Page 6): Select who conducted the review (Manager, HR, Peer, or Team Member).
* **Date of Last Review** (Page 6): Enter the date of the last regular review, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Last Review** (Page 6): Enter the time of the last regular review, in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Next Meeting** (Page 6): Enter the date and time of the next review, in the format mm/dd/yyyy and HH:MM:SS).

Note: Some fields may not be required, but all fields will be displayed for your convenience.
