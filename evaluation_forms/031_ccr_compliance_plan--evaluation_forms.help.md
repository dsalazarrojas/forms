# Skyview Estates CCR Compliance Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the Skyview Estates community's compliance with its Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions (CCRs).

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the Skyview Estates CCRs and complete this form.
2. Enter your name as the homeowner.
3. Enter your property address.
4. Answer the questions regarding landscaping, paint and siding condition, parking, trash and recycling bin storage, signage, and fence condition.
5. If there are any areas on your property not compliant with the CCRs, describe your plan to address them.
6. Enter a target date for full compliance.
7. If you need to submit a project for Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval, indicate this and proceed with your project plans.
8. Acknowledge and agree to have reviewed the CCRs by signing and selecting "I Acknowledge and Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner Full Name**: Enter your name as the homeowner of the property.
* **Property Address**: Enter your property's address.
* **Lot Number (if known)**: If you know your lot number, enter it; otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Landscaping and Lawn Maintenance**: Check "Compliant" if your lawn is well-maintained, free of weeds and overgrowth.
* **Exterior Paint and Siding Condition**: Check "Compliant" if your exterior paint and siding are in good condition.
* **Parking and Vehicle Storage**: Check "Compliant" if vehicles are parked in the garage or driveway and not on the street.
* **Trash and Recycling Bin Storage**: Check "Compliant" if trash and recycling bins are stored out of sight when not at the curb.
* **Signage and Holiday Decorations**: Check "Compliant" if your decorations and signage are within the community's timelines.
* **Fence Condition and Height**: Check "Compliant" if your fence is structurally sound and made of approved materials.
* **Description of Remedial Actions**: If any area of your property is not compliant with the CCRs, describe your plan to fix it.
* **Target Date for Full Compliance**: Enter a target date for completing any necessary repairs or improvements.
* **Do you need to submit a project for Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval?**: Check "True" if you need ARC approval for structural changes, painting, or major landscaping.
* **Homeowner Digital Acknowledgment**: Select "I Acknowledge and Agree" to confirm that you have reviewed the CCRs and understand your responsibility to maintain your property's compliance.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
