# Focus Group Screener Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Focus Group Screener Form is designed to collect demographic and employment information from focus group participants. This form helps to gather relevant data to understand the characteristics of the focus group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Answer the employment status, household income, age, years of service, education level, language spoken, race/ethnicity, gender, years of experience, occupation, marital status, and education level sections.
4. Ensure you've answered all the questions accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_info (text)**: This field is for you to provide your participant information, such as name and ID.
* **contact_info (text)**: Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **address (text)**: Enter your address.
* **employment_status (select_one)**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **household_income (select_multiple)**: Select the range of your household income from the options provided.
* **age (number)**: Enter your age.
* **years_of_service (number)**: Enter your years of service.
* **education_level (select_one)**: Select your education level from the options provided.
* **language_spoken (select_multiple)**: Select the languages you speak.
* **race_ethnicity (select_multiple)**: Select your race/ethnicity from the options provided.
* **gender (select_one)**: Select your gender.
* **years_of_experience (number)**: Enter your years of experience.
* **occupation (text)**: Enter your occupation.
* **marital_status (select_one)**: Select your marital status.
* **education_level_2 (select_one)**: Select your education level from the options provided.
* **years_of_service_2 (number)**: Enter your years of service.
* **years_of_service_3 (number)**: Enter your years of service.
* **years_of_service_4 (number)**: Enter your years of service.
* **education_level_3 (select_one)**: Select your education level from the options provided.
