# Role Play Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Play Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of actors in role plays, providing a structured evaluation framework for assessors to provide feedback on their performance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the role being played.
2. Indicate whether the role was played by a "Yes" or "No".
3. Identify the location where the role was played.
4. Select multiple "Yes" or "No" options to identify specific locations where the role was played.
5. Enter the date the role was played.
6. Enter the time the role was played.
7. Rate your performance on a scale (this field is currently not functional and will be removed).
8. Enter any comments you would like to make regarding your performance.
9. Enter any comments the assessor would like to make regarding your performance.
10. Enter your email address, so that your form completion can be tracked.
11. Enter the date and time you completed this form.
12. Enter the time you completed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role Title** (form_title, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the role being played.
* **Role Description** (role_description, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the role being played.
* **Role Actor** (role_actor, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the role was played by "Yes" or "No".
* **Role Location** (role_location, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple "Yes" or "No" options to identify specific locations where the role was played.
* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, required: false): Enter the date the role was played.
* **Evaluation Time** (evaluation_time, time, required: false): Enter the time the role was played.
* **Score** (score, number, required: false): This field is currently not functional and will be removed.
* **Comments** (comments, note, required: false): Enter any comments you would like to make regarding your performance.
* **Assessor Comments** (assessor_comments, note, required: false): Enter any comments the assessor would like to make regarding your performance.
* **Form Completed By** (form_completed_by, email, required: false): Enter your email address, so that your form completion can be tracked.
* **Form Completed At** (form_completed_at, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you completed this form.
* **Form Completed Time** (form_completed_time, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed this form.
