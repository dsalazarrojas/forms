# Morning Routine Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Morning Routine Evaluation Form is a tool to help you assess your morning habits and provide recommendations on how to improve your overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the options that best describe your morning experiences for each field.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, but do not overfill the text fields.
3. Submit your answers when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sleep_schedule** (Regular, Irregular, Unknown) - How do you usually schedule your sleep? (Regular: You follow a consistent sleep schedule, Irregular: Your sleep schedule varies, Unknown: You're not sure)
* **wake_up_time** (time) - What time do you usually wake up in the morning?
* **morning_feelings** (note) - How do you feel in the morning? (Please describe your thoughts and emotions)
* **exercise_frequency** (Daily, 2-3 times a week, Rarely) - How often do you exercise in the morning?
* **morning_activities** (Breakfast, Exercise, Meditation, Other) - What activities do you do in the morning? (Select all that apply)
* **breakfast_time** (On time, Late, Skipped) - Do you usually have breakfast on time?
* **caffeine_consumption** (Low, Moderate, High) - How much caffeine do you consume in the morning?
* **screen_time** (Less than 30, 30-60, More than 60) - How much time do you spend on screens in the morning?
* **wakefulness** (Easy to wake up, Difficult, Not a morning person) - How easy or difficult is it for you to wake up in the morning?
* **alertness** (Alert, Not alert, Somewhat alert) - How alert do you feel in the morning?
* **alertness_level** (number) - How much alertness do you feel? (Scale: 1-10)
* **alertness_level_how_to_improve** (Sleep hygiene, Exercise, Caffeine, Other) - How can you improve your alertness?
* **sleep_quality** (Good, Fair, Poor) - How would you rate the quality of your sleep?
* **sleep_quality_how_to_improve** (Sleep schedule, Exercise, Caffeine, Other) - How can you improve your sleep quality?
* **wakefulness_how_to_improve** (Regular sleep schedule, Morning exercise, Caffeine, Other) - How can you improve your wakefulness?
* **morning_routine** (text) - What is your current morning routine?
* **morning_routine_how_to_improve** (text) - How can you improve your morning routine?
* **exercise_frequency_improve** (Regular, Irregular, Not applicable) - How can you improve your exercise frequency?
* **exercise_duration** (15-30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, More than 60 minutes) - How long do you usually exercise in the morning?
* **exercise_frequency_improve** (Regular, Irregular, Not applicable) - How can you improve your exercise frequency?
* **sleep_duration** (number) - How many hours do you sleep each night?
* **wake_up_time_how_to_improve** (Regular wake up time, Early, Late) - How can you improve your wake-up time?
* **sleep_time** (On time, Late, Skipped) - When do you usually go to sleep at night?
