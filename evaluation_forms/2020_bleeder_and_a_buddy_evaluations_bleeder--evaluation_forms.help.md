# 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants in the 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder program. The collected information will help us identify areas of improvement and better understand your experience with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your email address for follow-up communications.
3. Enter your participant ID, if applicable.
4. Select the date of your evaluation.
5. For the "Evaluation Section", rate your satisfaction, overall rating, and ease of use.
6. Select all aspects that applied to your experience with the 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder in the "Experience Aspects" section.
7. Rate the quality of interaction with your buddy.
8. In the "Improvement Suggestions" section, provide specific suggestions on what you would like to improve or change about the program.
9. If any feature stood out as particularly helpful or unhelpful, please specify in the "Most helpful/least helpful feature" section.
10. Finally, rate how likely you are to recommend the program to others and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name for reference purposes.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We'll use this for follow-up communications. Please ensure it's accurate.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your assigned identification number, if applicable.
* **Date of evaluation** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your evaluation.
* **How satisfied are you with 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate your overall experience with the 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder on a scale of 1-10.
* **Ease of use rating** (`ease_of_use_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy or difficult it was to use the 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder.
* **Aspects applied to your experience** (`experience_aspects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all aspects that applied to your experience with the 2020 Bleeder And A Buddy Evaluations Bleeder.
* **Quality of interaction with your buddy** (`buddy_interaction_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of interaction with your buddy.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`open_feedback`, `text`, optional): Please provide specific suggestions on what you would like to improve or change about the program.
* **Most helpful feature** (`most_helpful_feature`, `text`, optional): If any feature stood out as particularly helpful, specify it here.
* **Least helpful feature** (`least_helpful_feature`, `text`, optional): If any feature stood out as particularly unhelpful, specify it here.
* **Likelihood of recommendation** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `integer`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend the program to others.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other feedback you'd like to share.
* **Participate in a follow-up survey** (`contact_for_followup`, `select_one`, optional): Would you be willing to participate in a follow-up survey?
