# Excavator Operator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the skills and performance of an excavator operator during an evaluation session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and truthfully.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
3. Use the available space to provide as much detail as necessary to support your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Excavator Operator Evaluation Form** (`excavator_operator_evaluation_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form. It's not required but can be filled out for reference.
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person being evaluated.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID number of the employee being evaluated.
* **Excavator type** (`excavator_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of excavator being used.
* **Evaluation date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation session.
* **Evaluation time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation session.
* **Evaluation rating** (`evaluation_rating`, text, optional): Provide a rating (e.g. "Excellent", "Good", etc.) of the operator's performance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Use this space to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the person or team assigned to the operator.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the specific tool or equipment used by the operator.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Specify the category of the evaluation (e.g. "Construction", "Mining", etc.).
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, text, optional): Enter a subcategory of the evaluation (e.g. "Site preparation", "Drilling", etc.).
* **Evaluation comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the operator's performance.
* **Evaluation result** (`evaluation_result`, text, optional): Enter the final result of the evaluation (e.g. "Pass", "Fail", etc.).
* **Evaluated by** (`evaluated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Evaluated on** (`evaluated_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluated at** (`evaluated_at`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation session.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Form submitted at** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and comprehensive information will help ensure a fair and accurate evaluation.
