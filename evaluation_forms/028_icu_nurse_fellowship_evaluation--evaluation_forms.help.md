# ICU Nurse Fellowship Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of the ICU Nurse Fellowship program. It is meant to capture feedback from participants on their experience with the program and help identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the evaluation period in the "Evaluation Period" field, which is a required date field.
2. If you have worked with the preceptor before, select "True" from the "Have you worked with this preceptor before?" dropdown menu.
3. If you are a Program Coordinator, select "True" from the "Are you a Program Coordinator?" dropdown menu.
4. Choose how often evaluations are performed for this program from the "How often are evaluations performed for this program?" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the evaluation due date in the "What is the evaluation due date?" field, which is a required date field.
6. Provide comments on your evaluation in the "What are your evaluation comments for this feedback?" text field.
7. Rate the program by entering a number in the "What rating would you give to this program?" field.
8. Enter the date for your next evaluation in the "What are your next evaluation plans?" field.
9. If you will evaluate this program again, select "True" from the "How often will you evaluate this program?" dropdown menu.
10. Provide additional comments on your evaluation in the "What are your evaluation comments?" text field.
11. Rate the program again by entering a number in the "Evaluation Rating 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation period.
* **Nurse Educator** (`nurse_educator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nurse educator you worked with.
* **Have you worked with this preceptor before?** (`worked_with_preceptor`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you have worked with the preceptor before, "False" otherwise.
* **Are you a Program Coordinator?** (`program_coordinator`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are a Program Coordinator, "False" otherwise.
* **How often are evaluations performed for this program?** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often evaluations are performed for this program (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **What is the evaluation due date?** (`evaluation_due_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which the evaluation is due.
* **What are your evaluation comments for this feedback?** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Provide comments on your evaluation.
* **What rating would you give to this program?** (`evaluation_rating`, number, optional): Rate the program by entering a number.
* **What are your next evaluation plans?** (`next_evaluation`, date, optional): Enter the date for your next evaluation.
* **How often will you evaluate this program?** (`evaluation_frequency_2`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you will evaluate this program again, "False" otherwise.
* **What are your evaluation comments?** (`evaluation_comments_2`, text, optional): Provide additional comments on your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Rating 2** (`evaluation_rating_2`, number, optional): Rate the program again by entering a number.

## Tips

* Be thorough and honest in your evaluation to help improve the ICU Nurse Fellowship program.
* Use clear and concise language to provide feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your program coordinator.
