# Job Hazard Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help analyze potential hazards for a job task. It is a crucial tool for identifying and assessing hazards that could pose a risk to an employee's safety and well-being on the job site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Job Hazard Form ID**: Provide a unique identifier for the job hazard analysis form (e.g., "JHA-001").
2. **Job Hazard Form Version**: Enter the version number of the job hazard analysis form (e.g., "1.0").
3. **Job Hazard Form Date**: Select the date the form was completed.
4. **Job Hazard Form Time**: Select the time the form was completed.
5. **Job Hazard Form Description**: Provide a brief description of the job task being analyzed.
6. **Job Hazard Form Hazards**: Indicate whether there are any hazards associated with the job task. Select "Yes" or "No" for each hazard.
7. **Job Hazard Form Strategies**: Describe any strategies or controls used to mitigate or eliminate the hazards identified.
8. **Job Hazard Form Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for future improvements or enhancements to prevent hazards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Hazard Form ID** (`job_hazard_form_id`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide a unique identifier for the job hazard analysis form.
- **Job Hazard Form Version** (`job_hazard_form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the form to track changes over time.
- **Job Hazard Form Date** (`job_hazard_form_date`, date, optional): Select the date the form was completed to ensure accurate tracking of when the analysis was performed.
- **Job Hazard Form Time** (`job_hazard_form_time`, time, optional): Choose the time the form was completed to record the timing of the analysis.
- **Job Hazard Form Description** (`job_hazard_form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job task being analyzed, including any relevant details that could impact hazard identification.
- **Job Hazard Form Hazards** (`job_hazard_form_hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for each hazard identified, indicating its presence or absence.
- **Job Hazard Form Strategies** (`job_hazard_form_strategies`, text, optional): Describe any strategies or controls used to mitigate or eliminate the hazards identified.
- **Job Hazard Form Recommendations** (`job_hazard_form_recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations for future improvements or enhancements to prevent hazards.

## Tips
- Be as thorough as possible when identifying hazards and describing strategies to prevent them.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Update the form regularly to reflect changes to the job task or hazard profile.
- Review and revise the form as needed to ensure accuracy and effectiveness.
- Consult with relevant stakeholders, such as supervisors, safety experts, or colleagues, to ensure comprehensive analysis and effective mitigation of hazards.
