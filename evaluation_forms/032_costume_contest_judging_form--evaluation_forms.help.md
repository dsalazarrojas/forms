# Costume Contest Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The costume contest judging form is used to evaluate costumes in a contest. Judges will use this form to provide feedback and ratings for each costume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form once per judge. If you're evaluating multiple costumes, fill out a separate form for each one.
2. Enter your judge ID number. This is a unique identifier that helps us keep track of your scores and comments.
3. Enter the name of the costume being evaluated.
4. Provide a numerical score for the costume. This is a rating out of 10 or other scale you're using. Please see the evaluation guidelines for more information on the scoring system.
5. Provide a comment or review of the costume. Keep your comment concise and specific, but feel free to include any relevant information or feedback you'd like to share with the contestants.
6. Enter the ID number of the costume. This is the unique identifier for the costume you're evaluating.
7. Enter the category ID number of the costume. This is the group the costume belongs to (e.g., best overall, best costume in a category).
8. Enter the subcategory ID number of the costume. This is a specific subcategory within the category (e.g., most creative use of materials, best costume for adults).
9. Check the "is favorite" option if you believe this costume is a standout or favorite among the contestants. This can be a subjective decision, but please use your best judgment.
10. Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Judge ID** (1): Enter your unique judge ID number to help us track your scores and comments.
* **Name** (2): Enter the name of the costume being evaluated.
* **Score** (3): Provide a numerical score for the costume out of 10 or your chosen scale.
* **Comment** (4): Write a brief comment or review of the costume.
* **Costume ID** (5): Enter the unique identifier for the costume you're evaluating.
* **Category ID** (6): Enter the category the costume belongs to (e.g., best overall, best costume in a category).
* **Subcategory ID** (7): Enter the subcategory the costume belongs to (e.g., most creative use of materials, best costume for adults).
* **Is Favorite** (8): Check this option if you believe this costume is a standout or favorite among the contestants.
