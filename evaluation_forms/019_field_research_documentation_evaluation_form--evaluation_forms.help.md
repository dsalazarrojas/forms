# Field Research Documentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Research Documentation Evaluation Form is designed to gather detailed information about field research projects. It's a flexible and customizable form that allows users to provide a clear and concise description of their research projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. In the **Field Research Documentation Evaluation Form** field, simply type the title of your project or research paper.
2. In the **Category** field, select the category that best describes your research project (e.g., Evaluation Forms).
3. In the **Description** field, provide a brief description of your research project.
4. In the **Assigned Tool** field, select the tool you've used in your research project (e.g., chatjimmy, or other).
5. In the **Field 1**, **Field 2**, **Field 3**, and **Field 4** fields, enter the relevant information about your research project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field 1:** Field 1 (text): A text field for entering information about Field 1. This field is optional.
* **Field 2:** Field 2 (text): A text field for entering information about Field 2. This field is optional.
* **Field 3:** Field 3 (text): A text field for entering information about Field 3. This field is optional.
* **Field 4:** Field 4 (text): A text field for entering information about Field 4. This field is optional.
* **Category (Evaluation Forms/Other):** Category: A dropdown field for selecting the category of your research project.
* **Description (A brief description of the field research project):** Description: A text field for entering a brief description of your research project.
* **Assigned Tool (chatjimmy/other):** Assigned Tool: A dropdown field for selecting the tool you've used in your research project.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all the required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the **Description** field.
* Use the **Assigned Tool** field to select the tool that best fits your research project.
* If you're unsure about what to enter in the **Field 1**, **Field 2**, **Field 3**, and **Field 4** fields, simply enter the information you have, and you can always add more details later.
