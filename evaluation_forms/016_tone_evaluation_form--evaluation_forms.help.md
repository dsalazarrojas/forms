The Tone Evaluation Form is used to provide feedback on a piece of writing and offer suggestions for improvement. It's designed to assess the overall effectiveness of the writing style, communication, and tone. This form ensures consistency in writing and communication standards across the team or organization. It is intended for evaluators to review and evaluate writing samples submitted by writers. 

# Tone Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tone Evaluation Form is used to provide feedback on a piece of writing and suggest improvements for better writing and communication style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the "Writing Style" and "Communication Style" sections to describe the writing style and communication style of the piece of writing.
2. Provide specific feedback on what the writer could improve on in the "Feedback" section.
3. Select the tone of the writing from the "Tone" section.
4. In the "Suggestions for improvement" section, be specific and provide concrete suggestions for the writer.
5. Select the overall quality of communication in the "Communication Skills" section and the overall clarity of the writing in the "Clarity" and "Completeness" sections.
6. In the "Feedback to the Writer" section, provide feedback on how to improve their writing and communication style.
7. In the "Overall Tone" section, describe the overall tone of the piece of writing.
8. Select the overall effectiveness of the writing in the "Tone Evaluation" section.
9. Enter the date you completed the evaluation in the "Date Submitted", "Time Spent Reading", "Date Evaluation Completed", and "Time Evaluation Completed" sections.
10. Enter the email and phone number of the writer if necessary.
11. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" section.
12. Enter your name and the name of the form completed by in the "User Name" and "Form Completed By" sections.
13. Enter your unique identifier in the "User ID" and "Form ID" sections.
14. Enter your email in the "User Email" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Writing Style** (`writing_style`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section allows you to evaluate the writing style of the piece of writing. Select all that apply from Friendly, Objective, Informal, and Formal.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section allows you to evaluate the communication style of the piece of writing. Select all that apply from Clear and concise, Unclear and confusing, Too short, and Too long.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing specific examples of what the writer could improve on.
* **Tone** (`tone`, `select_one`, required: false): This section allows you to select the overall tone of the piece of writing from Positive, Negative, and Neutral.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing specific and concrete suggestions for the writer.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section allows you to evaluate the overall quality of communication in the piece of writing. Select all that apply from Strong, Weak.
* **Clarity** (`clarity`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section allows you to evaluate the overall clarity of the writing in the piece of writing. Select all that apply from Clear, Not clear.
* **Completeness** (`completeness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section allows you to evaluate the overall completeness of the writing in the piece of writing. Select all that apply from Complete, Not complete.
* **Feedback to the Writer** (`feedback_to_writer`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing feedback on how to improve their writing and communication style.
* **Overall Tone** (`overall_tone`, `text`, required: false): This section is for describing the overall tone of the piece of writing.
* **Tone Evaluation** (`tone_evaluation`, `select_one`, required: false): This section allows you to evaluate the overall quality of the writing in terms of tone from Good, Needs improvement.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): This section is for entering the date you completed the evaluation.
* **Time Spent Reading** (`time_spent_reading`, `time`, required: false): This section is for entering the time spent reading the piece of writing.
* **Date Evaluation Completed** (`date_evaluation_completed`, `date`, required: false): This section is for entering the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Time Evaluation Completed** (`time_evaluation_completed`, `time`, required: false): This section is for entering the time the evaluation was completed.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, `number`, required: false): This section is for entering your unique identifier.
* **Form Completed By** (`form_completed_by`, `text`, required: false): This section is for entering the name of the form completed by.
* **Evaluation Completed** (`evaluation_completed`, `date`, required: false): This section is for entering the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required: false): This section is for entering your unique identifier for the form.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): This section is for entering your name.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required: false): This section is for entering your email.

Note: This guide is for general guidance, and the actual fields and their requirements might vary based on the specific implementation of the form. 

## Tips

* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* Evaluate the writing sample based on its content and purpose.
* Consider the context and the target audience of the writing when evaluating it.
* Keep the feedback and suggestions focused on improving the writing and communication style rather than personal opinions or feelings.
* Review and revise your feedback and suggestions before submitting the evaluation form.
