# Employee Time Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Time Management Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate an employee's ability to effectively manage their time. It assesses various aspects of time management, including work hours, task duration, self-evaluation, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the employee whose time management skills you wish to evaluate.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required".
3. Select the relevant options for each field according to your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (employee-name)** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
*   **Position (position)** (`position`, select_one, optional): Select the employee's position or role.
*   **Work Hours per Week (work-hours-per-week)** (`work_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the employee works per week.
*   **Work Duration per Task (work-duration-per-task)** (`work-duration-per-task`, number, optional): Enter the duration of each task the employee works on.
*   **Time Management Skill (time-management-skill)** (`time-management-skill`, select_multiple, optional): Select the employee's level of skill in time management.
*   **Time Management Score (time-management-score)** (`time-management-score`, number, optional): Enter a score out of a hundred to assess the employee's time management skills.
*   **Last Workday (work-duration-last-workday)** (`work-duration-last-workday`, select_one, optional): Select the day of the week the employee last worked.
*   **Time Management Effectiveness (time-management-effectiveness)** (`time-management-effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Select the level of effectiveness in time management.
*   **Last Break (work-duration-last-break)** (`work-duration-last-break`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the last break taken.
*   **Employee Time Management Plan (employee-time-management-plan)** (`employee-time-management-plan`, note, optional): Enter any relevant notes or plans for improving time management.
*   **Evaluation Period (evaluation-period)** (`evaluation_period`, date, optional): Select the start date of the evaluation period.
*   **Evaluation Duration (evaluation-duration)** (`evaluation_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the evaluation period.
*   **Employee Satisfaction (employee-satisfaction)** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select the level of satisfaction the employee feels with their current time management.
*   **Evaluation Period Start Date (evaluation-period-start-date)** (`evaluation_period_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
*   **Evaluation Duration Start Time (evaluation-duration-start-time)** (`evaluation_duration_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the evaluation period.
*   **Evaluation Period End Date (evaluation-period-end-date)** (`evaluation_period_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
*   **Evaluation Duration End Time (evaluation-duration-end-time)** (`evaluation_duration_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the evaluation period.
*   **Manager Evaluation (manager-evaluation)** (`manager_evaluation`, select_one, optional): Select the manager's evaluation of the employee's time management skills.
*   **Feedback for Manager (feedback-for-manager)** (`feedback_for_manager`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments for the manager's evaluation.
*   **Evaluation By Date (evaluation-by-date)** (`evaluation_by_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the manager or team lead, and is intended to be a supportive tool for employee development.
