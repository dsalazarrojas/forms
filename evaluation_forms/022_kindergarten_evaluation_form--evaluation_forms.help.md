# Kindergarten Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Evaluation Form is a comprehensive assessment tool used to evaluate a student's performance in various areas during the kindergarten year. The form provides a framework for teachers to provide feedback and evaluations on a student's growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for each page.
2. For each field, enter the student's score or comments as instructed.
3. Review each page carefully to ensure you are providing thorough and accurate feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's full name.
* **student_id** (`student_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **teacher_comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback you have for the student.
* **kindergarten_level** (`kindergarten_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the student's current kindergarten level (e.g., High, Low).
* **math_score** (`math_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for mathematics (on a scale of 1-10).
* **reading_score** (`reading_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for reading (on a scale of 1-10).
* **creativity_score** (`creativity_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for creativity (on a scale of 1-10).
* **problem_solving_score** (`problem_solving_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for problem-solving (on a scale of 1-10).
* **communication_skills_score** (`communication_skills_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for communication skills (on a scale of 1-10).
* **social_skills_score** (`social_skills_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for social skills (on a scale of 1-10).
* **emotional_maturity_score** (`emotional_maturity_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score for emotional maturity (on a scale of 1-10).
* **art_score** (`art_score`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the student's art skills.
* **music_score** (`music_score`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the student's music skills.
* **physical_education_score** (`physical_education_score`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the student's physical education skills.
* **character_score** (`character_score`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the student's character.
* **science_score** (`science_score`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the student's science skills.
