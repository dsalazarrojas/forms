# Civil Service Employee Self-Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help civil service employees evaluate their performance over a specific review period, providing a systematic and objective way to assess strengths and weaknesses, set goals for improvement, and facilitate growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name and employee ID in the designated fields.
2. Select your department/agency from the drop-down menu.
3. Specify the review period (e.g., Jan-Dec 2025).
4. Rate your performance in each category (1-5) and provide a brief explanation for each rating.
5. List your major achievements during this period.
6. Describe the biggest challenges you faced during this period.
7. Identify your primary goals for the next review period.
8. If applicable, mention specific training or development areas you would like to explore.
9. Indicate whether you would like to schedule a one-on-one review meeting with your supervisor.
10. Date and submit the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`): This is the full name of the employee completing the evaluation form.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): This is the unique identification number assigned to the employee.
* **Department/Agency** (`department_name`): Select your department or agency from the list provided.
* **Review Period** (`review_period`): Specify the time period over which you are being evaluated (e.g., Jan-Dec 2025).
* **Knowledge of Job Duties and Procedures** (`job_knowledge_rating`): Rate your knowledge of your job duties and procedures on a scale of 1 ( Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Quality and Accuracy of Work** (`quality_of_work_rating`): Rate the quality and accuracy of your work on a scale of 1 ( Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Dependability and Attendance** (`dependability_rating`): Rate your dependability and attendance on a scale of 1 ( Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Cooperation and Teamwork** (`cooperation_rating`): Rate your ability to cooperate with colleagues and contribute to teamwork on a scale of 1 ( Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **List Major Achievements** (`major_achievements`): Describe your major accomplishments and contributions during this period.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`): Describe the biggest challenges you faced during this period.
* **Goals for Next Period** (`goals_next_period`): Identify your primary goals for the next review period.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`): If applicable, mention specific training or development areas you would like to explore.
* **Supervisor Meeting Request** (`supervisor_meeting_request`): Indicate whether you would like to schedule a one-on-one review meeting with your supervisor.
* **Date of Self-Evaluation** (`submission_date`): Date and submit the evaluation form.

Note: This form should be completed honestly and accurately, and any ratings should be based on your self-evaluation of your performance over the specified review period.
