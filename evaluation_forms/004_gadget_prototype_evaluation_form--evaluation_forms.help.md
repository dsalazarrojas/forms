# Gadget Prototype Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gadget Prototype Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and data on the usability, user experience, and overall rating of a gadget prototype. This form is meant to be filled out by individuals who have interacted with the gadget, whether as part of a trial or testing phase.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by writing a brief introduction to give context about your experience with the gadget.
2. Describe the gadget you evaluated, including its features and any notable aspects of your experience.
3. Answer the usability question with a simple "Yes" or "No" if you have used the gadget in the past and have an opinion on its usability.
4. Select all the options that apply to your user experience with the gadget.
5. Rate the gadget on a scale of 1 to 5, using the options provided.
6. If applicable, indicate if you are the person who submitted the form, or if you are a proxy for someone else.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the gadget.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Write a brief introduction to give context about your experience with the gadget.
* **prototype_description** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the gadget you evaluated, including its features and any notable aspects of your experience.
* **usability** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Answer the usability question with a simple "Yes" or "No" if you have used the gadget in the past and have an opinion on its usability.
* **user_experience** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the options that apply to your user experience with the gadget.
* **feedback** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any feedback or comments about the gadget.
* **additional_comments** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Add any additional comments or feedback about the gadget.
* **rating** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Rate the gadget on a scale of 1 to 5, using the options provided.
* **submitters_info** (`8`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Indicate if you are the person who submitted the form, or if you are a proxy for someone else.
* **email** (`9`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **phone** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up questions.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unable to answer a question, please leave it blank.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the additional_comments field.
* If you want to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up questions, please provide your email or phone number.
