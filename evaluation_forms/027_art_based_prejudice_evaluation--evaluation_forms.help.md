# Art Based Prejudice Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Based Prejudice Evaluation form is designed to assess an individual's reaction to and perception of an artwork, specifically in relation to prejudice and stereotypes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please read through each section carefully and respond to each question honestly and thoughtfully.
2.  Answer the questions based on your personal and subjective experiences with the artwork.
3.  Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple questions to guide your response.
4.  If you need more time to complete the form, feel free to take a break and come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, required): Please provide your participant ID or a unique identifier to ensure your response is associated with your data.
*   **Date of Evaluation** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you are completing this evaluation.
*   **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select your age group from the provided options.
*   **Artwork ID or Title** (`artwork_id`, `text`, required): Please enter the artwork title or ID you are evaluating.
*   **Brief Description of What You See** (`artwork_description`, `text`, required): Provide a concise description of the artwork's visual elements.
*   **Your Immediate Reaction** (`immediate_reaction`, `text`, required): Express your initial thoughts and feelings about the artwork.
*   **Emotional Impact** (`emotional_impact`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the provided options describing how the artwork affects you emotionally.
*   **Bias and Stereotype Assessment** (`stereotypes_present`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you perceive stereotypes in the artwork.
*   **If yes, describe the stereotypes you perceive** (`stereotype_examples`, `text`, required): If you answered "Yes, clear stereotypes" in the previous question, provide examples of what you perceive.
*   **Bias Awareness** (`bias_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Choose how the artwork's message affects your awareness of your own biases.
*   **Perspective Shift** (`perspective_shift`, `select_one`, required): Select how the artwork shifts your perspective.
*   **Artist Intent and Message** (`artist_intent`, `text`, required): What do you think the artist intended, as indicated by the artwork.
*   **Does this artwork provide social commentary** (`social_commentary`, `select_one`, required): Choose how strong you believe the artwork's message is.
*   **Personal Reflection** (`personal_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Select how relevant the artwork is to your personal experiences.
*   **Would you discuss this artwork with others** (`conversation_starter`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would discuss the artwork with others.
*   **Additional Thoughts or Observations** (`additional_thoughts`, `text`, required): Share any other thoughts or observations about the artwork.

Note to users: This form is designed to be completed honestly and sincerely, and all questions are intended to spark reflection and discussion.
