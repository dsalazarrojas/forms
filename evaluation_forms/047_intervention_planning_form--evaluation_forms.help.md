# Intervention Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intervention Planning Form is used to gather information about a specific intervention or action that you would like to take. This form helps us to better understand your needs and to provide effective support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first and second fields, respectively.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Fill out one of the Select One Option fields, making sure to select only one of the options provided.
5. Fill out one to three of the Select Multiple Options fields, making sure to select all relevant options.
6. If applicable, enter a Date and/or Time for the intervention.
7. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
