# Fitness Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fitness Class Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from students after a fitness class. This form helps instructors and staff assess the quality of the class and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the fitness class you attended.
2. Enter the name of the instructor who led the class.
3. Provide a rating for the class (on a scale of 1-5).
4. Enter the date you participated in the class.
5. Enter the time you participated in the class.
6. Choose the difficulty level of the class (Easy, Medium, Hard).
7. Provide any feedback about your experience in the class.
8. Indicate whether you would attend the class again (True, False, Maybe).
9. Enter your email address (for contact purposes).
10. Enter your phone number (for contact purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **fitness class name**: Enter the name of the fitness class you attended, such as "Yoga for Beginners" or "Cardio Kickboxing".
* **instructor name**: Enter the name of the instructor who led the class.
* **rating**: Provide a rating for the class on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **date of participation**: Enter the date you participated in the class in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time of participation**: Enter the time you participated in the class in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **difficulty level**: Choose the difficulty level of the class from the options "Easy", "Medium", or "Hard".
* **feedback**: Provide any feedback or comments about your experience in the class. This is an optional field, and you can enter as much or as little text as you like.
* **would you attend again**: Indicate whether you would attend the class again, with options "True", "False", or "Maybe".
* **email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
