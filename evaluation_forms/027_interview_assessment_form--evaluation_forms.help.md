# Interview Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used for assessing candidates after an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and a new tab will open where you can fill it out.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
    This field is for entering any relevant information about the candidate.
* **Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Enter the candidate's contact information such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Interview Details** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Provide a brief summary of the interview, including the date, time, and location.
* **Candidate Assessment** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Evaluate the candidate's performance during the interview, including their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Feedback** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
    Enter any feedback you have about the candidate, including suggestions for improvement.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out each field.
* You can add any additional comments or notes that you feel are relevant.
* Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
