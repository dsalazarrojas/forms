# Performance And Attention Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance And Attention Evaluation form is designed to assess an employee's performance and attention level, providing a structured framework for supervisors or evaluators to provide feedback and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Introduction** field with a brief overview of the performance being evaluated.
2. Enter the **Employee Name** and **Supervisor Name** fields with the relevant information.
3. Select the **Evaluations Period** field and choose the time period being evaluated (e.g., "Last Month", "This Quarter", etc.).
4. Answer the **Rating Scale** question by selecting one of the three options: "Excellent", "Satisfactory", or "Unsatisfactory".
5. Answer the **Focus Level** question by selecting one of the three options: "High", "Medium", or "Low".
6. Write any additional **Comments** about the employee's performance in the space provided.
7. Provide **Recommendations** for future performance improvements.
8. Enter the **Evaluation Date** and select the date of the evaluation.
9. Write the **Evaluation By** field to identify the person evaluating the employee.
10. Select the **Performance Level** question by choosing one of the three options: "Excellent", "Satisfactory", or "Unsatisfactory".
11. In the **Evaluation Form**, add any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Text): A brief overview of the performance being evaluated.
* **Employee Name** (Text): The name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Supervisor Name** (Text): The name of the supervisor or evaluator.
* **Evaluation Period** (Time): The time period being evaluated (e.g., "Last Month", "This Quarter", etc.).
* **Rating Scale** (Select One): How would you rate the employee's performance? ("Excellent", "Satisfactory", or "Unsatisfactory").
* **Focus Level** (Select One): How would you rate the employee's focus level? ("High", "Medium", or "Low").
* **Comments** (Note): Additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Recommendations** (Note): Recommendations for future performance improvements.
* **Evaluation Date** (Date): The date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation By** (Text): The person evaluating the employee.
* **Performance Level** (Select One): How would you rate the employee's performance level? ("Excellent", "Satisfactory", or "Unsatisfactory").
* **Evaluation Form** (Note): Any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
