# Diabetes Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diabetes Analysis Form is a tool used to collect and analyze data related to diabetes management. This form is designed to gather information about patients' contact details, medical history, observations, and follow-up requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
2. Provide details about your medical history in the **Medical History** section.
3. Select all relevant observations from the **Observation** section, such as blood sugar levels, medications, dietary habits, and physical activity.
4. Enter any comments or notes from your doctor in the **Doctor Observation** section.
5. Share your personal observations or comments in the **Patient Observation** section.
6. Choose your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text) in the **Contact Method** section.
7. If applicable, schedule a follow-up date in the **Follow Up** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section is for your contact details. Please provide your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history related to your diabetes management.
* **Observation**: This section is for selecting all relevant observations related to your diabetes management. Please choose from the options:
	+ Blood sugar levels
	+ Medications
	+ Dietary habits
	+ Physical activity
* **Doctor Observation**: Enter any comments or notes from your doctor about your diabetes management.
* **Patient Observation**: Share your personal thoughts or comments about your diabetes management.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-ups (phone, email, or text).
* **Follow Up**: If you need to schedule a follow-up, enter the date and time.
