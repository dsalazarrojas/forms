# cross_functional_team_performance_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The cross-functional team performance evaluation form is designed to evaluate a team's performance in various aspects, providing a comprehensive framework for assessment and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and feedback required for each field.
2. Read each field's explanation below to understand its purpose and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`1`, `number`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to rate the team's overall performance on a numerical scale.
* **Team Collaboration** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the team's collaboration skills, choosing from Collaboration and communication, Problem solving, Time management, Teamwork and adaptability, and Adapting to change.
* **Feedback Comments** (`3`, `note`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the team's performance.
* **Performance Improvement** (`4`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose one area where the team can improve, from Communication, Time management, Teamwork and adaptability, and Problem solving.
* **Project Outcome** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the project outcome, choosing from Successful project outcome, Partially successful project outcome, and Unsuccessful project outcome.
* **Team Dynamics** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the team dynamics, choosing from Positive team dynamics, Negative team dynamics, and Neutral team dynamics.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`7`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how often the team is evaluated, from Frequently, Occasionally, and Rarely.
* **Evaluation Basis** (`8`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the basis of the evaluation, from Feedback from team members, Manager feedback, and Performance reports.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`9`, `note`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the team's performance.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the team's overall satisfaction, choosing from High, Medium, and Low.
* **Project Manager Performance** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the project manager's performance, choosing from Strong, Medium, and Weak.
* **Team Member Performance** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the team member's performance, choosing from Strong, Medium, and Weak.
* **Communication** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the team's communication skills, choosing from Effective, Ineffective, and Neutral.
* **Conflict Resolution** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the team's conflict resolution skills, choosing from Effective, Ineffective, and Neutral.
* **Adaptability** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the team's adaptability, choosing from Strong, Medium, and Weak.
* **Time Management** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the team's time management skills, choosing from Yes, No, and Neutral.
* **Feedback Loops** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the team's feedback loops, choosing from Yes, No, and Neutral.
* **Continuous Improvement** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the team's continuous improvement, choosing from Yes, No, and Neutral.
* **Team Goal** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Rate the team's goal, choosing from High, Medium, and Low.
* **Individual Goal** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the team member's goal, choosing from Yes, No, and Neutral.
* **Evaluation Date** (`21`, `date`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to record the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`22`, `time`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to record the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Location** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the evaluation location, choosing from Yes, No, and Neutral.
* **Evaluated By** (`24`, `email`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to record the evaluator's email address.
* **Evaluator Notes** (`25`, `note`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to record any notes from the evaluator.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback in each field.
* Use the options provided to rate each field honestly and consistently.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the field's explanation above.
* This form is designed to be comprehensive, so make sure to fill it out thoroughly.
