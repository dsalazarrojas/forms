# dog_health_certificate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a dog's health certificate, which is typically a document that confirms a dog's health status or vaccination history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the owner's information (Page 1).
2. Provide details about the dog (Page 2).
3. Share the vaccination history of the dog (Page 3).
4. Upload or enter the health certificate (Page 4).
5. Enter information about the veterinarian who issued the certificate (Page 5).
6. Provide certification details (Page 6).
7. Sign and date the form (Page 7).
8. Review and submit the form (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner's Information** (`owner_info`, text, required): The owner's name, address, and contact information.
* **Dog's Information** (`dog_info`, text, required): The dog's name, breed, age, weight, and any other relevant details.
* **Vaccination History** (`vaccination_history`, text, required): A record of the dog's vaccination dates, types, and locations.
* **Health Certificate** (`health_certificate`, text, required): A scanned or uploaded copy of the dog's health certificate.
* **Veterinarian Information** (`veterinarian_info`, text, required): The name, address, and contact information of the veterinarian who issued the certificate.
* **Certification Information** (`certification_info`, text, required): Additional details about the certificate, such as the date issued and expiration date.
* **Signature and Date** (`signature_info`, text, required): The owner's signature and the date the form is signed.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required): A review or final comment from the owner.
