# Business Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to aid our marketing and promotional strategies. By filling out this form, you'll help us understand your shopping habits and preferences, enabling us to tailor our offers to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose your options carefully, especially for fields with multiple selections.
3. Double-check your selections and details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: A required field to capture your complete name as we'll use it for communication.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: Your complete name
* **Email Address**: A required field for promotional updates and communication.
	+ Type: email
	+ Hint: Your email for promotional updates
* **Phone Number**: A required field for easy contact.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: Your contact phone number
* **Mailing Address**: An optional field for mail communications. (Leave it blank if not applicable)
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: Street address for mail communications
* **City**: An optional field for geographical location.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: City of residence
* **State or Province**: An optional field for geographical location.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: State or province
* **Postal Code**: An optional field for postal code. (Leave it blank if not applicable)
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: ZIP or postal code
* **Products or Services of Interest**: A required field to capture your purchase interests. Choose multiple options.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Hint: What are you interested in buying
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: A required field to understand your awareness source. Choose one option.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Where did you learn about our business
* **How Often Do You Visit**: An optional field for shopping frequency.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Your shopping frequency
* **Shopping Preference**: An optional field to express your preference.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: What do you prefer to shop for
* **Typical Purchase Budget**: An optional field to capture your spending range.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Your usual spending range
* **Interested in Special Promotions**: A required field to decide on promotional offers. Choose one option.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Would you like to receive promotions
* **Loyalty Program Interest**: An optional field for loyalty program preferences.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Interested in our loyalty rewards program
* **Satisfaction with Store**: An optional field to rate your shopping experience.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Rate your overall shopping experience
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: An optional field for improvement suggestions.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: How can we serve you better
* **Merchandise Quality Feedback**: An optional field for product feedback.
	+ Type: text
	+ Hint: Your opinion on our products
* **Newsletter Signup**: An optional field to subscribe to our newsletter.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: Subscribe to our email newsletter
* **Permission to Contact**: A required field to decide on direct contact.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Hint: May we contact you with special offers
* **Submission Date**: A field automatically filled with the current date.
	+ Type: date
	+ Hint: Date this form was completed
