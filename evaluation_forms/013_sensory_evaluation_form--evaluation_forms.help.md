# Sensory Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the sensory aspects of a product or service. It helps gather information on the overall rating, description, and sensory qualities such as appearance, taste, smell, texture, and mouthfeel. Additionally, it collects information on the product's price, quality, presentation, service, packaging, and value.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the page with the Overall Rating question and choose your rating.
2.  Fill in the product name as it appears on the label or packaging.
3.  Write a brief description of the product.
4.  Select the options for each sensory question, such as Appearance, Taste, Smell, Texture, and Mouthfeel, to rate the product. Please note that for each question, you can choose one option: Very good, Good, Neutral, Bad, or Very bad.
5.  Enter the price you paid for the product.
6.  Provide an overall impression of the product.
7.  Check the box to indicate if you would recommend this product.
8.  Check the box to indicate if you would buy this product again.
9.  Check the box to indicate if you would visit the store to buy this product again.
10. Enter any additional comments or feedback about the product.
11. Finally, check the boxes for the additional questions, such as Will Revisit, Would Recommend, and Will Buy, to provide more information about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating (1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **Product Name (2)**: Enter the name of the product as it appears on the label or packaging.
* **Description (3)**: Write a brief description of the product.
* **Appearance (4)**: Rate the product's appearance.
* **Taste (5)**: Rate the product's taste.
* **Smell (6)**: Rate the product's smell.
* **Texture (7)**: Rate the product's texture.
* **Mouthfeel (8)**: Rate the product's mouthfeel.
* **Price (9)**: Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Overall Impression (10)**: Provide an overall impression of the product.
* **Recommended (11)**: Check the box to indicate if you would recommend this product.
* **Will Revisit (12)**: Check the box to indicate if you would visit the store to buy this product again.
* **Comments (13)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the product.
* **Additional Comments (14)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the product.
* **Will Buy (15)**: Check the box to indicate if you would buy this product again.
* **Would Recommend (16)**: Check the box to indicate if you would recommend this product.
* **Will Revisit (17)**: Check the box to indicate if you would visit the store to buy this product again.
* **Rating (18)**: Enter a numerical rating for the product.
* **Quality (19)**: Rate the product's quality on a scale of 1-100.
* **Presentation (20)**: Rate the product's presentation.
* **Service (21)**: Rate the service provided with the product.
* **Packaging (22)**: Rate the product's packaging.
* **Value (23)**: Rate the value of the product.
* **Would Recommend (24)**: Check the box to indicate if you would recommend this product.
* **Will Revisit (25)**: Check the box to indicate if you would visit the store to buy this product again.

## Tips

* Please take a few minutes to carefully read and complete each question to provide accurate and informative feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the Comments field to provide more information.
* This form is designed to help us improve our products and services. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
