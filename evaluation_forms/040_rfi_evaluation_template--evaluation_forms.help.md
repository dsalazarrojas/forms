# rfi_evaluation_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rfi_evaluation_template is a form designed to collect information about a request for information (RFI) or a customer inquiry. It is intended to gather details on a product or service, the customer's interest, and the date of the evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the product or service being evaluated.
2. Provide a brief description of the customer interest in the product or service.
3. Enter the date of the evaluation.
4. Specify who conducted the evaluation.
5. Optionally, add any comments about the evaluation.
6. Optionally, identify the person assigned to the evaluation (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product or Service** (Product or Service, text, **required**): Enter the name of the product or service being evaluated.
* **Customer Interest** (Customer Interest, text, **required**): Describe the customer's interest in the product or service.
* **Evaluation Date** (Evaluation Date, date, **required**): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation By** (Evaluation By, text, **required**): Specify who conducted the evaluation.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, **optional**): Add any additional comments about the evaluation (if needed).
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, text, **optional**): Identify the person or team assigned to the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Request for Info** (Request for Info, text, **optional**): You can add any other information about the request for information (if needed).
