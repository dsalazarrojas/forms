# Club Feroviar Conference Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Feroviar Conference Feedback form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on your experience with the conference. Your input will help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant information, including your name and email address.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference.
3. Indicate whether you would recommend this conference.
4. Evaluate the quality of the conference content and its relevance to your needs and interests.
5. Identify the most valuable session or topic.
6. Suggest topics that you feel should be covered in future conferences.
7. Assess the conference schedule and organization, venue and facilities, and catering and refreshments.
8. Rate your experience with networking opportunities and valuable professional connections.
9. Identify areas for improvement and provide suggestions for future conferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: Participant Information
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification card or business card.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Organization or Club** (`organization`): If you're affiliated with an organization or club, enter it here.
* **Position or Role** (`position`): If applicable, provide your position or role within your organization or club.

### Section 2: Overall Conference Experience
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the conference on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Would You Recommend This Conference** (`would_recommend`): Indicate whether you would recommend this conference, selecting one of the options: Yes, definitely, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably not, No.

### Section 3: Content and Sessions
* **Quality of Conference Content** (`content_quality`): Rate the quality of the conference content on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Relevance to Your Needs and Interests** (`relevant_content`): Rate the relevance of the conference content to your needs and interests on a scale from 1 (not relevant) to 5 (very relevant).
* **Most Valuable Session or Topic** (`favorite_session`): Choose the most valuable session or topic you found helpful.
* **Topics You Wish Were Covered** (`missing_topics`): Suggest topics that you feel should be covered in future conferences.

### Section 4: Organization and Logistics
* **Conference Schedule and Organization** (`schedule_quality`): Rate the conference schedule and organization on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Venue and Facilities** (`venue_facilities`): Rate the venue and facilities on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Catering and Refreshments** (`catering`): Rate the catering and refreshments on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Registration and Check-in Process** (`registration_process`): Rate the registration and check-in process on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

### Section 5: Networking and Connections
* **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`): Rate the networking opportunities on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Made Valuable Professional Connections** (`new_connections`): Indicate whether you made any valuable professional connections, selecting one of the options: Yes, several, Yes, a few, No.

### Section 6: Areas for Improvement
* **Main Areas for Improvement** (`main_concerns`): Identify the main areas that could be improved in future conferences.
* **Suggestions for Future Conferences** (`suggestions`): Provide suggestions on how next year's conference could be better.

**Tips**

- Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can understand your thoughts and ideas.
- Be honest and thorough in your evaluation, as it will greatly help us improve future conferences.
- We appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
