# Simple Instructor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Instructor Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from students on the performance of their instructor. This form helps administrators or managers evaluate the instructor's overall effectiveness in the classroom and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant feedback options for each field based on your experience with the instructor. The form consists of multiple sections, each with a series of options to rate the instructor's performance in a specific area. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback On Instructor Preparedness** (`feedback_on_instructor_preparedness`, text, optional): Describe the instructor's level of preparation for class, such as how organized their materials were.
* **Feedback On Course Materials** (`feedback_on_course_materials`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options to rate the quality of the course materials provided by the instructor. Options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
