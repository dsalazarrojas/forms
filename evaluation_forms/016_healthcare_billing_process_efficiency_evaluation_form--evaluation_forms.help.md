# Healthcare Billing Process Efficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help healthcare professionals identify areas of inefficiency in their current billing process and provide suggestions for improvement. By completing this form, you will help us better understand your challenges and needs, and we will use this information to inform our development of tools and resources to support your work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the most accurate response for each multiple-choice question.
3. You can add additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your current billing process?** (`healthcare_billing_process_efficiency_form_1_section_1`)
	+ Select one of the following ratings: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent
	+ This question helps us understand your overall experience with the billing process.
* **What are the main challenges you face in your current billing process?** (`healthcare_billing_process_efficiency_form_1_section_2`)
