# Trainee Progress Report Form Cellular Sales - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trainee Progress Report Form is used to track and evaluate the progress of trainees in cellular sales training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form title to describe the purpose of the report.
*   Enter the name of the trainee and their mentor.
*   Specify the reporting period.
*   Select the trainee's communication, time management, and overall performance.
*   Choose the skills set and work knowledge that the trainee has acquired.
*   Provide feedback for the trainee.
