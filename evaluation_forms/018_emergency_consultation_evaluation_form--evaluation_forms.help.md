# Emergency Consultation Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Consultation Evaluation Form is used to quickly gather information from patients during emergency consultations. This form helps healthcare professionals evaluate the patient's situation and make informed decisions about their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "patient info" section with the patient's basic information, such as name, age, and contact details.
2. In the "consultation details" section, describe the reason for the consultation and the symptoms or conditions that brought the patient to the emergency room.
3. In the "evaluation results" section, write a brief summary of the consultation, including any diagnoses, treatments, or plans for future care.
4. If relevant, provide information on the patient's medical history in the "medical history" section.
5. Enter the patient's doctor's contact information in the "doctor info" section.
6. If necessary, provide information on the patient's emergency contact in the "emergency contact" section.
7. Use the "other comments" field for any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.
8. In the "date of visit" and "time of visit" fields, enter the date and time of the consultation.
9. Use the "medical conditions" field to select any medical conditions that the patient has.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient info**: Enter the patient's basic information, such as name, age, and contact details.
* **consultation details**: Describe the reason for the consultation and the symptoms or conditions that brought the patient to the emergency room.
* **evaluation results**: Write a brief summary of the consultation, including any diagnoses, treatments, or plans for future care.
* **doctor info**: Enter the doctor's contact information.
* **medical history**: Provide information on the patient's medical history, including any previous consultations, illnesses, or treatments.
* **emergency contact**: Enter the contact information for the patient's emergency contact person.
* **other comments**: Use this field for any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.
* **date of visit**: Enter the date of the consultation.
* **time of visit**: Enter the time of the consultation.
* **medical conditions**: Select any medical conditions that the patient has.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure that healthcare professionals have the best information to make informed decisions about the patient's care.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it, as it will be used to inform the patient's treatment and care.
