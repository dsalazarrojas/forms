# Nursing Evaluation And Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate and self-assess nursing professionals' performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read each page carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. For each question, select the most accurate option that reflects your performance or self-assessment.
3. If you are unsure about any question, use the space provided for additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction**
* **Introduction** (text): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please type a short description of yourself and your role in the nursing profession.

### 2. **Evaluating Core Competencies**
* **Evaluating Core Competencies** (select_one): Choose one of the options below to evaluate your core competencies:
	+ Excellent: This option indicates that you consistently perform at a high level in this competency area.
	+ Satisfactory: This option indicates that you meet the minimum expectations in this competency area.
	+ Needs Improvement: This option indicates that you need to improve in this competency area.

### 3. **Reflecting on Strengths**
* **Reflecting on Strengths** (text): Describe your strengths and why you think they are beneficial to your role and patients.

### 4. **Identifying Development Areas**
* **Identifying Development Areas** (select_multiple): Choose one or more of the options below to identify areas where you would like to improve:
	+ Time Management: This option indicates that you struggle with time management, prioritizing tasks, and meeting deadlines.
	+ Communication: This option indicates that you need to improve your communication skills, such as articulating information effectively or listening actively.
	+ Problem Solving: This option indicates that you need to develop your problem-solving skills, such as analyzing complex situations or making sound decisions.
	+ Leadership: This option indicates that you need to develop your leadership skills, such as motivating others or guiding teams.
	+ Teamwork: This option indicates that you need to improve your teamwork skills, such as collaborating effectively or delegating tasks.
	+ Adaptability: This option indicates that you need to develop your adaptability skills, such as adjusting to changing situations or embracing new ideas.
	+ Emotional Intelligence: This option indicates that you need to improve your emotional intelligence, such as managing your emotions or empathizing with others.
	+ Conflict Resolution: This option indicates that you need to develop your conflict resolution skills, such as mediating disputes or finding mutually beneficial solutions.
	+ Delegation: This option indicates that you need to improve your delegation skills, such as assigning tasks effectively or trusting others with responsibilities.
	+ Empathy: This option indicates that you need to develop your empathy skills, such as understanding others' perspectives or feelings.
	+ Flexibility: This option indicates that you need to improve your flexibility skills, such as adapting to changes or being open to new ideas.

### 5. **Data Collection**
* **Data Collection** (note): This is an optional field for any additional information you would like to provide about your performance or growth areas.

### 6. **Core Competencies Rating**
* **Core Competencies Rating** (number): Rate your performance in each of the core competencies using a number between 1-5, where 1 indicates "Needs Improvement" and 5 indicates "Excellent".

### 7. **Strengths Rating**
* **Strengths Rating** (number): Rate your strengths using a number between 1-5, where 1 indicates "Needs Improvement" and 5 indicates "Excellent".

### 8. **Development Areas Rating**
* **Development Areas Rating** (number): Rate your development areas using a number between 1-5, where 1 indicates "Needs Improvement" and 5 indicates "Excellent".

### 9. **Overall Rating**
* **Overall Rating** (number): Rate your overall performance using a number between 1-5, where 1 indicates "Needs Improvement" and 5 indicates "Excellent".

### 10. **Comments**
* **Comments** (text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your performance or growth areas.

### 11. **Suggestions**
* **Suggestions** (note): This is an optional field for any suggestions or recommendations you have for improvement.

### 12. **Additional Comments**
* **Additional Comments** (text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your performance or growth areas.

### 13. **Submit**
* **Submit** (text): This is the final step to complete the form.
