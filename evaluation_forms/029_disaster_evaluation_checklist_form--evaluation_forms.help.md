# Disaster Evaluation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Evaluation Checklist Form is designed to help organizations and individuals assess and evaluate the impact of disasters, such as floods, fires, earthquakes, landslides, and other natural disasters. This form collects information on emergency contact details, incident details, response and rescue teams, damage levels, and evaluation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the emergency contact information (page 1).
2. Enter the incident date and time (page 2).
3. Specify the incident location (page 3).
4. Choose the type of disaster that occurred (page 4).
5. Select the affected areas (page 5).
6. Assess the damage level (page 6).
7. Provide damage information (page 7).
8. Specify the response and rescue teams involved (page 8).
9. Choose the support services provided (page 9).
10. Enter the damage assessment (page 10).
11. List mitigation measures taken (page 11).
12. Outline the preparedness plan (page 12).
13. Document the response plan (page 13).
14. Add any additional communication or notes (page 14).
15. Review and evaluate the incident (page 15).
16. Record follow-up actions (page 16).
17. Add any final notes (page 17).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name, phone number, and email of the emergency contact person.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required/optional): Record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the location of the incident.
* **Disaster Type** (`disaster_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of disaster from the list: Flood, Fire, Earthquake, Landslide, Tornado, Hurricane, Tsunami, Other.
* **Affected Area** (`affected_area`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the areas affected by the disaster.
* **Damage Level** (`damage_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Assess the level of damage: Minor, Moderate, Extensive.
* **Damage Information** (`damage_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about the damage.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, `date`, required/optional): Record the time the response team arrived.
* **Rescue Team** (`rescue_team`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the rescue teams involved.
* **Support Services** (`support_services`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the support services provided.
* **Damage Assessment** (`damage_assessment`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about the damage assessment.
* **Mitigation Measures** (`mitigation_measures`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): List the mitigation measures taken.
* **Preparedness Plan** (`preparedness_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the preparedness plan.
* **Response Plan** (`response_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Document the response plan.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional communication or notes.
* **Incident Review** (`incident_review`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Review and evaluate the incident.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, `text`, required/optional): Record follow-up actions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any final notes.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the incident status: Active, Resolved, Cancelled, Not Started.
* **Response Status** (`response_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the response status: Active, Resolved, Cancelled, Not Started.
* **Evaluation Status** (`evaluation_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the evaluation status: Active, Resolved, Cancelled, Not Started.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required/optional): Outline next steps.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the review status: Active, Resolved, Cancelled, Not Started.
* **Final Review** (`final_review`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Review the incident for final evaluation.
