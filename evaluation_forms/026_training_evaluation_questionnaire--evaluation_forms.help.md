# Training Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and impact of a training program. It is meant to gather feedback from trainees on their experience with the training and its relevance to their job responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best answer your questions for each section.
2. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as required.
3. Use the options provided for each question or select "N/A" if the question does not apply to your experience.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <label>  -  Training Evaluation Questionnaire 
* This field asks you to select all the training programs that you have attended or will be attending. Please choose all the relevant options that apply.

### 2. <label>  -  Overall Impact Rating 
* This field asks you to rate the overall impact of the training program on your work or job responsibilities. Please select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means "low" and 5 means "high".

### 3. <label>  -  Training Effectiveness 
* This field asks you to evaluate the effectiveness of the training program. Please select "Yes" if you found the training to be effective, and "No" if you did not.

### 4. <label>  -  Relevance to Job 
* This field asks you to evaluate how relevant the training program was to your job responsibilities. Please select "Yes" if the training was relevant, and "No" if it was not.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your supervisor or the training department.
* This form is for gathering feedback, so please keep your answers concise and focused on the training experience.
