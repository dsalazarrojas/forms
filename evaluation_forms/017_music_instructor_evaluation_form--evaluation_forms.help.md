# Music Instructor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Instructor Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback on the performance and teaching style of music instructors. This feedback is used to inform decisions on instructor evaluations and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating question (Rating) with a rating from 1 Star to 5 Stars, indicating your overall satisfaction with the instructor's performance.
2. Provide comments on the instructor's teaching style, performance, and any other relevant feedback in the Comments field.
3. Add any additional feedback or comments about the instructor's lesson plan, performance, or technical skill in the Feedback field.
4. Enter the length of the lesson in minutes in the Lesson Length field.
5. Enter the number of students that were present during the lesson in the Number of Students field.
6. Enter the topic of the lesson in the Lesson Topic field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title
* **Form Title** (`title`, text, optional): This field is the title of the form, which is not used for scoring or evaluation.

### Rating
* **Rating** (`music_instructor_form_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the instructor's performance from 1 Star to 5 Stars.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`music_instructor_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments or feedback on the instructor's teaching style or performance.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`music_instructor_form_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the instructor's performance or lesson plan.

### Lesson Length
* **Lesson Length** (`music_instructor_form_lesson_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the lesson in minutes.

### Number of Students
* **Number of Students** (`music_instructor_form_number_of_students`, number, optional): Enter the number of students that were present during the lesson.

### Lesson Topic
* **Lesson Topic** (`music_instructor_form_lesson_topic`, text, optional): Enter the topic of the lesson.

### Note: All fields are optional, please only fill in the fields that are relevant to your evaluation.

### Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback or comments.
* Try to provide constructive criticism that is actionable and specific.
* Remember that your feedback will help the instructor grow and improve their skills.
