# Editing Tools Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Editing Tools Assessment" form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with editing tools. This form helps stakeholders understand how well the tools are being used, what features are needed, and what tools are recommended.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the form pages:** The form consists of 5 pages. Please review each page and answer the corresponding questions.
2. **Answer each question:** Answer each question truthfully to provide a clear assessment of the editing tools.
3. **Submit your feedback:** Once you have completed all the pages, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Editing tools usage** (`editing_tools_usage`, text, optional)
*This field is for you to describe how you use the editing tools. Please provide a brief explanation of your experience with the tools.*

### 2. **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional)
*Choose one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the editing tools. The options are: Very high, High, Medium, Low, Very low.*

### 3. **Feature needs** (`feature_needs`, text, optional)
*In this field, describe what features you think are missing or needed in the editing tools.*

### 4. **Training needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional)
*Explain what kind of training or guidance you need to effectively use the editing tools.*

### 5. **Tool recommendations** (`tool_recommendations`, select_multiple, optional)
*Choose one or more of the options to indicate whether you recommend or do not recommend each tool. The options are: True, False, Maybe.*
