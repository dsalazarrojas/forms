# kitchen_staff_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from kitchen staff about their work environment, management, and performance. It is intended for use by kitchen staff to provide their honest assessment of their workplace, helping kitchen management make data-driven decisions to improve kitchen operations. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the kitchen staff evaluation form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have a clear and honest understanding of your experiences in the kitchen.
2. Fill in the form with your genuine responses, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be respectful and professional in your answers, as the evaluation is for internal use only.
4. If you have any concerns or suggestions, please address them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, required/optional): Please type the name of the restaurant you work in. 
* **Restaurant Location** (`restaurant_location`, text, required/optional): Please type the location of the restaurant you work in. 
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, required/optional): This is a space to provide any general comments or feedback you have about your supervisors. Keep in mind that this is for their eyes only and is a private field. 
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required/optional): Please type the date you completed this evaluation. Note: You can use the format MM/DD/YYYY for this field. 

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing your responses.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please ask your supervisor or HR representative.
* Remember, your honesty and integrity are crucial in providing a thorough evaluation.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
