# health_equity_initiative_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health equity initiative evaluation form is used to gather information about health equity initiatives that have been implemented or are being planned. This information will be used to review and evaluate these initiatives to ensure that they align with the organization's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields that are marked as required are completed.
3. For fields that are not required, provide any relevant information that you feel is necessary for the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: (`program_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the health equity initiative.
* **Contact Name**: (`contact_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the contact person for the initiative.
* **Organization**: (`organization`, text, required)
Enter the name of the organization associated with the initiative.
* **Contact Email**: (`contact_email`, email, required)
Enter the email address of the contact person for the initiative.
* **Contact Phone**: (`contact_phone`, text, required)
Enter the phone number of the contact person for the initiative.
* **Program Start Date**: (`program_start_date`, date, required)
Enter the start date of the health equity initiative.
* **Program End Date**: (`program_end_date`, date, required)
Enter the end date of the health equity initiative.
* **Evaluation Area**: (`evaluation_area`, text, optional)
Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Type**: (`evaluation_type`, select_one, required)
Select the type of evaluation that this initiative will undergo (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Submitter Name**: (`submitter_name`, text, required)
Enter your name and title to ensure that you are the submitter of this evaluation.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure that the form is reviewed thoroughly.
* Enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to the contact person listed above.
