# Information Retrieval Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Retrieval Assessment form is designed to gather information about your search experience, including your search term, explanation, confidence rating, evaluation result, search results, search time, form version, form build time, form build date, assigned tool, form ID, output file, description, category, category label, category value, and form titles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Search Term** in the text field. This is the keyword or phrase you used to search for the information.
2. Provide a brief **Explanation** of your search, describing what you were looking for and how you found the information.
3. Rate your **Confidence Rating** as a number between 1 and 10, indicating how confident you are in the accuracy of your search results.
4. Choose your **Evaluation Result** from the dropdown menu, indicating whether you found the information you were looking for.
5. Enter your **Search Results** in the text field, describing the information you found.
6. Enter the **Search Time**, which is the time it took you to find the information.
7. Enter the **Form Version**, which is the version of the form you are using.
8. Enter the **Form Build Time**, which is the time it took to build the form.
9. Enter the **Form Build Date**, which is the date the form was built.
10. Enter the **Assigned Tool**, which is the tool used to build the form.
11. Enter the **Form ID**, which is the unique identifier for the form.
12. Enter the **Output File**, which is the file generated by the form.
13. Enter a brief **Description** of the form.
14. Enter the **Category**, which is the category the form belongs to.
15. Enter the **Category Label**, which is the label for the category.
16. Enter the **Category Value**, which is the value of the category.
17. Enter a list of **Form Titles**, which are the titles of the form.

* **Search Term** (required): Enter the keyword or phrase you used to search for the information.

* **Evaluation Result** (required): Choose from the dropdown menu whether you found the information you were looking for.
* **Search Results** (optional): Enter a description of the information you found.
* **Search Time** (required): Enter the time it took you to find the information.
* **Form Version** (optional): Enter the version of the form you are using.
* **Form Build Time** (optional): Enter the time it took to build the form.
* **Form Build Date** (optional): Enter the date the form was built.
* **Assigned Tool** (optional): Enter the tool used to build the form.
* **Form ID** (optional): Enter the unique identifier for the form.
* **Output File** (optional): Enter the file generated by the form.
* **Description** (optional): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Category** (optional): Enter the category the form belongs to.
* **Category Label** (optional): Enter the label for the category.
* **Category Value** (optional): Enter the value of the category.
* **Form Titles** (optional): Enter a list of titles for the form.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and detailed information for each field to ensure the assessment is comprehensive.
