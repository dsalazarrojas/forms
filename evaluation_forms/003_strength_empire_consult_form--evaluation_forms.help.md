## Purpose

This form is designed to assess and evaluate individual's strength and fitness goals, habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Choose relevant options for select fields such as select_one, select_multiple, and select_one_goal.
3. Enter numbers for numerical fields like days_per_week, intensity_of_workout, etc.
