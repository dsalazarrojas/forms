# Janitorial Services Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for evaluating the performance of janitorial services. It helps to assess the cleanliness, quality, timeliness, and overall performance of the service providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the relevant fields.
2. Select the evaluation category that best describes your experience with the janitorial services.
3. Provide scores for cleanliness, quality, and timeliness of the service provided.
4. Choose the status of the work order you are evaluating.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
6. Enter your email address and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Work Order Number** (`work_order_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the work order being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Janitor Name** (`janitor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the janitor who provided the services.
* **Evaluation Category** (`evaluation_category`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following categories:
	+ Cleanliness
	+ Quality
	+ Timeliness
	+ Overall
* **Cleanliness Score** (`cleanliness_score`, number, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the services provided (e.g., 1-10).
* **Quality Score** (`quality_score`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the services provided (e.g., 1-10).
* **Timeliness Score** (`timeliness_score`, number, optional): Rate the timeliness of the services provided (e.g., 1-10).
* **Work Order Status** (`work_order_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following status:
	+ Complete
	+ Incomplete
	+ Partial
	+ Cancelled
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your contact information.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your contact information.
* **Contact Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of your contact information.
* **Contact Country** (`contact_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of your contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the janitorial services.
