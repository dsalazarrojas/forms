# Research Hypothesis Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Hypothesis Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate research hypotheses submitted by multiple researchers. This form collects qualitative and quantitative feedback on each hypothesis, allowing evaluators to assess the relevance, impact, and overall rating of each hypothesis. The form is structured to collect feedback from up to 20 researchers, making it suitable for large-scale evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the researcher whose hypothesis you are evaluating.
2. Provide your assessment of their hypothesis by answering the required fields.
3. Rate the relevance, impact, and overall rating of the hypothesis.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 for each researcher listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher**: Enter the name of the researcher whose hypothesis is being evaluated.
* **Research Hypothesis**: Provide a brief description or summary of the research hypothesis being evaluated.
* **Relevance Rating**: Rate the relevance of the research hypothesis, with higher numbers indicating higher relevance.
* **Impact Rating**: Rate the potential impact of the research hypothesis, with higher numbers indicating higher impact.
* **Confidence Rating**: Rate your confidence in the research hypothesis, with higher numbers indicating higher confidence.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the overall quality of the research hypothesis, with higher numbers indicating higher quality.
* **Researcher [1-20]**: Repeat the fields above for each researcher listed, providing separate feedback and ratings for each.
