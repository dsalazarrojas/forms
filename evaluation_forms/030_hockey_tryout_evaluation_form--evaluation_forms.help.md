# Hockey Tryout Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hockey Tryout Evaluation Form is used to evaluate players after a tryout. The form captures key information about the player's performance and is essential for coaches and evaluators to make informed decisions about player selection and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the player's name.
2. Enter the tryout date (the date the player attended the tryout).
3. Enter the player's number.
4. Indicate whether the position being evaluated is "Yes" or "No".
5. Enter any skills or comments about the player's performance.
6. Enter any additional comments about the player.
7. Enter the name of the person who evaluated the player (Assessor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name (Player Name)**: Enter the player's name as it appears on their identification or as they prefer to be referred to.
* **Tryout Date (Tryout Date)**: Enter the date the player attended the tryout, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Player Number (Player Number)**: Enter the player's number worn during the tryout.
* **Evaluation Date (Evaluation Date)**: Enter the date this evaluation is being made, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Position Evaluated (Position Evaluated)**: Check "Yes" if this is the position that the player is being evaluated for, or "No" otherwise.
* **Skills (Skills)**: Enter any skills or comments about the player's performance.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Enter any additional comments about the player's performance.
* **Assessor (Assessor)**: Enter the name of the person who evaluated the player.
