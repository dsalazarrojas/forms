# EMT Clinical Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EMT Clinical Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a patient's medical history and current condition. This form helps healthcare professionals quickly and accurately assess a patient's condition, providing them with the necessary data to provide proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's **name**, the location where the patient is currently being treated, and a brief description of their chief complaint or reason for seeking medical attention.
2. Record the patient's **vital signs**, such as their blood pressure, pulse, and respiratory rate.
3. Evaluate each of the patient's **body systems**, including **respiratory**, **cardiovascular**, **neurological**, **musculoskeletal**, **genitourinary**, **hematological**, **gastrointestinal**, **endocrine**, **skin**, **eye**, **ear**, **neurological**, **musculoskeletal**, and **genitourinary** systems.
4. Note any **dates** and **times** related to the patient's injury or illness, such as the date and time of the injury.
5. Record the patient's **date of birth**.
6. Select the **tool** assigned to the patient from the provided options.
7. Finally, review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **medical_center** (text, required): Type the location where the patient is currently being treated, such as their hospital room number or treatment room.
* **chief_complaint** (text, required): Describe the reason the patient is seeking medical attention, including any symptoms they are experiencing.
* **vital_signs** (text, required): Record the patient's vital signs, such as their blood pressure, pulse, and respiratory rate.
* **respiratory_system** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's respiratory system, including any issues with breathing or oxygenation.
* **cardiovascular_system** (text, required): Assess the patient's cardiovascular system, including any issues with heart rate, blood pressure, or circulation.
* **neurological_system** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's neurological system, including any issues with their brain, nervous system, or senses.
* **musculoskeletal_system** (text, required): Assess the patient's musculoskeletal system, including any issues with their muscles, bones, or joints.
* **genitourinary_system** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's genitourinary system, including any issues with their reproductive organs or urinary system.
* **hematological_system** (text, required): Assess the patient's hematological system, including any issues with their blood or bone marrow.
* **gastrointestinal_system** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's gastrointestinal system, including any issues with their digestive system or abdomen.
* **endocrine_system** (text, required): Assess the patient's endocrine system, including any issues with their hormone regulation or metabolism.
* **skin** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's skin, including any issues with their skin condition or irritation.
* **eye** (text, required): Assess the patient's eyes, including any issues with their vision or eye injuries.
* **ear** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's ear, including any issues with their hearing or ear infections.
* **neuro_system** (text, required): Assess the patient's nervous system, including any issues with their brain, nerves, or spinal cord.
* **musculoskeletal** (text, required): Evaluate the patient's musculoskeletal system, including any issues with their muscles, bones, or joints.
* **genitourinary** (text, required): Assess the patient's genitourinary system, including any issues with their reproductive organs or urinary system.
* **other_system** (text, required): Evaluate any other systems not covered in the previous fields.
* **date_of_injury** (date, required): Record the date of the patient's injury or illness.
* **time_of_injury** (time, required): Record the time of the patient's injury or illness.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Record the patient's date of birth.
* **assigned_tool** (select, required): Select the tool assigned to the patient from the provided options.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any information or have questions, consult with a supervisor or medical expert.
- This form is designed to provide a thorough evaluation of the patient's condition, but it is not a substitute for medical training or expertise.
