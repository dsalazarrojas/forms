# longwood_prostaff_eval_ad_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect evaluation input from users to assess the quality and effectiveness of a submitted Advertisement (AD).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the submitted Advertisement (AD) and ensure you are evaluating the correct AD.
2.  Select the category that best describes the AD (e.g., Advertising, Marketing, Sales).
3.  Choose the person who submitted the AD (e.g., Jim, Jane, John, Other).
4.  Optionally, provide a date and time for the evaluation.
5.  Enter your evaluation score for the AD, if applicable.
6.  Add any additional comments about your evaluation.
7.  Review and confirm that your submission is correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **submitted_advertisement** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the submitted Advertisement (AD).
*   **evaluation_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation. (Note: This field is not strictly necessary, as the form can still be completed without it.)
*   **evaluation_time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation. (Similar to evaluation_date, this field is not strictly necessary.)
*   **evaluation_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the AD.
*   **ad_score** (number, optional): Enter a score for the AD (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest). (Note: This field is not strictly necessary, as the form can still be completed without it.)
*   **category** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more categories that best describe the AD (e.g., Advertising, Marketing, Sales).
*   **sub_category** (select_one, optional): Select a subcategory that further describes the AD (e.g., Product, Service, Other).
*   **submitted_by** (select_one, required): Select the person who submitted the AD (e.g., Jim, Jane, John, Other).
*   **evaluation_criteria** (text, optional): Enter any specific criteria or factors that influenced your evaluation.
*   **additional_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the AD.
