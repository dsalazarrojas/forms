# Military Promotion Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Military Promotion Assessment Form is a document used to evaluate the promotion of military personnel. It is designed to assess the performance and suitability of an officer for promotion. The form helps assessors gather information on the officer's qualifications, performance, and potential for promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required personal details, including First Name, Last Name, and Promotion Date.
2. Choose the Promotion Status from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide evaluation comments, if necessary.
4. Indicate the Unit Recommendation.
5. Indicate the Commander Recommendation.
6. Enter the Evaluation Date.
7. Choose the Evaluation Type.
8. Enter the Evaluation Period.
9. Sign the form with the Officer Signature and Date Signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the officer's first name.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the officer's middle name, if applicable.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the officer's last name.
* **Promotion Date** (`promotion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of promotion.
* **Promotion Status** (`promotion_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the promotion status (Active Duty, Retired, or Reserve).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments regarding the officer's promotion.
* **Unit Recommendation** (`unit_recommendation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether the unit recommends the promotion.
* **Commander Recommendation** (`commander_recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether the commander recommends the promotion.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of evaluation.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the officer's name (optional).
* **Officer Rank** (`officer_rank`, `text`, optional): Enter the officer's rank (optional).
* **Evaluation Type** (`evaluation_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of evaluation (Form A, Form B, or Form C).
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, `text`, required): Enter the evaluation period.
* **Officer Signature** (`officer_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with the officer's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date signed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant personnel or superiors.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are completed.
