# Gap Correction Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gap Correction Feedback Form is a tool designed to help identify and address performance gaps that individuals or teams may be experiencing. By providing a clear and concise platform for feedback and suggestions, it aims to improve performance and bridge these gaps.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the First page with any initial comments or questions you may have.
2. Proceed to the Select one page, where you will choose one of the provided options that best describes the performance gaps you are experiencing.
3. If necessary, provide your personal identification and contact information in the First Name, Last Name, and Email fields.
4. Add any additional contact information, such as your Phone number and Date, if applicable.
5. Provide the correct Time for your feedback session or meeting.
6. Describe the Performance Gaps you've identified in detail.
7. Share your thoughts or concerns about the performance gaps in the Feedback field.
8. Suggest potential improvements or solutions in the Suggestions field.
9. Add any additional comments or observations in the Comments field.
10. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page (Page 1)** (Text, Optional) - Initial comments or questions.
* **Select one (Page 2)** (Select, Optional) - Choose one of the provided options.
* **First Name (Page 3)** (Text, Optional) - Your given name.
* **Last Name (Page 4)** (Text, Optional) - Your surname.
* **Email (Page 5)** (Email, Optional) - Your email address.
* **Phone (Page 6)** (Text, Optional) - Your phone number.
* **Date (Page 7)** (Date, Optional) - The date you are providing feedback.
* **Time (Page 8)** (Time, Optional) - The time of your feedback session or meeting.
* **Performance Gaps (Page 9)** (Text, Optional) - Describe the performance gaps you've identified.
* **Feedback (Page 10)** (Text, Optional) - Share your thoughts or concerns about the performance gaps.
* **Suggestions (Page 11)** (Text, Optional) - Suggest potential improvements or solutions.
* **Comments (Page 12)** (Text, Optional) - Additional comments or observations.
* **Submit (Page 13)** (Text, Optional) - Submit your feedback.
