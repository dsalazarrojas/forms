# Meeting Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Effectiveness Survey is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of a meeting. This survey aims to collect information on the relevance and usefulness of the meeting, as well as gather demographic information to help tailor future meetings to the attendees' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant ratings and descriptions for each question on the first page of the form.
2. Rate the relevance of the meeting on questions 2 and 4.
3. Provide a brief description of your thoughts on prophets, question 3.
4. Select the correct meeting from the list on question 5.
5. Provide your date and time of attendance on questions 6 and 7.
6. Enter your email address on question 8.
7. Optionally, provide your phone number on question 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Effectiveness** (meeting_effectiveness_survey_start, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose one or more of the options "Yes" to indicate that the meeting was effective, or "No" if not.
- **Relevance of the Meeting** (question_2, Number, Not Required): On a scale of 1 to 10, rate the relevance of this meeting. A higher rating indicates a greater sense of relevance.
- **Prophets** (question_3, Text, Not Required): Please describe any prophets or messages received during the meeting that resonated with you.
- **Relevance of the Meeting** (question_4, Number, Not Required): Similar to question 2, rate the relevance of this meeting on a scale of 1 to 10.
- **Meeting** (question_5, Select One, Not Required): Select the meeting this form is related to.
- **Date** (question_6, Date, Not Required): Provide the date on which you attended the meeting.
- **Time** (question_7, Time, Not Required): Provide the start and end time of the meeting.
- **Email** (question_8, Email, Not Required): Enter your email address for follow-up or communication.
- **Phone** (question_9, Text, Not Required): Optionally, provide your phone number for further communication or follow-up.
