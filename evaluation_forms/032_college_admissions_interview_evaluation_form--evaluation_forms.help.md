# College Admissions Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The College Admissions Interview Evaluation Form is a tool used by interviewers to assess the fitment of applicants to our institution. It helps evaluate the applicant's communication skills, intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and other qualities that are essential for a successful college experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the interviewer's name and email address.
2. Select the interview date and start time.
3. Choose the interview location (on campus, off campus, virtual video, or phone).
4. Select the interview duration (15-20 minutes, 20-30 minutes, 30-45 minutes, 45-60 minutes, or over 60 minutes).
5. Enter the applicant's name and ID number (if available).
6. Enter the applicant's high school name and graduation year.
7. Rate the applicant's communication skills, intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and other qualities.
8. Enter specific comments about the applicant's communication skills, intellectual curiosity, and other qualities.
9. Rate the overall impression of the applicant.
10. Enter any concerns or red flags about the applicant.
11. Recommend whether or not to admit the applicant (strongly advocate, advocate, neutral, have reservations, or do not recommend).
12. Enter any additional comments about the applicant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interviewer Full Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Email Address** (`interviewer_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title** (`interviewer_title`, text, required): Enter your position title or role.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the interview.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, select_one, required): Choose the location where the interview was held (on campus, off campus, virtual video, or phone).
* **Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the interview (15-20 minutes, 20-30 minutes, 30-45 minutes, 45-60 minutes, or over 60 minutes).
* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the applicant.
* **Applicant ID Number** (`applicant_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID number of the applicant, if available.
* **Applicant High School** (`applicant_high_school`, text, required): Enter the name of the applicant's high school.
* **Graduation Year** (`applicant_graduation_year`, number, required): Enter the year the applicant graduated.
* **Intended Major** (`intended_major`, text, required): Enter the major the applicant is interested in.
* **Communication Skills Rating** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's verbal expression and communication skills (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Communication Skills Comments** (`communication_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's communication skills.
* **Intellectual Curiosity Rating** (`intellectual_curiosity`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's love of learning and intellectual curiosity (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Intellectual Curiosity Comments** (`intellectual_curiosity_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's intellectual curiosity.
* **Critical Thinking Rating** (`critical_thinking`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's analysis abilities (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Critical Thinking Comments** (`critical_thinking_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's critical thinking.
* **Enthusiasm for Our College Rating** (`enthusiasm_for_college`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's interest level in our college (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Enthusiasm Comments** (`enthusiasm_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's enthusiasm.
* **Knowledge of Our College Rating** (`knowledge_of_college`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's knowledge about our college (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Knowledge Comments** (`knowledge_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's knowledge.
* **Personal Qualities Rating** (`personal_qualities`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's character and maturity (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Personal Qualities Comments** (`personal_qualities_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's personal qualities.
* **Extracurricular Involvement Rating** (`extracurricular_involvement`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's engagement in extracurricular activities (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Extracurricular Comments** (`extracurricular_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's extracurricular activities.
* **Clarity of Goals Rating** (`goals_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's future plans and goals (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Clarity of Goals Comments** (`goals_comments`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's goals.
* **Quality of Questions Asked** (`questions_asked`, select_one, required): Rate the applicant's ability to ask thoughtful questions (excellent, very good, good, fair, poor, or no questions asked).
* **Overall Impression Rating** (`overall_impression`, select_one, required): Rate the overall impression of the applicant (excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor).
* **Standout Qualities** (`standout_qualities`, text, optional): Enter specific comments about the applicant's standout qualities.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, text, optional): Enter any concerns or issues about the applicant.
* **Would You Advocate for Admission** (`would_advocate`, select_one, required): Recommend whether or not to admit the applicant (strongly advocate, advocate, neutral, have reservations, or do not recommend).
* **Admission Recommendation** (`admit_recommendation`, select_one, required): Make a final decision about admitting the applicant (strong admit, admit, waitlist, deny).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other comments or observations about the applicant.
