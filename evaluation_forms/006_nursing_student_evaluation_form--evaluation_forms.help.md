# Nursing Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluate the experience of nursing students in a class or course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Select the course: Choose the name of the course you are evaluating.
3. Answer the questions: Provide your honest opinion on the instructor's performance and your overall experience in the course.
4. Submit: Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to the instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (Form Title, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your evaluation.
* **Course Name** (Course Name, text, required/optional): Type in the name of the course you are taking.
* **Instructor Name** (Instructor Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **Classroom Experience** (Classroom Experience, text, required/optional): Describe your experience in the classroom environment.
* **Teacher Performance** (Teacher Performance, text, required/optional): Evaluate the performance of your teacher.
* **Classwork** (Classwork, text, required/optional): Rate the classwork and assignments provided.
* **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, text, required/optional): Share your overall thoughts on the course.
* **Suggestions** (Suggestions, text, required/optional): Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required/optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Course Materials** (Course Materials, text, required/optional): Rate the course materials and resources provided.
* **Instructor Availability** (Instructor Availability, text, required/optional): Evaluate the availability and response time of your instructor.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your opinions.
* Keep your answers clear and concise.
* Remember, this form is a confidential and anonymous evaluation.
