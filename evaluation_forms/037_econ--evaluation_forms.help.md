# ECON - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ECON form is used to collect information about users' purpose for sharing information and collected information on a personal page. This form helps us understand how users are utilizing the platform and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal page information on the first page.
2. Select the purpose of sharing information on the second page.
3. If necessary, provide additional information on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **personal_page_info** (`text`, required: false): Enter your personal page information here. You can provide any relevant details about your page.
* **information_sharing_purpose** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply to the purpose of sharing information. You may choose "to share" or "for research" as your information sharing purpose.
* **collected_info** (`note`, required: false): If you have any additional information to share, please provide it here. This is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant details.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any) and provide accurate information.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
