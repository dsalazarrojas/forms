# Evaluation Of Relevance And Reliability - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evaluation Of Relevance And Reliability form is used to assess the relevance and reliability of information or sources. It is designed to be filled out by assessors or evaluators to provide a clear understanding of a source's relevance and reliability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes the evaluation.
2. Provide information about the source, including its type and any associated link.
3. Enter your rating of the source's relevance and reliability.
4. Add any additional comments or notes related to the evaluation.
5. Select the team or teams assigned to review the evaluation.
6. Choose the status of the evaluation.
7. Repeat the evaluation process for any additional sources.
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information** (`form_information`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of the evaluation being performed.
* **Evaluation Category** (`evaluation_category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the category that best describes the evaluation (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Evaluation Reliability** (`evaluation_reliability`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the source's reliability on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Evaluation Relevance** (`evaluation_relevance`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the source's relevance on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes related to the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Source Type** (`evaluation_source_type`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate whether the source is a 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if it is a source or not.
* **Evaluation Source Link** (`evaluation_source_link`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the associated link to the source.
* **Evaluation Rating** (`evaluation_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the source's overall rating (1 to 10).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the team(s) assigned to review the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments related to the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Status** (`evaluation_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the status of the evaluation (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
