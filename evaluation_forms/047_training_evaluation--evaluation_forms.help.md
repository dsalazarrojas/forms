# Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness of a training session, enabling trainers and instructors to assess the program's quality, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions for future trainings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question or field carefully, and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most accurate response to each multiple-choice question.
3. Provide free-text answers to open-ended questions, offering specific comments and feedback.
4. Fill out all required fields. Some fields are optional, but providing additional feedback can help trainers and instructors better understand your experience and make positive changes.
