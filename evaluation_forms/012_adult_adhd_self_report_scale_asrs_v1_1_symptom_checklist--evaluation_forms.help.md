# Adult ADHD Self Report Scale (ASRS V1.1) Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Adult ADHD Self Report Scale (ASRS V1.1) Symptom Checklist is a comprehensive assessment tool used to evaluate the severity of ADHD symptoms in adults. This self-report questionnaire helps identify the frequency and presence of ADHD symptoms that are essential for diagnosis and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields marked as `true` in bold text (e.g., **Patient Name**, **Evaluation Date**).
2.  For select fields (e.g., **Frequency of Difficulty Finishing Details**, **Frequency of Difficulty Getting Organized**), choose the answer that best describes your experiences over the past 6 months.
3.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, taking your time to reflect on your symptoms.
