# Job Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge an employee's job satisfaction, contentment, and perception of their manager's support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly, and take as much time as you need to complete the form.
2. If you are unsure about a question, please feel free to ask your HR representative for clarification.
3. The form is split into two main pages. Please complete all questions on each page before moving on to the next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This question asks how satisfied you are with your job overall. Please select 'Satisfied' if you are content with your job, or 'Dissatisfied' if you are not.
* **Job Satisfaction Rating** (`job_satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): This question asks you to rate your job satisfaction on a scale. Please choose a number between 1 (very dissatisfied) and 5 (very satisfied).
* **Job Contentment** (`job_contentment`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks if you feel content with your job. Please select 'Yes' if you feel content, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your perception of your manager's support. Please select 'Yes' if you feel supported, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Job Satisfaction Score** (`job_satisfaction_score`, number, required: false): This question asks you to rate your overall job satisfaction. Please choose a number between 1 (very dissatisfied) and 5 (very satisfied).
* **Main Page 2**: This is a note section for any additional thoughts or comments you may have.
* **Open-Ended Question**: This question allows you to provide any additional feedback or thoughts about your job.
* **Manager Support 2** (`manager_support_2`, select_one, required: false): This question is similar to the previous question, but asks about your perception of your manager's support on a different occasion.
* **Job Satisfaction Score 2** (`job_satisfaction_score_2`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to rate your overall job satisfaction on a different occasion.
* **Job Contentment 2** (`job_contentment_2`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks if you feel content with your job on a different occasion. Please select 'Yes' if you feel content, or 'No' if you do not.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
* Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our work environment.
