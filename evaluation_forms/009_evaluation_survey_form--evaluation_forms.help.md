# Evaluation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students on the quality of the educational experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields. These fields are marked as "required" in the form.
3. You can leave optional fields blank if you are unsure or do not have the information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (student_name)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your university-provided email address or a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your contact number where we can reach you.
* **School Experience (school_experience)**: Share your thoughts about your educational experience.
* **Education Quality (education_quality)**: Rate your satisfaction with the quality of education provided at your school.
* **Suggestions (suggestions)**: Share any suggestions or comments you have about the educational experience.
* **First Page Suggestions (first_page_suggestions)**: Share any suggestions or comments you have about the first page of the evaluation system.
* **Second Page (second_page)**: Share your thoughts about the second page of the evaluation system.
* **Second Page Suggestions (second_page_suggestions)**: Share any suggestions or comments you have about the second page of the evaluation system.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
