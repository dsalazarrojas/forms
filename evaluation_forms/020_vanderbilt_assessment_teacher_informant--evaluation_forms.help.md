# vanderbilt_assessment_teacher_informant - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vanderbilt Assessment Teacher Informant form is used to gather information from teachers about students' behavior, social skills, and emotional regulation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the options for each page and select the most accurate responses based on your observations of the student.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Save the form and submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Observation Period (Page 1)
• **Observation Period**: This field asks you to indicate if you have observed the student during a specific observation period (Yes/No). This helps us understand if you have direct experience with the student in a particular time frame.
### Attention (Page 2)
• **Attention**: Select "Yes" if the student is able to focus and maintain attention, "No" otherwise. This helps us assess the student's ability to focus in class.
### Mood (Page 3)
• **Mood**: Indicate if the student's mood is generally positive (Yes/No), or if they exhibit signs of anxiety, anger, or other negative emotions (No). This helps us gauge the student's emotional state.
### Activities (Page 4)
• **Activities**: Choose "Yes" if the student participates actively and completes tasks, "No" otherwise. This helps us evaluate the student's engagement in activities.
### Social Interactions (Page 5)
• **Social Interactions**: Select "Yes" if the student interacts positively with peers and teachers, "No" otherwise. This helps us assess the student's social skills.
### Communication (Page 6)
• **Communication**: Indicate if the student communicates effectively with peers and adults (Yes/No), or if they have difficulty expressing themselves (No). This helps us evaluate the student's communication skills.
### Independence (Page 7)
• **Independence**: Choose "Yes" if the student is able to complete tasks independently, "No" otherwise. This helps us assess the student's ability to work independently.
### Responsibility (Page 8)
• **Responsibility**: Select "Yes" if the student demonstrates responsibility in their daily routines (Yes/No), or if they have difficulty taking care of themselves and their belongings (No). This helps us evaluate the student's ability to take care of themselves.
### Relatedness (Page 9)
• **Relatedness**: Indicate if the student forms positive relationships with peers and teachers (Yes/No), or if they struggle to form connections (No). This helps us assess the student's ability to form relationships.

## Tips
- Be honest in your observations, and only respond based on your direct experience with the student.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please select "No" or discuss with your supervisor or school counselor.
- Please submit the form as soon as possible after completing the observations.
- If you need assistance or clarification, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or school counselor.
