# Business Client Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information about your business clients, including their contact information, business characteristics, and financial and operational details. This data will help us better understand your business needs and provide tailored support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Client Name
	* Client Company
	* Client Email
	* Client Phone
	* Industry Sector
	* Client Relationship Length
	* Client Annual Revenue
	* Number of Employees
	* Market Position
	* Growth Trajectory
	* Financial Health
	* Credit Rating/History
	* Overall Risk Level
	* Operational Efficiency
	* Technology Adoption Level
	* Management Quality
	* Overall Recommendation

2. For optional fields:
	* Key Risks or Concerns
	* Business Opportunity Assessment

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required)
Client's full name, as per company records or contact information.
### 2. **Client Company** (Client Company, text, required)
Client's company name, as per company records or contact information.
### 3. **Client Email** (Client Email, email, required)
Client's business email address.
### 4. **Client Phone** (Client Phone, text, required)
Client's business contact number.
### 5. **Industry Sector** (Industry Sector, text, required)
Client's primary industry.
### 6. **Client Relationship Length** (Client Relationship Length, select_one, required)
How long you have worked with the client. Choose from:
	* Less than 6 months
	* 6-12 months
	* 1-2 years
	* 2-5 years
	* 5+ years
### 7. **Client Annual Revenue** (Client Annual Revenue, select_one, required)
Approximate annual revenue range.
	* Under 1 million
	* 1-5 million
	* 5-10 million
	* 10-50 million
	* 50-100 million
	* 100+ million
### 8. **Number of Employees** (Number of Employees, select_one, required)
Total employee count.
	* 1-10
	* 11-50
	* 51-250
	* 251-1000
	* 1000+
### 9. **Market Position** (Market Position, select_one, required)
Client's competitive position in their industry.
	* Market Leader
	* Strong Competitor
	* Stable Player
	* Emerging Player
	* Niche Provider
### 10. **Growth Trajectory** (Growth Trajectory, select_one, required)
Current business growth status.
	* Rapidly Growing
	* Steady Growth
	* Stable
	* Declining
	* Turnaround Mode
### 11. **Financial Health** (Financial Health, select_one, required)
Overall financial condition.
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Concerning
	* At Risk
### 12. **Credit Rating/History** (Credit Rating/History, select_one, required)
Payment history assessment.
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
	* No History
### 13. **Operational Efficiency** (Operational Efficiency, select_one, required)
How efficiently client operates.
	* Highly Efficient
	* Efficient
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Inefficient
### 14. **Technology Adoption Level** (Technology Adoption Level, select_one, required)
Client's use of modern technology.
	* Advanced
	* Above Average
	* Average
	* Below Average
	* Minimal
### 15. **Management Quality** (Management Quality, select_one, required)
Quality of client management team.
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Competent
	* Needs Improvement
	* Poor
### 16. **Overall Recommendation** (Overall Recommendation, select_one, required)
Your assessment recommendation.
	* Highly Recommended
	* Recommended
	* Proceed with Caution
	* Not Recommended
	* Further Review Needed

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out every required field accurately and completely.
* Provide as much detail as possible for optional fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the responsible party for guidance.
