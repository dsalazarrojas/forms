# Medical Science Liaison Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for evaluating the performance of a candidate during a medical science liaison interview. The evaluator will assess the candidate's knowledge, skills, and behavior, and provide feedback on their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Candidate Details**: Enter the candidate's name and any other relevant information about them.
2.  **Interview Questions**: Record the answers to the interview questions asked to the candidate.
3.  **Evaluation Scores**: Assess the candidate's performance by assigning scores out of a maximum of 100.
4.  **Evaluator Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the candidate's performance.
5.  **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., Dr., Prof., etc.).
6.  **Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the interview.
7.  **Evaluator Email**: Enter your email address.
8.  **Evaluator Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details**: A field for entering the candidate's details.
* **Interview Questions**: A field for recording the answers to the interview questions asked to the candidate.
* **Evaluation Scores**: A field for assessing the candidate's performance by assigning scores out of 100.
* **Evaluator Comments**: A field for providing any additional feedback or comments about the candidate's performance.
* **Evaluator Name**: A field for entering your name and title.
* **Date and Time**: A field for recording the date and time of the interview.
* **Evaluator Email**: A field for entering your email address.
* **Evaluator Phone**: A field for entering your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Evaluate the candidate's performance based on the assessment criteria provided.
* Keep the comments section concise and to the point.
* Use the scores and comments to guide future evaluations.
* If you need any support or clarification, contact your supervisor or HR representative.

Please note that this form is not intended to be used as a comprehensive evaluation tool, but as a starting point for more in-depth evaluation and assessment.
