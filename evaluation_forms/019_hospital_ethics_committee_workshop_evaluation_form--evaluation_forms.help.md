# Hospital Ethics Committee Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback and evaluation of the Hospital Ethics Committee Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. 
2. Select the options that best describe your experience and opinion.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is where you can briefly introduce yourself and mention your role in the workshop.
* **workshop_topic**: Please select the topic that best describes the focus of the workshop you attended.
* **Evaluation Form**: These multiple-choice questions will help us evaluate your overall satisfaction and experience with the workshop. Select "Yes" if you agree or disagree with each statement.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback about the workshop, please provide them here.
* **Workshop Attendees**: List all the attendees you met during the workshop.
* **Workshop Facilitators**: List all the facilitators or instructors who conducted the workshop.
* **Date**: Please provide the date you attended the workshop.
* **Time**: Provide the time you attended the workshop.
* **Evaluation Form 2**: These multiple-choice questions will help us evaluate your experience with specific sessions or topics during the workshop.
* **Evaluation Form 3**: These multiple-choice questions will help us evaluate your experience with specific sessions or topics during the workshop.
* **Email**: Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Submitted By**: You can provide any name you like for the record.
* **Submitted At**: Please provide the date and time you are submitting the form.
* **Workshop Length**: If you wish to provide the duration of the workshop you attended.
* **Total Hours**: If you wish to provide the total hours you attended the workshop.
* **Session 1 Attendance**: Please select if you attended Session 1 or not.
* **Session 2 Attendance**: Please select if you attended Session 2 or not.
* **Session 3 Attendance**: Please select if you attended Session 3 or not.
* **Total Attendance**: If you wish to provide the number of attendees you saw at the workshop.
* **Workshop Fees**: If you wish to provide the fees you paid for the workshop.
* **Evaluation Form 4**: These multiple-choice questions will help us evaluate your experience with specific sessions or topics during the workshop.
* **Workshop Satisfaction**: If you wish to provide additional comments about your satisfaction with the workshop.
* **Workshop Recommendation**: If you wish to provide recommendations for future workshops.
* **Submit**: Please submit the form when complete to help us evaluate the workshop.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out to submit the form.
