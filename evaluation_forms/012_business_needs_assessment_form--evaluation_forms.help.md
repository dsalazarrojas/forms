# Business Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Needs Assessment Form is designed to evaluate a company's internal and external factors, helping identify areas that require improvement and provide recommendations for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of this assessment.
2. **Assessor Name**: Provide your name and role.
3. **Assessor Title**: Enter your position or role within the organization.
4. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the organization being assessed.
5. **Company Size**: Select the approximate number of employees in the company.
6. **Primary Industry**: Enter the main business sector of the organization.
7. **Assessment Focus Area**: Select the area being evaluated (e.g., Operations, Technology, Finance, etc.).
8. **Organizational Structure Assessment**: Rate the clarity of roles and responsibilities on a scale of 1-5.
9. **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate internal communication quality on a scale of 1-5.
10. **Process Documentation**: Rate documentation completeness on a scale of 1-5.
11. **Technology Infrastructure**: Rate technology adequacy on a scale of 1-5.
12. **Employee Skills Assessment**: Rate employee capability on a scale of 1-5.
13. **Financial Management**: Rate financial controls on a scale of 1-5.
14. **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate customer satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5. (Note: This field is not required, as the assessment may not pertain to customer-facing operations.)
15. **Competitive Position**: Rate competitive strength on a scale of 1-5.
16. **Primary Business Strengths**: List the main strengths of the organization.
17. **Primary Business Weaknesses**: List the main weaknesses of the organization.
18. **Immediate Needs**: Identify areas that require urgent attention.
19. **Resource Requirements**: If applicable, list resources needed for improvement.
20. **Timeline for Improvement**: Select the suggested improvement timeline (0-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, or 12+ months).
21. **Recommended Actions**: Provide specific recommendations for improvement.
