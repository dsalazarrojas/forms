# Individual Self-Assessment Form For Program Management Office - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess an individual's performance and progress in specific skills, goals, and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please refer to the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self Assessment Form** (`self_assessment_form`): A text field for the individual to describe their self-assessment of their performance.
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`): A text field for the individual to describe their progress towards their goals and objectives.
* **Skills and Strengths** (`skills_and_strengths`): A text field for the individual to identify their skills and strengths.
* **Weaknesses and Areas of Improvement** (`weaknesses_areas_of_improvement`): A text field for the individual to identify their weaknesses and areas for improvement.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`): A date field for the individual to indicate the period being evaluated.
* **Next Evaluation Period** (`next_evaluation_period`): A date field for the individual to indicate the next evaluation period.
* **Mentor Notes** (`mentor_notes`): A text field for the individual's mentor to record their notes and feedback.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`): A text field for the individual's manager to record their notes and feedback.
* **Evaluation Score 1-5** (`evaluation_score_1`, `evaluation_score_2`, `evaluation_score_3`, `evaluation_score_4`, `evaluation_score_5`): Numerical fields for the individual to provide scores for their performance.
* **Select Score** (`select_score`): A select field for the individual to choose one option that best describes their performance.
* **Select Score 2** (`select_score_2`): A select multiple field for the individual to choose multiple options that best describe their performance.
