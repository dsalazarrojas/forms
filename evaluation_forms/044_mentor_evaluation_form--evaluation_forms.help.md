# Mentor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of a mentor in their role. The form is designed to collect feedback on the mentor's strengths, areas for improvement, and overall performance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
3. Select only the options that apply to your evaluation.
4. Save your progress and come back to it later if needed.
