# Client Evaluation Of Company Services - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from clients to help us assess our performance and identify areas for improvement. The evaluation will provide valuable insights to our team to enhance our services and better serve our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Carefully read each question and consider your experience with our services
* Enter your responses in the corresponding fields
* Use the available options or provide text answers where applicable
* Make sure to complete all required fields

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first and last name as it appears on your official documents (<code>client_first_name</code>) 
	+ This field is required and should match the information we have on file.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your official documents (<code>client_last_name</code>) 
	+ This field is required and should match the information we have on file.
* **Email Address**: Your primary contact email (<code>client_email</code>) 
	+ This field is required and should be the email we have on file.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number (<code>client_phone</code>) 
	+ This field is optional and can be used for follow-up communication.
* **Company Name**: Your business name (<code>company_name</code>) 
	+ This field is optional and can be your personal name if you are a sole proprietor.
* **Service Category**: The type of service you received from us (<code>service_category</code>) 
	+ Please select from the available categories, and we will use this information to tailor our services to your needs.
* **Service Duration**: How long you have been a client (<code>service_duration</code>) 
	+ This field is required to help us understand our relationship with you.
* **Account Manager Name**: Your primary contact person (<code>account_manager</code>) 
	+ This field is optional and can be used to direct any follow-up communication.
* **Overall Service Quality Rating**: Your overall assessment of our service quality (<code>service_quality_rating</code>) 
	+ Rate from Excellent to Poor to provide us with a clear picture of our performance.
* **Deliverable Quality**: The quality of our work products (<code>deliverable_quality</code>) 
	+ Rate from Excellent to Poor to help us improve our deliverables.
* **Timeliness of Service Delivery**: Our on-time performance (<code>timeliness</code>) 
	+ Rate from Always On Time to Always Late to help us assess our timeliness.
* **Communication Rating**: How well we communicate with you (<code>communication_rating</code>) 
	+ Rate from Excellent to Poor to help us improve our communication.
* **Responsiveness to Inquiries**: How quickly we respond to your inquiries (<code>responsiveness</code>) 
	+ Rate from Excellent to Poor to help us assess our responsiveness.
* **Problem-Solving Ability**: Our ability to solve problems (<code>problem_solving</code>) 
	+ Rate from Excellent to Poor to help us assess our problem-solving skills.
* **Level of Expertise Demonstrated**: The knowledge and expertise of our staff (<code>expertise_level</code>) 
	+ Rate from Expert to Below Average to help us improve our staff knowledge.
* **Value for Money**: Is our service worth the cost (<code>value_for_money</code>) 
	+ Rate from Excellent Value to Very Poor Value to help us improve our pricing and value proposition.
* **Expectations Met**: How well did we meet your expectations (<code>expectations_met</code>) 
	+ Rate from Exceeded Expectations to Far Below Expectations to help us improve our service offerings.
* **Service Reliability**: Our consistency in service delivery (<code>service_reliability</code>) 
	+ Rate from Very Reliable to Very Unreliable to help us improve our reliability.
* **Satisfaction with Contract Terms**: Your satisfaction with our agreement terms (<code>contract_terms</code>) 
	+ This field is optional and can be used to help us improve our contracts and agreements.
* **Billing Accuracy**: The accuracy of our invoices (<code>billing_accuracy</code>) 
	+ This field is optional and can be used to help us improve our billing processes.
* **Comparison to Competitors**: How do we compare to our competitors (<code>competitive_advantage</code>) 
	+ Rate from Much Better to Much Worse to help us improve our competitive edge.
* **Plan to Continue Services**: Do you plan to continue using our services (<code>continue_services</code>) 
	+ Rate from Definitely Yes to Definitely No to help us understand our client retention.
* **Expand Services with Us**: Would you consider expanding services with us (<code>expand_services</code>) 
	+ Rate from Definitely Yes to Definitely No to help us explore new opportunities.
* **Our Greatest Strengths**: What do we do well (<code>service_strengths</code>) 
	+ Provide any specific strengths or services we excel in.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Where can we improve (<code>improvement_areas</code>) 
	+ Provide any areas where we can improve our services.
* **Additional Services Needed**: What additional services do you need (<code>additional_services_needed</code>) 
	+ Provide any additional services you require or are interested in.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or comments (<code>additional_comments</code>) 
	+ Provide any other comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Evaluation Date**: The date of this evaluation (<code>evaluation_date</code>) 
	+ This field is required and will be auto-populated by our system.
