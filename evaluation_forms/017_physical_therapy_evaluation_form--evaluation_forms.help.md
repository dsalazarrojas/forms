# Physical Therapy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a physical therapy evaluation, which is used to assess the patient's health status, evaluate their physical therapy progress, and record any relevant comments or observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Fill out the patient's basic information, including any relevant medical history or contact details.
2. **Physical Therapy Information (Page 1)**: Provide details about the patient's physical therapy sessions, including dates, times, and any notable events or milestones.
3. **Evaluation Date (Page 1)**: Enter the date of the evaluation, which is when the physical therapist conducted the assessment.
4. **Patient Health Status (Page 2)**: Select the current health status of the patient from the options provided:
	* **Excellent**: The patient is experiencing excellent health and is recovering well.
	* **Good**: The patient is experiencing good health, but may have some minor issues.
	* **Fair**: The patient is experiencing fair health, with noticeable limitations or concerns.
	* **Poor**: The patient is experiencing poor health, with significant limitations or concerns.
5. **Evaluation Comments (Page 2)**: Record any relevant comments or observations made during the evaluation. This is a free-text field where you can provide detailed information about the patient's condition, progress, or any notable events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's full name, age, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Physical Therapy Information (Page 1)**: Describe the patient's physical therapy sessions, including dates, times, and any notable events or milestones.
* **Evaluation Date (Page 1)**: Enter the date of the evaluation, which is when the physical therapist conducted the assessment.
* **Patient Health Status (Page 2)**: Select the current health status of the patient from the options provided.
* **Evaluation Comments (Page 2)**: Record any relevant comments or observations made during the evaluation, providing detailed information about the patient's condition, progress, or any notable events.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Use the evaluation comments field to record any notable events or observations that may not fit into the pre-defined options.
- If a patient has multiple evaluations, use the evaluation date field to track progress over time.
- Review and update the form as needed to accommodate changing patient information or therapy progress.
