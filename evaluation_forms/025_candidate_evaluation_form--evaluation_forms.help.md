# Candidate Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Candidate Evaluation Form is used by interviewers to provide an objective evaluation of a candidate's performance during an interview. It helps hiring managers and recruiters make informed decisions about the candidate's potential fit for a role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the Candidate Name field with the full name of the applicant.
2. Provide the Position Applied For field with the title of the position the candidate is being evaluated for.
3. Enter your name in the Interviewer Name field.
4. Select the Date of Interview from the calendar or enter it manually.
5. Evaluate the candidate's Educational Background, Relevant Experience, Technical Skills, Communication Skills, Leadership Potential, Problem-Solving Ability, and Cultural Fit using the provided scale ratings.
6. Provide a brief description of the candidate's Key Strengths and Areas for Development.
7. Enter the Overall Evaluation Score out of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
8. Select a final recommendation from the options: Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Strongly Do Not Recommend.
9. If hired, suggest a proposed compensation package.
10. Finally, mark the Date of Evaluation Completion.

## Tips

* Be as objective and honest as possible when providing evaluations and recommendations.
* Use the rating scales consistently and fairly.
* Provide detailed descriptions for Key Strengths and Areas for Development.
* Consider the company's values and culture when making recommendations.
* Keep the Overall Evaluation Score in mind when making the final hiring decision.
* Suggest a proposed compensation package if hired, taking into account the candidate's performance and the company's budget.
