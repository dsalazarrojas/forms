# Swimming Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Swimming Assessment Form is used to collect information from swimmer participants to assess their swimming abilities, experience, and preferences. The form is designed to gather data that can help swim instructors, coaches, or program directors make informed decisions about swimmer placement, skill level, and lesson plans.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Select your gender from the "Gender" field.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
6. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
7. Provide any relevant medical information in the "Medical Info" field.
8. Enter the date you are assessed for swimming in the "Assessment Date" field.
9. Select how much time you spend in swimming pools from the "Time in Pools" field.
10. Indicate whether you are comfortable swimming in deep water from the "Water Depth" field.
11. Indicate whether you are comfortable swimming in cold water from the "Water Temperature" field.
12. Indicate whether you swim in open water from the "Water Type" field.
13. Indicate how frequently you swim from the "Swim Frequency" field.
14. Indicate your swim style, such as freestyle or breaststroke, from the "Swim Style" field.
15. Select the specific swimming strokes you are proficient in from the "Swim Strokes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`swimmer_information`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`swimmer_information_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`swimmer_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`swimmer_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the available options.
* **Contact Info** (`swimmer_contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Emergency Contact** (`swimmer_emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Info** (`swimmer_medical_info`, note, required): Provide any relevant medical information that may affect your swimming abilities or safety.
* **Assessment Date** (`swimmer_assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time in Pools** (`swimmer_time_in_pools`, time, required): Select how much time you spend in swimming pools.
* **Water Depth** (`swimmer_water_depth`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are comfortable swimming in deep water.
* **Water Temperature** (`swimmer_water_temperature`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are comfortable swimming in cold water.
* **Water Type** (`swimmer_water_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you swim in open water.
* **Swim Frequency** (`swimmer_swim_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how frequently you swim.
* **Swim Style** (`swimmer_swim_style`, select_one, required): Indicate your swim style, such as freestyle or breaststroke.
* **Swim Strokes** (`swimmer_swim_strokes`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific swimming strokes you are proficient in.
