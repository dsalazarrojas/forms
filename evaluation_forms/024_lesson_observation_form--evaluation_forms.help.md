# Lesson Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lesson Observation Form is a tool used to collect and evaluate information about a teacher's lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the relevant information for the observed lesson.
2. Make sure to enter the correct school ID, date, and teacher's name and email.
3. Select the correct lesson subject, topic, and grade level from the available options.
4. Enter the observation start and end times to record the duration of the lesson.
5. Leave comments and notes about the lesson to provide feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lesson Observation Form**:
	+ This is the main form title.
	+ Please fill out this field with the name of the lesson being observed.
* **School Id** (Number):
	+ Enter the ID of the school where the lesson took place.
	+ If unsure, check with the school administration for the correct ID.
* **Date Of Observation** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of the lesson observation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Lesson Teacher Id** (Text):
	+ Enter the ID of the teacher who conducted the lesson.
	+ If unsure, check with the school administration for the correct ID.
* **School Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the school where the lesson took place.
* **Lesson Subject** (Text):
	+ Enter the subject of the lesson being observed.
* **Lesson Topic** (Text):
	+ Enter the topic of the lesson being observed.
* **Teacher Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the teacher who conducted the lesson.
* **Teacher Email** (Email):
	+ Enter the email of the teacher who conducted the lesson.
* **Teacher Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the teacher who conducted the lesson.
* **Class Level** (Text):
	+ Enter the grade level of the class being observed.
* **Student Count** (Text):
	+ Enter the number of students present during the lesson.
* **Observation Start Time** (Time):
	+ Enter the start time of the lesson in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Observation End Time** (Time):
	+ Enter the end time of the lesson in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Comments** (Note):
	+ Enter any observations or comments about the lesson.
* **School Type** (Text):
	+ Enter the type of school where the lesson took place (e.g., public, private).
* **School Status** (Text):
	+ Enter the status of the school (e.g., operational, closed).
* **School Location** (Text):
	+ Enter the location of the school.
* **Teacher Level** (Text):
	+ Enter the level of the teacher's experience.
* **Lesson Duration** (Text):
	+ Enter the duration of the lesson in minutes.
* **Teacher Experience** (Text):
	+ Enter the teacher's level of experience (e.g., years, months).
* **Teacher Education** (Text):
	+ Enter the teacher's educational background.
* **School Grade** (Text):
	+ Enter the grade level of the school.
* **Lesson Notes** (Note):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about the lesson.
* **Teacher Rating** (Number):
	+ Enter a rating of the teacher's performance (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Additional Tips**:
	+ Review all fields before submission to ensure accuracy.
	+ If unsure about any field, check with the school administration or relevant parties for guidance.
	+ Use this form regularly to track teacher performance over time.
