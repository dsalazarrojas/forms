# Vendor Reference Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from end-users about a vendor's reference, which is an individual or organization that can vouch for the vendor's quality of work, reliability, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Only fill out the form for vendors who have been previously vetted or are being evaluated for a new opportunity.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
4. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor you are referencing.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the company name where the vendor worked with.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the vendor's company.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor's company.
* **Past Reference Name** (`past_reference_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who can vouch for the vendor.
* **Past Reference Title** (`past_reference_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person who can vouch for the vendor.
* **Past Reference Company** (`past_reference_company`, text, optional): Enter the company where the vendor's reference worked with.
* **Past Reference Start Date** (`past_reference_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the vendor's reference's employment with the company.
