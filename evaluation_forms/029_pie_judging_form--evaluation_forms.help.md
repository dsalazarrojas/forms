# Pie Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pie Judging Form is a tool used to collect information from judges during pie-evaluation events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the competitor's name in the "Competitor Name" field.
2. Enter the title of the pie being evaluated in the "Pie Title" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the pie in the "Pie Description" field.
4. Select the category of the pie from the "Category ID" dropdown menu.
5. Rate the pie using one or more of the available ratings in the "Rating" field.
6. Enter any comments or observations about the pie in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter a score for the competitor out of a total possible score in the "Competitor Score" field.
8. Enter a score for you as the judge out of a total possible score in the "Judge Score" field.
9. Finally, select the date of the evaluation from the "Evaluation Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competitor Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the competitor being evaluated.
* **Pie Title** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief title of the pie being evaluated.
* **Pie Description** (text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the pie.
* **Category ID** (select one, required/optional): Select the category of the pie from the available options.
* **Rating** (select multiple, required/optional): Rate the pie using one or more of the available ratings.
* **Comments** (text, required/optional): Enter any comments or observations about the pie.
* **Competitor Score** (number, required/optional): Enter a score for the competitor out of a total possible score.
* **Judge Score** (number, required/optional): Enter a score for you as the judge out of a total possible score.
* **Evaluation Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of the evaluation.
