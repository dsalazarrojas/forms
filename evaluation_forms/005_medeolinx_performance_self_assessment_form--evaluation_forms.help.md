# MedeoLinx Performance Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals evaluate their own performance in a specific period of time. It allows them to assess their progress, identify areas that need improvement, and provide feedback on their performance. This form can be used as a tool for personal growth and self-awareness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the performance period you wish to evaluate (e.g., Jan, Feb, Mar, etc.).
2. Choose how you would rate your self-assessment (e.g., Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory).
3. In the "Goals and Achievements" section, write down any goals and achievements you have accomplished during the selected period.
4. If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement, you can write them down in the "Feedback" section.
5. Select your performance level (e.g., Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Performance Period**: This field allows you to choose the period you wish to evaluate your performance in (e.g., Jan, Feb, Mar, etc.). Please select the correct period from the provided options.
- **Self Assessment**: This field is where you rate your self-assessment, indicating how you feel about your performance (e.g., Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory).
- **Goals and Achievements**: In this section, write down any specific goals and achievements you have accomplished during the selected period.
- **Feedback**: If you have any comments or suggestions for improvement, this is the place to write them down.
- **Performance Level**: Select your performance level based on how you feel about your progress (e.g., Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations).

## Tips
- Take your time to reflect on your performance and progress.
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Use specific examples and details to support your self-assessment.
- Review and revise your answers as needed before submitting the form.
