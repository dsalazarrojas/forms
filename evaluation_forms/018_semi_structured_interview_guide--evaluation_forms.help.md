# semi_structured_interview_guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Semi-Structured Interview Guide is a form designed to collect detailed information from researchers and interviewees. It captures crucial details about the research process, including researcher and interviewee information, interview settings, and the structure of the interviews. This guide is essential for conducting thorough and comprehensive interviews, ensuring that all necessary data is collected for analysis and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in your researcher information, which includes your name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the interview information, such as the number of questions, the interview location, and the date.
3. Provide a list of questions for the interview, specifying whether they are open-ended, closed-ended, or both.
4. Select the type of questionnaire to be used, whether open-ended, closed-ended, or both.
5. Enter any notes or comments from the researcher and interviewee.
6. Indicate if the interview was conducted by you or someone else.
7. Specify the time required for the interview.
8. Enter the location and any other details about the interview setting.
9. Select the skills you, as the researcher, possess, such as active listening and questioning.
10. Enter any comments or notes from the researcher.
11. Enter any additional details about the interview plan.
12. Finally, enter the interviewee's information, including their phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Researcher Information** (Researcher Information, note, required): Enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
