# Team Building Activity Success Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the success of team building activities. It's used to assess how participants rate the activity, their level of participation, and whether or not they would recommend it to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Familiarize yourself with the questions and rating scales provided.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields accordingly:
	* Rate the activity using the "Rate the activity" question.
	* Indicate how likely you are to participate in this type of activity again using the "Participation" question.
	* Choose whether or not you think the activity was valuable using the "How valuable do you think this activity was?" field.
	* Select whether or not you would recommend the activity to employees.
	* Provide any comments you have about the activity.
	* Enter your email address.
	* Enter any phone number you want to include.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields, submit the form to complete the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate the activity**: Rate how successful you think the team building activity was using the scale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Participation**: Indicate how likely you are to participate in this type of activity again:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **How valuable do you think this activity was?**: Rate the value of the activity on a number scale.
* **Recommend to employees?**: Check whether or not you would recommend this activity to employees:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Comments**: Provide any comments you have about the activity.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to reach out to the organiser.
