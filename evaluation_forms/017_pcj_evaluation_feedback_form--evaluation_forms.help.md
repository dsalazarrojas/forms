# PCJ Evaluation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PCJ Evaluation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply from the evaluation questions on page 2.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the product or service on page 3.
3. Provide any comments or feedback in the text fields on pages 4 and 5.
4. Enter your email address on page 6 to help us get in touch with you.
5. Optionally, enter your phone number and any additional comments on pages 7 and 8.
6. Select the date and time of your evaluation on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **evaluation_questions** (Select Multiple, page 2): This field allows you to choose one or more options from the list provided to describe your experience.
* **satisfaction_rating** (Number, page 3): Rate your satisfaction with the product or service on a scale of 1-5.
* **comments** (Text, page 4): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **comments_2** (Text, page 5): Provide any further comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **email** (Email, page 6): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **phone** (Text, page 7): Optionally, enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **date** (Date, page 8): Select the date of your evaluation.
* **time** (Time, page 8): Select the time of your evaluation.
* **date_time** (Date, page 9): Select the date and time of your evaluation.

## Tips

* Please provide your feedback in a constructive and honest manner.
* Only submit your feedback once.
* Ensure your email address is accurate so we can get in touch with you.
* If you have any further comments or questions, please provide them in the text fields.
