# online-interview-questionnaire-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online-interview-questionnaire-form is designed to collect demographic and contact information, as well as feedback and preferences from users. It is used to gather data that helps us better understand the audience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Provide your date and time of availability for an interview (if applicable).
3. Select one or multiple options from the provided list (if applicable).
4. Enter any additional note or comment that you would like to provide.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information, such as your phone number.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Date and Time** (Date and Time, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date and time of your availability for an interview.
* **Address** (Address, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address if you would like to share it.
* **Note** (Note, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes that you would like to share.

### Select Options
* **Select One** (Select One, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option from the provided list:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the provided list:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in your contact information to ensure that we can reach you efficiently.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Note" field to share them.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure that you have provided all the necessary information.
