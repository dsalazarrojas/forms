# Online Seminar Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Seminar Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees after an online seminar. The purpose of this form is to help seminar organizers understand how well the event was received and what they can do to improve it for future attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate the seminar overall by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
2. Rate the presentation by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
3. Enter any comments or feedback you may have about the materials provided.
4. Rate the support provided by the instructor by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
5. Enter a brief description of the overall value you found in the seminar.
6. Provide any comments or feedback you have about the seminar.
7. Select all the options that you would recommend this seminar to others.
8. Enter your email address to be contacted for future seminar opportunities.
9. Enter your phone number to be contacted for future seminar opportunities.
10. Enter your name so we can address any feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you rate the seminar overall** (seminar_rating): Rate the seminar overall by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
* **How would you rate the presentation** (presentation_content): Rate the presentation by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
* **Were the materials provided sufficient** (materials_used): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the materials provided.
* **How would you rate the support provided by the instructor** (instructor_support): Rate the support provided by the instructor by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
* **What was the overall value of the seminar** (overall_value): Enter a brief description of the overall value you found in the seminar.
* **Do you have any comments or feedback** (feedback_comments): Provide any comments or feedback you have about the seminar.
* **Would you recommend this seminar** (recommended_seminars): Select all the options that you would recommend this seminar to others.
* **Your Email Address** (email): Enter your email address to be contacted for future seminar opportunities.
* **Your Phone Number** (phone): Enter your phone number to be contacted for future seminar opportunities.
* **Your Name** (name): Enter your name so we can address any feedback you may have.
