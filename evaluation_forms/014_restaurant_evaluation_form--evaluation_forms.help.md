# Restaurant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Evaluation Form is used to collect user feedback on their dining experience at our restaurants. It will help us understand how you feel about the food, service, and overall quality of our restaurants. This form is completely anonymous and is meant to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on each page and fill out the fields provided. Each page is designed to be easy to complete, and you can submit your evaluation at any time. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food Quality** (`number`, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how you would rate the quality of the food you ordered.
- **Overall Service Quality** (`number`, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how you would rate the overall service quality, including the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff.
- **Cleanliness** (`number`, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how clean you found the restaurant to be.
- **Order Accuracy** (`number`, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how accurate the order was.
- **Speed of Service** (`number`, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how quickly the order was served.
- **Staff Friendliness** (`text`, optional): Describe the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff.
- **Food Price** (`number`, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how reasonable you found the food prices to be.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your feedback. Your input will help us improve our services.
- You can submit your evaluation at any time, and it will be kept completely anonymous.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
