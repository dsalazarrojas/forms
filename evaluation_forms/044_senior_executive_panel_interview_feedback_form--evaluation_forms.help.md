# Senior Executive Panel Interview Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Executive Panel Interview Feedback Form is designed to collect comprehensive feedback from reviewers who have participated in an interview with a candidate. The form will help to gather detailed information about the candidate's performance, strengths, and weaknesses, and ensure that the review process is fair and transparent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the candidate's last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter the date of the interview in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the time of the interview in the "Time" field.
5. Enter the position title of the candidate in the "Position Title" field.
6. Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate the candidate's performance in the "Feedback Score" field.
7. Provide comments about the candidate's performance in the "Feedback Comments" field.
8. Enter the reviewer's name in the "Review Name" field.
9. Enter the reviewer's title in the "Review Title" field.
10. Enter the reviewer's email in the "Review Email" field.
11. Enter the reviewer's phone number in the "Review Phone" field.
12. Select the number of members in the review panel that the reviewer was part of in the "Panel Size" field.
13. Select the agreement score between 1 and 5 in the "Reviewer Agreement Score" field.
14. Enter comments from the reviewer about the candidate's performance in the "Reviewer Comments" field.
15. Select the time of day when the interview took place in the "Time Range" field.
16. Enter any additional notes from the reviewer in the "Reviewer Notes" field.
17. Enter a score between 0 and 5 to rate the candidate's performance in the "Candidate Score" field.
18. Select the reviewer's score between 0 and 5 in the "Reviewer Score" field.
19. Select the range of the reviewer's agreement score in the "Reviewer Score Range" field.
20. Enter comments from the candidate about their agreement with the reviewer in the "Candidate Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter the candidate's first name.
* **Last name**: Enter the candidate's last name.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the interview.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the interview.
* **Position Title**: Enter the position title of the candidate.
* **Feedback Score**: Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate the candidate's performance.
* **Feedback Comments**: Provide comments about the candidate's performance.
* **Review Name**: Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Review Title**: Enter the reviewer's title.
* **Review Email**: Enter the reviewer's email.
* **Review Phone**: Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Panel Size**: Select the number of members in the review panel that the reviewer was part of.
* **Reviewer Agreement Score**: Select a score between 1 and 5 to rate the candidate's performance based on your agreement.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Provide comments from the reviewer about the candidate's performance.
* **Reviewer Agreement Score Range**: Select the range of the reviewer's agreement score.
* **Time Range**: Select the time of day when the interview took place.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Candidate Score**: Enter a score between 0 and 5 to rate the candidate's performance.
* **Reviewer Score**: Select a score between 0 and 5 to rate the candidate's performance based on your opinion.
* **Reviewer Score Range**: Select the range of the reviewer's score.
* **Candidate Agreement**: Enter comments from the candidate about their agreement with the reviewer.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when providing feedback.
* Provide specific examples to support your ratings and comments.
* Do not reuse feedback on candidates you have not previously evaluated.
* Use this form for new feedback submissions only.
