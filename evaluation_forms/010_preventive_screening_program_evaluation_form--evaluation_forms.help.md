# Preventive Screening Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the Preventive Screening Program. It gathers information on participant satisfaction, provider satisfaction, screening location, and other key factors to help us better understand the program's impact and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and provide accurate answers based on your experience with the Preventive Screening Program.
2. Complete the form in its entirety, or select the "Skip" option for any fields that are not applicable.
3. Please provide clear and concise answers in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screening Program** (`screening_program`, select_one, required: false):
  This question asks whether the participant was part of the Preventive Screening Program during the screening. Please select "Yes" if they were and "No" otherwise.
* **Screening Location** (`screening_location`, select_one, required: false):
  Please select the location where the screening took place (e.g., Clinic, Hospital, Wellness Program).
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, date, required: false):
  Enter the date when the screening was conducted.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required: false):
  Enter the name of the healthcare provider who performed the screening.
* **Screening Type** (`screening_type`, select_one, required: false):
  Please select the type of screening that was conducted (e.g., Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Diabetes).
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, number, required: false):
  Rate your satisfaction with the screening experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Participant Satisfaction Comments** (`participant_satisfaction_comments`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional comments about your satisfaction with the screening.
* **Provider Satisfaction** (`provider_satisfaction`, number, required: false):
  Rate your satisfaction with the screening experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Provider Satisfaction Comments** (`provider_satisfaction_comments`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional comments about your satisfaction with the screening.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, number, required: false):
  Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-10.
* **Program Satisfaction Comments** (`program_satisfaction_comments`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional comments about your satisfaction with the program.
* **Participant Contact Information** (`participant_contact_info`, email, required: false):
  Enter your email address or other contact information.
* **Provider Contact Information** (`provider_contact_info`, text, required: false):
  Enter the contact information for the healthcare provider.
* **Screening Outcome** (`screening_outcome`, select_one, required: false):
  Please indicate whether the screening outcome was positive (Yes) or negative (No).
* **Follow-up Scheduling** (`follow_up_scheduling`, select_one, required: false):
  Indicate whether follow-up scheduling was conducted (Yes or No).
* **Next Screening** (`next_screening`, date, required: false):
  Enter the date for the next scheduled screening.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, required: false):
  Provide any comments about the follow-up session.
* **Participant Contact Information** (`participant_contact_info`, email, required: false):
  Enter your email address or other contact information.
* **Provider Contact Information** (`provider_contact_info`, text, required: false):
  Enter the contact information for the healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please refer to the "Provider Contact Information" field to get in touch with the provider.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us improve the Preventive Screening Program!
