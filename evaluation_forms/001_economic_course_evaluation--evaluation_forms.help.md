# Economic Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate an economic course and gather feedback from students. It is designed to be completed by students who have taken the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk \* (e.g. \*).
* Fill in the optional fields if you wish to provide additional feedback or comments.
* Make sure to fill in all the fields that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please enter any additional feedback or comments you have about the course.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Please enter a number from 0 to 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments you have about the course.
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) to rate the course.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the methods that apply to how you took the course (Online, Offline, or Both).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you took the course.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time you took the course.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
