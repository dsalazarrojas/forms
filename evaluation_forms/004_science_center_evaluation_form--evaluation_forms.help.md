# Science Center Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from visitors to the Science Center. It aims to measure the quality of exhibits, programs, and overall experience provided by the Science Center. This form is not required but will help us improve the Science Center's offerings and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and answer all questions that apply to your experience.
2. Fill out the form sections as directed by the labels and prompts.
3. Submit your feedback and evaluation when you've completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (general_info, note, Optional)**: Please provide any additional information about your visit, such as your name, contact details, or any other relevant context.
* **Rating (rating, number, Optional)**: Use the provided scale to rate your overall experience with the Science Center. Note that a higher number indicates a more positive experience.
* **Rating Scale (rating_scale, select_one, Required)**: Select one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to indicate your rating of the Science Center's quality.
* **Exhibit Experience (exhibit_experience, note, Optional)**: Share your thoughts about the exhibits you visited during your stay. Be specific about what you liked or disliked about the exhibits.
* **Overall Experience (overall_experience, note, Optional)**: Describe your overall impression of the Science Center. What did you enjoy about your visit? Was there anything that left a positive or negative impression?
* **Program Information (program_info, note, Optional)**: If you participated in any programs or events during your visit, share your thoughts about them. What did you like or dislike about the programs?
 
**Tips**

* Be honest and specific when providing feedback and evaluation.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improvement, please include them in the relevant fields.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form! Your input helps us improve the Science Center's services.
