# Training Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the effectiveness of a training program. Please take a few minutes to review the form and provide honest feedback on your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form for each training session you've attended. 
- Be accurate and honest in your responses.
- You can save your progress and come back to it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (optional):
    Participant Information is where you provide a short bio, usually your name and title, so that we can easily identify you in our records.
* **Training Information** (optional):
    Provide a brief summary of the training session you've attended, including its title and the date.
* **Assessment Questions** (optional):
    Answer the questions in the space provided, giving us an idea of your understanding of the material covered in the training.
* **Comments** (optional):
    Add any comments or suggestions you think can improve the training program.
* **Rating** (optional):
    Rate your overall experience with the training program, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Recommendations** (optional):
    Choose whether you recommend or not recommend this training program to others. 
    - **Yes**: You highly recommend this training program.
    - **No**: You do not recommend this training program.
* **Date** (optional):
    Enter the date when you attended the training session.
* **Time** (optional):
    Enter the start and end time of the training session.

Note: This help guide is meant to be used in conjunction with the provided form. The field descriptions above should be used in conjunction with the actual form fields for a complete understanding.
