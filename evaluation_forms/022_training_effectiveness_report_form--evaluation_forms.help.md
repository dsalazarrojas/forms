## Purpose

This form is used to collect data on the effectiveness of training programs. It will help in assessing the quality and impact of the training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Form Title to give a short description of the training.
2. Enter the name of the Organization.
3. Enter the Program Name.
4. Enter the Date of training.
5. Enter the Trainer's name.
6. Enter the Training Outcomes.
7. Enter your Feedback on the training.
8. Select the Evaluation Method used.
9. Rate the Evaluation Outcomes using the provided options.
10. Add any recommendations for improvement.
11. Add any suggestions for future trainings.
12. Enter the Contact Name.
13. Enter the Contact E-mail.
14. Enter the Contact Phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the training.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the organization that provided the training.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the training program.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Required/Optional): Enter the date on which the training took place.
* **Trainer** (`trainer_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the trainer.
* **Training Outcomes** (`training_outcomes`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the outcomes or results of the training.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Required/Optional): Enter any feedback you have for the training.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, select_one, Required): Select the method used for evaluation (e.g., Evaluation Form, Online Form, Phone Call, In Person).
* **Evaluation Outcomes** (`evaluation_outcomes`, select_multiple, Required): Rate the outcomes of the training using the provided options (e.g., Very Good, Good, Fair, Not Very Good).
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text, Required/Optional): Enter any recommendations for improvement.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, Required/Optional): Enter any suggestions for future trainings.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, Required/Optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

Please fill out the form accurately and provide as much detail as possible. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the relevant organization or contact person.
