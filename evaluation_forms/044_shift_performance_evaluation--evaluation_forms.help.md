# Shift Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of employees on a shift. It helps supervisors and team leaders assess an employee's productivity, efficiency, and quality of work during a specific shift. The form captures important information such as employee ID, shift date, supervisor ID, performance rating, feedback, action items, notes, and email address.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are logged in to access the form.
2. Choose the employee you want to evaluate by entering their Employee ID.
3. Select the shift date for which you want to evaluate the employee's performance.
4. Rate the employee's performance by selecting one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
5. Provide feedback on the employee's performance, if any.
6. Check the status of action items assigned to the employee by selecting Complete, In Progress, or Delayed.
7. Add any notes you'd like to keep record of.
8. Enter the employee's email address for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Required): Enter the unique ID assigned to the employee you want to evaluate.
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, Date, Not Required): Select the date for which you want to evaluate the employee's performance.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, Number, Not Required): Enter the ID of the supervisor who will be evaluating the performance.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, Select One, Not Required): Rate the employee's performance as Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Not Required): Provide any feedback you'd like to offer to the employee.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Check the status of any action items assigned to the employee.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Not Required): Add any notes you'd like to keep record of.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Not Required): Enter the employee's email address for further communication.

## Tips

* Make sure to be specific and objective when providing feedback and evaluating performance ratings.
* Use action items to keep track of tasks assigned to the employee and their progress.
* Notes can be used to document any important events or observations during the shift.
* Use the email address to keep in touch with the employee for any follow-up discussions or updates.
