# Qualitative Fit Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Qualitative Fit Test Form is designed to collect data on the fit test results of patients for respirators, allowing for assessment and improvement of the fit and usability of the respirator.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of respirator being tested (Resoirator Type).
2. Choose the patient's gender (Patient Gender).
3. Select the patient's size for the test (Patient Size).
4. Choose the fit test result for the patient (Fit Test Result).
5. Choose the size of the respirator being tested (Resoirator Size).
6. Select the patient's height (Patient Height).
7. Choose the patient's weight range (Patient Weight).
8. Provide the name of the medical professional conducting the test (Medical Professional).
9. Enter the patient's name (Patient Name).
10. Enter the patient's birthday (Patient Birthday).
11. Enter the patient's phone number (Patient Phone).
12. Enter the patient's email address (Patient Email).
13. Enter any additional comments about the test (Additional Comments).
14. Enter the model of the respirator being tested (Resoirator Model).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resoirator Type** (`respirator_type`, select_one): Select the type of respirator being tested.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one): Choose the patient's gender.
* **Patient Size** (`patient_size`, select_multiple): Select the patient's size for the test.
* **Fit Test Result** (`fit_test_result`, select_one): Choose the fit test result for the patient.
* **Resoirator Size** (`respirator_size`, select_multiple): Select the size of the respirator being tested.
* **Patient Height** (`patient_height`, select_multiple): Select the patient's height.
* **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`, select_multiple): Choose the patient's weight range.
* **Medical Professional** (`medical_professional`, text): Enter the name of the medical professional conducting the test.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Birthday** (`patient_birthday`, date): Enter the patient's birthday.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note): Enter any additional comments about the test.
* **Resoirator Model** (`respirator_model`, text): Enter the model of the respirator being tested.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or medical professional.
* This form is designed to be completed once per patient, so please do not duplicate effort by completing this form multiple times.
