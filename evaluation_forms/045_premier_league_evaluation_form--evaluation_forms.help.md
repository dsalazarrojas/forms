# Premier League Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Premier League Evaluation Form is used to assess a player's performance in a specific game or series of games. It helps coaches, scouts, and analysts evaluate the player's skills, strategy, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:
- Player Name: Enter the player's name as it appears on their official team roster.
- Game Position: Choose the player's position in the game from the options below:
  • Goalkeeper
  • Defender
  • Midfielder
  • Forward
- Coach Name: Enter the coach's name who led the team during the game or series of games.
- Player Rating: Rate the player's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
- Evaluation Date: Enter the date of the game or series of games being evaluated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (player_name, text, optional): Enter the player's name as it appears on their official team roster.
* **Game Position** (game_position, select_one, required): Choose the player's position in the game from the options below:
  • Goalkeeper
  • Defender
  • Midfielder
  • Forward
* **Coach Name** (coach_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the coach who led the team during the game or series of games.
* **Player Rating** (player_rating, number, required): Rate the player's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, required): Enter the date of the game or series of games being evaluated.

## Tips
Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and according to your observations. If you've already completed an evaluation for the same player, please make sure to update your previous evaluation to track changes in a player's performance over time.
