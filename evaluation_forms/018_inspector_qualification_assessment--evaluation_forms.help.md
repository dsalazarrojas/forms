# inspector_qualification_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inspector_qualification_assessment form is used to evaluate an inspector's qualifications for a specific role. The evaluation process helps to determine the inspector's level of expertise and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions accurately.
2. Provide your performance cycles, training plans, and any additional information required in the relevant fields.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### inspector_qualification (required)
\* This field is for you to provide a brief description of your qualifications and experience as an inspector.

### performance_cycles (required)
\* Select the performance cycles that you have completed or are currently undergoing.

### training_plans (required)
\* Choose the training plans that you have completed or are currently undergoing.

### notes (optional)
\* This field is for any additional comments or information that you would like to provide.

### email (required)
\* Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### phone (optional)
\* If you have a phone number, enter it for contact purposes.

### hired_date (optional)
\* If applicable, enter the date you were hired for this role.

### evaluation_start_date (optional)
\* If applicable, enter the start date of the evaluation period.

### evaluation_end_date (optional)
\* If applicable, enter the end date of the evaluation period.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your entries for any errors or inconsistencies.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
