# Sales Executive Interview Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sales Executive Interview Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from Sales Executives after an interview. This feedback will help the interviewer to assess the candidate's performance and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form has several sections, each representing a different page. You will be asked to navigate through each section to provide feedback.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. You may use the "Submission Review" field to indicate if the submission was reviewed or not.
4. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to provide actionable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any relevant information about the candidate that you feel is necessary for your feedback.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation scale for the candidate's performance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the candidate's performance.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, note, required): This field is used to provide actionable recommendations for the candidate's improvement:
	+ Please provide specific and constructive feedback for the candidate's growth and development.
	+ Ensure that your recommendations are clear and actionable for the candidate.
* **Submission Review** (`submission_review`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the submission has been reviewed:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Applicable
