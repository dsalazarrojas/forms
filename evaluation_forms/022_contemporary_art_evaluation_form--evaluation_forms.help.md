# Contemporary Art Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contemporary Art Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the artistic value, technical skill, and overall quality of a piece of contemporary art. It helps the evaluator to provide a comprehensive and accurate evaluation of the artwork, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the Artwork Details section (Section 1) for each artwork being evaluated.
2. Enter the Artwork Title, Artist Name, and Artwork Medium in the respective fields.
3. Select the Artwork Dimensions if applicable.
4. Evaluate the Artwork's Artistic Merit and Technical Skill in the respective sections (Section 3-5).
5. Provide your recommendation (Section 6) and confirm that the information provided is accurate (Section 8).
6. Confirm that the evaluation is completed (Section 7).
7. Enter any additional comments or details if necessary (Section 9).
8. Sign off on the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate (Section 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artwork Title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the artwork being evaluated.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
* **Artwork Medium** (`artwork_medium`, select multiple, required): Select all media that the artwork utilizes (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, Photography, etc.).
* **Artwork Dimensions** (`artwork_dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions of the artwork (if applicable).
* **Artistic Merit Rating** (`artistic_merit_rating`, select one, required): Evaluate the artistic merit of the artwork on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Technical Skill Rating** (`technical_skill_rating`, select one, required): Evaluate the technical skill of the artwork on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Originality and Conceptual Approach** (`original_conceptual_approach`, select one, required): Evaluate the originality and conceptual approach of the artwork on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Identification Number** (`evaluator_id`, text, required): Enter your identification number as the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Recommendation** (`evaluator_recommendation`, select one, required): Provide your recommendation for the artwork (e.g., Highly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, Re-evaluate).
* **Evaluation Completed?** (`evaluation_completed`, select one, required): Confirm that the evaluation is completed.
* **Any additional details or comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional details or comments about the artwork.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, select one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Report Generation** (`report_generated_date`, date, required): Enter the date the report is generated.

Note: The evaluation form is designed to be completed by the evaluator, and all fields are required for accurate and consistent evaluation.
