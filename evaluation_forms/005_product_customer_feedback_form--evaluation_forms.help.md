# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on products used by our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the product, including what you like and dislike about it.
3. Rate the product and customer service.
4. Submit your feedback form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback Survey** (`customer_feedback_survey`, text, not required): Answer any questions or comments you have about your experience with the product.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **What do you like about this product?** (`feedback_message`, text, not required): Share what you like about the product.
* **What do you not like about this product?** (`what_dont_like`, text, not required): Share what you don't like about the product.
* **Product Service** (`product_service`, select_one, not required): Rate the quality of our product service.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, not required): Rate the quality of our customer service.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Feedback Form Submitted** (`feedback_form_submitted`, date, not required): This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.
* **Feedback Form Submitted Time** (`feedback_form_submitted_time`, time, not required): This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.
* **Customer Service Rating** (`customer_service_rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer service.
* **Customer Service Improvement** (`customer_service_improvement`, text, not required): Share any suggestions for how we can improve our customer service.
* **Customer Service Improvement List** (`customer_service_improvement_list`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the ways in which we can improve our customer service.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, not required): Enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company** (`contact_company`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`contact_jobtitle`, text, not required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`contact_department`, text, not required): Enter your department.
* **Comments** (`contact_comments`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the product you're providing feedback on.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, text, not required): Enter the version of the product you're using.
* **Purchase Date** (`product_purchase_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Time** (`product_purchase_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you purchased the product.
* **Submit Feedback** (`submit_feedback`, select_one, not required): Submit your feedback to us.
