# Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Evaluation Form is a tool to gather feedback and evaluations from workshop attendees. It is used by our organization to improve the quality and relevance of future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the workshop. Please review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Evaluation Form 1** (text field, optional):
	+ This field is for general feedback about the workshop.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 2** (number field, optional):
	+ This field is for rating your overall satisfaction with the workshop.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 3** (time field, optional):
	+ This field is for indicating the time you spent on each activity or exercise during the workshop.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 4** (date field, optional):
	+ This field is for marking down the date of the next time you plan to use the skills or knowledge acquired during the workshop.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 5** (select_one field, optional):
	+ This field asks if you found the workshop to be helpful in your work or personal life.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 6** (select_multiple field, optional):
	+ This field asks if there are any topics or areas that you did not cover or that you would like to see covered in future workshops.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 7** (email field, optional):
	+ This field is for providing an email address where we can reach you for follow-up or to share more resources related to the workshop.
* **Workshop Evaluation Form 8** (text field, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.

## Tips
Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities. If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to contact us. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our workshops.
