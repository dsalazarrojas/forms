# SBAR Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SBAR Form is a standardized tool for patient evaluations, providing a clear and structured format for collecting patient information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the patient's medical history and current status.
2. Complete each field as it applies to the patient's situation.
3. Answer each question based on the patient's information.
4. Use the form as a guide, but feel free to add any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's basic information, including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **name** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **age** (number, required/optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **medical_record_number** (text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical record numbers or identifiers.
* **bar_section** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the patient is currently on the waiting list or if there are any barriers to treatment.
* **section_b** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **section_c** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **section_d** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the patient has any known medical conditions.
* **date_of_admission** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's admission.
* **time_of_admission** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the patient's admission.
* **section_e** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **section_f** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **section_g** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **section_h** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **note** (text, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **date_of_discharge** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's discharge.
* **time_of_discharge** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the patient's discharge.
* **evaluation** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the patient's evaluation is complete.
* **email_address** (email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **medical_contact** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the patient has a primary medical contact.
* **medical_specialty** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the patient has any medical specialty requirements.
* **medical_team** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers from the options provided.
* **next_steps** (text, required/optional): Enter any next steps or instructions for the patient's care.
* **comments** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about the patient's condition.

Note: The fields marked as "required/optional" may or may not be required depending on the specific situation and context in which the form is being used.
