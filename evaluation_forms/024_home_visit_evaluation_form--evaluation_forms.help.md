# home_visit_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback, evaluation, and ratings from parents and teachers during home visits made by teachers to students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the parent whose home was visited.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Select your evaluation and rating (as a parent/teacher).
4. Enter any feedback you wish to provide.
5. Enter the date and time of the home visit.
6. Enter the location of the visit.
7. Provide any comments you wish to leave.
8. Select the category and school of the student.
9. Select the teacher who made the visit.
10. Select the subject of the visit.
11. Enter your ratings (as a parent/teacher).
12. If applicable, enter any comments or suggestions from the parent/teacher.
13. Finally, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent who was visited.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student who was visited.
* **Teacher Evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, select_one, required): Select how you evaluated the visit from the teacher's point of view.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Enter your feedback for the visit.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the home visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the home visit.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the visit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the parent or teacher.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Enter your rating for the visit as a parent or teacher.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes from the visit.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Enter the category of the visit.
* **School** (`school`, text, required): Enter the school name.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required): Enter the grade level of the student.
* **Teacher** (`teacher`, select_one, required): Select the teacher who made the visit.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Enter the subject of the visit.
* **Parent Rating** (`parent_rating`, number, optional): Enter your rating as a parent.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, number, optional): Enter your rating as a teacher.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the parent.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the teacher.
* **Parent Suggestions** (`parent_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions from the parent.
* **Teacher Suggestions** (`teacher_suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions from the teacher.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click submit to send the form.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access this form.
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data is reliable.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities.
