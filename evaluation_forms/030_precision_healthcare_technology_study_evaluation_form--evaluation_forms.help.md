# precision_healthcare_technology_study_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness and usability of precision healthcare technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and fill out the form accordingly.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Use the dropdown menus to select the options that best answer each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study ID** (`study_id`, Number, Optional): This field is intended to collect the unique identifier for the study being evaluated. Please enter a numerical value that corresponds to the study.
* **Healthcare Technology Name** (`healthcare_technology_name`, Text, Optional): This field is meant to collect the name of the healthcare technology being evaluated. Enter the name of the technology as it appears on its packaging or documentation.
* **Evaluation Questions** (`evaluation_questions`, Text, Optional): This field is for any comments or observations related to the evaluation. Describe your thoughts and experiences with the technology.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, Select One, Optional): This field asks whether the technology has been effective in its intended use case. Select either "Yes" or "No".
* **Usability** (`usability`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field asks about the usability of the technology. Select all that apply from the options.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, Select One, Optional): This field asks whether you would recommend the technology to others. Select either "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or observations about the technology.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field allows you to rate the technology's overall performance. Select one or more from the options.
	+ Good
	+ Fair

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Keep your answers clear and concise.
- If you have any additional comments or observations, use the "Comments" field.
- Use the "Evaluation Questions" field to provide detailed descriptions of your experiences.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
