# ohc_staff_effectiveness_appraisal_and_goal_setting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of OHCS staff members based on their job responsibilities, effectiveness rating, alignment with set goals, and other performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3.  If a field is marked as optional, please provide any additional information if you have it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job responsibilities** (`1`): Describe the staff member's job responsibilities.
* **Effectiveness rating** (`2`): Rate the staff member's effectiveness in their job on a scale of 1-100.
* **Goals alignment** (`3`): Indicate if the staff member's goals are aligned with the team's or organization's objectives. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Current goals** (`4`): List the staff member's current goals.
* **Job satisfaction** (`5`): Rate the staff member's job satisfaction on a scale of 1-100. (Optional)
* **Performance comments** (`6`): Leave comments about the staff member's performance.
* **Evaluation date** (`7`): Fill out the date the evaluation was made.
* **Evaluation time** (`8`): Fill out the time the evaluation was made.
* **Evaluation location** (`9`): Describe the location where the evaluation was made.
* **Evaluation form submitted** (`10`): Indicate if the form was submitted by the staff member or the manager. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Evaluated by** (`11`): Indicate if the evaluation was done by the manager or someone else. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Evaluation status** (`12`): Indicate if the evaluation is active or inactive. Choose 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Evaluation comments** (`13`): Leave comments about the evaluation.
* **Evaluated by employee** (`14`): Leave comments from the staff member about their evaluation.
* **Evaluation form version** (`15`): Leave the version of the evaluation form used. (Optional)
