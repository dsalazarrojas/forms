# Apprentice Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Apprentice Evaluation Checklist" form is designed to help evaluators assess an apprentice's performance in a particular trade or skill area. It is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the apprentice's strengths and weaknesses, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the apprentice's name and ID in the respective fields.
2. Select the trade or skill area that the apprentice is being evaluated in.
3. Evaluate the apprentice's technical skills and competencies using the select_one options.
4. Evaluate the apprentice's safety compliance and practices using the select_one options.
5. Evaluate the apprentice's quality of work, attendance, and punctuality using the select_one options.
6. Evaluate the apprentice's teamwork and collaboration using the select_one options.
7. Describe the apprentice's key strengths and areas for improvement in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Apprentice Name** (`apprentice_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the apprentice being evaluated.
* **Apprentice ID** (`apprentice_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the apprentice being evaluated.
* **Trade or Skill Area** (`trade`, text, required): Select the trade or skill area that the apprentice is being evaluated in.
* **Evaluator Information** (`evaluator_info`, note, required): Enter any additional information about the evaluator, such as their name and title.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person evaluating the apprentice.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Technical Skills and Competency** (`technical_skills`, select_one, required): Evaluate the apprentice's technical skills and competencies using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement.
* **Safety Compliance and Practices** (`safety_compliance`, select_one, required): Evaluate the apprentice's safety compliance and practices using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement.
* **Quality of Work** (`work_quality`, select_one, required): Evaluate the apprentice's quality of work using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement.
* **Attendance and Punctuality** (`attendance`, select_one, required): Evaluate the apprentice's attendance and punctuality using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration** (`teamwork`, select_one, required): Evaluate the apprentice's teamwork and collaboration using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement.
* **Key Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Describe the apprentice's key strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Describe areas where the apprentice needs improvement.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required): Provide any recommendations for the apprentice's further development.

## Tips

* Please complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure that the apprentice receives a fair evaluation.
* Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your evaluations and recommendations.
* This form is designed to be completed by the person evaluating the apprentice, but may be reviewed and discussed with other evaluators or mentors for additional feedback and guidance.
