# Humanization Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect feedback from humanization specialists, helping us understand their experiences and interactions with clients. Your input will aid us in refining our services and services' quality.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely.
- Enter your name and contact information on the "Contact info" page.
- Evaluate the quality of the specialist's work on the "Specialist info" page, using the options provided (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
- In the "Submission" page, provide any additional information or comments about your experience.
- Click "Submit" to finalize your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact info** (Text, Optional): Please provide your name and contact information so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
- **Specialist info** (Select One, Optional): Rate the quality of the specialist's work using the scale: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Submission** (Text, Optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the specialist.
- **Submit** (Text, Optional): Confirm that you have filled out the form completely and accurately.
