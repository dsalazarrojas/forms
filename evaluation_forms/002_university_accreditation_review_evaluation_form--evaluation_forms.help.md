# University Accreditation Review Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather data for the evaluation of a university's accreditation. It collects information from various categories to help assess the institution's quality and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Provide detailed feedback in the "Feedback" section.
3. Include any additional comments or remarks in the "Comments" section.
4. Enter your institutional contact information in the "Institutional Contact" section.
5. Provide your name and email address in the "Reviewer Name" and "Institution E-mail" sections, respectively.
6. Enter the institution's website URL in the "Institution URL" section.
7. Select the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" section.
8. Enter your signature as the reviewer in the "Reviewer Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This field is for entering any general information about the institution.
* **Institutional Data** (`institutional_data`, text, optional): This field is for entering any data related to the institution.
* **Academic Program Data** (`academic_program_data`, text, optional): This field is for entering any data related to the academic programs offered by the institution.
* **Faculty Data** (`faculty_data`, text, optional): This field is for entering any data related to the faculty members of the institution.
* **Assessment Criteria** (`assessment_criteria`, text, optional): This field is for entering any criteria used in the assessment process.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is for providing detailed feedback on the institution.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional comments or remarks.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional comments or remarks.
* **Institutional Contact** (`institutional_contact`, text, optional): This field is for entering contact information for the institution.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering your name as the reviewer.
* **Institution E-mail** (`institution_email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your institutional email address.
* **Institution URL** (`institution_url`, text, optional): This field is for entering the institution's website URL.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date of the assessment.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): This field is for entering your signature as the reviewer.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If any of the fields are not applicable, please indicate this by entering "N/A" or a similar notation.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
