# Hiring Manager Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from hiring managers on a candidate's performance during the interview or evaluation process. The form provides a quick and easy way for hiring managers to share their impressions and insights about each candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the candidate's interview or evaluation details.
2. Answer the fields below, providing as much information as you can about the candidate's strengths and weaknesses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, note, required: false): 
	Please provide any relevant details about the interview or evaluation.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required: false): 
	Enter the candidate's name.
* **Candidate Job Title** (`candidate_job_title`, text, required: false): 
	Enter the candidate's job title or position.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): 
	Enter the job title or position for which the candidate was being evaluated.
* **Candidate Salary** (`candidate_salary`, number, required: false): 
	Enter the candidate's salary (if known).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): 
	Provide any comments or feedback about the candidate's performance.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): 
	Rate the candidate's performance out of a scale of 1-5.
* **Candidate Score** (`candidate_score`, number, required: false): 
	Rate the candidate's score out of a scale of 1-5.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): 
	Enter the date of the evaluation or interview.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required: false): 
	Enter the time of the evaluation or interview.
* **Evaluation Duration** (`evaluation_duration`, number, required: false): 
	Enter the duration of the evaluation or interview.
* **Evaluation Location** (`evaluation_location`, text, required: false): 
	Enter the location of the evaluation or interview.

Note: Fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be filled out as needed.
