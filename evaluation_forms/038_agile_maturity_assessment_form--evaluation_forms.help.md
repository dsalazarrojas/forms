# Agile Maturity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agile Maturity Assessment Form is designed to help teams and organizations evaluate their current state of Agile maturity and identify areas for improvement. The form is based on the Agile Maturity Model and is a valuable tool for teams and organizations looking to improve their Agile maturity and achieve better results.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment in the "Assessment Conducted By" field.
2. Enter the name of the organization being assessed in the "Organization Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
4. Define the scope of the assessment in the "Assessment Scope" field.
5. Enter the name of the team or department being assessed in the "Team/Department Assessed" field.
6. Rate the level of collaborative behavior in the "Collaborative Behavior Score" field.
7. Rate the organization's adaptability to change in the "Adaptability to Change Score" field.
8. Rate the continuous improvement mindset in the "Continuous Improvement Mindset Score" field.
9. Rate the effectiveness of iteration planning in the "Iteration Planning Effectiveness Score" field.
10. Rate how well feedback is integrated in the "Feedback Integration Score" field.
11. Rate the maturity of continuous delivery practices in the "Continuous Delivery Practices Score" field.
12. Rate the level of cross-functional skills in the "Cross-functional Skills Score" field.
13. Rate the maturity of self-organizing teams in the "Self-organizing Teams Score" field.
14. Rate the coaching and mentoring practices in the "Coaching and Mentoring Score" field.
15. Rate the maturity of automation practices in the "Automation Maturity Score" field.
16. Rate the visibility of information and progress in the "Information Visibility Score" field.
17. Rate the frequency of value delivery in the "Value Delivery Frequency Score" field.
18. Rate customer satisfaction levels in the "Customer Satisfaction Score" field.
19. Select the overall maturity level of the organization in the "Overall Maturity Assessment" field.
20. Provide any recommendations for improvements in the "Recommendations for Improvements" field.
