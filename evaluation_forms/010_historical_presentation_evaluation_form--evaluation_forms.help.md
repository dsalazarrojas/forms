# Historical Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate presentations and provide feedback to the presenter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the presentation title and date to be evaluated.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the evaluator.
3. Rate the presentation using the provided evaluation scales.
4. Leave any additional comments or feedback for the presenter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the presentation being evaluated.
* **Presenter Name** (`presenter_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the presenter.
* **Presentation Date** (`presentation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the presentation was given.
* **Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, required: false): Rate the presentation on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Leave any additional feedback or comments for the presenter.
* **Evaluation Scale 1 to 5** (`evaluation_scale_1_to_5`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select a rating from 1 to 5 to evaluate the presentation's content.
* **Evaluation Scale 6 to 10** (`evaluation_scale_6_to_10`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all applicable ratings from 6 to 10 to evaluate the presentation's organization.
* **Evaluation Scale 11 to 15** (`evaluation_scale_11_to_15`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select a rating from 11 to 15 to evaluate the presentation's overall effectiveness.
* **Evaluation Scale 16 to 20** (`evaluation_scale_16_to_20`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all applicable ratings from 16 to 20 to evaluate additional aspects of the presentation.
* **Evaluation Comment** (`evaluation_comment`, text, required: false): Leave any additional feedback or comments for the presenter.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
- Use the evaluation scales to provide numerical ratings for each aspect of the presentation.
- Make sure to include your contact information so the presenter can reach out to you with questions or for further feedback.
