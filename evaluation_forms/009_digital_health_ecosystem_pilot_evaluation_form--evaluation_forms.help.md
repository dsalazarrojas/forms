# digital_health_ecosystem_pilot_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the success and effectiveness of the digital health ecosystem pilot project. The goal is to gather feedback from participants on the project's benefits, challenges, and overall feel, as well as to provide an overall score and rating. This information will be used to refine and improve the project's development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the pilot partners involved in the project.
2. In the "Evaluation form benefits" field, describe the advantages of the project.
3. In the "Evaluation form challenges" field, describe the difficulties you faced during the project.
4. In the "Overall Feel" field, choose whether the project had a Positive or Negative overall feel.
5. In the "Evaluation form score" field, type a numerical value from 1 to 5, representing your evaluation of the project's success.
6. In the "Rating" field, choose whether the project was "Good" or "Fair".
7. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback about the project.
8. In the "Contact Information" field, provide your name and contact details for follow-up.
9. In the "Form Completion Info" field, provide any additional information about completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pilot Partners** (`partner_info`, select_multiple): Choose all the partners involved in the project.
  • Please select all the companies or organizations that took part in the pilot project.
- **Evaluation form benefits** (`benefits`, text): Describe the advantages of the project.
  • Please provide a brief description of the benefits you gained from the project.
- **Evaluation form challenges** (`challenges`, text): Describe the difficulties you faced during the project.
  • Please provide a brief description of the challenges you faced during the project.
- **Overall Feel** (`overall_feel`, select_one): Choose whether the project had a Positive or Negative overall feel.
  • Please select the overall feeling you had after completing the project.
- **Evaluation form score** (`evaluation_score`, number): Type a numerical value from 1 to 5, representing your evaluation of the project's success.
  • Please type a number between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
- **Rating** (`rating`, select_one): Choose whether the project was "Good" or "Fair".
  • Please select the overall rating of the project.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the project.
  • Please provide any additional information or suggestions for improvement.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text): Provide your name and contact details for follow-up.
  • Please provide your name and any relevant contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
- **Form Completion Info** (`form_completion_info`, text): Provide any additional information about completing the form.
  • Please provide any information about how you completed the form (e.g., difficulties, suggestions).
