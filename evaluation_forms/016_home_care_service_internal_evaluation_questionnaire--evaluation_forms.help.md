# Home Care Service Internal Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate the Home Care Service, ensuring that it meets our internal standards for quality and effectiveness. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to provide valuable feedback and insights about your experience with our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
* Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required".
* You can select multiple options for the "category" field.
* Enter your feedback in the "description" field.
* Select the date and time of your experience with the Home Care Service.
* Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
* If you have any contact information (email or phone number), please provide it so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire** (Page 1, Text, Optional): This is the title of the form. You can enter any text to describe the purpose or scope of your evaluation.
* **category** (Page 2, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the category that best describes your experience with the Home Care Service. Please select all relevant options from the list.
* **description** (Page 3, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your experience with the Home Care Service. This will help us understand your perspective and provide insights for improvement.
* **date** (Page 4, Date, Optional): If possible, enter the date of your experience with the Home Care Service.
* **time** (Page 5, Time, Optional): If possible, enter the time of your experience with the Home Care Service.
* **note** (Page 6, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share about your experience with the Home Care Service.
* **email** (Page 7, Email, Optional): If you have an email address where we can contact you, please provide it. We may follow up with you for more information or clarification.
* **phone** (Page 8, Text, Optional): If you have a phone number where we can contact you, please provide it.
