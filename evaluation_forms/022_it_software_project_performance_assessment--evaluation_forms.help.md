# IT Software Project Performance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the performance of an IT software project, providing a structured way to document and evaluate project details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project details in the corresponding fields.
2. Fill in the team leadership, project manager, and team member details.
3. Provide information about the project scope, timeline, budget, and success criteria.
4. Describe any challenges faced during the project.
5. Share lessons learned and project impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter a brief summary of the project, including its title, description, and any relevant background information.
* **Team Leadership**: Provide the names and titles of the team leaders involved in the project.
* **Project Manager**: Enter the name and title of the project manager responsible for overseeing the project.
* **Team Members**: List the names and roles of all team members involved in the project.
* **Project Scope**: Describe the project's objectives, goals, and deliverables.
* **Timeline**: Enter the project's start and end dates, including any notable milestones and deadlines.
* **Budget**: Enter the project budget, including any costs, expenses, or financial considerations.
* **Success Criteria**: Define what constitutes project success, including metrics or measures used to evaluate outcomes.
* **Challenges Faced**: Describe any obstacles or challenges encountered during the project, including solutions or mitigations implemented.
* **Lessons Learned**: Share key takeaways or insights gained from the project, including areas for improvement.
* **Project Impact**: Describe the project's overall impact, including its value, benefits, or results achieved.
* **Retrospective**: Provide a brief summary of the project's progress, including lessons learned, successes, and challenges faced.
