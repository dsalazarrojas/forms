# employee_recognition_program_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_recognition_program_evaluation_form is designed to gather feedback from employees on the effectiveness and effectiveness of the recognition program. This form helps management identify areas of improvement and suggestions for enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant evaluation form from the options provided in the "Evaluation Form" field.
2. Enter the employee title in the "Employee Title" field.
3. Choose the channels through which recognition was received (e.g. "Channel 1", "Channel 2", "Channel 3") in the "Recognition Channels" field.
4. Rate the recognition frequency in the "Recognition Frequency" field.
5. Rate the recognition amount in the "Recognition Amount" field.
6. Choose how effective the recognition was in achieving its goals (e.g. "High", "Medium", "Low") in the "Recognition Effectiveness" field.
7. Select the impact of recognition on the employee in the "Recognition Impact" field.
8. Choose how satisfied the employee is with the recognition in the "Recognition Satisfaction" field.
9. Rate how likely the employee is to participate in the recognition program in the future (e.g. "Very Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Not Likely") in the "Recognition Likelyhood" field.
10. Provide any additional suggestions or comments in the "Recognition Suggestions" field.
11. Enter any open-ended comments in the "Recognition Open-Ended Comments" field.
12. Enter any feedback from the employee in the "Employee Feedback" field.
13. Enter any feedback from the manager in the "Manager Feedback" field.
14. Choose the status of the evaluation form (e.g. "Complete", "Partially Complete", "Incomplete") in the "Evaluation Form Status" field.
15. Enter any comments on the evaluation form in the "Evaluation Form Comments" field.
16. Enter the count of submissions in the "Submission Count" field.
17. Choose the status of the submission (e.g. "Complete", "Partially Complete", "Incomplete") in the "Submission Status" field.
18. Enter any comments on the submission in the "Submission Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Title**: Enter the employee's title in this field.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee in this field.
* **Recognition Channels**: Choose the channels through which recognition was received (e.g. "Channel 1", "Channel 2", "Channel 3").
* **Recognition Frequency**: Rate the frequency of recognition in the employee's role (e.g. "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly").
* **Recognition Amount**: Rate the amount of recognition the employee received (e.g. "High", "Medium", "Low").
* **Recognition Value**: Choose how effective the recognition was in achieving its goals (e.g. "High", "Medium", "Low").
* **Recognition Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of recognition in the employee's role (e.g. "Very Effective", "Somewhat Effective", "Not Effective").
* **Recognition Impact**: Choose the impact of recognition on the employee (e.g. "Significant", "Moderate", "Minimal").
* **Recognition Satisfaction**: Rate how satisfied the employee is with the recognition (e.g. "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", "Not Satisfied").
* **Recognition Likelyhood**: Rate how likely the employee is to participate in the recognition program in the future (e.g. "Very Likely", "Somewhat Likely", "Not Likely").
* **Recognition Suggestions**: Enter any additional suggestions or comments on the recognition program.
* **Recognition Open-Ended Comments**: Enter any open-ended comments on the recognition program.
* **Employee Feedback**: Enter any feedback from the employee.
* **Manager Feedback**: Enter any feedback from the manager.
* **Evaluation Form**: Choose the evaluation form from the provided options.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Evaluation Form Status**: Choose the status of the evaluation form (e.g. "Complete", "Partially Complete", "Incomplete").
* **Evaluation Form Comments**: Enter any comments on the evaluation form.
* **Submission Count**: Enter the count of submissions.
* **Submission Status**: Choose the status of the submission (e.g. "Complete", "Partially Complete", "Incomplete").
* **Submission Comments**: Enter any comments on the submission.
