# Ctl U Leasing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ctl U Leasing Survey form is designed to collect basic demographic information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. If applicable, enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter your company name in the **company** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first_name
* **first_name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### last_name
* **last_name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### date_of_birth
* **date_of_birth**: If you want to provide your date of birth, please enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy. (Optional)

### company
* **company**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business documents.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form owner or submit your response and let us follow up with further questions.
