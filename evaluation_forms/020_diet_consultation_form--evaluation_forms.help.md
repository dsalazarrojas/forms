# Diet Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diet Consultation Form is designed to gather information from users about their dietary preferences, health conditions, and other relevant details to help healthcare professionals provide personalized diet recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your Date of Birth (DOB) to ensure accurate age calculation.
3. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any dietary restrictions or preferences.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about your diet or health concerns.
6. Complete the form by answering the questions and providing any necessary information for each page.
