# Vendor Selection Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Selection Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the suitability of a vendor for potential partnership or collaboration by assessing their ability to provide value through various aspects, including pricing, quality, support, compliance, security, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vendor Selection Evaluation Form, follow these steps:

1. **Select one of the two options for "Select Vendor" on Page 1**:
	* Choose 'Yes' if the vendor meets your organization's needs.
	* Choose 'No' if the vendor does not meet your organization's needs.
2. **Enter a numerical value for "Pricing" on Page 1**:
	* Enter the vendor's pricing value (e.g., 1-10, where 1 is lowest and 10 is highest).
3. **Rate "Quality" on Page 1**:
	* Enter a numerical value (e.g., 1-10, where 1 is lowest and 10 is highest).
4. **Describe "Support" on Page 1**:
	* Type a brief description of the vendor's support experience.
5. **Select all applicable options for "Compliance" on Page 1**:
	* Choose all the options that apply to the vendor's compliance.
6. **Select one of the two options for "Security" on Page 1**:
	* Choose 'Yes' if the vendor meets your organization's security standards.
	* Choose 'No' if the vendor does not meet your organization's security standards.
7. **Describe "Communication" on Page 2**:
	* Type a brief description of the vendor's communication experience.
8. **Describe "Training" on Page 2**:
	* Type a brief description of the vendor's training experience.
