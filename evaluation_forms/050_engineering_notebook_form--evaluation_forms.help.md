# Engineering Notebook Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Engineering Notebook Form is used to collect meeting notes, ideas, and other relevant information from engineers and other stakeholders during meetings. It serves as a reference point for capturing and organizing the key discussions, ideas, and decisions made during the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant meeting notes and ideas from the text fields on pages 1 and 2.
2. Choose the teams that attended the meeting from the dropdown options on page 3.
3. Enter a brief report or summary of the meeting on page 4.
4. Outline the objectives of the meeting on page 5.
5. Record your reflections on the meeting on page 6.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **meeting_notes** (Page 1):
  Meeting notes: Enter a brief description of the key discussions, decisions, and actions taken during the meeting.

* **ideas** (Page 2):
  Ideas: Share any key ideas, insights, or suggestions generated during the meeting.

* **sketches** (Page 3):
  Sketches: Attach any relevant sketches or diagrams created during the meeting.

* **teams_attendance** (Page 4):
  Teams Attendance: Select the teams that attended the meeting.

* **report** (Page 5):
  Report: Enter a brief summary of the key takeaways from the meeting.

* **objectives** (Page 6):
  Objectives: Outline the key objectives or outcomes of the meeting.

* **reflections** (Page 7):
  Reflections: Record your reflections on the meeting, including what worked well and areas for improvement.

* **comments** (Page 8):
  Comments: Add any additional comments or feedback on the meeting.

## Tips
* Be as brief and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Use the text fields to capture the key discussions and ideas.
* Use the select multiple option for teams attendance to ensure accurate representation.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all relevant information is captured.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
