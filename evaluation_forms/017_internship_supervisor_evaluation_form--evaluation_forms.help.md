# internship_supervisor_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internship_supervisor_evaluation_form is designed to gather feedback from the supervisor on an intern's performance during their internship period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supervisor_name**: Enter the name of the person completing the evaluation.
* **internship_start_date**: Select the start date of the internship.
* **evaluation_date**: Select the date of the evaluation.
* **evaluation_frequency**: Choose the evaluation frequency (Internship Period, Half Year, Final evaluation, or Not Applicable).
* **performance_achievement**: Describe the intern's performance achievement.
* **communication_skills**: Rate the intern's communication skills (Strong, Fair, or Weak).
* **teamwork_skills**: Rate the intern's teamwork skills (Strong, Fair, or Weak).
* **initiative**: Rate the intern's initiative (Strong, Fair, or Weak).
* **feedback_to_improve**: Provide feedback on areas for improvement.
* **overall_performance**: Rate the overall performance (Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement).

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
* Be specific when providing feedback on areas for improvement.
* Rate the intern's skills and performance based on the evaluation period.
