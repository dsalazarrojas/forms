# Trainee Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trainee Assessment Form is used to evaluate the progress and performance of trainees in various training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Trainee Assessment Form Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the training program being assessed. This will help you identify the specific program you're evaluating.
2. **Trainee Assessment Rating** (Number): Rate the trainee's overall performance in the training program using a numerical score out of 10.
3. **Trainee Assessment Comments** (Note, Optional): Provide feedback and comments about the trainee's performance in the training program. This can include areas of improvement and strengths.
4. **Trainee Assessment Supervisor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the supervisor who completed this assessment. This is for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainee Assessment Form Name**: Enter the name of the training program being assessed.
*   **Trainee Assessment Rating**: Rate the trainee's overall performance in the training program using a numerical score out of 10. A higher score indicates better performance.
*   **Trainee Assessment Comments**: Provide feedback and comments about the trainee's performance in the training program.
*   **Trainee Assessment Supervisor Name**: Enter your name as the supervisor who completed this assessment. This is for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you are filling out the assessment for the correct trainee and that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Provide specific, concrete feedback that is helpful to the trainee.
*   Keep your comments professional and respectful.
*   If you are unsure about any part of this form, contact your supervisor or HR department for clarification.
