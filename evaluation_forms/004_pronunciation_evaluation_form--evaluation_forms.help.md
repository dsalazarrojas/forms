# Pronunciation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by evaluators to collect and evaluate the pronunciation of students. It is intended to provide a structured way to gather feedback from students and help them improve their pronunciation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the name of the student being evaluated.
2. If applicable, upload an audio file of the student's pronunciation in the **Student Audio** field.
3. Enter your **Evaluator Name** in the field provided.
4. Enter the date of the evaluation in the **Evaluation Date** field.
5. Provide a score out of 5 for the student's pronunciation in the **Score** field.
6. Provide feedback on the student's pronunciation in the **Feedback** field.
7. Add comments on the student's audio recording in the **Student Audio Comment**, **Audio Feedback**, and **Evaluator Audio Comment** fields.
8. Finally, provide an overall evaluation comment in the **Evaluation Comment** field and a score comment in the **Score Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: The name of the student being evaluated. Please enter the student's name in this field.
* **Student Audio**: An optional audio file of the student's pronunciation. If you do not have an audio file, you can leave this field blank.
* **Evaluator Name**: The name of the evaluator filling out this form.
* **Evaluation Date**: The date of the evaluation. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Score**: A score out of 5 for the student's pronunciation. Please use numbers only.
* **Feedback**: Feedback for the student's pronunciation. Please be specific and helpful.
* **Student Audio Comment**: Comments on the student's audio recording.
* **Audio Feedback**: Comments on the audio recording.
* **Evaluator Audio Comment**: Comments on the audio recording from the evaluator's perspective.
* **Evaluation Comment**: Overall feedback for the student's pronunciation.
* **Score Comment**: Comments on the score given.
* **Student Audio Comment**: Comments on the student's audio recording.
* **Evaluator Audio Comment**: Comments on the evaluator's own audio recording.
* **Evaluation Comment** and **Score Comment**: Comments on the evaluation and score respectively, please provide feedback on the student's pronunciation.
