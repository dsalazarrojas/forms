# Research Study Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Study Evaluation Form is designed to collect evaluations and feedback from participants and stakeholders on research studies. It gathers information on the effectiveness of a research study, including participant feedback, participant demographics, and ratings of the research instrument and institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the participant feedback questions on page 1.
2. Rate the effectiveness of the research instrument on page 2.
3. Rate the effectiveness of the research institution on page 3.
4. Provide any additional comments on pages 2 and 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_feedback (Page 1)**: Please provide your honest feedback on your experience with the research study.
* **study_effectiveness (Page 2)**: Rate the effectiveness of the research instrument, with higher ratings indicating better performance.
* **participant_demographics (Page 2)**: Please provide your demographic information, such as age, sex, and occupation.
* **participant_comments (Page 2)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the research study.
* **instrument_effectiveness (Page 3)**: Rate the effectiveness of the research instrument, with higher ratings indicating better performance.
* **institution_effectiveness (Page 3)**: Rate the effectiveness of the research institution, with higher ratings indicating better performance.
* **instrument_comments (Page 3)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the research instrument.
* **institution_comments (Page 3)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the research institution.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Be as honest and accurate as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
* This form should only be completed by participants or stakeholders who have been part of the research study.
