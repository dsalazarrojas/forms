# Food Taste Testing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the taste, appearance, and overall experience of a food item. It is intended to be filled out by customers or taste testers after they have sampled a food product. The data collected will help our team to understand how well we have met our quality and taste standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with the food item.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Item** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the food item you are evaluating.
* **Taste Rating** (2, number, required/optional): Rate the taste of the food item on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Aroma Level** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that describe the aroma of the food item. Choose from: Strong, Medium, or Weak.
* **Appearance Color** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that describe the color of the food item. Choose from: White, Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black, Brown, Gray, or Other.
* **Food Taste** (5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that describe the taste of the food item. Choose from: Sweet, Savory, Sour, Bitter, or Other.
* **Texture** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that describe the texture of the food item. Choose from: Smooth, Crunchy, Soft, Hard, or Other.
* **Overall Experience** (7, select_one, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with the food item. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Would You Recommend** (8, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you would recommend the food item. Choose from: Yes or No.
* **Comments** (9, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the food item.
* **Email** (10, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone** (11, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

Please fill out this form as completely as possible to help us better understand your experience with the food item.
