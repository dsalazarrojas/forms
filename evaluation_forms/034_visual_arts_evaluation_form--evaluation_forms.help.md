# Visual Arts Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Visual Arts Evaluation Form is designed to collect information and feedback from students on their artistic creations. This form is used to evaluate a student's artwork, gather feedback, and track progress in visual arts courses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information and fields to complete it.
2. Enter the student's information, including their name and any relevant details.
3. Provide the student's artwork information, including any relevant details about the artwork itself.
4. Enter your evaluation of the student's artwork, using the scale provided.
5. Add any comments or feedback you have about the student's artwork.
6. Enter your grade for the student's artwork.
7. Record the submission date and time of the artwork.
8. Provide feedback to the student, if applicable.
9. Finally, review and submit the form for review and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student Information)** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter the student's name, student ID, or any other relevant details.
* **Artwork Information (Artwork Information)** (`artwork_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the artwork, such as the medium used, dimensions, or inspiration.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, number, optional): Use the scale provided to evaluate the student's artwork. The scale is:
	+ Yes ( indicates excellent work)
	+ No ( indicates poor or incomplete work)
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the student's artwork. Keep your feedback specific, constructive, and respectful.
* **Grade (Grade)** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter a numerical grade for the student's artwork. Be fair and objective in your grading.
* **Submission Date (Submission Date)** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Record the date and time the artwork was submitted.
* **Student Feedback (Student Feedback)** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Allow the student to provide their feedback about their own artwork.
* **Teacher Feedback (Teacher Feedback)** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback to the student about their artwork, if applicable.
* **Evaluation Scale (Evaluation Scale)** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Evaluation Scale Label (Evaluation Scale Label)** (`evaluation_scale_label`, text, optional): Enter a description or label for the evaluation scale, if desired.
