# research_conference_paper_quality_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the quality of a research conference paper. It helps assess the paper's introduction, research question, relevance, originality, impact, and overall quality score.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each field according to your honest evaluation of the paper's quality.
2. Select the most suitable option for each field from the provided choices.
3. Enter your comments and suggestions for the author in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (<type>: text, <required>: false): The introduction section of the paper should provide a clear and concise overview of the research topic, research goals, and objectives.
* **Research question** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Evaluate the paper's research question by selecting one of the provided options. This will help assess the clarity and focus of the research.
* **Paper quality** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Evaluate the overall quality of the paper by selecting one of the provided options. This will help assess the paper's organization, structure, and overall presentation.
* **Relevance** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Evaluate the relevance of the paper to the research conference topic by selecting one of the provided options.
* **Originality** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Evaluate the originality of the paper by selecting one of the provided options. This will help assess the paper's contribution to the research community.
* **Impact** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Evaluate the potential impact of the paper on the research community by selecting one of the provided options.
* **Paper quality 1-10** (<type>: number, <required>: false): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate the paper's quality.
* **Paper quality 2-10** (<type>: number, <required>: false): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate the paper's quality from another perspective.
* **Paper quality 3-10** (<type>: number, <required>: false): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate the paper's quality from another perspective.
* **Overall quality score** (<type>: number, <required>: false): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate the overall quality of the paper.
* **Comments** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Enter any comments or suggestions for the author in this field.

Note: Please answer each field according to your honest evaluation of the paper's quality.
