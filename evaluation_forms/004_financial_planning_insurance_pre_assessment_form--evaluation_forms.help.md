# Financial Planning Insurance Pre Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking financial planning and insurance services to better understand their needs and provide tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of application that best describes your needs.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the type(s) of insurance you are interested in (e.g., Auto, Home, Health).
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your street address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Specify your current insurance status (Active or Inactive).
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your current financial situation.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select any relevant medical conditions (e.g., Heart Issues, Cancer, Diabetes).
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your insurance company name.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your policy number (if applicable).
* **Agent ID** (`agent_id`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your agent's ID.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your agent's name.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the end date of your policy.
* **Premium Frequency** (`premium_frequency`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how often you pay premiums (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select your preferred payment methods (Auto, Manual, Online).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter your payment date.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Specify your payment status (Paid, Pending, Overdue).
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide additional information about your payment method.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of policy you are interested in (Term Life, Whole Life, Universal Life).
