# Judge Eval Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for evaluators to provide feedback on a group of contest participants. It will help you assess and rank the participants based on specific criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form's questions and ensure you have all the necessary information to provide accurate and relevant feedback.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability, based on your knowledge and expertise.
* If you are unsure about a particular question, do not hesitate to contact the form's administrators for clarification.
* Ensure that you complete all required fields and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Profile** (group_profile, text, optional): This field is used to gather general information about the group being assessed. Please provide a brief description of the group, including any relevant details that may impact the evaluation.
* **Prioritizing Contestants** (prioritizing_contestants, text, optional): In this field, rank the participants in order of priority based on your assessment. You can use a simple ranking system, such as 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Present Performance** (present_performance, text, optional): This field is used to assess the participants' present performance in the competition. Please provide a brief description of their performance, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Summary Assessment** (summary_assessment, text, optional): In this field, provide a summary of your overall assessment of the participants. This should include a brief statement of your evaluation, highlighting any key findings or recommendations.
