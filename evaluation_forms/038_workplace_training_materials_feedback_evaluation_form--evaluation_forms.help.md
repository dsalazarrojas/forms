# Workplace Training Materials Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Workplace Training Materials Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the effectiveness and quality of the training materials provided to them. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and ensure that future training programs are well-structured and effective.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Employee ID to identify yourself as the respondent.
2. Select the type of training session you attended from the options provided.
3. If you wish to provide feedback, enter your rating on the rating scale and write any comments or feedback in the provided text fields.
4. Indicate whether you would recommend the training to others and how effective you found the training to be.
5. Enter your E-Mail address to ensure we can contact you for any further information.
6. Finally, if you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, `required`): Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, `required`): This field is not applicable for this specific form as training session is selected on the next field.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of training session you attended (e.g. Online, Classroom, Video Conference, or Other).
* **Date Attended** (`date_attended`, `date`, `optional`): If you remember the date you attended the training session, please enter it in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This field is not required but can help us to track the progress of training sessions.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your rating of the training session using the provided scale (Scale 1-5 or Other).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, `optional`): If you want to provide any feedback, please enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `optional`): If you want to provide any comments, please enter them here.
* **E-Mail** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your valid E-Mail address so we can contact you for any further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `optional`): This field can be filled if you want to provide your phone number for any further information.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **Training Materials** (`training_materials`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the type of training materials you used (e.g. Online, Classroom, Video Conference, or Other).
* **Training Provided By** (`training_provided_by`, `select_one`, `required`): Select who provided the training (e.g. Manager, Supervisor, Peer, or Training Provider).
* **How Effective Was The Training?** (`training_effectiveness`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how effective you found the training to be (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Neutral, Somewhat Ineffective, or Very Ineffective).
* **Would You Recommend This Training To Others?** (`will_recommend`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you would recommend the training to others (True, False, or Undecided).

Note that each field is clearly labeled with the corresponding label to ensure that you know which field is which.
