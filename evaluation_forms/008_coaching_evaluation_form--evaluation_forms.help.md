# Coaching Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their coaching experience with their coach. This form helps us understand the effectiveness of our coaching services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form with your honest and candid feedback.
2. Answer each question carefully, providing specific examples or anecdotes where possible.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (id: 2, required): Enter your full name as the client.
* **Coach Name** (id: 3, required): Enter your coach's name.
* **Coaching Period** (id: 4, required): Select the duration of your coaching engagement (e.g., 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2+ years).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Coaching** (id: 6, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coaching experience.
* **Coach Competence and Knowledge** (id: 7, required): Rate your coach's expertise in coaching.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (id: 8, required): Rate how well your coach communicated with you.
* **Progress Toward Your Goals** (id: 9, required): Rate your progress on your stated goals.
* **Coach Professionalism** (id: 10, required): Rate your coach's professionalism and conduct.
* **What did your coach do well?** (id: 12, required): Describe your coach's strengths.
* **What could your coach improve?** (id: 13, optional): Describe areas where your coach could improve.
* **Impact on Your Performance or Skills** (id: 14, required): Describe how coaching has affected you.
* **Would you recommend this coach to others?** (id: 15, required): Rate whether you would recommend your coach to others.
* **Would you like to continue coaching?** (id: 17, required): Indicate your willingness to continue coaching.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 18, optional): Provide any other feedback or suggestions.
* **Evaluation Date** (id: 19, required): Enter the date of your evaluation.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed. Optional fields can provide additional information but are not mandatory.
