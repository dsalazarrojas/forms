# Participant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Feedback Form is designed to gather valuable insights from participants about their experiences. By completing this form, you will help us understand what worked well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions that apply to your trip experience. Since all fields are optional, feel free to skip any that are not relevant.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to ensure we can effectively use your feedback.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to ensure correct categorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Planning a trip? (1)** (`page_one`, `text`, optional): A brief description of your trip planning process, e.g., "I planned a trip to the beach for a week."
* **your feedback (2)** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Your thoughts and opinions about our service or experience.
* **type of trip (3)** (`trip_type`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ `adventurer`: A trip with an emphasis on adventure or excitement.
	+ `tourist`: A more leisurely or sightseeing-focused trip.
	+ `both`: A mix of both adventure and sightseeing.
* **rating (4)** (`rating`, `number`, optional): A numerical value indicating your satisfaction level (e.g., 1-5).
* **trip location (5)** (`trip_location`, `text`, optional): The location(s) you visited during your trip.
* **trip duration (6)** (`trip_duration`, `number`, optional): The length of your trip in days.
* **trip date (7)** (`trip_date`, `date`, optional): The date(s) of your trip.
* **trip time (8)** (`trip_time`, `time`, optional): The time of day(s) you spent on your trip.
* **email (9)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Your email address (e.g., `[user.name@example.com](mailto:user.name@example.com)`).
* **phone (10)** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number or any other contact information.
* **participant name (11)** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Your name, if applicable.
* **participant rating (12)** (`participant_rating`, `number`, optional): Your satisfaction level with our service (e.g., 1-5).
* **participant notes (13)** (`participant_notes`, `note`, optional): Any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **participant email (14)** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Your email address, if different from the one above.
* **participant phone (15)** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number or any other contact information.
* **participant comments (16)** (`participant_comments`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Do you agree (17)** (`participant_agree`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ `yes`: You are generally satisfied with our service.
	+ `no`: You are not satisfied with our service.

Please note that the "Do you agree" question is not a yes/no question but rather a satisfaction check.
