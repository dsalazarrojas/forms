# Computer Based Driving Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants who have undergone a computer-based driving evaluation. It will help assess their control skills, hazard recognition, and reaction time during the evaluation. This data will be used to evaluate their driving abilities and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's information, including their full name and any relevant license details.
2. Enter the date of the assessment.
3. Specify the simulator or software used for the evaluation.
4. Evaluate the participant's control skill rating, selecting from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
5. Assess the participant's hazard recognition, choosing from the options: Strong, Adequate, or Weak.
6. Evaluate the participant's reaction time, selecting from the options: Fast, Average, or Slow.
7. Provide feedback and recommendations in the "Evaluator comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`Participant name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Driving license number** (`Driving license number`, text, optional): Enter the license number or state issued license details.
* **Evaluation date** (`Evaluation date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Simulator used** (`Simulator used`, text, optional): Specify the model or software used for the evaluation.
* **Control skill rating** (`Control skill rating`, select one, required): Assess the participant's control skills by selecting from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hazard recognition** (`Hazard recognition`, select one, required): Evaluate the participant's ability to recognize and respond to hazards by choosing from the options: Strong, Adequate, or Weak.
* **Reaction time** (`Reaction time`, select one, required): Select the participant's reaction time category: Fast, Average, or Slow.
* **Evaluator comments** (`Evaluator comments`, text, optional): Provide feedback and recommendations for the participant.
