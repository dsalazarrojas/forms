# Functional Behavior Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Functional Behavior Assessment Form is designed to assess and document an individual's functional behavior, which refers to the observable actions or behaviors that an individual exhibits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, Text, Optional): This field is for a brief introduction or context about the assessment or evaluation.
* **Behavior Observed** (`2`, Text, Optional): Describe the behaviors or actions that have been observed.
* **Behavior Observed 1** (`3`, Text, Optional): Describe the first observed behavior.
* **Behavior Observed 2** (`4`, Text, Optional): Describe the second observed behavior.
* **Behavior Observed 3** (`5`, Text, Optional): Describe the third observed behavior.
* **Behavior Observed 4** (`6`, Text, Optional): Describe the fourth observed behavior.
* **Behavior Observed 5** (`7`, Text, Optional): Describe the fifth observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs** (`8`, Text, Optional): Describe any signs or indicators of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 1** (`9`, Text, Optional): Describe the first sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 2** (`10`, Text, Optional): Describe the second sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 3** (`11`, Text, Optional): Describe the third sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 4** (`12`, Text, Optional): Describe the fourth sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 5** (`13`, Text, Optional): Describe the fifth sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 6** (`14`, Text, Optional): Describe the sixth sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 7** (`15`, Text, Optional): Describe the seventh sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 8** (`16`, Text, Optional): Describe the eighth sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 9** (`17`, Text, Optional): Describe the ninth sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Behavioral Signs 10** (`18`, Text, Optional): Describe the tenth sign or indicator of the observed behavior.
* **Functional Behavior Scale** (`19`, Text, Optional): Describe the functional behavior scale used in the assessment.
* **Functional Behavior Scale 1** (`20`, Text, Optional): Describe the first scale used in the assessment.
* **Functional Behavior Scale 2** (`21`, Text, Optional): Describe the second scale used in the assessment.
* **Functional Behavior Scale 3** (`22`, Text, Optional): Describe the third scale used in the assessment.
* **Functional Behavior Scale 4** (`23`, Text, Optional): Describe the fourth scale used in the assessment.
* **Behavioral Signs 11** (`24`, Text, Optional): Describe the eleventh sign or indicator of the observed behavior.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can fill them in as needed for your specific assessment or evaluation.
