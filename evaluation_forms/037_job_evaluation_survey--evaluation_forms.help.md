# job_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Evaluation Survey is designed to collect information about a job's characteristics and evaluate its suitability for completion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. For each field, provide a brief description or answer according to your knowledge.
3. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) unless otherwise specified.
4. Use the select fields (e.g., 'Yes', 'No') to indicate your evaluation.
5. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (job_title): A brief description of the job title.
* **Job Description** (job_description): A detailed description of the job's responsibilities and duties.
* **Job Responsibilities** (job_responsibilities): Check all job responsibilities that apply. (Select all that apply, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Requirements** (job_requirements): Check if the job requires any specific skills or qualifications. (Select all that apply, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Abilities** (job_abilities): Check if the job requires any specific abilities. (Select all that apply, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Knowledge** (job_knowledge): Check if the job requires any specific knowledge. (Select one, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Skills** (job_skills): Check all job skills that apply. (Select all that apply, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Experience** (job_experience): A numerical value indicating the level of job experience.
* **Job Awareness** (job_awareness): Check all job awareness that apply. (Select all that apply, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Training** (job_training): Check all job training that apply. (Select all that apply, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Evaluation** (job_evaluation): A brief description of your evaluation of the job.
* **Job Evaluation Comment** (job_evaluation_comment): Additional comments or feedback about the job.
* **Job Recommendations** (job_recommendations): Additional recommendations or suggestions for the job.
* **Job Improvement Plan** (job_improvement_plan): A brief description of the plan to improve the job.
* **Job Evaluation Date** (job_evaluation_date): The date of the job evaluation.
* **Job Evaluation Time** (job_evaluation_time): The time spent on the job evaluation.
* **Job Urgency** (job_urgency): Check if the job has high or low urgency. (Select one, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Urgency Justifications** (job_urgency_justifications): Additional justifications or reasons for the job urgency.
* **Job Category** (job_category): Check if the job belongs to a specific category. (Select one, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Frequency** (job_frequency): Check if the job occurs frequently or infrequently. (Select one, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Frequency Justifications** (job_frequency_justifications): Additional justifications or reasons for the job frequency.
* **Job Priority** (job_priority): Check if the job has high or low priority. (Select one, e.g., 'Yes', 'No')
* **Job Priority Justifications** (job_priority_justifications): Additional justifications or reasons for the job priority.
* **Job Status** (job_status): Check the current status of the job. (Select one, e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive')
