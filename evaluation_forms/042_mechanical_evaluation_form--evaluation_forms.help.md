# Mechanical Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mechanical Evaluation Form is used to collect information about a mechanical issue or repair on a machine or vehicle. This form helps assess the severity and impact of the issue, and provides relevant details to facilitate a prompt resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, follow these steps:
    1.  Select the relevant page for the mechanical issue or repair you are evaluating.
    2.  Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.
    3.  Review your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mechanical Evaluation** (1): Enter a brief description of the mechanical issue or repair, including any relevant details such as symptoms or symptoms' causes.
* **Repair Type** (2): Select the type of repair or evaluation you are performing (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Date** (3): Enter the date of the mechanical issue or repair occurred.
* **Time** (4): Enter the time of day when the mechanical issue or repair occurred (if known).
* **Description** (5): Enter a detailed description of the issue or repair, including any relevant notes or comments.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear understanding of the mechanical issue or repair.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
* For optimal form submission, please review and verify your entries before submitting the form.
