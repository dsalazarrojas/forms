# Workstation Ergonomic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workstation Ergonomic Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the ergonomic comfort and safety of a workstation. It is used to collect information about the workstation's layout, user's position, and potential ergonomic issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Enter the required information for each question, marked as "Required" in the form.
3. Use the available options to select relevant information for each question.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workspace Description** (`workspace_description`): A brief description of the workstation, including its location and any notable features.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`): The number of hours spent working at the workstation.
* **Workstation ID** (`workstation_id`): A unique identifier for the workstation.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): The name of the employee using the workstation.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): The name of the supervisor or manager of the employee.
* **Workstation Type** (`workstation_type`): The type of workstation being used (e.g., chair, desk, keyboard, mouse, monitor, headset, or other).
* **Workstation Location** (`workstation_location`): The location of the workstation, such as the office, home, or warehouse.
* **Additional Workstation Description** (`workstation_description`): Any additional information about the workstation.
* **Workspace Dimensions** (`workspace_dimensions`): The dimensions of the workspace, including width and height in inches or centimeters.
* **Monitor Position** (`monitor_position`): The position of the monitor, such as left, right, straight, above, below, behind, or in front of.
* **Keyboard Position** (`keyboard_position`): The position of the keyboard, such as left, right, straight, above, below, behind, or in front of.
* **Mouse Position** (`mouse_position`): The position of the mouse, such as left, right, straight, above, below, behind, or in front of.
* **Ergonomic Checklist** (`ergonomic_checklist`): A checklist of potential ergonomic issues, such as True or False.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Any additional comments or observations about the workstation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): The name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`): The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Workstation Ergonomic Score** (`workstation_ergonomic_score`): A score indicating the ergonomic comfort level of the workstation.
* **Ergonomic Rating** (`ergonomic_rating`): A rating of the workstation's ergonomics, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Workstation Ergonomic Score Scale** (`workstation_ergonomic_score_scale`): A scale used to rate the ergonomic comfort level of the workstation.
* **Ergonomic Score Category** (`ergonomic_score_category`): The category of the ergonomic score, such as high, medium, or low.
