# summer_camp_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The summer camp evaluation form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from parents or guardians on their child's experience at the summer camp. This feedback is essential for the camp administrators to evaluate and improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to answer each question accurately.
2. For the rating fields (Program Quality, Fellow Campers, Counselors, Food, Facilities, Program Value, and Worth Attending), select one of the options provided:
	* High: Excellent, met expectations
	* Medium: Good, average
	* Low: Fair, not met expectations
	* Poor: Very poor, not recommended
3. For the open-ended fields (Participant Feedback and Additional Comments), provide your honest feedback on your child's experience at the camp.
4. For the contact information fields (Contact Information, First Name, Last Name, and Phone), enter your contact information if you would like to be contacted for further questions or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Form** (Page 1)
	+ **Participant Feedback**: Provide your general feedback on your child's experience at the camp. This could include positive or negative comments, suggestions, or areas for improvement.
* **Participant Feedback**: This field allows you to share any feedback or comments about your child's experience at the camp. Keep it concise and clear, and avoid repeating information already provided in other fields.
* **Rating - Program Quality**: Rate the quality of the summer camp program.
* **Rating - Fellow Campers**: Rate the quality of the interactions and relationships between campers.
* **Rating - Counselors**: Rate the quality of the camp counselors.
* **Rating - Food**: Rate the quality of the food provided at the camp.
* **Rating - Facilities**: Rate the quality of the camp facilities.
* **Rating - Program Value**: Rate the overall value of the program.
* **Rating - Worth Attending**: Rate how likely you would recommend the camp to others.
* **Rating - Would Recommend**: Rate how likely you would recommend the camp to others.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Contact Information**: If you would like to be contacted for further questions or clarification, enter your contact information here.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

Note: If a field is not filled out, it will not be submitted as part of the form.
