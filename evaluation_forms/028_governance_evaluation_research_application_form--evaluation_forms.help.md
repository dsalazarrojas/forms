## governance_evaluation_research_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect detailed information from applicants for project evaluation research applications, including their background, project specifics, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections for **Applicant Information** (page 1), providing details about yourself as the applicant.
2. Move on to the **Project Information** page (page 2) and fill out the sections with information about your project.
3. Continue to the **Research Information** page (page 3) and enter any relevant details about your research.
4. Next, go to the **Contact Information** page (page 4) and provide your contact details.
5. Finally, move on to the **Project Organizer Information** page (page 5) and enter any relevant details about the project organizer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information (Page 1)
* **Applicant Information (applicant_info)** (`text`, optional): This page is where you should provide an overview of your background as an applicant.
* **Applicant Information (applicant_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter your personal or organization's name and any relevant background details.

### Project Information (Page 2)
* **Project Information (project_info)** (`text`, optional): In this page, enter a brief description of your project, including its main objectives and goals.
* **Project Information (project_info)** (`text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of your project, including its context and relevance.

### Research Information (Page 3)
* **Research Information (research_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about your research, including its methodology and expected outcomes.
* **Research Information (research_info)** (`text`, optional): Describe your research approach and how it will be conducted.

### Contact Information (Page 4)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, optional): Provide any other relevant contact details that may be needed to reach you.

* **Project Organizer Information (project_organiser_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter any details about the project organizer, including their name and role.
* **Project Organizer Information (project_organiser_info)** (`text`, optional): Describe the role of the project organizer and how they will be involved in the project.
