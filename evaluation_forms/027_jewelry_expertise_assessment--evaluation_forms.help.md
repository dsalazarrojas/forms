# Jewelry Expertise Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the expertise level of individuals in the field of jewelry. It will help determine their knowledge, experience, and certifications in this field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Start by answering the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
2. Be sure to read each field's label carefully to understand what information is being asked.
3. If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (1)**: This is a note field, so simply read the information provided.
* **What is your knowledge of jewelry? (2)**: This field asks about your general knowledge of jewelry. Please provide a brief description of your knowledge in this field.
* **What level of expertise do you have in jewelry? (3)**: Choose the level that best describes your level of expertise:
	+ Novice: You have little to no experience in jewelry.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience in jewelry, but are not yet an expert.
	+ Advanced: You have significant experience in jewelry.
	+ Expert: You are a highly experienced professional in jewelry.
* **Do you have experience in this field? (4)**: Answer this question with a numerical value, indicating the number of years you've had experience in jewelry.
* **What type of jewelry do you have experience with? (5)**: Choose all that apply from the list of options, which include gold, silver, pearl, diamond, platinum, and other.
* **How many years do you have of work experience? (6)**: Answer this question with a numerical value, indicating the number of years you've worked in the field of jewelry.
* **What level of education do you have? (7)**: Choose the level of education that best describes your qualifications:
	+ High School: You have a high school diploma or equivalent.
	+ Bachelor: You have a bachelor's degree in a relevant field.
	+ Master: You have a master's degree in a relevant field.
	+ Doctorate: You have a doctorate degree in a relevant field.
	+ Other: You have other relevant education or certifications.
* **Are you certified in this field? (8)**: Answer this question with 'True' or 'False', indicating whether you have any certifications in jewelry.
* **Have you received any training? (9)**: Answer this question with 'True' or 'False', indicating whether you've received any training in jewelry.
* **What type of certification do you have? (10)**: Choose all that apply from the list of options, which include jewelry appraiser, gemologist, and other.
* **What level of certification do you have? (11)**: Choose the level that best describes your certification:
	+ Basic: You have a basic certification.
	+ Intermediate: You have an intermediate certification.
	+ Advanced: You have an advanced certification.
	+ Expert: You have an expert certification.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* This form is designed to assess your expertise level, so be specific and detailed in your answers.
