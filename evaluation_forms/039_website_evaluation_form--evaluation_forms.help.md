# Website Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Website Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and ratings on a website's overall performance, user experience, and other key aspects. This form helps identify areas of improvement and ensures that the website meets the expectations of its users.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please ensure you have the website URL ready.
2. Fill in the required fields with your genuine feedback and ratings.
3. Select the corresponding options for each field based on your experience.
4. Enter your email address and phone number to allow us to contact you for further clarification or feedback.
5. Finally, review your responses and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the website you want to evaluate. This will be used as a reference point for your feedback.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the website's overall performance out of 5. A higher rating indicates a better experience.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the website. This helps us understand your perspective and identify areas for improvement.
* **Website Design** (`website_design`, text, required): Comment on the website's design, including its layout, visual appeal, and overall aesthetic.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the website.
* **Website URL score** (`website_url_score`, number, required): Rate the website's URL score out of 5. A higher score indicates a well-designed and user-friendly URL.
* **Navigation** (`navigation`, select_one, required): Select how clear and easy to follow you found the website's navigation.
* **Website Design score** (`website_design_score`, select_one, required): Rate the website's design score out of 5. A higher score indicates an excellent design.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further clarification or feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for further clarification or feedback.
* **Website satisfaction score** (`website_satisfaction_score`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the website out of 5. A higher score indicates a positive experience.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for further clarification or feedback.
