# Emotional Intelligence Test Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Intelligence Test Template is designed to evaluate an individual's emotional intelligence by rating 24 different scores, each representing a specific aspect of emotional intelligence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page of the form carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Each question is rated on a numerical scale.
3. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_text`, text, required/optional): This section introduces the concept of emotional intelligence and explains the purpose of the form.
* **Self-Awareness Score** (`self_awareness_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to recognize and understand your emotions.
* **Self-Control Score** (`self_control_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to regulate your emotions and behaviors.
* **Social-Awareness Score** (`social_awareness_score`, number, required/optional): This score rates your ability to understand others' emotions and behaviors.
* **Relationship-Maintenance Score** (`relationship_maintenance_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to maintain healthy relationships.
* **Positive Emotions Score** (`positive_emotions_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to experience and express positive emotions.
* **Trust Score** (`trust_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to trust others and trust yourself.
* **Emotional Regulation Score** (`emotional_regulation_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to regulate your emotions.
* **Empathy Score** (`empathy_score`, number, required/optional): This score rates your ability to understand and share the feelings of others.
* **Open-Mindedness Score** (`open_mindedness_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to be open-minded and receptive to new ideas.
* **Adaptability Score** (`adaptability_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to adapt to new situations.
* **Emotional Approach-Avoidance Motivation Score** (`emotional_approach_avoidance_motivation_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your motivation to approach or avoid emotions.
* **Impulse Control Score** (`impulse_control_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to control your impulses.
* **Situational Awareness Score** (`situational_awareness_score`, number, required/optional): This score rates your ability to understand the context and situation.
* **Self-Motivation Score** (`self_motivation_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to motivate yourself.
* **Personal Growth Score** (`personal_growth_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to grow and develop as a person.
* **Positive Attitude Score** (`positive_attitude_score`, number, required/optional): This score rates your positive attitude towards life.
* **Stress Tolerance Score** (`stress_tolerance_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to tolerate stress.
* **Self-Assertiveness Score** (`self_assertiveness_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to assert yourself.
* **Independence Score** (`independence_score`, number, required/optional): This score rates your ability to be independent.
* **Self-Actualization Score** (`self_actualization_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to realize your full potential.
* **Achievement Score** (`achievement_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to achieve your goals.
* **Intrinsic Tenacity Score** (`intrinsic_tenacity_score`, number, required/optional): This score assesses your ability to persist in your goals and desires.
* **Resilience Score** (`resilience_score`, number, required/optional): This score rates your ability to bounce back from challenges.
* **Stress Management Score** (`stress_management_score`, number, required/optional): This score evaluates your ability to manage stress.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about a question, choose the answer that best reflects your feelings and behaviors.

This guide is designed to help you understand and fill out the Emotional Intelligence Test Template. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out.
