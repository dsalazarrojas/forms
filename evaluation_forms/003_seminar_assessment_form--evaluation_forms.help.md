# Seminar Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on seminars to improve their quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your assessment score, feedback, and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **seminar_assessment_form** (1, required/optional): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the seminar's overall quality.
* **overall_content** (2, required/optional): Provide a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the overall content of the seminar.
* **seminar_content** (3, required/optional): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the content of the seminar.
* **materials_used** (4, required/optional): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the materials used during the seminar.
* **presenter_comments** (5, required/optional): Enter any feedback on the presenter's performance.
* **future_gathering** (6, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you think the seminar should be held again in the future, or 'No' if not.
* **future_gathering_comments** (7, required/optional): Add any comments on why the seminar should or should not be held again.
* **seminar_assessment_form_comments** (8, required/optional): Enter any additional comments on the seminar.
