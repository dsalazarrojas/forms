# leadership_practice_inventory_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership_practice_inventory_questionnaire form is designed to collect data on various leadership practices, including categories, descriptions, assigned tools, and forms IDs. This data will be used to develop a comprehensive leadership practice inventory, which can help organizations refine their leadership strategies and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, *): Select the appropriate category that best describes the leadership practice or tool being evaluated.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, *): Provide a detailed description of the leadership practice or tool being evaluated.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, *): Indicate whether the leadership practice or tool is assigned or not.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, `number`, *): Enter the form ID number as a numerical value.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, ): This field is for internal purposes only and not required for users.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `text`, ): Provide a detailed description of the leadership practice or tool being evaluated in the second category.
* **Description 2** (`description_2`, `text`, ): Provide a detailed description of the leadership practice or tool being evaluated in the second category.
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, *): Enter the title of the leadership practice or tool being evaluated.
* **Form IDs 2** (`form_ids_2`, `number`, *): Enter the form ID number as a numerical value.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required to be answered. 

Please review the form carefully before submission and ensure all required fields are filled in.
