# University Curriculum Effectiveness Review Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a university curriculum. It gathers feedback from reviewers on various aspects of the program, including its name, level, and overall impact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are evaluating. This field is required and should be filled out accurately.
2. **Program URL**: If available, enter the URL of the program's website. This field is optional.
3. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. This field is required.
4. **Evaluation Period**: If applicable, enter the period of the evaluation. This field is optional.
5. **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer. This field is required.
6. **Reviewer Title**: Enter your title or position. This field is optional.
7. **Reviewer Institution**: Enter the name of your institution. This field is optional.
8. **Reviewer Department**: Enter the name of your department. This field is optional.
9. **Program Level**: Select the level of the program (Undergraduate, Graduate, Certificate, or Other). This field is required.
10. **Evaluation Type**: Enter the type of evaluation (optional).
11. **Evaluation Methodology**: Enter the methodology used for the evaluation (optional).
12. **Program Outcomes**: Enter any outcomes or benefits of the program (optional).
13. **Program Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations or suggestions for improvement (optional).
14. **Program Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions or comments (optional).
15. **Program Observations**: Enter any observations or comments about the program (optional).
16. **Overall Impact**: Select the overall impact of the program (Significant, Moderate, or None) from the list. This field is optional.
17. **Areas for Improvement**: Enter any areas where the program could be improved (optional).
18. **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the program (optional).
19. **Overall Satisfaction**: Select the overall satisfaction level (Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Highly Dissatisfied) from the list. This field is required.
20. **Overall Rating**: Select the overall rating (1-5) from the list. This field is required.
21. **Rating Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the rating (optional).
22. **Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. This field is required.
23. **Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation (optional).
24. **Location**: Enter the location of the evaluation (optional).
25. **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Please do not duplicate any evaluations. 

Note: The fields marked as required are necessary for a complete evaluation.
