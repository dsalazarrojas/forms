# Student Grade And Assessment Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track student grades and assessments in a general sense. It is meant to be used by teachers to keep a record of student performance and progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for general information on the student's situation.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Enter the date of the assessment.
4. Enter the time of the assessment.
5. Select the grade of the assessment from the provided options (A, B, or C).
6. Add any additional notes about the assessment.
7. Enter the student's email address.
8. Enter the student's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **general_info** (select_multiple): This field allows you to indicate if the student is currently in general information.
* **student_name** (text): Enter the name of the student.
* **assessment_date** (date): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **assessment_time** (time): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **assessment_grade** (select_one): Select the grade of the assessment (A, B, or C).
* **notes** (note): Add any additional notes about the assessment.
* **student_email** (email): Enter the student's email address.
* **student_phone** (text): Enter the student's phone number.
* **teacher_notes** (text): This field is meant to be used by teachers to record any additional information related to the assessment.
 
## Tips

- This form is meant for general student assessment and should not be used for tracking specific student grades in a class.
- Please make sure to enter accurate information in each field, especially the student's name, email address, and phone number.
- If you need to track student progress or grades in a specific class, use a different form.
