# Research Development Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Research Development Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the progress and quality of research projects. This form helps project leaders assess the projects' strengths, weaknesses, and next steps, and provide evaluation comments for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of the research project, including its goals and objectives.
2.  **Project Team Members**: Indicate if the project has a team of members.
3.  **Project Goals**: Enter a brief description of the project's goals.
4.  **Research Objectives**: Choose whether the research project has the following objectives:
	*   'Yes'
	*   'No'
5.  **Strengths**: Enter the strengths of the research project.
6.  **Weaknesses**: Enter the weaknesses of the research project.
7.  **Next Steps**: Enter the next steps for the research project.
8.  **Evaluation Comments**: Enter any evaluation comments for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Description
*   **project_description**: Project Description (`project_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the research project, including its goals and objectives.

### Project Team Members
*   **project_team_members**: Project Team Members (`project_team_members`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if the project has a team of members.

### Project Goals
*   **project_goals**: Project Goals (`project_goals`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the project's goals.

### Research Objectives
*   **research_objectives**: Research Objectives (`research_objectives`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the research project has the following objectives:
	*   'Yes'
	*   'No'

### Strengths
*   **strengths**: Strengths (`strengths`, text, required: false): Enter the strengths of the research project.

### Weaknesses
*   **weaknesses**: Weaknesses (`weaknesses`, text, required: false): Enter the weaknesses of the research project.

### Next Steps
*   **next_steps**: Next Steps (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Enter the next steps for the research project.

### Evaluation Comments
*   **evaluation_comments**: Evaluation Comments (`evaluation_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any evaluation comments for the project.

**Tips**

*   Be as specific as possible when completing each field to ensure a thorough evaluation.
*   Use concise language to ensure easy understanding of the project.
