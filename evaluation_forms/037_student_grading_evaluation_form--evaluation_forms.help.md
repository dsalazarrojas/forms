# Student Grading Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Grading Evaluation Form is used to assess a student's work and progress in a class. It is intended to provide instructors or evaluators with a structured way to evaluate each student's skills and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Assignment Name to identify the specific assignment being evaluated.
2. Enter the Student Name to ensure the evaluation is attributed to the correct student.
3. Fill out the Grade field to provide a numerical value for the student's performance.
4. Add any additional comments about the student's work in the Comments field.
5. Select the submit_comments to indicate the level of completion and quality of work (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
6. Choose the Overall Participation to rate the student's overall participation in the course (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
7. Optionally, enter the Classroom Participation to provide additional feedback on the student's engagement in class.
8. Enter the Date Completed to record the date the evaluation was completed.
9. Enter the Time Completed to record the time the evaluation was completed.
10. Add any final Evaluation Comments to provide additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assignment Name** (`assignment_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assignment being evaluated.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student being evaluated.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value to assess the student's performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the student's work.
* **Submit Comments** (`submit_comments`, select multiple, required): Select the level of completion and quality of work (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Participation** (`overall_participation`, select one, required): Rate the student's overall participation in the course (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Classroom Participation** (`classroom_participation`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value to provide feedback on the student's engagement in class.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Record the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Record the time the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Add any final comments about the student's work.
