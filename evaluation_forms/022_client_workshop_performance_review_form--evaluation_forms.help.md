# Client Workshop Performance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from workshop participants, helping us to evaluate the performance of the client workshop and make informed decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form sections, which are organized into the following areas:
	* Workshop Information
	* Participant Information
	* Content and Delivery
	* Facilitator Performance
	* Value and Outcomes
	* Feedback
2. Answer each question based on your experience and interaction with the workshop.
3. Select your response for each question from the provided options.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture the name of the workshop you attended.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the workshop you attended.
* **Facilitator/Instructor Name** (`facilitator_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the facilitator or instructor who led the workshop.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Content was relevant to my needs** (`content_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "The content was relevant to my needs."
* **Content was clear and well-organized** (`content_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "The content was clear and well-organized."
* **Workshop pacing was appropriate** (`pacing`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "The workshop pacing was appropriate."
* **Facilitator demonstrated subject expertise** (`facilitator_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "The facilitator demonstrated subject expertise."
* **Facilitator was engaging and interactive** (`facilitator_engagement`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "The facilitator was engaging and interactive."
* **Facilitator responded to participant questions** (`facilitator_responsiveness`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "The facilitator responded to participant questions."
* **I gained valuable knowledge and skills** (`learning_value`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "I gained valuable knowledge and skills."
* **I can apply what I learned in my work** (`practical_application`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "I can apply what I learned in my work."
* **Overall workshop satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select an option that reflects your level of agreement with the statement "Overall workshop satisfaction."
* **What did you like most about the workshop?** (`strengths`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or strengths you liked about the workshop.
* **What could be improved?** (`improvements`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or suggestions for improvement.
