# Virtual Mental Wellness Session Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users on their virtual mental wellness session experience. This includes details about the session, the type of session attended, and the outcomes and objectives of the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please review the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the mental wellness organization or service provider for assistance.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) or indicated in the field's label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (Session Details, text, required): This field is where you can provide any additional details about your virtual mental wellness session. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.
* **Session Type** (Session Type, select_one, required): This field asks you to indicate whether your session was a Yes/No type. Please select the most accurate answer based on your experience.
* **Session Date** (Session Date, date, required): This field is where you can enter the date of your virtual mental wellness session. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Session Time** (Session Time, time, required): This field is where you can enter the time of your virtual mental wellness session. Please use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Session Length** (Session Length, number, required): This field asks you to enter the length of your virtual mental wellness session in minutes.
* **Session Objectives** (Session Objectives, text, optional): This field is where you can provide information about the objectives of your virtual mental wellness session. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.
* **Session Outcomes** (Session Outcomes, text, optional): This field is where you can provide information about the outcomes of your virtual mental wellness session. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.
* **Mental Health Concerns** (Mental Health Concerns, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any mental health concerns that arose during your session. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.
* **Mental Health Goals** (Mental Health Goals, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your mental health goals that you discussed during your session. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.
* **Post-Session Follow-Up** (Post-Session Follow-Up, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any follow-up information or suggestions for future sessions. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your virtual mental wellness session. Please be as specific and thorough as possible.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the form, please contact the mental wellness organization or service provider.
* This form is designed to improve the mental wellness services provided, so your input is greatly appreciated!
