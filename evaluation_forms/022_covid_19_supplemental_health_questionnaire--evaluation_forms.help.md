## COVID 19 Supplemental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This COVID-19 Supplemental Health Questionnaire form is designed to gather essential information about your health status, medical history, and any potential risks or concerns. The information collected will help the dental team ensure a safe and effective experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. Provide any additional information or comments if necessary.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent and understanding of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Questionnaire Date** (id: 1, date, required): This is the date of the screening.
* **Patient Full Name** (id: 2, text, required): Please provide your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (id: 5, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Appointment Type** (id: 6, select_one, required): Choose the type of dental service you are seeking.
* **Are you currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms** (id: 7, select_one, required): Choose if you are currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Which symptoms** (id: 8, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days** (id: 9, select_one, required): Choose if you have recently tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Have you been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days** (id: 10, select_one, required): Choose if you have been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case.
* **Does anyone in your household have COVID-19 symptoms** (id: 11, select_one, required): Choose if anyone in your household is experiencing symptoms.
* **Have you traveled outside the area in the past 14 days** (id: 12, select_one, required): Choose if you have recently traveled outside the area.
* **What is your COVID-19 vaccination status** (id: 13, select_one, optional): Choose your current vaccination status.
* **Do you have any high-risk medical conditions** (id: 14, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of high-risk medical conditions.
* **Do you experience dental anxiety** (id: 15, select_one, required): Choose your level of dental anxiety.
* **Do you have any medical conditions we should be aware of** (id: 16, text, optional): Provide any medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **List current medications** (id: 17, text, optional): Include dosages and any relevant information.
* **Do you have any medication allergies** (id: 18, text, optional): Provide any medication allergies.
* **Do you have a latex allergy** (id: 19, select_one, required): Choose if you have a latex allergy.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 20, text, required): Provide the full name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 21, text, required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Consent to Treatment** (id: 22, select_one, required): Choose to consent to treatment.
* **Understand aerosol procedure risks** (id: 23, select_one, required): Choose to acknowledge the risks associated with aerosol procedures.
* **Agree to wear a mask** (id: 24, select_one, required): Choose to wear a mask at all times except during treatment.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (id: 25, text, optional): Provide any additional information or concerns.
* **Signature Date** (id: 26, date, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submission to avoid errors.
* Ask the dental staff if you are unsure about any aspect of the form.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the COVID-19 Supplemental Health Questionnaire form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the dental staff for assistance.
