# Employee Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Initiative Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess and document the effectiveness of a specific employee initiative or project, providing valuable feedback for employees and the management team to improve future projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **employee_name** (id: 2) of the employee who initiated the project.
2. Provide a brief **description** (id: 3) of the project, including its goals and objectives.
3. Evaluate the **impact** (id: 4) of the project, including its effects on the organization and its stakeholders.
4. Select whether the project had **outcomes** (id: 5) and **future_initiatives** (id: 6) were achieved. For each outcome, select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
5. Add any additional **manager_comments** (id: 7) or **people_comments** (id: 8) regarding the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee who initiated the project.
* **description**: Provide a brief description of the project, including its goals and objectives.
* **impact**: Evaluate the impact of the project on the organization and its stakeholders.
* **outcomes**: Select 'Yes' if the project achieved its intended outcomes, 'No' otherwise.
* **future_initiatives**: Select 'Yes' if the project has future initiatives planned or proposed, 'No' otherwise.
* **manager_comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the manager regarding the project.
* **people_comments**: Add any comments or feedback from other team members or stakeholders regarding the project.
