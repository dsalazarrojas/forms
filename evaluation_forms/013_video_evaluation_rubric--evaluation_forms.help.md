# video_evaluation_rubric - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate video submissions for a job posting. It helps you assess the candidate's video and provide feedback in a structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Introduction" field and briefly describe the video submission you are evaluating. This will serve as a general introduction to your assessment.
2. Evaluate each of the criteria (1-7) on a scale of "Yes" or "No", or "Good" or "Poor", depending on the criteria type. 
	* "Content and Concept": Check if the video clearly and effectively conveys the candidate's idea or concept.
	* "Creativity and Originality": Rate the video's creativity and originality.
	* "Sound and Audio": Check if the video's sound and audio are clear and professional.
	* "Storytelling": Rate the video's storytelling ability.
	* "Technical Skills": Check if the video demonstrates the candidate's technical skills.
	* "Visual Style": Rate the video's visual style and presentation.
	* "Overall Quality": Rate the overall quality of the video.
3. In the "Score Video" field, provide a numerical score out of 10 for the video, based on your assessment.
4. In the "Feedback" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the candidate.
5. Fill out the "Reviewer Comments" field with any comments or feedback you have for the reviewer.
6. Fill out the "Reviewer Name", "Reviewer Email", "Reviewer Phone", "Reviewee Name", "Reviewee Email", "Reviewee Phone", "Reviewee Address", "Reviewee Institution", "Reviewee Job Title", "Reviewee Department", "Reviewee Job Title 2", "Reviewee Job Title 3", "Reviewee Job Title 4", "Reviewee Job Title 5" fields with your contact information and the candidate's information.
7. Click on "Submit" to submit your evaluation.
