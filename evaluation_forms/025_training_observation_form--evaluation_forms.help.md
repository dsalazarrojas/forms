# Training Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Observation Form is used by a trainer to evaluate a trainee's performance after a training session. It helps the trainer to provide constructive feedback and support the trainee's growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Training Observation Form, follow these steps:

1. **Form Header**: This is the introductory page of the form.
2. **Trainee Information**: Enter the trainee's information, including their name or ID number.
3. **Trainer Evaluation**: Evaluate the trainee's performance by selecting one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the trainee.
5. **Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the observation.
6. **Trainer Signature**: Sign your name as the trainer to confirm that you have completed the form.
7. **Trainee Signature**: The trainee should sign their name to confirm they have received the feedback.
8. **Submit**: Select "Yes" if you have completed the form and provided feedback, or "No" if you are unable to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainee Information (text)**: Enter the trainee's name or ID number.
* **Trainer Evaluation (select_one)**: Evaluate the trainee's performance as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments (text)**: Provide additional feedback or comments for the trainee.
* **Date and Time (time)**: Record the date and time of the observation.
* **Trainer Signature (text)**: Sign your name as the trainer to confirm you have completed the form.
* **Trainee Signature (number)**: This field is not clearly defined; assuming it is for the number of days or months since the observation, enter the number of days or months.
* **Submit (select_multiple)**: Select "Yes" if you have completed the form and provided feedback, or "No" if unable to complete.
