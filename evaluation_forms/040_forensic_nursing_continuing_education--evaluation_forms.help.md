# Forensic Nursing Continuing Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from forensic nursing professionals for continuing education purposes. It's intended to track individual progress and completion of required courses and training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, indicated by a * (star), with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
3. If applicable, enter your Date of Birth for verification purposes.
4. Provide a valid Email address for contact and communication.
5. If you have a Phone number, enter it for additional verification and contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (*): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Last Name** (*): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date if you want to provide it for verification purposes. This field is optional.
* **Email** (*): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional verification and contact purposes. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid delays in processing your information.
* Double-check the accuracy of your contact information to ensure that you can be reached effectively.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact [support personnel] for assistance.
