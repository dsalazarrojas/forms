# Wine Tasting Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wine Tasting Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect and record wine tasting experiences. This form is used by wine enthusiasts and professionals to provide detailed feedback on the various attributes of a wine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information such as the name of the wine and its type.
2. Rate the wine's appearance on a scale of 1-10.
3. Describe the aroma of the wine in the text field provided.
4. Describe the taste of the wine in the text field provided.
5. Rate the finish of the wine on a scale of 1-10.
6. Rate the overall impression of the wine on a scale of 1-10.
7. Provide the name of the wine and select its type and vintage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wine Name** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, text): Enter the name of the wine you are evaluating.
- **Type Of Wine** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, select_one): Select the type of wine you are evaluating (Red, White, Sparkling).
- **Region** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, select_multiple): Select the region where the wine is from (Bordeaux, Burgundy, Rioja).
- **Vintage** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, select_one): Select the vintage of the wine (1990, 1995, 2000).
- **Appearance** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, number): Rate the appearance of the wine on a scale of 1-10.
- **Aroma** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, text): Describe the aroma of the wine in the text field provided.
- **Taste** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, text): Describe the taste of the wine in the text field provided.
- **Finish** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, number): Rate the finish of the wine on a scale of 1-10.
- **Overall Impression** (Wine Tasting Evaluation Form, number): Rate the overall impression of the wine on a scale of 1-10.

Note: Make sure to fill out all required fields and rate fields on a scale of 1-10 as necessary.
