# AI Literacy In Healthcare Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Literacy In Healthcare Evaluation Form is designed to assess a healthcare professional's familiarity with AI concepts, their confidence in using AI tools, and their opinions on AI adoption and usage in their organization.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, make sure to have a general understanding of AI concepts and its current state in your organization.

1. Begin by entering your personal information, which will help us understand your role and background.
2. Rate your familiarity with AI concepts, from None to Expert.
3. Indicate your confidence in using AI tools for clinical tasks, rating from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
4. Select up to three most important benefits of AI in healthcare that you believe in.
5. Identify your primary concerns regarding AI adoption, selecting all that apply.
6. If applicable, list any previous AI training you've received, and indicate your preferences for further training topics.
7. Finally, add any additional comments or recommendations you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Department or unit** (Department or unit, text, required): Enter the department or unit you work in.
* **Role or job title** (Role or job title, text, required): Enter your role or job title in the organization.
* **Work email address** (Work email address, email, required): Enter your work email address, using your institutional email.
* **Familiarity with AI concepts** (Familiarity with AI concepts, select_one, required):
	+ None: I have no experience with AI.
	+ Basic: I have a basic understanding of AI concepts.
	+ Intermediate: I have an intermediate understanding of AI concepts.
	+ Advanced: I have an advanced understanding of AI concepts.
	+ Expert: I have an expert-level understanding of AI concepts.
* **Previous AI training** (Previous AI training, text, optional): List any AI training or workshops you've attended.
* **Confidence in using AI tools** (Confidence in using AI tools, number, required): Rate your confidence in using AI tools from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Most important benefits of AI in healthcare** (Most important benefits of AI in healthcare, select_multiple, required):
	+ Diagnostic support
	+ Administrative automation
	+ Patient triage
	+ Documentation assistance
	+ Predictive analytics
* **Primary concerns about AI adoption** (Primary concerns about AI adoption, select_multiple, required):
	+ Patient safety
	+ Data privacy
	+ Bias and fairness
	+ Lack of training
	+ Integration cost
* **Preferred training topics** (Preferred training topics, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Tool usage and workflows
	+ Data privacy and compliance
	+ Clinical validation
	+ Interpreting model output
	+ Troubleshooting and escalation
* **Additional comments or recommendations** (Additional comments or recommendations, text, optional): Share any additional comments or recommendations you have.
