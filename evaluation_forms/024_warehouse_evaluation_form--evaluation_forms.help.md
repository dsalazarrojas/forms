# Warehouse Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from clients or users about their experience with a warehouse. This form helps the facility or organization to understand their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas of improvement, and make necessary adjustments to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate answer for each field based on your experience with the warehouse. Make sure to answer every required field.
2.  Provide accurate and detailed feedback in the optional fields, such as the 'report' and 'comments' sections.
3.  Fill out the 'date visited' and 'time visited' fields with the actual dates and times you visited the warehouse.
4.  Enter the client's email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
5.  Rate the warehouse's service in the 'rating' section.
6.  Fill out the 'category' field with a brief description of the service you visited the warehouse for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warehouse Service** (`warehouse_service`, select_one, required): This field asks you to evaluate the overall service provided by the warehouse. Please choose 'Yes' if you were satisfied with the service, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the warehouse. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, required): This field is to rate the communication of the staff at the warehouse. Please provide any feedback or comments about the communication you experienced.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): This field asks you to evaluate the cleanliness of the warehouse. Please choose 'Yes' if the facility was clean, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required): This field asks you to evaluate the condition of the equipment at the warehouse. Please choose 'Yes' if the equipment was in good condition, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Staff Prof.** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate the professionalism of the staff at the warehouse. Please choose 'Yes' if the staff was professional, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Parking Facility** (`parking`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to evaluate the availability and condition of the parking facility at the warehouse. Please choose 'Yes' if the facility has a parking area and was well-maintained, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Accessibility** (`accessibility`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to evaluate the accessibility of the warehouse, including the availability and condition of wheelchair ramps or elevators. Please choose 'Yes' if the facility was accessible, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Report** (`report`, note, optional): This field is an open-ended field where you can provide any feedback or comments about the warehouse.
* **Client Email** (`email`, email, required): This field asks you to enter your email address, which will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This field asks you to enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, required): This field asks you to enter the date you visited the warehouse.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, time, required): This field asks you to enter the time you visited the warehouse.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate the warehouse's service. Please choose 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This field is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the warehouse.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): This field asks you to briefly describe the service you visited the warehouse for, such as 'pick up supplies' or 'delivery service'.
