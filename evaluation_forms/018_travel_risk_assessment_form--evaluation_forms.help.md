# travel_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess potential travel risks for individuals. It collects information about the trip, medical history, medications, medical documents, and travel dates to evaluate the individual's risk level for travel-related activities. This form is intended to identify potential risks and take necessary precautions for a safe journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing **trip details** in the "trip_details" section.
2. Next, describe your **medical history** in the "medical_history" section.
3. If you have any medical conditions, list them in the "medical_conditions" section.
4. If you are taking any medications, list them in the "medications" section.
5. If you have any important medical documents, provide their details in the "medical_documents" section.
6. Finally, enter your **travel dates** in the "travel_dates" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trip_details**: Enter a brief description of your trip, including the destination, dates, and activities you plan to do. This will help assess your risk level for travel-related activities.
* **medical_history**: Describe any medical conditions you have that may affect your travel plans, such as allergies, chronic illnesses, or previous injuries. This will help identify potential risks and take necessary precautions.
* **medical_conditions**: List any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to your travel, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or recent surgeries. This will help your travel companions or organizers understand your needs and provide necessary support.
* **medications**: If you are taking any medications, list them here. This includes prescription and over-the-counter medications, as well as any supplements or vitamins you take regularly.
* **medical_documents**: If you have any medical documents that may be relevant to your travel, such as a doctor's note or a medical certificate, provide their details here. This will help your travel companions or organizers understand your medical needs and take necessary precautions.
* **travel_dates**: Enter your planned travel dates, including the start and end dates of your trip. This will help assess your risk level for travel-related activities.
