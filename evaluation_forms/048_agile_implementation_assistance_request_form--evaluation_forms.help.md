# Agile Implementation Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agile Implementation Assistance Request Form is a tool used to gather information from organizations requesting assistance with Agile implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization name" field.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the primary contact person for the request in the "Contact person" field.
3. Enter the email address of the primary contact person in the "Contact email" field.
4. Describe the key objectives to achieve through the assistance in the "Implementation goals" field.
5. Outline the current challenges facing your organization in the "Current challenges" field.
6. Indicate the number of people involved in the implementation in the "Team size" field (if known).
7. Select the preferred engagement model for assistance in the "Preferred engagement model" dropdown menu (Consulting, Coaching, Training, or Hybrid).
8. Enter the approximate budget for the assistance in the "Budget estimate" field (if known).
9. Specify the target start and duration of the desired assistance in the "Desired timeline" field (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name**: Enter the name of your company that is requesting assistance with Agile implementation.
* **Contact person**: Provide the name of the primary contact person for this request.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person for follow-up and proposal purposes.
* **Implementation goals**: Describe the key objectives you aim to achieve through the assistance, such as improving productivity, reducing cycle time, or enhancing collaboration.
* **Current challenges**: Outline the obstacles that currently hinder your organization's Agile adoption, such as resistance to change, lack of resources, or unclear processes.
* **Team size**: If known, enter the approximate number of people involved in the implementation, including team members, stakeholders, and sponsors.
* **Preferred engagement model**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **Consulting**: Expert guidance on Agile practices and principles.
	+ **Coaching**: One-on-one coaching for team members or leaders.
	+ **Training**: Workshops, classes, or online courses on Agile topics.
	+ **Hybrid**: A combination of consulting, coaching, and training.
* **Budget estimate**: Enter an approximate budget for the assistance, if known. This will help us better understand your financial constraints.
* **Desired timeline**: Specify the target start and duration of the assistance, if known. This will help us schedule the assistance effectively.
