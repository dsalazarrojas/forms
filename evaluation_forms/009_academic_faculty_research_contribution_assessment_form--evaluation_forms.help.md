# Academic Faculty Research Contribution Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to assess the research contributions of faculty members within the Academic Faculty Research Contribution Assessment context. It is designed to evaluate a faculty member's research productivity, quality, and impact during a specific assessment period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the faculty member's research contributions.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing specific examples and details when necessary.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, taking into account the faculty member's actual research performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`): Enter the full name of the faculty member being assessed.
* **Faculty ID** (`faculty_id`): Enter the employee identification number of the faculty member.
* **Department** (`department`): Indicate the faculty member's academic department affiliation.
* **Rank/Position** (`rank_position`): Specify the faculty member's current academic rank or position.
* **Assessment Period** (`assessment_period`): Define the time period being evaluated (e.g., academic year).
* **Number of Publications** (`number_publications`): Report the number of peer-reviewed publications the faculty member has during the assessment period.
* **Publication Quality Rating** (`publication_quality_rating`): Rate the average quality of publications on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Grant Activity** (`grant_activity`): If applicable, report the number and value of grants secured during the assessment period.
* **Research Collaborations** (`research_collaborations`): Indicate the number of research collaborations initiated or maintained.
* **Conference Presentations** (`conference_presentations`): Report the number of presentations made at academic conferences during the assessment period.
* **Research Impact Metrics** (`research_impact_metrics`): Provide any relevant research impact metrics, such as citations or h-index.
* **Research Funding Secured** (`research_funding_secured`): If applicable, report the total amount of external funding secured during the assessment period.
* **Graduate Student Mentoring** (`graduate_student_mentoring`): Indicate the number of graduate students mentored in research.
* **Research Outreach Activities** (`research_outreach_activities`): Report any public engagement, media appearances, or community outreach activities.
* **Research Ethics Compliance** (`research_ethics_compliance`): Indicate the faculty member's level of compliance with institutional review board requirements (Fully compliant, Minor issues, Major issues, or Not applicable).
* **Overall Research Rating** (`overall_research_rating`): Rate the faculty member's overall research contributions on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Identified Strengths** (`strengths_identified`): Specify the faculty member's research strengths.

## Tips

* Make sure to have all necessary information and data available before filling out the form.
* Take your time to answer each question thoroughly, and do not hesitate to ask for help if needed.
* Review and edit your answers carefully before submitting the form.
