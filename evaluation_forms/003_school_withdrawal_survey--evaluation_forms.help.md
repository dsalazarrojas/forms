# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students or school representatives regarding their withdrawal from the school or program. This information will help the school administration understand the reasons for withdrawal and take necessary steps to improve the institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide personal information about yourself, including your name, email, phone number, and school details.
3. Share your thoughts, disappointments, and reasons for withdrawal.
4. Specify the grade level and program you are enrolled in.
5. If you are a student, provide information about your student grade level and who informed you about the withdrawal.
6. If you are a school representative, provide information about the school programs and other details.
7. Finally, submit the form and ensure that your response is accurate and honest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (1): Enter your personal details, including your name and contact information.
* **Thoughts** (2): Share any thoughts or comments you have about your experience at the school or program.
* **Disappointments** (3): Specify any disappointments or issues you faced while being a part of the school or program.
* **Reasons** (4): Clearly state the reasons for your withdrawal from the school or program.
* **School Info** (5): Provide information about the school you are withdrawing from.
* **Grade Level** (6): Specify the grade level you are currently in or were in when you withdrew.
* **Student Grade Level** (7): If you are a student, specify the grade level you were in or are currently in.
* **Who Told You** (8): If you are a student, specify who informed you about the withdrawal (e.g., teacher, counselor, parent).
* **Name of Student** (9): Enter your name if you are a student.
* **Student Email** (10): Enter your email address if you are a student.
* **Student Phone** (11): Enter your phone number if you are a student.
* **School of Student** (12): If you are a student, specify the school you are currently or were attending.
* **School Program** (13): If you are a student, specify the program you are enrolled in or were enrolled in.
* **School Program Year** (14): If you are a student, specify the year of the program you are or were in.
* **Program** (15): If you are a student, specify your program name.
* **Student Program Grade** (16): If you are a student, specify your program grade level.
* **School Year** (17): If you are a student, specify the year of the school you are or were attending.
* **Other Programs** (18): If you are a student, list any other programs you are or were enrolled in.
* **School Programs** (19): If you are a school representative, list any school programs you are or were involved with.
* **Other Programs (2)** (20): If you are a student, list any other programs you are or were in.
* **School/Programs Info** (21): If you are a school representative, provide additional information about the school or program you are or were involved with.
* **Other Programs Info** (22): If you are a school representative, provide additional information about other programs you are or were involved with.
* **Other School Info** (23): If you are a school representative, provide any other school information.
* **Student Programs Other Info 2** (24): If you are a student, provide additional information about your programs.
* **Submitted By** (25): If you are a school representative, enter your name and title.
* **Submit On** (26): Date you are submitting the form.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Ensure that you have permission to complete the form on behalf of a student or school representative.
- If you are a student, please provide as much detail as possible.
- If you are a school representative, ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
