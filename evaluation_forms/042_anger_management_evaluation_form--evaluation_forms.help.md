# Anger Management Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anger Management Evaluation Form is used to assess the severity of an individual's anger issues, guiding evaluators in providing effective recommendations for interventions and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling in the individual being evaluated's information (name and date).
* Review the assessment areas and select all relevant information.
* Answer the questions regarding the frequency, duration, and severity of anger outbursts.
* Provide a detailed evaluation of the individual's ability to control verbal outbursts, history of physical aggression, and level of insight into their triggers.
* Summarize your findings and provide recommendations for interventions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Individual Being Evaluated** (Required): Enter the name of the individual being assessed.
* **Evaluator Professional Name** (Required): Enter your professional name.
* **Date of Evaluation** (Required): Record the date of the evaluation.
* **Frequency of Anger Outbursts** (Required): Select one of the following:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Average Duration of Anger Episodes** (Required): Select one of the following:
	+ Less than 10 mins
	+ 10-30 mins
	+ 30-60 mins
	+ Hours
	+ Days
* **Ability to Control Verbal Outbursts** (Required): Select one of the following:
	+ Always
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **History of Physical Aggression** (Required): Select 'True' or 'False'
* **Impact on Personal Relationships** (Optional): Describe the impact of anger on the individual's personal relationships.
* **Impact on Professional Life** (Optional): Describe the impact of anger on the individual's professional life.
* **Observed Level of Insight into Triggers** (Required): Select one of the following:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ None
* **Recommended Interventions** (Optional): Select all applicable interventions:
	+ Individual Therapy
	+ Group Counseling
	+ Medication Evaluation
	+ Stress Management Workshop
	+ Regular Exercise
* **Evaluator Summary and Conclusion** (Required): Summarize your evaluation and provide a conclusion.
