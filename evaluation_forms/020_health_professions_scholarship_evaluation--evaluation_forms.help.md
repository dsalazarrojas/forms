# Health Professions Scholarship Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of individuals in health professions. It helps reviewers to assess the candidate's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement based on specific evaluation criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much information as possible for each field.
2. Reviewer information is a required field as it is used to track the identity of the person conducting the evaluation.
3. Select all applicable evaluation criteria that the candidate has demonstrated.
4. Provide overall rating and feedback for the candidate.
5. If necessary, add any additional comments that would be helpful for the candidate's development.
6. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
7. Enter the duration of the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reviewer Info** (`reviewer_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect the information of the person conducting the evaluation. Please enter the reviewer's name and ID number.
* **EVALUATION CRITERIA** (`evaluation_criteria`, `select_one`, required): Choose all of the following criteria that the candidate has demonstrated:
	+ Communication
	+ Interpersonal Skills
	+ Professionalism
	+ Problem Solving
	+ Teamwork
	+ Time Management
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the candidate's performance as:
	+ 1 - Excellent
	+ 2 - Good
	+ 3 - Fair
	+ 4 - Needs Improvement
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the review process.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions for the candidate's development.
