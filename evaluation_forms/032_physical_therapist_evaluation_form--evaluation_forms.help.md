# Physical Therapist Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you evaluate the performance of your physical therapist (PT). It is used to gather feedback on the PT's communication skills, technical skills, attitude, and other aspects of care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the form questions honestly and to the best of your ability. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help improve the quality of care provided by our physical therapists.
2. Complete each question in the space provided, and be as specific as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1)
	+ A brief text entry field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the physical therapist.
* **Physical Therapist** (2)
