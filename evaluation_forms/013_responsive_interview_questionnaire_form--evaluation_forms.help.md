# Responsive Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experiences and thoughts about the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as **required**).
3. Use the available space to provide detailed responses.
4. Choose your answers from the options provided (e.g., select multiple or single options).
5. Submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is this? (Introduction)**: This field is for any initial comments or questions you may have about the form or the company.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in any future correspondence.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **How do you feel about this company? (Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, Very negative)**: Choose one or more of the provided options to describe your feelings about the company.
* **What motivates you? (Career advancement, Financial gain, Work-life balance, Personal growth, Other)**: Select the one option that best describes your motivations for working with the company.
* **How long have you been working?**: Enter the length of time you've been working with the company.
* **What do you do for work?**: Describe your job role or position within the company.
* **Why did you choose this job? (Optional)**: If you have time, share your reasons for choosing your job or the company.
* **What do you like about this job? (Optional)**: Share your positive experiences or things you enjoy about your job.
* **What do you dislike? (Optional)**: Share any challenges or difficulties you've faced in your role.
* **Would you recommend this company? (Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not likely, Not at all likely)**: Choose one or more of the provided options to indicate if you would recommend the company to others.
* **What can we improve? (Optional)**: If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, share them here.

Note: This form has been designed with the end-user in mind, to be as approachable and easy to use. Please don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need further assistance.
