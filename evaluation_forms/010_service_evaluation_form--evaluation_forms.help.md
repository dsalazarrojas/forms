# Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our service. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For the "Would you recommend this course to your colleagues?" field, select "True" if you would definitely recommend the course, "False" if you would not, and "Maybe" if you are unsure.
3. Take your time to provide thoughtful and detailed feedback in the "What suggestions do you recommend for improving the program?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are filling out the form on behalf of your company, please enter your company's name.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, optional): If the service is related to a specific course, please enter the course title.
* **Telephone** (`telephone`, `number`, required): Please enter your contact telephone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, `text`, optional): If you had a facilitator, please enter their name.
* **Knowledge of Subject Matter** (`knowledge_of_subject_matter`, `text`, optional): If you feel that the facilitator's knowledge of the subject matter is important for your evaluation, please provide details.
* **Suggestions for Improving the Program** (`suggestions_for_improving_the_program`, `text`, optional): Please share your ideas on how we can improve the program.
* **Would you Recommend this Course to your Colleagues?** (`recommend_course_to_colleagues`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you would definitely recommend the course, "False" if you would not, and "Maybe" if you are unsure.
