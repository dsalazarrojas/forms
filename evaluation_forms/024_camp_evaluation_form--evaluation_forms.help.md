# Camp Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your experience at the camp. Your feedback is essential in helping us improve our programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose who you are (Camper, Parent or Guardian, or Staff Member).
2. Fill in the date you attended the session.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the camp experience.
4. Rate your experience with various activities and programs offered by the camp.
5. Choose your favorite activity or highlight from the session.
6. If relevant, rate how well homesickness was managed.
7. Decide if you would recommend the camp to others.
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
9. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Who is completing this evaluation?** (evaluator_role): This field helps us understand who is providing the feedback.
	+ Camper: A participant in the camp program.
	+ Parent or Guardian: A parent or guardian of a camper.
	+ Staff Member: A staff member who works at the camp.
* **Session Attended** (session_date_evaluated): Enter the date you attended the session.
* **Overall Camp Satisfaction** (overall_satisfaction_rating): Rate your overall satisfaction with the camp experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Favorite Activity or Highlight** (favorite_camp_activity): Tell us about your favorite activity or highlight from the session.
* **Homesickness Management** (homesickness_management): Rate how well homesickness was managed, if relevant.
* **Would You Recommend This Camp?** (recommend_to_others): Decide if you would recommend the camp to others.
* **Additional Feedback** (additional_feedback_camp): Provide any comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Email Address** (evaluators_email_optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address.
