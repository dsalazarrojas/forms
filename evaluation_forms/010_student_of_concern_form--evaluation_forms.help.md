# Student Of Concern Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Student Of Concern Form" is a tool used to report and document incidents or concerns regarding students' behavior in the classroom. This form helps educators identify potential issues and provide support to students who may be struggling.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by selecting the "Student Of Concern Form" from the available options.
2.  Begin by answering the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
3.  Once you've completed all the questions, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4.  Submit the form for review and follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Of Concern Form** (`student_of_concern_form`, text, optional): This is the form title, which is used to identify the specific form being completed.
* **Is The Student Concerned** (`student_concerned`, select_one, optional): Choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the student is concerned or not.
* **Classroom Setting** (`classroom_setting`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable settings where the incident occurred, such as "Yes" for "Counseling" or "No" for "Hallway".
* **Behavior Description** (`behavior_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the student's behavior that led to the concern.
* **Teacher Observation** (`teacher_observation`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or observations from the teacher or staff member completing the form.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the student's behavior.
* Use the "Classroom Setting" field to provide context for the incident.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or school administration for guidance.
