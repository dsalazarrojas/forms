# Emergency Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Claim Form is designed to gather information from customers or users regarding emergency situations, making it easier to process and manage claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information on Page 1 and 2 (contact_us and contact_us_page_2).
* Provide detailed information about the incident on Page 3 (incident_info).
* Give information about your insurance coverage on Page 5 (insurance_info).
* Upload any relevant documents that support your claim on Page 7 (upload_documents) and Page 8 (upload_documents_page_2).
* Make sure to fill in all required fields as some pages are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, optional): Please fill in your contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding your claim. This will help us communicate with you efficiently.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, optional): Please provide detailed information about yourself, including name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Incident Info** (`incident_info`, optional): Describe in detail what happened, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant information about the incident.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, optional): Fill in your contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding your claim.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, optional): Repeat the contact information you provided in the previous page.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, optional): Provide information about the contact person responsible for your claim, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, optional): Give information about your insurance coverage, including policy number and provider.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, optional): Upload any relevant documents that support your claim, such as receipts, invoices, or photos. 
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents_page_2`, optional): Repeat the process of uploading documents as in the previous page.
 

Note: The required fields are not specified in the form schema but based on the form name and purpose, we assume that all fields are optional. However, it is recommended to fill in all fields to provide complete information.
