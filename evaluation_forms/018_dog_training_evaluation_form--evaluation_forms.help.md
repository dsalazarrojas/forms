# Dog Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Evaluation Form is designed to collect data on a dog's training level, behavior, and overall progress. It will help dog owners, trainers, or evaluators assess the effectiveness of a training session and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the dog's ID number.
*   Choose the training level of the dog (High or Low).
*   Select the evaluation date and time.
*   Describe the dog's behavior during the training session.
*   Mention the dog's training needs.
*   Choose the training status (Active or Inactive).
*   Add any additional notes about the session.
*   Select whether you were the evaluator of the dog during the training session (Yes or No).
*   Enter the evaluation form's ID number.
*   Enter the name of the evaluation form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Title**: This is the title of the form.
    *   Type: Text, not required.
    *   A brief description to be filled in by the user.
*   **Training Evaluation Form**: This is the main form where the evaluation of the dog's training level and behavior is done. Enter your observations and descriptions of what you saw.
    *   Type: Text, not required.
    *   Enter your observations and descriptions of the dog's training level and behavior.
*   **Dog ID**: Enter the ID number of the dog you are evaluating.
    *   Type: Number, not required.
    *   Enter the dog's ID number.
*   **Training Level**: Choose the training level of the dog (High or Low).
    *   Type: Select multiple, not required.
    *   Choose the correct training level from the options provided.
*   **Evaluation Date**: Select the date of the evaluation.
    *   Type: Date, not required.
    *   Choose the date of the evaluation in the format DD/MM/YY or MM/DD/YY depending on your region.
*   **Evaluation Time**: Select the time of the evaluation.
    *   Type: Time, not required.
    *   Choose the time of the evaluation.
*   **Behavior**: Describe the dog's behavior during the evaluation.
    *   Type: Text, not required.
    *   Enter a brief description of the dog's behavior during the evaluation.
*   **Training Needs**: Describe the dog's training needs.
    *   Type: Text, not required.
    *   Enter a brief description of what the dog needs for improvement.
*   **Training Status**: Choose the status of the dog's training (Active or Inactive).
    *   Type: Select one, not required.
    *   Choose the correct status from the options provided.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the evaluation.
    *   Type: Note, not required.
    *   Enter any additional comments or observations about the session.
*   **Evaluator**: Select whether you were the evaluator of the dog during the training session (Yes or No).
    *   Type: Select multiple, not required.
    *   Choose Yes if you were the evaluator, No otherwise.
*   **Evaluation Form ID**: Enter the ID number of the evaluation form.
    *   Type: Number, not required.
    *   Enter the ID number of the form.
*   **Evaluation Form Name**: Enter the name of the evaluation form.
    *   Type: Text, not required.
    *   Enter the name of the form.
