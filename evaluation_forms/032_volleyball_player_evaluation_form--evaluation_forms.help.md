# Volleyball Player Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a player's skills, effort, and overall performance during a volleyball game or practice. It helps coaches, trainers, and evaluators to provide detailed feedback and ratings for a player's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the player's name** (`Player Name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the player being evaluated.
2. **Select the player's position** (`Playing Position`, select_one, required: false): Choose the player's position on the court (e.g. Setter, Libero, etc.).
3. **Rate the player's overall performance** (`Overall Performance`, number, required: false): Rate the player's overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
4. **Select the player's skills** (`Skills`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all the skills that the player demonstrated during the game or practice, such as Movement, Ball-Handling, etc.
5. **Provide your assessment of the player's effort** (`Effort`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the player's effort and work ethic during the game or practice.
6. **Comment on the player's communication** (`Communication`, text, required: false): Provide feedback on the player's communication with teammates and coaches during the game or practice.
7. **Rate the player's sportsmanship** (`Sportsmanship`, note, required: false): This field is not applicable for this form. Please disregard.
8. **Add any additional comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the player's performance.
9. **Enter any comments from the player** (`Player Comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback from the player themselves.
10. **Provide an overall rating for the player** (`Overall Rating`, number, required: false): Rate the player's overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
