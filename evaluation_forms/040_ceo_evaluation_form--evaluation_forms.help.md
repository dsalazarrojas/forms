# CEO Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CEO Performance Evaluation Form is a tool used by the Board of Directors to assess the Chief Executive Officer's (CEO) performance, focusing on various aspects of their role and responsibilities. The form is designed to provide an objective evaluation, ensuring that the CEO's performance is measured fairly and comprehensively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please fill in the form carefully, section by section, following the instructions and guidelines provided below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. For each section, provide clear and concise comments or ratings as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Board Member Name
* **Board Member Name** (`evaluator_name_board`, text, required): Enter your name as a member of the Board of Directors.

### 2. Evaluation Period (Year)
* **Evaluation Period (Year)** (`evaluation_period_ceo`, number, required): Select the year(s) being evaluated, e.g., "2022" or "2020-2022".

### 3. Strategic Leadership and Vision
* **Strategic Leadership and Vision** (`strategic_leadership_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the CEO's ability to set direction and lead the organization towards goals. Rate on a scale from 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 5 (Exceptional).

### 4. Comments on Strategic Leadership
* **Comments on Strategic Leadership** (`strategic_leadership_comments`, text, optional): Provide additional comments or insights on the CEO's strategic leadership.

### 5. Financial Management and Performance
* **Financial Management and Performance** (`financial_performance_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the CEO's oversight of budget, profitability, and resource allocation. Rate on a scale from 1 (1) to 5 (5).

### 6. Comments on Financial Management
* **Comments on Financial Management** (`financial_mgmt_comments`, text, optional): Add comments or insights on the CEO's financial management.

### 7. Organizational Culture and Team Building
* **Organizational Culture and Team Building** (`organizational_culture_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the CEO's ability to foster a positive environment and develop talent. Rate on a scale from 1 (1) to 5 (5).

### 8. Relationship with the Board of Directors
* **Relationship with the Board of Directors** (`relationship_board_rating`, select_one, required): Assess the CEO's communication, transparency, and collaboration with the Board. Rate on a scale from 1 (1) to 5 (5).

### 9. Innovation and Adaptability
* **Innovation and Adaptability** (`innovation_and_growth_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the CEO's ability to respond to market changes and drive new initiatives. Rate on a scale from 1 (1) to 5 (5).

### 10. List Top 3 CEO Achievements This Period
* **List Top 3 CEO Achievements This Period** (`key_achievements_ceo`, text, required): Enumerate the top three significant achievements or accomplishments of the CEO during the evaluation period.

### 11. Identified Areas for Growth or Improvement
* **Identified Areas for Growth or Improvement** (`areas_for_development_ceo`, text, required): Specify areas where the CEO could improve or grow, if applicable.

### 12. Overall Performance Summary Rating
* **Overall Performance Summary Rating** (`overall_performance_rating_ceo`, select_one, required): Rate the CEO's overall performance as Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Effective, Highly Effective, or Outstanding.

### 13. Recommended Compensation Adjustment (%)
* **Recommended Compensation Adjustment (%)** (`compensation_adjustment_recommendation`, number, optional): Suggest any percentage adjustment to the CEO's compensation, if applicable.

### 14. I Pledge to Keep this Evaluation Confidential
* **I Pledge to Keep this Evaluation Confidential** (`evaluator_confidentiality_pledge`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to maintain the confidentiality of this evaluation.

### 15. Date of Evaluation
* **Date of Evaluation** (`evaluation_submission_date_ceo`, date, required): Enter the date this evaluation was submitted.

## Tips

- When providing ratings, refer to the provided scales for each section.
- Ensure that comments are clear, concise, and specific.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
