# e_learning_program_evaluation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation of the e-learning program from participants. It helps us understand their experiences, perceptions, and opinions about the program's course learning objectives, instructor support, course satisfaction, organization, content, time spent, and overall value.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Fill in the form with your feedback and evaluation of the e-learning program.
3. Make sure to provide detailed answers to all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Feedback** (Participant Feedback, Text, Optional): 
  This field is used to gather any additional comments or feedback from the participant about their experience in the e-learning program.

* **Course Learning Objectives** (Course Learning Objective, Number, Optional): 
  This field asks you to rate how well the course met its learning objectives. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Not Met" and 5 means "Fully Met".

* **Instructor Support** (Instructor Support, Number, Optional): 
  This field asks you to rate how well the instructor supported you during the e-learning program. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means "Poor" and 5 means "Excellent".

* **Course Satisfaction** (Course Satisfaction, Select One, Optional): 
  This field asks you to select how satisfied you are with the course. Please choose one of the following options: "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".

* **Course Organization** (Course Organization, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  This field asks you to select which of the following best describes the organization of the course. Please choose one or more of the following options: "Yes" or "No".

* **Course Content** (Course Content, Date, Optional): 
  This field asks for the date you completed the course. Please enter the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".

* **Time Spent** (Time Spent, Time, Optional): 
  This field asks you to report how much time you spent on the course. Please enter the time spent in hours and minutes, using the format "HH:MM".

* **Course Value** (Course Value, Note, Optional): 
  This field is used to gather any additional comments or feedback about the overall value of the e-learning program to you.
