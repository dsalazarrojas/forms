# Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate and track progress on a project or task. It's designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the assessment process, helping users to identify areas of improvement and areas of success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Self Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to fill out based on your project or task specific needs.
2. Fill out the fields according to your project or task specific needs.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Score (overall_score)**: Enter a number score out of 10 to evaluate your project or task performance.
* **Self Assessment Date (self_assessment_date)**: Enter the date of the self-assessment, using the standard format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Self Assessment Description (self_assessment_description)**: Briefly describe your project or task, including any relevant details or context.
* **Self Assessment Category (self_assessment_category)**: Select all categories that apply to your project or task (e.g., "Yes" for all categories).
* **Self Assessment Subcategory (self_assessment_subcategory)**: Select the specific subcategory that best fits your project or task (e.g., "Yes" for a specific subcategory).
* **Self Assessment Level (self_assessment_level)**: Select the level of your project or task (e.g., "High" for a challenging task).
* **Self Assessment Comment (self_assessment_comment)**: Enter any additional comments about your project or task.
* **Self Assessment Status (self_assessment_status)**: Select the status of your project or task (e.g., "Active" for an ongoing project).
* **Self Assessment Form Type (self_assessment_form_type)**: Select the type of self-assessment form (e.g., "Yes" for a specific form type).
* **Self Assessment Frequency (self_assessment_frequency)**: Select how often you perform self-assessments (e.g., "Yes" for regular self-assessments).
* **Self Assessment Score (self_assessment_score)**: Enter a numerical score out of 10 to evaluate your project or task performance.
* **Self Assessment Comment (self_assessment_comment)**: Enter any additional comments about your project or task.
