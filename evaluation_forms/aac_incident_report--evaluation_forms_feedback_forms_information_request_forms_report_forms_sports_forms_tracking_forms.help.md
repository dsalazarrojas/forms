# AAC Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and report incidents that occur during sports events, including player misconduct, spectator disturbances, coaching violations, safety concerns, equipment failures, weather-related incidents, and medical emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of incident that best describes what happened.
2. Choose the severity level of the incident.
3. Enter the name of the sports event.
4. Select the date and time of the incident.
5. Enter the location of the incident.
6. Enter the name of the person filing the report.
7. Describe the incident in detail.
8. Indicate if witnesses were present.
9. If witnesses were present, enter their names and contact information.
10. Indicate if immediate action was taken after the incident.
11. Suggest any disciplinary actions or consequences.
12. Indicate if follow-up is required.
13. If follow-up is required, enter the date for the next review or hearing.
14. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of the sports event** (id: 2): Enter the official name of the event. This will help identify the specific event and its location.
* **Date of the incident** (id: 3): Enter the date of the incident in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of incident** (id: 4): Enter the approximate time when the incident occurred.
* **Venue/location of incident** (id: 5): Enter the name and address of the stadium, field, or gym where the incident happened.
* **Reporting official name** (id: 6): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Type of incident** (id: 8): Select the primary category of incident (e.g., player misconduct, spectator disturbance).
* **Severity level** (id: 9): Choose the severity level of the incident (e.g., Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
* **Parties involved** (id: 10): Enter the names of players, coaches, or spectators involved.
* **Detailed description of incident** (id: 11): Provide a factual account of what happened.
* **Witnesses present** (id: 12): Indicate if witnesses were present.
* **Witness names** (id: 13): If witnesses were present, enter their names and contact information.
* **Immediate action taken** (id: 15): Describe any immediate action taken after the incident.
* **Disciplinary recommendation** (id: 16): Suggest any disciplinary actions or consequences.
* **Follow-up required** (id: 17): Indicate if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up date** (id: 18): If follow-up is required, enter the date for the next review or hearing.
* **Additional comments** (id: 19): Enter any other relevant information.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by officials who have witnessed or have information about the incident. If you are not sure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or refer to the relevant policies and procedures.
