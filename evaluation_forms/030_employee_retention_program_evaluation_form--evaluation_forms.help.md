# Employee Retention Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from employees on the company's employee retention programs. This form helps identify areas of improvement and provides a clear picture of the effectiveness of the programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the pages, each with its own set of fields.
2. Fill out all relevant fields, ensuring that you have provided sufficient information.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required: false): Please enter your name as an employee.
* **Email** (employee_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or actions).
* **Retention initiatives** (retention_initiatives, select_multiple, required: false): Select up to 8 initiatives from the list provided:
	+ Onboarding process
	+ Recognition and reward
	+ Professional development
	+ Job security
	+ Good communication
	+ Work-life balance
	+ Team environment
	+ Autonomy
* **Employee Retention** (employee_retention, select_one, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with the retention initiatives:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Reasons not retained** (reasons_not_retained, select_multiple, required: false): Select up to 6 reasons why you are not retained:
	+ Lack of challenge
	+ Lack of recognition
	+ Lack of career development
	+ Lack of autonomy
	+ Lack of communication
	+ Lack of work-life balance
* **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or concerns from your manager.
* **Action Plan** (action_plan, text, required: false): Describe a brief action plan to address areas with opportunities for improvement.
* **Retention initiatives rating** (retention_initiatives_rating, select_one, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with the retention initiatives:
	+ 1 (Very dissatisfied)
	+ 2 (Dissatisfied)
	+ 3 (Neutral)
	+ 4 (Satisfied)
	+ 5 (Very satisfied)
* **Onboarding process** (retention_initiatives_1, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with the onboarding process.
* **Recognition and reward** (retention_initiatives_2, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with recognition and reward.
* **Professional development** (retention_initiatives_3, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with professional development.
* **Job security** (retention_initiatives_4, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with job security.
* **Good communication** (retention_initiatives_5, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with good communication.
* **Work-life balance** (retention_initiatives_6, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with work-life balance.
* **Team environment** (retention_initiatives_7, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with team environment.
* **Autonomy** (retention_initiatives_8, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with autonomy.
* **Diversity and inclusion** (retention_initiatives_9, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with diversity and inclusion.
* **Other** (retention_initiatives_10, number, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with other initiatives.
* **Form Submission** (form_submit, number, required: false): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the form.
Note: Please ensure that you have filled out all relevant fields, and if any field is left blank or marked "N/A", it may lead to an incomplete evaluation.
