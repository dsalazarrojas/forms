# First Day Of School Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students on their first day of school to help educators understand student demographics and previous school experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any family background information you'd like to share, type it in the "Family Background" field.
4. If you have any school interests, type them in the "School Interest" field.
5. If you have any notes or comments you'd like to share with your teacher, type them in the "Teacher Notes" field.
6. If you have attended a school previously, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Previous School" field.
7. If you answered "Yes", type in your previous school address in the "Previous School Address" field.
8. If you answered "Yes", type in your previous school city in the "Previous School City" field.
9. If you answered "Yes", select your grade level at your previous school from the "Previous School Grade Level" dropdown menu.
10. If you answered "Yes", type in your previous school phone number in the "Previous School Phone" field.
11. If you answered "Yes", type in your previous school email address in the "Previous School Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name)**: Your name as the student.
* **Grade Level (Grade Level)**: Your current grade level in school.
* **Family Background (Family Background)**: Any relevant family background information you'd like to share with your teacher.
* **School Interest (School Interest)**: Any school interests or activities you are excited about or would like to join.
* **Teacher Notes (Teacher Notes)**: Any notes or comments you'd like to share with your teacher.
* **Previous School (Previous School)**: If you have attended a school previously, select "Yes".
* **Previous School Address (Previous School Address)**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, type in your previous school address.
* **Previous School City (Previous School City)**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, type in your previous school city.
* **Previous School Grade Level (Previous School Grade Level)**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, select your grade level at your previous school.
* **Previous School Phone (Previous School Phone)**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, type in your previous school phone number.
* **Previous School Email (Previous School Email)**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, type in your previous school email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask your teacher for clarification.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
