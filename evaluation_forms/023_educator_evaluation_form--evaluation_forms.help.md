# Educator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on educator performance and provide an overall rating. It's divided into several sections, each evaluating a specific aspect of the educator's skills and traits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter your feedback for each of the sections. For select fields, choose one or multiple options as instructed. For number fields, enter a numerical value between 0 and 100.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Performance** (Select one or multiple: Yes / No): This field assesses the teacher's teaching effectiveness and ability to engage students.
* **Classroom Management** (Number): This field measures the teacher's ability to manage the classroom environment and maintain order.
* **Subject Knowledge** (Select one: Yes / No): This field evaluates the teacher's knowledge and confidence in the subject matter.
* **Communication Skills** (Select one or multiple: Yes / No): This field assesses the teacher's ability to communicate effectively with students and colleagues.
* **Time Management** (Select one: Yes / No): This field measures the teacher's time management skills and ability to stay organized.
* **Student Engagement** (Number): This field measures the level of student engagement during lessons and activities.
* **Feedback Frequency** (Select one or multiple: Yes / No): This field assesses the frequency and quality of feedback the teacher provides to students.
* **Educator Availability** (Select one: Yes / No): This field evaluates the teacher's availability to support students outside of class time.
* **Classroom Environment** (Select one: Yes / No): This field assesses the cleanliness, organization, and safety of the classroom.
* **Overall Rating** (Number): Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to provide an overall rating of the educator's performance.
