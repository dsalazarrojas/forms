# Automated Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used for assessments of employees' performance, skills, and behavior. It is used to evaluate an individual's work quality, productivity, communication skills, teamwork, leadership abilities, and other core competencies. The form is intended to provide an overall rating and recommendations for development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill out the form completely and honestly**. This will help to ensure that your assessment is thorough and accurate.
2. **Read each question carefully** before providing your answers. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
3. **Use the provided hints for guidance**. They are there to help you complete the form correctly.
4. **Save your progress** if you need to exit and come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Assessee Information (2)
* **Last Name (4)**: Enter the last name of the person being assessed.
* **First Name (3)**: Enter the first name of the person being assessed.
* **Employee ID (5)**: If applicable, enter the employee ID of the person being assessed.
* **Email Address (6)**: Enter the contact email of the person being assessed.
* **Department (7)**: Enter the department or division of the person being assessed.
* **Position or Role (8)**: Enter the job title of the person being assessed.
### Assessment Information (9)
* **Type of Assessment (10)**: Select the type of assessment that this form is for (e.g., Performance Review, Skills Assessment, etc.)
* **Assessment Date (11)**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Period (12)**: Select the time period covered by this assessment (e.g., Last 3 months, Last 6 months, etc.)
* **Who is conducting the assessment (13)**: This is a free text field for the assessor to indicate who is conducting the assessment.
* **Assessment Details (9)**: This is a free text field for the assessor to provide additional information.
