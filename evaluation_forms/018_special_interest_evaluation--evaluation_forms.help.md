# Special Interest Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate their special interests by selecting their primary interests, engagement level, and motivations. The form also collects demographic information such as submitted date and time, IP address, and country.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your primary interests from the list.
2. Choose your engagement level (High, Medium, or Low).
3. Select your motivations (Personal Growth, Financial Gain, Social Recognition, Helping Others, or Other).
4. Enter any additional comments you may have.
5. Select who submitted this form (User or Admin).
6. Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
7. Enter your IP address.
8. Enter your country of origin.
9. Choose how you completed this form (Partial or Complete).
10. Enter the date and time you submitted this form again.
11. Enter your form's IP address.
12. Enter your form's country of origin.
13. Select the assigned tool (ChatJIMMY or Another Tool).
14. Select the version of the assigned tool you used.
15. Choose whether the assigned tool was web-based or mobile-based.
16. Enter the URL of the assigned tool.
17. Enter the username and password of the assigned tool (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are your primary interests**: Select your top interests from the list provided. This field helps us understand your core passions and hobbies.
* **What is your engagement level**: Choose how engaged you are in the activity or interest you selected. High, Medium, or Low.
* **What motivates you**: Select all that apply to your motivations. This field helps us understand what drives you to pursue your interests.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about your interests.
* **Submitted by**: Select who submitted this form (User or Admin).
* **Submitted date and time**: Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted IP address**: Enter the IP address you used to submit this form.
* **Submitted country**: Enter the country where you submitted this form.
* **Form completion**: Choose how you completed this form (Partial or Complete).
* **Form submission date and time**: Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Form IP address**: Enter the IP address of your form.
* **Form country**: Enter the country of origin of your form.
* **Assigned tool**: Select the tool you used to complete this form (ChatJIMMY or Another Tool).
* **Assigned tool version**: Select the version of the assigned tool you used.
* **Assigned tool type**: Choose whether the assigned tool was web-based or mobile-based.
* **Assigned tool URL**: Enter the URL of the assigned tool.
* **Assigned tool username**: Enter the username of the assigned tool (not required).
* **Assigned tool password**: Enter the password of the assigned tool (not required).
* **Assigned tool license**: Enter the license of the assigned tool (not required).
* **Assigned form ID**: Enter the ID of the assigned form (not required).
* **Assigned form name**: Enter the name of the assigned form (not required).
