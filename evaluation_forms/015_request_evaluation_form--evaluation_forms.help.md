# request_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The request_evaluation_form is used to collect user information and feedback for internal purposes. Fill out this form when you need to request an evaluation from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your company or organization in the "Company" field.
6. Specify your requirements in the "Requirements" field.
7. Add any additional comments or information about your evaluation needs in the "Comments" field.
8. Select the assigned tool from the list provided in the "Assigned Tool" field. You can choose from "chatjimmy" or "Other" if the tool is not listed.
9. Once you have filled out all the required information, click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
- **Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Company** (Text, Required/Optional): Briefly describe your company or organization.
- **Requirements** (Text, Required/Optional): Specify your evaluation needs.
- **Comments** (Text, Required/Optional): Add any additional comments or information about your evaluation needs.
- **Assigned Tool** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the tool you are requesting to be assigned to.
  - **chatjimmy** (Select One): Select this option if you want to be assigned chatjimmy.
  - **Other** (Select One): Select this option if the assigned tool is not chatjimmy.
- **Submit** (Text, Required/Optional): Click this button to send your evaluation request.
