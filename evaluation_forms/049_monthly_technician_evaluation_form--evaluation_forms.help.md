# Monthly Technician Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of technicians on a monthly basis. It helps managers and supervisors provide feedback and identify areas for improvement for their team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the evaluatee (technician being evaluated) from the dropdown menu in the "Evaluatee" field.
2. Enter the date of the evaluation period in the "Date" field.
3. Sign and date the "Manager Signature" field if you are the manager completing the evaluation.
4. Sign and date the "Supervisor Signature" field if you are the supervisor completing the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluatee (month_technician_evaluatee)** (`Select`, `required: false`): Choose the technician being evaluated from the dropdown menu. This field is optional, but selecting a value will allow the evaluation to be completed more efficiently.
* **Date (evaluation_date)** (`Date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the evaluation period. This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide a specific date to reference the evaluation period.
* **Manager Signature (manager_signature)** (`Note`, `required: false`): Sign and date this field to confirm that you are the manager completing the evaluation.
* **Supervisor Signature (supervisor_signature)** (`Note`, `required: false`): Sign and date this field to confirm that you are the supervisor completing the evaluation.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide specific feedback on the technician's performance during the evaluation period.
* Be thorough and concise in your evaluation, covering all relevant areas of performance.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
