# Student Academic Achievement Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a student's academic performance, helping educators to evaluate and provide feedback to the student. It covers various aspects of the student's achievement, including grade level, subject areas, and evaluation date and time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Select the grade level of the student from the dropdown list.
3. Choose one or more subject areas where the student excels or requires improvement.
4. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
5. Provide any additional comments about the student's performance.
6. If relevant, specify any tools or resources assigned to the student during the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name as it is written in their records.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address for communication and record-keeping.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian, if applicable.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`): Select the grade level of the student from the list (1 to 10).
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`): Choose one or more subject areas where the student has demonstrated strength or needs improvement (Mathematics, Science, History, Language Arts, or Physical Education).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date of the evaluation, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`): Enter the time of the evaluation, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the student's performance.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): If relevant, specify the tools or resources assigned to the student during the evaluation.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries.
* Choose only relevant subject areas for the student's evaluation.
* Use the comments field to provide specific and actionable feedback.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for accurate and thorough evaluation.
