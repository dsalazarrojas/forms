# Menu Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Feedback Form is designed to gather information from customers about their experience with your menu and service. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our menu and service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions in a clear and honest manner. You will see a series of questions about your experience with the menu and service. Please select the best answer that reflects your opinion. You can submit your feedback by clicking the submit button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Feedback** (`menu_feedback_form`, text, Optional): 
Please provide any comments or feedback about the menu.
* **How would you rate the overall experience?** (`question1`, number, Optional): 
Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **What type of cuisine did you dine?** (`question2`, select_one, Optional): 
Please select the type of cuisine you ordered.
* **How would you rate the value for money?** (`question3`, number, Optional): 
Rate how good a value you got for the money you spent, on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness?** (`question4`, number, Optional): 
Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant, on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate the service?** (`question5`, number, Optional): 
Rate the service experience, on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate the food quality?** (`question6`, number, Optional): 
Rate the quality of the food, on a scale of 1-10.
* **How would you rate the value for money?** (`question7`, number, Optional): 
Rate how good a value you got for the money you spent, again, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Would you recommend this restaurant to a friend?** (`question8`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select yes or no, whether you would recommend the restaurant to a friend.
* **Would you like to make a comment?** (`question9`, note, Optional): 
If you have any additional comments, please type them here.
* **How would you rate the price of the dishes?** (`question10`, number, Optional): 
Rate the price of the dishes, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Was the food too spicy?** (`question11`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select yes or no, whether the food was too spicy.
* **Did you experience any issues during your visit?** (`question12`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select yes or no, if you experienced any issues during your visit.
* **How was the staff's attitude?** (`question13`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Rate the staff's attitude, on a scale of 1-10.
* **What was the reason for your visit?** (`question14`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select one or more reasons for your visit.
* **How did you find out about our restaurant?** (`question15`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select how you found out about the restaurant.
* **How often do you visit restaurants similar to this?** (`question16`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Rate how often you visit similar restaurants.
* **Do you have a preferred contact method?** (`question17`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select your preferred contact method.
* **What is your age?** (`question18`, number, Optional): 
Please select your age.
* **What is your occupation?** (`question19`, text, Optional): 
Please type your occupation.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions?** (`question20`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select yes or no, whether you have any dietary restrictions.
* **Do you want to be contacted?** (`question21`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select yes or no, whether you want to be contacted.
* **How long will you wait until calling?** (`question22`, time, Optional): 
Type the time you will wait until calling.
* **What time do you plan on calling?** (`question23`, time, Optional): 
Type the time you plan on calling.
* **Do you plan on coming back?** (`question24`, select_multiple, Optional): 
Select yes or no, whether you plan on coming back.
