# Building Service Vendor Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the services provided by a vendor, capturing feedback on their quality, performance, and overall value.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Vendor Information**: Provide a brief description of the vendor being evaluated.
2. **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the service vendor.
3. **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided by the vendor (e.g., Cleaning Services, Security Services, etc.).
4. **Evaluation Period**: Enter the time period during which you evaluated the vendor's services.
5. **Evaluator Information**: Provide your details (e.g., name, department, role).
6. **Evaluator Name**: Enter your full name.
7. **Evaluator Role**: Enter your position or title.
8. **Department**: Select the department you belong to (e.g., Facilities, Administration, etc.).
9. **Quality Ratings**: Rate the vendor's performance on various aspects, such as service quality, timeliness, professionalism, etc. (see below for explanations).
10. **Service Quality**: Rate the overall quality of services rendered.
11. **Timeliness**: Rate how punctual and timely the vendor is.
12. **Professionalism**: Rate the staff behavior and professionalism of the vendor.
13. **Reliability**: Rate the consistency of service delivery.
14. **Value for Money**: Rate the quality relative to cost.
15. **Communication**: Rate how well the vendor communicates.
16. **Detailed Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
17. **Vendor Strengths**: Describe what the vendor does well.
18. **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas that could be improved.
19. **Would You Recommend This Vendor**: Indicate whether you would recommend the vendor to others.
20. **Contract Renewal Recommendation**: Suggest whether the contract should be renewed.
21. **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name**: The name of the service vendor.
* **Service Type**: The type of service provided by the vendor (e.g., Cleaning Services, Security Services, etc.).
* **Evaluator Name**: Your full name.
* **Evaluator Role**: Your position or title.
* **Department**: The department you belong to (e.g., Facilities, Administration, etc.).
* **Service Quality**: Rate the overall quality of services rendered.
* **Timeliness**: Rate how punctual and timely the vendor is.
* **Professionalism**: Rate the staff behavior and professionalism of the vendor.
* **Reliability**: Rate the consistency of service delivery.
* **Value for Money**: Rate the quality relative to cost.
* **Communication**: Rate how well the vendor communicates.
* **Would You Recommend This Vendor**: Indicate whether you would recommend the vendor to others.
* **Contract Renewal Recommendation**: Suggest whether the contract should be renewed.

Note: Ratings for quality and performance questions (6-15) are scored on a scale from 1 (Excellent/Always On Time) to 5 (Poor/Consistently Late).
