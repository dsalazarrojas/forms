# Outcome Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outcome Assessment Form is used to evaluate the outcome and effectiveness of a program, intervention, or project. It collects information on the impact, effectiveness, and time frame of an event or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure to answer accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter any relevant information that is not captured in other fields.

### Outcome Area
* **Outcome Area** (`outcome_area`, select_one, optional): Select one option that describes the area of outcome being assessed. Choose from:
	+ Program Effectiveness
	+ Program Impact
	+ Program Outcomes

### Effectiveness Scale
* **Effectiveness Scale** (`effectiveness_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that describe the level of effectiveness of the outcome. Choose from:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

### Effectiveness Score
* **Effectiveness Score** (`effectiveness_score`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score for the effectiveness of the outcome.

### Outcomes
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the outcomes of the program or intervention.

### Impact Score
* **Impact Score** (`impact_score`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score for the impact of the outcome.

### Time Frame
* **Time Frame** (`time_frame`, select_one, optional): Select the time frame in which the outcome was assessed. Choose from:
	+ Long
	+ Medium
	+ Short

### Evaluation Period
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, date, optional): Enter the period during which the outcome was evaluated.

### Evaluation Date
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the outcome was evaluated.

### Evaluation Time
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the outcome was evaluated.
