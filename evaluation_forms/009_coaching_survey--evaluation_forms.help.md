# Coaching Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a coach's performance, strengths, and areas for development. It is a comprehensive survey that will help to identify the coach's areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for their development.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Read each question carefully**: Each question is designed to gather specific feedback on the coach's performance and areas of improvement.
- **Choose the correct rating**: Select the rating that best corresponds to your opinion on the coach's performance in each category.
- **Provide specific feedback**: In addition to rating, provide specific feedback and examples to support your answer.
- **Be honest**: Provide your genuine opinion, and avoid giving overly positive or negative responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sports club or organization being evaluated.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name and position or title within the club.
* **Your Role in the Club** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Select your role within the club (Club Manager, Committee Member, Administrator, Coach, Member, Other).
* **Coach Being Evaluated** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach being evaluated.
* **How long has this coach worked with your club** (`coaching_tenure`, select_one, required): Select the approximate duration of the coach's work with your club (Less than 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, More than 5 years).
* **Coach's Technical Knowledge and Expertise** (`technical_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's technical knowledge and expertise from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Effectiveness in Developing Athlete Skills** (`athlete_development`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's effectiveness in developing athlete skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Ability to Motivate and Inspire Athletes** (`athlete_motivation`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's ability to motivate and inspire athletes from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Quality of Training Planning and Organization** (`training_planning`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's quality of training planning and organization from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Communication Skills with Athletes and Staff** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's communication skills with athletes and staff from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Adherence to Safety Standards and Protocols** (`safety_standards`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's adherence to safety standards and protocols from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Measurable Improvements in Athlete Performance** (`athlete_results`, select_one, required): Rate the measurable improvements in athlete performance from 1 (No improvement) to 5 (Significant improvement).
* **Overall Team Performance Improvement** (`team_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the overall team performance improvement from 1 (Declined) to 5 (Significant improvement).
* **Athlete Satisfaction with Coaching** (`athlete_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete satisfaction with coaching from 1 (Very low) to 5 (Very high).
* **Overall Rating of Coach** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall coach performance from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Coach's Greatest Strengths** (`coach_strengths`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the coach's greatest strengths.
* **Areas for Coach Development** (`coach_development`, text, optional): Provide feedback on areas where the coach needs to improve.
* **Would You Continue Employing This Coach** (`continue_employment`, select_one, required): Select whether you would continue employing this coach or not.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or comments on the coach's performance.
