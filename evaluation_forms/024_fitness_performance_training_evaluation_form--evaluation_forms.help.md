# fitness_performance_training_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the fitness performance and training effectiveness for individuals. It gathers information about physical performance, cardiovascular performance, muscular endurance, and overall feeling after exercise. The form can be completed by individuals to assess their current fitness level and track changes over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by reviewing the form's introduction page.
2. Answer each question according to your current fitness level and training experience.
3. Select the options that best describe your current state.
4. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
5. Submit the form to generate the evaluation report.
