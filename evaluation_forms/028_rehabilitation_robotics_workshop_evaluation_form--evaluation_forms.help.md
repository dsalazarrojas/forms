# Rehabilitation Robotics Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Robotics Workshop Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience, opinions, and suggestions for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete this form by providing honest and constructive comments on your experience in the workshop.
2. You can start by providing your name (optional) and then proceed to the next sections.
3. The form is divided into two sections: "Event Details" and "Evaluation".
4. In "Event Details", you can provide a brief description of your experience.
5. In "Evaluation", you will be asked to rate your experience on a scale of "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
6. In the "Comments" field, you can provide more detailed comments on your experience.
7. Finally, please provide the date you attended the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field is optional. If you wish to be recognized for your feedback, please provide your name.
* **Event Details**: This field is a text area where you can provide a brief description of your experience in the workshop.
	+ Please keep it concise and to the point.
* **Evaluation Scale**:
	+ "Excellent": You had an outstanding experience in the workshop.
	+ "Good": You had a satisfactory experience in the workshop.
	+ "Fair": You had a neutral experience in the workshop.
	+ "Poor": You had a disappointing experience in the workshop.
* **Comments**: This field is optional. If you wish to provide more detailed comments on your experience, please use this field.
* **Date**: Please provide the date you attended the workshop.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective in your responses.
* Keep your answers concise and to the point.
* If you have any suggestions for future improvements, please include them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
