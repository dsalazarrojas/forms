# Lecture Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your experience of a recent lecture. This will give us an idea of the quality of our courses so we can improve for you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form once per lecture.
- Answer all the fields, even if you are unsure or don't know an answer.
- Be as specific as you can when answering.
- Don't worry about the order of the questions, just answer them in the order they appear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How engaging was the lecture?** (`lecture_content`, select_one, optional)
	+ This question is meant to understand how enjoyable and captivating you found the lecture. Answer either "Yes" or "No" if you feel the lecture was or wasn't engaging. 
* **How would you rate the speaker?** (`speaker`, number, optional)
	+ Please rate the speaker based on their performance. A 0 would mean not good and 10 would mean excellent. 
* **How well was the presentation organized?** (`presentation_materials`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Answer "Yes" for each part of the presentation you felt well-organized. For example, you can answer "Yes" for "Yes" to both "Yes" and "Yes" or "No" if you didn't like the presentation.
* **How relevant was the content?** (`content`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose "Yes" if you found the content of the lecture relevant to you. Choose "No" if you felt it wasn't relevant.
* **How well was the time management?** (`time_management`, select_one, optional)
	+ Answer "Yes" if you felt the time was well-managed. Answer "No" if you felt it wasn't well-managed.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose "Satisfied" if you were satisfied with the lecture. Choose "Dissatisfied" if you weren't.
* **Additional Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional)
	+ If you have any extra comments or suggestions, please add them here. 
* **How long did the lecture feel too short?** (`lecture_length`, number, optional)
	+ If the lecture felt too short, enter how many minutes longer you think it should have been.
