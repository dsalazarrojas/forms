# momentous_affairs_sop_draft_for_cloning - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the purpose of evaluating momentous affairs. This form is a part of the standard operating procedure (SOP) for cloning and drafting momentous affairs. It is crucial to ensure that all required information is provided accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "user_input" field with the relevant information.
2. Enter the date of the momentous affair in the "date" field.
3. Enter the time of the momentous affair in the "time" field.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "phone" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of the momentous affairs in the "momentous_affairs_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional relevant information about the momentous affair.
* **Date**: (`date`, date, optional): This field is for you to enter the date of the momentous affair.
* **Time**: (`time`, time, optional): This field is for you to enter the time of the momentous affair.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional): This field is for you to enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Momentous Affairs Info**: (`momentous_affairs_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a detailed description of the momentous affairs.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately to ensure the form is complete and can be processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the designated contact person for clarification.
* Please note that the information provided in this form will be used for the purpose of evaluating momentous affairs and may be shared with relevant parties.
