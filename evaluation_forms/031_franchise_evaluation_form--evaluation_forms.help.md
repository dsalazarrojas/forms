# Franchise Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Evaluation Form is used to collect information from potential franchisees to gauge the feasibility of a franchise opportunity. This form helps our team evaluate your business and assist you in making informed decisions about your franchise opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Select your franchise type from the options provided.
4. Provide your investment amount, business hours, and territory details.
5. Describe your business hours and experience.
6. Indicate your franchise ownership status and type.
7. Confirm your business hours worked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Evaluation Form (\*)**: This is the title of the form and is not editable.
* **Franchise Company Name (\*)**: Please enter the name of your company as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Franchise Location (\*)**: If applicable, please enter the address of your business location.
* **Contact Name (\*)**: This is the name of the person filling out the form, typically the owner or representative of the company.
* **Email (\*)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number (\*)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Franchise Type**: Select the type of franchise you are interested in (Franchise, Non-Franchise, Partnership, or Joint Venture).
* **Investment Amount**: Enter the estimated investment amount for your franchise opportunity.
* **Business Hours**: Enter the number of hours per week your business operates.
* **Territory**: If applicable, enter the area or region your business serves.
* **Franchise Agreement**: Please paste the URL of your existing franchise agreement or describe any agreements you have in place.
* **Franchise Agreement Length**: Enter the length of your existing franchise agreement, or the duration you're expecting.
* **Training and Support**: Describe the training and support you provide to your franchisees.
* **Franchise Ownership**: Indicate whether you own your business entirely, partially, or not at all.
* **Franchise Ownership Type**: Enter the type of ownership you have in your business (e.g., sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, etc.).
* **Business Experience**: Describe your experience in the business, including relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Business Hours Worked**: Enter the average number of hours worked per week.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and comprehensively, and avoid submitting duplicate or irrelevant information. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please feel free to contact us.
