# Preventive Care Outcome Measurement Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the outcomes of preventive care services. It helps care providers to assess how their clients or patients are doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Info section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the appropriate Care Plan from the list provided.
3. Select one or more outcomes that you are satisfied with regarding the care you received.
4. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your experience with the care provider.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience with the care provider in the User Notes field.
8. Finally, review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: This is the personal information section where you can enter your name and other details relevant to your evaluation.
* **Care Plan**: Choose the type of care plan that you were under. This will help the care provider understand which type of care you received.
* **Outcomes**: Select one or more outcomes that you are satisfied with. These are the results or effects of the care you received.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. This helps the care provider to track when you were evaluated.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation. This helps the care provider to track when you were evaluated.
* **Notes**: Add any comments or notes about your experience with the care provider.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the care provider can contact you for any further information.
* **User Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience with the care provider.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to choose the correct care plan and outcomes for your evaluation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the care provider.
