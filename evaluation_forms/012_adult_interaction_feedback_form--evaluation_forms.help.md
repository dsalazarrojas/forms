# Adult Interaction Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Interaction Feedback Form is designed to gather insights on the quality of interactions between participants and facilitators/staff members in adult programs. This form helps to ensure that interactions are effective and satisfactory for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide Session Information (if known)
	* This section is used to collect information about the session you're providing feedback on.
2. Enter Participant Name (optional)
	* If you're comfortable sharing your name, enter your participant name here.
	* If not, please leave this field empty.
3. Specify Session Date
	* Enter the date of the interaction you're providing feedback on.
4. Identify the Facilitator or Staff
	* Enter the name of the person who interacted with you.
5. Rate Quality of Interaction
	* Select one of the options to rate the overall quality of your interaction.
6. Rate Communication Clarity
	* Choose the option that best describes how clear and helpful the information was.
7. Provide Suggestions for Improvement
	* If you have any suggestions on how to improve interactions, enter them here.
8. Decide if You'd Recommend the Service
	* Indicate whether or not you would recommend this service.
9. Add Additional Comments
	* Any additional feedback or comments can be provided here.
