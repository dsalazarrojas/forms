# Pediatric Symptom Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Symptom Checklist form is used to collect medical history information about a child's health. This form helps healthcare professionals to assess the child's symptoms and provide necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question in the space provided, providing as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): This field is a general section where you can describe your child's medical history, including any past medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Medical History Questions** (`medical_history_questions`, `text`, required): In this field, answer the following questions:
	+ Has your child ever been diagnosed with a medical condition?
	+ Has your child ever been hospitalized?
	+ Has your child ever undergone surgery?
* **Medical History Questions 2** (`medical_history_questions_2`, `text`, optional): In this field, answer the following questions:
	+ Has your child ever experienced any chronic or recurring pain?
	+ Has your child ever experienced any allergic reactions or sensitivities?
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, optional): This field is a general section where you can describe any family medical history, including any conditions that affect your family members.
* **Family History Questions** (`family_history_questions`, `text`, required): In this field, answer the following questions:
	+ Do any family members have a history of heart disease?
	+ Do any family members have diabetes?
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, optional): This field is a continuation of the medical history section, where you can describe any additional medical conditions or experiences your child has had.
* **Family History 2** (`family_history_2`, `text`, optional): This field is a continuation of the family history section, where you can describe any additional family medical history or experiences.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, `text`, required): In this field, answer the following questions:
	+ Has your child ever had any previous medical conditions that have affected their mental health?
	+ Has your child ever experienced any developmental delays or disabilities?
