# MRR Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MRR Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) of a specific month in a year. It helps gather information about the current revenue and growth of a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Select the Month and Year
Select the month and year you want to evaluate from the respective dropdown menus.

### Step 2: Enter the Revenue and Growth Details
Enter the actual revenue, growth rate, and target MRR for the selected month and year.

### Step 3: Enter Additional Information
Enter any additional comments, notes, or explanations for the MRR evaluation.

### Step 4: Identify the Growth Driver
Select the main growth driver for the selected month, which can be Increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), Increase in User Growth, or other (with a brief explanation).

### Step 5: Plan Next Steps
List the next steps and key initiatives to achieve the MRR target.

### Step 6: Assign and Review
Select the person assigned to review and manage the MRR evaluation, and select the person who reviewed and approved the MRR evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Monthly Revenues**: The page where you select the month and year you want to evaluate.
- **Month**: Select the month you want to evaluate.
- **Year**: Select the year for which you want to evaluate the MRR.
- **Revenue**: Enter the actual revenue for the selected month and year.
- **Growth**: Enter the growth rate for the selected month and year.
- **Growth Rate**: Enter the growth rate for the selected month and year.
- **MRR**: Enter the target MRR for the selected month and year.
- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the MRR.
- **Email**: If required, enter your email address.
- **Phone**: If required, enter your phone number.
- **Growth Driver**: Select the main growth driver for the selected month, which can be Increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), Increase in User Growth, or other (with a brief explanation).
- **Next Steps**: List the next steps and key initiatives to achieve the MRR target.
- **Assigned To**: Select the person assigned to review and manage the MRR evaluation.
- **Reviewed By**: Select the person who reviewed and approved the MRR evaluation.
