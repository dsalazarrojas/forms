# Drone Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the risk associated with drones in various settings, such as construction sites, industrial areas, or public events. It's designed to help reviewers evaluate the risk of drones and provide recommendations for mitigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Evaluate the drone's presence in the area and determine if it poses a risk.
2. Select the appropriate risk level (1-5) based on your assessment.
3. Identify any mitigation actions that can be taken to reduce the risk.
4. Provide a risk summary and recommendation.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Type** (Drone Type, select_one, required): Indicate whether a drone is present in the area ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Risk Assessment** (Risk Assessment, select_multiple, required): Select the risks associated with the drone (e.g., 'Yes' for each risk).
* **Risk Level** (Risk Level, number, required): Enter a risk level from 1-5, with 1 being low risk and 5 being high risk.
* **Risk Level Details** (Risk Level Details, text, optional): Provide additional details about the risk level.
* **Mitigation Actions** (Mitigation Actions, select_multiple, optional): Identify any actions that can be taken to reduce the risk (e.g., 'Yes' for each action).
* **Next Steps** (Next Steps, text, optional): Describe the next steps to take to address the risk.
* **First Page Footer** (First Page Footer, note, optional): Leave any notes or comments about the risk assessment.
* **Second Page** (Second Page, number, optional): Enter the number of the next page if more information is required.
* **Risk Summary** (Risk Summary, select_one, required): Indicate whether the risk is high or low.
* **Risk Recommendation** (Risk Recommendation, select_one, required): Select whether a risk recommendation is needed.
* **Final Risk Score** (Final Risk Score, number, required): Enter a final risk score from 1-5.
* **Final Risk Summary** (Final Risk Summary, text, optional): Provide a final risk summary.
* **Final Risk Recommendation** (Final Risk Recommendation, text, optional): Provide a final risk recommendation.
* **Second Page Footer** (Second Page Footer, note, optional): Leave any notes or comments about the risk assessment.
* **Third Page** (Third Page, number, optional): Enter the number of the next page if more information is required.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your review.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the review.
* **Reviewer Signature** (Reviewer Signature, text, required): Sign the form to confirm your review.
* **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, required): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (Reviewer Title, text, required): Enter your title as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Organization** (Reviewer Organization, text, required): Enter the organization you represent.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Reviewer Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
