# special_education_program_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The special education program evaluation form is used to provide input on the performance of a special education program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Name field with the name of the student who participated in the program.
2. Enter the name of the school where the student attended the program in the School Name field.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Program Type field to indicate whether the student participated in the program.
4. Select multiple options for the Student Progress field to indicate the student's progress in the program. You can select one or more of the following options: 'Yes', 'No'.
5. In the Program Feedback field, enter any comments or feedback about the program.
6. Enter the date the program ended or completed in the Date field.
7. Enter the amount of time the student spent in the program in the Time field.
8. In the Note field, enter any additional notes or comments about the program.
9. Enter the student's email in the Email field.
10. Enter the student's phone number in the Phone field.
11. In the Program Evaluation field, enter your overall evaluation of the program.
12. Select one of the options for the Overall Satisfaction field to indicate your overall satisfaction with the program. You can select 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student who participated in the program.
* **School Name** (School Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school where the student attended the program.
* **Program Type** (Program Type, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the student participated in the program.
* **Student Progress** (Student Progress, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the following options to indicate the student's progress in the program: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Program Feedback** (Program Feedback, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the program.
* **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date the program ended or completed.
* **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): Enter the amount of time the student spent in the program.
* **Note** (Note, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the program.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Program Evaluation** (Program Evaluation, text, required/optional): Enter your overall evaluation of the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied' to indicate your overall satisfaction with the program.

## Tips

* Make sure to select only the options that are relevant to the student's progress in the program.
* Enter any relevant comments or feedback in the Program Feedback field.
* Enter the date and time accurately for the Date and Time fields.
