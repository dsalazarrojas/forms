# Color Street Product Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on Color Street products, helping us understand how well our products meet your expectations. Your input will be used to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about product development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Respondent Name (Optional)**: If you'd like, please provide your name so we can contact you if needed.
2. **Purchase Date**: Enter the date you made your purchase.
3. **Product Purchased**: Enter the name or SKU of the product you purchased.
4. **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with our product, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
5. **Likelihood to Recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend our product, from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
6. **Favorite Feature**: If you'd like to, describe a specific feature that you enjoyed about our product.
7. **Improvement Suggestions**: Share any ideas you have on how we could improve our product.
8. **Would Purchase Again**: Answer whether you would buy our product again.
9. **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name**: Your name for our records, optional.
* **Purchase Date**: The date you made your purchase, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Product Purchased**: The name or SKU of the product you purchased.
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with our product, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Satisfaction Rating**: This is a subjective rating of how well our product met your expectations.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend our product, from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Favorite Feature**: Describe a specific feature that you enjoyed about our product.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Share any ideas you have on how we could improve our product.
* **Would Purchase Again**: Answer whether you would buy our product again.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

Note: All fields except "Respondent Name" and "Would Purchase Again" are optional, but required for submission. 

## Tips

* Please take a moment to fill out all required fields, they are labeled as such.
* For fields with multiple options (e.g., select_one), select a number or option that best describes your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
