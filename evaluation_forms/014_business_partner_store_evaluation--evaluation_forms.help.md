# Business Partner Store Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the quality of a business partner store, covering various aspects such as store appearance, customer service, product availability, and compliance with brand standards. The evaluation is conducted by an evaluator, who assesses the store's strengths and weaknesses, providing feedback that can help improve the store's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Section 1: Evaluation Information**
   * Fill in your name and the date of the store visit.
   * Enter the name of the store being evaluated.
   * Provide the store's location.

2. **Section 2: Store Appearance and Cleanliness**
   * Rate the overall cleanliness and organization of the store.
   * Evaluate the quality of product display and merchandising.
   * Assess the quality of store signage.

3. **Section 3: Customer Service**
   * Rate the professionalism and courtesy of store staff.
   * Evaluate the staff's product knowledge.
   * Assess the wait times for customers.
   
4. **Section 4: Inventory and Stocking**
   * Rate the availability of products.
   * Evaluate the management of stock rotation and expiration dates.

5. **Section 5: Compliance and Standards**
   * Rate the store's adherence to brand standards.
   * Evaluate the accuracy of pricing.

6. **Section 6: Additional Notes**
   * Record any additional strengths or areas of improvement.
   * Specify any required action items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date of the store visit.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`): Enter the name of the store being evaluated.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`): Enter the city and address of the store.
* **Store Manager Name** (`store_manager`): Enter the name of the store manager on duty during the visit (if applicable).
* **Store Cleanliness and Organization** (`store_cleanliness`): Rate the overall cleanliness and organization of the store. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Product Display and Merchandising** (`product_display`): Evaluate the quality of product display and merchandising. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Signage Quality and Visibility** (`signage_quality`): Rate store signage. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Staff Professionalism and Courtesy** (`staff_professionalism`): Rate staff interactions. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Staff Product Knowledge** (`product_knowledge`): Evaluate staff expertise. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Customer Wait Times** (`customer_waiting_time`): Rate service speed. Choose one of the following options: Unacceptable, Slow, Reasonable, Good, Excellent.
* **Product Availability** (`product_availability`): Rate stock levels. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Stock Rotation and Expiration Dates** (`stock_rotation`): Evaluate inventory management. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Brand Standard Compliance** (`brand_compliance`): Rate adherence to brand standards. Choose one of the following options: Non-compliant, Partially Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Fully Compliant, Exceeds Standards.
* **Pricing Accuracy and Consistency** (`pricing_accuracy`): Evaluate the accuracy of pricing. Choose one of the following options: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent.
* **Store Strengths** (`strengths`): Record any store strengths or positive attributes.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`): Specify any areas where the store needs improvement.
* **Required Action Items** (`action_items`): List specific actions that need to be taken to address areas of improvement.

## Tips
* Be as objective as possible when evaluating the store.
* Take notes on any additional comments or observations.
* Provide specific feedback for each category.
* Ensure that you answer all required questions accurately and thoroughly.
