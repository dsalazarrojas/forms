# Teacher Performance Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Performance Evaluation Questionnaire is a form used to assess a teacher's performance in the classroom. It is designed to gather information on various aspects of a teacher's effectiveness, such as their teaching approach, student satisfaction, and staff satisfaction. The form is used to inform personnel decisions and support teacher development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Start by selecting the correct page to begin the evaluation process.
2. Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
3. For select_one fields, choose the most accurate option from the available choices.
4. For text fields, type in your answers clearly and concisely.
5. Make sure to provide your email and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Answer all questions accurately and honestly to the best of your ability.
