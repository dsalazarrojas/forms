# post_presentation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post-presentation survey aims to gather feedback from attendees on the presentation's content, quality, and overall experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the presentation date in the "Presentation Date" field.
2. Rate your experience with the presentation using the rating scale provided in the "Evaluation Form" field.
3. Enter your comments on the presentation in the "Comments" field.
4. Provide your name and title in the "Speaker Name" and "Speaker Title" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the name of the organization you represent in the "Organization" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the session ID (or any other relevant identifier) in the "Session ID" field.
7. Select the evaluation form (1, 2, or 3) that best describes your experience in the "Evaluation Form" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Date** (1): The date of the presentation.
* **Rating** (2): A rating scale to rate your experience with the presentation (Evaluation Form 1, 2, or 3).
* **Comments** (3): Any comments or feedback you have about the presentation.
* **Time** (4): The time of the presentation (though this field is not required, it's useful for organizers to keep track of presentation timing).
* **Speaker Name** (5): Your name as the attendee.
* **Speaker Title** (6): Your title or position (if you'd like to share it).
* **Organization** (7): The name of the organization you represent, if applicable.
* **Session ID** (8): A unique identifier for the session (you can enter any relevant identifier here).
* **Evaluation Form** (9): Select the evaluation form that best describes your experience (Evaluation Form 1, 2, or 3).
* **Additional Comments** (10): Any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share.
