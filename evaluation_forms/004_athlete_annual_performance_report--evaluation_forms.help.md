# Athlete Annual Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Annual Performance Report is a comprehensive evaluation tool used to assess an athlete's performance over the course of a season. This form helps to identify strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for coaches, evaluators, or administrators to offer constructive feedback and guidance to athletes for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please provide your Athlete's Full Name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2.  Enter the year of the evaluation in the "Report Year" field.
3.  Select the season during which the evaluation took place in the "Season" field (Fall, Winter, Spring, or Summer).
4.  Choose the sport played in the "Sport" field.
5.  For the "Competition Level" field, select the level of competition (Varsity, Junior Varsity, Freshman, or Club/Recreational).
6.  Rate the athlete's technical proficiency using the "Overall Technical Proficiency" field, with ratings ranging from Novice to Elite.
7.  Specify the athlete's technical skills strengths in the "Specific Skill Strengths" field.
8.  Identify areas for improvement in the "Areas for Technical Improvement" field.
9.  Assess the athlete's physical conditioning by rating their endurance, strength, and agility on a scale of 1-10.
10. Evaluate the athlete's mental aspects, such as teamwork, leadership, and mental toughness, by selecting the corresponding ratings.
11. Write a brief "Overall Performance Summary" of the season.
12. Set actionable "Goals for Next Season" to help the athlete improve.
13. Enter your name as the "Evaluator Name".
14. Choose the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name (2)**: Enter the athlete's full name as it appears on their official records.
* **Report Year (3)**: Enter the year the evaluation took place.
* **Season (4)**: Select the season during which the evaluation occurred (Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer).
* **Sport (5)**: Specify the sport the athlete competes in.
* **Competition Level (6)**: Choose the level of competition (Varsity, Junior Varsity, Freshman, Club/Recreational).
* **Overall Technical Proficiency (8)**: Rate the athlete's technical skills from 1 (Novice) to 5 (Elite).
* **Specific Skill Strengths (9)**: Describe the athlete's technical skills in which they excelled.
* **Areas for Technical Improvement (10)**: Identify the areas where the athlete needs to improve their technical skills.
* **Endurance and Stamina (12)**: Rate the athlete's endurance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Strength and Power (13)**: Rate the athlete's strength and power on a scale of 1-10.
* **Agility and Speed (14)**: Rate the athlete's agility and speed on a scale of 1-10.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration (16)**: Evaluate the athlete's teamwork and collaboration on a scale of 1-5.
* **Leadership Qualities (17)**: Rate the athlete's leadership qualities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Mental Toughness and Resilience (18)**: Assess the athlete's mental toughness and resilience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Performance Summary (19)**: Write a brief description of the season's performance and notable accomplishments.
* **Goals for Next Season (20)**: Set actionable goals and areas to improve for the next season.
* **Evaluator Name (21)**: Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Date (22)**: Choose the date of the evaluation.
