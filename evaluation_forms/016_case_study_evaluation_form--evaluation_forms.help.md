# Case Study Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of case study presentations, providing a structured framework for assessors to provide feedback on various aspects of the presentation, including content, organization, and presentation skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the presentation and take note of your observations and feedback.
2.  Enter the evaluation date, which is the date you evaluated the presentation.
3.  Provide the title of the case study being presented.
4.  Identify the presentation session or event where the case study was presented.
5.  Enter your name and role as the evaluator (e.g., faculty, peer student, industry expert, administrator, or other).
6.  List the names of all presenters.
7.  Evaluate the relevance and importance of the topic, using the provided scale (Highly Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Irrelevant).
8.  Assess the presenter's mastery of the subject matter (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
9.  Determine if the content was presented clearly and understandably (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
10.  Evaluate if the content was well-organized and logical (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
11.  Assess the presenter's time management skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
12.  Evaluate the presenter's public speaking and delivery quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
13.  Assess the quality and effectiveness of visual aids used (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, No Visuals Used).
14.  Evaluate if the presenters communicated effectively with the audience (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
15.  Assess if the presenters handled questions well (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
16.  Provide a comprehensive evaluation of the overall quality of the case study presentation (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date you evaluated the presentation in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Case Study Title** (`case_study_title`, `text`, required/true): Enter the title of the case study being presented.
*   **Presentation Session or Event** (`presentation_session`, `text`, required/true): Identify the presentation session or event where the case study was presented.
*   **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, optional/false): Enter your name as the evaluator (e.g., faculty member, peer student, industry expert, or administrator).
*   **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, `select_one`, required/true): Select your role as the evaluator from the options: Faculty, Peer Student, Industry Expert, Administrator, or Other Observer.
*   **Presenter Names** (`presenter_names`, `text`, required/true): List the names of all presenters.
*   **Topic Relevance** (`topic_relevance`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate the relevance and importance of the topic using the provided scale (Highly Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Irrelevant).
*   **Subject Mastery** (`subject_mastery`, `select_one`, required/true): Assess the presenter's mastery of the subject matter (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
*   **Content Clarity** (`content_clarity`, `select_one`, required/true): Determine if the content was presented clearly and understandably (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
*   **Content Organization** (`content_organization`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate if the content was well-organized and logical (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
*   **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_one`, required/true): Assess the presenter's time management skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Public Speaking** (`public_speaking`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate the presenter's public speaking and delivery quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
*   **Visual Aids** (`visual_aids`, `select_one`, required/true): Assess the quality and effectiveness of visual aids used (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, No Visuals Used).
*   **Audience Engagement** (`audience_engagement`, `select_one`, required/true): Evaluate if the presenters communicated effectively with the audience (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
*   **Question Handling** (`question_handling`, `select_one`, required/true): Assess if the presenters handled questions well (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
*   **Overall Quality** (`overall_quality`, `select_one`, required/true): Provide a comprehensive evaluation of the overall quality of the case study presentation (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
*   **Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, optional/false): Provide specific feedback on the strengths of the presentation.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, optional/false): Constructive feedback on areas for improvement and suggestions for the presenters.

## Tips

*   Please answer the questions based on your observations and feedback during the presentation.
*   Use the scales provided for the evaluation questions to ensure consistency in your assessment.
*   Provide constructive and specific feedback for the presenter's improvement.
*   Use the space provided for strengths and areas for improvement sections to offer specific suggestions or comments.
