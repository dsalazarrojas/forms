# Grady EVALUATION FORM TEMPLATE - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grady EVALUATION FORM TEMPLATE is used to collect and document evaluations of projects, courses, or other items.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the First Name field.
*   Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
*   Enter your email address in the Email field.
*   Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
*   Enter any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
*   Select the date of the evaluation in the Evaluation Date field.
*   Select the time of the evaluation in the Evaluation Time field.
*   Enter a score for the evaluation in the Score field.
*   Enter any comments or feedback in the Evaluation Comments field.
*   Select the course or project being evaluated in the Course field.
*   Select the instructor or evaluator in the Instructor Name field.
*   Select the submitted date in the Submitted At field.
*   Select the submitted to field in the Submitted To field.
*   Select the submitted by field in the Submitted By field.
*   Select the submitted on field in the Submitted On field.
*   Select the evaluation status in the Evaluation Status field.
*   Select the evaluation result in the Evaluation Result field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
*   **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the evaluation.
*   **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the evaluation.
*   **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter a score for the evaluation.
*   **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback.
*   **Course** (`course`, select_one, optional): Select the course or project being evaluated.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, select_multiple, optional): Select the instructor or evaluator.
*   **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Select the submitted date.
*   **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional): Select the submitted to field.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select the submitted by field.
*   **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Select the submitted on field.
*   **Evaluation Status** (`evaluation_status`, select_one, optional): Select the evaluation status.
*   **Evaluation Result** (`evaluation_result`, select_multiple, optional): Select the evaluation result.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
*   Double-check your submissions for accuracy.
*   Review the form before submitting it.
*   Use clear and concise language in your submissions.
*   Follow the submission guidelines and requirements.
