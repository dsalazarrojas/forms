# Damage Dispute Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and dispute damage to a property. It captures necessary information about the property, the damage, and supporting evidence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct Property Manager from the list.
3. Enter the date the property damage occurred or was noticed.
4. Describe the damage in as much detail as possible.
5. Attach any supporting evidence, such as photos or videos.
6. Enter the amount of money claimed for repair or reimbursement.
7. Add any additional comments or context about the dispute.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Claimant**: Enter your name as the property owner or manager.
* **Property Manager**: Select the person responsible for the property from the list.
* **Property Damage Date**: Enter the date the property damage occurred or was noticed.
* **Property Damage Description**: Describe the damage in as much detail as possible.
* **Evidence Attachment(s)**: Attach any supporting evidence, such as photos or videos, that document the damage.
* **Damage Amount**: Enter the amount of money claimed for repair or reimbursement.
* **Supporting Evidence**: Add any additional information about the evidence attached, such as where it can be found.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional context or comments about the dispute.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is completed thoroughly.
* Use the property damage date as the date the damage occurred or was noticed, not the date the form is submitted.
* Attach any supporting evidence that is relevant to the dispute.
* Enter accurate and honest information in the damage amount field to avoid delays in processing the claim.
