# Nonprofit Nominee Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Nominee Evaluation Survey is designed to gather feedback and ratings on nonprofits nominated for recognition or awards. This form helps evaluators evaluate the impact, leadership, and overall performance of nominated individuals or organizations. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your name and contact information, including email and phone numbers, in the fields provided.
2. Rate the nominee's impact and service using the numerical scales provided.
3. Provide a comment on the nominee's leadership and service in the corresponding fields.
4. Choose the type of prophet (Nominee or Nominator) that best describes the individual being evaluated.
5. If necessary, provide an impact description and evaluation date.
6. Finally, select the evaluation time and complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominee name**: Please enter the name of the individual being nominated. (id: 8, required: false)
* **Nominee position**: Enter the nominee's job title or position. (id: 9, required: false)
* **Nominee organization**: Enter the name of the organization or institution where the nominee works. (id: 10, required: false)
* **Nominator name**: Enter your name as the person evaluating the nominee. (id: 11, required: false)
* **Nominator position**: Enter your job title or position. (id: 12, required: false)
* **Nominator organization**: Enter the name of the organization or institution where you work. (id: 13, required: false)
* **Impact** (id: 1, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the impact of the nominee's work using the numerical scale.
* **Leadership** (id: 2, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the nominee's leadership skills using the numerical scale.
* **Leadership comment** (id: 3, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief comment on the nominee's leadership skills.
* **Service** (id: 4, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the quality of service provided by the nominee using the numerical scale.
* **Service comment** (id: 5, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief comment on the service provided by the nominee.
* **Overall** (id: 6, number, required: false)
	+ Rate the overall performance of the nominee using the numerical scale.
* **Overall comment** (id: 7, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief comment on the nominee's overall performance.
* **Prophets**: Select the type of prophet (Nominee or Nominator) that best describes the individual being evaluated. (id: 15, select_multiple, required: false)
* **Impact description**: Provide a brief description of the nominee's impact. (id: 14, text, required: false)
* **Evaluation date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. (id: 16, date, required: false)
* **Evaluation time**: Enter the time of the evaluation. (id: 17, time, required: false)
* **Email**: Enter your email. (id: 18, email, required: false)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (id: 19, text, required: false)

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to rate the impact, leadership, and service according to your genuine assessment.
* Provide comments on specific areas of improvement or strengths.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date.
* Proofread your responses before submitting the form.
