# CLEAN TEAM Annual Performance Review Self Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for self-evaluation by employees within the Clean Team division. It will help you reflect on your key accomplishments, challenges faced, and goals for the next review cycle. Please complete this form honestly and thoroughly to ensure an accurate assessment of your performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in your personal details:
   - **Employee name**: Enter your full name as per your personnel records.
   - **Position title**: Enter your current role within the Clean Team division.
2.  Select the review period:
   - **Review period start**: Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
   - **Review period end**: Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
3.  Evaluate your performance:
   - **Key accomplishments**: Describe your notable contributions or projects completed during the review period.
   - **Challenges faced**: Describe any obstacles you faced and how you managed them.
   - **Goals for next period**: Set specific, measurable objectives for the next review cycle.
4.  Rate your overall performance:
   - **Self rating overall**: Choose from three options: Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, or Needs improvement.
5.  Confirm your submission:
   - **Employee signature name**: Enter your full name as a digital signature.
