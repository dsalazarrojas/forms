# Training Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Program Evaluation Form is a form used to gather feedback from participants after attending a training session. It helps evaluate the effectiveness and quality of the training program, providing valuable insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill in the required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
3. For non-required fields, answer them based on your honest assessment and personal experience.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details (1)** (`session_details`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the training session you attended.
* **Training Session Type (2)** (`session_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options: Online, Offline, or Hybrid.
* **Session Topic (3)** (`session_topic`, text, required: false): Describe the topic(s) covered during the training session.
* **Participant Name (4)** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Evaluation Comments (5)** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and suggestions about the training session.
* **Rating (6)** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session (1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest).
* **Session Date (7)** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Time In (8)** (`time_in`, time, required: false): Enter the time you arrived at the training session.
* **Time Out (9)** (`time_out`, time, required: false): Enter the time you left the training session.
* **Evaluation Notes (10)** (`evaluation_notes`, note, required: false): Write any additional comments or notes about the training session.
* **Email (11)** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (12)** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Notification (13)** (`email_notification`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you want to receive email notifications about upcoming trainings.
* **Follow Up Questions (14)** (`follow_up_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you have any follow-up questions.
* **Additional Comments (15)** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the training session.
* **Session Attendance (16)** (`session_attendance`, number, required: false): Enter the number of participants who attended the training session.
* **Session Length (17)** (`session_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of the training session.
* **Session Capacity (18)** (`session_capacity`, number, required: false): Enter the maximum capacity of the training session.
* **Session Start Time (19)** (`session_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **Session End Time (20)** (`session_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the training session.
* **Evaluation Form Completed By (21)** (`evaluation_form_completed_by`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed the evaluation form.
* **Participant Signature (22)** (`participant_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name as the participant.
* **Training Program Attendant (23)** (`training_program_attendant`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the training program you attended.
* **Evaluation Form Completion (24)** (`evaluation_form_completion`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you completed the evaluation form.
* **Evaluation Form Completed On (25)** (`evaluation_form_completed_on`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed the evaluation form.

Note: Please review each field carefully and answer honestly to provide valuable feedback for future improvements.
