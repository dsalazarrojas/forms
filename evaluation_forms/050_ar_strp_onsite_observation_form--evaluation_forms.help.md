# Ar Strp Onsite Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ar Strp Onsite Observation Form is designed to collect information about the on-site observation of properties for the purpose of assessing the condition and functionality of septic systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before starting the form.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, as the information collected will be used to inform decision-making and recommendations.
3. Please refer to the field-by-field explanations below for more detailed guidance on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Information**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about the evaluation, such as the purpose of the visit or any notable circumstances.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name as the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Agency**: If you are affiliated with an agency or organization, enter their name here. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Property Information**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about the property, such as its address or relevant details.
* **Property Address**: Enter the physical address of the property being evaluated.
* **County**: Enter the county where the property is located.
* **Watershed**: Enter the watershed where the property is located.
* **Septic System Evaluation**: This is a note field where you can provide any general observations about the septic system, such as its type or age.
* **Current System Condition**: Select the current condition of the septic system from the options: Good, Fair, Poor, Failed.
* **Tank Structural Integrity**: Select the current condition of the tank from the options: Intact, Minor Damage, Major Damage.
* **Drain Field Condition**: Select the current condition of the drain field from the options: Functional, Partially Functional, Non-Functional.
* **Evidence of Ground Contamination**: Select the level of evidence of ground contamination from the options: None, Minor, Significant.
* **Property Eligible for STRP Remediation**: Select whether the property is eligible for STRP remediation from the options: Yes, No, Conditional.
* **Detailed Observations and Findings**: Provide a detailed description of your observations and findings during the evaluation.
* **Recommended Actions or Improvements**: Provide any recommended actions or improvements for the septic system.
