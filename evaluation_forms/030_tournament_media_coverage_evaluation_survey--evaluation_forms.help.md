# Tournament Media Coverage Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tournament Media Coverage Evaluation Survey is a form to gather feedback on the media coverage of a tournament event. It helps to identify areas of improvement and assess the overall quality of the media coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as per your opinion.
3. Provide any improvement ideas or suggestions in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizers** (Yes/No): Please select the level of organization of the event from the given options.
* **Event Info** (Text): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Media Coverage Quality** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor): Assess the quality of the media coverage based on the provided options.
* **Audience Reach** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor): Evaluate the reach of the audience covered by the media coverage.
* **Improvement Ideas** (Text): Share any thoughts or suggestions for improvement with the media coverage team.
* **Assigned To** (Jim, Jane, John): Identify which team member or department the media coverage was assigned to.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Event Date** (mm/dd/yyyy): Provide the date of the event in the correct format.
* **Event Time** (HH:MM): Provide the time of the event in the correct format.
* **Improvement Ideas** (Text): Share any other thoughts or suggestions that might be helpful.
* **Note**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback. 

Please note that the event date and time fields are for optional input, but if you have that information, please provide it.
