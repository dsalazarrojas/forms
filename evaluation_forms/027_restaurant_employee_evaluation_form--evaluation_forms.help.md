# Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form is used for supervisors to evaluate the performance of their employees. It collects relevant information to track employee performance and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields:
	* Supervisor ID: Enter the ID of the supervisor completing the evaluation.
	* Employee ID: Enter the ID of the employee being evaluated.
	* Date Completed: Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
	* Evaluation Date: Enter the date the evaluation is relevant to (optional).
2.  Fill in the optional fields:
	* Overall Rating: If you feel it's necessary, enter a rating between 0 and 10 to indicate the employee's overall performance.
	* Feedback Comments: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the employee's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id** (Employee ID, Number, Required): Enter the ID of the employee being evaluated.
* **supervisor_id** (Supervisor ID, Number, Required): Enter the ID of the supervisor completing the evaluation.
* **date_completed** (Date Completed, Date, Required): Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **evaluation_date** (Evaluation Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date the evaluation is relevant to. 
* **rating_average** (Rating Average, Number, Optional): If you feel it's necessary, enter a rating between 0 and 10 to indicate the employee's overall performance.
* **feedback_comments** (Feedback Comments, Text, Optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the employee's performance.
* **overall_rating** (Overall Rating, Number, Optional): If you feel it's necessary, enter a rating between 0 and 10 to indicate the employee's overall performance.
