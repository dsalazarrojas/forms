# Player Ratings Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to evaluate players' performance during a match or exercise. It gathers essential information about the player's rating and score, which will help in assessing their skills and providing feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the "Match Date" field with the date of the match or exercise evaluation.
2.  Enter your rating for each of the players being evaluated in the corresponding "Player Rating" fields.
3.  Assign a score between 1 and 5 for each player's score in the "Score 1", "Score 2", "Score 3", "Score 4", and "Score 5" fields.
4.  Select the exercise level of the evaluated exercise in the "Exercise Level" field.
5.  Choose the type of evaluation (Match or Exercise) in the "Evaluation Type" field.
6.  (Optional) Enter any additional comments about the exercise or player performance in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7.  Fill in the "Exercise Duration" field with the actual duration of the exercise if necessary.
8.  (Optional) Enter any comments or feedback about the rating in the "Rating Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Match Date**: Enter the date of the match or exercise evaluation.
*   **Player Rating**: Enter a rating between 1 and 5 for each player being evaluated.
*   **Exercise Name**: Enter the name of the exercise being evaluated.
*   **Evaluation Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the exercise or player performance.
*   **Score 1**, **Score 2**, **Score 3**, **Score 4**, **Score 5**: Enter a score between 1 and 5 for each player's score.
*   **Exercise Level**: Select the level of difficulty of the exercise (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Evaluation Type**: Choose the type of evaluation (Match or Exercise).
*   **Exercise Duration**: Enter the actual duration of the exercise if necessary.
*   **Rating Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the rating.
*   **Player 1 Rating**, **Player 2 Rating**, **Player 3 Rating**: Enter a rating between 1 and 5 for each player being evaluated.
