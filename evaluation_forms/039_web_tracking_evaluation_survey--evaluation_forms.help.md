# Web Tracking Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about their experience with web tracking technologies. It is intended to help the website's owner or administrator improve the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your company name (if applicable) in the "Company" field.
4. Enter the URL of the website you are evaluating in the "Website" field.
5. Select the tracking technologies you have used from the "Which of the following tracking technologies have you used?" field.
6. Provide a numerical rating for this survey in the "Rating for this survey" field.
7. Optionally, provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the website you are evaluating.
* **Which of the following tracking technologies have you used?** (`tracking_technology_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the tracking technologies you have used. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Rating for this survey** (`first_page_rating`, note, required: false): Provide a numerical rating for this survey.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Optionally, provide any additional feedback you may have about the survey or your experience with the website.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have used multiple tracking technologies, select all the options that apply.
* You can provide feedback about your experience with the website, including any issues you encountered.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the website's administrator.
