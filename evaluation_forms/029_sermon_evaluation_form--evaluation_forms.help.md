# Sermon Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sermon Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from the audience after a sermon. This form helps us to assess the content, delivery, and overall impact of the sermon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read each question carefully and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. If a question does not apply to your experience, you can skip it or leave it blank.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers.
4. If you have any comments or questions, use the comments section to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: This section is designed to capture your overall impression of the sermon. Please provide a brief answer to this question.
* **What was your overall impression of the sermon?**: Share your thoughts on what resonated with you or what you liked/disliked about the sermon.
* **What did you find most impactful from the sermon?**: Describe the part of the sermon that had the most impact on you.
* **How did the sermon impact you today?**: Share how the sermon affected your thoughts, feelings, or actions in a positive or negative way.
* **Rate the content of the sermon**: Rate the content of the sermon on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Will you recommend this pastor to others?**: Answer this question with a "yes" or "no".
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them here.
* **Email**: Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone**: This field is not required, but providing your phone number will help us to follow up with you if needed.
* **Submission**: This is a confirmation field to confirm your submission.
