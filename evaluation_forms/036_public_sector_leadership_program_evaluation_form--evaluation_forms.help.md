# public_sector_leadership_program_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the Public Sector Leadership Program, a program aimed at developing leadership skills in public sector employees. It is used to gather feedback from participants and evaluators about their experience in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the form and understand each question.
- Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Take your time to provide detailed comments and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the participant who is submitting the evaluation form. It is a required field and should be filled out with the participant's full name.
* **Program Participation**: This question asks you to select the way you participated in the program. Options include Leadership Training, Mentorship, Coaching, Coaching and Mentoring, and Other. Select the one that best describes your participation in the program.
* **Program Focus Area**: This question asks you to select one or more areas of focus that the program covered. Options include Leadership and Management, Communication and Collaboration, Problem Solving and Analysis. Select all that apply.
* **Feedback Comments**: This is an open-ended question that allows you to provide any additional comments about the program. Please provide any feedback or comments you have about your experience in the program.
* **Overall Experience**: This question asks you to rate your overall experience in the program. Options include Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Recommendations**: This is an optional question that allows you to provide any recommendations or suggestions for the program. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Additional Comments**: This is another open-ended question that allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Participant Email**: This is the email address of the participant who is submitting the evaluation form. It is a required field and should be filled out with the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: This is the phone number of the participant who is submitting the evaluation form. This field is optional.
* **Participant Organization**: This is the organization where the participant works. This field is optional.
* **Participant Job Title**: This is the job title of the participant who is submitting the evaluation form. This field is optional.
* **Participant Location**: This is the location where the participant works. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Name**: This is the name of the evaluator who is completing the evaluation form. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Title**: This is the job title of the evaluator who is completing the evaluation form. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Organization**: This is the organization where the evaluator works. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Job Title**: This is the job title of the evaluator who is completing the evaluation form. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Location**: This is the location where the evaluator works. This field is optional.
* **Evaluator Email**: This is the email address of the evaluator who is completing the evaluation form. It is a required field and should be filled out with the evaluator's email address.
* **Evaluator Phone**: This is the phone number of the evaluator who is completing the evaluation form. This field is optional.
* **Submitted Date**: This is the date when the form was submitted. It is a required field and should be filled out with the correct date.
* **Submitted Time**: This is the time when the form was submitted. It is a required field and should be filled out with the correct time.
* **Submitted By**: This is the name of the person who submitted the form. This field is optional.
* **Submitted**: This question asks you to select whether the form was submitted or not. Options include True or False.
* **Submitted Date/Time**: This is the date and time when the form was submitted. It is a required field and should be filled out with the correct date and time.
