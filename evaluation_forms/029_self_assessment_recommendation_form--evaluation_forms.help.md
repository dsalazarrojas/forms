# Self Assessment Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self-Assessment Recommendation Form is a tool for team members to assess their own performance and recommend areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read the form instructions and questions carefully.
- Fill in the required fields with as much detail as possible.
- For questions with options, select the most relevant choice.
- Review and submit the form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Name** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_1`, text, required): Please enter your team name to associate this self-assessment with your team.
- **Job Title** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_2`, text, optional): Enter your job title to help us understand your role in the team.
- **Evaluation Criteria** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_3`, text, required): Describe the criteria you've used to evaluate your performance.
- **Evaluation Period** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_4`, date, optional): Specify the period you've been evaluating yourself.
- **Self-Assessment Recommendation** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_5`, text, required): Provide a brief self-assessment of your performance, including areas of strength and weakness.
- **Recommended Development Opportunities** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_6`, select_multiple, required): Choose the development opportunities you recommend for yourself.
  - Select 'Yes' if the opportunity is recommended.
  - Select 'No' if it's not recommended.
- **Overall Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_11`, number, required): Rate your overall self-assessment from 1-10, where 1 is 'very poor' and 10 is 'excellent'.
- **Self-Assessment Recommendation Form Submission** (`self_assessment_recommendation_form_12`, text, required): Briefly describe your submission and any supporting materials you've attached.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individual team members for their own self-assessment and recommendation. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please ask your team lead or support staff for assistance.
