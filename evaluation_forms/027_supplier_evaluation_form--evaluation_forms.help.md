# Supplier Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Evaluation Form is a tool for collecting and documenting information about your suppliers. This form is used to gather feedback on the supplier's performance, products, and services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your supplier's name.
* Rate them based on their performance (on a scale).
* Provide any relevant information about the pricing and delivery times.
* Enter your contact person's name.
* Enter your contact email address.
* Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* Enter the contact address (if applicable).
* Leave any additional notes about your interaction with the supplier.
* Provide any comments or feedback about the supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the supplier you are evaluating.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the supplier's performance out of a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Price** (`price_range`, number, required/optional): Provide any information about the price of the products or services.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the delivery time for the products or services.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who handled the interaction.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Supplier Notes** (`supplier_notes`, text, required/optional): Leave any additional notes about your interaction with the supplier.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the supplier.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the rating.

Note: All fields with "required/optional" labels are optional unless stated otherwise.
