# pros_and_cons_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pros and cons evaluation form is designed to help users evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of a particular idea, product, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Introduction" field with a brief description of the subject being evaluated.
2.  In the "What are the Pros" field, list the advantages or benefits of the subject.
3.  In the "What are the Cons" field, list the disadvantages or drawbacks of the subject.
4.  In the "Evaluation Form" field, provide any additional comments or insights related to the evaluation.
5.  Enter the "Overall Score" to rate the subject's performance based on the pros and cons.
6.  In the "List of Pros" field, select from the list of options to indicate the presence or absence of each pro.
7.  In the "List of Cons" field, select from the list of options to indicate the presence or absence of each con.
8.  In the "Weighted Pros" field, select from the list of options to assign weightage to each pro.
9.  In the "Pros Score" field, enter a numerical score based on the weighted pros.
10.  In the "Weighted Cons" field, select from the list of options to assign weightage to each con.
11.  In the "Cons Score" field, enter a numerical score based on the weighted cons.
12.  Enter the "Overall" field with the date of completion.
13.  In the "Time Spent" field, enter the total time spent on evaluating the subject.
14.  In the "List of Pros 2" field, select from the list of options to confirm the presence or absence of each pro.
15.  In the "List of Cons 2" field, select from the list of options to confirm the presence or absence of each con.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`title`, text, required: false): A brief description of the subject being evaluated.
*   **What are the Pros** (`pros`, text, required: true): List the advantages or benefits of the subject.
*   **What are the Cons** (`cons`, text, required: true): List the disadvantages or drawbacks of the subject.
*   **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, text, required: false): Additional comments or insights related to the evaluation.
*   **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required: true): Rate the subject's performance based on the pros and cons.
*   **List of Pros** (`pros_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the list of options to indicate the presence or absence of each pro.
*   **List of Cons** (`cons_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the list of options to indicate the presence or absence of each con.
*   **Weighted Pros** (`pros_weighted`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the list of options to assign weightage to each pro.
*   **Pros Score** (`pros_score`, number, required: true): Enter a numerical score based on the weighted pros.
*   **Weighted Cons** (`cons_weighted`, select_multiple, required: false): Select from the list of options to assign weightage to each con.
*   **Cons Score** (`cons_score`, number, required: true): Enter a numerical score based on the weighted cons.
*   **Overall** (`overall`, date, required: true): Enter the date of completion.
*   **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, time, required: true): Enter the total time spent on evaluating the subject.
*   **List of Pros 2** (`pros_list_2`, select_one, required: false): Select from the list of options to confirm the presence or absence of each pro.
*   **List of Cons 2** (`cons_list_2`, select_one, required: false): Select from the list of options to confirm the presence or absence of each con.
