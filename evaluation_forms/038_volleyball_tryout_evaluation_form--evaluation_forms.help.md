# Volleyball Tryout Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a player's compatibility and skills during a volleyball tryout.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the form with as much information as you can about the player's abilities.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
3. Make sure to sign the form to confirm that it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Compatibility (1)**: This field measures how well the player gets along with their teammates. Please rate the player's ability to fit in with the team on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Skills (2)**: Describe the player's skills and abilities, such as serving, hitting, blocking, and setting.
* **Plays By (3)**: Note the plays and positions that the player is most comfortable playing.
* **Team Compatibility (4)**: Check if the player is a good fit for the team by rating their ability to work with the team as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Coach's Notes (5)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the player's performance.
* **Evaluator's Signature (6)**: Sign your name to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question carefully and honestly.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are complete.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
