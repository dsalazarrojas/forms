# Teaching Method Effectiveness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teaching Method Effectiveness Report Form is designed to collect information about a teaching method's effectiveness. This form is intended to gather feedback or ratings on a specific teaching method, such as a course or instructional approach, to help identify areas of improvement or recognition of successful practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the teaching method you are reporting on (e.g., a course or instructional approach).
2.  Enter the name of the teacher responsible for the reported teaching method.
3.  Enter the teacher's email address.
4.  Enter the teacher's phone number (optional).
5.  Choose the period of the class the teaching method was used (e.g., period 1, period 2, or period 3).
6.  Select the date of the evaluation.
7.  Choose the time of the evaluation (optional).
8.  Enter any additional comments about the effectiveness of the teaching method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Teaching Method Report**: This is where you select the teaching method you are reporting on, such as a course or instructional approach. Choose the method that best describes the teaching strategy used.
*   **Teacher**: Enter the name of the teacher who was responsible for the reported teaching method.
*   **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher who was responsible for the reported teaching method.
*   **Teacher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the teacher who was responsible for the reported teaching method (optional).
*   **Teaching Method**: Select the period of the class where the teaching method was implemented. This will help identify the specific class period the evaluation refers to.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
*   **Time**: Select the time of the evaluation (optional).
*   **Comment**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback about the effectiveness of the teaching method.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as "required" in the original form specification.
