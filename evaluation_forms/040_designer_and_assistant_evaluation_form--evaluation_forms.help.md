# Designer And Assistant Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect evaluations from users on the performance of designers and their assistants. The feedback provided will help improve the quality of services offered by these individuals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions and ensure you are evaluating the designer and their assistant.
2.  Rate the designer and assistant based on their performance.
3.  Provide comments on their strengths and areas for improvement.
4.  Add any additional feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Design Assistant Form** (<code>design_assistant_form</code>, Optional): Please provide any relevant information about the designer's assistant that may help in evaluating their performance.
*   **Rate The Designer** (<code>design_rating</code>, Optional): Rate the designer based on their performance. You can select one of the following options:
    •   Unsatisfactory
    •   Satisfactory
    •   Very Satisfactory
    •   Highly Satisfactory
*   **Rate The Assistant** (<code>assistant_rating</code>, Optional): Rate the assistant based on their performance. You can select one of the following options:
    •   Unsatisfactory
    •   Satisfactory
    •   Very Satisfactory
    •   Highly Satisfactory
*   **Comment On The Assistant** (<code>assistant_comment</code>, Optional): Add any comments about the assistant's performance.
*   **Overall Rating** (<code>overall_rating</code>, Optional): Rate the overall performance of the designer and their assistant. You can select one of the following options:
    •   Unsatisfactory
    •   Satisfactory
    •   Very Satisfactory
    •   Highly Satisfactory
*   **Comments** (<code>overall_comment</code>, Optional): Add any comments about the overall performance of the designer and their assistant.
*   **Comments** (<code>design_comment</code>, Optional): Add any comments about the designer's performance.
*   **Comments** (<code>assistant_comment</code>, Optional): Add any comments about the assistant's performance.
*   **Additional Feedback** (<code>feedback</code>, Optional): Add any additional feedback you may have about the designer and their assistant.
