# Annual Self Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be used during the employee's annual review process to gather information about their progress and performance over the past year. The information gathered will be used by the employee's manager and HR department to assess their performance, set goals, and make decisions about career development and growth.

## How to Complete This Form

- Fill out the form thoroughly and honestly, providing clear and concise answers for each field.
- Ensure you complete all required fields, as they are essential for a comprehensive review.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult with your manager or HR representative for guidance.
- Review your responses carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name (1)** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID (2)** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your employee ID, if applicable.
* **Position Title (3)** (`position_title`, `text`, required): Your current role or job title.
* **Department (4)** (`department`, `text`, required): The department where you work.
* **Direct Manager Name (5)** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Name of your direct manager.
* **Reporting Period (6)** (`review_period`, `text`, required): Date range for this review period.
* **Time in Current Position (7)** (`time_in_position`, `select_one`, required): Select from Less than 6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, More than 5 years.
* **Key Accomplishments This Year (8)** (`key_accomplishments`, `text`, required): Major achievements and milestones you've achieved in the past year.
* **Goals Achieved (9)** (`goals_achieved`, `text`, required): Annual objectives you've completed.
* **Percentage of Goals Achieved (10)** (`goals_achievement_percentage`, `number`, optional): Percentage of goals achieved (e.g., 100%).
* **Self-assessment of overall performance (11)** (`performance_self_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select from Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Meets most expectations, Below expectations.
* **Quality of work produced (12)** (`work_quality`, `select_one`, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement.
* **Productivity and efficiency (13)** (`productivity_efficiency`, `select_one`, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement.
* **Reliability and dependability (14)** (`reliability`, `select_one`, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement.
* **Teamwork and collaboration (15)** (`teamwork`, `select_one`, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement.
* **Communication skills (16)** (`communication`, `select_one`, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement.
* **Initiative and proactivity (17)** (`initiative`, `select_one`, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs improvement.
* **Your Key Strengths (18)** (`key_strengths`, `text`, required): What you do particularly well.
* **Areas for Development (19)** (`areas_development`, `text`, required): Skills to improve or learn.
* **Challenges Faced (20)** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, optional): Obstacles encountered during the year.
* **How You Overcame Challenges (21)** (`how_overcome`, `text`, optional): Solutions and approaches used.
* **Professional Development Activities (22)** (`professional_development`, `text`, optional): Courses, training, certifications completed.
* **Learning Goals for Next Year (23)** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required): Skills or knowledge to acquire.
* **Career Goals and Aspirations (24)** (`career_goals`, `text`, required): Where you want to go professionally.
* **Support needed from manager (25)** (`manager_support`, `text`, optional): Resources or help required.
