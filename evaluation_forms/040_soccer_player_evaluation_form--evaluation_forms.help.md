# Soccer Player Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Player Evaluation Form is used to assess a soccer player's skills and abilities in a standardized and objective way. It is designed to help coaches, trainers, and evaluators make informed decisions about a player's fitness for a team or a specific position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the player's name in the **Player Name** field. This is the name of the player being evaluated.
2.  Select the player's position from the **Player Position** dropdown menu. This will help us understand their specific role on the team.
3.  Evaluate the player's technical skills by entering a score from 1 to 10 in the **Technical Skills** field. This score represents their ball handling and shooting abilities.
4.  Assess the player's tactical skills by entering a score from 1 to 10 in the **Tactical Skills** field. This score represents their strategy and decision-making.
5.  Evaluate the player's physical skills by entering a score from 1 to 10 in the **Physical Skills** field. This score represents their speed, strength, and agility.
6.  Assess the player's psychological skills by entering a score from 1 to 10 in the **Psychological Skills** field. This score represents their leadership and teamwork abilities.
7.  Finally, enter an overall rating from 1 to 10 in the **Overall Rating** field. This score represents the player's performance based on their technical, tactical, physical, and psychological skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Player Name** (text): Enter the name of the player being evaluated. This is a required field.
*   **Player Position** (select_one): Choose from the options: Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, or Forward. This field is required.
*   **Technical Skills** (number): Enter a score from 1 to 10 to represent the player's ball handling and shooting abilities. This field is optional.
*   **Tactical Skills** (number): Enter a score from 1 to 10 to represent the player's strategy and decision-making. This field is optional.
*   **Physical Skills** (number): Enter a score from 1 to 10 to represent the player's speed, strength, and agility. This field is optional.
*   **Psychological Skills** (number): Enter a score from 1 to 10 to represent the player's leadership and teamwork abilities. This field is optional.
*   **Overall Rating** (number): Enter a score from 1 to 10 to represent the player's performance. This field is optional.
