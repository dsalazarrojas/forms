# Safety Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Exam Form is designed to collect information related to safety procedures and participant evaluations. This form helps ensure that participants are aware of and understand the safety protocols and procedures in place during training or evaluation sessions. It also allows examiners to record participant information and evaluate their understanding of the subject matter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Safety Exam Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your exam information in the **Exam Information** field.
2. Provide your participant information in the **Participant Information** field.
3. Select your options for the **Exam Questions** field.
4. Describe the **Safety Procedure** that you have followed during the exam.
5. Enter your **Exam Score** out of the provided options.
6. Choose the **Exam Status**.
7. Sign your **Participant Signature** to acknowledge that you have read and understood the safety procedures.
8. Sign your **Examiner Signature** as a confirmation of your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Information** (Exam Information, text, required: false): Enter your exam title and any relevant details.
* **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
* **Exam Questions** (Exam Questions, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the options that apply to your exam.
* **Safety Procedure** (Safety Procedure, text, required: false): Describe the safety procedure you followed during the exam.
* **Exam Score** (Exam Score, number, required: false): Enter the score you achieved out of the provided options.
* **Exam Status** (Exam Status, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your exam.
* **Participant Signature** (Participant Signature, note, required: false): Sign and date to acknowledge that you have read and understood the safety procedures.
* **Examiner Signature** (Examiner Signature, note, required: false): Sign and date as a confirmation of your understanding.

## Tips
* Please take the time to carefully read and understand the safety procedures before completing this form.
* Make sure to answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your examiner.
