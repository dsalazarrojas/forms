# Teacher Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Observation Form is designed to document and collect feedback or observations about a teacher's performance, behavior, or teaching methods. It helps administrators, colleagues, or supervisors evaluate and improve teacher quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the purpose of the form: The form is meant to document and collect feedback or observations about a teacher's performance, behavior, or teaching methods.
2. Rate the teacher's performance using the rating scale (Field 2): Choose one of the five ratings (rating1, rating2, rating3, rating4, or rating5) that best reflects your observation.
3. Provide detailed observations using the text areas (Fields 3-6): Share your thoughts and insights about the teacher's performance in each of the four text areas.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "teacher_comments" field (Field 8): Use this field to provide any further insights or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rating Scale (Field 2, rating_scale_label)**: Rate the teacher's performance using one of the five available ratings.
* **Observation Area 1 (Field 3, observation_area_1_label)**: Describe your observations about the teacher's performance in this area.
* **Observation Area 2 (Field 4, observation_area_2_label)**: Share your thoughts on the teacher's performance in this area.
* **Observation Area 3 (Field 5, observation_area_3_label)**: Provide feedback about the teacher's performance in this area.
* **Observation Area 4 (Field 6, observation_area_4_label)**: Describe your observations about the teacher's performance in this area.
* **Observation Area 5 (Field 7, observation_area_5_label)**: Share your insights about the teacher's performance in this area.
* **Teacher Comments (Field 8, teacher_comments_label)**: Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the teacher's performance.

## Tips
* Be specific and descriptive when providing feedback in the text areas (Fields 3-6).
* Use the rating scale (Field 2) to provide a quick and easy way to evaluate the teacher's performance.
* Use the "teacher_comments" field (Field 8) to provide any additional insights or suggestions for improvement.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
