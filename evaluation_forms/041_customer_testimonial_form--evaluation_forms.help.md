# Customer Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather customer feedback and opinions about our product or service. This will help us understand customer satisfaction and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.
2. Answer all questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Provide a detailed account of your experience with our product or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, `text`, required: false`): A written account of your experience with our product or service. Please provide as much detail as you can to help us understand your satisfaction.
* **Testimonial Question** (`testimonial_question`, `text`, required: false`): If you have any questions or concerns about our product or service, please list them here.
* **Product or Service** (`product_or_service`, `text`, required: false`): Please specify the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false`): Rate your satisfaction with our product or service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, `select_one`, required: false`): Would you recommend our product or service to others? Please select "True" if you would recommend, and "False" if you would not.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, `text`, required: false`): This field is a duplicate of the first "Testimonial" field. You can use this field to provide additional feedback or comments.
