# Peer Based Curriculum Evaluation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register a peer-based curriculum evaluation. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the institution, curriculum, and evaluator's role in the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information in the required fields.
2. Select your role from the dropdown menu.
3. Complete any additional optional fields for your institution and curriculum.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the evaluation process. This is a required field.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your institution's name if you are evaluating or reviewing a curriculum.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your institution's address if you are evaluating or reviewing a curriculum.
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, `text`, required/optional): Select the type of your institution if you are evaluating or reviewing a curriculum.
* **Curriculum Name** (`curriculum_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the curriculum being evaluated if you are evaluating or reviewing a curriculum.
