# Player Coaching Strategy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Player Coaching Strategy Evaluation Form is used to assess the effectiveness of coaching strategies employed during a player's session. The form helps the coaching staff evaluate their approach, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the date of the player's session** (`session_date`): Choose the date when the player's coaching session took place.
2. **Enter the name of the coaching staff member** (`coach_name`): Type the name of the staff member who conducted the coaching session.
3. **Provide session notes** (`session_notes`): Briefly describe the events and activities that occurred during the session.
4. **Record the player's comments** (`player_comments`): Ask the player to share their thoughts, feelings, and insights about the session.
5. **Evaluate the coaching strategy** (`coaching_strategy`): Select one or more strategies that were employed during the session, such as "Good" or "Fair".
6. **Assess the evaluation form** (`evaluation_form`): Choose the level of evaluation for the session, ranging from "Very Good" to "Poor".
7. **Rate the overall session** (`overall_rating`): Provide a numerical rating from 0 to 10 to express your overall satisfaction with the session.
8. **Add any additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any further thoughts or observations about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **session_date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the player's session.
* **coaching staff member name** (`coach_name`, text, required: false): Type the name of the staff member who conducted the session.
* **session notes** (`session_notes`, note, required: false): Describe the events and activities that occurred during the session.
* **player comments** (`player_comments`, text, required: false): Ask the player to share their thoughts, feelings, and insights about the session.
* **coaching strategy** (`coaching_strategy`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more strategies that were employed during the session, such as "Good" or "Fair".
* **evaluation form** (`evaluation_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the level of evaluation for the session, ranging from "Very Good" to "Poor".
* **overall rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): Provide a numerical rating from 0 to 10 to express your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any further thoughts or observations about the session.
