# Security Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Security Service Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback and evaluations from clients regarding the security services provided to them. This form is designed to gather information about the client's experience, satisfaction, and any issues they may have encountered during the service. The form is used to identify areas of improvement and to provide valuable insights for our team to enhance our security services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*)
2. Be as detailed as possible when answering open-ended questions, such as the "Feedback" and "Additional Info" fields
3. Use the dropdown menus to select your preferred contact method and the service level rating
4. Make sure to submit the form when complete

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Id** (1, number, optional):
	+ Enter your unique client ID, which can be found on your service agreement or previous communication from us
* **Service Name** (2, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the service you received, such as "Penetration Testing" or "Vulnerability Assessment"
* **Service Level** (3, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the level of service you received: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor
* **Rating** (4, number, optional):
	+ Enter a numerical rating of your experience, from 1-5
* **Feedback** (5, text, optional):
	+ Share any positive or negative comments about your experience
* **Contact Method** (6, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the method you would like to be contacted, such as Email, Phone, or In-person
* **Date** (7, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you received the service
* **Time** (8, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you received the service
* **Contact Person** (9, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and title of the contact person who provided the service
* **Contact Info** (10, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant contact information, such as email or phone number
* **Additional Info** (11, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional details about your experience
* **Assigned Tool** (12, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the tool used during the service
* **Notes** (13, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience
* **Submitted By** (14, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate who submitted the form, such as an Administrator or Client
* **Submitted On** (15, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date the form was submitted
