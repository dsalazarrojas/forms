# Manufacturing Process Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Process Evaluation form is designed to assess various aspects of the manufacturing process, including process steps, safety checks, quality checks, time required, and submitter information. By using this form, you can identify areas for improvement and optimize the process to ensure efficiency, safety, and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please describe the process steps used in the manufacturing process.
* Select all applicable safety checks from the safety checklist.
* Choose one quality check that applies to the process.
* Add any comments on the process.
* Select the time required for the process.
* Enter your name and title.
* Add any comments from the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Process Steps** (`process_steps`, required): Describe the process steps used in the manufacturing process.
* **Safety Checklist** (`safety_checklist`, required): Check all the applicable safety checks:
	+ Compliance 1
	+ Compliance 2
	+ Compliance 3
* **Quality Checks** (`quality_checks`, required): Select one quality check that applies to the process:
	+ Check 1
	+ Check 2
	+ Check 3
* **Comments** (`comments`, required): Add any comments on the process.
* **Time Required** (`time_required`, required): Select the time required for the process:
	+ Less than 1 hour
	+ 1-2 hours
	+ 2-4 hours
	+ More than 4 hours
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Comments from Submitter** (`comments_submitter`, required): Add any comments from the submitter.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
* Be specific when describing process steps and selecting quality checks.
* Use clear and concise language when entering your name and comments.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
