# logistics_delivery_personnel_efficiency_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the efficiency of delivery personnel in logistics-related tasks, such as delivery scheduling, inventory management, and personnel management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the delivery team.
2. Select the date of the delivery.
3. Enter the time of the delivery.
4. If necessary, enter any personnel-related information (this field is optional).
5. If necessary, select whether or not there is a personnel assigned.
6. Enter a score for the efficiency of the delivery (this field is optional).
7. Add any additional comments about the delivery.
8. Enter the contact email (this field is optional).
9. Enter the contact phone number (this field is optional).
10. Enter the person assigned to follow up on the delivery.
11. Enter a performance rating for the delivery (this field is required).
12. Add any additional comments about the follow up.
13. Enter a follow-up deadline (this field is optional).
14. Select the status of the follow-up (this field is optional).
15. Enter any comments about the follow-up (this field is optional).
16. Enter the next follow-up deadline (this field is optional).
17. Select the status of the next follow-up (this field is optional).
18. Enter any comments about the next follow-up (this field is optional).
19. Enter the frequency of follow-ups (this field is optional).
20. Enter a reminder for follow-ups (this field is optional).
21. Enter the date of the last contact (this field is optional).
22. Enter the date of the next contact (this field is optional).
23. Enter any last action about the follow-up (this field is optional).
24. Select the status of the last follow-up (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **delivery_team** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the delivery team.
* **delivery_date** (2, date, required): Select the date of the delivery.
* **delivery_time** (3, time, required): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **personnel** (4, text, optional): If necessary, enter any personnel-related information.
* **personnel_id** (5, select_one, optional): If necessary, select whether or not there is a personnel assigned. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **efficiency_score** (6, number, optional): Enter a score for the efficiency of the delivery.
* **notes** (7, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the delivery.
* **email** (8, email, optional): Enter the contact email.
* **phone** (9, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **assigned_to** (10, text, optional): Enter the person assigned to follow up on the delivery.
* **performance_rating** (11, number, required): Enter a performance rating for the delivery.
* **comments** (12, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the follow up.
* **follow_up_action** (13, text, optional): Enter a follow-up action.
* **follow_up_deadline** (14, date, optional): Enter a follow-up deadline.
* **follow_up_status** (15, select_one, optional): Select the status of the follow-up. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **follow_up_comments** (16, text, optional): Enter any comments about the follow up.
* **next_follow_up_deadline** (17, date, optional): Enter the next follow-up deadline.
* **next_follow_up_status** (18, select_one, optional): Select the status of the next follow-up. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **next_follow_up_comments** (19, text, optional): Enter any comments about the next follow-up.
* **follow_up_frequency** (20, number, optional): Enter the frequency of follow-ups.
* **follow_up_reminder** (21, text, optional): Enter a reminder for follow-ups.
* **last_contact** (22, date, optional): Enter the date of the last contact.
* **next_follow_up_last_contact** (23, date, optional): Enter the date of the next contact.
* **follow_up_last_action** (24, text, optional): Enter any last action about the follow-up.
* **follow_up_last_status** (25, select_one, optional): Select the status of the last follow-up. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
