# Advanced Cardiac Life Support ACLS Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) survey is designed to evaluate the knowledge and confidence of healthcare professionals providing ACLS care. It assesses the level of expertise in ACLS practices and identifies areas where further training is needed to enhance team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure accurate self-assessment.
2. Answer all questions honestly, as they are intended for self-assessment.
3. Rate your knowledge and confidence levels using the provided scales.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as a participant in the survey.
* **Current ACLS certification status** (`certification_status`, text, Optional): If you hold an ACLS certification, provide the expiry date.
* **Self-rated ACLS knowledge** (`knowledge_rating`, select_one, Required): Rate your current knowledge level from 1 to 5, with 1 being "1" and 5 being "5".
* **Frequency of recent ACLS practice** (`recent_practice_frequency`, select_one, Required): Select how often you have practiced ACLS in the past year:
	+ Never
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Regularly
* **Confidence leading ACLS team** (`confidence_in_team_lead`, select_one, Required): Select your level of confidence in leading an ACLS team:
	+ Not confident
	+ Slightly confident
	+ Confident
	+ Very confident
* **Specific training needs** (`training_needs`, text, Optional): If you need additional training, indicate the specific topics requiring reinforcement.
* **Preferred training format** (`preferred_training_format`, select_one, Optional): Choose your preferred training format:
	+ In person simulation
	+ Online modules
	+ Hybrid
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Provide any other relevant feedback or comments.
