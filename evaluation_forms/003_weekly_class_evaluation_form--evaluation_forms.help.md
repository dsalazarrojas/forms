# Weekly Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students after each weekly class session. It is designed to help instructors understand student thoughts and insights, and adjust their teaching approach accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the text boxes with your thoughts, insights, and feedback.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions, even if you don't have an answer for a specific question, you can simply write "not applicable" or "not relevant".
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.
