# customer_feedback_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the company. It allows customers to provide information about themselves, their feedback, and their rating of the experience. This form should only be used when a customer has had a specific interaction with the company, such as a customer service experience or a product inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow the instructions below.
2.  Fill out the **Customer Information** section with your name and email address.
3.  In the **Customer Feedback** section, provide a detailed description of your experience.
4.  In the **Rating** section, select a rating for your experience out of 5.
5.  In the **Contact Us** section, select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
6.  In the **Additional Information** section, provide any additional information you would like to share.
7.  In the **Submit** section, select how you would like to submit your feedback.
8.  Finally, submit the form by selecting the desired submit option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing your name and email address. You can enter your name and email address in the provided fields.
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering your name.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for entering your email address.
*   **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `note`, required: false): This field is for providing a detailed description of your experience.
*   **Customer Rating** (`customer_rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating your experience out of 5.
*   **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
*   **Additional Information** (`customer_info_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information you would like to share.
*   **Name** (`customer_info_2_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering a name.
*   **Email** (`customer_info_2_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for entering an email address.
*   **Phone** (`customer_info_2_phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering a phone number.
*   **Info** (`customer_info_2_info`, `note`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information you would like to share.
*   **Address** (`customer_info_2_address`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering an address.
*   **Country** (`customer_info_2_country`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select a country.
*   **City** (`customer_info_2_city`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering a city.
*   **State** (`customer_info_2_state`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering a state.
*   **ZIP/Postal** (`customer_info_2_postal`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering a zip or postal code.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select how you would like to submit your feedback.
*   **Rate** (`customer_info_2_rate`, `number`, required: false): This field is for rating your experience out of 5.
*   **Rate Comment** (`customer_info_2_rate_comment`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a rate comment.
*   **Submit** (`customer_info_2_submit`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select how you would like to submit your feedback.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide detailed feedback in the **Customer Feedback** field.
*   Choose a rating that reflects your experience.
*   Select the desired contact option in the **Contact Us** field.
*   Enter accurate information in the **Customer Information** and **Additional Information** fields.
*   Select the desired submit option in the **Submit** field.
