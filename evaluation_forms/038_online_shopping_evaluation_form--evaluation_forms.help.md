# Online Shopping Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers who have purchased an item online or in a physical store. The purpose of this form is to allow customers to share their experience with their purchase, providing valuable insights that can be used to improve the shopping process and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport. This information is required for us to verify your feedback and follow up with you if needed.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you purchased the item: Online Shopping, Offline Shopping, or Store Visit.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your purchase on a scale of 1-5.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences with your purchase. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Rating Value** (`rating_value`, number, optional): If you selected a rating, enter the corresponding value (e.g., 3/5).
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Recommended** (`recommended`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you would recommend this product or service to others.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions, please provide your email address or phone number.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- If you have any issues or problems with your purchase, describe them in the "Review" field.
- If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, share them in the "Comment" field.
- If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions, provide your contact information.
