# Restaurant Personnel Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Restaurant Personnel Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance of restaurant personnel, evaluating their skills, work attitude, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Name in the designated field.
2. Select the Employee Role from the provided options (e.g., Manager, Server, Host, etc.).
3. Choose the Evaluation Date, if applicable.
4. Rate the employee's performance in each of the required fields (Communication Skills, Time Management, Work Quality, Work Attitude, Punctuality, Team Work, Adaptability, Initiative, Reliability, and Recommendation) using the provided scales (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor, Excellent).
5. Enter any additional feedback or comments in the Feedback and Notes fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Employee Role**: Select the role the employee is performing (e.g., Manager, Server, Host, etc.).
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Overall Rating**: Enter a numerical value to rate the employee's overall performance.
* **Communication Skills**: Rate the employee's communication skills as Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Time Management**: Rate the employee's time management as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Work Quality**: Rate the employee's work quality as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Work Attitude**: Rate the employee's work attitude as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Punctuality**: Enter a numerical value to rate the employee's punctuality.
* **Team Work**: Rate the employee's teamwork as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Adaptability**: Rate the employee's adaptability as Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Initiative**: Rate the employee's initiative as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Reliability**: Rate the employee's reliability as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Attendance**: Enter a numerical value to rate the employee's attendance.
* **Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the employee.
* **Recommendation**: Enter any recommendations for future development or support for the employee.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when providing feedback.
* Ensure that the evaluation is fair and constructive.
* Use the provided scales to make it easy to evaluate the employee's performance.
* Enter any additional feedback or comments in the Feedback field.
* Use the Notes field to record any additional comments or observations.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
