# Educational Program Impact Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Program Impact Evaluation Questionnaire is a tool used to gather feedback from participants about their experience with an educational program. This form will help program staff and administrators understand how the program met participants' expectations and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and provide specific examples to help program staff and administrators better understand your experience.
3. Please do not fill out this form if you did not participate in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section provides a brief overview of the form and explains its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Participant Info**: This section asks for demographic information about yourself. Please provide your name, email, and phone number.
* **Program Details**: Choose the options that best describe the program you attended. You can select multiple options.
	+ **Program Name**: The name of the program you attended.
	+ **Program Type**: The type of the program (e.g., workshop, seminar, etc.).
	+ **Program Description**: A brief description of the program.
	+ **Program Date**: The date you attended the program.
	+ **Program Location**: The location where the program took place.
	+ **Number of Participants**: The number of participants in the program.
* **Training Session**: Choose the session you attended.
* **Feedback and Rating**: Choose how satisfied you were with the program.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: You were extremely happy with the program.
	+ **Somewhat Satisfied**: You were somewhat happy with the program.
	+ **Neutral**: You were neither happy nor unhappy with the program.
	+ **Somewhat Dissatisfied**: You were somewhat unhappy with the program.
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**: You were extremely unhappy with the program.
* **Participant Experience**: Choose how you felt about your experience with the program.
	+ **Positive**: You had a positive experience.
	+ **Somewhat Positive**: You had a somewhat positive experience.
	+ **Neutral**: You had a neutral experience.
	+ **Somewhat Negative**: You had a somewhat negative experience.
	+ **Negative**: You had a negative experience.
* **Outcome Rating**: Choose how successful you think the program was.
	+ **Very Successful**: The program was extremely successful.
	+ **Somewhat Successful**: The program was somewhat successful.
	+ **Neutral**: The program was neither successful nor unsuccessful.
	+ **Somewhat Unsuccessful**: The program was somewhat unsuccessful.
	+ **Very Unsuccessful**: The program was extremely unsuccessful.
* **Outcome Expectation**: Choose how your experience met your expectations.
	+ **Much More Than Expected**: The program exceeded your expectations.
	+ **Somewhat More Than Expected**: The program somewhat exceeded your expectations.
	+ **As Expected**: The program met your expectations.
	+ **Somewhat Less Than Expected**: The program somewhat failed to meet your expectations.
	+ **Much Less Than Expected**: The program failed to meet your expectations.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the program.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Program Rating**: Choose how you rate the program.
	+ **Excellent**: You highly rate the program.
	+ **Good**: You somewhat rate the program.
	+ **Fair**: You neither rate the program highly nor poorly.
	+ **Poor**: You poorly rate the program.
* **Overall Rating**: Choose how satisfied you are with the program overall.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: You are extremely happy with the program overall.
	+ **Somewhat Satisfied**: You are somewhat happy with the program overall.
	+ **Neutral**: You are neither happy nor unhappy with the program overall.
	+ **Somewhat Dissatisfied**: You are somewhat unhappy with the program overall.
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**: You are extremely unhappy with the program overall.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the program.
