# Ballet Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ballet Student Evaluation Form is designed to assess a ballet student's technical skills, artistry, and overall development in a class setting. This form helps the instructor or evaluator to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the student's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the student's **Student full name** in the provided text field. Please ensure that you enter the student's first and last name.
2.  Enter your **Instructor or evaluator name** and **Instructor or evaluator title** in the text fields provided.
3.  Select the student's **Class level** from the drop-down menu.
4.  Rate the student's **Technical skills** on a scale of 1 to 5.
5.  Rate the student's **Artistry and musicality** on a scale of 1 to 5.
6.  In the **Technique comments** field, provide your observations and recommended exercises for the student.
7.  In the **Goals and recommendations** field, suggest goals for the next evaluation period.
8.  If there are any attendance issues, note this in the **Overall attendance** field and indicate whether it has **No impact**, **Minor impact**, or **Significant impact**.
9.  Set the **Evaluation date** by selecting the date the evaluation was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Student full name**: The student's first and last name, e.g., John Doe.
*  **Instructor or evaluator name**: Your name as the instructor or evaluator.
*  **Instructor or evaluator title**: Your title, e.g., Teacher or Coach.
*  **Class level**: Choose the student's class level, e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Pre-professional.
*  **Technical skills rating**: Rate the student's technical skills on a 1-5 scale.
*  **Artistry and musicality rating**: Rate the student's artistry and musicality on a 1-5 scale.
*  **Technique comments**: Provide observations and recommended exercises for the student.
*  **Goals and recommendations**: Suggest goals for the next evaluation period.
*  **Overall attendance**: Note if attendance is impacting the student's progress and select the appropriate impact level.
*  **Evaluation date**: Set the date the evaluation was completed.

## Tips

*  Please complete the form thoroughly and accurately.
*  Ensure that you provide clear and concise comments for each section.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to your instructor or evaluator for guidance.
*  This form is designed to help the student track their progress and set goals for the next evaluation period.
