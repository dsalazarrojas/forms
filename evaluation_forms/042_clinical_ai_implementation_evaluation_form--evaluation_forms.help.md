# Clinical AI Implementation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the implementation of AI systems in clinical settings. It will help assess the ease of use, clinical impact, and challenges faced during the implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the type of facility where the AI system was implemented (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, etc.).
2. Enter the name of the facility where the AI system was implemented.
3. Provide contact information for the person who can be reached for more details.
4. Specify the timeframe when the AI system was implemented.
5. Select all the clinical applications that the AI system was used for (e.g., Diagnosis support, Treatment planning, etc.).
6. Assess the usability of the system, its learning curve, and its integration with existing systems.
7. Evaluate the performance of the system, including its accuracy, speed, and reliability.
8. Consider the clinical impact of the system, including its effect on clinical outcomes and workflow efficiency.
9. Provide feedback on staff satisfaction with the system.
10. If there were any main challenges experienced during implementation, describe them in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Type**: Choose the type of facility where the AI system was implemented (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, etc.). This will help us understand the context in which the system was deployed.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the AI system was implemented.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person who can be reached for more details.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **AI System Name**: Enter the name of the AI system being evaluated.
* **Implementation Timeframe**: Select the timeframe when the AI system was implemented (e.g., Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Clinical Applications**: Select all the clinical applications that the AI system was used for (e.g., Diagnosis support, Treatment planning, etc.).
* **Ease of Use**: Assess whether the system is easy to use or not.
* **Learning Curve**: Evaluate the ease of learning the system.
* **Integration**: Assess the system's integration with existing systems.
* **Accuracy**: Evaluate the accuracy of AI recommendations.
* **Speed**: Assess the speed of processing.
* **Reliability**: Evaluate the system's reliability.
* **Clinical Outcomes**: Evaluate the impact on clinical outcomes.
* **Efficiency**: Evaluate the impact on workflow efficiency.
* **Staff Satisfaction**: Evaluate staff satisfaction with the system.
* **Main Challenges**: Describe any main challenges experienced during implementation.
* **Recommendations**: Provide recommendations for improvement.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* Provide concrete examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Focus on the specific AI system being evaluated.
* Do not provide general feedback on the industry or clinical practices.
