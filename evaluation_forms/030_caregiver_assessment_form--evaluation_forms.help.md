# Caregiver Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Caregiver Assessment Form is a tool used to assess the experiences, needs, and challenges of caregivers. It helps identify areas of support and resources that can be beneficial for caregivers, ensuring they receive adequate assistance and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and answer questions based on your current caregiving situation.
2. Fill out the form completely and honestly, as your responses will help tailor support and services to your individual needs.
3. If you are unsure about a question or feel that it does not apply, skip it or provide as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as the primary caregiver.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, `date`, `required`): Record the date of this assessment.
* **Care Recipient Name** (`care_recipient_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the person being cared for.
* **Relationship to Care Recipient** (`relationship`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how you are related to the care recipient, such as family member, professional, or friend.
* **Duration of Caregiving** (`caregiving_duration`, `text`, `required`): Describe how long you have been the primary caregiver.
* **Frequency of Caregiving** (`caregiving_frequency`, `text`, `required`): State the number of hours you care for the care recipient daily or weekly.
* **Type of Care Provided** (`caregiving_type`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the types of care you provide, such as personal hygiene, medication management, and transportation.
* **Primary Health Condition of Care Recipient** (`care_recipient_condition`, `text`, `required`): List the main illness or disability of the care recipient.
* **Care Recipient Mobility Status** (`care_recipient_mobility`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate the care recipient's mobility level, such as fully independent or bedridden.
* **Care Recipient Cognitive Status** (`care_recipient_cognition`, `select_one`, `required`): Report the care recipient's cognitive status, like being alert and oriented or unable to assess.
* **Caregiver Age** (`caregiver_age`, `number`, `required`): Enter your age as the primary caregiver.
* **Caregiver Overall Health Status** (`caregiver_health`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your overall health status, including excellent, fair, or poor.
* **Chronic Health Conditions of Caregiver** (`caregiver_health_conditions`, `text`, `optional`): List any chronic health conditions or limitations you experience.
* **Physical Demands of Caregiving** (`physical_demands`, `select_one`, `required`): Describe the level of physical demands caregiving has on you, such as minimal or heavy physical demands.
* **Emotional and Mental Stress Level of Caregiving** (`emotional_stress`, `select_one`, `required`): State the level of emotional and mental stress you experience while caregiving, such as minimal or severe stress.
* **Financial Impact of Caregiving** (`financial_impact`, `select_one`, `required`): Report the financial impact of caregiving on your life, such as no impact or severe financial strain.
* **Social Isolation or Loneliness** (`social_isolation`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether caregiving has isolated you socially, such as not isolated or severely isolated.
* **Impact on Employment** (`employment_impact`, `select_one`, `optional`): Describe the impact of caregiving on your work, such as no impact or severe impact.
* **Support System Available** (`support_system`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the sources of support you have for caregiving, such as family members or professional services.
* **Access to Respite Care** (`respite_care_access`, `select_one`, `required`): Report your access to respite care, such as regular or rare respite care availability.
* **Training or Skills Needed** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose the skills or training you believe would be beneficial for you as a caregiver.
* **Resources or Support Needed** (`resource_needs`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the resources or support you need most, such as financial assistance or in-home help.
* **Coping Strategies Used** (`coping_strategies`, `text`, `optional`): Describe how you manage stress and challenges as a caregiver.
* **Self-Care Engagement** (`self_care`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate how often you prioritize self-care, such as regularly or never.
* **Safety Concerns or Risk Factors** (`safety_concerns`, `text`, `optional`): Report any safety concerns or vulnerabilities you have as a caregiver.
* **Plans for Long-Term Care Arrangements** (`future_planning`, `text`, `optional`): Discuss your plans for sustaining the current care arrangement.
* **Cultural or Spiritual Factors** (`cultural_considerations`, `text`, `optional`): Mention any cultural or spiritual practices that are important to you as a caregiver.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any other details about your caregiving situation that may be helpful for support and services.
