# Clinical Faculty Evaluation Template 3 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template is used by evaluators to assess the performance and suitability of a clinical faculty member for a specific position. It helps to evaluate the faculty member's clinical knowledge, skills, and personal qualities, and provides a comprehensive recommendation for their appointment or continuation in the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Evaluator's Name and the Candidate or Faculty Name in the respective fields.
2. Select the Date of Evaluation using the calendar picker or date input field.
3. Evaluate the faculty member's performance in each category, using the scales provided.
	* Clinical Knowledge and Expertise: Select the level of clinical knowledge and expertise, using the 1-5 scale.
	* Communication and Interpersonal Skills: Select the level of communication and interpersonal skills, using the 1-5 scale.
	* Teaching and Mentorship Ability: Select the level of teaching and mentorship ability, using the 1-5 scale, if applicable (this field is optional).
4. Provide a brief description of the primary strengths of the faculty member.
5. Identify areas for improvement or weaknesses of the faculty member.
6. Provide a final overall recommendation, using the provided options ( Highly Recommend, Recommend, Recommend with Reservations, Do Not Recommend).
7. Add any additional comments or feedback.
8. Sign and date the form with your full name as the Evaluator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (1): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Candidate or Faculty Name** (2): Enter the name of the faculty member being evaluated.
* **Date of Evaluation** (3): Select or input the date on which the evaluation was conducted.
* **Clinical Knowledge and Expertise** (5): Evaluate the level of clinical knowledge and expertise, using the 1-5 scale.
* **Communication and Interpersonal Skills** (6): Evaluate the level of communication and interpersonal skills, using the 1-5 scale.
* **Teaching and Mentorship Ability** (7): Evaluate the level of teaching and mentorship ability, using the 1-5 scale, if applicable.
* **Enthusiasm towards the position** (8): Evaluate the level of enthusiasm towards the position, using the 1-5 scale.
* **Primary Strengths** (10): Provide a brief description of the primary strengths of the faculty member.
* **Areas for Improvement or Weaknesses** (11): Describe the areas where the faculty member needs improvement or weaknesses.
* **Overall Recommendation** (13): Select the final overall recommendation, using the provided options.
* **Additional Comments** (14): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Evaluator Signature - Type Full Name** (15): Sign and date the form with your full name as the Evaluator.

Tips:
* Be honest and objective in your evaluation, focusing on the specific qualities and characteristics that are essential for the position.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your evaluation, if possible.
* Remember to date and sign the form after completing it.
