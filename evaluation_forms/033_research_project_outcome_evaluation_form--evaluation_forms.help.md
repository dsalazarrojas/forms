# research_project_outcome_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "research_project_outcome_evaluation_form" form is used by project leads to provide an update on their completed research project. It ensures that all necessary data is collected and recorded in a structured manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Go to the "research_project_outcome_evaluation_form" page.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, providing the required information for your project.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project, including any relevant details that you want to share.

### Evaluation Questions
* **Evaluation Questions** (`evaluation_questions`, text, optional): Answer any evaluation questions that are relevant to your project.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about your project.

### Project Outcome
* **Project Outcome** (`project_outcome`, select_one, optional): Select one or more of the provided values to indicate the outcome of your project:
	+ Value 1
	+ Value 2
	+ Value 3

### Date Completed
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date your project was completed.

### Time Completed
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time your project was completed.

### Email Project Lead
* **Email Project Lead** (`email_project_lead`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your project lead.

### Phone Project Lead
* **Phone Project Lead** (`phone_project_lead`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your project lead.

**Tips**

* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure that your project's status is properly recorded.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your project lead or support team.
