# Proposal Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proposal Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the quality and feasibility of proposals submitted by potential clients. This form helps to gather relevant information about the project, the proposal, and the evaluator's opinion on the proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the proposal carefully and answer each question based on your evaluation.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and clear answers.
3. Make sure to provide enough detail in the "Evaluation Reason" field to justify your score and evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, not required): This field is not required to be filled out. It's meant to be filled out by the form creator to provide additional information about the form.
* **Project Information** (`project_info`, not required): Enter any relevant details about the project, such as project name, client, or project description.
* **Proposal Information** (`proposal_info`, not required): Enter any relevant details about the proposal, such as proposal name, client, or proposal description.
* **Proposal Score** (`proposal_score`, not required): Score the proposal out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, not required): Select the options that best describe your evaluation of the proposal. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Evaluation Reason** (`evaluation_reason`, not required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your evaluation score and selection. This field should be filled out if you select option 8 in the "Evaluation" field.
* **Date** (`date`, not required): Enter the date on which you evaluated the proposal.
* **Time** (`time`, not required): Enter the time on which you evaluated the proposal.
* **Email** (`email`, not required): Enter your email address (e.g., for follow-up communication).
* **Phone** (`phone`, not required): Enter your phone number (e.g., for follow-up communication).

# Tips

* Make sure to provide enough detail in the "Evaluation Reason" field to justify your score and selection.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to these guidelines.
* If you need to submit multiple proposals for evaluation, please fill out a new form for each proposal.
