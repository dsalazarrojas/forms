# end_user_satisfaction_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The end-user satisfaction evaluation form is designed to collect data on customer satisfaction levels, ratings, and comments after a visit or interaction with our services. This data helps us understand our services' effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Customer Name.
2. Choose your Overall Satisfaction Level (select from 1 to 8).
3. Enter your Satisfaction Rating (a numerical value).
4. Provide a Comment about your experience.
5. Enter the Contact Person's Name.
6. Enter the Department you interacted with.
7. Enter the Contact Number.
8. Enter the Date and Time of your visit.
9. Enter your Email Address.
10. Enter your Phone Number.
11. If applicable, Enter the Service Level you encountered.
12. If applicable, enter Follow-up contact information (name, date, time, and comments).
13. If applicable, provide details about any issues you faced.
14. If applicable, describe the action taken to resolve the issue.
15. If applicable, describe the solution or resolution.
16. If applicable, select the resolution outcome.
17. If applicable, provide a rating comment.
18. If applicable, describe the follow-up next action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on the documentation or records.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your overall satisfaction level from 1 to 8.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Enter a numerical value (e.g., 1-5) to rate your satisfaction.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Provide any comments about your experience.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person you interacted with.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you interacted with.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit or interaction.
* **Time Visited** (`time_visited`, time, required): Enter the time of your visit or interaction.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Level** (`service_level`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the service level you experienced.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): If follow-up contact is needed, enter the name, date, time, and comments.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If follow-up contact is needed, enter the date.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): If follow-up contact is needed, enter the time.
* **Follow-up Comment** (`follow_up_comment`, text, optional): If follow-up contact is needed, enter comments about the follow-up.
* **Issue** (`issue`, text, optional): If an issue arose, enter details about it.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): If an issue arose, describe the action taken to resolve it.
* **Solution** (`solution`, text, optional): If an issue arose, describe the solution or resolution.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`, select_one, optional): Select the resolution outcome.
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the resolution time.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, text, required): If an issue arose, enter details about it.
* **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, select_one, optional): Select the resolution outcome.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, optional): If applicable, provide a rating comment.
* **Follow-up Next Action** (`follow_up_next_action`, text, optional): If follow-up contact is needed, describe the next action to take.
