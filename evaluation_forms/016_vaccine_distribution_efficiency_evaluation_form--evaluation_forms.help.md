# Vaccine Distribution Efficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vaccine Distribution Efficiency Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from vaccine distributors on their experiences with the distribution process. The form helps us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the current distribution system and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple fields to choose the most relevant answer.
3. Enter your feedback in the notes fields (e.g., vaccine_distribution_efficiency, feedback, comments).
4. Fill out any required fields (indicated by a *).
5. Review your completed form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vaccine Distribution Efficiency
This field is a note field where you can provide your overall impression of the vaccine distribution efficiency.

* **Vaccine Distribution Efficiency** (`vaccine_distribution_efficiency`, note, *): A brief description of your experience with the vaccine distribution process.

### Overall Rating
This field asks for your overall rating of the vaccine distribution process.

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, *): Rate the vaccine distribution process as either Good or Fair.

### Distribution Strategy
Select the distribution strategy that best describes your approach to vaccine distribution.

* **Distribution Strategy** (`distribution_strategy`, select_multiple, *): Choose the options that apply to your distribution process: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.

### Distribution Channel
Select the distribution channel you used for vaccine distribution.

* **Distribution Channel** (`distribution_channel`, select_one, *): Choose the option that best describes your distribution channel: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, Option 6, Option 7.

### Feedback
This field is for any additional feedback you may have on the vaccine distribution process.

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, *): Provide any comments or suggestions you have on the vaccine distribution process.

### Rating Scale
This field asks for a numerical rating of your experience with the vaccine distribution process.

* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, *): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 to rate your experience.

### Date
Enter the date of your experience with the vaccine distribution process.

* **Date** (`date`, date, *): Enter the date of your experience.

### Time
Enter the time of your experience with the vaccine distribution process.

* **Time** (`time`, time, *): Enter the time of your experience.

### Email
Enter your email address for contact purposes.

* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your email address.

### Phone
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, *): Enter your phone number.

### Comments
This field is for any additional comments you may have.

* **Comments** (`comments`, note, *): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

### Name
Enter your name for identification purposes.

* **Name** (`name`, text, *): Enter your name.

### Title
Enter your title for identification purposes.

* **Title** (`title`, text, *): Enter your title.

### Department
Enter the department you are from.

* **Department** (`department`, text, *): Enter your department.

### Location
Enter your location for reference purposes.

* **Location** (`location`, text, *): Enter your location.

### Role
Enter your role for reference purposes.

* **Role** (`role`, text, *): Enter your role.

### Vaccine Dose
Enter the vaccine dose you administered.

* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`, number, *): Enter the number of vaccine doses you administered.

### Vaccine Type
Enter the type of vaccine you distributed.

* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, text, *): Enter the type of vaccine you distributed.
