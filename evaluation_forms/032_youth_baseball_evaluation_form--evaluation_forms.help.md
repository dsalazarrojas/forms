# Youth Baseball Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Baseball Evaluation Form is designed to assess a player's skills in different areas of baseball. It helps evaluators provide an objective and comprehensive evaluation of the player's abilities, which can inform coaching decisions, team selections, and development plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are an authorized evaluator and have a good understanding of the player's skills and abilities.
2. Review the form to understand the fields and their purpose.
3. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the player's name as it appears on their jersey or identification card.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the player's age as of the current year.
* **Position** (`position`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the player's primary position, such as Catcher, First Base, Pitcher, Second Base, Short Stop, or Third Base.
* **Batting Skill Level** (`batting_skill_level`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the player's batting skills, such as power, accuracy, or speed.
* **Fielding Skill Level** (`fielding_skill_level`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the player's fielding skills, such as range, agility, or quick reflexes.
* **Throwing Skill Level** (`throwing_skill_level`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the player's throwing skills, such as accuracy, velocity, or control.
* **Running Skill Level** (`running_skill_level`, text, optional)
	+ Describe the player's running skills, such as speed, endurance, or agility.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose the overall rating for the player, from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments or observations about the player's skills and abilities.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Evaluated By** (`evaluated_by`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select who completed the evaluation, such as Player Name, Coach Name, or Parent Name.
* **Evaluator Notes** (`evaluator_notes`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any notes or comments about the evaluation process or the player's skills and abilities.
* **Player Notes** (`player_notes`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any notes or comments from the player about their skills and abilities.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest when completing each field.
* Ensure you understand the player's skills and abilities before completing the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* Avoid duplication of effort and review previous evaluations for the same player.
