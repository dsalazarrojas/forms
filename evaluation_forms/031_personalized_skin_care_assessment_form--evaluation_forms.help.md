# Personalized Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your skin type, skin problems, and skincare habits and preferences. The collected data will be used to create personalized skincare recommendations tailored to your individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields (fields marked as "required").
2. For fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", select one or multiple options that best describe your skin type, skin problems, or skincare preferences.
3. Use the "text" fields to provide detailed information about your skin type, medical history, and current products.
4. Use the "note" field to provide any additional notes or comments about your skin care routine or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: 
  - (optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself or your skin care needs.
* **Skincare Questions**: 
  - (optional): Select all the skin problems you currently have, such as Dry, Oily, Combination, Acne, or Rosacea.
* **Medical Conditions**: 
  - (optional): Please mention any medical conditions you have that may affect your skin, such as eczema, psoriasis, or acne vulgaris.
* **Skin Tone**: 
  - (optional): Select the tone of your skin, which is Fair, Medium, Tanned, or Dark.
* **Skin Color**: 
  - (optional): Select the natural color of your skin, which is Fair, Medium, Tanned, or Dark.
* **Hair Color**: 
  - (optional): Select the natural color of your hair, which is Blonde, Brown, Black, or Red.
* **Eye Color**: 
  - (optional): Select the natural color of your eyes, which is Blue, Green, Brown, or Hazel.
* **Preferred Skin Type**: 
  - (optional): Select the skin type you prefer, which is Normal, Dry, Oily, or Combination.
* **Skin Problems**: 
  - (optional): Select all the skin problems you currently have, such as Acne, Aging, Hyper-Pigmentation, or Scarring.
* **Medical History**: 
  - (optional): Please mention any medical history or allergies you have that may affect your skin care.
* **Current Products**: 
  - (optional): Mention any skincare products you are currently using or have tried in the past.
* **Notes**: 
  - (optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your skin care routine or preferences.
* **Skincare Goals**: 
  - (optional): Select all the skincare goals you have, such as Anti-Aging, Hydration, Protection, Acne, or Brightening.
* **Preferred Treatments**: 
  - (optional): Select all the treatments you prefer, such as Chemical Peels, Microdermabrasion, Extractions, or Laser.
* **Skin Care Frequency**: 
  - (optional): Select how often you perform your skin care routine, which is Daily, 2-3 times a week, or 1-2 times a month.
* **Skin Care Method**: 
  - (optional): Select your preferred skin care method, which is Routine or Occasional.
* **Products**: 
  - (optional): Mention any additional products you would like to use or any products you would like to avoid in your skincare routine.
