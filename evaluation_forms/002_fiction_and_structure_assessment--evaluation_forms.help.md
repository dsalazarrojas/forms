# Fiction And Structure Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the structure and quality of a story in the fiction genre. It assesses the plot, characters, and writing style of a story and provides a comprehensive evaluation of its overall structure and relevance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Story Title** field with the title of the story you want to evaluate.
2. In the **Author Name** field, enter the name of the author of the story.
3. Select the genre of the story from the **Story Genre** dropdown menu.
4. Enter the number of **Chapters** in the story.
5. Describe the **Character Development** in the story in the provided text field.
6. Evaluate the **Plot Tension** in the story in the provided text field.
7. Describe the **Writing Style** of the story in the provided text field.
8. Assess the **Pacing** of the story in the provided text field.
9. Evaluate the **Plot Relevance** of the story in the provided text field.
10. Assess the **Character Relevance** of the story in the provided text field.
11. Write a brief **Overall** evaluation of the story in the provided text field.
12. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the story you want to evaluate.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the author of the story.
* **Story Genre** (`story_genre`, select_one, required: false): Select the genre of the story from the dropdown menu.
	+ Fantasy
	+ Science fiction
	+ Romance
	+ Mystery
	+ Horror
	+ Historical fiction
	+ Young Adult
	+ Thriller
	+ Comedy
	+ Drama
* **Chapters** (`chapter_count`, text, required: false): Enter the number of chapters in the story.
* **Character Development** (`character_depth`, text, required: false): Describe the character development in the story.
* **Plot Tension** (`plot_tension`, text, required: false): Evaluate the plot tension in the story.
* **Writing Style** (`writing_style`, text, required: false): Describe the writing style of the story.
* **Pacing** (`pacing`, text, required: false): Assess the pacing of the story.
* **Plot Relevance** (`plot_relevance`, text, required: false): Evaluate the plot relevance in the story.
* **Character Relevance** (`character_relevance`, text, required: false): Assess the character relevance in the story.
* **Overall** (`story_confidence`, note, required: false): Write a brief overall evaluation of the story.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click the submit button to complete the form.
