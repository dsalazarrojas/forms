# Online Wellness Evaluation Form Gym Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Wellness Evaluation Form Gym Template is a comprehensive form designed to help evaluate a person's overall well-being, specifically in a gym setting. This form is meant to gather information on food intake, medicine dosage, and exercise routines to provide insight into an individual's lifestyle choices and progress towards their fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Fill out the form in sections, starting from "General Information" to "Exercise Routines 7".
* For each section, select or enter the relevant information based on your personal experiences or preferences.
* You don't need to fill out any field that is not relevant to you.
* Be as accurate and honest as possible when providing information about your food intake and medicine dosage.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, you can always reach out to your gym staff for guidance.
