# Screen For Child Anxiety Related Disorders 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a comprehensive evaluation tool used to assess a child's anxiety levels by examining their behavioral and symptomatic indicators. It helps caregivers, mental health professionals, or educators identify potential anxiety-related disorders in children.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and respond with the corresponding option(s) that best describe the child's behavior or symptoms.
2. For select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. Ensure you answer each question truthfully to provide accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist**: This is a general question to determine if the child exhibits any anxiety-related disorder symptoms.
	+ **Has Child Anxiety Disorder**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you've observed any symptoms or behaviors that may indicate an anxiety-related disorder.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 0: Difficulty Focusing**: If the child has difficulty paying attention or concentrating, select all that apply.
	+ **Difficulty Focusing**: Select 'Yes' if the child has trouble focusing on tasks or activities.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 0: Fear of Social Situations**: If the child exhibits fear or anxiety in social situations, select all that apply.
	+ **Fear of Social Situations**: Select 'Yes' if the child avoids social interactions or exhibits anxiety in social settings.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 1: Fear of Unknowns**: If the child shows fear or anxiety towards unknown or uncertain situations, select all that apply.
	+ **Fear of Unknowns**: Select 'Yes' if the child has a fear of unexpected events or situations.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 2: Fear of Loss or Panic**: If the child exhibits fear or anxiety when faced with a loss or panic, select all that apply.
	+ **Fear of Loss or Panic**: Select 'Yes' if the child has a fear of losing control or experiencing panic attacks.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 3: Difficulty Sleeping**: If the child has trouble sleeping or experiences difficulty falling asleep, select all that apply.
	+ **Difficulty Sleeping**: Select 'Yes' if the child has trouble falling or staying asleep.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 4: Difficulty Feeling Safe**: If the child has difficulty feeling safe or secure, select all that apply.
	+ **Difficulty Feeling Safe**: Select 'Yes' if the child exhibits fear or anxiety about their physical or emotional well-being.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 5: Difficulty Controlling Emotions**: If the child has difficulty managing their emotions, select all that apply.
	+ **Difficulty Controlling Emotions**: Select 'Yes' if the child has trouble regulating their emotions or experiences frequent mood swings.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 6: Difficulty Regulating Emotions**: If the child has difficulty calming down or managing their emotions, select all that apply.
	+ **Difficulty Regulating Emotions**: Select 'Yes' if the child has trouble calming down after experiencing emotional arousal.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 7: Fear of Mis**: If the child exhibits fear or anxiety towards mistakes or failures, select all that apply.
	+ **Fear of Mis**: Select 'Yes' if the child is overly critical of themselves or others.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 8: Fear of Social Situations 0**: If the child exhibits fear or anxiety in social situations, select all that apply.
	+ **Fear of Social Situations 0**: Select 'Yes' if the child has difficulty interacting with others or avoids social situations.
* **Child Anxiety Disorder Checklist 9: Anxiety Disorder**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you've observed any symptoms or behaviors that may indicate an anxiety-related disorder.
* **Notes**: This is a free-text field for any additional information or comments about the child's behavior or symptoms.
