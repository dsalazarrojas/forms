# Game Release Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from players about a game release. Please answer the questions to help us improve our game.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Select the version of the game you played.
3. Choose the platform on which you played the game.
4. Share what you enjoyed about the game in the "what_did_you_enjoy" field.
5. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the "suggestions" field.
6. Rate your experience with the game in the "rating" field.
7. Add any comments about your experience.
8. If you would like to be contacted for more information, please enter your email address.
9. If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **player_name (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your account or profile.
* **version (select_multiple)**: Select the version of the game you played (alpha, beta, release).
* **platform (select_one)**: Choose the platform on which you played the game (console, PC, mobile, web).
* **what_did_you_enjoy (text)**: Share what features or mechanics you enjoyed about the game. This helps us know what to build on next.
* **suggestions (text)**: Suggest new features, mechanics, or content you'd like to see added to the game.
* **rating (number)**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **comments (text)**: Add any additional comments about your experience with the game.
* **email (email)**: If you'd like to be contacted for more information, please enter your email address here.
* **phone (text)**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.

## Tips

* Please be honest and open in your feedback.
* If you have any technical issues, please contact us at [support email].
* We will use your feedback to improve the game and make it more enjoyable for you and all players.
