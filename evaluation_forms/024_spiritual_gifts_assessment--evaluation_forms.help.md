# Spiritual Gifts Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form explores personal experiences that might indicate which spiritual gifts you are most and least likely to have. It's designed to help you gain insight into your spiritual growth, guidance, comfort, and empowerment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question with the frequency of your experience. Select one of the options:
	* Often
	* Sometimes
	* Rarely
	* Never
2. Take your time to answer each question thoughtfully and honestly.
3. Move on to the next question when you're ready.
4. If you're unsure about a question or need clarification, ask for help.
