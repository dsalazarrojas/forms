# Food Defense Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Defense Assessment Template is designed to help food safety inspectors or auditors evaluate a food defense system or process within a facility. This form is intended to collect relevant information about the food item being assessed, the facility being inspected, and other details such as location and date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the program or initiative being assessed in the **Program Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the facility being inspected in the **Facility Name** field.
3. Choose the **Location** of the facility from the provided options (North, South, East, West).
4. Enter the date of the assessment in the **Assessment Date** field.
5. Select the **Food Item** being assessed.
6. Choose the type of **Contamination Type** present (Physical, Chemical, Biological).
7. Enter your **Assessment Results**, including any observations or findings.
8. Provide **Comments** about the assessment, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, required): Enter the name of the program or initiative being assessed.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, required): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Location** (`location`, required): Choose the location of the facility from the options provided (North, South, East, West).
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Food Item** (`food_item`, required): Select the food item being assessed.
* **Contamination Type** (`contamination_type`, required): Choose the type of contamination present (Physical, Chemical, Biological).
* **Assessment Results** (`assessment_results`, required): Enter the results of the assessment, including any observations or findings.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the assessment.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field completely and accurately.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled.
- Take note of any specific guidance or regulations related to food defense that apply to your facility or program.

Note: This template is for general use and should be reviewed and adapted according to specific regulatory requirements and guidelines of your facility or organization.
