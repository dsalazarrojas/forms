# Employee Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track employee complaints, providing a structured way for employees to report issues or incidents within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and title in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have permission to report complaints to your manager in the "Manager ID" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the complaint in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the date and time the complaint was submitted using the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields.
5. If you are reporting a complaint against another employee, enter their name in the "Employee Against" field.
6. Specify the reason for the complaint in the "Reason for Complaint" field.
7. Describe the actions taken to resolve the issue in the "Actions Taken" field.
8. Enter the current resolution status of the complaint in the "Resolution Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification badge.
* **Employee Against** (`employee_against`, text, optional): If you are reporting a complaint against another employee, enter their name here.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" to indicate that you have permission to report complaints to your manager. 
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including any relevant dates, times, or circumstances.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the complaint.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the complaint.
* **Reason for Complaint** (`reason_for_complaint`, text, optional): Specify the reason why you are filing the complaint.
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, optional): Describe any actions you have taken to try to resolve the issue.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, text, optional): Enter the current resolution status of the complaint.
* **Employee Complaint Form Label** (`employee_complaint_form_label`, text, hidden): This field is not visible to the user.
