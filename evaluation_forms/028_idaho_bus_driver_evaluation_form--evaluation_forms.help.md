# idaho_bus_driver_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The idaho_bus_driver_evaluation_form is a comprehensive evaluation form designed to assess the suitability of a bus driver for a particular evaluation or assessment. This form helps collect information about the driver's qualifications, vehicle condition, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure that you have all necessary information about the bus driver at hand.
* Select the relevant fields and fill them in according to the provided instructions.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you have included all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the bus driver being evaluated.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the bus driver's driver's license number.
* **Driver License State** (`driver_license_state`, `select_one`, optional): Select the state that issues the bus driver's driver's license.
* **Driver Experience** (`driver_experience_years`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years the bus driver has been driving.
* **Driving History** (`driving_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the overall driving history of the bus driver (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of vehicle the bus driver operates (e.g., bus, van, etc.).
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Select the overall condition of the vehicle (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Ford, Toyota, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Camry, etc.).
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, optional): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Accident History** (`accident_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether the bus driver has an accident history (True, False, N/A).
* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`vehicle_maintenance`, `select_one`, optional): Select the overall maintenance condition of the vehicle (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Driver Signature** (`driver_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter the bus driver's signature.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that the information entered is accurate and complete.
* If a field is optional, only enter information if it is relevant and applicable to the evaluation.
