# Buddy Program Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants and Buddies in the Buddy Program to assess the effectiveness and overall satisfaction of the program. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and strengths of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name and role in the program.
2. Answer the questions about your participation duration, relationship effectiveness, communication frequency, and types of support provided.
3. Evaluate the program's effectiveness by selecting the most accurate answer from the provided options.
4. Provide any additional feedback on what you think the program could improve on.
5. Finally, indicate your overall satisfaction with the program and whether you'd like to continue participating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as a participant or Buddy in the program.
* **Your Role in Program** (`participant_role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the program (Buddy or Mentee, Program Coordinator, etc.).
* **Name of Buddy Program** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you participated in.
* **How Long Have You Participated** (`participation_duration`, text, optional): If applicable, estimate the duration of your participation in the program (in months or weeks).
* **How Effective Was the Buddy Relationship** (`relationship_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Evaluate the effectiveness of the buddy relationship, choosing from the options:
	+ Extremely Effective
	+ Very Effective
	+ Moderately Effective
	+ Slightly Effective
	+ Not Effective
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you interacted with your buddy or program team:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ As Needed
* **Types of Support Provided** (`support_received`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply from the list:
	+ Professional Development
	+ Technical Training
	+ Emotional Support
	+ Career Guidance
	+ Networking
	+ Problem-Solving
* **Were Program Goals Achieved** (`goals_achieved`, select_one, required): Select the most accurate answer from the options:
	+ All Goals Met
	+ Most Goals Met
	+ Some Goals Met
	+ Few Goals Met
	+ No Goals Met
* **Growth in Confidence or Skills** (`confidence_growth`, select_one, required): Evaluate the growth in confidence or skills you experienced:
	+ Significant Growth
	+ Moderate Growth
	+ Slight Growth
	+ No Growth
	+ Decline
* **Would You Recommend This Program to Others** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of recommendation for the program:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Uncertain
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **What Were the Program Strengths** (`program_strengths`, text, optional): Provide any comments on what you think the program's strengths are.
* **What Could Be Improved** (`program_improvements`, text, optional): Share any feedback on what you think the program could improve on.
* **What Additional Support Would Help** (`additional_support`, text, optional): Suggest any additional support you think would be beneficial.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the program:
	+ 1 - Very Dissatisfied
	+ 2 - Dissatisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 4 - Satisfied
	+ 5 - Very Satisfied
* **Would You Like to Continue in the Program** (`continue_program`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in continuing to participate in the program:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, probably
	+ Undecided
	+ No, probably not
	+ No, definitely not
