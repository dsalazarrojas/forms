# Kitchenette Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kitchenette Evaluation Form is designed to help kitchen staff evaluate the usefulness and discard rates of kitchen items. It's a crucial tool to collect data on items that are useful and not useful, and any comments or feedback about them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your feedback and evaluation of the kitchen items.
2. Answer each field as it applies to your experience with the kitchen item.
3. If a field is not applicable, please mark it as such or leave it blank.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Kitchen Item ID (1)** (`kitchen_item_id`, number, required/optional): This field is the unique ID of the kitchen item you're evaluating.
* **Useful Kitchen Item (2)** (`useful_kitchen_item_id`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes of the kitchen items you find useful.
* **Discarded Kitchen Item (3)** (`discarded_kitchen_item_id`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes of the kitchen items you discard.
* **Evaluation Notes (4)** (`evaluation_notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the kitchen items.
* **Evaluation Date (5)** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time (6)** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comments (7)** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the kitchen items.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when evaluating the kitchen items.
* If you're unsure about a field, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Make sure to save your work regularly while filling out the form.
