# Safety Orientation Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the safety orientation training provided to employees. It helps ensure that employees have received the necessary information to maintain a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer "Training Completed" with whether you have completed the safety orientation training.
2. Answer "Was the safety orientation training effective" with your evaluation of the training's effectiveness.
3. Select "Safety Orientation" as either "Yes" or "No".
4. Provide feedback about your experience with the safety orientation training in the "Feedback" text box.
5. If you have any additional comments about the training, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" text box.
6. Enter the training date in the "Training Date" field.
7. Select "Attended Training" as either "Yes" or "No".
8. Enter your Employer Name, Employee Name, and Employee Title.
9. If you have any comments about the training, please provide them in the "Employee Comments" text box.
10. If you have any comments from your supervisor, please provide them in the "Supervisor Comments" text box.
11. If you have any comments from the safety manager, please provide them in the "Safety Manager Comments" text box.
12. Enter the date in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Completed**: Check this box if you have completed the safety orientation training.
* **Was the safety orientation training effective**: Select "Yes" if you found the training effective and "No" if you did not.
* **Safety Orientation**: Select "Yes" if you received the necessary information and "No" if you did not.
* **Feedback**: Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the training.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments you have about the training, if any.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you completed the training.
* **Attended Training**: Select "Yes" if you attended the training and "No" if you did not.
* **Employer Name**: Enter your employer's name.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Employee Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Title**: Enter your supervisor's title, if applicable.
* **Safety Manager Name**: Enter the name of the safety manager, if applicable.
* **Training Facilitator**: Enter the name of the training facilitator, if applicable.
* **Training Facilitator Title**: Enter the title of the training facilitator, if applicable.
* **Evaluated By**: Enter the name of the person evaluating the training, if applicable.
* **Evaluated Title**: Enter the title of the person evaluating the training, if applicable.
* **Training Facilitator Comments**: Enter any comments from the training facilitator, if applicable.
* **Employee Comments**: Enter any comments from the employee, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Enter any comments from the supervisor, if applicable.
* **Safety Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from the safety manager, if applicable.
* **Date**: Enter the current date.

Note: The "Additional Comments", "Training Facilitator Comments", "Employee Comments", "Supervisor Comments", and "Safety Manager Comments" fields are optional and only required if you have comments to provide.
