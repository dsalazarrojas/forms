# Student Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate a student's presentation, providing a structured way to assess their performance in different areas and provide an overall score.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the teacher who evaluated the presentation.
2. Enter the student's ID number.
3. Enter the date of the presentation.
4. Enter the time spent during the presentation.
5. Enter the scores for the presentation based on the provided criteria.
6. Enter any feedback or comments regarding the presentation.
7. Enter the overall score for the presentation.
8. Finally, enter the date of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Form** (`student_presentation_evaluation_form`, text, optional): This is the main evaluation section where you can enter your observations, comments, and scores for the presentation.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name and title/position as the evaluating teacher.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number for identification purposes.
* **Presentation Date** (`presentation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the presentation.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Enter the amount of time spent during the presentation.
* **Score 1** (`score_1`, number, optional): Enter a score for the first aspect of the presentation (e.g., content, organization, etc.).
* **Score 2** (`score_2`, number, optional): Enter a score for the second aspect of the presentation (e.g., delivery, clarity, etc.).
* **Score 3** (`score_3`, number, optional): Enter a score for the third aspect of the presentation (e.g., creativity, confidence, etc.).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any comments, suggestions, or feedback you have for the student.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, optional): Enter the overall score for the presentation, based on your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
