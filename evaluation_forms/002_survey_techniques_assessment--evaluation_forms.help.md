# Survey Techniques Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate your knowledge and understanding of survey techniques and methods. It is an essential tool for anyone involved in research, data collection, or data analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and understand what is being asked.
2. Select the most appropriate answer for each question.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
4. Submit your answers to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the primary purpose of a survey, and how does it relate to data collection?** (`question_1`, text, required)
	+ This question is about the main objective of a survey. A survey is a data collection method used to gather information from a sample population. It is essential to understand the primary purpose of a survey to ensure that it collects the right data.
* **What are some common survey methods used in research?** (`question_2`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks about the different methods used to conduct surveys. Common methods include face-to-face interviews, phone or mobile surveys, online surveys, and postal surveys.
* **How do you calculate the response rate for a survey?** (`question_3`, text, required)
	+ This question is about calculating the response rate, which is the percentage of participants who respond to a survey. It is essential to calculate the response rate to understand the survey's validity and reliability.
* **What is the difference between a Likert scale and an ordinal scale?** (`question_4`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is about the difference between a Likert scale and an ordinal scale. A Likert scale is a scale used to measure attitudes, while an ordinal scale is a scale used to order or rank items.
* **How do you handle missing data in a survey?** (`question_5`, text, required)
	+ This question is about handling missing data in a survey. Missing data can be a significant problem in survey research. It is essential to know how to handle it to ensure the survey's accuracy.
* **What is the importance of pilot testing a survey before distribution?** (`question_6`, select_one, required)
	+ This question is about the importance of pilot testing a survey before distribution. Pilot testing helps to identify any errors or issues with the survey before it is distributed to participants.
* **How do you calculate the mean and median of a dataset?** (`question_7`, text, required)
	+ This question is about calculating the mean and median of a dataset. The mean is the average value, while the median is the middle value.
* **What is the purpose of a survey pilot study?** (`question_8`, text, required)
	+ This question is about the purpose of a survey pilot study. A pilot study is a small-scale trial of a survey to test its effectiveness and identify any issues.
* **How do you ensure survey questions are clear and unbiased?** (`question_9`, text, required)
	+ This question is about ensuring survey questions are clear and unbiased. Clear questions help to minimize confusion and ensure that participants respond accurately.
* **What is the difference between a survey and an interview?** (`question_10`, text, required)
	+ This question is about the difference between a survey and an interview. A survey is a self-reporting method, while an interview is an interactive method.
* **How do you collect sensitive or personal data in a survey?** (`question_11`, text, required)
	+ This question is about collecting sensitive or personal data in a survey. It is essential to handle sensitive data with care to protect participant privacy.
* **How do you analyze the results of a survey?** (`question_12`, text, required)
	+ This question is about analyzing the results of a survey. It is essential to analyze the data to understand the survey's findings.
* **What is the role of informed consent in survey research?** (`question_13`, text, required)
	+ This question is about the role of informed consent in survey research. Informed consent is essential to ensure that participants understand the survey's purpose and risks.
* **How do you ensure the privacy of survey participants?** (`question_14`, text, required)
	+ This question is about ensuring the privacy of survey participants. It is essential to protect participant privacy to build trust and avoid bias.
