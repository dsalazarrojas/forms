# Energy Efficiency Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Program Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from participants in the Energy Efficiency Program. It helps us understand their experience with the program and gather information on their satisfaction and performance improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "participant name" field.
2. Type your program name in the "program name" field.
3. If you wish, rate your satisfaction level with the program on a scale of 5 options: Very Low, Low, Neutral, High, Very High.
4. Select all performance improvement areas you experienced with the program by choosing multiple options from the list: Cost, Time, Productivity, Environment, Satisfaction.
5. If you have any additional comments, please type them in the "open ended comments" field.
6. Provide your email address and phone number if you wish for us to contact you.
7. Finally, input your date and time of completion, and the date when the program started and ended (if applicable). 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Energy Efficiency Program Survey**: This is the title of the form, which should not be filled in.
* **participant name**: A field where you enter your name as a participant.
* **program name**: A field where you type the name of the program you participated in.
* **rating score**: A field where you rate your satisfaction level with the program on a scale of 1-5 (Very Low to Very High).
* **satisfaction level**: A field where you choose your satisfaction level with the program on a scale of 5 options (Very Low, Low, Neutral, High, Very High).
* **performance improvement**: A field where you choose multiple options from a list of areas where you experienced performance improvement (Cost, Time, Productivity, Environment, Satisfaction).
* **open ended comments**: A field where you can provide any additional comments on your experience with the program.
* **email**: A field where you can enter your email address if you wish for us to contact you.
* **phone**: A field where you can enter your phone number if you wish for us to contact you.
* **date**: A field where you input the date when you completed the form.
* **time**: A field where you input the time when you completed the form.
* **program start date**: A field where you input the date when the program started (if applicable).
* **program end date**: A field where you input the date when the program ended (if applicable).
