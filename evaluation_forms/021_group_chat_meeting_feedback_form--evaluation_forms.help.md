# Group Chat Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about group chat meetings, aiming to identify areas for improvement and enhance the quality of future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, based on your experience.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, or leave blank if not applicable.
3. Provide a brief explanation or answer for questions that have a text field.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields ( Participant Name and Participant Email).
5. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your organization's records.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your organization's records.
* **Meeting Leadership** (`meeting_leadership`, text, required): Describe the leadership style of the meeting. Please be specific and provide details.
* **Meeting Attendance** (`meeting_attendance`, select_one, false): If you attended the meeting, select "Yes" to indicate that you attended.
* **Participant Survey** (`participant_survey`, select_multiple, false): Select all that apply regarding your satisfaction with the participant experience.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_multiple, false): Select all that apply regarding the communication style of the meeting.
* **Meeting Effectiveness** (`meeting_effectiveness`, select_one, false): Rate the effectiveness of the meeting.
* **Meeting Preparedness** (`meeting`, select_one, false): Rate the preparedness of the meeting.
* **Meeting Facilitator** (`meeting_facilitator`, text, false): Describe the facilitator's performance. Please be specific and provide details.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, false): Provide an overall rating for the meeting.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, select_multiple, false): Select all that apply regarding your overall experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, false): Leave any additional comments or suggestions regarding the meeting.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, false): Please enter a numerical rating for the meeting.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, false): Select the scale used for your rating.
* **Meeting Length** (`meeting_length`, select_one, false): Rate the length of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, false): Please enter the time of the meeting.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your organization's records.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your organization's records.
