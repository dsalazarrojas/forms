# Performance Review Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback and evaluation from managers on their team members' performance. It helps identify areas of improvement, recognize strengths, and set goals for the next evaluation period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee(s) you are evaluating.
2. Answer the questions on the form based on your evaluation of the employee's performance.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Form Fields**: This section is for the employee to fill out and is not editable for the manager. Please do not attempt to complete this section.
* **Manager Form Fields**: This section is for the manager to fill out and is used to evaluate the employee's performance.
* **Performance Rating**: Rate the employee's performance based on your evaluation (e.g., 1-5 or 1-4 with specific criteria).
* **Employee Form Questions**: This section is for the employee to answer questions about their own performance. Please do not attempt to complete this section.
* **Manager Form Questions**: This section is for the manager to answer questions about the employee's performance. Please do not attempt to complete this section.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the employee's performance.

## Tips
* Be specific and constructive in your feedback.
* Focus on behavioral and performance-based feedback rather than personality or personal preferences.
* Keep your comments concise and relevant.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and clarity.
