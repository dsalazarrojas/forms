# Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sunday School Teacher Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance and effectiveness of Sunday School teachers in providing quality education to their students. This form helps teachers reflect on their teaching methods, lesson plans, and interactions with students, allowing for growth and improvement in the teaching environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review the form's pages and understand the fields that are required for each question.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form before starting to fill it out.
3. Fill out the form with honesty and accuracy, providing specific examples or anecdotes if possible.
4. Review your answers and make sure they are clear and concise.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lesson Plan** (1): Please provide a brief description of the lesson plan you used during the Sunday School session. This should include the topic, objectives, and any notable activities or discussions.
* **Lesson Plan Rating** (2): Rate the quality of your lesson plan, using the scale provided (excellent, satisfactory, or needs improvement).
* **Teaching Skills** (3): Select the teaching skills you used during the Sunday School session, choosing from excellent, satisfactory, or needs improvement.
* **Any Other Feedback** (4): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your teaching experience, such as successes or challenges encountered.
* **Sunday School Teacher** (5): Select the name of the Sunday School teacher being evaluated.
* **Sunday School Student** (6): Indicate whether the Sunday School student participated in the session or not.
* **Sunday School Class** (7): Choose the Sunday School class the session was conducted with.
* **Date** (8): Enter the date of the Sunday School session.
* **Start Time** (9): If applicable, enter the start time of the Sunday School session.
* **End Time** (10): If applicable, enter the end time of the Sunday School session.
