# Thesis Defense Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thesis Defense Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from committee members on the quality and presentation of a thesis defense. This feedback is crucial for improving the defense's overall quality and helping the presenter grow. By filling out this form, committee members can provide constructive comments and suggestions, ensuring that the defense meets the expected standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form to begin.
2.  Select the committee member who attended the defense (if applicable).
3.  Choose your evaluation on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4.  Provide specific feedback about the defense presentation.
5.  Leave any additional comments or notes about the defense.
6.  Enter the date and time of the defense.
7.  Enter your email address (if you wish to receive follow-up questions or comments).
8.  Leave any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Defense Feedback** (required): Provide a brief summary of your overall impression about the defense. What were your expectations, and how did the presentation meet or exceed them?
* **Committee Member** (optional): If you are a member of the thesis defense committee, select yourself from the dropdown list to help the presenter identify specific feedback from each member.
* **Evaluation Scale** (optional): Choose your evaluation of the defense on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback** (optional): Provide specific, constructive feedback on the defense presentation. Suggest areas for improvement, and offer concrete ideas for future development.
* **Comments** (optional): Leave any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the defense.
* **Date of Defense** (optional): Enter the date on which the defense took place.
* **Time of Defense** (optional): Enter the time on which the defense took place.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address if you wish to receive follow-up questions or comments from the presenter.
* **Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number if you have any follow-up questions or comments.
* **Note** (optional): Leave any additional notes or comments you may have about the defense.
