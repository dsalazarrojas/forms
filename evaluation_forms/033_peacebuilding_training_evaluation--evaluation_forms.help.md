# Peacebuilding Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback and evaluation tool designed to gather information from participants after a peacebuilding training session. It helps us understand your experience, thoughts, and suggestions for the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Share your thoughts and feedback about the training session in the text field.
* Rate your experience with the training using the numerical input field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (1)**: This field is for your personal identification. Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email (3)**: This field is for contact purposes. Enter your email address to enable us to reach out to you.
* **Phone (4)**: This field is also for contact purposes. Enter your phone number to enable us to reach out to you.
* **Feedback (5)**: This field is for sharing your thoughts and suggestions about the training session. Please write your feedback in a clear and concise manner.
* **Rating (6)**: This field is for evaluating your experience with the training. Use the numerical input field to rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in helping us improve the training session.
