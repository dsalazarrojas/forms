# internship_experience_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Internship Experience Evaluation Form** is a tool designed to gather detailed information about an individual's past internship experience. This form helps to collect data on the company, department, dates, supervisor, work location, job title, evaluation score, and feedback provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Enter relevant information for each field.
3. Make sure to fill out one form for each unique internship experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **internship_company**: Enter the name of the company where you completed your internship.
* **internship_department**: Enter the name of the department where you worked during the internship.
* **supervisor_name**: Enter the name of your internship supervisor.
* **supervisor_title**: Enter your supervisor's title or position.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of your internship in date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your internship in date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **work_location**: Enter the location where you worked during the internship.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title or position during the internship.
* **evaluation_score**: Enter an evaluation score (e.g., 1-5) based on your performance during the internship.
* **feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments about your internship experience.
