# Digital Wellness Research Impact Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the impact of digital wellness programs on participants' habits, wellbeing, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each page carefully, ensuring you have answered all required and optional questions.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Digital Habits
* **Digital Habits** (`digital_habits`, text, optional): Please describe your current digital habits, including any changes you've made to your online behavior in the past month.

### 2. Wellbeing Ratings
* **Wellbeing Ratings** (`wellbeing_ratings`, text, optional): Rate your overall wellbeing on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent".

### 3. Feedback Comments
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or feedback about the digital wellness program or your experience as a participant.

### 4. Wellbeing Changes
* **Wellbeing Changes** (`wellbeing_changes`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to describe any changes you've observed in your wellbeing since participating in the program:
    + Increased
    + No Change
    + Decreased
