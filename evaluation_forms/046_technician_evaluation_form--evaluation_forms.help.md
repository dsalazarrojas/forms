# Technician Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technician Evaluation Form is a tool used to evaluate the performance of a technician after a job has been completed. This form helps assess the quality of the work, communication skills, and overall experience with the technician.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the technician's name as it appears on their ID badge in the "Technician's Name" field.
2. Enter the technician's ID number in the "Technician's ID Number" field.
3. Select the date of the visit from the calendar in the "Date of Visit" field.
4. Rate the technician's workmanship, skill, effort, and communication skills using the provided options in the "Rating" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician's Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Enter the technician's name as it appears on their ID badge. 
* **Technician's ID Number** (`technician_id_number`, text, required): Enter the technician's ID number.
* **Date of Visit** (`rating_date`, date, required): Select the date of the visit from the calendar.
* **Rating - Workmanship** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the work done by the technician. You may choose from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Technician's Skill Level** (`rating_technician_skill`, select_one, optional): Rate the technician's skill level in performing the job, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Technician's Effort** (`rating_technician_effort`, select_one, optional): Rate the technician's effort in performing the job, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Communication Skills** (`rating_communication_technician`, select_one, optional): Rate the technician's communication skills, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when rating the technician's workmanship, skill, effort, and communication skills.
* Select only one rating for each category.
* If there are any issues with the form or you are unsure about how to complete it, contact your supervisor or the person in charge of the technician's evaluation.

Note: The form is divided into different sections or "pages". Each section is labeled as "form_header", "rating", "rating_technician_skill", "rating_technician_effort", "rating_communication_technician". These sections are not editable and serve as a separator between different fields. 

This note is provided for the user's understanding, and not for the user to edit the form content.
