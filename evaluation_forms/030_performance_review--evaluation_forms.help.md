# Performance Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review form is designed to help managers and supervisors evaluate an employee's work performance over a specific period. It's meant to be a helpful tool to identify areas of improvement and provide feedback for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the employee's name and the review period in the corresponding fields.
2. Rate the employee's performance using the scale provided, and provide comments for the evaluation.
3. List any specific performance improvement areas where the employee can work on to improve.
4. Provide an overall rating of the employee's performance during the review period.
5. Review and submit the form for accurate and fair evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)**: Please fill out the employee's full name as it appears on their ID card or in our HR system.
* **Review Period (2)**: Enter the dates of the review period, using the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Rating (3)**: Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Comments (4)**: Provide any comments or feedback for the evaluation, such as strengths and weaknesses.
* **Recommendations (5)**: List any recommendations for improvement, such as training or additional resources needed.
* **Evaluation Date (6)**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **Rating Scale (7)**: Select the rating for the employee's performance using the provided scale.
* **Evaluation Comments (8)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback for the evaluation.
* **Overall Rating (9)**: Provide an overall rating of the employee's performance during the review period.
* **Performance Improvement Areas (10)**: Select any areas where the employee needs to improve, such as Communication, Time Management, Leadership, Teamwork, Problem Solving, Adaptability, or Organization.
