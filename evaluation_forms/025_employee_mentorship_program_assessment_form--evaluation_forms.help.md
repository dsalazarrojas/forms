# Employee Mentorship Program Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees who participate in the mentorship program. The goal is to evaluate the program's effectiveness, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights to organizers and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
3. If you have a manager assigned, enter their email in the "Manager Email" field.
4. Rate your experience of the mentorship program using the "How would you rate the experience of your mentor in this program?" select multiple options.
5. In the "Comments on your experience" field, provide detailed comments about your experience.
6. Write recommendations for improvement in the "Recommendations for improvement" field.
7. In the "Employee 1 Comments" field, enter any comments from employee 1 (the form does not specify who employee 1 is; this field might be redundant or a placeholder).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee participating in the mentorship program.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager who is part of the program.
* **Manager Email**: If you have a manager assigned, enter their email address.
* **Manager Email**: This field seems to be a duplicate or a placeholder; please disregard it.
* **How would you rate the experience of your mentor in this program?**:
	+ Select one or more of the options:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Comments on your experience**: Enter detailed comments about your experience in the mentorship program.
* **Recommendations for improvement**: Provide suggestions on how the program can be improved.
* **Employee 1 Comments**: This field is likely redundant or a placeholder; please enter anything you like.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* Ensure you provide specific examples and feedback about your experience.
* Use clear and concise language in your comments and recommendations.
* This form will help the organization improve the mentorship program; your input is invaluable.
