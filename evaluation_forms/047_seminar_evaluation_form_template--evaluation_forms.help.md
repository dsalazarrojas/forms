# Seminar Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees about the seminar they attended. It's essential to provide accurate and honest opinions to help organizers and speakers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question as accurately as possible based on your experience at the seminar.
2.  If you don't have an opinion or answer to a question, it’s okay to leave it blank or mark it as N/A.
3.  Make sure to complete all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Content (seminar_content)** (`text`, optional): Please describe the seminar's content, including its relevance to the topic, clarity, and overall understanding.
* **Presentation Quality (presentation_quality)** (`select_one`, optional): How would you rate the seminar's presentation quality? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **Overall Effectiveness (overall_effectiveness)** (`select_one`, optional): Did the seminar achieve its intended goals or meet your expectations? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Seminar Organization (seminar_organization)** (`text`, optional): Provide feedback on the seminar's organization, including the layout, navigation, and overall flow.
* **Content (content)** (`text`, optional): Describe the seminar's content, including its relevance, accuracy, and usefulness.
* **Speaker (speaker)** (`text`, optional): Please provide feedback about the speaker's performance, including their communication skills, knowledge, and engagement.
* **Time Management (time_management)** (`text`, optional): Describe the seminar's time management, including punctuality, pace, and overall duration.
* **Materials (materials)** (`text`, optional): Provide feedback on the seminar's materials, including their quality, relevance, and usefulness.
* **Interaction (interaction)** (`select_multiple`, optional): How would you rate the interaction between the seminar's organizers and attendees? Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Value for Money (value_for_money)** (`text`, optional): Please describe how you feel about the seminar's value for money, including its cost, quality, and overall worth.
