# Formal Communication Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Formal Communication Skills Assessment is designed to evaluate an individual's ability to communicate effectively in formal settings. This form helps evaluators assess verbal, written, and nonverbal communication skills, as well as adaptability to different communication styles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate communication skills you wish to evaluate (e.g., Verbal, Written, Nonverbal, Visual).
2. Rate your level of proficiency for each selected skill on a scale of 1-5.
3. Provide comments on your current level of proficiency and areas for improvement.
4. Choose your preferred submission method (Online, Email, In-person).
5. Enter your contact information (Email, Phone, Name, Department, Location).
6. Select the date and time of the evaluation.
7. Enter the evaluation details (Form title, Evaluation date, Evaluation method).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Introduction (Text)
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This field is for you to briefly introduce yourself and the context of the evaluation. Please provide a short overview of your communication skills and the purpose of this form.

### 2. Communication Skills (Select One)
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, optional): Select the type of formal communication skills you wish to evaluate (Verbal, Written, Nonverbal, Visual, Other). This will help focus the evaluation on the specific skill area.

### 3. How Important is this Skill? (Number)
* **How Important is this Skill?** (`body`, number, optional): Rate the importance of the selected communication skill on a scale of 1-5. A higher score indicates a higher level of importance.

### 4-8. Proficiency Ratings (Number)
* **Rating 1** (`rating1`, number, optional): Rate your current level of proficiency for the selected communication skill on a scale of 1-5.

* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or insights about your current level of proficiency and areas for improvement.
