# Training Need Analysis Form Engro - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Need Analysis Form Engro is a tool used to gather information about an employee's tasks, job performance, and potential training needs. This form will help in identifying areas where an employee may require additional support or training to perform their job tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all fields marked as required.
2. For each field, provide a brief description or answer.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it if you feel it is not relevant to the employee's current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required): This field is used to gather general information about the employee.
* **Tasks Section 1** (`tasks_section_1`, text, required): Provide a list of tasks that the employee is currently performing.
* **Tasks Section 1 Description** (`tasks_section_1_description`, text, required): Describe the tasks listed in Tasks Section 1 in more detail.
* **Task 1 Description** (`task_1_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the first task.
* **Task 1 Importance** (`task_1_importance`, number, required): Estimate the importance of Task 1 on a scale of 1-10.
* **Task 1 Frequency** (`task_1_frequency`, number, required): Estimate the frequency of Task 1, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Tasks Section 2** (`tasks_section_2`, text, required): Provide a list of additional tasks that the employee is currently performing.
* **Task 2 Description** (`task_2_description`, text, required): Describe the tasks listed in Tasks Section 2.
* **Task 2 Importance** (`task_2_importance`, number, required): Estimate the importance of Task 2 on a scale of 1-10.
* **Task 2 Frequency** (`task_2_frequency`, number, required): Estimate the frequency of Task 2, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Tasks Section 3** (`tasks_section_3`, text, required): Provide a list of additional tasks that the employee is currently performing.
* **Task 3 Description** (`task_3_description`, text, required): Describe the tasks listed in Tasks Section 3.
* **Task 3 Importance** (`task_3_importance`, number, required): Estimate the importance of Task 3 on a scale of 1-10.
* **Task 3 Frequency** (`task_3_frequency`, number, required): Estimate the frequency of Task 3, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Views and Thoughts** (`views_and_thoughts`, text, required): Share any views or thoughts you have about the employee's performance and potential training needs.
* **Employee Views** (`employee_views`, text, optional): If you are an employee, share your views about your own performance and potential training needs.
* **Employee Thoughts** (`employee_thoughts`, text, optional): If you are an employee, share your thoughts about your own performance and potential training needs.
* **Manager Views** (`manager_views`, text, optional): If you are a manager, share your views about the employee's performance and potential training needs.
* **Manager Thoughts** (`manager_thoughts`, text, optional): If you are a manager, share your thoughts about the employee's performance and potential training needs.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, text, required): Summarize the training needs of the employee based on their tasks and performance.

## Tips

* Please provide clear and concise answers to each field.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* Remember to review the current training plans and procedures before submitting this form to prevent duplication.
