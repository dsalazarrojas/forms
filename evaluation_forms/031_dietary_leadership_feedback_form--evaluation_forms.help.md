# Dietary Leadership Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the performance of team members in a department. The feedback provided will help in identifying areas of improvement and provide opportunities for growth and development of team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the person submitting the feedback.
2. Indicate whether the team member is a member of your department.
3. Provide a score out of 5 for the team member's performance.
4. Enter the date when you last interacted with the team member.
5. If applicable, enter the time when you last interacted with the team member.
6. Enter the team member's email address.
7. Optionally, add additional comments about the team member's performance.
8. If you have any other comments, add them below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the team member you are providing feedback on.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the team member you are providing feedback on.
- **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the team member you are providing feedback on.
- **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the team member is a member of your department.
- **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the team member holds a team lead position.
- **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional): Provide a score out of 5 for the team member's performance.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when you last interacted with the team member.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you last interacted with the team member.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the team member's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the team member's phone number.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the team member's performance.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Provide a score out of 5 for the team member's performance.
- **Additional Comments** (`comments_2`, note, optional): Add any other comments about the team member's performance.
