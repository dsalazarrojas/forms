# Employee Training Platform Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees about their experience with the Employee Training Platform. The purpose of this form is to evaluate the usability and effectiveness of the platform in meeting the needs of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please provide your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* Select the training programs you have participated in using the platform.
* Answer the evaluation questions, selecting one option that best answers each question.
* Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the platform.
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform, using a number to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the platform, please include them in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (1, Text, Required): Please provide your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Training Programs** (2, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the training programs you have participated in using the platform. This will help us understand which programs are most engaging and effective.
* **Evaluation Questions** (3, Select One, Optional): Answer the evaluation questions, selecting one option that best answers each question. This will help us understand your thoughts and opinions about the platform.
* **Evaluation Comments** (4, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the platform. This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and ideas about how we can improve the platform.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (5, Number, Optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the platform, using a number to indicate your level of satisfaction. Please note that this is a subjective rating and is meant to provide general feedback.
* **Suggestions** (6, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the platform, please include them in this field. This will help us understand how we can better meet the needs of employees like you.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the platform for future employees.
