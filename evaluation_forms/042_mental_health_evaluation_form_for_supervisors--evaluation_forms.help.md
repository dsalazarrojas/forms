# mental_health_evaluation_form_for_supervisors - Help Guide

## Purpose

The mental health evaluation form for supervisors is used to assess an employee's mental health, identify potential issues, and create a plan for recovery. This form is designed to be completed by supervisors to help employees get the support and resources they need to maintain their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form's purpose and context before proceeding.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to complete the form.
3. Answer each section of the form honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Use the information collected to create an action plan to support the employee's recovery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: The employee's name is the primary identifier for this evaluation. Please enter the employee's name as it appears in our records.
* **Date**: The date of the evaluation is used to track progress and provide a historical context for the assessment.
* **Evaluation Date**: The date of the evaluation is used to ensure that the form is completed up-to-date and not duplicated.
* **Supervisor Name**: Your name and title are required to provide context and ownership for the evaluation.
* **Employee Performance**: Select all that apply for employee performance issues, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes' and 'No' for 'No'. This will help identify areas that require attention.
* **Mental Health Issues**: Select all that apply for mental health issues, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes' and 'No' for 'No'. This will help identify potential areas of concern.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Enter any comments or observations made during the evaluation.
* **Recommended Resources**: Select all that apply for recommended resources, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes' and 'No' for 'No'. This will help guide the next steps.
* **Action Plan**: Enter a plan for recovery and support, including any necessary steps or actions to be taken.
* **Next Steps**: Select all that apply for next steps, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes' and 'No' for 'No'. This will help determine the next stage of the recovery process.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the next follow-up evaluation or meeting.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time for the next follow-up evaluation or meeting.
* **Follow-up Comments**: Enter any comments or observations made during the follow-up evaluation or meeting.

Note: Please be sure to review and follow up on the action plan created in this form to ensure the employee's recovery and well-being.
