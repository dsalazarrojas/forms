# Training Program Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness and quality of a training program. It consists of 15 questions that cover various aspects of the program, including overall satisfaction, program rating, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information:
	* **Training Program Name**: This is the name of the training program you are evaluating.
	* **Training Program Length**: Please enter the number of hours or days you spent on the program.
	* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale from 1-5.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly:
	* **Overall Rating**: Rate the program's overall rating on a scale from 1-5.
	* **Training Program Evaluation**: Enter your comments about the program's evaluation.
	* **Training Program Satisfaction**: Enter your comments about your level of satisfaction with the program.
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the program.
	* **Would you recommend this program?**: Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Undecided' to indicate whether you would recommend the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the program's overall rating on a scale from 1-5.
* **Training Program Evaluation**: Enter your comments about the program's evaluation.
* **Training Program Satisfaction**: Enter your comments about your level of satisfaction with the program.
* **Training Program Name**: This is the name of the training program you are evaluating.
* **Training Program Length**: Please enter the number of hours or days you spent on the program.
* **Training Program Frequency**: This is not a required field, but if you want to provide additional information about the program's frequency, you can enter it here.
* **Training Program Evaluation Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the program's evaluation.
* **Training Program Evaluation Scale**: Rate your evaluation of the program on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is Unsatisfactory and 5 is Excellent.
* **Training Program Improvement Areas**: Enter any areas where you think the program could be improved.
* **Would you recommend this program?**: Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Undecided' to indicate whether you would recommend the program.
* **Undecided**: This field is for participants who are undecided about recommending the program. It's not a requirement for filling out this form.
