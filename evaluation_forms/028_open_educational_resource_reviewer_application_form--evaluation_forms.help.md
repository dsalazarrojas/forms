# Open Educational Resource Reviewer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Educational Resource Reviewer Application Form is designed to collect information from potential reviewers about their experience, expertise, and qualifications to evaluate open educational resources (OER). This form helps us ensure that reviewers possess the necessary skills and knowledge to provide valuable feedback on OERs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your application details, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide information about your review experience, including your areas of expertise and any previous OER reviews you've completed.
3. Describe your review process, including how you approach evaluating OERs and what criteria you use.
4. Specify your availability for reviews, including the frequency and timing of your reviews.
5. Indicate your rating and recommendation preferences.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
7. Finally, sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: This is the first step in filling out the form. Please provide your name and contact information, and make sure to include your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Reviewer Info**: This section is where you can provide more detailed information about yourself, including your background, education, and any relevant experience you have as an evaluator or educator.
* **Review Information**: Describe your experience and approach to evaluating OERs, including your criteria and process. Be specific about what you look for when evaluating OERs.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you to discuss your application.
* **Reviewer Statement**: In this section, you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience and qualifications as an evaluator.
* **Review Process**: Describe your approach to evaluating OERs, including how you balance the quality and quantity of your reviews.
* **Review Scheduling**: Specify your availability for reviews, including the frequency and timing of your reviews.
* **Review Frequency**: Indicate how often you're able to review OERs, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Reviewer Rating**: Rate your own experience and qualifications as an evaluator, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Review Type**: Select the types of OERs you're interested in reviewing, such as textbooks, online courses, or videos.
* **Review Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide about your experience and qualifications as an evaluator.
* **Review Conclusion**: Indicate your conclusion or outcome from your evaluations, such as recommendations or ratings.
* **Review Recommendation**: Indicate your recommendations for OERs, including what types of resources you'd recommend and why.
* **Reviewers Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your identity and commitment to the application.
* **Reviewee's Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement with the review process and terms.
* **Review Issuer Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the review process and terms.
* **Submission Date**: Date and time of when you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time**: Time of day when you submitted the form.

Note that fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory, but providing accurate information is essential to ensure your application is considered. If a field is missing or left blank, it may affect the processing of your application.
