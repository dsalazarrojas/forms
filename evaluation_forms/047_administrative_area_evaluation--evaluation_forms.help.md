# Administrative Area Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the administrative areas within the organization, providing feedback and suggestions for improvement. The evaluation results will help identify areas that need attention and contribute to creating a safer and more functional work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete the Evaluator Information Page** (page 1):
	* Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
	* Take a photo of the area (if applicable).
2. **Enter Area Information** (page 2):
	* Enter the name of the administrative area being evaluated.
3. **Rate Cleanliness and Functionality** (page 3):
	* Rate the cleanliness of the area from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	* Rate the area's functionality, indicating how well it supports work activities.
4. **Document Safety Concerns and Needed Improvements** (page 4):
	* Describe any safety concerns or hazards observed.
	* List any needed repairs or replacements.
	* Provide recommendations for improvement.
5. **Attach Photos and Confirm Evaluation** (page 5):
	* Indicate if supporting photos are attached.
	* Confirm who evaluated the area (your name).
6. **Submit Evaluation Results** (page 6):
	* Enter the date of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Information**: This page is used to collect your contact information and name. Please ensure that you provide accurate contact information so that the evaluator can follow up with you regarding any concerns or actions taken.
* **Area Name**: Enter the exact name of the administrative area being evaluated, such as "Reception Desk" or "Break Room".
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the area from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), where 1 indicates a need for serious improvement and 5 indicates excellent cleanliness.
* **Functionality Rating**: Rate the area's functionality, indicating how well it supports work activities, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Safety Concerns**: Describe any safety concerns or hazards observed in the area.
* **Equipment Needs**: If there are any needed repairs or replacements, list them here.
* **Suggested Improvements**: Provide recommendations for improving the area.
* **Photos Attached**: Indicate if supporting photos are attached to the form.
* **Evaluated By**: Enter your name, as the person evaluating the area.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
