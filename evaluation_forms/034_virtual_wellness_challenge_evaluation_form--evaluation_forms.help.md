# Virtual Wellness Challenge Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Wellness Challenge Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation data from participants in a Virtual Wellness Challenge.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several pages with different sections.
2. Each page is focused on a specific topic, such as participant feedback, challenge experience, and overall satisfaction.
3. Please complete each page carefully, providing as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Feedback**
	+ **Participant Feedback**: This section is designed to collect any comments or thoughts you have about your experience in the Virtual Wellness Challenge.
	+ Type: Text, 100 characters minimum
	+ Required: No
* **Challenge Experience**
	+ **Challenge Experience**: Share your thoughts on what you liked or disliked about the challenge.
	+ Type: Text, 100 characters minimum
	+ Required: No
* **Suggestions for Improvement**
	+ **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any ideas for how we could improve the challenge, please share them here.
	+ Type: Text, 100 characters minimum
	+ Required: No
* **Overall Satisfaction**
	+ **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with your experience in the challenge.
	+ Type: Select One, options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
	+ Required: No
* **Rating (1-5)**
	+ **Rating**: Rate your experience in the challenge from 1 to 5.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Email**
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address for feedback purposes only.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **Phone**
	+ **Phone**: This field is not currently active.
* **Participant Name**
	+ **Participant Name**: Enter your name for feedback purposes only.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Participant Email**
	+ **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for feedback purposes only.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
