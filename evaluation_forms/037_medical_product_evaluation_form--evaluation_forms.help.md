# Medical Product Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate a medical product's attributes, such as its name, rating, description, performance, overall evaluation, product type, medical condition, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Product Name" field with the name of the medical product being evaluated.
2. Rate the product's quality on the numerical scale provided in the "Rating" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Description" field (optional).
4. Describe the product's performance in the "Performance" field (optional).
5. Evaluate the product's overall quality by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Overall" field.
6. Select the product type from the list provided in the "Product Type" field (select all that apply).
7. Check if the product is related to a specific medical condition by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Medical Condition" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the product, fill in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the medical product being evaluated.
* **Rating (Number, Required)**: Rate the product's quality on a numerical scale.
* **Description (Text, Optional)**: Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Performance (Text, Optional)**: Describe the product's performance.
* **Overall (Select One, Required)**: Evaluate the product's overall quality by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Product Type (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select all product types that apply to the medical product being evaluated.
* **Medical Condition (Select One, Optional)**: Check if the product is related to a specific medical condition.
* **Comments (Text, Optional)**: Fill in any additional comments or thoughts about the product.
