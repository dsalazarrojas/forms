# Pitch Competition Scoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pitch Competition Scoring Form is a tool used by judges to evaluate and provide feedback on pitches presented in a pitch competition. The form ensures consistency and fairness in the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each pitch individually, providing feedback and scores for each team's presentation.
2. Ensure that the form is completed accurately and honestly, taking into consideration the pitch's merits and areas for improvement.
3. Review and adjust your feedback and scores as needed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team** (`team`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the teams that you have evaluated.
* **Judge Name** (`judge_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title/position as a judge.
* **Score** (`score`, `select_one`, required): Select the score for this pitch, using the provided options.
* **Pitch** (`pitch`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of the pitch, including its key points and main ideas.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `select_one`, required): Select the name of the team that presented the pitch.
* **Presentation** (`presentation`, `text`, required): Describe the presentation's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Innovation** (`innovation`, `text`, required): Discuss the innovative aspects of the pitch, including new ideas and approaches.
* **Business Model** (`business_model`, `text`, required): Evaluate the business model presented in the pitch, including its feasibility and potential for implementation.
* **Pitch 2** (`pitch_2`, `text`, optional): Provide an optional summary of the second pitch (if presented).
* **Team 2** (`team_2`, `text`, required): Select the name of the team that presented the second pitch.
* **Innovation 2** (`innovation_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the innovative aspects of the second pitch.
* **Business Model 2** (`business_model_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the strong points of the business model presented in the second pitch.

## Tips
* Take your time to review and think about each pitch carefully before providing feedback.
* Be thorough and honest in your evaluation, but also be kind and constructive in your feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the organizers for guidance.
