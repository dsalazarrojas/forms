# Healthcare Team Collaboration Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a healthcare team collaboration workshop. It assesses various aspects of the workshop, including its quality, collaboration effectiveness, staff satisfaction, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing detailed feedback on your experience with the workshop.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, and enter dates in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. If a field is not applicable, please mark it as "N/A" or "Not Applicable" to avoid confusion.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Quality (1)**: Rate the overall quality of the workshop, using a number value (e.g., 1-5).
* **Collaboration Effectiveness (2)**: Describe the effectiveness of collaboration among team members, providing specific examples or details.
* **Improvement Ideas (3)**: Share your thoughts on areas for improvement and potential suggestions for future workshops.
* **Staff Satisfaction (4)**: Assess the level of staff satisfaction, using a number value (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comments (5)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Submission (8)**: Select "Yes" if you submitted your evaluation, "No" otherwise.
* **Submitted By (9)**: Select "Yes" if you submitted your evaluation, "No" otherwise.
* **Submitted At (10)**: Enter the date and time you submitted your evaluation.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in evaluating the workshop.
