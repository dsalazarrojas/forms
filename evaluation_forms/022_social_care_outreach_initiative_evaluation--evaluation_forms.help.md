# Social Care Outreach Initiative Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Care Outreach Initiative Evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from participants in social care outreach initiatives. This form helps in understanding the participant's experience, satisfaction, and perceived service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the social care outreach initiative you are evaluating.
2. Provide your full name as a participant.
3. Enter the date of the visit or interaction with the service.
4. Specify the time of the visit or interaction.
5. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the service, using the "Participant Satisfaction" dropdown menu.
6. Select the level of service quality you experienced during the visit or interaction, using the "Service Quality" dropdown menu. You can choose one or more options that apply.
7. If you have any comments or feedback about the staff support you received, please type them in the "Staff Support" text box. This is optional.
8. If you have any suggestions for improvement of the service, please type them in the "Service Improvement" text box. This is optional.
9. Share your comments about your experience with the participant in the "Participant Comments" text box. This is optional.
10. If you have any feedback or comments for the staff, please type them in the "Staff Comments" text box. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Care Outreach Initiative Evaluation** (`outreach_initiative`, text, required): Enter the title of the social care outreach initiative being evaluated.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a participant.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the visit or interaction with the service.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the visit or interaction.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of satisfaction with the service, choosing from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more levels of service quality, choosing from "Good", "Poor", etc. (Note: This field allows multiple selections)
* **Staff Support** (`staff_support`, text, optional): Optional comments about the staff support you received. If no comments, leave blank.
* **Service Improvement** (`service_improvement`, text, optional): Optional suggestions for service improvement. If no suggestions, leave blank.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Optional comments from the participant. If no comments, leave blank.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): Optional feedback or comments for the staff. If no comments, leave blank.
