# Child Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the child's development and skills in various areas, including social and emotional, academic performance, and behavioral observations. It is intended to provide an overall picture of the child's strengths and areas for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the student's full name in the "Student Full Name" field.
3. Enter your full name and title in the "Evaluator Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Date of Evaluation" field.
5. For each section, select the level of demonstration or performance for the child:
	* Social and Emotional Skills: Select the level of demonstration for "Interactions with Peers" and "Emotional Self-Regulation".
	* Academic Performance: Select the level of performance for "Reading and Literacy" and "Mathematics Skills".
	* Behavioral Observations: Select the level of demonstration for "Ability to Follow Directions" and "Focus and Attention".
6. In the "Summary of Strengths and Areas for Growth" field, provide detailed qualitative feedback about the child's strengths and areas for growth.
7. In the "Specific Recommendations for Support" field, provide intervention strategies or goals for the child's development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Full Name (2)
 Student Full Name: Enter the full name of the child being evaluated.

### Evaluator Name (3)
 Evaluator Name: Enter your full name and title.

### Date of Evaluation (4)
 Date of Evaluation: Enter the date of the evaluation took place.

### Interactions with Peers (6)
 Interactions with Peers: Select how often the child consistently demonstrates interactions with peers:
	* Consistently Demonstrates
	* Often Demonstrates
	* Sometimes Demonstrates
	* Rarely Demonstrates

### Summary of Strengths and Areas for Growth (15)
 Summary of Strengths and Areas for Growth: Provide detailed qualitative feedback about the child's strengths and areas for growth.
