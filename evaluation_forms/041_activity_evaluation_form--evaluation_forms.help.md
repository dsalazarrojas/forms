# Activity Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Activity Evaluation Form" is designed to gather feedback from students on their experiences and perceptions of a learning activity. This form will help instructors and educational professionals to improve the effectiveness and quality of future learning activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and the name of the course you are evaluating.
2. Enter the title of the activity being evaluated.
3. Specify the date the activity was conducted.
4. Rate the activity's performance in terms of meeting its learning objectives.
5. Assess the relevance of the activity to the course material.
6. Evaluate the difficulty level of the activity.
7. Rate the engagement level of the activity.
8. Assess the time allocated for the activity.
9. Rate the clarity of instructions provided.
10. Provide any recommended improvements.
11. Add any additional comments or feedback about the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)**: Enter your full name as a student who participated in the activity.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Course Name (2)**: Enter the name of the course the activity was part of.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Name of Learning Activity (3)**: Enter the title of the activity being evaluated.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Date of Activity (4)**: Specify the date the activity was conducted.
	+ **Type:** date
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Were the Learning Objectives Met (5)**:
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** Completely met, Mostly met, Partially met, Did not meet, Uncertain
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Relevance to Course Material (6)**:
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** Very relevant, Relevant, Somewhat relevant, Not very relevant, Not relevant at all
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Difficulty Level (7)**:
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** Too easy, Appropriate, Challenging but fair, Too difficult, Extremely difficult
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Level of Engagement (8)**:
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** Very engaging, Engaging, Moderately engaging, Slightly engaging, Not engaging
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Appropriateness of Time Allocation (9)**:
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** Too much time, Adequate time, Could use more time, Not enough time, Far too little time
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Clarity of Instructions (10)**:
	+ **Type:** select_one
	+ **Options:** Very clear, Clear, Moderately clear, Unclear, Very unclear
	+ **Required:** Yes
* **Recommended Improvements (11)**: If you have any suggestions for improving the activity, provide them here.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** No
* **Additional Comments (12)**: If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the activity, please share them.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** No
