# stem_teacher_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Stem Teacher Assessment Form** is designed to collect information from teachers on their professional evaluations, assessing their performance, skills, and qualifications in teaching and subject-specific knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*)
2. Leave the optional fields blank if not applicable or not known
3. Use the provided options for select multiple fields (e.g., teacher subjects)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where you are currently teaching or have taught.
* **Teacher Subjects**: Select all the subjects you teach or have taught (e.g., Math, Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics).
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Time of Evaluation**: Enter the time of the evaluation, if applicable.
* **Note**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or information about your teaching performance.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact form@gic.mx for guidance.
* Ensure that you are completing the form in the correct context and with a clear understanding of the evaluation criteria.
* The information collected will be used to evaluate your performance and provide feedback for professional development.
