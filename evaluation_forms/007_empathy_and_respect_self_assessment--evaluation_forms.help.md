# empathy_and_respect_self_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this empathy and respect self-assessment form is to help you reflect on your understanding and behavior towards empathy and respect, particularly in difficult situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and consider your experiences and behaviors related to empathy and respect.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you have difficulty answering a question, try to provide a brief explanation of why you are unable to answer.
