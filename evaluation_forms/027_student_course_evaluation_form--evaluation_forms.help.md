# Student Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Course Evaluation Form is a tool for students to provide feedback on their experience in a course. This form helps instructors and course administrators understand student perspectives and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions according to your experience with the course.
2. Use the "Select One" fields to choose your level of agreement or satisfaction with each question.
3. Use the "Select Multiple" fields to choose all that apply from the options provided.
4. For "Note" fields, provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the course.
5. Use the "Date" fields to select the date you completed the course or the dates relevant to your feedback.
6. Use the "Email" field to provide your email address, if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
7. The "Phone" field is for you to provide your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
8. If you have any additional thoughts or comments, use the "Text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_course_evaluation_form_1** (`text`, optional): A field for any additional comments or thoughts you have about the course.
* **student_course_evaluation_form_2** (`number`, optional): This field is not clearly labeled, but it appears to be meant for numerical ratings or feedback. Please rate your satisfaction with the course.
* **select_one_student_course_evaluation_form** (`select_one`, optional): Choose your level of agreement or satisfaction with the course.
* **select_multiple_student_course_evaluation_form** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **date_student_course_evaluation_form_1** (`date`, optional): Select the date you completed the course or the date relevant to your feedback.
* **date_student_course_evaluation_form_2** (`date`, optional): This field is identical to "date_student_course_evaluation_form_1".
* **email_student_course_evaluation_form** (`email`, optional): Provide your email address, if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **phone_student_course_evaluation_form** (`text`, optional): A field for you to provide your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **text_student_course_evaluation_form** (`text`, optional): A field for any additional thoughts or comments you have about the course.
