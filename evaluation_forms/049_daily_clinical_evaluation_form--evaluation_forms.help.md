# Daily Clinical Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Clinical Evaluation Form is a tool for collecting and documenting a patient's medical conditions. It is designed to be completed by medical professionals to assess and track changes in a patient's medical status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. If you have a medical history, enter it in the "medical history" field.
3. Select all applicable medical conditions from the "medical conditions" section.
4. List any medications you are currently taking in the "Medications" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have a medical history, enter it here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition_1`, `medical_condition_2`, ..., `medical_condition_20`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable medical conditions from the list.
* **Medications** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your responses.
