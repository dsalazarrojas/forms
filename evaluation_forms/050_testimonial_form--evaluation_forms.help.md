# Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user testimonials with different levels of privacy and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose whether the testimonial content is public or private by selecting "Public" or "Private" from the "Testimonial Public" dropdown menu.
2. Select the current status of the testimonial by choosing "Active" or "Inactive" from the "Status" dropdown menu.
3. If desired, add a video or image to further describe your experience.
4. Write a testimonial in the "Testimonial" text field.
5. Choose whether you want to share your testimonial with other users by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Private" dropdown menu.
6. If you have a relevant file you'd like to attach, you can add it to the "Attachments" text field.

* **Testimonial Public** (`Public/None`, select_one, required): Choose whether your testimonial content will be public or private.
