The FSC Evaluation Survey is a tool used to gather information and feedback from users. It is meant to be completed by users who have used the FSC system to evaluate their experience and provide suggestions for improvement.

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and suggestions from users on how to improve the FSC system. By completing this survey, users can provide their thoughts on their experience with the FSC system, including what worked well and what didn't work well. This feedback will be used to inform improvements and updates to the system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The survey consists of multiple pages. Please complete each page in order.
2. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
3. For fields marked as "required", please make sure to fill out this field.
4. For fields marked as "text" or "email", enter a value in a format that matches the expected type.
5. For fields marked as "number", enter a value between 1 and 5, inclusive.
6. For fields marked as "score_1", "score_2", and "score_3", enter a number between 1 and 5.
7. For the "comments" field, enter any additional information or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, **Required**): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (Email, **Required**): Enter your email address.
* **Score 1-5** (Number): Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate your experience with the feature/aspect of the system that this score corresponds to.
* **Score 2-5** (Number): Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate your experience with the feature/aspect of the system that this score corresponds to.
* **Score 3-5** (Number): Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate your experience with the feature/aspect of the system that this score corresponds to.
* **Comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the system.
