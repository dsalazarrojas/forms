# Independent Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for independent assessments, which are evaluations of a person's performance, skills, or qualifications outside of a structured testing environment.

## How to Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please ensure you have all the necessary information available.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as the assessment date and rating.
3. You may select an option for the type of assessment in question 5.
4. Enter any comments and recommendations in fields 8 and 21.
5. Enter any other relevant information, such as names and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Independent Assessment Form** (`1`, `title`, `required: false`): This is the form title.
*   **Name** (`2`, `name`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the individual being assessed.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter the email of the individual being assessed or the assessor's own email.
*   **Phone Number** (`4`, `phone_number`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the individual being assessed or the assessor's own phone number.
*   **Type of Assessment** (`5`, `assessment_type`, `required: false`): Select the type of assessment being performed.
*   **Assessment Date** (`6`, `assessment_date`, `required: true`): Enter the date of the assessment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Assessment Time** (`7`, `assessment_time`, `required: false`): Enter the time of the assessment in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
*   **Recommendation** (`8`, `recommendation`, `required: true`): Enter any recommendations or comments for the individual being assessed.
*   **Rating** (`10`, `rating`, `required: true`): Enter a rating for the individual being assessed on a scale.
*   **Review Date** (`11`, `review_date`, `required: false`): Enter the date the review was completed in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Reviewed By** (`12`, `reviewed_by`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the person performing the review.
*   **Reviewee** (`13`, `reviewee`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the individual being reviewed.
*   **Review Type** (`14`, `review_type`, `required: false`): Enter the type of review being performed.
*   **Review Comment** (`15`, `review_comment`, `required: false`): Enter any comments about the review.
*   **Review Rating** (`16`, `review_rating`, `required: false`): Enter a rating for the individual being reviewed.
*   **Review Phone** (`17`, `review_phone`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the individual being reviewed or the reviewer's own phone number.
*   **Review Email** (`18`, `review_email`, `required: false`): Enter the email of the individual being reviewed or the reviewer's own email.
*   **Review Website** (`19`, `review_website`, `required: false`): Enter the website of the individual being reviewed or the reviewer's own website.
*   **Review Note** (`20`, `review_note`, `required: false`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the review.
*   **Review Recommendation** (`21`, `review_recommendation`, `required: false`): Enter any additional recommendations or comments about the review.
*   **Reviewee (Person)** (`22`, `reviewedee`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the individual being reviewed.
*   **Reviewee Organisation** (`23`, `reviewedee_organisation`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the organisation or company of the individual being reviewed.
*   **Reviewee Job** (`24`, `reviewedee_job`, `required: false`): Enter the job title or position of the individual being reviewed.
*   **Reviewee Position** (`25`, `reviewedee_position`, `required: false`): Enter the position or rank of the individual being reviewed.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields.
*   Be thorough and accurate in your responses.
*   Follow your organisation's guidelines and policies for assessments and reviews.
*   Proofread your work before submitting.
