# Employee Annual Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate an employee's performance over the past year, providing a platform for the employee to reflect on their accomplishments, challenges, and areas for improvement. This form will help managers and HR teams assess an employee's performance, set future goals, and identify areas for professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your job title, as this is a required field to ensure accurate tracking of your role and responsibilities.
3. Select the evaluation date, as this is a required field to track when your performance was evaluated.
4. Enter your performance score, which should be a numerical value that reflects your performance over the past year. Please note that the performance score is a required field.
5. Optionally, add any comments or feedback you have about your performance or areas for improvement.
6. If desired, include any additional comments or feedback from the evaluator, such as strengths and areas for development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (1)
Enter your given name in this field.

### **Last Name** (2)
Enter your surname in this field.

### **Job Title** (3)
Enter your job title, which is a required field to accurately track your role and responsibilities.

### **Evaluation Date** (4)
Select the date on which your performance was evaluated.

### **Performance Score** (5)
Enter a numerical value between 1 and 100 that reflects your performance over the past year.

### **Comments** (6)
Optional field for any additional comments or feedback about your performance or areas for improvement.

### **Evaluation Form Comments** (7)
Optional field for any additional comments or feedback from the evaluator.
