# Church Ministry Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>This form is designed to help us better understand the effectiveness and areas of improvement for your church ministries. We value your honest feedback and it will help us refine our ministries to serve you better.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback.
2. Rate the effectiveness of the ministry leadership and various aspects such as communication, spiritual impact, and frequency of meetings/events.
3. Share your thoughts on the main strengths and areas for improvement of the ministry.
4. Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for further discussion on your feedback.
5. Finally, provide any additional suggestions or comments, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **-- Church Ministry Feedback -- (Page 1)**
* **Ministry Being Evaluated (1)**: Please enter the name of the ministry you are evaluating (e.g., Youth, Worship, Children's).
* **Your Role in this Ministry (2)**: Select your role in the ministry (Regular Participant, Occasional Attendee, Volunteer, Leader/Coordinator).
* **Rate the effectiveness of the ministry leadership (3)**: Rate the leadership effectiveness on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **How would you rate the communication within this ministry? (4)**: Rate the communication within the ministry as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Has this ministry helped you grow spiritually? (5)**: Select whether you Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree.
* **What are the main strengths of this ministry? (6)**: Share the main strengths of the ministry (optional).
* **What areas could be improved? (7)**: Suggest areas for improvement (optional).

### **Additional Information (Page 2)**
* **Are the resources (physical, financial, etc.) adequate for this ministry? (8)**: Select whether the resources are Yes, completely, Mostly adequate, Not really, or Not at all.
* **Is the frequency of meetings/events appropriate? (9)**: Select whether the frequency of meetings/events is Too frequent, Just right, or Not frequent enough.
* **How welcoming is this ministry to new people? (10)**: Select whether the ministry is Very welcoming, Somewhat welcoming, Neutral, or Not very welcoming.
* **Feedback on a specific recent event (if applicable) (11)**: Share your thoughts on a specific recent event (optional).
* **Any other suggestions or comments? (12)**: Leave any additional suggestions or comments.
* **May we contact you to discuss your feedback? (13)**: Select whether you give permission to contact you to discuss your feedback.
* **Date of Evaluation (14)**: Enter the date you submitted your evaluation.

This guide will help you complete the form in a smooth and efficient manner.
