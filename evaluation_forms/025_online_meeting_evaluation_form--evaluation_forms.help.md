# Online Meeting Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on your recent online meeting experience. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to access the form.
2. Select the meeting you wish to evaluate from the dropdown menu.
3. Answer the questions in a clear and concise manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Participants**: Enter the names of all participants who attended the meeting, including the presenter and any attendees.
* **Meeting Presentation**: Describe the presentation's content and whether it was well-structured. Was the content relevant and well-structured?
* **Meeting Organizers**: Enter the names of all organizers or facilitators involved in the meeting.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a numerical scale (1-5).
* **Presentation Content**: Describe the presentation's content and whether it was well-structured. Was the presentation delivered well? 
* **Presentation Delivery**: Rate the presentation delivery (e.g., pace, clarity, etc.).
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Meeting Length**: Enter the duration of the meeting.
* **Meeting Materials**: Describe any materials provided during or after the meeting.
* **Overall Experience**: Share your thoughts about your overall experience with the meeting.

Note: You can add as much or as little information as you feel necessary in each field.
