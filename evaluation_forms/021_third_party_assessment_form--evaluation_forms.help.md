# Third Party Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects evaluations and feedback from third parties about an individual's performance in a specific initiative or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Evaluate the person's performance in the overall initiative or project by providing your honest feedback.
2. Rate the person's overall performance.
3. Rate their teamwork and communication skills.
4. Provide feedback on their initiative and willingness to take on new challenges.
5. Provide feedback on whether their coaching was effective or not.
6. Rate their performance evaluations (e.g., positive, negative).
7. Select one or more areas for improvement.
8. Provide feedback on their overall performance effectiveness.
9. Select one or more areas where their performance was highly effective or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, text, required: false):
	+ Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the individual's performance.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select how you rate the individual's teamwork:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select one or more how you rate the individual's communication skills:
		- Strong
		- Weak
* **Initiative** (`initiative`, number, required: false):
	+ Please rate your experience with their initiative on a scale of 1-5 (1 = very low, 5 = very high).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false):
	+ Please provide any feedback or comments you have about the individual's performance.
* **Coaching** (`coaching`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select whether coaching was required or not during the initiative or project:
		- Required
		- Not Required
		- Not Applicable
* **Evaluations** (`evaluations`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select one or more how you rate the individual's performance evaluations:
		- Positive
		- Negative
* **Coaching Effectiveness** (`coaching_effectiveness`, text, required: false):
	+ Please provide feedback on the effectiveness of the coaching provided by the individual.
* **Evaluations Effectiveness** (`evaluations_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select one or more how you rate the individual's performance evaluations:
		- Highly Effective
		- Generally Effective
		- Somewhat Effective
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Ineffective
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select one or more areas where the individual could improve:
		- Communication
		- Teamwork
		- Initiative
		- Coaching
		- Evaluations
