# pregnancy_and_parenting_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The <i>pregnancy_and_parenting_consent_form</i> is a form designed to gather information from students who are pregnant or have parenting responsibilities. It is intended to gather demographic data, accommodation requirements, and other relevant details. Before filling out this form, please ensure you have the necessary authorization to access the related records and data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your <b>Student Information</b> with your name and other relevant student details.
2. Provide your <b>Contact Information</b> for the person responsible for supporting your child or children (if applicable).
3. Answer the <b>Accommodation Request</b> question, which asks whether you require any accommodation for your pregnancy or parenting responsibilities.
4. If you answered "Yes" to the <b>Accommodation Request</b>, please specify the <b>Accommodation Solution</b> you require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (Student Information, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to capture your student information, including your name and other relevant details. Please enter your name and other relevant details as required.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to capture the contact information of the person responsible for supporting your child or children. Please enter the name and contact details of this person.
* **Accommodation Request (Accommodation Request, select_multiple, required)**: This field asks whether you require any accommodation for your pregnancy or parenting responsibilities. Select either "Yes" or "No" to answer this question.
* **Accommodation Solution (Accommodation Solution, select_multiple, required)**: If you answered "Yes" to the accommodation question, please specify the accommodation solution you require in the space below.
