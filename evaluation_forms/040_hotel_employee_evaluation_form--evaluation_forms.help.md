# hotel_employee_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hotel employee evaluation form is designed to gather feedback and insights from employees on their job satisfaction, performance, and work completion. It's an internal tool used for employee evaluations and performance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand its purpose and fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and concisely, providing relevant details and examples when possible.
3. Fill in the required fields (if any) accurately and completely.
4. Use the optional fields to provide additional context or information that's relevant to your evaluation.
5. Ensure you're submitting the form accurately and completely, double-checking for errors before finalizing your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **evidence_of_work_completed** (number, optional): Provide any evidence or documentation of tasks completed, such as proof of reports submitted or projects finished.
* **job_satisfaction** (text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions on your job satisfaction, highlighting areas you enjoy and areas where improvement is needed.
* **job_satisfaction_rating** (number, optional): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale (though the scale is not specified, please use your best judgment).
* **recommendations_for_improvement** (text, optional): Suggest ways to improve your work environment or processes, providing specific ideas and suggestions.
* **performance_rating** (number, optional): Rate your own performance on a scale (though the scale is not specified, please use your best judgment).
* **date** (date, optional): Record the date of the evaluation, using a format like MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **time** (time, optional): Record the time spent on a specific task or project, using a format like HH:MM.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Use clear and concise language to ensure your responses are easily understandable.
- Review and double-check your submission before finalizing it.
- If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
