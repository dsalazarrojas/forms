# Abon Construction Bhd Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Abon Construction Bhd Interview Questionnaire Form is used to collect information from candidates during an interview session, which will help us determine their suitability for a position within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out each section as directed by the section headers.
2.  Provide accurate and truthful information for all fields.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate full name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Please provide the candidate's full name as it appears on their identification card.
* **Email address** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Phone number** (`candidate_phone`, text, required): Provide the candidate's phone number.
* **Position applied for** (`position_applied`, select_one, required): Choose the position the candidate is applying for from the list of options provided.
* **Date of interview** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interviewer name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the interviewer.
* **Highest education level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose the candidate's highest level of education from the options provided.
* **Years of experience in construction industry** (`years_construction_experience`, select_one, required): Choose the number of years the candidate has worked in the construction industry.
* **Relevant certifications or licenses** (`relevant_certifications`, text, optional): If applicable, list any relevant certifications or licenses held by the candidate.
* **Technical knowledge rating** (`technical_knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's technical knowledge using the scale provided.
* **Communication skills rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's communication skills using the scale provided.
* **Teamwork and collaboration rating** (`teamwork_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's ability to work in a team using the scale provided.
* **Safety awareness rating** (`safety_awareness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's safety awareness using the scale provided.
* **Key strengths observed** (`strengths_observed`, text, required): List any key strengths observed during the interview.
* **Areas of concern or improvement** (`areas_of_concern`, text, optional): If applicable, list any areas of concern or improvement observed during the interview.
* **Hiring recommendation** (`hiring_recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not to recommend the candidate for the position.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If additional information is needed, enter it here.
