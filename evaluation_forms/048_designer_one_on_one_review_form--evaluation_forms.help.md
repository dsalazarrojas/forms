# Designer One On One Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate a thorough review of a design concept, providing a comprehensive overview of the design's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided design concept and carefully consider your feedback.
2. Answer the schedule critique question to indicate whether or not the schedule aligns with your expectations.
3. Provide any relevant feedback or comments about the design.
4. If applicable, provide additional notes or comments for the designer's reference.
5. Specify if you will need to follow up on any issues.
6. Confirm your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
7. Optionally select the "Submit" option to indicate that you have completed the review.
8. Enter your name and any additional comments or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Designer One On One Review Form #1** (`review_form`, number, not required): This field is not required but provides an option for the reviewer to provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Schedule Critique** (`schedule_critique`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether or not the schedule provided aligns with your expectations. Options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Designer Feedback** (`designer_feedback`, text, not required): Provide any relevant feedback or comments about the design.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments for the designer's reference.
* **Follow Up Question** (`follow_up`, text, not required): If applicable, provide any follow-up questions or concerns you have for the designer.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have completed the review. Options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submitter** (`submittter`, text, not required): Enter your name for reference.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, not required): Enter the current version of the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, not required): Enter the form ID for reference.
* **Submitter Email** (`submittter_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submittter_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submittter_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments for the designer's reference.
