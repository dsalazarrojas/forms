# End Of Year Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the End Of Year Teacher Evaluation Form. This form is used to gather feedback and performance data about students at the end of the year.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by entering the student's name in the text field at the top of the page.
- Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
- Provide the date the evaluation took place in the corresponding field.
- Rate the student's overall performance in the "overall_performance_rating" field.
- Provide comments or feedback for the student in the "student_comments" field.
- Identify areas where the student needs improvement in the "areas_for_improvement" field.
- Enter comments from the teacher in the "teacher_comments" field.
- Enter comments from the parent in the "parent_comments" field.
- List specific areas for improvement in the "areas_for_improvement_list" field by selecting relevant subjects from the dropdown menu.
- Enter the date the evaluation was performed by the teacher in the "date_last_evaluated_by" field.
- Sign the form with the teacher's signature in the "teacher_signature" field.
- Sign with the parent's signature in the "parent_signature" field.
- Sign with the student's signature in the "student_signature" field.
- Enter the date the evaluation took place in the "date_evaluated" field.
- Optionally, enter comments from school administrators in the "school_administrators_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Date Last Evaluated** (`date_last_evaluated`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation took place.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, number, optional): Rate the student's overall performance.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments or feedback for the student.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas where the student needs improvement.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments from the teacher.
* **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments from the parent.
* **Areas for Improvement List** (`areas_for_improvement_list`, select_multiple, optional): List specific areas for improvement by selecting relevant subjects.
* **Date Last Evaluated By** (`date_last_evaluated_by`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation was performed by the teacher.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Sign with the teacher's signature.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Sign with the parent's signature.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, required): Sign with the student's signature.
* **Date Evaluated** (`date_evaluated`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation took place.
* **Evaluated By** (`evaluated_by`, text, optional): Optionally enter the name of the person who evaluated the student.
* **School Administrators** (`school_administrators`, text, optional): Enter comments from school administrators.
* **School** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school.
* **School Admin** (`school_admin`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school administrator.
* **School Administrators Comments** (`school_administrators_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments from school administrators.
