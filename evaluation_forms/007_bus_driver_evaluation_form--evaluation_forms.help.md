# Bus Driver Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Driver Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of a bus driver. It gathers information on their skills, behavior, and overall fitness to operate a vehicle, providing a comprehensive evaluation of their abilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the driver's full name in the "Driver Full Name" field.
2. Provide the driver's employee ID in the "Driver Employee ID" field.
3. Specify the assigned bus route in the "Assigned Bus Route" field.
4. Enter the vehicle body number, which is the unique ID painted on the bus exterior.
5. Select the fleet operator's name from the list of options.
6. Enter the date of the evaluation using the date field.
7. Evaluate the driver's performance in the following categories:
	* Road Safety Awareness: Rate their adherence to traffic signs, speed limits, and signals.
	* Vehicle Control and Smoothness: Assess their braking, acceleration, and steering precision.
	* Schedule Adherence and Timeliness: Evaluate their ability to maintain route timing safely.
	* Passenger Interaction Quality: Rate their professionalism and helpfulness towards commuters.
8. Provide any observations on physical or cognitive limitations if necessary.
9. Enter a numerical score from 1 (Low) to 10 (High) for the Overall Performance Score.
10. Select the final evaluation status from the options provided.
11. Finally, enter your full name to sign off the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Full Name**: Enter the full name of the driver being evaluated.
* **Driver Employee ID**: Provide the driver's staff identification number.
* **Assigned Bus Route**: Specify the bus route the driver is assigned to.
* **Vehicle Body Number**: Enter the unique ID painted on the bus exterior.
* **Fleet Operator Name**: Select the company or agency name from the list of options.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date of the evaluation using the date field.
* **Evaluation Date**: This field is not applicable as the evaluation date is already recorded in the "Date of Evaluation" field.
* **Road Safety Awareness**: Rate the driver's adherence to traffic signs, speed limits, and signals.
* **Vehicle Control and Smoothness**: Assess the driver's braking, acceleration, and steering precision.
* **Schedule Adherence and Timeliness**: Evaluate their ability to maintain route timing safely.
* **Passenger Interaction Quality**: Rate their professionalism and helpfulness towards commuters.
* **Observations of Physical/Cognitive Limitations**: Provide any notable concerns regarding the driver's fitness.
* **Overall Performance Score**: Enter a numerical score from 1 (Low) to 10 (High) for the driver's performance.
* **Final Evaluation Status**: Select the recommendation for continued operation from the provided options.
* **Evaluator Digital Signature**: Enter your full name to certify the evaluation.
