# Teacher Appreciation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Appreciation Form is designed to allow teachers and students to express their gratitude and appreciation for a teacher's dedication and hard work. This form helps to provide constructive feedback and support to the teacher.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Introduction**: Briefly introduce yourself and explain why you are filling out this form.
2.  **Teacher's Name**: Enter the name of the teacher you are appreciating.
3.  **Message**: Write a heartfelt message expressing your appreciation for the teacher's work.
4.  **Appreciation Type**: Select 'Yes' if you are expressing appreciation for the teacher's work, or 'No' if you have no feedback.
5.  **Appreciation Date**: If you are expressing appreciation, select the date when you last appreciated the teacher's work.
6.  **Appreciation Time**: If you are expressing appreciation, select the time when you last appreciated the teacher's work (HH:MM format).
7.  **Appreciation Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the teacher's work.
8.  **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
9.  **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
10. **Submitted By**: Enter your name and title (if applicable) to indicate who is submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ This field is the start of the form where you can briefly introduce yourself and explain why you are filling out this form.
* **Teacher's Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the teacher you are expressing appreciation for. This is required to ensure that the feedback is directed to the right person.
* **Message**:
	+ Write a heartfelt message expressing your appreciation for the teacher's work. This can be a few words, sentences, or paragraphs.
* **Appreciation Type**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are expressing appreciation for the teacher's work, or 'No' if you have no feedback.
* **Appreciation Date**:
	+ Select the date when you last appreciated the teacher's work. This is optional, and you can choose a specific date or leave it blank if you are expressing appreciation for the teacher's overall work.
*   **Appreciation Time**:
    + Select the time when you last appreciated the teacher's work (HH:MM format). This is optional, and you can choose a specific time or leave it blank if you are expressing appreciation for the teacher's overall work.
*   **Appreciation Note**:
    + Provide any additional comments or notes about the teacher's work. This is optional, but it can help to further express your appreciation.
*   **Email**:
    + Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
*   **Phone**:
    + Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
*   **Submitted By**:
    + Enter your name and title (if applicable) to indicate who is submitting this form.
