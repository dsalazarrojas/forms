# Workplace Mentorship Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Mentorship Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from employees on the effectiveness of the workplace mentorship initiative. The form helps to understand what has been accomplished and areas that need improvement to further enhance the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the required questions and provide honest feedback.
2. Select all the relevant answers for the select_multiple fields.
3. Choose the most accurate answer for the select_one fields.
4. Write down any comments or suggestions you have in the open-ended comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (1)**: Enter your name for reference and tracking purposes.
- **Program Type (2)**: Select all the programs you participated in or were involved with.
- **Goals Met (3)**: Choose whether you met the goals set for the program, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Frequency (4)**: Select all the frequencies you met with your mentor, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Satisfaction (5)**: Choose how satisfied you are with the program, either Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
- **Comments (6)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the program.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide specific feedback whenever possible.
- Avoid using jargon or overly complex language.
- Try to focus on the program's effectiveness and areas that need improvement.
