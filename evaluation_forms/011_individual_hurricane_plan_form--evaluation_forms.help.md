# Individual Hurricane Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Individual Hurricane Plan Form is designed to collect information from individuals about their plans and preparations for a hurricane. This form is meant to be completed by individuals in the affected area and is a quick and easy way to collect critical information that will help emergency responders and authorities assess their situation and provide assistance if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide clear and concise answers to each question.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. Use the provided options for select fields, as they are designed to help you quickly and accurately answer each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (ID: 1)
* **Introduction** (`title`, not required): This section is a brief introduction to the form and is meant to be filled out with any relevant details about your situation.

### Evacuation Plans (ID: 2)
* **Evacuation Plans** (`evacuation_plans`, text, not required): Describe your evacuation plans, including the location where you will evacuate and the mode of transportation you will use.

### Essential Supplies (ID: 3)
* **Essential Supplies** (`essential_supplies`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ **Yes**: You have the necessary supplies to last for at least 3 days.
	+ **No**: You do not have the necessary supplies.

### Important Contacts (ID: 4)
* **Important Contacts** (`important_contacts`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ **Very Important**: The contact is essential for your safety and well-being.
	+ **Not Important**: The contact is not essential for your safety and well-being.

### Additional Info (ID: 5)
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional information that is relevant to your situation, such as medical conditions or special needs.

### Weather Updates (ID: 6)
* **Weather Updates** (`weather_updates`, select_one, not required):
	+ **Yes**: You have the means to receive weather updates.
	+ **No**: You do not have the means to receive weather updates.

### Emergency Precautions (ID: 7)
* **Emergency Precautions** (`emergency_precautions`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ **Yes**: You have taken emergency precautions to prepare for the hurricane.
	+ **No**: You have not taken emergency precautions.

### Safety Plan (ID: 8)
* **Safety Plan** (`safety_plan`, select_one, not required):
	+ **Yes**: You have a safety plan in place.
	+ **No**: You do not have a safety plan.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about a question, choose the option that best describes your situation.
* If you have any other important information that is not listed in the form, use the "Additional Info" field to provide it.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.
