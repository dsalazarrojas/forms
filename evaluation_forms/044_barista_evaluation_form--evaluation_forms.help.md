# Barista Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of baristas in a coffee shop, assessing their skills and knowledge in various areas, including technical proficiency, workflow and operations, customer service, and teamwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have filled out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter the Barista Name and Reviewer Name in the respective fields.
3. Select the Review Date from the calendar picker.
4. For each section, choose the corresponding rating for the barista's performance.
5. In the Key Strengths and Areas for Improvement fields, provide a brief description of the barista's strengths and areas for improvement.
6. Enter your Manager Signature to confirm the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Barista Name**: Enter the name of the barista being evaluated.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name, as the reviewer of the barista's performance.
* **Review Date**: Select the date of the performance review.
* **Espresso Dial-in and Extraction**: Rate the barista's ability to adjust grind and pull consistent shots (1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Fair, 3 = Good, 4 = Excellent, 5 = Exceptional).
* **Milk Steaming and Texture**: Rate the barista's ability to control micro-foam consistency and temperature (1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Fair, 3 = Good, 4 = Excellent, 5 = Exceptional).
* **Latte Art and Presentation**: Rate the barista's aesthetic quality of drink presentation (1 = Needs Improvement,  2 = Fair, 3 = Good, 4 = Excellent, 5 = Exceptional).
* **Menu and Recipe Knowledge**: Rate the barista's understanding of specialty drink ratios and ingredients (1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Fair, 3 = Good, 4 = Excellent, 5 = Exceptional).
* **Speed of Service**: Rate the barista's ability to manage a high volume of customers while maintaining quality (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Bar Sanitation and Organization**: Rate the barista's ability to keep the station clean during and between rushes (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Customer Interaction and Friendliness**: Rate the barista's greeting, eye contact, and rapport building with customers (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Teamwork and Communication**: Rate the barista's ability to support fellow baristas and floor staff (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Key Strengths**: Describe the barista's key strengths and areas of excellence.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe areas where the barista needs improvement.
* **Overall Performance Rating**: Rate the barista's overall performance (Unsatisfactory, Meeting Expectations, Exceeding Expectations).
* **Manager Signature**: Enter your name to confirm the evaluation.
* **Barista Self-Reflection or Comments**: Provide any additional feedback or comments from the barista.
