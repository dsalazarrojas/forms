# Probation Period Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Probation Period Form is designed to collect information regarding the start and end dates of a probation period, as well as any remarks or comments related to the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Start Date" field and enter the date when the probation period begins.
2. Click on "End Date" field and enter the date when the probation period ends.
3. In the "Remarks" field, you can add any additional comments or notes regarding the probation period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the probation period starts. This field is not mandatory.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the probation period ends. This field is not mandatory.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or notes related to the probation period. This field is not mandatory.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the start and end dates correctly to avoid any errors.
- Use the "Remarks" field to add any relevant information that might be useful for reference.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
