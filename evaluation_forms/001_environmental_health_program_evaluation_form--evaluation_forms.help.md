# Environmental Health Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the performance of an Environmental Health Program. This form helps program participants or evaluators to provide feedback and comments on their experience with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the Environmental Health Program in the "Program Name" field.
2. Enter the location of the program in the "Program Location" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the program in the "Program Start Date" and "Program End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Enter a brief description of the program in the "Program Description" field.
5. Select the performance rubric that best describes the program's performance in the "Performance Rubric" field.
6. Enter your overall ratings of the program in the "Ratings" field.
7. Enter any feedback or comments you have about the program in the "Feedback" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the form in the "Comments" field.
11. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field to indicate whether you have completed the form accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the Environmental Health Program you are evaluating.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the program you are evaluating.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the program.
* **Performance Rubric** (`performance_rubric`, select_one, required): Select the performance rubric that best describes the program's performance. Options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Performance Rubric Other** (`performance_rubric_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the performance rubric if necessary.
* **Ratings** (`ratings`, select_multiple, required): Enter your overall ratings of the program.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments you have about the program.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel, and the information provided should be accurate and relevant to the program being evaluated.
