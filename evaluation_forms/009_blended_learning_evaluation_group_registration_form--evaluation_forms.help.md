# Blended Learning Evaluation Group Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your group for our blended learning evaluation program. This program aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your group's blended learning program, allowing us to understand their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information about your group before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form in sections, starting with the institution and participant details.
3. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form, as the information will be used to tailor the evaluation to your group's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of your institution where you are registering your group.
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the type of institution you represent (e.g., K-12 School, University, etc.).
* **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the primary contact for your group.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter the email address of the primary contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of the primary contact.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the address of your institution.
* **Number of Participants in Group** (`group_size`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total number of participants in your group.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select the types of participants in your group (e.g., Students, Faculty, Administrators, etc.).
* **Learning Level** (`learning_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the level of learning (e.g., Elementary, Middle School, High School, etc.).
* **Program Focus Area** (`program_focus`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the focus area of your program (e.g., STEM, Humanities, Business, etc.).
* **Current Blended Learning Format** (`blended_format`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the current blended learning format of your program (e.g., Synchronous online sessions, Asynchronous modules, etc.).
* **Primary Learning Platform Used** (`technology_platform`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the primary learning platform used by your group (if applicable).
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the start date of your blended learning program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the end date of your blended learning program.
* **Primary Evaluation Goals** (`evaluation_goals`, `text`, **required**): Enter the primary evaluation goals of your program.
* **Have you participated in evaluations before?** (`previous_evaluation`, `select_one`, **optional**): If you have participated in evaluations before, please select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **I consent to participate in this evaluation program** (`consent_to_participate`, `select_one`, **required**): Select "I agree" to participate in the evaluation program.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of your registration.

Note: Make sure to review the information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
