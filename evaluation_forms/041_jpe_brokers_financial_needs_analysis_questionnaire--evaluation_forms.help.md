# jpe_brokers_financial_needs_analysis_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to help brokers analyze the financial needs of a customer or prospect. The form is used to collect demographic information, financial needs, and employment status to provide a comprehensive understanding of the individual's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "name" field.
2. Enter the customer's organization name in the "organization" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Indicate the customer's financial needs by selecting one of the options in the "financial_needs" field.
6. If the customer has other financial needs, enter them in the "financial_needs_other" field.
7. Enter the customer's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
8. Indicate the customer's employment status by selecting one of the options in the "employment_status" field.
9. Enter the customer's income in the "income" field.
10. If the customer has other income sources, indicate them by selecting multiple options in the "income_other" field.
11. If the customer has other income sources, enter their values in the "income_other_value" field.
12. If the customer is employed, select their employment income options in the "employment_income" field.
13. If the customer has other employment income sources, enter their values in the "employment_income_value" field.
14. If the customer has other employment income sources, enter their values in the "employment_income_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2): Enter the customer's name (e.g., John Doe).
* **Organization** (3): Enter the customer's organization name.
* **Email** (4): Enter the customer's email address in the format johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone** (5): Enter the customer's phone number in the format 1 123 123 1234.
* **Financial needs** (6): Select "Yes" if the customer has financial needs, "No" otherwise.
* **Financial needs other** (7): If the customer has other financial needs, enter them in this field.
* **Date of birth** (9): Enter the customer's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Employment status** (10): Select the customer's employment status.
* **Income** (11): Enter the customer's income.
* **Income other** (12): If the customer has other income sources, select multiple options.
* **Income other value** (13): If the customer has other income sources, enter their values.
* **Employment income** (14): If the customer is employed, select their employment income options.
* **Employment income value** (15): If the customer has employment income sources, enter their values.
* **Employment income frequency** (17): If the customer is employed, select whether they receive income from the selected employment options.
* **Employment income other** (20): If the customer has other employment income sources, enter them in this field.
* **Employment income other value** (22): If the customer has other employment income sources, enter their values.
* **Income other** (21): If the customer has other income sources, enter them in this field.
* **Income other value** (22): If the customer has other income sources, enter their values.
