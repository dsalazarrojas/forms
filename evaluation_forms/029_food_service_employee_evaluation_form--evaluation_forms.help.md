# Food Service Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of food service employees. It collects feedback and ratings on various aspects of their work, including their overall performance, departmental performance, and evaluation status. The form is used to provide a comprehensive assessment of an employee's strengths and weaknesses, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, select the name of the employee you are evaluating from the dropdown list.
2.  Provide feedback on the employee's performance, using the text field for your comments.
3.  Rate the employee's performance using the rating field.
4.  Enter the date the evaluation is being conducted.
5.  Select the time of the evaluation.
6.  Choose the department the employee belongs to.
7.  Select the shift the employee was working during the evaluation.
8.  Add any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
9.  Enter the employee's email address (optional).
10. Enter the employee's phone number (optional).
11.  Provide an additional rating for the employee's performance.
12.  Enter your comments about the employee's performance.
13.  Enter the date and time the evaluation was completed.
14.  Enter the date the form was created.
15.  Enter the last time the form was updated.
16.  Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee**: <select> (<name>): Select the name of the employee being evaluated from the list.
*   **feedback**: <text> (<feedback>): Enter your feedback about the employee's performance.
*   **rating**: <number> (<rating>): Rate the employee's performance, with higher numbers indicating better performance.
*   **date**: <date> (<date>): Enter the date of the evaluation.
*   **time**: <time> (<time>): Select the time of the evaluation.
*   **supervisor**: <select> (<supervisor>): Select the name of the employee's supervisor.
*   **department**: <select> (<department>): Select the department the employee belongs to.
*   **shift**: <select> (<shift>): Select the shift the employee was working during the evaluation.
*   **note**: <text> (<note>): Enter any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
*   **email**: <text> (<email>): Enter the employee's email address.
*   **phone**: <text> (<phone>): Enter the employee's phone number.
*   **rating2**: <number> (<rating2>): Enter an additional rating for the employee's performance.
*   **comments**: <text> (<comments>): Enter your comments about the employee's performance.
*   **evaluation_date**: <date> (<evaluation_date>): Enter the date of the evaluation.
*   **employee_rating**: <number> (<employee_rating>): Enter the employee's performance rating.
*   **departmental_rating**: <number> (<departmental_rating>): Enter the departmental rating for the employee.
*   **overall_rating**: <number> (<overall_rating>): Enter the overall rating for the employee.
*   **evaluation_period**: <date> (<evaluation_period>): Enter the evaluation period.
*   **employee_comments**: <text> (<employee_comments>): Enter comments from the employee.
*   **supervisor_comments**: <text> (<supervisor_comments>): Enter comments from the supervisor.
*   **evaluation_status**: <select> (<evaluation_status>): Select the status of the evaluation (e.g., Pending, In Progress, Completed).
*   **completed_at**: <date> (<completed_at>): Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
*   **created_at**: <date> (<created_at>): Enter the date the form was created.
*   **updated_at**: <date> (<updated_at>): Enter the last time the form was updated.
*   **submitted_at**: <date> (<submitted_at>): Enter the date the form was submitted.
