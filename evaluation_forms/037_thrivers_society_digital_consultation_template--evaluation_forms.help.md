# Thrivers Society Digital Consultation Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thrivers Society Digital Consultation Template is a tool designed to gather information from clients to provide them with better digital consultation services. This form will help us understand your specific needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor our support to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your Client Name in the space provided.
2. Input your Email address, where we can contact you with updates and information.
3. If you have a Phone Number, please enter it so we can reach out to you more easily.
4. In the Feedback section, provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client you are representing or the name of the person you are contacting us on behalf of.
* **Email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you with updates, questions, and information. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number where we can reach out to you, please enter it so we can stay in touch more easily.
* **Feedback**: In this section, you can provide any additional comments, questions, or concerns you may have. This is your chance to let us know how we can better assist you.
