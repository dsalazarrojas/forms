# Manuscript Review Process Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document the process of manuscript review, including submission details, reviewer information, and evaluation scores. It will help in ensuring that the review process is thorough, transparent, and maintainable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have the necessary information ready, such as the manuscript details and the reviewer's information.
2. Fill in the Submission Information section with the manuscript ID number and title.
3. Complete the Manuscript Details section with the manuscript type, length, version, and references.
4. Enter the Reviewer Information section with the reviewer's name, affiliation, department, and contact information.
5. Evaluate the manuscript using the Evaluation Scale and select the relevant level of relevance.
6. Provide detailed feedback in the Feedback section.
7. Enter the Reviewer Rating and Evaluation Score.
8. Enter the Review Date and Manuscript Status.
9. Enter any Additional Comments or Reviewer Comments if necessary.
10. Sign and date the form with your reviewer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Information** (1, text, required): Fill in the manuscript ID number and title.
* **Manuscript Details** (2, text, required): Provide the manuscript type, length, version, and references.
* **Reviewer Information** (3, text, required): Enter the reviewer's name, affiliation, department, and contact information.
* **Evaluation Scale** (4, select_one, required): Select the level of relevance of the manuscript (Highly Relevant, Not Relevant, Somewhat Relevant).
* **Feedback** (5, text, required): Provide detailed feedback on the manuscript.
* **Reviewer Rating** (6, number, required): Enter a numerical rating of the reviewer's evaluation.
* **Review Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer Comments** (8, text, required): Provide any comments on the manuscript.
* **Additional Comments** (9, text, optional): Enter any additional comments on the manuscript.
* **Reviewer Signature** (10, text, optional): Sign and date the form with the reviewer's signature.
* **Submission Date** (11, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Publication Date** (12, date, optional): Enter the expected or actual publication date.
* **Manuscript Status** (13, select_one, required): Select the manuscript status (In Progress, Rejected, Published, Withdrawn).
* **Evaluation Score** (14, number, required): Enter a numerical score for the evaluation.
* **Reviewer Name** (15, text, required): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Affiliation** (16, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's affiliation.
* **Department** (17, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's department.
* **Institution** (18, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's institution.
* **Reviewer Email** (19, email, required): Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Reviewer Phone** (20, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Manuscript Version** (21, text, optional): Enter the manuscript version.
* **Manuscript Type** (22, text, optional): Enter the manuscript type.
* **Manuscript Length** (23, number, optional): Enter the manuscript length.
* **Manuscript References** (24, text, optional): Enter the manuscript references.
* **Reviewer Notes** (25, text, optional): Enter any additional reviewer notes.

## Tips
1. Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
2. Use specific, clear, and concise language in all fields.
3. Be thorough and detailed in your feedback and review comments.
4. Keep the form organized and neat for easy reference.
