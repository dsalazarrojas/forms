# Fitness Section Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness Section Questionnaire is used to collect information about an individual's fitness profile. This form will help us better understand their fitness habits, lifestyle, and medical history to provide tailored recommendations and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information on the first page.
2. Answer the questions on the second page about your fitness level, exercise frequency, and other relevant health-related topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First name**: Enter your first name.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This is your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.

  - **Last name**: Enter your last name.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This is your surname as it appears on your identification documents.

  - **Email**: Enter your email address.
  * Required: No
  * Type: email
  * Description: This is the address you use to send and receive emails.

  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This is the number you use to make phone calls.

  - **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  * Required: No
  * Type: date
  * Description: The date you were born.

  - **Occupation**: Enter your occupation or job title.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This is the title of your job or profession.

  - **Address**: Enter your address.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This is the address where you reside.

  - **Medical conditions**: Enter any pre-existing medical conditions.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: List any medical conditions you have, including but not limited to allergies, injuries, or chronic illnesses.

  - **Health lifestyle**: Describe your current lifestyle.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This will help us understand your habits, including diet, exercise, and other factors that influence your health.

  - **Fitness level**: Describe your current fitness level.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This will help us assess your current physical fitness and ability to perform daily tasks.

  - **Exercise frequency**: Describe how often you exercise.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: Tell us about your exercise habits, including frequency, intensity, and type of exercise.

  - **Diet**: Describe your current diet.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This will help us understand your current eating habits, including food choices and portion sizes.

  - **Sleep**: Describe your sleep habits.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This will help us understand how well you sleep and any issues you've experienced.

  - **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This will help us understand your current medication regimen.

  - **Medical history**: Describe your medical history.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: This will help us understand any significant health events or experiences you've had.

  - **Emergency contacts**: List emergency contacts.
  * Required: No
  * Type: text
  * Description: Provide contact information for family members or other people you want us to reach out to in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Be as honest and thorough as possible when answering these questions.
* Use clear and concise language to describe your fitness and medical profile.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, please provide as much detail as you can.

This will help us provide the most accurate and beneficial recommendations for you.
