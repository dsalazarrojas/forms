# Appraisal Dispute Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and resolve disputes regarding appraisal values. It is intended for property owners to report their concerns or disagreements with the appraised value of their property. The information collected will be reviewed and addressed by the relevant parties to facilitate a fair and accurate resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property details (Page 1: Property Details).
2. Enter the property's address (Page 2: Property Address).
3. Select the county of the property (Page 3: County).
4. Enter the owner's name, email address, and phone number (Page 4: Owner Information).
5. Provide your estimated value for the property (Page 10: Your Estimated Value).
6. Select the reason for your dispute (Page 12: Reason for Dispute).
7. Provide a detailed explanation for your dispute (Page 13: Detailed Explanation).
8. Attach supporting documentation (Page 14: Supporting Documentation Attached). Note that this field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details** (Page 1: property_details): Enter a brief description of your property.
* **Property Address** (Page 2: property_address): Enter the full address of the property, including street number and city.
* **County** (Page 3: property_county): Select the county where the property is located.
* **Owner Information** (Page 5: owner_info): This field is provided for your reference only.
* **Owner Name** (Page 6: owner_name): Enter the full name of the property owner.
* **Email Address** (Page 7: owner_email): Enter the email address of the property owner.
* **Phone Number** (Page 8: owner_phone): Enter the phone number of the property owner.
* **Dispute Information** (Page 9: dispute_info): This field is provided for your reference only.
* **Appraised Value** (Page 10: appraised_value): Enter the appraised value of your property as stated on the appraisal report.
* **Your Estimated Value** (Page 11: disputed_value): Enter your estimated value for the property.
* **Reason for Dispute** (Page 12: dispute_reason): Select all that apply from the reasons provided for the dispute. You can select multiple reasons.
* **Detailed Explanation** (Page 13: dispute_details): Provide a detailed explanation for your dispute.
* **Supporting Documentation Attached** (Page 14: supporting_docs): Check if you have attached supporting documentation for your dispute.
