# Global Disease Prevention Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Disease Prevention Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and insights from individuals who have participated in or been affected by the program. Your input will help us evaluate the program's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form page by page, starting from the "First Page" and moving on to the subsequent pages.
2. For each field, please provide your accurate and honest response to the best of your abilities.
3. If a field has an "other" option, please use it only if your selected option is not listed in the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Please enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, **required**): Please rate your overall experience with the program, from 1 to 5.
* **Select Option** (`select_option`, select_one, **required**): Please choose one option that best describes your experience.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **required**): Please enter the date you participated in or were affected by the program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **required**): Please enter the time you participated in or were affected by the program.
* **Note** (`note`, note, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them here.

### Second Page

* **Program Outcome** (`program_outcome`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select all the outcomes that apply to your experience with the program.
* **Other Program Outcome** (`program_outcome_other`, text, **optional**): If none of the above options apply, please describe your experience with the program's outcome.
* **Second Page** (`global_disease_prevention_program_evaluation_form_second_page`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this page.

### Third Page

* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, **required**): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the program's services.
* **Other Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_other`, text, **optional**): If none of the above options apply, please describe your satisfaction with the program's services.
* **Third Page** (`global_disease_prevention_program_evaluation_form_third_page`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this page.

### Fourth Page

* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select all the locations that apply to your experience with the program.
* **Other Location** (`location_other`, text, **optional**): If none of the above options apply, please describe your experience with the program's location.
* **Location Distance** (`location_distance`, text, **optional**): If you'd like to provide more specific details about the location's distance from your residence or the program's facilities.
* **Fourth Page** (`global_disease_prevention_program_evaluation_form_fourth_page`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this page.

### Fifth Page

* **Program Outcome Measure** (`program_outcome_measure`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select all the measures that apply to your experience with the program.
* **Other Program Outcome Measure** (`program_outcome_other`, text, **optional**): If none of the above options apply, please describe your experience with the program's measures.
* **Fifth Page** (`global_disease_prevention_program_evaluation_form_fifth_page`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this page.

### Sixth Page

* **Evaluation Outcome** (`evaluation_outcome`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this page.
* **Sixth Page** (`global_disease_prevention_program_evaluation_form_sixth_page`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about this page.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Please review your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We appreciate your participation in evaluating the Global Disease Prevention Program, and your feedback will help us improve the program for future participants.
