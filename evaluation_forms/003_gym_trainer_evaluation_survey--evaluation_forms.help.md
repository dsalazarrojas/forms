# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from gym-goers about their experiences with trainers at the gym. The evaluation survey helps us understand how well our trainers are performing and identify areas for improvement.

To ensure that this form is filled out in a way that is helpful for both the trainer and the gym, please take a few minutes to carefully consider your answers and provide honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Choose the page that corresponds to the question that best fits your experience with the trainer.
* Select the answer that best describes your experience for each question.
* Provide any additional feedback you may have in the text box at the end of the survey.
* Double-check that you've filled out all required fields and that your answers make sense in the context of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is the trainer professional?** (`professionalism`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose "True" if you believe the trainer has a high level of professionalism.
	+ Choose "False" if you do not believe the trainer has a high level of professionalism.
* **How does the trainer communicate during classes?** (`communication`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose "Always" if the trainer communicates clearly and consistently with you.
	+ Choose "Often" if the trainer usually communicates well, but sometimes struggles.
	+ Choose "Seldom" if the trainer rarely communicates with you.
	+ Choose "Never" if the trainer does not communicate at all.
* **How would you rate the trainer's motivation during class?** (`motivation`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose "High" if you believe the trainer is highly motivated during classes.
	+ Choose "Medium" if the trainer's motivation is average.
	+ Choose "Low" if you believe the trainer lacks motivation.
* **Is the trainer available when you need them?** (`availability`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose "True" if the trainer is usually available when you need them.
	+ Choose "False" if the trainer is rarely or never available.
* **How clear is the trainer's training plan?** (`training_plan`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose "Clear" if the trainer's training plan is well-structured and easy to follow.
	+ Choose "Somewhat clear" if the trainer's plan is mostly clear, but has some gaps.
	+ Choose "Not clear" if the trainer's plan is often unclear or confusing.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional):
	+ Use this text box to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience with the trainer.
* **Trainer name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the trainer's name as you know it.
* **Training date** (`training_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your training session in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Gym name** (`gym_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the gym where you took the training session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the trainer's email address, if you know it.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the trainer's phone number, if you know it.
