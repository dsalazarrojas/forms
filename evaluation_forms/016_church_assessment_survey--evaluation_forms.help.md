# Church Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to gather feedback from church members, attendees, and visitors to understand their experiences, satisfaction, and spiritual growth within the church community. The collected data will aid in identifying areas for improvement and help the church to better serve its members and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate answer option for each question.
2. If a question requires a numerical rating, choose the most suitable rating from 1 to 5.
3. If a question allows for text input, provide any relevant information or feedback.
4. Review and submit your answers to ensure that you have provided the most accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a member of this church?** (Membership status): Please select "Member" if you are officially a member of the church. If not, choose "Regular Attendee" if you regularly attend services, "Occasional Visitor" if you occasionally visit, or "First-time Guest" if this is your first visit.
* **How long have you been attending?** (Years attending): Select the number of years you have been attending the church.
* **Your Age Range** (Age range): Select the age range that best describes you.
* **Rate your satisfaction with your spiritual growth here** (Spiritual growth satisfaction): Rate your level of satisfaction with your spiritual growth, choosing from "Very Satisfied," "Satisfied," "Neutral," "Dissatisfied," or "Very Dissatisfied."
* **How helpful do you find the weekly sermons?** (Sermon helpfulness): Rate the helpfulness of the weekly sermons, choosing from "Extremely Helpful," "Helpful," "Somewhat," or "Not Helpful."
* **Rate the worship/music experience** (Worship experience): Rate the worship and music experience, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Fair," or "Poor."
* **Are you currently serving in any ministry?** (Ministry participation): Choose "Yes - Regularly" if you regularly participate in a ministry, "Yes - Occasionally" if you occasionally participate, "No - But Interested" if you are interested but do not participate, or "False" if you are not interested or participating.
* **Which ministry areas do you participate in?** (Ministry areas): Select all the areas where you participate in the church's ministries.
* **Do you feel welcomed and included in our community?** (Feeling welcomed): Rate your feeling of being welcomed and included in the church community, choosing from "Always," "Usually," "Sometimes," "Rarely," or "Never."
* **Rate the effectiveness of our communication** (Communication effectiveness): Rate the effectiveness of the church's communication, such as news, announcements, and social media, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Needs Improvement," or "Poor."
* **How would you rate our physical facilities?** (Facility rating): Rate the comfort, cleanliness, and accessibility of the church's physical facilities, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Fair," or "Poor."
* **What is one thing you would change or improve?** (Top priority change): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the church.
* **Any other feedback you'd like to share?** (Additional comments): Enter any other feedback or suggestions you have for the church.
