# Basketball Player Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Player Evaluation Form is designed to collect evaluations of a basketball player's performance during a game or practice. This form is used to assess a player's skills, physical attributes, and intangible qualities, providing a comprehensive understanding of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Evaluation Date**: Please enter the date of the game or practice being evaluated.
2. **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name as the person evaluating the player.
3. **Evaluator Role**: Select the role you play in the organization (Coach, Assistant Coach, Athletic Director, Scout, or Other).
4. **Player Name**: Enter the name of the player being evaluated.
5. **Player Number**: Enter the player's number (if applicable).
6. **Position**: Select the player's position on the team (Point Guard, Shooting Guard, Small Forward, Power Forward, Center, or Utility).
7. **Team Name**: Enter the name of the player's team.
8. **Offense Section**: Rate the player's skills in the following areas:
	* **Ball Handling**: Rate the player's ball-handling skills on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Shooting Accuracy**: Rate the player's shooting accuracy on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Court Vision and Passing**: Rate the player's court vision and passing ability on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Footwork and Footspeed**: Rate the player's footwork and footspeed on a scale of 1-10.
9. **Defense Section**: Rate the player's skills in the following areas:
	* **Man-to-Man Defense**: Rate the player's man-to-man defense on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Rebounding Ability**: Rate the player's rebounding ability on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Lateral Quickness**: Rate the player's lateral quickness on a scale of 1-10.
10. **Physical Attributes Section**: Rate the player's physical attributes in the following areas:
	* **Strength and Conditioning**: Rate the player's strength and conditioning on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Overall Athleticism**: Rate the player's overall athleticism on a scale of 1-10.
11. **Intangibles Section**: Rate the player's intangibles in the following areas:
	* **Basketball IQ and Decision Making**: Rate the player's basketball IQ and decision-making on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Coachability and Attitude**: Rate the player's coachability and attitude on a scale of 1-10.
	* **Leadership Qualities**: Rate the player's leadership qualities on a scale of 1-10.
12. **Summary Section**: Provide a brief summary of the player's strengths and areas for improvement.
13. **Strengths**: List the player's main strengths.
14. **Areas for Improvement**: List areas where the player needs to improve.
15. **Overall Rating**: Rate the player's overall performance on a scale of 1-10.
16. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the player.
