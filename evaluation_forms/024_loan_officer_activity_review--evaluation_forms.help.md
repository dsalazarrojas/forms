# Loan Officer Activity Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loan Officer Activity Review form is designed to help evaluate the activities and performance of loan officers. This form provides a structured approach to tracking and assessing loan officer activities, allowing for regular feedback, guidance, and improvement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and context before starting to fill out the form.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your understanding of the loan officer's activities and performance.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for all required fields.
4. Use the provided options to select the most accurate responses.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Officer Review Period** (`review_period`): This field allows you to select the period of time you are reviewing the loan officer's activities. Please choose the most relevant time frame, such as 'daily', 'weekly', or 'monthly'.
* **Review Start Date** (`review_start_date`): Enter the start date of the review period. This date should reflect the beginning of the time frame selected in the previous field.
* **Review End Date** (`review_end_date`): Enter the end date of the review period. This date should reflect the end of the time frame selected in the previous field.
* **Activities** (`activities`): Please select all activities that the loan officer was engaged in during the review period. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for each activity.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`): Rate the loan officer's performance during the review period. Please choose from 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the loan officer's performance or activities during the review period.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Suggest any next steps or actions that the loan officer can take to improve their performance or activities.
* **Assigned Manager** (`assigned_manager`): Indicate whether the loan officer has been assigned a manager for the review period. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): Enter the date the loan officer was assigned a manager.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`): Select the frequency of loan officer reviews, such as 'daily', 'weekly', or 'monthly'.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Provide any comments or feedback from the assigned manager.
* **Loan Officer** (`loan_officer`): Indicate whether the loan officer is currently active or not.
* **Loan Product** (`loan_product`): Indicate whether the loan officer is working on a loan product or not.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary information and context before completing this form.
* Use clear and concise language when providing answers for all fields.
* If unsure about any field or its purpose, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you need help or clarification, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
