# Personal Wellness Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Personal Wellness Assessment Form" is a tool designed to collect information about an individual's personal well-being. This form helps in assessing an individual's health status and providing relevant insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your **Age** in the "Age" field.
5. Record your **Height** in the "Height" field.
6. Enter your **Weight** in the "Weight" field.
7. Enter your **Blood Pressure** in the "Blood Pressure" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Wellness Assessment Form (Text Field)**: This field is used to describe the form's title, which is not a required field.
* **Name (Text Field)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the form.
* **Email (Email Field)**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth (Date Field)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Age (Number Field)**: Enter your age as a whole number.
* **Height (Number Field)**: Record your height in centimeters or meters (e.g., 170cm or 1.7m).
* **Weight (Number Field)**: Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds (e.g., 70kg or 154lbs).
* **Blood Pressure (Number Field)**: Enter your blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (e.g., 120/80mmHg).
* **Blood Pressure**: Please note that this field requires both systolic and diastolic values. For example, "120/80".
