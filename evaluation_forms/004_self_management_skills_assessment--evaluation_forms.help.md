# Self Management Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an individual's self-management skills, which are essential for personal and professional growth. The form will help you identify areas of strength and weakness in your self-management skills, allowing you to focus on developing and improving them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Information**: Fill in your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
2. **Self-Management Skills**: Select all the skills you are proficient in from the list provided. You can choose as many or as few skills as you like.
3. **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you completed this assessment.
4. **Assessment Time**: Enter the time you completed this assessment.
5. **Self-Management Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your self-management skills.
6. **Submitter**: Enter your name and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **personal_info**: Your personal details, including your name.
- **self_mgmt_skills**: Check all the skills you are proficient in, such as Skill 1, Skill 2, Skill 3, Skill 4, and Skill 5.
- **assessment_date**: Enter the date you completed this assessment.
- **assessment_time**: Enter the time you completed this assessment.
- **self_mgmt_comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your self-management skills.
- **submitter**: Your name and any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
* You can take as much time as you need to complete this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
