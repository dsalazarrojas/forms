# Film Course Participant Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from course participants about their overall satisfaction and experience with the film course. It aims to help us understand what worked well and what could be improved to enhance future courses. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select one of the options that best describes your overall satisfaction with the course.
2. Choose the materials provided during the course that you found most helpful or useful.
3. Evaluate the performance of the instructors, using the provided scale.
4. Select the areas of improvement you feel the course needs to focus on.
5. Rate the course format and structure.
6. Choose your level of satisfaction with the communication from the instructors.
7. Evaluate your overall experience with the course.
8. Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the course.
9. Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
10. If applicable, provide your contact number for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (1)**: Select one of the options to indicate your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Course Materials (2)**: Choose the materials you found most helpful or useful during the course. Please select all that apply.
* **Instructors' Performance (3)**: Rate the performance of the instructors using the provided scale.
* **Overall Improvement (4)**: Select the areas of improvement you feel the course needs to focus on.
* **Course Format (5)**: Rate the course format and structure using the provided scale.
* **Communication with Instructors (6)**: Choose your level of satisfaction with the communication from the instructors. Please select all that apply.
* **Overall Experience (7)**: Evaluate your overall experience with the course.
* **Comments and Suggestions (8)**: Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the course.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Contact Number (10)**: If applicable, provide your contact number for further discussion.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing your email and contact number will allow us to follow up with you about your feedback.
