# Library Director Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate the effectiveness of the Library Director in providing quality services to library patrons. The evaluation form gathers feedback on the director's performance, suggestions for improvement, and ratings on their overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your accurate and honest information.
2. Answer the questions as thoroughly as possible, providing clear and concise descriptions of your experiences and opinions.
3. Submit the form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it should be displayed in the evaluation form.
- **Last Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it should be displayed in the evaluation form.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Please enter your email address, phone number, or other contact details where we can reach you for follow-up questions or concerns.
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): If you have an appointment with the library director, please select the date of the evaluation.
- **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, `time`, required): If you have an appointment with the library director, please select the time of the evaluation.
- **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, `text`, optional): If you have feedback on a specific period or timeframe, please describe it here.
- **Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, required): Provide detailed feedback on your experience with the library director, including any positive or negative comments.
- **Suggestions** (`evaluation_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for the library director or the library as a whole.
- **Rating** (`evaluation_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the library director's effectiveness on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Feedback** (`evaluation_comments_2`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the library director or the library.
- **Suggestions 2** (`evaluation_suggestions_2`, `text`, optional): Share any additional suggestions or ideas you have for the library director or the library.
- **Form Submitted By** (`evaluation_form_submitted`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the type of form submitter, either "Form Submitter" or "Evaluation Form".
