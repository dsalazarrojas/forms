# Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Form is designed to assess and document potential risks associated with a specific situation or activity. It helps to identify, categorize, and evaluate the severity of risks to inform decision-making and mitigation strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the `risk_assessment_form` field, which is the title of the form you are about to complete.
2.  Move on to select the relevant `risk_category` from the list provided, which helps to further categorize the risk.
3.  For each selected `risk_category`, choose the corresponding `risk_level` (Low, Medium, or High) to indicate the severity of the risk.
4.  In the `risk_description` field, provide a brief description of the risk you are assessing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **risk_assessment_form**: This field is the title of the form you are about to complete.
	+ Enter a brief and descriptive title for the risk assessment.
* **risk_category**: This field helps to categorize the risk into specific areas.
	+ Select the category that best suits the risk you are assessing (e.g., financial, operational, strategic, etc.).
* **risk_level** (Low, Medium, High): This field determines the severity of the risk.
	+ Choose the level that best matches the risk you have selected (Low for minimal impact, Medium for moderate impact, High for significant impact).
* **risk_description**: This field provides a detailed description of the risk.
	+ Type in a clear and concise description of the risk, including its context and potential consequences.
