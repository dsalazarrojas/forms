# Operational Readiness Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to help evaluators assess the operational readiness of a team or project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the options provided for each field to select the most appropriate response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This field is for you to provide a brief overview of the team or project you are assessing.
* **Operational Readiness**: Select one of the options to indicate the current operational readiness of the team or project. This field is required and has three options:
	+ On-time: The team or project is on schedule and meeting its objectives.
	+ Behind schedule: The team or project is not meeting its objectives or is delayed.
	+ Delayed: The team or project is significantly delayed and not meeting its objectives.
* **Team Performance**: Select multiple options that best describe the team's performance. This field is required and has four options:
	+ Excellent: The team is performing exceptionally well.
	+ Good: The team is performing well.
	+ Fair: The team is performing adequately.
	+ Poor: The team is not performing well.
* **Resource Availability**: Enter the current availability of resources for the team or project. This field is required and should be a number.
* **Situational Awareness**: Select one of the options to indicate the situational awareness of the team or project. This field is required and has three options:
	+ High: The team or project has a high level of situational awareness.
	+ Medium: The team or project has a medium level of situational awareness.
	+ Low: The team or project has a low level of situational awareness.
* **Operational Challenges**: Select multiple options that best describe the operational challenges faced by the team or project. This field is required and has three options:
	+ Technical issues: The team or project is facing technical challenges.
	+ Communication breakdown: The team or project is experiencing communication issues.
	+ Resource constraints: The team or project is facing resource constraints.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information regarding the team or project. This field is required and should be a detailed note.
* **Communication**: Select multiple options that best describe the communication between team members or project stakeholders. This field is required and has four options:
	+ Excellent: Communication between team members or project stakeholders is excellent.
	+ Good: Communication between team members or project stakeholders is good.
	+ Fair: Communication between team members or project stakeholders is fair.
	+ Poor: Communication between team members or project stakeholders is poor.
* **Submit**: Select one of the options to confirm that you have finished answering the form. This field is required and has two options:
	+ True: You have finished answering the form.
	+ False: You have not finished answering the form.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person completing the form. This field is required.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person completing the form. This field is required.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are completing the form. This field is required.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are completing the form. This field is required.
* **Submit Comment**: Provide any additional comments regarding submitting the form. This field is required.
* **Submit Answer**: Select one of the options to confirm that you have finished answering the form. This field is required and has two options:
	+ True: You have finished answering the form.
	+ False: You have not finished answering the form.

Note: Make sure to answer all fields thoroughly and accurately to get a comprehensive assessment of the operational readiness of the team or project.
