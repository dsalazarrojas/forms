# online_selfcare_course_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluations from end-users on an online self-care course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `course_name` field with the name of the course you are evaluating.
2. Provide the name of the course instructor in the `course_instructor` field (if applicable).
3. Describe the specific section(s) of the course you are evaluating in the `course_section` field.
4. Enter your comments and thoughts in the `form_body` field.
5. Select whether the course outcomes were achieved (Yes/No) in the `course_outcomes` field.
6. Provide any additional comments on the course in the `form_comments` field (optional).
7. Make any course suggestions in the `course_suggestions` field (optional).
8. Indicate whether you would definitely recommend, maybe recommend, or not recommend the course in the `course_recommend` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **online_selfcare_course_evaluation_form (Note)**: This is a brief description of the form, reminding you that this is the online self-care course evaluation form.
* **course_name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the course being evaluated.
* **course_instructor (Text, Optional)**: If you know the name of the instructor, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **course_section (Text, Required)**: Describe the specific section or part of the course you are evaluating.
* **form_body (Note, Required)**: Enter your comments and thoughts on the course.
* **course_outcomes (Select Multiple, Required)**: Indicate whether the course outcomes were achieved.
	+ Select "Yes" if the course outcomes were achieved as expected.
	+ Select "No" if the course outcomes were not achieved.
* **course_evaluation (Text, Optional)**: Provide any additional evaluation or feedback on the course (optional).
* **form_footer (Note)**: This is a brief note reminding you to provide any final comments or suggestions on the form.
* **course_comments (Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments on the course (optional).
* **course_suggestions (Text, Optional)**: Suggest any improvements or changes to the course (optional).
* **course_recommend (Select One, Optional)**: Indicate whether you would definitely recommend, maybe recommend, or not recommend the course.
	+ Select "Definitely Recommend" if you highly recommend the course.
	+ Select "Maybe Recommend" if you somewhat recommend the course.
	+ Select "Not Recommend" if you do not recommend the course.
