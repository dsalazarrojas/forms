# Volleyball Player Evaluation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volleyball Player Evaluation Form Template is a tool for objective assessment of a player's skills and progress. It is meant to be used by coaches, trainers, or evaluators to evaluate a player's abilities in various aspects of the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the player's recent performance**: Before filling out the form, take a moment to review the player's recent games, practices, or other relevant information to get a clear picture of their abilities.
2. **Complete each field**: Go through each section of the form, and answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
3. **Be specific and objective**: When evaluating the player's skills, be specific and objective. Use examples or specific situations to illustrate your points.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Player Information**
* **Player Information** (`player_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing the player's name, age, position, or other relevant details. Please enter any information you think is relevant to the evaluation.

### **Skill Levels**
* **Skill Levels** (`skill_levels`, text, optional): In this field, rate the player's skills in various areas such as:
	+ Serving: 1-5, where 1 is below average and 5 is excellent
	+ Passing: 1-5, where 1 is below average and 5 is excellent
	+ Setting: 1-5, where 1 is below average and 5 is excellent
	+ Hitting: 1-5, where 1 is below average and 5 is excellent
	+ Blocking: 1-5, where 1 is below average and 5 is excellent

### **Training Plan**
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, text, optional): In this field, suggest a plan for the player's improvement, including specific drills or exercises they should focus on, or areas of improvement.

### **Notes**
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or suggestions for the player's improvement, or any other relevant information that may not fit into the other fields.
