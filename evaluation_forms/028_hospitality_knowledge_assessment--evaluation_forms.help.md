# Hospitality Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Knowledge Assessment form is designed to gather information about a staff member's experiences and knowledge within a hospitality industry setting. This form is used to assess their understanding of their role, work environment, and any incidents or issues they have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form by selecting the correct answer or entering the required information for each question.
- Be truthful and honest when answering each question.
- If you are unsure about a question, please ask for clarification or assistance from a supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, not required): Select the department where you work.
* **What is the name of your department?** (`question1`, text, not required): Please enter the name of your department.
* **What hours do you work?** (`question2`, select_multiple, not required): Select the hours of the day you typically work.
* **What is your role?** (`question3`, select_one, not required): Select your current role within the hospitality industry.
* **What is the name of your manager?** (`question4`, text, not required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **What is the name of your shift leader?** (`question5`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who oversees your shift.
* **Do you have any certifications or training?** (`question6`, select_multiple, not required): Select any relevant certifications or training you have received.
* **Have you completed a hospitality course?** (`question7`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have completed a hospitality course.
* **How long have you been working in the industry?** (`question8`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working in the hospitality industry.
* **How would you rate your overall experience in the industry?** (`question9`, number, required): Rate your overall experience in the industry on a scale of 1-10.
* **Have you attended any training or seminars?** (`question10`, select_multiple, not required): Select any relevant training or seminars you have attended.
* **Have you completed any certifications or training related to safety?** (`question11`, select_multiple, not required): Select any relevant certifications or training related to safety.
* **What type of certification or training?** (`question14`, text, not required): Enter the type of certification or training you have received.
* **When was the certification or training completed?** (`question15`, date, not required): Enter the date the certification or training was completed.
* **Have you ever received a formal complaint?** (`question16`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever received a formal complaint.
* **Have you ever received a formal written warning?** (`question17`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever received a formal written warning.
* **Have you ever received a verbal warning?** (`question18`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever received a verbal warning.
* **Have you ever received an informal warning?** (`question19`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever received an informal warning.
* **Have you ever been involved in a workplace injury?** (`question20`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever been involved in a workplace injury.
* **Have you ever been involved in a near miss?** (`question21`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever been involved in a near miss.
* **Have you ever been involved in an incident report?** (`question22`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever been involved in an incident report.
* **Have you ever received a workplace injury award?** (`question23`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever received a workplace injury award.
* **Have you ever received a workplace recognition?** (`question24`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever received a workplace recognition.
* **Is your work environment supportive and inclusive?** (`question25`, select_multiple, required): Select whether your work environment is supportive and inclusive.
* **Have you ever experienced discrimination?** (`question26`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever experienced discrimination.
* **Have you ever experienced harassment?** (`question27`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever experienced harassment.
* **Have you ever experienced bullying?** (`question28`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever experienced bullying.
* **Have you ever experienced sexual harassment?** (`question29`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever experienced sexual harassment.
* **Have you ever experienced other types of harassment?** (`question30`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have ever experienced other types of harassment.
