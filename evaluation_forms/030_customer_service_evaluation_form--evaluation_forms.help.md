# Customer Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool for customers to evaluate the service they have received and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all the fields that you feel are relevant to your experience.
2. If a field is not relevant to your experience, you can leave it blank.
3. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service** (`customer_service_form`, text, required/optional):
    This field is where you can provide a brief description of your experience with our service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional):
    Please select one of the following ratings:
    * 1: Very dissatisfied
    * 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
    * 3: Neutral
    * 4: Somewhat satisfied
    * 5: Very satisfied
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional):
    This field is where you can provide more detailed feedback about your experience with our service.
* **Service Provided** (`service_provided`, select_multiple, required/optional):
    Please select all the services that were provided to you:
    * Service A
    * Service B
    * Service C
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional):
    Please select how satisfied you are with the service you received:
    * 1: Very dissatisfied
    * 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
    * 3: Neutral
    * 4: Somewhat satisfied
    * 5: Very satisfied
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional):
    Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional):
    Please enter your phone number if you want us to contact you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional):
    This is a field for any additional comments you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest in your evaluation and feedback. Your input helps us improve our service.
* You can fill out as much or as little of the form as you feel necessary.
* If you need help, contact us.
