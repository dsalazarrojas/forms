# social_work_practice_innovation_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The social_work_practice_innovation_evaluation_form is designed to assess the effectiveness of social work practices, focusing on innovation and improvement. This form is used to gather data for research, improvement, or evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you are filling out this form for a new program evaluation.
2. Enter the name of the program in the "Program Name" field.
3. Enter the location where the program is located in the "Program Location" field.
4. Select the type of program ("New" or "Existing") in the "Program Type" field.
5. Enter the length of the program in months in the "Program Duration" field.
6. Select the source(s) of funding for the program (optional) in the "Program Funding" field.
7. Select the expected outcome of the program ("Significant", "Moderate", or "Low") in the "Program Outcome" field.
8. Enter the rating for the program in the "Program Rating" field.
9. Enter your name in the "Evaluator's Name" field.
10. Enter your title in the "Evaluator's Title" field.
11. Enter the name of the organization you represent in the "Evaluator's Organization" field.
12. Enter the date of evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
13. Enter the time of evaluation (optional) in the "Evaluation Time" field.
14. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Evaluation Notes" field (optional).
15. Enter your email address in the "Evaluator's Email" field (optional).
16. Enter your phone number in the "Evaluator's Phone" field (optional).
17. Add any additional comments or comments from the evaluator in the "Evaluator's Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the program being evaluated.
* **Program Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the program is located.
* **Program Type** (select_one, required): Select "New" if the program is new, or "Existing" if it is an existing program.
* **Program Duration** (number, required): Enter the length of the program in months.
* **Program Funding** (select_multiple, optional): Select the source(s) of funding for the program (Government, Private, or Hybrid).
* **Program Outcome** (select_one, required): Select the expected outcome of the program (Significant, Moderate, or Low).
* **Program Rating** (number, required): Enter a rating for the program.
* **Evaluator's Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator's Title** (text, required): Enter your title as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator's Organization** (text, required): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **Evaluation Date** (date, required): Enter the date of evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of evaluation.
* **Evaluation Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Evaluator's Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Evaluator's Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluator's Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or comments from the evaluator.

Note: The fields with an asterisk (\*) are mandatory, while those without an asterisk are optional.
