# Quality Calibration Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting a quality calibration review. It is intended for employees to request a review of their work quality and for reviewers to document the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the required fields, including:
	* Manager Name
	* Review Type (Yes/No)
	* Review Date
	* Rating Scale (select the relevant ratings)
	* Submitted By
	* Submitted At
	* Reviewer Name
	* Review Due Date
	* Review Status (Active/Inactive)
	* Next Review Date
2. You can optionally fill in your employee name and provide comments.
3. Once you've filled in all the required fields, click submit to send the review request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Text): Enter your name as an employee requesting the review.
* **Manager Name** (Text): Enter the name of your manager who will be conducting the review.
* **Review Type** (Select One): Select 'Yes' if you want to request a review, 'No' if you don't.
* **Review Date** (Date): Enter the date you are requesting the review to take place.
* **Rating Scale** (Select Multiple): Select the relevant ratings that apply to your work.
* **Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments you'd like to share with the reviewer.
* **Submitted By** (Text): Enter your name for reference.
* **Submitted At** (Date): Enter the date and time you submitted the review request.
* **Reviewer Name** (Text): Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to your review.
* **Review Due Date** (Date): Enter the date by which the review needs to be completed.
* **Review Status** (Select One): Select 'Active' if the review is currently active, 'Inactive' if it's not.
* **Next Review** (Date): Enter the date for the next review if applicable.
* **Review Completed** (Time): Not required, but can be filled in if you have already completed the review.
* **Quality Issues** (Select Multiple): Select any quality issues that were identified during the review.
* **Action Taken** (Text): Enter any actions taken as a result of the review.
* **Follow Up** (Text): Enter any follow-up information.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Text): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Review Completed** (Time): Not required, but can be filled in if you have already completed the review.
* **Next Review Date** (Date): Not required, but can be filled in if you have already scheduled a next review.
