# Youth Officers Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the performance of youth officers during a specific performance period. This form helps supervisors and youth officers identify areas of strength and areas for improvement, providing a basis for development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the performance period start and end dates to specify the evaluation period.
* Enter the supervisor's name for accurate attribution of feedback.
* Select a date for the evaluation date.
* Choose the evaluation cycle to ensure the form is submitted for the correct cycle.
* Enter a brief overall performance rating (1-5) and select relevant performance areas of focus.
* Provide a space for comments on the areas of focus.
* Enter a supervisor comment and youth officer response.
* Provide a space for supervisor recommendations and areas of improvement.
* Enter a date and time for when the form was submitted by the evaluator.
* Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Period Start**: Enter the start date of the performance period being evaluated.
* **Performance Period End**: Enter the end date of the performance period being evaluated.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor providing feedback.
* **Performance Rating**: Select a rating from 1-5 to indicate overall performance.
* **Performance Areas of Focus**: Select all relevant areas of focus for the performance evaluation (e.g., Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving).
* **Performance Areas of Focus Comments**: Provide comments on the selected areas of focus.
* **Supervisor Comment**: Enter a comment from the supervisor.
* **Youth Officer Response**: Enter a response from the youth officer.
* **Supervisor Recommendations**: Provide recommendations from the supervisor.
* **Areas of Improvement**: Enter areas where the youth officer can improve.
* **Evaluation Form Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Evaluation Form Submitted On**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Evaluation Form Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Performance Rating Comments**: Provide comments on the performance rating.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is intended to support the growth and development of youth officers. It is not for external use or publication.
