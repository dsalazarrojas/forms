# Typing Speed Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Typing Speed Assessment" form is designed to evaluate an individual's typing speed and accuracy during a test period. This form is intended to collect relevant data to help assess typing performance in various settings, such as in educational institutions or professional environments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **student ID** (if applicable) in the "student_id" field.
2. Indicate the duration of the test in the "test_duration" field.
3. Enter your accuracy score in the "accuracy_score" field.
4. Type your actual typing speed in words per minute (wpm) in the "typing_speed" field.
5. Record any errors made during the test in the "errors" field.
6. Enter the total number of characters typed in the "total_chars" field.
7. Enter the total time spent on the test in minutes in the "time_spent_minutes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional)
Enter your student ID or any other identifier if applicable.

### **Test Duration** (`test_duration`, text, optional)
Indicate the duration of the test in the format of "X minutes" (e.g., 30 minutes).

### **Accuracy Score** (`accuracy_score`, text, optional)
Enter your accuracy score as a percentage (e.g., 99%).

### **Typing Speed** (`typing_speed`, text, optional)
Type your actual typing speed in words per minute (wpm).

### **Errors** (`errors`, text, optional)
Record any errors made during the test.

### **Total Characters** (`total_chars`, text, optional)
Enter the total number of characters typed during the test.

### **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, text, optional)
Enter the total time spent on the test in minutes.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
- If unsure about any field, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
- This form is for internal use only; the collected data will be used to support internal evaluation purposes.
- The data collected may be used to improve typing performance and provide insights for future assessments.
