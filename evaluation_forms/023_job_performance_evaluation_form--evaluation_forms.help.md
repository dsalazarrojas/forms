# job_performance_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The job performance evaluation form is used to assess and document an employee's performance during a specific period. This form is designed for HR managers, supervisors, and team leaders to gather feedback and ratings on an employee's job performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select the performance period for the evaluation in the "Performance Period" field.
4. Rate the employee's overall performance in the "Overall Performance Rating" field using the dropdown options "Excellent", "Fair", or "Needs Improvement".
5. Provide feedback on the employee's performance in the "Feedback" field.
6. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time of the evaluation in the "Time" field.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional notes on the employee's performance in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.

### Job Title (`position`, text, required)
Enter the job title of the employee being evaluated.

### Performance Period (`performance_period`, text, required)
Select the performance period for which the evaluation is being made. This can be a specific date range or a specific period such as Q1, Q2, etc.

### Overall Performance Rating (`job_performance_rating`, select_one, required)
Rate the employee's overall performance using the options "Excellent", "Fair", or "Needs Improvement".

### Feedback (`feedback`, text, optional)
Provide any additional feedback on the employee's performance. This can include strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

### Date (`date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the evaluation.

### Time (`time`, time, required)
Enter the time of the day when the evaluation was made.

### Note (`note`, note, optional)
Enter any additional notes or comments on the employee's performance.

## Tips
- Make sure to keep the feedback and notes concise and clear.
- Ensure that the evaluation is accurate and fair.
- Review the evaluation regularly to ensure that it is up-to-date and relevant.
- Store the evaluation in a secure and accessible location.
