# Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide an objective evaluation of an employee's performance and work quality. It assesses their strengths and areas for improvement, helping to guide future development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill out the form completely and accurately** for each employee to ensure a fair and comprehensive evaluation.
2. **Review each field carefully**, and provide thoughtful answers to demonstrate your understanding of the employee's performance.
3. **Use the available options** for select and select_multiple fields to choose the most suitable answer.
4. **Be honest and objective** in your assessment, focusing on specific incidents or behaviors rather than general opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you enter the employee's name. Please type the full name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all departments where the employee works. This helps to understand their scope of work and responsibilities.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the employee's current job title. This is essential for identifying their role and responsibilities within the organization.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional):
	+ List the project(s) the employee is currently involved in or has recently completed. This helps to understand their workload and contribution.
* **Performance** (`performance`, time, optional):
	+ Record the employee's performance metrics, such as hours worked or tasks completed. Please use the format `HH:MM:SS` or a numerical value.
* **Work Quality** (`work_quality`, select_one, optional):
	+ Evaluate the employee's work quality using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. Please select the most accurate answer based on your observation.
* **Communication** (`communication`, number, optional):
	+ Assess the employee's communication skills, using a scale from 1 to 5 (where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest).
	+ 1 (Poor) - The employee frequently struggles with clear and effective communication.
	+ 2 (Fair) - The employee occasionally lacks clarity or effectiveness in communication.
	+ 3 (Average) - The employee generally communicates well, but with some room for improvement.
	+ 4 (Good) - The employee consistently communicates clearly and effectively.
	+ 5 (Excellent) - The employee has exceptional communication skills.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the employee's ability to work with others using the same 1-5 scale:
		+ 1 (Poor) - The employee rarely participates in team projects or initiatives.
		+ 2 (Fair) - The employee occasionally struggles with team collaboration or communication.
		+ 3 (Average) - The employee generally works collaboratively, but with some limitations.
		+ 4 (Good) - The employee often participates in team projects or initiatives.
		+ 5 (Excellent) - The employee consistently and effectively collaborates with others.
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, number, optional):
	+ Evaluate the employee's leadership skills, using the 1-5 scale:
		+ 1 (Poor) - The employee rarely shows leadership or initiative.
		+ 2 (Fair) - The employee occasionally struggles with leadership or decision-making.
		+ 3 (Average) - The employee generally shows some leadership, but with limitations.
		+ 4 (Good) - The employee often takes charge of projects or initiatives.
		+ 5 (Excellent) - The employee consistently demonstrates strong leadership skills.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the employee's adaptability, using the 1-5 scale:
		+ 1 (Poor) - The employee rarely adapts to changes or new situations.
		+ 2 (Fair) - The employee occasionally struggles with adapting to changes or new situations.
		+ 3 (Average) - The employee generally adapts moderately well to changes or new situations.
		+ 4 (Good) - The employee often adapts effectively to changes or new situations.
		+ 5 (Excellent) - The employee consistently adapts exceptionally well to changes or new situations.

## Tips
* **Be specific** when providing feedback, focusing on concrete behaviors or incidents.
* **Use examples** to support your assessment, if applicable.
* **Focus on the employee's growth and development**, suggesting areas for improvement and recommendations for growth.
* **Review and update the form regularly**, ensuring it remains relevant and effective for the organization.
