# exit_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit interview form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. It aims to collect feedback on their reasons for leaving, job satisfaction, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start with the introduction page, which provides an overview of the purpose of the form.
2. On the following pages, you will be asked to provide information about your company experience, reasons for leaving, job title, job satisfaction, manager performance, company satisfaction, communication effectiveness, job security, team performance, and job satisfaction (again, with a multiple-choice option).
3. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
4. You may choose multiple options for some questions, such as job satisfaction and job title.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief welcome message that explains the purpose of the form.
* **Company Info** (`company_info`, `note`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your experience with the company.
* **Why Leaving** (`why_leaving`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Info** (`job_info`, `note`, required: false): Describe your job title and any relevant details.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments about your manager or their performance.
* **Manager Performance** (`manager_performance`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Company Satisfaction** (`company_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Security** (`job_security`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Satisfaction (Multiple)** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

Note: The form will guide you through each page, and you will be able to review your answers before submitting the form.
