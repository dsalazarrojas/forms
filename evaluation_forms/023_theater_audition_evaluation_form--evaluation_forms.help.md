# Theater Audition Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Theater Audition Evaluation Form" is used to evaluate the performance of actors in a theater audition process. This form helps theater professionals provide constructive feedback and assess the suitability of actors for productions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the actor being evaluated in the "Actor" field.
2. Select the correct "Audition Date/Time" for the evaluated performance.
3. Choose the "Production" and "Scene" the actor was auditioning for.
4. Select the "Role" the actor was auditioning for.
5. If applicable, provide the "Evaluation Date" for when the form was completed.
6. Fill out any other relevant fields as required or necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditioner** (`auditioner`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the actor being evaluated.
* **Actor** (`actor_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the internal ID of the actor being evaluated.
* **Audition Date/Time** (`audition_date_time`, `date`, optional): Select the date and time of the audition.
* **Production** (`production_id`, `number`, optional): Select the production the actor was auditioning for.
* **Scene** (`scene_id`, `number`, optional): Select the scene the actor was auditioning for.
* **Role** (`role_id`, `number`, optional): Select the role the actor was auditioning for.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date when the form was completed.

## Tips
* Ensure you are evaluating the correct actor and audition details to prevent duplication of work.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments for each evaluation.
* Use clear and concise language when writing your feedback.
* Save your form as a record for future reference and evaluation.
