# Fellowship Program Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback and evaluations from participants and stakeholders about the Fellowship Program. The information collected will be used to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the Program Impact question briefly describing your experience with the program.
2. Rate the program using the Ratings Scale and provide your reasons for the rating in the Ratings Comments field.
3. Provide any feedback about the program in the Feedback field.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the Improvement Suggestions field.
5. Rate the overall performance of the program using the Overall Rating field.
6. Select a rating for the program using the Rating Scale field.
7. Enter your name as the Participant in the Participant Name field.
8. Enter your email address as the Participant in the Participant Email field.
9. Enter your phone number as the Participant in the Participant Phone field.
10. Enter the name of the stakeholder who is responsible for your feedback in the Stakeholder Name field.
11. Enter the email address of the stakeholder who is responsible for your feedback in the Stakeholder Email field.
12. Enter the phone number of the stakeholder who is responsible for your feedback in the Stakeholder Phone field.
13. Enter the name of the evaluator who will use the feedback in the Evaluator Name field.
14. Enter the email address of the evaluator who will use the feedback in the Evaluator Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Impact**: A brief description of your experience with the program.
* **Ratings Scale**: A 1-5 rating scale to evaluate the program.
* **Ratings Comments**: A field to provide reasons for your rating.
* **Feedback**: Any comments or suggestions about the program.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Any ideas you have to enhance the program.
* **Overall Rating**: A final rating of the program's performance.
* **Rating Scale**: A 1-5 rating scale to evaluate the program.
* **Participant Name**: The name of the person providing feedback.
* **Participant Email**: The email address of the person providing feedback.
* **Participant Phone**: The phone number of the person providing feedback.
* **Stakeholder Name**: The name of the person responsible for the participant's feedback.
* **Stakeholder Email**: The email address of the person responsible for the participant's feedback.
* **Stakeholder Phone**: The phone number of the person responsible for the participant's feedback.
* **Evaluator Name**: The name of the person who will use the feedback.
* **Evaluator Email**: The email address of the person who will use the feedback.
