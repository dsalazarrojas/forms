# Evaluation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from event participants to help us better understand their experiences and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form on each page according to your best knowledge and recollection of the event.
* Make sure to answer all required fields. If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank if you're not sure or don't have information about it.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): This field is for brief description of the event you're evaluating. Please provide a concise summary of the event's key points.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the event on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your evaluation, which should be as close as possible to the actual event date.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required: false): If possible, enter the time of your evaluation. This will help us understand your timing-related insights better.
* **Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments, thoughts, or suggestions you have about the event. This is your chance to share any details that haven't been addressed in the other fields.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you for any follow-up questions or to share updates about our services.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to reach you if needed.

Note that the Evaluation Feedback Form is designed to be completed by event participants. If you're not a participant or have already provided feedback, please do not submit another form. If you have any questions or concerns about the form or its purpose, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
