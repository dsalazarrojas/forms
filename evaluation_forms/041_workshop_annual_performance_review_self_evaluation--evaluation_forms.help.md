# Workshop Annual Performance Review Self Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Workshop Annual Performance Review Self Evaluation form, which is a private and confidential assessment for workshop participants to evaluate their performance and progress during the past year.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your honest evaluation of your performance and progress over the past year in the "Self Evaluation Form" section (Page 1). Answer any questions or prompts to the best of your abilities, being as specific and detailed as you can.
2. In the "Job Performance Comments" section (Page 2), provide any comments or observations about your job performance. This section is a chance for you to provide feedback and insights on how you've performed in your role.
3. For each of the remaining sections, "Knowledge Level", "Skills Level", "Attitude Level", and "Overall Job Satisfactions", select the best option that reflects your self-evaluation. These questions are designed to gauge your level of expertise, skills, and job satisfaction.
4. In the "Overall Performance Comments" section (Page 5), provide any final thoughts or observations about your performance and progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self Evaluation Form** (Page 1, Required: False)
	+ Please provide a detailed evaluation of your performance and progress over the past year.
* **Job Performance Comments** (Page 2, Required: False)
	+ Provide any comments or observations about your job performance.
* **Knowledge Level** (Page 3, Required: False)
	+ Select the option that best reflects your level of expertise and knowledge in your role.
	+ Options: High, Medium, Low
* **Skills Level** (Page 4, Required: False)
	+ Select the option that best reflects your level of skills and abilities in your role.
	+ Options: High, Medium, Low
* **Attitude Level** (Page 4, Required: False)
	+ Select the option that best reflects your attitude and behavior in your role.
	+ Options: High, Medium, Low
* **Overall Job Satisfactions** (Page 5, Required: False)
	+ Select the option that best reflects your overall satisfaction with your job.
	+ Options: High, Medium, Low
* **Overall Performance Comments** (Page 5, Required: False)
	+ Provide any final thoughts or observations about your performance and progress.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your evaluation. This form is meant to be a private and confidential assessment, so don't worry about being too self-critical.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and focused on your performance and progress.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, just do your best to provide an honest evaluation.
