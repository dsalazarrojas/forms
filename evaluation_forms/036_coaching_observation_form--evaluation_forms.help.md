# Coaching Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Observation Form is designed to evaluate the performance of coaches during sessions, identifying strengths and areas for improvement. It helps coaches grow professionally by providing constructive feedback and suggestions for development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field accurately, providing your best assessment of the coach's performance based on what you observed during the session.
2. Rate each field using the provided options (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. In fields with multiple lines or text boxes, describe specific instances or moments that demonstrate your assessment.
4. Leave blank fields that were not observed or rated during the session.
5. Complete the form thoroughly to provide comprehensive feedback to the coach.

### Tips
* This form is designed to be completed by observers who have witnessed a coaching session. Please answer honestly and provide constructive feedback to support the coach's growth.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank or ask for clarification.
* Complete the form thoroughly to provide the coach with a comprehensive evaluation.
* This form is for evaluation purposes only, and all responses are confidential.
