# Technical Co-Founder Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the technical co-founder of a project or company. It assesses the candidate's technical abilities, leadership skills, project fit, and other relevant qualities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by writing a brief introduction to provide background information about the candidate.
2. Fill in the candidate's information, including their email and phone number.
3. Evaluate the candidate's technical ability, leadership skills, project fit, and other relevant qualities using the provided options.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the candidate.
5. Summarize the next steps to be taken in the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): Write a brief introduction to provide background information about the candidate.
* **Candidate Information** (`2`, `text`, required: true): Enter the candidate's name, job title, and other relevant details.
* **Technical Ability** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the level of the candidate's technical ability:
	+ Proficient: The candidate has a good understanding of the relevant technologies and can perform tasks with minimal supervision.
	+ Advanced: The candidate has a strong understanding of the relevant technologies and can perform complex tasks with minimal supervision.
	+ Expert: The candidate has an in-depth understanding of the relevant technologies and can perform highly complex tasks with minimal supervision.
* **Leadership Skills** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the level of the candidate's leadership skills:
	+ Strong: The candidate has demonstrated strong leadership skills, such as motivating teams and making informed decisions.
	+ Average: The candidate has average leadership skills, such as leading small projects or teams.
	+ Weak: The candidate has limited leadership skills, such as following instructions.
* **Project Fit** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the level of the candidate's project fit:
	+ Strong: The candidate has a strong understanding of the project's goals and can contribute to its success.
	+ Average: The candidate has an average understanding of the project's goals and can contribute somewhat to its success.
	+ Weak: The candidate has a weak understanding of the project's goals and may not be able to contribute significantly to its success.
* **Communication Skills** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the candidate's communication skills.
* **Problem Solving** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the level of the candidate's problem-solving skills:
	+ Strong: The candidate has demonstrated strong problem-solving skills, such as analyzing complex issues and finding effective solutions.
	+ Average: The candidate has average problem-solving skills, such as addressing minor issues.
	+ Weak: The candidate has limited problem-solving skills, such as requiring close supervision.
* **Teamwork** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the level of the candidate's teamwork skills:
	+ Strong: The candidate has demonstrated strong teamwork skills, such as collaborating effectively with team members.
	+ Average: The candidate has average teamwork skills, such as contributing to small teams.
	+ Weak: The candidate has limited teamwork skills, such as working independently.
* **Time Management** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the level of the candidate's time management skills:
	+ Strong: The candidate has demonstrated strong time management skills, such as completing tasks efficiently.
	+ Average: The candidate has average time management skills, such as completing tasks with some supervision.
	+ Weak: The candidate has limited time management skills, such as requiring close supervision.
* **Additional Comments** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the candidate.
* **Candidate Email** (`11`, `email`, required: true): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Next Steps** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Summarize the next steps to be taken in the evaluation process.
* **Final Comments** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Enter any final comments or feedback about the candidate.
* **Candidate Information 2** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the candidate.
