# executive_director_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive director evaluation form is used to collect feedback and ratings on the performance of the executive director. This form helps to evaluate the executive director's leadership skills, job performance, and overall effectiveness in their role. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to provide valuable insights for future development and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the executive director's name in the "Executive Director's Name" field.
2. Fill in the "Job Title" field with the executive director's current job title.
3. Select the evaluation date in the "Evaluation Date" field.
4. Choose the time of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Time" field.
5. Rate the executive director's performance on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Evaluation Score" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7. Enter the executive director's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the executive director's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Executive Director's Name** (executive_director_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the executive director being evaluated.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required: false): Fill in the current job title of the executive director.
* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, required: false): Select the date the evaluation is being conducted.
* **Evaluation Time** (evaluation_time, time, required: false): Choose the time of day when the evaluation is being conducted.
* **Evaluation Score** (evaluation_score, number, required: false): Rate the executive director's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Evaluation Comments** (evaluation_comments, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the executive director.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the executive director's email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter the executive director's phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to provide valuable insights for future development and growth opportunities.
* Make sure to keep the evaluation score between 1 and 10.
* Use the "Evaluation Comments" field to provide specific examples or feedback for the executive director.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
