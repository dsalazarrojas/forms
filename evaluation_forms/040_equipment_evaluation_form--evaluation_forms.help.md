# Equipment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the performance, reliability, and maintenance of various equipment. Please fill out this form to provide valuable insights that will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to submit the form completely before moving on to the next section.
4. Review and revise your answers as needed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance** (`form_1`, text, required/optional): This is the first section of the form where you can provide feedback on the performance of the equipment. Please describe your experiences and observations about how well the equipment has met your needs and expectations.
* **Reliability** (`form_2`, text, required/optional): This section is for evaluating the reliability of the equipment. Please share your thoughts on how often the equipment has failed or malfunctioned and any issues you've encountered.
* **Maintenance** (`form_3`, text, required/optional): This is the last section of the form where you can discuss any maintenance tasks you've performed on the equipment and how they affected its performance.
