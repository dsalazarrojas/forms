# Hospital Accessibility Design Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the accessibility of a hospital facility. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Evaluator Name**, **Evaluator Title**, and **Evaluator Institution** fields with your name, title, and institution's name.
2. Rate the accessibility of the hospital facility using the **Evaluator Rating** field.
3. Enter your score in the **Evaluator Rating Score** field.
4. Select the **Accessibility Features** that are present in the facility.
5. Enter the **Facility Address** and **Facility Location** if available.
6. Enter any additional comments or observations in the **Evaluator Comments** field.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Evaluator Notes** field.
8. Enter the **Facility Name**, **Facility Type**, and **Facility Size**.
9. Enter the **Facility ID** and **Facility Location 2** if available.
10. If applicable, enter the **Evaluator Title 2** and **Evaluator Institution 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the person evaluating the facility.
* **Evaluator Title**: Enter the title of the person evaluating the facility.
* **Evaluator Institution**: Enter the name of the institution of the person evaluating the facility.
* **Evaluator Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the facility.
* **Evaluator Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Evaluator Title 2**: Enter the title of the person evaluating the facility (if applicable).
* **Evaluator Institution 2**: Enter the name of the institution of the person evaluating the facility (if applicable).
* **Evaluator Rating**: Rate the accessibility of the facility, using the options provided.
* **Evaluator Rating Score**: Enter the score for the evaluator's rating.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Type**: Enter the type of facility.
* **Facility Size**: Enter the size of the facility.
* **Accessbility Features**: Select all the accessibility features present in the facility.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the ID of the facility.
* **Facility Location 2**: Enter the location of the facility (if applicable).

Tips:

* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant department for further guidance.
* Please fill in all required fields, and provide as much information as possible.
