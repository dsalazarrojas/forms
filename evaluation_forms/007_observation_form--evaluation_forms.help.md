# Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluations from team leaders on their team members' performance, behavior, and quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team leader's name in the `Team Leader Name` field.
2. Enter the observed employee's name in the `Observed Employee Name` field.
3. Select the date of observation from the calendar or enter it manually in the `Date of Observation` field.
4. Describe the observed behavior in the `Observed Behavior` field.
5. Enter your feedback on the observed employee's performance in the `Feedback` field.
6. If applicable, enter your recommendations for the observed employee's development in the `Recommendations` field.
7. Rate the observed employee's demeanor, communication, teamwork, initiative, leadership, problem-solving, quality of service, and overall impression using the number fields for `Observed Demeanor`, `Communication`, `Teamwork`, `Initiative`, `Leadership`, `Problem Solving`, `Quality of Service`, and `Overall Impression`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Leader Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the team leader who is completing this form.
* **Observed Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being observed.
* **Date of Observation** (3, date, required): Select or enter the date on which the observation took place.
* **Observed Behavior** (4, text, required): Describe the specific behavior you observed during the team leader's evaluation.
* **Feedback** (5, text, required): Enter your overall feedback on the observed employee's performance.
* **Recommendations** (6, text, required): If applicable, enter recommendations for the observed employee's development.
* **Observed Demeanor** (7, text, optional): Describe the observed employee's demeanor during the observation.
* **Communication** (8, number, optional): Rate the observed employee's communication skills on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Teamwork** (9, number, optional): Rate the observed employee's teamwork skills on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Initiative** (10, number, optional): Rate the observed employee's initiative on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Leadership** (11, number, optional): Rate the observed employee's leadership skills on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Problem Solving** (13, number, optional): Rate the observed employee's problem-solving skills on a scale (e.g. 1-5).
* **Quality of Service** (14, text, optional): Describe the observed employee's quality of service.
* **Overall Impression** (15, text, optional): Enter your overall impression of the observed employee's performance.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed to submit the form. Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
