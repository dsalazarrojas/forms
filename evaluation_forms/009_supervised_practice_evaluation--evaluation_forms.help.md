# Supervised Practice Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervised Practice Evaluation form is designed to assess the effectiveness of a supervised practice session. It helps instructors to evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the session, identify areas for improvement, and provide feedback to participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the Session Feedback field with a brief description of the session.
2. Rate the Performance Rating field on a scale of 1-5, indicating the overall quality of the session.
3. Select the Strengths Recorded field to identify the strengths demonstrated during the session.
4. Select the Areas for Improvement field to identify areas where the participant needs to improve.
5. Enter the Date of Session field with the date of the supervised practice session.
6. Enter the Time of Session field with the start and end time of the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback (text)**: Enter a brief description of the session, summarizing the key points discussed and any notable events or issues that arose.
* **Performance Rating (number)**: Rate the overall quality of the session on a scale of 1-5. A rating of 1 indicates a poor performance, while a rating of 5 indicates an excellent performance.
* **Strengths Recorded (select multiple)**: Select all the strengths demonstrated during the session.
* **Areas for Improvement (select multiple)**: Select all the areas where the participant needs to improve.
* **Date of Session (date)**: Enter the date of the supervised practice session (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time of Session (time)**: Enter the start and end time of the session (HH:MM format).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide detailed and constructive feedback to participants.
* Be specific and objective when evaluating the session.
* Use the Strengths Recorded and Areas for Improvement fields to provide actionable insights for participants.
* Keep the Date of Session and Time of Session fields accurate and up-to-date.
