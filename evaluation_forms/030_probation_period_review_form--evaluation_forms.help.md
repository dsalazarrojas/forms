# Probation Period Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Probation Period Review Form is used to review and document the progress of individuals on probation, ensuring that their progress is accurately and thoroughly evaluated. This form helps to gather essential information about the case, review period, and relevant details to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form pages and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the relevant fields with accurate and concise data.
3. Make sure to save your progress before moving to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Form** (`review_form`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief summary of the case being reviewed.
* **Case Info** (`case_info`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant case-specific details, if applicable.
* **Review Period** (`review_period`, date, required: false): Select the review period date to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the review period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the review period.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, required: false): Please select either Active or Inactive status for the case.
* **Case Details** (`case_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all applicable case details.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, note, required: false): Add any relevant comments or notes for the review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, text, required: false): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from the reviewer.
