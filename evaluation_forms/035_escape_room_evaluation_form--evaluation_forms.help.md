# escape_room_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from players about their experience in an escape room. It is used by escape room operators to understand what they can do to improve their games and provide a better experience for future players.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section as accurately as possible, based on your experience in the escape room you visited.
2. If you don't have experience with a particular question, please mark the 'Don't know' or 'N/A' option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Room Name** (Room Name, text, optional): 
Please fill out the name of the escape room you played. This will help us understand which specific room you are referring to in your feedback.
* **Room Theme** (Room Theme, select_one, required): 
Choose the theme of the escape room you played. This will help us understand what type of escape room experience you had. Options include:
	+ Fantasy
	+ Horror
	+ Mystery
	+ Western
	+ Sci-Fi
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Room Difficulty** (Room Difficulty, select_one, required): 
Rate how challenging you found the game. Options include:
	+ Easy
	+ Moderate
	+ Challenging
	+ Difficult
* **Clue Difficulty** (Clue Difficulty, select_one, required): 
Rate how clear you found the clues. Options include:
	+ Very clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Not clear at all
* **Puzzles** (Puzzles, select_one, required): 
Rate how engaging you found the puzzles. Options include:
	+ Very engaging
	+ Somewhat engaging
	+ Not engaging at all
* **Puzzle Types** (Puzzle Types, select_multiple, required): 
Select the types of puzzles you encountered during the game. Options include:
	+ Word puzzles
	+ Visual puzzles
	+ Physical puzzles
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Immersive Environment** (Immersive Environment, select_one, required): 
Rate how immersive you found the environment. Options include:
	+ Very immersive
	+ Somewhat
	+ Not at all
* **Overall Experience Value** (Overall Experience Value, select_one, required): 
Rate whether or not you would play this game again. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Escape Prophets** (Escape Prophets, text, optional): 
Please use this field to give us any additional feedback about the escape room operators or staff. This will help us know if they were helpful or not.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): 
Please leave any additional comments about your experience in the escape room. This will help us to understand your perspective and improve our games.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible with your answers. Your feedback will help us understand what we can do to improve.
* If you are not sure about any of the questions, please mark the 'Don't know' or 'N/A' option.
* If you have any additional feedback, please use the 'Escape Prophets' or 'Comments' field to provide more information.
