# Equine Massage Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from equine massage owners about their session. It includes fields to gather feedback, document the session, and potentially schedule follow-up actions or appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your owner's consent to complete this evaluation.
2. Enter your name as the owner of the equine massage.
3. Enter any evaluation notes about the session.
4. Describe the outcome of the equine massage session.
5. If necessary, enter follow-up notes or actions.
6. Schedule a follow-up date if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Consent** (`owner_consent`, text, required): Please confirm that you are the owner of the equine massage and are authorized to complete this form.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the owner of the equine massage.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes or comments about the equine massage session.
* **Equine Massage Session Notes** (`equine_massage_session_notes`, text, required): Enter notes about the equine massage session.
* **Evaluation Outcome** (`evaluation_outcome`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the equine massage session (Very Satisfactory, Moderately Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory).
* **Equine Massage Session Notes Follow-up** (`equine_massage_session_notes_follow_up`, text, optional): Enter follow-up notes about the equine massage session (if needed).
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, text, required): Enter the action or next steps for the equine massage session (if necessary).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up date if needed.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and accurate information about the equine massage session.
* Ensure that the Owner Consent field is selected to confirm your ownership and authorization.
* Use the Evaluation Outcome field to provide a clear and objective assessment of the session.
* Schedule a follow-up date if necessary and enter the corresponding action or next steps.
