# specialist_dl_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the performance of specialists, providing a structured framework for evaluating their work and identifying areas for improvement. It is intended to be used by managers or supervisors to gather information about a specialist's progress, evaluate their performance, and make informed decisions about their future development or projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee or specialist from the provided list of options. This is a crucial field as it helps identify the specialist being evaluated.
2. Choose the evaluation date for the specialist's work. This date should reflect the period during which the specialist's performance was being evaluated.
3. Select the evaluation period for the specialist's work. This helps to categorize their performance within specific time frames.
4. Enter any comments or feedback about the specialist's performance. This is a free-text field where you can provide detailed comments.
5. Enter the specialist's email address for communication purposes.
6. Input the specialist's phone number for contact.
7. Select the specialist being evaluated. This is a crucial field as it ensures that the correct specialist is being evaluated.
8. Choose the status of the evaluation form, whether it's still a draft or ready to be published.
9. Select the review date for the evaluation form. This date is important for tracking when the review was completed.
10. Choose the tool assigned to the specialist for this evaluation. This helps to keep track of which tools are being used by the specialist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee/Other**: Select the employee or specialist being evaluated from the provided list. This field is required to ensure that the correct specialist is being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date during which the specialist's performance was being evaluated. This field is optional but helps to track the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Period**: Choose the period during which the specialist's performance was evaluated. This helps to categorize their performance within specific time frames.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the specialist's performance. This field is optional but provides valuable information about their work.
* **Email**: Enter the specialist's email address for communication purposes. This field is optional but helps in tracking and contacting the specialist.
* **Phone Number**: Input the specialist's phone number for contact. This field is optional but ensures that you can reach them directly.
* **Specialist**: Select the specialist being evaluated from the provided list. This field is crucial as it ensures that the correct specialist is being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Form Status**: Choose the status of the evaluation form, whether it's still a draft or ready to be published. This field is optional but helps in tracking the form's publication status.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date when the review was completed. This field is optional but helps in tracking the review's completion date.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned to the specialist for this evaluation. This field is required as it helps in tracking which tools are being used by the specialist.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary information about the specialist before completing the form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Use the correct specialist's email and phone number to ensure that you can contact them effectively.
- Enter detailed comments or feedback to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the specialist's performance.
