# Soft Skills Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Skills Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate an employee's soft skills, set goals for improvement, and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
2. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the employee being evaluated.
3. **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the department of the employee being evaluated.
4. **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Describe the employee's strengths, such as their communication skills or problem-solving abilities.
5. **Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, text, required): Describe the employee's weaknesses, such as difficulties with time management or delegation of tasks.
6. **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): If applicable, describe areas where the employee needs improvement, such as taking on new challenges or developing a growth mindset.
7. **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant soft skills that apply to the employee, such as software development, communication, teamwork, problem-solving, analytical thinking, time management, or leadership.
8. **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Date of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name to ensure accurate tracking of the evaluation and future progress.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title to provide context for the evaluation.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter the department to provide additional context.
* **Strengths**: Describe the employee's strengths to identify their key competencies.
* **Weaknesses**: Describe the employee's weaknesses to identify areas for improvement.
* **Areas for Improvement**: If applicable, describe areas where the employee needs improvement.
* **Skills**: Select all relevant soft skills that apply to the employee to create a comprehensive picture of their competencies.
* **Evaluation Date**: Date of the evaluation to track progress over time.

## Tips

* Be specific when describing strengths and weaknesses.
* Select only relevant soft skills that apply to the employee.
* Use specific dates for the evaluation date.
* Keep the evaluation focused on the employee's soft skills, rather than job performance.
* Use this form as a guide to set goals and track progress over time.
