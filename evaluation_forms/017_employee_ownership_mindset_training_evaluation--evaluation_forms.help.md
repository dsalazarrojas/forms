# Employee Ownership Mindset Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Ownership Mindset Training Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of the training program. Your responses will help us understand your experiences and perceptions, which will inform improvements to the training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the quality of the training content.
2. Evaluate the trainer's effectiveness.
3. Indicate your confidence in applying the ownership concepts.
4. Describe your understanding of the key concepts.
5. (Optional) Provide the duration of the training.
6. (Optional) Describe the training facilities.
7. (Optional) Describe the trainer's facilities.
8. (Optional) Evaluate the trainer's attitude.
9. (Optional) Add any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, text, required): This field asks how you rate the quality of the content presented in the training. Please provide your honest evaluation of the quality of the material provided.
* **Trainer Effectiveness** (`trainer_effectiveness`, text, required): This field assesses how effective you found the trainer in delivering the training. Rate their ability to present the information effectively.
* **Confidence in Applying Ownership Concepts** (`confidence_in_owning`, number, required): This field measures how confident you are in applying the ownership concepts learned in the training. Rate your confidence on a scale (e.g., 0-10).
* **Content Comprehension** (`content_comprehension`, text, required): Describe how well you understand the key concepts of the training. Try to be as specific as possible.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, optional): If you choose to provide this information, please enter the duration of the training in hours or minutes.
* **Training Facilities** (`training_facilities`, text, optional): Describe the training facilities provided, such as the venue, equipment, or amenities.
* **Trainer Facilities** (`trainer_facilities`, text, optional): Describe the trainer's facilities, such as their communication style or interaction with participants.
* **Trainer Attitude** (`trainer_attitude`, text, optional): Evaluate the trainer's attitude towards the training material and participants.
* **Any Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about the training program.
