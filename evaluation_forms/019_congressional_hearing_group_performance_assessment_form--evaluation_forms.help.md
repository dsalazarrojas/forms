# Congressional Hearing Group Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a group or panel's performance at a congressional hearing. It assesses their preparation, testimony, and overall impact on the hearing topic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields: Assessment Date, Assessor Name, Assessor Role, Hearing Name, Committee Name, Group Name, Group Type, Hearing Topic, and Overall Performance Rating.
2. For each field, provide your honest and objective assessment of the group's performance. Use the provided options to guide your response.
3. For fields with "select_one" type, choose the option that best describes your assessment.
4. For fields with "text" type, provide a brief description of the group's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): <date> - Date of the hearing when the group performed.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`): <text> - Name of person completing the assessment.
* **Assessor Role** (`assessor_role`): Select the role of the person completing the assessment from:
	+ Committee Staff
	+ Observer
	+ Media
	+ Subject Matter Expert
	+ Government Liaison
	+ Other
* **Hearing Name** (`hearing_name`): <text> - Official title of the hearing.
* **Committee Name** (`committee_name`): <text> - Name of the committee that convened the hearing.
* **Group or Panel Name** (`group_name`): <text> - Name of the group being assessed.
* **Type of Group** (`group_type`): Select the category of the group:
	+ Government Agency
	+ Industry Representatives
	+ Advocacy Organization
	+ Academic or Research Institution
	+ Labor Union
	+ State or Local Government
	+ International Organization
	+ Other
* **Hearing Topic or Subject** (`hearing_topic`): <text> - Main subject of the hearing.
* **Quality of Group Preparation** (`preparation_quality`): Select the quality of the group's preparation:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Clarity of Testimony** (`testimony_clarity`): Select the clarity of the group's testimony:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Relevance of Testimony** (`testimony_relevance`): Select the relevance of the group's testimony:
	+ Very Relevant
	+ Relevant
	+ Somewhat Relevant
	+ Not Relevant
* **Quality of Evidence Presented** (`evidence_quality`): Select the quality of evidence presented:
	+ Excellent - Well Documented
	+ Good - Adequately Supported
	+ Fair - Some Support
	+ Poor - Lacking Evidence
* **Responsiveness to Questions** (`responsiveness`): Select how well the group responded to questions:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Professionalism Displayed** (`professionalism`): Select the level of professionalism displayed:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Time Management** (`time_management`): Select how well the group managed time:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Level of Expertise Demonstrated** (`expertise_demonstrated`): Select the level of expertise demonstrated:
	+ Expert
	+ Very Knowledgeable
	+ Knowledgeable
	+ Somewhat Knowledgeable
	+ Lacking Knowledge
* **Overall Credibility** (`credibility`): Select the overall credibility:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **Were Key Points Communicated Effectively?** (`key_points_communicated`): Select if key points were communicated effectively:
	+ Yes - Very Effectively
	+ Yes - Somewhat Effectively
	+ No - Ineffective
	+ No - Key Points Unclear
* **Bipartisan Appeal of Testimony** (`bipartisan_appeal`): Select if testimony appealed across party lines:
	+ Yes - Strong Bipartisan Appeal
	+ Yes - Some Bipartisan Appeal
	+ No - Partisan Nature
	+ Not Applicable
* **Potential Impact on Policy** (`impact_on_policy`): Select the potential impact on policy:
	+ High Impact Expected
	+ Moderate Impact Expected
	+ Low Impact Expected
	+ No Impact Expected
	+ Too Soon to Tell
* **Group Strengths** (`strengths`): <text> - What did the group do well.
* **Group Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`): <text> - Areas needing improvement.
* **Notable Moments or Exchanges** (`notable_moments`): <text> - Any memorable exchanges.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance`): Select the overall performance rating:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): <text> - Any other observations.

## Tips

* Please answer each field based on your objective and honest assessment of the group's performance.
* Use the provided options to guide your response.
* If a field does not apply to the group's performance, choose "Not Applicable" or "No" accordingly.
* Be as specific and descriptive as possible in your assessment.
* Review the form carefully before submitting your evaluation.
