# internal_audit_function_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "internal_audit_function_evaluation_form" is a tool used to evaluate the internal audit process within an organization. It helps audit leaders and stakeholders assess the overall risk level and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and description to provide a brief overview of the audit.
2. Provide your assessment of the overall risk level on the "Overall Risk" field.
3. Describe the risk factors that were identified during the audit.
4. Add any comments from the audit leaders on the "Audit Leaders Comments" field.
5. Add any comments from stakeholders on the "Stakeholders Comments" field.
6. Select the submitted date and time on the "Submitted Date" and "Submitted Time" fields.
7. Enter the name of the audit leader on the "Audit Leader Name" field.
8. Enter the name of the stakeholder on the "Stakeholder Name" field.
9. Choose the assigned tool for the audit on the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Enter your name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This field is a title for the form. It should be a brief description of the audit.
* **Form Title**: This field is for a more detailed description of the audit.
* **Form Description**: This field is for a longer description of the audit process.
* **Overall Risk**: Assess the overall risk level of the audit.
* **Risk Factors**: Describe the risk factors that were identified during the audit.
* **Audit Leaders Comments**: Enter any comments from the audit leaders.
* **Stakeholders Comments**: Enter any comments from stakeholders.
* **Form Footer**: This field is not used in the form functionality, it's just for aesthetics.
* **Submitted**: Select the submitted option that best describes the state of the audit.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when the audit was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time when the audit was submitted.
* **Audit Leader Name**: Enter the name of the audit leader.
* **Stakeholder Name**: Enter the name of the stakeholder.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned for the audit.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name and submit the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately to get a comprehensive evaluation.
* Review the overall risk level and risk factors carefully to identify potential issues.
* Use the comments sections to provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* Select the correct submitted option to reflect the current state of the audit.
* Enter the correct submitted date and time.
* Ensure that the audit leader and stakeholder names are accurate.
* Choose the correct assigned tool for the audit.
* Submit the form only after ensuring that all information is accurate and complete.
