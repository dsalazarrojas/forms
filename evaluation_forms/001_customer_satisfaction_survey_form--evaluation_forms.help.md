# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from customers.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Survey" button to begin filling in the form.
2. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Select your Phone Number type from the Phone Number field.
5. Rate your satisfaction level by selecting one of the options in the How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our service? field.
6. If you have any feedback or suggestions, type them in the Any feedback or suggestions? field.
7. Select all that apply for the Category, Department, Level, Position, Location, Industry, Sector, Company Size, Type, and Manager fields.
8. Fill in your Job Title and Manager's name if applicable.
9. Rate your overall satisfaction by selecting one of the options in the Satisfaction field.
10. Provide any comments or open comments in the Comments, Open Comments, and Closed Comments fields.
11. Fill in your Company Size and Type in the respective fields.
12. Select your Rating in the Rating field.
13. Click on the Close button to finish the form and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, required/optional): First name of the customer.
* **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required/optional): Last name of the customer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Contact email of the customer.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Phone number of the customer.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our service?** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options to rate your satisfaction level.
* **Any feedback or suggestions?** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): Any feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Category** (`customer_category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply for category.
* **Department** (`customer_department`, select_one, required/optional): Select the department you belong to.
* **Level** (`customer_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your level.
* **Position** (`customer_position`, text, required/optional): Your job position.
* **Location** (`customer_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply for your location.
* **Industry** (`customer_industry`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply for your industry.
* **Sector** (`customer_sector`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply for your sector.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required/optional): Select the company size.
* **Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of company.
* **Job Title** (`customer_job_title`, text, required/optional): Your job title.
* **Manager** (`customer_manager`, select_one, required/optional): Select your manager's name.
* **Reporter** (`customer_reporter`, text, required/optional): Your name as a reporter.
* **Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required/optional): Enter your satisfaction level.
* **Rating** (`customer_rating`, select_one, required/optional): Select your rating.
* **Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any comments.
* **Open Comments** (`customer_open_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any open comments.
* **Closed Comments** (`customer_close_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any closed comments.
* **Close** (`customer_close`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you spent on the form.
* **Close** (`customer_close`, select_one, required/optional): Select the close field.
 

Please note that the fields with a red asterisk (*) next to the label are required fields. Make sure to fill them in as they are crucial for your feedback to be processed correctly. Also, be specific and detailed in your comments and open comments.
