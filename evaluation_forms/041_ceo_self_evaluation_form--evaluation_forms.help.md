# CEO Self-Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help CEOs assess their performance and progress towards the company's vision, goals, and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, reflecting on your performance and progress towards the company's goals and objectives.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question, using the provided options.
3. Provide specific examples and details to support your answers.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your submission.
