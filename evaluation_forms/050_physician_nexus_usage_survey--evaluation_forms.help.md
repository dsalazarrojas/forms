# physician_nexus_usage_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and usage information from physicians who use Nexus, a medical software platform. The form is intended to help the development team understand how often physicians use Nexus, how many patients they see per week, and their overall satisfaction with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the first name field with your name as a physician.
2.  Next, fill out the last name field with your surname.
3.  Enter your job title to indicate your role in the healthcare industry.
4.  Type your department or affiliation to help us understand your workplace.
5.  Enter the date you joined Nexus to help us understand when you started using the platform.
6.  Choose a role that best describes your level of involvement with Nexus.
7.  Answer the survey questions truthfully, and select the options that best reflect your experiences with Nexus.
8.  In the comments section, you can provide additional feedback or suggestions.
9.  Enter your email address and phone number to confirm your contact information.
10. Finally, confirm your email and phone number to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as a physician.
*   **User Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as a physician.
*   **Job Title (3)**: Enter your role in the healthcare industry.
*   **Department (4)**: Enter your department or affiliation in the healthcare industry.
*   **Date Joined (5)**: Enter the date you joined Nexus as a physician.
*   **Role (6)**: Select a role that best describes your level of involvement with Nexus.
*   **Physician Survey Question 1 (7)**: How often do you use Nexus?
*   **Physician Survey Question 2 (8)**: How many patients do you see per week?
*   **Physician Survey Question 3 (9)**: How many hours do you spend on Nexus per week?
*   **Physician Survey Question 4 (10)**: What feature in Nexus would you like to see next?
*   **Comments (11)**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
*   **Email (12)**: Enter your email address for confirmation.
*   **Phone Number (13)**: Enter your phone number for confirmation.
*   **Email Verified (14)**: Enter your email address for verification.
*   **Phone Verified (15)**: Enter your phone number for verification.
*   **Email Verified (16)**: (no label, only field for confirmation)
*   **Phone Verified (17)**: (no label, only field for confirmation)

Please note that some fields have been labeled as "no label" or "(no label)" due to lack of explicit labels in the original YAML.
