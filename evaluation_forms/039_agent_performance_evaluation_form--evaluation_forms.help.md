<thinking>**Agent Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide**
This form is intended to collect information on an agent's performance, focusing on sales, customer service, teamwork, and overall evaluation. The form should be filled out after observing or interacting with the agent, and its purpose is to assess their abilities and provide feedback for improvement. Please ensure you fill out the form only once for each evaluation period, avoiding duplication of entries.

## Purpose
The Agent Performance Evaluation Form is designed to gauge the performance of an agent across various key areas. It helps in identifying strengths and weaknesses, and providing feedback for future development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the agent being evaluated.
2. Select the start and end dates for the evaluation period.
3. Evaluate the agent's sales performance, customer service quality, and ability to work with peers.
4. Identify any areas where the agent needs training or development.
5. Provide an overall rating for the agent.
6. Add any additional comments or recommendations for the agent's improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agent being evaluated.
* **Evaluation period start** (`evaluation_period_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation period end** (`evaluation_period_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* **Sales performance** (`sales_performance`, select_one, required): Rate the agent's sales performance as Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, or Below expectations.
* **Customer service quality** (`customer_service`, select_one, required): Rate the agent's customer service quality as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Teamwork and collaboration** (`teamwork_and_collaboration`, select_one, required): Rate the agent's ability to work with peers as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Training needs identified** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Identify any areas where the agent needs training or development.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Provide an overall rating for the agent as Outstanding, Strong, Satisfactory, or Needs improvement.
* **Reviewer comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or recommendations for the agent's improvement.
