# Hospital Green Energy Transition Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Green Energy Transition Evaluation Form is a tool designed to help hospitals assess their progress towards a more sustainable future. This form will guide you through a comprehensive evaluation of your hospital's current state of sustainability, identifying areas for improvement and providing a baseline for future progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Please answer each question with as much detail as possible.
3. If you have already filled out a similar form in the past, review your previous responses and update any changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sustainability Assessment** (page 1, `id: 1`): Select all options that apply to your hospital's current state of sustainability. This section assesses your hospital's current initiatives, goals, and progress towards a more sustainable future.
* **Energy Management** (page 2, `id: 2`): Enter a numerical value indicating your hospital's current energy consumption. This is used to track your hospital's energy usage over time.
* **Green Energy Transition** (page 3, `id: 3`): In this text field, describe your hospital's current plans or initiatives for transitioning to green energy.
* **Energy Initiatives** (page 4, `id: 4`): Describe any energy-related initiatives or projects your hospital is currently undertaking or planning.
* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions** (page 5, `id: 5`): Enter a numerical value indicating your hospital's current greenhouse gas emissions. This is used to track your hospital's progress over time.
* **Carbon Footprint** (page 6, `id: 6`): Select a date indicating when your hospital's sustainability progress began. This is used to track your hospital's progress over time.
* **energy_consumption** (page 7, `id: 7`): Describe your hospital's current energy consumption habits.
* **Green House Energy Initiatives** (page 8, `id: 8`): Select one option that best describes your hospital's current approach to green energy initiatives.
* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions** (page 9, `id: 9`): Select one option that best describes your hospital's current approach to greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions** (page 10, `id: 10`): Select one option that best describes your hospital's current approach to greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions** (page 11, `id: 11`): Select multiple options that best describe your hospital's current approach to greenhouse gas emissions.

Note: Please select all options that apply to your hospital's current state of sustainability. For example, if your hospital has implemented energy-efficient lighting, select both "Option 1" and "Option 2" in the Sustainability Assessment section.
