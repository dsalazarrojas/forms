# This form is an evaluation tool for Continuing Medical Education (CME) programs. It aims to gather feedback on the clarity, relevance, and effectiveness of the learning objectives in a CME session. The form is designed to help program organizers and educators assess the quality of their programs and identify areas for improvement. To complete this form, please provide honest and constructive feedback on your experience with the CME program.

## CME Learning Objectives Evaluation - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
### 2. Email Address
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address, this will be used for confirmation and follow-up.
### 3. Healthcare Profession
* **Healthcare Profession** (Select One, Required): Choose your profession from the provided options.
### 4. Years in Practice
* **Years in Practice** (Select One, Required): Select your level of experience in practice.
### 5. CME Program Name
* **CME Program Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the CME program you are evaluating.
### 6. Program Date
* **Program Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you attended the CME program.
### 7. Learning Objectives Were Clear
* **Learning Objectives Were Clear** (Select One, Required): Rate how clear the learning objectives were.
### 8. Learning Objectives Were Relevant
* **Learning Objectives Were Relevant** (Select One, Required): Rate how relevant the learning objectives were to your professional needs.
### 9. Learning Objectives Were Achievable
* **Learning Objectives Were Achievable** (Select One, Required): Rate how realistic the learning objectives were for the session length.
### 10. Content Aligned With Objectives
* **Content Aligned With Objectives** (Select One, Required): Rate how well the content aligned with the stated goals.
### 11. All Objectives Were Met
* **All Objectives Were Met** (Select One, Required): Rate how well the objectives were achieved during the session.
### 12. Knowledge Gap Addressed
* **Knowledge Gap Addressed** (Select One, Required): Rate how well the session addressed your learning needs.
### 13. Competence Improvement
* **Competence Improvement** (Select One, Required): Rate the potential impact on your competence.
### 14. Performance Improvement Expected
* **Performance Improvement Expected** (Select One, Required): Rate the potential impact on your performance.
### 15. Patient Outcomes May Improve
* **Patient Outcomes May Improve** (Select One, Required): Rate the potential impact on patient outcomes.
### 16. Objective Difficulty Level
* **Objective Difficulty Level** (Select One, Required): Rate the difficulty level of the objectives.
### 17. Suggested Improvements
* **Suggested Improvements** (Text, Optional): Suggest any improvements for future CME programs.
### 18. Additional Learning Needs
* **Additional Learning Needs** (Text, Optional): Suggest additional topics you would like to see covered.
### 19. Overall Effectiveness Rating
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (Select One, Required): Rate the overall effectiveness of the CME program.
### 20. Would Attend Similar Programs
* **Would Attend Similar Programs** (Select One, Required): Rate your likelihood of attending similar programs.

Tips:
* Be honest in your evaluation. Your feedback will help us improve our CME programs.
* Use the provided options for your answers where possible.
* If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggested Improvements" and "Additional Learning Needs" fields.
* Please provide accurate information in the "Participant Name" and "Email Address" fields.
* Your feedback is appreciated, thank you for taking the time to evaluate this CME program!
