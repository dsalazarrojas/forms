# Science Fair Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Fair Judging Form is designed for science fair judges to evaluate and provide feedback on science fair projects. This form helps judges to record their thoughts, impressions, and scores for each project, ensuring that their evaluations are thorough and consistent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each project carefully and take notes on the **Introduction** page (page 1). You may want to ask questions or clarify any doubts with the project creator.
2. Enter the **Project Title** (page 2) and select the correct **Project Category** (page 3) for each project.
3. Enter your **Judge Initials** (page 4) so that we can associate your evaluation with your name.
4. Provide a score for the project (page 5) on a scale of 0-5, considering factors such as creativity, innovation, and scientific accuracy.
5. Enter any **Comments** (page 6) you have for the project, explaining your score or providing constructive feedback.
6. Add your **Signature** (page 7) so that we can verify your evaluation.
7. Record the **Date Judged** (page 8) for each project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the project, including its title, category, and a summary of its key points.
* **Project Title**: The name of the science fair project, which should be clear and concise.
* **Project Category**: The category that the project belongs to (e.g., physics, biology, chemistry).
* **Judge Initials**: Your initials to associate your evaluation with your name.
* **Score (0-5)**: A numerical score out of 5, reflecting your overall assessment of the project.
* **Comments**: A written feedback or remark about the project, providing insights or suggestions for improvement.
* **Signature**: Your signature to verify that you've evaluated the project.
* **Date Judged**: The date you've completed the evaluation, ensuring that we can track and record the progress of the science fair.
