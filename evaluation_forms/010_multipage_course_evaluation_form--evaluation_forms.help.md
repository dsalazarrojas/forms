# multiPageCourseEvaluationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation from users on a course. It is divided into several pages, each focusing on different aspects of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what information is being asked.
2. Fill out the form by providing detailed feedback and responses to each question.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers.
4. If necessary, use the provided options (e.g., multiple selection, single selection) to select the most relevant answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro (Page 1)**: This is an introductory page where you can provide a brief background information about the course. This page is not required but can be used to give context to your feedback.
* **subjectMatter (Page 2)**: This page asks for feedback on the subject matter of the course. Please provide detailed and specific feedback on the course content, such as its relevance, coherence, and overall effectiveness.
* **courseMaterials (Page 3)**: On this page, you can provide feedback on the course materials, such as textbooks, handouts, or online resources. Please select all the options that apply to your experience with the course materials.
* **instructingTechniques (Page 4)**: This page is for feedback on the instructing techniques used in the course. Please select the option that best describes your experience with the instructor's teaching style.
* **subjectMatterFeedback (Page 5)**: This page is for providing feedback on the subject matter of the course, similar to the previous page, but with more space to elaborate on your thoughts.
* **courseMaterialsFeedback (Page 6)**: This page is for providing feedback on the course materials, similar to the previous page, but with more space to elaborate on your thoughts.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
- Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided at the end of each page.
