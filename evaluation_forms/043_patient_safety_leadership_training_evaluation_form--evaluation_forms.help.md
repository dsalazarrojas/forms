# Patient Safety Leadership Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Patient Safety Leadership Training Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and satisfaction of patient safety leadership training sessions. It is essential to provide constructive feedback to help improve future trainings and ensure that trainees gain the most out of the training.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you are filling out this form after attending the correct training session.
2.  Read each field carefully and complete the form thoroughly.
3.  Answer all required fields marked as "Required".
4.  If you are unsure about any field, please ask your trainer or the training team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Training Session** (`1`, `select_one`, required):  This field asks you to rate the intensity of the training session, with options for High, Medium, and Low. Select the option that best describes your experience.
*   **Leadership Training** (`2`, `text`, required):  Please provide a detailed description of what you learned during the leadership training, focusing on the key takeaways and areas of improvement.
*   **Safety Risk Factors** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required):  Indicate the level of safety risk factors that were discussed during the training, with options for High, Medium, and Low. Select all that apply.
*   **Leadership Skills** (`4`, `number`, required):  This field asks you to rate the leadership skills demonstrated by the trainer, with a scale from 1-5.
*   **Leadership Behavior** (`5`, `number`, optional):  If applicable, rate the leadership behavior demonstrated by the trainer, with a scale from 1-5.
*   **Training Effectiveness** (`6`, `time`, required):  Please enter the length of time you spent on the training, in hours.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`7`, `select_one`, required):  Rate your overall satisfaction with the training, with options for High, Medium, and Low.
*   **Feedback** (`8`, `text`, optional):  Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the training.
*   **Additional Comments** (`9`, `text`, optional):  Add any additional comments or suggestions for future trainings.
*   **Name** (`10`, `email`, required):  Please enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
*   **Department** (`12`, `text`, required):  Indicate the department you belong to.
*   **Hospital** (`13`, `text`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the hospital you are working at.
*   **Location** (`14`, `text`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the location of the training.
*   **Date** (`15`, `date`, required):  Enter the date you completed the training.
*   **Time** (`16`, `time`, required):  Enter the time you completed the training.
*   **Training Session Length** (`17`, `number`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the length of the training session.
*   **Evaluation Form Rating** (`18`, `select_one`, required):  Rate the overall quality of this evaluation form, with options for Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
*   **Evaluation Form Usefulness** (`19`, `select_one`, optional):  If applicable, rate the usefulness of this evaluation form.
*   **Training Recommendation** (`20`, `select_one`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the level of training recommendation for future trainings.
*   **Additional Feedback** (`21`, `text`, optional):  Add any additional feedback or comments about the form.
*   **Evaluation Form Completion Status** (`22`, `select_one`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the status of the evaluation form completion.
*   **Evaluation Form Status** (`23`, `select_one`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the status of the evaluation form.
*   **Evaluation Form Last Updated By** (`24`, `text`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the last updated by.
*   **Evaluation Form Last Updated Date** (`25`, `date`, optional):  If applicable, indicate the last updated date.
