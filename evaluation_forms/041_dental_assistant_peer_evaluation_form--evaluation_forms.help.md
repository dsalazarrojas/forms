# Dental Assistant Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the performance of dental assistants in a clinical setting, focusing on their communication skills, technical abilities, and teamwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and concisely, providing specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
2. Be objective and constructive in your feedback, focusing on performance and potential areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assistant Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the dental assistant being evaluated.
* **Assistant Performance** (Text, Required): Describe the dental assistant's overall performance during this evaluation period. Provide specific examples of their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Communication Skills** (Text, Required): Evaluate the dental assistant's ability to communicate effectively with patients, colleagues, and other healthcare professionals.
* **Technical Skills** (Text, Required): Assess the dental assistant's proficiency in performing dental procedures and maintaining clinical equipment.
* **Problem Solving** (Text, Required): Rate the dental assistant's ability to analyze and resolve problems in a clinical setting.
* **Team Work** (Text, Required): Evaluate the dental assistant's collaboration with other team members, such as dentists, hygienists, and lab technicians.
* **Comments** (Text, Required): Add any additional comments or observations about the dental assistant's performance.
* **Additional Feedback** (Text, Required): Provide any further comments or suggestions for improvement.
