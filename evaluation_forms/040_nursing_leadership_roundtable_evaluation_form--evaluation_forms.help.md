# Nursing Leadership Roundtable Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Nursing Leadership Roundtable Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience at the roundtable event. This form aims to assess the attendance, rating, and overall satisfaction of the event. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the event and make future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully, making sure to answer all the required questions.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience.
3. Fill out the form entirely before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Attendance** (`event_attendance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the dates and sessions you attended at the roundtable. Please choose all that apply.
* **Event Rating** (`event_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Leadership Team** (`leadership_team`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the effectiveness of the leadership team at the event. Choose 'Yes' if you felt they were helpful and 'No' if not.
* **Session Leaders** (`session_leaders`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the session leaders you found most helpful during the event.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if your overall experience at the roundtable was positive and 'No' otherwise.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Offer any suggestions for future improvement.
* **Event Recommendation** (`event_recommendation`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate whether you would recommend the event to others. Choose 'Yes' if you would and 'No' otherwise.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, date, required: false): The date you are submitting your evaluation.
* **Time of Evaluation** (`time_of_evaluation`, time, required: false): The time you are submitting your evaluation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate whether you submitted your evaluation. Choose 'Yes' if you did and 'No' otherwise.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate whether your evaluation was submitted on time. Choose 'Yes' if it was and 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide your genuine feedback to help us improve future events.
* Fill out the form completely to ensure your input is valuable.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
