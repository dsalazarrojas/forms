# Curriculum Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate student performance and progress in a course, providing valuable feedback to instructors and contributing to the overall improvement of the course.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Course Information
Provide the course name as it's actually taught. If it's a recurring course, you can mention the specific session or semester being evaluated.

### Step 2: Enter Instructor Information
Enter the instructor's name and any other relevant contact details, such as their email or phone number for follow-up questions.

### Step 3: Describe Course Progress
Describe the student's course progress, mentioning any notable achievements or areas where the student might need improvement.

### Step 4: Evaluate Progress Score
Evaluate the student's progress using a scale of 1 (Weak), 2 (Average), or 3 (Strong).

### Step 5: List Strengths and Weaknesses
List the strengths and weaknesses of the course using the same scale (1, 2, or 3).

### Step 6: Add Comments
Include any additional comments or feedback for the student or instructor.

### Step 7: Enter Contact Information
Enter the student's email and phone number for contact purposes.

### Step 8: Date Submitted
Finally, record the date the form is submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Course Name
* **Course Name** (text): Enter the actual name of the course being evaluated.

### 2. Instructor Name
* **Instructor Name** (text): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.

### 3. Student Name
* **Student Name** (text): Enter the name of the student being evaluated.

### 4. Course Progress
* **Course Progress** (note): Describe the student's progress in the course, mentioning any notable achievements or areas where the student might need improvement.

### 5. Progress Score
* **Progress Score** (number): Evaluate the student's progress using a scale of 1 (Weak), 2 (Average), or 3 (Strong).

### 6. Strengths
* **Strengths** (select multiple): List the strengths of the course using the same scale (1, 2, or 3).

### 7. Weaknesses
* **Weaknesses** (select multiple): List the weaknesses of the course using the same scale (1, 2, or 3).

### 8. Comments
* **Comments** (text): Include any additional comments or feedback for the student or instructor.

### 9. Email
* **Email** (email): Enter the student's email for contact purposes.

### 10. Phone
* **Phone** (text): Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.

### 11. Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted** (date): Record the date the form is submitted.

Tips: Always complete this form accurately and consistently, using the same format and criteria for each student.
