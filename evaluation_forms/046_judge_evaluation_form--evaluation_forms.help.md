# Judge Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Judge Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback and evaluation from judges about their experiences and ratings of events. This form helps to assess the performance of judges, track their progress, and evaluate their scores.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Info**: This section is optional and allows you to provide general information about yourself, such as your name, email, and phone number.
2. **Judge Performance**: Enter a numeric value to evaluate your performance as a judge.
3. **Score**: Enter a numeric value to evaluate the event.
4. **Evaluation**: Choose one of the following options to evaluate the event:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
5. **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
6. **Judge Name**: Enter your name as a judge.
7. **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
8. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
9. **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation.
10. **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your experience.
11. **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
12. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
13. **Assigned Judge**: Check if you were assigned to judge the event:
	* Yes
	* No
14. **Assigned Event**: Check if you were assigned to the event:
	* Yes
	* No
15. **Form Completion Status**: Choose the status of your form completion:
	* Not Started
	* In Progress
	* Completed
16. **Judge Rating**: Enter a numeric value to evaluate your performance.
17. **Form Submitted**: Check if you have submitted the form:
	* Yes
	* No
18. **Evaluation Comments**: Provide any comments about your experience.
19. **Evaluation Score**: Enter a numeric value to evaluate the event.
20. **Evaluation Status**: Choose the status of your evaluation:
	* Open
	* Closed
21. **Evaluation Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your evaluation.
22. **Submission Status**: Check if the submission is:
	* Yes
	* No
