# Online Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Course Evaluation Form" is designed to gather feedback from students about their online course experiences. This form helps instructors and course creators understand what works well and what needs improvement in the course.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form fields carefully to ensure you fill them out correctly.
2.  Fill out the required fields, marked with a star (\*). These fields are crucial for the form's purpose.
3.  Provide honest and detailed feedback in the text fields (Course Name, Course Materials, Course Content, Course Instructor, Course Instructor 2, and Course Organization).
4.  Select the correct options for Course Format and Submission Status.
5.  Enter the correct dates and times for Submission Date and Submission Date 2.
6.  Enter a numerical rating for Course Rating and Course Quality.
7.  Check the "Would You Recommend This Course?" option and choose the best answer based on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Course Name** (\*): Please provide the name of the course you're evaluating.
*   **Course Rating** (\*): Rate the course on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
*   **Course Instructor** (\*): Enter the name of the course instructor who taught the course.
*   **Course Materials** (\*): Provide feedback about the course materials, such as textbooks, resources, and equipment.
*   **Course Format**: Select the type of course format (Online, Hybrid, or In-person) based on your experience.
*   **Course Quality** (\*): Rate the course quality on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
*   **Would You Recommend This Course?**: Choose whether you would recommend the course based on your experience.
*   **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission (True or False).
*   **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
*   **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
*   **Course Length**: Enter the length of the course.
*   **Course Content**: Provide feedback about the course content, such as the syllabus, course objectives, and overall content.
*   **Course Organization**: Enter the name of the course organization that developed the course.
*   **Submission Date 2**: Enter a second submission date, if necessary.
*   **Submission Time 2**: Enter a second submission time, if necessary.
*   **Course Rating 2**: Enter a second course rating, if necessary.
*   **Course Instructor 2**: Enter the name of the second course instructor.
*   **Submission Status 2**: Select the status of your second submission (True or False).

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you're unsure about a field, contact the course administrators for guidance.
*   Provide detailed and honest feedback to help improve the course.
