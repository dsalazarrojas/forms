# Crowd Management Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on crowd management software products, focusing on their features, performance, and overall value. Please fill out this form to provide detailed evaluations of the software you are currently using or considering for use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **What is the purpose of the form?**: Briefly explain the software's primary purpose and what problem it aims to solve.
2. **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the software vendor or supplier.
3. **Software Name**: Enter the name of the software product being evaluated.
4. **User Satisfaction**: Provide a number indicating your level of satisfaction with the software, on a scale from 1 to 10.
5. **Vendor Rating**: Provide a number indicating your rating of the software vendor, on a scale from 1 to 10.
6. **Overall Rating**: Provide a number indicating your overall rating of the software, on a scale from 1 to 10.
7. **What features do you like?**: Select all features that you particularly like about the software.
8. **What features do you dislike?**: Select all features that you do not particularly like or find less useful.
9. **Date**: Enter the date you started using the software or the date you plan to start using it.
10. **Time**: Enter the time you spent evaluating the software or your expected time commitment.
11. **Email**: Enter a contact email for follow-up questions or discussions.
12. **Phone**: Enter a contact phone number for follow-up questions or discussions.
13. **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience with the software.
14. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or suggestions you have about the software.

## Tips
- Be thorough and detailed in your answers, especially when describing features you like or dislike.
- Ensure your feedback is specific and provides concrete examples, if possible.
- If you are unsure about any question or field, please reach out to the vendor or contact email provided in this form for further assistance.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments" section to provide them.
