# Autonomous Taxi Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Autonomous Taxi Service Evaluation Form is designed to gather customer feedback on their experience with the autonomous taxi service. By completing this form, you will provide valuable insights that will help us improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Ride Details" page and enter your full name and email address.
2. On the "Ride Details" page, enter the date of your ride, the ride ID (found in your trip history), and the pickup and drop-off locations.
3. On the "Service Evaluation" page, rate the vehicle cleanliness, ease of pickup, route efficiency, and driving safety.
4. Select all areas that you would like to see improved.
5. Finally, rate your overall experience and indicate whether you would recommend our service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your name for our records.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Ride ID** (text, required): Enter the ride ID you received from your trip history.
* **Date of Ride** (date, required): Enter the date of your ride.
* **Pickup and Drop-off Locations** (text, optional): Enter both locations separated by a comma.
* **Vehicle Cleanliness** (select_one, required): Rate the interior and exterior condition of the vehicle.
* **Ease of Pickup** (select_one, required): Rate how well the driver found you.
* **Route Efficiency** (select_one, required): Rate how well the car took the most optimal path.
* **Perception of Driving Safety** (select_one, required): Rate how safe you felt during the trip.
* **Overall Ride Experience** (select_one, required): Rate your overall experience.
* **Areas for Improvement** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply.
* **Would you recommend our service?** (select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend our service.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional feedback you would like to share.
