# Substance Misuse Prevention Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Substance Misuse Prevention Evaluation form is designed to assess the effectiveness and impact of a substance misuse prevention program in a school setting. This form helps school staff and administrators gather data on program performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all relevant options for participant goals, program goals, and program evaluation processes.
3. Be specific when providing information about program location, participant demographics, and other program details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Program Info**: This field is for providing general information about the school program, such as its name, address, and a brief description.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where the program is being implemented.
* **Program Manager**: Identify the person responsible for managing the program.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school where the program is located.
* **Program Description**: Provide a brief description of the program, including its purpose and goals.
* **Program Location**: Specify the location where the program is being implemented.
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the date the program started.
* **Program End Date**: Enter the date the program is expected to end (or leave blank if ongoing).
* **Program Goals**: Select all relevant options that apply to the program's objectives, such as educating participants about substance misuse, providing support, and helping participants stop using substances.
* **Participant Goals**: Select all relevant options that apply to the participant's objectives, such as learning about substance misuse prevention, gaining support, and stopping substance use.
* **Staff Support**: Indicate whether the program has sufficient staff support, such as adequate resources and training.
* **Participant Engagement**: Evaluate the level of participant engagement in the program, choosing High, Medium, or Low.
* **Program Satisfaction**: Evaluate the level of program satisfaction, choosing High, Medium, or Low.
* **Evaluation Process**: Select all relevant options that apply to the evaluation process, such as regular or irregular assessments.
* **Follow-up**: Indicate if follow-up activities are conducted to monitor program progress.
* **Staff Training**: Evaluate the level of staff training, choosing High, Medium, or Low.
* **Program Participants**: Select the number of participants in the program, such as 10-20, 21-50, or 51+.
* **Participant Satisfaction**: Evaluate the level of participant satisfaction, choosing High, Medium, or Low.
* **Participant Engagement Level**: Describe the level of participant engagement, such as regular attendance or limited participation.
* **Participant Support Level**: Describe the level of support provided to participants, such as regular check-ins or limited support.
* **Program Evaluation Frequency**: Select the frequency of program evaluation, such as monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly.
* **Participant Evaluation Frequency**: Select the frequency of participant evaluation, such as monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly.
* **Participant Fidelity**: Evaluate the level of participant fidelity, choosing High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data Collection Method**: Select the method used to collect data, such as phone, email, survey, or in-person.
* **Form Completion**: Indicate if the form has been completed, choosing Yes or No.
