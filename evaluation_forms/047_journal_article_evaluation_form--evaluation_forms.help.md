# Journal Article Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a journal article, specifically assessing its quality, relevance, completeness, originality, and grammar.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the article's title and year of publication in the respective fields.
2. Select the quality of the article using the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the relevance of the article using the dropdown menu.
4. Select the completeness of the article using the dropdown menu (this field is only required if you have read the article in its entirety).
5. Choose the originality of the article using the dropdown menu (this field is optional).
6. Select the clarity of the article using the dropdown menu.
7. Evaluate the grammar of the article using the dropdown menu.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Article Title** (text): Enter the title of the journal article being evaluated.
* **Author** (text): Enter the author(s) of the journal article.
* **Journal Article Title** (text): Enter the title of the journal article, if different from the main title.
* **Journal Article Year** (number): Enter the year of publication of the journal article.
* **Quality** (select_one): Select the overall quality of the article from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Relevance** (select_multiple): Select the relevance of the article, if applicable, from High, Medium, Low, or None.
* **Completeness** (select_multiple): Select the completeness of the article from Complete, Partial, or Not Complete (only required if you have read the article in its entirety).
* **Originality** (number): This field is optional. If you want to evaluate this aspect, enter a number value.
* **Clarity** (select_multiple): Select the clarity of the article from High, Medium, Low, or Not Clear.
* **Grammar** (select_multiple): Select the grammar of the article from Good, Very Good, or Poor.

## Tips
* Please be objective and provide honest feedback.
* Evaluate each aspect of the article based on your understanding and expertise.
* If you are unsure about any aspect, choose the option that best represents your evaluation.
* You can only evaluate one article at a time using this form. If you need to evaluate multiple articles, refer to this guide for each article individually.
