# Delivery Personnel Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the delivery performance of personnel. The evaluation consists of 4 attributes: punctuality, professionalism, communication, and overall satisfaction. The form is used to gather feedback on how well a delivery person met expectations during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the evaluation date and delivery person's name.
2. Answer each question by selecting a score between 1 (lowest) and 10 (highest) for each attribute.
3. Make sure to provide comments if you have any feedback for the delivery person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **delivery_punctuality**
 * Label: delivery_punctuality
 * Type: number
 * Required: false
 * Explanation: Rate the delivery person's punctuality during their visit. This attribute assesses how well the delivery person kept their scheduled appointment and arrived on time.

### 2. **delivery_professionalism**
 * Label: delivery_professionalism
 * Type: number
 * Required: false
 * Explanation: Rate the delivery person's level of professionalism during their visit. This includes their behavior, attitude, and overall conduct.

### 3. **delivery_communication**
 * Label: delivery_communication
 * Type: number
 * Required: false
 * Explanation: Rate the delivery person's communication skills during their visit. This includes their ability to clearly explain the delivery, handle questions, and provide information.

### 4. **overall_satisfaction**
 * Label: overall_satisfaction
 * Type: number
 * Required: false
 * Explanation: Rate your overall satisfaction with the delivery person's service. This includes how well they met your expectations and needs.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when evaluating the delivery person's performance.
- Use scores that reflect your actual experience with the delivery person.
- Provide comments if you have any feedback that doesn't fit into the score.
