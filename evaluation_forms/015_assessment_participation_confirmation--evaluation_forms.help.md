# Assessment Participation Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assessment Participation Confirmation form is used to gather essential information from participants to prepare for and administer assessments. It confirms their participation, gathers details about their availability and requirements, and ensures they understand the assessment's format and content.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Review each field carefully, as they're all crucial for assessment preparation and execution.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Participant ID Number** (`participant_id`, `text`, required): Provide your student or employee ID number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Assessment Name** (`assessment_name`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the assessment you're participating in.
* **Assessment Code** (`assessment_code`, `text`, required): Enter the assessment code or reference number.
* **Scheduled Assessment Date** (`scheduled_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Scheduled Time** (`scheduled_time`, `text`, required): Select the start time of the assessment.
* **Assessment Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the assessment will take place.
* **Will You Participate** (`participation_status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your attendance by selecting 'Yes I Will Participate'.
* **Reason for Non-Participation** (`conflict_reason`, `text`, required): If you cannot participate, enter a valid reason.
* **Do You Need to Reschedule** (`reschedule_needed`, `select_one`, optional): If you need to reschedule, select 'Yes'.
* **Preferred Reschedule Date** (`preferred_reschedule_date`, `date`, optional): If rescheduling is needed, enter a valid alternative date.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`accommodations_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Check any required accommodations you need, such as extended time, separate room, assistive technology, sign language interpreter, large print materials, or breaks.
* **Describe Accommodations** (`accommodations_description`, `text`, optional): Provide details about the accommodations you require.
* **Have You Received Preparation Materials** (`preparation_materials_received`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you've received the necessary study guides or resources.
* **Do You Understand Assessment Requirements** (`understand_requirements`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the assessment's format and rules.
* **Questions or Concerns** (`questions_concerns`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you have.
* **I Consent to Participate** (`consent_participate`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent to participate voluntarily.
* **I Agree to Maintain Confidentiality** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of maintaining assessment content confidentiality.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Confirmation Date** (`confirmation_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you're confirming your participation.
