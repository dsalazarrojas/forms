# Textbook Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Textbook Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from students and teachers on the quality, effectiveness, and overall experience of a textbook. This feedback helps authors, educators, and publishers make informed decisions about future textbook editions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **General Information** (Page 1): Enter your name, teacher's name (if applicable), and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. Enter the **Textbook ID** (Page 2): Please enter the specific ID of the textbook you are evaluating.
3. Enter the **Textbook Title** (Page 3): Enter the title of the textbook you are evaluating.
4. Rate the **Teacher Rating** (Page 4): Rate the teacher's performance and teaching style on a scale from 1 to 5.
5. Rate the **Textbook Rating** (Page 5): Rate the textbook's overall quality and effectiveness on a scale from 1 to 5.
6. Enter the **Evaluation Date** (Page 6): Enter the date of the evaluation.
7. Enter your **Comments** (Page 7): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the textbook.
8. Choose the **Teacher Recommendation** (Page 8): Select one of the options for teacher recommendation.
9. Enter your **Student Feedback** (Page 9): Provide any feedback about your experience with the textbook.
10. Enter the **Teacher Feedback** (Page 10): Provide feedback about the teacher's performance and teaching style.
11. Select the **Evaluation Status** (Page 11): Select one of the options to indicate the status of the evaluation.
