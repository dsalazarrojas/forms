# medical_wearable_technology_pilot_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations on the medical wearable technology pilot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully and ensure that you are the correct participant.
2. Answer each question or select an option that best describes your experience with the medical wearable device.
3. Be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (1): This is a brief introduction to the form. You can skip this section if you have already read the introduction.
### What is the purpose of this form?
* **What is the purpose of this form?** (2): Please explain the purpose of this form in your own words.
### Medical Wearable Device Details
* **Medical Wearable Device Details** (3): Select the medical wearable device you used during the pilot.
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (4): Select all the participants who took part in the pilot.
### Usability
* **Usability** (5): Select all the usability aspects that applied to your experience with the device. 
### Benefits
* **Benefits** (6): Describe the benefits you experienced with the medical wearable device.
### Challenges
* **Challenges** (7): Describe the challenges you faced while using the medical wearable device.
### Overall
* **Overall** (8): Rate your overall experience with the medical wearable device. 
### How would you rate the overall usability of this device?
* **How would you rate the overall usability of this device?** (9): Rate your experience with the device on a scale of 1-5.
### Will you participate in another study?
* **Will you participate in another study?** (10): Select whether you are willing to participate in another study.
### Comments
* **Comments** (11): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the device.
### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (12): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback.
### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone** (13): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further feedback.
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (14): Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.

Note: Some fields are optional, so you can skip them if you do not have the information or feel it is not relevant.
