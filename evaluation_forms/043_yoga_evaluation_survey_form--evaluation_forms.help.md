# Yoga Evaluation Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by yoga students to evaluate their experience with a class led by an instructor. Your feedback is valuable to the instructor and the studio, and will help them to improve their teaching and service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much information as possible.
2. If you're unsure about a field, try to provide the best answer based on your experience.
3. Submit the form and make sure to click on the "Submit" button.
4. If you've already filled out this form in the past, there's no need to complete it again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Form** (`survey_form`, text, not required): This is where you can provide any general feedback or comments about your experience with the class. You can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the instructor who led your class.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the class you attended.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you attended the class.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you attended the class.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, not required): Choose one of the following ratings to express your satisfaction:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): If you'd like to provide more in-depth comments, use this field. You can write as much or as little as you like.

## Tips
- Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
- Keep your comments professional and respectful.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
