# Barwis Methods Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barwis Methods Health Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential patient information for health and fitness programs. It is intended to gather details about the patient's personal information, medical history, physical metrics, and goals related to health and fitness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form completely and accurately to ensure the best possible outcomes for your health and fitness journey.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*).
3. For fields with options, select the option that best describes your current situation.
4. If you are unsure about a particular field, please ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Please enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the given options.
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Height in Inches**: Enter your height in inches.
* **Weight in Pounds**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Please select any existing medical conditions**: Select all existing medical conditions that apply to you.
* **List any known allergies**: List any known allergies, including medications, food, environmental, etc.
* **List all current medications and dosages**: List all current medications and their dosages.
* **Have you had any surgeries in the past 12 months?**: If yes, please describe.
* **Current Physical Activity Level**: Select your current physical activity level from the given options.
* **What is your primary health or fitness goal?**: Describe your primary health or fitness goal.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Form Completion Date**: Enter the date you are completing this form.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask for assistance. This form is designed to be completed in one session. If you need to make changes, please let us know and we will guide you through the process.
