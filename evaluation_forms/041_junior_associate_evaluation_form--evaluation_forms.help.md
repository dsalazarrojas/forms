# Junior Associate Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of junior associates and provide guidance and feedback to help them grow in their roles.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the junior associate's progress and performance over the selected evaluation period.
2.  Select the evaluation period from the options provided.
3.  Enter the junior associate's name in the "junior associate name" field.
4.  Enter your name as the manager in the "manager name" field.
5.  Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "evaluation comments" field.
6.  Select the performance areas to focus on for the junior associate, such as "Communication", "Teamwork", "Leadership", "Adaptability", and "Initiative".
7.  Enter the scores for each performance area, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
8.  Enter the overall score for the junior associate's performance.
9.  Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "evaluation comments other" field.
10.  Select the evaluation status, whether it is "Completed" or "Not Completed".
11.  Enter the completed status, whether it is "True" or "False".
12.  Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "evaluation comments other" field.
13.  Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "evaluation comments other" field.
14.  Enter your signature as the manager.
15.  Enter the junior associate's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **junior associate name**: Enter the junior associate's name.
* **manager name**: Enter your name as the manager.
* **evaluation date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **evaluation period**: Select the evaluation period from the options provided.
* **evaluation period**: This field allows you to select the time period for the evaluation. Please choose from "First 90 days", "Next 90 days", or "Beyond 6 months".
* **prophets**: Not applicable (this field is not relevant).
* **performance area 1**: Select the performance area to focus on for the junior associate. Please choose from "Communication", "Teamwork", "Leadership", "Adaptability", or "Initiative".
* **performance area 2**: Select the performance area to focus on for the junior associate. Please choose from "Communication", "Teamwork", "Leadership", "Adaptability", or "Initiative".
* **evaluation comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the junior associate.
* **score 1**: Enter the score for the junior associate's performance in the selected area.
* **score 2**: Enter the score for the junior associate's performance in the second selected area.
* **score 3**: Enter the score for the junior associate's performance in the third selected area.
* **performance rating**: Enter the performance rating for the junior associate.
* **overall score**: Enter the overall score for the junior associate's performance.
* **feedback from junior**: Enter any feedback from the junior associate.
* **feedback from manager**: Enter any feedback from you, the manager.
* **feedback from other**: Enter any feedback from other team members or stakeholders.
* **action plan**: Enter any action plan or recommendations for the junior associate.
* **next steps**: Enter any next steps or follow-up actions for the junior associate.
* **evaluation status**: Select the evaluation status, whether it is "Completed" or "Not Completed".
* **completed status**: Select the completed status, whether it is "True" or "False".
* **evaluation comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the junior associate.
* **evaluation comments other**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the junior associate.
* **manager signature**: Enter your signature as the manager.
* **junior signature**: Enter the junior associate's signature.
* **evaluation comments other**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the junior associate.
