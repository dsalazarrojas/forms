# Site Visit Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Visit Evaluation Checklist is designed to gather information about a site visit to provide feedback on its effectiveness and help identify areas for improvement. Please fill out this form to evaluate your site visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

* Start by reviewing the site visit and think about what happened during the visit.
* For each field, choose the relevant options or answer the questions as accurately as possible.
* Make sure to provide detailed answers for text fields (like comments and feedback).
* Select all applicable options for multiple-choice fields (like stakeholder engagement and recommendations).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Visit Effectiveness** (`site_visit_effectiveness`, number, optional): This field asks for a numerical score to rate the overall effectiveness of the site visit. Please provide a score between 1 (least effective) and 5 (most effective).
* **Logistics** (`logistics`, note, optional): This field is for noting any issues or challenges related to logistics, such as transportation, parking, or facility accessibility.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (`stakeholder_engagement`, select multiple, optional): Check all the stakeholders you interacted with during the site visit, such as project team members, customers, or vendors. This field is optional and not required.
* **Overall Outcomes** (`overall_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the overall outcomes of the site visit, such as any goals achieved or lessons learned.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments about the site visit.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select one, optional): Check "Yes" if you would recommend that the site visit be repeated or modified in some way. This field is optional and not required.
* **Logistics Score** (`logistics_score`, number, optional): This field asks for a numerical score to rate the logistics of the site visit, such as transportation, parking, or facility accessibility. Please provide a score between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, number, optional): This field asks for a numerical score to rate the engagement of the stakeholders during the site visit. Please provide a score between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).
* **Outcomes Score** (`outcomes_score`, number, optional): This field asks for a numerical score to rate the overall outcomes of the site visit. Please provide a score between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the site visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the site visit coordinator.
* This form is for evaluation purposes only, so feel free to provide honest and constructive feedback.
