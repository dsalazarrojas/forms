# NGO Program Evaluation Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants in an NGO program evaluation. The data collected will help the program staff and evaluators to understand the program's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and the name of the program you are participating in.
2. Enter your email address and the contact information of the person who signed this form below.
3. Check the box next to "Yes, please use my data" to indicate your consent for data collection.
4. Fill in the contact information for the program staff member who signed this form below.
5. Sign the form with your participant signature and date.
6. The program staff member who signed below will also sign the form after reading and understanding the participant's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and follow-up if needed.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Check the box next to "Yes, please use my data" if you consent to the data collection.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the person who signed this form below.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Evaluation Start Date** (`evaluation_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation End Date** (`evaluation_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the evaluation.
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional consent information, if needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign this form with your signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of signing this form.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you signed this form.
* **Staff Member Signature** (`staff_member_signature`, text, required): Sign this form with your signature as a program staff member.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization running the program.
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program staff member who signed this form.
* **Staff Title** (`staff_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the program staff member.
* **Staff Contact Info** (`staff_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the staff member.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the program staff member.
* **Staff Organization** (`staff_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization the staff member belongs to.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the program staff member.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately.
* Make sure to check the box next to "Yes, please use my data" to consent to data collection.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to contact the program staff.
* Make sure to sign the form with your participant signature and date.
* The program staff member who signed below will also sign the form after reading and understanding your information.
* This form is for internal use by the program staff and evaluators, and your data will be kept confidential.
