# Independent Party Interview Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Party Interview Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from interviews conducted independently of any company or organization. It is used to evaluate the performance of a candidate after an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the person interviewed.
*   **Candidate Position**: Select the candidate's job position from the provided options, e.g., Account Manager, Data Scientist, or Software Engineer.
*   **Candidate Rating**: Provide a numerical score for the candidate's performance.
*   **Candidate Feedback**: Add any additional feedback or comments about the candidate's performance.
*   **Interviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the interview.
*   **Interviewer Title**: Provide the title or position of the interviewer, e.g., Manager, Director, or Team Lead.
*   **Interviewer Position**: Select the position of the interviewer from the provided options.
*   **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview.
*   **Interview Time**: Enter the time of the interview.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about the interview.
*   **Email**: Enter the candidate's contact email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter the candidate's contact phone number.
*   **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback.
