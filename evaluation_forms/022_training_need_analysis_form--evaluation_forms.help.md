# Training Need Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and analyze training needs and objectives for organizations, helping them create more effective training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Organization, Training Program, and Job Title information, as these fields are not required but recommended for context.
2. Provide a brief description of your Training Need, Evaluation Method, and Evaluation Scale to give insight into your current state and goals.
3. Indicate the Training Objective you are aiming for, as this will help in tailoring the training program to your needs.
4. Report your Evaluation Result to give a clear picture of your evaluation process and outcome.
5. Mention the Training Impact you expect from the program, if any.
6. If needed, provide comments for any further clarification.
7. Finally, provide the Evaluation Date and Evaluation Time for the purpose of tracking and record keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter your organization name for context and reference.
* **Training Program** (`Training Program`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the training program you are seeking or have in place.
* **Training Specialist** (`Training Specialist`, `text`, required: false): Mention the person responsible for the training program or the individual being trained.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title for context and reference.
* **Training Need** (`Training Need`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of what training needs you have.
* **Evaluation Method** (`Evaluation Method`, `text`, required: false): Describe the method used for evaluation or assessment.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`Evaluation Scale`, `text`, required: false): Mention the scale or criteria used for evaluation.
* **Training Objective** (`Training Objective`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the specific goal or target of the training program.
* **Evaluation Result** (`Evaluation Result`, `text`, required: false): Report the outcome or result of the evaluation process.
* **Training Impact** (`Training Impact`, `text`, required: false): Mention any expected impact or benefits from the training program.
* **Training Recommendation** (`Training Recommendation`, `text`, required: false): Provide any recommendations for the training program or further development.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or additional information about the training program.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation for tracking and reference.
* **Evaluation Time** (`Evaluation Time`, `time`, required: false): Provide the time of the evaluation for tracking purposes.
* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your organization's name for context and reference.

Please ensure that each section is completed accurately and concisely, and that the form is filled out once for each specific training need or objective.
