# SPA Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the Single-Photon Amplification (SPA) process. It is designed to collect feedback from customers who have undergone the SPA process, providing valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title.
2. Provide a detailed description of the medical condition you are experiencing.
3. Enter the name of the product used in the SPA process.
4. Provide a detailed description of the product used.
5. Enter the price of the product.
6. Rate the product's performance (if applicable).
7. Indicate whether or not you would recommend the product.
8. Enter your name.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Provide a detailed medical history.
12. Enter any additional comments about your experience.
13. Select any additional services you received during the treatment (if applicable).
14. If applicable, provide any other services you received.
15. Add any additional comments about your experience.
16. Add your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for this form.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Please describe your medical condition you are experiencing.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product used in the SPA process.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the product used.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): Rate the product's performance (1-5).
* **Recommendation** (`product_recommendation`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether or not you would recommend the product.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical History** (`customer_medical_history`, text, required): Provide a detailed medical history.
* **Customer Comment** (`customer_comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your experience.
* **Additional Services** (`customer_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you received during the treatment.
* **Other Services** (`customer_services_other`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any other services you received.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Form Signature** (`form_signature`, text, optional): Add your signature.
