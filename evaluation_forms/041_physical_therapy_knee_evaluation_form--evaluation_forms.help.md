# Physical Therapy Knee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Physical Therapy Knee Evaluation Form" is a questionnaire used by physical therapists to gather information about a patient's knee health and medical history. This form helps the therapist to understand the patient's condition better, identify potential issues, and provide targeted treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (Field 1) and **Last Name** (Field 2) accurately.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** (Field 3).
3. Share your **Medical History** (Field 4) and **Knee History** (Field 5) in the text fields provided.
4. Answer the questions regarding **Knee Injury** (Field 6) and **Knee Surgery** (Field 7) with the available options.
5. Indicate your **Pain Level** (Field 8) using a numerical value.
6. Describe your **Knee Pain** (Field 9) in the provided text field.
7. Answer whether you have any **Knee Function** (Field 10) issues.
8. Select the applicable **Knee Range** (Field 11) options.
9. Indicate your **Knee Stability** (Field 12) using a numerical value.
10. Choose whether your **Knee Alignment** (Field 13) is satisfactory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Field 1, Text, Required: False)
	+ Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (Field 2, Text, Required: False)
	+ Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Field 3, Date, Required: False)
	+ Enter your date of birth to help the therapist understand your age and medical history.
* **Medical History** (Field 4, Text, Required: False)
	+ Write down any relevant medical history that may affect your knee health.
* **Knee History** (Field 5, Text, Required: False)
	+ Provide any information about your knee condition or history.
* **Knee Injury** (Field 6, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Answer whether you have experienced any knee injuries. Options: Yes, No
* **Knee Surgery** (Field 7, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Indicate whether you have undergone any knee surgery. Options: Yes, No, Planned
* **Pain Level** (Field 8, Number, Required: False)
	+ Use a numerical value to indicate your current pain level. Higher values represent more pain.
* **Knee Pain** (Field 9, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe your knee pain in as much detail as possible.
* **Knee Function** (Field 10, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Answer whether you have any issues with knee function. Options: Yes, No
* **Knee Range** (Field 11, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Select any knee range issues you are experiencing. Options: Yes, No
* **Knee Stability** (Field 12, Number, Required: False)
	+ Use a numerical value to indicate your knee stability. Higher values represent better stability.
* **Knee Alignment** (Field 13, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Choose whether your knee alignment is satisfactory. Options: Yes, No
