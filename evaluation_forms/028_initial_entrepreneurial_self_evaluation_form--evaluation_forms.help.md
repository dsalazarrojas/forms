# Initial Entrepreneurial Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Entrepreneurial Self Evaluation Form is designed to help you assess the feasibility and potential of your business idea. It covers various aspects of your business, including its growth potential, financial projections, management structure, and market trends.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question thoroughly, considering your business idea carefully.
2. For multi-select questions (e.g., "Business Ideas"), select all applicable options that apply to your business concept.
3. For single-select questions (e.g., "Risk Tolerance"), choose the option that best describes your business idea.
4. If a field is marked as "required," please complete it to ensure accurate evaluation.
5. For optional fields, provide as much detail as possible to gain more insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (id: 1, required): Enter your unique identifier or code for this form submission.
* **Business Ideas** (id: 2, required): Check all the business ideas that apply to your concept, such as "Yes" for ideas like "Create a new product" or "Develop a new service."
* **Risk Tolerance** (id: 3, required): Choose "Yes" if your business idea involves high risk, or "No" if it's relatively low-risk.
* **Financial Resources** (id: 4, optional): Check "Yes" if you have sufficient financial resources to support your business idea.
* **Marketing Strategies** (id: 5, optional): Select "Good" if you have a well-planned marketing strategy, or "Fair" if it's still developing.
* **Product Development** (id: 6, optional): Enter the date your product or service is expected to be developed.
* **Business Model** (id: 7, optional): Briefly describe your business model, such as " subscription-based" or "advertising revenue."
* **Management Team** (id: 8, optional): Check "Yes" if you have a strong management team in place.
* **Market Size** (id: 9, optional): Choose "Yes" if your target market is large and growing.
* **Customer Base** (id: 10, optional): Select "Yes" if you have a defined customer base or a clear understanding of your target audience.
* **Revenue Streams** (id: 11, required): Check "Yes" if your business generates revenue from multiple sources.
* **Growth Potential** (id: 12, required): Choose "Yes" if your business has the potential for significant growth.
* **Management Structure** (id: 13, optional): Check "Yes" if your management structure is well-organized and effective.
* **Competition** (id: 14, optional): Select "Yes" if your business faces strong competition in the market.
* **Market Trends** (id: 15, optional): Check "Yes" if you're aware of and are adapting to current market trends.
* **Industry Niches** (id: 16, optional): Select "Yes" if your business operates within a specific industry niche.
* **Business Model Canvas** (id: 17, optional): Check "Yes" if you have a well-defined business model canvas in place.
* **Value Proposition** (id: 18, optional): Briefly describe the unique value your business offers to customers.
* **Unique Selling Points** (id: 19, optional): Check "Yes" if your business has distinctive features that set it apart from competitors.
* **Revenue Streams Details** (id: 20, optional): Provide a detailed description of your revenue streams.
* **Financial Projections** (id: 21, required): Check "Yes" if you have accurate financial projections for your business.
* **Risk Factors** (id: 22, optional): Select "Yes" if you're aware of potential risks and have plans to mitigate them.
* **Market Segment** (id: 23, optional): Check "Yes" if you've identified a specific market segment for your business.
* **Exit Strategy** (id: 24, required): Choose "Good" if you have a well-planned exit strategy, or "Fair" if it's still developing.
* **Evaluation Date** (id: 25, required): Enter the date this form is being filled out.
