# Research Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Evaluation form is designed to assess the quality and effectiveness of a research process or study. It helps evaluators provide feedback on various aspects of the research, including its purpose, methodology, findings, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the research process or study carefully to understand its purpose, methodology, and findings.
2. Rate each section of the form based on your experience and understanding of the research process.
3. Provide comments on each section to support your ratings.
4. Check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
5. Review your ratings and comments to ensure they are fair and balanced.
