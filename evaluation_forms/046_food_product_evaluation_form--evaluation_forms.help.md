# Food Product Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Product Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback from customers on a specific food product. This form collects information on the product's quality, effectiveness, and overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input in the "User Input" field.
2. Select a rating for the product (1-5) in the "Rating" field.
3. Provide any additional comments about the product in the "Comment" field.
4. Enter the name of the product being evaluated.
5. Select the scale used for the evaluation (1-5) in the "Evaluation Scale" field.
6. Enter the date of purchase for the product.
7. Enter the time of purchase for the product.
8. Enter the weight of the product.
9. Select the price range of the product (low, medium, high).
10. Choose the method of purchase (online, in person).
11. Choose the store where the product was purchased.
12. Enter the store location.
13. Enter the phone number of the store.
14. Enter your email address (if applicable).
15. Provide any additional feedback about the product in the "Feedback" field.
16. Add any notes about the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter your comments or observations about the product.
* **Rating**: Select a rating for the product on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest rating and 5 is the highest rating.
* **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the product.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Scale**: Select the scale used for the evaluation (1-5).
* **Date of Purchase**: Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Time of Purchase**: Enter the time you purchased the product.
* **Product Weight**: Enter the weight of the product.
* **Price Range**: Select the price range of the product (low, medium, high).
* **Purchase Method**: Choose how you purchased the product (online, in person).
* **Store**: Choose the store where you purchased the product.
* **Store Location**: Enter the store location.
* **Store Phone**: Enter the phone number of the store.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the product.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the evaluation.
