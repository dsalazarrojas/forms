## Relax Kids Term Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Relax Kids Term Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from parents or guardians about their child's experience with our programs. We value your input and would like to hear about your thoughts, concerns, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct answers that best reflect your feelings about your child's experience.
2. Enter any additional comments or thoughts you may have.
3. If you would like to contact us about a specific issue or concern, you can include your email and phone number in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **testimonial** (`testimonial`, `note`, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can share your thoughts about your child's experience with our programs. Please provide any comments or testimonials that you feel comfortable sharing with us.
* **session_block** (`session_block`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the blocks that best describes your child's experience. You can choose from:
	+ Block 1
	+ Block 2
	+ Block 3
	+ Block 4
* **date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the session your child attended. This will help us track your feedback over time.
* **rating** (`rating`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ratings that best describe your experience with our programs. You can choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your child's experience.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback. We will use this email to follow up on any issues or concerns you may have.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback. We will use this number to follow up on any issues or concerns you may have.
* **comments_phone** (`comments_phone`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your child's experience.
