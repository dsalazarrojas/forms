# Youth Sports Coach Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help sports organizations or programs evaluate their youth sports coaches, gathering feedback from players or parents about their experience with the coach.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by reviewing the form questions, which are divided into sections that cover different aspects of the coach's performance.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Coordinator** (`Evaluation Coordinator`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all that apply.
* **Evaluation Coordinator Name** (`Evaluation Coordinator Name`, text, **required**): Please enter the name of the evaluation coordinator.
* **Coach Name** (`Coach Name`, text, **optional**): If you know the coach's name, you can enter it. If not, it's okay to leave this blank.
* **Coaching Style** (`Coaching Style`, select_one, **optional**): Choose how the coach approaches their role.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all that apply.
* **Feedback to Coach** (`Feedback to Coach`, text, **optional**): If you have any feedback for the coach, enter it here.
* **Feedback to Coach 2** (`Feedback to Coach 2`, text, **optional**): If you have any feedback for the second coach, enter it here.
* **Feedback to Coach 3** (`Feedback to Coach 3`, text, **optional**): If you have any feedback for the third coach, enter it here.
* **Evaluation of Coach** (`Evaluation of Coach`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether you're satisfied or dissatisfied with the coach's performance.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether you're satisfied or dissatisfied overall.
* **Improvement Areas** (`Improvement Areas`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all areas where the coach could improve.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`Evaluation Comments`, text, **optional**): Add any comments or thoughts about the coach.
* **Evaluation of Coach 2** (`Evaluation of Coach 2`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether you're satisfied or dissatisfied with the second coach's performance.
* **Overall Satisfaction 2** (`Overall Satisfaction 2`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether you're satisfied or dissatisfied with the second coach overall.
* **Evaluation of Coach 3** (`Evaluation of Coach 3`, select_one, **optional**): Choose whether you're satisfied or dissatisfied with the third coach's performance.

## Tips
- Be honest in your responses, as they help us understand the coach's effectiveness.
- Please fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
- If you're unsure about any question, it's okay to ask.
