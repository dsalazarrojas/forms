# leadership_training_curriculum_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Training Curriculum Evaluation Form is designed to assess the effectiveness and quality of leadership training programs. This form helps participants evaluate and provide feedback on their experience, rating the overall and facilitator performance, and offering suggestions for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your participant name in the `Participant Name` field.
2.  Select the `Training Program` that you are evaluating.
3.  Choose the date of the evaluation.
4.  Rate the overall quality of the training program on a scale of 1-5.
5.  Provide any additional comments about your experience in the `Evaluation Comments` field.
6.  Rate the participant's performance on a scale of 1-5.
7.  Enter the name of the facilitator who led the training.
8.  Rate the facilitator's performance on a scale of 1-5.
9.  Enter the name of the training facility where the program was held.
10. Enter the name of the facilitator's supervisor (if applicable).
11. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training on a scale of 1-5.
12. Provide feedback on the training in the `Feedback` field.
13. Offer suggestions for improvement in the `Suggestions` field.
14. Select any additional feedback from the options provided in the `Participant Feedback` field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a participant in the evaluation.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, required: false): Select the name of the training program you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date you attended the training.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the overall quality of the training program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facilitator who led the training.
* **Facilitator Rating** (`facilitator_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the facilitator's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Training Facility** (`training_facility`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the training facility where the program was held.
* **Facilitator Supervisor** (`facilitator_supervisor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facilitator's supervisor (if applicable).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Provide feedback on the training program.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, required: false): Offer suggestions for improvement.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional feedback from the provided options.
