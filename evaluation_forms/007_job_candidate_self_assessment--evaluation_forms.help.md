# Job Candidate Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Candidate Self Assessment form is a self-reporting tool designed to collect information about your job candidacy. Please answer the questions accurately and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reading each field carefully and providing the requested information.
- Ensure you have any necessary permissions or approvals before answering sensitive questions.
- Enter your information accurately and thoroughly in the corresponding fields.
- Submit your form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Title** (`Job Candidate Self Assessment`, not required)
- This is the title of the form and is not a field that requires input.
- Please disregard this field and proceed to the next section.

### **Address** (`address`, required)
- Please enter your address in this field. This will help us understand your location.
- Use the format: Street Address, City, State, Zip (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, CA 12345).

### **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, required)
- Enter your contact number in this field. This can be a phone or a mobile number.
- Please use the format: 123-456-7890 (no international codes or extensions).

### **Email** (`email`, required)
- Enter your email address in this format: user@example.com.
- Please ensure the email address is correct and active.

### **Job Applied** (`job_applied`, select_one, optional)
- Select the job you have applied for (Job A, Job B, or Job C).
- This is the job for which you are applying.

### **Jobs Applied 2** (`job_applied_2`, select_multiple, optional)
- If you have applied for multiple jobs, select the ones that match your interests and qualifications.
- Multiple selections are allowed.

### **Job Applied 3** (`job_applied_3`, date, optional)
- If you have applied for a job, enter the date you applied (yyyy-mm-dd).
- Please use the format: 2022-01-01.

### **Job Applied 4** (`job_applied_4`, time, optional)
- If you have applied for a job, enter the time of application (hh:mm AM/PM).
- Please use the format: 08:00 AM.

### **Job Applied 5** (`job_applied_5`, text, optional)
- If you have any specific notes about your job application, enter them here.
- Please provide any relevant information that can help with your evaluation.

### **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
- If you have any personal notes about your application, enter them here.
- Please provide any relevant information that can help with your evaluation.
