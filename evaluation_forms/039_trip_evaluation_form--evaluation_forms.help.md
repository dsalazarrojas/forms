# Trip Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trip Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather information and feedback about a trip or project evaluation. It helps event planners or project managers to collect data about the trip's date, services provided, client details, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the trip date.
2. Choose the services that were provided during the trip.
3. Enter the client's name.
4. Select the services used.
5. Choose the dates of the trip.
6. Enter the client's phone number.
7. Enter the client's email address.
8. Select the project organization's status.
9. Enter any additional notes about the trip.
10. Enter the event planner's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Trip Services** (`trip_services`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the services that were provided during the trip, such as "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the services used during the trip.
* **Dates** (`dates`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the dates of the trip, such as "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Project Organization** (`project_organization`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the project organization's status.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the trip.
* **Event Planner** (`event_planner`, text, required: false): Enter the event planner's name.

Tips:
- Be sure to select the correct trip date and services provided.
- Make sure to enter accurate client contact information.
- Use the services and dates fields to provide detailed feedback.
- Enter any relevant notes to help improve future trips.
