# Counselor Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselor Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with a counselor, which will be used to evaluate the performance of the counselor and make informed decisions about their training, support, or potential termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Feedback**: Enter your honest and thorough feedback about your experience with the counselor. This could include comments, suggestions, or concerns.
2. **Evaluation Scale**: Choose the evaluation scale that best represents your opinion about the counselor's performance. Select all that apply.
	* Yes
	* No
3. **Rating**: If applicable, enter a rating (text) to further describe your experience.
4. **Date of Last Visit**: Enter the date of your last visit with the counselor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Feedback
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required: false): Enter your honest and thorough feedback about your experience with the counselor. This could include comments, suggestions, or concerns.

### Evaluation Scale
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the evaluation scale that best represents your opinion about the counselor's performance. Select all that apply.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter a rating (text) to further describe your experience.

### Date of Last Visit
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last visit with the counselor.
