# Spa Massage Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Spa Massage Feedback Form Template" is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about your experience at the spa. Your feedback will help the spa staff to understand what you liked and disliked about your visit, and provide valuable insights to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions on each page according to your genuine experience. You can skip any questions that are not applicable to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the service?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_1`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your overall impression of the spa service. Write as much or as little as you feel is necessary.
* **Overall experience** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_2`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your overall experience at the spa? Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What was the best part of the service?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_3`, text, optional): Describe the most impressive aspect of your visit.
* **What was the least impressive part of the service?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_4`, text, optional): If there was something that didn't quite meet your expectations, please describe it here.
* **How would you rate the therapist?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_5`, number, optional): Please rate the therapist's performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Would you recommend this service?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_6`, select_multiple, optional): Do you think others would benefit from this service? Please select all that apply.
* **Do you have any feedback for us?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_7`, text, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to share about your experience?
* **Do you have any other feedback?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_8`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to mention, please include it here.
* **How did you feel after the massage?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_9`, select_one, optional): How would you describe your emotional state after the massage? Choose one of the options: Relaxed, Stressed, Anxious, Tense, or Other.
* **Would you come back?** (`spa_massage_feedback_form_template_page_10`, select_one, optional): Would you return to this spa for another massage? Please select one of the options: True or False.
