# Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback and evaluation data from interviewers after conducting an interview with a candidate. This information will help in making informed decisions about the candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have conducted an interview with the candidate.
2. Fill out the form with the relevant information, making sure to answer all required fields.
3. Provide accurate and unbiased feedback.
4. Submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Performance** (`candidate_performance`, text, required: false): This field is for noting the candidate's performance during the interview. Please provide a brief description of their performance, including any strengths or areas for improvement.
* **Candidate Answers** (`candidate_answers`, text, required: false): This field is for recording the candidate's answers during the interview. Take notes on their responses to any questions asked.
* **Interviewer Rating** (`interviewer_rating`, number, required: false): This field is for rating the candidate's performance based on your evaluation. Please rate on a scale of 1-10.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): This field is for selecting the date the interview took place.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required: false): This field is for selecting the time the interview took place.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any additional feedback you may have about the candidate.
* **Candidate Score** (`candidate_score`, number, required: false): This field is for recording the final score you gave the candidate. Please note that this score should reflect your overall evaluation of the candidate.
