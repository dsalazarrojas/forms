# Human Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Development Assessment form is designed to gauge your current state of mind, emotions, and priorities. This information will help identify areas for improvement and potential coaching opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each question, answer based on your current feelings and experiences. Please be as honest as possible.
2. Use the scale provided for numerical questions (overall feeling, energy level, stress level, happiness level).
3. For open-ended questions (personal goal, wellness goal, coaching goal), provide a brief description or phrase that best represents your current priorities or objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What brings you to this form?** (`form_start`, text, required: false)
	+ A brief description of your motivation or reason for taking this form. This could be something like "I'm feeling overwhelmed and want to assess my well-being" or "I'm seeking coaching to improve my mental health."
* **Overall feeling** (`overall_feeling`, number, required: false)
	+ Please rate your overall feeling on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the worst and 10 being the best. Consider how you're feeling right now, and choose the number that best represents your current emotional state.
* **Energy level** (`energy_level`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate your energy level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest. Consider how much energy you have to tackle daily tasks and activities.
* **Stress level** (`stress_level`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate your stress level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest. Think about how stressed or anxious you're feeling right now.
* **Happiness level** (`happiness_level`, number, required: false)
	+ Rate your happiness level on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest. Consider how satisfied or content you are in your current situation.
* **Personal goal** (`personal_goal`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe a personal goal or objective you're currently working towards. This could be related to your relationships, career, health, or personal growth.
* **Wellness goal** (`wellness_goal`, text, required: false)
	+ Share your current wellness goals or objectives, such as improving sleep, exercise, or nutrition.
* **Coaching goal** (`coaching_goal`, text, required: false)
	+ Explain what you hope to achieve through coaching or guidance. This could be to improve specific skills, overcome challenges, or develop strategies for success.

Note: The fields marked as 'required: false' are optional and can be skipped if you feel they don't apply to your situation. However, providing answers to these fields can offer more context and insight into your current state.
