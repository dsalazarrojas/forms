# AI Speech Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from evaluators on the quality and performance of a given speech, specifically focusing on its clarity, engagement, structure, vocal technique and pacing, content quality, and overall recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the evaluator's name in the "Evaluator name" field, which is optional but allows for personal feedback.
* Enter the speech title, also optional, to help identify the speech being evaluated.
* Rate the clarity, engagement, structure, vocal technique and pacing, and content quality of the speech using the provided rating scales (1-5). Higher ratings indicate better performance.
* Provide specific feedback for improvement in the "Suggestions for improvement" field, which is optional.
* Select an overall recommendation from the options provided: "Recommend", "Recommend with reservations", or "Do not recommend".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluator name (text, optional)**: Enter the name of the evaluator who is providing feedback on the speech.
* **Speech title (text, optional)**: Enter a brief title or description of the speech being evaluated.
* **Clarity of delivery (number, 1-5)**: Rate the clarity of the speech, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Audience engagement (number, 1-5)**: Rate the engagement of the audience with the speech, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Structure and organization (number, 1-5)**: Rate the structure and organization of the speech, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Vocal technique and pacing (number, 1-5)**: Rate the vocal technique and pacing of the speaker, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Content quality and relevance (number, 1-5)**: Rate the quality and relevance of the content, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Suggestions for improvement (text, optional)**: Provide specific feedback for the speaker to improve.
* **Overall recommendation (select_one)**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your overall recommendation: "Recommend", "Recommend with reservations", or "Do not recommend".
