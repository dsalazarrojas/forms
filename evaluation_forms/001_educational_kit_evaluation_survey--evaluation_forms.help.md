# Educational Kit Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Kit Evaluation Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with educational kits. It is meant to provide valuable insights and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have an educational kit in mind, either one you have used or one you have an idea for.
2. Start by filling out your school ID, if you are affiliated with an educational institution.
3. Then, rate the usefulness, content quality, and ease of use of the educational kit.
4. Provide any feedback you have on the usefulness and content quality of the educational kit.
5. If you have any feedback on the ease of use of the educational kit, please provide it in the feedback on ease of use field.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
7. Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
8. Finally, select the date and time you used the educational kit.
9. You can also rate the date range and time range of your experience with the educational kit.
10. Select the rating scale for the content, usability, and date range.
11. Provide additional feedback about your experience with the educational kit by selecting multiple rating scale options.
12. If there is anything else you would like to say about your experience with the educational kit, please use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required/optional): Please enter your school ID, if applicable.
* **Rating Usefulness** (`rating_usefulness`, number, required/optional): Rate the usefulness of the educational kit from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Content Quality** (`rating_content_quality`, number, required/optional): Rate the content quality of the educational kit from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Ease of Use** (`rating_ease_use`, number, required/optional): Rate the ease of use of the educational kit from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback on Usefulness** (`feedback_useful`, text, required/optional): Please provide any feedback you have on the usefulness of the educational kit.
* **Feedback on Content** (`feedback_content`, text, required/optional): Please provide any feedback you have on the content quality of the educational kit.
* **Feedback on Ease of Use** (`feedback_ease_use`, text, required/optional): Please provide any feedback you have on the ease of use of the educational kit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you used the educational kit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you used the educational kit.
* **Date Range** (`rating_date_range`, select_one, required/optional): Select the date range of your experience with the educational kit.
* **Time Range** (`rating_time_range`, select_one, required/optional): Select the time range of your experience with the educational kit.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_multiple, required/optional): Rate the content quality, usability, and date range using the provided scales.
* **Rating Scale Text** (`rating_scale_text`, select_multiple, required/optional): Provide additional feedback about your experience with the educational kit.
* **Rating Scale Text 2** (`rating_scale_text_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Provide any additional feedback about your experience with the educational kit.
* **Phone Number** (this seems to be a duplicate of the `phone_number` field, please ignore)
