# Integrative Health Program Evaluation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to capture the necessary information and consent from participants in an integrative health program, allowing for the evaluation and assessment of the program. The form is meant to be a comprehensive record of the participant's involvement and consent in the program. It will be reviewed and assessed to ensure the best possible care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as you wish to be identified in the program.
2.  Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
3.  Provide the date you signed this form.
4.  Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
5.  Enter a brief description of the program you are participating in.
6.  Specify how often you will be evaluated for this program.
7.  Indicate whether you have given your consent to participate in the program.
8.  If you have given your consent, enter the date you gave it.
9.  Enter your preferred contact information for your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **participant_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you wish to be identified in the program.
*   **program_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
*   **date_signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
*   **provider_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
*   **program_details** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the program you are participating in.
*   **evaluation_frequency** (Number, Optional): Specify how often you will be evaluated for this program (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
*   **evaluation_frequency** (Number, Optional): This field is meant to be a number that indicates the frequency of evaluations. For example, 1 for weekly, 2 for biweekly, etc.
*   **consent_given** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you have given your consent to participate in the program.
*   **consent_given** (Select One, Optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent status.
*   **consent_date** (Date, Optional): If you have given your consent, enter the date you gave it.
*   **provider_contact** (Text, Optional): Enter your preferred contact information for your healthcare provider.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended to be a comprehensive or legal document. It is a tool to help evaluate and improve the Integrative Health Program.
