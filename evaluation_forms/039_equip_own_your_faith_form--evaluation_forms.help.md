# Equip Own Your Faith Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equip Own Your Faith Form is a self-assessment tool designed to help you evaluate your current faith and values. By completing this form, you'll be able to reflect on your spiritual foundation, identify areas for growth, and create a plan for personal development and spiritual growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing each section of the form, working through the pages one by one.
2. For each field, provide a brief answer or description, as instructed.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
4. Once you've completed all required fields, review your answers for completeness and accuracy.
