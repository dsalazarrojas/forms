# CEU Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us improve the quality and relevance of our Continuing Education Unit (CEU) courses. Your feedback is invaluable in shaping the future of our professional development programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the required fields**, such as Participant Name, Course Title, Instructor Name, Date of Completion, Relevance of course content to your profession, and the other fields labeled as "required."
2. **For the open-ended questions**, such as "What was your most significant takeaway from this course?" and "Suggested topics for future CEU courses," please provide as much detail as possible.
3. **Select the appropriate options** for the select_one type questions based on your honest opinion, for example, selecting "Relevant" if the course content was helpful and relevant to your profession, or "Definitely Yes" if you would recommend this course to others.
4. **Finally, review your answers to ensure all required fields are filled in and that they reflect your true opinions and experiences.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Name** (`participant_name_ceu`, text, **required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your CEU certificate or as you'd like it to appear.
### 2. **Course Title** (`course_title_ceu`, text, **required**): Enter the title of the course you just completed.
### 3. **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name_ceu`, text, **required**): Type in the name of the instructor who delivered this course.
### 4. **Date of Completion** (`completion_date_ceu`, date, **required**): Select the date on which you completed the course.
### 5. **Relevance of course content to your profession** (`content_relevance_rating`, select_one, **required**): Choose how relevant the course content was to your profession. Options include:
    * Not Relevant
    * Slightly Relevant
    * Relevant
    * Highly Relevant
### 6. **Instructor Effectiveness and Knowledge** (`instructor_effectiveness_rating`, select_one, **required**): How would you rate the instructor's effectiveness and knowledge?
    * 1 - Poor
    * 2 - Fair
    * 3 - Good
    * 4 - Very Good
    * 5 - Excellent
### 7. **Were the stated learning objectives achieved?** (`learning_objectives_met`, select_one, **required**): Select whether the objectives set forth in the course were met in your opinion.
    * All objectives met
    * Most objectives met
    * Some objectives met
    * Objectives not met
### 8. **Quality of course materials and handouts** (`course_material_quality`, select_one, **required**): How would you rate the quality of the course materials and handouts?
    * Poor
    * Fair
    * Good
    * Excellent
### 9. **Pace of the course** (`pace_of_instruction`, select_one, **required**): How would you rate the pace of the course?
    * Too Slow
    * Just Right
    * Too Fast
### 10. **What was your most significant takeaway from this course?** (`key_takeaway_ceu`, text, **required**): Please describe the most important idea or concept you took away from the course.
### 11. **Level of course difficulty** (`difficulty_level_ceu`, select_one, **required**): How difficult did you find the course? Options include:
    * Easy
    * Appropriate for level
    * Challenging
    * Overly Complex
### 12. **Would you recommend this CEU course to others?** (`recommend_course_check`, select_one, **required**): Would you recommend this course to your colleagues or peers?
    * Definitely Yes
    * Probably Yes
    * 'False'
### 13. **Suggested topics for future CEU courses** (`future_course_topics`, text, **optional**): If you could suggest topics or areas of interest for future courses, please do so here.
### 14. **I request the issuance of my CEU Certificate** (`certificate_issuance_ack`, select_one, **required**): Please confirm whether you wish to receive a CEU certificate upon completion of this form.
