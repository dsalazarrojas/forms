# Organizational Health Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Health Fellowship Application Form is used to collect information from organizations interested in applying for the fellowship. Please ensure that your responses accurately reflect your organization's goals and context to help the selection committee make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before starting your application.
- Fill out all required fields (identified as "required" in the form).
- Use clear and concise language in your responses.
- Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization details (<id: 1>)**
	+ This section is used to collect basic details about your organization.
	+ Please provide a clear and concise description of your organization.
* **Fellow-ship goals (<id: 2>)**
	+ Describe your fellow-ship goals in this section. Be specific about what you hope to achieve through the fellowship.
* **Fellow-ship context (<id: 3>)**
	+ Explain the context surrounding your fellow-ship goals. This can include relevant background information or circumstances that led to your organization's interest in the fellowship.
* **Fellow-ship experience (<id: 4>)**
	+ Share your organization's relevant experience in the area you're applying for. This can be in the form of projects, events, or initiatives related to the fellowship's focus area.
* **Fellow-ship motivation (<id: 5>)**
	+ Explain why your organization is motivated to apply for the fellowship. Please be specific about what drives your interest in this program.
* **Fellow-ship context**  **(<id: 6>)**
	+ Similar to the "Fellow-ship context" field, provide a brief description of the context that supports your fellow-ship goals.
* **Contact details (<id: 7>)**
	+ Provide your organization's contact details, including your name, email, and phone number. This will help us contact you with any questions or for further information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your responses are accurate and thorough.
- Proofread your application carefully before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
