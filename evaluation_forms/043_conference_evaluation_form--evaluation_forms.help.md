# Conference Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience at the conference. Your responses will help us improve future events and provide better experiences for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly. 
2. Use the provided labels and instructions to guide you through the form.
3. If a field is marked as "Optional", you can choose to leave it blank or provide additional information.
4. For rating fields, select one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
5. For text fields, provide as much information as possible to help us understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, Optional): This field is for you to identify yourself.
* **Session title** (`session_title`, text, Optional): Enter the title of the session you attended.
* **Overall conference experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, True): Rate your overall experience at the conference. Please select one of the following options: 
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
* **Content quality rating** (`content_quality`, select_one, True): Rate the quality and relevance of the sessions. Please select one of the following options: 
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
* **Logistics and organization rating** (`logistics_quality`, select_one, True): Rate the venue, signage, and timeliness of the conference. Please select one of the following options: 
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
* **Networking opportunities rating** (`networking_opportunities`, select_one, False): This field is optional. Rate the value of networking at the event. Please select one of the following options: 
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
* **Favorite session or speaker** (`favorite_session`, text, Optional): If you had a favorite session or speaker, please enter their name here.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional): Provide any ideas or suggestions for improving future events.
* **Likelihood to recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, select_one, True): Rate how likely you are to recommend this conference. Please select one of the following options: 
  - Very likely
  - Likely
  - Unlikely
  - Very unlikely
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about the conference.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve.
* If you have any concerns or issues, we appreciate your report so we can address them.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form. Your input is valuable to us!
