# Youth Baseball Tryout Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Baseball Tryout Evaluation Form is used to assess a player's skills and suitability for a team or program. It collects information about the player's performance in batting, fielding, throwing, and other key areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the player's personal information, including their name and height.
2. Rate the player's performance in each category (batting, fielding, throwing) with a numerical score.
3. Provide any additional comments or observations about the player's performance.
4. Determine whether the player is recommended to be on the team or program.
5. Choose whether the player's status is "yes" (recommended), "no" (not recommended), or "maybe" (needs further evaluation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name (1)**: Enter the player's name, as it appears on their registration form or ID.
* **Player Height (2)**: Enter the player's height in inches or feet and inches (e.g., 5' 8").
* **Batting (3)**: Rate the player's batting performance, using a numerical score (e.g., 1-10).
* **Fielding (4)**: Rate the player's fielding performance, using a numerical score (e.g., 1-10).
* **Throwing (5)**: Rate the player's throwing performance, using a numerical score (e.g., 1-10).
* **Comments (6)**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the player's performance.
* **Recommended For (7)**: Choose whether the player is recommended for the team or program (yes/no).
* **Tryout Status (8)**: Choose the player's status (yes/no/maybe) based on their performance.
