# Speaking Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Speaking Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback on a speaker's performance in a presentation, meeting, or similar communication scenario.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the evaluation criteria: Before completing this form, take a few minutes to review the evaluation criteria and ensure you have a clear understanding of what each section assesses.
2. Answer each question based on your observation: Answer each question based on your observation of the speaker's performance, rather than relying on personal opinions or biases.
3. Use the provided options: Select the most accurate option for each question, or use the text field to provide more detailed comments.
4. Complete all required fields: Ensure you fill out all required fields, but feel free to provide additional comments or feedback where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Score**: (`overall_score`, number, required: false): Rate the speaker's overall performance on a scale of 0-10.
* **Communication Skills**: (`communication_skills`, select_one, required: false): How well did the speaker communicate their ideas? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Fluency**: (`fluency`, select_multiple, required: false): How clear and fluent was the speaker? Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Pronunciation**: (`pronunciation`, select_one, required: false): How clear was the speaker's pronunciation? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Body Language**: (`body_language`, select_multiple, required: false): How effective was the speaker's body language? Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Speaker Self Reflection**: (`speaker_self_reflection`, text, required: false): This field is for the speaker to provide a self-assessment of their performance.
* **Trainer Self Reflection**: (`trainer_self_reflection`, text, required: false): This field is for the trainer to provide a self-assessment of the speaker's performance.
* **Overall Comments**: (`overall_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments on the speaker's performance.
* **Communication Comments**: (`communication_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments about the speaker's communication.
