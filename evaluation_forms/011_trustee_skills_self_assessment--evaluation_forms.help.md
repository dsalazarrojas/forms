# trustee_skills_self_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "trustee_skills_self_assessment" form is designed to help a trustee evaluate their own skills and competencies in relation to their role on a board of directors. This form provides a comprehensive list of key skills and competencies, allowing the trustee to reflect on their own strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each section based on your own self-assessment. You can refer to your own experience, knowledge, and skills as a trustee.
2. Be honest and accurate in your self-assessment. This form is for your personal use, so don't worry about providing "perfect" answers.
3. Take your time to answer each section thoroughly. Reflect on your experiences and think about how you can apply your skills and competencies to your role on the board.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **board_development** (Page 2): This section assesses your ability to develop and maintain a strong and effective board culture. Please select all the skills that you feel are relevant to your experience and practice as a trustee.
* **leadership** (Page 3): This section evaluates your leadership skills and experience. Think about your ability to lead and motivate others, make decisions, and drive results.
* **communication** (Page 4): This section assesses your ability to communicate effectively with others, both within and outside the board. Think about your verbal and written communication skills, including your ability to present information and negotiate.
* **decision_making** (Page 5): This section evaluates your ability to make informed and effective decisions. Consider your experience with data analysis, problem-solving, and risk management.
* **conflict_resolution** (Page 6): This section assesses your ability to resolve conflicts and manage disagreements within the board. Think about your experience with mediation, arbitration, and negotiation.
* **problem_solving** (Page 7): This section evaluates your ability to analyze problems and develop effective solutions. Consider your experience with critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration.
* **strategic_planning** (Page 8): This section assesses your ability to develop and implement strategic plans. Think about your experience with goal-setting, resource allocation, and risk management.
* **risk_management** (Page 9): This section evaluates your ability to identify and manage risks. Consider your experience with risk assessment, mitigation, and monitoring.
* **adaptability** (Page 10): This section assesses your ability to adapt to changing situations and priorities. Think about your experience with flexibility, resilience, and change management.
* **innovation** (Page 11): This section evaluates your ability to think creatively and develop new ideas. Consider your experience with innovation, entrepreneurship, and innovation management.
* **evaluation** (Page 12): This section assesses your ability to evaluate progress and outcomes. Think about your experience with metrics, benchmarks, and performance measurement.
* **reporting** (Page 13): This section evaluates your ability to report on progress and results. Consider your experience with reporting, communication, and presentation.
* **financial_management** (Page 14): This section assesses your ability to manage finances effectively. Think about your experience with budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
* **legal_compliance** (Page 15): This section evaluates your ability to ensure legal compliance. Consider your experience with regulatory requirements, risk management, and governance.
* **stakeholder_engagement** (Page 16): This section assesses your ability to engage with stakeholders effectively. Think about your experience with stakeholder analysis, communication, and relationship-building.
* **collaboration** (Page 17): This section evaluates your ability to collaborate with others. Consider your experience with team-building, partnership, and networking.
* **emotional_intelligence** (Page 18): This section assesses your ability to understand and manage emotions. Think about your experience with empathy, self-awareness, and social skills.
* **diversity_inclusion** (Page 19): This section evaluates your ability to promote diversity and inclusion. Consider your experience with diversity awareness, equity, and inclusion.
* **digital_literacy** (Page 20): This section assesses your ability to effectively use digital technologies. Think about your experience with digital tools, data analysis, and online communication.
* **technical_literacy** (Page 21): This section evaluates your ability to understand and apply technical concepts. Consider your experience with technical analysis, problem-solving, and data-driven decision-making.
* **leadership_skills** (Page 22): This section assesses your ability to lead and motivate others. Think about your experience with leadership development, coaching, and mentoring.
* **self_awareness** (Page 23): This section evaluates your ability to understand yourself and your impact. Consider your experience with self-reflection, self-awareness, and personal growth.
* **board_dynamics** (Page 24): This section assesses your ability to understand and navigate board dynamics. Think about your experience with board operations, governance, and leadership.
* **final_comments** (Page 25): This section is an opportunity for you to add any additional comments or thoughts about your skills and competencies as a trustee.

Note: This form is for your personal use and should not be shared with anyone without the permission of the board.
