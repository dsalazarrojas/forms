# Game Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Observation Form is used to gather feedback on an athlete's performance after a game or competition. It helps identify areas of improvement and provides recommendations for development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Athlete** who was observed during the game.
2. Enter the **Game Observation Date** when the observation took place.
3. Fill out the **Game Observation Form Comments** with any relevant observations or feedback.
4. Enter a **Game Observation Score** out of 10 to provide a general assessment of the athlete's performance.
5. Enter the **Game Observation Duration** in minutes to provide context for the score.
6. Enter the **Game Observation Rating** out of 10 to provide a more detailed assessment of the athlete's performance.
7. Fill out the **Game Observation Notes** with any additional comments or observations.
8. Enter the **Game Observation Next Steps** for any recommended actions or activities for the athlete to improve.
9. Enter the **Game Observation Recommendations** for any specific skills or areas to focus on.
10. Finally, enter any **Game Observation Additional Comments** with any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete**: Select the athlete who was observed during the game. This field is used to identify the athlete being assessed.
* **Game Observation Date**: Enter the date and time when the observation took place. This field provides context for the assessment.
* **Game Observation Form Comments**: Fill out this field with any relevant observations or feedback on the athlete's performance. This field is used to provide detailed comments or notes.
* **Game Observation Score**: Enter a score out of 10 to provide a general assessment of the athlete's performance. A higher score indicates better performance.
* **Game Observation Duration**: Enter the length of the game or competition in minutes. This provides context for the score.
* **Game Observation Rating**: Enter a score out of 10 to provide a more detailed assessment of the athlete's performance. This field is used to provide a more nuanced evaluation.
* **Game Observation Notes**: Fill out this field with any additional comments or observations. This field is used to provide more detailed information.
* **Game Observation Next Steps**: Enter any recommended actions or activities for the athlete to improve. This field is used to provide guidance on next steps.
* **Game Observation Recommendations**: Enter any specific skills or areas to focus on. This field is used to provide targeted recommendations.
* **Game Observation Additional Comments**: Enter any other relevant information or comments. This field is used to provide additional context.
* **Position**: Select the position played by the athlete during the game. This field is used to provide context for the assessment.
* **Forward/Backward**: Select any relevant positions or roles played by the athlete during the game. This field is used to provide more detailed information.
* **Game Observation Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes. This field is not required but can be used to provide more information.

Note: Please fill out the form accurately and to the best of your ability. The information provided will be used to support the athlete's development and will not be shared publicly.
