# Athlete Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about an athlete's performance, skills, and overall evaluation. It is meant to be filled out by a coach, trainer, or evaluator to provide a comprehensive assessment of an athlete's abilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   * Athlete Full Name
   * Team or Division
   * Evaluator Name
   * Evaluation Period (e.g., Pre-season, Mid-season, Final Review)
   * Speed and Agility (select a score from 1-5)
   * Strength and Power (select a score from 1-5)
   * Stamina and Endurance (select a score from 1-5)
   * Flexibility and Mobility (select a score from 1-5)
   * Technical Execution of Skills (select a score from 1-5)
   * Tactical Awareness (select a score from 1-5)
   * Decision Making Under Pressure (select a score from 1-5)
   * Discipline and Focus (select a score from 1-5)
   * Teamwork and Communication (select a score from 1-5)
   * Response to Coaching and Feedback (select a score from 1-5)
   * Punctuality and Attendance (select a score from 1-5)
   * Overall Performance Rating (select a final score from 1-5)
   * Key Strengths (describe the athlete's strengths)
   * Recommended Areas for Development (provide specific goals for improvement)
   * Date of Evaluation (select a date)

2. Select scores for each category using the scale provided:
   * 1: Below Average
   * 2: Average
   * 3: Good
   * 4: Very Good
   * 5: Exceptional

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the athlete's full name.
* **Team or Division** (`division`, `text`, required): Enter the team or division the athlete belongs to.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Period** (`period`, `text`, required): Enter the evaluation period (e.g., Pre-season, Mid-season, Final Review).
* **Speed and Agility** (`speed_agility`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's speed and agility from 1-5.
* **Strength and Power** (`strength_power`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's strength and power from 1-5.
* **Stamina and Endurance** (`stamina_endurance`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's stamina and endurance from 1-5.
* **Flexibility and Mobility** (`flexibility_mobility`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's flexibility and mobility from 1-5.
* **Technical Execution of Skills** (`technical_execution`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's technical execution of skills from 1-5.
* **Tactical Awareness** (`tactical_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's tactical awareness from 1-5.
* **Decision Making Under Pressure** (`decision_making_pressure`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's decision making under pressure from 1-5.
* **Discipline and Focus** (`discipline_focus`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's discipline and focus from 1-5.
* **Teamwork and Communication** (`teamwork_communication`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's teamwork and communication from 1-5.
* **Response to Coaching and Feedback** (`coachability_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's response to coaching and feedback from 1-5.
* **Punctuality and Attendance** (`punctuality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select a score for the athlete's punctuality and attendance from 1-5.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select a final score for the athlete's overall performance from 1-5.
* **Key Strengths** (`key_strengths`, `text`, required): Describe the athlete's key strengths.
* **Recommended Areas for Development** (`development_areas`, `text`, required): Provide specific goals for improvement.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the evaluation.

## Tips

* Make sure to be objective and fair when evaluating the athlete's performance.
* Use the scores provided to give a balanced assessment of the athlete's abilities.
* Provide specific examples and reasons for the scores to help the athlete understand their strengths and weaknesses.
* Use the "Key Strengths" and "Recommended Areas for Development" sections to provide feedback that is actionable and meaningful for the athlete.
