# Speaker Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees or viewers about a speaker's performance. The form helps to evaluate the speaker's overall presentation quality, communication style, relevance, organization, and helpfulness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
2. Select one answer for each field, except for the "Helpfulness" field which allows multiple selections.
3. Make sure to provide specific examples or details to support your answers, especially for the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Presentation Quality** (`overall_presentation_quality`, text, required): Rate the speaker's overall presentation quality, describing whether it was well-organized, clear, and effective.
* **Speaker's Communication Style** (`speaker_communication_style`, select_one, required): Rate the speaker's communication style, whether it was clear, concise, engaging, confusing, or boring.
* **Relevance** (`relevance`, number, required): Rate the relevance of the speaker's content to the topic.
* **Organization** (`organization`, number, required): Rate the speaker's organization and structure of their presentation.
* **Helpfulness** (`helpfulness`, select_multiple, required): Rate how helpful you found the speaker's presentation in terms of providing useful information or insights.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the speaker's performance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further feedback or to share your feedback with the speaker.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective in your feedback.
* Try to provide specific examples or details to support your answers.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [your contact information].
