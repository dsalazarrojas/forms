# K 12 Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K 12 Parent Survey is a tool designed to collect information and feedback from parents of students in the K-12 education system. This form aims to gather your thoughts, opinions, and experiences regarding your child's educational journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Review each page carefully and fill in the required information.
3. Make sure to read each field's explanation to understand its purpose.
4. Submit your completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info (Optional)
**General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, Optional):
This field is for you to provide any general information about yourself and your child.

### Parent Feedback (Optional)
**Parent Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, `text`, Optional):
Please share your thoughts, opinions, or concerns about your child's education.

### Classroom Info (Optional)
**Classroom Info** (`classroom_info`, `text`, Optional):
This field is for you to provide information about your child's classroom experience.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
- If you need help or have questions, contact the school administration.
- You can submit multiple times, but please avoid duplicate responses.
