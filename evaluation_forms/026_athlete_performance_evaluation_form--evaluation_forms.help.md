# Athlete Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of an athlete, assessing their physical condition, technical skills, and behavioral attributes. The goal is to provide a comprehensive overview of the athlete's abilities and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the identification section, which includes the athlete's name, sport, and evaluator's name.
2. Evaluate the athlete's physical condition by selecting a rating for speed and agility, power and strength, endurance and stamina, and flexibility and range of motion.
3. Assess the athlete's technical skills by selecting a rating for technical accuracy of execution, tactical application during competition, and performance consistency.
4. Evaluate the athlete's behavioral attributes by selecting a rating for coachability and receptiveness to feedback, leadership and positive influence, and mental toughness and resilience.
5. Provide a summary of the athlete's performance and highlight their strengths, key areas for improvement, and recommended training focus for the next period.
6. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the athlete being evaluated.
* **Primary Sport** (`athlete_sport`, text, required): Select the primary sport or activity the athlete competes in.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Physical Condition Assessment** (`physical_condition_header`, note, required): This section assesses the athlete's physical condition. Please provide a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is exceptional.
* **Speed and Agility** (`speed_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's speed and agility on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Power and Strength** (`power_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's power and strength on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Endurance and Stamina** (`endurance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's endurance and stamina on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Flexibility and Range of Motion** (`flexibility_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's flexibility and range of motion on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Technical Accuracy of Execution** (`technical_accuracy`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's technical accuracy of execution on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Tactical Application during Competition** (`tactical_application`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's tactical application during competition on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Performance Consistency** (`skill_consistency`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's performance consistency on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Coachability and Receptiveness to Feedback** (`coachability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's coachability and receptiveness to feedback on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Leadership and Positive Influence** (`leadership_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's leadership and positive influence on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Mental Toughness and Resilience** (`mental_toughness`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's mental toughness and resilience on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's overall performance on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Noteworthy Strengths** (`strengths_text`, text, required): Highlight the athlete's noteworthy strengths.
* **Key Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Identify specific skills or attributes the athlete needs to improve.
* **Recommended Training Focus for Next Period** (`recommended_focus`, text, required): Suggest a recommended training focus for the next period.
* **Evaluator Electronic Signature** (`evaluator_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately.
