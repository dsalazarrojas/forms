# online_wellness_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's online wellness evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title.
2. Provide your medical history.
3. List any medications you are currently taking.
4. Describe your lifestyle habits.
5. State your wellness goals.
6. Express any concerns or preferences.
7. Make selections as required for the select_one fields.
8. Select multiple options for the select_multiple fields.
9. Enter your date and time as required.
10. Add any additional notes.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Make further selections as required for the select_one fields.
14. Select multiple options for the select_multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title that describes you.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a description of your medical history.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you currently have.
- **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Lifestyle Habits** (`lifestyle_habits`, text, optional): Describe your daily habits and routines.
- **Wellness Goals** (`wellness_goals`, text, optional): State your goals for achieving wellness.
- **Concerns/Preferences** (`concerns_preferences`, text, optional): Express any concerns or preferences you have.
- **Select One 1** (`select_one_1`, select_one, optional): Make a single selection from the available options.
- **Select Multiple 1** (`select_multiple_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the available choices.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your date as required.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter your time as required.
