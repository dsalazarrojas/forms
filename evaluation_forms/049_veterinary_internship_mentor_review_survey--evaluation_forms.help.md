# Veterinary Internship Mentor Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from veterinary internship mentors about the performance and qualities of their mentees. The form asks about the mentee's technical skills, communication, problem-solving abilities, and overall professionalism.

## How To Complete This Form

Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Read the introduction and review the questions carefully.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately, providing feedback about each of your mentees.
3. Choose the options that best fit your experiences with each mentee.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the survey and its purpose. Take a moment to read through and understand the questions before beginning.
* **Internship Details**: This section asks you to provide a brief description of the internship your mentee completed, including the duration and any notable events or challenges.
* **Mentor Feedback**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the mentee's performance. Choose "Yes" if you were satisfied with their work, and "No" if you had concerns or issues.
* **Communication Skills**: For this section, please select all the skills you believe the mentee demonstrated strong communication skills, such as "Yes" for clear written and verbal communication, "Yes" for active listening, and "Yes" for effective use of technology.
* **Time Management**: This question asks you to rate your mentee's ability to manage their time effectively during the internship. Choose "Yes" if they demonstrated good time management, and "No" if you had concerns.
* **Problem-Solving**: Select all the skills you believe the mentee showed in terms of problem-solving, such as "Yes" for critical thinking, "Yes" for analytical skills, and "No" for creative skills.
* **Professionalism**: This question asks you to rate your mentee's overall professionalism during the internship. Choose "Yes" if they demonstrated a professional attitude, and "No" if you had concerns.
* **Mentor Support**: For this section, please select all the ways in which you provided support to your mentee, such as "Yes" for regular check-ins, "Yes" for timely feedback, and "No" for opportunities to take on more responsibilities.
* **Comments**: This is an optional section for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your mentee's performance. Use this space to elaborate on your answers or provide more specific examples.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it to the survey administrators.
