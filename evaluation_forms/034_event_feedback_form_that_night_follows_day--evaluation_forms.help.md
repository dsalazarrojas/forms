# event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "event_feedback_form_that_night_follows_day" form is a tool to gather feedback from participants after an event has taken place. This feedback is crucial in helping us understand what went well and what didn't during the event, and will inform our planning and organization for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Make sure you're filling the form for the correct event and date.
2.  Fill in the event name, which is the title of the event that took place the night before.
3.  Write a brief description of your experience with the event. This can be positive or negative feedback.
4.  Rate the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Bad" and 5 is "Excellent".
5.  Provide more specific feedback about why you rated the event as you did.
6.  Enter your name for the form submission. This is your identifier for the form.
7.  Enter a working email address for contact purposes.
8.  Finally, rate your experience with the participant scale of 1-5, and provide more specific feedback with the comments section.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What was your experience** (that_night_follows_day_event, Text, Required: False): This field is where you should write a brief description of your experience with the event. Be as specific as possible with what you liked or disliked about the event.
*   **Event** (Event, Text, Required: True): Here, enter the title of the event that took place the night before.
*   **Participant Name** (participant_name, Text, Required: False): This is where you enter your name for the form submission. This will help us understand who is providing the feedback.
*   **Rating Scale 1-5** (rating_scale, Number, Required: True): Rate your experience with the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Bad" and 5 is "Excellent".
*   **Rating Scale Point 1** (rating_scale_point_1, Number, Required: False): This field is not used in the current form design.
*   **Rating Scale Point 2** (rating_scale_point_2, Number, Required: False): This field is not used in the current form design.
*   **Comments** (comments, Text, Required: False): Enter more specific feedback about your experience with the event, if you have more to add.
*   **Email** (Email, Email, Required: False): Enter a working email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required: False): This field is not used in the current form design.
*   **Date** (Event Date, Date, Required: False): Enter the date of the event that took place the night before.
*   **Time** (Event Time, Time, Required: False): Enter the time of the event that took place the night before.
*   **Participant Rating** (Participant Rating, Number, Required: True): This is where you rate your experience as a participant. Use the 1-5 scale.
*   **Participant Comment** (Participant Comment, Text, Required: False): Enter more specific feedback about your experience as a participant.
