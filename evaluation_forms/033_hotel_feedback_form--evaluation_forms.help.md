# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their stay at the hotel. It's a quick and easy way for customers to share their thoughts and suggestions about their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the hotel you're providing feedback about.
2. Choose an overall rating for your stay.
3. Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
4. Answer questions about specific aspects of your stay, such as service quality, room cleanliness, and so on.
5. Enter your email and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up).
6. Finally, tell us if you would recommend this hotel to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`): Enter the name of the hotel you're providing feedback about.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`): Choose an overall rating for your stay, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comment** (`comment`): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about your stay. This is an optional field, feel free to use it to provide more information.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`): Rate the quality of service you experienced during your stay.
* **Room Cleanliness** (`room_cleanliness`): Rate the cleanliness of your room.
* **Check-in Time** (`check_in_time`): If you have any comments about your check-in time, please add them here. This field is not meant for actual check-in time, but rather for comments about the check-in process.
* **Staff Friendly** (`staff_friendly`): Rate how friendly and helpful the hotel staff were during your stay.
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`): Rate the quality of food and drinks served at the hotel.
* **Room Amenities** (`room_amenities`): Rate the quality of room amenities, such as towels, bedding, and so on.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`): Rate how you feel about the value you received for the money you paid.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`): Rate your overall experience at the hotel.
* **Email** (`email`): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`): Enter the website of the hotel or the website where you originally booked your stay.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about your stay.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`): Tell us if you would recommend this hotel to others.
