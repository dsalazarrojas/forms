# junior_member_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for evaluating the performance of junior members, providing a structured way to gather feedback and ratings on their progress. It helps managers and evaluators assess a junior member's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the junior member evaluation form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Performance Rating** field with a number between 0 and 10, reflecting your assessment of the junior member's performance.
2. Provide **Comments** about the junior member's strengths and areas for improvement.
3. Choose the **Performance Scale** (Excellent, Good, or Fair) that best represents your assessment of the junior member's performance.
4. If applicable, specify the **Development Goals** that the junior member has achieved or is working towards.
5. Select the **Evaluation Date** and **Evaluation Time** of the evaluation.
6. Enter the **Evaluation Form ID**, which is a unique identifier for the evaluation.
7. Select the **Submitted By** field with the name of the person who conducted the evaluation (Jimmy, Joe, or Jane).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Rating (required)**: This field requires a numerical rating of the junior member's performance, with higher numbers indicating better performance.
* **Comments (required)**: Provide a brief description of the junior member's strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Performance Scale (required)**: Choose one of the three options (Excellent, Good, or Fair) that best represents your assessment of the junior member's performance.
* **Development Goals (optional)**: If relevant, provide information on the junior member's development goals, such as training or projects they are working on.
* **Evaluation Date (required)**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Evaluation Time (required)**: Enter the time of the evaluation in the format HH:MM (12:00 to 23:59).
* **Evaluation Form ID (required)**: Enter a unique identifier for the evaluation, which will be used for tracking purposes.
* **Submitted By (required)**: Select the name of the person who conducted the evaluation (Jimmy, Joe, or Jane).
