# Employee Job Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Job Performance Evaluation form is a tool used to assess an employee's performance in their job role. It helps managers gather relevant information about their team members, including their job areas, required skills, overall performance, and potential areas for improvement. This form is essential for evaluating employee growth and development and making informed decisions about employee promotions, training, and other development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Select Job Functional Areas

*   **job_functional_areas**: Select one or more areas where the employee works. This helps the manager understand the employee's work scope and responsibilities. Choose from the following options:
    *   Accounting and Finance
    *   Customer Support
    *   Marketing
    *   Product Management
    *   Sales
    *   Software Development

### 2. Describe Skills Required for Success

*   **skills_required_for_success**: Describe the skills and abilities the employee needs to excel in their role. For example, if an employee in a software development role requires proficiency in coding languages, you can mention that here.

### 3. Rate Overall Performance

*   **overall_performance**: Evaluate the employee's overall performance in their job role. Choose from the following options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Very Good
    *   Good
    *   Bad
    *   Poor

### 4. Provide Feedback and Recommendation

*   **feedback_and_recommendation**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about the employee's performance. What they're doing well, what they need to work on, and what they can improve on.

### 5. Select Areas for Improvement

*   **areas_for_improvement**: Identify areas where the employee can improve. Choose from the following options:
    *   Time Management
    *   Communication
    *   Team Management
    *   Problem Solving
    *   Adaptability

### 6. Enter Date of Evaluation

*   **date**: Enter the date this evaluation is being conducted. This helps track evaluation history and ensure that evaluations are done on a consistent schedule.

### 7. Rate Performance

*   **performance_rating**: Provide a numerical rating from 0 to 5 to represent the employee's performance. Use this field to provide a quick snapshot of their performance level.

### 8. Add Comments

*   **comments**: Add any additional comments about the employee's performance. Use this field to elaborate on your thoughts and suggestions.

### 9. Enter Email Address

*   **email**: Enter the employee's email address. This will help the employee receive the evaluation and allow them to easily contact you with questions or concerns.

### 10. Enter Phone Number

*   **phone_number**: Enter the employee's phone number. This will allow the employee to easily contact you with questions or concerns.

## Tips

*   Always fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Use the correct format for each field.
*   Ensure that the employee is aware of the evaluation and its contents.
*   Save the form after completion.
*   Notify the employee's manager of the submission.
*   Use this form for the intended purpose only.
*   If unsure, contact your HR representative.
