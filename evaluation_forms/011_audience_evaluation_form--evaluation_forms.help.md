# Audience Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Audience Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback from participants after an event. It helps us understand the event's impact and quality, allowing us to refine and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Name of Event" field with the name of the event you attended.
2. Enter the date of the event in the "Date of Event" field.
3. If applicable, provide the name of the speaker or performer in the "Speaker or Performer Name" field.
4. Answer the questions in the sections "Overall Satisfaction", "Was the content relevant to you?", "How engaging was the session?", and "Audio and Visual Quality" as accurately as possible.
5. If you have the time, provide more detailed feedback in the "Detailed Feedback" section.
6. If you want to suggest specific improvements for future events, type them in the "How can we improve this event in the future?" field.
7. Lastly, let us know if you would recommend this event to others in the "Would you recommend this event to others?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Information (Section 1)
• **Name of Event**: Enter the name of the event you attended.
• **Date of Event**: Enter the date the event took place.

### Performance Evaluation (Section 2)
• **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, choosing from "Excellent", "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
• **Was the content relevant to you?**: Rate the relevance of the content from "Extremely Relevant", "Somewhat Relevant", "Not Very Relevant", "Not Relevant at All".
• **How engaging was the session?**: Rate the engagement level of the session from "5 - Very Engaging", "4 - Engaging", "3 - Average", "2 - Unengaging", "1 - Very Boring".
• **Audio and Visual Quality**: Rate the audio and visual quality from "Perfect", "Acceptable", "Needs Improvement".

### Feedback
• **What was the most useful part of the event?**: Describe the part that you found most useful or interesting.
• **What was the least useful part?**: Describe the part that you found least useful or interesting.
• **How can we improve this event in the future?**: Suggest specific improvements or changes you think we should make.
• **What other topics would you be interested in?**: Suggest other topics you would like to see at future events.
• **Would you recommend this event to others?**: Let us know if you would recommend this event to others, choosing from "Yes, definitely", "Maybe", or "No".

### Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "How can we improve this event in the future?" field.
* If you have any other comments or ideas, please use the "Detailed Feedback" section.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us!
