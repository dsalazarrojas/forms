## Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from trainees and trainers to evaluate the quality of a training session. It helps in identifying areas of improvement and ensures that the training is delivered effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Trainee Name field with the name of the trainee.
* Choose the correct options for Evaluation Scale, Assigned Tool, and Training Session.
* Provide your rating on the Rating Scale.
* Enter your feedback in the Feedback field.
* Make sure to select the correct Assigned Tool that was used during the training.
* Once all fields are filled, click the Submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainee Name (`trainee_name`):** Enter the name of the trainee who attended the training session.
* **Rating Scale (`rating_scale`):** Rate the training session using the provided scale (Yes/No).
* **Feedback (`feedback`):** Provide any comments or suggestions about the training session.
* **Evaluation Scale (`evaluation_scale`):** Indicate whether the training session met the required standards (Yes/No).
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`):** Select the tool that was assigned to the trainee during the training session (Assigned Tool 1, 2, or 3).
* **Training Session (`training_session`):** Confirm whether the trainee attended the training session (Yes/No).
* **Submit (submit):** Click this button to send your feedback after filling all other fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill all required fields.
* Use the correct options for Evaluation Scale, Assigned Tool, and Training Session.
* Keep the feedback fields concise and clear.
* Use the Rating Scale carefully to give accurate feedback.
* If you need help, contact the training administrator.
