# Beverage Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the knowledge and skills of an employee in the beverage industry, specifically in the areas of wine, spirits, and cocktails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section, including your full name and employee ID if applicable.
2. Answer the questions related to wine knowledge, including:
	* Major wine-producing regions
	* Names of five red wine grape varieties
	* Principles of wine and food pairing
3. Show your knowledge of spirits, including:
	* Definition of major spirit categories
	* Names and descriptions of three types of whiskey
	* Differences between tequila and mezcal
4. Demonstrate your knowledge of cocktails, including:
	* Names and preparation methods of five classic cocktails
	* Common bartending techniques
	* Ability to prepare a standard cocktail on request
5. Show your understanding of beer knowledge, including:
	* Major beer styles and characteristics
	* Brief explanation of the beer brewing process
	* Proper serving temperature for different beverages
6. Answer questions related to service and professional practices, including:
	* Handling allergen information
	* Responsible beverage service
	* Checking for proper identification
7. Rate your overall assessment and provide any recommended areas for improvement or training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Your full name, as displayed on your company ID or badge.
* **Department or Position** (`department`, select_one, required): The department or position where you work, such as Bar staff, Server, Bartender, etc.
* **Years of Service Experience** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): The number of years you have spent in the beverage industry, including:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months - 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ Over 10 years
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): The date on which this assessment is being conducted.
* **Major Wine-Producing Regions** (`wine_regions`, text, required): Name three major wine-producing regions.
* **Five Red Wine Grape Varieties** (`grape_types`, text, required): List five red wine grape varieties.
* **Wine and Food Pairing Principles** (`wine_pairing`, text, required): Describe the principles of pairing wine with food.
* **Definition of Major Spirit Categories** (`spirit_classifications`, text, required): Define the six major spirit categories.
* **Three Types of Whiskey** (`whiskey_types`, text, required): Name three types of whiskey and describe each.
* **Differences Between Tequila and Mezcal** (`tequila_classification`, text, required): Explain the difference between tequila and mezcal.
* **Classic Cocktails** (`classic_cocktails`, text, required): Name and describe five classic cocktails, including ingredients and preparation methods.
* **Common Bartending Techniques** (`cocktail_techniques`, text, required): Explain common bartending techniques, such as shake, stir, muddle, strain, etc.
* **Prepare a Standard Cocktail on Request** (`cocktail_demo`, select_one, required): Indicate your ability to prepare a standard cocktail on request, including:
	+ Yes, confidently
	+ Yes, with minor guidance
	+ Somewhat, needs practice
	+ No, requires training
* **Proper Serving Temperature for Different Beverages** (`proper_serving`, text, required): Specify the proper serving temperature for different beverages.
* **Handling Allergen Information** (`allergen_awareness`, text, required): Describe how you handle allergen information for beverages.
* **Responsible Beverage Service** (`responsible_service`, text, required): Describe responsible beverage service practices, including checking identification and refusing service when necessary.
* **Recommended Areas for Improvement or Training** (`training_recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommended areas for improvement or training.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_assessment`, select_one, required): Rate your overall assessment, including:
	+ Excellent - exceeds requirements
	+ Good - meets requirements
	+ Satisfactory - adequate knowledge
	+ Needs improvement - requires training
	+ Unsatisfactory - significant gaps
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): The name of the person conducting the assessment.
