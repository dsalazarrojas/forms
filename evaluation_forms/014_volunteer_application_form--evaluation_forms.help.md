# Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers about their personal details, availability, and motivations for volunteering. This information will be used to determine the best match for volunteer opportunities within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required fields with your personal and professional details. You can leave optional fields blank if not applicable. Make sure to provide your correct email address to ensure that you receive any relevant communications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`volunteer_form_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`volunteer_form_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`volunteer_form_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`volunteer_form_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number that you'd like to provide, please enter it here.
* **Organization** (`volunteer_form_organization`, text, optional): If you are already associated with an organization, please enter the name of that organization.
* **Position** (`volunteer_form_position`, text, optional): If you have a specific position or role within your organization, please enter it here.
* **Available Hours** (`volunteer_form_available_hours`, number, optional): If you have specific hours that you are available to volunteer, please enter them here.
* **Why do you want to volunteer?** (`volunteer_form_why_do_you_want_to_volunteer`, text, optional): Please describe your motivations for volunteering with us.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can contact you and match you with the best volunteer opportunities.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
