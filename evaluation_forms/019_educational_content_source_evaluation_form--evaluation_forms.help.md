# Educational Content Source Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the quality, accuracy, and reliability of educational content sources. It is designed to gather information that can inform decisions about the adoption or use of these sources in educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of educational content source you are evaluating.
2. Enter the name of the source.
3. Provide a brief evaluation of the content's quality, accuracy, and reliability using two numerical scales.
4. Make a recommendation for the source, selecting from the provided options.
5. Make a second recommendation, selecting from the provided options.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the source.
7. Enter the date of creation and publication (if available).
8. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
9. Record the time of evaluation.
10. Identify the educational level of the content (e.g., elementary, high school, university).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Content Source Evaluation Form** (`educational_content_source_evaluation`): This is the title of the form.
* **Source Type** (`source_type`): Please select the type of educational content you are evaluating (e.g., textbook, online resource, article).
* **Source Name** (`source_name`): Enter the name of the source (e.g., textbook title, website URL, article title).
* **Evaluation Scale 1** (`evaluation_scale_1`): Rate the content's quality on a scale from 1-5.
* **Evaluation Scale 2** (`evaluation_scale_2`): Rate the content's reliability on a scale from 1-5.
* **Recommendation 1** (`recommendation_1`): Suggest a use or recommendation for the content (e.g., recommended for high school students, suitable for university-level courses).
* **Recommendation 2** (`recommendation_2`): Suggest an alternative or additional use for the content (e.g., for advanced students, for specific subjects).
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the source.
* **Date of Creation** (`date_of_creation`): Enter the date the content was created (if available).
* **Date of Publication** (`date_of_publication`): Enter the date the content was published (if available).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Time of Evaluation** (`time_of_evaluation`): Record the time you completed the evaluation.
* **Educational Level** (`educational_level`): Identify the level of education that the content is suitable for (e.g., elementary, high school, university).
