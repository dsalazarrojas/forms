# phone_interaction_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phone_interaction_assessment_form is used to gather feedback and assessment information about phone interactions between customers and customer service representatives (CSRs). It helps customer service teams evaluate the effectiveness of CSRs and identify areas for improvement in phone communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant section that corresponds to the phone interaction you are assessing.
2. Fill in the required fields with the information related to the interaction.
3. Provide feedback and assessment as requested in the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Service Representative** (`customer_service_representative`):
	* Enter the name of the customer service representative who handled the phone interaction.
* **Rating** (`rating`):
	* Rate the quality of the interaction with the customer service representative. This field is not explicitly explained in the YAML, so assume a scale or options (e.g., 1-5, excellent/good/neutral/poor/very poor).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`):
	* Provide any additional feedback about the interaction.
* **Was the CSR helpful?** (`was_the_csr_helpful`):
	* Check if the customer service representative was helpful during the interaction.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`):
	* Select if the issue was resolved or not.
* **Follow-up needed?** (`follow_up_needed`):
	* Check if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up reason** (`follow_up_reason`):
	* If follow-up is needed, enter the reason why.
* **Follow-up date** (`follow_up_date`):
	* Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up time** (`follow_up_time`):
	* Enter the time for the follow-up.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`):
	* Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`):
	* Enter the customer's email.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`):
	* Enter the customer's phone number.
* **CSR Name** (`csr_name`):
	* Enter the name of the customer service representative.
* **CSR Email** (`csr_email`):
	* Enter the email of the customer service representative.
* **CSR Phone** (`csr_phone`):
	* Enter the phone number of the customer service representative.
* **Call Date** (`call_date`):
	* Enter the date of the call.
* **Call Time** (`call_time`):
	* Enter the time of the call.
* **Duration** (`duration`):
	* Enter the duration of the call.
* **Quality** (`quality`):
	* Enter the quality of the interaction.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	* Provide any additional comments about the interaction.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`):
	* Upload any attachment related to the interaction (e.g., a photo or document).
* **Notes** (`notes`):
	* Enter any additional notes about the interaction.
