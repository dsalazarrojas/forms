# Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Form is a tool used to collect and document information about a patient's referral to a healthcare provider. It is designed to ensure that all necessary information is gathered in a consistent and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully to ensure you are providing all required information.
2. Enter the required fields (indicated by "required: true" in the form).
3. Fill in the relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Form** (`id: 1`, `label: Referral Form`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with a brief description of the referral reason, if necessary.
* **Referral Name** (`id: 2`, `label: referral_name`, `required: false`): This field should be completed with the name of the healthcare provider that the patient is being referred to.
* **Contact Number** (`id: 3`, `label: contact_number`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with the contact information for the healthcare provider.
* **Email** (`id: 4`, `label: email`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with the email address of the healthcare provider.
* **Referral Date** (`id: 5`, `label: referral_date`, `required: false`): This field should be completed with the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (`id: 6`, `label: referral_time`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with the time of the referral.
* **Referral Reason** (`id: 7`, `label: referral_reason`, `required: false`): This field should be completed with a brief description of the referral reason.
* **Referring Doctor** (`id: 8`, `label: referring_doctor`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with the name of the doctor referring the patient.
* **Referring Department** (`id: 9`, `label: referring_department`, `required: false`): This field should be completed with the department of the healthcare provider.
* **Referral Patient** (`id: 10`, `label: referral_patient`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with the name of the patient being referred.
* **Referral Patient Number** (`id: 11`, `label: referral_patient_number`, `required: false`): This field should be completed with the patient ID number of the healthcare provider's system.
* **Referral Comments** (`id: 12`, `label: referral_comments`, `required: false`): This field should be filled with any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Tips**: Please double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
