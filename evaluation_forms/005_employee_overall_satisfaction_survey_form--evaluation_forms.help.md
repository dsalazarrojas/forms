# Employee Overall Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their overall satisfaction with their job and work environment. It aims to gather insights into their happiness, understanding of the company's mission, and their perception of their work-life balance. The survey also inquires about the frequency of feedback from managers, growth opportunities, and support for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have understood what each question is asking.
2. Answer the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For numerical questions, select a value between 1 and 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
4. For select questions, choose an answer that best describes your experience or feelings.
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Overall Happiness
* **Overall Happiness** (`overall_happiness`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about your overall happiness with your job. Please rate your happiness on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 1: Mission Understanding
* **Mission Understanding** (`mission_understanding`, `text`, required: false): This question inquires about your understanding of the company's mission and vision. Please provide a brief description of what you think the company's mission and vision are.

### Page 1: Recommend Company
* **Recommend Company** (`recommend_company`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks how likely you are to recommend the company as a great place to work. Choose from: very likely, somewhat likely, not likely, or not at all likely.

### Page 1: Overall Happiness 2
* **Overall Happiness 2** (`overall_happiness_2`, `number`, required: true): This question is similar to the first question, but is required to be filled out.

### Page 2: Mission Understanding 2
* **Mission Understanding 2** (`mission_understanding_2`, `text`, required: false): This question is similar to the first one, but is optional.

### Page 2: Recommend Company 2
* **Recommend Company 2** (`recommend_company_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is similar to the previous one, but allows for multiple answers.

### Page 2: Work-Life Balance
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about your perception of your work-life balance. Please rate it on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 2: Feedback from Manager
* **Feedback from Manager** (`feedback_from_manager`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the frequency of feedback you receive from your manager. Choose from: often, occasionally, rarely, or never.

### Page 2: Growth Opportunities
* **Growth Opportunities** (`growth_opportunities`, `select_one`, required: false): This question inquires about your perception of growth opportunities within the company. Choose from: strongly agree, somewhat agree, neutral, somewhat disagree, or strongly disagree.

### Page 3: Feedback Importance
* **Feedback Importance** (`feedback_importance`, `number`, required: true): This question asks about the importance of feedback to you. Please rate it on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 3: Communication
* **Communication** (`communication`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about your perception of communication within the company. Please rate it on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 3: Growth Opportunities 2
* **Growth Opportunities 2** (`growth_opportunities_2`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is similar to the previous one, but is optional.

### Page 3: Report Issues
* **Report Issues** (`report_issues`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about how likely you are to report issues within the company. Choose from: very likely, somewhat likely, not likely, or not at all likely.

### Page 3: Empowered to Own Work
* **Empowered to Own Work** (`empowered_to_own_work`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about how often you feel empowered to own your work. Choose from: often, occasionally, rarely, or never.

### Page 4: Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: true): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your job. Please rate it on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 4: Communication 2
* **Communication 2** (`communication_2`, `number`, required: false): This question is similar to the previous one, but is optional.

### Page 4: Support for Growth
* **Support for Growth** (`support_growth`, `number`, required: true): This question asks about the level of support for growth and development within the company. Please rate it on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Page 4: Support Growth 2
* **Support Growth 2** (`support_growth_2`, `number`, required: false): This question is similar to the previous one, but is optional.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR representative.
