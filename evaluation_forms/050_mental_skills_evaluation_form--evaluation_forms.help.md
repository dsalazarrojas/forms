# Mental Skills Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate a person's mental skills, specifically in areas such as self motivation, confidence, visualization, and faith. It is meant to provide a comprehensive assessment of an individual's mental strengths and weaknesses in these areas.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Use clear and concise language when answering the questions.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
