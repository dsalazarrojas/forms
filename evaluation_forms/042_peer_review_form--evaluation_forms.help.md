# Peer Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Peer Review Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from peers regarding a research article. Reviewers will provide a brief evaluation of the article, including a rating and any comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, make sure you have the article open in front of you.
2.  Start by writing a brief introduction that summarizes your thoughts about the article (field 1: **Introduction**).
3.  Enter the title of the article (field 2: **article title**).
4.  Provide the name of the article's author (field 3: **article author**).
5.  Enter your own name as the reviewer (field 4: **reviewer name**).
6.  If applicable, provide your affiliation or institution (field 5: **reviewer affiliation**).
7.  Rate the article using the number rating system (field 6: **review rating**).
8.  Finally, enter any additional comments or feedback you have for the author (field 7: **comments**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: A brief summary of your thoughts about the article. This can include your overall impression, main points, or any relevant context.
*   **article title**: The title of the article being reviewed.
*   **article author**: The name of the article's author.
*   **reviewer name**: Your name as the reviewer.
*   **reviewer affiliation**: If you have an affiliation or institution related to your review, enter it here.
*   **review rating**: A number rating system to evaluate the article (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high).
*   **comments**: Any additional feedback or comments you have for the author.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide a clear and concise introduction that summarizes your thoughts about the article.
*   Double-check the article title and author to ensure accuracy.
*   Be honest and objective when providing your rating.
*   Keep your comments concise and focused on the article's content.
*   If you have any relevant affiliations or institutions, include them to provide context.

This form is intended to be completed by peers who are familiar with the article and its content. If you are not familiar with the article, it's best to review it before completing this form.
