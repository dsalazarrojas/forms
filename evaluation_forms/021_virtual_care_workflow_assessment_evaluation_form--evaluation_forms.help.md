# Virtual Care Workflow Assessment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the quality and availability of services provided during a virtual care session. It gathers feedback from both hospital staff and patients to identify areas for improvement in the virtual care workflow assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions about your experience with the virtual care session.
2. Provide feedback on the quality of the service you received.
3. Indicate whether the service was available when you needed it.
4. Rate your satisfaction level with the service.
5. Suggest any improvements to the service.
6. Confirm or deny any safety concerns.
7. Indicate whether improvements were made to address any safety concerns.
8. Confirm or deny whether improvements were made to address any service availability concerns.
9. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
10. Suggest any further improvements to the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, `note`, required): Provide any feedback you have about the staff who facilitated the virtual care session.
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, `note`, required): Provide any feedback you have about your experience with the virtual care session as a patient.
* **Service Improvement Suggestions** (`service_improvement`, `note`, required): Suggest any improvements to the service that you think would benefit patients.
* **Staff Safety Concerns** (`staff_safety_concerns`, `note`, required): If you have any concerns about the safety of the virtual care session, describe them here.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the quality of the service as Good or Poor.
* **Service Availability** (`service_availability`, `number`, required): Indicate whether the service was available when you needed it, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service as a patient, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Hospital** (`staff_satisfaction_hospital`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the hospital, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Patient Satisfaction Hospital** (`patient_satisfaction_hospital`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the hospital, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Service Improvement Ideas** (`service_improvement_ideas`, `note`, required): Suggest any further improvements to the service.
* **Service Improvement Action Item** (`service_improvement_action_item`, `note`, required): Confirm or deny that any improvements were made to address any service concerns.
* **Service Quality Improvement** (`service_quality_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the quality of the service improvement as Good, Poor, or Not Applicable.
* **Staff Safety Concerns Improvement** (`staff_safety_concerns_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm or deny any improvements to address any safety concerns.
* **Service Availability Improvement** (`service_availability_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm or deny any improvements to address any service availability concerns.
* **Patient Satisfaction Improvement** (`patient_satisfaction_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the patient satisfaction improvement as Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Not Applicable.
* **Staff Satisfaction Improvement** (`staff_satisfaction_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the staff satisfaction improvement as Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Not Applicable.
* **Staff Safety Concerns Improvement 2** (`staff_safety_concerns_improvement_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm or deny any improvements to address any staff safety concerns.
* **Service Availability Improvement 2** (`service_availability_improvement_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm or deny any improvements to address any service availability concerns.
* **Patient Satisfaction Improvement 2** (`patient_satisfaction_improvement_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the patient satisfaction improvement as Satisfied, Dissatisfied, or Not Applicable.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any questions, try your best to provide a response.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please do not hesitate to share them in the corresponding fields.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out to the support team.
