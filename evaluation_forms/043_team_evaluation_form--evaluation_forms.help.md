# team_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team_evaluation_form is used to gather feedback on team members' performance and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the team evaluation form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: Team Name, Position, and Player ID.
2. If applicable, choose the Position(s) from the dropdown list for each team member.
3. Rate each team member based on the scale provided.
4. Provide any comments or feedback for each team member.
5. Enter the team member's contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team you are evaluating.
* **Player ID** (`player_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the team member you are evaluating.
* **Position** (`position`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the position(s) of the team member from the dropdown list (Goalkeeper, Defense, Midfielder, Forward).
* **Player Rating** (`player_rating`, number, optional): Rate the team member's performance on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter a grade for the team member (e.g., A-F).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments or feedback for the team member.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the team member's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the team member's phone number.
