# facilitator_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The facilitator_evaluation_form is a tool used to evaluate the performance and behavior of facilitators in courses, training sessions, or other events. It assesses their support, communication skills, time management, and overall rating. Evaluators provide feedback in the form of ratings and comments, helping facilitators identify areas for improvement and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter the name of the course or training session in the "course_name" field (optional).
3. Rate the facilitator's overall support from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
4. Rate the facilitator's communication skills from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
5. Rate the facilitator's time management from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
6. Rate the facilitator's content quality from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
7. Rate the participant's engagement from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
8. Select any additional evaluation forms used, if applicable.
9. Add any additional comments for the facilitator in the "additional_comments" field (optional).
10. Enter the facilitator's name and contact information in the "facilitator_name" and "facilitator_contact_info" fields (optional).
11. Rate the participant's overall experience with the facilitator (optional).
12. Add comments about the participant's experience with the facilitator (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the participant being evaluated.
* **course_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the course or training session being evaluated (if applicable).
* **overall_rating** (Number, Required): Rate the facilitator's overall performance from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
* **facilitator_support** (Select One, Required): Rate the facilitator's support as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **communication_skills** (Select One, Required): Rate the facilitator's communication skills as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **time_management** (Select One, Required): Rate the facilitator's time management as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **content** (Select One, Required): Rate the facilitator's content quality as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **engagement** (Select One, Required): Rate the participant's engagement as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **evaluation_form** (Select Multiple, Required): Select any additional evaluation forms used (if applicable).
* **additional_comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments about the facilitator's performance.
* **facilitator_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the facilitator's name (if not provided by the participant).
* **facilitator_contact_info** (Text, Optional): Enter the facilitator's contact information (if not provided by the participant).
* **participant_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the participant's overall experience with the facilitator.
* **participant_rating_comments** (Text, Optional): Add comments about the participant's experience with the facilitator.

Note: Some fields are optional; however, providing accurate and complete information will result in more valuable feedback for the facilitator.
