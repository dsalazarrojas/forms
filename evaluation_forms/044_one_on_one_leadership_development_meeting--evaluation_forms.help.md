# One On One Leadership Development Meeting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for one-on-one meetings to facilitate discussions between supervisors and team members on performance, progress, and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Meeting Focus" field with a brief description of the meeting's topic or objective.
2. Take notes during the meeting in the "Meeting Notes" field.
3. Fill in the "Meeting Focus Date" field with the date of the meeting.
4. Fill in the "Meeting Focus Time" field with the time of the meeting.
5. Provide evaluation comments in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
6. Evaluate the team member's performance by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Evaluation Score" field.
7. Choose the relevant evaluation criteria from the "Evaluation Scale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **One On One Meeting** (`one_on_one_meeting`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the meeting's topic or objective.
* **Meeting Focus** (`meeting_focus`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the meeting's topic or objective.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_focus_note`, `note`, optional): Take notes during the meeting to track important information and action items.
* **Meeting Focus Date** (`meeting_focus_date`, `date`, optional): The date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Focus Time** (`meeting_focus_time`, `time`, optional): The time of the meeting.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): A field for supervisors to provide feedback or comments on the team member's performance.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `select_one`, optional): Evaluate the team member's performance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the relevant evaluation criteria for the performance evaluation.
