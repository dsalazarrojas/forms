# Teacher-Student Interaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher-Student Interaction Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students on their interactions with teachers. This feedback is used to help teachers evaluate their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Select a rating for the teacher's performance from 1 to 5.
2. Provide a brief response to describe your interaction with the teacher.
3. Enter the date and time of the interaction, as well as the duration of the interaction.
4. Choose the outcome of the interaction (positive, negative, or neutral).
5. Rate the teacher's attentiveness, responsiveness, clarity, openness, and helpfulness.
6. Answer questions about whether the teacher provided sufficient information and asked for questions or confirmation.
7. Finally, indicate your level of satisfaction with the help received from the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the teacher** (teacher_rating): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate how well you think the teacher performed.
* **What would you say to the teacher** (student_response): Write a brief description of your interaction with the teacher. This can include any feedback or comments you have for the teacher.
* **Additional Feedback** (feedback): Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have for the teacher.
* **Suggestions for the teacher** (teacher_suggestions): Use this field to suggest ways the teacher can improve their performance.
* **Suggestions for students** (student_suggestions): Use this field to suggest ways students can improve their performance.
* **Date of interaction** (date_of_interaction): Enter the date of the interaction with the teacher.
* **Time of interaction** (time_of_interaction): Enter the time of the interaction with the teacher.
* **Duration of interaction** (duration_of_interaction): Enter the duration of the interaction with the teacher.
* **What was the outcome of the interaction** (interaction_outcome): Choose one of the three options: Positive outcome, Negative outcome, or Neutral outcome.
* **How attentive was the teacher** (teacher_attentiveness): Rate the teacher's attentiveness from 1 to 5.
* **How responsive was the teacher** (teacher_response_time): Rate the teacher's responsiveness from 1 to 5.
* **How clear were the instructions** (teacher_clarity): Rate the teacher's clarity from 1 to 5.
* **How open to feedback were you** (teacher_openness): Rate how open you felt the teacher was to feedback from 1 to 5.
* **Did the teacher provide sufficient information** (student_clarification): Answer with True or False.
* **Did the teacher ask for questions** (student_confirmation): Answer with True or False.
* **Did the teacher ask you questions** (teacher_confirmation): Answer with True or False.
* **Did the teacher help when you needed it** (teacher_helpfulness): Answer with True or False.
* **Were you satisfied with the help received** (teacher_satisfaction): Answer with True or False.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your feedback to help the teacher improve.
* Take your time to answer each question thoroughly.
* Keep the feedback concise and clear.
* Use this form regularly to provide feedback on each interaction with the teacher.
* Review the teacher's performance over time to track progress.
