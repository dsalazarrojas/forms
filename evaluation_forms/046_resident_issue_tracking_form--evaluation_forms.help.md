# resident_issue_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resident_issue_tracking_form is designed to collect information about issues or concerns raised by residents. This form helps in tracking and resolving these issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have not already submitted this form for the same resident issue.
2. Fill in the required details as accurately as possible.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the resident's contact information, including their name, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the issue or concern raised by the resident. Be as detailed as possible to help in resolving the issue.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the resident's email address, if available.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the resident's phone number, if available.
* **Issue Location** (`issue_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the issue occurred or is expected to occur.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or context that might be helpful in resolving the issue.

## Tips
- Please ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any field, it's better to clarify with the relevant person or team before proceeding.
- This form is meant to assist in tracking and resolving resident issues efficiently, so please use it for its intended purpose only.
