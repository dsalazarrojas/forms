# core_standards_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The core_standards_assessment form is used to evaluate student progress and determine if they are meeting core standards in their subject area. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the student's current information, including their grade level and subject area.
2. Determine if the student has met the core standards for their grade level and subject area.
3. Answer the questions and complete any additional fields as directed.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`1`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information that you need to share with the assessor or student.

### Assessment Questions
* **Assessment Questions** (`2`, select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to each question to indicate whether the student has met the core standards.

### Student's Answer
* **Student's Answer** (`3`, text, required): Enter the student's written response to the assessment questions.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`4`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the student's progress.

### Assessment Score
* **Assessment Score** (`5`, number, required): Enter a score out of 100 to indicate the student's level of understanding.

### Progress Status
* **Progress Status** (`6`, select_multiple, required): Select the student's current progress status, such as "Not Started", "Ongoing", or "Completed".

### Next Action or Follow-up
* **Next Action or Follow-up** (`7`, text, optional): Enter any next steps or follow-up actions for the student.

### Meeting Standards
* **Meeting Standards** (`8`, checkbox, required): Check the box if the student is meeting the standards, and select one of the options if they are not meeting the standards.

### Assessor's Signature
* **Assessor's Signature** (`9`, text, required): Enter your signature to confirm that you have completed the assessment.

Note: All fields with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
