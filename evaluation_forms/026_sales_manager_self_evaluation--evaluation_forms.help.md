# Sales Manager Self Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Manager Self Evaluation form is designed to gather information on sales managers' performance, goals, and leadership skills. This form will help us evaluate and develop our sales team's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "Self Evaluation Form Name" field with a brief description of the form, such as a project or initiative name.
2.  Rate your performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent, in the "Evaluation Form Rating" field.
3.  Briefly describe your performance in the "Sales Manager Performance" field.
4.  List your sales goals and objectives in the "Sales Manager Goals" field.
5.  Select the leadership style that best describes your approach in the "Sales Manager Leadership Style" field.
6.  Choose the leadership skills that you possess or are working on developing in the "Sales Manager Leadership Skills" field. You can select multiple skills.
7.  If you have any other leadership skills not listed, you can briefly describe them in the "Other Leadership Skills" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Self Evaluation Form Name** (`self_evaluation_form_name`, text, required): A brief description of the form, such as a project or initiative name.
* **Evaluation Form Rating** (`evaluation_form_rating`, number, required): Your rating of your performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Sales Manager Performance** (`sales_manager_performance`, text, required): A brief description of your performance.
* **Sales Manager Goals** (`sales_manager_goals`, text, required): A list of your sales goals and objectives.
* **Sales Manager Leadership** (`sales_manager_leadership`, text, required): A brief description of your leadership approach.
* **Sales Manager Leadership Style** (`sales_manager_leadership_style`, select_one, required): Select the leadership style that best describes your approach (Strong Leadership, Fair Leadership, or Good Leadership).
* **Sales Manager Leadership Skills** (`sales_manager_leadership_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select the leadership skills you possess or are developing (Coaching, Strategic Thinking, Communication, Problem Solving, Team Building).
* **Other Leadership Skills** (`sales_manager_leadership_skills_other`, text, optional): If you have any other leadership skills not listed, please describe them briefly.
* **Other Leadership Skills Other** (`sales_manager_leadership_skills_other_other`, text, optional): If you have any other leadership skills not listed, please describe them briefly.

## Tips
Please complete this form thoroughly and honestly, as your input is valuable to us.
