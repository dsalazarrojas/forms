# Academic Department Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the performance of an academic department. It assesses various aspects of departmental performance, including teaching quality, research output and impact, student support services, financial management, and more. The form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness and provides recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the department name in the "Department name" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Evaluator name" field.
3. Specify the evaluation period in the "Evaluation period" field.
4. Rate the department's teaching quality, research output and impact, student support services, and financial management using the provided options.
5. Provide a list of notable strengths in the "Department strengths" field (optional).
6. Provide actionable recommendations for improvement in the "Key recommendations for improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Department name
Department name (`department_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the department being evaluated.

### Evaluator name
Evaluator name (`evaluator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the person completing the evaluation.

### Evaluation period
Evaluation period (`evaluation_period`, `text`, required): Specify the academic year or dates covered by the evaluation.

### Teaching quality rating
Teaching quality rating (`teaching_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the department's overall teaching effectiveness using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Research output and impact
Research output and impact (`research_output`, `select_one`, required): Rate the department's research productivity and impact using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Student support services
Student support services (`student_support`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the department's advising and support services using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### Financial management
Financial management (`financial_management`, `select_one`, optional): Assess the department's budget and resource use using the options: Strong, Adequate, Needs improvement, Concern.

### Department strengths
Department strengths (`strengths`, `text`, optional): List notable achievements and strengths of the department.

### Key recommendations for improvement
Key recommendations for improvement (`recommendations`, `text`, required): Provide actionable recommendations for the department to improve.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective in your evaluation and recommendations.
- Use the provided options to rate and provide feedback accurately.
- Keep the "Department strengths" field for notable achievements, but do not repeat what is already covered in other sections.
- Keep the "Key recommendations for improvement" field actionable and specific.
