# Care Coordination Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness of your care coordination program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions carefully and honestly.
2. For fields with multiple choices, select all that apply or choose one option that best fits your answer.
3. Enter dates in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
4. For fields that require a percentage value, enter a whole number (e.g., 80).
5. Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional information or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluator Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the evaluator of the program.
* **Evaluator Role** (`2`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose your role in the program, such as Program Administrator, Quality Assurance Specialist, or Healthcare Provider.
* **Organization Name** (`3`, `text`, required/true): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Care Coordination Program Name** (`4`, `text`, required/true): Enter the name of the care coordination program being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`5`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period Start Date** (`6`, `date`, required/true): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Period End Date** (`7`, `date`, required/true): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* **Program Duration** (`8`, `select_one`, required/true): Select how long the program has been operating.
* **Target Population Served** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all that apply from the list of target populations served.
* **Average Monthly Enrollment** (`10`, `number`, required/true): Enter the approximate number of patients enrolled in the program.
* **Program Goals Met** (`11`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate to what extent the program's goals are being met.
* **Patient Satisfaction Rate** (`12`, `number`, required/false): Enter the estimated percentage of patient satisfaction.
* **Provider Satisfaction Rate** (`13`, `number`, required/false): Enter the estimated percentage of provider satisfaction.
* **Impact on Hospital Readmission Rates** (`14`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the impact on hospital readmission rates.
* **Program Cost Effectiveness** (`15`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the program's cost effectiveness.
* **Care Quality Indicators** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all that show improvement from the list of care quality indicators.
* **Technology Utilization** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all that apply from the list of technology utilization.
* **Staffing Adequacy** (`18`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the program's staffing adequacy.
* **Interdisciplinary Collaboration** (`19`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the level of interdisciplinary collaboration.
* **Barriers to Effectiveness** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all that apply from the list of barriers to effectiveness.
* **Notable Success Stories or Outcomes** (`21`, `text`, required/false): Enter brief descriptions of notable success stories or outcomes.
* **Key Recommendations for Improvement** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all that apply from the list of recommendations for improvement.
* **Recommend Program Continuation** (`23`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate whether you recommend program continuation.
* **Overall Program Effectiveness Rating** (`24`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the overall program effectiveness.
* **Additional Comments** (`25`, `text`, required/false): Enter any additional comments or explanations.

Please note that the fields marked as "required/true" are mandatory fields and must be filled out. Fields marked as "required/false" are optional and can be skipped if no information is available.
