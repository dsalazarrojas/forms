# Emergency Preparability Drill Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Preparability Drill Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the emergency preparedness drill, which is an important exercise to assess how effectively an organization or individual is prepared to respond to emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by providing your name and position.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with emergency drills and your position in the organization.
3. Fill out the rest of the form based on the information you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <img src="emergency_preparability_drill" alt="Emergency Drill">
*   **Emergency Drill** (emergency_preparability_drill, text, not required): This field is used to capture the name of the emergency drill. Please provide a brief description of the drill.
*   **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, not required): This field is for the name of the participant. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Participant Position** (participant_position, select_one, not required): This field is to determine your position within the organization. Please select 'Yes' if you hold a position within the organization and 'No' if you do not.
*   **Participant Organization** (participant_organization, select_multiple, not required): This field is to determine your organization affiliation. Please select all organizations you have an affiliation with.
*   **Observer Name** (observer_name, text, not required): This field is for the name of the observer. Please enter the name of the person who was observing the drill.
*   **Observer Position** (observer_position, text, not required): This field is for the observer's position. Please provide a brief description of the observer's position.
*   **Drill Date** (drill_date, date, not required): This field is used to capture the date of the emergency drill. Enter the date of the drill.
*   **Drill Start Time** (drill_start_time, time, not required): This field is used to capture the time of the start of the emergency drill. Enter the start time of the drill.
*   **Drill End Time** (drill_end_time, time, not required): This field is used to capture the time of the end of the emergency drill. Enter the end time of the drill.

## Tips
*   Please fill out this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the organization or the person who conducted the drill.
*   This form is for your convenience, and you can fill it out at your own pace.
