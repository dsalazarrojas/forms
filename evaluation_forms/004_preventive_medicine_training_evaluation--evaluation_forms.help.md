# Preventive Medicine Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of a Preventive Medicine Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the option "Yes" or "No" for "training_completed" if you have indeed completed the training program.
2. If you completed the training, enter the name of the course you participated in for "course_name".
3. Enter the date you attended the training for "training_date".
4. Enter the number of hours spent in training for "course_hours".
5. If applicable, enter the name of the training location and facility for "training_location" and "training_facility".
6. Enter the name of the person who facilitated the training for "training_facilitator".
7. Enter the name of the person who evaluated the training for "training_evaluator".
8. Enter any additional comments about the training for "training_comments".
9. Finally, rate the overall quality of the training on a scale from 1 to 10 for "training_score".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **training_completed** (`training_completed`, select_multiple, required: false): This field indicates whether you have completed the training program. Choose "Yes" if you have attended the training, "No" otherwise.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): If you have completed the training, enter the name of the course you participated in.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Course Hours** (`course_hours`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours spent in training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the training took place.
* **Training Facility** (`training_facility`, text, required: false): Enter the facility where the training took place.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who facilitated the training.
* **Training Evaluator** (`training_evaluator`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who evaluated the training.
* **Training Comments** (`training_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the training.
* **Training Score** (`training_score`, number, required: false): Rate the overall quality of the training on a scale from 1 to 10.
