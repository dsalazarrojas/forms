# CLEAN TEAM Performance Review Client - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clean Team Performance Review Client form is used to collect feedback from clients on the services provided by Clean Team. This form helps in understanding the client's perception of the quality of service, timeliness, communication, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the required fields: "Client name", "Service date", "Service quality", "Timeliness of service", "Communication clarity", and "Would recommend service".
2.  For the optional fields: "Client contact" can be filled out if you want to provide additional information, but it's not mandatory.
3.  In "Issues reported", describe any problems you encountered while receiving services from Clean Team.
4.  In "Recommendations", provide any suggestions you may have for improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client name** (Client name, text, required): This is the name of the organization or individual who is providing feedback.
    *   Type the name of the client accurately.
*   **Service date** (Service date, date, required): This is the date when the services were provided.
    *   Select the correct date from your calendar or enter it manually.
*   **Service quality** (Service quality, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of the service provided to you.
    *   Choose one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Timeliness of service** (Timeliness of service, select_one, required): Evaluate how punctual the team was in completing the service.
    *   Choose one of the two options: True or False.
*   **Communication clarity** (Communication clarity, select_one, required): Assess how clearly you were informed about the service process and any issues.
    *   Choose one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Issues reported** (Issues reported, text, required): If you encountered any problems, describe them here.
    *   Type a detailed account of the issues you faced.
*   **Recommendations** (Recommendations, text, optional): Suggest any improvements that could be made to the service.
    *   Type any ideas you have for enhancing the service.
*   **Would recommend service** (Would recommend service, select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to recommend Clean Team to others.
    *   Choose one of the two options: True or False.
*   **Client contact** (Client contact, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or questions, type your contact details here.
    *   Provide your phone number or email address.
