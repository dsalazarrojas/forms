# Field Instructor Assessment Of Student Competencies - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student's competencies in various areas, which can be used to provide feedback and support their development.
## How To Complete This Form
See the instructions above for a step-by-step guide on how to complete the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification or official documentation.
* **Grade**: Enter the student's current grade or level of proficiency, if applicable.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter your name as the instructor who is assessing the student's competences.
* **Instructor Title**: Enter your title or position as the instructor who is assessing the student's competences.
* **Rating**: Select a score from 1 to 5 to indicate the student's performance, where 1 is "Not Applicable" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Overall Competence**: Select all applicable skills from the list to indicate the student's overall competence.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
