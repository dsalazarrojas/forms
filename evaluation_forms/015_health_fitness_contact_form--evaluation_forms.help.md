# Health & Fitness Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to provide information related to their health and fitness needs, such as seeking advice or guidance on a specific area they need help with.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To start, click on the "User Information" field and enter any additional information about yourself.
2.  Next, select a date that may be relevant to your health and fitness needs in the "Date" field.
3.  Choose which advisor you would like to reach out to for help in the "Advisor" field.
4.  In the "Details" field, provide any additional details about your health and fitness needs.
5.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Problematic Area" field to identify any areas you are experiencing.
6.  Finally, enter any other relevant information in the "Key Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Information**: This field is intended for the user to provide any additional information about themselves that may be relevant to their health and fitness needs.
    -   Type: `text`
    -   Required: No

*   **Date**: This field is used for the user to select a specific date that may be relevant to their health and fitness needs.
    -   Type: `date`
    -   Required: No

*   **Advisor**: This field allows the user to select which advisor they would like to reach out to for help.
    -   Type: `select_one`
    -   Options: `Advisor 1`, `Advisor 2`, `Advisor 3`
    -   Required: No

*   **Details**: This field is for the user to provide any additional details about their health and fitness needs.
    -   Type: `text`
    -   Required: No

*   **Problematic Area**: This field asks the user to identify any problematic areas they are experiencing.
    -   Type: `select_multiple`
    -   Options: `Yes`, `No`
    -   Required: No

*   **Key Information**: This field is for the user to provide any other relevant information about their health and fitness needs.
    -   Type: `text`
    -   Required: No

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information about yourself and your health and fitness needs.
*   Be clear and concise in your answers.
*   If you have any questions or need further guidance, do not hesitate to contact us.
