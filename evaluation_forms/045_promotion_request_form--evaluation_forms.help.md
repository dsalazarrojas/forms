# Promotion Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Promotion Request Form is a template used to request a promotion for an employee. It captures key information about the employee's current situation and the promotion details, which will aid in evaluating and processing the request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, making sure to provide accurate information.
2.  Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the promotion process before submitting this form.
3.  If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the correct employee for whom the promotion is being requested.
* **Manager** (`manager`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the manager(s) that the employee reports to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the new job title that the employee is eligible for.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department in which the promotion will take effect.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief explanation for the promotion request.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the desired start date of the promotion.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time at which the promotion will take effect.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and position.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required: false): The date the form was submitted.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will aid in the evaluation and processing of the promotion request.
