# Patient Education Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient education platform evaluation form is used to gather feedback on the effectiveness and user experience of the platform. This form helps us improve the platform and better serve our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to the instructions provided for each section. Make sure to answer all required questions, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
