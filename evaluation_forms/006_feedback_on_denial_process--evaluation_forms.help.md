# Feedback On Denial Process - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the denial process, allowing us to understand the reasons behind the denial and make improvements for future evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the evaluation that was denied in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide your name as the applicant or representative of the denied evaluation.
3. Describe the reason for the denial in the "Reason For Denial" field.
4. Enter the date and time the denial was received.
5. If applicable, provide the date and time of the reason for the denial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the evaluation that was denied.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): Provide your name as the applicant or representative of the denied evaluation.
* **Reason For Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, optional): Describe the reason for the denial in a clear and concise manner.
* **Date Denied** (`date_denied`, date, optional): Enter the date the denial was received.
* **Time Denied** (`time_denied`, time, optional): Enter the time the denial was received.
* **Reason For Denial Date** (`reason_for_denial_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the reason for the denial.
* **Reason For Denial Time** (`reason_for_denial_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of the reason for the denial.

## Tips
Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form to help us better understand the denial process. If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to include them in the form's comments section.
