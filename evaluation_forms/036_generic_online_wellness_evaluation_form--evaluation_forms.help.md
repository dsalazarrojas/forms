# Generic Online Wellness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic Online Wellness Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from users on various aspects of a product or service. This form is intended to be used in an online context, allowing users to provide their opinions and experiences with ease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best answer your evaluation for each of the questions presented.
2. For questions with text fields (Evaluation Form 8 and 9), provide a brief description in the available space.
3. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as "required" to ensure your evaluation is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Form 1** (select_multiple): Select all the evaluation options that apply to your experience. You may select multiple options.
* **Evaluation Form 2** (select_one): Choose one option that best represents your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Form 3** (number): Enter a whole number to indicate your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Form 4** (date): Enter the date of your evaluation in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Evaluation Form 5** (time): Enter the time of your evaluation in the HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Evaluation Form 6** (note): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding your evaluation in this field.
* **Evaluation Form 7** (email): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further follow-up.
* **Evaluation Form 8** (text): Enter a brief description of your evaluation in this field.
* **Evaluation Form 9** (text): Enter another brief description of your evaluation in this field.

**Tips**: Please answer all questions that are marked as required, and provide as much detail as possible for non-required questions to help us improve our services.
