# #transformationchallenge Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The transformation challenge evaluation form is designed to gather information about your experience and progress with the challenge. This will help us to improve the program and provide better support to you and future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information about yourself and your experience with the transformation challenge.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed answers to each question.
3. If you need help or clarification about any field, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): We'll send your results here.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Optional, for urgent updates.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Optional, helps with age-specific guidance.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender for demographic analysis.
* **Primary goal for** (`participant_goal`, select_one, required): Select the main outcome you seek.
* **Challenge start date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date you began the program.
* **Expected completion date** (`expected_completion_date`, date, optional): If you have a target finish date.
* **Current weight (kg)** (`current_weight`, decimal, optional): Enter your most recent weight.
* **Target weight (kg)** (`target_weight`, decimal, optional): Your goal weight for the challenge.
* **Current body fat (%)** (`current_body_fat`, decimal, optional): Optional, if you have a recent measurement.
* **Target body fat (%)** (`target_body_fat`, decimal, optional): Desired body fat percentage.
* **How satisfied are you with** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Overall rating (1-10)** (`overall_rating`, integer, required): 1 = worst, 10 = best.
* **Current motivation level** (`motivation_level`, select_one, required): How motivated are you to continue the program?
* **Sessions per week** (`workout_frequency`, integer, optional): Number of workouts you plan to do each week.
* **Preferred diet type** (`diet_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the diet that best fits your lifestyle.
* **Dietary restrictions (select all)** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Leave blank if none.
* **Which aspects applied to your experience with** (`experience_aspects`, select_multiple, optional): Leave blank if none.
* **What would you improve or change about** (`open_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Anything else you'd like to share.
* **Consent to share data with program staff** (`consent_to_share_data`, select_one, required): This allows us to personalize your experience.
* **Date of completion** (`completion_date`, date, required): The date you finish the challenge.
* **--- Completion Confirmation ---** (`section_header_completion`, note, required): A confirmation of completion.
* **Open-ended question or note** (`open_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments. 

Note: Please make sure to fill out all the required fields and provide accurate information. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
