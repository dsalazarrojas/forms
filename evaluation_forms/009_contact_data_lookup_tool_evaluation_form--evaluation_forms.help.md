# Contact Data Lookup Tool Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Data Lookup Tool Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback on the performance of contact data lookup tools. It assesses the user's experience and evaluation of the tool in various aspects, including accuracy, completeness, ease of use, integration, search speed, API quality, pricing, vendor support, security, and overall recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each section of the form thoroughly, but feel free to skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.
2. For each field, answer the options that best describe your experience or opinion.
3. If you are unsure about a question or answer, explain your reasoning or provide a brief comment.
4. Use the "Final Decision" field to note any disagreements or differences in opinion among evaluators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tool Evaluation Context**: This field is for any additional information about your evaluation experience. Please provide any relevant context or background that will help us understand your evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name**: This is your name, which will be used to identify your evaluation.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to (e.g., Sales Operations, Marketing Operations, Procurement, IT, Data Management, Executive).
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date when you evaluated the tool.
* **Tool Being Evaluated**: Enter the name of the tool you are evaluating.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the company providing the tool.
* **Evaluation Period**: Indicate how long you have evaluated the tool (e.g., Less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, 1-3 months, Over 3 months).
* **Data Accuracy Rating**: Rate the accuracy of the data provided by the tool (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Data Completeness Rating**: Rate the completeness of the data provided by the tool (e.g., Very Complete, Mostly Complete, Adequate, Incomplete, Very Incomplete).
* **Ease of Use**: Rate how easy it is to use the tool (e.g., Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **Integration Capability**: Rate how well the tool integrates with your systems (e.g., Excellent Integration, Good Integration, Average Integration, Poor Integration, No Integration Tested).
* **Search Speed**: Rate the lookup speed of the tool (e.g., Very Fast, Fast, Average, Slow, Very Slow).
* **API Quality**: If the tool has an API, rate its quality (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Not Applicable).
* **Pricing and Value**: Rate if the pricing is fair for the value provided (e.g., Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value).
* **Vendor Customer Support**: Rate the vendor support (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Security and Compliance**: Rate if the tool meets security requirements (e.g., Exceeds Requirements, Meets All Requirements, Meets Most Requirements, Partial Compliance, Does Not Meet Requirements).
* **Recommendation**: Indicate if you would recommend this tool (e.g., Highly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, Strongly Do Not Recommend).
* **Key Strengths**: Describe what the tool does well.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe what the tool needs improvement on.
* **Final Decision**: Note your final recommendation for adoption (e.g., Proceed with Purchase, Proceed with Negotiations, Continue Evaluation, Consider Alternatives, Do Not Purchase).
