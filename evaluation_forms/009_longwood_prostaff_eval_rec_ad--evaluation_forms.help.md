# longwood_prostaff_eval_rec_ad - Help Guide
## Purpose
The longwood_prostaff_eval_rec_ad form is used to evaluate the quality of an advertisement. Fill out this form to provide feedback on advertisements that you have reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the advertisement's evaluation form URL and enter your feedback.
2. Click on each field to enter your feedback.
3. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advertisement URL**: Enter the URL of the advertisement you are evaluating.
* **Rating**: Select the rating that best describes your opinion on the advertisement's quality (Good or Fair).
* **Evaluation Form Date**: Enter the date you evaluated the advertisement.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments you have about the advertisement.
* **Employees Evaluated**: Check if employees were evaluated while reviewing this advertisement (True or False).
* **Comments Text**: Enter any additional text comments about the advertisement.
* **Score**: Enter a numerical score for the advertisement.
* **Evaluation Form Rating**: Select the rating that best describes your opinion on the advertisement's quality (Good or Fair).
* **Evaluation Form Comments 2**: Check if employees were evaluated while reviewing this advertisement (True or False).
* **Evaluation Form Employees Evaluated 2**: Check if employees were evaluated while reviewing this advertisement (True or False).
* **Employees Evaluated Options**: Enter any additional options for employees that were evaluated while reviewing this advertisement.
* **Employees Rating**: Select the rating that best describes your opinion on the employees' quality (Good or Fair).
* **Employees Evaluated Options**: This field seems to be for entering additional options for employees, but it's currently labeled as "text" and doesn't have any validation. It's best to leave it blank unless specifically instructed otherwise.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when entering your feedback.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
