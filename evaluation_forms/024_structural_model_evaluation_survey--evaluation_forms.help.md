# structural_model_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The structural model evaluation survey is designed to gather feedback from users who have reviewed the structural model. This form helps assess the model's design, quality, and overall performance against certain standards or benchmarks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the structural model carefully.
2. Provide your honest ratings and comments for each section.
3. Ensure that you answer all the required questions.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Design Review Rating (1)** (`design_review_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall design of the structural model, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Structural Model Quality Rating (2)** (`structural_model_quality_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the structural model, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Design Comprehension Rating (3)** (`design_comprehension_rating`, number, optional): Rate how well the structural model is understood by the reviewer, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Design Review Comment (4)** (`design_review_comment`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the design of the structural model.
* **Comparison To Standard Rating (5)** (`comparison_to_standard_rating`, number, optional): Rate how well the structural model meets the standard or benchmark, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Structural Model Evaluation Score (6)** (`structural_model_evaluation_score`, number, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Comparison To Other Rating (7)** (`comparison_to_other_rating`, number, optional): Rate how well the structural model compares to other similar models or designs, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Documentation Rating (8)** (`documentation_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality and clarity of the model's documentation, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Comparison Comment (9)** (`comparison_comment`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the comparison to other models.
* **Overall Rating (10)** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall performance of the structural model, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Evaluation Form Comments (11)** (`evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional): Provide any general comments or feedback about the form.
* **Evaluation Comments (12)** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Evaluation Form Comments (13)** (`evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional): Provide any general comments or feedback about the model.
* **Structural Model Evaluation Form Comments (14)** (`structural_model_evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Rating Scale Comments (15)** (`rating_scale_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the rating scale.
* **Evaluation Scale (16)** (`evaluation_scale`, number, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Evaluation Form Scale (17)** (`evaluation_form_scale`, number, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Rating Scale (18)** (`rating_scale`, number, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Evaluation Scale Rating (19)** (`evaluation_scale_rating`, number, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.
* **Evaluation Form Scale Rating (20)** (`evaluation_form_scale_rating`, number, optional): This field is not intended to be used by users.

Note: Please only fill out the required fields, unless you have additional comments or feedback.
