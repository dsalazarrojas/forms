# Store Features Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Features Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the features of a store. This form is designed to gather information about the store's features and assess their quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store name in the "store_name" field.
2. Select the relevant features of the store from the "store_features" field.
3. Choose one of the available options for each feature from the "evaluation" field.
4. Add any additional comments or observations in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your evaluation score in the "score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_name (text)**: Enter the name of the store you are evaluating.
* **store_features (multiple selection)**: Select all the features that are available at the store, such as feature1, feature2, and feature3.
* **evaluation (select one)**: Choose the evaluation option that best describes your opinion about each feature:
	+ feature1
	+ feature2
	+ feature3
* **comments (text)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the store and its features.
* **score (number)**: Enter a score out of 100 to evaluate the overall quality of the store's features.
