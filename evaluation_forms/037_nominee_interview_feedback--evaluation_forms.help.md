# Nominee Interview Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on nominees for an evaluation process. It helps to gather information about the nominee's performance, rating, and overall feedback from interviewers. The feedback will be used to evaluate and decide on the nominee's suitability for a position or promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the nominee from the available options.
2. Rate the nominee's performance with a number (e.g., 1-10).
3. Enter any comments or additional feedback about the nominee's position.
4. Select overall feedback options (e.g., Yes, No) to rate the nominee's suitability.
5. Enter any additional comments about the interview.
6. Enter the interview date and time.
7. Enter the nominee's name and position.
8. Enter the interviewer's name.
9. Enter the department and team that the nominee belongs to.
10. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee** (`Nominee`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the nominee for whom you are providing feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the nominee's performance (e.g., 1-10).
* **Prophets position** (`position`, `text`, required: false): Enter the nominee's position.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional feedback about the nominee's performance.
* **Prophets recommendation** (`recommendation`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional feedback about the nominee's suitability.
* **Prophets score** (`score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the nominee's score.
* **Interviewers score** (`interviewers_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the interviewer's score.
* **Overall feedback** (`overall_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select overall feedback options (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the interview.
* **Final rating** (`final_rating`, `number`, required: false): Enter the final rating.
* **Interview date** (`interview_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the interview date.
* **Interview time** (`interview_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the interview time.
* **Nominee name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the nominee's name.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the nominee's job title.
* **Nominee position** (`nominee_position`, `text`, required: false): Enter the nominee's position.
* **Interviewer name** (`interviewer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the interviewer's name.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department that the nominee belongs to.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required: false): Enter the team that the nominee belongs to.
* **Comments 2** (`comments2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
* **Rating scale** (`rating_scale`, `number`, required: false): Enter the rating scale.
* **Rating scale range** (`rating_scale_range`, `number`, required: false): Enter the rating scale range.
* **Rating scale unit** (`rating_scale_unit`, `text`, required: false): Enter the rating scale unit.
* **Rating scale label** (`rating_scale_label`, `text`, required: false): Enter the rating scale label.
* **Interviewers comments** (`interviewers_comment`, `text`, required: false): Enter the interviewer's comments.
* **Interviewers score** (`interviewers_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the interviewer's score.

Please note that all fields are optional and can be filled in according to your discretion.
