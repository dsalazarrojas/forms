# Research Fellow Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Fellow Performance Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the performance of research fellows in a structured and systematic way, taking into account their work and progress throughout the specified evaluation period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Research Fellow Name" field with the name of the research fellow being evaluated.
2. Enter the "Evaluation Period" in which the research fellow's performance is being assessed.
3. Rate the research fellow's performance on a scale of 1 to 5 using the "Performance Rating" field.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about the research fellow's performance in the "Research Group" field.
5. If the research fellow holds a different job title than the default "Research Fellow", please enter their job title in the "Job Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Fellow Name**: Enter the name of the research fellow being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period**: Enter the period during which the research fellow's performance is being assessed.
* **Performance Rating**: Rate the research fellow's performance on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Research Group**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the research fellow's performance.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the research fellow, if different from the default "Research Fellow".
