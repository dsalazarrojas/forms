# Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the packaging prototype. It assesses their overall impression, design, functionality, usability, and user experience. The form aims to collect data on the prototype's strengths and weaknesses, which will help in refining and improving the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly, providing your genuine thoughts and feelings about the packaging prototype.
2.  Rate the overall impression, design, and user experience using the provided scale.
3.  Describe the primary benefit of the packaging prototype in the "Benefit" field.
4.  Estimate the time you would spend using the packaging prototype in a typical day.
5.  Share your comments and thoughts on each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What's your overall impression of the packaging prototype?
*   overall_impression:
    *   **Overall Impression** (select_one): Rate your overall impression using the provided options: Good or Fair.

### 2. What's your feedback about the packaging prototype?
*   comments: 
    *   **Comments** (note): Share your thoughts on the packaging prototype. This is an open-ended field for any additional comments or feedback.

### 3. Signature
*   signature:
    *   **Signature** (time): Sign your name, indicating that you have completed the form and provided your genuine feedback.

### 4. How do you like the overall design of the packaging prototype?
*   design:
    *   **Design** (select_one): Rate the design using the provided options: Good or Fair.

### 5. What is your rating for the packaging prototype's functionality?
*   functionality:
    *   **Functionality** (select_multiple): Rate the functionality using the provided options: Good, Fair, or multiple selections.

### 6. How user-friendly is the packaging prototype?
*   usability:
    *   **Usability** (select_one): Rate the user-friendliness using the provided options: Good or Fair.

### 7. How many hours would you spend using the packaging prototype in a typical day?
*   time_spent:
    *   **Time Spent** (number): Estimate the time you would spend using the packaging prototype in a typical day.

### 8. Share your thoughts on the packaging prototype (optional)
*   comments2:
    *   **Comments 2** (note): Share any additional comments or thoughts on the packaging prototype.

### 9. What is the primary benefit of the packaging prototype?
*   benefit:
    *   **Benefit** (text): Describe the primary benefit of the packaging prototype.

### 10. What's your overall rating for the packaging prototype's user experience?
*   user_experience:
    *   **User Experience** (select_one): Rate the user experience using the provided options: Good or Fair.

### 11. Share your thoughts on the packaging prototype (optional)
*   comments3:
    *   **Comments 3** (note): Share any additional comments or thoughts on the packaging prototype.

### 12. Would you use the packaging prototype in your work/project/personal life?
*   will_use:
    *   **Will Use** (select_multiple): Answer using the provided options: True or False.

### 13. Share your thoughts on the packaging prototype (optional)
*   comments4:
    *   **Comments 4** (note): Share any final comments or thoughts on the packaging prototype.
