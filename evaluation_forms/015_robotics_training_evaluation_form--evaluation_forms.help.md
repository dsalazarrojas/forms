The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness of robotics training sessions. It is used to collect feedback and assess the performance of students in robotics training programs. This form should be completed by students who have attended a robotics training session to provide their honest feedback and rating on the session's quality, instructors, and materials.

## Purpose
The Robotics Training Evaluation Form is designed to gather information from students about their experience with the robotics training session. This form will help us understand what worked well and what did not, so we can improve the training program to better meet the needs of our students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your name and the title of the training session you attended.
2. Select the model of the robot you worked with during the session.
3. Rate your overall score of the training session on a scale of 0 to 100.
4. Provide any additional feedback you have about the session in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, optional): Enter the title of the training session you attended.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and identify yourself as the student who attended the session.
* **Robot Model** (`robot_model`, select_one, optional): Select the model of the robot you worked with during the session.
* **Training Score** (`training_score`, number, optional): Rate your overall score of the training session on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback you have about the session.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback to help us improve the training program.
* You can rate your score out of 100, where 100 is excellent and 0 is poor.
* You can select only one robot model from the options provided.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the feedback field.
