# Productivity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Productivity Assessment Form is designed to evaluate an employee's productivity in the work environment. This form is used to track and assess an employee's work habits, time management, and other productivity-related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and select the options that best describe your productivity habits and level.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as Employee ID and Form ID.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes about your productivity.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, optional): This field is a header for the form. It's not a required field and is just for informational purposes.
* **Productivity Level** (`productivity_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level that best describes your productivity, such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Changes to Improve** (`changes_to_improve`, select_multiple, optional): Select any changes you would like to make to improve your productivity, such as Time Management or Goal Setting.
* **Productivity Levels** (`productivity_levels`, number, optional): Enter your current productivity level, if any.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have an Employee ID.
* **Date Last Updated** (`date_last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Time Last Updated** (`time_last_updated`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last update.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about your productivity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, text, optional): Enter the date and time of the form submission.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, text, optional): Enter the date and time of the form completion.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your second job title (if applicable).
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, text, optional): Enter your third job title (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or feedback.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Date Last Update** (`date_last_update`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Time Last Update** (`time_last_update`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last update.
