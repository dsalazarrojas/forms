# Covid 19 Questionnaire And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and medical details required for COVID-19 evaluation and administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each required field with the relevant information.
2. Select only one option for fields with a "select one" type.
3. Select multiple options for fields with a "select multiple" type.
4. Ensure that all fields with the "select multiple" type are populated with at least one option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): If you have a valid email address associated with this account, please enter it here.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Medical Group** (5, select_one, required): Select the medical group you belong to.
* **Vaccine Administered** (6, date, optional): Enter the date when the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccine Name** (7, select_multiple, required): Select the type of COVID-19 vaccine administered.
* **Medical Condition** (8, select_one, required): Select the medical condition you are experiencing, or choose "Other" if it's not listed.
* **Medical Professional** (9, text, optional): If you have a medical professional associated with this account, please enter their name here.
* **Medical Professional ID** (10, text, optional): If you have a medical professional associated with this account, please enter their ID here.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Double-check that your information is accurate before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your medical professional for guidance.
