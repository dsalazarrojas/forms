# Clinic Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinic Intake form is used to gather essential information from clients about their personal details, allergies, and service goals. This form is a crucial part of the evaluation process, ensuring the client's comfort and suitability for the services provided by the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the client information section with your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your street address and city.
3. Answer the questions about your hair type, texture, condition, and scalp condition.
4. Disclose any allergies or sensitivities you may have to hair products or chemicals.
5. Answer questions about previous allergic reactions to hair services.
6. Specify your primary goal for the service and desired style or look.
7. Select the services you are interested in.
8. Describe your current at-home care routine and your willingness to maintain your style.
9. Review and submit the form for our evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address where you can be reached.
* **City (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Hair Type (`hair_type`, select_one, required): Choose your hair type from the options: Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily, or Very coily/Kinky.
* **Hair Texture (`hair_texture`, select_one, required): Choose your hair texture from the options: Fine, Medium, or Coarse.
* **Current Hair Condition (`hair_condition`, select_one, required): Select the current condition of your hair from the options: Healthy, Dry, Oily, Combination, Damaged, or Color-treated.
* **Scalp Condition (`scalp_condition`, select_one, required): Choose the condition of your scalp from the options: Healthy, Dry, Oily, Sensitive, Irritated, or Other.
* **Known Allergies to Hair Products (`product_allergies`, text, optional): If you have any known allergies to hair products, list them here.
* **Sensitivities to Chemicals (`chemical_sensitivities`, text, optional): If you have any sensitivities to chemicals, list them here.
* **Previous Allergic Reactions to Hair Services (`previous_reactions`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have experienced any previous allergic reactions to hair services.
* **Primary Goal for Today (`primary_goal`, text, required): Specify your primary goal for the service.
* **Desired Style or Look (`desired_style`, text, optional): Describe the style or look you desire.
* **Services Interested In (`treatments_interested`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you are interested in from the options: Cut, Color, Highlights/Lowlights, Perm, Relaxer, Extensions, Deep conditioning, Styling, or Other.
* **Current At-Home Care Routine (`home_care_routine`, text, optional): Describe your current at-home care routine, including products used and frequency.
* **Willingness to Maintain Style (`maintenance_commitment`, select_one, optional): Indicate your willingness to maintain your style from the options: High maintenance OK, Low maintenance preferred, or Flexible.
