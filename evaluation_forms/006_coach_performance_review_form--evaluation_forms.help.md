# Coach Performance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of a coach in a specific sport, providing a comprehensive assessment of their technical skills, leadership qualities, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the coach's name in the "Coach Name" field.
2. Select the sport in which the coach works (e.g., Soccer, Basketball, Tennis).
3. Specify the season (e.g., Fall 2025).
4. Enter your name as the reviewer.
5. Rate the coach's performance in each category using the given scales (1-5).
6. Provide additional comments on areas for growth and improvement.
7. Add any additional comments about the coach's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach being evaluated.
* **Sport** (`sport`, text, required): Select the sport in which the coach works (e.g., Soccer, Basketball, Tennis).
* **Season** (`season`, text, required): Specify the season (e.g., Fall 2025).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Knowledge of Sport Fundamentals** (`fundamentals_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's knowledge of the sport's fundamentals on a scale of 1-5.
* **Ability to Explain Concepts** (`concept_explanation`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's ability to explain complex concepts on a scale of 1-5.
* **Strategy and Game Management** (`strategy_management`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's strategic management on a scale of 1-5.
* **Professionalism and Conduct** (`professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's professionalism and conduct on a scale of 1-5.
* **Motivation of Athletes** (`athlete_motivation`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's ability to motivate athletes on a scale of 1-5.
* **Fairness and Equity** (`fairness_equity`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's fairness and equity on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance`, select_one, required): Provide a summary rating of the coach's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Areas for Growth** (`areas_for_growth`, text, optional): Provide suggestions for areas where the coach can improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the coach's performance.
