# Training Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from trainees about their experience with a trainer. This feedback will be used to evaluate and improve the quality of training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Trainer Name** field with the name of the trainer who led the session.
2. Enter the **Trainee Name** field with your own name.
3. Select the **Session Date** field to choose the date of the session you attended.
4. Choose the **Session Time** field to select the time of the session you attended.
5. In the **Feedback** field, describe your honest opinion about your experience with the trainer and the session.
6. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the **Suggestions** field.
7. If you have any additional comments, type them in the **Evaluation Form Comments** field.
8. Finally, choose your **Overall Rating** by selecting either "Good" or "Fair" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Trainer Name (Required)
* **Trainer Name** is the field where you enter the name of the person who led the session.
### Trainee Name (Required)
* **Trainee Name** is the field where you enter your own name as the person who attended the session.
### Session Date (Required)
* **Session Date** is the field where you select the date of the session you attended using the calendar picker.
### Session Time (Required)
* **Session Time** is the field where you select the time of the session you attended using the time picker.
### Feedback (Required)
* **Feedback** is the field where you describe your honest opinion about your experience with the trainer and the session.
### Suggestions (Optional)
* **Suggestions** is the field where you can type any suggestions you have for improvement.
### Evaluation Form Comments (Optional)
* **Evaluation Form Comments** is the field where you can type any additional comments about the session.
### Overall Rating (Required)
* **Overall Rating** is the field where you select either "Good" or "Fair" to rate your overall experience with the session.
