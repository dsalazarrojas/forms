# resident_assistant_training_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to evaluate and document the progress of residents undergoing training.
## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions before starting.
* Complete all required fields, indicated by <code>required:</code> = <code>true</code>.
* Use the evaluation scale to rate the trainee's performance.
* For the `recommendations` field, provide specific and actionable feedback for the trainee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Evaluation Form** (`id: 1`): This is the main section for evaluating the trainee's performance.
* **Trainee Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the name of the trainee being evaluated.
* **Trainee ID** (`id: 3`): Enter the ID of the trainee, if applicable.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`id: 4`): Enter the date on which the evaluation took place.
* **Time of Evaluation** (`id: 5`): Enter the time on which the evaluation took place.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`id: 6`): Provide any relevant comments about the evaluation.
* **Training Level** (`id: 7`): Select the trainee's current training level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`id: 8`): Rate the trainee's performance on the following scale: Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Satisfactory, Good, or Excellent.
* **Recommendations** (`id: 9`): Provide specific and actionable feedback for the trainee.
* **Action Taken** (`id: 10`): Enter any actions taken as a result of the evaluation.
* **Action Due Date** (`id: 11`): Enter the date by which any actions taken should be completed.
* **Trainee Rating** (`id: 12`): Rate the trainee's overall performance: 1-2, 3, 4, or 5.
* **Trainer Name** (`id: 13`): Enter your name as the trainer.

## Tips
* Use clear and concise language when providing feedback and recommendations.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* Keep the `recommendations` field specific and actionable.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure trainees' progress is accurately documented.
