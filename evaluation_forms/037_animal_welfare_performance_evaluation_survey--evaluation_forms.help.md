# Animal Welfare Performance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the performance of facility or clinic staff members in animal welfare. The form assesses the adherence to feeding protocols, accuracy of medical records, cleaning and sanitation performance, animal handling and safety skills, interaction with the public and adopters, knowledge of local welfare laws, and other essential skills and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the facility or clinic name and the date of the evaluation.
2. Then, enter the name of the staff member being evaluated, if applicable.
3. For each of the following questions, select the corresponding rating from the provided options:
   * Adherence to Feeding Protocols: Select Excellent - Exceeds standards, Good - Meets all standards, Fair - Occasional lapses, or Poor - Significant failure.
   * Accuracy of Medical Records: Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
   * Cleaning and Sanitation Performance: Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
   * Animal Handling and Safety Skills: Select Very Skilled, Skilled, Needs Improvement, or Unsafe.
   * Interaction with Public and Adopters: Select Professional and Helpful, Standard Interaction, Neutral, or Unprofessional.
   * Knowledge of Local Welfare Laws: Select High - Expert level, Moderate - Functional, Low - Needs training.
4. If you have any specific areas of improvement, list them in the corresponding field.
5. Finally, enter your name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility or Clinic Name**: Enter the name of the facility or clinic where the evaluation took place.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation in dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Staff Member Being Evaluated**: If you are evaluating a specific individual, enter their name.
* **Adherence to Feeding Protocols**: Rate the staff member's adherence to feeding protocols, considering frequency, quantity, and special diets.
* **Accuracy of Medical Records**: Rate the accuracy of the staff member's medical records, considering log completeness and timing.
* **Cleaning and Sanitation Performance**: Rate the staff member's cleaning and sanitation performance.
* **Animal Handling and Safety Skills**: Rate the staff member's animal handling and safety skills, considering stress-free handling techniques.
* **Interaction with Public and Adopters**: Rate the staff member's interaction with the public and adopters, considering professionalism and helpfulness.
* **Knowledge of Local Welfare Laws**: Rate the staff member's knowledge of local welfare laws.
* **Key Performance Strengths**: If applicable, list specific strengths or areas of excellence in the staff member's performance.
* **Areas for Immediate Improvement**: If applicable, list specific areas where the staff member can improve.
* **Evaluator Name and Title**: Enter your name and title.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help the staff member improve their performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* This form is for internal use only and is not meant to be shared with the public.
