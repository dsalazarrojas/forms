# First Aid Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The First Aid Risk Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a facility's first aid supplies, training, knowledge, environment, access, risk factors, and control measures. This form helps assess the risk level of a facility and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the Facility Company School information**: Provide the facility's name, company name, and school name. This information is not required but helps in identifying the facility.
2. **Select First Aid Supplies and Equipment**: Choose all the first aid supplies and equipment available at the facility, such as First Aid Kits, First Aid Boxes, Defibrillators, and others.
3. **Select First Aid Training**: Choose the frequency of first aid training, such as every 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or never.
4. **Select First Aid Knowledge**: Choose the level of first aid knowledge, such as First Aid Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Specialist, or Trainer.
5. **Select First Aid Environment**: Choose the environment of the facility, such as clear, cluttered, dusty, wet, or hot.
6. **Select First Aid Access**: Choose whether first aid is readily available, such as yes or no.
7. **Select Risk Factor**: Choose the risk level, such as low, medium, or high.
8. **Enter Risk Control Measures**: Provide any additional measures taken to control risks.
9. **Assess Risk**: Choose whether the facility has a risk assessment in place, such as yes or no.

* **Facility Company School**: This field is not required but helps in identifying the facility.

* **Risk Factor**: This field is a multiple-choice question where you can select the risk level, such as low, medium, or high.
