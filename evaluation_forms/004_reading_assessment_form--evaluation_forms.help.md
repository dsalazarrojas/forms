# Reading Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Reading Assessment Form" is designed to collect information about your reading habits, preferences, and abilities. This form aims to provide a general understanding of your reading behaviors and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, and ensure you answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If a field is marked as `required`, make sure to provide a response.
3. For select-type fields (e.g., "reading_speed", "reading_comprehension", "reading_preference", "reading_interest", "reading_form_comments"), select one or more options that best describe your reading habits.
4. For text-type fields (e.g., "reading_frequency"), provide a brief description of your reading habits.
5. You can add any additional comments or information in the "reading_other_comments" and "reading_additional_comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Reading Assessment Form
The "Reading Assessment Form" itself is the title of the form.

### 2. Reading Speed (Required)
* **Reading Speed (reading_speed)** (Number): Enter your average reading speed in [X] words per minute.

### 3. Reading Comprehension (Required)
* **Reading Comprehension (reading_comprehension)** (Select One): Choose one that best describes your reading comprehension:
	+ choice1: Easy to understand
	+ choice2: Moderate understanding
	+ choice3: Difficulty understanding

### 4. Reading Preference (Optional)
* **Reading Preference (reading_preference)** (Select One): Choose one that best describes your reading preference:
	+ choice1: Fiction
	+ choice2: Non-fiction
	+ choice3: A mix of both

### 5. Reading Difficulty (Optional)
* **Reading Difficulty (reading_difficulty)** (Select Multiple): Choose all that apply:
	+ choice1: Easy to read
	+ choice2: Moderate difficulty
	+ choice3: Challenging to read

### 6. Reading Frequency (Optional)
* **Reading Frequency (reading_frequency)** (Text): Describe your reading frequency, e.g., daily, weekly, occasionally, etc.

### 7. Reading Interest (Optional)
* **Reading Interest (reading_interest)** (Select One): Choose one that best describes your reading interest:
	+ choice1: Highly interested
	+ choice2: Somewhat interested
	+ choice3: Not interested

### 8. Reading Other Comments (Optional)
* **Reading Other Comments (reading_other_comments)** (Text): Add any additional comments or information about your reading habits.

### 9. Reading Additional Comments (Optional)
* **Reading Additional Comments (reading_additional_comments)** (Text): Add any further comments or information about your reading habits.

### 10. Reading Form Comments (Optional)
* **Reading Form Comments (reading_form_comments)** (Select Multiple): Choose all that apply:
	+ choice1: This form was easy to complete
	+ choice2: This form was challenging to complete
	+ choice3: I needed help completing this form
