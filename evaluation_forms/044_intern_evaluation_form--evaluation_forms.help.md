# Intern Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance and behavior of interns during their tenure. It is used by supervisors or managers to evaluate the intern's skills, attitude, and work efficiency, as well as their communication skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials before starting the evaluation.
2. Fill in the required fields: Intern Performance, Communication Skills, and Intern Performance Rating.
3. Provide feedback and comments in the text fields: Evaluation Comments, Recommendations, and Evaluation Comments (if necessary).
4. Choose the correct options for Communication Skills and Intern Performance Rating.
5. Enter the date and time of evaluation in the Date of Evaluation and Time of Evaluation fields.
6. Enter the evaluator's contact information (optional): Email and Phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intern Evaluation Form (1)**: This field is a text box where you can enter any additional comments or information about the intern.
* **Intern Performance (2)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the intern's performance.
* **Intern Attitude (3)**: Enter any comments or feedback about the intern's attitude and behavior.
* **Communication Skills (4)**: Select all the options that apply to the intern's communication skills, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
* **Work Efficiency (5)**: Enter a numerical value to rate the intern's work efficiency.
* **Feedback (6)**: Provide any feedback or comments about the intern's performance.
* **Evaluation Comments (7)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the intern.
* **Intern Performance Rating (8)**: Select one of the options to rate the intern's performance, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3", "Option 4", or "Option 5".
* **Recommendations (9)**: Enter any recommendations for the intern's future growth or development.
* **Evaluation Comments (10)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the intern.
* **Date of Evaluation (11)**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time of Evaluation (12)**: Enter the time of the evaluation in the format hh:mm.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your contact email address (if you wish to provide feedback or comments).
* **Phone (14)**: Enter your contact phone number (if you wish to provide feedback or comments).

Note: The optional fields can be filled in if you wish to provide additional information.
