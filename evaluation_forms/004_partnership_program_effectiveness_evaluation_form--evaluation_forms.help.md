# Partnership Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate the effectiveness of your partnerships. It's intended to be filled out by partners to provide feedback and assess the success of your partnership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form sections and questions to ensure you understand what is being asked.
* Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
* If you're unsure about any question or section, please consult the field explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Program (partner_program)**: This is the name of your partnership program. Please enter the name of the partnership you're evaluating.
* **Partnership Program (partnership_program)**: This is the description of your partnership program. Please enter a brief description of the program.
* **Program Effectiveness (program_effectiveness)**: Select all the ways your partnership program has been effective. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Partnership Goals (partnership_goals)**: Enter the goals of your partnership program. This will help us understand what you aimed to achieve.
* **Goals Achieved (goals_achieved)**: Select all the goals you've achieved with your partnership program. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Evaluation Period (evaluation_period)**: Enter the period during which you've been evaluating the partnership. This will help us track the progress over time.
* **Feedback (feedback)**: Provide any additional feedback about your partnership program.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any comments or observations about your partnership program.
* **Evaluation Score (evaluation_score)**: Enter a numerical score out of 10 to assess the effectiveness of your partnership program.
* **Score Justification (score_justification)**: Explain the reasoning behind your evaluation score.
* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction)**: Select your overall satisfaction with the partnership program. You can choose one option.
* **Evaluation Score Justification (evaluation_score_justification)**: Add any further justification for your evaluation score.
* **Recommendations (recommendation)**: Suggest any recommendations for future partnership programs.
* **Partnership Type (partnership_type)**: Select all the types of partnerships you've engaged in. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Evaluation Method (evaluation_method)**: Select all the methods you've used for evaluating the partnership. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Evaluation Frequency (evaluation_frequency)**: Select how frequently you've evaluated the partnership. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Partner Contact (partner_contact)**: Enter the contact details of your partner, including name and email.
* **Partner Name (partner_name)**: Enter your partner's name.
* **Partner Email (partner_email)**: Enter your partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone (partner_phone)**: Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Partner Website (partner_website)**: Enter your partner's website URL.
* **Partner Twitter (partner_twitter)**: Enter your partner's Twitter handle.
* **Partner Facebook (partner_facebook)**: Enter your partner's Facebook page URL.
* **Partner Instagram (partner_instagram)**: Enter your partner's Instagram handle.
* **Form Submission (form_submission)**: This field is for internal tracking purposes only and should not be filled out by end-users.
