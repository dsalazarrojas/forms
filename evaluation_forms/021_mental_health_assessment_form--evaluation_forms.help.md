# Mental Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on an individual's mental health status, including their current mental and emotional states, anxiety and depression levels, suicidal tendencies, treatment history, and assessment details. This information will be used by mental health professionals to inform treatment decisions and improve patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible to help mental health professionals understand the individual's situation.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all relevant options that apply.
4. Date and time fields are required for accurate tracking and reference.
5. Comments fields are for additional information or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Page 1)**: Introduce yourself and the individual being assessed.
* **What is the current mental state of the individual? (Page 2)**: Describe the individual's current mental state, including any relevant details or concerns.
* **What is the emotional state of the individual? (Page 3)**: Describe the individual's emotional state, including any feelings or concerns.
* **Anxiety Level (Page 4)**: Rate the individual's anxiety level from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Depression Level (Page 5)**: Rate the individual's depression level from 1 to 10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Are there suicidal tendencies? (Page 6)**: Answer "Yes" or "No" based on the individual's situation.
* **Treatment History (Page 7)**: Describe the individual's treatment history, including any relevant medical or psychological interventions.
* **Who is the mental health professional administering this form? (Page 8)**: Select "Doctor", "Nurse", or "Therapist" as the mental health professional administering the form.
* **Date of mental health assessment (Page 9)**: Enter the date of the mental health assessment.
* **Time of mental health assessment (Page 10)**: Enter the time of the mental health assessment (optional).
* **Any comments about the mental state of the individual? (Page 11)**: Provide any additional comments or concerns about the individual's mental state.
* **Any comments about the emotional state of the individual? (Page 12)**: Provide any additional comments or concerns about the individual's emotional state.
* **What is the treatment plan for the individual? (Page 13)**: Describe the treatment plan for the individual, including any recommended steps or interventions.
* **Next Steps (Page 14)**: Outline any next steps or follow-up actions for the individual's treatment plan.
* **Any comments from the mental health professional? (Page 15)**: Provide any additional comments or concerns from the mental health professional.
* **Additional notes about the mental health assessment form? (Page 16)**: Provide any additional notes or feedback about the form itself.
* **Evaluation Result (Page 17)**: Select "Yes" or "No" based on whether the individual has met the evaluation criteria (this option is required).
