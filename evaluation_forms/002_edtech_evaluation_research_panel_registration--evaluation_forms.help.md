# EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EdTech Evaluation Research Panel is a group of experts who provide insights and guidance on the evaluation of educational technology products and services. By registering your interest in joining the panel, you will be able to contribute your expertise and participate in evaluations that shape the future of EdTech.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of yourself and your area of expertise in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Name** field (id: 2).
2. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field (id: 3).
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field (id: 4).
4. Select your relevant research interests from the list of options in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Research Interests** field (id: 6).
5. Indicate your level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D.) in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Education** field (id: 7).
6. Share your professional experience in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Professional Experience** field (id: 8).
7. Specify how many years of professional experience you have in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Professional Experience Years** field (id: 9).
8. Select the relevant research areas you are interested in or have expertise in from the list of options in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Research Areas** field (id: 10).
9. If you have other areas of research interest not listed, please specify them in the **EdTech Evaluation Research Panel Registration Research Areas Other** field (id: 11).

* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete.
