# Observation Sheet For HR Practices - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to observe and document HR practices in various departments or sections within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully, indicating "Yes" or "No" for sections where practices are present or not.
2. For text and date fields, enter the required information according to the field type.
3. If a section is not applicable to a particular department, leave it blank.
4. Fill out the form for a specific department or section at a time, to avoid duplication of effort.
