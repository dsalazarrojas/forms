# Futsal Referee Mentor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Futsal Referee Mentor Form is designed to collect information about referees in the context of Futsal. This form is intended for evaluating or mentoring referees, and the collected data will help in understanding their strengths and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the text field.
2. Provide your Email address in the email field.
3. Fill in your Phone Number in the number field.
4. Select your Referee Experience from the dropdown list (e.g., Referee Level 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
5. Choose your Referee Mentorship Level from the dropdown list (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
6. Select your Evaluation Frequency from the dropdown list (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
7. Enter the Evaluation Date in the date field.
8. Select the Evaluation Time in the time field.
9. Choose your Referee Mentorship Level Frequency from the dropdown list (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
10. Provide any Evaluation Notes in the text field.
11. Select your Referee Education from the dropdown list (e.g., Referee Education Level 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
12. Indicate whether you have Referee Certification (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Referee Experience** (`referee_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your referee experience level (e.g., Referee Level 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Referee Mentorship Level** (`mentorship_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose your referee mentorship level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you are evaluated (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your evaluation.
* **Referee Mentorship Level Frequency** (`mentorship_level_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how often you are mentored (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, note, required: false): Add any evaluation notes.
* **Referee Education** (`referee_education`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your referee education level (e.g., Referee Education Level 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Referee Certification** (`referee_certification`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you have referee certification (True or False).

## Tips
* Please fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct options for each field based on your actual experience and qualifications.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact our support team.
