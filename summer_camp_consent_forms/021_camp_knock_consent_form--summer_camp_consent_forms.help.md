# Camp Knock Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Knock Consent Form is used to obtain electronic consent for a camper's participation in camp activities. This form ensures that the camper's parent or guardian is informed and agrees to their child's participation in camp.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several sections:

1. **Parent or Guardian Name**: Please provide your full name.
2. **Camper Name**: Enter the name of the camper to whom this consent applies.
3. **Consent Description**: Explain what activities or interactions you are consenting to.
4. **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you are providing consent.
5. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter a phone number that can be contacted in case of an emergency.
6. **Signature Name**: Type your full name as an electronic signature.
7. **Relationship to Camper**: Select "Parent", "Guardian", or "Other" to indicate your relationship to the camper.
8. **Duration of Consent**: If applicable, enter the time period for which consent is valid. (Note: This field is optional and only applies if you are not consenting for the entire camp season.)
9. **How to Withdraw Consent**: If you need to withdraw consent, please enter the instructions on how to do so.
10. **Additional Comments**: Provide any other relevant information about the camper or the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent or Guardian Name**: Your name as the authorized representative of the camper.
  • **Camper Name**: Name of the child to whom this consent applies.
  • **Consent Description**: Description of what activities or interactions the camper will be participating in.
  • **Date of Consent**: Date when consent is provided.
  • **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number to contact in case of an emergency.
  • **Signature Name**: Full name as electronic signature.
  • **Relationship to Camper**: Relationship to the camper (Parent, Guardian, Other).
  • **Duration of Consent**: Time period for which consent is valid (if not for the entire season).
  • **How to Withdraw Consent**: Instructions on how to withdraw consent (if necessary).
  • **Additional Comments**: Any other relevant information about the camper or consent.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before filling it out.
- Make sure to enter the correct phone number for emergency contact.
- If you need to withdraw consent, please enter the instructions in the "How to Withdraw Consent" field.
- If you have any additional comments, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
