# Summer Camp Parental Release Medical Information Code Of Conduct - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Parental Release Medical Information Code Of Conduct form is designed to collect essential medical information and consent from parents/guardians for their child's participation in a summer camp program. It ensures the camp staff has the necessary details to provide proper care and attention to the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with your child's name, birthdate, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Medical Information section, provide any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs your child may have.
3. Read and sign the Consent section to confirm your understanding and agreement to the camp's terms and conditions.
4. Complete the Contact Information section with your contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
5. List the emergency contacts in the Emergency Contact section.
6. Fill in the Medical Authorization sections with any medical authorizations you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`text`, required): Enter your child's name, birthdate, and any other relevant details to help the camp staff know your child's identity.
* **Medical Information** (`text`, required): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs your child may have. This information will help the camp staff provide proper care and attention.
* **Consent** (`text`, required): Read and sign this section to confirm your understanding and agreement to the camp's terms and conditions.
* **Contact Information** (`text`, required): Provide your contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses, so the camp staff can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact** (`text`, required): List the names, phone numbers, and relationships of the emergency contacts.
* **Parent Consent** (`text`, required): This section is for you to confirm your consent for your child's participation in the summer camp program.
* **Student Consent** (`text`, optional): This section is for your child to confirm their consent to participate in the summer camp program. If your child is a minor, this section is not applicable.
* **Medical Authorization** (`text`, optional): This section is not clearly defined, so we will treat it as optional.
* **Allergies and Medical Conditions** (`text`, required): Describe any allergies or medical conditions your child may have.
* **Medications and Medical Treatment** (`text`, required): List any medications or medical treatments your child is currently taking.
* **Medical Authorization 2-13** (`text`, optional): These sections are not clearly defined, so we will treat them as optional.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`text`, required): List the names, phone numbers, and relationships of the emergency contacts.
