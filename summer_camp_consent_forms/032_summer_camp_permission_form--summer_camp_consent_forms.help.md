# Summer Camp Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Permission Form is used to gather essential contact information and relationships from parents or guardians of children attending summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form completely and accurately. Each page is self-explanatory, with labels and instructions guiding you through each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Permission Form** (`summer_camp_permission_form_parent`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the permission form you are submitting.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name. If you are a guardian, enter the name of the person signing on behalf of the parent.
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, select_one, required): Choose the relationship between you and the child, from the options Mother, Father, or Guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person that the camp staff should reach out to, enter their name here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child, from the options Mother, Father, or Guardian.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone_2`, text, optional): If you have a secondary emergency contact person that the camp staff should reach out to, enter their phone number here.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the child attending summer camp.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the child attending summer camp.
* **Student Date of Permission** (`student_date_of_permission`, date, optional): If you are granting permission for the child to attend summer camp, enter the date of permission here.

## Tips
Please review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the camp staff for assistance.
