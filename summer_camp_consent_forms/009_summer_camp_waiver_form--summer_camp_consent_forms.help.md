# Summer Camp Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Waiver Form is used to collect essential information from campers and their guardians for the summer camp program. Please complete this form carefully to ensure all necessary details are accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the camper's personal details:
	* Enter the camper's First Name and Last Name.
	* Enter the Parent/Guardian's First and Last Names.
2. Provide contact information:
	* Enter the camper's Email and Phone numbers.
	* Select the relationship between the camper and the guardian (Yes/No).
3. Provide medical information:
	* Enter any known Medical Conditions.
	* List any known Medical Allergies.
	* List any current Medical Medications.
	* Provide any Medical Notes.
4. Enter Emergency Contact information:
	* Enter the Emergency Contact's Name and Phone number.
	* Enter any known Emergency Medical Conditions.
	* Provide any Emergency Medical Notes.
5. Enter additional details:
	* Enter the Date of Birth of the camper.
	* Enter the Camp Start and End Dates.
	* Enter the Camp Hours.
	* Enter the Camp Location.
	* Select the Camp Supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's last name.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Enter the Parent/Guardian's first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Enter the Parent/Guardian's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the camper's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the camper's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship between the camper and the guardian (Yes/No).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the Emergency Contact's name.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the Emergency Contact's phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Enter any known medical conditions.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, text, required): Enter any known medical allergies.
* **Medical Medication** (`medical_medication`, text, required): Enter any current medical medications.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required): Enter any medical notes.
* **Emergency Medical Condition** (`emergency_medical_condition`, text, required): Enter any known emergency medical conditions.
* **Emergency Medical Notes** (`emergency_medical_notes`, text, required): Enter any emergency medical notes.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the Emergency Contact's information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the camper's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Camp Start Date** (`camp_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the camp.
* **Camp End Date** (`camp_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the camp.
* **Camp Hours** (`camp_hours`, time, required): Enter the camp hours in the HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Camp Location** (`camp_location`, text, required): Enter the camp location.
* **Camp Supervisor** (`camp_supervisor`, select_one, required): Select the camp supervisor.
