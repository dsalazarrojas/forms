# Camp Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legally binding contract that requires parents or guardians to acknowledge and accept the risks involved in participating in the camp activities. It releases the camp and its staff from liability in case of any accidents or injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the risks involved in participating in the camp activities.
2. Complete the form by filling in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the risks and agreements.
4. Return the completed form to the camp staff before your child participates in the camp activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Full Name* (Page 1, Field 2)**: Please enter your full name as the parent or guardian of the camper.
* **Camper Full Name* (Page 1, Field 3)**: Please enter your child's full name.
* **Camper Date of Birth* (Page 1, Field 4)**: Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Acknowledgment of Inherent Risks* (Page 1, Field 6)**: Please select "I Have Read and Understand" to acknowledge that camp activities involve risks of injury or illness.
* **Release and Waiver of Liability* (Page 1, Field 7)**: Please select "I Have Read and Agree" to release the camp and its staff from liability in case of any accidents or injuries.
* **Indemnification Agreement* (Page 1, Field 8)**: Please select "I Have Read and Agree" to agree to indemnify the camp against costs resulting from your child's actions.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone* (Page 1, Field 12)**: Please enter your name and phone number as the emergency contact.
* **Parent - Guardian Electronic Signature* (Page 1, Field 13)**: Please type your full name as the electronic signature.
* **Date Signed* (Page 1, Field 14)**: Enter the date you sign this form in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Witness Name (Optional)* (Page 1, Field 15)**: If you have a witness, please enter their name.
* **Consent to Electronic Record* (Page 1, Field 17)**: Please select "I Agree" to agree to use electronic signatures for this document.
* **Certification of Physical Condition* (Page 1, Field 10)**: Please select "I Certify This Is True" to certify that your child is physically fit to participate in activities.
* **List any specific activity restrictions (Page 1, Field 11)**: If your child has any specific activity restrictions, please list them. For example, "No high-ropes, no deep-water swimming."
* **Legal Disclaimer (Page 1, Field 16)**: This document is a legally binding contract that affects your legal rights.
* **Electronic Record Consent (Page 1, Field 17)**: Please select "I Agree" to agree to use electronic signatures for this document.

Note: Please review each field carefully and make sure to answer truthfully and accurately.
