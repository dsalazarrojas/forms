# Summer Camp Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a summer camp participant, including their contact and activity preferences. It is likely used to gather consent from parents or guardians for minor children participating in summer activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Camper Info** (`camper_info`, `text`, required: false)
    Provide your child's basic information such as name, address, and date of birth.
2.  **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter your information as the parent or guardian of the child participating in the summer camp.
3.  **Activity Selection** (`activity_selection`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Select all the activities your child is interested in or would like to participate in during the summer camp.
4.  **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false)
    Provide any medical information that is relevant to your child's participation in the summer camp.
5.  **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter your contact information in case of an emergency.
6.  **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false)
    Add your signature to confirm that you are the parent or guardian of the child participating in the summer camp.
7.  **Medical Release** (`medical_release`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Indicate whether you grant permission for your child to receive medical treatment in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Camper Info**: This field is intended to capture basic information about the camper, such as their name, address, and date of birth.
*   **Parent Info**: Enter your name, address, and contact information as the parent or guardian of the child participating in the summer camp.
*   **Activity Selection**: Select all activities you're interested in or your child would like to participate in during the summer camp. This could be arts and crafts, games, or sports.
*   **Medical Info**: Provide any medical information relevant to your child's participation in the summer camp, such as allergies or medications.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Provide your contact information in case of an emergency, allowing the camp to quickly get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Signature**: Add your signature to confirm that you are the parent or guardian of the child participating in the summer camp.
*   **Medical Release**: Grant or deny permission for your child to receive medical treatment in case of an emergency.

## Tips

*   This form is designed to gather relevant information for your child's participation in the summer camp.
*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the summer camp administrators for further assistance.
