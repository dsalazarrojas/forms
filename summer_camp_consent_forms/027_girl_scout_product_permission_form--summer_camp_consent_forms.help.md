## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help Girl Scout units and volunteers understand whether a product has been properly permitted for use within their unit. This form helps ensure that all necessary permissions have been granted and that any products being sold for fundraising purposes are in line with Girl Scout policies and regulations.

**Is this form relevant?**: Before proceeding, please consider whether this form is relevant to your specific use case. If you are not purchasing Girl Scout products for a unit or individual, this form may not be necessary.

**Are you duplicating efforts?**: If you have already collected the necessary information via another means, you may not need to fill out this form. Please double-check your processes and policies to ensure you are not duplicating efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your submitter information on page 1.
2.  Choose whether you purchased Girl Scout products for your unit.
3.  If you purchased for other units, select "Yes" and fill out additional information in the "Other Units" section.
4.  If you selected "Yes" for "Other units," provide a detailed description of the type of products you purchased.
5.  If you have any additional comments or information about your purchase, you may include them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Submitter Information** (<submitter_info>)

*   **Submitter Information**: This field is where you will enter your name and contact information as the person completing this form. Please ensure this information is accurate and up-to-date.

**Girl Scout Products Purchased for Unit** (<unit_products>)

*   **Girl Scout Products Purchased for Unit**: Please select "Yes" if you have purchased Girl Scout products for your unit, and "No" if you have not. If you have purchased for other units, see the "Other Units" section for further instructions.

**Purchased Girl Scout Products for Other Units?** (<other_units>)

*   **Purchased Girl Scout Products for Other Units?**: If you selected "Yes" for "Girl Scout Products Purchased for Unit," this field will appear. Select "Yes" to indicate that you purchased products for other units, or "No" if you did not. You may also select "Other (please specify)" and provide a detailed explanation of your purchase in the "Other Units" field.

**What type of Girl Scout products did you purchase for other units?** (<other_units_products>)

*   **What type of Girl Scout products did you purchase for other units?**: If you selected "Yes" for "Purchased Girl Scout Products for Other Units," this field will appear. This is where you will enter a detailed description of the specific Girl Scout products you purchased for other units.

**Additional Comments** (<comments>)

*   **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information or clarifications regarding your purchase, you may include them here.

**Submitter Information Review** (<review_info>)

*   **Submitter Information Review**: This field is a text box for any review or feedback from the submitters about their experience with this form. It is optional and not required to be filled out.
