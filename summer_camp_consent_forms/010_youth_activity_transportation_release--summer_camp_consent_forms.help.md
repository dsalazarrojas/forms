# Youth Activity Transportation Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Youth Activity Transportation Release form, its purpose, and the steps to fill it out.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is intended to be completed by the parents or guardians of the children participating in activities. To complete it, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Consent** (required): Please fill this field with your name and contact information. This will allow us to contact you in case of any updates or concerns about your child's participation.
- **Emergency Contact** (optional): If possible, provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency. This can be a family member, family friend, or another trusted contact.
- **Signature** (required): Please sign this field to indicate your agreement to let your child participate in the activity. This is a formal acknowledgment of your consent.

## Tips
- Ensure that you're the parent or guardian of the child participating in the activity.
- Review each field carefully to provide accurate information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the institution or the relevant staff member for assistance.
