# Parental Consent For Stay - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect parental consent for a child to stay at a summer camp. It includes information about the child's details, contact information, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's name and details in the "Child Name" field.
2. Select the relationship between you and the child in the "Relationship" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Number", "email", and "Emergency Contact Name" fields.
4. Select the dates of stay from the "Dates of Stay" field.
5. Fill in any medical or personal concerns in the "Any medical or personal concerns" field.
6. Select "True" or "False" for the "Consent" field to indicate your consent.
7. If necessary, fill in any medical history, medical conditions, medications, medical procedures, doctor contact information, doctor facility, doctor phone, and doctor email fields.
8. Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
9. Fill in the emergency contact's phone, email, and relationship fields.
10. Finally, select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Name** (1, text, required): Enter the child's name.
* **Relationship** (3, select_one, required): Select your relationship with the child, which can be Parent, Guardian, or Other.
* **Contact Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **email** (5, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **camp details** (6, date, required): Select the dates of stay.
* **Dates of Stay** (7, select_multiple, required): Select the dates of stay from the list.
* **Signature** (8, note, required): Sign your name and date to confirm your consent.
* **Any medical or personal concerns** (9, text, required): Fill in any medical or personal concerns about the child.
* **Emergency Contact** (10, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (11, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact, which can be Parent, Guardian, or Other.
* **Consent** (12, select_one, required): Select "True" or "False" to indicate your consent.
* **Medical History** (13, text, required): Fill in any medical history.
* **Medical Conditions** (14, text, required): Fill in any medical conditions.
* **Medications** (15, text, required): Fill in any medications.
* **Medical Procedure** (16, text, required): Fill in any medical procedures.
* **Doctor Contact** (17, text, required): Enter the doctor's contact information.
* **Doctor Phone** (18, text, required): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (19, email, required): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Facility** (20, text, required): Enter the doctor's facility.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (21, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (22, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (23, email, required): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (24, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Submit** (25, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you need to update or change any information, contact the summer camp administration directly.
* If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please fill them in carefully and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
