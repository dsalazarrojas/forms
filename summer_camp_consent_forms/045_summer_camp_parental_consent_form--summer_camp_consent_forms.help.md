# summer_camp_parental_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The summer camp parental consent form is designed to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child to participate in a summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that you provide all required information.
2. Please note that some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill them out if you have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the name of the child you are enrolling in the summer camp program.
* **Camper Name (Alias)** (`camper_name_alias`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and can be used to provide an alias or nickname for the child.
* **Camper DOB** (`camper_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the phone number of the contact person for the child.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person for the child.
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Camper Medical Conditions** (`camper_medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Please list any medical conditions or allergies that the child has.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any medical authorization or clearance information for the child.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required: false): Enter the child's medical insurance information.

## Tips
Please note that some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill them out if you have the necessary information. Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to guarantee the child's safety and well-being.
