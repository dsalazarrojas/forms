# Skate School Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from students and parents for participation in the skate school summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the required fields.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Consent Forms** (`summer_camp_consent_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can add any additional comments or concerns regarding the summer camp program.
* **Sports Forms** (`sports_forms`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the sports forms you would like to participate in. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each sport.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your experience, skills, or any other relevant information.
* **Rules and Regulations** (`rules_and_regulations`, text, optional): Please provide a clear understanding of the rules and regulations of the program. 

## Tips
* Be sure to carefully review the rules and regulations before participating in the program.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Make sure to sign and return the form to confirm your participation.
* If you would like to withdraw from the program, please let us know as soon as possible.
