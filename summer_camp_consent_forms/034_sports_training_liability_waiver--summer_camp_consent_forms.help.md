# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from participants in a sports training program and is used for liability protection and to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to certain risks and responsibilities associated with the training program.

## How To Complete This Form

- **First, read the form carefully**: Before starting to fill out the form, make sure you understand what information is being collected and what is required.
- **Enter your first and last name**: Enter your first name in the "first_name" field and your last name in the "last_name" field.
- **Enter your address**: Enter your current address in the "address" field.
- **Enter your phone number**: Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
- **Check if you have insurance**: Check the "insurance_provider" field to indicate if you have insurance. If yes, provide the name of your insurance provider.
- **Emergency contact information**: Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_name" field and their phone number in the "emergency_contact_phone" field. Indicate the relationship with the emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_relationship" field.
- **Additional emergency contact information**: Enter any additional information about your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_message" field.
- **Insurance and medical information**: If applicable, enter any relevant insurance or medical information in the "insurance_info" and "medical_info" fields.
- **Sign and date the waiver**: Sign and date the waiver in the "waiver" field.
- **Add coach and facility information**: If applicable, enter the name of your coach and their contact information in the "coach_name", "coach_email", and "coach_phone" fields. Also, enter the name of the facility where you are training and their contact information in the "facility_name", "facility_phone", and "facility_address" fields.
- **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, submit the form.
