# it-services-fee-schedule-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request information about the services you offer and the terms related to their fee schedule. The goal is to provide a clear understanding of your services and fees, ensuring that you can efficiently communicate with clients and manage their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. In the **Service Options** field, briefy describe your services and pricing.
2. In the **Contact Information** field, enter your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Select your available **Service Times** from the options provided.
4. Choose how you would like to be priced in the **Pricing** field.
5. Provide more details about your services in the **Service Details** field.
6. If you have additional comments, type them in the **Additional Comments** field (optional).
7. Select your **Payment Terms** from the options provided.
8. Finally, leave a review about your services in the **Review** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Options** (text, required): Briefly describe your services and pricing in this field.
* **Contact Information** (email, required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Service Times** (select multiple, required): Select the days and times when you are available to provide services.
	+ Monday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
	+ Tuesday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
	+ Wednesday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
* **Pricing** (select one, required): Choose how you would like to be priced.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
	+ Other
* **Service Details** (text, required): Provide more details about your services.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Type any other information you would like to share about your services.
* **Payment Terms** (select one, required): Select your payment terms.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
	+ Other
* **Review** (text, required): Leave a review about your services.
