# SSL Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information about your SSL certificates for support and security purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the on-screen instructions and fill out the relevant fields according to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domain** (`domain`, text, required): Enter the domain name related to the SSL certificate you want to report.
* **SSL Type** (`ssl_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of SSL certificate you are reporting (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **S S L** (`type`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, indicate whether the certificate has expired or not ("Yes" or "No").
* **Expiration** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): If the certificate has expired, enter the date of expiration.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you have contact information for the certificate holder ("Yes" or "No").
* **CSR Data** (`csr_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional data about the Certificate Signing Request (CSR).
* **CSR File** (`csr_file`, text, optional): If available, upload a Certificate Signing Request file.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the SSL certificate.
* **Domain** (`domain`, text, optional): Enter the domain name of the certificate.
* **CSR Type** (`csr_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the certificate is a Certificate Signing Request ("Yes" or "No").
* **Certificate** (`csr`, select_multiple, optional): Mark whether the certificate is currently issued or not ("Yes" or "No").
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for further communication.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the correct information is collected. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
