# Laptop Purchase Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect accurate and complete information about a laptop purchase for internal documentation and consistency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form and ensure you have all the required and optional fields filled out.
2. Enter accurate and truthful information for each field as required.
3. Use the available options to select relevant choices for laptop details such as processor, memory, storage, OS, battery, screen size, weight, color, warranty, and accessories.
4. Specify the purchase date and time accurately.
5. Check the price range of the laptop correctly.
6. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laptop Details:** (`laptop_details`, text, Optional) Describe the laptop you are purchasing.

* **Date of Purchase:** (`date_of_purchase`, date, Optional) Enter the date of the purchase in DD/MM/YY format.
