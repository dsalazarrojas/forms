# Network Inventory Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Network Inventory Survey form is used to collect and track information about the network devices and equipment across the organization.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the department that the network device belongs to from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the location of the network device.
3. Provide a brief description of the device or equipment.
4. Enter the current inventory count.
5. Indicate if the device has any upgrades.
6. Choose the priority level for the upgrade.
7. Enter the review date.
8. Provide any comments or notes about the device.
9. Enter the submitter's name.
10. Enter the submitter's email address.
11. Enter the submitter's phone number.
12. Enter the submitter's department.
13. Provide any additional comments from the submitter.
14. Choose if the device is assigned to a tool.
15. Choose if the device is assigned to a user.
16. Select the review status of the device.
17. Enter any reviewer comments.
18. Enter the submitter's ID.
19. Enter the timestamp of the submission.
20. Enter the reviewer's ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (Select One): This field is used to select the department that the network device belongs to. Please choose the department from the list of options provided.
* **Location** (Text): Enter the location where the network device is currently located.
* **Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the device or equipment.
* **Inventory Count** (Number): Enter the current inventory count of the device.
* **Upgrades** (Select Multiple): Indicate if the device has any upgrades.
* **Priority** (Number): Choose the priority level for the upgrade (if applicable).
* **Review Date** (Date): Enter the date of the review.
* **Comments** (Note): Enter any comments or notes about the device.
* **Submitter** (Text): Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Email** (Email): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (Text): Enter the submitter's department.
* **Submitter Comments** (Note): Provide any additional comments from the submitter.
* **Assigned Tool** (Select One): Choose if the device is assigned to a tool.
* **Assigned User** (Select One): Choose if the device is assigned to a user.
* **Review Status** (Select One): Select the review status of the device.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Text): Enter any reviewer comments.
* **Submitter Id** (Text): Enter the submitter's ID.
* **Timestamp** (Date): Enter the timestamp of the submission.
* **Reviewer Id** (Text): Enter the reviewer's ID.
