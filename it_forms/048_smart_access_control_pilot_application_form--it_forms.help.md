# Smart Access Control Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Access Control Pilot Application Form is used to collect user data for access control purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the "application_form" field.
2. Provide your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
3. Enter your user input in the "user_input" field.
4. Select a date for the event in the "date" field.
5. Enter the time for the event in the "time" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
8. Add a note about your application in the "note" field.
9. Select one option from the "select_one" field.
10. Select multiple options from the "select_multiple" field.
11. Enter a decimal number in the "decimal" field.
12. Enter an integer in the "integer" field.
13. Enter text in the "text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (application_form, text, optional): This field is for entering your title.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact details.
* **User Input** (user_input, text, optional): This field is for entering your user input.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): This field is for selecting a date for the event.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): This field is for entering the time for the event.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): This field is for adding a note about your application.
* **Select One** (select_one, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting one option from the list.

## Tips
