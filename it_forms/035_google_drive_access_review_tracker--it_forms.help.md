# Google Drive Access Review Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Google Drive Access Review Tracker is used to document and track access requests for Google Drive access permissions. This form helps in maintaining a record of access requests, reviewer comments, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate access level (admin, edit, read, or none) for the access request.
2. Choose the relevant access group(s) (IT, security, admin, or management) that needs access to Google Drive.
3. Enter the access date and access time to record the request time.
4. Enter your reviewer comments, if any.
5. Add your reviewer name, email, and phone number (if available).
6. Add your reviewer title and signature for verification.
7. Choose the access review status (pending, approved, denied, or withdrawn).
8. Enter any additional comments for the reviewer.
9. Add your approver name, email, and phone number (if available).
10. Add your approver title and signature for verification.
11. Record the access reviewed date and time.
12. Add the form ID and reviewer ID for internal reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account details**: Enter the account details associated with this access request.
* **Access level**: Select one of the following options:
	+ admin: Grant admin privileges to the user.
	+ edit: Grant editing privileges to the user.
	+ read: Grant reading privileges to the user.
	+ none: No access to be granted.
* **Access group**: Select the access groups that need access to Google Drive:
	+ IT: Information technology department.
	+ security: Security team.
	+ admin: Administrative team.
	+ management: Management team.
* **Access date**: Enter the date when access was requested.
* **Access time**: Enter the time when access was requested.
* **Reviewer comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer email**: Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer title**: Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer signature**: Enter your reviewer's digital signature.
* **Approver name**: Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver email**: Enter the email of the approver.
* **Approver phone**: Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Approver title**: Enter the title of the approver.
* **Approver signature**: Enter your approver's digital signature.
* **Access reviewed**: Record the date and time when access was reviewed.
* **Access review status**: Choose the status of the access review:
	+ pending: Review is pending.
	+ approved: Access granted.
	+ denied: Access denied.
	+ withdrawn: Request withdrawn.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments.
* **Reviewer ID**: Enter the ID of the reviewer.
* **Approver ID**: Enter the ID of the approver.
* **Reviewer IP**: Enter the IP address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer country**: Enter the country of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer region**: Enter the region of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer city**: Enter the city of the reviewer.
* **Access review ID**: Enter the form ID for internal reference.
