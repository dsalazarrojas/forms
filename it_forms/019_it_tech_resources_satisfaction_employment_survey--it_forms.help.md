# IT Tech Resources Satisfaction Employment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from IT department employees on their satisfaction with IT resources, such as tools, support, and equipment, as well as their job satisfaction and feedback on improvement priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best describes your response to each question.
2. If you have no preference or no opinion, you can select "Not sure" or "None of the above".
3. If you have any further comments or feedback, please use the "Feedback Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IT Tech Resources Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the IT tech resources provided by the company. (Scale: 1-5)
* **IT Tech Support**: Select how satisfied you are with the IT support you receive. (Select one: Support, Neutral, Dissatisfied)
* **IT Tools Use**: Select all IT tools you use. (Select multiple: Microsoft Office, Other, None)
* **IT Tools Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the IT tools provided by the company. (Scale: 1-5)
* **Tech Budget**: Select how sufficient you think the tech budget is. (Select one: Sufficient, Insufficient, Not sure)
* **IT Support Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the IT support you receive. (Scale: 1-5)
* **IT Support Availability**: Select how available you think the IT support is. (Select one: Always available, Sometimes available, Never available)
* **IT Department Communication**: Select how excellent you think the IT department communication is. (Select multiple: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **IT Department Training Opportunities**: Select how often you think you have training opportunities. (Select one: Regular, Occasionally, Never)
* **IT Equipment**: Select how sufficient you think the IT equipment is. (Select one: Sufficient, Insufficient, Not sure)
* **Employee Feedback**: Select how encouraging or discouraging you find the feedback from your manager. (Select multiple: Encourage, Neutral, Discourage)
* **Improvement Priorities**: Select all areas you think require improvement. (Select multiple: IT Tools and Software, IT Support, Both, Other)
* **Feedback Comments**: Share any further comments or suggestions you have.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address (e.g. example@example.com)
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number (e.g. 123-456-7890)
* **Other**: Share any other relevant information not captured above.
* **Other Phone**: Enter any other phone number.
* **Other Email**: Enter any other email address.
* **Other Phone Number**: Enter any other phone number.
* **Manager**: Select whether you have a manager. (Select one: True, False, Not sure)
* **Department**: Select your department. (Select one: IT, Other, None)
* **Assigned To**: Select who you would like to be assigned to. (Select one: chatjimmy, Other, None)
* **Created By**: Select who created this form. (Select one: chatjimmy, Other, None)
