# employee_monitoring_software_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees for software monitoring system enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields and ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2.  Fill out the form by selecting the corresponding answers for each field.
3.  Enter any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Enrollment Requests** (`employee_enrollment_requests`, text, required): This field is a free-text area for employees to describe their monitoring requests.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you agree to monitor your computer or mobile device.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like the monitoring software to be installed.
* **Monitoring Software** (`monitoring_software`, select multiple, required): Select the type of monitoring software you would like to use on your computer or mobile device.
* **Monitor Frequency** (`monitor_frequency`, select one, required): Choose how often you want your computer or mobile device to be monitored (Every hour, Every 2 hours, or Every 4 hours).
* **Monitor Duration** (`monitor_duration`, select multiple, not required): If you select a monitor frequency, select the duration you would like the monitor to run for (1 hour, 1 hour 2 hours, 4 hours, or 8 hours).
* **Monitor Frequency Unit** (`monitor_frequency_unit`, number, required): Enter the number of minutes or hours you would like the monitor to run for.
* **Unit Label** (`monitor_frequency_unit_label`, text, required): Enter a label for the unit (e.g., minutes, hours).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your monitoring requests.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address so we can contact you about your monitor requests.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your work phone number for us to contact you about your monitor requests.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is listed in our HR system.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select one, required): Choose whether your manager approves or denies your monitor request.
* **Manager Approval Reason** (`manager_approval_reason`, text, not required): Enter a reason for your manager's approval or denial.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your monitor requests.

Note: Please fill out all required fields and be as detailed as possible in your responses.
