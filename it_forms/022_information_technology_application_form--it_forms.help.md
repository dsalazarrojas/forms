# information_technology_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to submit their information for an IT or technology-related job position. By filling this form, you will provide the hiring team with an overview of your background and qualifications.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
* Enter your name and email address for contact purposes.
* Select the relevant options for your professional experience and qualifications.
* You can upload your resume and cover letter if you like.
* Fill in any other necessary information about your current or previous job positions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that you use for professional purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as it is written on your official documents.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the IT or technology-related field where you have the most experience.
* **Resume** (`resume_upload`, note, optional): If you have a resume, please upload it here for easy review.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, note, optional): If you have a cover letter, please upload it here for easy review.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the skills you have experience with:
		- Development
		- Programming
		- Networking
		- Data Analytics
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select your highest level of education:
		- Bachelor
		- Master
		- Doctorate
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in the IT or technology field.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select where you are working:
		- Full-time
		- Part-time
		- Freelance
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, optional): Enter the salary range you expect or are expecting.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your current or previous job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your current or previous job.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your current or previous job.
* **Work Place** (`work_place`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or previous work place.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the status of your current or previous job:
		- Active
		- Inactive
		- Retired
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select where you are working:
		- Full-time
		- Part-time
		- Freelance
* **Resume Link** (`resume_link`, text, optional): If you have a public resume, enter the URL.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): If you have a public portfolio, enter the URL.
* **LinkedIn Link** (`linkedin_link`, text, optional): If you have a public LinkedIn profile, enter the URL.
* **Work License** (`work_license`, text, optional): If you have any professional work licenses, enter them here.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): If you have any professional certifications, enter them here.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Enter any other skills you have that are not listed above.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to fill it based on the best available information.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the hiring team directly.
