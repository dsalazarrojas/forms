# new-hardware-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "New Hardware Request Form" is designed to help staff members or users request new hardware for various business needs. It's a crucial tool for gathering essential information to process and fulfill these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by * in the label).
2. Enter your company name, contact name, and other relevant details accurately.
3. Provide a detailed description of the hardware requested.
4. Specify the reason for requesting the new hardware.
5. If applicable, select the priority level and deadline for the request.
6. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.
7. Enter your contact email and phone number for follow-up communication.
8. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hardware Request Form** (1, text, required): This is the main heading of the form, providing a clear label for your request.
* **Company Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of your company, ensuring it matches your official business name.
* **Contact Name** (3, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Hardware Description** (4, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the hardware requested, including its purpose and any specific requirements.
* **Reason for Request** (5, text, required): Explain why you need the new hardware, helping the department to understand your needs.
* **Requested By** (6, select multiple): Indicate whether the request is for yourself or others within your department.
* **Assigned To** (7, select one): Choose the person or department responsible for managing the new hardware.
* **Priority Level** (8, select one): Set the priority level for your request, helping the department to prioritize tasks efficiently.
* **Deadline Date** (9, date, required): Enter the date by which you would like the hardware to be acquired or delivered.
* **Deadline Time** (10, time, required): If applicable, specify the exact time for the deadline.
* **Additional Notes** (11, note, required): Add any further comments or details to support your request.
* **Email** (12, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (13, text, required): Provide your phone number for any urgent matters.
* **Company Website** (14, text, required): Enter your company's website for reference.
* **Address** (15, text, required): Enter your company's address for any necessary delivery or setup.
* **Contact Email** (16, email, required): Enter the contact email of the person or department responsible for the request.
* **Contact Phone** (17, text, required): Provide the contact phone number for this person or department.
* **Contact Address** (18, text, required): Enter the address of the person or department responsible for the request.
* **Contact Fax** (19, text, required): Enter the fax number for the person or department responsible for the request.

Note: This guide is a summary of the form's requirements and fields. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately to avoid errors and delays.
