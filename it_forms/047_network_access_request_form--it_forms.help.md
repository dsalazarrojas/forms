# Network Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a network resource or service. Please fill out the form with accurate and comprehensive information to ensure that your request is properly processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date and time for your request in the "Date Request" and "Time Request" fields.
6. Provide a brief description of your request in the "Description" field (optional).
7. Enter the department you belong to in the "Department" field. The options are:
	* Sales
	* Marketing
	* IT
8. Select the manager who will be responsible for processing your request in the "Manager" field. The options are:
	* John Smith
	* Jane Doe
	* Bob Johnson
9. Click the "Submit Request" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address that you use to communicate with the team.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Date Request** (required): Select a date for your request. This is the date you would like to start using the network resource or service.
* **Time Request** (required): Select a time for your request. This is the time you would like to start using the network resource or service.
* **Description** (optional): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Reason** (optional): Provide a brief reason for your request.
* **Department** (required): Select the department you belong to:
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ IT
* **Manager** (required): Select the manager who will be responsible for processing your request:
	+ John Smith
	+ Jane Doe
	+ Bob Johnson
* **Submit Request** (required): Click the button to submit your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your manager or IT department.
