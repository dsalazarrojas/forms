# cybersecurity_alert_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Alert Release Form is used to report and release information about security incidents that occur on the network. It helps gather essential details to identify and address potential security threats efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options in the "security_incident" field and select the correct option that best describes the security incident you're reporting.
2. Choose the appropriate "severity_level" for the incident.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
4. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
5. Specify the location of the incident.
6. List the affected resources.
7. Select the "impact_level" of the incident.
8. Choose the priority level of the incident.
9. Choose the status of the incident (new, in progress, or resolved).
10. Specify whether there are related incidents (yes or no).
11. Enter the incident number (if applicable).
12. Specify who reported the incident.
13. Enter the time the incident was reported.
14. Select the release group for the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **security_incident** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes the type of security incident.
* **severity_level** (Select One): Select the level of severity for the incident (high, medium, or low).
* **description** (Text): Provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
* **date_incident_happened** (Date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **time_incident_happened** (Time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **location** (Text): Specify the location of the incident.
* **affected_resources** (Select Multiple): List the resources affected by the incident.
* **impact_level** (Select One): Select the impact level of the incident (high, medium, or low).
* **priority** (Select One): Choose the priority level of the incident (high, medium, or low).
* **status** (Select One): Choose the status of the incident (new, in progress, or resolved).
* **related_incidents** (Select Multiple): Specify whether there are related incidents (yes or no).
* **incident_number** (Number): Enter the incident number (if applicable).
* **reported_by** (Text): Specify who reported the incident.
* **report_time** (Time): Enter the time the incident was reported.
* **release_group** (Select One): Select the release group for the incident.

## Tips
- Ensure the incident is legitimate and not a duplicate before submitting this form.
- Provide accurate and detailed information to help identify and address potential security threats efficiently.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
