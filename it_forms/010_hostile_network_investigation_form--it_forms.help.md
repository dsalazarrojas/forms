# Hostile Network Investigation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hostile Network Investigation Form is used to collect and document information about hostile network incidents. It helps in maintaining a record of all incidents, which is useful for monitoring and responding to future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Category** of the incident from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief **Description** of the incident.
3. Specify the **Location** where the incident occurred.
4. Choose the **Incident Type** from the options provided.
5. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the incident, if known.
6. Select the **Incident Status**, indicating the current resolution status.
7. Choose the **Resolution** options that apply to the incident.
8. Indicate whether the incident was submitted by an **authorized personnel** or not.
9. Enter the **Submitted Date** of the incident.
10. Enter the **Form ID**, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hostile Network Investigation Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Category**: Select one of the options provided to indicate the type of incident (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the incident, providing as much detail as possible.
* **Location**: Select the location where the incident occurred (e.g., "Location 1", "Location 2", "Location 3").
* **Incident Type**: Choose one of the options provided to indicate the type of incident (e.g., "Incident Types", "Incident Types 1", "Incident Types 2").
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the incident, in 12-hour format.
* **Incident Status**: Select the current resolution status of the incident (e.g., "Status 1", "Status 2", "Status 3").
* **Incident Resolution**: Choose one or more resolution options that apply to the incident.
* **Authorized Personnel**: Indicate whether the incident was submitted by an authorized personnel or not (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date and time the incident was submitted.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form, if required.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form only for genuine incidents that are not part of regular business operations.
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and completely.
* The form can be submitted at any time and the status can be updated later.
* This form is intended for authorized personnel within the organization.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
