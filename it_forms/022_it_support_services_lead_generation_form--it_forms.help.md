# it_support_services_lead_generation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_support_services_lead_generation_form is used to generate a new lead for IT support services. This form is designed to gather information from customers, partners, or internal stakeholders about their IT support needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Lead (1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new lead.
* **Lead Description (2)** (`text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the lead, including its purpose and scope.
* **Priority (3)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the priority level of the lead. This field allows you to select one or more priorities.
* **Lead Status (4)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the lead. This field has three options: Lead, Not Qualified, and Qualified.
* **Contact Number (5)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the lead.
* **Email (6)** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the lead.
* **Service Providers (7)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the service providers associated with the lead. This field allows you to select one or more options.
* **Follow Up Method (8)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the follow-up method to be used for the lead. This field has four options: Email, Phone Call, In App, and SMS.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for the email address field (e.g., `example@example.com`).
