# Wi Fi Settings Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your Wi-Fi network settings and experiences. Please answer the questions accurately to help us improve the performance and reliability of our Wi-Fi network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with your relevant information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Please note that some fields may have multiple options, and you can select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current network status** (`network_status`, text, \*): This field is used to report your current network status. Please type in the status of your network, e.g., "connected," "disconnected," or "slow."
* **What type of connection do you have** (`connection_type`, select_one, \*): Select one of the following options: "Home," "Public," or "Mobile." This will help us understand your connection type.
* **What is your current network speed** (`network_speed`, number, \*): Enter your current network speed in Mbps (megabits per second).
* **What Wi-Fi device are you using** (`wi_fi_device`, text, \*): Describe the type of Wi-Fi device you are using, e.g., "router," "access point," or "laptop."
* **What is your Wi-Fi signal strength** (`wi_fi_signal`, number, \*): Rate your Wi-Fi signal strength on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is weak and 10 is strong.
* **How often do you access the Wi-Fi network** (`access_point`, select_multiple, \*): Select all that apply from the following options: "Frequently," "Occasionally," "Rarely," or "Never."
* **How would you rate the performance of your Wi-Fi network** (`network_performance`, number, \*): Rate your network performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is slow and 10 is fast.
* **Do you have any problems with your Wi-Fi network** (`wi_fi_support`, note, \*): Yes or No, indicate if you have any issues with your Wi-Fi network.
* **What are some issues you have with your Wi-Fi network** (`network_issues`, text, ): Enter a brief description of any issues you have with your network, if applicable.
* **Do you have any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, ): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your Wi-Fi network experience.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you experience any issues with your Wi-Fi network, please provide a detailed description.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
