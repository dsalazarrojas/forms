# Tone Frequency Adjustment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tone Frequency Adjustment Form is used to collect information from submitters who need to request changes in tone frequency for a specific application or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the submitter.
2. Briefly describe the tone change request in the Tone Change Request field.
3. Choose the new tone frequency range from the options provided.
4. If necessary, add any additional notes for the reviewer to understand the request.
5. Attach any relevant files or supporting documentation.
6. Indicate whether you want to submit the request.
7. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter** (text field): Enter your name and title as the submitter, so we can identify you and understand your request.
* **Tone Change Request** (text field): Briefly describe the specific tone change request you need to make, including the reason for it.
* **Tone Frequency** (number field): Choose the new tone frequency you require from the options provided. Please note that some frequencies might not be available or applicable in certain contexts.
* **New Frequency Range** (select multiple field): Choose the new frequency range you want to achieve. Multiple selections are allowed.
* **Notes** (note field): Add any additional notes or explanations to support your request. This could include context or specific requirements.
* **Files** (text field): Attach any relevant files or documentation that support your request, such as audio samples or technical specifications.
* **Submit** (select one field): Choose whether you want to submit this request or not. If you choose to submit, we will review your request and follow up accordingly.
* **Email** (email field): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or questions.
* **Phone** (text field): Enter your phone number for further contact, especially if you need to discuss your request over phone.
