# software_company_responsive_contact_us_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software company responsive contact us form is a user-friendly form designed to allow visitors to get in touch with the company. It is intended for potential customers, current customers, or partners who want to ask questions, inquire about the company's products or services, or request additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Contact Name" field with your full name, as it will help the company's support team identify you more efficiently.
2. Enter your "Contact EMail" in the format of [name]-[company-domain]. This will ensure that the support team can respond to you quickly and correctly.
3. If you have a "Contact Phone" number, enter it in the format of international phone numbers (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
4. If you have a "Contact Company" name, enter it to provide more context about your affiliation.
5. For "Contact Job Position", enter your role within the company, such as Sales, Marketing, or Support.
6. In the "Contact Message" field, briefly describe the reason you are contacting us and what you are seeking.
7. The "Contact Message Details" field is optional and should be used for any additional information you'd like to provide about your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it will be displayed on your contact information.
* **Contact EMail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address in the format of [name]-[company-domain].
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, optional): Enter your affiliated company or organization name.
* **Contact Job Position** (`contact_job_position`, text, optional): Enter your job title or role within your organization.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your reason for contacting us.
* **Contact Message Details** (`contact_message_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about your message.
