# IP Backup Processing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The IP Backup Processing Survey is a form designed to collect information about your IP backup processes. It will help us understand your current backup procedures, allowing us to identify potential improvements and optimize our backup systems for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select a date for when the backup was performed or is scheduled to be performed.
2.  Choose the frequency at which you perform backups (e.g., how often).
3.  Select where the backups are stored (e.g., on-premises, off-premises, or cloud).
4.  Choose which days of the week you perform backups (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday).
5.  Select the type of backup process (e.g., manual or automated).
6.  List any teams involved in the backup process (e.g., Team A, Team B, or Team C).
7.  Choose how often backups are performed after recovery (e.g., how many days between recoveries).
8.  Add any additional notes about your backup process.
9.  Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11.  Select the person assigned to handle the backups (e.g., IT or Finance).
12. Select the person assigning the backups (e.g., Manager or Team Lead).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the backup was performed or is scheduled to be performed.
* **Backup Frequency** (`backup_frequency`, number, required): Select how often you perform backups (e.g., every day, week, etc.).
* **Backup Location** (`backup_location`, select_one, optional): Choose where the backups are stored (e.g., on-premises, off-premises, or cloud).
* **Backup Schedule** (`backup_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Select which days of the week you perform backups (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
* **Backup Type** (`backup_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of backup process (e.g., manual or automated).
* **Team Involved** (`team_involved`, select_multiple, optional): List any teams involved in the backup process (e.g., Team A, Team B, or Team C).
* **Recovery Frequency** (`recovery_frequency`, number, required): Choose how often backups are performed after recovery (e.g., every day, every week, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your backup process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to handle the backups (e.g., IT or Finance).
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, select_one, required): Select the person assigning the backups (e.g., Manager or Team Lead).
