# Membership Management System Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership Management System Audit Form is used to collect data for the regular audit of the Membership Management System. The information collected will be used to evaluate the system's performance and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the organization that uses the Membership Management System.
2. Choose the date of the audit.
3. Specify who conducted the audit (e.g., the person's name and title).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the system.
5. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization that uses the Membership Management System. This is the name of the company, institution, or entity that the MMS is serving.
* **Audit Date**: Select the date on which the audit was conducted. This date should be in a format that can be easily understood by your system, such as mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Audit By**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the audit and their title (e.g., John Smith, Manager). This will help identify who performed the audit and what their role was.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the Membership Management System. This is an opportunity to highlight areas of improvement or successes you've experienced with the system.
