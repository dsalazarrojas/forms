# Developer Authentication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developer Authentication Form is designed to collect necessary information from developers for authentication and authorization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title.
2. Enter your company name (if applicable).
3. Provide your name and email address.
4. Enter a secure password.
5. Select your access level (admin, moderator, or user).
6. Choose your approval status (approve, pending, or reject).
7. Add any additional notes (optional).
8. Enter your company URL (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Developer Authentication Form** (form_title, text, required): This is the title of the form, which should be filled out to identify the form's purpose.
* **Company** (company, text, required): Enter your company name if it applies to your authentication process. This field is required for internal tracking and verification.
* **Name** (name, text, required): This is your personal name that will be associated with your account.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and verification purposes. Ensure that it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Password** (password, text, required): Enter a secure password that meets the required standards. Please use a mix of characters, numbers, and symbols for maximum security.
* **Access Level** (access_level, select_one, required): Select your access level:
	+ **Admin**: Grants full access and control.
	+ **Moderator**: Provides limited access, but with some restrictions.
	+ **User**: Basic access with restricted permissions.
* **Approval** (approval, select_one, required): Choose your approval status:
	+ **Approve**: Indicate that you've been approved for access.
	+ **Pending**: Your request is pending, awaiting approval.
	+ **Reject**: Indicate that your request has been denied.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or context to your request.
* **Company Name** (company_name, text, optional): Enter your company's name (if different from your own name).
* **Company URL** (company_url, text, optional): Enter your company's URL (if applicable).

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Review the form's requirements and instructions before proceeding.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access the resources associated with this form.
