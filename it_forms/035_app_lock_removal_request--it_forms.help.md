# App Lock Removal Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The App Lock Removal Request form is used to request access to an app that has been locked or restricted on your company device. Please fill out this form to provide necessary information for the IT department to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be as detailed as possible in your response to the "Reason for Lock Removal Request" field.
3. If you have a manager's authorization code, please enter it in the "Manager Authorization Code" field.
4. Read and agree to the "I agree to follow corporate security protocols" section at the bottom of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Requestor (\*)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your company directory.
* **Employee or Student ID (\*)**: Enter your employee or student ID number as assigned to you by the company.
* **Department (\*)**: Enter the name of your department as it is listed in the company directory.
* **-- Device Information --**: This is a note field to provide information about your device, such as the type of device you are using (e.g. iPhone, Android Tablet, MacBook).
* **Device Type**: Select the type of device you are using from the dropdown menu.
* **Device Serial or IMEI Number**: Enter the serial or IMEI number of your device.
* **Name of Locked App (\*)**: Enter the name of the app that you are requesting access to.
* **Reason for Lock Removal Request (\*)**: Provide a detailed explanation as to why you need access to the locked app.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you are requesting access to the app.
* **Urgency of Request**: Select the urgency level of your request from the dropdown menu.
* **Manager Authorization Code (if applicable)**: If your manager has given you a code to enter here, please do so.
* **I agree to follow corporate security protocols**: This is a required field where you agree to follow company security protocols after the app has been unlocked.
* **IT Technician Notes (Internal Use Only)**: This is a field for the IT department to take notes on your request. Please do not enter any information here unless instructed to do so by the IT department.
