# onsite_service_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onsite_service_log_form is designed to record information about customer service interactions on-site, typically in a retail or hospitality setting. This form captures essential details about the customer's visit and is intended to improve customer service quality, facilitate data collection and analysis, and enhance communication among staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant fields to fill in according to your role and interaction with the customer.
2. Fill in the onsite_engineer field with the name of the staff member or engineer who interacted with the customer.
3. Enter the onsite_clinic field with the name of the clinic or department the customer visited.
4. Select the onsite_arrival field to enter the customer's arrival time using the provided date picker.
5. Select the onsite_departure field to enter the customer's departure time using the provided time picker.
6. In the onsite_actions_taken field, describe any actions taken during the customer's visit, such as providing assistance or resolving an issue.
7. If applicable, enter the customer's signature in the onsite_customer_signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **onsite_engineer (Text)**: Enter the name of the staff member or engineer who interacted with the customer.
- **onsite_clinic (Text)**: Enter the name of the clinic or department the customer visited.
- **onsite_arrival (Date)**: Select the customer's arrival time using the provided date picker.
- **onsite_departure (Time)**: Select the customer's departure time using the provided time picker.
- **onsite_actions_taken (Text)**: Describe any actions taken during the customer's visit, such as providing assistance or resolving an issue.
- **onsite_customer_signature (Text)**: If applicable, enter the customer's signature, as a confirmation of their visit.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete this form accurately and honestly to maintain high-quality records.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, consult with your supervisor or colleagues for guidance.
* Review and update your records regularly to ensure consistency and accuracy.
* Store the completed form in a secure location, as required by your organization's policies.
