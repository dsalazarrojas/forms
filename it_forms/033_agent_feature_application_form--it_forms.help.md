# Agent Feature Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for agents to submit feature requests to the development team. It collects relevant information to help prioritize and schedule feature development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and team or department in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief title and description of the feature you would like to see developed.
3. Describe the expected impact or metrics that will be improved by the feature.
4. Choose the priority level of the feature based on its importance to your team.
5. If applicable, include attachments or links to mockups or documentation related to the feature.
6. Enter the email address you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
7. If you have a preferred deadline for the feature, provide it in the format MM/DD/YY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (text, required): Enter your name to associate with the feature request.
* **Team or department** (text, optional): Enter the team or department requesting the feature.
* **Feature title** (text, required): Enter a brief title of the feature you're requesting.
* **Feature description** (text, required): Describe the feature and its functionality.
* **Business impact** (text, required): Explain the expected impact or metrics improved by the feature.
* **Priority level** (select one, required): Choose the priority level of the feature based on its importance to your team (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
* **Attachments** (text, optional): Attach any relevant mockups, documents, or links related to the feature.
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter the email address you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Preferred deadline** (date, optional): Enter the preferred target date for the feature's delivery (MM/DD/YY).

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and clear information to help the development team understand your feature request.
* Be specific about the expected impact or metrics improved by the feature.
* Use attachments or links to provide supporting evidence or examples of the feature.
* Check the required fields to ensure you have completed all the necessary information.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask someone from the development team for assistance.
