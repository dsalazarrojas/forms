# Business Intelligence Solutions Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information from potential clients or users seeking business intelligence solutions, such as data analysis, business analytics, or data visualization. It is designed to be a quick and easy way to share project details and requirements with the business intelligence solutions team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. In the first section, "Contact Request", provide a brief description of your project needs.
3. Enter your company name in the "Company name" field.
4. Enter the name and email of the person you would like to be contacted in the "Contact name" and "Contact email" fields, respectively.
5. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project summary" field.
6. If applicable, select the estimated start date for your project in the "Expected start date" field.
7. If you have an estimated budget for your project, select it from the "Budget range" options.
8. If you have any primary challenges you would like the business intelligence solutions team to address, provide them in the "Primary challenges" field.
9. Choose your preferred method of contact in the "Preferred contact method" field.
10. Finally, provide any additional information you would like to share with the business intelligence solutions team in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Contact Request --** (`header`): This section is a heading to introduce the form and its purpose.
* **Company name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of your organization or company seeking business intelligence solutions.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person to be contacted about your project.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the person to be contacted about your project.
* **Project summary** (`project_summary`): Provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Expected start date** (`expected_start_date`): If known, enter the expected start date for your project.
* **Budget range** (`budget_range`): Select an estimated budget for your project from the provided options.
* **Primary challenges** (`primary_challenges`): List any key challenges you want the business intelligence solutions team to address.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by email, phone, or video call.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`): Share any other relevant details about your project with the business intelligence solutions team.

## Tips

* Please answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible to help the business intelligence solutions team provide the best possible solutions for your project.
* You can attach any additional documents or files to your response if needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
