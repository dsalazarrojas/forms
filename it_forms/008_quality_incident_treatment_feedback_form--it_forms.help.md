# Quality Incident Treatment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users on the quality incident treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a description of the incident that you experienced.
2. Indicate whether the treatment was effective or not.
3. Choose how satisfied you were with the treatment.
4. Provide any additional comments about the incident.
5. Suggest any recommended next steps for the incident treatment process.
6. If the incident was handled by a specific tool, select the tool from the list.
7. Choose how you felt about the incident overall.
8. Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
9. Enter any additional input you have about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, Required): Enter a brief description of the incident you experienced.
* **Treatment Effectiveness** (`treatment_effectiveness`, select_multiple, Required): Select all that apply from the list of available options to indicate the effectiveness of the treatment.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, Required): Choose how satisfied you were with the treatment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Required): Provide any additional comments about the incident.
* **Recommended Next Steps** (`recommended_next_steps`, text, Optional): Suggest any changes that could be made to the incident treatment process.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, Optional): Select the tool that handled the incident from the list.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_one, Required): Choose how you feel about the incident overall.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, Required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, Required): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, Optional): Enter any additional input you have about the incident.

## Tips
- Please be specific when describing the incident to help us understand the situation better.
- Choose the options that best fit your experience.
- If you are not satisfied with the treatment, provide suggestions for improvement.
- Make sure to provide accurate dates and times when submitting this form.
