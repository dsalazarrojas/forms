# Bluetooth Device Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Bluetooth Device Registration form is designed to collect information about Bluetooth devices, including the device owner, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the device owner's name in the "Device owner name" field.
2. Select the type of Bluetooth device from the options provided in the "Device type" field.
3. Enter the device's MAC address and serial number (if available) in the "Device MAC address" and "Device serial number" fields, respectively.
4. Select the department to which the device is assigned in the "Department" field.
5. Enter the date the device was registered in the "Date registered" field.
6. Optionally, enter an internal asset tag for the device in the "Asset tag" field.
7. Provide any additional information about the device in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device owner name**: Enter the name of the employee or department responsible for the device. (Required)
* **Device type**: Select the type of Bluetooth device you are registering (e.g. Headset, Speaker, Keyboard, etc.). (Required)
* **Device MAC address**: Enter the device's MAC address for inventory purposes. (Required)
* **Device serial number**: Enter the manufacturer's serial number of the device, if available. (Optional)
* **Department**: Select the department to which the device is assigned. (Required)
* **Date registered**: Enter the date the device was registered. (Required)
* **Asset tag**: Enter any internal asset tag assigned to the device. (Optional)
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about the device, such as its purpose or any issues. (Optional)
