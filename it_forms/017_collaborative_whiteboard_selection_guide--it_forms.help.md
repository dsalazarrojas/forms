# Collaborative Whiteboard Selection Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you determine the needs and preferences for a whiteboard solution that fits your specific needs. It will guide you through a series of questions to understand your requirements and suggest the best whiteboard option for your team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your information, including your name, email, and department.
2. Answer the questions about your team size, primary use case, and other specific needs.
3. Select the collaboration features and drawing tools you require for your whiteboard.
4. Choose the integrations and platform you need for your whiteboard.
5. Select your storage and security needs.
6. Specify your board size preference and export format requirements.
7. Choose your template and budget needs.
8. Select your licensing preference and trial period needs.
9. Finally, answer the questions about your technical support and training needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name to help us understand who you are and what team you belong to.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your work email to receive any updates or notifications about your whiteboard selection.
* **Department** (Required): Specify your department to help us understand your team size and needs.
* **Team Size** (Required): Select the number of users in your team from the options 1-5 users, 6-10 users, 11-25 users, 26-50 users, 51-100 users, or Over 100 users.
* **Primary Use Case** (Required): Select the main purpose of your whiteboard, such as Brainstorming Sessions, Project Planning, or General Collaboration.
* **Use Case Details** (Required): Describe your specific needs and requirements for your whiteboard.
* **Required Collaboration Features** (Required): Select all the features you need for your whiteboard, such as Real-Time Multi-User Editing, Video Conferencing Integration, Chat Functionality, and others.
* **Drawing Tools Needed** (Required): Select all the drawing tools you need for your whiteboard, such as Freehand Drawing, Shapes and Lines, Sticky Notes, and others.
* **Required Integrations** (Required): Select all the integrations you need for your whiteboard, such as Microsoft Teams, Slack, Zoom, and others.
* **Platform Preference** (Required): Select the platforms where you will use your whiteboard, such as Web Browser, Windows Desktop, Mac Desktop, and others.
* **Security Requirements** (Required): Select all the security features you need for your whiteboard, such as SSO Single Sign-On, Two-Factor Authentication, Data Encryption, and others.
* **Storage Needs** (Required): Select the storage size you need for your whiteboard, such as Minimal Under 10GB, Moderate 10-50GB, and others.
* **Board Size Preference** (Required): Select the board size you prefer, such as Standard Fixed Size, Large Fixed Size, Infinite Canvas, and others.
* **Required Export Formats** (Required): Select all the export formats you need for your whiteboard, such as PDF, PNG Image, and others.
* **Template Needs** (Required): Select the templates you need for your whiteboard, such as Brainstorming Templates, Project Planning Templates, and others.
* **Budget Range** (Required): Select the annual budget per user from the options Free Only, Under $5, $5-10, and others.
* **Licensing Preference** (Required): Select how you would like to license your whiteboard, such as Free Tier, Individual License, Team License, and others.
* **Trial Period Needed** (Required): Select whether you need a trial period, a demo request, or are ready to purchase.
* **Technical Support Level Needed** (Required): Select the level of technical support you need, such as Self-Service Only, Email Support, and others.
* **Training Requirements** (Required): Select the training you need for your whiteboard, such as Video Tutorials, Documentation, and others.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (Required): Select all the accessibility features you need for your whiteboard, such as Screen Reader Compatible, Keyboard Navigation, and others.
* **Mobile Functionality Importance** (Required): Select the importance of mobile functionality for your whiteboard.
* **Offline Access Needed** (Required): Select whether you need offline access for your whiteboard.
* **Decision Timeline** (Required): Select the timeline for your decision.
* **Number of Decision Makers** (Required): Select the number of people who need to approve your whiteboard selection.
* **Additional Requirements** (Optional): Enter any other needs or requirements you have for your whiteboard.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). You can leave optional fields blank.
