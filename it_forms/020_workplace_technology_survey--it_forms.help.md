# workplace_technology_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your workplace technology advancements. This form is meant to be completed by end users to provide insights into their current state of technology in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select the "category" field and choose one of the three options provided: "IT Forms", "Other", or "None".
2. In the "description" field, provide a brief description of your workplace technology advancements.
3. In the "workplace_technology_survey_description" field, provide details about the status of your technological advancements in the workplace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **category**: (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes your workplace technology advancements. This will help us understand the context of your response.
- **description**: (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your workplace technology advancements. This will help us understand what kind of technology you are working with.
- **workplace_technology_survey_description**: (`workplace_technology_survey_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the status of your technological advancements in the workplace. This will help us understand the progress and development of your technology.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
- Make sure to choose the correct category for your response.
- You can provide any additional details in the "description" field if necessary.
- The more information you provide, the better we can understand your workplace technology advancements.
