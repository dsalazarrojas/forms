# Network Access Restrictions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experiences with the current network access restrictions. The purpose of this survey is to understand the impact of the restrictions on employee productivity, workflow, and overall work experience. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future network access restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select one or multiple options depending on the type of question.
2. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to provide additional comments or explanations in the space provided.
3. Please note that all questions are optional, and you can skip questions that don't apply to your situation.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
