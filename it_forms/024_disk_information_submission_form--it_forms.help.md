# Disk Information Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disk Information Submission Form is a tool used to collect and track information about disk drives. Fill out this form to report and manage disk drive details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the disk ID, which is a unique identifier for the disk drive.
2. Enter the serial number of the disk drive.
3. Select whether the disk drive is "Yes" or "No" regarding its status.
4. Select the status of the disk drive: "Active" or "Inactive".
5. Enter the capacity of the disk drive in a numerical value.
6. Enter the size of the disk drive in a numerical value.
7. Enter the model of the disk drive.
8. Enter the vendor of the disk drive.
9. Enter the location of the disk drive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **disk_id** (text): A unique identifier for the disk drive. Enter the actual disk ID as provided by the manufacturer or vendor.
* **disk_serial** (text): The serial number of the disk drive. Enter the serial number as printed on the disk or provided by the manufacturer.
* **disk_type** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the disk drive is active or inactive.
* **disk_status** (select_multiple): Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to describe the current status of the disk drive.
* **disk_capacity** (number): Enter the storage capacity of the disk drive in a numerical value.
* **disk_size** (number): Enter the physical size of the disk drive in a numerical value.
* **disk_model** (text): Enter the model of the disk drive, as provided by the manufacturer.
* **disk_vendor** (text): Enter the vendor of the disk drive, as provided by the manufacturer.
* **disk_location** (text): Enter the current location of the disk drive, such as the server room or rack number.
