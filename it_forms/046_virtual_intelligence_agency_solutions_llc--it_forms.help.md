# virtual_intelligence_agency_solutions_llc_it_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to capture information about IT forms submitted by users. It provides fields to record details about the form id, company name, description, assigned tool, category, date submitted, time submitted, email, phone number, note, form version, created by, modified by, created at, modified at, created by name, modified by name, form version id, assigned to, submitted by, submission date, form status, and form id.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form id**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
2. **Company name**: Enter the name of the company associated with the form.
3. **Description**: Enter a brief description of the form.
4. **Assigned tool**: Select the tool assigned to the form.
5. **Category**: Select the category for the form (e.g., IT Forms).
6. **Category**: Select additional categories for the form (e.g., Other).
7. **Date submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
8. **Time submitted**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
9. **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the form.
10. **Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the form.
11. **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the form.
12. **Form version**: Enter the version of the form.
13. **Created by**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
14. **Modified by**: Enter the name of the person who modified the form.
15. **Created at**: Enter the date the form was created.
16. **Modified at**: Enter the date the form was last modified.
17. **Created by name**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
18. **Modified by name**: Enter the name of the person who modified the form.
19. **Form version id**: Enter the unique identifier for the form version.
20. **Assigned to**: Enter the person assigned to the form.
21. **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
22. **Submission date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
23. **Form status**: Enter the status of the form.
24. **Form id**: Enter the unique identifier for the form.
25. **User**: Enter the name of the user submitting the form.

* **Form version** (`form_version`, number, required): Enter the version of the form.
* **Created by** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
