# Virus Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who need help with virus issues on their devices. It's designed to gather relevant information from users to facilitate effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, "User Information."
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
4. On the second page, "Contact Details," provide your contact information so we can reach out to you.
5. Click "Next" again to move to the last page.
6. On the "Device Information" page, provide details about the device(s) affected by the virus.
7. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)** (`text`, required): This page collects basic details about you, such as your name and email.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`text`, optional): This page asks for your contact information so we can reach out to you for further assistance.
* **Device Information (Device Information)** (`text`, required): This page is for providing details about the device(s) affected by the virus.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help while filling the form, feel free to reach out to our support team.
