# Security Priorities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Priorities Assessment form is designed to evaluate the security focus areas, readiness levels, and planning status of various teams or individuals. This assessment will help identify areas that require improvement and readiness levels to enhance overall security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the relevant options for each section.
2. Select the correct answers for the select_multiple and select_one fields.
3. Enter any additional information in the note fields if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk Focus Areas** (`risk_focus_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable focus areas for your team.
* **Readiness** (`readiness`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the current readiness level of your team for security.
* **Improvement Planning** (`improvement_planning`, note, required: false): Describe the improvement planning status of your team.
* **Security Team Members** (`security_team_members`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable team members involved in security.
* **Risk Focus Areas Awareness** (`risk_focus_areas_awareness`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose areas where your team is aware of the risks.
* **Risk Focus Areas Readiness** (`risk_focus_areas_readiness`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the current readiness level of your team for risk focus areas.
* **Improvement Planning Readiness** (`improvement_planning_readiness`, select_one, required: false): Describe the current readiness level of your team for improvement planning.
* **Security Planning** (`security_planning`, note, required: false): Enter any relevant information about security planning.
* **Security Readiness** (`security_readiness`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current readiness level of your team for security.
* **Improvement Planning Security Readiness** (`improvement_planning_security_readiness`, select_one, required: false): Describe the current readiness level of your team for improvement planning security.
* **Security Priorities** (`security_priorities`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable security priorities for your team.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant options for each field.
* Enter accurate and detailed information in the note fields.
* This form is meant to be completed by individuals or teams, so choose the options that best represent your team's status.
