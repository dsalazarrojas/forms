# Full Stack Development Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Full Stack Development Agreement Form" is a comprehensive form used to outline the terms and conditions of a project, ensuring all parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the project details section, including project name, description, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter the lead information, including contact details and any other relevant information.
3. Specify the deadline for the project, including the date and any other relevant deadlines.
4. Define the payment terms, including payment schedules and any other financial terms.
5. Update the project status, including any changes or updates.
6. Enter the approval details, including any necessary approvals or sign-offs.
7. Outline the agreement details, including any terms, conditions, or clauses.
8. Enter the agreement terms, including any relevant terms or conditions.
9. Add any comments or notes from the project manager.
10. Set the agreement date and status, including any relevant dates or statuses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details (project_details)**: Enter the project name and description to provide context for the agreement.
* **Lead Information (lead_info)**: Enter the contact details for the lead or responsible person, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Deadline (deadline)**: Specify the deadline for the project, including the date and any other relevant deadlines.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Define the payment terms, including payment schedules and any other financial terms.
* **Project Status (project_status)**: Update the project status, including any changes or updates.
* **Approval Details (approval_details)**: Enter any necessary approvals or sign-offs, including dates and times.
* **Agreement Details (agreement_details)**: Outline the agreement details, including terms, conditions, or clauses.
* **Agreement Terms (agreement_terms)**: Enter any relevant terms or conditions.
* **Comments (project_manager_comments)**: Add any comments or notes from the project manager.
* **Agreement Date (agreement_date)**: Set the agreement date and status, including any relevant dates or statuses.
* **Agreement Status 1 (agreement_status_1)**: Select the agreement status (Approved, Pending, Rejected) to update the agreement status.
* **Signature (signature)**: Enter your signature to confirm agreement.
* **Date (signature_date)**: Set the date of the signature.
* **Agreement Status 2 (agreement_status_2)**: Select the agreement status (Approved, Pending, Rejected) to confirm agreement status.
* **Additional fields are not used in this form.**
