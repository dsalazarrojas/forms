# security_risk_and_compliance_monitoring_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and monitor the security and compliance risks within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the risk level of the identified risk (Low, Moderate, or High).
2. Provide a brief description of the risk.
3. Select the frequency of the risk (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
4. Choose the type of risk impact (Financial, Data Security, or Regulatory).
5. Select the compliance status of the risk (Compliant or Non-Compliant).
6. Provide any remediation plan to mitigate the risk.
7. Choose the status of remediation progress (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
8. Describe next steps to be taken.
9. Select the risk and compliance team members responsible for the risk.
10. Enter the name and contact information of the risk and compliance owner.
11. Schedule reviews and updates of the risk at a frequency (Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Yearly).
12. Set the date for the next review.
13. Update the form with the current status and any attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. risk_level 
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, true): This field helps to categorize the risk into Low, Moderate, or High levels.
*   - Select the risk level as Low, Moderate, or High based on the identified risk.

### 3. risk_description 
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, `text`, true): Provide a brief description of the identified risk.

### 4. risk_frequency 
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, `select_multiple`, true): Choose the frequency of the risk (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).

### 5. risk_impact 
* **Risk Impact** (`risk_impact`, `select_one`, true): Select the type of risk impact (Financial, Data Security, or Regulatory).

### 6. compliance_status 
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, `select_one`, true): Choose the compliance status of the risk (Compliant or Non-Compliant).

### 7. remediation_plan 
* **Remediation Plan** (`remediation_plan`, `text`, false): Provide any remediation plan to mitigate the risk.

### 8. remediation_status 
* **Remediation Status** (`remediation_status`, `select_one`, true): Choose the status of remediation progress (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).

### 9. next_steps 
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, false): Describe next steps to be taken.

### 10. risk_and_compliance_team 
* **Risk and Compliance Team** (`risk_and_compliance_team`, `select_multiple`, true): Select the team members responsible for the risk (Security Team, Compliance Team, or Both).

### 11. risk_and_compliance_owner 
* **Risk and Compliance Owner** (`risk_and_compliance_owner`, `text`, true): Enter the name and contact information of the risk and compliance owner.

### 12. risk_and_compliance_email 
* **Risk and Compliance Email** (`risk_and_compliance_email`, `email`, true): Enter the email address of the risk and compliance owner.

### 13. risk_and_compliance_phone 
* **Risk and Compliance Phone** (`risk_and_compliance_phone`, `text`, true): Enter the phone number of the risk and compliance owner.

### 14. review_frequency 
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `select_multiple`, true): Choose the review frequency (Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Yearly).

### 15. review_date 
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, true): Set the date for the next review.

### 16. last_updated 
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, `date`, false): Update the form with the current status.

### 17. created_by 
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, false): Update the form with the name and contact information of the person who created the form.

### 18. last_reviewed 
* **Last Reviewed** (`last_reviewed`, `date`, false): Update the form with the date of the last review.

### 19. next_review_date 
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`, `date`, false): Set the date for the next review.

### 20. review_frequency_status 
* **Review Frequency Status** (`review_frequency_status`, `select_multiple`, true): Choose the review frequency status (On Time, Overdue).

### 21. notes 
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, false): Provide any additional information or notes about the risk.

### 22. attachments 
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, false): Upload any attachments related to the risk.

### 23. uploaded_by 
* **Uploaded By** (`uploaded_by`, `text`, false): Update the form with the name and contact information of the person who uploaded the attachment.

### 24. last_updated_by 
* **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, `text`, false): Update the form with the name and contact information of the person who last updated the form.

### 25. review_date 
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, true): This field is a duplicate of field 15.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" which means they need to be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
