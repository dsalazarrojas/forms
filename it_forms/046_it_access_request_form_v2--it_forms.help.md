# IT Access Request Form V2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Access Request Form V2 is a tool to help you submit access requests to the IT department. This form is designed to gather essential information from you to expedite the processing of your requests.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you have a valid reason for submitting this form and understand that your request will be reviewed by the IT department.
- Fill out all mandatory fields (identified by an asterisk \*) with accurate and truthful information.
- If you require access to multiple access requests, select all applicable options in the "Access Requests" field.
- Provide a detailed description of your request in the "Request Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name to help us get to know you better.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name to help us complete your record.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **User Phone Number** (`user_phone_number`, text, not required): If you have a phone number, enter it to help us contact you more easily.
* **Access Requests** (`access_requests`, select_one, required): Choose the access requests you require (e.g., "Access Request 1", "Access Request 2", etc.).
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required): Describe your request in detail, including any relevant context or deadlines. This is an essential field for us to understand and process your request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, text, not required): Please ignore this field, as it's not a real form field.
 
## Tips

- Please ensure your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any supporting documentation or files related to your request, attach them separately.
- Our IT team will review your request and contact you to discuss further details.
- You will receive an email confirmation once your request is received and processed.
