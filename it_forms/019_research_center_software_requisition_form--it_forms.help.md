# Research Center Software Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Center Software Requisition Form is designed to collect necessary information from requesters to support the evaluation and procurement process for software needs at the Research Center. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your software requisition is properly assessed and managed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read through the form carefully before filling it out.
- Enter your lab name and email address.
- Provide your phone number, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **lab_name** (lab_name, text, required): Enter the name of your lab or department. This helps us identify the specific group making the software requisition request.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter your official email address. This is the primary means of communication for software requisition updates and discussions.
* **phone_number** (phone number, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please provide it. This can be used as an alternative contact method.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us. 
- This form is intended to be completed once per software requisition request; please do not submit multiple requests for the same purpose.
