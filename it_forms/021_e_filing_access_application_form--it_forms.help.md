# e_filing_access_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a file or system, allowing users to provide necessary information for approval and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "User Details" field to enter your name, username, and any other identifying information.
2.  Select the "Request Date" field and enter the date on which you first requested access or the date you expect to use the file/system.
3.  Choose your role from the "User Role" dropdown menu, which may be "admin", "editor", or "user".
4.  Select the appropriate "Approval Level" for your request, which could be "high", "low", or "medium".
5.  Provide a brief description of why you need access to the file/system in the "Reason for Request" field.
6.  Select the department responsible for the file/system in the "Department" dropdown menu, which may be "IT", "Finance", "Sales", or "Marketing".
7.  Choose the status of your request in the "Manager Approval" dropdown menu, which should be "approved", "pending", or "denied".
8.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
9.  Enter your work email address in the "Email" field.
10.  Enter your work phone number in the "Phone" field.
11.  Enter the location of the file/system in the "File Location" field, which may be "local" or "remote".
12.  You may also need to upload a file to the system by providing the path to the file on your computer in the "File Path" field, or provide the name of the file in the "File Name" field.
13.  Enter the size of the file in kilobytes in the "File Size" field.
14.  Select the type of file in the "File Type" dropdown menu, which may be "pdf", "docx", or "excel".
15.  Select the format of the file in the "File Format" dropdown menu, which may be "csv" or "txt".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (text, not required): Enter your name, username, and any other identifying information.
* **Request Date** (date, not required): Enter the date on which you first requested access or the date you expect to use the file/system.
* **User Role** (select_one, not required): Choose your role, which may be "admin", "editor", or "user".
* **Approval Level** (select_multiple, not required): Select the approval level, which may be "high", "low", or "medium".
* **Reason for Request** (text, not required): Provide a brief description of why you need access to the file/system.
* **Department** (select_one, not required): Select the department responsible for the file/system, which may be "IT", "Finance", "Sales", or "Marketing".
* **Manager Approval** (select_one, required): Choose the status of your request, which should be "approved", "pending", or "denied".
* **Notes** (note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Email** (email, not required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (text, not required): Enter your work phone number.
* **File Path** (text, not required): Provide the path to the file on your computer.
* **File Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the file.
* **File Size** (number, not required): Enter the size of the file in kilobytes.
* **File Type** (select_multiple, not required): Select the type of file, which may be "pdf", "docx", or "excel".
* **File Format** (select_multiple, not required): Select the format of the file, which may be "csv" or "txt".
* **File Location** (select_multiple, not required): Select the location of the file, which may be "local" or "remote".
