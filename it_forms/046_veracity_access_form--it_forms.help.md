# Veracity Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veracity Access Form is designed to collect information about access requests for employees or contractors within a company or organization. This form helps gather the necessary details to grant or deny access based on the submitted information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Requester** field with the name of the person requesting access.
2. Provide the **Requested By** field with the name of the person requesting access on behalf of the person being accessed.
3. In the **Reason For Request** field, briefly explain the reason for the access request.
4. Choose the **Access Type** from the dropdown menu (Employee, Contractor, or Other) that best describes the type of access being requested.
5. Select the desired **Access Level** (Full Access, Limited Access, or No Access).
6. Enter the **Start Date** in YYYY-MM-DD format for when the access should begin.
7. Enter the **End Date** in YYYY-MM-DD format for when the access should end.
8. Add any additional **Notes** regarding the access request in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester**: Enter the name of the person requesting access.
* **Requested By**: Enter the name of the person requesting access on behalf of the person being accessed.
* **Reason For Request**: Briefly describe the reason for the access request.
* **Access Type**: Select the type of access being requested from the dropdown menu (Employee, Contractor, or Other).
* **Access Level**: Choose the desired access level (Full Access, Limited Access, or No Access).
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date in YYYY-MM-DD format for when the access should begin.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date in YYYY-MM-DD format for when the access should end.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments regarding the access request in this text area.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the access request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to your organization's access policies or seek assistance from an administrator.
