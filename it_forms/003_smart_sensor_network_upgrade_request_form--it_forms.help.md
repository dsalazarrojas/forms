# Smart Sensor Network Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Sensor Network Upgrade Request Form is designed to request a hardware upgrade or maintenance for a specific smart sensor. This form will help us track and manage such requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Assigned To" field to choose who will be responsible for the upgrade (IT, Facilities, or Engineering).
2. Enter the "Assigned Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. In the "Justification" field, briefly explain the reason for the upgrade.
4. Enter the "Impact" field with a numerical value indicating the upgrade's impact.
5. Choose the preferred "Timeline" for the upgrade.
6. In the "Requested By" field, enter the name of the person requesting the upgrade.
7. Enter the "Request Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Select the "Approved By" field to choose who will approve the upgrade (IT, Facilities, or Engineering).
9. Enter the "Approved Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upgrade Scope**: This field allows you to describe the specific scope of the upgrade.
* **Justification**: Briefly explain the reason for the upgrade.
* **Impact**: Enter a numerical value indicating the upgrade's impact.
* **Preferred Timelines**: Choose your preferred timeline for the upgrade.
* **Assigned To**: Select who will be responsible for the upgrade (IT, Facilities, or Engineering). This field is required.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date you would like the upgrade to be assigned to in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is required.
* **Requested By**: Enter the name of the person requesting the upgrade.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting the upgrade in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Approved By**: Select who will approve the upgrade (IT, Facilities, or Engineering). This field is required.
* **Approved Date**: Enter the date you would like the upgrade to be approved in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This field is required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Only submit this form for a genuine request.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the IT department.
