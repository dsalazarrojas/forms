# efin_software_access_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the efin software application. Please fill out the form below to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your employee ID in the `employee_id` field.
2. Enter your first name in the `employee_first_name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **efin_software_access_application_form** (`title`, text, required: false): This is the form title, please enter a brief description of your request for access to the efin software application.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, text, required: false): Enter your employee ID as issued by your HR department or other relevant identification number.
* **employee_first_name** (`employee_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out both fields completely and accurately.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact your HR department for assistance.
* This form will be reviewed and access will be granted or denied based on company policies and procedures.
