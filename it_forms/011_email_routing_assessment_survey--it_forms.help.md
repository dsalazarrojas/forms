# Email Routing Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users on the current email routing process. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions to help us understand how to improve the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required fields. You can add additional comments or pain points in the optional fields if you have more to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department that you are affiliated with (e.g., IT, Operations, Support, Finance).
* **Email Routing Process** (`email_routing_process`, number, required): Rate how often you use the current email routing process (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is "Rarely" and 5 is "Very Often").
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you experience issues with the email routing process (e.g., Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Very Often).
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the current email routing process (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is "Too slow" and 5 is "Very Often", but note the mismatch in options, let's assume it's 1-5 where 1 is "Too slow" and 5 is "Very Satisfied").
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the email routing process.
* **Pain Points** (`pain_points`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any pain points you experience with the email routing process (e.g., Too slow, Too fast, Confusion, Lack of information).
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, note, required): Suggest any improvements to the email routing process. This is an opportunity to provide concrete suggestions on how we can make the process more efficient and effective.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and as accurately as possible.
* Feel free to add additional comments or pain points if you have more to share.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations, please make them as concrete and actionable as possible.
