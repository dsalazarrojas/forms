# Telecommunications Service Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents related to telecommunications services. It helps in tracking and resolving issues in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Report Date
* Enter the date on which the incident occurred or is currently happening.

### 2. Customer Name
* Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account or the name of the staff member reporting the incident.

### 3. Contact Number
* Enter the contact number of the customer or the staff member reporting the incident.

### 4. Service Issue
* Describe what happened, including any error messages or symptoms experienced.

### 5. Affected Equipment
* Specify the model number or equipment ID of the affected device or system.

### 6. Affected Location
* Enter the address or location affected by the issue.

### 7. Description
* Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details.

### 8. Reportee
* Select whether the incident was reported by "Customer", "Staff", or "Other". If "Staff", enter the staff member's name in the next field.

### 9. Customer Name (if Staff reporting)
* If the incident was reported by a staff member, enter their name here.

### 10. Staff Name (if Customer reporting)
* If the incident was reported by a customer, enter the staff member's name here.

### 11. Submitter
* Select whether the incident was submitted by "Customer" or "Staff". If "Staff", enter your name in the next field.

### 12. Name (if Customer submitting)
* If the incident was submitted by a customer, enter your name here.

### 13. Email
* Enter your email address.

### 14. Phone
* Enter your contact phone number.

### 15. Status
* Select the status of the incident: "Open", "In Progress", "Resolved", or "Closed".

### 16. Category
* Select the category of the incident: "Network", "Service", "Hardware", "Software", or "Other".

### 17. Priority
* Select the priority level of the incident: "Low", "Medium", "High".

### 18. Type
* Select the type of incident: "Outage", "Faulty", or "Other".

### 19. Impact
* Describe the impact of the incident.

### 20. Resolution
* Provide a brief resolution of the issue, if known.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the incident.
* Attach any supporting documentation or evidence, if available.
* Ensure the incident status is updated correctly to reflect its current stage of resolution.
