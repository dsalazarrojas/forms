# Component Compatibility Verification Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request verification on a specific component's compatibility with a given system or environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and contact email address in the "Requester name" and "Contact email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide the model number or name of the component you are requesting verification for in the "Component model" field.
3. Specify the existing system specifications, such as CPU, RAM, and OS, in the "Existing system specifications" field.
4. Describe the compatibility question or issue you are experiencing in the "Compatibility concern" field.
5. If applicable, provide links or file references to any attached documents that may be relevant to your request in the "Attached documents" field.
6. Select the urgency level of your request from the options provided in the "Urgency" field.
7. Choose your preferred contact method (email or phone) in the "Preferred contact method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for technical follow-up.
* **Component model**: Provide the model number or name of the component you are requesting verification for.
* **Existing system specifications**: Describe the key specifications of your existing system, including CPU, RAM, and OS.
* **Compatibility concern**: Describe the compatibility question or issue you are experiencing.
* **Attached documents**: If relevant, provide links or file references to any attached documents that may be useful for verification.
* **Urgency**: Select the urgency level of your request, which will help determine the priority of your verification request.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose your preferred method of contact, either email or phone.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the verification request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any attached documents, provide links or file references to make it easier for the reviewer to access the information.
* Select the urgency level that best reflects the time-sensitive nature of your request.
