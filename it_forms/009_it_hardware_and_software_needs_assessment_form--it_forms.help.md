# IT Hardware And Software Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The IT Hardware And Software Needs Assessment Form is designed to gather information about the needs of different departments within an organization, specifically related to IT equipment and software.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely to ensure that your department's needs are accurately represented.
2. Select the relevant equipment and software that your department currently uses.
3. Indicate the specific location where your department is based.
4. Specify the department that you belong to.
5. If applicable, provide an estimate of the number of employees that require the equipment or software.
6. If applicable, provide an estimate of the budget for the requested equipment or software.
7. Provide any additional comments or requests that you may have.
8. Finally, indicate the date and time you completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)** (`General Information`, note, optional): This field is a note for you to provide any general information that you feel is relevant to your department's needs.
* **IT Equipment (2)** (`IT Equipment`, select multiple, optional): Select all the IT equipment that you currently use in your department. Examples include laptops, desktop computers, printers, and scanners.
* **Software Used (3)** (`Software Used`, select multiple, optional): Select all the software that your department currently uses. Examples include Google Chrome, Microsoft Office, and Adobe Acrobat.
* **Software Requests (4)** (`Software Requests`, select multiple, optional): Select all the software that your department needs, but does not currently use. Examples include Cloud Storage, Antivirus, and Microsoft Office.
* **Location (5)** (`Location`, select one, optional): Select where your department is based. Options include Office, Remote, and Home.
* **Department (6)** (`Department`, select one, optional): Select the department that you belong to. Options include IT, Marketing, Sales, and Other.
* **Employees (7)** (`Employees`, number, optional): If applicable, provide an estimate of the number of employees that require the equipment or software.
* **Budget (8)** (`Budget`, number, optional): If applicable, provide an estimate of the budget for the requested equipment or software.
* **Comments (9)** (`Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests that you may have.
* **Date (10)** (`Date`, date, optional): Indicate the date you completed the form.
* **Time (11)** (`Time`, time, optional): Indicate the time you completed the form.
* **Form Completed By (12)** (`Form Completed By`, text, optional): Indicate who completed the form.
* **Department Requests (13)** (`Department Requests`, select multiple, optional): If your department has specific requests, select all the categories that apply. Examples include Hardware, Software, and IT Services.
