# Multi Factor Authentication Opt Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users opt out of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), a security feature that requires users to provide additional verification factors beyond just a username and password to access certain systems or applications. MFA adds an extra layer of security, but may be inconvenient or unnecessary for some users. This form allows users to provide reasons for opting out and acknowledge the risks associated with disabling MFA.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Details** page with your user information.
2. In the **Reason for Opting Out** field, provide a brief explanation for why you would like to opt out of MFA.
3. Select the level of risk you are willing to take by choosing from the **Risk Acknowledgments** dropdown menu. You may choose **Low Risk**, **Medium Risk**, or **High Risk**.
4. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the **Notes** field.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete the opt-out process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: This field is where you enter your basic user information, such as your name and username (if not already logged in).
* **Reason for Opting Out**: This field allows you to explain why you want to opt out of MFA. Please be brief and clear in your explanation.
* **Risk Acknowledgments**: This field allows you to acknowledge the risks associated with disabling MFA. Select the level of risk you are willing to take:
	+ **Low Risk**: Selecting this option means you are aware of the risks of disabling MFA and are willing to accept them. 
	+ **Medium Risk**: Selecting this option means you are aware of the risks of disabling MFA and are willing to take a moderate level of risk.
	+ **High Risk**: Selecting this option means you are aware of the risks of disabling MFA and are willing to take a high level of risk.
* **Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or notes about your decision to opt out of MFA.
* **Risk Acknowledgments 2, 3, and 4**: These fields are similar to **Risk Acknowledgments**, but allow you to select multiple risks or enter a free text response.
