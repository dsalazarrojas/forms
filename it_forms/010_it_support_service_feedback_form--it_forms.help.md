# it_support_service_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The it_support_service_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from users about the IT support service they received. This form helps the support team to understand what they did well and what they could improve on for future services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply to describe your experience with the IT support service you received.
	+ Yes: You received a positive experience with the service.
	+ No: You received a negative experience with the service.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of service from 1 to 5.
* **Service Availability** (`service_availability`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you were able to access the service at the scheduled time.
	+ Yes: You were able to access the service at the scheduled time.
	+ No: You were not able to access the service at the scheduled time.
* **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, `text`, required): Describe any technical issues you experienced.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply to describe the communication skills of the support team.
	+ Yes: The support team communicated effectively with you.
	+ No: The support team did not communicate effectively with you.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have for the support team.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool that was assigned to you.
	+ chatjimmy: This tool was assigned to you.
	+ Other: Other tool was assigned to you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you back.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of your feedback.
	+ IT Forms: This is a feedback form.
	+ Other: Other category.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Select who submitted this form.
	+ user1: This user submitted this form.
	+ user2: This user submitted this form.
	+ user3: This user submitted this form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you submitted this form.

Note: All fields with a `*` symbol are required fields.
