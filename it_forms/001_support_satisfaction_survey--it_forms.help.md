# Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our support team. The information collected will help us understand how well we're meeting your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant fields with your feedback and experiences.
2. Choose the correct options for the "Resolution Method" field, as it is a multiple-choice question.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
4. If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Support Rating
* **Customer Support Rating (customer_support_rating)**: Rate your overall experience with our support team, on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

* **Satisfaction With Agent (satisfaction_with_agent)**: Rate how satisfied you are with the agent who handled your support issue, on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### Resolution Time
* **Resolution Time (resolution_time)**: Estimate the amount of time it took to resolve your support issue.

### Resolution Method
* **Resolution Method (resolution_method)**: Choose how your support issue was resolved (Yes, No).

### Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience (overall_experience)**: Rate your overall experience with our support team, on a scale of 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### Feedback
* **Feedback (feedback)**: Enter your thoughts about your experience with our support team.

### Contact Information
* **Email (support_satisfaction_survey_form_email)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone (support_satisfaction_survey_form_phone)**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like us to contact you about your feedback.
* **Comments (support_satisfaction_survey_form_comments)**: Enter any additional comments or concerns you have about your experience.
* **Additional Comments (support_satisfaction_survey_form_additional_comments)**: Enter any other comments or suggestions you have for us to improve our support services.

Note: All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the better we can understand your experience and improve our services.
