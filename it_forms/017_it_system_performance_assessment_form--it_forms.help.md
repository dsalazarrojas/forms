# IT System Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT System Performance Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the performance of systems or systems' users. This form helps IT personnel or management to gather information about the systems' performance, which can be used to identify areas for improvement and optimize system functioning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **first name** in the "First name" field (id: 1). This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (id: 2). This is a required field.
3. Provide the system's name in the "System name" field (id: 3). This is a required field.
4. Enter your location in the "Location" field (id: 4). This is a required field.
5. Select your performance level by choosing one of the options in the "Performance level" field (id: 5). Options are:
	* High
	* Low
6. For the "Select multiple" field (id: 6), choose one or multiple options from:
	* Yes
	* No
7. If applicable, enter the date and time in the "Date and time" field (id: 7). This field is not required.
8. Enter the time in the "Time input" field (id: 8). This field is not required.
9. If you have any notes or comments, enter them in the "Note" field (id: 9). This field is not required.
10. The remaining fields are duplicates of the above fields, so you can simply follow the same steps for:
	* "Email" (id: 10, 17, 19)
	* "User input" (id: 11, 20, 22)
	* "Select multiple" (id: 13, 22, 24)
	* "Date and time" (id: 14, 23)
	* "Time input" (id: 15, 24)
	* "Note" (id: 16, 18, 25)
	* "System name" (id: 17, 22, 23)
