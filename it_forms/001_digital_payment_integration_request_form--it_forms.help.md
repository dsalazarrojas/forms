# Digital Payment Integration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for digital payment integration into your system. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide you with the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of digital payment integration you require.
2. Fill in any relevant account information (if applicable).
3. Provide your preferred start and end dates for integration.
4. Specify your preferred payment method(s).
5. Enter the amount you wish to process.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
7. If you have an account number, please enter it.
8. If you have a bank transit number, please enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Integration Type** (`integration_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of digital payment integration you require. Choose from:
	+ Stripe
	+ PayPal
	+ Square
	+ Braintree
	+ Authorize.net
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, text, optional): Enter your account name (if applicable).
* **Merchant ID** (`merchant_id`, number, optional): Enter your merchant ID (if applicable).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for integration.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for integration.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred payment method(s). Choose from:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Digital Wallet
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to process.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter your account number (if applicable).
* **Bank Transit** (`bank_transit`, text, optional): Enter your bank transit number (if applicable).

**Tips**: Please ensure that you have provided all relevant information to avoid delays in processing your request. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
