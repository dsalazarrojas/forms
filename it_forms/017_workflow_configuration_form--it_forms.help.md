# Workflow Configuration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the essential details and attributes of a workflow configuration, providing a structured way to document and analyze the workflow's status, type, and other relevant settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant information for each field as prompted.
2. Ensure accuracy and consistency in your input, especially for fields with specific types, such as dates or numbers.
3. If a field has a specific type, ensure you enter it correctly to avoid errors.
4. Save your progress as you fill out the form to avoid losing information.
