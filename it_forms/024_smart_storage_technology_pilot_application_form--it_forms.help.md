# Smart Storage Technology Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Storage Technology Pilot Application Form is designed to help organizations and individuals submit applications for the Smart Storage Technology Pilot program. This form will gather necessary information about your project, allowing our team to review and assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and accurate manner.
3. Provide detailed and concise answers for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for this project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your project in 200-300 words.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the physical location of your project.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): List the names and titles of team members involved in the project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Enter the estimated budget for your project.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, number, optional): Enter the project duration in months.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Storage, Data, Both.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Archived.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form.
