# it_support_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to assess the knowledge and experience of IT support personnel in troubleshooting and resolving general issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Provide detailed and specific information in the text fields.
3. Complete all fields as required, as indicated by the label (required/optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Troubleshooting Knowledge** (`general_troubleshooting_knowledge`, Text, Optional):
	+ A brief description of general troubleshooting knowledge and experience.
* **Hardware Components** (`hardware_components`, Text, Optional):
	+ List the types of hardware components you are familiar with.
* **Software Components** (`software_components`, Text, Optional):
	+ List the types of software components you are familiar with.
* **Troubleshooting Procedure** (`troubleshooting_procedure`, Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your typical troubleshooting process for general issues.
* **Hardware Problems** (`hardware_problems`, Text, Optional):
	+ List specific types of hardware problems you have encountered.
* **Software Problems** (`software_problems`, Text, Optional):
	+ List specific types of software problems you have encountered.
* **Hardware Troubleshooting** (`hardware_troubleshooting`, Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your experience with hardware troubleshooting.
* **Software Troubleshooting** (`software_troubleshooting`, Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your experience with software troubleshooting.
* **Confidence in Knowledge** (`confidence_in_knowledge`, Text, Optional):
	+ Rate your confidence level in your troubleshooting knowledge (1-10).
* **Confidence in Procedure** (`confidence_in_procedure`, Text, Optional):
	+ Rate your confidence level in your troubleshooting procedure (1-10).
* **Experience** (`experience`, Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your overall experience in IT support.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure or lack experience in a particular area, it's okay, just indicate so.
* This form is designed to gauge your knowledge and experience, not to assess your worth as an IT support person.
