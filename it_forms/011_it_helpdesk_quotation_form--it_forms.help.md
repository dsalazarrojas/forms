This form is used for managing quotations for services provided by the help desk team. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the customer, service details, and other relevant data to create a quotation. This form is typically used by the help desk team to respond to customer inquiries, track customer information, and create a record of services provided. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the quotation is correctly generated.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Customer Information section, where you can input the customer's name, email address, phone number, and other relevant details.
2.  In the Service Details section, provide the description of the service, service price, service duration, start date, and start time.
3.  In the Quotation Terms section, choose whether the quotation includes specific terms and enter any additional notes for these terms.
4.  In the Quotation Terms Options section, select the applicable options from the provided choices.
5.  In the Assignee section, select the person assigned to serve the customer.
6.  In the Customer Service Type section, select the type of service provided to the customer.
7.  In the Customer Notes section, enter any additional notes about the customer.
