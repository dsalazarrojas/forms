# IT Helpdesk Efficiency Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for evaluating the efficiency of the IT helpdesk services. It will help us measure the time taken to respond to tickets, the quality of resolution, and user satisfaction. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about your experience with our IT helpdesk services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields (marked as "Required")
- Select a category from the "Category" field
- Enter a brief description of your experience with our IT helpdesk services
- Provide response times for the tickets you have submitted
- Rate the resolution quality and user satisfaction for each ticket
- Select the tool used to resolve the issue (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "other")
- Provide any additional information or comments (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, Required): A brief title for your report.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, Required): Select the category of your experience (e.g., "IT Forms", "IT Reports", "IT Dashboards").
* **Description** (`description`, text, Optional): A brief description of your experience with our IT helpdesk services.
* **Response Times** (`response_times`, number, Optional): Enter the average response time taken to resolve your issue.
* **Resolution Rates** (`resolution_rates`, number, Optional): Rate the quality of resolution for each issue (e.g., 1-10).
* **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`, select_multiple, Optional): Rate your user satisfaction (e.g., "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor").
* **Date** (`date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Time** (`time`, time, Optional): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, Required): Select the tool used to resolve the issue (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "other").
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, Optional): Provide the output file name (if applicable).
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, Optional): Enter any relevant IDs associated with the form (if applicable).
* **Form 2 IDs** (`form_ids_2`, text, Optional): Enter any additional IDs associated with the form (if applicable).
* **Form 3 IDs** (`form_ids_3`, text, Optional): Enter any additional IDs associated with the form (if applicable).
* **Form 4 IDs** (`form_ids_4`, text, Optional): Enter any additional IDs associated with the form (if applicable).
* **Form 5 IDs** (`form_ids_5`, text, Optional): Enter any additional IDs associated with the form (if applicable).
