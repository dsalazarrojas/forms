# Cloud Adoption Readiness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloud Adoption Readiness Questionnaire is a tool designed to help organizations evaluate their current cloud adoption state and identify areas for improvement. It's a comprehensive assessment that covers various aspects of cloud adoption, from infrastructure and services to organizational readiness and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and concisely, using the provided options as a guide. Each section is designed to assess a specific aspect of your organization's cloud adoption. Please take your time, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if you're unsure about any question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your organization's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Respondent Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, as the person completing this form.
* **Job Title** (`4`, `text`, required): Please enter your job title or role within the organization.
* **Email Address** (`5`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address, which will be used for communication and follow-up questions.
* **Organization Size - Employees** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Please select the number of employees in your organization.
	+ 1-50: Small organization with fewer than 50 employees.
	+ 51-250: Small-to-medium organization with 51-250 employees.
	+ 251-1000: Large organization with 251-1000 employees.
	+ 1001-5000: Enterprise organization with 1001-5000 employees.
	+ 5000+: Very large organization with more than 5000 employees.
* **Industry - Sector** (`7`, `text`, required): Please enter the industry or sector your organization operates in.
* **Percentage of infrastructure currently in cloud** (`9`, `number`, required): Please enter the percentage of your organization's infrastructure currently hosted in the cloud (e.g., 0% for no cloud use, 100% for all cloud use).
* **Current primary data center location** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Please select where your organization's primary data center is currently located.
	+ On-premises: Data center located within your organization's physical premises.
	+ Private cloud: Data center hosted privately for your organization.
	+ Public cloud: Data center hosted publicly (e.g., AWS, Azure).
	+ Hybrid: Combination of on-premises and public cloud.
	+ Other: Other (please specify).
* **Cloud services currently in use** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the cloud services your organization is currently using.
	+ SaaS applications: Software as a Service (e.g., Office 365, Salesforce).
	+ IaaS infrastructure: Infrastructure as a Service (e.g., AWS EC2, Google Compute Engine).
	+ PaaS platforms: Platform as a Service (e.g., Heroku, Google App Engine).
	+ Serverless: Serverless computing (e.g., AWS Lambda, Google Cloud Functions).
	+ None yet: No cloud services currently used.
* **Primary goals for cloud adoption** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the primary goals for your organization's cloud adoption.
	+ Cost reduction: Reduce IT costs.
	+ Scalability: Enhance scalability and flexibility.
	+ Flexibility: Improve flexibility and agility.
	+ Innovation: Encourage innovation and experimentation.
	+ Disaster recovery: Ensure business continuity and disaster recovery.
	+ Compliance: Ensure regulatory compliance.
* **Target cloud provider or platform** (`14`, `text`, required): Please enter the target cloud provider or platform (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
* **Desired cloud adoption timeline** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Please select the desired timeline for your organization's cloud adoption.
	+ Less than 6 months: Short-term goal.
	+ 6-12 months: Medium-term goal.
	+ 1-2 years: Long-term goal.
	+ 2+ years: Extended long-term goal.
	+ TBD: To be determined.
* **Current cloud skill level in organization** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Please select your organization's current cloud skill level.
	+ Advanced: Expert-level cloud skills and knowledge.
	+ Intermediate: Basic to intermediate-level cloud skills and knowledge.
	+ Basic: Entry-level cloud skills and knowledge.
	+ Minimal: Limited cloud skills and knowledge.
	+ None: No cloud skills and knowledge.
* **Training needed for cloud adoption** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Please select the level of training needed for your organization's cloud adoption.
	+ Extensive: Requires significant training and education.
	+ Significant: Requires substantial training and education.
	+ Moderate: Requires moderate training and education.
	+ Minimal: Requires minimal training and education.
	+ None: No training needed.
* **Level of executive support for cloud initiatives** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Please select the level of executive support for your organization's cloud initiatives.
	+ Strong commitment: Executives are fully supportive and committed.
	+ Good support: Executives are generally supportive but may require persuasion.
	+ Neutral: Executives are neutral or indifferent.
	+ Some resistance: Executives are resistant or skeptical.
	+ Strong resistance: Executives strongly oppose cloud initiatives.
* **Applicable compliance requirements** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the applicable compliance requirements for your organization.
	+ GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation.
	+ HIPAA: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.
	+ PCI-DSS: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard.
	+ SOC 2: Security of Customer Data.
	+ Industry-specific: Other industry-specific compliance requirements.
	+ None: No compliance requirements.
* **Main concerns about cloud adoption** (`22`, `text`, required): Please enter your main concerns about cloud adoption.
* **Overall cloud readiness rating** (`23`, `number`, required): Please rate your organization's overall cloud readiness on a scale of 1 (not ready) to 5 (very ready).

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question honestly and concisely.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
- Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- The results will provide an indicative assessment of your organization's cloud adoption readiness.
- Please use this assessment as a starting point for further discussions and decision-making within your organization.
