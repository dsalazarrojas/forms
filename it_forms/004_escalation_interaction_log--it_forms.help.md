# escalation_interaction_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The escalation_interaction_log form is used to document customer interactions and escalations. It captures key details about the customer, the escalation reason, and the actions taken to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's **Customer Number** to quickly identify the customer.
2. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Escalation Reason** to indicate whether the interaction was an escalation.
3. Enter the **What Happened** section to describe the customer's experience or issue.
4. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Customer Rep** to indicate if a customer representative was involved in the interaction.
5. Enter the **Customer Rep Email** and **Customer Rep Phone** fields if applicable.
6. In the **Customer Notes** field, enter any relevant notes or comments from the customer.
7. In the **Tech Notes** field, enter any technical notes or details about the issue.
8. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Action Taken** to indicate if any actions were taken to resolve the issue.
9. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Outcome** to indicate the outcome of the actions taken.
10. In the **Action Description** field, enter a brief description of the actions taken.
11. Enter any additional **Action Outcome Notes** if necessary.
12. In the **Follow-up Next Steps** field, outline any next steps or follow-up actions that need to be taken.
13. Enter any **Next Steps** if applicable.
14. Enter **Follow-up Notes** if necessary.
15. Enter **Next Actions** 1-5 fields to capture additional next actions or steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Number** (`customer_number`, number, required): Enter the customer's unique number for easy identification.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Escalation Reason** (`escalation_reason`, select_one, required): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether the interaction was an escalation.
* **What Happened** (`what_happened`, number, required): Enter a brief description of the customer's experience or issue.
* **Customer Rep** (`customer_rep`, select_one, required): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if a customer representative was involved in the interaction.
* **Customer Rep Email** (`customer_rep_email`, email, required): Enter the customer representative's email address.
* **Customer Rep Phone** (`customer_rep_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer representative's phone number.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required): Enter any relevant notes or comments from the customer.
* **Tech Notes** (`tech_notes`, text, required): Enter any technical notes or details about the issue.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select_multiple, required): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if any actions were taken to resolve the issue.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, select_one, required): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate the outcome of the actions taken.
* **Action Description** (`action_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the actions taken.
* **Action Outcome Notes** (`action_outcome_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the outcome.
* **Follow-up Next Steps** (`follow_up_next_steps`, text, required): Outline any next steps or follow-up actions that need to be taken.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, number, required): Enter any additional next steps.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional follow-up notes or comments.
* **Next Actions** 1-5 (`next_actions_1`, `next_actions_2`, `next_actions_3`, `next_actions_4`, `next_actions_5`, text, required): Enter additional next actions or steps.

## Tips

* Be sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you've already submitted an entry for the same customer interaction, update that entry instead of creating a new one.
* Use this form consistently to maintain a clear and organized record of customer interactions and escalations.
