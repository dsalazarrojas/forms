# Autodialer Schedule Setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Autodialer Schedule Setup form is used to configure and manage your autodialer campaigns, allowing you to set up automatic calls to customers, prospects, or leads as part of a marketing campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Campaign Information**: Enter the basic details of your campaign, including name, description, and type.
2. **Call Settings**: Configure the call settings, including start and end dates, daily start and end times, time zone, and other relevant details.
3. **Reporting Preferences**: Select your reporting preferences, including the frequency and recipients of reports.
4. **Campaign Configuration**: Complete the campaign configuration, including contact list, list name, list source, and other details.
5. **Approval and Submission**: Review your submission for accuracy and completeness, and submit it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, required): Enter a name for your campaign.
* **Campaign Description** (`campaign_description`, required): Enter a brief description of your campaign.
* **Campaign Type** (`campaign_type`, required): Select the type of campaign (e.g., Sales, Customer Service, Survey, etc.).
* **Campaign Priority** (`campaign_priority`, required): Set the priority level of your campaign (e.g., Low, Normal, High, Urgent).
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Select your department (e.g., Sales, Customer Service, Support, etc.).
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, optional): Enter your team name.
* **Schedule Information** (`schedule_info`, optional): Enter any additional schedule information.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, required): Enter the start date of your campaign.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, optional): Enter the end date of your campaign.
* **Daily Start Time** (`start_time`, required): Enter the daily start time of your campaign.
* **Daily End Time** (`end_time`, required): Enter the daily end time of your campaign.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, required): Select your time zone.
* **Days of Week** (`days_of_week`, required): Select the days of the week you want to call.
* **Exclude Holidays** (`exclude_holidays`, required): Select whether to exclude holidays.
* **Maximum Calls Per Hour** (`max_calls_per_hour`, optional): Set the maximum number of calls per hour.
* **Maximum Agents** (`max_agents`, optional): Set the maximum number of agents.
* **Max Calls Per Day** (`max_calls_per_day`, optional): Set the maximum number of calls per day.
* **Retry Attempts** (`retry_attempts`, required): Select the number of retry attempts.
* **Retry Interval** (`retry_interval`, optional): Select the time between retries.
* **Target Abandon Rate** (`call_abandon_rate`, required): Set the target abandon rate.
* **Contact List** (`contact_list`, optional): Enter your contact list details.
* **List Name** (`list_name`, required): Enter the name of your list.
* **List Source** (`list_source`, required): Select where your list is from (e.g., CRM, Manual Upload, API Integration, etc.).
* **Total Contacts** (`total_contacts`, optional): Enter the total number of contacts.
* **List Upload Date** (`list_upload_date`, optional): Enter the date your list was uploaded.
* **Data Compliance** (`data_compliance`, required): Select your data compliance status.
* **Script Information** (`script_info`, optional): Enter any additional script information.
* **Script Name** (`script_name`, required): Select the name of your script.
* **Script Approved** (`script_approved`, required): Select whether your script is approved.
* **Voicemail Detection** (`voicemail_detection`, required): Select whether to enable voicemail detection.
* **Voicemail Action** (`voicemail_action`, required): Select what to do on voicemail.
* **Agent Information** (`agent_info`, optional): Enter any additional agent information.
* **Agent Assignment** (`agent_assignment`, required): Select how to assign agents.
* **Specific Agents** (`specific_agents`, optional): Enter the names of specific agents.
* **Maximum Agents** (`max_agents`, optional): Set the maximum number of agents.
* **Reporting** (`reporting`, optional): Enter any additional reporting preferences.
* **Report Frequency** (`report_frequency`, required): Select the frequency of reports (e.g., Real-Time, Daily, Weekly).
* **Report Recipients** (`report_recipients`, optional): Enter the recipients of your reports.
* **Key Metrics to Track** (`kpis`, optional): Select the key metrics to track (e.g., Call Volume, Connection Rate, etc.).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Approval Required** (`approval_needed`, required): Select whether this submission requires approval.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, required): Enter your digital signature.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required): Enter the submission date.

## Tips
