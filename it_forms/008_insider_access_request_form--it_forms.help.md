# Insider Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and reasons for access requests from individuals seeking insider access to certain areas or resources within an organization. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details for review and approval of these requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the first field.
2. Share your reasons for requesting insider access in the second field.
3. Select your preferred timing for the access request in the third field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): 
This field is where you can provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details. Your contact information will be used to facilitate communication and processing of your request.

### 2. **Reasons for Access** (`reasons_for_access`, `text`, optional): 
Please provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting insider access. This could include information about your role, purpose, or specific requirements.

### 3. **Preferred Timing** (`preferred_timing`, `time`, optional): 
Here, you can select a suitable time frame for when you would like to have access. You can choose a specific date and time, or select from the available options to indicate your preferred timing.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when providing your contact and reasons for access details.
* Choose a realistic timing that allows for a smooth processing and approval of your request.
