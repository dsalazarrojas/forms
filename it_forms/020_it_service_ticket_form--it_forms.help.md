# it_service_ticket_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting help with IT services. Please use it to describe your issue and provide relevant details so that our team can assist you effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are filling out a new ticket and not duplicating a previous issue.
2. Fill in your email address in the "Requestor's Email" field.
3. Choose the current status of the ticket from the "Ticket Status" dropdown.
4. Select the priority of the ticket from the "Ticket Priority" dropdown.
5. Enter the due date for the ticket in the "Due Date" field.
6. Select when the ticket was created from the "Creation Date" dropdown.
7. Choose whether the ticket has been assigned to someone in the "Assigned To" field.
8. Select when the ticket was last updated in the "Update Date" field.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about the issue in the "Notes" field.
10. Indicate whether the ticket relates to a specific tool in the "Tool Type" field.
11. Choose which tool is related to the ticket from the "Tool" dropdown.
12. If the ticket is related to a specific tool, enter the tool's name in the "Tool Name" field.
13. Enter a brief description of the issue in the "Description" field.
14. Select the due time for the ticket in the "Due Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requestor's Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your ticket.
- **Ticket Status** (select_one, required): Choose the current status of your ticket, whether it's Open, In Progress, Closed, or Waiting On.
- **Ticket Priority** (select_one, required): Select the priority of your ticket, whether it's High, Low, or Medium.
- **Due Date** (date, required): Enter the date by which you need the ticket resolved.
- **Creation Date** (time, required): Select when the ticket was created.
- **Assigned To** (select_one, required): Choose whether the ticket has been assigned to someone.
- **Update Date** (time, required): Select when the ticket was last updated.
- **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information about the issue in this field.
- **Tool Type** (select_one, required): Indicate whether the ticket is related to a specific tool.
- **Tool** (select_one, required): Choose which tool is related to the ticket.
- **Tool Name** (text, optional): If the ticket is related to a tool, enter the tool's name.
- **Description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the issue.
- **Due Time** (time, required): Select the due time for the ticket.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our IT support team for assistance.
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
