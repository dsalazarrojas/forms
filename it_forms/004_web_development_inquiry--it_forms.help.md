# web_development_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their web development needs and preferences. It will help the development team create tailored responses to meet these requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, which is a required field.
2. Provide your Email address if you'd like to be contacted.
3. Enter your Phone number for any necessary follow-up communication.
4. If you have a Company Name, please enter it here.
5. If you have a Website, please enter it here.
6. Choose the type of Services you are looking for (select one).
7. Select the areas of Expertise you possess (select multiple).
8. Enter the Date you are available to start your project.
9. You can also choose a Time for your availability.
10. Finally, enter any additional Notes you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: A required text field where you can enter your name.
- **Email**: An optional text field where you can provide your email address.
- **Phone**: A required text field for follow-up communication.
- **Company Name**: An optional text field for your company name.
- **Website**: An optional text field for your website URL.
- **Services**: A select_one field where you can choose one of the provided options.
- **Expertise**: A select_multiple field where you can choose multiple options.
- **Date**: A required date field for project start date availability.
- **Time**: An optional time field for availability.
- **Note**: An optional note field for any additional information.
