# Moderator Application Form For Chat - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to apply to become moderators for the chat forum. Moderators are responsible for maintaining the community's rules and providing a positive user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Select your job title**: Choose one of the three options provided, indicating your role within the chat forum. This helps us understand your position and responsibilities.
2.  **Enter your name**: Provide your full name, as you would like it to appear in our chat forum.
3.  **Enter your email address**: Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you. This should be a working email address that you check regularly.
4.  **Enter your phone number**: This is an optional field, but providing your phone number can help us reach you if needed.
5.  **Enter your bio**: This is an optional field, but it helps us understand a bit more about you and your experience with our chat forum.
6.  **Explain why you want to be a moderator**: Share your motivation and reasons for applying to be a moderator. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your fit for the role.
