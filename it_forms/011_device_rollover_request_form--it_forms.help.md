# Device Rollover Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Device Rollover Request Form is used by employees to submit a request for a device rollover, which involves replacing an old device with a new one. This form helps the manager or IT manager review and approve the request, ensuring that the necessary information is collected and processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your employee name in the **Employee Name** field.
3.  Select the correct **Role** for your device from the options provided.
4.  Choose the correct **Device Type** for your device from the options provided.
5.  Enter the **Replacement Reason** for the rollover in the text field.
6.  Enter the **Serial Number** of the device to be replaced.
7.  Enter the **Purchase Date** of the device, if applicable.
8.  Enter the **Purchase Cost** of the device, if applicable.
9.  Select the current **Asset Status** of the device from the options provided.
10.  Enter the name of your **Department** responsible for the device.
11.  Select **Yes** or **No** for **Manager Approval**, depending on whether you have the approval from your manager.
12.  Enter your **Employee Signature**, as a confirmation of your request.
13.  Enter your **Manager Signature**, as a confirmation of their approval.
14.  Select **Yes** or **No** for **IT Manager Approval**, depending on whether you have the approval from the IT manager.
15.  Enter the **IT Manager Signature**, as a confirmation of their approval.
16.  Enter the **Date Approved**, if the request has been approved.
17.  Enter the name of the person who **Approved** the request.
18.  Enter any **Notes** about the request, if necessary.
19.  Enter the **Form Submitted** date.
20.  Enter your **Submitted By** name.
21.  Enter the time you **Submitted** the form.
22.  Enter the time the **Created At** date and time.
23.  Enter the time the **Updated At** date and time.
24.  Enter the time the **Deleted At** date and time, if the form has been deleted.
25.  Enter the name of the person who **Deleted** the form.
26.  Enter the time the **Deleted By** person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
*   **Role**: Select the role you are submitting the request for.
*   **Device Type**: Select the type of device you are requesting to replace.
*   **Replacement Reason**: Briefly describe the reason you are requesting the device rollover.
*   **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the device to be replaced.
*   **Purchase Date**: Enter the date the device was purchased, if applicable.
*   **Purchase Cost**: Enter the purchase cost of the device, if applicable.
*   **Asset Status**: Select the status of the device from the options provided.
*   **Department**: Enter the name of your department responsible for the device.
*   **Manager Approval**: Select **Yes** if you have approval from your manager.
*   **Employee Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your request.
*   **Manager Signature**: Sign your manager's name to confirm their approval.
*   **IT Manager Approval**: Select **Yes** if you have approval from the IT manager.
*   **IT Manager Signature**: Sign the IT manager's name to confirm their approval.
*   **Date Approved**: Enter the date the request was approved.
*   **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved the request.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
*   **Form Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
*   **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person who submitted the form.
*   **Submitted At**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
*   **Created At**: Enter the time the form was created.
*   **Updated At**: Enter the time the form was updated.
*   **Deleted At**: Enter the time the form was deleted.
*   **Deleted By**: Enter the name of the person who deleted the form.

Note: Some fields like **Created At**, **Updated At**, **Deleted At**, **Deleted By** seem to be system-generated fields and do not require user input. They provide additional metadata about the form's lifecycle.
