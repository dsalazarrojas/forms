# Request A Walkthrough Session - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a walkthrough session for a customer. The walkthrough session allows our team to guide a customer through a specific process or task, providing personalized assistance and support. This form is meant for customers who require additional guidance or have questions about a particular process or task.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes the customer's name, contact information, and any additional details about their request.
2. Select a scheduled walkthrough time that works best for you.
3. Choose your preferred priority level (high, medium, or low) and scheduling flexibility (ASAP, flexible, or not flexible at all).
4. Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone number and email address.
5. Provide your company and job title, if applicable.
6. Add any notes about the walkthrough session, such as specific questions or areas of focus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Scheduled Walkthrough Time** (`scheduled_walkthrough_time`, date, required): Enter a date and time for the walkthrough session.
* **Walkthrough Priority** (`walkthrough_priority`, select_one, optional): Choose how urgently you need the walkthrough session to take place:
	+ **High**: You have critical tasks or deadlines and need the session to occur as soon as possible.
	+ **Medium**: You have important tasks or deadlines and need the session in a timely manner.
	+ **Low**: The walkthrough session is not time-sensitive and can occur whenever.
* **Customer Scheduling Preferences** (`customer_scheduling_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose your availability for the walkthrough session:
	+ **ASAP**: You are available at any time and want the session to occur as soon as possible.
	+ **Flexible**: You have some flexibility in your schedule and can accommodate different times.
	+ **Not Flexible at All**: You have a very tight schedule and can only accommodate specific times.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, optional): Enter the customer's company name.
* **Customer Job Title** (`customer_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the customer's job title.
* **Walkthrough Notes** (`walkthrough_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or notes about the walkthrough session, such as specific questions or areas of focus.
