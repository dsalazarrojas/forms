# Net User Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to provide feedback on the service they have experienced. The survey aims to collect information on their satisfaction level, any issues encountered, and their future expectations. It's essential to fill out this form to help us understand your needs and improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions according to your honest experience with our service.
2.  For questions with a "select\_one" type, choose the option that best describes your feelings or experiences.
3.  For questions with a "select\_multiple" type, choose all the options that apply to your situation.
4.  For the "email", "phone", and "comments" fields, type in your actual email address, phone number, or comments.
5.  Make sure to provide an email address and phone number that you are willing to have us contact you at.
6.  Answer the last question about whether you would like us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Welcome to the Net User Feedback Survey** (`survey_intro`, `note`, `required: false`): This is the introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully, as it contains important information about the purpose and expectations of this feedback form.
*   **How satisfied are you with the service?** (`rating_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Please select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with the service.
*   **Have you experienced any issues while using our service?** (`rating_issues`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Check all the options that apply to the issues you have experienced.
*   **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, `text`, `required: false`): This is your chance to provide any additional comments or suggestions about our service.
*   **What's your email address?** (`email`, `email`, `required: true`): Type in your actual email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **What's your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, `required: false`): Type in your actual phone number that we can use to contact you.
*   **Can we contact you at this email address?** (`contact_email`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select "True" if you are willing to have us contact you at this email address.
*   **Can we contact you at this phone number?** (`contact_phone`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select "True" if you are willing to have us contact you at this phone number.
*   **Would you like us to follow up with you?** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Answer whether you would like us to follow up with you after submitting this feedback form.
*   **How do you prefer to be contacted?** (`contact_preference`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Select all the contact methods you prefer.
*   **Is your email address up to date?** (`email_confirmation`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select "True" if your email address is up to date.
*   **Is your phone number up to date?** (`phone_confirmation`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select "True" if your phone number is up to date.
*   **What are your next steps for our service?** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Check all the options that apply to your future actions for our service.
*   **How likely are you to recommend our service to a friend or colleague?** (`rating_likelihood`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select the option that best describes your likelihood of recommending our service.
*   **How likely is it that the service will meet your needs in future?** (`rating_satisfaction_likely`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select the option that best describes how likely it is that the service will meet your needs in future.
*   **How likely are you to choose our service again in future?** (`rating_likelihood_likely`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select the option that best describes how likely you are to choose our service again.
*   **How likely is it that you will continue to use our service again?** (`rating_satisfaction_likely_again`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select the option that best describes how likely you are to use our service again.
*   **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments_likely`, `note`, `required: false`): This is your chance to provide any additional comments or suggestions about our service.
*   **How likely is it that you will refer our service to a friend or colleague?** (`rating_likely_again`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Select the option that best describes how likely you are to refer our service.
*   **How satisfied are you with our service overall?** (`rating_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with our service.
*   **How likely is it that the service will meet your needs again?** (`rating_likely_again_again`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Select the option that best describes how likely it is that the service will meet your needs again.
*   **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`comments_again`, `note`, `required: false`): This is your chance to provide any additional comments or suggestions about our service.
