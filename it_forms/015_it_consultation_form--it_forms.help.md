# it consultation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT consultation form is designed to collect information related to IT consultation services. This form can be used by multiple users, such as IT staff or consultants, to manage and view different aspects of the consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the relevant information to be filled out on each page.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`Client Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture general information about the client. Enter any relevant details that can be used to identify or describe the client.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture contact details for the client or individual responsible for the consultation. Enter the name, email, or phone number as relevant.
* **Service Details** (`Service Details`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture details about the service provided, such as the type of service, features, or any unique characteristics.
* **Service Description** (`Service Description`, `text`, required: false): This field provides an opportunity to provide a detailed description of the service, including any relevant details or context.
* **Client Questions** (`Client Questions`, `text`, required: false): This field is for capturing any questions or comments from the client. Enter any relevant information that might be useful for the consultation or service.
* **Service Cost** (`Service Cost`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to capture the cost associated with the service. Enter the financial amount or cost related to the service.
* **Service Duration** (`Service Duration`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to capture the duration or time required for the service. Enter the length or time frame for the service.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to determine if a person is directly responsible for the consultation or service. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the available options.
* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to capture the type of service provided. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the available options.
* **Service Status** (`Service Status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to capture the status of the service. Select one or more options from 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
