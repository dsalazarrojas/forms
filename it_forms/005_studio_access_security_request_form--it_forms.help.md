# Studio Access Security Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Studio Access Security Request Form" is used to request access to the studio for work purposes. This form is typically completed by employees who need access to the studio for a specific period of time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your studio information and employee information, including your job title, department, and phone number.
2. Specify the reason for your access request and the start and end dates of the access period.
3. Select the duration of access required.
4. Choose your access level (if not specifying, it will default to Security Level 1).
5. Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
6. Ensure that your reporting manager reviews and signs the form.
7. Ensure that the reporting manager adds their comments and signs the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Studio Information
* **Title** (`text`): Enter the title of the studio you are requesting access to.
### Employee Information
* **Employee Information** (`text`): Enter your employee information, including your name and ID number.
### Work Hours
* **Work Hours** (`text`): Enter your work hours, including the number of hours you will work per day.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`text`): Enter your job title.
### Department
* **Department** (`text`): Enter your department.
### Reason for Access Request
* **Reason for Access Request** (`text`): Enter the reason for your access request.
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`date`): Enter the start date of your access period.
### End Date
* **End Date** (`date`): Enter the end date of your access period.
### Duration of Access
* **Duration of Access** (`number`): Enter the duration of access required (e.g., 1 day, 1 week, etc.).
### Access Level
* **Access Level** (`select_one`): Choose the access level required (Security Level 1, Security Level 2, Security Level 3).
### Other Security Level (Please specify)
* **Other Security Level (Please specify)** (`text`): If you select "Other", specify the additional level of access required.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`text`): Add any additional comments or notes about your access request.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`): Enter your phone number.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`text`): Add any additional comments about your access request.
### Employee Signature
* **Employee Signature** (`text`): Sign the form with your employee signature.
### Date
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you are signing the form.
### Reporting Manager Information
* **Reporting Manager** (`text`): Enter your reporting manager's name and title.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`email`): Enter your reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (`text`): Enter your reporting manager's phone number.
* **Comments to Reporting Manager** (`text`): Add any comments to your reporting manager.
### Reporting Manager Signature
* **Reporting Manager Signature** (`text`): Sign the form with your reporting manager's signature.
### Reporting Manager Date
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date the reporting manager signs the form.
### Review Comments
* **Review Comments** (`text`): Add any comments for the review process.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the reporting manager.
* The reporting manager will review and sign the form, and add their comments.
* This form is not meant to be filled out by non-employees.
