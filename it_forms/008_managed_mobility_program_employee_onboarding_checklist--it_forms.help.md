# managed_mobility_program_employee_onboarding_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The managed mobility program is designed to assist employees in their onboarding process, providing a tailored experience for each individual. This form helps to streamline the process by collecting essential information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date you started your new role.
* **Manager Name** (2, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Department** (3, text, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Device Handoff Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the date you received your company device(s).
* **Mobility Services** (5, select multiple, optional): Select the services you require from the options provided.
	+ Note: You can choose multiple services.
* **Approval Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date you received approval for your role.
* **Notes** (7, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your onboarding experience.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Name** (10, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Employee Title** (11, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Title** (12, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Team** (13, text, optional): Enter the name of your team.
* **Supervisor** (14, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Reporting Manager** (15, text, optional): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Direct Manager** (16, text, optional): Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Employee ID** (17, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department Manager** (18, text, optional): Enter the name of your department manager.
* **Team Manager** (19, text, optional): Enter the name of your team manager.
* **Job Code** (20, text, optional): Enter your job code.
* **Job Number** (21, text, optional): Enter your job number.
* **Employee Number** (22, text, optional): Enter your employee number.
