# Information Technology Master Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Technology Master Data Request Form is a tool for submitting requests for information technology support. This form helps IT teams to efficiently gather and track requests for assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. On the first page, fill in the "IT Form Data" field if you need to provide additional information not captured by the other fields.
3. Select the type of request you are submitting (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
4. Select the category/categories relevant to your request (multiple selections allowed).
5. Enter a detailed description of your request.
6. Enter your name and email address (both are required fields).
7. If applicable, enter your phone number.
8. Ensure that the "Submit Date" is automatically populated with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **IT Form Data** (optional): Use this field to provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your request.
* **Request Type** (required): Select the type of request you are submitting (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Data Request Category** (required): Select all relevant categories for your request (multiple selections allowed).
* **Description** (required): Enter a detailed description of your request.
* **Submitter Name** (required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Submit Date** (required): This field will automatically be populated with the current date.
* **Submitted By** (required): This field will display the name of the person who submitted the form.
