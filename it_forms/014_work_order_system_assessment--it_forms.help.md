# Work Order System Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the status of work orders across various teams, including IT, Maintenance, and Facilities. It helps track progress, report issues, and gather feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Form Title:** Enter a title for the work order system assessment. This will serve as a reference point for your assessment.
2.  **Category:** Select the category for this assessment. Choose from "IT Forms" or "Other".
3.  **Description:** Provide a brief description of the assessment, if needed.
4.  **Issued By:** Enter the name of the person who issued the work order.
5.  **Assigned To:** Select the team members responsible for handling the work order. Choose from "Maintenance Team", "Facilities Team", "IT Team", or "Other".
6.  **Issue:** Identify the type of issue encountered. Choose from "IT Issues", "Maintenance Issues", "Facilities Issues", or "Other".
7.  **Satisfaction:** Indicate the level of satisfaction with the current status of the work order. Select "Satisfied" or "Not Satisfied".
8.  **Feedback:** Provide any additional feedback or comments about the work order.
9.  **Date:** Enter the date the assessment was submitted.
10. **Time:** Enter the time the assessment was submitted.
11. **Issue Description:** Describe the issue encountered in detail.
12. **Improve:** Suggest any improvements that could be made to the work order process.
13.  **Attachment:** Upload any relevant attachments or files related to the work order.
14. **Suggestions:** Add any additional suggestions or comments about the work order.
15. **Satisfaction Department:** Identify the department responsible for handling the work order. Choose from "Maintenance", "Facilities", "IT", or "Other".
16. **Satisfaction Date:** Enter the date the satisfaction was submitted.
17.  **Satisfaction Time:** Enter the time the satisfaction was submitted.
18.  **Satisfaction Department:** Identify the department responsible for handling the work order. Choose from "Maintenance", "Facilities", "IT", or "Other".
