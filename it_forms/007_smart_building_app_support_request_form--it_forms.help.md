# Smart Building App Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users who need help with the Smart Building App. Please fill out this form to provide your issue or question, and our support team will do their best to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

1. **Support Request Form 1** (<type>: text, required: false)
	* Please provide a brief description of your issue or question related to the Smart Building App.
2. **Support Request Form 2** (<type>: text, required: false)
	* Provide additional details about your issue or question.
3. **Support Request Form 3** (<type>: text, required: false)
	* If you have any screenshots or files to attach, please do so here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Support Request Form 1**: This field is for the initial description of your issue or question.
* **Support Request Form 2**: This field is for providing additional context about your issue or question.
* **Support Request Form 3**: This field is for attaching any relevant files or screenshots that may help our support team understand your issue better.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your issue or question.
* If you're unsure what information to provide, feel free to ask, and our support team will guide you through the process.
* If you have any further questions or need to add more information after submitting the form, please contact us directly.

This guide provides a clear and concise explanation of the Smart Building App Support Request Form, helping users to complete the form effectively and get the support they need.
