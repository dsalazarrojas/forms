## Software Analytics Collection User Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user consent for software analytics data collection. By filling out this form, users can provide their contact information and consent for the collection of software analytics data. This form is an essential step in ensuring that users are aware of and agree to the collection of their data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand its purpose and the information it collects.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* User Consent (id: 1)
	* Name (id: 2)
	* Email (id: 3)
3. Optionally, you can fill out the following fields:
	* Phone (id: 4)
	* Software Analytics Data Collection (id: 5)
	* Consent Date (id: 6)
4. Sign the form by filling out the Signed By (id: 7) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Consent (id: 1)** (`User Consent`, text, required): This field is where you declare your willingness to have your software usage data collected.
* **Name (id: 2)** (`Name`, text, required): This field is where you provide your name as a way to identify you.
* **Email (id: 3)** (`Email`, email, required): This field is where you provide your email address as a way to contact you.
* **Phone (id: 4)** (`Phone`, text, optional): This field is where you provide your phone number as an optional way to contact you.
* **Software Analytics Data Collection (id: 5)** (`Software Analytics Data Collection`, note, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your software analytics data collection preferences.
* **Consent Date (id: 6)** (`Consent Date`, date, optional): This field is where you provide the date you gave your consent for software analytics data collection.
* **Signed By (id: 7)** (`Signed By`, text, required): This field is where you sign your name to confirm that you have filled out the form accurately.
