# ux_research_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UX Research Consent Form is used to collect demographic information from users who want to participate in UX research studies. This form helps us gather details about the participant, such as their name, email, and contact information, as well as project-specific information like project name and start date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
5. Enter a brief description of the project (if applicable).
6. Enter the project's start and end dates (if applicable).
7. Enter the name of the contact person.
8. Enter the contact person's email address.
9. Enter the contact person's phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (user_first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (user_last_name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (user_email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (user_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
* **Project Name** (project_name, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
* **Project Description** (project_description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the project (if applicable).
* **Project Start Date** (project_start_date, date, optional): Enter the start date of the project (if applicable).
* **Project End Date** (project_end_date, date, optional): Enter the end date of the project (if applicable).
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the project.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us.
* If you have filled out this form before, do not resubmit it as it is not necessary and may lead to duplicate entries.
