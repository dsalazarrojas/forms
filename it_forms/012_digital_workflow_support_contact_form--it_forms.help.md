# digital_workflow_support_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to contact the digital workflow support team for assistance with issues or problems you're experiencing with the workflow. Please fill out this form with your information and details about the issue you're facing, and our support team will respond as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as prompted. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (User Information, text, optional): Provide a brief description of yourself, which will help the support team understand your context.
- **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your name as you want to be addressed by the support team.
- **Department** (department, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more departments that your issue is related to.
- **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter your email address where the support team can contact you.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number so the support team can contact you if needed.
- **Issue Description** (Issue Description, text, optional): Describe the issue you're experiencing with the digital workflow.
- **Attachments** (Attachments, text, optional): If relevant, attach any files or screenshots that might help the support team understand your issue.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your issue to help the support team assist you faster.
* If you have any attached files, make sure they are related to your issue and not just random attachments.
* If you have any follow-up questions or need further clarification on your issue, feel free to ask the support team.
