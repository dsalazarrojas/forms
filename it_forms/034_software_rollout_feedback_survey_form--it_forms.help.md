# Software Rollout Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience with our software. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about future software releases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the third field.
4. Enter the name of the software you are using in the fourth field.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the software in the fifth field.
6. Provide any feedback about your experience with the software in the sixth field.
7. If you have any suggestions for improvements, please enter them in the seventh field.
8. If you have any suggestions for new features, please enter them in the eighth field.
9. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Software Rollout Feedback** (`survey_form`, note, not required): This is a text area where you can provide general feedback about your experience with our software.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name so we can get in touch with you about your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you would like to receive follow-up emails or updates about our software, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you would like to receive follow-up calls or have any urgent matters that need our attention.
* **Software Name** (`software_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the software you are using.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, not required): Rate your level of satisfaction with our software. You can be as detailed as possible in your answer.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Provide any feedback or concerns about your experience with our software.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggest_improvements`, text, not required): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our software, please enter them here.
* **Suggest Features** (`suggest_features`, text, not required): If you have any ideas for new features that we should consider, please enter them here.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, not required): Click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your answers.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve our software.
* We will not share your contact information with anyone without your consent.
* We will use your feedback to improve our software and make it more user-friendly.
