# Network Device Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a network device within the organization. The purpose of this form is to collect information about a network device, including its serial number, device type, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you have all the necessary information about the network device before filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions and guidelines provided.
3.  Please make sure that the device to be registered does not already exist in the system.
4.  Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Serial Number** (1: `network_device_registration_form_1`): A unique identifier for the network device. Please provide the serial number of the device you are registering.
*   **Device Type** (3: `device_type`): Choose the type of device you are registering. Please select one of the options: Router, Switch, or Other.
*   **Network Location** (4: `network_location`): The location of the network device. Please provide the location of the device, e.g., floor number, building name, or room number.
*   **Network IP** (5: `network_ip`): The IP address of the network device. Please provide the IP address of the device.
*   **Network Subnet** (6: `network_subnet`): The subnet of the network device. Please provide the subnet of the device.
*   **Network Gateway** (7: `network_gateway`): The gateway of the network device. Please provide the gateway of the device.
*   **Network Vlan** (8: `network_vlan`): The VLAN of the network device. Please provide the VLAN of the device.
*   **MAC Address** (9: `mac_address`): The MAC address of the network device. Please provide the MAC address of the device.
*   **Vendor** (10: `vendor`): The vendor of the network device. Please provide the vendor of the device.
*   **Device Model** (11: `device_model`): The model of the network device. Please provide the model of the device.
*   **Registration Date** (12: `registration_date`): The date of registration. Please provide the date of registration in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Registration Time** (13: `registration_time`): The time of registration. Please provide the time of registration in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
*   **Comments** (14: `comments`): Any additional comments or notes about the network device. Please provide any relevant information about the device.
*   **Registered By** (15: `registered_by`): The person who registered the device. Please provide the name of the person who registered the device.
*   **Contact Person** (16: `contact_person`): The contact person for the network device. Please provide the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Number** (17: `contact_number`): The contact number of the contact person. Please provide the contact number of the contact person.
*   **Email** (18: `email`): The email of the contact person. Please provide the email of the contact person.
*   **Company** (19: `company`): The company of the contact person. Please provide the name of the company.
*   **Address** (20: `address`): The address of the contact person. Please provide the address of the contact person.
*   **City** (21: `city`): The city of the contact person. Please provide the city.
*   **State** (22: `state`): The state of the contact person. Please provide the state.
*   **Country** (23: `country`): The country of the contact person. Please provide the country.
*   **Notes** (24: `notes`): Any additional notes or comments about the contact person. Please provide any relevant information about the contact person.
*   **Submitted By** (25: `submitted_by`): The person who submitted the form. Please provide the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Tips

*   Please make sure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
*   Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant contact person or department.
