# Software Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback about your experience with our software. Your input will help us understand what you like or dislike about the software, which will aid in its development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. For each page, review the questions and select the corresponding answer that best fits your experience.
2. For numerical fields (Ease of Use, Ease of Resolution, Overall Experience, and Quality of Design), enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is extremely unhappy and 10 is very satisfied.
3. For select_one fields (Overall Satisfaction, Quality of Support, Satisfaction with Support, Satisfaction with Design), choose one of the options that best describes your experience.
4. For select_multiple fields (How Often Do You Use), select all that apply from the given options.
5. For text fields (Comments), provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Describe how satisfied you are with the software overall. Options:
	+ very satisfied
	+ somewhat satisfied
	+ not at all satisfied
	+ extremely unhappy
	+ very unhappy

### Ease of Use
* **Ease of Use**: Assess how easy or difficult it is to use the software. Enter a number between 1 and 10.
