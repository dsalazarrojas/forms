# Inventory Management Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a company in the inventory management system. It collects information about the company, including details about the company itself, its representatives, and the start and end dates of the trial period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company details, such as company name, address, email, and phone number.
2. Select whether the company has segment leads.
3. Choose the start and end dates of the trial period.
4. Enter the company representative's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
5. Add any additional notes about the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details** (`company_details`): Enter a brief description of the company.
* **Segment Leads** (`segment_leads`): Select whether the company has segment leads ("Yes" or "No").
* **Schedule Start Dates** (`schedule_start_dates`): Choose the start date of the trial period.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter the company's address.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`): Enter the company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`): Enter the company's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the company's name.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`): Enter the company's website URL.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`): Enter a brief description of the company.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Choose the start date of the trial period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Choose the end date of the trial period.
* **User** (`user`): Enter a brief description of the user responsible for the company.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes about the company.
* **Status** (`status`): Choose the status of the company ("Active" or "Inactive").
* **Trial Start Date** (`trial_start_date`): Choose the start date of the trial period.
* **Trial End Date** (`trial_end_date`): Choose the end date of the trial period.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`): Enter the company representative's name.
* **Company Representative Email** (`company_representative_email`): Enter the company representative's email address.
* **Company Representative Phone** (`company_representative_phone`): Enter the company representative's phone number.
* **Company Representative URL** (`company_representative_url`): Enter the company representative's website URL.
