# cyber_security_workshop_lead_generation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from potential attendees of a cyber security workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Event Details section with relevant information about the workshop you're interested in.
2. Enter your details in the Attendee Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Page 1, Text): Enter a brief description of the workshop, including its title, date, time, and location.
* **Attendee Information** (Page 2, Text): Enter your name, email address, and any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself as an attendee.
* **Attendee Info** (Page 3, Text): This field is another opportunity to provide additional information about yourself as an attendee.
## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
