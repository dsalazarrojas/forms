# IT Support Contract Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew IT support contracts. It helps IT staff to track and manage existing contracts, including their start and end dates, submitted by, and review status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief title for the contract in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select the category of the contract from the "Category" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the contract in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the start date of the contract in the "Contract Start Date" field.
5. Enter the end date of the contract in the "Contract End Date" field.
6. Select the type of contract from the "Contract Type" field.
7. Select the documents that support the contract from the "Uploaded Documents" field.
8. Set the review status of the contract to "Pending Review", "Approved", or "Rejected" in the "Review Status" field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the contract in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter an email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
11. Optionally, select the person who submitted the contract in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the contract.
* **Category** (2, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the contract (e.g. IT Forms, Other Forms).
* **Description** (3, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the contract.
* **Form ID** (4, number, required/optional): Enter the contract ID (leave blank if unknown).
* **Contract Start Date** (5, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (6, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Submitted By** (7, select_one, required/optional): Select the person who submitted the contract (e.g. John Doe, Jane Doe).
* **Submitted On** (8, date, required/optional): Enter the date the contract was submitted.
* **Notes** (9, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the contract.
* **Email** (10, email, required/optional): Enter an email address related to the contract.
* **Phone** (11, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number related to the contract.
* **Contract Type** (12, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of contract (e.g. IT Support, Maintenance, Consulting).
* **Uploaded Documents** (13, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any documents that support the contract (e.g. Document 1, Document 2, Document 3).
* **Review Status** (14, select_one, required/optional): Set the review status of the contract to "Pending Review", "Approved", or "Rejected".
