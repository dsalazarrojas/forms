# PDA Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PDA Information Collection Form is designed to gather information about Personal Digital Assistants (PDAs) for internal use only. It is essential to submit accurate and complete information to ensure the correct categorization and utilization of the PDA.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2.  Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
3.  Ensure you have submitted the form correctly and completely before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Please enter the first name of the PDA.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Optional): This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Required): Enter the email address associated with the PDA.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required): Enter the phone number associated with the PDA.
* **PDA Type** (`pda_type`, `select_one`, Required): Select the type of PDA from the provided options.
