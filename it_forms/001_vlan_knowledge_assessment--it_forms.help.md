# vlan_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VLAN Knowledge Assessment Form is designed to help IT teams evaluate their knowledge and skills in VLAN configurations, providing insights into their strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your IT team from the options provided in the "IT Teams" field.
2. Answer the questions and provide feedback on your experiences with VLAN configurations.
3. Choose the frequency and type of feedback you receive on your VLAN configurations.
4. Describe your challenges with VLAN configurations.
5. Rate your readiness to face VLAN challenges.
6. Provide details on how you configure VLANs.
7. Enter any questions you usually ask when faced with a VLAN challenge.
8. Write down any additional notes or observations.
9. Optionally, you can view your VLAN Knowledge Assessment Results after completing the form.

- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
