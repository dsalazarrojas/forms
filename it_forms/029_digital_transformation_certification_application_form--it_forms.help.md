# digital_transformation_certification_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital transformation certification application form is used to collect information from candidates applying for digital transformation certification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your details in the "Candidate Details" field.
2. Provide a brief statement about your motivation for seeking digital transformation certification.
3. Select the training provider that you completed your digital transformation training with.
4. Enter your experience level in the "Experience" field.
5. If applicable, enter your certification date in the "Certification Date" field.
6. Enter your contact details, including any other relevant contact information.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`Candidate Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief statement about why you are seeking digital transformation certification.
* **Training Provider** (`Training Provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the training provider that you completed your digital transformation training with from the list of options provided.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, `number`, required: false): Enter your level of experience related to digital transformation.
* **Certification Date** (`Certification Date`, `date`, required: false): If you have already been certified, enter the date of your certification.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other relevant contact information, such as your company or department.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
