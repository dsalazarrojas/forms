# Technical Report Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting technical reports and associated supporting documents for submission to a team or department within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, click on each field and enter the required information.
2. For the Technical Report field, enter a detailed description of your technical report.
3. In the Supporting Documents field, select one or more documents that are relevant to your report.
4. Enter your name and title in the Author Details field.
5. Set the Submission Date and Submission Time fields to the date and time of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technical Report**: A text field to input the main technical report.
* **Supporting Documents**: A multi-select field to choose one or more documents to include with your report.
* **Author Details**: A text field to input authorship information.
* **Submission Date**: A date field to document the submission date.
* **Submission Time**: A time field to document the submission time.

## Tips
- Make sure to include relevant and accurate information in all fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to your team or department's guidelines for submission requirements.
- Ensure that you have attached all required supporting documents before submitting the form.
