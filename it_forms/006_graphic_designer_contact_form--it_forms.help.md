# Graphic Designer Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for clients to get in touch with our team of graphic designers. It allows users to share their ideas, ask questions, or inquire about design services for their projects. Our team will receive the submitted data and use it to create personalized responses to the user's inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as necessary. Please make sure to fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure our team can best assist you with your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Your first name is requested here. You can enter your first name as it appears on any official documents or records you have.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Your last name is requested here. You can enter your last name as it appears on any official documents or records you have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where our team can contact you regarding your project. Please ensure this is a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here. Please format the number in the international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): If you have a website related to your project, provide the URL here. This will help our team get a better understanding of your project context.
* **About Project** (`about_project`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your project in a few words or sentences. This will help our team understand the nature of your inquiry.
* **Need Help With** (`need_help_with`, text, required: false): If you have a specific need or question related to your project, describe it here. This could be anything from design concept to project timeline concerns.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to help our team better understand your project.
* Ensure all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about our services, don't hesitate to contact us separately via email or phone.
* Please allow our team up to 48 hours to respond to your inquiry, and we'll get in touch as soon as possible.
