# Hybrid Classroom Technology Testing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hybrid Classroom Technology Testing Registration Form is designed to gather information from participants for the Hybrid Classroom Technology Testing process. It helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the demo session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately and completely.
2. Enter your Phone number for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Organization name.
4. Indicate your role in the organization.
5. Choose your preferred Demo Date and Demo Time.
6. Confirm whether you will be attending the Demo Site or not.
7. Select the Demo Equipment you would like to use.
8. Provide any additional Demo Notes if necessary.
9. Optionally, you can add any Participant Details or Testing Notes for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your work phone number for communication purposes.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Enter your organization name.
* **Role** (Role, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you are a decision maker, or 'No' if you are not a decision maker.
* **Demo Date** (Demo Date, date, required): Choose your preferred demo date for the testing session.
* **Demo Time** (Demo Time, time, required): Choose your preferred demo time for the testing session.
* **Demo Site** (Demo Site, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you will be attending the demo site, or 'No' if you will not be attending.
* **Demo Equipment** (Demo Equipment, select_multiple, required): Select all the demo equipment you would like to use.
* **Demo Notes** (Demo Notes, text, optional): Add any additional demo notes if necessary.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, optional): Add any participant details if necessary.
* **Testing Notes** (Testing Notes, text, optional): Add any testing notes if necessary.
