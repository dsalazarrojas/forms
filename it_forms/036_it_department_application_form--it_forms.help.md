# IT Department Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Department Application Form is a tool for employees to submit applications for various job openings within the IT department. This form collects information about the applicant, including their first name, last name, email, phone number, job title, years of experience, job level, department, date of birth, time of availability, and any additional notes. The purpose of this form is to streamline the application process and allow the IT department to efficiently review and process applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** in the format of `example@example.com`.
3. If you have a **Phone**, enter it in the format `123-456-7890`.
4. Select your **Job Title** from the list of options (Software Engineer, System Administrator, or Database Manager).
5. Enter your **Years of Experience** in numerical value (e.g., 5 years).
6. Select your **Job Level** from the list of options (Junior, Mid-level, or Senior).
7. Choose your **Department** from the list of options (IT, Sales, or Marketing).
8. If you have a **Date of Birth**, enter it in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
9. If you have a specific **Time** of availability, enter it in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
10. Add any additional **Note** about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Job Title**: Select the job title that best describes your position or the role you're interested in.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Job Level**: Choose the level of experience you have:
	+ **Junior**: 0-2 years of experience.
	+ **Mid-level**: 3-5 years of experience.
	+ **Senior**: 6+ years of experience.
* **Department**: Select the department you are applying for:
	+ **IT**: Information technology department.
	+ **Sales**: Sales department.
	+ **Marketing**: Marketing department.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time**: Enter your available time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Note**: Add any additional information about your application.
