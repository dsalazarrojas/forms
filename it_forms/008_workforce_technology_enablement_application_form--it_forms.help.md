# Workforce Technology Enablement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from a requester regarding workforce technology enablement applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Requester Details**: Please fill out the "Requester Details" section with your name, email, and job title. This will help us understand who is submitting the request.
2. **Manager Info**: Enter your manager's name, email, and job title. This information will be used to communicate with your manager about your technology requests.
3. **Justification**: Briefly explain why you are requesting the technology enablement. This should include the tool or technology you are requesting and your intended use case.
4. **Policy Acknowledgment**: Select the relevant policy acknowledgment from the dropdown menu. This ensures that you have read and agreed to the applicable policies.
5. **Policy Acknowledgment 2**: This is another policy acknowledgment section, select the relevant option.
6. **Tool Request**: Enter a detailed description of the tool or technology you are requesting.
7. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool you are requesting from the dropdown menu.
8. **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
9. **Submitter Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
10. **Submitter Contact**: Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
11. **Submitter Job Title**: Enter your job title.
12. **Manager Info**: Enter your manager's name, email, and job title.
13. **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
14. **Manager Job Title**: Enter your manager's job title.
15. **Tool Requester Info**: Enter any information about the tool requester.
16. **Tool Requester Name**: Enter the name of the tool requester.
17. **Tool Requester Department**: Enter the department of the tool requester.
18. **Tool Requester Title**: Enter the title of the tool requester.
