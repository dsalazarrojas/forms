## Laptop Rental Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on their overall experience with the laptop rental service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the rating that best describes your experience for the laptop condition and support quality.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Service** (`rental_service_form`, text, required): Describe your overall experience with the laptop rental service.
* **Laptop Condition** (`laptop_condition`, select_one, required): Rate the condition of the laptop you rented. Choose from:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Support Quality** (`support quality`, text, required): Rate the quality of support you received during your rental experience.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall experience`, text, required): Describe your overall experience with the laptop rental service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your comments will help us improve the service for future customers.
