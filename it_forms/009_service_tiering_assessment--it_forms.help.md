# Service Tiering Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Tiering Assessment form is used to gather information about a service or project within an organisation. This form helps to determine the priority and urgency of the service, enabling the organisation to allocate resources more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the service's organisational structure in the "Service Tiering Organisation" field.
2. Enter the name of the organisation in the "Organisation Name" field.
3. Determine whether the service is tiered or not in the "Service Tiering Team" field. Choose 'Yes' if the service is tiered and 'No' otherwise.
4. Select the tier level of the service in the "Service Tiering Level" field. Choose from 'High' and 'Low'.
5. Enter a brief description of the service in the "Description" field.
6. Enter the business impact of the service in the "Business Impact" field. Enter a numerical value.
7. Select the support level of the service in the "Support Level" field. Choose from 'High' and 'Low'.
8. Select the priority level of the service in the "Priority Level" field. Choose from 'High' and 'Low'.
9. Enter the date of the tiering decision in the "Tiering Decision" field.
10. Enter the review date of the service in the "Review Date" field.
11. Enter the name of the reviewer in the "Reviewer" field.
12. Enter any additional comments about the service in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Tiering Organisation** (1): Describe the service's organisational structure.
* **Organisation Name** (2): Enter the name of the organisation providing this service.
* **Service Tiering Team** (3): Indicate whether the service is tiered or not.
* **Service Tiering Level** (4): Select the tier level of the service, either 'High' or 'Low'.
* **Description** (5): Enter a brief description of the service.
* **Business Impact** (6): Enter a numerical value indicating the business impact of the service.
* **Support Level** (7): Select the support level of the service, either 'High' or 'Low'.
* **Priority Level** (8): Select the priority level of the service, either 'High' or 'Low'.
* **Tiering Decision** (9): Enter the date of the tiering decision for this service.
* **Review Date** (10): Enter the date of the review of this service.
* **Reviewer** (11): Enter the name of the person who reviewed this service.
* **Comments** (12): Enter any additional comments about this service.
