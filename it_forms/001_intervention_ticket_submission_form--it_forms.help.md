# Intervention Ticket Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intervention Ticket Submission Form is designed to collect and document incident information in a structured and organized manner. This form is used to ensure that all relevant details about an incident are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and carefully, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2.  Review each field to ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
3.  Double-check for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info (`General Info`, text, false):** This field is for entering general information related to the incident.
    -   Enter a brief description of the incident.
*   **Incident Details (`Incident Details`, text, false):** Provide a detailed description of the incident in this field.
    -   Be as descriptive as possible to help in the resolution process.
*   **Technical Info (`Technical Info`, text, false):** Enter any technical information related to the incident.
    -   This can include hardware or software issues.
*   **Submit Ticket (`Submit Ticket`, text, false):** Enter the ticket submission information.
    -   Enter the ticket submission details.
*   **Technical Issues (`Technical Issues`, text, false):** Enter a detailed description of any technical issues related to the incident.
    -   Be as detailed as possible to help in troubleshooting.
*   **Ticket Type (`Ticket Type`, select_one, false):** Select the type of ticket being submitted.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Incident Status (`Incident Status`, select_one, false):** Select the status of the incident.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Incident Description (`Incident Description`, text, false):** Enter a detailed description of the incident.
    -   Be as descriptive as possible to help in the resolution process.
*   **Incident Date (`Incident Date`, date, false):** Enter the date of the incident.
    -   Format should follow the ISO 8601 format.
*   **Incident Time (`Incident Time`, time, false):** Enter the time of the incident.
    -   Format should be in 24-hour time format.
*   **Incident Date/Time (`Incident Date/Time`, date, false):** Enter the date and time of the incident.
    -   This is a combination of date and time fields.
*   **Incident Date/Time Format (`Incident Date/Time Format`, select_one, false):** Select the format of the incident date and time.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Incident Location (`Incident Location`, text, false):** Enter the location of the incident.
    -   Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Reported By (`Incident Reported By`, text, false):** Enter the person who reported the incident.
    -   Enter the name or identifier of the person who reported the incident.
*   **Reporter (`Reporter`, text, false):** Enter the name or identifier of the reporter.
    -   Enter the name or identifier of the person who reported the incident.
*   **Priority (`Priority`, select_one, false):** Select the priority of the incident.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Urgency (`Urgency`, select_one, false):** Select the urgency of the incident.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Impact (`Impact`, select_one, false):** Select the impact of the incident.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Escalation (`Escalation`, select_one, false):** Select the escalation level of the incident.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Resolution (`Resolution`, select_one, false):** Select the resolution status of the incident.
    -   Choose one of the available options.
*   **Incident Tags (`Incident Tags`, text, false):** Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the incident.
    -   Enter any tags or keywords related to the incident.
*   **Incident Notes (`Incident Notes`, note, false):** Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
    -   Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.
*   **Submitter (`Submitter`, text, false):** Enter the person submitting the form.
    -   Enter the name or identifier of the person submitting the form.
*   **Form Submission (`Form Submission`, text, false):** Enter the submission details.
    -   Enter any submission details.

Note:
Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
