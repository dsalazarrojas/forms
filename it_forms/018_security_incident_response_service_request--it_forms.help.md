# security_incident_response_service_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting and documenting security incidents that have occurred on our system. Please fill out this form to provide as much detail as possible about the incident, including any relevant contact information and incident details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
2. Enter the contact information for the person who reported the incident in the "Contact Details" field.
3. If applicable, enter the risk level of the incident in the "Incident Risk" field.
4. Select the status of the incident from the "Incident Status" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
6. Enter any additional information about the person who reported the incident in the "Incident Reported By" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details (Incident Details)**: A clear and concise description of the incident.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: The contact information for the person who reported the incident, including their name and email.
* **Incident Risk (Incident Risk)**: The risk level of the incident, if known.
* **Incident Status (Incident Status)**: The current status of the incident (Active, Closed, or Pending).
* **Incident Date (Incident Date)**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time (Incident Time)**: The time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Reported By (Incident Reported By)**: The name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Email (Incident Reported By Email)**: The email address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Phone (Incident Reported By Phone)**: The phone number of the person who reported the incident.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Incident Details" field to help us understand the incident.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us for guidance.
* If you have already filled out this form for the same incident, please do not submit additional requests.
