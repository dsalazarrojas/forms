# IT Support Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees to aid in creating a positive and productive support experience. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that our team receives the necessary information to assist you effectively. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, click on the "Submit" button once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Email (3)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your business email address or a personal email address where you can be reached by our support team.
### Phone Number (4)
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number or a phone number where our support team can reach you.
