# robot_escort_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The robot escort service request form is designed to collect information for a robot escort service request. This form is intended for customers or clients who need to request a robot escort service for a specific occasion or event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions to fill it out.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked with asterisk \*). These fields are essential for processing your request correctly.
3. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **robot_escort_service_request_form_page_2** (select_one): Select the specific option that best describes the reason for your robot escort service request.
	+ Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3: Choose the option that corresponds to your reason for requesting the robot escort service.
