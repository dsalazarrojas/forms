# Trace System Malfunction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trace System Malfunction Report Form is used to report malfunctions in the trace system. It is recommended that users complete this form to provide accurate information about the malfunctions they experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Date of Malfunction** field with the date and time when the malfunction occurred.
2. Describe the **Description of Problem** in the field provided.
3. Select the **Impact** that the malfunction had on your work (Yes or No).
4. If the malfunction affected a **System**, select the system that was affected (Yes or No).
5. If a **User** was affected by the malfunction, select the user who was affected (Yes or No).
6. In the **Contact Details** field, provide any contact information that may be relevant to the report.
7. Describe any **Action Taken** to resolve the malfunction (Yes or No).
8. In the **Additional Information** field, provide any additional context or details that may be helpful.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trace System Malfunction Report Form** (`trace_system_malfunction_report_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can describe the malfunction and its details.
* **Date of Malfunction** (`date_of_malfunction`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the malfunction occurred.
* **Time of Malfunction** (`time_of_malfunction`, time, optional): Enter the exact time when the malfunction occurred.
* **Description of Problem** (`description_of_problem`, text, optional): Describe the malfunction in detail, including any relevant symptoms or error messages.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_one, optional): Select Yes if the malfunction had an impact on your work, otherwise select No.
* **System Affects** (`system_affects`, select_multiple, optional): Select all systems that were affected by the malfunction.
* **Affected User** (`affected_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who was affected by the malfunction.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, select_one, optional): Select Yes if you took any action to resolve the malfunction, otherwise select No.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional context or details that may be helpful.
* **Trace System Malfunction Report Form Subform** (`trace_system_malfunction_report_form_subform`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the malfunction was related to the trace system or not.
