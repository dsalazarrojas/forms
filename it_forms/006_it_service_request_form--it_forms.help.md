# IT Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Service Request Form is designed to enable users to submit service requests for technical assistance in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
2. Select the most relevant category that best describes your service request.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your service request.
4. Specify the priority level of your request.
5. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address.
6. Optionally, provide your phone number and location.
7. If necessary, include any additional details about your service request.
8. Choose the tool assigned to handle your request.
9. Confirm if you created the request or not.
10. Confirm if the request was updated by someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request Form** (`it_service_request_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or summary of your service request.
* **Category** (`category`, select one, optional): Select a category that best describes your service request. This will help us understand the nature of your request and assign it to the right team.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your service request, including any relevant information or context.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select one, optional): Specify whether your service request is technical or non-technical.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select one, optional): Choose the priority level of your service request, which will help us understand the urgency and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the person submitting the request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the location where the service request applies.
* **Details** (`details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or context that may be helpful in resolving your service request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, optional): Choose the tool that will be used to handle your service request.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select one, optional): Confirm if you created the service request.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select one, optional): Confirm if the request was updated by someone else.
