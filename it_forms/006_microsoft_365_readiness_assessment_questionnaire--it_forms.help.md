# Microsoft 365 Readiness Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microsoft 365 Readiness Assessment Questionnaire helps organizations evaluate their readiness to use Microsoft 365. By answering the questions in this form, you will provide valuable information about your organization's current state of Microsoft 365 adoption, identify areas that need improvement, and receive guidance on how to plan and implement Microsoft 365 in your organization.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. For each question, choose the best option that describes your organization's current state.
3. Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
4. If you are unsure about any question, select "Other" and describe your situation in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required=false): This question determines the size of your organization, which will help us understand the scope of your Microsoft 365 adoption.
  * Small: Your organization has fewer than 100 employees.
  * Medium: Your organization has between 100 and 1,000 employees.
  * Large: Your organization has between 1,000 and 5,000 employees.
  * Enterprise: Your organization has more than 5,000 employees.

* **Number of Users** (`number_of_users`, number, required=false): This question asks for the total number of users in your organization.
* **User Type** (`user_type`, select_one, required=false): This question helps us understand the types of users in your organization.
  * IT Department: Employees who use Microsoft 365 for IT-related tasks.
  * Marketing Department: Employees who use Microsoft 365 for Marketing-related tasks.
  * Sales Department: Employees who use Microsoft 365 for Sales-related tasks.
  * HR Department: Employees who use Microsoft 365 for HR-related tasks.
  * Other: Other user types, such as contractors or third-party users.

* **Microsoft 365 License Status** (`ms_365_license_status`, select_one, required=false): This question determines the current status of Microsoft 365 licensing in your organization.
  * Not Started: Your organization has not yet implemented Microsoft 365.
  * Pilot: Your organization is currently piloting Microsoft 365.
  * Production: Your organization has fully implemented Microsoft 365.
