# Building Access Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Building Access Authorization Form is used to request access to specific buildings within the organization. This form is used to collect information about the applicant, their access needs, and the duration of their access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
* Select your employee ID or contractor number (if applicable).
* Specify the department you work for.
* Choose the type of access required (e.g., Building Entry, Parking, Server Room, etc.).
* Select the buildings you need access to.
* Enter the start date and end date of your access (if temporary).
* Choose whether your access is permanent or temporary.
* Provide a brief business justification for your access request.
* Specify your direct manager's name and email address.
* Indicate your security clearance status.
* Choose whether you are an employee, contractor, visitor, or vendor.
* Confirm whether a background check has been completed (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (applicant_name): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (applicant_email): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (applicant_phone): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Employee ID/Contractor Number** (employee_id): Enter your employee ID or contractor number (if applicable).
* **Department** (department): Specify the department you work for.
* **Type of Access Required** (access_type): Choose the type of access required (e.g., Building Entry, Parking, Server Room, etc.).
* **Buildings to Access** (buildings_list): Select the buildings you need access to.
* **Access Start Date** (access_start_date): Enter the date you need access to start.
* **Access End Date** (access_end_date): Enter the date your access will expire (if temporary).
* **Access Duration** (access_duration): Choose whether your access is permanent or temporary.
* **Business Justification** (justification): Provide a brief reason for your access request.
* **Direct Manager Name** (manager_name): Enter your direct manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (manager_email): Enter your direct manager's email address.
* **Security Clearance Status** (security_clearance): Indicate your current security clearance status.
* **Contractor Status** (contractor_status): Choose whether you are an employee, contractor, visitor, or vendor.
* **Background Check Completed** (background_check): Confirm whether a background check has been completed (if applicable).

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting this form.
* Please note that this form is a one-time submission form and should not be resubmitted for the same access request.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
