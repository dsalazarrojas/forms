# Cybersecurity Data Recovery Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about cybersecurity incidents that require attention and recovery actions. It's designed to help gather necessary details for incident investigation and resolution processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user information section with your details.
2. Provide a clear description of the incident in the incident details section.
3. Outline the recovery plan and any actions taken or proposed in the recovery plan section.
4. If necessary, attach an incident report in the incident report section.
5. Indicate the incident status, type, and severity in their respective fields.
6. Choose the correct incident type and report type from the dropdown menus.
7. Enter the incident start and report dates in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information for communication.
* **Incident Details**: Provide a clear and concise description of the incident, including its cause, impact, and affected assets.
* **Recovery Plan**: Outline the steps taken or proposed to resolve the incident, including any actions, timelines, and resources required.
* **Incident Report**: Attach a relevant incident report or provide a brief summary.
* **Incident Status**: Select the current status of the incident (Active or Inactive).
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Incident Severity**: Indicate the severity of the incident (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Incident Report Type**: Select the type of incident report (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Incident Report Date**: Enter the date the incident report was created or updated.
* **Incident Start Date**: Enter the date the incident started.
* **Incident Priority**: Enter a numerical value indicating the incident's priority level (1-10).
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Incident Severity**: Indicate the severity of the incident (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Incident Report Type**: Select the type of incident report (e.g., Yes or No).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the incident.
* Attach relevant incident reports or documents if necessary.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
* Review and submit the form carefully before sending it to the relevant teams or partners.
