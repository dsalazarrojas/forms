# PDF Conversion Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users convert PDF files by providing essential information about their conversion needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all files you want to convert from the "file_uploads" field.
2.  Choose the desired format for your converted files in the "file_format" field.
3.  If you have priority needs for your conversions, select "Yes" in the "priority" field.
4.  Set a deadline for your submission if you need to have your conversions done by a specific date in the "submission_deadline" field.
5.  Add any additional comments or instructions in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **file_uploads** (`<select_multiple>`, Required: False)
*   This is the list of files you want to convert. You can select multiple files at once.

### 2. **file_format** (`<select_one>`, Required: False)
*   Select the desired format for your converted files. Currently available options are 'Yes' and 'No'.

### 3. **priority** (`<select_one>`, Required: False)
*   If you have urgent or priority needs for your conversions, select "Yes" here.

### 4. **submission_deadline** (`<date>`, Required: False)
*   If you need your conversions completed by a specific date, select it in this field.

### 5. **notes** (`<note>`, Required: False)
*   Add any additional comments or instructions here to help us better understand your needs.
