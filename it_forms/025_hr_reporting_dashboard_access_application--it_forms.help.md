# hr_reporting_dashboard_access_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request access to the HR reporting dashboard for employees. This form allows employees and managers to track and request access requests, approvals, and comments. It provides a simple and organized way to manage reporting access requests and approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "employee_requester" field to confirm or deny the employee's request.
2. In the "manager_approver" field, select "Yes" or "No" to approve or reject the request.
3. Enter a comment in the "manager_comments" field if you wish to provide additional context or explanation.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "manager_approval" field to confirm or deny the approval.
5. Enter the date of the request in the "date_requested" field.
6. Enter the time of the request in the "time_requested" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or details in the "reporting_dashboard_access" field.
8. Select the desired "reporting_access_level" for the employee (High or Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_requester** (select_one, Required): Confirm or deny the employee's request.
* **manager_approver** (select_multiple, Required): Approve or reject the request.
* **manager_requester** (number, Optional): Enter the manager's request number.
* **employee_request_details** (text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or details from the employee.
* **manager_comments** (text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback for the employee.
* **manager_approval** (select_one, Required): Confirm or deny the approval.
* **date_requested** (date, Optional): Enter the date of the request.
* **time_requested** (time, Optional): Enter the time of the request.
* **reporting_dashboard_access** (note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the reporting access.
* **reporting_access_level** (select_multiple, Optional): Select the desired access level for the employee (High or Low).

## Tips
* Make sure to select "No" if you are not the employee's manager.
* Use the "manager_comments" field to provide clear and concise feedback.
* Enter the correct "date_requested" and "time_requested" fields if available.
