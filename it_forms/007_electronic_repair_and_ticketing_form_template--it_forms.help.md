# Electronic Repair And Ticketing Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Electronic Repair And Ticketing Form Template. The form is designed to capture information about electronic devices that need repair or service. It is intended for use by technicians, customer support staff, and other authorized personnel to document and track electronic repair and ticketing requests in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and making sure you have all the necessary information to complete it.
2. Click on each page to progress through the form, and fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Request** (`service_request`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of service requested, such as "Service Request 1", "Service Request 2", or "Service Request 3", from the dropdown list.
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's information, including their name, contact number, or any other relevant details.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of service required for the electronic device, such as repair, maintenance, or replacement.
- **Repair Order** (`repair_order`, text, required: false): Describe the specific repair or maintenance required for the electronic device.
- **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the service request was made.
- **Service Request Time** (`service_request_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time when the service request was made.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your relevant documentation or policies for guidance.
- If you're still unsure, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or colleague for assistance.
