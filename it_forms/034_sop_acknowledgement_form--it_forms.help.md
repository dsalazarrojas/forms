# SOP Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SOP Acknowledgement Form is designed to ensure that security personnel are informed and compliant with Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). This form is used to collect information about security personnel's understanding and acknowledgment of SOPs, as well as their work schedule and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account your specific situation and role.
3. Complete the form accurately and honestly, as it will be used for record-keeping and evaluation purposes.

* **Security Agency** (`security_agency`, `text`, `false`): Enter your security agency or department's name.
* **Security Personnel** (`security_personnel`, `select_one`, `false`): Select your role within the security agency:
	+ Security Head
	+ Team Member
	+ Support Staff
* **SOPs Acknowledged** (`sops_acknowledged`, `select_multiple`, `false`):

		- SOP1

		- SOP3
* **SOPs Not Acknowledged** (`sops_not_acknowledged`, `select_multiple`, `false`):
	+ Select all SOPs you have not acknowledged, from:
		- SOP4
		- SOP5
* **Manual Reviewed** (`manual_reviewed`, `select_multiple`, `false`):
	+ Select sections that have been manually reviewed:
		- Section 1
		- Section 2
		- Section 3
* **Manual Understood** (`manual_understood`, `select_multiple`, `false`):
	+ Select clauses that you have understood from manual reviews:
		- Clause 1
		- Clause 2
		- Clause 3
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `false`): Describe any comments you have about this assessment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, `false`): Enter your signature (name and title).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `false`): Enter the date of completion.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `false`): Enter the time of completion.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `false`): Enter your email address.

* **Department** (`department`, `text`, `false`): Enter your department's name.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, `false`): Enter your role within the security agency.
