# Process Documentation Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Documentation Access Request form is used to request access to process documentation. This form helps to gather necessary information about the document and the intended use of the access request.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, fill out the fields with the required information.
- Ensure that you provide a valid reason for your request.
- Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info**: Enter your name and contact information.
- **Document Info**: Enter a description of the document you are requesting access to.
- **Request Reason**: Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you need access to the document.
- **Intended Use**: Describe how you plan to use the document.
- **Approval Requester**: If this request is being made on behalf of someone else, enter the name and title of the person making the request.
- **Requesting Date**: Enter the date of the request.
- **Requesting Time**: Enter the time of the request.
- **Document Access Level**: Choose the level of access you require (Public, Private, or Restricted).
- **Access Type**: Choose the type of access you require (Edit, View, or Both).
- **Request Status**: Select the status of your request (Active, Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
- **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your request.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the administrator.
- Review and update your request status regularly to track the progress of your request.
