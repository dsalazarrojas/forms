# IT Marketing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Marketing Agreement Form is used to record and manage marketing agreements with customers for IT services. This guide will walk you through the fields and instructions to help you fill out the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields according to the instructions below.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Form** (`it_marketing_agreement_form`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the agreement. Enter a short statement of the agreement's scope and objectives.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
* **Scope of Work** (`agreement_scope`, note, required): Describe the scope of work for the agreement, including specific tasks and responsibilities.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Marketing Services** (`marketing_services`, select multiple, required): Indicate which marketing services will be used (e.g., 'Yes' for social media, 'No' for email marketing).
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select multiple, required): Indicate which marketing channels will be used (e.g., 'Yes' for website, 'No' for social media).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Agreement Sign Date** (`agreement_sign_date`, date, required): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, select one, required): Confirm whether the agreement has been signed.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date the agreement was approved.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, select one, required): Indicate the current status of the agreement.
* **Customer Email Address** (`customer_email_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Agreement Status Comments** (`agreement_status_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments regarding the agreement status.
* **IT Services** (`it_services`, select one, required): Indicate whether IT services will be provided (e.g., 'Yes' for technical support, 'No' for no IT services).
* **Services Start Date** (`services_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the IT services.
* **Services End Date** (`services_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the IT services.
* **Agreement Status Comments** (`agreement_status_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments regarding the agreement status.
* **Customer** (`customer`, select one, required): Indicate whether the customer is (e.g., 'Yes' for company, 'No' for individual).
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select one, required): Indicate the type of customer (e.g., 'Yes' for company, 'No' for individual).
* **Agreement Signed By** (`agreement_signed_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Approval** (`approval`, select one, required): Confirm whether the agreement has been approved.
* **Approval By** (`approval_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person approving the agreement.
