# Performance Analytics Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Analytics Support Request Form is designed to collect information from end-users who require support for performance analytics issues. This form will help our support team to quickly understand the nature of the issue and provide timely assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form data in the text field at the top of the page.
2. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your phone number for our support team to contact you.
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing with performance analytics in the description field.
5. Specify the urgency of your issue by selecting the priority level (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Choose the relevant dashboard(s) related to your issue.
7. Optionally, provide any additional analytics information that may be helpful to diagnose the issue.
8. Add any relevant notes or comments about your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Fill in this field with any relevant information about your performance analytics issue. This could include screenshots, error messages, or any other details that might help our support team to quickly understand your problem.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name so that our support team can address you directly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so that we can contact you to provide assistance.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so that our support team can contact you for further discussion.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe the specific issue you are experiencing with performance analytics. Be as detailed as possible to help our support team to quickly understand your problem.
* **Priority** (`urgent_priority`, select_one, optional): Specify the level of urgency for your issue. Choose from High, Medium, or Low to help our support team prioritize your request.
* **Dashboard** (`dashboard`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant dashboard(s) related to your issue.
* **Analytics** (`analytics`, text, optional): Provide any additional analytics information that may be helpful to diagnose the issue.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about your issue. This can help our support team to better understand your problem and provide a more effective solution.
