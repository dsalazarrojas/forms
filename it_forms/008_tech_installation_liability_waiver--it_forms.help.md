# Tech Installation Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by customers and technicians to provide important information and ensure mutual understanding before and after a tech installation. It includes fields for customer and technician details, installation dates, and agreement to terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the "Customer Consent" field and choose "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of this waiver.**
2. Fill in the **Installation Details** field with a brief description of the installation.
3. In the **Signature** field, sign and date to confirm your agreement to the terms.
4. Enter the **Installation Date** in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
5. Fill in the **Technician Name** field with the name of the technician performing the installation.
6. In the **Technician Signature** field, select "Yes" to confirm the technician's agreement to the terms.
7. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field.
8. In the **Customer Signature** field, sign and date to confirm your agreement to the terms.
9. If you have a phone number, enter it in the **Phone** field (optional).
10. Enter your **Customer Address** in the text field.
11. Fill in the **Technician Address** field with the address of the technician performing the installation.
12. Enter the **Technician License** number (e.g., state license number) in the text field.
13. Select the technician's **Technician Experience** level from the options provided.
14. Fill in the **Installation Location** field with a brief description of the location of the installation.
15. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number in the **Emergency Contact** field (optional).
16. In the **Agree to Terms** field, select "I have read and agree to the terms of this waiver" to confirm your agreement.
17. Fill in the **Customer Experience** field with a brief description of your experience with the technology installed.
18. Enter the **Signature Date** in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
19. Enter the technician's **Technician Experience Date** in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
20. In the **Technician Agreement** field, sign and date to confirm the technician's agreement to the terms.
21. In the **Customer Signature 2** field, sign and date to confirm your agreement to the terms.
22. Fill in the **Agreement** field with a brief description of any additional agreements or terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Consent** (Yes/No): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms of this waiver.
* **Installation Details**: Briefly describe the installation, including the type and location.
* **Signature**: Sign and date to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date of the installation in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician performing the installation.
* **Technician Signature** (Yes/No): Confirm that the technician has read and agrees to the terms.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign and date to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Technician Address**: Enter the address of the technician performing the installation.
* **Technician License**: Enter the technician's license number (e.g., state license number).
* **Technician Experience**: Select the technician's experience level.
* **Installation Location**: Briefly describe the location of the installation.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact (optional).
* **Agree to Terms** (I have read and agree/ I have read but do not agree): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
* **Customer Experience**: Briefly describe your experience with the technology installed.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Technician Experience Date**: Enter the date of the technician's experience in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Technician Agreement**: Sign and date to confirm the technician's agreement to the terms.
* **Customer Signature 2**: Sign and date to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Agreement**: Enter any additional agreements or terms.
