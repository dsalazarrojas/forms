# Network And Internet Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a user's network and internet connection quality, devices used, and any issues encountered. This information will be used to help identify potential issues and provide a way for users to report issues or provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as prompted. You will need to select or enter information about your network and internet connection, devices used, and any issues encountered. You can also provide additional feedback if you have any comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Network Quality** (`connectivity_quality`, number, optional): Use the slider to rate the quality of your network connection.
* **Devices Used** (`devices_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all devices you have used to access the internet or network.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`, select_one, optional): Select any issues you have encountered while accessing the network or internet.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your network or internet connection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with further questions or to follow up on your issues.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with further questions or to follow up on your issues.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
- Be as specific as possible when reporting issues or providing feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
