# Service Ticket And Part Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a service ticket and part request for maintenance and repair services. Please use this form to provide details about the service required, part needed, and customer information. This form is not meant for general customer inquiries or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to select one of the options for "Service Ticket Type" as this will help us determine the correct course of action for your request.
- For "Part Request" select "Yes" if you are requesting a part, and "No" if you are not.
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in the fields marked as "Required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Ticket And Part Request Form** (`service_ticket_and_part_request_form`): A text field where you can describe the service request in your own words.
* **Service Ticket Type** (`service_ticket_type`): Select one of the options to specify the type of service required (Repair or Replacement).
* **Part Request** (`part_request`): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a part, and "No" if you are not.
* **Part Details** (`part_details`): A text field where you can provide any additional details about the part needed.
* **Part Number** (`part_number`): A text field where you can provide the part number (if known).
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): A text field where you can provide the serial number (if known).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): A text field where you can provide the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): A text field where you can provide the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): A text field where you can provide the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`): A text field where you can provide the customer's address.
* **Ticket Description** (`ticket_description`): A text field where you can describe the issue or problem with your service request.
* **Ticket Priority** (`ticket_priority`): Select one of the options to specify the priority level of your service request (High, Medium, or Low).

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance or clarification on any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact us.
- Our team will review and respond to your service request as soon as possible.
