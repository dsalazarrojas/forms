# Remote Access VPN Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Access VPN Request Form is designed to help employees request remote access to company resources for work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** and **Email**.
2. Select your **Department** and **Role** that you will be accessing remotely.
3. Provide a clear **Reason for Access** explaining why you need remote access.
4. Choose the correct **Approved By** and **Date Approved** based on your department or role.
5. Optionally, select the **Expiration Date** for your access.
6. Choose the correct **Access Duration Type**.
7. Provide any additional **Notes**, **IP Address**, **Device**, **Location**, **Remote Access Type**, and **Reason for Request** details.
8. Review your information and submit the form.

* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details related to your access request.
