# Purple Brick Real Estate Mortgage Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a mortgage loan application. Please fill in the required information accurately to help us assess your loan eligibility and provide you with loan options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Enter your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Address, City, State, Email, and Phone.
* Provide your date of birth, Social Security Number, Employment Status, and Employment Type.
* Enter your income and loan specifics, including Loan Amount, Loan Term, Loan Type, Loan Rate, and Property Type.
* Select the purpose of the loan and provide additional details such as Property Value, Down Payment, Closing Cost, Appraisal Fees, and Closing Fees.
