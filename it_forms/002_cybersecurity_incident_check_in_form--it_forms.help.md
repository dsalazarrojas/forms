# cybersecurity_incident_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track cybersecurity incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of incident (e.g., Network Breach, Data Loss, Malware).
2. Provide the date of the incident.
3. Enter the details of the incident.
4. Select the impact scope (Internal or External).
5. Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, In progress).
6. Identify the party responsible for the incident (Internal or External).
7. Enter any affected assets.
8. Provide your contact information as the incident reporter.
9. Enter the date the incident was reported.
10. Enter the date the incident was last updated.
11. Select any affected services.
12. Select any affected teams.
13. Choose the priority of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
14. Select the category of the incident (Phishing, Ransomware, Malware).
15. Enter the contact information for the person who reported the incident.
16. Enter the email address for the person who reported the incident.
17. Enter the name of the person who resolved the incident.
18. Enter the contact information for the person who resolved the incident.
19. Enter the name of the person who last updated the incident.
20. Enter the contact information for the person who last updated the incident.
21. Enter any comments about the incident.
22. Attach any relevant files or documents.
23. Enter any additional notes about the incident.
24. Enter your contact information for follow-up.
25. Enter the email address for the person who last updated the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident type** (Select one): The type of incident being reported (e.g., Network Breach, Data Loss, Malware).
* **Date of incident** (Date): The date of the incident.
* **Incident details** (Note): Enter as much detail as possible about the incident.
* **Impact scope** (Select multiple): The scope of the incident's impact (Internal or External).
* **Incident status** (Select one): The status of the incident (Open, Closed, In progress).
* **Incident responsible party** (Select one): The party responsible for the incident (Internal or External).
* **Affected assets** (Text): List any assets that were affected by the incident.
* **Incident reporter** (Text): Enter your contact information as the incident reporter.
* **Reported on** (Date): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Last updated on** (Date): Enter the date the incident was last updated.
* **Affected services** (Select multiple): Select any services that were affected by the incident.
* **Affected teams** (Select multiple): Select any teams affected by the incident.
* **Priority** (Select one): Choose the priority of the incident (Low, Medium, High).
* **Category** (Select one): Select the category of the incident (Phishing, Ransomware, Malware).
* **Reported by** (Text): Enter the contact information for the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported by email** (Text): Enter the email address for the person who reported the incident.
* **Resolved by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who resolved the incident.
* **Resolved by email** (Text): Enter the contact information for the person who resolved the incident.
* **Last updated by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who last updated the incident.
* **Last updated by email** (Text): Enter the contact information for the person who last updated the incident.
* **Comments** (Note): Enter any additional comments about the incident.
* **Attachments** (Note): Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Reporter contact number** (Text): Enter your contact information for follow-up.
* **Last update by** (Text): Enter the name of the person who last updated the incident.
* **Last update email** (Text): Enter the email address for the person who last updated the incident.
