# Digital Workforce Enablement Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to contact the Digital Workforce Enablement team with questions, feedback, or requests related to our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Input (a brief message about your query) in the first field.
2. Enter your name in the second field.
3. Provide your email address in the third field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., user@example.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
5. If applicable, provide the name of your company in the fifth field.
6. Type your message in the sixth field. This is where you can provide more detailed information about your query.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: A brief description of your query or question.
* **Name**: A field to enter your name.
* **Email**: A field to enter a valid email address, such as user@example.com.
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
* **Company (optional)**: If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, you can enter its name here.
* **Message**: A field to type your detailed message or question.
