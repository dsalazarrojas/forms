# screen_capture_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "screen_capture_feedback_form" is used to provide feedback on screen captures. Your input will help us improve the quality and relevance of our screen captures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the "Screen Capture" page you would like to provide feedback on.
2. Complete the form fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screen Capture** (1): Enter the page number of the screen capture you are providing feedback on.
* **Description** (2): Please describe what the screen capture shows or the issue you are experiencing. Be as specific as possible.
* **Upload Files** (3): Select the relevant file(s) to attach to your feedback. You can choose from 1, 2, or 3 file options.
* **Context** (4): This is an optional field for any additional information about the screen capture or your feedback.
* **Ratings** (5): Rate the relevance or quality of the screen capture using the available scale. Please select 1, 2, or 3.
* **Comments** (6): Additional comments or questions about the screen capture.
* **Email** (7): Enter your email address (if you want to receive a copy of the feedback).
* **Phone** (8): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted to discuss further).

## Tips
- Please provide detailed and specific feedback to help us improve the quality of our screen captures.
- Make sure to complete the form with all required fields to ensure accurate feedback processing.
- You can upload more than one file but please make sure to select relevant ones.
