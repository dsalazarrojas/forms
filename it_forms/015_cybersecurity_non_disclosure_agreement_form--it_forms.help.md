# Cybersecurity Non Disclosure Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Non Disclosure Agreement Form is a legal document that ensures the confidentiality and security of sensitive information shared with employees, vendors, or contractors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, following the instructions provided.
3. Sign and date the agreement as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information (<code>employee_info</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Provide your personal details including employee name, title, department, and any other relevant information.
* **Agreement Type (<code>agreement_type</code>, <type>select_one</type>, <required>false>):** Choose the type of agreement you are entering into (Employee, Vendor, or Contractor).
* **Agreement Start Date (<code>agreement_start_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Enter the date the agreement starts.
* **Agreement End Date (<code>agreement_end_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Enter the date the agreement ends.
* **Agreement Terms (<code>agreement_terms</code>, <type>note</type>, <required>false>):** Describe the terms of the agreement in detail.
* **Signed By (<code>signed_by</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Provide the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Employee Title (<code>employee_title</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your job title.
* **Employee Department (<code>employee_department</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your department name.
* **Vendor Name (<code>vendor_name</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** If a vendor, enter your name.
* **Vendor Address (<code>vendor_address</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your company's address.
* **Contractor Name (<code>contractor_name</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** If a contractor, enter your name.
* **Contractor Address (<code>contractor_address</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your address.
* **Manager Name (<code>manager_name</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Title (<code>manager_title</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager Department (<code>manager_department</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Enter your manager's department name.
* **Employee Sign Date (<code>employee_sign_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Date you signed the agreement.
* **Vendor Sign Date (<code>vendor_sign_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Date the vendor signed the agreement.
* **Contractor Sign Date (<code>contractor_sign_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Date the contractor signed the agreement.
* **Manager Sign Date (<code>manager_sign_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Date your manager signed the agreement.
* **Company Sign Date (<code>company_sign_date</code>, <type>date</type>, <required>false>):** Date the company signed the agreement.
* **Employee Signature (<code>employee_signature</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Sign your signature.
* **Vendor Signature (<code>vendor_signature</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** If a vendor, sign your signature.
* **Contractor Signature (<code>contractor_signature</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** If a contractor, sign your signature.
* **Manager Signature (<code>manager_signature</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Sign your manager's signature.
* **Company Signature (<code>company_signature</code>, <type>text</type>, <required>false>):** Sign the company's signature.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly before signing the agreement. Review the agreement terms carefully before signing.
