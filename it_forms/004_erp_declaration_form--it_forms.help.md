# ERP Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employees' work-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company information in the "Company" field.
2. Select your role in the workplace by choosing from the options provided.
3. For your department, select all that apply from the options.
4. Enter the date and time you will be working. This is a required field.
5. Enter your name, which is also a required field.
6. In the "Declaration" field, provide any necessary comments or explanations.
7. Choose the correct route for your work-related activities from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the workplace from the options provided.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select all the departments you are responsible for or associated with.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your work-related activity.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of your work-related activity.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name, which will be used for identification purposes.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, note, required): Provide any necessary comments or explanations for your work-related activity.
* **Route** (`route`, select_multiple, required): Choose the correct route for your work-related activities from the provided options.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your selections and entries before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be used for administrative purposes.
