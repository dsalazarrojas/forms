# Business Internet Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather business internet quote requirements from customers. It is intended for business customers who require a quote for their internet services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name and contact information, including contact name, phone number, and email.
2. Provide the address where the service is needed.
3. Select the type of internet service you require.
4. Select your business type and employee count.
5. If you currently have an internet provider, please provide the name and speed of your current provider.
6. Specify your internet usage patterns and required download and upload speeds.
7. Select the number of connected devices.
8. Choose how critical uptime is for your business and if you have or need a backup connection.
9. Select your preferred contract length.
10. Indicate your expected monthly budget.
11. Choose when you need the service installed.
12. If desired, select additional services you are interested in.
13. If you have any special requirements or considerations, please provide them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the primary contact person's name for your business.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the direct phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Service Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full address where the service is needed.
* **Service Type Needed** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of internet service required (Fiber optic, Cable, DSL, Satellite, Fixed wireless, Dedicated ethernet, Not sure).
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry classification of your business (Office, Retail, Restaurant, Healthcare, Education, Manufacturing, Warehouse, Home office, Other).
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate number of employees at this location.
* **Current Internet Provider** (`current_provider`, `text`, optional): If you currently have an internet provider, enter the name of the provider.
* **Current Download Speed** (`current_speed`, `number`, optional): If you currently have an internet provider, enter your current download speed in Mbps.
* **Required Download Speed** (`speed_requirements`, `select_one`, required): Select the minimum download speed needed (Up to 25 Mbps, 25-100 Mbps, 100-500 Mbps, 500 Mbps - 1 Gbps, 1+ Gbps, Not sure).
* **Required Upload Speed** (`upload_speed_requirements`, `select_one`, required): Select the minimum upload speed needed (Up to 5 Mbps, 5-20 Mbps, 20-50 Mbps, 50-100 Mbps, 100+ Mbps, Not sure).
* **Primary Internet Usage** (`usage_patterns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply (Email and web browsing, Video conferencing, Cloud applications, Large file transfers, Video streaming, VoIP phone system, Customer wifi, Server hosting).
* **Number of Connected Devices** (`number_of_devices`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate number of devices that will connect.
* **Reliability Requirements** (`reliiability_requirements`, `select_one`, required): Choose how critical uptime is for your business (Mission critical - 99.99 percent uptime, Very important - 99.9 percent uptime, Important - 99 percent uptime, Standard availability acceptable).
* **Backup Internet Connection** (`backup_connection`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have or need a backup connection (Have backup already, Need backup solution, Not needed).
* **Contract Term Preference** (`contract_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contract length (Month-to-month, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, Flexible).
* **Monthly Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select your expected monthly budget for internet (Under $100, $100-$250, $250-$500, $500-$1000, Over $1000).
* **Installation Timeline** (`installation_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you need the service installed (Immediately, Within 1 week, Within 2 weeks, Within 1 month, Flexible).
* **Additional Services Interested In** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select other services you are interested in (Static IP addresses, Managed router, Network security, Web hosting, Email hosting, VoIP phone service, None).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requirements or considerations, enter them in the text field.

Note: Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure that the quote provided is accurate and meets your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
