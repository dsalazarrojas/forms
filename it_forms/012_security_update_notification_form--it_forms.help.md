# Security Update Notification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information about potential security threats and help the security team take action to address them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu in response to each question.
   - "form_label_1" is a confirmation that the IT teams have received a security update.
   - "acknowledgment" is a confirmation that the IT teams acknowledge the update.
2. Provide feedback about the update in the text field "feedback".
3. If applicable, provide more information about the security update in the "security_update" text field.
4. Enter the date and time the update was submitted in the "submit_date" and "submit_time" fields.
5. Enter the IT team's name, email, and phone number in the "it_teams_name", "it_teams_email", and "it_teams_phone" fields.
6. Confirm if the IT team is located in the "it_teams_department" field.
7. Please enter your name, email, and phone number in the "submitter_name", "submitter_email", and "submitter_phone" fields.
8. Enter your department in the "submitter_department" field.
9. Provide any additional comments in the "submitter_comment" field.
10. Enter your position in the "submitter_position" field.
11. Confirm if you have any comments in the "submitter_comments" field.
12. Enter the date and time of the review in the "review_date" and "review_time" fields.
13. Enter the name and phone number of the reviewer in the "reviewer_name" and "reviewer_phone" fields.
14. Provide any additional comments in the "reviewer_comments" field.

Note: All fields marked "required" are required, and you must fill them out to submit the form. The other fields are optional, but providing information in them can help the security team better understand and address the issue.
