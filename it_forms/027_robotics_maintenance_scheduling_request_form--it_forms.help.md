# Robotics Maintenance Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Maintenance Details
**Maintenance Details (text)** (`maintenance_details`, text, optional): In this field, please provide a detailed description of the maintenance task, including any specific issues or problems you are experiencing with the robot.

### Preferred Maintenance Times
**Preferred Maintenance Times (Select Multiple)** (`preferred_dates`, select_multiple, false): This field allows you to select one or more times when maintenance can be performed. This will help the maintenance team plan ahead and schedule maintenance around your preferred times.

### Submitter Information
**Submitter Information (text)** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant information about the submitter in this field.

### Robot Information
**Robot Information (text)** (`robot_info`, text, optional): In this field, please provide any relevant information about the robot that requires maintenance.

### Equipment Support
**Equipment Support (text)** (`equipment_support`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant information about the equipment that requires maintenance.

### Submitter
**Submitter (text)** (`submitter`, text, optional): In this field, please enter the name of the submitter who is requesting maintenance.

### Submitter Email
**Email (email)** (`submitter_email`, email, false): Enter the submitter's email address in this field so that we can contact them with updates on their maintenance request.

### Submitter Phone
**Phone (text)** (`submitter_phone`, text, false): Enter the submitter's phone number in this field so that we can contact them with updates on their maintenance request.
