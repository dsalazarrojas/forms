# data_storage_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The data_storage_billing_form is designed to collect information about users and their payment details for data storage services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user information fields on the first page.
2. Provide the required payment information on the second page.
3. Review the terms of service on the last page, which is a non-editable field for informational purposes only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Info** (user_info, text, optional): Fill in the user's name, email, and other relevant information as prompted.
  - This field is optional and allows you to provide additional context about the user.
  - You can fill in any relevant details about the user, such as their company name or location.

- **Payment Info** (payment_info, text, optional): Enter your payment details, such as the amount due, billing address, and payment method.
  - This field is optional but necessary for payment processing.
  - Make sure to fill in accurate and detailed information to ensure smooth payment processing.

- **Terms of Service** (terms_of_service, note, optional): Review and acknowledge our terms of service.
  - This field is a non-editable note that provides additional information about our services.
  - Review the terms carefully before proceeding with payment processing.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in accurate and detailed information in the user and payment fields.
* Review the terms of service carefully to understand our services and terms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
