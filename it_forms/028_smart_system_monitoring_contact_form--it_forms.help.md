# Smart System Monitoring Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide contact information and send messages to the support team regarding smart system monitoring issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your contact name from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If you have an attachment related to your issue, you can upload it in the "Attachment" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your issue in the "Description" field.
6. Select the type of system you are experiencing issues with from the "System Type" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your message in the "Message" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or information about your system, enter it in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name (select_one)**: Select your name from the dropdown menu to identify yourself.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to respond to your query.
* **Phone Number (text)**: If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you directly.
* **Attachment (text)**: Upload any relevant attachment related to your issue.
* **Description (text)**: Provide a brief description of your issue.
* **System Type (select_one)**: Select the type of system you are experiencing issues with.
* **Message (text)**: Enter your message to the support team.
* **Additional Comments (text)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your system.
