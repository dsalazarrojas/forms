# AI Inventory Tracking Pilot Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants who wish to participate in the AI inventory tracking pilot application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your company name in the field labeled "Company name".
2. Enter your name in the field labeled "Primary contact name".
3. Enter your contact email in the field labeled "Contact email".
4. Describe the expected scope of inventory in the field labeled "Expected scope of inventory".
5. State what the pilot aims to validate in the field labeled "Pilot objectives".
6. If applicable, enter the names and emails of technical contacts in the field labeled "Technical contacts".
7. If you have any known constraints or blockers, enter them in the field labeled "Known constraints or blockers".
8. Choose a preferred start date for the pilot project in the field labeled "Preferred start date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of the company applying for the pilot project.
- **Primary contact name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project lead or manager.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for pilot coordination.
- **Expected scope of inventory** (`expected_scope`, text, required): Describe the types and size of assets you expect to track.
- **Pilot objectives** (`objectives`, text, required): State what the pilot aims to validate or achieve.
- **Technical contacts** (`technical_contacts`, text, optional): List the names and emails of engineers or IT contacts involved in integration.
- **Known constraints or blockers** (`constraints`, text, optional): Enter any known constraints, limitations, or blockers.
- **Preferred start date** (`preferred_start_date`, date, optional): Choose a preferred start date for the pilot project in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the fields.
- Use standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for the start date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
