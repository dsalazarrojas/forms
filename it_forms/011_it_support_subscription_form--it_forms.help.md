# it_support_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users requesting support from the organization. It is designed to gather specific details necessary for efficient support request handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Details" section with your name and contact information.
2. In the "Support Request" section, provide a detailed description of your support request.
3. In the "IT Needs" section, indicate whether you require immediate support or not.
4. In the "Contact Information" section, provide your contact details.
5. In the "Preferred Contact Method" section, select your preferred method of contact.
6. If you have provided a valid email address, you can enter it in the "Email" field.
7. If you have a phone number, you can enter it in the "Phone" field.
8. If you are using a support chat, enter the chat URL in the "Support Chat URL" field.
9. Finally, provide the name and contact information of your support agent in the "Support Agent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`User Details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information in this section.
* **Support Request** (`Support Request`, note, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your support request.
* **IT Needs** (`IT Needs`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you require immediate support or not.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details in this field.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred Contact Method`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred method of contact.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted through email.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted through phone.
* **Support Chat URL** (`Support Chat URL`, text, required: false): If you are using a support chat, enter the chat URL in this field.
* **Support Agent** (`Support Agent`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of your support agent.
