# Database Audit Extension Documentation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for requesting documentation related to database audit extensions. It collects information to help developers and maintainers create accurate and thorough documentation for the extension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request Form** section with a clear and concise description of your request.
2. Attach any **Supporting Materials** that are relevant to your request.
3. List any necessary **Approvals** from relevant individuals or teams.
4. Provide any **Review Notes** that you would like the reviewer to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form**: A brief description of your request for database audit extension documentation. Please provide the context and details necessary for us to understand your needs.
* **Supporting Materials**: Attach any documents, screenshots, or other materials that support your request. This could include error logs, screenshots, or diagrams.
* **Approvals**: List the names and titles of any individuals or teams that have reviewed and approved your request.
* **Review Notes**: Any comments or feedback that you would like the reviewer to consider when reviewing your request. 

## Tips
- Please be clear and concise with your request and supporting materials.
- If you have any additional information that you would like to provide, attach it to the supporting materials section.
- If you are unsure about what information to provide, feel free to ask us for guidance.
