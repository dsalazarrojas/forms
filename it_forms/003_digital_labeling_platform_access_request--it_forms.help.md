# Digital Labeling Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the digital labeling platform. Please fill out the form below to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Project Details" field with a brief description of your project or purpose for accessing the platform.
2.  Enter your "Requester Name" as you want it to be displayed on the platform.
3.  Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  If you have read and agree to the "Terms of Use", select the "Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details (project_details)
*   **Project Details**: A brief description of your project or purpose for accessing the platform. Please provide as much detail as possible.
### Requester Name (requester_name)
*   **Requester Name**: Enter the name as you want it to be displayed on the platform.
### Email (email)
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
### Phone (phone)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number that the platform administrators can contact you with.
### Terms of Use (terms_of_use)
*   **Terms of Use**: If you have read and agree to the platform's terms of use, select this field.
### Agreement (agreement)
*   **Agreement**: If you have read and agree to the platform's terms of use, select this field.

## Tips
*   Double-check your information before submission to avoid errors.
*   If you have any issues or questions, contact the platform administrators for assistance.
