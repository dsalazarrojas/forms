# network_communication_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on network communication practices, communication channels, and response times within a team or department.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For questions with options, select the answer that best describes your experience.
3. Enter your feedback and comments in the text fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.
