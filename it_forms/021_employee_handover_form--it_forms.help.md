# Employee Handover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Handover Form is used to collect information about an employee's transfer, ensuring a smooth handover process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read this guide carefully before filling out the form.
- Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals from the relevant parties involved.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee
- **Employee** (text, required: false): Enter the name and employee ID of the transferred employee.

### Employee Transfer Date
- **Employee Transfer Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date the employee is being transferred to a new role or position.

### Asset Transfer Details
- **Asset Transfer Details** (text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the assets being transferred, including any equipment, software, or other resources involved.

### Assets Handover
- **Assets Handover** (select multiple, required: false): Check all the boxes that apply to the assets being transferred:
    - 'Yes' for assets that have been transferred.
    - 'No' for assets that have not been transferred.

### Asset Confirmation
- **Asset Confirmation** (select one, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you are confirming the asset transfer, 'No' otherwise.

### Signatures
- **Signatures** (select multiple, required: false): Check all the boxes that apply for signatures required for the transfer:
    - 'Yes' for signatures required.
    - 'No' for signatures not required.

### Employee Transfer Confirmation
- **Employee Transfer Confirmation** (select one, required: false): Check 'Yes' to confirm that the employee transfer is complete, 'No' otherwise.
