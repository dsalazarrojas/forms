# software_tool_release_it_professional_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on the release of a software tool, providing insight into its quality and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on this form based on your experience with the software tool.
2. If you haven't used the software tool before, you should not fill out this form.
3. Fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional):
	+ Please enter the date you first released (or last used) the software tool.
* **Release Version** (`release_version`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the version of the software tool you released or used.
* **Feedback on Feature Improvements** (`feedback_on_feature_improvements`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- Very Good
		- Somewhat Good
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Bad
		- Very Bad
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- Technical Issues
		- Functional Issues
		- User Interface Issues
		- Other (please specify)
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with the software tool:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional):
	+ Share your thoughts on your experience with the software tool.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your contact email (we may reach out for follow-up questions).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number (we may call you for follow-up questions).

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* You can choose multiple options for "Feedback on Feature Improvements" and "Issues Encountered".
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us.
