# Workforce Planning Technology Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Planning Technology Pilot Application Form is designed to collect information from potential applicants for the Workforce Planning Technology Pilot Application. This form helps the organizers to determine eligibility and suitability for the pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2. Provide your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select your work location from the options in the "Work Location" field.
4. Choose the types of work you will be undertaking in the "Work Type" field.
5. If applicable, enter your expected start and end dates of work in the "Work Start Date" and "Work End Date" fields respectively.
6. Enter your expected work hours in the "Work Hours" field.
7. Select the days you will be working in the "Work Days" field.
8. Choose the shifts you will be working in the "Work Shift" field.
9. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
10. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
11. Use the "Additional Info" field to provide any additional details or information not covered by the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Job Title**: Provide your current or proposed job title in the organization.
* **Work Location**: Select the location where you will be working (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Work Type**: Choose the types of work you will be undertaking (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Work Start Date**: If you know the start date of your work, enter it. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Work End Date**: If you know the end date of your work, enter it. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you expect to work per day or week.
* **Work Days**: Select the days you will be working (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Work Shift**: Choose the shifts you will be working (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Additional Info**: Use this field to provide any additional information not covered by the other fields.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you are unsure about any field, use the "Additional Info" field to provide context or clarification.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the application administrators for assistance.
