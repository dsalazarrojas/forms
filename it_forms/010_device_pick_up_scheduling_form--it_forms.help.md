# Device Pick Up Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Device Pick Up Scheduling Form is designed to make it easy for customers to schedule a device pick up from a repair shop. This form helps you provide your contact details, device information, and preferred pick up time, ensuring that the repair shop has all the necessary information to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Provide your device information in the "Device Info" section.
3. Specify your preferred pick up time in the "Preferred Time" section.
4. Enter your location in the "Location" section.
5. Choose your appointment time in the "Appointment Time" section.
6. If applicable, provide any additional information about the repair shop in the "Repair Shop Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your name, phone number, and email address so that the repair shop can contact you for further information or to confirm the pick up.

### Device Info
* **Device Info (Device Info)** (`Device Info`, `text`, optional): Describe your device and its current condition, including any issues you want to be addressed during the repair.

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time (Preferred Time)** (`Preferred Time`, `text`, optional): Specify a preferred time for the repair shop to pick up your device. Please use the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).

### Location
* **Location (Location)** (`Location`, `text`, optional): Enter your location so that the repair shop knows where to pick up your device.

### Appointment Time
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time)** (`Appointment Time`, `time`, optional): Select the appointment time that works best for you from the available options.

### Repair Shop Info
* **Repair Shop Info (Repair Shop Info)** (`Repair Shop Info`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide any additional information about the repair shop that you are using, you can do so in this field. 

### Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for the appointment time (HH:MM).
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact the repair shop directly.
