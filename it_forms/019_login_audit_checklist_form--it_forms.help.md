# Login Audit Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct a comprehensive review of login controls on a system. This form is used to ensure that all login controls are properly set up and functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the system's login controls and answer the questions on the form based on your findings.
2. Answer the first three questions (it_forms_login_controls, login_controls_checked, and follow_up_actions) to determine the status of the login controls.
3. Review the remaining questions on the form, answering each one based on your findings.
4. Enter any comments or follow-up actions in the relevant fields.
5. Update the form as necessary based on changes to the system's login controls.
