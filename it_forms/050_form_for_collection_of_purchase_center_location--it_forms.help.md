# Form For Collection Of Purchase Center Location - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a purchase center location. It will ask for the location's name, its status, and any additional notes about it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the **Collection of Purchase Center Location** field with the name of the location.
2. Select the **purchase_center_location** field to choose the correct location from the options provided.
3. For the **collection of purchase center location description**, you can add any additional text that describes the location.
4. In the **purchase_center_location_status** field, select one or more of the statuses that apply to the location.
5. Finally, in the **purchase_center_location_notes** field, you can add any extra comments about the location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Collection of Purchase Center Location** (`collection_of_purchase_center_location`, text, required): Enter the name of the purchase center location.
* **purchase_center_location** (`purchase_center_location`, select_one, required): Select the correct location from the options provided.
* **collection of purchase center location description** (`collection_of_purchase_center_location_description`, text, required): Add a brief description of the location.
* **purchase_center_location_status** (`purchase_center_location_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more statuses that apply to the location.
* **purchase_center_location_notes** (`purchase_center_location_notes`, note, optional): Add any extra comments or notes about the location.
