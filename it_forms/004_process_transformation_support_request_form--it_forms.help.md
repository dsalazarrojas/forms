# Process Transformation Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Transformation Support Request Form is designed to gather information about process transformation requests, ensuring that the support team has all the necessary details to address the issue effectively. This form is typically used by team members, team leads, or support requestors to submit requests for process transformation support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's purpose and understand that it's designed for process transformation support requests.
2.  Ensure that you have all the necessary information and details before starting to fill out the form.
3.  Fill in the required fields:
    *   Team Members: Select 'Yes' if you are part of the team involved in the process transformation.
    *   Team Lead: Enter the name of the team lead responsible for the process transformation.
    *   Problem Statement: Describe the problem or issue you're experiencing with the current process.
    *   Current Process: Describe the current process or steps involved in the transformation.
    *   Impact: Explain the potential impact of the process transformation on the business or organization.
    *   Support Requestor: Enter the name of the person or team requesting support.
    *   Support Requestor Email: Enter the email address of the support requestor.
    *   Support Requestor Message: Provide a detailed message describing the support request.
    *   Submitter: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
    *   Submitter Email: Enter your email address.
    *   Support Requestor Message 2: If necessary, provide additional information or context for the support request.
4.  Fill in any other optional fields as required or desired:
    *   Team Lead Email: Enter the email address of the team lead, if applicable.
    *   Ideal Process: Describe the ideal process or steps you envision for the transformation.
    *   Change Requestors: If there are multiple people or teams requesting changes, list their names.
    *   Submitter Phone: Enter your phone number, if desired.
    *   Submitter Message: If you want to provide additional context or information, use this field.
