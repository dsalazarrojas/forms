# it_support_contact_form_for_teachers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable teachers to contact the support team for assistance with IT-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used to contact you for further communication.
* Enter a brief description of the issue you are experiencing in the "Description" field.
* Select the category of the issue from the options provided in the "Category" field. This will help the support team to prioritize and direct your request.
* You can select one or multiple users to assign to handle your issue in the "Assign to" field.
* If applicable, choose the priority of your issue from the options provided in the "Priority" field.
* If you are able to, provide the date and time of when the issue started in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
* If you are reporting an issue from a specific location, please enter it in the "Location" field.
* You can add any comments or additional information about the issue in the "Comments" field.
* Finally, select the tool assigned to handle your issue from the options provided in the "Assigned tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): If you have a preferred contact number, enter it here.
* **Description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Category** (select one, optional): Select the category of the issue.
* **Assign to** (select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple users to assign to handle your issue.
* **Priority** (select one, optional): If applicable, choose the priority of your issue.
* **Date** (date, optional): If you are able to, provide the date when the issue started.
* **Time** (time, optional): If you are able to, provide the time when the issue started.
* **Location** (text, optional): If you are reporting an issue from a specific location, enter it here.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any comments or additional information about the issue.
* **Assigned tool** (select one, optional): Select the tool assigned to handle your issue.
* **Submit** (text, required): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to send your request to the support team.
