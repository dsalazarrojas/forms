# Submit Product - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit new products to a database or platform for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are creating a new product entry, not updating an existing one.
2. Check that you have not already submitted this product.
3. Fill out all required fields marked with a red asterisk (*).
4. Complete the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Product** (`submit_product_form`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the product you are submitting.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the product name, this will help identify the product.
* **description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the product in detail, including its features and benefits.
* **product price range** (`product_price_range`, number, optional): Enter the price range for the product (e.g., $0-$100).
* **product link** (`product_link`, text, required): Enter a link to the product website or online store.
* **product image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of the product for better visibility.
* **product category** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Select the product category from the dropdown menu (e.g., Electronics, Furniture, Software).
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to complete the submission.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before clicking the "Submit" button.
* Use a clear and concise description to help reviewers understand the product better.
* Upload a high-quality image to increase the product's visibility.
* Review the product category carefully to ensure it matches the product's actual category.
