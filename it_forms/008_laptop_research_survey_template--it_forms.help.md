# Laptop Research Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights into users' laptop usage habits, preferences, and purchasing behavior. The collected data will help us better understand how people use their laptops and what features they value most.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, being as specific as possible.
2. Make sure to answer truthfully, as your responses will help us improve our understanding of laptop usage.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to answer "don't know" or "not applicable."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **how often do you use your laptop** (`laptop_usage_frequency`, number, optional): Answer how often you use your laptop in terms of the number of days per week.
* **what is your primary use of laptops** (`laptop_usage_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your primary use of laptops. This could be for work, personal, school, or other purposes.
* **what is your preference for screen size** (`laptop_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the screen size options that you prefer. You can choose from 11-12 inches, 13-14 inches, 15-16 inches, 17-18 inches, or don't know.
* **how often do you buy new laptops** (`laptop_purchase_frequency`, select_one, required): Answer how often you buy new laptops. You can choose from rarely, occasionally, frequently, all the time, or never.
* **what drives your laptop purchase decisions** (`laptop_purchase_reason`, text, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about what drives your laptop purchase decisions.
* **how much do you typically spend on laptops** (`laptop_purchase_cost`, number, optional): Answer how much you typically spend on laptops, in dollars.
* **do you usually purchase warranty for laptops** (`laptop_purchase_warranty`, select_one, required): Choose whether you usually purchase warranty for laptops. You can select True, False, or don't know.
* **how many years have you used laptops** (`laptop_usage_duration`, number, optional): Answer how many years you have used laptops.
* **what would you recommend to a friend** (`laptop_recommended`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more laptop brands that you would recommend to a friend. You can choose from Apple, Dell, HP, Lenovo, or other.
* **how satisfied are you with your laptop** (`laptop_satisfaction`, number, optional): Answer on a scale of 1-5 how satisfied you are with your laptop.
* **reason for satisfaction or dissatisfaction** (`laptop_satisfaction_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with your laptop.
* **who would you recommend to buy your current laptop** (`laptop_recommender`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more people you would recommend to buy your current laptop. You can choose from friend, family member, colleague, or not applicable.
* **relationship to the person you would recommend** (`laptop_recommender_relation`, text, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about your relationship to the person you would recommend.
* **reason for recommendation or non-recommendation** (`laptop_recommendation_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your recommendation or non-recommendation.
* **who purchases your laptop** (`laptop_purchaser`, select_one, required): Choose who purchases your laptop. You can select self, family member, colleague, or friend.
* **relationship to the person purchasing the laptop** (`laptop_purchaser_relation`, text, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about your relationship to the person purchasing the laptop.
* **do you purchase warranty for your current laptop** (`laptop_warranty_purchased`, select_one, required): Choose whether you purchase warranty for your current laptop. You can select True, False, or don't know.
* **satisfaction of the person purchasing the laptop** (`laptop_satisfaction_purchaser`, number, optional): Answer on a scale of 1-5 how satisfied the person purchasing the laptop is.
* **reason for satisfaction or dissatisfaction of purchaser** (`laptop_satisfaction_purchaser_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the purchaser's satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **what is the relationship of the purchaser to you** (`laptop_warranty_purchaser`, text, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about the relationship of the purchaser to you.
* **reason for satisfaction or dissatisfaction of warranty purchaser** (`laptop_warranty_purchaser_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the purchaser's satisfaction or dissatisfaction.
* **how satisfied are you with your current laptop** (`laptop_satisfaction_self`, number, optional): Answer on a scale of 1-5 how satisfied you are with your current laptop.
* **reason for satisfaction or dissatisfaction with laptop** (`laptop_satisfaction_self_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with your laptop.
* **would you recommend a laptop to someone else** (`laptop_recommendation`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend a laptop to someone else. You can select True, False, or maybe.
* **reason for recommendation or non-recommendation** (`laptop_recommendation_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your recommendation or non-recommendation.
