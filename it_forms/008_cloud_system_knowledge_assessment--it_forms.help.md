# Cloud System Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloud System Knowledge Assessment form is designed to help you evaluate your cloud system knowledge and experience. It's a self-assessment tool that guides you through a series of questions and ratings to help you identify areas where you need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Candidate or Employee Name: Please type in your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. Enter your Email Address: This is your work email address. Please ensure it's accurate.
3. Enter the Assessment Date: Select the date of your assessment.
4. Select your Cloud Experience Level: Choose the level of your experience from Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
5. Select your Primary Cloud Platform Experience: Choose the cloud platform you have the most experience with from AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Multiple Platforms, or None.
6. (Optional) Select your Years of Cloud Experience: If you have experience, enter the number of years.
7. (Optional) Select any Cloud Certifications you hold: Choose all that apply.
8. Rate your IaaS Architecture Knowledge: Select your knowledge level from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
9. Rate your PaaS and Containerization Knowledge: Select your knowledge level from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
10. Rate your SaaS Integration Knowledge: Select your knowledge level from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
11. Rate your Cloud Networking Knowledge: Select your knowledge level from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
12. Rate your Cloud Security and Compliance Knowledge: Select your knowledge level from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
13. (Optional) Select your Automation Tools Proficiency: Choose all the tools you know.
14. Rate your API Development and Integration Knowledge: Select your experience level from No Experience, Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
15. (Optional) Describe a Complex Cloud Problem You Solved: Share a brief description of a challenging cloud problem you've solved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate or Employee Name** (1, text, required): Type in your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your work email address for identification purposes.
* **Assessment Date** (3, date, required): Enter the date of your assessment.
* **Cloud Experience Level** (4, select_one, required): Select the level of your experience from Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Primary Cloud Platform Experience** (5, select_one, required): Choose the cloud platform you have the most experience with from AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Multiple Platforms, or None.
* **Years of Cloud Experience** (6, number, optional): Enter the number of years of cloud experience you have (if applicable).
* **Cloud Certifications Held** (7, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the cloud certifications you hold.
* **IaaS Architecture Knowledge** (8, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of IaaS architecture from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
* **PaaS and Containerization Knowledge** (9, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of PaaS and containerization from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
* **SaaS Integration Knowledge** (10, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of SaaS integration from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
* **Cloud Networking Knowledge** (11, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of cloud networking from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
* **Cloud Security and Compliance Knowledge** (12, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of cloud security and compliance from Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Expert.
* **Automation Tools Proficiency** (13, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the automation tools you know.
* **API Development and Integration Knowledge** (14, select_one, optional): Rate your knowledge of API development and integration from No Experience, Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Cloud Problem-Solving Example** (15, text, optional): Share a brief description of a complex cloud problem you've solved.

Note: Please review this help guide to ensure you're using the correct terminology and format for your form.
