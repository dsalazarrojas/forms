# Data Security Standards Comparison - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and compare data security standards and risk factors. It helps to identify potential risks and make informed decisions about data security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the first column "Security Standards Comparison" (questions 1-4).
2. In the second column, answer the questions related to risk factors (questions 5-6).
3. In the third column, provide your recommendations and evaluation comments.
4. Make sure to fill out all the required fields and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Security Standards Comparison** (1) (Select multiple): This field asks you to compare the security standards of multiple options. Select all the options that apply.
* **Security Standards** (2) (Text): This field is for entering your security standards, such as regulatory requirements or internal policies.
* **Risk Factors** (3) (Select one): This field asks you to identify one risk factor that is relevant to your security standards comparison.
* **Recommendations** (4) (Select multiple): This field is for providing recommendations related to your security standards comparison.
* **Evaluations** (5) (Select multiple): This field is for evaluating your security standards comparison.
* **Decision Ready** (6) (Select multiple): This field is for indicating if you are decision ready with your security standards comparison.
* **Security Standards Recommendations** (7) (Text): This field is for entering security standards recommendations.
* **Decision Ready Insights** (8) (Text): This field is for providing insights on your decision-ready status.
* **Security Standards Comparison Template** (9) (Text): This field is for entering a template used for security standards comparison.
* **Template Version** (10) (Text): This field is for entering the version of the template used.
* **Evaluation Method** (11) (Text): This field is for entering the evaluation method used.
* **Evaluation Comments** (12) (Text): This field is for entering comments on the evaluation.
* **Recommendation Method** (13) (Text): This field is for entering the recommendation method used.
* **Recommendation Comments** (14) (Text): This field is for entering comments on the recommendation.
* **Decision Ready Recommendation** (15) (Text): This field is for entering a decision-ready recommendation.
* **Evaluation Method Other** (16) (Text): This field is for entering an alternative evaluation method.
* **Security Standards Comparison Form Version** (17) (Text): This field is for entering the version of the form used.
* **Form Last Updated** (18) (Date): This field is for tracking when the form was last updated.
* **Form Last Updated Time** (19) (Time): This field is for tracking the time the form was last updated.
