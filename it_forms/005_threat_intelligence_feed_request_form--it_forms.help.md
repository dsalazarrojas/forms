# Threat Intelligence Feed Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Threat Intelligence Feed Request Form is used to gather information about potential threats from users. It helps us to track and analyze threats more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure you are reporting a new threat, not an updated or duplicate one.
3. Enter a brief description of the threat in the "Threat Description" field.
4. Select the severity of the threat in the "Threat Severity" field.
5. Select the status of the threat in the "Threat Status" field.
6. Enter the URL related to the threat in the "Threat URL" field.
7. Enter the date and time of when you reported the threat in the "Requestor Reported Date" and "Requestor Reported Time" fields.
8. Enter your name, email, phone number, and job title in the "Requestor Name", "Requestor Email", "Requestor Phone", and "Requestor Job Title" fields.
9. Enter your organization, department, job function, and job location in the "Requestor Organization", "Requestor Department", "Requestor Job Function", and "Requestor Job Location" fields.
10. Enter any additional comments about the threat in the "Requestor Reported Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required): Enter a brief name for this request.
* **Threat Type** (`threat_type`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if this is a new threat.
* **Threat Description** (`threat_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the threat.
* **Threat Severity** (`threat_severity`, `select_one`, required): Select the severity of the threat (High, Medium, Low).
* **Threat Status** (`threat_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the threat (New, Updated, Duplicate).
* **Threat URL** (`threat_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL related to the threat.
* **Threat Date** (`threat_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you reported the threat.
* **Threat Time** (`threat_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you reported the threat.
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Requestor Notes** (`requestor_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Requestor Organization** (`requestor_organization`, `text`, required): Enter your organization name.
* **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name.
* **Requestor Job Title** (`requestor_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Requestor Job Title Abbreviation** (`requestor_job_title_abbreviation`, `text`, required): Enter a abbreviation for your job title.
* **Requestor Job Function** (`requestor_job_function`, `text`, required): Enter your job function.
* **Requestor Job Location** (`requestor_job_location`, `text`, required): Enter your job location.
* **Requestor Phone Extension** (`requestor_phone_extension`, `text`, required): Enter your phone extension.
* **Requestor Fax Number** (`requestor_fax_number`, `text`, required): Enter your fax number.
* **Requestor Reported Date** (`requestor_reported_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you reported the threat.
* **Requestor Reported Time** (`requestor_reported_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you reported the threat.
* **Requestor Reported Status** (`requestor_reported_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your report (New, Updated, Duplicate).
* **Requestor Reported URL** (`requestor_reported_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL related to your report.
* **Requestor Reported Comments** (`requestor_reported_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about your report.

Note: This help guide is not intended to be comprehensive, but rather a quick reference for users to fill out the form correctly.
