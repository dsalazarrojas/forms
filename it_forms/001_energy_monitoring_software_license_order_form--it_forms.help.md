# Energy Monitoring Software License Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential customers interested in purchasing energy monitoring software licenses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title, your company name, and address.
2. Select the license plan that suits your company's needs (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
3. Choose the duration for which you want the license to be active (30, 60, 90, or 120 days).
4. Enter your company's energy usage details.
5. Provide your contact information (first name, last name, email, and phone number).
6. Select your company's size (Small, Medium, or Large).
7. Choose the industries your company operates in (Finance, IT, or Manufacturing).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your company, if desired.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **License Plan** (`license_plan`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of license plan that suits your company's needs. The options are:
	+ Monthly: A one-month subscription to the energy monitoring software.
	+ Quarterly: A three-month subscription to the energy monitoring software.
	+ Yearly: A one-year subscription to the energy monitoring software.
* **License Term** (`license_term`, select_multiple, optional): Select the duration for which you want the license to be active. The options are:
	+ 30 days
	+ 60 days
	+ 90 days
	+ 120 days
* **Energy Usage** (`energy_usage`, number, optional): Enter your company's energy usage details (if any).
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your company. The options are:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the industries your company operates in. The options are:
	+ Finance
	+ IT
	+ Manufacturing
