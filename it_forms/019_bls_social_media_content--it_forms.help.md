# BLS Social Media Content - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect social media content submissions from authorized personnel. It is intended for internal use by the BLS team to review, approve, and schedule content for posting on various social media platforms. The form ensures that all social media content submitted meets the required standards and guidelines of the BLS team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name and email address.
2. Provide a clear and concise title for the content.
3. Choose the type of content you are submitting (e.g., text post, image, video, etc.).
4. Select the target social media platform for the content.
5. Enter a brief description of the content.
6. Identify the target audience for the content.
7. If applicable, provide more details about the associated campaign or initiative.
8. Set a desired posting date for the content.
9. If you have media attachments, such as images or videos, let us know.
10. Suggest any relevant hashtags for the content.
11. Add a call to action if desired.
12. Confirm if the content has been approved by management.
13. Add any additional notes or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name for reference.
* **Email Address** (`submitter_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for submission confirmation and contact purposes.
* **Your Title or Role** (`submitter_title`, text, not **required**): Optional field for adding a title or role for context.
* **Content Title** (`content_title`, text, **required**): Provide a clear and concise title for the content.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of content you are submitting (e.g., Text Post, Image, Video, etc.).
* **Target Platform** (`platform_target`, select_one, **required**): Select the social media platform where you want the content to be posted (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.).
* **Content Text or Description** (`content_body`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the content.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, **required**): Identify the target audience for the content.
* **Associated Campaign or Initiative** (`campaign_association`, text, not **required**): Provide more details about the associated campaign or initiative if applicable.
* **Desired Posting Date** (`posting_date`, date, **required**): Set a desired posting date for the content.
* **Do you have media attachments?** (`media_attachments`, select_one, not **required**): Let us know if you have media attachments.
* **Suggested Hashtags** (`hashtags`, text, not **required**): Suggest any relevant hashtags for the content.
* **Call to Action** (`call_to_action`, text, not **required**): Add a call to action if desired.
* **Has this been approved by management?** (`approval_status`, select_one, not **required**): Confirm if the content has been approved by management.
* **Additional Notes or Instructions** (`additional_notes`, text, not **required**): Add any additional notes or instructions.

Please complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your social media content submissions meet the required standards and guidelines of the BLS team.
