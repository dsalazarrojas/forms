# industrial-data-exchange-platform-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for companies to register on the Industrial Data Exchange Platform. It collects information about the company, its contact person, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as `required` in the form.
2. Enter the `Organization Name` of your company.
3. Enter the name and title of the `Contact Person`.
4. Enter your company's `Email`, `Phone`, and `Company Address`.
5. Select your company's `Company Type` from the options provided.
6. Select your company's `Company Industry` from the options provided.
7. Enter your company's `Registration Date`.
8. Enter your company's `Company Legal Name`, `Tax ID Number`, and `Website URL`.
9. Optionally, add a `Social Media Link` and `Company Description`.
10. Optionally, add a `Logo URL` and `Contact Person Title`, `Contact Person Phone`, and `Contact Person Email`.
11. Optionally, add the `Company Representative Title` and `Country`, `City`, `State`, and `Zip Code` of your company's representative.
12. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (Required, text): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (Required, text): Enter the name and title of the person to contact.
* **Email** (Required, email): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone** (Required, text): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Address** (Required, text): Enter your company's address.
* **Company Type** (Required, select_one): Select your company type from the options provided.
* **Company Industry** (Required, select_multiple): Select your company's industry from the options provided.
* **Registration Date** (Required, date): Enter the date your company is registering with the platform.
* **Company Legal Name** (Required, text): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Tax ID Number** (Required, text): Enter your company's tax ID number.
* **Website URL** (Required, text): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Social Media Link** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your company's social media link.
* **Company Description** (Optional, text): Optionally, add a description of your company.
* **Logo URL** (Optional, text): Optionally, add a URL to your company's logo.
* **Contact Person Title** (Optional, text): Optionally, add the title of your contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (Optional, email): Optionally, add your contact person's email address.
* **Contact Person Address** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your contact person's address.
* **Company Representative Title** (Optional, text): Optionally, add the title of your company representative.
* **Country** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your company's country.
* **City** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your city.
* **State** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your state.
* **Zip Code** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your zip code.
* **Company Representative Phone** (Optional, text): Optionally, add your company representative's phone number.
