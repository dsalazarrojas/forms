# Data Management Assessment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow end-users to request assistance from the IT department regarding data management issues. This form helps the IT team understand the scope of the issue and prioritize requests accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have read and understand the purpose of this form. Please do not submit a request if you are not sure if you have already contacted the IT department about this issue.
2. Fill out this form with the required information to ensure a complete understanding of your data management challenge.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
4. Submit the form. The IT department will review and respond to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`text`, optional): Enter your organization's name or department.
* **Contact Person** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person requesting assistance, ideally the user's name or the name of the person in charge of data management.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, optional): Enter the contact email address of the person requesting assistance.
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person requesting assistance.
* **Data Challenges** (`text`, optional): Describe the specific data management challenges you are facing, including any relevant details and context.

Note: Required fields are not explicitly marked as required in the form, but filling out all fields will help the IT department better understand your request. Please ensure to fill out as much information as possible, especially the contact fields, to enable efficient communication with the IT department.
