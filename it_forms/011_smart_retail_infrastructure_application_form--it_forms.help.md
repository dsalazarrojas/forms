# Smart Retail Infrastructure Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Smart Retail Infrastructure Application Form" is a tool used to collect information about retail infrastructure projects. It helps retailers to provide necessary details about their projects, making it easier for the project team to understand their needs and provide support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the appropriate category for your project.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as retailer details, project requirements, and store details.
3. Select the project status and store status.
4. Provide necessary details about project manager and store manager.
5. Confirm the centralised data collection method and submission status.
6. Choose the correct technology vendor.
7. Enter the date and time of data submission.
8. Finally, add any additional submission notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form id** (number): This is the unique identifier for your project.
* **category id** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the project is related to smart retail infrastructure.
* **retailer details** (text): Enter a brief description of your retail project.
* **project requirements** (text): Specify the specific requirements for your project.
* **project status** (select_multiple): Choose one or both of the options: Active or Inactive.
* **store details** (text): Provide details about your store, such as its name and address.
* **store location** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the store is located within the city or region.
* **project manager details** (text): Enter the project manager's details, including their name and designation.
* **project manager phone** (text): Enter the project manager's phone number.
* **project manager email** (email): Enter the project manager's email address.
* **technology vendor details** (text): Provide details about the technology vendor.
* **technology vendor** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the technology vendor has been selected.
* **project manager title** (select_multiple): Choose one or both of the options: Yes or No.
* **store manager details** (text): Enter the store manager's details, including their name and designation.
* **store manager phone** (text): Enter the store manager's phone number.
* **store manager email** (email): Enter the store manager's email address.
* **smart store initiatives** (text): Enter any smart store initiatives, such as smart shelves or smart displays.
* **centralised data collection** (select_multiple): Choose one or both of the options: Yes or No.
* **centralised data collection method** (select_multiple): Select one or both of the options: Yes or No.
* **data submission date** (date): Enter the date when you submitted your data.
* **data submission time** (time): Enter the time when you submitted your data.
* **submission status** (select_multiple): Choose one or both of the options: Active or Inactive.
* **submission status details** (text): Provide additional details about your submission status.
* **submission notes** (text): Enter any additional notes about your submission.
