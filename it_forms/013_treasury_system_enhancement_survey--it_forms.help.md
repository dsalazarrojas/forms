# Treasury System Enhancement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from users to enhance the Treasury system. This will help the development team understand how to improve the system to better meet the needs of its users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as you can in open-ended fields.
4. Ensure you complete all fields that are marked as required (if any).
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`, text, optional): Describe your experiences and observations about the Treasury system. What have you liked or disliked about it?
* **How often do you use the Treasury system?** (`system_usage`, select_one, optional):
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the Treasury system on a scale of 0-10.
* **What areas of the Treasury system need improvement?** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ System Navigation
	+ Reporting
	+ Data Entry
* **Any other comments or suggestions?** (`open_ended`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about the Treasury system.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your name and email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Your Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
