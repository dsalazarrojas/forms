## Factory AI Integration Council Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Factory AI Integration Council Registration Form is a mandatory form for registering members of the Factory AI Integration Council. This form is used to gather information about your background, experience, and motivations for participating in the Council.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select one or more roles that best describe your position or expertise: AI Expert, Factory Leader, or Data Scientist.
2. Choose one or more areas of interest that you are passionate about: AI Development, AI Integration, or Factory Operations.
3. Please provide a brief motivation or reason for joining the Council.
4. Provide a numerical value for your factory experience (years).
5. Enter your name as you would like to be recognized on the Council.
6. Enter a valid email address for official communications.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. If applicable, please provide the name of the tool(s) assigned to you.
9. Enter a unique numerical identifier for the tool(s) assigned to you.
10. Enter the version number of the tool(s) assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **role** (Select One or Multiple): 
  Select one or more roles that best describe your position or expertise: AI Expert, Factory Leader, or Data Scientist.
  - If you hold a Ph.D. in AI, Machine Learning, or a related field, consider selecting AI Expert.
  - If you have experience leading factory operations or teams, consider selecting Factory Leader.
  - If you have a background in Data Science, consider selecting Data Scientist.
  - Multiple selections are allowed, choose one or more that fit your experience.
- **interests** (Select One or Multiple): 
  Choose one or more areas of interest that you are passionate about: AI Development, AI Integration, or Factory Operations.
  - If you enjoy developing AI models, consider selecting AI Development.
  - If you have experience integrating AI systems into factory operations, consider selecting AI Integration.
  - If you work closely with factory operations teams, consider selecting Factory Operations.
  - Multiple selections are allowed, choose one or more that fit your interests.
- **motivation** (Text): 
  Provide a brief motivation or reason for joining the Council.
  - Be as specific as possible, e.g., "I want to share my expertise in AI model development to help improve factory operations."
  - This field is not required, but it helps the Council understand your goals and motivations.
- **factory_experience** (Number): 
  Provide a numerical value for your factory experience (years).
  - Enter the number of years you have worked in a factory setting.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the Council understand your background.
- **Name** (Text): 
  Enter your name as you would like to be recognized on the Council.
  - Please use your real name.
- **Email** (Email): 
  Enter a valid email address for official communications.
  - This email address will be used for Council communications and updates.
- **Phone** (Text): 
  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  - This field is not required, but it helps the Council reach you for important updates.
- **AssignedTool** (Text): 
  If applicable, please provide the name of the tool(s) assigned to you.
  - Enter the name of the tool you are using for Council work.
- **AssignedToolID** (Number): 
  Enter a unique numerical identifier for the tool(s) assigned to you.
  - Enter the numerical ID for the tool you are using.
- **AssignedToolVersion** (Text): 
  Enter the version number of the tool(s) assigned to you.
  - Enter the version number of the tool you are using.
- **AssignedToolVersionID** (Number): 
  Enter a unique numerical identifier for the tool(s) assigned to you.
  - Enter the version ID for the tool you are using.

## Tips
- Please fill in all fields accurately and consistently.
- Multiple selections for a field are allowed unless otherwise specified.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, please contact the Council administrators.
