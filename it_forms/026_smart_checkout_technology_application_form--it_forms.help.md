# Smart Checkout Technology Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Checkout Technology Application Form is designed to collect information about your business interest in implementing Smart Checkout Technology. This form will help us understand your business needs and assess the potential fit of our technology with your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the application process.
2. Enter your name as the applicant in the "Applicant Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to answer the "Application Type" question (e.g., are you applying for a demo, pilot, or full-scale implementation).
5. Choose your answer for "Business Fits" questions (you can select multiple options).
6. Provide the expected rollout timing for the implementation in the "Rollout Timing" field, if applicable.
7. In the "Operational Needs" field, provide a brief description of your business needs and requirements.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Retail Needs" and "Service Needs" questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start** (`form_start`, text, required): This is the first step to begin the application process.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the applicant.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can reach you for follow-ups and communication.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" if you are applying for a demo, pilot, or full-scale implementation.
* **Business Fits** (`business_fits`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that best describe your business needs.
	+ This question is meant to help us understand your business context and requirements.
* **Rollout Timing** (`rollout_timing`, date, optional): If you have a specific date for the expected implementation, please enter it here.
* **Operational Needs** (`operational_needs`, note, optional): Please provide a brief description of your business operational needs and requirements.
* **Retail Needs** (`retail_needs`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you need retail services, such as in-store kiosk or online retailing.
* **Service Needs** (`service_needs`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you need services like technical support or training.
* **Service Needs** (optional): Please note that if you select "Yes" for both Retail and Service Needs, we will follow up with additional questions to gather more information.
