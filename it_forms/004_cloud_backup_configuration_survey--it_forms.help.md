# Cloud Backup Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your current cloud backup configuration and gather feedback to improve the service provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Your Email Address" field.
3. Select your department or team from the "Department" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your current cloud backup provider from the "Current Cloud Backup Provider" dropdown menu.
5. Rate your satisfaction with your current provider on a scale of 0-5 in the "Overall Satisfaction with Current Provider" field.
6. Select how often backups are performed on the "Current Backup Frequency" field.
7. Enter the approximate size of data backed up in terabytes in the "Total Data Volume Backed Up" field.
8. Select your Recovery Time Objective (RTO) requirement from the "Recovery Time Objective (RTO)" dropdown menu.
9. Select your Recovery Point Objective (RPO) requirement from the "Recovery Point Objective (RPO)" dropdown menu.
10. Select all relevant compliance or regulatory requirements from the "Compliance or Regulatory Requirements" dropdown menu.
11. If you are considering upgrading or changing your provider, select the option that best describes your situation in the "Considering Provider Upgrade or Change" field.
12. Select your top concerns with your current setup from the "Main Concerns with Current Setup" dropdown menu.
13. Describe the features you would like to see in a new backup service in the "Most Desired New Features" field.
14. Select who has budget approval authority from the "Budget Approval Authority" dropdown menu.
15. Select your preferred implementation timeline for the new backup service from the "Preferred Implementation Timeline" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address as it is registered with your current provider.
* **Department**: Select your department or team that is responsible for the backup service.
* **Current Cloud Backup Provider**: Choose your current cloud backup service provider from the list.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Current Provider**: Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Current Backup Frequency**: Select how often backups are performed by your current provider.
* **Total Data Volume Backed Up**: Enter the approximate size of data backed up by your current provider in terabytes.
* **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**: Select how quickly your organization requires recovery from backups.
* **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)**: Select how frequently backups are made to ensure data is recoverable.
* **Compliance or Regulatory Requirements**: Select all compliance or regulatory requirements your organization must meet.
* **Considering Provider Upgrade or Change**: If you are planning to change providers, select the reason.
* **Main Concerns with Current Setup**: Select your top concerns with your current backup setup.
* **Most Desired New Features**: Describe the features you would like to see in a new backup service.
* **Budget Approval Authority**: Select who has authority to approve budgets for the new backup service.
* **Preferred Implementation Timeline**: Select when you would like to implement the new backup service.
