The M3U List URL Submission Form is designed to collect user-provided information for M3U (Multi-Stream) usage tracking purposes. This form is used to track and record the usage details and consent of users when accessing M3U-listed content. The form is not intended for general feedback or support, but rather for collecting specific data related to the M3U usage.

## M3U List URL Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user-provided information for M3U usage tracking, which enables us to better understand how users interact with M3U-listed content. By submitting this form, you are providing essential details about your usage of the content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the URL of the M3U-listed content you are accessing.
2. Provide a brief description of your usage details, such as the specific section or page you visited.
3. Select the type of content you were using (e.g., M3U, EPG).
4. Confirm your consent to share your usage details.
5. Enter your email address (if provided) to help us contact you if needed.
6. Enter your phone number (if provided) to help us contact you if needed.
7. If applicable, add any additional notes about your usage.
8. Click the "Submit" button to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **m3u_url**: A text input field where you enter the URL of the M3U-listed content you are accessing. Please enter the exact URL, including any required parameters.
* **usage_details**: A text input field where you provide a brief description of your usage details, such as the specific section or page you visited. Try to be as detailed as possible for accurate tracking.
* **usage_details_type**: A multiple-select option field where you choose the type of content you were using. Select one or more of the provided options (M3U, EPG, Other, Other usage).
* **consent**: A single-select option field where you confirm your consent to share your usage details. Choose "True" if you agree to share your data, or choose "False" if you do not.
* **email**: An email input field where you enter a valid email address if you want to be contacted by us for follow-up or additional information. This is optional but helps us contact you if needed.
* **phone**: A text input field where you enter a valid phone number if you want to be contacted by us for follow-up or additional information. This is optional but helps us contact you if needed.
* **date**: A date input field where you enter the date of your usage. This helps us track the timing of your usage.
* **time**: A time input field where you enter the time of your usage. This helps us track the timing of your usage.
* **note**: A note input field where you can add any additional comments or details about your usage. This is optional but helps us get a better understanding of your experience.
* **submit**: A submit button that finalizes your submission. Once submitted, your data will be recorded and reviewed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are providing accurate information about your usage details and consent.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us understand your M3U usage.
