# Power Supply Restoration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Supply Restoration Request Form is a tool used to record and track power supply restoration requests. This form is typically filled out when there is an issue with power supply at one of our facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the relevant information.
2. Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
3. Double-check that the form fields are accurate and complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1, text, Optional): Enter a brief and descriptive title for the form, e.g., "Power Supply Restoration Request for [Facility Name]".
* **Customer Name** (2, text, Optional): Enter the name of the customer or user requesting the power supply restoration.
* **Contact Email** (3, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for this request.
* **Facility Name** (4, text, Optional): Enter the name of the facility that is affected by the power supply outage.
* **Outage Date** (5, date, Optional): Enter the date of the power supply outage.
* **Start Time** (6, time, Optional): Enter the start time of the power supply outage.
* **End Time** (7, time, Optional): Enter the end time of the power supply outage.
* **Outage Duration** (8, text, Optional): Enter the duration of the power supply outage.
* **Number of Outages** (9, number, Optional): Enter the number of outages that occurred.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant team or supervisor.
* Double-check that the form fields are accurate and complete before submitting.
