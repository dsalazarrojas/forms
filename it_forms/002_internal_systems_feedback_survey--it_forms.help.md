# Internal Systems Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internal Systems Feedback Survey is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about the internal systems you use. Your feedback will help us improve and enhance these systems, making your work experience better.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide your feedback, simply:

1. Fill out this form by selecting or typing your answers to each question.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your feedback** (`feedback_survey`, text, Optional): This is the main question where you can write your thoughts and opinions about the internal systems you're using. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Internal Systems Rating** (`internal_systems_rating`, number, Optional): Use a number from 1 to 5 to rate how well you think the internal systems are performing. 1 is low, 5 is high.
* **Which tool are you using** (`tool_name`, select_one, Optional): Choose the internal tool you're using from the provided options: Tool 1, Tool 2, Tool 3.
* **Feedback Rating Score** (`feedback_rating_score`, number, Optional): Similar to the rating question, use a number from 1 to 5 to rate the feedback score of your experience with the internal systems. 1 is low, 5 is high.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): If you have additional comments or suggestions, you can write them in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, provide your phone number here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, Optional): Click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips
Before submitting your feedback, please make sure to:

1. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
2. Use a number from 1 to 5 for the ratings.
3. Double-check your email and phone (if provided) for accuracy.
