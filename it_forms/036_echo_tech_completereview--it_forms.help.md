# echo_tech_completereview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and status updates for tracking and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
3. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
4. Optionally, provide a note in the "User Note" field.
5. Enter your date and time of interaction in the "User Date" and "User Time" fields.
6. Select a category status in the "User Category" field.
7. Select any applicable categories in the "Category Options" field.
8. Enter a description of your interaction in the "User Description" field.
9. Add any relevant tags in the "User Tags" field.
10. Optionally, add any additional notes in the "User Notes" field.
11. Set the "COMPLETEREVIEW Status" field to "Active" or "Inactive".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address for identification and notification purposes.
* **User Phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **User Name (text, required)**: Enter your name for identification and recognition purposes.
* **User Note (note, optional)**: Provide a brief note about your interaction, if applicable.
* **User Date (date, required)**: Enter the date of your interaction.
* **User Time (time, required)**: Enter the time of your interaction.
* **User Category (select_one, required)**: Select the category status for your interaction.
* **Category Options (select_multiple, optional)**: Select any additional categories that apply to your interaction.
* **User Description (text, required)**: Enter a detailed description of your interaction.
* **User Tags (text, optional)**: Add relevant tags to your interaction for easy search and filtering.
* **User Notes (note, optional)**: Provide additional notes about your interaction, if needed.
* **COMPLETEREVIEW Status (select_one, required)**: Set the status of your interaction to "Active" or "Inactive".

## Tips
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "User Description" field.
* Select all applicable categories in the "Category Options" field, if necessary.
* Use relevant tags in the "User Tags" field to make it easy to search and filter interactions.
* Set the correct "COMPLETEREVIEW Status" field based on the outcome of your interaction.
