# Mobile App Feature Suggestion Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit suggestions for new features to be included in our mobile app. The purpose of this form is to collect ideas from users and provide feedback on their feasibility and potential impact on the app's development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Suggestions" field with a brief description of your feature idea, including any relevant details.
2. Select the desired "Priority" for your suggestion, indicating its urgency and importance.
3. Describe the feature in detail in the "Description" field.
4. Choose the current "Status" of your feature, indicating its current stage of development.
5. Optionally, provide any relevant "Screenshots" or other visual evidence to support your suggestion.
6. If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your suggestion, you can provide them in the "Impact" field.
7. Finally, fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Suggestions (Feature Suggestion)**: A text field where you can describe your feature idea, including any relevant details.
* **Priority (Priority)**: A dropdown list of three options: High, Medium, Low. Choose the one that best reflects the urgency and importance of your feature suggestion.
* **Description (Description)**: A text field where you can elaborate on your feature idea in more detail.
* **Status (Status)**: A dropdown list of seven options: In Progress, In Review, Rejected, On Hold, On Review, Not Planned. Select the current status of your feature idea.
* **Screenshots (Screenshots)**: A numerical field where you can upload up to 5 screenshots relevant to your feature suggestion.
* **Impact (Impact)**: A text field where you can provide any additional thoughts or comments about your feature suggestion.
* **Submitter (Submitter)**: A text field where you can provide your name, as it will appear on your submitted feature suggestion.
* **Email (Submitter Email)**: A field where you can enter your email address, which will be used for further communication about your feature suggestion.
* **Phone (Submitter Phone)**: A text field where you can optionally enter your phone number, which may be used for additional discussion or clarification.
