# Cloud Based Inventory Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from companies interested in participating in the cloud-based inventory pilot application. This form helps us understand your company's needs, requirements, and expectations from the pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company information (fields 1-4).
2. Describe your current inventory system and setup (fields 5-8).
3. Specify your goals and objectives for cloud inventory (fields 9-12).
4. Identify your current pain points and required features (fields 13-15).
5. Provide information about your integration needs and data migration requirements (fields 16-18).
6. Indicate your availability for the pilot program (fields 19-21).
7. Finally, add any additional information or questions you might have (field 22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Your official company name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): The name of the primary contact person for the pilot program.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Your company phone number.
* **Company Information** (`section_company`, `note`, not required): Provide details about your organization, such as company description and history.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required): Your primary business sector.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of your organization from the options provided:
	+ Startup (1-50 employees)
	+ Small (51-200 employees)
	+ Medium (201-1000 employees)
	+ Large (1001-5000 employees)
	+ Enterprise (5000+ employees)
* **Headquarters Location** (`headquarters_location`, `text`, required): City and country of your headquarters location.
* **Current Inventory System** (`current_system`, `text`, required): Describe the system you are currently using for inventory management.
* **System Type** (`system_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of system you are currently using from the options provided:
	+ On-premises software
	+ Legacy system
	+ Cloud-based SaaS
	+ Spreadsheet-based
	+ Custom built
	+ Other
* **Approximate Number of SKUs** (`sku_count`, `number`, required): How many products you manage.
* **Number of Inventory Locations** (`locations_count`, `number`, required): Number of warehouses, stores, or facilities.
* **Pilot Program Goals** (`section_goals`, `note`, not required): Describe what you hope to achieve with the cloud inventory pilot.
* **Primary Objectives for Cloud Inventory** (`primary_objectives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the primary objectives for cloud inventory:
	+ Real-time inventory visibility
	+ Reduce inventory costs
	+ Improve accuracy
	+ Enable scalability
	+ Better reporting and analytics
	+ Improve collaboration
	+ Disaster recovery capability
	+ Multi-location management
* **Current System Pain Points** (`pain_points`, `text`, required): Identify the problems with your current system.
* **Required Features** (`required_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the required features:
	+ Real-time tracking
	+ Barcode scanning
	+ Mobile app
	+ API integrations
	+ Automated reordering
	+ Multi-language support
	+ Custom reports
	+ User management
* **Systems to Integrate With** (`integrations_needed`, `text`, not required): List the systems you need to integrate with (e.g., ERP, accounting, e-commerce).
* **Data Migration Requirements** (`data_migration`, `select_one`, required): Select the data migration requirement:
	+ Minimal historical data
	+ 1 year of data
	+ 3 years of data
	+ Full historical data
	+ Not applicable
* **Pilot Timeline** (`section_timeline`, `note`, not required): Provide information about your availability for the pilot program.
* **Desired Pilot Duration** (`pilot_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired pilot duration:
	+ 1 month
	+ 2-3 months
	+ 4-6 months
	+ 6+ months
	+ Flexible
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date you prefer to begin the pilot program.
* **Team Availability for Implementation** (`team_availability`, `select_one`, required): Select the availability of your team for the implementation:
	+ Full-time dedicated team
	+ Part-time resources
	+ Limited resources
	+ External consultant needed
* **Additional Information or Questions** (`additional_information`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information or questions you might have.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your company and inventory system.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or team to clarify.
- Ensure that the answers provided reflect your actual needs and requirements.
- This form is meant to be filled out by companies interested in participating in the pilot program.
