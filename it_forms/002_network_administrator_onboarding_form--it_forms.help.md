# Network Administrator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Network Administrator Onboarding Form is designed to collect information from new network administrators for the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details such as first name, last name, email address, password, date of birth, and phone number.
2. Select your role from the dropdown list (Network Admin, System Admin, or Help Desk).
3. Fill out your emergency contact information.
4. Select your onboarding status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
5. Fill out any comments from your manager in the manager comments field.
6. Select the status of your manager approval (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
7. Select the status of IT department approvals (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
8. Select the status of department approvals (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
9. Select who submitted the form (User A, User B, or User C).
10. Fill out the onboarding form submitted date and time.
11. Select who verified the form (User A, User B, or User C).
12. Fill out the onboarding form verified date and time.
13. Leave the Network Administrator Onboarding Form ID field blank.
14. Select who verified the form from the IT department (User A, User B, or User C).
15. Select who verified the form from the department (User A, User B, or User C).
16. Fill out your name in the form submitter name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text)** (`1`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (Text)** (`2`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out your last name in this field.
* **Email (Email)** (`3`, `email`, required):
	+ Fill out your email address in this field.
* **Password (Text)** (`4`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out your password in this field.
* **Date of Birth (Date)** (`5`, `date`, required):
	+ Fill out your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Role (Select One)** (`6`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your role from the dropdown list (Network Admin, System Admin, or Help Desk).
* **Phone Number (Text)** (`7`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out your phone number in this field.
* **Confirm Password (Text)** (`8`, `text`, required):
	+ Re-enter your password to confirm it.
* **Confirm Date of Birth (Date)** (`9`, `date`, required):
	+ Re-enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Emergency Contact Info (Text)** (`10`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out the emergency contact information.
* **Onboarding Status (Select Multiple)** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select your onboarding status according to your current status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Manager Comments (Text)** (`12`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out any comments from your manager.
* **Manager Approval (Select Multiple)** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select your manager's approval status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **IT Department Approvals (Select Multiple)** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the IT department's approval status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Department Approvals (Select Multiple)** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the department's approval status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Onboarding Form Submitted By (Select One)** (`16`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select who submitted the form (User A, User B, or User C).
* **Onboarding Form Submitted Date (Date)** (`17`, `date`, required):
	+ Fill out the onboarding form submitted date.
* **Onboarding Form Submitted Time (Time)** (`18`, `time`, required):
	+ Fill out the onboarding form submitted time.
* **Onboarding Form Verified By (Select One)** (`19`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select who verified the form (User A, User B, or User C).
* **Onboarding Form Verified Date (Date)** (`20`, `date`, required):
	+ Fill out the onboarding form verified date.
* **Onboarding Form Verified Time (Time)** (`21`, `time`, required):
	+ Fill out the onboarding form verified time.
* **Network Administrator Onboarding Form ID (Text)** (`22`, `text`, required):
	+ This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **IT Department Verified By (Select One)** (`23`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select who verified the form from the IT department (User A, User B, or User C).
* **Department Verified By (Select One)** (`24`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select who verified the form from the department (User A, User B, or User C).
* **Form Submitter Name (Text)** (`25`, `text`, required):
	+ Fill out your name in this field.
