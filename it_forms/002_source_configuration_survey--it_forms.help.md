# Source Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Source Configuration Survey form is designed to gather information about your role, source configurations used, and other relevant details for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the best way possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Fill in any required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role?** (`what_is_your_role`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks about your role. Choose the option that best describes your role.
* **Source configurations used today** (`source_configurations_listed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks if you used any source configurations today. Select all the options that apply to your use of source configurations today.
* **Have source configurations been surveyed?** (`source_configurations_surveyed_today`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks if source configurations have been surveyed before. Select the option that best answers your survey experience.
* **How often have you surveyed source configurations?** (`how_often_surveyed`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks about the frequency of your source configurations surveys. Choose the option that best describes how often you have surveyed source configurations.
* **Is the source configuration a priority?** (`priority`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks about the priority of the source configuration. Select the option that best answers its priority.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the source configuration.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the date of the survey.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for the time of the survey.
* **Surveied by** (`surveyed_by`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks who surveyed you. Select the option that best answers your survey experience.
* **Surveyed at** (`surveyed_at`, `date`, required: false): This field asks the date the survey was conducted.
* **Surveyed by at** (`surveyed_by_at`, `time`, required: false): This field asks the time the survey was conducted.

Note: Please provide accurate information to ensure the most helpful and reliable survey results.

Tips: 

- Be honest and thorough when completing this form.
- Review your answers before submitting.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, contact us for assistance.
