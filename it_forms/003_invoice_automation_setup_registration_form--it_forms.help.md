# Invoice Automation Setup Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Automation Setup Registration Form is a form designed to help businesses set up and manage their invoices automatically. This form collects necessary information to enable our system to process and send invoices on your behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `form_data` page with any additional information you'd like to provide.
2. Enter your company's `company_name` in the company name field.
3. Enter your `contact_person`'s name and email address.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
6. Choose your `business_type` as either Company or Individual.
7. Select the size of your `company_size` from Small, Medium, or Large.
8. Select the `industry` related to your business from the provided options (e.g., Financial, Healthcare, IT).
9. Fill in your `country` name, `city`, and `state` name.
10. Enter your `zip_code` to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_data**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about your company or business.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **company_name**: Enter your company's name here.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **contact_person**: Enter the name and email of the contact person for your company.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: false
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **business_type**: Choose between Company or Individual.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: false
* **company_size**: Select the size of your company from Small, Medium, or Large.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: false
* **industry**: Select the industry related to your business from the provided options (e.g., Financial, Healthcare, IT).
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: false
* **country**: Enter your country name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **city**: Enter your city name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **state**: Enter your state name.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **zip_code**: Enter your zip code.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: false
