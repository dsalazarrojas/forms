# Fresh Product Data Integration Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for data integration purposes. It is intended for individuals or entities registering their fresh product data for integration with our platform. The form is used to gather relevant details about the product, including supplier and retailer information, data feed, and integration type.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Choose the appropriate options for each field as per the provided list.
3. Double-check your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Supplier ID** (`supplier_id`, text, required): Enter your supplier ID.
* **Retailer ID** (`retailer_id`, text, required): Enter your retailer ID.
* **Supplier** (`supplier`, select_one, required): Select your supplier from the provided options.
* **Retailer** (`retailer`, select_multiple, required): Select the retailers you are associated with.
* **Data Feed** (`data_feed`, time, required): Select the data feed type.
* **Integration Type** (`integration_type`, select_one, required): Select the integration type.
* **Standardize Data** (`standardize_data`, select_one, required): Select whether to standardize data or not.
* **Route Form Submission** (`route_form_submission`, select_one, required): Select where to route the form submission.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about your product.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of submission.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submit`, select_one, required): Select whether to submit the form or not.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, required): Select whether the form has been submitted or not.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Select the date of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, required): Enter the recipient of submission.
* **Submit Status** (`submit_status`, select_one, required): Select the submit status.
* **Submit Notes** (`submit_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the submission.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the submitter.

Note: This form requires the submission of multiple fields, including dates, so ensure you fill in the correct format for each.
