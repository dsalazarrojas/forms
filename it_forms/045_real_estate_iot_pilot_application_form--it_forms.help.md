# Real Estate IoT Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information required for the Real Estate IoT Pilot Application process. The purpose of this form is to gather the necessary details from real estate companies or individuals interested in participating in the pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the application ready.
2. Fill out the form with your company's information:
   * **Application Form**: A brief description of your company and its interests in participating in the pilot program.
   * **Contact Information**: Provide your contact details for future communication.
   * **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
   * **Company Address**: Enter your company address.
   * **Contact EMail**: Enter your email address.
   * **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
   * **Application Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the application.
3. Provide details about the IoT equipment you are applying for:
   * **IoT Equipment Details**: Describe the specific IoT equipment you are interested in.
4. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: A text field where you can describe your company and its interests in participating in the pilot program.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact name, email, and phone number for future communication.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company address.
* **Contact EMail**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the application.
* **IoT Equipment Details**: Describe the specific IoT equipment you are interested in.
* **Application Status**: Select the status of your application.
* **Notes**: A field for any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Agreement Form**: A text field where you can enter any agreement terms or conditions.
* **Agreement Form Signed**: Check if you have signed the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Status**: Select the status of the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Comments**: A field for any comments or notes about the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Due Date**: Enter the date the agreement form was due.
* **Agreement Form Revisions**: Enter any revisions to the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Agreement Form Approver**: Enter the name of the approver.
* **Agreement Form Version**: Enter the version of the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Status 2**: Select the status of the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Due Date 2**: Enter the date the agreement form was due.
* **Agreement Form Comments 2**: Enter any comments or notes about the agreement form.
* **Agreement Form Revisions 2**: Enter any revisions to the agreement form 2.
