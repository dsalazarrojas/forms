# Retail Chatbot Integration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for retailers and developers to register their chatbot integration projects. It collects information about the project, contact details, and integration requirements. Please use this form to submit your project for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your project details, including the name and description of your project.
2. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss your project.
3. Specify the type of integration you are creating, such as Retail Chatbot, E-Commerce, or Self-Service.
4. Include any additional details about your integration type, such as its features and functionalities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Chatbot Integration Registration Form (1)**: Enter your project name and brief description.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Requirements (3)**: List any requirements or constraints for your project, such as technical specifications or deadlines.
* **Chatbot Requirements (4)**: Describe the specific requirements of your chatbot, such as features and functionalities.
* **Integration Type (5)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Retail Chatbot
	+ E-Commerce
	+ Self-Service
* **Integration Type Details (6)**: Add any additional information about your selected integration type.
* **Project Name (7)**: Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description (8)**: Provide a brief description of your project, including its objective and scope.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your project description is clear and concise.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your form submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
