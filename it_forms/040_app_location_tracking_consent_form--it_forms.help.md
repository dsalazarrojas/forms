# App Location Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain your consent for location-based features within our app. By filling out this form, you're giving us permission to track your location while using the app, which helps us improve the overall user experience and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the provided information about location tracking consent.
2. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your preferred options for sharing location data with third-party services.
4. Read and follow the instructions on how to revoke your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Identifier** (User identifier, text, optional): Please enter your account ID or email address if applicable. This helps us associate your consent with your app account.
* **I Consent to Location Tracking** (I consent to location tracking while using the app, select_one, required): Choose between "Yes I consent" or "No I do not consent" to indicate your preference for location tracking. Note: You can revoke this consent in the app settings.
* **Purpose of Tracking Explained** (Purpose of tracking explained, note, optional): Here, describe how we will use your location data. For example, "To provide personalized recommendations based on your location" or "To improve our app's navigation features."
* **Data Retention Period** (Data retention period, number, optional): Specify the number of days you'd like your location data to be retained. This setting determines how long we store your location data.
* **Share with Third Parties** (Allow sharing location data with third parties, select_one, optional): Choose whether you'd like to share your location data with third-party services, such as analytics providers.
* **How to Revoke Consent** (How to revoke consent, text, optional): Follow the instructions here to disable location tracking in your app settings.
