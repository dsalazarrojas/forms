# dentek_onboarding_document - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from new employees as part of the company's onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Onboarding Company** field with the name of the company you work for.
2. Select your **Onboarding Location** from the provided options.
3. Enter your **Onboarding Job Title**.
4. Enter your **Onboarding Email** address.
5. Enter your **Onboarding Phone** number.
6. Enter your **Onboarding Password** (note: this field is not secure for password storage, but it's likely used for login or other system access purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Company**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Description: Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Onboarding Location**:
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Description: Select the location where you work (e.g., office, remote, etc.).
* **Onboarding Job Title**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Description: Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Onboarding Email**:
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Description: Enter your work email address.
* **Onboarding Phone**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Description: Enter your work phone number.
* **Onboarding Password**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Description: Enter your password (note: this field is not secure for password storage, but it's likely used for login or other system access purposes).

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the designated onboarding authority for assistance.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for the actual onboarding process or procedures within your organization. Consult your company's onboarding policies for more details.
