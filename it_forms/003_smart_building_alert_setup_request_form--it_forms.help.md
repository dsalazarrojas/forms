# Smart Building Alert Setup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to set up a Smart Building Alert system, which allows teams to receive timely and relevant alerts and notifications for various scenarios. It is essential to ensure that the setup process is completed correctly to prevent any potential issues or duplication of efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Smart Building Alert Setup Request**: Enter a short description of the alert setup request. This should be a clear and concise statement of the reason for the request. (Required)
2.  **Trigger Type**: Select the type of trigger that will activate the alert, such as "Alert" for critical issues or "Notification" for non-critical issues. You can also choose "Both" to trigger both types of alerts. (Required)
3.  **Notification Preferences**: Choose the notification channels you would like to use, such as email, SMS, or push notifications. Select "Both" to send notifications through multiple channels. (Required)
4.  **Alert Triggers**: Enter the specific details of the alert triggers, such as a specific temperature reading or a leak in the system. This should be a clear and concise statement of the trigger condition. (Required)
5.  **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building where the alert setup is required. (Required)
6.  **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for handling the alert. (Required)
7.  **Department**: Enter the department that will be handling the alert. (Required)
8.  **Description**: Provide a brief description of the alert setup request. This can include any additional details that are not captured in the other fields. (Optional)
9.  **Building Address**: Enter the physical address of the building where the alert setup is required. (Optional)
10.  **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person responsible for handling the alert. (Required)
11.  **Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for handling the alert. (Optional)
12.  **Priority**: Set the priority level of the alert, such as Low, Medium, High, or Urgent. (Optional)
13.  **Status**: Set the status of the alert, such as Open, Closed, or Pending. (Optional)
14.  **Assigned To**: Indicate if the alert is assigned to a specific team or person. (Optional)
15.  **Comment**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the alert setup request. (Optional)
16.  **Date Created**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
17.  **Time Created**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
18.  **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
19.  **Submitted Date**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
20.  **Submitted Time**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
21.  **Updated By**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
22.  **Updated Date**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
23.  **Updated Time**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited. (Not Required)
24.  **Submitted To**: Select the team or department that the alert will be submitted to. (Optional)
25.  **Submitted To Department**: Enter the department that the alert will be submitted to. (Required)
26.  **Submitted To Email**: Enter the email address of the person or team that the alert will be submitted to. (Optional)

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the administrator for guidance.
*   Do not duplicate existing alert setups.
