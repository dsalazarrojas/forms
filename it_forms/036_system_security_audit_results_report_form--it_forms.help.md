# System Security Audit Results Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information from system security audits to identify potential risks and areas for improvement within the system. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the results are informative and actionable.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **system_security_audit_results_report_form** field with a brief title that describes the purpose of the security audit.
2. Enter a detailed **description** of the audit, including any relevant context or background information.
3. Select the relevant **category** of the audit results. Please choose the correct category for your audit results.
4. Select the **risk_level** of the identified risks. Indicate the level of risk associated with the identified threats.
5. Enter the **risk_level_value**, providing a specific value related to the risk level you selected.
6. Describe the **next_steps** required to mitigate or address the identified risks.
7. Indicate the **outcome** of the audit, including any successful or unsuccessful actions taken to address the risks.
8. Provide a detailed account of the **outcome_details**, including any relevant context or background information.
9. Select the **assigned_tool** used for the audit, if applicable.
10. Add any additional **form_notes** related to the audit results.
11. Add any notes, observations, or comments in the **notes** field.
12. Enter any additional **form_notes_2**, if necessary.
13. Enter your **email**, if you'd like to be notified about any further actions or updates on the audit results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **system_security_audit_results_report_form** (Text): Enter a brief title that describes the purpose of the security audit.
* **description** (Text): Enter a detailed description of the audit, including relevant context or background information.
* **category** (Select One): Select the relevant category for your audit results.
* **risk_level** (Select One): Select the level of risk associated with the identified threats. Options: High, Low.
* **risk_level_value** (Text): Enter a specific value related to the risk level you selected.
* **next_steps** (Text): Describe the next steps required to mitigate or address the identified risks.
* **outcome** (Select Multiple): Indicate the outcome of the audit, including any successful or unsuccessful actions taken to address the risks. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **outcome_details** (Text): Provide a detailed account of the outcome, including any relevant context or background information.
* **output_file** (Text): This field is not used in this form.
* **assigned_tool** (Select One): Select the tool used for the audit, if applicable.
* **form_notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the audit results.
* **notes** (Note): Add any notes, observations, or comments in this field.
* **form_notes_2** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email to be notified about any further actions or updates on the audit results.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the results are informative and actionable.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Do not duplicate this form unnecessarily, as it is intended to be a one-time collection of information for each security audit.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you haven't missed any important information.
