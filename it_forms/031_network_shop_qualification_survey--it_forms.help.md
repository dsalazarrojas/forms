# network_shop_qualification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "network_shop_qualification_survey" form is designed to collect information about a vendor's qualifications and experiences in the network shop industry. This form is used to assess a vendor's suitability for network shop projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the correct answers for each field based on your qualifications and experiences.
3. Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Provide accurate information in the text fields (e.g., Vendor Address, Vendor Email, Vendor Phone).
5. Ensure the date in the Contract Duration field is in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Introduction, note, required: false):
	+ This field is for you to provide any relevant introduction or background information about your qualifications and experiences.
* **Services Offered** (Services Offered, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all services you offer, such as "Yes" if you offer services A, B, C, etc.
* **Business Experience** (Business Experience, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of years you have experience in the network shop industry.
* **Vendor Certification** (Vendor Certification, select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" if you have vendor certification, "No" if you don't, or "Other" if you have other certifications.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Vendor Address** (Vendor Address, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the address of your organization.
* **Vendor Email** (Vendor Email, email, required: false):
	+ Enter the email address of your organization.
* **Vendor Phone** (Vendor Phone, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of your organization.
* **Contract Start Price** (Contract Start Price, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the starting price of your contract (in currency).
* **Contract End Price** (Contract End Price, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the ending price of your contract (in currency).
* **Contract Duration** (Contract Duration, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of your contract (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Services Used** (Services Used, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all services you have used, such as "Yes" if you have used service A, B, C, etc.
* **Contract Status** (Contract Status, select_one, required: true):
	+ Select "Yes" if your contract is active, "No" if it's not, or "Other" if it has other status.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about your qualifications and experiences.

By following these guidelines, you will be able to complete the "network_shop_qualification_survey" form accurately and efficiently.
