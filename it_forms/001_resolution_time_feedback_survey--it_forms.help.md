# Resolution Time Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resolution Time Feedback Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from customers or end-users about the response and resolution times of support teams. This form is meant to help the support team evaluate their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options that apply to your experience with the support team.
2. Enter your response time in the "Response Time" field.
3. If applicable, select the resolution speed and resolution time that best describes your experience.
4. Enter your email address and/or phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
5. Provide any additional feedback or notes about your experience in the "Feedback" and "Note" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Response Time (<name>: response_time)
* Response Time (numeric): Enter the time in hours, minutes, and seconds that it took the support team to respond to your inquiry. For example, 2 hours and 15 minutes should be entered as 2.25.

### Resolution Speed (<name>: resolution_speed)
* Resolution Speed (numeric): Select one of the options to indicate how quickly the support team resolved your issue. Please select the option that best describes your experience.

### Resolution Time (<name>: resolution_time)
* Resolution Time (numeric): Enter the time it took for the support team to resolve your issue. For example, 1 hour and 30 minutes should be entered as 1.5.

### Support Team Response Time (<name>: support_team_response_time)
* Support Team Response Time (numeric): Enter the time in hours, minutes, and seconds that it took the support team to respond to your initial inquiry. For example, 1 hour and 20 minutes should be entered as 1.33.

### Support Team Resolution Time (<name>: support_team_resolution_time)
* Support Team Resolution Time (numeric): Enter the time it took for the support team to resolve your issue. For example, 2 hours and 15 minutes should be entered as 2.25.

### Feedback (<name>: feedback)
* Feedback (text): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the support team.

### Email (<name>: email)
* Email (email): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by the support team about your feedback.

### Phone (<name>: phone)
* Phone (text): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the support team about your feedback.

### Select One (<name>: select_one_1)
* Select One (select-one): Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the response time, resolution speed, and resolution time of the support team. Select "No" otherwise.

### Select Multiple 1 (<name>: select_multiple_1)
* Select Multiple 1 (select-multiple): Select "Yes" if multiple issues were resolved simultaneously. Select "No" otherwise.

### Date (<name>: date)
* Date (date): Enter the date of your inquiry or issue.

### Time (<name>: time)
* Time (time): Enter the time of day when you received the response from the support team. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).

### Note (<name>: note)
* Note (text): Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience with the support team.
 
# Tips
* Please be as accurate as possible when entering your response and resolution times.
* If you have any issues or concerns that you would like to report, please select "No" for all applicable fields.
* If you have any suggestions or improvements for the support team, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
* If you would like to be contacted by the support team about your feedback, please enter your email address and/or phone number.
