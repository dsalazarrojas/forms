# E Commerce App Integration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting e-commerce app integration with our system. Please fill it out to submit your app for integration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your e-commerce app.
2. Select the correct integration type (e.g., Ecommerce, Marketplaces, Payment gateways, Shipping Integrations).
3. Provide any additional use cases or notes that will help us understand your app's integration requirements.
4. Ensure that all contact information (email, phone, company name, company URL, etc.) is accurate and up-to-date.
