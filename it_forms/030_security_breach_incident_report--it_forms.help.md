# Security Breach Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report security breaches or incidents that have occurred within the organization. This form will help to gather all necessary information about the incident to facilitate a prompt and effective response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the incident, including the location where it occurred.
2. Select the type of incident (Physical, Cyber, or Other).
3. Enter the date and time of the incident.
4. Attach any evidence of the incident (e.g., photos or files).
5. Provide the outcome of the incident (Confirmed or Unconfirmed).
6. Enter the name of the Reporting Officer.
7. Enter the name and contact information (email and phone number) of the Incident Reporter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details (1)**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Type (2)**: Select the type of incident (Physical, Cyber, or Other).
* **Incident Location (3)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date (4)**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time (5)**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Description (6)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Evidence (7)**: Attach any evidence of the incident, such as photos or files.
* **Incident Outcome (8)**: Select the outcome of the incident (Confirmed or Unconfirmed).
* **Reporting Officer (9)**: Enter the name of the Reporting Officer.
* **Incident Reporter (10)**: Enter the name of the Incident Reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Email (11)**: Enter the email address of the Incident Reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Phone (12)**: Enter the phone number of the Incident Reporter.

## Tips
* Make sure to attach any relevant evidence to support your report.
* If the incident outcome is Confirmed, please ensure that you have all necessary information to support the confirmation.
* Double-check the contact information of the Incident Reporter before submitting the report.

This help guide is designed to ensure that end-users understand the purpose and requirements of the Security Breach Incident Report form, and can complete it accurately and effectively.
