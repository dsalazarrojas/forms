## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category from the available options.
2. Enter a brief description of the user.
3. Choose whether the form has an ID or not.
4. Select the assigned tool.
5. Upload any relevant files.
6. Enter the user's date and time.
7. Enter the user's phone number.
8. Enter the user's email address.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.
10. Verify your password to ensure accuracy.
