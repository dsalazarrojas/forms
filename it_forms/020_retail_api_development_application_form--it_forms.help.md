# Retail API Development Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail API Development Application Form is a tool used to gather information from users about their retail API development needs. This form helps us understand the requirements and details necessary for our team to develop and provide effective API support.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in your User Name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your Email address to allow us to contact you about your application.
3. Select the type of API you are interested in developing (Yes/No).
4. Choose your desired Access Level (High/Low).
5. Enter your Company details if applicable.
6. Specify the Department you are from.
7. Define your Role within your organization.
8. Provide your contact information for future reference.
9. Enter your Phone number for us to contact you.
10. Add any additional notes about your application.
11. Select a Date and Time to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter a valid email address to allow us to communicate with you about your application.
* **API Type** (`api_type`, select_one, not required): Choose "Yes" if you are interested in developing a Retail API, "No" otherwise.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_multiple, not required): Select "High" for high-level access and "Low" for low-level access.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Specify the department you are from within your company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, not required): Define your role within your organization.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, not required): Enter your contact information for future reference.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your address if necessary.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Select a date to confirm your application.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Select a time to confirm your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Please enter a title for your application or a brief description.
