# Retail Process Automation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Process Automation Application Form is a tool used to gather information from retailers who want to implement process automation in their businesses. The form helps us understand their specific needs and requirements, allowing us to provide tailored guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Application Details page with your basic contact information and a brief description of your project.
2. Provide detailed information about your retailer on the Retailer Details page.
3. Describe your current systems and processes on the Systems and Processes page.
4. Outline the scope of your project on the Project Scope page.
5. Specify your preferred technology stack on the Technology Stack page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <i class="fa fa-bullseye" aria-hidden="true"></i> Application Details
• **Title** (Application Details, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your project.
• **Description** (Application Details, text, required/optional): Provide a concise description of your project.
### <i class="fa fa-bullseye" aria-hidden="true"></i> Retailer Details
• **Retailer Name** (Retailer Details, text, required/optional): Enter your store's name or the name of the company you represent.
• **Contact Person** (Retailer Details, text, required/optional): Provide the name and email of the contact person for your store or company.
### <i class="fa fa-bullseye" aria-hidden="true"></i> Systems and Processes
• **System Description** (Systems and Processes, text, required/optional): Describe your current systems and processes, including any existing software or tools used.
### <i class="fa fa-bullseye" aria-hidden="true"></i> Project Scope
• **Project Objective** (Project Scope, text, required/optional): State the primary objective of your project.
• **Project Goals** (Project Scope, text, required/optional): Outline the specific goals you hope to achieve with your project.
### <i class="fa fa-bullseye" aria-hidden="true"></i> Technology Stack
• **Preferred Technology** (Technology Stack, text, required/optional): Specify the technologies you prefer to use for your project.
