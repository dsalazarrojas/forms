# Retail Cloud Solution Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Cloud Solution Certification Application Form is designed to collect information for the purpose of evaluating the eligibility for a Retail Cloud Solution certification. Please complete this form to apply for certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional information as instructed. Please note that all fields are optional, unless otherwise specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName** (Text): Your first name.
* **LastName** (Text): Your last name.
* **Company** (Text): The name of your company or organization.
* **Email** (Email): A valid email address to contact you for certification process updates.
* **Phone** (Text): Your phone number for communication.
* **Address** (Text): Your physical address for shipping purposes.
* **City** (Text): The name of your city.
* **State** (Text): The name of your state.
* **Country** (Text): The name of your country.
* **Zip** (Text): Your zip or postal code.
* **SolutionName** (Text): The name of your Retail Cloud Solution.
* **Description** (Text): A brief description of your solution.
* **SupportingDocumentation** (Text): Any additional documentation or support information for your solution.

## Tips
* Please provide valid and accurate information in all fields.
* Make sure your email address is valid and can be used for communication.
* If you have any additional documentation or support information for your solution, please attach it in the SupportingDocumentation field.
* All fields are subject to verification and validation before approval.
