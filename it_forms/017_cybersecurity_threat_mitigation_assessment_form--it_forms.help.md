**Cybersecurity Threat Mitigation Assessment Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess and identify potential threats to cybersecurity, allowing organizations to understand their vulnerabilities and develop strategies to mitigate them. This form is designed to gather information on the purpose, goals, and objectives of the threat assessment, as well as identify potential risks and mitigation strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question thoroughly**: Provide detailed answers to each question to ensure accurate and comprehensive assessment.
2. **Use the hints**: Refer to the hints provided for each question if you need additional guidance.
3. **Save and review**: Save your progress and review your answers before submitting.
