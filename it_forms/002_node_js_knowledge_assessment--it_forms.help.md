# Node.js Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you navigate the Node.js Knowledge Assessment form. The form is meant to evaluate your knowledge and expertise in Node.js, covering various aspects of the technology.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and instructions to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Go through each section of the form, reading each field's label and explanation.
3. Answer each question carefully, providing accurate information about your Node.js skills and experience.
4. Don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_page** (1, text, required=false): This is the first page of the form where you will start your assessment. Please provide your response in the text box.
* **node_js_skills** (2, text, required=false): Share your knowledge and experience with Node.js, including areas where you have applied it in your projects or contributions.
* **confidence_level** (3, number, required=false): Rate your confidence level in Node.js on a scale, providing a numerical value.
* **Node_js_frameworks** (4, select_multiple, required=false): Select the Node.js frameworks you are familiar with or have experience working with.
* **Node_js_libraries** (5, select_multiple, required=false): Choose the Node.js libraries you have worked with or are familiar with.
* **Node_js_versions** (6, select_multiple, required=false): Select the Node.js versions you have experience with.
* **next_steps** (7, text, required=false): Provide any additional information or next steps you think would be beneficial for further growth and development in Node.js.
* **feedback** (8, text, required=false): Share any feedback, suggestions, or comments about the assessment form or Node.js ecosystem.

## Tips
* Take your time to answer each question carefully, as this will help ensure accurate results.
* If you are unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Remember to provide specific examples or experiences to support your answers.
* This assessment is a tool for personal growth and development, so be honest and open in your responses.
