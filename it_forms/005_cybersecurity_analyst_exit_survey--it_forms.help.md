# Cybersecurity Analyst Exit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cybersecurity Analyst Exit Survey is designed to gather feedback from cybersecurity analysts about their experiences, preferences, and concerns. This information will help organizations understand the strengths and weaknesses of their current cybersecurity setup and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form below, answering all applicable questions honestly.
2. Take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete the form.
3. Review each question carefully and answer based on your experiences and perceptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`form_title`, text, optional)
  Enter your name, if you'd like to be associated with your feedback.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
  Enter your email address for follow-up or to receive any additional information.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, text, optional)
  Describe your current work environment, including your role, department, and any other relevant context.
* **Tools Used** (`tools_used`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select all the tools you've used during your role, such as security information and event management systems, threat intelligence platforms, or other tools used in your daily tasks.
* **Processes Used** (`processes_used`, select_multiple, optional)
  Choose all the processes you've followed during your role, like incident response, vulnerability assessment, or penetration testing.
* **Job Retention** (`job_retention`, select_multiple, optional)
  Indicate whether your job is secure or at risk, and if your job retention is true or false.
* **Process Satisfaction** (`process_satisfaction`, text, optional)
  Share your thoughts on the processes used and how they've contributed to your job satisfaction.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional)
  Offer any suggestions or ideas for improving work processes or tools.
* **Tool Frequency** (`tool_frequency`, text, optional)
  Report how frequently you've used the tools mentioned earlier.
* **New Tools** (`new_tools`, text, optional)
  Suggest any new tools you think would be helpful for your role or organization.
* **New Processes** (`new_processes`, text, optional)
  Propose any new processes that would improve your job performance and satisfaction.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
  Share any additional thoughts or concerns you have about your role or work environment.
* **Surprises or Concerns** (`surprise`, text, optional)
  Describe any surprising experiences or concerns you've faced during your role.
* **Changes in Work Environment** (`work_environment_changes`, text, optional)
  Share any changes you've witnessed or suggest changes that would improve the work environment.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, radio, optional)
  Rate the effectiveness of team communication during your role.
* **Favorite Tool** (`favorite_tool`, text, optional)
  Nominate a favorite tool you've used during your role.
* **Least Favorite Tool** (`least_favorite_tool`, text, optional)
  Identify a tool that didn't meet your expectations.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional)
  Share any other comments or feedback you have about your role or work environment.

Please remember to answer all questions honestly and provide detailed responses to help organizations improve their work environment, tools, and processes. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
