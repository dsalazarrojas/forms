# technical_integration_assistance_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The technical_integration_assistance_contact_form is designed to collect basic information from users that require technical integration assistance. This form will help the support team provide effective and efficient assistance by gathering essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information to help the support team understand your issue quickly and effectively.
2. **Provide detailed descriptions**: For the issue description and technical details fields, please provide as much information as possible to help the support team diagnose and resolve your issue.
3. **Attach relevant files**: If you have any relevant files related to your issue, such as screenshots or logs, please attach them to the form to help the support team understand your problem better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Information** (`basic_information`, text, optional): Please fill out this field with your name, company name, or any other identifier that will help us identify you or your company.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description_1`, text, optional): Please provide a detailed description of the issue you are facing, including any relevant steps you have taken to troubleshoot or resolve the problem.
* **Files** (`files`, text, optional): If you have any files related to your issue, such as screenshots or logs, please attach them to this field. This will help us understand your problem better.
* **Technical Details** (`technical_details`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant technical details about your issue, such as error messages or configuration settings.
* **Technical Details 1** (`technical_details_1`, text, optional): This field is similar to the previous one. Please provide any additional technical details that will help us diagnose and resolve your issue.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us assist you efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please provide a brief explanation of what you are trying to do or what you are experiencing.
* If you have any relevant files or attachments, please attach them to the form to help us understand your issue better.
