# office_laptop_maintenance_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help office employees report on the maintenance status of their laptops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question, considering the actual state of your laptop's maintenance status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief description of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Laptop Model** (`laptop_model`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if your laptop has maintenance needs.
* **Battery Life** (`battery_life`, number, required: false): Enter the approximate number of hours your laptop's battery has left.
* **RAM** (`ram`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of RAM (in GB) installed in your laptop.
* **Processor** (`processor`, number, required: false): Enter the speed of your laptop's processor (in GHz).
* **Hard Drive** (`hard_drive`, number, required: false): Enter the size of your laptop's hard drive (in GB).
* **Maintenance** (`maintenance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply regarding your laptop's maintenance:
	+ 'Yes' for necessary maintenance
* **Submission Status** (`submission`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have submitted your laptop for maintenance.
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ 'Yes' for employee information submission
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Enter your name for submission acknowledgement.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review each field carefully and select the correct options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your IT department or supervisor.
