# Inventory System Failure Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to report inventory system failures or issues they have encountered. Please fill out this form to report any problems you've experienced with the inventory system, including any details about the incident and your contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the form title or start date to access the form.
2. Fill in the "issue_details" field with a brief description of the problem you experienced.
3. Select "report_priority" to indicate the severity of the problem, if applicable.
4. Choose "report_location" to provide the location where the issue occurred.
5. Add any additional details or attachments in the "report_description" field.
6. If you have any attachments (e.g., screenshots, log files), upload them using the "report_attachment" fields.
7. Fill in your "name" and "email" fields with your contact information.
8. Type in your message in the "message" field to communicate any additional information.
9. Click "submit" when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Issue details** (`incident_report`): Describe the problem you experienced with the inventory system.
- **Report Priority** (`incident_priority`): Choose the severity of the issue, if applicable (None, Critical, High, Low, Medium).
- **Report Date** (`incident_report_date`): Enter the date of the incident.
- **Report Time** (`incident_report_time`): Enter the time of the incident.
- **Report Location** (`incident_location`): Provide the location where the issue occurred.
- **Report Description** (`incident_description`): Add any additional details or attachments about the incident.
- **Attachments** (`incident_attachment`): Upload any attachments related to the incident (e.g., screenshots, log files).
- **Name** (`user_name`): Enter your name.
- **Email** (`user_email`): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`user_phone`): Enter your phone number.
- **Message** (`user_message`): Type in any additional information you'd like to communicate.
- **Attachment 1** (`incident_attachment_1`): Upload an attachment related to the incident (e.g., screenshot).
- **Attachment 2** (`incident_attachment_2`): Upload another attachment related to the incident (e.g., another screenshot).
- **Attachment 3** (`incident_attachment_3`): Upload yet another attachment related to the incident (e.g., log file).
- **Attachment 4** (`incident_attachment_4`): Upload an additional attachment related to the incident (e.g., another log file).
- **Attachment 5** (`incident_attachment_5`): Upload yet another attachment related to the incident (e.g., another screenshot).
- **Attachment 6** (`incident_attachment_6`): Upload an additional attachment related to the incident (e.g., another file).
- **Attachment 7** (`incident_attachment_7`): Upload another attachment related to the incident (e.g., yet another file).
- **Attachment 8** (`incident_attachment_8`): Upload an additional attachment related to the incident (e.g., another file).
- **Attachment 9** (`incident_attachment_9`): Upload another attachment related to the incident (e.g., another file).
- **Attachment 10** (`incident_attachment_10`): Upload an additional attachment related to the incident (e.g., another file).

Note: Some fields are optional.
