# System Configuration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The System Configuration Feedback Form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions on the system's configuration. Your input will help us improve the system's performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the "Feedback on System Configuration" page (Page 1) and provide your feedback in the text area provided.
2. On the "Rating" page (Page 2), select one of the five rating options (Very Poor, Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent) that best describe your experience with the system.
3. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Comments" field (Page 3) to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback on System Configuration** (`Feedback on System Configuration`, text, optional): Please provide your thoughts on the system's configuration, including any issues or areas that need improvement.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, select_multiple, optional): Select one of the five rating options that best describe your experience with the system.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Provide additional comments or information that can help us better understand your feedback.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us improve the system.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the system, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Your input is valued and will help us make the system better for everyone.
