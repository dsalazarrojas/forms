# Educational Technology Prototype Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about educational technology prototypes. It is intended for educational institutions, researchers, and developers to share their ideas, experiences, and outcomes related to educational technology. The form collects various details about the prototypes, including text descriptions, selection options, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand what each field collects and how it contributes to the overall purpose of the form.
2. Complete each field with relevant information. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Use the provided help text as a guide to ensure you are submitting correct and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Educational Technology Prototype Form 1** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_1`, text, not required):
	+ This field is a free-text field for users to provide a description of their educational technology prototype.
* **Select One** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_2`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose one option from the dropdown list to indicate the type of educational technology prototype.
* **Select Multiple** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_4`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose multiple options from the dropdown list to specify multiple educational technology prototypes.
* **Date** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_5`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date related to the educational technology prototype, if applicable.
* **Time** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_6`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the time related to the educational technology prototype, if applicable.
* **Educational Technology Prototype Form 7** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_7`, note, not required):
	+ This field is for users to provide additional information or notes about the educational technology prototype.
* **Email** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_9`, email, not required):
	+ Enter your contact email address for further communication about the educational technology prototype.
* **Phone** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_10`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your contact phone number for further communication about the educational technology prototype.
* **Educational Technology Prototype Forms 11-25** (`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_11`-`educational_technology_prototype_application_form_25`, text, not required):
	+ These fields are for users to provide additional information or details about the educational technology prototype.
