# it_service_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_service_request_form is a tool for submitting service requests to internal teams or departments. It allows users to submit requests for non-urgent, low-impact tasks that don't require immediate attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a service request, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting in the "Service Request" field.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Name" field.
3. Choose a category for your request from the "Category" field (if applicable).
4. Enter a detailed description of the task or service needed in the "Description" field.
5. Set the priority of your request using the "Priority" dropdown menu (low, medium, or high).
6. Set a deadline for completion (if applicable).
7. Enter the name and email address (if available) of the person who should be contacted for more information in the "Contact Name" and "Contact Email" fields (if applicable).
8. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (if applicable).
9. Select the team or department you would like to be assigned to in the "Assigned to" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select a category for your request (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the task or service needed.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Choose the priority of your request (low, medium, or high).
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, optional): Set a deadline for completion (if applicable).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who should be contacted for more information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who should be contacted for more information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Select the team or department you would like to be assigned to.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your requests are non-urgent and low-impact to ensure they are handled efficiently.
* Use the "Category" field to help your request be directed to the correct team or department.
* If you have any questions or need help with your request, please do not hesitate to reach out to the team that manages this form.
