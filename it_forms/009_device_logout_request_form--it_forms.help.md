# Device Logout Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request logout of a device. Fill out this form to provide necessary information for the logout process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **user name** in the "User Name" field.
2. Select the **reason for logout** from the provided options.
3. Provide additional **comments** if necessary.
4. Enter the **device ID** of the device you'd like to logout.
5. Enter your **email address** to ensure we can contact you about the logout process.
6. Enter your **phone number** if you'd like to be contacted about the logout status.
7. Select the current **user status**, which will be used to verify your account information.
8. Choose the **user type** to confirm your identity.
9. Choose the **account status** to ensure accuracy.
10. Select the **reason code** for the logout request.
11. Enter the **device name** for reference.
12. Enter the **account ID** for device association.
13. Assign the logout request to a specific **person** for follow-up.

* **Assigned To** (Required): Assign the logout request to a specific person for follow-up.
