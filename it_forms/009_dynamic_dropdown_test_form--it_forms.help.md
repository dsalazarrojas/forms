# Dynamic Dropdown Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dynamic Dropdown Test Form is a test form designed to collect user preferences and demographic information. It has several dropdown menus and text fields to gather a range of responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your favorite color from the options provided in the "What is your favorite color?" dropdown.
2. Select your favorite food from the options provided in the "What is your favorite food?" dropdown.
3. Choose whether you have siblings or not from the "How many siblings do you have?" dropdown.
4. Select your favorite season from the options provided in the "What is your favorite season?" dropdown.
5. Select your favorite month from the options provided in the "What is your favorite month?" dropdown.
6. Enter your answer to the question "Text" in the text field.
7. Enter a numerical value in the "Integer" field.
8. Enter a decimal value in the "Decimal" field.
9. Enter a date in the "Date" field (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
10. Select a time in the "Time" field (format: HH:MM).
11. Choose whether you want to select one or two options from the next dropdowns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite color?** (`select_first`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list of colors to indicate your favorite color.
* **What is your favorite food?** (`select_second`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list of foods to indicate your favorite food.
* **How many siblings do you have?** (`select_third`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list of options to indicate the number of siblings you have.
* **What is your favorite season?** (`select_fourth`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list of seasons to indicate your favorite season.
* **What is your favorite month?** (`select_fifth`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list of months to indicate your favorite month.
* **Text** (`text`, text, required: false): Enter a text response to this question.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value in this field.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required: false): Enter a decimal value in this field.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time in the format HH:MM.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list to indicate your choice.
* **Select Two** (`select_two`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list to indicate your second choice.
