# daily-performance-report-form-in-indonesian - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect daily performance reports from users in an Indonesian context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User (User)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is where you enter your user identification or name.
* **date (date)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date for which you are providing the performance report.
* **time (time)** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of day for which you are providing the performance report.
* **note (note)** (`note`, required/optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or notes about your performance.
* **email (email)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
* If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, please contact the form administrator.
