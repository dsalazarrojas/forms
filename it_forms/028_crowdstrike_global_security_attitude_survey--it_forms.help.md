# CrowdStrike Global Security Attitude Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your organization's current security posture from your perspective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your job title and organization name.
2. Answer each question with one of the following options: Very poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, or Very good.
3. Enter your email, phone number, and any additional comments or remarks in the provided fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name as you know it.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your title within the organization.
* **How would you rate your current security posture?** (`security_attitude_score_1`, select_one, optional): Evaluate your current organization security posture with the given options.
* **How would you rate your overall security posture?** (`security_attitude_score_2`, select_one, optional): Evaluate your overall organization security posture with the given options.
* **How would you rate your overall security posture (continued)?** (`security_attitude_score_3`, select_one, optional): Evaluate your organization security posture again, as it might be redundant. Please provide your feedback.
* **How would you rate your overall security posture (continued 2)?** (`security_attitude_score_4`, select_one, optional): Another evaluation of your overall organization security posture.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up calls.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the current date, although not clearly needed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the current time, which also seems unimportant in this context.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **How would you rate the importance of security?** (`security_attitude_score_5`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the importance of security within your organization.
