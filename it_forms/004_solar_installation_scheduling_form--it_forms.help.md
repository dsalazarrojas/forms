# Solar Installation Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Installation Scheduling Form is a tool used to collect relevant information from customers to schedule an installation or service appointment for their solar system. The form is designed to be filled out by customers to ensure that the installation and servicing of the solar system is done efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the customer information page with your name, appointment date, appointment time, installation address, phone number, and email address.
2. On the appointment and installation details page, select the type of installation or service you require (Installation Type).
3. Choose the size of the installation (Installation Size).
4. If you need a service technician, select "Yes" for the Service Technician option.
5. Enter the phone number of your service technician (Service Technician Phone).
6. Provide any additional notes about the installation (Installation Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Appointment Date
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the installation or service appointment.

### Appointment Time
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule the installation or service appointment.

### Installation Address
* **Installation Address**: Enter your installation address where the solar system will be installed or serviced.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates or reminders.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can receive updates or reminders.

### Installation Type
* **Installation Type**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting a new installation or "No" if you are requesting service on an existing installation.

### Installation Size
* **Installation Size**: Select the size of the installation you require (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

### Service Technician
* **Service Technician**: Select "Yes" if you need a service technician to assist with the installation or service.

### Service Technician Phone
* **Service Technician Phone**: Enter the phone number of your service technician if you selected "Yes" for the Service Technician option.

### Installation Notes
* **Installation Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the installation or service appointment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to ensure efficient and effective installation and servicing of the solar system.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your local representative for assistance.
