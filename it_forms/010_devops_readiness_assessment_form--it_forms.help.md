# DevOps Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an organization's DevOps readiness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For team size, select the number of team members that you have in your team.
3. For experience, select the type of experience you have in implementing DevOps practices.
4. For tools, select the tools you are currently using or have experience with.
5. For processes, select the processes you are currently using or have experience with.
6. For culture, select the characteristics of your team culture.
7. For metrics, select the metrics you use to measure DevOps performance.
8. For feedback, enter any feedback or comments you have about your DevOps implementation.
9. For comments, enter any additional comments or questions.
10. Finally, enter your name, email address, phone number, location, company, department, job title, and role. This information is required for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Size (2)**: How large is your team? Please select the number of team members.
* **Experience (3)**: What type of experience do you have in implementing DevOps practices? Please select one of the options.
* **Tools (4)**: What tools are you using or have experience with? Select all that apply.
* **Processes (5)**: What processes are you using or have experience with? Select all that apply.
* **Culture (6)**: What characteristics of team culture do you have? Select all that apply.
* **Metrics (7)**: What metrics do you use to measure DevOps performance? Select all that apply.
* **Feedback (8)**: Enter any feedback or comments you have about your DevOps implementation.
* **Comments (9)**: Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Name (11)**: Enter your name.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Location (14)**: Enter your location.
* **Company (15)**: Enter your company name.
* **Department (16)**: Enter your department name.
* **Job Title (17)**: Enter your job title.
* **Role (18)**: Enter your role.
* **Feedback (19)**: Enter any feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Additional Feedback (20)**: Enter any additional feedback or comments.
* **Rating (21)**: Rate your overall experience with DevOps practices. Select one of the options.
* **Comment (22)**: Enter any additional comments about your experience.
* **Rating Reason (23)**: Enter the reason for your rating.
* **Name (24)**: Enter your name (again).
* **Date (25)**: Enter the date.
