# Fitness App Malfunction Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about malfunctions or issues experienced with the fitness app. It will help the support team to identify and resolve the issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to fill out the form accurately and completely:

1.  Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
2.  Enter your **first name** in the field labeled "first name".
3.  Enter your **email** address in the field labeled "email".
4.  Enter your **phone number** in the field labeled "phone number".
5.  Provide a detailed description of the issue you encountered with the app in the field labeled "description".
6.  Indicate the severity of the issue by selecting one of the following options:
    *   Critical
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
7.  If the issue is related to a specific category, choose the relevant option:
    *   Critical
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
8.  Describe the steps you took to reproduce the issue in the field labeled "steps to reproduce".
9.  Choose whether you have **screenshots** to attach with the issue:
    *   Screenshots
    *   No Screenshots
10.  If you have attached screenshots, provide the URL where they can be accessed:
    *   Yes
    *   No
11.  If you have a **video recording** to share, indicate that you have one available:
    *   Yes
    *   No
12.  Indicate whether an **attachment** is provided:
    *   Yes
    *   No
13.  Enter the **device model** you are using.
14.  Enter the **operating system** you are using.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **first name**: Enter your first name to help us identify you.
*   **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
*   **phone number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to call you for further information.
*   **description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with the app in detail.
*   **prophets Severity**: Choose the severity of the issue to help us prioritize it.
*   **category**: If your issue is related to a specific category, choose the relevant option.
*   **steps to reproduce**: Describe the steps you took to reproduce the issue to help us understand it better.
*   **Screenshots**: If you have screenshots related to the issue, choose this option.
*   **Screenshots URL**: If you have attached screenshots, provide the URL where they can be accessed.
*   **Video URL**: If you have a video recording of the issue, provide the URL where it can be accessed.
*   **attachment**: If you have an attachment related to the issue, choose this option.
*   **device model**: Enter the model of your device to help us understand the issue better.
*   **operating system**: Enter the operating system you are using to help us understand the issue better.
*   **Field 14**: This field is reserved for future use, please ignore it for now.
