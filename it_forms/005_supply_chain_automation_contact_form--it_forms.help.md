# Supply Chain Automation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Automation Contact Form is designed to help the automation team understand and address your automation needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose the correct company and job title that best represents your role.
3. Select the department and location of your job within the company.
4. Specify the type of automation inquiry you have (e.g., "Yes" for general inquiry, "No" for a specific project).
5. Describe your automation inquiry in the "Inquiry Description" field.
6. Specify your request for follow-up (e.g., "Yes" for follow-up, "No" for no follow-up).
7. Choose the tool assigned to you for the automation task.
8. Fill in any additional notes or comments about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`1`): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for follow-up.
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your work email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Automation Inquiry** (`2`): Specify what your automation inquiry is about.
* **Name** (`3`): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`4`): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`5`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Company** (`6`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your company has a presence in multiple locations.
* **Job Function** (`7`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you're a part of the automation team.
* **Department** (`8`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your department is involved in the automation process.
* **Location** (`9`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your location is relevant to the automation process.
* **Company Name** (`10`): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`11`): Enter your job title.
* **Inquiry Type** (`12`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to specify the type of inquiry.
* **Inquiry Description** (`13`): Describe your automation inquiry in detail.
* **Request for** (`14`): Specify if you're requesting automation for a specific task or project.
* **Follow-up** (`15`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want follow-up on your inquiry.
* **Follow-up by** (`16`): Choose a date for the follow-up.
* **Assigned to** (`17`): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you're assigned to this task.
* **Notes** (`18`): Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submitted by** (`19`): Enter your full name.
* **Submitted Date** (`20`): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submitted Time** (`21`): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submitted by Email** (`22`): Enter your work email address.
* **Submitted by Phone** (`23`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`24`): Enter the tool assigned to you for the automation task.
* **Assigned User** (`25`): Enter the user assigned to you for the automation task.
