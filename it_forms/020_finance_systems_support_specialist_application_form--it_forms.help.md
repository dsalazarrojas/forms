# Finance Systems Support Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in applying for various financial roles within the company. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit Application" button to start your application process. 
2. Fill out all relevant fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your job history.
3. Select your desired job title and type from the dropdown menus.
4. Upload a resume and a cover letter in PDF format.
5. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information (email address, phone number, etc.).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Share your work experience relevant to the position you're applying for.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Mention your highest level of education completed.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List your relevant skills.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide contact information for two professional references.
* **Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, optional): Attach a PDF copy of your resume.
* **Job Applications** (`job_applications`, text, optional): List job applications you've submitted in the past 6 months.
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Job ID** (`job_application_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Choose the job title you're applying for.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of position you're applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the job you're applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Describe the job you're applying for in your own words.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the job is located.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected start date for the job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected end date for the job.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, optional): Enter the expected salary for the job.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): List the job requirements.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the job category you're applying for.
* **Job Category Other** (`job_category_other`, text, optional): Enter any other job category not listed.
* **Job Education** (`job_education`, text, optional): Enter your education level for the job.
* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, optional): Enter any other information about the job application form.
