# it_service_ticket_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Service Ticket Form is a tool to help you report and manage IT service tickets. The form is designed to gather necessary information about the issue or request you're submitting, such as category, priority, and due date. This form is meant for internal use within the organization, and the information collected will help the IT team to efficiently manage and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Choose the Category of the Ticket
Select the category that best describes the nature of your request or issue. This will help the IT team understand the scope of the problem and prioritize accordingly. The available categories are:

* IT Forms
* Other

### Step 2: Set the Priority Level
Choose the priority level of your issue. Please note that this field is not required but it can help the IT team to allocate resources and time for resolution more effectively.

### Step 3: Assign a User to Resolve the Issue
Select a team member or user who will be responsible for resolving the issue. You can choose from the following options: chatjimmy, other option

### Step 4: Set the Due Date for Resolution
Indicate the date by which you expect the issue to be resolved. Please note that this field is not required, but it helps the IT team to plan and schedule the resolution of the issue.

### Step 5: Add Any Additional Comments or Description
You can provide any additional details or comments about the issue. This field is optional but can provide more context for the team resolving the issue.

### Step 6: Specify the Status of the Ticket
Please indicate whether the ticket is Open or Closed. This field is required to track the progress of the issue resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose the category that best describes the nature of your request or issue. (Required)
* **Priority**: Choose the priority level of your issue. (Optional)
* **Assigned To**: Select a team member or user who will be responsible for resolving the issue. (Required)
* **Due Date**: Indicate the date by which you expect the issue to be resolved. (Optional)
* **Status**: Specify whether the ticket is Open or Closed. (Required)
* **Description**: Provide any additional comments or details about the issue. (Optional) 
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the issue. (Optional)
* **Resolution**: Add the resolution of the issue. (Optional)
