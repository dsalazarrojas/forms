# uplers_design_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The uplers_design_questionnaire form is used to gather information about your team's design projects. This form helps you to track and report on various aspects of your projects, such as team size, project status, client type, start and end dates, budget, designer involvement, design system, and technology used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages and fill out each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Design Questionnaire** (`design_questionnaire`, `note`, **required**): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your project.
* **Uplers Team Size** (`uplers_team_size`, `number`, **required**): Enter the size of your team, including the number of team members working on the project.
* **Uplers Team Size Hint** (`uplers_team_size_hint`, `note`, **optional**): If you need to provide additional information about your team size, you can enter it here.
* **Uplers Project Status** (`uplers_project_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the status of your project: Active or Inactive.
* **Uplers Client Type** (`uplers_client_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select whether the client is Yes or No.
* **Uplers Start Date** (`uplers_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the start date of your project in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-03-15).
* **Uplers End Date** (`uplers_end_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the end date of your project in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-06-15).
* **Uplers Budget** (`uplers_budget`, `number`, **required**): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Uplers Designer** (`uplers_designer`, `select_one`, **required**): Select whether you are the designer or not: Yes or No.
* **Uplers Designer Hint** (`uplers_designer_hint`, `note`, **optional**): If you need to provide additional information about your designer, you can enter it here.
* **Uplers Design System** (`uplers_design_system`, `text`, **required**): Enter the design system used for your project.
* **Uplers Technology** (`uplers_technology`, `text`, **required**): Enter the technology used for your project.
* **Uplers Technology Hint** (`uplers_technology_hint`, `note`, **optional**): If you need to provide additional information about your technology, you can enter it here.
* **Uplers Design Notes** (`uplers_design_notes`, `note`, **optional**): If you have any additional information about your project, you can enter it here.
