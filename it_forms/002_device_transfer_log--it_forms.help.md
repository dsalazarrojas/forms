# Device Transfer Log - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture essential details about the transfer of a device between organizations. It's a quick and easy way to document the transfer, helping you keep track of your assets and ensure they are properly accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
2. Enter the date of the transfer in the `Transfer Date` field.
3. Provide the current asset's name and serial number.
4. Indicate the current organization (from) and the organization the asset is being transferred to (to).
5. Select the asset's status (active or inactive).
6. Choose the reason for the transfer.
7. Specify the asset's condition (e.g., good, fair, poor).
8. Indicate the transfer responsibility (e.g., which team or department is responsible for the transfer).
9. Finally, enter any additional notes about the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Transfer**: Enter the date when the asset was transferred.
* **Asset Name**: Enter the name of the device being transferred (e.g., laptop, smartphone, etc.).
* **Asset Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the device (if applicable).
* **From Organisation**: Choose the organization that currently possesses the asset (yes/no).
* **To Organisation**: Choose the organization the asset is being transferred to (yes/no).
* **Asset Condition**: Select all that apply for the asset's current condition.
* **Asset Status**: Select all that apply for the asset's status (active or inactive).
* **Transfer Responsibility**: Select all that apply for the transfer responsibility.
* **Transfer Reason**: Enter a brief explanation of the transfer reason.
* **Transfer Date**: Enter the date of the transfer (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Asset Serial Number**: Enter the asset's serial number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Use a new form for each distinct transfer.
* If a field doesn't apply to your specific situation, just leave it blank. The form will still work correctly.
