# APS Infrastructure Tailboard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The APS Infrastructure Tailboard Form is a tool used to document and review infrastructure work tasks, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and communicated among team members and supervisors. This form aims to streamline the tailboard process, making it more efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information:
	* **Tailboard Date**: Enter the date of the tailboard meeting.
	* **Lead Supervisor**: Enter the name of the lead supervisor conducting the tailboard.
	* **Team Members Present**: List all team members attending the tailboard.
	* **Work Area Location**: Specify the location of the infrastructure work.
	* **Work Description**: Briefly describe the infrastructure work to be performed.
	* **Equipment to be Used**: List the equipment that will be used during the work.
	* **Safety Hazards Identified**: Identify any safety hazards associated with the work.
	* **PPE Requirements**: Select the personal protective equipment (PPE) needed.
	* **Risk Level Assessment**: Assess the overall risk level of the work (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
	* **Emergency Procedures**: Describe the emergency procedures for the work area.
	* **Communication Protocol**: Describe how team members will communicate during the work.
	* **Work Completion Criteria**: Define how you will know when the work is complete.
	* **Signatures Required**: Signatures of all team members acknowledging the tailboard.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tailboard Date**: Date of the tailboard meeting.
* **Tailboard Time**: Time of the tailboard meeting. Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Lead Supervisor**: Name of the lead supervisor conducting the tailboard.
* **Team Members Present**: List all team members attending the tailboard.
* **Work Area Location**: Specific location of the infrastructure work.
* **Work Description**: Brief description of the infrastructure work to be performed.
* **Equipment to be Used**: List the equipment that will be used during the work.
* **Safety Hazards Identified**: Identify any safety hazards associated with the work.
* **PPE Requirements**: Select all necessary PPE (Hard Hat, Safety Glasses, Gloves, Steel Toe Boots, High Visibility Vest, Face Shield, Hearing Protection, Fall Protection).
* **Risk Level Assessment**: Assess the overall risk level of the work (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Emergency Procedures**: Describe the emergency procedures for the work area.
* **Communication Protocol**: Describe how team members will communicate during the work.
* **Work Completion Criteria**: Define how you will know when the work is complete.
* **Signatures Required**: Signatures of all team members acknowledging the tailboard.

Note: The optional field **Next Steps/Follow-up** can be used to describe any additional information or next steps after completing the work.
