# Building Information Model Access Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the project Building Information Model (BIM). Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your access request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to fill out this form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email address**: Enter your official email address associated with your work or organization.
* **Phone number**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Company name**: Enter your company name.
* **Job title or position**: Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Professional Role**: Select the role that best describes your job function or discipline. (e.g., Architect, Engineer, Contractor, etc.)
* **Discipline or Specialty**: Select the discipline or specialty that best describes your area of expertise. (e.g., Architecture, Structural engineering, MEP engineering, etc.)
* **Firm type**: Select the type of firm or organization you represent. (e.g., Design firm, Engineering firm, Construction/contractor, etc.)
* **Project name**: Enter the name of the project you are requesting access to.
* **Project phase**: Select the current phase of the project you are accessing. (e.g., Schematic design, Design development, Construction documents, etc.)
* **Required access level**: Select the access level you require to access the project BIM. (e.g., View only, Read access, Contributor, Full access, etc.)
* **BIM components or models needed**: Select the BIM components or models you need access to. (e.g., Architectural model, Structural model, MEP model, etc.)
* **Specific elements or areas of focus**: Enter any specific elements or areas of focus you need access to.
* **Purpose of BIM access**: Select the purpose of your BIM access. (e.g., Project design, Construction planning, MEP coordination, etc.)
* **Detailed description of intended use**: Provide a detailed description of how you intend to use the project BIM.
* **Duration of access**: Select the duration of access you require. (e.g., 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, etc.)
* **Start date of access**: Enter the start date you require access to begin.
* **End date of access**: Enter the expected end date for access. (Note: If this is an ongoing access, select "Ongoing" instead.)
* **Additional team members needing access**: Enter the names and email addresses of any additional team members needing access.
* **Total number of team members needing access**: Enter the total number of team members needing access.
* **BIM software platform to be used**: Select the BIM software platform you will be using.
* **Software version**: Enter the software version you will be using.
* **BIM experience level**: Select your level of BIM experience. (e.g., Expert, Intermediate, Beginner, etc.)
* **Confidentiality/NDA agreement**: Select your agreement status regarding confidentiality and NDA.
* **Completed BIM security training**: Select whether you have completed BIM security training.
* **Agreement to proper BIM data handling**: Select whether you agree to proper BIM data handling.
* **Direct manager or supervisor name**: Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Manager's email for approval notification**: Enter your manager's email address for approval notification.
* **Additional information or special requests**: Enter any additional information or special requests.

## Tips

* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your project manager or supervisor.
* This form is intended for external users, so please do not use it if you already have access to the project BIM as an internal team member.
* If you are submitting this form for multiple team members, please ensure that each team member's information is accurate and up-to-date.
