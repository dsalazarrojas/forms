# Cloud Commerce Infrastructure Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from e-commerce organizations about their infrastructure requirements and preferences for a cloud commerce infrastructure application. This data will help us better understand your needs and provide a tailored solution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section: Organization Information**
	* Fill in your organization's name and type (e.g., e-commerce company, retail chain, marketplace, etc.).
	* Select your organization's annual revenue range (e.g., under 1M, 1M-10M, etc.).
2. **Section: Contact Information**
	* Enter your full name and job title as an authorized representative.
	* Provide your email address and phone number.
3. **Section: Infrastructure Requirements**
	* Describe your current infrastructure setup (on-premises, cloud, hybrid, etc.).
	* Estimate your expected monthly traffic (commerce transactions) and peak load capacity (transactions per second).
	* Provide the estimated data storage needed for product data, customer data, and transactions.
4. **Section: Required Capabilities**
	* Select the features you require for your cloud commerce application (e.g., payment processing, inventory management, etc.).
5. **Section: Evaluation Criteria**
	* Choose an estimated annual budget range (e.g., under 50k, 50k-100k, etc.).
	* Select your desired implementation timeline (e.g., immediate, quick, standard, etc.).
	* Identify your key priorities for the project (e.g., cost, performance, scalability, security, etc.).
6. **Additional Information**
	* Provide any additional notes or context about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: The name of your organization.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., e-commerce company, retail chain, etc.).
* **Annual Revenue**: Estimate your organization's annual revenue range.
* **Full Name**: The full name of the authorized representative.
* **Job Title**: The job title of the authorized representative.
* **Email Address**: The email address of the authorized representative.
* **Phone Number**: The phone number of the authorized representative.
* **Current Infrastructure**: Describe your current infrastructure setup.
* **Expected Traffic**: Estimate your expected monthly traffic (commerce transactions).
* **Peak Load**: Provide the peak load capacity needed (transactions per second).
* **Data Storage**: Estimate the data storage needed for product data, customer data, and transactions.
* **Required Features**: Select the required features for your cloud commerce application.
* **Integration Needs**: Describe any integration needs with existing systems (e.g., CRM, ERP, accounting, etc.).
* **Compliance Requirements**: Describe any compliance and regulations needed (e.g., PCI-DSS, GDPR, HIPAA, etc.).
* **Budget Range**: Choose an estimated annual budget range.
* **Implementation Timeline**: Select the desired implementation timeline.
* **Key Priorities**: Identify your key priorities for the project.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional notes or context about your project.
