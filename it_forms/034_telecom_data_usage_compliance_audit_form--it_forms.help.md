# Telecom Data Usage Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Telecom Data Usage Compliance Audit Form is a tool used to assess the compliance status of data usage practices within a Telecom company. It helps to identify areas of non-compliance, evaluate collection methods, and determine policy effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form sections carefully, and ensure you have the necessary information and authorization to complete it accurately.
2.  Fill in the required fields according to your knowledge and experience.
3.  Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4.  If you're unsure about any field or section, please reach out to your supervisor or internal compliance team for guidance.
