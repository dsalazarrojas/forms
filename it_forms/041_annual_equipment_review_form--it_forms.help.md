# Annual Equipment Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential data about an equipment's condition, functionality, and maintenance status, enabling the organization to make informed decisions about its maintenance, upgrade, or replacement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your reviewer's information, including your name, department/team, and email address.
2. Provide the equipment's name, description, or model number.
3. Choose the equipment type from the given options.
4. Enter the equipment's current location, if applicable.
5. If applicable, specify the date and cost of purchase.
6. Select the overall condition and functionality of the equipment.
7. Describe any performance or technical issues with the equipment.
8. Provide any recent maintenance or repair history.
9. Specify if maintenance is needed and provide details about it.
10. Make recommendations for the equipment's future (e.g., keep in service, upgrade, etc.).
11. Select a replacement timeline, if applicable.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, required): Please fill in your full name to help identify you as the reviewer of this equipment.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, required): Specify the department or team that owns the equipment.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address in case further information is needed.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, required): Enter the date of this review.
* **Equipment Name or Description** (`equipment_name`, required): Enter the equipment's name, model number, or description.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, required): Choose the category of equipment from the provided options.
* **Equipment ID or Serial Number** (`equipment_id`, optional): Enter the equipment's asset tracking number, if applicable.
* **Current Location** (`asset_location`, optional): Specify the current location of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Date of Purchase** (`purchase_date`, optional): Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **Original Purchase Cost** (`purchase_cost`, optional): Enter the cost of the equipment when purchased.
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`, optional): Select whether the warranty is active or expired.
* **Overall Equipment Condition** (`overall_condition`, required): Choose the overall physical condition of the equipment from the provided options.
* **Equipment Functionality** (`functionality`, required): Select whether the equipment is fully functional or has issues.
* **Performance or Technical Issues** (`performance_issues`, optional): Describe any problems with the equipment.
* **Maintenance or Repairs Performed** (`maintenance_records`, optional): Enter any recent maintenance or repair history.
* **Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_needed`, optional): Specify if maintenance is required and provide details.
* **Action Recommended** (`action_needed`, required): Choose the recommended action for the equipment from the provided options.
* **Replacement Timeline** (`replacement_timeframe`, optional): Select a timeline for replacing the equipment, if applicable.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the equipment.
