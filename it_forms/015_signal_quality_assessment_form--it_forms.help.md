# signal_quality_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess signal quality in a series of signal levels from 1 to 15. It gathers information about the location, signal level, and other relevant details to help evaluate the signal's quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the location where the signal was assessed.
2. Select the signal level from 1 to 15 for each of the corresponding fields.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
4. If you have any further locations to report, enter them in the "Location 2", "Location 3", and "Location 4" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location**: Enter the location where the signal was assessed.
* **Signal Level**: Select the signal level from 1 to 15, where 1 is the best and 15 is the worst. The signal level will determine the quality of the signal in that location.
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time of day when the signal was assessed. This is a required field.
* **Issue Reported**: Enter any issues or problems reported with the signal.
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician who assessed the signal.
* **Technician Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the technician.
* **Supervisor Notes**: Enter any supervisor notes or comments.
* **Signal Level 2** to **Signal Level 15**: Select the signal level from 1 to 15 for each corresponding field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Location 2**, **Location 3**, and **Location 4**: Enter any further locations to report.
* **Signal Level 2** to **Signal Level 15**: Select the signal level from 1 to 15 for each corresponding field for the additional locations.
