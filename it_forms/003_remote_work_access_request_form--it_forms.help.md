# Remote Work Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request remote work access for a set period of time. Fill out the form to provide necessary information for your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure accurate information is provided for each field.
3. Click submit to send your request to the approver for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your company records.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your company email address.
* **Contact Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Department (4)**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Remote Start Date (5)**: Enter the date you want to start working remotely.
* **Remote End Date (6)**: Enter the date you want to stop working remotely.
* **Remote Reason (7)**: Explain the reason for your remote work request.
* **Approval Status (8)**: Select the approval status from the options provided by the approver.
* **Approver (9)**: Enter the name of the person who approved your request.
* **Manager Approve Date (10)**: Enter the date the manager approved your request.
* **IT Approver (11)**: Enter the name of the IT person who approved your request.
* **IT Approve Date (12)**: Enter the date the IT person approved your request.
* **Remote Access Duration (13)**: Enter the duration of remote work you are requesting.
* **Comments (14)**: Add any additional comments for the approver.
* **Approver Comments (15)**: Add any comments from the approver.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate and complete information is provided in each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your approver or HR department.
