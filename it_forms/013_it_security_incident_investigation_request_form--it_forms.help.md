# it_security_incident_investigation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about security incidents, including details about the incident, its impact, type, and status. The purpose of this form is to facilitate a swift and accurate investigation, which will enable the incident response team to respond effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully, ensuring that you provide accurate and relevant information about the incident.
2. Make sure to provide all the required information, as indicated by the * symbol in each field.
3. If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details that can help the incident response team understand the situation.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Security Incident** (`security_incident`, `text`, required): Describe the type of security incident that occurred (e.g., Phishing, Malware, Ransomware).
* **Impact** (`impact`, `text`, required): Explain the impact of the incident (e.g., financial, data loss, reputational).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of incident (Phishing, Malware, Ransomware, Other).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the incident (Active, Resolved).
* **Incident Reporter Name** (`incident_reporter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Role** (`incident_reporter_role`, `text`, required): Enter your role as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Department** (`incident_reporter_department`, `text`, required): Enter your department as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Team** (`incident_reporter_team`, `text`, required): Enter your team as the incident reporter.
* **Incident Reporter Comments** (`incident_reporter_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about the incident.
* **Incident Assignee Name** (`incident_assignee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person assigned to investigate the incident.
* **Incident Assignee Role** (`incident_assignee_role`, `text`, required): Enter the role of the person assigned to investigate the incident.
* **Incident Assignee Department** (`incident_assignee_department`, `text`, required): Enter the department of the person assigned to investigate the incident.
* **Incident Assignee Team** (`incident_assignee_team`, `text`, required): Enter the team of the person assigned to investigate the incident.
* **Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, `text`, required): Enter the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Status Comments** (`incident_status_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments about the incident status.
* **Last Updated** (`incident_last_updated`, `date`, required): This field is automatically updated by the system.
* **Created** (`incident_created`, `date`, required): This field is automatically updated by the system.
* **Last Status Update** (`incident_last_status_update`, `date`, required): This field is automatically updated by the system.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above for guidance.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the incident response team for assistance.
