# IT Room Weekly Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for conducting weekly inspections of the IT room to ensure the upkeep and maintenance of the equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your details**: Fill in your name and any other identifying information as instructed in the "Inspector Details" field.
2. **Date and Time**: Enter the current date and time of the inspection in the "Date Inspected" field.
3. **Fill out equipment condition questions**: Answer the questions about the condition of various equipment in the IT room, using the options provided.
4. **Note any issues**: In the "Issues" field, briefly describe any problems or concerns you have about the equipment or the IT room.
5. **Add comments**: If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
6. **Sign and date**: Finally, sign the form with your name and date in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Details**: This field is where you enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Date Inspected**: Enter the current date and time of the inspection.
* **Date Created**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was created.
* **Equipment Condition**: Answer the questions about the condition of each piece of equipment, using the options provided.
* **Cleanliness**: Assess the overall cleanliness of the IT room, using the options provided.
* **Temperature**: Enter the current temperature of the IT room in degrees Celsius.
* **Security**: Check the security of the IT room, using the options provided.
* **Issues**: Briefly describe any problems or concerns you have about the equipment or the IT room.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the inspection.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your name and date, confirming you have completed the inspection.
