# Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Device Tracking Consent Form is designed to gather information about devices from customers, including their contact information, device details, and consent for tracking. This form helps us understand the needs of our customers and ensures that we are providing them with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you consent to device tracking.
2. Enter your customer information, including any relevant details.
3. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your device's tracking status.
4. Enter your device's serial number for identification purposes.
5. Choose the frequency of tracking for your device (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
6. Enter any specific tracking frequency options (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
7. Provide any necessary device information.
8. Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the current tracking status of your device.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about your device.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Device Tracking Consent
* **Device Tracking Consent** (`device_tracking_consent_1`, `select_one`, required): Check "True" to consent to device tracking, or "False" to decline.
### Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info_1`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
### Email
* **Email** (`email_1`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your device's tracking status.
### Device Serial Number
* **Device Serial Number** (`device_serial_number_1`, `text`, required): Enter your device's serial number for identification purposes.
### Tracking Frequency
* **Tracking Frequency** (`tracking_frequency_1`, `text`, required): Choose the frequency of tracking for your device (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
### Tracking Frequency Options
* **Tracking Frequency Options** (`tracking_frequency_options_1`, `text`, required): Enter specific tracking frequency options (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
### Device Information
* **Device Information** (`device_info_1`, `text`, required): Provide any necessary device information.
### Tracking Status
* **Tracking Status** (`tracking_status_1`, `select_one`, required): Select "Active" to indicate the current tracking status of your device, or "Inactive".
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments_1`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your device.
### Tracking Frequency (2)
* **Tracking Frequency (2)** (`tracking_frequency_2`, `text`, optional): If you have additional tracking frequency information, enter it here.
### Device Information (2)
* **Device Information (2)** (`device_info_2`, `text`, optional): If you have additional device information, enter it here.
### Tracking Status (2)
* **Tracking Status (2)** (`tracking_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): If you have an updated tracking status for your device, select it here.
