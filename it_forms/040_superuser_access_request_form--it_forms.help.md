# Superuser Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Superuser Access Request Form is used to request elevated access to certain systems and resources within the organization. This form is typically used by users who need to perform tasks that require higher privileges or access to sensitive data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for tracking purposes.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select your role to determine which level of access you are requesting.
* **Access Request** (`access_request`, note, optional): Provide a clear explanation of why you need elevated access.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select the names of the users who will approve your request.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your request for tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed the necessary documentation before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about which level of access you need, consult with your supervisor or a system administrator.
* Be clear and concise in your **Access Request** field to ensure a smooth approval process.
