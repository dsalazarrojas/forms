# software-budget-estimation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you estimate software budget for your project. It collects information about your project and team to provide an estimation of the costs involved.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
  1. Scroll down to the "Project Details" page and fill in the necessary information about your project.
  2. Move on to the "Budget Planning" page and enter any relevant details about your budget planning.
  3. Finally, go to the "Team Estimation" page and provide an estimate of the team's size and experience.
  4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project, including its name and a short summary of its goals and objectives.
  - This field is used to get a better understanding of your project's requirements and scope.
- **Budget Planning** (`budget_planning`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about your budget planning, including your financial goals and constraints.
  - This field helps us understand how you plan to manage your budget for the project.
- **Team Estimation** (`team_estimation`, text, optional): Estimate the number of people involved in the project and their levels of experience.
  - This field is used to estimate the cost of labor and expertise required for the project.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to get an accurate budget estimation.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help.
- This form is for internal use only and all collected data will be kept confidential.
