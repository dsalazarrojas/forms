# Agent Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Submission Form is used to collect information from agents who need to submit data on behalf of others. This form is essential for tracking and managing submissions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your full name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter your job title and the nature of your submission (new agent, update details, or remove agent).
3. Enter any additional information or details related to your submission.
4. Select the date of your submission.
5. If you have a reference number for this submission, enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Company address** (`company_address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the address of your broker office or mailing address.
* **Submission type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the nature of your submission: New agent, Update details, or Remove agent.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any extra details related to your submission.
* **Date submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, `required`): Select the date of your submission.
* **Reference number** (`reference_number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the internal reference or ticket number for your submission.

## Tips
- Please ensure that each reference number is unique to avoid duplicate submissions.
- Double-check your submission details for accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask your contact person for clarification.
