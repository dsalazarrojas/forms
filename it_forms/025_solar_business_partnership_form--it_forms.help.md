# Solar Business Partnership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Business Partnership Form is designed to collect information about your solar business, including its title, system details, contact information, and business address. This form is used to facilitate the partnership process between your solar business and our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your solar business information, including its title and a brief description.
2. Provide details about your system, such as its type, size, and technology.
3. Enter your business contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter your business address.
5. If you have a system administrator, provide their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Information** (`business_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and a brief description of your solar business.
- **System Details** (`system_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe your solar system, including its type, size, and technology.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
- **Solar System Details** (`solar_system_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide further details about your solar system, including its features and capabilities.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business address.
- **System Admin** (`system_admin`, `text`, required: false): If you have a system administrator, enter their name and contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
