# Digital Trace Data Exchange Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Digital Trace Data Exchange Application Form is designed to collect information from users about their trace data requests, contact information, and consent for data usage. This form is used by the organization's data exchange team to manage and review user requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Application" field with a brief description of your data request.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. In the "Contact Details" field, provide detailed information about the PI or their contact.
4. Describe your data request in the "Data Request" field.
5. Indicate your intended use for the data in the "Intended Use" field.
6. Select "Agree" to confirm your consent to the Data Usage Policy in the "Consent to Data Usage Policy" field.
7. Verify that the contact information provided is correct in the "Confirm Contact" field.
8. Confirm that you agree with the terms of service in the "Terms of Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application** (application_form, text, optional)
	+ A brief description of your data request.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional)
	+ Provide your name and contact details, such as email and phone number.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, optional)
	+ Provide detailed contact information for the PI or their contact.
* **Data Request** (data_request, text, optional)
	+ Describe what trace data you are requesting and why you are requesting it.
* **Intended Use** (intended_use, text, optional)
	+ Indicate your intended use for the data.
* **Consent to Data Usage Policy** (consent, select_one, optional)
	+ Select "Agree" to confirm that you understand and agree to the data usage policy.
* **Confirm Contact** (contact_info_confirmation, select_one, optional)
	+ Select "True" to confirm that the contact information provided is correct.
* **Confirm** (data_request_confirmation, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select "Agree" to confirm that you agree with the terms of service.
* **Verification** (contact_info_verification, select_one, optional)
	+ Select "Agree" to confirm that you have verified your contact information.
* **Terms of Service** (terms_of_service, select_one, optional)
	+ Select "Agree" to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of service.
