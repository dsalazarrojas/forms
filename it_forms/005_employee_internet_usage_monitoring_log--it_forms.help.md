# Employee Internet Usage Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor an employee's internet usage during a specific period. It is designed to be completed by the person who monitored the employee's internet usage.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have all the necessary information and approvals to complete the form.
2. Select the employee whose internet usage you are monitoring from the "Monitored User" dropdown list.
3. Choose the time period for which you are monitoring the employee's internet usage from the "start_date" and "end_date" fields.
4. Indicate the categories of internet usage that were monitored by selecting from the "Internet Usage Category" dropdown list.
5. Record the amount of time the employee spent on the internet and on the workplace internet by filling in the "Time Spent On Internet" and "Time Spent On Workplace Internet" fields.
6. List the devices used by the employee during the monitored period in the "Devices Used" field.
7. Provide any additional notes about the employee's internet usage in the "Internet Usage Notes" field.
