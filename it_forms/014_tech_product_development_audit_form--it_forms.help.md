> # tech_product_development_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Product Development Audit Form is a tool used to collect detailed information about projects' development processes. It helps project managers and leads to document and evaluate projects, highlighting successes, lessons learned, and areas for improvement.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to the project's actual circumstances.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for text fields.
3. Choose the relevant options for select_multiple fields where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Project Info** (text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the project.

### 2. **Project Status** (select_multiple, required: false)
Select from the following options:
* Project In Progress
* Project Under Review
* Project Live
* Project Cancelled

### 3. **Team Members** (text, required: false)
List the names of team members involved in the project.

### 4. **Project Risks** (text, required: false)
Provide detailed descriptions of any project risks or concerns.

### 5. **Development Practices** (text, required: false)
Describe the software development practices used during the project.

### 6. **Team Structure** (text, required: false)
Explain how the team was structured during the project.

### 7. **Communication Plan** (text, required: false)
Describe the communication plan employed by the team.

### 8. **Testing Plan** (text, required: false)
Specify the type of testing performed during the project.

### 9. **Quality Control** (text, required: false)
Outline the measures the team took to ensure quality control.

### 10. **Project Management Tools** (text, required: false)
List the project management tools used during the project.

### 11. **Risk Management** (text, required: false)
Explain how the team handled risk management during the project.

### 12. **Lessons Learned** (text, required: false)
Highlight some of the important lessons learned during the project.

### 13. **Project Status Update** (text, required: false)
Provide the current status of the project.

### 14. **Additional Comments** (text, required: false)
Include any additional comments or observations about the project.

### 15. **Project Lead** (text, required: false)
Enter the name of the project lead or team lead.

This form is a useful tool for project review and improvement, and we recommend filling it out carefully and accurately to get the most value out of it.
