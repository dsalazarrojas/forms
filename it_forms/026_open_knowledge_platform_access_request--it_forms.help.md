# Open Knowledge Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Open Knowledge Platform. It helps us understand who is requesting access and how they plan to use the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Requester Details" field.
2. Briefly describe how you plan to use the Open Knowledge Platform in the "Intended Use" field.
3. Check the "Terms Agreement" field to indicate your understanding and agreement to the terms of access.
4. Provide any additional comments or information about your request in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Enter any further details about your intended use of the Open Knowledge Platform in the "Intended Use" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Open Knowledge Platform Access Request** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form that is automatically populated by the form's metadata.
* **Requester Details** (`form_requester_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you if necessary.
* **Intended Use** (`intended_use`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to use the Open Knowledge Platform. This will help us understand your needs and ensure you have the necessary access.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, optional): Check "Agreed" to indicate that you have read and understand the terms of access.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further information or comments about your request, please provide them here.
* **Intended Use** (`form_intended_use`, text, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide further details about your intended use of the Open Knowledge Platform.
