# Log Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your role, log management frequency, and satisfaction level with the current log management system. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and optimize our processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the frequency at which you perform log management activities.
3. Rate your satisfaction level with the current log management system.
4. Enter a numerical value for the log management savings (if applicable).
5. Enter a numerical value for the log management cost (if applicable).
6. Describe your main pain point with the current log management system.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role** (required): Select the department or team you belong to, such as IT or Security.
* **Frequency of log management** (required): Choose the frequency at which you perform log management activities, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Satisfaction Level** (required): Rate your satisfaction level with the current log management system, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Log Management Savings** (optional): Enter a numerical value for the log management savings you've experienced.
* **Log Management Cost** (optional): Enter a numerical value for the log management cost you've incurred.
* **Log Management Pain Point** (required): Describe your main pain point with the current log management system in the text field.
* **Comment** (optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the log management system.
