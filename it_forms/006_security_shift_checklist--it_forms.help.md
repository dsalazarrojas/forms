# Security Shift Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Security Shift Checklist is a form designed to collect information from shift leaders on the security of their shifts. This form is intended to ensure that shift leaders are aware of and adhere to security standards during their shifts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your observations during the shift. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
2. Ensure that you answer each field accurately and honestly.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "comments" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Security Team Members** (`security_team_members`): Indicate if your team members are aware of and adhering to security standards during the shift. Select "Yes" if they are, "No" if they are not.
* **Security Standards** (`security_standards`): Indicate if the security standards have been adopted during the shift. Select "Yes" if they have, "No" if they have not.
* **Potential Lapses** (`potential_lapses`): Enter any potential lapses or areas of concern regarding security during the shift.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding security during the shift.
* **Security Standards Adopted** (`security_standards_adopted`): Indicate if security standards have been adopted during the shift. Select "Yes" if they have, "No" if they have not.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you are unsure of a field, please select the option that best reflects your understanding.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities.
