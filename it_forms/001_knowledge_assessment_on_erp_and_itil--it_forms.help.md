# knowledge_assessment_on_erp_and_itil - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess your knowledge on ERPs and ITIL. Please take your time to fill in this form to demonstrate your understanding of the concepts, principles, and terminology related to ERPs and ITIL.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions in the form, and make sure you fill in all the required fields. You can skip the optional fields if you are not sure about the answers.
2. For multiple-select questions, select all the options that apply to your understanding.
3. For text fields, give your answers in a clear and concise manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_erp** (`what_is_erp`, text, required): This field is used to assess your understanding of what an ERP is. Please explain briefly what you know about ERPs.
* **what_is_itil** (`what_is_itil`, text, required): This field is used to assess your understanding of what ITIL is. Please explain briefly what you know about ITIL.
* **how_do_you_know_erp_and_itil** (`erp_and_itil_relation`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to evaluate how you know the relation between ERPs and ITIL. Select all the options that apply to your understanding.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you would like to add to your answers. You can use this field to provide more information or clarify your answers.

## Tips
- Please make sure to answer the questions accurately and truthfully.
- If you are not sure about some concepts, it's better not to fill in the form.
- You can skip the optional fields if you are not sure about the answers.
