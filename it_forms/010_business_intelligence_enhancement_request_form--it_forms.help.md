# Business Intelligence Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about the business intelligence enhancements needed by your team or department. It helps the BI team to understand the requirements and prioritize the requests effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person requesting this enhancement.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department or team from the options provided.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when this enhancement request was made.
* **Enhancement Type** (`enhancement_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of BI enhancement needed from the options provided.
* **Enhancement Title** (`enhancement_title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for this request.
* **Enhancement Description** (`enhancement_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the enhancement needed.
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, `text`, required): Explain why this enhancement is necessary.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the urgency level of this request from the options provided.
* **Affected BI Systems** (`affected_systems`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the BI systems affected by this enhancement.
* **Data Sources Involved** (`data_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the relevant data sources for this enhancement.
* **Current Limitations** (`current_limitations`, `text`, required): Describe the current limitations with the existing setup.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, `text`, required): Specify the expected results from this enhancement.
* **Target Users** (`target_users`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify the users who will use this enhancement.
* **Estimated User Count** (`user_count`, `number`, optional): Enter an approximate number of users who will use this enhancement.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when this enhancement is needed by.
* **Dependencies** (`dependencies`, `text`, optional): List any dependencies for this request.
* **Reporting Requirements** (`reporting_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reporting needs for this enhancement.
* **KPI Metrics to Track** (`kpi_metrics`, `text`, optional): List key metrics to be included in the enhancement.
* **Management Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if management has approved this request.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant information about this request.

Note: Fields marked with `required` are essential to fill out to submit the form.
