# vpn_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a VPN (Virtual Private Network) for employees. It collects information about the employee, their job title, department, and team, as well as their reason for requesting access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Employee Email" field.
3. Select your Job Title from the "Job Title" dropdown menu.
4. Select your Department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
5. Select the type of VPN access you require from the "VPN Type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose a reason for your VPN request from the "Reason for VPN request" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your Supervisor's Name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
8. Enter your Manager's Name in the "Manager Name" field.
9. Select your Team from the "Team" dropdown menu.
10. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
11. Enter the tool you will be using with the VPN access in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Employee Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your work account.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): Select your job title from the dropdown menu.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select the department you belong to from the dropdown menu.
* **VPN Type** (`vpn_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of VPN access you require from the dropdown menu.
* **Reason for VPN request** (`reason`, select_one, required): Choose a reason for your VPN request from the dropdown menu.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): Select your team from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments or information about your VPN request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool you will be using with the VPN access.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select the correct options for your job title, department, VPN type, reason, supervisor, manager, team, and assigned tool.
* Add any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field if necessary.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it for approval.
