# IT Operation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and usage of the IT Operation Checklist form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Computer Status** (`Computer Status`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the status of the computer system(s) being monitored. Choose one or more of the following options: Active or Inactive.
* **Server Status** (`Server Status`, select_one, required): This field indicates the status of the server(s) associated with the computer system. Choose one of the following options: Active or Inactive.
* **Network Status** (`Network Status`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the status of the network. Choose one or more of the following options: Active or Inactive.
* **User Impact** (`User Impact`, select_multiple, required): This field indicates if users will be impacted by the issue. Choose one or more of the following options: Yes or No.
* **Tech Support Team** (`Tech Support Team`, select_one, required): This field determines if the Tech Support Team is involved or not. Choose one of the following options: Yes or No.
* **Priority** (`Priority`, select_one, required): This field prioritizes the issue based on its urgency. Choose one of the following options: Yes or No.
* **Date Last Updated** (`Date Last Updated`, date, optional): This field is for tracking the date and time of the last update. Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format and the time in 24-hour format.
* **Time Last Updated** (`Time Last Updated`, time, optional): This field is for tracking the time of the last update. Enter the time in 24-hour format.

## Tips
* Always refer to the IT team if you are unsure about any of the fields or options.
* This form is intended for quick reference and tracking, not for comprehensive analysis or long-term planning.
* Make sure to update the form regularly to reflect changes and updates made to the IT systems and services.
