# Internet Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet Service Request Form is used to collect user's internet service request details, including personal contact information, service type, and problem description, to efficiently route it to the assigned technician for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the "name" field. Please make sure to provide your full name as it appears in your identity documents.
2. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field. Make sure the email address you provide is correct and up-to-date.
3. Enter your **phone number** in the "phone" field. This is used by the support team to contact you for further assistance or to update you on the status of your request.
4. Type your **location** in the "location" field. This will help the assigned technician know your exact location, making it easier to resolve the issue.
5. Describe your **problem description** in the "problem description" field. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure the technician understands your concern.
6. Select your **service type** from the drop-down menu in the "service type" field. This helps the support team to identify the correct type of service needed.
7. If necessary, select your **priority** level in the "priority" field. This will help the support team to prioritize the request based on urgency.
8. Finally, please enter the name of the **assigned technician** who will be handling your request in the "assigned technician" field. If you are not sure, please leave this field blank and our team will assign a technician to your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: This field is used to collect your full name as it appears in your identity documents.
* **Email**: This field requires a valid email address that the support team can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number for further assistance or to keep you updated on the status of your request.
* **Location**: This field helps the assigned technician know your exact location to efficiently resolve the issue.
* **Problem Description**: This field is used to gather a detailed description of your problem to ensure the technician understands your concern.
* **Service Type**: This dropdown menu offers different types of services that you may need assistance with. Please select the one that best describes your issue.
* **Priority**: This field allows you to specify the level of urgency for your request. Please select the priority level that best reflects the severity of your problem.
* **Assigned Technician**: This field is used to enter the name of the technician assigned to handle your case. If you are not sure, please leave this field blank and our team will assign a technician to your case.

Note: This form is not intended for emergency situations. Please call our emergency hotline for urgent issues.
