# CRM Setup Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CRM Setup Guide is a form designed to collect essential information from organizations and teams setting up a CRM system. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your organization's name, the name of your IT contact, and their contact email address.
2. Select the preferred features of your CRM system, such as contact management, workflow automation, reporting, and integrations.
3. Indicate whether historical data migration is required and select the desired go-live date for your production system.
4. If necessary, provide a brief description of external systems you'd like to integrate with your CRM.
5. Specify any security and compliance requirements, such as encryption or SSO.
6. Fill out all fields thoroughly and accurately, as this information will help us configure your CRM system properly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of your company or team.
* **IT contact name** (`it_contact`, required): Provide the name of your IT contact person.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address of your IT contact.
* **Preferred CRM features** (`preferred_crm_features`, required, multiple select): Choose the features you need from our list:
	+ Contact management
	+ Workflow automation
	+ Reporting
	+ Integrations
* **Is data migration required** (`data_migration_needed`, required, select one): If you need to import historical data into your CRM, select "True".
* **Integrations required** (`integrations_required`, optional, text): If you have external systems you'd like to integrate with your CRM, list them briefly.
* **Security and compliance requirements** (`security_requirements`, optional, text): Specify any security and compliance requirements, such as encryption or SSO.
* **Target go live date** (`go_live_date`, optional, date): Select the desired production date for your CRM system.
