# service_continuity_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the continuity of services within an organization, aiming to identify potential risks and threats to service disruptions. It provides a comprehensive overview of the service continuity, allowing stakeholders to evaluate and plan for mitigating these risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your organization's name, email address, and job title.
3. Answer the questions regarding potential service disruptions and the level of risk, impact, likelihood, and recovery times.
4. Indicate whether there is a recovery team and the recovery process in place.
5. Select the criticality and impact level of the service.
6. Confirm if business and technical owners are aware of the service.
7. Evaluate the business and technical impact of the service.
8. Confirm if a continuity plan is in place.
9. Describe the next steps to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Business Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your organization or work site.
* **Business Unit**: Enter the business unit you belong to.
* **Service Disruptions**: Indicate whether there are any service disruptions (Yes/No).
* **Risk Level**: Select the level of risk (Low/Medium/High).
* **Impact**: Select the impact level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Likelihood**: Select the likelihood level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Recovery Time**: Select the recovery time (Less than 1 hour/1-4 hours/More than 4 hours).
* **Recovery Team**: Indicate whether a recovery team is in place (Yes/No).
* **Recovery Process**: Describe the recovery process.
* **Criticality**: Select the criticality level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Impact Level**: Select the impact level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Business Owners**: Indicate whether business owners are aware of the service (Yes/No).
* **Technical Owners**: Indicate whether technical owners are aware of the service (Yes/No).
* **Business Impact**: Select the business impact level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Technical Impact**: Select the technical impact level (Low/Medium/High).
* **Continuity Plan**: Indicate whether a continuity plan is in place (False/True/In Progress).
* **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps to be taken by your organization.

## Tips
This form is used to provide a comprehensive overview of service continuity within an organization. It is essential to fill in the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the assessment is reliable and effective. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please refer to your organization's policies and procedures for guidance.
