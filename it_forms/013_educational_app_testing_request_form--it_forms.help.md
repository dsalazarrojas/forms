# Educational App Testing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to test an educational app. The form will help the developers to understand the users' needs and improve the app accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about the tester, including the application, testing goals, participant qualification, and testing duration.
2. Choose how often you want to test the app (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. If you have any other testing goals, you can add them in the "Other Testing Goals" field.
5. Finally, fill in the participant's name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tester Application**: Please describe your application for testing the educational app. This can be a short paragraph or a few sentences.
* **Testing Goals**: Describe the goals you want to achieve by testing the app. This can be to test the app's functionality, to see how easy it is to use, or any other relevant purpose.
* **Participant Qualification**: Check 'Yes' if you are qualified to test the app. This is likely to ensure that the person testing the app has the necessary qualifications or expertise to do so.
* **Testing Pilot**: Select a date for the testing pilot. This will likely be the date when the testing will start.
* **Testing Duration**: Enter the duration of the testing period. This can be a number of days or hours.
* **Testing Frequency**: Choose how often you want to test the app. You can select Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Tester Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number so that the developers can contact you for further information.
* **Other Testing Goals**: If you have any other goals that are not mentioned above, you can add them here.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be participating in the testing.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Testing Pilot Comment**: Add any comments about the testing pilot.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the developers have a good understanding of your needs and goals.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the developers.
* Make sure to check the 'Participant Qualification' field and select 'Yes' if you are qualified to test the app.
