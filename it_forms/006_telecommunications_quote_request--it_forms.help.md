# Telecommunications Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request quotes from telecommunications service providers for specific services. Please fill out the form with your company and contact information, details about the service locations, and any other required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Quote Request" page.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* Service Locations: List the specific locations where the service is needed, separated by commas.
	* Service Provider: Choose the provider of the service.
	* Quote Type: Select the type of quote you are requesting.
	* Quote Amount: Enter the amount of the quote.
	* Quote Currency: Select the currency of the quote (USD, CAD, or Other).
3. Fill out any other fields as necessary:
	* Company: Enter your company name.
	* Contact: Enter your contact person's name and title.
	* Address Line 1-2: Enter your company's address.
	* City: Enter the city where your company is located.
	* State: Enter the state where your company is located.
	* Zip: Enter the zip code of your company's location.
	* Email: Enter your company's email address.
	* Phone: Enter your company's phone number.
	* Quote Date: Enter the date of the quote request.
	* Quote Time: Enter the time of the quote request.
	* Service Description: Enter a brief description of the service needed.
4. Review and submit the form to request a quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (Text): Enter your company name.
* **Contact** (Text): Enter your contact person's name and title.
* **Address Line 1** (Text): Enter your company's street address.
* **Address Line 2** (Text): Enter your company's additional address details.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State** (Text): Enter the state where your company is located.
* **Zip** (Text): Enter your company's zip code.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Service Locations** (Select Multiple): List the locations where the service is needed, separated by commas.
* **Service Location Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the service locations.
* **Service Provider** (Select One): Choose the service provider.
* **Quote Date** (Date): Enter the date of the quote request.
* **Quote Time** (Time): Enter the time of the quote request.
* **Quote Type** (Select One): Select the type of quote you are requesting.
* **Quote Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the quote.
* **Quote Currency** (Select One): Select the currency of the quote (USD, CAD, or Other).
* **Service Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the service needed.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Note): This is a read-only field displaying the terms and conditions of the quote request.
