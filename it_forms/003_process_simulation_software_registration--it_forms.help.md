# process_simulation_software_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register process simulation software for management and monitoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: **Vendor Name**, **Software Title**, **License Request**, and **Consent**.
2. Provide optional fields with relevant information for the software.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding to the next step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name (1, Vendor Name, required)**: Enter the name of the vendor who developed the software.
* **Software Title (2, Software Title, required)**: Enter a short title for the software.
* **License Request (3, License Request, required)**: Describe your license request for the software.
* **Intended Use (4, Intended Use, required)**: Describe how you plan to use the software.
* **Contact Person (5, Contact Person, required)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the software.
* **Email (6, Email, required)**: Enter the contact email for the software.
* **Contact Phone (7, Contact Phone, required)**: Enter the contact phone number for the software.
* **Company (8, Company, required)**: Enter the name of the company that uses the software.
* **Consent (9, Consent, required)**: Confirm that you have read and understood the terms of use and license agreement for the software.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid errors.
* Be as detailed as possible with the optional fields to help us better understand your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns during the registration process, contact us at [support contact email].
