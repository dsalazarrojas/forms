# Digital Wallet Issue Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report digital wallet-related issues. Please use this form to provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing, along with any supporting documentation such as screenshots.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2.  Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field. This will be used as a contact point for further communication about your issue.
3.  If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
4.  In the "Issue Details" field, describe the issue you are experiencing with your digital wallet in as much detail as possible.
5.  Please indicate your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field. You can select "Phone", "Email", or "Chat".
6.  If applicable, provide your device information in the "Device Info" field.
7.  Attach any screenshots that may help illustrate the issue in the "Screenshots" field.
8.  In the "Issue Description" field, provide a detailed description of the issue.
9.  Select the category that best describes the issue you are experiencing in the "Category" field. You can choose from "Card Issue", "Wallet Issue", or "Other".
10. Choose the sub-category that best describes the issue in the "Sub Category" field. You can select from the available options.
11.  Indicate the priority of the issue in the "Issue Priority" field. You can choose from "High", "Medium", or "Low".
12.  Enter the date and time the issue occurred in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields if applicable.
13.  Indicate the current status of the issue in the "Issue Status" field. You can choose from "Open", "Closed", or "In Progress".
14.  If the issue has been previously assigned to a support agent, select the name in the "Assigned" field.
15.  If you have any comments about the issue, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
16.  Add any relevant tags to the "Tags" field.
17.  If you have attached any files or images to the issue, add a brief description of them in the "Attachments" field.
18.  In the "Comments Status" field, indicate whether comments are currently open or closed.
19.  Finally, provide the resolution to the issue, if known, in the "Resolution" field.
20.  Indicate whether the issue was reopened or not in the "Reopened Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
*   **Issue Details**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with your digital wallet in as much detail as possible.
*   **Preferred Contact Method**: Indicate your preferred method of contact. You can select "Phone", "Email", or "Chat".
*   **Device Info**: If applicable, provide your device information.
*   **Screenshots**: Attach any relevant screenshots.
*   **Issue Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue.
*   **Category**: Select the category that best describes the issue. You can choose from "Card Issue", "Wallet Issue", or "Other".
*   **Sub Category**: Choose the sub-category that best describes the issue.
*   **Issue Priority**: Indicate the priority of the issue. You can choose from "High", "Medium", or "Low".
*   **Issue Date**: Enter the date the issue occurred, if applicable.
*   **Issue Time**: Enter the time the issue occurred, if applicable.
*   **Issue Status**: Indicate the current status of the issue. You can choose from "Open", "Closed", or "In Progress".
*   **Assigned**: If the issue has been previously assigned to a support agent, select the name.
*   **Comments**: Add any comments about the issue.
*   **Tags**: Add any relevant tags to the issue.
*   **Attachments**: Add a brief description of any attachments you have made to the issue.
*   **Comments Status**: Indicate whether comments are currently open or closed.
*   **Resolution**: Provide the resolution to the issue, if known.
*   **Reopened Status**: Indicate whether the issue was reopened or not.
