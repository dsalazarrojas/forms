# employee_undertaking_and_indemnity_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to provide their undertakings and indemnity, which includes acknowledging company policies, risk management, and other company details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your employee details, including your name, department, and employee ID.
2. Provide your contact details, such as your email and phone number.
3. Select your awareness of the company policy, indicating whether you are aware or not.
4. Enter the date for employee signature and date.
5. Add your employee signature.
6. Add the review date and reviewed by.
7. Complete the other fields as required and/or necessary for your role or position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Provide your basic employee information, such as name and department.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number.
* **Risk Management** (`risk_management`, text, optional): Explain the risk management related to your role or position.
* **Employee Agreement** (`employee_agreement`, text, optional): Confirm your understanding of the company policy.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Enter the company's name, address, and other basic information.
* **Indemnity Clauses** (`indemnity_clauses`, text, optional): Explain any indemnity clauses that apply to your role or position.
* **Employee Awareness** (`employee_awareness`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your understanding of the company policy, whether you are aware or not.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Date** (`employee_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for your employee signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date for the manager's signature.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the manager's name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, optional): Explain any risk assessment related to your role or position.
* **Risk Management Agreement** (`risk_management_agreement`, text, optional): Confirm your understanding of the risk management.
* **Company Signature** (`company_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date for the company signature.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the company's name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the company's address.
* **Contact Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter the company's contact phone number.
* **Contact E-mail** (`company_email`, email, optional): Enter the company's email address.
* **Risk Management Contact** (`risk_management_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact person for risk management.
* **Risk Management E-mail** (`risk_management_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address for risk management contact.
* **Risk Management Phone** (`risk_management_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for risk management contact.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for review.
* **Reviewed By** (`review_by`, text, optional): Enter the person reviewed by.
