# Data Integration Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from organizations interested in the Data Integration Pilot program. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the organization's technical environments, pilot goals, and data sources, which will be used to evaluate their eligibility and suitability for the pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
	* Read each question carefully and provide accurate information about your organization.
	* Fill in the required fields, as they are marked with an asterisk (\*). Note that all fields are optional.
	* You can submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your organization. Please enter a short statement about your organization, including its name, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Technical Environments** (`technical_environments`, text, optional): In this field, describe the technical environments you are currently using or plan to use for the data integration pilot. This can include information about your hardware, software, and infrastructure.
* **Pilot Goals** (`pilot_goals`, text, optional): Here, describe the goals and objectives of your pilot project. What specific data integration challenges are you trying to address, and what outcomes do you hope to achieve?
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, text, optional): List the data sources you plan to use or are currently using for your pilot project. This can include databases, APIs, or other data repositories.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* We will review your application carefully and will be in touch with you soon to discuss further details.
