# retail_personalization_api_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies interested in integrating our retail personalization API into their systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your company's information.
2. Provide accurate and complete information about your company.
3. Select all applicable API access levels.
4. Review and agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **API Key** (`api_key`, text, optional): Enter your API key.
* **API Type** (`api_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of API (API Key Type, Client, or Server).
* **Use Case** (`use_case`, note, optional): Briefly describe how you plan to use the API.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of company (SaaS, Retail Tech, or Other).
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, select_one, optional): Select the contact person's job title (API Manager, API Developer, or Other).
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's department.
* **Contact Job Function** (`contact_job_function`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's job function.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Company City** (`company_city`, text, optional): Enter your company's city.
* **Company State** (`company_state`, select_one, optional): Select the state where your company operates (CA, IL, NY, or TX).
* **Company Country** (`company_country`, select_one, optional): Select the country where your company operates (USA, Mexico, Canada, or Other).
* **Company Zip** (`company_zip`, text, optional): Enter your company's zip code.
* **API Access** (`api_access`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable API access levels.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, text, optional): Enter your company's timezone.
* **IP Address** (`ip_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's IP address.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you agree to the terms of service (Yes or No).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you agree to the terms of service and usage of the API (Yes or No).
