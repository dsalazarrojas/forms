# kid_castle_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or modify a kid's profile in the kid's castle system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have all the required information before filling out the form.
2. Fill in the User ID, if it is applicable.
3. Enter the kid's name in the Name field.
4. Enter the kid's age in the Age field.
5. Enter the kid's email in the Email field.
6. Enter the kid's phone number in the Phone field.
7. Enter the kid's Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID**: A unique identifier for the user. If you don't have one, you can skip this field.
* **Name**: Enter the kid's name as it is known to them, e.g., John Doe.
* **Age**: Enter the kid's age in numeric format, e.g., 10.
* **Email**: Enter the kid's email address in a valid format, e.g., johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone**: Enter the kid's phone number in E.164 format, e.g., +1 123 456 7890.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the kid's Date of Birth in the yyyy-mm-dd format, e.g., 1992-05-25.

## Tips
* Use a valid email format for the email field.
* Use the correct Date of Birth format (yyyy-mm-dd).
