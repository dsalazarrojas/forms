# Fiber Installation Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fiber Installation Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate the skills and experiences of individuals in fiber installation, including technicians and operators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability based on your real-world experience and knowledge.
2. Choose the options that accurately reflect your skills and experiences.
3. Fill out the required fields, and if a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
4. Please answer each question honestly, and if you are unsure of an answer, choose the option that best reflects your current level of confidence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Experience** (Select multiple): This field asks about your experience and skills in fiber optic installation, splicing, testing, and safety procedures. Choose all options that apply to your real-world experience.
* **Safety Procedure** (Select one): This field asks about your level of certification in safety procedures. Choose the option that best reflects your current level of certification.
* **Fiber Optical Testing** (Number): This field asks for your proficiency in fiber optical testing. Enter the number that reflects your level of expertise.
* **Troubleshooting Confidence** (Select one): This field asks about your confidence in your ability to troubleshoot fiber optic systems. Choose the option that best reflects your current level of confidence.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text): In case of an emergency, this field asks for the name and phone number of the person you would contact.
* **Certification** (Select multiple): This field asks about your level of certification in fiber optic installation and safety procedures. Choose all options that apply to your real-world experience and knowledge.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): This field asks for the phone number of the person you would contact in case of an emergency.
* **Certification Date** (Date): If you have a certification, enter the date it was obtained.
* **Safety Procedures** (Select multiple): This field asks about your knowledge of safety procedures. Choose all options that apply to your real-world experience and knowledge.
* **Fiber Installation** (Number): This field asks for your proficiency in fiber installation. Enter the number that reflects your level of expertise.
* **Safety Procedure 2** (Select one): This field asks about your level of certification in safety procedures. Choose the option that best reflects your current level of certification.
* **Fiber Optical Testing 2** (Number): This field asks for your proficiency in fiber optical testing. Enter the number that reflects your level of expertise.
* **Certification Date 2** (Date): If you have a certification, enter the date it was obtained.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you are unsure of an answer, choose the option that best reflects your current level of confidence or expertise.
* This form is designed to be completed by individuals with fiber installation experience. If you are not sure about a particular field, choose the option that best reflects your current level of knowledge or experience.
