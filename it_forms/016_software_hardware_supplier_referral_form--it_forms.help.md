# software_hardware_supplier_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate referrals to software and hardware suppliers for various purposes, such as requesting a demo, requesting a quote, or submitting a purchase request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the referral in the **Software Hardware Supplier Referral Form** field.
2. Select the category of the referral from the **Category** dropdown menu.
3. Enter a brief description of the referral in the **Description** field.
4. Select the tool assigned to handle the referral from the **Assigned Tool** dropdown menu.
5. Select relevant referral information for the referral, such as email and phone number, from the **Referral Information 0-4** fields.
6. Enter the referral date and time in the **Date** and **Time** fields, respectively.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the referral in the **Note** field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the **Email** field.
9. Enter the customer's phone number in the **Phone** field.
10. Enter a referral ID for the supplier in the **Referral ID** field.
11. Indicate the source of the referral in the **Referral Source** field.
12. Select the status of the referral from the **Referral Status** dropdown menu.
13. Enter any additional information about the referral source in the **Referral Source Info** field.
14. Enter any notes or comments about the referral in the **Notes** field.
15. Indicate whether the referral has been agreed upon in the **Agreed** field.
16. If agreed, enter the date and time of agreement in the **Agreed Date** and **Agreed Time** fields, respectively.
17. Select the type of referral source from the **Referral Source Type** dropdown menu.
18. Select relevant referral information for the referral, such as email and phone number, from the **Referral Information 5-6** fields.
