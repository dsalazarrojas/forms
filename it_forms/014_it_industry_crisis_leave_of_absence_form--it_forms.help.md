# it-industry-crisis-leave-of-absence-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it-industry-crisis-leave-of-absence-form is used to submit a formal leave of absence request for work-related crises, such as technical difficulties, server errors, or other emergencies. This form helps gather essential details needed for review and approval by management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details as completely as possible.
2. Ensure all fields with an asterisk (\*) are completed.
3. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Your full name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your official company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your direct dialable phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The date you would like to begin your leave of absence.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The date you would like to end your leave of absence.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): A brief explanation of the reason for your leave of absence.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options provided:
	+ Option 1: Your immediate manager has already approved this leave.
	+ Option 2: Your manager has reviewed and approved this leave.
	+ Option 3: This leave requires additional review by higher management.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Your unique employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three departments involved in the crisis:
	+ Department 1
	+ Department 2
	+ Department 3

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure a smooth review process.
* If your leave of absence is urgent, please contact your manager directly to expedite the review process.
* If you have any issues with the form or the submission process, please reach out to HR for assistance.
