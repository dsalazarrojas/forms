**Smartwatch Usage Acknowledgement - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge the usage of a smartwatch. The goal of this form is to gather information about the usage status of a smartwatch, which is a critical device that should be kept up-to-date to ensure its proper maintenance and support. Filling out this form will help us understand who is using the smartwatch, for how long, and what issues may arise with its usage. This information is essential to prevent non-renewals and lost assets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct status of the smartwatch usage from the dropdown menu at page 1.
2. Choose the departments to which the smartwatch is assigned at page 3.
3. Enter the date the smartwatch was issued at page 4.
4. Enter the date the smartwatch is expected to be returned at page 5.
5. Enter the unique identifier of the smartwatch at page 6.
6. Enter the user ID of the person to whom the smartwatch is assigned at page 7.
7. Select whether the smartwatch is assigned to a user at page 8.
8. Choose whether the responsible department is aware of the smartwatch usage at page 9 (this is an optional field).
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about the smartwatch usage at page 10 (this is an optional field).
10. Enter the email address of the person who is assigned the smartwatch at page 11 (this is an optional field).
11. Enter the phone number of the person who is assigned the smartwatch at page 12 (this is an optional field).
12. Select whether comments are provided about the smartwatch usage at page 13 (this is an optional field).
13. Choose the status of the smartwatch at page 14.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **smartwatch usage** (page 1, select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the smartwatch is currently in use or not.
- **department** (page 3, select_multiple): Choose the departments that the smartwatch is assigned to. This will help us understand which teams or departments are using the smartwatch.
- **assigned to** (page 3, select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the smartwatch is assigned to a user.
- **issue date** (page 4, date): Enter the date the smartwatch was issued.
- **return date** (page 5, date): Enter the date the smartwatch is expected to be returned.
- **device id** (page 6, number): Enter the unique identifier of the smartwatch.
- **user id** (page 7, number): Enter the ID of the user to whom the smartwatch is assigned.
- **assigned to user** (page 8, select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the smartwatch is assigned to a user.
- **responsible department** (page 9, select_multiple): Choose whether the responsible department is aware of the smartwatch usage (this field is optional).
- **notes** (page 10, note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the smartwatch usage (this field is optional).
- **email** (page 11, email): Enter the email address of the person who is assigned the smartwatch (this field is optional).
- **phone** (page 12, text): Enter the phone number of the person who is assigned the smartwatch (this field is optional).
- **comments** (page 13, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether comments are provided about the smartwatch usage (this field is optional).
- **device status** (page 14, select_one): Choose the status of the smartwatch, either "Active" or "Inactive".
