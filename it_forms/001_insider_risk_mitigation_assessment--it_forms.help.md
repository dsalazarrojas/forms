# Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment form helps employees identify and document potential insider risks within the organization. It's designed to gather information on incidents, threats, and controls that can help mitigate these risks.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully before entering information.
- Enter your responses in the text fields. If you need to select an option, choose the relevant option from the dropdown list.
- You may only complete each page once.
- Ensure that the form fields that are marked as required are completed before proceeding.
- Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. page_1** (`text`, not required): A page title to help distinguish the form sections.
- **2. user_info** (`text`, not required): Enter a brief description of yourself and your role in the organization.
- **3. threat_controls** (`text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the threat controls implemented in your department or team.
- **4. threat_controls_control_1** (`select_one`, not required): Select one or more controls that apply to your department or team (Security, IT, HR, or Compliance).
- **5. threat_controls_control_2** (`select_one`, not required): Select one or more controls that apply to your department or team (Physical, Data, or Cyber).
- **6. incidents** (`select_multiple`, not required): Indicate if any incidents have occurred (Yes or No).
- **7. incident_details** (`text`, not required): If incidents have occurred, enter a brief description of each incident.
- **8. incident_1** (`select_one`, not required): Indicate if the first incident occurred (Yes or No).
- **9. incident_2** (`select_one`, not required): Indicate if the second incident occurred (Yes or No).
- **10. improvement_ideas** (`text`, not required): Enter any suggestions for improving insider risk mitigation within your department or team.
- **11. page_2** (`text`, not required): A page title to help distinguish the form sections.
- **12. incident_3** (`date`, not required): Enter the date of the first incident, if applicable.
- **13. date_incident_occurred** (`date`, not required): Enter the date of the latest incident, if applicable.
