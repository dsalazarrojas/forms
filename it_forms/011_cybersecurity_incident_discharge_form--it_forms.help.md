# Cybersecurity Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and track cybersecurity incidents that have occurred within an organization. It collects information about the incident date, time, type, location, and description, as well as impact category, status, disposition, and related reports. The form is likely used by IT or cybersecurity teams for reporting and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
2. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Security Breach, Denial of Service, etc.).
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
4. Describe the incident in detail.
5. Categorize the impact of the incident (e.g., Financial, Security, Personal, etc.).
6. Select the status of the incident (e.g., Active, Inactive, Resolved, etc.).
7. Choose the disposition of the incident (e.g., Accepted, Declined, Referred, etc.).
8. Enter any comments or notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Security Breach, Denial of Service, etc.). This field helps to categorize the incident and track its type.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in detail, including what happened and how it was handled.
* **Impact Category**: Categorize the impact of the incident (e.g., Financial, Security, Personal, etc.). This helps to understand the severity and potential consequences of the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Select the current status of the incident (e.g., Active, Inactive, Resolved, etc.). This field indicates whether the incident is still ongoing or has been resolved.
* **Incident Disposition**: Choose the disposition of the incident (e.g., Accepted, Declined, Referred, etc.). This field indicates how the incident was handled or resolved.
* **Incident Report**: Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
* **Security Incident Report**: Enter any relevant details about the incident report, such as the report number or date.
* **User 1**, **User 2**, **User 3**, **User 4**, **User 5**, **User 6**, **User 7**, **User 8**: Enter the names and relevant details of individuals involved in the incident.
* **Incident Report Issued Date**: Enter the date the incident report was issued.
* **Incident Report Issued By**: Enter the name of the person or team that issued the incident report.
* **Incident Report Issued Time**: Enter the time the incident report was issued.
* **Incident Closed Date**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
* **Incident Closed By**: Enter the name of the person or team that closed the incident.
* **Incident Closed Time**: Enter the time the incident was closed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have permission to complete this form and that the information collected aligns with your organization's policies and procedures.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and its impact.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
