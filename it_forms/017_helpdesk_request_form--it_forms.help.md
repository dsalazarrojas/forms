# Helpdesk Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Helpdesk Request Form is used to submit requests or issues to the helpdesk team for assistance. It's a simple form that allows users to report problems, provide details, and assign a priority level to their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the issue or problem you're experiencing in the "Description" field.
2. Choose the type of request you're making (e.g. bug, feature, or feedback) from the "Request Type" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field so that we can contact you for further discussion.
4. Provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Number" field in case we need to call you.
5. If you have any attachments or files related to your request, you can upload them in the "Attachments" field.
6. If you'd like to assign the request to a specific team member, you can choose from the "Assigned To" field.
7. Set the priority of your request using the "Priority" field (low, medium, high, or urgent).
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your request to the helpdesk team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Need Help with Something**
This field is a text area where you can type in a brief description of the issue or problem you're experiencing.

### 2. **Request Type**
Choose the type of request you're making from the dropdown menu:

* **Bug**: Report a bug or error in our product or service.
* **Feature**: Suggest a new feature or enhancement to our product or service.
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on our product or service.

### 3. **Description**
This field is a text area where you can provide more details about the issue or problem you're experiencing.

### 4. **Email**
Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussion or clarification on your request.

### 5. **Contact Number**
Provide your phone number in case we need to call you for further discussion or clarification on your request.

### 6. **Date Submitted**
This field is automatically filled in with the current date and time when you submit the form.

### 7. **Assigned To**
Choose a team member from the dropdown menu to assign the request to. If you choose "Other", you can enter a custom name in the next field.

### 8. **Status**
Select the status of your request from the dropdown menu:

* **Open**: Your request has not been reviewed or actioned yet.
* **In Progress**: Our team is working on your request.
* **Closed**: Your request has been resolved or completed.
* **Cancelled**: Your request has been cancelled or postponed.

### 9. **Notes**
This field is a text area where you can add any additional notes or comments about your request.

### 10. **Attachments**
If you have any files or attachments related to your request, you can upload them here.

### 11. **Priority**
Choose the priority of your request from the dropdown menu:

* **Low**: This request is not time-sensitive.
* **Medium**: This request is moderately important and should be reviewed within a few days.
* **High**: This request is time-sensitive and should be reviewed within a short period of time.
* **Urgent**: This request is extremely time-sensitive and requires immediate attention.
