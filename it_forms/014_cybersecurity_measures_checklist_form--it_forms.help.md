# cybersecurity_measures_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on the cybersecurity measures in place within an organization. This form is designed to be a quick and easy way to assess an organization's cybersecurity posture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as truthfully and accurately as possible.
2. Select all applicable options for each field.
3. Enter your answers in the text fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your organization's cybersecurity measures. You can enter any relevant details that are not covered by the other fields.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the risk levels that apply to your organization. This could be "low", "medium", or "high" depending on the level of risk your organization faces.
* **Data Classification** (`data_classification`, select_one, optional): Choose the classification of data that applies to your organization. This could be "public", "private", or "confidential".
* **Security Standards** (`security_standards`, text, optional): Enter any security standards or regulations that your organization adheres to.
* **Data Protection** (`data_protection`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the data protection measures that apply to your organization. This could include "firewall", "antivirus", or "backup".
* **Incident Response** (`incident_response`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the incident response procedures that apply to your organization. This could be "True" or "False" depending on whether you have incident response procedures in place.
* **Security Practices** (`security_practices`, text, optional): Enter any security practices or protocols that your organization follows.
* **Compliance Reporting** (`compliance_reporting`, text, optional): Enter any information about compliance reporting requirements or procedures.

## Tips

* Be as thorough as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure of an option, select "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" to indicate that it's not relevant to your organization.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for a comprehensive risk assessment or audit.
