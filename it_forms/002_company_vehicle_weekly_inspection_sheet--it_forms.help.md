# Company Vehicle Weekly Inspection Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the condition of company vehicles after a weekly inspection. The purpose of this form is to ensure that vehicles are being properly maintained and are safe for operation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Date of inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection. This will help track and record the vehicle's maintenance history.
2. **Inspector's name and employee ID**: Enter your name and employee ID number. This ensures that the inspection is properly credited and tracked.
3. **Vehicle ID Number**: Enter the company vehicle identification number.
4. **Year make and model**: Enter the year make and model of the vehicle.
5. **License Plate Number**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
6. **Odometer reading**: Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
7. **Exterior Condition**: Select the exterior condition of the vehicle from the options provided.
	* **Excellent**: The vehicle is in excellent condition with no visible damage.
	* **Good**: The vehicle is in good condition with minor scratches or dents.
	* **Fair**: The vehicle has some notable damage or wear.
	* **Poor**: The vehicle has significant damage or wear.
	* **Needs Repair**: The vehicle requires immediate attention.
8. **Exterior Damage Notes**: Enter any notable exterior damage or issues.
9. **Tire Condition**: Select the condition of the vehicle's tires from the options provided.
	* **Good - adequate tread**: The tires are in good condition with adequate tread.
	* **Fair - some wear**: The tires are fair condition with some wear.
	* **Poor - needs replacement**: The tires need immediate replacement.
	* **Critical - immediate replacement needed**: The tires are critical and need immediate replacement.
10. **Tire Pressure Checked**: Select if the tire pressure was checked and if it's correct.
	* **Yes - all correct**: The tire pressure is correct.
	* **No - adjustment needed**: The tire pressure is not correct and needs adjustment.
	* **Not checked**: The tire pressure was not checked.
11. **Headlights, brake lights, and turn signals**: Select if all lights are functioning correctly.
	* **Yes - all working**: All lights are functioning correctly.
	* **No - some not working**: Some lights are not functioning correctly.
	* **Not checked**: The lights were not checked.
12. **Non-functional lights description**: Enter any non-functional lights.
13. **Fluid Levels Status**: Select the fluid levels status from the options provided.
	* **All levels adequate**: The fluid levels are adequate.
	* **Some levels low**: Some fluid levels are low.
	* **Needs immediate attention**: The fluid levels need immediate attention.
	* **Not checked**: The fluid levels were not checked.
14. **Fluid Notes**: Enter which fluids need attention.
15. **Interior Cleanliness**: Select the interior cleanliness of the vehicle from the options provided.
	* **Clean**: The interior is clean.
	* **Acceptable**: The interior is acceptable with some minor issues.
	* **Needs cleaning**: The interior needs cleaning.
	* **Very dirty**: The interior is very dirty.
16. **Brakes Condition**: Select the condition of the vehicle's brakes from the options provided.
	* **Good - responsive**: The brakes are in good condition with normal response.
	* **Fair - some concern**: The brakes have some concern.
	* **Poor - needs inspection**: The brakes need inspection.
	* **Not tested**: The brakes were not tested.
17. **Steering Condition**: Select the condition of the vehicle's steering from the options provided.
	* **Good - normal operation**: The steering is in good condition with normal operation.
	* **Fair - slight issues**: The steering has slight issues.
	* **Poor - needs attention**: The steering needs attention.
	* **Not tested**: The steering was not tested.
18. **Warning lights**: Select if there are any warning indicators on the dashboard.
	* **None**: No warning indicators.
	* **Check engine**: Engine-related warning.
	* **Oil pressure**: Oil pressure warning.
	* **Battery**: Battery-related warning.
	* **Other**: Other warning indicators.
19. **Warning Light Details**: Enter any observed warning lights.
20. **Windshield and wipers**: Select the condition of the vehicle's windshield and wipers from the options provided.
	* **Good - no issues**: The windshield and wipers are in good condition.
	* **Fair - minor chips**: The windshield and wipers have minor chips or issues.
	* **Poor - cracks or damage**: The windshield and wipers have cracks or damage.
	* **Wipers need replacement**: The wipers need replacement.
21. **Fuel Level**: Select the current fuel level from the options provided.
	* **Full**: The fuel tank is full.
	* **3/4**: The fuel tank is 3/4 full.
	* **1/2**: The fuel tank is half full.
	* **1/4**: The fuel tank is 1/4 full.
	* **Empty - needs fuel**: The fuel tank is empty and needs fuel.
22. **Safety Equipment**: Select if all safety equipment is present and accessible.
	* **All present and accessible**: All safety equipment is present and accessible.
	* **Some missing**: Some safety equipment is missing.
	* **None present**: No safety equipment is present.
	* **Not checked**: The safety equipment was not checked.
23. **Maintenance Required**: Select if any maintenance or repair is needed.
	* **No - vehicle is ready**: The vehicle is ready for use.
	* **Yes - routine maintenance**: The vehicle needs routine maintenance.
	* **Yes - urgent repair needed**: The vehicle needs urgent repair.
24. **Maintenance Details**: Enter the required maintenance or repairs.
25. **Vehicle Ready for Use**: Select if the vehicle is ready for use.
	* **Yes - approved for use**: The vehicle is approved for use.
	* **No - do not use**: The vehicle is not approved for use.
	* **Conditional - see notes**: The vehicle is conditionally approved for use and see notes for details.
26. **Inspector's Signature**: Enter your full name as signature.
27. **Additional Comments**: Enter any other observations or concerns.

## Tips

* Be thorough when inspecting the vehicle and recording your findings.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the inspection, consult your supervisor or maintenance manager.
