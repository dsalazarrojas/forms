# seo_business_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for SEO business reports. Business owners or administrators can use it to assess the relevance of keywords to their business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your business in the **business_name** field.
2. Enter the URL of your business in the **business_url** field.
3. Answer each of the "keyword relevance" questions by selecting either "Yes" or "No" for each keyword.
4. Once you have answered all the questions, click the **Submit** button to save your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This field is a note that serves as a title for the form.
* **business_name**: The name of your business.
* **business_url**: The URL of your business.
* **target_keyword**: This field is not currently used, but you can add your target keyword here. 
* **keyword_density**: This field is not currently used.
* **keyword_relevant**, **keyword_relevant_2**, ..., **keyword_relevant_19**: Each of these fields asks if a specific keyword is relevant to your business. Select "Yes" if the keyword is relevant, and "No" if it is not.
* **Submit**: Click this button to save your report after completing all the fields.
