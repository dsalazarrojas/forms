# IT Administrator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about IT administrators, including their contact details, departmental affiliation, and reporting structures within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field (optional).
4. Select your role within the organization from the "Role" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field (optional).
7. Enter the name of your reporting manager in the "Reporting Manager" field (optional).
8. Select the name of your team lead from the "Team Lead" dropdown menu (optional).
9. Enter the name of your manager in the "Manager" field (optional).
10. Enter the name of your reporting team lead in the "Reporting Team Lead" field (optional).
11. Enter the name of your supervisor in the "Supervisor" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name to help the system recognize you as an IT administrator.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to complete your full name.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address to ensure that you can be contacted regarding any issues or requests.
* **Role**: Choose your role within the organization, e.g., "Administrator," "Technician," "Manager," or "Other."
* **Department**: Enter your department to help the system understand your organizational structure.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title to provide more details about your position within the organization (optional).
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of your reporting manager (optional).
* **Team Lead**: Choose the name of your team lead from the list of options (optional).
* **Manager**: Enter the name of your manager (optional).
* **Reporting Team Lead**: Enter the name of your reporting team lead (optional).
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name of your supervisor (optional).

## Tips
* Double-check your entered information for accuracy.
* Ensure that you have chosen the correct role and department to help the system process your request correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
