# Customer Support Software Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect specific information from customers regarding their software requirements. It helps our customer support team understand and address the needs of our customers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of software required from the options provided in the 'software_type' field.
2. Provide a numeric value for the 'business_impact' field, which represents the impact of the software on your business.
3. Enter a brief description of your software requirements in the 'description' field.
4. Enter a numeric value for the 'customer_impact' field, which represents the expected impact of the software on your customers.
5. Enter a numeric value for the 'budget' field, which represents your budget for the software implementation.
6. Enter the name and contact details of the person who will be our primary contact person in the 'contact_person' field.
7. Enter the name of your company in the 'company' field.
8. If you have any additional information that you think is relevant to your software requirements, enter it in the 'additional_info' field. (This field is optional.)
9. Upload any relevant attachment or document related to your software requirements in the 'attachment' field. (This field is optional.)
10. Enter the email address of the person who will be our contact person in the 'contact_email' field.
11. Enter the contact phone number of the person who will be our contact person in the 'contact_phone' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Software Type** (`software_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of software you require from the options provided.
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, number, required): Enter a numeric value that represents the expected impact of the software on your business.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your software requirements.
* **Customer Impact** (`customer_impact`, number, required): Enter a numeric value that represents the expected impact of the software on your customers.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required): Enter a numeric value that represents your budget for the software implementation.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact details of the person who will be our primary contact person.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information that is relevant to your software requirements, enter it here. (This field is optional.)
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): If you have any relevant document or attachment that supports your software requirements, upload it here. (This field is optional.)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person who will be our contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person who will be our contact person.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or doubts, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
