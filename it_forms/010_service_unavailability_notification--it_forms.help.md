# Service Unavailability Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Unavailability Notification form is designed to alert the system of potential or actual service unavailability. 
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the Service Description field with a brief description of the service affected.
- Select the Impacted Services from the provided options (Service A, Service B, Service C).
- Enter the Start and End Time of the service unavailability.
- Explain the Cause and Impact of the service unavailability in the Cause and Impact fields.
- Select the Affected Teams from the provided options (Team A, Team B, Team C).
- Choose the Update Frequency for communication (Immediately, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour).
- Add any additional comments or information in the Notes field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Description** (text, required): A brief description of the service affected.
  This field is mandatory to provide clear information about the service.
- **Impacted Services** (select multiple, optional): Select all services that are affected.
  Choose all services that are not available during the unavailability period.
- **Start Time** (time, optional): The start time of the service unavailability.
  Enter the start time of the service unavailability in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **End Time** (time, optional): The end time of the service unavailability.
  Enter the end time of the service unavailability in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Cause** (text, optional): A brief explanation of the reason for service unavailability.
  Describe the reason for the service unavailability in a concise manner.
- **Impact** (text, optional): A brief explanation of the impact of service unavailability.
  Explain the potential consequences of the service unavailability.
- **Affected Teams** (select multiple, optional): Select all teams affected by the service unavailability.
  Choose all teams that will be impacted by the service unavailability.
- **Update Frequency** (select one, optional): Choose the frequency of updates for the affected teams.
  Select the interval between updates for the affected teams.
- **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
  Use this field to provide any additional details or context about the service unavailability.
## Tips
- Fill out the form as soon as possible when a service becomes unavailable.
- Double-check the accuracy of the selected options and entered information.
- Use clear and concise language in the Cause and Impact fields.
