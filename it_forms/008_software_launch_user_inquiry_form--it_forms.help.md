# Software Launch User Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and interests from users regarding our software launch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your level of interest in our software.
2. Provide any feedback you have about our product in the notes section.
3. Select any areas of interest in our product.
4. Enter your contact information (email or phone number) if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
5. Enter your phone number for our records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Interest
* **User Interest** (Select One, required: false): Select "Yes, interested" if you are interested in our software, or "No, not interested" if you are not.

### Product Feedback
* **Product Feedback** (Note, required: false): This is a text area where you can provide any feedback you have about our product. Please keep your comments brief and to the point.

### Product Interest
* **Product Interest** (Select Multiple, required: false): Select all areas of interest from the list. You can choose more than one area of interest, if applicable.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (Email, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further discussion. This information will only be used for internal record-keeping and will not be shared with third parties.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (Text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone for further discussion. This information will only be used for internal record-keeping and will not be shared with third parties.
