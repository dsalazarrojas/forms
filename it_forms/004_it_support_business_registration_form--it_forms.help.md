# it_support_business_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for businesses to register their support requests with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact person's name and phone number (fields 2 and 3).
2. Enter the company's email address (field 4).
3. Provide the company name and address (fields 5-8).
4. Choose the support service required (field 11).
5. Enter a brief description of the support request (field 12).
6. Optionally, select the status of the form submission (field 16).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the form submission (fields 17 and 18).
8. If necessary, indicate who created and updated the form (fields 19 and 20).
9. Finally, confirm the form submission date and time (fields 13 and 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Person** (field 2, text): Enter the name of the person to contact.
- **Contact Phone** (field 3, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Email** (field 4, email): Enter the company's email address.
- **Company Name** (field 5, text): Enter the name of the company.
- **Company Address** (field 6, text): Enter the company's physical address.
- **Company City** (field 7, text): Enter the city where the company is located.
- **Company Province** (field 8, text): Enter the province or state where the company is located.
- **Company Postal Code** (field 9, text): Enter the postal code of the company's location.
- **Company Country** (field 10, text): Enter the country where the company is located.
- **Support Service** (field 11, text): Choose the support service required.
- **Company Description** (field 12, text): Provide a brief description of the support request.
- **Form Submission Date** (field 13, date): Automatically populated with the date of submission.
- **Form Submission Time** (field 14, time): Automatically populated with the time of submission.
- **Form Submission User ID** (field 15, select_one): Select the user who submitted the form (e.g. "chatjimmy" or "admin" or "manager").
- **Form Submission Status** (field 16, select_multiple): Select the status of the form submission (e.g. "open", "pending", "completed").
- **Form Submission Note** (field 17, text): Add any additional notes or comments about the form submission.
- **Form Submission Comments** (field 18, text): Add any additional comments or notes about the form submission.
- **Form Submission Created By** (field 19, text): Automatically populated with the user who created the form.
- **Form Submission Updated By** (field 20, text): Automatically populated with the user who updated the form.
- **Form Submission Created At** (field 21, date): Automatically populated with the date the form was created.
- **Form Submission Updated At** (field 22, date): Automatically populated with the date the form was last updated.
- **Form Submission Deleted By** (field 23, text): Automatically populated with the user who deleted the form.
- **Form Submission Deleted At** (field 24, date): Automatically populated with the date the form was deleted.
