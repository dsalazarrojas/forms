# Cybersecurity Analyst Leave Of Absence Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Analyst Leave Of Absence Assessment form is used to assess the need for a leave of absence for a Cybersecurity Analyst. It helps managers determine the appropriate amount of time needed for the leave and ensures that the necessary steps are taken to maintain the Analyst's work and security responsibilities during the absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to review your employee handbook or speak with HR to confirm that you have not already completed a leave of absence request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **full_name** (Text): Please enter your full name.
* **contact_email** (Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **start_date** (Date): Enter the start date of your leave of absence.
* **end_date** (Date): Enter the end date of your leave of absence.
* **reason_for_leave** (Text): Enter a brief description of your reason for taking the leave of absence.
* **manager_approval** (Select One):
	+ **Yes**: If you have received approval from your manager for the leave of absence.
	+ **No**: If you do not have approval from your manager or if you need to seek approval.
* **comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments or information about your leave of absence.
* **Form Footer**: This field is for additional comments or information from the manager reviewing the form.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
- Review your employee handbook or speak with HR to confirm that you have not already completed a leave of absence request.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to your manager or HR for assistance.
