# Smart Access Card Replacement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a replacement for a lost, stolen, or damaged Access Card. It is intended to be completed by users who need a new card to access certain facilities or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you are requesting a replacement card.
2. Provide your contact details, including your email and phone number.
3. Specify the reason for requesting a replacement card (optional).
4. Indicate whether you are a Student, Staff, or Visitor.
5. Enter your department or location (if applicable).
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the replacement card is being requested due to it being lost, stolen, or damaged.
7. Select the reason why the original card was not available (optional).
8. Enter the date and time of your request (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Card Request**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you are requesting a replacement card.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you to process your request.
* **Reason For Request**: This is an optional field where you can provide a brief explanation for requesting a replacement card.
* **Staff, Student or Visitor**: Select your status to ensure that we provide you with the correct replacement card.
* **Department or Location**: If you are affiliated with a department or location, please enter it here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you to process your request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to process your request.
* **Reason For Rejection**: Select the reason why the original card was not available (e.g. "Lost", "Stolen", "Damaged").
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date and time of your request (if applicable).
* **Time of Request**: Enter the time of your request (if applicable).
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have accurately filled out the form by selecting "Yes" and indicating that you have read and understand the terms of service.

Note: The fields with "optional" in the original YAML file have been marked as "optional" in the help guide to ensure that users understand what they need to fill out and what they can leave blank.
