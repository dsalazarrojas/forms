# IT Autonomy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Autonomy Assessment form is designed to assess the autonomy and support needs of employees, managers, and reviewers. It helps to identify areas where employees need improvement and provides a framework for developing and implementing a support plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources before completing the form.
2. Fill out each field carefully, providing accurate and relevant information.
3. Review the form to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (id: 1)**: Enter your username or ID to identify yourself in the assessment.
* **Manager Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Email (id: 3)**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Employee Name (id: 4)**: Enter your name as the employee being assessed.
* **Department (id: 5)**: Select the department where you work.
* **Job Function (id: 6)**: Select the specific job function you perform.
* **Support Needs (id: 7)**: Identify areas where you need technical support.
* **Current Level (id: 8)**: Assess your current level of expertise in this area.
* **Training Needs (id: 9)**: Identify the level of training needed to meet your job requirements.
* **Assessment Completed (id: 10)**: Record the date when this assessment is completed.
* **Assessment Comments (id: 11)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on your performance.
* **Support Plan (id: 12)**: Describe the support plan for your development and growth.
* **Follow-up Plan (id: 13)**: Outline the next steps and actions to be taken.
* **Next Steps (id: 14)**: Identify any next steps or actions to be taken.
* **Manager Signoff (id: 15)**: Confirm whether your manager has approved the assessment and plan.
* **Manager Comments (id: 16)**: Record any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Employee Signoff (id: 17)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and signed off on the assessment and plan.
* **Review Date (id: 18)**: Set a date for the review of your performance.
* **Reviewer Name (id: 19)**: Enter the name of the reviewer or evaluator.
* **Reviewer Title (id: 20)**: Enter the title of the reviewer or evaluator.
* **Reviewer Department (id: 21)**: Enter the department of the reviewer or evaluator.
* **Reviewer Email (id: 22)**: Enter the email address of the reviewer or evaluator.
* **Reviewer Signoff (id: 23)**: Confirm whether the reviewer has signed off on the assessment and plan.
* **Review Due Date (id: 24)**: Set a due date for the review.
* **Review Frequency (id: 25)**: Identify the review frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually).
* **Review Comments (id: 26)**: Provide any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
