# Call Forwarding Deactivation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the deactivation of call forwarding for an employee's phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as the requester.
2.  Select the department that you belong to.
3.  Enter your contact phone number so that we can reach you about this request.
4.  Enter the phone extension associated with your call forwarding, if applicable.
5.  Provide the number to which calls are currently forwarded.
6.  Choose a date for when you want call forwarding to be deactivated.
7.  Briefly describe the reason for cancelling call forwarding.
8.  Enter the email of your manager for approval or confirmation.
9.  If necessary, enter any additional notes or instructions for IT support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester Full Name** (`requester_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the requester, to help us identify you and confirm your request.
    
*   **Employee ID or Staff Number** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Provide your internal employee identifier, if applicable, to help us confirm your identity.
    
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to, as this will help us understand the context of your request.
    
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the best number to reach you about this request.
    
*   **Extension Number** (`extension_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone extension, if you have one, to help us associate with your call forwarding.
    
*   **Current Call Forwarding Target Number** (`current_forwarding_target`, text, required): Enter the number to which calls are currently forwarded.
    
*   **Desired Deactivation Date** (`desired_deactivation_date`, date, required): Choose the date when you want call forwarding to be deactivated.
    
*   **Reason for Deactivation** (`reason_for_deactivation`, text, optional): Briefly describe why you are cancelling call forwarding.
    
*   **Manager Approval Email** (`manager_approval_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email for approval or confirmation of this request.
    
*   **Additional Notes or Instructions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or instructions for IT support, if necessary.
