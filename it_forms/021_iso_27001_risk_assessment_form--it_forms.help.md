# ISO 27001 Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to identify and document potential security risks related to the ISO 27001 standard in an IT context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the General Information section to provide context about the risk assessment.
2. Answer the Risk Assessment Questions section to provide details about the identified risks.
3. Document your findings in the Risk Assessment Findings section.
4. Identify and document corrective actions that need to be taken in the Corrective Actions section.
5. Provide a summary of your risk assessment in the Risk Assessment Summary section.
6. Identify and document any actions taken by the Compliance Team in the Compliance Team section.
