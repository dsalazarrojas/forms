# Restaurant Pos Installation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your restaurant's point of sale (POS) installation. Please complete this form to help us process your installation efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your restaurant's name and contact information (fields 2-3).
2. Provide your restaurant's address (field 4).
3. Choose your restaurant's type (field 6) from the select options: POS, Cafe, Restaurant, or Bar.
4. (Optional) Enter the number of seats and tables (fields 7-8) for accurate operational details.
5. Finally, review all fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant name** (restaurant_name, text, required/optional): Enter your restaurant's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required/optional): Enter your main phone number for our support staff to contact you.
* **Address** (address, text, required/optional): Provide your restaurant's physical address for our delivery and installation team to access.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for any communication or updates.
* **Restaurant Type** (restaurant_type, select_one, required/optional): Choose your restaurant type from the options: POS, Cafe, Restaurant, or Bar.
* **Number of Seats** (number_of_seats, number, required/optional): Enter the number of seats available in your restaurant, if applicable.
* **Number of Tables** (number_of_tables, number, required/optional): Enter the number of tables available in your restaurant, if applicable.
