# Document_Compatibility_Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Compatibility Assessment form is designed to collect information about a specific document and its compatibility with different platforms or versions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your User Name and Platform fields.
2.  Select the Document Type and specify any issues with the document.
3.  Enter any Compatibility Problem, issue, and version details.
4.  Fill in the Submitter's information, including Name, Email, Phone, Department, Position, and Date (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
*   **Platform** (`platform`, `select_one`, required): Select the platform you are using.
    *   Options: Mobile, Desktop
*   **Document Type** (`document_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the type(s) of document(s) you are dealing with.
    *   Options: Word, PDF, Image, Audio, Video, Other
*   **Document Problem** (`document_problem`, `text`, required): Enter the problem with the document.
*   **Document Version** (`document_version`, `text`, optional): Enter the version of the document (if applicable).
*   **Document Issue** (`document_issue`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the document issue.
*   **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required): Describe the issue with the document.
*   **Compatibility Problem** (`compatibility_problem`, `text`, required): Enter the compatibility issue with the platform or software.
*   **Compatibility Issue** (`compatibility_issue`, `text`, required): Enter the compatibility issue with the platform or software.
*   **Compatibility Version** (`compatibility_version`, `text`, required): Enter the version of the platform or software where the issue occurred.
*   **Submission Platform** (`submission_platform`, `select_one`, optional): Select the platform you used for submission.
    *   Options: Mobile, Desktop
*   **Submission Version** (`submission_version`, `text`, optional): Enter the version of the submission platform (if applicable).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
*   **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the report.
*   **Submitter E-mail** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, `text`, required): Enter your department or affiliation.
*   **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, `text`, required): Enter your position or title.
*   **Date** (`submitter_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of submission (if applicable).
*   **Time** (`submitter_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of submission (if applicable).
*   **Comments** (`submitter_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
*   **Attachment** (`submitter_attachment`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents.
*   **Signature** (`submitter_signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your signature.
*   **Location** (`submitter_location`, `text`, optional): Enter your location.
*   **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.

Please fill in the form accurately and according to your needs.
