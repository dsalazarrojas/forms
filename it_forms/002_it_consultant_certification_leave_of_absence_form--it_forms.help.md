# it_consultant_certification_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The it_consultant_certification_leave_of_absence_form is used to manage leave of absence requests from employees. This form gathers information about the category of absence, dates, manager's contact details, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Form Title: Type the title of the request in this field.
2. Enter the Form Description: Provide a brief description of the absence request in this field.
3. Select Category: Choose the category of absence from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your Name: Type your name as it appears on your employee ID or in your company records.
5. Select Department: Choose your department from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter Manager's Email: Type the email address of your manager.
7. Enter Manager's Phone: Type the phone number of your manager.
8. Select Date Of Absence Start: Choose the start date of your absence from the calendar.
9. Select Date Of Absence End: Choose the end date of your absence from the calendar.
10. Enter Reason For Absence: Type the reason for your absence.
11. Select Status: Choose the status of your absence request from the dropdown menu.
12. Enter Comments: Provide any additional comments or information about your absence request.
12. Submit Request: Select whether to submit or cancel your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Type a brief title of your absence request.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your absence request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the category of absence from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: IT Consultation, IT Forms
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Type your name as it appears on your employee ID or in your company records.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your department from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: IT, Marketing, Sales
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Type the email address of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Type the phone number of your manager.
* **Date Of Absence Start** (`date_of_absence_start`, date, required/optional): Choose the start date of your absence from the calendar.
* **Date Of Absence End** (`date_of_absence_end`, date, required/optional): Choose the end date of your absence from the calendar.
* **Reason For Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required/optional): Type the reason for your absence.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your absence request from the dropdown menu.
	+ Options: New, In Progress, Approved, Rejected
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your absence request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether to submit or cancel your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR representative.
