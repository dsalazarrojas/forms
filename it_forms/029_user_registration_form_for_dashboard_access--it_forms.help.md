# User Registration Form For Dashboard Access - Help Guide
## Purpose
The User Registration Form for Dashboard Access is used to create new user accounts for access to the dashboard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid **Password** and confirm it in the **Confirm Password** field.
3. Select your desired **User Role** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your **Company**, **Job Title**, and **Department** details as required.
5. Fill in your **Date Of Birth**, **Date Of Hire**, and **Time Of Hire** (if applicable).
6. Add any **User Notes** you would like to include.
7. Select your **User Status** and **User Access Level** from the dropdown menus.
8. Choose your **User Access Group** from the dropdown menu.
9. Fill in the **Custom Field 1** through **Custom Field 7** fields as required (these fields might not be applicable).
10. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form to create your new user account.
