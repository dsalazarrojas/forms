# Severity 1 Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report incidents that require immediate attention and resolution. It helps to gather essential information about the incident, including its severity level, type, and location, to ensure that incidents are handled efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine the severity level of the incident by selecting the relevant option from the drop-down menu.
2. Identify the type of incident (hardware, software, or other) and select the corresponding option.
3. If necessary, provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant information that may aid in its resolution.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" and "HH:MM am/pm" respectively.
5. Enter the location of the incident, including the address or any other relevant details.
6. Provide as much detail as possible about the incident, including any relevant documentation or evidence that may be required for further investigation.
7. Specify the resolution outcome and the status of the incident.
8. Choose the team that will be handling the incident from the drop-down menu.
9. Select the priority level of the incident based on its severity and potential impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the severity level (Severity Level)**: Select the level of severity that best describes the incident.
	+ Critical: The incident has a significant impact on the operation or has resulted in a major issue.
	+ High: The incident has some impact on the operation or has resulted in a moderate issue.
	+ Medium: The incident has a minor impact on the operation or has resulted in a low-level issue.
	+ Low: The incident has no impact on the operation or has resulted in no issue.
* **Incident type (Incident Type)**: Identify the type of incident (hardware, software, or other) that occurred.
* **Description (Description)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant information that may aid in its resolution.
* **Incident date (Incident Date)**: Enter the date of the incident in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Incident time (Incident Time)**: Enter the time of the incident in the format "HH:MM am/pm".
* **Incident location (Incident Location)**: Enter the location of the incident, including the address or any other relevant details.
* **Incident details (Incident Details)**: Provide as much detail as possible about the incident, including any relevant documentation or evidence that may be required for further investigation.
* **Resolution outcome (Resolution Outcome)**: Specify the outcome of the resolution, whether it was resolved or unresolved.
* **Incident status (Incident Status)**: Specify the current status of the incident, whether it is active or closed.
* **Incident team (Incident Team)**: Choose the team that will be handling the incident from the drop-down menu.
* **Incident priority (Incident Priority)**: Select the priority level of the incident based on its severity and potential impact.
