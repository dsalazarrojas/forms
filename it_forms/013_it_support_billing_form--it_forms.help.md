# it_support_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_support_billing_form" is used to collect billing and payment information from the end-user. This form is designed to gather essential details such as payment method, billing address, and contact information. It also includes fields for terms and conditions acceptance, payment terms acceptance, and agreement to billing terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter your information accurately.
2. Fill out the payment details, including the method and any required information.
3. Enter your billing address, including city, state, and postal code.
4. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
5. Review and select the relevant terms and conditions acceptance options.
6. Sign the form by typing your signature in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Details** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your payment method, including any required information such as card number, expiration date, and CVV.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your billing address, including street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where your billing address is located.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where your billing address is located.
* **Billing Postal Code** (`billing_postal_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter the postal code for your billing address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`billing_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you accept the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Billing Terms Acceptance** (`billing_terms_acceptance`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you accept the billing terms of the service.
* **Payment Terms Acceptance** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you accept the payment terms of the service.
* **Payment Terms Agreed** (`payment_terms_agreed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you agree to the payment terms.
* **Billing Terms Agreed** (`billing_terms_agreed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you agree to the billing terms.
* **Payment Terms Agreed** (`payment_terms_agreed`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you agree to the payment terms.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Type your signature to complete the form.

Note: The fields marked as `required: false` are optional and can be filled out if necessary.
