# IT Cost Optimization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new IT Cost Optimization Programs. It helps us track and manage existing and future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for your program in the "Program Title" field.
2. Provide the name of your program in the "Program Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the category that your program belongs to in the "Category" field.
4. Enter the name of the department that your program is associated with in the "Department" field.
5. Describe your program in the "Program Description" field.
6. Indicate if you are a participant in the "Participant" field.
7. List the goals of your program in the "Goals" field.
8. Select the focus areas of your program in the "Focus Areas" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (program_title, text, required): Enter a brief title for your program.
* **Program Name** (program_name, text, required): Enter the full name of your program.
* **Category** (category_name, text, required): Enter the category that your program belongs to.
* **Department** (department_name, text, required): Enter the department that your program is associated with.
* **Program Description** (program_description, text, required): Describe your program in a few sentences.
* **Participant** (participant, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you are a participant in the program.
* **Goals** (goals, text, required): List the goals of your program.
* **Focus Areas** (focus_areas, select_multiple, required): Select the focus areas of your program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the title and name of your program are clear and concise.
* Be specific when selecting focus areas, as this will help us understand your program's goals.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
