# User Interface Design Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool for designers to evaluate and improve the user interface of a website or application. It checks for adherence to design principles such as usability, accessibility, and visual hierarchy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and concisely, providing relevant details about your design.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief but clear explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Design Principles** (`design_principles`): This field asks about your approach to design, considering principles such as user-centered design, consistency, and clarity.
	+ This field is optional but helps us understand your design philosophy.
- **Navigation Menu** (`navigation_menu`): This field inquires about your navigation menu, considering factors such as accessibility and discoverability.
	+ For multiple-choice options:
		- Menu items are easily accessible from every screen.
		- Menu items are easily discoverable.
		- Menu items have icons that are easily recognizable.
	+ Select all applicable options.
- **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`): This field is about the color scheme used in your design, considering options like monochromatic, analogous, and complementary colors.
	+ This is a required field.
- **Visual Hierarchy** (`visual_hierarchy`): This field asks about the visual hierarchy used in your design, considering the way visual elements are organized and prioritized.
	+ This field is optional.
- **Typography** (`typography`): This field inquires about the typography used in your design, considering factors like font sizes, styles, and line spacing.
	+ This field is optional.
- **Images** (`images`): This field is about the use and presentation of images in your design, considering factors like image clarity and documentation.
	+ For multiple-choice options:
		- Images are used consistently.
		- Images are clear and concise.
		- Images are well-documented.
	+ Select all applicable options.
- **Layout** (`layout`): This field asks about the overall layout of your design, considering factors like spacing and organization.
	+ This field is optional.
- **Usability** (`usability`): This field inquires about the usability of your design, considering options like user-friendliness and intuitiveness.
	+ This field is required.
- **Accessibility** (`accessibility`): This field asks about the accessibility of your design, considering factors like readability and screen reader compatibility.
	+ This field is optional.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`): This field inquires about how users can provide feedback in your design, considering options like email, phone, or in-app messaging.
	+ This field is optional.
- **User Experience** (`user_experience`): This field asks about the overall user experience of your design, considering factors like user satisfaction and engagement.
	+ This field is optional.
- **Consistency** (`consistency`): This field inquires about the consistency of your design, considering factors like repeated elements and visual coherence.
	+ This field is optional.
- **User Experience Score** (`user_experience_score`): This field requires you to rate your overall user experience on a scale of 1-10.
	+ This field is required.
- **Overall Score** (`overall_score`): This field requires you to rate the overall quality of your design on a scale of 1-10.
	+ This field is required.
- **Comments** (`comments`): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to make about your design.
	+ This field is optional.

Note: Please answer these questions honestly and with as much detail as possible to get the most accurate evaluation of your design.
