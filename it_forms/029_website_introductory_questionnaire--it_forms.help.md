# website-introductory-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form will help you provide essential details about the website you are creating or managing. The information collected will aid in understanding the website's audience, content, and technical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each field based on the website you are creating or managing.
2. Ensure you answer all fields that apply to your website.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **websiteIntroForm**: Enter a brief title or description of the website. This field is optional.
* **websiteDescription**: Provide a brief description of the website.
* **websiteTargetAudience**: Select the type of people you are targeting with your website (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websitePurpose**: Select all applicable purposes for your website (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTechnologies**: If your website uses specific technologies, enter them in this field.
* **websiteColorScheme**: Select the color scheme you will use for your website (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetPlatform**: Select the platform your website will be on (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetDevice**: Select the devices your website will be accessible on (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteColorSchemes**: Select all applicable color schemes for your website (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteLanguage**: Select the language your website will be in (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteDesignStyle**: Select the design style of your website (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteLayout**: Select the layout of your website (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteContent**: Enter any additional content requirements for your website.
* **websiteImages**: Enter any images or image requirements for your website.
* **websiteVideos**: Enter any videos or video requirements for your website.
* **websiteAudio**: Enter any audio or audio requirements for your website.
* **websiteTargetAudienceAges**: Select the age group(s) of your target audience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetAudienceAgesRanges**: Enter any age range requirements for your target audience.
* **websiteTargetAudienceGenders**: Select the gender(s) of your target audience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetAudienceLocations**: Enter any locations of your target audience.
* **websiteTargetAudienceLanguages**: Select all applicable languages for your target audience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetAudienceInterests**: Enter any interests of your target audience.
* **websiteTargetAudienceEducation**: Select the education level(s) of your target audience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetAudienceIncome**: Enter any income level requirements for your target audience.
* **websiteTargetAudienceEmployment**: Select the employment status(es) of your target audience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **websiteTargetAudienceEmploymentTypes**: Select the employment type(s) of your target audience (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form.
