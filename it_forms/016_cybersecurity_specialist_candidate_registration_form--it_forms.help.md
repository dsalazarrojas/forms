# Cybersecurity Specialist Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in applying for the Cybersecurity Specialist role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Candidate Information section with your name, and other relevant details.
2.  Enter your Contact Information, including your email address and phone number.
3.  Provide your Education Background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
4.  Describe your Work Experience, highlighting any relevant skills or achievements.
5.  Select your skills from the list provided, including Programming, Networking, and Cybersecurity skills.
6.  If you have any relevant Certifications, please list them.
7.  Indicate your availability for the role.
8.  Enter your email address in the Email field.
9.  Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
10. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Email field.
11. Confirm your phone number in the Confirm Phone field.
12. Add any Additional Notes or comments in the Additional Notes field.
13. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
14. Enter your Time zone in the Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information**: This is where you provide your name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Education Background**: List any degrees or certifications relevant to the role.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your work experience, highlighting any relevant skills or achievements.
* **Skills**: Select any relevant skills you have, such as Programming, Networking, or Cybersecurity skills.
* **Certifications**: List any relevant certifications you have obtained.
* **Availability**: Indicate your availability for the role.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time**: Enter your time zone.
*   **Submit**: This is where you submit your application.
