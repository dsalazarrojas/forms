# Device Erasure Verification Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Device Erasure Verification Issue Report Form" is a tool used to report issues related to device erasure, allowing your team to track and resolve these problems efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*).
2. Enter the Asset ID, which is the unique identifier for the affected asset.
3. Enter the Device ID, which is the unique identifier for the device in question.
4. Select the Asset Type from the provided options (Laptop, Desktop, Mobile, Tablet, or Other).
5. Describe the issue with the device, providing as much detail as possible.
6. Choose the priority level of the issue (High, Medium, or Low).
7. Select the status of the issue (New, In Progress, Done, Rejected, or Closed).
8. Enter your name, email, and phone number to be contacted for further clarification or updates.
9. Optionally, provide additional notes for the reviewer to consider.
10. Select the department and team of the submitter and reviewer for tracking and reporting purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the issue with the device?** (`What is the issue with the device?`): Enter a brief description of the problem with the device.
* **Asset ID** (`asset_id`): The unique identifier for the affected asset.
* **Device ID** (`device_id`): The unique identifier for the device in question.
* **Asset Name** (`asset_name`): A brief description of the asset.
* **Device Name** (`device_name`): A brief description of the device.
* **Asset Type** (`asset_type`): The type of asset (Laptop, Desktop, Mobile, Tablet, or Other).
* **Asset Status** (`asset_status`): The status of the asset (Active, Inactive, Pending, Unknown).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`): A detailed description of the issue with the device.
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`): The level of priority for the issue (High, Medium, Low).
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`): The status of the issue (New, In Progress, Done, Rejected, Closed).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`): Your name for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`): The date you're reporting the issue on.
* **Time** (`time`): The time you're reporting the issue on.
* **Submitter Email** (`email`): Your email address for further communication.
* **Submitter Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number for further communication.
* **Submitter Notes** (`notes`): Additional notes for the reviewer.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`): The department of the submitter.
* **Submitter Team** (`submitter_team`): The team of the submitter.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): The name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`): The department of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Team** (`reviewer_team`): The team of the reviewer.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): The date the issue was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`): Additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`): The company of the submitter.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`): The location of the submitter.
