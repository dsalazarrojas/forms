# Virus Removal Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virus Removal Analysis Form is used to collect information for the removal of viruses from a computer system. This form is designed to help IT departments or help desks gather all necessary details to analyze and remove viruses efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a **Title** for the form, briefly describing the case.
2. Identify the **Suspected Malware** type that is causing the issue.
3. Provide a detailed description of the **Symptoms** you are experiencing.
4. Enter relevant **System Information**, including any recent updates or changes made to the system.
5. Provide any relevant **User Information**, including user's name, role, or any other relevant details.
6. Choose the **Virus Removal Technician** who will handle the case.
7. Enter the **Submission Date and Time**, including the date and time the form was submitted.
8. Choose the **Organisation** involved in the virus removal process (e.g., IT Department, Help Desk).
9. Enter the **Virus Removal Technician's Notes**, any additional information about the case.
10. Confirm if the **Case is Complete**.
11. Select the **Type of Case**, e.g., Virus Removal, Data Recovery.
11. Select the **Type of Malware**, e.g., virus, other.
12. Optionally, provide additional **Time** and **Date** fields if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suspected Malware**: Enter the type of malware you suspect is causing the issue.
* **Symptoms**: Describe the symptoms you are experiencing with the system.
* **System Info**: Enter relevant system information, such as recent updates or changes.
* **User Info**: Provide any relevant user details.
* **Virus Removal Technician**: Choose the technician who will handle the case.
* **Submission Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you submit this form.
* **Organisation**: Select the organisation involved in the virus removal process.
* **Virus Removal Technician's Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the technician.
* **Case Complete**: Confirm if the case is complete.
* **Type of Case**: Select the type of case, e.g., Virus Removal, Data Recovery.
* **Type of Malware**: Select the type of malware, e.g., virus, other.
* **Time**: Enter the time spent on the case (optional).
* **Date**: Enter the date of submission (optional).
* **Other Notes**: Enter any additional information about the case.
