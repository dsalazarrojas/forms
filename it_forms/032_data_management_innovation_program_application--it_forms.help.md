# Data Management Innovation Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your project, team, and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. For each field, select the option that best describes your project or team.
3. If a field is not applicable to your project or team, please select "N/A" and provide a brief explanation in the "Impact Description" field.
4. Ensure you have completed all required fields to submit the form successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives.
* **Team Members**: Select all team members who will be working on the project.

* **Project Lead**: Select the person who will be leading your project.
* **Team Lead**: Select the person who will be leading your team.
