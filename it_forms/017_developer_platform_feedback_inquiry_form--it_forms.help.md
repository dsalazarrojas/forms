# Developer Platform Feedback Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from developers who are using our platform, allowing us to understand their experience and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question, "How do you feel about our platform?" in the text field provided.
2. Fill out the "Contact Name" field with your name as it should be displayed for contact purposes.
3. Enter your "Contact Email" in the format of a standard email address (e.g., example@example.com).
4. Optionally, provide your "Contact Phone" number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
5. In the "Feedback" field, describe the issue or concern you'd like to discuss.
6. Select the "Category" that best matches the nature of your feedback.
7. For "Subcategory", you can select multiple options that apply to your feedback.
8. If you'd like to provide a more detailed "Description" of the issue, you can do so in the text field.
9. Rate your experience with the "Rating" field, using a number from 1 to 5.
10. Provide the date and time you experienced the issue in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
11. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you feel about our platform?** (<type:text>): This field allows you to express your overall feelings towards our platform, so be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Name** (<type:text>): Enter your name as you'd like to be displayed for contact purposes. This will be displayed when we respond to your feedback.
* **Contact Email** (<type:email>): Enter your contact email address in the format of example@example.com. We'll use this to contact you for further clarification or to provide updates.
* **Contact Phone** (<type:text>): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Feedback** (<type:text>): Describe the issue or concern you'd like to discuss. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Category** (<type:select_one>): Select the category that best matches your feedback, such as "Technical Issue", "Feature Request", or "General Feedback".
* **Subcategory** (<type:select_multiple>): Select one or more subcategories that apply to your feedback. For example, "User Interface", "Performance", "Security".
* **Description** (<type:text>): If you'd like to provide more context or details about your issue, this field is available for you to expand on.
* **Rating** (<type:number>): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Date** (<type:date>): Enter the date you experienced the issue. This helps us understand when the issue occurred.
* **Time** (<type:time>): Enter the time you experienced the issue. This helps us pinpoint the timing of the issue.
* **Time** (<type:time>): Please note that time and date are not required fields but can be useful in providing additional context.
