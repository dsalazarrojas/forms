**School District Technology Audit Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the technology infrastructure of a school district. It gathers data on various aspects of technology usage, including whether a school district has certain technologies, the number of devices and software used, and the status of these technologies. The form is used to track and analyze the technology landscape of school districts, helping decision-makers make informed decisions about resource allocation and improvement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the school district from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the type of audit being performed (e.g., initial, updated, or cancelled).
3. Enter the school name and location (if applicable).
4. Indicate whether the school district has the following technologies: devices, software, budgeted items, etc.
5. Enter the actual and budgeted counts for devices and software.
6. Select the status of the technology (active, inactive, or both).
7. Provide a brief note on the technology status, if applicable.
8. Enter the last updated date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School District** (school_district): Select the school district from the list of available options.
* **Audit Type** (audit_type): Choose the type of audit being performed (e.g., initial, updated, or cancelled).
* **School Name** (school_name): Enter the name of the school.
* **Location** (location): Select multiple locations where the technology is used (if applicable).
* **Devices Count** (devices_count): Enter the number of devices used.
* **Software Count** (software_count): Enter the number of software used.
* **Budgeted Items Count** (budgeted_items_count): Enter the number of budgeted items.
* **Actual Cost** (actual_cost): Enter the actual cost of the technology.
* **Budgeted Cost** (budgeted_cost): Enter the budgeted cost of the technology.
* **Technology Status** (technology_status): Select the status of the technology (active, inactive, or both).
* **Technology Status Note** (technology_status_note): Provide a brief note on the technology status, if applicable.
* **Last Updated** (last_updated): Enter the last updated date of the technology.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct school district and audit type.
* Fill in all required fields.
* Enter accurate counts for devices, software, and budgeted items.
* Select the correct status for the technology (active, inactive, or both).
* Provide a clear note on the technology status, if applicable.
* Enter the correct last updated date.
