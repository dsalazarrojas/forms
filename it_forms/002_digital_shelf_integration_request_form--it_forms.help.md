# Digital Shelf Integration Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information from customers or users who want to integrate their products on our digital shelf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the product, company, and yourself.
2. Ensure that all required fields (e.g., Request Status) are filled out correctly.
3. Double-check your submission for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form (Text)**: This field is used to describe the product or service you want to integrate on our digital shelf.
* **Customer Info (Text)**: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact Info (Text)**: Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number, so we can get in touch with you about your integration request.
* **Company Info (Text)**: Enter your company name, URL, and address to help us get a better understanding of your business.
* **Product Info (Text)**: Describe the product or service you'd like to integrate on our digital shelf.
* **Payment Info (Text)**: If you're providing payment information, please enter it accurately to avoid any issues with your integration request.
* **Shipping Info (Text)**: Enter any shipping information that's relevant to your integration request.
* **Order Details (Text)**: Describe the specific details about the order you'd like to integrate on our digital shelf.
* **Notes (Note)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your integration request, if you'd like.
* **Request Status (Select One)**: Select the correct status of your integration request from the provided options (New, Pending, Rejected, Approved, Cancelled).
* **Request Date (Date)**: Enter the date of when you submitted this integration request.
* **Request Time (Time)**: Enter the time of when you submitted this integration request.
* **Customer Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for communication about your integration request.
* **Customer Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you about your integration request.
* **Customer Note (Note)**: Leave any notes or comments about your integration request that you'd like to share with us.
* **Requester Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person making the integration request.
* **Company Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the company related to this integration request.
* **Company URL (Text)**: Enter the URL of the company related to this integration request.
* **Company Address (Text)**: Enter the address of the company related to this integration request.
* **Product Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the product you'd like to integrate on our digital shelf.
* **Requester Name (Text)**: (This field is a duplicate of the Requester Name field, please disregard)

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your submission for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our support team for clarification.
