# Website Maintenance Renewal Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Maintenance Renewal Agreement form is designed to collect information necessary for maintaining and renewing website agreements. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure that all fields have been filled out correctly.
2. Fill in your User information (page 1).
3. Provide your Company Name (page 1).
4. Select the desired Support Term (page 3, 12, 18 or 24 or 36 months).
5. Choose the Support Level (Basic, Premium, or Advanced).
6. Enter the Renewal Date (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
7. Provide the Contact Person's Name (page 2).
8. Enter the Contact Person's Email (page 2).
9. Enter the Contact Person's Phone Number (page 2).
10. Select the current Renewal Status (Pending, Active, or Inactive).
11. Add any Comments (page 3).
12. Confirm your agreement to the terms by selecting "Agree to terms" (page 5).
13. Enter the Agreement Date (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User**:
	+ This field captures the user's information, which may be used to track who submitted the agreement.
* **Company Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the company associated with this website maintenance agreement.
* **Support Term**:
	+ Select the term of the support period, which may be 12, 24, 36, or 48 months.
* **Support Level**:
	+ Choose the level of support desired, which may be Basic, Premium, or Advanced.
* **Renewal Date**:
	+ Enter the date the agreement is to be renewed, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the person who will be contacted regarding this agreement.
* **Contact E-mail**:
	+ Enter the email address of the person who will be contacted regarding this agreement.
* **Contact Phone**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who will be contacted regarding this agreement.
* **Renewal Status**:
	+ Select the current status of the agreement, which may be Pending, Active, or Inactive.
* **Comments**:
	+ Add any additional comments or notes regarding this agreement.
* **Agree to terms**:
	+ Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Agreement Date**:
	+ Enter the date and time the agreement was made, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
