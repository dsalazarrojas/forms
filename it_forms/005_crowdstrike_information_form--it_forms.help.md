# CrowdStrike Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CrowdStrike Information Form is designed to gather essential information from users about their company or organization. This information will help the support team to effectively assist and communicate with users regarding their queries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For any optional fields, provide relevant details if available.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First-name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address for communication.
* **Security Insights**: Provide any relevant security insights or concerns related to your company or organization.
* **Product- Services**: Select all the product or service options that apply to your company or organization. (Cloud-based or On-premises)
* **Company Name**: Enter your company or organization name for identification purposes.
* **Company URL**: Enter the official URL of your company or organization.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name and title of the person responsible for contacting you or your organization.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the official phone number of your company or organization.
* **Note**: Any additional notes or comments regarding your company or organization.

**Tips**:
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of the information before submitting.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the support team for assistance.
