# FASTA File Analysis Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a submission template for users to request analysis on their FASTA file. 
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the user's FASTA file and their analysis preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the type of analysis you need from the "Select Analysis Type" dropdown menu. 
Next, provide information about your sample by selecting the "Sample Type", "Organism", and "Organism Type". 
Additionally, you can choose to upload any necessary files for analysis. 
Finally, fill in the "Project", "Notes", "Email", and "Phone" fields if applicable. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Input Form**: This is the primary input field for your FASTA file details. Enter your FASTA file information here.
* **Input Fasta File**: Enter the path to your FASTA file for analysis.
* **Select Analysis Type**: Choose the type of analysis you need, such as Alignment, Gene prediction, or Gene prediction and annotation.
* **Input File**: Upload any additional files required for analysis.
* **Select Sample Type**: Choose the type of sample, such as Genomic DNA, Genomic RNA, Metagenomic DNA, or Metagenomic RNA.
* **Project**: Choose whether your project is "Yes" or "No".
* **Organism**: Choose the organism for your sample, such as Human, Mouse, or Zebrafish.
* **Organism Type**: Choose whether your organism type is "Yes" or "No".
* **Genome Size**: Choose whether your genome size is "Yes" or "No".
* **Genome Size Type**: Choose whether your genome size type is "Yes" or "No".
* **File Type**: Choose whether your file type is "Yes" or "No".
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your sample or analysis.
* **Upload File**: Upload any additional files required for analysis.
* **Upload Additional File**: Upload any additional files required for analysis.
* **Select Additional File Type**: Choose whether you have additional files to upload, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Sample Organism**: Choose whether your sample organism is "Yes" or "No".
* **Sample Notes**: Add any additional notes about your sample.
* **Additional File Type**: This is a placeholder field and will always be "No". 
* **Select Sample Organism**: This is a placeholder field and will always be "No". 

Note: The form is designed to collect relevant information for analysis. Please ensure you have necessary permissions and follow guidelines before submitting this form.
