# Network Reliability Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to report incidents related to network reliability issues. The primary goal is to gather detailed descriptions of incidents, allowing the team to analyze and resolve the issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the form fields with the requested information.
2.  Be thorough and accurate in your descriptions to help the team better understand the incidents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your name, email, or any other identifying information you'd like to share with us.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including the date, time, and location (if applicable).
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): If you have any relevant files or images related to the incident, upload them here. This will help the team better understand the issue.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your report.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the incident.
* Include any relevant screenshots, logs, or other supporting materials if applicable.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* Your report will be reviewed and addressed promptly, and you'll receive updates on the resolution status.

Please note that this form is designed to be a helpful guide and not a replacement for human judgment. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
