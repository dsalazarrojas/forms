# Digital Access Key Reset Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a reset of a digital access key for users who have forgotten or lost their access credentials. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form ID**, which is a unique identifier for this form.
2. Provide a brief **Description** of the reason you're requesting a reset.
3. Enter your **User Name** as it appears in our system.
4. Enter your **Email** address that is associated with your access key.
5. Provide a reason for the **Reason** for requesting a reset.
6. Enter the **Authorization Date** when your access key was initially granted.
7. Select the **Status** of your request (New, In Progress, or Resolved).
8. Optionally, add any **Notes** regarding your request.
9. Provide any **User Comments** about your request.
10. Finally, add any **IT Comments** if you have additional information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (1): Enter the unique identifier for this form.
* **Description** (2): A brief explanation of the reason you're requesting a reset.
* **User Name** (3): Enter the name as it appears in our system for your access key.
* **Email** (4): Enter the email address associated with your access key.
* **Reason** (5): Provide a reason for requesting a reset.
* **Authorization Date** (6): Enter the date when your access key was initially granted.
* **Status** (8): Select the status of your request (New, In Progress, or Resolved).
* **Notes** (9): Add any additional notes about your request.
* **User Comments** (10): Provide any additional comments from your end.
* **IT Comments** (11): Add any additional comments from the IT team.
