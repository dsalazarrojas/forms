# csc_vle_society - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a society or organization. It captures details about the company, contact person, email, phone number, meeting date and time, and any additional notes. This form is likely used for scheduling meetings or events with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information, starting with the "Company" field, which should be the name of the organization you are dealing with.
2. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact" field.
3. Type in the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a date for the meeting or event in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time for the meeting or event in the "Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the meeting or event in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization you are dealing with.
* **Contact** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Type the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Type the contact person's phone number.
* **Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a date for the meeting or event.
* **Time** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): Select a time for the meeting or event.
* **Note** (`7`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the meeting or event.
* **Email 2** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): This field is likely for a backup or alternative email address.
* **Phone 2** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): This field may be for a backup or alternative phone number.

Note: The form is not strictly enforcing any specific rules for these fields, but it seems that the "Company", "Contact", "Email", "Phone", "Date", and "Time" fields are likely required for the form's purpose, while the "Note", "Email 2", and "Phone 2" fields are optional and may be used for additional information.
