# ServiceNow Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help the IT team understand your level of knowledge and training in ServiceNow, an IT service management platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information, making sure to be honest about your level of knowledge and experience.
2. Select the correct options for each field, based on your actual experience and knowledge.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for help from your manager or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (1): A text field to enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (2): A text field to enter your last name.
*   **Email** (3): A text field to enter your work email.
*   **Phone** (4): A text field to enter your phone number.
*   **Skill Level** (5): A dropdown menu to select your level of skill in ServiceNow (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
*   **Knowledge Adoption** (6): A multiple-choice dropdown menu to select your level of knowledge adoption (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).
*   **Years of Experience** (7): A number field to enter your years of experience with ServiceNow.
*   **Job Title** (8): A text field to enter your job title.
*   **Department** (9): A text field to enter your department.
*   **Manager Name** (10): A text field to enter your manager's name.
*   **Training Hours** (11): A number field to enter the number of training hours you've completed.
*   **Training Frequency** (12): A dropdown menu to select the frequency of your training (Regular, Occasional, Rarely).
*   **Last Updated** (13): A date field to enter the last time you updated your training status.
*   **Last Trained By** (14): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.
*   **Training Provider** (15): A multiple-choice dropdown menu to select the training provider (ServiceNow, Other).
*   **Training Topic** (16): A multiple-choice dropdown menu to select the training topic (IT Service Management, ITIL, Cloud).
*   **Training Goal** (17): A multiple-choice dropdown menu to select the training goal (ITIL, Cloud, Other).
*   **Feedback** (18): A note field to enter any feedback about your training experience.
*   **Additional Comments** (19): A text field to enter any additional comments about your training experience.
*   **Training Status** (20): A dropdown menu to select your training status (Active, Completed, Pending).
*   **Training Status Reason** (21): A text field to enter the reason for your training status.
*   **Training Status Comments** (22): A text field to enter any comments about your training status.
*   **Training Status Last Updated** (23): A date field to enter the last time you updated your training status.
*   **Training Last Updated By** (24): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.
*   **Training Last Updated By 2** (25): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.
*   **Training Last Updated By 3** (26): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.
*   **Training Last Updated By 4** (27): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.
*   **Training Last Updated By 5** (28): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.
*   **Training Last Updated By 6** (29): A time field to enter the time you last updated your training status.

## Tips

* Be honest about your level of knowledge and experience to help the IT team better understand your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for help from your manager or HR representative.
* Please note that this form is intended for new assessments, so if you have filled it out before, please do not fill it out again.
