The retail technology pilot application form is designed for retail technology vendors to submit their solutions for evaluation and potential inclusion in our pilot program. The form captures essential information about the vendor and their solution, including its details, objective, category, and supporting documents.

This form is meant for new vendors to submit their applications, but please note that we only allow one application per solution and vendor. If you've already submitted an application, please do not submit another.

## Retail Technology Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for retail technology vendors to submit their solutions for evaluation and potential inclusion in our pilot program. Please ensure that you have all the required information before filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Fill in the "Solution Details" field with a brief description of your solution.
3. Select the "Objective" for your solution.
4. Upload "Supporting Documents" relevant to your solution.
5. Select the "Solution Category" and "Solution Subcategory" for your solution.
6. Enter the "Solution Name" and "Vendor Name" for your company.
7. Enter a brief "Objective Description" for your solution.
8. Upload the "Solution Image" and "Solution File" for your solution.
9. Select "Supporting Documents" 1-5 relevant to your solution.
10. Enter your "Email", "Phone", and "Address" contact information.
11. Add any additional "Notes" about your solution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Solution Details
* **Solution Details** (`Solution Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for a brief description of your solution, including its main features and benefits.
* **Objective** (`Objective`, `text`, required/optional): Please select the objective of your solution.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Upload up to 5 supporting documents relevant to your solution, such as product brochures, technical specifications, or marketing materials.
* **Solution Category** (`Solution Category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the category of your solution, such as In-Store Solutions, Online Solutions, or Both.
* **Solution Subcategory** (`Solution Subcategory`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the subcategory of your solution, such as Inventory Management or Marketing & Advertising.
* **Solution Name** (`Solution Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your solution.
* **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Objective Description** (`Objective Description`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your solution's objective.
* **Solution Image** (`Solution Image`, `text`, required/optional): Upload an image of your solution.
* **Solution File** (`Solution File`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a file for your solution.
* **Supporting Document 1** (`Supporting Document 1`, `text`, required/optional): Upload the first supporting document for your solution.
* **Supporting Document 2** (`Supporting Document 2`, `text`, required/optional): Upload the second supporting document for your solution.
* **Supporting Document 3** (`Supporting Document 3`, `text`, required/optional): Upload the third supporting document for your solution.
* **Supporting Document 4** (`Supporting Document 4`, `text`, required/optional): Upload the fourth supporting document for your solution.
* **Supporting Document 5** (`Supporting Document 5`, `text`, required/optional): Upload the fifth supporting document for your solution.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your solution.
* **Solution Status** (`Solution Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the current status of your solution, such as In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled.
* **Vendor Status** (`Vendor Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the current status of your vendor, such as Approved, Rejected, or Pending.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the required information before submitting the form.
* You may only submit one application per solution and vendor.
* Please do not upload sensitive or confidential information.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our team for assistance.
