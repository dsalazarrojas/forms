# Cybersecurity Incident Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cybersecurity Incident Check Out Form is designed to help gather information about cybersecurity incidents that have occurred within your organization or system. This form provides a structured approach to collecting details about the incident, making it easier to track and analyze incidents, and implement measures to prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Malware, Ransomware, Phishing, or Other).
2.  Provide a brief description of the incident.
3.  Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4.  Choose the severity level of the incident (Low, Medium, or High).
5.  List all the resources that were affected by the incident.
6.  Update the status of the incident (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
7.  Select the category of the incident (Security, Data Breach, or Other).
8.  Enter the date and time when the incident occurred.
9.  Enter the date and time when the incident was reported.
10.  Enter the date and time when the incident was closed (if applicable).
11.  Provide additional notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Description**:
    A text box where you can provide a detailed description of the incident.
    Be as specific as possible to help the incident response team understand the circumstances surrounding the incident.
*   **Incident Type**:
    Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Malware, Ransomware, Phishing, or Other).
    This information helps the incident response team understand the nature of the incident and respond accordingly.
*   **Incident Location**:
    Enter the location where the incident occurred.
    This can be a physical or virtual location (e.g., server room, data center, or cloud-based system).
*   **Incident Date**:
    Enter the date when the incident occurred.
    Use the date format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/2022).
*   **Incident Time**:
    Enter the time when the incident occurred.
    Use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00).
*   **Incident Severity**:
    Choose the severity level of the incident (Low, Medium, or High).
    This helps the incident response team understand the potential impact of the incident.
*   **Affected Resources**:
    List all the resources that were affected by the incident.
    Include details about systems, servers, databases, or any other resources impacted.
*   **Incident Status**:
    Update the status of the incident (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
    This indicates the current state of the incident and helps the incident response team prioritize tasks.
*   **Incident Category**:
    Select the category of the incident (Security, Data Breach, or Other).
    This helps the incident response team understand the incident's context and respond accordingly.
*   **Incident Reporter**:
    Enter the name and contact information of the person reporting the incident.
    This information helps the incident response team contact the reporter for additional details or follow-up.
*   **Incident Report Date**:
    Enter the date when the incident was reported.
    Use the date format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/2022).
*   **Incident Close Date**:
    Enter the date and time when the incident was closed.
    Use the date format mm/dd/yyyy and 24-hour clock format (e.g., 01/01/2022 14:00).
*   **Incident Notes**:
    Provide additional notes about the incident.
    This is an optional field for any additional information that might be relevant to the incident.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
*   Use the correct date and time formats.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the incident response team for guidance.
*   Review the form for completeness before submitting it.
*   Make sure to save your progress if you need to exit the form before completing it.
