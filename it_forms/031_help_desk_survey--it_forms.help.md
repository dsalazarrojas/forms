# help_desk_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers to help us improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with your relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for the rating fields (e.g. very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **prophets** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the interaction with our company you are providing feedback on.
* **customer name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **customer email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your interaction with our company.
* **customer phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with our company (very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
* **comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback you have about your experience.
* **rating 2** (`rating_2`, select_one, optional): Another opportunity to rate your experience with our company (very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
* **rating 3** (`rating_3`, select_one, optional): Yet another opportunity to rate your experience with our company (very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
* **rating 4** (`rating_4`, select_multiple, optional): Rate multiple aspects of your experience with our company (e.g. very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
* **comment 2** (`comment_2`, note, optional): Provide additional feedback about your experience.
* **assigned date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you were assigned to someone.
* **due date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the date that was due.
* **completed date** (`completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date that was completed.
* **priority** (`priority`, text, optional): Enter the priority of your interaction with our company.
* **urgency** (`urgency`, text, optional): Enter the urgency of your interaction with our company.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Categorize your interaction with our company (urgent, not urgent).
