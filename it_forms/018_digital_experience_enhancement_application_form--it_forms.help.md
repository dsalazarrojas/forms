# Digital Experience Enhancement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect enhancement requests for digital experiences. Please use this form to submit your suggestions or proposals for improvements to our digital products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Go to the "Application Details" page and fill out your application title, project team, and request type.
2. On the "Project Team" page, select the team that best fits the project you are applying for.
3. On the "Request Type" page, choose the type of request you are making (e.g., feature enhancement, bug fix, etc.).
4. In the "Request Description" page, provide a brief description of the enhancement request.
5. On the "Request Urgency" page, indicate the level of urgency for the request.
6. In the "Contact" page, fill out your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
7. On the "Request Comments" page, provide any additional comments or information about the request.
8. Finally, review and submit the form to ensure that all required information is accurate and complete.
