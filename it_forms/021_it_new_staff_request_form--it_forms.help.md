# it_new_staff_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for new staff members, providing essential details for processing and onboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as *required*) with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to select the correct department and job category from the provided options.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New IT Staff Request Form** (`form_title`, text, not required): This is the title of the form, which you can leave blank if not applicable.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the new staff member's employer.
* **Employer Email** (`employer_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the new staff member's employer.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the new staff member's employer.
* **New Employer Info** (`new_employer_info`, date, not required): Enter the date of the new employer's information.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, not required): Enter the ID number of the new staff member.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter the first name of the new staff member.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter the last name of the new staff member.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter the job title of the new staff member.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, not required): Select the department of the new staff member from the options (IT Department, Marketing Department, Finance Department).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the new staff member's job.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, not required): Select the job category of the new staff member from the options (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the new staff member was hired.
* **Reason for Hire** (`reason_for_hire`, text, not required): Enter the reason for hiring the new staff member.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the new job from the options (Open, Closed, On Hold).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, not required): Enter the person assigned to manage this new staff member.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Enter any relevant comments or notes about the new staff member.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the user who submitted this form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, time, not required): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitting User** (`form_submitting_user`, text, not required): Enter the username of the user who submitted this form.
* **Form Submitting Date** (`form_submitting_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitting Time** (`form_submitting_time`, time, not required): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitting IP** (`form_submitting_ip`, text, not required): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted this form.
* **Form Submitting Browser** (`form_submitting_browser`, text, not required): Enter the browser used to submit this form.
* **Form Submitting OS** (`form_submitting_os`, text, not required): Enter the operating system used to submit this form.
