# IT Network Security Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Network Security Inspection Form is designed to help IT teams, auditors, and network administrators quickly assess and report on the security posture of their networks. This form provides a structured approach to identifying vulnerabilities, potential issues, and recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a single sitting, or save your progress and return to it later.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If a question is not applicable or doesn't fit your current network's situation, choose "Never" or indicate as such in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Network Inspection Form**
This section is the main body of the form where you will describe your network's current security posture and any observations or concerns.

*   **Network Inspection Status**: Choose the level of your network's current security posture, where:
    *   **High**: Indicates your network is secure with no significant issues.
    *   **Medium**: Suggests some minor issues or concerns that do not pose an immediate risk.
    *   **Low**: Implies that your network has several significant issues or concerns that need immediate attention.
*   **Network Inspection Frequency**: Choose the frequency with which your network inspections are performed, where:
    *   **Weekly**: Indicates regular, frequent inspections.
    *   **Monthly**: Suggests regular, scheduled inspections.
    *   **Quarterly**: Implies less frequent inspections but still within a set schedule.
    *   **Bi-Annually**: Means inspections are performed twice a year, possibly as needed.
    *   **Never**: Indicates that inspections have not been performed or are not part of your process.
*   **Network Inspection Comments**: Describe any specific concerns or observations you have about your network's security.
*   **Network Inspection Findings**: Document any security-related issues or problems you've encountered during your inspections.
*   **Network Inspection Recommendations**: Share your suggestions or recommendations for improving your network's security posture.
