# Virtual Meeting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a virtual meeting, allowing you to schedule and attend meetings with others over the internet. Fill out this form to initiate the meeting request process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the meeting details.
2. Choose the attendees you wish to invite.
3. Provide a brief description of the meeting's purpose.
4. Enter the email address and phone number of the attendees.
5. Optionally, provide the meeting location.
6. Assign the tools to be used during the meeting (e.g., chatjimmy).
7. Confirm your selection for the second assigned tool (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, select_one): Select the type of meeting you are requesting (Yes or No).
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_one): Choose the attendees you wish to invite (John Doe, Jane Doe, or Jim Doe).
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text): Provide a brief description of the meeting's purpose.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter the virtual meeting location (optional).
* **Attendees Email** (`attendees_email`, email): Enter the email address of the attendees (required).
* **Attendees Phone** (`attendees_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the attendees (required).
* **Meeting URL** (`meeting_url`, text): Enter the virtual meeting URL (optional).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Optionally, provide any additional notes about the meeting.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Assign the tool to be used during the meeting (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool2`, select_multiple): Optionally, select additional tools to be used during the meeting (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Assigned Tool 3** (`assigned_tool3`, select_one): Optionally, assign another tool to be used during the meeting (chatjimmy or Other).

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information when completing this form.
* Double-check your selections to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* Review your form submission before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
