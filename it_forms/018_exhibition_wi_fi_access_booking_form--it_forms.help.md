# Exhibition Wi Fi Access Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from exhibitors for Wi-Fi access bookings during exhibitions, conferences, trade shows, or expos. It will help event organizers and technical teams to prepare and configure Wi-Fi access for a seamless experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the type of event you are participating in (e.g., Conference, Trade Show, Expo).
2.  Select the areas where you would like to have Wi-Fi access (e.g., Conference Floor, Networking Area, Outside Area).
3.  Choose the start and end dates of your event.
4.  Enter your name and email address for communication purposes.
5.  Provide any additional information or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **exhibitor_wi_fi_access** (`text`, optional): This field is where you can enter a brief description or title of your exhibition or event.
* **device_details** (`text`, optional): If you have any specific device details or requirements for Wi-Fi access, please provide them here.
* **usage_agreement** (`text`, optional): This is a text field for you to agree or acknowledge the usage agreement for Wi-Fi access. Please type 'yes' to confirm your agreement.
* **event_details** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of event you are participating in. Options include Conferences, Trade Shows, Expos.
* **location** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the areas where you would like to have Wi-Fi access. Options include Conference Floor, Networking Area, Outside Area.
* **start_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the start date of your event. Please use the format dd/mm/yy or dd/mm/aaaa for dates in this format.
* **end_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the end date of your event. Please use the format dd/mm/yy or dd/mm/aaaa for dates in this format.
* **exhibitor_name** (`text`, optional): Enter your name as the exhibitor.
* **exhibitor_email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately.
- Be precise with the event dates and location details.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, please use the 'device_details' field to provide additional information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at [insert contact email or contact info].
