# Software And Device Needs Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Software And Device Needs Assessment form is used by the company to gather information about its current software and hardware usage as well as their needs. This information will help the IT department provide the best possible support to the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question in as much detail as possible
* Be accurate and thorough in your responses

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What software does the company currently use?** (`software_uses`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to list all the software that the company currently uses.
* **What hardware does the company currently use?** (`hardware_uses`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to list all the hardware that the company currently uses.
* **What software does the company need to install, update or remove?** (`software_needs`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to list any software that the company needs to install, update, or remove.
* **What hardware does the company need to purchase, update or remove?** (`hardware_needs`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to list any hardware that the company needs to purchase, update, or remove.
* **What departments or teams need software or hardware?** (`department_needs`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to specify which departments or teams need software or hardware.
* **Who is the primary contact person for this request?** (`contact_name`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the primary contact person for this request.
* **Email address of primary contact person** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone number of primary contact person** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Department of primary contact person** (`contact_department`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the department of the primary contact person.
* **Job title of primary contact person** (`contact_job_title`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the job title of the primary contact person.
* **Location of primary contact person** (`contact_location`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the location of the primary contact person.
* **Request reason** (`request_reason`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to specify the reason for the request.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate in your responses.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
* This form should only be filled out by company employees who are familiar with the company's current software and hardware inventory.
