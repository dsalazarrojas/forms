# Smart Lighting Calibration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lighting Calibration Consent Form is a document that requires users to provide their consent for smart lighting adjustments on their premises. It is used to gather essential information and authorization details for utility management or building maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If applicable, provide any additional contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
6. In the "Authorization" field, please provide any necessary authorization or approval for smart lighting adjustments.
7. Select your approval status in the "Approval Confirmation" field. You may choose from the following options: Approved, Not approved, Conditional approval, or Declined.
8. If necessary, provide any additional details about the service provider in the "Service Provider Details" field.
9. Lastly, provide any necessary details about the property manager in the "Property Manager Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Details** (note): If you have any additional contact details, please provide them here.
* **Authorization** (text): Please provide any necessary authorization or approval for smart lighting adjustments.
* **Approval Confirmation** (select_one): Select your approval status from the following options: Approved, Not approved, Conditional approval, or Declined.
* **Service Provider Details** (note): If necessary, provide any additional details about the service provider.
* **Property Manager Details** (note): If necessary, provide any additional details about the property manager.
