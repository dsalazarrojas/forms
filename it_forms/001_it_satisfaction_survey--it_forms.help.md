# IT Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the satisfaction of our users with our IT service. It will help us identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your satisfaction and experiences with our IT service.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "Any comments?" field to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with our IT service?** (`it_satisfaction`, select_one, required)
  - Please select "Yes" if you are satisfied with our IT service and "No" if not.
  - This field helps us understand the overall level of satisfaction with our service.
- **Rate our service from 1 to 5** (`it_rating`, number, required)
  - Choose a number between 1 and 5 to rate your experience with our service.
  - A higher number indicates a higher level of satisfaction.
- **What do you think we could improve?** (`it_comment`, note, optional)
  - Share your thoughts on what we could do better.
  - This helps us identify areas of improvement.
- **Your e-mail address** (`it_email`, email, required)
  - Please enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
  - Make sure to enter the correct email address associated with your account.
- **Your phone number** (`it_phone`, text, optional)
  - If you have a phone number associated with your account, you can enter it here.
  - This will help us reach out to you for further assistance if needed.
- **Which department did you interact with?** (`it_department`, select_multiple, required)
  - Select all departments that you interacted with during your visit.
  - This will help us understand which teams you had contact with.
- **When did you last interact with us?** (`it_date`, date, required)
  - Choose the date you last interacted with us.
  - This helps us track your last visit and identify trends.
- **At what time?** (`it_time`, time, required)
  - Select the time you last interacted with us.
  - This helps us understand the timing of your visit.
- **Any comments?** (`it_comments`, note, optional)
  - Share any additional comments or feedback you have.
  - This is an opportunity to express your thoughts and suggestions.
