# smart_equipment_systems_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "smart_equipment_systems_contact_form" is designed for clients to get in touch with the support team regarding smart equipment systems. This form allows clients to provide their contact information and details about their inquiry or issue, enabling the team to respond and assist promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, click the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. In the first field, "client_name", enter your name as the client or representative.
3. If applicable, provide your company's name in the "company" field.
4. Fill in your email address in the "email" field for contact purposes.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field for further communication.
6. Briefly describe the subject of your inquiry or issue in the "subject" field.
7. In the "description" field, provide a detailed description of your concern.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "team" field to indicate whether you are part of a team or not.
9. Fill in the name of the contact person in the "contact_person" field, if applicable.
10. Provide the contact person's email address in the "contact_email" field, if applicable.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact_phone" field, if applicable.
12. Lastly, choose a submission date for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the client or representative.
* **company** (Text, Optional): If you are contacting on behalf of a company, please provide its name.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Your email address for us to reach you.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Your phone number for further communication.
* **subject** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe the subject of your inquiry or issue.
* **description** (Text, Optional): A detailed description of your concern.
* **team** (Select Multiple, Required): Select "Yes" if you are contacting as part of a team; otherwise, select "No".
* **contact_person** (Text, Optional): If you are contacting on behalf of someone else, provide their name.
* **contact_email** (Email, Optional): If you are not the primary contact, provide their email address.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Optional): If you are not the primary contact, provide their phone number.
* **submission_date** (Date, Optional): Select a date for submission.

## Tips
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can effectively assist you.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer back to this guide for clarification.
- For urgent matters, please include your submission date to expedite our response.
