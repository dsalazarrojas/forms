# Domain support contact form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Domain support contact form is used to submit requests for support or assistance with your domain(s). Please use this form to provide detailed information about your domain(s) and the type of support you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your domain(s).
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Select the correct domain(s) that you are requesting support for.
* Provide a clear and concise message about the issue you are experiencing.
* If applicable, provide any additional details or attachments that may help us understand your support request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Domain Name**: Enter the name of your domain(s) that you are requesting support for. This will help us identify the issue and provide accurate assistance.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your support request. If you have an existing email address associated with your account, please ensure you use that email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. This is optional, but it will help us reach you more efficiently.
* **Message**: This is the main text area where you can describe the issue you are experiencing with your domain(s). Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your request.
* **Select Domain**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting support for a specific domain. This will help us identify the domain you are requesting assistance for.
* **Select Multiple Domains**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting support for multiple domains. This will help us understand that you are experiencing issues with multiple domains.
* **Select Domain (2)**: Select "Option A" or "Option B" to indicate the type of support you are requesting. This will help us understand your specific needs.
* **Select Domains**: Select multiple domains that you are requesting support for. This will help us understand that you are experiencing issues with multiple domains.

## Tips
* If you have already submitted a support request, please do not fill out this form again. Instead, reply to the existing request with any additional information or updates.
* Be as detailed as possible in the message field to help us understand your issue.
* If you have any attachments or files that may help us understand your issue, please attach them to the message field.
