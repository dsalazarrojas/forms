# phishing risk assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on phishing incidents and assess the risk level associated with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the department you belong to from the "What is your department?" field.
2.  Choose the frequency of phishing emails you have received from the "Frequency of Phishing Emails Received" field.
3.  Select the type of phishing email you received from the "What type of Phishing Email were you sent to?" field.
4.  Indicate whether you reported the phishing incident from the "Have you reported the Phishing Incident to our team?" field.
5.  If you reported the incident, select the person or team you reported it to from the "To whom have you reported the Phishing Incident?" field.
6.  If you did not report the incident, select why from the "Why did you not report the Phishing Incident?" field.
7.  Choose how you reported the incident from the "How did you report the Phishing Incident?" field.
8.  Leave a note about the incident in the "Comments" field.
9.  Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
10.  Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
11.  Enter your department ID in the "Department ID" field.
12.  Enter the location ID in the "Location ID" field.
13.  Enter the risk factor associated with the incident in the "Risk Factor" field.
14.  Enter the risk score of the incident in the "Risk Score" field.
15.  Choose who submitted the form from the "Submitted by" field.
16.  Enter the employee ID of the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted by Employee ID" field.
17.  Enter a justification for the risk factor in the "Risk Factor Justification" field.
18.  Enter an action plan to mitigate the risk in the "Action Plan" field.
19.  Enter any follow-up actions in the "Follow-up" field.
20.  Select the outcome of the incident from the "Outcome" field.
21.  Enter a justification for the outcome in the "Outcome Justification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your department?** (`risk_category`, Select One, required): Select the department you belong to from the options provided.
* **Frequency of Phishing Emails Received** (`frequency_of_phishing_emails_received`, Select Multiple, required): Choose the frequency of phishing emails you have received.
* **What type of Phishing Email were you sent to?** (`phishing_type`, Select One, required): Select the type of phishing email you received.
* **Have you reported the Phishing Incident to our team?** (`report_phishing_incident`, Select One, required): Indicate whether you reported the phishing incident.
* **To whom have you reported the Phishing Incident?** (`reported_to_who`, Select Multiple, required): If you reported the incident, select the person or team you reported it to.
* **Why did you not report the Phishing Incident?** (`reason_not_reported`, Select Multiple, required): If you did not report the incident, select why from the options provided.
* **How did you report the Phishing Incident?** (`reported_method`, Select One, required): Choose how you reported the phishing incident.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, required): Leave a note about the incident.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, Date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, Time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Department ID** (`department_id`, Number, required): Enter your department ID.
* **Location ID** (`location_id`, Number, required): Enter the location ID.
* **Risk Factor** (`risk_factor`, Number, required): Enter the risk factor associated with the incident.
* **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, Number, required): Enter the risk score of the incident.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, Select One, required): Choose who submitted the form.
* **Submitted by Employee ID** (`submitted_by_employee_id`, Number, required): Enter the employee ID of the person who submitted the form.
* **Risk Factor Justification** (`risk_factor_justification`, Note, required): Enter a justification for the risk factor.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, Note, required): Enter an action plan to mitigate the risk.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, Note, required): Enter any follow-up actions.
* **Outcome** (`outcome`, Select Multiple, required): Select the outcome of the incident.
* **Outcome Justification** (`outcome_justification`, Note, required): Enter a justification for the outcome.
