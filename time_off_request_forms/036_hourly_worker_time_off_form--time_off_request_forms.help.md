### Hourly Worker Time Off Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Hourly Worker Time Off Form is a request for time off for hourly workers. This form should be completed when an employee is requesting time off for a specific period.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed your schedule and discussed your request with your team lead or manager.
2. If you're certain that your time off request is valid, fill out the form with the required information:
	* Start Date: Enter the date you plan to take your time off.
	* End Date: Enter the date you plan to return to work after taking time off.
	* Days Off: Select the days you will be taking off from the list of available options.
	* Reason (optional): Provide a brief explanation for your time off request.
	* Notes (optional): Add any additional comments or details about your time off request.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_submit** (`form_submit`, number, required: false): This is the submission page of the form. You will submit your time off request here.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to take your time off. Format should be MM/DD/YYYY.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to return to work after taking time off. Format should be MM/DD/YYYY.
* **days_off** (`days_off`, select_one, required: true): Select the days you will be taking off from the available options:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **reason** (`reason`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for your time off request. This will help your team understand the context for your request.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or details about your time off request. 

### Tips
Please remember to review your team lead or manager's schedule as well to ensure your time off does not conflict with their availability.
