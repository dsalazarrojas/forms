# Request For Leave - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Request For Leave form is a tool for employees to formally request time off, including leave dates, reason, and other relevant details. This form helps to centralize leave requests, making it easier for everyone involved to stay informed and on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Request For Leave**: Enter a brief description of your leave request.
2. **Start Date**: Select the date you plan to start your leave.
3. **End Date**: Choose the date you plan to return to work.
4. **Reason**: Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request.
5. **Status**: Select the status of your leave request from the options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
6. **Comment**: Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
7. **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
8. **Contact Number**: Provide your contact phone number.
9. **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for handling your leave request (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe, Bob Smith).
10. **Category**: Choose a category for your leave request (e.g., Time Off Request Forms, Other Forms).
11. **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for your form.
12. **Form Title**: Enter a title for your form.
13. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to handle your leave request.
14. **Description**: Enter a brief description of your leave request.
15. **Category**: Choose a category for your leave request (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
16. **Subcategory**: Select a subcategory for your leave request.
17. **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for handling your leave request (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe, Bob Smith).
