# Exercise Program Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Exercise Program Time Off Form is used to request a break from the exercise program. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee request field with a brief description of your reason for the time off.
2. Select the start date of your time off from the calendar.
3. Select the start time of your time off.
4. Select the end date of your time off.
5. Select the end time of your time off.
6. Choose the type of exercise you would like to take a break from (e.g. Running, Biking, etc.).
7. Provide a reason for your time off (e.g. Injury, Illness, etc.).
8. If approved, select the person who will approve your time off.
9. Add any additional comments about your time off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Request** (employee_request, text, optional): A brief description of your reason for the time off.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, optional): The date you would like to start your time off.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, optional): The time you would like to start your time off.
* **End Date** (end_date, date, optional): The date you would like to end your time off.
* **End Time** (end_time, time, optional): The time you would like to end your time off.
* **Exercise Type** (exercise_type, select_one, optional): The type of exercise you would like to take a break from (e.g. Running, Biking, etc.).
* **Reason** (reason, text, optional): A description of your reason for the time off.
* **Approval Status** (approval_status, select_multiple, optional): The status of your request (e.g. Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Approved By** (approved_by, select_one, optional): The person who will approve your time off.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): Any additional comments about your time off.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a valid reason for your time off.
* Select the correct exercise type and dates for your time off.
* If approved, make sure to follow the instructions of the person who approved your time off.
