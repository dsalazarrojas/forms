# License Renewal Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees submit a request for time off for license renewal purposes. Please use this form to provide necessary details for processing and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, "License Renewal Details", provide a brief description of your license renewal request.
2. On the second page, "Contact Information", enter your name and other relevant contact details.
3. On the third page, "Additional Contact Information", provide any additional contact details that may be relevant for processing your request.
4. On the fourth page, "Manager Approval", confirm your manager's approval for your time off request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **License Renewal Details**: This is the page where you will provide a brief description of your license renewal request.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and other relevant contact details to ensure that we can reach you for further information or clarification.
* **Additional Contact Information**: If there's any additional contact information you'd like to share, please enter it here.
* **Manager Approval**: Confirm your manager's approval for your time off request.
