# Domestic Travel Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for requesting time off for domestic travel, such as vacations, business trips, or personal travel. It helps employees request and track approval for their time off, ensuring a smooth and efficient process for both employees and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Information (Page 1 of 1)**: Enter your employee details to provide your identity for travel approval purposes.
	* This field is mandatory and must be filled out before proceeding.
2. **Domestic Travel Dates**: Choose the dates of your domestic travel.
	* Make sure to select the correct dates to ensure accurate approval processing.
3. **Travel Type**: Select the reason for your travel.
	* Choose from: Vacation, Business, or Personal.
4. **Travel Destination**: Enter the location of your travel.
	* Provide a clear and complete address to ensure efficient processing.
5. **Travel Reason**: Describe your travel reason.
	* Provide a brief but thorough explanation.
6. **Total Duration**: Enter the total duration of your travel.
	* Enter the correct duration to facilitate accurate approval processing.
7. **Domestic Travel Cost**: Enter the domestic travel cost.
	* Provide the actual cost to help with expense management.
8. **Approval Status**: The system will automatically update this field once approved or denied.
	* This field is for internal use only.
9. **Comments (Optional)**: Add any comments or additional information.
	* Use this field if you have any further explanations or requests.
10. **Submitted Info**: The system will automatically populate this field once submitted.
	* This field is for internal use only.
11. **Approval Info**: The system will automatically update this field once approved or denied.
	* This field is for internal use only.

## Tips
- Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
- Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid delays or re-submission.
