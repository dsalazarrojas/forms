# Mining Industry Vacation Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a vacation or absence leave for mining industry employees. It is used to capture essential information for processing and approving the leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the employee information (Base Info Form).
2. Enter your Employee ID number.
3. Enter your First Name.
4. Enter your Last Name.
5. Select your Job Title.
6. For the Start Date and End Date, enter the dates you plan to be absent from work.
7. Enter the reason for your leave in the Reason for Leave field.
8. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Vacation, Sick Leave, or Emergency Leave).
9. Enter the number of leave days you will be taking.
10. Enter the Return Date, which is the date you will return to work.
11. Enter your Contact Number for the approver.
12. Enter your Manager's Name.
13. Enter your Manager's Email.
14. Enter your Manager's Phone number.
15. Enter the Company Name.
16. Enter your Department.
17. Enter your Manager's Department.
18. Enter your Manager's Title.
19. Select the Shift you will be on during your leave.
20. Enter the Break information.
21. Enter any additional Notes about your leave.
22. Select the Approval Status.
23. Enter the name of the person who will be Approving your request.
24. Enter the name of the person who Approved your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Info Form**: This is the employee information section. Fill out your Employee ID, First Name, Last Name, Job Title, and other relevant details.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique Employee ID number.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason For Leave**: Enter a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Leave Type**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (Vacation, Sick Leave, or Emergency Leave).
* **Leave Days**: Enter the number of leave days you will be taking.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you will return to work.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for the approver.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter the company name.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Manager Department**: Enter your manager's department.
* **Manager Title**: Enter your manager's title.
* **Shift**: Select the shift you will be on during your leave.
* **Break**: Enter the break information.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your leave.
* **Approval Status**: Select the approval status.
* **Approver**: Enter the name of the person who approved your request.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved your request.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately to avoid any delays in processing your leave request.
