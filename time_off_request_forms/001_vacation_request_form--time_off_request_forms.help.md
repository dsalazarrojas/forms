# Vacation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Request Form is used by employees to submit their vacation time off requests, including the start and end dates of their vacation, their name, job title, and reason for the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the `Employee Name` field.
2. Enter your job title in the `Job Title` field.
3. Enter the start date of your vacation in the `Start Date` field.
4. Enter the end date of your vacation in the `End Date` field.
5. Enter the length of your vacation in the `Vacation Length` field.
6. Enter the start date of your vacation in the `Vacation Start Date` field (this is likely a duplicate of the `Start Date` field, but we'll follow the form's structure).
7. Enter the end date of your vacation in the `Vacation End Date` field (this is likely a duplicate of the `End Date` field, but we'll follow the form's structure).
8. Enter your reason for requesting vacation in the `Reason For Request` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date (`start_date`, date, required: false):** Enter the date you wish to start your vacation.
* **End Date (`end_date`, date, required: false):** Enter the date you wish to end your vacation.
* **Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, required: false):** Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Job Title (`job_title`, text, required: false):** Enter your job title as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Vacation Length (`vacation_length`, number, required: false):** Enter the number of days you wish to take for vacation.
* **Vacation Start Date (`vacation_start_date`, date, required: false):** Enter the date you wish to start your vacation (this is likely a duplicate of the `Start Date` field).
* **Vacation End Date (`vacation_end_date`, date, required: false):** Enter the date you wish to end your vacation (this is likely a duplicate of the `End Date` field).
* **Reason For Request (`reason_for_request`, text, required: false):** Enter a brief description of why you are requesting vacation time off.
