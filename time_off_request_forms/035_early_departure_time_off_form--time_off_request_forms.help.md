# Early Departure Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting to leave work early, without needing to fill out a full vacation request form. You can submit this form when you want to take a shorter break during the day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the required fields (if any).
2. Ensure that your submission is accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Early Departure Time Off Form** (<label name="early_departure_time_off_form", <type>text, required>false): This form's title is for your reference. Please do not fill this field.
* **Start Time** (<label name="start_time", <type>time, required>false): Enter the time you plan to start early.
* **End Time** (<label name="end_time", <type>time, required>false): Enter the time you plan to leave early.
* **Reason** (<label name="reason", <type>text, required>false): Please provide a brief reason for your early departure request. This will help your manager understand your needs.
* **Manager Approval** (<label name="manager_approval", <type>select_one, required>false): Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your request, or 'No' if not.
* **Request Date** (<label name="request_date", <type>date, required>false): Enter the date of your request.

## Tips

* Be accurate and clear in your submission.
* Ensure your manager has enough information to approve your request.
* If your request is denied, do not submit another request without discussing with your manager first.
