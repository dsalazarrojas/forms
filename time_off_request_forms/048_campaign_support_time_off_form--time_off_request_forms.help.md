# Campaign Support Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campaign Support Time Off Form is a tool for staff members to submit time off requests for various reasons such as vacation, personal, or medical.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct leave type from the provided options.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of time off, if applicable.
4. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member submitting the time off request.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the staff member's job role or assignment in the campaign. This helps HR and campaign leadership to better understand the impact of the absence on team operations.
* **Start Date of Time Off** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the first day of the requested leave.
* **End Date of Time Off** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the last day of the requested leave.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave requested from the provided options: Vacation, Personal, Medical, or Other.
* **Reason for Time Off** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of time off, if applicable.
* **Coverage Plan** (`coverage_plan`, text, required): Describe how duties will be covered during the absence of the staff member.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager approving the time off request.
* **Contact While Away** (`contact_while_away`, select_one, optional): Select a preferred contact method during leave (Email, Phone, or No Contact).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any other notes for HR or campaign leadership.
 

## Tips
Please note that HR and campaign leadership will review and process time off requests in a timely manner. Ensure that you submit accurate and complete information to avoid delays in the processing of your request.
