# Religious Holiday Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Religious Holiday Time Off Form is used to submit requests for time off due to religious holidays. This form is meant to ensure that all necessary information is provided so that your time off request can be reviewed and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the relevant holiday from the list of available options.
3. Provide a valid start and end date for your time off period.
4. Specify the duration of your time off period.
5. Enter your contact information and your manager's contact information (if applicable).
6. Submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Religious Holiday Time Off Request** (Text): A brief description of why you are requesting time off for a religious holiday.
* **Employee Name** (Text): Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (Number): Your unique employee ID number.
* **Date Off** (Date): The date or dates you are requesting time off.
* **Time Off Reason** (Text): A brief explanation of why you need time off on this date(s).
* **Department** (Select One): The department you belong to.
* **Manager Approval** (Select Multiple):
	+ **Approve**: Your manager will approve your request.
	+ **Deny**: Your request will be denied.
	+ **Forward to Supervisor**: Your request will be forwarded to your supervisor for further review.
* **Time Off Start Date** (Date): The start date of your time off period.
* **Time Off End Date** (Date): The end date of your time off period.
* **Time Off Duration** (Text): The length of your time off period (e.g., days, weeks, months).
* **Employee Email** (Email): Your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (Text): Your work phone number.
* **Employee Address** (Text): Your work address.
* **Employee Location** (Text): Your location (e.g., building, floor).
* **Religious Holiday** (Select One): The holiday you are requesting time off for.
* **Time Off Requester Name** (Text): The name of the person making the time off request.
* **Employee Title** (Text): The title of the employee making the time off request.
* **Time Off Requester Email** (Email): The email of the person making the time off request.
* **Time Off Requester Phone** (Text): The phone number of the person making the time off request.
* **Department Manager** (Text): The name of your manager.
* **Manager Name** (Text): The name of the manager reviewing your request.
* **Manager Email** (Email): The email of the manager reviewing your request.
* **Manager Phone** (Text): The phone number of the manager reviewing your request.
* **Manager Location** (Text): The location of the manager reviewing your request.
* **Manager Title** (Text): The title of the manager reviewing your request.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form settings.
