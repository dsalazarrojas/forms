# Industry Conference Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for attending an industry conference. Please use this form to request a specific duration of time off for a known conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (Requester, Start Date, Duration, and Approved By).
2. Enter the requester's email address in the Requester Email field.
3. Enter the requester's phone number in the Requester Phone field.
4. Select the approver who will be reviewing your request in the Approved By field.
5. Enter the conference details in the Conference Name, Conference Location, and Conference URL fields.
6. Enter any additional notes about your conference attendance in the Notes field.
7. Fill in the Reason field with a brief description of why you are requesting time off for this conference.
8. Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester** (`requester`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your conference.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your conference.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of time you need off.
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required): Briefly describe why you are requesting time off for this conference.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `select_one`, required): Select the approver who will be reviewing your request.
* **Conference Name** (`conferencename`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the conference.
* **Conference Location** (`conf_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the conference.
* **Conference URL** (`conference_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the conference.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your conference attendance.
* **Conference Name** (`conferencename`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the conference you are attending.
