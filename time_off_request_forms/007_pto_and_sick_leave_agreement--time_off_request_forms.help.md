# pto_and_sick_leave_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request paid time off (PTO) or sick leave for an employee. It is an essential document that must be signed by the employee before the leave can be approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your Employee Name accurately and completely.
2.  Enter your Job Title (if applicable).
3.  Provide your Manager's Name.
4.  Select the Start Date and End Date for your leave.
5.  Provide a valid Reason for your leave (e.g., sick, family emergency, vacation, etc.).
6.  Check the Policy Acknowledgment box, indicating that you have read and agree to the company's paid time off policy.
7.  Enter your Agreement, which is your understanding of the company's paid time off policy and your commitment to abide by it.
8.  Finally, sign and select the appropriate option from the Signature dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employment records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you have a job title, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name as it appears on your employment records.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to begin your leave.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your leave.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a valid reason for your leave, such as sick, family emergency, vacation, etc.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, note, required): Check this box to indicate that you have read and agree to the company's paid time off policy.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required): Enter your understanding of the company's paid time off policy and your commitment to abide by it.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Select the appropriate option from the dropdown to indicate that you understand and agree to the company's paid time off policy.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
* Remember, this form is for requesting paid time off or sick leave. Do not use it for any other purpose.
