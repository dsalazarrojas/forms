# flexible_schedule_time_off_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Flexible Schedule Time Off Request Form" is designed for employees to request flexible time off in advance, providing a structured format for submitting their requests and allowing managers to review and approve or decline them.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
  - Select the desired "Schedule Start Date" and "Schedule End Date" for your time off.
  - Choose the "Schedule Duration" you require, which is the length of your time off.
  - Select your preferred "Schedule Frequency" from the options provided.
  - Choose your "Work Arrangement" for your time off, which is the type of schedule you require.
  - Provide the "Start Time" and "End Time" of your time off.
  - Enter the "Reason for Requesting" for your time off, this will help your manager understand your request.
  - Select the "Manager Approval" required for your request, which may involve multiple managers.
  - Optionally, select the "Approval Status" of your request, which will display as "Pending", "Approved", or "Declined".
  - You can also add any "Approval Comments" for the managers reviewing your request, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Start Date**: Enter the start date of your time off.
* **Schedule End Date**: Enter the end date of your time off.
* **Schedule Duration**: Select the number of days you want to be off.
* **Schedule Frequency**: Select how often you want your schedule to be flexible, e.g., "Flexible 1 day", "Flexible 3 days", or "Flexible 5 days".
* **Work Arrangement**: Choose your work arrangement for the time off, e.g., "Work From Home", "Flexible Schedule", or "Compressed Schedule".
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your time off.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your time off.
* **Reason for Requesting**: Enter a brief description of why you are requesting this time off.
* **Manager Approval**: Select who needs to approve your time off request, e.g., "Manager Approval", "Director Approval", or "HR Approval".
* **Manager Approval Status**: Select the status of your request, e.g., "Pending", "Approved", or "Declined".
* **Approval Comments**: Add any comments for the managers reviewing your request, if needed.
