# Personal Time Off Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Time Off Request Form is designed to help employees submit their requests for personal time off. This form should be used when you need to take a vacation day, sick leave, or other types of personal absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") as accurately as possible.
2. Provide as much detail as you can in the optional fields.
3. Review your submission for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time Off Request** (id: 1, label: Time Off Request, required: false):
	+ This field is where you should indicate the nature of your time off request (e.g., vacation, sick leave, bereavement).
* **Start Date** (id: 2, label: Start Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you plan to start your time off from work.
* **End Date** (id: 3, label: End Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you plan to return to work after your time off.
* **Time Off Reason** (id: 4, label: Time Off Reason, required: false):
	+ Provide a brief explanation for your time off request (e.g., personal reasons, family emergency, medical reasons).
* **Work Location** (id: 5, label: Work Location, required: false):
	+ If your work location is different from your regular workplace, enter the location where you will be during your time off (e.g., home, another office, etc.).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have already checked with your manager or HR department before submitting this form.
- If you have any supporting documents or evidence, attach them to your form submission for additional context.
