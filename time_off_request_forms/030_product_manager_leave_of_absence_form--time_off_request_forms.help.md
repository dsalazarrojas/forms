# Product Manager Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting time-off requests for product managers to take a leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details in the **product_manager_leave_of_absence_form_page** field.
2. Provide your manager's details in the **manager_details** field.
3. Specify the **start_date** and **end_date** of your leave.
4. Enter the **reason_for_leave** in the **reason_for_leave** field.
5. Indicate the **time_off_request_duration** and select the **time_off_request_type** and **time_off_request_frequency** from the respective dropdowns.
6. Select the **time_off_request_status** and enter the **time_off_request_start_time** and **time_off_request_end_time** in the respective fields.
7. Optionally, add additional notes in the **time_off_request_note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Manager Leave Of Absence Form Page** (`product_manager_leave_of_absence_form_page`): Enter your product manager details in this field.
* **Manager Details** (`manager_details`): Enter your manager's details, including their name and title, in this field.
* **Time Off Request** (`time_off_request`): Enter a brief description of your time-off request.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Select the start date of your leave using the date picker.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Select the end date of your leave using the date picker.
* **Reason For Leave** (`reason_for_leave`): Enter the reason for your leave.
* **Time Off Request Duration** (`time_off_request_duration`): Enter the duration of your leave.
* **Time Off Request Type** (`time_off_request_type`): Select the type of time-off you are requesting (e.g., vacation, sick).
* **Time Off Request Frequency** (`time_off_request_frequency`): Select the frequency of your time-off requests (e.g., daily, weekly).
* **Time Off Request Status** (`time_off_request_status`): Select the status of your time-off request (e.g., open, approved, denied).
* **Time Off Request Start Time** (`time_off_request_start_time`): Enter the start time of your leave using the time picker.
* **Time Off Request End Time** (`time_off_request_end_time`): Enter the end time of your leave using the time picker.
* **Time Off Request Note** (`time_off_request_note`): Add any additional notes regarding your time-off request.
