# shift_realignment_time_off_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shift realignment time-off request form is used to request a shift realignment for employees. This form is used to inform the manager of the request and to ensure that the necessary information is provided to process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee name to identify yourself as the employee making the request.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' if your manager has approved or denied your request.
3. Enter the shift start date you would like your new shift to begin.
4. Enter the shift end date you would like your new shift to end.
5. Enter a brief reason for requesting this shift realignment.
6. Enter the specific shift realignment request (e.g., shift start time, shift end time, or shift length).
7. Enter any comments that your manager may need to consider when reviewing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Time Off Request**: Enter a brief description of your request.
  * This field is optional but helps the manager understand the request.
- **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
  * This field is required to identify you as the employee making the request.
- **Manager Approvals**: Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your request, or 'No' if they have denied it.
  * This field is used to track the status of your request.
- **Shift Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your new shift to start.
  * This field is used to specify the start date of your new shift.
- **Shift End Date**: Enter the date you would like your new shift to end.
  * This field is used to specify the end date of your new shift.
- **Reason for Request**: Enter a brief description of the reason for your request.
  * This field is optional but helps the manager understand the request.
- **Shift Realignment Request**: Enter the specific shift realignment request (e.g., shift start time, shift end time, or shift length).
  * This field is optional but helps the manager understand the request.
- **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments that your manager may need to consider when reviewing your request.
  * This field is optional but helps the manager understand your request better.
- **Shift Start Time**: Enter the specific start time of your new shift.
  * This field is used to specify the start time of your new shift.
- **Shift End Time**: Enter the specific end time of your new shift.
  * This field is used to specify the end time of your new shift.
- **Shift Length**: Enter the length of your new shift.
  * This field is used to specify the duration of your new shift.
- **Shift Overlap**: Enter the number of shifts that overlap with your new shift.
  * This field is used to specify the number of shifts that overlap with your new shift.
- **Shift Realignment Request**: This field is the main request for shift realignment and is optional but helps the manager understand the request better.
