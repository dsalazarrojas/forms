# Internal Review Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a time off from work. It's used to submit a formal request for a specific period of absence from the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your job title to ensure that your request is associated with your correct work role.
2. Enter the start date of your time off, ensuring that it's in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Provide a clear reason for your time off, as this will help with the review process.
4. Check whether you have any meetings or events that you need to attend during your time off.
5. Indicate whether your time off will be paid or unpaid.
6. Confirm whether your time off is for vacation or personal reasons.
7. If you have any comments or notes, please provide them in the text box.
8. If you have additional information, you can provide it in the text box.
9. Specify the duration of your time off in days.
10. Enter the end date of your time off, ensuring that it's in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): Select your correct job title to ensure that your time off request is associated with your work role.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your time off in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Reason for Time Off** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a clear explanation for your time off request, which will help with the review process.
* **Meetings or Events** (`has_meeting_or_event`, select_one, required): Check whether you have any meetings or events that you need to attend during your time off.
* **Paid Time Off** (`paid_off`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your time off will be paid or unpaid.
* **Vacation or Personal Reasons** (`vacation_or_personal`, select_one, required): Confirm whether your time off is for vacation or personal reasons.
* **Comments or Notes** (`comments`, note, required): If you have any comments or notes, provide them in this text box.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): If you have any additional information, provide it in this text box.
* **Time Off Duration** (`time_off_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of your time off in days.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your time off, in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-31).
