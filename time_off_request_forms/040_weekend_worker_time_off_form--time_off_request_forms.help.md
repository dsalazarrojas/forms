# Weekend Worker Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekend Worker Time Off Form is used to record and request time off for weekend work, allowing you to specify your start and end times, work hours, and provide any additional notes or information for the approver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Weekend Work Start Time, which is the time you plan to start your weekend work.
2. Enter your Weekend Work End Time, which is the time you plan to finish your weekend work.
3. Specify the total number of Weekend Work Hours you plan to work.
4. Add any additional Notes you'd like to share with the approver.
5. Provide your Submitter Info, which is your name and contact information.
6. Enter the Approver Info, which is the name and contact information of the person responsible for approving your time off request.
7. Finally, enter your Manager Info, which is the name and contact information of your supervisor or manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekend Work Start Time** (weekend_work_start_time, number, required): Enter the time you plan to start working on the weekend. Please note that the format of this field is a number, which we assume represents the time in hours or minutes.
* **Weekend Work End Time** (weekend_work_end_time, number, required): Enter the time you plan to finish working on the weekend. Similar to the start time, this field is expected to be a number representing hours or minutes.
* **Weekend Work Hours** (weekend_work_hours, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you plan to work on the weekend. This field should also be a number.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details you'd like to share with the approver. This field is not required but can be helpful for context.
* **Submitter Info** (submitter_info, note, optional): Enter your name and contact information, which will be used to contact you regarding your time off request.
* **Manager Info** (manager_info, note, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your supervisor or manager, who will be reviewing and approving your time off request.
* **Approver Info** (approver_info, note, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for approving your time off request.
