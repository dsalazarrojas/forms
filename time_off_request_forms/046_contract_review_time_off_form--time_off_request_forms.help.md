# Contract Review Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Review Time Off Form is used for employees to request time off from work, allowing them to take breaks and recharge. This form is used by management to review and approve requests based on business needs and contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** and **Employee ID** fields.
2. Choose your **Department** from the list of available options.
3. Enter your **Position Title** and **Request Date** (the date you plan to take time off).
4. Select the **Time Off Type** (e.g., Vacation, Sick leave, etc.).
5. Enter your **Start Date** and **End Date** (the dates you will be taking time off).
6. Enter the **Total Days Requested** for your time off.
7. Enter your **Return Date** (when you will return to work).
8. Provide a brief **Reason for Request** for your time off.
9. List any **Active Contracts** you are working on during your time off.
10. Choose the **Contract Review Status** of your contracts (e.g., all reviews complete, some reviews pending, etc.).
11. Check if there are any **Pending Deadlines** during your time off.
12. Enter **Coverage Arrangements** for your tasks and responsibilities during your absence.
13. Enter any **Handover Notes** for your colleagues or manager.
14. Check if you have any **Urgent Matters** to address during your time off.
15. Check if you have **Client Notifications** set up.
16. Choose your **Availability During Time Off** (e.g., completely unavailable, available for emergencies only, etc.).
17. Enter your **Emergency Contact Method** (if applicable).
18. Enter the **Manager Approval** (if applicable).
19. Enter the **Approval Status** of your request (e.g., approved, pending, denied, etc.).
20. Add any **Additional Comments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department from the list of available options.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`): Enter your job title.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date you are requesting time off.
* **Time Off Type** (`time_off_type`): Select the type of leave (e.g., vacation, sick leave, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the first day of your time off.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the last day of your time off.
* **Total Days Requested** (`total_days_requested`): Enter the number of days you are requesting time off.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the date you will return to work.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`): Enter a brief explanation for your request.
* **Active Contracts** (`active_contracts`): List any active contracts you are working on during your time off.
* **Contract Review Status** (`contract_review_status`): Choose the status of your contracts (e.g., all reviews complete, some reviews pending, etc.).
* **Pending Deadlines** (`pending_deadlines`): Check if there are any deadlines during your time off.
* **Coverage Arrangements** (`coverage_arrangements`): Enter arrangements for your tasks and responsibilities during your absence.
* **Handover Notes** (`handover_notes`): Enter any handover notes for your colleagues or manager.
* **Urgent Matters** (`urgent_matters`): Check if you have any urgent matters to address during your time off.
* **Client Notifications** (`client_notifications`): Check if you have notified your clients.
* **Availability During Time Off** (`availability_during_time_off`): Choose your availability during your time off (e.g., completely unavailable, available for emergencies only, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Method** (`emergency_contact_method`): Enter your emergency contact method (if applicable).
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Enter your manager's name (if applicable).
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Enter the current status of your request (e.g., approved, pending, denied, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth review process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR representative.
* Please review your form before submitting it to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
