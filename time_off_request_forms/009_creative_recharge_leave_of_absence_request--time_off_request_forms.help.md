# Creative Recharge Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Creative Recharge Leave Of Absence Request form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (`Introduction`, `note`, not required)
* The introduction page serves as a brief overview of the form. Read through it to understand the purpose of the form.
* You can add any additional notes or comments to this page if necessary.

### Employee Details (`employee details`, `text`, not required)
* This section is for providing your employee details, such as your name and employee ID.
* You can add any relevant information about yourself in this section.

### Request Type (`request type`, `select one`, not required)
* Select the type of leave you are requesting:
	+ Leave: A general leave of absence for any reason.
	+ Recharge: A creative recharge, which is a type of leave specifically for recharging your creative energies.

### Start Date (`start date`, `date`, not required)
* This is the date you plan to start your leave.
* Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### End Date (`end date`, `date`, not required)
* This is the date you plan to end your leave.
* Use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Reason (`reason`, `text`, not required)
* This section is for providing a brief reason for your leave request.
* Keep it concise and clear, as it will help your team lead and manager understand your request.

### Manager Approval (`manager approval`, `select multiple`, not required)
* Select the manager(s) you would like to approve your leave request:
	+ Approve: Approve the request.
	+ Decline: Decline the request.

### Team Lead Approval (`team lead approval`, `select multiple`, not required)
* Select the team lead(s) you would like to approve your leave request:
	+ Approve: Approve the request.
	+ Decline: Decline the request.

### Coverage (`coverage`, `text`, not required)
* This section is for providing information about coverage arrangements during your leave.
* You can add any relevant details about who will cover your responsibilities while you're away.

### Comments (`comments`, `text`, not required)
* This section is for any additional comments or questions you have about your leave request.
* Keep it brief and clear.

### Submit (`submit`, `text`, not required)
* Once you've filled out all required fields, click the submit button to send your request.
* Please review your request carefully before submitting.

### Back to Top (`back to top`, `text`, not required)
* If you have any questions or need clarification, click this button to return to the top of the form.
* You can review and edit your form as needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any duplication or miscommunication.
* If you're unsure about any part of the process, don't hesitate to ask your team lead or manager for clarification.
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
