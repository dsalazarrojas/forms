# Night Shift Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Shift Time Off Request Form is used to request time off from night shifts for various reasons such as vacation, sick leave, or holidays. It helps managers to approve or reject these requests and for employees to provide relevant information and reasons for their absences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the necessary information such as Employee Name, Job Title, Department, and Employee ID.
2. Choose the Shift type, which can be Day Shift, Night Shift, or Other.
3. Select the Time Off Type, which can be Vacation, Sick Leave, or Holiday.
4. Enter the date when you want to take time off.
5. Specify the start and end time of your time off.
6. Enter a brief Reason for your time off.
7. If required, get approval from your manager.
8. Add any comments or notes if necessary.
9. Review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department you belong to as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): If applicable, enter your manager's name for reference.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Select the shift type for which you are requesting time off, which can be Day Shift, Night Shift, or Other.
* **Time Off Type** (`time_off_type`): Select the type of time off you are requesting, which can be Vacation, Sick Leave, or Holiday.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date when you want to take time off.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of your time off.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of your time off.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Enter a brief reason for your time off.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): If required, select Approve, Reject, or Pending for manager's approval.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Add any comments or notes you think are necessary for your manager.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Add any comments or notes from your manager if required.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Select the shift for which you are requesting time off, which can be Day Shift, Night Shift, or Other.
* **Time Off Type** (`time_off_type`): Select the type of time off you are requesting, which can be Vacation, Sick Leave, or Holiday.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click on this button to submit your request.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`): If you want to cancel your request, click on this button.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and clear when providing information and reasons for your time-off request.
* Make sure to follow your company's policies and procedures for requesting time off.
* If you are unsure about any information or procedures, consult with your HR department or supervisor.
