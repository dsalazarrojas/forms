# Extended Weekend Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request extended time off for a weekend period. The form requires the following information:

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to fill out this form.
2. Enter your Employee ID number.
3. Select the start and end dates of your requested time off.
4. Indicate if you will be taking weekends off during your time off period.
5. Specify the number of work hours you will need to take off.
6. If necessary, enter any comments about your request.
7. Indicate if you will be submitting this request yourself.
8. Identify who will be approving this request.
9. Indicate your manager's level of involvement in the approval process.
10. Identify the approval level required for this request.
11. If necessary, enter a comment from your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your time off period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your time off period.
* **Weekends Off** (`weekends_off`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you will be taking weekends off during your time off period.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of work hours you will need to take off.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If necessary, enter any comments about your request.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you will be submitting this request yourself.
* **Approver** (`approver`, select_one, optional): Identify who will be approving this request.
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your manager will be involved in the approval process.
* **Manager Level** (`manager_level`, select_one, optional): Select if your manager's approval level is High or Low.
* **Approval Level** (`approval_level`, select_one, optional): Select the approval level required for this request.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): If necessary, enter a comment from your manager.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for assistance.
