# days_off_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "days_off_request_form" is used to submit a request for time off, such as leaves of absence or personal time off, to be reviewed and approved by management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Employee Request" field and enter a brief description of your time off request.
2. Select the "Start Date" field and enter the date you would like your time off to begin.
3. Select the "End Date" field and enter the date you would like your time off to end.
4. Choose the "Request Type" field and select one of the options: "Leave of absence", "Personal time off", or "Other".
5. If required, enter the number of days you are requesting for time off in the "Number of Days Off" field.
6. In the "Comments" field, add any additional information or context about your time off request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Request** (employee_request, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your time off request.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, optional): The date you would like your time off to begin.
* **End Date** (end_date, date, optional): The date you would like your time off to end.
* **Request Type** (request_type, select_one, required): Choose from the options: "Leave of absence", "Personal time off", or "Other".
* **Number of Days Off** (days_off, number, optional): The number of days you are requesting for time off. (Note: only required if you have selected "Other" as your request type.)
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Add any additional information or context about your time off request.
