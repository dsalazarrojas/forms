# Hybrid Work Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Work Time Off Form is designed to capture information for hybrid work time off requests, allowing employees to submit their time off requests with details such as date, reason, and duration of the off-period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the employee's ID in the **Employee ID** field.
3. Enter the date you wish to take off in the **Date** field.
4. Enter the time you wish to take off in the **Time** field.
5. Select the reason for your time off in the **Reason** field.
6. Select the type of request you are submitting (e.g., Time Off for Personal/Family Events or Time Off for Business Reasons) in the **Request Type** field.
7. Enter the start date of your time off period in the **Start Date** field.
8. Enter the duration of your time off period in the **Duration** field.
9. Select the status of your request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected) in the **Status** field.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes from your manager in the **Manager's Notes** field.
11. Finally, submit the form by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date**: Enter the date you wish to take off.
* **Time**: Enter the time you wish to take off.
* **Reason**: Enter the reason for your time off.
* **Request Type**: Select the type of request you are submitting.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your time off period.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your time off period.
* **Status**: Select the status of your request.
* **Manager's Notes**: Enter any comments or notes from your manager.
* **Manager's Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager's E-Mail**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager's ID**: Enter your manager's ID.
* **Approver ID**: Enter the ID of the person approving your request.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your own name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your own ID.
* **Form Submitted**: This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.
* **Manager's Approvals**: Select the approval status of your request (Approve, Do Not Approve, Pending).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from your manager.
