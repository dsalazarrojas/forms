# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is designed for employees to submit their requests for time off from work, ensuring that essential information is gathered to facilitate a smooth approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to provide accurate contact details and specific dates for your time off.
2. Enter the time intervals you are requesting off in the designated field.
3. Add any additional comments or notes about your request if necessary.
4. Review your submission before hitting the "Submit" button to ensure everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, required): Please enter your name and contact information so that your manager can get in touch with you regarding your request.
* **Dates** (`dates`, optional): Enter the specific dates you are requesting off. If you are requesting multiple dates, separate them with commas (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday).
* **Time Intervals** (`time_intervals`, required): Enter the time intervals you are requesting off (e.g., morning, afternoon, entire day).
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): If you have any additional information or context about your request, please provide it here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required): Review and submit your request to complete the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to check with your manager before submitting your request if you are unsure about any specific dates or time intervals.
* Keep your comments brief and concise to facilitate easy review.
* Submitting multiple requests for the same dates and time intervals will likely cause delays in the processing of your initial request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department or manager.
