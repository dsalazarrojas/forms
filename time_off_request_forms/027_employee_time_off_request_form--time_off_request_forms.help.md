# Employee Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Time Off Request Form is used to submit a time off request to your approver for review and approval. This form will help your approver understand your request and make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your Time Off Requestor from the dropdown list.
2. Fill out the Submitter section with your information.
3. Enter your Start Date and End Date.
4. Choose the number of Hours Offered you need.
5. Provide a reason for your time off request.
6. Add any additional Comments if needed.
7. Select your Approver from the dropdown list.
8. Enter your Approver's Email and Phone Number.
9. Choose the approver's Department and Job Title.
10. Enter your Approver's Time Off Request Form.
11. Choose the Approver Submit Form option.
12. Select your Submitter Approver.
13. Enter your Submitter Approver's Email and Phone Number.
14. Enter your Submitter Approver's Job Title.
15. Choose the Approver Submit option.
16. Select your Approver Rejected option (if your request is denied).
17. Enter your Submitter Status.
18. Enter your Submitter Status Text.
19. Enter your Submitter Status Date and Time.
20. Enter your Submitter Status Comments.
21. Enter your Approver Status.
22. Enter your Approver Status Text.
23. Enter your Approver Status Date and Time.
24. Enter your Approver Status Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time Off Requestor**: The person submitting the time off request. 
* **Submitter**: The information of the person submitting the request, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Start Date**: The date when the time off will start.
* **End Date**: The date when the time off will end.
* **Hours Offered**: The number of hours the employee needs off.
* **Reason**: The reason for the time off request.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or explanation for the request.
* **Approver**: The person or department responsible for reviewing and approving the request.
* **Approver Email**: The email address of the approver.
* **Approver Phone**: The phone number of the approver.
* **Approver Department**: The department of the approver.
* **Approver Job Title**: The job title of the approver.
* **Approver Time Off Request Form**: The time off request form filled out by the approver.
* **Submitter Approver**: The information of the person the employee has designated as their approver.
* **Submitter Approver Email**: The email address of the employee's approver.
* **Submitter Approver Phone**: The phone number of the employee's approver.
* **Submitter Approver Job Title**: The job title of the employee's approver.
* **Approver Submit**: A confirmation of the approver's approval or denial of the request.
* **Approver Rejected**: A confirmation of the approver's denial of the request.
* **Submitter Status**: The current status of the time off request.
* **Submitter Status Text**: A brief explanation of the status.
* **Submitter Status Date**: The date the status was updated.
* **Submitter Status Time**: The time the status was updated.
* **Submitter Status Comments**: Any additional comments on the status.
* **Approver Status**: The current status of the time off request.
* **Approver Status Text**: A brief explanation of the status.
* **Approver Status Date**: The date the status was updated.
* **Approver Status Time**: The time the status was updated.
* **Approver Status Comments**: Any additional comments on the status.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Contact your approver or HR representative if you have any questions or need clarification.
* Save a copy of this form for your records before submitting it to your approver.
