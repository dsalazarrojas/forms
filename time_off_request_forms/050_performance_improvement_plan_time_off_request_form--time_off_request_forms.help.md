# Performance Improvement Plan Time Off Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request time off for employees. It captures the details of the request, including the employee's name, dates of leave, reason for the request, and other relevant information. Before submitting this form, please ensure that you have discussed and received approval from your manager for the time off request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with the requested information.
2. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
3. Ensure that you have attached any supporting documentation or evidence as required by your manager or organization's policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your official personnel records or ID badge.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of your requested time off. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required): Enter the end date of your requested time off. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/15/2024).
* **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, not required): Provide a brief explanation for your time off request. This can include details about any planned activities, appointments, or emergencies.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your manager or the person to whom you report.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, not required): Enter your manager's email address. Please use the standard email format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, not required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or details about your time off request.
* **Approved Status** (`approved_status`, select_one, not required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ Pending
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who approved or denied your time off request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have discussed and received approval from your manager before submitting this form.
* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
* Attach any supporting documentation or evidence as required by your manager or organization's policies.
* If you are unsure about any field or requirement, please consult with your HR or manager for clarification.
