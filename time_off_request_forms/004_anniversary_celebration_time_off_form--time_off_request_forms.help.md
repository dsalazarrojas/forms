# Anniversary Celebration Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Anniversary Celebration Time Off Form" is a document used to request time off for anniversary celebrations, which is a company-wide policy to acknowledge and appreciate employees' years of service. This form helps employees to provide necessary information for their leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name.
2. Enter your Employee ID.
3. Select your department.
4. Enter the company anniversary date you are celebrating.
5. Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
6. Enter the dates you would like to take off.
7. Enter the total number of work days you are requesting.
8. Enter the name of your reporting manager.
9. (Optional) Provide a handover or coverage plan, in case you are unable to work on your tasks.
10. (Optional) Describe how you plan to celebrate your anniversary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required): Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Select the department you belong to from the provided options.
* **Company Anniversary Date** (`anniversary_date`, required): Enter the date your company anniversary is celebrated.
* **Number of Years with Company** (`years_of_service`, required): Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Leave Start Date** (`start_date`, required): Enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`end_date`, required): Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Total Work Days Requested** (`total_days`, required): Enter the total number of work days you are requesting.
* **Reporting Manager Name** (`manager_approval_name`, required): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Handover or Coverage Plan** (`coverage_plan`, optional): If you are unable to work on your tasks, provide a plan for handover or coverage.
* **Anniversary Celebration Plans** (`celebration_plans`, optional): Describe how you plan to celebrate your anniversary.

## Tips
- Ensure you have reviewed the company's leave policy and understand your eligibility before completing this form.
- Double-check the dates and details you enter to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any doubts or questions, contact your HR department for clarification.
