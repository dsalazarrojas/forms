# Physical Therapy Time Off Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by physical therapists to request time off for leave, vacation, or other personal health reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID to identify yourself as the physical therapist requesting time off.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your time off request.
3. Select the reason for your time off request (Leave, Vacation, or Sick).
4. Provide details about your time off request, including start and end times, duration, frequency, and recurrence start and end dates.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id**: Enter your unique employee ID number to identify yourself as the physical therapist requesting time off.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start your time off.
* **End Date**: Enter the date your time off will end.
* **Time Off Reason**: Select the reason for your time off request from the options provided (Leave, Vacation, or Sick).
* **Time Off Request Type**: Select the type of time off you are requesting (Leave, Vacation, or Sick).
* **Approved**: This field is not required, but you may need to check this box if your request is approved.
* **Reason for Denial**: If your request is denied, enter a reason for the denial here.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your time off request.
* **Time Off Request**: Enter the duration of your time off request.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your time off request.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your time off request.
* **Duration**: Enter the total duration of your time off request.
* **Frequency**: Select the frequency of your time off request (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Recurrence Start**: Enter the date you would like to start your recurring time off requests.
* **Recurrence End**: Enter the date you would like to end your recurring time off requests.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your time off request.
