# Half Day Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Half Day Time Off Request Form is used to request a half-day time off from work. This form is intended for employees who need to take a short break or have a valid reason for being absent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the date you want to start your half-day time off in the "Start Date" field.
3. Select the date you want to end your half-day time off in the "End Date" field.
4. Provide a valid reason for your half-day time off in the "Reason" field.
5. Choose your manager's approval by selecting "Approve" or "Reject" in the "Manager Approval" field.
6. Optionally, add any additional comments for your manager in the "Manager Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (`employee_name`, text, required: false)**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Start Date (`start_date`, date, required: false)**: Select the date you want to start your half-day time off.
* **End Date (`end_date`, date, required: false)**: Select the date you want to end your half-day time off.
* **Reason (`reason`, text, required: false)**: Provide a valid reason for your half-day time off. This could be a personal or family emergency, a doctor's appointment, or any other valid reason.
* **Manager Approval (`manager_approval`, select_one, required: true)**: Choose whether your manager approves or rejects your request.
* **Manager Comment (`manager_comment`, text, required: false)**: Optionally, add any additional comments for your manager to review.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in your request.
* Do not submit duplicate requests.
* Wait for a few days for your manager's approval or rejection.
