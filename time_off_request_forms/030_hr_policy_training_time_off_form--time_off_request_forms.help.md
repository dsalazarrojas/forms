# HR Policy Training Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Training Time Off Form is a simple form used to request time off for training purposes. This form is used by employees to submit their requests for approved training programs or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic contact information: Enter your employee name and department to ensure that we have accurate contact information.
2. Select the date(s) you would like to take off: Enter the start and end dates of the time period you would like to take off.
3. Specify your job title and department: Enter your job title and the department you belong to.
4. Provide the reason for your time off request: Enter the reason for your time off request in brief.
5. Request status and submit: Select the status of your request and submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time Off Request Form** (`time_off_request_form`):
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter a brief description of your time off request. (Note: This field is not required, but is recommended to give a brief overview of your request.)
* **Start Date** (`start_date`):
    * Type: Date
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter the start date of your time off period. (Note: This field is not required, but should be filled if you are requesting a specific date or range of dates.)
* **End Date** (`end_date`):
    * Type: Date
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter the end date of your time off period. (Note: This field is not required, but should be filled if you are requesting a specific date or range of dates.)
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`):
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter your employee name.
* **Department** (`department`):
    * Type: Select One
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Select the department you belong to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`):
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter your job title.
* **Reason** (`reason`):
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter the reason for your time off request in brief.
* **Status** (`status`):
    * Type: Select Multiple
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Select the current status of your request.
*   *   **Submit** (`submit`):
        * Type: Select One
        * Required: Not Required
        * Purpose: Select this option to submit your request for review.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: Enter any comments or additional information about your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`):
    * Type: Text
    * Required: Not Required
    * Purpose: This field is not used in the current implementation.
