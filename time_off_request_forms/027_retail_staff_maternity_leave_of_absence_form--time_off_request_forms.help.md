# retail_staff_maternity_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for retail staff members to request a maternity leave or absence due to other reasons. It is used to submit a formal request to the management, providing necessary details and approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Employee Name and Manager's Name in the respective fields.
2. Select the Leave Date and End Date from the calendar.
3. Choose the Reason for Leave (Maternity or Other).
4. Enter the Return Date.
5. Specify the number of Days of Absence.
6. Select whether to Approve or Reject the request.
7. Optionally, add Comments, Employee Confirmation, Manager Confirmation, Manager Signature, Manager Note, and Employee Note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee.
* **Manager**: Enter your manager's name who will review your request.
* **Leave Date**: Select the date you would like to start your leave.
* **End Date**: Select the date you expect to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave**: Choose the reason for your leave as 'Maternity' or 'Other'.
* **Return Date**: Enter the expected return date after your leave.
* **Days of Absence**: Specify the number of days you need to be off from work.
* **Manager Approval**: Select 'Approve' or 'Reject' to confirm or deny your request.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments regarding your request (optional).
* **Employee Confirmation**: Confirm that you are submitting this request (select 'True').
* **Manager Confirmation**: Confirm whether you have received approval or rejection (select 'True' or 'False').
* **Manager Signature**: Add your manager's signature (optional).
* **Manager Note**: Add a note for your manager to reference your request (optional).
* **Employee Note**: Add a note for your reference (optional).
* **Approval Status**: This field will display the status of your request (will be filled in automatically after manager approval).

Note: This form is designed for employees to submit formal requests for maternity leave or absence due to other reasons. Ensure that you accurately fill out the form and follow the necessary steps to avoid any delays or issues with your request.
