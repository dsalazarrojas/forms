# Client Leave Of Absence Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify clients of their leave of absence. It's a crucial step in ensuring that clients are aware of their absence and any necessary preparations or arrangements can be made.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the "Client Name" field with the client's full name.
2. Enter the "Advisor Name" field with the name of the advisor responsible for the client.
3. Provide details in the "Leave Start Date" and "Anticipated Return Date" fields.
4. Select the primary reason for the absence in the "Primary Reason for Absence" field.
5. Answer "Will you be reachable during this period?" with one of the options provided in the "Will you be reachable during this period?" field.
6. If necessary, provide urgent contact information and/or additional comments in the "Urgent Contact Information during absence" and "Additional Comments or Instructions" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Advisor Name**: Enter the name of the advisor responsible for the client.
* **Leave Details**: This section is a space for any additional details regarding the leave of absence.
* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the date the client will be starting their leave.
* **Anticipated Return Date**: Enter the date the client plans to return from their leave.
* **Primary Reason for Absence**: Select the primary reason for the client's absence from the options provided.
* **Will you be reachable during this period?**: Answer with one of the options provided:
	+ Yes - Email Only
	+ Yes - Phone and Email
	+ No
* **Urgent Contact Information during absence**: If necessary, provide contact information for urgent matters during the client's absence.
* **Are there any pending items that require immediate attention?**: If there are any urgent matters that require immediate attention, please provide details in this field.
* **Designated contact person for your accounts (if applicable)**: If there are multiple contacts, enter the designated person's name.
* **Additional Comments or Instructions**: Provide any additional comments or instructions for the advisor or other relevant parties.
