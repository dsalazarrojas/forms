# Public Health Information Credibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Information Credibility Survey aims to gather information about public health sources and individual trust levels associated with those sources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

* Select the sources where you get most of your health information (1)
* Indicate how often you use these sources (2)
* Choose how confident you are in the information from these sources (3)
* Identify the type of information you usually seek from these sources (4)
* Rate your trust level for each source (5)
* If you don't trust a source, provide a brief explanation (6)
* If you don't trust a source, tell us why (7)
* If you don't trust a source, list the reasons why (8)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Health Information Source (1)**: Select all the sources where you get most of your health information (e.g., Health News, Health Information, Medical Literature, Social Media, etc.).
 
- **How Often (2)**: Indicate how often you use these sources of health information (e.g., 1-2 times per year, Daily, Monthly, etc.).
 
- **How Confident (3)**: Choose how confident you are in the information from these sources (e.g., Not at all, Slightly, Somewhat, Very, Extremely).
 
- **Information Type (4)**: Identify the type of information you usually seek from these sources (e.g., Health News, Health Information, Medical Literature, etc.).
 
- **Trust Level (5)**: Rate your trust level for each source (e.g., No Trust, Low, Moderate, High, Extremely High).
 
- **Information Source (6)**: If you don't have a source for a specific item, please leave this field blank.
 
- **Why (7)**: If you don't trust a source, provide a brief explanation for why you don't trust it.
 
- **Reasons (8)**: If you don't trust a source, list the specific reasons why.
