# Digital Therapy Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Digital Therapy Program Feedback Survey form, explaining each field and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
5. **Rating**: Rate your experience with the program, using the scale provided.
6. **Satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the program, using the scale provided.
7. **Comments**: Please provide any comments about what you liked most about the program.
8. **What did you like most about the program?**: Provide any comments about what you enjoyed most about the program.
9. **What did you like least about the program?**: Provide any comments about what you did not enjoy most about the program.
10. **How likely are you to recommend the program?**: Choose one of the provided options to indicate your level of likelihood to recommend the program.
11. **Do you have any comments or suggestions?**: Please provide any comments or suggestions about your experience.
12. **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?**: Provide any suggestions about how the program could be improved.
