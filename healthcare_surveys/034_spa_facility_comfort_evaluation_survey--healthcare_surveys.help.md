# Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience at a spa facility. It aims to evaluate the overall comfort and quality of the spa, including the staff, services, and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the required information.
2. Answer the questions based on your actual experience at the spa facility.
3. Select the options that best describe your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spa Facility Comfort** (`spa_facility_comfort_form`): This field is meant to be filled in with a brief description of your experience at the spa facility. Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.
* **Overall Comfort** (`overall_comfort`): This question asks about the overall comfort level of your experience. Select 'Yes' if you felt comfortable throughout the visit, and 'No' if you did not.
* **Relaxation Area** (`relaxation_area`): This question asks you to rate the relaxation area of the spa. Choose the option that best describes your experience, based on the ambiance, quietness, and overall atmosphere of the area.
* **Ambiance** (`ambiance`): This field is meant to be filled in with a brief description of the ambiance you experienced at the spa facility.
* **Spa Staff Friendliness** (`spa_staff_friendliness`): This question asks if the spa staff were friendly and welcoming during your visit. Select 'Yes' if they were, and 'No' if not.
* **Spa Facility Cleanliness** (`spa_facility_cleanliness`): This question asks about the cleanliness of the spa facility. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Spa Facility Layout** (`spa_facility_layout`): This question asks you to rate the layout of the spa facility. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Spa Facility Equipment** (`spa_facility_equipment`): This question asks about the equipment and facilities available at the spa. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Spa Facility Services** (`spa_facility_services`): This question asks about the variety and quality of services offered by the spa. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Spa Facility Staff Skills** (`spa_facility_staff_skills`): This question asks about the skills and expertise of the spa staff. Choose the option that best describes your experience.

Note: This form is for evaluation purposes only. Your feedback will help us improve and provide better services to our clients. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
