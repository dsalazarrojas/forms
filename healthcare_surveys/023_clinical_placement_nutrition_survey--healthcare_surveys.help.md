This form is designed for healthcare professionals, specifically dietitians and nutrition students, to report their experiences during a clinical placement in a healthcare setting. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the quality of the placement, including the support and resources provided, the effectiveness of learning objectives, and the overall satisfaction of the students. This form is meant to help evaluate and improve the clinical placement program in healthcare settings.

This form is divided into several sections, each addressing a specific aspect of the clinical placement experience. Here is a detailed explanation of each section:

## Clinical Placement Nutrition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the quality of clinical placement experiences for healthcare professionals and students in nutrition-related fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Clinical Placement Nutrition Survey**
2. **Student Name**: This field is for you to provide your name as a student participating in the clinical placement. This is optional, but it helps the administrators to identify your responses.
3. **Student ID**: This field is for you to provide your ID number or student ID. This is also optional.
4. **Placement Site Name**: In this field, please enter the name of the hospital, clinic, or facility where you completed your clinical placement.
5. **Department or Unit**: Select the department or unit you were placed in during your clinical experience. Choose one or more of the following options:
	* Inpatient Nutrition
	* Outpatient Clinic
	* Pediatric Nutrition
	* Renal Nutrition
	* Oncology Nutrition
	* Diabetes Education
	* Eating Disorders
	* Community Nutrition
	* Food Service Management
6. **Placement Start Date**: Enter the start date of your clinical placement.
7. **Placement End Date**: Enter the end date of your clinical placement.
8. **Preceptor Name**: This field is for you to report the name of your preceptor, who provided guidance and support during your placement.
9. **Preceptor Support**: Rate the level of support provided by your preceptor. Choose one of the following:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
10. **Preceptor Availability**: Rate the availability of your preceptor. Choose one of the following:
	* Always Available
	* Usually Available
	* Sometimes Available
	* Rarely Available
	* Never Available
11. **Learning Objectives Met**: Rate how well the learning objectives of your clinical placement were met. Choose one of the following:
	* Exceeded Expectations
	* Fully Met
	* Mostly Met
	* Partially Met
	* Not Met
12. **Hands-On Experience**: Rate your hands-on experience during the clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
13. **Patient Interactions**: Report the number of patient interactions you had during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Many - Daily
	* Several - Few Times Per Week
	* Moderate - Once Per Week
	* Few - A Few Total
	* None
14. **Nutrition Assessments**: Report the number of nutrition assessments you performed during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* 20+
	* 11-20
	* 6-10
	* 1-5
	* None
15. **Care Plans Developed**: Report the number of care plans you developed during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* 10+
	* 6-10
	* 3-5
	* 1-2
	* None
16. **Interdisciplinary Exposure**: Rate your exposure to an interdisciplinary team during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Extensive
	* Moderate
	* Limited
	* None
17. **Team Members Worked With**: Select all the team members you worked with during your clinical placement. You can choose one or more of the following:
	* Physicians
	* Nurses
	* Pharmacists
	* Social Workers
	* Physical Therapists
	* Occupational Therapists
	* Speech Therapists
	* Psychologists
	* Other Dietitians
	* Food Service Staff
18. **Documentation Training**: Rate the quality of documentation training you received during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
	* Did Not Receive
19. **Resource Availability**: Rate the availability of resources during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
20. **Feedback Quality**: Rate the quality of feedback you received during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Excellent - Timely and Constructive
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair - Infrequent
	* Poor - Not Helpful
21. **Confidence Improvement**: Report how much your confidence improved during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Significantly Improved
	* Moderately Improved
	* Slightly Improved
	* No Change
	* Decreased
22. **Skills Improved**: Select all the skills you improved during your clinical placement. You can choose one or more of the following:
	* Nutrition Assessment
	* Care Plan Development
	* Patient Counseling
	* Medical Nutrition Therapy
	* Documentation
	* Critical Thinking
	* Communication
	* Time Management
	* Interdisciplinary Collaboration
	* Other
23. **Challenges Faced**: Select all the challenges you faced during your clinical placement. You can choose one or more of the following:
	* None
	* Time Management
	* Complex Cases
	* Documentation System
	* Communication
	* Confidence
	* Knowledge Gaps
	* Workload
	* Preceptor Availability
	* Other
24. **Site Environment**: Rate the quality of the site environment during your clinical placement. Choose one of the following:
	* Excellent
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
25. **Would Recommend**: Report whether you would recommend this clinical placement to other students. Choose one of the following:
	* Definitely Yes
	* Probably Yes
	* Neutral
	* Probably No
	* Definitely No
26. **Best Aspect**: In this field, provide any additional comments about the best aspect of your clinical placement experience.
27. **Areas for Improvement**: In this field, provide any additional comments about what could be improved in the clinical placement program.
28. **Additional Comments**: In this field, provide any additional comments about your clinical placement experience.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and detailed answers to each question to help evaluate and improve the clinical placement program.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the program administrators.
* This form is for your benefit, and your feedback will help improve future clinical placements.
