# Food Insecurity In Urban Areas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Insecurity In Urban Areas Survey is designed to gather information on households' experiences with food insecurity, focusing on income, size, accessibility, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. The form is divided into sections with the following fields:

1. **Introduction**: A brief explanation of the survey and its purpose.
2. Household income and size.
3. Experiences with food scarcity.
4. Access to food sources.
5. Food coping mechanisms.
6. Use of food benefits.
7. Outreaches for food assistance.
8. Access to food pantries.
9. Use of food stamps.
10. Other food sources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **household_income** (Number): Please enter your household's income per month.
- **household_size** (Number): Enter the number of people in your household.
- **food_scarcity** (Select One): Please select one of the following:
	+ Option 1: Always struggle to access food.
	+ Option 2: Sometimes struggle to access food.
	+ Option 3: Never struggle to access food.
- **food_accessibility** (Select Multiple): Please select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Food markets or grocery stores.
	+ Option 2: Food pantries or soup kitchens.
	+ Option 3: Food banks or food assistance programs.
- **food_coping** (Select Multiple): Please select all that apply:
	+ Option 4: Reduce food spending.

	+ Option 6: Other (please specify).
