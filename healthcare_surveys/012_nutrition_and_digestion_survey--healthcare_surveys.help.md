# Nutrition And Digestion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to gather information about your eating habits, digestive health, symptoms, medical conditions, medications, and supplements. Your input will help us better understand your nutritional needs and provide more accurate guidance on maintaining optimal health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. For the "Digestive Health" question, select one of the options provided.
3. If you have any medical conditions, medications, or supplements, provide detailed information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Info (Page 1)
• **Survey Info** (`survey_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief title or heading that identifies this survey.

### Diet Info (Page 1)
• **Diet Info** (`diet_info`, text, optional): Describe your typical daily diet, including the types of food you eat, your eating schedule, and any dietary restrictions.

### Hydration Info (Page 1)
• **Hydration Info** (`hydration_info`, text, optional): Share details about your hydration habits, including how much water or other fluids you consume daily.

### Digestive Health (Page 2)
• **Digestive Health** (`digestive_health`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describes your overall digestive health:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Symptoms (Page 2)
• **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any digestive symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as bloating, constipation, or diarrhea.

### Medical Conditions (Page 3)
• **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your digestive health, please describe them.

### Medications (Page 3)
• **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.

### Supplements (Page 3)
• **Supplements** (`supplements`, text, optional): Share details about any nutritional supplements you are currently taking, including type and dosage.

# Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
- This survey will only take a few minutes to complete.
