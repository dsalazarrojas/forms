# health_information_sharing_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals' preferences regarding the sharing of their health information. It aims to gather data on what type of health information should be shared with whom, how often, and any concerns or preferences individuals may have about sharing their data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. For the "Health Information Sharing Preferences" field, simply enter your preferences in the provided text box.
3. For the "Data Sharing Purpose" field, select one or more of the options that best describe the reasons why you would share your health information.
4. In the "Data Sharing Frequency" field, select the frequency with which you would like to share your health information.
5. For the "With Whom" field, choose the individuals or organizations you would like to share your health information with.
6. In the "Health Data" field, select the types of health data you would like to share.
7. For the "Sharing Preferences" field, choose how you would like to share your health information.
8. If you have any concerns or other preferences not listed, enter them in the "Data Sharing Concerns" field.
9. Finally, if you would like to specify a different purpose for sharing your health information, enter it in the "Other (please specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Information Sharing Preferences** (`Health Information Sharing Preferences`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your personal preferences regarding sharing health information. Describe your preferences in this box.
* **Data Sharing Purpose** (`Data Sharing Purpose`, select one, required): This field asks about the reasons why you would share your health information. Select one or more of the options that best describe your purpose.
	+ General Health Information: This option is for general health information sharing, such as medical history or wellness goals.
	+ Healthcare and Insurance: This option is for sharing health information with healthcare providers or insurance companies.
	+ Wellness and Prevention: This option is for sharing health information related to wellness and prevention, such as exercise routines or nutrition plans.
	+ Other (please specify): This option is for any other reasons not listed.
* **Data Sharing Frequency** (`Data Sharing Frequency`, select multiple, optional): This field asks about how often you would like to share your health information. Select one or more of the options that best describe your frequency.
	+ Once: This option is for sharing health information once, such as when you are diagnosed with a condition.
	+ Several times: This option is for sharing health information on multiple occasions, such as regular check-ups or progress updates.
	+ Always: This option is for sharing health information continuously, such as through a health app.
	+ Never: This option is for not sharing health information at all.
* **With Whom** (`With Whom`, select multiple, optional): This field asks about the individuals or organizations you would like to share your health information with. Select one or more of the options that best describe your preferences.
	+ Healthcare Providers: This option is for sharing health information with healthcare providers, such as doctors or nurses.
	+ Insurance Companies: This option is for sharing health information with insurance companies.
	+ Family and Friends: This option is for sharing health information with family members or close friends.
	+ Other (please specify): This option is for any other individuals or organizations not listed.
* **Health Data** (`Health Data`, select multiple, required): This field asks about the types of health data you would like to share. Select one or more of the options that best describe your preferences.
	+ Personal Data (e.g., medical history): This option is for sharing personal health information, such as medical history or allergies.
	+ Family Data (e.g., family medical history): This option is for sharing family health information, such as family medical history or genetic information.
	+ Medical Data (e.g., lab results, medications): This option is for sharing medical data, such as lab results or medications.
	+ Treatment Data (e.g., treatment plans, diagnoses): This option is for sharing treatment plans or diagnoses.
* **Sharing Preferences** (`Sharing Preferences`, select multiple, required): This field asks about how you would like to share your health information. Select one or more of the options that best describe your preferences.
	+ Share with healthcare providers: This option is for sharing health information with healthcare providers.
	+ Share with insurance companies: This option is for sharing health information with insurance companies.
	+ Share with family and friends: This option is for sharing health information with family members or close friends.
	+ Keep private: This option is for not sharing health information with anyone.
* **Data Sharing Concerns** (`Data Sharing Concerns`, text, optional): This field is for entering any concerns or preferences not listed in the previous fields. Enter any concerns or preferences you have about sharing your health information.
* **Other (please specify)** (`Other (please specify)`, text, optional): This field is for specifying any other reasons for sharing your health information that are not listed. Enter your reason in this box.
