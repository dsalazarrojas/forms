# Clinical Evaluation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinical Evaluation Feedback form is designed to collect information about the clinical evaluation process, allowing evaluators to assess its quality, fairness, and usefulness. This form helps evaluators identify areas for improvement and provide constructive feedback to ensure better evaluation processes in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name or ID (if desired) and email address (if desired).
2. Choose the clinical setting where the evaluation took place (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Testing center, Educational institution, Research facility, or Other).
3. Select the type of evaluation that occurred (e.g., Patient care, Student performance, Staff competency, Program quality, Treatment outcome, or Other).
4. Evaluate the clarity of the evaluation process by selecting one of the following options:
	* Strongly Disagree
	* Disagree
	* Neutral
	* Agree
	* Strongly Agree
5. Assess the fairness and objectivity of the evaluation by selecting one of the following options:
	* Strongly Disagree
	* Disagree
	* Neutral
	* Agree
	* Strongly Agree
6. Rate the professionalism and respect of the evaluator by selecting one of the following options:
	* Strongly Disagree
	* Disagree
	* Neutral
	* Agree
	* Strongly Agree
7. Evaluate the usefulness of the feedback by selecting one of the following options:
	* Strongly Disagree
	* Disagree
	* Neutral
	* Agree
	* Strongly Agree
8. Assess the clarity and specificity of the feedback by selecting one of the following options:
	* Strongly Disagree
	* Disagree
	* Neutral
	* Agree
	* Strongly Agree
9. Select one of the following options to indicate your overall satisfaction with the evaluation:
	* Very Dissatisfied
	* Dissatisfied
	* Neutral
	* Satisfied
	* Very Satisfied
10. Indicate your confidence in the evaluation results by selecting one of the following options:
	* Strongly Disagree
	* Disagree
	* Neutral
	* Agree
	* Strongly Agree
11. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
12. If desired, provide positive feedback on what went well during the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name or ID**: This field is optional and allows you to identify yourself as the participant.
* **Email Address**: This field is optional and provides a contact address for further correspondence.
* **Date of Evaluation**: This is the date when the evaluation took place.
* **Clinician/Evaluator Name**: Please fill in the name of the clinician or evaluator who conducted the evaluation.
* **Clinical Setting**: Select the location where the evaluation occurred (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Testing center, Educational institution, Research facility, or Other).
* **Type of Evaluation**: Choose the type of evaluation that occurred (e.g., Patient care, Student performance, Staff competency, Program quality, Treatment outcome, or Other).
* **Evaluation Process was Clearly Explained**: Rate the clarity of the evaluation process.
* **Evaluation was Fair and Objective**: Rate the fairness and objectivity of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator was Professional and Respectful**: Rate the professionalism and respect of the evaluator.
* **Feedback was Clear and Constructive**: Rate the usefulness and specificity of the feedback.
* **Feedback was Useful for Improvement**: Rate the usefulness of the feedback.
* **Feedback Included Specific Examples**: Rate the feedback for its clarity and specificity.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Evaluation**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the evaluation.
* **I am Confident in the Evaluation Results**: Rate your confidence in the evaluation results.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Positive Feedback**: Share positive feedback on what went well during the evaluation.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas for improvement in the evaluation process.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Use specific examples when providing feedback or comments.
* Provide clear and concise answers to rate your satisfaction with each aspect of the evaluation.
* If you're unsure or have no opinion, select the "Neutral" option.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
