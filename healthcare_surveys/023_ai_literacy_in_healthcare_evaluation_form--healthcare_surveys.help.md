# AI Literacy In Healthcare Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your level of AI literacy and familiarity in the healthcare sector. It will help us evaluate your current understanding and comfort level with AI-related concepts, applications, and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section and answer the questions based on your real-life experience and knowledge.
2. Select the correct options for each question, and provide as much detail as possible for open-ended questions.
3. Make sure to provide honest and accurate answers to ensure a reliable evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification or professional registration.
* **Healthcare Professional Role** (`job_role`, `select_one`, required): Select the role you currently hold in the healthcare sector (e.g., Physician / Specialist, Registered Nurse / Practitioner, Medical Technician / Radiographer, etc.).
* **Department / Unit** (`department_unit`, `text`, required): Indicate the department or unit where you work or the area you are most familiar with (e.g., Radiology, Oncology, Intensive Care).
* **Years of Clinical Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been working in the clinical field.
* **Familiarity with AI Termology** (`ai_term_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Answer how familiar you are with general AI terminology, such as Deep Learning, Neural Networks, and NLP.
* **Frequency of AI Tool Usage** (`clinical_ai_usage_freq`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how often you use AI tools in your current clinical practice (e.g., Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Daily).
* **AI Applications** (`ai_application_types`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the AI applications you have encountered or used in your clinical practice (e.g., AI-assisted Diagnostic Imaging, Predictive Risk Analytics, Clinical Decision Support Systems, etc.).
* **Comfort with AI-Assisted Decision Making** (`comfort_decision_making`, `number`, required): Answer on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is Very Uncomfortable and 5 is Very Comfortable, how you feel about AI-assisted clinical decision making.
* **Perceived Benefits of AI** (`perceived_benefits`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the primary benefits of AI in your field (e.g., Increased Diagnostic Accuracy, Reduced Administrative Burden, Early Detection of Diseases, etc.).
* **Primary Concerns with AI** (`primary_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your primary concerns regarding AI in healthcare (e.g., Patient Data Privacy & Security, Algorithmic Bias & Inequality, Liability & Accountability for Errors, etc.).
* **Interest in Advanced AI Literacy Training** (`training_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would be interested in participating in advanced AI literacy training (e.g., Yes, Definitely, Yes, if provided during work hours, etc.).
* **Open-Ended Questions** (`open_feedback_training`, `text`, optional): You can provide any specific AI topics you would like to learn more about.
* **Agreement with AI Impact** (`agreement_quality_impact`, `select_one`, required): Answer how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement that "AI will significantly improve the quality of patient care in your department over the next 5 years."
* **Date of Evaluation** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this evaluation.

Note: This form is for internal use only and should not be filled out for any other purpose. Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure a reliable evaluation.
