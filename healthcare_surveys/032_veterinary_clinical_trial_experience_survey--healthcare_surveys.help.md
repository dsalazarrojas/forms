# Veterinary Clinical Trial Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Clinical Trial Experience Survey is designed to collect information about veterinary clinical trials, helping us to better understand their characteristics, scope, and funding sources. This data will aid in the quality assurance and monitoring of clinical trials in the veterinary healthcare sector.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, please ensure you have all necessary information about the clinical trial at hand.
- Select the "Clinical Trial ID" field, where you should enter the unique identifier of the clinical trial.
- For "Pet Owner Survey", select all that apply from the list, indicating whether pet owners were involved in the survey or not.
- In "Study Design", please provide a brief description of the clinical trial's design.
- If applicable, select "Study Inclusion" if the trial includes pet owners or others as participants.
- In "Study Exclusion" select all that apply, indicating any participants that were excluded from the study.
- Enter the duration of the clinical trial in "Study Duration" using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Select the phase of the clinical trial from "Clinical Trial Phase".
- In "Veterinary Clinical Trial Site", select all applicable locations where the clinical trial took place.
- Enter the name of the investigator(s) responsible for the clinical trial in "Clinical Trial Investigator".
- In "Pet Owners Participation", select all that apply, indicating the participation level of pet owners.
- If applicable, enter the fees associated with the clinical trial in "Clinical Trial Fees".
- Select the source of the funding for the study from "Study Funding Source".
- Enter the amount of funding allocated to the clinical trial in "Study Funding Amount".
- Select the currency of the funding from "Study Funding Currency".
- In "Veterinary Clinical Trial Institution", select all applicable locations where the clinical trial took place.

* **Clinical Trial ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the clinical trial. This is a required field and should be a distinct identifier for easy tracking and reference.

* **Study Design**: A brief description of the clinical trial's design. This helps in understanding the scope and methodology of the trial.

* **Clinical Trial Phase**: Select the phase of the clinical trial. This is a required field as it is essential for categorizing the trial's stage.
* **Veterinary Clinical Trial Site**: Select all that apply indicating locations where the clinical trial took place.
* **Clinical Trial Investigator**: Enter the name(s) of the investigator(s) responsible for the clinical trial. This is an optional field but helps in tracking trial leadership.
* **Pet Owners Participation**: This field tracks the participation level of pet owners. Select all that apply from the options.
* **Clinical Trial Fees**: If applicable, enter the fees associated with the clinical trial. This could include any expenses or charges related to the trial.

* **Study Funding Amount**: Enter the amount allocated for the clinical trial. This is an optional field but helps in assessing the trial's financial scope.
* **Study Funding Currency**: Select the currency of the funding. This is an optional field but helps in understanding the financial backing of the trial.
