# Health AI Explainability Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from healthcare professionals on their awareness and understanding of AI in the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review each field carefully and fill in the required information. You can select multiple options where applicable. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Comments" field. If you have already participated in this survey before, please let us know in the "Form Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (Form Submission, Note, Optional): This field is for indicating whether you have already submitted a similar survey. Please select "Yes" if you have, so we can avoid duplicating your input.
* **Patient Info** (Patient Info, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select all that apply to indicate your level of understanding of AI in the healthcare industry.
* **AI Understanding** (AI Understanding, Number, Optional): This field is for rating how well you understand how AI works. Please select a number between 0 and 10, where 0 is "no understanding" and 10 is "very clear understanding".
* **AI Impact** (AI Impact, Select One, Optional): Select how much impact you think AI has on your healthcare practice, with options being Significant, Somewhat Significant, Not Significant, or Other (if you have a different opinion).
* **Survey Conducted** (Survey Conducted, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select how often you participate in surveys about AI, with options being Every Time, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Trust In AI** (Trust In AI, Select One, Optional): Select your level of trust in AI, with options being Very High, High, Medium, Low, or None.
* **Concerns About AI** (Concerns About AI, Select Multiple, Optional): Please select all that apply to indicate your concerns about AI in the healthcare industry, with options being Data Privacy, Bias, or Other (if you have a different concern).
* **Other Concerns** (Other Concerns, Text, Optional): If you have any additional concerns not listed above, please specify them here.
* **Comments** (Comments, Note, Optional): Any additional comments you may have about AI or this survey.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Date, Date, Optional): Please enter the date you are submitting this survey.
