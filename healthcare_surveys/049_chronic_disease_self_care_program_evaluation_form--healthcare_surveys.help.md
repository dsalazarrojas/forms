# Chronic Disease Self Care Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Self Care Program Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about your experiences and outcomes with the program. Your responses will help us understand how the program is performing and where it can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please complete the form in a quiet and comfortable setting, free from distractions and influences.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* For select-one fields, choose the option that best describes your experience.
* For select-multiple fields, select all that apply.
* If a field is not applicable to your situation, choose the "Not applicable" option.
* For text fields, provide a brief but clear answer.
* If you need help or clarification, contact the program administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**
	+ **Participant ID**: A unique identifier for the participant.
	+ **First Name**: Your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Your last name.
	+ **Email Address**: Your email address for contact and follow-up.
* **Program Details**
	+ **Program Name**: The name of the program you participated in.
	+ **Program Enrollment Date**: The date you enrolled in the program.
	+ **Duration of Participation (weeks)**: The number of weeks you participated in the program.
* **Knowledge and Skills**
	+ **How Much Did Your Knowledge Improve?**: Choose how much your knowledge about self care improved.
	+ **New Self-Care Skills Acquired**: Select all new skills you learned from the program.
* **Behavior Changes**
	+ **Have You Made Lifestyle Changes?**: Choose how much you changed your lifestyle.
	+ **Dietary Improvements Made**: A brief description of any dietary improvements you made.
	+ **Exercise or Physical Activity Improvements**: A brief description of any exercise or physical activity improvements.
* **Health Outcomes**
	+ **Ability to Manage Symptoms**: Choose how well you can manage your symptoms.
	+ **Quality of Life Improvement**: Choose how much your quality of life improved.
	+ **Healthcare Visits (fewer visits is better)**: Choose how many healthcare visits you made compared to before.
* **Program Satisfaction**
	+ **Overall Program Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you were with the program.
	+ **Was the Program Relevant to Your Needs?**: Choose how relevant the program was to your needs.
	+ **Would You Recommend This Program?**: Choose if you would recommend the program to others.
* **Suggested Improvements**
	+ **Suggested Program Improvements**: A brief description of any improvements you would suggest for the program.

Please complete this form carefully and honestly, and don't hesitate to ask if you need help or clarification.
