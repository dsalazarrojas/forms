# Healthcare Knowledge Sharing Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to help us better understand how healthcare staff share knowledge and collaborate with each other in the workplace. The survey aims to collect information on your experiences, challenges, and suggestions for improving our knowledge-sharing culture. Your responses will be kept anonymous and confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an * (asterisk).
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, please use the "Additional Note" field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
