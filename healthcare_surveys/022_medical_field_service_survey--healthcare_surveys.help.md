# Medical Field Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Field Service Survey is a feedback form designed to collect information from patients about their recent interactions with a medical field service. This survey will help us understand the level of satisfaction and quality of care provided by our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have recently interacted with our medical field service.
2. Answer the survey questions accurately and honestly.
3. Provide your provider's name and your own name, if applicable.
4. Rate your experience using the rating score field.
5. Share any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, not required):
This is the main section where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the medical field service.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, not required):
Please enter the name of the healthcare provider who assisted you during your interaction with our service.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required):
If you're submitting the survey on behalf of someone else, please enter the patient's name.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number, not required):
Rate your experience using a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 means "very poor" and 10 means "excellent".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required):
Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the medical field service.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can better understand your experience.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the comments field.
* You can rate your experience based on the quality of care, communication, and overall satisfaction with our service.
