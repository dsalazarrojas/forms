# COVID 19 Customer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Customer Consent Form is a mandatory form that must be completed by customers visiting a healthcare facility during the pandemic. This form helps the facility assess the customer's health status, understand their purpose of visit, and ensure they are aware of and agree to follow safety protocols and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**:
	* Customer Name: Enter your full legal name.
	* Email Address: Provide a valid email address for communication.
	* Phone Number: Enter your best contact number.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
	* Address: Enter your complete street address.
	* City: Enter your city of residence.
	* State: Enter your state or province.
	* ZIP Code: Enter your postal code.
	* Visit Date: Enter the date of your visit.
2. **Select relevant answers**:
	* Purpose of Visit: Choose why you are visiting the facility.
	* Health Screening: Indicate if you have any COVID-19 symptoms.
	* Current Symptoms: Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing.
	* Known Exposure: Indicate if you have been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
	* Recent Positive Test: Indicate if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
	* Vaccination Status: Indicate your COVID-19 vaccination status.
3. **Acknowledge and agree**:
	* Risk Acknowledgment: Confirm you understand COVID-19 risks.
	* Safety Protocols Agreement: Agree to follow safety protocols.
	* Mask Requirement: Agree to wear a mask.
	* Social Distancing: Agree to maintain 6 feet distance.
	* Hand Hygiene: Agree to sanitize your hands.
	* Visitor Limit: Understand that visitors may be limited.
	* Capacity Limitations: Understand that services may be delayed.
	* Liability Release: Release the facility from liability.
	* Assumption of Risk: Assume all risks.
	* Contact Tracing Consent: Consent to contact tracing.
	* Information Sharing: Consent to share information with health authorities.
	* Policy Changes: Understand that rules may change.
	* Symptom Reporting: Agree to report symptoms.
4. **Optional fields**:
	* Parent or Guardian Name: Enter your name if you are visiting as a minor (under 18 years).
	* Parent or Guardian Signature: Type your full name if you are a minor.
	* Customer Signature: Type your full name as the customer.
	* Witness Name: Enter the name of an optional witness.
	* Witness Signature: Type the full name of the witness.
5. **Sign and date**:
	* Customer Signature: Sign with your full name.
	* Date Signed: Enter the date you signed the form.

Note: The form requires a customer signature and date, while optional fields are for minors or witnesses. Make sure to review and understand all questions before signing the form.
