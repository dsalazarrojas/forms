# Mpox Vaccine Distribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Vaccine Distribution Survey is designed to collect information about the distribution of Mpox vaccine to recipients. This form will help healthcare professionals and administrators track vaccine doses, distribution dates, locations, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Vaccine Type" field and choose the type of Mpox vaccine distributed.
2. Choose the number of vaccine doses administered using the "Vaccine Dose Administered" field.
3. Enter the recipient's name, contact number, and email address in their respective fields.
4. Provide the distribution date and time in the "Distribution Date" and "Distribution Time" fields.
5. Enter the location where the vaccine was distributed in the "Distribution Location" field.
6. Finally, add any additional comments or notes about the distribution in the "Survey Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccine Type** (`Mpox Vaccine`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of Mpox vaccine distributed. This field has two options: Mpox Vaccine and Other.
* **Vaccine Dose Administered** (`First Dose`, `Second Dose`, `Booster Dose`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the number of doses administered. This field has three options: First Dose, Second Dose, and Booster Dose.
* **Recipient Name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who received the vaccine.
* **Contact Number** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact number of the person who received the vaccine.
* **Email Address** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person who received the vaccine.
* **Distribution Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date the vaccine was distributed.
* **Distribution Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time the vaccine was distributed.
* **Distribution Location** (`text`, optional): Enter the location where the vaccine was distributed.
* **Survey Comments** (`text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the distribution.
* **Survey Questions** (`text`, optional): This field does not appear to be used in this form.
* **Recipient Information** (`text`, optional): This field does not appear to be used in this form.
* **Vaccine Dose** (`number`, optional): This field does not appear to be used in this form.
