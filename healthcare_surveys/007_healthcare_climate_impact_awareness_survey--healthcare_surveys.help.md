# Healthcare Climate Impact Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Healthcare Climate Impact Awareness Survey is a tool designed to gather data on healthcare professionals' awareness, observations, and actions taken in response to climate change impacts on their work and the environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question before proceeding.
2. Complete the form by selecting the most accurate options for each question.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions (marked as required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a brief overview of the survey's purpose and importance.
* **Staff-Awareness**: This question assesses your level of awareness about climate change impacts on your work. Select the option that best describes your current level of awareness.
	+ Option 1: I am highly aware of the climate impacts on my work.
	+ Option 2: I have some awareness, but need more information.
	+ Option 3: I am not aware of the climate impacts on my work.
* **Observed-Impacts**: This question asks about the impacts you have observed on your work and the environment. Select all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Increased extreme weather events.
	+ Option 2: Changes in weather patterns.
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify).
* **Priority-Climate-Action**: This question asks about the actions you take priority over others due to climate change impacts. Select the option that best describes your current actions.
	+ Option 1: I take no actions.
	+ Option 2: I take some actions, but they are not priority.
	+ Option 3: I take priority actions.
	+ Option 4: I take actions that go beyond what is necessary.
* **Climate-Action-Strategies**: This question asks about the strategies you use to address climate change impacts on your work. Please provide a brief description of your strategies.
* **Climate-Action-Plans**: This question asks about the plans you have in place to address climate change impacts on your work. Select all that apply.
	+ Option 1: We have a plan in place.
	+ Option 2: We are developing a plan.
	+ Option 3: We do not have a plan.
* **Observed-Physical-Changes**: This question asks about the physical changes you have observed on your work environment. Select all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Changes in temperature.
	+ Option 2: Changes in precipitation.
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify).
* **Observed-Climate-Impacts**: This question asks about the climate impacts you have observed on your work environment. Please provide a brief description of your observations.
* **Climate-Impact-Priorities**: This question asks about the climate impacts you prioritize most. Select all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Extreme weather events.
	+ Option 2: Changes in weather patterns.
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify).
* **Perceived-Climate-Risks**: This question asks about the climate risks you perceive as most significant. Please provide a brief description of your risks.
* **Climate-Risk-Communication**: This question asks about how you communicate climate risk to others. Please provide a brief description of your communication methods.

**Tips**

* Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Be as specific as possible when providing descriptions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
