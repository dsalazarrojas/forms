# Mpox Post Vaccination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Post Vaccination Survey is a form designed to collect information from individuals who have received the Mpox vaccination. The form aims to gather data on vaccination history, symptoms experienced, and other relevant details to improve public health outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have not already submitted it.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Provide detailed information on your vaccination history, including the number of doses received.
4. Report any symptoms you experienced after vaccination.
5. Enter the date of your vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Input Page**: This page is where you will enter your personal details and vaccination information.
  
- **Vaccination History**: Select the number of doses you received (Mpox 1st Dose, Mpox 2nd Dose, Mpox 3rd Dose).
  
- **Vaccination Site**: Enter the location where you received your vaccination.
  
- **Symptoms**: Select any of the symptoms you experienced after vaccination (None, Fatigue, Fever, Muscle or joint pain, Nausea, Rash, Redness, Vomiting, Weakness).
  
- **Severity**: Enter the severity of your symptoms.
  
- **Vaccine Dose**: Enter the dose of the vaccine you received.
  
- **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date you received the Mpox vaccination.
  
- **Contact Tracing Form Data**: Enter any relevant information on contact tracing for public health officials.
  
- **Contact Tracing History**: Enter any relevant information on contact tracing for public health officials.
  
- **Exposure Site**: Enter the location of exposure to Mpox.
  
- **Exposure Date**: Enter the date of exposure to Mpox.

Note: This form is intended to be completed by individuals who have received the Mpox vaccination. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
