# Cardiologists Event Log Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiologists Event Log Survey is designed to gather feedback from cardiologists about their experiences with events related to their field. This helps event organizers understand what topics were most useful, what areas need improvement, and whether the event was enjoyable for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the official title of the event you're surveying.
2. Enter the date when the event occurred.
3. Enter your name.
4. If you're not affiliated with a specific department, you can leave this field blank.
5. Choose the level of overall satisfaction you experienced with the event (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
6. Select all topics that applied to your experience with the event (Clinical updates, Case studies, Research findings, Networking).
7. Provide practical recommendations for future events.
8. Choose whether you would recommend the event to your colleagues.
9. Decide if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the official title of the event you attended. This will help us track which events are being surveyed.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, required): Enter the date when the event took place. This ensures that we have a accurate timeline of event occurrences.
* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, required): Enter your name as a cardiologist who attended the event. This will help us understand who provided feedback.
* **Department affiliation** (`department_affiliation`, optional): If you're affiliated with a specific department, such as Cardiology or Cardiovascular Surgery, please enter it here. This helps us understand the scope of your role.
* **Overall satisfaction with event** (`satisfaction_overall`, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with the event from the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied: You were highly satisfied with the event.
	+ Satisfied: You were generally satisfied with the event.
	+ Neutral: You were neutral about the event.
	+ Dissatisfied: You were dissatisfied with the event.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You were highly dissatisfied with the event.
* **Topics most useful** (`topics_most_useful`, optional): Select all topics that applied to your experience with the event:
	+ Clinical updates: Information about clinical practices and procedures.
	+ Case studies: In-depth examinations of real-world cases.
	+ Research findings: New research or studies related to cardiology.
	+ Networking: Opportunities to connect with other cardiologists.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, optional): Provide practical recommendations for future events. This is your chance to suggest changes or additions that would make future events more enjoyable and useful.
* **Would recommend event to colleagues** (`would_recommend`, required): Choose whether you would recommend the event to your colleagues:
	+ 'True': Yes, I would recommend the event to colleagues.
	+ 'False': No, I would not recommend the event to colleagues.
* **Consent to be contacted for follow-up** (`follow_up_consent`, optional): Allow event organizers to contact you for follow-up questions or discussions:
	+ 'True': Yes, allow organizers to contact you.
	+ 'False': No, do not contact me.
