# Clinical Team Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from clinical team members about their experiences and perceptions of their workplace. The goal is to identify areas of strength and weakness within the team and to gather information that can inform future improvements to team dynamics, communication, and overall work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the survey questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. You can provide feedback anonymously, but please note that your response will be kept confidential.
3. If you need to answer a question multiple times (e.g., if you've worked in multiple departments or shifts), please select the answer that best reflects your overall experience.
4. For open-ended questions, please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinical Team Feedback Survey** (`survey_intro`, note, not required): This is a note field to provide additional information about the survey.
* **Your Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name (optional).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, not required): Enter your employee ID (optional).
* **Department/Unit** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department or unit from the list.
* **Your Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role or position from the list.
* **How Long Have You Worked Here** (`tenure`, select_one, required): Select the length of time you've worked in the unit.
* **Primary Shift** (`shift`, select_one, required): Select your primary shift (e.g., Day, Evening, Night, Rotating).
* **How Would You Rate Team Communication** (`team_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication within your immediate team.
* **How Would You Rate Interdisciplinary Communication** (`interdisciplinary_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication between different departments.
* **How Effectively Is Important Information Shared** (`information_sharing`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of sharing important information.
* **How Would You Rate Shift Handoffs** (`handoff_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of shift handoffs.
* **Do You Feel Safe Speaking Up About Concerns** (`psychological_safety`, select_one, required): Rate how safe you feel speaking up about concerns.
* **How Would You Rate Respect From Colleagues** (`respect_from_colleagues`, select_one, required): Rate the level of respect you receive from your colleagues.
* **How Would You Rate Respect From Leadership** (`respect_from_leadership`, select_one, required): Rate the level of respect you receive from leadership.
* **How Would You Rate Collaboration on Your Team** (`collaboration_level`, select_one, required): Rate the level of collaboration on your team.
* **How Well Are Conflicts Resolved** (`conflict_resolution`, select_one, required): Rate how well conflicts are resolved.
* **How Supported Do You Feel by Your Team** (`support_from_team`, select_one, required): Rate how supported you feel by your team.
* **How Fairly Is Workload Distributed** (`workload_distribution`, select_one, required): Rate the fairness of workload distribution.
* **Do You Feel Your Contributions Are Recognized** (`recognition`, select_one, required): Rate how much your contributions are recognized.
* **How Would You Rate the Feedback Culture** (`feedback_culture`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of feedback culture.
* **How Effective Are Team Meetings** (`meetings_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of team meetings.
* **Are You Included in Relevant Decisions** (`decision_making_involvement`, select_one, required): Rate how included you feel in relevant decisions.
* **Are Team Resources Adequate** (`resources_adequate`, select_one, required): Rate the adequacy of team resources.
* **How Would You Rate Team Morale** (`morale_level`, select_one, required): Rate the level of team morale.
* **Would You Recommend This Unit as a Place to Work** (`would_recommend_workplace`, select_one, required): Rate your willingness to recommend the unit as a workplace.
* **What Are Your Team's Greatest Strengths** (`team_strengths`, text, not required): Provide specific examples of your team's greatest strengths.
* **What Areas Need Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, not required): Provide specific examples of areas that need improvement.
* **Any Specific Concerns to Address** (`specific_concerns`, text, not required): Provide specific concerns or issues to address.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, not required): Provide suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Provide additional comments or thoughts about team dynamics.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and honesty.
* If you need to provide more information or examples, use the open-ended questions.
* If you have any specific concerns or suggestions, use the dedicated fields for that purpose.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and provide your valuable feedback!
