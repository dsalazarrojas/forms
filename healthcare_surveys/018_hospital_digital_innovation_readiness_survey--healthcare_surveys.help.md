# Hospital Digital Innovation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Digital Innovation Readiness Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to gauge the hospital's capacity for digital innovation. It helps hospitals evaluate their current digital readiness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Hospital Digital Innovation Readiness Survey (Page 1)**
	* Fill in the title to confirm it matches the actual survey.
2. **Category (Page 2)**
	* Select the most relevant category related to your experience in the hospital (Healthcare Surveys, Other, or None).
3. **Description (Page 3)**
	* Provide a brief description of your experience in the hospital.
4. **What is Digital Innovation? (Page 4)**
	* Select all relevant digital innovation areas from the list, such as Artificial intelligence, Cloud computing, etc. (Note: Multiple selections are allowed.)
5. **Form Submission (Page 5)**
	* Enter a valid email address to receive the survey results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category**: Select the category that best describes your experience in the hospital. This helps us understand which type of survey you are responding to.
  - **Healthcare Surveys**: This category is related to your experience with surveys about healthcare topics.
  - **Other**: This category is for any other type of survey not related to healthcare.
  - **None**: If none of the above applies, please select this option.
- **Description**: Briefly describe your experience in the hospital. This will help us understand your background and context.
- **What is Digital Innovation?**: Select all relevant digital innovation areas from the list below:
  - **Artificial intelligence**: The use of AI to analyze data or make decisions.
  - **Cloud computing**: The use of cloud-based services to store and process data.
  - **Digital transformation**: The process of adapting digital solutions to improve hospital operations.
  - **Data analytics**: The analysis of data to inform hospital decisions.
  - **Data science**: The use of data to drive hospital decisions and operations.
  - **Internet of things**: The integration of IoT devices and systems in hospital operations.
  - **Robotics**: The use of robotics in hospital operations, such as in surgery or patient care.
  - **None**: If none of the above applies.
- **Form Submission**: Enter a valid email address to receive the survey results. This will allow you to receive an email with the survey's output.
