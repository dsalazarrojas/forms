# Healthcare Employee Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your opinions and experiences as a healthcare employee. Your feedback is anonymous and confidential, and will be used to help us understand and improve our work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button to access the survey questions. Follow the instructions below for each question to ensure you provide accurate and complete responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Healthcare Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, Select One, Required: False): Please select one of the options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied, to indicate your overall satisfaction with your job.
* **Employee Engagement** (`employee_engagement`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Please select all that apply from the options: Yes or No, to indicate whether you are engaged in your work.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, Number, Required: False): Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of job satisfaction. 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Communication** (`communication`, Note, Required: False): Please enter any comments or feedback about our communication within our organization.
* **Management** (`management`, Select One, Required: False): Please select one of the options: Yes or No, to indicate whether you have confidence in your management.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Number, Required: False): Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with your work experience. 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Time, Required: False): Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your work experience or any suggestions for improvement.
* **First Page** (`first_page`, Text, Required: False): This field is not actually a field in the form, but a page label. You will not see this field in the form, but it's here to explain the structure of the form.
* **Management** (page 2, Select One, Required: False): This field is actually the "employee management" section, not the "overall management" section. You will be asked to rate whether you have confidence in your management.
* **Job Satisfaction** (page 2, Number, Required: False): This field is actually the "overall satisfaction" section, not the "job satisfaction" section. You will be asked to rate your overall satisfaction with your work experience.
* **Feedback** (page 2, Time, Required: False): This field is actually for any additional comments or feedback you have about your work experience or any suggestions for improvement.
* **Employee Engagement** (page 2, Select Multiple, Required: False): This field is actually for selecting all that apply from the options: Yes or No, to indicate whether you are engaged in your work.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (page 2, Select One, Required: False): This field is actually for selecting one of the options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied, to indicate your overall satisfaction with your job.

Note: Please be as accurate as possible when providing your feedback, as it will help us create a more positive work environment for everyone. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
