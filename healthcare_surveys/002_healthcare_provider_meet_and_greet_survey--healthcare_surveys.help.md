# healthcare_provider_meet_and_greet_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meet and Greet Survey is designed to collect feedback about the experience of patients with healthcare providers, nurses, and office staff. This survey aims to assess their overall satisfaction, clinical skills, communication effectiveness, and quality of care. Your responses will help us improve our services and better serve our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your honest opinions and experiences. You may skip questions that do not apply to your encounter. Please answer each question based on your recent interaction with the healthcare provider, nurse, or office staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meet And Greet Provider** (`1`, text, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the healthcare provider's meet and greet experience.
* **Meet And Greet Nurse** (`2`, text, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the nurse's meet and greet experience.
* **Meet And Greet Office Staff** (`3`, text, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the office staff's meet and greet experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`4`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the care received from the healthcare provider, nurse, or office staff on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`5`, number, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's, nurse's, or office staff's communication skills on a scale of 1-5.
* **Provider Welcome** (`6`, select_one, optional): Rate how well the healthcare provider welcomed you during the visit.
* **Nurse Welcome** (`7`, select_one, optional): Rate how well the nurse welcomed you during the visit.
* **Office Staff Welcome** (`8`, select_one, optional): Rate how well the office staff welcomed you during the visit.
* **Wait Time** (`9`, time, optional): Please report the amount of time you spent waiting during the visit.
* **Provider Clinician** (`10`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about the healthcare provider's clinical skills.
* **Nurse Clinician** (`11`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about the nurse's clinical skills.
* **Overall Experience** (`12`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience with the healthcare provider, nurse, or office staff.
* **Provider Clinical Skills** (`13`, number, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's clinical skills on a scale of 1-5.
* **Nurse Clinical Skills** (`14`, number, optional): Rate the nurse's clinical skills on a scale of 1-5.
* **Office Staff Clinical Skills** (`15`, number, optional): Rate the office staff's clinical skills on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication Effectiveness Rating** (`16`, select_one, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's, nurse's, or office staff's communication skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Time Effectiveness Rating** (`17`, select_one, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's, nurse's, or office staff's time management as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Provider Clinical Quality** (`18`, select_one, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's clinical quality as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Nurse Clinical Quality** (`19`, select_one, optional): Rate the nurse's clinical quality as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Office Staff Clinical Quality** (`20`, select_one, optional): Rate the office staff's clinical quality as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Time Quality Rating** (`21`, select_one, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's, nurse's, or office staff's time management as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Communication Quality Rating** (`22`, select_one, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's, nurse's, or office staff's communication as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Clinical Skills Rating** (`23`, select_one, optional): Rate the healthcare provider's, nurse's, or office staff's clinical skills as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`24`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the care received from the healthcare provider, nurse, or office staff as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Additional Comments** (`25`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions about the healthcare provider, nurse, or office staff's meet and greet experience.
