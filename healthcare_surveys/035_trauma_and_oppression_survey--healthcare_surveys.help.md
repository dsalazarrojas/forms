# Trauma And Oppression Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users report on their experiences with trauma and oppression. It will help users identify the types of trauma and oppression they have faced and their impact on their lives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into multiple sections, each with its own set of questions. Users will need to complete each section in order.
2. Users can select one or multiple options for each field, as indicated in the field-by-field explanation below.
3. Some fields are optional, as indicated in the field-by-field explanation below.
4. Users will be able to navigate between sections using the next/previous buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction (how_do_you_feel)
* **Introduction** (`how_do_you_feel`, note, optional): This is an introduction section where users can briefly describe how they feel.

### 2. Trauma History (trauma_history)
* **Trauma History** (`trauma_history`, number, optional): This section asks users to report the number of times they have experienced a traumatic event.

### 3. Traumatic Experience (trauma_experience)
* **Traumatic Experience** (`trauma_experience`, date, optional): This section asks users to report the date of their first traumatic experience.

### 4. Oppression History (oppression_history)
* **Oppression History** (`oppression_history`, date, optional): This section asks users to report the date of their first experience with oppression.

### 5. Support Systems (support_systems)
* **Support Systems** (`support_systems`, select_multiple, optional): This section asks users to report the support systems they have during and after the traumatic or oppressive experience.
	+ Family

	+ Mental health professional
	+ Support group

### 6. Impact (impact)
* **Impact** (`impact`, text, optional): This section asks users to describe how trauma and oppression have impacted their lives.
