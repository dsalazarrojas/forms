# Hospital Waste Reduction Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Waste Reduction Practices Survey is a standardized form designed to help healthcare facilities assess their current waste reduction efforts, identify barriers, and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
2. Select the most appropriate options for each question.
3. Enter any additional information or comments where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient flow
* **Patient flow** (`patient_flow`, `text`, not required): This field is not applicable for this form, as it does not appear to be related to waste reduction practices. Please leave this field blank.

### Waste identification
* **Waste identification** (`waste_identification`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of your facility's current waste identification processes and procedures.

### Current status
* **Current status** (`current_status`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Not applicable
	+ No waste reduction practices in place
	+ In place and being followed
	+ In place but not being followed

### Barriers
* **Barriers** (`barriers`, `text`, not required): Describe any barriers or challenges you face in implementing waste reduction practices.

### Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional suggestions or ideas for improving waste reduction practices.

### Data collection
* **Data collection** (`data_collection`, `text`, not required): Describe your facility's current data collection processes and procedures.

### Reporting
* **Reporting** (`reporting`, `text`, not required): Explain how you report waste reduction data and metrics.

### Training
* **Training** (`training`, `text`, not required): Describe the training programs or education provided to staff on waste reduction practices.

### Education
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, not required): Explain the educational programs or materials provided to staff on waste reduction practices.

### Communication
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, not required): Describe the communication channels or methods used to promote waste reduction practices.

### Policies
* **Policies** (`policies`, `text`, not required): Provide an overview of your facility's waste reduction policies.

### Procedures
* **Procedures** (`procedures`, `text`, not required): Describe the procedures in place for waste reduction.

### Monitoring
* **Monitoring** (`monitoring`, `text`, not required): Explain how you monitor waste reduction practices.

### Evaluation
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `text`, not required): Describe how you evaluate waste reduction efforts.

### Corrective action
* **Corrective action** (`corrective_action`, `text`, not required): Outline any corrective actions taken to address waste reduction challenges.

### Certification
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the following:
	+ Certified
	+ Not certified
	+ Pending

### Certification date
* **Certification date** (`certification_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the certification date if applicable.

### Waste Reduction Practices Survey Form
* **Waste Reduction Practices Survey Form** (`waste_reduction_practices_survey_form`, `text`, not required): This field is not a question to be answered, but rather an informative statement about the form itself.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your responses.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or consult with your facility's sustainability team.
