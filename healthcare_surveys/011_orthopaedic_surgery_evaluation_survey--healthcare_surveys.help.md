# Orthopaedic Surgery Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthopaedic Surgery Evaluation Survey is used to gather feedback from patients about their experience with orthopaedic surgery care. This form helps us understand how our services are meeting your expectations and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out each section with your honest feedback.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Select the corresponding options for multiple-choice questions.
4. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, date of birth, and contact information to help us identify you for future correspondence.
* **Surgical Details (2)**: Describe the procedure you underwent, including the type of surgery and any complications or issues you experienced.
* **Post-Operative Experience (3)**: Share your overall experience after the surgery, including any pain, medication side effects, or other concerns.
* **Overall Satisfaction (4)**: Select one of the following options to express your satisfaction with our care:
	+ Very Satisfied: You were extremely happy with your care and would recommend us to others.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: You were somewhat happy with your care, but with some minor issues.
	+ Not Satisfied: You were not happy with your care and would not recommend us to others.
* **Pain Control (5)**: Select all that apply to indicate your level of pain control after the surgery:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Communication (6)**: Enter any comments about how well you felt you were communicated with by our staff, including any concerns or issues you raised.
* **Staff Friendly (7)**: Select "True" if you felt our staff was friendly and approachable, or "False" if you had a poor experience.
* **Facilities (8)**: Select all that apply to indicate your satisfaction with our facilities:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Clinic Staff (9)**: Enter any comments about our clinic staff, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **Would Recommend (10)**: Select "True" if you would recommend our services to others, or "False" if you would not.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our orthopaedic surgery services.
