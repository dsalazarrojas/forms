# Magnesium Chloride Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your usage habits and preferences regarding magnesium products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to the instructions and prompts provided. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information about your usage habits and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Do You Use Magnesium Chloride For** (`magnesium_chloride_usage`):
	+ This is a text field where you can describe the reasons or purposes for which you use magnesium chloride products. Please provide a brief explanation of your usage habits.
* **User Id** (`user_id`):
	+ This field is used to collect your unique identifier, which will help us to keep track of your responses and identify patterns in our data collection. Please enter a valid number.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ This field is used to collect your email address, which will be used to contact you for further information or to verify your identity. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ This field is used to collect your phone number, which will be used to contact you for further information or to verify your identity. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Note** (`note`):
	+ This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your usage habits and preferences. Please keep your note concise and relevant.
* **Which Magnesium Product Do You Use** (`select_one_magnitude`):
	+ This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the type of magnesium product you use most frequently. Please choose one of the options provided: Magnesium Citrate, Magnesium Glycinate, Magnesium Oxide, or Other (please specify).
* **How Often Do You Use Magnesium Product In A Week?** (`select_multiple_magnesium_use`):
	+ This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select how often you use magnesium products in a week. Please choose one or more of the options provided: Daily, Several times a day, Once a week, Rarely.
* **What Product Characteristics Do You Prefer?** (`select_multiple_magnitude_product_preferences`):
	+ This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select the characteristics or features of magnesium products that you prefer. Please choose one or more of the options provided: Magnesium Citrate, Magnesium Glycinate, Magnesium Oxide, or Other (please specify).
* **Date** (`date`):
	+ This field is used to collect the date of your last usage of magnesium products. Please enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`):
	+ This field is used to collect the time of your last usage of magnesium products. Please enter a valid time in the format HH:MM.
* **How Often Do You Use Magnesium Products At Home** (`decimal_magnitude_level`):
	+ This field is used to collect a measure of how often you use magnesium products at home, on a scale from 1 to 10. Please enter a number between 1 and 10.
* **How Often Do You Use Magnesium Products On The Go** (`integer_magnitude_level`):
	+ This field is used to collect a measure of how often you use magnesium products on-the-go, on a scale from 1 to 10. Please enter a number between 1 and 10.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`):
	+ This is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your usage habits and preferences. Please keep your note concise and relevant.
* **Magnesium Frequency** (`select_multiple_magnitude_frequency`):
	+ This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select the frequency of your magnesium usage. Please choose one or more of the options provided: Daily, Several times a day, Once a week, Rarely.
* **How Often Do You Use Magnesium Product For** (`select_multiple_magnitude_frequency_2`):
	+ This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select how often you use magnesium products for a specific purpose. Please choose one or more of the options provided: Daily, Several times a day, Once a week, Rarely.
