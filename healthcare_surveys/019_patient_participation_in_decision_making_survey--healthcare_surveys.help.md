# Patient Participation In Decision Making Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your preferences and needs as a patient. Your responses will help your healthcare provider better understand and meet your individual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred language of contact from the options provided.
2. Select your current occupation from the options provided.
3. Choose how often you would like to receive information from your healthcare provider.
4. Select your preferred method of contact with your healthcare provider.
5. Indicate how confident you are in your ability to understand and communicate with your healthcare provider.
6. Answer other questions to help your healthcare provider better understand your preferences and needs.
