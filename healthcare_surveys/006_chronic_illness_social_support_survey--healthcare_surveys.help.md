# Chronic Illness Social Support Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Illness Social Support Survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with chronic illnesses, including your social support systems, satisfaction with support, and any unmet needs or challenges you face. The data collected will be used to improve healthcare services and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the required fields, marked as such in the explanations below.
2. For select fields, choose the most accurate response.
3. Enter text fields in a clear and concise manner.
4. If you have any difficulties or questions, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent ID** (`respondent_id`, text, optional): This field is an assigned study ID or a leave it blank if you don't have one. It's not required to be filled out.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Primary diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, text, required): Describe the chronic condition you're currently dealing with.
* **Primary sources of social support** (`support_sources`, select_multiple, required): Select all the sources of support you have, such as Family, Friends, Community groups, Healthcare providers, or Online forums.
* **Satisfaction with support** (`satisfaction_with_support`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with support on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Unmet needs** (`unmet_needs`, text, optional): Describe any areas where you feel you need more support.
* **Access to services** (`access_services`, select_one, required): Choose whether services are easy to access (True) or not (False) or sometimes.
* **Most helpful resources** (`helpful_resources`, text, optional): Describe the resources that have been most helpful to you.
* **Preferred communication method** (`preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of communication (Phone, Email, SMS, or In person).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further information or insights you'd like to share, enter them here.

Remember, please complete the form accurately and honestly to contribute valuable information to the study. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.</thinking>
