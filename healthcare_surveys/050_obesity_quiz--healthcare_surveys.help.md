# Obesity Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about users' demographics, health status, and lifestyle habits related to obesity. It is designed for research or survey purposes in the healthcare field.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Age**: Enter your age in the age field.
2. Select your **Sex**: Choose between Male, Female, or Other.
3. Select your **Education Level**: Choose from None, High school, Some college, Bachelor's degree, Master's or more.
4. Select your **Occupation**: Choose from Healthcare, Business, Student, or Other.
5. Enter your **Height**: Fill in your height in the required format (e.g., 6 ft 2 in).
6. Select your **Height Unit**: Choose from ft, in, cm, or m.
7. Enter your **Weight**: Fill in your weight in the required format (e.g., 150 lb).
8. Select your **Weight Unit**: Choose from lb, kg, or st.
9. Enter your **Waist Circumference**: Fill in your waist circumference in the required format.
10. Select your **Waist Circumference Unit**: Choose from in or cm.
11. Enter your **Body Mass Index (BMI)**: Fill in your BMI.
12. Select your **Body Mass Category**: Choose from Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese Class 1, Obese Class 2, or Obese Class 3.
13. Enter your **Date of Birth**: Fill in your date of birth in the required format (e.g., 1990-02-15).
14. Select your **Smoking Status**: Choose from Never, Former, or Current.
15. Enter your **Income**: Fill in your income.
16. Select your **Income Frequency**: Choose from Annually, Monthly, or Weekly.
17. Select your **Marital Status**: Choose from Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, or Separated.
18. Note that the **Income Frequency 2** field is optional and not required.
19. Select your **Ethnicity**: Choose from African American, American Indian, Asian, Black or African American, or Other.
20. Note that the **Smoking Status 2** field is optional and not required.

* **Weight Unit** (`weight_unit`, select_one, required): Choose from lb, kg, or st.

* **Waist Circumference Unit** (`waist_circumference_unit`, select_one, required): Choose from in or cm.
* **Body Mass Index (BMI)** (`body_mass_index_bmi`, number, required): Enter your BMI.
* **Body Mass Category** (`body_mass_category`, select_one, required): Choose from Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese Class 1, Obese Class 2, or Obese Class 3.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Fill in your date of birth in the required format (e.g., 1990-02-15).
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, select_one, required): Choose from Never, Former, or Current.
* **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your income.
