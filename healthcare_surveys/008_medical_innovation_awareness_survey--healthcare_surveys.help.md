# Medical Innovation Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Innovation Awareness Survey is designed to gather information on your awareness and familiarity with various medical innovation topics. This survey is intended for your convenience, and your participation is voluntary.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the survey in its entirety.
2. Be as accurate and honest as possible when providing your responses.
3. If you need assistance or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us.

* **Survey Title**: *Enter a brief title or heading for this survey response.*
* **Category**: Select one option that best describes the category of medical innovation you are familiar with (Option 1, 2, or 3).
* **Description**: *Provide a brief description of the innovation you are familiar with.*
* **Innovation Title**: *Enter the title of the innovation, if applicable.*
* **Familiarity**: Select all the options that describe your level of familiarity with medical innovations (Option 1, 2, or 3).
