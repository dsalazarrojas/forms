# Healthcare Design Lab Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Design Lab Participation Consent Form is a survey designed to gather information from healthcare providers to improve patient care and experience. It is not intended for personal medical records but for research purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required questions to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Double-check your responses for accuracy.
3. Ensure you provide up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Details**: A brief description of the study you are participating in. Please provide a concise overview of the study and its objectives.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your name, title, and contact details so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address, where you can be reached for further questions or clarification.
* **Date Of Birth**: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Visit**: Provide the date and time of your visit to the healthcare provider. This will help us understand your experience during that visit.
* **Medical Info**: Please provide any relevant medical information you are comfortable sharing. This will help us understand your medical history and potential needs.
* **Healthcare Provider**: If you have a healthcare provider, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Medical Record Number**: If you have a medical record number, please enter it here. This will help us understand your medical history and records.
* **Tips**:
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
