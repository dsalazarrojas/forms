# Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey is a form designed to collect information on potential Measles exposure incidents, allowing for quick and accurate data collection by healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and complete the required fields.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Info** (`survey_info`, text, optional)
    * This field is a general description of the survey and its purpose.
* **Date of Exposure** (`survey_date`, date, optional)
    * Enter the date of the potential exposure incident.
* **Time of Exposure** (`survey_time`, text, optional)
    * Enter the time of the potential exposure incident.
* **Location of Exposure** (`location`, text, optional)
    * Enter the location of the potential exposure incident.
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select_one, optional)
    * Select the type of contact that occurred (Direct, Indirect, Unknown).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
    * Enter any additional information about the contact that may be relevant.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, note, optional)
    * Enter any symptoms experienced by the individual exposed.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, text, optional)
    * Enter the vaccination status of the individual exposed.
* **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, select_one, optional)
    * Select the type of vaccination the individual received (True, False, Don't Know).
* **Travel Info** (`travel_info`, text, optional)
    * Enter any information related to travel that may be relevant.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, optional)
    * Enter the location of travel.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional)
    * Enter the date of travel.
* **Duration of Travel** (`travel_duration`, number, optional)
    * Enter the duration of travel.
* **Reason for Travel** (`travel_reason`, text, optional)
    * Enter the reason for travel.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, note, optional)
    * Enter any additional details related to travel.
* **Exposure Status** (`exposure_status`, select_one, optional)
    * Select the exposure status (Confirmed, Suspected, Not Suspected).
* **Exposure Risk Level** (`exposure_risk_level`, select_multiple, optional)
    * Select the exposure risk level (High, Medium, Low).
* **Reporting Source** (`reporting_source`, select_one, optional)
    * Select the source of the report (Public Health Agency, School, Work).
* **Date of Exposure** (`exposure_date`, text, optional)
    * Enter the date of the exposure incident.
* **Survey Status** (`survey_status`, text, optional)
    * Enter the status of the survey.
* **Survey Status Comments** (`survey_status_comments`, text, optional)
    * Enter any comments related to the survey status.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional)
    * Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional)
    * Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional)
    * Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional)
    * Enter the contact person's address.

## Tips

* Always review the form carefully before proceeding.

* Enter all relevant information accurately and thoroughly.
