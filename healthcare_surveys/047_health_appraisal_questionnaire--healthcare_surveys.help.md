# Health Appraisal Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Appraisal Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about an individual's health status, medical history, and lifestyle factors. This form will help healthcare professionals assess the patient's overall health and identify potential risks or areas of concern. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Health Appraisal Questionnaire, follow these steps:
1. Please fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. For each field, provide accurate and honest information about your health status, medical history, and lifestyle factors.
3. Use the options provided to select the correct answers for multiple-choice questions.
4. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (1)
* **General Information (1)** (`text`, optional): This section is for any other important information you'd like to share about your health or medical history.

### Health Status (2)
* **Health Status (2)** (`text`, optional): Describe your current health status, including any illnesses, injuries, or medical procedures you've experienced in the last 6 months.

### Medical History (3)
* **Medical History (3)** (`text`, optional): Provide a list of any past medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments you've received.

### Allergies (4)
* **Allergies (4)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Do you have any allergies or sensitivities? Select "Yes" if you have any known allergies or sensitivities.

### Medications (5)
* **Medications (5)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Do you take any regular medications or supplements? Select "Yes" if you do.

### Medical Conditions (6)
* **Medical Conditions (6)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Do you have any known medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, etc.? Select "Yes" if you do.

### Family History (7)
* **Family History (7)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Do you have a family history of any medical conditions or illnesses? Select "Yes" if you do.

### Lifestyle (8)
* **Lifestyle (8)** (`text`, optional): Please describe your current lifestyle, including any habits or activities that may impact your health.

### Social History (9)
* **Social History (9)** (`text`, optional): Provide any information about your social circumstances, such as job, education, or relationships.

### Risk Factors (10)
* **Risk Factors (10)** (`text`, optional): Describe any factors that may pose risks to your health, such as smoking, drinking, or substance use.

### Mental Health (11)
* **Mental Health (11)** (`text`, optional): Please share any concerns or issues related to your mental health.

## Tips
Please be honest and thorough when completing this form. This information will help healthcare professionals provide you with accurate and effective care. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
