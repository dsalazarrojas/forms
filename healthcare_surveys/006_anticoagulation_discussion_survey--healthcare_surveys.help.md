# Anticoagulation Discussion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients or caregivers about their experience with anticoagulation discussions with healthcare providers. The goal is to assess the quality of these discussions and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you are responding as a patient, provide your name and contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
3. If you are responding as a healthcare provider, please answer the questions as if you were the patient or caregiver who discussed anticoagulation with you.
4. Be as specific as possible when answering the questions, providing examples or details when needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your full name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): This is the name of the patient or caregiver responding to this form.
* **Are you responding as a** (`respondent_type`, select_one, required): Please select "Patient", "Caregiver", "Healthcare provider", or "Family member" to indicate who you are responding on behalf of.
* **When did you discuss anticoagulation therapy?** (`discussion_date`, date, required): Please provide the date when you had the anticoagulation discussion.
* **Type of healthcare provider who discussed anticoagulation** (`provider_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of healthcare provider who discussed anticoagulation with you, such as "Cardiologist", "Primary care physician", "Pharmacist", "Nurse", or "Other specialist".
* **How clear was the discussion about anticoagulation therapy?** (`discussion_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the discussion on a scale from "Very clear" to "Unable to assess".
* **Which topics were discussed?** (`topics_covered`, select_multiple, required): Select all the topics that were discussed, such as "Why anticoagulation is needed", "How the medication works", "Side effects and risks", etc.
* **Were different anticoagulation options discussed?** (`medication_options`, select_one, required): Select whether the discussion included different options, such as "Yes, thoroughly", "Yes, briefly", etc.
* **How well do you understand your anticoagulation therapy?** (`understanding_level`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding on a scale from "Very well" to "Not at all".
* **Were potential side effects adequately explained?** (`side_effects_discussed`, select_one, required): Rate whether the side effects were explained thoroughly, adequately, or not at all.
* **Were dietary restrictions or considerations discussed?** (`diet_restrictions`, select_one, required): Select whether the discussion included dietary restrictions or considerations.
* **Was a monitoring plan explained?** (`monitoring_plan`, select_one, required): Rate whether a monitoring plan was discussed, such as "Yes, with appointment scheduled", "Yes, general guidelines provided", etc.
* **Were all your questions answered satisfactorily?** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Rate whether all your questions were answered, such as "Yes, all answered", "Most answered", etc.
* **If questions remain, what were they about?** (`unanswered_questions`, text, optional): If you have any questions that remain unanswered, please provide details.
* **Do you feel confident taking this medication as prescribed?** (`medication_adherence`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence level on a scale from "Very confident" to "Not at all confident".
* **What, if any, barriers might prevent adherence?** (`barrier_adherence`, select_multiple, optional): Select potential barriers to adherence, such as "Cost of medication", "Side effects", etc.
* **How could the discussion have been improved?** (`communication_improvements`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the discussion, please provide them.
* **Did the provider demonstrate good knowledge about anticoagulation?** (`provider_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the provider's knowledge on a scale from "Yes, very knowledgeable" to "Not knowledgeable".
* **Was a follow-up plan discussed?** (`follow_up_plan`, select_one, required): Rate whether a follow-up plan was discussed, such as "Yes, with appointment scheduled", "Yes, general follow-up mentioned", etc.
* **Overall satisfaction with the anticoagulation discussion** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale from "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
* **Additional feedback or comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them.
