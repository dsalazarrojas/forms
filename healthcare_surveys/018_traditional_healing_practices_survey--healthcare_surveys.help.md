# Traditional Healing Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about your awareness, usage, and perceptions of traditional healing practices. This will help us understand the extent of your awareness, adoption, and confidence in recommending these practices to others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form by providing your responses to each question.
2. For questions with options, select one or multiple responses as applicable.
3. You can add any additional comments about traditional healing practices in the "Additional comments" section.
4. If you are interested in being contacted for follow-up purposes, please provide your name, email address, and contact information.
