# Health Screening Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an employee's absence, including the reason for their absence, dates of absence, and whether a medical certificate is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Employee ID** (if provided) in the **Employee ID** field.
2.  Enter a brief explanation for the **reason for absence** in the **reason for absence** field.
3.  Enter the **Absence Start Date** and **Absence End Date** using the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
4.  Select the **assigned tool** used for the absence (e.g., Chat, Other).
5.  Indicate whether a **Health Screening Form Completed** (e.g., True or False).
6.  Enter the **Medical Certificate Date** if applicable.
7.  Add any additional comments in the **Comments** field.
8.  Enter the **Email** and **Phone Number** of the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the employee.
*   **reason for absence (2)**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of absence.
*   **Absence Start Date (3)**: Enter the date the employee was away from work.
*   **Absence End Date (4)**: Enter the date the employee returned to work.
*   **assigned tool (5)**: Select the tool used for the absence, such as Chat or Other.
*   **Health Screening Form Completed (6)**: Indicate whether a health screening form was completed.
*   **Medical Certificate Date (7)**: Enter the date the medical certificate was issued.
*   **Comments (8)**: Add any additional comments or information related to the absence.
*   **Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the employee.
*   **Phone Number (10)**: Enter the phone number of the employee.
