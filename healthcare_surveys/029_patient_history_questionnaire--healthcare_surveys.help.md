# Patient History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient History Questionnaire is a collection of questions about a patient's medical history to aid in the assessment and management of their care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Enter the patient's identifier in the "patient_identifier" field. This is a unique code assigned to the patient.
2. For each question, select the relevant options from the drop-down menus or enter the required information in the text field.
3. Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to provide the most comprehensive picture of the patient's medical history.
4. Once all questions have been answered, click "Submit" to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Identifier** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the patient's assigned unique code.
### **Medical Conditions** (Select One, Required: False)
Indicate if the patient has any medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
### **Medication Name** (Text, Required: False)
Enter the name of any medications the patient is currently taking.
### **Frequency** (Number, Required: False)
Enter the frequency of the medication administration, if applicable.
### **Medical Specialist** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Check all medical specialists the patient has seen or is currently seeing, such as general practitioner, cardiologist, etc.
### **Medical Tests** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Check all medical tests or exams the patient has undergone or is currently undergoing, such as blood work, X-rays, etc.
### **Medical Results** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Check all medical results or diagnoses from past medical tests or exams, such as abnormal readings or results, etc.
### **Medical Procedures** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Check all medical procedures the patient has undergone or is currently undergoing, such as surgeries, injections, etc.
### **Family History** (Select Multiple, Required: False)
Check all medical conditions or concerns that exist in the patient's family history, such as heart disease, diabetes, etc.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or seek guidance from a healthcare professional.
* This form is intended to be used for new patients only. If the patient has been previously registered, please do not use this form.
