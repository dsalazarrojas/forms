# Pet Healthcare Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Healthcare Communication Survey is a tool designed to gather information about your pet's healthcare needs and preferences. It is intended to help veterinary professionals provide better care and services to your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your pet's name on the first page.
2. On the second page, provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
3. On the third page, add any comments or notes about your pet's healthcare needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your pet's name** (pet_healthcare_communication_survey_page_1):
	+ This is where you will enter your pet's name.
* **What is your contact information** (pet_healthcare_communication_survey_page_2):
	+ This is where you will enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **What are your comments** (pet_healthcare_communication_survey_page_3):
	+ This is where you can add any additional information about your pet's healthcare needs or any comments you have for the veterinary professional.
