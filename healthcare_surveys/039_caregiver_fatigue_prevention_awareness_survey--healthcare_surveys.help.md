# Caregiver Fatigue Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to assess caregiver fatigue and burnout prevention awareness among caregivers. The goal is to gain insights into their current understanding, experience, and attitudes towards fatigue prevention strategies, and how they perceive the value of training and resources to prevent burnout.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability. If you're unsure or need help with any question, don't hesitate to ask.
2. Select the options that best describe your current situation or understanding.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply if you experience multiple symptoms or conditions.
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Enter the role you play as a caregiver, such as family caregiver, healthcare worker, or social worker.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date you're completing this survey.
* **Years of Caregiving Experience** (`caregiver_experience`, number, required): Estimate the number of years you've been caregiving.
* **Understanding of Caregiver Fatigue or Burnout** (`fatigue_definition_understanding`, select_one, required):
	+ Very well - familiar with the definition.
	+ Well - general understanding.
	+ Somewhat - vague understanding.
	+ Not well - limited knowledge.
	+ Not at all - unfamiliar with the term.
* **Do You Personally Experience Caregiver Fatigue?** (`personally_experiencing_fatigue`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - significant fatigue.
	+ Yes - moderate fatigue.
	+ Yes - mild fatigue.
	+ No - not experiencing fatigue.
	+ Unsure.
* **Fatigue Symptoms You Experience** (`fatigue_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Physical exhaustion.
	+ Emotional exhaustion.
	+ Mental or cognitive fatigue.
	+ Loss of motivation.
	+ Irritability or mood changes.
	+ Sleep problems.
	+ Physical health problems.
	+ Difficulty concentrating.
	+ Feeling overwhelmed.
	+ Depressed or anxious.
* **Main Causes of Your Fatigue** (`fatigue_causes`, text, optional): Describe the factors that contribute to your fatigue.
* **Impact of Fatigue on Your Life** (`fatigue_impact`, text, optional): Explain how fatigue has affected you.
* **Awareness of Fatigue Prevention Strategies** (`prevention_strategies_awareness`, select_one, required):
	+ Very aware - know many strategies.
	+ Aware - know some strategies.
	+ Somewhat aware - limited knowledge.
	+ Not very aware - little knowledge.
	+ Not aware - no knowledge.
* **Are You Using Prevention Strategies?** (`using_prevention_strategies`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - actively using strategies.
	+ Yes - using some strategies.
	+ No - not using strategies.
	+ Aware but not practicing.
* **Fatigue Prevention Strategies You Use** (`strategies_used`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Taking regular breaks.
	+ Exercise or physical activity.
	+ Meditation or mindfulness.
	+ Support group attendance.
	+ Professional counseling.
	+ Setting boundaries.
	+ Delegating tasks.
	+ Time for hobbies or interests.
	+ Adequate sleep.
	+ Healthy diet.
	+ Social connections.
	+ Respite care.
* **Access to Respite Care** (`respite_care_access`, select_one, required):
	+ Easy access - use regularly.
	+ Some access - use occasionally.
	+ Limited access - difficult to arrange.
	+ No access - unavailable.
	+ Don't know what respite care is.
* **Support System Strength** (`support_system`, select_one, required):
	+ Strong support - can rely on.
	+ Adequate support - some available.
	+ Limited support - few options.
	+ No support - isolated.
	+ Unsure of available support.
* **Awareness of Professional Help Resources** (`professional_help_awareness`, select_one, required):
	+ Very aware - know many resources.
	+ Aware - know several resources.
	+ Somewhat aware - know a few.
	+ Not very aware - limited knowledge.
	+ Not aware - don't know resources.
* **Have You Accessed Professional Help?** (`accessed_professional_help`, select_one, required):
	+ Yes - regularly using services.
	+ Yes - occasionally using services.
	+ No - not used services.
	+ Want to but unable to access.
	+ Don't feel I need help.
* **Organizational Support for Caregiver Wellness** (`organizational_support`, select_one, optional):
	+ Excellent support - many programs.
	+ Good support - adequate programs.
	+ Fair support - some programs.
	+ Poor support - minimal programs.
	+ No organizational support.
	+ N/A - not part of organization.
* **Value of Fatigue Prevention Training** (`awareness_training_value`, select_one, required):
	+ Very valuable - essential.
	+ Valuable - important.
	+ Somewhat valuable - nice to have.
	+ Not very valuable - not needed.
	+ Not valuable at all.

	+ Yes - multiple trainings.
	+ Yes - one training.
	+ No - but interested.
	+ No - not interested.
	+ No - unavailable in my area.

	+ Very effective - made significant difference.
	+ Effective - helped somewhat.
	+ Somewhat effective - some benefit.
	+ Not effective - little benefit.
	+ Did not attend.
* **Barriers to Using Prevention Strategies** (`barriers_to_prevention`, text, optional): Describe the obstacles preventing you from practicing prevention strategies.
* **Resources or Support You Need Most** (`needed_resources`, text, optional): Specify the resources or support that would aid in managing fatigue.
* **Additional Comments About Caregiver Fatigue** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions you may have about caregiver fatigue.

**Tips**
