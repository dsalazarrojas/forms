# Healthcare Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions on healthcare technology adoption. Your responses will help us better understand how technology affects your work and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate option for each question from the dropdown or text fields.
2. If an option is "Other," select "Yes" or "No" and provide a detailed explanation in the corresponding text field.
3. Complete all questions on each page before moving to the next page.
4. Double-check your responses to ensure they accurately reflect your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Is Your Occupation** (`form_what_is_your_occupation`): Please select your occupational category.
* **What Is Your Position** (`form_what_is_your_position`): Please select your current position or role in the healthcare sector.
* **Which Department** (`form_which_department`): Please indicate whether you work in a department related to healthcare technology.
* **What Are Your Areas Of Interests** (`form_what_are_your_areas_of_interests`): Choose areas related to healthcare technology that interest you. 
* **Which Technology Adoption Barriers** (`form_which_technology_adoption_barriers`): Select the barriers you face when adopting new technologies.
* **Other Technology Adoption Barriers** (`form_which_technology_adoption_barriers_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Which Technology Are You Satisfied With** (`form_which_technology_are_you_satisfied_with`): Choose technologies that you are satisfied with.
* **Other Technology Are You Satisfied With** (`form_which_technology_are_you_satisfied_with_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.
* **What Is The Level Of Difficulty** (`form_what_is_the_level_of_difficulty`): Select the level of difficulty you experience with certain technologies.
* **Other Level Of Difficulty** (`form_what_is_the_level_of_difficulty_other`): If "Other," provide a brief explanation of the difficulty you experience.
* **Which Technology Do You Use** (`form_which_technology_do_you_use`): Choose technologies that you use regularly.
* **Other Technology Do You Use** (`form_which_technology_do_you_use_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Which Technology Do You Use For** (`form_which_technology_do_you_use_for`): Select the purpose or function for which you use each technology.
* **Other Technology Do You Use For** (`form_which_technology_do_you_use_for_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.
* **What Is Your Satisfaction Level** (`form_what_is_your_satisfaction_level`): Select your level of satisfaction with certain technologies.
* **Other Satisfaction Level** (`form_what_is_your_satisfaction_level_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Which Technology Features** (`form_which_technology_features`): Select features of technologies that you find useful.
* **Other Technology Features** (`form_which_technology_features_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.
* **Other Technology Features** (`form_what_is_your_satisfaction_level_other`): If "Yes," please select "Yes" and provide a brief explanation.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Select only options that apply to you.
* If you have any difficulty understanding a question, ask for clarification.
