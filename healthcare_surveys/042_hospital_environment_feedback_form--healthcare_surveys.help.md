# Hospital Environment Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hospital Environment Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from hospital users about their experience with the hospital's facilities, cleanliness, overall satisfaction, and other aspects such as parking and communication effectiveness. This feedback is used to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the hospital's environment and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, considering your experience with the hospital.
2. Use the scale provided for each question to rate your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the text boxes provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facilities (5)** (`facilities_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospital's facilities, such as the building, furniture, and equipment.
* **Cleanliness (5)** (`cleanliness_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospital's cleanliness, including the state of the hospital's facilities and the availability of clean and hygienic conditions.
* **Overall (1-5)** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the hospital, considering all aspects of your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Select Facilities Option** (`select_facilities_option`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options from the list to describe your experience with the hospital's facilities.
	+ 5: Excellent facilities
	+ 4: Good facilities
	+ 3: Average facilities
	+ 2: Poor facilities
	+ 1: Very poor facilities
* **Select Cleanliness Option** (`select_cleanliness_option`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options from the list to describe your experience with the hospital's cleanliness.
	+ 5: Excellent cleanliness
	+ 4: Good cleanliness
	+ 3: Average cleanliness
	+ 2: Poor cleanliness
	+ 1: Very poor cleanliness
* **Parking (5)** (`parking_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospital's parking facilities.
* **Waiting Time (5)** (`waiting_time`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the waiting time in the hospital, including the waiting time in the emergency room, doctor's appointments, and other services.
* **Communication Effectiveness (5)** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the hospital's communication effectiveness, including the clarity and timeliness of information provided.
* **Select Parking Option** (`select_parking_option`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options from the list to describe your experience with the hospital's parking facilities.
	+ 5: Excellent parking facilities
	+ 4: Good parking facilities
	+ 3: Average parking facilities
	+ 2: Poor parking facilities
	+ 1: Very poor parking facilities
* **Select Waiting Time Option** (`select_waiting_time_option`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options from the list to describe your experience with the hospital's waiting time.
	+ 5: Excellent waiting time
	+ 4: Good waiting time
	+ 3: Average waiting time
	+ 2: Poor waiting time
	+ 1: Very poor waiting time
* **Select Communication Effectiveness Option** (`select_communication_effectiveness_option`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options from the list to describe your experience with the hospital's communication effectiveness.
	+ 5: Excellent communication effectiveness
	+ 4: Good communication effectiveness
	+ 3: Average communication effectiveness
	+ 2: Poor communication effectiveness
	+ 1: Very poor communication effectiveness
* **Communication Comments** (`select_communication_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the hospital's communication effectiveness, please provide them here.
* **Parking Comments** (`select_parking_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the hospital's parking facilities, please provide them here.
* **Waiting Time Comments** (`select_waiting_time_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the hospital's waiting time, please provide them here.
* **Communication Effectiveness Comments** (`select_communication_effectiveness_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the hospital's communication effectiveness, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve the hospital's environment and services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is confidential and will be used solely for the purpose of improving the hospital's environment and services.
