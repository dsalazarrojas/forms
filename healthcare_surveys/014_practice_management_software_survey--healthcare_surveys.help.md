# Practice Management Software Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Practice Management Software Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from healthcare professionals about their experience with practice management software. This survey aims to understand the current state of practice management systems, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights to software developers and vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your response.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your supervisor or contact the survey administrators for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Management Systems** (Page 1)
	+ This question is asking about your experience with practice management systems. Please provide the name of the system you're currently using or have used in the past.
* **Firm Name** (Page 1)
	+ Please enter your firm's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Firm Size** (Page 2, Select One: Small, Medium, Large)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the size of your firm.
	+ Small: 1-10 employees
	+ Medium: 11-50 employees
	+ Large: 51 or more employees
* **Firm Type** (Page 2, Select One: Practice, Firm)
	+ Select one of the options to indicate the type of your firm.
	+ Practice: A private practice or clinic
	+ Firm: A larger organization with multiple locations or specialties
* **User Satisfaction Rating** (Page 3, Select Multiple: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
	+ Please select all the options that best describe your overall satisfaction with your current practice management system.
* **Current Features** (Page 4)
	+ Please list the features you are currently using in your practice management system.
* **Key Features** (Page 5)
	+ Please list the features that are essential to your practice management system.
* **Missing Features** (Page 6)
	+ Please list the features that are missing from your current practice management system.
* **Improvement Ideas** (Page 7)
	+ Please share any ideas you have for improving the practice management system.
* **Vendor Relationship** (Page 8)
	+ Please describe your experience with the vendor providing your practice management system.
* **User Experience** (Page 9)
	+ Please provide any feedback or suggestions on how to improve the user experience with the practice management system.
* **Support** (Page 10)
	+ Please describe your experience with the support provided by your practice management system vendor.
