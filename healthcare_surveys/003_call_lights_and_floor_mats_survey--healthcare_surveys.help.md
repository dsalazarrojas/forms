# Call Lights And Floor Mats Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from healthcare staff on the effectiveness and reliability of call lights and floor mats used in their work area. This information will help us identify areas for improvement and optimize the safety and care of our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your department and work area from the provided options.
2. Choose your staff role and position within the department.
3. Indicate your typical work shift.
4. Answer the questions regarding call lights and floor mats, providing your professional opinion based on your experience.
5. Rate the overall equipment satisfaction score.
6. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
7. Finally, select the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department - Unit** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary work area, such as Medical-Surgical, Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Emergency Room, Pediatrics, Geriatrics, or Other.
* **Staff Role** (`staff_role`, `select_one`, required): Choose your professional designation, such as Registered Nurse (RN), Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN), Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA), Therapist, or Administrator.
* **Typical Work Shift** (`work_shift`, `select_one`, required): Select your typical work shift, such as Day Shift, Evening Shift, Night Shift, or Rotating.
* **Call Light System** (`call_lights_section`, `note`, required): This is just a placeholder label; this field is not visible and not part of the form.
* **Reliability of Call Light System** (`call_light_reliability`, `select_one`, required): How often does the call light system function correctly? Choose from Always Reliable, Mostly Reliable, Sometimes Fails, or Frequently Fails.
* **Ease of Use for Patients** (`call_light_ease_of_use`, `select_one`, required): Based on your observations and patient feedback, how easy or difficult is the call light system to use? Choose from Very Easy, Easy, Difficult, or Very Difficult.
* **Common Call Light Issues** (`call_light_issues`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all issues you have encountered with the call light system, such as buttons sticking, volume too low, delay in notification, visual indicator not working, or cord length insufficient.
* **-- Safety Floor Mats --** (`floor_mats_section`, `note`, required): This is just a placeholder label; this field is not visible and not part of the form.
* **Frequency of Floor Mat Usage** (`floor_mat_frequency`, `select_one`, required): How often are safety mats deployed for at-risk patients? Choose from Every Shift, Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Effectiveness in Fall Prevention** (`floor_mat_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): What is your professional opinion on the effectiveness of safety mats in preventing falls? Choose from Highly Effective, Moderately Effective, Slightly Effective, or Not Effective.
* **Maintenance Issues with Mats** (`mat_maintenance_issues`, `text`, required): Describe any maintenance issues you have encountered with the safety mats, such as curling edges or cleaning difficulties.
* **Overall Equipment Satisfaction Score** (`equipment_satisfaction_score`, `number`, required): Rate the overall satisfaction with the equipment, from 1 (lowest) to 10 (highest).
* **Suggestions for Safety Equipment Improvements** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, required): Provide any ideas to make the environment safer.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your submission.
