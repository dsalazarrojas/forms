# Pediatric Eye Care Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from parents or guardians about their child's experience at an eye care clinic. It helps to measure patient satisfaction and gather insights to improve the quality of care provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or skip it if you're unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the parent or guardian filling out this form.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, optional): The date you are providing feedback for the visit.
* **Visit to the Eye Clinic** (`eye_clinic_visit`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if this visit was to an eye clinic. Options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Describe your overall satisfaction with the care provided. Options: "Very dissatisfied", "Somewhat dissatisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat satisfied", or "Very satisfied".
* **Feedback** (`feedback_comment`, note, optional): Write any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address you would like to be used for any future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number where you can be reached.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Indicate who submitted this feedback. Options: "Parent" or "Guardian".
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_multiple, optional): Select the age group of the child who received care. Options: "0-1", "2-4", "5-10", "11-16", "17-24", "25+"

**Tips**

* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
