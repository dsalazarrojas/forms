# Pregnancy And Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pregnancy and Development Survey is designed to gather information about pregnancy, birth, and postnatal data. This form will help healthcare professionals track and analyze pregnancy-related data, enabling them to better understand pregnancy development and outcomes. The data collected will help in improving maternal and newborn care, as well as inform public health policies and research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill out the form for each unique pregnancy case or individual.
3.  Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to complete the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Pregnancy Status
*   **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   True: The individual is pregnant.
    *   False: The individual is not pregnant.
    *   Undetermined: The pregnancy status is unknown or indeterminate.

### 2. Gestation Week
*   **Gestation Week** (`gestation_week`, number, required: false): Enter the gestation week of the pregnancy, ranging from 1 to 36.

### 3. Prenatal Care Habit
*   **Prenatal Care Habit** (`prenatal_care_habit`, text, required: false): Provide a description of the individual's prenatal care habits or routine.

### 4. Hospitalization Status
*   **Hospitalization Status** (`hospitalization_status`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   True: The individual has been hospitalized during the pregnancy.
    *   False: The individual has not been hospitalized during the pregnancy.

### 5. Hospitalization Length
*   **Hospitalization Length** (`hospitalization_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of hospitalization in days.

### 6. Hospitalization Type
*   **Hospitalization Type** (`hospitalization_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   Normal Delivery
    *   C-Section
    *   Induced Labor

### 7. Complications
*   **Complications** (`complications`, text, required: false): Describe any complications that occurred during the pregnancy or postnatally.

### 8. Complications Details
*   **Complications Details** (`complications_details`, text, required: false): Provide more details about the complications described in the previous field.

### 9. Birth Weight
*   **Birth Weight** (`birth_weight`, number, required: false): Record the birth weight of the baby.

### 10. Birth Length
*   **Birth Length** (`birth_length`, number, required: false): Record the length of the baby at birth.

### 11. Birth Status
*   **Birth Status** (`birth_status`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   Alive
    *   Dead

### 12. Maternal Care Provider
*   **Maternal Care Provider** (`maternal_care_provider`, text, required: false): Record the name of the healthcare provider who provided prenatal care.

### 13. Maternal Care Provider Type
*   **Maternal Care Provider Type** (`maternal_care_provider_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   Public Hospital
    *   Private Hospital

### 14. Medical History
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe the individual's medical history, if any.

### 15. Medical History Details
*   **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, required: false): Provide more details about the medical history described in the previous field.

### 16. Health Insurance
*   **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   True: The individual has health insurance.
    *   False: The individual does not have health insurance.

### 17. Insurance Provider
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required: false): Record the name of the insurance provider.

### 18. Insurance Provider Type
*   **Insurance Provider Type** (`insurance_provider_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   Public
    *   Private

### 19. Health Insurance Type
*   **Health Insurance Type** (`health_insurance_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the following options:
    *   Public
    *   Private

### 20. Insurance Provider Phone
*   **Insurance Provider Phone** (`insurance_provider_phone`, text, required: false): Record the phone number of the insurance provider.

### 21. Insurance Provider Email
*   **Insurance Provider Email** (`insurance_provider_email`, email, required: false): Record the email of the insurance provider.

### 22. Insurance Provider Address
*   **Insurance Provider Address** (`insurance_provider_address`, text, required: false): Record the address of the insurance provider.

### 23. Hospital Name
*   **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required: false): Record the name of the hospital where the individual gave birth.

### 24. Hospital Address
*   **Hospital Address** (`hospital_address`, text, required: false): Record the address of the hospital where the individual gave birth.

### 25. Hospital Phone
*   **Hospital Phone** (`hospital_phone`, text, required: false): Record the phone number of the hospital where the individual gave birth.

**Tips**

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions and information to complete the form accurately.
*   If you're unsure about any field or option, please refer to external resources or consult with a healthcare professional.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Pregnancy and Development Survey form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to a healthcare professional or the form's administrator.
