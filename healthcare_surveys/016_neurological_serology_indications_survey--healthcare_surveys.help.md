# Neurological Serology Indications Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurological Serology Indications Survey is a form designed to collect information about a patient's serology test history, symptoms, and medical history for neurological consultations. This form helps healthcare professionals to better understand the patient's condition and provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form

* Carefully read each section and answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If a field is unclear or you are unsure about the answer, please consult the healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptoms** (`symptoms_page`, text, required): Describe the patient's current symptoms, such as headache, dizziness, numbness, tingling sensation in hands and feet, fatigue, weakness, confusion or disorientation, vision changes, hearing loss, speech or language changes, and memory loss.
* **Medical History** (`history_page`, note, required): Provide the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Neurological Serology Testing** (`neurological_serology_testing_page`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of serology tests ordered, including Blood tests, Imaging studies, and Physical exam.
* **Test Frequency** (`frequency_page`, select_one, required): Choose how often the serology test is performed, either Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Preferred Time** (`time_page`, select_one, required): Select the patient's preferred time for the test, either Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Test Results** (`results_page`, select_one, required): Choose the patient's serology test results, either Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive.
* **Previous Tests** (`frequency_page_2`, select_one, required): Select how many times the patient has undergone a serology test, either Yes, No, or Other.
* **Age Group** (`age_group_page`, select_one, required): Choose the patient's age group, either 0-10 years, 11-20 years, or 21-30 years.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty_page`, select_one, required): Select the patient's medical specialty, either Neurologist, Primary care physician, or Specialist.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms_page_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the symptoms the patient has experienced, including Headache, Dizziness, Numbness, Tingling sensation in hands and feet, Fatigue, Weakness, Confusion or disorientation, Vision changes, Hearing loss, Speech or language changes, and Memory loss.
* **Last Serology Test Date** (`last_serology_test_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last serology test in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Last Serology Test Time** (`last_serology_test_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the last serology test in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Last Serology Test Note** (`last_serology_test_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the last serology test.
* **Total Serology Tests** (`total_serology_tests`, select_one, required): Select the total number of serology tests the patient has undergone, either 1-5, 6-10, or 11-15.
* **Total Serology Tests 2** (`total_serology_tests_2`, select_one, required): Select the total number of serology tests the patient has undergone, either 1-10, 11-20, or 21 or more.
* **Total Serology Tests 3** (`total_serology_tests_3`, select_one, required): Select the total number of serology tests the patient has undergone, either 1-10, 11-20, or 21 or more.
