# Insomnia Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Insomnia Questionnaire is a tool used to collect data about patients' sleep patterns and experiences. The form aims to gather information about their sleep habits, symptoms, and medical history, which can help healthcare professionals better understand their insomnia and provide targeted treatment options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your Patient Name**: This field is used to record the name of the patient completing the form.
2. **Select your Age**: Please enter your age to help us understand your demographic profile.
3. **When did you first experience sleep problems?**: Describe when you first started experiencing sleep difficulties.
4. **How long have you had sleep problems?**: Indicate how long you've been dealing with sleep issues.
5. **How often do you have difficulty falling asleep?**: Choose how frequently you experience difficulty falling asleep.
6. **How often do you wake up at night?**: Select how often you wake up during the night.
7. **How would you rate your sleep quality?**: Choose your sleep quality rating.
8. **Do you believe stress has disrupted your sleep?**: Check if you believe that stress has affected your sleep.
9. **Are you taking any prescription medications?**: Indicate if you're currently taking prescription medications.
10. **Enter Sleep Quality Score**: Please enter your sleep quality score as per the provided scale.
11. **Do you have any sleep disorder diagnoses?**: Check if you have any sleep disorder diagnoses.
12. **Enter another sleep disorder (if applicable)**: If you have another sleep disorder, please specify its type.
13. **Have you ever been diagnosed with insomnia?**: Check if you've ever been diagnosed with insomnia.
14. **Have you ever received sleep treatment?**: Indicate if you've received any sleep treatment.
15. **If you have received sleep treatment, what was it?**: If you've received treatment, describe the type.
16. **Has sleep deprivation caused problems in your life?**: Check if you believe sleep deprivation has caused problems.

* **Patient Name (text)**: This field is used to record the name of the patient completing the form.
