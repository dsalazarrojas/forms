# Dyspnea Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about the patient's shortness of breath, its severity, triggers, and associated symptoms. This data will help healthcare providers assess and manage the patient's condition effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to you or describe your symptoms or experiences in the text field.
3. For medication-related questions, please specify the type and dose of the medication used.
4. If you have any medical provider, please include their name and location.
5. Provide your patient ID for identification purposes.
6. Record the date and time of your last visit to the healthcare provider.
7. Answer questions regarding your contact information and relationship with the healthcare provider.

* **Breath History** (`breath_history`): Record the duration of your shortness of breath.
* **Breath Symptoms** (`breath_symptoms`): Select all symptoms that you are experiencing, such as Chest tightness, Wheezing, Difficulty breathing, or Blue discoloration of the lips and fingers.
* **Other Breath Symptoms** (`other_breath_symptoms`): Describe any other symptoms not listed above.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`): Indicate if you are taking any medication for your condition, and select 'True' or 'False'.
