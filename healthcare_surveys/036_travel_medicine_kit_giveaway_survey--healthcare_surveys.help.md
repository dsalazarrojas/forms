# Travel Medicine Kit Giveaway Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Medicine Kit Giveaway Survey is a form designed to gather information about travelers who are requesting a free medicine kit. This information will help the healthcare organization understand the traveler's medical needs and ensure that the kits are provided to those who really need them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter the date of your trip in the Travel Date field.
5. Select the type of travel from the Travel Type dropdown list.
6. Choose your travel destinations from the Travel Destination multi-select list.
7. If you have a specific need for a medicine kit, enter the number of kits you require in the Medicine Kit field.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of your trip.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of travel you are taking, such as business or leisure.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the destinations you will be visiting on your trip.
* **Medicine Kit** (`medicine_kit`, number, optional): If you require a medicine kit, please enter the number of kits you need.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or information about your travel.
