# Health History General Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health History General Survey is a form used to gather general medical information from patients at the beginning of their care episode.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
3. If you are unsure about any question, you can ask for clarification from a healthcare provider.
4. Please fill out this form as completely as possible, but do not repeat information in multiple fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Please describe your current medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
### 2. Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the medical conditions that you have been diagnosed with, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
### 3. Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List all the medications you are currently taking, including prescription and over-the-counter medications.
### 4. Medical History Date
* **Medical History Date** (`medical_history_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date of your current medical history, or the date of your last medical history update.
### 5. Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): List all the substances or items to which you are allergic, such as latex, penicillin, etc.
### 6. Surgeries
* **Surgeries** (`surgeries`, text, required: false): Describe any surgeries you have had, including the date and location of the surgery.
### 7. Medical Tests
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, required: false): List any medical tests or exams you have had, such as blood work, X-rays, etc.
### 8. Additional Medical History Info 1
* **Additional Medical History Info 1** (`medical_history_additional_info`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional information about your medical history that is not already mentioned.
### 9. Additional Medical History Info 2

### 10. Additional Medical History Info 3
