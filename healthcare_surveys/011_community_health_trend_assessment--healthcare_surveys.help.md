# Community Health Trend Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Trend Assessment form is designed to collect information about the current health trends and concerns of your community. It is a survey that aims to gather insights from community members to understand their health-related needs and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Neighborhood or area" to identify the specific area you are representing.
2. Choose up to three top health concerns that are currently affecting your community.
3. Rate the accessibility of healthcare services in your community.
4. Indicate how frequently you use preventive services in your community (optional).
5. List any known local health resources available in your community.
6. Select any main barriers that are preventing better health in your community (optional).
7. Provide suggestions for improvement in community health (optional).
8. If willing, indicate your willingness to participate in community initiatives.
9. If desired, provide your email address for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Neighborhood or Area (Respondent Area)
• **Neighborhood or area**: The name of the area you are representing, e.g., "Town", "District", "Community name".

### Top Health Concerns
• **Top health concerns in community**: Select up to three top health concerns currently affecting your community, e.g., "Chronic disease management", "Mental health", "Substance misuse".

### Accessibility of Healthcare Services
• **Accessibility of healthcare services**: Rate how accessible healthcare services are in your community, e.g., "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor".

### Use of Preventive Services
• **Use of preventive services**: Indicate how frequently you use preventive services in your community, e.g., "Regularly", "Occasionally", "Rarely", "Never".

### Known Local Health Resources
• **Known local health resources**: List any known local health resources available in your community, e.g., health clinics, hospitals, support groups.

### Main Barriers to Better Health
• **Main barriers to better health**: Select any main barriers that are preventing better health in your community, e.g., "Cost", "Transportation", "Lack of information", "Language".

### Suggested Actions for Improvement
• **Suggested actions for improvement**: Provide any suggestions for improving community health.

### Willing to Participate
• **Willing to participate in community initiatives**: If willing, select "True" to indicate your willingness to participate in community initiatives.

### Contact Email for Involvement
• **Contact email for involvement**: If desired, provide your email address for follow-up contact.
