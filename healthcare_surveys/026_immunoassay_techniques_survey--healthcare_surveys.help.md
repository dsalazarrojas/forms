# Immunoassay Techniques Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunoassay Techniques Survey is a form designed to gather information from users about their experience with immunoassay techniques. It aims to understand the frequency and applications of different techniques, methods, and challenges faced by users in the field of immunoassay. This data will be used to improve the development and implementation of immunoassay techniques in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the method(s) used for immunoassay from the list provided.
2. Indicate the frequency of the method(s) used.
3. Specify the applications of the method(s) used.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments about the experience with immunoassay techniques.
5. Provide your email address for any follow-up contact.
6. Enter your phone number for any additional contact.
7. Select the category of the immunoassay technique used.
8. Indicate the frequency of the category.
9. Specify the applications of the category.
10. Repeat the process for each additional page of questions.
