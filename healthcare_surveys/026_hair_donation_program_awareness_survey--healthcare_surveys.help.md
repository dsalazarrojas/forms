# Hair Donation Program Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your awareness and experiences with hair donation programs. It will help us better understand how you find, share, and participate in these programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best applies to your experience.
2. Fill in all the questions that are relevant to you.
3. If a question does not apply to you, you can skip it.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
