# Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information from healthcare providers on gender violence awareness and prevention. It consists of 8 questions that cover different aspects of gender violence and its consequences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the entire form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer all questions that apply to you.
3. Fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gender Violence Prevention Awareness Survey** (`gender_violence_prevention_awareness_survey_page`, text, optional): This is an optional text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about gender violence prevention awareness survey.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information_page`, text, optional): This is an optional text field where you can provide your contact information, such as your email address or phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider_page`, select_one, optional): This is an optional select_one field where you can choose your healthcare provider type, such as Primary Care Physician or Specialist.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_information_page`, select_multiple, optional): This is an optional select_multiple field where you can select your demographic information, such as Male or Female.
* **Survey Instruments** (`survey_instruments_page`, note, optional): This is an optional note field where you can provide additional information about the survey instruments used.
* **Survey Instruments 2** (`survey_instruments_2_page`, note, optional): This is an optional note field where you can provide additional information about the survey instruments used.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_information_2_page`, email, optional): This is an optional email field where you can provide your email address.
* **Healthcare Provider 2** (`healthcare_provider_2_page`, select_one, optional): This is an optional select_one field where you can choose your healthcare provider type, such as Primary Care Physician or Specialist.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your organization's email address.
