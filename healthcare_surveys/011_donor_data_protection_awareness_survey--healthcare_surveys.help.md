# Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey is a form designed to gather information about a user's understanding of data protection awareness in the healthcare industry. This form will help us assess the user's knowledge and awareness of data protection practices and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the general questions in page 1 (General Questions).
2. Provide information about the donor (Donor Info).
3. Specify your preferences regarding data privacy (Data Privacy).
4. Provide information about data security (Data Security).
5. Answer questions about data retention (Data Retention).
6. Enter your email address (Email Address).
7. Enter your phone number (Phone Number).
8. Confirm your consent to certain practices (Consent).
9. Provide demographic information (Demographic Info).
10. Review and confirm that all information is accurate and complete (Submit).

- **General Questions**: This section asks basic questions to gauge your overall understanding of data protection awareness. Please answer these questions based on your general knowledge and understanding.

	+ *Donor Info*: This section is optional.

	+ *Data Privacy*: This section is optional.

- **Email Address**: Please enter your email address.
	+ *Email Address*: This section is optional.
- **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number.
	+ *Phone Number*: This section is optional.
- **Consent**: Please select all the options that apply to you.
