# Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather patient feedback on their experience and satisfaction with the care or services they received. The data collected will help us improve our services and better serve our patients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last name** field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the **date of birth** field, using the standard date format (e.g., 01/01/2000).
4. Provide your email address in the **email** field.
5. Enter your contact number in the **contact number** field.
6. Answer the question **experience of care** by describing your experience with the service.
7. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the service by selecting one of the options in the **satisfaction with service** field.
8. Choose all that apply from the options in the **recommendation of service** field.
9. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the date of your birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact number**: Enter the contact number where you can be reached.
* **Experience of care**: Describe your experience with the service. This will help us understand how we can improve.
* **Satisfaction with service**: Choose how satisfied you are with the service:
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Recommendation of service**: Check all that apply:
	+ Not Recommended
	+ Somewhat Recommended
	+ Highly Recommended
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your responses to help us improve our services.
* If you have any issues or concerns about your experience, feel free to provide details in the **comments** field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can ask our staff for assistance.
