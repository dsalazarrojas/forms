# Health Equity And Inclusion Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess their own healthcare services' level of health equity and inclusion. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform. The purpose of this form is to help healthcare providers evaluate how well they include and serve diverse patients, staff, and communities in their care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions as a healthcare provider who serves diverse patients.
2.  Read each question carefully and choose the option that best reflects your experience and opinions.
3.  For questions with multiple options, choose all options that apply.
4.  For questions that ask for a numerical rating (e.g., Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor), choose the rating that best reflects your opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Healthcare Providers** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_1`): This question asks about the type of healthcare providers you work with. Choose the type of healthcare providers that best describes your work. Options: Primary Care Providers, Specialty Care Providers, Community Health Workers.
* **Patient Care Experience** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_2`): This question asks about your experience with patient care. Please type in a brief description of your experience.
* **Staff Diversity** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_3`): This question asks about the diversity of your staff. Choose all options that apply. Options: Diverse and Inclusive Workforce, Non-Diverse and Exclusive Workforce, Diverse but Non-Inclusive Workforce.
* **Language Accessibility** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_4`): This question asks about the language accessibility of our services. Choose the rating that best reflects your opinion. Options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Accessibility of Services** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_5`): This question asks about the accessibility of our services. Choose all options that apply. Options: Very Accessible, Somewhat Accessible, Not Very Accessible, Not At All Accessible.
* **Patient-Centered Care** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_6`): This question asks about the patient-centered care in our services. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion. Options: Patient-Centered Care, Non-Patient-Centered Care, Somewhat Patient-Centered Care.
* **Patient Feedback** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_7`): This question asks about patient feedback. Please type in a brief description of your experience with patient feedback.
* **Clinic Accessibility** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_8`): This question asks about the accessibility of our clinic. Choose the rating that best reflects your opinion. Options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Services Accessibility** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_9`): This question asks about the accessibility of our services. Choose all options that apply. Options: Very Accessible, Somewhat Accessible, Not Very Accessible, Not At All Accessible.
* **Patient-Centered Care** (`health_equity_and_inclusion_survey_10`): This question asks about the patient-centered care in our services. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion. Options: Patient-Centered Care, Non-Patient-Centered Care, Somewhat Patient-Centered Care.

## Tips

*  Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
*  If you are unsure about an option, choose the option that best reflects your experience and opinions.
*  This form is meant to be a helpful tool for healthcare providers to evaluate their own health equity and inclusion practices. It is not a pass/fail evaluation.
