# Urology Grand Rounds Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the Urology Grand Rounds, a regular educational session for healthcare professionals. The purpose of this form is to help organizers understand attendees' opinions and suggestions about the session's content, speakers, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please select the corresponding answer options for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Rating** (1, number, optional): This field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from 1 to 5. Please provide a rating that reflects your opinion about the session's content and quality.
* **Overall Rating** (2, number, optional): Similar to the session rating, this field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Session Suggestions** (3, text, optional): Here, you can provide any suggestions for future sessions. This could be related to the speakers, content, or logistics. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Speaker Rating** (4, number, optional): This field is for rating the performance of the speaker(s) during the session. Please provide a rating that reflects your opinion about the speaker's communication style, expertise, and presentation skills.
* **Content Rating** (5, number, optional): This field is for rating the quality and relevance of the session's content. Please consider the accuracy, comprehensiveness, and relevance of the information presented during the session.
* **Organization** (6, text, optional): If you're associated with an organization or institution, please provide it in this field to help us understand your affiliation with the urology community.
* **Job Title** (7, text, optional): If you're a healthcare professional with a specific job title, please provide it in this field to help us better understand your role within the urology community.
* **Contact Email** (8, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further questions or follow-up discussions, please provide your email address in this field. This is optional and won't be displayed publicly.
* **Contact Phone** (9, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further questions or follow-up discussions, please provide your phone number in this field. This is optional and won't be displayed publicly. 

Please note that all fields are optional, and you may provide feedback only on the fields that you feel are relevant to you. Thank you for your participation in this form!

## Tips

* Be specific when providing feedback, as it will help us improve future sessions.
* If you have any personal or company-specific comments, please keep them to yourself.
* This form is designed to be completed within a few minutes; if you have extensive feedback, consider contacting us directly.
* Your feedback and suggestions are greatly appreciated and will help us improve the Urology Grand Rounds experience.
