# Life Saving Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to collect information on your attitudes and experiences related to life-saving measures such as CPR.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by reviewing the form's pages and questions.
2. Fill out each page in order, using the navigation buttons to move between pages.
3. Take your time to answer each question carefully, and don't worry if you need to refer back to the form later.
4. For questions with options, select the one that best reflects your attitude or experience.
5. For questions with numerical fields (e.g., number), enter a whole number only.
6. If you're unsure about a question or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact the researchers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: School settings

* **School settings** (`school_settings`, text, required): Please describe your current school setting or work environment.
* **Confidence in CPR** (`confidence_in_cpr`, select_one, required): How confident are you in your ability to perform CPR?
	+ 'Yes' - You are confident in your ability to perform CPR.
	+ 'No' - You are not confident in your ability to perform CPR.
* **Training needs** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, required): Which of the following training needs do you have for life-saving measures such as CPR?
	+ Option 1 - More training on CPR skills.
	+ Option 2 - More training on first aid equipment.
	+ Option 3 - More training on other life-saving measures.
* **First aid kit availability** (`first_aid_kit_availability`, number, optional): How many first aid kits are available in your school or work setting?
* **CPR equipment availability** (`cpr_equipment_availability`, number, optional): How many pieces of CPR equipment are available in your school or work setting?
* **CPR training hours** (`cpr_training_hours`, number, optional): How many hours of CPR training have you received?
* **CPR certification** (`cpr_certification`, select_multiple, optional): What type of CPR certification do you have?
	+ Option 1 - Basic Life Support Certification.
	+ Option 2 - Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Certification.
	+ Option 3 - Other (please specify).
* **Confidence in CPR** (`cpr_confidence`, select_one, required): How confident are you in your ability to perform CPR?
	+ 'Yes' - You are confident in your ability to perform CPR.
	+ 'No' - You are not confident in your ability to perform CPR.
* **Frequency of CPR** (`cpr_frequency`, select_one, optional): How often do you perform CPR?
	+ 'Yes' - You perform CPR frequently.
	+ 'No' - You do not perform CPR.
* **CPR training** (`cpr_training`, select_one, optional): Have you received CPR training?
	+ 'Yes' - You have received CPR training.
	+ 'No' - You have not received CPR training.
* **Type of CPR certification** (`cpr_certification_type`, select_multiple, optional): What type of CPR certification do you hold?
	+ Option 1 - Basic Life Support Certification.
	+ Option 2 - Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Certification.
	+ Option 3 - Other (please specify).
* **Status of CPR certification** (`cpr_certification_status`, select_one, required): What is the status of your CPR certification?
	+ Active - Your certification is current and active.
	+ Inactive - Your certification has expired or is not current.
* **First aid informal training** (`first_aid_informal_training`, select_one, optional): Have you received first aid training through informal means (e.g., online courses, videos, or apps)?
	+ 'Yes' - You have received first aid training through informal means.
	+ 'No' - You have not received first aid training through informal means.
* **Informal first aid training** (`informal_first_aid_training`, select_one, optional): Have you received first aid training through informal means (e.g., online courses, videos, or apps)?
	+ 'Yes' - You have received first aid training through informal means.
	+ 'No' - You have not received first aid training through informal means.
* **CPR training facility** (`cpr_training_facility`, select_one, optional): Where did you receive CPR training?
	+ 'Yes' - You received CPR training at a facility.
	+ 'No' - You did not receive CPR training at a facility.

Please note that some questions are marked as optional, which means you can choose to answer them or not. However, your responses will help us better understand your attitudes and experiences, so please take the opportunity to share your thoughts and feelings if you're able.
