# Skincare SPF Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your skincare habits, specifically your sunscreen usage habits, to help us create more effective skincare products and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to your sunscreen usage habits. You can choose as many answers as necessary for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you use sunscreen?**
  (sunscreen_frequency, select_multiple, required: false)
  Answer: How often do you use sunscreen? Choose all that apply:
  + Daily
  + Occasionally
  + Rarely
  + Never
* **What SPF level do you use?**
  (spf_level, select_one, required: false)
  Answer: What SPF level do you use?
  + 15
  + 30
  + 50
* **What type of sunscreen do you use?**
  (sunscreen_type, select_one, required: false)
  Answer: What type of sunscreen do you use?
  + Aerosol
  + Lotion
  + Cream
  + Spray
* **How do you apply sunscreen?**
  (apply_suntan, select_one, required: false)
  Answer: How do you apply sunscreen?
  + Hand
  + Spray
  + Roll-on
* **What ingredients do you look for in a sunscreen?**
  (sunscreen_ingredients, select_multiple, required: false)
  Answer: What ingredients do you look for in a sunscreen? Choose all that apply:
  + Zinc
  + Titanium
  + Fragrance
  + Moisturizer
* **Age**
  (age, number, required: false)
  Answer: Your age
* **Location**
  (location, text, required: false)
  Answer: Where you live/work/go for work
* **Duration**
  (duration, time, required: false)
  Answer: How long have you been using sunscreen?
* **Additional note**
  (note, note, required: false)
  Answer: Any additional comments about your sunscreen usage habits

## Tips
* Answer each question truthfully to help us create more effective products and recommendations.
* If you have any additional information or comments, use the "Additional note" field.
