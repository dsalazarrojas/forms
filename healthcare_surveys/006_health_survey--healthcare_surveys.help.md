# health-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health survey form is designed to collect patient information and health-related data. This form aims to provide a comprehensive overview of a patient's health condition and medical history. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the patient's medical needs are understood correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Patient Info" page and fill in your personal details (name, etc.) if you're completing the form as a patient.
2. On the "Symptoms" page, choose all the symptoms that apply to your current health condition from the dropdown list. You can select multiple symptoms.
3. On the "Medical History" page, provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your healthcare provider.
4. On the "Test Result" page, select the outcome of your recent medical test (if applicable).
5. On the "Medication" page, list any medications you're currently taking.
6. On the "Allergies" page, select any medications or substances you're allergic to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (Patient Info, Text, Optional): Enter your personal details such as your name, contact information, etc.
- **Symptoms** (Symptoms, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the symptoms that apply to your current health condition from the dropdown list. Symptoms include:
	+ Idiopathic: No known cause or origin.
	+ Infectious: Caused by an infectious agent (bacteria, virus, etc.).
	+ Allergic: Triggered by an allergen or substance.
	+ Autoimmune: Condition where your immune system attacks your own body.
- **Medical History** (Medical History, Text, Optional): Provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your healthcare provider.
- **Test Result** (Test Result, Select One, Optional): Select the outcome of your recent medical test (if applicable).
	+ Positive: Indicates a confirmed diagnosis or presence of a disease.
	+ Negative: Indicates no presence of a disease or no diagnosis.
	+ Inconclusive: Uncertain or unclear diagnosis.
- **Medication** (Medication, Text, Optional): List any medications you're currently taking.
- **Allergies** (Allergies, Select Multiple, Required): Select any medications or substances you're allergic to.
	+ Penicillin
	+ Erythromycin
	+ Ciprofloxacin
