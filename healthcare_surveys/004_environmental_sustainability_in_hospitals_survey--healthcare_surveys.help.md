# Environmental Sustainability In Hospitals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help hospitals assess and improve their environmental sustainability practices. It is intended to be completed by healthcare professionals who are responsible for implementing and maintaining sustainable practices in the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
