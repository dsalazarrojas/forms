# AI Assisted Care Workflow Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Assisted Care Workflow Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from healthcare professionals on their interactions with AI-assisted care workflows. This form is intended to identify areas of improvement and provide insights for developers to create a better AI system for healthcare professionals and patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Provide your full name**: Enter your full name as a healthcare professional.
2.  **Select your healthcare role**: Choose your primary role in healthcare from the list of options.
3.  **Specify your department or unit**: Enter the name of your department or unit.
4.  **Date of evaluation**: Select the date of your evaluation.
5.  **Choose the AI system used**: Select the AI system you are evaluating.
6.  **Frequency of use**: Choose how often you use AI-assisted tools in your workflow.
7.  **Used AI features**: Select all the features of the AI system you regularly use.
8.  **Satisfaction with accuracy**: Rate the accuracy of AI recommendations from 1 to 5.
9.  **Efficiency improvement**: Choose how AI has improved your workflow efficiency.
10. **Time saved**: Estimate the time saved daily due to AI use.
11. **Trust level**: Rate your level of trust in the AI system's recommendations from 1 to 5.
12. **Main challenges**: Select all the challenges you face with AI-assisted care.
13. **Patient concerns**: Choose how often patients express concerns about AI involvement in their care.
14. **Error incidents**: Choose whether you have encountered errors or incorrect recommendations from the AI system.
15. **Improvement suggestions**: Select the top 3 improvements you would like to see in AI-assisted care.
16. **Additional training needs**: Enter any additional training you need to effectively use AI tools.
17. **Recommendation likelihood**: Rate how likely you are to recommend the AI system to colleagues.
18. **Additional feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about AI-assisted care workflows.
