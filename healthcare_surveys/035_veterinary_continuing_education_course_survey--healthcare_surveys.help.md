# Veterinary Continuing Education Course Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from veterinary professionals about their experience and preferences for continuing education courses. This data will help educational institutions or organizations improve their course offerings and better serve their target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role within the veterinary profession from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
3. Provide your email address to allow us to contact you with any updates or further information.
4. Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for contact purposes).
5. Specify your department or specialty (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of your location (city, state, or hospital name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role?** (Veterinarian/Veterinary student/Veterinary technician/Other): Select the option that best describes your role within the veterinary profession.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email**: Provide your email address to allow us to contact you with any updates or further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for contact purposes).
* **Department**: If applicable, specify your department or specialty (e.g., surgery, dentistry, etc.).
* **Location**: Enter the name of your location (city, state, or hospital name).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible to provide valuable insights.
* If you have any suggestions or comments about the form or continuing education courses, feel free to provide them in the space below.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form. Your input is essential to help us improve our continuing education courses.
