# Health Equity Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Equity Initiative Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from healthcare providers on their experiences and observations related to the initiative's impact and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your current role or affiliation:
  * Option 1: Healthcare Provider
  * Option 2: Other (please specify)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (`contact_page`): Please select the option that best describes your current role or affiliation:
  * Option 1: Healthcare Provider
  * Option 2: Other (please specify)
* **Email** (`email_page`): Enter your email address where we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone_page`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about your evaluation.
* **Date** (`date_page`): Select the date you first became aware of the Health Equity Initiative or the date you last interacted with the initiative.
* **Time** (`time_page`): Select the time you first became aware of the Health Equity Initiative or the time you last interacted with the initiative.
* **Additional Comments** (implicit, not shown in YAML): This section is not explicitly shown in the YAML, but it is implied as a field that allows for free-form text. Please feel free to add any additional comments or suggestions regarding the Health Equity Initiative.
