# Dietary Habits And Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about an individual's dietary habits and frequency of eating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the on-screen instructions and fill in the required fields. Each field is labeled clearly, so take a moment to read each label to understand what it is asking for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (text, required)
Enter your first name in this field.

### **Last Name** (text, required)
Enter your last name in this field.

### **Email** (email, required)
Enter your email address in this field. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you.

### **Phone Number** (text, required)
Enter your phone number in this field.

### **How often do you eat breakfast?** (select_one, required)
Choose how often you eat breakfast from the options provided:
  * Every day
  * Some days
  * Rarely
  * Never

### **What time do you usually eat dinner?** (time, required)
Choose the time you usually eat dinner from the options provided. Please use the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).

### **Do you eat any of the following foods?** (select_multiple, required)
Select any of the foods you eat from the options provided:
  * Fresh fruit
  * Leafy greens
  * Nuts
  * Seeds

### **Additional Notes** (note, optional)
If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share about your dietary habits, please enter them here. This field is optional, but any information you provide will help us better understand your preferences.

### Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns about this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for your convenience and will help us to better understand your dietary habits and preferences.
