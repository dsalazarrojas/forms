# Public Health Nutrition Education Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from community health workers and other public health professionals on the effectiveness and impact of nutrition education sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page carefully. You will be guided through a series of questions that ask about your experience and knowledge regarding nutrition education sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Introduction**: This page is a note type field with a label "Introduction". It's a general page that introduces the survey.

**What do you already know about nutrition?**: On this page, select all that apply from the list of options. Please choose 'Yes' if you already have some knowledge about nutrition, and 'No' if you do not.

* **Yes** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): If you already have some knowledge about nutrition.
* **No** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): If you do not have any knowledge about nutrition.

**How do you currently get your nutrition information?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose how you currently get your nutrition information.

* **Yes** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you currently get your nutrition information from a source.
* **No** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you do not currently get nutrition information from any source.

**How often do you get your nutrition information from a public health professional?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose how often you get your nutrition information from a public health professional.

* **Yes** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you often get your nutrition information from a public health professional.
* **No** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you do not often get nutrition information from a public health professional.

**How often do you get your nutrition information from a community health worker?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose how often you get your nutrition information from a community health worker.

* **Yes** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you often get your nutrition information from a community health worker.
* **No** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you do not often get nutrition information from a community health worker.

**Have you ever attended a community nutrition education session?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose if you have ever attended a community nutrition education session.

* **Yes** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you have ever attended a community nutrition education session.
* **No** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you have not ever attended a community nutrition education session.

**Have you attended a community nutrition education session in the last 6 months?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose if you have attended a community nutrition education session in the last 6 months.

* **Yes** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you have attended a community nutrition education session in the last 6 months.
* **No** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you have not attended a community nutrition education session in the last 6 months.

**What is your highest level of education completed?**: On this page, please enter your highest level of education completed.

* **<label>** (`number`, required/optional): Enter your highest level of education completed.

**How would you rate your overall knowledge about nutrition after attending the session?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose how you would rate your overall knowledge about nutrition after attending the session.

* **Good** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you would rate your overall knowledge about nutrition as good.
* **Fair** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you would rate your overall knowledge about nutrition as fair.

**How likely are you to recommend this session to a friend or family member?**: On this page, select only one option from the list. Please choose how likely you are to recommend this session to a friend or family member.

* **Yes** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you are likely to recommend this session to a friend or family member.
* **No** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you are not likely to recommend this session to a friend or family member.
