# Cardiovascular Health Outcomes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiovascular Health Outcomes Survey is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect data on patient demographics, medical history, lifestyle habits, and treatment information related to cardiovascular health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the Respondent Information section, which includes demographic data such as age and gender.
2. Answer the questions about the respondent's history of cardiovascular disease, smoking status, and blood pressure reading.
3. Provide information about the respondent's cholesterol levels and physical activity level.
4. List any medications they are taking for cardiovascular conditions.
5. Add any additional notes or follow-up recommendations from clinicians.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Age**: Enter the patient's age in years. 
* **Respondent Gender**: Select the patient's sex from the options provided.
* **History of Cardiovascular Disease**: Check 'True' if the patient has a prior diagnosis of heart disease.
* **Smoking Status**: Select 'Current smoker', 'Former smoker', or 'Never smoked' based on the patient's current tobacco use.
* **Most Recent Blood Pressure Reading**: Enter the patient's blood pressure reading (systolic over diastolic) if known.
* **Cholesterol Levels Known**: Enter any available cholesterol levels, typically total cholesterol.
* **Physical Activity Level**: Select 'Low', 'Moderate', or 'High' based on the patient's typical weekly activity level.
* **Medications for Cardiovascular Conditions**: List any current medications the patient is taking for cardiovascular conditions.
* **Notes and Follow-up Recommendations**: Enter any notes or follow-up recommendations from clinicians.
* **Respondent Information**: Note: This section is optional but contains demographic information that is helpful for research and data analysis. Please fill it out if applicable.
