# Caregiver Wellbeing Index Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to assess the well-being of caregivers, focusing on their relationship to the care receiver, caregiving hours, stress levels, sleep duration, perceived social support, burnout symptoms, coping strategies, need for additional support, immediate concerns, and any additional comments. This survey aims to identify potential areas of support and help caregivers better manage their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your information, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible, as this will help us better understand your situation.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **-- Caregiver Wellbeing Screening --
This is the introductory page of the survey. No action is required here.

### 2. **Respondent name**
* Label: Respondent name
* Type: Text
* Required: False
* Explanation: This field is optional and will be used to identify you for any follow-up conversations or support. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.

### 3. **Relationship to care receiver**
* Label: Relationship to care receiver
* Type: Select One
* Required: True
* Explanation: Please select how you are related to the person you care for (Family, Professional, Friend, Other).

### 4. **Average hours spent caring per week**
* Label: Average hours spent caring per week
* Type: Number
* Required: True
* Explanation: Estimate the average hours you spend caring for the care receiver each week. This can include physical care, emotional support, or other forms of care.

### 5. **Current stress level**
* Label: Current stress level
* Type: Select One
* Required: True
* Explanation: Assess your stress level over the past two weeks. Choose the option that best describes your feelings (Low, Moderate, High, Very High).

### 6. **Average sleep hours per night**
* Label: Average sleep hours per night
* Type: Number
* Required: False
* Explanation: Indicate your typical sleep duration in hours per night.

### 7. **Perceived social support**
* Label: Perceived social support
* Type: Select One
* Required: True
* Explanation: Assess how much support you feel you receive from friends, family, or your community (None, Some, Adequate, Strong).

### 8. **Burnout symptoms experienced**
* Label: Burnout symptoms experienced
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: False
* Explanation: Select all the burnout symptoms you have experienced (Fatigue, Sleep problems, Iritability, Loss of interest, Physical aches).

### 9. **Coping strategies used**
* Label: Coping strategies used
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: False
* Explanation: Select all the strategies you use to cope with caregiving (Exercise, Support groups, Professional counseling, Relaxation techniques, None).

### 10. **Need for additional support services**
* Label: Need for additional support services
* Type: Select One
* Required: True
* Explanation: Indicate if you would like to be contacted about support options (True, False).

### 11. **Immediate concerns or safety risks**
* Label: Immediate concerns or safety risks
* Type: Text
* Required: False
* Explanation: Describe any pressing issues or concerns related to the care receiver.

### 12. **Additional comments**
* Label: Additional comments
* Type: Text
* Required: False
* Explanation: Provide any other information that you think would be helpful for us to better understand your situation.

Please note that some fields are required for the survey, while others are optional. If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to reach out.
