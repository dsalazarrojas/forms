# operating_room_design_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operating room design survey form is designed to gather user feedback on the design of operating rooms in a healthcare setting. Its purpose is to collect data that will inform design decisions and improve the overall operating room experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the user type that best describes your role in the operating room.
2. Choose the room type that you typically work in or visit.
3. Provide your layout preferences (e.g., Comfortable, Functional, Aesthetic).
4. Select specific layout preferences (e.g., Comfortable, Functional, Aesthetic).
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter the start and end times of your shift.
7. Enter the date of your visit.
8. Rate your overall experience with the operating room design.
9. Select the department you belong to.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Type (1)** (`user_type`, select_one, required): Select the user type that best describes your role in the operating room (e.g., Doctor, Staff, Visitor).
* **User Type (2)** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to (e.g., Surgical, Recovery, ICU).
* **Room Type (3)** (`room_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the room type that you typically work in or visit (e.g., Surgeon, Recovery, ICU).
* **Layout Preferences (4)** (`layout_preferences`, note, optional): Provide your layout preferences (e.g., Comfortable, Functional, Aesthetic).
* **Design Preferences (5)** (`design_preferences`, note, optional): Select specific layout preferences (e.g., Comfortable, Functional, Aesthetic).

* **Email (8)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
