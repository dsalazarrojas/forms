# Human Cell Expansion Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for collecting feedback and information from individuals involved in the Human Cell Expansion process. It is used to gather data on samples, lab locations, contact information, and other relevant details. This form is typically used by researchers, lab technicians, or others involved in the Human Cell Expansion process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections as instructed, providing accurate and relevant information for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed (if required fields are present).
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown list for Lab Location.
4. Enter the correct contact information, including a valid email address.
5. Add any additional comments or comments 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample Info** (`sample_info`, text, required: false): Enter some sample information as instructed by the lab.
* **Lab Info** (`lab_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional lab information as instructed by the lab.
* **Lab Location** (`lab_location`, select_one, required: false): Select the correct lab location from the dropdown list:
	+ Lab 1
	+ Lab 2
	+ Lab 3
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Title** (`contact_person_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Contact Tel** (`contact_tel`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, required: false): Enter the date of submission in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time of Submission** (`time_of_submission`, time, required: false): Enter the time of submission in the correct format (hh:mm).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments_2`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date of Submission 2** (`date_of_submission_2`, date, required: false): Enter the date of submission 2 in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time of Submission 2** (`time_of_submission_2`, time, required: false): Enter the time of submission 2 in the correct format (hh:mm).

Note: This form has been designed to be as user-friendly as possible, but if you have any difficulties or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
