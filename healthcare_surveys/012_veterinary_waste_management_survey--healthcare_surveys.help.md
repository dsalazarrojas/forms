# Veterinary Waste Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from veterinary clinics and hospitals about their waste management practices. The data collected will be used to improve waste management policies and practices within the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of institution you are representing (e.g., Animal Care Facility, Clinic, Veterinary Hospital, etc.).
2. Choose the types of waste generated by your institution (e.g., Anatomical, Cytotoxic, Infectious, etc.).
3. Select the disposal practices used by your institution (e.g., On-site autoclaving, Off-site incineration, etc.).
4. Identify any challenges faced by your institution related to waste management (e.g., lack of training, high costs, etc.).
5. If applicable, select any training methods your institution has received (e.g., online courses, in-person workshops, etc.).
6. Provide contact information for the person responsible for waste management at your institution.
7. If applicable, specify any assigned tools used for waste management.
8. Upload any additional files or information related to waste management at your institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Type**
  (clinic_type): Select the type of institution you are representing. This will help us understand the specific needs and challenges of your institution.
* **Waste Types**
  (waste_types): Choose all the types of waste generated by your institution. This will help us understand the scope of waste management at your institution.
* **Disposal Practices**
  (disposal_practices): Select all the disposal practices used by your institution. This will help us understand how waste is handled and managed at your institution.
* **Challenges**
  (challenges): Identify any challenges faced by your institution related to waste management. This will help us understand the areas where improvement is needed.
* **Training**
  (training): If applicable, select any training methods received by your institution. This will help us understand how staff are educated and trained on waste management practices.
* **Contact Information**
  (contact_info): Provide contact information for the person responsible for waste management at your institution. This will enable us to reach out to you for further information or clarification.
* **Assigned Tool**
  (assigned_tool): If applicable, specify any tools assigned to your institution for waste management. This will help us understand the resources and tools available to manage waste.
* **Output File**
  (output_file): Upload any additional files or information related to waste management at your institution. This will provide context and supporting evidence for your responses.
