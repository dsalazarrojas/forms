# Virtual Health Consultation Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with virtual health consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the patient's name, email address, and phone number in the patient information section.
2.  Answer the multiple-choice questions honestly, selecting the appropriate options for each question.
3.  Rate the telehealth equipment and overall satisfaction using the numerical rating system.
4.  Provide open-ended comments about your experience and any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up.
* **Telehealth Visit** (`telehealth_visit`, date, optional): Select the date of your virtual health consultation.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you had a healthcare provider during the consultation.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of appointment you had (e.g., routine, emergency).
* **Telehealth Equipment** (`telehealth_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the telehealth equipment you used during the consultation (e.g., video conferencing tool, phone).
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 0-100.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you had clear and effective communication during the consultation.
* **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you encountered any technical issues during the consultation.
* **Telehealth Support** (`telehealth_support`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you received sufficient support from the telehealth platform.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the consultation on a scale of Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up or have any requests for further support.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the preferred method of follow-up contact (e.g., phone, email).
* **Healthcare Team** (`healthcare_team`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you had a healthcare team assigned to your consultation.
* **Telehealth Platform** (`telehealth_platform`, select_one, optional): Choose the telehealth platform used for your consultation.
* **Comments on Telehealth Platform** (`comments_telehealth_platform`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback on the telehealth platform.
* **Patient Survey Date** (`patient_survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, time, optional): Estimate the duration of your virtual health consultation.
* **Telehealth Equipment Rating** (`telehealth_equipment_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the telehealth equipment used during the consultation on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the consultation on a scale of Satisfied or Dissatisfied.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have previously completed a similar survey, please do not submit duplicate responses.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback, which will help us improve our virtual health services.
