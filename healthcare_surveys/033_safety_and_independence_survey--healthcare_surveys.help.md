# Safety And Independence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a user's personal safety and independence concerns. It helps healthcare professionals understand the user's care and support needs, independence levels, and safety concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information (Page 1).
2. Answer the care-related questions (Page 2).
3. Report your independence levels (Page 3).
4. Share information about your support network (Page 4).
5. Describe your safety concerns (Page 5).
6. Confirm your form submission (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **User Info** (user_info)
**Type:** text
**Required:** true
**Explanation:** Please enter your name, age, and contact information (if any).

### 2. **Care Info** (care_info)
**Type:** text
**Required:** true
**Explanation:** Describe your current care needs and medical conditions. Please include any allergies, medications, or special care requirements.

### 3. **Independence Info** (independence_info)
**Type:** text
**Required:** true
**Explanation:** Report your daily independence activities, such as managing your finances, cooking, or bathing. Specify which activities you need help with.

### 4. **Support Info** (support_info)
**Type:** text
**Required:** true
**Explanation:** Describe your support network, including family members, caregivers, or support staff.

### 5. **Safety Info** (safety_info)
**Type:** text
**Required:** true
**Explanation:** Share any safety concerns, such as falls, medication side effects, or mental health conditions that may affect your safety.

### 6. **Submission** (submission)
**Type:** text
**Required:** true
**Explanation:** Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your information accurately. Please review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
