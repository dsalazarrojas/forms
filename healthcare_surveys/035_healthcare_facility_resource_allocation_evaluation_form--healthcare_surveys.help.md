# healthcare_facility_resource_allocation_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by healthcare professionals to evaluate the resources and facilities in a healthcare setting. It helps to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately based on your experience and knowledge.
2. For each field, select the most appropriate answer or enter the relevant information.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Healthcare Facility (Page 1)
* **Healthcare Facility** (`healthcare_facility`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the healthcare facility being evaluated.
* **Available Resources** (`available_resources`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total number of resources available at the facility.
* **Resource Distribution** (`resource_distribution`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether resources are distributed fairly or not.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Healthcare Provider ID** (`healthcare_provider_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the healthcare provider evaluating the facility.

### Additional Information (Page 2)
* **Facility Evaluation** (`facility_evaluation`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the facility.
* **Operational Efficiency** (`operational_efficiency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments about the operational efficiency of the facility.
* **Resource Utilization** (`resource_utilization`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the percentage of resources being utilized.
* **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether resources are available or not.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns.

### Evaluation and Assessment (Page 3)
* **Healthcare Providers** (`healthcare_providers`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether healthcare providers are available or not.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of facility being evaluated.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of resource being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate the facility.
* **Resource Allocation Plan** (`resource_allocation_plan`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether a resource allocation plan is in place or not.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or concerns about the evaluation.
* **Assessment Score** (`assessment_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score between 1-10 to assess the facility.
* **Resource Availability Score** (`resource_availability_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate resource availability.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select how often the facility is evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the period of the evaluation.
* **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the facility.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of assessment being performed.
* **Resource Availability Frequency** (`resource_availability_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select how often resources are available.
* **Evaluation Frequency Score** (`evaluation_frequency_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate evaluation frequency.
* **Resource Availability Frequency Score** (`resource_availability_frequency_score`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score between 1-10 to evaluate resource availability frequency.

Please note that some fields are required, and you should fill them out accurately and honestly based on your experience and knowledge.
