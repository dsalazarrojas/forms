# Sustainable Healthcare Operations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on hospital operations and sustainability practices. It's a crucial tool for hospitals to assess their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the correct options for each field based on your hospital's current status.
2. For numerical fields, enter the exact numbers.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1, note, no): This field contains a brief description of the purpose and goals of the survey.
* **Hospital Name** (2, text, yes*): Enter the full name of your hospital.
* **Hospital Department** (3, text, yes*): Enter the department or unit within the hospital that this survey applies to.
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (4, select_one, yes*): Indicate whether your hospital has sustainability initiatives in place.
	+ Yes: The hospital has sustainability initiatives.
	+ No: The hospital does not have sustainability initiatives.
* **Current Challenges** (5, select_multiple, yes*): Identify the current challenges faced by your hospital.
	+ Yes: The hospital is facing this challenge.
	+ No: The hospital is not facing this challenge.
* **Future Goals** (6, select_one, yes*): Indicate your hospital's future goals for sustainability.
	+ Yes: The hospital aims to improve sustainability.
	+ No: The hospital does not have sustainability goals.
* **Environmental Impact** (7, select_one, no): Indicate the hospital's environmental impact.
	+ Yes: The hospital has a significant environmental impact.
	+ No: The hospital has a minimal environmental impact.
* **Operational Impact** (8, select_one, no): Indicate the hospital's operational impact.
	+ Yes: The hospital has a significant operational impact.
	+ No: The hospital has a minimal operational impact.
* **Staff Satisfaction** (9, number, yes*): Enter a score from 1-10 for staff satisfaction.
* **Staff Feedback** (10, text, yes*): Provide any additional feedback from staff on sustainability practices.
* **Date Completed** (11, date, yes*): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time Completed** (12, time, yes*): Enter the time you completed this survey.
* **Phone Number** (13, text, no): Enter the phone number of your hospital (if available).
* **Email Address** (14, email, no): Enter the email address of your hospital (if available).
* **Hospital Size** (15, select_one, no): Indicate the size of your hospital.
	+ Yes: The hospital is large.
	+ No: The hospital is small.
* **Number of Doctors** (16, number, no): Enter the number of doctors at your hospital.
* **Number of Patients** (17, number, no): Enter the average number of patients your hospital serves per week.
* **Number of Bedspaces** (18, number, no): Enter the number of bedspaces at your hospital.
* **Number of Rooms** (19, number, no): Enter the number of hospital rooms.
* **Number of Floors** (20, number, no): Enter the number of floors at your hospital.
* **Number of Female Doctors** (21, number, no): Enter the number of female doctors at your hospital.
* **Number of Nurses** (22, number, no): Enter the number of nurses at your hospital.
* **Number of Administrative Staff** (23, number, no): Enter the number of administrative staff at your hospital.
* **Number of Support Staff** (24, number, no): Enter the number of support staff at your hospital.
* **Submission** (25, select_one, yes*): Indicate whether your submission is complete and accurate.
