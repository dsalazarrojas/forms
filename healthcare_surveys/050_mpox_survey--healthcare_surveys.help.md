# Mpox Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Survey is designed to gather information on various aspects of mpox, a viral disease. This form collects data on public awareness, risk factors, symptoms, healthcare access, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully and honestly to provide accurate information.
2. You can select multiple options from the lists for some questions.
3. Some sections will have text fields for written responses or open-ended questions.
