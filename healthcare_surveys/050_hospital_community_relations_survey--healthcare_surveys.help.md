# Hospital Community Relations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from hospital staff, patients, and visitors about their experiences and perceptions of the hospital's community relations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Submission" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Satisfaction with Services** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): Please describe your experiences and perceptions of our hospital's services.
* **Services Used** (`services_used`, select_one, required): Select the services you have used during your visit or interaction with our hospital.
	+ Service 1
	+ Service 2
	+ Service 3
	+ Service 4
	+ Service 5
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Service Used Other** (`service_used_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous question, please describe the service you are referring to.
* **Community Communication** (`community_communication`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the ways you have been informed about our hospital's community relations.
	+ Newsletter
	+ Social Media
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text Message
	+ In Person
* **Satisfaction with Communication** (`satisfaction_with_communication`, select_one, required): Please express your satisfaction with our hospital's communication methods.
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree nor Disagree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide more information here.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions as honestly as possible.
- If you experience any issues or difficulties while completing this form, please don't hesitate to contact our team.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and provide us with your feedback!
