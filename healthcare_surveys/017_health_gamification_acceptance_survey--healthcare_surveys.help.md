# Health Gamification Acceptance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is a collection of questions that aim to understand your opinions and experiences with health gamification activities. Your input will help us improve the implementation and design of health gamification for your health care provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can select multiple answers for some questions and provide as much detail as you need in the text fields. Remember that all answers are anonymous and will be kept confidential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (note): This is an introductory note to the survey. Please read it before proceeding.

### How much do you currently use health related gamification in your daily activities?
* **How much do you currently use health related gamification in your daily activities?** (text): This question is about your current level of engagement with health-related gamification activities. Please describe your current use of health gamification in your daily life.

### Which health gamification activities do you use currently?
* **Which health gamification activities do you use currently?** (select_multiple): Select all the activities you use currently from the list below:
	+ I use health gamification activities
	+ I don't use health gamification activities
	+ Not sure

### How often do you use health gamification activities in your daily life?
* **How often do you use health gamification activities in your daily life?** (number): This question is about the frequency of your engagement with health gamification activities. Please answer on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means you never use health gamification and 10 means you use it very frequently.

### Would you like to see more health gamification activities offered by your health care provider?
* **Would you like to see more health gamification activities offered by your health care provider?** (select_one): Please select one of the options below:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Somewhat
	+ Not sure
	+ No

### What motivates you about health gamification?
* **What motivates you about health gamification?** (text): This question is about what drives your engagement with health gamification activities. Please describe what motivates you to use health gamification.

### Would you like to participate in future studies about health gamification?

	+ Yes, definitely

	+ Not sure
	+ No

* **How do you feel about using your electronic health records in health gamification?** (select_one): Please select one of the options below:

* **How important is it that health gamification is fun?** (select_one): Please select one of the options below:

	+ Not very important
	+ Not at all

* **Would you like to be able to share your progress in health gamification with friends?** (select_one): Please select one of the options below:
	+ Yes, definitely
