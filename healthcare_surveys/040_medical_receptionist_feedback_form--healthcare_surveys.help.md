# Medical Receptionist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Receptionist Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the medical receptionist. This feedback will help the clinic to understand areas that need improvement to provide better patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in the Receptionist Name field with the name of the receptionist who provided you with service.
- Rate the Staff Rating field using the number rating scale provided.
- Rate the Communication Rating field using the number rating scale provided.
- Rate the Professionalism Rating field using the number rating scale provided.
- In the Front Desk Experience field, describe your overall experience with the receptionist.
- In the Comments field, provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
- Select your Clinic Location from the dropdown menu provided.
- Check your Email field for accuracy.
- Check your Phone Number field for accuracy.
- Click the Submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Rating** (`Staff Rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the staff from 1 to 5.
* **Receptionist Name** (`Receptionist Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the receptionist who provided you with service.
* **Communication Rating** (`Communication Rating`, number, required): Rate the receptionist's communication skills from 1 to 5.
* **Professionalism Rating** (`Professionalism Rating`, number, required): Rate the receptionist's professionalism from 1 to 5.
* **Front Desk Experience** (`Front Desk Experience`, text, required): Describe your overall experience with the receptionist.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Clinic Location** (`Clinic Location`, select_one, optional): Select the clinic where you received service.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, not required): Click this button to send your feedback, but note that it is not required to submit this form.
