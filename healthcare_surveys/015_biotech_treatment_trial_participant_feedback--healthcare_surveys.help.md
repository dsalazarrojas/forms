# Biotech Treatment Trial Participant Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in biotech treatment trials. Your responses will help us understand your experiences, satisfaction, and perception of the treatment, as well as identify areas of improvement. Please provide honest and detailed feedback to help us better design future trials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all required fields (denoted by asterisks (\*)).
2. Rate your satisfaction and effectiveness of the treatment using numbers (1-10).
3. Choose the best description that fits your experience in select_one fields.
4. Provide clear and brief answers in text fields.
5. Review and edit your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID** (Participant ID, text, *): Your study identification number, which helps us track your progress and results.
* **Feedback Date** (Feedback Date, date, *): The date you are providing this feedback, which is used for tracking and data analysis.
* **Overall Treatment Satisfaction** (Overall Treatment Satisfaction, number, *): Rate your satisfaction with the treatment from 1 to 10.
* **How Effective Was the Treatment** (How Effective Was the Treatment, number, *): Rate the effectiveness of the treatment from 1 to 10.
* **Symptom Improvement** (Symptom Improvement, select_one, *): Choose the best description that fits your experience:
	+ Significantly Improved
	+ Moderately Improved
	+ Slightly Improved
	+ No Change
	+ Worsened
* **Timeline for Improvement** (Timeline for Improvement, select_one, ): Choose when you noticed improvement:
	+ Within Days
	+ Within 1-2 Weeks
	+ Within 1 Month
	+ After 1 Month
	+ No Improvement Yet
* **Did You Experience Side Effects** (Did You Experience Side Effects, select_one, *): Choose the best description that fits your experience:
	+ No Side Effects
	+ Mild Side Effects
	+ Moderate Side Effects
	+ Severe Side Effects
* **Describe Side Effects Experienced** (Describe Side Effects Experienced, text, ): Provide a clear and brief description of any side effects you experienced.
* **Side Effects Impact on Daily Life** (Side Effects Impact on Daily Life, select_one, ): Choose the best description that fits your experience:
	+ No Impact
	+ Minimal Impact
	+ Moderate Impact
	+ Significant Impact
	+ Unable to Continue Treatment
* **Duration of Side Effects** (Duration of Side Effects, select_one, ): Choose how long side effects lasted:
	+ Resolved Quickly
	+ Persistent
	+ Intermittent
	+ Still Experiencing
* **Treatment Adherence** (Treatment Adherence, select_one, *): Choose how well you followed the treatment plan:
	+ Excellent - Followed All Instructions
	+ Good - Missed Few Doses
	+ Fair - Missed Some Doses
	+ Poor - Missed Many Doses
* **Reason for Missed Doses** (Reason for Missed Doses, text, ): If applicable, provide a reason for any missed doses.
* **Difficulty Following Treatment Plan** (Difficulty Following Treatment Plan, select_one, ): Choose how easy or hard it was to follow the treatment plan:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Somewhat Easy
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Very Difficult

	+ Yes, Definitely
