# Veterinary Data Sharing Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your data sharing practices in the veterinary sector, including the types of data you share, the tools you use for sharing, the barriers you face, and your willingness to share data for research and public health projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Read each question carefully to understand its context and purpose.
2. Select all the options that apply to your data sharing practices.
3. If you select "Other" in any of the questions, please provide a brief description of your answer.
4. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **What data do you share?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the types of data you share, such as clinical data, treatment data, patient data, research data, or other types of data you share.
* **What tools do you use?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This question asks about the tools you use for data sharing, such as data sharing platforms, data analytics tools, data repositories, or other tools.
* **What are the main barriers?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the barriers you face when sharing data, including data quality, data security, data format, lack of standardization, cost, or other barriers.
* **Will you share data for research?** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your willingness to share data for research purposes.
* **Will you share data for public health projects?** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your willingness to share data for public health projects.
* **Will you share data with partners?** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about your willingness to share data with partners.
* **Who are your partners?** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about the organizations or individuals you share data with.
* **Other data sharing purposes?** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about any other purposes for which you share data.
* **Other tools?** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about any other tools you use for data sharing.

Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
