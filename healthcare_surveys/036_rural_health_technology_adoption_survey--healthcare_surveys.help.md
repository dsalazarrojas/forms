# Rural Health Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rural Health Technology Adoption Survey aims to gather information about the adoption and use of different health technologies among rural populations to understand their effectiveness, accessibility, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. **Answer all required questions**: Questions marked as "required" are essential to providing useful insights and must be answered. Please ensure you answer all required questions truthfully.
2. **Select all that apply**: If a question allows for multiple selections (e.g., select_multiple), choose all that apply to your situation.
3. **Choose one answer**: If a question allows for only one selection (e.g., select_one), choose the most accurate response.
4. **Provide detailed answers**: When asked to write comments or describe your experience, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Technologies Used** (`health_technologies`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
The question asks about the health technologies you use. Health technologies include:
	+ Telehealth: Virtual visits with healthcare providers through video conferencing.
	+ Health Information Exchange (HIE): Electronic access to medical records.
	+ Mobile Health Applications: Mobile apps for tracking health data or accessing health services.
	+ Health Information Systems (HIS): Electronic health records and administrative systems.
	+ Telephony (e.g., phone, text, video calls): Phone, text, or video calls with healthcare providers.
	+ Patient Portal: Secure, online access to your health information.
	+ Electronic Health Records (EHRs): Electronic health records for healthcare providers.
	+ Health and Wellness Apps (e.g., fitness, nutrition): Mobile apps for health and wellness tracking.
Select all health technologies you use.
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, `number`, **required**):
This question asks about how often you use the health technologies selected above.
* **Access to Digital Technologies** (`access`, `text`, **optional**):
If you experience any challenges accessing or using health technologies, please describe them in detail.
* **Barriers to Use** (`barriers`, `text`, **optional**):
Please describe any barriers you experienced while trying to use health technologies (e.g., cost, language, internet access).
* **Impact on Healthcare Outcomes** (`healthcare_outcomes`, `select_one`, **required**):
Choose one of the following answers:
	+ Positive: The use of health technologies improved your health outcomes.
	+ Negative: The use of health technologies had a negative impact on your health outcomes.
	+ Neutral: The use of health technologies had no impact on your health outcomes.
* **Open-ended Comments** (`comments`, `note`, **optional**):
Please provide any other comments or suggestions you have about your experience with health technologies.
* **Other Technologies Used** (`other_technologies`, `select_multiple`, **optional**):
If you have used any other health technologies not listed above, please select them.
