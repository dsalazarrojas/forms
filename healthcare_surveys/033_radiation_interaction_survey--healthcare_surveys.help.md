# Radiation Interaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Radiation Interaction Survey is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about your experiences with radiation interactions. This form is used to gather data that will help our team better understand and address potential issues related to radiation interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, as indicated in the field-by-field explanation below.
2. Fill in the required fields with your answers.
3. For fields with options, select one or multiple answers.
4. For fields with a date or time field, enter the correct date or time.
5. Enter your comments or answers in the text fields.
6. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
