# Adolescent Body Image Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help healthcare professionals, researchers, and educators understand the body image perceptions and experiences of adolescents. Please answer honestly, and your responses are confidential.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please read each question carefully and answer according to your experiences and feelings.
2. Select one or more answers from the provided options for each question.
3. If you feel comfortable sharing additional information, please use the space provided.
4. If you need support or have questions, you can provide your contact email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Overall Body Satisfaction

* **Overall Body Satisfaction**: This question is about how you generally feel about your body. Please select the answer that best describes your feelings.

  * Very satisfied
  * Satisfied
  * Neutral
  * Dissatisfied
  * Very dissatisfied

### 2. Influence Sources

* **Influence Sources**: Think about the things that influence how you feel about your body. Select all that apply:

  * Social media
  * Peer groups
  * Family
  * Magazines and Ads
  * Television and Movies

### 3. Media Platform Influence

* **Media Platform Influence**: If you could choose a social media platform that impacts you the most, please type it in.

### 4. Frequency of Comparison

* **Frequency of Comparison**: How often do you compare your appearance to others? Please select the option that best describes your behavior.

  * Daily
  * Weekly
  * Occasionally
  * Rarely
  * Never

### 5. Focus on Specific Features

* **Focus on Specific Features**: Think about whether you focus on parts of your body that you dislike. Please select the answer that best describes your behavior.

  * Always
  * Often
  * Sometimes
  * Seldom
  * Never

### 6. Impact on Mood

* **Impact on Mood**: How much does your body image affect your mood? Please select the answer that best describes your experience.

  * Significant impact
  * Moderate impact
  * Neutral
  * Minor impact
  * No impact

### 7. Positive Body Aspects

* **Positive Body Aspects**: Describe one thing you appreciate about your body.

### 8. Pressure to Change Appearance

* **Pressure to Change Appearance**: Do you feel pressure from others to look a certain way? Please select the answer that best describes your experience.

  * High pressure
  * Moderate pressure
  * Low pressure
  * No pressure

### 9. Interest in Support Groups

* **Interest in Support Groups**: Would you be interested in attending body positivity workshops? Please select the answer that best describes your interest.

  * True
  * False
  * Maybe

### 10. Contact Email - Optional

* **Contact Email - Optional**: If you would like to be contacted for support, please provide your email address.

### 11. Additional Comments

* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns about your body image, please share them here.
