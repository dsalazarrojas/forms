# Pressure Injury Prevention Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pressure Injury Prevention Survey is used to gather information on the effectiveness and challenges associated with pressure injury prevention practices from healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section and fill in the required information. Each section is explained in more detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Prevention Practices
* **Prevention Practices** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): This field is used to gather information on the different prevention practices that healthcare professionals use to prevent pressure injuries. Please provide a short description of any practices you use or have used in your experience.
### Training Needs
* **Training Needs** (id: 2, type: select_one, required: false): This field is used to determine what training the healthcare professional feels they need to improve their pressure injury prevention practices. Please select one of the options that best describes what you need training on.
