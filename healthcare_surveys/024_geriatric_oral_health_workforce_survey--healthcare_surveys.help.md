# Geriatric Oral Health Workforce Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from healthcare workers about their experiences and perceptions of providing care to geriatric patients. The survey aims to understand the challenges and barriers faced by healthcare workers in providing care to geriatric patients, as well as their training needs and priorities in this area.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Age, Email, Phone) in the "Personal Information" section.
2. Answer questions about your experience working with geriatric patients, including whether you provide care to geriatric patients, your training in geriatric care, and your confidence and priority level in providing care to these patients.
3. Describe any barriers you face when providing care to geriatric patients.
4. Indicate whether you have received any geriatric care training or education.
5. Select any additional barriers that you face when providing care to geriatric patients, and describe how you would address these barriers.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving care to geriatric patients.
