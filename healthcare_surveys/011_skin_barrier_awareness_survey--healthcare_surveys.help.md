# Skin Barrier Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skin health awareness, habits, and concerns. Your responses will help us understand how to better support your skin health.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your birth date (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select the type of skin you have (normal, dry, oily, combination, sensitive).
7. Select any skin concerns you may have (acne, aging, irritation, sensitivity, other).
8. Describe your skin habits (e.g., skincare routine, products used).
9. Enter your education level (number of years of education).
10. Enter your occupation.
11. Enter the type of work you do.
12. Indicate if you use skin care products (yes/no).
13. Describe your current knowledge about skin health.
14. Select any skin health concerns you may have (yes/no).
15. Enter the brand(s) of skin care products you use.
16. Enter how often you perform skincare (number of times per week).
17. Indicate if you use skin protection methods (yes/no).
18. Describe any skin health concerns you may have.
19. Enter any skin care services you have used.
20. Enter any services related to skin health you have received.
21. Describe any additional skin education you have received.
22. Enter any additional information about your skin awareness.
23. Enter any additional information about skin health education.
24. Enter any additional information about skin health care.
25. Add any additional notes about your skin health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your birth date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of skin you have (normal, dry, oily, combination, sensitive).
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select any skin concerns you may have (acne, aging, irritation, sensitivity, other).
* **Skin Habits** (`skin_habits`, text, optional): Describe your skin habits.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, number, optional): Enter your education level.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of work you do.
* **Skin Care Products** (`skin_care_products`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you use skin care products (yes/no).
* **Skin Knowledge** (`skin_barrier_knowledge`, text, optional): Describe your current knowledge about skin health.
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_barrier_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select any skin health concerns you may have (yes/no).
* **Skincare Brand** (`skincare_brand`, text, optional): Enter the brand(s) of skin care products you use.
* **Skincare Frequency** (`skincare_frequency`, number, optional): Enter how often you perform skincare.
* **Skin Protection** (`skin_protection`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you use skin protection methods (yes/no).
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_health_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any skin health concerns.
* **Skincare Services** (`skincare_services`, text, optional): Enter any skin care services you have used.
* **Skin Health Services** (`skin_health_services`, text, optional): Enter any services related to skin health you have received.
* **Skin Education** (`skin_education`, text, optional): Describe any additional skin education you have received.
* **Skin Awareness** (`skin_awareness`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about skin awareness.
* **Skin Health Education** (`skin_health_education`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about skin health education.
* **Skin Health Care** (`skin_health_care`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about skin health care.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your skin health.
