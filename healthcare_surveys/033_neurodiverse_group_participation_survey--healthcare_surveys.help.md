# Neurodiverse Group Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from neurodiverse groups to understand their needs and preferences for participation, accessibility, and other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the form by providing accurate and complete answers to the questions.
3. Make sure to provide contact information so that we can follow up with you.
4. If you need any assistance or accommodations, please select the relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself or the participant group you represent. This can be a name, organization, or description.

### Accessibility Needs
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose one or none of the options below:
		- **Wheelchair**: Are you in need of wheelchair accessibility accommodations?
		- **Visual Impairment**: Do you have any visual impairments that require assistance?
		- **Hearing Impairment**: Do you have any hearing impairments that require assistance?
		- **None**: No accessibility needs

### Accessibility Accommodations
* **Accessibility Accommodations** (`accessibility_accommodations`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply:
		- **Wheelchair Accessibility**: Do you require wheelchair accessibility accommodations?
		- **American Sign Language (ASL)**: Would you prefer to communicate through American Sign Language?
		- **Large Print**: Do you need large print materials or assistance?
		- **Braille**: Do you require Braille materials or assistance?

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): This is a note field for you to acknowledge that you understand the terms of the survey.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information so that we can follow up with you.

### Submission Tracking
* **Submission Tracking** (`submission_tracking`, number, optional): This field is not currently used, but please leave it blank for future use.

### Submission Tracking 1
* **Submission Tracking 1** (`submission_tracking_1`, number, optional): This field is not currently used, but please leave it blank for future use.

### Submission Tracking 2
* **Submission Tracking 2** (`submission_tracking_2`, number, optional): This field is not currently used, but please leave it blank for future use.

## Tips
* If you need any assistance or accommodations, please select the relevant options in the "Accessibility Needs" and "Accessibility Accommodations" sections.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us through the "Contact Information" section.
