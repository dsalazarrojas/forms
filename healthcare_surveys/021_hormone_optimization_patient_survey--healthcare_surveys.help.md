# Hormone Optimization Patient Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hormone Optimization Patient Survey is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about patients' health and lifestyle habits related to hormone optimization. It consists of 11 questions that cover various aspects of the patient's medical history, symptoms, and lifestyle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. If a question does not apply to you, select "Not Applicable" or "Other (please specify)".
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (2)** (`Patient Info`, `number`, required): This field is intended for patients to provide demographic information such as age and date of birth. Please enter your age and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
* **Symptoms (3)** (`Symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all symptoms that apply to you, such as Fatigue, Weight gain, and Mood swings.
* **Medical History (4)** (`Medical History`, `text`, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Medications (5)** (`Medications`, `select_one`, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, please select one of the options: Birth control, Hormone replacement therapy, or Other (please specify).
* **Sleep Patterns (6)** (`Sleep Patterns`, `number`, optional): Please enter your typical sleep duration in hours.
* **Diet Restrictions (7)** (`Diet Restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you follow a specific diet, please select all that apply, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, or Gluten-free.
* **Social Life (8)** (`Social Life`, `text`, required): Describe your social life, including your relationships, work schedule, and social interactions.
* **Exercise Patterns (9)** (`Exercise Patterns`, `number`, optional): Please enter your typical exercise duration in hours.
* **Mental State (10)** (`Mental State`, `text`, required): Describe your current mental state, including any feelings of anxiety, stress, or depression.
* **Stress Levels (11)** (`Stress Levels`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options: Low, Moderate, or High.
* **Work Life (12)** (`Work Life`, `text`, required): Describe your work schedule and any changes you have made to your work-life balance.

## Tips

* Please be thorough in your answers, as they will help healthcare professionals better understand your health and lifestyle habits.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Remember to ask your healthcare provider about any medications or supplements you are currently taking, as they may interact with your hormone levels.
