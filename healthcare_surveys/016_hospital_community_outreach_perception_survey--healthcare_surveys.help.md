# Hospital Community Outreach Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the hospital's community outreach programs, services, and overall satisfaction. Your responses will help us improve our community engagement and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following pages and questions. You can choose one or multiple answers for each question, depending on the field type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Community Outreach** (`community_outreach`, number, required: false): This page is not actually present in the form, it's a page id 1. Please disregard. Instead, please consider it a placeholder. This field is intended to gather information about the community's perception of the hospital's outreach programs.
* **Services Used** (`services_page`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the services you have used or are interested in using from the hospital. Please select all that apply.
	+ Option 1: Service 1
	+ Option 2: Service 2
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction_page`, number, required: false): This question assesses your satisfaction with the services provided by the hospital. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_page`, note, required: false): Share any additional feedback or comments about the hospital's community outreach programs and services.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide your contact number, if you have one.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you first heard about the hospital's community outreach programs.
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
* **Additional Information About How You Heard** (`how_did_you_hear_page`, select_multiple, required: false): If you selected Option 1 or 2, please provide more details about how you heard about the hospital's community outreach programs.
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic`, select_one, required: false): Please select your demographic information.
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
* **Additional Demographic Information** (`question11`, select_multiple, required: false): If you selected Option 1 or 2, please provide more details about your demographic information.
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
* **Additional Demographic Information** (`question12`, note, required: false): Share any additional demographic information not captured in previous questions.
* **Additional Demographic Information** (`question13`, text, required: false): Provide any other demographic information you'd like to share.

Note that some fields may not be present in the actual form and are placeholders for the sake of this help guide.
