# Public Perception Of Health AI Tools Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from individuals working with or familiar with health AI tools to understand their perceptions of the current state of these tools and their potential for future development. Your input will help us understand how you use and experience health AI tools, what features are missing, and what you expect from future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select all options that apply to your experience with health AI tools.
3. Provide brief descriptions in text fields where prompted.
4. If you don't have experience with a particular health AI tool, select "None of the above" or "Other (please specify)".
5. Review your answers before submitting the survey to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the main goal of this survey?** (`survey_title`, `text`, optional): This question is a warm-up question to gauge your understanding of the survey's purpose.
* **Which of the following health AI tools do you work with or are familiar with?** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all health AI tools you have experience with, such as Google Cloud Healthcare API, Amazon Comprehend Medical, or IBM Watson Health.
* **Please describe your experience with health AI tools.** (`description`, `text`, optional): Share your overall experience, successes, and challenges with health AI tools in a brief paragraph.
* **What kind of healthcare-related survey are you most familiar with?** (`healthcare_survey`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of survey you're most familiar with, such as Healthcare Surveys, Clinical Trials, or Patient Outcomes.
* **What is your current form ID?** (`form_id`, `number`, optional): Enter your current form ID for participating in health surveys.
* **How often do you participate in health surveys?** (`healthcare_survey_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select how frequently you participate in health surveys, such as Frequently, Occasionally, or Rarely.
* **Briefly describe how you perceive health AI tools in the following categories.** (`description_2`, `note`, optional): In this text field, provide a brief description of your perception of health AI tools in each category.
* **How would you rate the ease of use of health AI tools for each category?** (`ai_category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Evaluate the ease of use of health AI tools in each category, such as Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, or Very difficult.
* **What features are missing from the current health AI tools in each category?** (`ai_category_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select missing features in each category, such as Data quality, Integration with other tools, or User interface.
* **What is your current form ID for participating in health surveys?** (`form_id_2`, `number`, optional): Enter your current form ID for participating in health surveys.
* **What kind of healthcare-related survey topic are you interested in learning more about?** (`healthcare_survey_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select topics you're interested in learning about, such as AI for healthcare data analysis, AI for healthcare data visualization, or AI for healthcare decision support systems.
* **What are the potential risks or limitations associated with using health AI tools?** (`description_3`, `note`, optional): Discuss potential risks or limitations associated with using health AI tools.
* **Which health AI tool would you recommend using for the following tasks?** (`assigned_tool_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select health AI tools you would recommend for specific tasks, such as Google Cloud Healthcare API, Amazon Comprehend Medical, or IBM Watson Health.
* **What are your expectations for future health AI tool development?** (`description_4`, `text`, optional): Share your expectations for future health AI tool development.
* **What is your current form ID?** (`form_id_3`, `number`, optional): Enter your current form ID, but this field is not actually used in the form.
