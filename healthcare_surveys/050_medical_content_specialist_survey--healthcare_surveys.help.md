# Medical Content Specialist Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about medical content specialists' qualifications, experiences, and background to better understand their skills and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. For select multiple fields, select all applicable options.
3. For select one fields, select only one option.
4. For time fields, enter the time in 24-hour format.
5. For date fields, enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Page 1**: 
    * **Form Page 1** (`form_page_1`, text, not required): This is the first page of the form. Please fill out any relevant information here.
* **Medical Background**: 
    * **Medical Background** (`medical_background_1`, text, not required): Enter your background in the medical field.
    * **Medical Background 2** (`medical_background_2`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable medical backgrounds that you have experience in.
    * **Medical Background 3** (`medical_background_3`, select one, required): Select one medical background that you specialize in.
* **Medical Specialist**: 
    * **Medical Specialist 1** (`medical_specialist_1`, time, not required): Enter the time you have been working as a medical specialist.
    * **Medical Specialist 2** (`medical_specialist_2`, text, not required): Enter any other relevant information about your medical specialization.
    * **Medical Specialist 3** (`medical_specialist_3`, number, not required): Enter your level of expertise in this area (e.g., 1-10).
    * **Medical Specialist 4** (`medical_specialist_4`, date, not required): Enter the date you received your qualification.
    * **Medical Specialist 5** (`medical_specialist_5`, select one, required): Select one medical specialization that you have.
    * **Medical Specialist 6** (`medical_specialist_6`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable medical specializations that you have.
    * **Medical Specialist 7** (`medical_specialist_7`, text, not required): Enter any other relevant information about your medical specialization.
* **Form Comments**: 
    * **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments about this form or your experiences. 

Note: Please fill out the remaining "Form Comments" fields as needed, following the same guidelines above. 

**Tips**: Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
