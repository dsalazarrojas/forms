# Hypertension Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences with communication from healthcare providers regarding hypertension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2.  Select the option that best describes your experience with each question.
3.  Provide any additional information you'd like to share in the "Comments" fields.
4.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
