# hospital_green_energy_transition_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help hospitals evaluate their current energy usage and identify potential areas for improvement. It will guide hospitals in creating a plan to transition to a more sustainable energy source.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field and provide information about your hospital's current energy usage and sustainability initiatives.
2. Answer each question in as much detail as possible, but feel free to skip any fields that are not applicable to your hospital.
3. Make sure to select all relevant options that apply to your hospital's energy management and sustainability goals.
