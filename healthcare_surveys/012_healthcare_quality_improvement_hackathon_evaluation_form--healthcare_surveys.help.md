# Healthcare Quality Improvement Hackathon Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Quality Improvement Hackathon Evaluation Form is designed to assess the success of a hackathon event focused on quality improvement in healthcare. This form will help organizers and participants evaluate the event's innovation, impact, clarity, effectiveness, sustainability, value, and other related factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the Event Id, which is the unique identifier for the event.
2. Rate the event's various aspects using the provided numerical ratings (e.g., Innovation, Impact, Clarity, Effectiveness, Sustainability, Value).
3. For each rating field, select one of the provided options.
4. In the Additional Comments field, provide any other thoughts or suggestions about the event.
5. If applicable, enter the Prophets Id or Prophets Id, which are not clearly defined, but may refer to a unique identifier or a specific feature of the hackathon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Id** (1, Number, Required): Enter the Event Id, which is the unique identifier for the event.
* **Guests Prophets** (2, Text, Optional): This field is intended for feedback on the quality of guests or participants. You can enter any relevant information or comments.
* **Overall Rating** (3, Number, Optional): Rate the overall success of the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Innovation Rating** (4, Number, Optional): Rate the event's innovation, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Impact Rating** (5, Number, Optional): Rate the event's impact, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Quality Rating** (6, Number, Optional): Rate the quality of the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Safety Rating** (7, Number, Optional): Rate the safety of the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Effectiveness Rating** (8, Number, Optional): Rate the event's effectiveness, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Easiness Rating** (9, Number, Optional): This field is not clearly defined, but may refer to ease of use or ease of participation.
* **Clarity Rating** (10, Number, Optional): Rate the clarity of the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Culture Rating** (11, Number, Optional): Rate the culture of the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Teamwork Rating** (12, Number, Optional): Rate the teamwork involved in the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Time** (13, Text, Optional): Provide any comments or feedback on the time management or duration of the event.
* **Resources** (14, Number, Optional): Rate the resources provided for the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Sustainability Rating** (15, Number, Optional): Rate the sustainability of the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Value Rating** (16, Number, Optional): Rate the value provided by the event, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Innovation** (17, Select One, Optional): Rate the event's innovation, selecting one of the provided options (Guests, Not sure, No answer).
* **Impact** (18, Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the event's impact, selecting one or more of the provided options (High impact, Low impact, Not sure, No answer).
* **Clarity** (19, Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the event's clarity, selecting one or more of the provided options (Low clarity, High clarity, Not sure, No answer).
* **Effectiveness** (20, Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the event's effectiveness, selecting one or more of the provided options (Not sure, No answer, Effective, Low effectiveness, High effectiveness).
* **Sustainability** (21, Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the event's sustainability, selecting one or more of the provided options (High sustainability, Not sure, Low sustainability, No answer).
* **Value** (22, Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the event's value, selecting one or more of the provided options (High value, Low value, Not sure, No answer).
* **Additional Comments** (23, Text, Optional): Provide any other thoughts or suggestions about the event.
* **Prophets Id** (24, Text, Optional): Enter the Prophets Id, which is not clearly defined, but may refer to a unique identifier or a specific feature of the hackathon.
* **Prophets Id** (25, Text, Optional): This field is a duplicate and should be reviewed and potentially removed or merged.
* **Innovation** (25, Text, Optional): This field is a duplicate and should be reviewed and potentially removed or merged.
