# Preventive Medicine Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the quality of a preventive medicine training course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
2. Select the date you attended the course.
3. If applicable, enter the location where you attended the course.
4. Enter the name of the course instructor.
5. Rate your experience with the course using one of the five options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent).
6. Enter any comments about the course.
7. Indicate whether you would recommend the course to others.
8. Enter any additional comments about the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name**: This is the title of the course you are evaluating. Enter the actual name of the course as it was presented or described to you.
* **Date**: This is the date you attended the course. Please enter the exact date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Location**: This is the location where the course was held. If you attended an online course, you can enter the URL or any other relevant information.
* **Instructor**: This is the name of the person who taught or presented the course.
* **Rating**: This is an evaluation of your experience with the course. Choose one of the five options:
	+ Poor: The course was not useful or helpful.
	+ Fair: The course was somewhat useful, but had some significant issues.
	+ Good: The course was helpful, but had some minor issues.
	+ Very Good: The course was very helpful and mostly problem-free.
	+ Excellent: The course was outstanding and exceeded your expectations.
* **Comments**: This is the opportunity to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the course. Be as specific as possible to help improve future courses.
* **Would you recommend this course?**: Indicate whether or not you would recommend the course to others. You can select one or more of the options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Additional Comments**: This is the opportunity to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the course that did not fit in the previous field. Be as specific as possible to help improve future courses.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any concerns or issues about the course, please bring them up in the comments section.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the additional comments section.
