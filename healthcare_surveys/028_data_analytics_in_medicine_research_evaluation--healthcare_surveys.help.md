# Data Analytics In Medicine Research Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about research projects in medicine for data analytics evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the research institution conducting the project.
2. Choose the area of research (e.g., Research in Medical Imaging and Diagnostics, Research in Genomics, etc.).
3. Indicate the expected outcomes of your research project (e.g., Positive impact on patients, Significant financial benefits, etc.).
4. Provide any improvement suggestions for your project.
5. Enter the contact person's details (name, email, phone number, etc.).
6. Select the tool assigned for the project.
7. Enter the date and time of submission.
8. Enter the details of the person submitting the project (name, email, phone number, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution conducting the research project.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, select_one, required): Choose the area of research from the list provided.
* **Research Outcomes** (`research_outcomes`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable outcomes expected from your research project.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improving your research project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the project.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned for the project.
* **Submission Date** (`submitted`, date, optional): Select the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): Select the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the project.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the project.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the project.
* **Submission Time** (`submitted_by_at`, time, optional): Select the time of submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately.
* Provide as much information as possible to ensure a comprehensive evaluation of your project.
* Double-check your submissions for any inaccuracies or errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the evaluation team.
