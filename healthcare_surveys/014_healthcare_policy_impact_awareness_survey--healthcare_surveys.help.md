# Healthcare Policy Impact Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and understanding from healthcare professionals on the impact of healthcare policies. It is meant to gauge awareness and understanding of policy-related information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by reviewing the provided policy information carefully.
2. Complete each section of the form, providing your thoughts and opinions on the questions asked.
3. Use the options provided as a guide to answer your question. If your response does not match any option, please feel free to provide it in the provided text area.
