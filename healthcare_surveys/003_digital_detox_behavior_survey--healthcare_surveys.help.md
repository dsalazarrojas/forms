# Digital Detox Behavior Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about digital detox behavior, which refers to the practice of disconnecting from digital devices and activities to improve mental and physical health. The goal of this form is to help healthcare professionals understand the relationship between digital device use and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by reading the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the survey.
2. Answer the questions on each page carefully and truthfully. You will be asked about your digital habits, device ownership, and usage patterns.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click submit to finalize your answers.
