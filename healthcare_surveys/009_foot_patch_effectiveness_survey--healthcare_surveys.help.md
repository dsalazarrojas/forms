# Foot Patch Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the effectiveness of foot patches, a medical device used to provide pain relief and wound care. The survey aims to evaluate how users experience and perceive the benefits and drawbacks of using foot patches. The questions in this form will help us understand how well foot patches meet your needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Usage Survey** (`usage_survey`, 1, Optional):
	+ This question asks you about your experience with using foot patches. Please answer in terms of how many times you have used them and for what duration.
* **Results Survey** (`results_survey`, 2, Optional):
	+ This question asks you about the results you have experienced when using foot patches. Please describe any positive or negative effects you have observed.
* **Satisfaction Survey** (`satisfaction_survey`, 3, Optional):
	+ This question asks you about your overall satisfaction with using foot patches. Please rate your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not satisfied at all" and 5 being "very satisfied".
* **Side Effects Survey** (`side_effects_survey`, 4, Optional):
	+ This question asks you about any side effects you have experienced when using foot patches. Please describe any adverse effects, if any.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any feedback or suggestions on how to improve foot patches, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section (not shown in this help guide).
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
