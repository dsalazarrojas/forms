# Speech And Language Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a Speech and Language Screening Survey in the healthcare context. The purpose of this survey is to assess an individual's communication skills, social abilities, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the "General Information" section if you have any additional information to share about your health status.
2. For the "Communication Questions" section, select all the options that apply to your ability to communicate effectively.
3. In the "Social Skills" section, describe your social interaction abilities.
4. Answer the "Behavioral Assessment" section by selecting the most relevant option that describes your behavioral patterns.
5. If applicable, provide information about your medical history in the "Medical History" section.
6. Indicate whether you have received speech therapy in the "Speech Therapy" section.
7. Indicate whether you need language therapy in the "Language Therapy" section.
8. List any medical professionals you have consulted in the "Medical Professionals" section.
9. Evaluate your speech and language pathology in the "Speech-Language Pathology Assessment" section.
10. Add any additional comments or information about your health status in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Text)**:
	+ Enter any relevant information about your health status.
* **Communication Questions (Select Multiple)**:
	+ Select all the options that apply to your ability to communicate effectively.
* **Social Skills (Text)**:
	+ Describe your social interaction abilities.
* **Behavioral Assessment (Select One)**:
	+ Evaluate your behavioral patterns by selecting the most relevant option.
* **Medical History (Text)**:
	+ Provide information about your medical history.
* **Speech Therapy (Select One)**:
	+ Indicate whether you have received speech therapy.
* **Language Therapy (Select Multiple)**:
	+ Indicate whether you need language therapy.
* **Medical Professionals (Select Multiple)**:
	+ List any medical professionals you have consulted.
* **Speech-Language Pathology Assessment (Select One)**:
	+ Evaluate your speech and language pathology.
* **Additional Comments (Text)**:
	+ Add any additional comments or information about your health status.
