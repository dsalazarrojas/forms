# Compassion Fatigue Among Nurses Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the survey questions and provide explanations for each field to help you complete the survey accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your personal experience and feelings as a nurse.
2. Choose one response option for each question from the provided options.
3. Select all applicable answers for questions with "Select all that apply" options.
4. Enter any additional comments or thoughts in the "Additional Comments" field if you have more to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Compassion Fatigue Survey** (Page 1)
	+ This is an introduction to the survey. You will not need to answer this question.
* **Nurse ID Number** (Page 2)
	+ This is an optional anonymous identifier for your responses. You can leave this blank if you prefer.
* **Years of Nursing Experience** (Page 3)
	+ Choose the number of years you have been a nurse.
* **Primary Work Setting** (Page 4)
	+ Select the setting where you primarily work (e.g., Hospital - ICU, Outpatient Clinic, etc.).
* **Average Hours Worked Per Week** (Page 5)
	+ Choose the number of hours you typically work per week, including overtime.
* **Primary Shift Type** (Page 6)
	+ Select the type of shift you usually work (e.g., Day shift, Evening shift, etc.).
* **Average Number of Patients Per Shift** (Page 7)
	+ Choose the typical number of patients you are assigned to care for during your shifts.
* **I feel Emotionally Drained from My Work** (Page 8)
	+ Rate how often you feel emotionally drained from your work.
* **I Dread Going to Work** (Page 9)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **I Feel Overwhelmed by My Patients' Emotions** (Page 10)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **I Feel Detached or Numb Toward Patients** (Page 11)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **I Have Lost My Ability to Feel Compassion** (Page 12)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **Work Problems Follow Me Home** (Page 13)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **I Have Trouble Sleeping Due to Work Stress** (Page 14)
	+ Rate how often you experience this problem.
* **I Have Become More Irritable at Work** (Page 15)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction** (Page 16)
	+ Choose how satisfied you are with your nursing job.
* **I Feel Supported by My Management Team** (Page 17)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **I Feel Supported by My Colleagues** (Page 18)
	+ Rate how often you feel this way.
* **I Have Access to Mental Health Resources** (Page 19)
	+ Choose whether you have access to mental health resources and if you use them.
* **I Engage in Self-Care Activities** (Page 20)
	+ Choose how often you engage in self-care activities.
* **Have You Considered Leaving Nursing Due to Stress** (Page 21)
	+ Choose whether you have considered leaving nursing in the past 6 months.
* **Current Burnout Level** (Page 22)
	+ Rate your current burnout level from 1 (none) to 10 (severe).
* **Helpful Interventions** (Page 23)
	+ Select all the interventions that you think would be helpful for you.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 24)
	+ Share any additional thoughts or experiences.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when responding to each question.
* Select only one response option for each question, unless otherwise indicated.
* Choose "Other" if you have an option not listed for a field.
* Select all applicable answers for questions with "Select all that apply" options.
* Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have in the "Additional Comments" field.
