# Patient Education Platform Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients on the effectiveness and usability of our patient education platform. Your input will help us improve the platform and provide better services to patients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Education Platform Name" field to enter the name of the platform you are evaluating.
2. Enter a rating for the platform using the number field provided.
3. In the "Description" field, provide a brief description of your experience with the platform.
4. Enter the URL of the platform you are evaluating in the "Platform URL" field.
5. Select the date of your evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
6. Select the time of your evaluation in the "Evaluation Time" field.
7. Answer the "Ease of Use" question by selecting how easy or hard it was to use the platform.
8. Answer the "Relevance to Needs" question by selecting how relevant the platform was to your needs.
9. For the "Effectiveness" question, select all the options that apply to describe how effective the platform was in meeting your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Platform Name** (`education_platform_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the platform you are evaluating.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, **required**): Provide a number rating for the platform (e.g., 1-5).
* **Description** (`description`, text, **optional**): Describe your experience with the platform.
* **Platform URL** (`platform_url`, text, **required**): Enter the URL of the platform you are evaluating.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, **required**): Select the date on which you completed this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, **required**): Select the time at which you completed this evaluation.
* **Ease of Use** (`platform_ease_of_use`, select_one, **required**): Answer how easy or hard it was to use the platform. Select from: Excellent, Good, Poor.
* **Relevance to Needs** (`relevance_to_needs`, select_one, **required**): Answer how relevant the platform was to your needs. Select from: Very Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant.
* **Effectiveness** (`platform_effectiveness`, select_multiple, **required**): Answer all that apply about how effective the platform was in meeting your needs. Select from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
