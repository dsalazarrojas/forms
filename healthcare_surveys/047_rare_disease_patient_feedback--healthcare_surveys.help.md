# rare_disease_patient_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rare_disease_patient_feedback form is designed to gather feedback from patients with rare diseases on their experience with the healthcare system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. You can skip questions that don't apply to you.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can provide them in the "Symptoms Management" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptoms Management** (`symptoms_management`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-text field where you can describe any symptoms you're experiencing related to your rare disease. This will help healthcare providers understand your condition better.
* **Treatment Challenges** (`treatment_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the challenges you face while receiving treatment for your rare disease. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Support Satisfaction** (`support_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how satisfied you are with the support you received. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, please provide your email address.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like to be identified in the feedback report, please provide your name. 

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately.
- If you have any further comments or concerns, please add them in the "Symptoms Management" field.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, you can contact us for clarification.
