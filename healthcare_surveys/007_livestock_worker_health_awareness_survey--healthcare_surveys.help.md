# Livestock Worker Health Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from livestock workers about their health awareness, including safety equipment usage, health issues, and access to care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title to identify the survey.
2. Select the category as "Healthcare Surveys".
3. Provide a brief description of your experience.
4. Fill in the farm name, cooperative name, and location where you work.
5. Enter the number of years you have been working with livestock.
6. Specify the number of hours you work per week.
7. Select all the safety equipment you use.
8. Choose any health issues you have experienced.
9. Select all the types of care you have access to.
10. Add any comments or additional information about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Fill in a brief title to identify your survey.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select "Healthcare Surveys".
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your experience, for example, "I work with beef cattle."
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select "Healthcare Surveys".
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the farm where you work.
* **Cooperative** (`cooperative_name`, text, optional): If you work for a cooperative, enter its name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you work.
* **Years Working** (`years_working`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working with livestock.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked_per_week`, number, required): Enter the average number of hours you work per week.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Select all the safety equipment you use, such as "Hard Hats, Gloves, Mask, Safety Vest".
* **Health Issues** (`health_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Select any health issues you have experienced, such as "Heat-Related Illnesses, Physical Injuries, Mental Health".
* **Access to Care** (`access_to_care`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of care you have access to, such as "Primary Care, Specialist Care, Emergency Care".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your experience.
