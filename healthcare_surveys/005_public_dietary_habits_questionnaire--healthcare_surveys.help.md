# public_dietary_habits_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a non-clinical, public health survey aimed to understand the dietary habits and demographic characteristics of the user population.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer each question in the space provided. If a question does not apply to you, please select the "Other" or "Unknown" option or leave it blank. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic info**: This section asks for general demographic information about you.
* **height**: Please enter your height in inches.
* **weight**: Please enter your weight in pounds.
* **waist_circumference**: Please enter your waist circumference in inches.
* **what does your waist circumference mean**: This section is a text field where you can enter any information you feel relevant to your waist circumference measurement.
* **what is height**: This section is a text field where you can enter any information you feel relevant to your height measurement.
* **what does your weight mean**: This section is a text field where you can enter any information you feel relevant to your weight measurement.
* **gender**: Please select your gender from the options provided.
* **age**: Please enter your age.
* **ethnicity**: Please select all the ethnicities that apply to you.
* **highest level of education completed**: Please select your highest level of education completed from the options provided.
* **income**: Please select your household income from the options provided.
* **employment status**: Please select your employment status from the options provided.
* **occupation**: This is a text field where you can enter your occupation.
* **family members**: Please enter the number of family members you have in your household.
* **household_income**: Please select your household income from the options provided.
* **household type**: Please select the type of household you live in.
* **do you smoke**: Please select whether you smoke or not.
* **do you drink**: Please select whether you drink or not.
* **physical activity level**: Please select your level of physical activity from the options provided.
* **breakfast frequency**: Please select how often you have breakfast.
* **breakfast time**: Please enter the time of day you usually have breakfast.
* **lunch frequency**: Please select how often you have lunch.
* **dinner time**: Please enter the time of day you usually have dinner.
* **physical health status**: Please select your physical health status from the options provided.
