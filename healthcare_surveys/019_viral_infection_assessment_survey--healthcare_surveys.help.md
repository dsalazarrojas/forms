# Viral Infection Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's viral infection symptoms, exposure history, and test results. It helps healthcare providers gather essential data to assess the infection and guide further treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct page (questionnaire or section) to fill out based on the patient's condition.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing all relevant information.
3. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose the appropriate option from the list provided.
4. For text fields, provide a detailed description or answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptom Questionnaire**: This section is intended to gather information about the patient's current symptoms.
	+ **Fever**: A temperature above 38°C (100.4°F) that may indicate a viral infection.
	+ **Cough**: A persistent or severe cough that could be related to a viral infection.
	+ **Sore Throat**: A sore or irritated throat that may be caused by a viral infection.
* **Exposure History**: This section aims to collect data on the patient's exposure to potential viral infection sources.
	+ **Fever**: A temperature above 38°C (100.4°F) that may indicate a viral infection.
	+ **Cough**: A persistent or severe cough that could be related to a viral infection.
	+ **Sore Throat**: A sore or irritated throat that may be caused by a viral infection.
* **Health History**: This section is meant to capture additional information about the patient's health background.
	+ This field is primarily for the healthcare provider to note any relevant health information.
* **Patient Info**: This section captures contact information for the patient.
	+ **Patient Name**: The full name of the patient (e.g., John, Jane, Jack, or Jill).
	+ **Patient Phone**: The patient's phone number for follow-up or communication.
* **Medical History**: This section is intended to collect data on the patient's medical background.
	+ **Medical Condition**: The current medical condition of the patient (e.g., Positive, Negative, Pending).
* **Test Results**: This section is meant to gather information on the patient's test results.
	+ **Positive**: Indicates a positive test result for a viral infection.
	+ **Negative**: Indicates a negative test result for a viral infection.
	+ **Pending**: The test result is still pending or not yet available.
* **Healthcare Provider**: This section captures information about the healthcare provider involved.
	+ **Dr. Smith**: The healthcare provider's name (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, Dr. Brown).
* **Workplace Setting**: This section is intended to determine where the patient was exposed to the virus.
	+ **Hospital**: A healthcare setting where patients receive treatment.
	+ **Clinic**: A medical facility where patients receive non-emergency care.
	+ **School**: An educational institution where students receive instruction.
	+ **Other**: Any other setting where exposure may have occurred.
* **School Setting**: This section is similar to the previous one but specific to a school setting.
	+ **Elementary School**: An educational institution for young children.
	+ **Middle School**: An educational institution for pre-teens.
	+ **High School**: An educational institution for teenagers.
* **Exposure Location**: This section is meant to identify where the patient was exposed to the virus.
	+ **Office**: A place of work or business setting.
	+ **Home**: The patient's home environment.
	+ **Park**: A public outdoor area.
* **Symptoms**: This section gathers more information about the patient's symptoms.
	+ **Fever**: A temperature above 38°C (100.4°F) that may indicate a viral infection.
	+ **Cough**: A persistent or severe cough that could be related to a viral infection.
	+ **Sore Throat**: A sore or irritated throat that may be caused by a viral infection.
	+ **Fatigue**: Feeling tired or lacking energy, which may be related to a viral infection.
	+ **Runny Nose**: A runny or congested nose that could be related to a viral infection.
	+ **Headache**: A headache that may be caused by a viral infection.
* **Symptoms Duration**: This section aims to determine how long the patient has experienced symptoms.
	+ **Less than 24 hours**: The symptoms have persisted for less than 24 hours.
	+ **More than 24 hours**: The symptoms have persisted for more than 24 hours.
	+ **More than 2 days**: The symptoms have persisted for more than 2 days.
* **Exposure Date**: This section is intended to capture the date of exposure to the virus.
* **Test Location**: This section is meant to identify where the patient was tested for the virus.
	+ **Clinic**: A medical facility where patients receive non-emergency care.
	+ **Hospital**: A healthcare setting where patients receive treatment.
	+ **Lab**: A testing facility where patients undergo laboratory tests.
* **Patient Age**: This section gathers information about the patient's age.
	+ **Newborn**: The patient is less than one month old.
	+ **Adult**: The patient is an adult.
	+ **Elderly**: The patient is an elderly individual.
* **Test Type**: This section is intended to determine the type of test used to diagnose the infection.
	+ **Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)**: A molecular test used to detect genetic material.
	+ **Rapid Viral Antigen Test (RAT)**: A quick test used to detect viral antigens.
	+ **Rapid Antibody Test**: A fast test used to detect antibodies to the virus.
* **Patient Sex**: This section captures information about the patient's sex.
	+ **Male**: The patient is male.
	+ **Female**: The patient is female.
	+ **Other**: The patient's sex is not male or female.
* **Symptoms Description**: This section is for the patient or healthcare provider to describe the symptoms in more detail.
* **Healthcare Provider Info**: This section is for the healthcare provider to note any additional information.
* **Test Result Details**: This section is for the healthcare provider to provide additional test result information.
* **Follow Up**: This section is intended to determine if follow-up is required.
	+ **True**: Follow-up is necessary.
	+ **False**: No follow-up is required.
	+ **Unknown**: The requirement for follow-up is unknown.
