# Virtual Care Workflow Assessment Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Care Workflow Assessment Evaluation Form is designed to help healthcare professionals evaluate and improve their care workflow. This form will aid in identifying areas of improvement and help healthcare professionals to provide better care to their patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Virtual care workflow assessment evaluation form** page (Page 1). This page serves as an introduction to the form and its purpose.
2. Proceed to the **provider_info** page (Page 2), where you will provide information about yourself as the provider.
3. On the **provider_experience** page (Page 3), describe your experience with the current care workflow.
4. The **patient_info** page (Page 4) is used to determine if the patient was present during the assessment. Select 'Yes' if the patient was present, 'No' if they were not.
5. The **workflow_info** page (Page 5) is used to determine if the current care workflow was followed. Select the options that apply to your assessment.
6. Provide a numerical value on the **assessment** page (Page 6) to rate your evaluation of the care workflow.
7. On the **evaluation** page (Page 7), enter the date you completed the assessment.
8. The **comments** page (Page 8) is for any additional comments or feedback about the care workflow.
9. Provide your signature on the **provider_signature** page (Page 9) to confirm that you completed the form.
10. If the patient was present, ask them to sign on the **patient_signature** page (Page 10) to confirm their presence.
11. Enter the date and time you submitted the form on the **submission_date** and **submission_time** pages (Pages 11-12).
12. Enter the location where you submitted the form on the **submission_location** page (Page 13).
13. On the **submitted_by** page (Page 14), enter your name.
14. Select 'Yes' or 'No' on the **form_submitted** page (Page 15) to confirm if the form was submitted.
15. Finally, select 'Yes' or 'No' on the **form_completed** page (Page 16) to confirm if the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **provider_info** (Page 2, text, optional): This field is for you to provide information about yourself as the provider. Enter your name, title, and any other relevant information.
* **provider_experience** (Page 3, text, optional): Describe your experience with the current care workflow.
* **patient_info** (Page 4, select_one, optional): Indicate if the patient was present during the assessment.
* **workflow_info** (Page 5, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that apply to your assessment of the care workflow.
* **assessment** (Page 6, number, optional): Provide a numerical value to rate your evaluation of the care workflow.
* **evaluation** (Page 7, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the assessment.
* **comments** (Page 8, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the care workflow.
* **provider_signature** (Page 9, number, optional): Sign to confirm that you completed the form.
* **patient_signature** (Page 10, number, optional): If the patient was present, ask them to sign to confirm their presence.
* **submission_date** (Page 11, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **submission_time** (Page 12, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **submission_location** (Page 13, text, optional): Enter the location where you submitted the form.
* **submitted_by** (Page 14, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **form_submitted** (Page 15, select_one, optional): Confirm if the form was submitted.
* **form_completed** (Page 16, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if the form was completed.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If the patient was present, ask them to sign the **patient_signature** page.
* Enter the date and time you submitted the form accurately.
* Complete all the required fields.
* Review the form for any errors before submission.
