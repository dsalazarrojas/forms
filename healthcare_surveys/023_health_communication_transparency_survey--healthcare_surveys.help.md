# health-communication-transparency-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality of healthcare communication and transparency. It helps healthcare providers understand how well they are communicating with their patients and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each question, choose the answer that best reflects your experience or perception.
2. Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. If you are unsure or have no opinion on a particular question, choose the option that best represents your current state.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is a text field where you can briefly describe your experience with the healthcare system. You can provide any additional context or information that you think is relevant to your responses.

### Perceived Transparency
* **Perceived Transparency** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Choose how transparent you perceive the healthcare system to be. Options range from `Very High` to `Not Sure`.

### Trust
* **Trust** (`3`, `number`, required): Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate how much trust you have in the healthcare system. A higher number means you have more trust.

### Clarity
* **Clarity** (`4`, `number`, required): Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate how clear the health information provided to you was. A higher number means the information was clearer.

### Health Message
* **Health Message** (`5`, `text`, optional): You can provide any additional comments or feedback about the health message you received.

### Healthcare Team
* **Healthcare Team** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how strongly you agree or disagree with the following statements about the healthcare team:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree

### Perceived Clarity
* **Perceived Clarity** (`7`, `text`, required): You can provide any additional comments or feedback about how clear the health information provided to you was.

### Perceived Trust
* **Perceived Trust** (`8`, `text`, required): You can provide any additional comments or feedback about how much trust you have in the healthcare system.

### Communication Strategy
* **Communication Strategy** (`9`, `text`, required): Please describe any positive or negative communication strategies you experienced with the healthcare team.

### Data Collection
* **Data Collection** (`10`, `text`, required): Please describe how the healthcare team collected data related to your care.

### Transparency
* **Transparency** (`11`, `text`, required): You can provide any additional comments or feedback about the level of transparency you experienced with the healthcare team.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`12`, `text`, required): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the healthcare system.
 
### Tips
* Please make sure to review your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the healthcare team.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us improve the quality of care and communication within the healthcare system!
