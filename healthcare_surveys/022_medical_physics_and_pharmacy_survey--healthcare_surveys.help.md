# Medical Physics And Pharmacy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects feedback from healthcare professionals about their experiences and challenges with medical physicists and pharmacists. It aims to identify areas for improvement and prioritize future research and knowledge sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the requested information, including your name and email address.
2. Answer the questions regarding your experiences and challenges with medical physicists and pharmacists.
3. Provide suggestions for improving collaboration between these fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Fill in your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Specify your department or institution.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required/optional): Describe your position or role.
* **What do you think is the most challenging part of working with a medical physicist?** (`collaboration`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on the most challenging part of working with medical physicists.
* **Are you aware of any key topics you would like to see included in future medical physics and pharmacy courses?** (`collaboration_key_topics`, text, required/optional): Specify any key topics you think should be included.
* **What do you think is the most challenging part of being a medical physicist or pharmacist?** (`challenge`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on the challenges faced by medical physicists or pharmacists.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving collaboration between medical physics and pharmacy professionals?** (`collaboration_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Suggest ways to improve collaboration.
* **Which research area would you like to see prioritized in the future?** (`research_area`, text, required/optional): Select a research area to be prioritized.
* **Which clinical area would you like to see prioritized in the future?** (`clinical_area`, text, required/optional): Choose a clinical area to be prioritized.
* **Which clinical area would you like to see prioritized in the future?** (`clinical_area_2`, text, required/optional): Select another clinical area to be prioritized.
* **Which academic area would you like to see prioritized in the future?** (`academic_area`, text, required/optional): Choose an academic area to be prioritized.
* **Other** (`other_area`, text, required/optional): Describe any other areas you would like to see prioritized.
* **Other** (`other_area_2`, text, required/optional): Specify another area to be prioritized.
* **Which area of research planning would you like to see prioritized in the future?** (`research_planning_area`, text, required/optional): Select an area of research planning to be prioritized.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them.
* This form is for your convenience, and your responses will help shape the future of medical physics and pharmacy knowledge sharing.
