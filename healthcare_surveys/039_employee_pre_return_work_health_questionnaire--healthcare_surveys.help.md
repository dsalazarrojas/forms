# employee_pre_return_work_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee's health information before their return to work.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including your name and ID number.
2. Select your current contact information.
3. Choose your current work status.
4. Select your current health status.
5. If necessary, select any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
6. If necessary, select any ergonomic issues you are experiencing at work.
7. Check if you are following the pre-return protocol.
8. Provide your health insurance status.
9. Enter your supervisor's contact information.
10. Enter your manager's contact information.
11. Provide the date and time of your expected return to work.
12. Check if you are following the return protocol.
13. Check if you have a medical certificate.
14. Sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Enter your name and ID number.
* **Contact**: Enter your current contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status, which is either "available", "unavailable", or "on leave".
* **Health Status**: Select your current health status, which is either "healthy", "sick", or "absent".
* **Medical Condition**: If you have a medical condition, select from the list of options.
* **Work Ergonomic**: If you are experiencing ergonomic issues, select from the list of options.
* **Pre-Return Protocol**: Check if you are following the pre-return protocol.
* **Health Insurance**: Check if you have health insurance.
* **Supervisor's Contact Information**: Enter your supervisor's name, phone number, and email.
* **Manager's Contact Information**: Enter your manager's name, phone number, and email.
* **Date of Return**: Enter the date of your expected return to work.
* **Time of Return**: Enter the time of your expected return to work.
* **Employee Return Protocol**: Check if you are following the return protocol.
* **Return Date Protocol**: Check if you have a protocol for your return.
* **Employee Pre-Return Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of your expected return.
* **Return Comments**: Enter any comments you have about your return.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about your return.
* **Medical Certificate**: Check if you have a medical certificate.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and submit the form.

Please ensure to review the form carefully before submission.
