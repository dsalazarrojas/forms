# health_informatics_system_usability_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the usability of a health informatics system. It assesses various aspects of the system's user interface, navigation, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following questions and provide your honest feedback about your experience with the health informatics system. Please answer each question based on your actual experience with the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, required: false): This is the introduction to the form.
* **System Usability** (`system_usability`, `text`, required: false): Describe how easy or difficult it was to use the system overall. 
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the system on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is very good.
* **Navigation Menu** (`navigation_menu`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How clear and easy to navigate is the menu system? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Not clear or hard to use
	+ Somewhat clear but hard to navigate
	+ Easy to use
	+ Very easy to use
	+ Extremely easy to use
* **Search Bar** (`search_bar`, `text`, required: false): Enter your experience with the search bar feature.
* **Search Bar Visibility** (`search_bar_visibility`, `select_one`, required: true): How often is the search bar visible?
	+ Always visible
	+ Sometimes visible
	+ Never visible
* **Navigation Speed** (`navigation_speed`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the navigation system on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **Navigation Speed Rating** (`navigation_speed_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the navigation system on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **Navigation Menu 2** (`navigation_menu_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How clear and easy to navigate is the menu system? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Not clear or hard to use
	+ Somewhat clear but hard to navigate
	+ Easy to use
	+ Very easy to use
	+ Extremely easy to use
* **Search Bar 2** (`search_bar_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your experience with the search bar feature on the second page.
* **Search Bar Visibility 2** (`search_bar_visibility_2`, `select_one`, required: true): How often is the search bar visible on the second page?
	+ Always visible
	+ Sometimes visible
	+ Never visible
* **Navigation Speed 2** (`navigation_speed_2`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the navigation system on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **Search Bar Speed** (`search_bar_speed`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the search bar on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **User Interface** (`user_interface`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with the user interface.
* **User Interface Speed** (`user_interface_speed`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the user interface on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **User Interface Speed Rating** (`user_interface_speed_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the user interface on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback you have about your experience with the health informatics system.
* **User Interface 2** (`user_interface_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with the user interface on the second page.
* **Help and Support** (`help_and_support`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback you have about the help and support features of the system.
* **System Usability Rating** (`system_usability_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the system's usability on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is very good.
* **User Interface Speed Rating 2** (`user_interface_speed_rating_2`, `number`, required: false): Rate the speed of the user interface on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is slow and 5 is fast.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any feedback you have about your experience with the health informatics system.
* **Overall System Usability** (`overall_system_usability`, `note`, required: false): Enter your overall rating for the system's usability.
* **Thank You** (`thank_you`, `note`, required: false): Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form.
