# Holistic Health Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your overall health and wellness, including your demographic information, stress levels, coping mechanisms, and physical and mental health conditions. It is intended to help healthcare providers and researchers understand your needs and provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. For select multiple-choice questions, choose all that apply from the available options.
4. For text-based questions, provide as much information as you need to answer the question.
5. If you have any additional comments or information to share, please use the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.
