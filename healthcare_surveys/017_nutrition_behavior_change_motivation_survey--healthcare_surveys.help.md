# Nutrition Behavior Change Motivation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to understand your motivations and behaviors related to nutrition and food choices. It will help identify areas for improvement in your eating habits and provide insights for developing effective behavior change strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and its corresponding label.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Fill in the required fields as indicated in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
4. If you need help or clarification, contact form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction_page`, text, optional): Briefly introduce yourself and explain your motivation for participating in this survey.
* **Motivation** (`motivation_page`, text, optional): Share your reasons for wanting to make changes to your eating habits.
* **Current Eating Habits** (`current_eating_habits`, text, optional): Describe your current eating habits, including frequency, quantity, and typical food choices.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, text, optional): Specify your preferences for different food types, including fruits, vegetables, meats, and dairy products.
* **Dietary Knowledge** (`dietary_knowledge`, text, optional): Explain your understanding of nutrition and healthy eating principles.
* **Self-Monitoring** (`self_monitoring`, text, optional): Describe how you currently monitor your eating habits, including methods and frequency.
* **Support System** (`support_system`, text, optional): Identify any individuals or resources that support your nutrition-related goals and motivations.
* **Barriers to Change** (`barriers_to_change`, text, optional): List any obstacles or challenges you face when trying to make changes to your eating habits.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, text, optional): Set specific, achievable goals for your nutrition-related motivations and behaviors.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, text, optional): Outline steps you plan to take to achieve your nutrition-related goals.
* **Self-Efficacy** (`self_efficacy`, text, optional): Rate your confidence level for successfully achieving your nutrition-related goals.
* **Maintenance** (`maintenance`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to maintain healthy eating habits in the long term.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your motivations and behaviors.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, text, optional): Provide demographic information such as age, sex, occupation, etc. for data analysis purposes.
