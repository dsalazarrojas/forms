# Community Health Data Program Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information on your experiences with the "Community Health Data Program" to help improve its quality and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your actual experiences with the "Community Health Data Program".
2. For each question, choose the option that best represents your perception or experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name`, Not required): This is your name if you wish to share it. You can leave it blank if you prefer.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Not required): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Not required): Enter your organization name if you have one.
* **Your Role** (`role`, Not required): Describe your role or position related to the program.
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, Required): Choose how you were involved in the program:
	+ Program Participant
	+ Healthcare Provider
	+ Program Staff
	+ Community Partner
	+ Family Member
	+ Other
* **Duration of Participation** (`participation_duration`, Required): Indicate how long you were involved in the program:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ More than 2 years
* **Overall Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Data Collection Quality** (`data_collection_quality`, Required): Rate the quality of data collected:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Program Accessibility** (`program_accessibility`, Required): Indicate how accessible the program was:
	+ Very Accessible
	+ Accessible
	+ Somewhat Accessible
	+ Not Accessible
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, Required): Rate the helpfulness of program staff:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, Required): Rate the quality of program communications:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Health Outcomes Improvement** (`health_outcomes`, Required): Indicate if you noticed any improvement in health outcomes:
	+ Significant Improvement
	+ Some Improvement
	+ No Change
	+ Decline
* **Specific Outcomes Achieved** (`specific_outcomes`, Not required): Describe any specific improvements you noticed (if any).
* **Program Strengths** (`program_strengths`, Not required): Describe the program's strengths or what worked well.
* **Program Challenges** (`program_challenges`, Not required): Describe any challenges you faced while participating in the program.
* **Barriers to Participation** (`barriers_to_participation`, Not required): Check any barriers that prevented fuller participation:
	+ Transportation
	+ Time Constraints
	+ Childcare
	+ Language Barriers
	+ Health Issues
	+ Lack of Information
	+ None
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, Not required): Provide any suggestions for improving the program.
* **Would You Participate Again** (`would_participate_again`, Required): Indicate your willingness to participate again:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, Required): Indicate your likelihood to recommend the program to others:
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Not required): Enter any additional feedback or comments.
