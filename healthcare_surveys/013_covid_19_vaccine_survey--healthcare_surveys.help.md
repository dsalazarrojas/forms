# COVID 19 Vaccine Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experiences, attitudes, and concerns regarding COVID-19 vaccination. Your responses will help inform public health initiatives, policy, and education programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select one that best reflects your opinion or experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Optional): This is a text field where you can input your name.
* **Age Range** (`respondent_age`, `select_one`, Required): Please select the age category that best describes you.
* **Current COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, `select_one`, Required): Please select one of the options that best describes your current vaccination status.
* **Are You Interested in Getting Vaccinated** (`vaccine_interest`, `select_one`, Required): Please select one of the options that best describes your level of interest in getting vaccinated.
* **What Are Your Main Concerns** (`vaccine_concerns`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Please select all the concerns that apply to you.
* **Where Do You Get Vaccine Information** (`vaccine_information_source`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Please select all sources that provide you with vaccine information.
* **How Much Do You Trust Available Information** (`information_trust`, `select_one`, Required): Please select one that best describes your level of trust in the information.
* **Has Your Healthcare Provider Discussed Vaccine** (`healthcare_provider_communication`, `select_one`, Optional): Please select one that best describes your experience with your healthcare provider.
* **Do You Believe Vaccine Is Effective** (`vaccine_effectiveness`, `select_one`, Required): Please select one that best describes your opinion on the vaccine's effectiveness.
* **Likelihood of Experiencing Serious Side Effects** (`vaccine_side_effects`, `select_one`, Optional): Please select one that best describes your likelihood of experiencing serious side effects.
* **Opinion on Workplace Vaccination Requirements** (`vaccination_mandate`, `select_one`, Optional): Please select one that best describes your opinion on workplace vaccination requirements.
* **COVID-19 Has Impacted Me Personally** (`covid_impact`, `select_one`, Optional): Please select one that best describes how COVID-19 has affected you.
* **When Would You Consider Getting Vaccinated** (`vaccine_decision_timeline`, `select_one`, Optional): Please select one that best describes when you would consider getting vaccinated.
* **What Additional Information Would Help** (`additional_information_needed`, `text`, Optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`feedback`, `text`, Optional): If you have any other comments or concerns, please provide them here.
