# influenza_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The influenza_questionnaire form is designed to collect information from patients about their recent influenza symptoms, potential exposure, and related factors. This information will be used by medical professionals to further evaluate and treat the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you are unsure about any of the questions.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare provider for further guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Influenza Symptoms (ID 1)
* **Influenza Symptoms**: This section is asking about any symptoms you may be experiencing related to the flu. Please answer 'Yes' if you are experiencing any of the following symptoms:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Sore throat
	+ Runny nose
	+ Sore muscles or body aches

### Potential Exposure (ID 2)
* **Potential Exposure**: Please answer 'Yes' if you may have been exposed to the influenza virus within the past 2 weeks. This includes being in close contact with someone who has the flu, traveling to an area with high flu activity, or being in a crowded area such as an airplane or public transportation.

### Related Factors (ID 3)
* **Related Factors**: Please answer 'Yes' if you have any of the following conditions or factors:
	+ Underlying medical conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, or lung disease
	+ Taking medications that may weaken your immune system
	+ Having a weakened immune system

### Demographic Data (ID 4)
* **Demographic Data**: Please answer 'Yes' if you have any of the following characteristics:
	+ Age 65 or older
	+ Pregnant or breastfeeding
	+ Have a family history of medical conditions such as heart disease or diabetes

### Medical History (ID 5)
* **Medical History**: Please select a date for any medical procedures or treatments you may have received in the past.

### Treatment Received (ID 6)
* **Treatment Received**: Please select a date and time for any treatment you may have received for the flu.

### Follow-up Date (ID 7)
* **Follow-up Date**: Please select a date for your follow-up appointment.

### Medical Conditions (ID 8)
* **Medical Conditions**: Please list any medical conditions you may have or have had in the past.

### Allergies (ID 9)
* **Allergies**: Please list any allergies you may have or be allergic to.

### Medications (ID 10)
* **Medications**: Please list any medications you may be taking or have taken in the past.

### Phone Number (ID 11)
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.

### Email (ID 12)
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you.

### Emergency Contact (ID 13)
* **Emergency Contact**: Please answer 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact that we can reach out to in case of an emergency.

This guide is for the purpose of helping patients complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the healthcare provider for further guidance.
