# Donor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from our existing donors about their relationship with our organization, their donation habits, and their support for various causes. This will help us improve our communication and outreach strategies to better serve our donors and the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out as much information as you can. Don't worry if you are unsure about some of the questions - provide the best answer you can, and we will take it into consideration.
2. Fill out the form in one page at a time. Each page will have a unique label (e.g. "Donor Survey Form") and you can scroll down to view the next page.
3. Read each field label carefully and answer accordingly. If you need help with a field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Survey Form (1)**: This is the main form where you will answer questions about your relationship with our organization, your donation habits, and your support for various causes.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Provide your contact information, including email and phone number, so we can reach out to you in the future.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your physical address.
* **How Did You Hear About (6)**: Choose from the options how you heard about us (e.g. Social Media, Friend, Online Search).
* **Why Did You Support (7)**: Choose from the options why you support our organization (e.g. Mission, Values, Charitable Work).
* **What Causes Do You Support (8)**: Choose from the options which causes you support (e.g. Medical Research, Education, Environmental).
* **How Often Do You Donate (9)**: Choose from the options how often you donate (e.g. Occasionally, Frequently, Never).
* **Frequency (10)**: Choose from the options how frequently you donate (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely).
* **Amount Donated (11)**: Choose from the options how much you donate (e.g. Small, Medium, Large).
* **What Methods Do You Use to Donate (12)**: Choose from the options which methods you use to donate (e.g. Online, Mail, In-Person).
* **When Did You Start Donating (13)**: Enter the date you started donating.
* **When Did You Last Donate (14)**: Enter the date you last donated.
* **How Did You Get Involved (15)**: Choose from the options how you got involved (e.g. Volunteer, Fundraiser, Board Member).
* **How Did You Get Involved Frequency (16)**: Choose from the options how frequently you got involved (e.g. Often, Occasionally, Rarely).
* **What Causes Do You Support Frequency (17)**: Choose from the options which causes you support frequently (e.g. Frequently, Occasionally, Never).
* **Why Did You Stop Supporting (18)**: Choose from the options why you stopped supporting our organization (e.g. Lack of Impact, Conflict of Interest, Other).
* **Why Did You Stopped Supporting Frequency (19)**: Choose from the options how frequently you stopped supporting (e.g. Frequently, Occasionally, Never).
* **How Did You Hear About Us (20)**: Choose from the options how you heard about us (e.g. Social Media, Friend, Online Search).
* **How Did You Hear About Us Frequency (21)**: Choose from the options how frequently you heard about us (e.g. Frequently, Occasionally, Never).
* **Do You Support Other Charities (22)**: Choose from the options whether you support other charities (e.g. Yes, No, Other).
* **Do You Support Other Charities Frequency (23)**: Choose from the options how frequently you support other charities (e.g. Frequently, Occasionally, Never).
* **Comments (24)**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about our organization.
* **Survey Comments (25)**: Enter any comments or feedback about the survey process.

Note: If you have any issues or need help with the survey, please do not hesitate to contact us at [insert contact info].
