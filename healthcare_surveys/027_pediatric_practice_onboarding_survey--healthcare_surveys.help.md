# Pediatric Practice Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Practice Onboarding Survey is designed to gather essential information from new patients, their parents, and emergency contacts to facilitate a smooth onboarding process in a pediatric healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section with the required information.
2. Make sure to enter accurate details for each field.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Patient Details

* **New Patient Details** (`new_patient_details`, text, optional): Enter the new patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details about the patient.

### Parent Details

* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, optional): Enter the parent(s) name(s) and contact information, such as phone numbers and email addresses.

### Emergency Contact

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

### Consent Details

* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the consent process, such as the date and time of the consent.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are a parent or guardian, please ensure you have the authority to submit this information on behalf of the patient.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
