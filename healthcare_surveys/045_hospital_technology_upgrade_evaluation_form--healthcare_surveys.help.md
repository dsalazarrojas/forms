# Hospital Technology Upgrade Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Technology Upgrade Evaluation Form is designed to assess the current state of technology infrastructure and services within the hospital. It helps hospital administrators and IT staff to evaluate the effectiveness of existing technology and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the technology overview section and provide a brief description of the current technology infrastructure and services used within the hospital.
2. In the technology upgrades and investments section, list and describe any upgrades or investments made in the past year, including costs and outcomes.
3. In the technology features and functionality section, list and describe the features and functionalities of the current technology, including their benefits and limitations.
4. In the technology risks section, identify and describe any risks associated with the current technology, including security concerns, data breaches, or other potential issues.
5. In the vendor performance section, evaluate the performance of current vendors providing technology services to the hospital.
6. In the technology budget section, provide an estimate of the current technology budget and any proposed changes.
7. In the technology timeline section, provide a brief description of the timeline for technology upgrades and investments, including milestones and deadlines.
8. In the current vendors section, list and describe the current vendors providing technology services to the hospital.
9. In the vendor performance section, evaluate the performance of current vendors providing technology services to the hospital.
10. In the vendor budget section, provide an estimate of the current vendor budget and any proposed changes.
11. In the submission status section, indicate the current status of the submission (pending, approved, or rejected).
12. In the submission comments section, provide any comments or feedback on the submission.
13. In the technology benefits section, list and describe the benefits of the current technology, including any cost savings or increased efficiency.
14. In the submission date section, provide the date of submission.
15. In the reviewer name and email section, provide your name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technology Overview** (`technology_overview`, text, required: false): A brief description of the current technology infrastructure and services used within the hospital.
* **Technology Upgrades and Investments** (`technology_upgrades`, text, required: false): A list of any upgrades or investments made in the past year, including costs and outcomes.
* **Technology Features and Functionality** (`technology_features`, text, required: false): A list of the features and functionalities of the current technology, including their benefits and limitations.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): Any other relevant information about the current technology, including challenges or issues.
* **Submission and Review** (`submission_form`, text, required: false): A brief description of the submission process and any review comments.
* **Technology Budget** (`technology_budget`, number, required: false): An estimate of the current technology budget and any proposed changes.
* **Technology Timeline** (`technology_timeline`, date, required: false): A brief description of the timeline for technology upgrades and investments, including milestones and deadlines.
* **Current Vendors** (`current_vendors`, select_multiple, required: false): A list of current vendors providing technology services to the hospital.
* **Vendor Performance** (`vendor_performance`, text, required: false): An evaluation of the performance of current vendors providing technology services to the hospital.
* **Vendor Budget** (`vendor_budget`, number, required: false): An estimate of the current vendor budget and any proposed changes.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required: false): The current status of the submission (pending, approved, or rejected).
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, required: false): Any comments or feedback on the submission.
* **Technology Risks** (`technology_risks`, text, required: false): A list of any risks associated with the current technology, including security concerns, data breaches, or other potential issues.
* **Technology Benefits** (`technology_benefits`, text, required: false): A list of the benefits of the current technology, including any cost savings or increased efficiency.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: true): The date of submission.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required: false): Your name and title.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, text, required: false): Your email address.

## Tips
* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help in making informed decisions for future upgrades and investments.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the technology department.
