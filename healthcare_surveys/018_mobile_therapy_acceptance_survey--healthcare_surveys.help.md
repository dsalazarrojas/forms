# Mobile Therapy Acceptance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Mobile Therapy Acceptance Survey is to collect information about the acceptance of mobile therapy tools or services among professionals in the healthcare industry. This form is designed to gather feedback from different groups of professionals such as therapists, clinics, researchers, and mental health startups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully before providing answers.
2. Complete the form in order, one page at a time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Mobile Therapy Acceptance Survey Form

* **Mobile Therapy Acceptance Survey Form**: 
   This is the form you will be completing to gather information about your acceptance of mobile therapy tools or services.
* **Category**: Select one or multiple options for which group you belong to (Therapist, Clinic, Researcher, Mental Health Startup).
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of what you do and your role in the healthcare industry. For example, "I am a therapist with 10 years of experience."
* **Therapist** (optional): If you are a therapist, provide your name and credentials.
* **Clinic** (optional): If you are a clinic, provide the name and address of your clinic.
* **Researcher** (optional): If you are a researcher, provide a brief description of your research work and any relevant credentials.
* **Mental Health Startup** (optional): If you are a mental health startup, provide a brief description of your service or product.

### Page 2: Additional Details

* **Mental Health Application**: Provide a brief description of any mental health application you are familiar with.
* **Feelings**: Provide any comments or feelings about the mental health application you provided in the previous field.
* **Likert Scale 1** (optional): Select a number to rate how satisfied you are with the mental health application. The Likert scale will be used to measure your level of satisfaction.
* **Likert Scale 2** (optional): Select a number to rate how likely you are to recommend the mental health application. The Likert scale will be used to measure your willingness to recommend.
* **Likert Scale 3** (optional): Select a number to rate how satisfied you are with the support provided by the mental health application. The Likert scale will be used to measure your level of satisfaction.
* **Likert Scale 4** (optional): Select a number to rate how easy you find the mental health application to use. The Likert scale will be used to measure your ease of use.

## Tips
- Please answer all fields that are marked as required.
- You may skip fields that are marked as optional.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the survey creator for further guidance.
- Please provide accurate and honest answers to ensure the quality of the data collected.
