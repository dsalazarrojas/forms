# Healthcare Process Innovation Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Process Innovation Workshop Evaluation form is designed to assess the effectiveness and outcome of a process innovation workshop. The form helps to collect feedback from attendees on the workshop's objectives, content, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the workshop's objectives and content in your mind.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each field based on your experience at the workshop.
3. Fill out the form completely and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the main objective of the workshop (Innovation, Improvement, or None).
* **Workshop type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of workshop that was conducted (Innovation, Improvement, or None).
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, optional): Describe in a few words or a short sentence the main objective of the workshop.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the workshop took place.
* **Duration** (`duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the workshop in hours or minutes.
* **Time** (`time`, number, optional): Enter the total time spent in the workshop in minutes.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to evaluate the workshop:
	+ Good: The workshop met its objectives and was well conducted.
	+ Neutral: The workshop was satisfactory, but could be improved.
	+ Bad: The workshop did not meet its objectives or was poorly conducted.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter a brief statement indicating that you have reviewed and completed the form accurately.

## Tips
* Please be as objective and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the workshop organizers or your supervisor for guidance.
* This form should not be used as a substitute for other evaluation or survey methods.
