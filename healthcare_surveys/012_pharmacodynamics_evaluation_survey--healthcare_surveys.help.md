# Pharmacodynamics Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of training programs in the field of pharmacodynamics. Your input will help us understand your experience and perceptions of the training, and will inform future program development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. You can choose one or multiple options, depending on the field type. When you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Training Effectiveness
* **Training Effectiveness** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Rate your overall effectiveness of the training. Choose one of the options that best reflects your experience.

### 2. Knowledge Levels
* **Knowledge Levels** (`Knowledgeable`, `Not knowledgeable`, `Not sure`, `Unsure`): Select all the levels of knowledge you gained from the training. You can choose one or multiple options.

### 3. Support Needs
* **Support Needs** (`Strongly agree`, `Somewhat agree`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat disagree`, `Strongly disagree`): Indicate your level of agreement with the statement "I felt I needed support during the training." Choose one option that reflects your experience.

### 4. Training Topics
* **Training Topics** (`Pharmacodynamics`, `Clinical Trials`, `Clinical Research`, `Regulatory Compliance`): Select all the topics that were covered in the training. You can choose one or multiple options.

### 5. Training Outcomes
* **Training Outcomes** (`Increased knowledge`, `Increased skills`, `Increased confidence`, `Increased motivation`): Select all the outcomes you experienced as a result of the training. You can choose one or multiple options.

### 6. Training Support
* **Training Support** (`Yes`, `No`, `Maybe`): Indicate whether you received the level of support you needed during the training. Choose one option that reflects your experience.

### 7. Training Hours
* **Training Hours**: Report the number of hours you spent in training. Enter a numerical value.

### 8. Training Location
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where you attended the training. Type in the full address or a brief description.

### Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- You can skip any field that you are not comfortable answering.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section (not shown here).
