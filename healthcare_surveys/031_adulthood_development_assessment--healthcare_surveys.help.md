# Adulthood Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adulthood Development Assessment is a form used to measure an individual's adult developmental progress in various areas, including financial management, career stability, interpersonal relationships, emotional regulation, health, and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name, which is a required field.
2. Enter your age, which is a required field.
3. Select your level of financial management proficiency from the options provided.
4. Indicate your level of career stability and growth from the options provided.
5. Choose your level of interpersonal relationship quality from the options provided.
6. Select your level of emotional regulation ability from the options provided.
7. Choose your level of health and wellness maintenance from the options provided.
8. Select all the milestones that you have achieved in your adult life from the options provided.
9. If applicable, indicate the next major step in your adult life (primary developmental goal).
10. If applicable, describe the biggest challenge you face in adulthood.
11. Enter the name of the assessor or healthcare professional who conducted the assessment.
12. Enter the date of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Please enter your age.
* **Financial Management Proficiency** (`financial_management_proficiency`, `select_one`, required): This field assesses your ability to manage your finances effectively. Choose from the following options:
	+ High: You have a solid understanding of personal finance and budgeting.
	+ Moderate: You have some understanding of personal finance, but may struggle with budgeting.
	+ Low: You have limited understanding of personal finance and budgeting.
	+ None: You have no understanding of personal finance or budgeting.
* **Career Stability and Growth** (`career_stability_and_growth`, `select_one`, required): This field evaluates your career stability and growth prospects. Choose from the following options:
	+ High: You have a stable and growing career.
	+ Moderate: You have some career stability, but may experience growth challenges.
	+ Low: You have limited career stability.
	+ None: You have no career stability.
* **Interpersonal Relationship Quality** (`interpersonal_relationship_quality`, `select_one`, required): This field assesses your relationship quality. Choose from the following options:
	+ High: You have strong, healthy relationships.
	+ Moderate: You have some relationship quality, but may experience challenges.
	+ Low: You have limited relationship quality.
	+ None: You have no healthy relationships.
* **Emotional Regulation Ability** (`emotional_regulation_ability`, `select_one`, required): This field evaluates your emotional regulation ability. Choose from the following options:
	+ High: You effectively manage stress and emotional challenges.
	+ Moderate: You have some emotional regulation ability, but may struggle with stress.
	+ Low: You have limited emotional regulation ability.
	+ None: You have no emotional regulation ability.
* **Health and Wellness Maintenance** (`health_and_wellness_maintenance`, `select_one`, required): This field assesses your health and wellness maintenance habits. Choose from the following options:
	+ High: You have a strong focus on physical activity and nutrition.
	+ Moderate: You have some health and wellness habits, but may struggle with maintenance.
	+ Low: You have limited health and wellness habits.
	+ None: You have no health and wellness habits.
* **Milestones Achieved** (`milestones_achieved`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the milestones you have achieved in your adult life:
	+ Financial Independence
	+ Stable Housing
	+ Career Path Established
	+ Long-term Partnership
	+ Continued Education
* **Primary Developmental Goal** (`primary_developmental_goal`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the next major step in your adult life.
* **Biggest Challenge in Adulthood** (`biggest_challenge_in_adulthood`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the biggest challenge you face in adulthood.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the assessor or healthcare professional who conducted the assessment.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
