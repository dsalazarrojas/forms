# Primary Care Speaker Presentation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Primary Care Speaker Presentation Survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees on a primary care speaker's presentation. This survey aims to gather information about the speaker's overall experience, comments, and feedback on the topic and session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your overall experience with the speaker from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
2. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the speaker.
3. Select your rating for the topic from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the topic.
5. Select your overall experience with the session from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.
7. Select your overall rating for the presentation from the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. Provide any other feedback about the presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience with Speaker**
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, to rate your overall experience with the speaker.
* **Additional Comments about Speaker**
	+ Type in any comments or feedback you have about the speaker.
* **How would you rate this topic?**
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, to rate your experience with the topic.
* **Additional Comments about Topic**
	+ Type in any comments or feedback you have about the topic.
* **Overall Experience with Session**
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, to rate your overall experience with the session.
* **Additional Comments about Session**
	+ Type in any comments or feedback you have about the session.
* **Overall Experience with Presentation**
	+ Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, to rate your overall experience with the presentation.
* **Other Feedback about Presentation**
	+ Type in any other comments or feedback about the presentation.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can provide comments or feedback in the designated fields if you have something specific to share.
* If you have any other feedback that is not addressed in the provided options, please use the "Other Feedback about Presentation" field.
