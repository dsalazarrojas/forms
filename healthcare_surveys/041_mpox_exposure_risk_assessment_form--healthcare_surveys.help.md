# Mpox Exposure Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess an individual's risk of exposure to Mpox (also known as the smallpox virus). Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand your situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. **Travel history**: Describe your recent travel history, including any countries or regions you have visited recently.
2. Choose all destinations you have visited from the provided options: **United States, Canada, Mexico**.
3. **Symptoms onset date**: If you have experienced any symptoms, enter the date they started.
4. **Contact with symptomatic person**: If you have had contact with someone who has Mpox, check the box next to **True**.
5. **Type of exposure**: Select the type of exposure you had, such as **Direct contact with an individual with Mpox, Indirect contact with an individual with Mpox, or Exposure to Mpox contaminated materials**.
6. **Exposure location**: Describe the location where you were exposed to Mpox.
7. **Travel frequency**: Enter the number of times you have traveled recently (e.g., 1, 2, or more times).
8. **Travel reason**: If you have traveled, describe the reason for your trip.
9. **Symptoms**: Check all the symptoms you have experienced, such as **Rash, Fever, Fatigue**.
10. **Healthcare provider name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
11. **Healthcare provider phone**: Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
12. **Follow-up instructions**: Choose any follow-up instructions you need from the provided options, such as **Schedule a follow-up visit with your healthcare provider, Monitor symptoms closely and seek immediate care if they worsen, Contact your healthcare provider for guidance on self-isolation**.
