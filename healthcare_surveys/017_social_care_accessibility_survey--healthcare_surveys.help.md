# Social Care Accessibility Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your experiences and perceptions regarding social care services. Your input will help us understand how well we are meeting your needs and provide insights to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions based on your understanding of the services you have received or are aware of.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences or perceptions.
3. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers.
5. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Service Awareness** (`service_awareness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether you are aware of the following services:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Barriers to Access** (`barriers_to_access`, `text`, required: false): Describe any barriers you face when trying to access social care services.
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the service you received (1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about the service.
* **Service Awareness Barriers** (`service_awareness_barriers`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you face any barriers when trying to access social care services:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Service Support Planning** (`service_support_planning`, `text`, required: false): Describe any support or planning you received from social care services.
* **Service Satisfaction Barriers** (`service_satisfaction_barriers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all barriers that affect your service satisfaction:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Service Satisfaction Scale** (`service_satisfaction_scale`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your overall satisfaction with the service you received:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Comments Awareness** (`comments_awareness`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about the service awareness.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us. Your feedback is valuable, and we are here to help.
