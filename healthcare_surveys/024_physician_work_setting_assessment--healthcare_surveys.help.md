# Physician Work Setting Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the work settings and environments of healthcare professionals, such as physicians, to better understand their needs and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Use the available options for select fields and choose the most relevant answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. form_page_1 (page 1, note)
* **Page 1: General Information**: This page serves as a general introduction to the form. Please take a moment to review the following pages and ensure you have understood the purpose and context of this assessment.

### 2. form_page_2 (page 2, select_multiple)
* **Work Setting**: Select all the options that apply to your work setting.
	+ Yes (e.g., urban, rural, academic, private practice)
	+ No (e.g., other)

### 3. form_page_3 (page 3, text)
* **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or information about your work setting that may not be addressed in the previous questions.

### 4. form_page_4 (page 4, select_one)
* **Work Setting Type**: Select the type of work setting that best describes your primary practice or work location.
	+ Yes (e.g., hospital, clinic, private practice, other)

### 5. form_page_5 (page 5, number)
* **Number of Staff**: Please enter the number of staff members in your work setting.

### 6. form_page_6 (page 6, date)
* **Start Date**: Please enter the date you started your current work setting.

### 7. form_page_7 (page 7, time)
* **Work Hours**: Please enter your typical work hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-17:00).

### 8. form_page_8 (page 8, text)
* **Work Environment**: Please describe your work environment (e.g., busy, quiet, fast-paced).

### 9. form_page_9 (page 9, text)
* **Challenges Faced**: Please describe any challenges you face in your work setting (e.g., patient volume, staffing issues, limited resources).

### 10. form_page_10 (page 10, text)
* **Supportive Measures**: Please describe any measures in place to support your work setting (e.g., peer support, mentorship, training programs).

### 11. form_page_11 (page 11, text)
* **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or information about your work setting that may not be addressed in the previous questions.

### 12. form_page_12 (page 12, select_one)
* **Work Setting Satisfaction**: Please select one option that best describes your satisfaction with your current work setting.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 13. form_page_13 (page 13, text)
* **Reasons for Satisfaction or Dissatisfaction**: Please describe the reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with your current work setting.

### 14. form_page_14 (page 14, text)
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Please provide any suggestions for improvement for your current work setting.

# Tips
* Please ensure you have reviewed and completed all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this assessment, and we appreciate your input!
