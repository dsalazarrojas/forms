# Internet Addiction And Eating Disorders Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who may be experiencing internet addiction and eating disorders. The data collected will help researchers and healthcare professionals understand the prevalence and characteristics of these issues, ultimately informing the development of effective interventions and support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your demographic information, including email and phone number (optional).
2. Provide details about your internet addiction frequency and any related behaviors.
3. Share your goals and aspirations related to managing your internet addiction.
4. Indicate the amount of time you spend on the internet each day (optional).
5. Take a moment to add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include.
6. Confirm your email address for verification purposes (optional).
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional).
8. Provide your date of birth for age verification (optional).
9. Specify any time of birth details (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographic Info** (`demographic_info_form`, text, optional): Please fill in your basic demographic information, including any relevant contact details.
* **Email** (`email_form`, text, optional): Enter your email address for verification purposes. This will help us ensure that your responses are properly attributed to you.
* **Phone** (`phone_form`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your responses or for follow-up support.
* **Internet Addiction Frequency** (`internet_addiction_frequency_form`, text, optional): Please describe your internet use habits, including how often and how much time you spend online.
* **Goals** (`goals_form`, text, optional): Share your goals and aspirations related to managing your internet addiction. This will help us understand what you hope to achieve through this survey.
* **Time** (`time_form`, text, optional): Indicate how many hours per day you spend on the internet (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note_form`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to include to provide context for your responses.
* **Email Validation Form** (`email_validation_form`, text, optional): This field is for internal use only, please ignore.
* **Phone Number Form** (`phone_number_form`, text, optional): This field is for internal use only, please ignore.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth_form`, text, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes (if applicable).
* **Time Of Birth Form** (`time_of_birth_form`, text, optional): This field is for internal use only, please ignore.

## Tips
Please take your time to answer each question honestly and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out. Your participation is invaluable to this research, and your responses will help create a better understanding of internet addiction and eating disorders.
