# Readmission Reduction Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather your feedback and suggestions on the effectiveness of the readmission reduction program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question in the space provided.
2. Provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your thoughts and experiences.
3. If you have trouble understanding a question, please ask for clarification.
4. You are free to skip questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Readmission Reduction Program Survey**: Please share your thoughts about the overall experience with the program.
* **Follow-up Care**: If you felt you received inadequate or excessive follow-up care, describe your experience.
* **Patient Challenges**: If you faced any challenges during your participation in the program, please describe them.
* **Quality Improvement**: Suggest ideas for improving the program to make it more effective in reducing readmissions.

## Tips
* Please provide concrete examples or specific details to support your answers.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to share them.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and helping us improve the program.
