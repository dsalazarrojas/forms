# health_program_self_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about their experience with a health program. This feedback will help the program administrators assess the effectiveness of the program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as a participant in the `participant_name` field.
2. Enter the name of the program being evaluated in the `program_name` field.
3. Enter the date you completed the program or the specific session being evaluated in the `evaluation_date` field.
4. Select all the wellness initiatives you participated in from the `wellness_initiatives` dropdown menu.
5. Enter the location of the program in the `program_location` field.
6. Rate your overall experience with the program using the `overall_experience` field (1-10 scale).
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments about your experience in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name (Text)**: This is your name as a participant in the program. Please enter it accurately.
* **program_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the program being evaluated. This will help program administrators track feedback for each program.
* **evaluation_date (Date)**: Enter the date you completed the program or the specific session being evaluated. This helps track progress and changes over time.
* **wellness_initiatives (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the wellness initiatives you participated in during the program.
	+ Option 1: Choose this if you participated in initiative 1.
	+ Option 2: Choose this if you participated in initiative 2.
	+ Option 3: Choose this if you participated in initiative 3.
* **program_location (Text)**: Enter the location where the program was held or where you participated.
* **overall_experience (Number)**: Rate your overall experience with the program on a scale of 1-10. A higher score indicates a more positive experience.
* **comments (Note)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the program. This is an optional field, but your comments will help program administrators understand your perspective.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the program administrators for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input!
