# Human Albumin Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on human albumin usage in various departments of a healthcare facility.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as follows:

* Select the department that uses albumin from the list of options.
* Choose the frequency of albumin use (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
* Select all departments that use albumin from the list of options.
* Provide any additional comments or feedback about albumin usage in your department.
* Indicate your satisfaction with the current state of albumin usage in your department.
