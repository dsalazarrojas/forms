## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions from the patient to collect and store their data.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on the patient's self-reporting.
3. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
4. Select the patient's current pain level by choosing a number from 1-10.
5. Choose how much their current mobility impacts their daily activities.
6. Select their current pain level.
7. Enter any information about their pain medication.
8. Enter any details about their treatment or therapy.
9. Add any additional notes about the patient's condition or progress.
10. Enter the patient's contact information for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the patient's unique ID number.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, `number`, required: false): Select a number from 1-10, where 1 is minimal pain and 10 is extreme pain.
* **Mobility Impact** (`mobility_impact`, `number`, required: false): Choose how much their mobility impacts their daily activities, with options ranging from 1 (not at all) to 10 (extremely).
* **Current Pain** (`current_pain`, `number`, required: false): Select their current pain level, from 1-10.
* **Pain Medication** (`pain_medication`, `text`, required: false): Enter any information about their current pain medication usage.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, `text`, required: false): Enter any details about their treatment or therapy.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the patient's condition or progress.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, required: false): Enter the patient's contact information for further communication.
