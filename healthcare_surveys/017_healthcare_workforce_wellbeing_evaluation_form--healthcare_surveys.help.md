# healthcare_workforce_wellbeing_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare workforce wellbeing evaluation form is used to assess the overall wellbeing of healthcare workers, focusing on physical and mental health, stress levels, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, even if you're unsure or don't know.
3. Fill in all required fields, but feel free to leave optional fields blank if you're not sure or don't have information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wellbeing Evaluation Form 1**: This is a text field where you can provide any relevant information about your wellbeing. Please write your thoughts, feelings, or experiences related to your wellbeing.
* **Wellbeing Evaluation Form 2**: This field is for selecting a date when you last took a break or did something relaxing. Please choose the date from the provided calendar or enter the date manually.
* **Wellbeing Evaluation Form 3**: This is a multiple-choice field where you can indicate if you have experienced stress or burnout. Please select 'Yes' if you have, and 'No' if you haven't.
* **Wellbeing Evaluation Form 4**: This field is a text area for any additional comments or thoughts about your wellbeing. Please use this space to elaborate on your answers or add any relevant information.
* **Wellbeing Evaluation Form 5**: Another text field for you to provide any further thoughts or feelings about your wellbeing. 

## Tips

* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your HR representative for support.
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can, as it will help to better understand your wellbeing.
* Remember, this form is designed to support your health and wellbeing, not to judge or evaluate you.
