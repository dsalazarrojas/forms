# Virtual Counseling Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Counseling Experience Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and satisfaction ratings from users who have participated in virtual counseling sessions. The survey aims to understand the overall experience and identify areas of improvement for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the entire form to understand each question and field.
2. Answer each question based on your genuine experience and satisfaction with the virtual counseling session.
3. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as these are essential for us to understand your feedback.
4. Provide detailed feedback in the text fields, such as "Feedback" and "Additional Comments".
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Satisfation* (`session_satisfaction`, number, **required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the virtual counseling session, using the numerical scale.
* **Technology Satisfation* (`technology_satisfaction`, select multiple, **required**): Choose how satisfied you were with the technology used during the session, such as video conferencing tools.
* **Service Improvement* (`service_improvement`, select one, **required**): Select one option to rate the improvement of the virtual counseling service, indicating areas that need improvement.
* **Session Format* (`session_format`, select multiple, **required**): Choose the format of your virtual counseling session, such as individual or group sessions.
* **Session Duration* (`duration`, number, **required**): Enter the actual duration of your virtual counseling session.
* **Schedule* (`schedule`, select one, **required**): Choose the time slot of your virtual counseling session, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Time* (`time`, time, **required**): Enter the actual start and end time of your virtual counseling session.
* **Session Type* (`session_type`, select one, **required**): Choose the type of your virtual counseling session, such as in-person, virtual, or phone call.
* **Next Steps* (`next_steps`, note, **required**): Provide any next steps or follow-up actions that you would like us to take based on your experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **optional**): Share any additional comments or suggestions about your virtual counseling experience.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, **optional**): Record any relevant session notes or details that might be helpful for future sessions.
* **Rating* (`rating`, number, **required**): Rate your overall experience with the virtual counseling service, using a numerical scale.
