# Hemiparesis Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hemiparesis Assessment Survey is designed to evaluate the functional impact of hemiparesis on patients' daily lives. It gathers information on the severity of hemiparesis symptoms, functional needs, and mobility limitations, providing a comprehensive picture of the condition's effect. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the severity of hemiparesis and make informed decisions about patient care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each question carefully and answer honestly to provide accurate information about your hemiparesis symptoms and impact.
* For questions with multiple options, select the answer that best describes your situation.
* Enter text responses in the provided text fields.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hemiparesis Assessment Survey** (`hemiparesis_assessment_survey`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional comments or information about your hemiparesis symptoms or concerns.
* **Functional Impact** (`functional_impact`, select_one, required): This question asks about the functional impact of your hemiparesis on your daily life. Please select the option that best describes the effect of your hemiparesis on your activities of daily living.
* **Mobility Needs** (`mobility_needs`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about the mobility needs that you have due to your hemiparesis. Please select all the options that apply to your situation.
* **Hemiparesis Symptom Severity** (`hemiparesis_symptom_severity`, number, optional): This field is a numerical scale that asks about the severity of your hemiparesis symptoms. Please answer with a number between 0 and 10, with 0 indicating no symptoms and 10 indicating severe symptoms.
* **Mobility Needs Description** (`mobility_needs_description`, text, optional): This field is a text area where you can provide more details about your mobility needs due to your hemiparesis.
* **Functional Impact Description** (`functional_impact_description`, text, optional): This field is a text area where you can provide more details about the functional impact of your hemiparesis on your daily life.
* **Hemiparesis Assessment Survey Comments** (`hemiparesis_assessment_survey_comments`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional comments or information about your hemiparesis symptoms or concerns.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately to ensure that your responses are helpful for your healthcare provider's assessment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form should not be used as a substitute for a professional medical evaluation. If you have concerns about your hemiparesis, please consult a healthcare professional for proper diagnosis and treatment.
