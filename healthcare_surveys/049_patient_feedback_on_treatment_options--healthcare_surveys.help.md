# Patient Feedback On Treatment Options - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient feedback on their experiences with treatment options. This feedback will help us understand how well our treatment options are meeting your needs and identify areas for improvement to provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Be as detailed and honest as possible with your answers.
3. If you have any comments or additional information, please include them in the corresponding fields.
