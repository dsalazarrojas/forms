# X Ray Exam Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The X Ray Exam Assessment Survey is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect and record information about patient X Ray examinations. This helps ensure that patients receive accurate and personalized care, and allows for efficient treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and up-to-date information for each patient.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the correct medical specialty from the options listed.
4. Provide clear and concise feedback in the text fields.
5. Enter the patient's phone number and email (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (form_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's X Ray examination form.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's X Ray examination.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time of the patient's X Ray examination.
* **Exam ID** (exam_id, number, optional): Enter the unique ID for the patient's X Ray examination.
* **Physician** (physician, text, optional): Enter the name of the physician performing the X Ray examination.
* **Physician Name** (physician_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient's primary care physician.
* **Reason for Visit** (reason_for_visit, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the patient's X Ray examination.
* **Medical Specialty** (medical_specialty, select_one, optional): Select the medical specialty of the patient's X Ray examination:
	+ Cardiology
	+ General
	+ Neurology
	+ Orthopedic
	+ Plastic
	+ Radiology
	+ Other
* **Patient Feedback** (patient_feedback, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the patient.
* **Physician Notes** (physician_notes, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations from the physician.
* **Patient Phone Number** (patient_phone_number, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number (if available).
* **Patient Email** (patient_email, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address (if available).
