# Dietary Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about your dietary habits and preferences. This data will be used to provide personalized nutrition advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your dietary preference from the options provided.
2. Enter the amount of each food group (sugar, sodium, fat, fiber, water) you consume per day.
3. Choose your food preferences from the options provided.
4. Indicate any dietary restrictions you may have.
5. Add any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your dietary preference**: Select one or more options that best describe your dietary preference. Options include:
	+ Vegetarian (eats vegetables, fruits, and sometimes meat or fish)
	+ Non-Vegetarian (eats meat, fish, and sometimes vegetables or fruits)
	+ Vegan (does not eat animal products, including eggs and honey)
	+ Gluten-free (has gluten intolerance)
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How much do you eat per day**: Enter the number of servings or servings size for each of the following food groups. This will help us understand your daily food intake:
	+ Sugar
	+ Sodium
	+ Fat
	+ Fiber
	+ Water
* **Daily sugar intake**: Enter the number of grams of sugar you consume per day.
* **Daily sodium intake**: Enter the number of milligrams of sodium you consume per day.
* **Daily fat intake**: Enter the number of grams of fat you consume per day.
* **Daily fiber intake**: Enter the number of grams of fiber you consume per day.
* **Daily water intake**: Enter the number of milliliters of water you consume per day.
* **What are your food preferences**: Choose one or more options that best describe your food preferences. Options include:
	+ Vegetables (eats a variety of vegetables)
	+ Fruits (eats a variety of fruits)
	+ Proteins (eats meat, fish, or eggs)
	+ Fats (eats foods high in fat, such as oils and fats)
	+ Carbohydrates (eats foods high in carbohydrates, such as grains and sweets)
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions**: Select any options that apply to you. Options include:
	+ None (no dietary restrictions)
	+ Gluten-free (has gluten intolerance)
	+ Dairy-Free (lactose intolerant)
	+ Vegan (does not eat animal products, including eggs and honey)
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Additional information**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your dietary habits and preferences. This could include any allergies, medical conditions, or other relevant details.
