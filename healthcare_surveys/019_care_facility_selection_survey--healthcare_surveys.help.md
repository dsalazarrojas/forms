# Care Facility Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Facility Selection Survey is designed to help users find the best care facility for their needs. It collects information about the user's relationship to the patient, their preferences for selecting a facility, and other important details that will influence the facility selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your relationship to the patient (self, family member, care manager, or other).
2. Choose the most important factors when selecting a facility (up to three options can be selected).
3. Enter your maximum budget per month (optional).
4. Specify your preferred location or area (optional).
5. Select the required services the patient must have (up to three options can be selected).
6. Choose the priority for an in-person visit (within one week, within one month, or no preference).
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
8. Give your consent to share information with facilities (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you'd like to be contacted about your responses.
- **Relationship to patient** (`relationship_to_patient`, select_one, required): This is the relationship you have to the patient (e.g., self, family member, care manager, or other).
- **Most important factors when selecting a facility** (`important_factors`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to three factors that are most important to you when selecting a facility. Examples include location, cost, quality of care, staff qualifications, and amenities.
- **Maximum budget per month** (`maximum_budget`, number, optional): Enter your approximate budget per month for the facility.
- **Preferred location or area** (`preferred_location`, text, optional): Enter the city neighborhood or postcode of your preferred location.
- **Required services** (`required_services`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three services the patient must have (e.g., skilled nursing, physical therapy, memory care, or respite care).
- **Priority for in-person visit** (`visit_priority`, select_one, optional): Choose how soon you'd like a tour of the facility (within one week, within one month, or no preference).
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other notes or preferences about your needs.
- **Consent to share information with facilities** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Give your consent to share your information with care facilities (yes or no).
