# Responsible Person Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the responsible person in charge of a patient's care. This form is used to gather detailed information about the responsible person, including their name, contact details, and role in the care process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have permission to complete this form for the patient.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Be thorough in providing details about the responsible person.
4. Review your entries carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Responsible Person Details** (`responsible_person_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the responsible person. Please enter a short statement explaining their role or position in the care process.
* **Responsible Person Name** (`responsible_person_name`, text, required): This is the name of the person responsible for the patient's care.
* **Responsible Person Email** (`responsible_person_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the patient's care.
* **Responsible Person Phone Number** (`responsible_person_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the patient's care.
* **Responsible Person Address** (`responsible_person_address`, text, optional): If the person responsible for the patient's care has a physical address, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Relationship with Patient?** (`responsible_person_relationship`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if the person is related to the patient, or 'No' if not.
* **Responsible Person Position** (`responsible_person_position`, text, optional): Enter the position or job title of the person responsible for the patient's care.
* **Date of Birth** (`responsible_person_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the person responsible for the patient's care.
* **Notes** (`responsible_person_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the person responsible for the patient's care.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information about the responsible person.
* Be thorough in providing details about their role and responsibilities.
* If you are unsure about any information, please seek clarification from the patient or their representative.
