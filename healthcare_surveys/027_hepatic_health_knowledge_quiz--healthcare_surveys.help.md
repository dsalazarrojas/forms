# Hepatic Health Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hepatic Health Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess the knowledge and understanding of healthcare professionals regarding hepatic health and disease. This form is used to collect information about participants, their healthcare providers, programs, and other relevant details. The quiz is aimed at evaluating the participant's knowledge and understanding of the main causes of liver disease, liver failure, and cirrhosis, as well as their ability to identify important medical information and additional comments.


## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required information for the participant, such as their name, quiz date, and participant ID.
* Answer the multiple-choice questions about the main causes of liver disease, liver failure, and cirrhosis.
* Add any additional comments or notes as a participant.
* If you are a healthcare provider, program administrator, or submitter, fill in your details and comments as required.
* Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required information.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer the multiple-choice questions about the main causes of liver disease, liver failure, and cirrhosis.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required: false): Enter your score out of the number of questions.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's ID.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's healthcare provider's name and information.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the program or organization.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as a healthcare provider or submitter.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as a healthcare provider or submitter.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of program or activity (e.g., Health Education, Wellness Program, Training Activity).
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the program or activity.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the program or activity.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any important medical information or additional comments from the participant.
* **Participant Additional Comments** (`participant_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments from the participant.
* **Program Additional Comments** (`program_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the program or activity.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as a submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number as a submitter.
* **Submitter Program** (`submitter_program`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your program or organization.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments from the submitter.
* **Submitter Additional Comments** (`submitter_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments from the submitter.
* **Submitter Program Type** (`submitter_program_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of program or activity (e.g., Health Education, Wellness Program, Training Activity).
* **Submitter Program Location** (`submitter_program_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your program or activity.
* **Submitter Program Description** (`submitter_program_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your program or activity.
