# Medication Adherence Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help healthcare providers understand patients' motivations and reasons for taking their medications as prescribed. The data collected will help healthcare providers make more informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, without leaving any fields blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before filling out the form.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant contact information for the patient, such as phone number or email.
* **Contact Info Phone** (`contact_info_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's phone number if applicable.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the medication the patient is taking.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, `text`, required): Enter the dosage of the medication as prescribed.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `text`, required): Enter the frequency of the medication as prescribed.
* **Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, `text`, optional): If the patient is experiencing any side effects, describe them here.
* **Motivation Scale** (`motivation_scale`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether the patient is motivated to take their medications.
* **Reasons** (`motivation_reasons`, `text`, required): Provide reasons for the patient's motivation to take their medications.
* **Healthcare Team** (`healthcare_team`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the healthcare team.
* **Patient Adherence** (`patient_adherence`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's adherence to their medication regimen.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's name and contact information.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Adherence Scale** (`patient_adherence_scale`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply for the patient's adherence to their medication regimen.
* **Dosage Frequency** (`medication_dosage_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the patient's dosage frequency.
* **Reasons (2)** (`reasons_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply for the patient's motivations for not adhering to their medication regimen.
* **Reasons for Adherence** (`reasons_for_adherence`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply for the patient's reasons for adhering to their medication regimen.
* **Adherence Scale (2)** (`adherence_scale_2`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether the patient is adhering to their medication regimen.
* **Healthcare Team Comments** (`healthcare_team_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the healthcare team.
* **Healthcare Team Recommendations** (`healthcare_team_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Enter any recommendations for the healthcare team.
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or observations from the patient.
* **Patient Comments 2** (`patient_comments_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations from the patient.
* **Patient Comments 3** (`patient_comments_3`, `text`, optional): Enter any further comments or observations from the patient.
