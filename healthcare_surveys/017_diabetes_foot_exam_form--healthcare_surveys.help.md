# Diabetes Foot Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical history and examination results for diabetes foot exams. The form is used to gather data for patient care and treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and the patient's condition before completing this form.
- Review each section carefully and follow the instructions in each section to provide accurate and complete information.
- If you need help or have questions, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider or other relevant healthcare professionals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Patient Name
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, *required*): Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.

### 2. Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, *required*): Please enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 3. Medical Record Number
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, *optional*): This field is optional but may be helpful for patient care and treatment decisions. Please enter the patient's medical record number if available.

### 4. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Please select all the conditions that apply to the patient's medical history, using the provided options.

### 5. Examination Details
* **Examination Details** (`examination_details`, `text`, *required*): Describe the examination results and any other relevant details about the patient's foot exam.

### 6. Foot Exam Form Results
* **Foot Exam Form Results** (`foot_exam_form_results`, `note`, *required*): Please enter the examination results and any other relevant information about the patient's foot exam.

### 7. Patient Notes
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, *optional*): This field is optional but may be helpful for providing additional context about the patient's condition. Please enter any relevant notes or comments about the patient's care.

Note: This form will be reviewed by healthcare professionals for patient care and treatment decisions. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
