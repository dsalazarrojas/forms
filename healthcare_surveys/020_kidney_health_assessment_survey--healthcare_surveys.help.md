# Kidney Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kidney Health Assessment Survey is designed to collect information about patients' medical history, symptoms, and lifestyle habits to help healthcare professionals assess their kidney health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please provide your basic patient information on the first page.
2. On the second page, describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medications.
3. On the third page, report any symptoms you are experiencing, such as pain or discomfort.
4. On the fourth page, share information about your lifestyle habits, including diet, exercise, and sleep patterns.
5. Finally, provide any follow-up information on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Info, text, required):** Please enter your basic patient information, including your name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History (Medical History, text, optional):** Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or medications. This will help us understand your overall health status.
* **Symptoms (Symptoms, text, optional):** Report any symptoms you are experiencing, such as pain or discomfort, that you think might be related to your kidney health.
* **Lifestyle (Lifestyle, text, optional):** Share information about your lifestyle habits, including your diet, exercise, and sleep patterns. This will help us understand how your lifestyle might be affecting your kidney health.
* **Follow-Up (Follow-Up, text, optional):** Provide any additional follow-up information that you think is relevant to your kidney health assessment.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for assistance.
* This form is for your convenience, and your participation is greatly appreciated.
* Please note that this form is not a substitute for a medical consultation or diagnosis. If you are experiencing severe symptoms, please contact your healthcare provider immediately.
