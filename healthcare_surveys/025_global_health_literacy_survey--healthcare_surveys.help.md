# Global Health Literacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Health Literacy Survey is designed to collect information on a person's overall health literacy and their understanding of various health-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question based on your understanding of the topic.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose the best answer that reflects your understanding.
3. For text fields, provide a short description or answer in the space provided.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).
