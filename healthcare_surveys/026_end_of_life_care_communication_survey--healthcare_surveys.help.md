# End Of Life Care Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "End Of Life Care Communication Survey" form is used by healthcare professionals to collect information about the end-of-life care experience of patients in hospitals and healthcare facilities. This information helps improve communication between healthcare teams, care teams, and patients' families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer the survey questions in the text field on page 1.
2. Select the patient's information from the dropdown menu on page 2.
3. Choose the care team information from the dropdown menu on page 3.
4. Enter the communication quality and additional comments on page 4.
5. Fill out the facility information on page 5.
