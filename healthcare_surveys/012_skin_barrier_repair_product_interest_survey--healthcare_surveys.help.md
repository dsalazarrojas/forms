# Skin Barrier Repair Product Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your skin concerns and interests in skin barrier repair products. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, and choose the options that best match your experiences and interests.
2. Select one or more options for each question, depending on your preferences.
3. You can skip any question that doesn't apply to you or your concerns.
4. Click "Submit" when you've finished answering all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Demographics**: Have you experienced any skin concerns recently? (Yes or No)
	+ This question is used to determine your level of experience with skin concerns.
* **How Often Do You Experience Skin Concerns?** (Yes or No)
	+ This question helps us understand how frequently you experience skin concerns.
* **Product Preferences**: Which of the following features are you interested in for a skin barrier repair product? (Select all that apply)
	+ This question allows you to express your preferences for specific product features.
	+ Features may include:
		- Moisturizing
		- Antioxidant
		- Anti-aging
		- Soothing
		- Anti-inflammatory
* **Key Features**: Which of the following key features are you interested in for a skin barrier repair product? (Select all that apply)
	+ This question helps us understand which features are most important to you.
	+ Features may include:
		- Gentle and non-irritating
		- Effective and fast-acting
		- Natural and organic
		- Affordable
* **Brand**: Which skin barrier repair brands are you interested in or currently use? (Select all that apply)
	+ This question allows you to express your brand loyalty or preferences.
	+ Brand names are not specified, please select all that apply.
* **Product Interest**: If you could describe the product you are interested in, please provide a brief description.
	+ This question gives us a better understanding of what you are looking for in a skin barrier repair product.
* **Product Features**: Which features from the following list are you interested in for a skin barrier repair product? (Select all that apply)
	+ This question helps us understand which features are most important to you.
