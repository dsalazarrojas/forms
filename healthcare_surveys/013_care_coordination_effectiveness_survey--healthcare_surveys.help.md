**Care Coordination Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information on the effectiveness of care coordination in healthcare settings. Your input will help us understand your experiences and perspectives on care coordination, enabling us to improve the quality of care and services provided.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and options.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best match your experiences and opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Respondent Information --
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name if you're comfortable sharing it. If not, you can leave this field blank.
### Role in care coordination (`role_in_care`, select_one, Required)
* Select your primary role in care coordination: 
  * Care coordinator: You're involved in coordinating care for patients.
  * Clinician: You're a healthcare professional providing medical care.
  * Patient: You're receiving care from healthcare providers.
  * Family member: You're involved in a family member's care.
### Communication effectiveness (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, Required)
* Rate the effectiveness of communication in care coordination:
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Fair
  * Poor
### Access to services (`access_to_services`, select_one, Required)
* Rate the ease of accessing needed services:
  * Very easy
  * Somewhat easy
  * Difficult
  * Very difficult
### Timeliness of care transfers (`timeliness_of_transfers`, select_one, Required)
* Rate the timeliness and coordination of care transfers:
  * Always
  * Usually
  * Sometimes
  * Rarely
### Overall satisfaction with coordination (`satisfaction_overall`, select_one, Required)
* Rate your overall satisfaction with care coordination:
  * Very satisfied
  * Satisfied
  * Neutral
  * Unsatisfied
### Barriers identified (`barriers_identified`, text, Optional)
* List any main obstacles to effective coordination.
### Suggestions for improvement (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, Optional)
* Share practical recommendations for improving care coordination.
### Willing to participate in follow up (`willing_to_participate_in_follow_up`, select_one, Optional)
* Are you willing to participate in follow-up discussions:
  * True
  * False
