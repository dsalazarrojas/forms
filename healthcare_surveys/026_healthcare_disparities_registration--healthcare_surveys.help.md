# Healthcare Disparities Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Disparities Registration form is designed to collect data on the experiences of patients and healthcare providers in accessing and providing healthcare services, with a focus on identifying and addressing potential barriers and disparities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, providing accurate information about your experiences and observations.
2. For each field, select the options that apply to you or your situation.
3. If you're unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Address**: Enter the address of the clinic where you or your patient receives healthcare services.
* **Clinic Name**: Enter the name of the clinic where you or your patient receives healthcare services.
* **Clinic Phone**: Enter the phone number of the clinic where you or your patient receives healthcare services.
* **Nonprofit Name**: Enter the name of the nonprofit organization associated with the clinic, if applicable.
* **Nonprofit Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nonprofit organization associated with the clinic, if applicable.
* **Public Health Department**: Enter the name of the public health department, if applicable.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address for any follow-up or additional information, if necessary.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the clinic's location.
* **What race/ethnicity do you identify with?**: Select all the races/ethnicities that you or your patient identify with (e.g., American Indian or Alaska Native, Asian, Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander, Some Other Race, White).
* **Sex**: Select the sex of the patient (e.g., Female, Male).
* **What barriers do you face in accessing health care?**: Select all the barriers you face in accessing healthcare services (e.g., Cost, Distance, Fear of provider bias, Fear of stigma or discrimination, Health insurance coverage, Language barriers, Other, Transportation cost).
* **What type of healthcare service is the patient seeking?**: Select all the healthcare services the patient is seeking (e.g., Ambulatory services, Behavioral health, Dental care, Emergency room, Family planning and reproductive health, Health education, Home health care, Laboratory, Mental health, Pharmacy, Radiology, Specialist, Surgical services).
* **How do you feel about your access to health care services?**: Select how you feel about your access to healthcare services (e.g., Always, Almost always, Almost never, Never).
* **What barriers do you face in accessing healthcare?**: Select all the barriers you face in accessing healthcare services (e.g., Cost, Distance, Fear of provider bias, Fear of stigma or discrimination, Health insurance coverage, Language barriers, Other, Transportation cost).
* **Race/ethnicity of patient's child**: Select all the races/ethnicities of the patient's child (e.g., American Indian or Alaska Native, Asian, Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander, Some Other Race, White).
* **What sex is the patient?**: Select the sex of the patient (e.g., Female, Male).
* **What barriers does the patient face in accessing healthcare?**: Select all the barriers the patient faces in accessing healthcare services (e.g., Cost, Distance, Fear of provider bias, Fear of stigma or discrimination, Health insurance coverage, Language barriers, Other, Transportation cost).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.
* You can select multiple options for any field, if applicable.
* The information collected will be used to help identify and address potential healthcare disparities, improving access to healthcare services for all individuals.
