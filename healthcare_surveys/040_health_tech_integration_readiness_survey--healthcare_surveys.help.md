# Health Tech Integration Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Tech Integration Readiness Survey is a form designed to help healthcare organizations assess their level of integration with health technology.
## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you have all necessary information and resources before filling out the form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you select the correct answer.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or support team for guidance.
- Submit the form once you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, not required): A brief introduction to your organization, including its size, type, and relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your organization:
	+ Has a dedicated team for health tech integration.
	+ Has a clear plan for health tech integration.
	+ Has existing integration with other systems.
	+ Has a budget allocated for health tech integration.
* **Organizational Readiness** (`3`, `number`, required): Select a number between 1-10 to indicate your organization's level of health tech integration readiness.
* **Barriers** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Choose the most significant barrier to health tech integration:
	+ Option 1: Lack of resources.
	+ Option 2: Limited technical expertise.
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify).
* **Integration Level** (`5`, `number`, required): Select a number between 0-10 to indicate your organization's current health tech integration level.
* **Tech Stack** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate:
	+ Does your organization use cloud-based platforms.
	+ Does your organization use on-premise systems.
	+ Does your organization use open-source systems.
* **Submission Information** (`7`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional information you feel is necessary about your submission.
