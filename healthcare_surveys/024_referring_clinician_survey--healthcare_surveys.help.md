# Referring Clinician Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Referring Clinician Survey is designed to collect information from referring clinicians about their patients. This information will help us improve our services and provide better care to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the appropriate options from the dropdown menus.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact details so we can get back to you.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your practice or healthcare organization.
* **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your practice or healthcare organization.
* **Practice Phone** (`practice_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your practice or healthcare organization.
* **Practice Email** (`practice_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of your practice or healthcare organization.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Please explain why you are referring this patient to us.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If known, please enter the patient's age.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, required): Select if the patient is new, existing, or both (new and existing).
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's condition.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Please explain why you are referring this patient to us.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* We appreciate your time and input in helping us provide better care to our patients.
