# healthcare_experience_review_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "healthcare_experience_review_request_form" is a tool designed to gather feedback and ratings from patients about their experience at healthcare facilities. This form helps healthcare providers identify areas of excellence and areas that need improvement, ultimately enhancing patient outcomes and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step instructions below:
	1. Fill in the "Patient Name" field with the patient's name.
	2. Enter the "Date Of Visit" field with the date of the patient's visit.
	3. In the "Doctor Name" field, enter the doctor's name who treated the patient.
	4. Select the rating for the patient's experience in the "Rating" field.
	5. In the "Feedback" field, provide any additional comments or suggestions the patient may have.
	6. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field (optional).
	7. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
	8. Select the rating for the patient's satisfaction in the "Rating Satisfaction" field (optional).
	9. Select the rating for the patient's comfort level with the doctor in the "Rating Comfort" field (optional).
	10. Select the specialties the doctor has expertise in from the "Doctor Specialty" field (optional).
	11. Select the doctor's title from the "Doctor Title" field (optional).
	12. Select the doctor's department from the "Doctor Department" field (optional).
	13. Enter the doctor's license number in the "Doctor License" field.
	14. Select the doctor's certification status in the "Doctor Certification" field (optional).
	15. Select the languages the doctor speaks from the "Doctor Languages" field (optional).
	16. Enter any other languages the doctor speaks in the "Doctor Languages Other" field (optional).
	17. Select the submission status of the form in the "Submission Status" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date Of Visit**: Enter the date of the patient's visit to the healthcare facility.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the doctor's name who treated the patient.
* **Rating**: Select a rating for the patient's experience using the scale provided.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions the patient may have.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
* **Rating Satisfaction**: Select a rating for the patient's satisfaction with their experience (optional).
* **Rating Comfort**: Select a rating for the patient's comfort level with the doctor (optional).
* **Doctor Specialty**: Select the specialties the doctor has expertise in (optional).
* **Doctor Title**: Select the doctor's title (optional).
* **Doctor Department**: Select the doctor's department (optional).
* **Doctor License**: Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Doctor Certification**: Select the doctor's certification status (optional).
* **Doctor Languages**: Select the languages the doctor speaks (optional).
* **Doctor Languages Other**: Enter any other languages the doctor speaks (optional).
* **Submission Status**: Select the submission status of the form (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* The patient's email address and phone number are optional, but can be useful for follow-up communication.
* If the patient has any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
* The submission status field should be selected once the form is complete and ready for review.
* The patient's ID, doctor's ID, and hospital ID fields are required for data tracking and analysis.
