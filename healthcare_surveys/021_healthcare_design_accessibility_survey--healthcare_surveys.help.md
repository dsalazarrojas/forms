# Healthcare Design Accessibility Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the accessibility of healthcare services from patients' perspectives. It aims to collect detailed information about your experiences with healthcare services, focusing on accessibility aspects such as communication, navigation, and overall comfort. Please answer these questions based on your recent interaction with our healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all necessary permissions and consent to provide your feedback.
2. If you're a healthcare professional, please ensure you're completing the form on behalf of a patient.
3. Please answer each question based on your genuine experiences with our healthcare services.
4. Ensure that you answer all required fields, marked as `required: true`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`1`, `text`, `required: false`): This section captures your basic contact information for our records.
	+ Please provide your name, age, and other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`2`, `text`, `required: true`): Share any relevant medical history that may affect your access to healthcare services.
	+ Be as detailed as possible, including any ongoing treatments, allergies, or previous surgeries.
* **Meds** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If applicable, list any medications you take, including painkillers, antibiotics, and anti-inflammatories.
* **Accessibility Score** (`4`, `number`, `required: true`): Rate your overall experience with accessibility on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Accessibility Issues** (`5`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe any issues or challenges you faced with our healthcare services' accessibility.
	+ Be as specific as possible, including any barriers or obstacles you encountered.
* **Healthcare Team** (`6`, `select_one`, `required: true`): Identify the healthcare professional(s) you interacted with during your visit.
	+ Choose one or multiple from the options provided.
* **Feedback** (`7`, `note`, `required: false`): Share any additional comments or suggestions about our services.
	+ This section is optional but highly valued.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, `required: true`): Enter your contact email for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, `required: false`): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Date of Visit** (`10`, `date`, `required: false`): If applicable, enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`11`, `time`, `required: false`): If applicable, enter the time of your visit.
* **Age** (`12`, `number`, `required: true`): Enter your age.
* **Date of Birth** (`13`, `date`, `required: false`): If applicable, enter your date of birth.
* **Gender** (`14`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Choose your gender.
	+ Select one or multiple from the options provided.
* **Ethnicity** (`15`, `text`, `required: true`): Describe your ethnicity or cultural background.
* **Race** (`16`, `text`, `required: true`): Describe your racial background.
* **Disability** (`17`, `text`, `required: true`): If applicable, describe any disability or impairment affecting your access to healthcare services.
* **Language** (`18`, `text`, `required: true`): Identify your native or dominant language.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`19`, `select_one`, `required: false`): If applicable, identify the healthcare provider you saw.
* **Hospital** (`20`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If applicable, select the hospital or facility you visited.
* **Department** (`21`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If applicable, select the department you visited.
* **Unit** (`22`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If applicable, select the unit you were at.
* **Shift** (`23`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If applicable, select the shift of the healthcare professionals you interacted with.
* **Role** (`24`, `select_multiple`, `required: true`): Identify your role during the visit.
	+ Select one or multiple from the options provided.
* **Comments** (`25`, `note`, `required: false`): Share any additional comments or suggestions about our services.
	+ This section is optional but highly valued.

Please take a few minutes to review each question carefully and answer them accurately based on your genuine experiences. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort.
