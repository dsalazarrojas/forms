# patient_health_questionnaire_phq9 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The patient_health_questionnaire_phq9 form is designed to collect information about a patient's health, including their mental health, social support, and other relevant aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide honest and accurate information about your health and well-being.
2. Answer each question carefully, selecting the most suitable option that reflects your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to collect information about your social history, including your family, friends, and healthcare providers.
* **General Health** (`general_health`, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the following options: Anxiety, Depression, Psychiatric Disorders.
* **Psychological Health** (`psychological_health`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to provide more detailed information about your psychological health.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
	+ Please provide any relevant medical history information.
* **Social Support** (`social_support`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply: Family, Friends, Healthcare provider.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time you have been experiencing mental health issues.
* **Alcohol History** (`alcohol_history`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the frequency of alcohol consumption.
* **Substance History** (`substance_history`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the frequency of substance use.
* **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the frequency of physical activity.
* **Sleep History** (`sleep_history`, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the following options: 'True', 'False', Not Sure.
* **Diet History** (`diet_history`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select all that apply: Fruits, Vegetables, Protein-rich foods.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, text, optional)
	+ Please provide any information about your medication history.
* **Pain History** (`pain_history`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the following options: Mild, Moderate, Severe.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- If you are unsure about any question, select the 'Not Sure' or 'Unknown' option.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact your healthcare provider.
- This form is for your benefit, and your responses will help improve your care and health outcomes.
