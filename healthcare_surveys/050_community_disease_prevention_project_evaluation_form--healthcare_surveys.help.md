# Community Disease Prevention Project Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from community members about our disease prevention projects. Your input is crucial in helping us improve our initiatives and better serve our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as the evaluator in the "Full Name of Evaluator" field.
2. Enter your email address for evaluation results in the "Email Address for Evaluation Results" field.
3. Provide your primary contact phone number in the "Primary Contact Phone Number" field.
4. Select the name of the project being evaluated in the "Name of the Project Being Evaluated" field.
5. In the "Project Assessment and Results" section, provide an assessment of the project's results and impact.
6. Answer the following questions:
	* Rate the project's outcomes achievement ("Were the project's intended outcomes achieved").
	* Rate the project's impact on the community's health ("Rate the project's impact on our community's health").
	* Rate the level of community engagement in the project ("Rate the level of community engagement in the project").
7. Provide your ideas for improving our disease prevention projects in the "What are your ideas for improving our disease prevention projects" field.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the project ("Rate your overall satisfaction with the project").
9. Share your ideas for future disease prevention projects in the "What are your ideas for future disease prevention projects" field.
10. Indicate your willingness to support a disease prevention project ("Would you be willing to support a disease prevention project").
11. Sign with your full name in the "Digital Signature (Type Full Name)" field, certifying that the information provided is correct.
12. Enter the date of evaluation submission in the "Date of Evaluation Submission" field.
13. Choose whether you would like to receive our newsletter ("I would like to receive the community center's newsletter").
14. Select how you heard about this project evaluation form ("How did you hear about this project evaluation form").
15. Enter the full name of an emergency contact person (not living with the evaluator).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Evaluator** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as the person evaluating the project.
* **Email Address for Evaluation Results** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for evaluation results, where we will send you the outcome.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (Text, Required): Provide your primary contact phone number.
* **Name of the Project Being Evaluated** (Text, Required): Select the name of the project being evaluated.
* **Project Assessment and Results** (Note, Optional): Provide an assessment of the project's results and impact.
* **Were the project's intended outcomes achieved** (Select One, Required): Rate whether the project's outcomes were achieved.
* **Rate the project's impact on our community's health** (Number, Required): Rate the project's impact on the community's health on a scale of 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High).
* **Rate the level of community engagement in the project** (Number, Required): Rate the level of engagement in the project on a scale of 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High).
* **What are your ideas for improving our disease prevention projects** (Text, Required): Provide ideas for improving our disease prevention projects.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with the project** (Number, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the project on a scale of 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **What are your ideas for future disease prevention projects** (Text, Required): Share your ideas for future disease prevention projects.
* **Would you be willing to support a disease prevention project** (Select One, Required): Indicate your willingness to support a disease prevention project.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)** (Text, Required): Sign with your full name, certifying that the information provided is correct.
* **Date of Evaluation Submission** (Date, Required): Enter the date of evaluation submission.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you would like to receive our newsletter.
* **How did you hear about this project evaluation form** (Select One, Optional): Select how you heard about this form.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of an emergency contact person not living with the evaluator.
