# Preventive Screening Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a preventive screening program. It captures participant information, screening data, and satisfaction scores to assess the program's outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Screening Participant ID.
2. Enter the Program Name.
3. Select the Date of Screening.
4. Choose the Time of Screening.
5. Enter the Screening Location.
6. Choose the Screening Type (Screening A, B, or C).
7. Select the Screening Result (Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive).
8. Enter your Screening Comments.
9. Provide your Participant Feedback.
10. Enter the Program Satisfaction Score.
11. Enter the Participant Satisfaction Score.
12. Enter the Screening Score.
13. Enter the Participant Comments.
14. Enter the Evaluation Notes.
15. Select the Screening Outcome (Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive).
16. Select the Participant Outcome (Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive).
17. Enter the Participant Satisfaction Score.
18. Enter the Evaluation Score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screening Participant ID** (`screening_participant_id`, text, required): Enter your participant ID to identify yourself in the screening program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you were screened for.
* **Date of Screening** (`date_screening`, date, required): Enter the date you underwent the screening.
* **Time of Screening** (`time_screening`, time, required): Choose the time of the screening.
* **Screening Location** (`screening_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the screening took place.
* **Screening Type** (`screening_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of screening you underwent (Screening A, B, or C).
* **Screening Result** (`screening_result`, select_multiple, required): Select the outcome of the screening (Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive).
* **Screening Comments** (`screening_comments`, text, required): Provide your comments about the screening process.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required): Share your feedback about the screening experience.
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, number, required): Enter a satisfaction score from 1 to 5 for the program.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, number, required): Enter a satisfaction score from 1 to 5 for your experience.
* **Screening Score** (`screening_score`, number, required): Enter a score from 1 to 5 for the screening.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required): Share your comments about the participant experience.
* **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional evaluation notes.
* **Screening Outcome** (`screening_outcome`, select_multiple, required): Select the outcome of the screening (Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive).
* **Participant Outcome** (`participant_outcome`, select_multiple, required): Select the outcome of your experience (Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive).
* **Participant Satisfaction Score** (`participant_satisfaction_score`, number, required): Enter a satisfaction score from 1 to 5 for your experience.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, required): Enter a evaluation score from 1 to 5.

**Note:** Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the screening program.
