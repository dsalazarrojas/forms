# Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from trainers and evaluators on the effectiveness of medical simulation training events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the simulation training event that has just occurred.
2. Select the level of training that was being evaluated during the simulation (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
3. Choose the medical specialties that were simulated during the training session (General Surgery, Cardiology, or Dermatology).
4. Provide a score between 1-10 to evaluate how well the trainee performed during the simulation.
5. If desired, add any additional comments about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, optional): This field is for a brief description of the simulation training event that has just occurred.

### Simulation Details
* **Simulation Details** (`simulation_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the simulation training event that has just occurred.

### Simulation Date
* **Simulation Date** (`simulation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the simulation training event.

### Training Level
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of training that was being evaluated during the simulation (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).

### Training Session
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the training sessions that occurred during the simulation (1, 2, or 3).

### Medical Specialty
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, required): Choose the medical specialty that was being simulated during the training session (General Surgery, Cardiology, or Dermatology).

### Simulation Score
* **Simulation Score** (`simulation_score`, number, required): Provide a score between 1-10 to evaluate how well the trainee performed during the simulation.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the training session.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback on the simulation training event.
* Make sure to select all relevant medical specialties that were simulated during the training session.
* When providing a score, consider factors such as the trainee's level of understanding, their ability to apply knowledge, and their overall performance during the simulation.
