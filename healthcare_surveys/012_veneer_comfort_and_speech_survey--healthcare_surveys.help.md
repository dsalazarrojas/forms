# Veneer Comfort And Speech Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veneer Comfort And Speech Survey is designed to collect information about the comfort and satisfaction of patients or caregivers during a medical procedure or appointment. This feedback is essential for healthcare professionals to understand the effectiveness of their care and services, allowing them to make necessary improvements.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields provided.
2. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Comfort** (`overall_comfort`, `number`, required): 
The "Overall Comfort" field asks you to rate the level of comfort you felt during the procedure or appointment on a scale (we assume a numerical scale, e.g., 1-5). Be honest and provide your best assessment of your comfort level.
* **Sensitivity** (`sensitivity`, `number`, required): 
The "Sensitivity" field also requests a numerical rating, similar to the "Overall Comfort" field. This will help healthcare professionals understand your experience and provide better care.
* **Speech Change** (`speech_change`, `text`, optional): 
The "Speech Change" field is an open-ended field where you can describe any changes you noticed in your speech during or after the procedure/appointment. This can include any difficulties you experienced with communication or any changes in your ability to speak.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): 
The "Overall Satisfaction" field asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the care and services provided on a numerical scale. If you are not satisfied, please explain why in the "Speech Change" field.
## Tips
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in each field.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any suggestions or comments about the form or the care provided, please use the "Speech Change" field to share them.
