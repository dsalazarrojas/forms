# Restaurant Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the health and well-being of restaurant employees, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic. It helps to track employee health status, identify potential risks, and provide guidance on follow-up instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Fill out the employee's personal details, such as their name, phone number, and email.
3. Report any health symptoms or concerns, including temperature, chills, cough, runny nose, headache, sore throat, or fever.
4. Select the follow-up instructions and contact information if the employee needs to be contacted for further evaluation.
5. Sign and date the form with both the employee and their manager.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (ID 2): The name of the employee answering the questionnaire.
* **Employee Phone** (ID 3): The employee's phone number for communication.
* **Employee Email** (ID 4): The employee's email address for further communication.
* **Restaurant Name** (ID 5): The name of the restaurant where the employee works.
* **Manager Name** (ID 6): The name of the employee's manager or supervisor.
* **Employee Location** (ID 7): The location where the employee is working, such as the restaurant's address.
* **Employee Position** (ID 8): The employee's job title or position.
* **Health Screening Date** (ID 9): The date of the health screening assessment.
* **Has Temperature** (ID 10): Check "True" if the employee has a temperature above 100.4°F (38°C).
* **Has Chills** (ID 11): Check "True" if the employee has chills or feels cold.
* **Has Cough** (ID 12): Check "True" if the employee has a cough.
* **Has Runny Nose** (ID 13): Check "True" if the employee has a runny or stuffy nose.
* **Has Headache** (ID 14): Check "True" if the employee has a headache.
* **Has Sore Throat** (ID 15): Check "True" if the employee has a sore or scratchy throat.
* **Has Fever** (ID 16): Check "True" if the employee has a fever above 100.4°F (38°C).
* **Health Screening Question** (ID 17): If the employee has any specific health concerns or questions, please provide them here.
* **Follow-Up Instructions** (ID 18): Provide any follow-up instructions or guidance given to the employee.
* **Follow-Up Contact Name** (ID 19): The name of the person the employee should contact for further evaluation.
* **Follow-Up Contact Phone** (ID 20): The phone number to contact the employee or follow-up person.
* **Follow-Up Contact Email** (ID 21): The email address to contact the employee or follow-up person.
* **Follow-Up Date** (ID 22): The date for follow-up contact or evaluation.
* **Employee Signature** (ID 23): The employee's signature confirming they have answered the questionnaire accurately.
* **Manager Signature** (ID 24): The manager or supervisor's signature confirming they have reviewed the questionnaire.
* **Review Status** (ID 25): The status of the review, such as Approved, Rejected, or In Progress.
* **Review Notes** (ID 26): Any additional notes or comments from the reviewer.
