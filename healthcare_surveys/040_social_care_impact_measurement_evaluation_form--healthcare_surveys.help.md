# Social Care Impact Measurement Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the impact of social care services on individuals. It is used to collect feedback and evaluations from service users and service providers. The form is used to gauge the effectiveness and quality of the support services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Service User Info section (questions 1-4) with your information:
   * Service User Info: This section collects demographic information about the service user, such as their email address and phone number.
   * Date of Support: Record the date on which you received the service support.
   * Time of Support: Record the time of day when you received the service support.
   * Have you ever been surveyed before? Select one of the options to indicate if you have been surveyed before.
2. Evaluate the service you received:
   * What was the impact on you? Rate the impact of the service on a scale of 1-5.
   * Impact Rating Scale: This is the scale used to evaluate the impact of the service.
   * How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the support received? Select one of the options.
   * Would you recommend this support service to a friend or family member? Select one of the options.
3. If you are a service provider, fill in your information:
   * Service Provider Name: Enter your name as a service provider.
   * Service Provider Title: Enter your title as a service provider.
   * Service Provider Email: Enter your email address as a service provider.
   * Service Provider Phone: Enter your phone number as a service provider.
   * Service Provider Notes: Enter any additional notes about yourself as a service provider.
4. Add any Additional Comments:
   * Additional Comments: Enter any feedback or comments about the service you received or the support you provided.
