# Health Product Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to measure the satisfaction of healthcare products and services. It aims to gather feedback and ratings to help improve the quality and effectiveness of these products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction carefully, which provides additional information about the form and its purpose.
2. Select the healthcare product ID to associate your feedback with the specific product you are using.
3. Choose your overall satisfaction level from the provided options.
4. Share your feedback about the product or service.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
6. Rate the product or service.
7. Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
8. Enter your phone number (optional).
9. Add any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Health Product ID**:
	+ Enter the ID of the healthcare product you are providing feedback on. This is a required field.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level**:
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction level with the healthcare product or service. Choose from:
		+ Option 1: Very Dissatisfied
		+ Option 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
		+ Option 3: Neutral
		+ Option 4: Satisfied
		+ Option 5: Very Satisfied
* **What is your Feedback?**:
	+ Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about the healthcare product or service.
* **What do you Suggest?**:
	+ Provide any suggestions you have for improving the product or service.
* **Rating**:
	+ Rate the healthcare product or service. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the healthcare product or service.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you if needed. This is a required field.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional Information**:
	+ Share any additional information you'd like to provide about the healthcare product or service.
