# Medical Device Usability Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Device Usability Evaluation Survey is designed to gather feedback on the ease of use and usability of medical devices. The form aims to collect information on how easy or difficult it is to use these devices, areas where improvement is needed, and user behavior such as frequency of use and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections. Each section is a separate page.
2. Please read each question carefully and answer it according to your experience with the medical device.
3. For questions with options, choose the answer that best fits your response.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
5. If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the space provided for notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Device Usability Evaluation Survey**:
	+ This is the title of the form. Please do not modify it.
* **Introduction**:
	+ This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How easy to use is the device overall?**:
	+ This question asks about your overall experience with the device. Choose the answer that best fits your experience.
	+ Very easy: The device is very easy to use.
	+ Somewhat easy: The device is somewhat easy to use.
	+ Not very easy: The device is not very easy to use.
	+ Not at all easy: The device is not at all easy to use.
* **What are some areas where the device could be improved?**:
	+ Please provide specific areas where you think the device could be improved.
* **Do you use the device?**:
	+ Yes: You use the device regularly.
	+ No: You do not use the device.
* **How often do you use the device?**:
	+ Daily: You use the device every day.
	+ Weekly: You use the device weekly.
	+ Monthly: You use the device monthly.
	+ Rarely: You use the device rarely.
* **What would make you use the device more?**:
	+ Please provide specific suggestions or ideas that would make you use the device more.
* **Do you have any suggestions to improve the device?**:
	+ Please provide specific suggestions or ideas that would improve the device.
* **Is there anything you would like to add, or change about the device?**:
	+ Please provide any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about the device.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can provide feedback on the device, even if you do not use it.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
