# Health Symptoms Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's health symptoms, including general symptoms, pain locations, specific symptoms, and personal details such as phone number and email. This data will be used to aid healthcare professionals in providing accurate diagnoses and treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the general symptoms that you are experiencing from the options provided.
* Choose the specific locations where you are experiencing pain.
* Select the symptoms you are experiencing from the options provided for each symptom.
* Enter your phone number and email for contact purposes.
* Provide the date and time you are experiencing the symptoms.
* Select any additional symptoms that you are experiencing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Symptoms** (`general_symptoms`, `select_one`): Choose the general symptoms that you are experiencing. Please select only one option.
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
* **Pain** (`pain_location`, `select_multiple`): Choose the locations where you are experiencing pain. You can select multiple options.
	+ Head
	+ Neck
	+ Back
	+ Stomach
	+ Legs
* **Cough** (`symptom_1`, `select_one`): Choose the type of cough you are experiencing. Please select only one option.
	+ Cough
	+ Coughing Up Blood
	+ Chest Pain
	+ Shortness of Breath
* **Fatigue** (`fatigue`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of fatigue you are experiencing. You can select multiple options.
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
* **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`): Enter any additional information you would like to share with the healthcare professional.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`): Enter the date you are experiencing the symptoms.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`): Enter the time you are experiencing the symptoms.
* **Fever** (`fever`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of fever you are experiencing. You can select multiple options.
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
* **Nausea** (`nausea`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of nausea you are experiencing. You can select multiple options.
	+ Nausea
