# Inner Cycle Pattern Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inner Cycle Pattern Survey" is designed to collect information on the inner cycle pattern of a patient, helping healthcare professionals understand the type, frequency, and impact of this pattern on their daily life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page, which provides an overview of the survey.
2. On the "Cycle type" page, select all options that apply to your inner cycle pattern.
3. On the "Cycle regularity" page, enter a numerical value representing how regular or frequent your inner cycle pattern occurs.
4. On the "Symptoms" page, describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible.
5. On the "Daily life impact" page, provide information on how your symptoms affect your daily life.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page introduces the survey and provides background information on the inner cycle pattern.
* **Cycle type**: Select all that apply from the options provided to describe the type of inner cycle pattern you experience. This will help us understand the characteristics of your inner cycle pattern.
* **Cycle regularity**: Estimate the frequency of your inner cycle pattern using a numerical value. For example, if you experience the pattern every day, enter "1". If it occurs only occasionally, enter a smaller value.
* **Symptoms**: Describe your symptoms in as much detail as possible, including any physical, emotional, or cognitive effects.
* **Daily life impact**: Explain how your inner cycle pattern affects your daily life, including any significant changes or challenges you experience.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please provide honest and accurate information about your inner cycle pattern. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
