# HealthCare AI Bias Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this HealthCare AI Bias Awareness Survey is to collect feedback and information from healthcare professionals or individuals with healthcare experience on their experiences with AI systems in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the **AI Bias** value that best represents your experience with AI systems in healthcare.
3. Enter your **Healthcare Background** and **Patient Data** to provide context to your answers.
4. Choose one or more **AI Use** options that apply to your experience.
5. Select the **AI Concerns** option that best represents your concerns with AI systems in the healthcare sector.
6. If you have any **Comments** or additional thoughts, please include them in the space provided.
7. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address used for work-related correspondence.
* **Phone**: Enter your work-related phone number.
* **AI Bias**: Choose a number from 1 to 4 to represent your experience with AI systems in healthcare, where 1 indicates "No experience" and 4 indicates "Strongly Agree" with the given statement.
* **Healthcare Background**: Enter a brief description of your healthcare-related experience and background.
* **Patient Data**: Enter the date of your last patient interaction or the date you last accessed patient data.
* **AI Training**: Choose the time period during which you received AI training related to healthcare.
* **AI Use**: Select one or more options that apply to your experience with AI systems in healthcare:
	+ Option 1: Data analysis
	+ Option 2: Predictive modeling
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **AI Concerns**: Choose the option that best represents your concerns with AI systems in healthcare:
	+ Option 1: Lack of transparency
	+ Option 2: Data quality issues
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about AI systems in healthcare.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete the survey.
