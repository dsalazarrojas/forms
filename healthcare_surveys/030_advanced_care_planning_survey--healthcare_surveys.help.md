# Advanced Care Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advanced Care Planning Survey aims to gather information on patients' care planning preferences, including their living will status, health care agent appointment, end-of-life care preferences, organ donation preference, interest in palliative care, concerns or questions, and contact information for follow-up. This survey is an essential tool for healthcare providers to understand patients' wishes and preferences, ensuring they receive the necessary care and support during critical periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Advanced Care Planning Survey, follow these steps:

1.  Review the survey carefully, and consider each question carefully.
2.  Enter your patient's name (if applicable) in the "Patient name" field.
3.  Indicate your living will status by selecting "True," "False," or "Unsure."
4.  If you have a health care agent appointed, enter their name and contact information.
5.  Describe your end-of-life care preferences in the text field.
6.  Choose your organ donation preference from the dropdown menu.
7.  Indicate your interest in palliative care by selecting "True" or "False."
8.  If you have any concerns or questions, enter them in the text field.
9.  Provide your contact information for follow-up, including a phone number or email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Care Planning Preferences --**: This section serves as a header for the survey, providing a brief introduction to the questions that follow.
*   **Patient name**: Enter the name of the patient you are filling out the survey for. This field is optional and only required if the patient's identity is known.
*   **Living will in place**: Select "True" if you have a living will, "False" if you do not, or "Unsure" if you are unsure or do not have one.
*   **Health care agent appointed**: If you have appointed a health care agent, enter their name and contact information, such as a phone number or email.
*   **End of life care preferences**: Describe your wishes and preferences for end-of-life care in this text field. Be succinct and clear in your description.
*   **Organ donation preference**: Choose your organ donation preference from the dropdown menu. Select "True" if you wish to be an organ donor, "False" if you do not, or "Undecided" if you are undecided.
*   **Interest in palliative care**: Indicate your interest in palliative care by selecting "True" or "False." This question helps healthcare providers understand your wishes for comfort care during a critical period.
*   **Concerns or questions**: If you have any concerns or questions, enter them in this text field. This is an opportunity to discuss any issues or worries you may have.
*   **Contact info for follow up**: Provide your contact information, such as a phone number or email, so healthcare providers can follow up with you regarding your survey responses.
