# Nonclinical Study Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a nonclinical study. It's meant to gather participant thoughts and opinions on the study design, questions, and overall experience. Your feedback is valuable and helps us improve future studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions that apply to you.
2. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
3. If you have any contact information, please provide it in the "Contact Info" field.
4. Finally, submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Feedback** (`study_feedback`, `text`, not required): Share your thoughts and opinions about the study design, questions, and overall experience.
* **Research Questions** (`research_questions`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the questions that you have about this study.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Please provide your email address if you selected "Email".
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Provide your phone number if you selected "Phone".
* **Other** (`other_contact_info`, `text`, not required): If you selected "Other", please provide your other contact information.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `text`, not required): This field is used for internal submission tracking purposes. Do not fill it out unless you're sure it's correct.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Please select the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Please select the time you completed this form.
* **Study Name** (`study_name`, `text`, not required): If you know the name of the study, please provide it.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, `text`, not required): If you have any information about the study, please provide it.

## Tips

- Be honest and thorough with your feedback.
- Make sure to answer all questions that apply.
- Keep your responses concise and clear.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the research team.
- All feedback will be reviewed and kept anonymous.
