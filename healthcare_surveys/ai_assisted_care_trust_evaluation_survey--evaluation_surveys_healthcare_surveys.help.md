# AI Assisted Care Trust Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Assisted Care Trust Evaluation Survey is designed to gather information about your perception of AI's trustworthiness in providing healthcare services. Your responses will help us improve AI's performance and ensure it meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. For each field, please provide accurate and honest responses based on your experiences with AI.
3. If you're unsure or have questions, feel free to ask or seek clarification from the AI system.
4. Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for help if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
• **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal name.
• **Date of birth** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
• **Age** (`patient_age`, `integer`, required): This field is calculated automatically, but you can override it if you prefer.
• **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): Include your country code.
• **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We'll use this for follow-up.

### Visit Details
• **Date of visit** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
• **Preferred appointment time** (`visit_time`, `time`, optional): Choose your preferred appointment time.

### Health Assessment
• **Current symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the symptoms that apply.
• **Pain level** (`pain_level`, `integer`, optional): Rate your pain level from 0 to 10.
• **Current medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List all your current medications, including OTC.
• **Known allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any known allergies.
• **Relevant medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Include any previous conditions or surgeries.

### AI Trust Evaluation
• **Trust in AI** (`trust_in_ai`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best reflects your overall trust level.
• **Confidence in AI** (`confidence_in_ai`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best reflects your confidence level in AI's accuracy.
• **Understand AI explanation** (`understand_ai_explanation`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you understood AI's explanation.
• **Rate your trust in AI** (`trust_rating`, `integer`, optional): Rate your trust in AI from 1 to 10.

### Feedback
• **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any other comments or suggestions you have.
