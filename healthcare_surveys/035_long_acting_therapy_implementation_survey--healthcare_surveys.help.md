# long-acting-therapy-implementation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience and needs related to implementing long-acting therapies in your practice. It aims to gather insights on the benefits, barriers, and support needs associated with the implementation of long-acting therapies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the benefits you see when implementing long-acting therapies in your practice.
2. Choose the barriers you experience with implementing long-acting therapies.
3. Rate your confidence in recommending long-acting therapies.
4. Select the support you need to implement long-acting therapies in your practice.
5. Share the benefits you've experienced with implementing long-acting therapies in your practice (optional).
6. Select the barriers you experience with implementing long-acting therapies in your practice (optional).
7. Rate the support you get from your practice for implementing long-acting therapies (optional).
8. Choose the specific knowledge you think you need to effectively implement long-acting therapies.
9. Select the resources you have for implementing long-acting therapies (optional).
10. Select the resources you need to effectively implement long-acting therapies.
11. Rate your experience with long-acting therapies.
12. Select the barriers you experience with implementing long-acting therapies (optional).
13. Choose the benefit you think your practice will get from implementing long-acting therapies (optional).
