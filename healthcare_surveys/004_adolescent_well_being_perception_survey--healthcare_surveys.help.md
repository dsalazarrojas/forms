# Adolescent Well Being Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adolescent Well Being Perception Survey is designed to gather your perceptions on various aspects of your well-being as an adolescent. Your feedback will help us improve support systems and better understand your needs and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section and questions carefully.
2. Answer questions honestly, selecting the best option that represents your current feelings and experiences.
3. Be sure to complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
4. If you have any difficulties or concerns, contact your school support personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Happiness** (`General Happiness`, select_one, *):
	+ How would you describe your overall happiness lately?
	+ Select the option that best matches your current emotional state.
- **School Stress Level** (`School Stress Level`, select_one, *):
	+ Rate your current academic and social stress levels.
	+ Choose an option that best describes your stress level.
- **Sense of Belonging at School** (`Sense of Belonging at School`, select_one, *):
	+ How connected do you feel to your school community?
	+ Select the option that best reflects your current sense of belonging.
- **Peer Support Availability** (`Peer Support Availability`, select_one, *):
	+ Can you count on friends when you need help?
	+ Choose the option that best describes your friends' availability.
- **Adult Support at School** (`Adult Support at School`, select_one, *):
	+ Is there an adult at school you trust?
	+ Select the option that best reflects your current relationship with school adults.
- **Extracurricular Involvement** (`Extracurricular Involvement`, select_multiple,):
	+ What activities do you participate in?
	+ Choose all that apply from the list.
- **Hobby or Interest Description** (`Hobby or Interest Description`, text, ):
	+ What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
	+ Type a brief description of your favorite activities or interests.
- **Sleep Quality** (`Sleep Quality`, select_one, *):
	+ Rate your sleep over the past week.
	+ Select the option that best describes your sleep quality.
- **Average Hours of Sleep** (`Average Hours of Sleep`, number, ):
	+ On a typical weeknight, how many hours do you sleep?
	+ Enter the number of hours you typically sleep.
- **Wellness Goals** (`Wellness Goals`, text, ):
	+ One thing you would like to improve about your well-being?
	+ Type a brief description of your goals.

Note: This guide assumes that the provided YAML fields are the only ones included in the form. If additional fields are added or modified, this guide will require updates accordingly.
