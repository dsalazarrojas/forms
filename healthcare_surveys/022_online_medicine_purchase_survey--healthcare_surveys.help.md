# online_medicine_purchase_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online medicine purchase survey is designed to collect information about your online purchase habits, motivations, and concerns related to buying medicine. This survey aims to understand your preferences and pain points when it comes to buying medicine online.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. You can skip any question that you feel uncomfortable answering.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **purchase_habits** (Text): Describe your online purchase habits. This could include how often you purchase medicine, where you purchase it from, and any other relevant details.
* **purchase_motivations** (Text): Explain what motivates you to make an online purchase. This could be due to convenience, price, availability, or any other reason.
* **concerns** (Text): Share any concerns you have about buying medicine online. This could include worries about safety, authenticity, or any other issues.
* **satisfaction** (Text): Describe your overall satisfaction with online medicine purchases. This could be based on your previous experiences or expectations.
* **age** (Number): Enter your age, as it might be relevant to your online purchase habits and motivations.
* **income** (Number): Please enter your annual income, as it might influence your online purchase decisions.
* **education** (Text): Provide your highest level of education completed.
* **medical_conditions** (Select Multiple): List any medical conditions you have, such as heart disease, diabetes, or allergies.
* **prescription_meds** (Select One): Indicate whether you have purchased prescription medicine online or not.
* **online_meds** (Select One): Tell us if you have used online medicine or not.
* **purchase_location** (Select One): Choose where you typically purchase medicine from, such as a pharmacy, online, or in person.
* **satisfaction_reason** (Text): Explain why you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your online purchase experience.
* **concerns_reason** (Text): Share why you are concerned about buying medicine online.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address, if you're comfortable sharing it.
* **phone** (Text): Provide your phone number, if you're comfortable sharing it.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date you last purchased medicine online.
* **time** (Time): Record the time you usually make an online purchase.
* **note** (Text): Add any additional information you'd like to share about your experience.
* **age_range** (Text): Enter your age range.
* **purchase_frequency** (Select Multiple): Choose how often you make online purchases.
* **purchase_method** (Select Multiple): Tell us how you usually find out about online purchases.
* **concerns_frequency** (Select Multiple): Describe how often you have concerns about buying medicine online.
* **age_group** (Text): Enter your age group.
* **purchase_satisfaction** (Select One): Rate your satisfaction with online medicine purchases.
* **email_confirmed** (Select One): Confirm your email address, if you've provided it.
