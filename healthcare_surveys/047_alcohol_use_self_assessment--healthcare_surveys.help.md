# Alcohol Use Self-Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you assess your alcohol use habits and behaviors over the past 12 months. It will guide you through a series of questions to help you reflect on your drinking habits and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and without embellishment.
2. Select the options that best describe your behavior in each section.
3. Enter your desired goals for improvement in the "Next Steps" field, if applicable.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Why are you taking this assessment today?** (`assessment_reason`): 
  This question helps us understand why you are taking this assessment. Choose the option that best describes your reason for taking this assessment.
  * Personal curiosity
  * Concern from family/friends
  * Suggestion from healthcare professional
  * Requirement for work/school
  * Monitoring progress in recovery
* **How often do you have a drink containing alcohol?** (`frequency_drinking_self`): 
  This question asks about your drinking frequency over the past 12 months. Choose the option that best describes how often you drink.
  * Never
  * Monthly or less
  * 2-4 times a month
  * 2-3 times a week
  * 4 or more times a week
* **How many drinks containing alcohol do you have on a typical day when you are drinking?** (`quantity_per_occasion`): 
  This question asks about your drinking quantity over the past 12 months. Choose the option that best describes how many drinks you have on a typical day when you are drinking.
  * 1 or 2
  * 3 or 4
  * 5 or 6
  * 7, 8, or 9
  * 10 or more
* **How often have you had six or more drinks on one occasion?** (`binge_frequency_self`): 
  This question asks about your binge drinking frequency over the past 12 months. Choose the option that best describes how often you have had six or more drinks in one occasion.
  * Never
  * Less than monthly
  * Monthly
  * Weekly
  * Daily or almost daily
* **How often have you found that you were not able to stop drinking once you had started?** (`fail_to_stop`): 
  This question asks about your ability to stop drinking once you started. Choose the option that best describes how often you were unable to stop drinking.
  * Never
  * Less than monthly
  * Monthly
  * Weekly
  * Daily or almost daily
* **How often have you failed to do what was normally expected from you because of drinking?** (`role_performance_impact`): 
  This question asks about how often you fail to meet your responsibilities due to drinking. Choose the option that best describes how often you have failed to do what was normally expected from you.
  * Never
  * Less than monthly
  * Monthly
  * Weekly
  * Daily or almost daily
* **How often have you needed a first drink in the morning to get yourself going after a heavy drinking session?** (`morning_drinking`): 
  This question asks about your morning drinking habits. Choose the option that best describes how often you have needed a first drink in the morning.
  * Never
  * Less than monthly
  * Monthly
  * Weekly
  * Daily or almost daily
* **How often have you felt a feeling of guilt or remorse after drinking?** (`blackouts_history`): 
  This question asks about your feelings after drinking. Choose the option that best describes how often you have felt guilty or remorseful after drinking.
  * Never
  * Less than monthly
  * Monthly
  * Weekly
  * Daily or almost daily
* **Have you or someone else been injured as a result of your drinking?** (`injury_related_drinking`): 
  This question asks about any injuries you or someone else have suffered as a result of your drinking. Choose the option that best describes your situation.
  * False
  * Yes, but not in the last year
  * Yes, during the last year
* **How concerned are you about your own drinking habits?** (`personal_concern_level`): 
  This question asks about your level of concern about your drinking habits. Choose the option that best describes your level of concern.
  * 1 (Not at all) to 10 (Extremely)
* **Would you like to receive information about support services?** (`support_interest`): 
  This question asks if you would like to receive information about support services. Choose the option that best describes your interest.
  * True
  * False
  * Maybe later
* **What is one goal you have regarding your alcohol use?** (`next_steps_reflection`): 
  This question asks about your goals for improving your drinking habits. Enter a goal you have for your alcohol use.
* **Date of Self-Assessment** (`assessment_date`): 
  This field is for recording the date you completed this assessment.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Take your time to reflect on your drinking habits.
* If you need help or support, consider reaching out to a healthcare professional or a support service.
* Remember that this assessment is for your personal use and improvement.
