# Oxygen Concentrator Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patient experiences and opinions on oxygen concentrator usage. It will help healthcare providers and medical suppliers understand patient feedback, satisfaction, and any issues encountered with the oxygen concentrators. Your input is valuable in improving the quality and effectiveness of oxygen therapy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer the questions based on your personal experience with the oxygen concentrator.
3. Make sure to provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Please provide your basic information such as name, date of birth, etc.
* **Patient Device Use (2)**: Choose the type of oxygen concentrator you are using (e.g., portable oxygen concentrator, home oxygen concentrator, etc.).
* **Maintenance Habits (3)**: Describe how often you perform routine cleaning of the device.
* **Device Satisfaction (4)**: Rate your satisfaction with the device, from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Issues Reported (5)**: Describe any issues you are experiencing with the device.
* **Service Support (6)**: Indicate how frequently you receive service support for the device (e.g., "Regularly", "Occasionally", "Never").
* **Patient Feedback (7)**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving the oxygen concentrator.
* **Supplier Name (8)**: If you purchased the device from a supplier, enter their name.
* **Supplier Phone (9)**: If you purchased the device from a supplier, enter their phone number.
* **Patient Email (10)**: Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Healthcare Provider Name (11)**: If you are receiving oxygen therapy, enter your healthcare provider's name.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone (12)**: If you are receiving oxygen therapy, enter your healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Email (13)**: If you are receiving oxygen therapy, enter your healthcare provider's email address.
* **Medical Supervisor Name (14)**: If you are under a medical supervisor's care, enter their name.
* **Medical Supervisor Phone (15)**: If you are under a medical supervisor's care, enter their phone number.
* **Medical Supervisor Email (16)**: If you are under a medical supervisor's care, enter their email address.
* **Hospital Clinic Name (17)**: If you received oxygen therapy in a hospital or clinic, enter their name.
* **Hospital Clinic Phone (18)**: If you received oxygen therapy in a hospital or clinic, enter their phone number.
* **Hospital Clinic Email (19)**: If you received oxygen therapy in a hospital or clinic, enter their email address.
* **Patient Signature (20)**: Please sign your name to confirm your identity.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature (21)**: If you are receiving oxygen therapy, please sign your healthcare provider's name to confirm their identity.
* **Medical Supervisor Signature (22)**: If you are under a medical supervisor's care, please sign their name to confirm their identity.
* **Hospital Clinic Signature (23)**: If you received oxygen therapy in a hospital or clinic, please sign their name to confirm their identity.
* **Supplier Signature (24)**: If you purchased the device from a supplier, please sign their name to confirm their identity.
* **Patient Date (25)**: Enter the date you are completing this form.

Please note that some fields are optional, marked as "Not Required" in the form. If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
