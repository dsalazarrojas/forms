# Microorganism Growth Control Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather data on various practices and conditions related to microorganism growth control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each section.
2. Select the relevant options for "Microbial-control-practices" and ensure that you have considered all relevant practices.
3. Enter the specific procedures for sampling and inoculation in the "Sampling-and-inoculation" section.
4. Select a specific time value for incubation conditions in the "Incubation-conditions" section.
5. Select a specific option for incubation period in the "Incubation-period" section.
6. Enter a numerical value for temperature control in the "Temperature-control" section.
7. Enter a numerical value for incubation duration in the "Incubation-duration" section.
8. Enter any results or observations obtained from the survey in the "Results-obtained" section.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the survey in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Microbial-control-practices** (`Microbial-control-practices`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): This section asks you to select one or more practices that are used to control microorganism growth. Please choose all the options that apply to your survey.
* **Sampling-and-inoculation** (`Sampling-and-inoculation`, `text`, `required: false`): This section asks you to enter specific details about sampling and inoculation procedures. Please provide clear and concise information about how you collected and prepared your samples.
* **Incubation-conditions** (`Incubation-conditions`, `time`, `required: false`): This section asks you to select a specific time value for incubation conditions. Please choose a value that accurately reflects the conditions under which you collected your samples.
* **Incubation-period** (`Incubation-period`, `select_one`, `required: false`): This section asks you to select a specific option for incubation period. Please choose the option that best describes the period you used for incubation.
* **Temperature-control** (`Temperature-control`, `number`, `required: false`): This section asks you to enter a numerical value for temperature control. Please enter the value that was used to control temperature during your survey.
* **Incubation-duration** (`Incubation-duration`, `number`, `required: false`): This section asks you to enter a numerical value for incubation duration. Please enter the value that was used during your survey.
* **Results-obtained** (`Results-obtained`, `text`, `required: false`): This section asks you to enter any results or observations obtained from the survey. Please provide clear and detailed information about your results.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, `required: false`): This section is for you to add any additional comments or notes about the survey. Please use this section to provide any further information that may be relevant to your survey.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood all sections before proceeding.
- If you are unsure about any section, please seek guidance from a relevant expert or supervisor.
- This form is intended to be completed in a quick and efficient manner, so please be concise and clear in your responses.
