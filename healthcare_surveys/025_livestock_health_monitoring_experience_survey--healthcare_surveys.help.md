# Livestock Health Monitoring Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your experiences with monitoring livestock health. Your feedback will help identify areas for improvement and provide insights for enhancing existing animal health monitoring practices.

## How To Complete This Form
- To get started, read each question carefully and choose the answer that best reflects your experiences.
- Select one or more options for each question, as indicated by the "required" and "options" labels.
- If you have any additional comments or observations, feel free to add them in the text fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Livestock Health Monitoring Survey** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the survey, and you are not required to answer it.
  - This field is for reference only and should be ignored for the purposes of this form.
- **Monitoring Methods** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the given monitoring methods that best describes your current practices.
  - Farmers and veterinarians use different monitoring tools: This option is for users who employ unique methods not listed here.
  - Electronic monitoring systems: This option is for users who utilize electronic systems for monitoring.
  - Monitoring methods vary depending on the size of the operation: This option is for users who use different methods based on their operation size.
  - Monitoring is not done: This option is for users who do not engage in monitoring activities.
- **Monitoring Frequency** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the given frequencies that best describe how often you monitor your animals.
  - 5 days: This option is for users who monitor their animals every 5 days.
  - 3 days: This option is for users who monitor their animals every 3 days.
  - 1 week: This option is for users who monitor their animals weekly.
  - 2 weeks: This option is for users who monitor their animals every 2 weeks.
  - 1 month: This option is for users who monitor their animals monthly.
  - Never: This option is for users who never engage in monitoring activities.
- **Animal Health Programs** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the given programs that you currently use.
  - Animal health monitoring: This option is for users who use programs related to monitoring animal health.
  - Vaccination: This option is for users who use vaccination programs.
  - Animal health insurance: This option is for users who use insurance programs for animal health.
  - Animal health education: This option is for users who use educational programs for animal health.
  - Veterinary care: This option is for users who use veterinary care programs.
  - No programs: This option is for users who do not use any programs.
- **Monitoring Challenges** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any challenges you face when monitoring animal health.
- **How often do you encounter these challenges?** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the given frequencies that best describe how often you encounter the challenges described above.
  - Often: This option is for users who frequently encounter these challenges.
  - Occasionally: This option is for users who occasionally encounter these challenges.
  - Rarely: This option is for users who rarely encounter these challenges.
  - Never: This option is for users who never encounter these challenges.
- **Improvement Opportunities** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Describe areas for improvement you see when monitoring animal health.
- **How often do you identify areas for improvement?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the given frequencies that best describe how often you identify areas for improvement.
  - Often: This option is for users who frequently identify areas for improvement.
  - Occasionally: This option is for users who occasionally identify areas for improvement.
  - Rarely: This option is for users who rarely identify areas for improvement.
  - Never: This option is for users who never identify areas for improvement.
- **Animal Health Program Implementation** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how you think you could better implement animal health programs.
- **Efficacy of Animal Health Programs** (`10`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the effectiveness of animal health programs on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is not effective and 10 is very effective.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you have any additional comments or observations, feel free to add them in the text fields.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey. Your feedback is invaluable!
