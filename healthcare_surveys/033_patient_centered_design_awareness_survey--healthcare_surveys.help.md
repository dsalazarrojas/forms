# Patient Centered Design Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
<Thinking>
The patient-centered design awareness survey is designed to assess your understanding and awareness of patient-centered design principles. This survey is intended to be a self-assessment tool for healthcare professionals to gauge their knowledge on patient-centered design and its applications in the healthcare system. It is also meant to be a tool for healthcare organizations to evaluate their staff's understanding and identify areas for improvement.

Please answer the questions to the best of your ability. Your responses will help us to better understand your current level of awareness and understanding of patient-centered design principles. Your participation is voluntary and all information provided will be treated confidentially.

Please note that the survey is divided into different sections, and each question is designed to help us understand a specific aspect of patient-centered design. Your answers will be used to identify areas of improvement for healthcare professionals and organizations.

</Thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your answer to each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" section.
