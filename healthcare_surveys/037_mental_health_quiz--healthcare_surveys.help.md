# mental_health_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health quiz is a form designed to assess an individual's mental health risks, demographics, and other relevant factors. This quiz is meant to be a self-assessment tool to provide a general overview of potential mental health concerns and recommendations for further evaluation or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the options provided for each field and select the ones that best apply to you.
2. Answer all the questions honestly, as this will help ensure accurate results.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quiz Questions** (`quiz_questions`, text, optional): Answer up to 5 questions that you feel are relevant to your mental health. Please type them in as you would in a normal conversation.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the risk factors that you feel you may be experiencing, such as depression or anxiety. This will help us better understand your current mental state.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, select_one, optional): Select your demographic information, including sex, to help us tailor our recommendations to your specific needs.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, optional): Select your age range to help us understand your development stage and potential risks.
* **Quiz Results** (`quiz_results`, note, optional): This is a text field where you can provide additional information or comments about your results.
* **Recommended Resources** (`recommended_resources`, text, optional): If you would like to receive recommendations or resources, you can enter them here.
* **Mental Health Specialties** (`mental_health_specialties`, select_multiple, optional): Select the mental health specialties that you are seeking help for, such as depression or anxiety.
* **Mental Health Professionals** (`mental_health_professionals`, select_one, optional): Select the type of mental health professional you would like to receive help from, such as a psychologist or therapist.
* **Quiz Comments** (`quiz_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or questions about the quiz or your results, you can enter them here.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_multiple, optional): Select the next steps you would like to take to address your mental health concerns, such as follow-up or further evaluation.
* **Medical Interventions** (`medical_interventions`, select_multiple, optional): Select if you have undergone any medical interventions to address your mental health concerns.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical history related to your mental health concerns, you can enter it here.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, optional): Select your age range to help us understand your development stage and potential risks.
* **Mental Health Specialties** (`mental_health_specialties`, select_multiple, optional): Select the mental health specialties that you are experiencing, such as depression or anxiety.
* **Recommended Resources** (`recommended_resources`, text, optional): If you would like to receive recommendations or resources, you can enter them here.

## Tips
Please note that this quiz is for informational purposes only. If you are experiencing severe mental health concerns, please seek professional help from a licensed mental health professional. The results of this quiz should not be considered a diagnosis or substitute for professional advice.
