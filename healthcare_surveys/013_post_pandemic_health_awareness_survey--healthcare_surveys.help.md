# Post Pandemic Health Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect data on individuals' health awareness and experiences during the pandemic period. It aims to provide insights into the population's health awareness, symptoms, and vaccination status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (general-info)** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your general comments or concerns about the pandemic.
* **Demographics (demographics)** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide your demographic information such as age, occupation, or any other relevant details.
* **Symptoms (symptoms)** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe any symptoms you experienced during the pandemic period.
* **Health Care Coverage (health-care-coverage)** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Share your information about your health care coverage, including insurance or other relevant details.
* **Vaccination Status (vaccination-status)** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Let us know your vaccination status, including any vaccination type or dates.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can modify the form according to your needs, but please do not share the original form without proper attribution.
