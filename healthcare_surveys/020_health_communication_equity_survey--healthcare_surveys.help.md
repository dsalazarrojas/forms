# Health Prophets Equity Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Prophets Equity Survey is designed to gather information about your interactions with healthcare services. Your feedback is crucial in helping us understand how we can better serve our community. Your responses will help us tailor our services to meet your needs and preferences.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have read and understand the purpose of the survey.
2.  Review each section carefully, and fill in the required information.
3.  For select_multiple questions, choose as many options as apply.
4.  For select_one questions, choose only one option.
5.  Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Community Feedback** (`demographic_info`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have about your healthcare interactions. Feel free to share any thoughts or concerns you have.
*   **Age** (`demographic_info_2`, number, required): Please enter your age.
*   **Health Information** (`health_info`, select_one, required): Please indicate if you have any health information that you'd like to share.
*   **Preferred Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose the channels you prefer to get healthcare information from (Phone, Email, Social Media, Print Newspaper, Television, Radio, None).
*   **How often do you get in touch with health information?** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you get in touch with health information (Daily, 2-3 times a week, 1 time a week, 2 times a week, 1 time a month, Less than 1 time a month, Never).
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Please indicate your gender (Male, Female, Other).
*   **race/ethnicity** (`race_ethnicity`, select_multiple, required): Select your race/ethnicity (American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian, Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander, White, Black or African American, Some other race/ethnicity, Prefer not to report).
*   **What is your preferred language?** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, required): Please choose your preferred language for healthcare information (English, Spanish).
