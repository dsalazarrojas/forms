# Healthcare Leadership Inclusivity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the level of inclusivity in healthcare leadership practices, specifically focusing on how supportive leaders are and the presence or absence of barriers to inclusivity. Your feedback will help healthcare professionals understand their experiences and perceptions of leadership behaviors, enabling areas for improvement to be identified and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability, based on your experiences with healthcare leaders.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inclusiveLeadership (Strongly Supportive, Somewhat Supportive, Neither)**: This question asks about the level of support you feel from healthcare leaders. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **leadershipBarriers (None, Some, Many)**: This question is about the barriers you've faced when trying to be inclusive in your work. Select all that apply to describe your experiences.
* **improvementIdea (No Ideas, Some Ideas, Many Ideas)**: This question is about areas where you think healthcare leaders could improve to be more inclusive. Select all that apply to describe your thoughts.
 
## Tips
- Please answer this form thoughtfully and honestly, as your feedback is essential for improving healthcare leadership inclusivity.
- If you're unsure or lack experience with healthcare leaders, it's okay to skip this form.
