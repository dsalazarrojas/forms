# Animal Health Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Health Policy Awareness Survey aims to assess the awareness of animal health policies within a department. This survey is designed to gather information on various aspects of policy awareness, including training, document accessibility, and compliance challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your department name in the "Department Name" field.
2. Select your current role or position from the options provided in the "Current Role or Position" field.
3. Indicate your level of awareness regarding current animal health policies using the "Awareness of Current Animal Health Policies" field.
4. Choose whether you have received policy training in the last 12 months using the "Have you received policy training in the last 12 months?" field.
5. Select how easy or difficult it is for you to access policy documents using the "Ease of Accessing Policy Documents" field.
6. Rate the clarity of quarantine procedures using the "Clarity of Quarantine Procedures" field.
7. If applicable, describe any major compliance challenges you face using the "Major Challenges in Policy Compliance" field (optional).
8. Provide suggestions for improving policy awareness using the "Suggestions for Policy Improvement" field (optional).
9. Select your preferred frequency of policy updates using the "Preferred Frequency of Policy Updates" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department Name**: Enter the name of your department where you work.
* **Current Role or Position**: Select your current role or position within your department.
* **Awareness of Current Animal Health Policies**: Indicate your level of awareness regarding current animal health policies. Choose from:
	+ Very Aware
	+ Somewhat Aware
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Aware
* **Have you received policy training in the last 12 months?**: Choose whether you have received policy training in the last 12 months.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Ease of Accessing Policy Documents**: Rate how easy or difficult it is for you to access policy documents.
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Clarity of Quarantine Procedures**: Rate the clarity of quarantine procedures.
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
* **Major Challenges in Policy Compliance**: If you face any challenges in policy compliance, describe them in this field (optional).
* **Suggestions for Policy Improvement**: Provide suggestions for improving policy awareness (optional).
* **Preferred Frequency of Policy Updates**: Select your preferred frequency of policy updates.
	+ Quarterly
	+ Bi-Annually
	+ Annually
	+ Only as needed
