# Public Health Data Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new collaboration between agencies, research institutions, or non-profit organizations for public health initiatives. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to facilitate smooth data sharing and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your agency type from the dropdown menu. You can choose from Agency, Research Institution, or Nonprofit.
2. Enter the names of your partner organizations (if any) in the Partner Organizations field.
3. Specify your collaboration interests in the Collaboration Interests field.
4. Provide any relevant notes about your collaboration initiatives in the Collaboration Initiatives field.
5. Fill out the contact information, including your first and last name, email, and phone number.
6. Submit the form to create a new registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Type** (`agency_type`, select_one): Select the type of organization you represent (Agency, Research Institution, or Nonprofit).
* **Partner Organizations** (`partner_organizations`, text): Enter the names of any partner organizations involved in your collaboration.
* **Collaboration Interests** (`collaboration_interests`, text): Describe your interests in collaborating on public health data initiatives.
* **Collaboration Initiatives** (`collaboration_initiatives`, text): Provide details about the specific initiatives you plan to collaborate on.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Leave any additional notes about your collaboration.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one): Select the type of organization that submitted this form (Agencies, Research Institutions, or Nonprofit).
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time): Enter the time you submitted the form (e.g., 2023-03-16T14:30).
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date): Enter the date you submitted the form (e.g., 2023-03-16).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one): Select the type of organization that submitted this form (Agencies, Research Institutions, or Nonprofit).
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, note): Leave any additional notes about your organization.
* **Submitter Time** (`submitter_time`, time): Enter the time you submitted the form (e.g., 2023-03-16T14:30).
* **Submitter Date** (`submitter_date`, date): Enter the date you submitted the form (e.g., 2023-03-16).
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, text): Enter your organization's location.
* **Submitter Organisation** (`submitter_organisation`, text): Enter your organization's name.
* **Submitter Organisation Type** (`submitter_organisation_type`, select_one): Select the type of organization you represent (Government Agency, Nonprofit, or Academic/Research Institution).
* **Submitter Organisation Address** (`submitter_organisation_address`, text): Enter your organization's address.
* **Submitter Organisation Phone** (`submitter_organisation_phone`, text): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Submitter Organisation Email** (`submitter_organisation_email`, email): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Submitter Organisation Notes** (`submitter_organisation_notes`, note): Leave any additional notes about your organization.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is for public health collaborations only, so please do not submit for non-related purposes.
