# Virtual Therapy Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Therapy Effectiveness Survey is designed to gather feedback from users after their virtual therapy sessions. Your responses will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

### To start, review each section and answer the questions according to your experience with the virtual therapy sessions. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: This is a free-text field where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about your virtual therapy sessions.
* **How do you feel about your virtual therapy sessions?**: Select all that apply from the list of options.

* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the virtual therapy sessions on a numeric scale.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive any follow-up communications or next session reminders.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to provide additional contact information.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of your last virtual therapy session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of your last virtual therapy session.
* **Therapy Provider**: Select the provider who conducted your virtual therapy session.
* **Therapy Location**: Select the location where your virtual therapy session took place.
* **Therapy Duration**: Enter the duration of your virtual therapy session.
* **Follow-up Sessions**: Select all that apply from the list of options for follow-up sessions.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Next Session**: Enter the date of your next virtual therapy session.
* **Follow-up Next Session**: Enter the time of your next virtual therapy session.
* **Follow-up Last Session**: Enter the time of your last virtual therapy session.
* **Survey Completed**: Confirm the date and time you completed this survey.

**Tips**
* Be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses, as your feedback is valuable for improving the virtual therapy service.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number to receive any follow-up communications or next session reminders.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "Additional Comments" field to share them.
