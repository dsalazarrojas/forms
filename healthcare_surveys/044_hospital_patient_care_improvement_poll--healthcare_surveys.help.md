# Hospital Patient Care Improvement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience in the hospital, specifically in the areas of care and services provided, cleanliness, wait time, comfort of the hospital room, food service, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the response that best fits your experience for each question. Selecting "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" for each question will help the hospital understand your satisfaction with the services provided. If you have any additional comments, you can type them in the "Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Care Improvement
* **What was the overall quality of care received?** (`patient_care_improvement`, select_one, required: false): Choose the rating that best fits your experience, from Very Good to Poor.
* **How would you rate the care provided by your nurse?** (`nurse_care`, select_one, required: false): Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the care provided by your doctor?** (`doctor_care`, select_one, required: false): Choose the rating that best fits your experience, from Very Good to Poor.
* **How would you rate the billing process?** (`billing`, select_one, required: false): Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate your satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness of the hospital room?** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required: false): Choose the rating that best fits your experience, from Very Good to Poor.
* **How would you rate the wait time in the hospital?** (`wait_time`, select_one, required: false): Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate your satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the comfort of your hospital room?** (`patient_room`, select_one, required: false): Choose the rating that best fits your experience, from Very Good to Poor.
* **How would you rate the food service provided?** (`food_service`, select_one, required: false): Select Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate your satisfaction.
* **Overall, what was your experience like at this hospital?** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required: false): Choose the rating that best fits your experience, from Very Good to Poor.
* **Would you recommend this hospital to a friend or family member?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required: false): Select True or False.
* **Do you have any comments about your visit?** (`comments`, note, required: false): Type any additional comments or thoughts you may have.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide ratings that accurately reflect your experience.
* Select the answer that best fits your experience for each question.
* If you have any comments, please type them in the "Comments" field.
* This form is designed to be completed quickly and easily, so take a few minutes to provide your feedback.
