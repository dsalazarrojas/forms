# Oncology Nursing Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oncology Nursing Innovation Survey is a tool designed to gather information about current trends, challenges, and opportunities in oncology nursing innovation. The survey aims to collect data from nurses who are actively engaged in oncology nursing practices, with the ultimate goal of identifying areas for improvement and innovation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to you on the "Survey" page.
2. Choose one option for "New Practices" that best describes your current role or responsibilities.
3. Provide a description of the barriers you are currently facing on the "Barriers" page.
4. Choose all the options that are relevant to your current work on the "Technologies" page.
5. Describe your current innovative approaches or practices on the "Innovations" page.
6. Select all the options that apply to your current support needs on the "Support Needs" page.
7. Describe the barriers you are facing with innovation on the "Innovation Barriers" page.
8. Choose one option for "Research Projects" that best describes your current involvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey** (`select_multiple`): Select all the options that apply to your current role or responsibilities as an oncology nurse. Options include:
  - Select all that apply
  - Other
- **New Practices** (`select_one`): Choose one option that best describes your current role or responsibilities. Options include:
  - Barriers
  - Innovations
  - Technologies
- **Barriers** (`text`): Describe the barriers you are currently facing in your work as an oncology nurse.
- **Technologies** (`select_multiple`): Choose all the options that are relevant to your current work as an oncology nurse. Options include:
  - Artificial Intelligence
  - Data Analytics
  - Robotics
  - Virtual Reality
- **Innovations** (`text`): Describe your current innovative approaches or practices in oncology nursing.
- **Support Needs** (`select_multiple`): Select all the options that apply to your current support needs. Options include:
  - Education
  - Training
  - Consultation
  - Mentorship
  - Resources
- **Innovation Barriers** (`text`): Describe the barriers you are facing with innovation in oncology nursing.
- **Research Projects** (`select_one`): Choose one option that best describes your current involvement. Options include:
  - Hospitals
  - Cancer Centers
  - Research Projects
