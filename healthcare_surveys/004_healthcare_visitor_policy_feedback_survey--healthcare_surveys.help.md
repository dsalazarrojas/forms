# Healthcare Visitor Policy Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the "Healthcare Visitor Policy Feedback Survey". The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback on your experience with the healthcare organization's visitor policy. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve our visitor policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully and make sure you've completed all the required fields.
2. For each field, read the label and explanation provided to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Take your time to complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
4. Once you've completed all the fields, review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction**
*   This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of gathering your feedback.

### 2. **Policy Compliance**
*   **Policy Compliance (Select all that apply)** (`policy_compliance`, `select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to select all the areas where you felt the healthcare organization's policy was followed or not followed. Choose all the applicable options from the list provided.

### 3. **Communication**
*   **Communication (Select One)** (`communication`, `select_one`, required): This field is for rating the effectiveness of communication between the healthcare staff and visitors. Choose the option that best describes your experience.

### 4. **Experience**
*   **Experience (Number)** (`experience`, `number`, required): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the healthcare organization's visitor policy. Please enter a numerical value from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 5. **Staff Feedback**
*   **Staff Feedback (Select One)** (`staff_feedback`, `select_one`, required): This field is for rating the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff. Choose the option that best describes your experience with the staff.

### 6. **Suggestions**
*   **Suggestions (Text)** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional suggestions you may have for improving the visitor policy. Please provide any thoughts or ideas you may have, no matter how small they may seem.

### 7. **Comments**
*   **Comments (Text)** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments you may have about your experience with the healthcare organization. Please provide any thoughts or observations that may be helpful for us to know.

### 8. **Visitor Info**
*   **Visitor Info (Text)** (`visitor_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself, such as your name, email, or contact information. This is completely optional, but any information you provide will help us contact you if needed.
