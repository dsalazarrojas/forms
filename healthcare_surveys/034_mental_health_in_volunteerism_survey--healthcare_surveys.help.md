# Mental Health In Volunteerism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your mental health status and experiences with volunteerism. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand the relationship between volunteerism and mental health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each page of the form carefully, providing as much detail as you can.
2. For select multiple options, you can choose more than one option if it applies.
3. If you have any comments or additional information, please use the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Organization**: Please enter the name of the organization where you volunteered.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you spent volunteering per week.
* **Volunteer Hours per Week**: Enter the total number of hours you spent volunteering each week.
* **Volunteer Activities**: Select the activities you participated in during your volunteer work (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, and Option 4).
* **Volunteer Fulfillment**: Enter the level of fulfillment you felt while volunteering on a scale of 1-5.
* **Mental Health Status**: Select your current mental health status (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Volunteer Benefits**: Select the benefits you experienced from volunteering (e.g., Benefit 1, Benefit 2, Benefit 3).
* **Volunteer Challenges**: Enter any challenges you faced while volunteering.
* **Comments**: Share any additional information or comments about your experience.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Mental Health Influence**: Select how volunteering influenced your mental health (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
* **Volunteer Influence**: Select how volunteering influenced your mental health (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
* **Mental Health In**: Select if volunteering improved your mental health ('Yes', 'No').
* **Volunteer Out**: Select if volunteering affected your mental health ('Yes', 'No').
* **Mental Health Status 1-10**: Enter any mental health status information specific to each status category.

Note: You can choose more than one option for select multiple fields.
