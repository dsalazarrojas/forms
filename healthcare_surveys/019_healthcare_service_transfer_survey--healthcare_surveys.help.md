# Healthcare Service Transfer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Service Transfer Survey is designed to gather essential information about healthcare services transfer from patients to other facilities. This form is meant to help healthcare professionals track and record patient transfers accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information (Page 1: Patient Information)
2. Provide details about the healthcare service transfer (Page 2: Healthcare Service Transfer)
3. Enter transfer details (Page 3: Transfer Details)
4. Input facility information (Page 4: Facility Information)
5. Update the transfer status (Page 5: Transfer Status)
6. Specify the transfer reason (Page 6: Transfer Reason)
7. Choose the transfer type (Page 7: Transfer Type)
8. Enter the transfer facility ID (Page 8: Transfer Facility ID)
9. Add any additional transfer notes (Page 9: Transfer Notes)
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, Optional):
	+ This section is for the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Healthcare Service Transfer** (`healthcare_service_transfer`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Describe the healthcare service being transferred, including the type and nature of care provided.
* **Transfer Details** (`transfer_details`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the transfer process, including the reason for transfer and any relevant medical history.
* **Facility Information** (`facility_info`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Enter the name and address of the facility where the patient will be transferred to or from.
* **Transfer Status** (`transfer_status`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Update the transfer status, such as admitted, discharged, or pending.
* **Transfer Reason** (`transfer_reason`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Specify the reason for the transfer, including medical or administrative reasons.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Choose the type of transfer, such as within-hospital or between institutions.
* **Transfer Facility ID** (`facility_id`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Enter the facility ID or code for the receiving or sending facility.
* **Transfer Notes** (`transfer_notes`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the transfer process.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, Optional):
	+ Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the survey.
