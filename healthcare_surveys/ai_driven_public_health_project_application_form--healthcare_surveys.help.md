# AI Driven Public Health Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about AI-driven public health projects. This form is used to help reviewers evaluate and assess the feasibility and potential impact of each project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project title, organization name, and contact person's full name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the email address and phone number of the contact person.
3. Specify the proposed project start date and estimated project duration in months.
4. Enter the total project budget in USD.
5. Select the primary public health focus area and target population.
6. Choose the geographic scope of the project.
7. Select the type of AI technology to be used and describe its specific application in the project.
8. List the data sources that will feed the AI system.
9. Describe the expected outcomes and how they will be measured.
10. Outline the plan for project sustainability after initial funding.
11. Fill in the submission date and any additional comments or notes for the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): A concise title for your project.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): The name of your organization submitting this proposal.
* **Contact Person Full Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required): The primary contact person for this project.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address for project correspondence.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, phone, required): The phone number for project correspondence.
* **Proposed Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): When do you anticipate the project will begin?
* **Project Duration (Months)** (`project_duration_months`, integer, required): Estimated duration of the project in months.
* **Total Project Budget (USD)** (`project_budget`, decimal, required): Total budget requested for this project.
* **Primary Public Health Focus Area** (`health_focus_area`, select_one, required): Select the primary area of public health your project addresses.
* **Target Population** (`target_population`, text, required): Who will benefit from this project?
* **Geographic Scope** (`geographic_scope`, select_one, required): What geographic area will this project serve?
* **Type of AI Technology** (`ai_application_type`, select_multiple, required): What type of AI technology will be used?
* **Specific AI Use Case** (`ai_use_case`, text, required): Describe how AI will be applied in your project.
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, text, required): What data sources will feed your AI system?
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): What outcomes do you expect from this project?
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, text, required): How will you measure the success of this project?
* **Sustainability Plan** (`sustainability_plan`, text, optional): How will the project continue after initial funding?
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Date of application submission.
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, text, optional): Any additional information for reviewers.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field.
- Use clear and concise language in your responses.
- Ensure that your project meets the eligibility criteria.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
