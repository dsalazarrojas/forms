# mental_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health survey is designed to gather information about an individual's experiences with mental health concerns, helping clinicians identify potential issues and provide a baseline for future assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible. You can select one or multiple options for each question, depending on your experiences. If you have any doubts or concerns, you can seek support from a healthcare professional.
