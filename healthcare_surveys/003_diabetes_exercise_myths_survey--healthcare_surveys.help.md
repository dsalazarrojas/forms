# Diabetes Exercise Myths Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Diabetes Exercise Myths Survey is designed to gather information on common myths or misconceptions related to exercise and diabetes management. This survey will help us understand how people perceive and address these myths, which can inform education and awareness efforts for individuals living with diabetes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the response that best reflects your opinion or experience.
2. Please answer all questions, even if you are unsure or disagree with a particular statement.
3. Be honest and provide your genuine thoughts and opinions.
