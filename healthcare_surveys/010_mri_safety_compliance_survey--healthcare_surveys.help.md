# MRI Safety Compliance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MRI Safety Compliance Survey form is a pre-screening tool to assess a patient's safety risks related to an MRI scan. This form helps healthcare professionals identify potential safety risks and take necessary precautions to ensure the patient's safety during the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information** (Page 1)
	* Please provide your name and date of birth.
2. **Medical History** (Page 2)
	* Select all medical conditions that apply to you.
3. **Medications** (Page 3)
	* Select all medications you are currently taking.
4. **Medical Devices** (Page 4)
	* Select any medical devices you are currently using.
5. **MRI Safety Risks** (Page 5)
	* Enter the number of minutes you are willing to wait for an MRI scan.
6. **Claustrophobia** (Page 6)
	* Please note if you have any fears or concerns about enclosed spaces (claustrophobia).
7. **Scheduling Risks** (Page 7)
	* Select the risk level that best describes your situation.
8. **Safety Acknowledgements** (Page 8)
	* Select all acknowledgements that apply to you.
9. **Safety Risks Acknowledged** (Page 9)
	* Select the acknowledgement that best describes your understanding of the risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (Page 1):
	* This field is for your name and date of birth.
* **Medical History** (Page 2):
	* Select all medical conditions you have or have had in the past, such as diabetes, heart disease, or any other conditions that may affect your safety during an MRI scan.
* **Medications** (Page 3):
	* Choose from the list of medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Devices** (Page 4):
	* Select any medical devices you are currently using, such as pacemakers, implantable cardioverter-defibrillators, or cochlear implants.
* **MRI Safety Risks** (Page 5):
	* Enter the number of minutes you are willing to wait for an MRI scan. Please consider the length of time you can tolerate for the scan.
* **Claustrophobia** (Page 6):
	* This field is for you to note if you have any fears or concerns about enclosed spaces.
* **Scheduling Risks** (Page 7):
	* Select the risk level that best describes your situation. Choose from the options:
		+ 10 minutes
		+ 15 minutes
		+ 30 minutes
* **Safety Acknowledgements** (Page 8):
	* Select all acknowledgements that apply to you, such as:
		+ 1
		+ 2
		+ 3
* **Safety Risks Acknowledged** (Page 9):
	* Select the acknowledgement that best describes your understanding of the risks. Choose from the options:
		+ 0
		+ 1

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure your safety during the MRI scan.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask your healthcare provider or the MRI staff for assistance.
* This form is a pre-screening tool to help identify potential safety risks. It is not a replacement for a thorough medical evaluation by a healthcare professional.
