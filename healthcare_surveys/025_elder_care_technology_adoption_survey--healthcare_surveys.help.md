# Elder Care Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather data from caregivers and care receivers about their experiences with elder care technologies. It aims to help understand how technology affects the quality of care provided and the challenges faced by caregivers and care receivers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a quiet and private space to ensure accurate answers.
2. Respond to each question truthfully, based on your personal experiences.
3. Use the provided options for select and multiple-choice questions, and select all that apply.
4. Leave notes if you have additional comments or information about the technology mentioned.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Identity**: Please provide a short description of your identity as a caregiver, e.g., "I am the primary caregiver for my mother" or "I care for my friend."
* **Caregiver Role**: Select the option that best describes your role as a caregiver, which is either "Primary Caregiver," "Secondary Caregiver," or "Other" (please specify).
* **Care Receiver Identity**: Please provide a short description of the care receiver's identity, e.g., "My elderly aunt."
* **Care Receiver Care Levels**: Select all that apply from the list of options, which describe the types of care the care receiver receives. Please choose one or more from the options: "Basic care," "Skilled care," or "Custodial care."
* **Technology Usage**: Select all that apply from the list of options, which describe the types of technology used in elder care. Please choose one or more from the options: "Smart home," "Telecare," or "Video monitoring."
* **Technology Impact**: Select all that apply from the list of options, which describe the effects of technology on the care process. Please choose one or more from the options: "Improved quality of care," "Cost savings," or "Enhanced caregiver engagement."
* **Technology Challenges**: Select all that apply from the list of options, which describe the difficulties faced with technology in elder care. Please choose one or more from the options: "Cost," "Training," or "Adoption."
* **Care Quality**: Please provide a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the quality of care you or your care receiver experiences. A 1 is poor, and a 5 is excellent.
* **Tech Assistance**: Select all that apply from the list of options, which describe the types of technical assistance provided. Please choose one or more from the options: "In-person," "Remote," or "Both."
* **Survey Response**: Please provide the date and time you completed this survey.
* **Survey Completion**: Please report how long it took you to complete the survey.
* **Tool Used**: Please select the tool you used to complete this survey, which is either "Chatjimmy," "Carequal," or "Other" (please specify).
* **Other Notes**: If you have any additional comments or information about elder care technology, please leave them here.
