# Clinical Presentation Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals monitor the progress of patients and gather essential information about their treatment adherence, side effects, and overall health status after a clinical visit. It's used to identify areas of improvement, assess treatment effectiveness, and facilitate patient care planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as registered in your medical records.
2. Enter your medical record number for identification purposes.
3. Enter the date of your original clinic visit.
4. Enter the current date.
5. In the "Current Symptoms" section, report any symptoms you're currently experiencing.
6. For "Have your primary symptoms improved, worsened, or stayed the same?", select one of the options provided.
7. If you're experiencing any new or persistent symptoms, provide details in the "Describe any new or persistent symptoms" text field.
8. In the "Treatments and Medications" section, answer whether you're taking your prescribed medications as directed and provide an explanation if not.
9. If you're not taking medications, describe the reason for non-adherence.
10. Select the severity of side effects from your medications.
11. Describe any side effects you're experiencing.
12. Confirm whether you've completed all recommended treatments.
13. In the "Lifestyle and Wellness" section, report any lifestyle changes you've made.
14. Rate your current activity level.
15. In the "Follow Up Care" section, indicate whether you need additional appointments or specialist referrals.
16. List any health concerns you have since your last visit.
17. Confirm whether you feel you can contact your clinician if needed.
18. Provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (Full name as registered in medical records)**: Please enter your full name as recorded in your medical files.
* **Patient ID (Medical record number)**: Enter your unique medical record number for identification purposes.
* **Original Clinic Visit Date (Date of the appointment being followed up)**: Enter the date of your original clinic visit for reference.
* **Follow Up Date (Today's date)**: Enter the current date to track the passage of time.
* **Current Symptoms**: Report any symptoms you're currently experiencing, including new or ongoing ones.
* **Have your primary symptoms improved, worsened, or stayed the same?**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the change in your symptoms.
* **Describe any new or persistent symptoms**: Provide details about the symptoms you're experiencing.
* **Treatments and Medications (Are you taking your prescribed medications as directed)**: Answer whether you're taking your medications as instructed, and provide an explanation if not.
* **Medications Taken (If not taking medications, explain)**: If you're not taking your medications, describe the reason for non-adherence.
* **Side Effects (Have you experienced any side effects from your medications)**: Select the severity of side effects you're experiencing.
* **Side Effect Details (Please describe the side effects)**: Provide details about the type, timing, and severity of side effects.
* **Treatment Compliance (Have you completed all recommended treatments)**: Confirm whether you've completed all recommended treatments.
* **Lifestyle Changes (Have you made any lifestyle changes recommended by your clinician)**: Report any lifestyle changes you've made, such as dietary changes or increased physical activity.
* **Activity Level (How would you rate your current activity level)**: Select your current activity level.
* **Follow Up Care (Do you need any additional appointments or specialist referrals)**: Indicate whether you need additional appointments or specialist referrals.
* **Concerns (What health concerns do you have since your last visit)**: List any new or ongoing concerns you have.
* **Clinician Contact (Do you feel you can contact your clinician if needed)**: Confirm whether you feel you can contact your clinician if needed.
* **Additional Notes (Additional comments or notes)**: Provide any other information you'd like to share about your health status.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure accurate assessment of your health status.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask your clinician for guidance.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your symptoms, side effects, and concerns.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact your clinician or healthcare team.
