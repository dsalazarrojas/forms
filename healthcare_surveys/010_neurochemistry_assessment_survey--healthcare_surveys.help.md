# Neurochemistry Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurochemistry Assessment Survey is designed to help healthcare professionals understand your emotional state, behavior, and lifestyle. This information will help them provide personalized recommendations to improve your mental health.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page or section of the form you wish to complete first.
2. For each field, follow the corresponding instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (user_info, text, optional): Provide your contact information, such as name and address. This information will help us to reach out to you with any further questions or follow-up.
* **Emotional State** (emotional_state, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Mood Scale** (mood_scale, number, optional): Rate your current mood from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Neurochemistry Scale** (neurochemistry_scale, number, optional): Rate your current neurochemical state from 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Behavior** (behavior, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
* **Lifestyle** (lifestyle, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Active
	+ Sedentary
	+ Somewhat Active
* **Additional Comments** (notes, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information that you would like to share with us.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the current time.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the current date.
* **User Comments** (user_comments, text, optional): Enter any comments or information you would like to share with us.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information that you would like to share with us.
