# Dietary Fat Intake Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a patient's dietary fat intake goals and sources to help healthcare professionals provide personalized recommendations for fat intake management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2. Answer the questions about your daily fat goals.
3. Provide information about your total daily fat intake.
4. Specify your sources of fat from food categories such as animal sources, dairy products, and other categories.
5. If you have other sources of fat not listed, please specify them in the "Other (specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This is an optional text field to provide any relevant information about your background or medical history that may impact your fat intake goals.
- **What are your daily fat goals**: This field is for you to describe your current fat intake goals or targets. Please provide a brief description of your goals.
- **Total Daily Fat Intake**: This is a numerical field for you to report your total daily fat intake.
- **Food Sources**: Select "Yes" if you consume fat from this food category, "No" otherwise. You can choose multiple options if applicable.
  - **Animal Sources**: Select "Yes" for animal fat from meat sources, "No" otherwise.
  - **Animal Sources from Dairy Products**: Select "Yes" for animal fat from dairy products, "No" otherwise.
  - **Other (specify)**: This field is for you to specify any other sources of fat not listed, such as plant-based fats or supplements.
  - **Other (specify)**: This field is for you to provide more detailed information about the other sources of fat you specified.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when reporting your fat intake goals and sources.
- Please provide as much detail as possible, especially for sources not listed.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
