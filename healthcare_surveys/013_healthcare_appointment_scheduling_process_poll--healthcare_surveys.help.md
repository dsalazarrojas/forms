# Healthcare Appointment Scheduling Process Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare appointment scheduling process poll is designed to collect data on the healthcare appointment scheduling process. This data will be used to identify areas of improvement for the healthcare system.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read the introduction page to understand the purpose of the form.
2.  Select the type of appointment you are referring to (e.g., Appointment, Walk-in, or No-show).
3.  Choose how you would usually schedule appointments (e.g., Online Scheduling, Call Center, Walk-in, Email, or Other (please specify)).
4.  Select your preferred scheduling time (if applicable).
5.  Choose your preferred day of the week for scheduling (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
6.  If applicable, select the average wait time for scheduling (e.g., 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour).
7.  Rate your satisfaction level with the scheduling process (e.g., Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neither Satisfied).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Introduction**: This page provides an overview of the form's purpose and instructions for completion.
*  **appointment\_type**: 
  *   *Appointment*  - This represents a scheduled appointment with a healthcare provider.
  *   *Walk-in*  - This represents a walk-in appointment without a scheduled time.
  *   *No-show*  - This represents a missed appointment without a scheduled time.
*  **Scheduling Method**:
  *   *Online Scheduling*: This is the process of scheduling an appointment online, typically through a digital platform or patient portal.
  *   *Call Center*: This is the process of scheduling an appointment by calling a call center to schedule a time.
  *   *Walk-in*: This is the process of scheduling an appointment without a scheduled time, usually by walking into a healthcare facility.
  *   *Email*: This is the process of scheduling an appointment through email, usually by sending a message to a healthcare provider or facility.
  *   *Other (please specify)*: This option allows users to select any other method not listed above.
*  **Preferred Scheduling Time**: 
  *   *time*: This field allows users to input their preferred time for scheduling appointments.
*  **Preferred Scheduling Day**: 
  *   *Monday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
  *   *Tuesday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
  *   *Wednesday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
  *   *Thursday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
  *   *Friday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
  *   *Saturday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
  *   *Sunday*: This option represents the preferred day to schedule appointments.
*  **Scheduling Wait Time**: 
  *   This field is used to provide feedback on the average wait time for scheduling.
*  **Satisfaction Level**: 
  *   *Very Satisfied*: This option represents a high level of satisfaction with the scheduling process.
  *   *Somewhat Satisfied*: This option represents a moderate level of satisfaction with the scheduling process.
  *   *Neither Satisfied*: This option represents a low level of satisfaction with the scheduling process.
