# Pet Owner Relationship With Vet Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the relationship between pet owners and their veterinarians. It aims to gather data about the frequency of visits to the vet, quality of communication, and overall care received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field in the following order:
   - Page 1: Enter your name as the pet owner.
   - Page 2: Select the type of pet you own (e.g., Pet, Animal, or Companion Animal).
   - Page 3: Describe your relationship with your vet.
   - Page 4: Indicate how often you visit your vet (e.g., Rarely, Regularly, or Occasionally).
   - Page 5: Rate the quality of communication with your vet (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
   - Page 6: Rate the quality of care received from your vet (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
   - Page 7: Indicate how frequently you visit your vet (e.g., Rarely, Regularly, or Frequently).
   - Page 8: Answer whether you would recommend your vet to others (e.g., True or False).
   - Page 9: Provide any additional comments about your experience with your vet.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner Relationship With Vet Survey** (`pet_owner_relationship_with_vet_survey_form_title`, text, required: false): Please enter the title of this survey.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the pet owner who is completing the form.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of pet you own (e.g., Pet, Animal, or Companion Animal).
* **Owner Vet Relationship** (`owner_vet_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe the nature of your relationship with your vet.
* **How Often Visit Vet** (`how_often_visit_vet`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate how often you visit your vet (e.g., Rarely, Regularly, or Occasionally).
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of communication you receive from your vet (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Care Quality** (`care_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of care you receive from your vet (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate how frequently you visit your vet (e.g., Rarely, Regularly, or Frequently).
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required: false): Answer whether you would recommend your vet to others (e.g., True or False).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience with your vet.
