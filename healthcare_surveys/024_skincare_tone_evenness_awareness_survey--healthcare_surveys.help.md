# Skincare Tone Evenness Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skincare Tone Evenness Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about individuals' perceptions and practices related to skincare. It is used to better understand how people perceive their skin tone, their skincare routines, and their attitudes towards skincare products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully, and make sure you understand each question.
2. Select the answers that best reflect your experiences and perceptions.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
