# Mucosal Knowledge Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mucosal Knowledge Assessment Survey is a form designed to collect information about your education and experience in immunology. This includes details about your education level, hours of immunology training, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For questions with options, select the correct answer from the list.
3. Enter your contact information and any additional comments in the text fields.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Educators Hospitals And Research Teams
* **Educators Hospitals And Research Teams** (`education_institutions`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the institutions you have worked with or studied at. Please enter the names of the institutions that have provided you with education or research experience in immunology.

### Mucosal Immunology Concepts
* **Mucosal Immunology Concepts** (`mucosal_concepts`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple concepts related to mucosal immunology that you are familiar with. Please select all the concepts that apply to your education and experience.

### Collect Feedback For Training Or Studies
* **Collect Feedback For Training Or Studies** (`assessment_feedback`, select_one, optional): This field is used to provide feedback on your training or studies experience. Please select "True" if you have provided feedback, and "False" if you have not.

### What Is Your Education Level
* **What Is Your Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): This field is used to collect information about your education level. Please select the level that best matches your education in immunology.

### How Many Hours Of Immunology Training Have You Had?
* **How Many Hours Of Immunology Training Have You Had?** (`experience_hours`, number, optional): This field is used to collect information about your hours of immunology training. Please enter the total number of hours you have had.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your email address so that we can contact you.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your name.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any additional comments you would like to provide. Please enter any relevant comments or feedback.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This field is used to submit your completed form. Please click "Submit" when you have completed all the fields.

### Page 1
* **Page 1** (`page1`, note, required): This field is used to indicate the first page of the form. Please do not fill out this field.

### Page 2
* **Page 2** (`page2`, note, required): This field is used to indicate the second page of the form. Please do not fill out this field.

Note: The form has two pages (1 and 2), but they are not fillable and are used to separate the form into sections.
