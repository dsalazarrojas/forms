# Microbiology Proficiency Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about microbiology proficiency testing in a healthcare setting.
## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form by following these steps:

* Answer all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or more options from the provided list.
* For text fields, provide a concise and accurate description.
* For note fields, add any additional information or comments that might be helpful.
* Review and submit your answers when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participation** (1): Please describe your participation level in microbiology testing. This field is optional.
	+ If you have never participated in microbiology testing, select "None".
	+ If you have participated in microbiology testing but not frequently, select "Rarely".
	+ If you frequently participate in microbiology testing, select "Frequently".
* **Methods** (2): Describe the methods used in your laboratory for microbiology testing. This field is optional.
* **Challenges** (3): Describe any challenges or difficulties you face when performing microbiology testing. This field is optional.
* **Improvement ideas** (4): Share any suggestions or ideas for improving microbiology testing in your laboratory. This field is optional.
* **Laboratory Participation** (5): Indicate the number of times you have participated in laboratory testing. This field is optional.
* **Testing Frequency** (6): Report the frequency of microbiology testing performed in your laboratory. This field is optional.
* **Sample Handling** (7): Choose one or more options that best describe the responsibilities for sample handling and transportation. Options are:
	+ Laboratory personnel are responsible for sample handling and transportation.
	+ Samples should be handled by designated laboratory personnel.
	+ The laboratory is responsible for sample handling and transportation.
* **Sample Handling** (8): Choose one or more options that best describe the responsibilities for sample handling and transportation. Options are:
	+ Laboratory personnel are responsible for sample handling and transportation.
	+ Samples should be handled by designated laboratory personnel.
	+ The laboratory is responsible for sample handling and transportation.
* **Testing Method** (9): Choose one or more options that best describe the testing method used. Options are:
	+ PCR (Polymerase chain reaction)
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ Selective media
	+ Culture (culture method)
