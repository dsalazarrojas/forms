# health_services_capacity_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a hospital or healthcare facility's capacity, services, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the hospital's or facility's name.
2. Specify the department within the hospital or facility.
3. Enter the total number of staff members working at the facility.
4. Enter the total number of beds available at the facility.
5. Enter the number of medical equipment available at the facility.
6. Select the medical staffing level of the facility.
7. Select the services offered by the facility (multiple selection allowed).
8. Enter the dates of operation for the facility.
9. Enter the time of operation for the facility.
10. Provide any additional comments or information about the facility.
11. Enter the facility's email address.
12. Enter the facility's phone number.
13. Enter the facility's fax number.
14. Enter the facility's website.
15. Enter the facility's address.
16. Enter the facility's zip code.
17. Enter the facility's city.
18. Enter the facility's state.
19. Enter the facility's region.
20. Select the services offered by the facility (multiple selection allowed).
21. Select the facility type.
22. Enter the facility's name.
23. Enter the facility's address.
24. Enter the facility's zip code.
25. Enter the facility's city.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the hospital or healthcare facility.
* **Department** (`2`, `text`, required): Select the department within the hospital or facility.
* **Staff Count** (`3`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of staff members working at the facility.
* **Bed Count** (`4`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of beds available at the facility.
* **Equipment Count** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the number of medical equipment available at the facility.
* **Medical Staffing** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Select the medical staffing level of the facility.
* **Services Offered** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services offered by the facility (multiple selection allowed).
* **Dates of Operation** (`8`, `date`, required): Enter the dates of operation for the facility.
* **Time of Operation** (`9`, `time`, required): Enter the time of operation for the facility.
* **Comments** (`10`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or information about the facility.
* **Email** (`11`, `email`, required): Enter the facility's email address.
* **Phone** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Fax** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's fax number.
* **Website** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's website.
* **Address** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's address.
* **Zip Code** (`16`, `number`, required): Enter the facility's zip code.
* **City** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's city.
* **State** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's state.
* **Region** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's region.
* **Services** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services offered by the facility (multiple selection allowed).
* **Facility Type** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Select the facility type.
* **Facility Name** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's name.
* **Facility Address** (`23`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's address.
* **Facility Zip** (`24`, `number`, required): Enter the facility's zip code.
* **Facility City** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's city.

Note: The fields marked as "required" in the form are mandatory and must be filled in to complete the form.
