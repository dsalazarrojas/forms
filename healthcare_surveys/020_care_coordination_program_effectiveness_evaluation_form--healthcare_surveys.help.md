# Care Coordination Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Care Coordination Program Effectiveness Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance of a care coordination program. It helps program evaluators and assessors to collect data and insights on program enrollment, clinical metrics, patient satisfaction, staff collaboration, resource utilization, and program successes, barriers, and future goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to provide an accurate evaluation of the care coordination program.
2. Answer each question based on your evaluation of the program's performance during the specified evaluation period.
3. Use the provided fields to collect data and notes on program metrics, patient satisfaction, and other important aspects of the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Care Coordination Program Name**: Enter the title of the program being evaluated, e.g., "Care Coordination Program for Diabetes Patients".
* **Evaluator/Assessor Name**: Enter your full name, e.g., "John Doe, Program Evaluator".
* **Evaluation Period**: Specify the time frame in which the program was evaluated, e.g., "FY 2024" or "Q1-Q3 2025".
* **Total Patients Enrolled**: Provide the total number of active patients who participated in the program during the evaluation period.
* **Readmission Rate Change (%)**: Calculate the percentage change in readmission rates compared to the baseline, e.g., "10% decrease".
* **Average Patient Satisfaction Score**: Rate patient satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Staff Collaboration Rating**: Rate the level of collaboration among care team members on a scale of 1 to 5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Resource Utilization Efficiency**: Rate the effectiveness of program resources used on a scale of 1 to 5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Identified Program Barriers and Challenges**: Describe any obstacles that hindered program effectiveness.
* **Key Milestone or Success Story**: Share a notable achievement or success story of the program.
* **Future Program Goals and Objectives**: Outline goals and objectives for the next evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Conclusion & Final Notes**: Summarize your evaluation of the program's effectiveness and provide any final notes or recommendations.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when evaluating the program's performance.
* Use examples or data to support your answers when possible.
* Focus on specific examples or instances that illustrate the program's successes and challenges.
* Keep your answers clear and concise for easy reading and understanding.
