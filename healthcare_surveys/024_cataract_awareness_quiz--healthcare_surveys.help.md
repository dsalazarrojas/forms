# Cataract Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cataract Awareness Quiz is a self-assessment tool designed to educate individuals about cataracts, their symptoms, risks, and when to seek medical attention. It's intended to be a helpful resource for the public to increase awareness and understanding of cataracts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name and email address.
2. Select your age group from the list provided.
3. Answer the multiple-choice questions related to cataracts, choosing from the options given.
4. Indicate whether you currently experience any vision changes.
5. Schedule a follow-up eye health consultation if desired.
6. Provide your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted) (optional).
7. Evaluate the helpfulness of the quiz.
8. Enter the clinic or provider code (if you were referred) (optional).
9. Select the date of submission.
10. Confirm understanding of the disclaimer (you're not relying on this quiz for diagnosis or treatment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`full_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive your quiz results.
* **Select your age group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Choose your age group from the list to help us tailor the quiz results to your specific needs.
* **True or False - A cataract is a clouding of the normally clear lens of the eye** (`what_is_cataract`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer to this true or false question.
* **Which of the following are symptoms of cataracts** (`cataract_symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the symptoms that apply from the list to help identify potential cataract symptoms.
* **When is cataract surgery typically recommended** (`surgery_necessity`, select_one, required): Select when cataract surgery is usually indicated.
* **What are common risk factors for developing cataracts** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the risk factors that apply from the list.
* **When was your last comprehensive eye exam** (`last_eye_exam`, select_one, required): Indicate when you last had a comprehensive eye exam.
* **Do you currently experience any vision changes** (`current_vision_issues`, text, optional): If you're experiencing any vision changes, please describe them briefly.
* **Would you like to schedule an eye health consultation** (`consultation_interest`, select_one, required): If desired, select yes to schedule a follow-up consultation.
* **Phone Number (if you want us to call you)** (`follow_up_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Was this quiz helpful in learning about cataracts** (`feedback_quiz`, select_one, optional): Evaluate how helpful you found the quiz.
* **Clinic or Provider Code (if referred)** (`clinic_name`, text, optional): If you were referred, please enter the clinic or provider code.
* **Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the current date for submission purposes.
* **I understand this quiz is for educational purposes and not a diagnosis** (`disclaimer_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of the disclaimer.
