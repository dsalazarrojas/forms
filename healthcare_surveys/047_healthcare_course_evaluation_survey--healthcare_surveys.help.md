# Healthcare Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with a healthcare course. It will help us understand what works well and what could be improved in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. If you need to provide a brief explanation or comment for a particular question, use the "note" field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your name? (participant_info)
* This is a required field where you need to enter your name.
### 2. What is the name of the course you participated in? (course_title)
* This field is also required to help us understand which course you are evaluating.
### 3. How would you rate your satisfaction with the course? (1-5) (course_satisfaction)
* Please rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
### 4. How relevant was the course material? (High, Medium, Low) (course_materials)
* Choose one of the three options to rate the relevance of the course material.
### 5. How would you rate the instructor's performance? (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) (instructor_performance)
* Choose one of the four options to rate the instructor's performance.
### 6. What were your overall experience with the course? (overall_experience)
* This field is optional, but please provide a brief explanation if you'd like to share your thoughts.
### 7. Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement? (feedback)
* This field is also optional, but feel free to share any suggestions or comments you might have.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer every question that applies to you, and don't hesitate to skip questions that don't.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
