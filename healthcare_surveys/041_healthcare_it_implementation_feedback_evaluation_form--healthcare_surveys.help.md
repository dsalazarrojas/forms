# Healthcare IT Implementation Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the healthcare organization's HIT implementation. Please fill out this form to provide your honest opinion on the effectiveness and usability of the implemented technology.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Healthcare IT Implementation Feedback Evaluation Form**
	* Enter the name of this form in the title field.
2. **Category**
	* Select the category that best fits your answer from the dropdown options.
3. **Description** (optional)
	* Provide a brief description of your experience with the HIT implementation, if you wish.
4. **Healthcare IT Implementation**
	* Describe the specific HIT implementation you are providing feedback on.
5. **Healthcare IT Implementation Rating**
	* Rate the overall performance of the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
6. **Overall Satisfaction**
	* Rate your overall satisfaction with the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
7. **Effectiveness of Technology**
	* Rate the effectiveness of the technology in meeting your needs on a scale of 1-5.
8. **Ease of Use**
	* Rate how easy or difficult it was to use the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
9. **Ease of Training**
	* Rate how easy or difficult it was for you to receive training on the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
10. **User Friendly Interface**
	* Rate the user-friendliness of the HIT implementation's interface on a scale of 1-5.
11. **Support**
	* Rate the level of support you received while using the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
12. **Comments** (optional)
	* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the HIT implementation.
13. **Assigned Tool** (optional)
	* If you were assigned a specific tool for the HIT implementation, enter its name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Healthcare IT Implementation**: This field is used to describe the specific HIT implementation being evaluated.
* **Healthcare IT Implementation Rating**: This field is used to rate the overall performance of the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Effectiveness of Technology**: This field is used to rate the effectiveness of the technology in meeting your needs on a scale of 1-5.
* **Ease of Use**: This field is used to rate how easy or difficult it was to use the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Ease of Training**: This field is used to rate how easy or difficult it was for you to receive training on the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
* **User Friendly Interface**: This field is used to rate the user-friendliness of the HIT implementation's interface on a scale of 1-5.
* **Support**: This field is used to rate the level of support you received while using the HIT implementation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments**: This field is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the HIT implementation.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is optional and used to enter the name of the tool assigned to you for the HIT implementation.

## Tips
* Please be as honest as possible with your feedback.
* Rate your answers based on your experience with the HIT implementation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or IT department for guidance.
