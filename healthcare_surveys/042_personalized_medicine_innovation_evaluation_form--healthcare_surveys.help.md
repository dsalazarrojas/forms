# Personalized Medicine Innovation Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate innovations related to personalized medicine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the introduction for background information about the innovation.
2. Fill out the innovation name in the **Innovation Name** field.
3. Select the team members who are in charge of the project in the **Project Team Leads** field. You can select multiple team members by separating their names with commas.
4. Score the innovation's **Innovation Score**, **Impact Score**, **Feasibility Score**, and **Evaluation Score** fields based on your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Innovation Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the innovation you are evaluating.
* **Project Team Leads** (select multiple, optional): Identify the project team leads responsible for the innovation. You can select multiple team members by separating their names with commas.
* **Innovation Score** (number, optional): Score the innovation based on its innovative qualities, such as novel approach, scientific evidence, and potential impact.
	+ A higher score indicates a more innovative approach.
* **Impact Score** (number, optional): Score the innovation based on its potential impact on patients, healthcare providers, or healthcare system.
	+ A higher score indicates a greater potential impact.
* **Feasibility Score** (number, optional): Score the innovation based on its feasibility and practicality.
	+ A higher score indicates a more feasible approach.
* **Evaluation Score** (number, optional): Score the innovation based on its overall evaluation.
	+ A higher score indicates a better innovation.
