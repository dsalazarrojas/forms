# Health Care Practical Work Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather feedback on the practical work experience of students in healthcare settings. It helps assess the effectiveness of placements and provides valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus as required.
2. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
3. Fill in any contact information of the program director, site director, and placement coordinator as required.
4. Provide a date and time for the site visit.
5. Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any errors.

* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is where students can provide any feedback they may have on their practical work experience.
* **Placement Experience** (`placement_experience`, `select_one`, optional): This field is where students indicate whether they had a placement experience (`Yes`), didn't have one (`No`), or are unsure (`Unknown`).
* **Practical Work Hours** (`practical_work_hours`, `number`, optional): This field is where students can indicate the number of hours they spent on practical work.
* **Clinical Site** (`clinical_site`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is where students can choose the clinical site(s) they visited.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): This field is where students can rate their experience (though the exact scale is not specified, please enter a number).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This field is where students can provide additional comments on their experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This field is where students can enter their email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This field is where students can enter their phone number.

* **Site Visit** (`site_visit`, `date`, optional): This field is where students can enter the date of their site visit.
