**rural_healthcare_access_challenges_survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the challenges faced by rural healthcare workers in accessing necessary services. The survey aims to collect data on the availability and effectiveness of various healthcare services in rural areas, which will help identify areas for improvement and inform policy decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them accordingly.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience or situation.
3. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
