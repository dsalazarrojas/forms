# Healthcare Concepts Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients share their thoughts and feelings about their healthcare concepts and experiences. It will help us identify areas where we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the healthcare concepts listed on the form.
2. If you select "No" for any of the healthcare concepts, please provide feedback in the text field "Feedback" to help us understand why you chose "No".
3. Rate your experience with the provided rating questions.
4. Finally, submit the form to help us understand your healthcare concepts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Providers
* **Patient Providers** (`patient_providers`, select_multiple, required): This field is a list of healthcare providers you work with. Please select all the providers that apply to you.
* **Patient Providers** (`patient_providers_hint`, note, required): This field is a note field where you can provide more information about your relationship with your healthcare providers.
* **Select a Provider** (`patient_providers_0`, select_one, required): Select a healthcare provider that you work with.
* **Select a Provider** (`patient_providers_0_hint`, note, required): Please provide more information about this healthcare provider.
