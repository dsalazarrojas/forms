# Healthcare Outpatient Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Healthcare Outpatient Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from patients about their healthcare experiences, providing valuable insights for healthcare providers to improve patient care and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please complete this form honestly, and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
*   Answer each question carefully, and if you are unsure about any field, please consult the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended to collect your contact information, which will help us get in touch with you for further communication.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your contact details so we can follow up with any questions or concerns.
*   **Healthcare Visit** (`healthcare_visit`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your recent healthcare visit, including the date and any notable events.
*   **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of visit you had (e.g., routine check-up, emergency visit, etc.).
*   **Visit Date** (`visit_during`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your visit.
*   **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of your visit.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required/optional): If you saw a doctor during your visit, please provide their name.
*   **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, `text`, required/optional): Identify the staff member(s) you interacted with during your visit.
*   **Nurse Name** (`nurse_name`, `text`, required/optional): If a nurse was involved in your care, please provide their name.
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a medical record number, please provide it.
*   **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how you felt about your visit outcome.
*   **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Share your general experience during the visit.
*   **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate which aspects of our care you would recommend to others.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

*   Be honest and open in your feedback, and try to provide specific examples or details to help us understand your experience better.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
