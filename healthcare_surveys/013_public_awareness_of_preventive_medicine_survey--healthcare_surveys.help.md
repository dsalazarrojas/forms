# public_awareness_of_preventive_medicine_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The public_awareness_of_preventive_medicine_survey is designed to gather information on public awareness and perception of preventive medicine practices. This survey aims to collect demographic data, medical history, and awareness of preventive medicine practices from respondents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the required fields first, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Fill out the remaining fields at your own pace, ensuring that you have provided accurate information for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **first_name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify you for this survey.
*   **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide your email address to enable follow-up contact.
*   **phone_number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number (this field is actually not required, it seems we can safely remove it)
*   **age** (`age`, `number`, required): Select your age in years.
*   **sex** (`sex`, `select_one`, required): Choose your sex: Female, Male, or Other.
*   **medical_condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please specify.
*   **preventive_medicine** (`preventive_medicine`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the areas where you practice preventive medicine: Regular check-ups, Healthy eating, Exercise, Stress management.
*   **awareness** (`awareness`, `number`, optional): Rate your awareness level of 0-5, where 0 means not very aware and 5 means extremely aware.
*   **submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Fill out the remaining fields first before proceeding to submit.
*   **notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or information not captured by other questions.
*   **medical_history** (`medical_history`, `date`, optional): If you have any medical history, please specify the date it started.
*   **medication** (`medication`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if your medication is Prescription, Non-prescription, or No medication.
*   **religious_affiliation** (`religious_affiliation`, `select_one`, optional): Select your religious affiliation: Protestant, Catholic, Muslim, Other.
*   **education_level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your educational level: Some, High school, Bachelor degree, Master degree, PhD.
*   **income** (`income`, `number`, optional): Enter your annual income.
*   **marital_status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your marital status: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed.
*   **ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_one`, optional): Select your ethnicity: Asian, Caucasian, Black, Hispanic.

Note: The following optional fields can be filled in if you wish to provide more information: medical\_condition, preventive\_medicine, awareness, medical\_history, medication, religious\_affiliation, education\_level, income, marital\_status, ethnicity.
