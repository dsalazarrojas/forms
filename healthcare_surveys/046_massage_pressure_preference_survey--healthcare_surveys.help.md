# Massage Pressure Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's preference for massage pressure, which can help healthcare professionals tailor their massage therapy sessions to the individual's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the preferred massage pressure from the options provided in the first page.
2. Choose one or more areas where you would like to receive massage pressure from the options listed in the second page.
3. If you have any additional comments or preferences, please type them in the third page.
4. Enter your preferred massage pressure rating (on a scale of 1-10) in the fourth and fifth pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Massage Pressure** (`page_1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how strong or gentle you would like your massage to be. Options include Light, Medium, Firm, Very Firm, and Very Light.
* **Pressure Preference** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate which areas you would like to receive massage pressure, such as Chest, Back, Arms and Legs, Hands and Feet, or Other.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_3`, `note`, optional): If you have any further comments or preferences, you can type them here.
* **Massage Pressure Preference Survey** (`page_4`, `number`, optional): Enter a rating from 1-10 to indicate your preferred massage pressure.
* **Massage Pressure Preference Survey** (`page_5`, `number`, optional): Enter another rating from 1-10 to confirm or update your preferred massage pressure.

## Tips
- Be honest about your preferences to help healthcare professionals provide the best possible massage experience.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, you can always ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
- Take a moment to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your preferences.
