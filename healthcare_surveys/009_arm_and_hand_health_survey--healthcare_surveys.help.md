# Arm And Hand Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arm and Hand Health Survey is a form used to collect information on the health status of patients with arm or hand issues. This form is designed to gather data on symptoms, medical history, and functional impact of these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. If available, enter the patient's ID or Medical Record Number in the "Patient ID or Medical Record Number" field.
3. Select the date of the survey from the calendar in the "Survey Date" field.
4. Enter the patient's age in the "Age" field.
5. Select the dominant hand (right, left, or ambidextrous) from the "Dominant Hand" dropdown list.
6. In the "Symptom Assessment" section, describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
7. Answer the questions regarding arm or hand pain, its location, duration, and severity.
8. Indicate the level of weakness or loss of strength.
9. Select the presence or absence of numbness or tingling sensations.
10. Answer the questions regarding swelling in the arm or hand.
11. Assess the level of stiffness or reduced range of motion.
12. In the "Functional Impact" section, describe any impact on daily activities and work or occupational tasks.
13. Answer the questions regarding sleep disturbances due to arm or hand issues.
14. In the "Medical History" section, describe previous injuries to the arm or hand.
15. In the "Medical History" section, describe any relevant medical conditions.
16. In the "Medical History" section, list any medications currently affecting the arm or hand.
17. In the "Medical History" section, describe any previous treatment received.
18. In the "Medical History" section, indicate if the patient has any previous injury.
19. In the "Medical History" section, list any medical conditions relevant to the patient's arm or hand.
20. In the "Medical History" section, list any medications currently affecting the patient's arm or hand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears in their medical records.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: If available, enter the patient's ID or Medical Record Number for reference.
* **Survey Date**: Select the date on which the survey was completed.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Dominant Hand**: Select the patient's dominant hand (right, left, or ambidextrous).
* **Symptom Assessment**: Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing in this section.
* **Do you experience arm or hand pain**: Select the level of pain experienced (none, mild, moderate, severe, or very severe).
* **Primary location of pain**: Select the location of the pain (upper arm, elbow, forearm, wrist, hand, or fingers).
* **How long have you had these symptoms**: Select the duration of symptoms (less than 1 week, 1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, 1-3 months, or more than 3 months).
* **Do you experience weakness or loss of strength**: Select the level of weakness (none, mild, moderate, or severe).
* **Numbness or tingling sensations**: Select the presence or absence of numbness or tingling sensations (none, occasional, frequent, or constant).
* **Swelling in arm or hand**: Select the presence or absence of swelling (none, mild, moderate, or severe).
* **Stiffness or reduced range of motion**: Select the level of stiffness (none, mild, moderate, or severe).
* **Impact on daily activities**: Select the impact on daily activities (no impact, minor impact, moderate impact, significant impact, or unable to perform activities).
* **Impact on work or occupational tasks**: Select the impact on work or occupational tasks (no impact, minor impact, moderate impact, significant impact, or unable to work).
* **Sleep disturbance due to arm or hand issues**: Select the presence or absence of sleep disturbance (none, occasional, frequent, or severe).
* **Previous arm or hand injury**: Select the presence or absence of previous injury (true, false, or not sure).
* **Relevant medical conditions**: Describe any relevant medical conditions affecting the arm or hand.
* **Current medications affecting arm or hand**: List any medications currently affecting the arm or hand.
* **Previous treatment received**: Describe any previous treatment received for arm or hand issues.
* **Previous injury to arm or hand**: Indicate if the patient has had any previous injury to the arm or hand.
* **Medical conditions relevant to arm or hand**: Describe any medical conditions relevant to the arm or hand.
* **Medications currently affecting arm or hand**: List any medications currently affecting the arm or hand.
