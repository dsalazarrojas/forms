# Healthcare Ethics Decision Making Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather healthcare professionals' insights on making decisions that involve challenging situations, patient-reported outcomes, handling conflicts, and technology use in patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of multiple pages with multiple fields. To complete it, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question on each page by selecting the relevant option that best reflects your attitude or behavior.
2. For text fields, enter a brief explanation of your thoughts or experiences.
3. Review each page to ensure you have completed all the required fields before proceeding.
