# Medical Wearable Technology Pilot Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation of the Medical Wearable Technology Pilot Program. The purpose of this form is to understand the usability of the device, benefits experienced, challenges faced, and overall participant satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your experience with the Medical Wearable Technology Pilot Program.
2. Be as detailed as possible when describing your experiences and challenges.
3. Select the options that best match your experiences for the multiple-choice questions.
4. Do not hesitate to provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Participant Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, job title, and department or institution where you work.
* **How would you rate the usability of the device** (`device_usability`, text, required): This question asks you to rate the usability of the device on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very difficult" and 5 is "very easy".
* **What benefits did you experience from using the device** (`benefits`, text, optional): This question is designed to understand the benefits you experienced from using the device. Please provide specific benefits you found useful.
* **What challenges did you experience while using the device** (`challenges`, text, optional): This question is designed to understand any challenges you faced while using the device.
* **What benefits did you experience from using the device (select all that apply)** (`device_benefits`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to select all the benefits you experienced from using the device, such as "Benefits of device" or "Other benefits not mentioned".
* **How would you rate the usability of the device (Likert scale)** (`device_usability_likert_scale`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate the usability of the device on a Likert scale, where 1 is "very difficult" and 5 is "very easy".
* **On a scale from 1 to 5, how would you rate the scheduling of the pilot program** (`pilot_scheduling`, number, optional): This question is designed to understand your experience with the scheduling of the pilot program.
* **Any additional feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the Medical Wearable Technology Pilot Program.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): This question asks you to provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, job title, and department or institution where you work.
