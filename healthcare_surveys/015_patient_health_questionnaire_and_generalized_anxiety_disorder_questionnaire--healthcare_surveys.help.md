# Patient Health Questionnaire And Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Health Questionnaire and Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about a patient's health status, including their level of anxiety, medical conditions, and coping mechanisms. This form is intended for use by healthcare providers to assess the patient's overall health and identify potential areas of concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For fields with dropdown options, select the most appropriate answer based on the patient's condition.
4. If a question does not apply to the patient, please indicate "Not Applicable" or "N/A" in the designated field.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Health** (`patient_health`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect general information about the patient's health status. Please provide a brief description of the patient's overall health.
### 2. **Generalized Anxiety Disorder** (`generalized_anxiety_disorder`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect information about the patient's generalized anxiety disorder. Please provide any relevant details about the patient's condition.
### 3. **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, `select_one`, Optional)
	* This field has three dropdown options: **Mild**, **Moderate**, **Severe**. Please select the most accurate level of anxiety the patient is experiencing.
### 4. **Patient Health Rating** (`patient_health_rating`, `number`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect a numerical rating of the patient's health status. Please rate the patient's health from 1 to 10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best.
### 5. **Anxiety Rating** (`anxiety_rating`, `number`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect a numerical rating of the patient's anxiety level. Please rate the patient's anxiety from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
### 6. **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect the name of the healthcare provider completing the form. Please enter your name and any relevant credentials or certifications.
### 7. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect any medical conditions or illnesses the patient has been diagnosed with. Please list any relevant conditions.
### 8. **Patient History** (`patient_history`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect information about the patient's medical history. Please provide any relevant details, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.
### 9. **Anxiety Symptoms** (`anxiety_symptoms`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect any symptoms the patient is experiencing related to anxiety. Please list any relevant symptoms.
### 10. **Coping Mechanisms** (`coping_mechansims`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect information about any coping mechanisms the patient is using to manage their anxiety. Please provide any relevant details.
### 11. **Patient Preferences** (`patient_preferences`, `text`, Optional)
	* This field is used to collect any personal preferences the patient has for their care or treatment. Please list any relevant details.

Note: The fields marked "Optional" are not required to be completed, but please only mark them as such if they do not apply to the patient.
