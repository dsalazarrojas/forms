# smoking_management_course_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from learners who have attended the smoking management course. This feedback will help us improve the course and make it more effective for future learners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Introduction" page and briefly describe your experience with the course.
2. On the "Course Rating" page, rate the course by giving it a numerical score out of 5.
3. On the "Instructor Rating" page, rate the instructor by giving it a numerical score out of 5.
4. On the "Course Comments" page, write any additional comments about the course.
5. On the "Comments" page, write any general comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, Optional): A brief description of your experience with the course.
* **Course Rating** (`2`, `number`, Optional): Rate the course by giving it a numerical score out of 5.
* **Instructor Rating** (`3`, `number`, Optional): Rate the instructor by giving it a numerical score out of 5.
* **Course Comments** (`4`, `text`, Optional): Write any additional comments about the course.
* **Comments** (`5`, `text`, Optional): Write any general comments or suggestions about the course.
