# Vivitrol Enrollment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about patients who are potential candidates for the Vivitrol program. The collected data is used to assess patient readiness and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and make sure all required fields are filled out.
2. Fill in the patient's details in the "Patient Details" page.
3. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" page.
4. Check "Yes" or "No" for "Follow-up Outreach" based on the patient's situation.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
6. Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
7. Provide any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Select the user assigned to the patient in the "Assigned User" field.
9. Choose the date of assignment from the calendar in the "Assigned Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details (page 1, text)**: Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History (page 2, text)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or conditions.
* **Consent (page 3, note)**: Provide a brief explanation of the patient's consent for the program.
* **Follow-up Outreach (page 4, select_one)**: Check "Yes" if the patient requires follow-up outreach and "No" otherwise.
* **Patient Phone (page 5, text)**: Enter the patient's phone number for communication.
* **Patient Email (page 6, email)**: Enter the patient's email address for communication.
* **Treatment Eligibility (page 7, text)**: Indicate if the patient is eligible for treatment.
* **Notes (page 8, note)**: Provide any additional notes about the patient's situation.
* **Assigned User (page 9, text)**: Select the healthcare professional assigned to the patient.
* **Assigned Date (page 10, date)**: Choose the date of assignment for the patient.
