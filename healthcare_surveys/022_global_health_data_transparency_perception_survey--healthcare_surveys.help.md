# global_health_data_transparency_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on the perception of healthcare data transparency among participants in the healthcare industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions truthfully, as they are designed to help us understand your perspective on healthcare data transparency.
2. For select multiple options, choose all the options that apply.
3. For select one or select multiple options, choose the options that best align with your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Participants (Text)**: Describe yourself as a participant in the healthcare industry.
* **Survey Questions (Text)**: Enter any additional questions you have regarding the survey topic.
* **Trust Level (Select Multiple)**: Rate your level of trust in sharing healthcare data, from Very Low to Very High.
* **Transparency Concerns (Select One)**: Choose the concern that best aligns with your thoughts, from Concern 1 to Concern 3.
* **Data Sharing (Select Multiple)**: Indicate how willing you are to share your healthcare data, from Not willing to share to Very willing to share.
* **Data Sharing Reasons (Text)**: Enter any reasons why you are willing or not willing to share your healthcare data.
* **Data Sharing Fear (Select Multiple)**: Choose the fear that best aligns with your thoughts, from Fear 1 to Fear 3.
* **Public Sharing Data (Select Multiple)**: Indicate how willing you are to share your healthcare data publicly, from Not willing to share to Very willing to share.
* **Public Sharing Reasons (Text)**: Enter any reasons why you are willing or not willing to share your healthcare data publicly.
* **Public Sharing Fear (Text)**: Enter any fears you have regarding sharing healthcare data publicly.
* **Public Sharing Concerns (Select Multiple)**: Choose the concerns that best align with your thoughts, from Concern 1 to Concern 3.
* **Research Participants (Select Multiple)**: Indicate what type of participant you are, from Researchers to NGOs.
* **Research Participants Other (Text)**: Enter any other type of participant you are.
* **Survey Participant Type (Select Multiple)**: Indicate what type of participant you are, from Researchers to Other.
* **Survey Participant Other (Text)**: Enter any other type of participant you are.
* **Survey Participant Employed (Select Multiple)**: Indicate your employment status, from Employed to Unemployed.
* **Survey Participant Employed Other (Text)**: Enter any other employment status you are.
* **Survey Participant Education (Text)**: Enter your educational background.
* **Survey Participant Education Other (Text)**: Enter any other educational background you have.
* **Survey Participant Income (Select Multiple)**: Indicate your income range, from Less than $20,000 to $100,000 or more.
* **Survey Participant Income Other (Text)**: Enter any other income range you have.
* **Data Sharing Frequency (Select Multiple)**: Indicate how often you share your healthcare data, from Never to Very Often.
* **Data Sharing Frequency Other (Text)**: Enter any other frequency of data sharing you have.
* **Data Sharing Concerns Other (Text)**: Enter any other concerns you have regarding sharing healthcare data.
* **Survey Participant (Text)**: Enter any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Research Participants (Select Multiple)**: Indicate what type of participant you are, from Researchers to NGOs.
* **Survey Participant Other (Text)**: Enter any other type of participant you are.
* **Data Sharing (Select Multiple)**: Indicate how willing you are to share your healthcare data, from Not willing to share to Very willing to share.
* **Public Sharing Data (Select Multiple)**: Indicate how willing you are to share your healthcare data publicly, from Not willing to share to Very willing to share.
* **Data Sharing Reasons (Text)**: Enter any reasons why you are willing or not willing to share your healthcare data.
* **Public Sharing Reasons (Text)**: Enter any reasons why you are willing or not willing to share your healthcare data publicly.
