# Physiological Adaptations To Exercise Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Physiological Adaptations To Exercise Survey is a tool used to gather information about your exercise habits and perceived physiological changes that may occur as a result of regular physical activity. The survey aims to understand how your body adapts to exercise and what you have observed in your body over time. This data will be used to improve the accuracy of our exercise recommendation models.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select all that apply for select multiple questions.
3. Provide as much detail as you can in the text fields.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
