# Digital Inclusion In Care Delivery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Digital Inclusion In Care Delivery Survey is a tool used to gather insights on the use and access of digital tools in healthcare settings. It collects data on various aspects of digital inclusion, including clinician and patient comfort, barriers, skills, and access to technology. This information is essential for healthcare organizations to improve patient care and clinician support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions as they apply to your situation.
2. Ensure that you are answering based on your own experiences and perceptions.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please contact the support team for clarification.
4. Fill out the form completely, and submit your feedback.
