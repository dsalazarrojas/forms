# Healthcare Team Collaboration Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Healthcare Team Collaboration Workshop Evaluation form. The form is designed to help evaluate the quality and effectiveness of healthcare team collaboration workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question honestly and thoroughly. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Quality**: Evaluate the overall quality of the workshop (1-10 scale).
- **Collaboration Effectiveness**: Assess the effectiveness of collaboration during the workshop (1-10 scale).
- **Improvement Ideas**: Provide specific and practical ideas to improve the workshop, if applicable.
- **Team Collaboration**: Select how you perceive the team collaboration during the workshop (Effective, Ineffective, Somewhat Effective, Neutral, Somewhat Ineffective).
- **Workshop Participants**: Select which participants were effective, ineffective, or neutral during the workshop (Effective, Ineffective, Somewhat Effective, Neutral, Somewhat Ineffective).
- **Improvement Ideas Description**: If you have improvement ideas, provide a detailed description of them.
- **Will Implement**: Indicate whether you intend to implement any of the improvement ideas (True or False).
- **Feedback Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and thoroughly to provide meaningful feedback.
- Be specific and concrete when providing improvement ideas.
- If you are unsure about any question, please use your best judgment.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
