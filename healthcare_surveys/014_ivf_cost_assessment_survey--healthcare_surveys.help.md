# IVF Cost Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IVF Cost Assessment Survey is a form used to gather information about a patient's demographics, insurance coverage, and treatment interests for In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) and other fertility treatments. This form helps healthcare providers understand the patient's needs and provide accurate quotes for treatment costs. By completing this form, you will be able to assess the costs associated with IVF and other fertility treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Answer the questions about your demographics and insurance coverage.
3. Indicate which type of treatment you are interested in, including IVF, IUI, ICSI, and other options.
4. Provide details about your insurance coverage, including the number of IVF cycles covered and the type of coverage.
5. Answer questions about the treatment options you are interested in for each cycle of IVF.
6. Provide financial information, including payment method and amount.
7. Add any additional patient, physician, and clinic notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on your records.
* **Patient Email** (`Patient Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Patient Phone** (`Patient Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates.
* **Patient Address** (`Patient Address`, text, optional): Enter your address for billing and shipping purposes.
* **Demographics Information** (`Demographics Information`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your demographics, including age, occupation, and other relevant information.
* **Insurance Information** (`Insurance Information`, text, optional): Please provide details about your insurance coverage, including policy number and provider name.
* **Insurance Type** (`Insurance Type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of insurance coverage you have, including Private, Medicare, Medicaid, and Tricare.
* **Treatment Interests** (`Treatment Interests`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which type of treatment you are interested in, including IVF, IUI, ICSI, and other options.
* **What type of treatment are you interested in?** (`What type of treatment are you interested in?`, select_multiple, optional): Repeat the previous question, with options IVF, IUI, ICSI, and Other.
* **How many IVF cycles are covered by your insurance coverage?** (`How many IVF cycles are covered by your insurance coverage?`, select_one, optional): Choose the number of IVF cycles covered by your insurance, including Full Coverage, Partial Coverage, and No Coverage.
* **Which treatment options are you interested in for each cycle of IVF?** (`Which treatment options are you interested in for each cycle of IVF?`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which treatment options you are interested in for each cycle of IVF, including Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET), Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI), Donor Egg, and Surrogacy.
* **Financial Information** (`Financial Information`, text, optional): Please provide details about your financial information, including payment method and amount.
* **Total Cycles** (`Total Cycles`, number, optional): Enter the total number of cycles you are interested in.
* **Cycle Cost** (`Cycle Cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost for each cycle of IVF.
* **Insurance Company** (`Insurance Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your insurance company.
* **Insurance Plan** (`Insurance Plan`, text, optional): Enter the name of your insurance plan.
* **Billing Cycle** (`Billing Cycle`, date, optional): Enter the billing cycle date.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, text, optional): Enter the payment method, such as credit card or check.
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Patient Notes** (`Patient Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the patient.
* **Physician Notes** (`Physician Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the physician.
* **Clinic Notes** (`Clinic Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from the clinic.
* **Total Cycles Cost** (`Total Cycles Cost`, number, optional): Calculate the total cost for all cycles.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Be honest and transparent about your financial information and treatment interests.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
