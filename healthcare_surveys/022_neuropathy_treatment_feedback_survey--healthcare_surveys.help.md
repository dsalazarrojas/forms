# neuropathy_treatment_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback about the treatment and care received by patients with neuropathy. The goal is to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your honest and detailed feedback.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a star *).
3. Provide clear and concise answers to the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1):
	+ A brief welcome message and instructions for the survey.
* **patient_id** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the unique ID number of the patient.
* **treatment_id** (Page 3):
	+ Enter the ID number of the treatment received by the patient.
* **How_satisfied** (Page 4):
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the treatment, using a numerical value from 1 (not satisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **questions** (Page 5-26):
	+ Answer each question with a brief description of your experience and thoughts.
	+ Questions are designed to gather more specific information about your treatment and care.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, don't hesitate to share them.
- Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve our services.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please refer to the form's instructions or contact us directly.
