# Virtual Reality Therapy Research Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from patients or their guardians for research purposes related to virtual reality therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. The form is divided into several sections, and each section is marked with a label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gender** (ID: 16, Required): Please select the patient's gender from the options provided.
* **Age** (ID: 17, Required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Contact Preference** (ID: 18, Required): Please select all the contact methods you would like to be contacted through (e.g., Phone, Email, Text, Social Media).
* **Email** (ID: 19, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (ID: 20, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Address** (ID: 21, Required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Condition** (ID: 22, Required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition.
* **How long have you had this medical condition?** (ID: 23, Required): Enter the length of time the patient has had their medical condition.
* **Treatments/Therapies** (ID: 24, Required): Enter any treatments or therapies the patient has had for their medical condition.
* **Medical Insurance** (ID: 25, Required): Enter information about the patient's medical insurance, including any relevant policy numbers or providers.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask a healthcare professional for clarification.
* If you need help with completing the form, please contact the research team for assistance.
