# Healthcare Conference Attendee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a healthcare conference. It helps us understand your experience at the conference and provide an opportunity for you to rate the conference on a scale from 1 to 5.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
- Select the options that best fit your experience and preferences.
- Be as detailed as possible in your feedback, especially in the "Comments" field.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, you can contact us at the email address provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Conference Name**: The name of the conference you attended.
	* This field should be filled with the actual name of the conference you attended.
	* For example, "Annual Healthcare Conference 2022".
- **Attended Conference**: Which conference did you attend?
	* Choose the option that accurately reflects your experience.
	* For example, Option 1: "I attended a keynote speech."
	* For example, Option 2: "I attended a breakout session."
- **Attended Session**: Which sessions did you attend?
	* Select all the sessions that you attended.
	* For example, Option 9: "I attended a session on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare."
	* For example, Option 10: "I attended a session on Telemedicine."
- **Feedback**: Share your thoughts about the conference.
	* This is your opportunity to provide any comments or suggestions about the conference.
	* Be as detailed as possible.
	* For example, "The conference was well-organized and the speakers were knowledgeable."
- **Rating (1-5)**: Rate your overall experience at the conference.
	* Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
	* For example, 5: "I had an excellent experience."
- **Rating Scale**: What does the rating mean to you?
	* This field helps us understand your interpretation of the rating scale.
	* For example, "I base my rating on the quality of the content and speakers."
- **Comments**: Any additional comments or suggestions.
	* This is your opportunity to provide any additional thoughts or ideas about the conference.
	* Be as detailed as possible.
- **Contact Information**: Your contact information for future correspondence.
	* This field is for your email address and phone number.
	* Ensure it is accurate to help us contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
- **Email**: Your email address for us to contact you.
	* This field should be filled with your active email address.
	* For example, "johndoe@example.com".
- **Phone**: Your phone number for us to contact you.
	* This field should be filled with your active phone number.
	* For example, "(123) 456-7890".
- **Name**: Your name.
	* This field should be filled with your actual name.
	* For example, "John Doe".
- **Company**: The name of your company.
	* This field should be filled with the name of your company.
	* For example, "ABC Healthcare Inc.".

## Tips
- Please fill in all the required fields.
- Ensure your email address and phone number are accurate.
- Be as detailed as possible in your feedback.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at the email address provided.
