# Antibody And Antigen Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Antibody And Antigen Survey is a tool to gather information about the respondent's health status, including their age group, previous diagnosis, vaccination status, symptoms, exposure history, and other relevant health-related details. This form is intended to collect data for research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "respondent_name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "contact_email" field for follow-up communication.
3.  Select your age group from the options in the "age_group" field.
4.  Answer whether you have been diagnosed with any infectious disease in the "previous_diagnosis" field.
5.  If yes, specify the disease(s) in the "diagnosis_details" field.
6.  Indicate your vaccination status in the "vaccination_status" field.
7.  Select all symptoms you have experienced in the past 30 days in the "recent_symptoms" field.
8.  Identify potential exposure to infectious disease in the "exposure_history" field.
9.  Answer whether you have had antibody or antigen testing done in the "antibody_test_history" and "antigen_test_history" fields.
10. If yes, indicate the date of the most recent test in the "test_dates" field.
11.  Answer whether you are currently taking medications in the "medication_use" field.
12. If yes, list your current medications in the "medication_details" field.
13. Indicate any chronic health conditions you have in the "chronic_conditions" field.
14. Identify your occupation and select whether you are a healthcare worker in the "healthcare_worker" field.
15. If healthcare worker, select your exposure risk level in the "occupational_exposure_risk" field.
16. Enter the number of household members in the "household_members" field.
17. Answer whether anyone in your household has been confirmed to have an infection in the "household_infection" field.
18. Indicate your willingness to participate in follow-up testing in the "testing_willingness" field.
19. If desired, provide additional information about your health status or concerns in the "additional_information" field.
20. Confirm your consent to use your data for research purposes in the "privacy_consent" field.
