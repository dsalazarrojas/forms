# Pet Nutrition Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess pet owners' awareness and habits regarding pet nutrition. It is intended to collect data on pet owners' knowledge, dietary habits, and services related to pet nutrition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer.
4. For select fields, choose the option that best describes your pet's characteristics or habits.
