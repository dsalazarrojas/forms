# Dementia Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information and informed consent from individuals participating in dementia research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
This form can be completed by participants and researchers. If you are completing this form as a participant, please provide your details as prompted. If you are a researcher, please ensure that you have obtained informed consent from the participant and have signed to confirm this. The form has multiple pages with text input fields that require completion as you proceed through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required: false): This page is meant to collect basic information about the participant. Please input your details in the provided text field.

### Study Details
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can provide any additional information about the study. This field is optional and can be left blank.

### Study Location
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, `text`, required: false): If the study is taking place at a specific location, please enter the details here.

### Informed Consent
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, `text`, required: false): This is an important section where you can state your informed consent to participate in the study. Please ensure that you have read and understood the study's terms before signing.

### Participant Signature
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the study's informed consent. This can be a digital or physical signature.

### Researcher Signature
* **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, `text`, required: false): As a researcher, please sign to confirm that you have obtained informed consent from the participant and have explained the study's terms to them.

## Tips
- Ensure you have read and understood the study's terms before proceeding with the form.
- Verify that this form is relevant to your research needs.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form owner for clarification.
