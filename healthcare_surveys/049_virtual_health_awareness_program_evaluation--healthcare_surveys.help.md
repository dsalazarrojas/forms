# virtual_health_awareness_program_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of the virtual health awareness program. It is used to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session type from the options provided.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the program, including the number of sessions you attended and your overall satisfaction.
3. Enter your contact information and any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Review and submit your form.
