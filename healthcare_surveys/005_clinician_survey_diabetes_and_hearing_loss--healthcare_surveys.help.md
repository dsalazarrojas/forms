# Clinician Survey - Diabetes and Hearing Loss - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information from clinicians about their practices, attitudes, and perceived knowledge regarding diabetes and hearing loss.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Fill out the form by providing answers to each question, using the options provided.
* Make sure to answer all questions as they apply to your specific healthcare setting and practices.
* Use the notes section for additional comments or to expand on any answers you provide.
* If you have any difficulty or need help with a question, please don't hesitate to reach out to the survey administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (clinician_name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your medical license or personnel records.
* **Your Primary Medical Specialty** (specialty, select_one, required): Select your primary medical specialty, such as Endocrinology or Otolaryngology.
* **Years of clinical experience** (years_experience, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been practicing medicine.
* **Type of healthcare institution** (institution_type, select_one, required): Select the type of institution where you work, such as Academic Medical Center or Community Health Center.
* **Approximate number of patients with diabetes you care for** (patient_volume_diabetes, select_one, required): Estimate the number of patients with diabetes you care for on average.
* **Awareness of the link between diabetes and hearing loss** (diabetes_awareness, select_one, required): Indicate how aware you are of the link between diabetes and hearing loss.
* **Hearing screening for diabetic patients** (hearing_screening, select_one, required): Choose how often you screen diabetic patients for hearing loss.
* **Barriers to hearing screening in diabetes care** (screening_barriers, select_multiple, optional): Select all the barriers you face when screening for hearing loss in diabetic patients.
* **Educating diabetic patients about hearing loss risk** (hearing_education, select_one, required): Indicate how often you educate diabetic patients about the risk of hearing loss.
* **Referring diabetic patients to audiology** (referral_practices, select_one, required): Choose how often you refer diabetic patients with hearing loss to audiology.
* **Accessibility of audiology services** (audiology_access, select_one, required): Indicate how accessible audiology services are for your patients.
* **Formal protocols for diabetes and hearing loss assessment** (current_protocols, select_one, required): Select how formalized your protocols are for assessing diabetes and hearing loss.
* **Interdisciplinary collaboration** (interdisciplinary, select_one, required): Choose how often you collaborate with other specialists on diabetes care.
* **Confidence in knowledge about diabetes-related hearing loss** (knowledge_confidence, select_one, required): Indicate your level of confidence in your knowledge about diabetes-related hearing loss.
* **Additional education or training value** (training_needs, select_one, required): Select how valuable additional education on this topic would be for you.
* **Most helpful resources** (needed_resources, select_multiple, optional): Select all the resources that would be most helpful to improve your practice.
* **Interested in participating in research** (interested_collaboration, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of interest in participating in research on this topic.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (additional_comments, text, optional): Share any additional comments, thoughts, or suggestions you may have about this form or its questions.

This form should take approximately [X] minutes to complete, and your answers are confidential.
