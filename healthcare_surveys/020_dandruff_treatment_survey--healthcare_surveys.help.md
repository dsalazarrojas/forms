# Dandruff Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about dandruff treatments from users. It aims to collect data on symptoms, treatment effectiveness, and other related factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list in **Symptoms checklist**.
2. Enter the duration of your dandruff experience in **How long have you been experiencing dandruff?** and **How long have you been using your current dandruff treatment?** fields.
3. Choose your current treatment from **What type of treatment have you used?** field.
4. Select how often you use your current treatment in **How often did you use your chosen treatment?** field.
5. Rate the effectiveness of your current treatment in **How effective has your current treatment been?** field.
6. Choose the reason you stopped using your treatment in **Why did you stop using your chosen treatment?** field.
7. Answer the questions in **Do you have any medical conditions?**, **How did you get your dandruff?**, and **What type of dandruff do you have?** fields.
8. If applicable, report any other medical conditions that might have contributed to your dandruff in **Any other medical conditions you would like to mention?** field.
9. Rate the effectiveness of your other treatments in **What was the effectiveness of your other dandruff treatments?** field.
10. Answer the question in **Did you experience any side effects?** field.
11. If applicable, report any side effects you experienced in **What was the side effect?** field.
12. Rate the effectiveness of your other treatments in **What was the effectiveness of your other dandruff treatments?** field.
13. Report any other treatments you have tried in **Have you tried any other treatments?** field.
14. Rate the effectiveness of your other treatments in **What was the effectiveness of your other dandruff treatments?** field.
15. Report any side effects you experienced with other treatments in **Have you experienced any side effects with other dandruff treatments?** field.
16. Confirm your answer to **Do you know the cause of dandruff?** field.
17. Rate whether you have experienced dandruff-related skin issues in **Have you experienced dandruff-related issues with your skin?** field.
18. Report any family history of dandruff in **Do you have any family history of dandruff?** field.
19. Confirm the type of dandruff you have in **What type of dandruff do you have?** field.
20. Rate the effectiveness of your treatment in **What was the effectiveness of your dandruff treatment?** field.
21. Confirm whether you experienced any side effects in **Did you experience any side effects?** field.
22. Report any other medical conditions that might have contributed to your dandruff in **Have you experienced any other medical conditions that might have contributed to dandruff?** field.
