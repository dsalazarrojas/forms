# emdr_session_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from clients after a therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have completed a therapy session and are providing feedback for the actual session.
2. Complete each field with accurate information:
	* Session Feedback: Describe your experience and any comments you have about the session.
	* Therapist Rating: Rate the effectiveness of your therapist using the options provided.
	* Session Effectiveness: Provide a detailed description of how effective the session was.
	* Rating: Give a numerical rating of your satisfaction with the session (e.g., 1-5).
	* Feedback: Add any additional comments or feedback about the session.
	* Session Date: Enter the date of the actual therapy session.
	* Session Time: Enter the time of the actual therapy session.
	* Client Email: Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience and any comments about the session.
* **Therapist Rating** (`therapist_rating`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of your therapist using the options provided.
* **Session Effectiveness** (`session_effectiveness`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of how effective the session was.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Give a numerical rating of your satisfaction with the session (e.g., 1-5).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the actual therapy session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the actual therapy session.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate information about the session date and time fields.
* Use the numerical rating scale provided to rate your satisfaction with the session.
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback to help the therapist improve.
