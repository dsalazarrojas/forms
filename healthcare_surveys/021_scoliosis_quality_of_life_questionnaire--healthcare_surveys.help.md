# scoliosis quality of life questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scoliosis Quality of Life Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about your scoliosis condition, its impact on your daily life, and any relevant medical history. This information will help your healthcare provider understand your condition better and provide you with more effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as accurately and completely as possible. Be sure to provide your contact information, medical record number, and follow-up details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Demographics**: Please provide your basic demographic information, such as your name, age, and contact details.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years to help your healthcare provider understand your condition in the context of your age.
* **Condition Duration**: Report the length of time you have been living with scoliosis.
* **Condition Severity**: Select the severity of your scoliosis condition, which affects the degree of its impact on your daily life. Choose from: mild, moderate, or severe.
* **Condition Impact**: Select all that apply from: work, social, emotional, and physical, to describe how scoliosis affects your daily activities.
* **Follow-up**: Indicate whether you have a follow-up appointment scheduled with your healthcare provider.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to ensure your healthcare provider can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Medical Record Number**: Provide your medical record number for reference.
* **Medical Record Type**: If applicable, select the type of your medical record.
* **Provider Name**: Enter your healthcare provider's name.
* **Provider Contact Information**: Provide contact information for your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Contact Information**: Enter your contact information for reference.
* **Follow-up Date**: If applicable, enter the scheduled follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Time**: If applicable, enter the scheduled follow-up time.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Provide any important notes or comments for your follow-up appointment.
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including allergies, medications, and other relevant information.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your medical history.
* **Provider Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from your healthcare provider.
* **Follow-up Questions**: Provide any questions or concerns you have for your follow-up appointment.
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any personal notes or comments.
* **Provider Comments**: Enter any comments or recommendations from your healthcare provider.

Please note that some fields are not required but are included for your healthcare provider's reference and information gathering purposes.
