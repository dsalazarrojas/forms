# healthcare_service_efficiency_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their healthcare service experience.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your honest and personal experience at the healthcare provider.
2. Use the provided options or select "Other" if the options do not apply to your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your wait time at the visit?** (`visit_efficiency`, `number`, optional): This question asks you to report your wait time at the visit.
* **How would you rate the staff attitude?** (`staff_attitude`, `number`, optional): This question asks you to rate the staff's attitude during your visit.
* **How satisfied were you with your visit?** (`visit_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with your visit using the provided options.
* **How would you rate the service quality?** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate the quality of service you received during your visit.
* **What was the most frustrating part of your visit?** (`visit_bottlenecks`, `note`, optional): This question asks you to provide any frustrating parts of your visit, if any.
* **How satisfied are you with your healthcare provider?** (`patient_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the healthcare provider.
* **How would you rate the healthcare provider's knowledge of your health condition?** (`staff_knowledge`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks you to rate the healthcare provider's knowledge of your health condition.
* **How convenient was your visit?** (`visit_convenience`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate the convenience of your visit.
* **How helpful were the staff during your visit?** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks you to rate the helpfulness of the staff during your visit.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your visit?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
* **How often do you visit this healthcare provider?** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks you to report how often you visit this healthcare provider.
