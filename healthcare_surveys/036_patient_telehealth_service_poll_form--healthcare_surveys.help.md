# Patient Telehealth Service Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from patients who have used our telehealth services. The information collected will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better care to our patients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select the correct answers based on your experiences with our telehealth service.
3. Enter your email address and phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up surveys.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Type** (Contact Type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of contact you had with our telehealth service, such as phone, video, or in-person visit.
* **First Visit Rating** (First Visit Rating, number, required: false): Rate your experience with our first visit, using a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Follow-Up Scheduling** (Follow-Up Scheduling, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how you would like to schedule future visits, such as online, phone, or in-person.
* **Technical Issues** (Technical Issues, text, required: false): If you experienced any technical issues during your visit, please describe them here.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our telehealth service.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up surveys.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up calls.
* **Date of First Visit** (Date of First Visit, date, required: false): Enter the date of your first visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Follow-up Visit** (Follow-up Visit, time, required: false): Enter the time of your follow-up visit, using AM/PM format.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (Next Follow-up Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your next follow-up visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Follow-up Visit Frequency** (Follow-up Visit Frequency, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you would like to have follow-up visits, such as weekly, biweekly, or monthly.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (Follow-up Frequency, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the methods you would like to use for follow-up visits, such as phone, email, or in-person.
* **Follow-up Method** (Follow-up Method, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the methods you would like to use for follow-up visits, such as online, phone, or in-person.
* **Follow-up Comments** (Follow-up Comments, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our telehealth service.
* **Next Steps** (Next Steps, text, required: false): Describe any next steps or follow-up actions you would like to take.
* **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our telehealth service, using a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Would Recommend** (Would Recommend, number, required: false): Let us know if you would recommend our telehealth service to a friend or family member, using a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not recommended and 5 is highly recommended.
* **Rating** (Rating, number, required: false): Rate your experience with our telehealth service, using a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comments 2** (Comments 2, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our telehealth service.
* **Email 2** (Email 2, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up surveys.
* **Phone 2** (Phone 2, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to receive follow-up calls.
* **Follow-up Date** (Follow-up Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your next follow-up visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Follow-up Time** (Follow-up Time, time, required: false): Enter the time of your next follow-up visit, using AM/PM format.
* **Next Follow-up** (Next Follow-up, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the method and frequency of your next follow-up visit, such as online, phone, or in-person, and weekly, biweekly, or monthly.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any technical issues or concerns, please describe them in the Technical Issues field.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the Comments field.
* If you would like to receive follow-up surveys, please enter your email address and phone number.
* If you have any next steps or follow-up actions you would like to take, please describe them in the Next Steps field.
