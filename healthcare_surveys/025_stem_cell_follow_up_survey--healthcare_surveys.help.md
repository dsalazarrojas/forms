> # Stem Cell Follow Up Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Cell Follow Up Survey is designed to collect information from patients who have undergone stem cell therapy. This data will help us understand the effectiveness and impact of the treatment and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question before answering.
2. Complete the form in its entirety to ensure that we have the most accurate and comprehensive information about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (form_page_6): Enter your name and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Treatment Information** (form_page_2): Enter the name and dose of the treatment you received. This will help us understand your treatment history.
* **Health Status** (form_page_3): Describe your current health status, including any changes or improvements since your treatment.
* **Side Effects** (form_page_4): If you experienced any side effects, select all the options that apply.
* **Follow-up Date** (form_page_10): Enter the date of your most recent follow-up appointment.
* **Treatment Centre** (form_page_8): Enter the name of the treatment centre where you received your stem cell therapy.
* **Treatment Provider** (form_page_9): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered your treatment.
* **Treatment Dates** (form_page_7): Enter the dates of your stem cell therapy sessions.
