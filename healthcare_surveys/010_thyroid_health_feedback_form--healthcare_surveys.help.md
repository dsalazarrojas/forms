# Thyroid Health Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thyroid Health Feedback Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect feedback from patients about their thyroid health. This form helps gather information on the patient's overall health and symptoms, medical tests, diagnoses, medication, treatment plan, and any follow-up appointments scheduled or completed.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Thyroid Health Feedback Form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer the questions as honestly as you can.
2. For questions with multiple options (e.g., "Thyroid Health Clinics", "Thyroid Health Symptoms"), select all that apply.
3. Enter your email address and phone number if you have any comments or need to be contacted for follow-up.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Thyroid Health Comments" and "Additional Comments" sections.
5. Rate your overall thyroid health in the "Thyroid Health Rating" section.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all the questions and sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Thyroid Health Form** (`thyroid_health_form`): A text field for entering any general comments or feedback about your thyroid health.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Select "Yes" if you have feedback about your thyroid health, "No" otherwise.
* **Thyroid Health Clinics** (`thyroid_health_clinics`): Check "Yes" if you have visited a thyroid clinic, "No" otherwise.
* **Thyroid Health Symptoms** (`thyroid_health_symptoms`): Check all symptoms that apply to your thyroid health, such as fatigue, weight changes, or swelling.
* **Thyroid Health Tests** (`thyroid_health_tests`): Check all tests you have undergone, such as blood tests or ultrasound exams.
* **Thyroid Health Diagnoses** (`thyroid_health_diagnoses`): Check all diagnoses you have received from a healthcare professional, such as hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism.
* **Thyroid Health Medication** (`thyroid_health_medication`): Check all medications you are taking for your thyroid health.
* **Thyroid Health Treatment Plan** (`thyroid_health_treatment_plan`): Enter your current treatment plan for your thyroid health.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your thyroid health.
* **Follow-up Scheduled** (`follow_up_scheduled`): Enter the date of your next follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Completed** (`follow_up_completed`): Enter the time of your last follow-up appointment completion.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`): Enter any notes from your follow-up appointments.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Thyroid Health Rating** (`thyroid_health_rating`): Rate your overall thyroid health as "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Thyroid Health Comments** (`thyroid_health_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your thyroid health.
* **Additional Comments** (`thyroid_health_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your thyroid health.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions as honestly as you can.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
* If you have any follow-up appointments scheduled, make sure to enter the date and time in the "Follow-up Scheduled" and "Follow-up Completed" sections.
