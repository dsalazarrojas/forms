# Patient Blood Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from healthcare staff on their experiences with patient blood management. The information collected will help the organization identify areas where staff need training or support to improve their patient blood management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and provide feedback on your experiences with patient blood management.
2. Select the options that best answer each question.
3. Make sure to submit your responses to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Staff Feedback** (id: 1)
* **Staff Feedback (required)**: This field is for you to share any thoughts or opinions you have about patient blood management. Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can.

### 2. **Training** (id: 2)
* **Training (optional)**: If you have received training on patient blood management, select all the options that apply to you.

### 3. **Blood Management Practices** (id: 3)
* **Blood Management Practices (required)**: Select one of the options that best answers your current practices.

### 4. **Barriers** (id: 4)
* **Barriers (required)**: This field is for you to list any obstacles or challenges you face when implementing patient blood management practices.

### 5. **Patient Blood Management Survey Form** (id: 5)
* **Patient Blood Management Survey Form (optional)**: If you have used the Patient Blood Management Survey Form, select the option that best answers your use.

### 6. **Other Blood Management Practices** (id: 7)
* **Other Blood Management Practices (optional)**: If you have other practices not listed, please describe them here.

### 7. **Training Facilitators** (id: 8)
* **Training Facilitators (optional)**: If you have received training on patient blood management from facilitators, list their names or roles here.

### 8. **Have you used the Patient Blood Management Survey Form?** (id: 8)
* **Have you used the Patient Blood Management Survey Form (required)**: Select yes or no to indicate if you have used the form.

### 9. **How often do you use the Patient Blood Management Survey Form?** (id: 9)
* **How often do you use the Patient Blood Management Survey Form (optional)**: Select the option that best answers how often you use the form.

### 10. **What is the level of satisfaction with the Patient Blood Management Survey Form?** (id: 10)
* **What is the level of satisfaction with the Patient Blood Management Survey Form (optional)**: Select the option that best answers your level of satisfaction.

### 11. **How often do you use the Patient Blood Management Survey Form on your daily work?** (id: 11)
* **How often do you use the Patient Blood Management Survey Form on your daily work (optional)**: Select the option that best answers how often you use the form.
