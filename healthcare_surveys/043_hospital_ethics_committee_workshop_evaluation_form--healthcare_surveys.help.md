# Hospital Ethics Committee Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Ethics Committee Workshop Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and satisfaction of the hospital's ethics workshop. This form is intended to gather feedback from attendees on the workshop's quality, content, and overall experience. The form will help the hospital's ethics committee to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are essential for providing a complete evaluation.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
3. Choose the most suitable options for each select multiple and select one question.
4. Write detailed comments and feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workshop Details (1)
* **Workshop Details**: This is a note field to provide a brief description of the workshop you attended. Please keep your answer concise and focused on the topic.

### Name (2)
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your hospital ID or badge.

### Position (3)
* **Position**: Enter your title or job description as it relates to your role in the hospital.

### Department (4)
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department or team you belong to.

### Date of Workshop (5)
* **Date of Workshop**: Enter the date on which you attended the workshop.

### Start Time (6)
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the workshop.

### End Time (7)
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the workshop.

### Evaluation Period (8)
* **Evaluation Period**: Evaluate the workshop duration, from 1 (very short) to 6 (very long). This will help us understand how time-efficient the workshop was.

### Total Score (9)
* **Total Score**: Enter an overall score out of 10 to evaluate the workshop's quality. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction.

### Overall Satisfaction (10)
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose whether you are Satisfied or Dissatisfied with the workshop. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction.

### Satisfaction Level (11)
* **Satisfaction Level**: Choose all the ways in which you were satisfied or dissatisfied with the workshop. This will help us understand specific aspects of the workshop that need improvement.

### Evaluation Form (12)
* **Evaluation Form**: Choose whether you used the evaluation form provided during the workshop.

### Feedback (13)
* **Feedback**: Please provide any comments or suggestions about the workshop, its content, or its organization.

### Suggestions (14)
* **Suggestions**: Provide any additional suggestions on how to improve the workshop.

### Comments (15)
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the workshop.

### Rating (16)
* **Rating**: Evaluate the workshop on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent). This will help us understand your overall rating.

### Rating Scale (17)
* **Rating Scale**: Evaluate the workshop on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent). This will help us understand your overall rating.

### Comments (18)
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the workshop.

### Rating 2 (19)
* **Rating 2**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your daily work.

### Rating 3 (20)
* **Rating 3**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your team's work.

### Rating 4 (21)
* **Rating 4**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your department's work.

### Rating 5 (22)
* **Rating 5**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your hospital's work.

### Rating 6 (23)
* **Rating 6**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your role's work.

### Rating 7 (24)
* **Rating 7**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your team's work.

### Rating 8 (25)
* **Rating 8**: Enter a number from 1 (Poor) to 7 (Excellent) to evaluate the workshop's relevance to your department's work.

Please submit your form after filling out all required fields.
