# pandemic_mental_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help individuals assess their mental health status, coping mechanisms, and access to support during the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2. For questions with dropdown options, select the response that best fits your situation.
3. For questions with text fields, provide a brief description of your answer.
4. If you have a mental health professional, please provide their name and relationship.
5. If you don't have a mental health professional, please select 'no' for the corresponding questions.
