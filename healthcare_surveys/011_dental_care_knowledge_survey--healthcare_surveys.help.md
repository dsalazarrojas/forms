# Dental Care Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Care Knowledge Survey is designed to collect information from dental care professionals and educators on their experiences and perceptions related to oral health. This form will help us understand the current state of oral health awareness, assessments, and risk factors among your patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Dentists, Hygienists, or Other**: Please select the type of dental professional or educator you are.
2. **Oral Health Awareness**: Please choose one of the three options to describe your perception of oral health among your patients:
	* Good oral health
	* Fair oral health
	* Bad oral health
3. **Oral Health Assessment**: Please provide your assessment of oral health, if any.
4. **Risk Factors**: List any risk factors that you have observed among your patients.
5. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or observations, please provide them here.
6. **Email Address**: Please provide your email address, if you'd like to receive follow-up information or surveys.
7. **Phone Number**: Please provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further questions or information.
8. **Submit**: You can submit the survey or save it as a draft for later completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dental Professionals and Educators**: 
	* Choose the type of dental professional or educator you are. This will help us understand the perspective of different dental care providers.
* **Oral Health Awareness**: 
	* Select one of the three options to describe your perception of oral health among your patients.
* **Oral Health Assessment**: 
	* Please provide a numerical value to describe the assessment you have observed or conducted.
* **Risk Factors**: 
	* List any risk factors that you have observed among your patients.
* **Additional Comments**: 
	* If you have any additional comments or observations, please provide them here.
* **Additional Comments (optional)**: 
	* This field is optional and allows you to add any additional information that you think is relevant.
* **Date of Completion**: 
	* Please provide the date and time when you completed this survey.
* **Time of Completion**: 
	* Please provide the time when you completed this survey.
* **Oral Health Survey**: 
	* This field is optional and allows you to provide a numerical value to describe your oral health assessment.
* **Phone Number**: 
	* Please provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further questions or information.
* **Email Address**: 
	* Please provide your email address, if you'd like to receive follow-up information or surveys.
* **Submit**: 
	* You can submit the survey or save it as a draft for later completion.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields before submitting the survey.
* You can save the survey as a draft and complete it later.
* If you have any questions or need help with the survey, please contact us.

Note: The form will automatically validate the required fields. If you encounter any errors, please let us know so we can assist you further.
