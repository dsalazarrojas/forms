# Remote Healthcare Services Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience with remote healthcare services. The collected information will be used to understand the effectiveness of these services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Fill in any required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Question 1: How did you feel about the care provided by your healthcare professional?
* This question asks for your overall satisfaction with the care provided by your healthcare professional.
* Please answer with a brief description of your experience.

### Question 2: How likely are you to recommend our service to others?
* This question asks how likely you would be to recommend our service to your friends and family.
* Answer with a number from 1 (not likely) to 5 (likely).

### Question 3: How would you rate the quality of service?
* This question asks you to evaluate the quality of our service.
* Answer with a number from 1 (not good) to 5 (excellent).

### Question 4: How likely are you to use our service again?
* This question asks about your future use of our service.
* Answer with a number from 1 (not likely) to 5 (likely).
