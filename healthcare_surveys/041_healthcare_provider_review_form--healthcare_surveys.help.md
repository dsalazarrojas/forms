# Healthcare Provider Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with their healthcare providers. It helps us understand what worked well and what didn't, so we can improve the care we provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "Provider Name" field.
2. Choose the rating that best reflects your experience with the provider from the "Rating" field.
3. Answer each of the following questions:
   - How would you rate the quality of care received? (Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the "Quality of care received" dropdown).
   - How would you rate the communication with the provider? (Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the "Communication" dropdown).
   - What time the service was provided? (Choose "Early", "On time", or "Late" from the "Time of service" dropdown).
   - How would you rate the provider's willingness to listen to you? (Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the "Willingness to listen" dropdown, but this field is actually question_7 and question_6 is actually "Willigness to help", we will use just question_7 for this answer).
   - Finally, write any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Rating (Number, Optional)**: Rate your experience with the provider on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Quality of care received (Select Multiple, Optional)**: How would you rate the quality of care received? Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Communication (Select One, Optional)**: How would you rate the communication with the provider? Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Time of service (Select One, Optional)**: What time the service was provided? Choose "Early", "On time", or "Late".
* **Willingness to listen (Select One, Optional)**: How would you rate the provider's willingness to listen to you? Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Overall experience (Select One, Optional)**: Rate your overall experience with the provider. Choose "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor".
* **Comments (Text, Optional)**: Write any additional comments about your experience.
* **Contact Info (Text, Optional)**: Enter any contact information you would like to provide.
* **Submit (Select One, Optional)**: Choose "Submit" to send the feedback or "Other" to cancel.
* **Cancel (Select One, Optional)**: Choose "Cancel" to cancel the form without sending feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as accurate and honest as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any additional comments, please use the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
