# Vitamin B 12 Treatment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on patient experiences and information from healthcare providers regarding Vitamin B 12 treatment. The form will help gather insights into the effectiveness and side effects of the treatment, as well as demographic information about the patient and healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field carefully, making sure to answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use the explanations provided for each field as a guide.
3. If you have any questions or need clarification, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **Vitamin B 12 Therapy Effectiveness** (`vitamin_b_12_therapy_effectiveness`, text, optional): This field is for describing your overall experience with Vitamin B 12 therapy.
* **Vitamin B 12 Therapy Duration** (`vitamin_b_12_therapy_duration`, number, required): This field is for reporting the duration of your Vitamin B 12 therapy.
* **Vitamin B 12 Side Effects** (`vitamin_b_12_side_effects`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the side effects you experienced during your Vitamin B 12 therapy. You can choose multiple options.
* **Vitamin B 12 Treatment Schedule** (`vitamin_b_12_treatment_schedule`, date, required): This field is for reporting your treatment schedule for Vitamin B 12 therapy.
* **Vitamin B 12 Treatment Dosages** (`vitamin_b_12_treatment_dosage`, number, required): This field is for reporting your Vitamin B 12 treatment dosages.

### Page 2
* **Vitamin B 12 Treatment Frequency** (`vitamin_b_12_treatment_frequency`, text, optional): This field is for describing the frequency of your Vitamin B 12 treatment.
* **Vitamin B 12 Treatment Side Effects Other** (`vitamin_b_12_treatment_side_effects_other`, note, optional): This field is for reporting any additional side effects you experienced during your Vitamin B 12 therapy.
* **Patient Experience** (`patient_experience`, select_one, required): This field is for rating your overall experience with Vitamin B 12 therapy. Choose from the following options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Very Poor.
* **Provider Info** (`provider_info`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for listing the names of healthcare providers involved in your treatment. You can choose multiple options.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or information about your experience with Vitamin B 12 therapy.

### Page 3
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): This field is for reporting your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for reporting your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for reporting your phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required): This field is for reporting the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Title** (`healthcare_provider_title`, text, required): This field is for reporting the title of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Department** (`healthcare_provider_department`, text, required): This field is for reporting the department of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Clinic** (`healthcare_provider_clinic`, select_one, required): This field is for reporting the name of your healthcare provider's clinic. Choose from the following options: Clinic A, Clinic B, or Clinic C.
* **Healthcare Provider Specialty** (`healthcare_provider_specialty`, text, required): This field is for reporting the specialty of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider NPI** (`healthcare_provider_npi`, text, required): This field is for reporting the NPI (National Provider Identifier) of your healthcare provider.
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, required): This field is for reporting the name of your practice.
* **Practice Address** (`practice_address`, text, required): This field is for reporting the address of your practice.
* **Practice City** (`practice_city`, text, required): This field is for reporting the city of your practice.
* **Practice State** (`practice_state`, text, required): This field is for reporting the state of your practice.
* **Practice Zip** (`practice_zip`, text, required): This field is for reporting the zip code of your practice.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is for reporting the assigned tool for your practice.

Tips:
* Be thorough and honest when answering each question.
* If you need help or clarification, refer to this help guide.
* If you have any additional comments or information, use the "Additional Comments" field.
