# Healthcare Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience at a healthcare facility. Your input will help the healthcare staff improve their services and provide better care for future patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, considering your experience at the healthcare facility.
2. Choose the correct options from the drop-down menus or provide a brief answer in the text fields.
3. You can skip any optional fields that you're not comfortable providing information for.
4. Once you've completed the form, click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback (page_1)
* **Feedback (Select Multiple)** (`Very dissatisfied`, `Dissatisfied`, `Neutral`, `Satisfied`, `Very Satisfied`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the healthcare service. Choose the option that best describes your experience.

### Overall Experience (page_2)
* **Overall Experience (Text)**: This field is for any additional comments or descriptions about your visit. You can write as much or as little as you'd like to help us understand your experience.

### Likely to Recommend (page_3)
* **Likely to Recommend (Select One)** (`No`, `Yes`): This question asks whether you would recommend this healthcare facility to friends and family. Choose the option that best reflects your intention.

### Healthcare Staff Responsiveness (page_4)
* **Healthcare Staff Responsiveness**: This field allows you to rate how responsive the healthcare staff were during your visit. You can select a number between 1 (poor) and 10 (excellent).

### Cleanliness (page_5)
* **Cleanliness**: This field asks you to rate the cleanliness of the healthcare facility. You can select a number between 1 (poor) and 10 (excellent).

### Facility Conditions (page_6)
* **Facility Conditions (Text)**: This field is for any comments or descriptions about the condition of the healthcare facility. You can write as much or as little as you'd like.

### Pain Management (page_7)
* **Pain Management (Select One)** (`Poor`, `Good`, `Excellent`): This question asks about the effectiveness of the pain management during your visit. Choose the option that best describes your experience.

### Waiting Time (page_8)
* **Waiting Time**: This field allows you to report the waiting time for your appointment. You can select a time in minutes.

### Date of Visit (page_9)
* **Date of Visit (Date)**: This field is for the date you visited the healthcare facility.

### Healthcare Provider (page_10)
* **Healthcare Provider (Select One)** (`Nurse`, `Doctor`, `Other`): This question asks about the type of healthcare provider you saw during your visit. Choose the option that best describes your experience.

### Additional Comments (page_11)
* **Additional Comments (Note)**: This field is for any additional comments or descriptions about your visit. You can write as much or as little as you'd like.
