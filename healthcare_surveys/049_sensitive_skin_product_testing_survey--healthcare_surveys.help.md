# Sensitive Skin Product Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects data from patients who have used sensitive skin products for testing and evaluation. Your input is crucial in helping us improve our products and services for better skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product you used from the options provided.
2. Choose the areas of your skin affected by the product.
3. Select the level of irritation you experienced (Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
4. Specify the location of the irritation on your skin (Face, Neck, or Hands).
5. Rate the product (1, 2, or 3).
6. Indicate if you recommend the product (True or False).
7. For any skin condition, please describe it briefly.
8. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the text area.
9. If you are under a medical condition, please specify it (Allergy, Acne, or Rosacea).
10. If you have any medical information related to your skin condition, such as medication, please provide it in the text area.
11. If you have a doctor's name, phone number, and email, please provide them for further contact.
12. Please indicate your age.
13. Select your gender (Male or Female).
14. Finally, confirm that you have read and understood the consent statement and indicate your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_used** (Select One, required):
	+ Select the product you used during the test (Product A, Product B, or Product C).
* **skin_area** (Select Multiple, required):
	+ Choose the area of the skin affected by the product (Face, Neck, or Hands).
* **skin_condition** (Select Multiple, required):
	+ Select any skin conditions you are experiencing (Redness, Itching, or Burning).
* **irritation_level** (Select One, required):
	+ Choose the level of irritation you experienced (Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
* **irritation_location** (Select Multiple, required):
	+ Specify the location of the irritation on your skin (Face, Neck, or Hands).
* **product_rating** (Select One, required):
	+ Rate the product (1, 2, or 3).
* **product_recommended** (Select One, required):
	+ Indicate if you recommend the product (True or False).
* **skin_condition_description** (Note, optional):
	+ Briefly describe any skin conditions you are experiencing.
* **additional_comments** (Note, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the product.
* **medical_condition** (Select Multiple, required):
	+ Specify any medical conditions you are experiencing (Allergy, Acne, or Rosacea).
* **medication** (Text, optional):
	+ If you have any medical information related to your skin condition, provide it here.
* **doctor_name** (Text, optional):
	+ If you have a doctor's name, please provide it here.
* **doctor_phone** (Text, optional):
	+ If you have a doctor's phone number, please provide it here.
* **doctor_email** (Email, optional):
	+ If you have a doctor's email, please provide it here.
* **patient_age** (Number, required):
	+ Please indicate your age.
* **gender** (Select One, required):
	+ Select your gender (Male or Female).
* **consent** (Select One, required):
	+ Confirm that you have read and understood the consent statement and indicate your agreement.
