# Hair Damage Source Identification Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals identify potential sources of hair damage. Answering this form will help collect information that can inform advice or treatment for hair conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the select_one fields.
3. Select multiple options for the select_multiple fields where applicable.
4. Do not worry if you're unsure about certain questions; answer according to your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Damage Source**: Select "Yes" if you believe your hair damage is caused by a specific source, "No" otherwise.
* **Hair Condition**: Select "Yes" if you have a known hair condition, such as dandruff or itchiness.
* **Hair Length**: Select "Yes" if your hair is excessively long or has an unusual length.
* **Chemical Processing**: Select "Yes" if you've undergone chemical processing for your hair, such as dyeing or bleaching.
* **Hair Color**: Select "Yes" if your hair is colored or dyed.
* **Hair Style**: Select "Yes" if you have a specific hair style that may be causing damage.
* **Hair Damaging Habits**: Select all applicable habits that may be causing damage to your hair, such as excessive heat styling, tight hairstyles, or excessive brushing.
* **Hair Damaging Products**: Select all applicable products that may be causing damage to your hair, such as hair dye, hair spray, or hair gel.
* **Hair Damaging Treatments**: Select all applicable treatments that may be causing damage to your hair, such as hair relaxers or hair straightening treatments.
