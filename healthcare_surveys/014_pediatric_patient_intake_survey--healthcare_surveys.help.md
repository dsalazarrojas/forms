# Pediatric Patient Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Patient Intake Survey is a tool for healthcare providers to gather essential information about pediatric patients. This form is designed to collect general data, medical history, allergies, medications, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that you're providing complete and clear information.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
4. Review your answers carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**: Please provide a brief description of the patient's condition or issue.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: Provide the patient's contact information, including their name, address, and phone number.
* **Patient Information (Page 3)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous treatments.
* **Visit Details (Page 4)**: Specify the reason for the visit, including any symptoms or concerns.
* **Medical History (Page 5)**: List any previous medical conditions or procedures.
* **Medication Use (Page 6)**: Describe any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergy and Contraindications (Page 7)**: List any allergies or medical conditions that may impact treatment.
* **Medical Conditions (Page 8)**: Describe any ongoing medical conditions.
* **Social History (Page 9)**: Provide information about the patient's social and family background.
* **Family History (Page 10)**: Discuss any family medical history.
* **Immunizations (Page 11)**: Record any immunizations or vaccinations.
* **Medications (Page 12)**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Devices (Page 13)**: Describe any medical devices the patient is using.
* **Surgical History (Page 14)**: List any previous surgeries or medical procedures.
* **Patient Support (Page 15)**: Specify any support systems in place for the patient.
* **Patient Educational Background (Page 16)**: Provide information about the patient's educational background.
* **Patient and Family (Page 17)**: Discuss any family medical history or concerns.
* **Patient Goals (Page 18)**: Specify any treatment goals or expectations.
* **Patient Consent (Page 19)**: Obtain patient consent for treatment.
* **Patient Informed Consent (Page 20)**: Ensure patient understanding of treatment and risks.
* **Patient Signature (Page 21)**: Sign to confirm patient consent.
* **Medical Notes (Page 22)**: Record any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Visit Notes (Page 23)**: Document any visit notes or observations.
* **Provider Information (Page 24)**: Specify provider contact information.
* **Staff (Page 25)**: Record any staff involved in the patient's care.

Please review each page carefully and ensure that you're providing accurate and complete information. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
