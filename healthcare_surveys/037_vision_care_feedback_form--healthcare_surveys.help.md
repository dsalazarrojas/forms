# Vision Care Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vision Care Feedback Form is a tool used to collect patient feedback and suggestions for improvement of the clinic's services. Your input will help us understand your experiences and make necessary changes to provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the "Any other comments" field.
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up.

* **How would you rate the staff's professionalism?** (`question_7`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the staff from Excellent to Poor.
* **What is your age?** (`question_8`, `number`, optional): Enter your age for demographic purposes.
* **What is your contact information?** (`question_9`, `text`, optional): Enter your address, phone number, or any other contact information.
* **Your comments** (`question_10`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have.
