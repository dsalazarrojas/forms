# Local Anesthesia Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their experience with local anesthesia.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section and provide as much detail as possible. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Describe the procedure you had (procedure_description)**: In this section, please describe the procedure you underwent that involved local anesthesia. This will help us understand the context of your experience.
* **How would you rate your comfort level with the anesthesia administered? (comfort_level)**: Please select one of the options to indicate how you felt during the procedure. This will help us understand how we can improve our anesthesia administration techniques.
* **Have you experienced any of the following side effects? (side_effects)**: Select all that apply from the list of possible side effects. This will help us understand any potential issues with the anesthesia we used.
* **What was your overall experience like? (overall_experience)**: Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the procedure. This will help us understand the impact of the procedure on your well-being.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with us? (additional_comments)**: If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your experience, please share them here. This is an opportunity for you to provide any feedback that didn't fit in the previous questions.
* **How would you rate the outcome of the procedure? (procedure_outcome)**: Please select one of the options to indicate how you feel about the outcome of the procedure. This will help us understand how effective the procedure was.
* **How would you rate the care you received from the provider? (provider_care)**: Please select one of the options to indicate how you felt about the care you received from your provider. This will help us understand how well we are meeting our care standards.
* **How would you rate the service from the clinic? (clinic_service)**: Please select one of the options to indicate how you felt about the service from the clinic. This will help us understand how well we are meeting our service standards.
* **Would you recommend our clinic to a friend or family member? (will_recommend)**: Please select one of the options to indicate if you would recommend our clinic to someone else. This will help us understand our reputation and how we are meeting our patient referral standards.

## Tips
Please provide as much detail as possible in your responses, as this will help us understand your experience better. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
