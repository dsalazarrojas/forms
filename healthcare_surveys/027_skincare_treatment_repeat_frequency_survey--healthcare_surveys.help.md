# Skincare Treatment Repeat Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Treatment Repeat Frequency Survey is designed to gather information about your skincare treatment habits, preferences, and experiences. This survey will help us understand how often you visit a skincare treatment, what types of treatments you prefer, and what your overall experience has been like. This will help us to better understand our clients' needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Treatment Habits** (`client_treatment_habits`, text, optional):
  Please describe your typical skincare treatment habits, such as how often you visit a skincare treatment, what type of treatments you receive, and any other relevant information.
* **Preferred Visit Frequency** (`preferred_visit_frequency`, select_multiple, optional):
  Please select all the frequencies that apply to you:
  + Yes
  + No
* **Treatment Satisfaction** (`treatment_satisfaction`, text, optional):
  Please describe your overall satisfaction with your skincare treatments. If you have had positive experiences, please share what made them so great. If you have had negative experiences, please share what could be improved.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, select_one, optional):
  Please select your level of satisfaction with your skincare treatments:
  + Very satisfied
  + Somewhat satisfied
  + Neutral
  + Somewhat dissatisfied
  + Very dissatisfied
* **Recommended Treatment** (`recommended_treatment`, select_one, optional):
  Please select a treatment that you would recommend:
  + Skin Analysis
  + Chemical Peel
  + Microdermabrasion
  + Microdermabrasion with Chemical Peel
  + Other
* **Skincare Products** (`skincare_products`, text, optional):
  Please list any skincare products you use, such as creams, serums, or cleansers.
* **Treatment Frequency** (`treatment_frequency`, date, optional):
  Please select the date you last visited a skincare treatment:
* **Visit Satisfaction** (`visit_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional):
  Please select all the ways in which you were satisfied or dissatisfied with your visits:
  + Satisfied
  + Dissatisfied
* **Client Service Experience** (`client_service_experience`, text, optional):
  Please describe your overall experience with our skincare services, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Spa Facility** (`spa_facility`, text, optional):
  If you have any suggestions or comments about our spa facility, please share them here.
