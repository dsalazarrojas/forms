# Veterinary Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Technology Adoption Survey is designed to collect information about the current state of technology adoption in veterinary practices. This includes the types of technology used, the frequency of use, and the factors that influence its adoption. Your input is valuable in helping us understand how technology affects veterinary practices and inform future development and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask and we will do our best to clarify.
4. Take your time to complete the form thoroughly.

* **Introduction** (`1`, text, optional): This is a brief explanation of the survey and its purpose. Please do not answer this question.
* **tech_usage** (`2`, text, optional): Describe your current use of technology in your veterinary practice.
