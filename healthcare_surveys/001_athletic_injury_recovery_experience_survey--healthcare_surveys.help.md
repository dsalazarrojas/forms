This help guide will cover the Athletic Injury Recovery Experience Survey form. The form is designed to gather information from athletes on their recovery experience after an injury. The purpose of this form is to collect data on the athlete's personal information, injury details, recovery methods, treatment experience, and overall satisfaction with the treatment. This form will help us understand the effectiveness of different recovery methods and identify areas for improvement in the treatment process.

To ensure that this form is filled out correctly, please review the following instructions carefully. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to this guide for clarification.

The form is divided into several sections, each with its own specific questions. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form correctly.

## Purpose
The Athletic Injury Recovery Experience Survey is designed to collect data on athletes' recovery experience after an injury. This data will help us understand the effectiveness of different recovery methods and identify areas for improvement in the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section: Personal Information**
	* This section is where you will provide your personal information. Please enter your full name and age.
2. **Section: Injury Details**
	* Enter your athlete name and the type of injury you experienced (e.g., Sprain, Fracture, ACL Tear).
3. **Section: Recovery Experience**
	* Select all the recovery methods that were used to treat your injury (e.g., Physical Therapy, Surgery, Rest and Ice).
4. **Section: Treatment Experience**
	* Rate your satisfaction with the treatment you received (1 is very dissatisfied, 5 is very satisfied).
5. **Section: Pain Levels**
	* Rate your initial pain level at the time of the injury (1-10) and your current pain level after the recovery process (1-10).
6. **Section: Additional Feedback**
	* Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name**:
	+ Enter your full name, as this will help us identify you in our database.
* **Age**:
	+ Enter your age at the time of the injury.
* **Primary Sport**:
	+ Enter the primary sport you were participating in when the injury occurred.
* **Type of Injury**:
	+ Enter the type of injury you experienced (e.g., Sprain, Fracture, ACL Tear).
* **Date of Injury**:
	+ Enter the date of the injury.
* **Recovery Methods Used**:
	+ Select all the recovery methods that were used to treat your injury (e.g., Physical Therapy, Surgery, Rest and Ice, Chiropractic Care, Massage Therapy, Medication).
* **Primary Treatment Professional**:
	+ Select the primary treatment professional who treated your injury (e.g., Sports Physician, Physical Therapist, Surgeon, General Practitioner, Trainer).
* **Recovery Duration (in weeks)**:
	+ Enter the total time until you returned to your sport after the injury.
* **Satisfaction with Treatment**:
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the treatment you received (1 is very dissatisfied, 5 is very satisfied).
* **Initial Pain Level (1-10)**:
	+ Rate your initial pain level at the time of the injury.
* **Current Pain Level (1-10)**:
	+ Rate your current pain level after the recovery process.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments**:
	+ Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.
