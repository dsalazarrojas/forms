# Police Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather personal and sensitive information about police officers to ensure their well-being and performance on the job.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each section to understand the questions and ensure you provide accurate and honest answers.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, and provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question, consult with a supervisor or medical professional if necessary.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Officer Information** (`officer_information`, text, required: false): This section is meant to collect basic information about the officer, such as their name, badge number, and contact details. Please provide your name, badge number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or conditions that may impact your health or performance.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, required: false): Share any concerns or challenges you're facing related to mental health, including stress, anxiety, or other issues.
* **Physical Health** (`physical_health`, text, required: false): Report any physical health concerns, such as chronic conditions, injuries, or limitations that may affect your ability to perform your duties.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): List any existing or ongoing medical conditions that may impact your health or performance.
* **Substance Use** (`substance_use`, text, required: false): Disclose any substance use or abuse concerns, including nicotine, alcohol, or other substances.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): Indicate any allergies or sensitivities you have, including environmental or food allergies.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure accurate and effective support.
* If you have concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to a supervisor or medical professional.
* Remember that this form is designed to support your well-being and performance; it's not a form of discipline or punishment.
