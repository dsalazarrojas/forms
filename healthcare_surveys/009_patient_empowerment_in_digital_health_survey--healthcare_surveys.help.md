# patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to measure patient empowerment in the digital healthcare environment, focusing on patients' experiences and perceptions of their health data, digital tool accessibility, and related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select a category from the options provided.
2. Fill in the title of the digital health survey as it should appear in the output file.
3. Describe the purpose and scope of the digital health survey.
4. Select the category that best describes the type of survey you are creating.
5. Optionally, provide any additional help or instructions for users.
6. Choose the format(s) in which you would like the output to be saved (e.g., survey, PDF, CSV, JSON, XML, or TXT).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **healthcare_surveys** (`survey_category`, select_one): Select the category that best describes the type of digital health survey you are creating (e.g., "healthcare surveys", "health surveys", "surveys", "health", or "survey").
* **patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey** (`patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey`, text): Provide a descriptive title for the digital health survey.
* **patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey_description** (`patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey_description`, text): Enter a brief description of the digital health survey, including its purpose and scope.
* **patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey_category** (`patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey_category`, select_one): Select the category that best describes the type of digital health survey you are creating (e.g., "healthcare surveys", "health surveys", "surveys", "health", or "survey").
* **patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey_form_helps** (`patient_emptowerment_in_digital_health_survey_form_helps`, text): Optionally, provide additional help or instructions for users filling out the survey.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): Enter the name of the tool or software assigned for the survey, if applicable.
* **output_file** (`output_file`, select_multiple): Choose the format(s) in which you would like the output to be saved (e.g., "survey", "PDF", "CSV", "JSON", "XML", or "TXT").
