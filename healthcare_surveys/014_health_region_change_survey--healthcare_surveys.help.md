# Health Region Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Region Change Survey is designed to collect feedback from healthcare professionals on their experience with region transfers. This survey helps us understand areas of improvement and ensures that the region transfer process is efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the most appropriate response for each question.
3. If you experience any issues or have trouble filling out this form, please contact your healthcare organization's support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the reason for the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_question`, text, required)
	+ This question asks you to briefly describe the reason for the region transfer.
* **What is your satisfaction score for the region transfer?** (`satisfaction_score`, number, required)
	+ This question asks for your satisfaction score on a scale from 1-5.
* **What improvement opportunities do you see with the new region transfer process?** (`improvement_opportunity`, note, required)
	+ This question asks you to provide any suggestions or ideas for improving the region transfer process.
* **What do you think about the overall satisfaction with the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required)
	+ This question asks you to select all that apply from a list of satisfaction options.
* **If you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied, what date did you experience the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_satisfaction_date`, date, required)
	+ This question asks for the date you experienced the region transfer, if you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied.
* **If you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied, what time of day did you experience the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_satisfaction_time`, time, required)
	+ This question asks for the time of day you experienced the region transfer, if you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied.
* **What health care organization is this transfer with?** (`healthcare_organization`, text, required)
	+ This question asks you to provide the name of the health care organization involved in the transfer.
* **What is the region transfer date?** (`region_transfer_date`, date, required)
	+ This question asks for the date of the region transfer.
* **What is the region transfer time?** (`region_transfer_time`, time, required)
	+ This question asks for the time of the region transfer.
* **What is the reason for the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_reason`, select_one, required)
	+ This question asks you to select the reason for the region transfer from a list of options.
* **What was your location during the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_location`, text, required)
	+ This question asks you to describe your location during the region transfer.
* **If you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied, what date did you experience the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_satisfaction_date`, date, optional)
	+ This question asks for the date you experienced the region transfer, if you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied.
* **If you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied, what time of day did you experience the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_satisfaction_time`, time, optional)
	+ This question asks for the time of day you experienced the region transfer, if you selected dissatisfied or very dissatisfied.
* **What do you think about the overall satisfaction with the region transfer?** (`region_transfer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This question asks you to select all that apply from a list of satisfaction options.
* **What is the region transfer reason?** (`region_transfer_reason`, text, optional)
	+ This question asks you to provide a brief description of the reason for the region transfer.
* **What is the region transfer location?** (`region_transfer_location`, text, optional)
	+ This question asks you to describe your location during the region transfer.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you need to provide more information, use the "Other (please specify)" option where available.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Improvement Opportunities" field.
