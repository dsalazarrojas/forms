# Hospital Health Campaign Effectiveness Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Health Campaign Effectiveness Report is a form designed to evaluate the effectiveness of a health campaign in a hospital setting. It gathers information on various aspects of the campaign, including its name, dates, outcomes, and target audience. This report aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the campaign's performance and help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the campaign name, start date, and end date of the campaign.
* Provide a list of the outcomes measured during the campaign.
* Add any improvement ideas or suggestions for the campaign.
* Enter any submission comments or feedback on the campaign.
* Specify the location of the campaign.
* Choose a hospital and enter its name.
* Enter a list of goals for the campaign.
* List the outcomes of the campaign.
* Identify the target audience and number of target audience members.
* Enter information about the target population.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the health campaign being evaluated.
* **Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **End Date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **Outcomes Measured** (`outcomes_measured`, text, required): List the outcomes measured or achieved during the campaign.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Suggest ideas or suggestions for improving the campaign.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, required): Enter any submission comments or feedback on the campaign.
* **Location** (`campaign_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the campaign was conducted.
* **Hospital** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Choose the hospital where the campaign was held.
* **Goals** (`campaign_goals`, text, required): List the goals of the campaign.
* **Outcomes** (`campaign_outcomes`, text, required): Enter the outcomes of the campaign.
* **Target Audience** (`campaign_target_audience`, text, required): Identify the target audience of the campaign.
* **Number of Target Audience** (`campaign_target_audience_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of target audience members.
* **Target Population** (`target_population`, text, optional): Enter information about the target population.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the campaign.
