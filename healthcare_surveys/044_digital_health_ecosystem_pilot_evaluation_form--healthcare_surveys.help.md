# Digital Health Ecosystem Pilot Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Health Ecosystem Pilot Evaluation Form is a survey designed to gather feedback and information from teams and partners involved in the Digital Health Ecosystem Pilot project. This feedback will help us evaluate the pilot's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer each question as thoroughly and concisely as possible.
2.  If you are unsure about a question, use the "Other (please specify)" option where applicable.
3.  Be as concrete as possible when providing dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What benefits does this pilot provide?**: Describe the benefits you have experienced or observed during the pilot.
* **What challenges did you face during the pilot?**: Outline the difficulties you encountered during the pilot.
* **What improvements did you suggest or implement?**: Describe any suggestions or improvements you made or would like to make during the pilot.
* **What lessons or takeaways did you gain from the pilot?**: Share any important lessons or takeaways you learned from the pilot experience.
* **Who are the team members involved in this pilot?**: List the team members involved in the pilot, using the provided options or specify their roles if not listed.
* **Who are the partner organizations involved in this pilot?**: List the partner organizations involved in the pilot, using the provided options or specify them if not listed.
* **What roles do the partner organizations play in this pilot?**: Describe the roles each partner organization plays in the pilot.
* **How long did the pilot last?**: Enter the duration of the pilot in the format MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY.
* **When did the pilot start?**: Enter the start date of the pilot in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **When did the pilot end?**: Enter the end date of the pilot in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What is the current status of the pilot?**: Choose the status of the pilot from the provided options.
* **Any further comments?**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the pilot.
* **Contact email address**: Enter the email address of the contact person responsible for the pilot.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for the pilot.
* **Name of contact person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the pilot.
* **Title of contact person**: Enter the title of the contact person responsible for the pilot.
* **Email address of contact person**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone number of contact person**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Organization of contact person**: Enter the organization of the contact person.
* **Department of contact person**: Enter the department of the contact person.
* **Other contact title**: Enter the title of the contact person, if different from the one provided.
* **Other contact phone number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person, if different from the one provided.
* **Other contact email**: Enter the email address of the contact person, if different from the one provided.
* **Other contact organization**: Enter the organization of the contact person, if different from the one provided.
* **Other contact department**: Enter the department of the contact person, if different from the one provided.
