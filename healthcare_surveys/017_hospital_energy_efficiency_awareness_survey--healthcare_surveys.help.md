# Hospital Energy Efficiency Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the awareness and understanding of hospital staff regarding energy efficiency measures. It will help in identifying areas where staff requires improvement and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge. You can select one or multiple options for select fields. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hospital_energy_efficiency_survey**:
	+ This is the title of the survey, which you cannot edit.
* **energy_usage**:
	+ Please select 'Yes' if you use energy-efficient practices in your daily work.
	+ Please select 'No' if you do not use energy-efficient practices.
* **energy_consumption**:
	+ Please select the type of energy consumed in your department (Energy, Water, Electricity, Gas, Steam, Others).
* **building_location**:
	+ Please provide the location of your department in the hospital.
* **department**:
	+ Please select the department you belong to (Engineering, Maintenance, Operations, Others).
* **staff_size**:
	+ Please provide the number of staff members in your department.
* **time_of_day**:
	+ Please specify the time of the day you usually work.
* **awareness_level**:
	+ Please rate your level of awareness about energy efficiency (High, Medium, Low).
* **awareness_frequency**:
	+ Please rate how frequently you think about energy efficiency (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **awareness_source**:
	+ Please select where you usually get information about energy efficiency (Colleagues, Management, Training, Online Resources, Others).
* **awareness_level_awareness**:
	+ Please confirm if you are aware that energy efficiency is important (True, False).
* **awareness_level_awareness_source**:
	+ Please select where you got information about the importance of energy efficiency (Colleagues, Management, Training, Online Resources, Others).
* **suggestions_received**:
	+ Please select if you have received suggestions for energy efficiency (Yes, No).
* **energy_efficiency_measures**:
	+ Please select if energy efficiency measures have been implemented in your department (Yes, No).
* **implementation_status**:
	+ Please select the status of energy efficiency measures (In Progress, Done, Not Done).
* **implementation_status_source**:
	+ Please select where you got information about the implementation status of energy efficiency measures (Colleagues, Management, Training, Online Resources, Others).
* **implementation_progress_percentage**:
	+ Please provide a percentage indicating the progress of energy efficiency measures.
* **comments_or_suggestions**:
	+ Please provide any comments or suggestions you have about energy efficiency.
* **comments_or_suggestions_source**:
	+ Please select where you got information for comments or suggestions (Colleagues, Management, Training, Online Resources, Others).
* **next_steps**:
	+ Please provide any next steps or actions you plan to take regarding energy efficiency.
* **follow_up**:
	+ Please provide any follow-up questions or actions you plan to take regarding energy efficiency.
* **follow_up_source**:
	+ Please select where you got information for follow-up questions or actions (Colleagues, Management, Training, Online Resources, Others).
* **follow_up_status**:
	+ Please select the status of your follow-up questions or actions (In Progress, Done, Not Done).
* **follow_up_status_source**:
	+ Please select where you got information about the status of your follow-up questions or actions (Colleagues, Management, Training, Online Resources, Others).
* **follow_up_next_steps**:
	+ Please provide any next steps or actions you plan to take regarding energy efficiency.
* **<name>**:
	+ Please provide any other information or comments about energy efficiency.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
