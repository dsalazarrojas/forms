# Juvenile Spacing Survey - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page, which provides background information about the survey.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, selecting one of the provided options for each question.
3. For open-ended questions (e.g. question 8 and 9), provide as much detail as you feel is relevant to your experience.
4. Once you have answered all the questions, review your responses to ensure you have provided complete and accurate information.
