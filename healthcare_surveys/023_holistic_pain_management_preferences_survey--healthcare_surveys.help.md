# Holistic Pain Management Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an individual's pain management preferences, including their current pain level, medical treatment, and interest in holistic therapies. This data will help us better understand individual needs and provide personalized recommendations for managing pain.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form with the following steps:

1. Answer the select multiple questions (e.g., "Current Pain Level", "Pain Locations", "Medical Treatment", etc.) by selecting all that apply from the provided options.
2. For the text fields (e.g., "Medical History", "Other Pain Comments"), please enter any relevant information in the space provided.
3. For the date field ("Date of Birth"), enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. For the number fields (e.g., "Age", "Pain Score", "Pain Duration", "Pain Frequency"), please enter a whole number.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Notes" field.
