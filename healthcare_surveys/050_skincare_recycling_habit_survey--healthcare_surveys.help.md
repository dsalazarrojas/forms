# Skincare Recycling Habit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your skincare recycling habits. Your responses will help us understand how to improve recycling programs and promote sustainable skincare practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all the options that apply to you.
3. Take your time to read through each question carefully before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your skincare recycling habit?** (`1`, `text`, optional): This question is a text input field where you can describe your current skincare recycling habit. Please provide a brief answer, such as "I always recycle my skincare products" or "I occasionally recycle some of my skincare products".
* **Which skincare products do you use?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the types of skincare products you use. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Which materials are you recycling?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the types of recyclable materials you are currently recycling. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How often do you recycle?** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about how often you recycle. Please select one of the options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **What motivates you to recycle?** (`5`, `text`, optional): This question is a text input field where you can describe what motivates you to recycle. Please provide a brief answer, such as "I want to reduce my environmental impact" or "I want to save money".

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Take your time to read through each question carefully before submitting your response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
