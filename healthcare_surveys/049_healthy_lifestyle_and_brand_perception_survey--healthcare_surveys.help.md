# Healthy Lifestyle And Brand Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on individuals' healthy lifestyle habits and their perception of a specific brand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions according to your honest responses. You can choose one or multiple answers depending on the question type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false)
* Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. **Age** (`age`, `number`, required: false)
* Enter your age.

### 3. **Occupation** (`occupation`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Healthcare Professional
	+ Non-Healthcare Professional
	+ Student
	+ Other

### 4. **Healthcare Provider Experience** (`healthcare_provider_experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* Select all that apply:
	+ 1-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10-20 years
	+ 20+ years

### 5. **Brand Loyal** (`brand_loyalty`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Loyal
	+ Somewhat Loyal
	+ Not Loyal

### 6. **Brand Awareness** (`brand_awareness`, `number`, required: false)
* Enter your level of brand awareness.

### 7. **Section 1 - Lifestyle and Habits**
* This section is a brief introduction to the survey's second part.

### 8. **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Active
	+ Moderately Active
	+ Somewhat Active
	+ Not Active

### 9. **Sleep Pattern** (`sleep_pattern`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
* Select all that apply:
	+ 7-8 hours
	+ 8-9 hours
	+ 9-10 hours
	+ 10+ hours

### 10. **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ Rarely

### 11. **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Effective

### 12. **Nutrition** (`nutrition`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Balanced
	+ Mostly Healthy
	+ Mostly Unhealthy
	+ Very Unhealthy

### 13. **Section 2 - Brand Perception**
* This section is a brief introduction to the survey's second part.

### 14. **Brand Image** (`brand_image`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral

### 15. **Product Liking** (`product_liking`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Strongly Like
	+ Somewhat Like
	+ Neither Like Nor Dislike
	+ Somewhat Dislike
	+ Strongly Dislike

### 16. **Price Sensitivity** (`price_sensitivity`, `select_one`, required: false)
* Choose one of the following options:
	+ Not Sensitive
	+ Somewhat Sensitive
	+ Very Sensitive

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us.
