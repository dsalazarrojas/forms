# Healthcare Facility Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients regarding their experiences with a healthcare facility. This feedback is essential for the facility to understand areas of improvement and make necessary changes to provide better care and services to patients.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing patient information on the Main Page 2, including their contact details.
2. Answer the questions on the Main Page, such as whether you have visited the facility before and how your experience was.
3. If you have visited the facility, provide feedback on what they can improve on your next visit.
4. Finally, indicate if you would like to be contacted for follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page 2** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This page is where you enter your patient information, including your name, contact number, and any other relevant details. Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Patient Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your patient contact details in this field. This includes your phone number, address, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Have you ever visited this hospital or clinic?** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting 'Yes' if you have visited the facility before or 'No' if you haven't.
* **How was your overall experience with this healthcare facility?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What can we improve on your next visit?** (`question_3`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions or feedback on how the healthcare facility can improve, enter it in this field. Be as specific as possible so they can make informed changes.
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up information?** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer this question by selecting 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted or 'No' if you don't.
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience with the healthcare facility. This will help them understand your concerns and improve their services.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be specific when providing feedback, especially on what the healthcare facility can improve.
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up information.
