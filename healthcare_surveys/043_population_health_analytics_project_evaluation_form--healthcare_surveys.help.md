# population_health_analytics_project_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate projects related to population health analytics, gathering insights from various stakeholders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Answer all the required fields (project objectives, project challenges, future improvements, and project leadership) as clearly and concisely as possible.
* For the project outcomes field, select all the applicable outcomes that best describe the project's goals and results.
* For the project leadership field, choose one of the three options provided: Public Health Team, Hospitals, or Researchers.
* Optional fields (project outcomes, project collaboration, and feedback and comments) can be filled in as needed, but are not required.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Objectives** (`project_objectives`, text, required): Describe the project's main objectives, goals, or objectives.
* **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, select_multiple, required): Select all the outcomes that best describe the project's goals and results. Options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Project Challenges** (`project_challenges`, text, required): Describe the project's challenges or obstacles.
* **Future Improvements** (`future_improvements`, text, required): Suggest potential improvements for future projects.
* **Project Leadership** (`project_leadership`, select_one, required): Choose one of the three leadership options: Public Health Team, Hospitals, or Researchers.
* **Project Collaboration** (`project_collaboration`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about project collaboration.
* **Feedback and Comments** (`feedback_and_comments`, note, optional): Offer any additional comments or suggestions.
