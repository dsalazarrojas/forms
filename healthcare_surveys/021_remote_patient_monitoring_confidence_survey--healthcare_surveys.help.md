# Remote Patient Monitoring Confidence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Remote Patient Monitoring Confidence Survey is designed to gauge the confidence of patients in their remote monitoring program. This form is used to gather information about patients' confidence in their ability to use remote monitoring technology and receive care remotely. The survey is intended to help healthcare providers understand patient confidence levels and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and select the answer that best matches your confidence level for each question.
2. Select "Yes" if you are confident in your ability to use remote monitoring technology.
3. Select "No" if you are not confident in your ability to use remote monitoring technology.
4. Submit the form once you have answered all questions.
