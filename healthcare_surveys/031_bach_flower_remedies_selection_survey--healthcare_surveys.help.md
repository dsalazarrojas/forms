# Bach Flower Remedies Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's emotional state, symptoms, and desired outcomes from Bach flower remedies.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the **Client name** field, if you would like to identify yourself as the client. Note that this field is not required.
2.  Next, select the date of the survey in the **Assessment date** field. This is a required field.
3.  Choose the primary emotional states you are currently experiencing from the list in the **Primary emotional state** field. You can select multiple emotions.
4.  Select the level of severity of your symptoms in the **Symptom severity** field, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
5.  Choose the level of your recent sleep quality in the **Sleep quality** field, which can be poor, fair, good, or excellent.
6.  Choose the primary stress triggers you are experiencing from the **Primary stress triggers** field. You can select multiple triggers.
7.  If you would like to describe what you hope to achieve from using the remedies, type in the **Desired outcome** field.
8.  If you have used previous Bach flower or other remedies, mention this in the **Prior remedy use** field.
9.  If you have any additional comments or information for the clinician, type them in the **Practitioner notes** field.
10. Finally, select **Consent to treatment and record keeping** to indicate your consent to treatment and record keeping.
