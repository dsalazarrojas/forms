# Medical Career Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Career Motivation Survey is a form designed to gather information about the motivations behind individuals' career choices in the medical field. This survey will help us understand the driving factors influencing people's decisions to pursue a career in medicine. Your participation will contribute to a better understanding of the medical career motivations and help shape future initiatives in this field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each question, provide a brief description of your motivations and influences.
3. Select the appropriate options for the questions that require multiple or single-choice answers.
4. Submit your survey once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical career motivation** (`Medical career motivation`): This is a text field where you can describe your motivations for pursuing a career in medicine.
* **School influence** (`School influence`): This is a text field where you can share how your school experiences or influences have contributed to your decision to pursue a medical career.
* **Role model** (`Role model`): This is a text field where you can identify a person who has been a significant influence on your career choices, such as a medical professional or family member.
* **Career pathway** (`Career pathway`): This is a text field where you can describe the path you took to get to where you are in your medical career.
* **Perceived challenges** (`Perceived challenges`): This is a text field where you can share any challenges you have faced or anticipate facing in your medical career.
* **Role model influence** (`Role model influence`): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select from the following options:
	+ High school counselor
	+ Parent
	+ Medical professional
	+ Other
* **Career choice** (`Career choice`): This is a single-choice field where you can select from the following options:
	+ Family
	+ Personal fulfillment
	+ Financial gain
	+ Other
	+ Uncertain
* **Role model characteristics** (`Role model characteristics`): This is a text field where you can describe the characteristics of the person who has been a significant influence on your career choices.
