# Provider Network Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from healthcare providers for onboarding into our network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information about your organization and contact person.
2. Choose your organization type and onboarding readiness from the provided options.
3. Enter your onboarding date and any follow-up questions or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your organization's onboarding survey.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Type in the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your organization type from the provided options (HOSPITAL, CLINIC, MEDICAL GROUP, or OTHER).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the contact person responsible for your organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact person's email address.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to onboard onto our network.
* **Survey Response** (`survey_response`, text, required): Provide a brief response or message for our review.
* **Onboarding Feedback** (`onboarding_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Readiness** (`onboarding_readiness`, select_one, required): Select your onboarding readiness from the provided options (NOT READY, READY).
* **Follow Up Questions** (`follow_up_questions`, note, required): Enter any follow-up questions or concerns you may have about the onboarding process.

Note: All required fields must be completed before submitting the form.
