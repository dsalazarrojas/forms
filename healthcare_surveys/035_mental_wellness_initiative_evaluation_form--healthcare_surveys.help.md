# Mental Wellness Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from program participants regarding their experiences and outcomes in the mental wellness initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required questions (denoted by *required*).
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into consideration the provided options.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
6. Rate your experience in the "Rating" field.
7. If you have additional comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Participant Info** (`program_participant_info`, text, required/optional): This field asks for general information about you as a program participant. Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, required/optional): In this field, describe your experience with the mental wellness initiative in as much detail as possible.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your experience with the mental wellness initiative:
	+ option 1: Highly Satisfied
	+ option 2: Satisfied
	+ option 3: Neutral
	+ option 4: Dissatisfied
	+ option 5: Highly Dissatisfied
* **Program Outcome** (`program_outcome`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the outcomes that apply to your experience with the mental wellness initiative:
	+ option 1: Improved mental health
	+ option 2: Increased confidence
	+ option 3: Better coping mechanisms
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, required/optional): If you have any suggestions or improvements for the mental wellness initiative, enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have anything else to add, enter it here.
* **Participant Survey** (`participant_survey`, time, required): Fill out the time field, if applicable.
