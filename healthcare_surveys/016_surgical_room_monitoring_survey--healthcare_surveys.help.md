# Surgical Room Monitoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgical Room Monitoring Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from healthcare professionals about their experience with the surgical room monitoring system. The survey aims to gather information on the ease of use, relevance, and overall quality of the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with a time field (e.g., "How many minutes did it take to complete this survey?"), please enter the actual time taken, including any fractions of minutes.
3. For questions with a select_one, select_multiple, or select_one options (e.g., "Was the form easy to navigate?"), choose one or more options that best describe your experience.
4. For questions with a note field (e.g., "Do you have any additional comments?"), provide any relevant comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How many minutes did it take to complete this survey?** (question2): Enter the actual time it took you to complete the survey.
* **Was the form easy to navigate?** (question3): Rate how easy or difficult it was to complete the form. Choose one or more options that apply:
	+ Option 1: Very easy
	+ Option 2: Somewhat easy
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **What would you rate the overall experience?** (question4): Rate your overall experience with the surgical room monitoring system:
	+ Option 1: 1 (Very dissatisfied)
	+ Option 2: 2 (Somewhat dissatisfied)
	+ Option 3: 3 (Neutral)
	+ Option 4: 4 (Somewhat satisfied)
	+ Option 5: 5 (Very satisfied)
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (question5): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **What time of day did you take the survey?** (question6): Enter the actual time you took the survey.
* **Was the survey relevant to your work?** (question7): Rate how relevant the survey was to your work. Choose one or more options that apply:
	+ Option 1: Very relevant
	+ Option 2: Somewhat relevant
	+ Option 3: Neutral
* **Would you like to be contacted for further discussion?** (question8): Choose one option that best describes your preference:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **How would you rate the quality of the form design?** (question9): Rate the quality of the form design:
	+ Option 1: 1 (Very poor)
	+ Option 2: 2 (Poor)
	+ Option 3: 3 (Neutral)
	+ Option 4: 4 (Good)
	+ Option 5: 5 (Excellent)
* **Do you work in a surgical setting?** (question10): Indicate whether you work in a surgical setting:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **What type of facility do you work in?** (question11): Choose one or more options that best describe the type of facility where you work:
	+ Option 1: Hospital
	+ Option 2: Clinic
* **Additional feedback** (question12): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
