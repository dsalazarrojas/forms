# Home Care Worker Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by agencies to gather information from home care workers, including their contact information, availability, and preferences for follow-up contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
3. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
4. Sign and date the form, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Home Care Worker Intake (text)
This field is where you can provide a brief description of your background and experience as a home care worker.

### 2. Caregiver Info (select_one)
Select 'Yes' if you are willing to provide care for the agency, or 'No' if you are not.
### 3. Contact Info (select_multiple)
Select any relevant contact methods you prefer, such as Phone or Email.

### 4. Address (text)
Enter your address, including street number and name.

### 5. Date of Contact (date)
Enter the date you would be available for a follow-up contact.

### 6. Time of Contact (time)
Enter the time you would be available for a follow-up contact.

### 7. Contact Method (select_one)
Select how you would prefer to be contacted, such as Phone, Email, or In-person.

### 8. Agency Contact Name (text)
Enter the name of the agency contact person or representative.

### 9. Agency Contact Number (text)
Enter the contact number of the agency.

### 10. Caregiver Signature (note)
This is where you can add a note or comment about your contact information or experience.

### 11. Agency Signature (note)
This is where the agency contact person or representative can add a note or comment.

### 12. Notes (text)
Enter any additional notes or comments about your contact information or experience.

### 13. Follow-up Tasks (select_multiple)
Select any follow-up tasks you would be available for, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 14. Next Follow-up Date (date)
Enter the date of your next follow-up contact.

### 15. Next Follow-up Time (time)
Enter the time of your next follow-up contact.

### 16. Next Follow-up Method (select_one)
Select how you would prefer to be contacted for your next follow-up, such as Phone, Email, or In-person.

### 17. Next Follow-up Contact (text)
Enter the name or title of the person to contact for your next follow-up.

### 18. Next Follow-up Location (text)
Enter the location of your next follow-up contact.

### 19. Next Follow-up Tasks (select_multiple)
Select any follow-up tasks you would be available for, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 20. Follow-up Notes (text)
Enter any notes or comments about your follow-up contacts.

### 21. Completed Follow-ups (select_multiple)
Select any completed follow-ups, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by home care workers and agency staff. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your agency representative.
