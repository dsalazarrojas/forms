## Purpose
The Dental Anesthesia Complications Survey is designed to collect information about complications that have occurred during anesthesia procedures in a dental setting. This form helps gather data on the types of complications and their severity to aid in the improvement of patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
 1. Select the complications that occurred during the patient's anesthesia procedure.
  2. Enter the patient's dose, if applicable.
  3. Enter the dose unit, if applicable.
  4. Enter the dose administered, if applicable.
  5. Select any further complications from the options provided.
  6. Provide any additional details about each complication.
  7. Choose the submit option to finalize your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dental Anesthesia Survey** (`survey_start`): This is the starting page of the survey, which lists the complications that could have occurred during the patient's anesthesia procedure. Choose all the complications that apply.
* **Patient's Dose** (`patient_dose`): Enter the amount of anesthesia given to the patient, if applicable.
* **Dose Unit** (`anes_dose_unit`): Enter the unit of measurement for the dose administered (e.g., mg, mL).
* **Dose Administered** (`anes_dose_administered`): Enter the actual amount of anesthesia given to the patient, if applicable.
* **Anesthesia Complication 1** (`anes_complication_1`): Select the type of complication that occurred during the patient's anesthesia procedure.
* **Anesthesia Complication 1 Details** (`anes_complication_1_details`): Enter any additional details about the complication that occurred.
* **Anesthesia Complications 2** (`anes_complications_2`): Select any additional complications that occurred during the patient's anesthesia procedure.
* **Anesthesia Complications 2 Details** (`anes_complications_2_details`): Enter any additional details about the complications that occurred.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Choose this option to finalize and submit your input.
