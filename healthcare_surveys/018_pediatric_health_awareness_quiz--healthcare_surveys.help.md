# Pediatric Health Awareness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Health Awareness Quiz is designed to gauge the knowledge and attitudes of healthcare providers regarding pediatric health topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your age to determine your qualification to participate in the quiz.
2. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further information.
3. Indicate your level of education to help us understand your background.
4. Answer a series of questions about your participation in quizzes and attitudes towards healthcare providers.
5. Complete any additional demographic information as required.
6. Finally, provide your submission details, including date and time of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_age** (`Age`, `number`, required): Enter your age to determine your qualification to participate in the quiz.
* **participant_email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further information.
* **participant_education_level** (`Education Level`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of education (High or Low) to help us understand your background.
* **quiz_completed** (`Completed Quiz`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have completed the quiz.
* **quiz_score** (`Quiz Score`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer if you completed the quiz and if you scored well.
* **quiz_result** (`Quiz Result`, `note`, required): Enter any comments or feedback about the quiz.
* **participant_first_name** (`Participant First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify you as a participant.
* **participant_survey_id** (`Participant Survey ID`, `text`, required): Enter a survey ID to track your participation.
* **survey_completed** (`Survey Completed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you completed the survey.
* **survey_completed_date** (`Survey Completed Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of survey completion.
* **survey_completed_time** (`Survey Completed Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of survey completion.
* **survey_completed_by** (`Survey Completed By`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to confirm completion.
* **survey_completed_ip_address** (`Survey Completed IP Address`, `text`, required): Enter the IP address of the device used to complete the survey.
* **submitted_survey_id** (`Submitted Survey ID`, `text`, required): Enter a survey ID to track your submission.
* **quiz_participation** (`Quiz Participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have participated in a quiz.
* **participant_healthcare_provider** (`Participant Healthcare Provider`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you are a healthcare provider.
* **submitted_by** (`Submitted By`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to confirm submission.
* **ip_address** (`IP Address`, `text`, required): Enter the IP address of the device used to submit.
* **quiz_duration** (`Quiz Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the length of time taken to complete the quiz.
* **score_out_of** (`Score Out of`, `number`, required): Enter the total score for the quiz.
* **score_value** (`Score Value`, `number`, required): Enter the value of your score.
* **survey_quiz_participation** (`Survey Quiz Participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you participated in a quiz.
* **healthcare_provider_survey_completed** (`Healthcare Provider Survey Completed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you completed the survey as a healthcare provider.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and the explanations are based on the field labels and types. The actual purpose and use of the form may vary based on the intended use and context.
