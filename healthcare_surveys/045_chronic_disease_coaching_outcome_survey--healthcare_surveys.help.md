# Chronic Disease Coaching Outcome Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Coaching Outcome Survey is designed to gather feedback and outcomes from participants who have received coaching services. The survey aims to evaluate the effectiveness of the coaching program, identify areas of improvement, and collect feedback on program quality and participant experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are in a quiet and comfortable environment to focus on completing the survey.
2. Read each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
3. For select-one and select-multiple questions, choose the option that best reflects your experience.
4. Use the "Select all that apply" feature for questions with multiple options.
5. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID** (required): Enter your participant ID, a unique identifier for your coaching program.
* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter your email address, which may be verified by the program administrators.
* **Phone Number** (optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here (but it's not required).
* **Coaching Program Name** (required): Enter the name of the coaching program you participated in.
* **Program Duration** (optional): Enter the duration of the coaching program you participated in.
* **Program Start Date** (required): Enter the start date of the coaching program.
* **Program End Date** (required): Enter the end date of the coaching program.
* **How would you rate your condition management?** (required): Choose how you rate your condition management from the options provided.
* **Have your symptoms improved?** (required): Choose how much your symptoms have improved from the options provided.
* **How has your quality of life changed?** (required): Choose how your quality of life has changed from the options provided.
* **Has medication adherence improved?** (optional): Choose whether medication adherence has improved, if applicable.
* **Has your physical activity level changed?** (required): Choose how your physical activity level has changed from the options provided.
* **Coaching Satisfaction** (optional): Enter your satisfaction with the coaching services received.
* **How satisfied are you with your coach?** (required): Choose how satisfied you are with your coach.
* **How effective was the coaching in helping you?** (required): Choose how effective the coaching was in helping you.
* **Did you achieve your health goals?** (required): Choose whether you achieved your health goals.
* **Which areas were most helpful?** (optional): Select all the areas that were most helpful to you during the coaching program.
* **Areas for Improvement** (optional): Enter any areas that could improve the coaching program.
* **What could improve the coaching program?** (optional): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Would you recommend this program to others?** (required): Choose whether you would recommend this program to others.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you have any difficulty or need clarification, contact the program administrators.
* If you need assistance with filling out the survey, you can ask for help from the program staff.
