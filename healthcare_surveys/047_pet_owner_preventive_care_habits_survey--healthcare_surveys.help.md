# pet_owner_preventive_care_habits_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand pet owners' preventive care habits for their pets, specifically on vaccinations, flea and tick preventatives, and veterinary check-ups.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, considering the best practice for your pet.
2. Select one or multiple options for each field, as per the field type.
3. Submit the form to complete your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Vaxinations** (`pet_vaccination`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks whether you vaccinate your pet or not. Please select 'Yes' if you regularly vaccinate your pet, or 'No' otherwise.
- **Flea and Tick Preventions** (`parasite_prevention`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Here, you can select all the flea and tick preventatives you use for your pet, such as flea and tick medication, or 'No' if you don't use any.
- **Check-up Routines** (`veterinary_checkups`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you regularly take your pet to the vet for check-ups, or 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your response, as this information will help us provide better services for pets.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- This form is designed to be quick and easy to complete; it should take only a few minutes to complete.
