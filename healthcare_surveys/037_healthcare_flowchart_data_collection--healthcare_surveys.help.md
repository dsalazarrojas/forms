# Healthcare Flowchart Data Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects data for healthcare flowcharts, which are visual representations of patient care pathways.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read the form carefully and only use this form if you're authorized to do so.
2.  Fill in the required fields (if any) and provide accurate information.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare professional.
4.  Submit the completed form for review and further action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Concerns (text)**: This field is where you can list any concerns or issues that the patient has.
* **Symptoms (select_multiple)**: Here, you can select any symptoms that the patient is experiencing.
* **Medical History (text)**: Describe the patient's medical history, if applicable.
* **Contact Details (select_one)**: Choose 'Yes' if you have the patient's contact details, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical Specialties (select_multiple)**: Select any medical specialties that the patient has, e.g., cardiologist, oncologist, etc.
* **Contact Preferences (select_one)**: Choose 'Yes' if the patient has specific contact preferences, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Medication Details (text)**: Describe any medication the patient is taking, if applicable.
* **Medical History Details (text)**: Provide any additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Contact Preferences2 (select_one)**: This is another question asking about contact preferences.
* **Medication Details 2 (text)**: Describe any further medication the patient is taking.
* **Contact Number (text)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Contact Email (text)**: Enter the patient's contact email address.
