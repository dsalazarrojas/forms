# Public Health Data Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Data Ethics Survey is designed to collect feedback from participants on their perceptions and experiences with health data. This survey aims to ensure that the data collection process is transparent, user-friendly, and meets the needs of its users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as honestly and accurately as possible.
2. Select the options that best reflect your thoughts and feelings.
3. Provide as much detail as you need to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Health Data Ethics Survey** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the title of the survey.
* **What do you do in your daily work or activities?** (`2`, `text`, required): Please describe your daily work or activities.
* **What are your demographic information (age, gender, occupation)?** (`3`, `text`, required): Please provide your age, gender, and occupation.
* **How do you feel about using anonymous health data?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply. Options: 
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **How important do you feel about health data privacy?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply. Options: 
	+ Very important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important
* **What concerns do you have about using anonymous health data?** (`6`, `text`, required): Please describe your concerns.
* **What do you think about the use of anonymous health data?** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select one option. Options: 
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **How likely are you to use anonymous health data?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply. Options: 
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Not likely
* **How do you feel about your own participation in the survey?** (`9`, `text`, optional): Please describe your feelings.
* **How satisfied are you with the survey?** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply. Options: 
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Would you like to be informed about any changes in your health data?** (`11`, `text`, optional): Please describe how you would like to be informed.
* **Would you recommend this survey to others?** (`12`, `text`, optional): Please explain why or why not.
* **How would you like to be informed about the use of your health data?** (`13`, `text`, optional): Please describe how you would like to be informed.
* **How would you like to be informed about any changes in your health data?** (`14`, `text`, optional): Please describe how you would like to be informed.
