# Global Health Data Governance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Health Data Governance Evaluation Form is a self-assessment tool designed to evaluate your organization's global health data governance practices. This form will help you rate and identify areas for improvement in data privacy policies, security measures, data quality standards, data sharing arrangements, stakeholder involvement, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For text fields, provide detailed descriptions or explanations for each answer.
3. For multiple-choice fields, select the options that best describe your current practice or approach.
4. For optional fields, provide answers only if you feel they are relevant to your situation.
5. Take your time, and don't hesitate to seek assistance if you need help completing this form.
