# Patient Clinician Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Clinician Communication Survey is a tool designed to help healthcare providers assess and improve the communication between patients and clinicians. It's a self-reported questionnaire that gathers information on the effectiveness of communication in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the survey questions carefully, as they are meant to gather your honest opinions on your experiences with patient-clinician communication.
2. Answer each question truthfully, based on your genuine experiences and observations.
3. Select the options that best match your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Introduction**: This page provides a brief introduction to the survey and instructions on how to complete it.
* **2. Patient-Provider Communication Questionnaire**: This section asks about your experiences with communication with your healthcare provider. Please select 'Yes' if you have had positive experiences, and 'No' if you have not.
* **3. Communication Balance Questionnaire**: This section asks about the balance of communication in your interactions with healthcare providers. Please select 'Yes' if you feel communication is balanced, and 'No' if you do not.
* **4. Patient-Clinician Communication Satisfaction**: This section asks about your satisfaction with communication with healthcare providers. Please select 'Satisfied' if you're satisfied, and 'Dissatisfied' if you are not.
* **5. Clinician-Patient Communication Satisfaction**: This section is similar to the previous one, but focuses on your satisfaction with communication from healthcare providers.
* **6. Communication Skill Building**: This section asks about the development of communication skills in healthcare providers. Please select 'Yes' if you've seen improvement, and 'No' if you haven't.
* **7. Patient-Clinician Communication Preference**: This section asks about your preference for communication with healthcare providers. Please select 'Yes' if you prefer this type of communication, and 'No' if you do not.
* **8. Clinician-Patient Communication Preference**: Similar to the previous question, but focuses on your preference for communication from healthcare providers.
* **9. Clinician-Patient-Clinician Communication Preference**: This section asks about your preference for communication among healthcare providers.
* **10. Patient-Clinician Communication Questions**: This section asks about communication questions asked by healthcare providers. Please select 'Yes' if you've been asked, and 'No' if you haven't.
* **11. Clinician-Patient Communication Questions**: Similar to the previous question, but focuses on questions from healthcare providers.
* **12. Clinician-Clinician Patient Communication Questions**: This section asks about communication questions asked by healthcare providers about patients.
* **13. Clinician Communication Preference**: This section asks about your preference for communication from healthcare providers.
* **14. Patient-Clinician Communication Balance Questions**: This section asks about balance in communication between you and healthcare providers. Please select 'Yes' if you feel balanced, and 'No' if you don't.
* **15. Clinician-Patient-Clinician Communication Balance Questions**: Similar to the previous question, but focuses on balance in communication among healthcare providers.
* **16. Clinician-Clinician-Patient Communication Preference**: This section asks about your preference for communication among healthcare providers.
* **17. Clinician Communication Preference Questions**: This section asks about communication preferences from healthcare providers.
* **18. Patient-Clinician Communication Balance**: This section asks about balance in communication between you and healthcare providers.
* **19. Clinician-Clinician-Patient Communication Balance**: Similar to the previous question, but focuses on balance in communication among healthcare providers.
* **20. Clinician-Patient-Clinician Communication Score**: This section asks about your score for communication among healthcare providers.
* **21. Clinician-Patient-Clinician Communication Score 2**: Similar to the previous question, but with a focus on your second set of scores.
* **22. Clinician-Clinician-Clinician Communication Score 2**: This section asks about your scores for communication among healthcare providers.
* **23. Clinician-Patient Communication Score**: This section asks about your score for communication from healthcare providers.
* **24. Clinician-Patient-Clinician Communication Score 2**: Similar to the previous question, but with a focus on your second set of scores.
* **25. Clinician-Patient-Clinician Communication Score 3**: This section asks about your score for communication among healthcare providers.
* **Tips**: Please take your time to carefully answer each question, and do not hesitate to ask for clarification if you're unsure about any of the questions or options.
