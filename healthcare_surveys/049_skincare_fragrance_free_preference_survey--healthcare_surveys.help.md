# Skincare Fragrance Free Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your skin type, concerns, and preferences to help us better understand your skincare needs and preferences, specifically regarding fragrance-free products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, selecting the options that best describe your skin type, concerns, and preferences.
2. You may select multiple options for each question.
3. When completed, click the submit button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): This question helps us understand your age demographic.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of skin you have:
	+ Normal: If you have balanced and healthy-looking skin with minimal imperfections.
	+ Dry: If you have skin that tends to be dry and flaky.
	+ Oily: If you have skin that tends to be oily and shiny.
	+ Combination: If you have a mix of dry and oily skin.
	+ Sensitive: If you have skin that reacts easily to products or environmental factors.
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the concerns you have about your skin:
	+ Aging: Fine lines, wrinkles, or loss of skin elasticity.
	+ Acne: Whiteheads, blackheads, or inflamed skin.
	+ Dullness: Skin that looks dull or lackluster.
	+ Hyper-Pigmentation: Dark spots or patches on the skin.
	+ Large pores: Enlarged pores that make skin appear uneven.
	+ Pore size variation: Pores that are either too large or too small.
* **Fragrance Allergy** (`fragrance_allergy`, select_one, optional): Do you have a fragrance allergy?
	+ True: Yes, you have a fragrance allergy.
	+ False: No, you do not have a fragrance allergy.
* **Fragrance Preference** (`fragrance_preference`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your fragrance preference:
	+ None: You prefer no fragrance or perfumes.
	+ Strong: You prefer fragrances that are highly scented.
	+ Medium: You prefer fragrances that are moderately scented.
	+ Light: You prefer fragrances that are lightly scented.
* **Skincare Product Use** (`skincare_product_use`, select_multiple, optional): Please select where you use skincare products:
	+ Face: You use skincare products on your face.
	+ Body: You use skincare products on your body.
	+ Both: You use skincare products on both your face and body.
* **Skin Condition** (`skin_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Please select any skin conditions you have:
	+ Acne: Whiteheads, blackheads, or inflamed skin.
	+ Aging: Fine lines, wrinkles, or loss of skin elasticity.
	+ Dullness: Skin that looks dull or lackluster.
	+ Hyper-Pigmentation: Dark spots or patches on the skin.
	+ Oily: Skin that tends to be oily and shiny.
	+ Sensitive: Skin that reacts easily to products or environmental factors.
	+ Rosacea: A type of skin condition characterized by visible blood vessels and redness.
	+ Eczema: A type of skin condition characterized by inflammation and irritation.
* **Preferred Skincare Product** (`preferred_skincare_product`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your preferred skincare products:
	+ Moisturizer: Products that hydrate and moisturize the skin.
	+ Toner: Products that help balance skin pH and tighten pores.
	+ Exfoliator: Products that remove dead skin cells and smooth skin texture.
	+ Sunscreen: Products that protect skin from UV rays.
	+ Serum: Products that target specific skin concerns.
	+ Face Mask: Products that provide an intense dose of hydration or treatment.
* **Preferred Fragrance Product** (`preferred_fragrance_product`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your preferred fragrance-free products:
	+ None: You prefer no fragrance-free products or perfumes.
	+ Moisturizer: Fragrance-free moisturizers that hydrate and moisturize the skin.
	+ Toner: Fragrance-free toners that balance skin pH and tighten pores.
	+ Exfoliator: Fragrance-free exfoliators that remove dead skin cells and smooth skin texture.
	+ Sunscreen: Fragrance-free sunscreens that protect skin from UV rays.
	+ Serum: Fragrance-free serums that target specific skin concerns.
	+ Face Mask: Fragrance-free face masks that provide an intense dose of hydration or treatment.
* **Skincare Frequency** (`skincare_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Please select how often you use skincare products:
	+ Daily: You use skincare products every day.
	+ As-needed: You use skincare products only when your skin needs it.
	+ Weekly: You use skincare products once a week.
	+ Rarely: You rarely use skincare products.
* **Fragrance Frequency** (`fragrance_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Please select how often you use fragrances:
	+ Daily: You use fragrances every day.
	+ As-needed: You use fragrances only when you feel like it.
	+ Weekly: You use fragrances once a week.
	+ Rarely: You rarely use fragrances.
* **Skincare Pain Level** (`skincare_pain_level`, select_one, optional): How much pain do you experience from skincare products?
	+ None: You do not experience any pain from skincare products.
	+ Light: You experience mild pain or discomfort from skincare products.
	+ Moderate: You experience moderate pain or discomfort from skincare products.
	+ Severe: You experience severe pain or discomfort from skincare products.
* **Fragrance Pain Level** (`fragrance_pain_level`, select_one, optional): How much pain do you experience from fragrances?
	+ None: You do not experience any pain from fragrances.
	+ Light: You experience mild pain or discomfort from fragrances.
	+ Moderate: You experience moderate pain or discomfort from fragrances.
	+ Severe: You experience severe pain or discomfort from fragrances.
* **Skincare Pain Location** (`skincare_pain_location`, select_multiple, optional): Where do you experience pain from skincare products?
	+ Face: You experience pain on your face.
	+ Neck: You experience pain on your neck.
	+ Hands: You experience pain on your hands.
	+ Body: You experience pain on your body.
* **Fragrance Pain Location** (`fragrance_pain_location`, select_multiple, optional): Where do you experience pain from fragrances?
	+ Face: You experience pain on your face.
	+ Neck: You experience pain on your neck.
	+ Hands: You experience pain on your hands.
	+ Body: You experience pain on your body.
* **Skincare Pain Duration** (`skincare_pain_duration`, select_one, optional): How long do you experience pain from skincare products?
	+ None: You do not experience any pain from skincare products.
	+ Less than 24 hours: You experience pain for less than 24 hours.
	+ 24 hours to 1 week: You experience pain for 24 hours to 1 week.
	+ 1-2 weeks: You experience pain for 1-2 weeks.
	+ More than 2 weeks: You experience pain for more than 2 weeks.

## Tips
- If you experience skin problems or concerns, please let us know when they occurred and how long they have lasted.
- Please provide as much detail as you can about your skin conditions, concerns, or fragrance preferences.
- Your answers will help us improve skincare products that meet your needs and preferences.
