# Health Equity Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about health equity programs and evaluate their effectiveness in promoting health outcomes. This form will help us assess the progress and impact of these programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each page, marked with an asterisk (*). This information includes:
	* Program Name: The name of the health equity program.
	* Organization: The name of the organization or institution running the program.
	* Location: The location where the program is being implemented.
	* Contact Person: The person responsible for the program.
	* Start Date: The date when the program started.
	* End Date: The date when the program ended (if applicable).
	* Total Participants: The number of participants enrolled in the program.
	* Male Participants: The number of male participants in the program.
	* Female Participants: The number of female participants in the program.
	* Other Participants: The number of participants who are neither male nor female (e.g. non-binary, transgender, etc.).
	* Program Objectives: A description of the program's goals and objectives.
	* Health Outcomes: A description of the program's health outcomes and results.
	* Evaluation Tools: A description of the tools and methods used to evaluate the program.
	* Program Evaluation: A choice of pre-defined evaluation frameworks or other (with a text field for specifying).
	* Overall Impact: A selection of the program's overall impact (High, Medium, Low).
2. For the optional field, "Other (please specify)", you may provide additional information not covered in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: The name of the health equity program. This is a required field that helps us identify the program.
* **Organization**: The name of the organization or institution running the program. This is a required field that helps us understand who is implementing the program.
* **Location**: The location where the program is being implemented. This is a required field that helps us know where the program is taking place.
* **Contact Person**: The person responsible for the program. This is a required field that helps us contact the person in charge of the program.
* **Start Date**: The date when the program started. This is a required field that helps us understand when the program began.
* **End Date**: The date when the program ended (if applicable). This is a required field that helps us understand when the program ended.
* **Total Participants**: The number of participants enrolled in the program. This is a required field that helps us understand the scope of the program.
* **Male Participants**: The number of male participants in the program. This is a required field that helps us understand the demographics of the program.
* **Female Participants**: The number of female participants in the program. This is a required field that helps us understand the demographics of the program.
* **Other Participants**: The number of participants who are neither male nor female (e.g. non-binary, transgender, etc.). This is a required field that helps us understand the demographics of the program.
* **Program Objectives**: A description of the program's goals and objectives. This is a required field that helps us understand what the program aims to achieve.
* **Health Outcomes**: A description of the program's health outcomes and results. This is a required field that helps us understand the program's impact on health.
* **Evaluation Tools**: A description of the tools and methods used to evaluate the program. This is a required field that helps us understand how the program is being assessed.
* **Program Evaluation**: A choice of pre-defined evaluation frameworks or other (with a text field for specifying). This is a required field that helps us understand how the program is being evaluated.
* **Overall Impact**: A selection of the program's overall impact (High, Medium, Low). This is a required field that helps us understand the program's effectiveness.
* **Other (please specify)**: An optional field to provide any additional information not covered in the other fields.

## Tips
* Please fill in accurate and relevant information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program contact person for clarification.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: The fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields that must be filled in for the form to be complete.
