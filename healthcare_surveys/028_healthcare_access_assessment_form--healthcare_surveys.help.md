# Healthcare Access Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess healthcare access, identify barriers to access, and track follow-up activities for patients or individuals in need of healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the Access to Health Services section, which collects information about your access to healthcare services.
2. Answer the questions in the Follow-up section, which tracks follow-up activities and progress.
3. Fill out any additional demographic information and contact information you may have.
4. Enter the follow-up date and time, and any follow-up comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Services** (`Medical Services`, select_one, required): Check Yes or No to indicate whether you have access to medical services.
* **Barriers to Access** (`Barriers to Access`, select_multiple, required): Check Yes or No to indicate whether there are any barriers to access healthcare services.
* **Demographic Info** (`Demographic Info`, note, required): Enter any additional demographic information you may have (currently empty).
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number, etc.).
* **Follow-up** (`Follow-up`, text, required): Enter any follow-up information or activities (currently empty).
* **Follow-up Status** (`Follow-up Status`, select_one, required): Check Active or Inactive to indicate whether the follow-up activity is active or inactive.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, text, required): Enter any additional follow-up information or activities.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the follow-up activity.
* **Follow-up Time** (`Follow-up Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the follow-up activity.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`Follow-up Comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the follow-up activity.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`Follow-up Contact`, text, required): Enter any contact information for the follow-up activity (phone number, email, etc.).
* **Follow-up Notes** (`Follow-up Notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the follow-up activity.
