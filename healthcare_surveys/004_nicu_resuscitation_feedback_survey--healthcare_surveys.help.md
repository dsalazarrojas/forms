# NICU Resuscitation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NICU Resuscitation Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from healthcare professionals or parents/caregivers about their experiences with neonatal care in the NICU. This survey aims to gather information that can help improve the quality of care and service provided to newborns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Questions are designed to be self-explanatory, but if you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact the survey administrators for clarification.
3. You will be taken to each page of the form in sequence; answer all questions before proceeding to the next page.
4. Some questions may have multiple choice options; select all that apply.
5. For free-text questions, please provide as much detail as you're comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Question 1**: A numerical question asking for feedback on a specific aspect of care.
	+ (Required: True) This question is designed to gather numerical feedback about a particular care aspect.
* **Question 2**: A single-choice question with options A and B.
	+ (Required: False) Please select one option that best describes your experience.
* **Question 3**: A select_multiple question with options A and B.
	+ (Required: False) Select all applicable options that best describe your experience.
* **Question 4**: A date question.
	+ (Required: False) Please input a date if applicable.
* **Question 5**: A text question.
	+ (Required: False) Please provide as much detail as possible about your experience.
* This pattern continues for all questions, with the type of each question and its required status being the primary differentiator.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to contact the survey administrators for clarification.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them in the free-text questions.
* Completing the survey should take about 10-15 minutes to complete, depending on how much detail you choose to provide.
