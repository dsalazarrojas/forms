# Hospital AI Training Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the Hospital AI Training Initiative, a program aimed at training healthcare professionals in AI-related skills. The form is intended to gather feedback and comments from healthcare professionals on their experience with the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand its purpose and each field's requirements.
2. Provide your honest evaluation and feedback in the text fields.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
4. Fill out the form with your true and accurate information.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Form**: This is the main evaluation section where you can provide your feedback and comments on your experience with the Hospital AI Training Initiative.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Patient Information**: Please enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Evaluation Questionnaire**: This section is for you to provide any additional information or questions you have about the initiative.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Training Feedback**: Share any positive or negative feedback about the training you received from the initiative.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter your evaluation score for the initiative, if applicable.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide any final comments or suggestions you have about the initiative.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for any additional information or comments you want to share.
	+ Type: Text, required: False
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form.
	+ Type: Text, required: False

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
