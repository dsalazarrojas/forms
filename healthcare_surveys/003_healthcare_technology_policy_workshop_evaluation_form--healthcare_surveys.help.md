# healthcare technology policy workshop evaluation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience and feedback on the healthcare technology policy workshop. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and assess the workshop's overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your honest feedback. The form is divided into the following sections:

*   **Workshop Title**: A brief description of the workshop title.
*   **Event Name**: A brief description of the event name.
*   **Speaker Rating**: Your rating of the speaker's performance on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Speaker Presentation**: A description of the speaker's presentation.
*   **Speaker Knowledge**: A description of the speaker's knowledge.
*   **Speaker Clarity**: A description of how clear the speaker was.
*   **Speaker Enthusiasm**: A description of the speaker's enthusiasm.
*   **Content Rating**: Your rating of the content on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Content Relevance**: A description of how relevant the content was.
*   **Content Clarity**: A description of how clear the content was.
*   **Content Knowledge**: A description of the presenter's knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, required): A brief description of the workshop title to help us understand what the workshop was about.
*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): A brief description of the event name to help us know which event this feedback is referring to.
*   **Speaker Rating** (`speaker_rating`, select_one, required): Your rating of the speaker's performance, on a scale of 1-5.
	+   Not Satisfied: The speaker did not meet your expectations.
	+   Neutral: The speaker was average.
	+   Satisfied: The speaker met your expectations.
	+   Very Satisfied: The speaker went above and beyond your expectations.
	+   Extremely Satisfied: The speaker was outstanding.
*   **Speaker Presentation** (`speaker_presentation`, text, required): A description of what the speaker presented.
*   **Speaker Knowledge** (`speaker_knowledge`, text, required): A description of what the speaker knew on the topic.
*   **Speaker Clarity** (`speaker_clarity`, text, required): A description of how clear the speaker was.
*   **Speaker Enthusiasm** (`speaker_enthusiasm`, text, required): A description of how enthusiastic the speaker was.
*   **Content Rating** (`content_rating`, text, required): Your rating of the content on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, text, required): A description of how relevant the content was.
*   **Content Clarity** (`content_clarity`, text, required): A description of how clear the content was.
*   **Content Knowledge** (`content_knowledge`, text, required): A description of the presenter's knowledge.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
*   You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the space provided.
*   Your input is crucial in helping us improve the workshop.
