# Joint Health Support Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Health Support Interest Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information on your health support needs and preferences. It's used to help our team provide you with the right support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields, which are required fields.
2. If you'd like to be contacted, provide your Phone Number. This is an optional field.
3. Enter your Email address if you'd like to be contacted via email. This is also an optional field.
4. Select the joint health support services you or your family member needs from the dropdown list. This is a required field.
5. If you need any other joint health support services not listed, you can specify them in the Other joint health support services field.
6. Answer the question regarding consent for our team to follow up with you about your survey answers. This is a required field.
7. Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone call, Text message, Email, or Letter) for your contact details. This is an optional field.
8. Finally, select how you found out about our JHA Health Service. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Your last name.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): A contact number we can reach you at.
* **Email** (email, optional): Your email address for us to contact you at.
* **What joint health support services** (select_one, required): Choose the joint health support services you or your family member need.
* **Other joint health support services** (text, optional): Other services not listed that you need.
* **Do you consent** (select_one, required): Let us know if you're willing to be followed up with about your survey answers.
* **Contact details** (select_multiple, optional): How you'd like to be contacted (Phone call, Text message, Email, or Letter).
* **How did you find us** (select_one, optional): How you heard about our JHA Health Service.
