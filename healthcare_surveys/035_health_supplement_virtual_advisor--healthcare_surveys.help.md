# Health Supplement Virtual Advisor - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Supplement Virtual Advisor is a tool to help you get tailored recommendations on supplements to support your health goals. Please answer the questions below to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each section carefully, taking your time to provide accurate information.
2. Review each field label for a brief explanation of what it means.
3. For fields where you can select from multiple options, choose all that apply.
4. For fields where you can only select one option, choose the one that best fits your goals.
5. If you have any health goals that are not listed, type them in the "Health Goals" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Questions** (`general_questions`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional information that might be relevant to your health supplement needs.
	+ Please provide any relevant background information about your health and preferences.
* **Health Goals** (`health_goals`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select from the list one or more health goals that you have.
	+ Choose the goals that you want to achieve through supplementing your diet.
* **Preferred Supplement Types** (`preferred_supplement_types`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all the types of supplements you are interested in (e.g., vitamin, herbal, etc.).
	+ This field will help us get a better understanding of your supplement preferences.
* **Preferred Supplement Brands** (`preferred_supplement_brands`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all the brands of supplements that you prefer.
	+ This field will help us get a better understanding of your brand preferences.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional):
	+ If you have any known medical condition, please specify here.
	+ Note that this form is not intended for users with a known medical condition.
* **Health Goals Other** (`health_goals_other`, `text`, optional):
	+ If any other health goal not listed, please specify here.
	+ This field will help us get a better understanding of your other health goals.

## Tips
* Be honest when answering the questions, as your supplement recommendations will be tailored to your specific needs.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* Remember that this form is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have any concerns, please consult a healthcare professional.
