# Livestock Health Data Collection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the health of livestock on farms, to help farmers and veterinarians track and analyze health data for better farm management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read each field carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
- Enter your animal ID and owner information accurately.
- Provide details about your farm, including animal info, health status, and disease treatment.
- Select the correct options for health care services used, veterinary care, farm location, and region.
- Choose the correct date of data collection.
- Enter the name of the veterinarian involved in the animal's care.
- Complete any other required or optional fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is for providing general information about the form submission, such as notes or comments.
* **Animal ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the animal, such as a tag number or ID number.
* **Owner Information**: Enter the owner's name and contact information, as well as any other relevant details about the owner.
* **Animal Info**: Enter details about the animal, such as breed, age, and sex.
* **Health Status**: Choose the current health status of the animal from the options: Healthy, Diseased, or Dead.
* **Disease Treatment**: Select all disease treatments that have been administered to the animal, such as antibiotics or surgery.
* **Health Care Services Used**: Enter any health care services used by the animal, such as vaccinations or medications.
* **Veterinary Care**: Choose whether the animal receives regular veterinary care.
* **Farm Info**: Enter any additional information about the farm, such as farm size or type.
* **Herd Size**: Enter the number of animals in the herd.
* **Farm Location**: Select the location of the farm from the options: North, South, East, or West.
* **Region**: Select the region of the farm from the options: Urban, Rural.
* **Date of Data Collection**: Enter the date when the data was collected, in the correct format.
* **Veterinarian Name**: Enter the name of the veterinarian who has provided care to the animal.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information about your animals and farms.
* If unsure about any field, please seek help from your veterinarian or farm manager.
* Double-check your answers for errors before submitting the form.
* If you need to submit more animals or farms, please submit multiple forms.
