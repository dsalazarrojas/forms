# Clinical Research Participation Willingness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help researchers understand your willingness to participate in clinical research studies. Your participation will help us to better understand your preferences, concerns, and availability, which will aid in matching you with suitable studies. Your responses will be kept confidential and will not affect your medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select all that apply for multi-select options.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, please feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments or Questions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **City and State** (`address`, text, required): Enter your city and state of residence for locating nearby studies.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest education level completed.
* **Race and Ethnicity** (`race_ethnicity`, select_one, optional): Select your racial and ethnic background.
* **Do You Have Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, select_one, optional): Select if you have health insurance.
* **Do You Have a Primary Care Provider** (`primary_care_provider`, select_one, optional): Select if you have a primary care provider.
* **Do You Have Any Chronic Health Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, select_one, required): Select yes if you have any chronic health conditions.
* **Condition Details** (`condition_details`, text, optional): If you have any chronic health conditions, please specify them.
* **Are You Currently Taking Any Prescription Medications** (`current_medications`, select_one, optional): Select yes if you are currently taking any prescription medications.
* **Medication Details** (`medication_details`, text, optional): If you are currently taking any prescription medications, please list them.
* **Have You Ever Participated in Clinical Research Before** (`previous_research_participation`, select_one, required): Select yes if you have ever participated in clinical research before.
* **Previous Experience Details** (`previous_experience_details`, text, optional): If you have participated in clinical research before, please describe your experience.
* **How Willing Are You to Participate in Clinical Research** (`willingness_to_participate`, select_one, required): Select your level of willingness to participate.
* **Types of Studies You Would Consider** (`study_types_interested`, select_multiple, optional): Select all types of studies you would consider participating in.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, select_one, optional): Select how much time you could commit to participating in studies.
* **How Far Would You Travel for a Study** (`travel_willingness`, select_one, optional): Select how far you would be willing to travel for a study.
* **How Important Is Compensation** (`compensation_importance`, select_one, optional): Select the importance of compensation in your decision to participate.
* **What Concerns Do You Have About Participating** (`concerns_about_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Select all concerns you have about participating in clinical research.
* **Where Do You Get Health Information** (`information_sources`, select_multiple, optional): Select all sources where you get health information.
* **How Much Do You Trust Clinical Research** (`trust_in_research`, select_one, required): Select your level of trust in clinical research.
* **Preferred Contact Method for Study Opportunities** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of contact.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): Select a suitable time to contact you.
* **May We Contact You About Research Opportunities** (`permission_to_contact`, select_one, required): Select yes if we can contact you about research opportunities.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please feel free to add them.
