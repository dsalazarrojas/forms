# Community Health Behavior Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Community Health Behavior Change Survey" is a questionnaire designed to gather feedback from individuals who have participated in a health behavior change program. This survey aims to understand the effectiveness of the program in helping participants make positive changes to their health behavior, as well as identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill in your name and email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive further communication.
3.  Select your age group from the provided options.
4.  Choose which program you participated in from the list of available options.
5.  Indicate whether you completed the program as intended.
6.  Select all the changes you made to your health behavior during the program.
7.  Describe any dietary improvements you made (if applicable).
8.  Describe any exercise or physical activity improvements you made (if applicable).
9.  Indicate your level of confidence in maintaining the changes you made.
10. Indicate your motivation level to continue making healthy changes.
11. Indicate the level of support you have for maintaining these changes.
12. Select all the obstacles you faced while making changes.
13. Rate the helpfulness of the program.
14. Describe any health improvements you've noticed.
15. If you've experienced a change in weight, please estimate the amount of weight changed in pounds.
16. Indicate how your energy level has changed.
17. Indicate how your mood has changed.
18. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the program.
19. If there are any additional services you think would be beneficial, please describe them.
20. Indicate your interest in participating in future programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Optional)**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address (Optional)**: Enter your email address for follow-up questions or further communication.
* **Age Group**: Select your age range to categorize your demographic information.
* **Program Participation**: Choose the program you participated in to help us understand your experience.
* **Did You Complete the Program**: Indicate whether you completed the program as intended.
* **Behavior Changes Made**: Select all the health behavior changes you made during the program, such as improved diet or increased exercise.
* **Dietary Changes**: Describe any improvements you made to your diet, such as eating more fruits and vegetables.
* **Exercise Changes**: Describe any physical activity improvements you made, such as increased walking or jogging.
* **Confidence in Maintaining Changes**: Indicate your confidence level in maintaining the health behavior changes you made.
* **Motivation Level**: Indicate your level of motivation to continue making healthy changes.
* **Support System**: Select the level of support you have for maintaining these changes.
* **Barriers to Change**: Select all the obstacles you faced while trying to make these changes.
* **Program Helpfulness**: Rate the helpfulness of the program in achieving your goals.
* **Health Improvements Noticed**: Describe any positive health changes you've noticed since participating in the program.
* **Weight Change**: If you've experienced a change in weight, estimate the amount of weight changed in pounds.
* **Energy Level Change**: Indicate how your energy level has changed since participating in the program.
* **Mood Improvement**: Indicate how your mood has changed since participating in the program.
* **Would You Recommend Program**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the program to others.
* **Additional Services Needed**: If there are any additional services you think would be beneficial, describe them.
* **Interested in Continued Programs**: Indicate your interest in participating in future programs.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to help us improve the program.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to provide them in the "Additional Services Needed" field.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us refine the program and better serve future participants.
