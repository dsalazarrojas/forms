# Waiting Room Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiting Room Quiz is a brief form designed to collect information from patients while they wait to be seen by a doctor or healthcare professional. This form helps us understand patient demographics and appointment details, which can inform quality improvement initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Answer the questions below, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the applicable options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name**
 Patient Name (`patient_name`, text, required)
Enter your name as it appears on your medical records.

### 2. **How old are you?**
 How old are you? (`question1`, number, optional)
Enter your age in years.

### 3. **What time did you arrive at the waiting room?**
 What time did you arrive at the waiting room? (`question2`, time, required)
Enter the time you arrived, using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 12:30 PM).

### 4. **How many people in your party are taking this quiz?**
 How many people in your party are taking this quiz? (`question3`, select_one, required)
Select the number of people in your party.

### 5. **What type of doctor do you see today?**
 What type of doctor do you see today? (`question5`, select_multiple, required)
Select all applicable doctor types you will see today.

### 6. **How long did you wait in the waiting room?**
 How long did you wait in the waiting room? (`question6`, number, optional)
Enter the number of minutes you waited.

### 7. **What type of appointment did you come for?**
 What type of appointment did you come for? (`question7`, select_one, required)
Select the type of appointment you have.

### 8. **What time did you leave the waiting room?**
 What time did you leave the waiting room? (`question8`, time, optional)
Enter the time you left, using the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 02:00 AM).

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask a waiting room staff member for assistance.
* Thank you for participating in this quiz!
