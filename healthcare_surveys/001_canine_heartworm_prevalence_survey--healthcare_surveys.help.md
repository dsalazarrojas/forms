# Canine Heartworm Prevalence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for veterinarians and healthcare professionals to report and track heartworm cases in dogs. It collects information on various factors influencing heartworm prevalence, such as diagnostic methods, age groups, and prevention practices. The data collected will help identify trends and patterns in heartworm cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Clinic Information** page, providing your veterinary clinic's details. If you're unsure, you can use the hint provided for guidance.
2. On the **Clinic Information** page, you will be asked to provide your clinic's name and address. This is a required field.
3. Move on to the **State or Region** page, where you will select your clinic's location from the list provided.
4. Provide the name of the veterinarian reporting the data on the **Veterinarian Name** page.
5. For the **Survey Period** page, select the time period this survey covers.
6. Answer the questions regarding **Case Reporting**, **Total Cases Diagnosed**, **Diagnostic Methods Used**, **Case Severity Distribution**, and **Prevention Practices** on their respective pages.
7. On the **Preventive Products Recommended** page, select all preventive measures recommended.
8. Provide the **Patient Demographics** page with information about affected dogs.
9. Answer the questions regarding **Most Affected Age Group**, **Breed Predisposition Observed**, **Outdoor Exposure Level**, and **Treatment Success Rate** on their respective pages.
10. If applicable, report any **Treatment Complications** observed.
11. Provide any **Additional Comments** or observations on the **Additional Comments** page.
12. Finally, enter your **Contact Email** for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinic Information** (`1`): Provide your veterinary clinic's details.
  **(Clinic Name)**: The full name of your veterinary clinic or hospital.
  **(Clinic Address)**: The street address of your clinic.
  **(Clinic Phone)**: Your clinic's phone number (optional).
  **(Clinic Email)**: Your clinic's email address (optional).
* **Clinic Name** (`2`): The full name of your veterinary clinic or hospital.
* **Clinic Address** (`3`): The street address of your clinic.
* **State or Region** (`4`): Select your clinic's location from the list provided.
* **Veterinarian Name** (`5`): The name of the reporting veterinarian.
* **Survey Period** (`6`): The time period this survey covers. This could be a specific month or quarter, such as January 2025 or Q1 2025.
* **Case Reporting** (`7`): Report heartworm cases diagnosed (optional).
* **Total Cases Diagnosed** (`8`): The number of canine heartworm cases diagnosed in this period.
* **Diagnostic Methods Used** (`9`): Select all diagnostic methods used to diagnose heartworms, such as Antigen Testing, Microfilaria Testing, or Radiography.
* **Case Severity Distribution** (`10`): Break down case severity levels as Mostly mild cases, Mostly moderate cases, Mostly severe cases, or Mixed severity.
* **Prevention Practices** (`11`): Information about prevention recommendations.
* **Prevention Compliance Rate** (`12`): The estimated percentage of owners following prevention protocols.
* **Preventive Products Recommended** (`13`): Select all preventative measures recommended, such as Monthly Oral Medication, 6-Month Injectable, and Combination Products.
* **Patient Demographics** (`14`): Information about affected dogs.
* **Most Affected Age Group** (`15`): The age group with the highest heartworm prevalence.
* **Breed Predisposition Observed** (`16`): Whether you have noticed breed-specific patterns.
* **Outdoor Exposure Level** (`17`): The typical outdoor exposure level of affected dogs.
* **Treatment Outcomes** (`18`): Information about treatment outcomes.
* **Treatment Success Rate** (`19`): The estimated success rate of heartworm treatments.
* **Complications Observed** (`20`): Common complications observed during treatment.
* **Additional Comments** (`21`): Any additional observations or comments.
* **Contact Email** (`22`): Email for follow-up questions.
