# Help Seeking Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your attitudes and preferences when seeking help, particularly in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the format provided.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or information in the designated text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Help Seeking Attitudes Survey** (`survey_title`, text, required: no): This is the title of the form. You can enter any relevant title that describes your experience with help seeking.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: no): Choose the category that best describes your experience with help seeking. Options are: Healthcare Surveys, Questionnaire Templates, and Other.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: no): Provide a brief description of your experience with help seeking.
* **Survey Description** (`survey_description`, text, required: no): If you have any additional information about your experience with help seeking, you can provide it here.
* **Preferred Help Source** (`preferred_help_source`, select_multiple, required: no): Choose the help sources you prefer. Options are: Family and Friends, Doctor, and Other.
* **Comfortable with Seeking Support** (`comfortable_with_support`, number, required: no): Indicate your comfort level with seeking support on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Comfort Level 1** (`comfort_level_1`, number, required: no): Choose your comfort level with seeking help for the first time.
* **Comfort Level 2** (`comfort_level_2`, number, required: no): Choose your comfort level with seeking help for the second time.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, required: no): Choose the contact methods you prefer for seeking help. Options are: Phone, Email, and In-Person.
* **Follow up Question** (`follow_up_question`, text, required: no): If you have any follow-up questions, provide them here.
* **Follow up Answer** (`follow_up_answer`, text, required: no): If you have any follow-up answers, provide them here.

Note: Some fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help us better understand your attitudes and preferences.
