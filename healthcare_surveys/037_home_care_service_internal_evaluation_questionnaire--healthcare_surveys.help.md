# Home Care Service Internal Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Service Internal Evaluation Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from clients on the quality of services provided by home care service teams. This form will help teams identify areas of improvement and provide better services to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each question carefully and answer according to your experiences with the home care service teams. You may answer "Yes" or "No" for questions that have a select_one type, and provide a number between 1 and 5 for questions that have a number type. For text questions, please provide a brief description of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What type of services are provided by home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_1">What type of services are provided by home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about the types of services provided by the home care service teams. Please choose "Yes" if the team provides the service and "No" if not.

### 2. What is your overall satisfaction with our services? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_5">What is your overall satisfaction with our services?)
*   This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the services provided by the home care service teams. Please rate on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".

### 3. What type of equipment do you usually need from home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_9">What type of equipment do you usually need from home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about the types of equipment you usually need from the home care service teams. Please choose "Yes" if the team provides the equipment and "No" if not.

### 4. How would you rate the overall quality of the home care service teams' services? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_11">How would you rate the overall quality of the home care service teams' services?)
*   This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the quality of services provided by the home care service teams. Please rate on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".

### 5. How would you rate the communication of the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_13">How would you rate the communication of the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about your satisfaction with the communication of the home care service teams. Please rate on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Good" and 5 is "Very Good".

### 6. How would you rate the responsiveness of the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_14">How would you rate the responsiveness of the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about your satisfaction with the responsiveness of the home care service teams. Please rate on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Good" and 5 is "Very Good".

### 7. How would you rate the reliability of the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_15">How would you rate the reliability of the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about your satisfaction with the reliability of the home care service teams. Please rate on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not Good" and 5 is "Very Good".

### 8. What do you think is the biggest advantage of the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_16">What do you think is the biggest advantage of the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about your thoughts on the biggest advantage of the home care service teams. Please provide a brief description.

### 9. What do you think is the biggest limitation of the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_17">What do you think is the biggest limitation of the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about your thoughts on the biggest limitation of the home care service teams. Please provide a brief description.

### 10. Is there anything else you would like to add? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_18">Is there anything else you would like to add?)
*   This question asks if you have any additional comments or suggestions for the home care service teams. Please provide a brief description.

### 11. What would make you feel safe while using the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_19">What would make you feel safe while using the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about what would make you feel safe while using the home care service teams. Please choose "Yes" if you feel safe and "No" if not.

### 12. What would make you feel comfortable while using the home care service teams? (<label name="home_care_service_internal_evaluation_questionnaire_20">What would make you feel comfortable while using the home care service teams?)
*   This question asks about what would make you feel comfortable while using the home care service teams. Please choose "Yes" if you feel comfortable and "No" if not.

## Tips
*   Please answer questions honestly and thoroughly.
*   You may skip questions that do not apply to you.
