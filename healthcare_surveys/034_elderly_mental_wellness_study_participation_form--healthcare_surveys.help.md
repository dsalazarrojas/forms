### Elderly Mental Wellness Study Participation Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The form is designed to gather information about your mental wellbeing and health status, which will aid in developing targeted interventions to support your mental health.

#### How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. If you are unsure or have any issues with a particular question, feel free to ask for assistance.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Information (1)**: This is the title of the study, which is the "Elderly Mental Wellness Study Participation Form".
* **Contact Info (2)**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any relevant contact information, such as a phone number or email address.
* **Date of Last Visit (3)**: Enter the date of your last visit to a healthcare facility or study location.
* **Time of Last Visit (4)**: Enter the time of your last visit to a healthcare facility or study location.
* **Age (5)**: Enter your age.
* **Wellbeing Scale (6)**: Select "Yes" if you experience a good wellbeing, "No" if you experience a poor wellbeing, or "Other" if you have a different wellbeing status. Please provide a brief explanation in **Wellbeing Scale Comments (7)**.
* **Loneliness Scale (8)**: Select "Yes" if you feel lonely, "No" if you do not feel lonely, or "Other" if you have a different loneliness status. Please provide a brief explanation in **Loneliness Scale Comments (9)**.
* **Emotional Health Scale (10)**: Select "Yes" if you experience a good emotional health, "No" if you experience a poor emotional health, or "Other" if you have a different emotional health status. Please provide a brief explanation in **Emotional Health Scale Comments (11)**.
* **Wellbeing Scale Agenda (12)**: Enter the date of your wellbeing agenda.
* **Wellbeing Scale Comments 2 (13)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing status.
* **Emotional State (14)**: Select "Yes" if you experience an emotional state, "No" if you do not experience an emotional state, or "Other" if you have a different emotional state status. Please provide a brief explanation in **Emotional State Comments (15)**.
* **Wellbeing Scale Total (16)**: Select "Yes" if you have a good wellbeing total, "No" if you have a poor wellbeing total, or "Other" if you have a different wellbeing total status. Please provide a brief explanation in **Wellbeing Scale Total Comments (17)**.
* **Wellbeing Scale Agenda 2 (18)**: Enter the date of your wellbeing agenda 2.
* **Wellbeing Scale Comments 3 (19)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing status.
* **Wellbeing Scale Agenda Comments (20)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing agenda.
* **Wellbeing Scale Total Comments 2 (21)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing total status.
* **Wellbeing Scale Comments 4 (22)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing status.
* **Wellbeing Scale Agenda Comments 2 (23)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing agenda 2 status.
* **Wellbeing Scale Agenda 3 (24)**: Enter the date of your wellbeing agenda 3.
* **Wellbeing Scale Comments 5 (25)**: Provide any additional comments about your wellbeing status.
