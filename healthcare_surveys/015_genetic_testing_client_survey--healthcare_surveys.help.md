# Genetic Testing Client Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with genetic testing services. The feedback will help us understand how clear our test results were, how well our healthcare providers communicated with you, and what we could improve in the testing process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they apply to your experience. You may select one or multiple answers for some questions. Please be as detailed as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Results Feedback** (1) (`Test Results Feedback`, text, required/optional): Please provide feedback on how clear our test results were to you.
* **How clear were the test results explained?** (2) (`test_results_1`, number, required/optional): On a scale of 1 to 5, how clear were the test results explained to you?
* **Test Results 2** (3) (`test_results_2`, text, required/optional): Please provide feedback on your experience with test results.
* **Were you satisfied with the test results?** (4) (`test_results_3`, text, required/optional): How satisfied were you with the test results?
* **Communication with Healthcare Provider** (5) (`communication_1`, text, required/optional): Please describe your experience with communication from your healthcare provider.
* **How clear was the communication from your healthcare provider?** (6) (`communication_2`, number, required/optional): On a scale of 1 to 5, how clear was the communication from your healthcare provider?
* **Communication 3** (7) (`communication_3`, text, required/optional): Please provide feedback on your experience with communication from your healthcare provider.
* **Were you satisfied with the communication from your healthcare provider?** (8) (`communication_4`, text, required/optional): How satisfied were you with the communication from your healthcare provider?
* **Communication with Lab** (9) (`lab_1`, text, required/optional): Please describe your experience with communication from the lab.

* **Were you satisfied with the communication from the lab?** (12) (`lab_4`, text, required/optional): How satisfied were you with the communication from the lab?
