# Medical Technology Accessibility Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the accessibility of medical technology, such as electronic health records, telemedicine, and medical apps, to identify areas for improvement and provide input for healthcare organizations to enhance patient care. Please note that this form is specific to the Medical Technology Accessibility Evaluation Form and is used to gather information from patients or healthcare providers to understand the usability and accessibility of medical technology.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a different area of medical technology.
2. Please fill out the form in the following order:
	* Section 1: Electronic Health Records - Describe your experiences with electronic health records.
	* Section 2: Navigation Ease - Rate the ease of navigation within the electronic health records.
	* Section 3: Difficulties Encountered - Describe any difficulties you have faced while using electronic health records.
	* Section 4: Telemedicine Experience - Share your experiences with telemedicine services.
	* Section 5: Telemedicine Frequency - Rate how frequently you use telemedicine services.
	* Section 6: Medical Apps Experience - Describe your experiences with medical apps.
	* Section 7: Medical Apps Frequency - Rate how frequently you use medical apps.
	* Section 8: App Features - Choose which features you use in medical apps.
	* Section 9: Connectivity Issues - Describe any connectivity issues you have faced while using medical technology.
	* Section 10: Overall Accessibility - Rate the overall accessibility of medical technology.
	* Section 11: Suggestions - Provide any suggestions for improving medical technology accessibility.
	* Section 12: Additional Feedback - Share any additional comments or feedback about medical technology.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electronic Health Records** (`ehr_section`, text, required): Describe your experiences with electronic health records, such as accessing, navigating, and using the system.
* **Navigation Ease** (`navigation_ehr`, number, required): Rate the ease of navigation within electronic health records, with options:
	+ 1 (Very Easy)
	+ 2 (Somewhat Easy)
	+ 3 (Neutral)
	+ 4 (Somewhat Difficult)
	+ 5 (Very Difficult)
* **Difficulties Encountered** (`ehr_difficulties`, text, required): Describe any difficulties you have faced while using electronic health records.
* **Telemedicine Experience** (`telemedicine_section`, text, required): Share your experiences with telemedicine services, such as accessing, navigating, and using the system.
* **Telemedicine Frequency** (`telemedicine_frequency`, select_one, required): Rate how frequently you use telemedicine services, with options:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
* **Medical Apps Experience** (`medical_apps_section`, text, required): Describe your experiences with medical apps, such as accessing, navigating, and using the system.
* **Medical Apps Frequency** (`apps_frequency`, select_one, required): Rate how frequently you use medical apps, with options:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
* **App Features** (`apps_features`, select_multiple, required): Choose which features you use in medical apps:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Connectivity Issues** (`connectivity_section`, text, required): Describe any connectivity issues you have faced while using medical technology.
* **Overall Accessibility** (`overall_accessibility`, number, required): Rate the overall accessibility of medical technology, with options:
	+ 1 (Very Easy)
	+ 2 (Somewhat Easy)
	+ 3 (Neutral)
	+ 4 (Somewhat Difficult)
	+ 5 (Very Difficult)
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Provide any suggestions for improving medical technology accessibility.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any additional comments or feedback about medical technology.
