# Intensive Therapy Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients interested in intensive therapy sessions. Please complete this form to help us understand your goals, concerns, and preferences for therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- You can complete this form online or print it out and fill it in by hand.
- If you need assistance or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Therapy Goals**: This section is where you can list your reasons for seeking therapy. Please choose all that apply from the options provided.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Therapy Goals</span> (`therapy_goals`, `select_multiple`, required)
* **Client Details**: Please select all relevant details about yourself. Choose from the options provided.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Client Details</span> (`client_details`, `select_multiple`, required)
* **Availability**: If you have a preferred day and time for therapy sessions, please select it.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Availability</span> (`availability`, `date`, required)
* **Concerns**: Please list your concerns in the space provided.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Concerns</span> (`concerns`, `text`, required)
* **What Brings You In for Therapy**: This is where you can choose the reason(s) that brings you to therapy.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">What brings you in for therapy</span> (`therapy_goals_1`, `select_multiple`, required)
* **What Are You Concerned About**: You can list your concerns in the space provided.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">What are you concerned about</span> (`client_concerns`, `note`, required)
* **What Do You Hope to Achieve Through Therapy**: Please list your goals for therapy in the space provided.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">What do you hope to achieve through therapy</span> (`therapy_goals_2`, `text`, required)
* **Availability for Therapy Sessions**: If you have a preferred availability for therapy sessions, please select it.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Availability for therapy sessions</span> (`availability_1`, `time`, required)
* **Online Sessions Availability**: If you prefer online sessions, you can select the days and times that suit you.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Online Sessions Availability</span> (`therapy_goals_3`, `select_multiple`, required)
* **How Often Would You Like to Have Online Sessions**: If you have a preferred frequency for online sessions, please select it.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">How often would you like to have online sessions</span> (`therapy_goals_4`, `select_one`, required)
* **Therapy Session Preferences**: You can choose the type of therapy sessions that you prefer.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Therapy Session Preferences</span> (`therapy_session_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required)
* **Client Preferences**: Please list your preferences for a therapist in the space provided.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Client Preferences</span> (`client_preferences`, `note`, required)
* **Emergency Contact Information**: This is where you can provide emergency contact information.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Emergency Contact Information</span> (`emergency_contact`, `email`, required)
* **Preferred Contact Method**: You can choose your preferred method of contact.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Preferred contact method</span> (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required)
* **Contact Details**: Please provide contact information.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Contact Details</span> (`contact_details`, `text`, required)
* **Medical Notes**: This is where you can provide any relevant medical information.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Medical Notes</span> (`medical_notes`, `note`, required)
* **Therapy Session Notes**: This is where you can provide any additional notes about therapy sessions.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Therapy Session Notes</span> (`therapy_session_notes`, `note`, required)
* **Other Notes**: Please provide any additional information.
  - **Label**: <span style="font-weight: bold">Other Notes</span> (`other_notes`, `note`, required)

## Tips

* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* This form is for intensive therapy sessions only, so please do not use it for other purposes.
* Please keep in mind that the information you provide will help us tailor the therapy sessions to your individual needs.
