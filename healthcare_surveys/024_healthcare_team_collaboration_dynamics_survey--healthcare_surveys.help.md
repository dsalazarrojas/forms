# Healthcare Team Collaboration Dynamics Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Healthcare Team Collaboration Dynamics Survey" form is designed to collect feedback and insights from healthcare team members on their team collaboration, communication, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to open-ended questions.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.
