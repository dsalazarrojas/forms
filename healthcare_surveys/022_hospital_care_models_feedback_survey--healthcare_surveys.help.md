# Hospital Care Models Feedback Survey - Help Guide

### ### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the hospital care model you experienced (e.g., General Medicine, General Surgery, or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of your experience.
3. Rate your experience in terms of care (e.g., 1-10).
4. Rate the service delivery of your experience (e.g., 1-10).
5. Rate your overall experience (e.g., Positive, Negative, Neutral).
6. Indicate if you would recommend this care model to others (True or False).
7. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
8. Select the category of your experience (again, this field is likely to be a duplicate and can be ignored).
9. Enter the ID of the assigned tool, if applicable.
10. Enter the name of the assigned user, if applicable.
11. Enter the ID of this form submission (not applicable to users).
12. Select the assigned tool, if applicable (likely to be the same as field 8).
13. Record the date and time when you created this survey submission (not applicable to users).
14. Record the date and time when you last updated this survey submission (not applicable to users).
15. Enter the name of the user who created this survey submission (not applicable to users).
16. Enter the name of the user who last updated this survey submission (not applicable to users).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (General Medicine, General Surgery, Other): Select the category of the hospital care model you experienced.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your experience with the hospital care model.
* **Care Experience** (number 1-10): Rate your experience in terms of care, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Service Delivery** (number 1-10): Rate the service delivery of your experience, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Patient Satisfaction** (number 1-10): Rate your experience in terms of patient satisfaction, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Overall Experience** (Positive, Negative, Neutral): Select the overall quality of your experience (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Recommended** (True, False): Indicate if you would recommend this care model to others (True or False).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Category** (again): This field is likely to be a duplicate and can be ignored.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the ID of the assigned tool, if applicable.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the assigned user, if applicable.
* **Form ID**: This field is not applicable to users.
* **Output File**: This field is not applicable to users.
* **Assigned Tool** (again): This field is likely to be the same as field 15.
* **Created At**: This field records the date and time when you created this survey submission (not applicable to users).
* **Updated At**: This field records the date and time when you last updated this survey submission (not applicable to users).
* **Created By**: This field records the name of the user who created this survey submission (not applicable to users).
* **Updated By**: This field records the name of the user who last updated this survey submission (not applicable to users).
