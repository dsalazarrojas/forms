# mobile_health_app_usability_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "mobile_health_app_usability_survey" form, which is designed to gather feedback from users about the usability of a mobile health app.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the introduction page carefully and take a moment to think about your overall experience with the mobile health app.
2. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible to help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
3. For the "issues" page, select all the problems you have encountered while using the app.
4. For the "feature_requests" page, choose one option that best describes a feature you would like to see added or improved in the app.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`page_1`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully and then proceed to the next page.
* **App Navigation** (`page_2`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with navigating the app. Was it easy or difficult to find what you needed?
* **Satisfaction** (`page_3`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the app, with 0 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Issues** (`page_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the issues you have encountered while using the app, such as bugs, crashes, or confusing features.
* **Feature Requests** (`page_5`, select_one, required: true): Choose one feature you would like to see added or improved in the app. Options are:
	+ option 3: "Improve navigation"
	+ option 4: "Add more features"
	+ option 5: "Enhance user experience"
* **Page 6** (`page_6`, date, required: false): Date and time of last use of the app.
* **Page 7** (`page_7`, time, required: false): Time of last use of the app.
* **Email** (`page_8`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for any additional feedback or questions.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_9`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the app.
* **Other Comments** (`page_10`, text, required: false): Share any other comments or thoughts about the app.
* **Final Comments** (`page_11`, text, required: false): Share any final comments or thoughts about the app.
* **Submission** (`page_12`, text, required: false): Enter any final comments or thoughts before submitting the form.

Note: Some questions may ask for more detail, so feel free to elaborate as much as you like. Remember to submit the form once you have completed all the pages.
