# Health Condition Experience Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Health Condition Experience Submission" form is designed to collect information about your experiences with a specific health condition. This form is used to help researchers and healthcare providers better understand your experiences, which can lead to improved care and support for individuals with similar conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the introduction page to understand the purpose and importance of the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, including the health condition, your consent, and contact information.
3. Answer the questions regarding your experience with the health condition.
4. Select the purpose of your consent, which will determine how your information will be used.
5. Enter your contact information so that we can follow up with you regarding your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This is a non-mandatory page that provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read this page carefully to ensure you understand what the form is for.

### health_condition
* **health_condition**: This field is where you will describe the health condition you are experiencing. Please provide a clear and concise answer.

### experience
* **experience**: This field is where you will share your personal experience with the health condition. Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant dates, symptoms, and treatments.

### consent
* **consent**: This field requires you to select the purpose of your consent. Please choose one or both of the following options:
	+ Consent for research: Your information will be used for research purposes, which can help improve the understanding and treatment of the health condition.
	+ Consent for educational use: Your information will be used for educational purposes, such as creating resources and materials for healthcare providers.

### consent_date
* **consent_date**: This field is where you will enter the date of your consent.

### consent_signed
* **consent_signed**: This field is where you will confirm that you have signed the consent form.

### submitted_by
* **submitted_by**: This field is where you will enter your name.

### submitted_on
* **submitted_on**: This field is where you will enter the date and time of submission.

### submitted_at
* **submitted_at**: This field is where you will enter the time of submission.

### consent_note
* **consent_note**: This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information regarding your consent.

### contact_number
* **contact_number**: This field is where you will enter your phone number.

### contact_name
* **contact_name**: This field is where you will enter your name.

### contact_email
* **contact_email**: This field is where you will enter your email address.

### consented_to
* **consented_to**: This field requires you to select the entities to which you have consented. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Healthcare provider
	+ Research organization
	+ Support organization
	+ Other
	+ Both

### submitted_from
* **submitted_from**: This field requires you to select where you submitted this form from. Please choose one or both of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### submitted_by_other
* **submitted_by_other**: This field is where you will enter your name if you selected "Yes" in the previous field.

### submitted_from_other
* **submitted_from_other**: This field is where you will enter the location from which you submitted this form.

### submitted_from_other_info
* **submitted_from_other_info**: This field is where you will enter any additional information regarding your submission location.

### submitted_from_other_contact_email
* **submitted_from_other_contact_email**: This field is where you will enter the email address of the contact person at your submission location.

### submitted_from_other_contact_number
* **submitted_from_other_contact_number**: This field is where you will enter the phone number of the contact person at your submission location.

### submitted_from_other_note
* **submitted_from_other_note**: This field is where you will enter any additional comments or information regarding your submission location.
