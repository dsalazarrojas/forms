# Sober Living House Manager Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a sober living house manager to support the treatment and progress of residents in recovery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate option for each field carefully, taking into account the resident's circumstances.
2. Enter the resident's name and date of birth accurately.
3. Provide the resident's contact information, including their email and phone number.
4. Report the resident's address and city.
5. Indicate the resident's status and length of stay.
6. Provide the number of days since last treatment and the date of the last treatment.
7. Determine the number of days treatment is needed and the next treatment date.
8. Decide on the follow-up frequency and provide the follow-up date.
9. Select the follow-up method (e.g., phone call, email, etc.).
10. Provide any additional comments about the resident's progress and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Start**: Select whether the form is being filled out for the first time or if the resident has already been filled out previously.
* **House Name**: Enter the name of the sober living house where the resident is living.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager or contact person for the sober living house.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager or contact person.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager or contact person.
* **Manager Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the manager or contact person.
* **Resident Name**: Enter the name of the resident being surveyed.
* **Resident DOB**: Enter the date of birth of the resident.
* **Resident Age**: Enter the age of the resident.
* **Address**: Enter the address where the resident is living.
* **City**: Enter the city where the resident is living.
* **State**: Indicate whether the resident's address is in the state where the sober living house is located.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the resident's address.
* **Resident Status**: Enter the status of the resident's recovery progress (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Resident Length Stay**: Enter the number of days the resident has been in the sober living house.
* **Days Since Last Treatment**: Enter the number of days since the resident's last treatment.
* **Last Treatment Date**: Enter the date of the resident's last treatment.
* **Next Treatment Date**: Enter the date of the resident's next treatment.
* **Days Treatment Needed**: Enter the number of days the resident needs for treatment.
* **Follow Up Frequency**: Decide on the frequency of follow-ups (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up.
* **Follow Up Method**: Select the method of follow-up (e.g., phone call, email, etc.).
* **Follow Up Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Location**: Enter the location of the follow-up (e.g., sober living house, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the resident's progress and needs.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor for guidance.
* This form should not be filled out multiple times for the same individual.
