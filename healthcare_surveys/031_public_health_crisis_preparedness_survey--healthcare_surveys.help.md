# Public Health Crisis Preparedness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your emergency preparedness and contact information in case of a public health crisis. This data will help our organization better understand how to best support you and your community in such situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **First Page** section, which includes a text input field where you can provide any comments or information about your emergency plan.
2. If applicable, select either **Yes** or **No** for the **Second Page** to indicate your level of emergency preparedness.
3. In the **Emergency Plan** section, you can provide a descriptive text about your emergency plan. This is a required field.
4. If you have any contact information, you can select **Yes** or **No** for **Contact Info**. If you select **Yes**, you can also fill in your phone number, email, address, city, state, and zip in the respective text fields. Note that these fields are optional.
5. Fill in your phone number in the **Phone Number** field, if applicable.
6. Fill in your email address in the **Email** field, if applicable.
7. Fill in your address, city, state, and zip in the **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip** fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page**: A text input field where you can provide any comments or information about your emergency plan. (Text, required)
* **Second Page: Emergency Preparedness**: Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate your level of emergency preparedness. (Select One, optional)
* **Emergency Plan**: A descriptive text field where you can detail your emergency plan. (Note, required)
* **Contact Info**: Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you want to share your contact information. (Select Multiple, optional)
* **Phone Number**: A text input field to fill in your phone number, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Text, optional)
* **Email**: A text input field to fill in your email address, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Email, optional)
* **Address**: A text input field to fill in your address, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Text, optional)
* **City**: A text input field to fill in your city, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Text, optional)
* **State**: A text input field to fill in your state, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Text, optional)
* **Zip**: A text input field to fill in your zip, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Text, optional)
* **Country**: A text input field to fill in your country, if you selected **Yes** for **Contact Info**. (Text, optional)
