# healthcare_infrastructure_assessment_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and evaluate the healthcare infrastructure in a given facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the facility's name and address in the respective fields.
2. Answer the questions regarding the facility's infrastructure, including its type, status, patient flow, and emergency equipment.
3. Provide any necessary comments or explanations for your responses.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
