# Global Disease Prevention Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for evaluating the effectiveness of Global Disease Prevention Programs. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants on their experience with the program, and to collect data on the program's outcomes and demographics. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Program Location**, **Program Name**, and **Program Date** fields, which will help us understand the context and scope of the program.
2.  Choose the **Program Outcomes Measured** options to indicate which outcomes the program is trying to achieve, such as "Yes" if the program is measured.
3.  Select your **Participant Feedback** level, which will provide insights into how satisfied you were with the program. This can be **Very Satisfied**, **Somewhat Satisfied**, **Neutral**, **Somewhat Dissatisfied**, or **Very Dissatisfied**.
4.  Enter your **Participant Email** for verification purposes.
5.  Enter the **Participant ID** for identification purposes.
6.  Enter your **Participant Name** and **Participant Phone** for contact information.
7.  Select your **Participant Survey Language** to ensure you can understand the survey questions.
8.  Select your **Participant Survey Completed Time** to record the time when you completed the survey.
9.  Choose the **Survey Type** to indicate if this is a **Global Disease Prevention Program Evaluation Form** or other.
10. Choose the **Program Type** to indicate if it's a **Global Disease Prevention Program** or other.
11. Select your **Participant Survey Type** to indicate how the survey was completed, which can be **Online** or **Offline**.
12. Select your **Program Language** to indicate what language the program used.
13. Select your **Participant Survey Language** to indicate what language the survey was completed in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Program Location (1)*: Enter the location of the Global Disease Prevention Program.
*   *Program Name (2)*: Enter the name of the Global Disease Prevention Program.
*   *Program Date (3)*: Enter the date of the Global Disease Prevention Program.
*   *Program Time (4)*: Enter the time of the Global Disease Prevention Program.
*   *Program Outcomes Measured (5)*: Indicate which outcomes the program is measured. (Yes, No)
*   *Participant Feedback (6)*: Provide your feedback on how satisfied you were with the program. (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
*   *Participant ID (12)*: Enter your participant ID for identification purposes.
*   *Participant Name (13)*: Enter your name.
*   *Participant Contact Method (14)*: Enter your contact method.
*   *Contact Person (15)*: Enter the contact person's name.
*   *Participant Phone (16)*: Enter your phone number.
*   *Participant Email (17)*: Enter your email address. This will be confirmed in the next field.
*   *Participant Email Confirmation (18)*: Confirm your email address.
*   *Participant Survey Completed (19)*: Record the date and time when you completed the survey.
*   *Survey Type (20)*: Indicate if this is a **Global Disease Prevention Program Evaluation Form** or other.
*   *Participant Survey Type (23)*: Indicate how you completed the survey. (Online, Offline, Both)
*   *Program Type (22)*: Indicate if it's a **Global Disease Prevention Program** or other.
*   *Participant Survey Language (25)*: Indicate what language the survey was completed in.
*   *Program Language (24)*: Indicate what language the program used.
*   *Participant Survey Completed Time (26)*: Record the time when you completed the survey.
