# Chronic Disease Prevention Policy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Prevention Policy Evaluation Form is designed to gauge the effectiveness and accessibility of current policies related to chronic disease prevention. It assesses the awareness, familiarity, and implementation of various policy types, as well as their impact on health equity and accessibility. This form aims to identify areas that require improvement and gather recommendations for future policy development.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure you complete the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  **Provide your contact information**:
    - Enter your full name in the `respondent_name` field (optional).
    - Enter your email address in the `email_address` field (required).
2.  **Identify your organization**:
    - Provide the name of your organization in the `organization` field (optional).
    - Select the type of organization you belong to from the `organization_type` dropdown menu (required).
3.  **Describe your role**:
    - Choose your role from the `role` dropdown menu (required).
4.  **Assess policy awareness and effectiveness**:
    - Select how aware you are of current policies in the `policy_awareness` dropdown menu (required).
    - Choose how effective you think current policies are in the `policy_effectiveness` dropdown menu (required).
5.  **Evaluate policy accessibility and equity**:
    - Select how accessible current policies are to those who need them in the `policy_accessibility` dropdown menu (required).
    - Choose how well current policies address health disparities in the `policy_equity` dropdown menu (required).
6.  **Identify implementation barriers and challenges**:
    - Select all applicable barriers that hinder policy implementation from the `implementation_barriers` dropdown menu (required).
7.  **Assess data collection and evidence-based approaches**:
    - Select how well data is collected and used to inform policies in the `data_collection` dropdown menu (required).
    - Choose how well policies are based on evidence in the `evidence_based` dropdown menu (required).
8.  **Evaluate program sustainability and funding**:
    - Select how sustainable current programs are in the `sustainability` dropdown menu (required).
    - Choose how sufficient funding is for current programs in the `funding_adequacy` dropdown menu (required).
9.  **Identify priority areas for improvement**:
    - Select the top priority areas for improvement from the `priority_areas` dropdown menu (required).
10. **Submit recommendations**:
    - In the `recommendations` text field, provide your suggestions for policy development.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *respondent_name* (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your full name for contact purposes.
*   *email_address* (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
*   *organization* (`organization`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of your organization.
*   *organization_type* (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of organization you belong to from the list provided.
*   *role* (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role from the list provided.
*   *policy_awareness* (`policy_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Choose how aware you are of current policies.
*   *policy_types_familiar* (`policy_types_familiar`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all policy types you are familiar with.
*   *policy_effectiveness* (`policy_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Choose how effective you think current policies are.
*   *policy_access* (`policy_access`, `select_one`, required): Select how accessible current policies are.
*   *policy_equity* (`policy_equity`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well current policies address health disparities.
*   *implementation_barriers* (`implementation_barriers`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all barriers that hinder policy implementation.
*   *data_collection* (`data_collection`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well data is collected and used.
*   *outcome_measurement* (`outcome_measurement`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well policies are evaluated.
*   *evidence_based* (`evidence_based`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well policies are evidence-based.
*   *funding_adequacy* (`funding_adequacy`, `select_one`, required): Select how sufficient funding is.
*   *sustainability* (`sustainability`, `select_one`, required): Choose how sustainable current programs are.
*   *priority_areas* (`priority_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top priority areas for improvement.
*   *recommendations* (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide your suggestions for policy development.
*   *successful_examples* (`successful_examples`, `text`, optional): Share successful policy examples.
*   *lessons_learned* (`lessons_learned`, `text`, optional): Share lessons learned from policy development.
*   *future_directions* (`future_directions`, `text`, optional): Suggest where policy focus should next be.
*   *willing_to_participate* (`willing_to_participate`, `select_one`, required): Select your willingness to participate in follow-up.

## Tips

*   Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   Select all applicable barriers for policy implementation.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing recommendations and suggestions.
*   This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please try to be concise.
