# Breast Imaging History Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breast Imaging History Survey is designed to gather information on a patient's breast imaging history, helping healthcare providers assess risk factors and create informed decisions about future imaging procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's full legal name in the **Patient Name** field (page 1).
2. Provide the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field (page 1).
3. If applicable, enter the patient's medical record number in the **Patient ID** field (page 2).
4. Enter the date of this survey in the **Survey Date** field (page 2).
5. Answer the questions on pages 3-6 regarding the patient's previous breast imaging experiences, cancer history, and family history.
6. On page 7, select all types of imaging the patient has had in the past.
7. On page 8, select the outcome of any previous biopsy, if applicable.
8. On page 9, indicate if the patient has been diagnosed with breast cancer and select the corresponding options.
9. If the patient has been diagnosed with breast cancer, select the cancer treatments they have received on page 10.
10. On page 11, select all current breast symptoms the patient is experiencing.
11. On page 12, indicate the patient's family history of breast cancer.
12. On page 13, select any known genetic risk factors.
13. On page 14, select the patient's current hormone therapy use.
14. On page 15, select the recommended imaging schedule for the patient.
15. On page 16, enter any patient concerns about imaging.
15. On page 17, enter the recommended follow-up plan for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Date of Birth (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient ID (Page 2)**: Enter the patient's medical record number, if applicable.
* **Survey Date (Page 2)**: Enter the date of this survey.
* **Have you had breast imaging before (Page 3)**: Select the frequency of previous breast imaging experiences:
	+ Yes, multiple times
	+ Yes, a few times
	+ Yes, once
	+ No, this is my first
* **Date of Last Breast Imaging (Page 4)**: Enter the date of the patient's most recent breast imaging.
* **Types of Imaging You Have Had (Page 5)**: Select all types of imaging the patient has had:
	+ Mammogram
	+ Ultrasound
	+ MRI
	+ Tomosynthesis - 3D mammography
	+ PET scan
	+ Other imaging
	+ None
* **Previous Imaging Findings (Page 6)**: Select the outcome of previous imaging:
	+ Normal
	+ Benign findings
	+ Abnormalities requiring follow-up
	+ Cancerous findings
	+ Unknown
* **Have you had a breast biopsy (Page 7)**: Select the number of times the patient has had a breast biopsy:
	+ No biopsy
	+ Yes, one biopsy
	+ Yes, multiple biopsies
	+ Unsure
* **Biopsy Results (Page 8)**: Select the outcome of any previous biopsy:
	+ Benign
	+ Malignant
	+ Uncertain
	+ Not performed
* **Have you been diagnosed with breast cancer (Page 9)**: Select the patient's history of breast cancer diagnosis:
	+ No cancer diagnosis
	+ Yes, treated
	+ Yes, currently being treated
	+ Yes, under surveillance
* **Cancer Treatments Received (Page 10)**: Select all cancer treatments the patient has received:
	+ Surgery
	+ Chemotherapy
	+ Radiation therapy
	+ Hormone therapy
	+ Immunotherapy
	+ Combination therapy
	+ No treatment
* **Current Breast Symptoms (Page 11)**: Select all current breast symptoms the patient is experiencing:
	+ Breast pain or tenderness
	+ Breast lump
	+ Nipple discharge
	+ Nipple retraction
	+ Skin changes or dimpling
	+ Swelling
	+ No symptoms
* **Family History of Breast Cancer (Page 12)**: Select the patient's family history of breast cancer:
	+ No family history
	+ One relative with breast cancer
	+ Multiple relatives with cancer
	+ Early-onset cancer in family
	+ Unknown
* **Known Genetic Risk Factors (Page 13)**: Select the patient's known genetic risk factors:
	+ BRCA1 positive
	+ BRCA2 positive
	+ Other genetic mutation
	+ Genetic testing negative
	+ Not tested
* **Current Hormone Therapy Use (Page 14)**: Select all current hormone therapy use:
	+ Hormone replacement therapy
	+ Oral contraceptives
	+ Hormone-releasing IUD
	+ Tamoxifen or other therapy
	+ None
* **Recommended Imaging Schedule (Page 15)**: Select the recommended imaging schedule:
	+ Annual
	+ Every 2 years
	+ Every 3 years
	+ As needed based on findings
	+ Not yet determined
* **Patient Concerns About Imaging (Page 16)**: Enter any patient concerns about imaging.
* **Follow-Up Plan (Page 17)**: Enter the recommended follow-up plan for the patient.
