# Access To Affordable Care Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Access To Affordable Care Perception Survey" is a comprehensive form designed to gather your opinions and experiences regarding accessing healthcare services. Your responses will help inform policies and improve healthcare accessibility and affordability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the most relevant answer for each question based on your experiences and opinions.
2. Answer all questions, including the optional ones, to provide a comprehensive picture of your healthcare access situation.
3. Be honest and accurate when providing your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age Group (participant\_age\_group)**: Select the age group that best describes you.
	+ 18-24: You are between 18 and 24 years old.
	+ 25-34: You are between 25 and 34 years old.
	+ 35-44: You are between 35 and 44 years old.
	+ 45-54: You are between 45 and 54 years old.
	+ 55-64: You are between 55 and 64 years old.
	+ 65 or older: You are 65 years old or older.
* **Household Income Range (household\_income\_range)**: Select the income range that best describes your household's annual income.
	+ Less than $25,000: Your household's annual income is below $25,000.
	+ $25,000-$49,999: Your household's annual income is between $25,000 and $49,999.
	+ $50,000-$74,999: Your household's annual income is between $50,000 and $74,999.
	+ $75,000-$99,999: Your household's annual income is between $75,000 and $99,999.
	+ $100,000-$149,999: Your household's annual income is between $100,000 and $149,999.
	+ $150,000 or more: Your household's annual income is $150,000 or more.
* **Insurance Coverage Status (insurance\_coverage\_status)**: Select your current insurance coverage status.
	+ Private insurance: You have private health insurance.
	+ Employer-sponsored insurance: You have insurance through your employer.
	+ Medicare: You have Medicare coverage.
	+ Medicaid: You have Medicaid coverage.
	+ Uninsured: You do not have any health insurance coverage.
	+ Other government program: You have insurance through another government program.
* **Geographic Location (geographic\_location)**: Select the location where you live.
	+ Urban: You live in an urban area.
	+ Suburban: You live in a suburban area.
	+ Rural: You live in a rural area.
* **Overall Health Rating (overall\_health\_rating)**: Select your overall health rating.
	+ Excellent: You rate your overall health as excellent.
	+ Very Good: You rate your overall health as very good.
	+ Good: You rate your overall health as good.
	+ Fair: You rate your overall health as fair.
	+ Poor: You rate your overall health as poor.
* **Healthcare Access Difficulty (healthcare\_access\_difficulty)**: Select the difficulty level you face in accessing healthcare services.
	+ Very Difficult: You find it very difficult to access healthcare services.
	+ Difficult: You find it difficult to access healthcare services.
	+ Moderate: You find it moderately difficult to access healthcare services.
	+ Easy: You find it easy to access healthcare services.
	+ Very Easy: You find it very easy to access healthcare services.
* **Healthcare Cost Concern (healthcare\_cost\_concern)**: Select the level of concern you have regarding healthcare costs.
	+ Extremely Concerned: You are extremely concerned about healthcare costs.
	+ Very Concerned: You are very concerned about healthcare costs.
	+ Moderately Concerned: You are moderately concerned about healthcare costs.
	+ Slightly Concerned: You are slightly concerned about healthcare costs.
	+ Not Concerned: You are not concerned about healthcare costs.
* **Preventive Care Access (preventive\_care\_access)**: Select the ease of access you have to preventive care services.
	+ Very Easy: You find it very easy to access preventive care services.
	+ Easy: You find it easy to access preventive care services.
	+ Moderate: You find it moderately easy to access preventive care services.
	+ Difficult: You find it difficult to access preventive care services.
	+ Very Difficult: You find it very difficult to access preventive care services.
* **Specialist Access Difficulty (specialist\_access\_difficulty)**: Select the difficulty level you face in getting appointments with specialists.
	+ Very Difficult: You find it very difficult to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Difficult: You find it difficult to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Moderate: You find it moderately difficult to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Easy: You find it easy to get appointments with specialists.
	+ Very Easy: You find it very easy to get appointments with specialists.
* **Transportation Barrier (transportation\_barrier)**: Select the impact of transportation on your healthcare access.
	+ Yes, Always: Transportation always prevents you from accessing healthcare.
	+ Yes, Often: Transportation often prevents you from accessing healthcare.
	+ Yes, Sometimes: Transportation occasionally prevents you from accessing healthcare.
	+ Rarely: Transportation rarely prevents you from accessing healthcare.
	+ No: Transportation does not prevent you from accessing healthcare.
* **Healthcare Quality Perception (healthcare\_quality\_perception)**: Select the quality of healthcare available to you.
	+ Excellent: You rate the quality of healthcare as excellent.
	+ Good: You rate the quality of healthcare as good.
	+ Fair: You rate the quality of healthcare as fair.
	+ Poor: You rate the quality of healthcare as poor.
	+ Cannot Assess: You cannot assess the quality of healthcare.
* **Healthcare Affordability Rating (healthcare\_affordability\_rating)**: Select the affordability of healthcare services to you.
	+ Very Affordable: You find healthcare services very affordable.
	+ Affordable: You find healthcare services affordable.
	+ Moderately Affordable: You find healthcare services moderately affordable.
	+ Unaffordable: You find healthcare services unaffordable.
	+ Very Unaffordable: You find healthcare services very unaffordable.
* **Prescription Medicine Access (prescription\_medicine\_access)**: Select the ease of access you have to prescribed medications.
	+ Very Easy: You find it very easy to access prescribed medications.
	+ Easy: You find it easy to access prescribed medications.
	+ Moderate: You find it moderately easy to access prescribed medications.
	+ Difficult: You find it difficult to access prescribed medications.
	+ Very Difficult: You find it very difficult to access prescribed medications.
* **Mental Health Services Access (mental\_health\_services\_access)**: Select the accessibility of mental health services to you.
	+ Very Accessible: Mental health services are very accessible to you.
	+ Accessible: Mental health services are accessible to you.
	+ Moderately Accessible: Mental health services are moderately accessible to you.
	+ Inaccessible: Mental health services are inaccessible to you.
	+ Very Inaccessible: Mental health services are very inaccessible to you.
* **Healthcare Wait Times (healthcare\_wait\_times)**: Select the wait time for healthcare appointments.
	+ Excellent: You rate the wait times as excellent.
	+ Good: You rate the wait times as good.
	+ Fair: You rate the wait times as fair.
	+ Poor: You rate the wait times as poor.
	+ Terrible: You rate the wait times as terrible.
* **Healthcare Availability Perception (healthcare\_availability\_perception)**: Select the availability of healthcare providers in your area.
	+ More Than Enough: There are more healthcare providers than you need.
	+ Enough: There are enough healthcare providers for your needs.
	+ Somewhat Enough: There are somewhat enough healthcare providers for your needs.
	+ Not Enough: There are not enough healthcare providers for your needs.
	+ Far From Enough: There are far from enough healthcare providers for your needs.
* **Healthcare System Satisfaction (healthcare\_system\_satisfaction)**: Select your satisfaction with the healthcare system.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the healthcare system.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the healthcare system.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about the healthcare system.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are dissatisfied with the healthcare system.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are very dissatisfied with the healthcare system.
* **Policy Improvement Priority (policy\_improvement\_priority)**: Select the area that should be prioritized for improvement.
	+ Reduce costs: Reduce healthcare costs.
	+ Increase access: Increase access to healthcare services.
	+ Improve quality: Improve the quality of healthcare services.
	+ Expand coverage: Expand healthcare coverage.
	+ Reduce wait times: Reduce wait times for healthcare appointments.
	+ Improve provider availability: Improve the availability of healthcare providers.
* **Additional Barriers (additional\_barriers)**: If you have any other barriers to accessing affordable healthcare, describe them.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (suggestions\_for\_improvement)**: If you have any suggestions to improve access to affordable healthcare, describe them.

## Tips

* Answer all questions, including the optional ones, to provide a comprehensive picture of your healthcare access situation.
* Your responses will help inform policies to improve healthcare accessibility and affordability.
