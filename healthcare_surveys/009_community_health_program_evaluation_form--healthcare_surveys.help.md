# Community Health Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from community health program participants on their experience with the program. It will help us understand what works and what doesn't, and identify areas for improvement to better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the role you played in the program (participant, staff, volunteer, or other).
2.  If you attended a specific program or session, enter the name of the program or session.
3.  If applicable, choose one of the provided dates when you attended the program.
4.  Rate the overall quality of the program on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
5.  Describe the most important outcomes or takeaways from the program.
6.  Share any positive or negative changes that resulted from your participation in the program.
7.  If you faced any barriers to participating in the program, select them from the list.
8.  Provide suggestions for improvement, if any.
9.  If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`Respondent role`, `select_one`): This field is used to identify your role within the program (participant, staff, volunteer, or other).
* **Program attended** (`Program attended`, `text`): Enter the name of the program or session you attended. This will help us connect your feedback with the specific program you're commenting on.
* **Date of attendance** (`Date of attendance`, `date`): If you can remember the date you attended, please enter it here. This helps us track when the program was attended.
* **Satisfaction rating** (`Satisfaction rating`, `select_one`): Rate the overall quality of the program on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent). This will help us understand how well the program met your needs.
* **Most important outcomes** (`Most important outcomes`, `text`): Describe the most important outcomes or takeaways from the program. This will help us understand what worked best for you.
* **Program impact** (`Program impact`, `text`): Share any positive or negative changes that resulted from your participation in the program. This will help us understand the program's impact on your daily life.
* **Barriers to participation** (`Barriers to participation`, `select_multiple`): If you faced any barriers to participating in the program, select them from the list. This will help us identify potential areas for improvement.
* **Recommendations for improvement** (`Recommendations for improvement`, `text`): If you have any suggestions for improving the program, please share them here.
* **Contact email for follow up** (`Contact email for follow up`, `email`): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address here.

**Tips**

* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience and providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your input is invaluable, and we appreciate your time spent on this form!
