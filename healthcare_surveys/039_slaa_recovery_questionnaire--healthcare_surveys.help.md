# SLAA Recovery Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SLAA Recovery Questionnaire is a tool designed to collect information about your recovery progress and experiences. It's essential to answer these questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have permission to complete it.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about a question, try to provide an answer that best reflects your current understanding or experience.
4. Submit your responses when you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ This section is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
	+ Please read it carefully and understand the context before proceeding.
* **Name**:
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.).
	+ This will help us to identify you uniquely in our records.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you (if necessary).
	+ This will help us to reach out to you for any further clarification or follow-up.
* **Sex**:
	+ Select the correct option that best describes your sex (e.g., Female, Male, Non-binary, Other).
	+ This information is important for statistical purposes and to ensure we provide relevant support.
* **Birthdate**:
	+ Enter your birthdate in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
	+ This will help us to calculate your age and provide relevant information.
* **Phone Number**:
	+ Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you (if necessary).
	+ This will help us to reach out to you for any further clarification or follow-up.
* **Question 1: What is your favorite thing about recovery?**:
	+ Provide a detailed answer to this question, describing your favorite aspect of the recovery process.
	+ This will help us to understand your preferences and experiences.
* **Question 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8**:
	+ Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
	+ These questions are designed to gather more information about your recovery experiences and progress.
