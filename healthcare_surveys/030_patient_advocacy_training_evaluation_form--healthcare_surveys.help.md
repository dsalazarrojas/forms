# patient-advocacy-training-evaluation-form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of patient advocacy training programs in healthcare settings. It is used to collect feedback from participants on their experience with the training, their perception of its relevance and clarity, and any suggestions they may have for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing your feedback on the patient advocacy training program.
3. Select the options that best match your experience and perception.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required): This section introduces the form and explains its purpose. Please read it carefully to understand what the form is asking you to evaluate.
* **Do you consent to participate in this study?** (`2`, `select_one`, required): This question is used to check if you are willing to participate in the study. Please select either "I agree" or "I do not agree".
* **Has your patient advocacy training been satisfactory?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your experience with the training. Please select all the options that apply to you (e.g., "True", "False").
* **Was your patient advocacy training relevant to your job?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about the relevance of the training to your work. Please select all the options that apply to you (e.g., "True", "False").
* **Was the patient advocacy training evaluation process clear to you?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your understanding of the evaluation process. Please select all the options that apply to you (e.g., "True", "False").
* **Would you like to have a follow-up meeting?** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks if you would like to have a follow-up meeting to discuss your experience. Please select all the options that apply to you (e.g., "True", "False").
* **Comments about the patient advocacy training evaluation form** (`7`, `text`, required): This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback on the form itself. Please keep your comments clear and concise.
* **Suggestions for improving the patient advocacy training evaluation form** (`8`, `text`, required): This is another text field where you can suggest any improvements or changes to the form. Please keep your suggestions clear and specific.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the patient advocacy training program.
* Please return the form once you have completed all the questions and sections.
