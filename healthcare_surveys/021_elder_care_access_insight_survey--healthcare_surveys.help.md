# Elder Care Access Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elder Care Access Insight Survey is designed to gather information about your organization or your experiences within the elder care sector. This form aims to understand your services, challenges, and level of satisfaction to provide insights for improvements and development within the sector.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. For fields that allow multiple options to be selected (like "what_elder_care_services_do_you_provide" and "what_regions_do_you_provide_services_to"), make sure to select all applicable options.
4. If you have any other feedback or comments, please provide them in the "any_other_feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Role in the Elder Care Sector**: This should be your role or position within the organization you're representing.
* **What elder care services do you provide**: Select all elder care services your organization offers.
* **Barriers to Providing Services**: If your organization faces any challenges or barriers in providing services, please describe them here.
* **Level of Satisfaction**: On a scale of 1-10, rate your level of satisfaction with the services provided to you or your organization.
* **Regions You Provide Services To**: Select all the regions your organization serves.
* **Any Other Feedback**: If you have any other comments or feedback about the survey or elder care services, please provide them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Other**: If there's anything else you'd like to share that hasn't been covered, please enter it here.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate to ensure meaningful insights are captured.
- If you need to skip any field, please make a note of it and explain why you cannot provide the information.
- The more detailed your responses, the more valuable the insights provided will be to the sector.
