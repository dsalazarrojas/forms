# Digital Public Health Leadership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Public Health Leadership Application Form is a tool used to collect applications for leadership positions in a healthcare setting. This guide will walk you through the process of completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4. Attach your resume if applicable (see Resume Attachment field for details).
5. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification or official documents.
* **Email** (3): Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your work phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Title** (5): Enter your current job title or the job title you are applying for.
* **Work Experience** (6): Select all relevant experience you have, including but not limited to:
	+ Option 1: Leadership Role
	+ Option 2: Project Management
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in the field below)
* **Motivation** (7): Write a brief statement explaining why you are interested in the leadership position and what skills or experience you bring to the role.
* **Job Description** (8): Briefly describe the job you are applying for or the position you are currently in.
* **Resume** (9): Attach a copy of your resume or a brief summary of your relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Resume Attachment** (10): Upload a copy of your resume or a link to a publicly accessible online version of it.

Note: Fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are optional but highly encouraged to be filled out for a complete application.
