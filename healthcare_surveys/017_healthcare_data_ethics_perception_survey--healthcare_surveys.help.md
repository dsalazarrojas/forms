# Healthcare Data Ethics Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather healthcare professionals' opinions and experiences on various aspects of data ethics, including electronic health records, their role in healthcare, and using patient data for research. Your participation is essential to help us better understand these critical topics and improve our approach to data handling in healthcare.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate response based on your experiences and opinions.
3. If you're unsure or have no preference, please select "Neither agree nor disagree" for multiple-choice questions.
4. You can skip any questions that are not relevant to your role or experiences.
5. When completed, submit your responses to proceed to the next question or page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **user_demographics** (`user_demographics`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose your demographic category:
* Male: You identify as male.
* Female: You identify as female.
* Other: You identify as something else (please specify in the "Other" field).
* Prefer not to say: You prefer not to disclose your demographic category.

### 2. **age** (`age`, `number`, optional)
Enter your age in years. Please note that this question is optional and will not affect your survey completion.

### 3. **What is your role in healthcare?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all that apply:
* Doctor: You are a doctor.
* Nurse: You are a nurse.
* Other: You are in another healthcare role (please specify in the "Other" field).
* None of the above: This doesn't apply to you.

### 4. **How often do you use electronic health records?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, optional)
* Daily: You use electronic health records daily.
* Weekly: You use electronic health records weekly.
* Monthly: You use electronic health records monthly.
* Never: You do not use electronic health records.

### 5. **How do you feel about using patient data for research?** (`question_3`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all that apply:
* Agree: You agree with using patient data for research.
* Disagree: You disagree with using patient data for research.
* Neither agree nor disagree: You are neutral on this topic.
* Prefer not to answer: You prefer not to answer this question.
