# healthcare_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The healthcare_communication_form is designed to collect and store patient information in a structured and organized manner. This form is intended to be used in healthcare settings, such as medical offices, clinics, or hospitals, to gather essential details about a patient's medical history, medical conditions, allergies, medications, and other relevant health information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Fill in the patient's demographic information, including their name, date of birth, and contact preferences.
3.  Provide information about the patient's medical history, including any medical conditions, allergies, and medications.
4.  List any medical procedures or specialties related to the patient's care.
5.  Select the preferred contact method(s) for communication.
6.  Indicate an emergency contact person and their contact information.
7.  Sign and date the form to confirm its completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): This field collects the patient's name, date of birth, and other demographic information.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): This field is for listing any medical history relevant to the patient's care.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies the patient has.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required): List any current medications the patient is taking.
*   **Medical Practices** (`medical_practices`, `text`, required): List any medical practices or procedures relevant to the patient's care.
*   **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, `text`, required): List any medical procedures related to the patient's care.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions the patient has.
*   **Contact Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, SMS, Mail).
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate an emergency contact person and their contact information (Home, Work, Mobile, None).
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the contact person.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required): This field is for the patient's signature, acknowledging that they have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
*   **Practice Signature** (`practice_signature`, `note`, required): This field is for the healthcare provider's signature, confirming that they have reviewed and will use the information collected.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Provide the patient's date of birth.
*   **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, optional): Provide the time of the patient's visit.
*   **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, optional): Provide the date of the patient's visit.
*   **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, `text`, optional): List any medical specialties related to the patient's care.
*   **Medical Procedures 2** (`medical_procedures_2`, `text`, optional): List any other medical procedures related to the patient's care.
*   **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, `text`, optional): List any other medical conditions the patient has.
*   **Medical Practices 2** (`medical_practices_2`, `text`, optional): List any other medical practices or procedures relevant to the patient's care.
*   **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, optional): List any other medical history relevant to the patient's care.
*   **Allergies 2** (`allergies_2`, `text`, optional): List any other allergies the patient has.
*   **Contact Preferences 2** (`contact_preferences_2`, `text`, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (2nd).
*   **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, `text`, optional): Indicate an emergency contact person and their contact information (2nd).
*   **Contact Person 2** (`contact_person_2`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the 2nd contact person.
*   **Patient Signature 2** (`patient_signature_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for the patient's signature, acknowledging that they have reviewed and agreed to the information provided.
*   **Practice Signature 2** (`practice_signature_2`, `text`, optional): This field is for the healthcare provider's signature, confirming that they have reviewed and will use the information collected.

## Tips

*   Always review the form carefully before proceeding and ensure you have all the necessary information.
*   Be mindful of patient confidentiality and data security at all times.
*   Verify the information provided by the patient and consult with medical professionals whenever necessary.
*   Use this form in accordance with your organization's policies and procedures.
