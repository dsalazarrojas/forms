# Healthcare Outreach Material Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about healthcare outreach materials to ensure accurate and efficient delivery to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your personal details as required.
2.  Select the correct type of material you are delivering.
3.  Choose the distribution channel where the material will be delivered.
4.  Enter the date of distribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient to whom the material will be delivered.
*   **Material Type** (`material_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of material you are delivering, such as Educational Booklet, Brochure, Poster, or Other.
*   **Distribution Channel** (`distribution_channel`, select one, optional): Choose the channel where the material will be delivered, such as In Office, Mail, Online, Social Media, or Other.
*   **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the patient to whom the material will be delivered.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the material will be delivered.
*   **Date of Distribution** (`date_of_distribution`, date, optional): Enter the date when the material will be distributed.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the material delivery.
*   **Healthcare Provider Email** (`healthcare_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider responsible for the material delivery.
*   **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider responsible for the material delivery.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned for this material delivery.
*   **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the patient to whom the material will be delivered.
*   **Assigned Tool User** (`assigned_tool_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to this material delivery.
*   **Material ID** (`material_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for this material delivery.

## Tips

*   Please fill out all fields as accurately as possible.
*   Double-check the details before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
