# Health Coverage Qualification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about a patient's medical history and coverage for health coverage qualification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions carefully and accurately.
2. Fill out each section completely.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction**
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, note, no required): A brief note about the purpose of the form.

### **Age**
- **Age** (`Age`, number, required): Your age in years.
### **Date of Birth**
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
### **Phone**
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Your phone number.
### **Email**
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Your email address.
### **Medical Provider**
- **Medical Provider** (`Medical Provider`, select_multiple, no required): Check if you have a medical provider (Yes/No).

### **Health Care Plan**
- **Health Care Plan** (`Health Care Plan`, select_multiple, no required): Check if you have a health care plan (Yes/No).
