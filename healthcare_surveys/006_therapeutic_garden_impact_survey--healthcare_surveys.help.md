# therapeutic_garden_impact_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapeutic Garden Impact Survey is a tool used to assess the effectiveness of a therapeutic garden on participants' well-being. This survey aims to collect feedback from participants on their experiences and feelings during and after visiting the garden.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer every question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding to the next page.
3. Take your time to answer each question honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Garden Visit** (`garden_visit`, `text`, required: false): Describe your visit to the garden, including any notable events or experiences.
* **Feelings Before** (`feelings_before`, `number`, required: false): Rate your feelings before visiting the garden on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means "not good" and 10 means "excellent".
* **Feelings After** (`feelings_after`, `number`, required: false): Rate your feelings after visiting the garden on a scale of 1-10, where 1 means "not good" and 10 means "excellent".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `text`, required: false): Describe your overall satisfaction with the garden and its impact on your well-being.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Suggest ways to improve the therapeutic garden, if any.
* **Garden Visit Length** (`garden_visit_length`, `number`, required: false): Report the length of your visit to the garden.
* **Garden Visit Frequency** (`garden_visit_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how often you visit the garden, with options "Visited once a month", "Visited twice a week", "Visited daily", "Never", and "Not Sure".
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address, if you wish to provide it.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you wish to provide it.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_visit`, `date`, required: false): Report the date of your visit to the garden.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_visit`, `time`, required: false): Report the time of your visit to the garden.
* **Comments** (`garden_visit_comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any comments or observations about your visit to the garden.
* **Staff Feedback** (`staff_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback to the staff on their assistance or interaction with you during your visit.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your identity and participation in the survey.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `text`, required: false): Ask a staff member to sign their name to confirm their role and participation in the survey.
* **Date of Staff Signature** (`date_staff_signature`, `date`, required: false): Report the date of the staff signature.
* **Time of Staff Signature** (`time_staff_signature`, `time`, required: false): Report the time of the staff signature.
* **Participant Signature Confirmation** (`participant_signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your signature, with options "Confirmed" and "Not Confirmed".
* **Staff Signature Confirmation** (`staff_signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm the staff member's signature, with options "Confirmed" and "Not Confirmed".
* **Type of Garden** (`garden_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of garden that best describes it, with options "Butterfly Garden", "Sensory Garden", "Healing Garden", and "Other".
* **Location of Garden** (`garden_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the location of the garden, with options "Hospital", "School", "Home", and "Other".
* **Participant Age Range** (`participant_age_range`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the age range of the participant, with options "0-5", "6-17", "18-64", and "65+".
* **Participant Diagnosis** (`participant_diagnosis`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the participant's diagnosis, with options "Mental Health", "Physical Disability", and "Other".
* **Participant Goals** (`participant_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the participant's goals, with options "Stress Relief", "Cognitive Stimulation", "Social Interaction", and "Other".
