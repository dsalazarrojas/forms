# Daily Healthcare Workload Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare workers track and report their daily workload, interactions with patients, and work status. The information gathered through this form will help management and administrators make informed decisions and optimize work schedules.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately, providing details about your daily interactions with patients and your work status.
2. Use the provided options or select the correct answers based on your experiences.
3. Make sure to enter the correct time values for Work Start Time, Work Finish Time, and Shift Start/End.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Interactions
* **Patient Interactions** (`patient_interactions`, number, optional): Enter the number of patient interactions you had today.
### Work Start Time
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you started your work today.
### Work Finish Time
* **Work Finish Time** (`work_finish_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you finished your work today.
### Time Worked
* **Time Worked** (`time_worked`, number, optional): Enter the total time worked today.
### Break Time
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, number, optional): Enter the total break time taken today.
### Breaks
* **Breaks** (`breaks`, select multiple, optional): Select the number of breaks taken today. Options include:
	+ 30 minutes
	+ 60 minutes
	+ 90 minutes
### Patient Wait Time
* **Patient Wait Time** (`patient_wait_time`, number, optional): Enter the total wait time spent on patients.
### Time Between Patients
* **Time Between Patients** (`time_between_patients`, number, optional): Enter the time spent between patient visits.
### Work Status
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select one, optional): Select your work status for today.
	+ Busy
	+ Available
	+ On Break
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your work today.
### Number of Patients
* **Number of Patients** (`patient_count`, number, optional): Enter the total number of patients seen today.
### Number of Patient Visits
* **Number of Patient Visits** (`patient_visits`, number, optional): Enter the total number of patient visits today.
### Number of Surgeries
* **Number of Surgeries** (`patient_surgeries`, number, optional): Enter the total number of surgeries performed today.
### Number of Emergencies
* **Number of Emergencies** (`patient_emergencies`, number, optional): Enter the total number of emergencies responded to today.
### Number of Requests
* **Number of Requests** (`patient_requests`, number, optional): Enter the total number of patient requests received today.
### Work Level
* **Work Level** (`work_level`, select one, optional): Select the level of work intensity today.
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
### Priority
* **Priority** (`priority`, select one, optional): Select the level of priority given to your work today.
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
### Shift Start
* **Shift Start** (`shift_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
### Shift End
* **Shift End** (`shift_end`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
### Hours Worked Today
* **Hours Worked Today** (`hours_worked_today`, number, optional): Enter the total hours worked today.
### Breaks Taken
* **Breaks Taken** (`breaks_taken`, number, optional): Enter the total number of breaks taken today.
### Break Durations
* **Break Durations** (`break_durations`, note, optional): Enter a note about your break durations.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your work today.
### Total Break Time
* **Total Break Time** (`total_break_time`, number, optional): Enter the total break time taken today.
## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure accurate representation of your workload.
- Enter all relevant fields to provide a comprehensive view of your work today.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or administrator.
 
Please note that the fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled out, but it's recommended to provide as much information as possible for better analysis.
