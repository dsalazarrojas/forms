## Purpose
The Healthcare Policy Literacy Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions on healthcare policies from users.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each page and its instructions before proceeding.
2. Answer the questions accurately and honestly, based on your understanding of the policies.
