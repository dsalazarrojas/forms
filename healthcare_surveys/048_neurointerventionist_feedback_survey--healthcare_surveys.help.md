# neurointerventionist_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from patients or their representatives about their experience with a neurointerventionist. The purpose of this form is to help assess the quality of care provided by the neurointerventionist and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
*   Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
*   For fields with a checkbox, select the relevant option (True or False).
*   For the text fields, type your response directly into the field.
*   For numerical rating fields, select a rating from the provided options.
*   Make sure to provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Can you describe your current experience with this neurointerventionist?

*   **Can you describe your current experience with this neurointerventionist?** (`current_experience`, text, required)
    + This question is designed to understand your current experience with the neurointerventionist. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any notable events or interactions you've had with the neurointerventionist.

### 2. How would you rate the communication skills of the neurointerventionist?

*   **How would you rate the communication skills of the neurointerventionist?** (`rating_neurointerventionist`, number, required)
    + This question is meant to gauge your perception of the neurointerventionist's communication skills. Choose a rating that best reflects your experience with the neurointerventionist.

### 3. Were your questions and concerns addressed during the session?

*   **Were your questions and concerns addressed during the session?** (`concerns_addressed`, checkbox, required)
    + This question is designed to determine if the neurointerventionist adequately addressed your questions and concerns. Choose True if they were addressed, False otherwise.

### 4. How satisfied are you with the overall session?

*   **How satisfied are you with the overall session?** (`satisfaction`, number, required)
    + This question is meant to gauge your satisfaction with the session as a whole. Choose a rating that best reflects your level of satisfaction.

### 5. Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for the neurointerventionist?

*   **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for the neurointerventionist?** (`additional_comments`, text, required)
    + This question is designed to gather any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have about the neurointerventionist. Please provide clear and concise comments.

### 6. Would you recommend this neurointerventionist to a friend or family member?

*   **Would you recommend this neurointerventionist to a friend or family member?** (`recommendation`, checkbox, required)
    + This question is meant to determine if you would recommend the neurointerventionist to others. Choose True if you would recommend, False otherwise.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
*   If you have any difficulties or concerns, feel free to contact us.
*   Your feedback is greatly appreciated and helps us improve our services.
