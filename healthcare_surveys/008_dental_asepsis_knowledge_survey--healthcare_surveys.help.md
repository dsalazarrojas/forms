# Dental Asepsis Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to assess the knowledge, practice, and confidence of dental professionals regarding asepsis, which is a crucial aspect of infection control and prevention in dental settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the type of clinic you are working in (dental, medical, or other).
2. Answer the questions regarding your current asepsis knowledge and practice.
3. Select how often you have received asepsis training.
4. Indicate your level of awareness and confidence in asepsis practices.
5. Describe how often you follow asepsis practices in your daily work.
6. Select how often you follow asepsis practices.
7. Indicate your confidence level in asepsis supervision and policies.
8. Select how often you follow asepsis supervision and policies.
9. Indicate your confidence level in asepsis supervision and policy guidelines.
10. Select your level of confidence in your current asepsis policy.
11. Select how often your asepsis policy is reviewed or updated.
12. Describe the last time your asepsis policy was reviewed or updated.
13. Enter your name and contact information.
14. Please provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **clinic_type**: Select the type of clinic you are working in. This could be a dental clinic, a medical clinic, or another type of clinic.
* **Asepsis Knowledge**: This is a note field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your current asepsis knowledge.
* **Asepsis Training Needs**: Select whether you think asepsis training is required for you, not required, or you are unsure.
* **Asepsis Training Frequency**: Select how often you have received asepsis training. This could be less than a year, between 1-2 years, or more than 2 years.
* **Asepsis Awareness**: Select your level of awareness about asepsis practices. You can strongly agree, somewhat agree, not much agree, or not at all agree.
* **Asepsis Practice**: Select how often you follow asepsis practices in your daily work. You can always follow asepsis practices, often, sometimes, rarely, or never.
* **Asepsis Practice Frequency**: Select how often you follow asepsis practices. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Asepsis Practice Confidence**: Select your confidence level in following asepsis practices. You can be very confident, somewhat confident, not very confident, or not at all confident.
* **Supervision**: Select whether you always, sometimes, or rarely follow asepsis supervision.
* **Supervision Frequency**: Select how often you follow asepsis supervision. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Supervision Confidence**: Select your confidence level in asepsis supervision. You can be very confident, somewhat confident, not very confident, or not at all confident.
* **Dental Asepsis Policy**: Select whether you have a dental asepsis policy in place.
* **Dental Asepsis Policy Frequency**: Select how often your dental asepsis policy is reviewed or updated. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Dental Asepsis Policy Confidence**: Select your confidence level in your current dental asepsis policy. You can be very confident, somewhat confident, not very confident, or not at all confident.
* **Supervision and Policy**: Select whether you always, sometimes, or rarely follow asepsis supervision and policies.
* **Supervision and Policy Frequency**: Select how often you follow asepsis supervision and policies. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Supervision and Policy Confidence**: Select your confidence level in asepsis supervision and policies. You can be very confident, somewhat confident, not very confident, or not at all confident.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **Time**: Enter the time you completed the survey.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you completed the survey.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person who gave you the survey.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact person's email.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the most appropriate answer based on your knowledge and practice.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the comments field.
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
