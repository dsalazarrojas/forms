# hospitalization_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospitalization claim form is designed to document and report hospitalization claims, collecting information on patient medical conditions, treatments, and procedures. It's used for administrative and billing purposes, as well as for tracking patient outcomes and improving hospital operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the claim in the `claim_date` field.
2. Enter the time of the claim in the `claim_time` field.
3. Enter any relevant medical procedure code(s) related to the patient's care in the `medical_procedure_code` field.
4. Provide a brief description of the medical procedure in the `medical_procedure_description` field.
5. Indicate the patient's medical condition in the `medical_condition` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hospitalization_claim** (1, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the hospitalization claim.
* **claim_date** (2, date, not required): Enter the date of the claim (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **claim_time** (3, time, not required): Enter the time of the claim (e.g., 08:00).
* **medical_procedure_code** (4, text, not required): Enter any relevant medical procedure code(s) related to the patient's care (e.g., CPT-123).
* **medical_procedure_description** (5, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the medical procedure (e.g., "Surgery to repair a broken bone").
* **medical_condition** (6, text, not required): Indicate the patient's medical condition (e.g., "Heart attack").
