# Medicare Consent Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect the necessary information and signatures for a patient to release their medical consent with a new healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your patient's information, making sure to include their correct name, the provider's name, the organization providing care, the date of the consent, and a brief reason for the transfer.
2. Sign the form, indicating that you have given consent to share your medical information with the new healthcare provider.
3. Date the form with the date you signed it.
4. Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the patient. Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
- **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the healthcare provider or organization providing care. Please enter the name exactly as it appears on their identification.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the healthcare organization providing care. Please enter the name exactly as it appears on their identification.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date when the consent is being signed. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the reason for the transfer of care. Please enter a brief explanation.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date when the consent is being signed. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Signature Provider** (`signature_provider`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the healthcare provider signing the consent. Please enter the name exactly as it appears on their identification.
