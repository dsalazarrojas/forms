# Patient Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Photo Release Form is a document used to obtain a patient's consent for the release of their photos taken in a medical setting. This form is essential for ensuring that patients' photos are shared appropriately and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and implications of granting consent.
2. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement with the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_photo_release_form** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief statement explaining why you are requesting to release the patient's photo. This field is optional, but it's a good opportunity to provide context or justification for the request.
* **photo_release_consent** (Select One, Required): Choose "True" if you are granting consent for the release of the patient's photo, and "False" if you are not.
* **records_storage** (Note, Optional): This field is intended for you to describe how you will store the patient's photos. This could be for research, marketing, or other legitimate purposes.
