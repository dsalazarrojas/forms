# Hospital Discharge Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Discharge Checklist Form is a tool for gathering essential information for patient discharges. It is intended to be completed by healthcare professionals during the discharge process to ensure a smooth and effective transition for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the patient's basic information on Page 1, including their name, next appointment date, and other relevant details.
2. On Page 2, list any medications the patient is currently taking or being discharged on.
3. Enter the discharge date on Page 3.
4. Schedule a follow-up appointment on Page 4, including the contact information of the doctor or medical facility to be used for follow-up care.
5. Provide information on follow-up care, including any specific instructions or precautions for the patient on Page 5.
6. Document any relevant discharge diagnoses or instructions on Page 6.
7. Record the medications administered during the patient's stay on Page 7.
8. Specify the discharge location, ensuring the patient knows where to go or what to do next on Page 8.
9. Provide an update on the patient's condition on Page 9.
10. Fill in the patient's contact information and emergency contact details on Page 10.
11. Specify the relationship between the patient and their emergency contact on Page 11.
12. Provide details on the discharge caregiver, if applicable on Page 12.
13. Add any discharge notes or comments on Page 13.
14. Finally, fill in the follow-up doctor and contact information for future care on Page 14 and 15.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`patient_information`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, ensuring accuracy and consistency with hospital records.
* **Medication List (Page 2)** (`medication_list`, text, required): List all medications the patient is currently taking or being discharged on.
* **Discharge Date (Page 3)** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Record the date of discharge.
* **Next Appointment (Page 3)** (`next_appointment`, date, required): Schedule a follow-up appointment date and time.
* **Follow Up Care (Page 4)** (`follow_up_care`, text, required): Describe any specific instructions or precautions for the patient following discharge.
* **Discharge Diagnoses (Page 5)** (`discharge_diagnoses`, text, required): Record any final diagnoses for the discharge.
* **Discharge Instructions (Page 5)** (`discharge_instructions`, text, required): Provide detailed instructions for the patient's care post-discharge.
* **Medication Administered (Page 6)** (`medication_administered`, text, required): Document any medications given to the patient during their stay.
* **Discharge Location (Page 7)** (`discharge_location`, text, required): Specify where the patient is going after discharge.
* **Patient Condition (Page 8)** (`patient_condition`, text, required): Update the patient's condition at the time of discharge.
* **Contact Information (Page 9)** (`contact_information`, text, required): Fill in the patient’s contact information.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 10)** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the patient’s emergency contact's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Relation (Page 11)** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, required): Describe the relationship between the patient and their emergency contact.
* **Discharge Caring (Page 12)** (`discharge_caring`, text, required): Provide information on who will be taking care of the patient post-discharge.
* **Discharge Notes (Page 13)** (`discharge_notes`, text, required): Add any discharge notes or comments.
* **Follow Up Doctor (Page 14)** (`follow_up_doctor`, text, required): Fill in the follow-up doctor's name and contact information.
* **Follow Up Phone (Page 14)** (`follow_up_phone`, text, required): Enter the follow-up doctor's phone number.
* **Follow Up Email (Page 15)** (`follow_up_email`, text, required): Enter the follow-up doctor’s email address.

Note: The required fields are marked as `required` in the form and should be filled out as such to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
