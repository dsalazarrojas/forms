# Hematology Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the treatment and discharge process for patients undergoing hematology procedures. It captures essential information about the patient's medical history, diagnosis, test results, and treatment outcome. This form helps healthcare professionals ensure that critical details are noted and communicated effectively during the discharge process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the patient's consent or have access to their medical information.
2. Review the patient's medical history to provide context for their condition.
3. Document the hematology diagnosis and test results according to your medical assessment.
4. Describe the treatment outcome and any notable events during the patient's stay.
5. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, using the provided fields to capture all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, **required**): Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, **optional**): Describe any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may impact the patient's care.
* **Hematology Diagnosis** (`hematology_diagnosis`, text, **required**): Record the hematology diagnosis made based on test results and medical evaluation.
* **Hematology Test Results** (`hematology_test_results`, text, **required**): Document the results of any relevant hematology tests performed during the patient's stay.
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, text, **required**): Describe the outcome of the treatment, including any follow-up recommendations or ongoing care instructions.

## Tips
* Always ensure accuracy and clarity when filling out the form to avoid errors and misunderstandings.
* Use standard medical terminology and abbreviations to maintain consistency and readability.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, refer to the patient's medical record or consult with the treating physician.
