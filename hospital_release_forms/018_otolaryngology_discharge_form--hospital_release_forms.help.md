# Otolaryngology Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Otolaryngology Discharge Form is a document that records the information necessary for the discharge of a patient from an otolaryngology consultation. This form aims to gather general information, medical history, allergies, medications, surgical details, follow-up instructions, and the discharge diagnosis. It is designed to ensure that the patient's care is properly documented and that the necessary information is communicated to the patient or their representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing general information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
2. Next, provide the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments that may be relevant to their care.
3. List any allergies the patient has, including medications or substances they are allergic to.
4. Mention any medications the patient is currently taking, including their dosage and frequency.
5. Provide surgical details, including the date and time of the surgery, and any other relevant information.
6. Offer follow-up instructions to the patient, including any necessary appointments or tests.
7. State the discharge diagnosis, including any relevant findings or conclusions.
8. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will provide basic information about the patient, such as their name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
	+ Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact the patient's care.
	+ Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise and easy to read.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will list any allergies the patient has, including medications or substances they are allergic to.
	+ Ensure that you include all known allergies, including any recent additions or changes.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will list any medications the patient is currently taking, including their dosage and frequency.
	+ Include both prescription and non-prescription medications.
* **Surgical Details** (`surgical_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will provide details about the surgery, including the date and time of the procedure.
	+ Include any relevant information about the surgery, such as the type of anesthesia used.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will provide instructions for the patient's follow-up care, including any necessary appointments or tests.
	+ Be clear and concise, but also include any relevant details.
* **Discharge Diagnosis** (`discharge_diagnosis`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you will state the discharge diagnosis, including any relevant findings or conclusions.
	+ Ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where the physician signs and dates the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
	+ Please sign and date the form legibly and accurately.
