# urgent_care_work_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The urgent_care_work_release_form is a document that allows patients to release their medical work from an urgent care setting. This form collects personal and medical information from the patient to ensure that they understand their rights and responsibilities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information, including your name and address.
2.  Enter the date of your birth.
3.  Provide your phone number.
4.  Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the content.
5.  Indicate if you have medical supervision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter your personal information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Patient signature is required to acknowledge that you have read and understood the content.
* **date_of_signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your medical record.
* **patient_address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your home address or the address where you will be receiving care.
* **patient_phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you with important information.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for medical record purposes.
* **medical_supervision** (`medical_supervision`, select_one, required/optional): Check the box if you require medical supervision. This will help us understand your needs and provide the best care possible.
* **medical_supervision_note** (`medical_supervision_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your medical supervision needs, if applicable.
