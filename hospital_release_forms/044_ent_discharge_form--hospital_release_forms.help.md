# ENT Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ENT Discharge Form is a document used to record the details of a patient's discharge from the hospital, ensuring that all necessary information is documented accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the ENT Discharge Form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with the relevant details as per the hospital's guidelines and regulations.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **ENT Discharge Details** (`ent_discharge_details`, text, Required: False): This field is used to enter the details of the patient's discharge from the hospital. Enter a clear and concise description of the reason for discharge.
*   **Instructions** (`instructions`, text, Required: False): This field is used to enter any specific instructions or advice given to the patient at the time of discharge.
*   **Patient Acknowledgment** (`patient_acknowledgment`, select_multiple, Required: False): This field is used to obtain the patient's acknowledgment of their discharge. Select from the provided options to indicate whether the patient understands and acknowledges the instructions given.
*   **ENT Doctor's Name** (`ent_doctors_name`, text, Required: False): Enter the name of the ENT doctor who attended to the patient.
*   **ENT Doctor's Name 2** (`ent_doctors_name_2`, text, Required: False): Enter the name of the second ENT doctor who attended to the patient (if applicable).
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, Required: False): Enter the name of the patient being discharged.
*   **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, Required: False): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, Required: False): Enter the date on which the patient was discharged from the hospital.
*   **Time of Discharge** (`time_of_discharge`, time, Required: False): Enter the time of discharge from the hospital.
*   **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, Required: False): Enter the name of the hospital where the patient was treated.
*   **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, Required: False): Enter the room number where the patient was staying during their hospitalization.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, Required: False): Enter the department where the patient was treated.
*   **Date of Admission** (`date_of_admission`, date, Required: False): Enter the date on which the patient was admitted to the hospital.
*   **Time of Admission** (`time_of_admission`, time, Required: False): Enter the time of admission to the hospital.
*   **Patient Dossier** (`patient_dossier`, text, Required: False): Enter any additional information or comments about the patient's dossier.

Note: The fields marked as `Required: False` can be filled out or left blank depending on the situation and hospital guidelines. The fields marked as `select_multiple` allow the user to select multiple options.
