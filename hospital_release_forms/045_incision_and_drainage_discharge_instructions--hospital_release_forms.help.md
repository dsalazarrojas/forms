# Incision And Drainage Discharge Instructions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Incision And Drainage Discharge Instructions" form is used to gather information about a patient's discharge from incision and drainage procedures. This information is essential for follow-up care and medical record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Provide the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact Info" field.
3.  Enter the date of the procedure in the "Procedure Date" field.
4.  Specify the surgeon's name in the "Surgeon's Name" field.
5.  Select the surgical unit where the procedure was performed from the "Surgical Unit" field.
6.  Indicate the patient's pain level using the "Pain Level" field.
7.  List any medication allergies in the "Medication Allergies" field.
8.  Provide the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
9.  Enter the date of discharge in the "Discharge Date" field.
10. Set the follow-up date in the "Follow-Up Date" field.
11. Specify the follow-up time in the "Follow-Up Time" field.
12. Enter the contact information for the follow-up visit in the "Follow-Up Contact" field.
13. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, optional): Provide the patient's contact details, such as phone number and address.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`, required): Enter the date of the procedure.
* **Surgeon's Name** (`surgeon_name`, required): Specify the name of the surgeon who performed the procedure.
* **Surgical Unit** (`surgical_unit`, optional): Select the surgical unit where the procedure was performed.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, optional): Indicate the patient's pain level using a numerical scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, optional): List any medication allergies the patient has.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, optional): Provide the patient's medical history.
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, required): Enter the date of discharge.
* **Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, optional): Set the follow-up date.
* **Follow-Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, optional): Specify the follow-up time.
* **Follow-Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, optional): Enter the contact information for the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-Up Doctor** (`follow_up_doctor`, optional): Select the doctor who will be following up with the patient.
* **Follow-Up Clinic** (`follow_up_clinic`, optional): Select the clinic where the follow-up visit will take place.
* **Follow-Up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, optional): Select the phone number for the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-Up E-mail** (`follow_up_email`, optional): Select the email for the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-Up Address** (`follow_up_address`, optional): Enter the address for the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, optional): Add any additional follow-up notes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Signature** (`signature`, optional): Add the surgeon's signature.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, required): Add the patient's signature.
