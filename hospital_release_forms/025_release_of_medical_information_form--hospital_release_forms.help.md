> # Release Of Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the release of medical records, allowing the owner to grant authorization to access their medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Owner Name** field with the name of the individual whose medical records are being released.
2. Enter the **Owner Email** field with the individual's email address.
3. In the **Purpose of Release** field, describe the reason for releasing the medical information.
4. For **Date of Birth**, enter the individual's date of birth in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Enter the name of the person authorized to access the medical information in the **Release To** field.
6. Choose the specific medical information to be released, selecting all or some of the options in the **Medical Records Release** field.
7. Select the additional health information to be released in the **Other Health Information Release** field, choosing from the available options.
8. In the **Authorized Person** field, enter the name of the individual authorized to access the medical information.
9. For **Authorized Date**, enter the date the release is authorized, in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
10. Sign the form with your own signature in the **Signature** field.
11. If applicable, have the **Authorized Person Signature** field signed by the individual authorized.
12. Finally, enter the date you signed the form in the **Date Signed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the individual whose medical records are being released.
* **Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the individual whose medical records are being released. This helps ensure that the release is sent to the correct person.
* **Purpose of Release**: Explain the reason for releasing the medical information. This may include the name of the healthcare provider, the specific treatment, or any other relevant context.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the individual's date of birth in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Release To**: Enter the name of the person authorized to access the medical information. This could be a healthcare provider, family member, or caregiver.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the medical information is being released, in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Medical Records Release**: Select all or some of the options to specify the type of medical information to be released.
* **Other Health Information Release**: Select all or some of the options to specify any additional health information to be released.
* **Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the individual authorized to access the medical information.
* **Authorized Date**: Enter the date the release is authorized, in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Signature**: Sign your name in this field to confirm your authorization.
* **Authorized Person Signature**: If applicable, sign with your signature to confirm your authorization.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
