The purpose of this form is to collect important information from female patients when they are being discharged from the hospital. This form is essential for ensuring a smooth and safe transition for the patients, and providing them with the necessary guidance and resources for their post-discharge care. The form should only be used by authorized hospital staff, and all information collected is subject to confidentiality and HIPAA compliance.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
2. Provide the patient's address in the "patient address" field.
3. Select the discharge details from the available options in the "Discharge Details" dropdown menu.

The following fields are required to be completed for the form to be submitted successfully:

- **patient name**: This field is used to capture the name of the patient being discharged.
  - Please ensure that you enter the patient's name accurately and in the same format as it appears on their identification documents.
  - This field is essential for identifying the patient and ensuring that they receive the correct care and follow-up instructions.

- **patient address**: This field is used to capture the patient's home or hospital address.
  - Please provide the patient's address in the format "Street address, city, state, zip code".
  - This field is necessary for sending important documents and follow-up information to the patient.

- **Discharge Details**: This dropdown menu provides options for the type of discharge.
  - Please select the most accurate option from the list:
    - 1. Routine Discharge
    - 2. Urgent Discharge (e.g., emergency or unscheduled discharge)
    - 3. Against Medical Advice (AMA) - This is for patients who are discharged against medical advice, meaning they are leaving the hospital without following the recommended treatment plan.
