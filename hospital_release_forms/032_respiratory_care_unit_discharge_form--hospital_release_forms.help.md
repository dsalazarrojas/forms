## Respiratory Care Unit Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Respiratory Care Unit Discharge Form is a document used to record the details of a patient's stay in the Respiratory Care Unit and to ensure a smooth and safe transition out of the unit. It captures the necessary information about the patient's treatment, medical status, and discharge instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's personal information, including their name, admission date, and patient phone number.
2. Select the reason for discharge and enter the date of discharge.
3. Enter the patient's medical condition and any medications they are taking.
4. Fill out the vital signs page with the patient's current vital signs.
5. Select the discharge status and enter any additional comments.
6. Enter the name of the hospital and the signature of the physician and nurse.
7. Finally, review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
