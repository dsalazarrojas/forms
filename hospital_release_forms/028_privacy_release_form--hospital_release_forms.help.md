# Privacy Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Privacy Release Form is used to obtain permission from employees to share their medical information with third parties. This form ensures that employees understand and agree to the terms of the privacy release, which is an essential part of maintaining confidentiality and protecting sensitive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete the Employee Details section**: This is the first section of the form, where you will need to provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. **Select the Release Type**: Choose the type of release you are giving by selecting from the options (HIPAA, FCR, BIPA, or Other).
3. **Specify the Release Date**: Enter the date from which you want the release to be effective.
4. **Determine who to Release To**: Decide who will be receiving your medical information (choose 'Yes' or 'No').
5. **Sign the form**: Print your name and date the form to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms of this privacy release.
6. **Date the form**: Enter the date you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details (employee_details)**: This is the first section of the form where you need to provide your name and other relevant details about yourself.
* **Release Type (release_type)**: This field is used to select the type of release you are giving. The options are:
	+ **HIPAA**: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a federal law that sets national standards for protecting patient health information.
	+ **FCR**: Family and Medical Leave Act (FCR) is a federal law that provides eligible employees with up to 12 weeks of unpaid leave for certain family and medical reasons.
	+ **BIPA**: Illinois Insurance Department's Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) is a state law that requires employers to obtain informed consent from employees before collecting and disclosing personal data.
	+ **Other**: For any other type of release not listed above.
* **Release Date (release_date)**: This field is used to specify the date from which you want the release to be effective.
* **Release To (release_to)**: This field is used to decide who will be receiving your medical information. Choose 'Yes' if you are willing to release your information, and 'No' if you are not.
* **Employee Signature (employee_signature)**: This is a text field where you can print your name to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms of this privacy release.
* **Date Signed (date_signed)**: This is a date field where you can enter the date you signed the form.

Note: This form is not intended to be used for any other purpose, and completing this form once does not preclude the need for employees to complete it again in the future.
