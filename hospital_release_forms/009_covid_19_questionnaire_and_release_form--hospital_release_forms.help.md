# Covid 19 Questionnaire And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their COVID-19 experience, medical treatment, and release status. It is intended for medical staff to use in hospitals and healthcare settings to document patient data and track patient releases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in a comfortable and private environment to fill out the form.
2. Read each question carefully and answer it accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Enter all required information (if applicable).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main
• **Main** (id: 1): The main page is a general overview of the form.
### Patient Info
• **Patient Info** (id: 2): Enter the patient's personal details, such as their name and contact information.
### Medical Info
• **Medical Info** (id: 3): Provide information about the patient's medical condition, treatment, and medications.
### Vaccine Info
• **Vaccine Info** (id: 4): Enter details about the patient's vaccination status, if applicable.
### Release Status
• **Release Status** (id: 5): Report the patient's current release status, which can be "Home", "Hospitalized", "Released", or "Deceased".
### Date
• **Date** (id: 7): Enter the date of the patient's release or treatment, if applicable.
### Time
• **Time** (id: 8): Record the time of the patient's release or treatment, if applicable.
### Patient Signature
• **Patient Signature** (id: 6): Sign and date the form to confirm your identity as the patient.
### Patient Signature
• **Medical Staff Name** (id: 10): Sign and date the form to confirm your identity as the medical staff member.
### Medical Staff Signature
• **Medical Staff Signature** (id: 9): Sign and date the form to confirm your identity as the medical staff member, if applicable.
### Date
• **Date** (id: 7): Enter the date this form was completed.
### Time
• **Time** (id: 8): Record the time this form was completed.
### Medical Staff Name
• **Medical Staff Name** (id: 10): Enter your name and title/position as the medical staff member.

**Tips**:
- Always fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask your medical staff for guidance.
- Keep the form confidential and secure.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
