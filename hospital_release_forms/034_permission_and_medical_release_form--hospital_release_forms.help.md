# Permission And Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and obtain medical release permissions from patients or their guardians. It's essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that medical professionals can provide the best possible care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Details**: Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information in this section.
2. **Guardian Details**: Provide the guardian's name, relationship to the patient, and contact information if applicable.
3. **Medical Info**: Enter any relevant medical history, conditions, or allergies that may affect the patient's care.
4. **Permission Details**: Specify the permission type (e.g., for medical treatment, surgery, or medication) and any conditions or restrictions.
5. **Medical Release**: Choose a release date and time if necessary.
6. **Hospital Info**: Enter any relevant hospital information (e.g., room number, bed number).
7. **Emergency Info**: Provide emergency contact information for the patient.
8. **Hospital Info**: Note any other hospital-specific information that may be relevant for the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (Patient Details, text, required):
	+ This section asks for basic patient information, including name, date of birth, and contact details. Please ensure accuracy to ensure proper patient identification.
* **Guardian Details** (Guardian Details, text, optional):
	+ If the patient is a minor or unable to sign the form, provide the guardian's information. This section is optional but recommended for ensuring the patient's safety.
* **Medical Info** (Medical Info, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant medical history, conditions, or allergies that may impact the patient's care.
* **Permission Details** (Permission Details, text, optional):
	+ Specify the type of permission required (e.g., surgery, medication, or treatment). Add any conditions or restrictions related to this permission.
* **Medical Release** (Medical Release, time, optional):
	+ Choose a release date and time, if necessary, for medical treatment or medication.
* **Hospital Info** (Hospital Info, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant hospital information (e.g., room number, bed number) to ensure proper hospital coordination.
* **Emergency Info** (Emergency Info, text, optional):
	+ Provide contact information for emergency situations, such as contact names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
* **Emergency Info** (Hospital Info, text, optional):
	+ Note any other hospital-specific information that may be relevant for the patient's care, such as the patient's room number or hospital department.
