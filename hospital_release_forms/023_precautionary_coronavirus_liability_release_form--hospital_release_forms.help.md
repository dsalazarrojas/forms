# Precautionary Coronavirus Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release a patient's liability for medical treatment related to COVID-19, including acknowledging the risks associated with the virus, listing any medical conditions or symptoms, and providing information about insurance coverage and medical care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read and agree to the customer agreement section.
2.  Acknowledge your understanding of the risks associated with COVID-19.
3.  Indicate whether you have any medical conditions or not.
4.  List any symptoms you are experiencing.
5.  Confirm your insurance status.
6.  Provide information about your emergency contact.
7.  Confirm whether you have any medical attention or assistance available.
8.  Enter the hospital's contact information.
