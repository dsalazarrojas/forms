# COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from participants in activities or services that carry a risk of COVID-19 transmission. It ensures that participants understand and acknowledge the risks associated with these activities and services and waive any potential liability for the organization providing the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name, date of birth, address, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
2. Enter your email address and phone number.
3. Briefly describe the activity or service you will be participating in.
4. Select the type of activity or service you will be participating in.
5. Enter the date of the activity or service.
6. For the **Health Status and Risk Acknowledgment** section:
    * Select "True" to acknowledge the risk of COVID-19 transmission.
    * Select "True" if you do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
    * Select "True" if you have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
    * Select "True" if you have not had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
7. For the **Liability Waiver Agreement** section:
    * Select "I agree" to assume all risks related to COVID-19 exposure.
    * Select "I agree" to release and hold harmless the organization from any COVID-19 related claims.
8. Enter the date you are signing this waiver.
9. Select "Yes, I acknowledge" to accept this waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`state`): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Activity Description** (`activity_description`): Briefly describe the activity or service you will be participating in.
* **Activity or Service** (`activity_type`): Select the type of activity or service you will be participating in.
* **Date of Activity** (`activity_date`): Enter the date of the activity or service.
* **I acknowledge the risk of COVID-19 transmission** (`acknowledge_covid_risk`): Select "True" to acknowledge the risk.
* **I certify that I do not have COVID-19 symptoms** (`no_covid_symptoms`): Select "True" if you do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **I have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days** (`not_diagnosed`): Select "True" if you have not been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **I have not had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days** (`no_close_contact`): Select "True" if you have not had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
* **I voluntarily assume all risks related to COVID-19 exposure** (`assume_risk`): Select "I agree" to assume all risks.
* **I release and hold harmless the organization from any COVID-19 related claims** (`release_liability`): Select "I agree" to release liability.
* **Date of Waiver** (`waiver_date`): Enter the date you are signing this waiver.
* **I acknowledge and accept this waiver** (`acknowledgment_signature`): Select "Yes, I acknowledge" to accept this waiver.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review each field before submitting the form.
- Select "True" or "False" accordingly for the **Health Status and Risk Acknowledgment** section.
- Select "I agree" or "I disagree" accordingly for the **Liability Waiver Agreement** section.
- Enter the date accurately for the **Date of Waiver** field.
