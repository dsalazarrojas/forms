# Hospital Discharge Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the release of a hospital patient, ensuring that the patient's medical records are properly updated and communicated after discharge.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Select the hospital ID from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the authorized person's name in the "Authorized Person" field.
5. Enter the authorized person's phone number in the "Authorized Phone" field.
6. Enter the authorized person's email address in the "Authorized Email" field.
7. Enter the date of release in the "Release Date" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
8. Enter the type of authorization in the "Authorization Type" field.
9. Enter the reason for release in the "Reason for Release" field.
10. Enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" field.
11. Sign the "Patient Signature" field with the patient's signature.
12. Sign the "Authorized Signature" field with the authorized person's signature.
13. Enter the date and time of signing in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields, respectively.
14. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
15. Enter the authorized person's address in the "Authorized Address" field.
16. Enter the patient's city in the "Patient City" field.
17. Enter the authorized person's city in the "Authorized City" field.
18. Enter the patient's state in the "Patient State" field.
19. Enter the authorized person's state in the "Authorized State" field.
20. Enter the patient's post office code (zip code) in the "Patient Postal Code" field.
21. Enter the authorized person's post office code (zip code) in the "Authorized Postal Code" field.
22. Enter the patient's country in the "Patient Country" field.
23. Enter the authorized person's country in the "Authorized Country" field.
24. Enter the agreement between the patient and the hospital in the "Patient Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Hospital ID**: Select the hospital ID from the dropdown list.
* **Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the person authorized to act on the patient's behalf.
* **Authorized Phone**: Enter the phone number of the authorized person.
* **Authorized Email**: Enter the email address of the authorized person.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date of release in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Authorization Type**: Enter the type of authorization for the release.
* **Reason for Release**: Enter the reason for the patient's release from the hospital.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information related to the patient's release.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign this field with the patient's signature.
* **Authorized Signature**: Sign this field with the authorized person's signature.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Authorized Address**: Enter the authorized person's address.
* **Patient City**: Enter the patient's city.
* **Authorized City**: Enter the authorized person's city.
* **Patient State**: Enter the patient's state.
* **Authorized State**: Enter the authorized person's state.
* **Patient Postal Code**: Enter the patient's post office code (zip code).
* **Authorized Postal Code**: Enter the authorized person's post office code (zip code).
* **Patient Country**: Enter the patient's country.
* **Authorized Country**: Enter the authorized person's country.
* **Patient Agreement**: Enter the agreement between the patient and the hospital.
* **Authorized Agreement**: Enter the agreement between the authorized person and the hospital.
