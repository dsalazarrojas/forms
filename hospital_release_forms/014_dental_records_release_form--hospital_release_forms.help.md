# Dental Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Dental Records Release Form" is used to collect and manage information related to a patient's dental records, ensuring accuracy and up-to-date data for proper care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Review each field carefully, and ensure you have filled in correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name as it appears on their official identification documents (e.g., driver's license).

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name as it appears on their official identification documents (e.g., driver's license), and **Note**: This is a required field as it's a critical piece of identifying information.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).

### Address
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address in the standard address format, including street address, city, and zip code.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's contact phone number, including country code if applicable.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Enter any known medical history or allergies relevant to the patient's care.

### Dentist Name
* **Dentist Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the patient's treating dentist.

### Doctor Name
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the patient's doctor, if different from the dentist.

### Release Date
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the dental records are being released to, or the date the records will be kept private.

### Signature
* **Signature**: Provide a signature to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or their representative.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask the relevant personnel.
* Keep a record of this form, as it may be useful for future reference.
