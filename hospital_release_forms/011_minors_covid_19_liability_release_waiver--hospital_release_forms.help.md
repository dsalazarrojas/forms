# minors_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a liability release and waiver from minors for COVID-19 related activities or services provided by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the minor's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in the minor's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide the minor's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the minor's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide the name of the minor's parent or guardian in the "Parent's Name" field.
6. Specify the relationship between the parent or guardian and the minor in the "Relationship" field.
7. Enter the minor's age in the "Age" field.
8. Check the box to indicate if the parent or guardian has given their consent in the "Consent" field.
9. Select a date in the "Date Signed" field to sign the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the minor's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the minor's last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the minor's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the minor's email address.
* **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose how the parent or guardian is related to the minor (Mother, Father, Guardian, Other).
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the minor's age.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box to indicate if the parent or guardian has given their consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Select the date the waiver was signed.
