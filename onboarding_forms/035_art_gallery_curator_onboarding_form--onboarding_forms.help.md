# Art Gallery Curator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Curator Onboarding Form is designed to help new art gallery curators provide essential information for onboarding purposes. It helps the HR department and other stakeholders understand the curator's needs, background, and availability for onboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly with your information.
2. Ensure that you've reviewed the required fields carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New hire name** (`new_hire_name`, text, required): Your full name as the new curator.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): The official start date of your employment.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose the department that best aligns with your role and responsibilities: Curatorial, Education, Outreach, or Exhibitions.
* **Mentor assigned** (`mentor_assigned`, text, optional): The name of your primary onboarding mentor. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Required documents** (`required_documents`, text, required): List any documents you need to submit as part of the onboarding process.
* **Access credentials needed** (`access_credentials`, text, optional): If you need access to specific systems or keys, list them here.
* **Training schedule** (`training_schedule`, text, optional): Provide any planned onboarding sessions or training schedule for your reference.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): The name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Equipment issued** (`equipment_issued`, text, optional): If you've been issued any equipment such as a laptop or phone, list it here. This is for HR use only.
* **HR notes** (`hr_notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes for HR use only. This can be used to store relevant information about your onboarding process.
