# Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Onboarding Questionnaire is a form used to collect essential information about new suppliers to set up their account in our system and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all the required information.
3. For "Business Type" and "Phone" fields, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" for more details on what type of information is expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Supplier Details
* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the supplier's details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the supplier's contact information.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the supplier's shipping address.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any payment terms or conditions related to this supplier.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to track the supplier's onboarding status.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `select_one`, required/true): This field is used to select the type of phone number (Landline or Mobile) that the supplier can be contacted on.

### Business Information
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the supplier's tax ID number.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required/true): This field is used to select the type of business (Company, Individual, Partnership, or Other).

### Additional Information
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the supplier's address.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to enter any payment terms or conditions related to this supplier.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or need help with the onboarding process, feel free to contact us.
* This form is intended for one-time use. Please do not submit this form multiple times for the same supplier.
