# Advertising Designer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertising Designer Onboarding Form is used to collect essential information from new Advertising Designers during their onboarding process. This includes personal details, design proficiency, and preferences for their work setup and onboarding tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details (full name, employee ID, start date, contact information)
2. Select your design proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite
3. Specify your design specializations (choose from the list)
4. List any other design software you're proficient in
5. Identify your hardware and software requirements for your design work
6. Specify your initial project expectations

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, text, required): Enter your assigned employee identification number.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Enter your first day of work.
* **Department Team** (`Department Team`, text, required): Enter the name of the department/team you will be working in.
* **Personal Email** (`Personal Email`, email, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Adobe Creative Suite Proficiency** (`Adobe Creative Suite Proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite as Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Other Design Software** (`Other Design Software`, text, required): List any other design software you're proficient in.
* **Design Specializations** (`Design Specializations`, select_multiple, required): Choose from the list: Brand Identity, Print Design, Digital Design, Web Design, Motion Graphics, UI/UX, Typography, Illustration, Photography.
* **Hardware Requirements** (`Hardware Requirements`, text, required): Specify the hardware you need for your design work.
* **Software Licensing Needs** (`Software Licensing Needs`, text, required): Specify the software licenses you need.
* **Workspace Preferences** (`Workspace Preferences`, text, required): Specify your workspace preferences.
* **Initial Project Expectations** (`Initial Project Expectations`, text, required): Describe the type of projects you expect to work on initially.
