# ISA Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ISA Onboarding Form is a mandatory form for new employees to provide their details for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details, including your name, job title, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your manager's name and phone number for easy communication.
3. Enter your department and company name for directory purposes.
4. If you have a manager with a different job title, please provide their name and phone number as well.
5. Submit the form to complete the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`onboarding_form`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager's Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Job Title of Manager** (`job_title_manager`, text, required): If your manager has a different job title, enter their job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.

## Tips

* Double-check your details before submitting the form for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult your HR representative for guidance.
* This form will only take a few minutes to complete, please be as concise as possible.
