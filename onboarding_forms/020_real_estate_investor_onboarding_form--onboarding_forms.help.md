# Real Estate Investor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to onboard real estate investors into your organization. It collects information about the company and individual investor, including their contact details, job title, and company address. By completing this form, you will be able to provide the necessary information to facilitate a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Ensure that the email address and company email address are formatted correctly (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
3.  Enter your company address in the format of "Street Address", "City, State ZIP Code".
4.  Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your company.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, not required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, not required): Enter your company's physical address in the format "Street Address", "City, State ZIP Code". 

## Tips

- Double-check for spelling and formatting errors before submitting.
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any issues or require assistance, please reach out to our support team.

Note: The fields marked as "not required" are optional but recommended for a complete onboarding profile.
