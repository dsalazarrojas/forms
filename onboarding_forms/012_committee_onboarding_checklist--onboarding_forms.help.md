# Committee Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Committee Onboarding Checklist is a comprehensive form designed to guide new committee members through the onboarding process. It ensures that all necessary information is collected, and the member is fully aware of their role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before proceeding.
3. Fill out all required fields, indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
4. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Information** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This section is a general overview of the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Date** (`2`, `date`, required: true): The date when the onboarding process began.
* **Committee Name** (`3`, `text`, required: true): The name of the committee the member is joining.
* **New Member Information** (`4`, `note`, required: false): Any additional information about the new member.
* **Member Name** (`5`, `text`, required: true): The full name of the new member.
* **Member Email** (`6`, `email`, required: true): The email address of the new member.
* **Member Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: false): The phone number of the new member.
* **Member Title** (`8`, `text`, required: true): The member's job title or position.
* **Member Organization** (`9`, `text`, required: false): The organization the member represents.
* **Role Assignment** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Any additional details about the member's role.
* **Committee Role** (`11`, `select_one`, required: true): The assigned role on the committee (Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Member, Advisor, Ex-Officio).
* **Term Start Date** (`12`, `date`, required: true): The start date of the member's term.
* **Term End Date** (`13`, `date`, required: true): The end date of the member's term.
* **Onboarding Tasks** (`14`, `note`, required: false): Any required onboarding activities.
* **Orientation Completed** (`15`, `select_one`, required: true): Has the member completed orientation? (Yes, No, Scheduled).
* **Documents Reviewed** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Which documents have been reviewed? (Committee Charter, Bylaws, Meeting Schedule, Code of Conduct, Conflict of Interest Policy, Strategic Plan, None Yet).
* **System Access Granted** (`17`, `select_one`, required: true): Has access to necessary systems been granted? (Yes - All Access, Partial Access, Pending, Not Required).
* **Mentor Assigned** (`18`, `select_one`, required: true): Has a mentor been assigned? (Yes, No, Not Required).
* **Mentor Name** (`19`, `text`, required: false): The name of the assigned mentor.
* **First Meeting Date** (`20`, `date`, required: false): The date of the first committee meeting.
* **Training Required** (`21`, `select_one`, required: true): Is additional training needed? (Yes, No).
* **Training Topics** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required: false): What training topics are needed? (Parliamentary Procedure, Committee Governance, Financial Oversight, Strategic Planning, Public Speaking, None).
* **Onboarding Coordinator** (`23`, `text`, required: true): The name of the person coordinating onboarding.
* **Onboarding Status** (`24`, `select_one`, required: true): The current onboarding progress (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, On Hold).
* **Additional Notes** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Any additional comments or requirements.
