# onboarding_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is part of the onboarding process, designed to gather feedback from new team members about their experience. It helps us understand your satisfaction, engagement, and any areas for improvement in our onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as `true`) and answer the questions that are relevant to your experience.
2. For multiple-choice questions (like "questions" at id: 20), select all the options that apply.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in text fields, so we can get a clear understanding of your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **First Name**: Your name, as used on our system for communication and reporting.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your work email address.
* **Company**: The name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Your job title.
* **Department**: The department you belong to.
* **Onboarding Experience**: A number rating (1-10) of your overall onboarding experience with us.
* **Satisfaction with Onboarding Process**: A description of your level of satisfaction with the onboarding process.
* **Satisfaction with Manager**: A description of your satisfaction with your manager.
* **Manager Satisfaction**: A description of your manager's performance.
* **Communication Frequency**: A number rating (1-10) of how often you receive communication from your manager.
* **Communication Quality**: A number rating (1-10) of the quality of communication from your manager.
* **Support Provided**: A description of the support you've received from your manager and team.
* **Feedback**: Any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share about your onboarding experience.
* **Overall Experience**: A number rating (1-10) of your overall experience with us.
* **Overall Rating**: A number rating (1-10) of your overall satisfaction with us.
* **Recommendation**: Whether or not you would recommend us to others.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or observations.
* **Questions**: Select all questions that you've had during the onboarding process.
* **Suggestions**: Any suggestions you have for improving our onboarding process.
* **Feedback for Manager**: Comments or suggestions for your manager's feedback.
* **Feedback for HR**: Comments or suggestions for HR department's feedback.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional thoughts or comments you have for us.
* **Onboarding Completion**: A number rating (1-10) of the completeness of our onboarding process.

Note: This form is designed to gather feedback and improve our onboarding process. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort to provide honest feedback.
