# New Homeowner Onboarding Including Homeowner Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new homeowners for [insert purpose of collection, e.g., account setup, survey, etc.].

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details section with your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and home country code.
3. Provide your home postal code, province, and state.
4. Enter your home city, street, and street number.
5. Enter your home floor and unit number.
6. Provide your home zip code.
7. Indicate if you are the primary residence or not.
8. Select the type of homeowner (e.g., owner, tenant, etc.).
9. If you are not the primary residence, provide additional information in the "primary residence other" field.
10. Select the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
11. Optionally, select the type of property size.
12. Review the homeowner checklist and select the applicable options.
13. Optionally, provide additional information in the "home owner status" field.
