# Managed Mobility Program Employee Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Managed Mobility Program Employee Onboarding Checklist is a form used to onboard employees to the managed mobility program. This program is designed to provide employees with the flexibility to work from anywhere, and this form ensures that all necessary details are collected for program management.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through this guide carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill out each field with the requested information.
3.  Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Onboarding Details** (`title: Managed Mobility Program Employee Onboarding Checklist`, `id: 1`): This is the title of the form and the first field to fill out.
*   **Employee Information** (`id: 2`): In this field, provide your name and any other identifying information requested.
*   **Mobility Program Access** (`id: 3`): Choose the appropriate option to indicate your mobility program access. For example, if you have full access, choose "Option 1."
*   **Device Handoff** (`id: 4`): Select the devices you need access to or have access to, such as computers, phones, or other equipment.
*   **Notes** (`id: 5`): If you have any additional comments or notes regarding your mobility program access, enter them in this field.
*   **Submit** (`id: 6`): Once you have filled out all the fields, click "Submit" to finalize your onboarding.
*   **Email** (`id: 7`): Enter your email address in this field.
*   **Phone** (`id: 8`): Enter your phone number in this field.
*   **Date** (`id: 9`): Enter the date you plan on starting the mobility program.
*   **Time** (`id: 10`): Enter the time you plan on starting the mobility program.
*   **Category** (`id: 11`): Choose the category for your mobility program, such as "Full Access" or "Limited Access."
*   **Subcategory** (`id: 12`): Select the subcategory for your mobility program, such as "Remote Work" or "Business Travel."
*   **Comments** (`id: 13`): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in this field.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`id: 14`): Choose the tool assigned to you for the mobility program.
*   **Submitted By** (`id: 15`): Choose who submitted the form, such as yourself or another team member.

Tips:
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, reach out to your administrator or program manager for guidance.
