# Sales Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Staff Onboarding Form is used to collect information from sales staff members to facilitate the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is optional.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This field is optional.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This field is a valid email address and is optional.
4. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field. This field is optional.
5. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field. This field is optional.
6. Enter your **Department** in the "Department" field. This field is optional.
7. Enter your **Manager's Name** in the "Manager Name" field. This field is optional.
8. Enter your manager's **Email** in the "Manager Email" field. This field is optional.
9. Enter your manager's **Phone** in the "Manager Phone" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address that you use or are associated with.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your current job title within the company.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
