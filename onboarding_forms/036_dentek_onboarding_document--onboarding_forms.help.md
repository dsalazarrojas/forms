# Dentek Onboarding Document - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dentek Onboarding Document is a form used to gather essential information from new users during the onboarding process. Its purpose is to collect contact details, such as name, phone number, and email address, to facilitate a smooth and efficient onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select a Date (if required) in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your first name here. This will be used to address you personally.
* **Last Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your last name here. This will be used to personalize your account settings.
* **Phone (text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address here. This will be used for important notifications and updates.
* **Date (date, required: false)**: Enter a date if required for your onboarding process.
