# Work Trial Agreement Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Trial Agreement Confirmation form is used to confirm the details and agreements of a work trial, a temporary work arrangement for a participant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
2. Enter the participant's details, including their name, address, email, and phone number.
3. Fill in the work trial dates, start and end times, duration, and status.
4. Select the participant's agreement status.
5. Sign and date the participant's signature section.
6. Sign and date the HR and Recruiter signature sections.
7. Confirm the employment program signature section.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Work Trial Dates** (`work_trial_dates`, `text`, required: false): Enter the start and end dates of the work trial.
* **Consent To Terms** (`consent_to_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to the participant's agreement status.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `number`, required: false): Sign and date the participant's signature section.
* **HR Signature** (`hr_signature`, `number`, required: false): Sign and date the HR signature section.
* **Recruiter Signature** (`recruiter_signature`, `number`, required: false): Sign and date the Recruiter signature section.
* **Employment Program Signature** (`employment_program_signature`, `number`, required: false): Sign and date the Employment Program signature section.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes for the work trial.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Work Trial Start** (`worktrial_start`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the work trial.
* **Work Trial End** (`worktrial_end`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the work trial.
* **Work Trial Duration** (`worktrial_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the work trial.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the participant's agreement status.
* **Work Trial Start Time** (`worktrial_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the work trial.
* **Work Trial End Time** (`worktrial_end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the work trial.
* **Work Trial Location** (`worktrial_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the work trial.
* **Work Trial Status** (`work_trial_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the work trial.
* **Work Trial Notes** (`work_trial_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes for the work trial.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
* **Work Trial Dates** (`work_trial_dates`, `date`, required: false): Enter the dates of the work trial.
* **Work Trial Hours** (`work_trial_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the hours of the work trial.
* **Participant Agreement Signed** (`participant_agreement_signed`, `number`, required: false): Sign and date the participant's agreement signed section.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the participant's signature.
* **Participant Agreement Status** (`participant_agreement_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the participant's agreement status.
* **Tips**: Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any issues with the work trial.
