# pediatric_practice_onboarding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect new patient information and emergency contact details for a pediatric practice. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the practice has the necessary information to provide proper care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the New Patient Details section with the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Provide the new parent's details in the New Parent Details section.
3. Enter the emergency contact's name and contact number in the Emergency Contact section.
4. Select the contact preferences and medical contact options that best fit the patient's needs.
5. Enter any additional contact information in the Medical Contact section.
6. Provide consent to share the patient's information, if applicable.
7. Fill out the Contact Preferences and Medical Contact sections with the relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient Details** (`new_patient_details`, text, required false): Enter the patient's name.
* **New Parent Details** (`new_parent_details`, text, required false): Enter the parent's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required false): Enter the emergency contact's name and contact number.
* **Consent Details** (`consent_details`, text, required false): Enter any additional information related to consent.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required false): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required false): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, required false): Select the contact preferences that best fit the patient's needs, such as email, phone, or SMS.
* **Medical Contact** (`medical_contact`, select_one, required false): Select the medical contact option that best fit the patient's needs.
* **Medical Contact Type** (`medical_contact_type`, select_one, required false): Select the type of medical contact.
* **Medical Contact Number** (`medical_contact_number`, text, required false): Enter the medical contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required false): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, note, required false): Enter any additional information related to the patient.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, select_one, required false): Select the medical group that the patient belongs to.
* **Medical Group Type** (`medical_group_type`, select_one, required false): Select the type of medical group.
* **Medical Contact Email** (`medical_contact_email`, email, required false): Enter the medical contact's email address.
* **Medical Contact Address** (`medical_contact_address`, text, required false): Enter the medical contact's address.
* **Contact Preferences Other** (`contact_preferences_other`, text, required false): Enter any additional contact preferences.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required false): Select the reason for sharing the patient's information.
* **Consent to Share Reason** (`consent_to_share_reason`, text, required false): Enter the reason for sharing the patient's information.
* **Consent to Share Type** (`consent_to_share_type`, select_one, required false): Select the type of consent to share.
* **Consent to Share Reason Other** (`consent_to_share_reason_other`, text, required false): Enter any other reason for sharing the patient's information.
* **Consent to Share Date** (`consent_to_share_date`, date, required false): Enter the date for sharing the patient's information.
* **Contact Preferences Date** (`contact_preferences_date`, date, required false): Enter the contact preferences date.
* **Contact Preferences Time** (`contact_preferences_time`, time, required false): Enter the contact preferences time.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any errors or missed opportunities.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from the practice staff.
