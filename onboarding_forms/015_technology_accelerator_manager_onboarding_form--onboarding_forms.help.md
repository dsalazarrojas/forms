# Technology Accelerator Manager Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a necessary step for new Technology Accelerator Managers to complete in order to ensure a smooth onboarding process and to provide necessary information about their background, experience, and work history within the TAM role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information about yourself before starting the onboarding process.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by asterisk*) and provide accurate and truthful information about yourself.
3. Ensure that you have permission from your supervisor or manager to start the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName** (`title`, `text`, *: false): Please enter your first name.
* **LastName** (`title`, `text`, *: false): Please enter your last name.
* **email** (`label`, `email`, *: false): Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`label`, `text`, *: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **first time user** (`label`, `select_one`, *: false): Please select whether this is your first time working with the Technology Accelerator Manager role:
	+ New: You have never worked with the Technology Accelerator Manager role before.
	+ Experienced: You have worked with the Technology Accelerator Manager role before.
* **previous experience** (`label`, `select_multiple`, *: false): Please select your level of experience with the Technology Accelerator Manager role:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3 months to 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ More than 1 year
* **employed by tam** (`label`, `select_one`, *: false): Please select whether you are currently employed by the TAM team:
	+ True: You are currently employed by the TAM team.
	+ False: You are not currently employed by the TAM team.
* **tam employee id** (`label`, `text`, *: false): Please enter your TAM employee ID.
* **tam role** (`label`, `text`, *: false): Please enter your role within the TAM team.
* **tam start date** (`label`, `date`, *: false): Please enter the date you started working with the TAM team.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact your supervisor or manager for clarification.
- Please provide accurate and truthful information about yourself.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the TAM team.
