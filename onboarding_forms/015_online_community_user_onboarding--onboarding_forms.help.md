# Online Community User Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to onboard new users by collecting essential information and ensuring they understand and agree to the community's guidelines and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you're familiar with the community's guidelines and policies.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Confirm your acceptance of the community's policies by selecting the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Member Details
* **New Member Details** (`new_member_details`, text, optional): This field is an open text area for you to provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your onboarding process.

### Confirm Community Guidelines
* **Confirm Community Guidelines** (`confirm_agreement`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Terms of Use**: Check this box if you agree to abide by the community's terms of use.
	+ **Community Guidelines**: Check this box if you agree to abide by the community's guidelines.
	+ **Membership Agreement**: Check this box if you agree to abide by the community's membership agreement.

### Onboarding Data
* **Onboarding Data** (`onboarding_data`, text, optional): This field is for additional information about your onboarding process.

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for us to get to know you better.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for us to get to know you better.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with important community updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for our records.

### Confirm Privacy Policy
* **Confirm Privacy Policy** (`confirm_privacy_policy`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Check this box if you agree to abide by our privacy policy.
	+ **No**: Check this box if you do not agree.

### Confirm Membership Agreement
* **Confirm Membership Agreement** (`confirm_membership_agreement`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Agreed**: Check this box if you agree to our membership agreement.
	+ **Not Agreed**: Check this box if you do not agree.

### Onboarding Status
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, text, optional): This field is for our records to track your onboarding progress.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you're comfortable with the information you're providing.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the onboarding process, feel free to reach out to us.
