# Research Postdoctoral Fellow Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about you and your research group for onboarding as a research postdoctoral fellow.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
3. Enter your last name in the last_name field.
4. Enter your email address in the email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the phone_number field.
6. Enter your university in the university field.
7. Enter your department in the department field.
8. Enter your supervisor's name in the supervisor_name field.
9. Enter your supervisor's title in the supervisor_title field.
10. Enter your supervisor's email address in the supervisor_email field.
11. Enter your supervisor's phone number in the supervisor_phone_number field.
12. Enter your research area in the research_area field.
13. Enter the start date of your project in the project_start_date field.
14. Enter your research group's name in the research_group_name field.
15. Enter your research group's contact person's name in the research_group_contact field.
16. Enter your research group's email address in the research_group_email field.
17. Enter your research group's phone number in the research_group_phone_number field.
18. Enter your research group's website in the research_group_website field.
19. Enter your research group's institution in the research_group_institution field.
20. Enter your departmental approval status in the departmental_approval field, which should match one of the provided options (e.g., "Departmental approval", "Departmental approval pending", or "Not applicable").
21. Enter the date of approval in the approval_date field.
22. Enter a note about the departmental approval in the approval_note field.
23. Enter any additional notes about your research group in the departmental_note field.
24. Enter your department's contact person's name in the departmental_contact field.
25. Enter any additional notes about your research group in the research_group_notes field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, *): Your phone number.
* **University** (`university`, text, *): The name of your university.
* **Department** (`department`, text, *): The name of your department.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, *): Your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, *): Your supervisor's title.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, *): Your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone Number** (`supervisor_phone_number`, text, *): Your supervisor's phone number.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, *): The area of your research.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, *): The start date of your project.
* **Research Group Name** (`research_group_name`, text, *): The name of your research group.
* **Research Group Contact** (`research_group_contact`, text, *): The name of your research group's contact person.
* **Research Group Email** (`research_group_email`, email, *): The email address of your research group.
* **Research Group Phone Number** (`research_group_phone_number`, text, *): The phone number of your research group.
* **Research Group Website** (`research_group_website`, text, *): The website of your research group.
* **Research Group Institution** (`research_group_institution`, text, *): The institution of your research group.
* **Supervisor Institution** (`supervisor_institution`, text, *): The institution of your supervisor.
* **Departmental Approval** (`departmental_approval`, select_one, *): The status of your departmental approval, which should match one of the provided options.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, *): The date of approval.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`, text, *): A note about the departmental approval.
* **Departmental Note** (`departmental_note`, text, *): Any additional notes about your department.
* **Departmental Contact** (`departmental_contact`, text, *): The name of your department's contact person.
* **Research Group Notes** (`research_group_notes`, text, optional): Any additional notes about your research group.
