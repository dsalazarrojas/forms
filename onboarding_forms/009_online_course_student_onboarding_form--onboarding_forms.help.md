# online_course_student_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online course student onboarding form is a crucial step for students to provide essential details about themselves to enhance their learning experience in the online course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, including First Name, Last Name, Email, Date of Birth, and Phone.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the Course Name, Start Date, Start Time, and End Date in the format YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM AM/PM respectively.
5. Provide a brief Description of the course.
6. Optionally, you can add a personal Image related to the course.
7. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address (street, city, state, zip code).
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city you reside in.
* **Course Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the course in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of the course in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the course in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Goals** (Text, Optional): Enter your goals and objectives for the course.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Instructor** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your course instructor.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Image** (Text, Optional): Upload or enter the URL of a relevant image.
