# Workforce Mobility Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workforce Mobility Program Application Form is a crucial document for requesting and approving employee job changes within the organization. It captures essential details about the employee, the job they are requesting, and the necessary approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field according to your situation.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your job request.
3. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Form**: Select "Yes" if you are ready to onboard to a new job role.
  • This indicates your intention to start the onboarding process.
* **Employee Information**: Enter your full name, employee ID, and other relevant details.
  • This helps the approvers know who you are and contact you for further discussion.
* **Manager Approval**: Select "Yes" if your current manager is aware of and supports your job change.
  • This step ensures your manager's approval, making the process more efficient.
* **Job Information**: Provide the title of the new job you are requesting.
  • This ensures that the job details are accurately captured for approval and onboarding.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your new job responsibilities.
  • This provides context about your new role and expectations.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you will be working.
  • This helps to ensure that the new location is accurately captured.
* **Work Arrangement**: Select "Yes" if you have any specific work arrangement requirements (e.g., telecommuting).
  • This indicates any special requests you have for your new job.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start the new job.
  • This is the date when you expect to begin working in your new role.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you expect to end your current job.
  • This is essential for ensuring a smooth transition.
* **Job Title**: Select "Yes" if you are ready to assume the new job title.
  • This confirms your understanding of the new role's title.
* **Team**: Select "Yes" if you will be working with a specific team.
  • This helps the approvers determine the team you will be working with.
* **Role**: Select "Yes" if you will have a specific role within the team.
  • This clarifies your responsibilities within the team.
* **Manager**: Select "Yes" if your new manager is aware of and supports your job change.
  • This ensures that your new manager is informed and approves the move.
* **Department**: Select "Yes" if you are ready to join a specific department.
  • This helps the approvers determine the department you will be joining.
* **Location 2**: Enter any additional location details, if applicable.
  • This provides context for your new location.
* **Reason For Request**: Provide a brief explanation for your job change request.
  • This helps the approvers understand your motivations and requirements.
* **Manager Note**: Enter any additional comments or concerns your manager may have.
  • This ensures that all relevant information is captured and considered.
* **Approver**: Select "Yes" if the approver is aware of and supports your job change.
  • This final step confirms the approver's agreement and approval.

## Tips
Remember to:

* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure you have all necessary approvals before submitting your form.
* Communicate clearly and concisely with your manager and approvers throughout the process.
* Be prepared to discuss any issues or concerns that arise during the approval process.
