# Group Fitness Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from new members joining the group fitness program, ensuring accurate records and emergency contact details are on file.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of multiple sections. Please complete the following:

1. **New Member Info**: Select the type of new member you are (New Member or Other).
2. **Email**: Enter the new member's email address.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter the new member's phone number.
4. **Membership Type**: Choose the type of membership (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
5. **Workout Frequency**: Select how frequently the new member plans to attend workouts (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
6. **Start Date**: Enter the date the new member plans to start the program.
7. **End Date**: Enter the date the new member plans to end the program.
8. **Additional Info**: Provide any additional relevant information about the new member.
9. **Membership Status**: Choose the current status of the new member's membership (Active, Pending, or Canceled).
10. **Membership ID**: Enter the new member's unique membership ID.
11. **Fitness Goal**: If applicable, choose the new member's fitness goal (Weight Loss, Muscle Gain, or Endurance).
12. **Emergency Contact**: Choose who the new member's emergency contact is (Spouse, Partner, Friend, or Family).
13. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship type with the emergency contact (Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Son, or Daughter).
14. **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
15. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
16. **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
17. **Emergency Contact Relationship Info**: Enter any additional information about the emergency contact relationship.
18. **Emergency Contact Relationship Type**: If different from the previous choice, select the type of relationship with the emergency contact (Spouse, Partner, Friend, or Family).
19. **Emergency Contact Authorized**: Choose if the emergency contact is authorized to receive notifications (True or False).
20. **Medical Conditions**: Enter any relevant medical conditions that may impact the new member's workout routine or safety.
21. **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional information about the new member's medical conditions.
22. **Emergency Contact Medical Conditions**: Enter any emergency contact's medical conditions that may impact the new member's safety.
23. **Emergency Contact Medical Notes**: Enter any additional information about the emergency contact's medical conditions.
24. **Emergency Contact Medical History**: Enter any emergency contact's medical history that may impact the new member's safety.
25. **Fitness Onboarding Form Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the new member.
