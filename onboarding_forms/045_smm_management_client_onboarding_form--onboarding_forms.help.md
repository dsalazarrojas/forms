# SMM Management Client Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SMM Management Client Onboarding Form is a crucial step in the onboarding process for new clients. This form collects essential information to set up and manage the client's account effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the social media platforms the client is active on from the "Social Media Platforms" dropdown list.
5. Enter the client's social media accounts in the "Social Media Accounts" field.
6. Choose the primary marketing channel for the client from the "Marketing Channels" dropdown list.
7. Describe the target audience for the client in the "Target Audience" field.
8. Outline the marketing objectives for the client in the "Marketing Objectives" field.
9. Enter the onboarding date and time in the "Onboarding Date" and "Onboarding Time" fields, respectively.
10. Select the billing cycle for the client from the "Billing Cycle" dropdown list.
11. Enter the billing frequency for the client in the "Billing Frequency" field.
12. Enter the billing amount for the client in the "Billing Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Please enter the name of the client, as we'll be referring to them by name throughout our interactions.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address to ensure we can communicate with them effectively.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number so we can reach out to them in case of any urgent matters.
* **Social Media Platforms**: Select the social media platforms where the client is most active, such as Facebook, Instagram, or LinkedIn. This helps us tailor our communication strategies accordingly.
* **Social Media Accounts**: Enter the client's social media accounts, including handles or profiles, so we can tag and engage with them properly.
* **Marketing Channels**: Choose the primary marketing channel for the client, such as email, phone, or social media. This helps us determine the best communication approach.
* **Target Audience**: Describe the target audience for the client, including demographics, interests, or behaviors.
* **Marketing Objectives**: Outline the marketing objectives for the client, such as increasing brand awareness, generating leads, or driving sales.
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date when the onboarding process began.
* **Onboarding Time**: Enter the time of the onboarding process, if applicable.
* **Billing Cycle**: Select the billing cycle that best suits the client's needs, such as monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Billing Frequency**: Enter the billing frequency for the client, including the number of times we bill them per cycle.
* **Billing Amount**: Enter the billing amount for the client, including any additional costs or fees.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide the best service for your account.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact us for clarification.
* Review and update your information periodically to reflect any changes in your business or marketing strategies.
