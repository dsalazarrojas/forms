# Community Health Worker Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information about new Community Health Workers, including their basic details, training completion status, and emergency contact information. It is used to ensure they are correctly onboarded into our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your basic information in the first section.
   - Enter your full legal name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
   - Enter your assigned employee ID as provided by HR.
   - Enter the district or neighborhood where you are assigned to work.

2. Provide your contact details:
   - Enter your contact phone number.
   - Enter your work email address.

3. Indicate your completion of mandatory training modules:
   - Select all the training modules you have completed.

4. Enter your supervisor's name.

5. Provide emergency contact details:
   - Enter the name of your emergency contact.
   - Enter their phone number.

6. Confirm your agreement to follow the Worker Code of Conduct:
   - Select "Yes, I agree".

7. Finally, enter the official date of your hire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name**: Enter your full name as per your official identification documents.
* **Assigned Employee ID**: Enter the ID provided by HR.
* **Assigned District or Neighborhood**: Enter the area where you are primarily working.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your personal or work phone number where you can be reached.
* **Work Email Address**: Enter your official email address where you can be contacted.
* **Completion of Mandatory Training Modules**: Select all training modules you have completed.
	+ These include "Introduction to Public Health", "Ethics and Patient Privacy (HIPAA)", "Field Safety Protocols", "Incident Reporting Tools", and "Community Outreach Strategies".
* **Reporting Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your reporting supervisor.
* **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to follow the Worker Code of Conduct**: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your commitment.
* **Official Date of Hire**: Enter the date your employment started.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are completing all mandatory training modules before submitting this form.
- If you are unsure about any section, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
