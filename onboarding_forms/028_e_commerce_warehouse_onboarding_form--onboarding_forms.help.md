# e_commerce_warehouse_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee information, emergency contact details, and safety acknowledgments for employees working in an e-commerce warehouse environment. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a safe and efficient work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (if any) with accurate and truthful information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose before providing information.
3. Submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <employee_info>
* **Employee Information**: <text input field> (employee info, name, and contact details) - Enter your name and contact information as it appears on your official identification documents.

### <shift_preferences>
* **Shift Preferences**: <text input field> - Provide details about your preferred working hours and shifts, including any special requests or limitations.

### <emergency_contact>
* **Emergency Contact**: <text input field> - Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.

### <emergency_contact_details>
* **Emergency Contact Details (if other)**: <text input field> - If you have a different emergency contact, enter their name and contact information.

### <confirm_emergency_contact>
* **Confirm Emergency Contact**: <text input field> - Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact you entered in the previous field.

### <confirm_emergency_contact_details>
* **Confirm Emergency Contact Details (if other)**: <text input field> - Confirm the emergency contact details you entered in the previous field (if different from the primary emergency contact).

### <safety_acknowledgments>
* **Safety Acknowledgments**: <text input field> - Read and acknowledge the safety guidelines and policies of the e-commerce warehouse environment.

### <confirm_safety_acknowledgments>
* **Confirm Safety Acknowledgments**: <text input field> - Confirm that you have read and understood the safety guidelines and policies.

## Tips
- Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
