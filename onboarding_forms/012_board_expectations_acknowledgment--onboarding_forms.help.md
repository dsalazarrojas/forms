# Board Expectations Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new board members, ensuring they understand their roles, responsibilities, and expectations before joining the board. It gathers details about their name, role, term length, meeting attendance, committee service, fundraising expectations, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Board Member Name** in the text field.
2. Select your **Board Position** from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your **Board Service Start Date** in the date field.
4. Choose your **Term Length** from the dropdown list.
5. Select your **Meeting Attendance Expectation** from the dropdown list.
6. Choose your **Committee Service Expectation** from the dropdown list.
7. Choose your **Fundraising Expectation** from the dropdown list.
8. If applicable, select your **Expected Annual Contribution** from the dropdown list.
9. Confirm your understanding of your **Fiduciary Responsibility** by selecting "Yes, I understand".
10. Check the box to confirm you have read and agree to the **Confidentiality Agreement**.
11. Confirm you have reviewed the **Conflict of Interest Policy** by selecting "Yes, I Understand".
12. If you have any actual conflicts of interest, please type them in the **Actual Conflicts of Interest** field (optional).
13. Select your understanding of the time commitment by choosing "Yes, fully understand" from the dropdown list.
14. Choose your estimated **Expected Hours Per Month** from the dropdown list.
15. Confirm you have completed the **Orientation Completion** by selecting "Yes, completed" from the dropdown list.
16. Check if you have received the **Bylaws** by selecting "Yes, received and reviewed" from the dropdown list.
17. Confirm you understand and accept the **Expectations Acknowledgment** by selecting "Yes, I Acknowledge and Accept" from the dropdown list.
18. Sign your name in the **Member Signature** field.
19. Enter the date of acknowledgment in the **Signature Date** field.
20. If applicable, confirm the **Board Chair Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Board Member Name**: Enter your full name as it should be recorded on official documents.
* **Board Position**: Select the role you will be playing on the board (e.g., Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, etc.).
* **Board Service Start Date**: Enter the date when your term as a board member will begin.
* **Term Length**: Choose the length of your term as a board member (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 years, or until a successor is appointed).
* **Meeting Attendance Expectation**: Select the minimum percentage of meetings you are expected to attend (e.g., 75% minimum, 80% minimum, 90% minimum, or 100% expected).
* **Committee Service Expectation**: Choose the level of committee service you are expected to perform (e.g., serve on at least one committee, serve on two committees, chair one committee, or no committee requirement).
* **Fundraising Expectation**: Select your annual fundraising commitment (e.g., personal donation only, donate and solicit others, no fundraising requirement, or to be discussed).
* **Expected Annual Contribution**: If applicable, choose the minimum donation amount you are expected to make (e.g., under $1,000, $1,000 - $5,000, $5,001 - $10,000, or over $25,000).
* **Fiduciary Responsibility Acknowledgment**: Confirm your understanding of your fiduciary duties as a board member.
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Check the box to confirm you will maintain confidentiality.
* **Conflict of Interest Policy**: Confirm you have reviewed the conflict of interest policy.
* **Actual Conflicts of Interest**: If you have any actual conflicts of interest, type them in this field (optional).
* **Time Commitment Understanding**: Select your understanding of the time commitment as a board member (e.g., yes, fully understand, generally understand, or need more information).
* **Expected Hours Per Month**: Choose the estimated time commitment per month (e.g., 1-5 hours, 6-10 hours, 11-20 hours, or 20+ hours).
* **Orientation Completion**: Confirm you have completed the board orientation.
* **Bylaws Received**: Confirm you have received and reviewed the bylaws.
* **Expectations Acknowledgment**: Confirm you understand and accept the board expectations.
* **Member Signature**: Sign your name as a board member.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your acknowledgment.
* **Board Chair Signature**: If applicable, confirm the board chair's acknowledgment (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure you accurately fill in your board member name and other required fields.
* Review each field carefully to understand your commitments and expectations as a board member.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the board chair or a designated contact person.
* This form is a crucial step in onboarding new board members, so please take the time to complete it thoroughly and accurately.
