# Kitchen And Bath Onboarding Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users for onboarding purposes, ensuring a tailored and smooth onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select "Yes" or "No" for each question to indicate your preferences and goals.
2.  Fill out any text fields (e.g., email, phone, contact person) with your actual information.
3.  Ensure you select the correct tool or project status for your project.
4.  Fill out the description field with a brief description of your project.
5.  Finally, review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
