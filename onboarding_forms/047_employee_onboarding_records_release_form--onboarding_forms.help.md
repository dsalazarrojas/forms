# Employee Onboarding Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Records Release Form is used to document and track the onboarding process of new employees. This form helps to release records related to employee data, verify employment agreements, and confirm onboarding completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the employee's onboarding process.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Onboarding Records Release Form** (`employee_onboarding_records_release_form`, text, optional)
This field is used to enter a brief description of the onboarding records release form. Enter a short paragraph explaining the purpose and scope of the form.

### 2. **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, text, optional)
Describe the onboarding process used for the new employee, including any relevant steps, procedures, or activities performed during this process.

### 3. **Employee Data Release Form** (`employee_data_release_form`, text, optional)
This field is for entering a description of the employee data release form. Explain the purpose and scope of this form, including what kind of information is being released.

### 4. **Employment Agreement Form** (`employment_agreement_form`, text, optional)
Enter a description of the employment agreement form, including its purpose and scope. Explain what kind of information is covered in this form.

### 5. **Onboarding Confirmation** (`onboarding_confirmation`, text, optional)
Provide a confirmation statement or a note about the onboarding process completion.

### 6. **Employee Data Verification Form** (`employee_data_verification_form`, text, optional)
Describe the employee data verification form, including its purpose and scope. Explain how this form verifies employee data.

### 7. **Onboarding Completion Form** (`onboarding_completion_form`, text, optional)
Enter a description of the onboarding completion form, including its purpose and scope. Explain what kind of information is covered in this form.

### 8. **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, text, optional)
Provide a confirmation statement or a note about the employee's confirmation.

### 9. **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, text, optional)
Enter the current onboarding status of the employee, such as "active," "inactive," "pending," etc.

### 10. **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, optional)
Select the date the onboarding process started.

### 11. **Onboarding Time** (`onboarding_time`, time, optional)
Enter the time the onboarding process started.

### 12. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
Enter any additional comments or notes about the onboarding process.

### 13. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter the email address of the employee or responsible person handling the onboarding process.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the onboarding process and forms.
* Make sure to keep the onboarding process up-to-date and accurate.
