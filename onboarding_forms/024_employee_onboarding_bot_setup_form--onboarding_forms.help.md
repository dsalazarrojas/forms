# Employee Onboarding Bot Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help set up an onboarding process for new employees in your organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2.  Select the notification channel for onboarding messages (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Email, or SMS).
3.  Choose the time of day to send onboarding notifications (if applicable).
4.  Select the frequency of onboarding notifications (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
5.  Choose the type of document to be sent to new employees (HR Document, Manager Document, or Custom Document).
6.  Select the frequency of document sending (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
7.  Choose the type of task to be assigned to new employees (Task, Reminder, or Message).
8.  Select the frequency of task assignments (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
9.  Choose the frequency of sending documents and tasks (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
10.  Review and submit the form to set up the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name (Company Name)**: Enter the name of the company that the employee will be working for.
    *   This field is optional and is used to identify the company for onboarding purposes.
*   **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter the title of the job or position that the employee will be filling.
    *   This field is optional and is used to display the employee's job title on onboarding messages.
*   **Employee Number (Employee Number)**: Enter the unique identifier of the employee.
    *   This field is optional and is used to track employee numbers for onboarding purposes.
*   **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the employee.
    *   This field is optional but is used to send onboarding messages and documents to the employee.
*   **Phone (Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the employee.
    *   This field is optional and is used for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Message Template (Message Template)**: Select or input a custom onboarding message template.
    *   This field is optional and is used to send personalized messages to new employees.
*   **Task Template (Task Template)**: Select or input a custom onboarding task template.
    *   This field is optional and is used to assign specific tasks to new employees.
*   **Document Template (Document Template)**: Select or input a custom onboarding document template.
    *   This field is optional and is used to send specific documents to new employees.
*   **Notification Channel (Notification Channel)**: Select the notification channel for onboarding messages (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Email, or SMS).
    *   This field is required and determines how new employees will receive onboarding messages.
*   **Notification Time (Notification Time)**: Choose the time of day to send onboarding notifications (if applicable).
    *   This field is optional and is used to schedule onboarding messages for a specific time of day.
*   **Notification Frequency (Notification Frequency)**: Select the frequency of onboarding notifications (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
    *   This field is required and determines how often new employees will receive onboarding messages.
*   **Document Type (Document Type)**: Choose the type of document to be sent to new employees (HR Document, Manager Document, or Custom Document).
    *   This field is required and determines the type of document that will be sent to new employees.
*   **Document Frequency (Document Frequency)**: Select the frequency of document sending (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
    *   This field is optional and determines how often documents will be sent to new employees.
*   **Task Type (Task Type)**: Choose the type of task to be assigned to new employees (Task, Reminder, or Message).
    *   This field is required and determines the type of task that will be assigned to new employees.
*   **Task Frequency (Task Frequency)**: Select the frequency of task assignments (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
    *   This field is optional and determines how often tasks will be assigned to new employees.
*   **Document Frequency Days (Document Frequency Days)**: Select the frequency of sending documents (1, 3, or 5 days).
    *   This field is optional and determines how often documents will be sent to new employees.
*   **Task Frequency Days (Task Frequency Days)**: Select the frequency of task assignments (1, 3, or 5 days).
    *   This field is optional and determines how often tasks will be assigned to new employees.
*   **Notification Frequency Days (Notification Frequency Days)**: Select the frequency of sending onboarding notifications (1, 3, or 5 days).
    *   This field is optional and determines how often onboarding messages will be sent to new employees.
