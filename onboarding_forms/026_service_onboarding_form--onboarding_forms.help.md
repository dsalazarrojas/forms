# Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Onboarding Form is designed to collect vital information about your service. Please fill in this form to provide us with the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in your company's name.
2. Provide your service's address.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Add your phone number.
5. Select the date and time of onboarding.
6. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of your company.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your service's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format (123) 123-1234.
* **Date of Onboarding** (`date_of_onboarding`, date, required: false): Select the date of the onboarding process.
* **Time of Onboarding** (`time_of_onboarding`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the onboarding process.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and efficiently.
- Review your entries before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your cooperation in providing clear and concise information.
- Once submitted, you will receive further instructions on the next steps.

Please fill in the form with the requested information to complete the onboarding process. If you need any assistance or clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
