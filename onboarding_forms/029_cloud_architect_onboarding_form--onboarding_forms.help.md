# Cloud Architect Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cloud Architect Onboarding Form is a comprehensive document that helps us get to know you better and provide you with the necessary tools, resources, and support to excel in your new role. By filling out this form, you will help us:

* Gather key information about your work details
* Identify your areas of expertise and strengths
* Provide you with the required systems and resources
* Ensure you have necessary security clearance and access to critical systems

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information (sections 1-5)
2. Provide your job title, responsibilities, and reporting relationship (sections 6-8)
3. Select your primary cloud platforms and key areas of expertise (sections 9-10)
4. Specify the systems and resources you need access to (sections 11-12)
5. Mention any additional equipment or resources you require (section 13)
6. Indicate your security clearance level (section 14)
7. Add any additional onboarding notes or special requirements (section 15)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required)
  Your full legal name as per your official identity documents
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required)
  Your employee identification number or unique identifier
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required)
  First day of employment
* **Contact Information** (`section_contact`, note, required)
  How to reach you for work-related purposes
* **Work Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required)
  Your corporate email address
* **Work Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required)
  Your desk phone extension
* **Office Location** (`office_location`, text, required)
  Your primary work location
* **Department** (`department`, text, required)
  Cloud Architecture or related team
* **Direct Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required)
  Name of your supervisor
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required)
  Email address of your supervisor
* **Will work with which team members** (`team_members`, text, required)
  List of key team members you will be working with
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required)
  Role or position you hold
* **Reporting Relationship** (`reporting_relationship`, select_one, required)
  Direct report, Matrix reporting, or Dotted line relationship
* **Primary Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, text, required)
  Key job duties and focus areas
* **Primary Cloud Platforms** (`primary_cloud_platforms`, select_multiple, required)
  Select the primary cloud platforms you will use in your role
* **Key Areas of Expertise** (`key_expertise_areas`, select_multiple, required)
  Select the key areas of expertise you bring to your role
* **Systems Requiring Access** (`systems_access`, select_multiple, required)
  Select the systems you need access to for your role
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, text, required)
  List the equipment and resources you need for your role
* **Security Clearance Required** (`security_clearance`, select_one, required)
  Select the level of security clearance you require
* **Additional Onboarding Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, required)
  Any special requirements or accommodations

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible in your responses
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or onboarding team
* This form is confidential and will be reviewed only by authorized personnel.
