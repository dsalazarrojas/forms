# internship_rotation_overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about internship rotations, including dates, departments, supervisors' feedback, learning goals, intern's details, and rotation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the rotation dates by clicking on the calendar icon and choosing the dates for the rotation.
2. Enter the department(s) involved in the rotation in the text field.
3. Check the box next to 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the supervisor is available during the rotation.
4. Enter the intern's learning goals and any other relevant details in the text field.
5. Enter any additional details about the intern in the text field.
6. Select the current status of the rotation by choosing from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Rotation Dates**: Select the dates of the rotation.
  - If you have any dates in mind for the rotation, please select them on the calendar.
  - This will help track and schedule future rotations.
- **Departments**: Enter the department(s) involved in the rotation.
  - Please list all departments that are participating in the rotation.
  - This will help identify the teams and departments that are involved.
- **Supervisors**: Check 'Yes' if the supervisor is available during the rotation.
  - If the supervisor is not available, check 'No'.
  - This will help determine whether the supervisor is available to oversee the rotation.
- **Learning Goals**: Enter the intern's learning goals for the rotation.
  - Please describe what the intern hopes to learn or achieve during the rotation.
  - This will help tailor the rotation to the intern's needs.
- **Intern Details**: Enter any additional details about the intern.
  - Please provide any other relevant information about the intern.
  - This will help the supervisor and HR staff understand the intern's needs.
- **Rotation Status**: Select the current status of the rotation.
  - Choose from 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the rotation's current status.
  - This will help track the rotation's progress and make informed decisions.
