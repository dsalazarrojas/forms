# Telehealth Therapist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Therapist Onboarding Form is used to gather essential information about new telehealth therapists, ensuring a smooth and secure onboarding process. This form will ask for basic contact and professional details to create your account and set up your virtual office.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the required fields (indicated as `required: true`) and any additional information requested. 
- Ensure your email address is valid and formatted correctly (e.g., [your_name}@example.com)
- For select fields, choose one or more options as applicable
- If you have any issues or need assistance, please contact your internal team or system administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, `required: false`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, `required: false`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, `required: false`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, `required: false`): Choose the relevant role(s) you hold (e.g., licensed therapist, supervisor, or other).
* **License** (`license`, select_one, `required: false`): Choose your primary licensure (e.g., Licensure 1, Licensure 2, or Licensure 3).
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, select_multiple, `required: false`): Indicate your availability for documentation requirements (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, `required: false`): Confirm your consent for certain procedures or policies (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, `required: false`): Add any additional information or comments about your onboarding.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, `required: false`): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Address** (`address`, text, `required: false`): Enter your address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, `required: false`): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, `required: false`): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of your information to prevent delays in your onboarding process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your internal team or system administrators.
