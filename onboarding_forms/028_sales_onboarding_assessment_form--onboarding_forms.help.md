# Sales Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users assess their sales experience and onboarding process. It will be used by sales managers or team members to evaluate individual sales performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each field's label and description for clear understanding.
3. Complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
4. Use the provided hints and options (if available) for guidance.
5. Submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your sales experience?** (`sales_onboarding_assessment_form_1`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is intended to gather information about your background and history in sales.
* **What is your sales team?** (`sales_onboarding_form_2`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is meant to capture details about your sales team and colleagues.
* **Sales onboarding assessment** (`sales_onboarding_form_3`, time, required: false)
	+ This field is likely a numerical score or rating to evaluate your sales performance.
* **Sales onboarding form 5** (`sales_onboarding_form_4`, date, required: false)
	+ This field is intended for feedback or additional notes regarding your onboarding process.
