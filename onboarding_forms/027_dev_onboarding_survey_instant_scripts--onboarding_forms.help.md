# Dev Onboarding Survey Instant Scripts - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help new employees get started on their first day of work by providing an initial survey of our team's processes and what they need to do to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the pages and select all that apply to your onboarding needs. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Survey Form** (1, select_multiple): Select all the areas of the onboarding process you feel you need help with. 
* **Instant Scripts** (2, text): Write a brief description of what you hope to achieve by using instant scripts, and any relevant background information you want to share.
* **Category** (3, select_one): Indicate if the instant script is related to a specific category of work. 
* **Onboarding Form** (4, select_one): Select the specific onboarding form that you are working with. 
* **Description** (5, text): Write a brief description of what the instant script is about and what it does. 
* **Assigned Tool** (6, select_one): Indicate if the instant script is assigned to a specific tool. 
* **Category ID** (7, text): Enter the ID of the category assigned to the instant script.
* **Form ID** (8, text): Enter the ID of the form that the instant script is assigned to. 

## Tips
- Make sure to be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about what you need to do or who to contact, don't hesitate to reach out to your onboarding team for guidance. 
- Please be as specific as possible when providing information in the form. 

This guide is designed to help new employees complete the form effectively and efficiently, and to get started on their first day of work.
