# publishing_house_staff_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The publishing house staff onboarding form is a comprehensive tool used to gather essential information from new staff members. Its primary objective is to facilitate a smooth and efficient onboarding process, ensuring that new employees are properly set up with the necessary details to perform their job responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: Type your first name as it is written on your official documents.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Type your last name as it is written on your official documents.
3. **Enter your Email**: Provide your professional email address used for official correspondence.
4. **Enter your Job Title**: Type your official job title as assigned by the organization.
5. **Select your Department**: Choose from the available options (Accounting, Marketing, Sales).
6. **Enter your Hire Date**: If applicable, enter the date you were hired.
7. **Enter your Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
8. **Enter your Address**: Type your physical address.
9. **Enter your Manager's Name**: Type your direct manager's name.
10. **Enter your Manager's Email**: If applicable, provide your manager's email address.
11. **Enter your Manager's Phone**: If applicable, provide your manager's phone number.
12. **Enter your Reporting Manager's Name**: Type the name of your reporting manager.
13. **Enter your Emergency Contact Name**: Type the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
14. **Enter your Emergency Contact Phone**: If applicable, provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
15. **Enter the Relationship of your Emergency Contact**: Describe the relationship with your emergency contact.
16. **Enter your Manager's Name**: Type the name of your direct manager.
17. **Enter your Reporting Manager**: Type the name of your reporting manager.
