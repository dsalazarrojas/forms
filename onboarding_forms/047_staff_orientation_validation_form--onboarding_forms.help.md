**Staff Orientation Validation Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose:** This form is designed to collect information and feedback from new employees as they go through the onboarding process. It's meant to ensure a smooth transition and help identify areas for improvement. 

**How to Complete This Form:** 

This form has a series of fields that require you to input information about the new employee's details and your experience with the onboarding process. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

1. **New Employee Name** (Text): Enter the name of the new employee.
2. **Email** (Email): Enter the new employee's email address.
3. **Onboarding Date** (Date): Enter the date of the new employee's onboarding.
4. **Department** (Select One): Please select if the new employee is part of a team or department.
5. **New Employee Phone** (Text): Enter the new employee's phone number.
6. **Supervisor Name** (Text): Enter the name of the new employee's supervisor.
7. **Feedback** (Note): Enter any feedback or comments you have about the onboarding process.
8. **Onboarding Process Satisfaction** (Select Multiple): Rate your satisfaction with the onboarding process, choosing from the options Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
9. **New Employee Start Date** (Date): Enter the date the new employee started work.

**Tips:**

* Please ensure that you have the new employee's permission to input their personal details.
* Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your HR or onboarding team for clarification.
* This form is a valuable tool for improving our onboarding process, so please take the time to provide detailed feedback.
