# Brand Manager Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from new Brand Managers to help us onboard them efficiently and effectively. Please complete this form to provide us with the necessary details to start your onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions for each section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: <name> (id: 2, type: text, required: true)
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employment Start Date**: <start_dt> (id: 3, type: date, required: true)
	+ This is the date you started working as a Brand Manager. Please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee ID (if issued)**: <emp_id_opt> (id: 4, type: text, required: false)
	+ If you have an employee ID issued by your previous employer, please enter it here.
* **Work Email Address**: <work_email_addr> (id: 5, type: email, required: true)
	+ Enter your work email address as it appears on your company records.
* **Briefly describe your experience with previous brands**: <brand_exp_desc> (id: 7, type: text, required: true)
	+ Share your experience and skills gained from working with previous brands.
* **Core Competencies**: <core_competencies_select> (id: 8, type: select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Select all the core competencies that apply to you:
		+ Strategic Planning
		+ Creative Direction
		+ Market Research
		+ Digital Marketing
		+ Team Leadership
		+ Budget Management
* **Laptop Preference**: <laptop_pref> (id: 10, type: select_one, required: true)
	+ Choose your laptop preference:
		+ MacBook Pro
		+ Windows High-End
		+ Other
* **Software Access Required**: <software_needs> (id: 11, type: select_multiple, required: true)
	+ Select all the software you need access to:
		+ Adobe Creative Cloud
		+ Google Analytics
		+ Brandwatch
		+ Salesforce
		+ Slack
		+ Asana
* **Any specific areas of the business you want to learn more about first**: <learn_interests> (id: 13, type: text, required: true)
	+ Share your interests and areas of focus for onboarding.
* **Do you have any preferences for an onboarding buddy/mentor?**: <mentor_pref> (id: 14, type: text, required: false)
	+ If you have a preferred onboarding buddy or mentor, please let us know.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: <emergency_name_val> (id: 16, type: text, required: true)
	+ Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: <emergency_phone_val> (id: 17, type: text, required: true)
	+ Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

Note: Please make sure to review each field carefully and enter accurate information to ensure a smooth onboarding process. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to us.
