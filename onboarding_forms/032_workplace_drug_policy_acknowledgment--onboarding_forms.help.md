# Workplace Drug Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to have employees acknowledge and confirm their understanding of the company's drug policy. Completing this form is mandatory for all new employees or those who have not been properly onboarded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have read and understood the company's drug policy.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in your first name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in your last name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, **required**: false): Type in your current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select your department from the list:
	+ Department of Accounting
	+ Department of Marketing
	+ Department of IT
	+ Department of HR
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **required**: false): Fill in the current date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time in** (`time_in`, `time`, **required**: false): Fill in the start of your workday in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Time out** (`time_out`, `time`, **required**: false): Fill in the end of your workday in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Confirm date** (`confirm_date`, `date`, **required**: false): Confirm the date you work.
* **Confirm time in** (`confirm_time_in`, `time`, **required**: false): Confirm the start of your workday.
* **Confirm time out** (`confirm_time_out`, `time`, **required**: false): Confirm the end of your workday.
* **Signed** (`signed`, `text`, **required**: false): Type in your name as it appears on your government ID or passport.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `select_multiple`, **required**: true): Select one of the following signatures:
	+ John Doe
	+ Jane Doe
	+ Joe Doe
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**: false): Type in your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, **required**: false): Type in your phone number.
* **Department ID** (`department_id`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select your department ID from the list:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
* **Time zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select your time zone from the list:
	+ EST
	+ PST
	+ CST
	+ GMT
* **Language** (`language`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select your language from the list:
	+ English
	+ French
	+ Spanish
	+ German
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select your country from the list:
	+ USA
	+ Canada
	+ Mexico
	+ China
* **Confirm signed** (`confirm_signed`, `text`, **required**: false): Confirm your signed name.
* **Confirm signature** (`confirm_signature`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false): Confirm one of the following signatures:
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Joe
* **Confirm department ID** (`confirm_department_id`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false): Confirm your department ID.
* **Confirm time zone** (`confirm_time_zone`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Confirm your time zone.

## Tips

* Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly before signing the form.
* Ensure you have read and understood the company's drug policy before signing this form.
* Save the form as a reference for future reference.
