# Laboratory Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about new staff members as part of the onboarding process to help them get familiar with the lab's environment and work together as a team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Staff Details
* **New Staff Details** (`new_staff_details`, text, required: false): This field is a text box where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, job title, and any other information you think is relevant to your role in the lab.

### Experience Levels
* **Experience Levels** (`experience_levels`, text, required: false): Please describe your level of experience in a laboratory setting, including any relevant education, training, or work history. Be as specific as possible, highlighting your strengths and areas where you'd like to develop further.

### Safety Training Status
* **Safety Training Status** (`safety_training_status`, text, required: false): Describe your current status regarding safety training. If you're unsure, please check with the lab manager or team lead. This could include completed certifications, in-progress training, or areas where you'd like additional training.

### Equipment Proficiency
* **Equipment Proficiency** (`equipment_proficiency`, text, required: false): Explain your level of proficiency with lab equipment, including any specific machinery you've worked with before. If you're new to the lab's equipment, it's okay to be honest about your limited experience. We'll work together to get you up-to-speed quickly.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): If there's anything specific you'd like to add or any additional information that would be helpful for us to know about you, please include it here. This could include relevant skills you bring, any special requests, or areas of interest for your role in the lab.
