# new employee document upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect onboarding documents from new employees, including resumes, onboarding documents, proof of address, and other documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Upload each of the required documents for onboarding by selecting the corresponding field and uploading the file from your computer or device.
* Fill out the required fields for each document, such as resume, onboarding documents, proof of address, and other documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, required/optional): Upload your resume as a PDF or JPEG file. This document is required for all new employees.
* **Onboarding Documents** (`onboarding_documents`, text, required/optional): Upload any additional onboarding documents required by your department or location. This document is optional.
* **Proof Of Address** (`proof_of_address`, text, required/optional): Upload a proof of address document, such as a utility bill or lease agreement. This document is optional.
