# Self Commencement Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from users who have completed the onboarding process and are now using our platform. Your responses will help us understand your overall experience and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the survey questions on page 1 (Survey Questions) to provide us with your overall feedback.
2. On page 2, select one of the sentiment options (Excellent, Good, Bad) to rate your overall experience.
3. For each feedback option, select one of the provided suggestions (Feedback 1, Feedback 2, Feedback 3) and provide a brief explanation.
4. If you have any additional suggestions or comments, type them in the text field "Suggestions" on page 2.
5. If you'd like to provide more detailed comments, type them in the text field "Comments" on page 2.
6. If you'd like to be contacted for further clarification or to provide more information, provide your contact details in the "Contact Info" section on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (text): Please answer the questions on this page to provide your overall feedback on our platform.
* **Overall Experience** (number): Select one of the options to rate your overall experience on a scale of 1 (Bad) to 3 (Excellent).
* **Sentiment** (select_multiple): This field allows you to select one or more of the options to express your sentiment towards our platform.
* **Feedback** (select_one): Select one of the suggestions and provide a brief explanation in the text field provided.
* **Suggestions** (text): If you have any additional suggestions or ideas for improvement, type them in this field.
* **Comments** (text): If you'd like to provide more detailed comments or feedback, type them in this field.
* **Contact Info** (email): If you'd like to be contacted for further clarification or information, provide your email address in this field.
* **Contact Name** (text): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your name in this field.
* **Contact Title** (text): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your title or position in this field.
* **Contact Email** (email): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your email address in this field.
* **Contact Phone** (text): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your phone number in this field.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions and providing feedback.
* If you'd like to be contacted, make sure to provide your contact information accurately.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
