# Employee Orientation Week Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for new hires to provide important details about themselves as part of the onboarding process. It helps the HR department to efficiently collect and organize the necessary information for a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for session sign-ups, indicating your interest in attending sessions or not.
3. Enter your Employee Name, which is your official name as it appears on company documents and systems.
4. Enter your Job Title, which is your position within the company.
5. Enter your Employee ID, which is your unique ID number assigned to you by the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Sign-ups**: Select "Yes" if you want to attend sessions, or "No" if you do not.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents and company systems. This is the name we will use for company purposes.
* **Job Title**: Enter your official job title within the company. This will help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique ID number assigned to you by the company. This is a required field for company records and system access.
