# Digital Communication Policy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Communication Policy Agreement form is used to onboard new employees and gather information about their contact details, onboarding status, and agreement to company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and making sure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to review the acknowledgement fields carefully, as they are critical to completing the onboarding process.
