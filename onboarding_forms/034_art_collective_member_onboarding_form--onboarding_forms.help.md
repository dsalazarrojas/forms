# Art Collective Member Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Collective Member Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from artists interested in joining the collective. This information will be used to review applications, assign membership status, and facilitate internal processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill out the form in order, starting from "Personal Information" and moving down to "Agreement and Consent".
3. Use the provided hints for guidance on each section.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address for contact and logistical purposes.
* **Primary Artistic Medium** (`primary_medium`, text, required): Specify your primary medium of artistic practice (e.g., painting, photography, etc.).
* **Years of Artistic Experience** (`years_experience`, number, optional): If applicable, provide the number of years you have been practicing your artistic medium.
* **Artistic Statement or Bio** (`artistic_statement`, text, required): Write a brief description of your artistic practice and background.
* **Portfolio Website or Link** (`portfolio_url`, text, optional): If you have a public portfolio or website, provide the URL.
* **Instagram Handle or Social Media** (`instagram_handle`, text, optional): If applicable, provide your Instagram handle or other social media handle for further connection.
* **Portfolio Images or Links** (`portfolio_images`, text, optional): If applicable, provide URLs to your work or images you'd like to share.
* **Why Are You Interested in Joining** (`why_join`, text, required): Briefly explain why you're interested in joining the art collective.
* **Areas of Interest for Collaboration** (`collaboration_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more areas where you'd like to collaborate (e.g., group exhibitions, shared studio space, etc.).
* **Meeting and Event Availability** (`meeting_availability`, select_multiple, required): Specify your availability for meetings and events.
* **Commitment Level** (`commitment_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of commitment to the collective (full-time, part-time, or occasional).
* **Can you Contribute Studio or Exhibition Space** (`studio_space_available`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate if you can contribute studio or exhibition space.
* **I have read and Agree to the Collective Bylaws** (`acknowledge_bylaws`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the collective bylaws.
* **I understand Membership Dues and Fees** (`acknowledge_dues`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the membership dues and fees.
* **Desired Membership Start Date** (`membership_date`, date, required): Specify the desired start date for your membership.
