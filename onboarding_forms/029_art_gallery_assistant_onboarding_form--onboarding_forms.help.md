# Art Gallery Assistant Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Art Gallery Assistant Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from new art gallery assistants during the onboarding process. This form helps the assistant team understand your personal details, employment status, and technical requirements for access to internal systems.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the basic information section, providing your full name, date of birth, and personal email address.
2. Enter your preferred name or nickname, if applicable.
3. Provide your residential address for contact and delivery purposes.
4. Enter your emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
5. Enter your employment details, such as start date, employment status, and tax identification number.
6. Provide direct deposit information, including the bank name, account number, and routing number.
7. Answer the internal checklist questions regarding system access, gallery key or access card issuance, and orientation scheduling.
8. If applicable, enter any additional manager comments or notes.
9. Finally, submit the form once completed.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (assistant_full_name)** (`text`, Required): Your full name.
* **Preferred Name - Nickname (preferred_name)** (`text`, Optional): Your preferred name or nickname.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)** (`date`, Required): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Personal Email Address (contact_email)** (`email`, Required): Your personal email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Mobile Phone Number (contact_phone)** (`text`, Required): Your mobile phone number for emergency contact.
* **Residential Address (home_address)** (`text`, Required): Your current residential address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name)** (`text`, Required): The name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone)** (`text`, Required): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact (relationship_to_contact)** (`text`, Required): Your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Employment Start Date (start_date)** (`date`, Required): Your employment start date with the gallery.
* **Employment Status (position_status)** (`select_one`, Required): Your employment status with the gallery (Full-time, Part-time, Intern (Paid), or Intern (Unpaid)).
* **Tax Identification Number (tax_id_number)** (`text`, Required): Your tax identification number (SSN or TIN).
* **Direct Deposit Information (direct_deposit_details)** (`text`, Required): Your direct deposit information, including bank name, account number, and routing number.
* **Gallery Key or Access Card Issued (key_access_granted)** (`select_one`, Required): Whether you have received a gallery key or access card.
* **Systems Access Granted (systems_access_granted)** (`select_one`, Required): Whether you have access to internal systems (Email, CRM, POS).
* **Gallery Orientation Session Scheduled (orientation_scheduled)** (`select_one`, Required): Whether a gallery orientation session has been scheduled for you.
* **Manager Onboarding Notes (manager_comments)** (`text`, Optional): Any additional comments or notes from your manager regarding your onboarding process.
* **Form Submission Date (submission_date)** (`date`, Required): The date and time you submitted this form.

Please note that some fields are marked as "Required" to ensure that we have accurate and complete information for our records. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
