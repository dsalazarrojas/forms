# new_employee_welcome_packet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new employee welcome packet form is a collection of essential information and acknowledgments required for a new employee to complete during their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Select your title from the options provided.
3. Enter your start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your manager's name and email address.
5. Confirm that you have received and reviewed the employee handbook.
6. Provide emergency contact information, including the relationship and phone number.
7. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required: false): Enter your first name as it should be displayed on your business cards and company communications.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required: false): Enter your last name as it should be displayed on your business cards and company communications.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Required: false): Enter the date you started your new role in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Title** (`title`, text, Required: false): Select your job title as it should be displayed on your business cards and company communications.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, Required: false): Enter your direct supervisor's name as it should be displayed on company communications.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, Required: false): Enter your manager's email address as it should be displayed on company communications.
* **Handbook Acknowledgments** (`handbook_ack`, select_one, Required: false): Confirm that you have received and reviewed the employee handbook.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, Required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, Required: false): Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
	+ Options: Spouse, Parent, Parent and Spouse, Other
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, Required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Note:** All fields are optional but recommended to be completed accurately and completely.

# Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If any fields are not completed, it may delay your onboarding process.
* If you are having trouble completing a field, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for assistance.
* The form is intended to be completed during your onboarding process and is not intended to be repeated for the same employee.
