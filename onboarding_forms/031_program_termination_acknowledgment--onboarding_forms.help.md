# Program Termination Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Program Termination Acknowledgment form is used to document and confirm the termination of a participant's involvement in a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details in the "Participant Details" field.
2. Enter the date of termination in the "Termination Date" field.
3. Choose the reason for termination from the "Termination Reasons" dropdown list.
4. Confirm the termination by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirmation" field.
5. Enter the date of confirmation in the "Confirmation Date" field.
6. If the participant is being terminated due to a reason other than "Inactivity", "Financial", or "Academic", select "Other" in the "Termination Reasons" field and enter the specific reason in the "Termination Date 2" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (participant_details): 
    Enter the participant's details, including their name, ID, and any other relevant information.

* **Termination Date** (termination_date): 
    Enter the date when the participant's involvement in the program was terminated.

* **Reason** (reason): 
    Select the reason for the participant's termination from the available options.

* **Confirmation** (confirmation): 
    Confirm that the participant has been terminated by selecting "Yes".

* **Confirmation Date** (confirmation_date): 
    Enter the date when the participant confirmed their termination.

* **Termination Reasons** (termination_reasons): 
    Select all applicable termination reasons. For "Other" options, enter a specific reason.

* **Termination Date 2** (termination_date_2): 
    Enter the date when the participant was terminated if the termination reason is "Other".
