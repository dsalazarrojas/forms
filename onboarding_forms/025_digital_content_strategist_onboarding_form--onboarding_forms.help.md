# Digital Content Strategist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Content Strategist Onboarding Form is a mandatory form for new Digital Content Strategists to provide essential information about their onboarding process, readiness for the role, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your onboarding details in the text field marked "onboarding_details".
2. Select your readiness for the Digital Content Strategist role from the options provided in the "candidate_readiness" field.
3. Choose any applicable options for your onboarding status in the "onboarding_status" field.
4. Enter the date of your onboarding in the "onboarding_date" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Select your onboarding time in the "onboarding_time" field.
6. Add any additional onboarding notes in the "onboarding_note" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "onboarding_email" field.
8. Provide your phone number in the "onboarding_phone" field.
9. Add any relevant agency information in the "agency_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **onboarding_details**: A text field where you can provide an overview of your onboarding process. This is your chance to describe your initial impressions, any challenges you faced, and how you're getting accustomed to our systems.
* **candidate_readiness**: Select from the options to indicate your readiness for the Digital Content Strategist role. Choose the option that best matches your current level of preparedness.
* **onboarding_status**: Select multiple options that apply to your onboarding status. Choose any that accurately reflect your onboarding experience.
* **onboarding_date**: Enter the date of your onboarding in the mm/dd/yyyy format. This is an important field for tracking your onboarding timeline.
* **onboarding_time**: Select your onboarding time. This field is crucial for scheduling purposes.
* **onboarding_note**: Add any additional notes about your onboarding experience here. This is an open-ended field for you to provide any relevant information that isn't covered in other fields.
* **onboarding_email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for future communication.
* **onboarding_phone**: Provide your phone number where you can be reached during work hours.
* **agency_info**: Add any relevant information about your agency or department here. This can help us understand your background and experience.
