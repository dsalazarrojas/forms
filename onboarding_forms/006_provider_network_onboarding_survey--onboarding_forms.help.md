# Provider Network Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your onboarding process as a provider. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions in the order they appear.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. If you have any comments or additional information, please use the "Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can write a brief introduction about your provider organization. Please keep it concise and focused on your organization's onboarding process.
* **Provider Information** (`provider_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any relevant information about your provider organization, such as your contact details or any specific requirements you have.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, `text`, optional): Describe your onboarding process, including any steps or procedures that new providers go through when joining your network.
* **Onboarding Feedback** (`onboarding_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any feedback or comments you have about your onboarding process, including what works well and what doesn't.
* **Onboarding Insights** (`onboarding_insights`, `text`, optional): Consider sharing any insights or best practices you have about your onboarding process.
* **Provider Network** (`provider_network`, `text`, optional): If you have any information about your provider network or any partnerships you've made, please share it here.
* **Onboarding Readiness** (`onboarding_readiness`, `text`, optional): Describe your organization's onboarding readiness, including any tools, training, or support you offer to new providers.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This is your chance to add any additional comments or questions you have about your onboarding process.
