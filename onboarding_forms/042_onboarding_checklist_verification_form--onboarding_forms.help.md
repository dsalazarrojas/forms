# Onboarding Checklist Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onboarding checklist verification form is a critical tool used to collect and verify employee information during the onboarding process. It's essential to complete this form correctly to ensure that the HR department has accurate and up-to-date information about the new employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Onboarding Checklist Verification Form** (`onboarding_checklist_verification_form`, text, required):
	* This field is the title of the form and should not be changed.
2. **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required):
	* Enter a brief description of the employee's job, department, or any other relevant information.
3. **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required):
	* Enter the employee's ID number.
4. **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, required):
	* Select the employee's status as either 'active' or 'inactive'.
5. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required):
	* Enter the employee's job title.
6. **Department** (`department`, text, required):
	* Enter the employee's department.
7. **Reporting Manager ID** (`reporting_manager_id`, number, required):
	* Enter the reporting manager's ID number.
8. **Reporting Manager Name** (`reporting_manager_name`, text, required):
	* Enter the reporting manager's name.
9. **Job Family** (`job_family`, select_multiple, required):
	* Select the job family that best fits the employee's role.
10. **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required):
	* Enter a brief description of the employee's job function.
11. **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required):
	* Enter the employee's hire date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
12. **Employment Terms** (`employment_terms`, select_one, required):
	* Select the employee's employment terms as either 'full-time', 'contract', or 'internship'.
13. **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, text, required):
	* Enter a brief description of the employee's job classification.
14. **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, text, required):
	* Enter the employee's reporting line.
15. **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required):
	* Enter the employee's work location.
16. **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, required):
	* Select the team that the employee belongs to.
17. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required):
	* Enter the manager's email address.
18. **Direct Manager** (`direct_manager`, text, required):
	* Enter the direct manager's name.
19. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required):
	* Enter the manager's phone number.
20. **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, required):
	* Select the job level that best fits the employee's role.
21. **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required):
	* Select the job category that best fits the employee's role.
22. **Reporting Line Name** (`reporting_line_name`, text, required):
	* Enter the reporting line name.
23. **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional):
	* Enter a brief description of the employee's job type (if applicable).
24. **Job Family** (`job_family`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select the job family that best fits the employee's role (if applicable).
25. **Manager Email/Phone** (`manager_email_phone`, text, optional):
	* Enter the manager's email or phone number (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the HR department has up-to-date information about the employee.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the HR department or your manager for guidance.
* This form is a critical document that helps to ensure a smooth onboarding process and accurate employee data.
