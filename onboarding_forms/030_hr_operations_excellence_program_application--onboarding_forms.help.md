# HR Operations Excellence Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Operations Excellence Program Application is designed to collect information from employees to assess their eligibility for the HR Operations Excellence Program. This form helps the program team understand an employee's needs and interests to provide tailored support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your Job Title from the list provided.
3. Choose the relevant Onboarding Programs that interest you.
4. Provide a brief Motivation Statement explaining why you are interested in the program.
5. Select the Assigned Tool that best suits your needs.
6. Write a Description of your goals and expectations from the program.
7. If applicable, provide an additional Description of your assigned tool.
8. Finally, review the form carefully to ensure you have answered all the questions accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., identification card, passport).

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., identification card, passport).

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your official work email address.

### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Select your current job title from the provided list.

### Onboarding Programs
* **Onboarding Programs**: Choose the onboarding programs that interest you from the list provided. These programs are designed to support your professional growth and development.

### Motivation Statement
* **Motivation Statement**: Write a brief statement explaining why you are interested in the HR Operations Excellence Program. Be specific about your goals and expectations.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for the program. If not assigned, choose "other" and provide the name of the tool.

### Description
* **Description**: Write a brief description of your goals and expectations from the program. Be specific about what you hope to achieve.

### Category (Optional)
* **Category**: This field is not required, but if you have any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application, you can provide it here.

### Other Description
* **Other Description**: If you have been assigned a tool other than the ones listed, provide a brief description of it.

### Category 2, Category 3, Category 4, Category 5 (Optional)
* **Category 2, Category 3, Category 4, Category 5**: These fields are not required, but if you have any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application, you can provide it here.

### Experience
* **Experience**: If you have any relevant experience related to the program, provide a brief description of it.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately to ensure a smooth application process.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any mistakes or omissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the program team for assistance.
