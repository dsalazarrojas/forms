# Workplace Safety Trainer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Trainer Onboarding Form is used to gather essential information from users for the onboarding process into the Workplace Safety Trainer program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name.
2. Fill out the form with your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Answer the questions about your qualifications and certifications.
6. Answer the questions about the training sessions you have attended or will attend.
7. Fill out the "Assigned Tool" field if you have been assigned a tool.
