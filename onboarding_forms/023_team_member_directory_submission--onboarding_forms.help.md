# Team Member Directory Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about team members for inclusion in the directory. It's intended for internal use by the organization and is usually completed by someone with knowledge of the team member's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the team member.
2. Enter the last name of the team member.
3. Provide their email address.
4. Select the team member's role within the organization.
5. Enter their job title.
6. Identify their department.
7. Choose their reporting manager from the available options.
8. Select whether they are a member of a team (yes/no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Directory Submission** (Required): This is the name of the form. You can leave this field as is, as it's already pre-filled.
* **First Name** (Required): Enter the first name of the team member.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter the last name of the team member.
* **Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the team member. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Role** (Required): Select the team member's role within the organization. This will help the directory understand their responsibilities.
* **Job Title** (Required): Enter the team member's job title. This will help the directory understand their position.
* **Department** (Required): Enter the team member's department. This will help the directory understand their area of work.
* **Reporting Manager** (Required): Choose the team member's reporting manager from the available options. This will help the directory understand their reporting structure.
* **Team Members** (Required): Select whether the team member is part of a team (yes/no). This will help the directory understand their team dynamics.
