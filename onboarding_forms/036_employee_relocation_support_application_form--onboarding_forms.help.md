# Employee Relocation Support Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employees who are relocating, helping the HR team to provide support and assistance throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form
Please go through each page of the form, answering the questions as accurately as possible. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of relocation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 1. Onboarding Forms
* **Onboarding Forms** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select `Yes` if you are relocating to a new location and need onboarding support, `No` otherwise.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 2. Employee Information
* **Employee Information** (`text`, optional): Enter your employee information, including any relevant details.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 3. Job Details
* **Job Details** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your job role and responsibilities.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 4. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`text`, optional): Enter your job title.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 5. Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 6. Job Location
* **Job Location** (`text`, optional): Enter your current job location.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 7. Reason for Relocation
* **Reason for Relocation** (`text`, optional): Specify the reason for your relocation.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 8. Date of Job Offer
* **Date of Job Offer** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your job offer in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 9. Job Type
* **Job Type** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select `Yes` if your job is a new role, `No` otherwise.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 10. Job Status
* **Job Status** (`Active`, `Inactive`, optional): Select the status of your job.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 11. Job Level
* **Job Level** (`High`, `Low`, optional): Select the level of your job.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 12. Job Function
* **Job Function** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select whether you are relocating to a new job function.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 13. Job Department
* **Job Department** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select whether you are relocating to a new department.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 14. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`text`, optional): Enter your new job title.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 15. Team/Location
* **Team/Location** (`text`, optional): Enter your new team location.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 16. Relocating
* **Relocating** (`text`, optional): Enter your relocating location.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 17. Reason for Relocating
* **Reason for Relocating** (`text`, optional): Specify the reason for your relocation.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 18. Previous Location
* **Previous Location** (`text`, optional): Enter your previous location.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 19. Relocation Reason
* **Relocation Reason** (`text`, optional): Specify the reason for your relocation.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 20. Date
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your relocation.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 21. Start Date
* **Start Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the start date of your new job.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 22. Job Role
* **Job Role** (`text`, optional): Enter your new job role.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 23. Employee Number
* **Employee Number** (`text`, optional): Enter your employee number.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 24. Manager
* **Manager** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select whether you have a manager.

### <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> 25. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`text`, optional): Enter your new job title.

## Tips
* Ensure you have accurate and up-to-date information when filling out this form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you have answered all relevant questions.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with HR or your manager for clarification.
