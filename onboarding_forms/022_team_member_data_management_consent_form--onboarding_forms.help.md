# Team Member Data Management Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for team members to provide consent for the handling and management of their personal data. It is a necessary step to ensure that team members understand and agree to the data handling practices of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the introduction page carefully to understand the purpose and context of the form.
* Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
* Select your job title and department from the provided options.
* Choose whether you consent to handle personal data or not.
* Select a date for when you granted consent.
* Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your work email address where you can be reached.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Select your current job title within the organization.
* **Department** (Department, select one, required): Choose your department from the options provided.
* **Data Handling** (Data Handling, select multiple, required): Select one or both options to indicate your consent for handling personal data.
	+ Consent to handle personal data
	+ No consent
* **Submission Tracking** (Submission Tracking, date, optional): If you want to track when you submitted this form, please enter the date.
* **Consent Date** (Consent Date, date, required): Enter the date when you granted consent.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it. Ensure that you understand the context and implications of your consent. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities for more information.
