# Tech Company Employee Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new employees joining the company, ensuring a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Enter your job title, department, and reporting manager's information in the required fields.
3. Provide your email, phone number, and job start date.
4. If necessary, enter additional job title, department, and reporting manager information in the "Other" fields.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
	+ This field is required.
* **Department**: Enter your current department.
	+ This field is required.
* **Reporting Manager**: Enter your reporting manager's name.
	+ This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
	+ This field is required and must be in a valid email format.
* **Phone**: Enter your company phone number.
	+ This field is required.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter your job start date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
	+ This field is required.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information, such as address or emergency contact.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Work History**: Check if you have previous work history.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Education**: Check if you have relevant education.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Skills**: Check if you have relevant skills.
	+ This field is optional.
* **References**: Check if you have professional references.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Job Title Other**: Enter any additional job title information.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Department Other**: Enter any additional department information.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Reporting Manager Other**: Enter any additional reporting manager information.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Job Start Date Other**: Enter any additional job start date information.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes.
	+ This field is optional.
