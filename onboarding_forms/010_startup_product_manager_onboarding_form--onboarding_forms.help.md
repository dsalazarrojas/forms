# Startup Product Manager Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help new hires get on board with the team quickly and efficiently. It captures essential information about their preferences, role, and team coordination needs. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and we'll get you set up with the necessary tools and resources to succeed in your new role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Submit" button to save your progress and submit your responses when you're ready.
2. Fill out each field in the form according to your preferences and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Preferences** (`onboarding_preferences`, select multiple, required: false): Select all the onboarding preferences that apply to you, such as HR Onboarding, Product Onboarding, or any other relevant preferences.
* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of yourself and your role in the company. This will help us tailor your onboarding experience to your needs.
* **Product Team** (`product_team`, select one, required: false): Choose the team you'll be working with, such as Product Development or Product Launch.
* **Submission Updates** (`submission_updates`, select multiple, required: false): Select the teams that you'd like to receive updates from, such as Team Lead or HR.
* **Early Team Coordination** (`early_team_coordination`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the teams you'd like to coordinate with early in the onboarding process, such as Team Lead or HR.
* **Onboarding Form Submission** (`onboarding_form_submission`, select one, required: false): Let us know if you're ready to submit your onboarding form, and we'll get you set up with the necessary resources.
* **Email Notification** (`email_notification`, select one, required: false): Choose whether you'd like to receive email notifications about company updates and news, or not.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately as possible, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need help.
* Take your time, and don't rush through the form. We want to make sure you get the best onboarding experience possible.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask your colleagues or reach out to us for clarification.
