# Educational Administration Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Administration Onboarding Form is a comprehensive form used to collect and store information for new hires in the educational administration department. This form is used to gather essential details about the new hire, such as job title, job description, supervisor information, and district and school affiliations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that all information is correct.
3. Click "Submit" to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details**: This field is used to capture the basic details of the new hire, including their job title and job description.
	+ **Job Title**: Enter the new hire's official job title.
	+ **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the new hire's role and responsibilities.
* **Supervisor Information**: This field is used to collect information about the new hire's supervisor.
	+ **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the new hire's supervisor.
	+ **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email address of the new hire's supervisor.
* **District and School Affiliations**: This field is used to capture information about the new hire's district and school affiliations.
	+ **District**: Enter the district that the new hire will be affiliated with.
	+ **School**: Enter the school that the new hire will be affiliated with.
* **New Hire Onboarding Status**: This field is used to track the new hire's onboarding status.
	+ **New Hire Onboarding Status**: Select the current onboarding status of the new hire.
* **Metadata**: This field is used to capture metadata related to the onboarding process.
	+ **New Hire ID**: Enter the ID of the new hire.
	+ **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the new hire.
	+ **New Hire Onboarding Form ID**: Enter the ID of the onboarding form.
	+ **New Hire User ID**: Enter the ID of the new hire's user account.
	+ **Created By**: Enter the ID of the user who created the new hire record.
	+ **Updated By**: Enter the ID of the user who last updated the new hire record.
	+ **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the new hire record was last updated.
	+ **Created At**: Enter the date and time the new hire record was created.
	+ **Deleted At**: Enter the date and time the new hire record was deleted.
	+ **Version**: Enter the version number of the new hire record.
	+ **Status**: Enter the status of the new hire record.
	+ **Submitted By**: Enter the ID of the user who submitted the onboarding form.
	+ **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time the onboarding form was submitted.
	+ **Submitted Version**: Enter the version number of the onboarding form submitted.
