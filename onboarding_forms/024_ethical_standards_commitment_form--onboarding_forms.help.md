# Ethical Standards Commitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect commitments from staff members regarding the handling of ethical standards within the organization. It is used to ensure that all staff members understand and abide by the organization's ethical standards and codes of conduct.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. **Signature**: This is a required field that needs to be signed by you, the staff member.
3. **Date Acknowledged**: Please enter the date you are acknowledging the commitment.
4. **Role**: Enter your role within the organization.
5. **Team**: Enter the team you are part of.
6. **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
7. **Contact Phone**: This field is optional and only required if you need to be contacted.
8. **Contact Email**: This field is optional and only required if you need to be contacted.
9. **Other Contact Info**: This field is optional and only required if you have other contact information.
10. **Agreed to Terms**: Select 'True' if you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
11. **Acknowledged Organizational**: Select 'True' if you have acknowledged the organizational commitments.
12. **Staff**: Select 'Yes' if you are a staff member.
13. **Date**: Enter the date.
14. **Time**: Enter the time.
15. **Notes**: This field is optional and for additional comments.
16. **Staff Signature**: This is a required field that needs to be signed by you, the staff member.
17. **Organization Signature**: This is a required field that needs to be signed by an authorized representative of your organization.
18. **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed.
19. **Date Signed Time**: Enter the time you signed.
20. **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to you.
21. **Notes**: This field is optional and for additional comments.
22. **Assigned to**: Enter the person assigned to you.
23. **Assigned by**: Enter the person assigning you.
24. **Assigned Date**: Enter the date you were assigned.
25. **Assigned Date Time**: This field is optional and only required if you need to enter time.
