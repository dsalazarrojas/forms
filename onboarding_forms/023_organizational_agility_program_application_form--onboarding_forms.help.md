# Organizational Agility Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Agility Program Application Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the Organizational Agility Program. The form is intended to help us assess and process applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information readily available to fill in the form.
2. Read each field carefully to understand what information is being requested.
3. Fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
4. Ensure you have checked the submission field before submitting your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Name** (`user_name`, text, Required)
* This field is used to collect your full name.

### **Email** (`email`, email, Required)
* This field is used to collect your email address, which will be used as your contact information.

### **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Required)
* This field is used to collect your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.

### **Department** (`department`, text, Optional)
* This field is used to collect the department/ team you belong to. If you do not specify, this field can be left blank.

### **Team** (`team`, text, Optional)
* This field is used to collect the team you belong to. If you do not specify, this field can be left blank.

### **Role** (`role`, text, Required)
* This field is used to collect your role in the organization.

### **Job Function** (`job_function`, select_one, Required)
* This field is used to select the job function from the options provided. Please select the one that best describes your job function.

### **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, number, Required)
* This field is used to collect the number of years of work experience you have.

### **Expected Completion Date** (`expected_completion_date`, date, Required)
* This field is used to select the expected completion date of the training.

### **Preferred Training Time** (`preferred_training_time`, time, Required)
* This field is used to select the preferred training time. Please choose a time slot that best suits you.

### **Organizational Agility Program Commitment** (`organizational_agility_program_commitment`, select_one, Required)
* This field is used to select the commitment level from the options provided. Please choose the option that best describes your commitment level.

### **Training Method** (`training_method`, select_multiple, Optional)
* This field is used to select the preferred training method from the options provided. If you do not specify, this field can be left blank.

### **Preferred Training Language** (`preferred_training_language`, select_one, Required)
* This field is used to select the preferred training language from the options provided. Please choose the option that best describes your language preference.

### **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, Optional)
* This field is used to enter any additional comments or information. If you do not have any additional comments, you can leave this field blank.

### **Submit** (`submit`, text, Optional)
* This field is used to confirm your submission. Please ensure you have checked all required fields before submitting the form.

Please ensure you have filled in all required fields accurately and thoroughly before submitting your application. If you have any doubts or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
