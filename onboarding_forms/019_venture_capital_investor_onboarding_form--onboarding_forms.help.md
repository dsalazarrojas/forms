# Venture Capital Investor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Venture Capital Investor Onboarding Form is designed to gather information from venture capital investors for the purpose of potential investments, partnerships, or collaborations. This form helps to streamline the onboarding process and ensures that all necessary information is collected in a standardized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your firm's name in the Firm Name field.
2. Select your firm's location from the Firm Location field.
3. Choose your investment focus from the Investment Focus field.
4. Choose the stage of investment you are focused on from the Investment Stage field.
5. Select your investment size from the Investment Size field.
6. Choose the type of investment you are interested in from the Investment Type field.
7. Select the sector of investment from the Investment Sector field.
8. Choose your investor focus from the Investor Focus field.
9. Select the type of investor you are from the Investor Type field.
10. Choose the round of investment you are interested in from the Investment Round field.
11. Select your investor status from the Investor Status field.
12. Choose the due diligence process from the Due Diligence field.
13. Select the communication channels you prefer from the Communication Channels field.
14. Choose your preferred meeting method from the Meeting Preferred field.
15. Choose your preferred email from the Email Preferred field.
16. Select your preferred phone number from the Phone Preferred field.
17. Choose your time zone from the Time Zone field.
18. Select your preferred language from the Language field.
19. Choose your preferred currency from the Currency field.
20. In the Notes field, provide any additional information you think is relevant to your onboarding process.
21. In the Additional Info 1 field, provide any additional information you think is necessary.
22. In the Additional Info 2 field, provide any additional information you think is necessary.
23. In the Additional Info 3 field, provide any additional information you think is necessary.
24. In the Additional Info 4 field, provide any additional information you think is necessary.
25. In the Additional Info 5 field, provide any additional information you think is necessary.
