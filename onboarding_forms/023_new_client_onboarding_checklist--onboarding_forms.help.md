# new_client_onboarding_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the onboarding process of new clients. It collects essential information to create a comprehensive client profile. The form is typically used by client-facing staff to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to begin the onboarding process.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, not required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, not required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, not required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, not required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, not required): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Time of Day** (Time of Day, time, not required): Enter the client's preferred time of day for communication.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, not required): Add any additional information about the client.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submission.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- If any field is left blank, it may cause delays in the onboarding process.
- Use the "Notes" field for any additional information that may be helpful for the client's onboarding process.
