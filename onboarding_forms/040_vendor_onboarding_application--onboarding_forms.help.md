# Vendor Onboarding Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business and determine if you are a good fit for partnership opportunities. It is recommended that you fill out the form in its entirety to increase your chances of being considered for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the contact information section with your business's name, email, phone number, and address.
2. In the business details section, select your industry from the options provided.
3. Choose the services your company offers from the list of options.
4. Select your business type from the options provided.
5. Choose your company size from the options provided.
6. Fill out the company URL if applicable.
7. Finally, fill out any other company details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **VENDOR NAME** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **contact_first_name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **contact_last_name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **address_line_1** (`address_line_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the first line of the company address.
* **address_line_2** (`address_line_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the second line of the company address.
* **city** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where the company is located.
* **state** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state where the company is located.
* **postal_code** (`postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the postal code where the company is located.
* **description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your company.
* **company_name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry your company belongs to.
* **services_offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required): Select the services your company offers.
* **business_type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business your company is.
* **company_size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your company.
* **company_url** (`company_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of your company.
* **company_phone** (`company_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your company.
* **company_address** (`company_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your company.

Note: All fields are required unless specified as optional in the form.
