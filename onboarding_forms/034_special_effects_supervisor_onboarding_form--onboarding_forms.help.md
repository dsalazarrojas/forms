# Special Effects Supervisor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Effects Supervisor Onboarding Form is used to gather information from new hires in the Special Effects department. This form is designed to be completed by new hires who have been assigned a supervisor or studio representative to guide them through the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of each section before proceeding.
2. Answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, and enter free-form text for text fields.
4. Submit the form for review once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, job title, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all relevant skills and certifications you have, such as Animation Supervisor, Visual Effects Supervisor, etc.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, Note, Optional): Enter any relevant information about your onboarding process, such as dates, times, or specific steps.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of job you are being hired for, either Freelance, Part-time, or Full-time.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, Number, Optional): Enter the length of your contract.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of your contract.
* **Studio Address** (`studio_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your studio address.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact information.
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, Select One, Optional): Select your relevant skills, such as Visual Effects, Compositing, 3D Modeling, etc.
* **Special Effects Skills** (`special_effects_skills`, Text, Optional): Enter any specialized skills you have.
* **Compositing Skills** (`compositing_skills`, Text, Optional): Enter any compositing skills you have.
* **3D Modeling Skills** (`3d_modeling_skills`, Text, Optional): Enter any 3D modeling skills you have.
* **2D Animation Skills** (`2d_animation_skills`, Text, Optional): Enter any 2D animation skills you have.
* **Visual Effects Skills** (`visual_effects_skills`, Text, Optional): Enter any visual effects skills you have.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Contract Notes** (`contract_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional contract notes.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, Text, Optional): Enter your supervisor's signature.
* **Studio Signature** (`studio_signature`, Text, Optional): Enter your studio's signature.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date.

Note: All fields with `required: false` can be left blank, but please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
