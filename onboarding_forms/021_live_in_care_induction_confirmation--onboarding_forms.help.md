# Live In Care Induction Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect confirmation from clients and care providers on the live-in care induction process. It ensures that all parties involved in the care induction process have acknowledged and completed their parts of the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the appropriate options for each field based on your role (client or care provider/agency).
2. Fill in the necessary fields with accurate and truthful information.
3. Verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the client being inducted into the care provider agency.
* **client_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the client for contact purposes.
* **client_email** (text, required): Enter the email address of the client for correspondence.
* **care_agency_training** (select_one, optional): Confirm if the care agency has provided training for the carer.
* **care_provider_training** (text, optional): If yes is selected in the previous field, enter the type of training provided by the care provider agency.
* **carer_training_completed** (select_multiple, optional): Confirm if the carer has completed any required training.
* **date_completed** (date, required): Enter the date the carer completed the training.
* **time_completed** (time, required): Enter the time the carer completed the training.
* **signature** (note, required): Sign and date the form to acknowledge completion of the training.
* **live_in_care_induction_confirmation_agency** (select_multiple, required): Confirm if the agency has acknowledged the client's inducted status.
* **live_in_care_induction_confirmation_provider** (select_multiple, required): Confirm if the provider has acknowledged the client's inducted status.
* **training_acknowledgement** (text, required): Enter a statement acknowledging the training completion for the carer.
* **training_acknowledgement_agency** (text, required): Enter a statement acknowledging the training completion for the agency.
* **training_acknowledgement_provider** (text, required): Enter a statement acknowledging the training completion for the provider.
* **acknowledgement_agency** (note, optional): If the agency has acknowledged the training completion, enter the date and time of the acknowledgement.
* **acknowledgement_provider** (note, optional): If the provider has acknowledged the training completion, enter the date and time of the acknowledgement.
* **carer_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the carer being inducted into the care provider agency.
* **carer_role** (text, required): Enter the role of the carer being inducted into the care provider agency.
* **agency** (text, optional): Enter the name of the care agency.
* **training_acknowledgement_agency_issued** (text, required): Enter the date and time the agency issued the training acknowledgement.
* **training_acknowledgement_provider_issued** (text, required): Enter the date and time the provider issued the training acknowledgement.
* **acknowledgement_date** (date, required): Enter the date the acknowledgement was given to the carer.
* **acknowledgement_time** (time, required): Enter the time the acknowledgement was given to the carer.
* **acknowledgement_by** (text, required): Enter the name and title of the person or organization giving the acknowledgement.
* **training_acknowledgement_provider_issued_provider** (text, required): Enter the date and time the provider issued the training acknowledgement.
* **date_completed_provider_issued** (date, required): Enter the date and time the provider completed the training.
