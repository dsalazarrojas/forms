# talent_development_program_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and track training programs for employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and job title.
2. Select the job level you belong to.
3. Choose the type of training you need (Basic, Advanced, or Leadership Development).
4. Select the start and end dates of the training.
5. Choose the frequency of the training (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
6. Enter the name and email of the trainer you would like to have for the training.
7. Enter the location where you would like to have the training.
8. If applicable, select whether you want to be reimbursed for travel expenses.
9. If applicable, select whether the training will be funded.
10. Add any comments about the training you need.
11. Choose who will be submitting the request (Employee, Manager, or Team Lead).
12. Choose who will be approving the request (Employee, Manager, or Team Lead).
13. Enter the approver's email and phone number.
14. Fill in the approval date (if applicable).
15. Select the status of the approval (Approved, Denied, or In Progress).
16. Fill in the approval reason (if applicable).
17. Choose how you acknowledge the approval (Acknowledge, Decline, or Not Provided).
18. Fill in the acknowledgement date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your badge or ID.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of your position in the company.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, not required): Select the type of training you need.
*   * Basic Training*: This type of training is for general knowledge and skills improvement.
  * Advanced Training*: This type of training is for specialized skills development.
  * Leadership Development*: This type of training is for leadership development.
* **Training Proposal** (`training_proposal`, date, required): Enter the date and time you would like to start and end the training.
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like the training to start.
* **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you would like the training to end.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of time required for the training.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like the training to happen.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer you would like to have for the training.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the trainer you would like to have for the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to have the training.
* **Travel Expense Reimbursment** (`travel_expense_reimbursment`, number, optional): If you would like to be reimbursed for travel expenses, enter the amount.
* **Training Funding** (`training_funding`, select_one, optional): If you would like to be funded, select 'True'.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the training you need.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, required): Choose who will be submitting the request (Employee, Manager, or Team Lead).
* **Approver** (`approver`, select_one, required): Choose who will be approving the request (Employee, Manager, or Team Lead).
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, text, required): Enter the approver's email.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, required): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the request was approved.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the approval (Approved, Denied, or In Progress).
* **Approval Reason** (`approval_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the approval status.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Choose how you acknowledge the approval (Acknowledge, Decline, or Not Provided).
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you acknowledge the approval.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your manager or team lead for help.
* If you need any additional assistance, contact the training department.
