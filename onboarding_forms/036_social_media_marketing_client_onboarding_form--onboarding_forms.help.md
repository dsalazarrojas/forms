# Social Media Marketing Client Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help new clients provide necessary information to onboard with your social media marketing efforts. Please fill out this form to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): Please enter your company name, address, or any other general information about yourself or your business.
* **Business Objectives** (`business_objectives`, text, optional): Describe your business goals and objectives, including what you hope to achieve through social media marketing.
* **Social Media Presence** (`social_media_presence`, text, optional): List the social media platforms where you are present (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for future communication.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your business or social media presence.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Identify the user who will be working with you on social media marketing efforts.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, text, optional): Check if the review process is complete or if there's anything to be reviewed.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Please ignore this field and do not fill it out. It's a system-generated field.
