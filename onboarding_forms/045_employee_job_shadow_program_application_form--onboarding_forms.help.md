# Employee Job Shadow Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Job Shadow Program Application Form is used to apply for the job shadow program, which allows employees to observe and learn from experienced employees in various departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate whether you're interested in participating in the job shadow program.
3. Select your preferred work shift and job shadow type.
4. Provide any additional work location or job title information, if necessary.
5. Enter your supervisor's email and phone number, if applicable.
6. Add any notes or comments about your job shadow experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required): This is your personal information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Job Shadow Request** (`job_shadow_request`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you're interested in participating in the job shadow program. Select "Yes" to apply, or "No" to decline.
* **Work Shift Preferences** (`work_shift_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred work shifts, such as day, evening, or weekend shifts.
* **Job Shadow Start Date** (`jobshadowing_startdate`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to start your job shadow experience.
* **Job Shadow End Date** (`jobshadowing_enddate`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to end your job shadow experience.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you're willing to commit to for the job shadow experience.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title you're interested in shadowing, if different from your own role.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the work location you'd like to shadow, if different from your own work location.
* **Work Location (Other)** (`work_location_other`, text, optional): Enter any other work location you'd like to shadow.
* **Job Shadow Type** (`job_shadow_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of job shadow experience you're looking for, such as "hands-on" or "observation".
* **Job Shadow Frequency** (`job_shadow_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your job shadow experience, such as "once a week" or "as needed".
* **Work Shift Start** (`workshift_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your work shift.
* **Work Shift End** (`workshift_end`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your work shift.
* **Job Shadow Frequency (Other)** (`jobshadowing_frequency_other`, text, optional): Enter any other frequency of your job shadow experience.
* **Job Title (Other)** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): Enter any other job title you'd like to shadow.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you work with a team lead.
* **Team Lead (Other)** (`team_lead_other`, text, optional): Enter any other team lead you'd like to shadow.
* **Work Shift Notes** (`workshift_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about your work shifts or job shadow experience.
* **Job Shadow Notes** (`jobshadowing_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about your job shadow experience.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
