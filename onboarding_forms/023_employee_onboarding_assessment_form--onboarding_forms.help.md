# Employee Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data for onboarding assessment of employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the basic employee information such as email and phone number.
2. Provide new hire details including job title, department, hire date, and manager's name.
3. Select the onboarding status.
4. Indicate whether the new hire is on track or not.
5. Choose your job satisfaction level.
6. Provide feedback for new hires and their feedback comments.
7. Indicate if job title change is required.
8. Choose the reason for job performance change.
9. Indicate if job performance change is required.
10. Choose the job performance outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Basic Information**
*  (14, email, email, Optional) : Please fill in the employee's email address.
*  (15, phone, text, Optional) : Please fill in the employee's phone number.
*  (1, new_hire_details, text, Optional) : This is the new hire details section where you can provide any additional information about the new hire.
*  (2, new_hire_details, text, Optional) : This is another field for new hire details.
*  (4, new_hire_feedback, text, Optional) : This field is for providing feedback about the new hire.
*  (5, job_satisfaction, select_one, Optional) : This field asks for your job satisfaction level, choose from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

### 2. New Hire Outcomes
*  (6, new_hire_outcomes, select_multiple, Optional) : This field is to select new hire outcomes. You can choose one or more of New Hire, Not New Hire, or Onboarding Complete.
*  (7, new_hire_outcomes, text, Optional) : This field is for providing additional information about the new hire outcomes.
*  (8, date_of_birth, date, Optional) : Please provide the date of birth of the new hire.

### 3. Onboarding Process
*  (9, onboarding_status, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select the onboarding status, choose from Complete, In Progress, or Not Complete.
*  (10, new_hire_outcomes, text, Optional) : This field is for providing additional information about the new hire outcomes.
*  (11, hire_date, date, Optional) : Please provide the hire date of the new hire.
*  (12, manager_name, text, Optional) : Please fill in the manager's name.

### 4. Job Performance
*  (13, onboarding_status, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select the job performance outcome, choose from On Track, Off Track.
*  (14, job_performance_outcome, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select the job performance reason. 
*  (15, job_satisfaction_change, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select whether job performance change is required, choose from True, False, or Pending.
*  (16, job_performance_outcome, text, Optional) : This field is for providing additional information about the job performance outcome.
*  (17, job_performance_outcome_reason, text, Optional) : This field is for providing feedback comments about the job performance.

### 5. Feedback
*  (18, job_satisfaction_change_reason, text, Optional) : This field is for providing feedback comments.
*  (19, feedback_comments, text, Optional) : This field is for providing any additional feedback comments.
*  (20, job_performance_rating, number, Optional) : This field is for rating the job performance.
*  (21, feedback_suggestions, text, Optional) : This field is for providing any suggestions for improvement.
*  (22, job_performance_change_reason, text, Optional) : This field is for providing additional information about the job performance change reason.
*  (23, feedback_suggestions, text, Optional) : This field is for providing feedback suggestions.
*  (24, job_performance_outcome, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select the job performance outcome, choose from On Track, Off Track.

### 6. Job Title Change
*  (25, job_title_change, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select whether job title change is required, choose from True, False, or Pending.
*  (26, job_performance_outcome, text, Optional) : This field is for providing additional information about the job title change reason.
*  (27, job_title, text, Optional) : This field is for filling in the job title.
*  (28, department, text, Optional) : This field is for providing department name.
*  (29, job_title, text, Optional) : This field is for providing additional information about job title change.
*  (30, job_satisfaction_change, select_one, Optional) : This field is to select job satisfaction change, choose from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
*  (31, job_satisfaction_change_reason, text, Optional) : This field is for providing reason for job satisfaction change.

### 7. Tips

* Please fill in the form carefully and accurately.
* The data will be used for reference and future evaluations.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the HR department for assistance.
