# Agricultural Worker Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from new agricultural workers onboarding to the company, including contact details, medical conditions, and work schedule. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered for records and compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form accurately and completely.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you have completed this form before, please review the form to avoid duplicating information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Worker name** (`worker_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth for records and eligibility checks. This field is not required for onboarding but is necessary for company records.
- **Contact number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide your preferred phone number where you can be reached for work-related matters.
- **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will begin your duties as an agricultural worker.
- **Position or role** (`position`, select_one, required): Select your role within the company, such as Field worker, Machine operator, Supervisor, or Technician.
- **Expected weekly hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter your typical number of hours worked per week.
- **Relevant medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Provide any medical conditions that the employer should be aware of.
- **Training received during onboarding** (`training_received`, text, optional): Enter a short description of induction training received during onboarding.
- **Company policy acknowledgement** (`policy_acknowledgement`, note, required): Read and acknowledge receipt of company safety and conduct policies.
