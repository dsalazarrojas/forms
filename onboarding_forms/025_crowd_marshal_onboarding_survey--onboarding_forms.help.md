# Crowd Marshal Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowd Marshal Onboarding Survey is designed to gather essential information about new users and help us understand their needs. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you complete the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) or a "required" label.
3. Use this guide to understand what each field represents and what we need from you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: This field is a text box where you can provide a brief overview of your event.
* **Event Experience**: Please select the event you are most familiar with. This helps us understand your background and expertise.
* **Event Experience**: Select one event from the list:
	+ Event 1
	+ Event 2
* **Communication Preferences**: Select all the ways you would like to receive updates and notifications for our services. You can choose more than one option:
	+ Email
	+ Mobile Phone
	+ Phone
* **Email**: This is your email address. Please format it as: `yourname@domain.com` (e.g., `john.doe@gmail.com`).
* **Phone**: This is your phone number. Please format it as: `123-456-7890` (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Event ID**: This is the unique identifier for your event. If you are not sure, you can leave it blank.
* **Role**: Please select your role in the event:
	+ Marshal
	+ Leader
	+ Observer
* **Name**: This is your name, which will be used for internal purposes.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or details about your event, please write them here.
* **Time**: This field is not currently used. You can leave it blank.
