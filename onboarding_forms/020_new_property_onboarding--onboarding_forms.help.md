# new_property_onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_property_onboarding form is used to collect basic information about new properties during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with the correct information for the new property.
3. Double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check in info** (`check_in_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional check-in information for the property.
* **Check in** (`check_in`, date, optional): Select the date of the property's check-in.
* **Time in** (`time_in`, time, optional): Enter the time of the property's check-in.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the property's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the property's email address.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the property's name.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your administrator for clarification.
- Double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
