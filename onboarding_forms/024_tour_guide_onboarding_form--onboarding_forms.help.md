# Tour Guide Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tour Guide Onboarding Form is designed to collect basic contact information and other relevant details from tour guides to help them get started with our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com` in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, without any special characters or spaces.
5. Check the "Availability" field to select your preferred time slots for guiding tours.
6. Enter the number of years of experience you have as a tour guide in the "Experience Years" field.
7. Add any additional onboarding details in the "Onboarding Details" field.
8. If you have any notes or comments, please add them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number without any special characters or spaces.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred availability for guiding tours. Options include "Morning", "Afternoon", and "Evening".
* **Experience Years** (`Experience Years`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years of experience you have as a tour guide.
* **Onboarding Details** (`Onboarding Details`, text, required: false): Add any additional onboarding details or information about yourself.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required: false): If you have any notes or comments, please add them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is in the correct format before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* You can always log in to your account to edit your details later.
* If you have any issues while submitting the form, please let us know so we can assist you.
