# Employee Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback from new hires on their onboarding experience. This form will help the organization understand areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the organization.
	* **Department**: Enter the department or team you are part of.
2. Select the onboarding status:
	* **Onboarding Status**: Choose the status of your onboarding process. Select "Active" if the onboarding process is completed, otherwise select "Inactive".
3. Enter manager information:
	* **Manager First Name**: Enter your manager's first name.
	* **Manager Last Name**: Enter your manager's last name.
	* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
	* **Employee Email**: Enter your own email address.
4. Choose your onboarding experience:
	* **Onboarding Process**: Select the quality of the onboarding process. You can choose "None" or "Other" if you'd like to provide more information.
	* **Completed Onboarding Form**: Enter the date you completed the onboarding form.
	* **Onboarding Feedback**: Provide any feedback on your onboarding experience.
	* **Onboarding Experience**: Describe your overall onboarding experience.
	* **Feedback For Improvement**: Share any suggestions or feedback for improving the onboarding process.
	* **Onboarding Process Score**: Rate your onboarding experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
	* **Onboarding Support Score**: Rate your level of support provided during the onboarding process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
	* **Onboarding Documentation Score**: Rate the quality of documentation provided during the onboarding process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
	* **Onboarding Experience Score**: Rate your overall onboarding experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
	* **Onboarding Suggestions**: Provide any additional suggestions or ideas for improving the onboarding process.
