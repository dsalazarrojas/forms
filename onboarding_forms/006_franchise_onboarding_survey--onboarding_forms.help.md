# Franchise Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather essential information from new franchisees to streamline the onboarding process. It's crucial to provide accurate answers to help us understand your background, contact details, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions carefully, as the information you provide will help us tailor our onboarding process to your needs and preferences.
2. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or contact us for guidance.
3. For select fields, choose the option that best suits your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Background Information
* **Background Information** (`select_one`, required/optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have relevant background information, and 'No' otherwise.

### 2. Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the ways you prefer to be contacted, e.g., email, phone, or both.

### 3. Experience
* **Experience** (number, required/optional): Please provide the number of years of relevant experience you have.

### 4. Questions and Feedback
* **Questions and Feedback** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have any questions or concerns, and 'No' otherwise.

### 5. Franchisee Information
* **Franchisee Information** (text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your background and experience as a franchisee.

### 6. Franchisor Information
* **Franchisor Information** (text, required/optional): Please describe your background and experience as a franchisor.

### 7. Onboarding Process
* **Onboarding Process** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like a personalized onboarding process, and 'No' otherwise.

### 8. Training
* **Training** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the training methods you prefer, e.g., text, visual aids, or others.

### 9. Certification
* **Certification** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have any certification related to the franchise opportunity, and 'No' otherwise.
