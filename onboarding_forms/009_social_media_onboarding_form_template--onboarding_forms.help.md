# Social Media Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather basic information about users for social media onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the job position you want to associate with your user account.
2. Enter your company name.
3. Enter your role (e.g., employee, manager, etc.).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Fill in your social media profiles (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, optional): Enter your job position in the company.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role in the company (e.g., employee, manager, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **Twitter** (`twitter`, text, optional): Enter your Twitter profile URL.
* **Instagram** (`instagram`, text, optional): Enter your Instagram profile URL.
* **YouTube** (`youtube`, text, optional): Enter your YouTube profile URL.
* **TikTok** (`tiktok`, text, optional): Enter your TikTok profile URL.
* **Other Social Media** (`other_social_media`, text, optional): Enter any other social media profiles not listed above (e.g., Facebook, Pinterest, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure seamless integration with your social media account.
* If you need help with a specific field, please refer to your company's social media guidelines.
