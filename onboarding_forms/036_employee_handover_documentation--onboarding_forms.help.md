# Employee Handover Documentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Handover Documentation form is designed to streamline the handover process between an employee and their manager. This form ensures that all necessary information is exchanged, and the handover process is completed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields as follows:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being handed over to.
	* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
	* **Role Transition Date**: Enter the date when the role will be handed over.
	* **Previous Role**: Briefly describe the responsibilities and tasks associated with the previous role.
	* **New Role**: Describe the responsibilities and tasks associated with the new role.
	* **Manager Approval Date**: Enter the date when the manager has approved the handover.
	* **Employee Handover Notes**: Add any additional notes from the employee or manager.
	* **Manager Comments**: Add any comments from the manager regarding the handover.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being handed over to.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Role Transition Date** (`role_transition_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the role will be handed over.
* **Previous Role** (`previous_role`, text, optional): Briefly describe the responsibilities and tasks associated with the previous role.
* **New Role** (`new_role`, text, optional): Describe the responsibilities and tasks associated with the new role.
* **Manager Approval Date** (`manager_approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the manager has approved the handover.
* **Employee Handover Notes** (`employee_handover_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes from the employee or manager.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments from the manager regarding the handover.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use a clear and concise format when filling in the fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Use the form for internal purposes only.
