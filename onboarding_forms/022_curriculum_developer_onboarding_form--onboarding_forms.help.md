# Curriculum Developer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Developer Onboarding Form is a form used to onboard new curriculum developers into the organization. This form is meant to collect essential details about new developers for further processing and integration into the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your valid email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Select your role within the team from the dropdown options labeled "Role".
5. Enter your team size in the field labeled "Team Size".
6. Select the date you would like to be onboarded in the field labeled "Onboarding Date".
7. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
8. Enter any additional notes about your onboarding in the field labeled "Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
  - **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
  - **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
  - **Role** (select_one, required): Select your role within the team from the available options:
    - **Developer**
    - **Project Manager**
    - **Team Lead**
  - **Team Size** (number, optional): Enter the number of people you will be working with.
  - **Onboarding Date** (date, optional): Select the date you would like to be onboarded.
  - **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
  - **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your onboarding.
