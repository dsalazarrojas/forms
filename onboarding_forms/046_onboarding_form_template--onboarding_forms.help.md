# Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Form Template is a generic form designed to collect information about a user during the onboarding process. It is meant to be a starting point for creating onboarding forms tailored to specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "User Info" section, where you can provide general information about yourself.
2. Next, move on to the "Account Info" section, where you can enter details about your account or the onboarding process.
3. The "Onboarding Questionnaire" section is where you can provide additional information or questions to be answered during the onboarding process.
4. In the "Onboarding Acceptance" section, select the agreements you have read and accepted.
5. The "Onboarding Review" section allows you to select the reviews that apply to your onboarding process.
6. Finally, enter the date and time of the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Date" and "Onboarding Time" sections, respectively.
7. Optionally, add any notes about the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`User Info`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is meant to collect general information about the user.
* **Account Info** (`Account Info`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is where you can enter details about your account or the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Questionnaire** (`Onboarding Questionnaire`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is for providing additional information or questions to be answered during the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Acceptance** (`Onboarding Acceptance`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the agreements you have read and accepted.
* **Onboarding Review** (`Onboarding Review`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the reviews that apply to your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Date** (`Onboarding Date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Time** (`Onboarding Time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Notes** (`Onboarding Notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Add any notes about the onboarding process.
