# Employee Support Program Launch Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Support Program Launch Registration Form is a tool used to collect employee data for the Employee Support Program. This form helps us understand your job responsibilities, department, and other relevant details to tailor the support provided to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant options from the drop-down menus as needed.
3. Provide accurate and honest answers to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User name** (`user_name`, `text`, `required`): Your full name as it appears on your official documents or ID cards.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, `required`): A brief description of your current role or position within the company.
* **Job description** (`job_description`, `text`, `required`): A short description of your job responsibilities and key tasks.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): A valid email address associated with your company account or a personal email you'd like us to use for communication.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, `required`): The name of your department or organization within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, `required`): The name of your department or team within the company.
* **Job title department** (`job_title_department`, `text`, `required`): The department within the company where you hold your job title.
* **Job level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, `optional`): Your job level within the company. Please select one of the following options:
	+ C-level
	+ Director
	+ Manager
	+ Staff
	+ Intern
* **Other job level** (`job_level_other`, `text`, `optional`): If none of the above job levels apply, please specify your job level here.
* **Job family** (`job_family`, `text`, `required`): The job family or category you belong to within the company.
* **Other job family** (`job_family_other`, `text`, `optional`): If none of the above job families apply, please specify your job family here.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `optional`): The date of your birth.
* **Date of hire** (`date_of_hire`, `date`, `optional`): The date you started working for the company.
* **Time of hire** (`time_of_hire`, `time`, `optional`): The time you started working for the company.
* **Contact number** (`contact_number`, `text`, `optional`): A phone number or other contact information we can use to reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, `optional`): Your work address or location.
* **Job level agree** (`job_level_agree`, `select_one`, `optional`): Your agreement with your current job level. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
	+ Undecided
* **Other job level agree** (`job_level_agree_other`, `text`, `optional`): If you don't agree with any of the above options, please specify your agreement here.
* **Department agree** (`department_agree`, `select_one`, `optional`): Your agreement with your current department. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
	+ Undecided
* **Other department agree** (`department_agree_other`, `text`, `optional`): If you don't agree with any of the above options, please specify your agreement here.
* **Department family** (`department_family`, `text`, `optional`): The department family or category you belong to within the company.
* **Other department family** (`department_family_other`, `text`, `optional`): If none of the above department families apply, please specify your department family here.
* **Job family agree** (`job_family_agree`, `select_one`, `optional`): Your agreement with your current job family. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
	+ Undecided
* **Other job family agree** (`job_family_agree_other`, `text`, `optional`): If you don't agree with any of the above options, please specify your agreement here.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Please select one or more of the following job titles:
	+ Manager
	+ Director
	+ Team Lead
	+ Lead
	+ Associate
	+ Assistant
