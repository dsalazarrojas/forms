# Physical Therapist Onboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Physical Therapist Onboarding Form is designed to gather essential information about new physical therapists onto the system.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to enter accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, required): Enter your valid medical license number.
* **Medical License State** (`medical_license_state`, text, required): Enter the state in which your medical license is issued.
* **Clinic Address** (`clinic_address`, text, optional): Enter your work address if different from your home address.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Clinic, Home, Phone, Email).
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Enter your available work hours in the format hh:mm:ss (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): Enter your work hours in the format hh:mm:ss (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Schedule Type** (`schedule_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the times of the day you are available to work (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, select_multiple, optional): Select the length of your shifts (e.g., 30 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes).
* **Break Length** (`break_length`, select_one, optional): Select the length of your breaks (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes).

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your system administrator for assistance.
* Once you have completed this form, please review and submit it for review and approval.
