# Film Production Assistant Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about new production assistants during their onboarding process. It helps the team to better understand their strengths, availability, and contact details, and to prepare them for their new role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Production Details (Page 1)
* **Production Details** (`production_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your background and experience in film production. This will help the team understand your qualifications and how you can contribute to the team.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help the team contact you efficiently.

### Availability (Page 3)
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Let us know about your availability to work on productions. Select 'Yes' if you are available to work on any production, 'No' if you are not available, or 'Partially' if you are available for specific projects.

### Production Experience (Page 4)
* **Production Experience** (`production_experience`, text, optional): Share your previous experience in film production, including any notable projects you have worked on.

### Previous Projects (Page 5)
* **Previous Projects** (`previous_projects`, text, optional): List your previous projects that you are proud of and would like to share with the team.

### Equipment and Skills (Page 6)
* **Equipment and Skills** (`equipment_skills`, text, optional): Describe your proficiency in any equipment or skills you have that can be used in film production, such as camera operation or lighting.

### Availability Calendar (Page 7)
* **Availability Calendar** (`availability_calendar`, date, optional): If you are not available for a specific date, please mark it on the calendar to help the team plan productions accordingly.

### Emergency Contact (Page 8)
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can be reached on your behalf.

### Acknowledgments (Page 9)
* **Acknowledgments** (`acknowledgments`, text, optional): Acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms of service and any other relevant policies of the company.

# Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that you get off to a good start with the team.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification from the team.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that you have included all the necessary information.
