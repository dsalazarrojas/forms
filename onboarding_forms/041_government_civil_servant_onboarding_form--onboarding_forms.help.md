# Government Civil Servant Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new hires to ensure a smooth onboarding process. It gathers details about the new employee's job title, department, and status, as well as other relevant information such as job type, departmental unit, reporting manager, and employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and fill in all the required information accurately.
* Select the appropriate options from the drop-down menus for job title, department, job status, job type, employment status, onboarding status, and manager confirmation.
* Enter any additional details or comments in the text fields, such as new hire details, departmental unit, onboarding comments, and new hire documents.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and thoroughly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional details about the new hire.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select the job title of the new hire from the drop-down menu. This could be Director, Manager, or Coordinator.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional details about the new hire's job description.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department of the new hire from the drop-down menu. This could be HR, Finance, or IT.
* **New Hire Details 1** (`new_hire_details_1`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional details about the new hire.
* **New Hire Details 2** (`new_hire_details_2`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional details about the new hire.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`): Select the status of the new hire's job from the drop-down menu. This could be Active, Inactive, or Leave of Absence.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Select the type of job from the drop-down menu. This could be Full Time, Part Time, or Contract.
* **Departmental Unit** (`departmental_unit`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional details about the departmental unit.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`): Select the reporting manager from the drop-down menu. This could be Manager, Team Lead, or Supervisor.
* **Job Code** (`job_code`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional details about the job code.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`): Enter the date of hire for the new employee.
* **Salary** (`salary`): Enter the salary of the new hire. This field is for numerical input.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select the employment status from the drop-down menu. This could be Full Time, Part Time, or Contract.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`): Select the onboarding status from the drop-down menu. This could be Pending, In Progress, or Complete.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`): Select the manager confirmation from the drop-down menu. This could be Pending, Complete, or No Action.
* **Onboarding Comments** (`onboarding_comments`): This field is a free-text field where you can enter any additional comments about the onboarding process.
* **New Hire Documents** (`new_hire_documents`): This field is a free-text field where you can upload or enter any additional documents about the new hire.
* **Department Head Confirmation** (`department_head_confirmation`): Select the department head confirmation from the drop-down menu. This could be Pending, Complete, or No Action.
