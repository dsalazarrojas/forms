# it_employee_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects onboarding information from new employees to facilitate a smooth transition into our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
2. Scroll through the form and fill in the details as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address (`setup_email_address`, `email`, false):** Enter your company email address to enable internal communication and access to company resources.
* **Onboarding Tasks Status (`onboarding_tasks_status`, `select_multiple`, false):** Choose whether you've started or completed onboarding tasks. Select both "Active" and "Inactive" to indicate tasks are in progress or already finished.
* **Completed Onboarding Tasks (`completed_onboarding_tasks`, `note`, false):** Describe any onboarding tasks you've already completed. This is a text box where you can provide additional details about your onboarding experience.
