# website_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **website_onboarding_form** is designed to collect basic contact information and other essential details from new customers to facilitate their onboarding process on our website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form with your customer information**: Enter your details in the designated fields.
2. **Verify your email address**: Enter a valid email address that you own.
3. **Add your phone number (optional)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
4. **Select a date (optional)**: If necessary, select a date for any scheduled activities or appointments.
5. **Provide a note (optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Text): Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Company Info** (Text): Enter your company's name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Website Info** (Text): Enter the name and address of your website.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address that you own and check for any updates about your onboarding process.
* **Phone** (Text): If you have a phone number, enter it for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Date): If necessary, select a date for any scheduled activities or appointments.
* **Note** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.
