# customer_experience_specialist_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer experience specialist onboarding form is a comprehensive tool designed to capture essential information about new hires to support their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company and job title details.
2. Provide your contact information (email and phone).
3. Select your training requirements and preferences.
4. Enter your work location and schedule.
5. Write a brief job description.
6. Choose your onboarding status and dates.
7. Identify your onboarding manager and note any comments.
8. Provide feedback and next steps.
9. Review and verify the form details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (Text): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Training Requirements** (Select Multiple): Select your training requirements from the list.
*   * Basic Sales Training
	+ Basic Sales Training: Select this option if you need the basic sales training.
	+ Intermediate Sales Training: Select this option if you need intermediate-level sales training.
	+ Advanced Sales Training: Select this option if you need advanced-level sales training.
* **Training Preferences** (Select One): Choose how you'd like to receive training (In-person, Online, or Video Conference).
* **Work Location** (Text): Enter your work location.
* **Work Hours** (Time): Enter your work schedule.
* **Job Description** (Text): Write a brief job description.
* **Work Start Date** (Date): Enter your work start date.
* **Work Start Time** (Time): Enter your work start time.
* **Onboarding Status** (Select One): Choose your onboarding status (New Hire, In Onboarding, or Completed).
* **Completed Onboarding Date** (Date): Enter your completed onboarding date if you've already onboarded.
* **Onboarding Manager** (Text): Identify your onboarding manager.
* **Onboarding Notes** (Note): Add any onboarding notes.
* **Training Status** (Select Multiple): Select your training status from the list.
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
* **Feedback** (Text): Provide feedback on your onboarding experience.
* **Onboarding Comments** (Text): Add any onboarding comments.
* **Next Steps** (Text): Enter any next steps for the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Reviewed By** (Select Multiple): Choose who reviewed your onboarding (Onboarding Manager, Department Manager, or HR Manager).
* **Onboarding Reviewed Date** (Date): Enter the date reviewed.
* **Training Reviewed By** (Text): Choose who reviewed your training.
* **Training Reviewed Date** (Date): Enter the date reviewed.
* **Onboarding Reviewed Status** (Select One): Choose the review status (Not Reviewed, Reviewed, or Completed).
* **Onboarding Closed** (Select One): Choose whether your onboarding is closed (Not Closed, Closed, or Completed).

Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
