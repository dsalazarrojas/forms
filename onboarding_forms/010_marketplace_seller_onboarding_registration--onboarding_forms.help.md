# Marketplace Seller Onboarding Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new sellers to register their account on the marketplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Details page with your name, address, and other relevant information.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
3. Enter your product details, including product name and description.
4. Select your product categories.
5. Provide your consent to our seller terms and conditions.
6. Enter your shipping address.
7. Select your product categories.
8. Enter your store name and logo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details (1)** (`text`, required: false): This is the main registration information page where you can enter your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (2)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Product Details (3)** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your product or service.
* **Product Categories (4)** (`select_one`, required: true): Select 'Yes' if you have products that require a shipping address.
* **Seller Details (5)** (`text`, required: false): This is a field to provide additional information about yourself as the seller.
* **Consent (6)** (`text`, required: false): Read and agree to our seller terms and conditions.
* **Shipping Address (7)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address if applicable.
* **Product Categories (8)** (`select_multiple`, required: true): Select all relevant product categories for your products.
* **Store Name (9)** (`text`, required: true): Enter a unique and memorable name for your store.
* **Store Logo (10)** (`text`, required: true): Upload your store logo to represent your brand.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
