# Nurse Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Nurse Onboarding Form is designed to collect information from new nurses, ensuring they understand their role, responsibilities, and expectations within the organization. This form helps standardize the onboarding process, verifies credentials, and conducts background checks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your new nurse details on page 1.
2. List your relevant certifications on page 2.
3. Select your onboarding acknowledgement on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Nurse Details** (`new_nurse_details`, text, not required): Please fill in your new nurse details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Credentials** (`credentials`, text, not required): You can list any relevant medical certifications you hold, such as RN, LPN, or CNA.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant certifications you hold, such as Certified Registered Nurse (RN), Certified Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN), or Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA).
* **Onboarding Acknowledgement** (`onboarding_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the statements that you understand about the onboarding process, including the duration and the importance of completing all sections. You may choose "Other" and add a custom explanation.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the onboarding process.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please ask your HR representative for clarification.
- This form is intended to be completed by new nurses, not existing staff members. If you are an existing staff member, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
