# pva_onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from new users during their onboarding process. It captures user details, contact information, and onboarding status, which is used to ensure proper integration into the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
3.  Enter your information correctly in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required): Enter your personal details such as name and other relevant information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used as your login identifier.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Role** (`user_role`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your role within the system, which can be admin, moderator, or user.
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, `date`, required): Enter the date you joined the system.
* **Last Login** (`last_login`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your last login, if applicable.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, optional): Enter your password, if required.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required): Re-enter your password for verification.
* **Accept Terms** (`accept_terms`, `note`, required): Read and accept the terms and conditions of the system.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your onboarding status as completed or in progress.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Form** (`on_prophets`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the onboarding form, if applicable.
* **Onboarding Comments** (`onboarding_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your onboarding experience.
* **User Info Comments** (`user_info_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your user information.
* **Role Comments** (`role_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your role in the system.
* **Onboarding Status Comments** (`onboarding_status_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your onboarding status.
* **Password Comments** (`password_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about your password.
* **Accept Terms Comments** (`accept_terms_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about accepting the terms and conditions.

**Tips**

* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant team for support.
