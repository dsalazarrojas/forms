# onboarding_signature_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new employees during the onboarding process. It helps ensure that all new employees are aware of and understand the company's policies and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and options presented.
2. Answer each question according to your knowledge and understanding.
3. Provide accurate and complete information in all required fields.
4. Sign the "Signature" field to confirm that you have read and understand the policies and guidelines.
5. Once completed, submit the form to proceed with the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter the date you were hired, as indicated in your employment documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title as provided by HR.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name as provided by HR.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to confirm you have read and understand the Terms and Conditions.
* **Anti-bullying** (`anti_bullying`, select_multiple, required): Select "I understand" to confirm you have read and understand the Anti-bullying policy.
* **EEO** (`eeo`, select_multiple, required): Select "I understand" to confirm you have read and understand the EEO policy.
* **Accessibility** (`accessibility`, select_multiple, required): Select "I understand" to confirm you have read and understand the Accessibility policy.
* **Whistleblower** (`whistleblower`, select_multiple, required): Select "I understand" to confirm you have read and understand the Whistleblower policy.
* **Other Terms** (`other_terms`, select_multiple, required): Select "I understand" to confirm you have read and understand any other relevant policies.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Provide your signature to confirm that you have read and understand all policies and guidelines.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address as provided by HR.
