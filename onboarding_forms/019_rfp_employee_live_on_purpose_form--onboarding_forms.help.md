# rfp_employee_live_on_purpose_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect basic information from new employees during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling out the first page, which asks for your Onboarding Form information. This will help us to get started with your onboarding process.
2. On the second page, you will be asked to provide your Employee Name and Employee Email. Please make sure to enter your full name and a valid email address.
3. On the third page, you will be asked to select your Department. This will help us to assign you to the correct team and ensure that you receive the right information and updates.
4. On the fourth page, you will be asked to provide your Manager Name. Please make sure to enter your manager's name as it appears on their business card or email signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your reference and will not be used for any other purpose.
* **Employee Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents and business cards.
* **Employee Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter a valid email address that you use for work-related communications.
* **Department** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your department from the list. This will help us to assign you to the correct team and ensure that you receive the right information and updates.
* **Manager Name** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your manager's name as it appears on their business card or email signature.

## Tips

* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form, as it will be used to create your employee profile.
* If you have any issues or questions, please reach out to your HR representative or manager for assistance.
* If you are having trouble accessing this form, please contact our IT department for support.
