# Employee Getting To Know You Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's personal and professional details, including general employee details, work preferences, and emergency contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as they appear. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter your name.
* **Work Preferences** (`work_preferences`, text, required): This field is used to enter your work preferences, such as your work location, language, time zone, and work hours.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): This field is used to enter your emergency contacts, including their names, phone numbers, relationship, and email addresses.
* **Name** (`emergency_contacts_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Phone Number** (`emergency_contacts_phone_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contacts_relationship`, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. "yes" or "no").
* **Address** (`emergency_contacts_address`, text, required): This field is used to enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to indicate the location of where you work.
* **Team Leads** (`team_leads`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to indicate if you are the team lead.
* **Emergency Contact 1 - Name** (`emergency_contact_1_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of your first emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 1 - Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_1_phone_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter the phone number of your first emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 1 - Relationship** (`emergency_contact_1_relationship`, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate the relationship between you and your first emergency contact (e.g. "yes" or "no").
* **Emergency Contact 1 - Email** (`emergency_contact_1_email`, email, required): This field is used to enter the email address of your first emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 - Name** (`emergency_contact_2_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 - Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_2_phone_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter the phone number of your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 - Relationship** (`emergency_contact_2_relationship`, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate the relationship between you and your second emergency contact (e.g. "yes" or "no").
* **Emergency Contact 2 - Email** (`emergency_contact_2_email`, email, required): This field is used to enter the email address of your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 3 - Name** (`emergency_contact_3_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of your third emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 3 - Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_3_phone_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter the phone number of your third emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 3 - Relationship** (`emergency_contact_3_relationship`, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate the relationship between you and your third emergency contact (e.g. "yes" or "no").
* **Emergency Contact 3 - Email** (`emergency_contact_3_email`, email, required): This field is used to enter the email address of your third emergency contact.
* **Occupation** (`emergency_contacts_occupation`, text, required): This field is used to enter the occupation of your emergency contact.
## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
* If you have any additional emergency contacts that are not listed, please list them in the comments section at the end of the form.
