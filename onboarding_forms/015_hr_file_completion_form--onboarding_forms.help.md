# hr_file_completion_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR File Completion Form is a document used to track an employee's personnel file completion status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Gather any relevant documents and information required for the form.
2. Fill out the "Employee details" section with the employee's details.
3. In the "Emergency Contacts" section, list the employee's emergency contacts.
4. In the "Documents" section, upload any relevant documents required for the form.
5. In the "Policy Acknowledgments" section, acknowledge any policies that apply to the employee.
6. Sign the form with the employee's signature and the manager's signature.
7. Fill out the "Date Completed" and "Date Reviewed" fields.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Manager Comments" and "Manager Notes" sections.
9. Upload any additional files required in the "Uploaded Files" section.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee details** (Employee details, text, required): Enter the employee's details, such as their name, address, and contact information.
* **Emergency Contacts** (emergency_contacts, text, required): List the employee's emergency contacts, including their names and contact information.
* **Documents** (documents, text, required): Upload any relevant documents required for the form, such as ID documents, contracts, etc.
* **Policy Acknowledgments** (policy_acknowledgments, text, required): Acknowledge any policies that apply to the employee.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, text, required): Sign the form with the employee's signature.
* **Manager Signature** (manager_signature, text, required): Sign the form with the manager's signature.
* **Date Completed** (date_completed, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Date Completed By** (date_completed_by, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed by.
* **HR File Completion Form Uploads** (hr_file_completion_form_uploads, text, required): Upload any files required for the form.
* **Uploads** (uploads, text, required): Upload any additional files required.
* **Upload 1** (uploads_1, text, required): Upload an additional file.
* **Upload 2** (uploads_2, text, required): Upload another additional file.
* **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, note, required): Add any comments or notes from the manager.
* **Employee Details 1** (employee_details_1, text, required): Enter additional employee details.
* **Employee Details 2** (employee_details_2, text, required): Enter more employee details.
* **Manager Notes** (manager_notes, note, required): Add any notes from the manager.
* **Date Reviewed** (date_reviewed, date, required): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Date Reviewed By** (date_reviewed_by, date, required): Enter the date the form was reviewed by.
* **Manager Signature 2** (manager_signature_2, text, required): Sign the form with the manager's signature again.
* **Notes** (notes, text, required): Add any additional notes.
* **Date Completed Date** (date_completed_date, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Date Completed By** (date_completed_date_by, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed by.
* **File Completion Form Date** (file_completion_form_date, date, required): Enter the date the form was created.
* **File Completion Form Date By** (file_completion_form_date_by, date, required): Enter the date the form was created by.
* **Uploaded Files** (uploaded_files, text, required): Upload any additional files.
* **Uploaded File** (uploaded_file, text, required): Upload another file.
