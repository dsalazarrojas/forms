# Plant Onboarding Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide employees through the onboarding process for new plant equipment or processes, ensuring that all necessary steps and checks are completed before starting work on the new equipment or process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Employee Details** section with your personal information.
2. Provide the contact details for your **Emergency Contacts**.
3. Enter the relevant **Plant Details** about the new equipment or process.
4. Update your **Onboarding Status** to reflect your current progress.
5. Provide your **Manager Details** and **Manager Confirmation** for verification purposes.
6. Confirm your **Employee Confirmation** to complete the onboarding process.
7. Select **Onboarding Complete** to indicate that you have finished the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details (Employee Details)**: Please enter your name, email, and any other required information as specified.
* **Emergency Contacts (Emergency Contacts)**: Enter the name, phone number, and email of at least one emergency contact person.
* **Plant Details (Plant Details)**: Provide a brief description of the new plant equipment or process, including its location and relevant details.
* **Onboarding Status (Onboarding Status)**: Select one of the following options to reflect your current onboarding status: "Not Started", "In Progress", or "Complete".
* **Manager Details (Manager Details)**: Enter the name, title, and email of your direct manager.
* **Manager Confirmation (Manager Confirmation)**: Confirm that your manager has approved your onboarding.
* **Employee Confirmation (Employee Confirmation)**: Confirm that you have completed the onboarding process and are ready to start work on the new equipment or process.
* **Onboarding Complete (Onboarding Complete)**: Select "True" to confirm that you have finished the onboarding process, or "False" if there are still outstanding tasks or issues.
