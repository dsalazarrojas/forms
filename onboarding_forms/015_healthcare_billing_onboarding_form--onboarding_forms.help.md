# Healthcare Billing Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects and manages billing information for healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review and understand the form's purpose and the information it collects.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Double-check your entries for correctness and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Clinic Info
* Clinic Info: Enter the name and details of the clinic or medical provider.
### Patient Info
* Patient Info: Enter the patient's name, address, and relevant contact information.
### Insurance Info
* Insurance Info: Enter the patient's insurance information, if applicable.
### Billing Details
* Billing Details: Provide accurate billing information for the healthcare services rendered.
* Required fields: Insurance ID, Billing Amount.
### Confirm Insurance
* Confirm Insurance: Confirm the patient's insurance status (Active, InActive, or Pending).
### Follow Up
* Follow Up: Enter follow-up contact details for the patient.
* Optional field, but recommended for tracking and communication.

* Date of Service: Enter the date the healthcare services were rendered.
* Required field for accurate billing and payment processing.
### Time of Service
* Time of Service: Enter the time the healthcare services were rendered.
* Optional, but recommended for accurate billing and payment processing.
### Insurance ID
* Insurance ID: Enter the patient's insurance ID number.
* Required field for accurate insurance billing and payment processing.

* Billing Amount: Enter the total amount due for the healthcare services.
* Required field for accurate billing and payment processing.
