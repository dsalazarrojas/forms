# Business Analyst Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Analyst Onboarding Form is a collection of questions and fields that gather relevant information about your personal and professional background, experience, and requirements for a successful onboarding process in your new role as a business analyst.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each field as accurately and completely as possible. If you're unsure about any question, please provide your best guess or leave a note asking for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information**: Your personal details, which will be kept confidential and used for internal purposes only.
  * **Full Name** (id: 2, required): Your full legal name as per official identification.
  * **Preferred Name** (id: 3, optional): The name you would like to be known as within the organization.
* **Employee Information**:
  * **Employee ID** (id: 4, required): Your employee identification number, which will be used for internal purposes.
  * **Job Title** (id: 10, required): Your official title in the company.
  * **Department** (id: 11, required): Your department, which will help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Work Details**:
  * **Email Address** (id: 5, required): Your work email address, which will be used for official communication.
  * **Phone Number** (id: 6, required): Your contact number, which will be used for internal communication.
  * **Address** (id: 7, required): Your mailing address, which will be used for official documents and mail.
  * **Start Date** (id: 8, required): Your first day of employment.
* **Emergency Contact Information**:
  * **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 17, required): Full name of your emergency contact person.
  * **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 18, required): Relationship to you (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
  * **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 19, required): Contact phone number of your emergency contact.
* **IT Setup**:
  * **Laptop Preference** (id: 22, required): Your preferred operating system (Windows, macOS, etc.).
  * **Software Needs** (id: 23, required): List the software you require for your work, separated by commas (e.g., Microsoft Office, Visio, etc.).
  * **Access Requirements** (id: 24, optional): Systems you need access to, if any.
* **Onboarding and Training**:
  * **Training and Onboarding** (id: 25, optional): Your onboarding plan and training interests.
  * **Prior Business Analysis Experience** (id: 26, required): Years of experience in business analysis.
  * **Certifications** (id: 27, optional): Any business analyst certifications you hold.
  * **Training Interests** (id: 28, required): What training you're interested in.
  * **Mentorship Interest** (id: 29, required): Would you like to have a mentor?
  * **First 90-Day Goals** (id: 30, optional): What you hope to accomplish in your first 90 days.
* **Additional Information**:
  * **Additional Comments** (id: 31, optional): Any other information you'd like to share.
