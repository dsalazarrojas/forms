# Community Housing Initiative Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for onboarding participants in the Community Housing Initiative. It helps ensure that participants have the necessary knowledge and resources to navigate the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
2.  Enter your participant name in the "Participant name" field.
3.  Select your role in the initiative from the options provided.
4.  Enter your start date in the "Start date" field.
5.  If applicable, provide your emergency contact name and phone number.
6.  Check the training needs that apply to you, selecting multiple options as needed.
7.  Indicate if you have submitted any documentation (ID proof, income proof, lease agreement, or other).
8.  Check if any access credentials are needed for participation.
9.  If necessary, provide any special accommodations you may require.
10.  Confirm that you have read and acknowledged the program guidelines by selecting "I acknowledge."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`Participant name`, required): Enter your full name for onboarding records.
* **Role in initiative** (`Role in initiative`, required): Select your role in the initiative from the options: Tenant, Volunteer, Staff, or Partner.
* **Start date** (`Start date`, required): Enter the date you start participating in the initiative.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`Emergency contact name and phone`, optional): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Training needs** (`Training needs`, optional): Select the training topics you require, selecting multiple options as needed.
* **Documentation provided** (`Documentation provided`, optional): Indicate if you have submitted any documentation during onboarding.
* **Access credentials needed** (`Access credentials needed`, optional): Check if you need any access credentials for participation.
* **Special accommodations** (`Special accommodations`, optional): Provide any accessibility or support needs you may have.
* **Acknowledgements and agreements** (`Acknowledgements and agreements`, required): Confirm that you have read and acknowledged the program guidelines by selecting "I acknowledge."
