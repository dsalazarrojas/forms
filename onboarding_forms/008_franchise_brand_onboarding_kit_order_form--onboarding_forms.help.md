# Franchise Brand Onboarding Kit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by new franchisees to provide necessary information for the onboarding process. It collects contact and contact information to ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill in the Company Name field with your franchise's legal name.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Provide your business address in the Address field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): This is the legal name of your franchise business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This is your company email address, where you can be contacted for further information and updates.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the business address for your franchise.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number for your business.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and correctly.
* Double-check your information for spelling and format errors before submission.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact your onboarding representative for assistance.
