# Daycare Caregiver Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help daycare centers onboard new caregivers with their details, availability, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Please provide your basic information (step 1), select your available days of the week for work (step 2), acknowledge our required information (step 3), and provide your emergency contact details (step 4 and 5). 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Step 1: New Caregiver Details**
* **New Caregiver Details** (`title`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name and any additional information about yourself as a new caregiver.
### **Step 2: Availability**
* **Availability** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the days of the week you are available to work. Please note that this is a required field if you want to work on certain days.
* **Hint**: We will use this information to schedule your shifts and plan for our daycare center's operations.
### **Step 3: Required Acknowledgments**
* **Required Acknowledgments** (`note`, required: false): This is a note field for you to acknowledge and read our required information. Please take a moment to review our policies and procedures.
### **Step 4: Emergency Contact**
* **Emergency Contact** (`text`, required: false): Provide your primary emergency contact's name and phone number.
### **Step 5: Emergency Contact 2**
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`text`, required: false): Provide your secondary emergency contact's name and phone number. 

## Tips
* Please ensure your availability is accurate, as we will use this information to schedule your shifts.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your administrator for clarification.
* This form is an important part of our onboarding process, so please take the time to fill it out carefully and thoroughly.
