# Distributed Team Onboarding Automation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributed Team Onboarding Automation Checklist is designed to collect essential information about team leads and new team members, automating some of the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting to ensure you have filled out all required and optional fields.
3. Use the onboarding date to track when new team members have completed the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Lead Details** (`team_lead_details`, text, required: false): Provide your team lead's name, title, and any other relevant details.
* **New Team Member Details** (`new_team_member_details`, text, required: false): Enter the new team member's name, title, and any other relevant details.
* **Onboarding Setup** (`onboarding_setup`, text, required: false): Describe your onboarding requirements and process.
* **Access and Equipment** (`access_and_equipment`, text, required: false): List the necessary access and equipment for new team members.
* **First Week Readiness** (`first_week_readiness`, text, required: false): Check off what new team members will be ready to do during their first week.
* **Remote Work Setup** (`remote_work_setup`, text, required: false): Outline your team's remote work setup, including communication channels and expectations.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, text, required: false): Specify your preferred communication methods and protocols.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact information for your team, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Onboarding Comments** (`onboarding_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the onboarding process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact and communication.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your team lead's phone number for contact and communication.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, required: false): Enter your team lead's email address for contact and communication.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of onboarding for new team members.
