# New Model Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new model being onboarded into the system. It is a crucial step in the onboarding process, ensuring that all necessary details are captured to facilitate a smooth integration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about the new model by filling in the **New Model Info** field.
2. Enter the **Name** of the new model.
3. Enter the **Email** address associated with the new model.
4. Enter the **Phone** number of the new model.
5. For the **Date Of Birth** field, select the **Month** from the dropdown list and enter the **Year** in the field provided.
6. In the **Date Of Birth Time** field, select the **Time** of day.
7. In the **Select Multiple** field, choose one or more options from the list.
8. Add any additional **Note** about the new model.
9. Confirm the **Email** and **Phone** information in the respective fields.
10. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Model Info**: Enter a brief description of the new model.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the new model.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the new model.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the new model.
* **Date Of Birth**: Select the month of birth from the dropdown list and enter the year in the field provided.
* **Date Of Birth Time**: Select the time of day.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more options from the list.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about the new model.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter the email address to confirm its accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone**: Re-enter the phone number to confirm its accuracy.
* **Submit**: Click to complete the onboarding process.
* **Date of Birth Time**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Time**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Time of Day**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Date**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Select Multiple**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Select Multiple 2**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Date and Time**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Time Of Birth**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Date Time Of Birth**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Date and Time 2**: Not used in the current implementation.
* **Date and Time 3**: Not used in the current implementation.

Please note that some fields are not used in the current implementation, and you can ignore them.
