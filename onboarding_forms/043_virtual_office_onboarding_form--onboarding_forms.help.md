# Virtual Office Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from new employees to facilitate their integration into the virtual office setting. It is intended to make the onboarding process more efficient for both the new team members and the administrators managing the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully before providing your answers.
2. Complete each section accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your HR representative or onboarding contact for guidance.
4. Submit the form after you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Purchase Information**: This section is used to gather information about your company's purchase history and any relevant details about your company's purchase process.
	+ This field is optional but recommended for new employees to provide their company's purchase history.
* **Employee Information**: This section is used to collect your personal details as an employee.
	+ Please provide your full name, job title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Office Space Information**: This section is used to collect information about your office space and any specific needs you may have.
	+ Please provide details about your preferred work schedule and any specific requirements for your office space.
* **Onboarding Information**: This section is used to gather information about your onboarding experience and any questions or concerns you may have.
	+ Please provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the onboarding process.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or onboarding contact.
* This form is intended to be a helpful resource for new employees and administrators. If you have any suggestions for improvements, please let us know!
