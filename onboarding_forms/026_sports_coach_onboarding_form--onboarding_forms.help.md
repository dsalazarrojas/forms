# Sports Coach Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a coach profile and align the coach with our organization's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the "Coach Details" section with your personal details.
2. List your coaching qualifications in the "Coach Qualifications" section. This will help us understand your experience and expertise in the coaching field.
3. Specify your availability in the "Coach Availability" section. This will help us schedule training sessions and matches.
4. Add an emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact" section in case we need to reach out to you urgently.
5. If you have any additional information to share about yourself, please use the "Additional Info" section.
6. Review and confirm your agreement with our terms and conditions in the "Coach Agreement" section.
7. Select your school/league/club affiliation in the "School/League/Club" section.
8. Repeat steps 1-7 for the second coach in case there are multiple coaches on your team.
9. Make sure to review all sections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Details**: Enter your full name, address, phone number, and email in this section. This is the first part of creating your coach profile.
* **Coach Qualifications**: List your relevant qualifications, certifications, and experience in the coaching field. This will help us understand your background and expertise.
* **Coach Availability**: Specify your availability for training sessions and matches. This will help us schedule your sessions and ensure you can attend them.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a person who can reach out to you in case of an emergency.
* **Additional Info**: Share any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your coaching.
* **Coach Agreement**: Review and confirm your agreement with our terms and conditions. This is a required field.
* **School/League/Club**: Select your school/league/club affiliation from the options provided. This will help us understand your affiliation and ensure you are aligned with our organization.
* **Coach Reference**: Enter the name and contact information of a reference who can vouch for your coaching experience and skills.

## Tips
