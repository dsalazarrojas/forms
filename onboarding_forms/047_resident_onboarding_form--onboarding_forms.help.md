# Resident Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from new residents to facilitate a smooth transition into the apartment complex.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all required documents, such as government-issued ID and lease agreement, before starting the form.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the following steps:
    - Enter your first and last name.
    - Enter your valid email address.
    - Enter your phone number.
    - Enter your date of birth.
    - Select your relationship to the resident (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, Child, or Other).
    - Enter the address where you currently reside.
    - Select the apartment complex where you will be living (e.g., 1001, 1002, 1003, 1004, 1005).
    - Enter the date and time you will be moving into the apartment.
    - Enter the amount of rent you will be paying.
    - Select the duration of your lease (e.g., 6 months, 12 months, 24 months).
    - Enter the start and end dates of your lease.
    - Select the payment method you will be using (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit Card).
    - Enter any additional notes or comments about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
  - Example: John
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
  - Example: Doe
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
  - Example: resident@example.com
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
  - Example: 123-456-7890
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
  - Example: 01/01/1990
- **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
  - Example: Jane Doe
- **Relationship to Resident** (Relationship to Resident, select_one, required): Select the relationship between you and the resident.
  - Options: Mother, Father, Spouse, Child, Other
- **Resident Address** (Resident Address, text, required): Enter the address where you currently reside.
  - Example: 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345
- **Unit Number** (Unit Number, number, optional): Enter the unit number of the apartment you will be living in.
  - Example: 101
- **Apartment Complex Id** (Apartment Complex Id, select_multiple, required): Select the apartment complex where you will be living.
  - Options: 1001, 1002, 1003, 1004, 1005
- **Move in Date** (Move in Date, date, required): Enter the date you will be moving into the apartment.
  - Example: 01/01/2024
- **Move in Time** (Move in Time, time, required): Enter the time you will be moving into the apartment.
  - Example: 12:00 PM
- **Rent** (Rent, number, required): Enter the amount of rent you will be paying.
  - Example: 1000
- **Lease Duration** (Lease Duration, select_one, required): Select the duration of your lease.
  - Options: 6 months, 12 months, 24 months
- **Lease Start Date** (Lease Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of your lease.
  - Example: 01/01/2024
- **Lease End Date** (Lease End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of your lease.
  - Example: 06/30/2024
- **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select the payment method you will be using.
  - Options: Cash, Check, Credit Card
- **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your stay.
  - Example: "I will be moving in with my cat, Fluffy."
