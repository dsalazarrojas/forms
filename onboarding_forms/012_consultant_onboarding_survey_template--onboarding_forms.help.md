# Consultant Onboarding Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive survey designed to gather information about new consultants, enabling our onboarding process to be tailored to their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including first and last names, primary email address, and contact phone number.
2. Provide your start date, position title, department, and supervisor's name.
3. Specify your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you.
4. Choose your primary work location and employment type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
5. List your areas of expertise and select your level of comfort with technology and software tools.
6. Share your years of consulting experience, professional certifications, education background, and any additional certifications you hold.
7. Specify any training needs or areas you'd like to see further development on.
8. Describe your preferred work setup, including your ideal collaboration style.
9. Outline your career aspirations and goals.
10. Provide feedback on how we can improve the onboarding experience for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`consultant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`consultant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`consultant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`consultant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter your official start date.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or role.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team assignment.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Office Location** (`office_location`, select_one, required): Select your primary work location from the options provided.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select your employment classification from the options provided.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`areas_of_expertise`, select_multiple, required): Choose your primary areas of expertise from the provided options.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years of your consulting experience.
* **Certifications Held** (`certifications_held`, text, optional): List any professional certifications you hold (optional).
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required): Enter your highest degree and institution.
* **Technology Proficiency** (`technology_proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your comfort level with technology.
* **Software and Tools Proficiency** (`software_skills`, text, optional): List software tools you are proficient in.
* **Training or Development Needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Specify any training needs or areas you'd like to see further development on.
* **Work Preferences** (`work_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your ideal work setup.
* **Team Collaboration Style** (`team_collaboration_style`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred collaboration style.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, optional): Outline your professional aspirations and goals.
* **Onboarding Experience Feedback** (`onboarding_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on how we can improve onboarding for you.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Share any additional information about yourself.

Tips:

* Please be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* You can always reach out to us if you need help or guidance throughout the onboarding process.
* We appreciate your feedback and any suggestions for improvement.
* This form is designed to help us tailor the onboarding process to your specific needs; please don't hesitate to share any information you think will be helpful.
