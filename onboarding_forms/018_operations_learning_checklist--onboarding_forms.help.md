# Operations Learning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from new employees about their onboarding experience, including team composition, supervisor feedback, progress status, training program, training confidence, and training progress. The information collected will help management evaluate the effectiveness of the onboarding process and make data-driven decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Click on the "Onboarding Form" page.
2.  Answer the questions according to your onboarding experience.
3.  Select the relevant options for "Team Members", "Progress Status", and "Training Program" questions.
4.  Enter your answers in the "Supervisor Feedback", "Training Confidence", and "Training Progress" fields.
5.  Choose a date for "Form Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Onboarding Form** (`onboarding_form`, text, optional): This field is where you provide a brief description of your onboarding experience.
*  **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the team members you are working with during your onboarding.
*  **Supervisor Feedback** (`supervisor_feedback`, text, optional): Share your feedback about your supervisor's guidance and support during onboarding.
*  **Progress Status** (`progress_status`, select_one, optional): Choose how you feel about your current progress in the onboarding process.
*  **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, optional): Select the training program you are currently following.
*  **Training Confidence** (`training_confidence`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in the training program.
*  **Training Progress** (`training_progress`, number, optional): Enter the current progress level in the training program.
*  **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, optional): Choose the date when you completed this form.

## Tips
*  Please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately to help us understand your onboarding experience.
*  You can skip any field if you don't feel it's relevant to your situation.
*  If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
