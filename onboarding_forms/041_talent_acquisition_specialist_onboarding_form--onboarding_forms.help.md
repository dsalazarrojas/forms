# Talent Acquisition Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new hire to facilitate their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. If there is an existing record for this new hire, use that information to fill out the form and avoid duplication of effort.
3. Enter the required information for the new hire, including job title, job description, hire date, job type, job function, and other relevant contact information for their manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire** (`new_hire`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the new hire.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire's job role.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the new hire's job title.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the new hire was hired.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Talent Acquisition" as the job type, indicating the new hire is a talent acquisition specialist.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire's job function.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the new hire's reporting manager.
* **Job Grade** (`job_grade`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire's job grade.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department the new hire belongs to.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the new hire's job status.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the new hire will be working.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the new hire's manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the new hire's manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the new hire's manager.
* **Manager Ext** (`manager_ext`, text, required: false): Enter any additional contact information for the new hire's manager.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, required: false): Enter any important notes for the new hire's manager.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If there are any discrepancies or missing information, contact the HR department for assistance.
* Use this form to streamline the onboarding process and ensure a smooth transition for the new hire.
