# Onboarding Uniform Size Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a new employee's uniform size preferences during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Select your shirt size from the available options.
3. Select your pant size from the available options.
4. If necessary, enter any special requirements in the text field.
5. Select your jacket size from the available options.
6. If you have any other uniform items that require special sizes, select them from the available options.
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. Enter your employee ID number.
9. Enter your first name.
10. Enter your last name.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Enter the date of onboarding.
14. Enter your department.
15. Enter your job title.
16. Enter your job level.
17. Enter the name of your manager.
18. Enter the phone number of your manager.
19. Enter the email address of your manager.
20. Enter the name of the reporting manager.
21. Enter the phone number of the reporting manager.
22. Enter the email address of the reporting manager.
23. Enter the name of your supervisor.
24. Enter the phone number of your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shirt size** (`shirt_size`, select_multiple): Select your preferred shirt size. You can choose multiple sizes if you prefer.
* **Pant size** (`pant_size`, select_multiple): Select your preferred pant size. You can choose multiple sizes if you prefer.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text): If you have any special requirements for your uniform, please enter them here.
* **Jacket Size** (`jacket_size`, select_one): Select your preferred jacket size. This will apply to all jacket items.
* **Hat size** (`hat_size`, select_multiple): Select your preferred hat size. You can choose multiple sizes if you prefer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text): Enter your employee ID number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Onboarding** (`date_of_onboarding`, date): Enter the date you were onboarded in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Department** (`department`, text): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text): Enter your job level.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email): Enter the email address of your manager.
* **Reporting Manager Name** (`reporting_manager_name`, text): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (`reporting_manager_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`, email): Enter the email address of your reporting manager.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of your supervisor.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department.
* If you have any special requirements, please enter them in the Special Requirements field.
