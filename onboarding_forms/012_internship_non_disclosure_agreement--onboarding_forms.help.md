# Internship Non Disclosure Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between the company and the intern regarding confidentiality, non-use, and ownership of intellectual property during the internship. The intern agrees not to disclose any confidential information, trade secrets, or intellectual property to any third party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the fields provided.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Choose whether you agree to the non-disclosure agreement.
4. Select your role in the internship (Intern, Contractor, or Employee).
5. Enter the project name and location.
6. Enter the project duration (start and end dates).
7. Enter the supervisor's name, email, and title.
8. Sign and date the form with your supervisor's signature.
9. Sign and date the form with your own signature.
10. Enter the date you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement** (`non_disclosure_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to agree to the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the internship begins.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the internship ends.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's title.
* **Internship Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select your role in the internship (Intern, Contractor, or Employee).
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the project.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you will work per week.
* **Work Days** (`work_days`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you will work per week.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, number, optional): Enter your pay rate.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the form with your supervisor's signature.
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor's title.
* **Intern Signature** (`intern_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the form with your own signature.
* **Intern Signature Date** (`intern_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the internship.
