# Real Estate Agent Onboarding Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Agent Onboarding Employment Feedback Form is designed to gather information from new real estate agents who are joining our team. This form is used to collect feedback and insights that will help us improve the onboarding process, training, and support provided to new agents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title: Enter the title of your feedback (e.g. "Onboarding Experience with Real Estate Agent Onboarding Team").
2. Select your office location: Choose the location where you are working from the dropdown list.
3. Provide feedback on training satisfaction: Answer this question by selecting a number from 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
4. Provide feedback on support satisfaction: Similar to training satisfaction, answer this question by selecting a number from 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
5. Provide feedback on retention satisfaction: Answer this question by selecting a number from 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
6. Add any additional feedback: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the onboarding process.
7. Select your level of work experience: Choose one or more options from the list (e.g. 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5+ years).
8. Enter your email address: Enter your work email address.
9. Enter your phone number: Enter your work phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of your feedback. Please enter a brief description of your experience with the onboarding process.
* **Office Location** (`office_location`, select_one, required): This is the location where you are working. Please select the correct option from the dropdown list.
* **Training Satisfaction** (`training_satisfaction`, number, required): Please answer this question by selecting a number from 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
* **Support Satisfaction** (`support_satisfaction`, number, required): Similar to training satisfaction, answer this question by selecting a number from 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
* **Retention Satisfaction** (`retention_satisfaction`, number, required): Please answer this question by selecting a number from 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the onboarding process.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or more options from the list (e.g. 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5+ years).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your work phone number.
