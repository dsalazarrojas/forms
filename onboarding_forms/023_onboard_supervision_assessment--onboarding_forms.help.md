# Onboard Supervision Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the onboarding process for new hires, collecting feedback on the effectiveness of the onboarding process and identifying potential areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Onboarding Status" field to indicate the current status of the onboarding process for the new hire.
2. Choose the "New Hire Survey" field to select the area where the new hire received the most feedback on the onboarding process (e.g. "Onboarding Process", "Communication", "Training", etc.).
3. Enter the "Date of Joining" field to record the date the new hire started.
4. Answer the "Supervision" field with any information about the supervision received.
5. Select the communication methods used with the new hire in the "Communication Channels" field (e.g. "Email", "Phone", "Video Conferencing", "Instant Messaging").
6. Enter the frequency of communication with the new hire in the "Communication Frequency" field.
7. Choose the "Onboarding Status" field to indicate the current status of the onboarding process.
8. Enter any comments or feedback about the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Comments" field.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the overall onboarding process in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
10. Enter any comments about your overall satisfaction with the onboarding process in the "Overall Satisfaction Comments" field.
11. Describe your experience with communication with your supervisor in the "Supervisor Communication" field.
12. Enter any comments about the communication frequency with your supervisor in the "Communication Frequency Comments" field.
13. Enter any comments about the job expectations in the "Job Expectations Comments" field.
14. Enter any comments or suggestions on how to improve the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Process Improvements" field.
15. Enter any comments about the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Process Comments" field.

* **Onboarding Status** (Required/Optional: False): Choose the current status of the onboarding process for the new hire.
* **Feedback** (Required/Optional: False): Select any feedback the new hire received during the onboarding process.

* **Communication Channels** (Required/Optional: False): Select any communication methods used with the new hire.
* **Communication Frequency** (Required/Optional: False): Enter the frequency of communication with the new hire.
* **Job Expectations** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any comments or feedback about job expectations.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Required/Optional: False): Rate your satisfaction with the job.
* **Supervisor Satisfaction** (Required/Optional: False): Rate your supervisor's satisfaction with the job.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any comments or feedback about the supervisor's feedback.
* **New Hire Survey Comments** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any comments or feedback about the new hire survey.
* **Onboarding Issues** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any issues or problems with the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Recommendations** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any suggestions or recommendations on how to improve the onboarding process.
* **Communication Issues** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any issues or problems with communication.
* **Supervision Issues** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any issues or problems with supervision.
* **Onboarding Comments** (Required/Optional: False): Enter any comments about the onboarding process.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Required/Optional: False): Rate your overall satisfaction with the job.
