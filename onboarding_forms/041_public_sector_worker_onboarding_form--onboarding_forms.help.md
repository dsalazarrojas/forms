# Public Sector Worker Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new public sector workers to facilitate their onboarding process. It ensures that workers can access necessary resources and training to start their new role effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field according to your onboarding needs.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Info** (Employee Info, text, not required):
    This section is for providing general information about the employee. Enter your name and any other relevant details as needed.

- **Work Arrangement** (Work Arrangement, select_one, required):
    Please select 'Yes' if you will be working on an intermittent or part-time arrangement, and 'No' if you are working full-time.

- **Shift Schedule** (Shift, time, not required):
    If you have a specific shift schedule, you can enter it here.

- **Supervisor Info** (Supervisor Info, text, not required):
    Please enter the name and title of your supervisor, as well as any other relevant contact information.

- **Work Type** (Work Type, select_multiple, required):
    Select 'Yes' for any tasks or responsibilities you will be performing that involve working in the field, and 'No' for tasks that do not.

- **Work Schedule** (Work Schedule, select_one, required):
    Please select 'Yes' if you will be required to work a regular schedule, and 'No' if your schedule is more flexible.

- **Work Shift** (Work Shift, select_multiple, not required):
    Select 'Yes' for any shifts that apply to you, such as morning, afternoon, evening, or night shifts.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
