# Lab Data Analyst Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Data Analyst Onboarding Form is intended to help new staff members onboarding into the laboratory team by collecting relevant information about their roles, departments, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing accurate information.
2. For select_multiple fields (Role, Department, Prophets, Lab Technician), select all that apply.
3. For select_one fields (Lab Information), select only one option.
4. Enter your details in the text fields (Job Description, Supervisor, Supervisor Phone, Lab Location, Lab Manager, Onboarding Notes).
5. Fill in your job start and end dates and onboarding date and time if applicable.
6. Ensure to submit the form once you've completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role Setup** (2): This field is for indicating whether you have a role or not.
* **Job Title** (3): Enter a brief description of your job title.
* **Job Description** (4): Briefly describe your job in this field.
* **Internal Acknowledgements** (5): If you have internal acknowledgements, enter them here.
* **External Acknowledgements** (6): If you have external acknowledgements, enter them here.
* **Lab Information** (7): Indicate whether you have lab information or not.
* **Other** (8): If you have other lab information, enter it here.
* **Lab Equipment** (9): List any lab equipment you are responsible for.
* **Lab Location** (10): Enter the location of your lab.
* **Lab Manager** (11): Enter the name of your lab manager.
* **Prophets** (12): Indicate whether you have prophets or not.
* **Lab Technician** (13): Indicate whether you have lab technicians or not.
* **Other** (14): If you have other lab technicians, enter them here.
* **Onboarding Date** (15): Enter the date you're onboarding.
* **Onboarding Time** (16): Enter the time you're onboarding.
* **Email** (17): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (18): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Start Date** (19): Enter your job start date.
* **Job End Date** (20): Enter your job end date.
* **Role** (21): Indicate whether you have a role or not.
* **Department** (22): Indicate whether you have a department or not.
* **Supervisor** (23): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (24): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Onboarding Notes** (25): Add any onboarding notes here.
* **Submit** (26): Once you've completed the form, click submit to send the form.
