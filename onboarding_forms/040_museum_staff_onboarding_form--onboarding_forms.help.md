# Museum Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new museum staff members during the onboarding process. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your HR and administrative records are up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Staff Information (page 1): This section includes basic contact information such as your name, department, and job title.
2. Fill out the rest of the form: Continue to the next sections, which include date of joining, email, phone, manager's name, supervisor's name, and other emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Staff Information** (page 1): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
  * This field is a required field.
  * Make sure to enter a valid name.
- **Name** (page 1): Enter your full name.
  * This field is a required field.
  * Enter the name you use to sign documents and be recognized on official matters.
- **Position** (page 1): Enter your job title or position within the museum.
  * This field is a required field.
  * This is your official title within the museum.
- **Department** (page 1): Enter the department you belong to within the museum.
  * This field is a required field.
  * This will help us track your work area and contacts.
- **Date of Joining** (page 2): Enter the date you started working at the museum.
  * This field is a required field for all new staff members.
  * Enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Email** (page 2): Enter your work email address.
  * This field is a required field.
  * Make sure to use your official museum email address.
- **Phone** (page 2): Enter your contact phone number.
  * This field is not required but recommended for easy contact.
- **Onboarding Date** (page 2): Enter the date you completed the onboarding process.
  * This field is not required but recommended for future reference.
- **Manager Name** (page 3): Enter your direct manager's name.
  * This field is a required field.
  * Make sure to enter your immediate supervisor's name.
- **Supervisor Name** (page 3): Enter your supervisor's name.
  * This field is a required field.
  * This will be the person responsible for overseeing your work.
- **Emergency Contact** (page 4-7): Enter information about your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Enter the name, phone number, email address, and any other relevant details about your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 4-7): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Make sure to enter a valid phone number.
- **Relationship** (page 4-7): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Be as specific as possible.
- **Note** (page 4-7): Add any additional information about your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Use this for any relevant notes.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (page 4-7): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Make sure to enter a valid address.
- **City** (page 4-7): Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Enter the city name.
- **State** (page 4-7): Enter the state of your emergency contact's address.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Enter the state name.
- **Zip** (page 4-7): Enter the zip code of your emergency contact's address.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Enter the zip code.
- **Country** (page 4-7): Enter the country of your emergency contact's address.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Enter the country name.
- **Relationship Type** (page 4-7): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Be as specific as possible.
- **Relationship Note** (page 4-7): Add any additional information about your relationship with your emergency contact.
  * This field is not required but recommended for emergency purposes.
  * Use this for any relevant notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your HR representative for guidance.
* You can always reach out to your HR representative if you need any assistance while filling out the form.
