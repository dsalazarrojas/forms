# Onboarding Agreement (Pilot/Student Information, Rental Agreement, Cancellation Policy/Safety Procedures and Practices) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Agreement is a form designed to gather essential information from pilot students during the onboarding process. This includes their pilot/student information, rental agreement, and cancellation policy/safety procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully to ensure you understand the requirements and expectations.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review the form for completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **PilotStudentInformation (onboarding_pilot_student_information)**: This section is where you can provide your pilot/student information, such as your name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
* **RentalAgreement (onboarding_rental_agreement)**: This section is for agreeing to the rental terms and conditions. Please read through the agreement carefully and check the relevant boxes to confirm your understanding.
* **CancellationPolicySafetyProcedures (onboarding_cancellation_policy_safety_procedures)**: This section outlines the policy and procedures for cancellations. Please read through and understand the procedures before submitting the form.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to review the entire form before submitting it to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
