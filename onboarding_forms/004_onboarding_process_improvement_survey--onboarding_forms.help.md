# onboarding_process_improvement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onboarding process improvement survey is a tool used to gather feedback from new hires to identify areas of improvement in the onboarding process. This survey aims to help the organization better understand the experiences and needs of its new employees, ultimately enhancing the overall onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "Comments" field to provide additional information.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Survey (id: 1)**: Provide a brief description of your new hire experience.
* **Feedback (id: 2)**: Share your feedback on your overall experience during the onboarding process.
* **Date of Onboarding (id: 3)**: Enter the date you started your onboarding process.
* **Time of Onboarding (id: 4)**: Enter the time you started your onboarding process.
* **First Day Feelings (id: 5)**: Describe your emotions and thoughts on the first day of your onboarding.
* **First Week Feelings (id: 6)**: Describe your emotions and thoughts on the first week of your onboarding.
* **Onboarding Process (id: 7)**: Explain any challenges or issues you encountered during the onboarding process.
* **Overall Onboarding Experience (id: 8)**: Share your overall experience with the onboarding process.
* **Areas for Improvement (id: 9)**: Select any areas where you think the onboarding process can be improved.
	+ Option 1: Communication
	+ Option 2: Training
	+ Option 3: Feedback
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify)
	+ Option 5: Not Applicable
* **Comments (id: 10)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your onboarding experience.
* **Email (id: 11)**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone (id: 12)**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Date of Survey (id: 13)**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time of Survey (id: 14)**: Enter the time you completed this survey.
* **Survey Completion Status (id: 15)**: Select your completion status for this survey.
	+ Option 1: Completed
	+ Option 2: Incomplete
	+ Option 3: Not Started
* **Onboarding Survey Completion Status (id: 16)**: Select your completion status for the onboarding survey.
	+ Option 1: Completed
	+ Option 2: Not Completed
	+ Option 3: Not Started
* **Submission Status (id: 17)**: Select your submission status for this form.
	+ Option 1: Submitted
	+ Option 2: Pending
	+ Option 3: Cancelled
* **Onboarding Submission Status (id: 18)**: Select your submission status for the onboarding survey.
	+ Option 1: Submitted
	+ Option 2: Pending
	+ Option 3: Cancelled

Note: Please ensure you answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible, and use the "Comments" field to provide any additional information or feedback. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
