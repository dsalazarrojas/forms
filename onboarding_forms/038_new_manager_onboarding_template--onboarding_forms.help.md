# new_manager_onboarding_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new manager onboarding template is a form used to collect information from new employees during the onboarding process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Reporting Manager ID**: Enter your reporting manager's ID number.
* **Reporting Manager Name**: Enter your reporting manager's name.
* **Leadership Preferences**: Select one of the provided options for your leadership preferences.
* **Leadership Preferences Other**: If you have leadership preferences that are not listed, enter them here.
