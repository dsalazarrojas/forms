# New Member Readiness Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The New Member Readiness Survey Form is a series of questions designed to help us assess your preparedness for the onboarding process. This form is meant to provide a general understanding of your background, preferences, and needs to tailor the onboarding process to meet your individual requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please select the correct answer or enter your information in the relevant fields. Make sure to complete all the required fields as indicated by the asterisk symbol (*). If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, please don't hesitate to reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is your name?**: This field is where you enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. Please ensure that your name is accurate and spelled correctly.
- **Do you have any questions or concerns about the onboarding process?**: In this field, please share any questions or concerns you have about the onboarding process. This is your opportunity to let us know if there's anything that's worrying you or anything you'd like clarification on.
- **How can we best support you during onboarding?**: This multiple-choice field asks how you would like us to support you during the onboarding process. Select all that apply.
  - True: We will provide support.
  - False: We will not provide support.
- **What is your preferred contact method for onboarding updates and questions?**: In this field, please select your preferred contact method for onboarding updates and questions. You can choose from the following options:
  - Email
  - Phone
  - Other (please specify)
- **What time zone are you in?**: This field requires you to enter your time zone. Make sure to select the correct time zone to ensure proper scheduling and communication.
- **What is your expected start date?**: Enter your expected start date for the onboarding process.
- **What is your expected end date?**: Enter your expected end date for the onboarding process.
- **How can we improve the onboarding process?**: In this field, please share any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve the onboarding process for future new members.
- **Do you have any questions or concerns that we should be aware of?**: In this field, please share any questions or concerns that might impact your onboarding experience.
- **What is your preferred language for onboarding updates and questions?**: Select your preferred language for onboarding updates and questions from the following options:
  - English
  - Spanish
  - Other (please specify)
- **Do you have a preferred email address for onboarding updates and questions?**: Enter your preferred email address for onboarding updates and questions.
- **What is your preferred phone number for onboarding updates and questions?**: Enter your preferred phone number for onboarding updates and questions.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all the required fields as indicated by the asterisk symbol (*).
- If you're unsure about anything, please don't hesitate to reach out.
- Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
