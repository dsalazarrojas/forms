# New Manager Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about new managers' experiences and feedback on the onboarding process and their overall satisfaction with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Answer the feedback questions by selecting one of the options provided for each question.
4. If you have any comments or recommendations, type them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as a manager.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as a manager.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will help us contact you for further discussions or follow-ups.
* **Reporting manager**: If you have a reporting manager, enter their name here. (Optional)
* **Start date**: Enter the date when you started as a manager. (Optional)
* **End date**: Enter the date when your onboarding process ended. (Optional)
* **What was your first week as a manager like?**: Select one of the options to describe your experience during the first week.
	+ Very positive: Your first week was a great experience, and you felt well-prepared.
	+ Negative: Your first week was difficult or challenging.
	+ Neutral: Your first week was okay, but you had some issues.
* **How would you rate the quality of our onboarding process?**: Select one of the options to rate your experience.
	+ Excellent: The onboarding process was well-structured and effective.
	+ Good: The onboarding process was good, but could be improved.
	+ Poor: The onboarding process was not satisfactory.
* **Have you felt supported during your onboarding process?**: Select one of the options to describe your experience.
	+ 'Yes': You felt well-supported during the onboarding process.
	+ 'No': You did not feel well-supported during the onboarding process.
* **What do you wish we had done differently?**: This is an open-ended question where you can type anything you think could have been improved.
* **What have you learned or gained as a manager at this company?**: Select one of the options or type anything else you have learned or gained.
	+ Time management: This is an important skill for managers.
	+ Communication: Effective communication is key to success in management.
	+ Leadership skills: Leadership skills are essential for managers.
	+ Team building: Building a strong team is crucial for a manager's success.
	+ Other: If you have learned or gained anything else.
* **Manager recommendation**: If you have any comments or recommendations for the manager who onboarding you, type them here. (Optional)
* **Team member recommendation**: If you have any comments or recommendations for the team members who onboarding you, type them here. (Optional)
* **Department**: If you have any comments or recommendations for the department you are working in, type them here. (Optional)
* **Manager comments**: If you have any comments or recommendations for the company, type them here. (Optional)
* **Start date 2**: Enter the date when you started as a manager (if different from start date). (Optional)
* **End date 2**: Enter the date when your onboarding process ended (if different from end date). (Optional)
* **Have you had any challenges as a manager that we could have addressed?**: If you have any challenges, type them here.
* **What could we have done better to support you?**: If you think we could have done anything better, type it here.
* **Manager recommendation 2**: If you have any comments or recommendations for the manager who onboarding you, type them here. (Optional)
* **Team member recommendation 2**: If you have any comments or recommendations for the team members who onboarding you, type them here. (Optional)
* **What have you learned or taken away from this experience?**: Type anything you have learned or taken away from this experience.
* **Do you feel more confident as a manager?**: Select one of the options to describe your experience.
	+ 'Yes': You feel more confident as a manager.
	+ 'No': You do not feel more confident as a manager.
* **How likely are you to recommend this company to other managers?**: Select one of the options to rate your experience.
	+ Very likely: You are very likely to recommend this company to other managers.
	+ Not very likely: You are not very likely to recommend this company to other managers.

**Tips**

* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations, feel free to type them in the respective text fields.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we will use it to improve the onboarding process and overall management experience.
