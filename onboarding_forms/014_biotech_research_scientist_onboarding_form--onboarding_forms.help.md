# Biotech Research Scientist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotech Research Scientist Onboarding Form is a mandatory document that ensures new employees understand their role, responsibilities, and commitments to company policies and safety protocols. It helps to ensure a smooth and successful onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and complete the form in one session.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
3. Make sure to review and agree with all relevant commitments, training, and safety requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information**
	+ **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
	+ **Employee ID**: Please enter your assigned employee number.
	+ **Start Date**: Please enter your first day of work.
* **Position Title and Department**
	+ **Position Title**: Please enter your job title.
	+ **Department or Lab**: Please enter your assigned department.
	+ **Direct Manager**: Please enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Emergency Contact**
	+ **Emergency Contact Name**: Please enter the full name of your emergency contact.
	+ **Emergency Contact Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
	+ **Relationship to You**: Please describe how this person is related to you.
* **Equipment and Access**
	+ **Lab Access Required**: Please select whether you need lab facility access.
	+ **Equipment Being Assigned**: Please list computers, keys, or devices assigned to you.
* **Training Requirements**
	+ **Basic Safety Training Completed**: Please select whether you have completed basic safety training.
	+ **Bloodborne Pathogen and Biohazard Training**: Please select whether you have completed this required training.
	+ **Biosafety Level Training Required**: Please select the relevant biosafety level for your work.
	+ **Lab-Specific Training Topics**: Please select the relevant trainings for your position.
* **Company Policies**
	+ **Safety Acknowledgments**: Please confirm that you have received and read the Laboratory Safety Manual.
	+ **Chemical Hygiene Plan**: Please confirm that you have reviewed the Chemical Hygiene Plan.
	+ **Emergency Procedures**: Please confirm that you understand emergency procedures and evacuation routes.
	+ **PPE Requirements**: Please select whether you will comply with PPE requirements.
	+ **Hazard Communication**: Please confirm that you understand GHS labeling and SDS requirements.
	+ **Incident Reporting**: Please select that you will report all incidents and near-misses.
* **Final Section**
	+ **Date of Onboarding**: Please enter the date this form is being completed.
	+ **Supervisor Name**: Please enter your supervisor's name.
	+ **Acknowledgment**: Please select that you have received all required onboarding materials and training.
