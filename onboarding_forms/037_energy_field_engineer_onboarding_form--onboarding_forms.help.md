# Energy Field Engineer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Field Engineer Onboarding Form is designed to guide new hires through the onboarding process, ensuring they are prepared to start their role as an Energy Field Engineer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the field explanations below for guidance on what each field means.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details**
	+ `New Hire Details` (Text): This field is for your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. Please enter your name as you would like to be known on the site, and ensure your contact details are accurate so that we can reach you.
* **Safety Acknowledgments**
	+ `Safety Acknowledgments` (Select Multiple): Please select one of the three options:
		- I understand and agree (indicate that you have read and agree to follow all safety policies and procedures).
		- I do not understand and disagree (indicate that you have not read or do not agree to follow safety policies and procedures, and we will discuss this further).
		- I am unsure (indicate that you are unfamiliar with safety policies and procedures, and we will review them with you).
* **Onboarding Information**
	+ `Onboarding Information` (Select One): Please select one of the three options:
		- HR (you will receive onboarding information from HR).
		- Operations (you will receive onboarding information from Operations team).
		- Both (you will receive onboarding information from both HR and Operations teams).
* **Job Details**
	+ `Job Details` (Text): This field is for any additional information about your job role, responsibilities, or expectations. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Job Title**
	+ `Job Title` (Text): Enter your official job title as it appears on your contract or offer letter.
* **Job Location**
	+ `Job Location` (Text): Enter the location where you will be working, including the site or facility name.
* **Supervisor Name**
	+ `Supervisor Name` (Text): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Team Name**
	+ `Team Name` (Text): Enter the name of your team or department.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have discussed your onboarding information with your supervisor or HR representative before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* We will review and verify the information you provide to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* You will receive further guidance and instructions on the onboarding process after submitting this form.
