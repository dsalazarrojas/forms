# Leadership Shadow Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Shadow Program Application Form is a form used to collect information from applicants for the Leadership Shadow Program. This form is used to determine each applicant's eligibility and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer each question in as much detail as possible.
* Make sure to select the correct options for your preferred contact method and availability.
* Please provide clear and concise answers to the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in your organization?**: This field is used to understand your position within the organization. Please provide your job title, department, or any other relevant information.
* **What is your job title?**: This field is used to understand your current job title and responsibilities.
* **What is the name of your team or department?**: This field is used to understand the team or department you belong to.
* **What is your supervisor's name?**: This field is used to identify your direct supervisor.
* **What is your supervisor's title?**: This field is used to understand your supervisor's job title.
* **Are you interested in participating in the Leadership Shadow Program?**: This field is a selection field where you can choose "Yes" or "No" if you are interested or not.
* **What date did you first join your current organization?**: This field is used to understand how long you have been with the organization.
* **What date did you join your current role?**: This field is used to understand how long you have been in your current position.
* **What is your preferred contact method for program communication?**: This field is a selection field where you can choose one or more of the following options: email, phone, text.
* **What is your availability for the program?**: This field is used to understand your schedule availability for the program.
* **What do you hope to gain or achieve through this program?**: This field is used to understand your goals and expectations from the program.
* **Why are you interested in participating in the Leadership Shadow Program?**: This field is used to understand your motivation for participating in the program.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question honestly and clearly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the program administrators.
* Please note that the program administrators will review each application and respond to applicants with the results of their eligibility and suitability assessment.
