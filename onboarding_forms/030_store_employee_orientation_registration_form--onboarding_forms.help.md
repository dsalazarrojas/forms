# Store Employee Orientation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about new employees registering for store employee orientation. It will help store management keep track of employee details, schedules, and availability for smooth store operations. Before proceeding, ensure that you are not a duplicate entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the relevant options for "Employee Information", "Job Location", "Job Schedule", "Availability", and "Shift".
3. Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields correctly.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Employee Registration** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_1`, text, required: false): A brief description of the new employee registration.
* **Employee Information** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Full-time" or "Part-time" to indicate your employment status.
* **Job Title** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_3`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position.
* **First Name** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_4`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_5`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_6`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_7`, text, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Title Description** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_8`, text, required: false): A brief description of your job title.
* **Job Location** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_9`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the locations you will be working at.
* **Job Schedule** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_10`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the schedules you will be working (e.g., morning, afternoon).
* **Availability** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_11`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Shift** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_12`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the shifts you are available to work.
* **Date Available** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_13`, date, required: false): Enter your availability date.
* **Time Available** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_14`, time, required: false): Enter your availability time.
* **Availability Notes** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_15`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your availability.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_16`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_17`, select_one, required: false): Select your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, partner).
* **Contact Phone** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_18`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Email** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_19`, email, required: false): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_20`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Work Address** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_21`, text, required: false): Enter your work address.
* **Social Security Number** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_22`, text, required: false): Enter your SSN (Social Security Number).
* **SSN** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_23`, text, required: false): A duplicate field for the SSN.
* **SSN Confirmation** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_24`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm that you have provided your SSN correctly.
* **Additional Comments** (`store_employee_orientation_registration_form_25`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks.
