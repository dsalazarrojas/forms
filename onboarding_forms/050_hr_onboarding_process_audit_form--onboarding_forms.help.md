# HR Onboarding Process Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Onboarding Process Audit Form is designed to collect and verify team member information to streamline the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team members you are onboarding from the list provided.
2. Fill in the First Name, Last Name, and Email fields for each selected team member.
3. Enter the team member's phone number and start date.
4. Indicate the team member's onboarding status (Active or Inactive).
5. Enter any comments or notes about the team member in the Comments field.
6. Review and submit the form for review and verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members** (Select Multiple): Select all the team members you are onboarding from the list provided.
* **First Name** (`team_member_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter the first name of the team member.
* **Last Name** (`team_member_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter the last name of the team member.
* **Email** (`team_member_email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the team member. Please follow the standard email format (e.g., `[example@example.com](mailto:example@example.com)`).
* **Phone** (`team_member_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the team member.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the start date of the team member in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the onboarding status of the team member (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Required): Enter any comments or notes about the team member.
