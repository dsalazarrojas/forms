# SaaS Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SaaS Onboarding Survey is a form designed to collect essential information from new subscribers to a SaaS service. This information helps us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and personalized onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in your details in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your preferred options for the select-type questions.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
5. Submit the form to complete the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Software Subscription** (`new_software_subscription`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the name or description of the new software subscription you are interested in.
* **Software License** (`software_license`, text, optional): Here, you can enter the type or version of the software license you are working with.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Please enter your email address to ensure we can communicate with you about your subscription.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number to help us contact you if needed.
* **How do you** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Select one of the available options to help us understand your preferences.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the onboarding process. This is a good place to mention any specific needs or requirements you may have.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the available choices to help us tailor the onboarding experience to your needs.
* **Decimal Value** (`decimal_value`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a decimal value to help us understand your specific requirements.
* **Time Value** (`time_value`, time, optional): Select a time value to help us schedule the onboarding process accordingly.
* **Integer Value** (`integer_value`, number, optional): Enter an integer value to help us understand your specific requirements.
* **Date Value** (`date_value`, date, optional): Choose a date to help us schedule the onboarding process.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple2`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the available choices to help us tailor the onboarding experience to your needs.
