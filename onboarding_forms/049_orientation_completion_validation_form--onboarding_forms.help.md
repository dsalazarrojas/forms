# Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the onboarding process for new employees, including orientation, training, and employee status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "employee_id" field.
3. Indicate whether you have completed training materials in the "training_materials_completed" field.
4. Enter your supervisor's name in the "manager_name" field.
5. Provide any supervisor comments in the "supervisor_comments" field.
6. Sign your name in the "employee_signature" field.
7. Choose your training status in the "training_status" field.
8. Sign your manager's name in the "manager_signature" field.
9. Enter the completion date in the "completion_date" field.
10. List any training materials received in the "training_materials_received" field.
11. Enter your manager's name again in the "manager_name" field.
12. Choose the onboarding status in the "onboarding_status" field.
13. Enter the date of orientation in the "orientation_date" field.
14. Enter the date of training in the "training_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `string`, required): Enter the title of the onboarding form.
* **Onboarding Form** (`onboarding_form`, `note`, required): Please fill out this form to complete the onboarding process.
* **Date of Orientation Completion** (`orientation_completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of orientation completion.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Training Materials Completed** (`training_materials_completed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have completed training materials (option 1 or option 2).
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, `note`, required): Provide any comments from your supervisor.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm completion of the form.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current training status (option 1 or option 2).
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your manager's name to confirm completion of the form.
* **Date Completed** (`completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **List of Received Materials** (`training_materials_received`, `select_multiple`, required): List any training materials you have received.
* **Manager's Name (Again)** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name again.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the onboarding status (option 1 or option 2).
* **Date of Orientation** (`orientation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of orientation.
* **Date of Training** (`training_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of training.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
* Ensure that your manager has reviewed and signed the form before submitting it.
