# E Commerce Seller Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide you through the onboarding process as a new e-commerce seller on our platform. It's a crucial step in setting up your account and ensuring a smooth experience for both you and your customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and make sure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in your User Details, including your name and any other relevant personal information.
3. Provide your Store Information, including your store name and address.
4. Enter your Shipping Details, including your shipping address and any relevant contact information.
5. Fill in your Payment Info, including your payment methods and any relevant account numbers.
6. Enter your Tax Info, including your tax identification number and any other relevant tax information.
7. Fill in your Bank Info, including your bank account details.
8. Enter your Account Info, including your account name and any other relevant information.
9. Enter your Address Details, including your address and any relevant contact information.
10. Select your Tax ID Type from the dropdown menu.
11. Enter your Phone number.
12. Enter your Email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to enter your name and any other personal information.
- **Store Information** (`store_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you provide your store name and address.
- **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address and any relevant contact information.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your payment methods and account numbers.
- **Tax Info** (`tax_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your tax identification number and any other relevant tax information.
- **Bank Info** (`bank_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your bank account details.
- **Account Info** (`account_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your account name and any other relevant information.
- **Address Details** (`address_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address and any relevant contact information.
- **Business Info** (`business_info`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional field for providing additional business information.
- **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your tax identification number.
- **Tax ID Type** (`tax_id_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the type of tax ID you have (e.g., US Tax ID, EU VAT).
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
