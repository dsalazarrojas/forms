# Intern Onboarding Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Onboarding Accommodation Form is used to collect information about the accommodation details of new interns and their onboarding start date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your accommodation address.
2. Enter your accommodation city.
3. Type your accommodation state.
4. Enter your accommodation zip code.
5. Select your onboarding start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accommodation Address**: Please enter your current or future accommodation address.
* **Accommodation City**: Enter your city of residence or accommodation.
* **Accommodation State**: Enter your state of residence or accommodation.
* **Accommodation Zip**: Enter your zip code of your accommodation.
* **Start Date**: Select your onboarding start date to track and schedule your onboarding process.
