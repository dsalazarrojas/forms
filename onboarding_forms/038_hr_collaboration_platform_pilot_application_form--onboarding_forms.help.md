# HR Collaboration Platform Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Collaboration Platform Pilot Application Form is designed to gather information from interested teams to assess their fit for the pilot program. This form will help us understand your team's goals, structure, and requirements to better tailor the collaboration platform to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Application Information**: Provide a brief overview of your team's goals and objectives for implementing the HR Collaboration Platform.
2. **Team Details**: Describe your team's structure, including team members and their roles.
3. **Team Lead Information**: Enter the name, phone number, and email address of your team lead.
4. **Team Member Information**: Enter contact information for each team member, including name, phone number, and email address.
