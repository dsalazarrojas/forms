# Pet Product Retailer Onboarding Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for pet product retailers who want to onboard with the company and provide their contact, business, and pet-related information. The form is a multi-page document that collects data for various purposes such as data analysis, marketing, or customer relationship management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the first page, which is focused on pet information.
2. Move on to the next page, where you will provide your contact information.
3. Continue to the subsequent pages, which will ask for business and pet-related information.
4. Finally, review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Information 1** (`pet_product_retailer_onboarding_application_form_page_1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering your pet's information, such as name, age, breed, or species.
* **Pet Information 2** (`pet_product_retailer_onboarding_application_form_page_2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing additional information about your pet, such as weight, size, or health conditions.
* **Contact Information** (`pet_product_retailer_onboarding_application_form_page_3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering your contact details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Business Information** (`pet_product_retailer_onboarding_application_form_page_4`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing your business information, including your company name, website, and business description.
* **Product Information** (`pet_product_retailer_onboarding_application_form_page_5`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering information about the pet products you sell, such as product names, descriptions, and prices.
* **Submission** (`pet_product_retailer_onboarding_application_form_page_6`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for submitting your completed form for review.

Note that the "required/optional" label in each field indicates that some fields may be mandatory, while others are optional. Please fill out all mandatory fields to ensure that your form is complete and accurate.
