# Speaker Onboarding Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about speakers for our events and programs. We use this information to personalize our communication with you and make sure you have an amazing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with your name and title.
- Upload a photo of yourself for our records.
- Provide a brief bio and a list of your expertise and interests.
- Share your social media presence so we can stay connected.
- Fill in your schedule and availability for our events.
- Add your contact information.
- Select your language and experience level.
- Add any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Information** (`speaker_info`, text, required): Enter your name and title to introduce yourself.
* **Speaker Photo** (`speaker_photo`, text, required): Upload a clear and recent photo of yourself.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, required): Write a brief bio that describes your experience and expertise.
* **Social Media** (`speaker_social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select any social media platforms you'd like to share with us.
* **Bio Image** (`speaker_image`, text, optional): Upload an image that represents you.
* **Speaker Headshot** (`speaker_headshot`, text, optional): Upload a headshot photo of yourself.
* **Speaker Talk** (`speaker_talk`, text, optional): Share a brief description of your talk or presentation.
* **Speaker Topics** (`speaker_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select any topics you'd like to be associated with.
* **Speaker Schedule** (`speaker_schedule`, date, optional): Fill in your schedule and availability for our events.
* **Speaker Availability** (`speaker_availability`, date, optional): Share your availability for our events.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, optional): Add your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Speaker Facebook** (`speaker_facebook`, text, optional): Share your Facebook profile.
* **Speaker Instagram** (`speaker_instagram`, text, optional): Share your Instagram profile.
* **Speaker Twitter** (`speaker_twitter`, text, optional): Share your Twitter profile.
* **Speaker LinkedIn** (`speaker_linkedin`, text, optional): Share your LinkedIn profile.
* **Speaker YouTube** (`speaker_youtube`, text, optional): Share your YouTube profile.
* **Speaker Twitch** (`speaker_twitch`, text, optional): Share your Twitch profile.
* **Speaker Discord** (`speaker_discord`, text, optional): Share your Discord profile.
* **Speaker Website** (`speaker_website`, text, optional): Share your personal website.
* **Speaker Birthday** (`speaker_birthday`, date, optional): Share your birthday.
* **Speaker Occupation** (`speaker_occupation`, text, optional): Share your occupation.
* **Speaker Language** (`speaker_language`, select_one, optional): Select your native language.
* **Speaker Experience** (`speaker_experience`, number, optional): Share your experience level.
* **Speaker Interests** (`speaker_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select your interests.
