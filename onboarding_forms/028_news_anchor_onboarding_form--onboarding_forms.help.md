# News Anchor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The News Anchor Onboarding Form is a form that helps news anchors provide their onboarding information. It's a one-time process that ensures we have the most up-to-date information about you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Upload the required **File 1**, **File 2**, and **File 3**.
5. Sign and date the **Signature 1** and **Signature 2** fields.
6. Add any additional **Notes** you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This is the name you'd like to be known by in our system. Please enter your name as it appears on official records.
* **Email**: This is the email address you use to send and receive messages from us. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone**: This is your contact phone number. We may use this to reach you for important announcements or updates.
* **File 1**: This is the first required document to be uploaded. Please ensure it's in the correct format and size.
* **Signature 1**: This is the first signature required. Please date and sign this field accurately.
* **File 2**: This is the second required document to be uploaded. Please ensure it's in the correct format and size.
* **Signature 2**: This is the second signature required. Please date and sign this field accurately.
* **File 3**: This is the third required document to be uploaded. Please ensure it's in the correct format and size.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for any additional information you'd like to provide.
