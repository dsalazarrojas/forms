# Documentation Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Documentation Policy Acknowledgment form is a crucial part of the onboarding process, where new employees are required to acknowledge and confirm their understanding of the company's documentation policy. This form helps to ensure that employees are aware of the policies and procedures that govern their work and the company's operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Acknowledgment Date from the calendar and enter the date you are acknowledging the policy.
2. Choose the correct Policy Version from the list.
3. Select the Acknowledgment Of Policy number from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the Manager(s) who reviewed the policy.
5. Enter the Date Of Completion in the required format (HH:MM:SS).
6. Select the Acknowledgment Status as Approved or Pending.
7. If necessary, enter any Remarks about your acknowledgment.
8. Enter your Employee Name.
9. Enter your Job Title.
10. Enter your Employee Email.
11. Enter your Employee Phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Acknowledgment Date**: Enter the date you are acknowledging the policy. This date should be in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Policy Version**: This field requires you to select the correct version of the policy you are acknowledging.
* **Acknowledgment Of Policy**: Select the number of the policy you are acknowledging. This will ensure that the correct policy is associated with your acknowledgment.
* **Manager Review**: Select the manager(s) who reviewed the policy. This will help ensure that the correct manager's approval is recorded.
* **Date Of Completion**: Enter the date and time when you completed the acknowledgment. Use the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Acknowledgment Status**: Select either Approved or Pending to indicate the status of your acknowledgment.
* **Remarks**: If you have any comments or concerns about the policy, enter them here.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your employee ID card.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your employee ID card.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
