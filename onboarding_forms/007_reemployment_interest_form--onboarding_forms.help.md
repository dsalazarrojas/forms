# Reemployment Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reemployment Interest Form is designed to collect information from employees about their interest in being reemployed and their preferences for new job opportunities. This form helps to identify potential candidates for reemployment and ensures that their needs and interests are taken into account during the reemployment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out this form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  Please enter your User Interest, Availability, and Preferred Location.
3.  Select your Job Preferences from the available options (Role, Function, Team).
4.  Choose your Rehire Pool status (Yes or No).
5.  Provide a brief description of the Job Title and Job Description.
6.  Enter your Availability Date and Earliest Start Date.
7.  Specify your Notice Period.
8.  Enter a contact method (phone, email, etc).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Interest** (text, optional):
	+ A brief description of your interest in being reemployed.
* **Availability** (text, optional):
	+ A brief description of your current work availability.
* **Job Preferences** (select multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- **Role**: Choose the role you are interested in, such as Management, Sales, Engineering, etc.
		- **Function**: Select the function you are interested in, such as Marketing, IT, etc.
		- **Team**: Choose the team you would like to work with, if any.
* **Preferred Location** (text, optional):
	+ Enter your preferred location for work, including city or region.
* **Rehire Pool** (select multiple, optional):
	+ Select **Yes** if you are interested in being reemployed and willing to return to work.
	+ Select **No** if you are not interested in being reemployed.
* **Job Title** (text, optional):
	+ A brief description of the job title you are interested in.
* **Job Description** (text, optional):
	+ A brief description of the job description you are interested in.
* **Availability Date** (date, optional):
	+ Enter your expected availability date to start a new job.
* **Earliest Start Date** (date, optional):
	+ Enter your earliest expected start date for a new job.
* **Notice Period** (number, optional):
	+ Specify the notice period required for a new job opportunity.
* **Contact Method** (text, optional):
	+ Enter your preferred contact method, such as phone number or email address.
