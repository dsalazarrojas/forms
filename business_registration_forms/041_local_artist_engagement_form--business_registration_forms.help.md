# local_artist_engagement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The local artist engagement form is used for artists to apply to local festivals and events. The form is designed to gather essential information from artists, including their contact details and performance details, to help festival organizers and staff evaluate their application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the festival you would like to apply to from the "Apply to which festival" dropdown menu.
2. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your artist name and bio in the "Artist Name" and "Bio" fields respectively.
5. Select your performance type from the "Performance Type" dropdown menu.
6. Provide details about your performance in the "Performance Details" field.
7. Enter your contact address and city in the "Contact Address" and "Contact City" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Apply to which festival**: Choose the festival you would like to apply to from the dropdown menu.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Artist Name**: Enter your artist name as it is known to you.
* **Bio**: Write a brief description of your art style or performance.
* **Performance Type**: Choose the type of performance you will be doing (e.g., music, dance, comedy).
* **Performance Details**: Provide more details about your performance, such as the type of equipment needed.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address where you can be reached.
* **Contact City**: Enter the city where you can be contacted.

Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your application is complete and can be reviewed by festival staff.
