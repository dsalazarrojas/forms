# SaaS Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SaaS Onboarding Survey form is designed to collect essential information about a new SaaS subscription, including its name, license, description, contact information, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the required information.
3. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New SaaS Subscription
* **New SaaS Subscription** (`new_saassubscription`, `text`, optional): This field is where you enter the name of the new SaaS subscription. Please enter the name of the subscription in plain text.

### Saas Name
* **Saas Name** (`saasname`, `text`, optional): In this field, please enter the name of the SaaS product or application.

### SaaS License
* **SaaS License** (`saaslicense`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple licenses that apply to the SaaS subscription. Please choose from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### SaaS Description
* **SaaS Description** (`saasdescription`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the purpose or features of the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Contact Info
* **SaaS Contact Info** (`saascontactinfo`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information for the SaaS subscription, such as a phone number or email address.

### SaaS Company
* **SaaS Company** (`saascompany`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Address
* **SaaS Address** (`saasaddress`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the company associated with the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Phone
* **SaaS Phone** (`saasphone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Email
* **SaaS Email** (`saasemail`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address associated with the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Time Zone
* **SaaS Time Zone** (`saastimezone`, `time`, optional): Select the time zone relevant to the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Created by
* **SaaS Created by** (`saascreatedby`, `select_one`, optional): Select the person who created the SaaS subscription. Please choose from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### SaaS Created on
* **SaaS Created on** (`saascreatedon`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the SaaS subscription was created.

### SaaS Updated by
* **SaaS Updated by** (`saasupdatedby`, `select_one`, optional): Select the person who last updated the SaaS subscription. Please choose from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### SaaS Updated on
* **SaaS Updated on** (`saasupdatedon`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the SaaS subscription was last updated.

### SaaS Status
* **SaaS Status** (`saasstatus`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of the SaaS subscription. Please choose from the available options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### SaaS Notes
* **SaaS Notes** (`saasnotes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the SaaS subscription.

### SaaS Created Date
* **SaaS Created Date** (`saascreateddate`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the SaaS subscription was created.

### SaaS Updated Date
* **SaaS Updated Date** (`saasupdateddate`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the SaaS subscription was last updated.

## Tips
- Be accurate and detailed when filling out the form.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with the relevant person or department within the organization. 
- This form is for internal use only.
