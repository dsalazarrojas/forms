# Facebook Ad Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facebook Ad Intake Form is designed to collect essential information for your Facebook ad campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the User Input field with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. In the Client Info field, provide any relevant contact information for the client (if applicable).
3. In the Ad Info field, enter the specific details of the ad campaign you are creating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (`user_input`, text, required):** Enter your name, phone number, and email address to contact you for further communication.
* **Client Info (`client_info`, text, optional):** Provide any relevant contact information for the client, such as name, email, or phone number.
* **Ad Info (`ad_info`, text, required):** Enter the specific details of the ad campaign, including ad copy, targeting information, and other relevant ad settings.
