# Smoothie And Bowl Maker Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for smoothie and bowl makers to register their business with us. Please fill out the form with your business details to get listed in our database.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Make sure to format your phone number and email correctly.
3. For select-type fields, choose the options that best fit your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, text, optional): Enter your business name, e.g., "Smoothie Heaven".
* **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the owner of the business.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your business email in the standard "username@domain.com" format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
* **Menu Items** (`Menu Items`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or both of the following options:
	+ "Yes" (not specified, assume it means menu items are available)
	+ "No" (not specified, assume it means no menu items are available)
* **Experience** (`Experience`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ A (not specified, assume it's a level of experience or expertise)
	+ B (not specified, assume it's another level of experience or expertise)
	+ C (not specified, assume it's a third level of experience or expertise)
* **Menu Photo** (`Menu Photo`, text, optional): Enter a photo or URL of your menu.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): Enter your business location.
* **Menu Photo** (`Menu Photo`, text, optional): Enter a photo or URL of your menu.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your phone number and email formats.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
