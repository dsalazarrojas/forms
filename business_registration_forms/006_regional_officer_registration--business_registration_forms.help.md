# Regional Officer Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register and store information about regional officers in our organization. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your contact and role details are correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your **First Name**, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name**, which is also a required field.
3. Enter your **Email**, which should be a valid email address. This field is required.
4. Choose your **Region** from the provided options (Region A, Region B, or Region C). This field is required.
5. Select your **Role** from the list of provided options (Officer A, Officer B, or Officer C). This field is required.
6. Enter your **Contact Phone**, which is a required field.
7. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** by checking the box. This field is required.
8. Optionally, view the **Terms and Conditions** by clicking on the link.
9. Enter the **Region Name** (if not already provided), which is an optional field.
10. Enter your **Organization**, which is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address that can receive official communications.
* **Region** (`region`, required): Choose the region you are affiliated with from the provided options.
* **Role** (`role`, required): Select the specific role you hold within the region.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number for official communication.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`agreement`, required): Check this box to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **View Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, optional): Click on this link to review the terms and conditions.
* **Region Name** (`region_name`, optional): Enter the name of your region (if not already provided).
* **Organization** (`organization`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
