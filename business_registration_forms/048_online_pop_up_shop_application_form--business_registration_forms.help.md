# online-pop-up-shop-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a help guide for the online-pop-up-shop-application-form. This form is designed to collect business information and shop details from applicants who want to open an online pop-up shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your business information in the "Business Information" field.
2. Enter your owner's information in the "Owner Information" field.
3. Select the products you will be selling in the "Business Products" field.
4. Enter your store's contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Specify your store's location in the "Store Location" field.
6. Choose your application date in the "Application Date" field.
7. Select your store's operating hours in the "Store Hours" field.
8. Enter your store's operating hours in the "Store Hours Time" field.
9. Provide any additional information about your store in the "Store Information" field.
10. Add any notes or comments about your application in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, not required): This field is used to enter your business name, address, and other relevant details.
- **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, not required): This field is used to enter the owner's name, title, and other relevant information.
- **Business Products** (`business_products`, select multiple, not required): Select all the products you will be selling in your online pop-up shop.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
- **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, not required): Specify your store's location, including the address, city, state, and zip code.
- **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, not required): Choose the date you would like to submit your application.
- **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, select multiple, not required): Select the days your store will be open, e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
- **Store Hours Time** (`store_hours_time`, time, not required): Enter your store's operating hours in 24-hour format, e.g., 09:00, 12:00, 17:00.
- **Store Information** (`store_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about your store, such as its description or features.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any comments or notes about your application.
