# vat_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VAT Registration Form is a form used by businesses to provide the necessary information for registering with the tax authorities. It is used to capture the essential details about the business, including its name, address, contact information, VAT details, and registration date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company information on the **Company Information** page.
2. Provide your VAT details on the **VAT Details** page.
3. Enter your contact details on the **Contact Details** page.
4. Enter your address on the **Address** page.
5. Enter your registration date on the **Registration Date** page.
6. Enter your registration number on the **Registration Number** page.
7. Finally, enter your business name on the **Business Name** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name and other relevant information. This field is not mandatory, but it's a good place to start providing some basic details about your company.
* **VAT Details** (`vat_details`, text, required: false): This is where you can enter any relevant VAT-related information. Please note that this field is not required, so it's up to your discretion.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter the contact details of the person who will be handling your VAT registration. This could include their name, email, and phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your business's physical address. This could include the street address, city, state, and country.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when your business was registered for VAT. This is not a mandatory field, but it's recommended for accuracy.
* **Registration Number** (`registration_number`, number, required: false): Enter the VAT registration number assigned to your business. This field is also not required, but it's useful for identification purposes.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter your business's name. This is the last field on the form, so take your time and make sure it's accurate.
