# Business Registration Certificate Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to make changes to an existing business registration certificate. This may include changes to the business name, address, owner, or other details.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, ensure you have all necessary supporting documents to attach to this form. This form requires you to provide information about the current state of your business registration and the changes you wish to make. Please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your current business registration number, which can be found on your current registration certificate.
2. Provide the date when the original certificate was issued.
3. Enter your current business name as shown on the registration certificate.
4. Select the type of amendment you are making from the provided options.
5. If changing the business name, enter the new business name.
6. Enter your current business address as it appears on the registration.
7. If your address is changing, enter the new business address, city, state or province, and postal code.
8. Enter the name of the current owner of the business as shown on the registration.
9. If changing ownership, enter the new owner's name.
10. Enter the new owner's identification number, if applicable.
11. Enter the updated contact email address of your business.
12. Enter the updated phone number of your business.
13. Explain the reason for the amendment in the provided field.
14. Select the effective date when the changes should take effect.
15. List any supporting documents attached to this form, if applicable.
16. Enter the name of the person authorized to sign this form.
17. Finally, enter the date this form is being submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Registration Number** (1) (`text`, required): Enter your existing business registration number.
* **Certificate Issue Date** (2) (`date`, required): Enter the date when the original certificate was issued.
* **Current Business Name** (3) (`text`, required): Enter your business name as shown on the registration certificate.
* **Type of Amendment** (4) (`select_one`, required): Select the type of amendment you are making from the options provided.
* **New Business Name** (5) (`text`, optional): If changing your business name, enter the new name.
* **Current Business Address** (6) (`text`, required): Enter your current business address as shown on the registration.
* **New Business Address** (7) (`text`, optional): If relocating, enter the new address.
* **New City** (8) (`text`, optional): If relocating, enter the city of the new address.
* **New State or Province** (9) (`text`, optional): If relocating, enter the state or province of the new address.
* **New Postal Code** (10) (`text`, optional): If relocating, enter the postal code of the new address.
* **Current Owner Name** (11) (`text`, required): Enter the name of the current owner of the business.
* **New Owner Name** (12) (`text`, optional): If changing ownership, enter the new owner's name.
* **Owner Identification Number** (13) (`text`, optional): If changing ownership, enter the new owner's identification number.
* **Contact Email** (14) (`email`, required): Enter the updated contact email address of your business.
* **Contact Phone Number** (15) (`text`, required): Enter the updated phone number of your business.
* **Reason for Amendment** (16) (`text`, required): Explain why the change is needed.
* **Effective Date of Amendment** (17) (`date`, required): Select the date when the change should take effect.
* **Supporting Documents** (18) (`text`, optional): List any documents included to support the amendment.
* **Authorized Signatory Name** (19) (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to sign this form.
* **Amendment Submission Date** (20) (`date`, required): Enter the date this form is being submitted.
