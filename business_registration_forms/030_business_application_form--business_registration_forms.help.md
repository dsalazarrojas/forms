# Business Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Application Form is designed to collect business information for [Insert Company/Authority Name] to process your application. This form is used to gather essential details about your business, including its structure, industry, location, and other relevant particulars. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review and understand the form's instructions and requirements.
2.  Fill out the form fields accurately and completely, using the provided options and hints as a guide.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (id: 2, required: true): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **DBA or Trade Name** (id: 3, required: false): If your business operates under a different name, enter it here.
* **Business Type** (id: 4, required: true, options: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, S-Corporation, Non-Profit, Other): Select the legal structure of your business.
* **Industry** (id: 5, required: true, options: Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Food Service, Professional Services, Construction, Real Estate, Education, Other): Choose the primary industry of your business.
* **Year Established** (id: 6, required: true): Enter the year your business was founded.
* **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** (id: 7, required: true): Enter your federal tax ID number.
* **Business License Number** (id: 8, required: false): Enter your business license number if applicable.
* **Contact Information** (id: 9, required: false): Enter your contact information for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Business Address** (id: 10, required: true): Enter your physical business address.
* **City** (id: 11, required: true): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (id: 12, required: true): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (id: 13, required: true): Enter your business's postal code.
* **Business Phone** (id: 14, required: true): Enter your business contact number.
* **Business Email** (id: 15, required: true): Enter your business contact email.
* **Business Website** (id: 16, required: false): Enter your business website URL if applicable.
* **Owner Full Name** (id: 18, required: true): Enter the legal name of the primary owner.
* **Owner Title** (id: 19, required: true): Enter the title of the primary owner.
* **Owner Email** (id: 20, required: true): Enter the contact email of the primary owner.
* **Owner Phone** (id: 21, required: true): Enter the contact number of the primary owner.
* **Ownership Percentage** (id: 22, required: true): Enter the percentage of ownership held by the primary owner.
* **Number of Employees** (id: 24, required: true, options: 1-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100, More than 100): Choose the number of employees in your business.
* **Annual Revenue** (id: 25, required: true, options: Under $50,000, $50,000-$100,000, $100,000-$500,000, $500,000-$1,000,000, $1,000,000-$5,000,000, More than $5,000,000): Choose the approximate annual revenue of your business.
* **Business Description** (id: 26, required: true): Describe your business in a few sentences.
* **Products or Services** (id: 27, required: true): List the products or services your business offers.
* **Target Market** (id: 28, required: false): Describe your target market or customer base.
* **Main Competitors** (id: 29, required: false): List your main competitors or businesses that operate similarly to yours.
* **Business Goals** (id: 30, required: false): Enter your short-term business goals.
* **Banking Information** (id: 31, required: false): Enter your banking information for payment purposes.
* **Bank Name** (id: 32, required: false): Enter the name of your bank.
* **Account Type** (id: 33, required: false): Choose the type of account you hold with the bank.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 34, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about your business.
