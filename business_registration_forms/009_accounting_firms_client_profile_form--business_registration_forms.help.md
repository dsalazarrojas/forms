# Accounting Firms Client Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client profile information, allowing your accounting firm to understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name under "Client name."
2. Select the client's industry from the provided options under "Industry."
3. Enter the name of the primary contact person under "Primary contact name."
4. Enter the contact email under "Contact email." This will be the primary means of communication with the client.
5. Enter the client's contact phone number under "Contact phone" if desired.
6. Fill in the client's billing address under "Billing address" if applicable.
7. If applicable, enter the client's tax ID number under "Tax ID number."
8. Select the client's preferred contact method under "Preferred contact method" (Email, Phone, or Postal mail).
9. Choose the services the client requires under "Services required." Select all services that apply.
10. If switching from another accounting firm, enter the current provider's name under "Current accounting provider."
11. Enter any special notes or requirements under "Notes and requirements" if applicable.
12. If applicable, select the date when the client will be ready for onboarding under "Onboarding ready date."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name (individual or company).
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): Select the client's primary sector of business.
* **Primary contact name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the person handling communications with the client.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email for invoices and updates.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number for urgent contact if desired.
* **Billing address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter the address for invoices if applicable.
* **Tax ID number** (`tax_id_number`, text, required): Enter the VAT, EIN, or local tax identifier if applicable.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select how the client prefers to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Postal mail).
* **Services required** (`services_required`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable services (Bookkeeping, Payroll, Tax preparation, Advisory, or Audit support).
* **Current accounting provider** (`current_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the current accounting provider if switching.
* **Notes and requirements** (`notes_and_requirements`, text, required): Enter any special notes or requirements if applicable.
* **Onboarding ready date** (`onboarding_ready_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the client will be ready for onboarding if applicable.

By following these steps and explanations, you will be able to complete the form accurately and effectively.
