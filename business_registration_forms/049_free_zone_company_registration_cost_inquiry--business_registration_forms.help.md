# Free Zone Company Registration Cost Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from companies interested in registering their business in a free zone. This form will help the company gather the necessary data to determine the registration cost for their business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company's name, address, and other required fields.
2. Select the type of company and registration type.
3. Enter the email and phone number of the person who will be contacted for follow-up.
4. Choose the follow-up method (phone, email, or in-person).
5. Select the follow-up time and channel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company.
- **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of company (Private or Public).
- **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of registration (Sole-Proprietor, Partnership, or Company).
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who will be contacted for follow-up.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who will be contacted for follow-up.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of registration.
- **Registration Location** (`registration_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of registration.
- **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the business.
- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments for follow-up.
- **Next Step** (`next_step`, text, optional): Enter any next step or instructions for follow-up.
- **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, select_one, optional): Select the method of contact (Phone, Email, or In-person).
- **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of follow-up.
- **Follow-up Channel** (`follow_up_channel`, select_one, optional): Select the channel of contact (Email, Phone, or In-person).

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the information provided is correct and up-to-date.
- If you have already registered your business in a free zone, please do not fill out this form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us for further help.
