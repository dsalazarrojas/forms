# Business Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from business partners seeking to partner with your company. The goal is to gather the necessary details about your business entity, its structure, and its interests to determine potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out each page in sequence.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your business.
*   If you have any difficulties or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name (1)**: Enter the legal name of your business.
*   **Business Structure (2)**: Choose the type of business entity (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).
*   **Company Registration Number (3)**: Enter your business registration number or ID. 
*   **Years in Business (4)**: Indicate how long your company has been in operation.
*   **Primary Contact Name (5)**: Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
*   **Contact Title (6)**: Enter the contact person's position or role.
*   **Email Address (7)**: Enter the primary business email address.
*   **Phone Number (8)**: Enter the contact phone number.
*   **Business Address (9)**: Enter the physical location of your business.
*   **Company Website (10)**: Enter the URL of your company website.
*   **Industry Sector (11)**: Choose the primary industry your business operates in (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, etc.).
*   **Business Description (12)**: Briefly describe what your company does.
*   **Type of Partnership Sought (13)**: Choose the type of partnership interests you are seeking (e.g., Supplier, Distributor, etc.).
*   **Target Market (14)**: If applicable, enter the geographic or demographic focus of your business.
*   **Competitive Advantage (15)**: Describe what makes your business unique.
*   **Annual Revenue (16)**: Choose an approximate range of your yearly revenue. 
*   **Number of Employees (17)**: Enter the current number of employees.
*   **Financial Standing (18)**: Choose the stability of your business's financial standing (e.g., Very Strong, Stable, etc.).
*   **Business References (19)**: Enter the names and contact information of 2-3 professional references.
*   **Partnership Agreement Terms (20)**: If applicable, enter any key terms that are important to you.
*   **Additional Information (21)**: Provide any further information you think is relevant to your application. 

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submission. If you have any difficulties or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
