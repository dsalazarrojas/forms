# Promotion Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotion Partnership Application Form is designed to gather essential information from potential partners interested in promoting our organization. It helps us understand their needs, interests, and capabilities to determine the potential fit for a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic personal details: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Select your preferred partner from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief description of your interest in the partnership and any relevant notes.
4. Enter the name and email of the contact person for the partnership.
5. Provide the contact person's phone number.
6. Enter the start and end dates for the promotion.
7. Enter the location where the promotion will take place.
8. Provide any relevant notes for the contact person.
9. Finally, submit the form and attach any required documentation or tools as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or as it is commonly known to you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or as it is commonly known to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Partner**: Select the partner you are interested in promoting our organization.
* **Partner Note**: Provide any additional information about your partnership interest or needs.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the partnership.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Promotion Description**: Briefly describe your interest in the partnership and any relevant information.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date for the promotion.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date for the promotion.
* **Partner Location**: Enter the location where the promotion will take place.
* **Contact Note**: Provide any additional information for the contact person.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any specific tool or equipment required for the promotion.
* **Output File**: Attach any relevant documentation or output files as requested.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Attach any required documentation or tools as requested.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
