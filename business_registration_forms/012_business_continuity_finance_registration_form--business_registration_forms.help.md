# Business Continuity Finance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from businesses to aid in making informed decisions regarding continuity funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Organization Information** section with your business's details.
2. Provide your **Business Name**, **Business Registration Number**, **Type of Business**, and **Industry Sector**.
3. Enter the date your business was **founded**.
4. Enter the **Primary Contact Person** and their contact information (email and phone number).
5. Fill in the **Annual Revenue**, **Number of Employees**, and **Years in Operation**.
6. Specify the **Loan Amount Requested** and its **Primary Purpose of Funding**.
7. Select the **Timeline for Funding Need** and **Expected Repayment Timeline**.
8. Describe the **Expected Business Impact** of the loan.
9. Identify the **Primary Business Risk** and outline the **Risk Mitigation Plans**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name**: Enter the official name of your business.
- **Business Registration Number**: Enter your tax ID or registration number.
- **Type of Business**: Select the type of business structure (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, or Other).
- **Industry Sector**: Enter the primary business sector.
- **Date Business Established**: Enter the date your business was founded.
- **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of your main contact person.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the business email address.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the business phone number.
- **Annual Revenue**: Enter your approximate annual revenue.
- **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees.
- **Years in Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been established.
- **Loan Amount Requested**: Enter the amount of money needed.
- **Primary Purpose of Funding**: Select the primary purpose of the loan (Working Capital, Equipment Purchase, Facility Expansion, Debt Repayment, Payroll Support, or Operational Support).
- **Timeline for Funding Need**: Select the timeline for the funding need (Immediate, Within 1 Month, Within 3 Months, Within 6 Months).
- **Expected Repayment Timeline**: Select the expected repayment timeline (6 Months, 12 Months, 24 Months, 36 Months, 5+ Years).
- **Expected Business Impact**: Describe how this loan will improve your business continuity.
- **Primary Business Risk**: Identify the primary risk that threatens your business continuity.
- **Risk Mitigation Plans**: Outline the plans to manage the identified risk.
