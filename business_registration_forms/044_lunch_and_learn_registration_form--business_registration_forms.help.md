# Lunch And Learn Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering employees for the company's Lunch And Learn events. Employees can use this form to provide their name, contact information, and schedule a date and time to attend the event. The form is intended to help event organizers gather necessary information from attendees and ensure a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4. Select a date for the event in the "date" field.
5. Choose a time for the event in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the event schedule.
* **email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with event details.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, `number`, required/optional: false): Provide your contact number so that we can reach you if needed.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional: false): Select a date for the event from the available options.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional: false): Choose a time for the event that suits you best.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (although none are marked as required on this form, please fill in all fields for a complete registration).
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact event organizers at your email address provided.
