# Business Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your business and your goals for coaching. The information gathered will help us understand your business context, challenges, and objectives, allowing us to provide personalized coaching to support your growth and success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section carefully and provide accurate information about your business. You can skip sections that are not relevant to you, but please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it is registered.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required): Describe the type of business you operate.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Select the approximate annual revenue range of your business.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, number, required): Enter the current number of employees in your business.
* **Primary Business Challenge** (`primary_challenge`, text, required): Describe the biggest challenge your business is currently facing.
* **Impact of Challenge** (`challenge_impact`, text, required): Explain how this challenge is affecting your business.
* **Timeline of Challenge** (`challenge_duration`, select_one, required): Select the time frame of this challenge.
* **Solutions Already Attempted** (`attempted_solutions`, text, optional): If you have tried any solutions to address this challenge, please describe them.
* **Primary Goal for Coaching** (`primary_goal`, text, required): State the primary goal you want to achieve through coaching.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, text, required): Explain how you will measure success for this goal.
* **Timeline for Goal Achievement** (`timeline_goal`, select_one, required): Select the desired timeline for achieving this goal.
* **Preferred Coaching Format** (`coaching_format`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred format for coaching.
* **Desired Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to meet for coaching sessions.
* **Biggest Support Factor** (`biggest_success_factor`, text, optional): If there's anything specific you need from us, please describe it.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us.
