# regional-accommodation-data-project-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The regional-accommodation-data-project-registration-form is used to register regional accommodation data projects for an organization. This form collects essential details from the contact person, ensuring that the project is accurately recorded and tracked within the project scope.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Organization Name field with the name of your organization.
2.  Enter the Contact Name of the person responsible for this project.
3.  Provide the Contact Phone number for quick communication.
4.  Enter the Organization Email address for official correspondence.
5.  Type the full Address of your project location.
6.  Specify the City where your project is located.
7.  Enter the Region where your project is situated.
8.  Fill in the Zip Code of your project location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (1)**: Enter the official name of your organization.
* **Contact Name (2)**: Provide the name of the person responsible for this project.
* **Contact Phone (3)**: Enter a valid phone number for quick and efficient communication.
* **Organization Email (4)**: Enter the official email address of your organization.
* **Address (5)**: Type the full address where your project is located.
* **City (6)**: Specify the city where your project is situated.
* **Region (7)**: Enter the region where your project is located.
* **Zip Code (8)**: Fill in the zip code of your project location.
