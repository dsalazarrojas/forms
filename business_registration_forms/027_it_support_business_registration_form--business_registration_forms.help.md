# it_support_business_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Support Business Registration Form is designed for businesses to register for IT support services. This form helps the IT support team understand your business needs and provide the right support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as necessary.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Click submit once you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Registration Form** (`id: 1`, `name: business_registration_form`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This is the title of the form. Please do not complete this field.
* **Company Name** (`id: 2`, `name: company_name`, `label: Company Name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of your company.
* **Business Description** (`id: 3`, `name: business_description`, `label: Business Description`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Briefly describe your business.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 4`, `name: contact_email`, `label: Contact Email`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 5`, `name: contact_phone`, `label: Contact Phone`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **IT Support Service** (`id: 6`, `name: it_support_service`, `label: IT Support Service`, `type: select_one`, `options: [IT Support Service, Hardware Support, Software Support]`, `required: false`): Select the type of IT support service you require (e.g., IT Support Service, Hardware Support, Software Support).
* **Service Frequency** (`id: 7`, `name: service_frequency`, `label: Service Frequency`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: [Once, Weekly, Daily, Monthly]`, `required: false`): Choose how often you require IT support services (e.g., Once, Weekly, Daily, Monthly).
* **Contact Person** (`id: 8`, `name: contact_person`, `label: Contact Person`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Address** (`id: 9`, `name: address`, `label: Address`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your business address.
* **City** (`id: 10`, `name: city`, `label: City`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your city.
* **Country** (`id: 11`, `name: country`, `label: Country`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your country.
* **State** (`id: 12`, `name: state`, `label: State`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`id: 13`, `name: zip`, `label: Zip`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your zip code.
* **Company Size** (`id: 14`, `name: company_size`, `label: Company Size`, `type: select_one`, `options: [Small, Medium, Large]`, `required: false`): Choose the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Industry** (`id: 15`, `name: industry`, `label: Industry`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter your industry.
