# retail_tech_integration_partner_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from retail technology integration partners. The collected data will help us understand their integration capabilities, interests, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the requested information. Most fields are optional, so feel free to fill out the ones you're comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Integration Partner** (Text): Please enter the name of your integration partner.
* **Integration Interests** (Select Multiple): Please select the integration platforms you're interested in integrating with. For example, you can choose "Partner API".
* **Consent Given** (Select One): Indicate whether you've given consent to be listed as a integration partner. You can select "Yes" to indicate that you've given consent, "No" to decline, or "Decline" to indicate that you're not sure.
* **Partner Details** (Note): This is a text area where you can provide more details about your integration partner.
* **Email Address** (Email): Please enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Onboarding Process** (Time): Indicate the time it takes for onboarding with your partner.
* **Integration Platforms** (Select Multiple): Select the integration platforms you've integrated with or planning to integrate with. For example, Magento, Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce.
* **Consent Date** (Date): If you've given consent, select the date when you gave it.
* **Consent Time** (Time): If you've given consent, select the time when you gave it.
* **Integration Process** (Select One): Describe how you typically integrate with our platform - manually, automatically, or hybrid.
* **Partner Name** (Text): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Name** (Text): Enter your company's name.
* **Website** (Text): Enter your company's website.
* **Location** (Text): Enter your company's location.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Company Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of your company.
