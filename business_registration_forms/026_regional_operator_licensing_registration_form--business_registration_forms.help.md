# Regional Operator Licensing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Operator Licensing Registration Form is a mandatory form for regional operators to register their businesses and obtain a license to operate. The form collects essential information about the operator, their business, and contact details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your operator name in the "Operator Name" field.
2. Enter your business information in the "Business Info" field (if required).
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
4. Select your operator business type in the "Operator Business Type" field.
5. Enter your license information in the "License Info" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact E-mail" and "Contact Phone" fields.
8. Enter the license number and license expiration date (if applicable).
9. Select the license status (Active or Inactive) in the "License Status" field.
10. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operator Info** (`operator_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter your business name and details.
* **Business Info** (`business_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your business information (if required).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name and contact details.
* **License Info** (`license_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter your license number and details.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required):
	+ Enter your business address.
* **Operator Business Type** (`operator_business_type`, select_one, required):
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have an operator business.
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your operator name.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter your license number.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the license expiration date (if applicable).
* **License Status** (`license_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate your license status.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your contact name.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Business E-mail** (`contact_business_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your contact business email.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information.
