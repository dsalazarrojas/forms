# Affiliate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to become affiliates for a business registration program. It captures necessary information about the applicant, including their personal details, website and promotion methods, and payout setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, and address.
2. Provide your website URL where you plan to promote the business.
3. Share your social media links where you can be reached.
4. Explain how you plan to promote the business.
5. Estimate the size of your audience or followers.
6. Set up your payout information, including your PayPal email address.
7. If applicable, provide your tax ID or SSN for tax reporting purposes.
8. Agree to the Affiliate Program Agreement.
9. Sign to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This will be your account login. Ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete address, including street, city, state, and zip.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of your website where you plan to promote the business.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_profiles`, text, optional): Share links to your social media profiles where you can be reached.
* **How will you promote us?** (`promotion_method`, text, required): Briefly describe how you plan to promote the business.
* **Estimated Audience Size** (`audience_size`, select_one, required): Choose the estimated number of visitors or followers you have.
* **PayPal Email** (`paypal_email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your PayPal account for receiving commissions.
* **Tax ID / SSN** (`tax_id`, text, optional): If you're a US citizen, enter your tax ID or SSN for tax reporting purposes.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, select_one, required): Choose 'I Agree' to accept the Affiliate Program Agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Sign to confirm your registration.
