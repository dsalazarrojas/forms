# Meeting Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Attendance Record Form is designed to record and track attendance at meetings. It collects information about the meeting, including attendee's name, role, meeting duration, subject, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Attendee's Name** in the designated field.
2. Select the **Role** the attendee played in the meeting (e.g. Manager, Employee, or Guest).
3. Enter the **Attendance Date** of the meeting.
4. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the meeting.
5. Enter the **Meeting Duration**, which is the total time the meeting lasted.
6. Enter the **Meeting Subject**, a brief description of the meeting's topic.
7. Select the **Platform Connection** used to hold the meeting (e.g. Zoom, Skype, Google Meet, or None).
8. Optionally, enter **Meeting Notes** to provide additional context about the meeting.
9. Enter the **Attendees Count**, the total number of attendees.
10. Enter the **Attendees List**, a comma-separated list of attendees.
11. Enter any **Manager Comments** or **HR Comments**, if applicable.
12. Finally, select the **Attendance Status** and **Attendance Type** to indicate whether the attendee was in attendance, not in attendance, or not available, and whether it was in-person, virtual, or both.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Attendance Form** (`meeting_attendance_form`): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter the name of the attendee, as they were known to the meeting's host.
* **Role** (`role`): Select the role of the attendee (Manager, Employee, or Guest).
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`): Enter the total time the meeting lasted.
* **Meeting Subject** (`meeting_subject`): Enter a brief description of the meeting's topic.
* **Platform Connection** (`platform_connection`): Select the platform used to connect to the meeting (e.g. Zoom, Skype, Google Meet, or None).
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`): Enter any additional context about the meeting.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`): Enter the total number of attendees.
* **Attendees List** (`attendees_list`): Enter a comma-separated list of attendees.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Enter any comments from the meeting manager.
* **HR Comments** (`hr_comments`): Enter any comments from HR.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`): Select whether the attendee was in attendance, not in attendance, or not available.
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`): Select whether the meeting was in-person, virtual, or both.
