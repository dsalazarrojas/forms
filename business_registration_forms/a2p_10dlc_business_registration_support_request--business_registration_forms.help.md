# A2P 10DLC Business Registration Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request support for A2P 10DLC business registration. Please fill it out to provide the required information for the support team to process your registration efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your business name, which is the legal entity name for registration.
2. Provide your Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Tax ID for registration.
3. If your business has a DBA or brand name different from the business name, enter it here.
4. Enter your contact email address for the registration team, which should be a monitored business email.
5. Enter your contact phone number, which will be used by carriers for verification if needed.
6. Describe how you plan to use messaging services, in 1-3 sentences.
7. Estimate the total number of messages you will send per month.
8. Choose the category that best fits your use case (Marketing, Transactional, Mixed, or Customer care).
9. Select the type of legal entity for your business (Corporation, LLC, Sole Proprietorship, or Non-profit).
10. Choose if assistance with carrier vetting is required.
11. If needed, paste sample message content for review.
12. Select a preferred timeline for registration support (1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, or 1-3 months).
13. If necessary, provide additional technical or compliance requirements.
14. Add internal notes for the support team, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business name**: Enter the official name of your business for registration purposes.
* **EIN / Tax ID**: Provide the numeric identifier used for registration. This could be an Employer Identification Number (EIN) or a Tax ID.
* **DBA or brand name**: If your business uses a different name for branding or public appearance, enter it here.
* **Contact email for registration team**: Enter a business email address that is actively monitored to ensure the support team can contact you effectively.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter a phone number that carriers can use for verification if needed.
* **Primary messaging use case**: Briefly describe how you plan to use messaging services (1-3 sentences).
* **Estimated monthly message volume**: Enter a whole-number estimate of messages you will send per month.
* **Campaign type**: Choose the category that best fits your use case (Marketing, Transactional, Mixed, or Customer care).
* **Brand entity type**: Select the legal type of your business (Corporation, LLC, Sole Proprietorship, or Non-profit).
* **Assistance with carrier vetting**: Choose if you require assistance with carrier-level approvals and documentation.
* **Sample message content**: If required, paste 1-3 examples of typical message content.
* **Preferred timeline**: Select a preferred timeline for registration support (1-2 weeks, 2-4 weeks, or 1-3 months).
* **Additional requirements**: If necessary, describe any additional technical or compliance requirements.
* **Support notes**: Enter internal notes for the support team to reference, if required.
