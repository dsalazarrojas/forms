# Business Customer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for businesses to register with the system, providing contact details, business information, and account preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address to register your personal contact information.
2. Provide your Business Name, Type of Business (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, or Other), Industry, and Years in Business (if applicable).
3. Enter your Business Phone, Business Address, City, State or Province, Zip or Postal Code, and Country of Business.
4. Choose your preferred Account Type (Basic, Professional, Enterprise, or Custom) and Communication Method (Email, Phone, Text Message, or All Methods).
5. Select any Services of Interest (Consulting, Support Services, Training, Software Solutions, or Custom Solutions) that you would like to receive information about.
6. Enter the number of Employees in your business (if applicable).
7. Review all the information provided to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: Your business name.
* **Type of Business**: What type of business are you (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, or Other).
* **Industry**: Your industry sector.
* **Years in Business**: If you have been in business for more than 1 year, please provide the number of years.
* **Business Phone**: Your business phone number.
* **Business Address**: Your physical business location.
* **City**: Your city.
* **State or Province**: Your state or province.
* **Zip or Postal Code**: Your postal code.
* **Country of Business**: The country where your business is located.
* **Account Type**: What type of account do you want (Basic, Professional, Enterprise, or Custom)?
* **Services of Interest**: Select any Services of Interest that you would like to receive information about (Consulting, Support Services, Training, Software Solutions, or Custom Solutions).
* **Number of Employees**: The number of employees in your business (if applicable).
* **Preferred Communication Method**: How would you like us to contact you (Email, Phone, Text Message, or All Methods)?
* **Website**: Your company website (if you have one).
* **Services of Interest**: Select any Services of Interest that you would like to receive information about (Consulting, Support Services, Training, Software Solutions, or Custom Solutions).
