# corporate_partner_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate partner information form is designed to collect essential details from corporate partners, including their name, contact information, and agreement status. This form ensures that all corporate partners are properly registered and informed about the terms of service and privacy policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Select your company type from the "Company Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your industry from the "Industry" field by selecting one or multiple options.
4. Enter your company's address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your company's ZIP code in the "ZIP" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter your company's website in the "Website" field.
9. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.
10. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
11. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
12. Select your role from the "Role" dropdown menu.
13. Enter your fax number in the "Fax" field.
14. Enter the date and time of the agreement in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
15. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.
16. Indicate if the agreement has been signed by selecting "True" in the "Agreement Signed" field.
17. Confirm acceptance of the terms of service by selecting "Terms and Conditions" or "Acceptance of Terms" in the "Terms of Service" field.
18. Confirm acceptance of the privacy policy by selecting "Privacy and Data Security" or "Acceptance of Privacy" in the "Privacy Policy" field.
19. Enter the agreement date in the "Agreement Date" field.
20. Enter the agreement time in the "Agreement Time" field (if applicable).
21. Enter any additional notes about the agreement in the "Agreement Note" field.
22. Enter the date the agreement was signed in the "Agreement Signed Date" field (if applicable).
23. Enter the time the agreement was signed in the "Agreement Signed Time" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Type** (`Company Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of company you represent (Limited, Unlimited, Other).
* **Industry** (`Industry`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple industries related to your company.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **ZIP** (`ZIP`, number, optional): Enter your company's ZIP code.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`Website`, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Role** (`Role`, select_one, optional): Select your role (CEO, COO, Other).
* **Fax** (`Fax`, text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Agreement Signed** (`Agreement Signed`, select_one, required): Indicate if the agreement has been signed.
* **Terms of Service** (`Terms of Service`, select_multiple, required): Confirm acceptance of the terms of service.
* **Privacy Policy** (`Privacy Policy`, select_multiple, required): Confirm acceptance of the privacy policy.
* **Agreement Date** (`Agreement Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`Agreement Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Note** (`Agreement Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (`Agreement Signed Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed Time** (`Agreement Signed Time`, time, optional): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
