# Color Street Wish List - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' personal preferences and interests related to Color Street, allowing the platform to tailor its offerings or services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the form's sections and labels to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in your user information in the "User Information" section.
3. Provide your wishes or preferences in the "Your Top Wishes", "Another Set", "Other Sets", and "Other Wishes" sections.
4. Select your notification preferences for stock updates in the "Notify on Stock" and "Notify me on Stock 2" sections.
5. Choose your birthday month in the "Birthday Month" and "Another Birthday" sections.
6. Review your entries and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Wish List** (`section_wish_intro`, label, required: false): This section provides a brief introduction to the form.
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): This is where you can input your name, email address, or any other relevant user information.
* **Your Top Wishes** (`wish_sets`, text, required: false): Here, you can enter your top wishes or preferences related to Color Street.
* **Another Set** (`wish_sets_2`, text, required: false): Similar to "Your Top Wishes", this section is for entering additional sets of wishes or preferences.
* **Notify on Stock** (`notification`, select_one, required: true): Select "True" if you want to receive notifications when the stock updates, and "False" otherwise.
* **Birthday Month** (`birthday`, select_one, required: false): Choose your birthday month from the list. This might be used to send you gifts or other personalized messages.
* **Additional Wishes** (`additional_wishes`, text, required: false): This section is for entering any additional wishes or preferences you might have.
* **Other Sets** (`other_sets`, text, required: false): Similar to "Your Top Wishes" and "Another Set", this section is for entering more sets of wishes or preferences.
* **Other Styles** (`other_styles`, text, required: false): This section is for entering wishes or preferences related to other styles.
* **Other Wishes** (`other_wishes`, text, required: false): Another section for entering additional wishes or preferences.
* **Notify me on Stock 2** (`notify_on_stock_2`, select_one, required: true): Similar to "Notify on Stock", this is another notification preference option.
* **Another Birthday** (`birthday_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose your birthday month again, which might be used for sending gifts or personalized messages.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Make sure to provide enough information for Color Street to tailor its offerings to your needs.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
