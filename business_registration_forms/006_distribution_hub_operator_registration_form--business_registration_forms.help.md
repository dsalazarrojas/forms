# Distribution Hub Operator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential "Distribution Hub Operators" or "Service Providers" who wish to be registered on the Distribution Hub.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.
3. Submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address 1 Line** (`contact_address_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the first line of the business address.
* **Address 2 Line** (`contact_address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the second line of the business address.
* **Address 3 Line** (`contact_address_3`, text, required/optional): Enter the third line of the business address.
* **Address 4 Line** (`contact_address_4`, text, required/optional): Enter the fourth line of the business address.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of business you are registering (Distribution Hub Operator or Service Provider).
* **Website** (`website`, text, required/optional): Enter the website address of your business.
* **Brief Description** (`business_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Business License** (`business_license`, text, required/optional): Enter the business license number.
* **Business Registration Number** (`business_registration_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the business registration number.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the business location.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Business Address 1** (`business_address_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the first line of the business address.
* **Business Address 2** (`business_address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the second line of the business address.
* **Business Address 3** (`business_address_3`, text, required/optional): Enter the third line of the business address.
* **Business Address 4** (`business_address_4`, text, required/optional): Enter the fourth line of the business address.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit the form and complete the registration process.

Please note that all fields are optional, but it is recommended to fill out as many fields as possible to provide a complete and accurate registration.
