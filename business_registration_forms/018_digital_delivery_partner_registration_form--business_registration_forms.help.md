# Digital Delivery Partner Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from digital delivery partners for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your company's information.
2. Enter your contact person's details.
3. Provide your delivery information, including delivery type, frequency, and vehicle details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, `type: text`): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, `type: text`): Please enter your last name.
* **Company** (id: 3, `type: text`): Please enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person** (id: 4, `type: text`): Please enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (id: 5, `type: email`): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 6, `type: text`): Please enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (id: 7, `type: text`): Please enter your company's name again for verification purposes.
* **Address** (id: 8, `type: text`): Please enter your company's address.
* **City** (id: 9, `type: text`): Please enter the city of your company.
* **State** (id: 10, `type: select_one`): Please select your state from the list.
* **Post Code** (id: 11, `type: text`): Please enter the post code of your company's address.
* **Country** (id: 12, `type: select_one`): Please select your country of operation from the list.
* **Date Registered** (id: 13, `type: date`): Please enter the date your company was registered.
* **Time Registered** (id: 14, `type: time`): Please enter the time your company was registered.
* **Notes** (id: 15, `type: note`): Please enter any additional comments or notes about your company.
* **Delivery Type** (id: 16, `type: select_one`): Please select the type of delivery your company offers from the list.
* **Delivery Area** (id: 17, `type: select_one`): Please select the area of delivery from the list.
* **Delivery Frequency** (id: 18, `type: select_multiple`): Please select the frequency of delivery from the list.
* **Vehicle Type** (id: 19, `type: select_one`): Please select the type of vehicle used for delivery from the list.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (id: 20, `type: text`): Please enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Business License Number** (id: 21, `type: text`): Please enter your business license number.
* **Driver License Number** (id: 22, `type: text`): Please enter your driver's license number.
* **Insurance Details** (id: 23, `type: text`): Please enter your insurance details.
* **Insurance Start Date** (id: 24, `type: date`): Please enter the start date of your insurance.
* **Vehicle Insurance Number** (id: 25, `type: text`): Please enter your vehicle insurance number.
