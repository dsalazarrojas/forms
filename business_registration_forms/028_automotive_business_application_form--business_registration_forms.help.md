# Automotive Business Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about an automotive business applying for registration. The form is divided into sections that cover various aspects of the business, including business information, contact details, business operations, services and inventory, and certifications. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the application process is efficient and successful.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Business Information** section.
    - Enter the **Business Name**, which should match the official name of the business.
    - Enter the **Business License Number**, which is a unique identifier for the business.
    - Enter the **Employer Identification Number (EIN)**, which is a tax identification number assigned to the business.
2.  Move on to the **Business Address** section.
    - Enter the **Street Address**, including the street number and name.
    - Enter the **City**, which should match the city where the business is located.
    - Enter the **State**, which should match the state where the business is located.
    - Enter the **Zip Code**, which is the postal code for the business location.
3.  Complete the **Contact Information** section.
    - Enter the **Owner or Principal Name**, which is the name of the owner or principal of the business.
    - Enter the **Owner Email**, which is the email address of the business owner or principal.
    - Enter the **Owner Phone**, which is the phone number of the business owner or principal.
    - Enter the **Business Phone**, which is the phone number of the business.
4.  Proceed to the **Business Operations** section.
    - Enter the **Years in Business**, which is the number of years the business has been in operation.
    - Enter the **Number of Employees**, which is the number of people employed by the business.
    - Select the **Annual Revenue** range that accurately reflects the business's annual revenue.
5.  If applicable, fill out the **Services and Inventory** section.
    - Select all that apply from the list of services offered by the business.
    - Enter the **Average Vehicle Inventory**, which is an approximate number of vehicles on the lot.
6.  Finally, fill out the **Certifications and Compliance** section.
    - If applicable, enter any **Manufacturer Certifications** the business holds.
7.  Complete the form by entering the **Application Date**, which is the date the application is submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the official name of the business.
* **Business License Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the business.
* **Employer Identification Number (EIN)**: Enter a tax identification number assigned to the business.
* **Street Address**: Enter the business's street address, including street number and name.
* **City**: Enter the city where the business is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where the business is located.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the postal code for the business location.
* **Owner or Principal Name**: Enter the name of the business owner or principal.
* **Owner Email**: Enter the business's email address.
* **Owner Phone**: Enter the business owner or principal's phone number.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the business's phone number.
* **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years the business has been in operation.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the number of people employed by the business.
* **Annual Revenue**: Select the range that accurately reflects the business's annual revenue.
* **Services Offered**: Select all services applicable to the business.
* **Average Vehicle Inventory**: Enter an approximate number of vehicles on the lot.
* **Manufacturer Certifications**: If applicable, enter any certifications held by the business.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date the application is submitted.
