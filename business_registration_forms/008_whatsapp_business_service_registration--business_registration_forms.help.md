# whatsapp_business_service_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses register their services on the WhatsApp platform. It allows you to provide essential information about your business, making it easier for users to find and interact with your service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), and review the available options in the "Permissions" field.
2. Provide your business name, description, and contact details to help users understand your service.
3. Review the "Contact Preferences" field to indicate how you'd like users to interact with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for your business service.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, optional): Write a short description of your business service.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Number** (`business_number`, text, optional): Enter your business number or identifier.
* **Business Service Details** (`business_service_details`, text, optional): Provide a short description of your business service.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`, select multiple, required): Select all the permissions you'd like to grant users:
	+ Allow users to see your business profile
	+ Allow users to see your business contact info
	+ Allow users to chat with your business
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about your clients or customers.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, optional): Indicate how you'd like users to contact you (e.g., email, phone, chat).
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences for your clients or customers.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Review and submit your registration.

## Tips
Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your business service is accurately represented on the platform. If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
