# Business Licensing Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Licensing Approval Form is a document used by businesses or individuals to apply for a license or renew an existing one. It gathers essential information required for the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Your Details**: Fill in your name as the applicant in the "Applicant name" field.
2. **Provide Business Name (if applicable)**: If you are representing a business, enter the business name in the "Business name" field.
3. **Choose License Type**: Select the type of license you are applying for from the "License type" dropdown menu:
	* New license
	* Renewal
	* Amendment
4. **Enter Application Date**: Type in the date you submitted the application in the "Application date" field.
5. **List Supporting Documents**: Indicate the documents you have submitted to support your application in the "Supporting documents" field.
6. **Enter Approver's Name**: If an approver is present, enter their name in the "Approver name" field.
7. **Update Approval Status**: Select the current status of your application from the "Approval status" dropdown menu:
	* Approved
	* Denied
	* Pending
8. **Add Comments (optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes from the reviewer in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as the applicant.
* **Business name** (Text, Optional): If you are representing a business, enter the name of the business.
* **License type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of license you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Application date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Supporting documents** (Text, Optional): List any documents submitted to support your application.
* **Approver name** (Text, Optional): If an approver is present, enter their name.
* **Approval status** (Select One, Required): Select the current status of your application.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes from the reviewer.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above for clarification.
- Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
