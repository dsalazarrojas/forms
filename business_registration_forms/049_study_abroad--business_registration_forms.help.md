# Study Abroad - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a business's registration for the purpose of studying abroad.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure correctness and completeness.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Study Abroad** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the form, which is not required but recommended for contextualization.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the contact person responsible for this registration.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the full name of the company or entity registering.
- **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the company or entity.
- **Date of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, date, required: false): Enter the date the company or entity was registered.
- **Registered By** (`registered_by`, text, required: false): Enter the person or entity responsible for the registration.
- **Registered Person** (`registered_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person registering the company or entity.
- **Agency Details** (`agency_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the agency is involved in the registration process. This field is optional.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Agency License No** (`agency_license_no`, text, required: false): Enter the license number of the agency, if applicable.
- **Agency License Date** (`agency_license_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the agency license was issued.
- **Agency License Status** (`agency_license_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the agency license, which can be 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
- **Agency License Type** (`agency_license_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the agency has a license, which is optional.

Note to self: Be cautious with the date fields as they might have different date formats or requirements. Always check the format and date range before submitting.
