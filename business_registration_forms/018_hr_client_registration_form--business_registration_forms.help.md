# HR Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from HR clients regarding their company and job postings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately, making sure to include as much information as possible.
2. Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to attach any required documentation or supporting materials as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): The official name of your company as per your business registration documents.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): The name of the person who will be our primary contact for this job posting.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): The official address of your company.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required): The type of business your company is (e.g., LLC, Corporation, Partnership, etc.).
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required): The location of your business (city, state, country, etc.).
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): The industry your company operates in.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): A brief description of your company.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_employees`, number, required): The number of employees in your company.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): The URL of your company website.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, required): The URL of your company's website.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): The title of the job you are posting.
* **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): If your job title does not fit into our standard categories, please fill out this field with a description of your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): A detailed description of the job you are posting.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required): The type of job you are posting (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, required): The level of job you are posting (Entry, Mid, Senior, etc.).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): The location of the job you are posting.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, required): The category of the job you are posting (IT, Marketing, Sales, etc.).
* **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, text, required): The industry of the job you are posting.
* **Job Experience** (`job_experience`, text, required): The required experience for the job you are posting.
* **Job Education** (`job_education`, text, required): The required education for the job you are posting.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, text, required): The required skills for the job you are posting.
* **Job References** (`job_references`, text, required): The name and contact information of at least 2 professional references.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, text, required): The status of the job you are posting (Active, Closed, Pending, etc.).
