# Accounting Data Management Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Data Management Registration form is designed to collect information about your business to ensure that we can provide the best possible support and services for your accounting data management needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide the required business information, including company name, contact person name, contact email, and billing preference.
2. Choose the correct industry sector that best describes your business.
3. Answer the questions about data migration and expected data volume.
4. Select the preferred start date for onboarding.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about your business or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal or trading name of your company.
* **Contact person name** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address to be used for invoices and correspondence.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the best phone number for business contact.
* **Business address** (`business_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the headquarters or primary mailing address of your business.
* **Industry sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary industry sector that best describes your business.
* **Number of employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate headcount of employees in your business.
* **Accounting software used** (`accounting_software_used`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and version of the accounting software used by your business.
* **Data migration required** (`data_migration_required`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you need to migrate data from another system.
* **Expected data volume** (`expected_data_volume`, `text`, optional): Enter the number of years of records and file types to be managed.
* **Preferred start date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the preferred start date for onboarding.
* **Security and compliance notes** (`security_and_compliance_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any regulatory or compliance requirements for your business.
* **Billing preference** (`billing_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the invoicing frequency and payment method for your services.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes to help us prepare for your onboarding.

Note: Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide the best possible support and services for your business. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
