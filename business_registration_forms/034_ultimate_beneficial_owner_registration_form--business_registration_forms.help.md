# Ultimate Beneficial Owner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the beneficial owner of a business or organization for regulatory compliance and due diligence purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your information in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If you are unsure about any information, please seek guidance from an authorized representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Beneficial Owner** (`beneficial_owner`, text, required): Enter the name of the beneficial owner.
* **Owner Details** (`owner_details`, note, optional): This field is not required but can be used to provide additional information about the beneficial owner.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the beneficial owner.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_one, required): Select the nationality of the beneficial owner from the available options (US, Canada, Other).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the beneficial owner.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select the country of residence of the beneficial owner from the available options (US, Canada, Other).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the beneficial owner.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the beneficial owner.
* **Beneficial Owner Type** (`beneficial_owner_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of beneficial owner (Individual, Organization).
* **Beneficial Owner Status** (`beneficial_owner_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the beneficial owner (Active, Dormant, Inactive).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact an authorized representative for assistance.
