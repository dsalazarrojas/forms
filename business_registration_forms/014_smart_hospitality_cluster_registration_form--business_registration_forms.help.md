# Smart Hospitality Cluster Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Hospitality Cluster Registration Form is designed to provide essential information for businesses to register themselves as part of a smart hospitality cluster. This includes gathering business information, such as business type, company details, contact information, and interest areas of operation, to support cluster operations, networking, and potential collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. **Business Information**
	* The "Business Information" section asks you to provide the type of business you are (Hospitality, Tech Provider, or Tourism Organization). Please select the option that best describes your business type.
2. **Business Type**
	* This field allows you to select your business type (Hospitality, Tech Provider, or Tourism Organization). This will help the cluster understand your business category.
3. **Company Details**
	* Provide a brief description of your company, such as its name and any other relevant details.
4. **Name**
	* Enter your business's name as it appears on your business card or branding materials.
5. **Website**
	* Add your company's website address for easy access to your online presence.
6. **Email**
	* Enter your company's email address for direct contact.
7. **Phone**
	* Add your company's phone number for easy contact.
8. **Interest Areas**
	* Select the areas of interest your business operates in (Hospitality, Tech Provider, or Tourism). This will help the cluster understand your areas of specialization.
9. **Note**
	* Add any additional notes or comments about your business or its operations.
10. **Date**
	* Choose the date for your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Type**: The business type field allows you to select your business category (Hospitality, Tech Provider, or Tourism Organization).
* **Company Details**: This field is for entering a brief description of your company.
* **Name**: Enter your business's name as it appears on your business card or branding materials.
* **Website**: Add your company's website address.
* **Email**: Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone**: Add your company's phone number.
* **Interest Areas**: Select the areas of interest your business operates in (Hospitality, Tech Provider, or Tourism).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your business or its operations.
* **Date**: Choose the date for your registration.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your registration carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the cluster administrators.
