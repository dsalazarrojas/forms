# Smart POS Integration Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart POS Integration Trial Registration form is designed for businesses interested in exploring the smart POS integration trial. This form collects necessary information about your business and its POS system to help facilitate the trial process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand each section.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, if applicable.
3. Provide accurate and truthful information.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details (<business_details>**): 
	+ This field is used to collect essential information about your business. Please enter your company name, contact person's name, and email address.
* **POS System Info (<pos_system_info>**): 
	+ In this field, describe your POS system's key features, such as its type, version, and any notable integrations.
* **Consent (<consent>**): 
	+ Please indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the smart POS integration trial by selecting "I agree" from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Be thorough in your responses to ensure the trial process can proceed efficiently.
* If you're unsure about any information required in the form, contact the support team for clarification.
* This form is designed to help us better understand your business and POS system, which will aid the trial process.
