# Retail Blockchain Partnership Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Retail Blockchain Partnership Registration" form is designed to collect information from potential partners interested in collaborating with your retail blockchain network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the questions about retail blockchain partnership interest, partner type, lead source, and agreement.
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the relevant fields.
3. Provide your phone number and company name as requested.
4. Add any additional notes about your partnership interest (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retail Blockchain Partnership Interest** (`retail_blockchain_partnership_interest`): Check "Yes" if you are interested in partnering with our retail blockchain network.
* **Retail Blockchain Partner Type** (`retail_blockchain_partner_type`): Check all that apply to describe the type of partner you are (e.g., Retailer, Wholesaler, etc.).
* **Retail Blockchain Lead Source** (`retail_blockchain_lead_source`): Check all that apply to describe how you came across our partnership opportunity (e.g., Online Search, Referral, etc.).
* **Retail Blockchain Lead Qualification** (`retail_blockchain_lead_qualification`): Check "Yes" if you have already qualified as a lead for our partnership program.
* **Retail Blockchain Contact First Name** (`retail_blockchain_contact_first_name`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Retail Blockchain Contact Last Name** (`retail_blockchain_contact_last_name`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Retail Blockchain Contact Email** (`retail_blockchain_contact_email`): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Retail Blockchain Contact Phone** (`retail_blockchain_contact_phone`): Enter your phone number as it appears on your official documents.
* **Retail Blockchain Contact Company** (`retail_blockchain_contact_company`): Enter your company name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Retail Blockchain Notes** (`retail_blockchain_notes`): Add any additional information about your partnership interest (optional).
* **Retail Blockchain Agreement** (`retail_blockchain_agreement`): Check "Yes" to agree to our terms of use and disclaimers.
* **Retail Blockchain Terms of Use** (`retail_blockchain_terms_of_use`): This field is for our internal reference only.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are completed before submitting the form.
