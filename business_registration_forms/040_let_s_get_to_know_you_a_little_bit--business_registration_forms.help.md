# Lets_Get_To_Know_You_A_Little_Bit - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect business registration information from users. It is essential to fill it out accurately and comprehensively to ensure that the business information is up to date and correct.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please start by providing contact details and type of business.
- Fill in the name of the business, address, and phone number.
- Enter the email addresses of the owner and the business.
- Fill in the owner's name, title, and personal description.
- Provide the owner's email address, phone number, date of birth, driver's license number, and other required information.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Contact Details**: Enter the business address. <br>
  This field is used to enter the business address.
  (id: 4, name: business_address, label: Business Address, type: text)

- **Type of Business**: Select the type of business from the options provided. <br>
  This field is used to determine the type of business.
  (id: 2, name: business_type, label: Type of Business, type: select_multiple)

- **Name of Business**: Enter the name of the business. <br>
  This field is used to enter the business name.
  (id: 3, name: name_of_business, label: Name of Business, type: text)

- **Phone Number**: Enter the business phone number. <br>
  This field is used to enter the business phone number.
  (id: 5, name: phone_number, label: Phone Number, type: text)

- **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the owner of the business. <br>
  This field is used to enter the owner's name.
  (id: 7, name: owner_name, label: Owner Name, type: text)

- **Owner Title**: Enter the title of the owner of the business. <br>
  This field is used to enter the owner's title.
  (id: 8, name: owner_title, label: Owner Title, type: text)

- **Business Description**: Enter a description of the business. <br>
  This field is used to enter the business description.
  (id: 9, name: business_description, label: Business Description, type: text)

- **Owner's Email**: Enter the email address of the owner of the business. <br>
  This field is used to enter the owner's email address.
  (id: 10, name: owner_email, label: Owner Email, type: email)

- **Owner Phone**: Enter the owner's phone number. <br>
  This field is used to enter the owner's phone number.
  (id: 11, name: owner_phone, label: Owner Phone, type: text)

- **Owner's Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the owner of the business. <br>
  This field is used to enter the owner's date of birth.
  (id: 12, name: owner_dob, label: Owner's Date of Birth, type: date)

- **Owner's Driver License Number**: Enter the owner's driver's license number. <br>
  This field is used to enter the owner's driver's license number.
  (id: 13, name: owner_license, label: Owner's Driver License Number, type: text)

- **Business License Number**: Enter the business license number. <br>
  This field is used to enter the business license number.
  (id: 14, name: business_license, label: Business License Number, type: text)

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Use a valid email address format for the owner's and business's email addresses.
- Enter dates in the format of YYYY-MM-DD for the date of birth.
- Enter phone numbers in the format of (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
