# 12 Minute Affiliate System Marketing System - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 12 Minute Affiliate System Marketing System is a platform designed to help individuals interested in online marketing and affiliate systems. This guide will walk you through the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, and mailing address.
2. Next, provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Choose your preferred day of availability for onboarding.
4. Select your experience level with online marketing and choose the primary marketing channels you plan to use.
5. Select your preferred payment method for fees.
6. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions and consent to receive marketing communications.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments or notes that will help tailor your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### --- Personal Information ---

* **Full name** (full_name): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Date of birth** (date_of_birth): Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Age** (age): This field will be auto-calculated based on the date of birth.
* **Mailing address** (mailing_address): Include street, city, state, and ZIP.

### --- Contact Information ---

* **Email address** (email_address): We will send confirmation and updates to this address.
* **Phone number** (phone_number): Include country code, e.g., +1 555 123 4567.

### --- Availability ---

* **Preferred day for onboarding** (availability_day): Select the day you are most available.

### --- Experience & Channels ---

* **Experience level with online marketing** (experience_level): Choose the level that best describes you.
* **Primary marketing channels** (marketing_channels): Select all that apply.
* **Preferred start date** (preferred_start_date): Date you would like to begin training.

### --- Technical Setup ---

* **Preferred level of technical involvement** (technical_preference): Select the option that best fits your comfort level.
* **Do you have an existing website?** (has_website): If yes, provide the URL below.
* **Website URL** (website_url): Include full URL, e.g., https://example.com.
* **Preferred payment method for fees** (payment_method): Select one payment option.
* **Terms and Conditions** (agreement_terms): You must agree to proceed.
* **Marketing Consent** (consent_marketing): Consent to receive marketing communications.
* **Additional comments or notes** (additional_comments): Provide any extra information to help tailor your experience.
