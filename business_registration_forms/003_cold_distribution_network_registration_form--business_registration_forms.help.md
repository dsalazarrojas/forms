# Cold Distribution Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cold Distribution Network Registration Form is designed to gather information about your company's services and capabilities in the cold distribution industry. This form helps us understand your organization's strengths and weaknesses, which will be used to assess your suitability for our network.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please make sure you have all required information and permissions. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of your company. Please provide accurate and complete information for each field to ensure a thorough evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Provide the legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Choose your organization's category, such as Logistics Provider, Manufacturer, or Distributor.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of your main contact person for registration.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide your direct phone number for urgent communications.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (`company_city`, text, required): Enter the city location of your organization.
* **State or Province** (`company_state`, text, required): Enter the state, province, or region of your operations.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`company_zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`company_country`, select_one, required): Choose your country of operation from the list.
* **Cold Chain Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you provide, such as Cold Storage, Refrigerated Transport, or Last Mile Delivery.
* **Total Cold Storage Capacity** (`storage_capacity`, text, required): Enter your capacity in cubic feet or meters.
* **Temperature Range Capability** (`temperature_range`, select_multiple, required): Choose the temperature ranges you can maintain, such as Frozen - Below 0°F or Controlled - 50°F to 60°F.
* **Geographic Coverage Area** (`coverage_area`, select_multiple, required): Select the regions you serve, such as Local, Regional, or International.
* **Refrigerated Vehicle Fleet Size** (`vehicle_fleet_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of refrigerated vehicles you own.
* **Vehicle Types Available** (`vehicle_types`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of refrigerated vehicles you have available, such as Small Van or Straight Truck.
* **Industry Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select the certifications you hold, such as HACCP or FDA Registered.
* **Temperature Monitoring Systems** (`monitoring_systems`, select_multiple, required): Choose the systems you use for tracking, such as Real-Time GPS Tracking or Data Loggers.
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, select_one, required): Select your business hours, such as 24/7 or Business Hours Only.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide your 24-hour emergency contact number.
* **Years in Cold Chain Operation** (`years_in_operation`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have been operating in the cold chain industry.
* **Insurance Coverage Level** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, required): Select your liability coverage amount.
* **Can You Provide References?** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if you can provide previous client references.
* **Additional Services Offered** (`additional_services`, text, optional): Enter any other services you provide not listed in the form.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any other information about your company you would like to share.

## Tips
Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field to ensure a thorough evaluation of your company's capabilities. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us directly.
