# Mining Operations Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register new mining operations, capturing relevant information about the site and operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready.
2.  Complete the **Registration Details** section, which includes providing the required information about the mining operation.
3.  In the **Site Registration** section, enter the site registration number as required.
4.  In the **Site Documents** section, upload any relevant documents related to the site.
5.  In the **Operational Info** section, provide any additional information about the operation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details**: This section is for entering general information about the mining operation, including the operation title, which is the name of the mining operation.
* **Site Registration**: This section is for entering the site registration number, which is a unique number assigned to the mining site.
* **Site Documents**: This section is for uploading any relevant documents related to the mining site, such as permits and licenses.
* **Operational Info**: This section is for providing any additional information about the mining operation, such as the location, type of mining, and other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek guidance from the mining industry experts.
* If you need to submit multiple mining operations, please submit each operation separately and ensure that each form is filled out accurately and completely.
