# Hiring Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Event Registration Form is used to gather business information and contact details for businesses participating in a hiring event. This form helps us understand your business profile, plans, and contact information to facilitate the event's smooth execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Business Information page.
2.  Enter your business profile details.
3.  Provide information about your workforce.
4.  Add your business plans.
5.  Enter your contact information.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Information**: This is the first section of the form where you will enter general details about your business. Please provide a brief description of your company.
* **Business Profile**: In this field, please write a detailed description of your business, including its history, mission, and goals.
* **Workforce**: Describe your workforce details, including the number of employees and their job roles.
* **Business Plans**: Outline your business plans and goals, including short and long-term objectives.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your company's address, contact name, and contact number.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your business.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business's phone number.
