# TM Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for companies to register their trademarks. To fill out the form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct pages by navigating through the form's pages (page_1, page_2, page_2_data, page_3, etc.).
2. Fill out the company information on page_1. Provide your company's name, address, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields. 
3. On page_2, enter the trademark information. Fill in the trademark name, description, type, class, and status.
4. Choose the correct trademark type, class, and country from the available options.
5. Upload an image of the trademark if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it is officially recognized.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Provide your company's street address, state, and zip code.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text): Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter your company's official email address.
* **Trademark Name** (`trademark_name`, text): Enter the name of the trademark you are registering.
* **Trademark Description** (`trademark_description`, text): Provide a brief description of the trademark.
* **Trademark Type** (`trademark_type`, select_one, required): Choose the correct type for your trademark from the available options (wordmark, logomark, combination mark, certification mark).
* **Trademark Class** (`trademark_class`, select_one, required): Select the correct class for your trademark from the available options.
* **Trademark Country** (`trademark_country`, select_one): Choose the country where the trademark is registered.
* **Trademark Date** (`trademark_date`, date): Enter the date of the trademark registration.
* **Trademark Status** (`trademark_status`, select_one): Choose the status of your trademark from the available options (active, inactive, cancelled).
* **Trademark Image** (`trademark_image`, note, required): This field is for uploading an image of the trademark. Please note that this field is not required but is recommended for a clearer representation of the trademark.
* **Upload Image** (`trademark_image_file`, text): Upload an image file of the trademark.
