# limited_liability_company_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Limited Liability Company Agreement Form is designed to collect necessary information for the setup of a Limited Liability Company (LLC). This form will guide you through the process of providing details about your LLC's key attributes, including its name, address, members, management structure, and financial details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary permissions and information required to complete this form.
2. Fill in the following information:
   * Enter the date of the LLC agreement.
   * Provide the name of your LLC.
   * Enter the address of your LLC.
   * Enter the city, state, and ZIP of your LLC's location.
   * Provide the name, email, and phone number of the first LLC member.
   * Enter the title of the first LLC member.
   * Provide the name, email, and phone number of the second LLC member.
   * Enter the title of the second LLC member.
   * Enter the percentage of ownership for each member.
   * Determine the management structure of your LLC (Member 1, Member 2, or Member 3).
   * Decide on profit distribution for each member.
   * Choose the members who will be signing the agreement.
   * Enter the manager's name, address, phone number, email, and title.
   * Optionally, add any additional notes regarding the LLC agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the LLC agreement. This date is crucial for reference and legal purposes.
* **LLC Name** (`llc_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your Limited Liability Company. This name should reflect the company's identity and branding.
* **LLC Address** (`llc_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where your LLC operates from or plans to operate from.
* **City, State ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the city, state, and ZIP of your LLC's location.
* **Member 1 Name** (`member_1_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the first LLC member. This could be a person or an entity.
* **Member 1 Email** (`member_1_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the first LLC member.
* **Member 1 Phone** (`member_1_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the first LLC member.
* **Member 1 Title** (`member_1_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or position of the first LLC member.
* **Member 2 Name** (`member_2_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of the second LLC member.
* **Member 2 Email** (`member_2_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the second LLC member.
* **Member 2 Phone** (`member_2_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the second LLC member.
* **Member 2 Title** (`member_2_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or position of the second LLC member.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `text`, required): Enter any additional or specific terms of the LLC agreement.
* **Member 1 Ownership Percentage** (`member_1_ownership_percentage`, `number`, required): Enter the ownership percentage of the first LLC member.
* **Member 2 Ownership Percentage** (`member_2_ownership_percentage`, `number`, required): Enter the ownership percentage of the second LLC member.
* **LLC Management Structure** (`llc_management_structure`, `select_one`, required): Decide on the management structure of your LLC from Member 1, Member 2, or Member 3 options.
* **Profit Distribution** (`profit_distribution`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how the profits will be distributed among the LLC members.
* **Agreement Signatures** (`agreement_signatures`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the members who will be signing the agreement.
* **Manager** (`manager`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's name for the LLC.
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, `text`, required): Enter the manager's title or position.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any other details or notes about the LLC agreement that are not included in the above fields.

By following this guide, you will be able to fill in the required information and create a comprehensive Limited Liability Company (LLC) agreement form.
