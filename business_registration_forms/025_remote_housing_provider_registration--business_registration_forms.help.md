# Remote Housing Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for remote housing providers to register themselves with the organization. It is necessary to provide accurate and truthful information for all fields to process your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct provider type from the options provided.
2.  Fill in your name and address information.
3.  Enter your city, state, and zip code.
4.  Provide a valid email address.
5.  Enter your phone number.
6.  Upload any necessary documents.
7.  Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the "I confirm that I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" option.
8.  Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have uploaded an agreement.
9.  Select "Yes" or "No" to submit or cancel the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Type**: This field is used to select the type of provider you are. Please choose "University", "Agency" or "Housing Coordinator" from the options.
* **Name**: This field is used to provide your name.
* **Address**: This field is used to provide your address.
* **City**: This field is used to provide your city.
* **State**: This field is used to provide your state.
* **Zip**: This field is used to provide your zip code.
* **Email**: This field is used to provide a valid email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to provide your phone number.
* **Document Upload**: This field is used to upload any necessary documents.
* **Agreement Upload**: This field is used to upload any necessary agreement documents.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is used to read and agree to the terms and conditions. Please select "I confirm that I have read and agree to the terms and conditions".
* **Confirm Terms**: This field is used to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: This field is used to submit the form. Please select "Yes" to submit the form.
* **Cancel**: This field is used to cancel the form. Please select "Yes" to cancel the form.
* **Provider Agreement**: This field is used to confirm that you are aware that you must provide accurate and truthful information for all fields. Please select "I am aware that I must provide accurate and truthful information for all fields".

Note: If you have any issues with completing this form, please contact the organization for assistance.
