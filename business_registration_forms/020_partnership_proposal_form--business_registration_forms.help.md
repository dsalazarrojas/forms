# Partnership Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Proposal Form is used to collect partnership information from businesses interested in collaborating with our company. It helps us understand the business and partner's details, terms, and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your partnership proposal details accurately and completely.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk *).
3. Select the correct options for partnership terms and conditions.
4. Check the agreement boxes to agree with the terms and conditions.
5. Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the partner or business.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the partner or business. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the partner or business. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the partner's business.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the partner's business.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the partner's business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the physical address of the partner's business.
* **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required/optional): Describe your business in 1-2 sentences.
* **Partner Description** (`partner_description`, text, required/optional): Describe your role in the partnership in 1-2 sentences.
* **Partnership Terms and Conditions** (`partnership_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you agree to the partnership terms and conditions.
* **Partner Agreement** (`partner_agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check to agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Business Agreement** (`business_agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check to agree with the terms and conditions.

## Tips

- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
- Be honest and transparent in your partnership proposal.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
