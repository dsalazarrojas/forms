# Business Trade License Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Trade License Registration Form is a mandatory document for businesses to register for trade licenses. This form is used to collect information about the business, including its name, type, and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information about your business.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Review the form for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, Text, Required): Enter the official business name.
* **Trade Name or DBA** (`trade_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the alternative name used by the business, if different from the official name.
* **Type of License Needed** (`license_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of license your business requires from the options provided.
* **Primary Business Activity** (`primary_activity`, Select One, Required): Choose the main type of business or trade your business engages in.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of what your business does.
* **Owner First Name** (`owner_first_name`, Text, Required): Enter the first name of the primary owner of the business.
* **Owner Last Name** (`owner_last_name`, Text, Required): Enter the last name of the primary owner of the business.
* **Social Security Number** (`owner_ssn`, Text, Optional): Enter the Social Security number of the primary owner for identification purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`owner_date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of birth of the primary owner.
* **Citizenship or Nationality Status** (`owner_nationality`, Select One, Required): Choose the citizenship status of the primary owner from the options provided.
* **Business Street Address** (`business_address`, Text, Required): Enter the physical location of the business.
* **City** (`business_city`, Text, Required): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **State or Province** (`business_state`, Text, Required): Enter the state or province where the business is located.
* **Postal Code** (`business_postal_code`, Text, Required): Enter the ZIP code of the business.
* **Business Phone Number** (`business_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the contact phone number of the business.
* **Business Email Address** (`business_email`, Email, Required): Enter the contact email of the business.
* **Expected Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, Number, Optional): Enter an estimate of the number of employees the business expects to have.
* **Business Start Date** (`startup_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the business will start operating.
* **Business Legal Structure** (`business_structure`, Select One, Required): Choose the legal structure of the business from the options provided.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_information`, Select One, Required): Check whether the business has or plans to obtain business liability insurance.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or contact support for assistance.
