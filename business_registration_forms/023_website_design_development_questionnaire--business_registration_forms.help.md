# Website Design & Development Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your business for website design and development services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each field carefully and provide accurate information for your business.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as such.
3. Select options from dropdown menus where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is the name of your business? (Page 1)**
  * **Business Name**: The name of your business.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with your business name.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What category does your business fit into? (Page 1)**
  * **Business Category**: Select the category that best describes your business.
  * **Type: select_one**: Choose one option from the list.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **Describe your business briefly (Page 1)**
  * **Business Description**: Provide a short description of your business.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What is your email address? (Page 2)**
  * **Contact Email**: Enter your business email address.
  * **Type: email**: Enter a valid email address.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What is your phone number? (Page 2)**
  * **Contact Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with your phone number.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What is the URL of your website? (Page 2)**
  * **Company Website**: Enter the URL of your business website.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with your website URL.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What is the address of your business? (Page 3)**
  * **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with your business address.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What hours does your business operate? (Page 3)**
  * **Business Hours**: Enter your business operating hours.
  * **Type: time**: Enter a time string in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **Do you have a social media profile? (Page 4)**
  * **Social Media Profile**: Select a social media platform your business is on.
  * **Type: select_one**: Choose one option from the list.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **What is the link to your social media profile? (Page 4)**
  * **Social Media Profile Link**: Enter the link to your social media profile.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with your social media profile link.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **Do you have a profile picture for your business? (Page 4)**
  * **Company Profile Picture**: Select whether you have a profile picture.
  * **Type: select_one**: Choose one option from the list.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **Is there any other information you'd like to share about your business? (Page 4)**
  * **Other Info**: Provide additional information about your business.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with any additional information.
  * **Required: true**: This field is required.

- **Social Media Profile 2 (Page 5)**
  * **Social Media Profile 2**: Select another social media platform your business is on.
  * **Type: select_one**: Choose one option from the list.
  * **Required: false**: This field is optional.

- **Social Media Profile Link 2 (Page 5)**
  * **Social Media Profile Link 2**: Enter the link to your social media profile 2.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with your social media profile link 2.
  * **Required: false**: This field is optional.

- **Company Profile Picture 2 (Page 5)**
  * **Company Profile Picture 2**: Select whether you have a profile picture for your business 2.
  * **Type: select_one**: Choose one option from the list.
  * **Required: false**: This field is optional.

- **Other Info 2 (Page 5)**
  * **Other Info 2**: Provide additional information about your business 2.
  * **Type: text**: Enter a text string with any additional information.
  * **Required: false**: This field is optional.
