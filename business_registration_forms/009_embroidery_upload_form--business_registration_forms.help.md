# Embroidery Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Embroidery Upload Form is used to provide information about an embroidery upload process for a business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the customer and upload process.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and detailed responses for each field.
3. Once you have completed the form, review your responses for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: Enter the customer's details, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Upload Details (2)**: Provide information about the file being uploaded, including the file name, size, and any other relevant details.
* **File Status (3)**: Enter the current status of the file, including any errors or issues encountered during the upload process.
* **User Information (4)**: Enter your own contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Uploaded File Information (5)**: Enter details about the uploaded file, including its name, size, and any other relevant information.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
- If you encounter any issues during the upload process, refer to the "File Status" field for further information.
