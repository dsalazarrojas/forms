# Wholesale Account Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wholesale Account Application Form is a necessary step for wholesale customers to provide their company information to create a new account with us. This form helps us understand your business and verify your identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your company's information, including the name, address, and contact details.
* Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
* Specify your company type, such as wholesale, distribution, or retail.
* Describe your business and products/services offered.
* Enter your date of establishment, if applicable.
* Add your CEO's name, position, and contact information.
* Enter your CEO's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Your company's name as it appears on your business license or registration.
* **Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Your company's address as it appears on your business license or registration.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address associated with your company. This is how we'll contact you about your account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your company's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): The person responsible for your company's account.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, text, optional): The type of company you operate (e.g., wholesale, distribution, retail).
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Your company's website (if applicable).
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): The industry your company operates in.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A brief description of your company and products/services offered.
* **Date of Establishment** (`date_of_establishment`, date, optional): The date your company was established (if applicable).
* **CEO Name** (`ceo_name`, text, optional): The name of your CEO or highest-ranking officer.
* **CEO Position** (`ceo_position`, text, optional): The position held by your CEO or highest-ranking officer.
* **Contact Info** (`ceo_contact_info`, text, optional): The contact information for your CEO or highest-ranking officer.
* **CEO Email** (`ceo_email`, email, optional): The email address of your CEO or highest-ranking officer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* We'll review and verify your application within a few working days. You'll receive an email notification once your account is created or if we need more information.
* If you've already registered with us, please log in with your existing account credentials instead of creating a new one.
