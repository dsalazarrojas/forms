# Solar Business Partnership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Business Partnership Form is a tool to collect business partnership information from users. It is designed to gather essential details about a business to be listed on a directory or platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Business Details" section with your business's general information, including its name and address.
2. Add your business's phone number and email address in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Provide system details and system name in the "System Information" section.
4. Review and submit the form when all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Details** (`business_details`): Enter your business's name and address.
- **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your business.
- **Business Description** (`business_description`): Provide a brief description of your business.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your business's physical address.
- **Business Phone** (`business_phone`): Enter your business's phone number.
- **Business Email** (`business_email`): Enter your business's email address.
- **System Details** (`system_details`): Describe the solar systems or equipment your business deals with.
- **System Name** (`system_name`): Enter the name of the solar system or equipment you specialize in.
- **System Description** (`system_description`): Describe the solar systems or equipment you provide.
- **System Address** (`system_address`): Enter the address of where your solar system or equipment is located.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure successful submission.
- Double-check the information provided for accuracy and completeness.
- If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
