# Business Setup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses ensure they have completed all necessary steps to set up their company. It covers various aspects of business setup, including registration, licensing, insurance, and other important tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. The options available for each field are:

*   Completed: I have completed this task.
*   In Progress: This task is ongoing, and I will complete it soon.
*   Not Started: I have not yet begun this task.
*   Not Applicable: This task does not apply to my business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name.
- **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the structure of your business.
