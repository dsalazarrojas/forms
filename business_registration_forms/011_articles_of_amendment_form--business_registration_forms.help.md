# Articles Of Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record changes made to a business's structure, articles of incorporation, or other relevant documents. It is intended to ensure accuracy in official records and provide evidence of changes made to a company's identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the current business information in the relevant fields.
2. Select the type of amendment you are making (e.g., Name Change, Address Change, etc.).
3. Provide the effective date of the amendment.
4. Explain the reason for the amendment.
5. If applicable, specify the changes being made to the business (e.g., previous and new information).
6. Enter the authorized representative's name, title, and contact information.
7. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
8. Select the method of submission (e.g., online, mail, etc.).
9. Date and sign the form, confirming that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (Business Name, text, required): Enter the current legal name of the business.
* **Business Type** (Select One, required): Choose the type of business (e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, etc.).
* **State of Incorporation or Organization** (Text, required): Enter the state where the business was originally formed.
* **Business Registration or License Number** (Text, required): Enter the state-issued business identification number.
* **Type of Amendment** (Select One, required): Choose the type of amendment being made (e.g., Name Change, Address Change, etc.).
* **Effective Date of Amendment** (Date, required): Enter the date when the amendment should take effect.
* **Reason for Amendment** (Text, required): Provide a brief explanation of why the amendment is needed.
* **Previous Information** (Text, required): If applicable, enter the information being changed from.
* **New Information** (Text, required): Enter the new information being changed to.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the person authorizing the amendment.
* **Representative Title** (Text, required): Enter the representative's position in the business.
* **Representative Phone Number** (Text, required): Enter the representative's contact phone number.
* **Representative Email Address** (Email, required): Enter the representative's email address for correspondence.
* **Board or Ownership Approval Status** (Select One, required): Confirm if the amendment has been approved by the Board of Directors, Members/Shareholders, Managers, or if no approval is required.
* **Date of Approval** (Date, optional): If applicable, enter the date the amendment was approved.
* **Supporting Documents Attached** (Select One, required): Confirm if supporting documents are attached (e.g., board resolutions, meeting minutes, etc.).
* **Filing Method** (Select One, optional): Choose how the amendment will be submitted (e.g., online, mail, etc.).
* **I Certify the information provided is accurate** (Select One, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Signature** (Date, required): Enter the date the authorized representative signs the form.
