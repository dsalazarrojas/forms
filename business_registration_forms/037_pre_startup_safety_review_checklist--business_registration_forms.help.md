# Pre Startup Safety Review Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Startup Safety Review Checklist is designed to evaluate the safety readiness of a workplace prior to start-up. This form helps ensure that you have taken all necessary measures to protect employees and the workplace from potential hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully, selecting "True" or "False" based on the current state of your workplace.
2. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Provide clear and concise answers.
4. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for support or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Ensure your workplace is safe before you start operations** (pre_start_up_safety_review)
	+ This field indicates whether your workplace is safe for employees and visitors.
* **Ensure your workplace is safe** (ensure_workplace_safety)
	+ Please answer "True" if your workplace is safe, and "False" otherwise.
* **Have all employees received safety training** (employee_safety_training)
	+ Answer "True" if all employees have received the required safety training, and "False" otherwise. You can select multiple options from the list.
* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) is available** (employee_personal_protective_equipment)
	+ Select "True" if PPE is readily available in your workplace, and "False" otherwise.
* **Safety manuals and procedures are available** (employee_safety_manuals)
	+ Answer "True" if safety manuals and procedures are accessible to employees, and "False" otherwise.
* **Fire suppression systems** (fire_suppression_systems)
	+ Select "True" if your workplace has a fire suppression system, and "False" otherwise.
* **Emergency exit routes** (emergency_exit_routes)
	+ Answer "True" if emergency exit routes are clearly marked and accessible, and "False" otherwise.
* **Fire extinguishers are available** (fire_extinguishers)
	+ Select "True" if fire extinguishers are readily available in your workplace, and "False" otherwise.
* **Fire drills have been conducted** (fire_drills)
	+ Answer "True" if fire drills have been conducted, and "False" otherwise.
* **Electrical hazard procedures** (electrical_hazard_procedures)
	+ Select "True" if electrical hazard procedures are in place, and "False" otherwise.
* **Spill response procedures** (spill_response_procedures)
	+ Answer "True" if spill response procedures are in place, and "False" otherwise.
* **Chemical hazards** (chemical_hazards)
	+ Select "True" if chemical hazards are identified and mitigated, and "False" otherwise.
* **Electrical hazard training** (electrical_hazard_training)
	+ Answer "True" if electrical hazard training is provided, and "False" otherwise.
* **Emergency contact info** (emergency_contact_info)
	+ Please provide the contact information for emergency responders.
* **Fire alarm systems** (fire_alarm_systems)
	+ Select "True" if fire alarm systems are in place, and "False" otherwise.
* **Fire extinguisher location** (fire_extinguisher_location)
	+ Answer "True" if fire extinguishers are located in accessible areas, and "False" otherwise.
* **First aid kits** (first_aid_kits)
	+ Select "True" if first aid kits are readily available, and "False" otherwise.
* **Exit routes** (exit_routes)
	+ Answer "True" if exit routes are clearly marked and accessible, and "False" otherwise.
* **Contact information for emergency responders** (emergency_contact_info)
	+ Please provide the contact information for emergency responders.
* **Evacuation plan** (evacuation_plan)
	+ Select "True" if an evacuation plan is in place, and "False" otherwise.
* **Evacuation routine** (evacuation_routine)
	+ Answer "True" if an evacuation routine is in place, and "False" otherwise.
* **Emergency equipment availability** (emergency_equipment)
	+ Select "True" if emergency equipment is readily available, and "False" otherwise.
* **Fire safety equipment** (fire_safety_equipment)
	+ Answer "True" if fire safety equipment is in place, and "False" otherwise.
* **Regular safety equipment inspection** (safety_equipment_inspection)
	+ Select "True" if regular safety equipment inspections are conducted, and "False" otherwise.
* **Emergency response plan** (emergency_response_plan)
	+ Answer "True" if an emergency response plan is in place, and "False" otherwise.

## Tips
* Review this form carefully before proceeding.
* Provide accurate and complete answers.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for support or clarification.
