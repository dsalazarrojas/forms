# Business Plan Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a business plan, specifically its name, type, description, goals, target market, competitive landscape, and financial projections. The collected data will help businesses provide a clear and concise overview of their plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required information, such as the Business Name, Type of Business, Business Description, and Start Date.
2. Provide a brief description of what your business does in the Business Description field.
3. Answer the following questions in the Vision and Mission section:
  * What is your core purpose (Mission Statement)?
  * Where do you see your business in 5 years (Vision Statement)?
4. Describe your main products or services in the Main Products or Services field.
5. Identify your ideal customer profile in the Target Customer Profile field.
6. Answer the following questions in the Competitive Landscape section:
  * Who is your main competition (Main Competitors)?
  * How will you compete with them (Competitive Advantage)?
7. Answer the following questions in the Financial Overview section:
  * What are your estimated startup costs (Estimated Startup Costs)?
  * What is your expected annual revenue for the first year (First Year Revenue Projection)?
  * When do you expect to break even (Break Even Timeline)?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of your business.
- **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of business that best describes your company (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Non-Profit, or Other).
- **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required): Describe what your business does.
- **Expected or Actual Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your business.
- **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, text, required): Describe your core purpose.
- **Vision Statement** (`vision_statement`, text, required): Describe where you see your business in 5 years.
- **Core Values** (`core_values`, text, optional): Describe the principles that guide your business.
- **Main Products or Services** (`main_products_services`, text, required): Describe the main products or services offered by your business.
- **Unique Selling Proposition** (`unique_selling_proposition`, text, required): Describe what makes your business different from others.
- **Target Customer Profile** (`target_customers`, text, required): Describe your ideal customer profile.
- **Estimated Market Size** (`market_size`, number, optional): Estimate the total addressable market.
- **Main Competitors** (`main_competitors`, text, optional): List the main competitors of your business.
- **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, text, required): Explain how your business will compete with others.
- **Estimated Startup Costs** (`startup_costs`, number, optional): Enter your estimated startup costs in dollars.
- **First Year Revenue Projection** (`first_year_revenue_projection`, number, optional): Project your expected annual revenue for the first year.
- **Break Even Timeline** (`break_even_timeline`, select_one, optional): Choose when you expect to break even (Less than 6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-3 years, 3+ years).
