# Sole Proprietorship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for sole proprietorship business registration. The details collected will be used for registration and other legal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read this guide carefully to understand each field's purpose and required status.
2. Fill out the form in multiple pages, with each page having a unique identifier.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Business Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your business name as it will be registered.
* **Address** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your business address for registration purposes.
* **Zip** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your zip code for postal purposes.
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **What type of business is this?** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of business you are registering (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Limited Liability Partnership, Non-profit Organization).
* **What kind of business activity is this?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable business activities (Consulting, Accounting, Software Development, IT Services, Other).
* **What is the founder's name?** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the founder's name.
* **Founder Email** (`10`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the founder's email address.
* **Founder Phone** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the founder's phone number.
* **Date of registration** (`12`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of registration.
* **Time of registration** (`13`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of registration.
* **Business Address** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your business address.
* **Zip Code** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your business zip code.
* **Business Activity** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the main business activity.
* **Registration Date** (`17`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of registration.
* **Registration Time** (`18`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of registration.
* **Notes** (`19`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Form Completion Date** (`20`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date this form was completed.
* **User** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (`22`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Address** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **User Zip** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
