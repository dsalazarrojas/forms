# SME Borrowers And Investors Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SME Borrowers And Investors Registration form is designed to collect essential information from both SMEs and investors to facilitate effective communication and partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the SME details section with your company or business information.
2.  Specify your funding requirements.
3.  If you are an investor, provide your details. Otherwise, skip this section.
4.  Select the funding platform you are associated with.
5.  Enter your business details.
6.  Provide your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### SME Details (sme_details)
* **SME Details** (`sme_details`, text, required): This section requires you to enter your company or business name, address, and other relevant details. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

### Funding Requirements (funding_requirements)
* **Funding Requirements** (`funding_requirements`, text, required): Specify the amount of funding you are seeking and any other requirements you have for the investment.

### Investor Details (investor_details)
* **Investor Details** (`investor_details`, text, optional): If you are an investor, please provide your details. This section is optional, but it's essential to fill it out if you want to be contacted by SMEs for investment opportunities.

### Funding Platform (funding_platform)
* **Funding Platform** (`funding_platform`, select_one, required): Select the funding platform you are associated with. Please choose one of the available options: Lending Platform A, Lending Platform B, or Lending Platform C.

### Business Details (business_details)
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, required): Enter your business name, address, and other relevant details. This section is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

### Contact Information (contact_info)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your email address, phone number, and other contact details. This will help us reach out to you for further discussion and communication.
