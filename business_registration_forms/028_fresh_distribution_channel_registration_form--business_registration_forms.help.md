# Fresh Distribution Channel Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresh Distribution Channel Registration Form is designed to register new distribution channels for businesses, providing a way to gather necessary information for the purpose of setting up or updating a business's registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your business's registration details in **Registration Details**.
2. Provide contact information about the person handling the registration in **Contact Person**.
3. Select one option in **Select One**.
4. Select multiple options in **Select Multiple**.
5. Enter your service details in **Service Details**.
6. Confirm your submission in **Submission Confirmation**.
7. Enter your email address in **Email**.
8. Enter your address in **Address**.
9. Enter your date of birth in **Date of Birth**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details**: Enter your business's name, address, and any other relevant details that you want to register.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name, title, and contact information (phone number and email) of the person handling the registration.
* **Select One**: Choose one option from the dropdown list to select a specific type or category.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the dropdown list to select specific types or categories.
* **Service Details**: Enter a brief description of your service or business.
* **Submission Confirmation**: Enter your name and a confirmation that the information provided is accurate.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
