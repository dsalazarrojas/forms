# retail_brand_accelerator_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register retail brands for participation in a business accelerator program. It collects essential information about each brand, allowing the program to provide tailored support and resources to help them grow and succeed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling in the required fields**:
	* Retail Brand Name: Enter the name of your retail brand.
	* Retail Brand Stage: Select the stage of your retail brand (Early Stage, Late Stage).
	* Retail Goals: Provide a brief description of your brand's goals.
	* Retail Email: Enter your email address associated with your brand.
	* Start Date (Optional): If you have a specific start date, please enter it.

2. **Fill in optional fields**:
	* Retail Program Type: Choose the type of program your brand is associated with (Accelerator, Incubator, Retail).
	* Retail Business Stage: Select the stage of your retail business (Pre-Series A, Series A, Series B, Series C).
	* Retail Phone: If you have a phone number associated with your brand, enter it.
	* Retail Program Manager: Enter the name of your program manager.
	* Retail Program URL: If you have a program URL, enter it.
	* Retail Program Duration: If applicable, enter the duration of your program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Brand Name** (`retail_brand_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your retail brand.
* **Retail Brand Stage** (`retail_brand_stage`, select_one, required): Select the stage of your retail brand (Early Stage, Late Stage).
* **Retail Details** (`retail_brand_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your brand, including its mission and vision.
* **Retail Program Type** (`retail_program_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of program your brand is associated with (Accelerator, Incubator, Retail).
* **Retail Goals** (`retail_goals`, text, required): Describe your brand's goals, including its short-term and long-term objectives.
* **Retail Business Stage** (`retail_business_stage`, select_one, optional): Select the stage of your retail business (Pre-Series A, Series A, Series B, Series C).
* **Retail Email** (`retail_email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your brand.
* **Retail Phone** (`retail_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number associated with your brand, enter it.
* **Retail Program Manager** (`retail_program_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of your program manager.
* **Retail Program URL** (`retail_program_url`, text, optional): If you have a program URL, enter it.
* **Retail Program Duration** (`retail_program_duration`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the duration of your program.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling in the required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your brand and goals.
* If you have any questions, please contact the program administrators.
* This form is intended to help us get to know your retail brand better.
