# fda_importer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FDA Importer Registration Form is a document used by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to gather information from importers of regulated products. This form is used to collect data on the importer's name, address, contact details, and product information, including the product's name, description, and import date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the importer's name and contact information, including street address, city, state, country, and phone number.
2. Provide the product's name and description.
3. Enter the import date and time.
4. Enter the shipping information, including shipping country, address, city, state, and postal code.
5. Enter the shipping contact name and phone number.
6. Enter the shipping email address.

* **Importer Name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the name of the company or individual importing the product.
* **Street Address**: This field is required and should be filled out with the street address of the importer.
* **City**: This field is required and should be filled out with the city where the importer is located.

* **Country**: This field is required and should be filled out with the country where the importer is located.
* **Postal Code**: This field is required and should be filled out with the postal code of the importer.
* **Contact Name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the name of the contact person for the importer.
* **Phone Number**: This field is required and should be filled out with the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email**: This field is required and should be filled out with the email address of the contact person.
* **Facility Name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the name of the facility where the product is being imported from.
* **Facility Address**: This field is required and should be filled out with the address of the facility where the product is being imported from.
* **State FDA Number**: This field is required and should be filled out with the FDA registration number of the facility where the product is being imported from.
* **Product Name**: This field is required and should be filled out with the name of the product being imported.
* **Product Description**: This field is required and should be filled out with a brief description of the product being imported.
* **Import Date**: This field is required and should be filled out with the date of import.
* **Import Time**: This field is required and should be filled out with the time of import.
* **Shipping Country**: This field is required and should be filled out with the country where the product is being shipped from.
