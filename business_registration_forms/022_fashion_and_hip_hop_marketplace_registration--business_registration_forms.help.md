# Fashion And Hip Hop Marketplace Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration of a buyer in a Fashion and Hip Hop marketplace. It collects information about the buyer and the marketplace they would like to register with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Buyer Information** (`buyer_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant buyer information.
- **Seller Information** (`seller_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the seller or vendor you would like to register.
- **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor or brand you would like to register.
- **Marketplace Details** (`marketplace_details`, text, required): Enter any relevant details about the marketplace you would like to register with.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
