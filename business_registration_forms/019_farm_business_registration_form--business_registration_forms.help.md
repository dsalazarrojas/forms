# farm_business_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a farm business, collecting information about the farm's name, location, owner, contact details, email, phone number, business type, and products offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your farm's name in the "Farm Business" field.
2. Enter the farm's location in the "Location" field.
3. Provide your name as the farm's owner in the "Owner" field.
4. Fill in your contact information, including email address and phone number, in the "Contact" fields.
5. Select your farm's business type from the "Business Type" options.
6. Choose the products your farm offers from the "Farm Products" options.
7. Provide additional business details in the "Business Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Business** (`Farm Business`, text, required): Enter the name of your farm business.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Enter the physical location of your farm.
* **Owner** (`Owner`, text, required): Enter your name as the farm's owner.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your farm's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your farm's phone number.
* **Business Type** (`Business Type`, select one, optional): Choose one of the listed business types to categorize your farm.
* **Farm Products** (`Farm Products`, select multiple, optional): Choose the products your farm offers.
* **Business Details** (`Business Details`, text, optional): Enter additional information about your farm business.
