# Responsive Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a business for registration purposes. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your business is registered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by completing the **First Page** section, which is currently blank. This section is intended to be a placeholder for any additional pages that may be needed for the form.
2.  Proceed to the **Business Name** field, where you can enter your business name.
3.  Select the **Business Type** from the drop-down menu that best describes your business.
4.  Enter your **Business Address**, including the street address, in the provided field.
5.  Enter your **City** in the designated field.
6.  Enter your **State** in the provided field.
7.  Enter your **Zip** code in the designated field.
8.  Enter your **Country** in the provided field.
9.  Enter your **Phone** number in the provided field.
10. Enter your **Email** address in the provided field, formatted as a standard email (e.g. `username@example.com`).
11. If you have a **Website**, enter the URL in the provided field.
12. If your business has a **Fax**, enter the number in the provided field.
13. If you have a **Representative**, enter their name and title in the provided field.
14. If your business has employees, enter the **Number of Employees** in the provided field.
15. If your business has an ownership structure, select the **Ownership Type** from the drop-down menu (e.g. **Individual**, **Joint**, **Partnership**).
16. If applicable, enter the **Ownership Percentage** for each type of ownership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Page**: This field is currently blank and is intended to be a placeholder for additional pages that may be needed for the form.
*   **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
*   **Business Type**: Select the type of business that best describes your company (e.g. **Business**, **Partnership**, **LLC**, **Corporation**).
*   **Business Address**: Enter the physical address of your business.
*   **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
*   **State**: Enter the state where your business is located.
*   **Zip**: Enter the postal code where your business is located.
*   **Country**: Enter the country where your business is located.
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your business.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of your business, in the standard email format (e.g. `username@example.com`).
*   **Website**: If your business has a website, enter the URL.
*   **Fax**: If your business has a fax number, enter it in the provided field.
*   **Representative**: If your business has a representative, enter their name and title in the provided field.
*   **Number of Employees**: If your business has employees, enter the number of employees.
*   **Ownership Type**: Select the type of ownership structure for your business (e.g. **Individual**, **Joint**, **Partnership**).
*   **Ownership Percentage**: If applicable, enter the percentage of ownership for each type of ownership.
