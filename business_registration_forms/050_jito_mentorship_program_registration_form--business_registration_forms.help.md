# Jito Mentorship Program Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a business or program with Jito's mentorship program. The information provided will be used to create your business's profile on our platform and to connect you with other businesses and teams in the mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of program you are registering (yes/no)
2. Enter the name of your program
3. Select the start and end dates of your program
4. Enter the name of your mentor
5. Enter the email of your mentor
6. Select the status of your program (active/inactive)
7. Enter the location of your program
8. Enter the name of your team
9. Enter the email of your team
10. Enter the phone number of your team
11. Enter the contact information for your team

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Program Type**: This field is used to select the type of program you are registering. Please choose "yes" if your program is a mentorship program and "no" if it is not.
* **Enter Program Name**: This field is used to enter the name of your program. Please enter the official name of your program as it will be displayed on our platform.
* **Start Date**: This field is used to select the start date of your program. Please enter the date when your program started.
* **End Date**: This field is used to select the end date of your program. Please enter the date when your program will end.
* **Mentor Name**: This field is used to enter the name of your mentor. This should be the name of the person who will be mentoring your team.
* **Mentor Email**: This field is used to enter the email of your mentor. This should be the email address of the person who will be mentoring your team.
* **Select Program Status**: This field is used to select the status of your program. Please choose "active" if your program is currently active and "inactive" if it is not.
* **Location**: This field is used to enter the location of your program. Please enter the physical location of your program.
* **Team Name**: This field is used to enter the name of your team. This should be the official name of your team.
* **Team Email**: This field is used to enter the email of your team. This should be the email address of your team.
* **Team Phone**: This field is used to enter the phone number of your team. This should be the contact number of your team.
* **Team Contact**: This field is used to enter the contact information for your team. This should be the name of the person who will be handling communications for your team. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, you can reach out to us for clarification. 
* You can register your business or team at any time, but please note that your registration will not be active until your program is approved by our team.
