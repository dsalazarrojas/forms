# downline_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the business registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and field carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Fill out the fields accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
4. Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **applicant_name** (Text, Required)
Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.

### 2. **contact_form** (Select One, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* email
* phone
* both

### 3. **personal_info** (Note, Optional)
Enter any additional personal information you would like to provide.

### 4. **business_details** (Text, Required)
Enter your business details.

### 5. **business_name** (Text, Required)
Enter your business name.

### 6. **address** (Select Multiple, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* physical
* mailing
* both

### 7. **date_of_birth** (Date, Required)
Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.

### 8. **place_of_birth** (Text, Optional)
Enter the place of your birth.

### 9. **nationality** (Select One, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* American
* Nigerian
* other

### 10. **occupation** (Text, Optional)
Enter your occupation.

### 11. **employment_status** (Select One, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* employed
* unemployed
* other

### 12. **annual_income** (Number, Required)
Enter your annual income.

### 13. **education** (Select Multiple, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* primary
* secondary
* tertiary
* postgraduate

### 14. **marital_status** (Select One, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* single
* married
* engaged
* other

### 15. **gender** (Select One, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* male
* female
* other

### 16. **religion** (Select One, Required)
Select one of the following options:
* Christian
* Muslim
* other

### 17. **nationality_2** (Text, Optional)
Enter your nationality (if different from the previous field).

### 18. **address_2** (Text, Optional)
Enter your address (if different from the business address).

### 19. **phone_number** (Text, Required)
Enter your phone number.

### 20. **email_2** (Email, Required)
Enter your email address.

### 21. **website** (Text, Optional)
Enter your website (if applicable).

### 22. **profile_picture** (Select One, Optional)
Select one of the following options:
* uploaded
* upload

### 23. **business_address** (Text, Optional)
Enter your business address.

### 24. **business_phone** (Text, Optional)
Enter your business phone number.

Note:
This form is designed to collect accurate and complete information from applicants. Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
