# Net Zero Building Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Zero Building Partnership Registration form is designed to collect information from partners who wish to collaborate with us on building-related projects. This form is used to gather contact and partnership details, allowing us to assess potential collaborations and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Provide your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Choose your area of expertise from the "What is your area of expertise?" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the key details of your organization in the "What are the key details of your organization?" field.
5. Describe the onboarding requirements of your partnership in the "What are the onboarding requirements of your partnership?" field.
6. Select the tools you currently use from the "What tools do you currently use?" dropdown menu (this is optional).
7. Confirm that you are an authorized representative of your company and understand the partnership by selecting the "Submit" button.
8. Finally, fill in your name, phone number, and address in the respective fields (these are optional but recommended for your contact information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`user_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Company Name** (`user_company_name`, text, **Required**): Provide your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **What is your area of expertise?** (`partner_interests`, select_one, **Required**): Choose one of the areas where you specialize in, such as Sustainability, Building Efficiency, or Energy Management.
* **What are the key details of your organization?** (`partner_organisation_details`, text, **Required**): Write a brief description of your company's main activities, services, or products.
* **What are the onboarding requirements of your partnership?** (`partner_onboarding_requirements`, text, **Required**): Describe the requirements and expectations for your partnership, such as specific meetings, reports, or communication channels.
* **What tools do you currently use?** (`assigned_tools`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select one or more tools you use in your work, like Google Docs, Excel, or Jotform.
* **By clicking submit, I confirm I am the authorized representative of my company and understand this partnership.** (`partner_agree`, note, **Required**): This field is a confirmation statement; please select "Submit" to confirm your representation and understanding.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, **Optional**): Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, **Optional**): Fill in your name for contact and reference purposes.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Address** (`user_address`, text, **Optional**): Provide your organization's address for contact and reference.

Note that fields marked as "Required" are necessary to submit the form, while "Optional" fields are not required but recommended for your contact information.
