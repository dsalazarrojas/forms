# Business Partner Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Partner Contract Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a partnership between two businesses. This form serves as a reference point for both parties to ensure that everyone is on the same page regarding their responsibilities, commissions, payment terms, and contract duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a date for the contract to commence.
2. Provide the name and address of both companies (Party A and Party B).
3. Enter the name and contact information (email and phone number) of representatives from both parties.
4. Specify the purpose of the partnership.
5. Determine the duration of the partnership and select a contract term (e.g., 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, 5 years, or At-will).
6. Describe how commissions will be calculated and the percentage of sales/revenue attributed to each party.
7. Determine how payments will be made (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Annual, or On Delivery).
8. Define the geographic or product scope of the partnership.
9. Outline the key responsibilities of both parties.
10. Specify the termination clause, which describes how the contract can end.
11. If applicable, add any special conditions or agreements.
12. Confirm that both parties agree to these terms by selecting "Yes, I Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, required): The effective date of the contract.
* **First Company Name** (`party_a_company`, text, required): The name of the first party (Company A).
* **Party A Representative** (`party_a_representative`, text, required): Authorized signatory from Company A.
* **Party A Address** (`party_a_address`, text, required): Business address for Company A.
* **Party A Email** (`party_a_email`, email, required): Contact email for Company A.
* **Party A Phone** (`party_a_phone`, text, required): Contact phone number for Company A.
* **Second Company Name** (`party_b_company`, text, required): The name of the second party (Company B).
* **Party B Representative** (`party_b_representative`, text, required): Authorized signatory from Company B.
* **Party B Address** (`party_b_address`, text, required): Business address for Company B.
* **Party B Email** (`party_b_email`, email, required): Contact email for Company B.
* **Party B Phone** (`party_b_phone`, text, required): Contact phone number for Company B.
* **Purpose of Partnership** (`partnership_purpose`, text, required): A brief description of the partnership's objective.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the partnership.
* **Commission Structure** (`commission_structure`, text, required): Describe how commissions will be calculated and distributed.
* **Commission Percentage** (`commission_percentage`, number, required): The percentage of sales/revenue attributed to each party.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Outline the payment schedule and method.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how payments will be made (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Annual, or On Delivery).
* **Territory or Scope** (`territory_or_scope`, text, required): Define the geographic or product scope of the partnership.
* **Key Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, text, required): Outline the key responsibilities of both parties.
* **Termination Clause** (`termination_clause`, text, required): Describe how the contract can end.
* **Special Conditions** (`special_conditions`, text, optional): Any special agreements or conditions that are not covered by the standard terms.
* **Both parties agree to these terms** (`signatures_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that both parties have read and agree to the terms of the contract.

Note: This form and guide are for general information purposes and should be reviewed and customized according to specific business requirements and local laws. Always consult with legal experts for specific guidance.
