# Stakeholder Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Registration Form is used to collect information about stakeholders in a business event. The form helps to manage stakeholder's contact details and event-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Planner information (if available).
2. Enter the Stakeholder's Name and their contact details (Email, Phone, and Address).
3. Select the stakeholder's Role(s) in the event.
4. Fill in the event-related information (Event Date, Time, Duration, Start and End Date/Time).
5. Select the event's Location (City, State, and Country).
6. Add any additional Notes about the stakeholder.
7. Assign a user to manage the stakeholder (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Planner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for planning the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Stakeholder Name**: Enter the name of the stakeholder. (Required: 0/1)
* **Role**: Select one or more roles the stakeholder plays in the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **E-mail**: Enter the stakeholder's email address. (Required: 0/1)
* **Phone**: Enter the stakeholder's phone number. (Required: 0/1)
* **Address**: Enter the stakeholder's physical address. (Required: 0/1)
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the stakeholder's ZIP code. (Required: 0/1)
* **City**: Enter the city where the event will take place. (Required: 0/1)
* **State**: Select the state where the event will take place. (Required: 0/1)
* **Country**: Select the country where the event will take place. (Required: 0/1)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the stakeholder. (Required: 0/1)
* **Assigned User**: Assign a user to manage this stakeholder. (Required: 0/1)
* **Assigned User Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the assigned user. (Required: 0/1)
* **Assigned User E-mail**: Enter the email address of the assigned user. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event End Date**: Enter the end date of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event. (Required: 0/1)
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created this form. (Required: 0/1)
* **Modified By**: Enter the name of the person who last modified this form. (Required: 0/1)
* **Created At**: Enter the date and time this form was created. (Required: 0/1)
* **Modified At**: Enter the date and time this form was last modified. (Required: 0/1)

Please note that all fields are optional except for the **Event Planner**, **Stakeholder Name**, **E-mail**, **Phone**, **Address**, **ZIP Code**, **City**, **State**, and **Country** fields, which are required.
