# Dealer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Registration Form is a required form to register a dealer with our company. It helps us to verify your identity and ensure that you have the necessary information to become a registered dealer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your dealer information, including your dealer name, contact person, email, phone number, etc.
2. Provide your business information, including your business name, address, business type, and business tax ID.
3. Enter your owner's information, including their name, email, phone number, and the date they have been dealing with you.
4. Select your dealer type (Dealer, Distributor, or Wholesaler).
5. Select your business type (Retail, Wholesaler, Distributor, or Service Provider).
6. Enter your dealer and business addresses, cities, states, and countries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dealer Name (text)**: Enter your dealer name as it appears on all your business documents.
* **Contact Person (text)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for communicating with our company.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Business Name (text)**: Enter your business name as it appears on all your business documents.
* **Business Address (text)**: Enter your business address.
* **Dealer Since (date)**: Enter the date you have been dealing with us.
* **Contact Number (text)**: Enter your secondary phone number.
* **Dealer Type (select_one)**: Select your dealer type from the options provided (Dealer, Distributor, or Wholesaler).
* **Business Type (select_multiple)**: Select all the business types that apply to your business (Retail, Wholesaler, Distributor, or Service Provider).
* **Owner Name (text)**: Enter the name of the owner of your business.
* **Owner Email (email)**: Enter the official email address of the owner.
* **Owner Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the owner.
* **Owner Since (date)**: Enter the date the owner has been dealing with us.
* **Business Address 2 (text)**: Enter an additional address line for your business.
* **Business City (text)**: Enter the city of your business.
* **Business State (text)**: Enter the state of your business.
* **Business Country (text)**: Enter the country of your business.
* **Business Postal Code (text)**: Enter the postal code of your business.
* **Business Tax ID (text)**: Enter your business tax ID number.
* **Dealer Address (text)**: Enter your dealer address.
* **Dealer City (text)**: Enter the city of your dealer location.
* **Dealer State (text)**: Enter the state of your dealer location.
* **Dealer Country (text)**: Enter the country of your dealer location.
* **Dealer Postal Code (text)**: Enter the postal code of your dealer location.

## Tips
* Double-check your information to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use a format that matches the format provided for address fields.
* Select only one dealer type and multiple business types if applicable.
