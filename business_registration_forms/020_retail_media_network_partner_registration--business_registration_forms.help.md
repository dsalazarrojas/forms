# Retail Media Network Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Retail Media Network Partner Registration" form is designed for partners to register with the Retail Media Network. This form gathers essential information to facilitate a smooth and effective partnership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields to provide your basic information.
2. Enter your contact details for us to get in touch with you.
3. Share your partnership proposal, detailing your goals and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (Basic Information)**: Enter your company or individual name.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Partnership Proposal (Partnership Proposal)**: Outline your partnership goals, objectives, and expectations. Be concise and clear in your proposal.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Keep your partnership proposal specific and tailored to your business needs.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
