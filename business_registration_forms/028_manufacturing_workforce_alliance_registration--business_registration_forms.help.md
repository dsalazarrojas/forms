# Manufacturing Workforce Alliance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Workforce Alliance Registration form is used to collect information from manufacturers or partners who wish to join or participate in the alliance. This form gathers essential details about the partner's identity, contact information, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the **Partner Name** field.
2. Provide your contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your contact number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your industry group in the **Industry Group** field.
6. Choose your areas of interest by selecting multiple options in the **Interest Areas** field.
7. Indicate whether you are willing to sign up for the alliance by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Sign Up** field.
8. Provide a brief description of your company in the **Partner Description** field.
9. (Optional) Enter any additional information in the **Additional Info** field.
10. Accept the **Terms and Conditions** by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
11. Click on the **Submit** button to complete the form.
12. (Optional) Add any additional notes in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
- **Industry Group** (`industry_group`, text, required): Select your industry or group.
- **Interest Areas** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, required): Choose your areas of interest (yes or no).
- **Sign Up** (`sign_up`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are willing to sign up for the alliance (yes or no).
- **Partner Description** (`partner_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your company.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your company.
- **Accept Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions (yes or no).
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.
