# Social Movement Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Movement Community Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining our community. This form helps us understand who you are, what brings you to our community, and ensures your contact details are accurate.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide a valid Email address associated with your social media platform or personal account.
4. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
5. Share a bit about your profession or current occupation.
6. Share with us what drives your main motivation for joining our community.
7. Please review your submission for accuracy before submitting it.
