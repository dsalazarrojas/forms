# Fulfillment Robotics Partnership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Fulfillment Robotics Partnership Registration Form is used to register a new partnership with Fulfillment Robotics. It is intended for both internal and external partners to provide their information and details about the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Basic Info section with the company name, address, and other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the partnership in the Partnership Info section.
3. Add your contact information in the Contact Information section.
4. Specify your role within the partnership in the Partnership Role section.
5. List your skills and languages spoken in the Skills and Languages section.
6. Add any additional notes about the partnership in the Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info (1)**: This section is for providing basic information about the partnership, including the company name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Partnership Info (2)**: In this section, please provide any relevant information about the partnership.
* **Address Information (3)**: Enter the address of the partnership.
* **Contact Information (4)**: This is where you should enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Partnership Role (5)**: Specify your role within the partnership.
* **Skills and Languages (6)**: List your skills and languages spoken.
* **Notes (7)**: Add any additional notes about the partnership.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* This form is intended for both internal and external partners, so please fill it out thoroughly and completely.
