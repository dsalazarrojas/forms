# Business License Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business License Release Form is used to request the release of business license records. This form gathers essential information to process the request effectively and ensure that the correct records are released to the authorized person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name associated with the license.
2. Provide the license number, which is the identifier for the license.
3. Give a reason for the request to release the records.
4. Specify which records are being requested for release.
5. Enter the name of the person authorized to receive the records.
6. Enter a valid email address for the contact email field.
7. (Optional) Add instructions for signing any release forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name (business_name)**: Enter the name associated with the license.
* **License Number (license_number)**: The identifier for the license.
* **Request Reason (request_reason)**: Explain the reason for requesting the release of records.
* **Records Requested (records_requested)**: Specify which specific records are being requested for release.
* **Authorized Person Name (authorized_person_name)**: Enter the name of the person authorized to receive the records.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter a valid email address for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Signature Instructions (signature_instructions)**: (Optional) Provide any necessary instructions for signing release forms.

## Tips
When completing this form, ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field. The requested records will be released according to the provided instructions and reason.
