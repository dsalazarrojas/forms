# Business Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your business for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and select the correct options for each required field.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure you have all necessary documents or details before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Business Name (Required)
• **Business Name**: Enter your registered business name exactly as it appears on your business certificate or license.

### 2. Type of Business (Required)
• **Type of Business**: Choose the correct structure of your business from the provided options: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Non-profit, or Cooperative.

### 3. Industry Sector (Required)
• **Industry Sector**: Select the industry your business operates in: Retail, Services, Manufacturing, Technology, Healthcare, Education, Finance, Real Estate, Construction, or Hospitality.

### 4. Business Registration Date (Required)
• **Business Registration Date**: Enter the date your business was registered or will be registered.

### 5. Owner First Name (Required)
• **Owner First Name**: Enter the first name of the primary owner or representative.

### 6. Owner Last Name (Required)
• **Owner Last Name**: Enter the last name of the primary owner or representative.

### 7. Owner Email Address (Required)
• **Owner Email Address**: Enter the primary contact email address for your business.

### 8. Owner Phone Number (Required)
• **Owner Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number for your business.

### 9. Business Street Address (Required)
• **Business Street Address**: Enter the physical location of your business.

### 10. City (Required)
• **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.

### 11. State or Province (Required)
• **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your business is located.

### 12. Postal Code (Required)
• **Postal Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of your business location.

### 13. Country (Required)
• **Country**: Enter the country where your business is registered.

### 14. Business Description (Required)
• **Business Description**: Briefly describe what your business does.

### 15. Number of Employees (Required)
• **Number of Employees**: Enter the current or projected employee count for your business.

### 16. Estimated Annual Revenue (Required)
• **Estimated Annual Revenue**: Select the estimated annual revenue for your business in the provided options.

### 17. Tax Identification Number (Optional)
• **Tax Identification Number or EIN**: Enter your federal or local tax identification number if applicable.

### 18. Previous Business Experience (Required)
• **Previous Business Experience**: Select your level of experience with running a business from the provided options.

### 19. Business License Status (Required)
• **Business License Status**: Choose the status of your business license from the provided options.

### 20. Additional Information (Optional)
• **Additional Information**: Enter any other details about your business if necessary.
