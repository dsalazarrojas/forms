# Craft Producer Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential members of the Craft Producer Network register themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Member Details.
2. Select your Member Type (Artisan Network, Craft Collective, or Marketplace).
3. Enter your Contact Details (Name, email address, etc.).
4. Enter your Producer Name.
5. Provide Producer Information.
6. Upload a Producer Image.
7. Enter Member Details 2 (additional information).
8. Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Details
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, required: false): Enter your basic contact information.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your category (Artisan Network, Craft Collective, or Marketplace).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information (Name, phone number, etc.).
### Member Information
* **Producer Name** (`producer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your producer name.
* **Producer Information** (`producer_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Producer Image** (`producer_image`, text, required: false): Upload an image of yourself or your logo.
### Additional Member Details
* **Member Details 2** (`member_details_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional member details.
### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
### Tips
* Please ensure that your Member Type is accurate to reflect your network status.
* Upload a clear and recent image to represent yourself.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you are an existing member, please do not register again.
