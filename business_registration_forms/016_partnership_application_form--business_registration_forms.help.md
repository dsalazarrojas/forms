# partnership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The partnership application form is used to collect information about a partnership application. This includes details about the partner's name, business name, contact information, and the reason for the application.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the **partner name** in the text field provided. This is the name of the person or entity applying for the partnership.
2.  Enter the **business name** in the text field provided. This is the name of the business or organization the partner is associated with.
3.  Enter your **email address** in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`). This is the contact information for the partner or their representative.
4.  Enter your **phone number** in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX XXX XXX`. This is the phone number for the partner or their representative.
5.  Provide a brief description of the **application reason** in the text field provided. This will help the reviewing authority understand the purpose of the application.
6.  Select a **status** for the application from the dropdown menu. Choose from Active, Pending, or Denied.
7.  Enter your **partner address** in the text field provided. This is the address of the partner or their representative.
8.  Enter your **business address** in the text field provided. This is the address of the business or organization the partner is associated with.
9.  Select the **business type** from the available options. This will help the reviewing authority understand the nature of the business.
10.  Enter the **partner duration** in the numeric field provided. This is the number of months, days, or years the partner has been associated with the business.
11.  Select the **partner duration unit** from the dropdown menu. Choose from Month, Day, or Year.
12.  Enter the **partner duration year** in the numeric field provided. This is the year the partner has been associated with the business.
13.  Enter the **partner duration month** in the numeric field provided. This is the month the partner has been associated with the business.
14.  Enter the **partner duration day** in the numeric field provided. This is the day the partner has been associated with the business.
15.  Enter the **partner duration time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`. This is the time of day the partner has been associated with the business.
16.  Select the **partner duration period** from the dropdown menu. Choose from AM, PM, or Both.
17.  Select the **partner duration frequency** from the dropdown menu. Choose from Week, Day, Month, or Year.
18.  Enter the **partner duration start time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`. This is the start time of the partner’s duration.
19.  Enter the **partner duration start date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This is the start date of the partner’s duration.
20.  Select the **partner duration start period** from the dropdown menu. Choose from AM, PM, or Both.
21.  Select the **partner duration start frequency** from the dropdown menu. Choose from Week, Day, Month, or Year.
22.  Enter the **partner duration end time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`. This is the end time of the partner’s duration.
23.  Enter the **partner duration end date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This is the end date of the partner’s duration.
24.  Select the **partner duration end period** from the dropdown menu. Choose from AM, PM, or Both.
25.  Select the **partner duration end frequency** from the dropdown menu. Choose from Week, Day, Month, or Year.
26.  Enter a **partner duration note** in the text field provided. This is any additional information about the partner’s duration.
