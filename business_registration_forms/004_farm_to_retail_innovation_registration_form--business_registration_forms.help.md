# Farm To Retail Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Farm To Retail Innovation Registration Form" is a form used to register and showcase innovative practices and products in the farm-to-retail sector. Complete this form to provide detailed information about your business and its innovation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the basic business information on **page 1**.
2.  Select the **farm-to-retail sector** that best describes your business operation.
3.  Choose the **product category** for your innovative product or practice.
4.  Provide the **registration date** of your innovation.
5.  Describe your **innovation name** and **innovation description** in detail.
6.  Outline the **challenge or problem** your innovation addresses and the **accelerator** you used to develop it.
7.  Indicate if your innovation is part of a **challenge program**.
8.  Describe the **collaboration** involved in developing your innovation.
9.  Specify the **innovation stage** of your product or practice.
10.  Outline the **expected outcome** of your innovation.
11.  Indicate if your innovation is part of a **farm-to-retail network**.
12.  Provide a **tagline** for your innovation.
13.  Add any relevant **innovation logo** and **innovation URL**.
14.  Finally, include any **additional comments** and **innovation video** or **innovation file** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Farm to Retail Innovation Registration Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill out but a label for the form itself.
*   **Business Name**: Enter your business name accurately and in its current state.
*   **Contact Person**: Provide the name and title of the person responsible for your business.
*   **Email**: Enter your business email address accurately.
*   **Phone**: Enter your business phone number accurately.
*   **Farm to Retail Sector**: Select one or more of the sectors that describe your business operation. Options include **Agro-processing**, **Farm to table**, **Farm to Retail**, **Farm to school**, and **Other**.
*   **Product Category**: Choose the category that best describes your innovative product or practice. Options include **Produce**, **Dairy**, **Meat**, **Poultry**, **Grain**, **Crops**, and **Other**.
*   **Registration Date**: This is the date you registered your innovation.
*   **Innovation Name**: Enter the name of your innovation accurately.
*   **Innovation Description**: Provide a detailed description of your innovation.
*   **Challenge Program**: Indicate if your innovation is part of a challenge program.
*   **Accelerator**: Describe the accelerator you used to develop your innovation.
*   **Collaboration Program**: Describe the collaboration involved in developing your innovation.
*   **Innovation Stage**: Specify the stage of your innovation, which is either **Concept**, **Proof of concept**, **Pilot**, **Commercialization**, or **Other**.
*   **Expected Outcome**: Outline the expected outcome of your innovation.
*   **Farm to Retail Network**: Indicate if your innovation is part of the farm-to-retail network.
*   **Innovation Tagline**: Enter a tagline that summarizes your innovation.
*   **Innovation Logo**: Add the logo of your innovation.
*   **Innovation URL**: Provide the URL of your innovation.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your innovation.
*   **Innovation Video**: Add any video related to your innovation.
*   **Innovation File**: Add any related files or documents.
*   **Farm to Retail Challenge**: Describe the challenge or problem your innovation addresses.
*   **Farm to Retail Accelerator**: Describe the accelerator used to develop your innovation.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your innovation.
*   **Innovation Video**: Enter any video related to your innovation.
*   **Innovation File**: Enter any related file or document.
*   **Innovation File 2**: Enter any additional related file or document.

Note that some of the fields are optional and should only be filled out if applicable to your business and innovation. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
