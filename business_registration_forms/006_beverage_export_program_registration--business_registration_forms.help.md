# Beverage Export Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Beverage Export Program Registration" form is designed to gather information from businesses involved in the export of beverages to support their participation in the export program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as 'true' in the schema.
2. Select all applicable options for fields with 'select_multiple' and 'select_all' types.
3. Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Legal Name** (Company Name, text, required): This is your official registered business name.
* **Company Registration Number** (Company Registration Number, text, required): This is your license or registration ID.
* **Year Company Was Established** (Year Company Was Established, text, required): The year your company was founded.
* **Headquarters Location** (Headquarters Location, text, required): The city and country of your headquarters.
* **Number of Employees** (Number of Employees, number, required): An approximate total headcount.
* **Primary Contact Person** (Primary Contact Person, text, required): Name of the key contact person.
* **Contact Person Title** (Contact Person Title, text, required): Position or role of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Primary business email.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): International format preferred.
* **Years of Export Experience** (Years of Export Experience, select_one, required): How many years have you been exporting? Options include No prior export experience, Less than 2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, and Over 10 years.
* **Current Export Countries** (Current Export Countries, text, optional): List the countries you currently export to.
* **Annual Export Revenue** (Annual Export Revenue, select_one, required): Approximate annual export sales. Options include Under $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, $500,000 - $1 million, $1 million - $5 million, and Over $5 million.
* **Types of Beverages You Export** (Types of Beverages You Export, select_multiple, required): Select all beverage categories you produce. Options include Soft drinks, Fruit juices, Sports drinks, Energy drinks, Water, Coffee beverages, Tea beverages, Alcoholic beverages, Plant-based drinks, and Other.
* **Target Export Markets** (Target Export Markets, select_multiple, required): Which regions interest you most? Options include North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Africa, South America, Central America, and Other.
* **Monthly Production Capacity** (Monthly Production Capacity, text, required): How much can you produce monthly.
* **Food Safety Certifications** (Food Safety Certifications, select_multiple, required): Select all certifications you hold. Options include ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, HACCP, FDA approved, Local health department, and Other.
* **Proper Export Documentation** (Proper Export Documentation, select_one, required): Do you have proper export documentation setup? Options include Yes, fully prepared, Partially prepared, Need assistance, and Not yet started.
* **Primary Objectives for Export Program** (Primary Objectives for Export Program, select_multiple, required): What do you hope to achieve? Options include Expand market reach, Increase revenue, Build brand recognition, Access new customer base, Achieve growth targets, and Regulatory compliance support.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (Additional Information or Questions, text, optional): Anything else we should know.
