# Business Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a business agreement between two parties. This form is used to record the details of the agreement, including the parties involved, the agreement type, and the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide information about Party A, including their name, type, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide information about Party B, including their name, type, email, phone number, and address.
3. Specify the type of agreement, its description, and scope of work.
4. Determine the payment terms and conditions.
5. Specify the start and end dates of the agreement.
6. Determine the renewal terms, including any conditions for termination.
7. Specify if confidentiality is required.
8. Specify the governing law or jurisdiction of the agreement.
9. Add any additional terms or conditions.
10. Sign the form with the names and dates of authorized signatories for both parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party A Name** (id: 8, Label: Party A Name, Type: text, Required: true): The legal name of Party A.
* **Party A Type** (id: 9, Label: Party A Type, Type: select_one, Required: true): The type of entity (Individual, Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Other).
* **Party A Email** (id: 4, Label: Party A Email, Type: email, Required: true): The contact email of Party A.
* **Party A Phone** (id: 5, Label: Party A Phone, Type: text, Required: true): The contact phone number of Party A.
* **Party A Address** (id: 6, Label: Party A Address, Type: text, Required: true): The mailing address of Party A.
* **Party B Name** (id: 8, Label: Party B Name, Type: text, Required: true): The legal name of Party B.
* **Party B Type** (id: 9, Label: Party B Type, Type: select_one, Required: true): The type of entity (Individual, Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Other).
* **Party B Email** (id: 10, Label: Party B Email, Type: email, Required: true): The contact email of Party B.
* **Party B Phone** (id: 11, Label: Party B Phone, Type: text, Required: true): The contact phone number of Party B.
* **Party B Address** (id: 12, Label: Party B Address, Type: text, Required: true): The mailing address of Party B.
* **Agreement Type** (id: 14, Label: Agreement Type, Type: select_one, Required: true): The type of business agreement (Service Agreement, Partnership Agreement, Vendor Agreement, Client Agreement, Licensing Agreement, Non-Disclosure Agreement, Employment Agreement, Other).
* **Agreement Description** (id: 15, Label: Agreement Description, Type: text, Required: true): A brief description of the agreement.
* **Scope of Work** (id: 16, Label: Scope of Work, Type: text, Required: true): What work or services will be provided.
* **Payment Terms** (id: 17, Label: Payment Terms, Type: text, Required: true): How payments will be made.
* **Agreement Value** (id: 18, Label: Agreement Value, Type: number, Required: false): The total value of the agreement.
* **Start Date** (id: 19, Label: Start Date, Type: date, Required: true): When the agreement begins.
* **End Date** (id: 20, Label: End Date, Type: date, Required: false): When the agreement ends.
* **Renewal Terms** (id: 21, Label: Renewal Terms, Type: text, Required: false): How the agreement can be renewed.
* **Termination Clause** (id: 22, Label: Termination Clause, Type: text, Required: false): The conditions for termination.
* **Confidentiality** (id: 23, Label: Confidentiality Required, Type: select_one, Required: true): Whether confidentiality is required (True, False).
* **Governing Law** (id: 24, Label: Governing Law, Type: text, Required: true): The jurisdiction that governs this agreement.
* **Additional Terms** (id: 25, Label: Additional Terms, Type: text, Required: false): Any other terms or conditions.
* **Party A Signature** (id: 26, Label: Party A Signature, Type: text, Required: true): The name of the authorized signatory for Party A.
* **Party A Signature Date** (id: 27, Label: Party A Signature Date, Type: date, Required: true): The date signed by Party A.
* **Party B Signature** (id: 28, Label: Party B Signature, Type: text, Required: true): The name of the authorized signatory for Party B.
* **Party B Signature Date** (id: 29, Label: Party B Signature Date, Type: date, Required: true): The date signed by Party B.
