# TEMPLATE_TREASURY_client_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new client for the company's services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Enter a valid email address in the `contact_email` field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
4. Submit the form once all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Client (\*)**: Enter the name of the new client you are registering.
* **contact_name (\*)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email (\*)**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This must be a valid email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Check that your email address is entered correctly in the `contact_email` field.
* If you have any issues or difficulties with the form, please contact [support email address].
