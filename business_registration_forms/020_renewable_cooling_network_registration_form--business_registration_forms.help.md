# Renewable Cooling Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Cooling Network Registration Form is designed for registration of organizations or individuals interested in a renewable cooling network. This form aims to collect contact, organization, and interest details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure all required fields are completed (if any)
2. Fill in the **Form Contact** page with the necessary contact information.
3. Provide **Organization Details** in the second page.
4. In **Interest Details** page, describe your interests related to the renewable cooling network.
5. Finally, you may add any additional **Notes** in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Contact (text, optional)**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information you want to be associated with your registration.
* **Organization Details (text, optional)**: Briefly describe your organization and its connection to the renewable cooling network (e.g., company name, industry, role).
* **Interest Details (text, optional)**: Specify why you're interested in the renewable cooling network (e.g., research, business, personal).
* **Notes (text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or information not covered in the above fields.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure your email address and contact information are up-to-date.
* If you need help or have questions, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
