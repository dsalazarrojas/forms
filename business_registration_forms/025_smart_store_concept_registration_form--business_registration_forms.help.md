# Smart Store Concept Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from retailers who want to register a new smart store concept. It will help our team assess and develop the proposed store concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Review and ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart Store Concept Registration** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your proposed store concept.
* **Retailer Info** (`retailer_info`, text, optional): Provide your company's name and other relevant information.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your proposed store.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the store.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your business.
* **Technology Interests** (`technology_interests`, text, optional): Enter any specific technologies or features you plan to use in your store.
* **Innovation** (`innovation`, text, optional): Describe the innovative features or services you plan to offer in your store.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Store Concept** (`store_concept`, text, optional): Describe your proposed store concept in detail.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of store (e.g. retail, restaurant, etc.).
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): Enter the address or location of your proposed store.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): Enter the operating hours of your proposed store.
* **Store Description** (`store_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your proposed store.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill in as much information as possible to help us better understand your proposed store concept.
