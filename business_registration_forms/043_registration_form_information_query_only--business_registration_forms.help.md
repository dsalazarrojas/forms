# Registration Form (Information Query Only) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Form (Information Query Only) is designed to collect basic personal information from users for marketing, customer service, or similar business purposes. Please note that this form is for information query only and is not intended for registration or submission of sensitive data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your relevant information. You do not need to submit this form if you are only browsing the website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`name`, text, optional): This field is a free-text input where you can enter your name.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is a text input where you can enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is an email input where you can enter your email address.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is a date input where you can enter your date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is a time input where you can enter your time of day.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is a text area where you can provide any additional information you want to share with us.
* **Select one** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the dropdown list to indicate your preference.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2
* **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown list to indicate your preference.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the form fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form is for information query only and is not intended for registration or submission of sensitive data.
