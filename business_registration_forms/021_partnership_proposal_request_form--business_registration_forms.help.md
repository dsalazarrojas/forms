# partnership_proposal_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential partners about their proposal, which is then reviewed by designated team members.

## How To Complete This Form
### User Information
* **User Information**: This form section collects information about the person submitting the proposal (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number). 

### Business Information
* **Business Information**: This form section collects details about the company or organization submitting the proposal (Company Name, Address, and any other relevant business information).

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: This form section gathers details about the person or organization's contact information (Address, Phone Number, and Email).

### Partnership Details
* **Partnership Details**: This form section captures information about the proposal itself, including details about the partnership, collaboration, or project.

### Proposal Documents
* **Proposal Documents**: This section is meant for uploading documents related to the proposal.

### Submission
* **Submission**: Please choose 'Yes' if you are ready for your proposal to be reviewed, or 'No' if you would like to submit it for review at a later time.

### Review Status
* **Review Status**: The status of your proposal's review will be displayed here.

### Review Comments
* **Review Comments**: Any comments or feedback from the reviewers will be listed here.

### Reviewer Information
* **Reviewers Name**: The name of the person reviewing your proposal.
* **Reviewer Email**: The email address of the person reviewing your proposal.
* **Reviewers Phone**: The phone number of the person reviewing your proposal.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Any additional notes from the reviewer will be listed here.

### Review Details
* **Review Date**: The date and time of the proposal review.
* **Review Time**: The time of the proposal review.
* **Reviewer Signature**: The signature of the person reviewing your proposal.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth review process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the review team.
* This form is meant to be used for partnership proposals only, please do not use this form for other purposes.
