# Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partner Application Form is a collection of questions designed to help the organization evaluate and assess potential partnership opportunities. This form gathers necessary information about the business or organization applying for partnership and their representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business name and contact person's name in the respective fields.
2.  Provide your business address and phone number.
3.  Enter your email address, website (if applicable), and a brief description of your business.
4.  Select the type of partner you are applying to be (General, Value-Added Service, or Product/Equipment).
5.  Describe the services you offer and the expected value you bring to the partnership.
6.  Specify how you wish to be contacted (Email, Phone, Post, or Fax).
7.  Enter any additional contact details or notes if necessary.
8.  If applicable, provide the start and end dates of the partnership agreement and any specific notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your business or organization.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person representing the business or organization.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your business or organization.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your business email address.
*   **Website** (`website`, text, optional): If your business has a website, enter the URL here.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your business or organization and its activities.
*   **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of partner you wish to be (General, Value-Added Service, or Product/Equipment).
*   **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, optional): Describe the services your business or organization offers.
*   **Expected Value** (`expected_value`, text, optional): Specify the expected value you bring to the partnership.
*   **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): Describe the benefits you expect to gain from the partnership.
*   **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Choose how you wish to be contacted (Email, Phone, Post, or Fax).
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact details.
*   **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, date, optional): If applicable, specify the start and end dates of the partnership agreement.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of the partnership agreement.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the partnership agreement.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the partnership.
*   **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the partnership.
