# wholesale_business_data_collection_form_in_bengali - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business-related data from wholesale businesses for internal registration and processing purposes. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth business registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields marked as "Required"
2. Enter the business entity name as it appears on the business license or registration document
3. Enter the business address, city, state, and country where the business is located
4. Enter the business zip code
5. Enter the business phone number
6. Enter the business email address
7. Enter the business website (if applicable)
8. Enter the business registration number (if applicable)
9. Enter the date of business registration (if applicable)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the company as it appears on the business license or registration document.
* **Business Entity Name** (`business_entity_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the business entity as it appears on the business license or registration document.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, Optional): Enter the street address where the business is located.
* **Business City** (`business_city`, text, Optional): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **Business State** (`business_state`, text, Optional): Enter the state where the business is located.
* **Business Country** (`business_country`, text, Optional): Enter the country where the business is located.
* **Business Zip** (`business_zip`, text, Optional): Enter the zip code where the business is located.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the business.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, Optional): Enter the website of the business (if applicable).
* **Business Reg Num** (`business_reg_num`, text, Optional): Enter the business registration number.
* **Business Reg Date** (`business_reg_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of business registration (if applicable).
