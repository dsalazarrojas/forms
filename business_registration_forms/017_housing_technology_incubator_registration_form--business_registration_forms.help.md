# Housing Technology Incubator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Technology Incubator Registration Form is used to register businesses that wish to participate in the Housing Technology Incubator program. This form is essential for businesses to provide detailed information about their application, business operations, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the Application Name, which should be the name of your business as it appears on all official documents.
- Provide a detailed description of your business in the Application Description field. This is an essential field for our team to understand your business operations.
- Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your official documents in the Business Name field.
- Enter your business address in the Business Address field, ensuring that it is accurate and complete.
- Enter your business email address in the Business Email field. Ensure that this is a valid email address that you frequently check.
- Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field, including the country code.
- If applicable, enter the contact name of the person submitting the application in the Contact Name field.
- For businesses that are part of the incubation program or have a specific job title, enter it in the Job Title field.
- Select one or more roles that describe your business operations by using the Role options (CEOs, Directors, Managers).
- Determine the type of business you are registering by selecting from the Business Type options (Start-up, Incubator, Innovation Program).
- Provide the start and end dates of your incubation in the Date of Incubation Start and Date of Incubation End fields, if applicable.
- Indicate the status of your application using the Application Status options (Pending, Approved, Denied).
- If you have any additional information or comments to provide, enter them in the Notes field.
- Finally, select the type of incubation program your business is registering for using the Incubation Program options (Technology, Business, Research and Development).
- Select one or more innovation categories and fields that describe your business operations using the Innovation Category and Innovation Field options (Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Machine Learning, Computer Vision).
- Select the business sector your company operates in using the Business Sector options (Real Estate, Construction, Healthcare).
- Enter the date you submitted your application in the Application Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Name** (`application_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your business as it appears on all official documents.
* **Application Description** (`application_description`, text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your business operations.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, Optional): Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, Optional): Enter your business address, ensuring that it is accurate and complete.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address that you frequently check.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): If applicable, enter the contact name of the person submitting the application.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Optional): For businesses that are part of the incubation program or have a specific job title, enter it.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more roles that describe your business operations.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, Optional): Determine the type of business you are registering by selecting from the Business Type options.
* **Date of Incubation Start** (`date_of_incubation_start`, date, Optional): Provide the start date of your incubation, if applicable.
* **Date of Incubation End** (`date_of_incubation_end`, date, Optional): Provide the end date of your incubation, if applicable.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, Optional): Indicate the status of your application using the Application Status options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, Optional): If you have any additional information or comments to provide, enter them here.
* **Incubation Program** (`incubation_program`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of incubation program your business is registering for.
* **Innovation Category** (`innovation_category`, select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more innovation categories and fields that describe your business operations.
* **Innovation Field** (`innovation_field`, select_multiple, Optional): Select one or more innovation fields that describe your business operations.
* **Business Sector** (`business_sector`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the business sector your company operates in.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date you submitted your application.
