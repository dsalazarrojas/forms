# Business Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for businesses seeking to form a partnership or strategic alliance with our organization. The goal is to provide a comprehensive understanding of your business and its goals, as well as to determine the suitability of your business for a partnership or alliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents ready for the submission. This includes, but is not limited to, the application date, your name and contact information, and any other relevant details about your business.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and completely, and ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
3.  For fields that require it, provide supporting documentation or attachments, such as proof of business establishment and financial statements.
4.  Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly and all supporting documentation is attached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: The date on which you are submitting your application. This should be in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/01/2023).
* **Applicant Name**: Your full name as it appears on your business documentation.
* **Email Address**: Your business email address where we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Phone Number**: Your business phone number where we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Company Name**: Your business's legal name as it appears on your registration documents.
* **Company Website**: The URL of your business website, if applicable.
* **Years Established**: The length of time your business has been established, in years.
* **Business Structure**: The type of business entity (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation).
* **Industry Sector**: The primary industry your business operates within.
* **Business Description**: A brief description of what your business does and its products or services offered.
* **Mission Statement**: A statement of your business's mission or values, if applicable.
* **Number of Employees**: The approximate number of employees in your business, if applicable.
* **Annual Revenue**: The approximate annual revenue of your business, if applicable.
* **Partnership Type**: The type of partnership or alliance you are seeking (e.g., Strategic Alliance, Joint Venture, Supplier Partnership).
* **Partnership Goals**: A statement of the goals you hope to achieve through the partnership or alliance.
* **Target Market**: A description of the geographic or demographic focus of your business, if applicable.
* **Competitive Advantage**: A description of what makes your business unique or competitive in its industry.
* **Financial Stability**: A description of your business's financial stability, if applicable.
* **Business References**: 2-3 professional references that can vouch for your business's credibility and capabilities.
* **Additional Information**: Any other relevant information about your business that you believe would be helpful for our consideration.
* **I understand partnership requirements**: A confirmation that you understand and agree to the partnership requirements and expectations.
