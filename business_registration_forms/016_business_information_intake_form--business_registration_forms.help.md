# Business Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects the essential details of your business to facilitate our services or verification processes. Please answer these questions accurately to ensure we have the correct information to serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as instructed.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the legal trading name of your business.
* **Registration number** (`registration_number`, text, optional): Provide your business registration or tax ID number, if applicable.
* **Primary contact name** (`primary_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for onboarding your business.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the official email address used for business communications.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter an optional phone number for contact purposes.
* **Business address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the headquarters or mailing address of your business.
* **Industry sector** (`industry_sector`, select one, required): Select the primary industry of your business from the options provided: Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Technology, or Other.
* **Number of employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): Enter an approximate headcount of your business.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your company website if available.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields truthfully to ensure accurate data processing.
- For industry sector, select the option that best represents your primary business type.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
