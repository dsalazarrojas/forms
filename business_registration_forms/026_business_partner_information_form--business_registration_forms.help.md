# Business Partner Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential business information for our records. Please provide accurate and up-to-date details about your company to ensure proper collaboration and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Use the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Company Registration Date** (`company_registration_date`, date, optional): If your company is registered, provide the date of registration. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If your company has a website, provide the URL.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`, text, optional): Describe the job position or role of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the primary business email of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the direct phone number of the contact person.
* **Secondary Contact Name** (`secondary_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the secondary contact person (if applicable).
* **Secondary Contact Email** (`secondary_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the backup contact email (if applicable).
* **Secondary Contact Phone** (`secondary_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the backup phone number (if applicable).
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the physical location of your business.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): If different from the business address, enter the mailing address.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select the primary industry sector of your business (Technology, Finance, Healthcare, Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Other).
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the main activities and products/services offered by your company.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): Approximate the current workforce size.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Select the approximate yearly revenue range of your company (Under $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, $500,000 - $1,000,000, $1,000,000 - $5,000,000, Over $5,000,000).
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, text, optional): Provide the federal tax identification number or EIN.
* **Banking Information** (`bank_information`, text, optional): For payment purposes, provide any relevant banking information.
* **Insurance Details** (`insurance_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant coverage information.
* **Business References** (`business_references`, text, optional): Provide 2-3 professional references (if applicable).
