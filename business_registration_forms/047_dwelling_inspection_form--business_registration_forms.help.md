# Dwelling Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the results of an inspection of a dwelling or building.
## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps outlined above to complete this form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dwelling Type** (`dwelling_type`): Choose the type of dwelling being inspected from the options provided.
* **Dwelling Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the dwelling being inspected.
* **Inspection Location** (`location`): Specify the location of the inspection.
* **Date Of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector`): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector License** (`inspector_license`): Check yes or no if the inspector has a license.
* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`): Enter the name of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Property Manager Phone** (`property_manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of the property manager (if applicable).
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Duration** (`inspection_duration`): Enter the duration of the inspection.
* **Dwelling Age** (`dwelling_age`): Enter an estimate of the dwelling's age.
* **Dwelling Condition** (`dwelling_condition`): Describe the overall condition of the dwelling.
* **Foundation** (`foundation`): Describe the condition of the foundation of the dwelling.
* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`): Describe the condition of the roof of the dwelling.
* **Walls** (`walls`): Describe the condition of the walls of the dwelling.
* **Windows** (`windows`): Describe the condition of the windows of the dwelling.
* **Doors** (`doors`): Describe the condition of the doors of the dwelling.
* **Floor Plan** (`floor_plan`): Describe the layout and design of the floor plan of the dwelling.
* **Electrical** (`electrical`): Describe the condition of the electrical system of the dwelling.
* **Plumbing** (`plumbing`): Describe the condition of the plumbing system of the dwelling.
* **Heating** (`heating`): Describe the condition of the heating system of the dwelling.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any comments or observations about the dwelling.
