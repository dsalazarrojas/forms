# Green Retail Architecture Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the Green Retail Architecture program. It is intended for store owners and managers who want to join the program and contribute to its sustainability goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details, including name, in the "Participant Details" field.
2. Provide your name and other relevant information in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Select the type of store you manage from the "Store Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your store's location in the "Location" field.
5. Provide your store's operating hours in the "Store Hours" field.
6. Describe your store in a few words in the "Store Description" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
9. Select your areas of interest from the "Participant Interests" dropdown menu (you can select multiple options).
10. If you have a specific sustainability commitment, enter it in the "Sustainability Commitment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details, including name, to help us get to know you better.
* **Participant Name**: Provide your name, so we can address you personally.
* **Store Type**: Choose the type of store you manage from the options below:
	+ Green Store: A store that prioritizes sustainability and eco-friendliness.
	+ Online Store: A store that operates primarily online.
	+ Brick and Mortar Store: A physical store with a fixed location.
	+ Other: A store that doesn't fit into the above categories.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your store, so we can get a sense of where you operate.
* **Store Hours**: Enter your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Store Description**: Briefly describe your store and what makes it unique.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can reach you if needed.
* **Participant Interests**: Select the areas where your store has the most interest (you can select multiple options):
	+ Sustainability: Focus on reducing waste and carbon footprint.
	+ Retail: Emphasize customer experience and retail innovation.
	+ Community: Engage with your local community and support local initiatives.
	+ Education: Provide educational resources and workshops to customers.
	+ Other: Other interests not listed above.
* **Sustainability Commitment**: If you have specific sustainability goals or initiatives, enter them here.

# Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure we can get in touch with you and provide you with the right information.
* Select all relevant interests to help us tailor our support to your specific needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
