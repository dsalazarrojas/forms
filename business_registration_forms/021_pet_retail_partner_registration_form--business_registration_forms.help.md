# Pet Retail Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential pet retail partners for business operations and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out (indicated by an asterisk *) as they are essential for processing your registration.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it, as incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to delays or errors in processing your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your business phone number for easy communication.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your business address.
* **Retailer Details** (`retailer_details`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the provided options to describe your business (Retails Partner, Other, Pet Shop, Pet Store).
* **Brand Logo** (`brand_logo`, `text`, not required): Please enter your business logo name.
* **Product Lines** (`product_lines`, `select_one`, not required): Select a category from the provided options that best describes the product lines of your business (Pet Food, Pet Toy, Pet Grooming, Pet Health Supplements).
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, not required): Enter your business website URL if applicable.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more locations where your store is located (City, State, Country, Zip Code).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues during the submission process, contact [support email/department].
