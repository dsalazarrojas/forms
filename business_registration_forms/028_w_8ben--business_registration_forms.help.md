# W 8Ben - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for business registrations and is intended to collect accurate and complete information. The form collects information about the business, including name, address, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the form ID (a unique identifier for the form submission).
2. Select the tax year (the year the form is being submitted for).
3. Enter your name as it appears on your business documents.
4. Enter your business address.
5. Enter the city where your business is located.
6. Enter the state where your business is located.
7. Enter the zip code for your business location.
8. Enter your business name (as it appears on your business documents).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): This is a unique identifier for the form submission. It is not required to be filled out but is useful for tracking and reference purposes.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): This field is used to select the tax year for which the form is being submitted.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): This is where you enter your name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business address here.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code for your business location.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name as it appears on your business documents.
