# Green Manufacturing Standard Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information, organizational details, sustainability information, and confirmations from registrants for the Green Manufacturing Standard.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields on each page.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **Name**: `general_information` (`text`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your name, which will be used to identify you in this registration process.
* **General Information (Page 1)**: `title` (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your general information.
### Organization Details (Page 2)
* **Organization Name**: `organization_details` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization Address**: `organization_details` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your organization's address as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization Contact**: `organization_details` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your organization's contact information as it appears on official documents.
### Sustainability Information (Page 3)
* **Sustainability Information**: `sustainability_information` (`text`, required/optional): Enter your organization's sustainability information as it appears on official documents.
* **Sustainability Details**: `sustainability_information` (`text`, required/optional): Enter additional details about your organization's sustainability information.
### Confirmations (Page 4)
* **Confirmations**: `confirmations` (`text`, required/optional): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields have completed responses before proceeding to the next page.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, contact form support at form@gic.mx.
