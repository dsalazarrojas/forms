# Food System Digitalization Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for registering your food system digitalization program. This includes details about the program name, type, location, participants, interests, and other relevant information. The form is designed to help us understand your program and provide you with the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the **Food System Digitalization Program Name** in the first field. This is the title of your program, and it will be displayed on our platform.
2. Select the **Program Type** from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Food Innovation Hub", "NGO", "Innovation Hub", or "Other".
3. Enter your **Location** in the text field. This is where your program is located.
4. Provide **Participant Details** in the text field. This information will help us understand who is participating in your program.
5. Select your **Participant Interests** from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Digitalization", "Food Security", "Food Safety", or "Other".
6. If your program is not for public display, select **Consent** as "False". If it is, select "True".
7. Enter your **Email** address in the text field. This is required for communication purposes.
8. Provide your **Phone** number in the text field.
9. Add any additional **Note** about your program in the text field.
10. Enter your **Other Program Details** in the text field. This is optional but can provide further context about your program.
11. Select the **Output File** for your program. This will be used for further processing and tracking.
12. Assign the **Assigned Tool** for your program. This will be used for task delegation and management.
13. Enter your **Form IDs** in the text field. This is a unique identifier for your form.
14. Enter the **Category** of your form in the text field. This is a categorization for your form.
15. Provide a brief **Description** of your form in the text field.
16. Enter the **Form Title** in the text field. This will be the title of your form.
17. Select the **Form Category** from the dropdown menu.
18. Enter the **Form Description** in the text field.
19. Assign the **Form Assigned Tool** for your form.
20. Select the **Form Output File** for your form.
21. Enter the **Form Version** in the text field. This will track the version of your form.
22. If you want to track the last updated date and time of your form, you can select the **Form Last Updated** as a date and **Form Last Updated Time** as a time.
23. If you want to track who last updated your form, enter their **Form Last Updated By** in the text field.
24. If you want to track the IP of the last person who updated your form, enter it in the **Form Last Updated IP** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your food system digitalization program.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your program from the dropdown menu.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of your program.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Provide details about the participants in your program.
* **Participant Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the interests of the participants in your program.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): If your program is not for public display, select "False". If it is, select "True".
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional note about your program.
* **Other Program Details** (`other_program_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your program.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required): Select the output file for your program.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Assign the tool for your program.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, required): Enter your form ID.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Enter the category of your form.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your form.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your form.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, required): Select the category of your form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your form.
* **Form Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, text, required): Assign the tool for your form.
* **Form Output File** (`form_output_file`, text, required): Select the output file for your form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, required): Enter the version of your form.
* **Form Last Updated** (`form_last_updated`, date, optional): If you want to track the last updated date of your form.
* **Form Last Updated Time** (`form_last_updated_time`, time, optional): If you want to track the last updated time of your form.
* **Form Last Updated By** (`form_last_updated_by`, text, optional): If you want to track who last updated your form.
* **Form Last Updated IP** (`form_last_updated_ip`, text, optional): If you want to track the IP of the last person who updated your form.

## Tips
