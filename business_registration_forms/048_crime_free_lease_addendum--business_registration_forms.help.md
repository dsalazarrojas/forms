# Crime Free Lease Addendum - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide explains the purpose and requirements of a "Crime Free Lease Addendum" form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Answer each question accurately and completely.
3. Review and agree with the terms and conditions of the addendum.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Site Address (Page 1)

* **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the rented property.

### Site Location (Page 1)

* **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location of the property, including any notable features or landmarks.

### Crime Free Status (Page 2)

* **Crime Free Status** (`crime_free_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select "True" if the property is free from any criminal activity.
	+ Select "False" if the property has a history of criminal activity or has been involved in any crimes.

### Site Type (Page 2)

* **Site Type** (`site_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of site:
		- Commercial
		- Residential
		- Retail

### Lease Start Date (Page 3)

* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the lease.

### Lease End Date (Page 3)

* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the lease.

### Lease Term (Page 4)

* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, number, required: false): Enter the term of the lease in months or years.

### Assigned User (Page 4)

* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the assigned user or contact person.

## Tips

* Review and understand the terms and conditions of the lease before completing this form.
* Ensure you answer each question accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the assigned user or property owner.
