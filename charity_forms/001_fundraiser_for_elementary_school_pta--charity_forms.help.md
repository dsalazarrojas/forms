# Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage a digital fundraiser form for Elementary School PTA. It is meant to collect and display information about the fundraiser campaign in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Digital Fundraiser Form" page.
2. On this page, you will find fields for various pieces of information related to the fundraiser, such as "Organization Info", "Fundraiser Details", and "Location".
3. Fill out the relevant fields as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Info (Text)**: This field is for entering any additional details or information about the organization or group behind the fundraiser.
* **Fundraiser Details (Text)**: This field is for entering details about the fundraiser, such as its purpose, objective, or specifics.
* **Fundraising Goal (Number)**: Enter the fundraising goal of the campaign.
* **Start Time (Time)**: Enter the start date and time of the fundraiser.
* **End Time (Time)**: Enter the end date and time of the fundraiser.
* **Location (Text)**: Enter the location of the fundraiser.
* **Description (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the fundraiser.
* **Images (Text)**: Upload or enter relevant images related to the fundraiser.
* **Donors (Select One)**: Choose the type of donors involved (PTA, School, Community, or Other).
* **Amount Raised (Number)**: Enter the total amount raised for the fundraiser.
* **Share Form (Select Multiple)**: Choose how to share the form (Text, Email, Social Media).
* **Share Message (Text)**: Enter a message to share with the selected platforms.
* **Share via (Text)**: Enter the platforms to share the form on.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address to share the form on.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number to share the form on.
* **Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the fundraiser.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the fundraiser.
* **Form Status (Select One)**: Choose the status of the form (Published, Unpublished, Archived).
* **Form Owner (Text)**: Enter the name of the form owner.
* **Organization (Text)**: Enter the name of the organization or group behind the fundraiser.
* **Form ID (Number)**: Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **User ID (Number)**: Enter a unique ID for the user.
* **User Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the user.
* **Submit (Text)**: Click this button to submit the form.
