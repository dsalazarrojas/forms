# Car Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Club Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from users for car club registration purposes. This includes personal details, vehicle attributes, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2.  Enter your full name and email address in the required fields.
3.  Provide your phone number and postal address (if applicable).
4.  Specify your vehicle year, make, and model accurately.
5.  List any notable modifications to your vehicle.
6.  Choose the relevant membership type for your vehicle (Regular, Family, Lifetime, or Student).
7.  If applicable, provide an emergency contact name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full name
*   **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
### 2. Email address
*   **Email address**: Enter your preferred contact email for club communication.
### 3. Phone number
*   **Phone number**: Enter your mobile or landline phone number for emergency contact.
### 4. Postal address
*   **Postal address**: Enter your street address for official correspondence, if applicable.
### 5. Vehicle year
*   **Vehicle year**: Enter the four-digit year of manufacture for your vehicle.
### 6. Vehicle make
*   **Vehicle make**: Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, or BMW.
### 7. Vehicle model
*   **Vehicle model**: Enter the specific model of your vehicle, e.g., Corolla or F-150.
### 8. Notable modifications
*   **Notable modifications**: List any major aftermarket changes or upgrades to your vehicle.
### 9. Membership type
*   **Membership type**: Choose the applicable membership type for your vehicle (Regular, Family, Lifetime, or Student).
### 10. Emergency contact
*   **Emergency contact name and phone**: Provide a contact name and phone number in case of an emergency.
