This guide is for the orphanage_donation_form, a form designed to collect information from potential donors for the orphanage donation process. The form collects data on donation amounts, contact information, and other details necessary for the donation process. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the donation amount you wish to donate.
2. Choose the frequency of your donation (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
3. Select the date of your donation.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. If desired, enter a message or any additional information you'd like to share with the orphanage.
6. Select your preferred payment type (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash).
7. Indicate your preferred payment method (Online, Offline).
8. Enter your donor name and relationship with the orphanage (e.g., Friend, Family, Colleague).
9. Enter a message for the orphanage, if desired.
10. Enter the name of the orphanage you are donating to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (Number): Enter the amount you wish to donate. This field is not required.
* **Donation Frequency** (Select Multiple): Choose the frequency of your donation from the options provided (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly). This field is not required.
* **Donation Date** (Date): Select the date you wish to make the donation. This field is not required.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for the orphanage. This field is not required.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter your email address. This field is not required.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number. This field is not required.
* **Message** (Note): Enter any message you'd like to share with the orphanage. This field is not required.
* **Payment Type** (Select One): Choose your preferred payment type from the options provided (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash). This field is not required.
* **Payment Method** (Select Multiple): Indicate your preferred payment method from the options provided (Online, Offline). This field is not required.
* **Donor Name** (Text): Enter your name as the donor. This field is not required.
* **Donor Relationship** (Select One): Choose your relationship with the orphanage from the options provided (Friend, Family, Colleague). This field is not required.
* **Donor Message** (Text): Enter any message you'd like to share with the orphanage. This field is not required.
* **Orphanage Name** (Text): Enter the name of the orphanage you are donating to. This field is not required.
