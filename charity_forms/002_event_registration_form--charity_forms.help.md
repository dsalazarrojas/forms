# Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Registration Form is used to collect and store information about events for the organization. This form helps to ensure that all events are properly registered and documented, making it easier to track and manage events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Event Registration Form" link or access it through the provided URL.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_start_date** (Date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **event_end_date** (Date): Enter the end date of the event.
* **event_organizer** (Text): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **event_location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
* **event_description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **event_time** (Time): Enter the time of the event.
* **event_type** (Select One): Select "Yes" if the event is of type "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **event_category** (Select Multiple): Select all categories that apply to the event (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **user_name** (Text): Enter your name.
* **user_email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **user_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **user_organization** (Text): Enter your organization name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the event you're registering is not a duplicate.
* Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, contact your supervisor or support team for guidance.
