# Fundraising Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by charities and fundraising organizers to register for a fundraising event. The form collects information about the charity and the fundraiser.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first page with general information about your fundraiser.
2. Fill in the second page with payment information.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Organizer** (`name_of_organizer`): Enter your name or the name of your organization.
* **Name of Organization** (`name_of_organization`): Enter the name of the organization or group raising funds for the charity.
* **Name of Charity** (`charity_name`): Enter the name of the charity being supported.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`): Select the method of payment (e.g. bank transfer, credit card, etc.).
* **Payment Options** (`payment_option_1` to `payment_option_20`): Select the additional payment options (e.g. donation amount, payment frequency, etc.). 

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the charity directly for assistance.
