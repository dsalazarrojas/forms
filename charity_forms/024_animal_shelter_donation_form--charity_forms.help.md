# Animal Shelter Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donation information from individuals who wish to support the local animal shelter. It allows donors to provide their personal details, specify their donation amount, frequency, and focus area, as well as payment method and preference for recognition. The form ensures that donors can specify how they would like their donation to be recognized and if they would like to receive updates from the shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
2. Specify your donation details (Donation Amount, Donation Frequency, and Donation Focus Area).
3. Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or PayPal).
4. Confirm if your donation is tax-deductible.
5. Select your recognition preferences (Public List, Website Donor Roll, Annual Report Only, or No Public Recognition).
6. Decide if you want to subscribe to the shelter's newsletter.
7. If you have a message or dedication for the donation, provide it in the "Message to the Shelter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates or receipts.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address**: Enter your residential street address (optional).
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside (optional).
* **State**: Enter your state or province of residence (optional).
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code (optional).
* **Donation Amount**: Specify the amount you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to donate (One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
* **Donation Focus Area**: Select the area of focus for your donation (Medical Care, Food and Supplies, Shelter Operations, Animal Rescue, Adoption Programs, Education, or Undesignated).
* **Donation in Honor of**: If you are making a donation in honor of someone, enter their name (optional).
* **Donation in Memory of**: If this is a memorial donation, enter the name of the person you are honoring (optional).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to make your donation (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or PayPal).
* **Employer Matching Available**: Confirm if your employer matches donations (optional).
* **Employer Name**: Enter your company name if you would like to take advantage of the employer matching program (optional).
* **Donor Preferences**: Specify your preference for recognition (Public List, Website Donor Roll, Annual Report Only, or No Public Recognition).
* **Keep Donation Anonymous**: Decide if you prefer to keep your donation anonymous.
* **Recognition Level**: Choose how you would like to be recognized (Public List, Website Donor Roll, Annual Report Only, or No Public Recognition).
* **Subscribe to Newsletter**: Decide if you would like to receive updates and stories from the shelter.
* **Confirm Tax-Deductible Status**: Confirm if this donation is tax-deductible.
* **Message to the Shelter**: Provide any message or dedication for the donation (optional).
