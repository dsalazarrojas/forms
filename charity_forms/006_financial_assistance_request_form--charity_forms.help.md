# Financial Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Assistance Request Form is a simple and easy-to-use form designed to capture demographic and financial information from individuals or families in need of financial assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your name and email address under "applicant name" and "applicant email" sections.
2. Enter your household income.
3. Select whether you need financial aid by answering "financial aid needed".
4. If you need financial aid, enter the amount required under "financial aid amount".
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Choose your current employment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Assistance Request Form Data**: This is the main section where you can provide a brief description of your financial situation.
* **Applicant name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Applicant email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Household income**: Enter your household's total monthly income.
* **Financial aid needed**: Select 'Yes' if you require financial assistance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Financial aid amount**: If you selected 'Yes' for financial aid needed, enter the required amount.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Employment status**: Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Employed
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Retired
