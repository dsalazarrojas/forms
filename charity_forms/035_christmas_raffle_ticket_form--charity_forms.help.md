# Christmas Raffle Ticket Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Raffle Ticket Form is designed to gather information from customers or individuals interested in purchasing raffle tickets for a charity event. The form is divided into several sections to ensure accurate and efficient data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Purchaser Information** section with your details:
	* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
	* Enter your **Email Address**.
	* Enter your **Phone Number**.
	* Enter your **Mailing Address**.
	* Enter your **City** and **State or Province**.
	* Enter your **ZIP or Postal Code**.
2. If you are representing an organization, fill out the **Organization Information** section:
	* Enter your organization's **Name** (optional).
3. Purchase **Raffle Tickets**:
	* Select the **Number of Tickets** you wish to purchase.
	* Set the **Price Per Ticket**.
	* Calculate the **Total Cost**.
4. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**:
	* Select the payment method you prefer (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit card, Debit card, Online payment, or Other).
5. Set your **Payment Status**:
	* Choose how you wish to make the payment (e.g., Paid in full, Partial payment, Balance due, or Installments arranged).
6. Choose whether to **Notify me if I win**:
	* Select if you want to be notified if you win (True or False).
7. Set your **Communication Preferences**:
	* Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail, or Either).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 2, type: text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 3, type: text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (id: 4, type: email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5, type: text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (id: 6, type: text, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (id: 7, type: text, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (id: 8, type: text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (id: 9, type: text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Organization Name** (id: 11, type: text, optional): Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Number of Tickets** (id: 13, type: number, required): Select the number of raffle tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Price Per Ticket** (id: 14, type: number, required): Set the price per ticket.
* **Total Cost** (id: 15, type: number, required): Calculate the total cost.
* **Payment Method** (id: 19, type: select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit card, Debit card, Online payment, or Other).
* **Payment Status** (id: 20, type: select_one, required): Choose how you wish to make the payment (e.g., Paid in full, Partial payment, Balance due, Installments arranged).
* **Notify me if I win** (id: 24, type: select_one, required): Select if you want to be notified if you win (True or False).
* **Communication Preferences** (id: 23, type: select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, Mail, or Either).
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (id: 25, type: text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
