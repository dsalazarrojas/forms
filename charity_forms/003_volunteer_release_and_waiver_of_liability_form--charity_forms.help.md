# Volunteer Release And Waiver Of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new volunteers for onboarding purposes. It helps to gather essential details about the volunteer, including their name, contact information, and emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the volunteer's information, following the instructions below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out. However, please note that most fields are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter the name of the volunteer.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the volunteer.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the volunteer.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the volunteer and the emergency contact person.
* **Volunteer Role**: Enter the role the volunteer will be taking on.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the volunteer will start working. If the volunteer is already working, please select the current date.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the volunteer will stop working. If the volunteer is already working, please select the current date.
* **Hours Worked**: Enter the number of hours the volunteer has worked.

Note that most fields are optional, and you can fill out as much or as little information as you need to. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate onboarding of the volunteer.
