# Voluntary Resignation Form Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voluntary Resignation Form Report is used to document an employee's voluntary resignation, including their reason for leaving, effective date, and other necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have the employee's consent and understanding of the process before proceeding.
2.  Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
3.  Sign the form in the presence of the manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department where the employee works.
* **Date of last visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the last visit or interaction with the employee.
* **Reason for leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, required): Describe the reason for the employee's voluntary resignation.
* **Effective date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the employee's resignation will take effect.
* **Last day of work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, optional): Enter the last date the employee will work.
* **Manager's name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Company** (`company`, select_one, optional): Select whether the resignation is company-wide or departmental.
* **Manager sign date** (`manager_sign_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the manager signed the form.
* **Employee signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Enter the employee's signature, usually in the presence of the manager.
* **Manager signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Enter the manager's signature, usually in the presence of HR or another authorized representative.
