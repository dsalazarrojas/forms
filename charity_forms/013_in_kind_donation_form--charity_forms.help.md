# In Kind Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Kind Donation Form is a tool used to collect information about non-monetary contributions, such as goods or services, that are given to support a cause or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill in the charity name**: Enter the name of the charity or organization you are donating to.
2. **Describe the item(s) donated**: Briefly describe the goods or services you are offering. Be as detailed as possible to help the charity or organization understand the value of your contribution.
3. **Enter the value of the item(s)**: Provide an estimated value of the goods or services offered, if applicable.
4. **Provide your name and contact information**: Enter your name, email (if available), and phone number to enable the charity or organization to contact you for further details.
5. **Select the type of donation**: Choose the type of donation from the provided options: goods, services, or volunteer time.
6. **Add any additional notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Charity Name**: Enter the name of the charity or organization you are donating to. This will help the charity or organization understand who you are supporting and ensure your contribution is credited correctly.
* **Item Description**: Briefly describe the goods or services you are offering. This will help the charity or organization understand the value and specifics of your contribution.
* **Item Value**: If you are able to estimate a value for the goods or services offered, enter it here. This helps the charity or organization to assess the monetary value of your contribution.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title (if applicable) to enable the charity or organization to contact you for further details.
* **Contact Email**: If you have an email address, enter it here to ensure the charity or organization can contact you efficiently.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number to allow the charity or organization to contact you for further information.
* **Donation Type**: Select the type of donation you are making from the provided options: goods, services, or volunteer time.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about the donation, such as any conditions or restrictions that apply.

**Tips**

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the goods or services you are offering.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the charity or organization directly.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to submit in-kind donations on behalf of your organization or entity.
