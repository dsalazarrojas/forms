# Nonprofit Partners Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Partners Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a partnership between a nonprofit organization and another entity. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of their roles, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On page 1, provide a brief description of the partnership and its purpose.
2. On page 2, select one of the options (Partner Name, Partner Contact, or Agreement Terms) that best describes the nature of the partnership.
3. On page 3, add any additional information about the partnership.
4. On page 4, select all relevant contact information for the partner (Partner Name, Partner Position, and Partner Contact Info).
5. On page 5, describe the terms of the agreement in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Nonprofit Partners Agreement Form 1
* **Nonprofit Partners Agreement Form 1** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the partnership and its purpose.

### Page 2: Partnership Details
* **Partnership Details** (`type: select_one`, optional):
	+ **Partner Name**: The name of the partner organization or individual.
	+ **Partner Contact**: The contact information of the partner.
	+ **Agreement Terms**: A brief description of the terms of the agreement.

### Page 3: Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`type: text`, optional): Add any additional details about the partnership.

### Page 4: Partner Contact Information
* **Partner Contact Information** (`type: select_multiple`, optional):
	+ **Partner Name**: The name of the partner organization or individual.
	+ **Partner Position**: The position or title of the partner contact person.
	+ **Partner Contact Info**: The contact information of the partner contact person.

### Page 5: Agreement Terms
* **Agreement Terms** (`type: text`, optional): Describe the terms of the agreement in detail.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or consult with the partner.
