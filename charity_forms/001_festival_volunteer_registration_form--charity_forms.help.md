# Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for festival volunteers to register their interest and availability for a specific festival event. It is a straightforward form with several sections that help us get the most accurate information about your volunteer profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select your availability for the festival (Available Slots).
3. Choose the areas you are interested in volunteering for (Interest Areas).
4. If you have any special skills or talents, please let us know (Special Talents).
5. Provide a contact phone number in case we need to reach out to you (Contact Phone).
6. Write a brief message about yourself and how you can contribute to the festival (Volunteer Message).
7. Confirm your email address (Confirm Email) and contact phone number (Confirm Phone) to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, required): This is the name of the festival event you are interested in volunteering for. Please enter the correct name as it appears on our event schedule.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): This is your first name. Please enter your real name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): This is your last name. Please enter your real name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): This is your email address. Please enter a working email address we can use to contact you.
* **Volunteer Start Time** (Volunteer Start Time, time, optional): This is the start time you are available to volunteer. Please select the time that suits you best.
* **Volunteer End Time** (Volunteer End Time, time, optional): This is the end time you are available to volunteer. Please select the time that suits you best.
* **Available Slots** (Available Slots, select multiple, optional): Select all the time slots that you are available to volunteer for. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Interest Areas** (Interest Areas, select multiple, optional): Select all the areas you are interested in volunteering for. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Special Talents** (Special Talents, select multiple, optional): If you have any special skills or talents, please let us know. This will help us place you in the right roles.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): This is your contact phone number. Please enter a working phone number we can use to reach out to you.
* **Volunteer Message** (Volunteer Message, text, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide more information about yourself and how you can contribute to the festival. Please keep it brief.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, select one, optional): Confirm your email address. Please select 'Yes' if your email is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (Confirm Phone, select one, optional): Confirm your contact phone number. Please select 'Yes' if your phone number is accurate.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have submitted the form only once.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your submission and get back to you soon.
* Thank you for your interest in volunteering for our festival!
