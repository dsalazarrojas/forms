# donate-form-title - Help Guide
## Purpose
The <b>donate-form-title</b> form is designed to collect donations for a charity. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form, which helps us better understand the donor's details and preferences for their contribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To start, please select the page that best represents your contribution. You can choose from the following options:
* <a href="#donation_page">Donation Page</a>

Please proceed to the <a href="#donor_information">Donor Information</a> page, which requests additional details to help us understand your contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Page** (`donation_page`, text, required/optional): This is the initial page where you can describe your contribution. Be as detailed and concise as possible in your description. 
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options to provide more information about your donation. This helps us to better understand the purpose behind your contribution.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): If you wish to provide a specific amount for your donation, you can enter it here.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable options for how you would like to make your donation. 
* **Charity Choice** (`charity_choice`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the options to choose a charity for your donation. 
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, note, required/optional): Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your information to us. Note that this is not a real submit button but an instruction.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): If you want to specify a particular date for your donation, please select the corresponding date. 
* **Donation Time** (`donation_time`, time, optional): If you want to add a specific time for your donation, you can provide it here. 
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted regarding your donation, please enter your email address. 
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide your phone number for further contact. 
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please provide your address for further contact and communication. 
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide the city for your contact information, please do so here. 
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Please select the state for your contact information. 
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for further contact and communication. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding. 
* You can always contact us with any questions or concerns about your donation. 

Please note that this is a basic guide and might not cover all possible edge cases. For more information on the form, please contact the support team. 

This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and explanations. If there are any discrepancies or unclear information, please let me know.
