# Rotary Club Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Club Membership Application Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals interested in joining the Rotary Club. This form helps the membership team assess applications and ensures that all necessary details are collected for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Reason for joining the Rotary Club.
3. List any Previous Affiliations you may have.
4. Describe your expected Privileges (e.g., attending events) and Responsibilities (e.g., participating in community service projects).
5. Enter your Email address.
6. Provide your Phone number.
7. Select one option from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Required: False): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Required: False): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Reason for joining** (Reason for joining, Text, Required: False): Describe your motivation for joining the Rotary Club, such as wanting to give back to the community or be part of a like-minded group.
* **Previous Affiliations** (Previous Affiliations, Text, Required: False): List any previous membership or affiliations with other charitable organizations or community groups.
* **Privileges** (Privileges, Text, Required: False): As a member, you will have access to our annual gala and other events. Describe how you plan to utilize these privileges.
* **Responsibilities** (Responsibilities, Text, Required: False): You will be expected to participate in our community service projects. Describe how you plan to contribute your time and skills to these projects.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required: False): Enter a valid Email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required: False): Enter your Phone number.
* **Select One** (Select One, Select One, Required: False): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
