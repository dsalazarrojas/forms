# Rotary Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rotary Membership Form is used to collect and track membership applications and information for the Rotary Club organization. This form helps ensure accurate and organized data entry for efficient management of membership details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form accurately**: Double-check that all required fields (marked with *) are filled in correctly.
2. **Select the correct Rotary Club**: Choose the correct club that you are applying to or affiliated with.
3. **Choose the correct Membership Type**: Select the type of membership you are applying for.
4. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out the form, click the submit button to send your application for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Rotary Club**: Select the Rotary Club you are applying to or affiliated with.
* **Membership Type**: Choose the type of membership you are applying for (e.g., Type 1 or Type 2).
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Application Fee**: Enter the fee you paid for your membership application.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Assigned Tool**: Not applicable, but this field seems to be a duplicate of the "Assigned To" field.
* **Assigned Tool ID**: Not applicable, but this field seems to be a duplicate of the "Assigned To ID" field.
* **Assigned Tool User**: Not applicable, but this field seems to be a duplicate of the "Assigned To User" field.
* **Submitted At**: This field is likely for tracking when the form was submitted, but it is not visible to the user.
* **Submitted By**: This field is likely for tracking who submitted the form, but it is not visible to the user.
* **Submitted User**: Enter the name of the user who submitted the form. This field seems to be a duplicate of the "Submitted By" field.
* **Updated At**: This field is likely for tracking when the form was updated, but it is not visible to the user.
* **Updated By**: This field is likely for tracking who updated the form, but it is not visible to the user.
* **Created At**: This field is likely for tracking when the form was created, but it is not visible to the user.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the user who created the form. This field seems to be a duplicate of the "Submitted User" field.
* **Assigned To**: Select the user(s) you would like to be assigned to review or manage your application.
* **Assigned To ID**: Enter the ID of the user(s) you assigned to review or manage your application.
* **Assigned To User**: Enter the name(s) of the user(s) you assigned to review or manage your application.
* **Submitted Status**: Choose the status of your application (e.g., Status 1 or Status 2).
* **Submitted Status ID**: Not applicable, but this field seems to be a duplicate of the "Submitted Status" field.

Note: The fields "Assigned Tool", "Assigned Tool ID", "Assigned Tool User" are not relevant and can be removed. The fields "Submitted User", "Assigned To ID", "Assigned To User" are also not visible to the user and can be removed or made hidden. The field "Submitted Status ID" is also not applicable and can be removed.
