# Community Needs Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Needs Assessment Template is a tool used to gather data from individuals or families to identify areas of need within a community. It helps organizations provide effective support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please note that the form is best completed by one person per household, and all fields apply to the entire household unless otherwise specified.**
1. **Respondent name**: Enter your full name for the purpose of record-keeping.
2. **Household size**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
3. **Employment status**: Select one of the following options that applies to your current situation:
	* Employed
	* Unemployed
	* Student
	* Retired
4. **Approximate monthly income**: Enter your approximate monthly income.
5. **Housing status**: Select one of the following options that applies to your current situation:
	* Owned
	* Rented
	* Temporary
	* Homeless
6. **Household needs**: Select all needs that apply to your household:
	* Food assistance
	* Utility support
	* Healthcare access
	* Childcare
	* Job training
7. **Number of children in household**: Enter the number of children living in your household (if applicable).
8. **Disabilities or health needs**: If you have any special health requirements, please describe them.
9. **Additional comments**: If you have any other relevant information that may help us better serve your needs, please provide it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name (Respondent name, text, required)**: Your full name for record-keeping purposes.
* **Household size (Household size, number, required)**: Number of people living in your household.
* **Employment status (Employment status, select_one, required)**: Your current employment situation.
* **Approximate monthly income (Approximate monthly income, number, required)**: Your approximate monthly income.
* **Housing status (Housing status, select_one, required)**: Your current housing situation.
* **Household needs (Household needs, select_multiple, required)**: Select all needs that apply to your household.
* **Number of children in household (Number of children in household, number, required)**: Number of children living in your household.
* **Disabilities or health needs (Disabilities or health needs, text, required)**: Any special health requirements.
* **Additional comments (Additional comments, text, required)**: Any other relevant information.
* **Additional comments (Additional comments, text, required)**: Any other relevant information.
