# Non Profit Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Profit Dinner RSVP Form is designed to collect RSVPs from guests attending a non-profit dinner event. It allows you to provide essential details such as name, contact information, and attendance details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a asterisk symbol (\*).
2. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you with any updates or changes.
4. If you are attending with someone else, select the correct option from the "Attending with" dropdown menu.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
6. Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing with you in the "Guest Count" field.
7. Optionally, add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.
8. Select whether you will be attending the event by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Will Attend" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you with any updates or changes.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any last-minute updates or changes.
- **Attending with**: Select the correct option to indicate who you will be attending with (Myself, Spouse, Friend, or Family).
- **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them here (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
- **Guest Count**: Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing with you.
- **Comments**: Optionally, provide any additional information or comments about your attendance.
- **Will Attend**: Select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, or "No" if you will not be attending.
