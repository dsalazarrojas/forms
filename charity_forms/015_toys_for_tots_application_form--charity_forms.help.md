# Toys For Tots Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Toys For Tots Application Form" is a tool to gather information from individuals or organizations interested in supporting the Toys For Tots program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice from the provided list.
3. For fields with text inputs, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Review your application before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or organization name as you would like to be listed as a sponsor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address or physical location.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your donation, provide it in this field.
* **Donation Time** (`donation_time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time for your donation, specify it here.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Type of Donation** (`select_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of donation you would like to make:
	+ Gift: A physical item, such as toys, books, or clothing.
	+ Monetary: A monetary contribution to support the Toys For Tots program directly.
	+ Other: Describe your unique donation in the "Additional Comments" field.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or additional information, provide it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and submit your application carefully to avoid errors.
* Provide accurate and complete information to increase the chances of successful review and approval.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Toys For Tots committee for assistance.
