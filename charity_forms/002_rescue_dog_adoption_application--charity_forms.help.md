# rescue_dog_adoption_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rescue dog adoption application form is designed to gather information about you and your household to help us find the best match for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and contact information (Fields 1-3: "Name", "Address", and "Email" or "Phone").
2. Answer the questions about the number of people in your household (Field 5: "How many people in the household?").
3. Provide information about any pets you already own (Field 7: "Pets Owned").
4. Select the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Field 8: "Type of pet owned").
5. Indicate whether or not you own any pets currently (Field 9: "Age of pet").
6. Specify the gender of any pets you currently own (Field 10: "Gender of pet").
7. Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Field 11: "Desired Pet").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Field 1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (Field 2, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Email** (Field 3, text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Field 4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **How many people in the household?** (Field 5, number, required): Indicate the number of people living in your household.
* **Other Household Members** (Field 6, text, required): List any other people living in your household.
* **Pets Owned** (Field 7, select multiple, required): Select from the options (Dog, Cat, Other) that describe the type of pet you currently own.
* **Type of pet owned** (Field 8, select one, required): Select the type of pet you currently own (Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Age of pet** (Field 9, select one, required): Indicate whether you currently own pets (Yes, No).
* **Gender of pet** (Field 10, select one, required): If you own pets, choose the gender of the pets you own (Male, Female, Other).
* **Desired Pet** (Field 11, select one, required): Choose the type of pet you are interested in adopting (Dog, Cat, Other).
