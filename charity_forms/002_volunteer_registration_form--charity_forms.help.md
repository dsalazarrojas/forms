# Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Registration Form is used to collect information about volunteers for projects and events. This form helps us to keep track of volunteer details, ensuring that we can manage volunteer roles, schedules, and responsibilities effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Volunteer Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the third field.
4. Select the **Project Date** for which you are registering as a volunteer.
5. Choose the **Time** of the project.
6. Enter the **Event Name**, if applicable.
7. Choose the **Event Date** for the event.
8. Enter the **Project Start Time** and **Project End Time**, if applicable.
9. Enter the **Project Duration**, if applicable.
10. Enter the **Project Location**, if applicable.
11. Select your **Volunteer Role**, e.g., Volunteer or Other.
12. Enter the number of **Volunteer Hours** you are committing to.
13. Enter your **Volunteer Shift**, if applicable.
14. Fill in any additional **Volunteer Notes**, if needed.
15. Enter any **Additional Info**, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Project Date**: Choose the date you are registering for.
* **Time**: Choose the time you will be volunteering.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are volunteering for, if applicable.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Project Start Time**: Enter the start time of the project.
* **Project End Time**: Enter the end time of the project.
* **Project Duration**: Enter the duration of the project.
* **Project Location**: Enter the location of the project.
* **Volunteer Role**: Select your role as a volunteer, e.g., Volunteer or Other.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are committing to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Enter your shift details, if applicable.
* **Volunteer Notes**: Add any additional notes that might be helpful for us to know.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
Please ensure that you register only once for each project or event. If you have previously registered, do not re-register. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
