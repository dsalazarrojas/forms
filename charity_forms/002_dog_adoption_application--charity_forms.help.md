# Dog Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential adopters to determine their suitability for adopting a dog. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that we have all necessary details to consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Household Details** page with your household's relevant information.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** on the next page.
3. On the **Family Details** page, select all family members who live in your household.
4. If you have a veterinarian who can vouch for your care of animals, provide their name and contact information on the **Veterinary Reference** page.
5. Answer the **Pet Experience** question to demonstrate your understanding of caring for animals.
6. On the final page, indicate your understanding of the adoption contract by checking the box on the **Adoption Terms** section.
7. Finally, sign the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Details** (`household_details`, `text`, optional): This section is meant for you to describe your household, including number of people living in the house, square footage, and any other relevant details that might help us understand your home environment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide your address, phone number, and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Family Details** (`family_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate who lives in your household, including any children, spouses, or other family members.
* **Veterinary Reference** (`vet_ref`, `text`, optional): If you have a veterinarian who knows you and your pet care skills, please provide their name and contact information so we can verify your care of animals.
* **Pet Experience** (`pet_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how long you have experience with animals, and we'll use this information to gauge your suitability as an adopter.
* **Adoption Terms** (`adoption_terms`, `note`, optional): Check this box if you have read and understand the adoption contract. This ensures we're both on the same page about the terms of the adoption.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm your application and agree to the terms outlined in the adoption contract. 

Note: Please fill out all fields marked as `required` and ensure you understand the adoption terms before submitting your application.
