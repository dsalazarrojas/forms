# Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for a charity. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your donation is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your donation details.
2. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`): This is your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to help us contact you regarding your donation.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number will help us contact you for any further information or to confirm your donation.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your postal address to help us process your donation.
* **City** (`city`): The city where your address is located.
* **State** (`state`): The state where your address is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ Card: Credit card payment (Visa, Mastercard, or American Express).
	+ PayPal: Pay via PayPal.
	+ Bank Transfer: Bank transfer payment.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`): Enter your credit card number.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`): Select the type of your credit card (VISA, Mastercard, or American Express).
* **Card Exp. Month** (`card_exp_month`): Enter the month of your card expiration date (01-12).
* **Card Exp. Year** (`card_exp_year`): Enter the year of your card expiration date (2020-2035).
* **Card CVV** (`card_cvv`): Enter the 3-digit security code on the back of your card.
* **Card Zip** (`card_zip`): Enter the zip code of the address associated with your card.
* **Bank Transfer Name** (`bank_transfer_name`): Enter the name associated with your bank transfer.
* **Bank Transfer Account** (`bank_transfer_account`): Enter your bank transfer account number.
* **Bank Transfer Account Number** (`bank_transfer_account_number`): Enter your bank transfer account number.
* **Bank Transfer Routing Number** (`bank_transfer_routing_number`): Enter your bank transfer routing number.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date of your payment.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional notes about your donation.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`): Confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Not required for regular users.
