# donation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The donation_request_form is designed to collect donations from individuals and organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the General info section with your general information.
2. In the Contact info section, provide your contact details.
3. Select the type of donation you would like to make in the Donation details section.
4. Specify the amount of your donation in the Donation amount field.
5. Provide the name and details of the contact person in the Contact person field.
6. If desired, provide your email, phone number, address, date, and time for further communication or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General info**: This field captures general information about you, such as your name and other details. (text, text, optional)
* **Contact info**: This is a mandatory field that captures your contact information for follow-up and communication purposes. (text, text, required)
* **Donation details**: Select the type of donation you would like to make. (select one, select_one, required)
* **Donation amount**: Specify the amount of your donation. (number, number, required)
* **Contact person**: Provide the name and details of the contact person. (text, text, required)
* **Email**: If desired, provide your email address for further communication. (email, email, optional)
* **Phone**: If desired, provide your phone number for follow-up. (text, text, optional)
* **Address**: If desired, provide your address for further communication. (text, text, optional)
* **Date**: If desired, provide the date of the donation. (date, date, optional)
* **Time**: If desired, provide the time of the donation. (time, time, optional)
* **Note**: This is an optional field for any additional notes you would like to include. (note, note, optional)
