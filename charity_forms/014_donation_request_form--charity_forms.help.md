# donation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The donation_request_form is designed to collect donations from individuals and organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the General info section with your general information.
2. In the Contact info section, provide your contact details.
3. Select the type of donation you would like to make in the Donation details section.
4. Specify the amount of your donation in the Donation amount field.
5. Provide the name and details of the contact person in the Contact person field.
6. If desired, provide your email, phone number, address, date, and time for further communication or follow-up.
