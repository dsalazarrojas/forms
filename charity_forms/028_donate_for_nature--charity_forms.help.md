# Donate For Nature - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the "Donate For Nature" form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field. This is the amount you wish to donate.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred donation frequency from the "Donations Frequency" dropdown menu. Choose from: Quarterly, Monthly, or One-time.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu. Choose from: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
7. Enter the date you wish to make the payment in the "Payment Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Select a time for the payment from the "Payment Time" field.
9. If you have any additional comments about your donation frequency, enter them in the "Frequency Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (donation_amount, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate. This is the amount that will be processed for the donation.
* **Full Name** (name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information.
* **Donations Frequency** (donation_frequency, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred donation frequency from the following options: Quarterly, Monthly, or One-time.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method from the following options: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
* **Payment Date** (payment_date, date, required): Enter the date you wish to make the payment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Payment Time** (payment_time, time, required): Select a time for the payment from the following: 00:00 to 23:59.
* **Frequency Comment** (frequency_comment, note, required): Enter any additional comments about your donation frequency.
