# Fundraiser Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraiser Order Form is designed to collect information and details for a fundraiser, allowing donors to input their order details, donation amount, payment method, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Details** page with the required information.
2. Move on to the next page and fill out the **Donor Information** page with the required details.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): This page is the first part of the form, where you need to input the order details. This section is optional, but it's recommended that you fill it out for accurate processing of your order.
- **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field if you're making a donation.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the order if you have one. This will help the organization keep track of the orders.
- **Amount Raised** (`amount_raised`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount raised for the fundraiser.
- **Donation Target** (`donation_target`, number, required/optional): Enter the target donation amount for the fundraiser.
- **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that the organization can contact you if needed.
- **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that the organization can contact you if needed.
- **Message to Donor** (`message_to_donor`, text, required/optional): Enter a message to the donor if you have any special requests.
- **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, select_one, required/optional): Select the donation amount from the dropdown menu.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the payment method from the dropdown menu.
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required/optional): Enter the total order amount.
- **Tax** (`tax`, number, required/optional): Enter the tax amount.
- **Tips** (`tips`, number, required/optional): Enter any tips or additional details about the order.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the order.
- **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the donor's address.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts, refer to this help guide for clarity.
* Please do not leave any field blank without a specific reason.
* Ensure that you have filled out the correct payment method.
* If you have any other questions, contact the organization's support team.
