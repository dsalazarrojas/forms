# Online Foundation Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Foundation Donation Form is a tool designed to collect information from donors who wish to make a donation to a foundation. This form is intended for online donations and is meant to be used by donors to provide their information in order to process their donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
2. Enter your donor information, including your name and email address (if applicable).
3. Choose your donation frequency and amount.
4. Select the charity you wish to donate to (if applicable).
5. Review and confirm your donation details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information**: This field is required to collect your name and contact information.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. Please note that this field is optional, but required for donation processing.
* **Recurring Donor**: Select whether you wish to be a recurring donor, either Monthly or One-time.
* **Donation Frequency**: If you are a recurring donor, select the frequency of your donations, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Charity Name**: Select the charity you wish to donate to. Please note that this field is optional and will be used for tracking purposes only.
* **Charity Address**: Enter the address of the charity you wish to donate to. Please note that this field is optional and will be used for tracking purposes only.
