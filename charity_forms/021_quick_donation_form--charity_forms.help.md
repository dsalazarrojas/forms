# Quick Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Donation Form is designed to gather information for charity donations and allow users to give easily and securely. This form will collect your information and donation details for the charity's use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out the optional fields as desired and submit your information at your convenience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Information
* **Donation Information** (`donation_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your donation.
### Donation Amount
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
### Choose a Fund
* **Choose a Fund** (`donation_fund`, select_one, optional): Select the fund you'd like to donate to.
### Affiliation
* **Affiliation** (`donation_affiliation`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with the charity or a related organization, enter your affiliation.
### Custom Message
* **Custom Message** (`custom_message`, text, optional): If you'd like to leave a personal message with your donation.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to stay up-to-date on charity news and events.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information or updates.
### Subscribe to Mailing List
* **Subscribe to Mailing List** (`mailing_list`, select_one, optional): Would you like to stay up-to-date on charity news and events?
### Mailing List Message
* **Mailing List Message** (`mailing_list_message`, text, optional): If you'd like to include a message with your email subscription.
### Mailing List Affiliation
* **Mailing List Affiliation** (`mailing_list_affiliation`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with the charity or a related organization, enter your affiliation.
