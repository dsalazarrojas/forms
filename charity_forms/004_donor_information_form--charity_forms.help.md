# Donor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Information Form is a crucial tool to gather essential details from donors who are making a donation to our organization. This form helps us understand your background, preferences, and other relevant information to effectively manage and recognize your generosity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your name and address in the designated fields.
3.  Select your organization status from the options provided.
4.  Enter your phone number and email address for communication purposes.
5.  Provide your date of birth for verification purposes.
6.  Indicate the amount donated for accurate record-keeping.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Donor Organization** (`donor_organization`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate your organization status. If you don't have an organization, select "Yes" if you are a private donor or "No" if you are not affiliated with any organization.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, `number`, required): Enter the exact amount donated for accurate record-keeping.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure efficient processing and recognition of your donation.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for communication purposes.
- Review each field carefully to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
