# Pledge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users pledge to donate money to a specific campaign. It is intended for campaigns that are registered on our platform. Please note that users can only pledge if they have not already pledged to the same campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the campaign details provided.
2. Select the amount you wish to pledge.
3. Choose your preferred frequency of donation.
4. Specify your preferred donation method.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Optionally, enter a date and time for your donation.
7. Add any comments you would like to include with your pledge.
8. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Details (Campaign Details)**: This field is for you to review the campaign details provided. Please make sure to read and understand the campaign before proceeding.
* **Amount (Amount)**: Enter the amount you wish to pledge. This field is for numerical input only.
* **Frequency (Frequency)**: Choose your preferred frequency of donation from the options provided.
* **Donation Method (Donation Method)**: Select your preferred donation method from the options provided.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Date (Date)**: If you have a preferred date for your donation, enter it here.
* **Time (Time)**: If you have a preferred time for your donation, enter it here.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Add any comments you would like to include with your pledge.
* **Name (Name)**: Enter your name for our records.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the campaign details carefully before proceeding.
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
- If you have any comments or questions, please include them with your pledge.
- You can only pledge once per campaign. If you have already pledged, you will not be able to submit this form.
