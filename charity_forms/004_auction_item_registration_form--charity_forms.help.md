# Auction Item Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auction Item Registration Form is a tool for consignors to provide necessary information about their donated items. This form helps in ensuring that all required details are captured accurately and promptly, facilitating the auction process. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure smooth coordination with the pickup and sale of the item.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Click on the 'Start' button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
3.  Provide accurate and thorough information in each field.
4.  Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Consignor Information

* **Consignor name**: Enter the full name of the person or organization donating the item. This will help us identify the correct person to contact for coordination and communication.
 
### Consignor Email

* **Consignor email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization donating the item. This will allow us to contact them for pickup coordination, receipts, and any other necessary communication. 

### Item Title

* **Item title**: Enter a name or title for the auction listing of the item. This will help us create an accurate and attractive listing for potential bidders.

### Item Description

* **Item description**: Provide a detailed description of the item, including its condition and any notable features or details. This will help bidders understand the item's value and potential.

### Estimated Value

* **Estimated value**: Enter the estimated fair market value of the item. This will help us determine the starting price for the auction and ensure a smooth and fair bidding process. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide an accurate estimate for a better auction experience.)

### Pickup Required

* **Pickup required**: Indicate if the consignor needs assistance with pickup or if the item can be picked up directly. This will help us coordinate logistics and ensure that the item is delivered safely and efficiently.

### Pickup Location

* **Pickup location**: If the consignor requires pickup, provide the address or details of the location where the item can be picked up. This will help our team locate the item accurately.

### Restrictions or Notes

* **Restrictions or notes**: Enter any conditions of sale or special instructions for the item. This will help us handle any specific requirements or restrictions for the item's sale, such as condition of sale or any other relevant details. 

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the process of registering your auction item. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help.
