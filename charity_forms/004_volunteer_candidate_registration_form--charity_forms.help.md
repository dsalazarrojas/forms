# Volunteer Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to join our volunteering program. It's used to collect information about your availability, skills, and preferences to help us assign you to shifts that fit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the first two fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Choose the shifts you are available to volunteer for in the "Volunteer Shifts" field. You can select multiple shifts if you're available during different time periods.
4. If you have a specific shift in mind for which you'd like to volunteer, enter it in the "Volunteer Shift Time" field.
5. Select your volunteer start and end dates.
6. If you have a preferred shift length, please enter it in the "Volunteer Shift Length" field.
7. In the "Volunteer Why Register" field, briefly explain why you'd like to register as a volunteer.
8. If you have an existing reference from a volunteer who has volunteered with us before, you can enter their name in the "Volunteer Reference" field.
9. If you have any other information about your availability, please enter it in the "Volunteer Available Time" field.
10. If you'd like to volunteer for additional shifts, select them in the "Volunteer Shifts 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`volunteer_candidate_registration_form`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we'll use to contact you.
* **Volunteer Shifts** (`volunteer_shifts`, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you are available to volunteer for. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Volunteer Day
	+ Volunteer Evening
	+ Volunteer Weekend
	+ Volunteer Holiday
* **Volunteer Shift Time** (`volunteer_shift_time`, time, optional): Enter the shift time for which you'd like to volunteer. This field is optional, but it helps us know your availability more precisely.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to start volunteering. This is the date we'll consider as your volunteering start date.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to end volunteering with us. This is the date we'll consider as your volunteering end date.
* **Volunteer Shift Length** (`volunteer_shift_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the shifts you'd like to volunteer for. This is an optional field, but it helps us know your availability more precisely.
* **Volunteer Why Register** (`volunteer_why_register`, note, optional): Briefly explain why you'd like to register as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Reference** (`volunteer_reference`, text, optional): If you have an existing reference from a volunteer who has volunteered with us before, please enter their name.
* **Volunteer Available Time** (`volunteer_available_time`, time, optional): If you have any other information about your availability, please enter it here.
* **Volunteer Shifts 2** (`volunteer_shifts_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional shifts you are available to volunteer for. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Volunteer Day
	+ Volunteer Evening
	+ Volunteer Weekend
	+ Volunteer Holiday

## Tips

* Please make sure to check if you're already registered before submitting this form.
* If you have any questions, feel free to ask.
* We'll review your application and get back to you soon.
