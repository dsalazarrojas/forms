# Church Guest Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from our guests to help us understand their experiences and preferences when visiting our church.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Use the select_one and text fields as instructed.
3. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to share them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`guest_name`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone Number (Optional)** (`phone_number`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, optional): Select the age range that best fits your current age.
* **Is This Your First Time Visiting Our Church?** (`first_time_visitor`, required): Please indicate whether this is your first time visiting our church.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Church?** (`how_heard_about_church`, required): Select how you heard about our church.
* **What Attracted You to Visit Our Church?** (`what_attracted_you`, optional): Share what drew you in to visit our church.
* **How Welcome Did You Feel?** (`welcome_experience`, required): Rate your experience of how welcome you felt during your visit.
* **Parking and Accessibility** (`parking_accessibility`, optional): Rate the parking and accessibility of our facilities.
* **Facility Cleanliness and Appearance** (`facility_cleanliness`, optional): Rate the cleanliness and appearance of our facilities.
* **Quality of Worship Service** (`worship_service_quality`, required): Rate the quality of our worship service.
* **Music and Worship Experience** (`music_rating`, optional): Rate the quality of our music and worship experience.
* **Teaching or Sermon Quality** (`teaching_rating`, required): Rate the quality of our teaching or sermon.
* **Relevance of Message to Your Life** (`relevance_to_life`, optional): Rate how relevant the message was to your life.
* **What Did You Like Best About Your Visit?** (`best_aspects`, optional): Share what you liked most about your visit.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for us to improve.
* **How Likely Are You to Return?** (`likelihood_return`, required): Rate how likely you are to return to our church.
* **Interested in Learning More About Our Church?** (`interested_learning_more`, optional): Indicate if you're interested in learning more about our church.
* **Are You Interested in Exploring Your Spiritual Journey?** (`spiritual_interest`, optional): Indicate if you're interested in exploring your spiritual journey.
* **May We Contact You to Follow Up?** (`contact_permission`, required): Indicate if you'd like us to follow up with you.

## Tips

* Be honest and open in your responses.
* Please answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
