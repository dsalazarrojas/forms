## Charity Partnership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Charity Partnership Application Form is designed to help us understand your charitable organization's goals, needs, and capabilities. The information collected will be used to review and assess your partnership potential.

## How To Complete This Form

Please fill out the form in the following order:

1. Organization Information
2. Contact Information
3. Funding or Partnership Needs
4. Impact and Outcomes
5. Governance and Compliance

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`org_name`): The legal name of your charitable organization.
* **Type of Organization** (`org_type`): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., 501c3 Nonprofit, Community Organization, etc.).
* **Organization Mission Statement** (`org_mission`): Briefly describe your organization's mission in a sentence.
* **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`): Specify how long your organization has been operating.
* **Organization Website** (`org_website`): Provide your organization's website URL (optional).
* **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Identify the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Specify the title of the primary contact person (e.g., Executive Director, Program Manager, etc.).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Provide the primary contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the primary contact person's phone number.
* **Amount of Funding Sought** (`funding_amount_sought`): Specify the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **How Will Funds Be Used** (`funding_use`): Describe how the funding will be used.
* **Type of Partnership Desired** (`partnership_type`): Select the type of partnership you are seeking (e.g., Financial Grant, In-Kind Donation, etc.).
* **Timeline for Funding** (`time_frame`): Choose when you need support (e.g., Immediate - Within 30 days, Short-term - 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Number of Beneficiaries Served Annually** (`beneficiaries_served`): Specify the number of people you help annually.
* **Key Outcomes and Achievements** (`key_outcomes`): Describe the impact and achievements of your organization.
* **Current 501c3 Status** (`501c3_status`): Indicate whether your organization has 501c3 status.
* **Latest Financial Audit** (`audited_financials`): Specify if you have audited financial statements (within the last 2 years).
* **Application Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Select today's date to confirm your application.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Charity Partnership Application Form accurately and effectively.
