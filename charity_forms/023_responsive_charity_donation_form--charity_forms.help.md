# Responsive Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Charity Donation Form is a tool to facilitate internal charity donations within the organization. It allows users to select a charity and make a donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the amount of your donation in the "Donate Amount" field.
2. In the "What Is This Donation For?" field, briefly describe the purpose of your donation.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
6. Select the charity you wish to donate to from the "Select Charity Name" dropdown menu.
7. If you have any additional message, you can enter it in the "Message" field.
8. Select the date and time of your donation from the "Date of Donation" and "Time of Donation" fields.
9. Finally, select the method of payment you wish to use (e.g., 1-3 options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donate Amount** (`donation_amount`): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **What Is This Donation For?** (`purpose`): Briefly describe the purpose of your donation.
* **Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`): Provide your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name to be recognized as a donor.
* **Select Charity Name** (`charity_name`): Choose the charity you wish to donate to from the dropdown menu.
* **Message** (`message`): Enter any additional message or comments about your donation.
* **Date of Donation** (`donation_date`): Select the date of your donation.
* **Time of Donation** (`donation_time`): Select the time of your donation.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Choose how you wish to submit your donation (1-3 options).
