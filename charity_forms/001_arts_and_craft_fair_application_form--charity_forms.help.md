# Arts And Craft Fair Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Arts and Craft Fair Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants who want to participate in the fair, showcasing their art and craft works. This form ensures that the fair organizers have the necessary details to assess and select participants for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, business name, and contact information.
2. Provide your craft category and a brief description of your artwork and creative style.
3. Share your business experience, including the length of time you have been in business and any relevant references or portfolio links.
4. Confirm your participation in previous fairs, liability insurance, and booth size requirements.
5. Pay the non-refundable application/booth fee.
6. Check the box confirming that you agree to the fair's terms and conditions.
7. Finally, submit your application by providing the date you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, **required**): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, **required**): Enter your family name.
* **Business or Studio Name** (3, text, **required**): Enter the name of your arts or craft business.
* **Email Address** (4, email, **required**): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Address** (6, text, **required**): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City** (7, text, **required**): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State/Province** (8, text, **required**): Enter the state or province code where your business is located.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (9, text, **required**): Enter the postal code where your business is located.
* **Primary Craft Category** (10, select_one, **required**): Select one category from the list:
	+ Paintings and Drawings
	+ Sculpture and Pottery
	+ Jewelry
	+ Textiles and Fiber Arts
	+ Woodworking
	+ Glass Art
	+ Metalwork
	+ Other
* **Description of Your Crafts** (11, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of your artwork and creative style.
* **Years in Business** (12, number, **required**): Enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Have you Participated in Craft Fairs Before?** (13, select_one, **required**): Select one option:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Preferred Booth Size** (14, select_one, **required**): Select one booth size option:
	+ 6x6 feet
	+ 8x8 feet
	+ 10x10 feet
	+ 12x12 feet
	+ Other
* **Booth Fee Amount** (15, number, **required**): Enter the non-refundable application/booth fee amount.
* **Liability Insurance** (16, select_one, **required**): Select one option:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Special Requirements or Accommodations** (17, text, **optional**): Enter any special setup needs or accessibility requirements.
* **References or Portfolio Links** (18, text, **optional**): Enter any relevant references or portfolio links.
* **I Agree to the Fair Terms and Conditions** (19, select_one, **required**): Select the option:
	+ I Agree
	+ Other
* **Application Submission Date** (20, date, **required**): Enter the date you are submitting this application.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for typos and correct formatting.
* If you have any special requirements or accommodations, provide detailed descriptions to ensure fair organizers can accommodate your needs.
* Review the fair's terms and conditions carefully before confirming your agreement.
