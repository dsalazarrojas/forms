# Funds Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a funds request. To complete the form, please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields and provide accurate information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information**: This field is to collect basic user information.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: This field is to collect contact details for further communication.
### Request Amount
* **Request Amount**: This field is for the amount of funds being requested.
### Purpose of Request
* **Purpose of Request**: This field is to specify the reason for the request.
### Description of Request
* **Description of Request**: This field is to provide more details about the request.
### Requested Funds
* **Requested Funds**: This field is to specify the specific funds being requested.
### Status of Request
* **Status of Request**: This field is to track the status of the request.
  Please select one of the provided options: Awaiting review, Awaiting approval, Awaiting funds, Rejected.
### Additional Information
* **Additional Information**: This field is a text area for any additional information the user wants to provide.
### Submission Date
* **Submission Date**: This field is to record the date of submission.
### Contact Name
* **Contact Name**: This field is to collect the contact person's name.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: This field is to collect the contact person's email address.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone**: This field is to collect the contact person's phone number.
### Contact Address
* **Contact Address**: This field is to collect the contact person's address.
### Submitter
* **Submitter**: This field is to collect the person submitting the request.
### Submitter Email
* **Submitter Email**: This field is to collect the email address of the person submitting.
### Submitter Name
* **Submitter Name**: This field is to collect the name of the person submitting.
### Status of Submission
* **Status of Submission**: This field is to track if the submission is true or false.
  Please select one of the provided options: True, False.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to prevent errors in the submission process. If you have any questions, please contact the relevant contact person.
