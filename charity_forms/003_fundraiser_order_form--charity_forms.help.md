# Fundraiser Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fundraiser Order Form is designed to collect information from fundraisers for orders. It ensures accurate and efficient processing of orders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the fundraiser's name in the **Fundraiser** field.
2. Enter the fundraiser's email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter the amount of the donation in the **Donation** field.
4. Enter the name of the product in the **Product Name** field.
5. Select the quantity of the product being ordered in the **Quantity** field.
6. Add any additional order notes in the **Order Notes** field (optional).
7. Select the order date from the calendar in the **Order Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fundraiser** (`fundraiser_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the fundraiser.
* **Email** (`fundraiser_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the fundraiser.
* **Donation** (`donation`, number, required): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date of the order from the calendar.
