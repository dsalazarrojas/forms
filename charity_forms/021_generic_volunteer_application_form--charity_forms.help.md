# Generic Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with our organization. The data collected will be used to determine suitability and assign volunteers to available opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number, if you have one, in the "Phone" field (this is optional).
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Select your city of residence in the "City" field.
6. Select your state in the "State" field.
7. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field (this is optional).
8. Choose your volunteer interest from the list provided in the "Volunteer Interest" field.
9. Select the times of the day you are available to volunteer in the "Volunteer Time" field (this is optional).
10. Sign and date the form with your actual signature in the "Volunteer Signature" field.
11. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
12. Select your volunteer role from the options provided in the "Volunteer Role" field.
13. Indicate your volunteer status in the "Volunteer Status" field (this is optional).
14. Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteering in the "Volunteer Hours" field (this is optional).
15. Select the frequency of your volunteering in the "Volunteer Frequency" field (this is optional).
16. If you have any prior volunteer commitments, enter the date and reason for termination in the "Volunteer Termination Date" and "Volunteer Termination Reason" fields (these are optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for contact purposes.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home or work address.
- **City** (`city`, text, required): Select your city of residence.
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Select your state of residence.
- **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code (if applicable).
- **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your interest in volunteering.
- **Volunteer Time** (`volunteer_time`, select_multiple, optional): Select the times you are available to volunteer.
- **Volunteer Friday** (`volunteer_friday`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to volunteer, if applicable.
- **Volunteer Note** (`volunteer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your volunteer experience.
- **Volunteer Signature** (`volunteer_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form with your actual signature.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
- **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information.
- **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the organization.
- **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current volunteer status.
- **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteering.
- **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of your volunteering.
- **Volunteer Commitment** (`volunteer_commitment`, date, optional): Enter the date of your volunteer commitment.
- **Volunteer Termination Date** (`volunteer_termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your volunteer termination (if applicable).
- **Volunteer Termination Reason** (`volunteer_termination_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your volunteer termination (if applicable).
