# monthly_appointment_scheduling_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly_appointment_scheduling_form is used to schedule appointments with users, gathering date, time, and contact information for confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your scheduled date and time.
2. Enter any additional notes for your appointment.
3. Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your appointment.
4. Enter your phone number to be reached for confirmation (optional).
5. Confirm the scheduled date and time.
6. Confirm any additional notes for your appointment.
7. Enter your confirmed email address for confirmation.
8. Enter your confirmed phone number to be reached for confirmation (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Select Time** (`select_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the time of your appointment.
* **Additional Note** (`schedule_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your appointment.
* **Contact Email** (`email_contact`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your appointment.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to be reached for confirmation (optional).
* **Confirm Schedule Date** (`confirm_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the confirmed date of your appointment.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the confirmed time of your appointment.
* **Confirm Additional Note** (`confirm_note`, note, required/optional): Confirm any additional notes for your appointment.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your confirmed email address to receive confirmation of your appointment.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your confirmed phone number to be reached for confirmation (optional).
