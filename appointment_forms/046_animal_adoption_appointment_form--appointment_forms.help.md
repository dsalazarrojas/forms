# Animal Adoption Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for scheduling an appointment to adopt an animal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your full name in the "Applicant name" field.
3. Select a preferred date for the appointment using the "Preferred appointment date" field.
4. Choose a time slot for the appointment using the "Preferred appointment time" field.
5. If you know the name or ID of the animal you're interested in adopting, enter it in the "Pet of interest" field.
6. Provide a valid phone number for confirmation and follow-up purposes in the "Contact phone" field.
7. If you'd like to receive confirmation emails, enter a valid email address in the "Email address" field.
8. If you have any special requests or needs for accommodations, such as accessibility concerns, enter them in the "Special requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Preferred appointment date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the appointment that works best for you.
* **Preferred appointment time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Select a time slot that suits your schedule.
* **Pet of interest** (`pet_of_interest`, text, optional): If you know the name or ID of the animal you're interested in, enter it here to help the adoption team prepare for your appointment.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): A valid phone number for appointment confirmation and follow-up.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive confirmations or updates about your appointment.
* **Special requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Let us know if you have any specific needs or requirements for your appointment.
