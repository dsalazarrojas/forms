# Car Seat Inspection Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a car seat inspection appointment. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the parent/guardian and the child to be inspected, as well as information about the vehicle and the inspection appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the parent/guardian information section, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Street Address.
2. Provide details about the child, including their name, age, weight, and height.
3. Enter the vehicle information, including the Vehicle Year, Vehicle Make, Vehicle Model, and Type of Vehicle.
4. Schedule the appointment by selecting a preferred date and time for the inspection, and choose an inspection location.
5. Select the type of inspection you require (new or existing).
6. Answer the questions about the car seat, including its age and condition.
7. Provide any additional comments or services you may require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Parent/Guardian Information**
* **Parent/Guardian Information** (`id: 1`): Please enter your contact details, including your name, address, and email address.
* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`): Enter your email address for appointment confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`): Enter your contact number.
* **Street Address** (`id: 6`): Enter your address.
* **City** (`id: 7`): Enter your city.
* **State** (`id: 8`): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`id: 9`): Enter your ZIP code.

### 2. **Child Information**
* **Child Name** (`id: 11`): Enter the full name of the child.
* **Child Age** (`id: 12`): Enter the age of the child in years.
* **Child Weight** (`id: 13`): Enter the child's weight in pounds.
* **Child Height** (`id: 14`): Enter the child's height in inches.
* **Number of Children Needing Inspection** (`id: 15`): Select how many car seats are needed for inspection.
* **Car Seat Information** (`id: 16`): Enter details about the car seat to be inspected.
* **Seat Type** (`id: 17`): Select the type of car seat.
* **Seat Brand** (`id: 18`): Enter the brand of the car seat.
* **Seat Model** (`id: 19`): Enter the model of the car seat. 
* **Seat Age** (`id: 20`): Select the age of the seat.
* **Seat Condition** (`id: 21`): Select the overall condition of the seat.

### 3. **Vehicle Information**
* **Vehicle Year** (`id: 24`): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`id: 25`): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`id: 26`): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`id: 27`): Select the type of vehicle.

### 4. **Appointment and Inspection**
* **Appointment Type** (`id: 32`): Select the type of inspection (new or existing).
* **Inspection Location** (`id: 33`): Select the location for the inspection.
* **Specific Location** (`id: 34`): Enter the address if known.
* **Appointment Details** (`id: 28`): Enter any additional information about the inspection.
* **Preferred Date** (`id: 29`): Select the preferred date for the inspection.
* **Preferred Time** (`id: 30`): Select the preferred time for the inspection.
* **Backup Date** (`id: 31`): Select an alternative inspection date.
* **Reminder Preference** (`id: 41`): Select how you would like to be reminded about the appointment.

### 5. **Additional Information**
* **Concerns** (`id: 35`): Select any concerns or issues with the car seat.
* **Additional Services** (`id: 36`): Select any additional services required.
* **Previous Inspection** (`id: 37`): Select if you have had an inspection before.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`id: 38`): Select how you heard about our service.
* **Special Needs** (`id: 39`): Enter any special needs or requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 40`): Enter any additional comments or information. 

Please ensure that you fill out all required fields and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth inspection process.
