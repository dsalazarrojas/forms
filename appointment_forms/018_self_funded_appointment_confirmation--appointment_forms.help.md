# self_funded_appointment_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-funded appointment confirmation form is used to collect information from patients about their appointments. This form is designed to be completed once per appointment, and it's essential to ensure accuracy and consistency in the data collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Enter the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
3. Fill in the payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
4. Select the appointment info from the options provided in the "Appointment Info" field.
5. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields respectively.
9. Add any additional information about the submitter in the "Submitter Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, Optional): Enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_term_info`, text, Optional): Describe the payment terms and conditions for the appointment.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, number, Optional): Enter the payment amount and any other relevant payment details.
* **Appointment Info** (`appointment_info`, select_one, Optional): Select the appointment type or date from the provided options.
* **Note** (`note`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, Optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitter Info** (`submitter_info`, text, Optional): Add any additional information about the submitter.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information is collected.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* This form should only be filled once per appointment, and it's not intended for duplication.
