# Primary Care Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to request an appointment at a primary care office. To complete this form, you will need to provide your contact information and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll down to the first page, "Patient Information".
2. Fill out the form with your details in the text fields. You can type in your full name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Scroll down to the second page, "Appointment Details".
4. Provide any additional information you would like to share with the primary care office regarding your appointment.
5. Scroll down to the third page, "Contact Information".
6. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`text`, optional): Enter your home or work address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`text`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### Appointment Details
* **Appointment Details** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share with the primary care office regarding your appointment.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`text`, optional): Enter the email address where you would like the primary care office to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number where you would like the primary care office to contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing your appointment details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the primary care office directly.
