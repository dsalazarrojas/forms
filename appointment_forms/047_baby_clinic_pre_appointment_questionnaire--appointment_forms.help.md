# Baby Clinic Pre Appointment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the baby's health and medical history before their scheduled appointment at the baby clinic. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and effective consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
	* Mother's information (section 1)
	* Baby's information (section 2)
	* Feeding information (section 3)
	* Health history (section 4)
	* Additional concerns (section 5)
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your knowledge.
3. If you are unsure or need help with any question, please don't hesitate to ask.
4. Once you have filled out all the sections, submit the form to ensure a complete and thorough review of the baby's medical history before the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother's Full Name** (page 2, id: 2): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Mother's Age** (page 3, id: 3): Enter your age in numbers.
* **Mother's Email** (page 3, id: 4): Enter your email address.
* **Mother's Phone Number** (page 3, id: 5): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Baby's Name** (page 7, id: 7): Enter the baby's name.
* **Baby's Date of Birth** (page 7, id: 8): Enter the baby's date of birth.
* **Baby's Age (in weeks)** (page 9, id: 9): Enter the baby's age in weeks.
* **Baby's Gender** (page 9, id: 10): Select the baby's gender (Male, Female, or Prefer not to say).
* **Birth Weight (in pounds/kg)** (page 10, id: 11): Enter the baby's birth weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Birth Length (in inches/cm)** (page 10, id: 12): Enter the baby's birth length in inches or centimeters.
* **Current Weight (in pounds/kg)** (page 11, id: 13): Enter the baby's current weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Feeding Method** (page 12, id: 15): Select how the baby is currently fed (Exclusively breastfed, Exclusively formula fed, Mixed - breast and formula, Solids started).
* **Feeding Frequency** (page 12, id: 16): Enter how many times the baby is fed per day.
* **Any Feeding Concerns?** (page 12, id: 17): Enter any feeding concerns or difficulties you are experiencing.
* **Pregnancy Complications?** (page 15, id: 19): Select if there were any pregnancy complications (True, False, or Unsure).
* **Type of Delivery** (page 15, id: 20): Select the type of delivery (Vaginal, Cesarean section, Assisted vaginal, Other).
* **Current Symptoms or Concerns** (page 16, id: 21): Enter any current symptoms or concerns.
* **Known Allergies** (page 16, id: 22): Enter any known allergies.
* **Vaccinations Up to Date?** (page 17, id: 23): Select if the baby's vaccinations are up to date (True, False, In progress).
* **Family Medical History** (page 17, id: 24): Enter any significant health conditions in the family.
* **Current Medications or Supplements** (page 18, id: 25): Enter any current medications or supplements.
* **Reason for Appointment** (page 19, id: 26): Select the reason for the appointment (Why is the baby coming in?).
* **Additional Concerns** (page 19, id: 27): Enter any other questions or concerns.
