# Talent Performance Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Talent Performance Scheduling Request Form is used by talents or their representatives to schedule a performance, providing details such as date, time, and venue for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately with your requested performance details.
*   Ensure that you provide up-to-date information to avoid scheduling conflicts.
*   Review your form before submitting to catch any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**:
    \*\* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional)
    Enter your name and contact information for us to contact you back.
*   **Appointment Date**:
    \*\* **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, date, optional)
    Enter the date of the performance. If left blank, we will schedule based on our best available time.
*   **Start Time**:
    \*\* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, optional)
    Enter the start time of the performance.
*   **End Time**:
    \*\* **End Time** (End Time, time, optional)
    Enter the end time of the performance.
*   **Performance Time**:
    \*\* **Performance Time** (Performance Time, time, optional)
    Enter the time you will be performing. If left blank, we will schedule around your availability.
*   **Venue**:
    \*\* **Venue** (Venue, text, optional)
    Enter the venue where the performance will take place.
*   **Talent Name**:
    \*\* **Talent Name** (Talent Name, text, optional)
    Enter the name of the talent or artist.
*   **Talent ID**:
    \*\* **Talent ID** (Talent ID, text, optional)
    Enter the talent's ID or performer's ID number.
*   **Description**:
    \*\* **Description** (Description, text, optional)
    Enter a brief description of the performance.
*   **Event Date**:
    \*\* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, optional)
    Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Time**:
    \*\* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, optional)
    Enter the time of the event.
*   **Event Venue**:
    \*\* **Event Venue** (Event Venue, text, optional)
    Enter the venue of the event.

## Tips

*   Please review your form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
*   Ensure that you provide up-to-date information to avoid scheduling conflicts.
