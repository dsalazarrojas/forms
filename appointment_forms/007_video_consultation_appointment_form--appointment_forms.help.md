# Video Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients before scheduling a video consultation. It helps healthcare professionals to gather necessary details to facilitate a smooth and effective video consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your client information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Select your preferred time slot for the video consultation from the "Preferred Time" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the date for your video consultation in the "Date" field.
4. Select the service provider you would like to consult with from the "Service Provider" dropdown menu.
5. If you prefer a different language for your video consultation, choose from the "Preferred Language" dropdown menu.
6. Add any additional notes about your consultation in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Text)**: This is where you provide your basic information such as name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Preferred Time (Select Multiple)**: Select your preferred time slot for the video consultation. You can choose from 09:00-10:00, 10:00-11:00, 11:00-12:00, 14:00-15:00, or 15:00-16:00.
* **Date (Date)**: Choose a date for your video consultation.
* **Service Provider (Select One)**: Select the service provider you would like to consult with.
* **Preferred Language (Select One)**: Choose your preferred language for the video consultation.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any additional notes about your consultation.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
