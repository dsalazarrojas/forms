# Hairdressing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a client's personal details, appointment schedule, hair service preferences, and hair condition for hairstylists to provide the best service possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's personal information, including their name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose a suitable appointment date and time for the consultation.
3. Select the type of hair service the client is interested in (e.g., Hair Cut, Hair Coloring, Hair Treatment).
4. Choose the service level the client prefers (e.g., Wash and Style, Cut and Style, Wash and Cut).
5. Specify the client's hair condition (e.g., Dry, Curly, Oily, Greasy).
6. Provide any relevant client history or notes for the stylist's reference.
7. Fill in any other required or optional information as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required): This field is where you can enter the client's name.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Select a time for the appointment.
* **Hair Service Type** (`hair_service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of service the client needs, such as Hair Cut, Hair Coloring, or Hair Treatment.
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose how the client wants the service to be performed, such as Wash and Style, Cut and Style, or Wash and Cut.
* **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, select_one, optional): Select the client's hair condition, such as Dry, Curly, Oily, or Greasy.
* **Client History** (`client_history`, note, optional): Provide any relevant information about the client's hair history or preferences.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Stylist Name** (`styalis_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the stylist providing the service.
* **Service Cost** (`service_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the service.
