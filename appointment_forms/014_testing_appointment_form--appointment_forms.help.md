# Testing Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Testing Appointment Form" is a tool for patients to provide essential details about their appointments, including their personal and appointment-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your patient name and suspected condition in the text fields.
3. Select the suspected condition from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter your appointment date and time in the date and time fields.
5. Provide your phone number so that our staff can contact you with any updates.
6. Enter your doctor's name and phone number (optional).
7. Enter your email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title:** *Required (text)*: This field is the title of this form.
* **Suspected Condition:** *Required (select one):** This field allows you to choose a condition you are suspected of having.
* **Patient Name:** *Required (text)*: This field is for entering your patient name.
* **Doctor Name:** *Optional (text)*: This field is for entering your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Phone:** *Optional (text)*: This field is for entering your doctor's phone number.
* **Patient Phone:** *Required (text)*: This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Patient Email:** *Optional (text)*: This field is for entering your email address.
* **Appointment Date:** *Required (date)*: This field is for entering your appointment date.
* **Appointment Time:** *Required (time)*: This field is for entering your appointment time.
* **Custom Fields:** *Optional (note)*: This field is for adding any additional information about your appointment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid errors.
- Be as detailed as possible in the "Custom Fields" section to provide our staff with essential information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
