# Project Management Interview Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The project management interview schedule form is designed to collect information on project timelines, interviewee availability, and contact details for project management interviews. It is essential to have this data for efficient and effective project planning and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.
2. Fill in the form with the required information, making sure to accurately represent the project details.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Management Schedule** (`project_management_schedule`): A brief description of the project management schedule.
- **Candidates Name** (`candidate_name`): The name of the candidate or interviewee.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Contact information for the candidate or interviewee, including phone number, email, or any other relevant contact details.
- **Available Dates** (`available_dates`): Select one or more dates that the candidate or interviewee is available for the project management interview.
  - Choose one or more dates from the available options.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`): The start time of the project management interview. 
  - Enter the start time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
- **End Time** (`end_time`): The end time of the project management interview.
  - Enter the end time in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
- **Duration** (`duration`): The expected duration of the project management interview in hours.
  - Enter the duration in numbers (e.g., 2 for 2 hours).

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately represent the project details and candidate information.
- Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, please seek clarification from your supervisor or team lead.
