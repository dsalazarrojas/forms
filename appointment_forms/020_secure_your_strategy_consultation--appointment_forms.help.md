# Secure Your Strategy Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule a secure strategy consultation with our team. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of what you're looking for from this consultation before proceeding. If you've already submitted a consultation request, please do not submit another. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address. This is where you'll receive a confirmation email with your appointment details.
4. Enter your Phone number. This is for scheduling and follow-up purposes.
5. Enter your Company name.
6. Enter your Job Title.
7. Select a date for the appointment.
8. Select a start time for the appointment.
9. Select an end time for the appointment.
10. Select the appointment time slot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name, as you would like it to be displayed on your appointment confirmation email.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name, as you would like it to be displayed on your appointment confirmation email.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address where you can receive a confirmation email with your appointment details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for scheduling and follow-up purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): Enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title or role within your company.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, not required): Select a date for the appointment. 
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, not required): Select the start time of the appointment.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, not required): Select the end time of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, not required): This field will auto-fill with your selected start and end times, indicating the duration of your appointment. You do not need to fill it out manually.
