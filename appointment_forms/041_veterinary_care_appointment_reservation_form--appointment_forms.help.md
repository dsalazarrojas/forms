# Veterinary Care Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Care Appointment Reservation Form is designed for clients to schedule a visit with a veterinarian. This form helps collect essential information about the client and their pet to ensure a smooth and efficient appointment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select the species of your pet from the options provided (Dog, Cat, Bird).
3. Choose a date for the appointment.
4. Select a time for the appointment.
5. Enter the reason for your visit.
6. Select the veterinarian you would like to see.
7. Add any relevant details about your pet.
8. Choose the duration of the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (<name>, <type: text>, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be listed on our records.
* **Owner Email** (<owner_email>, <type: email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you with appointment reminders and updates.
* **Pet Species** (<pet_species, <type: select_one, required: false): Select the species of your pet (Dog, Cat, Bird).
* **Appointment Date** (<appointment_date, <type: date, required: true): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (<appointment_time, <type: time, required: true): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Reason for Visit** (<reason_for_visit, <type: text, required: false): Explain the reason for your visit so we can prepare accordingly.
* **Veterinarian** (<veterinarian_id, <type: text, required: true): Select the veterinarian you would like to see.
* **Pet Details** (<pet_details, <type: text, required: false): Add any relevant details about your pet.
* **Appointment Duration** (<appointment_duration, <type: number, required: true): Choose the duration of your appointment in minutes.
* **Notes** (<notes, <type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Select the correct species of your pet.
* Choose a date and time that works for you and the veterinarian.
* Be as specific as possible when providing reason for visit and pet details.
* Ensure you have sufficient time for your appointment duration.
* Add any additional comments or notes to help us prepare for your visit.
