# Schedule A Free Inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Schedule A Free Inspection" form is designed to gather information from users to schedule a free inspection of their property. This form is an essential step in the inspection process, allowing users to provide their property details and preferred schedule for the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the "User" and "Email" fields, respectively. The "User" field is optional, but we recommend filling it out for our records.
2. Enter your property address in the "Property Address" field. This is a required field, as it helps us locate your property for the inspection.
3. Choose a start date for the inspection using the "Start Date" field. This is also a required field, as it determines when the inspection will take place.
4. Select a start time for the inspection using the "Start Time" field. Similar to the start date, this is a required field.
5. In the "Property Details" field, provide any necessary information about your property that you think would be relevant to the inspection.
6. Enter the name of the inspector who will be visiting your property in the "Inspector" field. This is a required field, as it ensures the correct inspector is allocated for your property.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field. Although optional, providing any relevant information will help us prepare for the inspection.
8. Choose a schedule for the inspection using the "Schedule" field. The options are "Today", "Tomorrow", "Next 5 days", and selecting one of these will determine when we will schedule the inspection.
9. Enter your contact phone number in the "User Phone" field. This is an optional field, but we recommend providing it to ensure we can contact you about any issues or updates.
10. Enter the contact phone number of the service provider (e.g., contractor) in the "Inspector Phone" field. This is a required field, as it helps us contact the service provider with any questions or updates.
11. Finally, enter any booking details in the "Booking Details" field. Although optional, providing any relevant information will help us with the booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User** (text, optional): Enter your name for our records.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the inspection.
- **Property Address** (text, required): Enter your property address for us to locate your property.
- **Start Date** (date, required): Choose a date for the inspection.
- **Start Time** (time, required): Select a start time for the inspection.
- **Property Details** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your property that you think would be relevant.
- **Inspector** (text, required): Enter the name of the inspector who will visit your property.
- **Notes** (note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the inspection.
- **Schedule** (select_one, optional): Choose a schedule for the inspection (Today, Tomorrow, Next 5 days).
- **Inspector Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the inspector who will visit your property.
- **User Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Service Provider** (text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the service provider (e.g., contractor).
- **Booking Details** (text, required): Enter any booking details to ensure we can complete the inspection process.
