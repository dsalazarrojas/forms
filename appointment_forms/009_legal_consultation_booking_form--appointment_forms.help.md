# Legal Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a legal consultation with a legal expert. Please fill out the form with your contact information, consultation preferences, and follow-up settings to schedule an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select a consultation date and time that suits you.
3. Choose your preferred legal expertise (e.g., Family Law, Commercial Law, Labor Law).
4. Indicate whether you want to receive a confirmation email or phone call after booking the appointment.
5. Choose the method of follow-up (e.g., Email, Phone Call, No Follow-up).
6. Specify the follow-up duration, if applicable.
7. Add any additional notes for the legal expert, if necessary.
8. Confirm that you are the person booking the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication with the legal expert.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Consultation Date**: Select the date you would like to book the appointment.
* **Consultation Time**: Select the time you would like to book the appointment.
* **Consultation Subject**: Briefly describe the reason for your consultation.
* **Legal Expertise**: Choose the area of law you would like to consult with (e.g., Family Law, Commercial Law, Labor Law).
* **Other (Specify)**: If you have chosen "Other" as your legal expertise, provide more details in this field.
* **Booking Confirmation**: Choose whether to receive a confirmation email or phone call after booking the appointment.
* **Confirmation Method**: Select the method of confirmation you prefer (e.g., Email, Phone Call, No Confirmation).
* **Appointment Duration**: Enter the duration of the appointment in minutes (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information for the legal expert.
* **Confirmation Date**: This field is automatically populated with the date of the confirmation.
* **Confirmation Time**: This field is automatically populated with the time of the confirmation.
* **Confirmation Status**: This field will indicate the status of the confirmation (e.g., Awaiting Client Response, Awaiting Legal Expert, Completed).
* **Follow-up Date**: Select the date for follow-up purposes (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Time**: Select the time for follow-up purposes (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Status**: This field will indicate the status of the follow-up (e.g., No Follow-up, Awaiting Client Response, Completed).
* **Follow-up Method**: Select the method of follow-up you prefer (e.g., Email, Phone Call, No Follow-up).
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Enter the frequency of follow-up in number (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Notes**: Add any additional comments or information for follow-up purposes.
* **Notes for Legal Expert**: Add any additional comments or information for the legal expert.
* **Legal Expert Name**: Enter the name of the legal expert, if applicable.
* **User Confirmation**: Confirm that you are the person booking the appointment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information, if necessary.
