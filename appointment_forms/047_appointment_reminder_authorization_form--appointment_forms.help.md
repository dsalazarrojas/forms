This form is used to collect information from patients to authorize the sending of appointment reminders via email, SMS, or phone call. The purpose of this form is to ensure that patients are informed and agree to receive reminders for their upcoming appointments. This form is typically used in healthcare settings to facilitate communication between patients and healthcare providers.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

## Purpose
This form is a crucial tool for healthcare providers to ensure that patients receive timely reminders for their appointments, improving their experience and enabling them to plan their schedules accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
Please provide the following information to complete this form:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (patient_name): Please enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification card or document.
- **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter the patient's phone number for SMS reminders. This will enable the healthcare provider to send reminders via SMS during non-quiet hours specified.
- **Email Address** (email_address): Enter the patient's email address for email reminders. This will enable the healthcare provider to send reminders via email during non-quiet hours specified.
- **May we send you email appointment reminders?** (email_reminders): Select "Yes, authorize" to allow the healthcare provider to send email reminders at the specified timing and frequency.
- **May we send you SMS text message reminders?** (sms_reminders): Select "Yes, authorize" to allow the healthcare provider to send SMS reminders at the specified timing and frequency.
- **May we send you phone call reminders?** (phone_call_reminders): Select "Yes, authorize" to allow the healthcare provider to send phone call reminders at the specified timing and frequency.
- **When would you prefer to receive reminders?** (preferred_timing): Select a preference for when you would like to receive reminders. Options include 24 hours before, 12 hours before, Morning of appointment, 2-3 days before, or No preference.
- **Do not send reminders during these hours** (quiet_hours): If you have specific quiet hours when you do not want to receive reminders, enter those hours here. 
- **Reminder Frequency** (reminder_frequency): Select how often you would like to receive reminders. Options include Send once, Send reminders until confirmed, or Multiple reminders.
- **Upcoming Appointment Date** (appointment_date): Enter the date of the upcoming appointment.
- **Appointment Time** (appointment_time): Enter the time of the upcoming appointment.
- **Provider or Healthcare Professional** (provider_name): Enter the name of the healthcare professional responsible for your care.
- **Consent Statement** (consent_statement): Read and acknowledge the consent statement, indicating your authorization to receive reminders as specified above.
- **I authorize this organization to send me appointment reminders as indicated above** (consent_acknowledgment): Select "I consent" to acknowledge your authorization for reminders as specified above.
- **I acknowledge that my contact information will be used only for appointment reminders and related communications** (privacy_acknowledgment): Select "I agree" to acknowledge the use of your contact information for reminders and related communications.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information to ensure that you receive reminders effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please do not hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.
* Remember to review and acknowledge the consent statement before submitting the form.
* By completing this form, you are authorizing the healthcare provider to send you reminders as specified above.
