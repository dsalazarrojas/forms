# Reading Appointment Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reading Appointment Booking form is used to book a reading session with a reader. It collects information from users to facilitate the provision of reading services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. If you have a specific date and time in mind, enter it in the "Preferred Date" and "Preferred Time" fields.
4. If you have any personal message for the reader, type it in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Book A Reading Session**: This is the title of the form.
* **Reader Details**: Please provide details about yourself as the reader.
* **Preferred Date**: Enter the date you would like to book the reading session on. If no date is specified, the session will be booked on the first available date.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter the time you would like to book the reading session on. If no time is specified, the session will be booked at the earliest available time.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the reader.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Message**: If you have any personal message for the reader, type it in this field.

## Tips
* Ensure that your name and contact information are up-to-date.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, include them in the "Message" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
