# massage_therapy_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and bill patient information for massage therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "patient_dob" field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "patient_email" field.
4. (Optional) Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient_phone" field.
5. Enter the patient's address in the "patient_address" field.
6. Select the type of treatment the patient received in the "treatment_type" field.
7. Enter the number of sessions the patient had in the "num_sessions" field.
8. (Optional) Enter the duration of each session in the "session_duration" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the frequency of sessions in the "session_frequency" field.
10. Enter the start date of the treatment in the "treatment_start" field.
11. (Optional) Add any notes about the treatment in the "treatment_notes" field.
12. Select the payment method used by the patient in the "payment_method" field.
13. Enter the payment amount received in the "payment_amount" field.
14. Enter any additional payment notes in the "payment_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name (Required)**: Enter the patient's name.
* **patient_dob (Required)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **patient_email (Required)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **patient_phone (Optional)**: Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
* **patient_address (Required)**: Enter the patient's address.
* **treatment_type (Optional)**: Select the type of treatment the patient received (Massage, Physical Therapy, or Other).
* **num_sessions (Required)**: Enter the number of sessions the patient had.
* **session_duration (Optional)**: Enter the duration of each session.
* **session_frequency (Optional)**: Enter the frequency of sessions (e.g., weekly, biweekly, etc.).
* **treatment_start (Required)**: Enter the start date of the treatment.
* **treatment_notes (Optional)**: Add any notes about the treatment.
* **payment_type (Required)**: Select the payment type used (Cash, Credit Card, or Insurance).
* **payment_amount (Required)**: Enter the payment amount received.
* **payment_method (Required)**: Select the payment method used (Online, In-Office).
* **payment_notes (Optional)**: Enter any additional payment notes.

## Tips
* Double-check the patient's information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and prevent errors.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the "treatment_type" field to record the type of treatment received, if applicable.
