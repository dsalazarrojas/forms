# Autism Screening Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians to request an appointment for their child to be screened for autism. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to help the screening process run smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information about yourself and your child.
2. Enter the correct date you are requesting the appointment.
3. Complete the fields with details about your child, such as their name, date of birth, age, and gender.
4. Indicate your preferred appointment date and time.
5. Provide your insurance information, if applicable.
6. Describe your concerns about your child's development and behavior.
7. Answer questions about your child's medical history and any relevant medications.
8. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Request Date** (`appointment_request_date`, required): Enter the date you are scheduling the appointment.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, required): Your full name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, required): Your email contact.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, required): Name of the child for screening.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, required): Child's birth date.
* **Child Current Age** (`child_age`, required): Age in years.
* **Child Gender** (`child_gender`, optional): Male, female, or other.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, required): When would you like the screening.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_time`, required): Morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Clinician Preference** (`clinician_preference`, optional): Do you have a preferred clinician.
* **Who Referred You** (`referral_source`, required): How did you learn about this service.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, optional): Your insurance company name.
* **Insurance Member ID** (`insurance_member_id`, optional): Policy or member ID number.
* **Primary Concerns** (`primary_concerns`, required): Select specific concerns you have about your child's development or behavior.
* **Detailed Description of Concerns** (`concerns_details`, required): Explain your concerns in detail.
* **Developmental History** (`developmental_history`, optional): Any delays in development.
* **Speech and Language Status** (`speech_language_status`, optional): Current speech/language abilities.
* **Social Interaction Abilities** (`social_interaction`, optional): How does your child interact socially.
* **Sensory Sensitivities** (`sensory_sensitivities`, optional): Any sensory concerns.
* **School Status** (`school_status`, optional): Is your child in school.
* **School Performance Concerns** (`school_concerns`, optional): Any issues at school.
* **Relevant Medical History** (`medical_history`, optional): Any medical conditions relevant to screening.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, optional): Any medications your child is taking.
* **Family History of Autism or ADHD** (`family_history_autism`, optional): Any family members diagnosed with autism or ADHD.
