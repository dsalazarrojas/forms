# Promotional Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotional Tour Reservation Form is designed to collect essential information about tour reservations for promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Tour Details section with the required information about the tour.
2. Enter the Customer Details section with the customer's relevant information.
3. Provide the tour date and time in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter the customer's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
5. Add any additional customer notes if necessary.
6. Update the tour status to reflect the current status of the tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Details**: Enter the title of the tour, which is a required field. This will help tour staff identify the tour and manage it accordingly.
* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, which is a required field. This is essential for communication and identification purposes.
* **Tour Date**: Select the date of the tour from the calendar. This is a required field, as it helps the tour staff plan and schedule the tour.
* **Tour Time**: Select the time of the tour from the available options. This is a required field, ensuring the tour starts and ends at the correct time.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name. This is a required field and helps tour staff address communication to the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address. This is a required field and allows the tour staff to communicate with the customer through email.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number. This is a required field, allowing tour staff to contact the customer for any updates or information.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes about the customer, if necessary. This field is optional and can be used for special requests or requirements.
* **Tour Notes**: Enter any notes about the tour, which is a required field. This helps tour staff understand and manage the tour's requirements and challenges.
* **Tour Status**: Select the current status of the tour from the available options (Active, Cancelled, Completed). This is a required field, ensuring that the tour is accurately represented and reflected in the tour management system.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to update the tour status regularly to reflect any changes.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact the tour staff for assistance.
