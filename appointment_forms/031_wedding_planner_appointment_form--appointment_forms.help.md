# Wedding Planner Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from couples seeking wedding planning services. It helps wedding planners schedule and prepare for the wedding appointment efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide all necessary information to the wedding planner.
2. Ensure you have the couple's permission to fill out the form on their behalf if you are filling it out as their representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Planner** (text): Enter the name of the wedding planner you are working with or the wedding planner's company.
* **Couple Details** (text): Provide a brief description of the couple or their needs, if necessary.
* **Couple Name** (text): Enter the couple's name.
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter the couple's contact number, if applicable.
* **Couple Email** (email): Enter the couple's email address, if applicable.
* **Date of Visit** (date): Enter the date of the planned visit.
* **Appointment Time** (time): Enter the scheduled appointment time.
* **Appointment Length** (number): Enter the expected length of the appointment.
* **Preferred Language** (select multiple): Select the languages the couple prefers to communicate in (e.g., English, French, Mandarin, Spanish).
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or requests the couple would like to share with the wedding planner.
* **Confirm Visit** (select one): Confirm that the visit is scheduled.
* **Appointment Status** (select one): Indicate the status of the appointment (e.g., Pending, Confirmed, Not Confirmed).
* **Form Submission Status** (select one): Indicate the status of the form submission (e.g., Submitted, Not Submitted, In Progress, Done).

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure the wedding planner has all necessary information.
* If you are filling out the form as a representative, ensure you have the couple's permission to do so.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the wedding planner directly.
