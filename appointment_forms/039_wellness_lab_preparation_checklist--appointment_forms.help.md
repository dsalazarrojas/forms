# Wellness Lab Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Lab Preparation Checklist is a form used by medical staff to prepare for a patient's lab visit. It collects relevant information to review and ensure that all necessary steps are taken before the lab test.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out each section carefully**. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
2. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. **Lab Details**: Select the lab tests or results that need to be reviewed or prepared for.
4. **Appointment Information**: Enter the appointment details, such as the date and time of the lab visit.
5. **Pre-Lab Steps**: Check any pre-lab steps that need to be taken before the lab test.
6. **Physician Information**: Select the physician's name and contact information (if available).
7. **Patient History**: Review and record any relevant patient medical history.
8. **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information for follow-up purposes.
9. **Test Results Status**: Select the status of the test results (e.g., "None", "Other").
10. **Lab Report**: Record any additional comments or notes from the lab report.
11. **Patient Info**: Enter any additional patient information.
12. **Physician Info**: Enter any additional information from the physician.
13. **Lab Status Update**: Record any updates to the lab status.
14. **Contact**: Check if contact needs to be made with the patient.
15. **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information (if applicable).
16. **Test Results Notes**: Record any notes from the test results.
17. **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant.
