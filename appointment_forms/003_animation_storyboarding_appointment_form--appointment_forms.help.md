# Animation Storyboarding Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Animation Storyboarding Appointment Form" is for clients to schedule appointments for animation storyboarding sessions, meetings, or other related activities. This form helps ensure that clients provide all necessary information for successful appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client's information, including their full name and contact email.
2. Provide a project title and brief description to help us understand the project's goals and tone.
3. Select the project type, such as a short film, series episode, commercial, or music video.
4. Choose a preferred date and time for the appointment.
5. Optionally, provide a brief description of the project and file upload instructions if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client full name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's full name, including their title and surname.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address for appointment confirmation and communication.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): If you prefer, provide the client's phone number for urgent changes or confirmations.
* **Project title** (`project_title`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the animation project, e.g., "Short Film: Fantasy Adventure".
* **Project type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the project type from the options provided, such as "Short film", "Series episode", "Commercial", etc.
* **Preferred appointment date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select a date and time for the appointment, considering our availability.
* **Preferred appointment time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): If flexible, provide a time range for the appointment.
* **Brief project description** (`brief_description`, `text`, optional): Summarize the project's goals and tone to help us better understand the project.
* **File upload instructions** (`file_upload_instructions`, `note`, optional): If necessary, provide instructions for file uploads or references for the project.
* **File upload instructions** (`file_upload_instructions`, `note`, optional): This field is a note, but it's not required, and users may not need to fill it in.
