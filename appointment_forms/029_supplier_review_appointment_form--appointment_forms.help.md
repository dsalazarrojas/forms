# Supplier Review Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Review Appointment Form is used to schedule and review appointments with suppliers. This form captures essential information about the supplier, such as their name, email, and appointment details. It also allows you to track the status of the appointment and any notes or messages exchanged with the supplier.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Enter the supplier's email address in the "Supplier Email" field.
3. Select the appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.
4. Enter the start and end times of the appointment in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
5. Indicate the duration of the appointment in the "Duration" field.
6. Provide any additional meeting details in the "Meeting Details" field.
7. Enter any review notes or messages for the supplier in the "Review Notes" field.
8. Choose the current status of the appointment in the "Status" field.
9. Enter any message from the supplier in the "Supplier Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Supplier Name (text)
* **Supplier Name:** Enter the name of the supplier.

### Supplier Email (email)
* **Supplier Email:** Enter the email address of the supplier.

### Appointment Date (date)
* **Appointment Date:** Select the date of the appointment.

### Start Time (time)
* **Start Time:** Enter the start time of the appointment in the format "HH:MM:SS".

### End Time (time)
* **End Time:** Enter the end time of the appointment in the format "HH:MM:SS".

### Duration (number)
* **Duration:** Enter the duration of the appointment in hours.

### Meeting Details (text)
* **Meeting Details:** Provide any additional details about the meeting.

### Review Notes (text)
* **Review Notes:** Enter any notes or messages for the supplier.

### Status (select_one)
* **Status:** Choose the current status of the appointment (Pending, In Progress, or Completed).

### Supplier Message (text)
* **Supplier Message:** Enter any message from the supplier.

## Tips
* Double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct status of the appointment to track its progress.
* Enter any important notes or messages for future reference.
