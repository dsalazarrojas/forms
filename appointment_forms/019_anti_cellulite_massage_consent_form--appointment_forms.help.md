# Anti Cellulite Massage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to inform and protect you as a client before undergoing anti-cellulite massage treatment. It's essential to review the information provided and provide your consent to proceed with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read the information provided on the first page to understand the treatment and its potential risks.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself.
3. Review and agree to the terms of the treatment, including potential risks and side effects.
4. If you have any allergies or medical conditions that may impact the treatment, please list them in the "Relevant medical conditions" field.
5. Provide an emergency contact phone number in case of an urgent situation.
6. Include any additional comments or instructions in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth**: Please enter your date of birth for medical record keeping purposes.
* **Known allergies or sensitivities**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have that may affect the treatment, such as skin allergies or sensitivities to certain oils or lotions.
* **Relevant medical conditions**: Inform the therapist about any medical conditions that may impact the treatment, such as pregnancy, pacemakers, or other conditions that may be relevant.
* **I consent to receive anti-cellulite massage treatment**: Please select "Yes I consent" to confirm that you are willing to receive the treatment.
* **I acknowledge potential risks and side effects**: Please select "Acknowledged" to confirm that you have read and understand the potential risks and side effects associated with the treatment.
* **Emergency contact phone number**: Please provide the phone number of a contact person that can be reached in case of an urgent situation.
* **Additional comments or instructions**: Include any special requests or limitations you have that may impact the treatment.
