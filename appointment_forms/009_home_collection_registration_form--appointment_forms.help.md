# Home Collection Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Collection Registration Form is used to register your home collection services. Please complete the form accurately to ensure we can schedule a collection time that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Contact Number**.
2. Enter your **Address**.
3. Select a valid **Schedule Date** (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Choose a **Schedule Time** from the available options.
5. Select the **Service Type** that suits your needs.
6. If required, choose the **Schedule Time Slot** from the available options.
7. Review your submission to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you for scheduling confirmations.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete address for accurate scheduling and delivery.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required): Choose the date you'd like the collection to take place.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, required): Select a specific time slot for your collection.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of service you need from the available options.
* **Schedule Time Slot** (`schedule_time_slot`, select_multiple, optional): If required, choose an additional time slot for your collection (only available if Service Type requires it).
