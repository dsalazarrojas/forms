# scheduling_setup_and_customer_notification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments and set up customer notification preferences. It is designed to collect information for scheduling appointments and configure notification settings for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the purpose of this form.
2. Fill out the **Scheduling Setup** page with the required information:
	* **Appointment Time**: Enter the date and time of the scheduled appointment.
	* **End Time**: Enter the end date and time of the appointment.
3. Proceed to the **Customer Information** page and fill out the customer's details:
	* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's contact information, such as their name and phone number.
4. On the **Customer Notification** page, choose the desired notification settings:
	* **Customer Notification**: Select the type of notification you want to send to the customer.
	* **Reminder Interval**: Choose the interval at which reminders should be sent.
5. Review and adjust the **Additional Settings** fields as needed:
	* **Additional Settings**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Scheduling Setup
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the start date and time of the appointment.
* **End Time**: Enter the end date and time of the appointment.

### 2. Customer Information
* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's contact information, such as their name and phone number.

### 3. Customer Notification
* **Customer Notification**: Select the type of notification you want to send to the customer.
	+ **Send Notification**: Send a notification to the customer before the appointment.
	+ **Do Not Send**: Do not send any notifications to the customer.
	+ **Only Send Reminder**: Send a reminder to the customer before the appointment.
* **Reminder Interval**: Choose the interval at which reminders should be sent to the customer.
* **Reminder Time**: Enter the time at which reminders should be sent to the customer.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of the appointment.
* **Max Reminders**: Set the maximum number of reminders to send to the customer.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the appointment.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the appointment.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the appointment.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the appointment.

### 4. Additional Settings
* **Additional Settings**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the appointment.

### 5. Save & Send
* **Save & Send**: Save the appointment and send notifications to the customer.
* **Save Only**: Save the appointment without sending notifications to the customer.
