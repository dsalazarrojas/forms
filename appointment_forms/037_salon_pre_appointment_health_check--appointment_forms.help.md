# salon_pre_appointment_health_check - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from clients before their appointment at a salon, ensuring that the client's medical history is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, please fill in the client information page.
2. On this page, provide your name and any other relevant details.
3. If applicable, fill in your date of birth for age verification purposes.
4. Please provide your medical history in the text field below. This will help us understand any allergies, medical conditions, or treatments you may be undergoing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information** (client_information): This field is not required, but please fill it in with any other relevant details about yourself.
  - **client_name** (client_name): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Please provide your date of birth for age verification purposes.
  - **Medical History** (medical_history): Describe any allergies, medical conditions, or treatments you are currently undergoing or have had in the past.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling in your medical history.
- If you are unsure about any medical condition or allergy, please do not hesitate to ask your doctor or healthcare provider for clarification.
- Please note that this form is for internal use only and will be kept confidential.
