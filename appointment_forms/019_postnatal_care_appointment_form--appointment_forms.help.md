# Postnatal Care Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Postnatal Care Appointment Form is designed to collect information for new mothers to schedule postnatal care appointments. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential details to provide the best possible care for the new mother and her baby.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the new mother's name and date of birth in the respective fields.
2.  Select the postnatal care provider(s) that will be attending to the new mother's needs.
3.  Choose the appointment date and time for the postnatal care.
4.  Specify the duration of the appointment.
5.  Indicate if follow-up care is required and select the follow-up care provider.
6.  Select the type of follow-up care required.
7.  Enter any allergies the new mother may have.
8.  Enter the new mother's phone number and email address.
9.  Add any additional notes about the new mother's health and well-being.
10.  Submit the form once all the required information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Mother Name
*   **New Mother Name** (`new_mother_name`, `text`, required: false)
    *   Enter the name of the new mother.

### New Mother Date of Birth
*   **New Mother Date of Birth** (`new_mother_date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false)
    *   Enter the new mother's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.

### New Mother Weight
*   **New Mother Weight** (`new_mother_weight`, `number`, required: false)
    *   Enter the new mother's weight.

### Postnatal Care Provider
*   **Postnatal Care Provider** (`postnatal_care_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    *   Select the healthcare provider(s) that will be attending to the new mother's needs.

### Appointment Date
*   **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required: false)
    *   Enter the appointment date.

### Appointment Time
*   **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required: false)
    *   Enter the appointment time.

### Appointment Duration
*   **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, `number`, required: false)
    *   Enter the duration of the appointment.

### Follow Up Care Required
*   **Follow Up Care Required** (`follow_up_care_required`, `select_one`, required: false)
    *   Indicate if follow-up care is required after the appointment.

### Follow Up Care Date
*   **Follow Up Care Date** (`follow_up_care_date`, `date`, required: false)
    *   Enter the follow-up care date if follow-up care is required.

### Follow Up Care Time
*   **Follow Up Care Time** (`follow_up_care_time`, `time`, required: false)
    *   Enter the follow-up care time if follow-up care is required.

### New Mother Phone Number
*   **New Mother Phone Number** (`new_mother_phone_number`, `text`, required: false)
    *   Enter the new mother's phone number.

### New Mother Email
*   **New Mother Email** (`new_mother_email`, `email`, required: false)
    *   Enter the new mother's email address.

### New Mother Notes
*   **New Mother Notes** (`new_mother_notes`, `note`, required: false)
    *   Add any additional notes about the new mother's health and well-being.

### Follow Up Care Provider
*   **Follow Up Care Provider** (`follow_up_care_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    *   Select the healthcare provider that will be providing follow-up care.

### Follow Up Care Type
*   **Follow Up Care Type** (`follow_up_care_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
    *   Select the type of follow-up care required.

### New Mother Allergies
*   **New Mother Allergies** (`new_mother_allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    *   Select any allergies the new mother may have.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required information is provided before submitting the form.
*   Select multiple providers for postnatal care if necessary.
*   Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the new mother's health and well-being.
