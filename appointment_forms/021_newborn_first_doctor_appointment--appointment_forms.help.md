# Newborn First Doctor Appointment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents about their newborn baby's health and medical history. The purpose of this form is to gather essential data for the doctor's assessment and to identify any potential health concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the baby's details:
	* Newborn weight
	* Mother's first name
	* Mother's last name
	* Father's first name
	* Father's last name
	* Newborn date of birth
	* Newborn time of birth
	* Newborn place of birth
2. Select the baby's:
	* Gender
	* Reflexes (normal, delayed, or absent)
	* Muscle tone (normal or decreased)
	* Skin (normal, dry, scaly, cracked, or foul)
	* Reflexes (same options as above)
	* Motor function (normal, delayed, or absent)
3. Enter any comments or additional information about the baby
4. Enter contact details:
	* Newborn phone number
	* Newborn email
	* Newborn address

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Newborn weight**: Enter the baby's current weight
* **Mother's first name**: Enter your first name as the mother of the newborn
* **Mother's last name**: Enter your last name as the mother of the newborn
* **Father's first name**: Enter your first name as the father of the newborn
* **Father's last name**: Enter your last name as the father of the newborn
* **Newborn date of birth**: Enter the baby's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format
* **Newborn time of birth**: Enter the time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM)
* **Newborn place of birth**: Enter the hospital or location where the baby was born
* **Gender**: Select the baby's gender (male or female)
* **Newborn reflexes**: Select the baby's reflexes (normal, delayed, or absent)
* **Newborn muscle tone**: Select the baby's muscle tone (normal or decreased)
* **Newborn skin**: Select the baby's skin (normal, dry, scaly, cracked, or foul)
* **Newborn reflexes (same as above)**: Select the baby's reflexes (normal, delayed, or absent)
* **Newborn motor function**: Select the baby's motor function (normal, delayed, or absent)
* **Newborn comments**: Enter any additional comments about the baby
* **Newborn phone**: Enter the baby's phone number
* **Newborn email**: Enter the baby's email address
* **Newborn address**: Enter the baby's address

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to provide the best possible care for your baby.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is confidential and will only be shared with authorized healthcare professionals.
