# Window Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a consultation or submission related to windows. It is used to provide your contact information, specify the services you need, and provide additional details such as window size and type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Select the services you need from the "Services Needed" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred time for the appointment in the "Appointment Preferences" select menu.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Specify the service area you need the consultation or submission for in the "Service Area" field.
7. Choose your preferred time for the appointment in the "Preferred Time" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. Confirm you agree to the submission agreement terms by typing it in the "Submission Agreement" field.
10. Provide any additional details in the "Submission Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false) Enter your name, company (if applicable), and any other relevant contact information.
* **Services Needed**: (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, required: false) Choose the services you need from the dropdown menu, such as Window Installation, Window Repair, or Window Replacement.
* **Appointment Preferences**: (`appointment_preferences`, `select_one`, required: false) Select when you would like to have the appointment, such as Today, Tomorrow, or This week.
* **Contact Number**: (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false) Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: (`email`, `text`, required: false) Enter your email address.
* **Service Area**: (`service_area`, `text`, required: false) Specify the area where the services are needed.
* **Preferred Time**: (`preferred_time`, `time`, required: false) Choose your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Additional Notes**: (`notes`, `note`, required: false) Add any additional information or comments.
* **Submission Agreement**: (`submission_agreement`, `text`, required: false) Confirm you agree to the submission agreement terms.
* **Submission Details**: (`submission_details`, `text`, required: false) Provide any additional details about your submission.
* **Window Types**: (`window_types`, `select_multiple`, required: false) Choose the type of window you need, such as Double Hung or Single Hung.
* **Window Sizes**: (`window_sizes`, `select_multiple`, required: false) Choose the size of the window you need, such as 12 Inches or 24 Inches.
* **Submission Agreement Terms 1-12**: (`submission_agreement_terms_1` to `submission_agreement_terms_12`, `text`, required: false) Please see the "Submission Agreement" field above for the intended purpose of these fields.
