# Zoom Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for clients to provide their appointment and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read through each section carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
- Fill in the required information for your appointment and payment details.
- Choose your preferred payment method and payment type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Zoom Appointment Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Schedule Appointments**: This field is not required but will be useful if you would like to schedule an appointment.
* **Payment Method** (`Card`, `Bank Transfer`): Please select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Details**: Enter any relevant payment details here. This field is not required but can be useful if you have additional payment information.
* **HIPAA Compliance**: This field is not required but is a reminder of the HIPAA compliance policies.
* **Payment Status** (`Pending`, `Paid`, `Cancelled`): This field is not required and will be updated automatically by the system.
* **Payment Method** (`Card`, `Bank Transfer`): This is the same field as above, please select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Type** (`One-Time Payment`, `Subscription`): Choose the type of payment you wish to make.
* **Payment Frequency** (`One-Time Payment`, `Ongoing Payment`): This field is not required but will influence your payment type.
* **Payment Amount**: This is the amount you are paying for the appointment. Enter a number.
* **Schedule Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Schedule Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Schedule Duration**: Enter the duration of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Duration**: Enter the duration of your appointment.
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Practitioner Name**: Enter the name of the practitioner you are scheduling with.
* **Practitioner Email**: Enter the email address of the practitioner.
* **Practitioner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the practitioner.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Payment Terms** (`Paid at Time of Service`, `Invoiced and Paid Later`): This field is not required but will influence your payment method.
