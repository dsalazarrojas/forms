# Nail Brand Demo Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Brand Demo Appointment Form is used for customers to schedule a demo appointment with a nail brand. This form will help us to determine their availability and preferences for the demo appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Enter your Salon Name in the first field.
3. Enter your Email in the second field.
4. Enter your Salon Phone in the third field.
5. Enter your Name in the fourth field.
6. Enter your Email again in the fifth field (this field is for internal use only).
7. Enter your Phone in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Salon Name
* **Salon Name** (`salon_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your nail salon.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

### Salon Phone
* **Salon Phone** (`salon_phone`, text, required): Enter your salon's phone number.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.

### Email (Internal Use)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for internal use only. Please enter the email address associated with your account.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us.
* We will be in touch with you to schedule the demo appointment once we receive your form submission.
