# Personal Image Makeover Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Image Makeover Appointment Form is designed to gather information from clients for a personal image makeover session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Info, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Specify your Session Goals, describing what you want to achieve with your image makeover.
3. Choose your Session Preferences, selecting the options that suit your needs.
4. Schedule your Appointment Time and Date, ensuring we can accommodate you at a time and date that suits you.
5. If required, provide Salon and Stylist Info, so our team knows who you're working with.
6. Choose your Payment Method, specifying how you'd like to pay for your session.
7. Optionally, input your Payment Amount, which will be calculated based on your chosen services.
8. Add any extra Notes about your session or preferences.
9. If you have an Email, enter it to receive communication from us.
10. Provide your Phone number, so we can contact you if needed.
11. If applicable, assign a specific Form ID, which may be used for internal reference.
12. Select the Assigned Tool, which might be a specific equipment or resource required for your session.
13. Choose multiple options for Category, depending on your specific needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details relevant to your session.
* **Session Goals** (`2`, `text`, optional): Describe the goals you hope to achieve with your image makeover.
* **Session Preferences** (`3`, `text`, optional): Specify any preferences or requirements for your session.
* **Appointment Time** (`4`, `time`, optional): Choose a time slot for your session. Please note that this field is not required but strongly recommended.
* **Appointment Date** (`5`, `date`, optional): Select a date for your session. Like Appointment Time, this field is also not required but essential for scheduling.
* **Stylist Info** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred stylist, mention their name or ID.
* **Salon Info** (`7`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific salon requirements or preferences.
* **Payment Method** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your session.
* **Payment Amount** (`9`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you're willing to pay for your session.
* **Notes** (`10`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or requests for your session.
* **Email** (`11`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **Phone** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Form ID** (`13`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter a unique ID for your session.
* **Assigned Tool** (`14`, `text`, optional): Specify any specific equipment or resources required for your session.
* **Category** (`15-20`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable categories that relate to your session.
* **Category 2-11** (`16-21`, `select_multiple`, optional): These fields represent additional category options, which may also apply to your session.

Note: Some fields might have specific requirements or limitations, so be sure to review each field carefully to ensure you're providing the correct information. If unsure, please contact us for clarification.
