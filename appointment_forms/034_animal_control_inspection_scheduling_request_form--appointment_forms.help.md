# Animal Control Inspection Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an Animal Control inspection for your facility or business. Please fill out the form with your details to schedule an inspection that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
- For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if they do not apply to your situation.
- If you are unsure about any field, it is recommended to contact Animal Control for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, Required): Your full name as it appears on your personal identification or business documentation.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Required): Your contact email where we can send updates and information about your inspection.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Required): Your contact phone number for any additional information or questions.
* **Organization or Business** (`organization`, `text`, Optional): If your request is on behalf of a business or organization, please provide the name.
* **Animal Control Inspection Scheduling Request** (`request_intro`, `note`, Optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the inspection you are requesting.
* **Type of Inspection Needed** (`inspection_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the type of inspection you are requesting.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, Required): Provide the name of the facility or business being inspected.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, Required): Provide the address of the facility or business being inspected.
* **Type of Facility** (`facility_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the type of facility or business being inspected.
* **Approximate Number of Animals on Site** (`number_of_animals`, `select_one`, Required): Provide an estimate of the number of animals at the facility or business.
* **Species Housed** (`species_housed`, `select_multiple`, Required): Choose all species types that apply to the facility or business.
* **Current License Status** (`license_status`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the current status of the facility or business license.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, `text`, Optional): If applicable, provide the license number.
* **Preferred Inspection Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, Required): Choose a date for the inspection, and we will do our best to accommodate it.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the preferred time slot for the inspection.
* **Alternative Dates** (`alternative_dates`, `text`, Optional): If the preferred date is not available, provide alternative dates.
* **On-Site Contact Person** (`site_contact`, `text`, Required): Provide the name of the contact person at the facility or business.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, Required): Provide the contact phone number of the on-site contact.
* **Special Access Instructions** (`access_instructions`, `text`, Optional): If there are any special instructions for access to the facility or business, please provide them.
* **Have You Had Previous Inspections** (`previous_inspections`, `select_one`, Optional): Check yes if the facility or business has had previous inspections.
* **Were There Any Previous Violations** (`previous_violations`, `select_one`, Optional): If yes, check the type of violations.
* **Have Corrections Been Made** (`corrections_made`, `select_one`, Optional): If yes, check if corrections were made for the previous violations.
* **Are There Any Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`, `text`, Optional): If there are any special circumstances, please provide them.
* **Do You Have Required Documents Ready** (`documents_ready`, `select_one`, Optional): Check if you have all required documents ready.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them.
* **Requestor Signature** (`requestor_signature`, `text`, Required): Type your full name to confirm the submission of this form.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, Required): Date of signing.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or need guidance, contact Animal Control for assistance.
- This form is only for scheduling an inspection, not for submitting reports or complaints. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact Animal Control separately.
