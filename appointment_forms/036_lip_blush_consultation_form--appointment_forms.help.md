# lip_blush_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lip blush consultation form is designed to gather essential information about a patient's medical history, consent, and other relevant details before performing a lip blush procedure. This form helps healthcare professionals make informed decisions about the patient's care and ensures that they are aware of any potential risks or complications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the introduction to understand the purpose and procedure of the lip blush.
2.  Provide a brief introduction to the patient about the procedure and its risks.
3.  Fill out the medical history section, listing any existing medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
4.  Answer the patient consent questions to confirm understanding of the procedure.
5.  Provide contact details, such as phone number and email.
6.  List any additional medical conditions or allergies.
7.  Provide detailed notes about other medical conditions.
8.  Describe any other medical conditions.
9.  If applicable, select any additional medical conditions from the options.
10. Review and confirm patient consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is a lip blush?** (note): This field is a brief introduction to the procedure. Please provide a concise description of what a lip blush is.
* **Medical History** (text): List any existing medical conditions, allergies, or medications the patient has.
* **Patient Consent** (select_one): Indicate whether the patient has read and understands the procedure, or if they need further clarification.
* **Contact Details** (text): Enter the patient's contact information, such as their phone number and email.
* **Medications** (text): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (text): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Condition Notes** (note): Provide detailed notes about the patient's medical conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (select_multiple): Select any medical conditions that apply to the patient, such as hypersensitivity to certain medications or allergies to certain substances.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (text): List any other medical conditions not included in the previous option.
* **Other Conditions Notes** (note): Provide detailed notes about other medical conditions.
* **Other Conditions Description** (text): Describe any other medical conditions in detail.
* **Patient Consent 2** (select_one): Indicate whether the patient has read and understands the procedure again, or if they need further clarification.
* **Other Medical Conditions Description** (text): Enter any other medical conditions not included in the previous options.
