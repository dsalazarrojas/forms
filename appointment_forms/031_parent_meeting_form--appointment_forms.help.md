# Parent Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Meeting Form is used to record information about parent-teacher meetings. This form helps administrators track and analyze meeting data for better decision-making and communication with parents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the meeting details page and fill in the relevant meeting information.
2. Choose the meeting date from the calendar or enter it manually.
3. Enter the meeting time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
4. Type the parent's name as it appears on their records or as they prefer to be called.
5. Enter the meeting location, such as the classroom or office.
6. Add any relevant meeting notes for future reference.
7. If applicable, enter the meeting duration (e.g., 45 minutes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including any relevant context or topics discussed.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): Select or enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the meeting in 24-hour format.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): Type the parent's name as it appears on their records or as they prefer to be called.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the meeting, such as the classroom or office.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any important points discussed, action items, or agreements from the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the meeting (e.g., 45 minutes).
