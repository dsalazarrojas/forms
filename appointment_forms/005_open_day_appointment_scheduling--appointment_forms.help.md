# Open Day Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Day Appointment Scheduling form is designed for users to schedule visits during an open day event, providing an efficient way to plan and organize meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Schedule A Visit**: Enter the schedule for your visit. This is where you can select the time slot you would like to visit.
2. **Visitor Information**: Choose the type of visitor you are.
3. **Select A Time Slot**: Choose the time slot that you would like to schedule your visit.
4. **Visit Campus**: Choose whether you would like to visit the campus during the scheduled time.
5. **Booking Status**: Select the booking status of your chosen time slot.
6. **Submit**: Submit the form to schedule your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule A Visit**: Choose a time slot for your visit.
    * Type: `schedule_form`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Visitor Information**: Choose your type of visitor.
    * Type: `select_one` with options: `Option 1, Option 2`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Select A Time Slot**: Choose one or more time slots for your visit.
    * Type: `select_multiple` with options: `9:00 am - 10:00 am, 10:00 am - 11:00 am, 11:00 am - 12:00 pm`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Visit Campus**: Choose whether you want to visit the campus.
    * Type: `select_one` with options: `True, False`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Booking Status**: Choose the booking status of your time slot.
    * Type: `select_one` with options: `Available, Not Available`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your visit.
    * Type: `text`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
    * Type: `email`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
    * Type: `text`
    * Mandatory: Not mandatory

**Tips**: Please ensure that you have selected a time slot that is not already booked as 'Not Available'. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
