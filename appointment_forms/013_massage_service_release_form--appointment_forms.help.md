# Massage Service Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Service Release Form is designed to collect information from clients about their massage services. This includes details about the service provided, risks associated with it, and client contact information. The form helps massage service providers to gather essential data for their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form. This will be used as the header for the form.
2. Provide details about the massage service, such as the type of massage, duration, and any specific requirements or restrictions.
3. List any risks associated with the massage service, such as injuries or allergic reactions.
4. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the consent.
5. Write your acknowledgement, stating that you have read and understood the information provided.
6. Provide your contact information, including your name and contact details.
7. Sign the form to indicate your acceptance of the service and the terms outlined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Massage Service Release Form** (`form_title`, required: false):
	+ A brief title for the form.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, required: false):
	+ Describe the massage service, including the type, duration, and any specific requirements or restrictions.
* **Risks** (`risks`, required: false):
	+ List any risks associated with the massage service, such as injuries or allergic reactions.
* **Consent** (`consent`, required: false):
	+ Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the consent information.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, required: false):
	+ Write a statement indicating that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, required: false):
	+ Provide your name and contact details.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, required: false):
	+ Sign the form to indicate your acceptance of the service and the terms outlined.
* **Therapist Signature** (`therapist_signature`, required: false):
	+ Sign the form to indicate your acceptance of the service and the terms outlined.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the consent information before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the massage service provider.
* Please keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
