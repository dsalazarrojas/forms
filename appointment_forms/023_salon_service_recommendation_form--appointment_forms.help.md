## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their preferred salon services. This information will be used to ensure that our services are tailored to their specific needs, making their experience more enjoyable and satisfactory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with the required and optional information. Note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified.

* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, false): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you if needed.
