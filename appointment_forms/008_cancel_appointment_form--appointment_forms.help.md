# Cancel Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Cancel Appointment Form is designed for individuals to cancel their existing appointments with the organization. This form is used to gather essential information to facilitate the cancellation process and to help the organization understand the reason for cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name** as it appears on the original appointment.
2. Enter your **Email Address** for confirmation of cancellation.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for further contact.
4. Provide **Appointment Details** about your current booking, including the date and time.
5. Select the **Service or Department** you were initially booked with.
6. Choose the **Primary Reason** for cancellation from the provided options.
7. If you chose **Other** as the primary reason, provide a **Detailed Reason** for cancellation.
8. If you want to **Reschedule**, select your preference and provide a preferred new date and time.
9. Review and confirm the **Cancellation Policy**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on the original appointment.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation of cancellation.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Appointment Details** (appointment_details_section, note, optional): Provide information about your current booking.
* **Appointment Date** (appointment_date, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Original Appointment Time** (appointment_time, time, required): Enter the approximate time of your original booking.
* **Service or Department** (service_department, select_one, required): Select the service or department you were initially booked with.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (reason_section, note, optional): We appreciate your feedback, and you can write a brief explanation of your reason for cancellation.
* **Primary Reason** (primary_reason, select_one, required): Choose the most accurate reason for cancellation.
* **Detailed Reason** (detailed_reason, text, optional): If you chose **Other** as the primary reason, provide a brief explanation of your reason.
* **Rescheduling** (rescheduling_section, note, optional): Let us know your future plans.
* **Would you like to Reschedule?** (reschedule_interest, select_one, required): Choose your preference for a future booking.
* **Preferred New Date** (preferred_new_date, date, optional): If you want to reschedule, enter a preferred new date.
* **Preferred New Time Range** (preferred_time_range, select_one, optional): If you want to reschedule, choose a preferred time range.
* **Confirmation of Cancellation Policy** (policy_confirmation, select_one, required): Review and confirm the cancellation policy regarding fees or rebooking.
* **Submission Date** (signature_date, date, required): This will be automatically generated upon submission.
