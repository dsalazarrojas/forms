# Uniform Fitting Appointment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for uniform fittings, ensuring that you are well-prepared for your appointment. Please complete the form accurately and truthfully to ensure the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form completely and truthfully, ensuring all required fields are filled.
* Choose your School, Team or Business from the list.
* Enter your name and email address.
* Enter your phone number.
* Select a date and time for your appointment.
* Provide your measurements in centimeters or inches.
* If applicable, provide any additional comments or notes.
* If necessary, upload supporting documents, images, videos, or audio files.
* Choose whether you have an appointment or not, and if you're unsure, select "Maybe".
* Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School, Team or Business**: Select your affiliation from the given options.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Select the date for your appointment.
* **Time**: Select the time for your appointment.
* **Measurements (cm, in, etc)**: Provide your measurements in centimeters or inches.
* **Preference**: Enter any additional preference or note.
* **Appointment Booking**: Choose whether you have an appointment or not.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Upload Document**: If required, upload any supporting document.
* **Upload Image**: If required, upload an image.
* **Upload File**: If required, upload a file.
* **Upload Video**: If required, upload a video.
* **Upload Audio**: If required, upload an audio file.
* **Upload PDF**: If required, upload a PDF file.
* **Upload Attachment**: If required, upload any attachment.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your form.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* Upload only relevant and supporting files.
* Choose "Maybe" if you're unsure about your appointment status.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure it's complete and accurate.
