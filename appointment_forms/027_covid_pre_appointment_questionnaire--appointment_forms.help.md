# Covid Pre Appointment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Pre Appointment Questionnaire is a form used to gather information about a person's health and appointment scheduling needs before a medical appointment. This form is designed to help healthcare providers gather necessary information to prepare for the appointment and ensure a smooth and efficient visit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "How To Complete This Form" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. This is the first step in filling out the form.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Select a date for your appointment here. This is an important step in scheduling your appointment.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Select a time for your appointment here (if required).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here. This is a helpful way for us to reach out to you with any last-minute updates or reminders.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here (if required).
* **Reason** (`Reason`, text, optional): Describe your reason for visiting us here (if required).
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Share your medical history with us here (if required).
* **Travel History** (`Travel History`, text, optional): Share your travel history with us here (if required).
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter contact information for an emergency contact here (if required).
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider here (if required).
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions you are experiencing here (if required).
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, text, optional): Describe your symptoms here (if required).
* **Medication** (`Medication`, text, optional): List any medication you are currently taking here (if required).
* **Appointment ID** (`Appointment ID`, select_one, required): Select the assigned appointment ID for your visit.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select any assigned tools or equipment that you will need for your appointment here (if required).
* **Output File** (`Output File`, text, optional): Upload an output file here (if required).
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, text, required): Enter a unique ID for this form.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your appointment here (if required).
* **Category** (`Category`, text, optional): Select a category for your appointment here (if required).
* **Form Title** (`Form Title`, text, optional): Enter a title for this form here (if required).
* **Form Category** (`Form Category`, text, optional): Select a category for this form here (if required).
* **Form Description** (`Form Description`, text, optional): Enter a description for this form here (if required).
* **Form Assigned Tool** (`Form Assigned Tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select any assigned tools or equipment for this form here (if required).
