# Skincare Franchise Discovery Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your information so that we can schedule a discovery meeting with a representative from our organization. The meeting will be used to discuss your interests in franchising your skincare business and determine the feasibility of our support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address to allow us to identify you and contact you about your franchise inquiry.
2. Provide your Phone number so that we can reach you to schedule the meeting.
3. Choose a date and time that works best for you for the meeting.
4. Specify the duration of the meeting.
5. Select your preferred location for the meeting.
6. Add any notes that you would like to share with us to get the most out of the meeting.
7. Enter the name of the franchise you are interested in and any relevant website and email address associated with it.
8. Enter the phone number and location of your franchise business.
9. Specify the hours of operation for your franchise business.
10. Finally, add any additional notes about your franchise that will help us prepare for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your franchise inquiry.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can reach you to schedule the meeting.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date that works best for you for the meeting.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Choose a time that works best for you for the meeting.
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required): Specify the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Choose a location for the meeting that works best for you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information that you would like to share with us.
* **Franchise Name** (`franchise_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the franchise you are interested in.
* **Franchise Website** (`franchise_website`, `text`, required): Enter the website of your franchise business.
* **Franchise Email** (`franchise_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for your franchise business.
* **Franchise Phone** (`franchise_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your franchise business.
* **Franchise Hours** (`franchise_hours`, `time`, required): Specify the hours of operation for your franchise business.
* **Franchise Location** (`franchise_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your franchise business.
* **Franchise Notes** (`franchise_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your franchise that will help us prepare for the meeting.
