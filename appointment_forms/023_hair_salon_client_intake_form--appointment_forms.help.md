# hair_salon_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from new clients, including contact details and appointment preferences, to help the hair salon prepare and manage their schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "prophets" field. This is the primary identifier for our records.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact_information" field. This includes your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
3. Select your preferred appointment duration in the "appointment_preferences" field. Choose from 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
4. Choose a date for your appointment in the "appointment_date" field. This is the date you'd like to schedule your appointment for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **prophets** (text): Enter your name as you'd like it to be used in our records.
- **contact_information** (text): Provide your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
- **appointment_preferences** (select_multiple): Select one or more of the following appointment durations: 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
- **appointment_date** (date): Choose a date for your appointment.
