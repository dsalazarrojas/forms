# Skincare Product Innovation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us gather information from skincare professionals and enthusiasts to better understand their needs and preferences for skincare products. By completing this form, you will be helping us to create innovative and effective skincare products that cater to the diverse needs of users like you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your details.
2. Provide accurate and honest information to help us better understand your skin condition and concerns.
3. Select the skin condition that best describes your skin.
4. Choose the skin type that best fits your skin.
5. Describe your skin concerns in the text area.
6. If this is a test session, indicate by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. Provide feedback about your experience with our products or services.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Title** (text, required): Enter a brief title for this session, e.g., "Regular Check-up" or "Product Trial".
* **Session Date** (date, required): Select the date of your session.
* **Session Time** (time, required): Select the time of your session.
* **Skin Condition** (select one, required): Choose the skin condition that best describes your skin, e.g., Acne, Dry skin, Eczema, or Psoriasis.
* **Skin Type** (select multiple, required): Choose the skin type that best fits your skin, e.g., Normal, Sensitive, Oily.
* **Skin Concerns** (text, required): Describe your skin concerns in a few sentences.
* **Test Session** (select multiple, required): If this is a test session, indicate by selecting 'Yes'.
* **Feedback** (select one, required): Provide feedback about your experience with our products or services, e.g., Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your session.
 
## Tips

* Be as accurate and honest as possible when completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We value your input and appreciate your participation in helping us improve our products and services.
