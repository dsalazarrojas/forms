# Therapeutic Massage Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapeutic Massage Appointment Form is used to schedule and customize a therapeutic massage appointment with a client. The form collects information about the client's appointment preferences, including date, time, treatment type, and contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a date for your appointment.
2. Choose a time for your appointment.
3. Select the type of massage you would like to receive.
4. Provide any additional comments or information about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Appointment Date
* `Appointment Date`: Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.

* `Appointment Time`: Choose the time you would like to schedule your appointment.

* `Treatment Type`: Select the type of massage you would like to receive:
	+ Massage
	+ Swedish Massage
	+ Deep Tissue Massage
	+ Sports Massage
	+ Aromatherapy
	+ Reflexology

* `Appointment Time Length`: Choose the length of time you would like to schedule your appointment. (Note: This field may not be relevant if you select a shorter massage type.)

* `Massage Length`: (Note: This field seems redundant; it's assumed you will select a massage type with its corresponding length from the options above.)

* `Appointment Location`: Enter any relevant information about the location of your appointment.
* `Client Health Information`: If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please enter that information here.
* `Preferred Contact Method`: Check all methods you would like to be contacted by:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Message
* `Additional Comments`: Provide any additional comments or information about your appointment.
