# Virtual Screening Slot Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book virtual screening slots for medical consultations. You can use it to schedule appointments with doctors or therapists for video consultations, phone calls, or text messaging.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the time slot you prefer from the options provided.
2.  Choose the date for your virtual screening.
3.  Select the type of virtual screening you require (Video Consult, Phone Call, or Text Message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Virtual Screening Slot Booking Form** (`Virtual Screening Slot Booking Form`, `text`, optional): This is the form title, which is not a field to fill, but rather a label for the form.
*   **Select Time Slot** (`Select Time Slot`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred time slot for the virtual screening. You can select from 09:00, 10:00, or 11:00.
*   **Select Date** (`Select Date`, `date`, optional): Pick the date for your virtual screening.
*   **Select Virtual Screening Type** (`Select Virtual Screening Type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of virtual screening you require, which can be Video Consult, Phone Call, or Text Message.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct time and date for your virtual screening.
*   Ensure that you've selected the correct type of virtual screening you need.
*   Double-check your submission before sending the form to prevent errors.
