# Occupational Therapy Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment with an occupational therapist. Please fill out the required fields to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all required fields, marked with an *.
2. For the "Therapist Name" field, please enter the name of the occupational therapist assigned to you for this appointment.
3. For the "Follow-up" field, please select "Yes" if you require additional follow-up information or "No" if you do not.
4. For the "Confirm" field, please select all options that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or government-issued document.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for any last-minute instructions or notifications.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the date of your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please enter the time of your appointment.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Please enter the expected duration of your appointment.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your assigned occupational therapist.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Please enter the reason for your appointment.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Please enter the location of your appointment.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments for your appointment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please enter any comments or questions you may have.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" if you require additional follow-up information or "No" if you do not.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_multiple, required): Please select all options that apply, indicating your understanding of the above information.

Note: Please review and confirm that the information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
