# Pediatric Nephrology Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Nephrology Appointment Request form is used to schedule an appointment at the pediatric nephrology department. It collects necessary information for the patient and appointment scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as applicable.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
3. Indicate the preferred visit type and date.
4. Select the preferred visit time.
5. Provide a reason for the appointment and any other relevant details.
6. Enter the patient's contact phone number and email.
7. Indicate the contact relationship with the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pediatric Nephrology Appointment Request** (`pediatric_nephrology_appointment_request_form`, text, not required): This is the title of the form and serves as a header.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, not required): Enter the patient's date of birth. This is helpful for verifying the patient's age.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the patient's contact email.
* **Visit Preferences** (`visit_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Select the preferred visit type, such as "Kidney Care", "General Health", or "Other".
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to schedule the appointment for.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, not required): Select the preferred visit time.
* **Appointment Reason** (`appointment_reason`, text, not required): Provide a reason for the appointment, such as regular check-up or specific concerns.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of appointment, such as "Initial Visit", "Follow-up Visit", or "Routine Visit".
* **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, not required): Indicate your relationship with the patient, such as parent or guardian.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, not required): Enter your contact address, which is optional but may be helpful for follow-up communication.
