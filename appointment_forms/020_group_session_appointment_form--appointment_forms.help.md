# Group Session Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Group Session Appointment Form is used to gather participant information and session details for group sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the participant information, including their name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to communicate with the participant and send them session reminders and updates.
2. Select the session details, including the session type (e.g., Session 1, Session 2, Session 3) and the date and time of the session.
3. Enter any additional workshop information, if necessary.
4. Enter the participant's email and phone number again, ensuring that they are accurate and up-to-date.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the participant or session.
6. Select the payment terms, including the payment method and terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session 1** (`session_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the session type for this participant.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, number, optional): Enter any additional details about the workshop or session.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Session Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for this session.
* **Payment Terms Assistant** (`payment_terms_assistant`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Assistant** (`payment_terms_assistant_assistant`, select_one, optional): Select whether or not the participant has agreed to the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Assistant Assistant** (`payment_terms_assistant_assistant_assistant`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Assistant Assistant Assistant** (`payment_terms_assistant_assistant_assistant_assistant`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the payment terms.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by participants who are booking a group session with a facilitator or trainer. The fields are labeled and explained in a way that is easy to understand and complete.
