# Lash Extension Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients book an appointment for lash extension services. It gathers necessary information about the client and the service they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of service you want to book an appointment for from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar in the "Date" field.
4. Select a time for your appointment from the dropdown menu in the "Time" field.
5. Choose the lash technician you want to book with from the "Lash Technician" dropdown menu.
6. If you have any additional notes or details, enter them in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. If this appointment is a follow-up for a previous appointment, enter the "Appointment ID" number.
10. Select the type of lash service you want to book from the "Lash Type" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one): Select the type of service you want to book an appointment for. This can be Eyelashes, Lash Extensions, Eyeliner, Lash Lifting, Lash Tints, Lash Perms, or Lash Lifting.
  - **Client Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name as it should appear in your appointment booking confirmation and any communication about your appointment.
  - **Date** (`date`, date): Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar. The date should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
  - **Time** (`time`, time): Select a time for your appointment. You can choose from the available time slots.
  - **Lash Technician** (`technician`, select_one): Choose the lash technician you want to book with. This will ensure that you are booked with the same technician as your previous appointments.
  - **Notes** (`notes`, text): If you have any additional notes or details about your appointment, enter them here. This can be anything from a special request to a medical condition that may affect your appointment.
  - **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address as it should appear in your appointment booking confirmation and any communication about your appointment.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number as it should appear in your appointment booking confirmation and any communication about your appointment.
  - **Appointment ID** (`appointment_id`, number): If you are booking a follow-up appointment, enter the number of your previous appointment.
  - **Lash Type** (`lash_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of lash service you want to book from the available options: Individual Lashes, Full Lashes, Lash Fills, Lash Touch-ups, Lash Removal, Lash Lift, Lash Curling, or Other.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct service type and lash technician to ensure that you are booked with the right person.
* Double-check your email and phone number to ensure they are correct.
* If you have any special requests or medical conditions, include them in the "Notes" field.
