# executive_coaching_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive coaching appointment form is designed to collect information from executives for the purpose of scheduling coaching appointments. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured, making it easier for the coaching team to prepare for and conduct the coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first_name field.
2. Provide your email address in the email field. This is a required field, so make sure to fill it out correctly.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Select the type of meeting you would like to schedule (Yes/No).
5. Enter the start and end time of the meeting (in date and time format).
6. Specify the duration of the meeting (in minutes).
7. Indicate whether you would like coaching to be a part of the meeting (Yes/No).
8. Choose how frequently you would like coaching to be provided (Yes/No).
9. Add any additional notes about the meeting in the notes field (optional).
10. Confirm your meeting preferences by selecting all applicable options in the confirm field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Meeting Type** (meeting_type, select_one, optional): Select Yes if you would like to schedule a meeting, No otherwise.
* **Start Time** (start_time, date, required): Enter the date and time of the meeting (e.g., 2024-02-16 08:00).
* **End Time** (end_time, time, required): Enter the end time of the meeting (e.g., 08:00).
* **Duration** (duration, number, required): Enter the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Coaching Type** (coaching_type, select_one, optional): Select Yes if you would like coaching to be a part of the meeting, No otherwise.
* **Coaching Frequency** (coaching_frequency, select_one, optional): Select Yes if you would like coaching to be provided more frequently, No otherwise.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional information about the meeting, if needed.
* **Confirm** (confirm, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options that match your meeting preferences.
