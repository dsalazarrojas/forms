# Reschedule Your Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist users in rescheduling appointments. It collects information about the new date and time of the appointment, reason for rescheduling, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the new date for your appointment.
2. Choose the new time for your appointment.
3. Provide a reason for rescheduling.
4. Enter any new details about your appointment.
5. Enter the old date and time of your original appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Date** (`new_date`, date, optional): Enter the new date for your appointment. Select a date from the calendar or type it in manually.
* **New Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the new time for your appointment. Select a time from the time picker or type it in manually.
* **New Time (again)** (`new_time`, time, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, enter the new time for your appointment. Select a time from the time picker or type it in manually.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for rescheduling your appointment.
* **New Appointment Details** (`new_appointment`, text, optional): Enter any other details about your new appointment.
* **Old Date** (`old_date`, date, optional): Enter the original date of your appointment.
* **Old Time** (`old_time`, time, optional): Enter the original time of your appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to provide complete information.
* Check the format of the date and time fields to ensure they are correct.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact the form owner for assistance.
