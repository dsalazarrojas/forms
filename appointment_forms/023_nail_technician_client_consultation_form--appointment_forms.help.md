# Nail Technician Client Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nail Technician Client Consultation Form is a tool used to capture information from clients during a consultation session. This form helps nail technicians to gather vital details about the client's preferences, concerns, and expectations from the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. If a field is not applicable to you, please mark it as "N/A" or "None" if it's an option.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the technician or client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each section of the form is designed to gather specific information about the client's preferences and appointment details.

### Page 1: Client Consultation Form
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`): Write a brief description of the client's preferences, concerns, or expectations from the appointment. 
* **Nail Type** (`nail_type`): Select all the types of nail services required (Nail, Polish, Gel).
* **Nail Length** (`nail_length`): Enter the length of the nail (not applicable).
* **Nail Shape** (`nail_shape`): Select the shape of the nail (Oval, Square, Round).
* **Nail Color** (`nail_color`): Select all the desired nail colors (Red, Pink, Purple).
* **Nail Finish** (`nail_finish`): Select the finish style (Gloss, Matte, Texture).
* **Nail Strength** (`nail_strength`): Select the desired nail strength (Weak, Medium, Strong).
* **Client Concerns** (`client_concerns`): Write any concerns the client may have about their nails.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes from the client.

### Page 2: Appointment Details
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`): Enter the date of the next appointment.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`): Sign your name as the technician.
* **Technician Phone** (`technician_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Technician Email** (`technician_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Technician Address** (`technician_address`): Enter your work address.
* **Technician Hours** (`technician_hours`): Select your available hours of work (8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 7:00 PM).
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`): Enter your name.
* **Technician Title** (`technician_title`): Enter your title.
* **Appointment ID** (`appointment_id`): Enter the appointment ID.
* **Technician License Status** (`technician_license`): Select your active or inactive status as a technician.
* **Technician Notes** (`technician_notes`): Add any notes or comments as the technician.
* **Technician Comments** (`technician_comments`): Add any comments or notes as the technician.
