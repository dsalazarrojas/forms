# covid_19_pre_appointment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

<thinking>
This form is used to gather information about a patient's medical history, symptoms, and other relevant details prior to an appointment. The goal is to collect data that will help healthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and make informed decisions about their care.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the **General Info** section.
2. Describe your **Medical History** in the text field provided.
3. Select any **Symptoms** you are currently experiencing from the dropdown list.
4. Indicate your **Vaccination Status** as 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Enter any details about **Contact Tracing** you have been a part of, if applicable.
6. Provide your **Phone Number** and **Email** for communication purposes.
7. Choose a suitable **Appointment Time** and **Date** for your appointment.
8. Enter the name of the healthcare professional you will be seeing during your appointment in **Doctor Name**.
9. List any **Medical Conditions** you are currently being treated for.
10. Mention any **Medication Allergies** you have.
11. Provide information about your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact** field.
12. Enter the name of your **Insurance Provider**.
13. Specify the **Medical Group** you are visiting.
14. Choose a location for your appointment in **Appointment Location**.
15. If applicable, provide any additional details about your **Medical Conditions** in the **Medical Condition Note** field.
16. Enter any additional details about your **Medical History** in the **Medical History Additional** field.
17. Leave any **Patient Notes** for your healthcare professional to review.
18. Provide any additional details about your **Medical Group** in the **Medical Group Note** field.
19. Enter any **Vaccination Note** if necessary.
20. Enter any **Appointment Notes** for your healthcare professional to review.
21. Leave any **Contact Tracing Notes** for your healthcare professional to review.
22. Enter any **Phone Number Notes** for your healthcare professional to review.
23. Enter any **Emergency Contact Notes** for your healthcare professional to review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Symptoms**: Select any symptoms you are currently experiencing from the dropdown list. These may include Fever, Chills, Headache, Cough, Congestion, Shortness of breath, Muscle pain, Sore throat, Runny nose, Nausea, Vomiting, Dizziness, Numbness/Tingling sensation, Loss of appetite, Loss of taste.
* **Vaccination Status**: Indicate whether you have been vaccinated or not. Select 'Yes' if you have been vaccinated and 'No' if you have not.
* **Contact Tracing**: Provide any details about any contact tracing you have been a part of, such as recent travel or exposure to someone with COVID-19.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for your healthcare professional to contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for your healthcare professional to contact you.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose a suitable time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Date**: Choose a suitable date for your appointment.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional you will be seeing during your appointment.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you are currently being treated for.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medications you are allergic to.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Medical Group**: Specify the medical group you are visiting.
* **Appointment Location**: Choose a location for your appointment.
* **Medical Condition Note**: Provide any additional details about your medical conditions.
* **Medical History Additional**: Enter any additional details about your medical history.
* **Patient Notes**: Leave any personal notes for your healthcare professional to review.
* **Medical Group Note**: Provide any additional details about your medical group.
* **Vaccination Note**: Enter any additional details about your vaccination status.
* **Appointment Notes**: Leave any additional details about your appointment for your healthcare professional to review.
* **Contact Tracing Note**: Leave any additional details about your contact tracing status.
* **Phone Number Note**: Leave any additional details about your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Leave any additional details about your emergency contact.
