# Schedule A Zoom Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Schedule A Zoom Meeting Form" is designed to gather essential information for scheduling meetings with team members. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently, preventing duplication and ensuring smooth communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of meeting you would like to schedule (Video Meeting, Audio Meeting, or Phone Meeting).
2. Choose a meeting time that works for you and other participants.
3. Select a date for the meeting.
4. Enter the start and end times of the meeting.
5. Provide a brief description of the meeting (e.g., agenda, goals, or any other relevant details).
6. Select the duration of the meeting.
7. Indicate the location of the meeting (if applicable).
8. Select the meeting participants (e.g., team members, customers, or external partners).
9. Add any additional meeting details or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Type** (`Meeting Type`, select_one): Choose the type of meeting you need to schedule, such as Video Meeting, Audio Meeting, or Phone Meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`Meeting Time`, time): Enter the time of the meeting. Be sure to select the correct time that works for you and all participants.
* **Meeting Date** (`Meeting Date`, date): Choose a date for the meeting. Select a date that fits your schedule and the schedules of all participants.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time): Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time): Enter the end time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Subject** (`Meeting Subject`, text): Briefly describe the topic or purpose of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`Meeting Duration`, number): Select the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Location** (`Meeting Location`, text): Indicate the location of the meeting, if applicable.
* **Meeting Participants** (`Meeting Participants`, select_multiple): Select all participants involved in the meeting, such as team members or customers.
* **Meeting Description** (`Meeting Description`, text): Add any additional meeting details or notes.
