# Court Hearing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a court hearing appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for the hearing type, preferred dates, available dates, available time, and preferred time.
3. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required): Enter the case number.
* **Court Number** (`court_number`, text, required): Enter the court number.
* **Court Date** (`court_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the hearing.
* **Court Time** (`court_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the hearing.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **User Address** (`user_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Hearing Type** (`hearing_type`, select one, required): Select the type of hearing.
* **Prefered Dates** (`preferred_dates`, select multiple, required): Select your preferred dates for the hearing.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, select multiple, required): Select your available dates for the hearing.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, select multiple, required): Select your available time for the hearing.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select multiple, required): Select your preferred time for the hearing.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the assigned tool for the hearing.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, optional): Enter the form IDs.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Enter the category of the hearing.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the hearing.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the hearing.
* **Category Title** (`category_title`, text, required): Enter the category title.
* **Description Title** (`description_title`, text, required): Enter the description title.
* **Assigned Tool Title** (`assigned_tool_title`, text, required): Enter the assigned tool title.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, text, optional): Enter an additional assigned tool if necessary.
* **Category Title 2** (`category_title_2`, text, optional): Enter an additional category title if necessary.
* **Assigned Tool 2 Title** (`assigned_tool_2_title`, text, optional): Enter an additional assigned tool title if necessary.
