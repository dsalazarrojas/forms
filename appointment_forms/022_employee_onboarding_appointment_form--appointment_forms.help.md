# Employee Onboarding Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Appointment Form is a tool used to schedule onboarding appointments for new employees. This form will help streamline the onboarding process by collecting all necessary information from new employees and their managers in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your new hire information, including your name, job title, email, phone number, and location.
2. Select your preferred appointment date and time.
3. Provide any additional details about your schedule and preferred appointment time.
4. Choose the onboarding process and status that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter your name as a new employee.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): This field is used to enter your job title or position within the company.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field is used to enter your phone number.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): This field is used to enter your work location or office.
- **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required): This field is used to select a date for your onboarding appointment.
- **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, required): This field is used to select a time for your onboarding appointment.
- **Schedule Details** (`schedule_details`, text, required): This field is used to provide any additional details about your schedule.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter your manager's name.
- **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the onboarding processes that apply to you. Choose all that apply.
- **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, required): This field is used to select your onboarding status.
- **New Hire Info** (`new_hire_info`, text, required): This field is used to provide any additional information about your onboarding.
- **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required): This field is used to provide any additional details about your appointment.
- **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the onboarding status of the new employee.
