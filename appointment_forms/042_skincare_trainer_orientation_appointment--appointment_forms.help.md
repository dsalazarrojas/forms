# Skincare Trainer Orientation Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and plan skincare training sessions. It gathers information from trainers about their availability, preferences, and session details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the schedule for the session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
2. Provide your background as a trainer.
3. Choose the topics you'd like to focus on during the session (Skincare, Haircare, or Nailcare).
4. Enter your name (optional).
5. Enter your email address (required for communication purposes).
6. Select the type of establishment where you'll be conducting the session (Spa, Salon, or Academy).
7. Enter the address of the establishment.
8. Specify the topic of the session.
9. Choose a date for the session.
10. Select the time for the session.
11. Enter the duration of the session.
12. Add any additional notes about the session (optional).
13. Enter your phone number (optional).
14. Confirm the session details to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Session**: Select the time of day you're available to conduct the session. This will help us schedule the session accordingly.
* **Trainer Background**: Please briefly describe your background as a trainer. This will help us understand your qualifications and experience.
* **Topic Preferences**: Choose the topics you'd like to focus on during the session. Select all that apply.
	+ Skincare
	+ Haircare
	+ Nailcare
* **Trainer Name**: Enter your name (optional).
* **Trainer Email**: Enter your email address (required for communication purposes).
* **Salon or Academy**: Select the type of establishment where you'll be conducting the session. This can be a Spa, Salon, or Academy.
* **Salon or Academy Address**: Enter the address of the establishment.
* **Topic**: Specify the topic of the session.
* **Session Date**: Choose a date for the session.
* **Session Time**: Select the time for the session.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the session.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Confirm Session**: Confirm the session details to ensure accuracy. Please select 'True' to confirm or 'False' to indicate you need to make changes.
