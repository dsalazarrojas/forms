# Film Distribution Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to book a film distribution consultation. It is intended for customers to book appointments with our team to discuss film distribution-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant page for your consultation (First page, Second page, or Third page).
2.  Choose the consultation type that best suits your needs.
3.  Provide your preferred date and time for the consultation.
4.  Enter the duration of the consultation (in minutes).
5.  Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
6.  Enter any additional notes or comments.
7.  Confirm your booking details.
