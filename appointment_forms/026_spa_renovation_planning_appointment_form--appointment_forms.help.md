# Spa Renovation Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Renovation Planning Appointment Form is used to schedule and track appointments for spa renovations. It gathers essential information from clients, including their project details, budget, and appointment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the required field label (e.g., **Required**).
3. For select fields, choose the most relevant option for your appointment.
4. Enter your contact information so that the spa team can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (required): Enter your full name and contact information so that the spa team can communicate with you efficiently.
* **Project Details** (optional): Describe your project in a brief and clear manner. This will help the spa team understand your vision and needs.
* **Project Budget** (required): Enter the total budget for your project.
* **Appointment Requests** (optional, multiple selection): Choose the services you would like to schedule for your appointment.
* **Spa Manager** (optional): If applicable, enter the spa manager's name, email, and phone number to ensure that they are aware of your appointment.
* **Contact Info** (required): Enter your email address so that the spa team can contact you with updates and follow-up information.
* **Email** (optional): Enter your email address (if different from the contact info above).
* **Meeting Request** (optional, single selection): Indicate if you would like to schedule a meeting with the spa manager.
* **Follow-up** (optional, multiple selection): Choose how you would like the spa team to follow up with you after your appointment.
* **Spa Details** (optional): Enter any additional information about your spa that you think would be helpful for the spa team.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any notes or comments about your appointment.
* **Spa Manager's Email** (optional): Enter the email address of the spa manager (if different from the contact info above).
* **Spa Manager's Phone** (optional): Enter the phone number of the spa manager (if different from the contact info above).
* **Spa Manager's Phone Ext** (optional): Enter the phone extension for the spa manager (if applicable).
* **Appointment Status** (optional): Indicate the status of your appointment (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Follow-up Status** (optional, single selection): Choose how you would like the spa team to follow up with you after your appointment.
* **Meeting Status** (optional, single selection): Indicate the status of your meeting with the spa manager.
* **Next Step** (optional): Enter the next step or task to be completed for your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (required): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (required): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Spa Location** (optional, single selection): Choose the location of your spa.
* **Spa Manager's Name** (optional): Enter the name of the spa manager (if different from the contact info above).
* **Spa Manager's Email** (optional): Enter the email address of the spa manager (if different from the contact info above).
* **Spa Manager's Phone** (optional): Enter the phone number of the spa manager (if different from the contact info above).

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general explanation of the form's purpose and fields. Please refer to the actual form for specific field instructions and validation requirements.
