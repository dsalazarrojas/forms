# Live Session Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to schedule live sessions with clients, providing a structured way for coaches to collect information and confirm session details with their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session details, including the session date and time.
2. Select the session type and topic.
3. Choose the session recurrence (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
4. Confirm the session scheduling and follow-up details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the coach.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number (optional).
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session (Session Coaching, Session Tutoring, Session Consulting).
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, optional): Enter the topic of the session (optional).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the session (optional).
* **Session Recurrence** (`session_recurrence`, select_multiple, required): Choose how frequently the session will occur (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the session (optional).
* **Client Message** (`client_message`, text, optional): Enter a message to be sent to the client after scheduling (optional).
* **Coach Message** (`coach_message`, text, optional): Enter a message to be sent to the client after scheduling (optional).
* **Confirm Scheduling** (`confirm_scheduling`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the session is scheduled or not.
* **Follow-up Message** (`follow_up_message`, text, optional): Enter a message to be sent to the client after the session (optional).
* **Confirm Follow-up** (`confirm_follow_up`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the follow-up is scheduled or not.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information to avoid any scheduling conflicts.
* Double-check the session details before confirming scheduling.
* Communicate clearly with the client through the follow-up message.
