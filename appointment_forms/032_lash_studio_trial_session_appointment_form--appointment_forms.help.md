# lash_studio_trial_session_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Lash Studio Trial Session Appointment Form** is designed for clients and saloons to communicate and plan a trial session. The form helps gather necessary information for a successful booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for your preferences and choices.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trial Session** (`id: 1`): Enter the trial session number to confirm the session.
* **Client Preferences** (`id: 2`): Select your preferences for the session, such as "Yes" or "No" for certain services.
* **Allergies** (`id: 3`): Enter any allergies or medical conditions that may affect the session.
* **Saloon Details** (`id: 4`): Enter the saloon details, such as address or phone number.
* **Lash Artist** (`id: 5`): Select whether a lash artist will be present during the session.
* **Session Date** (`id: 6`): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`id: 7`): Enter the time of the session.
* **Note** (`id: 8`): Leave a note for the saloon or lash technician.
* **Email** (`id: 9`): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`id: 10`): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Saloon Location** (`id: 11`): Select whether the saloon is located in a certain area.
* **Saloon Hours** (`id: 12`): Select whether the saloon has certain hours of operation.
* **Saloon Services** (`id: 13`): Select the services offered by the saloon.
* **Saloon Equipment** (`id: 14`): Select the equipment available at the saloon.
* **Saloon Specialties** (`id: 15`): Select the specialties of the saloon.
* **Saloon License** (`id: 16`): Select whether the saloon has a certain license.
* **Saloon Certifications** (`id: 17`): Select the certifications held by the saloon.
* **Saloon Insurance** (`id: 18`): Select whether the saloon has insurance.
* **Saloon Payment Method** (`id: 19`): Select the payment method used by the saloon.
* **Session Duration** (`id: 20`): Enter the expected duration of the session.
* **Cancellation Fee** (`id: 21`): Enter a cancellation fee (if applicable).
* **Client Signature** (`id: 22`): Sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.
* **Saloon Signature** (`id: 23`): Sign the form to confirm the saloon's understanding and agreement.
* **Saloon License Number** (`id: 24`): Enter the saloon's license number (if applicable).
* **Saloon Address** (`id: 25`): Enter the saloon's address (if applicable).

Note: This guide assumes that all fields are filled in correctly, and the saloon has reviewed and confirmed the information. It is essential to double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
