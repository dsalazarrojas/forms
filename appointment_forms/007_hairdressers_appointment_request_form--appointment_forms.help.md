# Hairdressers Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hairdressers Appointment Request Form" is a form used to request an appointment with a hairdresser for various services such as haircuts, coloring, or highlights. This form allows users to select the service type, choose a stylist, select a date, and pick a time for their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the fields on this page.
2. Fill out the fields with your desired information:
	* `hairdressers_appointment_request_form_page_1`: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about your appointment.
	* `appointment_details`: This field is for any additional details you'd like to provide about your appointment.
	* `select_service`: Select the service you'd like to receive (e.g., Haircut, Coloring, Highlights).
	* `select_stylist`: Choose the stylist you'd like to work with.
	* `select_date`: Select the date you'd like to schedule your appointment.
	* `select_time`: Select the time you'd like to schedule your appointment.
	* `select_email`: Enter your email address.
	* `select_phone`: Enter your phone number.
	* `additional_comments`: This is a free text field for any additional comments or requests you'd like to share with the hairdresser.
