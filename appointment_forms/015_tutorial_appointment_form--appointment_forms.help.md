# Tutorial Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for scheduling and managing tutorials and appointments. It captures information for both individual and group sessions, including tutor and student details, session details, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the **Tutor** for the appointment. This will be the person leading the tutorial or session.
2. Choose the **Student** participating in the appointment. Select from the list of available options.
3. Enter the **Session Date** for the tutorial or appointment.
4. Choose the **Tutorial Type** (Individual or Group).
5. Enter the **Session Time** for the appointment.
6. Enter the **Session Duration** in minutes.
7. Enter the **Tutorial Location** where the session will take place.
8. Add any additional **Session Notes**.
9. Enter the **Student Email** and **Student Phone** for contact information.
10. Enter the **Tutor Email** and **Tutor Phone** for contact information.
11. Enter the **Coordinator Email** and **Coordinator Phone** for contact information.
12. Add any **Comments** about the session.
13. Select who **Submitted** the form (Student, Tutor, or Coordinator).
14. Enter the **Submitted On** date and time.
15. Select the **Session Status** (Confirmed, Pending, or Rescheduled).
16. Enter the **Rescheduled To** date and time (if applicable).
17. Select the **Rescheduled Status** (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled).
18. Enter the **Session Cancellation** reason (if applicable).
19. Select who **Cancelled** the session (Student, Tutor, or Coordinator).
20. Enter the **Cancellation Reason**.
