# Financial Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Service Appointment Form is designed to collect and store client appointment details for financial services. This form aims to gather information about the client's personal details, professional involvement, and appointment specifics to facilitate organized and efficient client interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Input**: Enter the client's name and provide any additional relevant information about the client.
2. **Appointment Date**: Select or enter the appointment date.
3. **Service Type**: Choose whether the service is active or inactive.
4. **Service Location**: Choose whether the service is provided at a specific location or not.
5. **Additional Services**: Select any additional services to be provided.
6. **Appointment Notes**: Add any additional notes about the appointment.
7. **Appointment Time**: Select or enter the appointment time.
8. **Professional Details**: Enter the professional's name, phone number, and email.
9. **Client Details**: Enter the client's phone number and email.
10. **Confirm**: Save and review the form before sending it for processing or saving it as a draft.
