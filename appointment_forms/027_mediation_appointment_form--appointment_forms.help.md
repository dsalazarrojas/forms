# Mediation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Appointment Form is designed to collect information from users to facilitate effective mediation sessions. This form will guide you through the fields you need to complete to ensure a smooth and successful mediation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user input in the **User Input** field (Field 1). This is an open-ended field where you can share your thoughts, concerns, or any relevant information about the mediation session.
2. In the **Mediation Session Details** field (Field 2), briefly describe the details of the mediation session, such as the topic, duration, and any specific requirements.
3. In the **Session Preferences** field (Field 3), specify your preferences for the mediation session, including any special requests or accommodations.
4. In the **Schedule Session** field (Field 4), provide information on the schedule you prefer for the mediation session, such as the date, time, and duration.
5. Finally, confirm your session preferences in the **Confirm Session** field (Field 5). This is an optional field, but it's essential to review and confirm your session details before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (Field 1, Text, Optional): A field for you to share any relevant information or thoughts about the mediation session.
* **Mediation Session Details** (Field 2, Text, Optional): This field is for describing the details of the mediation session, such as topic, duration, and specific requirements.
* **Session Preferences** (Field 3, Text, Optional): Specify your preferences for the mediation session, including any special requests or accommodations.
* **Schedule Session** (Field 4, Text, Optional): Provide information on the schedule you prefer for the mediation session, including date, time, and duration.
* **Confirm Session** (Field 5, Text, Optional): Review and confirm your session details before proceeding.
