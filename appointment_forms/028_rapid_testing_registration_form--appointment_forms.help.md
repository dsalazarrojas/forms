# rapid_testing_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rapid testing registration form is designed to collect patient information and details for a rapid testing process. This form helps the medical staff to gather essential information from patients before conducting testing or scheduling an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Enter the details of the testing event in the "Testing Event Details" section.
4. Schedule your appointment in the "Schedule Appointment" section.
5. If applicable, provide your medical history in the "Medical History" section.
6. List any allergies or medical conditions in the "Allergies" section.
7. Enter the results of your previous tests in the "Test Results" section.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (required): This section is for your personal details. Please fill out your name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (required): Enter your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
* **Testing Event Details** (required): Describe the testing event you are attending, including the date and time.
* **Schedule Appointment** (required): Schedule your appointment by selecting a date and time that works for you.
* **Medical History** (required): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect your testing.
* **Allergies** (optional): List any allergies or medical conditions you have, even if they are not part of your medical history.
* **Test Results** (optional): If you have previous test results, enter them here. This can be helpful for the medical staff to understand your current situation.
* **Additional Comments** (optional): If you have any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the medical staff, please enter them here.
