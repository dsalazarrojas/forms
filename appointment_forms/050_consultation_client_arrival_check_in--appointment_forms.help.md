# Consultation Client Arrival Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients upon arrival to a consultation meeting, ensuring a smooth and successful meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Arrival Identification** (`-- Client Arrival Identification --`): This section is for you to confirm your arrival and provide your identification.
2. **Client Full Name** (`Client Full Name`): Please enter your full name as per your identification.
3. **Arrival Time** (`Arrival Time`): Enter the time you arrived for the meeting.
4. **Scheduled Appointment Time** (`Scheduled Appointment Time`): Confirm the time of your scheduled appointment.
5. **Name of Consultant/ Mentor/Sponsor** (`Name of Consultant you are meeting with`): Identify the name of the consultant or mentor you are meeting with.
6. **Contact Information Verification** (`-- Contact Information Verification --`): This section is for you to confirm your contact information.
7. **Primary Email Address** (`Primary Email Address`): Enter your primary email address.
8. **Primary Phone Number** (`Primary Phone Number`): Enter your primary phone number in the format (555) 555-5555.
9. **Have you had any changes to your address recently?** (`Have you had any changes to your address recently?`): Answer `Yes` if you have had any changes to your address, and provide details in the `Specify Updated Address (If applicable)` field.
10. **Specify Updated Address (If applicable)** (`Specify Updated Address (If applicable)`): If you answered `Yes` to the previous question, provide the updated address here.
11. **Consultation Details and Readiness** (`-- Consultation Details and Readiness --`): This section is for you to confirm the details of your consultation.
12. **Primary Topic for Today's Consultation** (`Primary Topic for Today's Consultation`): Select the primary topic of discussion for today's meeting.
13. **One primary goal for today's meeting** (`One primary goal for today's meeting`): Enter a clear and actionable objective for the meeting.
14. **Arrival Logistics** (`-- Arrival Logistics --`): This section is for you to confirm your arrival status.
15. **Current Check-in Status** (`Current Check-in Status`): Select the status of your arrival.
16. **Additional Arrival Instructions or Special Requests** (`Additional Arrival Instructions or Special Requests`): If you have any additional instructions or requests, enter them here.
17. **Compliance and Authorization** (`-- Compliance and Authorization --`): This section is for you to acknowledge data privacy and GDPR compliance.
18. **Acknowledgment of Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance** (`Acknowledgment of Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance`): Confirm your acknowledgment of data privacy and GDPR regulations.
19. **Authorized Representative Name** (`Authorized Representative Name`): Enter the name of the authorized representative.
20. **Date of Arrival Check-in** (`Date of Arrival Check-in`): Enter the date of your arrival check-in.
21. **Intake Officer Name (Internal Use Only)** (`Intake Officer Name (Internal Use Only)`): This field is for internal use only.
22. **Would you like to receive a meeting summary via email?** (`Would you like to receive a meeting summary via email?`): Select `True` to receive a meeting summary via email.
