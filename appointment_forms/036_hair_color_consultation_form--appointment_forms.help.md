# Hair Color Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Color Consultation Form is a pre-appointment questionnaire designed to collect information from clients about their hair characteristics, preferences, and availability for a hair color consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a successful consultation.
2. Choose the options that best match your hair type, color, condition, texture, porosity, shape, density, length, and face shape.
3. Enter your appointment date and time in the YYYY-MM-DD and 24-hour format, respectively.
4. Confirm that you are available at the scheduled time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hair Type**: Select your natural hair type from the list of options (Blonde, Brown, Black, Red).
* **Hair Color**: Choose your hair color(s) from the list of options (Blonde, Brown, Black, Red).
* **Desired Length**: Select your desired hair length from the list of options (Short, Medium, Long, Extra Long).
* **Hair Condition**: Choose your hair condition from the list of options (Damaged, Healthy, Dry, Oily).
* **Hair Texture**: Select your hair texture from the list of options (Fine, Medium, Coarse, Very Coarse).
* **Hair Porosity**: Choose your hair porosity from the list of options (Porous, Low Porosity, Normal, High Porosity).
* **Hair Shape**: Select your hair shape from the list of options (Oval, Round, Square, Diamond).
* **Hair Density**: Choose your hair density from the list of options (Sparse, Normal, Thick, Very Thick).
* **Face Shape**: Select your face shape from the list of options (Oval, Round, Square, Diamond).
* **Hair Length**: Choose your hair length from the list of options (Short, Medium, Long, Extra Long).
* **Hair Type 2**: Enter any additional information about your hair type (optional).
* **Appointment Date**: Enter your appointment date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter your appointment time in 24-hour format.
* **Appointment Date 2**: Enter your appointment date in YYYY-MM-DD format (optional).
* **Appointment Time 2**: Enter your appointment time in 24-hour format (optional).
* **Appointment Note**: Enter any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Confirm**: Confirm your availability at the scheduled time.
* **Confirm 2**: Confirm your availability at the scheduled time (required).
* **Appointment Note 2**: Enter any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Appointment Time 2**: Enter your appointment time in 24-hour format (optional).
* **Appointment Date 2**: Enter your appointment date in YYYY-MM-DD format (optional).
* **Appointment Form Submitted**: Confirm if the appointment form has been submitted.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the submission date of the appointment form (optional).
