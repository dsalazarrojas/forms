# legislative-advocacy-planning-appointment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document key information related to legislative advocacy planning and appointment scheduling, including contact details, appointment scheduling, and meeting outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the planning_appointment_form page:**
	* Enter the necessary details in the planning_appointment_form field.
2. **Provide contact information:**
	* Enter your contact information in the contact_information field.
3. **Select legislative contacts:**
	* Choose the relevant legislative contacts from the legislative_contacts field.
4. **Add meeting details:**
	* Enter any additional meeting details in the meeting_details field.
5. **Document meeting notes and outcomes:**
	* Enter any meeting notes and outcomes in the meeting_notes and meeting_outcomes fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **planning_appointment_form (Text, required: false)**:
	+ This field is a text input where you can enter any additional details related to the appointment planning.
* **contact_information (Text, required: false)**:
	+ This field is for providing your contact information, which will be used for future reference.
* **date_of_appointment (Date, required: false)**:
	+ This field is for selecting the date of the appointment.
* **legislative_contacts (Select Multiple, required: true)**:
	+ Select the relevant legislative contacts from the list.
* **meeting_details (Text, required: false)**:
	+ Enter any additional details about the meeting, such as location or agenda.
* **meeting_notes (Text, required: false)**:
	+ Record any notes or minutes from the meeting.
* **meeting_outcomes (Text, required: false)**:
	+ Document any notable outcomes or actions items from the meeting.
* **next_steps (Text, required: false)**:
	+ Enter any next steps or follow-up actions from the meeting.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Select all relevant legislative contacts for the meeting.
- Enter detailed meeting notes and outcomes for future reference.
- Use this form to track and document your interactions with government representatives and other stakeholders.
