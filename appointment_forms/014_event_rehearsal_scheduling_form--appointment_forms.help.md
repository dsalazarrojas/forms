# Event Rehearsal Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their event rehearsal schedule and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the event for which you are scheduling your rehearsal.
2. Enter your participant information, including any relevant details about yourself.
3. Specify your schedule details, including your availability and preferences.
4. Indicate your preferred communication method and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, required: false): A brief description of the event you are participating in.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, required: false): Any relevant information about yourself, such as name, job title, or role in the event.
* **Schedule Details** (`Schedule Details`, text, required: false): Your schedule preferences, such as your availability and any specific dates and times you are able to attend.
* **Communication Details** (`Communication Details`, text, required: false): Your preferred method of communication, such as email or phone number, for coordinating with the event organizers.

## Tips
* Please be as concise as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to double-check your event and schedule details for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or needs, be sure to specify them in the relevant fields.
