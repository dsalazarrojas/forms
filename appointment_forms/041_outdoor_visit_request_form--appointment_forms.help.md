# Outdoor Visit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting an outdoor visit to a location. It gathers visitor details, visit specifics (date and time), and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide the visitor details in the "Visitor Details" section.
2. Enter the visit date and time in the "Visit Date" and "Visit Time" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the visitor's contact information, including phone number and email, in the "Contact Info" section.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the visit in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Details**: Enter a brief description of the visit purpose.
* **Visitor Details**: Provide the name and any other relevant information about the person visiting.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the visitor's phone number and email address.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of the visit in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Visit Time**: Enter the visit time in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Visitor Name**: Enter the name of the person visiting.
* **Visitor Phone**: Enter the visitor's phone number.
* **Visitor Email**: Enter the visitor's email address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* If you need to request changes or cancellations, contact the form's creator or administrator.
