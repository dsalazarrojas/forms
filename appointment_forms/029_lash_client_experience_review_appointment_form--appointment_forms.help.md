# lash_client_experience_review_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lash Client Experience Review Appointment Form is used to collect information about a client's experience at a lash appointment. The form is designed to gather details about the client's name, contact information, and feedback on their experience, including the date and time of the appointment, and any recommendations or comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Ensure you have all necessary information**: Make sure you have the client's name, email, phone number, and the date and time of the appointment.
2. **Enter the client's name**: In the "Client Name" field, enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
3. **Enter the client's email**: In the "Email" field, enter the client's email address.
4. **Enter the client's phone number**: In the "Phone" field, enter the client's phone number.
5. **Enter the review date**: In the "Review Date" field, select the date of the appointment using the date picker or enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. **Enter the review time**: In the "Review Time" field, select the time of the appointment using the time picker or enter it in the format hh:mm.
7. **Enter the client's experience**: In the "Experience" field, describe the client's experience in as much detail as possible, including any positive or negative comments they may have.
8. **Optional: Enter recommendations**: If the client has any recommendations or suggestions, enter them in the "Recommendations" field.
9. **Enter the client's review**: In the "Review" field, describe the client's overall experience, including any positive or negative comments they may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Select or enter the date of the appointment using the date picker or in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required): Select or enter the time of the appointment using the time picker or in the format hh:mm.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Describe the client's experience in as much detail as possible.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): If the client has any recommendations or suggestions, enter them here.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required): Describe the client's overall experience, including any positive or negative comments they may have.
