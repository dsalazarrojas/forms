# Appointment Request Form Dark And Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit an appointment request to the organization. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on "Submit" to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required information, making sure to select the correct option if a field is a dropdown or checkbox.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to confirm your appointment.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number with the area code.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Select your desired appointment date.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select your preferred time slot for the appointment (9:00 AM - 10:00 AM, 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM, etc.).
* **Type of Appointment**: Select the type of appointment you need (Consultation, Service, Follow-up, etc.).
* **Brief Description of Service Needed**: If necessary, provide a brief description of what you need help with.
* **Additional Notes or Requests**: If you have any special requirements or preferences, add them here.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about us (Website, Social Media, Referral, etc.).
* **I Agree to the Appointment Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

Please review and submit the form accurately to ensure that your appointment request is processed correctly.
