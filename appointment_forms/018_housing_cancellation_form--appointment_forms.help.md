# Housing Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from tenants who want to cancel their housing agreement. It's used to provide a structured and organized way to communicate the reason for cancellation, type of agreement, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a reason for cancelling in the "What is your reason for cancelling" field.
2. Choose the type of agreement being cancelled from the "Type of agreement" select menu.
3. Enter your contact details, including phone number, email, address, zip code, and city.
4. Enter the cancellation request date and contact date in the respective date fields.
5. Enter the notice period in days.
6. Add any additional information or notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your reason for cancelling**: This field is for you to enter a brief description of why you are cancelling the housing agreement.
* **Type of agreement**: Select the type of agreement that you are cancelling, such as Apartment or Lease.
* **contact_details**: This field is for you to enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_email**: This field is for you to enter your email address.
* **notice_address**: This field is for you to enter your address.
* **zip**: This field is for you to enter your zip code.
* **city**: This field is for you to enter your city.
* **notice_period**: This field is for you to enter the number of days you have given for the notice period.
* **notes**: This field is for you to add any additional information or notes about your cancellation.
* **contact_date**: This field is for you to enter the contact date.
* **other**: This field is for you to enter any other relevant information.
