# Aviation Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule an appointment for an aviation-related service. Please review each field carefully and select the most accurate options to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of aviation service you need from the "Service Type" field.
2.  Choose the category that best describes you from the "Customer Type" field.
3.  Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in their respective fields.
4.  If you have a pilot certificate, choose the level of your certification. If not applicable, select "Not applicable."
5.  If you have a specific pilot certificate number, enter it in the "Pilot Certificate Number" field.
6.  Choose the type of aircraft you are interested in from the "Aircraft Type of Interest" field.
7.  If you own or rent a specific aircraft, enter its tail number in the "Aircraft Tail Number" field.
8.  Select your preferred appointment date and time from the "Preferred Appointment Date" and "Preferred Appointment Time" fields.
9.  Choose your expected duration for the appointment from the "Expected Duration" field.
10. If you have a preference for an instructor, select it from the "Instructor Preference" field.
11. If you have a specific instructor name preference, enter it in the "Requested Instructor Name" field.
12. Enter the number of passengers (including yourself) for the appointment.
13. If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them in the "Special Requirements" field.
14. Indicate if you are a returning customer from the "Have You Used Our Services Before" field.
15. Select how you heard about us from the "How Did You Hear About Us" field.
16. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" field.
17. Enter any additional notes or information for your appointment in the "Additional Notes" field.
18. Select how you would like to be confirmed for the appointment from the "Confirmation Preference" field.
19. Finally, acknowledge the cancellation policy from the "I Understand the Cancellation Policy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of aviation service you need, such as Flight training lesson, Aircraft rental, etc.

### Customer Type
* **Customer Type** (`Customer Type`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes you, such as Student pilot, Licensed pilot, etc.

### Full Name
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your complete name for identification purposes.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for appointment confirmation.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.

### Pilot Certificate Level
* **Pilot Certificate Level** (`Pilot Certificate Level`, select_one, required): Choose your current pilot certification level, or select "Not applicable" if you do not have a pilot license.

### Pilot Certificate Number
* **Pilot Certificate Number** (`Pilot Certificate Number`, text, required): Enter your pilot certificate number if you have one.

### Aircraft Type of Interest
* **Aircraft Type of Interest** (`Aircraft Type of Interest`, select_one, required): Choose the type of aircraft you are interested in, such as Single-engine piston, etc.

### Aircraft Tail Number
* **Aircraft Tail Number** (`Aircraft Tail Number`, text, required): Enter the tail number of your specific aircraft if applicable.

### Preferred Appointment Date
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`Preferred Appointment Date`, date, required): Select your first-choice appointment date.

### Preferred Appointment Time
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`Preferred Appointment Time`, time, required): Select your first-choice appointment time.

### Alternate Date
* **Alternate Date** (`Alternate Date`, date, required): Select your second-choice appointment date.

### Alternate Time
* **Alternate Time** (`Alternate Time`, time, required): Select your second-choice appointment time.

### Expected Duration
* **Expected Duration** (`Expected Duration`, select_one, required): Choose the expected length of your appointment, such as 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.

### Instructor Preference
* **Instructor Preference** (`Instructor Preference`, select_one, required): Select your preference for an instructor, such as Senior instructor, etc.

### Requested Instructor Name
* **Requested Instructor Name** (`Requested Instructor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your preferred instructor if you have one.

### Number of Passengers
* **Number of Passengers** (`Number of Passengers`, number, required): Enter the number of passengers (including yourself) for the appointment.

### Special Requirements
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, text, required): Enter any special requirements or requests you have.

### Have You Used Our Services Before
* **Have You Used Our Services Before** (`Have You Used Our Services Before`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are a returning customer.

### How Did You Hear About Us
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`How Did You Hear About Us`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about us.

### Preferred Payment Method
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`Preferred Payment Method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method, such as Credit card, etc.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, required): Enter any additional information for your appointment.

### Confirmation Preference
* **Confirmation Preference** (`Confirmation Preference`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be confirmed for your appointment.

### I Understand the Cancellation Policy
* **I Understand the Cancellation Policy** (`I Understand the Cancellation Policy`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the cancellation policy.
