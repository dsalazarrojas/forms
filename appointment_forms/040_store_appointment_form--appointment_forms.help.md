# Store Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Appointment Form is designed to collect information from customers about their appointment schedule and preferences. This form is used to store and organize appointments at the store, ensuring that staff are well-prepared to handle customer requests. The form gathers data on customer details, appointment dates and times, and any additional notes that may be relevant to the customer's visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Store Appointment Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **customer name** in the designated field. This will help us identify the customer and their appointment details.
2. Select the **appointment date** from the calendar provided. Choose the date and time that suits the customer best.
3. Choose the preferred **appointment time** from the select options. Please select one of the available times from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the customer's **phone number** for easier contact.
5. Enter the store's **phone number** for staff reference.
6. If applicable, enter the customer's **email address** for communication.
7. Write any additional **customer notes** for the staff to be aware of any special requirements or requests.
8. Select the preferred **appointment date and time** from the list of options provided.
9. If the selected date and time is not available, you can enter a custom **alternative date and time** in the provided text field.
10. Enter any additional **customer notes** that may be relevant to the customer's visit.
11. Enter any additional **store notes** for staff reference.
12. Click the **submit** button to save the form data.
13. If you need to cancel the appointment, click the **cancel** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date of the appointment from the calendar. This helps us schedule the appointment accordingly.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose one of the available times from the dropdown menu for the appointment time.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for easy contact.
* **Store Phone**: Enter the store's phone number for staff reference.
* **Customer Email**: If applicable, enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes about the customer's visit, if needed.
* **Selected Date and Time**: Select the preferred date and time for the appointment from the provided options.
* **Selected Date and Time Other**: If the selected date and time is not available, enter an alternative date and time in this text field.
* **Customer Note 2**: Enter any additional customer-specific notes.
* **Store Note**: Enter any additional notes for staff reference.
* **Submit**: Click this button to save the form data and store the appointment.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to cancel the appointment.
