# Halal Investment Consultation Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a halal investment consultation with a consultant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Select the areas of interest you would like to discuss with the consultant in the "Interests" field.
3. Choose a preferred time slot (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) in the "Preferred Time" field.
4. If needed, select a specific time and date for the consultation in the "Time" and "Date" fields.
5. Choose a contact method (Phone, Email, or In Person) in the "Contact Method" field.
6. Enter your client's contact number and email in the "Contact Number" and "Email" fields.
7. Add any additional information or comments in the "Additional Info" field.
8. Enter any additional consultant details in the "Consultant Details" field.
9. Leave comments for the client and consultant in the "Client Comments" and "Consultant Comments" fields.
10. Select the assigned tool and user for the consultation in the "Assigned Tool" and "Assigned User" fields.
11. Set the form status to "In Progress", "Completed", or "Cancelled" in the "Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Enter your client's name and any other relevant information.
* **Interests**: Select all the areas of halal investment you would like to discuss with the consultant.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time slot (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) for the consultation.
* **Time**: If needed, select a specific time for the consultation. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Date**: If needed, select a specific date for the consultation. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Contact Method**: Choose a contact method (Phone, Email, or In Person) for the consultant to contact you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your client's contact number.
* **Email**: Enter your client's email.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information you would like to share with the consultant.
* **Consultant Details**: Enter any additional consultant details.
* **Client Comments**: Leave comments for the client.
* **Consultant Comments**: Leave comments for the consultant.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool to be used for the consultation (chatjimmy or other).
* **Assigned User**: Select whether a user is assigned for the consultation (Yes or No).
* **Status**: Set the form status to "In Progress", "Completed", or "Cancelled".

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
