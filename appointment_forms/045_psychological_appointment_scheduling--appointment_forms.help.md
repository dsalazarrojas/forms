# Psychological Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals and patients schedule appointments for psychological evaluations and sessions. It collects information about the patient's name, contact information, appointment date and time, and other relevant details such as the reason for the visit and preferred schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
*   Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
*   Provide your email address in the "email" field.
*   Choose the appointment date in the "date" field.
*   Select the appointment time in the "time" field.
*   Briefly describe the reason for your visit in the "reason for visit" field.
*   If you have a specific time in mind, you can select it in the "preferred time" field.
*   If the selected time slot is available, confirm it by selecting "Yes" in the "selected time" field.
*   If there are multiple time slots available, you can select multiple options by confirming "Yes" for each desired slot in the "selected time 2" field.
*   Leave any additional comments or information about your appointment in the "note" field.
*   If you have a preferred date for your appointment, select it in the "preferred date" field.
*   Finally, confirm your preferred date by selecting "Yes" in the "selected date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name**: Enter your given name, as you prefer to be addressed.
*   **Last name**: Enter your surname or family name.
*   **Email**: Provide the address you use to receive email communication.
*   **Date**: Choose the date for your appointment.
*   **Time**: Select the time for your appointment.
*   **Reason for visit**: Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
*   **Preferred time**: If you have a specific time in mind, select it.
*   **Selected time**: Confirm the time slot you prefer.
*   **Selected time 2**: Confirm multiple time slots if available.
*   **Note**: Leave any comments or information about your appointment.
*   **Preferred date**: If you have a preferred date, select it.
*   **Selected date**: Confirm your preferred date.
