# Repair And Reuse Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather registration information for a repair and reuse event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the registration information (if required).
2. Select the service center from the provided options.
3. Enter the event date and time.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your registration.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on your registration.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your registration.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your registration.
* **Service Center** (`service_center`, select_one, optional): Select the service center where you will be attending the event.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, date, optional): Enter the event information (e.g., event name, description).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
