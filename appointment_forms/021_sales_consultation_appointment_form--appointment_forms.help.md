# Sales Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a sales consultation with a client. It helps in collecting necessary details about the client and the appointment, ensuring a smooth and effective consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the client's information in the `Client Information` field.
2. Select the desired date for the consultation in the `Appointment Date` field.
3. Choose the time for the appointment in the `Appointment Time` field.
4. Indicate the topic of the consultation in the `Consultation Topic` field.
5. If required, select the preferred language for the consultation in the `Preferred Language` field.
6. Enter the client's email address in the `Email` field.
7. Provide the client's phone number in the `Phone` field.
8. Add any relevant notes about the client or the consultation in the `Notes` field.
9. If applicable, select the assigned salesperson for the consultation in the `Assigned Salesperson` field.
10. If applicable, choose the tool to be used during the consultation in the `Assigned Tool` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name and any other relevant information.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date for the consultation. This can be a specific date or a recurring date.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose the time slot for the consultation.
* **Consultation Topic**: Choose the topic of the consultation from the provided options (Sales, Marketing, Product, Service).
* **Preferred Language**: Select the language you would like to use during the consultation. This can be English, French, Spanish, Mandarin, or Arabic.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address. This is optional but helps in contacting the client before or after the consultation.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number. This is optional but helps in reaching the client before or after the consultation.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the client or the consultation that might be helpful to the assigned salesperson.
* **Assigned Salesperson**: If you know who will be handling the consultation, select their name from the provided options.
* **Assigned Tool**: If a specific tool is required for the consultation, choose the one from the provided options.

Note: The `Other` option is provided for scenarios where none of the listed options apply, or if you need to assign someone else.
