# Hotel Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Appointment Form is designed to collect information from guests for hotel appointments. It's a simple, easy-to-use form with no authentication or validation requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed below. If you encounter any issues, you can ask for help at <form@gic.mx>.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Appoinment Form** (`base_info`, text, not required): Enter the name of your hotel appointment form, e.g., "Wedding Reception".
* **Guest Details** (`guest_info`, text, not required): Enter the guest's name, contact number, and any other relevant information.
* **Room Details** (`room_info`, text, not required): Specify the room type, number of guests, and any special requests.
* **Reservation Info** (`reservation_info`, text, not required): Enter the reservation dates, check-in time, and any special instructions.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_info`, text, not required): Enter the payment method, amount, and any additional payment instructions.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information that may be useful for your hotel appointment.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, not required): Review and agree to the hotel's terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Choose whether to submit the form or go back.

Note: Fields marked as not required can be left empty if not applicable. 

**Tips**: Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your hotel appointment is processed correctly. If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
