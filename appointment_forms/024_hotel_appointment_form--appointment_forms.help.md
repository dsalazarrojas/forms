## Purpose
The Hotel Appointment Form is designed to collect information from guests for hotel appointments. It's a simple, easy-to-use form with no authentication or validation requirements.

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed below. If you encounter any issues, you can ask for help at <form@gic.mx>.

* **Hotel Appoinment Form** (`base_info`, text, not required): Enter the name of your hotel appointment form, e.g., "Wedding Reception".
* **Guest Details** (`guest_info`, text, not required): Enter the guest's name, contact number, and any other relevant information.
* **Room Details** (`room_info`, text, not required): Specify the room type, number of guests, and any special requests.
* **Reservation Info** (`reservation_info`, text, not required): Enter the reservation dates, check-in time, and any special instructions.
