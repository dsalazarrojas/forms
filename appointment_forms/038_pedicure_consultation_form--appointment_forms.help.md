# Pedicure Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about a client's nail health and preferences before a pedicure service is performed. It helps the nail technician understand the client's needs and provide a customized service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Choose the relevant services you are interested in receiving from the options provided.
3. Provide a brief description of your nail problems and any additional information relevant to your visit.
4. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)**: Please fill in your name, contact details, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Skin Conditions (2)**: Describe any skin conditions or allergies you have that may affect your pedicure experience.
* **Nail Conditions (3)**: List any nail issues or concerns you have, such as fungal infections, ingrown nails, or brittle nails.
* **Nail History (4)**: Provide a brief history of your nail care, including any previous pedicures or nail-related issues.
* **Medical History (5)**: Share any relevant medical information, such as medications or health conditions that may impact your pedicure experience.
* **Medication (6)**: List any medications you are currently taking that may affect your pedicure experience.
* **Allergies (7)**: Describe any allergies or sensitivities you have that may impact your pedicure experience.
* **Nail Services (8)**: Select the services you are interested in receiving, such as acrylic, gel, or polish applications.
* **Nail Problems (9)**: Describe any nail issues or concerns you have, such as pain, discomfort, or previous problems.
* **Foot Care (10)**: Share any foot care practices or concerns you have.
* **Nail Care (11)**: Provide any nail care advice or routines you follow.
* **Nail Growth (12)**: Measure and record the length of your nails.
* **Nail Length (13)**: Record the length of your nails in millimeters.
* **Nail Color (14)**: Choose the desired nail color from the provided options.
* **Appointment Time (15)**: Record the appointment time for your pedicure session.
* **Appointment Date (16)**: Record the appointment date for your pedicure session.
* **Notes (17)**: Provide any additional information or notes relevant to your pedicure experience.
* **Follow Up (18)**: Provide any follow-up information or contact details for future pedicure sessions.
* **Client Signature (19)**: Sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have provided accurate information.
* **Technician Signature (20)**: Sign and date this form to confirm that you have received the pedicure consultation form and understand the information provided.
* **Pedicure Consultation Form (21)**: This field is for internal use only.
* **Nail Condition (22)**: Describe the current condition of your nails.
* **Nail Problems Description (23)**: Describe any nail issues or concerns you have.
* **Nail Care Advice (24)**: Provide any nail care advice or routines you follow.
* **Additional Info (25)**: Provide any additional information relevant to your pedicure experience.

Note: Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the best pedicure experience. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your nail technician for clarification.
