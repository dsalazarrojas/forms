# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose

The appointment form is used to schedule appointments for home organization services. This form is designed to collect personal details, service requests, and follow-up information to ensure that the user's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required personal details: first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Specify the type of service needed: choose from organizing, cleaning, or packing.
3. Choose the available dates and times for the appointment.
4. Briefly describe the service requested.
5. Choose whether follow-up is required: yes, no, or other.
6. Choose the follow-up date and time.
7. Fill in any other required fields as needed.
