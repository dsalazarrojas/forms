# Skincare Workshop Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information for planning a skincare workshop. It's intended for clients who want to book an appointment at a skincare workshop. The form will help us plan and prepare for your visit, ensuring we can tailor the experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Client Details Form" section.
2. Provide information about the workshop you're interested in, including date and time preferences, in the "Workshop Details" section.
3. Enter payment details for your appointment in the "Payment Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details Form**
	+ **Client Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Please fill in your name.
	+ **Contact Number** (`<name>`, text, required): Please enter your contact number.
* **Workshop Details**
	+ **Workshop Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Please select the workshop you're interested in attending.
	+ **Date and Time** (`<name>`, text, required): Choose a date and time for your appointment.
* **Payment Details**
	+ **Payment Method** (`<name>`, text, required): Please select the payment method you'd like to use (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
	+ **Amount** (`<name>`, text, required): Enter the amount you're willing to pay.
