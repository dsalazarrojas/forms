# seo_strategy_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to provide information about their business and schedule appointments for search engine optimization (SEO) services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the `Client Info` field with your business name, description or any other relevant details.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Chat, Text) in the `Preferred Contact Method` field.
3. Choose the time you prefer to be contacted in the `Preferred Time` field.
4. Select the SEO services you require from the list in the `SEO Service Request` field.
5. Enter your business name in the `Business Name` field.
6. Enter your business type in the `Business Type` field.
7. Enter your business URL in the `Business URL` field.
8. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
9. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
10. Leave a note for the service provider in the `Note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info**: Enter a brief description of your business, including any relevant details. 
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Chat, Text).
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time that works best for you to be contacted.
* **SEO Service Request**: Select the SEO services you require from the list provided. 
* **Business Name**: Enter your business name.
* **Business Type**: Enter a brief description of your business type.
* **Business URL**: Enter your business URL.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: Leave a note for the service provider.
