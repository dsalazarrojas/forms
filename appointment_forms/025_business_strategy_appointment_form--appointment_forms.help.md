# Business Strategy Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Strategy Appointment Form is designed to gather information from clients before scheduling an appointment or meeting to discuss business strategy. This form ensures that their needs are met and that the session is tailored to their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to fill out this form on behalf of your company.
3. Submit the form to schedule an appointment or meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the client.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client's company.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Title or Position** (`client_title`, text, optional): Enter the client's title or position in the company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): Enter the client's industry sector.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in the client's company.
* **Years in Business** (`years_operating`, number, optional): Enter the number of years the company has been operating.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the preferred time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Format** (`appointment_format`, select_one, required): Select the preferred format for the appointment (in-person, phone call, video conference, or hybrid).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the desired duration of the session.
* **Strategy Focus Areas** (`strategy_focus`, select_multiple, required): Select the areas you want to focus on during the session (market expansion, product development, operational efficiency, financial planning, organizational structure, digital transformation, sales strategy, or other).
* **Current Business Situation** (`current_situation`, text, required): Provide a brief overview of your current business situation.
* **Short-term Business Goals** (`business_goals`, text, required): Enter the short-term business goals you are targeting.
* **Key Challenges** (`challenges_faced`, text, required): Identify the key challenges you are facing.
* **Anticipated Outcomes** (`anticipated_outcomes`, text, optional): Enter the anticipated outcomes from this session.
* **Supporting Documents to Provide** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Check if you will be bringing any documents or files to support your meeting.
* **Other Attendees** (`other_attendees`, text, optional): Enter the names of other attendees if any.
* **Appointment Request Date** (`booking_date`, date, required): Enter the date this request was submitted.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but providing accurate and complete information is recommended to ensure a productive session.
