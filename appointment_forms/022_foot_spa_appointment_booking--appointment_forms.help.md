# Foot Spa Appointment Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foot Spa Appointment Booking form is used to book appointments for clients at a foot spa. It is a crucial tool for spa staff and administrators to gather necessary information to manage appointments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Select a Spa" option, ensuring that you choose the correct spa location where the appointment is to be booked.
2. Pick a date for the appointment using the "Pick a Date" field.
3. Choose a treatment type by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Choose a Treatment" field.
4. Provide client details in the "Client Details" field, which is a text area for additional notes.
5. Enter the client's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Input the client's email address in the "Email Address" field.
7. Select the required spa preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Spa Preferences" field.
8. Choose a time for the appointment using the "Pick a Time" field.
9. Provide a booking message in the "Booking Message" field, which is optional.
10. Enter any special instructions in the "Special Instructions" field, which is optional.
11. Confirm the appointment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Appointment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select a Spa**: This field allows you to choose the specific spa location where the appointment is to be booked.
- **Pick a Date**: Choose a date for the appointment from the available dates.
- **Choose a Treatment**: Select "Yes" if the client requires a specific treatment, or "No" if not required.
- **Client Details**: Enter any additional information about the client in this text area.
- **Contact Number**: Enter the client's contact number for future reference.
- **Email Address**: Input the client's email address for communication purposes.
- **Spa Preferences**: Select any required spa preferences for the appointment.
- **Pick a Time**: Choose a time for the appointment.
- **Booking Message**: Provide any special message for the booking.
- **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the appointment.
- **Confirm Appointment**: Confirm that the appointment has been booked successfully by selecting "Yes" or "No".
