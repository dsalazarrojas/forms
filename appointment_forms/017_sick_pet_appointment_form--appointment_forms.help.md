# sick_pet_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sick_pet_appointment_form" is used to book an appointment with a veterinarian for a pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your pet's information in the "pet_info" field.
2. Provide your information as the pet owner in the "owner_info" field.
3. Choose whether you want an appointment during "appointment_time" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Select the "appointment_date" for the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_info** (Text, Optional): Enter your pet's name, species, age, and any relevant health information.
* **owner_info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, contact number, and any relevant medical history.
* **appointment_time** (Select multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" if you are available for an appointment during the chosen time, and "No" if not.
* **appointment_date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you would like to have the appointment.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help the veterinarian provide the best care for your pet.
* If you select "Yes" for "appointment_time", it means you are available for an appointment during the chosen time.
