# Counseling Appointment Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Appointment Communication Form is used to collect and document information for counseling appointments, ensuring clear communication and efficient scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
2. Select your preferred language.
3. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
4. Specify the reason for your visit.
5. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or SMS).
6. Add any additional comments.
7. If applicable, indicate your insurance status and appointment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Language** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Date** (Date, Required): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Reason for Visit** (Text, Required): Specify the reason for your visit.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or SMS).
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Insurance Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate your insurance status (True, False, or Pending).
* **Appointment Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate the status of your appointment (Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled).
