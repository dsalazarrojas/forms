# Financial Aid Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Aid Appointment Form is designed to gather information for the creation and management of appointments. This form helps to schedule appointments efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review each field carefully before proceeding.
- If you are unsure about any field, please review the field's label and hint for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Appointment** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of appointment you wish to schedule.
* **Appointment Date** (`<name>`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the scheduled appointment.
* **Contact Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`<name>`, `email`, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Additional Information** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about the appointment.
* **Appointment Notes** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter any notes for the scheduled appointment.
* **Financial Aid Program** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select the financial aid program that the appointment is for.
* **Support Agent** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select whether a support agent will be present during the appointment.
* **Appointment Requester** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): If the appointment was requested by someone other than the contact person, enter their name.
* **Support Agent Phone** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the support agent's phone number.
* **Support Agent Email** (`<name>`, `email`, optional): Enter the support agent's email address.
* **Follow-up Reminder** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want a follow-up reminder for the appointment.
* **Follow-up Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the follow-up reminder.
* **Next Appointment Request** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want to schedule another appointment.
* **Next Appointment Requester** (`<name>`, `text`, required): If you want to schedule another appointment, enter the name of the requester.
* **Follow-up Note** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter any notes for the follow-up reminder.
* **Schedule Follow-up** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want to schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Method** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select the method for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Location** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select the location for the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Status** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Comment** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter any comments for the follow-up appointment.
* **Support Agent Note** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter any notes for the support agent.
* **Follow-up Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the follow-up appointment.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form is being filled out by a user who has the necessary permissions to access it. If you are unsure about any field or the form's purpose, please review the form's fields and instructions carefully.
