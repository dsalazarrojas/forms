# Book Your Digital Marketing Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business needs and preferences for a digital marketing consultation. Please fill in your information accurately to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
- Provide a valid email address in the "Email Address" field. This is where we will send the consultation details.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
- Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
- If your company has a website, enter the URL in the "Company Website" field (optional).
- Select the type of industry your business falls under in the "Industry Type" field.
- Describe your current marketing challenges in the "What are your main marketing challenges" field.
- Select the marketing goals you are trying to achieve in the "What are your marketing goals" field. You can choose multiple options.
- If you have a marketing budget, select the range that best applies to you in the "Current Marketing Budget" field.
- If you have a marketing team, select the size of your team in the "Marketing Team Size" field (optional).
- Choose a preferred consultation date and time in the "Preferred Consultation Date" and "Preferred Time Slot" fields.
- Select the format of your preferred consultation in the "Preferred Consultation Format" field.
- If you have any additional information you'd like to share, enter it in the "Additional Information" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, `text`, required): This field is where you should enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, `text`, required): This field is where you should enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): This is the email address where we will send the consultation details.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): This is your best contact number for the consultation.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): This is your business name.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of industry your business falls under.
* **What are your main marketing challenges** (`marketing_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe your current marketing situation.
* **What are your marketing goals** (`marketing_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the marketing goals you are trying to achieve.
* **Current Marketing Budget** (`current_budget`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a budget, select the range that best applies to you.
* **Marketing Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a marketing team, select the size of your team.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`preferred_consultation_time`, `date`, required): Choose a date and time for your consultation.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, `select_one`, required): Select a time slot for your consultation.
* **Preferred Consultation Format** (`consultation_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose a format for your consultation.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information, enter it here.
