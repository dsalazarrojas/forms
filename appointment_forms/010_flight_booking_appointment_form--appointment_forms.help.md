# Flight Booking Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a flight booking appointment. It collects details about the customer's contact information, flight schedules, and passenger information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your customer email address in the **Customer Email** field.
2. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter the flight number for your booking in the **Flight Number** field.
6. Select the travel date for your booking from the calendar in the **Travel Date** field.
7. Select the travel time for your booking from the clock in the **Travel Time** field.
8. Enter the flight duration for your booking in the **Flight Duration** field.
9. Select the class of your flight booking (e.g., Economy, Business, First) in the **Flight Class** field.
10. Enter the name of the passenger(s) for your booking in the **Passenger Name** field.
11. Enter the email address of the passenger(s) for your booking in the **Passenger Email** field (if applicable).
12. Enter the phone number of the passenger(s) for your booking in the **Passenger Phone** field (if applicable).
13. Repeat the steps above for each additional passenger by filling out the fields with the corresponding labels (e.g., **Flight Number 2**, **Travel Date 2**, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, **Required:** No): Enter your customer email address.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, **Required:** No): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, **Required:** No): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, **Required:** No): Enter your phone number.
* **Flight Number** (Flight Number, number, **Required:** No): Enter the flight number for your booking.
* **Travel Date** (Travel Date, date, **Required:** No): Select the travel date for your booking.
* **Travel Time** (Travel Time, time, **Required:** No): Select the travel time for your booking.
* **Flight Duration** (Flight Duration, number, **Required:** No): Enter the flight duration for your booking.
* **Flight Class** (Flight Class, select one, **Required:** No): Select the class of your flight booking (e.g., Economy, Business, First).
* **Passenger Name** (Passenger Name, text, **Required:** No): Enter the name of the passenger for your booking.
* **Passenger Email** (Passenger Email, email, **Required:** No): Enter the email address of the passenger for your booking.
* **Passenger Phone** (Passenger Phone, text, **Required:** No): Enter the phone number of the passenger for your booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for each passenger, as this will aid in processing your flight booking.
* Ensure that each passenger has a unique email address and phone number, if applicable.
* If you have any issues or questions about this form, please contact our support team for assistance.
