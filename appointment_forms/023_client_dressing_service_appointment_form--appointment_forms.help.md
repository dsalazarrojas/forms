# Client Dressing Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Dressing Service Appointment Form is designed to help our clients provide their information and preferences for a personal styling service appointment. This form gathers necessary details to provide tailored styling services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Provide your first and last name
*   Enter your email address as your primary contact information
*   Enter your phone number as your best contact number
*   Fill in your complete mailing address if desired
*   Select the type of service you need (e.g., Personal Styling, Wardrobe Consultation)
*   Choose a preferred appointment date and time
*   Optionally, provide alternative dates and times
*   Choose a location for the appointment (e.g., at our studio, at your home, virtual consultation)
*   Select the occasion for styling (e.g., everyday wear, business professional, wedding)
*   Describe your style preferences, typical sizes, and favorite colors
*   Provide a budget range for any purchases involved
*   List any preferred brands or stores
*   List any brands or stores you would like to avoid
*   Describe any body type or fit concerns
*   Describe your lifestyle and activities
*   Specify your styling goals
*   Confirm previous experience with a stylist
*   Confirm understanding of the cancellation policy
*   Agree to the service terms and conditions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Text): Enter your legal first name.
*   **Last Name** (Text): Enter your legal last name.
*   **Email Address** (Email): Enter your primary contact email.
*   **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your best contact number.
*   **Street Address** (Text, optional): Enter your complete mailing address.
*   **City** (Text, optional): Enter your city.
*   **State** (Text, optional): Enter your state.
*   **ZIP Code** (Text, optional): Enter your ZIP code.
*   **Dressing Service Type** (Select One): Choose the type of service you need (e.g., Personal Styling, Wardrobe Consultation).
*   **Appointment Date** (Date): Choose your preferred appointment date.
*   **Appointment Time** (Time): Choose your preferred appointment time.
*   **Alternative Date** (Date, optional): Optionally, choose an alternative date.
*   **Appointment Location** (Select One): Choose where the appointment should take place (e.g., at our studio, at your home, virtual consultation).
*   **Occasion for Styling** (Select One): Choose the occasion for styling (e.g., everyday wear, business professional, wedding).
*   **Style Preferences** (Text, optional): Describe your style preferences.
*   **Clothing Size Information** (Text, optional): Enter your typical sizes.
*   **Color Preferences** (Text, optional): Enter your favorite colors or colors to avoid.
*   **Budget Range for Purchases** (Select One, optional): Choose a budget range for any purchases involved.
*   **Preferred Brands or Stores** (Text, optional): List any brands or stores you prefer.
*   **Brands or Stores to Avoid** (Text, optional): List any brands or stores you would like to avoid.
*   **Body Type or Fit Concerns** (Text, optional): Describe any body type or fit concerns.
*   **Lifestyle Description** (Text): Describe your lifestyle and activities.
*   **Styling Goals** (Text): Specify your styling goals.
*   **Previous Styling Experience** (Select One): Confirm previous experience with a stylist (e.g., yes, frequently, yes, once or twice, no, never).
*   **Cancellation Policy** (Select One): Confirm understanding of the cancellation policy (24-hour notice required).
*   **Service Agreement** (Select One): Confirm agreement to the service terms and conditions.
*   **Request Submission Date** (Date): This field is auto-populated based on the date of submission.
