# School Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and organize school tours for students, collecting necessary information from students and parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Provide accurate and relevant information in all required fields.
2. **Select the correct tour type**: Choose the type of tour that suits your needs from the options provided.
3. **Enter student and parent information**: Provide your student's name, email, and parent's name and email if applicable.
4. **Specify tour details**: Enter the date and start time of the tour, as well as the tour duration.
5. **Assign a facilitator**: Enter the name and contact information of the tour facilitator.
6. **Add school and appointment details**: Enter the school ID and appointment ID if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student for whom the tour is scheduled.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Contact**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian for communication purposes.
* **Tour Date**: Enter the date of the tour in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Tour Start Time**: Enter the start time of the tour in the format HH:MM.
* **Tour Duration**: Enter the duration of the tour in minutes.
* **Tour Facilitator**: Enter the name of the person leading the tour.
* **Tour Facilitator Email**: Enter the contact email of the tour facilitator.
* **Tour Facilitator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the tour facilitator.
* **School ID**: Enter the ID of the school associated with the tour.
* **Appointment ID**: Enter the ID of the appointment scheduled.
* **Tour Type**: Select the type of tour from the options provided (School Tour, Group Tour, Private Tour).
* **Student Grade**: Select the grade level of the student from the options provided.
* **Student Subject**: Enter the subject of interest for the tour, if applicable.

**Tips**:
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the tour facilitator or school administration for assistance.
