# Beauty Team Expansion Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from potential candidates for the beauty team expansion.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
2.  Be sure to select your preferred interview format and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (2): Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
*   **Email Address** (3): Enter your professional email address to ensure we can contact you about the interview and any further communication.
*   **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact phone number for any urgent matters related to the interview.
*   **Position of Interest** (6): Choose the position you are interested in applying for. Select from the following options:
    *   Senior Stylist
    *   Junior Stylist
    *   Salon Assistant
    *   Esthetician
    *   Nail Artist
    *   Salon Coordinator
*   **Years of Experience** (7): Please enter the number of years you have worked in the beauty industry.
*   **Current Employment Status** (8): Select your current employment status:
    *   Currently Employed
    *   Self-Employed / Booth Renter
    *   Not Currently Working
    *   Student / Recent Graduate
*   **Preferred Interview Date** (10): Choose a date you are available for an interview.
*   **Preferred Time Range** (11): Select a time range for your interview:
    *   Early Morning (8AM - 10AM)
    *   Late Morning (10AM - 12PM)
    *   Early Afternoon (12PM - 2PM)
    *   Late Afternoon (2PM - 5PM)
*   **Preferred Interview Format** (12): Choose your preferred format for the interview:
    *   In-Person (at Salon)
    *   Video Call (Google Meet/Zoom)
    *   Phone Interview
*   **Link to Portfolio or Work Samples** (13): If you have a link to your portfolio or work samples, enter it here (optional).
*   **I Consent** (15): You must agree to your data being processed for recruitment purposes.
*   **Date of Submission** (16): Enter the date you submitted this application form.

By following these steps and providing accurate information, you will be able to submit a complete and successful application for the beauty team expansion.
