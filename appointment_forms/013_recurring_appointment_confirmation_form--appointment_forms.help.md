# Recurring Appointment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recurring Appointment Confirmation Form is a tool used to record and confirm details of recurring appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Review and fill in the appointment details, providing as much information as possible about the appointment.
2. Choose the scheduled time for the appointment from the provided options (e.g., '1', '2', or '3').
3. Enter notes on the appointment (if necessary).
4. Add any relevant contact information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recurring Appointment Confirmation Form** (`recurring_appointment_confirmation_form`, text, not required): This field is the primary title of the form, which confirms the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of the appointment, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Appointment Schedule** (`appointment_schedule`, select_one, not required): Choose the scheduled time for the appointment from the options provided ('1', '2', or '3').
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, text, not required): Enter details about the next scheduled appointment, including the date, time, and location.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter any relevant contact information related to the appointment, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all relevant details are recorded.
- Review the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out before submission.
- Consider adding a clear subject line or reference for the appointment to make it easier to identify and manage recurring appointments.
