# cosmetic_surgery_appointment_checklist_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for cosmetic surgery appointments. It is intended to be used by patients to provide their medical history, contact details, and other relevant information for their surgeon. The form should be completed by the patient to ensure that their information is accurately recorded and that they receive the best possible care during their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the appropriate options for each field as instructed by your surgeon or clinic. 
2.  Make sure to answer each field truthfully and accurately.
3.  Enter your information in the text fields and date field as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cosmetic Surgery Appointment Checklist (Select 2):** 
    - Select the specific services you would like to undergo during your appointment, such as facelift, breast augmentation, or eyelid surgery.
* **Patient Information (Select 1):** 
    - Choose the best description of the person who is making the appointment. 
* **Medical History (Text):** 
    - Enter any relevant medical history, such as allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries. 
* **Surgical Plan (Date):** 
    - Enter the date of the planned surgery. 
* **Contact Details (Note):** 
    - Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses. 
* **Patient Information 2 (Text):** 
    - Enter any additional information about yourself, such as your address or emergency contact. 

## Tips

- Please answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your surgeon or clinic for guidance.
- Make sure to review your completed form before submitting it to your surgeon or clinic. 
- If you have any medical conditions that may be relevant to your appointment, be sure to mention it in the medical history field.
