# office_visit_scheduling_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office visit scheduling form is designed to collect information from visitors to your office. Completing this form helps ensure a smooth and organized visit process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and correctly to ensure accuracy.
2. Ensure that the visitor information is complete and up-to-date.
3. Fill out the visit date and time fields to schedule the visit.
4. Choose the correct visit type to specify whether the visitor is a new or returning patient.
5. Optionally, fill out the visit reason field if necessary.
6. Leave the staff information field empty if not needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Information (Visitor Information, text, optional)**: Enter the visitor's contact information, including their name and email address. This will help us prepare for their visit.
* **Visit Date (Visit Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the visit. This field is required to schedule the visit.
* **Visit Time (Visit Time, time, required)**: Enter the time of the visit. This field is also required to schedule the visit.
* **Visitor Name (Visitor Name, text, optional)**: Enter the visitor's name. If the visitor is not known, you can leave this field empty.
* **Visitor Phone (Visitor Phone, text, optional)**: Enter the visitor's phone number. This will help us contact them if needed.
* **Visit Type (Visit Type, select_one, required)**: Choose whether the visitor is a new or returning patient.
* **Visit Reason (Visit Reason, text, optional)**: Optionally, enter the reason for the visit. This will help us prepare for their needs.
* **Visitor Email (Visitor Email, email, optional)**: Enter the visitor's email address. This will help us contact them if needed.
* **Visitor Note (Visitor Note, note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes about the visitor. This is optional but can be helpful for our staff.
* **Staff Information (Staff Information, text, optional)**: Enter any relevant information about the staff member they will be visiting. If not necessary, you can leave this field empty.
