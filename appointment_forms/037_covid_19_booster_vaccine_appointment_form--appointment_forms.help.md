# COVID 19 Booster Vaccine Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential details from patients who are due for a COVID-19 booster vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Save your progress at any time by clicking on the "Save Progress" button.
3. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender.
*   * Male
*   * Female
*   * Other
*   * Prefer Not to Say
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for appointment confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete address.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`ZIP Code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Insurance Provider** (`Insurance Provider`, `text`, optional): If you have an insurance provider, enter their name.
* **Insurance ID** (`Insurance ID`, `text`, optional): If you have an insurance ID, enter it here.
* **Initial Vaccine Received** (`Initial Vaccine Received`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of initial vaccine you received.
*   * Pfizer-BioNTech - 2 doses
*   * Moderna - 2 doses
*   * Johnson and Johnson - 1 dose
*   * Other
*   * Unknown
* **Last Dose Date** (`Last Dose Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your last dose.
* **Last Dose Location** (`Last Dose Location`, `text`, optional): If you remember the location, enter it here.
* **Booster Vaccine Preference** (`Booster Vaccine Preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred booster vaccine brand.
*   * Pfizer-BioNTech
*   * Moderna
*   * No preference - Any available
* **Appointment Type** (`Appointment Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of appointment you would like to have.
*   * In-person at clinic
*   * Drive-through
*   * Home visit
*   * Pharmacy appointment
* **Preferred Clinic Location** (`Preferred Clinic Location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location that is most convenient for you.
*   * Main Street Clinic
*   * Downtown Health Center
*   * Westside Medical Plaza
*   * Eastside Community Center
*   * Any location
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`Preferred Appointment Date`, `date`, required): Enter your preferred appointment date.
* **Alternate Date** (`Alternate Date`, `date`, optional): If you have an alternate date, enter it here.
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred time of appointment.
*   * Morning - 8 AM to 10 AM
*   * Mid-morning - 10 AM to 12 PM
*   * Afternoon - 12 PM to 2 PM
*   * Late afternoon - 2 PM to 4 PM
*   * Evening - 4 PM to 6 PM
*   * Any time
* **Schedule Flexibility** (`Schedule Flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Select how flexible you are with your appointment time.
*   * Very flexible - Any time
*   * Somewhat flexible
*   * Not flexible - Specific time needed
* **Mobility Limitations** (`Mobility Limitations`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have any mobility limitations.
*   * No limitations
*   * Yes - Wheelchair accessible
*   * Yes - Need assistance walking
*   * Yes - Homebound
* **Interpreter Services Needed** (`Interpreter Services Needed`, `select_one`, required): Select if you need language assistance.
*   * False
*   * Yes - Spanish
*   * Yes - Mandarin
*   * Yes - Cantonese
*   * Yes - Vietnamese
*   * Yes - Other
* **Appointment Reminder Preference** (`Appointment Reminder Preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred method of appointment reminders.
*   * Email
*   * Text message
*   * Phone call
*   * All of the above
* **Current Health Status** (`Current Health Status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current health status.
*   * Feeling well - No symptoms
*   * Minor illness
*   * Moderate illness
*   * Should reschedule
* **Current COVID-19 Symptoms** (`Current COVID-19 Symptoms`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have any current COVID-19 symptoms.
*   * No symptoms
*   * Yes - Have symptoms
* **Recent COVID-19 Exposure** (`Recent COVID-19 Exposure`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have any recent COVID-19 exposure.
*   * No known exposure
*   * Yes - Known exposure
* **Transportation to Appointment** (`Transportation to Appointment`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you plan to arrive at the appointment.
*   * Personal vehicle
*   * Public transportation
*   * Walk
*   * Ride from family or friend
*   * Need assistance
* **Plan After Vaccination** (`Plan After Vaccination`, `select_one`, required): Select how you plan to rest after vaccination.
*   * Yes - Can go home and rest
*   * Yes - Can rest at work
*   * No - Must continue activities
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   * If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name.
*   * If not, please leave blank.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   * If you have an emergency contact, please provide their phone number.
*   * If not, please leave blank.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or notes you may have.
*   * Any additional comments or information about your appointment.
*   * This is a good place to mention any special needs or requirements.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm.
*   * Please enter your full name.
*   * This is a legal confirmation of your appointment.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team.
* Save your progress at any time to return later.
* Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please ask our support team.
* Please sign the form to confirm your appointment.
