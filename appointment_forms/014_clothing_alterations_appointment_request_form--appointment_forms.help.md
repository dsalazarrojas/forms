# Clothing Alterations Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clothing Alterations Appointment Request Form is a form used to request alterations for clothing items. To complete this form, please provide the required information to help our team schedule and fulfill your alterations.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that your alteration request is processed correctly, please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately. You can submit one form per customer. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your local tailor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your alteration order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email address so we can reach you with updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further information.
* **Appointment Preferences** (`section_appointment`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide any specific preferences for your appointment scheduling.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, date, **required**): Select a date that you would prefer to schedule your appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, **required**): Select a time that you would prefer to schedule your appointment.
* **Time Flexibility** (`time_flexibility`, select_one, **required**): Please select how flexible you are with timing:
	+ Very flexible
	+ Somewhat flexible
	+ Limited flexibility
	+ Specific time required
* **Information about Garments** (`section_garments`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide information about the items that need alterations.
* **Number of Garments** (`number_garments`, number, **required**): Enter the number of items you need altered.
* **Types of Garments** (`garment_types`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the types of garments that need alterations:
	+ Pants or Jeans
	+ Dress
	+ Shirt or Blouse
	+ Jacket or Coat
	+ Skirt
	+ Wedding Dress
	+ Suit
	+ Sweater
	+ Other
* **Alterations Needed** (`section_alterations`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide any specific alteration requests.
* **Types of Alterations Needed** (`alteration_types`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the types of alterations needed:
	+ Hemming
	+ Taking in or letting out
	+ Shortening sleeves
	+ Waist adjustment
	+ Zipper replacement
	+ Button replacement
	+ Repairs
	+ Other modifications
* **Detailed Description of Alterations** (`alteration_details`, text, **required**): Provide a detailed description of the alterations needed.
* **Measurements and Fitting** (`section_measurements`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide any size and fitting information.
* **Need Fitting** (`need_fitting`, select_one, **required**): Please select if you need a fitting appointment:
	+ Yes, fitting needed
	+ No fitting needed
	+ Unsure
* **Inseam Length** (`inseam_length`, text, **optional**): If the garment is a pair of pants, enter the desired inseam length in inches.
* **Other Measurements** (`other_measurements`, text, **optional**): If you have any other specific measurements for your garments, please enter them here.
* **Timeline and Delivery** (`section_timeline`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide any specific delivery instructions.
* **When do you need the Alterations completed** (`completion_date`, date, **required**): Select the date you need the alterations completed by.
* **Rush Service** (`rush_service`, select_one, **required**): Please select if you need rush or expedited service:
	+ No, standard timeline
	+ Yes, rush service
	+ Not sure
* **How would you like to receive your items** (`delivery_method`, select_one, **required**): Please select how you would like to receive your items:
	+ Pickup in person
	+ Local delivery
	+ Shipping
	+ Other
* **Pricing and Payment** (`section_pricing`, note, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide any specific pricing and payment preferences.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, **optional**): Select an estimated budget range for your alterations:
	+ Less than 25
	+ 25-50
	+ 50-100
	+ 100-200
	+ Over 200
	+ Unsure
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **optional**): Please select your preferred payment method:
	+ Cash
	+ Credit or debit card
	+ Check
	+ Payment plan
	+ Other
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests** (`additional_notes`, text, **optional**): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional notes or special requests.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your alteration request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your local tailor.
* If you have multiple garments to alter, please submit one form per garment.
