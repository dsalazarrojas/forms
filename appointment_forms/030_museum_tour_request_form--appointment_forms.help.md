# Museum Tour Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Tour Request Form is designed to collect information from visitors who want to book a guided tour at the museum. This form helps the museum staff to manage tour bookings, communicate with visitors, and provide a smooth tour experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "User Information" section, including your name, tour date, and start time.
2. Select the tour group you would like to join from the "Tour Group" dropdown menu.
3. If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, provide the required information in the "School Info", "School Address", "School Phone", and "School Email" sections.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete your tour request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, Text, Required): This section is where you provide your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Tour Date** (Tour Date, Date, Not Required): Choose a date for your tour from the calendar.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, Time, Not Required): Select the start time for your tour.
* **End Time** (End Time, Time, Not Required): Select the end time for your tour.
* **Tour Group** (Tour Group, Select One, Required): Choose the tour group you would like to join.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, Note, Not Required): If you have any important information or instructions for the tour staff, you can type it here.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, Text, Not Required): Add any additional comments or requests you have for your tour.
* **Tour Request ID** (Tour Request ID, Number, Not Required): This will be provided by the museum staff after your tour request is processed.
* **School Info** (School Info, Select Multiple, Not Required): If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, choose your school from the list.
* **School Address** (School Address, Text, Not Required): If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, provide the address of your school.
* **School Phone** (School Phone, Text, Not Required): If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, provide the phone number of your school.
* **School Email** (School Email, Email, Not Required): If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, provide the email address of your school.
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, Text, Not Required): If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, provide the name of the teacher leading the group.
* **Teacher Email** (Teacher Email, Email, Not Required): If you are visiting the museum as part of a school group, provide the email address of the teacher leading the group.
