# Physical Therapy Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Appointment Form is a form used to schedule and confirm a physical therapy appointment. It is intended for patients to provide their personal and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your patient information on Page 1.
2. Choose a schedule for your appointment on Page 2.
3. Enter your appointment details on Page 3.
4. Provide your payment information, including method and amount, on Page 4.
5. Sign and date both the Patient Signature and Staff Signature fields on Page 5.
6. Select the payment method and enter the payment date on Page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** 
  - **Patient Information (text)**: This field is for entering your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Schedule Appointment (Page 2)** 
  - **Schedule Appointment (text)**: This field is for selecting a date and time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Details (Page 3)** 
  - **Appointment Details (text)**: This field is for providing more details about your appointment, such as any areas of concern or medical conditions you are seeking treatment for.
* **Payment Information (Page 4)** 
  - **Payment Method (select_multiple)**: This field is for selecting how you intend to pay for your appointment, such as Cash, Credit Card, or Insurance.
  - **Payment Amount (number)**: This field is for entering the amount you are paying for your appointment.
  - **Payment Date (date)**: This field is for entering the date you are making the payment.
* **Patient Signature (Page 5)** 
  - **Patient Signature (note)**: This field is for signing your name, confirming that you have read and understand the terms of your appointment.
* **Staff Signature (Page 5)** 
  - **Staff Signature (note)**: This field is for signing your name as staff member, confirming that you have received the patient's information and have scheduled their appointment.
* **Payment Method (Page 6)** 
  - **Payment Method (select_multiple)**: This field is to select a payment method for your appointment.
* **Payment Amount (Page 6)** 
  - **Payment Amount (number)**: This field is for entering the payment amount for your appointment.
* **Payment Date (Page 6)** 
  - **Payment Date (date)**: This field is for entering the payment date for your appointment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting the form.
* If this is your first appointment, you may be required to fill out additional forms or provide additional information.
