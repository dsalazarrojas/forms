This form is meant to collect information from users who are interested in inquiring about hair extension services. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details such as name, email, phone number, preferred hair service (extension, removal, coloring), desired appointment date and time, and any additional service details or messages they may have. This information will be used to schedule and facilitate hair extension service appointments.

# hair_extension_service_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to inquire about hair extension services. Please fill it out to provide your details and preferences so we can schedule an appointment with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and preferences. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in their labels. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hair Extension Service Inquiry* (Service, select_one): Choose the type of hair service you are interested in, such as hair extension, removal, or coloring.
* **Name* (text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email* (email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone (text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Appointment Date* (date, required): Select the date you are interested in scheduling the appointment.
* **Appointment Time* (time, required): Choose the time slot you prefer for the appointment.
* **Service details (note, optional): If you have any specific requests or questions about the service, type them in here.
* **Message* (text, required): Type in any additional comments or questions you may have about the service.
* **Hair Extension Service Inquiry (form_title, text, optional): This field is not a required or relevant field for your inquiry, it is just a title for the form.
