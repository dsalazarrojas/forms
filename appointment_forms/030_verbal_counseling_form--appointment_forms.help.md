# Verbal Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verbal Counseling Form is designed to capture information on the main areas of concern, client interactions, and counselor notes for each verbal counseling session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the client's record to ensure that the new session is not a continuation of a previous session.
* Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
* Select the "Signed" option under "Verbal Counseling Form Signature" if the client has signed the form.
* Enter the date and time of the session in the "Date and Time" field.
* Describe the main areas of concern in the "Main Areas of Concern" field.
* Enter the client's response to the concerns in the "Client Response" field.
* Enter any next steps or actions to be taken in the "Next Steps" field.
* Add any additional comments or notes about the session in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Sign and date the form by selecting "Signed" under "Verbal Counseling Form Signature".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Areas of Concern** (`main_areas_of_concern`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the main areas of concern discussed during the session.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's name to ensure accurate identification.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the session.
* **Concern Description** (`concern_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the client's concerns in detail.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, note, required/optional): Enter a brief summary of the next steps or actions to be taken.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required/optional): Enter the next steps or actions to be taken after the session.
* **Client Response** (`client_response`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's response to the concerns discussed.
* **Counselor Notes** (`counselor_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes or observations made by the counselor during the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Verbal Counseling Form Signature** (`verbal_counseling_form_signature`, select multiple, required): Select "Signed" to confirm the client's signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the session.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and verify the client's record before filling out this form.
* Use clear and concise language when entering information.
* Do not use this form for existing client records.
