# Satellite Internet Service Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers who wish to sign up for satellite internet services. It collects necessary information for installation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** (required fields).
2. Provide your **Phone Number** (required).
3. Choose the **Installation Date** and **Installation Time** (required).
4. Enter your **Installation Address** (required).
5. Select the satellite internet services you wish to subscribe to (optional).
6. Add any **Installation Notes** (optional).
7. Select the **Installation Type** (required).
8. Optionally, you can provide the **Installation Method**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you to schedule installation.
* **Contact Method**: This field is optional, but please use this to describe how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail).
* **Installation Date**: Select the date on which you would like to install the satellite internet services.
* **Installation Time**: Select the time on which you would like to install the satellite internet services.
* **Installation Address**: Enter the address where the installation will take place.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which is required for installation purposes.
* **Services**: Select which services you wish to subscribe to (e.g., internet, TV, phone).
* **Installation Notes**: Add any additional information that is relevant to your installation.
* **Installation Type**: Select the type of installation you need (e.g., residential, commercial).
* **Installation Method**: This field is optional, but please describe how the installation will be done (e.g., in-home, outdoor).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact method.
