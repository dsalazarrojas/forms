# Home Visitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Visitation Form is used to record details of home visits made by staff or other visitors in a healthcare or social services setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field label.
* Enter the details of the visit, including the date and time, duration, frequency, and visited person and location.
* Select the type of visit (assessment, education, or support).
* Indicate if staff was present during the visit.
* Enter the name of any staff members who visited.
* Enter the name, relationship, phone number, and email of the person contacted.
* Schedule the next visit date and time if applicable.
* Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **visit_details**: Enter a brief description of the visit. 
  * This field is used to record a brief description of the home visit, including any notable events or observations.
* **visit_date**: Enter the date of the visit. 
  * This field is used to record the date of the home visit, which is an important aspect of tracking visit history and scheduling future visits.
* **visit_time**: Enter the time of the visit. 
  * This field is used to record the time of the visit, which helps to determine the duration of the visit and any time-sensitive information.
* **visit_duration**: Enter the duration of the visit. 
  * This field is used to record the length of time spent during the visit, which is useful for planning and scheduling future visits.
* **visit_frequency**: Select the frequency of the visit. 
  * This field is used to determine how often the home visits are made, which helps to identify patterns and trends.
* **visited_with**: Select the person visited with. 
  * This field is used to indicate who was present during the visit, which helps to track staff participation and any interactions with patients or families.
* **visited_persons**: Enter the number of people visited. 
  * This field is used to record the number of people visited, which helps to track visit demographics and statistics.
* **visited_person_details**: Enter any notable details about the person visited. 
  * This field is used to record any notable events or observations made during the visit.
* **visit_note**: Enter any notes or comments about the visit. 
  * This field is used to record any additional information about the visit that may not be captured in other fields.
* **staff_visiting**: Indicate if staff was present during the visit. 
  * This field is used to track if staff was present during the visit, which helps to determine visit responsibilities and staffing needs.
* **staff_name**: Enter the name of any staff members who visited. 
  * This field is used to record the name of staff members who participated in the visit.
* **contact_person_name**: Enter the name of the person contacted. 
  * This field is used to record the name of the person visited or contacted.
* **contact_person_relationship**: Enter the relationship between the visited person and contact person. 
  * This field is used to record the relationship between the visited person and the contact person.
* **contact_person_phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person. 
  * This field is used to record the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_person_email**: Enter the email of the contact person. 
  * This field is used to record the email of the contact person.
* **next_visit_date**: Enter the date of the next visit. 
  * This field is used to schedule the next visit and track visit schedules.
* **next_visit_time**: Enter the time of the next visit. 
  * This field is used to schedule the next visit time and ensure timely appointments.
* **next_visit_note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the next visit. 
  * This field is used to record any additional information about the next visit that may not be captured in other fields.
* **followup_details**: Enter any follow-up details or comments. 
  * This field is used to record any follow-up information or comments that require attention.
* **visit_location**: Enter the location of the visit. 
  * This field is used to record the location of the visit, which helps to identify visit locations and demographics.
* **visited_persons_other**: Enter any additional details about the visited persons. 
  * This field is used to record any additional information about the visited persons that may not be captured in other fields.
* **visit_type**: Select the type of visit. 
  * This field is used to determine the purpose or type of visit, which helps to track visit purposes and statistics.
* **visited_children**: Indicate if children were visited. 
  * This field is used to record if children were visited, which helps to track visit demographics and statistics.
* **visit_notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit. 
  * This field is used to record any additional information about the visit that may not be captured in other fields.
* **visit_status**: Select the status of the visit. 
  * This field is used to track the visit status, which helps to determine visit completion and any follow-up actions.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when entering field information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact your supervisor or administrator for guidance.
