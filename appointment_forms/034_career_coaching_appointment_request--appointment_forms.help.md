# Career Coaching Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Coaching Appointment Request form is used by career coaches to collect information from clients for upcoming appointments. This form helps the coach to understand the client's preferences and tailor the coaching experience accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Client's Full Name**: Enter the name of the person requesting coaching in the "Client Name" field.
2. **Provide a Contact Email and Phone Number (Optional)**: If you have a contact email and/or phone number, please provide them so we can reach out to you with appointment details.
3. **Select Preferred Date and Time**: Choose a date and time that suits you best for the coaching session.
4. **Select Coaching Focus Areas**: Choose one or more focus areas from the list provided (Resume development, Interview preparation, Career planning, Skills assessment).
5. **Choose Session Format**: Select your preference for the session format (In-person, Virtual, Phone).
6. **Briefly Describe Any Previous Coaching Experience**: If you have had any previous coaching experience, please provide a brief description.
7. **Provide Additional Notes**: If there's anything you think the coach should know before the session, please provide it in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the person requesting coaching.
* **Email**: Enter a contact email address that we can use for appointment confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a contact phone number that we can use for appointment confirmation (optional).
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date for the coaching session.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time window for the coaching session.
* **Coaching Focus Areas**: Select one or more areas of focus for the coaching session.
* **Session Format**: Select your preferred session format (In-person, Virtual, Phone).
* **Prior Coaching Experience**: Briefly describe any previous coaching experience you may have had.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information that the coach should know before the session.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have filled out the form for the correct purpose (client appointment request).
- Be as specific as possible when selecting coaching focus areas.
- Choose a session format that suits your needs and preferences.
