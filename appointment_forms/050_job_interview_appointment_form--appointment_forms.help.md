# Job Interview Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Interview Appointment Form is used to collect details for setting up and scheduling job interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the interview details (page 1).
2. Provide the candidate's information (page 2).
3. Indicate whether there will be interviewers present during the interview (page 3).
4. Schedule the interview by selecting a date and time (page 4).
5. Indicate the candidate's status (page 6) and whether they have a preferred interview location (page 7).
6. Add any additional notes about the interview (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Details (Interview Details, text, required: false)**: This is the section where you can enter any additional information specific to the interview, such as the interview type, location, or purpose.
* **Candidate Info (Candidate Info, text, required: true)**: Please provide the full name, email, and phone number of the candidate.
* **Interviewers (Interviewers, select_multiple, required: true)**: Choose all the interviewers who will be present during the interview. This helps ensure that everyone involved is aware of their role and expectations.
* **Schedule (Schedule, date, required: true)**: Select a date for the interview from the provided calendar.
* **Interview Time (Interview Time, time, required: true)**: Choose the specific time for the interview.
* **Candidate Status (Candidate Status, select_one, required: true)**: Indicate the candidate's status as Active or Inactive.
* **Interview Location (Interview Location, select_one, required: true)**: Specify whether the candidate has a preferred interview location.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required: false)**: Add any additional information about the interview, such as the location or equipment requirements.
