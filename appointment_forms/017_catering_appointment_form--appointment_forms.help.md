# Catering Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients planning events or meetings requiring catering services. It is meant to facilitate efficient and accurate communication between you, our catering team, and their guests.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the required information.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for all select fields (e.g., appointment type, event type, preferred cuisine).
3. Provide clear and concise answers for text fields (e.g., client name, email, event location).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required)
Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required)
Enter your email address where we can contact you for further discussion.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required)
Provide your contact phone number in case we need to reach out to you.

### 4. **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required)
If you are representing a company or organization, please enter its name.

### 5. **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required)
Choose a date for the appointment or meeting.

### 6. **Preferred Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required)
Select a time slot that suits you for the appointment.

### 7. **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, `select_one`, required)
Choose the type of appointment that best describes your needs:

    * In-Person Meeting
    * Phone Call
    * Video Call
    * Site Visit
    * Tasting Session

### 8. **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of event you are planning:

    * Wedding
    * Corporate Event
    * Birthday Party
    * Anniversary
    * Graduation
    * Conference
    * Private Party
    * Other

### 9. **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required)
Enter the date of your event.

### 10. **Estimated Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, required)
Provide an estimated number of guests.

### 11. **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required)
Enter the location where the event will take place.

### 12. **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, not required)
Choose your estimated budget for the event:

    * Under $1000
    * $1000 to $5000
    * $5000 to $10000
    * $10000 to $25000
    * Over $25000

### 13. **Service Type Needed** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required)
Choose the type of catering services you require:

    * Full Service Catering
    * Drop-off Only
    * Buffet Style
    * Plated Dinner
    * Cocktail Reception
    * Food Station

### 14. **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, not required)
If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them here.

### 15. **Preferred Cuisine** (`preferred_cuisine`, `text`, not required)
If you have a preferred cuisine, please let us know.

### 16. **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, not required)
If you have any special requests, please specify them here.

### 17. **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, not required)
How did you hear about us?

    * Website
    * Social Media
    * Referral
    * Advertisement
    * Previous Customer
    * Other

### 18. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required)
If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them here.
