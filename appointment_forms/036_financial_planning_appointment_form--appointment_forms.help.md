# financial_planning_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial_planning_appointment_form is used to schedule a meeting or appointment with a client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's information in the Client Information section.
2.  Provide the details of the meeting, including the dates and times, in the Meeting Details section.
3.  Specify the client's preferred meeting dates and time in the Preferred Meeting Dates and Meeting Time section.
4.  Ensure that the client has a valid email address and a phone number for communication.
5.  Choose the meeting location from the provided options in the Meeting Location section.
6.  Add any additional meeting notes that may be helpful for follow-up communication in the Meeting Notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
*   **Client Name** (text): Enter the client's name to identify who the meeting is with.
*   **Email** (email): Enter the client's email address to ensure they can be reached for follow-up communication.
*   **Phone** (text): Enter the client's phone number to facilitate direct communication.

### Meeting Details
*   **Meeting Details** (text): Provide a brief description of the meeting and its purpose.
*   **Meeting Dates** (text): Specify the date or dates of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Time** (text): Specify the time of the meeting.

### Meeting Location
*   **Meeting Location** (select multiple): Choose the location where the meeting will take place. Options include "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".

### Meeting Notes
*   **Meeting Notes** (text): Add any additional notes that may be helpful for follow-up communication.

## Tips
*   Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have checked for any existing appointments that may be relevant to the information you are providing.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing the client's information, meeting details, and preferred meeting dates and time.
*   Double-check your email address and phone number to ensure they are accurate.
*   Choose the most suitable meeting location based on the client's preferences.
*   Keep the meeting notes concise and clear for follow-up communication.
