# Artist Monetization Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Artist Monetization Consultation Form is designed to help artists gather relevant information about their current situation, goals, and needs. This form collects essential data that will be used to provide personalized advice and support for artists looking to monetize their creative work effectively.

## How to Complete This Form

1.  Begin by ensuring you have all necessary details about your artistic work before filling out the form.
2.  Read each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
3.  If you're unsure about a particular question, try to answer it to the best of your ability.
4.  Review each question and section to ensure you've provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Artist or Creator Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your professional name as an artist.
*   **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, required/true): Provide your primary contact email.
*   **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, required/true): Enter your contact number.
*   **Creative Field** (`creative_field`, `text`, required/true): Describe the type of creative work you do (music, visual art, writing, video, etc.).
*   **Current Income Streams** (`current_income_streams`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all current income streams (Direct Sales, Streaming Royalties, Subscriptions, Sponsorships, Advertising, Freelance Work, Commission Work, Grants or Funding, None Yet).
*   **Approximate Annual Income from Creative Work** (`annual_income`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the approximate annual income from your creative work from the options provided.
*   **Current Audience Size** (`audience_size`, `number`, required/false): Enter your total audience or followers size.
*   **Main Platforms Where You Have Presence** (`main_platforms`, `text`, required/true): Describe the main platforms where you have a presence (social media, websites, streaming services, etc.).
*   **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, `select_one`, required/false): Select the level of engagement from your audience (Low, Moderate, High, Very High).
*   **Primary Monetization Goals** (`monetization_goals`, `text`, required/true): Define your primary goals for monetization.
*   **Target Annual Income** (`target_income`, `number`, required/false): Enter the target annual income you aim to reach.
*   **Timeline for Achieving Goals** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required/false): Choose the timeline to achieve your monetization goals (3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, No specific timeline).
*   **Main Challenges with Monetization** (`main_challenges`, `text`, required/true): Describe the main challenges you face with monetization.
*   **Areas Where You Need Most Help** (`areas_needing_help`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the areas where you need the most help with monetization.
*   **Preferred Consultation Format** (`preferred_format`, `select_one`, required/false): Choose your preferred consultation format (Video call, Phone call, In-person meeting, Email consultation, No preference).
*   **Preferred Consultation Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required/false): Select the time of day when you prefer to be consulted (Mornings, Afternoons, Evenings, Weekends, Flexible).
*   **Additional Context or Background** (`additional_context`, `text`, required/false): If any additional context or background information that might be useful during the consultation.
*   **Budget Available for Consultation** (`budget_available`, `select_one`, required/false): Confirm your budget availability for the consultation (Yes, significant budget, Limited budget, Looking for free consultation, Willing to negotiate).
*   **I am Ready for a Monetization Consultation** (`confirm_consultation`, `select_one`, required/true): Confirm your readiness to proceed with the consultation (Yes, I am ready, I need more time to decide).

## Tips

- Please ensure you answer each question with your best effort.
- If you have any further information or questions, you can contact us directly.
- We'll use the provided information to tailor the consultation to your specific needs.
- Please note that the form will guide you through the essential information required.
