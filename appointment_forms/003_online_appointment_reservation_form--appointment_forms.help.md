# Online Appointment Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for online appointment reservations with a service business or provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and contact information.
2. Choose the date and time for your appointment.
3. Select the duration of your appointment.
4. Confirm whether you would like a specific service.
5. Choose your available time slot (morning, afternoon, or evening).
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
7. Confirm your form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Business Name** (`service_business_name`): Enter the name of the service business or provider you wish to schedule an appointment with.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your name, which will be used to associate with your appointment.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number, so we can get in touch with you about your appointment.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`): Choose the date for your appointment. Note: This field is not the same as the `service_date` field on page 2.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`): Select the time slot for your appointment.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`): Enter the duration of your appointment in minutes.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Confirm whether you would like a specific service.
* **Service Time Slot** (`service_time_slot`): Choose your preferred time slot for your appointment.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address, so we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`): Enter a short description of your form submission.
* **Confirm Service** (`confirm_service`): Confirm that you would like to schedule the service with us.

Note: The "Service Date" field on page 1 is intended for the appointment date, while the one on page 2 is not used in this form. 

Also, please make sure to fill out the "Service Date" on the first page with the correct date, otherwise your form submission might get rejected. 

Please be aware that the "Confirm Service" field is not actually used in this form but I assume it's for the user to confirm the service. If you're unsure, please let me know and I'll be happy to help!
