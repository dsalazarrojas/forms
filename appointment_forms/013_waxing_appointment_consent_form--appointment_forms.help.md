# Waxing appointment consent form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients before their waxing appointment. It ensures that clients have provided their informed consent and provides necessary medical and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Provide your medical history, including any allergies and medical conditions that may impact your waxing appointment.
3. Select your emergency contact(s) and provide their information.
4. List any medications you are currently taking.
5. Provide your consent for the waxing procedure.
6. Sign the "Signature" field to confirm your consent.
7. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required/optional): This section is where you provide your personal details, such as your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, number, required/optional): This field is used to provide your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions that may impact your waxing appointment.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please provide at least one emergency contact, including their name and relationship to you.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): List any allergies you have, including medication or foods.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the options to provide your informed consent for the waxing procedure.
* **Second Consent** (`consent2`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the options to confirm your understanding of the waxing procedure and its risks and benefits.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign this field to confirm your consent for the waxing procedure.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your waxing technician for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that you understand the risks and benefits of the waxing procedure.
