# Expert Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Expert Consultation Request" form is designed to be filled out by experts to request consultations or meetings with clients. The form collects personal details, consultation preferences, and submission information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the desired consultation date and time.
3. Choose the consultation type, duration, and preferred date.
4. Select the submission status and submitter(s).
5. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
6. Review your information before submitting the form.

* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Consultation Date (5)**: Select the date for the consultation.

* **Preferred Time (7)**: Enter the preferred time for the consultation.
* **Consultation Type (8)**: Choose the type of consultation (Consultation or Meeting).
* **Consultation Length (9)**: Select the desired consultation length (30, 60, or 90 minutes).
* **Notes (10)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the consultation.
* **Preferred Date (11)**: Select the preferred date for the consultation.
* **Assigned Tool (12)**: Enter any tools or resources assigned to the consultation (optional).
* **Submitted By (13)**: Check if the request was submitted by you.
* **Submitted To (14)**: Enter the person or team this request is submitted to.
* **Submitted At (15)**: Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Submitted Date (16)**: Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submission Status (17)**: Select the status of the submission (Draft, Completed, or Archived).
* **Label (18)**: Enter a label for the submission (optional).
* **Label 2 (19)**: Enter an alternative label for the submission (optional).

* **Select Prophets (21)**: Select any prophets or experts involved in the consultation (optional).
* **Selected Prophet (22)**: Select the prophet or expert for the consultation (optional).
* **Note 3 (23)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the consultation (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* Use the notes fields to provide any additional context or comments about the consultation.
