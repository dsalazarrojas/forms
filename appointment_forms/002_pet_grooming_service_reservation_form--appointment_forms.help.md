# Pet Grooming Service Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a grooming service for your pet. Please fill out the form with your pet's information and your contact details to ensure we have all the necessary information to provide the best service for your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pet you are reserving the service for.
2. Enter your pet's name.
3. Enter your pet's age.
4. Select the groomer you would like to schedule with.
5. Select a date and time for the appointment.
6. Select a reservation time.
7. Provide details about the service you would like to perform on your pet.
8. Select the size of your pet.
9. Enter your name.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your pet's weight (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`Pet Type`, select_one): Choose the type of pet you are reserving the service for.
* **Pet Name** (`Pet Name`, text): Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Age** (`Pet Age`, number): Enter your pet's age.
* **Groomer** (`Groomer`, select_multiple): Select the groomer you would like to schedule with.
* **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, date): Select a date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`Appointment Time`, time): Select a time for the appointment.
* **Reservation Time** (`Reservation Time`, select_one): Select whether you would like to reserve a time slot or not.
* **Service Details** (`Service Details`, text): Provide details about the service you would like to perform on your pet.
* **Pet Size** (`Pet Size`, select_multiple): Select the size of your pet.
* **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Owner Phone** (`Owner Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`Owner Email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Pet Weight** (`Pet Weight`, number): Enter your pet's weight (if applicable).
* **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select_one): Select the type of service you would like to perform on your pet.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please provide them in the "Service Details" field.
* If you have any questions or need to make changes to your reservation, contact us at your convenience.
