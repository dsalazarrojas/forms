# Retirement Counseling Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users request an appointment with a retirement counselor. It gathers essential information to schedule a meeting that suits both the user and the counselor's availability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into six pages. Please navigate through each page, reading and completing the required fields.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in according to the instructions provided.
3. Use the provided instructions to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retirement Counseling Information** (page 1, title): This section is a general description of your reasons for requesting the meeting.
* **Select Date and Time** (page 2, date): Please select a date and time that works for you. Ensure you select a time slot available for the counselor.
* **Select Counselor** (page 3, select_one): Choose one of the available counselors from the list. This ensures you are matched with the most suitable counselor for your needs.
* **Notes** (page 4, note): Use this section to add any additional information that may be helpful for the counselor to know before the meeting.
* **Email** (page 5, email): Enter your email address so that the counselor can contact you.
* **Phone** (page 6, text): Enter your phone number so that the counselor can contact you if needed.
