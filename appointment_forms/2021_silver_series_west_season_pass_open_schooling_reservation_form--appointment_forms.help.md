# 2021 Silver Series West Season Pass Open Schooling Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from interested students to help us better understand their needs and preferences. Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure we can provide the best possible experience for you.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Select your preferred subject area and learning style.
3. Choose your preferred start date and time (if applicable).
4. Enter your unique submission code.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name ** (`full_name`, `text`, required/True): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address ** (`email_address`, `email`, required/True): We will use this to confirm your reservation.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional/False): Provide your phone number for updates (optional).
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, optional/False): Enter your school ID (if applicable).
* **Grade/Year level ** (`grade_level`, `text`, required/True): Enter your grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12 or Freshman, Sophomore, etc.).
* **School or institution name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional/False): Enter the name of your school (if applicable).
* **Subject area ** (`subject_area`, `select_one`, required/True): Select the main subject area for your reservation.
* **Preferred learning style ** (`learning_style`, `select_one`, required/True): Choose your preferred learning style.
* **Average study hours per week** (`hours_studied`, `integer`, optional/False): Enter your approximate average study hours per week.
* **Preferred start date * ** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, required/True): Select your preferred start date for the session.
* **Preferred start time** (`preferred_start_time`, `time`, optional/False): Select your preferred start time (if applicable).
* **How satisfied are you with the reservation process** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/True): Rate your satisfaction with the reservation process.
* **Main challenges faced (select all)** (`challenges`, `select_multiple`, optional/False): Choose all the challenges you have faced during the reservation process.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional/False): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Unique submission code * ** (`unique_submission_id`, `text`, required/True): Enter the unique code provided to you.
* **Thank you for your reservation!** (`thank_you_note`, `note`, optional/False): You will be contacted regarding your reservation.
