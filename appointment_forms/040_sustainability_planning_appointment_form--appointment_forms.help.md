# Sustainability Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for sustainability planning appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Enter the required information in each field as prompted.
3. Review the form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Form**: submit the completed form to schedule the appointment.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization scheduling the appointment.
* **Consultant**: Enter the name of the person or team providing the sustainability consulting service.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment or meeting.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Duration**: Enter the expected length of the appointment in minutes.
* **Appointment Purpose**: Briefly describe the reason for the appointment.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any extra information related to the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for clarification.
