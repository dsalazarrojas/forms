# Property Showing Reminder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to create a reminder for a property showing event. It will capture the necessary details to facilitate a timely and effective reminder to the relevant parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Property Showing Reminder Form** (`property_showing_reminder_form`, Text, Required: No): A brief description of the property showing event.
2. **Contact Information**:
	* **Contact** (`contact_name`, Text, Required: No): The name of the person responsible for the contact.
	* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required: No): The phone number of the contact person.
	* **Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required: No): The email address of the contact person.
3. **Showing Details**:
	* **Showing Date** (`showing_date`, Date, Required: No): The date of the property showing event.
	* **Showing Time** (`showing_time`, Time, Required: No): The time of the property showing event.
	* **Property Address** (`property_address`, Text, Required: No): The address of the property being shown.
4. **Reminder Settings**:
	* **Show to Client** (`show_to_client`, Select, Required: No): Decide if the reminder should be shown to the client.
	* **Send Reminders** (`send_reminders`, Select, Required: No): Choose when to send reminders (one-time or recurring).
	* **Reminder Frequency** (`reminder_frequency`, Select, Required: No): Set the frequency of reminder send-outs (one-time, daily, weekly, etc.).
5. **Additional Information**:
	* **Confirmation Message** (`confirmation_message`, Text, Required: No): A message to be sent as confirmation of the property showing event.

## Tips

* Ensure that all contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check the showing details to avoid scheduling conflicts or missed appointments.
* Choose the correct reminder frequency to ensure timely communication.
* Use the confirmation message as a final touch-point to confirm the property showing event.
