# Employee Verification Appointment Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule employee verification meetings. Please use this guide to ensure you understand each step of the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Schedule Meeting**: Choose a date for the meeting by clicking the calendar icon or entering the date manually.
2. Select your **Manager** by choosing one of the options available from the dropdown list.
3. Select the employees who will be **verified** by choosing multiple options from the list.
4. Enter the **Schedule Time** for the meeting.
5. Define the **Schedule Duration** of the meeting.
6. **Enter a Verification Message** for the meeting, if required.
7. Enter the **Verification Date** of the meeting.
8. Select the **Verification Time** for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Meeting**: This is the date on which the meeting will take place. This field is required to schedule a meeting.
*   **Select Manager**: This field lets you choose who will be responsible for verifying the employee. You can select one of the available managers from the dropdown list.
*   **Select Employees**: This field allows you to select multiple employees who will be verified during the meeting. You can choose multiple employees from the list.
*   **Schedule Time**: This field is used to set the start time of the meeting. You can enter the time manually or select it using the calendar icon.
*   **Schedule Duration**: This field is used to define how long the meeting will last.
*   **Verification Message**: If required, enter a message to be sent to the selected employees and managers.
*   **Verification Date**: Enter the date when the verification will be performed.
*   **Verification Time**: Enter the time when the verification will be performed.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct manager and employees for the meeting.
*   Double-check the schedule time and duration before submitting the form.
*   Enter a clear and concise verification message, if required.
*   Verify the date and time of the verification before submitting the form.
