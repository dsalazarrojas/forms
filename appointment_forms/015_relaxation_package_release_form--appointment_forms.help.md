# Relaxation Package Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Relaxation Package Release Form is used by clients to release their previously purchased packages. This form captures essential information about the client and the package they are releasing, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please provide your details and the details of the package you wish to release.
2. Select the date you schedule the session for the package release.
3. Ensure you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the package release.
4. Choose the duration and frequency of the scheduled session.
5. Select the manager of the package, which could be you (the client), the spa or wellness provider, or a third party.
6. Add any additional notes about the session.
7. Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to finalize your package release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text): Please provide your name and any other relevant details you wish to share with the package manager.
* **Package Details** (`package_details`, text): Enter the name of the package you wish to release.
* **Schedule Session** (`schedule_session`, date): Select the date you have scheduled for the package release.
* **Capture Consent** (`capture_consent`, select_one): Select the option that best describes your understanding of the terms and conditions.
	+ Client has read, understands, and agrees to the terms and conditions
	+ Client has read, understands, but disagrees with the terms and conditions
	+ Client has not read nor understands the terms and conditions
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number): If applicable, enter the duration of the session in the format of number of months.
