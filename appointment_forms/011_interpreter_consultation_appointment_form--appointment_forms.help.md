# interpreter_consultation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users for interpreter consultations. It collects essential details about the user, the appointment, and any special requests or medical history that may be relevant to the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your user details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the appointment you are scheduling, including the date, time, and location.
3. Share any consultation preferences, such as the language you would like to use for the consultation.
4. Enter any medical history or special requests that may be relevant to the consultation.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
* **User Details**: This is where you will enter your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Appointment Details
* **Appointment Details**: This is where you will enter information about the appointment you are scheduling, including the date, time, and location.

### Consultation Preferences
* **Consultation Preferences**: This is where you will share any preferences you have for the consultation, such as the language you would like to use.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: This is where you will enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: This is where you will enter any relevant medical history or health information.

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests**: This is where you will enter any special requests or requirements for the consultation.
