# Inheritance Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the purpose of planning appointments with clients or their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Choose a preferred method of contact (Attorney, Estate Planner, Phone, or Video Call).
4. Add any additional information about the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter the name of the appointment.
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, not required): Choose a date for the appointment.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, not required): Choose a time for the appointment.
* **Location** (`form_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the appointment.
* **Reason for Visit** (`form_reason_for_visit`, text, not required): Briefly describe the reason for the appointment.
* **Preferred Method of Contact** (`form_preferred_method`, select_one, not required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Attorney, Estate Planner, Phone, or Video Call).
* **Additional Information** (`form_additional_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional details about the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a successful appointment.
* If you need to add or remove pages, use the corresponding options in the form settings.
