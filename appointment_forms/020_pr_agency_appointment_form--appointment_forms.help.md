# PR Agency Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PR Agency Appointment Form is used to schedule meetings with clients, including time, date, location, and other relevant details. It is an essential tool for PR agency representatives to efficiently coordinate meetings with potential or existing clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your meeting details, such as time and date.
2. Select a meeting location if applicable.
3. Enter the client's name and any additional notes or information.
4. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the meeting, such as its purpose or topic.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time slot for the meeting. Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date for the meeting.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization you will be meeting with.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Select an alternative date if you have a preferred date for the meeting.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select an alternative time slot if you have a preferred time for the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the meeting, if it is applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information or instructions for the meeting.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so the client can contact you directly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so the client can contact you directly.
