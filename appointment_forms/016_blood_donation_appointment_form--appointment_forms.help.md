# Blood Donation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from prospective blood donors to schedule a blood appointment at a preferred donation center. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for a successful blood donation appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Schedule Blood Donation Appointment" page.
2. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Enter your Phone Number, Date of Birth, and Blood Type (if known).
4. Answer the questions regarding your previous donation experience, including whether you have donated blood before, the date of your last donation (if applicable), and select your preferred donation center from the options provided.
5. Choose your preferred appointment date and time, as well as select your reminder preference (e.g., Email, SMS, Phone call, or No reminder needed).
6. Confirm that you are in good health and have not had any recent illness or fever.
7. Finally, confirm whether you are taking any medications and select your preferred appointment date and time for your blood donation appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`donor_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`donor_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for appointment reminders and confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for appointment reminders and confirmation.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Blood Type** (`blood_type`, `select_one`, optional): If known, select your blood type from the options provided (e.g., O+, O-, A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-).
* **Have you donated blood before?** (`previous_donor`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have donated blood before, 'No' if you have not, or 'Not sure' if unsure.
* **Date of Last Donation** (`last_donation_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last donation, if applicable.
* **Preferred Donation Center** (`donation_center`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred donation center from the options provided (e.g., Downtown Medical Center, Riverside Blood Bank, Crossroads Donation Center, Community Health Clinic).
* **Preferred Donation Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for your blood donation appointment from the calendar options provided.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred time slot for the appointment from the options provided (e.g., 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM).
* **Are you in good health?** (`current_health`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are in good health, 'No' if not, or 'Unsure' if unsure.
* **Have you had any recent illness or fever?** (`recent_illness`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have had a recent illness or fever, 'No' if not, or 'Unsure' if unsure.
* **Are you currently taking medications?** (`medications`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you are taking medications, 'No' if not, or 'Unsure' if unsure.
* **Reminder Preference** (`reminder_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred reminder method (e.g., Email, SMS, Phone call, No reminder needed).
* **Appointment Booking Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): This is the date your appointment will be scheduled for, which will be displayed after you have submitted the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date to receive appointment reminders and confirmations.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding your blood donation appointment, please contact your preferred donation center for assistance.
