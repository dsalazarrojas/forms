# Nail Salon Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Salon Appointment Form is used to book an appointment at a nail salon. It is designed to gather information from clients about their preferred time slot, desired services, appointment date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Choose a preferred time slot in the "Preferred time slot" field.
5. Select one or more services you would like to have done in the "Desired services" field.
6. Select a date for your appointment in the "Appointment date" field.
7. Choose a time for your appointment in the "Appointment time" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
9. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm phone number" field.
10. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Preferred time slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, `select_one`, required): Choose a time slot for your appointment.
* **Desired services** (`desired_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more services you would like to have done.
* **Appointment date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Additional comments** (`appointment_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.
* **Confirm phone number** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, optional): Confirm your email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Select a date and time that works for you.
* Choose one or more services you would like to have done.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
* Confirm your phone number and email address to ensure accuracy.
