# Online Doctor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to schedule appointments with doctors. It is an essential tool for both patients and medical professionals to ensure seamless communication and efficient appointment management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required details such as patient name, date, time, and doctor's name.
2. Select the available time and contact method for the doctor.
3. Add any necessary medical records or notes.
4. Specify the payment method and amount.
5. Confirm the appointment details such as date, time, and duration.
6. Indicate the medical record status and select follow-up reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the date for your appointment.
* **Time** (time, required): Select the time for your appointment.
* **Doctor** (select_one, required): Choose the doctor you would like to see.
* **Available Time** (time, required): Select the time you are available for the appointment.
* **Contact Method** (select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as email, phone, or in person.
* **Message** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the doctor.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for the doctor to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for the doctor to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Patient ID** (number, required): Enter your patient ID number.
* **Doctor ID** (number, required): Enter the doctor's ID number.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the doctor's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the doctor's contact phone number.
* **Medical Record** (text, optional): Add any relevant medical records for the doctor.
* **Medical Record Type** (text, optional): Specify the type of medical record.
* **Medical Record Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes for the medical record.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for the appointment, such as credit card or cash.
* **Payment Amount** (number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Appointment Date** (date, required): Select the date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (time, required): Select the time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (text, optional): Specify the duration of the appointment.
* **Medical Record Status** (select_one, required): Indicate the current status of your medical record.
* **Follow Up Reminders** (select_multiple, required): Choose whether you would like to receive follow-up reminders for your appointment.

**Tips**

* Double-check the form for any required fields before submitting.
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information for the doctor.
* Review the appointment details carefully before submitting the form.
