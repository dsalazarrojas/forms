# Pediatric Growth Check Appointment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential details during a pediatric growth check appointment. It includes information about the patient's name, visit date and time, growth parameters, medical history, allergies, medications, and next visit details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the patient's name, visit date, and visit time to ensure accurate records.
2. Record the patient's growth parameters, such as height, weight, and head circumference.
3. Report any medical history, allergies, and current medications.
4. Note down any medical notes or observations from the visit.
5. Check the "Schedule Appointment" option if this is a scheduled visit.
6. Enter your contact information, including email, phone, fax, and address.
7. Enter any relevant medical notes or comments.
