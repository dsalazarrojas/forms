# piercing_service_waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture client information for a piercing service waitlist. It helps to ensure accurate and efficient management of client requests and preferred services. The form is designed for clients to provide their details and preferences for piercing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Specify your preferred services and any additional notes in the "Preferred Services" and "Additional Notes" sections.
3. Choose your preferred appointment time in the "Appointment Time" section.
4. Indicate your artist preferences in the "Artist Preferred" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: This section is for providing your basic information as the client, such as your name and contact details. Please ensure you fill in this section accurately.
* **Preferred Services**: This section is for specifying which piercing services you would like to be performed during your appointment. You can list as many services as applicable.
* **Artist Preferred**: This section is for indicating your preferred artist(s) to perform your piercing services. If you have a specific artist in mind, please specify their name or initials.
* **Services Offered**: This section is for listing the specific services that the artist(s) you prefer is/are offering. Please ensure this aligns with your preferred services.
* **Appointment Time**: This section is for choosing your preferred appointment time for your piercing services. Please note that this is your preferred time, not a fixed appointment schedule.
* **Preferred Time**: This section is for indicating your available time slots for the appointment. Please ensure you are realistic with your time availability.
* **Additional Notes**: This section is for adding any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the piercing services.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately to avoid delays in the piercing service process.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the "Additional Notes" section.
- If you have any changes or updates to your preferred services or appointment time, please contact the piercing studio directly.
