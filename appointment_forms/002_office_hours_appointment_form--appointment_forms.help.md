# Office Hours Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for students to schedule an appointment with their professor's office hours. The purpose of this form is to provide a convenient and organized way for students to request meetings with their professors during office hours, allowing for a more efficient and productive use of time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or other official records.
	* **Email**: Enter your university email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
	* **Time**: Select the specific time you would like to schedule the appointment.
2. Provide any additional information or context about your appointment in the **Message** field.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or other official records.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Time** (time, required): Select the specific time you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Subject** (text, optional): Provide a brief subject or description of your appointment.
* **Message** (text, optional): Provide any additional information or context about your appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to include your university email address to ensure that the professor can contact you directly.
* Be clear and concise in your message to help the professor understand your needs.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements for your appointment, be sure to include them in the message field.
