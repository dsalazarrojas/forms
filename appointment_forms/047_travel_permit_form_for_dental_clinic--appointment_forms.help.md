# Travel Permit Form For Dental Clinic - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a travel permit for a dental clinic. The information collected will be used by the clinic for the user's travel purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including any relevant details.
2. Fill out the Prophets Information field, if required.
3. Select your Medical License from the available options.
4. Select your Vehicle License from the available options.
5. Enter your Travel Details in the text field.
6. Choose your Travel Reason from the available options.
7. Select your Prophets Information from the available options.
8. Enter your Travel Duration in the numeric field.
9. Fill out the Return Ticket Details field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering any general information you think is relevant to your travel.
* **Prophets Information** (`prophets_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering any relevant prophets information.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is for selecting your medical license. Please choose one of the available options.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for selecting your vehicle license. Please choose one or more of the available options.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering any travel details you think are relevant.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field is for choosing the reason for your travel. Please choose one of the available options.
* **Prophets Information** (`return_ticket`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This field is for selecting your prophets information. Please choose one or more of the available options.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, optional):
	+ This field is for entering your travel duration in numeric format.
* **Return Ticket Details** (`return_ticket_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for entering any details about your return ticket.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the clinic staff.
