# Window Cleaning Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Window Cleaning Appointment Form is a straightforward form designed to collect necessary details from clients to schedule a window cleaning service. Please take a few minutes to read through the guide below to ensure a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **User Information**: Enter your details to help us identify you and get in touch with you regarding the appointment.
2.  **Service Type**: Choose the type of service you need. We offer window cleaning and glass replacement services.
3.  **Appointment Date**: Select the date you'd like to schedule the appointment. Please format your date accordingly (YYYY-MM-DD).
4.  **Appointment Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the appointment.
5.  **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any updates or confirmations.
6.  **Email Address**: Provide your email address for any further communication or updates.
7.  **Note**: If you have any special requests or instructions, please add them here.
8.  **Confirm Appointment**: Confirm that you've scheduled this appointment to avoid any confusion.
9.  **Window Size**: If you're unsure about the size of your windows, don't worry! You can simply enter the approximate number of windows or panes you need service for.
10. **Glass Type**: Select the type of glass that needs service (Single Pane, Double Pane, or Tempered).
11.  **Window Location**: Choose the location of your windows that needs service (Front Door, Back Door, or Side Window).
12.  **Client Name**: This is an optional field to provide your name for our records, but it's not required for the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (Text): Enter your name, phone number, and any other details you'd like to share with us.
*   **Service Type** (Select One): Choose between Window Cleaning and Glass Replacement services.
*   **Appointment Date** (Date): Select the date you'd like to schedule the appointment.
*   **Appointment Time** (Time): Choose the time slot you prefer.
*   **Contact Number** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address for further communication.
