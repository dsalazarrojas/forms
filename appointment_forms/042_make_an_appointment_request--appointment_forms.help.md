# Make An Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request an appointment with a healthcare professional for any purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information in each field, making sure to provide accurate and complete answers.
2.  Click the "Submit" button to send your appointment request to the healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **make-an-appointment-request** (`title`, `text`, Optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your appointment request.
*   **appointment-date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, Optional): Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment for. Please use the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **appointment-time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, Optional): Choose the appointment time in 12-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM, 2:00 PM, etc.).
*   **appointment-day** (`appointment_day`, `text`, Optional): Enter the day you would like to schedule the appointment for. Please note that this field is for general scheduling and may not be taken into account by the healthcare professional.
*   **appointment-time-range** (`appointment_time_range`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select whether your appointment should be scheduled within a specific time range.
*   **appointment-repeat** (`appointment_repeat`, `number`, Optional): Enter the number of appointments you would like to schedule with the healthcare professional.
*   **phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number so that the healthcare professional can contact you.
*   **email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address so that the healthcare professional can contact you.
*   **appointment-note** (`appointment_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional information or notes about your appointment request.
*   **submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, Required): Select "Submit" to send your appointment request to the healthcare professional, or "Cancel" to discard your request.
