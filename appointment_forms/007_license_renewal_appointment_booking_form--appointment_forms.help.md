# License Renewal Appointment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an appointment for a license renewal process. Fill out the form with your details to schedule a convenient time to meet with a representative to complete your license renewal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for your license renewal appointment.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as `required` in red).
3. Select a preferred time slot for your appointment.
4. Review your details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date you would like to have your license renewal appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`Appointment Time`, `time`, **required**): Select the time you would like to have your license renewal appointment.
* **License Number** (`License Number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your license number for verification purposes.
* **License Expiration Date** (`License Expiration Date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your license expiration date for verification purposes.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`Preferred Time Slot`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select one or more time slots that you are available for your appointment. 
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, **optional**): Enter any additional information about your appointment that you would like to share with our representative.
* **Selected Time Slot** (`Selected Time Slot`, `select_one`, **required**): This field will be populated with your selected time slot after you've made your choice in the previous field.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
