# Psychological Appointment Pre Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential clients before their psychological appointment, helping our clinic or practice prepare for the session and ensure a smooth and effective process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to select your preferred contact method.
3. If you have any medical history, medication, or allergies, please provide a brief description.
4. If you have any previous experience with a therapist, feel free to mention their name.
5. If you have any reason for visiting our practice, please provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Request** (`session_request`, Text, Required): Please provide a brief description of what you hope to achieve or discuss during your session.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Select the date of your desired appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Choose a time slot that suits you best.
* **Duration** (`duration`, Number, Required): Indicate how many sessions you would like your appointment to last.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Provide your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In-person).
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, Text, Optional): If you have a specific reason for visiting our practice, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Previous Therapist** (`previous_therapist`, Text, Optional): If you have any previous experience with a therapist, please mention their name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, Optional): If you have any medical history or concerns, please provide a brief description.
* **Medication** (`medication`, Text, Optional): If you are currently taking any medication, please list them.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, Text, Optional): If you have any allergies, please list them.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask our staff for clarification.
* We will review your information and contact you for further instructions or to schedule your appointment.
