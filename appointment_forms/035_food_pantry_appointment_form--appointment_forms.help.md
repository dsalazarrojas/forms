# food_pantry_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and record non-profit organization visits to food pantries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the non-profit organization and its name.
2. Enter the name of the person visiting the food pantry.
3. Provide your contact email address (if available).
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose a date for your visit.
6. Choose a time for your visit.
7. Specify the reason for your visit.
8. (Optional) Add any additional comments about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Non-profit Organization** (`non_profit_organization`): Select the non-profit organization that will be visiting the food pantry.
* **Name of Food Pantry** (`name_of_food_pantry`): Enter the name of the food pantry being visited.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person visiting the food pantry.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the contact email address of the person visiting. This is optional.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the person visiting.
* **Date** (`date`): Choose a date for your visit to the food pantry.
* **Time** (`time`): Choose a specific time for your visit to the food pantry.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`): Specify the reason for your visit to the food pantry.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about your visit.
