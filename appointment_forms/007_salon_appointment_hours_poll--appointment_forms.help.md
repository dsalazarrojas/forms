# salon_appointment_hours_poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to poll salons about their available hours of operation. The form asks salons to indicate whether they are open (yes or no), their preferred hours of operation, days of operation, and time of operation. This data will be used to help stylists and customers determine the best time to make an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and select all that apply.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your preferred hours of operation from the list.
4. Choose the days you are available to work.
5. Enter your preferred date and time of operation.
6. Add any additional notes if necessary.
7. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **salon_details** (select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to indicate whether your salon is open or not. Select "Yes" if your salon is currently open, or "No" if it is closed.
* **prefered_hours** (select_one, optional): This field asks you to choose your preferred hours of operation. Select the time slot that best represents your salons hours.
* **prefered_days** (select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the days of the week you are available to work. Choose all days that apply.
* **preferred_time** (select_one, optional): This field asks you to choose your preferred time of operation. Select the time slot that best represents your salons hours.
* **notes** (note, optional): This field is for any additional notes you might have about your salons hours of operation.
* **email** (email, optional): This is your email address that customers and stylists can contact to make appointments.
* **phone** (text, optional): This is your phone number that customers and stylists can contact to make appointments.
* **preferred_date** (date, optional): This field is used to select a specific date you are available to work.
* **preferred_time_ending** (time, optional): This field allows you to select your preferred ending time of operation.

## Tips
Remember to fill out all required fields to ensure your preferences are accurately recorded. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact your administrator.
