# Hair Product Sample Testing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Product Sample Testing Appointment Form is used to schedule a hair product testing appointment. This form helps to capture the necessary information for conducting the test, including the type of hair product to be tested, the tester's information, appointment details, and confirmation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of hair product you want to test (Sample Type, Hair Type, or Product Type).
2.  Choose the person performing the testing (Tester, Tester Name, or Tester Phone).
3.  Select the date of the appointment.
4.  Choose the time of the appointment.
5.  Select the location and staff member involved in the testing (Location, Staff).
6.  Confirm the appointment status (Confirmed, Not Confirmed).
7.  Optionally, add any additional comments or notes about the appointment in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sample Type (Hair Details)** (`form_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of hair product you want to test (Sample Type, Hair Type, or Product Type).
* **Tester Info (`form_3`, `select_one`, required)**: Choose the person performing the testing (Tester, Tester Name, or Tester Phone).
* **Date (`form_4`, `date`, required)**: Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Time (`form_5`, `time`, required)**: Select the time of the appointment.
* **Salon Info (`form_6`, `select_multiple`, required)**: Choose the location and staff member involved in the testing (Location, Staff).
* **Notes (`form_7`, `note`, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the appointment.
* **Confirmation (`form_8`, `select_one`, required)**: Confirm the appointment status (Confirmed, Not Confirmed).
* **Comments (`form_9`, `text`, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the appointment.
