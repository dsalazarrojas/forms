# Modem Change Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Modem Change Appointment Form is a tool used to schedule a specific time with a technician for a customer's appointment. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the date for the appointment in the "Schedule Appointment Date" field.
3. Choose a start time for the appointment in the "Start Time" field.
4. Choose an end time for the appointment in the "End Time" field.
5. Choose a technician's name from the list provided in the "Technician's Name" field.
6. Enter the duration of the appointment in the "Appointment Duration" field.
7. (Optional) Add any additional notes for the appointment in the "Appointment Note" field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter the name of the customer who needs an appointment.
* **Schedule Appointment Date** (Schedule Appointment Date, date, required): Select the date you want to schedule the appointment for.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required): Choose the start time for the appointment. Please use 24-hour format.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required): Choose the end time for the appointment. Please use 24-hour format.
* **Technician's Name** (Technician's Name, select_one, required): Select the name of the technician who will perform the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (Appointment Duration, number, required): Enter the duration of the appointment in minutes.
* **Appointment Note** (Appointment Note, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the appointment.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter the phone number of the customer.
