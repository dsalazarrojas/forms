# new_patient_registration_dental_hygiene_ucba - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new patients for dental hygiene services. It helps healthcare professionals collect necessary data for patient care and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your name and contact information.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Share your medical history with us to ensure we can provide the best care for your specific needs.
4. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
5. Fill in your address and zip code.
6. Finally, provide your email address so we can keep you informed about your appointments and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should be displayed on your records.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for appointment reminders and other important updates.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1995-02-12).
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, note, required): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any allergies, conditions, or previous medical procedures you have had.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of emergency or scheduling conflicts.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Medical History 2** (`Medical History 2`, note, required): Please describe any other important medical history or allergies you may have.
* **Medical History 3** (`Medical History 3`, note, required): Continue describing your medical history or allergies.
* **Medical History 4** (`Medical History 4`, note, required): If you have any other medical conditions or allergies, please add them here.
* **Medical History 5** (`Medical History 5`, note, required): Add any additional medical history or allergies here.
* **Medical History 6** (`Medical History 6`, note, required): This is the last section for medical history, so please include any other important information here.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your address so we can send you appointment reminders and updates.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, number, required): Enter your zip code for the address.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can keep you informed about your appointments and services.
