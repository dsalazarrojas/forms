# Veterinarian Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a pet and its owner to schedule an appointment at the clinic. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient check-in process during the visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section thoroughly, as indicated by the required/optional status of each field.
2.  Select the visit date and time according to your preference.
3.  Enter your pet's name, age, and breed.
4.  Provide your own name and contact information for follow-up.
5.  Add any additional notes about the visit, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet info**: Enter your pet's name.
* **Visit Date**: Select the date you wish to schedule the visit.
* **Visit Time**: Select the time you prefer for the visit.
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Age**: Enter your pet's age.
* **Pet Breed**: Enter your pet's breed.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name, as the pet's owner.
* **Owner Contact**: Enter a phone number or email where you can be reached.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Confirm Visit**: Select all applicable options if any of the following apply:
	+ Pet has been confirmed to visit the clinic.
	+ We will send a reminder 24 hours before the visit.
	+ The pet will be picked up within 30 minutes of the visit.
	+ All of the above.
