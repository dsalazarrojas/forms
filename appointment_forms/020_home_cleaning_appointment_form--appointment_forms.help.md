# Home Cleaning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Cleaning Appointment Form is designed to allow customers to schedule a home cleaning service with a cleaning services company. This form collects necessary information to provide an effective and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Home Cleaning Form** field with a brief description of the areas that need cleaning.
2. Select a date and time from the calendar for the cleaning service to be performed. Please note that the schedule date is required to book the service.
3. Choose the type of cleaning service you require from the **Cleaning Type** dropdown menu.
4. Enter your **Address** for the cleaning service to be performed.
5. If you have a **Phone** number, please provide it so we can contact you to confirm the details.
6. If you have an **Email**, please provide it so we can contact you with updates on your cleaning service.
7. In the **Additional Info** field, provide any additional information you'd like us to know about your cleaning needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Cleaning Form**: A brief description of the areas that need cleaning (e.g., living room, kitchen, bathroom). (Text, Optional)
* **Schedule Date**: The date you would like the cleaning service to be performed. (Date, Required)
* **Schedule Time**: The time you would like the cleaning service to be performed. (Time, Optional)
* **Cleaning Type**: The type of cleaning service you require (e.g., one-time, regular, deep cleaning). (Select One, Optional)
* **Address**: The address where the cleaning service will be performed. (Text, Required)
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number. (Text, Optional)
* **Email**: Your email address. (Email, Optional)
* **Additional Info**: Any additional information you'd like us to know about your cleaning needs. (Note, Optional)

## Tips
* Please ensure that the schedule date is accurate and realistic for the cleaning service.
* Please select a cleaning type that suits your specific needs.
* If you have any special requests, such as specific cleaning products to be used, please provide them in the **Additional Info** field.
* Please note that we will contact you to confirm the details of your cleaning service once we receive this form.
