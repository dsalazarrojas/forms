# hr_policy_review_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "hr_policy_review_appointment_form" is designed to schedule a meeting for policy review and updates with employees. This form helps HR managers to track and document meetings, ensuring accurate and efficient communication with employees on policy changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as per your needs.
2. Start by selecting the "Policy Review Meeting" page, which is the first page of the form.
3. Enter the meeting date and time in the respective fields.
4. Fill in the employee's name and any policy updates you want to discuss.
5. Provide the effective date of the policy update, if applicable.
6. Optionally, specify the meeting location and duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Review Meeting** (`Policy Review Meeting`, text, required): Select this page to begin the meeting scheduling process.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting you want to schedule.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee(s) attending the meeting.
* **Policy Update** (`policy_update`, text, optional): Describe the policy updates or changes you want to discuss during the meeting.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the policy update becomes effective.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of the meeting, if not taking place at the office.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, optional): Enter the expected duration of the meeting.

## Tips
- Make sure to keep the meeting schedule up-to-date and accurate.
- Double-check the meeting date and time with the employee before scheduling.
- Keep the policy updates clear and concise for employees' understanding.
