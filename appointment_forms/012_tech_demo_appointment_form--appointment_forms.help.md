# tech_demo_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tech_demo_appointment_form" is a form designed to collect information for scheduling tech demos. It is intended for users who wish to schedule a demo session, which includes providing personal details and preferred demo time slots.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Demo Details" page with your name and any relevant details about the demo you are interested in.
2. Enter your email address on the "email" field for correspondence and confirmation.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field for further contact.
4. Select a date and time for the demo using the "demo_date" and "demo_time" fields.
5. Choose a time slot from the provided options in the "time_slot" field.
6. Specify the demo location in the "demo_location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Demo Details** (`demo_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description or details about the demo you are interested in. This will help us understand your needs better.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for our records and correspondence.
* **email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address for demo confirmation and follow-up communication.
* **phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **demo_date** (`demo_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for the demo session.
* **demo_time** (`demo_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time slot for the demo.
* **time_slot** (`demo_time_slot`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the provided time slots for your demo.
* **demo_location** (`demo_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location for the demo session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any confusion or delays in the demo process.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements for the demo, include them in the "Demo Details" field.
* You will receive a confirmation email with the demo details after submitting this form.
* If you need to make any changes to your demo details, please contact us directly.
* We will be in touch with you before the demo session to confirm the details.
