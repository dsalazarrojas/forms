# Sauna Use Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sauna Use Waiver form is designed to gather essential information from users prior to using a sauna facility. This form aims to ensure that users have provided the necessary details about themselves and their emergency contacts, in case of any unexpected situations that may arise during or after their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Sauna Use Waiver**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any information you'd like to note about your use of the sauna facility.
2. Complete all required fields. Required fields are marked as "Required" in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
3. If you have any emergency contacts, please fill out the **Contact Information** field.
4. If you have an emergency contact other than a family member or partner, please specify their relationship to you in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
5. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your use of the sauna, please fill out the **Medical Condition** field.
6. If you have any insurance coverage that may be relevant to your use of the sauna, please fill out the **Insurance** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sauna Use Waiver** (sauna_use_waiver): This field is optional. Please provide any information you'd like to note about your use of the sauna facility.
* **Contact Information** (contact_information): This field is optional. Please fill out if you have an emergency contact.
	+ Please include the contact person's name and contact information (e.g., phone number and/or email).
* **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact): This field is optional and required if you have an emergency contact other than a family member or partner.
	+ This field is where you would input the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship): This field is optional and required if you have an emergency contact other than a family member or partner.
	+ Please specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., friend, neighbor, or coworker).
* **Insurance** (insurance): This field is optional. Please include any relevant insurance information you may have.
* **Medical Condition** (medical_condition): This field is optional. Please describe any medical conditions that may impact your use of the sauna.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and clear as possible when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field or have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
- Please review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've completed all required fields.
