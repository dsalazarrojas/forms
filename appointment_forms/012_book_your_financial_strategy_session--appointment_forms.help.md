# Book Your Financial Strategy Session - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your financial situation, goals, and preferences to schedule a personalized financial strategy session with our expert advisors. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that we can tailor the session to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name, email address, and phone number in the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Choose how you would describe your "Current Financial Situation" by selecting one of the provided options.
3. Select your "Annual Income Range" from the provided options, if applicable.
4. Choose all that apply for your "Financial Goals" by selecting multiple options.
5. Indicate your "Primary Financial Concern" in the text field.
6. If you have existing investments, describe them in the text field.
7. Select your "Risk Tolerance" by choosing one of the provided options.
8. Determine your "Time Horizon for Goals" by selecting one of the provided options.
9. Choose your preferred "Session Date" and "Time Slot" from the provided options.
10. Select your preferred "Session Format" (Phone call, Video conference, or In-person).
11. If applicable, describe the "Documents you will bring" to the session.
