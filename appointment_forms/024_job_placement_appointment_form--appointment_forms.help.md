# job_placement_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job placement appointment form is designed to facilitate communication between job seekers and job placement agencies, enabling them to schedule meetings for potential job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the job title from the list of available job titles.
2. Enter your name as the job seeker.
3. Enter the preferred date and time for the appointment.
4. Confirm your availability to work at the selected location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job opening you are interested in.
* **Job Seeker Name** (`job_seeker_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the job seeker.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are looking for full-time employment, "No" if you are looking for part-time or contract work.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to work, such as the city or region.
* **Job Placement Agency** (`job_placement_agency`, text, required): Enter the name of the job placement agency that has offered you this opportunity.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, required): Select the time period you are available to work, such as morning or afternoon.
* **Preferred Date/Time** (`preferred_date_time`, date, required): Enter the date and time that you prefer for the appointment.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately to avoid scheduling conflicts.
- If you are unable to find your job title or agency, contact the relevant agency directly for assistance.
- Review your submission for errors before submitting the form.
