# health-fitness-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your health and fitness concerns, including contact details and specific issues you are facing. Please fill out this form to help our team better understand your situation and provide assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:
- Start by filling in your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
- Describe the date of the issue and the date it started.
- Indicate the area where you are experiencing problems.
- Let us know if you have an advisor or healthcare professional you are working with.
- Provide a brief description of your issue.
- Add any additional information that may be helpful in understanding your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Form Title** (1, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your contact, if applicable.
* **Date of Issue** (2, date, optional): Indicate the date when your health or fitness issue started or when you first became aware of the problem.
* **Date of Report** (3, date, optional): This field is for recording the date when you submitted this report, not the issue date. This will be populated automatically.
* **Problematic Area** (4, text, optional): Describe the specific area where you are experiencing your health or fitness problem (e.g., knee, back, etc.).
* **Advisor** (5, select_one, optional): Let us know if you have an advisor or healthcare professional you are working with. Choose "Yes" if you do.
* **Briefly Describe Your Issue** (6, text, optional): Give a clear and concise description of your health or fitness issue.
* **Additional Information** (7, text, optional): Add any extra details that may help us better understand your situation.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately and completely. Any missing or incorrect information may delay our response and assistance.

This help guide is subject to change based on the form's evolution and any modifications that may be made to it.
