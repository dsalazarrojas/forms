# solar_referral_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic contact information and date of birth from individuals being referred by someone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **2. Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Confirm your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **3. Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you need help, contact: form@gic.mx.
