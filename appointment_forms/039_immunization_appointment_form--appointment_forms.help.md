# Immunization Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and vaccination schedule details for immunization appointments. The form is not intended for high-security or HIPAA compliance, and its use is limited to general patient information collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant patient information.
2. **Vaccination Schedule** (`vaccination_schedule`, select multiple, optional): Select all the vaccinations you are due for. This field allows you to choose multiple options.
3. **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are scheduled for your vaccination(s).
4. **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your scheduled vaccination(s).
5. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or allergies you would like to inform us about.
6. **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of vaccination you are receiving.
7. **Vaccination Details** (`vaccination_details`, note, optional): Write any additional details about your vaccination, such as the location or any specific instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info**: Enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your name and date of birth.
* **Vaccination Schedule**: Select all the vaccinations you are due for, allowing you to choose multiple options.
* **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date you are scheduled for your vaccination(s).
* **Vaccination Time**: Enter the time of your scheduled vaccination(s).
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies you would like to inform us about.
* **Vaccination Type**: Choose the type of vaccination you are receiving.
* **Vaccination Details**: Write any additional details about your vaccination, such as the location or any specific instructions.
