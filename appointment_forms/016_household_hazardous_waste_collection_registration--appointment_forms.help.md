# Household Hazardous Waste Collection Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Hazardous Waste Collection Registration form is used to schedule a pickup for household hazardous waste collection. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure your waste is collected correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to enter all required information.
2. Select the types of hazardous waste you are collecting.
3. Choose a desired pickup date for your waste collection.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about your collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration** (Text Field):
	+ This field is where you enter your name, which will be used to identify you on the collection schedule.
* **Name** (Text Field):
	+ Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the collection schedule.
* **Email** (Email Field):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about your collection.
* **Phone** (Text Field):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Address** (Text Field):
	+ Enter your address where the waste will be collected.
* **Date** (Date Field):
	+ Select the date you would like your waste to be collected.
* **Time** (Time Field):
	+ Select the time of day you would like your waste to be collected.
* **Types of hazardous waste** (Select One Field):
	+ Choose the types of hazardous waste you are collecting (e.g., Gas, Paints, Chemicals, Batteries, Electronics).
* **Desired pickup date** (Date Field):
	+ Select the date you would like your waste to be collected.
* **Additional notes** (Text Field):
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about your collection (optional).

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient collection process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email address entered on this form.
* We will contact you before your scheduled collection date to confirm the details.
* Please note that we will only contact you about your collection, not for any other purpose.
