# Construction Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us schedule a consultation appointment and gather essential information about your construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select a date and time that works best for your consultation appointment.
3. Choose the type of consultation you require, e.g., New Construction or Home Renovation.
4. Provide a brief description of your project.
5. If applicable, mention your estimated project budget and how soon you plan to start the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (2): Enter your full name for us to address you and send updates.
- **Email Address** (3): We'll use this to send confirmation and follow-up emails.
- **Phone Number** (4): Please provide your phone number for easy contact.
- **Preferred Consultation Date** (6): Select a date that works best for you, and we'll do our best to accommodate it.
- **Preferred Consultation Time** (7): Choose a time that suits you best for the consultation.
- **Type of Consultation** (8): Select the type of consultation you require.
- **Project Site Address** (10): Provide the location where the project will take place.
- **Estimated Project Budget ($)** (11): If applicable, give a rough estimate of your project budget.
- **Urgency Level** (12): Indicate how quickly you plan to start the project.
- **Brief Project Description** (13): Give us a brief overview of your project.
- **How did you hear about us?** (14): Let us know where you found out about our company.
- **Additional Comments or Questions** (15): If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them here.

**Tips:**
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in scheduling your consultation.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.
- We will review and follow up on your form submission to confirm your consultation appointment.
