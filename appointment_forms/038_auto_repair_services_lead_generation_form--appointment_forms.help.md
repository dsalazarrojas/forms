## Purpose
The Auto Repair Services Lead Generation Form is designed to help customers provide their contact information and service requirements for repair and maintenance of their vehicles. This form collects essential details such as name, email, phone number, and preferred location or zip code to ensure that the service team can reach out to the customer at the right time and with the right information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure that the service team can contact you.
3. Provide your preferred location or zip code to help us find the nearest service point.
4. Select the primary area of concern for your vehicle (Brakes, Engine, Transmission, Suspension, Electrical, AC and Heating).
5. Briefly describe the issue with your vehicle.
6. Choose a convenient time for the service team to contact you (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
7. Indicate how you found our service (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, Drive-by).
8. If interested, select the option to hear about service financing options.
9. Add any comments or special instructions for the service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (2) (Required): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email Address** (3) (Required): Enter your email address so that we can send you repair quotes and updates.
* **Phone Number** (4) (Required): Enter your best contact number for the service team.
* **Preferred Location or Zip Code** (5) (Required): Provide your preferred location or zip code to help us find the nearest service point.
* **Service Category** (7) (Required): Select the primary area of concern for your vehicle.
* **Briefly Describe the Problem** (8) (Required): Describe the symptoms your car is showing in a brief and clear manner.
* **Best Time for Callback** (9) (Required): Choose a convenient time for the service team to contact you (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **How did you find us?** (10) (Not Required): Helps us understand how you found our service.
* **Interest in Service Financing** (11) (Required): Indicate whether you'd like to hear about our financing options.
* **Comments or Special Instructions** (12) (Not Required): Add any additional comments or special instructions for the service team.
