# personal_ruby_ribbon_fit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "personal_ruby_ribbon_fit_form" is a form used to gather information about a specific type of appointment or form related to Ruby Ribbons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to describe the purpose of the form.
2. Select the category for the appointment or form from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter a brief description of the appointment or form.
4. Choose the assigned tool for the appointment or form.
5. Select the input method for the appointment or form (e.g., in person, phone, or email).
6. Add any additional comments about the appointment or form.
7. Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the appointment or form.
8. Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the appointment or form (if applicable).
9. Enter the form ID (if applicable).
10. Select any additional categories for the appointment or form (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_title** (Text, Required: False): Enter a short title or name for the form.
- **Category** (Select One, Required: False): Choose the category for the appointment or form (e.g., Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire).
- **Description** (Text, Required: False): Enter a brief description of the appointment or form.
- **Assigned Tool** (Text, Required: False): Choose the tool assigned for the appointment or form (e.g., specific equipment or software).
- **Input Method** (Select One, Required: False): Select how the appointment or form will be inputted (e.g., In Person, Phone, Email).
- **Comments** (Note, Required: False): Add any additional comments or notes about the appointment or form.
- **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the appointment or form.
- **Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the appointment or form (if applicable).
- **Form ID** (Text, Required: False): Enter a unique ID for the form (if required).
- **Category 2** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Choose any additional categories for the appointment or form (e.g., Select 1, Select 2, Select 3).
