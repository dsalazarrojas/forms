# Trial Data Meeting Scheduler - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trial Data Meeting Scheduler form is designed to gather essential information from users to schedule meetings effectively. It helps meeting organizers to determine the best time and location for a meeting based on the user's availability and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose a date and time you are available for the meeting.
3. Select the duration of your availability.
4. Choose a preferred time for the meeting (you can select multiple times).
5. Select the location where you would like to have the meeting (currently only New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles are available).
6. If you have specific topics you would like to discuss during the meeting, provide them in the text fields.
7. Submit the form once you have provided all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`username`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Meeting date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the day you are available for the meeting.
- **Meeting time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you are available for the meeting.
- **Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how long you will be available for the meeting (30, 60, or 120 minutes).
- **Preferred meeting time** (`preferred_meeting_time`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the time of day you would like to have the meeting (9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 5:00 PM).
- **Discussion topics** (`discussion_topics`, `text`, required: false): Provide any specific topics you would like to discuss during the meeting.
- **Preferred meeting location** (`meeting_location`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the location where you would like to have the meeting (New York, Chicago, Los Angeles).
- **Discussion topics** (`discussion_topic`, `text`, required: false): This is a repetition of the previous field, providing an alternative text input for discussion topics.
- **Availability notes** (`availability`, `note`, required: false): This field is currently not used in the form, but you can provide any additional information about your availability.
- **Preferred meeting time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is currently not used in the form, and you can ignore it.

## Tips
- Be accurate when selecting your meeting time and location to ensure the meeting is scheduled effectively.
- If you have any specific requests or requirements for the meeting, use the discussion topics field to provide them.
- You can select multiple times for the preferred meeting time to provide flexibility for the meeting organizer.
