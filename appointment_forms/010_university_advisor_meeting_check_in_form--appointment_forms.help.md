# university_advisor_meeting_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about university advisor meetings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
* Enter the meeting details, including time and date.
* Enter the advisor and student information.
* Specify the meeting topic.
* Add any notes from the student and advisor.
* Enter the planned meeting duration.
* Choose the follow-up actions that apply to the meeting.
* Select the date and time for follow-up actions.
* Specify the details for follow-up actions.
* Choose the next meeting option from the available choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including any relevant context or background information.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required): Enter the time the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date the meeting took place.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the advisor who met with the student.
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the meeting topic or discussion.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the student.
* **Advisor Notes** (`advisor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the advisor.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more follow-up actions that apply to the meeting.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): Enter any details for the follow-up action.
* **Next Meeting** (`next_meeting`, select_one, optional): Choose the next meeting option from the available choices.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If a field is marked as optional, it is not required for submission.
* This form is designed to be a regular data collection tool, so make sure to complete it for each meeting.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or the system administrator for clarification.
