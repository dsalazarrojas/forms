# pet_examination_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for scheduling a pet examination. It's crucial to fill it out accurately to ensure the examination process runs smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to enter your details in the correct format (e.g., email addresses should be in the format `username@domain.com`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_examination_form_input** (`pet_examination_form_input`, text, required):
Enter a description of the examination to be performed (e.g., routine check-up, surgery preparation).
* **examination_notes** (`examination_notes`, text, not required):
Add any additional notes or comments about the examination (e.g., special instructions or concerns).
* **veterinarian_signature** (`veterinarian_signature`, note, not required):
Leave space for the veterinarian to sign and date this form.
* **veterinarian_name** (`veterinarian_name`, text, required):
Enter the name of the veterinarian performing the examination.
* **veterinarian_phone** (`veterinarian_phone`, text, not required):
Enter the phone number of the veterinarian (for follow-up or emergency contact).
* **veterinarian_email** (`veterinarian_email`, email, not required):
Enter the email address of the veterinarian (for follow-up or communication).
* **practice_name** (`practice_name`, text, required):
Enter the name of the veterinary practice or clinic.
* **practice_address** (`practice_address`, text, required):
Enter the address of the veterinary practice or clinic.
* **practice_phone** (`practice_phone`, text, required):
Enter the phone number of the veterinary practice or clinic.
* **practice_email** (`practice_email`, email, required):
Enter the email address of the veterinary practice or clinic.
* **practice_license** (`practice_license`, text, not required):
Enter the veterinary practice's license number (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
- Make sure to keep this form up-to-date with any changes to the examination or practice details.
