# Appointment Schedule Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Appointment Schedule Template" is a form used to schedule appointments for clients, patients, or customers. It captures key information such as name, email, phone number, and scheduling details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the "Appointment Type" from the dropdown menu in the "Appointment Type" field.
5. Choose the "Appointment Date" and "Start Time" in their respective fields.
6. Choose the "End Time" of the appointment.
7. Enter the "Meeting Location" (if applicable) and "Purpose of Appointment" fields.
8. Optionally, specify the number of "Additional Attendees" and provide any "Special Requirements or Setup Needs".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of appointment you are scheduling.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the appointment.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Choose the start time of the appointment.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Choose the end time of the appointment.
* **Meeting Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): If the appointment is at a specific location, enter it here.
* **Purpose of Appointment** (`purpose`, `text`, required): Describe the purpose of the appointment.
* **Number of Additional Attendees** (`additional_attendees`, `number`, optional): If there are other people attending the appointment, enter the number here.
* **Special Requirements or Setup Needs** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If there are any special requirements or setup needs for the appointment, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's label or ask your contact person for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by the individual scheduling the appointment. If you need to schedule for someone else, please adjust the form accordingly.
