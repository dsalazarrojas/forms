# Meeting Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Availability Survey is a form designed to collect information about meeting availability from users. This form is intended to be used in a variety of contexts, such as scheduling meetings, appointments, or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of your meeting or appointment in the "start_date" field.
2. Enter the end date of your meeting or appointment in the "end_date" field.
3. Enter the start time of your meeting or appointment in the "start_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_date (Date)**: Enter the date you are available to meet or schedule an appointment. (Required: false)
* **end_date (Date)**: Enter the date you are available to meet or schedule an appointment. (Required: false)
* **start_time (Time)**: Enter the time you are available to meet or schedule an appointment. (Required: false)

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate dates and times to ensure that your availability is accurately reflected.
* If you have any conflicts or unavailability during the specified dates and times, please let us know in the comments section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
