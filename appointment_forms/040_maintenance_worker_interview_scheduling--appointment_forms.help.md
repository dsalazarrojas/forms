# Maintenance Worker Interview Scheduling - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to schedule interviews with maintenance workers who will perform various tasks in a facility.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the facility from the dropdown list where the maintenance worker will be performing the task. Make sure to choose the correct facility.
2. Enter the name of the candidate (maintenance worker) being scheduled.
3. Choose the date of the interview from the calendar. This is the date you want to schedule the interview for.
4. Choose the start and end times of the interview.
5. Enter the name of the facility where the interview will take place.
6. Enter the location of the interview within the facility.
7. Enter the phone number of the candidate (maintenance worker) if available.
8. Enter the email of the candidate (maintenance worker) if available.
9. Enter the address of the candidate (maintenance worker) if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility** (`facility_id`, `select_one`, required): Select the facility where the maintenance worker will be performing the task.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the maintenance worker being scheduled.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the interview from the calendar.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Choose the start time of the interview.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Choose the end time of the interview.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the facility where the interview will take place.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the interview within the facility.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the candidate (maintenance worker) if available.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the candidate (maintenance worker) if available.
* **Candidate Address** (`candidate_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the candidate (maintenance worker) if available.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the facility name and location to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that the interview dates and times are correct to avoid conflicts.
* Use the email address if you have it for easy follow-up communication with the candidate.
* This form should not be used for data analytics or reporting; use it for scheduling interviews only.
