# Kidney Clinic Doctor Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information and schedule appointments at the Kidney Clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Patient Information page with your details.
2. Enter your Contact Number for us to contact you.
3. Select your preferred contact method (Phone call, Email, or Message).
4. Choose the type of appointment you need (New Patient Visit, Follow-up Visit, or Routine Checkup).
5. Select the date and time for your appointment.
6. If you have any comments, please provide them in the Patient Comment field.
7. Finally, our doctor will input their comments after reviewing your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Patient Information)**: This page is where you enter your basic patient information.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)**: Enter your phone number or email address so we can contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method (Preferred Contact Method)**: Choose how you want to be contacted (Phone call, Email, or Message).
* **Appointment Type (Appointment Type)**: Select the type of appointment you need (New Patient Visit, Follow-up Visit, or Routine Checkup).
* **Selected Date (Selected Date)**: Choose the date for your appointment.
* **Selected Time (Selected Time)**: Choose the time for your appointment.
* **Doctor Name (Doctor Name)**: Not used by patients, this field is meant for our doctors to input their name.
* **Patient Comment (Patient Comment)**: If you have any comments or questions, please enter them here.
* **Doctor Comment (Doctor Comment)**: Not used by patients, this field is meant for our doctors to input their comments.

## Tips
- Please fill the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to check the appointment date and time for any conflicts.
- Our team will review your form and get back to you within 24-48 hours.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to call us.
