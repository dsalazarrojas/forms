# 12 Week Appointment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 12 Week Appointment Checklist is a form designed to help track progress and plan follow-up appointments with clients in a 12-week program. This form collects client information, assesses progress, and outlines next steps and goals for upcoming weeks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and client ID number.
2. Choose the appointment date and time.
3. Select the week number in the 12-week program.
4. Assess the client's progress by rating their goal achievement and motivation level.
5. Identify any challenges faced by the client and select all that apply.
6. Record any physical metrics changes.
7. Outline the interventions and support provided to the client.
8. Specify any education topics covered and homework assigned.
9. Determine if additional support is needed.
10. Schedule the next appointment date and time.
11. Rate client satisfaction with the current services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the client.
* **Client ID number** (`client_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the client.
* **Appointment date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the current appointment.
* **Appointment time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the appointment.
* **Week number in program** (`week_number`, integer, required): Select the current week of the 12-week program.
* **Goals achieved this week** (`goal_achievement`, select_one, required): Rate achievement of planned goals by selecting one of the following options:
	+ Exceeded expectations
	+ Fully met goals
	+ Partially met goals
	+ Did not meet goals
	+ No goals set
* **Motivation level** (`motivation_level`, integer, required): Rate the client's motivation level on a scale from 1 (very low) to 10 (very high).
* **Challenges faced this week** (`challenges_faced`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Time constraints
	+ Lack of resources
	+ Low energy
	+ External stressors
	+ Motivation issues
	+ Physical limitations
	+ Social factors
* **Physical metrics change** (`physical_metrics`, text, optional): Record any physical metrics changes, such as weight or measurements.
* **Interventions applied** (`interventions_applied`, text, optional): List the techniques or strategies used to support the client.
* **Education topics covered** (`education_provided`, text, optional): Outline the topics discussed with the client.
* **Homework or tasks assigned** (`homework_assigned`, text, optional): Specify any activities or tasks assigned to the client.
* **Additional support needed** (`support_needed`, select_one, optional): Determine if additional support is needed by selecting one of the following options:
	+ None needed
	+ Nutritionist consultation
	+ Mental health support
	+ Specialist referral
	+ Equipment adjustment
	+ Family involvement
* **Next appointment date** (`next_appointment_date`, date, required): Choose the scheduled date for the next appointment.
* **Next appointment time** (`next_appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the scheduled time for the next appointment.
* **Priority goals for next week** (`priority_goals_next_week`, text, optional): Outline the key objectives for the upcoming week.
* **Client satisfaction** (`client_satisfaction`, integer, required): Rate the client's satisfaction with the current services on a scale from 1 (very low) to 10 (very high).
* **Additional notes or observations** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Record any other relevant information.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate tracking of client progress.
* Use the form to identify areas where the client may need additional support or resources.
* Communicate with the client to discuss progress, goals, and challenges.
* Schedule follow-up appointments based on the client's needs and progress.
* Review and adjust the form as needed to suit your specific use case.
