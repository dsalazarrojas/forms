# Dryer Queue Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register users for a dryer queue, allowing them to select their preferred time slot and receive an update on their availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Select your preferred time slot in the "time-slot" field.
3. Optionally, select one or more labels from the "dryer_queue" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone-number" field.
5. Confirm your password in the "confirm-password" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Click on the "submit" button to register for the dryer queue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user-info (user_info)**: A field to collect user information, which is currently optional. Please enter your details as prompted.
* **join (dryer_queue)**: A field to select one or more labels to indicate your interest in joining the dryer queue. Choose one or more labels that best describe your preferences.
* **time-slot (time_slot)**: A field to select your preferred time slot for the dryer queue. Choose from the options 8:00 AM, 9:00 AM, or 10:00 AM.
* **phone-number (phone)**: A field to enter your phone number.
* **confirm-password (confirm-password)**: A field to confirm your password. Please enter the same password you used when prompted earlier.
* **email (email)**: A field to enter your email address.
* **submit (submit)**: This is the submit button to complete your registration. Please review all fields before clicking this button.
