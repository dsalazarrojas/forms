# energy_efficiency_consultation_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an energy efficiency consultation appointment with our team. Please fill out the form with your details to schedule a consultation that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your address.
5. Choose the type of property you want to consult about.
6. Specify the size of your property.
7. Schedule the consultation by selecting a date and time that suits you.
8. If you have any preferred time for the consultation, please let us know.
9. Provide your consent for the consultation (you can type 'yes' or 'no' in the text area).
10. Once you've submitted the form, it will be reviewed and a consultation time will be arranged with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your full address to help us locate your property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select one, required/optional): Choose the type of property you want to consult about.
	+ House
	+ Apartment
	+ Office
	+ Other
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the approximate size of your property in square meters or square feet.
* **Schedule Consultation** (`schedule_consultation`, date, required/optional): Select a date and time that suits you for the consultation.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required/optional): If you have a preferred time for the consultation, please let us know.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required/optional): Please type 'yes' or 'no' to confirm your consent for the consultation.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, note, required/optional): This field will be automatically populated once you submit the form.
