## Digital Advertising Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Advertising Appointment Form is a tool used to schedule appointments for digital advertising services. This form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their scheduling preferences and requirements for meetings or consultations about their digital advertising needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
   - **Client**: Enter the name of the client.
   - **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the client.
   - **Email**: Enter the email of the client.

2. Select the desired appointment type: 
   - Choose the type of appointment that suits your needs: 
     - **Phone**: A scheduled call to discuss digital advertising services.
     - **Video**: A video call or online meeting.
     - **In Person**: A meeting in person.

3. Optional fields: 
   - **Notes**: Provide any additional information about the appointment, such as specific services needed or any special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client**: Enter the name of the client.
  - Required field to identify the client for whom the appointment is being scheduled.
- **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the client.
  - Required field to ensure you can reach out to the client before the appointment.
- **Email**: Enter the email of the client.
  - Required field to communicate with the client before and after the appointment.
- **Time**: Enter the desired time for the appointment.
  - Optional field to schedule a specific time for the appointment. 
- **Date**: Enter the desired date for the appointment.
  - Optional field to schedule a specific date for the appointment.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the appointment.
  - Optional field to add any specific services or requests.
- **Appointment Type**: Choose the type of appointment that suits your needs.
  - Optional field to select the type of meeting or consultation.
