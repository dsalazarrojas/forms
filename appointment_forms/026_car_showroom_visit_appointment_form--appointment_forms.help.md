# Car Showroom Visit Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you plan and schedule a visit to our showrooms, ensuring that our sales team can provide the best possible experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Your name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select your preferred visit date and time in the "Preferred visit date" and "Preferred visit time" fields.
5. Choose how flexible you are with your time in the "How flexible is your time?" field.
6. Specify which vehicles you are interested in by typing in the "Which vehicles are you interested in?" field.
7. Select the type of vehicle you are looking for from the options provided in the "Preferred vehicle type" field.
8. Choose your budget range from the options provided in the "Budget range" field.
9. Indicate if you have a vehicle to trade-in by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Do you have a vehicle to trade in?" field.
10. Check if you are interested in financing options by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Interested in financing options?" field.
11. Select your preferred salesperson (if desired) in the "Salesperson preference" field.
12. Choose your preferred vehicle type (new or used) in the "New or used vehicle?" field.
13. If desired, specify up to 3 most important features in the "Most important features" field.
14. Indicate if you are interested in a test drive by selecting "True", "Maybe", or "False" in the "Interested in a test drive?" field.
15. If you are interested in a test drive, specify which vehicles you would like to test drive in the "Which vehicles to test drive" field.
16. Select the reason for your visit from the options provided in the "Reason for visit" field.
17. Choose your preferred contact method from the options provided in the "Preferred contact method" field.
18. Indicate the number of people who will be visiting with you in the "How many people will be visiting?" field.
19. If you have any special requests or needs, type them in the "Special requests or needs" field.
20. Check if you have visited our showroom before by selecting "True", "False", or "First time" in the "Have you visited our showroom before?" field.
21. Indicate if this visit is a vehicle emergency/urgent need by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Is this a vehicle emergency/urgent need?" field.
22. Select how you heard about us from the options provided in the "How did you hear about us?" field.
23. If you have any additional information or questions, type them in the "Any additional information or questions?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name to be able to schedule a visit and have a personal sales experience.
* **Email address** (`visitor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your visit and follow up on any further questions or offers.
* **Phone number** (`visitor_phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute updates or offers.
* **Preferred visit date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to visit us, and we will do our best to accommodate your request.
* **Preferred visit time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose the time that works best for you to visit our showrooms.
* **How flexible is your time?** (`time_flexibility`, select_one, optional): Indicate how flexible you are with your visit time to help us schedule your visit efficiently.
* **Which vehicles are you interested in?** (`vehicle_interest`, text, required): Type the specific vehicles you are interested in so we can have them ready for you during your visit.
* **Preferred vehicle type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of vehicle you are looking for, either new or used.
* **Budget range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select your budget range to help us narrow down the vehicles we show you.
* **Do you have a vehicle to trade in?** (`trade_in_vehicle`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have a vehicle to trade-in, and we will be able to provide a trade-in value assessment.
* **Interested in financing options?** (`financing_interest`, select_one, optional): Check if you are interested in financing options, and we will be able to provide further guidance.
* **Salesperson preference** (`salesperson_preference`, text, optional): If you have a preferred salesperson, you can indicate their name here.
* **New or used vehicle?** (`new_or_used`, select_one, required): Choose if you are looking for a new or used vehicle.
* **Most important features** (`features_important`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to 3 features that are most important to you, such as fuel efficiency or performance.
* **Interested in a test drive?** (`test_drive_interest`, select_one, optional): Check if you are interested in a test drive, and we will be able to arrange one for you.
* **Which vehicles to test drive?** (`test_drive_vehicles`, text, optional): If you are interested in a test drive, specify which vehicles you would like to test drive.
* **Reason for visit** (`appointment_reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your visit, so we can tailor our sales experience to your needs.
* **Preferred contact method** (`contact_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method, be it phone, email, or text message.
* **How many people will be visiting?** (`group_size`, number, optional): Indicate the number of people who will be visiting with you.
* **Special requests or needs?** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or needs, please specify them here.
* **Have you visited our showroom before?** (`previous_customer`, select_one, optional): Check if this is your first visit to our showroom.
* **Is this a vehicle emergency/urgent need?** (`vehicle_emergency`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this visit is a vehicle emergency/urgent need.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about us, so we can track our marketing efforts.
* **Any additional information or questions?** (`additional_information`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or questions, please type them in here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that we can tailor our sales experience to your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask us for clarification.
* We will do our best to accommodate your preferred visit date and time, but please be flexible with your expectations.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "Special requests or needs" field.
