# Team Collaboration Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for teams to collaborate and plan meetings with ease. It allows you to schedule and organize meetings with teammates, keeping everyone on the same page. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured and comprehensive way to plan meetings, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your name in the **user_name** field, which is optional but helps us understand who is submitting the form.
2. Select a date for the meeting in the **meeting_date** field. This is also optional but helps us plan ahead.
3. Choose a time for the meeting in the **meeting_time** field. Like meeting date, this field is optional.
4. Indicate the duration of the meeting in the **meeting_duration** field. This field is also optional and allows you to plan for the meeting's length.
5. In the **attendees** field, select who will be attending the meeting. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
6. Provide a brief description of the meeting in the **meeting_subject** field. This is a required field and helps us understand the meeting's purpose.
7. Enter the location of the meeting in the **meeting_location** field. This field is optional but helps us understand where the meeting will take place.
8. Enter the start and end dates for the meeting in the **start_date** and **end_date** fields, respectively. These fields are optional.
9. Choose the start and end times for the meeting in the **start_time** and **end_time** fields, respectively. These fields are optional.
10. Add any additional notes about the meeting in the **meeting_note** field. This field is optional.
11. Enter the email addresses to receive reminders about the meeting in the **reminder_emails** field. This field is optional.
12. Enter phone numbers to receive reminder calls about the meeting in the **reminder_phones** field. This field is optional.
13. Choose whether the meeting will be one-time, repeated, or recurring in the **meeting_recurrence** field. You can select from "once", "repeat", or "recurring".
14. Enter the user who will organize the meeting in the **user_organizer** field. This field is optional.
15. Enter the name of the meeting's organizer in the **meeting_organizer** field. This field is optional.
16. Choose whether the meeting is a "Yes" or "No" meeting in the **meeting_type** field. This field is optional.
17. Enter a brief topic about the meeting in the **meeting_topic** field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_name (Text)**: Enter your name for reference.
* **meeting_date (Date)**: Select the date of the meeting.
* **meeting_time (Time)**: Select the time of the meeting.
* **meeting_duration (Number)**: Enter the duration of the meeting.
* **attendees (Select Multiple)**: Select who will attend the meeting.
* **meeting_subject (Text)**: Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting.
* **meeting_location (Text)**: Enter the location of the meeting.
* **start_date (Date)**: Enter the start date of the meeting.
* **start_time (Time)**: Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **end_date (Date)**: Enter the end date of the meeting.
* **end_time (Time)**: Enter the end time of the meeting.
* **meeting_note (Note)**: Add any additional notes about the meeting.
* **reminder_emails (Text)**: Enter email addresses to receive reminders.
* **reminder_phones (Text)**: Enter phone numbers to receive reminders.
* **meeting_recurrence (Select One)**: Choose the recurrence of the meeting.
* **user_organizer (Number)**: Enter the user who will organize the meeting.
* **meeting_organizer (Text)**: Enter the name of the meeting's organizer.
* **meeting_type (Select One)**: Choose whether the meeting is a "Yes" or "No" meeting.
* **meeting_topic (Text)**: Briefly describe the meeting's topic.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selections before confirming the meeting details.
* Use the "Select Multiple" option for attendees to ensure all necessary team members are included.
* Be as specific as possible when entering meeting dates and times to avoid confusion.
* Use the "meeting_note" field to add any important details that may not fit elsewhere on the form.
