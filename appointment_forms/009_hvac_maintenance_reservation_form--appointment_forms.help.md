# hvc_maintenance_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hvc_maintenance_reservation_form is a tool for customers to make reservations for maintenance services. This form collects essential details for the reservation process, ensuring seamless and efficient maintenance services are provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your reservation details in the "Reservation Details" field on the first page.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Customer Contact Information" field on the second page.
3. Select your preferred date and time from the available options in the "Date and Time" field on the second page.
4. Choose the technicians you would like to be assigned to your maintenance task in the "Select Technician" field on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reservation Details
* **Reservation Details (Page 1)**: This is the first page of the form where you can enter your reservation details. Please provide a brief description of the maintenance service you require.
* **Customer Contact Information (Page 2)**: On this page, please enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us contact you to confirm your reservation and provide updates.
* **Date and Time (Page 2)**: Select the date and time that works best for you from the available options. This will ensure we can schedule the maintenance service accordingly.
* **Select Technician (Page 3)**: On this page, choose the technicians you would like to be assigned to your maintenance task. You can select one or multiple technicians from the available options. This will help us assign the right technician for your specific needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure we can reach out to you for any updates or concerns.
* Please select a date and time that is realistic and suitable for your schedule.
* Choose technicians with expertise in the area of maintenance you require.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please include them in the "Reservation Details" field.
