# Lash_Curl_Style_Consultation_Appointment_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate a consultation process for clients in the Lash and Curl industry, allowing them to provide their details and preferences before their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully and provide the requested information accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields, but note that no fields are currently marked as required.
3. Double-check that you have provided all necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Details (1)
**Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for the client to provide any additional details they would like to share about themselves. Please enter any relevant information, such as medical conditions or allergies, to ensure we can better serve you.

### 2. Preferences (2)
**Preferences** (`preferences`, `text`, optional): In this field, please describe your preferences for the consultation, such as desired services or special requests. Be as specific as possible to help us tailor the appointment to your needs.

### 3. Consent (3)
**Consent** (`consent`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to provide your consent for the services you are requesting. Please indicate your understanding and agreement with our policies and procedures.

### 4. Schedule Consultation (4)
**Schedule Consultation** (`schedule_consultation`, `text`, optional): Here, you can indicate your preferred dates and times for the consultation. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure we can accommodate your schedule efficiently.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate and clear responses to each field to facilitate a smooth consultation process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We value your time and will do our best to accommodate your schedule preferences.
