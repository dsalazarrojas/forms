# Agent Appointment Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Appointment Notification Form is used to collect essential information for scheduling appointments with clients. This form helps agents and clients stay organized and on schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Agent name**, which is the name of the agent assigned to the appointment.
2.  Enter the **Client name**, which is the name of the client or customer.
3.  Select the **Appointment date**, which is the date of the appointment.
4.  Select the **Appointment time**, which is the time of the appointment.
5.  Enter the **Appointment location**, which may be a physical location or virtual meeting details.
6.  Choose the **Appointment type**, which should reflect the purpose of the meeting (e.g., consultation, follow-up, inspection, or other).
7.  Enter the **Contact phone**, which is the phone number for the client on the day of the appointment.
8.  Provide any **Special instructions**, which may include access or preparation requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the agent assigned to this appointment.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the client or customer.
* **Appointment date** (`appointment_date`, date, **required**): Select the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment time** (`appointment_time`, time, **required**): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Appointment location** (`appointment_location`, text, **required**): Enter the location of the appointment (physical or virtual).
* **Appointment type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of appointment (e.g., consultation, follow-up, inspection, or other).
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number for the client on the day of the appointment.
* **Special instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, **optional**): Enter any special instructions or requirements for the appointment.

## Tips
* Ensure that the agent name and client name are accurate and correct.
* Double-check the appointment date and time to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* Be specific when providing special instructions, as they may impact the meeting's success.
* Confirm that the client has received and accepted the appointment details before the meeting.
