# Fingerprinting Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a fingerprinting quote request. It is intended for customers who are interested in receiving a quote for fingerprinting services. The form helps us understand their service needs and preferences, which will be used to provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as follows:
   * Service Details: Provide a detailed description of the service you require.
   * Schedule Preferences: Choose your preferred schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
   * Schedule Date and Time: Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the service.
   * Schedule Note: Add any additional notes or instructions for the schedule.
   * Email and Phone: Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach you for any further questions or to provide the quote.
2. If you have any additional information or notes, you can add them in the "Note" field.
3. Review your information and submit the form to initiate the quote process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Details** (Required): Provide a clear and concise description of the service you require. This will help us understand your needs and provide a more accurate quote.
* **Schedule Preferences** (Required): Choose your preferred schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) for the service.
	+ **Morning**: Typically, 8am-12pm
	+ **Afternoon**: Typically, 12pm-4pm
	+ **Evening**: Typically, 4pm-8pm
* **Schedule Date** (Required): Enter the date you would like to schedule the service.
* **Schedule Time** (Required): Enter the time you would like to schedule the service.
* **Schedule Note** (Required): Add any additional notes or instructions for the schedule.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further questions or to provide the quote.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you via phone.
* **Note** (Optional): Add any additional information or notes about your service requirements.
* **Service Details 2** (Optional): If you have additional service details, you can provide them here.
* **Schedule Preferences 2** (Optional): Choose your preferred schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) for any additional services.
* **Fingerprinting Type 2** (Optional): Choose the type of fingerprinting service you require (Yes or No).
* **Schedule Date 2** (Optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule any additional services.
* **Schedule Time 2** (Optional): Enter the time you would like to schedule any additional services.
* **Schedule Note 2** (Optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for the schedule of any additional services.
* **Email 2** (Optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further questions or to provide quotes for additional services.
* **Phone 2** (Optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you via phone for any additional services.
* **Note 2** (Optional): Add any additional information or notes about any additional services.
* **Additional Info** (Optional): If you have any additional information or notes, you can provide them here.
* **Service Details 3** (Optional): If you have additional service details, you can provide them here.
* **Service Details 4** (Optional): If you have additional service details, you can provide them here.
* **Service Details 5** (Optional): If you have additional service details, you can provide them here.
* **Additional Info 2** (Optional): If you have additional information or notes, you can provide them here.
* **Additional Info 3** (Optional): If you have additional information or notes, you can provide them here.

Please note that all the fields with "2" at the end are for additional services or information, and are optional.
