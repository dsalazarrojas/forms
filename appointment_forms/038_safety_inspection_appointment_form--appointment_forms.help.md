# Safety Inspection Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and manage safety inspections by providing essential information for the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the relevant fields as required.
2. Choose the correct options for the inspection type and frequency.
3. Enter the start and end times, location, and date of the inspection.
4. Add any additional contact information for the inspector.
5. Provide any additional notes for the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (1)**: This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled and cannot be changed.
* **Inspection Information (2)**: This field is for entering information about the inspector. It is not a required field and can be left blank.
* **Start Time (3)**: This is the time when the inspection is scheduled to start. The format for entering the time is MM:HH:SS.
* **End Time (4)**: This is the time when the inspection is scheduled to end. The format for entering the time is MM:HH:SS.
* **Location (5)**: This is the physical location where the inspection will take place. Enter the full address or a brief description of the location.
* **Safety Inspection Type (6)**: This field allows you to select the type of inspection that will be conducted. The options are:
	+ Fire Safety
	+ Hazardous Materials
	+ Ergonomic
	+ Ergonomics
* **Inspection Frequency (7)**: This field allows you to select how often the inspection will be conducted. The options are:
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
* **Date (8)**: This is the date when the inspection will take place. Enter in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Info (9)**: This is the phone number and email of the inspector. This field is not required but recommended.
* **Additional Notes (10)**: This is an optional field for adding any additional information about the inspection.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the inspection can be scheduled correctly.
- Use the correct format for entering the start and end times, date, and location to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field explanations above or seek assistance from the facility management team.
