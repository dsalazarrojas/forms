# Nail Polish Collection Preview Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients schedule appointments for a nail polish collection preview at a salon. It includes fields to confirm the appointment, select preferences and options, and communicate with the salon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Preview Session" you would like to attend.
2. Choose your preferred "Date" and "Time" for the appointment.
3. Select the "Nail Artist" you would like to work with.
4. Add any specific "Client Preferences" or instructions in the text field.
5. Choose multiple "Nail Polish Preferences" for your appointment.
6. Enter your "Client Phone" and "Client Email" for communication purposes.
7. Add any important "Appointment Notes" in the text field.
8. Confirm or cancel the appointment using "Salon Confirmation" and "Client Confirmation" fields.
9. If you need to cancel the appointment, select a "Cancellation Reason" from the dropdown menu.
10. If you need to reschedule, select "Reschedule Appointment" and provide the "Reschedule Reason".
11. Record your "Client Signature" and "Salon Signature" in the respective fields.
12. Mark the "Appointment Status" and "Reschedule Status" accordingly.
13. If applicable, record the "Confirmation Date", "Confirmation Time", "Cancellation Date", and "Cancellation Time".
14. Add any additional "Notes" if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preview Session**: Select the preferred preview session for your appointment.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Time**: Choose the time for your appointment.
* **Nail Artist**: Select the nail artist you would like to work with.
* **Client Preferences**: Add any specific preferences or instructions for your appointment.
* **Nail Polish Preferences**: Select multiple nail polishes for your appointment.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Appointment Notes**: Add any important notes about the appointment.
* **Salon Confirmation**: Confirm or cancel the appointment with the salon.
* **Client Confirmation**: Confirm or cancel the appointment with the client.
* **Cancellation Reason**: Select the reason for canceling the appointment (if applicable).
* **Reschedule Appointment**: Reschedule your appointment if needed.
* **Reschedule Reason**: Provide the reason for rescheduling the appointment (if applicable).
* **Client Signature**: Record your signature to confirm the appointment.
* **Salon Signature**: Record the salon's signature to confirm the appointment.
* **Appointment Status**: Mark the appointment status as confirmed, not confirmed, or cancelled.
* **Reschedule Status**: Mark the reschedule status as confirmed, not confirmed, or cancelled.
* **Confirmation Date**: Record the confirmation date and time.
* **Confirmation Time**: Record the confirmation time.
* **Cancellation Date**: Record the cancellation date and time.
* **Cancellation Time**: Record the cancellation time.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the appointment.
