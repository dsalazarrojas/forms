# life coaching session booking contact form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from users who wish to book a life coaching session. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant details to ensure a successful and productive coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the booking request form (page 1).
2. Enter your name, email address, phone number, preferred date, time, and session length.
3. Select your preferred coach and the services you require.
4. Write a brief message to provide additional context or information about your session requirements.
5. Review your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Request**: Please describe the reason you wish to book a life coaching session. This can be a brief overview of your goals or concerns. (Required: 1/10)
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required: 1/10)
* **Email**: Provide your valid email address for communication. (Required: 1/10)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication. (Required: 1/10)
* **Date**: Choose the date you wish to book the coaching session. (Required: 1/10)
* **Time**: Select the preferred time for the coaching session. (Required: 1/10)
* **Session Length**: Please select the duration of the coaching session. (Required: 1/10)
* **Coach Name**: Choose from the list of available coaches. (Required: 1/10)
* **Services**: Select the services you require from the options provided. (Required: 1/10)
* **Message**: Write a brief message to provide additional context or information about your session requirements. (Required: 1/10)

## Tips
* Please ensure you submit accurate and truthful information to avoid delays or cancellation of your session.
* Review your form carefully before submission to ensure you've entered all necessary details.
* If you have any specific requests or concerns, please include them in the "Message" field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for clarification.
