# Vocational Counseling Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for vocation counseling appointments. Please fill out this form to schedule and confirm appointments with our vocation counselors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information page with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. On the Schedule Appointment page, select a time slot that works for you and our vocation counselor.
3. On the Counselor Information page, select a counselor that you would like to work with.
4. On the Notes page, add any additional comments or questions you have for the counselor.
5. Finally, enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you to confirm your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details about yourself.
* **Schedule Appointment (Schedule Appointment)** (`schedule_appointment`, text, optional): Select a time slot that works for you and our vocation counselor. Please note that these time slots are subject to change.
* **Counselor Information (Counselor Information)** (`counselor_info`, text, optional): Choose a counselor that you would like to work with from our available counselors.
* **Select Appointment Time (Select Appointment Time)** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Choose a specific time for your appointment.
* **Select Appointment Date (Select Appointment Date)** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Choose a specific date for your appointment.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have for the counselor.
* **Email (E-mail)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
