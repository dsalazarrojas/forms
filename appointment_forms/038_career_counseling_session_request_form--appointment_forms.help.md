# Career Counseling Session Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from individuals seeking career counseling sessions. It's a straightforward template that helps facilitate the process of booking a session. Please read through each section to understand how to fill it out properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions for each field:

1. Fill out your name in the "Requester name" field.
2. Provide your valid email address in the "Email" field for booking confirmation.
3. Enter up to three preferred dates for the session in the "Preferred dates" field.
4. Specify the time window for your sessions in the "Preferred time" field.
5. Clearly state the objectives you'd like to work on during the session in the "Session objectives" field.
6. If you have a specific counselor in mind, enter their name in the "Counselor preference" field.
7. If you have any special requirements or accessibility needs, type them in the "Special requirements" field.
8. Add any additional information or notes regarding your session request in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Session Request --** (`request_header`): This is an informational header section, indicating you are starting the form.
*   **Requester name** (`requester_name`): Please type your full name here for us to address you correctly.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address for session booking confirmation and further communication.
*   **Preferred dates** (`preferred_dates`): Provide up to three dates you prefer for the session. This will help us find a convenient time slot.
*   **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`): Specify a time window for your session, and we'll do our best to accommodate it.
*   **Session objectives** (`session_objectives`): Clearly state what you want to work on during the session, so we can tailor it to your needs.
*   **Session length preference** (`session_length_preference`): If you have a preference for the session length, choose from 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
*   **Counselor preference** (`counselor_preference`): If you have a specific counselor in mind, type their name here.
*   **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`): If you need any accessibility or accommodation assistance, please mention it here.
*   **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`): Use this field for any other information you'd like to share with the session organizers.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays or cancellations.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us for support.
- We'll do our best to accommodate your preferred dates and time slots.
