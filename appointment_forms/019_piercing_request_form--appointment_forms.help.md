This form is designed to collect information from clients about their piercing requests. The form collects contact information, appointment details, and payment method preferences from the client. This form should only be filled out by clients who are requesting a piercing service. Filling out this form is the first step in the piercing process, and the information collected will be used to schedule an appointment and process payment.

# Piercing_Request_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piercing Request Form is used to collect information from clients about their piercing requests, including contact details, appointment preferences, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
- Select the type of piercing you are requesting.
- Choose the area of the body where you want the piercing to be located.
- Enter the date and time you are available for the appointment.
- Specify the duration of the appointment.
- Confirm your consent for the piercing service.
- Choose your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Info** (`client_info`): This field is for your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please enter your real name, email address, and phone number accurately.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): This field is not currently used in the form and can be ignored.
- **Piercing Info** (`piercing_info`): This field is for you to specify the type of piercing you are requesting (e.g., Cartilage, Eyebrow, Helix).
- **Appointment Info** (`appointment_info`): This field is for you to specify the date, time, and duration of your appointment.
	+ **Date** (`appointment_info_date`): Enter the date you are available for the appointment.
	+ **Time** (`appointment_info_time`): Enter the time you are available for the appointment.
	+ **Duration** (`appointment_info_duration`): Specify the length of the appointment in minutes.
- **Consent** (`consent`): Confirm your consent for the piercing service by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit/Debit Card, PayPal).
- **Name** (`client_info_name`): Enter your real name accurately.
- **Email** (`client_info_email`): Enter your email address accurately.
- **Phone** (`client_info_phone`): Enter your phone number accurately.
- **Type** (`piercing_info_piercing_type`): Select the type of piercing you are requesting (e.g., Cartilage, Eyebrow, Helix).
- **Body** (`piercing_info_body_area`): Choose the area of the body where you want the piercing to be located (e.g., Earlobe, Eyebrow, Finger, Helix, Navel, Neck, Septum, Surrounding).
- **Consent** (`consent_consent`): Confirm your consent for the piercing service by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Method** (`payment_method_method`): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit/Debit Card, PayPal).

Note: This form is designed to collect information for the piercing service. Please ensure you have read and understood the piercing service details before filling out this form.
