# Limpieza De Costas - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about beach maintenance and cleaning activities. The fields in this form are used to gather data on different aspects of these activities, including the presence or absence of certain conditions or elements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the answers based on your observations or knowledge about the beach maintenance activities.
3. Make sure to answer all fields, as some are marked as required.
