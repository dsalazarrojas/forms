# Car Seat Inspection Scheduling - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment for a car seat inspection. The form captures the requester's information, details about their vehicle and the children to be inspected, and preferences for the appointment time and date. The form helps us to provide the most convenient and personalized service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify your relationship to the child(ren) to be inspected.
3. Enter the number of children requiring inspection.
4. Provide details about each child, including their age and weight.
5. Specify the type of car seat you need help with.
6. Select your preferred date and time for the appointment.
7. Specify your preferred method of communication.
8. Provide details about your vehicle, including its year, make, and type.
9. Answer any additional questions or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Information** (`requester_information`): This field is for your contact details. Please enter your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address for scheduling communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name.
* **Relationship to Child** (`relationship_to_child`): Specify your relationship to the child(ren) to be inspected (e.g., parent, guardian, grandparent, other relative).
* **Child Information** (`child_information`): This field is for details about the child(ren) to be inspected. This is a note field.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`): Enter the number of children requiring inspection.
* **Child 1 Age** (`child_1_age`): Enter the age of the first child in years.
* **Child 1 Weight** (`child_1_weight`): Enter the weight of the first child in pounds.
* **Child 2 Age** (`child_2_age`): Enter the age of the second child in years (if applicable).
* **Child 2 Weight** (`child_2_weight`): Enter the weight of the second child in pounds (if applicable).
* **Child 3 Age** (`child_3_age`): Enter the age of the third child in years (if applicable).
* **Child 3 Weight** (`child_3_weight`): Enter the weight of the third child in pounds (if applicable).
* **Car Seat Details** (`car_seat_details`): This field is for information about the car seat(s) to be inspected. This is a note field.
* **Number of Seats** (`number_of_seats`): Enter the total number of car seats to be inspected.
* **Seat Types** (`seat_types`): Select all types of car seats you need help with (e.g., infant seat, convertible, forward-facing seat, booster seat, all-in-one seat).
* **Seat Ownership** (`seat_ownership`): Specify whether you own all, some, or no car seats.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`): This field is for your availability and preferences for the appointment. This is a note field.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`): Enter your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`): Enter your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Alternative Date 1** (`alternative_date_1`): Enter a secondary preferred date for the appointment (if applicable).
* **Alternative Date 2** (`alternative_date_2`): Enter a tertiary preferred date for the appointment (if applicable).
* **Time Flexibility** (`time_flexibility`): Specify your level of flexibility regarding the appointment time (e.g., very flexible, somewhat flexible, not flexible - specific time needed).
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`): Select the location where you would like to meet for the appointment (e.g., fire station, police station, community center, hospital, health department, home visit).
* **Location Address** (`location_address`): Enter the specific address of the location you selected (if applicable).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service you need (e.g., installation check, new installation, education only, seat selection help, recall check, multiple services).
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_information`): This field is for details about your vehicle. This is a note field.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`): Select the type of vehicle you have (e.g., sedan, SUV, minivan, truck, wagon).
* **Installation Method** (`installation_method`): Specify the method of car seat installation (e.g., LATCH system, seat belt, both, not sure).
* **Concerns and Questions** (`concerns_questions`): This field is for any additional concerns or questions you may have. Select all that apply.
* **Specific Concerns** (`specific_concerns`): Enter any specific concerns or questions you may have.
* **Previous Inspection** (`previous_inspection`): Specify whether you have had a previous inspection (e.g., yes - within 1 year, yes - over 1 year ago, no - first time).
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`): Specify how you heard about our service (e.g., healthcare provider, fire department, police department, social media, friend/family, website, community event, other).
* **Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`): Enter any special needs or circumstances you may have.
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`): Specify your preferred language (e.g., English, Spanish, other).
* **Other Language** (`other_language`): If you selected "other" as your preferred language, enter the name of the language.
* **Reminder Preference** (`reminder_preference`): Specify how you would like to be reminded (e.g., email, text message, phone call, no reminder needed).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information you may have.
* **Scheduling Request Date** (`scheduling_date`): This field is not used in the form but is present as a required field.
