# Braces Adjustment Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Braces Adjustment Appointment Form is a patient-facing form used to collect information and preferences for scheduling an appointment for orthodontic adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your personal and appointment preferences information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
- **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Phone Number** (4): Enter your primary contact number.
- **Email Address** (5): Enter your email address for appointment confirmations.
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (6): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian (optional).
- **Parent or Guardian Phone** (7): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian (optional).
- **Requested Appointment Date** (8): Select your preferred date for the appointment.
- **Requested Appointment Time** (9): Choose your preferred time for the appointment.
  - Select from options: Morning (8AM-12PM), Afternoon (12PM-4PM), After school (4PM-6PM), Flexible
- **Appointment Type** (10): Select the type of adjustment you need, such as Regular adjustment, Wire change, Band replacement, etc.
- **Braces Type** (11): Choose the type of braces you currently have, such as Traditional metal, Ceramic, etc.
- **Months in Treatment** (12): Select the length of time you have had your braces, such as Less than 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, etc.
- **Current Issues** (13): Check any issues you are experiencing with your braces, such as Loose bracket, Poking wire, etc.
  - Select all applicable options
- **Pain Level** (14): Rate your current pain level from No pain to Severe pain.
- **Last Adjustment Date** (15): If applicable, enter the date of your last adjustment.
- **Oral Hygiene** (16): Rate your oral hygiene from Excellent to Poor.
- **Rubber Band Compliance** (17): Select if you are wearing elastics as directed.
- **Dietary Compliance** (18): Select if you are avoiding restricted foods.
- **Special Requests** (19): Enter any special requests you have for this appointment (optional).
- **Reminder Preference** (20): Select how you would like to be reminded of your appointment, such as Text message, Email, etc.
  - Select all applicable options
- **Insurance Verified** (21): Select if your insurance has been verified.
- **Payment Balance** (22): Select if you have an outstanding balance.
- **Transportation Needed** (23): Select if you need transportation assistance (optional).
- **Office Use Only** (24): Enter any staff notes (optional).
- **Confirmation Status** (25): Select your appointment confirmation status, such as Pending confirmation, Confirmed, etc.

Please fill out this form with accurate and honest information to ensure that your appointment is scheduled correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the clinic staff.
