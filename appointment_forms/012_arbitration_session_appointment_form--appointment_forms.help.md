# Arbitration Session Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arbitration Session Appointment Form is a tool used to schedule an arbitration session between participants in a dispute resolution process. It is designed to collect information about the participant's details, case details, schedule preferences, and representation details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and contact information (Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number) in the **Participant Information** section.
2. Select your role in the case (Claimant, Respondent, Legal representative, Witness, or Observer) in the **Your Role in the Case** field.
3. Provide the case number for reference and tracking in the **Case Number** field.
4. Specify your preferred dates and times for the arbitration session (First Preferred Hearing Date, Second Preferred Hearing Date, and Third Preferred Hearing Date) in the **Schedule Preferences** section.
5. Choose your preferred format for the arbitration session (In-person, Virtual meeting, or Hybrid option) in the **Preferred Session Format** field.
6. If you are a participant, select the location (if in-person) or leave it blank if not applicable in the **Preferred Hearing Location (If In-Person)** field.
7. If you have any special accessibility or accommodation needs, please specify them in the **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** field.
8. If you have a legal representative, enter their name and email address in the **Legal Representative Name (if applicable)** and **Legal Representative Email** fields.
9. If others will be attending the session with you, indicate the number of additional attendees in the **Additional Attendees** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your Role in the Case**: Select your role in the case (Claimant, Respondent, Legal representative, Witness, or Observer).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Case Number**: Enter the reference number for the case for tracking purposes.
* **First Preferred Hearing Date**: Choose the date you would prefer for the session to take place.
* **Second Preferred Hearing Date**: Choose the second date you would prefer for the session to take place.
* **Third Preferred Hearing Date**: Choose the third date you would prefer for the session to take place.
* **Preferred Session Format**: Select your preferred format for the session (In-person, Virtual meeting, or Hybrid option).
* **Preferred Hearing Location (If In-Person)**: If you have selected an in-person session, indicate the location you would prefer.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**: If you have any special needs, please specify them here.
* **Legal Representative Name (if applicable)**: If you have a legal representative, enter their name.
* **Legal Representative Email**: If you have a legal representative, enter their email address.
* **Additional Attendees**: If others will be attending the session with you, indicate the number of additional attendees.
