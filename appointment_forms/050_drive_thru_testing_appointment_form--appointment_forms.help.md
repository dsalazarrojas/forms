# Drive Thru Testing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drive Thru Testing Appointment Form is designed to collect patient information and schedule appointments for drive-thru testing. It is intended to be used by patients, healthcare providers, or other authorized personnel for the purpose of scheduling drive-thru testing appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form title to begin.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Drive-Thru Testing** (`drive_thru_testing_form`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter any comments or questions you have about the drive-thru testing process.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, not required): Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address as prompted.
* **Select Test** (`test_type`, select_one, not required): This field allows you to select a test type from the options provided.
* **Test Type** (`select_test_type`, select_multiple, not required): This field is used to select multiple test types from the options provided.
* **Date of Test** (`test_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to take the test.
* **Time of Test** (`test_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you would like to take the test.
* **Time of Test (AM/PM)** (`test_time_am_pm`, text, not required): Enter the AM or PM designation for the time of test.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location where you would like to take the test.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, not required): Enter the date and time of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of your appointment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Enter your name.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, not required): Enter your address.
* **Doctor** (`doctor_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Test Info** (`test_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about the test you are taking.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
* **Save and Submit** (`submit_button`, text, not required): Click this button to save and submit your form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before clicking the submit button.
* If you experience any errors or issues with the form, please contact your authorized personnel for assistance.
