# Student Advisor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students seeking academic advising. It helps you schedule meetings between students and advisors within an educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your desired date for the appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter the time of the preferred appointment time in the format 12:00 PM (or AM/PM).
3. Select the advisor you would like to meet with from the list of options.
4. Add any comments or notes you want to share with the advisor in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Advisor Date**: This field is where you enter the date you want to schedule your appointment. Please use the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/12/2024).
* **Student Advisor Time**: Enter the time of your preferred appointment. Use AM/PM format (e.g., 12:00 PM or 12:00 AM).
* **Advisor**: Choose the advisor you would like to meet with from the dropdown list.
* **Comment**: If you have any additional information or comments you want to share with your advisor, enter them here.
