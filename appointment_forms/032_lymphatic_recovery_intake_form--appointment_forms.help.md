# lymphatic_recovery_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lymphatic recovery intake form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their lymphatic recovery goals, health history, and personal preferences for appointments. This information will help our team better understand the client's needs and provide tailored support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please provide clear and concise answers to each of the questions. You can type your responses directly into the form fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (Client Details): This section asks for your basic contact information, which will help us keep in touch with you.
	+ Type your name, email, and phone number in the relevant fields.
* **Health History** (Health History): Please provide a brief description of your current health status, including any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
	+ Share any relevant medical history, such as previous surgeries, medications, or conditions.
* **Goals** (Goals): Describe your goals for your lymphatic recovery journey. What do you hope to achieve, and what are your expectations from our services?
	+ Be specific about your objectives, such as reducing swelling, improving mobility, or addressing specific symptoms.
* **Appointment Preferences** (Appointment Preferences): Specify your availability and preferences for appointments, including dates and times you are available to meet with our team.
	+ Provide your preferred contact method (phone, email, or in-person) and any other special requests or requirements.
