# HVAC Service Scheduling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information, service requests, and scheduling details from customers for HVAC services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your visit from the "Schedule A Visit" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Describe your service request in the "Service Request" field.
4. Choose the type of service you need from the "Service Type" field.
5. Choose a preferred time for your appointment from the "Schedule Appointment" field.
6. Select a service time from the "Service Time" field.
7. Optionally, select a date for your service from the "Service Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule A Visit**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your visit.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you about your visit.
* **Service Request**: Briefly describe the service you need (e.g., "Fix my air conditioner" or "Service my furnace").
* **Service Type**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Heating
	+ Air Conditioning
	+ Plumbing
	+ Electrical
* **Schedule Appointment**: Choose a time for your appointment from the available options.
* **Service Time**: Select a time for your service (e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Service Date**: Optionally, select a date for your service (if you have a specific date in mind).
* **Tips**: Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to help us understand your service needs.
