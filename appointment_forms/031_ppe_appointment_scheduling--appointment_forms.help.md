# ppe_appointment_scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments for staff members requiring personal protective equipment (PPE) for various procedures or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Staff Details** section with the staff member's information.
2. Select the required **PPE Needs** from the provided options.
3. If available, enter the desired **Appointment Slots** in the text field.
4. Add any additional **Note** to the scheduler in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Details** (`staff_details`, text, required): Enter the staff member's name and other relevant details.
* **PPE Needs** (`ppe_needs`, select_one, required): Select the type of PPE required for the appointment. Options include:
	+ Gloves
	+ Mask
	+ Face shield
	+ Safety glasses
* **Appointment Slots** (`appointment_slots`, text, optional): If available, enter the specific appointment slot(s) desired.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the scheduler.
