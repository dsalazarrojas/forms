# Satellite Technician Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Satellite Technician Service Request. The purpose of this form is to gather details from the user about their service request, including customer and service details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Satellite Technician Service Request" field with a brief description of your service request.
2. In the "customer_details" field, provide information about the customer, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send the request to the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Satellite Technician Service Request
* **Satellite Technician Service Request** (text, required/optional: false)
	+ A brief description of your service request. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure we can efficiently fulfill your request.

### 2. customer_details
* **customer_details** (text, required/optional: false)
	+ Information about the customer, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details. This is an optional field, but providing this information will help us better understand the customer's needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Please ensure you're providing as much detail as possible in the "Satellite Technician Service Request" field to help us efficiently fulfill your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
