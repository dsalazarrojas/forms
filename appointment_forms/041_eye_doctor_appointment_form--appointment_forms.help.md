# Eye Doctor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Doctor Appointment Form is used to schedule appointments for the eye doctor's services. Please use this form for booking appointments with the eye doctor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient details in the required fields.
2. Select a date and time for your appointment.
3. Indicate the duration of your appointment.
4. Specify the reason for your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Not applicable for this form, as it's for booking an appointment with the eye doctor. Please leave this field blank.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Choose the date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Choose the time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, number, required): Indicate how long your appointment will take.
* **Appointment Reason** (`appointment_reason`, text, required): Briefly explain the reason for your appointment.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill in the form accurately to help the eye doctor's office prepare for your appointment. If you have any special requests or requirements, you can mention them in the "Appointment Reason" field.
