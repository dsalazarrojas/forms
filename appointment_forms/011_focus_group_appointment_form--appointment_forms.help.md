# Focus Group Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Focus Group Appointment Form" is designed to schedule focus group sessions. This form helps users select a schedule and time slot for a session, providing options for different time slots and session durations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a schedule for your focus group session from the options provided (08:00-12:00, 13:00-17:00, 17:00-20:00).
2. Choose a time slot for your session from the available options (08:00-12:00, 13:00-17:00, 17:00-20:00).
3. Determine the duration of your session by selecting one of the available options (30 minutes, 60 minutes, 120 minutes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule** (`schedule_page`, Select Multiple, not required): Select a schedule for your focus group session.
* **Time Slot** (`time_slot`, Select One, not required): Select a time slot for your session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, Select One, not required): Choose the duration of your session.

## Tips
* Ensure you select a schedule and time slot that work for you and your focus group participants.
* Consider the available options for session duration to ensure it fits your group's needs.
