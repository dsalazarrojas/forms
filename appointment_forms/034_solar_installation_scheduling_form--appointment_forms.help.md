# Solar Installation Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule a solar installation. The user will be required to provide details about the installation, including the installation date and hour, the customer's name, email, and phone number, as well as other relevant information such as equipment type and service window.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all required fields (if any).
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for the installation.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i> (Text)
	+ A text field for any additional comments or information about the installation.
* **Installation Date**: <i class="fas fa-calendar-day"></i> (Date)
	+ The date on which the installation is scheduled to take place.
* **Installation Hour**: <i class="fas fa-clock"></i> (Time)
	+ The hour of the day on which the installation is scheduled to take place.
* **Technician Name**: <i class="fas fa-id-card"></i> (Text)
	+ The name of the technician assigned to the installation (if known).
* **Customer Name**: <i class="fas fa-id-card"></i> (Text)
	+ The name of the customer.
* **Customer Email**: <i class="fas fa-envelope"></i> (Email)
	+ The email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone**: <i class="fas fa-phone"></i> (Text)
	+ The phone number of the customer.
* **Job Type**: <i class="fas fa-thumbs-o"></i> (Select One)
	+ A selection to indicate whether the job is a yes or no installation.
* **Equipment Type**: <i class="fas fa-box"></i> (Text)
	+ The type of equipment to be installed.
* **Notes**: <i class="fas fa-sticky-note"></i> (Note)
	+ Any additional comments or information about the installation.
* **Customer Address**: <i class="fas fa-map-marked"></i> (Text)
	+ The address of the customer.
* **Service Window**: <i class="fas fa-clock"></i> (Select One)
	+ A selection to indicate whether a service window is required or not.
* **Service Window Start**: <i class="fas fa-calendar-day"></i> (Date)
	+ The start date of the service window (if service window is selected).
* **Service Window End**: <i class="fas fa-calendar-day"></i> (Date)
	+ The end date of the service window (if service window is selected).
* **Installation Location**: <i class="fas fa-map-marker"></i> (Text)
	+ The location of the installation.
* **Assigned Technician**: <i class="fas fa-id-card"></i> (Select One)
	+ A selection to indicate whether a technician is assigned to the installation.
* **Installation Status**: <i class="fas fa-circle"></i> (Select Multiple)
	+ A selection to indicate the status of the installation (active or inactive).

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* Verify the information before submitting the form.
* If the form is to be completed by multiple people, ensure that each person fills out their own form.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact the relevant person in charge.
