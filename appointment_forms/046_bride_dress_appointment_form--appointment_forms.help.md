# Bride Dress Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for brides-to-be who want to book an appointment with a wedding dress designer or stylist. It helps us understand your preferences, needs, and expectations to provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your basic contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose a preferred appointment date and time that suits you best.
3. Select the duration of your appointment, which can range from 30 minutes to 3 hours.
4. Provide your current dress size and whether you expect it to change before the wedding.
5. Choose your preferred dress style, neckline, sleeve style, and color.
6. Select your budget range for the dress.
7. Indicate the number of people you will be attending with you and their roles.
8. Specify any preferred designer or brand.
9. Mention any expected alterations or special requests.
10. Choose how you would like to be confirmed about your appointment (email, phone call, or text message).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bride Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to call or text you.
* **Wedding Date**: Choose the date of your wedding when you would like to book your appointment.
* **Appointment Type**: Select the type of appointment you want (initial dress shopping, continue shopping, first fitting, etc.).
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Choose a date that works best for your appointment scheduling.
* **Preferred Time**: Select a time slot for your appointment.
* **Appointment Duration**: Select the expected time needed for your appointment.
* **Dress Size**: Choose your current dress size. If unsure, select "Unsure".
* **Expect Size to Change Before Wedding**: Let us know if you expect your size to change before the wedding.
* **Dress Style Preference**: Select the style that appeals to you (ball gown, A-line, mermaid, etc.).
* **Preferred Neckline**: Choose your preferred neckline type (off-shoulder, sweetheart, V-neck, etc.).
* **Sleeve Style**: Select your preferred sleeve style (sleeveless, thin straps, cap sleeves, etc.).
* **Dress Color Preference**: Choose your preferred dress color.
* **Budget Range**: Select your budget range for the dress.
* **Number of People Coming With You**: Indicate the number of people attending with you.
* **Attendant Roles**: Select the roles of the attendants (mother, best friend, sister, etc.).
* **Dress Designer Preference**: If you have a specific designer or brand in mind, enter it here.
* **Alteration Needs**: Indicate any expected alterations or changes you might need.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Add any specific needs or preferences you might have.
* **Appointment Confirmation Method**: Choose how you would like to be confirmed about your appointment (email, phone call, text message, etc.).
