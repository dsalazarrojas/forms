# Thesis Examination Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a thesis examination for a student.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the defense date and time in the "Defense Date" and "Defense Time" fields.
* Enter the title of the thesis in the "Thesis Title" field.
* Enter the supervisor's last name and first name in the "Supervisor Last Name" and "Supervisor First Name" fields.
* Enter the student's last name and first name in the "Student Last Name" and "Student First Name" fields.
* Enter the department and defense location in the "Department" and "Defense Location" fields.
* Enter the defense room and floor number in the "Defense Room" and "Defense Floor" fields.
* Enter any additional room notes in the "Defense Room Notes" field.
* Enter the defense time zone in the "Defense Time Zone" field.
* Enter the supervisor's email and phone number in the "Supervisor Email" and "Supervisor Phone" fields.
* Enter the program coordinator's email and phone number in the "Program Coordinator Email" and "Program Coordinator Phone" fields.
* Enter the department's email and phone number in the "Department Email" and "Department Phone" fields.
* Select the defense type (e.g., oral or written) in the "Defense Type" field.
* If other attendees are to be invited, select them from the "Other Attendees" field.
* Enter the student's email and phone number in the "Student Email" and "Student Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Defense Date**: Enter the date on which the thesis examination will take place. (Required: False, Type: Date)
* **Defense Time**: Enter the time on which the thesis examination will take place. (Required: False, Type: Time)
* **Thesis Title**: Enter the title of the thesis being defended. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Supervisor Last Name**: Enter the last name of the supervisor. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Supervisor First Name**: Enter the first name of the supervisor. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Student Last Name**: Enter the last name of the student. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Student First Name**: Enter the first name of the student. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Department**: Enter the department involved in the thesis examination. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Defense Location**: Enter the location where the thesis examination will take place. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Defense Room**: Enter the room number where the thesis examination will take place. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Defense Floor**: Enter the floor number where the thesis examination will take place. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Defense Room Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the defense room. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Defense Time Zone**: Enter the time zone of the defense time. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email of the supervisor. (Required: False, Type: Email)
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Program Coordinator Email**: Enter the email of the program coordinator. (Required: False, Type: Email)
* **Program Coordinator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the program coordinator. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Department Email**: Enter the email of the department. (Required: False, Type: Email)
* **Department Phone**: Enter the phone number of the department. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Other Attendees**: Select any other attendees who will be present at the defense. (Required: False, Type: Select Multiple)
* **Department**: Enter the department involved in the thesis examination. (Required: False, Type: Text)
* **Defense Type**: Select the type of defense (e.g., oral or written). (Required: False, Type: Select One)
* **Student Email**: Enter the email of the student. (Required: False, Type: Email)
* **Student Phone**: Enter the phone number of the student. (Required: False, Type: Text)
