# Alumni Mentorship Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a mentorship session with a preferred mentor, allowing you to discuss a specific topic at a chosen date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Mentee name** (Full name) in the "Mentee name" field.
2. If you have a preferred mentor, enter their name in the "Mentor preference" field.
3. Choose a date for the mentorship session in the "Preferred session date" field.
4. Select a time slot for the session in the "Preferred session time" field.
5. Briefly describe the topic you want to discuss in the "Session topic" field.
6. Enter an email address that will be used for confirmation in the "Contact email" field.
7. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Contact phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mentee name** (Full name, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself in the mentorship session.
* **Mentor preference** (text, optional): If you have a preferred mentor, enter their name here.
* **Preferred session date** (date, required): Choose a date for the mentorship session.
* **Preferred session time** (time, required): Select a time slot for the session.
* **Session topic** (text, required): Briefly describe the topic you want to discuss.
* **Contact email** (email, required): Enter an email address that will be used for confirmation.
* **Contact phone** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here for contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the mentorship session can be scheduled successfully.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the support team.
