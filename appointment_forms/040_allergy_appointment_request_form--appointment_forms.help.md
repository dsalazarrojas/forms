# Allergy Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to request an appointment at the allergy clinic for testing, treatment, or consultation. Please fill out the form with your personal and medical details to schedule an appointment that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
4.  Select your gender from the provided options.
5.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7.  Provide your full mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
8.  Enter your insurance provider's name in the "Insurance Provider" field.
9.  Enter your insurance ID number in the "Insurance ID Number" field.
10. If you have a referral, select "Yes, I have a referral" in the "Referral Required?" field.
11. If you need to refer a physician, enter their name in the "Referring Physician" field.
12. Select the type of appointment you need from the "Appointment Type" field.
13. Describe the main reason for your visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
14. Select all types of allergies you experience in the "Types of Allergies" field.
15. If you know any specific allergens that trigger your allergies, list them in the "Specific Allergens" field.
16. Rate your symptom severity in the "Symptom Severity" field.
17. Select all symptoms you experience in the "Symptoms Experienced" field.
18. Choose how long you have had the symptoms in the "How Long Have You Had Symptoms?" field.
19. If you have been treated for allergies before, select the relevant option in the "Previous Allergy Treatment" field.
20. Enter any current medications you are taking in the "Current Medications" field.
21. If there is a family history of allergies, select the relevant option in the "Family History of Allergies" field.
22. Select your preferred clinic location from the "Preferred Clinic Location" field.
23. Choose a preferred appointment date in the "Preferred Appointment Date" field.
24. Select your preferred appointment time in the "Preferred Appointment Time" field.
25. If you have any alternative dates you would prefer, enter them in the "Alternative Dates" field.
26. If you have any special transportation needs, enter them in the "Transportation Needs" field.
27. If you need an interpreter, select the relevant option in the "Interpreter Needed" field.
28. If you need an interpreter, select the language in the "Interpreter Language" field.
29. Select how you heard about the clinic in the "How Did You Hear About Us?" field.
30. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
31. Finally, confirm your consent to treatment by selecting "Yes, I consent to treatment" in the "Consent to Treatment" field.
32. Acknowledge that you have read the HIPAA notice by selecting "Yes, I acknowledge" in the "HIPAA Acknowledgment" field.
