# Skin Detox Program Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Detox Program Appointment form is a critical component of the skin detox program, used to gather essential information from clients. This form is designed to collect client information, appointment details, skin and health information, and follow-up instructions to ensure accurate and efficient program implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's personal and contact information in the "Client Info" field.
2. Choose a suitable appointment time from the "Appointment Time" select field.
3. Provide detailed information about the client's skin and health in the "Skin Details" and "Health Details" fields.
4. Optionally, include additional notes about the client's appointment in the "Appointment Notes" field.
5. If required, provide instructions for follow-up appointments in the "Follow-up Instructions" field.
6. Decide on a follow-up reminder method in the "Follow-up Reminder" field.
7. Obtain both the client's and clinic's signatures in the "Client Signature" and "Clinic Signature" fields, respectively.
8. If desired, set up follow-up communication methods via email or phone using the "Email Follow-up" and "Phone Follow-up" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info (Text)**: Enter the client's personal and contact information to ensure accurate and efficient communication.
* **Appointment Time (Select One)**: Choose a suitable appointment time from the options (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) to schedule the appointment.
* **Skin Details (Text)**: Provide detailed information about the client's skin condition, including any relevant history or allergies.
* **Health Details (Text)**: Enter the client's health information, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
* **Appointment Notes (Text)**: Optionally, include any additional notes about the client's appointment.
* **Follow-up Instructions (Text)**: If required, provide instructions for follow-up appointments to ensure continuity of care.
* **Follow-up Reminder (Select Multiple)**: Decide on a follow-up reminder method (True or False) to keep the client informed.
* **Client Signature (Note)**: Obtain the client's signature to confirm their understanding of the appointment details.
* **Clinic Signature (Note)**: Obtain the clinic's signature to confirm the appointment details.
* **Email Follow-up (Email)**: If desired, set up follow-up communication via email to keep the client informed.
* **Phone Follow-up (Text)**: If desired, set up follow-up communication via phone to keep the client informed.
* **Additional Notes (Text)**: Optionally, include any additional notes about the client's appointment.
