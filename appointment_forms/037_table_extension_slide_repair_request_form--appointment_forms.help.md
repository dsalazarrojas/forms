# Table Extension Slide Repair Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for table extension slide repair requests. It helps in scheduling a suitable time slot for the repair within the provided options. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide any necessary details about the repair request on **Repair Request Form**.
2. Enter your customer details for efficient communication with our service team.
3. Select the preferred **Service Team Member** for your repair.
4. Choose a suitable **Schedule Appointment** time slot from the options provided.
5. Optionally, provide a specific **Repair Date** and **Appointment Time** for the repair, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Repair Request Form** (text): This field is for providing any details about the repair request.
- **Customer Details** (text): Enter your name and any other relevant details to help the service team contact you efficiently.
- **Service Team Member** (select multiple):
  - Please select one or more members from the given options. The service team members are likely different groups or teams handling the repair requests.
  - Example: Service Team A, Service Team B, or Service Team C.
- **Schedule Appointment** (select one): Choose a suitable time slot for your repair from the options provided: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 60 minutes.
- **Appointment Date** and **Appointment Time** (date and time): These fields are optional and might be used to specify a more precise date and time for the repair. If left blank, the system will default to the chosen schedule appointment time slot.

## Tips
- Ensure you select the correct service team member for your repair request.
- If you need to schedule a repair at a specific date and time, please fill in both the **Repair Date** and **Appointment Time** fields.
- If you have any additional information or requests that do not fit within the provided fields, use the **Repair Request Form** field to include it.
