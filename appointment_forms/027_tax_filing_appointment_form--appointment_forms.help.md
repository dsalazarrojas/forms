# tax_filing_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and manage tax filing appointments with clients. It captures essential information such as client contact details, session details, and appointment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name and contact number.
2. Select the tax professional (preparer) who will be handling the appointment.
3. Choose the appointment location from the available options.
4. Enter the session date and time.
5. Add any additional notes or comments as needed.
6. Set the appointment status as Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This is the header section of the form, intended to be left blank.
* **tax_filing_session_date**: Enter the date of the tax filing session.
* **tax_filing_session_time**: Enter the time of the tax filing session.
* **tax_filing_session_start**: Enter the start date of the tax filing session.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Contact Number**: Enter the client's contact number.
* **Tax Preparer**: Choose the tax professional who will be handling the appointment. Options include Tax Professional, Tax Consultant, and Tax Agent.
* **Appointment Location**: Choose the location where the appointment will take place. Options include Location 1, Location 2, and Location 3.
* **Appointment Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Tax Preparer Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the tax preparer.
* **Tax Filing Session Length**: Enter the length of the tax filing session in minutes.
* **Appointment Status**: Set the appointment status as Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review and double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues or need further assistance, contact your support team.
