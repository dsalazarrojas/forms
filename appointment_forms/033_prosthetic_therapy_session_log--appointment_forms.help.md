# Prosthetic Therapy Session Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a patient's prosthetic therapy sessions, including their name, visit dates and times, and session progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select the date of the visit in the "date_of_visit" field.
3. Choose the time of the visit in the "time_of_visit" fields.
4. Record the total number of planned therapy sessions in the "number_of_sessions" field.
5. Enter the number of sessions that have already been completed in the "number_of_sessions_completed" field.
6. Add any relevant notes or comments about the patient's progress in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
  - Please ensure to enter the correct spelling and capitalization of the patient's name.
- **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, Date, Required): Select the date of the patient's therapy session.
  - This field is required to track the session's date and ensure accurate records.
- **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, Time, Required): Choose the time of the patient's therapy session.
  - This field is required to track the session's time and ensure accurate records.
- **Number of Sessions** (`number_of_sessions`, Number, Required): Record the total number of planned therapy sessions.
  - This field helps the therapist and patient understand the expected duration and scope of the therapy.
- **Number of Sessions Completed** (`number_of_sessions_completed`, Number, Required): Enter the number of therapy sessions that have been completed.
  - This field helps track the patient's progress and progress towards the goal.
- **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Add any relevant comments or observations about the patient's therapy progress.
  - These notes can be useful for future reference or for communication with other healthcare professionals.
