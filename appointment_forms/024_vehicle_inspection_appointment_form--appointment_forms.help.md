# vehicle_inspection_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to schedule a vehicle inspection and collect relevant information from the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Provide your contact information in the "Client Info" field.
2. Enter your vehicle's information in the "Vehicle Info" field.
3. Select your preferred date and time for the inspection in the "Appointment Date" and "Appointment Time" fields.
4. Indicate your preferred date in the "Preferred Date" field.
5. Select your preferred time for the inspection in the "Preferred Time" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about your vehicle inspection in the "Notes" field.
7. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email, in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info (Client Info, Text, Required)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you about your vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Info (Vehicle Info, Text, Required)**: Enter your vehicle's make, model, and year to help our service representative identify your vehicle for inspection.
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, Date, Optional)**: Select the date you would like to schedule your vehicle inspection.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time, Time, Optional)**: Select the time you would like to schedule your vehicle inspection.
* **Preferred Date (Preferred Date, Select One, Optional)**: Indicate if you have a preferred date for your inspection.
* **Preferred Time (Preferred Time, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the preferred times for your inspection (e.g., morning, afternoons, etc.).
* **Notes (Notes, Note, Optional)**: Enter any comments or additional information about your vehicle inspection.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, Text, Required)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email, to ensure we can reach you with updates about your inspection.
