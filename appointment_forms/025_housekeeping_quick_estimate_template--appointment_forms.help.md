# Housekeeping Quick Estimate Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housekeeping Quick Estimate Template is designed to help you quickly collect information about a client's housekeeping needs. This form will help you provide a rough estimate of the cost and other required details for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **What type of housekeeping service do you provide?**: Select the type of housekeeping service you offer from the options listed. This will help us better understand the scope of work required.
2. **What location do you serve?**: Enter the location you serve, such as a city or town name.
3. **Estimated duration**: Enter the estimated number of hours or minutes you think the service will take to complete.
4. **Estimated hours**: Confirm or enter the estimated number of hours for the service.
5. **Price**: Enter the total price for the service.
6. **Payment Terms**: Describe your payment terms, such as payment on delivery or invoice upon completion.
7. **Client Notification Preference**: Choose how you prefer to communicate with the client, such as via email, phone, or text message.
8. **Would the client like to request a site visit for a more accurate quote?**: Select whether the client would like to request a site visit or not.
9. **Site Visit Details**: If the client has requested a site visit, provide any additional details about the visit, such as date, time, and location.
10. **What other housekeeping services do you offer?**: List any additional housekeeping services you offer that are not included in the initial request.
11. **Additional housekeeping services information**: Provide any additional information about the services you offer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of housekeeping service do you provide?** (`housekeeping_service_type`): This field collects the type of housekeeping service you offer, such as cleaning, laundry, or organization.
* **What location do you serve?** (`location`): Enter the location you serve, such as a city or town name.
* **Estimated duration** (`estimated_duration`): This field estimates the time it will take to complete the service.
* **Estimated hours** (`estimated_hours`): This field confirms or allows you to enter the estimated number of hours for the service.
* **Price** (`price`): Enter the total price for the service.
* **What are your payment terms?** (`payment_terms`): Describe your payment terms, such as payment on delivery or invoice upon completion.
* **Client notification preference** (`client_notification_preference`): Choose how you prefer to communicate with the client, such as via email, phone, or text message.
* **Would the client like to request a site visit for a more accurate quote?** (`request_site_visit`): Select whether the client would like to request a site visit or not.
* **Site visit details** (`site_visit_details`): If the client has requested a site visit, provide any additional details about the visit, such as date, time, and location.
* **What other housekeeping services do you offer?** (`housekeeping_services_offered`): List any additional housekeeping services you offer that are not included in the initial request.
* **Additional housekeeping services information** (`housekeeping_services_additional_info`): Provide any additional information about the services you offer.
