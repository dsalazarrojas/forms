# Assisted Stretch Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Assisted Stretch Sign Up Form is designed to gather information from clients about their personal details, medical conditions, and preferences for assisted stretch sessions. This information will be used to tailor their individualized sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Name, Email Address, Phone Number) accurately.
2. Select your preferred date and time for the session.
3. Choose your preferred stretch specialist.
4. Select your primary goal for the session.
5. Identify your problem areas that require assistance.
6. Inform us about any medical conditions you may have.
7. Disclose any current injuries.
8. Rate your current pain level.
9. Confirm your physician clearance status.
10. Inform us about your prior experience with assisted stretching.
11. Indicate how you heard about us.
12. Express interest in package deals.
13. Sign the liability waiver.
14. Confirm the accuracy of your health information.
15. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
16. Consent to receive promotional emails.
17. Sign your name to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name accurately.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for scheduling.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for records.
* **Gender**: Select your gender for our records.
* **Membership Status**: Indicate your current membership status.
* **Membership Number**: If applicable, enter your membership number.
* **Preferred Location**: Choose the location where you want to have your session.
* **Preferred Date**: Select your first preferred date for the session.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose your preferred time for the session.
* **Session Type**: Select the type of session you want (e.g., one-on-one, partner, group).
* **Session Duration**: Choose the duration of your desired session.
* **Preferred Stretch Specialist**: Choose who you want to be your stretch specialist.
* **Primary Goal**: Select your main reason for seeking assisted stretching.
* **Problem Areas**: Identify any areas you need assistance with.
* **Do You Have Any Medical Conditions**: Indicate if you have any medical conditions.
* **Describe Medical Conditions**: If applicable, describe your medical conditions.
* **Do You Have Current Injuries**: Indicate if you have any current injuries.
* **Describe Injuries**: If applicable, describe your current injuries.
* **Current Pain Level**: Rate your current pain level from 1 to 10.
* **Do You Have Physician Clearance**: Confirm if you have physician clearance.
* **Have You Done Assisted Stretching Before**: Inform us about your prior experience with assisted stretching.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Indicate where you heard about us.
* **Interested in Package Deals**: Express interest in package deals.
* **I Sign the Liability Waiver**: Sign the waiver to confirm your understanding of our policies.
* **I Confirm Health Information Is Accurate**: Confirm the accuracy of your health information.
* **I Understand the Cancellation Policy**: Confirm that you understand our cancellation policy.
* **Consent to Receive Promotional Emails**: Consent to receive promotional emails.
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name to complete the form and confirm your understanding of the above information.

Note that this form is designed to provide us with accurate and comprehensive information about you. Please fill it out carefully and truthfully.
