# Pet Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for scheduling appointments related to pet services. It will help our staff prepare for the appointment and ensure that we have all the necessary details to provide the best service to your pet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please select the type of pet you are bringing in for service (e.g., Cat, Dog, Bird, Fish).
2. Enter the name of the pet.
3. Enter your name (the pet owner).
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you with appointment details.
5. Enter your phone number for any last-minute updates or questions.
6. Choose a date and time for the appointment.
7. Select a location for the appointment (although this field is not currently used, it's left blank for future development).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of pet you are bringing in for service.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the pet owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with appointment details.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any last-minute updates or questions.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, text, required/optional): Select a location for the appointment (although this field is not currently used, it's left blank for future development).
