# interested_in_solar - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "interested_in_solar" form is used to collect contact information and appointment scheduling details from individuals interested in learning more about solar panels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name, last name, and email fields with your contact information.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Select the date you are available to meet or discuss solar panel options.
4. Choose the time you prefer to meet or discuss solar panels.
5. Indicate your interest in solar panels by selecting "Solar Panels" from the "Interested In Solar Panels" options.
6. Choose the type of appointment that best fits your needs from the "Appointment Type" options.
7. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your interest in solar panels in the "Notes" field.
8. Select the category you associate with your interest in solar panels from the "Category" options.
9. Finally, select the person you would like to be assigned to for this inquiry from the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name to help us understand who you are.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to help us understand who you are.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you with more information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you to discuss further.
* **Date**: Select the date you are available to meet or discuss solar panels.
* **Time**: Choose the time you prefer to meet or discuss solar panels.
* **Interested In Solar Panels**: Indicate your interest in solar panels by selecting "Solar Panels" from the options.
* **Appointment Type**: Choose the type of appointment that best fits your needs: "In-person", "Phone Call", or "Virtual Meeting".
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your interest in solar panels (optional).
* **Category**: Select the category you associate with your interest in solar panels: "Home", "Business", or "Other".
* **Assigned To**: Select the person you would like to be assigned to for this inquiry.
