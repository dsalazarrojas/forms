# Appointment Request Hairdressers Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients to book an appointment with a hairdresser. The form helps to schedule a time and date for a haircut, color, or other styling services, taking into account the client's preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Email Address** in the Client Information section. These details are required to confirm your identity and contact information.
2. Choose a **Preferred Date** for your appointment by selecting a date on the calendar.
3. Select a **Preferred Time** for your appointment from the available time slots (9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM).
4. Choose the **Service Requested** from the available options (Haircut, Color, Highlights, Styling, Perm, Extensions, or Other).
5. If desired, select your **Hair Type** (Straight, Wavy, Curly, or Coily).
6. If you have a style image or description, provide a brief **Style Inspiration** to help the stylist better understand your preferences.
7. Optionally, mention any **Special Requests or Allergies** that the stylist should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for the hairdresser to reach you.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Preferred Date**: The date you'd like to schedule your appointment.
* **Preferred Time**: The time slot you prefer for your appointment (9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM).
* **Service Requested**: The type of service you require (Haircut, Color, Highlights, Styling, Perm, Extensions, or Other).
* **Stylist Preference**: Optionally, provide a name or description of your preferred stylist.
* **Hair Type**: Choose your hair type (Straight, Wavy, Curly, or Coily).
* **Style Inspiration**: Provide a brief description or image of the style you're aiming for.
* **Special Requests or Allergies**: Mention any special requests or allergies the stylist should be aware of.

## Tips
* Please ensure your contact information (Phone Number and Email Address) are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask the hairdresser for clarification.
* If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please notify the hairdresser in advance to avoid conflicts with other clients.
