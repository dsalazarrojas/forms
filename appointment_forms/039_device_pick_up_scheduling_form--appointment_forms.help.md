# Device Pick Up Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information and scheduling details for device pick-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): Provide your name, phone number, and email address to get in touch with you.

### Step 2: Device Info
* **Device Info** (device_info, text, required): Share details about the device(s) you'd like to pick up.

### Step 3: Locations
* **Locations** (locations, text, required): Choose your preferred location for device pick-up.

### Step 4: Preferred Appointment
* **Preferred Appointment** (preferred_appointment, text, required): Select your preferred appointment date and time for device pick-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
This form has four fields that help us get in touch with you and understand your needs:
* **Contact Details**: This is where you can provide your name, phone number, and email address. Please ensure that these details are accurate to help us get in touch with you.
* **Device Info**: Share details about the device(s) you'd like to pick up. This will help us prepare for your appointment.
* **Locations**: Choose your preferred location for device pick-up. Please select from the options provided to ensure we can deliver your device(s) to the correct location.
* **Preferred Appointment**: Select your preferred appointment date and time for device pick-up. Please choose a date and time that works best for you.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your contact details for accuracy.
* If you have any issues with the form, feel free to contact us.
* Please note that we'll get back to you once we receive your form submission.
* If you have any further requests or questions, we're here to help.
