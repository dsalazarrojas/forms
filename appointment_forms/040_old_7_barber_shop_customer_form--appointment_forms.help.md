# old-7-barber-shop-customer-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The old-7-barber-shop-customer-form is used to capture customer information and preferences for a barber shop visit, helping the staff provide more personalized service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information, including your name and contact details.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Mail).
3. Choose the date and time for your appointment.
4. Specify the duration of your appointment.
5. Enter your customer name.
6. Provide your customer address.
7. Enter your customer phone number.
8. Enter your customer email address.
9. Add any relevant customer notes for the staff.
10. Enter employee information (staff name and title).
11. Enter employee phone number.
12. Enter employee email address.

* **Customer Info (Customer Info, text, required: false)**: Enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Contact Method (Contact Method, select_one, required: false)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, date, required: false)**: Select the date you'd like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time, time, required: false)**: Choose the time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Duration (Appointment Duration, number, required: false)**: Specify the length of your appointment.
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your name for the staff to address you by.

* **Customer Email (Customer Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
