# Monitoring And Evaluation Data - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect monitoring and evaluation data related to "Appointment Forms". The data collected will help in analyzing and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Appointment Forms" field and select the relevant information.
2. For "Activity Type", select all the activities related to your work. You can choose more than one activity.
3. Finally, select the "Frequency" of your activities from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Forms** (`appointment_forms`, text, required/optional): Enter the relevant information about the appointment forms you are working with.
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the activities related to your work, such as Activity 1, Activity 2, or Activity 3.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency of your activities, such as Option 1 or Option 2.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information about the appointment forms and your activities.
* If you are unsure about any activity or frequency, please contact the form administrator for further guidance.
