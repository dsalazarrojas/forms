# Tattoo Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers for scheduling a tattoo appointment. It is intended for customers who want to book a tattoo session with a preferred artist.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Info" section.
2. Provide details about your appointment in the "Appointment Details" section.
3. Select your preferred artist in the "Tattoo Info" section.
4. Specify your preferred date and time for the appointment in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. Choose your preferred location for the appointment in the "Appointment Location" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Leave any additional information or comments in the "Message" field.
9. Add any customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information to help us better understand your needs.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required: false): Describe the type of tattoo you want to get, the design, and any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Tattoo Info** (`tattoo_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about the tattoo, such as the design or any specific requirements.
* **Preferred Artist** (`preferred_artist`, select_one, required: false): Select the artist you prefer for your tattoo session.
* **Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date you want to schedule your appointment.
* **Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you want to schedule your appointment.
* **Tattoo Designer** (`tattoo_designer`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple artists who you prefer for your tattoo session.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location where you want to have your appointment (e.g., studio, parlor, etc.).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates or confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates or confirmation.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Leave any additional information or comments about your appointment.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required: false): Add any other relevant notes or instructions for the artist.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth and successful booking process.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
