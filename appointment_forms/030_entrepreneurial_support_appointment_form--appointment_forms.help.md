# entrepreneurial_support_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneurial Support Appointment Form is designed to collect essential information from customers or entrepreneurs to schedule appointments with advisors or business coaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the customer information fields: name, email, and business details.
2. Provide advisor information, including name and phone number.
3. Select a date for the appointment.
4. Choose an appointment time.
5. Indicate the preferred language for the appointment.
6. Select the scheduled day for the appointment.
7. Confirm the appointment by selecting 'True' in the confirmation field.
8. Review the form and submit it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: A text field to input your name and email address. 
* **Business Details**: A text field to input your business name and type.
* **Advisor Info**: A text field to input advisor name and phone number.
* **Appointment Date**: A date field to select an appointment date.
* **Appointment Time**: A time field to select an appointment time.
* **Preferred Language**: A dropdown menu to select your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, or Chinese).
* **Schedule**: A dropdown menu to select a scheduled day (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday).
* **Confirm**: A dropdown menu to confirm the appointment (True or False).
* **Form Footer**: A note field for any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in to ensure successful submission.
* Double-check the appointment date and time to ensure the selected time slot is available.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
