# eye_prescription_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The eye_prescription_form is designed to gather essential information from patients for eye-related medical consultations. This form is intended for healthcare providers to collect accurate and comprehensive data from patients, helping them to understand their medical history, prescriptions, and any relevant patient-specific details. By filling out this form, you'll be providing valuable insights to healthcare professionals, enhancing the efficiency and quality of care you receive.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the eye_prescription_form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the patient_info section, which includes details about your personal and contact information.
2.  Move on to the medical_history section, where you'll provide information about your previous medical experiences and conditions.
3.  Continue with the prescription_info section, which will ask about your current prescriptions and medication details.
4.  The medical_history_2 section is similar to the previous one, where you'll provide additional medical information.
5.  The prophets section, which we assume is a typo and is likely meant to be "procedures" or another relevant field, will require you to provide details about any medical procedures you've undergone.
6.  In the prescription_info_2 section, you'll provide further information about your prescriptions and medication details.
7.  The patient_info_2 section will ask for additional patient-specific information.
8.  The medical_history_3 section will require you to provide more medical history information.
9.  Finally, in the patient_question section, please provide any additional information or questions you have about your medical condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): This section requires your personal and contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please provide a detailed account of your previous medical experiences, including any illnesses, injuries, or conditions you've had.
*   **Prescription Info** (`prescription_info`, text, required): This section is for you to list your current prescriptions, including the name of the medication, dosage, and frequency.
*   **Medication Info** (`medication_info`, text, required): Similar to the previous section, this is for you to provide additional details about your medications.
*   **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required): Similar to the first medical history section, please provide more details about your medical experiences.
*   **Prophets** (`prophets`, text, required): We assume this is a typo and is likely meant to be "procedures" or another relevant field. Please provide details about any medical procedures you've undergone.
*   **Prescription Info 2** (`prescription_info_2`, text, required): This section is similar to the first prescription info section, but with further details about your current prescriptions.
*   **Patient Info 2** (`patient_info_2`, text, required): This section is for you to provide additional patient-specific information.
*   **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, required): Similar to the previous medical history sections, please provide more details about your medical experiences.
*   **Patient Question** (`question`, text, required): Finally, if you have any additional questions or concerns about your condition, please provide them here.
