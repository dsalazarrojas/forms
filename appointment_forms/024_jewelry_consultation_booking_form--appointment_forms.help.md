# Jewelry Consultation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewelry Consultation Booking Form is a tool to schedule a consultation for a client. It collects necessary details to facilitate a productive meeting with the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself that you'd like to share.
2. **Appointment Details**: Specify the date and time you'd like to have the consultation and choose a preferred time slot.
3. **Jewelry Details**: Describe the type of jewelry service you're interested in and any specific needs or requirements you have.
4. **Contact Details**: Leave your contact information, such as email or phone number, so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: Enter your name and any additional details about yourself.
* **Appointment Details**: Choose a date and time that works best for you and select a preferred time slot (e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Jewelry Details**: Describe the type of jewelry service you're interested in, such as repairing, customizing, or appraising.
* **Contact Details**: Leave your email address or phone number, and we'll get back to you to finalize the consultation.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing your client, appointment, and jewelry details.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, be sure to include them in the jewelry details section.
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information so we can reach out to you to confirm the consultation.
