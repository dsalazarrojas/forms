# Salon Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salon Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients for their salon consultations. This form will help our stylists understand the client's needs and preferences to provide the best possible experience. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill in the required information and click submit. Each page will guide you through the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Consultation Form** (`form_input`, text, optional):
  Please enter a title or description of the consultation, if needed.
* **Client Name** (`name`, text, optional):
  Enter your name for the consultation. This will help our stylist personalize their approach to your visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
  Provide your email address if you would like to receive updates or follow-up information after your consultation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional):
  Enter your phone number in case our stylist needs to reach you regarding any questions or changes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional):
  Select the date of your desired consultation. Please make sure to choose a date that works for you and our stylist.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional):
  Choose the time of your desired consultation. Please select a time that is convenient for you and our stylist.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure our stylist has all the necessary information.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Salon Consultation Form" field.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to assist you.
