# Medical Equipment Representative Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments with medical equipment representatives. It is intended for representatives to provide their availability and the type of appointment they will be conducting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with the date and title of the appointment.
2. Enter your **Name** in the field provided.
3. Choose a **Date** for the appointment, if desired.
4. Choose a **Time** for the appointment, as it is required.
5. Enter your **Email** address, if you wish to be contacted with appointment reminders.
6. Enter your **Phone** number, if you wish to be contacted with appointment reminders.
7. Choose your **Medical Equipment Representative** type (Equipment Specialist, Service Representative, or Sales Representative).
8. Select the type of **Appointment Type** (Medical Equipment Inspection, Medical Equipment Training, or Other).
9. Enter a brief **Reason** for the appointment.
10. Optionally, provide a **Note** for any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form, which is used to identify the appointment.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it will appear on the appointment schedule.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the appointment, if desired. If left blank, the system will use the current date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a time slot for the appointment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted with appointment reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted with appointment reminders.
* **Medical Equipment Representative** (`medical_equipment_representative`, select_one, required): Choose your role as a representative (Equipment Specialist, Service Representative, or Sales Representative).
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of appointment (Medical Equipment Inspection, Medical Equipment Training, or Other).
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation of the appointment reason.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the appointment.
* **Medical Equipment Representative Comment** (`medical_equipment_representative_comment`, text, optional): If desired, provide any additional comment for the representative.
* **Form Comment** (`form_comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the form.
