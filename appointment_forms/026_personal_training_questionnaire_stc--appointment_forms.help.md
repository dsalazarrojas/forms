# Personal Training Questionnaire STC - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form collects information from individuals interested in personal training services, allowing trainers to provide tailored services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a category that interests you (Personal, Fitness, Sports).
2. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your phone number, if available.
4. Describe your goals for training (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, endurance).
5. Enter your previous experience with training or related activities.
6. Confirm that you have medical clearance to participate in training.
7. Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
8. Indicate the relationship between you and the emergency contact (Parent, Spouse, Friend, None).
9. Enter a brief description of the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category**: Select a category that best represents your interest in personal training (Personal, Fitness, Sports).
* **name**: Enter your full name for our records.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address to contact you about your training sessions.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes, if you're willing to share it.
* **description**: Briefly describe your goals for the training (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, endurance).
* **date**: Enter the date you'd like to start your training sessions.
* **time**: Specify the time you're available for training sessions.
* **goal**: Describe your specific goals for the training (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, endurance).
* **previous_experience**: Enter your previous experience with training or related activities (e.g., sports team, previous trainer).
* **medical_clear**: Confirm that you have medical clearance to participate in training.
* **emergency_contact_name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact, if available.
* **emergency_contact_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (Parent, Spouse, Friend, None).
* **emergency_contact_info**: Enter a brief description of the emergency contact's information (e.g., relationship, location).
