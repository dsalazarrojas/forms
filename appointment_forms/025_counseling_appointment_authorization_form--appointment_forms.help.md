# counseling_appointment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counseling appointment authorization form is designed to collect necessary information from clients for counseling sessions. This form includes details such as client contact information, session dates and times, and a consent statement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information (client_info).
2. Enter the client's name (client_name).
3. Provide the client's contact information (client_contact).
4. Schedule the session date (session_date) and time (session_time).
5. Enter the session details (session_details).
6. Select the consent option (consent) to indicate whether you consent to participate or decline.
7. If required, sign your name (client_signature) in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info**: A text field to input general information about the client.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client, as it should match the name provided in the client_info field.
* **Client Contact**: A text field to input contact information (e.g., phone number, email, or address) for the client.
* **Session Date**: A date field to schedule the session date. Please ensure it is in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Session Time**: A time field to schedule the session time. Please ensure it is in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Session Details**: A text field to provide further details about the session.
* **Consent**: Select one of the two options to indicate whether you consent to participate or decline.
* **Client Signature**: A text field for the client to sign their name. If required, please provide a signature.

Please note that the client signature field is not required by default, but its requirement may be specified by the administrator or counselor.
