# Eyelash Extension Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for scheduling appointments related to eyelash extensions. It will help lash technicians determine which appointments to schedule and which technicians are available to perform the services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the scheduling date for your appointment.
2.  Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
3.  Indicate if you are a lash technician or not.
4.  Choose which technicians are available to perform the service.
5.  Select the duration of your appointment.
6.  Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
7.  Add any notes for the technician performing the service.
8.  Review and agree to the appointment details by confirming each appointment option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Schedule Appointment Date**: <small>This is the date you want to schedule your appointment.</small>
*   **Client Email**: <small>Enter your email address so we can contact you.</small>
*   **Lash Technicians**: <small>Please select which technicians you are interested in having perform the service.</small>
*   **Available Technicians**: <small>Select which technicians are available to perform the service.</small>
*   **Appointment Duration**: <small>Select the duration of your appointment in minutes.</small>
*   **Client Phone**: <small>Enter your phone number so we can contact you.</small>
*   **Lash Technician Notes**: <small>Add any notes for the technician performing the service.</small>
*   **Confirm Appointment**: <small>Please review and confirm each appointment option.</small>
*   **Confirm Appointment 1-8**: <small>Please review and confirm each appointment option.</small>

## Tips

*   Please review each field carefully before proceeding to the next.
*   Make sure to select the correct technicians and confirm your appointment details accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
*   This form is designed to be completed in a single session. If you need to make changes, please contact us.
