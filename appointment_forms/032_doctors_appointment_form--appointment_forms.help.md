# Doctors Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule appointments and collect information about the doctor and patient. It is meant to be used for scheduling appointments between a doctor and a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). The required fields are:
	* Appointment Date
	* Appointment Time
	* Doctor Fee
	* Doctor Fee Currency
	* Appointment Status
	* Doctor Available Until
	* Doctor Available From
	* Doctor Duration
2. Enter any other relevant information for the appointment, such as Doctor Info, Patient Info, and Patient Phone.
3. Sign the form with your signature to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the appointment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *Appointment Form* (`1`, `text`, not required) 
* A form header to identify the type of form being filled out.

### 2. *Patient Info* (`2`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter patient's personal information.

### 3. *Doctor Info* (`3`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter doctor's personal information.

### 4. *Appointment Date* (`4`, `date`, not required) 
* Enter the date of the scheduled appointment.

### 5. *Appointment Time* (`5`, `time`, not required) 
* Enter the time of the scheduled appointment.

### 6. *Doctor Fee* (`6`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter the doctor's fee for the appointment.

### 7. *Doctor Fee Currency* (`11`, `select_one`, not required) 
* Select the currency of the doctor's fee.

### 8. *Appointment Duration* (`15`, `select_multiple`, not required) 
* Select the duration of the scheduled appointment.

### 9. *Doctor Signature* (`17`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your signature as the doctor to confirm the appointment.

### 10. *Patient Phone* (`8`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter patient's contact phone number.

### 11. *Patient Signature* (`16`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter patient's signature to confirm the appointment.

### 12. *Doctor Language* (`22`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter the language you communicate with the patient.

### 13. *Doctor Address* (`24`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your practice's address.

### 14. *Patient Address* (`23`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter patient's address.

### 15. *Doctor Available Until* (`13`, `number`, not required) 
* Enter the date of last available appointment.

### 16. *Doctor Available From* (`14`, `number`, not required) 
* Enter the date of first available appointment.

### 17. *Doctor Note* (`25`, `note`, not required) 
* Enter any other notes about the appointment.

### 18. *Doctor Degree* (`20`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your degree earned by the doctor.

### 19. *Doctor Specialty* (`21`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your speciality.

### 20. *Doctor License* (`18`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your license number.

### 21. *Doctor License Country* (`19`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your license country.

### 22. *Doctor Phone* (`10`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your phone number.

### 23. *Appointment Status* (`12`, `select_one`, not required) 
* Select the status of the appointment.

### 24. *Doctor Address* (`24`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your address. 

### 25. *Appointment Notes* (`7`, `note`, not required) 
* Enter any other notes about the appointment. 

### 26. *Doctor Degree* (`20`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter degree earned by the doctor. 

### 27. *Doctor Specialty* (`21`, `text`, not required) 
* Enter your specialty.
