# Hair Renewal Program Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and preferences for the Hair Renewal Program appointment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details in the "client_details" field.
2. Select the salons of interest from the options provided.
3. Choose the hair concerns relevant to the client's hair issues.
4. Choose the preferred date and time for the appointment.
5. Add any additional notes as required.
6. Confirm the appointment by selecting "Agree" and entering the correct client and salon IDs.
7. Submit the form after filling out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter the client's name and any relevant details.
* **Select Salons** (`select_salons`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more salons from the provided options.
* **Select Hair Concerns** (`select_hair_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more hair concerns from the provided options.
* **Appointment Date** (`select_appointment_date`, select_multiple, required): Choose the preferred appointment date from the options provided.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Enter the preferred time of appointment.
* **Notes** (`select_notes`, select_multiple, optional): Add any additional notes as required.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, note, required): Enter a brief description of the appointment.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, select_one, required): Confirm the appointment by selecting "Agree".
* **Client ID** (`client_id`, number, required): Enter the client's ID number.
* **Salon ID** (`salon_id`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the salon's ID number.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm the form submission by selecting "Submitted".
* **Form Submittted DateTime** (`form_submitted_datetime`, date, required): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required): Enter the name of the person or entity the form was submitted to.
* **Submission Time** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission IP** (`submitted_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of submission.
* **Submission Country** (`submitted_country`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the country of submission.
* **Submission Region** (`submitted_region`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the region of submission.
* **Submission City** (`submitted_city`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the city of submission.
* **Submission Country Code** (`submitted_country_code`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the country code of submission.
* **Submission Latitude** (`submitted_latitude`, number, optional): Enter the latitude of submission.
* **Submission Longitude** (`submitted_longitude`, number, optional): Enter the longitude of submission.
* **Submission Timezone** (`submitted_timezone`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the timezone of submission.
