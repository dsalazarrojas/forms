# Walk In Haircut Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a walk-in haircut appointment at your local hair salon. Fill out the form with your details to schedule a spot for yourself or a friend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the time and date of your appointment.
3. Select your preferred time slot.
4. Choose the services you would like to receive during your appointment.
5. Select the stylist you would like to work with.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
7. Confirm your phone number to ensure we can reach you with any last-minute updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input (Text, Optional)**: Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
- **Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed during your visit.
- **Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your appointment.
- **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with last-minute updates.
- **Appointment Time (Time, Optional)**: Choose a time for your appointment. Please note that this is not your preferred time.
- **Appointment Date (Date, Optional)**: Choose a date for your appointment.
- **Preferred Time (Time, Optional)**: Select a time slot that you would like to have for your appointment.
- **Haircut Service (Select Multiple, Required)**: Choose the services you want to receive during your appointment.
- **Stylist (Select One, Required)**: Select the stylist you would like to work with.
- **Notes (Note, Optional)**: Add any additional comments or requests you have about your appointment.
- **Confirm Phone (Text, Optional)**: Re-enter your phone number to confirm that we can contact you with last-minute updates.
