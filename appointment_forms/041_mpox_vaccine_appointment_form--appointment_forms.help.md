# mpox_vaccine_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "mpox_vaccine_appointment_form" is a form used to schedule a vaccine appointment for patients. This form is used to collect necessary information from patients to schedule and administer the vaccine effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Vaccine Name**, which is the type of vaccine being administered.
2. Enter the **Patient Name**, the name of the individual receiving the vaccine.
3. Enter the **Contact Number**, the contact information for the patient or their guardian.
4. Choose a **Appointment Date** for the vaccine appointment.
5. Select a **Appointment Time** for the vaccine appointment.
6. Choose the **Vaccine Dose** to be administered.
7. Enter the **Patient Age**, the age of the individual receiving the vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccine Name**: Enter the type of vaccine being administered, e.g., MPox Vaccine.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the individual receiving the vaccine, including any relevant titles or prefixes (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Dr.).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number or email address of the patient or their guardian, so that they can be contacted with appointment details.
* **Appointment Date**: Select a date for the vaccine appointment, using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Appointment Time**: Select a time for the vaccine appointment, using the format `HH:MM`.
* **Vaccine Dose**: Enter the number of doses of the vaccine to be administered.
* **Patient Age**: Enter the age of the individual receiving the vaccine, as a whole number.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before administering the vaccine.
* Double-check the appointment date and time to ensure they are correct.
* Update the contact number if it changes before the appointment.
