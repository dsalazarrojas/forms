# Fitness Assessment Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness Assessment Appointment Form is designed to help clients provide their contact information and schedule preferences for a fitness assessment appointment. This form ensures that our team has the necessary details to schedule a suitable appointment time and date for the client's assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Client Info** (Page 1): Enter your name, email address, and other relevant details about yourself. This information will help us get in touch with you to schedule your appointment.
2. Enter your **Contact Info** (Page 2): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant contact details. This will ensure we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
3. Select a suitable **Schedule** (Page 3): Choose a date and time that suits you for your fitness assessment appointment. Please note that we'll do our best to accommodate your preferred schedule.
4. Add any additional **Note** (Page 4): If you have any specific requirements or preferences for your appointment, such as equipment or staff requirements, please let us know in the **Note** field.
5. Select a **Date** (Page 5): Choose a date for your fitness assessment appointment. We'll do our best to accommodate your preferred date.
6. Select a **Time** (Page 6): Choose a time for your fitness assessment appointment. Please ensure this time is within our business hours.
7. Enter your **Phone** (Page 7): Confirm or provide your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your phone number and any other relevant contact details so we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
* **Schedule**: Choose a date and time that suits you for your fitness assessment appointment.
* **Note**: If you have any specific requirements or preferences for your appointment, such as equipment or staff requirements, please let us know in this field.
* **Date**: Choose a date for your fitness assessment appointment.
* **Time**: Choose a time for your fitness assessment appointment within our business hours.
* **Phone**: Confirm or provide your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment details.
