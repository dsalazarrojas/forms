# Ultrasound Examination Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ultrasound Examination Registration form is designed to streamline and standardize the collection of patient information, medical history, and examination details during an ultrasound examination. It aims to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of the medical process by structuring the data collection process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the patient's basic information under "Patient Information."
*   Provide a brief medical history under "Medical History."
*   Fill in the contact details for the patient, including their address and any preferred contact method.
*   Enter the appointment details.
*   Document the examination details, including the examination date, time, and any relevant notes.
*   Obtain the patient's consent for the examination.
*   Select the doctor responsible for the examination from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This is the section where you can enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): This section allows you to document the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact details, such as their name, phone number, and email.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, email, required/optional): If applicable, enter the patient's email address.
*   **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required/optional): This section is for recording the appointment date, time, and any other relevant details.
*   **Examination Details** (`examination_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of examination, its purpose, and any other relevant notes.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, note, required/optional): Obtain the patient's consent for the examination by typing a statement indicating they understand and agree to the examination.
*   **Doctor's Information** (`doctor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's name, title, or any other relevant details.
*   **Doctor** (`doctor_info`, select one, required/optional): Select the doctor responsible for the examination from the available options.
