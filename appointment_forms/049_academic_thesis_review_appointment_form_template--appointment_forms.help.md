# Academic Thesis Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Thesis Review Appointment Form is a template designed to schedule thesis reviews for students and advisors. This form helps gather essential information to ensure that both parties are on the same page regarding the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's full name, email address, and identification number in the corresponding fields.
2. Fill in the thesis title, academic program, and advisor's name, email address, and name as well.
3. Select the type of review needed from the given options.
4. Choose two preferred appointment dates and a preferred time slot.
5. Select the expected appointment duration and preferred meeting format.
6. Provide the materials to be reviewed and any special requirements for the session.
7. Add any additional comments or information for the advisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Academic Thesis Review Appointment**: This field is not a data entry field, but a heading for the form.
* **Student Name**: Enter the full name of the student requesting the appointment.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's institutional email address.
* **Student ID**: Enter the official student identification number.
* **Advisor Name**: Enter the full name of the thesis advisor.
* **Advisor Email**: Enter the advisor's institutional email address.
* **Thesis Title**: Enter the working title of the thesis.
* **Academic Program**: Enter the degree program for which the thesis is being written.
* **Type of Review**: Select the type of thesis review needed (e.g., Initial proposal review, Chapter review, Full draft review, etc.).
* **Preferred Date Option 1**: Choose the first preferred date and time for the appointment.
* **Preferred Date Option 2**: Choose the second preferred date and time for the appointment (optional).
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select a time slot that works best for the appointment (e.g., Morning, Early afternoon, Late afternoon, Evening, or Flexible).
* **Expected Appointment Duration**: Select the expected duration of the review session.
* **Meeting Format**: Choose the preferred meeting format (e.g., In-person, Video conference, Phone call, Email exchange, or Other).
* **Materials to Review**: Provide a brief description of the materials to be reviewed during the session.
* **Special Requirements**: List any special requirements or instructions for the review session.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other information or comments for the advisor.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to facilitate a smooth and effective review process.
