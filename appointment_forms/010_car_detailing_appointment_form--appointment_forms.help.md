# Car Detailing Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment for detailing services for your vehicle. Please select the type of service you need and provide your vehicle and contact information to confirm the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of detailing service you require from the options listed.
2. Enter your vehicle's make and model in the provided field.
3. Specify the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
4. Provide your vehicle's 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) if desired.
5. Rate your vehicle's current condition based on the provided options.
6. Select the type of vehicle you have (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Coupe, or Motorcycle).
7. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to confirm your appointment.
8. Choose a preferred date and time for your appointment.
9. Finally, type your name to confirm your appointment and provide any additional requests or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Service Details --**: This is the header for the service details section, where you will select the type of detailing service you need.
  - **Type of Detailing Service**: Select the service package that best suits your needs from the provided options.
  - **Package Price Estimate**: This field displays the estimated price for the selected service package. It is optional, but it can give you an idea of what to expect.
- **-- Vehicle Information --**: This is the header for the vehicle details section, where you will enter information about your vehicle.
  - **Car Make and Model**: Enter the make and model of your vehicle in this field.
  - **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
  - **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: If you want to provide your vehicle's VIN, please enter it here. (Note: This field is optional)
  - **Vehicle Condition**: Rate your vehicle's current condition based on the options provided.
  - **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you have (Sedan, SUV, Truck, Coupe, or Motorcycle).
- **-- Contact Information --**: This is the header for the contact information section, where you will enter your details to confirm your appointment.
  - **Full Name**: Enter your full name to confirm your appointment.
  - **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive appointment reminders.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for appointment reminders.
- **Appointment Date**: Select your preferred appointment date.
- **Appointment Time**: Select your preferred appointment time.
- **Type your name for appointment confirmation**: Type your name to confirm your appointment.
- **Any additional requests or instructions?**: If you have any additional requests or instructions, please provide them here.
