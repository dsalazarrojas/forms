# schedule_your_strategy_call - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users schedule a strategy call with a lead or customer. The goal is to collect relevant information to facilitate effective communication and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of lead or customer you are working with (Lead, Customer, Prospect, or Other).
2. Choose the time of day you'd like to have the call (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
3. Choose the duration of the call (30 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes, or 120 minutes).
4. Select the follow-up methods you'd like to use (Email, Phone, Post, or None).
5. Enter any additional details about the lead or customer (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead/Client Type** (`lead_details`): This field helps us understand who the call is about. Please select Lead, Customer, Prospect, or Other.
* **Lead/Client Details** (`lead_details_page`): Here, you can add any extra information about the lead or customer that's helpful for the call.
* **Call Time** (`call_time`): Choose when you'd like to have the call (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime).
* **Call Duration** (`call_duration`): Select the length of the call (30 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes, or 120 minutes).
* **Follow-up Methods** (`follow_up`): Decide on the follow-up methods you'd like to use (Email, Phone, Post, or None).
* **Follow-up Methods** (`follow_up_page`): This field is similar to the above one but might be used for different purposes.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): This field is related to the follow-up methods. Here, you can choose when you'd like to send follow-ups (24 hours, 48 hours, 72 hours, or 96 hours).

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to help us have a productive and effective call.
- Use this form for scheduling strategy calls only. Do not use it for duplicate efforts or collecting unnecessary information.
