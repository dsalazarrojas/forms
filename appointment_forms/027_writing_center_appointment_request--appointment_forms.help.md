# writing_center_appointment_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request appointments for the writing center. It collects information from students to determine their writing needs and schedule an appointment with a suitable facilitator. The form is divided into two pages and requires basic information about the student, their preferred subject, and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first page with the requested information.
2. Choose your preferred level of support (e.g., Writing Specialist, Writing Tutor, English Professor).
3. Select the start and end dates and times for your appointment.
4. Provide any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_info**: Enter a brief description of your needs or concerns about writing. This can be a general statement about your writing challenges or specific questions you have.
* **subject**: Specify the subject or topic you need help with (e.g., essay writing, research paper, grammar).
* **level**: Choose the level of support you require:
	+ 0-2 (Beginner)
	+ 2-4 (Intermediate)
	+ 4-6 (Advanced)
* **start_date** and **start_time**: Select the date and time you want to have your appointment. This is the start date and time for your appointment.
* **end_time**: Select the date and time you want your appointment to end. This is the end date and time for your appointment.
* **preferred_facilitator**: Choose one or more facilitators you prefer to work with from the list. You can select multiple facilitators, and they will work with you together.
* **additional_comments**: If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here. This is an opportunity to provide more information about your needs or concerns.

Note that this form does not require any additional information and fields can be filled out in any order.
