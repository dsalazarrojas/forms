# Salon Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule an appointment at a salon, ensuring that the necessary information is collected from the customer for a smooth and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Salon Name (Salon Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the salon where you would like to make the appointment.
2. **Date (Date, date, optional)**: Select a date for your appointment. If you leave this blank, the salon will contact you to discuss available dates.
3. **Time (Time, time, optional)**: Choose a time for your appointment. If you leave this blank, the salon will contact you to discuss available times.
4. **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required)**: Enter your name so the salon can address you correctly during your appointment.
5. **Customer Email (Customer Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address so the salon can contact you with appointment details and reminders.
6. **Customer Phone Number (Customer Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number so the salon can contact you with appointment details and reminders.
7. **Appointment Reason (Appointment Reason, note, optional)**: If there's anything specific you'd like to mention about your appointment, such as a special occasion or a particular concern, please let us know.
8. **Appointment Duration (Appointment Duration, number, optional)**: If you know the length of your appointment, you can specify it here. This will help the stylist prepare accordingly.
9. **Services (Services, select multiple, optional)**: Choose the services you would like to receive during your appointment. You can select multiple options if applicable.
10. **Preferred Time Window (Preferred Time Window, select one, optional)**: Specify your preferred time of day for the appointment. Options include Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Salon Name (Salon Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the salon where you're making the appointment.
- **Date (Date, date, optional)**: Select a date for the appointment. If left blank, the salon will contact you to discuss dates.
- **Time (Time, time, optional)**: Choose a time for the appointment. If left blank, the salon will contact you to discuss times.
- **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required)**: Enter your name for salon staff to address you correctly during your appointment.
- **Customer Email (Customer Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address for appointment details and reminders.
- **Customer Phone Number (Customer Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number for appointment details and reminders.
- **Appointment Reason (Appointment Reason, note, optional)**: Mention any special occasions or concerns for the stylist to note.
- **Appointment Duration (Appointment Duration, number, optional)**: Specify the appointment duration if known for a more efficient experience.
- **Services (Services, select multiple, optional)**: Choose the services you require during your appointment. Multiple options can be selected if applicable.
- **Preferred Time Window (Preferred Time Window, select one, optional)**: Select a preferred time of day for the appointment.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing contact information and appointment details.
- If you leave certain fields blank, the salon will contact you to complete them.
- Please select all relevant services to get the most out of your appointment.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the 'Appointment Reason' field.
