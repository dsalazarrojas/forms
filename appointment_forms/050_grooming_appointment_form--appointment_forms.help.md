# Grooming Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grooming Appointment Form is designed to collect essential information from customers for their upcoming appointments. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently, making it easier for our team to prepare for each appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Customer Details" page and fill out the customer's name, which will be displayed in our system.
2.  Move on to the "Customer Phone" page and enter the customer's contact phone number.
3.  Proceed to the "Customer Email" page and input the customer's email address.
4.  On the "Notes" page, include any additional comments or instructions for the appointment that you would like to share with our team.
5.  In the "Date" page, choose a date for the appointment from the calendar.
6.  In the "Time" page, select a time for the appointment from the time picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details, text, required): Enter the customer's full name for our records.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number so we can get in touch with them easily.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter the customer's email address so we can send them appointment reminders and confirmations.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the appointment that you would like to share with our team.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Choose a date for the appointment from the calendar. Make sure to pick a date that is in the future and within our available time slots.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Select a time for the appointment from the time picker. Ensure the time you choose is within our available time slots and allows sufficient time for the appointment to take place.
