# Hippotherapy Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hippotherapy Interest Form is used to collect information from potential clients who are interested in hippotherapy services. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences and provide you with a better understanding of our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as required) to the best of your ability.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about yourself and your needs.
3. If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide detailed information in the medical condition details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name (<name>, <type>): text
* This field is used to collect your name, which will be used to identify you in our records.
* Please provide your full name as you would like it to be used in our records.

### Email Address (<name>, <type>): email
* This field is used to collect your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* Please provide a valid email address that you can be reached at.

### Phone Number (<name>, <type>): text
* This field is used to collect your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
* Please provide a valid phone number that you can be reached at.

### Date of Birth (<name>, <type>): date
* This field is used to collect your date of birth, which will help us understand your age and suitability for our services.
* Please provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Preferred Hippotherapy Type (<name>, <type>): select_one
* This field is used to collect your preferred type of hippotherapy service.
* Please select one of the options provided:

  - Horseback riding
  - Hippo therapy
  - Equine assisted therapy

### Frequency of Sessions (<name>, <type>): select_multiple
* This field is used to collect the frequency of sessions you are interested in.
* Please select one or more of the options provided:

  - Daily
  - Weekly
  - Monthly

### Available Start Time (<name>, <type>): time
* This field is used to collect the time you are available to start your sessions.
* Please provide a valid time in the format HH:MM.

### Note (<name>, <type>): note
* This field is used to collect any additional information or comments you may have.
* Please provide any relevant information that will help us better understand your needs.

### Medical Condition (<name>, <type>): select_one
* This field is used to collect information about any medical conditions you may have.
* Please select one of the options provided:

  - None
  - Physical disability
  - Emotional or psychological condition
  - Medical condition

### Medical Condition Details (<name>, <type>): text
* This field is used to collect additional information about your medical condition.
* Please provide a detailed description of your medical condition, if applicable.

### Emergency Contact Name (<name>, <type>): text
* This field is used to collect the name of your emergency contact.
* Please provide the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Phone (<name>, <type>): text
* This field is used to collect the phone number of your emergency contact.
* Please provide a valid phone number that your emergency contact can be reached at.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (<name>, <type>): select_one
* This field is used to collect the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* Please select one of the options provided:

  - Parent
  - Guardian
  - Partner
  - Friend
  - Family member

### Emergency Contact Email (<name>, <type>): email
* This field is used to collect the email address of your emergency contact.
* Please provide a valid email address that your emergency contact can be reached at.

### Medical Insurance Provider (<name>, <type>): text
* This field is used to collect the name of your medical insurance provider.
* Please provide the name of your medical insurance provider, if applicable.
