# hvac_maintenance_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book HVAC maintenance appointments or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the category for your maintenance or service.
2. Choose the type of service or repair needed.
3. Enter the date and time for the maintenance or service.
4. Select the staff member you would like to assign the task to.
5. Add any additional comments if necessary.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category for your maintenance or service. This field is required and must be one of the following options: Appointment Forms, Service Requests, or Maintenance.
- **Service Type** (`Service Type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of service or repair needed. This field is required and must be one of the following options: Service or Repair.
- **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date for the maintenance or service.
- **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the maintenance or service (if applicable).
- **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, select_one, required): Select the staff member you would like to assign the task to.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the maintenance or service.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (not required but recommended for communication purposes).
- **Submit** (`Submit`, text, optional): Submit the form to complete the maintenance or service booking.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct category and service type for your maintenance or service.
* Enter a valid date and time for the maintenance or service.
* Select a staff member who can perform the maintenance or service.
* Add any additional comments if necessary.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
