# Critical Incident Response Session Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to schedule and document critical incident response sessions. It helps responders gather information about the incident, including the date, location, and type, to ensure they are prepared for the session and can respond effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
* Use this form for critical incident response sessions only.
* Ensure you have the necessary approvals and authorities before scheduling the session.
* Review and update your answers as necessary throughout the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date for the critical incident response session. This field is optional, but recommended for planning and scheduling purposes.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, Text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Status** (`critical_incident_status`, Select, required: false): Choose the current status of the incident: Open, In Progress, or Closed.
* **Start Date** (`incident_date_start`, Date, required: false): Enter the start date of the incident, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`incident_date_end`, Date, required: false): Enter the end date of the incident, if applicable.
* **Category** (`critical_incident_category`, Text, required: false): Select the category of the incident, if applicable.
* **Responders** (`critical_incident_responders`, Select, required: false): Choose who responded to the incident: Team, Individual, or Both.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, Time, required: false): Enter the start time for the session, if applicable.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, Number, required: false): Enter the duration of the session in hours, if applicable.
* **Incident Type** (`critical_incident_type`, Select, required: false): Choose the type of the incident: Natural Disaster, Man-made, or Both.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, Note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or information about the session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, Select, required: false): Choose where the session will take place: Virtual or In Person.

Note: The fields that are marked as "required: false" are not mandatory, but it's recommended to fill them out for better planning and documentation.
