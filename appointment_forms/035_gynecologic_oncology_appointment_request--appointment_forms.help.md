# gynecologic_oncology_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an appointment in the gynecologic oncology department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2.  Make sure to select the correct appointment type from the options provided.
3.  Enter your preferred date and time for the appointment.
4.  Provide any additional contact information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Appointment Request**
*   Appointment Request: This field is where you can briefly explain the reason for your appointment request. Please provide as much detail as possible.

### 2. **Patient Info**
*   Patient Info: Enter any relevant information about the patient, such as their name, age, or medical condition.

### 3. **Medical History**
*   Medical History: Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments or surgeries.

### 4. **Appointment Details**
*   Appointment Details: Describe the specific details of the appointment, such as any special requirements or requests.

### 5. **Preferred Date & Time**
*   Preferred Date & Time: Select a date and time that suits you best for the appointment.

### 6. **Contact Details**
*   Contact Details: Provide any additional contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

### 7. **Appointment Type**
*   Appointment Type: Select the type of appointment you are requesting, such as Initial Consultation, Follow Up, or Surgery.

### 8. **Doctor's Name**
*   Doctor's Name: Enter the name of the doctor you are requesting to see, if applicable.
