# lash_technician_team_meeting_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect meeting details and attendees for a team meeting. It's crucial to fill it out accurately to maintain clear communication and ensure that the meeting is well-organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the meeting details (page 1) including meeting attendees, date, and notes.
- On page 2, select the desired outcome of the meeting and the assigned user.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields ( Meeting Details, Date, Meeting Outcome, and Assigned User) and select at least one attendee for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the meeting to be discussed during the team meeting.
* **Additional Meeting Details** (`meeting_details_2`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the meeting that are relevant to its discussion.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more team members who will be attending the meeting.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, `text`, required): Record any important notes or decisions made during the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcome** (`meeting_outcome`, `select_one`, required): Select the outcome of the meeting (Successful, Unsuccessful, or Pending).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_one`, required): Select the user assigned to the meeting (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Assigned User ID** (`assigned_user_id`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the assigned user ID is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned User Name** (`assigned_user_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the assigned user if different from the assigned user.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to submit the form successfully.
- Be specific and clear when filling in the meeting details and notes.
- Select the correct outcome of the meeting based on its progress.
