# Expedited Boarding Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Expedited Boarding Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your travel plans. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to help us better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address associated with your travel.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to travel.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your flight number for verification.
* **Travel Prophets** (`travel_prophets`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the reason for your travel.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reason for your travel.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of travel (e.g., business, leisure).
* **Airline** (`airline`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the airline you are traveling with.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the destination of your travel.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the duration of your trip.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter how often you travel.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the purpose of your travel.
* **Travel Origin** (`travel_origin_prophets`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the origin of your travel.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you return from your trip.
* **Travel Group** (`travel_group`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the group you are traveling with (e.g., family, friends).
* **Ticket Number** (`ticket_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your ticket number for verification.
* **Prophets Gate** (`boarding_gate`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the boarding gate.
* **Flight Status** (`flight_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the flight status (e.g., delayed, cancelled).
* **Travel Type Prophets** (`travel_type_prophets`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of travel (e.g., domestic, international).
* **Travel Class** (`travel_class`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the travel class (e.g., economy, business).
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the travel agent's name.
* **Prophets Note** (`travel_note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any notes about your travel.
* **Ticketing Fee** (`ticketing_fee`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the ticketing fee.
