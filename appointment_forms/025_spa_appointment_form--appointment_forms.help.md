# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "<string>" form is used to create or edit appointments for clients. This form collects client information, appointment details, and staff assignments to schedule a successful appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the appointment in the "Title" field.
2. Select the client from the "Client Name" dropdown list or enter their name manually.
3. Choose the service provided from the "Service" dropdown list.
4. Select the appointment date and time using the calendar and clock tools.
5. Enter the appointment duration in the "Duration" field.
6. Add any notes or messages for the appointment in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the client's email address and phone number.
8. Enter the client's address.
9. Choose the staff member assigned to the appointment from the "Staff" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Title** (Title, text, required): Enter a brief description of the appointment.
- **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional): Select the client's name from the dropdown list, or enter their name manually.
- **Service** (Service, select_one, optional): Choose the service provided for the appointment.
- **Date** (Date, date, optional): Select the appointment date using the calendar tool.
- **Time** (Time, time, optional): Select the appointment time using the clock tool.
- **Duration** (Duration, number, optional): Enter the appointment duration in minutes.
- **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any notes or messages for the appointment.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the client's address.
- **Message** (Message, text, optional): Add any additional information for the appointment.
- **Service Description** (Service Description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the service.
- **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): Enter any additional service details.
- **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, date, optional): Select the appointment date using the calendar tool.
- **Appointment Time** (Appointment Time, time, optional): Select the appointment time using the clock tool.
- **Client Email** (Client Email, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
- **Client Phone** (Client Phone, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Staff Name** (Staff Name, text, optional): Enter the staff member's name.
- **Staff Email** (Staff Email, email, optional): Enter the staff member's email address.
- **Staff Phone** (Staff Phone, text, optional): Enter the staff member's phone number.
- **Service 2** (Service 2, select_one, optional): Choose the service 2 provided for the appointment.
- **Appointment Note** (Appointment Note, note, optional): Add any additional appointment notes.
- **Client Address** (Client Address, text, optional): Enter the client's address.
- **Service Description 2** (Service Description 2, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the service 2.
- **Appointment Duration** (Appointment Duration, number, optional): Enter the appointment duration in minutes.
- **Staff** (Staff, select_one, optional): Choose the staff member assigned to the appointment.
- **Other Services** (Other Services, text, optional): This field is not used in the current form.
