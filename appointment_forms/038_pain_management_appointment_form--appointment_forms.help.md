# Pain Management Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pain Management Appointment Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to gather information about your pain management needs before your appointment. This form will help your healthcare provider understand your specific needs and tailor the appointment to address them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions accurately and honestly to ensure your healthcare provider has the necessary information to provide you with the best possible care.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information available before starting to complete the form.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or concerns.
* **Pain Type** (`pain_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of pain you are experiencing (e.g., chronic, acute, etc.).
* **Appointment Reason** (`appointment_reason`, text, required): Please describe the reason for your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Please select the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Please select the time of your appointment.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Please enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, number, required): Please estimate the length of your appointment.
* **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, date, required): Please select the date for your next visit.
* **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_one, required): Please select if you are part of a medical team (e.g., yes, no).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter the location of your appointment (e.g., clinic, hospital, etc.).
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Please provide any additional medical notes or concerns.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_multiple, required): Please select the type of appointment (e.g., routine, emergency, etc.).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, required): Please describe your follow-up needs.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments about your follow-up needs.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_multiple, required): Please select your patient status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Pain Scale** (`pain_scale`, number, required): Please select the pain scale rating (e.g., 1-10).
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, text, required): Please enter any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): Please provide any information about your medication.

Please take your time to complete this form accurately and honestly, and if you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
