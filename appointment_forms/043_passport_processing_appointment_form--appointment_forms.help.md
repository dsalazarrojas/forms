# passport_processing_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for processing passport-related appointments, collecting user information and preferences for efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the required and optional fields to provide your information and preferences.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information (User Information, text, optional)**: Enter your name and other identifying information.
*   **Contact Number (Contact Number, text, optional)**: Provide your contact number for follow-up communication.
*   **Preferred Language (Preferred Language, select_one, optional)**: Choose your preferred language for communication (English, French, Spanish).
*   **Schedule Time (Schedule Time, time, optional)**: Enter your preferred schedule time for appointment.
*   **Schedule Date (Schedule Date, date, optional)**: Enter your preferred schedule date for appointment.
*   **Passport Number (Passport Number, text, optional)**: Enter your passport number for identification.
*   **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Provide any additional notes or comments for the appointment.
*   **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional)**: Choose the tool to be assigned for your appointment (chatjimmy, chatbot, human agent).
*   **Category (Category, select_multiple, optional)**: Select relevant categories for your appointment (Appointment Forms, Application Forms, User Forms).

## Tips

*   Fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid delays in processing your appointment.
