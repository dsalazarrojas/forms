# Mary Kay Beauty Profile By Julie Potts - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect essential information about your beauty preferences and consultant's details for a personalized shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your favorite product from the list in the "Favorite Product" field.
5. Select your preferred features from the list in the "Favorite Feature" field.
6. Enter the consultant's name in the "Consultant Name" field.
7. Enter the consultant's email address in the "Consultant Email" field.
8. Enter the consultant's phone number in the "Consultant Phone" field.
9. Select the consultant's favorite product from the list in the "Consultant Favorite Product" field.
10. Select the consultant's favorite feature from the list in the "Consultant Favorite Feature" field.
11. Add any additional notes about your preferences or the consultant's expertise in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name to help us understand who you are.
* **Email**: Provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Date**: This field is not required.
* **Time**: This field is not required.
* **Experience**: This field is not required.
* **Favorite Product**: Choose one product that you love from our range.
* **Favorite Feature**: Select multiple features that interest you the most.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the consultant's name who will be working with you.
* **Consultant Email**: Enter the consultant's email address.
* **Consultant Phone**: Enter the consultant's phone number.
* **Consultant Experience**: This field is not required.
* **Consultant Favorite Product**: Select one product that the consultant loves.
* **Consultant Favorite Feature**: Select multiple features that the consultant specializes in.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or details about your preferences.
