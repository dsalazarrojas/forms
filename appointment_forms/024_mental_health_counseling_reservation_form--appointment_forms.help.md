# mental_health_counseling_reservation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you schedule an appointment with a mental health counselor. Fill out this form to reserve a session and provide essential information about your appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Appointment Information** (`appointment_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the appointment you are scheduling.
* **Therapist Information** (`therapist_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and role of the therapist you are scheduling with.
* **Schedule Information** (`schedule_info`, text, optional): Specify the date and time of the appointment.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment method and amount.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Indicate if you have any special requests for your appointment.
* **Confirm Information** (`confirm_info`, text, optional): Double-check your submission to ensure accuracy.
* **Review Information** (`review_info`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments about your appointment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this form to confirm your submission.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Specify the time of the appointment.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your therapist.
* **Therapist Role** (`therapist_role`, text, optional): Enter the role of your therapist.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the scheduled appointment.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Specify the time of the scheduled appointment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your payment method from the options provided:
	+ Cash
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Credit/Debit
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are paying.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
* **Special Request 1** (`special_request_1`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any special requests:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Special Request 2** (`special_request_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any other special requests:
	+ Yes
	+ No
