# Embassy Visit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you make a reservation for a visit to the embassy. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and efficient visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the visit date and time in the "Visit Date" and "Visit Time" fields.
2. Enter the embassy details in the "Embassy Details" field.
3. Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number in the "Contact Person" fields.
4. Enter your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
5. Provide any additional messages or requests in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Visit Date
 * **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, required/optional): Choose a date for your visit to the embassy.

### Embassy Details
 * **Embassy Details** (`embassy_details`, optional): Enter the details of the embassy you wish to visit.

### Visit Time
 * **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, optional): Choose a time for your visit to the embassy.

### Contact Person
 * **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your visit.

### Email
 * **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
 * **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

### Message
 * **Message** (`message`, optional): Provide any additional message or request for your visit.

### Additional Request
 * **Additional Request** (`reservation_request_form_2`, optional): Enter any additional requests or requirements for your visit.
