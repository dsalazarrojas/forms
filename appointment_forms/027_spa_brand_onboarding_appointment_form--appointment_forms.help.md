# Spa Brand Onboarding Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the onboarding process of a spa brand.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field carefully and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that you complete the form in the correct order, following the navigation provided on each page.
3. If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, consult with your supervisor or a colleague before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding Information**: This page provides an overview of the onboarding process.
* **Client Id**: Enter the client's ID number to identify the client.
* **Spa Branch**: Enter the name of the spa branch where the onboarding will take place.
* **Service Date**: Select the date for the onboarding service.
* **Service Type**: Choose the type of service to be provided during onboarding.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time for the onboarding service.
* **Finish Time**: Enter the finish time for the onboarding service.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the onboarding service.
* **Number of Clients**: Enter the number of clients participating in the onboarding service.
* **Scheduling**: Choose the scheduling option for the onboarding service.
* **Scheduling Time**: Enter the time for the scheduling option.
* **Scheduling Date**: Select the date for the scheduling option.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about the onboarding service.
* **Onboarding Agreement**: Enter the onboarding agreement terms and conditions.
* **Onboarding Signature**: Sign the onboarding agreement to confirm.
* **Onboarding Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the onboarding service.
* **Onboarding Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the onboarding agreement.
* **Onboarding Agreement Time**: Enter the time of the onboarding agreement.
* **Onboarding Agreement Signature**: Sign the onboarding agreement to confirm.
* **Onboarding Agreement Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the onboarding agreement.
* **Spa Branch Email**: Enter the email address of the spa branch.
* **Spa Branch Phone**: Enter the phone number of the spa branch.
* **Onboarding Agreement Status**: Choose the status of the onboarding agreement.
* **Onboarding Agreement Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the onboarding agreement.
* **Onboarding Review**: Enter any review or feedback about the onboarding service.

## Tips
- Be careful to complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the form before submission to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure or have any questions, consult with your supervisor or colleague before proceeding.
