# Nervous System Coaching Call Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to register for a coaching call with the system, allowing them to schedule a call at a time that suits them best. The information collected will be used to prepare for the call and ensure that the client gets the most out of their coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are ready to schedule a call.
2. If you selected 'Yes', provide a brief description of your goals for the call in the 'What are your goals for the call?' field.
3. Enter your contact details, including your email address.
4. Describe what you expect from the call in the 'What to Expect' field.
5. If you have any nervous system concerns, please enter them in the 'Nervous System Concerns' field.
6. Choose your preferred call frequency (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly).
7. Choose your preferred call length (30 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes).
8. Select a time for the call using the 'Call Time' field.
9. Select a date for the call using the 'Call Date' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule a Call** (`Yes/No`, select_one, required: false): This field determines whether you are ready to schedule a call or not.
* **What are your goals for the call?** (`What are your goals for the call?`, text, required: false): A short description of what you hope to achieve during the call.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you to schedule the call.
* **What to Expect** (`What to Expect`, text, required: false): Describe what you expect from the call, so we can tailor the experience to your needs.
* **Nervous System Concerns** (`Nervous System Concerns`, text, required: false): If you have any nervous system concerns, please describe them here.
* **Call Frequency** (`Call Frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you would like to have calls (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly).
* **Call Length** (`Call Length`, select_one, required: false): Choose the length of each call (30 minutes, 60 minutes, 90 minutes).
* **Call Time** (`Call Time`, time, required: false): Select a time for the call.
* **Call Date** (`Call Date`, date, required: false): Select a date for the call.
* **Client Email** (`Client Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you to schedule the call.
