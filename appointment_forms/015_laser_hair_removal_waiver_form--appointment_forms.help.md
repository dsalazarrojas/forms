# Laser Hair Removal Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Hair Removal Waiver Form is designed to collect essential information from clients before undergoing laser hair removal procedures. This information helps us understand their medical history, understand potential risks, and ensure they are aware of the procedures involved. Please take a few minutes to read through this guide to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by carefully reading each field explanation to understand its purpose.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3.  Ensure all required fields are completed, indicated by the **required** label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Consent (client_consent)**: Please select all the options that apply to you:
	+ I understand the risks and procedures involved with this service
	+ I am aware of any allergies or sensitivities I may have that could interfere with this service
	+ I am over 18 years of age
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: Please enter any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities you may have. If you have no medical conditions, please enter "None".
* **Laser Risks (laser_risks)**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or concerns you have about laser hair removal.
* **Client Information (client_info)**: Please select your gender:
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Laser Technician (laser_technician)**: Please select your laser technician preference:
	+ Certified laser technician
	+ Untrained but has experience
	+ Trained by a dermatologist
* **Laser Equipment (laser_equipment)**: Please select the type of laser equipment used:
	+ Lumenis
	+ Lumenis or similar
	+ Other

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* This form is designed to ensure your comfort and safety during the laser hair removal process. Please take the time to carefully review each field and ensure accurate input.
