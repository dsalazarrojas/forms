# Spa Appointment Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for spa appointments, including customer contact details, appointment details, and confirmation of the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Information** (fields 8-12) with your personal details.
2. Provide your preferred **Appointment Details** (field 2) such as date and time for your appointment.
3. Choose whether you have access to the spa (field 3) by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Add any **Customer Message** (field 4) if you have any special requests or comments.
5. Confirm your appointment by selecting "Confirm" (field 20) or decline (field 21) if necessary.
6. If you need to cancel or decline your appointment, select "Cancel" (field 22) or "Decline" (field 21) respectively.
