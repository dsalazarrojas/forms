# emergency_services_schedule_preference - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow healthcare professionals to schedule emergency services, such as doctor's visits or hospital admissions, in accordance with their preferences and constraints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your emergency services shift preferences from the options provided.
2. Enter your availability for emergency services, choosing a date if desired.
3. Indicate your preferred shifts for emergency services.
4. Indicate any constraints for emergency services, such as whether you are available or not for certain shifts or dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Services Shift Preferences**: (`emergency_services_shift_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false) 
Select one or more options to indicate your emergency services shift preferences.
* **Emergency Services Availability**: (`emergency_services_availability`, date, required: false) 
Enter a date for which you are available for emergency services.
* **Emergency Services Preferred Shifts**: (`emergency_services_preferred_shifts`, text, required: false) 
Enter any shifts you prefer for emergency services.
* **Emergency Services Constraint 0**: (`emergency_services_constraint_0`, select_one, required: false) 
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a constraint for emergency services for this shift.
* **Emergency Services Constraint 1**: (`emergency_services_constraint_1`, select_one, required: false) 
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a constraint for emergency services for this shift.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all relevant options for your emergency services shift preferences.
- Enter a specific date for emergency services availability if you are available on a particular date.
- Be accurate when indicating your preferred shifts and constraints for emergency services.
