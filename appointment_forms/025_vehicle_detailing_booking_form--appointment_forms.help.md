# vehicle_detailing_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle_detailing_booking_form is used to book a vehicle detailing service for customers. Customers can choose the type of detailing service they need and provide their contact information to schedule an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the type of detailing service you need for your vehicle from the "Vehicle Detailing" page.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and contact number.
3. Provide the vehicle's registration number, year made, and model.
4. Select the date and time for your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Detailing** (`vehicle_detailing_booking_form_page_1`, select_one): Choose the type of detailing service you need, such as Vehicle Detailing, Interior Detailing, or Exterior Detailing.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about your appointment.
* **Vehicle Registration Num** (`vehicle_registration_num`, text): Enter your vehicle's registration number for identification purposes.
* **Year Made** (`vehicle_year_made`, number): Enter the year your vehicle was made.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text): Enter the make and model of your vehicle for identification purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your contact number so we can reach you about your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date): Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time): Select the time you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, select_multiple): Choose the services you would like to book, such as Full detailing, Interior detailing, or Exterior detailing.

## Tips
- Make sure to choose the correct type of detailing service for your vehicle.
- Enter your contact information accurately so we can reach you about your appointment.
- Select the correct date and time for your appointment.
- Choose the services you need to book for your vehicle.
