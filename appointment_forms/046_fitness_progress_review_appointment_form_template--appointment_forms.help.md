# Fitness Progress Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store client appointment information, including appointment details and progress updates. It is intended to be filled out by staff members after each appointment with a client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the appointment in the "form_title" field. This field should be a brief description of the meeting or session.
2. In the "client_information" field, enter any relevant details about the client, such as their name, contact information, or other identifying details.
3. Choose the status of the appointment in the "appointment_details" field. Options include "Yes" and "No", likely indicating whether the appointment was held or not.
4. Select the date of the appointment in the "date" field. This should be a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the start time of the appointment in the "start_time" field. This should be in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
6. Enter the end time of the appointment in the "end_time" field. This should be in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
7. Enter the location of the appointment in the "location" field. This could include the address or other details about where the meeting took place.
8. Enter a brief description of the appointment in the "description" field.
9. Choose the status of the appointment in the "appointment_status" field. Options include "Active" and "Inactive", likely indicating whether the appointment is ongoing or complete.
10. Enter the client's phone number in the "client_phone_number" field.
11. Enter the client's email address in the "client_email" field.
12. Enter any relevant note or comments in the "note" field.
13. Choose whether an assigned user was present during the appointment in the "assigned_user" field. Options include "Yes" and "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the appointment.
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the client.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the appointment was held ("Yes") or not ("No").
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the appointment (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the appointment (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the appointment (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the appointment (e.g. address).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the appointment.
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the appointment is active or inactive ("Active" or "Inactive").
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any relevant note or comments about the appointment.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Choose whether an assigned user was present during the appointment ("Yes" or "No").
