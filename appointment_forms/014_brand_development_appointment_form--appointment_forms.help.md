# Brand Development Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by potential clients to provide information about their brand development goals and preferences for an appointment with a consultant. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, helping you to complete it efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Client name
	* Contact email
	* Preferred appointment date
	* Meeting format (in person or virtual)
2. Provide additional information if applicable:
	* Project goals
	* Budget range
	* Preferred appointment time (if you have a specific time in mind)
3. Add any additional notes that might be helpful for the consultant to know about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client name (1)
* **Client name**: `Client name` (text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the person attending the appointment.

### Contact email (2)
* **Contact email**: `Contact email` (email, required)
	+ Enter your email address for appointment confirmation and any follow-up communication.

### Preferred appointment date (3)
* **Preferred appointment date**: `Choose a convenient date` (date, required)
	+ Select a date that suits you best for the appointment.

### Preferred appointment time (4)
* **Preferred appointment time**: `Morning, afternoon, or evening preference` (time, optional)
	+ If you have a specific time in mind, choose it from the options. Otherwise, leave it blank.

### Project goals (5)
* **Project goals**: `Key objectives for brand development` (text, optional)
	+ Enter your key objectives and goals for the brand development project. This will help the consultant understand your requirements.

### Budget range (6)
* **Budget range**: `Estimated budget for services` (select one, optional)
	+ Choose your estimated budget range for the services provided.

### Meeting format (7)
* **Meeting format**: `In person or virtual` (select one, required)
	+ Choose whether you prefer an in-person or virtual meeting.

### Additional notes (8)
* **Additional notes**: `Any other details for the consultant` (text, optional)
	+ Enter any other details that might be helpful for the consultant to know about your appointment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information in the required fields.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
