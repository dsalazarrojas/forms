# Makeup Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Intake Form is used by clients to provide information about their appointment schedule and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully and fill out the required and optional fields as applicable.
* Make sure to check the box next to the "Required" label, indicating which fields are mandatory.
* Enter your responses in the corresponding fields.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting it to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be addressed during your appointment.
* **Appointment Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date and time of your appointment. If you don't have a specific date in mind, you can leave this field blank.
* **Start Time (Time, Optional)**: Enter the start time of your appointment. If you're not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Location (Text, Optional)**: Enter the location where your appointment will take place. If you're not sure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Service (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the service you would like to book. Please select only one option.
* **Client Preferences (Text, Optional)**: Enter any specific preferences or requests you have for your appointment. This could include things like "Please do not play any loud music" or "I have a specific skin type."
* **Artist Preferences (Text, Optional)**: Enter any specific preferences or requests you have for your artist. This could include things like "I would like to have a female artist" or "Please do not talk about politics."
* **Notes (Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional notes or information you feel is relevant to your appointment. This is a text field, but it's meant for any extra details you'd like to include. Please keep your notes concise and relevant.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can contact us to clarify.
* If you have any additional requests or preferences not listed here, please let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate them.
