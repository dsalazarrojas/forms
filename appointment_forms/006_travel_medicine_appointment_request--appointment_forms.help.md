# Travel Medicine Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from patients who are planning to travel, ensuring they receive proper medical care and advice for their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information in the "Patient Info" section.
2. Provide details about your travel in the "Travel Details" section.
3. Enter your travel dates and time in the "Travel Date" and "Travel Time" sections.
4. Specify the location of your travel in the "Travel Location" section.
5. Explain the reason for your travel in the "Reason for Travel" section.
6. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
7. If you have any medical information relevant to your travel, please share it in the "Medical Info" section.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (text, required/optional): This section is used to collect your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Travel Details** (text, required/optional): Please describe your travel plans, including the destination, mode of transportation, and duration of travel.
* **Travel Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Travel Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time you will be traveling.
* **Travel Location** (text, required/optional): Specify the destination(s) of your travel, including city and country names.
* **Reason for Travel** (text, required/optional): Explain the purpose of your trip, e.g., leisure, business, or medical reasons.
* **Contact Info** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including phone number, email, or other contact information.
* **Medical Info** (text, required/optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns, please provide relevant medical information.
* **Submit** (text, required/optional): Click "Submit" to complete and submit your form.
