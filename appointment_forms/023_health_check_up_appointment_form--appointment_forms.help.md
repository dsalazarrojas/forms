# health_check_up_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health check up appointment form is used for scheduling and confirming appointments with a healthcare provider, taking into account various aspects of the appointment such as doctor preferences, time slots, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

* Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, and Phone.
* Select your preferred Doctor's Name, Medical Record Number, and Medical Condition.
* Choose your preferred Time of Last Checkup and Date of Last Checkup.
* Select your preferred Doctor for the appointment (if applicable).
* Choose your preferred Time and Date for the appointment.
* Select your preferred Medical Specialty and Appointment Type.
* Update the appointment status as required.
* Add any additional notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Doctor's Name** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Please select the doctor you prefer to see.
* **Medical Record Number** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, please enter your medical record number.
* **Date of Last Checkup** (`8`, `date`, required/optional): Please enter the date of your last checkup.
* **Time of Last Checkup** (`9`, `time`, required/optional): Please enter the time of your last checkup.
* **Medical Condition** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your current medical condition.
* **Preferred Doctor** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred doctor.
* **Preferred Time** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Preferred Date** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Medical Specialty** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the medical specialty you require.
* **Appointment Type** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of appointment you prefer.
* **Appointment Status** (`16`, `select_one`, required/optional): Update the appointment status as required.
* **Notes** (`17`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Assigned Doctor** (`18`, `select_one`, required/optional): If applicable, select the doctor assigned to you.
* **Confirm Doctor** (`19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the doctor who confirmed your appointment.
* **Confirm Date** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the confirmed date of the appointment.
* **Confirm Time** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the confirmed time of the appointment.
* **Confirm Status** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the confirm status of the appointment.
* **Confirm Reason** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a reason for any confirm status changes.
* **Confirm Notes** (`24`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional confirm notes.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`25`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm or reject the appointment as required.

This form is designed to be flexible, so please feel free to adjust the fields according to your needs.
