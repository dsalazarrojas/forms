# home-security-system-bundle-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "home-security-system-bundle-order-form" is used to gather information for ordering a home security system bundle. This form captures essential details about the customer and the bundle they wish to purchase.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields: Customer Name, Contact Number, Customer Email, Bundle Type, Installation Date, Installation Address, System Description, Bundle Price, Customer Agreement, Bundle Status, Installation Method, and Installation Location.
2.  Provide optional fields as necessary: Bundle Type, Installation Time, Customer Comments, Customer References, Customer Name 2, Pros and Cons, System Features, System Advantages, System Disadvantages.
3.  Ensure the customer email, contact number, and installation address are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the customer.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Bundle Type** (`system_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of bundle you wish to purchase.
*   **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of installation.
*   **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of installation.
*   **Installation Address** (`installation_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the bundle will be installed.
*   **System Description** (`system_description`, `select_one`, required): Briefly describe the bundle's features and functionalities.
*   **Bundle Price** (`bundle_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the bundle.
*   **Customer Agreement** (`customer_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you agree to the terms of service.
*   **Bundle Status** (`bundle_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the bundle's status (Active or Inactive).
*   **Installation Method** (`installation_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method of installation (Yes or No).
*   **Installation Location** (`installation_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of installation.
*   **Customer Name 2** (`customer_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional names if required.
*   **Pros and Cons** (`pros_and_cons`, `select_multiple`, optional): List the pros and cons of the bundle if necessary.
*   **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about the bundle.
*   **Customer References** (`customer_references`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any customer references if applicable.
*   **System Features** (`system_features`, `text`, optional): Describe the features of the bundle.
*   **System Advantages** (`system_advantages`, `select_multiple`, optional): List the advantages of the bundle.
*   **System Disadvantages** (`system_disadvantages`, `select_multiple`, optional): List the disadvantages of the bundle.
