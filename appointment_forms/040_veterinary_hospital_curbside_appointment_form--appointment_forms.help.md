# Veterinary Hospital Curbside Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for pet owners to schedule a curbside appointment at the veterinary hospital. It helps to collect necessary information about the pet and the appointment details in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pet's type from the provided options.
2. Enter the date and time of the appointment.
3. Enter the pet's name, weight, height, and age.
4. Select the reason for the visit.
5. Select the desired services for the pet.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.
7. Select the veterinarian assigned to the appointment.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of pet you are scheduling the appointment for (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the pet.
* **Pet Owner Name** (`pet_owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the pet owner.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **Service** (`service`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the services needed for the pet (Nail Trim, Bath, Grooming, Vaccination, Health Check).
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, note, required: false): Enter any additional details about the selected services.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): Enter the appointment time.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the appointment date.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number, required: false): Enter the weight of the pet.
* **Pet Height** (`pet_height`, number, required: false): Enter the height of the pet.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, required: false): Enter the age of the pet.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_visit`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for the visit.
* **Pet Species** (`pet_species`, select_one, required: false): Select the species of the pet (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, required: false): Enter the breed of the pet.
* **Pet Health Conditions** (`pet_health_conditions`, text, required: false): Enter any health conditions the pet may have.
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments from the owner's perspective.
* **Veterinary Hospital Notes** (`hospital_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments from the veterinary hospital's perspective.
* **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you want to be seen by a veterinarian.
* **Vet Name** (`vet_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the veterinarian assigned to the appointment.
* **Vet Phone** (`vet_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the veterinarian assigned to the appointment.
* **Vet Email** (`vet_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the veterinarian assigned to the appointment.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the submission (In Progress, Completed, Canceled).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.

Note: Fields marked as `required: false` are optional and can be filled out if desired.
