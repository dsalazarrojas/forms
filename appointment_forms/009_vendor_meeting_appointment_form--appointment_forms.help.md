# Vendor Meeting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule meetings between vendors and internal teams. It helps us gather information about the meeting's date, time, purpose, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately.**
2. **Submit the form before the meeting date.**
3. **Make sure to answer "Yes" or "No" for the "Vendor" field accurately.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, Date, Required: No): Enter the meeting date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, Time, Required: No): Enter the meeting time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Purpose** (`meeting_purpose`, Text, Required: No): Describe the meeting's goal or topic.
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, Select One, Required: No): Check if this meeting is with a vendor. Check "Yes" if the meeting is with a vendor, and "No" otherwise.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, Number, Required: No): Enter the meeting duration in minutes. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your internal team representative.
