# Tarot Reading Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information needed to book a tarot reading appointment. You can use it to schedule a reading with a tarot card reader, specifying the type of reading, date, and start time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, marked as (Required).
2. Select the type of reading you're interested in (Tarot reading, Card reading, or Palm reading).
3. Provide your email address and phone number for communication with the reader.
4. Choose the location for the appointment (optional).
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your reading experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information, text, Required)**: 
  This field is for your contact information. Enter your name, address, or any other details about yourself that the reader needs to know.
* **Reading Type (Reading Type, select_one, Required)**: 
  Choose the type of reading you want to have (Tarot, Card, or Palm).
* **Date (Date, date, Required)**: 
  Enter the date of your appointment. This field is required to schedule the reading.
* **Start Time (Start Time, time, Required)**: 
  Enter the start time of your appointment. This field is essential for the reader to know when to expect you.
* **Duration (Duration, number, Required)**: 
  Specify how long you want the reading to last. This field should be used to inform the reader how much time they need to allocate for the reading.
* **Tarot Card Reader (Tarot Card Reader, select_one, Required)**: 
  Choose the tarot card reader you want to work with.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time, time, Required)**: 
  This field is for the reader to know when to expect you.
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, date, Required)**: 
  Enter the date of your appointment with the reader.
* **Location (Location, select_multiple, Optional)**: 
  Choose the location where you want to have the reading.
* **Note (Note, note, Optional)**: 
  Add any comments or notes about your reading experience.
* **Email (Email, email, Required)**: 
  Enter your email address so the reader can contact you.
* **Phone (Phone, text, Required)**: 
  Provide your phone number for the reader to contact you.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and help users schedule successful appointments with tarot card readers.
