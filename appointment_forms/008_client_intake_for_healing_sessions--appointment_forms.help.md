# Client Intake For Healing Sessions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for a healing session, a therapeutic process aimed at promoting physical, emotional, and mental well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. If you have any wellness history or previous experiences, please describe them in the "Wellness History" field.
5. Answer the question "What is the primary reason for seeking a healing session today?" in the "Primary Reason for Seeking a Healing Session" field.
6. Indicate if you have previous experience with professional healing sessions by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Have you Experienced Professional Healing Sessions Before?" field.
7. Describe your current physical health in the "Current Physical Health" field by selecting one of the provided options.
8. If you are currently under the care of a physician, select "True" or "False" in the "Are You Currently Under the Care of a Physician?" field.
9. Choose your preferred session length from the options provided in the "Preferred Session Length" field.
10. If you have any specific areas you wish to focus on during the session, please describe them in the "Areas of Focus" field.
11. Review and acknowledge that this session is not a replacement for traditional medical care in the "I Understand This Session is Not a Replacement for Traditional Medical Care" field.
12. Finally, sign your full name in the "Signature - Type Full Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (1)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Wellness History (4)**: Describe any relevant wellness experiences or history.
* **Primary Reason for Seeking a Healing Session (5)**: Explain why you are seeking a healing session today.
* **Have You Experienced Professional Healing Sessions Before? (6)**: Select "True" if you have had previous sessions, "False" otherwise.
* **Current Physical Health (7)**: Choose how you would describe your current physical health from the provided options.
* **Are You Currently Under the Care of a Physician? (8)**: Select "True" if you are under a physician's care, "False" otherwise.
* **Preferred Session Length (10)**: Choose your preferred session length from the options provided.
* **Areas of Focus (11)**: Describe any specific areas you wish to focus on during the session.
* **I Understand This Session is Not a Replacement for Traditional Medical Care (13)**: Acknowledge that this session is not a replacement for traditional medical care.
* **Signature - Type Full Name (14)**: Sign your full name.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any concerns or questions, contact us for assistance.
