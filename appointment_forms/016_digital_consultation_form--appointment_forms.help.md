# Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients for scheduled appointments at your establishment. This form helps to gather essential details about the client, their preferred appointment type, and the services they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Digital Consultation Form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form and select "Start Form".
2. Fill out each field according to your preferences and requirements.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Info**
* **Client Info** (`client-info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional information about yourself as a client. You can provide any details you think are relevant to your appointment.

### 2. **Appointment Type**
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment-type`, select one, optional): Please select the type of appointment you would like to schedule from the available options:
	+ Haircut
	+ Color correction
	+ Chemical treatment
	+ Other

### 3. **Appointment Date**
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment-date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment for.

### 4. **Appointment Time**
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment-time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to schedule your appointment for.

### 5. **Services**
* **Services** (`services`, select multiple, optional): Choose the services you would like to book for your appointment:
	+ Haircut
	+ Styling
	+ Trimming
	+ Chemical treatment
	+ Other

### 6. **Client Phone**
* **Client Phone** (`client-phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your appointment.

### 7. **Email**
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated about your appointment.

### 8. **Note**
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes or comments about your appointment.

### 9. **Submitted By**
* **Submitted By** (`submitted-by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form (usually yourself).

### 10. **Submitted Date**
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted-date`, date, optional): The date and time the form was submitted.
