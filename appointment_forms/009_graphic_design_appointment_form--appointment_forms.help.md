# Graphic Design Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a scheduled appointment between a client and a designer. It gathers necessary details for the meeting, including client contact information and project specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the type of project from the "project_type" dropdown list.
3. Enter the start date and start time for the appointment.
4. Enter the end date and end time for the appointment.
5. Provide the meeting location.
6. Enter any additional project details in the "Project Details" field.
7. Enter the client's email address.
8. Enter the client's phone number.
9. Enter the designer's email address.
10. Enter the designer's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text): Enter the name of the client for the scheduled appointment.
* **project_type** (Select One): Select the type of project from the provided options.
* **start_date** (Date): Enter the date the appointment is scheduled to start.
* **start_time** (Time): Enter the time the appointment is scheduled to start.
* **end_date** (Date): Enter the date the appointment is scheduled to end.
* **end_time** (Time): Enter the time the appointment is scheduled to end.
* **meeting_location** (Text): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **project_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the project.
* **client_email** (Email): Enter the client's email address.
* **client_phone** (Text): Enter the client's phone number.
* **designer_email** (Email): Enter the designer's email address.
* **designer_phone** (Text): Enter the designer's phone number.
* **note** (Note): Enter any additional notes about the appointment (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure successful communication and scheduling.
* If you have any questions or concerns while completing this form, reach out to the relevant parties or support team for assistance.
* Double-check the form for any required fields before submitting to avoid missing critical information.
