# Orthopedic Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthopedic Appointment Form is a digital form designed to help healthcare providers collect relevant information from patients before scheduling a consultation or treatment. This form is meant to gather essential details such as patient contact information, appointment scheduling preferences, and any specific medical notes or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information, including email and phone number.
2. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
3. Indicate the duration of your desired appointment.
4. Enter your email address so that we can contact you with reminders and updates.
5. Add any important notes about your condition or concerns related to your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): This is the field where you will enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar. Please select a date when you are available to attend the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select a time for your appointment that suits you best. Please choose from the available options or enter a custom time.
* **Provider Phone** (`provider_phone`, text, required): Enter your healthcare provider's contact number so that we can contact you with updates and reminders.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, text, required): Indicate the expected length of your appointment. This will help us ensure sufficient time for your consultation.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can send you appointment reminders and updates.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, note, required): Share any important medical notes, concerns, or questions you have for your appointment with us.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
- Please review your form submission carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any difficulties or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
