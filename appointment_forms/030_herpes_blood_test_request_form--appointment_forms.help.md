# herpes_blood_test_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a herpes blood test request. It will ask you for details about the patient, the blood sample, and the medical professional who will be performing the test. Please fill in the required fields to provide necessary information for the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **herpes_blood_test_request_form** field with a brief description of the test request.
2. Provide details about the **blood_sample_details_1**, including any relevant information about the sample.
3. Enter the **patient_information_1**, including any relevant medical history or conditions.
4. Enter the **patient_name_1**, the name of the patient who will be undergoing the test.
5. Select the **date_of_birth_1**, the patient's date of birth.
6. Enter the **time_of_collection_1**, the time the blood sample was collected.
7. Select the **medical_condition_1**, the medical condition that the test is related to (e.g. HSV-1).
8. Choose the **blood_collection_method_1**, the method used to collect the blood sample (e.g. Blood, Urine, or Saliva).
9. Select whether the patient **consent_1** is given for the test.
10. Enter the **contact_number_1**, the contact number of the patient or medical professional.
11. Enter the **email_1**, the email of the patient or medical professional.
12. Select the **medical_provider_1**, the medical provider who will be performing the test (e.g. Clinics).
13. Select the **medical_department_1**, the department where the test will be performed (e.g. Dermatology).
14. Select the type of **blood_type_1**, the type of blood to be tested (e.g. Blood).
15. Select the **blood_volume_1**, the volume of blood to be tested (e.g. 1, 2, or 3).
16. Enter the **test_request_reason_1**, the reason for the test request.
17. Enter any **additional_comments_1** about the test request.
18. Enter the name of the **contact_person_1**, the person to contact for further information.
