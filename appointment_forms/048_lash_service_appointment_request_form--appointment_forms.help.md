# Lash Service Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Service Appointment Request Form is a tool used to request and manage appointments for lash-related services. It is designed to gather essential information from clients to ensure that their appointments are scheduled correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact number in the "Client Name" and "Client Contact Number" fields, respectively.
2. Select the type of service you are requesting (e.g., Lash Services, Lash Lifts, Lash Treatments).
3. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
4. Select the duration of your appointment.
5. Choose the technician who will perform your service.
6. Provide any additional notes for the technician.
7. Confirm your email address to receive communication from us.
8. Confirm that you have received the confirmation email we send you.
9. Confirm that you have not received any SMS confirmation.
10. Confirm that the appointment has been confirmed.
11. Confirm that the appointment has not been cancelled.
12. Confirm that the appointment can be rescheduled.
13. Provide your signature to confirm your appointment.
14. The staff member performing the service will provide their signature to confirm their agreement.
15. The staff member performing the service will provide notes about your appointment.
16. Select the payment method you will use (e.g., online, cash, check).
17. Confirm your payment status.
18. Confirm that you have received the payment confirmation.
19. Confirm that you have informed the client of their payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it should appear on your appointment schedule.
* **Client Contact Number (2)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the appointment.
* **Service Type (3)**: Choose the type of service you are requesting (e.g., Lash Services, Lash Lifts, Lash Treatments).
* **Appointment Date (4)**: Select the date for your appointment in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Appointment Time (5)**: Choose the time for your appointment in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Appointment Duration (6)**: Enter the length of your appointment in minutes.
* **Lash Technician (7)**: Choose the technician who will perform your service.
* **Notes (8)**: Provide any additional information about your appointment that the technician should be aware of.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address to receive communication from us.
* **Confirm Email (10)**: Confirm that you have received the email we send you.
* **Has Sent Confirmation Email (11)**: Confirm that we have sent you a confirmation email.
* **Has Sent SMS Confirmation (12)**: Confirm that we have not sent you a SMS confirmation.
* **Has Received SMS Confirmation (13)**: Confirm that you have not received a SMS confirmation.
* **Has Confirmed Appointment (14)**: Confirm that the appointment has been confirmed.
* **Has Cancelled Appointment (15)**: Confirm that the appointment has not been cancelled.
* **Reschedule Appointment (16)**: Confirm that the appointment can be rescheduled.
* **Client Signature (17)**: Provide your signature to confirm your appointment.
* **Staff Signature (18)**: The staff member performing the service will provide their signature to confirm their agreement.
* **Staff Notes (19)**: The staff member performing the service will provide notes about your appointment.
* **Payment Method (20)**: Choose the payment method you will use (e.g., online, cash, check).
* **Payment Status (21)**: Confirm your payment status.
* **Has Confirmed Payment (22)**: Confirm that you have confirmed your payment status.
* **Has Sent Payment Confirmation (23)**: Confirm that you have sent a payment confirmation.
* **Has Received Payment Confirmation (24)**: Confirm that you have received a payment confirmation.
* **Has Informed Client of Payment Status (25)**: Confirm that you have informed the client of their payment status.
