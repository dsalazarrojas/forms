# Communication Skills Testing Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Communication Skills Testing Sign Up form is designed to collect information and schedule a suitable appointment for communication skills testing. This form collects personal details, testing preferences, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and efficient testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your full name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Select your organization affiliation, if applicable.
3. Choose the type of communication testing you require (e.g., Speech Assessment, Language Assessment, etc.).
4. Indicate the reason for testing (e.g., Academic Concerns, Workplace Requirements, etc.).
5. Specify whether you have had communication testing before.
6. Select your preferred date and time for testing.
7. Choose your preferred testing location (e.g., In-Person at Office, In-Person at Organization, etc.).
8. Indicate whether you require any special accommodations.
9. Specify if you will use insurance for testing.
10. Provide any additional comments or information.
11. Finally, confirm your consent to be contacted about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Signup Information** (`signup_information`, note, required: false): This section serves as a brief introduction to the form. Read the provided text to understand the form's purpose and guidelines.
* **Signup Date** (`signup_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date you would like to sign up for testing.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, note, required: false): This section provides space for you to add any relevant personal details about yourself.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: true): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates and appointment reminders.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter your date of birth.
