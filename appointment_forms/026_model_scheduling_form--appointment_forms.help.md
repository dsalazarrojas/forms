# Model Scheduling Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from models for photo shoots. The form includes fields to provide shoot details, model information, and other necessary documentation to facilitate the scheduling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shoot Details (shoot_details)** (`shoot_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the shoot.
* **Shoot Type (shoot_type)** (`shoot_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of shoot (Fashion, Commercial, Editorial, Portrait, Lifestyle). If unsure, leave this field blank.
* **Portfolio Details (portfolio_details)** (`portfolio_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about your portfolio experience.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the photographer.
* **Availability (availability)** (`availability`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available for the shoot.
* **Location (location)** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the shoot.
* **Campaign Details (campaign_details)** (`campaign_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about the campaign.
* **Casting Info (casting_info)** (`casting_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant casting information.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes (notes)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Confirmation (confirmation)** (`confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have confirmed (Confirmed), have not confirmed (Not Confirmed), or are pending (Pending).
* **Date (date)** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the shoot.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the shoot.
* **Model Info (model_info)** (`model_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant model information.
* **Agency Info (agency_info)** (`agency_info`, text, required): Enter your agency information.
* **Model Agreement (model_agreement)** (`model_agreement`, text, required): Enter your agreement with the model.
* **Campaign Terms (campaign_terms)** (`campaign_terms`, text, required): Enter any campaign terms or conditions.
* **Shoot Terms (shoot_terms)** (`shoot_terms`, text, required): Enter any shoot-specific terms or conditions.
* **Model Signature (model_signature)** (`model_signature`, text, required): Sign your name and date the form.
* **Agency Signature (agency_signature)** (`agency_signature`, text, required): Sign your agency's name and date the form.
* **Model Termination (model_termination)** (`model_termination`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any termination or cancellation information.
* **Agency Termination (agency_termination)** (`agency_termination`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any termination or cancellation information.
* **Submission (submission)** (`submission`, text, required): Enter any additional submission details.

Please note that all fields with a red asterisk (\*) are required fields and must be completed before submitting the form.
