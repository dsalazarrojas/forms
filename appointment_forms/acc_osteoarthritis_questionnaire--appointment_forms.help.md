# ACC Osteoarthritis Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "ACC Osteoarthritis Questionnaire" is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about your pet's osteoarthritis symptoms and treatment history. This form will help you provide detailed and accurate information about your pet's condition, which will aid in creating an effective treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your pet's information, including their name, your name, and email address (if applicable).
2.  Select the species of your pet (e.g., dog, cat, or other).
3.  Provide your pet's age and weight in kilograms.
4.  Answer the questions about your pet's symptoms, such as pain level, mobility issues, and stiffness duration.
5.  Report any previous diagnosis or treatment history, including medications and supplements.
6.  Describe your pet's lifestyle factors, such as exercise frequency and home environment.
7.  Add any additional comments or concerns about your pet's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet's name**: Enter the name of the animal being evaluated.
    *   This field is required to identify your pet.
*   **Owner's full name**: Enter your full name as the owner of the pet.
    *   This field is required to ensure accurate communication with you.
*   **Owner's email address**: Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
    *   This field is optional but recommended for future reference.
*   **Owner's phone number**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
    *   This field is optional but recommended for quick updates.
*   **Species**: Select the species of your pet (e.g., dog, cat, or other).
    *   This field helps identify the appropriate treatment options for your pet.
*   **Breed**: Enter the breed or mix of your pet if applicable.
    *   This field provides additional information about your pet's genetic predisposition.
*   **Age (in years)**: Enter your pet's age in years.
    *   This field helps identify the potential impact of age on your pet's condition.
*   **Current weight (in kg)**: Enter your pet's current weight in kilograms.
    *   This field aids in assessing the severity of your pet's condition.
*   **Is lameness observed in your pet?**: Answer "Yes, consistently," "Yes, occasionally," "False," or "Unsure."
    *   This field helps assess the level of lameness in your pet.
*   **Which limbs are affected? (Select all that apply)**: Choose from "Front left," "Front right," "Hind left," "Hind right," or "Multiple limbs."
    *   This field helps identify the specific limbs affected by lameness.
*   **Rate your pet's apparent pain level (1-10)**: Select a pain level from 1 to 10.
    *   This field helps measure the severity of your pet's pain level.
*   **Does your pet have difficulty with any of the following? (Select all that apply)**: Choose from "Going up/down stairs," "Jumping onto furniture," "Getting in/out of car," "Standing up after resting," "Walking longer distances," "Playing normally."
    *   This field helps identify any mobility issues your pet may have.
*   **How long does morning stiffness typically last?**: Select from "Less than 10 minutes," "10-30 minutes," "More than 30 minutes," "Throughout the day," or "None observed."
    *   This field helps assess the duration of morning stiffness.
*   **Has your pet's activity level decreased compared to 6 months ago?**: Choose from "Significantly decreased," "Somewhat decreased," "No change," "Increased," or "Unknown."
    *   This field helps compare your pet's current activity level with their past activity level.
*   **Has your pet been previously diagnosed with osteoarthritis?**: Select "True," "False," "Suspected but not confirmed," or "Unknown."
    *   This field helps identify any previous diagnosis of osteoarthritis in your pet.
*   **Current medications for joint health/pain (if any)**: Enter any medications or supplements your pet is currently taking.
    *   This field provides information about your pet's current treatment.
*   **Are you currently giving supplements (e.g., glucosamine, omega-3)?**: Select "Yes, regularly," "Yes, occasionally," "False," or "Planning to start."
    *   This field helps identify any supplements your pet is taking.
*   **Has your pet received physical therapy or rehabilitation?**: Choose from "Yes, ongoing," "Yes, completed," "False," or "Considering."
    *   This field provides information about your pet's physical therapy history.
*   **How often does your pet get moderate exercise?**: Select from "Daily," "Several times per week," "Once a week," "Rarely," or "None."
    *   This field helps identify your pet's exercise frequency and potential impact on their condition.
*   **Describe your pet's home environment**: Provide a description of your pet's living situation, including stairs, floors, and outdoor spaces.
    *   This field helps identify any environmental factors that may impact your pet's condition.
*   **Additional comments or concerns**: Enter any further information or concerns about your pet's condition.
    *   This field allows you to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your pet's treatment.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best possible assessment of your pet's condition.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please ask for help before continuing.
*   Remember to provide clear and concise information in each field to aid in creating an effective treatment plan.
