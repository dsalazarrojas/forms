# Compliance Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Review Appointment Form Template is used to request a compliance review appointment with the relevant department or person. This form is designed to gather necessary information for a smooth and efficient review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete name as the requester.
2. Enter your employee ID number.
3. Select your department from the list provided.
4. Enter your work email address for confirmation purposes.
5. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Select the type of compliance review needed (e.g., Annual Compliance Review, New Employee Compliance Review, etc.).
7. Choose a preferred date and time for the review.
8. Select an alternative date if your preferred date is unavailable.
9. Specify where you would like the review to take place.
10. Describe the scope or focus of the compliance review.
11. Indicate the urgency level of the review (e.g., Routine, Priority, Urgent, Critical).
12. If required, upload supporting documents.
13. Check if you have had a compliance review in the past 12 months.
14. If needed, indicate any special accommodations required for the appointment.
15. Decide if your manager should be notified of the appointment.
16. Provide any additional information to help prepare for your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name as the requester of the review.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the list provided (e.g., Human Resources, Finance, Operations, etc.).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your work email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Review Type** (`review_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of compliance review needed (e.g., Annual Compliance Review, New Employee Compliance Review, etc.).
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, date, required): Choose a preferred date and time for the review.
* **Alternative Date** (`alternative_date`, date, optional): Select an alternative date if your preferred date is unavailable.
* **Appointment Location Preference** (`appointment_location`, select_one, required): Select where you would like the review to take place (e.g., In Person - Main Office, In Person - Branch Office, Video Conference, etc.).
* **Review Scope** (`review_scope`, text, required): Describe the scope or focus of the compliance review.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Indicate the urgency level of the review (e.g., Routine, Priority, Urgent, Critical).
* **Supporting Documents Required** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): If required, upload supporting documents for the review.
* **Previous Compliance Reviews** (`previous_reviews`, select_one, required): Check if you have had a compliance review in the past 12 months.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If needed, indicate any special accommodations required for the appointment.
* **Manager Notification** (`manager_notification`, select_one, required): Decide if your manager should be notified of the appointment.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information to help prepare for your review.
