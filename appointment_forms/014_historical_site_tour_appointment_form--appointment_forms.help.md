# Historical Site Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to book a historical site tour. It collects personal and contact information to schedule a guided tour at a historical site. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Select a **Date/Time** for the tour. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be listed on the tour.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be listed on the tour.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address to help us contact you about the tour.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about the tour.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, Optional): Select a date and time for the tour.
