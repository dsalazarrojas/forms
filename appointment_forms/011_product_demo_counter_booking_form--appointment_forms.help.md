# Product Demo Counter Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book a product demo counter for a specific product, location, and time slot. This form helps the sales or marketing team understand your product interests and preferences and schedule a demo that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you are interested in learning more about.
2. Choose the location where you would like to schedule the demo.
3. Enter your name and email address (if applicable).
4. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
5. Select the date and time slot for the demo.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.
7. If applicable, choose the team member you would like to schedule the demo with.
8. Select any product preferences or options you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Product Counter**: Select the product you are interested in learning more about. Choose from the list of available products:
	+ Product 1
	+ Product 2
	+ Product 3
* **Select Location**: Choose the location where you would like to schedule the demo. Select one or more locations from the list:
	+ Location 1
	+ Location 2
	+ Location 3
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name. This is an optional field, but it helps us better understand who is booking the demo.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address. This is an optional field, but it helps us keep you updated on demo schedules and any product information.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This is an optional field, but it helps us contact you if there are any updates or changes to the demo schedule.
* **Demo Date**: Select the date for the demo. The date should be in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Demo Time**: Select the time slot for the demo. Choose from the available time slots.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the demo. This helps us understand your specific requirements and preferences.
* **Assigned To**: If you have a preferred team member to schedule the demo with, choose from the list:
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ Other
* **Product Preference**: Select any product preferences or options you have. Choose one or more from the list:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Preference 1**: Select the first product preference or option.
* **Preference 2**: Select the second product preference or option.
* **Preference 3**: Select the third product preference or option.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct product, location, and time slot for the demo.
* If you have any special requirements or preferences, please add them in the customer note field.
* If you have a preferred team member to schedule the demo with, please select them in the assigned to field.
