# Actor Profile Update Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from an artist or agency representative to schedule an update session for an actor's profile. The form will help us gather the necessary details to ensure an efficient and accurate update process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the artist's name in the "Artist Name" field. This is the most critical piece of information required.
2. If applicable, enter the agency's ID or artist's ID in the "Agency ID / Artist ID" field.
3. Select all the types of items you want to refresh during the update session from the "Items to Refresh" dropdown list.
4. Choose the preferred date and time for the update session using the "Preferred Update Session Date" and "Preferred Update Session Time" fields.
5. Select your preferred photographer or editor from the "Preferred Photographer / Editor" dropdown list (optional).
6. Provide a brief description of any major changes to the artist's current look in the "Description of Current Look" field (optional).
7. If required, specify any special props or wardrobe needs for the update session in the "Special Props or Wardrobe Needs" field.
8. Select your desired delivery timeline for the updated profile in the "Desired Delivery Timeline" dropdown list.
9. Enter a valid email address for confirmation and communication purposes in the "Confirmation Email" field.
10. Enter your mobile number for day-of-session communication in the "Mobile Number" field.
11. Confirm that you understand that session fees are non-refundable within 24 hours of the appointment by selecting "I Acknowledge" in the "I understand that session fees are non-refundable within 24 hours of the appointment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Artist Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the artist who needs their profile updated.
- **Agency ID / Artist ID** (Text, Optional): If you are representing an agency, enter the agency's ID; if you are the artist, enter your artist ID.
- **Items to Refresh** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the types of items you need updated, such as Headshots (Photography), Demo Reel (Video Editing), Voice Reel (Audio Recording), Online Profile / CV, and Physical Portfolio.
- **Preferred Update Session Date** (Date, Required): Select the date you prefer for the update session.
- **Preferred Update Session Time** (Time, Required): Choose the time of day you prefer for the update session.
- **Preferred Photographer / Editor** (Select One, Optional): Select your preferred photographer or editor for the session. Options include No Preference, Studio Lead, Associate Artist, and Guest Professional.
- **Description of Current Look** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe any major changes to the artist's current look.
- **Special Props or Wardrobe Needs** (Text, Optional): Specify any special props or wardrobe needs for the update session.
- **Desired Delivery Timeline** (Select One, Required): Choose your desired delivery timeline for the updated profile. Options include Standard (7-10 Days), Express (48 Hours), and Emergency (Same Day).
- **Confirmation Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for confirmation and communication purposes.
- **Mobile Number** (Text, Required): Enter your mobile number for day-of-session communication.
- **I understand that session fees are non-refundable within 24 hours of the appointment** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand the non-refundable nature of session fees within 24 hours of the appointment by selecting "I Acknowledge".
