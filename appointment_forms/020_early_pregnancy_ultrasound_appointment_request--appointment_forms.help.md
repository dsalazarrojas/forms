# Early Pregnancy Ultrasound Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Early Pregnancy Ultrasound Appointment Request" form is designed to collect information from expectant mothers to schedule an ultrasound appointment. This form helps us understand their pregnancy status, medical history, and preferences for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section, providing details such as name, contact information, and other relevant details.
2. Indicate whether an ultrasound is requested or not.
3. Enter appointment details, including the schedule date and time, and preferred time for the appointment.
4. Provide contact information and any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any additional details that may be relevant to the appointment.
* **Ultrasound Request (Page 2)** (`ultrasound_request`): Indicate whether an ultrasound is requested or not. This helps us understand the patient's needs and schedule the appointment accordingly.
* **Appointment Details (Page 2)** (`appointment_details`): Enter the schedule date and time for the appointment, and provide a preferred time for the appointment if possible.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`contact_info`): Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Preferred Time (Page 2)** (`preferred_time`): Enter your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Schedule Date (Page 2)** (`schedule_date`): Enter the date you prefer for the appointment.
* **Pregnancy Status (Page 2)** (`pregnancy_status`): Indicate the current pregnancy status - Active or Inactive.
* **Ultrasound Type (Page 2)** (`ultrasound_type`): Select which type of ultrasound is required - Yes or No.
* **Doctor Information (Page 2)** (`doctor_info`): Enter any additional information about the doctor or medical professional attending the appointment.
* **Appointment Status (Page 2)** (`appointment_status`): Indicate the current status of the appointment - Active or Inactive.
* **Schedule Time (Page 2)** (`schedule_time`): Enter the schedule time for the appointment.
* **Schedule Date 2 (Page 3)** (`schedule_date_2`): Enter the schedule date for the appointment (second attempt).
* **Pregnancy Information (Page 3)** (`pregnancy_info`): Enter any relevant pregnancy information.
* **Medical History (Page 3)** (`medical_history`): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Medical Conditions (Page 3)** (`medical_conditions`): Select any relevant medical conditions.
* **Appointment Time (Page 3)** (`appointment_time`): Enter the appointment time.
* **Appointment Date (Page 3)** (`appointment_date`): Enter the appointment date.
* **Ultrasound Information (Page 3)** (`ultrasound_info`): Enter any relevant ultrasound information.
* **Appointment Status 2 (Page 3)** (`appointment_status_2`): Indicate the current status of the appointment - Active or Inactive.
* **Medical Conditions 2 (Page 4)** (`medical_conditions_2`): Select any relevant medical conditions.
* **Medical History 2 (Page 4)** (`medical_history_2`): Provide any relevant medical history.

Note that some fields may have additional options or requirements depending on the specific circumstances of the patient.
