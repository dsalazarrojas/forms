# Healthcare Initial Consultation Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect important information about new patients and their consultation requests for healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your new patient information, including any relevant details about your medical history.
2. Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you for further information or updates.
3. If you have a personal email address, please enter it so we can communicate with you more efficiently.
4. Describe your medical condition, including any relevant details about your symptoms or health issues.
5. Write a brief message explaining your consultation request, including any specific questions or concerns you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient Information** (new_patient_info): This field is for you to enter your new patient information, such as your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number): Please enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you for further information or updates.
* **Email** (email): If you have a personal email address, please enter it so we can communicate with you more efficiently.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): This field is for you to describe any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses, allergies, or medications.
* **Medical Condition** (medical_condition): Please describe your current medical condition, including any specific symptoms or health issues you are experiencing.
* **Consultation Request** (consultation_request): Write a brief message explaining your consultation request, including any specific questions or concerns you have.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled in to avoid delays in the consultation process.
- You can write any additional relevant information about your medical condition or consultation request.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
