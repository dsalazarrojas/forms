# Veterinary Vaccine Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Vaccine Consent And Appointment Form is designed to collect information about pets, their owners, and appointment details for veterinary services. This form serves as a comprehensive guide for pet owners to provide their consent for vaccinations and schedule appointments with veterinarians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pet's information, including their name, owner, and contact details.
2. Choose the vaccines for the pet to receive.
3. Provide the owner's contact details.
4. Enter the pet's details, such as age, breed, and vaccination history.
5. Enter the owner's details, including their name, address, and phone number.
6. Select the appointment time with the veterinarian.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments.
8. Choose the payment method and amount.
9. Select the appointment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Information** (`pet_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the pet's name, age, breed, and any other relevant details.
- **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the appointment.
- **Select Vaccines** (`vaccine_list`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the vaccines the pet will receive.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the owner's contact details, including their name, phone number, and email.
- **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the pet's details, such as age, breed, and vaccination history.
- **Owner Details** (`owner_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the owner's details, including their name, address, and phone number.
- **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the appointment time with the veterinarian.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the owner's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the owner's phone number.
- **Veterinarian** (`veterinarian`, `text`, required: false): Enter the veterinarian's name.
- **Appointment Date 2** (`appointment_date_2`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the appointment.
- **Consent** (`consent`, `number`, required: false): Enter the consent for the vaccinations.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment method (Online Payment, Cash, or Credit).
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the payment terms (On-site, Off-site, or On-call).
- **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the appointment.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the payment date.
- **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the payment time.
- **Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the veterinarian's name (Dr. John Smith, Dr. Jane Doe, or Dr. Bob Johnson).
- **Veterinarian Info** (`veterinarian_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the veterinarian's availability (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 14:00 PM, or 14:00 PM - 16:00 PM).
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
- **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the appointment status (Scheduled, Rescheduled, or Cancelled).
