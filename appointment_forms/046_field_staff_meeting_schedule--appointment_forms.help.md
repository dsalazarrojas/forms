# Field Staff Meeting Schedule - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Staff Meeting Schedule form is used to schedule and coordinate meetings among field staff members. The form captures essential details such as meeting duration, date, time, location, agenda, participants, and meeting notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for scheduling the meeting:
	* Enter the meeting duration in minutes.
	* Choose the meeting date and time.
	* Select the meeting location.
2. Add or remove participants as necessary.
3. Describe the meeting agenda and add any important details.
4. Add any additional meeting notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Scheduling**: Enter the meeting duration in minutes to schedule the meeting.
* **Participants**: Select the participants that will be attending the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Date**: Choose the date for the meeting.
* **Meeting Time**: Select the time for the meeting.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Agenda**: Describe the topics to be discussed during the meeting.
* **Participants List**: Select the list of participants that will attend the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes**: Enter any additional meeting notes or details.
* **Meeting Agenda**: Enter the meeting agenda and any important details.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Participants**: Select the participants that will attend the meeting.

## Tips
* Ensure that the meeting duration is realistic and allows enough time for discussion.
* Be specific when selecting the meeting date and time to avoid conflicts.
* Clearly describe the meeting agenda to ensure all participants understand the purpose of the meeting.
* Add any additional meeting notes to help facilitate discussion and decision-making.
