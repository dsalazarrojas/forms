# head_spa_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The head_spa_intake_form is designed to gather information from clients regarding their appointment details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please fill in your details as prompted on page one.
- Specify your appointment preferences on page two.
- Enter your email address on page three.
- Enter your phone number on page four.
- Enter the date of your appointment on page five.
- Enter the time of your appointment on page six.
- Add any additional comments or notes on page seven.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: (`client_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about your visit.
* **Appointment Preferences**: (`appointment_preferences`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your preferences for your appointment, e.g., preferred time or date.
* **Spa Preferences**: (`head_spas_preferences`, `text`, required/optional): Let us know about any special requests or preferences you have regarding your visit to our spa.
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date**: (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the date for your appointment.
* **Time**: (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time for your appointment.
* **Note**: (`note`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information regarding your visit.
