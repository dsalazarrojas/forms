# Wedding Appointment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from couples requesting to schedule an appointment for wedding-related services or consultations. This form helps service providers to understand the couple's needs and requirements, making it easier to tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page.
2. Ensure that all required fields (if any) are completed accurately.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Appointment Request** (`wedding_appointment_request`, text, required: false): This is the main topic of the appointment request. Briefly describe the purpose of your appointment request.
* **Couple Details** (`couple_details`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant details about the couple, such as their names, ages, or any specific circumstances that may impact the appointment.
* **Service Provider Details** (`service_provider_details`, text, required: false): If you are contacting a specific service provider, please provide their name or any relevant information about their service.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required: false): Describe the type of appointment you are requesting, including any specific dates, times, or locations.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so that the service provider can get back to you.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and efficient process.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, be sure to include them in the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the form author at `form@gic.mx`.
