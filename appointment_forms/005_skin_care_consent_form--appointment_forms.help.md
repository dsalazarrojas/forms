# Skin Care Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skin Care Consent Form is a crucial document used to collect and record information about clients or patients undergoing skin care-related medical procedures. Its primary purpose is to ensure that clients are informed about the procedure, its risks, and their own medical history before treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's information, including your name, contact details, and any relevant medical history.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the consent question, indicating your agreement to the procedure.
3. Describe the procedure you are consenting to in the provided text field.
4. List any medical history, allergies, or contraindications that may impact the treatment.
5. List any medications you are currently taking.
6. Indicate the expected duration of the medical procedure.
7. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (client_information)**: This field is where you will enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Consent (consent)**: This is a yes/no question that asks for your consent to the medical procedure. Please answer "Yes" if you are aware and agree to the treatment.
* **Procedure Information (procedure_information)**: This field is where you will describe the specific medical procedure you are consenting to, including any details about the treatment.
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries that may impact the treatment, list them here.
* **Allergies/Contraindications (allergies_contraindications)**: List any allergies or medical conditions that may cause problems during or after the treatment.
* **Medications (medications)**: List any medications you are currently taking that may impact the treatment.
* **Medical Procedure (medical_procedure)**: This field is not used in this form (it seems to be a typo or an unused field). Please ignore it.
* **Client Signature (client_signature)**: This is where you will sign the form to confirm your consent. Please sign it carefully, as it is a legal document.
* **Tips**: If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
