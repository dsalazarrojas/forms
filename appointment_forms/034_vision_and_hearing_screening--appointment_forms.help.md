# Vision And Hearing Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vision And Hearing Screening form is used to collect information about a student's vision and hearing test results. This form is intended to be completed by teachers or other school staff to document the student's progress and provide a record of their vision and hearing abilities. It is usually filled out after a vision and hearing test is conducted on the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter the student's grade in the "Grade" field.
4. Enter any comments or observations in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the date of the vision test in the "Date of Vision Test" field.
6. Enter the results of the vision test in the "Results of Vision Test" field.
7. Enter the date of the hearing test in the "Date of Hearing Test" field.
8. Enter the results of the hearing test in the "Results of Hearing Test" field.
9. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments 2" field.
10. Enter the signature of the nurse who completed the form in the "Nurse's Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student who was tested.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher or staff member who is completing this form.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required: false): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1-12).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or observations about the student's vision and hearing abilities.
* **Date of Vision Test** (`date_of_vision_test`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the vision test was administered.
* **Results of Vision Test** (`results_of_vision_test`, text, required: false): Enter the results of the vision test (e.g., pass/fail).
* **Date of Hearing Test** (`date_of_hearing_test`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the hearing test was administered.
* **Results of Hearing Test** (`results_of_hearing_test`, text, required: false): Enter the results of the hearing test (e.g., pass/fail).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Nurse's Signature** (`nurses_signature`, text, required: false): This field is for the signature of the nurse who completed the form.
