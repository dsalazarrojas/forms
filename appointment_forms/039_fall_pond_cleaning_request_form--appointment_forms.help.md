# Fall Pond Cleaning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pond Cleaning Request Form is used to schedule and manage appointments for fall cleaning services. This form is used to collect information from users about the service they require and the status of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user-input fields with your name and contact information.
2. Select the services you require from the list provided.
3. Schedule the date and time for the service.
4. Provide a description of the pond to be cleaned.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
6. Confirm or cancel the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Your name and contact information.
	+ This field is used to collect your name and contact information.
	+ Enter your name and contact information as prompted.
* **Service Requested**:
	+ Select the services you require from the list provided.
	+ Choose the services you need for your pond.
* **Date Scheduled**:
	+ Enter the date you would like to schedule the service.
	+ Use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Scheduled**:
	+ Enter the time you would like to schedule the service.
	+ Use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Pond Description**:
	+ Provide a brief description of the pond to be cleaned.
	+ Include any relevant details about the size, location, or specific needs of the pond.
* **Service Notes**:
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the service.
	+ This field is used to provide more information about the service request.
* **Contact Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the contact person.
	+ This field is used to collect the contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone**:
	+ Enter the contact person's phone number.
	+ This field is used to collect the contact person's phone number.
* **Service Confirmed**:
	+ Confirm that the service has been scheduled.
	+ Choose "Confirmed" to indicate that the service has been scheduled.
* **Service Cancelled**:
	+ Cancel the service if necessary.
	+ Choose "Confirmed" to indicate that the service has been cancelled.
* **Service Rescheduled**:
	+ Reschedule the service if necessary.
	+ Choose "Confirmed" to indicate that the service has been rescheduled.
* **Service Postponed**:
	+ Postpone the service if necessary.
	+ Choose "Confirmed" to indicate that the service has been postponed.
* **Service Completed**:
	+ Confirm that the service has been completed.
	+ Choose "Confirmed" to indicate that the service has been completed.
* **Pond Cleaner**:
	+ Enter the name of the pond cleaner assigned to the service.
	+ This field is used to collect the name of the pond cleaner.
* **Notes**:
	+ Add any additional comments or information about the service.
	+ This field is used to provide more information about the service request.
