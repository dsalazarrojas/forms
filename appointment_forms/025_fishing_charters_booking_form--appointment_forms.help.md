# Fishing Charters Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing Charters Booking Form is a user-friendly form designed to gather essential information from users for a fishing charter experience. This form ensures that the user provides accurate and complete information to book a charter that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the relevant charter type.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3.  Ensure that all the required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`)
*   Enter your first name.
### Last Name (`last_name`)
*   Enter your last name.
### Email (`email`)
*   Enter your email address.
### Phone (`phone`)
*   Enter your phone number.
### Date (`date`)
*   Select a date for the charter.
### Time (`time`)
*   Select a time slot for the charter.
### Charter Type (`charter_type`)
*   Select the type of charter you wish to book (Fishing, Boat, Charter).
### Number of People (`number_of_people`)
*   Enter the number of people booking the charter.
### Number of Fishers (`num_fishers`)
*   Enter the number of fishers in the group.
### Fishers Names (`fishers_names`)
*   Enter the names of the fishers in the group.
### Notes (`notes`)
*   Capture any additional notes or comments.
### Assigned To (`assigned_to`)
*   Select who the charter will be assigned to (ChatJimmy, Other).
### Form ID (`form_id`)
*   This field is not applicable for end-users as it is used internally.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with the charter booking.
*   Use the charter type field to select the type of charter that suits your needs.
*   Enter the correct number of people and fishers for the charter.
*   Use the notes field to capture any additional information that may be relevant for the charter.
*   Select the correct person to be assigned to for the charter.
