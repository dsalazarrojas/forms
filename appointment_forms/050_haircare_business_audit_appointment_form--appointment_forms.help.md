# Haircare Business Audit Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule and document business audit sessions with contacts from the haircare industry. It collects essential information about the contact, such as their name, email, phone number, session preferences, and service interests. The form helps gather data to plan and conduct the audit sessions efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a Session Time**: Choose the time that best suits your schedule from the available options (10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM).
2. **Choose Session Type**: Select the type of session you would like to schedule (Service Interest, Audit Session, or Consultation).
3. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide the contact's name, email address, and phone number.
4. **Enter Business Details**: Type the address and name of the contact's business.
5. **Select Service Interests**: Choose all the haircare services that interest you, such as hair cutting, color, style, and treatment.
6. **Add Additional Notes**: Type any other relevant information about the contact or the audit session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Session**: Choose a time that suits your schedule for the audit session.
* **Session Type**: Select the type of session you would like to schedule (Service Interest, Audit Session, or Consultation).
* **Contact Name**: Type the name of the contact.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the contact's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact's phone number.
* **Business Details**: Type the address and name of the contact's business.
* **Service Interests**: Choose all the haircare services that interest you, such as hair cutting, color, style, and treatment.
* **Notes**: Provide any other relevant information about the contact or the audit session.
