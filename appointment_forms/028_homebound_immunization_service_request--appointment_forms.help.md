# Homebound Immunization Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request an appointment for a homebound immunization service, which is typically provided to patients who are unable to visit a healthcare facility in person due to medical conditions, mobility issues, or other reasons. The form allows patients or their caregivers to schedule and request this service, providing necessary information for the healthcare team to prepare for the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide visit details, including the date and time you would like to schedule the appointment.
2.  Enter patient information, including their name and any relevant medical details.
3.  Check the consent box to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the service.
4.  Add any additional notes or comments about the visit.
5.  Confirm the visit date and time.
6.  Provide your email address and phone number for communication.
7.  Enter your phone number (if different from email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, text, required): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name and any relevant medical details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the service.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Confirm Visit** (`confirm_visit`, date, required): Confirm the date of the scheduled visit.
* **Confirm Visit Time** (`confirm_visit_time`, time, required): Confirm the time of the scheduled visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if different from email).

## Tips
- Make sure to review the fields carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
- Double-check the schedule to ensure it's correct before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the healthcare team for further assistance.
