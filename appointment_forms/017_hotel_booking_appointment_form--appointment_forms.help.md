# Hotel Booking Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a hotel room. The form collects information about the user, their contact details, and their travel preferences to confirm their booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields.
- Enter your email address in the Email field.
- Provide your Contact Phone number in the Contact Phone field.
- Enter your Checkin and Checkout dates in the respective fields.
- Select your preferred Checkin and Checkout times in the respective fields.
- Choose the type of room you need (Single, Double, or Suite) in the Room Type field.
- Specify how many rooms you require in the Room Quantity field.
- Enter your Arrival Flight number in the Arrival Flight Number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Checkin** (`Checkin`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to check in to the hotel.
* **Checkin Time** (`Checkin Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you plan to check in to the hotel.
* **Checkout** (`Checkout`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to check out of the hotel.
* **Checkout Time** (`Checkout Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you plan to check out of the hotel.
* **Room Type** (`Room Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of room you need (Single, Double, or Suite).
* **Room Quantity** (`Room Quantity`, `number`, required: false): Specify how many rooms you require.
* **Arrival Flight Number** (`Arrival Flight Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your arrival flight number.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or preferences, please contact the hotel directly.
- The hotel will confirm your booking after receiving your submission.
