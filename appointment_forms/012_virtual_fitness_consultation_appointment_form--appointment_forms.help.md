# virtual_fitness_consultation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for virtual fitness consultations, including the type of consultation, appointment details, location, fitness professional's information, and any additional notes or messages. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate scheduling.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to make sure it's for the correct purpose and not a duplicate.
2. Fill out the "Appointment Type" field by selecting the type of virtual consultation you need.
3. Enter the "Appointment Date" in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the "Appointment Time" in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
5. Enter the "Appointment Location" where you will be attending the virtual consultation.
6. Select the "Fitness Professional" who will be conducting the virtual consultation.
7. Leave any additional "Appointment Notes" if necessary.
8. Enter any "Client Message" if you have something to communicate to the fitness professional.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of virtual consultation you need. This will help the fitness professional prepare for your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your virtual consultation in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your virtual consultation in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you will be attending the virtual consultation.
* **Fitness Professional** (`fitness_professional`, select_one, required): Select the fitness professional who will be conducting your virtual consultation.
* **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes or comments about your virtual consultation.
* **Message** (`client_message`, text, optional): Enter any messages or comments you have for the fitness professional.
## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accurate information.
* Review each field carefully and make sure to follow the required fields to ensure accurate scheduling.
