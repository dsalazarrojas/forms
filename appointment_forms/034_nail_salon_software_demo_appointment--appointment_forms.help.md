# Nail Salon Software Demo Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a demo appointment in a nail salon software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Decide if you would like to navigate the software demo (Yes/No)
2. Indicate whether you would like to schedule a lead appointment (Yes/No)
3. Provide any additional details about your interest in the software (free text field)
4. Select a date for the demo appointment
5. Choose a time for the demo appointment
6. Enter the manager's email address who will be conducting the demo
7. Provide your phone number for follow-up
8. Leave any additional comments about the demo (optional)
9. Confirm if you would like to schedule the demo (Yes/No)
10. Agree to the terms of the demo (Yes/No)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Navigate** (`salons_software_demo_navigate`, select_multiple): Select whether you would like to navigate the software demo (Yes/No).
* **Leads** (`salons_software_demo_leads`, select_one): Indicate whether you would like to schedule a lead appointment (Yes/No).
* **Demo Details** (`salons_software_demo_details`, text): Provide any additional details about your interest in the software.
* **Schedule** (`salons_software_demo_schedule`, date): Select a date for the demo appointment.
* **Time** (`salons_software_demo_time`, time): Choose a time for the demo appointment.
* **Manager** (`salons_software_demo_manager`, email): Enter the manager's email address who will be conducting the demo.
* **Phone** (`salons_software_demo_phone`, text): Provide your phone number for follow-up.
* **Comments** (`salons_software_demo_comments`, note): Leave any additional comments about the demo.
* **Submit** (`salons_software_demo_submit`, select_one): Confirm if you would like to schedule the demo (Yes/No).
* **Agree** (`salons_software_demo_agree`, select_one): Agree to the terms of the demo (Yes/No).
