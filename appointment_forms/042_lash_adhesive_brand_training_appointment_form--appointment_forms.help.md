# Lash Adhesive Brand Training Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lash Adhesive Brand Training Appointment Form is used to collect information for training sessions related to lash adhesive brands. This form helps to gather necessary details from trainees to schedule and conduct these sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select a training session (if provided as options) or enter a custom title for your session.
2. Provide the trainee's details, including their name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Training Session (Page 1)
* **Training Session** (`Training Session`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to select or enter the title of the training session you are interested in or have scheduled. Please ensure this title matches the one you have been assigned or is a valid choice if provided as options.

### Trainee Details (Page 2)
* **Trainee Details** (`Trainee Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing a general description about the trainee. Please keep it concise and focused on the key points for the training session.

### Trainee E-mail (Page 3)
* **Trainee E-mail** (`Trainee E-mail`, `email`, required): Enter the trainee's email address here. This will be used for communication purposes related to the training session.

### Trainee Phone (Page 4)
* **Trainee Phone** (`Trainee Phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for entering the trainee's phone number, if applicable. This will be used for any necessary follow-up calls or reminders related to the training session.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
- If you need to make any changes to the form or have further questions, please reach out to your trainer or the appointed administrator.
- Ensure that your email address and phone number are correct and up-to-date to guarantee timely communication.
