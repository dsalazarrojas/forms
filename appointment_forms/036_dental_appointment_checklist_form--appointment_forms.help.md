# Dental Appointment Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic patient information and medical history for a dental appointment. It helps the dentist or healthcare provider to prepare for the appointment and provides a clear overview of the patient's medical history and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the patient's information.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing medical history, as this will help the dentist or healthcare provider to better understand the patient's needs.
3. For appointment details, try to provide as much information as you can about the reason for the appointment and the expected duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required: false):
  * This field is used to collect the patient's basic identification and contact details.

- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false):
  * This field is used to record any existing medical conditions, previous treatments, or allergies the patient might have.

- **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required: false):
  * This field is used for collecting information about the reason for the appointment, the scheduled time, or the expected duration.

- **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, required: false):
  * This field is used for detailing the treatment or procedure the patient is to undergo during the appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to be as accurate as possible when filling in the form.
- If you have any specific requirements or requests, please do not hesitate to inform the receptionist or dentist about them.
- You can request for a specific time slot or procedure to be rescheduled if needed.
