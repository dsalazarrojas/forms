# online_reputation_management_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a consultation with a representative of a company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Schedule a Consultation" tab.
2. Fill in the required information:
	* Company Name
	* Available Time Slots ( select one or more time slots that fit your schedule)
3. Provide your contact information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (a valid email address)
4. Choose your preferred contact method (required):
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Mail
5. Select your preferred language (optional):
	* English
	* Spanish
	* French
6. Select your preferred contact channel (optional):
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Mail
7. Choose your time zone (optional):
	* EST
	* PST
	* CST

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for your company.
* **Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Available Time Slots** (`available_time_slots`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more time slots that fit your schedule (09:00-11:00, 11:00-13:00, 14:00-16:00, 16:00-18:00).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred language (English, Spanish, French).
* **Preferred Contact Channel** (`preferred_contact_channel`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact channel (Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, optional): Choose your time zone (EST, PST, CST).
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, text, optional): Enter your timezone offset (optional).
* **Timezone Description** (`time_zone_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your timezone (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address for your company.
* Select one or more time slots that fit your schedule.
* Choose your preferred contact method to ensure timely communication.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, you can enter them in the "Timezone Description" field.
