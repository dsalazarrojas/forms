# Custom Furniture Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Furniture Appointment Form is used to schedule appointments for custom furniture consultations and appointments with clients. This form allows you to select a client, choose a date and time, select the services required, and provide notes and contact information for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the client you wish to schedule an appointment with from the list of available clients.
2. Choose the date and time that works best for you and your client.
3. Select the services you wish to provide during the appointment.
4. Choose the staff member who will be attending the appointment.
5. Add any additional notes about the appointment.
6. Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
7. Enter your phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Client** (`select_client`, Select Client, required): Select the client you wish to schedule an appointment with from the list of available clients.
* **Select Date** (`select_date`, Select Date, required): Choose the date that works best for you and your client. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Select Time** (`select_time`, Select Time, required): Select the time of the appointment. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Select Services** (`select_services`, Select Services, required): Choose the services you wish to provide during the appointment. Select multiple services by holding down the Command key (or Ctrl key on Windows).
* **Select Staff** (`select_staff`, Select Staff, required): Choose the staff member who will be attending the appointment.
* **Schedule Note** (`schedule_note`, Schedule Note, optional): Add any additional notes about the appointment.
* **Schedule Email** (`schedule_email`, Schedule Email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Schedule Phone** (`schedule_phone`, Schedule Phone, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
