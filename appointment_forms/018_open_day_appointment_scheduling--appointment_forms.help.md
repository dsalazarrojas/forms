# Open Day Appointment Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for scheduling appointments for campus visits. Please use it to select a time slot, provide visitor information, and manage the booking status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a time slot for the campus visit from the "Campus Visit Time Slots" dropdown menu.
2. Provide accurate visitor information in the "Visitor Information" field.
3. Indicate if the booking has been confirmed in the "Is the booking confirmed?" dropdown menu.
4. Manage the booking status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campus Visit Time Slots** (`campus_visit_time_slots`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more time slots for the campus visit. Choose from Morning, Afternoon, and Evening options.
* **Visitor Information** (`visitor_information`, `text`, required): Enter the visitor's name, contact number, and any other relevant information.
* **Booking Information** (`booking_information`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the booking has been confirmed as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Campus Visit Booking Information** (`campus_visit_booking_information`, `date`, required): Select the date for the campus visit.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, `time`, required): Enter the time of booking (e.g., 08:00 AM or 03:00 PM).
* **Is the booking active?** (`booking_status_2`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the booking is active or inactive.
* **Visitor Contact Information** (`visitor_information_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if the visitor has provided their contact information as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Booking Status 3** (`booking_status_3`, `select_one`, required): Select the booking status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Is the booking confirmed?** (`booking_status_4`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the booking is confirmed as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Booking Status 5** (`booking_status_5`, `select_one`, required): Select the booking status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Booking Status 6** (`booking_status_6`, `select_one`, required): Select the booking status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
