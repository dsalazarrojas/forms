# Cleaning Service Time Estimator - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your property and cleaning needs to provide a time and cost estimate for our cleaning services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information (Customer Name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
2. Select the type of property you have (Residential, Commercial, etc.) and its approximate square footage.
3. Provide details about the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in your property.
4. Specify the number of floors that need cleaning and the type of cleaning service required.
5. Describe the current cleanliness level of your property.
6. Choose the number of rooms that need cleaning and select all areas that need cleaning.
7. Select any additional services you need beyond standard cleaning.
8. Indicate the level of clutter, if any, and whether you have pets or children in the home.
9. Provide any special features or accessibility issues that may affect cleaning time.
10. Select the estimated cleaning time and number of cleaners required.
11. Choose the estimated cost range.
12. Add any notes or comments about the estimate.
13. Select the duration of the estimate's validity.
14. Finally, indicate if you'd like to book the service and specify your preferred date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on any official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, etc.).
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`square_footage`, `select_one`, required): Select the approximate square footage of your property.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (`number_of_bedrooms`, `select_one`, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in your property (if applicable).
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, `select_one`, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in your property (if applicable).
* **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of floors that need cleaning.
* **Type of Cleaning** (`cleaning_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of cleaning needed (Standard, Deep, etc.).
* **Current Cleanliness Level** (`current_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Describe the current cleanliness level of your property.
* **When Was Property Last Cleaned** (`last_cleaned`, `select_one`, required): Select when the property was last cleaned.
* **Number of Rooms to Clean** (`rooms_to_clean`, `select_one`, required): Enter the total number of rooms that need cleaning.
* **Areas Included in Cleaning** (`areas_included`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas that need cleaning.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any extra services needed beyond standard cleaning.
* **Clutter Level** (`clutter_level`, `select_one`, required): Describe the amount of clutter present in your property.
* **Pets in Home** (`pets_in_home`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have pets in the home.
* **Children in Home** (`children_in_home`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have children in the home.
* **Special Features** (`special_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any special features that may affect cleaning time.
* **Accessibility Issues** (`accessibility_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any accessibility issues that may affect cleaning time.
* **Estimated Cleaning Time** (`estimated_cleaning_time`, `select_one`, required): Select the estimated time needed for cleaning.
* **Recommended Crew Size** (`recommended_crew_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the recommended number of cleaners.
* **Estimated Cost Range** (`estimated_cost_range`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the estimated cost range.
* **Estimate Notes** (`estimate_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any notes or comments about the estimate.
* **Estimate Valid Until** (`estimate_valid_until`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of the estimate's validity.
* **Would You Like to Book** (`would_like_to_book`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you'd like to book the service.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, optional): Specify the preferred date for service.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `select_one`, optional): Select the preferred time for service.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or questions.

## Tips
1. Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly for an accurate estimate.
2. Select options based on your actual property and cleaning needs.
3. Take your time and review your answers before proceeding.
4. If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us.

Please review this guide thoroughly before using the form to get the best possible estimation of our cleaning services for your property.
