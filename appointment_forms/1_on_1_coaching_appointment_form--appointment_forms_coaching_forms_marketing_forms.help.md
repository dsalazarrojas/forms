# 1 On 1 Coaching Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients for one-on-one coaching appointments. It collects essential details such as client contact information, appointment scheduling preferences, and feedback about the form itself. The form helps the coaching team to efficiently manage client sessions and gather valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number** (if available).
2. Select a **Date** and **Time** for your appointment.
3. Choose the type of appointment (**Coaching**, **Strategy Session**, **Feedback Review**, or **Career Planning**) and set the desired **Session duration** in minutes.
4. Decide on the **Location type** (In-person, Video Call, or Phone Call) and optionally enter a **Preferred coach** (if known).
5. Indicate any **Special needs** or **Accommodations** required.
6. Choose how you prefer to receive **Contact method** (Email, Phone, or SMS).
7. Finally, rate your **Satisfaction** with the form, select how often you **Use** the form, and provide any additional **Feedback**, **Improvement suggestions**, or **Additional comments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID or government documents.
* **Email address**: Your email address that we'll use for confirmations and updates.
* **Phone number**: Include the country code (e.g., +1 for the US).
* **Date of birth**: Provide your date of birth for age verification purposes (not required, but recommended for our records).
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you prefer to receive updates and reminders (Email, Phone, or SMS).
* **Appointment date**: Select a date that works best for your session.
* **Appointment time**: Pick a time that suits you.
* **Appointment type**: Choose the focus of your session (Coaching, Strategy Session, Feedback Review, or Career Planning).
* **Session duration**: Set the typical duration of your session in minutes.
* **Location type**: Decide how you'd like the session to be conducted (In-person, Video Call, or Phone Call).
* **Preferred coach**: If you have a preferred coach, enter their name; otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Special needs or accommodations**: Mention any special requirements or accommodations you need.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the form (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied).
* **Usage frequency**: Indicate how often you use the 1 On 1 Coaching Appointment Form (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never).
* **Features**: Select all features that influenced your opinion about the form (Design, Price, Performance, Features, Support, or Reliability).
* **Likelihood to recommend**: Rate your likelihood to recommend the form to others (0-10).
* **Competitor comparison**: Compare this form to alternatives (Much better, Somewhat better, About the same, Somewhat worse, or Much worse).
* **Pricing perception**: Rate your perception of the pricing (Great value, Fair price, Slightly expensive, or Too expensive).
* **Improvement suggestion**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional comments**: Add any other comments or feedback.
