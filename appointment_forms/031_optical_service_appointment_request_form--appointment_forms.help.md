# Optical Service Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an appointment for an optical service. The form is designed to gather patient information, appointment date and time preferences, and confirm the appointment details with a doctor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient information section with your details.
2. Select a preferred appointment date and time.
3. Enter your contact number.
4. Enter your medical record number (if applicable).
5. Select a doctor from the list.
6. Confirm your appointment details with the selected doctor.
7. Enter any additional comments or message for the doctor.

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Patient Message**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with the doctor.
* **Appointment Date**: Select a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Select a time for your appointment.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

* **Doctor Name**: Select a doctor from the list.

* **Confirm Appointment Status**: Confirm the status of your appointment (Confirmed, Rescheduled, or Cancelled).
