# Vendor Inspection Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a slot for a vendor inspection. The purpose of this form is to collect information from the supplier to schedule an inspection at a mutually convenient time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Enter the supplier's email address in the "Supplier Email" field.
3. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Supplier Phone" field.
4. Select the inspection date in the "Inspection Date" field.
5. Select the inspection time in the "Inspection Time" field.
6. Optionally, select a preferred inspection date and time in the "Preferred Inspection Date" and "Preferred Inspection Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the supplier to be inspected.
* **Supplier Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the supplier to be contacted.
* **Supplier Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the supplier to be contacted.
* **Inspection Date** (date, required): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (time, required): Select the time of the inspection.
* **Preferred Inspection Date** (date, optional): If you have a preferred date for the inspection, select it here.
* **Preferred Inspection Time** (time, optional): If you have a preferred time for the inspection, select it here.
* **Preferred Inspection Date** (date, optional): If you have a preferred inspection date, select it here.
* **Preferred Inspection Time** (time, optional): If you have a preferred inspection time, select it here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need to change the inspection date or time, contact us before filling out this form.
* We will do our best to accommodate your preferred inspection date and time, but we cannot guarantee it.
