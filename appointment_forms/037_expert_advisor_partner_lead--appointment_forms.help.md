# expert_advisor_partner_lead - Help Guide

## Purpose
The expert advisor lead form is used to collect information from potential customers who have shown interest in our services or products. This form will help us understand their appointment details, nurture lead status, and provide additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appointment date you would like to schedule.
2. Choose the appointment time that suits you best.
3. Provide your appointment lead's name, if applicable.
4. Enter the status of the nurture lead, whether it's active or inactive.
5. Add your phone number, so we can contact you.
6. Enter your email address, so we can keep in touch.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): Select the time that suits you best for the appointment.
* **Appointment Lead** (`appointment_lead`, text, required: false): If this is a lead for someone else, enter their name.
* **Nurture Lead** (`nurture_lead`, text, required: false): Enter the status of the nurture lead, whether it's active or inactive.
* **Nurture Status** (`nurture_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the nurture lead from the provided options.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, so we can contact you.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, so we can keep in touch.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* **Nurture Partner Lead** (`nurture_partner_lead`, text, required: false): If you are a nurture partner, enter your lead information here.
