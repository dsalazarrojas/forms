The purpose of this Estate Planning Appointment Form is to help professionals in the field of estate planning schedule consultations with clients and gather essential information to tailor their advice and services. This form is designed to be simple and user-friendly, making it easy for clients to provide the necessary details for a successful meeting. 

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## Purpose
This form is intended for professionals who wish to schedule a consultation with a client. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required information. You can choose from multiple professionals for each consultation and select the date and time that suits you best. Please note that providing all the required information is optional, but it will help the professional tailor their advice and services accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Schedule Consultation** (`schedule_consultation`, `date`, required/optional: false): This is the first page of the form where you will schedule the consultation. Choose a date that works for you.

- **Select Professional** (`select_professional`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the professional you would like to meet with from the provided options.

- **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date for the consultation. This will be used to confirm the date with the selected professional.

- **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time slot for the consultation. This will help the professional prepare for the meeting.

- **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): If you have a preferred meeting location, please provide it. This will help the professional arrange the meeting accordingly.

- **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name or the name of the person you will be meeting with. This will help the professional tailor their advice and services.

- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted at a different number, please provide it. This will help the professional contact you with any further questions or updates.

- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted via email, please provide your email address. This will help the professional send you any relevant information or updates.
