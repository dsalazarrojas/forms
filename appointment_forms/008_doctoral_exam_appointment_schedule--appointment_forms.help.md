# Doctoral Exam Appointment Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctoral Exam Appointment Schedule form is designed to collect information from doctoral students for scheduling appointments with faculty members. This form ensures that both students and faculty are on the same page regarding exam details, reducing miscommunications and errors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a preferred time slot for your exam appointment.
2. Choose a valid exam date (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Set the start and end times for your exam appointment.
4. Decide whether the exam will last more or less than an hour.
5. Choose if a faculty member will be present during the exam.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about your exam appointment.
7. Enter your email address (if applicable) and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, select_multiple, required): Choose a time slot that you prefer for your exam appointment. This can be "Yes" or "No".
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your exam appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Set the start time of your exam appointment.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Set the end time of your exam appointment.
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, select_one, required): Choose whether your exam will last more or less than an hour.
* **Faculty Member** (`faculty_member`, select_one, required): Decide if a faculty member will be present during your exam.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, optional): Provide the room number where your exam will take place (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your exam appointment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to receive updates about your exam appointment).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to receive updates about your exam appointment).
