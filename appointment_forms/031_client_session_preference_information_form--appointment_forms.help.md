# Client Session Preference Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your personal preferences and session goals, ensuring that your coaching or mentoring experience is tailored to your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully and accurately, as this information will be used to customize your sessions.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, marked with a *.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to ask your coach or mentor for clarification.
4. Once you have completed the form, please submit it to your coach or mentor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for calls or text messages.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Email: We will reach out to you via email.
	+ Phone: We will reach out to you via phone calls.
	+ Text message: We will reach out to you via text messages.
* **Type of Session Desired** (`session_type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ One-on-one coaching: A one-to-one coaching session tailored to your needs.
	+ Group session: A session with other clients on a similar topic or theme.
	+ Virtual session: A session held online or over the phone.
	+ In-person session: A session held in person.
	+ Hybrid: A combination of in-person and online sessions.
* **Preferred Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Weekly: Sessions will be held on a weekly basis.
	+ Bi-weekly: Sessions will be held every two weeks.
	+ Monthly: Sessions will be held once a month.
	+ As needed: Sessions will be scheduled when needed.
	+ Flexible: Sessions will be scheduled at your convenience.
* **Preferred Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required):
	+ 30 minutes: Sessions will be 30 minutes long.
	+ 45 minutes: Sessions will be 45 minutes long.
	+ 1 hour: Sessions will be 1 hour long.
	+ 1.5 hours: Sessions will be 1.5 hours long.
	+ 2 hours: Sessions will be 2 hours long.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`time_preference`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Morning: Sessions will be scheduled in the morning.
	+ Afternoon: Sessions will be scheduled in the afternoon.
	+ Evening: Sessions will be scheduled in the evening.
	+ Weekend: Sessions will be scheduled on a weekend.
	+ Flexible: Sessions will be scheduled at your convenience.
* **Your Timezone** (`timezone`, `text`, optional): Enter your time zone to ensure proper scheduling.
* **Times When You Are NOT Available** (`unavailable_times`, `text`, optional): List any recurring conflicts or unavailable times.
* **What Are Your Goals for These Sessions?** (`session_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your goals and objectives for these sessions.
* **Specific Topics to Cover** (`specific_topics`, `text`, optional): Specify any topics you would like to cover in these sessions.
* **Your Preferred Learning Style** (`learning_style`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Visual: Learning through visual aids and images.
	+ Auditory: Learning through sound and listening.
	+ Hands-on practice: Learning through practice and hands-on experience.
	+ Discussion: Learning through conversation and discussion.
	+ Mixed: A combination of learning styles.
* **Communication Style Preference** (`communication_style_preference`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Direct and structured: Clear and direct communication.
	+ Conversational: Open and casual communication.
	+ Collaborative: Cooperative communication.
	+ Motivational: Motivational and encouraging.
	+ Balanced: A balance between direct and collaborative communication.
* **What Do You Hope to Achieve?** (`expectations_outcomes`, `text`, required): Describe your desired outcomes from these sessions.
* **Any Other Preferences or Requirements?** (`additional_preferences`, `text`, optional): List any other preferences or requirements you may have.
