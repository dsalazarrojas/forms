# Facial Treatment Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Treatment Appointment Form is used to schedule a facial treatment appointment for a client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the first page of the form.
2. Select a date for the appointment from the calendar.
3. Enter the client's name and contact number.
4. Choose the treatment type from the list provided.
5. Select a time for the appointment.
6. Enter the client's email address (if desired).
7. Add any additional notes about the appointment preferences.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appointment Date** (`1`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the appointment from the calendar.
* **Client Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Number** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's contact number.
* **Treatment Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of treatment the client will receive (e.g., Face Lift, Chemical Peel, Microdermabrasion).
* **Appointment Time** (`5`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the appointment.
* **Client Email** (`6`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address (if desired).
* **Appointment Preferences** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the appointment preferences.
* **Salon Address** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the salon address (not currently used in the form, but included for future reference).

## Tips

* Make sure to select a date and time that works for you and the client.
* Enter the client's contact number and email address so they can be reached for appointment reminders.
* Choose a treatment type that meets the client's needs.
* Use the "Appointment Preferences" field to add any special requests or instructions for the appointment.
