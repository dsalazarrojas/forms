# Performance Feedback Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects booking information and contact details from users interested in attending a performance feedback session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages for your booking (page 1 for general session info, pages 2-8 for additional details)
2. Fill out the necessary fields for each page, providing accurate and relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: Performance Feedback Session Booking Form (Required: False)**
	+ This page provides a brief overview of the session.
* **Page 2: Contact Information (Required: False)**
	+ This page is for collecting contact information from the participant.
* **Page 3: Meeting Details (Required: False)**
	+ This page is for collecting meeting details, such as date and time.
* **Page 4: Reviewers (Required: False)**
	+ This page is for listing the reviewers of the meeting.
* **Page 5: Reviewee (Required: False)**
	+ This page is for listing the person being reviewed in the meeting.
* **Page 6: Review Meeting (Required: False)**
	+ This page is for providing additional meeting details.
* **Page 7: Review Meeting (Required: False)**
	+ This page is similar to page 6, for additional meeting details.
* **Page 8: Review Meeting (Required: False)**
	+ This page is also similar to page 6, for additional meeting details.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
