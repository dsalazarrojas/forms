# Doggie Meet & Greet Info Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a meet and greet service for dogs. It is intended to gather details about the user and their pet, such as name, phone number, pet breed, age, and desired services. This information will help us schedule appointments and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the type of service you are interested in (Walk, Sitter, or Both).
3. Choose the breed of your pet.
4. Enter your pet's age.
5. Select the size of your pet (Small, Medium, or Large).
6. Choose the type of pet you have (Dogs, Cats, or Both).
7. Enter the date and time of the meet and greet.
8. Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
9. Submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): A brief title to identify your meet and greet request.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Your full name.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Your email address.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, select_one, required: false): The breed of your pet.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, required: false): The age of your pet.
* **Dog Size** (`dog_size`, select_multiple, required: false): The size of your pet (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required: false): The type of service you are interested in (Walk, Sitter, or Both).
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required: false): The type of pet you have (Dogs, Cats, or Both).
* **Meet Date** (`meet_date`, date, required: false): The date of the meet and greet.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): The time of the meet and greet.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for both pet owners and users of the service.
