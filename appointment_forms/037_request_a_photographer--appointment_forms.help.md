# Request A Photographer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for clients to request a photo shoot with one of our photographers. Please fill out the form to provide necessary details about your session, and we'll get back to you with availability and pricing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed, and submit it when you're finished. If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your session, such as the occasion or the type of shoot (e.g., family portraits, wedding, etc.).
- **Select Photographer** (`photographer_id`, select_one, optional): Choose a photographer from our team who best fits your needs.
  Note: If you're unsure about our photographers, visit our photographer's page or ask us for recommendations.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Let us know where you would like to shoot. This can be your location or a specific venue.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of your session.
  Note: Please be flexible with start times as our photographers may have availability constraints.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Select the end time of your session.
  Note: Please be flexible with end times as our photographers may have availability constraints.
- **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, optional): If you have any specific requests or requirements for your session, such as equipment or location preferences, enter them here.
- **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of photo shoot you're interested in (e.g., portrait, wedding, commercial, etc.). You can select one or multiple options.
  Note: If you're unsure about session types, refer to our website or ask us for guidance.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit the form when you're finished.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll get back to you with availability and pricing information as soon as possible.
