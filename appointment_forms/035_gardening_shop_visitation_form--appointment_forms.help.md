# Gardening Shop Visitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gardening Shop Visitation Form is used to document visits from customers, including their contact information, appointment scheduling, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the following steps:

1. Start by entering the customer's information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose a date and time for the visit, or simply note that no appointment was made.
3. Provide any additional details or information relevant to the shop visit.
4. Add any customer notes or concerns that need to be communicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their ID or as it is commonly known.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the scheduled visit or leave blank if no appointment was made.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the visit, or leave blank if no appointment was made.
* **Shop Address** (`shop_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the shop where the visit took place.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): Note the duration of the visit in minutes.
* **Shop Contact Number** (`shop_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the shop contact number.
* **Shop Notes** (`shop_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional details or information relevant to the shop visit.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, optional): Add any customer concerns or requests that need to be communicated.

## Tips

* Always ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If a customer has multiple visits, please create a new instance of this form for each visit.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or colleague.
