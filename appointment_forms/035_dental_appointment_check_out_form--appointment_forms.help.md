# dental_appointment_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_appointment_check_out_form is a digital check-out form designed for dental clinics to collect important information from patients at the end of their appointments and gather feedback about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details as requested on the Patient Information page.
2. Provide information about your appointment, including date, time, and any relevant notes on the Appointment Details page.
3. If you have any comments or feedback about your experience, feel free to share them on the Feedback page.
4. Confirm your appointment by selecting "Yes" or "No" on the Confirm Appointment page.
5. If you have any questions or concerns about payment, you can provide further details on the Payment Information page.
6. Once you have completed all the fields, review your responses to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information (<name>)**: This is where you will enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: This field is not required, but providing your information helps us better serve you.
- **Appointment Details (<name>)**: Here, you will provide details about your appointment, including the date, time, and any notes about your visit.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: This field is not required, but providing this information helps us to better understand your experience.
- **Payment Information (<name>)**: If you have any questions or concerns about payment, you can provide further details here.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: This field is not required, but providing this information helps us to better serve you.
- **Feedback (<name>)**: We value your feedback and would love to hear about your experience. Your comments will help us to improve our services.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: This field is not required, but providing your feedback helps us to better serve you.
- **Confirm Appointment (<name>)**: Please select "Yes" to confirm your appointment, or "No" to cancel or reschedule.
  - Type: Select Multiple
  - Required: This field is not required, but confirming your appointment helps us to better serve you.
- **Payment Terms (<name>)**: This is where you can provide any additional details about your payment.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: This field is not required, but providing this information helps us to better serve you.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling in your personal details and appointment information.
- If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate any feedback you can provide to help us improve our services.
- Confirming your appointment helps us to better prepare for your next visit.
