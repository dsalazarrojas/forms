# Band Performance Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information for booking a band performance for events such as weddings, corporate events, and private parties. It will help us understand the client's needs, preferences, and requirements for the performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information and details about the event.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. For optional fields, you can leave blank or provide any additional information that may be helpful.
4. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person booking the performance.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Provide a contact number for updates and arrangements.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, optional): If this is a booking for a business, enter its name.
* **Band Name** (`band_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the band or performer you wish to book.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event this performance is for (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, Private Party, etc.).
* **Preferred Performance Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select the first choice date for the performance.
* **Alternate Date** (`alternate_date`, date, optional): Provide a backup date in case the first choice date is not available.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required): Select the start time for the performance.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the length of the performance (e.g., 1 Hour, 2 Hours, 3 Hours, etc.).
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, text, required): Provide the full address of the venue.
* **Expected Audience Size** (`expected_audience`, number, required): Estimate the number of attendees for the event.
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of performance space (e.g., Indoor, Outdoor, Both Indoor and Outdoor).
* **Sound Equipment** (`sound_equipment`, select_one, required): Indicate if sound equipment is provided by the venue or if the band will bring their own.
* **Lighting Requirements** (`lighting_requirements`, select_one, optional): Specify any special lighting needs for the performance.
* **Preferred Musical Style** (`musical_style`, select_multiple, required): Choose the genre(s) of music the band should play.
* **Special Song Requests** (`special_song_requests`, text, optional): List any specific songs you want the band to perform.
* **Do Not Play List** (`do_not_play_list`, text, optional): Provide a list of songs you do not want the band to perform.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Estimate the budget for the performance.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, optional): Provide an amount you can pay as a deposit.
* **Catering Provided** (`catering_provided`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you will provide meals for the band.
* **Parking Available** (`parking_available`, select_one, optional): Check if parking is available for the band.
* **Load-In Time** (`load_in_time`, time, optional): Specify when the band can load in and set up.
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): Provide any other needs or questions you may have.
