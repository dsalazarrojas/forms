# shoe_service_appointment_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an appointment for shoe services such as repair, shine, and maintenance. Please fill out the form to schedule a service with one of our providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the date and time of the service you would like to book.
2. Choose the service you need from the available options.
3. Describe the service you need in the text field.
4. Select the provider you would like to perform the service.
5. Enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you.
6. Add any additional comments you think would be helpful for the provider.
7. Review your information and submit the form to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Date
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to book the service.

### Service Time
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you would like to book the service.

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it would be spelled out on a business card.

### Service Type
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of service you need. Available options are Shoe Repair, Shoe Shine, and Shoe Maintenance.

### Shoe Description
* **Shoe Description** (`shoe_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the service you need, e.g., "Heel repair" or "Polish".

### Service Provider
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, optional): Select a service provider to perform the service.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.

### Appointment ID
* **Appointment ID** (`appointment_id`, text, optional): Please do not enter your appointment ID, this field is not actually used.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the appointment that would be helpful for the provider.
 
# Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct service provider for your appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
