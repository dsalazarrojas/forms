# Dental Open Enrollment Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Open Enrollment Scheduler is a form designed to assist dental clinics in scheduling appointments for open enrollment. This form captures information from both the employer and clinic sides to facilitate efficient appointment management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employer's name and any other relevant details in the "Employer Name" field.
2. Select the enrollment date and time in the "Enrollment Date" and "Enrollment Time" fields.
3. Choose the time zone for the appointment in the "Time Zone" field.
4. Add any additional notes about the enrollment time in the "Time Note" field.
5. Enter the name of the broker responsible for the appointment in the "Broker Name" field.
6. Enter the broker's phone number in the "Broker Phone" field.
7. Enter the name of the clinic where the appointment will take place in the "Clinic Name" field.
8. Enter the clinic's phone number in the "Clinic Phone" field.
9. Provide the employer's email address in the "Employer Email" field.
10. Provide the clinic's email address in the "Clinic Email" field.
11. Choose the appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.
12. Specify the appointment duration in the "Appointment Duration" field.
13. Select the appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (`Enter employer's name`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter the name of the organization or entity that is enrolling in the dental insurance program.
* **Enrollment Date** (`Enter enrollment date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the enrollment process to take place.
* **Enrollment Time** (`Enter enrollment time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for the enrollment process to take place.
* **Time Zone** (`Time zone`, `select_one`, required): Select the time zone for the appointment, such as PST, EST, CST, MST, AST, or GMT.
* **Time Note** (`Time note`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the enrollment time.
* **Broker Name** (`Broker name`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter the name of the broker responsible for the appointment.
* **Broker Phone** (`Broker phone`, `text`, required): Enter the broker's phone number.
* **Clinic Name** (`Clinic name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the clinic where the appointment will take place.
* **Clinic Phone** (`Clinic phone`, `text`, required): Enter the clinic's phone number.
* **Employer Email** (`Employer email`, `email`, required): Enter the employer's email address.
* **Clinic Email** (`Clinic email`, `email`, required): Enter the clinic's email address.
* **Appointment Time** (`Appointment time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration** (`Appointment duration`, `number`, required): Specify the duration of the appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (`Appointment date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the appointment.

## Tips

- Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness before submission.
- Use this form for scheduling appointments for open enrollment only.
- Do not duplicate the process unnecessarily.
