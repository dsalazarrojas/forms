# Tutor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Appointment Form is designed to collect information from students and parents to schedule a one-on-one tutoring session with a teacher. This form helps the teacher to prepare for the session and ensures that both the teacher and the student are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field, which will be used to contact you about the appointment.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Choose a date for the appointment in the "date" field.
5. Select the time for the appointment in the "time" field.
6. Indicate whether you will be meeting with a tutor ('Yes' or 'No') in the "tutor" field.
7. Describe the subject you would like to focus on during the appointment in the "subject" field.
8. Leave any additional comments or questions in the "message" field.
9. Finally, review your information and click the "submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the appointment schedule.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Provide your valid email address so that we can contact you about the appointment.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose a date for the appointment. Please format the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time slot for the appointment. Please choose from the provided options.
* **tutor** (`tutor`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if you will be meeting with a tutor, 'No' otherwise.
* **subject** (`subject`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the topic or subject you would like to focus on during the appointment.
* **message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about the appointment.
* **submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Review and submit the form to confirm your appointment request.
* **form_footer** (`form_footer`, `text`, required/optional): This is a reserved field for future use. Please do not fill in this field.
