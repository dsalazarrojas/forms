# Air Duct Cleaning Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request air duct cleaning services for their properties. It collects essential information for scheduling a suitable appointment and providing the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and street address.
2. Provide your property type and approximate size.
3. Choose the type of HVAC system in your property.
4. Select the estimated number of vents in your property (if known).
5. Let us know when you last had your air ducts cleaned.
6. Choose any services you would like to request for your appointment.
7. Select your primary concerns about your air ducts.
8. Indicate if you have pets or smokers in your home.
9. Select a preferred date and time for your appointment.
10. If needed, provide alternative dates.
11. Enter any special access instructions or requirements for our technicians.
12. If you have any specific requests or concerns, let us know.
13. Finally, select your preferred method for receiving an estimate (if needed).
14. Sign and date the form to authorize the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Street Address**: Enter the address of the property to be serviced.
* **City**: Enter the city of the property's location.
* **State**: Enter the state or province of the property's location.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the postal code of the property's location.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you own (e.g., single-family home, apartment, commercial building, etc.).
* **Property Size**: Choose the approximate size of your property (under 1,000 sq ft, 1,000-2,000 sq ft, 2,000-3,000 sq ft, 3,000-5,000 sq ft, over 5,000 sq ft).
* **HVAC System Type**: Choose the type of HVAC system installed in your property (e.g., central air and heat, heat pump, forced air only, etc.).
* **Number of Vents**: If known, enter the approximate number of vents in your property (choose from 1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20, 21+, or Not sure).
* **Last Cleaning**: Indicate when you last had your air ducts cleaned (e.g., never, within the last year, 1-3 years ago, 3-5 years ago, over 5 years ago, or Not sure).
* **Services Needed**: Select all services you would like to request for your appointment (e.g., air duct cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, HVAC system cleaning, sanitization treatment, etc.).
* **Primary Concerns**: Select all concerns you have about your air ducts (e.g., excessive dust, allergies or respiratory issues, musty odors, etc.).
* **Pets in Home**: Indicate if you have pets in your home and how many (yes, 1 pet, yes, 2 pets, yes, 3+ pets, no pets).
* **Smokers in Home**: Indicate if anyone smokes inside your home (yes or no).
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a preferred date for your appointment.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a preferred time of day for your appointment (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Alternative Date**: Provide an alternative date in case the preferred date is not available.
* **Access Instructions**: Enter any special access instructions or requirements for our technicians (e.g., gate code, doorbell, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or concerns you may have (e.g., specific equipment needs, etc.).
* **How You Heard About Us**: Indicate how you found out about our services (e.g., online search, social media, friend or family, advertisement, etc.).
* **On-site Estimate Needed**: Choose if you would like to schedule an estimate first (yes, please schedule estimate, no, ready to book, need phone quote first).
* **Customer Signature**: Sign and date the form to authorize the service.

Note: This help guide is for customers and should not be considered a legal document or a contract.
