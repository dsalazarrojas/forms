# Photo Shoot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and manage photo shoots for clients and photographers. It collects essential details such as date, time, location, and status updates to facilitate smooth and organized shoot planning and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a photo shoot using this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for the booking, including:
	* Booking date
	* Booking time
	* Location
	* Client photo shoot (if applicable)
2. Enter the photographer's name and contact information (phone number and email)
3. Update the booking status as needed (e.g., booking, cancelled, confirmed)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Information** (Page 1, required): Enter the booking details, including date and time.
* **Booking Date** (Page 2, required): Select the date of the photo shoot.
* **Booking Time** (Page 3, required): Enter the time of the photo shoot.
* **Location** (Page 4, required): Enter the location of the photo shoot.
* **Client Photo Shoot** (Page 5, optional): If applicable, enter the client's name and other relevant details.
* **Photographer Name** (Page 6, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Phone Number** (Page 7, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Email** (Page 8, required): Enter your email address for smooth communication.
* **Booking Status** (Page 9, required): Select the status of the booking (Booking, Cancelled, Confirmed).
* **Booking Status Hint** (Page 10, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the booking status.
* **Notes** (Page 11, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the photo shoot.
