# Inquiry Call Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback, questions, or support requests from customers or users. It allows us to understand their concerns and address them in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Inquiry Call Request" page.
2. Provide your inquiry or support request in the "Inquiry Call Request" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
4. Optionally, provide a detailed call request in the "Call Request" field.
5. Submit the form to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Call Request** (`<name>`, `<type>`, **required: false**): This is the main field where you can enter your feedback, question, or support request. Please provide a clear and concise description of your concern.
* **Contact Info** (`<name>`, `<type>`, **required: false**): This field is for your contact information, which will help us reach out to you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on your inquiry. Please enter your name and email or phone number.
* **Call Request** (`<name>`, `<type>`, **required: false**): This is an optional field for you to provide more detailed information about your call request, such as the best time to contact you or any specific questions you have. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that your inquiry is relevant and legitimate.
* If you have already sent us a duplicate inquiry, please do not submit another one.
* We will do our best to respond to your inquiry in a timely and efficient manner.
* For urgent matters, you can also contact us directly using other contact methods available on our website or social media channels.
