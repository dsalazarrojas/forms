# Skincare New Customer Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare New Customer Consultation Form is used to collect information from new customers about their skin type, medical history, and skincare preferences before their consultation with a skincare professional. This information will help the professional to provide a personalized consultation and treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **email** address in the format of your_username@example.com.
3. Enter your **phone number** in the format of (123) 456-7890.
4. Describe your **medical history** in the text field provided.
5. Indicate any **medical conditions** you have, if any.
6. Check the boxes that apply to your **allergies**.
7. Select your **skin type** from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose up to 2 **skin colors** that apply to you.
9. Select your **face shape** from the dropdown menu.
10. Check the boxes that apply to your **skin texture**.
11. Select up to 3 **skin problems** you are experiencing.
12. Enter your **skincare objective**, e.g., anti-aging, brightening, etc.
13. Select up to 2 **skincare products** you are interested in.
14. Choose your desired **consultation duration** in minutes.
15. Agree to the **treatment terms and conditions**, **privacy policy**, and **consent**.
16. Enter the date you agree to the **consent**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number in the format of (123) 456-7890.
* **medical_history**: Describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments you have had in the past.
* **medical_condition**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your skin, list them here.
* **allergies**: Check any allergies you have that may be related to skincare products or treatments.
* **skin_type**: Select your skin type from the dropdown menu.
* **skin_color**: Choose up to 2 skin colors that apply to you.
* **face_shape**: Select your face shape from the dropdown menu.
* **skin_texture**: Check up to 2 boxes that apply to your skin texture.
* **skin_problems**: Select up to 3 skin problems you are experiencing.
* **skincare_objective**: Enter your skincare objective, e.g., anti-aging, brightening, etc.
* **skincare_products**: Select up to 2 skincare products you are interested in.
* **schedule**: Choose your desired consultation duration in minutes.
* **consent**: Agree to the treatment terms and conditions, privacy policy, and consent.
* **consent_date**: Enter the date you agree to the consent.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments you think are relevant to your consultation.

Note: The information collected from this form will be used to provide you with the best possible service. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to help provide personalized treatment.
