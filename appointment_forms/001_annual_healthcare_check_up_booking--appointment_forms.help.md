# Annual Healthcare Check Up Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an annual healthcare check-up appointment with our clinic. Please provide your information and preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient check-up experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To book your annual check-up appointment, simply fill in the required fields below. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where we can send appointment confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Include your area code for contact purposes.
* **Insurance Provider**: If you have an insurance provider, enter their name (optional).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If you have an insurance policy number, enter it here (optional).
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Select the date you would like to have your check-up appointment.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Choose one of the available time slots for your appointment.
* **Primary Care Physician**: If you have an existing physician at our clinic, enter their name (optional).
* **Reason for Visit or Concerns**: Describe any specific health concerns or reasons for this appointment (optional).
* **Are you currently taking any medications?**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are taking any medications.
* **List of Current Medications**: If you are taking medications, list them here (optional).
* **Do you have any known allergies?**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any known allergies.
* **Allergy Details**: Describe your known allergies, including severity (optional).
* **Any relevant medical history**: Include any surgeries, chronic conditions, or major illnesses (optional).
* **Date of Last Check-Up**: If you know the approximate date of your last check-up, enter it here (optional).
* **Special Transportation Needs**: If you require any special transportation assistance, select 'Yes' and describe your needs.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Patient**: Select the relationship between you and the patient (e.g. Spouse, Parent, Child, etc.).
* **I consent to the collection and use of my health information**: Select 'I agree' to indicate your consent to the use of your health information.
* **Receive health updates and clinic news**: Select 'Yes' to receive health updates and clinic news, or 'No' if you do not wish to receive them.
