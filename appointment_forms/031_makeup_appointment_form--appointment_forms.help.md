# Makeup Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a makeup appointment. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to schedule a successful appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (Customer Name, Contact Number, Date, Time, Type, and Confirm E-mail)
2. Select your preferred Type of appointment
3. Add any additional Notes for the appointment
4. Enter your E-mail address
5. Confirm your E-mail address

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Make Up** (`makeup_appointment_form`, text, optional): Briefly describe your makeup preferences or appointment reason.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you for updates.
* **Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to book the appointment.
* **Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Type** (`appointment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of appointment you would like to book (e.g., "Bridal", "Event", "Maintenance").
* **Notes** (`appointment_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for the appointment.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to send you updates.
* **Confirm E-mail** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address for our records. 

## Tips
Please ensure that your email address entered in the E-mail and Confirm E-mail fields match.
