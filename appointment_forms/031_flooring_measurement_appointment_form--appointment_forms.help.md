# Flooring Measurement Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and manage flooring measurement appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please follow these steps:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your information as the customer. This field is not required, but it helps us keep track of your details.
- **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your visit.
- **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the type of visit you are scheduling:
  - On-Site Measurement
  - Virtual Measurement
- **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of your appointment.
- **Visit Start Time** (`visit_start_time`, time, required: true): Enter the start time of your appointment.
- **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, select_one, required: true): Choose the duration of your visit:
  - 30min
  - 60min
  - 90min
- **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_one, required: true): Select the status of your appointment:
  - Scheduled
  - Rescheduled
  - Cancelled
- **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you have.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
- **Customer Phone 2** (`customer_phone_2`, text, required: false): Enter an additional phone number (optional).
- **Sales Person** (`sales_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the sales person assigned to your appointment.
- **Sales Person Phone** (`sales_person_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the sales person.
- **Sales Person Email** (`sales_person_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the sales person.
- **Appointment Notes** (`appointment_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your appointment.
