# rehydration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rehydration_request_form is a tool used to collect information about an individual's rehydration needs in a medical or healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Address in the Address field.
6. Select a Date and Time for your rehydration request in the Date and Time fields.
7. Choose a Location where you would like to receive rehydration treatment in the Location field.
8. Indicate the Urgency level of your rehydration request (Low, Medium, or High).
9. Add any additional Notes about your rehydration request in the Notes field.
10. Finally, click the Submit button to send your rehydration request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for us to contact you with further information or instructions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you with further information or instructions.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your address where you would like to receive rehydration treatment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select a date for your rehydration request.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time for your rehydration request.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Choose a location where you would like to receive rehydration treatment.
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the level of urgency for your rehydration request (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional information about your rehydration request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to send your rehydration request.
