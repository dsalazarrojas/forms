# Free Client Consultation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a free client consultation with a consultant. It allows clients to provide their contact information and details about the consultation they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Name.
2. Enter your Client Email.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Choose a date and time for the appointment.
5. Fill in the name of the consultant you will be meeting with.
6. Fill in the Consultation Notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Your name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required/optional: not required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional: not required): Your phone number.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required/optional: not required): The date you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, `text`, required/optional: not required): The name of the consultant you will be meeting with.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required/optional: not required): The time you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Consultation Notes** (`consultation_notes`, `note`, required/optional: not required): Additional information about the consultation.
