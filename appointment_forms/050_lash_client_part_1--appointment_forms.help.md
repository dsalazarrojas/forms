# lash_client_part_1 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "lash_client_part_1" form is designed to collect information about a client's visit to a facility or clinic. It captures essential details such as client contact information, visit schedules, and additional notes. Use this form to schedule appointments, track client data, or create reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the date of the visit from the calendar or enter a date in the "Date of Visit" field.
5. Choose the time of the visit from the options or enter a time in the "Time of Visit" field.
6. Describe the reason for the visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email**: Provide the client's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number for contact.
* **Date of Visit**: Select the date of the visit from the calendar or enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit**: Choose the time of the visit from the options or enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Reason for Visit**: Describe the reason for the client's visit, e.g., appointment, consultation, or other.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the visit, such as special requests or concerns.
