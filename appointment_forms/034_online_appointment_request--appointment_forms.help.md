# Online Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request online appointments for various treatments or services. To use this form, you will need to provide your name, email, and phone number, as well as select the treatments and appointments you would like to book.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Patient Email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
4.  Select your preferred treatment options from the "Treatment 1" and "Treatment 2" dropdown menus.
5.  Choose the date and time for your appointment(s) in the "Date 1", "Time 1", and "Date 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Treatment 1** (`treatment_1`, select_one, optional): Select the first treatment you would like to book.
*   **Treatment 2** (`treatment_2`, select_one, optional): Select the second treatment you would like to book.
*   **Date 1** (`appointment_1`, date, optional): Select the date for your first appointment.
*   **Time 1** (`appointment_2`, time, optional): Select the time for your first appointment.
*   **Date 2** (`appointment_3`, date, optional): Select the date for your second appointment.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are booking multiple appointments, ensure that you select the correct dates and times for each appointment.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the space provided.
