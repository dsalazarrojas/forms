# Ride Share Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a ride share appointment, which could be useful for individuals who need a ride to a specific location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your ride share appointment title.
2.  Provide a time for pickup.
3.  Enter the location where you would like to be picked up.
4.  Enter the location where you would like to be dropped off.
5.  Enter any contact details you would like to share with the driver.
6.  Choose the type of vehicle you need.
7.  If you know the date and time for the ride share appointment, enter that information.
8.  Indicate the number of people in your group for the ride share appointment.
9.  Provide any special notes for the driver, such as allergies or special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ride Share Appointment Request Form** (Required/Optional: Not specified, Type: text): This is the title of your ride share appointment request.
* **Pickup Time** (Optional, Type: time): Enter the time you would like to be picked up.
* **Pickup Location** (Optional, Type: text): Enter the location where you would like to be picked up.
* **Dropoff Location** (Optional, Type: text): Enter the location where you would like to be dropped off.
* **Rider Contact Details** (Optional, Type: text): Enter any contact details you would like to share with the driver.
* **Vehicle Type** (Required/Optional: Not specified, Type: select_one): Choose the type of vehicle you need, such as car, bus, train, bike, or other.
* **Pickup Datetime** (Optional, Type: date): Enter the date and time for the ride share appointment if you know it.
* **Ride Capacity** (Optional, Type: number): Indicate the number of people in your group for the ride share appointment.
* **Special Notes** (Optional, Type: note): Enter any special notes for the driver, such as allergies or special requirements.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when entering your pickup and dropoff location.
* If you are unsure of the date and time for the ride share appointment, you can leave it blank and the driver will contact you to confirm the details.
* If you have any special requirements or allergies, please enter them in the special notes field.
