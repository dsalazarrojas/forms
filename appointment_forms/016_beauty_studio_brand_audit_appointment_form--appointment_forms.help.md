# Beauty Studio Brand Audit Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Beauty Studio Brand Audit Appointment Form" is designed to collect information from beauty studio brands for a brand audit. This form will help the audit team to understand the brand's business and contact information, schedule an appointment, and gather details about their brand's goals and challenges. By filling out this form, you'll be able to:

* Provide your business and contact information
* Share your brand's goals and challenges
* Schedule an appointment for the audit session
* Choose the audit method and delivery date

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your studio or brand name
2. Provide your primary contact person's name and email address
3. Enter your phone number
4. Choose your preferred date and time for the audit session
5. Select the areas you'd like the audit to focus on
6. Choose how you'd like to receive the audit (in-person, virtual, or written report)
7. Confirm you consent to share access to your brand assets for the audit

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Studio or Brand Name** (`business_name`): This is the name of your beauty studio brand.
	* Enter the name of your studio or brand in this field.
- **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`): This is the person we'll be contacting for the audit.
	* Enter the name and email address of the primary contact person.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`): This is the email address we'll use to contact you.
	* Enter your email address in this format: `example@email.com`.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): This is your phone number for any follow-up questions or confirmation.
	* Enter your phone number in the format `(xxx) xxx-xxxx`.
- **Preferred Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`): Choose a date and time for the audit session.
	* Select a date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 12/05/2024).
- **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time_slot`): Choose a time slot for the audit session.
	* Select a time slot from the available options: Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon.
- **Audit Focus Areas** (`audit_focus_areas`): Select the areas you'd like the audit to focus on.
	* Choose all that apply from the options:
		+ Logo and Visual Identity
		+ Social Media Presence
		+ Website User Experience
		+ Messaging and Tone
		+ Retail/In-Studio Experience
		+ Competitor Analysis
- **Target Audience** (`target_audience`): This is an optional field for you to describe your target audience.
	* If you'd like to share this information, enter a brief description.
- **Audit Method** (`audit_method`): Choose how you'd like to receive the audit.
	* Select from the options:
		+ In-Person Session
		+ Virtual Meeting (Zoom/Teams)
		+ Written Report only
- **Data Access Consent** (`data_access_consent`): Confirm you consent to share access to your brand assets.
	* Select "I Consent" to confirm.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): This is the date and time you're submitting this form.
	* This field is automatically populated, but you can review and confirm it's correct.
