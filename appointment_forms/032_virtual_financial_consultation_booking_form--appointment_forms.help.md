# virtual_financial_consultation_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to book a virtual financial consultation with a financial advisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (1, text, required/optional): This field requires your name so that the financial advisor can address you properly during the consultation.
* **Email** (2, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address so that the financial advisor can contact you with any last-minute questions or instructions before the consultation.
* **Phone** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so that the financial advisor can reach you if needed during or after the consultation.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Avoid submitting duplicate or unnecessary forms.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the financial advisor directly.

This form is designed to be a straightforward and efficient way for clients to book a virtual financial consultation. By following these steps and guidelines, you'll be able to prepare for your consultation and make the most out of your time with the financial advisor.
