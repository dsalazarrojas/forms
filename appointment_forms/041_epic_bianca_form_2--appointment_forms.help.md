# epic-bianca-form-2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The epic-bianca-form-2 form is used for collecting information about appointments or meetings within an organization or context. This form likely helps schedule and organize appointments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to briefly describe the purpose or nature of the appointment.
2. Provide a detailed description of the appointment.
3. Select the category of the appointment (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
4. Choose a time slot for the appointment from the options provided.
5. Enter any relevant phone number.
6. Provide an email address for communication.
7. Select the date for the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form-title**: A short title of the appointment or meeting. 
* **form-description**: A detailed description of the purpose or context of the appointment.
* **form-category**: Select if the appointment is categorized as "Yes" or "No".
* **form-appointment-time**: Select a time slot for the appointment from the options provided.
* **form-phone**: A relevant phone number for participants or organizers.
* **form-email**: An email address for communication and updates.
* **form-appointment-date**: The date of the appointment.
