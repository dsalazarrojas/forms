# Salt Therapy Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users for a salt therapy session. To use this form, please fill in the required fields with your personal and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the fields provided on each page (User Details, Appointment Details, Session Details, and Contact Info).
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your form before submitting it for any errors or missing fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing personal information such as your name, email, and other identifying details.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing details about your appointment, such as date and time.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing information about your therapy session, such as the type of session and therapist assigned.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional contact information, such as phone number or address.
