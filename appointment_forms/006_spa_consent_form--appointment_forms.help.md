# Spa Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Consent Form is a document that clients must sign before they receive any spa treatments. This form is used to gather essential information about their visit, such as name, contact details, and medical conditions, to ensure their safety and provide them with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Home Address.
4. Enter your Email (if applicable).
5. Enter your Phone Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (year-month-day).
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address where you can be contacted or sent mail.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted (if you want to be contacted via email).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any urgent matters.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any).
* Double-check that all entered information is correct and up-to-date.
* If you need to make any changes or corrections, please inform the staff immediately.
* We recommend completing this form at least 30 minutes before your appointment to avoid any delays.
