# Project Progress Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review the progress of a project, typically after a meeting or discussion. It helps gather information about the project's status, meeting details, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the project's progress and decide whether a review request is needed.
2. Select the appropriate option for "Review Request" and fill in the date and time of the meeting.
3. Specify the meeting location (if necessary).
4. Indicate the participants in the meeting.
5. Estimate the duration of the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Review Request** (`review_request`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to indicate that a review request is needed.
- **Review Request Date** (`review_request_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting.
- **Review Request Time** (`review_request_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the meeting.
- **Meeting Location** (`review_request_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location of the meeting (if necessary).
- **Meeting Participants** (`meeting_participants`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the participants in the meeting. Select 'Yes' for each participant who attended.
- **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required: false): Estimate the duration of the meeting in minutes.

## Tips
* Be specific when filling in the date and time of the meeting.
* Make sure to include all the participants who attended the meeting.
* The meeting duration should be an estimate, as it's not always easy to track the exact time.
* This form is not intended for external use, so you may not need to fill in the meeting location.
* If a review request is needed, select 'Yes' for the "Review Request" field.
