# Vehicle Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Service Request Form is used to collect information for vehicle maintenance or repair services. Please fill out this form to request a service for your vehicle.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Inputs section with your vehicle's details.
2. In the Submit Summary section, briefly describe the services you require for your vehicle.
3. Select 'Yes' if you are sure you want to submit the form, or 'No' if you need to cancel or modify your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Inputs** (`user_inputs`, `text`, optional): Enter the necessary details about your vehicle to help us provide the best possible service. This could include your vehicle's make, model, and year.
* **Submit Summary** (`submit_summary`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the services you require for your vehicle. This could include maintenance or repairs you need, any issues you've noticed, or questions you have.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' to submit your service request, or 'No' to cancel or modify your request.
