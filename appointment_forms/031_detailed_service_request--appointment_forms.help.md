# Detailed Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit a detailed service request, which can be in the form of a haircut or car repair, or any other kind of service they need.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a service request, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you are requesting from the options provided in the "Request Type" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Choose the date and time for the service you need in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Select the type of service you are requesting from the options provided in the "Service Type" field.
7. Provide any additional information about the service you need in the "Service Details" field.
8. Add any additional comments you have in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Type** (`select_one`, optional): Select the type of service you are requesting from the options provided (Haircut, Car Repair, or Other).
* **Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your name to associate it with your service request.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Choose the date for the service you need.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Choose the time for the service you need.
* **Service Type** (`select_one`, optional): Select the type of service you are requesting from the options provided (Haircut, Car Repair, or Other).
* **Service Details** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional information about the service you need.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your service request.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct type of service and provide accurate information to ensure that we can provide the best service for your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you with updates and to confirm your service request.
