# Ballad InBody Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and appointment scheduling information from users for InBody scans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Enter your personal information in the Personal Information section.
3. Select your preferred appointment date and time in the Appointment Details section.
4. Choose your preferred clinic location.
5. Confirm your ability to fast and avoid caffeine and alcohol before the scan.
6. Indicate if you have any electronic medical implants, such as a pacemaker.
7. Add any additional comments or requests in the Additional Comments or Special Requests section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): Enter the email address where you will receive appointment confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number for appointment reminders and updates.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (preferred_date, date, required): Select a date for your scan appointment.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (preferred_time, select_one, required): Select a time slot for your scan appointment.
* **Preferred Clinic Location** (location, select_one, required): Choose the facility nearest to you.
* **Will you be able to fast for 3 hours prior to your scan?** (fasting_confirmation, select_one, required): Confirm your ability to fast before the scan.
* **Will you avoid caffeine and alcohol for 24 hours prior?** (caffeine_avoidance, select_one, required): Confirm your ability to avoid caffeine and alcohol before the scan.
* **Do you have a pacemaker or other electronic medical implant?** (pacemaker_check, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any electronic medical implants.
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests** (additional_notes, text, optional): Add any additional comments or requests for your appointment.

## Tips

* Be sure to select a date and time that works for you and the InBody team.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Additional Comments or Special Requests section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the InBody team for assistance.
