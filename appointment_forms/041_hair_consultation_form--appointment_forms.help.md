# Hair Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Consultation Form is designed to gather essential information from clients for a one-time hair consultation. It helps the staff in understanding the client's needs, preferences, and availability for the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the "Client Details" field, including their name and any relevant details.
2. Select the services you would like to consult about from the "Services Selected" dropdown menu.
3. Specify your schedule request in the "Schedule Request" field, including your preferred date and time.
4. Choose your preferred staff member for the consultation from the "Preferred Staff" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your preferred date and time for the consultation in the "Preferred Date" and "Preferred Time" fields.
6. Specify your availability for the consultation in the "Available Date" and "Available Time" fields.
7. Indicate your preferred appointment duration in the "Appointment Duration" field.
8. Select the unit of measurement for the appointment duration from the "Appointment Duration Unit" dropdown menu.
9. Specify any additional notes or details in the "Notes" field.
10. Provide your email address and mobile number for communication.
