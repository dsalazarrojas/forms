# Typhoid Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Typhoid Vaccination Registration Form is a document used for registering and recording information about individuals who receive typhoid vaccinations. This form captures essential details such as user identification, vaccination history, and consent for medical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Enter your personal details and contact information in the respective fields.
3. If you have received a typhoid vaccination, provide the relevant registration information in the "Vaccination Registrations" field.
4. Select a time slot for your appointment in the "Appointment Schedule" field.
5. Provide a date and time for your consent in the "Consent Date" and "Consent Time" fields, respectively.
6. Sign the "Consent" field to confirm your consent.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (text)**: Enter your name, title, and other identifying information.
* **Vaccination Registrations (text)**: Provide a record of any previous typhoid vaccinations you have received, including the date and dose administered.
* **Appointment Schedule (text)**: Select a suitable time slot for your appointment.
* **Consent (text)**: Sign your name to confirm your consent for the vaccination.
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Notes (text)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your vaccination.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of your consent.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time of your consent.
* **Email (text)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Consent Date (date)**: Enter the date of your consent.
* **Consent Time (time)**: Enter the time of your consent.
* **Consent Signature (text)**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Consent Date Verification (date)**: Enter the date when your consent was verified.
* **Consent Time Verification (time)**: Enter the time when your consent was verified.
* **User Consent Status (select_one)**: Select your current consent status as Active, Inactive, or Pending.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure that your consent is provided willingly and with full understanding of the vaccination process.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is a confidential document and should be handled and stored securely.
* If you need to update your information, please contact your healthcare provider directly.
