# Hair Specialist Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from clients for review appointments with a hair specialist. It's meant to help the hair specialist assess client needs and provide an efficient and personalized experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your client's details.
2. Select the reason for your visit from the options provided (e.g., Hair Loss, Scalp Condition, Hair Color, or Other).
3. Choose your desired visit date.
4. Select your desired visit time.
5. If applicable, provide follow-up details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (Client Details, text, required: false):
    This field is for providing a brief description of yourself as a client.
    Please enter your name and any other relevant details as requested.
- **Visit Reasons** (Visit Reasons, select_one, required: false):
    Please select one of the provided reasons for visiting a hair specialist (e.g., Hair Loss, Scalp Condition, Hair Color, or Other).
    Be as specific as possible to help the hair specialist tailor your appointment to your needs.
- **Visit Date** (Visit Date, date, required: false):
    Choose the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
    Please ensure it's a date you are available for.
- **Visit Time** (Visit Time, time, required: false):
    Select the time of day you would like to schedule your appointment.
    Please note that the hair specialist may have availability restrictions.
- **Follow Up Details** (Follow Up Details, text, required: false):
    If you have any additional information or requests, please provide it in this field.
    This helps the hair specialist understand your needs and preferences better.
