# Piano Tuning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piano Tuning Appointment Form is designed to schedule appointments for piano-related services such as tuning, maintenance, or repair. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information: Please provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your appointment.
2. Select your service type: Choose the type of service you require (Piano Tuning, Piano Maintenance, or Piano Repair).
3. Enter your piano model: If you have a specific piano model, please let us know so we can prepare accordingly.
4. Schedule your appointment: Select a date and time that suits you best for the appointment.
5. Choose your appointment location: Select the location where you would like to have the appointment (Home, On site, Studio, or Other).
6. Provide additional details: If you have any specific requests or preferences, please provide them in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Piano Tuning Appointment Form (id: 1)**: This is the main title of the form, indicating what we're scheduling appointments for.
* **Customer Service Details (id: 2)**: This field is for providing a brief description of the issue with your piano or what service you're seeking.
* **Service Type (id: 3)**: Choose the type of service you require (Piano Tuning, Piano Maintenance, or Piano Repair). This will help us tailor the service to your specific needs.
* **Service Type Value (id: 4)**: This field is not required but can be used to provide more information about the service type you selected.
* **Piano Model (id: 5)**: If you have a specific piano model, please enter it here so we can prepare accordingly.
* **Appointment Date (id: 6)**: Select a date for your appointment. Please ensure it's within the timeframe we've agreed upon.
* **Appointment Time (id: 7)**: Choose a time that suits you best for the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration (id: 8)**: Please select the duration of the appointment, which will help us schedule the service accordingly.
* **Appointment Location (id: 9)**: Choose the location where you would like to have the appointment (Home, On site, Studio, or Other). If you select "Other", please provide the location in the next field.
* **Appointment Location Value (id: 10)**: If you selected "Other" for the location, please provide the specific location here.
* **Technician Name (id: 11)**: This field is currently not in use.
* **Technician Contact (id: 12)**: This field is currently not in use.
* **Customer Name (id: 13)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or documents.
* **Customer Email (id: 14)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you regarding your appointment.
* **Customer Phone (id: 15)**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.
* **Appointment Notes (id: 16)**: If you have any specific requests or concerns, please provide them here.
* **Service Details (id: 17)**: Please enter any specific details about the service you're requesting.
* **Special Requests (id: 18)**: If there's anything else you'd like us to know about your appointment, please provide it here.

Note: Please ensure you complete all required fields and provide accurate information to help us schedule your appointment successfully.
