# Online Course Development Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients regarding their online course development appointment, including their contact details, appointment specifics, availability, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Client Information** in the first section, including your name and contact details.
2. In the **Appointment Details** section, describe the purpose of your appointment and any specific requirements or questions you have.
3. If applicable, provide your **Availability** for the appointment in this section.
4. Finally, specify your **Communication Preferences** in this section, including your preferred contact method and frequency of communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, text, required):
	+ Please provide your name and contact details to help us get in touch with you regarding your appointment.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required):
	+ Describe the purpose of your appointment and any specific requirements or questions you have.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional):
	+ If you are unable to specify a specific time for your appointment, describe your available times or any constraints you have.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ Specify your preferred contact method and frequency of communication. This will help us tailor our communication to suit your needs.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and clear information in each section to ensure a smooth and efficient appointment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
- You can complete this form at your convenience, and we will be in touch with you within the next 24-48 hours to confirm your appointment.
