# Healthcare Appointment Scheduling Integration Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and manage healthcare appointment scheduling integrations. It is designed for healthcare professionals to schedule appointments with patients, track progress, and assign tasks to team members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate through the form's sections, starting with **Project Details**.
2. In the **Project Details** section, provide the project title, description, start and end dates (if applicable).
3. In the **Clinician Details** section, enter the clinician's name, email, and phone number.
4. In the **Integration Request** section, describe the integration request and select the integration type (Web API, Mobile App, or Custom).
5. In the **Integration Status** section, choose the current status of the integration (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
6. In the **Priority** section, select the priority level of the integration (High, Medium, or Low).
7. In the **Deadline** section, enter the deadline for the integration.
8. In the **Next Steps** section, describe the next steps for the integration.
9. In the **Assigned To** section, select the team member assigned to the integration.
10. In the **Integration Date** and **Integration Time** sections, enter the date and time for the integration.
11. In the **Notes** section, add any additional comments or notes about the integration.
12. Finally, in the **Form ID**, **Form Name**, and **Form Status** sections, enter the form ID, name, and status.
