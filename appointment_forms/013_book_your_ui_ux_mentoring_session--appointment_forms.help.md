# Book Your UI/UX Mentoring Session - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and tailor our UI/UX mentoring session to your specific needs. Please fill out this form to provide us with information about yourself, your goals, and your preferences for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email Address in the "Email Address" field. This is where we will send session details.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your current Experience Level in the "Your Experience Level" field.
6. Describe your design background in the "What is your design background" field.
7. Check all that apply for your mentoring goals in the "What are your mentoring goals" field.
8. Describe the current challenges you are facing in the "Current Design Challenges" field.
9. Select all applicable design tools you use in the "Which design tools do you use" field.
10. Check all that apply for the types of projects you are interested in in the "What type of projects interest you" field.
11. Choose your preferred Session Duration in the "Session Duration Preference" field.
12. Select your preferred Session Frequency in the "How often would you like sessions" field.
13. Choose a Preferred Session Date in the "Preferred Session Date" field.
14. Select your Preferred Time Slot in the "Preferred Time Slot" field.
15. Choose your Preferred Session Format in the "Preferred Session Format" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`mentee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`mentee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`mentee_email`, email, required): We will send session details to this email address.
* **Phone Number** (`mentee_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Your Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current level in UI/UX design:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Professional
	+ Career Changer
* **What is your design background** (`design_background`, text, optional): Describe your design experience or training.
* **What are your mentoring goals** (`mentoring_goals`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply:
	+ UI Design fundamentals
	+ UX Research
	+ Wireframing and prototyping
	+ Design systems
	+ Usability testing
	+ Portfolio development
	+ Career guidance
	+ Specific software skills
* **Current Design Challenges** (`current_challenges`, text, required): Describe the specific problems you are facing.
* **Which design tools do you use** (`design_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Figma
	+ Adobe XD
	+ Sketch
	+ Adobe Creative Suite
	+ Protopie
	+ Framer
	+ Other
* **What type of projects interest you** (`project_focus`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply:
	+ Web design
	+ Mobile app design
	+ Product design
	+ UX research
	+ Interaction design
	+ Visual design
	+ Design systems
	+ Other
* **Session Duration Preference** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred session duration:
	+ 30 minutes
	+ 45 minutes
	+ 60 minutes
	+ 90 minutes
* **How often would you like sessions** (`session_frequency`, select_one, required): Select your preferred session frequency:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ As needed
* **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your session.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select your preferred time slot:
	+ Morning (8-12 AM)
	+ Afternoon (12-4 PM)
	+ Evening (4-8 PM)
	+ Flexible
* **Preferred Session Format** (`session_format`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred session format:
	+ Video call
	+ Phone call
	+ In-person
