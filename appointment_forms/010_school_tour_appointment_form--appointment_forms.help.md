# School Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a school tour for a student. It is intended to be filled out by the student or parent to provide necessary information for the tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Select the school tour date using the date picker provided.
* Choose the tour time using the time picker provided.
* Enter the tour duration in minutes.
* Enter your name.
* Enter your parent or guardian's contact information.
* Enter your email address.
* Enter the name of the school you are visiting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Tour Date <code>date</code>**: Select the date you would like to have the school tour.
* **Tour Time <code>time</code>**: Select the time you would like to have the school tour.
* **Tour Duration <code>number</code>**: Enter the duration of the tour in minutes.
* **Student Name <code>text</code>**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Parent Contact <code>text</code>**: Enter your parent or guardian's contact information.
* **Email <code>email</code>**: Enter your email address.
* **School Name <code>text</code>**: Enter the name of the school you are visiting.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and successful school tour experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
