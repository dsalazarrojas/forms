# Tanning Preparation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tanning Preparation Guide is a form designed to assist individuals in preparing for a tanning appointment. It will guide you through the necessary steps and information required for a smooth and successful tanning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as required, taking into account the notes and hints provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preparation Instructions** (`Preparation Instructions`, `text`, required): Please provide detailed information about how you have prepared for the tanning appointment. Be as descriptive as possible.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one of the options to confirm if you have read and understand the Preparation Guide. Choose 'I have read and understand the Preparation Guide.' if you have reviewed the guide and agree to follow its instructions.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`Confirm Appointment`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the tanning appointment is scheduled or 'No' to indicate otherwise.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or information related to the tanning appointment. This is a free-text field for you to elaborate on your preparation and any questions or concerns.
* **Appointment Details** (`Appointment Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any specific details about the tanning appointment, such as date, time, and location.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information or questions you have related to the tanning appointment.
