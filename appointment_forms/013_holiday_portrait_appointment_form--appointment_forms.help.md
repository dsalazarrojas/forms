# Holiday Portrait Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Portrait Appointment Form is used to schedule a holiday portrait session with a photographer. This form is used to collect information about the session, including the appointment details, date, time, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the appointment details in the "Prophets Appointment Details" field.
2. Select the date and time of the session in the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields.
3. Choose whether the subject will be accompanied by a photographer during the session ("Photographer") or not.
4. Enter the subject's name in the "Subject Name" field.
5. Enter the contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Appointment Form** (`holiday_appointment_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the appointment.
* **Prophets Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the details of the appointment.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the subject will be accompanied by a photographer during the session.
	+ Select "Yes" if the subject will be accompanied by a photographer.
	+ Select "No" if the subject will not be accompanied by a photographer.
* **Subject Name** (`subject_name`, text, optional): Enter the subject's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the subject's guardian or parent.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the subject's guardian or parent.
