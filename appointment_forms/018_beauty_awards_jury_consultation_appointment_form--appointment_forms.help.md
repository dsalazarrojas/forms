# Beauty Awards Jury Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beauty Awards Jury Consultation Appointment Form is used to gather information from jury members about themselves and their availability for a consultation appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address.
2. Specify your phone number.
3. Choose your area of expertise (e.g., Makeup, Hair Care, Skincare, etc.).
4. Select your organization or company.
5. Choose a preferred date and time for the consultation appointment.
6. Specify your time zone.
7. Choose your preferred meeting format (in-person, video conference, phone call, or hybrid).
8. Choose a meeting location (if applicable).
9. Specify the topics you would like to discuss during the consultation.
10. Indicate your years of industry experience.
11. Confirm if you have previous judging experience.
12. Specify the estimated consultation duration needed.
13. Choose if you will need materials or documents beforehand.
14. Provide details about any materials or documents needed.
15. Specify any dietary restrictions or special accommodations.
16. Confirm if you would like to receive confirmation via email.
17. Provide any additional notes or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`jury_name`): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to during the consultation.
* **Email Address** (`jury_email`): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for confirmation and discussion.
* **Phone Number** (`jury_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for discussion or clarification.
* **Area of Expertise** (`jury_expertise`): Choose your area of expertise (e.g., Makeup, Hair Care, Skincare, etc.).
* **Organization/Company** (`organization`): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`preferred_date`): Choose a date for the consultation appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`): Choose a time for the consultation appointment.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`): Enter your time zone.
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`): Choose your preferred meeting format (in-person, video conference, phone call, or hybrid).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Enter the location for the meeting (if applicable).
* **Topics for Discussion** (`topics_to_discuss`): Specify the topics you would like to discuss during the consultation.
* **Years of Industry Experience** (`experience_level`): Enter the number of years you have experience in the industry.
* **Previous Judging Experience** (`previous_judging`): Confirm if you have previous judging experience.
* **Estimated Consultation Duration** (`consultation_duration`): Choose the estimated duration of the consultation.
* **Will You Need Materials or Documents Beforehand** (`materials_needed`): Choose if you will need materials or documents beforehand.
* **Describe Materials or Documents Needed** (`materials_details`): Provide details about any materials or documents needed.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Special Accommodations** (`dietary_restrictions`): Specify any dietary restrictions or special accommodations.
* **Send Confirmation to Email** (`confirmation_sent`): Confirm if you would like to receive confirmation via email.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`): Provide any additional notes or questions.
