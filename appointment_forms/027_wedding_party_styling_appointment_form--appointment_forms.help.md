## Wedding Party Styling Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Party Styling Appointment Form is a tool for wedding vendors to schedule appointments with potential clients. This form allows vendors to gather information about their clients, including their name, email, phone number, and appointment time and date. The form also includes a message field for clients to provide any additional information or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your preferred appointment time from the drop-down menu in the "Appointment Time" field.
5. Choose your preferred appointment date from the drop-down menu in the "Appointment Date" field.
6. Provide any additional information or requests in the "Message" field.
7. Select your desired option from the "Save Changes" field to confirm your appointment preferences.
8. Finally, select "True" or "False" from the "Submit" field to submit or cancel your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (wedding_party_styling_name)**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email (wedding_party_styling_email)**: Enter your email address as it appears in your email client.
* **Phone (wedding_party_styling_phone)**: Enter your phone number, including any leading zero or leading zeroes if applicable.
* **Appointment Time (wedding_party_styling_time)**: Choose a time that works best for you and your wedding party.
* **Appointment Date (wedding_party_styling_date)**: Choose a date that works best for you and your wedding party.
* **Message (wedding_party_styling_message)**: Provide any additional information or requests you have for your appointment.
* **Save Changes (save_changes)**: Choose an option to confirm your appointment preferences:
	+ Wedding Party Styling Form 1
	+ Wedding Party Styling Form 2
	+ Wedding Party Styling Form 3
* **Submit (submit)**: Confirm your appointment by selecting "True" or "False". If you select "True", your appointment will be submitted. If you select "False", your appointment will be cancelled.
