# Public Relations Strategy Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Relations Strategy Appointment Form is used to collect information about upcoming public relations appointments. It is intended for new appointments only and should not be used to update or change existing appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Enter your Email (if you have one)
3. Provide your Phone number (if you have one)
4. Specify the Date of the appointment
5. Choose the Time of the appointment
6. Add any additional Notes about the appointment
7. Describe the strategy behind the appointment

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field to enter your name for the appointment.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Date**: A field to select the date of the appointment.
* **Time**: A field to select the time of the appointment.
* **Notes**: A field to add any additional information or comments about the appointment.
* **Strategy**: A field to describe the strategy behind the appointment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have checked the company's existing calendar before submitting this form to avoid duplication.
* Please be thorough and accurate in your responses, as this information will be used to plan and coordinate the appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant team or department for assistance.
