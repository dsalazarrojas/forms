# Performance Evaluation Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Evaluation Appointment Form is designed to create appointments for employee evaluations, reviews, checks, or other types of assessments. This form is used to schedule and organize evaluation appointments for employees, ensuring that each evaluation is properly documented and managed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Employee ID: Enter the employee's unique identification number.
	* Manager ID: Enter the manager's unique identification number.
	* Date: Choose the date for the evaluation appointment.
	* Time: Select the time for the evaluation appointment.
	* Evaluation Type: Choose the type of evaluation (Evaluation, Review, or Check).
	* Status: Choose the status of the evaluation (Completed, Pending, or Cancelled).
2. Fill in optional fields:
	* Date of Birth: Enter the employee's date of birth for demographic purposes.
	* Contact Number: Enter the employee's contact number for communication.
	* Email: Enter the employee's email address for further communication.
	* Assigned To: Select the manager or HR representative responsible for the evaluation.
	* Other Status: Enter any other status that may apply (if not listed in the standard status options).
3. Add any additional comments or information in the Evaluation Comment field.
4. In the Evaluation Type field, you can also select "Other" and provide a custom evaluation type in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter the employee's unique identification number.
* **Manager ID** (Manager ID, text, required): Enter the manager's unique identification number.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Choose the date for the evaluation appointment.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Select the time for the evaluation appointment.
* **Evaluation Type** (Evaluation Type, select one, required): Choose the type of evaluation (Evaluation, Review, or Check).
* **Evaluation Comment** (Evaluation Comment, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the evaluation.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select multiple, optional): Select the manager or HR representative responsible for the evaluation.
* **Status** (Status, select one, optional): Choose the status of the evaluation (Completed, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Other Status** (Other Status, text, optional): Enter any other status that may apply (if not listed in the standard status options).
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, optional): Enter the employee's contact number for communication.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address for further communication.
* **Field 14** (Field 14, text, optional): This field is currently not in use.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's label or contact your HR representative for assistance.
* Double-check the status and evaluation type for accuracy to avoid confusion.
