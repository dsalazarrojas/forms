# Braiding Service Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their braiding service preferences and requirements. It helps us understand your needs and tailor our services to provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and fill out each field accordingly.
2. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) or a "required" label.
3. Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure we can serve you best.
4. If you have any special requests or notes, please include them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.
5. Review your form before submission to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, *Required*): Enter your full name as you would like it to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, *Required*): Enter your email address for appointment confirmations and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, *Required*): Provide your primary contact phone number.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_date`, *Required*): Choose a date that works best for you.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, *Required*): Select a time that works best for you.
* **Estimated Duration** (`estimated_duration`, *Required*): Choose the estimated duration of your appointment from the options provided.
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, *Required*): Select the type of hair you have from the options provided.
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, *Required*): Choose the approximate length of your hair from the options provided.
* **Current Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, *Optional*): If applicable, describe the current state of your hair.
* **Preferred Braiding Style** (`braiding_style`, *Required*): Select the braiding style you prefer from the options provided.
* **Style Variation** (`style_variation`, *Optional*): If you have a specific style variation in mind, provide details here.
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, *Optional*): If applicable, choose your preferred hair color.
* **Accessories Desired** (`accessories`, *Optional*): Select any accessories you might want during your braiding experience.
* **Previous Braiding Experience** (`braiding_experience`, *Optional*): If you've had braiding experience before, select accordingly.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, *Optional*): Share any additional information that might be helpful for your appointment.

# Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure we can serve you best.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Review your form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you need any clarification on any field, feel free to ask.
