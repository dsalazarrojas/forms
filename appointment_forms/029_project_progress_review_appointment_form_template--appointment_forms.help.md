# Project Progress Review Appointment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and review information for project progress. It helps to ensure that all stakeholders are informed and on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information. Start by providing an overview of the project progress, then move on to review meeting details, review date, review time, and so on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Progress Review Appointment (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_1`, text, optional)
This field is used to provide an overview of the project progress.

### Review Meeting Details (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_2`, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a review meeting is necessary for the project.

### Review Date (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_3`, date, optional)
Enter the date of the review meeting.

### Review Time (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_4`, time, optional)
Enter the time of the review meeting.

### Review Meeting Duration (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_5`, number, optional)
Enter the duration of the review meeting.

### Meeting Location (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_6`, text, optional)
Enter the location of the meeting.

### Review Meeting Notes (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_7`, note, optional)
Enter any notes related to the review meeting.

### Meeting Participants (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_8`, select_multiple, optional)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each participant.

### Project Lead Contact (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_9`, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the project lead is available for contact.

### Review Meeting Scheduling (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_10`, time, optional)
Enter the scheduling details of the review meeting.

### Project Manager Contact (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_11`, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the project manager is available for contact.

### Review Meeting Status (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_12`, select_one, optional)
Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the project meeting.

### Meeting Schedule (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_13`, select_one, optional)
Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the project schedule is set.

### Review Meeting Notes (`project_progress_review_appointment_form_template_14`, note, optional)
Enter any notes related to the review meeting.

This help guide is designed to assist you in completing the form accurately and efficiently.
