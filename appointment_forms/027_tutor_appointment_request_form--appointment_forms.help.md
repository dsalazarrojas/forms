# Tutor Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to schedule a meeting with a tutor. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that we can best assist you with your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below:
- Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
- Select the subject you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
- Choose a date and start time for your appointment.
- Choose a preferred tutor from the dropdown list.
- If you have any additional comments or instructions, type them in the "Message" field.
- Click the submit button to complete and send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Name** (Student Name)
* **(student_name)** (`text`, `required`): This is your name, which we need to know who you are so that we can best assist you with your request.
### **Student Email** (Student Email)
* **(student_email)** (`email`, `required`): This is your email address, which we will use to contact you with your appointment details and any further instructions.
### **Select a Subject** (Select a Subject)
* **(subject_id)** (`select_one`, `required`): Please choose one of the following subjects: Math, Science, or English. This will help us assign you to the right tutor.
### **Date** (Date)
* **(date)** (`date`, `required`): Choose a date for your appointment. Please make sure the date is in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### **Start Time** (Start Time)
* **(start_time)** (`time`, `required`): Choose a start time for your appointment. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
### **End Time** (End Time)
* **(end_time)** (`time`, `required`): Choose an end time for your appointment. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
### **Tutor Preference** (Tutor Preference)
* **(tutor_preference)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Choose one or more tutors from the list below. This will help us assign you to the right tutor.
* Options: Mr. Smith, Dr. Lee, Ms. Johnson
### **Message** (Message)
* **(message)** (`note`, `optional`): If you have any additional comments or instructions, please type them here. This will help us better understand your needs.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will respond to your form submission as soon as possible.
