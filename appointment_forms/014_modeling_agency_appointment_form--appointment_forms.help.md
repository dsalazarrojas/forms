# Modeling Agency Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Modeling Agency Appointment Form is used by agency staff to collect and organize information for upcoming appointments. This form helps the agency to manage appointments more effectively and ensures that all necessary details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the agency name and date of the appointment.
2. Select the appointment type (Meeting, Phone Call, or In-Person Meeting).
3. Enter the phone number, email, and a brief message for the appointment.
4. Specify the appointment reason and any additional client information if necessary.
5. Optionally, provide agency information and the assigned tool.
6. Review all fields to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency where the appointment is scheduled to take place.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the appointment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the appointment in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the agency or contact person for the appointment.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the agency or contact person for the appointment.
* **Message**: A brief description of the appointment or meeting purpose.
* **Appointment Type**: Select the type of appointment (Meeting, Phone Call, or In-Person Meeting).
* **Appointment Reason**: Enter the reason for the appointment.
* **Client Info**: Provide any additional information about the client, if necessary.
* **Agency Info**: Provide any additional information about the agency or contact person, if necessary.
* **Assigned Tool**: Specify the tool assigned for the appointment, if any.
* **Submit**: Review all fields to ensure accuracy and click submit to save the form.
