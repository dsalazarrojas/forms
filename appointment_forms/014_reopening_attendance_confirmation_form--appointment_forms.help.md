**reopening_attendance_confirmation_form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is used for the reopening of appointments and confirmation of schedules. It collects information from the user for the purpose of confirming appointment schedules and ensuring that all parties are aware of the dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **User Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Confirm your **Confirmation Status** by selecting either **True** or **False**.
4. Add any additional **Comments** if needed.
5. Set the date for the appointment in the **Date** field.
6. Set the time for the appointment in the **Time** field.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
8. Confirm the date and time for the appointment in the **Confirm Date** and **Confirm Time** fields.
9. Repeat steps 7-8 for multiple appointments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your records.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you will be attending or not attending the appointment.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Confirm Date**: Confirm the date of the appointment.
* **Confirm Time**: Confirm the time of the appointment.
* **Phone 2**: Enter your phone number for further contact (this field is optional).
* **Confirmation 2**: Confirm that you will be attending or not attending the appointment (this field is optional).
* **Date 2**: Enter the date of the appointment (this field is optional).
* **Time 2**: Enter the time of the appointment (this field is optional).

Note: The fields with **2** in their names are repeated for multiple appointments.

Please be aware that this form is used for the reopening of appointments, and the fields are designed to gather information for this specific purpose. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
