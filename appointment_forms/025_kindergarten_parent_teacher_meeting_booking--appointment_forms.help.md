# Kindergarten Parent Teacher Meeting Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents book a meeting with their child's teacher to discuss their child's progress in kindergarten.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the meeting you would like to book (Page 1).
2. Share your contact and other relevant details (Page 2).
3. Add your child's teacher's contact and other relevant details (Page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Meeting Details
* **meeting_details** (`meeting_details`, text, required: false): Please describe the meeting you would like to book, including any specific topics you'd like to discuss or any concerns you have.

### Page 2: Parent Information
* **parent_details** (`parent_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you.

### Page 3: Teacher Information
* **teacher_details** (`teacher_info`, text, required: false): Enter your child's teacher's name and contact information, so they know who to reach out to for the meeting.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have your child's teacher's permission to book this meeting.
* Be specific about the topics you'd like to discuss in the meeting.
* If you need to cancel or reschedule the meeting, please contact the teacher directly.
