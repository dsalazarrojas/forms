# Couples Counseling Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for couples counseling sessions. Please provide accurate and complete details to ensure a smooth and effective counseling experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the fields as prompted, making sure to provide accurate and complete details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Info** (`session_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any relevant details about the upcoming session.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): Please provide information about yourself and your partner, including names, contact numbers, and any relevant medical or mental health history.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): If you've already made payment arrangements, please include the payment details here. If you're unsure about payment, please contact us for further instructions.
* **Session Schedule** (`session_schedule`, date, optional): Provide the date and time of your session. Please check with your partner to ensure you're both on the same schedule.
* **Therapist Info** (`therapist_info`, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or questions for your therapist, please include them here.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share with us, please include it here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Please select how you plan to make payment (Yes/No).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, note, optional): This is a field for any notes or terms related to payment.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, optional): If you have any concerns about cancellations, please include them here.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your prompt and accurate submission of this form to facilitate a smooth and effective counseling experience.
