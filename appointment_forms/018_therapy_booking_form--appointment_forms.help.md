# Therapy Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Therapy Booking Form is designed for users to book appointments with therapists. It collects user information, appointment details, and other relevant data to schedule a successful therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the user information section with your name and contact details.
2.  Select a therapist from the provided options.
3.  Choose the appointment date and time that suits you best.
4.  Enter the desired appointment duration.
5.  Select the type of appointment you need.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
7.  Confirm your appointment details.
8.  Confirm whether you would like to be contacted to confirm the appointment.
9.  Enter a reason for cancellation, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (user_info):
	+ This section collects your name and contact details.
* **Appointment** (appointment):
	+ Enter a brief description of the reason for your appointment.
* **Appointment Date** (appointment_date):
	+ Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (appointment_time):
	+ Enter the time you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Details** (appointment_details):
	+ Add any additional information about your appointment.
* **Therapist** (therapist):
	+ Select the therapist you would like to work with.
* **Appointment Duration** (appointment_duration):
	+ Enter the length of your desired appointment.
* **Appointment Type** (appointment_type):
	+ Choose the type of appointment you need (Individual Session, Group Session, or Consultation).
* **Appointment Notes** (appointment_notes):
	+ Add any extra comments or information about your appointment.
* **Email** (email):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (phone):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Confirm Appointment** (confirm_appointment):
	+ Confirm whether you would like to be contacted to confirm your appointment.
* **Confirm Appointment Details** (confirm_appointment_details):
	+ Confirm your appointment details, if applicable.
* **Cancel Reason** (cancel_reason):
	+ Enter a reason for cancellation, if applicable.
* **Cancellation Policy** (cancellation_policy):
	+ Choose the policy for your cancellation (Free, Reschedule, or Late Cancellation).

## Tips
- Be honest and clear when entering your appointment details.
- Select the correct therapist and appointment type for your needs.
- Confirm your appointment details to avoid any confusion.
- If you need to cancel, provide a reason to help us understand your situation.
