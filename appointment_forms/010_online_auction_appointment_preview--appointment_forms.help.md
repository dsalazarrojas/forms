## Online Auction Appointment Preview - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Auction Appointment Preview form is used to create a preview of an online auction appointment. This form helps users understand and review relevant information before proceeding with the auction setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the **Schedule Preview** section and select the correct option from the dropdown menu. This section determines whether the auction will be a live or online event.
	* If you select "Yes" for Schedule Preview, you will be able to review the auction schedule details.
	* If you select "No" for Schedule Preview, you will not be able to preview the schedule.
2. In the **Contact Details** section, provide any relevant contact information for the auction, such as email or phone numbers.
3. In the **Auction Interests** section, select the interests related to the auction that you would like to highlight or promote.
4. In the **Platform Preferences** section, select the platforms where the auction will be held or promoted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Preview** (`schedule_preview`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether the auction will be a live or online event.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant contact information for the auction.
* **Auction Interests** (`auction_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the interests related to the auction that you would like to highlight or promote.
* **Platform Preferences** (`platform_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the platforms where the auction will be held or promoted.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the current state of your auction and its setup before proceeding with this form.
* Review the selected options carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your auction's needs.
* This form may be used in conjunction with other forms, so ensure you have all necessary information ready.
