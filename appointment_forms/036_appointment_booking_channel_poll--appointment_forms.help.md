# Appointment Booking Channel Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your preferences and needs for booking appointments. The information collected will help us create a more effective and user-friendly appointment booking system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address in the **Respondent Information** section.
2. Select your preferred method of booking an appointment in the **Booking Channel Preferences** section.
3. Indicate how often you book appointments and your preferred notice period in the **Appointment Details** section.
4. Choose your preferred method of receiving confirmation and reminders in the **Confirmation and Reminder** section.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the **Additional Comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This will help us contact you with appointment details.
* **Phone Number**: This is optional but can help us contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Preferred Booking Method**: Select how you prefer to book an appointment. This will help us tailor our system to your needs.
* **Second Preferred Booking Method**: Choose your second preferred method of booking an appointment.
* **Appointment Frequency**: Indicate how often you book appointments.
* **Advance Notice**: Choose how much advance notice you usually give before booking.
* **Device Preference**: Select the device you typically use to book appointments.
* **Online Booking Importance**: Indicate how important 24-7 online booking availability is to you.
* **Confirmation Method**: Select how you would like to receive appointment confirmations. You can select all applicable methods.
* **Reminder Preference**: Choose whether you want appointment reminders.
* **Barriers to Booking**: Select any barriers that prevent you from booking appointments online.
* **Additional Features**: Enter any additional features that would improve your booking experience.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any comments or suggestions about the appointment booking process.

Note that this form is designed to be completed once, to provide accurate and useful information for our system improvements. Thank you for your input!
