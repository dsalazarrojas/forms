# Lash Adhesive Product Demo Appointment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to book a demo appointment with a representative from a company offering lash adhesive products. The form requests general product demo information, appointment scheduling details, and contact information. The purpose of this form is to facilitate communication and scheduling between the representative and the customer for a personalized product demo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Product Demo Info** section with the required and desired information about the product demo you are interested in.
2. Select the **Appointment Time** and **Appointment Date** for your demo appointment.
3. Choose the number of **Product Samples** you would like to receive.
4. Enter your **Email** address and **Phone** number for contact purposes.
5. Select your **Name** to associate with the appointment.
6. Choose the number of **Product Demos Allowed** and the **Location(s)** for the demo.
7. Confirm whether you are interested in **Product Demo Product**.
8. Optionally provide any **Notes** about your appointment in the space provided.
9. Click **Submit** to submit your appointment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Demo Info** (`product_demo_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product you are interested in demoing.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Select the time slot for your demo appointment. Please note that the available time slots are based on the company's availability.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your demo appointment.
* **Product Samples** (`product_samples`, select_multiple, optional): Select the number of product samples you would like to receive. The options are 2, 3, or 5 samples.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to associate with your appointment.
* **Product Demos Allowed** (`product_demos_allowed`, select_one, optional): Choose the number of product demos you are interested in.
* **Location** (`product_demos_locations`, select_multiple, optional): Select the location(s) for your demo appointment. The options are Store, Home, or Clinic.
* **Product Demo Product** (`product_demos_products`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your interest in the product demo.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your appointment information and confirm your reservation.

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by customers interested in scheduling a product demo appointment with a representative. The information provided will be used to confirm the appointment details and schedule the demo accordingly.
