# Client Intake Form For Facial Treatments - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients for facial treatments. It helps our technicians provide personalized services by understanding client's skin type, primary skin concern, and treatment preferences. Please fill out this form accurately to help us tailor your appointment to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (\*). If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, \*): Enter your full legal name for record-keeping purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, \*): Provide your best number for appointment reminders and notifications.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, \*): Enter your email address for treatment notes and receipts.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, \*): Select the skin type that best matches yours:
	+ Normal
	+ Dry
	+ Oily
	+ Combination
	+ Sensitive
* **Primary Skin Concern** (`primary_skin_concern`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select all that apply:
	+ Acne
	+ Fine Lines and Wrinkles
	+ Hyperpigmentation
	+ Redness and Rosacea
	+ Dehydration
	+ Enlarged Pores
* **Recent Skin Procedures** (`recent_skin_procedures`, `select_one`, optional): If you've had any recent skin procedures (e.g., peels, lasers, injectables) within the last 6 months, select one:
	+ None
	+ Chemical Peel
	+ Laser Treatment
	+ Botox or Fillers
	+ Microdermabrasion
	+ Other
* **Medications and Topicals** (`medications_and_topicals`, `text`, optional): List any medications or topical products you're currently using, including retinoids and acne medications.
* **Known Allergies** (`known_allergies`, `text`, optional): Include any known allergies to products or medications.
* **Preferred Treatment** (`preferred_treatment`, `select_one`, \*): Choose the facial treatment you prefer:
	+ Express Facial
	+ Signature Facial
	+ Acne Treatment Facial
	+ Anti Aging Facial
	+ Microdermabrasion
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, \*): Select your preferred appointment date.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): Choose your preferred appointment time (if not selected, we'll contact you for scheduling).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Any other information to help us tailor your treatment.

Remember, providing accurate and thorough information helps us better understand your needs and provide personalized services. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
