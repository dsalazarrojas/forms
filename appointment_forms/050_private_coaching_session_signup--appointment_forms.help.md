# Private Coaching Session Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Coaching Session Signup form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending a private coaching session. This form helps our team to better understand your goals and preferences, ensuring a more effective and personalized session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the required fields.
2. Provide your Phone Number if you'd like to be reached directly.
3. Select a Date for your session.
4. Choose your preferred Session Time.
5. Describe your Goals for the coaching session.
6. If you have a specific Preferred Time for the session, enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to which our team can send session details.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly for session reminders or updates.
* **Date**: Select a date for your coaching session. Our team will work around your availability to accommodate your preferred date.
* **Session Time**: Choose a session time that suits you best. Our team will make sure to schedule the session at the selected time.
* **Goals**: Describe your specific goals or areas you'd like to focus on during the coaching session.
* **Preferred Time**: If you have a specific preferred time for the session, enter it here. Please note that our team will do our best to accommodate your preferred time, but we may need to adjust it to fit our team's schedule.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is entered correctly to receive session updates and reminders.
* If you have any special requests or requirements for the session, please mention them in the "Goals" field.
* If you need to make any changes to your session details, please contact our team directly for assistance.
