# Fashion Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Consultation Form is a tool used to collect information from clients seeking fashion consultations. It is intended to be used by fashion consultants to schedule appointments with clients and determine the best time and date for consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the Client Information section.
2. Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
3. Select your availability in the Availability field. This will help us determine the best time for your consultation.
4. Choose your preferred contact method (Mobile, Email, Phone, or In Person) in the Preferred Contact field.
5. Select a date and time for your appointment from the calendar in the Appointment Date and Appointment Time fields.
6. Choose a preferred stylist from the Stylist Preferred field.
7. Add any additional notes about your consultation in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (Client Information, Text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information for the fashion consultant.
* **Name** (Name, Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Availability** (Availability, Date, Optional): Select your availability for the consultation.
* **Preferred Contact** (Preferred Contact, Select One, Optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (Mobile, Email, Phone, or In Person).
* **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, Date, Optional): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (Appointment Time, Time, Optional): Select a time for your appointment.
* **Preferred Stylist** (Preferred Stylist, Select One, Optional): Choose a preferred stylist for your consultation.
* **Notes** (Notes, Note, Optional): Add any additional information about your consultation that you would like to share with the fashion consultant.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your appointment is scheduled correctly.
* If you have any allergies or special requests, please include them in the Notes field.
* If you would like to reschedule your appointment, please contact us directly.
