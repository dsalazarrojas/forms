# Waxing Consent & Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Consent & Appointment Form is used to gather essential information from clients, including their consent to receive specific services, appointment details, and payment method. This form helps ensure that the client is aware of the services they are receiving and have given their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each field represents.
2.  Read and agree to the terms of service by checking the box next to "Consent".
3.  Enter your appointment details, including the date and time of the appointment.
4.  Select the services you wish to receive.
5.  Enter the payment method you prefer.
6.  Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent**: Check this box to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms of service.
	+ If you are unsure about any of the services, please ask your provider for clarification.
* **Appointment Details**: Enter the date and time of the appointment in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" for the date and "HH:MM" for the time.
	+ Ensure that the appointment date and time are accurate to avoid confusion.
* **Services**: Select the services you wish to receive from the list of options (Waxing, Haircut, Shaving).
	+ You can select more than one service if applicable.
* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you wish to receive from the list of options (Waxing, Haircut, Shaving).
	+ You can select more than one service if applicable.
* **Services Price**: Enter the price for the selected services.
	+ This will be used to calculate the total cost.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (True or False) - if true, the client will be expected to pay for the services.
	+ This option is required as it indicates the client's agreement to pay for the services.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your agreement to the services and terms of service.
	+ This is a required field as it serves as a form of acknowledgment.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
	+ This is a required field as it confirms the date of your signature.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time of your signature in the format "HH:MM".
	+ This is a required field as it confirms the time of your signature.
