# Lash Brand Retailer Discussion Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule and prepare for an appointment with a lash brand retailer. It helps to facilitate clear communication between the client and the retailer, ensuring a smooth and productive meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your **Retailer Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
2. Select a suitable **Date** and **Time** for the appointment that works best for you.
3. Indicate the **Duration** of the appointment in minutes.
4. Choose the topics you would like to discuss with the retailer from the list of **Discussion Topics**.
5. Add any additional **Comments** or **Notes** about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retailer Name** (required): This is the name of the lash brand retailer you would like to schedule with.
* **Email** (required): Enter the email address of the retailer.
* **Date** (required): Choose a date for the appointment using the date format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Time** (required): Select a time for the appointment in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Duration** (required): Specify the length of the appointment in minutes.
* **Discussion Topics** (required): Choose the topics you would like to discuss with the retailer.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Name** (required): Enter your name for the purpose of identification.
* **Email** (required): This is your email address.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the meeting.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate appointment request.
* Choose a suitable date and time that works best for you and the retailer.
* Be clear and specific about the topics you would like to discuss to help the retailer prepare.
* Use this form only once for each appointment to avoid duplication of efforts.
