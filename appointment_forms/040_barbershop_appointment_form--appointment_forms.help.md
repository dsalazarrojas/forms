# Barbershop Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for clients to make appointments with barbers at a barbershop. The form is designed to gather necessary information to schedule an appointment, including client details, service selection, and schedule preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name and last name.
2. Provide your email address and phone number for contact and SMS reminders.
3. Choose your preferred barber from the selection provided.
4. Select your primary service from the options provided.
5. Add any additional services as needed.
6. Select your preferred date and time for the appointment.
7. If your preferred date and time are unavailable, provide an alternate date.
8. Agree to the terms and conditions of the barbershop.
9. Consent to have your data stored for booking purposes.
10. Provide any special notes for the barber, such as a late arrival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that the barber can use for contact and SMS reminders.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for SMS reminders and contact.
* **Choose Your Main Service**: Select your primary service from the options provided.
* **Add-on Services**: Select any additional services you would like to include with your appointment.
* **Select a Professional Barber**: Choose a barber from the selection provided.
* **Preferred Date**: Enter your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Alternate Date (If preferred is unavailable)**: If your preferred date and time are not available, enter an alternate date.
* **I agree to the cancellation policy**: Agree to the cancellation policy, which includes cancelling at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a no-show fee.
* **I consent to my data being stored for booking purposes**: Consent to have your data stored for booking purposes.
* **Any special notes for your barber?**: Provide any special notes for the barber, such as a late arrival.
* **Booking Date**: This is automatically generated and cannot be edited.

**Tips:**
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and successful appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the barbershop directly.
* Cancellation policy: Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a no-show fee.
