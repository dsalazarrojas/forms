# Lash Studio Interior Design Appointment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lash Studio Interior Design Appointment form is a crucial tool for managing design consultations with clients. It helps the design team to gather essential information and prepare for a productive consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the client.
2. Provide your email address for any potential communication with the design team.
3. If applicable, enter your phone number for further communication.
4. Select one or multiple design consultants with whom you'd like to consult.
5. Choose a date for the consultation.
6. Select the desired time for the consultation.
7. Optionally add any additional notes for the consultation in the "Consultation Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as the client. This information helps us to identify you and ensure we have accurate records of our consultations.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address, which we will use to communicate with you regarding the consultation and other relevant information.
* **Client Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone for further communication, please enter your phone number here.
* **Design Consultation Prophets**: Select one or multiple design consultants from the list who you'd like to consult with. This helps us to prepare the right team for your consultation.
* **Design Consultation Date**: Choose a date for the consultation. This is the day we will meet to discuss your design requirements.
* **Design Consultation Time**: Select the desired time for the consultation. This ensures we meet at a time convenient for you.
* **Consultation Notes**: If you have any specific requirements, questions or any other important details you'd like to share, please add them in the "Consultation Notes" field. This helps us to prepare and tailor the consultation to your needs. 

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* For any further queries, please reach out to the Lash Studio team at your earliest convenience.
