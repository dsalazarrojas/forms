## Media Buy Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Buy Planning Appointment Form is designed to collect information about a media buy planning appointment, including the company's and contact's details, meeting information, and meeting purpose. This form helps in streamlining the planning process and ensuring that all necessary information is collected in one place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company information, including the company name, in the "Company Info" field.
2. Provide the contact information, including the contact name and phone number, in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Enter the meeting information, including the meeting date and time, in the "Meeting Info" field.
4. Specify the meeting purpose, which is required, in the "Meeting Purpose" field. This will help the appointment planner understand the main objectives of the meeting.
5. Enter the company address, which is also required, in the "Company Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Info** (Company Info, Text, Required): Enter the name of the company to be met with.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, Text, Required): Provide the contact name and phone number to reach out to the representative.
* **Meeting Info** (Meeting Info, Text, Required): Enter the meeting date and time, e.g., "Monday, 14:00" or "2023-02-01 14:00".
* **Meeting Purpose** (Meeting Purpose, Text, Required): Briefly explain the main objective of the meeting, e.g., "Discuss ad campaign strategy" or "Meet to review current media plans".
* **Company Address** (Company Address, Text, Required): Enter the address of the company, e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA".

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the company and contact information for accuracy.
* Clearly specify the meeting purpose to help the appointment planner understand the main objectives of the meeting.
* Ensure that you enter the company address correctly to help with directions or logistics.
