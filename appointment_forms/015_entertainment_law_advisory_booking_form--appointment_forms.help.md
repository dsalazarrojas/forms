# Entertainment Law Advisory Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Law Advisory Booking Form is a tool for clients to book an appointment with the entertainment law advisory team. It provides a structured way to gather essential information about the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details in the `client_details` section.
2. Select the preferred session time (morning, afternoon, or evening) in the `advisory_session` section.
3. Input the client's name in the `client_name` field.
4. Enter the desired date and time for the appointment in the `date_time` field.
5. Select the time zone for the appointment in the `time_zone` field (if necessary).
6. Enter the client's email address in the `email` field.
7. Enter the client's phone number in the `phone` field.
8. Provide the client's address in the `client_address` field.
9. Choose one or more members of the legal team to work with from the `legal_team` field.
10. If applicable, add any additional comments or instructions in the `message` field.
11. Confirm whether the session is scheduled or not by selecting an option from the `confirm_session` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details (client_details)**: This field is for entering basic information about the client, such as contact details and any other relevant details.
*   **Advisory Session (advisory_session)**: Select the preferred time for the advisory session. This will help us schedule the appointment accordingly.
*   **Client Name (client_name)**: Please enter your full name so we can address you properly.
*   **Date and Time (date_time)**: Enter the date and time you prefer for the appointment. Be as specific as possible so we can schedule accordingly.
*   **Time Zone (time_zone)**: If you are not in our local time zone, please select your time zone so we can adjust the schedule.
*   **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any updates or confirmations.
*   **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Client Address (client_address)**: Enter your address so we can send any relevant information or reminders.
*   **Legal Team (legal_team)**: Choose one or more members of the legal team you would like to work with.
*   **Message (message)**: If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here.
*   **Confirm Session (confirm_session)**: Please let us know if the session is scheduled or not.
