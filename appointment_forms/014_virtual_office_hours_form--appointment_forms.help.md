# Virtual Office Hours Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Office Hours Form is designed to be a tool for students to schedule virtual appointments with their professors. This form helps professors manage their time and be prepared for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a time slot that is most convenient for you from the "Schedule Appointment" field.
2. Optionally, provide context for the meeting in the "Reason for Visit" field.
3. If you have a student ID, enter it in the "Student ID" field.
4. If you are scheduling on behalf of a student, enter the student's ID in the "Professor ID" field.
5. Enter the expected duration of the meeting in the "Meeting Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Appointment** (`Schedule Appointment`, select_one, optional): Select a time slot for your virtual office hour appointment. This is your preferred time for the meeting.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Provide context for the meeting, such as a question or topic you'd like to discuss.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): If you are a student, enter your Student ID number. This will help the professor identify you.
* **Professor ID** (`professor_id`, number, optional): If you are scheduling on behalf of a student, enter their Student ID number.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, optional): Enter the expected duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Selected Time** (`selected_time`, time, optional): This field is automatically populated with the selected time slot.
