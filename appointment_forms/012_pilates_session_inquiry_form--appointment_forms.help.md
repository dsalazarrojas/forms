# Pilates Session Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pilates Session Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from customers or clients who are interested in booking a Pilates session. This form is divided into several sections to help us understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section. This includes your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Schedule" section, select the days and times when you are available to attend a Pilates session.
3. In the "Classes" section, choose whether you are interested in attending group classes or individual one-on-one sessions.
4. In the "Pricing" section, select the pricing options that apply to you.
5. Finally, if you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info (text)**: This is where you can provide your contact information, such as your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
- **Schedule (select_multiple)**: Select the days and times you are available to attend a Pilates session. This will help us understand when you can fit in a session.
- **Classes (select_one)**: Choose whether you are interested in attending group classes or one-on-one sessions.
- **Pricing (select_multiple)**: Select the pricing options that apply to you. This will help us understand your budget and preferences.
- **Additional Comments (text)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Please provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
- We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible to discuss further details.
