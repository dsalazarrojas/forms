# Parent And Teacher Meeting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents and guardians book meetings with teachers for their children. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and effective communication process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the guardian's contact details, including name, surname, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the student's name and grade level.
3. Select the meeting date.
4. Choose the teacher(s) you'd like to meet with, indicating whether they are available to meet.
5. Choose a meeting slot.
6. If desired, add any additional meeting message or comment.
7. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guardian details** (Guardian details, text, required):
	+ Enter the parent or guardian's name and surname.
* **Name** (Name, text, required):
	+ Enter the student's name.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required):
	+ Enter a valid email address that the school can use to contact you.
* **Guardian phone** (Guardian phone, text, required):
	+ Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Student name** (Student name, text, required):
	+ Enter the student's name.
* **Student grade** (Student grade, number, required):
	+ Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Meeting date** (Meeting date, date, required):
	+ Select the date you'd like to meet with the teacher.
* **Teacher** (Teacher, select multiple, required):
	+ Choose the teacher(s) you'd like to meet with. Select 'Yes' for each available teacher.
* **Meeting slot** (Meeting slot, select one, required):
	+ Choose a suitable meeting slot for the teacher to meet with you.
* **Meeting message** (Meeting message, text, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or messages for the teacher.
 
 ## Tips
 
 * Be accurate and clear when filling in your contact details.
 * Ensure you've entered the correct student's name and grade level.
 * Double-check the meeting date and time.
 * Choose the correct teacher(s) and meeting slot.
 * If needed, add a meeting message to help the teacher prepare for the meeting.
