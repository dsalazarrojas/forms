# meeting_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect meeting-specific details and participant information for the purpose of confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll through the pages and fill out each section according to the instructions below.
2. Ensure you've filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your answers before confirming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details (`meeting_details`):** Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Attendee Details (`attendee_details`):** Enter your details in this field.
* **Participant Status (`participant_status`):** Choose how you intend to participate in the meeting (Confirmed, Declined, Pending, or No Response).
* **Participant Notes (`participant_notes`):** Leave any additional comments or notes for the participant.
* **Participant Email (`participant_email`):** Enter your email address associated with the participant.
* **Participant Phone (`participant_phone`):** Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Date (`participant_date`):** Enter the date of the participant's appointment.
* **Meeting Location (`meeting_location`):** Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time (`meeting_time`):** Enter the scheduled time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (`meeting_duration`):** Enter the duration of the meeting.
* **Participant Count (`participant_count`):** Enter the number of participants.
* **Participant Status Label (`participant_status_label`):** Enter the participant's status label.
* **Meeting Description (`meeting_description`):** Enter a description of the meeting.
* **Participant Id (`participant_id`):** Enter the participant's ID number.
* **Meeting Date (`meeting_date`):** Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time 2 (`meeting_time_2`):** Enter an additional meeting time.
* **Participant Email Label (`participant_email_label`):** Enter the label for the participant's email.
* **Participant Phone Label (`participant_phone_label`):** Enter the participant's phone label.
* **Participant Notes Label (`participant_notes_label`):** Enter the label for the participant's notes.
* **Meeting Location Label (`meeting_location_label`):** Enter the label for the meeting location.
* **Participant Count Label (`participant_count_label`):** Enter the label for the participant count.
* **Participant Status Label 2 (`participant_status_label_2`):** Enter the label for the participant status.
* **Meeting Duration Label (`meeting_duration_label`):** Enter the label for the meeting duration.
* **Meeting Description Label (`meeting_description_label`):** Enter the label for the meeting description.
* **Participant Id Label (`participant_id_label`):** Enter the label for the participant's ID.

Please ensure you fill out all required fields and review your answers carefully before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, contact support.
