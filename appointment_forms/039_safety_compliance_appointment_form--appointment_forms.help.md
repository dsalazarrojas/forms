# Safety Compliance Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Compliance Appointment Form is used to record and confirm appointments for safety-related activities, such as inspections or reviews. This form serves as a central repository for collecting information about scheduled meetings and appointments that may impact business operations or safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the business name and contact person relevant to the appointment.
2. Enter the contact email and phone number of the business contact.
3. Choose a specific date and time for the appointment.
4. Select a category and subcategory to associate with the appointment (if applicable).
5. Provide any additional address information if necessary.
6. Enter any relevant safety regulations or notes regarding the appointment.
7. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business or organization that is associated with this appointment.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this appointment.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person (Required).
- **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person (Required).
- **Appointment Date**: Select the date of the appointment (Required).
- **Appointment Time**: Select the time of the appointment (Required).
- **Inspector**: Enter the name or title of the inspector conducting the appointment (Optional).
- **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the appointment (Optional).
- **Category**: Select the category of the appointment (Required).
- **Sub Category**: Enter any subcategory or additional details for the appointment (Optional).
- **Address**: Enter the address of the business or location of the appointment (Optional).
- **Safety Regulations**: Enter any relevant safety regulations or guidelines for the appointment (Optional).

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for dates and HH:MM for times).
* Be as detailed as possible in the notes field to ensure a thorough record of the appointment.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
