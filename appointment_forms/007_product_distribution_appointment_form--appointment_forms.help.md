# Product Distribution Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for scheduling product distribution appointments with clients. It collects essential information to facilitate a smooth distribution process. Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the form's requirements before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the "Schedule Appointment" page.
- On this page, fill out the "Client Details" field with your client's information.
- Next, move to the "Product Details" field and enter the product-related information required for the distribution.
- Then, provide any necessary details about the distribution process in the "Distribution Details" field.
- Enter the appointment date and time in the "Date and Time" field using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- Finally, input the contact details, including phone number and email, in the "Contact Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Schedule Appointment**: (1, number, required: false) - Select the "Schedule Appointment" page to initiate the appointment process.
- **Client Details** (2, text, required: false) - Enter client information, such as name and address.
- **Product Details** (3, text, required: false) - Enter product-related information, like product codes or SKUs.
- **Distribution Details** (4, text, required: false) - Describe any special instructions or requirements for the distribution process.
- **Date and Time** (5, date, required: false) - Enter the appointment date and time using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Contact Details** (6, text, required: false) - Include phone number and email for effective communication.
