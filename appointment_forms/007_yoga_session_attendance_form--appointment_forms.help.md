# Yoga Session Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Yoga Session Attendance Form is a tool to collect attendance information for yoga sessions. It is meant to be used during or after the session to ensure that attendance is recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each field based on your experience and observations during the session.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Preview the form before submitting it to review your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1 (page_1)**: This is the first page of the form.
* **Page 2 (page_2)**: This field requires you to select whether the yoga session attendees were present or not. Select 'Yes' if they were present and 'No' if they were not.
* **Page 3 (page_3)**: This field is for additional notes or comments about the session. You can enter any relevant information here, but it is not a required field.
* **Page 3 Options**: As mentioned, this is a field for extra comments or details. Please use it for any notes that will help us better understand the session. 

## Tips
* Make sure to submit the form accurately and completely.
* Review your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your supervisor.
