# Date Selection And Confirmation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Date Selection And Confirmation Form" is a user-facing form designed to collect date and time preferences from users and confirm their booking status. This form is crucial for scheduling appointments, meetings, or other time-sensitive bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred date and time by clicking on the "Select Your Date" field.
2. Confirm your booking by checking "Confirm Booking" if you're sure about the date and time selected.
3. If you wish to cancel the booking, choose "Cancel Booking" to notify the system.
4. Optionally, enter your phone number for any additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Your Date** (`date_selection_and_confirmation_form`, date, required: false):
	+ Select a date and time that suits you best.
* **Confirm Booking** (`confirm_booking`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" to confirm your booking.
* **Cancel Booking** (`cancel_booking`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" to cancel your booking.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number for any additional contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to select a date and time that suits your schedule.
- Confirm your booking to ensure it's accurately recorded in the system.
- Cancel your booking if you're unable to attend or if there's a change in plans.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to use the phone number field for further communication.
