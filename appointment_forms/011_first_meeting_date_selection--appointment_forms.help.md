# first_meeting_date_selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist users in selecting key details for an initial appointment. The form is intended to be used in conjunction with other appointment scheduling tools to ensure consistency and accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the following sections to provide essential information for your appointment:
2. Select the first meeting date and time from the available options.
3. Indicate whether or not the appointment requires a service provider.
4. Optionally select one or multiple service providers for your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Meeting Date** (`First Meeting Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your first meeting.
* **Time of Day** (`Time of Day`, `time`, required: false): Select the time for your first meeting.
* **Service Provider** (`Service Provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Check 'Yes' if your appointment requires a service provider, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Select a Service Provider** (`Select a Service Provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If your appointment requires a service provider, select one or multiple providers from the available options.
* **Appointment Duration** (`Appointment Duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of your appointment in hours.

Note: The form does not save any data. It's intended to be a starting point for other appointment scheduling systems to use in conjunction with other forms and tools.
