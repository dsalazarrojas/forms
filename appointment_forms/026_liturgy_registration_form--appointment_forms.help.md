# liturgy_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a liturgy event. It collects your name, date of the event, time of the event, contact phone number, email, and any additional notes you would like to provide.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please start by ensuring that you have read and understood the information provided on the form purpose and instructions above.
*   Review each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
*   For each field, please use the format and guidelines provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name**: <label> (Name, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed for the event.
*   **Date**: <label> (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you are registering for.
*   **Event**: <label> (Event, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
*   **Time**: <label> (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you are registering for.
*   **Phone**: <label> (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Email**: <label> (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Notes**: <label> (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration, such as any special requests or accommodations you require.
*   **Assigned Tool**: <label> (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Select the tool you have been assigned to use during the liturgy event.
*   **Notes**: <label> (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your tool assignment, such as the specific settings or configurations required.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough when filling in your registration details.
*   Use clear and concise language in your notes field.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your administrator or team lead for assistance.
