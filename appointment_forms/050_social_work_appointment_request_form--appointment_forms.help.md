# Social Work Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Social Work Appointment Request Form is designed to collect information about your social work appointment needs. This form will help social workers understand your needs and preferences so they can tailor their services to best meet your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Select the options that apply to your needs.
3. Add any additional comments or details in the "Description" field.
4. Review your form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule An Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`): Briefly describe the purpose of your appointment.
* **Select Time** (`select_time`): Choose the time slot you would like for your appointment.
* **Select Date** (`select_date`): Choose the date for your appointment.
* **How long would you like to meet for?** (`select_duration`): Select the duration of your appointment.
* **Select Provider** (`select_provider`): Choose the social worker or staff member you would like to meet with.
* **Message** (`message`): Add any additional comments or details about your appointment.
* **Select Location** (`select_location`): Choose the location for your appointment.
* **Select Day of Week** (`select_day_of_week`): Choose the day of the week for your appointment.
* **Select Time of Day** (`select_time_of_day`): Choose the time of day for your appointment.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Sync with Google Calendar** (`sync_with_google_calendar`): If you have a Google Calendar account, you can sync your appointment with it.
* **Sync with Zoom** (`sync_with_zoom`): If you have a Zoom account, you can sync your appointment with it.
* **Sync with More** (`sync_with_more`): If you have another calendar or scheduling system, you can sync your appointment with it.
* **No Coding Required** (`no_coding`): Indicate if you don't require any coding during your appointment.
* **Easy to Customize** (`easy_to_customize`): Choose whether you would like the meeting to be easy to customize.
* **More Features** (`more_features`): Indicate if you would like to use any additional features.
* **Description** (`description`): Add any additional comments or details about your appointment.
* **Category** (`category`): Choose the category for your appointment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Choose the tool assigned to your appointment.
* **Output File** (`output_file`): Choose the output file format for your appointment.
* **Sync with Google Calendar** (`sync_with_google_calendar`): This field is not applicable for this form, please ignore.
* **Sync with Zoom** (`sync_with_zoom`): This field is not applicable for this form, please ignore.
* **Sync with More** (`sync_with_more`): This field is not applicable for this form, please ignore.
* **Note** (`note`): This field is not applicable for this form, please ignore.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This field is not applicable for this form, please ignore.
* **Output File** (`output_file`): This field is not applicable for this form, please ignore.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance.
