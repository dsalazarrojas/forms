# Streaming Moderator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Moderator Application Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in becoming stream moderators on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, carefully read the field-by-field explanations below to ensure you understand each section.
2. Fill out the form completely, as required fields are indicated by an asterisk (*).
3. Provide accurate and honest answers to help the review process.
4. Once you have filled out the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (**name**): Enter your real name for identification and verification purposes.
* **Streaming Name (**streaming_name**): Enter the name of your stream or channel. This can be a single name or your stream URL.
* **Availability (**availability**): Choose how often you are available to stream:
 	+ Option 1: Always Available
 	+ Option 2: Most Days
 	+ Option 3: Weekends Only
 	+ Option 4: Limited Availability
 	+ Option 5: Never Available
* **Schedule (**schedule**): Enter your preferred schedule in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00, 14:00, 18:00).
* **Email (**email**): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (**phone**): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Streaming Platform (**streaming_platform**): Select the platform on which you host your stream:
 	+ Twitch
 	+ Youtube
 	+ Facebook
 	+ Other (please specify)
* **Streaming Language (**streaming_language**): Select the language used in your stream:
 	+ English
 	+ French
 	+ Spanish
 	+ Chinese
* **Team Size (**team_size**): Choose the size of your streaming team:
 	+ Small
 	+ Medium
 	+ Large
* **Role (**role**): Select the role you are applying for:
 	+ Admin
 	+ Moderator
 	+ Other (please specify)

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
