# Social Gaming Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from social gamers on their gaming habits, satisfaction with various aspects of gaming, and preferences for game development tools and platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Carefully review the form questions and sections, ensuring you understand what information is being asked.
2. Answer all questions as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
3. Select your preferred options for each question, choosing from the options provided.
4. If a question has an "Other (Please specify)" option, feel free to provide more detailed information in the text field.
5. Make sure to fill out all sections, and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This section is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
- **Gaming Habits**: This question asks how often you play games, with options ranging from Rarely to Often.
- **Satisfaction**: This question asks how satisfied you are with different aspects of gaming, with options ranging from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
- **Suggestions**: This question asks for new features you would like to see in games or platforms, allowing you to provide specific ideas.
- **Email**: This question asks for your email address, which will be used to contact you for further questions or to provide updates.
- **Phone**: This question is optional, but if you provide your phone number, it will be used to contact you for further questions or to provide updates.
- **Social Media**: This question asks which social media platforms you use for gaming-related activities.
- **Follow Streams**: This question asks how often you follow streams, with options ranging from Never to Always.
- **Follow Channels**: This question asks how often you follow channels, with options ranging from Never to Often.
- **Follow Shoutouts**: This question asks how likely you are to get a shoutout in a game, with options ranging from 0% to 100%.
- **Follow Club Memberships**: This question asks how many game developer memberships you have, with options ranging from None to Three.
- **Follow Streamer Channels**: This question asks how many channels of streamers you follow, with options ranging from None to Three.
- **Follow Streamer Shoutouts**: This question asks how often you get shoutouts from streamers, with options ranging from Never to Often.
- **Follow Streamer Tips**: This question asks what type of tips or advice you find most helpful from streamers, with options ranging from Game Mechanics to Game Strategies.
- **Follow Streamer Tips Value**: This question is for any additional information you would like to provide about the type of tips you find most helpful.
- **Game Studio Preferences**: This question asks how important game studios are to you, with options ranging from Very Important to Not Important at All.
- **Community Preferences**: This question asks how important communities are to you, with options ranging from Very Important to Not Important at All.
- **Platform Preferences**: This question asks how important platforms are to you, with options ranging from Very Important to Not Important at All.
- **Game Developer Preferences**: This question asks how important game developers are to you, with options ranging from Very Important to Not Important at All.
- **Game Developer Favourite Language**: This question asks which programming language game developers use, with options ranging from C++ to Other (Please specify).
- **Game Developer Favourite Language Type**: This question asks for any additional information about the type of language you prefer.
- **Game Developer Favourite Language Framework**: This question asks which game development framework game developers use, with options ranging from Unity to Other (Please specify).
- **Game Developer Favourite Language Engine**: This question asks which language engine game developers use, with options ranging from C# to Other (Please specify).
- **Comments**: This section is for any further comments or thoughts you would like to provide.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have answered all relevant questions.
- Provide detailed answers to each question to help us better understand your preferences and habits.
- If a question has an "Other (Please specify)" option, feel free to provide more information in the text field.
- If you have any further comments or thoughts, please provide them in the "Comments" section.
