# Game Developer Asset Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from game developers to request support for their game development assets. Fill out this form to provide necessary details about your issue, and our support team will be able to assist you more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing with your game development asset.
2.  Select the type of asset that is experiencing the issue.
3.  Indicate the priority level of the issue, if applicable.
4.  Specify the game genre and platform(s) affected by the issue.
5.  Choose the current status of the asset.
6.  Provide any additional information that would be helpful for our support team to resolve the issue.
7.  Confirm that you are requesting support for the asset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Asset Type** (`Asset Type` , `select_one`, required): Please select the type of asset that is experiencing the issue. This will help our support team to better understand the context of your request.
*   **Issue Description** (`Issue Description` , `text`, required): Give a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing with your game development asset.
*   **Requested Resolution** (`Requested Resolution` , `text`, required): Describe the resolution you are looking for to resolve the issue.
*   **Issue Category** (`Issue Category` , `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, select any categories that are relevant to the issue.
*   **Priority Level** (`Priority Level` , `number`, optional): If you believe the issue is time-sensitive or critical, indicate the priority level. However, please note that priority levels are not always guaranteed to be addressed in that order.
*   **Game Genre** (`Game Genre` , `select_multiple`, required): Specify the genre of the game that is affected by the issue.
*   **Game Platform** (`Game Platform` , `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the platform(s) where the issue is occurring.
*   **Asset Version** (`Asset Version` , `number`, optional): If you are using a specific version of the asset, please indicate it.
*   **Current Status** (`Current Status` , `select_one`, required): Please select the current status of the asset.
*   **Support Requested** (`Support Requested` , `text`, required): Confirm that you are requesting support for the asset.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information to help our support team resolve your issue efficiently.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact our support team for clarification.
*   If the issue is critical, please mark it as such by selecting the highest priority level and describing the urgency in the issue description.
