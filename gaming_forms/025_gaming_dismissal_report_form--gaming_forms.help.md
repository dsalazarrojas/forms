# gaming_dismissal_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gaming dismissal report form is used to document the dismissal of a player from a game-related activity or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **player_name** field with the name of the player who was dismissed.
2. Enter the **game_date** field with the date of the game or activity where the player was dismissed.
3. Enter the **reason_for_dismissal** field with the reason why the player was dismissed, selecting one of the provided options.
4. Enter the **dismissal_details** field with any additional details about the dismissal.
5. If applicable, enter the **game_official_name**, **game_official_email**, **game_official_phone**, **game_official_title**, **game_official_team**, **game_official_position**, and **game_official_address** fields with the relevant information.
6. If applicable, enter the **player_official_name**, **player_official_email**, **player_official_phone**, **player_official_title**, **player_official_team**, **player_official_position**, and **player_official_address** fields with the relevant information.
7. Select the **dismissal_decision** field with the reason for the dismissal.
8. Select the **dismissal_status** field with the final status of the dismissal.
9. Enter any additional notes in the **game_official_notes** and **player_official_notes** fields if necessary.
