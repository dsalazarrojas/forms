# Gaming Community Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Community Network Membership Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the community. This form helps us understand your preferences, gaming habits, and agreement to our community rules and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Select your membership type (Gaming or Non-Gaming).
3. Choose your membership level (Casual, Active, Elite, or VIP).
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Choose the games you play (select one from the options).
6. Indicate your gaming experience.
7. Select your role in the community (Member, Moderator, Leader, or Admin).
8. Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to have you played games recently.
9. Agree to the Terms, Rules, and Code of Conduct.
10. Choose your membership status (Active or Inactive).
11. Enter the date you joined the community.
12. Enter the date you last logged in.
13. Enter the total time you have spent in the community.
14. Enter the total number of members in the community.
15. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to have you played games recently.
16. Enter the time of your last game played.
17. Agree to the Terms, Rules, and Code of Conduct again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name, as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as per your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication within the community.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for community communication.
* **Membership Type**: Select 'Gaming' if you are looking for gaming-related activities, 'Non-Gaming' for general community involvement.
* **Membership Level**: Choose your level of activity in the community (Casual, Active, Elite, or VIP).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time**: Enter the time you typically play games. (optional)
* **Note**: Add any additional notes you'd like to share with the community.
* **Gaming Experience**: Enter your total gaming experience. This is optional.
* **Games Played**: Choose the games you have played. Select one from the options.
* **Role**: Select your role in the community (Member, Moderator, Leader, or Admin).
* **Agree to Terms**: Select 'Agree' to acknowledge the community's terms.
* **Agree to Rules**: Select 'Agree' to acknowledge the community's rules.
* **Agree to Code of Conduct**: Select 'Agree' to acknowledge the community's code of conduct.
* **Membership Status**: Select 'Active' if you're an active member, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Membership Start Date**: Enter the date you joined the community.
* **Membership End Date**: Enter the date your membership will end.
* **Member Since**: Enter the time you have been a member.
* **Last Login**: Enter the time of your last login.
* **Total Time Spent**: Enter the total time you have spent in the community.
* **Total Members**: Enter the total number of members in the community.
* **Last Game Played**: Select 'Yes' if you have played games recently.
* **Last Game Time**: Enter the time of your last game played.
* **Agree to Code of Conduct**: Select 'Agree' to acknowledge the community's code of conduct.
* **Total Games Played**: Enter the total number of games you have played.
* **Total Members**: Enter the total number of members in the community.
