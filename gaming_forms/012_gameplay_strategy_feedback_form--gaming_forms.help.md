# Gameplay Strategy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gameplay Strategy Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from players on their preferred gameplay strategy, which will help game developers to better understand player preferences and improve the game experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred playstyle from the options provided in the "What is your preferred playstyle?" dropdown menu.
2. Choose your preferred game mode from the options provided in the "What game mode do you prefer?" dropdown menu.
3. Rate your performance on a scale from 1-10 in the "How would you rate your performance?" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the "Comments" field.
5. If you would like to provide your email address, please enter it in the "Email" field. This will allow us to contact you for further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
6. If you want to be notified of the submission date and time, please enter it in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields. (Note: These fields are not required.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Playstyle**: Please select the style of gameplay you enjoy the most. Do you prefer a slow and strategic approach, or a fast-paced action-packed experience?
* **Game Mode**: Choose the type of game mode you prefer. Do you like playing against the computer, or against other players?
* **Performance**: Rate your performance on a scale from 1 to 10. How well did you play the game?
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the game. What did you like or dislike about the game?
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your feedback, if you want to be notified of it.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted your feedback, if you want to be notified of it.
