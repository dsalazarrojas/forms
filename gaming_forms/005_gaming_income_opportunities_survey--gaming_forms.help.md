# Gaming Income Opportunities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your income streams from various gaming-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all income streams that apply to you from the "Income Streams" section.
2. Choose how often you earn money from these streams in the "How often" section.
3. If applicable, enter your average monthly income from each stream in the "Average Monthly Income" section.
4. Specify what motivates you to earn money from these streams in the "Motivation" section.
5. If you have other income streams not listed, describe them in the "Other Income Streams" section.
6. Finally, select your type of gamer from the "Gamer Type" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income Streams** (`income_streams`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select all income streams that apply to you from the list of options. These include Gaming, Streaming, YouTube, Twitch, and Patreon.
* **How often do you earn money from these streams?** (`income_frequency`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how often you earn money from these streams, such as Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Average monthly income from these streams?** (`income_amount`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your average monthly income from these streams in a numerical value.
* **What motivates you to earn money this way?** (`income_motivation`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all that apply from the list of motivations, such as Fun, Financial security, Social status, Recognition, or Other.
* **Other income streams** (`other_income_streams`, note, required: false):
	+ Describe other income streams that are not listed above.
* **What type of gamer are you?** (`gamer_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select your type of gamer from the list of options, such as Mainstream gamer, Esports player, Content creator, Professional gamer, or Casual gamer.
* **How often do you earn money from Twitch?** (`income_from_twitch`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how often you earn money from Twitch, such as Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **How often do you earn money from YouTube?** (`income_from_youtube`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how often you earn money from YouTube, such as Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **How much money do you earn per hour from Twitch?** (`income_from_twitch_payout`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your earnings per hour from Twitch in a numerical value.
* **How much money do you earn per hour from YouTube?** (`income_from_youtube_payout`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your earnings per hour from YouTube in a numerical value.
* **How much money do you earn per hour from streaming?** (`income_from_streaming`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your earnings per hour from streaming in a numerical value.
* **How much money do you earn per hour from merchandise?** (`income_from_merchandise`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your earnings per hour from merchandise in a numerical value.
* **How much money do you earn per hour from prophets?** (`income_from_prophets`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your earnings per hour from prophets in a numerical value.
* **Other streams** (`other_streams`, note, required: false):
	+ Describe other income streams that are not listed above.
* **What are some other income streams that you have not listed above?** (`income_streams_description`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a detailed description of other income streams not listed above.
