# Game Subscription Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to cancel a game subscription. To cancel a subscription, please fill out the form below with the relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Make sure to provide a valid reason for cancellation to help us process your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Subscription Cancellation** (`game_subscription_cancellation_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main reason for filling out this form. Please provide a clear and concise description of why you want to cancel your game subscription.
* **Game Name** (`game_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the game you want to cancel your subscription to.
* **Account ID** (`account_id`, number, required/optional): Please enter your Account ID to help us identify your subscription.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, required/optional): Please provide a clear and concise reason for cancelling your subscription. This will help us process your request.
* **Game Service Name** (`game_service_name`, text, required/optional): This field is not explicitly used in the provided YAML but may be used for future reference or to provide additional information.
* **Cancellation Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date you want to cancel your subscription.
* **Cancellation Time** (`cancellation_time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time you want to cancel your subscription.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them here.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): This field is likely for logging purposes and can be left blank.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
