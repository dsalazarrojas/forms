# Gaming Modding Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your understanding of gaming modding, a community-driven process of modifying games to expand their creative possibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, navigate through the pages and answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can choose from the provided options or type a brief explanation if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gaming Modding Basics** (`introduction`, optional): This section is meant to introduce you to the basics of modding. Provide a brief explanation of what you know about modding.
* **Types of mods** (`types_of_mods`, select_one): Choose one or more of the following types of mods:

	+ Other

* **Benefits of modding** (`modding_benefits`, text): Explain the benefits you think modding brings to games and the gaming experience.
