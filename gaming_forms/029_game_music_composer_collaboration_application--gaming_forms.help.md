# Game Music Composer Collaboration Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help game music composers collaborate on music projects with game developers, music supervisors, or other industry professionals. The form collects information about the composer's game studio experience, preferred genres, portfolio URL, genre preferences, experience details, and composer portfolio.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant game studios from the list provided.
2. Choose the preferred genres for your collaboration.
3. Enter your portfolio URL, if applicable.
4. Select the genres you prefer to work on.
5. Add any additional experience details in the text field.
6. Share your composer portfolio, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Studios** (`game_studio_experience`): Select the game studios you have experience working with or would like to collaborate with.
* **Genres** (`preferred_genres`): Choose the genres you are interested in working on, such as Option 4, Option 5, or Option 6.
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`): Enter the URL of your portfolio website or online presence.
* **Genre Preferences** (`genre_preferences`): Select the genres you have experience in or are passionate about working with.
* **Experience Details** (`experience_details`): Add any additional details about your experience as a music composer.
* **Composer Portfolio** (`composer_portfolio`): Share your portfolio by uploading a link or writing a short description.

## Tips
- Make sure to be specific and honest when selecting the game studios you have experience with.
- Choose genres that accurately reflect your skills and interests.
- Include a link to your portfolio website for better visibility.
- Be concise and clear when describing your experience details.
- Tailor your composer portfolio to showcase your best work and skills.
