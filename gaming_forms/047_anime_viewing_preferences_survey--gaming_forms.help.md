# Anime Viewing Preferences Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Anime Viewing Preferences Survey" form is designed to gather information about your anime viewing habits, preferences, and behaviors. Your responses will help us better understand the anime community and provide valuable insights to enhance your experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. If a question does not apply to you, simply select "None" or the option that best suits your situation.
3. For select-one questions, choose only one option that best represents your preference.
4. For select-multiple questions, choose all that apply.
5. For text questions, provide detailed and clear answers.
