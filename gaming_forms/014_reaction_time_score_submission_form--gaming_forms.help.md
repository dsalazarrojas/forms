# Reaction Time Score Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from players on their reaction time scores in a game. By filling out this form, players can help game developers improve the game's difficulty and timing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your reaction time score for level 1 in the "Reaction Time Score 1" field.
3. Enter your actual reaction time for level 1 in the "Reaction Time 1" field.
4. Check the "Submit Status" field and select "Yes" if your form is complete and accurate.
5. Enter your submission time in the "Submission Time" field.
6. If you have any further comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Check the "Submit Confirmation" field and select "Yes" to confirm your submission.
8. Enter your participant email address.
9. If you have any further comments, enter them in the "Further Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears in your account.
* **Reaction Time Score 1 (Reaction Time Score 1)** (`reaction_time_score_1`, `number`, required): Enter the score you achieved in the first level.
* **Reaction Time 1 (Reaction Time 1)** (`reaction_time_1`, `number`, required): Enter the actual reaction time it took you to complete the first level.
* **Reaction Time 2 (Reaction Time 2)** (`reaction_time_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the actual reaction time it took you to complete the second level, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Submit Status (Submit Status)** (`submit_status`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if your form is complete and accurate, "No" otherwise.
* **Submission Time (Submission Time)** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time and date you are submitting this form.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments you have about your experience.
* **Submit Confirmation (Submit Confirmation)** (`confirm_submission`, `radio`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that your submission is accurate and complete.
* **Participant Email (Participant Email)** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address associated with your game account.
* **Further Comments (Further Comments)** (`further_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any further comments you have about your experience.
