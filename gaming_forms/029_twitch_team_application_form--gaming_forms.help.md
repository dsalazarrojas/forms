# Twitch Team Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Twitch Team Application Form is a tool for streamers to apply to join the Twitch Team, a community of content creators on the Twitch platform. This form is designed to help the team get to know you better and understand why you would be a good fit for the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your profile information, including your streamer name, Discord username, Twitch username, and email.
2. Select your stream type (e.g. Gaming, IRL, Art, Music, Talk, or Other).
3. Provide a brief bio that describes yourself and your interests.
4. Answer the question "Why us?" and explain why you are interested in joining the Twitch Team.
5. Share your experience and any relevant skills or qualifications you have.
6. List your social media presence (e.g. YouTube, Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, or Other).
7. Share any additional information about your streams, such as your stream schedule and any notable achievements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Streamer Name** (`streamer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your streamer name, which will be displayed on your profile.
* **Discord Username** (`discord_username`, text, required/optional): Enter your Discord username, which will help us get in touch with you.
* **Twitch Username** (`twitch_username`, text, required/optional): Enter your Twitch username, which is the username you use to log in to the platform.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, which we will use to contact you in case of emergency.
* **Stream Type** (`stream_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of streams you focus on (e.g. Gaming, IRL, Art, Music, Talk, or Other).
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, required/optional): Write a brief description of yourself and your interests.
* **Why Us** (`why_us`, note, required/optional): Explain why you are interested in joining the Twitch Team and what you hope to achieve by being part of the team.
* **Experience** (`experience`, note, required/optional): Share your streaming experience and any relevant skills or qualifications you have.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, note, required/optional): List your social media presence (e.g. YouTube, Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, or Other).
* **Twitch Streams** (`twitch_streams`, date, required/optional): Share any information about your streams, such as your schedule and any notable achievements.
* **Discord Server** (`discord_server`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any Discord servers you are active on (e.g. YouTube, Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, or Other).
* **Discord Tag** (`discord_tag`, text, required/optional): Enter your Discord tag, which will help us get in touch with you.
* **Twitter Handle** (`twitter_handle`, text, required/optional): Enter your Twitter handle, which is the username you use to log in to Twitter.
* **Twitch Channel** (`twitch_channel`, text, required/optional): Enter your Twitch channel, which is the username you use to log in to Twitch.
* **Discord Link** (`discord_link`, text, required/optional): Enter your Discord link, which will help us get in touch with you.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required/optional): Enter your website URL, which will help us get to know you better.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers, but keep them concise and clear.
* Use proper grammar and spelling to make your application more professional.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
