# karaoke_contest_judgign - Help Guide
## Purpose
The karaoke contest judging form is a tool used to collect scores and feedback from multiple judges on a set of karaoke performances. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that each contestant is evaluated fairly and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the contestant.
2. Enter the last name of the contestant.
3. Enter your score for the song (1-5).
4. Enter the title of the song being judged.
5. Enter the name of the song's artist.
6. Select the genre of the song (Rock, Pop, or Hip-Hop).
7. Select the length of the song (1-2 minutes, 3-4 minutes, or 5-6 minutes).
8. Select your score for the song (1-5).
9. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the performance.
10. Click submit to submit your evaluation.
