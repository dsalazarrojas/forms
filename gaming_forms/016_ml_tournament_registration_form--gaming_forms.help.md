# ML_Tournament_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ML_Tournament_Registration_Form is used to register a new tournament. This form collects information about the team participating in the tournament, including team details and tournament settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the team leader's name in the "Team Leader Name" field.
3. Enter the team leader's email address in the "Team Leader Email" field.
4. Select the type of tournament you are registering in the "Tournament Type" field.
5. Enter the date of the tournament in the "Tournament Date" field.
6. Enter the time of the tournament in the "Tournament Time" field.
7. Enter the location of the tournament in the "Tournament Location" field.
8. Enter the number of players participating in the tournament in the "Number of Players" field.
9. Enter the tournament fee in the "Tournament Fee" field.
10. Add any additional information about the tournament in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the team participating in the tournament.
* **Team Leader Name (text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the team leader.
* **Team Leader Email (email, required: false)**: Enter the email address of the team leader.
* **Tournament Type (select_one, required: false)**: Select the type of tournament you are registering (Team or Individual).
* **Tournament Date (date, required: false)**: Enter the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Time (time, required: false)**: Enter the time of the tournament.
* **Tournament Location (text, required: false)**: Enter the location of the tournament.
* **Number of Players (number, required: false)**: Enter the number of players participating in the tournament.
* **Tournament Fee (number, required: false)**: Enter the tournament fee.
* **Additional Information (text, required: false)**: Add any additional information about the tournament.
