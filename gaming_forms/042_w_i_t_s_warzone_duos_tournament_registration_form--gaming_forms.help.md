# wits_warzones_tournament_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wits_warzones_tournament_registration_form is a registration form for players participating in the "Wits Warzones" tournament. 

## How To Complete This Form
### 1. Gather all necessary information before proceeding to the form. This includes your player name, team name (if applicable), player email, and player phone number.
### 2. Fill in the required fields (player_name and player_email) and any other fields you wish to complete.
### 3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **player_name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be recognized in the tournament.
* **player_email** (`player_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted about the tournament.
* **team_name** (text, optional): If you are registering as part of a team, enter the name of your team here.
* **player_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication from the tournament organizers.

## Tips
* Use a valid email address to ensure that you receive important communications from the tournament organizers.
* If you are representing a team, please ensure that the team name accurately reflects the team's identity.
* If you experience any issues during submission, contact the tournament support team for assistance.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting to avoid delays or cancellations.
