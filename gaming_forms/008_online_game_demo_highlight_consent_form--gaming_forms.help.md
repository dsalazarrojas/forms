# online_game_demo_highlight_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information from gamers for online game demo purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Gamer Details section (id: 1) with your relevant information, such as your name, age, and other relevant details.
2. Indicate your preference for highlight uploads (id: 2) by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the dropdown menu.
3. Select your gamer permissions (id: 3) by filling out the text field, providing details on what permissions you want us to have.
4. Enter your Game ID (id: 4) if you have it, or leave it blank.
5. Fill out the Game Title (id: 5), a short description of your game.
6. If you have a Player Clip, enter it in the Player Clip field (id: 6).
7. Enter the URL of your game (id: 7).
8. Select the platform on which your game is hosted (id: 8) from the dropdown menu.
9. Select the type of upload you're making (id: 9) from the dropdown menu.
10. If you're making a file upload, provide the file in the Upload File field (id: 10).
11. Provide the URL of your uploaded file (id: 11), if applicable.
12. Fill out the Gamer Name (id: 12), a short description of yourself.
13. Enter your Gamer Email (id: 13), a valid email address.
14. Enter your Gamer Phone (id: 14), a valid phone number.
15. Fill out the Game Description (id: 15), a short description of your game.
16. Enter the Game Version (id: 16), a specific version of your game.
17. Enter any Game Tags (id: 17) for your game, separated by commas.
18. Enter the date and time of your upload (id: 18 and id: 19), using the date and time formats provided.
19. Select the upload status (id: 20) as per the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gamer Details (id: 1)**: This section asks for general information about the gamer participating in the online game demo. This includes details like name, age, etc.
* **Highlight Uploads (id: 2)**: This field determines whether to highlight or not to upload. Select 'Yes' to agree, or 'No' to disagree.
* **Gamer Permissions (id: 3)**: This field is for you to specify your permissions, like what access level you want us to have.
* **Game ID (id: 4)**: Enter your game ID if you have it. It's not required to be filled out.
* **Game Title (id: 5)**: This field is for you to describe your game briefly.
* **Player Clip (id: 6)**: If you have a clip, enter it here.
* **Game URL (id: 7)**: Enter the URL of your game for easy access.
* **Game Platform (id: 8)**: Select the platform on which your game is hosted.
* **Upload Type (id: 9)**: Choose the type of file you are uploading.
* **Upload File (id: 10)**: Enter the file you are uploading, if applicable.
* **Upload URL (id: 11)**: Enter the URL of the uploaded file.
* **Gamer Name (id: 12)**: Enter your name.
* **Gamer Email (id: 13)**: Enter your email address.
* **Gamer Phone (id: 14)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Game Description (id: 15)**: Enter a short description of your game.
* **Game Version (id: 16)**: Enter the version of your game.
* **Game Tags (id: 17)**: Enter any tags for your game.
* **Upload Date (id: 18)**: Enter the date of your upload in the given format.
* **Upload Time (id: 19)**: Enter the time of your upload in the given format.
* **Upload Status (id: 20)**: Select the status of your upload from the options provided.
