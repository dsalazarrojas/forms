# e_sports_event_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect complaints or reports related to e-sports events. It is a tool to help us understand and address your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the issue you are reporting.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the event.
3. If applicable, provide details about the players or staff involved.
4. Describe any witness statements or other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide a detailed description of the event. Be as specific as possible to help us understand the issue.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the issue that occurred during the event. This will help us understand the nature of your complaint.
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, required/optional): If applicable, provide any details about the players involved in the event, such as their names or roles.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, required/optional): If you have any information from a witness, please describe their account of what happened.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes your complaint from the following options: Event Organizer, Event Staff, or Other.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required/optional): Please indicate who is reporting this complaint (yourself, another person, or a team).
* **Reported At** (`reported_at`, date, required/optional): Indicate when the event occurred or when you became aware of the issue.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): If you want to be contacted for further information, please provide your contact details.
* **Event Details 1-10** (`event_details_1-10`, text, required/optional): These fields are for you to provide any additional details about the event.
* **Other Details 1-6** (`other_details_1-6`, text, required/optional): These fields are for you to provide any other information relevant to your complaint.

## Tips
- Be honest and clear in your description.
- If you have any supporting evidence or documentation, please attach it to your report.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
