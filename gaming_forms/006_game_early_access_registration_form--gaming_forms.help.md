# Game Early Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from game developers or owners who want to participate in our Game Early Access program. The program allows them to register their game on our platform and connect with a community of players, developers, and industry professionals who can help them gain early access and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your game's name in the "Game Name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the platform where your game is available or will be available in the "Platform" field.
4. If you have a Discord server for your game, enter the link in the "Discord Link" field.
5. If you have a Twitter account, enter the link in the "Twitter Link" field.
6. If you have a Twitch account, enter the link in the "Twitch Link" field.
7. If you have a GitHub repository for your game, enter the link in the "GitHub Link" field.
8. If you want to connect your Discord account with your game, enter the number of your Discord account in the "Discord" field.
9. If you want to connect your Twitch account with your game, enter your Twitch username in the "Twitch Username" field.
10. If you want to connect your GitHub account with your game, enter your GitHub username in the "GitHub Username" field.
11. If you have a Discord server for your game, enter the link in the "Discord Server" field.
12. If you have a GitHub repository for your game, enter the link in the "GitHub Repo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Name** (game_name, text, required): Enter the name of your game.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your game's development or ownership.
* **Platform** (platform, select_one, required): Select where your game is available or will be available (e.g., Twitch, Discord, Other).
* **Discord Link** (discord_link, text, required): Enter the link to your game's Discord server.
* **Twitter Link** (twitter_link, text, required): Enter the link to your game's Twitter account.
* **Twitch Link** (twitch_link, text, required): Enter the link to your game's Twitch channel.
* **GitHub Link** (github_link, text, required): Enter the link to your game's GitHub repository.
* **Discord** (discord, number, required): Enter the number of your Discord account if you want to connect it with your game.
* **Twitch Username** (twitch_username, text, required): Enter your Twitch username if you want to connect it with your game.
* **GitHub Username** (github_username, text, required): Enter your GitHub username if you want to connect it with your game.
* **Discord Server** (discord_server, text, required): Enter the link to your game's Discord server.
* **GitHub Repo** (github_repo, text, required): Enter the link to your game's GitHub repository.
