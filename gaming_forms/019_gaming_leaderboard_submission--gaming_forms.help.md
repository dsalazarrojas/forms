# Gaming Leaderboard Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect user-submitted information for the Gaming Leaderboard. This includes user details, leaderboard details, scores, submission date and time, and timezone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that your submission date and time are correct.
3. Choose your timezone from the available options.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your user information as desired.
- **Leaderboard Info** (`leaderboard_info`, text, required: false): Enter the relevant leaderboard information as desired.
- **Scores Entry** (`scores_entry`, text, required: false): Enter your scores as desired.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your submission in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your submission in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
- **Submission Timezone** (`submission_timezone`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your timezone from the available options to ensure correct display of your submission time.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Check your submission date and time for correctness before submitting.
* Choose the correct timezone to display your submission time correctly.
* Do not submit duplicate submissions, as this will not be accepted.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
