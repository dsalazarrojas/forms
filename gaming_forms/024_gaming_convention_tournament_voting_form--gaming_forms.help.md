# Gaming Convention Tournament Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from attendees and participants on the outcomes of the gaming convention's tournaments. The purpose of this form is to help organizers understand which tournaments were the most popular, what worked well, and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Tournament name` field with the name of the tournament you attended or participated in.
2. If you want to provide feedback on the type of game played, select the corresponding game type from the dropdown menu. You can also use the "Game type other" field to provide a custom type if the game is not listed.
3. Indicate the outcome of the match you participated in by selecting the correct match outcome (First, Second, Third, or Other).
4. Enter the name of the competitor you voted for in the "Competitor name" field.
5. Provide a brief note on why you voted for that competitor in the "Vote" field.
6. If you want to share your email address or phone number with us, you can do so in the respective fields. This is optional and only used to contact you for further feedback or to follow up on your vote.
7. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can share them in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter the time of the match result in the "Match result" field. This should be in the format of a 24-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
9. If the game type is not listed, you can provide the name of the game in the "Game name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament name**: Provide the name of the tournament you attended or participated in.
* **Game type**: Select the type of game that was played in the tournament.
* **Match outcome**: Indicate the outcome of the match you participated in by selecting the correct option.
* **Competitor name**: Enter the name of the competitor you voted for.
* **Vote**: Provide a brief note on why you voted for that competitor.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further feedback or follow up.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback or follow up.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the tournament.
* **Game name**: Provide the name of the game if it's not listed in the game type dropdown menu.
* **Match result**: Enter the time of the match result in the format of a 24-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Game type other**: If the game type is not listed, provide the name of the game in this field.
