# mobile_game_daily_check_in_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mobile_game_daily_check_in_tracker form is designed to help you track your daily gaming habits for your mobile game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Name**: (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the game you are playing.
* **Game Type**: (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of game you are playing from the dropdown menu.
* **Platform**: (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the platform on which you are playing the game from the dropdown menu.
* **Start Date**: (`4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you started playing the game.
* **Play Time**: (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total time you spent playing the game.
* **Streak**: (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current streak of consecutive days played.
* **Progress**: (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current progress in the game.
* **Played Today**: (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notable events or thoughts from the day's playtime.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to get the most out of this tracking system.
- You can track multiple games by completing this form multiple times.
- If you're having trouble understanding a field, refer to the above explanation for further clarification.
