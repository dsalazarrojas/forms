# Gameplay Recording Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gameplay Recording Upload Form is designed to collect metadata for gameplay recordings, allowing for easy organization and analysis. This form is essential for our users to categorize and share their gameplay recordings effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Upload" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the video link where your gameplay recording is hosted. This will enable us to access the recording for further analysis.
3. Provide a title for your recording that accurately reflects its content.
4. Enter the date of when you made the recording. This helps us keep track of the recording's history and context.
5. Enter the time of the recording, if applicable. This adds more detail to the metadata for further analysis.
6. Upload a file that contains your gameplay recording. Please ensure the file is in a supported format.
7. Provide a brief description of your recording, including any relevant information that may not be immediately apparent from the title or video link.
8. Select the category that best fits your recording from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Link**: Enter the publicly accessible link to your gameplay recording video. This will allow us to access the recording and its contents.
* **Video Title**: A descriptive title for your recording that should clearly convey its nature and any relevant details.
* **Recording Date**: The date when you made the recording. This helps keep a record of when the recording was made, and aids in organization and analysis.
* **Recording Time**: If applicable, enter the time of when you made the recording. This adds an extra layer of detail to the metadata.
* **Upload File**: Upload a file containing your gameplay recording. Please ensure the file is in a supported format.
* **Description**: A brief description of your recording, providing context and details that may not be immediately apparent from the title or video link.
* **Category**: Select the category that best fits your recording from the options provided. This helps us to categorize and organize the recordings effectively.

## Tips
* Ensure you are uploading a relevant recording for the chosen category.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing the title and description to aid in analysis and organization.
* Use a supported file format for the upload.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
