## Discord Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for individuals to submit their application for a staff position within a Discord server. This form is used to gather necessary information to evaluate their suitability for the role and assess their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill out the first name field**: Enter your first name.
2. **Enter your email address**: Enter a valid email address associated with your Discord account.
3. **Enter your Discord username**: Enter your Discord username.
4. **Enter the Application Form URL (if applicable)**: If you are applying for a staff position that has an associated application form, enter the URL of the form.
5. **Describe the role you want to play**: Provide a brief description of the role you are applying for.
6. **Specify your availability**: Select the times of day you are available to work.
7. **Describe your experience**: Provide a brief description of your experience related to the role you are applying for.
8. **List your skills**: List any relevant skills you possess that align with the role.
9. **Explain why you want to join this community**: Provide a brief explanation of why you want to join this community as a staff member.
10. **Specify your timezone**: Enter your timezone offset (e.g., UTC+5).
11. **Choose a Discord rank**: Select which Discord rank you think you would be best suited for (e.g., Moderator, Community Manager, etc.).
12. **Select your Discord position**: Select any additional roles you are applying for within the server (e.g., Server Owner, Server Co-Owner, etc.).
