# Gaming Hardware Licensing Agreement Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a gaming hardware licensing agreement contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages to understand what information is required and what each field is used for.
2. Go through each page and fill in the relevant fields according to the explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: (No fields on this page)

### Page 2: User Details
* **Name** (`form_page2`, `text`, not required): This field is for your name as the user.
* **User Details** (`form_page2`, `text`, not required): In this field, provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to the gaming hardware licensing agreement.

### Page 3: Device Details
* **Device Details** (`form_page3`, `text`, not required): Describe the gaming hardware you are licensing, including any details about its condition, make, and model.

### Page 4: Event Details
* **Event Details** (`form_page4`, `text`, not required): If the gaming hardware is part of an event or tournament, describe the event in this field, including the name of the event, date, and location.

### Page 5: License Agreement
* **License Agreement** (`form_page5`, `text`, not required): Review and accept the terms of the gaming hardware licensing agreement. Please be sure to read and understand the agreement before proceeding.

### Page 6: Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`form_page6`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional information or comments about the gaming hardware or the licensing agreement, please include them here.

### Page 7: Submit
* **Submit** (`form_page7`, `note`, not required): This is the final page to review and submit the form.

### Page 8: Form Complete
* **Form Complete** (`form_page8`, `note`, not required): You have successfully submitted the form. Please review your responses for accuracy before proceeding.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. If you need assistance or clarification about any field, do not hesitate to contact the form creator.
