# Survivor Series Predictions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user's predictions and picks for a series of matches in a sports-related competition or tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select your match predictions for each match from the available options (Predictions 1, 2, 3).
2.  Choose your team picks for each match from the available teams (Team A, Team B).
3.  Decide on your game picks for each match (Yes, No).
4.  Select your fan picks for each match from the available teams (Team A, Team B, Team C, Team D).
5.  Enter the match ID and game ID for each match, if required.
6.  Input the date and time of each match.
7.  Provide any additional notes about your predictions or picks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match Predictions**: Select your prediction for this match from the available options (Predictions 1, 2, 3).
* **Team Picks**: Choose your team picks for this match from the available teams (Team A, Team B).
* **Game Picks**: Decide on your game pick for this match (Yes, No).
* **Fan Picks**: Select your fan pick for this match from the available teams (Team A, Team B, Team C, Team D).
* **Match ID**: Enter the match ID for this match, if required.
* **Game ID**: Enter the game ID for this match, if required.
* **Date Time**: Input the date and time of this match.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes about your predictions or picks.
