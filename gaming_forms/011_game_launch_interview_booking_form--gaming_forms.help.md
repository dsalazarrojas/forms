# Game Launch Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and confirm a game launch interview with a potential guest. It gathers necessary information from the guest to ensure a smooth and productive interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your role from the options provided. If you're a gamer, select "Yes". If you're a media outlet, select "No".
2. Enter the required information for "Media Inquiries", "Contact Details", "Interview Preferences", "Date of Interview", and "Interview Location". These fields are required, so make sure to fill them out carefully.
3. Answer the "Are you an Influencer?" and "Are you a Media?" questions. Select "Yes" if you're an influencer or a media outlet, and "No" otherwise.
4. If you have any special requests for the interview, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
5. Confirm that you're ready for the interview by selecting "Yes" for "Confirm Interview".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role**: Choose your role from the options provided. This will help us determine how to approach the interview.
	+ Options: "Yes" (Gamer), "No" (Media)
* **Media Inquiries**: Enter the number of media inquiries you'd like to have for the interview. This will help us prepare for the number of questions and time allocated.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number. This will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Interview Preferences**: Select one or more of the options provided to help us tailor the interview to your needs.
	+ Options: "Yes" (Gamer/Influencer), "No" (Other)
* **Date of Interview**: Enter the date you'd like to have the interview. Please use the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Interview Location**: Enter the location where you'd like to have the interview.
* **Are you an Influencer?**: Select "Yes" if you're an influencer and "No" otherwise.
* **Are you a Media?**: Select "Yes" if you're a media outlet and "No" otherwise.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests for the interview, such as equipment needs or specific questions, enter them here.
* **Confirm Interview**: Confirm that you're ready for the interview by selecting "Yes". This will help us finalize the arrangements.
