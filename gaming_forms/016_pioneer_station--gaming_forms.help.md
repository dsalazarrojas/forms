# Pioneer Station - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from gamers about their gaming experience and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Gamer Name in the "Gamer Name" field.
2. Select your level of gaming experience from the "Gamer Level" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the number of hours you have played games in the "Gamer Hours Played" field.
4. If you have any additional information about your gaming experience, you can add it in the "Gamer Notes" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Optionally, select a tool that you would like to be assigned to in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gamer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your profile.
* **Gamer Level**: Select your level of gaming experience:
	+ Beginner: You are new to gaming or have limited experience.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience with games and can navigate basic mechanics.
	+ Advanced: You are an experienced gamer with a deep understanding of game mechanics and strategies.
* **Gamer Hours Played**: Enter the total number of hours you have spent playing games.
* **Gamer Notes**: Add any additional information about your gaming experience or preferences.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optionally, select a tool that you would like to be assigned to.
