# Game Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from players about their experience with a game. This form will help game developers understand what players like or dislike about the game, allowing them to make improvements and updates to enhance the gaming experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the questions and rating scales provided and think about your experience with the game.
* Provide your player name and any other required information, if applicable.
* Answer each feedback question in as much detail as possible.
* Select the corresponding rating for your feedback, if applicable.
* Submit the form to provide your feedback to the game developers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`): This is the title of the form, which is for reference purposes only.
* **Game Feedback** (`game_feedback`): Provide a brief description of your experience with the game, including what you liked or disliked.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`): Enter your player name or username to associate your feedback with your account.
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`): Answer this question about your experience with the game, including any issues or concerns you may have had.
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`): Provide further details about your experience with the game.
* **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question_3`): Share any additional feedback about your experience with the game.
* **Feedback Question 4** (`feedback_question_4`): Provide any final thoughts or suggestions about the game.
* **Feedback Question 5** (`feedback_question_5`): Share any other feedback or comments about the game.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): Click this button to submit your feedback form to the game developers.
* **Player ID** (`player_id`): Enter your player ID if you have one, to associate your feedback with your account.
* **Player IP** (`player_ip`): Enter your player IP address, which will be used for tracking purposes only.
* **Game Title** (`game_title`): Select the correct game title from the dropdown menu to associate your feedback with the correct game.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall satisfaction with the game.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`): Select the type of feedback you are providing, such as bug reports or suggestions.
* **Platform** (`platform`): Select 'Yes' if you played the game on a specific platform, such as PC or console.
* **Game Version** (`game_version`): Select 'Yes' if you played the latest version of the game.
* **Game Mode** (`game_mode`): Select 'Yes' if you played the game in a specific mode, such as multiplayer or single-player.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your feedback, as it will help game developers improve the game.
* If you have any attachments or screenshots to support your feedback, you can attach them to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the game developers through their support channel.
