# Mobile Game Team Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to provide a clear understanding of how to fill in the Mobile Game Team Application Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Provide your User Information.
2. Specify your Gaming Experience, which is a dropdown menu with three options: Casual, Intermediate, and Advanced.
3. Choose your Skill Level, which is also a dropdown menu with three options: Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced.
4. List your available days and times in the Availability field.
5. Select the days you are available to work in the Availability Days field.
6. List the names of your team members in the Team Members field.
7. Specify the time of day you are available in the Availability Hours field.
8. Choose your Team Lead status in the Team Lead field.
9. Select the game you are interested in playing in the Game field.
10. Specify your Game Experience in the Game Experience field, which is another dropdown menu with three options: Casual, Intermediate, and Advanced.
11. Enter your availability time in the Availability Time field, using 24-hour format.
12. Choose your timezone from the dropdown list in the Timezone field.
13. Specify how long you are available to work in the Availability Duration field, using 24-hour format.
14. Select how often you are available to work in the Availability Frequency field.
15. Finally, provide a brief description of yourself in the Bio field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Gaming Experience** (Gaming Experience, select_one, required): Choose your level of gaming experience.
* **Skill Level** (Skill Level, select_multiple, optional): Choose the level of your skills.
* **Availability** (Availability, text, optional): List your available days and times.
* **Availability Days** (Availability Days, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, text, optional): List the names of your team members.
* **Availability Hours** (Availability Hours, time, optional): Specify the time of day you are available.
* **Team Lead** (Team Lead, text, optional): Choose your Team Lead status.
* **Game** (Game, select_one, optional): Choose the game you are interested in playing.
* **Game Experience** (Game Experience, select_multiple, optional): Choose your level of game experience.
* **Availability Time** (Availability Time, time, optional): Enter your availability time in 24-hour format.
* **Timezone** (Timezone, select_one, optional): Choose your timezone.
* **Availability Duration** (Availability Duration, number, optional): Specify how long you are available to work in 24-hour format.
* **Availability Frequency** (Availability Frequency, number, optional): Select how often you are available to work in 24-hour format.
* **Bio** (Bio, text, optional): Provide a brief description of yourself.
