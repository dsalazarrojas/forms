# Highscore Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Highscore Submission Form is a tool used by players to submit their highscores for games. This form ensures that highscores are properly reviewed and verified by moderators before they are displayed on leaderboards and other game-related resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" on the form. These fields are:
	* Submission review
	* Highscore submission form comments
	* Highscore submission form email
	* Highscore submission form phone
	* Highscore submission form category
	* Highscore submission form game title
	* Highscore submission form leaderboard name
	* Highscore submission form leaderboard link
	* Highscore submission form game url
	* Highscore submission form score
	* Highscore submission form score date
2. Ensure you provide a valid email address and phone number for contact.
3. Choose the correct category for your submission.
4. Select "Reviewed" as the submission review if your highscore has been verified by a moderator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player name**: Enter your player name as it appears on your in-game profile or account.
* **Score**: Enter the score you achieved in the game.
* **Proof link**: Provide a link to proof of your highscore (e.g. a screenshot or video).
* **Highscore submission form 2/3**: Enter any additional information or comments about your submission.
* **Submission review**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Not reviewed: Your highscore has not been reviewed by a moderator.
	+ Reviewed: Your highscore has been reviewed and verified by a moderator.
	+ In progress: Your highscore is currently being reviewed by a moderator.
* **Highscore submission form comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Highscore submission form email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for verification or further questions.
* **Highscore submission form phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted for verification or further questions.
* **Highscore submission form category**: Select the category that your highscore belongs to.
* **Highscore submission form game title**: Enter the title of the game you played.
* **Highscore submission form leaderboard name**: Enter the name of the leaderboard you achieved this score on.
* **Highscore submission form leaderboard link**: Enter the link to the leaderboard where your score is listed.
* **Highscore submission form game url**: Enter the URL of the game you played.
* **Highscore submission form score**: Enter the score you achieved in the game.
* **Highscore submission form score date**: Enter the date you achieved this score.
