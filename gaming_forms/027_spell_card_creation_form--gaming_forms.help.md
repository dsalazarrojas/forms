# Spell Card Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spell Card Creation Form is designed to collect information about a magical card (e.g., a card from a trading card game) with various attributes. This form is intended for users to input data about a card's name, mana cost, type, power, card type, rarity, keywords, description, images, and card set.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1:** Fill out the required fields: "Card Name", "Mana Cost", "Type of Card", "Power", "Card Type", and "Rarity". These fields are marked as required and are essential for creating a comprehensive card profile.
2. **Step 2:** Enter a brief description of the card in the "Card Description" field if it's a required field for your specific card.
3. **Step 3:** Upload images of the card from different angles in the "Card Image" and "Card Image 2" fields if they are not optional fields.
4. **Step 4:** Enter the card's set in the "Card Set" field if it's a required field.
5. **Step 5:** Review and verify that all fields are accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Card Name**: Enter the name of the card (e.g., "Fireball").
* **Mana Cost**: Enter the mana cost of the card (e.g., 1).
* **Type of Card**: Select the type of card (e.g., Common, Uncommon, Rare, or Mythic).
* **Power**: Enter the power of the card (e.g., 3).
* **Card Type**: Select the type of card (e.g., Creature, Instant, Enchantment, Planeswalker, Sorcery, or Artifact).
* **Card Rarity**: Select the rarity of the card (e.g., Common, Uncommon, Rare, or Mythic).
* **Keywords**: Enter any keywords related to the card (e.g., "fireball", "red", "attack").
* **Card Description**: Enter a brief description of the card.
* **Card Image**: Upload an image of the card from a specific angle.
* **Card Image 2**: Upload an image of the card from another angle.
* **Card Set**: Enter the card set the card belongs to.
