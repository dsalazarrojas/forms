# Video Game Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users' gaming habits and preferences. It includes questions about game preferences, playing frequency, game rating, and other aspects of gaming behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred game genre from the list provided.
2. Choose your favorite game from the list.
3. Indicate how often you play games.
4. Rate your game rating from the options provided.
5. Answer questions about your game purchasing history and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your preferred game genre?** (`game_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about your preferred game genre, such as Action, Adventure, Sports, etc.
* **What is your favorite game?** (`favorite_game`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about the game that you enjoy playing the most.
* **How often do you play games?** (`game_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): This question measures how often you play games, with options ranging from "Yes" to "No".
* **How long do you play games for?** (`game_duration`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about the duration of your gaming sessions.
* **What is your game rating?** (`game_rating`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks you to rate your game from "Good" to "Fair".
* **Have you ever purchased a game?** (`game_purchase_history`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about your game purchasing history.
* **What games have you purchased?** (`game_purchased_games`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the games you have purchased.
* **How much did you spend on your most recent game?** (`game_purchased_price`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about the amount you spent on your most recent game.
* **How often do you purchase new games?** (`game_purchased_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): This question measures how often you purchase new games.
* **On what platform do you purchase games?** (`game_purchased_platform`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the platform you use to purchase games.
* **Do you ask for game recommendations?** (`game_purchased_recommendations`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about whether you ask for game recommendations.
* **From whom do you usually get game recommendations?** (`game_purchased_recommended_friends`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the people from whom you get game recommendations.
* **On what platform do you usually get game recommendations?** (`game_purchased_recommended_platform`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the platform from which you get recommendations.
* **What time of day do you usually play games?** (`game_played_on`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about the time of day when you usually play games.
* **How long do you usually play games?** (`game_played_duration`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about the duration of your gaming sessions.
* **How often do you play games?** (`game_played_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): This question measures how often you play games.
* **How would you rate this game?** (`game_rating_scale`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about your rating of a game.
* **What game genres interest you?** (`game_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the game genres that interest you.
* **How often do new game releases interest you?** (`game_interest_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): This question measures how often new game releases interest you.
* **With whom do you usually play games?** (`game_played_with`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the people with whom you play games.
* **How often do you play with friends?** (`game_played_with_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): This question measures how often you play with friends.
* **What type of game do you usually play with?** (`game_played_with_type`, select_one, required/optional): This question asks about the type of game you usually play with.
* **On what platform do you usually play with friends?** (`game_played_with_platform`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the platform you use to play with friends.
* **What else interests you besides games?** (`game_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about other interests besides games.
* **What is your age?** (`demographic`, number, required/optional): This question asks about your age.
