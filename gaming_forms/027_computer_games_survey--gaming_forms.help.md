# Computer Games Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Computer Games Survey is a form designed to collect information about your gaming habits and preferences. This data will help us improve our platform and create a better experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields**. Fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the survey.
2. **Read the hints** for each field to understand what information we're looking for.
3. **Choose your options carefully** for select-one and select-multiple fields.
4. **Estimate your answers** for fields that require numbers or quantities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gamer Tag** (`<gamer_tag>`, text, optional): If you have a public gamer tag or a username that you use in online gaming communities, please enter it here.
* **Age Group** (`<age_group>`, select_one, required): Select your age bracket from the list:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45+
* **Preferred Platform** (`<preferred_platform>`, select_one, required): Choose the type of platform you prefer:
	+ PC
	+ Console
	+ Mobile
	+ Cloud
* **Favorite Genres** (`<favorite_genres>`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Action
	+ Strategy
	+ RPG
	+ Simulation
	+ Sports
	+ Puzzle
* **Weekly Play Hours** (`<weekly_play_hours>`, number, optional): Estimate the number of hours you play games per week.
* **In Game Purchases** (`<in_game_purchases>`, select_one, required): Do you make in-game purchases?
	+ Regularly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Never
* **Satisfaction with Games** (`<satisfaction_with_games>`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with games:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Feedback on Features** (`<feedback_on_features>`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have about new features or improvements you'd like to see in our platform.
