# gaming_scholarship_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gaming scholarship program application form is designed to collect information from applicants about their gaming skills, experience, and other relevant details to determine eligibility for a scholarship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the eligibility criteria for the scholarship program to ensure that you are eligible to apply.
2. Fill out the personal details section with your name, address, and contact information.
3. In the gamer details section, choose your gaming experience, skills, and equipment from the given options.
4. For the gaming experience and achievements section, select your gaming level, achievements, and other relevant details.
5. If applicable, provide information about your club memberships, sponsorships, and other relevant details.
6. Finally, review the terms and conditions and agree to them by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal details** (Personal details, text, optional): Fill out this section with your name, address, and contact information to ensure that we can contact you if needed.
* **Gamer details** (Gamer details, text, optional): Provide an overview of your gaming experience, skills, and other relevant details to increase your chances of getting the scholarship.
* **Gaming experience** (Gaming experience, text, optional): Describe how long you have been gaming and what type of games you play.
* **Gamer skills** (Gamer skills, select_multiple, required): Select all the skills you have, such as Gaming Mouse, Gaming Keyboard, Gaming Controller, or Gaming Joystick.
* **Gaming equipment** (Gaming equipment, select_one, required): Choose the gaming equipment you use, such as Gaming Mouse, Gaming Keyboard, Gaming Controller, or Gaming Joystick.
* **Gamer level** (Gamer level, select_one, required): Select your gaming level from the given options (Casual, Experienced, or Professional).
* **Gamer clubs** (Gamer clubs, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, list the gaming clubs you are a part of.
* **Gamer achievements** (Gamer achievements, select_multiple, optional): Select your gaming achievements, such as Gamer Achievement 1, Gamer Achievement 2, or Gamer Achievement 3.
* **Gamer references** (Gamer references, select_multiple, optional): If you have any gaming references, such as coaches or mentors, provide their names and contact information.
* **Gaming systems** (Gaming systems, select_multiple, required): Select all the gaming systems you use, such as System 1, System 2, or System 3.
* **Gamer games** (Gamer games, select_multiple, required): Choose all the games you are experienced in, such as Game 1, Game 2, or Game 3.
* **Gamer scores** (Gamer scores, select_multiple, optional): Select your gaming scores, such as Score 1, Score 2, or Score 3.
* **Gamer trophies** (Gamer trophies, select_multiple, optional): Select your gaming trophies, such as Trophy 1, Trophy 2, or Trophy 3.
* **Gamer friends** (Gamer friends, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, list your gaming friends who can vouch for your skills.
* **Gamer achievements dates** (Gamer achievements dates, date, optional): If applicable, provide the dates of your gaming achievements.
* **Gamer club memberships** (Gamer club memberships, select_one, optional): If you have any gaming club memberships, choose from the given options.
* **Gamer achievements titles** (Gamer achievements titles, select_multiple, optional): Select your gaming achievements titles, such as Title 1, Title 2, or Title 3.
* **Gamer references notes** (Gamer references notes, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your gaming references, provide it in this section.
* **Gamer sponsorships** (Gamer sponsorships, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, list your gaming sponsorships.
* **Gamer scholarship funding** (Gamer scholarship funding, number, optional): If applicable, provide information about the scholarship funding you are seeking.
* **Gamer scholarship amount** (Gamer scholarship amount, number, optional): If applicable, provide the amount of scholarship you are seeking.
* **Gamer terms and conditions** (Gamer terms and conditions, select_multiple, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the scholarship program.
* **Gamer scholarship agreement** (Gamer scholarship agreement, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional information or conditions for the scholarship.
* **Gamer signature** (Gamer signature, text, optional): Sign the application to confirm your information.
* **Gamer additional comments** (Gamer additional comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, provide it in this section.
