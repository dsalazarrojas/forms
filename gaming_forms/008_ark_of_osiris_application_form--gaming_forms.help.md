# Ark Of Osiris Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from players interested in joining the Ark of Osiris team. The form is intended to gather essential details about each applicant, including their contact information, gaming experience, and team preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information section (fields 1-4), providing your game username, real name (if different from your game username), application date, and contact email address.
2. Move on to the gaming experience section (fields 5-9), detailing your total hours played in Ark of Osiris, your experience level, and your favorite class or character.
3. In the team information section (fields 10-16), select your preferred position, choose your weekly availability, and specify your time zone.
4. Finally, in the additional information section (fields 17-20), describe your communication skills, language proficiency, and how your goals align with the team's objectives. Provide any additional comments or reasons for joining the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Game Username or Player Name** (`player_name`): Enter your username used for playing Ark of Osiris.
- **Real Name** (`player_real_name`): If different from your game username, enter your real name for team roster purposes.
- **Date of Application** (`application_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
- **Discord Username** (`discord_username`): Enter your Discord username for team communication.
- **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Total Hours Played in Ark of Osiris** (`hours_played`): Estimate the total hours played in Ark of Osiris.
- **Experience Level** (`experience_level`): Choose your level of experience in Ark of Osiris: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, or Professional.
- **Favorite Class or Character** (`favorite_class`): Specify your favorite class or character in the game.
- **Primary Playstyle** (`playstyle`): Select your preferred playstyle: Aggressive, Balanced, Defensive, Support, or Solo.
- **Previous Team Gaming Experience** (`team_experience`): Describe any relevant team gaming experience.
- **Position** (`team_position`): Choose the position you are applying for: Leader, Core member, Substitute, Bench player, Support role, or Any position.
- **Weekly Availability** (`availability`): Choose your weekly availability for team gaming: 5-10 hours, 10-15 hours, 15-20 hours, 20-30 hours, 30+ hours.
- **Timezone** (`timezone`): Specify your time zone.
- **Communication Skills and Language Proficiency** (`communication_skills`): Describe your communication skills and language proficiency.
- **Team Goals Alignment** (`team_goals_alignment`): Explain how your goals align with the team's objectives.
- **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_info`): Provide any additional information or comments about why you want to join the team.
