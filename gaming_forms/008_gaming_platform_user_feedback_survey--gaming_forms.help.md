# gaming_platform_user_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback about your experience with the gaming platform. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and develop new features that you would like to see.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question with the information that best describes your experience.
2. Select the options that best fit your response for the questions with multiple-choice options.
3. Enter your thoughts and ideas in the free text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the gaming platform?** (`how_satisfied_with_gaming_platform`, select_one):
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **What types of games do you enjoy playing on the platform?** (`what_games_do_you_enjoy-playing`, select_multiple):
	+ Action
	+ Adventure
	+ Role-playing
	+ Sports
	+ Strategy
* **What improvement opportunities do you have for the platform?** (`improvement_opportunities`, note): 
	+ This is a free text field for any suggestions or ideas you may have for improving the platform. Be as specific as possible.
* **How satisfied are you with the updates on the platform?** (`how_satisfied_with_updates`, number):
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **How satisfied are you with the features on the platform?** (`how_satisfied_with_features`, number):
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **How satisfied are you with the service on the platform?** (`how_satisfied_with_service`, select_one):
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **What would you recommend for improvement?** (`what_do_you_recommend_for_improvement`, text):
	+ This is a free text field for any suggestions or ideas you may have for improving the platform. Be as specific as possible.
* **Email** (`email`, email):
	+ Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on platform improvements.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text):
	+ Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive updates on platform improvements.
* **What do you like most about the platform?** (`what_do_you_like_most`, text):
	+ This is a free text field for you to express your favorite feature or aspect of the platform.
