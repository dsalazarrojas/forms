# Connect With Our Gaming Brand - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the gaming community and help us understand their interests, preferences, and contact details. By filling out this form, you'll be helping us provide better experiences for our players, partners, and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Gamer Tag / Alias, which is your unique identifier for our community.
2. Choose your Primary Gaming Platform from the options provided.
3. Enter your valid Email Address, which we'll use to communicate with you.
4. Select the Type of Inquiry that best describes your purpose for filling out this form.
5. Provide your Social Media / Streaming Links if you'd like to connect with us on these platforms.
6. Select your Preferred Game Genres from the list provided.
7. Decide if you'd like to receive brand updates and early access news by selecting Yes, sign me up!.
8. Write your Message to Us, telling us more about how you'd like to connect with us.
9. Consent to the processing of your data for communication purposes and confirm that you are at least 13 years of age.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gamer Tag / Alias (Required)**: Enter your unique identifier for our community.
* **Your Primary Gaming Platform (Required)**: Select your main gaming platform (e.g. PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, or Mobile).
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your valid email address, which we'll use to communicate with you.
* **Type of Inquiry (Required)**: Choose the category that best describes your purpose for filling out this form.
* **Social Media / Streaming Links (Optional)**: Provide links to your social media or streaming platforms.
* **Preferred Game Genres (Optional)**: Select your favorite game genres (e.g. FPS, RPG, MOBA, etc.).
* **Would you like to receive brand updates and early access news? (Required)**: Select Yes, sign me up! to receive updates and news from us.
* **Your Message to Us (Required)**: Write a brief message about how you'd like to connect with us.
* **Privacy Consent (Required)**: Select I consent to the processing of my data for communication purposes.
* **Age Verification (Required)**: Confirm that you are at least 13 years of age.

## Tips
* Ensure that your email address is accurate and valid.
* Be honest about your preferred game genres and type of inquiry.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask us in the "Message to Us" field.
* We'll use your information to improve our services and provide better experiences for our community.
