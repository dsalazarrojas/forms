# Character Statistics Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you track your character's statistics during gaming sessions, allowing you to monitor their development and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your player name and character name in the first two fields.
2. Enter the date and session number for this session.
3. Record your character's starting and ending levels, as well as experience points gained during this session.
4. Enter the character's health points at the start and end of the session.
5. If applicable, record any additional statistics such as damage dealt, damage taken, enemies defeated, gold earned/spent, items acquired, quests completed, and notable achievements.
6. Finally, select your character's current status from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter your player name as you are known in the game.
* **Character Name** (`character_name`, text, required): Enter your character's full name.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Record the date of this gaming session.
* **Session Number** (`session_number`, number, required): Enter the session number for this tracking session.
* **Starting Level** (`character_level_start`, number, required): Enter your character's level at the start of this session.
* **Ending Level** (`character_level_end`, number, required): Enter your character's level at the end of this session.
* **Experience Gained** (`experience_gained`, number, required): Enter the experience points earned during this session.
* **Total Experience** (`total_experience`, number, required): Record your character's total experience points.
* **HP at Session Start** (`hit_points_start`, number, required): Enter your character's health points at the start of this session.
* **HP at Session End** (`hit_points_end`, number, required): Enter your character's health points at the end of this session.
* **Total Damage Dealt** (`damage_dealt`, number, optional): Record the total damage dealt during this session (if applicable).
* **Total Damage Taken** (`damage_taken`, number, optional): Record the total damage taken during this session (if applicable).
* **Enemies Defeated** (`enemies_defeated`, number, optional): Record the number of enemies defeated during this session (if applicable).
* **Gold Earned** (`gold_earned`, number, optional): Record the currency earned during this session (if applicable).
* **Gold Spent** (`gold_spent`, number, optional): Record the currency spent during this session (if applicable).
* **Items Acquired** (`items_acquired`, text, optional): Record any new equipment or items acquired during this session.
* **Quests Completed** (`quests_completed`, text, optional): Record any quests completed during this session.
* **Ability Score Improvements** (`ability_score_improvements`, text, optional): Record any ability score improvements during this session.
* **New Abilities Learned** (`new_abilities`, text, optional): Record any new abilities or spells acquired during this session.
* **Notable Achievements** (`notable_achievements`, text, optional): Record any notable moments or achievements during this session.
* **Character Status** (`character_status`, select_one, required): Select your character's current status from the provided options.
