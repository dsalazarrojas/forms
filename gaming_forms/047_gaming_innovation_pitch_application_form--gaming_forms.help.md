# Gaming Innovation Pitch Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Innovation Pitch Application Form is designed to collect information about game-related projects or innovations from applicants. This information is used to evaluate and assess the project's potential to drive innovation and progress in the gaming industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of your game-related project or innovation in the **Game Concept** field.
2. In the **Game Developer Details** field, include information about the team members, their roles, and their experience in the gaming industry.
3. If you have any supporting files such as images, videos, or documents, upload them in the **Supporting Files** field.
4. In the **Contact Details** field, provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
5. Select the relevant **Game Themes** that your project aligns with.
6. Upload any submitted files in the **Submitted Files** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Applicant Details**: Please provide a brief overview of your team's experience and qualifications in the gaming industry.
* **Game Concept**: A concise description of your game-related project or innovation.
* **Supporting Files**: Upload any relevant images, videos, or documents that support your project.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your name, email, and phone number so that we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Game Developer Details**: Include information about the team members, their roles, and their experience in the gaming industry.
* **Game Jam Details**: A brief description of any game jams or hackathons you have participated in.
* **Game Details**: A detailed description of your game, including its mechanics, genre, and target audience.
* **Game Studio Details**: Information about the game development studio, including its name, location, and experience.
* **Game Accelerator Details**: Information about any game accelerators or incubators you have participated in.
* **Game School Details**: A brief description of any game schools or universities you have attended.
* **Game Engineer Details**: Information about the team's engineering experience and expertise.
* **Game Artist Details**: Information about the team's art expertise and experience.
* **Game Designer Details**: A brief description of the team's design experience and expertise.
* **Game Programmer Details**: Information about the team's programming experience and expertise.
* **Game Marketer Details**: Information about the team's marketing experience and expertise.
* **Game Publisher Details**: Information about the team's publishing experience and expertise.
* **Game Quality**: A brief description of the game's quality and how it stands out from other games in the industry.
* **Game Tech**: A detailed description of the game's technical specifications, including any unique features or innovations.
* **Game Style**: A brief description of the game's style and genre.
* **Game Themes**: Select the relevant themes that your project aligns with.
* **Game Platforms**: A list of the platforms where the game will be released.
* **Game Tags**: A list of keywords or tags that describe the game.
* **Supporting Files**: Upload any additional information or materials that support your project.
* **Submitted Files**: Upload any files you have submitted as part of your application.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your application for evaluation.

## Tips

* Ensure that your application is complete and accurate.
* Be concise and clear in your descriptions.
* Use supporting files to showcase your project's potential.
* Respond to any further questions or clarification requests from us.
