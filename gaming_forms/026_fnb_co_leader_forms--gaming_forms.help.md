# fnb_co_leader_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a game leader's performance in a specific game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Game Name, which is the name of the game you are playing.
2. Enter the Game URL, which is the URL of the game's webpage or platform.
3. Enter your Player Name, which is your in-game name or username.
4. Enter your Player ID, which is your unique identifier in the game.
5. (Optional) Enter the Try Date, which is the date you want to track your performance.
6. Select your Co Leader Status, which is your current status as a co-leader in the game.
7. (Optional) Choose the Game Type, which is the type of game you are playing (Multiplayer or Singleplayer).
8. (Optional) Choose the Try Type, which is the type of try you are making (Single, Multi, or Solo Try).
9. Enter your Form Submission ID, which is your unique identifier for this submission.
10. (Optional) Choose the Game Server, which is the server you are playing on (Server 1, Server 2, or Server 3).
11. Enter the Server IP, which is the IP address of the server you are playing on.
12. (Optional) Enter the Co Leader ID, which is your co-leader's unique identifier.
13. (Optional) Enter the Co Leader Name, which is your co-leader's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Name (1)** (`game_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the game you are playing.
* **Game URL (2)** (`game_url`, text, **Optional**): Enter the URL of the game's webpage or platform.
* **Player Name (3)** (`player_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your in-game name or username.
* **Player ID (4)** (`player_id`, number, **Required**): Enter your unique identifier in the game.
* **Try Date (5)** (`try_date`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date you want to track your performance.
* **Co Leader Status (6)** (`co_leader_status`, select_one, **Required**): Select your current status as a co-leader in the game.
* **Game Type (7)** (`game_type`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose the type of game you are playing (Multiplayer or Singleplayer).
* **Try Type (8)** (`try_type`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose the type of try you are making (Single, Multi, or Solo Try).
* **Form Submission ID (9)** (`form_submission_id`, number, **Required**): Enter your unique identifier for this submission.
* **Game Server (10)** (`game_server`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose the server you are playing on (Server 1, Server 2, or Server 3).
* **Server IP (11)** (`server_ip`, text, **Required**): Enter the IP address of the server you are playing on.
* **Co Leader ID (12)** (`co_leader_id`, number, **Optional**): Enter your co-leader's unique identifier.
* **Co Leader Name (13)** (`co_leader_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter your co-leader's name.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" can be filled out if you have additional information to provide, but are not required for the form submission.
