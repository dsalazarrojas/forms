# Online Gaming Platform Overview - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about their online gaming platform experience. The purpose of this form is to gather information on how users use the platform, their favorite game genre, how often they play games, and their overall satisfaction with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions in each section, being as detailed as possible.
2. Select the correct options from the drop-down menus.
3. Enter your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
4. Add any additional feedback you may have in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Game Platform Usage
* **Game Platform Usage** (`Game Platform Usage`, `note`, required/optional): This field is a text area where you can describe how you use the online gaming platform. Please provide as much detail as possible about your gaming habits, such as how often you log in, how many hours you play per session, and any other relevant information.

### Favorite Game Genre
* **Favorite Game Genre** (`favorite_game_genre`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to select your favorite game genre from the given options. Please choose the genre that you enjoy the most.

### How Often Do You Play Games?
* **How Often Do You Play Games?** (`game_playtime`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field has options for how often you play games on the platform. Please select all that apply to your gaming habits.

### How Satisfied Are You With The Platform?
* **How Satisfied Are You With The Platform?** (`platform_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to select how satisfied you are with the platform. Please choose all that apply to your experience.

### Rating (Rating, number, required/optional)
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required/optional): This field is where you can rate your overall experience with the platform on a scale.

### Additional Feedback
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a text area where you can add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the platform.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached in case we need to contact you.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* You can add any additional feedback you may have in the last field.
* If you have any support or technical issues, please contact us at [support email].
