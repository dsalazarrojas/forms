# esports_content_creator_partnership_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The esports_content_creator_partnership_application form is designed for esports content creators to apply for partnership opportunities with our company. We are looking for talented creators to collaborate with us on various projects, such as hosting events, creating content, or promoting our brand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed our guidelines and requirements for partnership applications.
2. Fill out the form with your information and answers.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, required): Please enter your team's name as it appears on your team's website, social media, or other public platforms.
* **Team Logo** (`team_logo`, optional): If you have a logo, please upload it here. This will help us recognize your team's identity.
* **Creator Details** (`creator_details`, required): Provide a brief description of your role and experience as a content creator.
* **Audience Size** (`audience_size`, required): Please enter the estimated number of viewers or followers you have on your social media channels or streaming platforms.
* **Collaboration Goals** (`collaboration_goals`, required): Select the type of collaboration you are looking for (Partnership, Collaboration, Sponsorship, Brand Ambassadorship, or Event Hosting).
* **Partnership Goals Details** (`partnership_goals_details`, optional): Provide a brief explanation of your goals and expectations for the partnership.
* **Collaboration Goals Details** (`collaboration_goals_details`, optional): Provide a brief explanation of your goals and expectations for the collaboration.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, required): Select the channels you are comfortable with for communication (Email, Phone, Social Media, In-app messaging).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, optional): If you are representing a team, please enter your name and title.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Social Media** (`contact_social_media`, optional): If you have a social media presence, please list it here.
* **Team Website** (`team_website`, optional): If you have a team website, please enter the URL.
* **Team Social Media** (`team_social_media`, required): Select the social media platforms your team is present on.
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, optional): Enter a brief description of your team's identity, mission, or goals.
* **Creator Info** (`creator_info`, optional): Enter a brief description of your role and experience as a content creator.
* **Creator Experience** (`creator_experience`, optional): Enter the number of years you have experience as a content creator.
* **Creator Achievements** (`creator_achievements`, optional): If you have any notable achievements, please select them (1st place, 2nd place, 3rd place).
* **Team Achievements** (`team_achievements`, optional): If your team has any notable achievements, please select them (Yes, No).
* **Team Qualifications** (`team_qualifications`, optional): Enter any qualifications or certifications your team holds.
* **Creator Qualifications** (`creator_qualifications`, optional): Enter any qualifications or certifications you hold.
