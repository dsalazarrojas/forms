# fn_open_staff_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fn_open_staff_application" form is designed for staff members to apply for open gaming positions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "staff_application" field to enter your application.
2. Provide your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred role from the "role" dropdown menu.
6. Select whether you are associated with a game by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "game" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
8. Provide any additional comments or information in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_application** (`text`, required): This field is the main application title.
* **name** (`text`, optional): Enter your full name.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **role** (`select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred role from the dropdown menu.
	+ Game Moderator: Choose this option if you are interested in moderating gameplay.
	+ Game Moderator Assistant: Choose this option if you are interested in assisting game moderators.
	+ Game Moderator Manager: Choose this option if you are interested in managing game moderators.
* **game** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you are associated with a game.
	+ Yes: Choose this option if you are associated with a game.
	+ No: Choose this option if you are not associated with a game.
* **date_of_birth** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **note** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
