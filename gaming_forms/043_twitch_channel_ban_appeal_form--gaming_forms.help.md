# Twitch Channel Ban Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to appeal against channel bans on Twitch. Please fill out the form with your channel details and reasons for the ban to help our team review and potentially unban your channel.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your channel ID, which can be found in your channel settings.
2.  Choose why you think the ban was unfair (Banned for Harassment, Banned for Spamming, Banned for Hateful Speech, Banned for Inactivity, Banned for Inappropriate Content).
3.  If you were a repeat offender, select 'True'.
4.  If the ban was a result of a moderator, select 'True'.
5.  Enter the date and time of the ban.
6.  Provide a detailed explanation of what happened and why you think the ban was unfair.
7.  Enter your Discord username, email, and phone number.
8.  Enter any additional notes you have.
9.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Channel ID** (`channel_id`, number, required): This is your Twitch channel ID, which can be found in your channel settings. Enter the exact ID to help us track your channel.
* **Channel Name** (`channel_name`, text, required): This is your channel name, which will help us identify your channel during the appeal process.
* **Channel URL** (`channel_url`, text, required): This is the URL of your channel, which will help us access your channel during the review process.
* **Channel Ban Reason** (`channel_ban_reason`, text, required): Describe the reason for the ban in detail. Be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Channel Ban Date** (`channel_ban_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the ban.
* **Channel Ban Time** (`channel_ban_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the ban.
* **Channel Who Banned** (`channel_who_banned`, text, required): Enter the name of the moderator who banned your channel.
* **Channel What to Do** (`channel_what_to_do`, text, required): Suggest any actions you would like to take after being unbanned.
* **Channel Owner Details** (`channel_owner_details`, text, required): Enter your Discord username, which is required to verify your identity.
* **Channel Owner Email** (`channel_owner_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which is required to verify your identity.
* **Channel Owner Phone** (`channel_owner_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Channel Owner Notes** (`channel_owner_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your channel or ban.
* **Reason for Ban** (`select_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for the ban from the options: Banned for Harassment, Banned for Spamming, Banned for Hateful Speech, Banned for Inactivity, Banned for Inappropriate Content.
* **Was this a Repeat Offender?** (`select_reason_2`, select_multiple, required): If you were banned multiple times, select 'True'.
* **Was the Ban a Result of a Moderator?** (`select_reason_3`, select_multiple, required): If the ban was a result of a moderator, select 'True'.
* **Appeal Details** (`appeal_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of why you think the ban was unfair.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, text, required): Once you have filled out all the fields, click the 'Submit' button to send your appeal.
