## Game Design Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
The purpose of this Game Design Fellowship Application Form is for aspiring game designers and enthusiasts to apply for a fellowship program that offers resources and guidance to help them develop their skills in the field of game design. The program aims to connect applicants with experienced professionals and mentors in the industry who can provide valuable insights and feedback.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form pages to gather the required information.
2. Fill out each page as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional)
This is the title of your application. Please provide a short and clear title that represents your name and game design background or interests.

### **Project Ideas** (`project_ideas`, text, optional)
List any game-related projects you have worked on or ideas you'd like to pursue. This can include game prototypes, concept art, or any other relevant work.

### **Project Ideas Description** (`project_ideas_description`, text, optional)
Provide a brief description of each project idea listed above. Explain your inspiration, goals, and any relevant details about the project.

### **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional)
Add links to your online portfolio, website, or any relevant examples of your game design work.

### **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, text, optional)
Write a statement that explains why you are applying for the Game Design Fellowship and what you hope to achieve or learn from the program.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers, as they will be reviewed by the fellowship committee.
* Use clear and concise language to make your application stand out.
* Proofread your form carefully before submitting to ensure there are no errors.
* Make sure to attach any required supporting documents or materials as instructed in the fellowship guidelines.
