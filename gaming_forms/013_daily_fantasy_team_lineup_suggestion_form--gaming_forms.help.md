# Daily Fantasy Team Lineup Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you suggest daily fantasy sports lineups based on your preferences. Please fill out the form below to provide information about your team and we will use it to suggest a lineup for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your first name.
2.  Enter your email address.
3.  Confirm your email address to prevent duplicate entries.
4.  Select the sport you play (e.g., NFL, MLB, NBA, or NHL).
5.  Choose the type of contest you are interested in (e.g., Head-to-Head, Roto, or Satellites).
6.  Select the position of your players in your team (e.g., Starting, Backup, or Reserve).
7.  Enter the name of your league.
8.  Choose the size of your league (e.g., Small, Medium, or Large).
9.  Select the type of your league (e.g., Public or Private).
10.  Provide a suggested lineup for your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to help us suggest a lineup for you.
*   **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to confirm your identity and prevent duplicate entries.
*   **Confirm Email** (`user_email_confirmation`, email, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm it matches the one above and prevent spam submissions.
*   **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, required): Select the sport you play (e.g., NFL, MLB, NBA, or NHL).
*   **Contest Type** (`contest_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of contest you are interested in (e.g., Head-to-Head, Roto, or Satellites).
*   **Lineup Position** (`lineup_position`, select_multiple, required): Select the position of your players in your team (e.g., Starting, Backup, or Reserve).
*   **Name** (`league_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your league.
*   **Size** (`league_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your league (e.g., Small, Medium, or Large).
*   **Type** (`league_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your league (e.g., Public or Private).
*   **Suggested Lineup** (`suggested_lineup`, text, required): Provide a suggested lineup for your team.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check your email address to ensure it is correct and matches your confirmation.
*   Provide a valid sport, contest type, lineup position, league name, league size, and league type to get accurate results.
*   A suggested lineup will be provided based on your preferences.
