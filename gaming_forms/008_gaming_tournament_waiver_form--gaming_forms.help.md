# Gaming Tournament Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to provide their information and consent for participating in a gaming tournament. It is meant to be filled out by participants to collect their details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4.  Fill in your address in the "Participant Address" field.
5.  Enter your city in the "Participant City" field.
6.  Enter your state in the "Participant State" field.
7.  Enter your zip code in the "Participant Zip" field.
8.  Check the "Legal Parental Consent" box if you are under 18, or if you are 18 or older, sign below.
9.  Enter your age in the "Participant Age" field.
10.  Enter your date of birth in the "Participant DOB" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer who is filling out this form.
* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the participant who will be participating in the tournament.
*   Participant Email: Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Address**: Enter the address of the participant.
*   Participant City: Enter the city of the participant.
*   Participant State: Enter the state of the participant's residence.
*   Participant Zip: Enter the zip code of the participant's residence.
*   Legal Parental Consent: If you are under 18, check this box to indicate that your parent or guardian has given their consent for you to participate. If you are 18 or older, sign below this box.
*   Participant Age: Enter the age of the participant.
*   Participant DOB: Enter the date of birth of the participant.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are under 18, make sure to have your parent or guardian sign the form before submitting it.
*   If you are 18 or older, make sure to sign the form below the "Legal Parental Consent" box.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer.
