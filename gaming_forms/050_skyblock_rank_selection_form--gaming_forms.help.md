# skyblock_rank_selection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users select their desired skyblock rank. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure you provide the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have filled out all required fields.
2. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification from the support team.
3. Once you've completed all fields, click on the "Submit" option to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you'd like to be addressed in the skyblock community.
* **application_type** (`application_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available application types to indicate the type of application you're submitting.
* **rank** (`rank`, select_one, optional): Select your desired rank from the available options.
* **game_experience** (`game_experience`, number, optional): Please enter the number of years you have been playing skyblock.
* **team_size** (`team_size`, number, optional): If you're applying as part of a team, please enter the size of your team.
* **role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select the role you're applying for in the skyblock community.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information, including your email address or Discord handle.
* **submit_application** (`submit_application`, select_one, optional): Select the type of application you're submitting, but note that it's not actually used in this form.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure you're honest and accurate in your answers.
