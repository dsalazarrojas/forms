# Fantasy League Player Stats Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect statistics from players participating in a fantasy sports league. The goal is to gather information about player performance in each game of the season. The form is intended for fantasy league administrators to track and analyze player data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the game(s) you want to report statistics for.
2. Enter the date of the game(s).
3. Enter the player's name.
