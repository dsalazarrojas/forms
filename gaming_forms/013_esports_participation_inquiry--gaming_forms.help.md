# Esports Participation Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Esports Participation Inquiry form is designed to gather information from players who are interested in participating in esports events. Please answer the questions below to help us understand your interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select one option from the select_one field and type in your text responses in the text fields. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you interested in participating in esports events?** (`player_interest`, select_one, required: false): Check 'Yes' if you are interested in participating in esports events, and 'No' if you are not interested.
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, required: false): Please provide us with any relevant details about yourself as a player. This could include your gaming experience, your current team or squad, or any other information you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, required: false): If you are part of a team, please provide us with details about your team. This could include your team's name, game, and any other relevant information.
* **Event Availability** (`event_availability`, text, required: false): If you are available to participate in events, please let us know what type of events you are interested in (e.g., online or offline, specific game titles, etc.).
* **Follow-up Preferences** (`follow-up_preferences`, text, required: false): Please provide us with any additional information you would like to share with us. This could include your contact information, preferred communication channels, or any other details you would like to add.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required: false): If there's anything else you'd like to share with us, please add it here. This could include your gaming setup, your experience with our platform, or any other relevant information.

## Tips
Please make sure to answer all the required fields and provide accurate information to help us better understand your interests and preferences. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
