# High Scores Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High Scores Survey form is used to collect user feedback and high score information for a game. This form helps the game community or rating system to track and share scores and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Input** field with your personal details.
2. Enter your **High Score**.
3. Provide **Feedback** about your experience with the game.
4. Enter your **Player Comments**.
5. Provide **Game Details** such as **Game Title**, **Game ID**, and **Game Platform**.
6. Choose the **Game Platform** where you played the game (e.g., Steam, XBox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Google, or Other).
7. Select the **Game Genre** (e.g., Action, Adventure, Puzzle, Strategy, Role Playing, Simulation, Sports, or Other).
8. Enter your **Submit Time** and **Submit Date**.
