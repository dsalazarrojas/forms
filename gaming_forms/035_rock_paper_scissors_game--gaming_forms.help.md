# Rock Paper Scissors Game - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to simulate a game of Rock Paper Scissors between two players. It's a simple and fun game used to break the ice or for entertainment.

## How To Complete This Form
To play the game, simply choose between rock, paper, and scissors using the "Choose" field. The game will then display the outcome in the "Answer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose (select_one)**: This field allows you to make your move in the game. You can choose between "rock", "paper", and "scissors" to play against the other player.
* **Answer (note)**: This field will display the outcome of the game. It will show the result of your choice against the other player's choice.

## Tips
* Make your choice carefully, as the game is based on a simple but strategic game theory problem.
* You can play multiple times to improve your chances of winning.
* The game is meant to be a fun way to spend time, so don't take it too seriously!
