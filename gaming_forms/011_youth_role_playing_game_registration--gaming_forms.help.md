# Youth Role Playing Game Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for creating registrations for players of a youth role-playing game. This includes collecting information about the player and their parents for the game's administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Session Name** (field 1) - This is the name of the game session for which the player is registering.
2. Enter the **Player Name** (field 2) - This is the name of the player.
3. Enter the **Player Age** (field 3) - This is the age of the player.
4. Select the **Player Gender** (field 4) - Choose from Female, Male, or Other.
5. Enter the **Parent Name** (field 5) - This is the name of the player's parent or guardian.
6. Enter the **Parent Email** (field 6) - This is the email address of the player's parent or guardian.
7. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** (field 7) - This is the name of the emergency contact person.
8. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone** (field 8) - This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
9. Enter **Player Notes** (field 9) - This is any additional information about the player that the game administrators might need to know.
10. Enter the **Player Date of Birth** (field 10) - This is the date of birth of the player.
11. Enter the **Player Time of Registration** (field 11) - This is the time of the player's registration.
12. Enter the **Parent Phone** (field 12) - This is the phone number of the parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name** (field 1): Enter the name of the game session for which the player is registering.
* **Player Name** (field 2): Enter the name of the player.
* **Player Age** (field 3): Enter the age of the player.
* **Player Gender** (field 4): Select the gender of the player.
* **Parent Name** (field 5): Enter the name of the player's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (field 6): Enter the email address of the player's parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (field 7): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (field 8): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Player Notes** (field 9): Enter any additional information about the player that the game administrators might need to know.
* **Player Date of Birth** (field 10): Enter the date of birth of the player.
* **Player Time of Registration** (field 11): Enter the time of the player's registration.
* **Parent Phone** (field 12): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the player and their parent or guardian.
* If any of the fields are not required, you can still submit the form, but be aware that the information might not be recorded correctly.
* If you need help or have any questions, please contact the game administrators.
