# Esports Broadcaster Licensing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esports Broadcaster Licensing Application form is a necessary step for individual broadcasters to obtain a license to broadcast esports content. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences for a customized license.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information:
	* BroaDcaster Name (ID: 2)
	* Broadcaster Email (ID: 3)
	* Broadcaster Phone (ID: 4)
2. Choose your license type:
	* Select one of the options: TV, Stream, Both (ID: 6)
3. Add any additional information (optional):
	* This is your opportunity to provide any extra details about your broadcasting setup or preferences (ID: 8)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **BroaDcaster Name (ID: 2)** (`title`, `text`, **required**): This field is your name as a broadcaster. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Broadcaster Email (ID: 3)** (`title`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address. This is how we'll contact you with updates and information about your license.
- **Broadcaster Phone (ID: 4)** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number. This is an additional contact method we'll use to reach you.
- **License Type (ID: 6)** (`title`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose one of the following options:
	* TV: If you primarily broadcast on television.
	* Stream: If you primarily broadcast on streaming platforms.
	* Both: If you broadcast on both television and streaming platforms.
- **License Details (ID: 7)** (`title`, `note`, **optional**): This field is optional, but if you have any specific details about your broadcasting setup or preferences, please enter them here. We'll use this information to tailor your license to your needs.
- **Additional Info (ID: 8)** (`title`, `note`, **optional**): Use this field to provide any extra details about your broadcasting preferences or setup. This is a good opportunity to highlight anything we should know about your broadcasting setup.
- **Form Footer (ID: 9)** (`title`, `note`, **optional**): This is a placeholder for any additional information or instructions from the form. Please read this section carefully before submitting your form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the contact information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
- Select a single license type that best represents your primary broadcast preference.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact us for assistance.
