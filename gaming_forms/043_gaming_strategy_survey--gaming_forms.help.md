# Gaming Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your gaming preferences and habits to help game developers and publishers create more engaging and relevant content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select all the platforms you play games on (e.g., PC, Console, Mobile).
2. Choose all the genres you enjoy playing (e.g., FPS, RPG, Strategy, etc.).
3. Select all the strategies you use while playing games (e.g., Min-max, Grind, Role-play, etc.).
4. If you're comfortable sharing, please provide your estimated time commitment for gaming per week (in hours).
5. Rate your skill level as a gamer from 1 to 10.
6. Choose the type of player you identify with (e.g., Hardcore, Casual, Social, or Solo).
7. If you have a favorite game, please mention it.
8. If you have a favorite gaming platform, please mention it.
9. Please provide your name and email (if you're willing to share it).
10. If you have a phone number, it would be helpful for us to follow up with you.

### Time Commitment

### Skill Rating
* **Skill Rating**: Rate your skill level as a gamer from 1 to 10.

### Player Type
* **Player Type** (`Hardcore`, `Casual`, `Social`, `Solo`, required: false): Choose the type of player you identify with.

### Favorite Game

Note: This form is designed to be as user-friendly as possible, and you can always ask for help if you have any questions or concerns.
