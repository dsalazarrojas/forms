# Illuminati Join Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Illuminati Join Application Form is used for potential members to provide personal and interested-related information for consideration of membership in the Illuminati.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your Discord username in the "Discord Username" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter your state in the "State" field.
9. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
10. Enter your website in the "Website" field, if applicable.
11. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
12. Select your gender from the "Gender" field.
13. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
14. Select your occupation from the "Occupation" field, if applicable.
15. Enter your nationality in the "Nationality" field.
16. Enter your religion in the "Religion" field.
17. Enter your ethnicity in the "Ethnicity" field.
18. Enter your height in the "Height" field.
19. Enter your weight in the "Weight" field.
20. Select your education level from the "Education" field, if applicable.
21. Select your experience in the "Experience" field, if applicable.
22. Select your interests from the "Interests" field, if applicable.
23. Select your skills from the "Skills" field, if applicable.
24. Select your languages from the "Languages" field, if applicable.
25. Enter any additional notes or information in the "Notes" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discord Username** (`discord_username`, text, optional): Enter your Discord username to connect with us.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please provide your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your physical address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for mailing purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): If applicable, select your occupation.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, optional): Enter your nationality.
* **Religion** (`religion`, text, optional): Enter your religion.
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, text, optional): Enter your ethnicity.
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of education if applicable.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of experience, if applicable.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select your hobbies or interests, if applicable.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant skills you possess, if applicable.
* **Languages** (`languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select any languages you speak, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information.
