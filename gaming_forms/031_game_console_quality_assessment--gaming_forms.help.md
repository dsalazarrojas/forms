This form is designed to assess the quality of a game console. The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about the condition and functionality of their gaming consoles. This form is likely used by support or quality assurance teams to gauge the console's performance and identify areas for improvement. This form is not intended for users to report bugs or issues with game performance, but rather to provide an overall assessment of the console's condition.

## Game Console Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand the overall quality of your gaming console.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To provide an honest assessment, please answer each question based on your personal experience with the console.
2. Select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" for Functionality to indicate how well the console has been performing.
3. For Console Condition, Accessory Condition, and Record Condition, describe the condition of each part of the console.
4. Rate the Quality Rating on a scale from 0 to 10, with 0 being the worst and 10 being the best.
5. Add any comments you may have about your console's condition in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Console Condition** (`console_condition`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the console, including any scratches, dents, or other visible imperfections.
* **Functionality** (`functionality`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to indicate how well the console's functions have been performing.
* **Accessories Condition** (`accessories_condition`, text, optional): Describe the condition of any accessories attached to the console, such as controllers, cables, or other peripherals.
* **Record Condition** (`record_condition`, text, optional): Describe the condition of any records or media stored on the console, such as games, saves, or music.
* **Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall quality of the console on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or observations about the console's condition.
