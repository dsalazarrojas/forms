# Mobile Arcade Gaming Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users on various aspects of the mobile arcade gaming experience, helping developers to improve and refine their games.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your overall rating of the game on a scale of 1-5.
2.  Indicate whether or not you have a favorite title in the series.
3.  Choose any feature ideas or improvements you would like to see in the game by selecting the "Yes" option for each idea you support.
4.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Player Comment" field.
5.  If you would like to hear back from the developers, please provide your email address in the "Developer Response" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Player Ratings (1-5)** (`select_one`, required):
    Select a rating from 1 to 5 that best represents your overall experience with the game.
*  **Favorite Titles (`Yes/No`, required):
    Indicate if you have a favorite title in the series. If you do, select "Yes".
*  **Feature Ideas (`Yes/No`, required):
    Choose any feature ideas or improvements you would like to see in the game. Select "Yes" for each idea you support.
*  **Player Comment (`text`, optional):
    Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your gaming experience.
*  **Developer Response (`text`, optional):
    If you would like to hear back from the developers, please provide your email address and they will be in touch with you to address any concerns or suggestions.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so developers can best understand your thoughts and ideas.
*   If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the "Player Comment" field.
*   If you would like to hear back from the developers, please provide your email address in the "Developer Response" field.
*   Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback on your gaming experience! Your input is valuable to us, and we look forward to hearing from you.
