# MSAP Chess Club Student Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from students who want to register for the MSAP Chess Club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. This is the name by which you will be addressed in club communications.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field. This is your surname.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be your primary contact method for club updates and notifications.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is your primary contact number.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter your chess rating in the "Chess Rating" field, if applicable.
7. Enter your parent's/guardian's name in the "Parent Name" field, if applicable.
8. Enter your emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
9. Select your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Guardian, or Emergency Contact) in the "Relationship" field.
10. If you give consent to share your information with the club, select "True" in the "Consent to Share Info" field.
11. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the "Emergency Contact Email" field, if applicable.
12. Enter your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Chess Rating** (number, optional): If you have a chess rating, enter it here.
* **Parent Name** (text, optional): If you are under 18 years old, enter your parent's/guardian's name here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name here.
* **Relationship** (select one, required): Select your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Guardian, Emergency Contact).
* **Consent to Share Info** (select multiple, optional): If you give consent to share your information with the club, select "True".
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address here.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address here.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your contact information accurately, as this will be used to reach you in case of emergencies.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
* Once you complete this form, you will be able to access club events, resources, and benefits.
