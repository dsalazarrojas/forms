# video_game_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a user's favorite video games.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections. Please follow these steps to complete it:

1. Answer the "What is your favorite video game?" question with a single line of text in the "intro" section.
2. Select your favorite video game genre from the list in the "genre" section.
3. Enter your favorite character in the "favorite_character" field.
4. Enter your favorite platform in the "favorite_platform" field.
5. Enter your favorite console in the "favorite_console" field.
6. Enter your favorite game in the "favorite_game" field.
7. Enter your favorite game console in the "favorite_game_console" field.
8. Enter your favorite game genre in the "favorite_game_genre" field.
9. Enter your favorite game platform in the "favorite_game_platform" field.
10. Enter your favorite game publisher in the "favorite_game_publisher" field.
11. Enter your favorite game developer in the "favorite_game_developer" field.
12. Enter your favorite game series in the "favorite_game_series" field.
13. Enter the release year of your favorite game in the "favorite_game_release_year" field.
14. Enter your favorite game rating in the "favorite_game_rating" field.
15. Enter the number of players in your favorite game in the "favorite_game_players" field.
16. Enter your favorite game developer (if different from the previous field) in the "favorite_game_developer_2" field.
17. Enter your second favorite game in the "favorite_game_2" field.
18. Enter your third favorite game in the "favorite_game_3" field.
19. Enter your fourth favorite game in the "favorite_game_4" field.
20. Enter your fifth favorite game in the "favorite_game_5" field.
21. Enter your sixth favorite game in the "favorite_game_6" field.
22. Enter your seventh favorite game in the "favorite_game_7" field.
23. Enter your eighth favorite game in the "favorite_game_8" field.
24. Enter your ninth favorite game in the "favorite_game_9" field.
25. Enter your tenth favorite game in the "favorite_game_10" field.
