# Gaming Equipment Calibration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects necessary details to initiate the calibration process for your gaming equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure accurate and efficient calibration, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for all fields marked as "required".
3. Select the correct "Equipment Type" from the options provided.
4. If you have any comments or notes regarding the calibration, please use the "Calibration Comments" field.
5. Verify that the "Date Submitted" is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This field is for your name, please enter your name as it appears on your official documentation or as you are known in the department.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address, please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number, please ensure it is correct and reachable during business hours.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of gaming equipment you are requesting calibration for. Examples: Laser Gun, Rifle, Pistol, Shotgun, or Sniper.
* **Equipment Status**: Indicate the condition of the equipment, using one of the following options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Serial Number**: This field is for the unique identifier of your gaming equipment.
* **Model Number**: Enter the model number of your gaming equipment.
* **Calibration Due**: If the calibration is due on a specific date, please enter it here.
* **Calibration Status**: Check if the equipment is due for calibration or if it has already been calibrated.
* **Calibration Type**: Select all calibration types that apply to your equipment. Choose from the options: Calibration 1, Calibration 2, or Calibration 3.
* **Calibration Frequency**: Enter the recommended calibration frequency for your equipment.
* **Calibration Comments**: If you have any additional comments or notes regarding the calibration, please enter them here.
* **Name of Submitter**: This field is for the name of the individual submitting the form, please ensure it matches the individual listed in the "Submitted By" field.
* **Email of Submitter**: This field is for the email address of the individual submitting the form, please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone of Submitter**: This field is for the phone number of the individual submitting the form, please ensure it is correct and reachable during business hours.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is automatically filled in by the system, please ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
* **Submitted By**: Select the individual who is submitting the form from the available options.
* **Calibration Due**: If the calibration is due on a specific date, please enter it here.
 
Note: The internal team will review and follow up on each submission. If you are unsure about any of the fields or have additional information please don't hesitate to contact your supervisor or calibration technician.
