# trivia_night_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trivia Night Sign Up Form is a registration form for participants to sign up for a Trivia Night event. This form collects necessary information from participants, including their name, contact details, team name, and other relevant details. The form is a critical component of the Trivia Night event registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, **Participant Phone**, **Team Name**, and **Address** fields.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
3. Enter the **Number of Participants** in your team.
4. (Optional) Add any **Additional Comments** you'd like to include with your registration.
5. Review your completed form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team.
* **Number of Participants**: Enter the number of participants in your team.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the participant in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the participant.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the participant (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you're part of a team, make sure to list all team members' names and dates of birth accurately.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, use the **Additional Comments** field to include them.
