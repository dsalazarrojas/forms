# Battle Arena Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Battle Arena Selection Survey is designed to collect information from gamers about their preferences and opinions on battle arena features, game settings, and playing habits.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the steps below to complete the form:

1. Enter your gamer name, email address, and other required information in the fields below.
2. Select your preferred game title from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose your preferred arena type for each question.
4. Select all the features you consider important for a battle arena.
5. Rate the importance of visual quality and performance for you.
6. Choose your preferred way of matching players.
7. Select when you usually play.
8. Choose your primary platform to play on.
9. Rate your satisfaction with current battle arenas.
10. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gamer Name or Tag (required)**: Enter your in-game name or a unique tag that represents you.
* **Email Address (required)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Team or Clan Name (optional)**: Enter your team or clan name if you belong to one.
* **Primary Game Title (required)**: Select the game you primarily play, such as "Arena of Valor" or "League of Legends".
* **Current Rank or Tier (required)**: Enter your current rank or tier in the game.
* **First Choice Arena (required)**: Select your preferred arena type, such as "Summoner Rift Style" or "5v5 Classic".
* **Second Choice Arena (optional)**: Select your backup arena type if you don't like your first choice.
* **Map Size Preference (required)**: Choose your preferred map size, such as "Small - Fast Paced" or "Large - Strategic".
* **Game Mode Preference (required)**: Select the game mode you enjoy the most, such as "Competitive Ranked" or "Casual Unranked".
* **Most Important Arena Features (required)**: Select all the features you consider most important, such as "Low Latency" and "Anti-Cheat System".
* **Visual Quality Priority (required)**: Rate the importance of visual quality, such as "Very Important" or "Neutral".
* **Performance Priority (required)**: Rate the importance of performance, such as "Critical - Must Have High FPS" or "Not a Concern".
* **Matchmaking Preference (required)**: Choose how you think players should be matched, such as "Skill Based" or "Region Based".
* **Typical Play Schedule (required)**: Select when you usually play, such as "Morning" or "Any Time".
* **Primary Platform (required)**: Choose the platform you primarily play on, such as "PC" or "Mobile".
* **Satisfaction with Current Arenas (required)**: Rate your satisfaction with the current battle arenas, such as "Very Satisfied" or "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Improvements Needed (required)**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
* **Additional Feedback (optional)**: Enter any other comments or suggestions you have.

## Tips
Please be honest and thorough in your answers. The more accurate your feedback, the better we can understand your opinions and preferences.
