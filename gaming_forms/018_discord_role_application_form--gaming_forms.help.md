# Discord Role Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for roles on a Discord server. It will help the server administrators understand your qualifications and interests, and determine the best fit for you in the server.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the roles you are interested in from the provided options.
3. Specify your timezone and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How do you know me**: Please describe how you know the person recommending you for this role.
* **What server is this for**: Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in applying for a role on this server.
* **What roles are you interested in**: Select all the roles you are interested in or available for. You can select multiple roles.
* **What is your email**: Please enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **What timezone are you in**: Choose your timezone from the dropdown menu.
* **What hours are you available**: Enter your available hours in the time format (HH:MM or 24:00).
* **About me**: Write a brief description of yourself and why you are interested in this role.
* **What Discord tags do you want**: Select all the tags you want to be associated with your role.
* **Why Jotform**: Please explain why you chose to use this form (Note: This field can be left blank).
* **Why this role**: Write a brief description of why you are interested in this specific role.
* **Contact method**: Choose how you want to be contacted (e.g., email, Discord message, phone).
* **Timezone offset**: If you have a timezone offset, enter it here (e.g., -5 for EST).
* **Timezone aware**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your timezone offset.
* **Roles qualified**: Select all the roles you have experience in or have been qualified for.
* **Timezone label**: Choose a label for your timezone (e.g., EST, PST).
* **Timezone aware label**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your timezone label.
* **Roles aware label**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your roles.
* **Timezone**: Choose your timezone (same as 'What timezone are you in').
* **Timezone aware**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your timezone.
* **Roles aware**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your roles.
* **Roles description**: Choose a description for your roles (e.g., 'Server Moderator').
* **Timezone aware description**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your timezone description.
* **Roles aware description**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your roles description.
* **Timezone aware aware**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your timezone aware status.
* **Roles aware aware**: Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of your roles aware status. 

Note: Some fields seem redundant and might be unnecessary. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask a server administrator.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
