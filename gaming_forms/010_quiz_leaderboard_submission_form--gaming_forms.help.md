# Quiz Leaderboard Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quiz Leaderboard Submission Form is a tool used to collect and display participant and event information for quizzes. It allows users to submit their results and details of the event they participated in, enabling the creation and maintenance of a leaderboard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Quiz Leaderboard Submission Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section.
2. Submit your quiz results.
3. Enter the event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details**: Please enter your name, username, or any other identifier that you would like to be displayed on the leaderboard. This field is optional, but it will be used to identify you as a participant.
* **quiz_results**: Enter your quiz results, such as the number of correct answers or a score out of a maximum score. This field is required as it is a crucial part of the submission.
* **event_details**: Enter the details of the event you participated in, such as the name of the event, the date, or any other relevant information. This field is also required as it will help to filter and categorize the submissions by event.
