# GANG Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GANG Application Form is a self-service application form designed for individuals interested in joining the GANG program. The form will be reviewed and evaluated for suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field, as it will be used for communication purposes.
3. For the "Phone" field, enter your phone number if you have one. This is not required but will be helpful for communication purposes.
4. For the "Date" field, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. For the "Time" field, enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication purposes. Make sure to double-check your email address for any typos.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will be used for communication purposes. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of your availability in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is the date you would be available to join the program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time you would be available to join the program in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
