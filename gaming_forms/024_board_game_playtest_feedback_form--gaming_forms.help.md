# Board Game Playtest Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from playtesters after they have played a board game. The information gathered from this form will help us refine the game design and improve the gaming experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, providing as much information as you can about your experience playing the board game.
- Start by filling in your tester's information if you'd like to be identified as the tester.
- Then, select a date when the playtest took place.
- After that, you can rate your overall enjoyment of the game and the clarity of its rules.
- In the "Overall enjoyment rating" field, please pick the rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
- In the "Rule clarity rating" field, please pick the rating from "Very unclear" to "Very clear".
- If you have any comments or suggestions for improving the game, please include them in the "Components quality feedback" and "Improvement suggestions" fields.
- You can also add any balance feedback, if you feel it's necessary.
- Don't forget to rate your play duration in minutes.
- If you want to provide more information, but don't know what to say, feel free to add it in the "Balance feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tester name (Tester name)**: Please enter your name, but this is optional.
* **Play date (Play date)**: Enter the date when you played the game. This is required.
* **Play duration minutes (Play duration minutes)**: Enter the length of time you played the game.
* **Overall enjoyment rating (Overall enjoyment rating)**: Rate how much you enjoyed the game from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Balance feedback (Balance feedback)**: Add any comments on the balance of the game, if necessary.
* **Rule clarity rating (Rule clarity rating)**: Rate how clear the game rules were, from "Very unclear" to "Very clear".
* **Components quality feedback (Components quality feedback)**: If you have any feedback on the game components (cards, board), add it here.
* **Improvement suggestions (Improvement suggestions)**: Share any ideas you have to improve the game. 
* **Balance feedback (Balance feedback)**: Add any balance-related comments, if you feel it's necessary.
