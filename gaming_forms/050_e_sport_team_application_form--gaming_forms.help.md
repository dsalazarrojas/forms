# E Sport Team Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Sport Team Application Form is used to gather information about your team's details for our evaluation. This form will help us to get to know your team better and evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose whether your team has any experience with Esports.
2. Enter the names of the team members and their roles.
3. Provide your team's contact email.
4. Enter your Discord username.
5. Enter your Discord tag (if applicable).
6. Provide a link to your Twitch channel (if applicable).
7. Enter a link to any social media or website related to your team.
8. Fill out the team's details, including name, game name, game version, game OS, and game server.
9. Enter the team's experience in the game.
10. Enter the team leader's name, role, and experience.
11. Enter any additional team images, videos, or audio files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Details** (Team Details, Select, required): This field allows you to select whether your team has any experience with Esports.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, Text, required): This field is for listing the names of the team members.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, Email, required): This field is for your team's contact email.
* **Discord Username** (Discord Username, Text, required): This field is for your Discord username.
* **Discord Tag** (Discord Tag, Text, optional): This field is for your Discord tag (if applicable).
* **Twitch Channel** (Twitch Channel, Text, optional): This field is for a link to your Twitch channel (if applicable).
* **Social Link** (Social Link, Text, optional): This field is for any link to a social media or website related to your team.
* **Tournament Link** (Tournament Link, Text, optional): This field is for any tournament link (if applicable).
* **Team Name** (Team Name, Text, required): This field is for your team's name.
* **Team Description** (Team Description, Text, optional): This field is for your team's description.
* **Game Name** (Game Name, Text, required): This field is for the name of the game your team plays.
* **Game Version** (Game Version, Text, required): This field is for the version of the game your team plays.
* **Game OS** (Game OS, Text, required): This field is for the operating system of the game your team plays.
* **Game Server** (Game Server, Text, required): This field is for the game server of the team.
* **Team Experience** (Team Experience, Text, optional): This field is for your team's experience in the game.
* **Team Leader** (Team Leader, Text, required): This field is for the team leader's name.
* **Team Leader Role** (Team Leader Role, Text, required): This field is for the team leader's role.
* **Team Leader Experience** (Team Leader Experience, Text, optional): This field is for the team leader's experience.
* **Team Image** (Team Image, Text, optional): This field is for any additional team images.
* **Team Video** (Team Video, Text, optional): This field is for any team videos.
* **Team Audio** (Team Audio, Text, optional): This field is for any team audio files.
* **Team Website** (Team Website, Text, optional): This field is for any team website link.
* **Team Discord** (Team Discord, Text, optional): This field is for any team Discord link.
* **Team Facebook** (Team Facebook, Text, optional): This field is for any team Facebook link.
* **Team Twitch** (Team Twitch, Text, optional): This field is for any team Twitch link.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you have any additional team images, videos, or audio files, you can upload them separately.
* If you have any other questions, feel free to contact us.
