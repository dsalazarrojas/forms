# Player Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Experience Feedback Survey is designed to gather information from players about their experience with a game, helping us to identify areas for improvement and make future versions more enjoyable for players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the survey, thinking about your experience with the game.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more of the provided options.
3. For text fields, type your thoughts and comments about your experience with the game.
4. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, which are marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Name** (`Game Name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the game you played.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the game, using the provided number scale.
* **Game Features** (`Game Features`, select multiple, optional): Select all the features of the game that you enjoyed, such as Game balance, Story, Artwork, or Soundtrack.
* **Usability Experience** (`Usability Experience`, number, required): Rate your experience with the game's interface and controls, using the provided number scale.
* **Game Length** (`Game Length`, number, optional): Tell us how long you played the game for.
* **Replay Value** (`Replay Value`, select one, optional): Tell us how many times you would like to play the game again, using the provided options.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the game, please type them here.
* **Feedback Comments** (`Feedback Comments`, text, optional): If you have any further thoughts or comments about your experience with the game, please type them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and thoughtful in your responses, as they will help us create better games for you.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation!
