# Online Gaming Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your online gaming experience to help us better understand your gaming habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. If you have used multiple platforms, select all that apply for the "Platforms Used" field.
3. For "Average Playtime per Day" and "Average Satisfaction Rating", use numbers only.
4. For "Improvement Ideas" and "Favorite Game", type in a specific text or phrase.
5. For "Platform Preference", select only one type of platform that you use most frequently.
6. If you have any suggestions or comments, type them in the "Gaming Community" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Platforms Used** (`platforms_used`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select all the platforms you have used for online gaming (e.g., PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, PC).
* **Average Playtime per Day** (`average_playtime`, `number`, required: false): This field asks for the average amount of time you spend playing games per day. Please enter a number only (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours).
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the online gaming platform you use. Please select a rating from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, required: false): Share your ideas on how we can improve the online gaming platform.
* **Play Habits** (`play_habits`, `date`, required: false): This field is not applicable for this form. Please disregard it.
* **Preferred Platform** (`platform_preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of platform you use most frequently (e.g., PC, Console, Mobile, Browser, Other).
* **Favorite Game** (`favorite_game`, `text`, required: false): Type in the name of your favorite game.
* **Gaming Community** (`gaming_community`, `note`, required: false): This field is not applicable for this form. Please disregard it.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Which Platform Do You Use Most Frequently?** (`platform_usage`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of platform you use most frequently (e.g., Console, Mobile, PC, Browser, Other).
