# e_sports_event_outcome_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_sports_event_outcome_report_form is designed to collect data on e-sports events, allowing you to track and analyze their outcomes. This form helps you to record essential information such as event ID, name, date, and participant details, as well as audience engagement metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **event_id**, a unique identifier for the event.
2. Enter the **event_name**, a descriptive name for the event.
3. Select the **event_date**, the date the event took place.
4. Enter the **player_id**, a unique identifier for the player.
5. Enter the **player_name**, the name of the player.
6. Select the **player_performance**, the level of performance the player achieved (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
7. Enter the **audience_size**, the number of spectators or viewers who attended the event.
8. Select the **audience_engagement_level**, the level of engagement the audience displayed during the event (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
9. Enter the **engagement_comments**, any additional comments about the audience's engagement.
10. (Optional) Enter the **event_success_rating**, a numerical rating of the event's success.
11. (Optional) Enter the **event_success_comments**, any additional comments about the event's success.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_id** (number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the event.
* **event_name** (text, required): Enter a descriptive name for the event.
* **event_date** (date, required): Select the date the event took place.
* **player_id** (number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the player.
* **player_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the player.
* **player_performance** (select_multiple, required): Select the level of performance the player achieved.
* **audience_size** (number, required): Enter the number of spectators or viewers who attended the event.
* **audience_engagement_level** (select_one, required): Select the level of engagement the audience displayed.
* **engagement_comments** (text, required): Enter any additional comments about the audience's engagement.
* **event_success_rating** (number, optional): Enter a numerical rating of the event's success (if applicable).
* **event_success_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the event's success (if applicable).
