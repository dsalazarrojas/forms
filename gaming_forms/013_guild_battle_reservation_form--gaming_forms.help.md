# Guild Battle Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guild Battle Reservation Form is used to schedule a battle for your guild. Filling out this form will help the battle organizers to prepare for the battle better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the match date and time on the "Match Date" field. 
2. Enter the name of your guild on the "Guild Name" field. 
3. Enter the name of your opponent on the "Opponent Name" field. 
4. Select the game mode for the match on the "Game Mode" field. You can choose from "Raid", "Raid Boss", or "PvP". 
5. Enter the number of players that will be participating in the match on the "Player Count" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Match Date** (`match_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the match. Please use the date format that is commonly used in your region.
* **Match Time** (`match_time`, number, required/optional): Enter the time of the match. Please note that this field will only take numerical values, so please enter the time in the format of 24-hour clock (e.g., 12:00 PM will be 1200).
* **Guild Name** (`guild_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your guild.
* **Opponent Name** (`opponent_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your opponent.
* **Game Mode** (`game_mode`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the game mode for the match. You can choose from "Raid", "Raid Boss", or "PvP".
* **Player Count** (`player_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of players that will be participating in the match.
