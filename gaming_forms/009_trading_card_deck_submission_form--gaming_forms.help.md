# Trading Card Deck Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect trading card deck submissions from players. It's a collection of various fields that gather information about the deck submitted, including the player's name, the name of the tournament, the deck's format, and other specific details about the deck.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your player's name in the "Enter Player Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the tournament in the "Tournament Name" field.
3. Select the correct format of your deck in the "Deck Format" field.
4. List the cards in your deck with their quantities separated by commas in the "Deck Decklist" field.
5. Choose whether your deck is located online or offline in the "Deck Location" field.
6. If your deck is for an online tournament, enter the tournament name in the "Tournament Name" field.
7. Finally, enter the date of the event (if offline) or the start time of the tournament (if online) in the "Tournament Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Player Name** (text, optional): This is where you enter your player's name.
* **Tournament Name** (text, optional): This is where you enter the name of the tournament.
* **Deck Format** (select_one, optional): This is where you select the format of your deck. Common formats include 8, 16, 64, 100, 160, and 300.
* **Deck Decklist** (text, optional): This is where you list the cards in your deck with their quantities separated by commas (e.g., 2x Card A, 3x Card B).
* **Deck Location** (select_one, optional): This is where you select whether your deck is located online or offline.
* **Tournament Date** (date, optional): This is where you enter the date of the event (if offline) or the start time of the tournament (if online).
* **Tournament Name** (text, optional): This is where you enter the name of the tournament (if your deck is for an online tournament).
## Tips
* Please ensure that you're submitting a new deck that hasn't been posted before.
* Please double-check the details about your deck to ensure they are accurate.
