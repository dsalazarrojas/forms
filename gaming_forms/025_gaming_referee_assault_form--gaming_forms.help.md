# Gaming Referee Assault Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Referee Assault Form is a tool designed to gather detailed information from referees or officials about incidents of assault or misconduct during games or events in the gaming industry. This form helps to provide a clear and structured account of what happened, facilitating swift and effective handling of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing Referee Info (Page 1) - This section is used to gather general information about the referee, including their name, contact details, and other relevant contact information.
2. Move on to Incident Info (Page 2) - This section is used to describe the incident in detail, including what happened, when it happened, and any other relevant information.
3. Add any Additional Info (Page 3) - This section is a free-form text field where you can include any other relevant details or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Referee Info (Referee Info, text, required: false)
This field is used to provide contact information and other details about the referee who reported or witnessed the incident.

*   **Name**: Provide the name of the referee who reported or witnessed the incident.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter the contact details of the referee, including their email address and phone number.

### Incident Info (Incident Info, text, required: false)
This field is used to gather a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

*   **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including what occurred, where it occurred, and when it occurred.

### Additional Info (Additional Info, note, required: false)
This field is used to provide any additional comments or details about the incident.

*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any other relevant information about the incident that wasn't already covered in the previous fields. 

## Tips
*   When filling out the form, ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently.
*   Use a clear and concise writing style to avoid confusion.
*   Avoid sharing sensitive or private information about players, teams, or other individuals involved.
*   If you're unsure about any information or need help, reach out to your supervisor or designated personnel for assistance.
