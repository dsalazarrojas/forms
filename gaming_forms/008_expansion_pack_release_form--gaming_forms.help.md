# Expansion Pack Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from game developers before releasing an expansion pack. The data collected will be used to ensure a smooth and efficient release process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on each field to enter the required information.
2. Fill in the fields as described in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.
3. Review your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Release Details (`game_release_details`)**: Enter a brief description of the expansion pack release details. This could include the title of the expansion pack, a brief summary, or any other relevant details.
* **Internal Approvals (`internal_approvals`)**: List any internal approvals that have been received. This could include approvals from management, marketing, or other departments within the company.
* **Promotional Assets (`promotional_assets`)**: Describe any promotional assets that will be used for the expansion pack release. This could include images, videos, or other multimedia content.
* **Release Planning (`release_planning`)**: Provide a summary of the release planning, including any timelines, deadlines, or other relevant details.
* **Team Info (`team_info`)**: Enter any relevant team information, such as the team members responsible for the release, their roles, and contact information.

## Tips

* Please fill in all fields as accurately as possible.
* Use a clear and concise format for your entries.
* Review your entries before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
