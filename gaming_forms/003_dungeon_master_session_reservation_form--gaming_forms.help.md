# Dungeon Master Session Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dungeon Master Session Reservation Form is designed to collect crucial information for a session of the game, including details about the reservation, player details, session details, time and date, game system, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Reservation Details section with information about the session, such as its purpose or any special requests.
2. In the Player Details section, list the names of all players participating in the session.
3. In the Session Details section, provide any relevant details about the session, such as its length or any special requirements.
4. Choose the game system you will be playing (e.g., Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, or Other).
5. Add any notes or comments about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reservation Details

* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`, text, required): This field is for providing a brief description of the session, such as its purpose or any special requests. Be concise and clear in your description.
* **Player Details** (`player_details`, text, optional): This field is for listing any additional details about the players participating in the session. If there are no additional details, leave it blank.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing any relevant details about the session, such as its length or any special requirements. If there are no additional details, leave it blank.

### Session Details

* **Session Time** (`session_time`, date, required): In this field, select the date and time of the session. Be sure to choose a time that works for all players.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, time, required): Select the time of day for the session. This will help ensure that all players are aware of the timing.
* **Game System** (`game_system`, select_one, required): Choose the game system you will be playing (e.g., Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Other).

### Additional Information

* **Players** (`players`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for listing the names of all players participating in the session. Select as many players as applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments about the session, such as any specific rules or requirements.

### Tips

* Be clear and concise in your submission to ensure that both you and the DM have all the necessary information.
* Check against previous submissions to avoid duplicate sessions.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
