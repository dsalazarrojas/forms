# Gaming Industry Developer Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for developers in the gaming industry to submit nominations for awards. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your nomination is considered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Start by reviewing the form to understand what information is required.
2. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide a brief description of your nomination.
* **Nomination Team** (`nomination_team`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the team that developed the nominated project consisted of members with disabilities.
	+ Select 'No' if the team did not include members with disabilities.
* **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`, note, optional):
	+ You can attach any supporting documents or materials that you feel are relevant to your nomination.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional):
	+ If you are nominating a project, please provide the date it was completed or released.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional):
	+ The date you are submitting this nomination.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional):
	+ The time you are submitting this nomination.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ A valid email address that we can use to contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ A contact phone number that we can use to reach you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
- Attach only relevant supporting materials to avoid cluttering the form with unnecessary information.
