# Modpack Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Modpack Development Survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with modpack development. Your input will help the development team identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the modpack development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the options that best match your experience with modpack development.
2. Provide detailed answers to the open-ended questions.
3. Fill out the form thoroughly and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Modpack Satisfaction** (`modpack_development_survey`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the options that best describe your satisfaction with modpack development.
	+ Satisfied: You are generally pleased with the current state of modpack development.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are not satisfied with the current state of modpack development.
* **Development Challenges** (`modpack_development_challenge`, text, required/optional): Describe the challenges you face while developing modpacks. This will help us understand the difficulties you encounter and how we can improve the development process.
* **Future Feature Ideas** (`modpack_feature_ideas`, text, required/optional): Share your ideas for new features that would improve the modpack development process. This will help us identify areas where we can innovate and enhance the user experience.
* **Satisfaction** (`modpack_satisfaction`, select_one, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with modpack development. (Note: This field is similar to Modpack Satisfaction but allows for a more specific response.)
	+ Satisfied: You are generally pleased with the current state of modpack development.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are not satisfied with the current state of modpack development.
* **Development Comments** (`modpack_dev_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about modpack development. This is your chance to share your thoughts and ideas.
* **Comments** (`modpack_comments`, text, required/optional): Is there anything else you'd like to share or discuss about modpack development? This is your space to provide any additional insights or thoughts you have about the modpack development process.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your responses. Your feedback is invaluable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form! Your input will help us improve the modpack development process.
