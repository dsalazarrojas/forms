# Multiplayer Game Administrator Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Multiplayer Game Administrator Application form is used to gather information from potential moderators and staff members in a game community. This form helps administrators to manage the application process and ensure that all necessary details are collected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your name as the moderator in the "Moderator Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your role from the options provided in the "Role" field.
6. Optionally, enter an application link in the "Application Link" field.
7. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Moderator Name** (`moderator_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the moderator.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the available roles (Admin, Moderator, or Staff).
* **Application Link** (`application_link`, text, required/optional): Enter the link to your application (optional).
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Click to submit your application or reset the form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your application (optional).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your application (optional).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your application (optional).
