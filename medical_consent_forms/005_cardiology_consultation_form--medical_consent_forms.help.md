# Cardiology Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cardiology Consultation Form is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to gather essential information about patients before their consultation. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary details are captured to provide the best possible care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and thoroughly, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the patient's name** (`Patient name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full legal name in this field.
2. **Enter the patient's date of birth** (`Date of birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. **Enter the patient's contact number** (`Contact number`, text, required): Enter the best phone number to reach the patient or their next of kin.
4. **Describe the presenting symptoms** (`Presenting symptoms`, text, required): Briefly describe the patient's current symptoms.
5. **List any current medications** (`Current medications`, text, optional): If applicable, list any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages.
6. **List known allergies** (`Known allergies`, text, optional): If applicable, list any medications or substances the patient is allergic to.
7. **Provide prior cardiac history** (`Prior cardiac history`, text, optional): If applicable, provide a brief history of any previous cardiac-related diagnoses, interventions, or surgeries.
8. **Confirm consent for treatment** (`Consent for treatment`, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm the patient's consent to receive treatment.
9. **Add any additional notes** (`Additional clinical notes`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to add about the patient's medical history, please do so here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with the patient or their healthcare provider.
- Please review the form carefully before submitting it to the healthcare team.
