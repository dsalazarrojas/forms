# Patient Privacy Protection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Privacy Protection Form is a critical document that helps healthcare professionals and patients maintain confidentiality and respect for patient privacy. This form collects essential information to ensure accurate medical records and facilitate smooth patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's identification details: first name, last name, address, and email.
2. Enter the patient's phone number, date of birth, and medical record number.
3. If applicable, provide the doctor's name, medical history, allergies, and medical insurance details.
4. Sign and date the form with your own signature.
5. Sign and date the form with the doctor's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `number`, required): If you have a medical record number, please enter it here.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required): If you are seeing a doctor, please enter their name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe your medical history, including any allergies or previous medical conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required): If you have medical insurance, please enter your policy number or details.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this form to confirm your identity.
* **Doctor's Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `note`, required): Sign this form to confirm your identity as the doctor.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Date of signing this form. 
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Date when form was completed.
