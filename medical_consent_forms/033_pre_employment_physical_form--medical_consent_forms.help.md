# pre_employment_physical_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-employment physical form is used by employers to collect medical information from job applicants before employment decisions are made. This form is for general pre-employment screening purposes and is not intended for clinical or medical diagnosis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your medical information on the first page of the form.
2. Enter your contact information on the second page of the form.
3. Report your medical history on the third page of the form.
4. List any allergies you have on the fourth page of the form.
5. Provide any medical clearances you may have on the fifth page of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required: false): Please enter any medical information you wish to share with the employer.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or injuries.
