# Sample Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Telemedicine Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients before starting telemedicine consultations. This form ensures that patients understand and agree to the terms of their care, which includes the use of technology, sharing of medical information, and the responsibilities of both the patient and the healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Provide your patient's email address in the **Patient Email** field.
3. Enter your patient's date of birth in the **Date Of Birth** field.
4. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **Consent Given** field to indicate whether or not you have given your consent to the telemedicine consultation.
5. If you have given your consent, you will need to provide your signature in the **Signature** field.
6. Enter the date on which you signed the form in the **Signature Date** field.
7. If you have any phone number, please enter it in the **Patient Phone** field.
8. Enter the name of your medical group or organization in the **Medical Group** field.
9. Enter the name of your healthcare provider in the **Provider Name** field.
10. Finally, enter the current date in the **Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  • Example: John Doe
- **Patient Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached by your healthcare provider.
  • Example: johndoe@example.com
- **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
  • Example: 01/01/1990
- **Consent Given**: Select **Yes** if you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of your telemedicine care. Select **No** if you do not agree.
- **Signature**: Sign your name to indicate your consent.
  • Please write your name in this field.
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date on which you signed this form.
  • Format: DD/MM/YYYY
- **Patient Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
  • Example: 123-456-7890
- **Medical Group**: Enter the name of your medical group or organization.
  • Example: ABC Medical Group
- **Provider Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
  • Example: Dr. John Smith
- **Date**: Enter the current date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
  • Example: 10/10/2022

Note: Please ensure that you understand the terms of your telemedicine care before signing this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
