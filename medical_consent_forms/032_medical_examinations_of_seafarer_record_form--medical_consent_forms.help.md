# medical_examinations_of_seafarer_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_examinations_of_seafarer_record_form is designed to record medical examinations for seafarers, ensuring they are fit for duty and providing medical documentation for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start at the beginning and work through it section by section.
2. Ensure that each section is filled in accurately and completely.
3. If any section is not applicable, indicate "NA" (not applicable) and provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seafarer Details** (`seafarer_details`, text, optional): This section is for providing basic information about the seafarer, such as name, job title, and contact details.
* **Medical Examination** (`medical_examination`, text, optional): This section is for recording the results of the medical examination, including any findings or recommendations.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, text, optional): This section is for obtaining the signature of the physician who performed the medical examination, indicating that the examination has been completed.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): This section is for obtaining the signature of the patient, indicating that they have read and understood the examination results.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and legible writing for all entries.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your organization's medical personnel for guidance.
