# medication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication consent form is a document used to obtain a patient's consent for medical treatment or procedures. It ensures that the patient is aware of their medical history, the treatment they will receive, and their rights as a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient information section with your personal details.
2. Read and agree to the medication terms outlined in the second section.
3. Sign and date the consent section, indicating your agreement to the treatment.
4. If you are an emergency contact, fill out the emergency contact information section.
5. Fill out the medical facility details if you are representing a facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (id: 1, text, required: false): This section is for entering your personal details as a patient.
* **Medication Terms** (id: 2, text, required: false): This section outlines the terms and conditions of your medication treatment.
* **Consent** (id: 3, text, required: false): This section is for signing and dating your agreement to the treatment.
* **Patient Signature** (id: 4, text, required: true): Sign your name to indicate your agreement.
* **Health Care Provider Signature** (id: 5, text, required: false): This is not required for patients to sign, but may be used by healthcare providers to sign on behalf of the patient.
* **Date** (id: 6, date, required: false): This is the date of signing the consent form.
* **Medical Facility** (id: 7, text, required: false): This is the name of the medical facility providing the treatment.
* **Medication Information** (id: 8, text, required: false): This section is for detailing any relevant medication information.
* **Medication Side Effects** (id: 9, text, required: false): This section is for noting any potential side effects of the medication.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (id: 10, text, required: false): This section is for providing contact information for an emergency contact.
* **Contact** (id: 11, text, required: false): This is the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Name** (id: 12, text, required: false): This is the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Number** (id: 13, text, required: false): This is the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (id: 14, text, required: false): This is the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person.
* **Address** (id: 15, text, required: false): This is the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (id: 16, email, required: false): This is the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Facility Address** (id: 17, text, required: false): This is the address of the medical facility.
* **Phone** (id: 18, text, required: false): This is the phone number of the medical facility.
* **Email** (id: 19, email, required: false): This is the email of the medical facility.
* **Fax** (id: 20, text, required: false): This is the fax number of the medical facility.
* **Other** (id: 21, text, required: false): This section is for any additional contact information.
* **Other Info** (id: 22, text, required: false): This is any additional information about the contact person or facility.
* **Medical Facility Notes** (id: 23, text, required: false): This section is for any notes about the medical facility.
* **Patient Info Notes** (id: 24, text, required: false): This section is for any notes about the patient.
* **Name** (id: 25, text, required: false): This is the patient's name.
