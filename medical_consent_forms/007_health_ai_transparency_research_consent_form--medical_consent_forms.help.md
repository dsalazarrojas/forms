# Health AI Transparency Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals participating in health AI research studies. It ensures that participants understand the purpose and scope of the research and agree to participate voluntarily.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided text carefully, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions outlined.
2. Fill in your general information, including your Participant ID, Study Name, Researcher Name, and Research Institution.
3. Provide your consent statement, indicating that you have read and understand the above statements.
4. Add the date you sign the consent form.
5. Sign the form by selecting one of the provided options.
6. Provide your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and website (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information**: Enter your Participant ID, Study Name, Researcher Name, and Research Institution. This section helps us gather essential details about you and the research study.
- **Participant ID**: Enter your unique Participant ID to identify you in the study.
- **Study Name**: Enter the name of the study you are participating in.
- **Researcher Name**: Enter the name of the researcher who is conducting the study.
- **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where the study is taking place.
- **Consent Statement**: This section is where you indicate that you have read and understand the above statements. Please choose the correct option to confirm your understanding.
- **Consent Date**: Enter the date you sign this consent form.
- **Consent Signature**: Sign the form by selecting one of the provided options, confirming that you have read and understand the above statements.
- **AI Developer Name** (optional): If you are an AI developer, enter your name and contact information.
- **Study Description** (optional): Provide a brief description of the study if you wish.
- **Research Institution Email**: Enter the email address of the research institution.
- **Participant Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and website (if applicable).
- **Research Institution Phone**: Enter the phone number of the research institution.
- **Research Institution Website** (optional): If the research institution has a website, enter the URL.

## Tips
- Please review the provided text carefully before proceeding.
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research institution or researcher in charge.
