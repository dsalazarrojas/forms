# Pediatrics Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatrics Medical Release Form is designed to collect necessary information from parents or guardians for their child's medical care. This form helps medical staff to understand the child's medical history, allergies, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's general information, including their name and date of birth.
2. Provide details about the child's medical conditions and allergies.
3. Enter the contact information for the child's emergency contact.
4. Sign the form to provide authorization for medical treatment.
5. Date and sign the form to record the consent date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the child's general information.
	+ Please provide any relevant information about the child's medical history.
* **Parent**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the parent's or guardian's name.
	+ Please enter the name of the person who is filling out this form.
* **Guardian Name**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the guardian's name (if different from the parent).
	+ Please enter the name of the guardian who is responsible for the child's care.
* **Guardian Contact**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the guardian's contact information.
	+ Please enter the contact details of the guardian, including phone number and address.
* **Child**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the child's name.
	+ Please enter the child's name.
* **Date of Birth**:
	+ This field is a date picker where you can select the child's date of birth.
	+ Please select the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Medical Conditions**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the child's medical conditions.
	+ Please provide details about any known medical conditions affecting the child.
* **Allergies**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the child's allergies.
	+ Please list any known allergies affecting the child.
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the emergency contact's information.
	+ Please enter the contact details of the emergency contact person.
* **Signature**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can sign and date the form.
	+ Please sign your name to provide authorization for medical treatment.
* **Physician**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the physician's name.
	+ Please enter the name of the physician who will be treating the child.
* **Physician Contact**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the physician's contact information.
	+ Please enter the contact details of the physician.
* **Medical Facility**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the medical facility's name.
	+ Please enter the name of the medical facility where the child will be receiving treatment.
* **Medical Staff**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter the medical staff's name.
	+ Please enter the name of the medical staff who will be treating the child.
* **Authorization**:
	+ This field is a multiple select field where you can choose whether the child can receive medical treatment.
	+ Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate whether the child can receive medical treatment.
* **Consent Date**:
	+ This field is a date field where you can select the consent date.
	+ Please select the date of consent in MM/DD/YYYY format.
