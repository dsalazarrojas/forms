# Emergency Medical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Medical Consent Form is used to provide critical information about a patient's medical emergency situation. This includes details about the patient's medical event, contact information, and other relevant medical history. The form is intended to be used by medical professionals to quickly gather necessary information in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before beginning to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Enter the patient's information on the "patient_info" page.
3. Provide details about the medical event on the "medical_event" page.
4. List the patient's contact and emergency contact information on the "patient_contact", "emergency_contact", and "emergency_contact_relationship" pages.
5. Enter any relevant medical history on the "medical_history" page.
6. List medications and medical services used on the "medications" and "medical_services" pages.
7. Confirm insurance information on the "insurance_info" page (if applicable).
8. Sign and date the "signature_block" and provide witness signature and contact information in the "witness_signature_block".
9. Record the medical event date and time on the "medical_event_date" and "medical_event_time" pages.
10. Enter the location of the medical event on the "medical_event_location" page.
11. Enter the witness's name and contact information on the "witness_name" and "witness_info" pages.
12. List emergency services provided on the "emergency_services" page.
13. Sign the "patient_signature" and provide witness signature in the "witness_signature" page.
14. Enter the emergency contact's name and relationship on the "emergency_contact_name" and "emergency_contact_relationship" pages.
15. Enter the medical facility used on the "medical_facility" page.
16. Enter the patient's date of birth on the "patient_dob" page.
17. Enter the patient's address on the "patient_address" page.
18. Enter the medical event cause on the "medical_event_cause" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`): This is where you will enter the patient's basic details such as name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Medical Event** (`medical_event`): Describe the medical event that occurred, including any symptoms or circumstances surrounding the event.
* **Contact Information** (`patient_contact`): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the patient.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): List any relevant medical history, including medications and medical services used.
* **Meds** (`medications`): List the patient's medications used.
* **Medical Services** (`medical_services`): List any medical services used.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`): Confirm the patient's insurance information, if applicable.
* **Signature Block** (`signature_block`): Sign and date the form as a witness.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature_block`): Sign and date the form as a witness.
* **Medical Event Date** (`medical_event_date`): Enter the date of the medical event.
* **Medical Event Time** (`medical_event_time`): Enter the time of the medical event.
* **Medical Event Location** (`medical_event_location`): Enter the location of the medical event.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`): Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Information** (`witness_info`): Enter the witness's contact information.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`): List any emergency services provided.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign and date the form as a patient or witness.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`): Sign and date the form as a witness.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Enter the relationship of the emergency contact to the patient.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`): Enter the medical facility used.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Event Cause** (`medical_event_cause`): Enter the cause of the medical event.

**Tips**

* Review the form carefully before completing to ensure all necessary information is provided.
* Use legible handwriting to ensure clear readability.
* Use this form in emergency situations only.
* Store the completed form with the patient's medical records.
* Ensure the form is signed and dated by the patient and witness.
