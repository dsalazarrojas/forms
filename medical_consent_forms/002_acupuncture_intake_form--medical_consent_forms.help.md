# Acupuncture Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from patients before they receive acupuncture treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each field accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your acupuncturist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, Select One, Required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where we can send you appointment reminders and instructions.
* **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, Text, Required): Describe the reason for your visit and what you are experiencing.
* **When did symptoms begin?** (`symptom_onset`, Select One, Required): Select when your symptoms started from the options provided.
* **How often do you experience symptoms?** (`symptom_frequency`, Select One, Required): Select how often you experience symptoms from the options provided.
* **Pain Severity on Scale 1-10** (`pain_severity`, Number, Required): Rate your pain level from 1 to 10, where 1 is minimal and 10 is the worst imaginable.
* **Previous treatments attempted** (`past_treatments`, Text, Optional): Describe any previous treatments you have tried for this condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history_section`, Note, Optional): If you have any medical history or conditions that may affect your treatment, please describe them here.
* **Serious illnesses or surgeries** (`serious_illnesses`, Text, Optional): If you have any serious illnesses or surgeries, please describe them here.
* **Current medications and supplements** (`current_medications`, Text, Optional): List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Known allergies** (`allergies`, Text, Optional): If you have any known allergies, please list them here.
* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, Select One, Required): Select if you are pregnant or could be pregnant.
* **Recent vaccinations** (`recent_vaccinations`, Text, Optional): If you have had any recent vaccinations, please list them here.
* **Lifestyle and Additional Information** (`lifestyle_section`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional information about your lifestyle or medical history, please describe them here.
* **Sleep quality and hours per night** (`sleep_quality`, Select One, Optional): Select your sleep quality and hours of sleep per night from the options provided.
* **Current stress level** (`stress_level`, Select One, Optional): Select your current stress level from the options provided.
* **Exercise frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Select your exercise frequency from the options provided.
* **Consent Acknowledgment** (`consent_acknowledgment`, Select One, Required): Select that you have read and understand the risks and benefits of acupuncture treatment.
* **Medical Provider Consent** (`medical_provider_consent`, Select One, Required): Select that you consent to contact your medical provider if necessary.
