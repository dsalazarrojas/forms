# substance_abuse_counseling_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The substance abuse counseling consent form is a crucial document that helps ensure clients understand their rights and responsibilities regarding treatment and disclosure of information. It is used to gather information about the client's consent to counseling, understanding of confidentiality, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and accurately answer the questions.
2. Select the correct responses for each question.
3. Fill out the sections that require your input.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent to Counseling** (`consent_to_counseling`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to counseling, which means you agree to participate in the counseling process. If you do not agree, please answer "no".
* **Understanding of Confidentiality** (`understanding_of_confidentiality`, select_multiple, required): Please select "yes" if you understand that all information shared during counseling sessions will be kept confidential. You may also select "sometimes" if you are unsure, or "no" if you do not understand or disagree with confidentiality.
* **Counseling Process** (`counseling_process`, text, required): Please describe your understanding of the counseling process and what you hope to achieve through it.
* **Counseling Frequency** (`counseling_frequency`, number, optional): If you have a specific idea of how often you would like to attend counseling sessions, please enter the number of sessions you would like to have per week.
* **Substance Abuse History** (`substance_abuse_history`, text, required): Please describe your substance abuse history, including any relevant details about your substance use.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Please describe your medical history, including any relevant details about your health conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you have an emergency contact person, "no" if you do not have one.
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_to_contact`, select_multiple, required): Please select "yes" if you consent to be contacted in case of an emergency or other urgent matter. You may also select "sometimes" if you are unsure, or "no" if you do not consent.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Consent to Medical Treatment** (`consent_to_medical_treatment`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to medical treatment, "no" if you do not.
* **Medical Treatment Details** (`medical_treatment_details`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about medical treatment, please describe them.
* **Consent to Testing** (`consent_to_testing`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to testing, "no" if you do not.
* **Testing Details** (`testing_details`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about testing, please describe them.
* **Consent to Other Services** (`consent_to_other_services`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to other services, "no" if you do not.
* **Other Services** (`other_services`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about other services, please describe them.
* **Consent to Share Results** (`consent_to_share_results`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to sharing results, "no" if you do not.
* **Consent to Disclosure** (`consent_to_disclosure`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to disclosure, "no" if you do not.
* **Disclosure Details** (`disclosure_details`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about disclosure, please describe them.
* **Consent to Release Information** (`consent_to_release_info`, select_one, required): Please answer "yes" if you consent to release information, "no" if you do not.
* **Release Information** (`release_info`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about release information, please describe them.
* **Consent to Other Organizations** (`consent_to_other_organizations`, select_one, optional): Please answer "yes" if you consent to other organizations, "no" if you do not.
* **Other Organizations** (`other_organizations`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about other organizations, please describe them.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
