# Hospitalization Authorization Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hospitalization Authorization Request form is a crucial document that allows patients to request hospitalization. It collects essential information from the patient, their family or representatives, and medical staff to ensure a smooth hospitalization process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Details** (`id: 12`, `name: patient`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name in the space provided.

2. **Patient Approvals** (`id: 2`, `name: patient_approvals`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the individuals authorized to make decisions on the patient's behalf, such as family members or friends.

3. **Admission Info** (`id: 3`, `name: admission_info`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the patient's admission, such as the reason for hospitalization or any previous medical conditions.

4. **Date of Stay** (`id: 4`, `name: date_of_stay`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date the patient is expected to stay in the hospital.

5. **Hospitalization Reason** (`id: 5`, `name: hospitalization_reason`, `type: text`, required: false): Specify the reason for the patient's hospitalization.

6. **Medical History** (`id: 6`, `name: medical_history`, `type: text`, required: false): Record any relevant medical history, including allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.

8. **Emergency Contact Info** (`id: 8`, `name: emergency_contact`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide contact information for the patient's emergency contact, including name, phone number, and relationship.

9. **Hospital Info** (`id: 9`, `name: hospital_info`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the hospital's name, address, and phone number where the patient will be staying.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays or complications.
* If you're unsure about any information, don't hesitate to reach out to the patient's medical staff or hospital administration.
* This form is only for patients who have not been admitted to the hospital before, or for those who need to update their previous hospitalization information.
* If you've already submitted a hospitalization request for this patient, please do not resubmit.
