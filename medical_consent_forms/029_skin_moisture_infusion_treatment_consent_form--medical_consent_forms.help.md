# skin_moisture_infusion_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain a client's consent for a skin moisture infusion treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Client Details** section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the questions in the **Health Information** section by providing a brief overview of your medical history and health concerns.
3. In the **treatment_consent** section, select the correct option to indicate your consent for the treatment. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
4. Read and sign the **Signed Consent** section, indicating that you have understood and agreed to the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Client Details**
* **Client Details**: This section collects your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about you. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.

### **Health Information**
* **Health Information**: This section is for you to provide a brief overview of your medical history and any health concerns you may have. Please keep your response concise and clear.

### **treatment_consent**
* **treatment_consent**: In this section, you will select whether or not you consent to the skin moisture infusion treatment. Please choose the correct option to indicate your consent.

### **Signed Consent**
* **Signed Consent**: Please read and sign this section to indicate that you have understood and agreed to the treatment. This is a required field to ensure that you are aware of the treatment and its implications.
