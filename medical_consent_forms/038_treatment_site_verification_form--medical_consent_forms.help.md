# treatment_site_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treatment site verification form is a crucial document used to confirm the details of a patient's treatment site. This form helps healthcare professionals ensure that the necessary information is accurately documented, which is essential for maintaining accurate patient records and facilitating effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's ID in the "Patient ID" field.
3. Enter the site name in the "Site Name" field.
4. Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field.
5. Enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "Provider Name" field.
6. Enter the patient's condition in the "Patient Condition" field.
7. Enter the site location in the "Site Location" field.
8. If available, enter the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact" field.
9. Select the verification method used (in-person, phone, or email) in the "Verification Method" field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's unique identification number.
* **Site Name**: Enter the name of the treatment site where the patient received care.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date the patient received treatment at the site.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated the patient.
* **Patient Condition**: Briefly describe the patient's condition or reason for treatment.
* **Site Location**: Enter the location of the treatment site (e.g., hospital, clinic, or facility).
* **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information, if available.
* **Verification Method**: Select the method used to verify the treatment site (in-person, phone, or email).
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's treatment site.
