# Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain participant consent for the Medical Technology Innovation Showcase. It captures user information, medical technology information, and other relevant details to ensure participants understand the scope and terms of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information (page 1).
2. Provide details about the Medical Technology Showcase on page 2.
3. Confirm your consent and provide any additional contact information if required.
4. Review the Agreement and Terms on the last page.
5. Select the correct options for the Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form.
6. Finally, provide any additional event information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **User Information (`user_info`, text, optional):**
	+ This section is used to capture your details as the participant of the Medical Technology Innovation Showcase.
	+ Please provide your name and any other relevant information you'd like to share about yourself.

### Showcase Information
* **Showcase Information (`showcase_info`, text, optional):**
	+ Describe the medical technology you will be showcasing at the event.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your innovation, including its name and any relevant features or benefits.

### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, optional):**
	+ Please select "Yes" if you consent to the terms and conditions of the event.
	+ You may also select "No" if you are not able to provide consent.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, optional):**
	+ If you'd like to provide additional contact information, such as an email or phone number, this is the place to do so.
	+ This information will help us get in touch with you if needed.

### Medical Technology Information
* **Medical Technology Information (`medical_technology_info`, text, optional):**
	+ Describe the medical technology you will be showcasing at the event.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your innovation, including its name and any relevant features or benefits.

### Agreement and Terms
* **Agreement and Terms (`agreement_terms`, text, optional):**
	+ Read through the terms and conditions of the event carefully.
	+ If you have any questions or concerns, please ask a member of the team.

### Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form
* **Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form (`medical_technology_innovation_showcase_consent_form`, select_multiple, optional):**
	+ Please select "Yes" if you consent to the terms and conditions of the event.
	+ You may also select "No" if you are not able to provide consent.

### Event Information
* **Event Information (`event_info`, text, optional):**
	+ Provide any additional information about the event, such as date and time or location.
	+ This information will help us prepare for the event.

### Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form (again)
* **Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form (`medical_technology_innovation_showcase_consent_form`, select_multiple, optional):**
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm your participation and consent to the terms and conditions of the event.
	+ Please note that this is a duplicate field and should only be selected if you are certain of your consent.
