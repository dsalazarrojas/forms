# Testosterone Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for testosterone treatment, which is a hormone replacement therapy that may have risks and side effects. To use this form, please ensure you are a relevant user (e.g., a healthcare professional) and not a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the user's name, date of birth, and medical group information.
2.  Provide the doctor's name and contact information (phone and email).
3.  Check the box to indicate that you have understood the risks and side effects of testosterone therapy and that you are aware of potential interactions with other medical conditions.
4.  Add any additional notes about the patient's medical history or treatment plan.
5.  Enter the patient's email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name.
    Please provide the patient's full name as it should appear on their medical records.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
    This field will help us keep track of the patient's age and medical history.
*   **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the medical group or practice where the patient is receiving treatment.
    This field is for administrative purposes and will help us route the patient to the correct department or team.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the doctor or healthcare provider who will be treating the patient.
    This field should match the name of the doctor who will be administering the treatment.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, select multiple, required: false): Check the box next to each of the following statements to indicate your understanding of the risks and side effects of testosterone therapy.
    *   I understand that testosterone therapy may have risks and side effects.
    *   I understand that hormone replacement may interfere with other medical conditions.
*   **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's medical history or treatment plan.
    These notes will be kept confidential and only accessible to healthcare providers.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
    We will use this email to send reminders or updates about the patient's treatment.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
    We will use this number to reach out to the patient with updates or reminders.
*   **Medical Group Location** (`medical_group_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the medical group or practice where the patient will receive treatment.
    This field is for administrative purposes and will help us route the patient to the correct department or team.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's medical history and treatment plan.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance from the healthcare team.
*   This form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals only. If you are not a healthcare provider, please do not use this form.
