# Complementary Medicine Treatment Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain the patient's consent for complementary medicine treatment. This form will guide the patient through an informed consent process, ensuring they understand the proposed treatment, its potential benefits and risks, and their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please answer all required fields.
2.  Review each section carefully before proceeding.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
4.  Sign and date the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic or Practice Name**: Please enter the name of the facility where you will be receiving treatment.
* **Practitioner Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be treating you.
* **Practitioner Credentials**: List the qualifications and licenses of your healthcare provider.
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your mailing address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact to you.
* **Type of Treatment**: Select the type of complementary medicine treatment you are receiving.
* **Treatment Description**: Describe the proposed treatment in detail.
* **Purpose of Treatment**: Describe the condition or issue being treated.
* **Expected Treatment Duration**: Estimate the length of time you will need treatment.
* **Treatment Frequency**: Select how often you will receive treatment.
* **Benefits Explained**: Confirm that you have been informed about the potential benefits of the treatment.
* **Risks Explained**: Confirm that you have been informed about the potential risks of the treatment.
* **Alternatives Explained**: Confirm that you have been informed about alternative options.
* **Potential Benefits**: Describe any benefits you expect from the treatment.
* **Potential Risks**: Describe any potential risks or complications associated with the treatment.
* **Current Medical Conditions**: List any health conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Pregnant or Nursing**: Confirm if you are pregnant or nursing.
* **Conventional Treatment**: Confirm if you are receiving conventional treatment.
* **Provider Communication**: Confirm if you want your healthcare provider to contact your conventional treatment provider.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Confirm that you consent to the proposed treatment.
* **Withdrawal Rights**: Confirm that you understand your right to withdraw consent.
* **Financial Responsibility**: Confirm that you accept financial responsibility for the treatment.
* **Privacy Policy**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign your name as the patient.
* **Signature Date**: Date the form after signing.
* **Witness Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of a witness, if applicable.
* **Witness Signature**: (Optional) Enter the witness's signature, if applicable.
* **Guardian Signature**: (Optional) Enter the guardian's signature, if applicable.
