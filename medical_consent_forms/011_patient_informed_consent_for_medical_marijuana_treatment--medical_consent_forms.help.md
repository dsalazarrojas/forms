# Patient Informed Consent For Medical Marijuana Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for medical marijuana treatment. It ensures that patients understand the potential risks and benefits associated with medical marijuana use and agree to its use in their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your doctor's name and contact information in the fields provided.
4. Explain why you are requesting medical marijuana treatment in the "Reason For Use" field.
5. List any medical conditions you are being treated for in the "Medical Condition" field.
6. List any potential side effects you are aware of in the "Potential Side Effects" field.
7. Specify the duration and frequency of treatment in the "Treatment Duration" and "Treatment Frequency" fields.
8. Provide any medical history or allergies in the "Medical History" field.
9. Enter the contact information for your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
10. Sign and date the consent statement in the "Signed Date" field.
11. Sign your name and the doctor's name in the corresponding fields.
12. Ensure that the doctor's name and institution are printed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Patient Name (<name>) * 
- Enter your full name as it appears on your medical record.

### 2. Patient Address (<address>) * 
- Enter your home address or contact address where you can be reached.

### 3. Patient Phone (<phone>) (Optional) 
- Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### 4. Patient Email (<email>) * 
- Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### 5. Doctor Name (<name>) * 
- Enter the name of your treating doctor.

### 6. Doctor Title (<title>) * 
- Enter the title of your treating doctor (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).

### 7. Reason For Use (<reason_for_use>) * 
- Provide a clear explanation of why you are requesting medical marijuana treatment.

### 8. Medical Condition (<medical_condition>) * 
- List any medical conditions you are being treated for.

### 9. Potential Side Effects (<potential_side_effects>) * 
- List any potential side effects you are aware of.

### 10. Treatment Duration (<treatment_duration>) * 
- Specify the length of time you will be receiving medical marijuana treatment.

### 11. Treatment Frequency (<treatment_frequency>) * 
- Specify how often you will receive medical marijuana treatment.

### 12. Medical History (<medical_history>) * 
- Provide any relevant medical history or allergies.

### 13. Allergies (<allergies>) (Optional) 
- List any allergies you have.

### 14. Emergency Contact (<emergency_contact>) * 
- Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.

### 15. Consent Statement (<consent_statement>) * 
- Review and sign the consent statement.

### 16. Signed Date (<signed_date>) * 
- Date and sign the consent statement.

### 17. Signature (<signature>) * 
- Sign your name.

### 18. Doctor Signature (<signature>) * 
- Sign your name as a doctor.

### 19. Doctor Name Printed (<doctor_name_printed>) * 
- Ensure that your name is printed correctly.

### 20. Doctor Institution (<doctor_institution>) * 
- Enter the name of your doctor's institution.

### 21. Date (<date>) * 
- Date and sign the consent statement.

### 22. Medical License Number (<medical_license_number>) * 
- Enter your doctor's medical license number.

### 23. Doctor License Number (<doctor_license_number>) * 
- Enter your doctor's license number.

### 24. Patient Signature (<patient_signature>) * 
- Sign your name.

## Tips

- Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- This form is a legal document and should be treated as such.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please consult your doctor or healthcare provider.
