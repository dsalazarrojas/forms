# Telemedicine Consent Form Florida - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for telemedicine services in Florida. It is essential for healthcare providers to have the necessary consent and contact information to deliver these services effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the `patient_name` field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field.
3. Share your medical history in the `medical_history` field.
4. Enter your contact information in the `contact_information` field.
5. If you are using telemedicine services, select "Yes" or "No" in the `telemedicine_service_provider` field.
6. Sign your electronic signature in the `electronic_signature` field.
7. If you have visited any medical facilities for treatment, please select all that apply from the `medical_facility` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or identification document.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **medical_history**: Provide a brief summary of any medical conditions, allergies, or previous illnesses that may impact your treatment.
* **contact_information**: Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **telemedicine_service_provider**: If you are using telemedicine services, please answer "Yes" or "No".
* **electronic_signature**: Sign your electronic signature by typing your name in the provided field.
* **medical_facility**: Please select all the medical facilities you have visited in the past or are currently visiting. For example, if you have visited 1st Medical Facility and 3rd Medical Facility, select both options.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
- Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
