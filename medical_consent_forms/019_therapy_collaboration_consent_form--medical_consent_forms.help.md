# Therapy Collaboration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Collaboration Consent Form is a document that helps clients understand and agree to the sharing of their information with their practitioners. This form ensures that clients are aware of what information will be shared and with whom it will be shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the name of your practitioner in the "Practitioner Name" field.
3. List the information that will be shared with your practitioner in the "Information to be Shared" field.
4. Enter the date you want to start sharing information in the "Date" field.
5. Select who will be sharing information with your practitioner in the "Shared With" field.
6. Choose your consent status ("Accepted", "Declined", or "Pending") in the "Consent Status" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
9. Enter your practitioner's phone number in the "Practitioner Phone" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the email address of who will be sharing information with your practitioner in the "Shared With Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the client who is providing consent.
* **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the practitioner with whom the client's information will be shared.
* **Information to be Shared** (`shared_info`, `text`, required): This is the specific information that will be shared with the practitioner (e.g., medical history, treatment plans, etc.).
* **Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): This is the date you want to start sharing information with the practitioner.
* **Shared With** (`shared_with`, `select_multiple`, required): This is the list of practitioners with whom the client's information will be shared (e.g., John, Jane, etc.).
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, `select_one`, required): This is the client's consent status (e.g., Accepted, Declined, Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): This is an optional field for adding any additional comments or notes.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): This is the client's phone number.
* **Practitioner Phone** (`practitioner_phone`, `text`, required): This is the practitioner's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the client's email address.
* **Shared With Email** (`shared_with_email`, `email`, required): This is the email address of the practitioner with whom the client's information will be shared.
