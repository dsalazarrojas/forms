# procedure_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for medical procedures. It is essential to complete this form to ensure that patients are aware of the risks and benefits of the procedure and to provide a legal record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the patient.
2. Describe your medical condition.
3. Provide a detailed description of the procedure.
4. List the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
5. Enter the date you consent to the procedure.
6. Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the procedure.
7. Enter the name of the facility where the procedure will be performed.
8. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
9. Sign the form as the healthcare provider to confirm your consent is obtained.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, required): Enter your full name as the patient.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, required): Describe your current medical condition.
- **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`, required): Provide a detailed description of the procedure.
- **Risks and Benefits** (`risks_benefits`, required): List the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, required): Enter the date you consent to the procedure.
- **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the procedure.
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, required): Enter the name of the facility where the procedure will be performed.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, required): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
- **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, required): Sign the form as the healthcare provider to confirm your consent is obtained.
- **Consented By** (`consented_to_by`, required): Enter "yes" to confirm you have read and understand the form and its contents.

Note: Please ensure that you have read and understood the form and its contents before signing. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider.
