# Pregnancy Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pregnancy Waiver Form is a simple, one-page form used to gather information from patients about their pregnancy status and the purpose of their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Patient Name
- Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID, or your alias/ nickname (if it's different from your official name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This is your personal name as it appears on your government-issued ID, or your alias/nickname if it's different from your official name.

### **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit.

### **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign your name to confirm that you are providing accurate information.

### **Are You Pregnant?** (`is_pregnant`, select_multiple, optional): Select one of the following options:
- **False**: You are not pregnant.
- **Unsure**: You are unsure or do not know if you are pregnant.
- **True**: You are pregnant.

### **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for your visit.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information about your pregnancy status and the reason for your visit.
- If you are unsure about your pregnancy status, select "Unsure" to indicate that you do not know.
- This form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have any health concerns, please consult a medical professional.
