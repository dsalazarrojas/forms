# Tubal Ligation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain patient consent and gather relevant information about the patient's medical history, the tubal ligation procedure, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
