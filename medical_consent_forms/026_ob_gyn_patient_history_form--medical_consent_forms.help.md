> # Ob Gyn Patient History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ob Gyn Patient History Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information about a patient's medical history, including obstetric care-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the patient's general information on page 1, including patient name, contact details, and age.
2. On page 2, provide details about the patient's medical history, such as allergies, medications, and medical conditions.
3. On page 3, report any family medical history, pregnancy information, and obstetric history.
4. On page 4, indicate any medical allergies, medical conditions, and medications that the patient is taking.
5. On page 5, provide details about the patient's consent to share medical information.
6. Finally, provide the obstetric history date and time, and any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Page 1):**
	+ **Patient Name** (`text`): Enter the patient's full name.
	+ **Phone** (`text`): Enter the patient's phone number.
	+ **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address.
	+ **Address** (`text`): Enter the patient's address.
	+ **Medical Contact Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the medical contact person.
	+ **Medical Contact Number** (`text`): Enter the number of the medical contact person.
* **Medical History (Page 2):**
	+ **Medical Allergies** (`text`): List any known medical allergies.
	+ **Meds** (`text`): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
	+ **Medical Conditions** (`text`): List any existing medical conditions.
* **Family Medical History (Page 3):**
	+ **Pregnancy Info** (`text`): Provide details about the patient's pregnancy.
* **Obstetric History (Page 3):**
	+ **Obstetric History Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the obstetric history.
	+ **Obstetric History Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the obstetric history.
* **Additional Info (Page 4):**
	+ **Medical Conditions** (`text`): List any additional medical conditions.
* **Consent (Page 5):**
	+ **Patient Consent** (`select_multiple`): Select one of the available options to indicate the patient's consent to share medical information.
* **Additional Notes (Page 5):**
	+ **Obstetric History Notes** (`note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's obstetric history.

Note: Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the healthcare provider has all necessary information to provide proper care and treatment to the patient.
