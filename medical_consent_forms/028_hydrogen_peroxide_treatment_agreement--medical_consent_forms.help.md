# Hydrogen Peroxide Treatment Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a medical consent form for patients undergoing hydrogen peroxide treatment. It is intended to gather information about the patient's medical history, contact information, and consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. **Patient Information**: Please provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
2. **Medical History**: Please describe your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions you have.
3. **Consent**: Please select whether you do or do not consent to the treatment.
4. **Contact Information**: Please select the individual or organization who will be responsible for your care during the treatment.
5. **Signature**: Please provide a note about your signature, but note that this field is not intended for a physical signature.
6. **Medical Procedure**: Please provide details about the medical procedure you are undergoing.
7. **Treatment Information**: Please provide any relevant information about the treatment you will receive.
8. **Medical Conditions**: Please list any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to the treatment.
9. **Allergies**: Please list any allergies you have that may be relevant to the treatment.
10. **Medications**: Please list any medications you are currently taking.
11. **Medical Procedure Details**: Please provide any additional details about the medical procedure.
12. **Date**: Please provide the date you sign this agreement.
13. **Time**: Please provide the time you sign this agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: This section is for your personal details and contact information. Please ensure you provide accurate information. (Patient Information, text, required: false)
* **Medical History**: This section is for you to describe your medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions you have. Please be honest about your medical history. (Medical History, text, required: false)
* **Consent**: This is to indicate whether you agree or disagree with the treatment. Please select "I do" if you consent. (Consent, select_one, required: false)
* **Contact Information**: This section is for you to select who will be responsible for your care during the treatment. Please select "Patient" if you will be responsible for your care. (Contact Information, select_multiple, required: false)
* **Signature**: This section is not for physical signature. Please provide a note about your signature, for example, "I have read and understand this agreement." (Signature, note, required: false)
* **Medical Procedure**: This section is for you to describe the medical procedure you are undergoing. Please provide details about the procedure. (Medical Procedure, text, required: false)
* **Treatment Information**: This section is for you to provide any relevant information about the treatment you will receive. Please be accurate. (Treatment Information, text, required: false)
* **Medical Conditions**: This section is for you to list any medical conditions you have that may be relevant to the treatment. Please list all relevant conditions. (Medical Conditions, text, required: false)
* **Allergies**: This section is for you to list any allergies you have that may be relevant to the treatment. Please list all relevant allergies. (Allergies, text, required: false)
* **Medications**: This section is for you to list any medications you are currently taking. Please list all medications. (Medications, text, required: false)
* **Medical Procedure Details**: This section is for you to provide any additional details about the medical procedure. Please provide accurate details. (Medical Procedure Details, text, required: false)
* **Date**: This section is for you to provide the date you sign this agreement. Please provide the date accurately. (Date, date, required: false)
* **Time**: This section is for you to provide the time you sign this agreement. Please provide the time accurately. (Time, time, required: false)

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory, but it's still important to provide accurate information in these fields for the best possible outcome of the treatment.
