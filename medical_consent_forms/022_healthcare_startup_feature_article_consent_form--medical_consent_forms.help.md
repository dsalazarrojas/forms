# HealthCare Startup Feature Article Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and consent from individuals whose features may be showcased in a healthcare startup article. It will help ensure that their privacy and confidentiality are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For the "Is the article about you?" field, select "True" if the article indeed features you, and "False" otherwise.
3. For the "Give consent for this article" field, select "True" if you consent to the publication of the article, and "False" otherwise.
4. Enter your first name, last name, email, phone, and address as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the article about you** (`feature_article_consent`, select_one, required: false): Please select "True" if the article features you, and "False" if it does not.
* **Give consent for this article** (`consent_given`, select_one, required: false): Select "True" to indicate that you consent to the publication of the article.
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.

## Tips
* Please answer the "Is the article about you" field truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification from the healthcare startup team.
* Once you've completed and submitted the form, you'll receive a confirmation from the healthcare startup.
