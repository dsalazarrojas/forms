# Acupuncture Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from patients before undergoing acupuncture treatment. It's essential to fill out this form to ensure that you understand the risks and benefits of the treatment and provide your consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand what's required.
3. Sign and date the form when you've finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (Patient name, text, **required**): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Medical history summary** (Medical history summary, text, **required**): Provide a brief summary of your major medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Acknowledgement of treatment risks** (Acknowledgement of treatment risks, note, **required**): Read and confirm that you understand the risks associated with acupuncture treatment.
* **Consent to acupuncture treatment** (Consent to acupuncture treatment, select_one, **required**): Select "I consent" to indicate your agreement to receive acupuncture treatment.
* **Emergency contact name and relation** (Emergency contact name and relation, text, **required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact and their relation to you (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Practitioner notes and recommendations** (Practitioner notes and recommendations, text, optional): This is for the clinician's use, so you don't need to fill this out unless required by your practitioner.
* **Signature name** (Signature name, text, **required**): Enter your typed name as your signature.
* **Date signed** (Date signed, date, **required**): Enter the date you're signing this form in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your consent is valid.
* Make sure to review each section before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your practitioner for clarification.
