# Financial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Consent Form is a document that captures the required information to obtain a patient's consent for medical services and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Medical Practitioner" field with the name and contact information of the medical professional providing the service.
2. In the "Practitioner License Number" field, enter the license number of the medical practitioner.
3. Enter the "Patient Name" field with the patient's full name.
4. Provide the "Patient Address" field with the patient's address and any other relevant contact information.
5. In the "Fee Description" field, describe the services or procedures that the patient will receive and the corresponding fee.
6. Specify the "Payment Terms" in the corresponding field, including payment methods and any late payment penalties.
7. Have the patient sign in the "Patient Signature" field to indicate their acceptance of the financial terms.
8. Finally, have the "Practitioner Signature" field signed by the medical practitioner to confirm their agreement with the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Practitioner**: Enter the name and contact information of the medical professional providing the service. This is the contact point for any further inquiries or communication.
* **Practitioner License Number**: Enter the license number of the medical practitioner, which is essential for identifying the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name, ensuring accurate identification and contact information.
* **Patient Address**: Provide the patient's address and any other relevant contact information to ensure efficient communication and follow-up.
* **Fee Description**: Describe the services or procedures that the patient will receive, along with the corresponding fee.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment methods, due dates, and any late payment penalties.
* **Patient Signature**: The patient must sign this field to indicate their acceptance of the financial terms.
* **Practitioner Signature**: The medical practitioner must sign this field to confirm their agreement with the patient.

## Tips
* Always ensure accuracy and legibility of all entered information.
* Be transparent about the services and fees to avoid any confusion or disputes.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure understanding and agreement.
* Maintain a signed copy of the form for future reference.
