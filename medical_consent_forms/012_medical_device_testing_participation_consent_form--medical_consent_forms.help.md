# Medical Device Testing Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that helps ensure patients understand and provide informed consent for medical device testing. It collects information related to testing participation, including patient contact details, testing device information, and testing procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient information in the Patient Information section.
2. Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number, in the Contact Information section.
3. Provide details about the testing device, including the type, model, manufacturer, and serial number, in the Testing Device Details section.
4. Specify the testing procedures and testing date and time in the Testing Procedures and Testing Date sections.
5. Provide any additional notes about your participation and testing device in the Participant Notes and Testing Device Notes sections.
6. Confirm the testing status and provide any additional notes in the Testing Status and Testing Status Notes sections.
7. Finally, provide the study title and description if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information (page 1)
* **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant patient information.
### 2. Contact Information (page 2)
* **Contact Person**: Enter the person's name and title who will be the primary contact for this testing participation.
