# Patient Education Animation Voiceover Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Education Animation Voiceover Consent Form is used to gather information about the patient's consent for voiceover animation or educational material. This form helps patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the animation or material.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read and understand the form's purpose and questions.
2.  Fill in the required fields (if any) with accurate and clear information.
3.  Make sure to answer the questions truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Information** (`form_information`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the animation or educational material being presented.
*   **Animation Description** (`animation_description`, text, optional): This field should provide a brief description of the animation or educational material.
*   **Animation Length** (`animation_length`, number, optional): This field is used to record the length of the animation or educational material, in minutes or seconds.
*   **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, date, optional): This field should be filled in with the date when the animation or educational material was recorded.
*   **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): This field should include the name of the healthcare provider who recorded the animation or educational material.
*   **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select one, optional): This field is used to record the patient's consent, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): This field should be filled in with the date when the patient provided their consent.
*   **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`, text, optional): This field should be signed by the patient to acknowledge their consent.
*   **Animation Type** (`animation_type`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the type of animation or educational material being presented.
*   **Animation Language** (`animation_language`, text, optional): This field should be filled in with the language of the animation or educational material.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This field should include the name of the patient.
*   **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, optional): This field should be signed by the healthcare provider.
*   **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, optional): This field should be filled in with the location where the animation or educational material was recorded.
*   **Recording Device** (`recording_device`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the device used to record the animation or educational material.
*   **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, text, optional): This field is used to record the status of the patient's consent.
*   **Animation ID** (`animation_id`, text, optional): This field is used to track the unique ID of the animation or educational material.
*   **Animation URL** (`animation_url`, text, optional): This field should be filled in with the URL of the animation or educational material.
*   **Animation Password** (`animation_password`, text, optional): This field should be filled in with the password of the animation or educational material.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the healthcare provider for assistance.
