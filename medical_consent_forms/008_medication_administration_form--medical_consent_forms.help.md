# Medication Administration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Administration Form is designed to document medication administration data for patients, ensuring accurate and standardized reporting. This form captures essential details about the patient, medication, and administration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Patient Name** in the field provided. If the patient's name is not known, please indicate this as "Unknown".
2. Enter the **Date of Administration** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD). This is the date the medication was administered to the patient.
3. Enter the **Medication Name** in the field provided. This is the name of the medication given to the patient.
4. Enter the **Dose** of the medication administered to the patient.
5. Select the **Route of Administration** from the list provided (e.g., IV, PO, etc.). This specifies how the medication was given to the patient.
6. Enter the **Time of Administration** in the time format (HH:MM:SS). This is the time the medication was administered.
7. Enter the **Medical Condition** being treated with the medication.
8. Enter the name of the person who **Administered** the medication.
9. Please have the **Patient Signature** in the field provided, confirming that they have read and understood the administration details.
10. Have the **Healthcare Provider Signature** sign in the field, confirming that the medication was administered as recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date of Administration**: Enter the date the medication was administered to the patient, in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Medication Name**: Enter the name of the medication given to the patient, including any brand or generic names.
* **Dose**: Enter the amount of medication administered to the patient.
* **Route of Administration**: Select the route through which the medication was administered (e.g., IV, PO, etc.).
* **Time of Administration**: Enter the time the medication was administered, in the format (HH:MM:SS).
* **Medical Condition**: Briefly describe the medical condition being treated with the medication.
* **Administered by**: Enter the name of the person who administered the medication.
* **Patient Signature**: This field is for the patient to sign and date, confirming they have read and understood the administration details.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: This field is for the healthcare provider to sign and date, confirming the medication was administered as recorded.
