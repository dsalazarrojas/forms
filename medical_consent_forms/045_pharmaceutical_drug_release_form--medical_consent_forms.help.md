# Pharmaceutical Drug Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to release pharmaceutical drugs from a facility to a user. It is a critical document that ensures the correct release of drugs to authorized personnel. Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to prevent errors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Make sure to include all necessary fields.
3. Check that the form is signed by the authorized personnel at the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, required: false): This field is for the name of the user picking up the drug. Please enter the name of the user as it appears on their ID or official documentation.
* **Release Date** (2, date, required: false): This field is for the date of the release. Please enter the date the drug is being released from the facility.
* **Facility** (3, text, required: false): This field is for the name of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the name of the facility as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Prescriber Name** (4, text, required: false): This field is for the name of the prescriber who authorized the release of the drug. Please enter the name of the prescriber as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Prescriber Role** (5, select_one, required: false): This field is for the role of the prescriber who authorized the release of the drug. Please select one of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Prescriber License Number** (6, text, required: false): This field is for the license number of the prescriber who authorized the release of the drug. Please enter the license number as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Facility Type** (7, select_one, required: false): This field is for the type of facility releasing the drug. Please select one of the options: Pharmacy, Clinic, Lab, Distributor, or Hospital.
* **Drug Name** (8, text, required: false): This field is for the name of the drug being released. Please enter the name of the drug as it appears on the label or official documentation.
* **Dosage** (9, text, required: false): This field is for the dosage instructions of the drug. Please enter the dosage as it appears on the label or official documentation.
* **Quantity** (10, text, required: false): This field is for the quantity of the drug being released. Please enter the quantity as it appears on the label or official documentation.
* **Unit Price** (11, number, required: false): This field is for the unit price of the drug. Please enter the price as it appears on the label or official documentation.
* **Total Price** (12, number, required: false): This field is for the total price of the drug. Please enter the total price as it appears on the label or official documentation.
* **Prescriber Signature** (13, text, required: false): This field is for the prescriber's signature who authorized the release of the drug. Please obtain a signature from the prescriber.
* **User Signature** (14, text, required: false): This field is for the user's signature who is picking up the drug. Please obtain a signature from the user.
* **Facility Signature** (15, text, required: false): This field is for the facility's signature who is releasing the drug. Please obtain a signature from the facility.
* **Release Note** (16, text, required: false): This field is for any additional notes about the release. Please enter any relevant information about the release.
* **Facility Name** (17, text, required: false): This field is for the name of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the name of the facility as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Contact Number** (18, text, required: false): This field is for the contact number of the facility releasing the drug. Please include phone number or email.
* **Contact Person** (19, text, required: false): This field is for the contact person of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the name of the person as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Facility Address** (20, text, required: false): This field is for the address of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the address as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Facility City** (21, text, required: false): This field is for the city of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the city as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Facility State** (22, text, required: false): This field is for the state of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the state as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Facility Zip** (23, text, required: false): This field is for the zip code of the facility releasing the drug. Please enter the zip code as it appears on their official documentation.
* **Release Date Time** (24, date, required: false): This field is for the date and time of the release. Please enter the date and time as it appears on the release documentation.
* **Release Time** (25, time, required: false): This field is for the time of the release. Please enter the time as it appears on the release documentation.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out.
- Review the form for any errors before submitting.
- Keep the form for record-keeping purposes.
