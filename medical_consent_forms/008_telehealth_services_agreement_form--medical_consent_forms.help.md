# Telehealth Services Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Services Agreement Form is designed to gather essential information from patients who are going to receive telehealth services. This form ensures that we have all the necessary details to provide proper care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your full name.
2.  Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3.  Select your preferred contact method for telehealth services (phone, email, or online).
4.  Indicate whether you are 18 years or older.
5.  Provide a detailed list of your medical history (e.g., allergies, conditions, medications).
6.  Confirm that you consent to share your medical history with our team.
7.  Indicate whether you have any emergency contact information (e.g., family member or caregiver).
8.  Provide phone numbers of your emergency contacts separated by commas.
9.  Provide addresses of your emergency contacts separated by commas.
10. Provide names of your emergency contacts separated by commas.
11.  Indicate whether you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in telehealth services.
12. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your name?** (`telehealth_services_agreement_form`, text, required): Provide your full name.
*   **What is your date of birth?** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **What is your preferred contact method for telehealth services?** (`telehealth_services_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or online).
*   **Are you 18 years or older?** (`patient_age_verification`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are 18 years or older.
*   **What is your medical history (e.g., allergies, conditions, medications)?** (`patient_medical_history`, text, required): Provide a detailed list of your medical history.
*   **Do you consent to share your medical history with our team?** (`patient_consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to share your medical history.
*   **Do you have any emergency contact information (e.g., family member or caregiver)?** (`patient_contact_info`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have any emergency contact information.
*   **Do you have any emergency contact phone numbers?** (`patient_contact_info_phone`, text, required): Provide phone numbers of your emergency contacts separated by commas.
*   **Do you have any emergency contact addresses?** (`patient_contact_info_address`, text, required): Provide addresses of your emergency contacts separated by commas.
*   **What are your emergency contact names?** (`patient_contact_info_name`, text, required): Provide names of your emergency contacts separated by commas.
*   **Do you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in telehealth services?** (`patient_medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate.
*   **What is your email address?** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   You may need to provide additional information if any of your medical conditions change.
*   You are required to provide an email address for contact and communication purposes.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us.
