# Dry Needling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the patient's medical history, contact information, and consent for the dry needling treatment. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's name and contact information in the "Patient Name" and "Contact Information" fields.
2. List any medical conditions that may affect the treatment in the "Medical History" field.
3. Provide a list of any allergies and medications in the "Allergies" and "Medications" fields.
4. Select your consent for the treatment in the "Consent" field.
5. Enter the date of the treatment in the "Date" field.
6. Sign the form with your signature in the "Signature" field.
7. Enter the doctor's name and other relevant clinic information in the "Doctor Name", "Medical Board", "Doctor License", and "Clinic License" fields.
8. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Contact Info" fields.
9. Enter the clinic address in the "Clinic Address", "Clinic Address Line 2", "Clinic Street", and "Clinic City" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number, email, and fax.
* **Medical History**: List any medical conditions that may affect the treatment.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Consent**: Select "I have read and understand the information provided" to indicate your consent for the treatment.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the treatment.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your signature.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor License**: Enter the doctor's license number or other relevant information.
* **Clinic Address**: Enter the clinic address.
* **Clinic License**: Enter the clinic license number or other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Medical Group**: Enter the medical group information.
* **Medical Supervising Doctor**: Enter the name of the supervising doctor.
* **Clinic Address Line 2**: Enter any additional address information for the clinic.
* **Clinic Street**: Enter the street address of the clinic.
* **Clinic City**: Enter the city where the clinic is located.
