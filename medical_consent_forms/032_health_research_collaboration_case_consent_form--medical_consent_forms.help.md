# health_research_collaboration_case_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Research Collaboration Case Consent Form is used to obtain consent from participants in a medical research collaboration. It is a critical document that outlines the terms and conditions of participation, including the study's purpose, scope, and potential risks and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please review the provided instructions carefully. You will need to provide information about yourself, your medical history, allergies, emergency contact details, and research collaboration information. You can fill out this form on your own or with the help of your research collaborator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Provide your name, date of birth, and contact information (e.g., address, phone number).
  - Please include your correct contact information to ensure that we can reach you easily.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have given permission for the research team to contact you or not.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you are comfortable with the research team contacting you.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, required: true): Indicate if you have any medical history or conditions that may impact the study.
  - Choose 'True' if you have any medical conditions or history that may be relevant to the study.
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any medical conditions you have (e.g., Diabetes, Hypertension, Other).
  - Choose any medical conditions that apply to you.
- **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
  - List all your current medications, including dosages and frequencies.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any allergies.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you have any allergies.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
  - Please include the name, phone number, and address of an emergency contact.
- **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your relationship with the emergency contact.
  - Choose 'Yes' if you have a close relationship with the emergency contact.
- **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
  - Please include the phone number of the emergency contact.
- **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required: false): Provide the email address of the emergency contact.
  - Please include the email address of the emergency contact.
- **Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required: false): Provide the address of the emergency contact.
  - Please include the address of the emergency contact.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required: true): Sign this form to indicate that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the research.
  - Please sign your name as an indication of your consent.
- **Date** (`date_signature_conducted`, date, required: true): Date when the participant signed this form.
  - Please date the form when you sign it.
- **Research Collaborator Signature** (`research_collaborator_signature`, text, required: true): Sign this form as the research collaborator.
  - Please sign this form as the research collaborator.
- **Study Title** (`study_title`, text, required: true): Provide the title of the study.
  - Please provide the title of the study you are participating in.
- **Study Institutions** (`study_institutions`, text, required: true): Provide the name of the research institution.
  - Please provide the name of the research institution.
- **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, date, required: true): Provide the start date of the study.
  - Please provide the start date of the study.
- **Study End Date** (`study_end_date`, date, required: true): Provide the end date of the study.
  - Please provide the end date of the study.
- **Study Completion Date** (`study_completion_date`, date, required: true): Provide the completion date of the study.
  - Please provide the completion date of the study.
- **Research Collaborator Name** (`research_collaborator_name`, text, required: true): Provide the name of the research collaborator.
  - Please provide the name of the research collaborator.
- **Research Collaborator Email** (`research_collaborator_email`, text, required: true): Provide the email address of the research collaborator.
  - Please provide the email address of the research collaborator.
- **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, required: true): Provide the name of the research institution.
  - Please provide the name of the research institution.
- **Collaborator Institution** (`collaborator_institution`, text, required: true): Provide the name of the collaborator institution.
  - Please provide the name of the collaborator institution.
- **Study Funding** (`study_funding`, select_multiple, required: true): Indicate if the study is funded or not.
  - Choose 'Yes' if the study is funded.
- **Funding Institution** (`study_funding_institution`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the funding institution.
  - Please provide the name of the funding institution.

## Tips
- Please review the study's purpose, scope, and terms and conditions carefully before filling out this form.
- Ensure that you understand what you are agreeing to by reading the form carefully.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask your research collaborator or contact the research institution for guidance.
