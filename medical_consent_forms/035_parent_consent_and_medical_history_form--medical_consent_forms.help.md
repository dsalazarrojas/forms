# Parent Consent And Medical History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parent Consent And Medical History Form is used to collect information about a patient's medical history, parent consent, and emergency contact information. This form is typically used in healthcare settings to ensure that patients and their parents/guardians understand their medical care and any potential risks or benefits associated with it. Completing this form helps healthcare providers gather accurate and thorough information about the patient's medical history and ensures that parents/guardians are aware of their child's medical condition and any necessary steps for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the option that best represents your response.
3. For text fields, please provide a clear and concise description.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask a healthcare provider for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Consent (`parent_consent`, select_one, required: false):** This question asks if you have given consent for your child's medical treatment. Select 'Yes' if you have given consent, 'No' otherwise.
* **Medical History**: This field allows you to provide additional information about your child's medical history. Please write a clear and concise description of their medical history, including any allergies, medications, and previous medical conditions.
* **Allergies**: If your child has any allergies, please list them in this field.
* **Medications**: If your child is taking any medications, please list them in this field.
* **Medical Conditions**: If your child has any medical conditions (e.g., diabetes, asthma), please list them in this field.
* **Doctor Info**: This field is for providing information about your child's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you would like to list emergency contact information for your child, please provide the contact person's name and relationship to your child.
* **Medical History 2** to **Medical History 18**: These fields allow you to provide additional information about your child's medical history, such as previous illnesses, treatments, or allergies.
* **Doctor Info 2**: Another field for providing information about your child's doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Medical History 19-25** and **Medical History 26-29**: Additional fields for providing more information about your child's medical history. 

Note: Fields 19-25 and 26-29 have been truncated for brevity. Please review the actual form carefully and use these fields as needed. 

Please complete the form carefully and accurately to help healthcare providers make informed decisions about your child's care. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask a healthcare provider for assistance.
