# Treatment Plan Development Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Treatment Plan Development Form is a document used to gather and organize information about a patient's medical history, treatment options, and treatment plan. This form is designed to ensure that all necessary information is captured and communicated between healthcare providers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Patient Name` field.
2. Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history in the `Medical History` field.
3. Select the treatment options that apply to the patient in the `Treatment Options` field.
4. Check if the patient has any medical conditions in the `Medical Condition` field.
5. Add any comments about the patient's treatment plan in the `Treatment Plan Comments` field.
6. Add any comments about the patient's medical history in the `Medical History Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's past medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or conditions.
* **Treatments Options** (`treatment_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable treatment options that may be suitable for the patient.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Check if the patient has a medical condition that may impact their treatment.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Outline the proposed treatment plan for the patient.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Medical History Comments** (`medical_history_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the patient's medical history.
* **Treatment Plan Development Form Comments** (`treatment_plan_development_form_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the form itself, such as any questions or concerns.
