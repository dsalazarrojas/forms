# Orthodontic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthodontic Informed Consent Form is a document that ensures patients understand and agree to the terms of their orthodontic treatment, providing informed consent for the process. This form must be filled out by the patient before commencing any orthodontic treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your personal details in the **patient_info** field.
2. Provide your **patient_name**.
3. Enter your date of birth in the **patient_dob** field.
4. Describe the treatment details in the **treatment_details** field.
5. List the risks and benefits of the treatment in the **risks_and_benefits** field.
6. Sign and date the form in the **patient_signature** field.
7. Sign and date the form in the **practitioner_signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any personal information or notes about your treatment.
* **patient_name** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **patient_dob** (Date, Optional):
	+ Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **treatment_details** (Text, Optional):
	+ Describe the orthodontic treatment you are undergoing or have undergone.
* **risks_and_benefits** (Text, Optional):
	+ List any known risks or benefits associated with your treatment.
* **patient_signature** (Note, Optional):
	+ Sign your name, indicating you understand and agree to the treatment terms.
* **practitioner_signature** (Note, Optional):
	+ Sign your name as the treating practitioner, confirming you have explained the treatment terms and risks.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out this form completely and accurately.
- Review the form carefully before signing it.
- Keep a copy of the form for your records.
- Return the form to your treating practitioner after completion.
