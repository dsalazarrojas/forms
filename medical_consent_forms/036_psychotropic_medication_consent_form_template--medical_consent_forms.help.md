# Psychotropic Medication Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for psychotropic medication, ensuring they understand their treatment and the potential risks and benefits associated with it. This template provides a structured approach to collecting crucial information from patients, including their medical history, dosage, and consent status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name and date of birth.
2. Provide contact information for the patient.
3. List the name and dosage of the psychotropic medication prescribed.
4. Enter the start and end dates for the medication treatment.
5. Specify the prescriber's name and title.
6. Check if consent was provided by the patient.
7. Document the consent date and reason for medication (if provided).
8. Note any known side effects of the medication.
9. Sign the form with the patient's signature (if applicable) and the prescriber's signature.
10. If the patient is under 18, provide the patient's name (if available), the prescriber's name, and the prescriber's title.
11. Select the patient's age, gender, race, ethnicity, and language.
12. Provide a brief medical history for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth to confirm their identity.
* **Contact Information**: Enter any relevant contact information for the patient, such as phone numbers or addresses.
* **Medication Name**: List the name of the psychotropic medication prescribed to the patient.
* **Medication Dosage**: Specify the dosage instructions for the medication.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the medication was started.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the medication is expected to end.
* **Prescriber Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who prescribed the medication.
* **Prescriber Title**: Enter the title of the healthcare provider (e.g., MD, RN, etc.).
* **Consent Provided**: Check the box if the patient has provided informed consent for the medication.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date the consent was provided (if applicable).
* **Reason for Medication**: Briefly describe the reason the medication was prescribed (if provided).
* **Side Effects**: List any known side effects of the medication.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form with the patient's signature (if applicable).
* **Prescriber Signature**: Sign the form with the prescriber's signature.
* **Patient Name (if under 18)**: Enter the name of the patient if they are under 18 years old.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Sign the form with the patient's parent/guardian's signature (if under 18).
* **Prescriber Name (if under 18)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider if the patient is under 18 years old.
* **Prescriber Title (if under 18)**: Enter the title of the healthcare provider if the patient is under 18 years old.
* **Age**: Select the patient's age.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
* **Race**: Select the patient's race.
* **Ethnicity**: Select the patient's ethnicity.
* **Language**: Select the patient's language.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief medical history for the patient.
