# Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccine Consent Form is a document used to gather information and obtain consent from patients and providers for vaccine administration. It is a crucial step in the vaccination process to ensure that both parties understand and agree to the terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field to understand its purpose.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately to ensure that your consent is valid.
3. Ensure that you have read and understand the form's terms before signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vaccine Consent Form** (`vaccine_consent_form`, text, required): Enter the name of the vaccine consent form you are filling out.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided:
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the terms of the vaccination process.
	+ Option 2: I do not understand the terms of the vaccination process.
	+ Option 3: I have questions about the terms of the vaccination process.
	+ Option 4: I refuse to participate in the vaccination process.
* **Provider Consent** (`provider_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the terms of the vaccination process.
	+ Option 2: I do not understand the terms of the vaccination process.
* **Vaccine Name** (`vaccine_name`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vaccine you are receiving:
	+ Option 1: Vaccine A
	+ Option 2: Vaccine B
	+ Option 3: Vaccine C

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood the form's terms before signing.
