# Hair Protein Infusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and consent for hair protein infusion procedures. It consists of three sections: Client Details, Health Information, and Consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Details section with your information: [Client Details label](#client_details).
2. Provide your Health Information in the Health Information section: [Health Information label](#health_info).
3. Read and sign the Consent section, indicating your acceptance of the hair protein infusion procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
• **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): 
  This section asks for your personal details such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.

### Health Information
• **Health Information** (`health_info`, text, required): 
  This section asks about your medical history, including any current or previous medical conditions, medications, or allergies. Be as detailed as possible, as this information is crucial for your safety during the procedure.

### Consent
• **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): 
  This is the last section where you will indicate your consent for the hair protein infusion procedure. Read and understand the procedure before signing, and sign this section to confirm your agreement. 

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand all the information provided before signing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- This form is only a guide; consult a healthcare professional for personalized guidance.
