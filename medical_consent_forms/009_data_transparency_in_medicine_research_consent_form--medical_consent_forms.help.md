# Data Transparency In Medicine Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants in medical research studies, ensuring transparency and obtaining necessary consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your details.
2.  Enter your **Contact Information**.
3.  Select one or multiple options for **Consent**.
4.  If you'd like, provide your **Sharing Preferences**.
5.  Provide details for **Research Information**.
6.  Enter information for **Study Records**.
7.  Sign the **Signature** field with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Participant Information**:
    +  Label: Participant Information
    +  Description: This section asks for your basic information like name, email, and date of birth.
    +  Required: Yes
*  **Contact Information**:
    +  Label: Contact Information
    +  Description: Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach out to you for more details.
    +  Required: Yes
*  **Consent**:
    +  Label: Select one or multiple options
    +  Description: Indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the study.
    +  Options:
    1.  I am 18 years or older
    2.  I am 17 years old or younger
*  **Sharing Preferences**:
    +  Label: Sharing Preferences
    +  Description: If you'd like, specify how you'd like your data to be shared.
    +  Required: No
*  **Research Information**:
    +  Label: Research Information
    +  Description: Provide any additional details about the research study.
    +  Required: No
*  **Study Records**:
    +  Label: Study Records
    +  Description: Enter information for study records.
    +  Required: No
*  **Signature**:
    +  Label: Signature
    +  Description: Please enter your signature here to confirm your consent.
    +  Required: Yes
