# Medical Study Publication Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the publication of medical studies, including the research team, study title, and publication details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the research team involved in the study:**
	* Choose one or more members of the research team that are responsible for the study, such as Researcher, Team Lead, Data Analyst, or Project Manager.
2. **Enter the study title:**
	* Type the title of the study in the text field.
3. **Enter the study ID:**
	* Enter the ID number assigned to the study.
4. **Enter the release date:**
	* Select the date the study was released for publication.
5. **Enter publication details:**
	* Provide a brief description of the publication.
6. **Select the publication status:**
	* Choose the current status of the publication, such as Draft, In Progress, Published, or In Review.
7. **Enter the publication plan:**
	* Describe the planned publication plan, including the expected date and venue.
8. **Enter the publication location:**
	* Type the location where the study was published, such as a journal or conference.
9. **Enter the research institution:**
	* Provide the name of the institution associated with the study.
10. **Indicate whether a publication agreement was signed:**
	* Select True or False to indicate whether an agreement was signed for the publication.
11. **Enter the date of agreement signing:**
	* If an agreement was signed, enter the date it was signed.
12. **Enter the agreement signed by:**
	* Type the name of the person or organization that signed the agreement.
13. **Enter the submission date:**
	* Select the date the publication was submitted.
14. **Select the submission status:**
	* Choose the current status of the submission, such as Draft, In Progress, Published, or In Review.
15. **Enter review comments:**
	* Provide any comments from the reviewer.
16. **Enter reviewer name:**
	* Type the name of the reviewer.
17. **Enter reviewer institution:**
	* Provide the name of the institution associated with the reviewer.
18. **Enter publication URL:**
	* Type the URL of the published study.
19. **Enter signed publication URL:**
	* Type the URL of the signed publication, if applicable.
20. **Enter review URL:**
	* Type the URL of the review, if applicable.
21. **Enter reviewer signature:**
	* Type the signature of the reviewer, if applicable.
22. **Enter reviewer email:**
	* Type the email of the reviewer.
23. **Select whether the institution is reviewing:**
	* Choose whether the institution is reviewing the study.

Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the integrity of the publication process.
