# Telehealth Patient Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Patient Consent Form is used to obtain a patient's consent for telehealth services. This form is essential for establishing a patient-provider relationship, ensuring that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached by our healthcare providers.
3. **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached by our healthcare providers.
4. **Address**: Enter your address where you can be reached by our healthcare providers (optional).
5. **Medical Record Number**: If you have a medical record number, enter it here (optional).
6. **Consent to Treatment**: Select 'Yes' to confirm that you understand and agree to receive telehealth services.
7. **Telehealth Agreement**: Select all the options that apply to confirm your understanding of telehealth services, including:
	* I understand that I will receive telehealth services.
	* I understand that I will not receive telehealth services.
8. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
9. **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of an emergency contact person.
10. **Patient Statement**: Enter any additional comments or information about your health status or medical history.
11. **Medical History**: Enter any medical conditions, treatments, or allergies that may be relevant to your care.
12. **Allergy Information**: If you have any allergies, enter them here.
13. **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
14. **Medication List**: If you are taking any medications, enter them here.
15. **Emergency Services**: If you have any emergency services or procedures, enter them here.
16. **Emergency Procedure**: If you have any emergency procedures, enter them here.
17. **Emergency Contacts 2**: Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have emergency contacts other than your primary contact.
18. **Telehealth Services**: Provide any additional comments or information about your telehealth services.
19. **Telehealth Agreement 2**: Select all the options that apply to confirm your understanding of telehealth services, including:
	* I will receive telehealth services.
	* I will not receive telehealth services.
20. **Telehealth Terms and Conditions**: Enter any additional comments or information about the telehealth terms and conditions.
21. **Signature**: Print your signature to confirm your consent.
22. **Digital Signature**: Upload a digital signature to confirm your consent (optional).
23. **Patient Info**: Enter any additional comments or information about your patient status.
24. **Patient Info 2**: Enter any additional comments or information about your patient status.
25. **Telehealth Agreement 3**: Select all the options that apply to confirm your understanding of telehealth services, including:
	* I have read and understand these terms.
	* I have not read and do not understand these terms.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before signing and uploading your digital signature.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider.

Please note that some fields are optional, but it's essential to complete all required fields to ensure that you can receive telehealth services.
