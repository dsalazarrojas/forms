# off_label_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The off-label_consent_form is a document used to gather information from patients regarding off-label use of medication. It is essential to ensure that the patient has been informed and consented to the use of medication for a purpose not specifically approved by the manufacturer or regulatory agencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the patient has not previously provided informed consent for off-label medication use.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Check the patient's existing medical records to ensure that the current information is not duplicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address as it appears on their medical records.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number as it appears on their medical records.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the medication is being used to treat a medical condition, or 'No' if not.
* **Medication Name** (`Medication Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medication being used.
* **Reason for Medication Use** (`Reason for Medication Use`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of why the medication is being used off-label.
* **Informed Consent** (`Informed Consent`, note, optional): Enter a statement confirming that the patient has been informed of the potential risks and benefits of off-label medication use.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient has signed the informed consent, or 'No' if not.

## Tips
* Ensure that the patient understands the potential risks and benefits of off-label medication use before completing the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Store the form in the patient's medical records to maintain accurate and up-to-date information.
