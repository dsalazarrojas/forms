# Psychiatric Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychiatric Records Release Form is used to obtain a patient's consent to access their psychiatric records, ensuring that the patient is aware of the information being shared and has granted permission for healthcare providers to access their records.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's information in the "patient information" field.
2.  Select the healthcare provider(s) authorized to access the patient's records in the "healthcare provider" field.
3.  Choose the release information that applies to the patient's records in the "release information" field.
4.  Select the authorized providers from the list in the "authorized providers" field.
5.  Enter the date the authorization is effective and the date it will end in the "authorization date" and "authorization end date" fields respectively.
6.  Indicate who authorized this in the "authorized by" field.
7.  Sign and date the form to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient information** (`patient_information`, text, required): Enter the patient's name and demographic information.
* **healthcare provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Select the healthcare provider who will be accessing the patient's records.
* **release information** (`release_information`, select_multiple, required): Choose which type of information to be released (e.g., medical history, medication list, etc.).
* **authorized providers** (`authorized_providers`, select_one, required): Select which provider(s) will have access to the patient's records.
* **authorization date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): Enter the date the authorization is effective.
* **authorization end date** (`authorization_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the authorization will end.
* **authorized by** (`authorized_by`, text, required): Indicate who authorized this release.
* **signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Provide a note for the healthcare provider or other relevant details.
* **patient signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): The patient's signature confirms their consent to release their records.
* **healthcare provider signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, note, optional): The healthcare provider's signature confirms their acceptance of the authorization.
## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing to ensure all information is accurate.
* Ensure the patient's consent is obtained before signing the form.
* Store the completed form securely to protect the patient's sensitive information.
Note: This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used without proper medical supervision. If you have questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
