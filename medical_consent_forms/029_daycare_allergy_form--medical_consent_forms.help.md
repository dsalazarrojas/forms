# Daycare Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Allergy Form is designed to gather information about a child's allergies. This form helps the daycare staff and caregivers to understand the child's allergy types, potential reactions, and any necessary treatment or medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Review the provided fields and ensure you have all the necessary information about the child's allergies.
2.  Select the relevant allergy types from the list.
3.  Enter the allergy name, description, and status in the respective fields.
4.  Fill in the allergy onset date and reaction in the provided fields.
5.  Enter any allergy medications or treatments as needed.
6.  Review and submit the form for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Allergy Form Fields (Select Multiple)**: Choose from the list of allergy types that apply to the child.
* **Allergy Type (Text)**: Enter the type of allergy (e.g., peanut, nut, milk, etc.).
* **Allergy Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the allergy (e.g., peanut allergy).
* **Allergy Description (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the allergy (e.g., severe reaction).
* **Allergy Status (Select One)**: Choose whether the allergy is active or inactive.
* **Allergy Onset Date (Date)**: Enter the date when the allergy began.
* **Allergy Reaction (Text)**: Describe the child's reaction to the allergy (e.g., swelling, itching, etc.).
* **Allergy Medication (Text)**: Enter any medications or treatments the child is currently taking for the allergy.
* **Allergy Treatment (Text)**: Provide any additional treatment or precautions for the allergy.
* **Allergy Allergy Medication Administered (Text)**: Enter any medications that have been administered to the child for the allergy.
