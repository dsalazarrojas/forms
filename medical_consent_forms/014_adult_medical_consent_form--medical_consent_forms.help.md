# Adult Medical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from adult patients for medical treatments or procedures. It gathers essential information to ensure patients understand the risks and benefits associated with a particular treatment or procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with the patient's information, including their full name, date of birth, and a description of the treatment or procedure.
2. Select the options for "Risks and benefits acknowledged" and "Alternative options explained" to confirm your understanding of the treatment or procedure.
3. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
4. Sign the form with your electronic signature.
5. Date the form with the date of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Medical Consent --** (Patient Header): This section is a note field that explains the purpose of this form.
* **Patient full name** (Patient Name): A required field for the patient's full name, as it appears on their ID.
* **Date of birth** (Date of Birth): A required field for the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Treatment or procedure description** (Treatment or Procedure Description): A required field for a clear explanation of the treatment or procedure.
* **Risks and benefits acknowledged** (Risks and Benefits Acknowledged): A select field where you choose to confirm your understanding of the risks and benefits of the treatment or procedure.
* **Alternative options explained** (Alternative Options Explained): A select field where you confirm that you have discussed alternative options with the healthcare provider.
* **Emergency contact** (Emergency Contact Name and Phone): A required field for the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Signature name** (Consent Signature Name): A required field for your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Consent date** (Consent Date): A required field for the date of signing.
