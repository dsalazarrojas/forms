# Medicare Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather basic medical information from patients to facilitate a consultation with a healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section with your name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. List any known medical conditions you have, such as high blood pressure or diabetes.
3. If you have any allergies, please list them.
4. Provide a list of your current medications.
5. Give a detailed description of your medical history.
6. Share your family medical history, including any health issues your parents or grandparents may have.
7. Finally, provide information about your younger family members' health history, including siblings or children.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (Patient Info, Text, Optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and contact details in this section.
- **Medical History** (Medical History, Text, Optional): Describe any known medical conditions you have, such as high blood pressure or diabetes.
- **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose any medical conditions that apply to you, such as High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, or Heart Issues.
- **Allergies** (Allergies, Select Multiple, Optional): List any allergies you have, such as Penicillin, Latex, or Eggs.
- **Medications** (Medications, Text, Optional): Enter a list of your current medications.
- **Medical History** (Medical History, Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your medical history.
- **Family History** (Family History, Text, Optional): Share your family medical history, including any health issues your relatives may have.
- **Older Family History** (Older Family History, Text, Optional): Describe any health issues your parents or grandparents may have.
- **Younger Family History** (Younger Family History, Text, Optional): Share your family medical history with siblings or children.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you need help with any of the fields, please contact us.
- This form is not a substitute for a comprehensive medical evaluation, so please follow up with a consultation with a healthcare provider after submitting this form.
