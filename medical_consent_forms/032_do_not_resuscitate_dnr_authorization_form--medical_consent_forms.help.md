# do_not_resuscitate_dnr_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Do Not Resuscitate (DNR) Authorization Form is a medical form used to document a patient's decision to refuse medical interventions in the event of a life-threatening emergency. This form is typically completed by a healthcare provider and must be signed by the patient or their legal representative. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the patient's wishes are respected and that healthcare providers are aware of their treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information:
	* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record.
	* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
2. Select the healthcare provider:
	* **Healthcare Provider**: Choose the name of the healthcare provider who is authorized to provide care to the patient.
3. Complete the DNR information:
	* **Date of DNR**: Enter the date when the DNR was established.
	* **Time of DNR**: Enter the time when the DNR was established.
4. Sign and date the form:
	* **Signature**: Sign the form as the healthcare provider.
	* **Printed Name**: Enter your printed name.
	* **Date Signed**: Enter the date and time you signed the form.
	* **Printed Date**: Enter the date the form was printed.
	* **Printed Time**: Enter the time the form was printed.
