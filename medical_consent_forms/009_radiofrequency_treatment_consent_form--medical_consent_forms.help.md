# Radiofrequency Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Radiofrequency Treatment Consent Form is used to document a patient's consent for radiofrequency treatment, which may involve the use of radiofrequency devices to treat certain medical conditions. This form helps ensure that the patient understands the treatment options, potential risks, and benefits, and provides informed consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting, read through the form to understand the purpose, sections, and requirements.
2. **Complete each section**: Answer all required fields, and provide as much information as possible in optional fields.
3. **Use correct formatting**: Use the format specified for dates and times, and make sure to sign and date all required fields.

* **patient-info** (page 2) (`patient-info`, number, required): Enter your patient's age.

* **medical-provider-signature** (page 5) (`medical-provider-signature`, date, required): Date and sign the form to indicate your agreement to provide treatment.

* **medical-provider-title** (page 7) (`medical-provider-title`, text, not required): Enter your medical provider's title.
