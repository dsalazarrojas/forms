# Patient Belongings Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Belongings Inventory Form is used to gather information about a patient's personal items, such as clothing, jewelry, or other items that are related to their care.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if the patient has any belongings that need to be inventoried. This can include clothing, jewelry, personal care items, or other items that are related to their care.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the question "Patient Belongings List" to indicate if the patient has any belongings that need to be inventoried.
3.  If the patient has belongings, enter the Patient ID, Patient Name, and Patient Date of Birth (DOB) to identify the patient and gather contact information.
4.  Enter the patient's date of birth (DOB) and time of birth, if applicable.
5.  Enter the patient's contact phone number.
6.  Optionally, provide a detailed description of the patient's address.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Patient Belongings List**: This field asks if the patient has any belongings that need to be inventoried. Please respond with a "Yes" or "No."
*  **Patient ID**: This field is used to identify the patient. Enter the patient's ID number.
*  **Patient Name**: This field is used to enter the patient's name.
*  **Patient Date of Birth (DOB)**: This field is used to enter the patient's date of birth.
*  **Patient Date of Birth Time**: This field is used to enter the patient's time of birth (if applicable).
*  **Patient Address**: This field is used to enter a detailed description of the patient's address.
*  **Patient Contact Phone**: This field is used to enter the patient's contact phone number.
*  **Patient Name**: This field is used to enter the patient's name.

## Tips
*  Ensure that the information gathered through this form is accurate and up-to-date.
*  Use this form consistently and correctly to gather information about patient belongings.
*  Consider having the patient or their authorized representative sign and date the form to confirm their consent.
*  This form is intended for internal use and should not be used for medical billing or record-keeping purposes.
