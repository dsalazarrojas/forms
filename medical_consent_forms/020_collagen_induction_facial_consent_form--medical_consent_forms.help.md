# Collagen Induction Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collagen Induction Facial Consent Form is used to gather information about your medical history, treatment preferences, and post-treatment care expectations. This form is necessary to ensure that you are aware of the risks and benefits associated with the Collagen Induction Facial treatment and to provide your provider with accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
4. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. **Address**: Enter your street address.
6. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
7. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
8. **Treatment Date**: Enter the date of your scheduled treatment.
9. **Treatment Provider**: Enter the name of your treatment provider.
10. **Skin Type**: Select the type of skin you have (Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive, or Acne-Prone).
11. **Skin Concerns**: Select all the concerns you have about your skin (Fine Lines and Wrinkles, Acne Scars, Uneven Skin Tone, Large Pores, Dull Skin, Sun Damage, Sagging Skin, Stretch Marks, or Other).
12. **Previous Collagen Induction Treatments**: Select if you have had this treatment before (Yes Multiple Times, Yes Once, or No Never).
13. **Medical Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions you have (Diabetes, Heart Disease, High Blood Pressure, Autoimmune Disorder, Bleeding Disorder, Skin Cancer, Active Acne, Rosacea, Eczema, Psoriasis, or None).
14. **Current Medications**: List all the medications you are currently taking.
15. **Accutane Use**: Select if you have used Accutane in the past (Yes Currently, Yes Within 6 Months, Yes Over 6 Months Ago, or No Never).
16. **Blood Thinners**: Select if you take blood thinners (True or False).
17. **Retinol Use**: Select if you use Retinol products (Yes Daily, Yes Occasionally, or False).
18. **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, especially to skincare products.
19. **Pregnancy or Nursing**: Select if you are currently pregnant or nursing (Yes Pregnant, Yes Nursing, False, or Prefer Not to Say).
20. **History of Keloid Scarring**: Select if you scar easily (True, False, or Not Sure).
21. **Recent Sun Exposure**: Select your level of recent sun exposure (Yes Significant, Yes Moderate, No Minimal).
22. **Cold Sores**: Select if you have a history of cold sores (True or False).
23. **Treatment Area**: Select where the treatment will be done (Full Face, Face and Neck, Forehead Only, Cheeks Only, Around Mouth, or Other).
24. **Numbing Cream Preference**: Select if you prefer numbing cream (Yes Please Apply, No Thank You, or Used Before Without Issues).
25. **Procedure Understanding**: Select if you understand the procedure (Yes Completely, Mostly, Somewhat, or No Need More Info).
26. **Risks Understood**: Select all the risks you understand (Redness and Swelling, Minor Bleeding, Bruising, Infection Risk, Scarring, Pigment Changes, Breakouts, Dry or Flaky Skin, Sun Sensitivity).
27. **Aftercare Commitment**: Select if you will follow aftercare instructions (Yes I Will, Yes I Understand).
28. **Results Expectation**: Select if you have realistic expectations (Yes I Understand Multiple Sessions Needed, Yes I Understand Results Vary, No I Expect Immediate Results).
29. **Photo Consent**: Select if you consent to treatment photos (Yes I Consent, No I Do Not Consent).
30. **Marketing Photo Consent**: Select if you consent to marketing photos (Yes I Consent, No I Do Not Consent).
31. **Financial Responsibility Acknowledgment**: Select if you understand your financial responsibilities (Yes I Understand, Other).
32. **Client Agreement**: Select if you consent to the treatment (Yes I Consent, False).
33. **Provider Confirmation**: Select if your provider confirms your consent (Yes Confirmed, Other).
34. **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness (Optional).
35. **Witness Confirmation**: Select if you have a witness (Yes I Witness, False).

* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.

* You can always ask your provider to review your form before you start the treatment.
* If you need help with any question, please feel free to ask.
