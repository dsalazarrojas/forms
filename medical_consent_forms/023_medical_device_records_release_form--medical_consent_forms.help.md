# Medical Device Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Device Records Release Form is a document that allows patients to release their medical device records. This form is used to obtain the patient's consent to share their medical device records with a medical provider.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
* Provide your patient information (page 1).
* Enter details about the medical device being released (page 2).
* Indicate if you consent to share your medical device records (page 3).
* Select a date and time for the release (page 4).
* Enter the name of the medical provider you are releasing your records to (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to collect the patient's contact information, such as name and contact number.
* **Medical Device** (`medical_device`, text, optional)
	+ Enter details about the medical device being released, such as the device type and any relevant documentation.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you consent to share your medical device records.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date when you want your medical device records to be released.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time when you want your medical device records to be released.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the medical provider you are releasing your records to.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure you understand what you are releasing and who you are releasing it to.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your medical provider for assistance.
