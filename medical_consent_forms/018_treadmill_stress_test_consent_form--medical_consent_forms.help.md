# Treadmill Stress Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients who are undergoing a treadmill stress test to confirm that they understand the nature of the test and have provided necessary information about themselves and emergency contact details. Please review the form carefully and sign where indicated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all relevant questions.
2. Ensure you have filled out all the required fields.
3. Sign and date the form where indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`text`, required): Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`select_one`, optional): Select the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency. Options:
	+ Mother or Father
	+ Grandparent
	+ Sister or Brother
	+ Wife or Husband
	+ Child or Children
	+ Other
### Relationship
* **Relationship** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select how you know the emergency contact person.
	+ Mother or Father
	+ Sister or Brother
	+ Wife or Husband
	+ Child or Children
	+ Other
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or injuries that may impact your participation in the treadmill stress test.
### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`text`, optional): List any allergies you have, including medications, food, or other substances.
### Medications
* **Medications** (`text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
### Signature
* **Signature** (`text`, required): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the questions asked.

## Tips
* Be honest and complete as much of the form as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Do not proceed with the treadmill stress test until this form is signed and submitted.
