# PHQ 9 Rating Scale - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PHQ 9 Rating Scale is a form designed to help you track your experiences with various health-related questions. This form is not intended for high-stakes clinical settings but can be used for general population or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each question and choose the answer that best describes your experiences.
2. For select_multiple and select_one questions, choose one or more answers from the options provided.
3. For text fields, type your answer in the provided space.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, optional): This is a note field that provides a space for you to read and understand the instructions before proceeding with the form.
* **Questions 1-9** (`questions1`, `questions2`, `questions3`, `questions4`, `questions5`, `questions6`, `questions7`, `questions8`, `questions9`, select_one, optional): These questions ask about your experiences with various health-related topics. Choose the answer that best describes your experience:
	+ Questions 1-5: Some or most of the time, Most of the time, Almost always
	+ Questions 6-9: Almost never, Some or most of the time
* **Additional Information** (note, text, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information or notes about your experiences.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a space where you can enter any comments or feedback about your experience with this form.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and honest as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please feel free to ask.
