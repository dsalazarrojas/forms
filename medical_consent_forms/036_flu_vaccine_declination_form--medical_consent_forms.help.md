# Flu Vaccine Declination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's declination of flu vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information (page 1).
2. Select the vaccination season you would like to decline the vaccine for (page 2).
3. Choose the type of vaccine you are declining (page 3).
4. Enter the patient's date of birth (page 4).
5. Enter your phone number and email (pages 5 and 6).
6. Enter any medical conditions the patient has (page 7).
7. Enter your doctor's information (pages 8-11).
8. Select how the patient understands they will receive the vaccine (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (page 1, `patient_info`):
	+ Please enter the patient's name and any other relevant details.
* **Vaccination History** (page 2, `vaccination_history`):
	+ This field is for the patient's prior vaccination history.
* **Decline Reason** (page 3, `decline_reason`):
	+ Please enter the reason why the patient is declining the flu vaccine.
* **Flu Season** (page 4, `flu_season`):
	+ Select which flu season you would like to decline the vaccine for.
* **Vaccine Type** (page 5, `vaccine_type`):
	+ Choose the type of vaccine you are declining.
* **Date of Birth** (page 6, `date_of_birth`):
	+ Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Phone** (page 7, `phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (page 8, `email`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Medical Condition** (page 9, `medical_condition`):
	+ Enter any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Doctor's Information** (page 10, `doctor_info`):
	+ Enter your name, title, facility, and address.
* **Doctor's Phone** (page 11, `doctor_phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Doctor's Email** (page 12, `doctor_email`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Patient Consent** (page 13, `patient_consent`):
	+ Select how the patient understands they will receive the vaccine.
* **Doctor's Signature** (page 14, `doctor_signature`):
	+ Sign and date your signature.
* **Medical Signature** (page 15, `medical_signature`):
	+ Sign and date your signature.
* **Patient's Signature** (page 16, `patient_signature`):
	+ Sign and date the patient's signature.
* **Note** (page 17, `note`):
	+ Enter any additional notes about the patient's declination.
* **Medical License Number** (page 18, `medical_license_number`):
	+ Enter your medical license number.
* **Doctor's Name** (page 19, `doctor_name`):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Doctor's Title** (page 20, `doctor_title`):
	+ Enter your title.
* **Doctor's Facility** (page 21, `doctor_facility`):
	+ Enter your facility.
* **Doctor's Address** (page 22, `doctor_address`):
	+ Enter your address.
* **Date Declined** (page 23, `date_declined`):
	+ Enter the date the vaccine was declined.
* **Doctor's Signature** (page 24, `doctor_signature`):
	+ Sign and date your signature.
* **Date Signed** (page 25, `date_signed`):
	+ Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Patient's Signature** (page 25, `patient_signature`):
	+ Sign and date the patient's signature.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check the patient's decline reason and understand that declining the vaccine can increase the risk of illness.
* Ensure that you, as the doctor, sign and date your signature in the correct fields.
