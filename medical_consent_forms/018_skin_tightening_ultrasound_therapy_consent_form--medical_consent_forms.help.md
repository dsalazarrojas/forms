# Skin Tightening Ultrasound Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Tightening Ultrasound Therapy Consent Form is a document that needs to be completed by clients before undergoing skin tightening ultrasound therapy. It is a legal requirement that ensures the client understands the treatment and its risks, benefits, and alternatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
4. The treatment provider will review and sign the form to confirm that you have given your informed consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Health Details** (`client_health_details`, text, optional): Please describe your current health status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are taking.
* **Treatment Acknowledgments** (`treatment_acknowledgments`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the acknowledgments that apply to you:
	+ Option 1: I understand that skin tightening ultrasound therapy is a non-invasive treatment.
	+ Option 2: I understand that skin tightening ultrasound therapy may cause temporary discomfort or pain.
* **Skin Condition** (`skin_condition`, text, optional): Describe your skin condition that you are seeking treatment for.
* **Treatment Area** (`treatment_area`, select_one, optional): Select the area of skin to be treated:
	+ Option 1: Face
	+ Option 2: Neck
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, optional): Please sign your name below to confirm that you have read and understood this consent form.
* **Treatment Provider Signature** (`treatment_provider_signature`, note, optional): The treatment provider will sign below to confirm that you have given your informed consent.
* **Consent To Treatment** (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Option 1: I give my informed consent to undergo skin tightening ultrasound therapy.
	+ Option 2: I decline skin tightening ultrasound therapy.
