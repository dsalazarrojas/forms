# physical_therapy_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a patient's authorization for medical records release.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow the steps below to complete this form.
2. Fill out all the required fields.
3. Ensure that you provide accurate information to ensure smooth data transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who is authorizing the release of their medical records.
* **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or DD MM/YY.
* **Medical Record Request Date**: Enter the date on which the patient's medical records request is made.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or organization handling the patient's medical records.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign here to confirm that you are the patient and that you have authorized the release of your medical records.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Sign here to confirm that you are the healthcare provider and that you have received the patient's authorization for medical records release.
* **Authorization Type**: Choose the type of authorization: Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, or Other.
* **Authorization Duration**: Enter the length of time for which the authorization is valid.
* **Medical Record Access**: Choose the level of access to the medical records: Full, Limited, or None.
* **Authorization Expiration Date**: Enter the date on which the authorization expires.
* **Authorization Terminating Condition**: Check if there is a terminating condition for the authorization.
* **Authorization Renewal Option**: Choose if the authorization can be renewed.
* **Medical Record Transfer**: Choose if the medical records can be transferred.
* **Medical Record Storage**: Choose how the medical records will be stored: Electronic, Paper, or Other.
* **Authorization Termination**: Enter the date on which the authorization terminates.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider or organization.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the patient's medical records are kept.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the patient's medical records.
* **Medical Record Date Created**: Enter the date on which the medical records were created.
* **Authorization Terminating Condition Note**: Enter any additional notes about the terminating condition.
* **Authorization Renewal Condition**: Choose if the authorization can be renewed.
* **Medical Record Transfer Destination**: Enter the location where the medical records will be transferred.
* **Authorization Transfer Expiration**: Enter the date on which the authorization transfer expires.
* **Medical Record Transfer Location**: Enter the location where the medical records will be transferred.
* **Authorization Transfer Termination**: Choose if the authorization transfer terminates.

This guide is intended to help patients understand the importance and implications of authorizing medical records release. Please consult with your healthcare provider or medical staff if you need further clarification.
