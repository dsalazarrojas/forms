# Nasal Flu Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nasal Flu Vaccine Consent Form is a document used to obtain a patient's consent for receiving a nasal flu vaccine. It is used to gather essential information about the patient, their contact details, and their consent status, as well as any allergies, medical conditions, and medications they may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details in this section.
2. **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact details, such as phone number and email.
3. **Consent**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that the patient is consenting to receive the nasal flu vaccine.
4. **Date**: Enter the date of the vaccination administration.
5. **Time**: Enter the time of the vaccination administration.
6. **Vaccine Information**: Enter any relevant information about the vaccine, such as the type and dose.
7. **Allergies**: Select 'Yes' if the patient has any allergies.
8. **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history, such as previous illnesses or conditions.
9. **Medical Conditions**: Select 'Yes' if the patient has any medical conditions.
10. **Medications**: Select 'Yes' if the patient is taking any medications.
11. **Patient Signature**: Sign the form to indicate that the patient has read and understood the information provided.
12. **Witness Signature**: Have a witness sign the form to confirm that they were present during the vaccination administration.
13. **Witness Name**: Enter the witness's name.
14. **Witness Title**: Enter the witness's title.
15. **Witness Signature Date**: Enter the date of the witness's signature.
16. **Witness Signature Time**: Enter the time of the witness's signature.
17. **Witness Title**: Enter the witness's job title or position.
