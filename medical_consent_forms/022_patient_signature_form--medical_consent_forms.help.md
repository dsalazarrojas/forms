# Patient Signature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to provide their medical consent and signature for their medical treatment. It captures important information about the patient, their medical provider, and contact details for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the "Signature Form" page with your signature and print your name.
2. Enter your "Patient ID" number and ensure it is correct.
3. Write a brief "Conservation of Related Information" in the text box, indicating any relevant medical history or allergies.
4. Enter the date you received the form and the date you signed it.
5. Enter your "Patient Name" and any other required fields as prompted.
6. Fill out the contact information for your "Medical Provider" and "Facility Name".
7. Enter your contact information, including "Contact Number", "Contact Email", and "Contact Address".
8. Confirm your digital signature and physical signature with the "Digital Signature Confirmed By" and "Physical Signature Confirmed By" fields.
9. Enter the "Contact Person" and "Contact Number" for follow-up contact.
10. Review and submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Signature Form** (Required): This is the signature block where you will sign your name to indicate your consent.
* **Patient ID** (Required): Please enter your unique patient ID number as assigned by your medical provider.
* **Conservation of Related Information** (Required): Write a brief description of any relevant medical history or allergies that your provider should be aware of.
* **Date Received** (Required): Enter the date you received this form.
* **Date Signed** (Required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Form Signed By** (Required): Enter the name of the person signing this form.
* **Patient Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Medical Provider** (Required): Enter the name of your medical provider who will be treating you.
* **Facility Name** (Required): Enter the name of the facility where you will be receiving treatment.
* **Contact Number** (Required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Contact Address** (Required): Enter your physical address for follow-up contact.
* **Digital Signature** (Required): This is where you will enter your digital signature.
* **Digital Signature Confirmed By** (Required): Confirm who confirmed your digital signature.
* **Physical Signature** (Required): This is where you will enter your physical signature.
* **Physical Signature Confirmed By** (Required): Confirm who confirmed your physical signature.
* **Contact Person** (Required): Enter the name of the contact person for follow-up contact.
* **Contact Address** (Required): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
