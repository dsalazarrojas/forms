# Brow Lift Retouch Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brow Lift Retouch Consent Form is a document that captures essential information from clients seeking a retouch procedure. It helps ensure that clients understand the risks and benefits associated with the procedure and provides a record of their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's full name in the "Client name" field.
2. Select the scheduled date for the retouch procedure in the "Procedure date" field.
3. If applicable, enter the date of the original lift procedure in the "Previous procedure date" field.
4. List any known allergies or sensitivities relevant to the procedure in the "Known allergies" field.
5. Indicate that the client has been informed of the risks and benefits of the procedure and has provided consent by selecting "True" in the "Consent given" field.
6. Enter the client's contact phone number for appointment confirmation in the "Contact phone" field.
7. Confirm that the client agrees to follow aftercare instructions by selecting "Agrees" in the "Aftercare agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Enter the client's full name, as per the "Full name of client" hint.
* **Procedure date**: Select the scheduled date for the retouch procedure.
* **Previous procedure date**: If the client has undergone a previous brow lift, enter the date of that procedure. This information will help the clinician understand the client's history.
* **Known allergies**: List any known allergies or sensitivities relevant to the procedure. This will help the clinician take necessary precautions during and after the procedure.
* **Consent given**: Check "True" to indicate that the client has been informed of the risks and benefits associated with the procedure and has provided consent.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the client's contact phone number for appointment confirmation.
* **Aftercare agreement**: Confirm that the client agrees to follow aftercare instructions by selecting "Agrees".
* **Clinician notes**: This field is for clinician use only and should not be completed by the client. If required, enter any additional notes or comments for the clinician's reference.
* **Aftercare agreement**: Confirm that the client agrees to follow aftercare instructions by selecting "Agrees".
