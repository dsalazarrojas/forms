# Medical Outreach Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used for patients to provide medical outreach vaccination information and consent. It is typically used in medical settings to collect relevant details from patients and obtain their consent for vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's details, including any relevant medical history, if known.
2. Select the specific vaccination details that apply to the patient.
3. Choose the medical team members who will be involved in the patient's care.
4. Sign the form to confirm your consent for the vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Details (page 1)
* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): This is the patient's basic information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details. Please provide this information as accurately and completely as possible.

### Medical History (page 2)
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for any relevant medical history that may impact the patient's treatment. You may include any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications that the patient has.

### Vaccination Details (page 3)
* **Vaccination Details** (`vaccination_details`, select one, optional): Please select the specific vaccination details that apply to the patient. The options are:
	+ Option 1: Vaccination A
	+ Option 2: Vaccination B
	+ Option 3: Vaccination C

### Medical Team (page 3)
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select multiple, optional): Choose the medical team members who will be involved in the patient's care. The options are:
	+ Option 1: Team Member 1
	+ Option 2: Team Member 2
	+ Option 3: Team Member 3

### Patient Signature (page 4)
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm that you have read and understand the form. Please provide your signature and date.

### Healthcare Provider Signature (page 5)
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, text, optional): Sign this field to confirm that you have received the patient's information and that you will respect their consent. Please provide your signature and date.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance from the medical team.
- This form is confidential and should be handled with care.
