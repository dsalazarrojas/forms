# Virtual Doula Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Doula Consent Form is a document that outlines the terms of consent for clients to share their personal information and medical consents with their designated healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information accurately and truthfully.
2. Select your preferred language.
3. If applicable, provide any medical conditions that may affect your care.
4. Enter the name of your doula provider.
5. Choose a contact method (phone, email, or text) and enter your contact number or email address.
6. Identify your emergency contact and their relationship to you.
7. Provide your emergency contact's name, address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
8. Confirm your consent to share information with your healthcare provider.
9. Specify who you want to share information with and the relationship with that person.
10. Add any special instructions or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information**: Enter your information accurately and truthfully to ensure that your healthcare provider has a clear understanding of your needs.
  - **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - **Birthdate**: Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
  - **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication with your healthcare provider.
- **Client Name**: Enter your doula provider's name as it will be used in communication with them.
- **Doula Agency**: If applicable, enter the name of the agency that the doula is affiliated with.
- **Contact Method**: Choose a method to contact you (phone, email, or text).
- **Contact Number/Email**: Enter your contact number or email address depending on the contact method chosen.
- **Emergency Contact**: Identify the person you want to be notified in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter your emergency contact's name.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (spouse, parent, sibling, other).
- **Emergency Contact Phone, Address, City, State, Postal Code**: Enter your emergency contact's contact information.
- **Consent to Share Information**: Confirm your consent to share information with your healthcare provider.
- **Consent to Share Information with**: Specify who you want to share information with and their relationship to you.
- **Share Information with**: Enter the name of the person you want to share information with.
- **Share Information with Relationship**: Confirm the relationship with the person you want to share information with.
- **Medical Consents**: If applicable, provide any medical conditions that may affect your care.
- **Consent to Share Information with Notes**: If applicable, provide any additional information to clarify your consent to share information with others.
- **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or notes as necessary for your healthcare provider.
