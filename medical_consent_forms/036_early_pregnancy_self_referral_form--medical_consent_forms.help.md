# **<string> - Help Guide**
## **Purpose**
This form is used to collect medical information from patients, including demographic details, contact information, medical history, and health insurance details.

## **How To Complete This Form**
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient's demographic information in the "Patient Information" section, including name, date of birth, and social security number.
2. Provide information about previous pregnancies, including due date and any previous pregnancy outcomes.
3. Enter contact information, including phone number and email.
4. List any previous medical tests, medical procedures, and medical history.
5. List any allergies, medications, and medical procedures.

## **Field-by-Field Explanation**
### **Patient Information**
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and social security number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birthday`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's social security number.
* **Pregnancy Info** (`pregnancy_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the patient's pregnancy.
* **Pregnancy Due Date** (`pregnancy_due_date`, date, required): Enter the patient's expected due date.
* **Previous Pregnancies**
	+ **Previous Pregnancies** (`previous_pregnancies`, text, required): List any previous pregnancies, including any complications or outcomes.
	+ **Previous Pregnancy Outcomes** (`previous_pregnancy_outcomes`, text, required): List any previous pregnancy outcomes, including miscarriages, abortions, or live births.
	+ **Previous Pregnancy Complications** (`previous_pregnancy_complications`, text, required): List any complications or issues that occurred during previous pregnancies.
* **Contact Info**
	+ **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
	+ **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Info**
	+ **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required): List any relevant medical information, including conditions, allergies, or medical procedures.
	+ **Doctor** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's primary care physician's name.
* **Health Insurance**
	+ **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, required): Enter the patient's health insurance information.
* **Primary Care Physician**
	+ **Primary Care Physician** (`primary_care_physician`, text, required): Enter the name of the patient's primary care physician.
* **Medical History**
	+ **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): List any relevant medical history, including conditions, procedures, or allergies.
* **Allergies**
	+ **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medications**
	+ **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any current or past medications.
* **Medical Tests**
	+ **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, required): List any medical tests or procedures performed.
* **Medical Procedures**
	+ **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, required): List any medical procedures performed.
* **Surgical History**
	+ **Surgical History** (`surgical_history`, text, required): List any surgical history, including surgeries performed.

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your healthcare provider or contact the form's administrators.
