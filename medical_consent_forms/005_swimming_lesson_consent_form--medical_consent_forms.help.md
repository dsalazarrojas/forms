# swimming_lesson_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from a parent or guardian for a child's swimming lesson program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's parent's name and contact information (pages 1-4).
2. Enter the child's name, date of birth, and medical conditions (pages 5-7).
3. Confirm the child's swimmer ID and emergency contact information (pages 8-10).
4. Indicate the medical insurance status and provide the emergency contact phone number (pages 11-12).
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter the child's parent's or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Please enter the child's parent's or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Please enter the child's parent's or guardian's phone number.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Please enter the child's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter the child's date of birth.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If the child has any medical conditions that may affect their participation in the swim lesson program, please list them here.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, text, optional): If the child has any allergies or medical allergies that may affect their participation in the swim lesson program, please list them here.
* **Swimmer ID** (`swimmer_id`, number, required): Please enter the child's swimmer ID.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Please select the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, select_multiple, optional): Please indicate if the child has medical insurance, is self-pay, or neither.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Parent's Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please enter the time.
* **Swim Session Length** (`swim_session_length`, number, optional): Please select the swim session length (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
