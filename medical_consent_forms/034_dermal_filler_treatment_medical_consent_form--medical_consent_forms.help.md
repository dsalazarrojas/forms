# dermal_filler_treatment_medical_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a medical consent form for dermal filler treatments. It is designed to educate and inform patients about the risks and considerations involved in the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient information (page 1) with your name, address, and contact details.
2. Provide your medical history (page 2) if you have any allergies or previous treatments that may be relevant to your condition.
3. Answer the question about your medical condition (page 3) to help the medical professional understand your condition and provide the best possible treatment.
4. Indicate your consent (page 4) to the treatment by selecting the correct answer.
5. Sign your name (page 5) to confirm that you have read and understood the risks and considerations involved in the treatment.
6. Fill in the date and time of your appointment (page 6) to schedule your treatment.
7. Provide the name of your medical consultant (page 7) if you have one.
8. Enter your patient's name (page 8) if you are not the patient.
9. Enter your medical provider's name and contact details (page 9) if you are a medical provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)** (`title`: `dermal_filler_treatment_medical_consent_form`) - This page is to collect your basic patient information such as name, address, and contact details. Please fill in your details accurately.
* **Medical History (page 2)** (`medical_history`) - If you have any allergies or previous treatments that may be relevant to your condition, please provide them in this page.
* **Allergies (page 3)** (`allergies`) - Please select any allergies you have, such as Allergic to Penicillin, Allergic to Latex, Allergic to Sulfites, or other allergies.
* **Surgical History (page 4)** (`surgical_history`) - If you have any past surgeries or medical procedures that may be relevant to your condition, please provide them in this page.
* **Medical Condition (page 5)** (`medical_condition`) - Please describe your current medical condition to help the medical professional understand your condition and provide the best possible treatment.
* **Consent (page 6)** (`consent`) - Please indicate your consent to the treatment by selecting the correct answer.
* **Signature (page 7)** (`signature`) - Please sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the risks and considerations involved in the treatment.
* **Date (page 8)** (`date`) - Please fill in the date of your appointment to schedule your treatment.
* **Time (page 9)** (`time`) - Please fill in the time of your appointment to schedule your treatment.
* **Medical Consultant (page 10)** (`medical_consultant`) - If you have a medical consultant, please provide their name.
* **Patient Name (page 11)** (`patient_name`) - If you are not the patient, please enter the patient's name.
* **Medical Provider (page 12)** (`medical_provider`) - If you are a medical provider, please enter your name and contact details.

Note: Please fill in the form carefully and accurately.
