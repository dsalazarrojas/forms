# Emotional Support Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting emotional support services consent from clients, ensuring they understand and agree to receive these services. It captures essential information, including client contact details, consent choices, and signature fields for both the client and service provider. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out the client contact section**: This includes the client's name, address, phone number, and email. Ensure accuracy and completeness.
2.  **Select your chosen services**: Choose which emotional support services you consent to receiving. You may select one or more of the services.
3.  **Choose how often you'd like to receive services**: Choose your preferred service frequency from the provided options.
4.  **Specify your service start and end dates**: Enter the date when you'd like services to begin and end. 
5.  **Sign and date the form**: Place your signature and date in the designated fields. This confirms your consent to receiving emotional support services.
6.  **Add any additional notes if necessary**: If you have any further information or requests, add them in the 'Additional notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Client Address (Required)**: Provide your address for contact and service delivery purposes.
* **Client Phone (Required)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact and service notifications.
* **Client Email (Required)**: Enter your email address for secure communication with the service provider.
* **Do you consent to receiving emotional support services? (Required)**: Select 'Yes' to indicate your consent.
*   **What services do you consent to receiving? (Required)**: Choose one or more emotional support services you consent to receive.
*   **How often would you like to receive services? (Required)**: Choose the frequency of service sessions from the provided options.
*   **Service Start Date (Required)**: Enter the date when you'd like services to start.
*   **Service End Date (Required)**: Enter the date when you'd like services to end.
*   **Client Signature (Required)**: Sign and date this field to confirm your consent.
*   **Provider Signature (Required)**: Sign and date this field to confirm your agreement to provide services.
*   **Date Signed (Required)**: Enter the date you signed this form.
*   **Emergency Contact (Optional)**: If applicable, provide an emergency contact person's name and contact information.
*   **Relation to Emergency Contact (Optional)**: If applicable, describe your relation to the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (Optional)**: If applicable, enter the emergency contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Email (Optional)**: If applicable, enter the emergency contact email address.
*   **Emergency Contact Address (Optional)**: If applicable, provide the emergency contact's address.
*   **Emergency Contact Notes (Optional)**: If applicable, add any additional notes about the emergency contact.
*   **Client Agreement (Required)**: Read and agree to the terms of service.
*   **Client Agreement Signature (Required)**: Sign and date this field to confirm your agreement.
*   **Provider Agreement (Required)**: Read and agree to the terms of service.
*   **Provider Agreement Signature (Required)**: Sign and date this field to confirm your agreement.
*   **Agreement Date (Required)**: Enter the date you signed this form.

Remember to carefully read each field and ensure accuracy before proceeding. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask.
