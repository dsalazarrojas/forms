# Infusion Therapy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infusion Therapy Form is used to collect information from patients undergoing infusion therapy treatment. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure timely and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill in your first name in the field marked `patient_first_name`.
2. Please fill in your last name in the field marked `patient_last_name`.
3. Enter your email address in the field marked `patient_email`.
4. Enter a valid phone number in the field marked `patient_phone`.
5. Select the date of your infusion treatment in the field marked `infusion_date`.
6. Select the time of your infusion treatment in the field marked `infusion_time`.
7. Add any additional notes from your therapist in the field marked `therapist_notes`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_first_name**: Enter your first name as it is recorded in your official documents.
* **patient_last_name**: Enter your last name as it is recorded in your official documents.
* **patient_email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **patient_phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **infusion_date**: Select the date of your infusion treatment appointment.
* **infusion_time**: Select the time of your infusion treatment appointment.
* **therapist_notes**: Any additional notes from your therapist will be recorded here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for medical care.
