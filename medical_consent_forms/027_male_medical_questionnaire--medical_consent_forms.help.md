# Male Medical Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Male Medical Questionnaire is a form designed to collect patient information for medical purposes. It is intended for male patients and is a crucial part of the medical process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your demographic information on the first page.
2. Enter your contact information on the second page.
3. Share your medical history on the third page.
4. Review and sign the consent section on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your basic demographic information such as name, date of birth, and gender.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant medical history, including your medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: true): Please review and select one of the options to give your consent for medical treatment.
