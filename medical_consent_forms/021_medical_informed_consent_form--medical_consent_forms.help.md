# Medical Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture medical information and consent from patients for medical procedures or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your information in the required fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Informed Consent Form** (`1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the medical procedure or treatment.
* **patient_information** (`2`, text, required): Please provide the following information about the patient.
* **doctor_information** (`3`, text, required): Please provide the following information about the doctor.
* **consent_date** (`4`, date, required): Enter the date of the consent.
* **patient_name** (`5`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name (if desired).
* **doctor_name** (`6`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's name (if desired).
* **patient_address** (`7`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address (if desired).
* **patient_email** (`8`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **phone** (`9`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number (if desired).
* **medical_history** (`10`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical history (if desired).
* **allergies** (`11`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if the patient has allergies.
* **emergency_contact** (`12`, text, optional): If the patient is unable to provide consent, please provide an emergency contact.
* **contact_name** (`13`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **contact_number** (`14`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **contact_relationship** (`15`, select_one, optional): Check the relationship between the contact and the patient (Parent/Guardian, Spouse, Family member, Other).
* **signature** (`16`, text, optional): Signature of the person filling out this form (if required by institution).
* **patient_signature** (`17`, text, optional): Signature of the patient (if desired).
* **doctor_signature** (`18`, text, optional): Signature of the doctor (if desired).
* **consent_date** (`19`, date, required): Enter the date of the consent (same as above, please use this field to enter the date of the consent).
* **time** (`20`, time, optional): Enter the time of the procedure or treatment (if desired).
* **medical_license_number** (`21`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **medical_license_state** (`22`, select_one, optional): Select your state of medical practice.
* **medical_license** (`23`, text, required): Enter your medical license number.
* **medical_institution** (`24`, text, required): Enter your medical institution or department.
* **medical_department** (`25`, text, required): Enter your medical department or specialty.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
