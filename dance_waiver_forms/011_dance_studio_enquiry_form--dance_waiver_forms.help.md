# Dance Studio Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from prospective dancers or their parents about their dance studio experience or needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your information in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. You can skip questions that are not relevant to your situation.
4. If you have any special requests or medical conditions that are not listed, please contact the dance studio staff to discuss them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Dance Studio Enquiry 
* **Dance Studio Enquiry**: Please provide a brief description of your interest or experience with dance studios. This could be your favorite type of dance, your current dance level, or your reason for enrolling in dance classes.

### Dance Class Details 
* **Dance Class Details**: Please specify the type of dance class you are interested in (e.g., ballet, modern, hip-hop, etc.) and any specific style or level (beginner, intermediate, advanced, etc.).

### Parent Details 
* **Parent Details**: If you are a parent enrolling your child in dance classes, please provide your name and contact information so the studio can contact you to discuss your child's registration.

### Emergency Details 
* **Emergency Details**: In case of an emergency, please provide the name and contact information of the person who will be contacted.

### Medical Conditions 
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in dance classes, please list them here.

### Special Requests 
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests (e.g., schedule accommodations, equipment needs, etc.), please list them here.
