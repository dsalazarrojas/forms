# Dance Performance Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Performance Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge and accept the risks involved in dance activities. It is a legal document that protects both the participant and the organization hosting the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the form carefully.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully.
3. Sign and date the participant's section to confirm that they have read and agreed to the waiver.
4. Sign and date the emergency contact's section to confirm that they have given permission for the participant to participate in the dance activities.
5. Ensure that all sections are completed correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I understand the risks involved in dance activities**: This is a statement that requires you to acknowledge that you are aware of the potential risks involved in dance activities. Please type 'Yes' in this field to confirm that you have read and understand the risks.
* **Participant Name**: This is your name, as the participant. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: This is your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency. If you have no emergency contact, please enter 'None'.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This is the phone number of the emergency contact. If you have no emergency contact, please leave this field blank.
* **Participant Email**: This is your email address. If you have no email address, please leave this field blank.
* **Relationship**: This is the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g. parent, guardian, spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Signature**: This is where your emergency contact signs to confirm that they have given permission for you to participate in the dance activities. Please have them sign and date this section.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: This is the address of the emergency contact. If you have no address, please leave this field blank.
* **Participant Signature**: This is where you sign to confirm that you have read and agreed to the waiver. Please sign and date this section.
