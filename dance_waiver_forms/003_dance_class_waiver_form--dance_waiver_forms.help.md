# Dance Class Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a waiver form for dance classes, designed to ensure the safety and well-being of students during classes. It collects information about the student's personal details, emergency contacts, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name and email.
2. Provide information about your emergency contacts, including their name, phone number, and relationship with you.
3. If you have any medical conditions, select the relevant options and provide a brief description of your condition.
4. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dance Class Waiver Form** (1): This is the form title, please note that this field is not editable.
* **Name** (2): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (5): Enter the name of your emergency contact, e.g., parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (6): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (7): Select the relationship between the student and emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Other** (8): If the relationship is "Other", provide a brief description of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (9): Enter the address of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (10): Enter your date of birth, in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (11): Provide any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition** (12): Select any medical conditions you have, e.g., Allergies/Phobias, Asthma, etc.
* **Medical Condition Details** (13): Provide a brief description of your medical condition.
* **Emergency Contact Name 2** (14): Enter the name of your secondary emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (15): Enter the phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (16): Select the relationship between the student and secondary emergency contact (Parent, Guardian, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Other 2** (17): If the relationship is "Other", provide a brief description of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (18): Enter the address of your secondary emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Note 2** (19): Provide any additional information about your secondary emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition 2** (20): Select any medical conditions you have, e.g., Allergies/Phobias, Asthma, etc.
* **Medical Condition 2 Details** (21): Provide a brief description of your medical condition 2.
* **Parent Signature** (22): The parent or guardian must sign this form to confirm that they have read and understood the waiver.
* **Student Name** (23): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Signature** (24): You must sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver.
* **Waiver** (25): This is a note field for any additional information or comments.
