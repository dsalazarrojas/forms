# dance_choreography_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dance_choreography_recording_consent_form is designed to obtain consent from students' parents or guardians for recording dance classes. This form helps to inform parents about the recording process and gather their consent, ensuring that all parties involved are aware and on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's dance class information (page 1).
2. Provide your name and email address if you're a parent or guardian (pages 2-3).
3. Indicate your consent as a parent or guardian (page 4).
4. Specify your recording preferences (page 5).
5. If you have a specific recording location outside of the provided options, you can specify it (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Class Info** (`dance_class_info`, text, required: false): Enter the student's dance class name, teacher, or other relevant information.
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **student_email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **parent_name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **parent_email** (`parent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as a parent or guardian.
* **guardian_consent** (`guardian_consent`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you grant consent for recording the dance class.
* **recording_preferences** (`recording_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple options for recording preferences:
	+ I have no preference for recording
	+ Record in class
	+ Record in studio
	+ Record in other (please specify)
* **other_recording_location** (`other_recording_location`, text, required: false): If you selected 'Record in other (please specify)', enter the location where you would like the recording to take place.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for completing this form and that the information you provide is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the dance school administration.
* This form is intended for dance schools to gather consent from parents or guardians. If you are not a parent or guardian, you may not be able to complete this form.
