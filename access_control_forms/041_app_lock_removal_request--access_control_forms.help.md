# App Lock Removal Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the removal of an app lock on a device.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name as the requester in the **Requester name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Contact email** field for updates about your request.
3. Provide the **Device identifier or serial number** for the device you're requesting the app lock removal for.
4. Briefly describe the **Reason for lock removal** in the text field.
5. If you have any **Authorization document reference**, please provide it here.
6. Choose the **Urgency level** that best describes your situation (Low, Medium, or High).
7. If your request has already been approved or denied, please select the appropriate **Approval status**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Enter your name as the person requesting the app lock removal.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for updates about your request.
* **Device identifier or serial number**: If the device has a unique identifier, enter it. This could be the IMEI or serial number.
* **Reason for lock removal**: Briefly describe why you're requesting the app lock removal. This will help us understand your situation and process your request.
* **Authorization document reference**: If you have any documentation that confirms your request, please provide the reference number or note here.
* **Urgency level**: Choose the level that best describes your situation:
	+ Low: Not urgent at all.
	+ Medium: Somewhat urgent, but can be processed within a few days.
	+ High: Very urgent, please expedite the process.
* **Approval status**: If your request has already been approved or denied, select the corresponding option.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you only submit one request per person and/or device.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the contact information provided at the end of this guide.
* We'll review and process your request as soon as possible, but please be patient as we work to verify your information and confirm the app lock removal.
