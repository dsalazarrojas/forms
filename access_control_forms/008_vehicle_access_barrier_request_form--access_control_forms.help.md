# Vehicle Access Barrier Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request vehicle access to a secure location or facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Input field with your employee ID or other relevant identifier.
2. Select the User Vehicle type from the dropdown list, if applicable.
3. Enter the Date and Time of your visit.
4. Choose the Gate Type from the dropdown list.
5. Provide a brief Reason for your visit in the Reason field.
6. If you are driving a vehicle, select all the vehicle types that apply to you from the Vehicle Type dropdown list.
7. Enter the Vehicle Plate Number.
8. Select your preferred method of contact from the Contact Information dropdown list.
9. Enter your additional contact details in the Contact Details field.
10. Optionally, add any additional Notes to the form.
11. Select the Gate Access type from the dropdown list, whether to Grant Access or Deny.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, Text, Optional): Enter your employee ID or other identifier.
* **User Vehicle** (`user_vehicle`, Text, Optional): Select from the list of available vehicle types.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, Date, Optional): Enter the date and time of your visit.
* **Gate Type** (`gate_type`, Select, Optional): Choose the type of gate you are visiting.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Text, Optional): Briefly explain the reason for your visit.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, Select, Multiple, Optional): Select all the vehicle types that apply to your visit.
* **Vehicle Plate Number** (`vehicle_plate`, Text, Optional): Enter the vehicle's plate number.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, Select, Optional): Choose your preferred method of contact.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, Text, Optional): Enter additional contact details if needed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Add any additional information relevant to your visit.
* **Gate Access** (`gate_access`, Select, Multiple, Optional): Select the access type you require, whether to Grant or Deny.

# Tips
* Be sure to fill out this form accurately and completely.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the facility manager for assistance.
* Duplicate submissions are not allowed, and each submission should be unique.
* If you need to make multiple submissions, please submit one form per vehicle.
