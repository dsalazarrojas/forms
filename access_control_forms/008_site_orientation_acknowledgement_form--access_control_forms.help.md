# Site Orientation Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Orientation Acknowledgement Form is designed to be a quick and simple form that allows site orientation completion status to be tracked and recorded. This form ensures that employees, contractors, and visitors acknowledge that they have completed the site orientation, which is a mandatory safety and security procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Site Orientation Acknowledgement Form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" to confirm that you have completed the site orientation.
2. Enter your name in the "Employee/Contractor/Visitor" field.
3. Enter the date you completed the site orientation in the "Date Completed" field.
4. Enter the time you completed the site orientation in the "Time Completed" field.
5. Enter the location where you completed the site orientation in the "Location" field.
6. Type in a statement to acknowledge that you have completed the site orientation in the "Acknowledgement Statement" field.
7. Enter the date you are acknowledging completion of the site orientation in the "Acknowledgement Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Orientation Form** (`site_orientation_acknowledgement_form`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have completed the site orientation.
* **Employee/Contractor/Visitor** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or badge number.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the site orientation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, required): Enter the time you completed the site orientation in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where you completed the site orientation (e.g. Building 1, 2nd floor).
* **Acknowledgement Statement** (`acknowledgement_statement`, text, required): Type in a statement to acknowledge that you have completed the site orientation (e.g. "I have completed the site orientation and understand the safety and security procedures.").
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the relevant authorities.
* This form is only for acknowledging completion of the site orientation, if you have not completed it, do not submit this form.
