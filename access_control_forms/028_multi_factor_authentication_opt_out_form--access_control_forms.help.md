# Multi Factor Authentication Opt Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request exemption from Multi Factor Authentication, a security feature that ensures an additional layer of verification for users accessing certain systems or resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field. This field is required and should be filled in with your full name.
2. Select your risk level from the options provided in the "What is your risk level?" field. This field is required and will help us assess your risk profile.
3. Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field. This field is required and should be filled in with a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field. This field is required and should be filled in with a valid phone number.
5. If you have any security questions for our team, you can answer "Yes" or "No" in the "Do you have any security questions for our team?" field. This field is optional.
6. If you have any additional comments or questions about MFA, you can enter them in the "Do you have any additional comments or questions about MFA?" field. This field is optional.
7. Select your preferred method of contact from the options provided in the "How would you like us to follow up with you?" field. This field is optional.
8. Acknowledge our security policies by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Do you acknowledge our security policies?" field. This field is required.
9. Enter the method by which you would like to be contacted in the "How may we contact you?" field. This field is required.
10. If you have any other comments or questions, you can answer "Yes" or "No" in the "Do you have any other comments or questions?" field. This field is optional.
11. Confirm that you have reviewed the terms and conditions of this form by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Have you reviewed the terms and conditions of this form?" field. This field is required.
12. Finally, please enter any additional comments or questions you may have in the "Do you have any other comments or questions?" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`form_1`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **What is your risk level?** (`form_2`, `select_one`, required): Select your risk level from the options provided. Our team will use this to assess your risk profile.
* **What is your email address?** (`form_3`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`form_4`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Do you have any security questions for our team?** (`form_5`, `select_one`, optional): If you have any security questions, please select "Yes" to indicate that you have questions for our team.
* **Do you have any additional comments or questions about MFA?** (`form_6`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about MFA.
* **How would you like us to follow up with you?** (`form_7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the methods by which you would like us to contact you. You can select multiple options.
* **Do you acknowledge our security policies?** (`form_8`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to acknowledge our security policies.
* **How may we contact you?** (`form_9`, `text`, required): Enter the method by which you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone number, email address, etc.).
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?** (`form_10`, `select_one`, optional): If you have any other comments or questions, please answer "Yes" to indicate that you have them.
* **Have you reviewed the terms and conditions of this form?** (`form_11`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have reviewed the terms and conditions of this form.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?** (`form_12`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
