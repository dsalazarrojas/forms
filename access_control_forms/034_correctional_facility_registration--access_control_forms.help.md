# Correctional Facility Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Correctional Facility Registration form is used to gather information about registrants visiting a correctional facility. This form is required to be completed in order to ensure proper processing and tracking of the registrant's visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, please ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and the information required.
2. Begin by completing the Registrant Information section.
3. Next, provide the Registrant Name.
4. Move on to the Contact Information section, where you can enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
5. In the Rule Acknowledgments section, select all the rules that apply to the registrant's visit.
6. Proceed to the Visit Details section, where you can enter the visit date and time.
7. Finally, provide the Visit Notes and Registrant Signature, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Information** (id: 1): Enter the registrant's personal details.
* **Registrant Name** (id: 2): Enter the registrant's name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Contact Information** (id: 3): Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Person** (id: 4): Select the contact person from the provided options.
* **Rule Acknowledgments** (id: 5): Acknowledge all the rules that apply to the registrant's visit, ensuring compliance with each rule.
* **Visit Details** (id: 6): Enter the visit date and time as they pertain to the registrant's visit.
* **Visit Date** (id: 7): Enter the date of the registrant's visit.
* **Visit Time** (id: 8): Enter the time of the registrant's visit.
* **Contact Email** (id: 9): Enter the contact email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 10): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Registrant ID Number** (id: 11): Enter the registrant's ID number, if applicable.
* **Registrant Type** (id: 12): Select the registrant type from the provided options.
* **Visit Notes** (id: 13): Enter any additional notes or comments about the registrant's visit.
* **Registrant Signature** (id: 14): Obtain the registrant's signature, if applicable.
* **Rule 1**, **Rule 2**, **Rule 3** (id: 5): Select all applicable rules for the registrant's visit.
*   *   **Rule 1** and **Rule 2** and **Rule 3** are examples of rules that may apply to the registrant's visit. 

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and completeness in each field.
* Verify the registrant's contact information and signature, if applicable.
* Review the rules carefully to ensure all applicable rules are acknowledged.
* Double-check the visit details and notes for correctness and accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, please ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant authorities.
