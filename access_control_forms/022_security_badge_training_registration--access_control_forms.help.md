# Security Badge Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Security Badge Training Registration" form is used to register users for security badge training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **session** option (Option 1 or Option 2) from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the **date** of the training session in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter the **time** of the training session in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
4. Enter your **email** address to receive updates about the training session.
5. Enter your **phone number** for contact purposes.
6. Provide **session details** about your training needs and preferences.
7. Select the **assigned tool** that you would like to use during the training session (Option 1 or Option 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session (session)
* **session**: Select the training session you would like to attend (Option 1 or Option 2)

### Date (date)
* **date**: Enter the date of the training session in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 2022-07-25)

### Time (time)
* **time**: Enter the time of the training session in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` (e.g., 02:00 PM)

### Email (attendees_email)
* **email**: Enter your email address to receive updates about the training session

### Phone Number (attendees_phone)
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes

### Session Details (session_details)
* **session_details**: Provide any additional details about your training needs and preferences

### Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)
* **assigned_tool**: Select the tool you would like to use during the training session (Option 1 or Option 2)

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information about your training preferences and availability.
* If you are having trouble selecting a session option, please contact your training administrator for assistance.
* This form is only for registering users for security badge training sessions.
