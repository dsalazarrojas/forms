# trust_service_provider_identity_verification_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity of a trust service provider, which is essential for any financial or legal transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information accurately and truthfully.
2. Double-check the filled-out fields for any errors or discrepancies.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trust service provider info (trust_service_provider_info, text, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the trust service provider.
* **name (name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the trust service provider.
* **email (email, text, optional)**: Enter the email address of the trust service provider. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* **address (address, text, optional)**: Enter the address of the trust service provider.
* **phone_number (phone number, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the trust service provider.
* **date of birth (date of birth, date, optional)**: Enter the date of birth of the trust service provider.
* **identity_document (identity document, text, optional)**: Describe the identity document of the trust service provider, such as a passport or ID card.
* **trust_service_provider_id (trust service provider id, text, optional)**: Enter the ID of the trust service provider.
