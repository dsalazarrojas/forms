# Open Knowledge Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Knowledge Platform Access Request form is used to request access to the Open Knowledge Platform, a platform that allows users to share and discover knowledge across the organization. This form helps us to process your request efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Requester Name** in the text field. This is the name of the person or department requesting access.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the text field. This will be used to contact you regarding the status of your request.
3. Describe the **Purpose** of your request in the text field. This will help us understand why you need access to the platform.
4. Confirm that you have read and agree to the **Terms Agreement** by checking the text field.
5. Select your **Intended Use** from the dropdown list to specify how you plan to use the platform.
6. Select your **Access Type** from the dropdown list to specify the type of access you need. This will determine the level of access you are granted.
7. Choose your **Access Level** from the dropdown list to specify the level of access you need. This will determine the scope of the platform features you can access.
8. Set your **Access Start** date by selecting a date from the calendar input field. This will determine when your access begins.
9. Set your **Access End** date by selecting a date from the calendar input field. This will determine when your access ends.
10. Add any **Additional Info** about your request in the text field. This is an optional field, but it can help us process your request more efficiently.
11. Enter your **Submitter Name** in the text field. This is the name of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
12. Enter your **Submitter Email** address in the text field. This will be used to contact you regarding the status of your request.
13. Enter your **Submitter Phone** number in the text field. This is an optional field, but it can be used to contact you if we need more information.
14. Enter your **Submitter Department** in the text field. This is the department of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
15. Enter your **Submitter Position** in the text field. This is the position of the person submitting the request on your behalf.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or department requesting access.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person or department requesting access.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required/optional): Describe the purpose of your request.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, text, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of use.
* **Intended Use** (`intended_use`, select_one, required/optional): Select how you plan to use the platform.
* **Access Type** (`access_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of access you need.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose the level of access you need.
* **Access Start** (`access_start`, date, required): Set the date when your access begins.
* **Access End** (`access_end`, date, required): Set the date when your access ends.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information about your request.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, required/optional): Enter the position of the person submitting the request on your behalf.
