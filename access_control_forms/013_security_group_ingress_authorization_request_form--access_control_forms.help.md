# Security Group Ingress Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a security group within the organization. It is intended for administrative purposes and to streamline the process of access control decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields as follows:
    *   **Security Group ID**: Enter the ID of the security group you are requesting access to.
    *   **Description**: Provide a brief description of the reason for your request.
    *   **Request Date**: Enter the date and time of your request.
    *   **Security Group**: Select the security group you are requesting access to from the dropdown menu.
    *   **Request Reason**: Enter a brief description of your request.
    *   **Approver**: Choose the person you are requesting to approve your access to the security group from the dropdown menu.
    *   **Approver Email**: Enter the email of the person you are requesting to approve your access to the security group.
    *   **Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you are requesting to approve your access to the security group.
    *   **Status**: Select the status of your request (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
    *   **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments you have regarding your request.
    *   **Approved By**: Choose the person who approved your request from the dropdown menu.
    *   **Approved At**: Enter the date and time when your request was approved.
    *   **Updated At**: Enter the last date and time when your request was updated.
    *   **Created At**: Enter the date and time when your request was created.
    *   **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time when your request was submitted.
2.  Click submit to send your request.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting your request.
*   Select the correct security group you are requesting access to.
*   Choose the correct approver for your request.
*   Enter the correct status of your request.
*   Provide any additional notes or comments to help the approver make an informed decision.
