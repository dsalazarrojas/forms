# Sea Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sea Access Request Form is used to request access to the sea or specific areas within it. This form helps the requestor provide necessary information for review and approval by relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of your request for access to the sea.
2.  Provide the name of the organization or individual making the request.
3.  Specify the type of access you are requesting (e.g., Commercial Use, Marina Usage, etc.).
4.  Enter the name and contact information of the person requesting access.
5.  Enter your contact phone number.
6.  Enter your contact email address.
7.  Select the start and end times for your access request.
8.  Enter the duration of your access request in hours.
9.  Enter the specific location you are requesting access to.
10.  Provide any additional details about your request.
11.  Add any comments or notes about your access request.
12.  Enter your name, indicating that you are submitting the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your request for access to the sea.
*   **Requesting Organisation** (`requesting_organisation`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the organization or individual making the request.
*   **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, required: false): Specify the type of access you are requesting (e.g., Commercial Use, Marina Usage, etc.).
*   **Requestor** (`requestor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or group making the request.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact for more information.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the person to contact for more information.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email address of the person to contact for more information.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Select the start time of your access request.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Select the end time of your access request.
*   **Duration** (`duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of your access request in hours.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the specific location you are requesting access to.
*   **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your request.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments or notes about your access request.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required: false): Enter your name, indicating that you are submitting the request.
