# Access Control System Issue Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report issues with the access control system, such as malfunctioning badge readers, access point unresponsiveness, or incorrect access permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, employee ID, and email address to identify yourself as the reporter.
2. Select your department from the options provided.
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing, including the type of issue, location, and date it occurred.
4. Choose the priority level of the issue (Low, Medium, High, or Emergency).
5. Provide a detailed description of the issue and any error messages or unusual behavior you've observed.
6. Specify the number of people affected by the issue.
7. If a temporary workaround is available, select "True".
8. If this issue has been reported before, provide the previous ticket number.
9. Choose your preferred method of contact for updates (Email, Phone, or In-person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, text, required): Enter your company-issued employee identification number.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your work email for follow-up communications.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number in case of urgent follow-up.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Type of Access Control Issue** (`Type of Access Control Issue`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the problem.
* **Location of the Issue** (`Location of the Issue`, text, required): Specify building, floor, and door or access point number.
* **Date the Issue Occurred** (`Date the Issue Occurred`, date, required): Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Approximate Time of Issue** (`Approximate Time of Issue`, time, optional): Enter the approximate time the issue occurred.
* **Priority Level** (`Priority Level`, select_one, required): Choose how urgently the issue needs to be resolved.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (`Detailed Description of the Issue`, text, required): Provide as much detail as possible including error messages or unusual behavior.
* **Number of People Affected** (`Number of People Affected`, number, optional): Enter the number of people affected by the issue.
* **Is a Temporary Workaround Available** (`Is a Temporary Workaround Available`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if a temporary workaround is available.
* **Has This Issue Been Reported Before** (`Has This Issue Been Reported Before`, text, optional): If yes, provide the previous ticket number if available.
* **Preferred Contact Method for Updates** (`Preferred Contact Method for Updates`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method of contact for updates.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue to help the IT department resolve it quickly and effectively.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can leave them blank or ask for help from the IT department.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, contact the IT department for support.
