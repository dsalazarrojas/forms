# Wheelchair Accessible Door Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals request wheelchair accessible doors in their buildings. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide a description of the door you are requesting to be made wheelchair accessible.
3. Enter the door number, floor, and building where the door is located.
4. Indicate if the facilities team and property manager are involved in the request.
5. Specify if accessibility modifications are required.
6. Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
7. Select the status of the request (Active or Inactive).
8. Enter the date you are requesting the modification.
9. Enter your name and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Address**: Enter your building address where the wheelchair accessible door is located.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the door you are requesting to be made wheelchair accessible.
* **Door Number**: Enter the number of the door you are requesting to be made wheelchair accessible.
* **Floor**: Enter the floor number where the door is located.
* **Building**: Enter the building name or number where the door is located.
* **Department**: Enter the department or section of the building where the door is located.
* **Manager Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the manager who has agreed to the modification.
* **Manager Phone Number**: If applicable, enter the phone number of the manager who has agreed to the modification.
* **Manager Email**: If applicable, enter the email of the manager who has agreed to the modification.
* **Facilities Team**: Check the box if the facilities team is involved in the modification process.
* **Property Manager**: Check the box if the property manager is involved in the modification process.
* **Organization**: If you represent an organization, select 'Yes' to indicate this.
* **Accessibility Modifications**: Check the box if accessibility modifications are required.
* **Modification Details**: Provide any additional information about the accessibility modifications needed.
* **Date Requested**: Enter the date you are requesting the modification.
* **Requested By**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Status**: Select the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools or equipment assigned to you for the modification.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to work on the modification.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the user who updated the status of your request.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the status of your request was updated.
