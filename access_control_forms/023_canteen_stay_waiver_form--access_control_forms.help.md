# Canteen Stay Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request after-hours access to the canteen or facility for various reasons, such as events, group activities, or personal use. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and safe access experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements before starting.
2. Enter your personal information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, and student/employee ID.
3. Select your role or status at the facility (e.g., student, faculty, staff, volunteer, visitor, or other).
4. Provide your contact information, including email address and phone number.
5. If applicable, list the names of other group members if you are part of a group.
6. Specify the details of your access request, including the date, start and end times, and reason for access.
7. Confirm that you will treat the facility's property with care and respect.
8. Acknowledge that you understand and agree to follow all facility rules.
9. Acknowledge that you take liability for potential risks associated with after-hours access.
10. Agree to release the facility from liability for injuries or damage.
11. If you are under 18, ensure a parent or guardian signs the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name.
- **Last Name**: Your last name.
- **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth (required for all participants).
- **Student ID or Employee ID**: Your facility-issued ID number.
- **Role or Status at Facility**: Select one of the options to indicate your role or status at the facility.
- **Email Address**: Your email address, where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number**: Your contact number for emergency situations.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of your emergency contact person.
- **Relationship to Participant**: Describe your relationship to the participant.
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: The contact number for your emergency contact.
- **Date of After-Hours Access**: Specify the date you need access.
- **Start Time**: The time you will arrive at the facility.
- **End Time**: The time you will leave the facility.
- **Reason for After-Hours Access**: Select one of the provided options or describe your reason for access.
- **Please describe your activity**: Briefly explain the purpose of your activity.
- **Group Size**: If you are part of a group, specify the number of participants.
- **Names of Other Group Members**: List the names of all group members.
- **Supervisor or Staff Member Present**: Confirm whether a supervisor or staff member will be present.
- **Supervisor Name (if applicable)**: Provide the name of the supervising staff member.
- **Safety Agreement and Waiver**: Confirm that you understand and agree to follow all facility rules.
- **I understand and agree to follow all facility rules**: Select "Yes, I agree".
- **I acknowledge the risks of after-hours facility access**: Confirm that you take liability for potential risks.
- **I release the facility from liability for injuries or damage**: Select "Yes, I release".
- **I agree to treat facility property with care and respect**: Confirm that you will treat the facility's property with care and respect.
- **If under 18, parent or guardian consent required**: Ensure a parent or guardian signs this section if you are under 18 years old.

## Tips
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the facility management or staff for assistance.
- Ensure you review the form carefully and accurately before submission.
- If you are part of a group, please list all participants and their contact information.
- If you are under 18, please ensure a parent or guardian signs the form before submission.
- Remember to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and safe access experience.
