# Customer Inquiry Status Login - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Inquiry Status Login form is used by customer service representatives to update the status of customer inquiries. This form gathers necessary information to provide accurate and timely updates to customers, ensuring that their inquiries are efficiently managed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the customer's information to ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Update the customer inquiry status by selecting the relevant option from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter any relevant notes or comments about the customer inquiry.
4. Save the form to update the customer's inquiry status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Inquiry Status (`customer_inquiry_status`)
* **Status**: The current status of the customer inquiry.
  * Select the relevant option (New, In Progress, Completed, Replied, Replied and Reassigned, Closed) to indicate the current status.

### Customer Inquiry Id (`customer_inquiry_id`)
* **Customer Inquiry Id**: A unique identifier for the customer inquiry.
  * Enter the corresponding Id for the customer inquiry.

### Customer Name (`customer_name`)
* **Customer Name**: The name of the customer associated with the inquiry.
  * Enter the customer's name.

### Customer Phone (`customer_phone`)
* **Customer Phone**: The phone number of the customer associated with the inquiry.
  * Enter the customer's phone number.

### Customer Email (`customer_email`)
* **Customer Email**: The email address of the customer associated with the inquiry.
  * Enter the customer's email address.

### Customer Note (`customer_note`)
* **Customer Note**: Any additional notes or comments about the customer inquiry.
  * Enter any relevant notes or comments.

### Customer Inquiry Date (`customer_inquiry_date`)
* **Customer Inquiry Date**: The date the customer inquiry was made.
  * Enter the date the customer inquiry was made.

### Customer Inquiry Time (`customer_inquiry_time`)
* **Customer Inquiry Time**: The time the customer inquiry was made.
  * Enter the time the customer inquiry was made.

### Customer Phone Number (`customer_phone`)
* **Note:** This field is optional and should only be completed if the customer's phone number is available. 
  * Enter the customer's phone number if available. 

### Tips
* Always review the customer's information to ensure accuracy and up-to-date information.
* Use the drop-down menu to select the correct status for the customer inquiry.
* Enter any relevant notes or comments to provide additional context.
