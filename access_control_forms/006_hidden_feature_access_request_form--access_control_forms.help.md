# Hidden Feature Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hidden Feature Access Request Form is designed to collect information from users who want to access a hidden feature in the system. The form is used to evaluate and grant or deny access to the feature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
4. Click on the submit button to send the form to the approver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Information (1)** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you enter your name and contact information. Please fill out your name, email, and phone number accurately.
* **Requested Features (2)** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select the feature(s) you are requesting access to. Please be specific about which feature(s) you need access to.
* **Request Description (3)** (Text, Optional):
	+ Write a brief description of why you need access to the feature. This will help the approver understand your request.
* **Approver Information (4)** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter their name and contact information. Please do not fill out this field unless instructed otherwise.
* **Approver Notes (5)** (Note, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter any additional notes or comments regarding your request.
* **Submitter Notes (6)** (Note, Optional):
	+ This field is where you can enter any additional comments or notes regarding your request.
* **Submitter E-mail (7)** (Email, Optional):
	+ This field is where you enter your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and valid.
* **Submitter Phone (8)** (Text, Optional):
	+ This field is where you enter your phone number. Please ensure it is accurate and valid.
* **Submitter Department (9)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field is where you select the department you belong to.
* **Submitter Title (10)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field is where you select your title.
* **Approver Department (11)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver selects their department.
* **Approver Title (12)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver selects their title.
* **Date Approved (13)** (Date, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter the date they approved your request.
* **Time Approved (14)** (Time, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter the time they approved your request.
* **Approved By (15)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter their name.
* **Approved At (16)** (Time, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter the time they approved your request.
* **Comments (17)** (Note, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter any additional comments or notes regarding your request.
* **Status (18)** (Select One, Optional):
	+ This field is where the approver will enter the status of your request.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you have a legitimate reason for accessing the hidden feature.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or issues, please reach out to the approver.

By following these steps and understanding the purpose and fields of the form, you should be able to complete the Hidden Feature Access Request Form accurately and effectively.
