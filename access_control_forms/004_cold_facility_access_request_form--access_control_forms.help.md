# Cold Facility Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cold Facility Access Request Form is used to request access to cold facilities, such as storage warehouses, laboratories, or other climate-controlled areas. It gathers essential information for security, safety, and access approval purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, email, phone number, department, job title, and organization.
2. Select the cold facility you need to access from the list provided.
3. Specify the purpose of your visit, choosing the relevant option from the list.
4. Provide more details about your visit, if necessary.
5. Enter the date and time you plan to enter the facility and the expected duration of your visit.
6. Select the level of access requested (one-time, temporary, or recurring).
7. If your access is recurring, specify the schedule and any equipment you need to bring.
8. Confirm that you've completed the required safety training and select the date of completion.
9. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to work in a cold facility, provide the relevant information.
10. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
11. Provide your direct supervisor's name.
12. Confirm that your supervisor has approved this request.
13. Agree to follow all safety protocols.
14. Add any additional comments or special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your direct contact number.
* **Department or Division** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department or division name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization Name** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter your company or institution name.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `select_one`, required): Choose the specific cold facility you need to access from the list provided.
* **Purpose of Access** (`access_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your visit from the list.
* **Purpose Details** (`purpose_details`, `text`, required): Provide more information about your visit, if necessary.
* **Requested Access Date** (`requested_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you need access to the facility.
* **Expected Entry Time** (`entry_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you plan to enter the facility.
* **Expected Exit Time** (`exit_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you plan to leave the facility.
* **Expected Duration** (`duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the length of your visit from the list.
* **Type of Access Requested** (`access_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of access requested from the list.
* **Recurring Schedule** (`recurring_schedule`, `text`, optional): If your access is recurring, specify the schedule.
* **Equipment You Will Bring** (`equipment_needed`, `text`, optional): List any equipment or tools you need to bring.
* **Personal Protective Equipment Needed** (`ppe_required`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any PPE you need to wear from the list.
* **Cold Facility Safety Training Completed** (`safety_training_completed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you've completed the required safety training.
* **Safety Training Completion Date** (`training_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of safety training completion.
* **Medical Conditions Affecting Cold Exposure** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Supervisor or Manager Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Approval Status** (`supervisor_approval`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your supervisor has approved this request.
* **Safety Rules Acknowledgment** (`safety_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Agree to follow all safety protocols.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or special requirements.
