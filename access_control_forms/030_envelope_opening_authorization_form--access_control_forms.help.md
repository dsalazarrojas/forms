# Envelope Opening Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to open an envelope. It captures essential information about the employee requesting access and their supervisor's details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name, surname, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and reason for opening the envelope.
3. Provide the date and time of your request.
4. Fill in your supervisor's name, phone number, email address, and title.
5. Optionally, enter your department and location (if applicable).
6. Choose an approval status.
7. Add any additional notes.
8. Provide your submitter information: your name, role, contact information (phone number, email, department, location), and date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Surname** (2, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Reason For Opening** (5, text, required): Explain why you need to open the envelope.
* **Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date you are opening the envelope.
* **Time** (7, time, required): Enter the time you are opening the envelope.
* **Supervisor Name** (8, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (9, text, required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (10, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Department** (11, text, required): Enter your supervisor's department (if applicable).
* **Location** (12, text, required): Enter your supervisor's location (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Title** (13, text, required): Enter your supervisor's title.
* **Supervisor Contact Information** (14, text, required): This field is for any additional supervisor contact information.
* **Approval Status** (15, select_one, required): Choose "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected" based on your supervisor's decision.
* **Notes** (16, text, required): Add any additional comments or instructions.
* **Submitter** (17, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Role** (18, text, required): Enter your role.
* **Submitter Contact Information** (19, text, required): Enter your contact information (phone number, email, department, location).
* **Submitter Email** (20, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (21, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (22, text, required): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Submitter Location** (23, text, required): Enter your location (if applicable).
* **Submitter Date** (24, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Submitter Time** (25, time, required): Enter the time you are submitting this request.
