# Family Portal Access Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Portal Access Request form is a necessary step for parents or guardians to gain access to their child's information and progress in school. By filling out this form, you will be able to view your child's grades, attendance, and other important information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your and your child's information, including:
	* Student Name
	* Guardian Name
	* Guardian Email
	* Guardian Phone
2. Choose your relationship to the student (Yes or No)
3. Select the school year and grade level your child is in (Yes or No)
4. Choose the school section your child is in (Yes or No)
5. If necessary, provide additional information in the Message field
6. Select the date and time you would like access to the Family Portal to begin

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Relationship To Student** (`relationship_to_student`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`guardian_message`, note, optional): If you have any additional information to provide, enter it here.
* **School** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Class** (`student_class`, select_multiple, required): Select the classes your child is in.
* **Address** (`guardian_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like access to the Family Portal to begin.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like access to the Family Portal to begin.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, required): Select the school year your child is in.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Select your child's grade level.
* **School Section** (`school_section`, select_one, required): Select the school section your child is in.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out this form as the authorized parent or guardian of the student.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your school's administration or support staff.
