# Hotel Amenities Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests about their access to hotel amenities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it should be displayed on your room key or in hotel records.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates about your preferences.
3. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with questions or for any urgent matters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (guest_name, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records. This will help us provide personalized service and address your concerns.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about our amenities and promotions.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you with urgent matters or updates.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can provide you with the best possible experience.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please do not hesitate to contact us using the contact information provided on this form.
- You can always contact us at the reception desk or by calling the hotel if you have any questions or concerns.
