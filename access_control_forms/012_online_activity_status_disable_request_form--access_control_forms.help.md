# Online Activity Status Disable Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Activity Status Disable Request Form is used to request disabling of online activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. List the online activities you would like to disable in the "Online Activities" field.
2. Explain how disabling these activities will affect you in the "Impact on Experience" field (optional).
3. Briefly explain why you want to disable these activities in the "Reason" field.
4. Choose your current online status in the "Online Status Preference" field.
5. If there are any other activities you would like to disable, list them in the "More Activities" field (optional).
6. If there are specific times you'd like to disable your activities, select them in the "Specific Times" field (optional).
7. If you have any other similar requests, select them in the "Other Requests" field (optional).
8. Confirm your current status in the "Current Status" field.
9. Click "Submit Request" to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online Activities** (`online_activities`, text, required): List the activities you would like to disable. This field is required because it's necessary to know which activities you would like to disable.
* **Impact on Experience** (`impact`, note, optional): Explain how disabling these activities will affect you. This field is optional because it's not always necessary to provide this information, but it can help us understand your request better.
* **Reason** (`reason`, note, required): Briefly explain why you want to disable these activities. This field is required because we need to understand your motivation for the request.
* **Online Status Preference** (`online_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current online status. This field is required because we need to know your current status in order to process your request.
* **More Activities** (`more_activities`, text, optional): List any other activities you would like to disable. This field is optional because there may not be any other activities you would like to disable.
* **Specific Times** (`specific_times`, select_multiple, optional): Select the specific times you'd like to disable your activities. This field is optional because there may not be specific times you'd like to disable your activities.
* **Other Requests** (`other_requests`, select_multiple, optional): If there are any other similar requests, select them. This field is optional because there may not be any other similar requests.
* **Current Status** (`current_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your current status. This field is required because we need to know your current status in order to process your request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_multiple, required): Click "Yes" to send your request. This field is required because it's necessary to submit your request for processing.
