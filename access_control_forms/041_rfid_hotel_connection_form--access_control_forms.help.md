# rfid_hotel_connection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rfid_hotel_connection_form is used by hotel staff to connect a guest's RFID card to their stay in the hotel. This form is used to collect the necessary information to link a guest's RFID card to their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the hotel's RFID card number in the "Hotel Connect RFID Card" field.
2. Select the checkin date for the guest's stay.
3. Enter the guest's name.
4. Select the start date of the guest's stay.
5. Select the end date of the guest's stay.
6. Enter the room number of the guest's room.
7. Enter the key card ID (if applicable).
8. Enter the guest's contact phone number.
9. Enter the guest's contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Connect RFID Card** (`hotel_connect_rfid_card_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the guest's RFID card number, which is used to identify the guest's RFID card.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date the guest is checking in to the hotel.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the guest's name as it appears on their RFID card.
* **Stay Start Date** (`stay_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the start date of the guest's stay.
* **Stay End Date** (`stay_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the end date of the guest's stay.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room number of the guest's room.
* **Key Card ID** (`keycard_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the key card ID (if applicable).
* **Guest Contact Phone** (`guest_contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the guest's contact phone number.
* **Guest Contact Email** (`guest_contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the guest's contact email.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct RFID card number to avoid any errors.
* Double-check the dates to ensure they are correct.
* Enter the guest's contact information to ensure they can be reached during their stay.
* This form is for hotel staff use, so if you're not a staff member, please do not fill out this form.
