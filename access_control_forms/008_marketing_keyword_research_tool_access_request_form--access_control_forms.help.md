# Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Keyword Research Tool Access Request Form is used to submit a request to access the Marketing Keyword Research Tool for a specific team and purpose. This form helps ensure that access is properly reviewed and approved by relevant team leads and managers. The form is used to avoid duplication of effort and to ensure that only authorized team members have access to the tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. Select your team lead and department from the provided options.
3. Provide a clear reason for your request.
4. Leave any additional notes for the reviewer.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your work or personal phone number where you can be reached.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_one`, required): Select your team lead from the provided options. This will ensure that your request is reviewed by the correct person.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the department you belong to from the provided options. This will help the reviewer understand your role and needs.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, `note`, optional): Provide a clear and concise reason for your request. This will help the reviewer understand your needs and make an informed decision.
* **Reviewers notes** (`reviewers_notes`, `date`, optional): Leave any additional notes for the reviewer. This is an optional field and should not be required.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `time`, optional): This field is not used for your submission, but it is a timestamp to track when the review was performed.

Note: Some fields may be required or have specific formats, such as email addresses, which are checked by our system to ensure accuracy.
