# Inventory Visibility Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to assets within the inventory. Fill out this form completely to ensure your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Select the relevant options for your request.
3. Provide any necessary details or comments for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form**: Provide a brief description of the asset(s) you are requesting access to.
* **Inventory Visibility**: Select the level of access you are requesting (Yes or No).
* **Asset**: If applicable, specify the asset(s) you are requesting access to.
* **Team Members**: List the team members you are requesting access for.
* **Team Lead**: If you are a team lead, list your name and title.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date you would like your request to be approved.
* **Approver**: Specify who you would like to approve your request (Yes or No).
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that you can be notified when your request is approved or denied.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that you can be contacted for any additional information.
* **Asset Visibility Request**: If you are requesting access to a specific asset, provide a brief description.
* **Asset Visibility Request Reason**: Provide a brief reason for requesting access to the asset.
* **Visibility Request Details**: Add any additional details about your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to submit your form after completing all required fields.
* Double-check your request for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your team lead or approver.
