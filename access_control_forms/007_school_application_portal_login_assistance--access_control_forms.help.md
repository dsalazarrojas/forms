# School Application Portal Login Assistance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users access the school's application portal and provide support when needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school ID (if required) in the "school_id" field.
2. Enter your applicant information in the "applicant_info" field.
3. Describe the issue you're experiencing in the "issue" field.
4. If you need help from someone at the school, provide their name in the "support_name" field.
5. If you'd like to contact them via email, enter their email address in the "support_email" field.
6. If you have their phone number, enter it in the "support_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **school_id (number)**: Enter your school's ID, if required.
* **applicant_info (number)**: Enter any information about yourself as an applicant.
* **issue (text)**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with the application portal.
* **support_name (text)**: If you have someone at the school you'd like to contact, enter their name here.
* **support_email (email)**: If you have their contact email address, enter it here.
* **support_phone (text)**: If you have their contact phone number, enter it here.
