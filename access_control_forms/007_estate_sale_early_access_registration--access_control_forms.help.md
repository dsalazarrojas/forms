# estate_sale_early_access_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for estate sales that have an early access option. Early access allows certain individuals or groups to view or attend a sale before the general public. Please use this form only for estate sales with this feature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information as follows:
	* Enter the details about the sale in the `sale_details` field.
	* Enter the details about the attendee in the `attendee_details` field.
	* Choose the appointment time for early access from the options listed in `early_access_appointments` field.
	* Provide the total capacity for the sale in the `capacity` field.
	* Enter any registration details in the `registration_details` field.
	* Select the sale date and time in the `sale_date` and `sale_time` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sale_details** (text, required): Enter the title and description of the sale, including any specific details or instructions.
* **attendee_details** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the attendee, such as their contact information.
* **early_access_appointments** (select multiple, optional): Choose from 1, 2, or 3 early access appointment options.
* **capacity** (number, optional): Enter the maximum number of attendees for the sale.
* **registration_details** (text, optional): Enter any additional information required for the sale registration.
* **sale_date** (date, required): Select the date for the sale.
* **sale_time** (time, required): Select the time for the sale.

## Tips

* Please ensure that this form is used for estate sales that have an early access option.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the sale administrator.
