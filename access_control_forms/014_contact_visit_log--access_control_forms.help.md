# Contact Visit Log - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect visitor information and security-related data for contact visits to the facility. It helps maintain accurate records and ensures compliance with security protocols.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the visitor information section: 
   - Provide the visitor's full name and company name (if applicable).
   - Enter the visitor's phone number and email address.
2. Select the purpose of the visit from the available options (Meeting, Interview, Delivery, Maintenance, or Other).
3. Provide the person visiting's name and ensure it matches the host's name.
4. Select whether a visitor badge was issued.
5. Confirm whether the visitor has signed the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
6. Confirm your agreement to have your data recorded for security purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Full Name**: Enter the full name of the visitor.
* **Visitor Company (if applicable)**: Enter the name of the company the visitor represents.
* **Visitor Phone Number**: Enter the visitor's phone number.
* **Visitor Email Address**: Enter the visitor's email address.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Entry**: Enter the time of entry using the time format (HH:MM).
* **Time of Exit**: (Optional) Enter the time of exit using the time format (HH:MM).
* **Purpose of Visit**: Select the purpose of the visit from the available options (Meeting, Interview, Delivery, Maintenance, or Other).
* **Person Visiting (Host Name)**: Enter the name of the person visiting, ensuring it matches the host's name.
* **Visitor Badge Issued?**: Select whether a visitor badge was issued.
* **I have signed the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**: Confirm whether the visitor has signed the NDA.
* **I agree to my data being recorded for security purposes**: Confirm your agreement to have your data recorded for security purposes.
