# Virtual Communication Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users requesting access to a Virtual Communication Platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your information: 
    a. Email: Enter your valid email address.
    b. Phone: Enter your phone number.
2. Explain your request: Reason
3. Identify your manager:
    a. Manager Name: Enter your manager's name.
    b. Manager Email: Enter your manager's email address.
    c. Manager Phone: Enter your manager's phone number.
4. Specify your team and department (optional):
    a. Team Name: Enter your team name.
    b. Department: Enter your department name.
5. Request status update:
    a. Status: Select the status of your request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
    a. Approval Date: Enter the date of approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Request** (`user_request`, `text`, optional): This field is where you provide a detailed description of your request for access to the Virtual Communication Platform.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact.
- **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a clear explanation for your access request.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name for record-keeping.
- **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your manager's email address for communication.
- **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's phone number for easier contact.
- **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your team name to facilitate team-related communication.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department name.
- **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name if your request has been approved.
- **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of approval.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your request.
- **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
- **User Request ID** (`user_request_id`, `text`, optional): This field is likely for internal tracking purposes.
- **Manager Request Status** (`manager_request_status`, `text`, optional): This field is likely for internal tracking purposes.
