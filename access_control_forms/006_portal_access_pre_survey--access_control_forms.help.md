# Portal Access Pre Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and justification for access to a portal. It helps administrators to evaluate and decide on the access level required for users to access specific portal features or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the access levels from the "Access level" field, which will be used to determine the type of access you need.
2. Enter a brief explanation for the reason you are requesting access in the "Reason for access" field.
3. Specify the intended use of the access in the "Intended use" field.
4. Provide a justification for your request in the "Justification" field.
5. Enter your portal ID or email address in the "User credentials" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Submitter name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Submitter email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason for access** (`pre_survey`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the reason you are requesting access.
* **Intended use** (`pre_survey_intended_use`, text, optional): Specify the intended use of the access you are requesting.
* **Justification** (`access_justification`, text, optional): Provide a justification for your request.
* **Access level** (`access_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following access levels:
	+ Standard Access
	+ Admin Access
	+ Moderate Access
* **Policy acknowledgement** (`policy_acknowledgement`, note, optional): This field is for your acknowledgement of the policies and guidelines of the portal.
* **Policy acknowledgement date** (`policy_acknowledgement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your acknowledgement.
* **Policy acknowledgement time** (`policy_acknowledgement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your acknowledgement.
* **Policy acknowledgement time zone** (`policy_acknowledgement_time_zone`, text, optional): Enter your time zone for the policy acknowledgement.
* **User credentials** (`user_credentials`, text, optional): Enter your portal ID or email address.
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill in all required fields for accurate processing.
