# PEO Employee Document Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PEO Employee Document Access Request form is used to request access to company documents for employees. This form helps employees inform management and HR about the necessary documents required for their work, and the management team can grant or deny access accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid reason for accessing company documents, as this information will be reviewed by management.
2. Select the correct options for "Document Type" based on the type of access you need.
3. Enter the name of the document you're requesting access to.
4. Choose a date for the request, if applicable.
5. Select the approver(s) who should be notified of your request.
6. Choose the level of access required for the document.
7. Provide any additional comments or explanations for your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`peo_employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (`peo_employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Department** (`peo_department`, select, required): Select the department you belong to for your job role.
* **Job Title** (`peo_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as per your role in the company.
* **Document Type** (`peo_document_type`, select multiple, required): Select the types of documents you need access to. This field will guide you to choose from the list of available document types.
* **Document Name** (`peo_document_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the document you need access to.
* **Request Date** (`peo_request_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when you require access to the document.
* **Expiration Date** (`peo_expiration_date`, date, optional): Choose the date after which you no longer need access to the document.
* **Status** (`peo_status`, select, required): Choose the status of your document access request.
* **Comment** (`peo_comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations for your request.
* **Manager's Name** (`peo_manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name for notification purposes.
* **Manager's Department** (`peo_manager_department`, text, optional): Enter your manager's department, if applicable.
* **Approval Status** (`peo_approval_status`, select, required): Choose the approval status for your document access request.
* **Request Type** (`peo_request_type`, select multiple, required): Select the type of request you're making.
* **Document Status** (`peo_document_status`, select, required): Choose the status of the document you're requesting.
* **Approval Date** (`peo_approval_date`, date, optional): Choose the date when the document access will be approved.
* **Requester** (`peo_requester`, select multiple, optional): Select the person making the request.
* **Approver** (`peo_approver`, select multiple, optional): Select the person approving the document access.
* **Document Access Level** (`peo_document_access_level`, select multiple, required): Select the level of access you need for the document.
