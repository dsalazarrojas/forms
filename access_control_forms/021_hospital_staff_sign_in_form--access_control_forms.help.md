# Hospital Staff Sign In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to facilitate hospital staff members to record their sign-in information. It captures essential details, such as shift start and end times, employee identification, departmental affiliations, roles, and contact information. This form enables administrators to track and manage staff availability and responsibilities accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your shift start and end times in the corresponding fields. These fields are optional but help administrators understand your working hours.
2. Enter your employee ID, which is your unique identifier within the hospital staff system.
3. Select the department(s) you are affiliated with. This ensures that your work is properly assigned and accounted for. You must choose at least one department.
4. Select your role(s) within the hospital staff. This helps administrators understand your job responsibilities. If applicable, you can choose multiple roles.
5. Add any additional notes about your shift, if necessary. This field is optional for personal comments or information not captured elsewhere.
6. Enter your email address and/or phone number for contact purposes. These fields are optional but useful for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Shift Start Time (Shift Start Time)
* **Shift Start Time** (shift_start_time): Enter the start time of your shift. This is the time when you began your work duties.

### Shift End Time (Shift End Time)
* **Shift End Time** (shift_end_time): Enter the end time of your shift. This is the time when you completed your work duties.

### Employee ID (Employee ID)
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): This is your unique identifier within the hospital staff system.

### Department (Department)
* **Department** (department): Choose the department(s) you are working in. Select at least one department to ensure accurate work assignment and accounting.

### Role (Role)
* **Role** (role): Choose your role(s) within the hospital staff. Select multiple roles if applicable.

### Notes (Notes)
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or information about your shift. This field is optional and for personal use.

### Email (Email)
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone (Phone)
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure you accurately select your department and role to ensure correct work assignment and accounting.
* Enter your shift start and end times correctly to avoid confusion.
* Use the notes field to add any important information not captured elsewhere.
* Keep your email and phone fields up-to-date to facilitate communication.
