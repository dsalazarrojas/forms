# Layover Exit Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help passengers request permission to exit the airport during a layover. This form will be reviewed and approved or denied by airport staff, and the passenger will be notified of the outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly, as incomplete or incorrect information may result in delays or denial of your request.
3. If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to airport staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Layover Exit Permission Request** (`layover_exit_permission_request_form`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason for your layover exit request.
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, optional): Your name as it appears on your travel documents.
* **Passenger ID** (`passenger_id`, number, optional): Your identification number as it appears on your passport or travel documents.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): The flight number you will be taking after your layover.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): The date you will be taking your flight after your layover.
* **Layover Time** (`layover_time`, time, optional): The time you will be leaving the airport during your layover.
* **Gate Number** (`gate_number`, text, optional): The gate number of your flight.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, text, optional): The type of travel you are undertaking (e.g. business, leisure, etc.).
* **Flight Status** (`flight_status`, text, optional): The status of your flight (e.g. on time, delayed, etc.).
* **Reason for Layover** (`reason_for_layover`, text, optional): Explain why you need to exit the airport during your layover.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a valid reason for your layover exit request.
* Be prepared to provide additional information or documentation if requested by airport staff.
* If your flight status changes, update this form accordingly to avoid confusion.
