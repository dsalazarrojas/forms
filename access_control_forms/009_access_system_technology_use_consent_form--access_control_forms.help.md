# Access System Technology Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Access System Technology Use Consent Form is a vital document for users to request access to various systems and technologies within the organization. It ensures that users have the necessary information and understand their responsibilities related to data access, usage, and security.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your legal name in the "User Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your unique user identifier in the "User ID" field.
3.  Select your department from the provided list in the "Department" field.
4.  Enter your job title in the "Position Title" field.
5.  Enter your work email address in the "Email Address" field.
6.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7.  Select the systems you need access to in the "System Access Requested" field.
8.  Choose the access level you require in the "Access Level Requested" field.
9.  Select the classification of data you will access in the "Data Classification Access" field.
10.  Explain the purpose of access in the "Purpose of Access" field.
11.  Specify the duration of access in the "Duration of Access" field.
12.  Confirm your agreement to use data only for authorized business purposes in the "Data Usage Consent" field.
13.  Acknowledge that you have read and will comply with the organization's security policies in the "Security Policy Acknowledgment" field.
14.  Confirm that you understand your responsibilities regarding data privacy and protection in the "Data Privacy Acknowledgment" field.
15.  Agree to report any security incidents or suspicious activities immediately in the "Incident Reporting Agreement" field.
16.  Consent to having your access and activities monitored for security purposes in the "Access Monitoring Consent" field.
17.  Acknowledge that your access will be revoked upon termination of employment or role change in the "Termination Procedure Acknowledgment" field.
18.  Sign the form with your full name in the "Electronic Signature" field.
19.  Date and sign the form in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Full Name** (`user_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name.
*   **User ID** (`user_id`, `text`, required): Enter your unique user identifier.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department or division from the provided list.
*   **Position Title** (`position_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **System Access Requested** (`system_access_requested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the systems you need access to.
*   **Access Level Requested** (`access_level_requested`, `select_one`, required): Choose the access level you require.
*   **Data Classification Access** (`data_classification_access`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the classification of data you will access.
*   **Purpose of Access** (`purpose_of_access`, `text`, required): Explain the purpose of access.
*   **Duration of Access** (`duration_of_access`, `select_one`, required): Specify the duration of access.
*   **Data Usage Consent** (`data_usage_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to use data only for authorized business purposes.
*   **Security Policy Acknowledgment** (`security_policy_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you have read and will comply with the organization's security policies.
*   **Data Privacy Acknowledgment** (`data_privacy_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand your responsibilities regarding data privacy and protection.
*   **Incident Reporting Agreement** (`incident_reporting_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to report any security incidents or suspicious activities immediately.
*   **Access Monitoring Consent** (`access_monitoring_consent`, `select_one`, required): Consent to having your access and activities monitored for security purposes.
*   **Termination Procedure Acknowledgment** (`termination_procedure_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that your access will be revoked upon termination of employment or role change.
*   **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with your full name.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date and sign the form.
