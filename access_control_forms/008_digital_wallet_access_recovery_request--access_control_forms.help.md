# Digital Wallet Access Recovery Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a digital wallet, allowing customers to recover access to their digital assets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Details" field with your correct information.
2. Select the "Reason for Request" field to explain why you are requesting access to your digital wallet.
3. If required, upload files by clicking on the "Attach Files" button.
4. Provide follow-up details in the "Follow-up Details" field.
5. Select the "Follow-up Status" field to choose the status of the follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your correct name, email address, and other relevant details.
- **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, required/optional): Explain the reason you are requesting access to your digital wallet.
- **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If required, upload files related to your request. You can select multiple files to attach.
- **Attach Files** (`file_uploads_2`, `text`, required/optional): You can attach files to support your request.
- **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional follow-up details that you would like to share.
- **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the follow-up to Active or Inactive as per your preference.

# Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
- Check your email for follow-up updates on your request status.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
