# HR Reporting Dashboard Access Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by HR and IT personnel to review and approve or reject access applications submitted by users or managers for the HR Reporting Dashboard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "User" or "Manager" as the "Submitted By" and "Review By" fields, as applicable.
2. Complete the "Date Range", "Time Period", or "Reporting Date" field according to the applicable dates.
3. Select one of the options from the "Role" field for the roles required by the request.
4. Select one of the options from the "Manager Comments" field to provide comments, if necessary.
5. Select one of the options from the "IT Comments" field to provide comments, if necessary.
6. Select one of the options from the "User Comments" field to provide comments, if necessary.
7. Select "Approve" or "Reject" from the "Manager Approvals", "IT Approvals", and "User Approvals" fields, as applicable.
8. Select one of the options from the "Review Status" field to indicate the status of the request.
9. Select one of the options from the "Approval Level" field to indicate the level of approval required.
10. Enter the "Email" and "Phone" fields with the relevant contact information.
11. Enter the "Notes" field with any additional comments or information.
12. Select one of the options from the "Submitted By" field to indicate who submitted the request.
13. Enter the "Submitted Date" and "Submitted Time" fields with the date and time the request was submitted.
14. Select one of the options from the "Review By" field to indicate who reviewed the request.
15. Enter the "Approval Date" and "Approval Time" fields with the date and time the request was approved or rejected.
16. Enter the "Manager Comments" and "IT Comments" fields with any comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (id: 1): Select "User" if the request was submitted by a user, or "Manager" if it was submitted by a manager.
* **Role** (id: 2): Select one of the roles required by the request (Analyst, Manager, or Developer).
* **Date Range** (id: 3): Enter the date range for the request (e.g., 01/01/2022 - 01/31/2022).
* **Time Period** (id: 4): Enter the time period for the request (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
* **Notes** (id: 5): Enter any additional comments or information related to the request.
* **Email** (id: 6): Enter the email address of the user or manager who submitted the request.
* **Phone** (id: 7): Enter the phone number of the user or manager who submitted the request.
* **Output** (id: 8): Enter any additional comments or information related to the request.
* **Review Status** (id: 9): Select one of the options (Pending, Approved, or Rejected) to indicate the status of the request.
* **Approval Level** (id: 10): Select one of the options (HR or IT) to indicate the level of approval required.
* **Reporting Date** (id: 11): Enter the date the request was submitted (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Reporting Time** (id: 12): Enter the time the request was submitted (e.g., 10:00).
* **Manager Comments** (id: 13): Enter any comments or information from the manager reviewing the request.
* **IT Comments** (id: 14): Enter any comments or information from IT reviewing the request.
* **User Comments** (id: 15): Enter any comments or information from the user who submitted the request.
* **Manager Approvals** (id: 16): Select "Approve" or "Reject" to indicate the manager's approval status.
* **IT Approvals** (id: 17): Select "Approve" or "Reject" to indicate the IT approval status.
* **User Approvals** (id: 18): Select "Approve" or "Reject" to indicate the user's approval status.
* **Approval Date** (id: 19): Enter the date the request was approved or rejected (e.g., 01/05/2022).
* **Approval Time** (id: 20): Enter the time the request was approved or rejected (e.g., 12:00).
* **Submitted By** (id: 21): Select "User" or "Manager" to indicate who submitted the request.
* **Review By** (id: 22): Select "User" or "Manager" to indicate who reviewed the request.
* **Submitted Date** (id: 23): Enter the date the request was submitted (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Submitted Time** (id: 24): Enter the time the request was submitted (e.g., 10:00).
* **Created By** (id: 25): Select "User" or "Manager" to indicate who created the request.
