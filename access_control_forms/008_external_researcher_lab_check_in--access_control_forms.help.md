# external_researcher_lab_check_in - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "external_researcher_lab_check_in" form is used to check-in external researchers at a lab, ensuring that their visit is properly recorded and their presence is acknowledged. This form collects necessary information about the researcher, their visit, and their purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name as per the instructions.
3. Select the appropriate Purpose of Visit from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the Time and Date of Check-in and Check-out.
5. Select the Visit Status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
6. Enter the Researcher Name and Research Area (if applicable).
7. Fill in any additional details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Date of Visit** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your visit to the lab.
* **Time of Visit** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your visit to the lab.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address (if available).
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number (if available).
* **Purpose of Visit** (Select One, Required): Select the reason for your visit from the dropdown menu.
* **Lab Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the lab you visited.
* **Researcher Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the researcher who is conducting the research.
* **Research Area** (Text, Optional): Enter the research area or department (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your supervisor (if applicable).
* **Lab Supervisor** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the lab supervisor (if applicable).
* **Facility Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the facility (if applicable).
* **Visit Status** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the status of your visit (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Check-in Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your check-in.
* **Check-in Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your check-in.
* **Check-out Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your check-out.
* **Check-out Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your check-out.
* **Reason for Visit** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason for your visit (if applicable).
* **Research Topic** (Text, Optional): Enter the research topic (if applicable).
* **Facility Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the facility (if applicable).
* **Visitor Type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of visitor (if applicable).
* **Visit Duration** (Text, Optional): Enter the duration of your visit (if applicable).
* **Submit** (Text, Required): Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.
