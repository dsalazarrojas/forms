# Veterinary Case Review Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request access to veterinary case reviews. Please fill out this form if you need to access a specific case review.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Make sure you are filling out the form for a legitimate purpose.
*   Enter your User details accurately.
*   Provide a valid Request Date for the access request.
*   Explain the Reason for Request to ensure your request is processed correctly.
*   Provide Justification for Request, if necessary, to help us evaluate your request.
*   Select the Access Type that best suits your needs.
*   Indicate whether you acknowledge the confidentiality of the case review.
*   Enter your Institution's name and affiliation, if applicable.
*   Specify the Reviewer who will be handling your request.
*   Add any additional Comments that are relevant to your request.
*   Indicate whether the request was submitted by you or someone else.
*   Enter the date the request was submitted.
*   Specify who updated the request and when.
*   Finally, select the Status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User** (1): Enter your user ID or name accurately.
*   **Request Date** (2): Enter the date when you submitted this request.
*   **Reason for Request** (3): Briefly explain why you need access to the case review.
*   **Justification for Request** (4): Select 'Yes' or 'No' if you have a valid justification for your request.
*   **Access Type** (5): Choose the type of access you need to the case review.
*   **Confidentiality Acknowledgment** (6): Confirm whether you acknowledge the confidentiality concerns of the case review.
*   **Institution** (7): If you are affiliated with a specific institution, enter their name.
*   **Reviewer** (8): Specify who will be handling your request.
*   **Comments** (9): Add any additional comments related to your request.
*   **Submitted by** (10): Confirm whether you or someone else submitted this request.
*   **Submitted on** (11): Enter the date the request was submitted.
*   **Updated by** (12): Specify who updated the request.
*   **Updated on** (13): Enter the date the request was updated.
*   **Status** (14): Select the current status of your request.
*   **Completed by** (15): Confirm whether your request is completed or not.
