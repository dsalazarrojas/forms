# Smart Access Card Replacement Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a replacement for a lost or damaged smart access card. This form is used by staff, students, and visitors to provide necessary information for the replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Staff Student Visitor**: Select whether you are a staff member, student, or visitor.
2. Enter your reason for requesting a replacement card.
3. Provide your contact information.
4. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your account.
5. Enter your smart access card number.
6. Confirm your contact information.
7. Confirm the reason for replacement.
8. Confirm your card number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Student Visitor** (`staff_student_visitor`):
	+ This field is a required selection that identifies whether you are a staff member, a student, or a visitor.
	+ Staff, students, and visitors are the target groups of people that use this form.
* **Reason for Replacement** (`reason`):
	+ Enter a clear and concise explanation for why you are requesting a replacement card.
	+ This will help us understand your specific situation and process your request efficiently.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`):
	+ Provide your name, email address, and phone number for us to contact you.
	+ This is important for us to get in touch with you regarding your request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`):
	+ Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for your account.
	+ This will ensure that your contact person is aware of your request.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`):
	+ Enter the number of your smart access card.
	+ This is necessary for us to verify your identity and process your request.
* **Confirm Contact** (`confirm_contact`):
	+ Re-enter your contact information to confirm its accuracy.
	+ This helps us verify your identity and process your request efficiently.
* **Confirm Reason** (`confirm_reason`):
	+ Re-enter the reason for replacement to confirm its accuracy.
	+ This ensures that we understand your request correctly.
* **Confirm Card** (`confirm_card`):
	+ Re-enter your card number to confirm its accuracy.
	+ This verifies your identity and ensures that we process your request correctly.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* Be clear and concise in your responses to help us understand your request efficiently.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
