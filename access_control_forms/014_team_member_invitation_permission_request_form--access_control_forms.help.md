# Team Member Invitation Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request team access and permissions for yourself or another team member. Fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the team member's email address in the "Team Member Email" field.
2. Choose the desired role for the team member in the "Requesting Role" field (Member, Guest, Admin, or Owner).
3. If necessary, provide the date and time of the request in the "Request Date" and "Request Time" fields.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments or details for the team lead or project lead to review in the "Team Lead Comment" and "Project Lead Comment" fields.
5. Select the current status of your request in the "Approval Status" field (Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Cancelled).
6. Optionally, add any additional comments for the team lead or project lead to provide feedback in the "Approval Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Email**: Enter the email address of the team member requesting access.
* **Requesting Role**: Select the role you would like the team member to have (Member, Guest, Admin, or Owner).
* **Request Date**: If necessary, enter the date you are requesting access (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Request Time**: If necessary, enter the time of your request (format: 24-hour clock).
* **Team Lead Comment**: Add any additional comments or details for the team lead to review.
* **Project Lead Comment**: Add any additional comments or details for the project lead to review.
* **Approval Status**: Select the current status of your request (Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Cancelled).
* **Approval Comment**: Add any additional comments or feedback from the team lead or project lead.
* **Assigned Tool**: If necessary, specify which tool has been assigned to the team member (e.g., "Trello" or "Asana").
* **Form ID**: This field is for internal use and should not be completed by users.
