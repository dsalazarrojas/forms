# Outdoor Access Guidelines Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users who are accessing outdoor areas or facilities. It collects basic contact information and date/time of access.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **User Name** in the provided text field. This is your name or a unique identifier for you as a user.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the provided email field. This is where your email will be used to contact you with any further instructions or updates.
3.  Choose the **Date of Access** from the calendar. Please select the correct date of your access.
4.  Choose the **Time of Access** from the time field. Please select the correct time of your access.
5.  Enter the **Location** of access in the provided text field. This should be the specific location you are accessing.
6.  Add your **Signature** to the provided signature field. This confirms that you understand and will follow the outdoor access guidelines.
