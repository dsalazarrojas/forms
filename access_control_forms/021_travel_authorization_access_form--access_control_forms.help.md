# Travel Authorization Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Authorization Access Form is a document used to request permission for company-approved travel. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the employee's travel plans are reviewed and approved by the relevant department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and department.
2. Enter the dates of your travel, including the start and return dates.
3. Provide the reason for your travel.
4. Include any relevant travel details, such as the destination and duration of your trip.
5. If applicable, specify the expected cost of your travel.
6. Choose your submitter's and approver's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Authorization Access Form**: This is the title of the form.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you are requesting approval from a specific manager, please enter their name.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Please select your department from the list.
- **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of your travel.
- **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you will return from your travel.
- **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, optional): Explain the reason for your travel.
- **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional travel details.
- **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Enter the destination of your travel.
- **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your travel.
- **Travel Cost** (`travel_cost`, number, optional): Enter the expected cost of your travel.
- **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact information.
- **Approver** (`approver`, text, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of the person who will be approving your travel.
- **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who will be approving your travel.
- **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who will be approving your travel.
- **Approver Phone Type** (`approver_phone_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of phone number (Mobile, Landline, Other).
- **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your department from the list.
- **Submitter Type** (`submitter_type`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your type (Employee, Manager, Other).
- **Department Approver** (`department_approver`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the department of the person who will be approving your travel.
- **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your department.
- **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`, select_multiple, optional): Please select your location.
- **Approver Notes** (`approver_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes for the approver.
- **Submitter Phone Type** (`submitter_phone_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of phone number (Mobile, Landline, Other).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all necessary fields to ensure a smooth approval process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your department's administration team.
* Once you have completed this form, please submit it for review and approval.
