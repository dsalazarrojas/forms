# Patient Starter Kit Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from patients to grant them access to the "Patient Starter Kit", a package containing information and resources for patients to take their care into their own hands.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to you from the "Starter Kit Access" field.
2. Fill in the "Delivery Details" field with any relevant information about your delivery needs.
3. In the "Patient Preferences" field, write down any specific preferences you have for the delivery.
4. Choose your preferred method of delivery from the "Delivery Method" field.
5. Select the date you would like to receive the package from the "Delivery Date" field.
6. Sign your name to confirm your information.
7. Enter your name as it should be displayed on the package.
8. Provide a contact number where we can reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Starter Kit Access** (`starter_kit_access_form`, select_multiple, **Required: No**):
	+ Select "Yes" if you want to access the Patient Starter Kit.
	+ Select "No" if you do not want to access the kit.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, **Required: No**):
	+ Enter any specific details you have about your delivery needs, such as any special instructions or requirements.
* **Patient Preferences** (`patient_preferences`, text, **Required: No**):
	+ Write down any specific preferences you have for the delivery, such as a preferred contact method or delivery schedule.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, select_one, **Required: No**):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you want to receive the package at your location.
	+ Choose "No" if you do not want to receive the package at your location.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, **Required: No**):
	+ Choose "Pickup" if you want to pick up the package at a designated location.
	+ Choose "Delivery" if you want the package delivered to your location.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, **Required: No**):
	+ Select the time you would like to receive the package.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, **Required: No**):
	+ Select the date you would like to receive the package.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, **Required: No**):
	+ Sign your name to confirm your information.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, **Required: No**):
	+ Enter your name as it should be displayed on the package.
* **Patient Contact Number** (`patient_contact_number`, text, **Required: No**):
	+ Enter a contact number where we can reach you.
