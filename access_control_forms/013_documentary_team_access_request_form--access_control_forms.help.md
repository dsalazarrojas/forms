# Documentary Team Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Documentary Team Access Request Form is a tool for users to request access to the documentary team for filming purposes. This form ensures that the documentary team is aware of user requests and can plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your project in the "Project Title" field.
2. Provide the name of the venue where your project will take place in the "Venue Name" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates for your project in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Check the "Access Term Agreement" field, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions of access.
5. Enter the start date of your visit in the "Visit Start Date" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your project, e.g., "Documentary on Wildlife Conservation."
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue where your project will take place, e.g., "Savannah National Park."
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your project, e.g., "2022-02-01."
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your project, e.g., "2022-02-14."
* **Access Term Agreement** (`access_term_agreement`, note, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of access, e.g., "I understand that I will provide 24-hour notice before filming."
* **Visit Start Date** (`visit_start_date`, date, optional): If your visit is not fixed, enter the start date you are planning for, e.g., "2022-02-05."
