# Building Admission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from visitors entering or exiting a building or facility. It helps host or facility management to ensure the safety and security of visitors and employees by gathering necessary details such as visitor information, visit details, and access requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for identification type, visit purpose, and access areas required.
3. Enter the host or contact person's information.
4. If you have additional visitors, select "Yes" and enter their names.
5. Indicate if you have special requirements or accommodations.
6. Confirm that you have read and agree to the building's rules and security procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor name** (`visitor_name`, **required**): Enter the visitor's name.
* **Company or organization** (`visitor_company`, **required**): Enter the visitor's company or organization name.
* **Email address** (`visitor_email`, **required**): Enter the visitor's email address.
* **Phone number** (`visitor_phone`, **required**): Enter the visitor's phone number.
* **Identification type** (`identification_type`, **required**): Select the type of identification you have (Driver license, Passport, Corporate ID, Government ID, or Other).
* **Identification number** (`identification_number`, **optional**): Enter the number or code on your identification if required.
* **Purpose of visit** (`visit_purpose`, **required**): Select the purpose of your visit (Business meeting, Delivery, Maintenance, Sales presentation, Interview, Consultation, or Other).
* **Detailed description of visit** (`purpose_description`, **required**): Enter a brief description of your visit.
* **Host or contact person** (`host_name`, **required**): Enter the name of the person being visited.
* **Host department** (`host_department`, **required**): Enter the department of the person being visited.
* **Host email** (`host_email`, **required**): Enter the host's email address.
* **Date of visit** (`visit_date`, **required**): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Start time** (`start_time`, **required**): Enter the start time of your visit.
* **Expected end time** (`end_time`, **required**): Enter the expected end time of your visit.
* **Building location** (`building_location`, **required**): Enter the building or facility location.
* **Floor or room number** (`floor_or_room`, **required**): Enter the floor or room number you are visiting.
* **Access areas required** (`access_areas`, **required**): Select the areas of the building that you need access to.
* **Additional visitors expected** (`additional_visitors`, **optional**): If you have additional visitors, select "Yes" and enter their names.
* **Number of additional visitors** (`additional_count`, **optional**): Enter the number of additional visitors if you select "Yes".
* **Names of additional visitors** (`additional_names`, **optional**): Enter the names of the additional visitors.
* **Background check** (`background_check`, **optional**): If required, select whether a background check is necessary or not.
* **NDA or confidentiality agreement** (`nda_agreement`, **optional**): If required, select whether you already have an NDA or need to sign one.
* **Completed safety training** (`safety_training`, **optional**): If required, select whether you have completed safety training or will do so on-site.
* **Special requirements or accommodations** (`special_requirements`, **optional**): Enter any special requirements or accommodations you have.
* **Pre-approval** (`pre_approval`, **optional**): If required, select whether the host has pre-approved your visit.
* **I acknowledge building rules and security procedures** (`visitor_acknowledge`, **required**): Select that you agree to follow the building's rules and security procedures.
