# Payroll Portal Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Portal Access Request Form is used to request access to the Payroll Portal system. This system is highly sensitive and only authorized personnel should be granted access to ensure data confidentiality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Employee ID in the Employee ID field.
2. Select your Job Title from the dropdown menu.
3. Select your Team Members from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your Access Level:
	* Basic: Access to basic features only.
	* Advanced: Access to advanced features.
5. Enter your Department (if applicable).
6. Provide the reason for your access request.
7. Select your Approval Status:
	* Approved: Your request has been approved.
	* Rejected: Your request has been rejected.
	* Pending: Your request is waiting for approval.
8. Indicate if you are approved by your manager:
	* Yes: Your manager has approved your request.
	* No: Your request is not approved by your manager.
9. (Optional) Enter the Submission Date and time of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: This is the unique identifier of the employee requesting access to the Payroll Portal system.
* **Job Title (2)**: Select your job title from the list to identify your role within the organization.
* **Team Members (3)**: Select your team members from the list to indicate who you work with and need to access the system for work-related purposes.
* **Access Level (4)**: Choose the level of access you need to the system: Basic or Advanced.
* **Department (5)**: Enter your department if it's not already listed in the Team Members field.
* **Reason (6)**: Provide a brief explanation for your access request, e.g., "To access payroll reports for team projects".
* **Approval Status (7)**: Select the current status of your request:
	+ Approved: Your request has been approved.
	+ Rejected: Your request has been rejected.
	+ Pending: Your request is waiting for approval.
* **Approved by (8)**: Indicate if you have approval from your manager:
	+ Yes: Your manager has approved your request.
	+ No: Your request is not approved by your manager.
* **Submission Date (9)**: (Optional) Enter the date and time of your request submission.
* **Submission Date Format**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Tips
* Please only submit this form once for each employee requesting access.
* Only authorized personnel should be granted access to the Payroll Portal system.
* Ensure that all fields are filled and accurate to avoid delays in the approval process.
