# Google Drive Access Review Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track and review access requests for Google Drive, ensuring that access is properly managed and monitored within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill out the required fields:
	* **form name**: Enter the name of the form being reviewed.
	* **access type**: Select the type of access being requested (Read/Write, Read Only, or Deny).
	* **request date**: Enter the date the access request was made.
	* **reviewer comments**: Add any additional comments or reasons for reviewing this access request.
2. If the reviewer is different from the person submitting the request, fill out:
	* **reviewer name**: Enter the name of the reviewer who will review the access request.
	* **reviewer email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
	* **reviewer phone**: Enter a phone number for the reviewer (optional).
	* **reviewer organisation**: Enter the organization or department of the reviewer.
	* **reviewer title**: Enter the title or position of the reviewer.
3. To complete the form, ensure that all required fields are filled out and review all the information carefully before submitting it.

## Tips
