# Process Safety Document Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request access to process safety documents. Fill out this form to submit a request for access to the relevant documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of document you are requesting access to from the "Document Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the location of the document.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
4. Enter your department as it appears on your employee ID.
5. Provide a reason for why you need to access the document.
6. (Optional) Enter the date you need access to the document.
7. Confirm who submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Document Type** (`Document Type`): Select the type of document you are requesting access to.
* **Document Location** (`Document Location`): Enter the location of the document.
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Department** (`Department`): Enter your department as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Document Request Reason** (`Document Request Reason`): Provide a reason for why you need to access the document.
* **Document Request Date** (`Document Request Date`): (Optional) Enter the date you need access to the document.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`): Confirm who submitted the request.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct document type and location to ensure that the request is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about the document location, please contact your supervisor or the document owner for clarification.
* If you need to request access to multiple documents, you can submit multiple requests, but please make sure to fill out a separate form for each document.
* Please make sure to provide a clear and concise reason for why you need to access the document.
