# referral_program_account_ban_appeal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Referral Program Account Ban Appeal Form" is used to appeal a ban on a referral program account. Fill out this form to provide your account information, reason for ban, and supporting documents to help us review your appeal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your account information in the "Account Information" field.
2. Provide a clear explanation for why you believe your account was banned in the "Reason for Ban" field.
3. Select the "Form Submission Status" field as "pending" to start the appeal process.
4. Upload up to 6 supporting documents by filling in the corresponding "Document" fields (Document 1, Document 2, Document 3, Document 4, Document 5, and Document 6).
5. Enter any additional comments about your appeal in the "Comments" field.
6. Review your form submission to ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about your account.
* **Account Information** (`account_information`, text, required): Enter your account details, such as your username or ID.
* **Reason for Ban** (`reason_for_ban`, text, required): Explain why you believe your account was banned.
* **Date of Ban** (`date_of_ban`, date, required): Select the date your account was banned.
* **Document 1** (`fileupload_1`, text, required): Upload a supporting document for your appeal.
* **Document 2** (`fileupload_2`, text, optional): Upload additional supporting documents for your appeal.
* **Document 3** (`fileupload_3`, text, optional): Upload any other supporting documents for your appeal.
* **Document 4** (`fileupload_4`, text, optional): Upload additional supporting documents for your appeal.
* **Document 5** (`fileupload_5`, text, optional): Upload any other supporting documents for your appeal.
* **Document 6** (`fileupload_6`, text, optional): Upload any additional supporting documents for your appeal.
* **Form Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your form submission.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your appeal.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Upload supporting documents that are relevant to your appeal.
* Be clear and concise in your account information and reason for ban fields.
* Double-check your form submission status to ensure it is correct.
