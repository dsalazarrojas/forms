# Plastic Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request access to plastic-related resources or information within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have a valid reason for requesting access before proceeding.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Requester** (Text, Optional): The name of the person submitting the request.
* **Requested User** (Text, Optional): The name of the person requesting access.
* **Department** (Select One, Required): The department of the requested user.
	+ Choose the department from the options: Engineering, Manufacturing, Facilities.
* **Material Requested** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the material or resource being requested.
* **Reason For Access** (Text, Optional): A clear explanation of why access is needed.
* **Date Of Access** (Date, Optional): The date when access is needed.
* **Start Time** (Time, Optional): The start time when access is needed.
* **End Time** (Time, Optional): The end time when access is needed.
* **Approved By** (Select Multiple, Required): The names of the personnel who have approved the access request.
	+ Choose from the options: John Doe, Jane Doe.
* **Date Approved** (Date, Optional): The date when access was approved.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals before proceeding.
* If the form is already approved or denied, please do not resubmit.
