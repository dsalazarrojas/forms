# Interview Preparation App Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to the Interview Preparation App. It is used to gather information about the user and their interview preparation needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Specify the date of your interview, if known.
3. Provide information about whether or not you are part of a team.
4. Select whether or not you are requesting access for yourself or others.
5. Choose the department you are representing or affiliated with.
6. Enter a brief purpose for requesting access to the app.
7. Add any additional notes about your access request.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us understand your access request.
* **Interview Date**: If you know the date of your interview, please enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Interview Time**: If you know the time of your interview, please enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Team Members**: Check if you are part of a team for which you are requesting access.
* **Access Requested For**: Select whether you are requesting access for yourself or others.
* **Department**: Choose the department you are representing or affiliated with.
* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of why you are requesting access to the app.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments to support your access request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
