# Mobile Money Transfer Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and review mobile money transfer requests. Users with access control permissions should complete this form to track and manage mobile money transfer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Access Control Forms" option to indicate that this request is for mobile money transfer access.
2. Decide if you want the request to be collected and reviewed or not.
3. Enter the relevant team or teams involved in the financial service.
4. Identify the programs that require consistent data.
5. Check if the form submission will be tracked (yes or no).
6. Indicate the current status of the form (in progress, approved, or rejected).
7. Enter the date and time of submission.
8. Enter the name, email, phone number, and department of the person who submitted the form.
9. Enter any additional notes or status updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mobile Money Transfer Access Request** (`mobile_money_transfer_access_request_form`, required): Enter the request description.
* **Access Control Forms** (`access_control_forms`, required): Choose "Mobile Money Transfer" to indicate that this request is for mobile money transfer access.
* **Collect and Review Requests** (`collect_and_review_requests`, optional): Check "With Review" to indicate that the request should be reviewed.
* **Financial Service Teams** (`financial_service_teams`, optional): Enter the relevant team or teams involved in the financial service.
* **Programs Needing Consistent Data** (`programs_needing_consistent_data`, optional): Enter the programs that require consistent data.
* **Organized Form Submission Tracking** (`organized_form_submission_tracking`, optional): This field is not applicable, as the form will be tracked automatically.
* **Jotform** (`jotform`, optional): Enter any relevant Jotform information.
* **Form Submission Tracking** (`form_submission_tracking`, required): Check "Yes" to track the form submission.
* **Form Submission Review Status** (`form_submission_review_status`, required): Indicate the current status of the form (in progress, approved, or rejected).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, optional): Enter the department or team the form was submitted to.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted For** (`submitted_for`, optional): Enter a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Extension** (`submitted_by_extension`, optional): Enter the extension number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, optional): Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the form status.
* **Form Status Notes** (`form_status_notes`, optional): Enter any notes regarding the form status.
* **Submitted By Department** (`submitted_by_department`, optional): Enter the department of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Location** (`submitted_by_location`, optional): Enter the location of the person who submitted the form.
