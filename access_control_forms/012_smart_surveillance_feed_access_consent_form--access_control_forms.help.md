# Smart Surveillance Feed Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is intended to help you complete the "Smart Surveillance Feed Access Consent Form" properly. Please note that the form's primary purpose is to collect the necessary information for users to access the surveillance feed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Form Title" field to enter your name.
2. In the "First Name" field, enter your first name as it appears on your personal identification documents.
3. In the "Last Name" field, enter your last name as it appears on your personal identification documents.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. In the "Contact Phone" field, provide your contact phone number.
6. In the "Date of Birth" field, enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
7. In the "Time of Birth" field, enter your time of birth in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
8. In the "Consent to Share" field, select which type of feed you consent to share your information with:
	* Surveillance Feed
	* Camera Feed
	* Other (Please specify in "Notes" field)
9. In the "Notes" field, if you selected "Other" in the "Consent to Share" field, provide a brief description of the type of feed you would like to share information with.
10. In the "Form Completion Status" field, select the status of your form completion:
	* Saved
	* Submitted
	* In Progress

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (text): A text field where you can enter your name.
* **First Name** (text, optional): A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): A field to enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): A field to enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): A field to enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time of Birth** (time, optional): A field to enter your time of birth in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Consent to Share** (select_one, optional): A field to select which type of feed you consent to share your information with.
	+ **Surveillance Feed**: Consent to share your information with the surveillance feed.
	+ **Camera Feed**: Consent to share your information with the camera feed.
	+ **Other**: Consent to share your information with other types of feed. Please specify in "Notes" field.
* **Notes** (note, optional): A field to provide a brief description of the type of feed you would like to share information with if you selected "Other" in the "Consent to Share" field.
* **Form Completion Status** (select_one, optional): A field to select the status of your form completion.
	+ **Saved**: Form is saved but not submitted.
	+ **Submitted**: Form is submitted and ready for review.
	+ **In Progress**: Form is partially completed and not submitted.

This guide is intended to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
