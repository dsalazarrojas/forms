# Media Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Access Registration Form is a tool for users to provide information about their media access requests for events. It helps event organizers manage registrations efficiently and ensure that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Select the event ID for which you are registering.
3. Fill in your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
4. Choose your media type (Photojournalist, Cameraman, Reporter, etc.).
5. Enter your email address.
6. Provide your phone number.
7. Select your role (Journalist, Photographer, Reporter, etc.).
8. If assigned a tool, enter the details.
9. Add any additional notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event ID** (1, required/optional): Select the ID of the event you wish to register for. This will help the event organizers track your registration and provide you with access to the event details.
- **Full Name** (2, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport. This is crucial for identification purposes.
- **Media Type** (3, required/optional): Choose your media type from the options provided. This will help event organizers understand your role and provide you with the right access to the event.
- **Email** (4, required/optional): Enter your email address. This is the primary contact method for event organizers to communicate with you.
- **Phone** (5, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This will help event organizers contact you if needed.
- **Role** (6, required/optional): Select your role from the options provided. This will help event organizers understand your responsibilities and provide you with the right access.
- **Assigned Tool** (7, required/optional): If you have been assigned a tool, enter the details here. This is for event organizers to track the tools assigned to you.
- **Notes** (8, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your registration. This is a free-text field for you to provide any necessary information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately.
- Review your registration carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
