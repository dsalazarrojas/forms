# Online Class Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Class Access Request Form is a tool designed to facilitate the process of requesting access to online classes. It is intended to ensure that all necessary information is collected in a single, unified manner, making the process of granting or denying access more efficient and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "What is your name" field.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Course code" field to indicate whether you are seeking access to a specific course.
3.  Enter the name of the course you are seeking access to in the "Course name" field.
4.  In the "Course description" field, briefly describe the course for which you are seeking access.
5.  Enter your learner's name in the "Learner name" field.
6.  Enter your learner's email address in the "Learner email" field.
7.  Enter your school's name in the "School name" field.
8.  Enter your school's email address in the "School email" field.
9.  Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher name" field.
10. Enter your teacher's email address in the "Teacher email" field.
11. Select the level of the course you are seeking access to in the "Course level" field.
12. In the "Course prerequisites" field, provide any relevant prerequisites for the course.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Approval method" field to indicate whether you will use a specific method for approval.
14. Choose a date for approval in the "Approval date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name** (`What is your name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
- **Course code** (`Course code`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are seeking access to a specific course, "No" otherwise.
- **Course name** (`Course name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course for which you are seeking access.
- **Course description** (`Course description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the course for which you are seeking access.
- **Learner name** (`Learner name`, `text`, required): Enter your learner's name.
- **Learner email** (`Learner email`, `email`, required): Enter your learner's email address.
- **School name** (`School name`, `text`, required): Enter your school's name.
- **School email** (`School email`, `email`, required): Enter your school's email address.
- **Teacher name** (`Teacher name`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher's name.
- **Teacher email** (`Teacher email`, `text`, required): Enter your teacher's email address.
- **Course level** (`Course level`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the level of the course you are seeking access to.
- **Course prerequisites** (`Course prerequisites`, `text`, required): Provide any relevant prerequisites for the course.
- **Approval method** (`Approval method`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you will use a specific method for approval, "No" otherwise.
- **Approval date** (`Approval date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for approval.
