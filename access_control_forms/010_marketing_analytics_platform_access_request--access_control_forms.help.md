# Marketing Analytics Platform Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Analytics Platform Access Request form is used to request access to the marketing analytics platform for users. This form is designed to gather necessary information from the user to evaluate their access request and ensure that they have the required permissions to access the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Info" section with your name, department, and job title.
2. Provide information about your request in the "Request Info" section.
3. Select the desired "Access Level" (Basic, Standard, or Advanced).
4. Choose whether your request is "Internal" or "External".
5. If applicable, list the team members you would like to grant access to (up to 3 team members).
6. Optionally, provide any additional comments or notes about your request in the "Comments" section.
7. If you need to upload any supporting files, use the "File Upload" field.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (1)**: This section is where you'll provide your basic contact information. Please fill in your name, department, and job title to help us understand your context.
* **Request Info (2)**: Here, describe the reason for your access request. This will help us evaluate your needs and ensure you have the necessary permissions.
* **Access Level (3)**: Choose the access level that suits your needs. "Basic" provides limited access, "Standard" offers more features, and "Advanced" includes everything.
* **Request Type (4)**: Indicate whether your request is for internal use or external partners.
* **Team Members (6)**: If you're requesting access for others, list their names here (up to 3 team members). This will help us grant access to the correct team members.
* **Department (7)**: If you're requesting access for your department, fill in the name here.
* **Job Title (8)**: Provide your job title for our records.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your work email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone (10)**: Provide your work phone number in case we need to discuss your request.
* **Comments (11)**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Date (12)**: If applicable, provide the date you'd like to start using the platform.
* **Time (13)**: If applicable, specify the time you'd like to start using the platform.
* **File Upload (14)**: If you have supporting files, such as a document or image, upload them here.
