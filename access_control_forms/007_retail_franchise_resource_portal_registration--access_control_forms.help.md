# Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for retail franchises to register and get access to exclusive resources on the portal. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and valid information.
2. Double-check your data before submission to ensure it is accurate and complete.
3. Submit the form to initiate the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company-name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your retail franchise.
* **Contact Person** (`contact-person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for contacting you.
* **Store Name** (`store-name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your store.
* **Store Address** (`store-address`, `text`, required): Enter the complete address of your store.
* **Store Telephone** (`store-telephone`, `text`, required): Enter your store's telephone number.
* **Store Email** (`store-email`, `text`, required): Enter your store's email address.
* **Store Website** (`store-website`, `text`, required): Enter your store's website (if applicable).
* **Store Fax** (`store-fax`, `text`, optional): Enter your store's fax number (if applicable).
* **Store Franchisee Owner** (`store-franchisee-owner`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or company that owns the store (if applicable).
* **Store Locations** (`store-locations`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of store locations (if applicable).
* **Store Locations 2** (`store-locations-2`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of store locations (if applicable) (this field appears to be a duplicate, but is included as it is in the original YAML).
* **Contact Information 2** (`Contact-Information-2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional contact information (if applicable).
* **Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration Form 2** (`retail-franchise-resource-portal-registration-form-2`, `text`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of another field and is not required. It can be left blank.
* **Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration Form 3** (`retail-franchise-resource-portal-registration-form-3`, `text`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration Form 4** (`retail-franchise-resource-portal-registration-form-4`, `text`, optional): This field is not required and can be left blank.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Double-check your data before submission to avoid errors.
- If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact the portal administrators.
