# Access Control Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from visitors and employees for access control purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Select the department you belong to or are visiting from the dropdown list.
3. Choose the level of access you require.
4. Enter the date and time of your visit.
5. Provide a brief description of the reason for your visit.
6. Select the areas you are authorized to access.
7. Indicate whether you require an escort in restricted areas.
8. Choose your visitor type classification.
9. (Optional) Provide the name of the contact person you are visiting and their emergency contact information.
10. Finally, mention any special instructions or requirements for access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Employee ID or Badge Number** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your employee identification number or badge number for access control purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the list provided.
* **Required Access Level** (`access_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of access you require.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY).
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Expected Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, `time`, optional): Estimate your departure time if you plan to leave within a specified timeframe.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
* **Authorized Areas** (`authorized_area`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas you are authorized to access from the list provided.
* **Escort Required** (`escort_required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you need an escort in restricted areas.
* **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your visitor classification from the list.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the person you are visiting.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Mention any special access requirements or instructions.

Note: The form is designed to guide users through the access control process, ensuring accurate and complete information collection for security and access control purposes.
