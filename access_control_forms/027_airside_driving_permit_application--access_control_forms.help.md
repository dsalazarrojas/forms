# Airside Driving Permit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airside Driving Permit Application form is designed to gather essential information from applicants who need to operate vehicles on airport grounds. This form ensures that applicants comply with airport regulations and are medically fit to drive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
2.  Enter your employee ID or contractor ID, if applicable.
3.  List your employer's name and your job title.
4.  Enter your contact phone number.
5.  Provide your driver's license information, including your state driver's license number and the license expiry date.
6.  List the categories of licenses you hold (e.g., B, C, D).
7.  Select the types of applications you are making (e.g., initial application, renewal, replacement, category upgrade).
8.  Choose the areas where you will be driving on the airport (e.g., ramp/apron, perimeter roads, taxiways, runways with ATC clearance, baggage makeup area).
9.  Specify the types of vehicles you will be operating (e.g., light vehicle, tug/tractor, high lift loader, fuel tanker, bus, fire/emergency vehicle).
10.  Enter the date you completed your airside driving theory training.
11.  Confirm that you are medically fit to operate vehicles on the airport premises.
12.  Enter the name of your authorizing manager.
13.  Declare that all provided information is correct and you will comply with airside driving regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
* **Employee ID/Contractor ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): If you are an employee, enter your employee ID. If you are a contractor, enter your contractor ID.
* **Employer/Company Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your employer's name.
* **Job Title/Role** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or role.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Driving License Information** (`section_license`, `note`, not required): This is a section header.
* **State Driving License Number** (`state_driving_license_no`, `text`, required): Enter the number of your driver's license.
* **License Expiry Date** (`license_expiry_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your driver's license will expire.
* **License Categories (Held)** (`license_categories`, `text`, required): List the categories of licenses you hold (e.g., B, C, D).
* **Permit Application Details** (`section_permit`, `note`, not required): This is a section header.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of application you are making (e.g., initial application, renewal, replacement, category upgrade).
* **Airside Areas Requested** (`airside_areas_required`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the areas where you will drive on the airport (e.g., ramp/apron, perimeter roads, taxiways, runways with ATC clearance, baggage makeup area).
* **Vehicle Types to be Operated** (`vehicle_types_operated`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the types of vehicles you will operate (e.g., light vehicle, tug/tractor, high lift loader, fuel tanker, bus, fire/emergency vehicle).
* **Airside Driving Theory Training Date** (`airside_training_completed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed your airside driving theory training.
* **Medical Fitness Confirmation** (`medical_fitness_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are medically fit to operate vehicles on the airport premises.
* **Authorizing Manager Name** (`manager_approval_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your authorizing manager.
* **I Declare** (`declaration_statement`, `select_one`, required): Declare that all provided information is correct and you will comply with airside driving regulations.
