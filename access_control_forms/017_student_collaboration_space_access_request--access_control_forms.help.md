# Student Collaboration Space Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows students to request access to a student collaboration space to work on projects or assignments with their peers. To complete the form, follow the steps outlined below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Request Form** field. This is the main description of your request, so be sure to provide a clear and concise explanation of why you want to join the collaboration space.
2. In the **Purpose** field, describe the specific purpose of your request. This could include the name of the project or assignment you are working on, or the specific skills or knowledge you are looking to gain from participating in the collaboration space.
3. Choose a **Timing** that works for you. This could be a specific date and time or a general range of dates and times when you are available to participate.
4. If you are willing to agree to the collaboration space guidelines, select **Yes** in the **Agreement** field. If you are not, select **No**.
5. Finally, add any additional **Note** about your request in the **Note** field. This is a good place to provide any additional context or information that might be helpful for the space administrators to consider your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form** (text, optional): A clear and concise description of your request to join the collaboration space.
* **Purpose** (text, optional): A description of the specific purpose of your request, including the name of the project or assignment you are working on or the specific skills or knowledge you are looking to gain.
* **Timing** (time, optional): Select a specific date and time or a range of dates and times when you are available to participate.
* **Agreement** (select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you are willing to agree to the collaboration space guidelines. Check "No" if you are not.
* **Note** (note, optional): A field for you to add any additional notes or information about your request.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise in your request, providing all the necessary details.
* If you are already a member of the collaboration space, do not need to fill out this form again.
* Make sure to agree to the collaboration space guidelines before submitting the form.
