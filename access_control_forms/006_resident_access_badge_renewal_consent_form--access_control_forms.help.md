# resident_access_badge_renewal_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resident_access_badge_renewal_consent_form is used for renewing access badges for residents. Before filling out this form, please make sure you meet the necessary criteria to use it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and fill out your information accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as you would like it to appear on your badge.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as you would like it to appear on your badge.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and information regarding your badge.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Please enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required): Please enter your street address as it appears on official documents.
* **Unit Number** (Unit Number, text, required): Please enter your unit number as it appears on your lease or official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Badge Type** (Badge Type, select_one, required): Please choose 'Yes' if your badge is a resident, 'No' if your badge is a guest or other type of badge.
* **Badge Number** (Badge Number, text, required): Please enter the number on your badge.
* **Badge Expiration Date** (Badge Expiration Date, date, required): Please enter the date you wish to renew your badge for.
* **Reason for Renewal** (Reason for Renewal, select_multiple, required): Please choose the reason why you are renewing your badge, 'Yes' if you are renewing due to a change in your lease, 'No' if you are renewing due to other reasons.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Please sign below to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_multiple, required): Please choose 'Yes' if you consent to the terms and conditions of the access badge renewal process.
* **Property Manager Signature** (Property Manager Signature, text, required): Please sign below to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Property Manager Notes** (Property Manager Notes, text, optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments the property manager may need to know.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Please enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, note, not required): Please read and understand the terms and conditions before signing this form.
* **Reason for Renewal** (Reason for Renewal, select_multiple, required): Not to be confused with the previous field, this field is used to confirm the reason for renewal.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and is subject to change based on the specific requirements and regulations of the property management company. Always review the terms and conditions for the most up-to-date information.
