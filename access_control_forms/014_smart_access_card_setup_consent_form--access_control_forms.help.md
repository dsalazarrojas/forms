# Smart Access Card Setup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Access Card Setup Consent Form is a document that employees must complete to set up their smart access cards, which grant access to specific systems, applications, and data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please contact your manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): This field is used to input your name as an employee.
* **Reason for Access** (`reason_for_access`, text, required): Please explain the purpose or reason for requesting smart access card setup.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department that corresponds to your work role.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name, as they will be the person responsible for reviewing and authorizing your access card setup.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If applicable, input your date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Date of Issue** (`date_of_issue`, date, optional): If applicable, input the date of issue for your smart access card.
* **Date of Expiry** (`date_of_expiry`, date, optional): If applicable, input the date of expiry for your smart access card.
* **Card ID** (`card_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for your smart access card.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, optional): If applicable, input the date your smart access card was issued.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Provide a signature to confirm your identity and consent to the access card setup.

**Tips**: Please ensure you have permission from your manager before completing this form. The form should only be used for setting up access cards and not for any other purpose. If you're unsure about any information or have questions, do not hesitate to contact your manager or the relevant department for guidance.
