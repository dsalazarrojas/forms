# Law Enforcement Training Portal Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to the Law Enforcement Training Portal for law enforcement personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request for access to the Law Enforcement Training Portal, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you are submitting on behalf of a law enforcement officer or agent who is eligible for access.
*   Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
*   Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Enter the first name of the law enforcement officer or agent requesting access.
*   **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the law enforcement officer or agent requesting access.
*   **Badge Number**: Enter the badge number of the law enforcement officer or agent requesting access.
*   **Supervisor Name**: Enter your name and rank, as the supervisor verifying the request.
*   **Supervisor Signature**: Enter your signature, as the supervisor, to authenticate the request.
*   **Supervisor Title**: Enter your title or position, as the supervisor.
*   **Request Date**: Select the date the request is being made (yyyy-mm-dd).
*   **Training Portal Access Request**: Enter a brief description of why the officer or agent needs access to the training portal. 

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used for access verification and processing.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or our support team for assistance.
