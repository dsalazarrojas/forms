# Guest Wi Fi Access Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Wi-Fi Access Booking Form is used to book guest Wi-Fi access for visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, text, required): Please fill out this field with a brief description of your visit, including the purpose and nature of your visit.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your visit.
* **Visit Reason** (`visit_reason`, text, required): Explain the reason for your visit in a few sentences.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Select the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required): Select the time of your visit.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, note, required): Read and acknowledge our policy by selecting the checkbox.
* **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`, email, required): Sign with your email address to confirm your visit.
* **Visit Details 2** (`visit_details_2`, text, optional): Please fill out this field with any additional information about your visit.
* **Visit Reason 2** (`visit_reason_2`, text, optional): Explain any additional reason for your visit.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Visit Duration 2** (`visit_duration_2`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your visit (if different from the initial visit duration).
* **Policy Acknowledgment 2** (`policy_acknowledgment_2`, note, optional): Read and acknowledge our policy by selecting the checkbox (if not already done so).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email or phone number].
* Please review our policy carefully and ask us if you have any questions or concerns.
* After submitting the form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
