Workplace Child Visitor Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a parent's consent for a child's visit to a workplace. It is intended to be completed by the parent or legal guardian before the child's visit. The information collected includes consent for the visit, visitor information, child details, and parent and visitor signatures. This form helps ensure that we have necessary information and consent before allowing the child to visit and interact with employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions according to the fields provided.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding.
3.  Sign and date the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Consent (`Parent Consent`, select_one, required)**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you give consent for your child to visit the workplace.
* **Visitor Information (`Visitor Information`, text, optional)**: Provide details about the visitor, such as their name, contact information, and any relevant medical or allergy information.
* **Child Details (`Child Details`, text, optional)**: Enter your child's name and any other relevant details about them.
* **Parent Signature (`Parent Signature`, note, not required)**: Please sign and date this field to confirm your consent. Note that this field is not required as it is a confirmation.
* **Visitor Signature (`Visitor Signature`, note, not required)**: Please sign and date this field to confirm that the visitor has received and understood the information provided.
* **Date of Visit (`Date of Visit`, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the visit.
