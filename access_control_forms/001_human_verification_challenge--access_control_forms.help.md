# Human Verification Challenge - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Human Verification Challenge form is designed to help us verify that you are a real human interacting with our system. This form is used to collect information about your preferences and behavior to ensure that our system is being used as intended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. For each page, fill in the required fields and select the appropriate options for each question.
2. Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. If you're unsure about any question, try to provide the most likely correct answer based on your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite color** (`verification_challenge`, text, optional): This question is meant to test your ability to respond to a generic question without any external influence.
* **Verify Captcha** (`verify_captcha`, select_one, optional): This question is designed to test your ability to recognize and respond to a common web security measure.
* **Are you human** (`human_verification`, select_multiple, optional): This question is meant to verify that you are indeed a human interacting with our system, not a machine or a script.
* **Verify email** (`verify_email`, email, optional): This question is meant to test your ability to provide a valid email address. Please format it as `example@example.com`.
* **Verify phone** (`verify_phone`, text, optional): This question is meant to test your ability to respond to a generic question without any external influence.
