# Clinician Portal Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide us with information necessary to evaluate and grant access to the Clinician Portal, allowing you to securely access patient information and perform various clinical tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name exactly as it appears in your official credentials.
2. Enter your valid email address where we can contact you for verification or further information.
3. Provide your best contact number.
4. Specify your professional title and title of the organization you are affiliated with.
5. Enter your medical license number and state of licensure.
6. Choose the department or specialty you are associated with.
7. Select the specific modules or features you need access to in the portal.
8. Indicate if you will be accessing pediatric patient records.
9. Provide the name and contact information of your direct supervisor or department head.
10. Explain your primary purpose for requesting access to the portal.
11. Indicate your training completion status, the date you need to start using the portal, and whether you will be completing any additional training before starting.
12. Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the HIPAA security and privacy policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal name as it appears in your official credentials.
* **Email Address**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your best contact number.
* **Professional Title**: Your title, such as physician or nurse practitioner.
* **Professional Credentials**: Your affiliated healthcare organization.
* **Medical License Number**: Your state medical license number.
* **State of Licensure**: The state where your license is issued.
* **Department or Specialty**: Your primary area of practice.
* **Modules or Features**: Select the specific modules or features you need access to in the portal.
* **Pediatric Patient Records**: Indicate if you will be accessing pediatric patient records.
* **Supervisor Name**: Name and title of your direct supervisor or department head.
* **Supervisor Email**: Email address of your supervisor or department head.
* **Purpose and Justification**: Explain your primary purpose for requesting portal access.
* **Training Completion Status**: Indicate your training completion status.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you need to start using the portal.
* **HIPAA Agreement**: Acknowledge that you understand and agree to the HIPAA security and privacy policies.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or department head for guidance.
* If you need help with completing this form, contact our support team.
