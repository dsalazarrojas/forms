# Project Key Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Key Sign Out Form is designed to gather user information and project details for sign-out purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the User Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide the key information about your project.
3. Specify the expected returns or outcomes of your project.
4. Explain your role and responsibilities in the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for entering your personal details, such as your name, email, or other relevant contact information.
* **Key Information** (`key_information`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, please provide the project's key details, including project name, project ID, or any other relevant information.
* **Expected Returns** (`expected_returns`, `text`, required/optional): Here, specify what you expect to return or achieve from the project, such as expected outcomes, deliverables, or benefits.
* **Accountability** (`accountability`, `text`, required/optional): Explain your role and responsibilities in the project, including what you're accountable for and what you're handing over to others.
