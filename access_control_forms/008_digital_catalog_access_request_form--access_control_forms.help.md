# Digital Catalog Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Catalog Access Request Form is used to request access to the digital catalog for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select all relevant catalogs that you need access to.
3. Specify the purpose of your access request.
4. Agree to the terms and conditions of access.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Choose a date and time for access.
8. Choose a time for access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Contact Information** (user_info)
Contact information is used to keep in touch with you regarding your access request.

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

### 2. **Select Catalogs** (catalog_selection)
Select all relevant catalogs you need access to.

* **Select all applicable options**: Choose the catalogs that match your needs.

### 3. **Purpose of Access** (purpose)
Describe the reason you need access to the digital catalog.

* **Purpose**: Enter the reason for your access request.

### 4. **Terms Agreement** (terms_agreement)
Please agree to the terms and conditions of access.

* **Agree to terms**: Choose the option that indicates you agree to the terms and conditions.

### 5. **Email** (email)
Enter your email address.

* **Email address**: Enter your email address.

### 6. **Phone** (phone)
Enter your phone number.

* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.

### 7. **Date** (date)
Choose a date for access.

* **Date**: Choose a date for access.

### 8. **Time** (time)
Choose a time for access.

* **Time**: Choose a time for access.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Only select catalogs that you need access to.
* Enter a valid email address and phone number.
* Choose a valid date and time for access.
