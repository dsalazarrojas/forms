# Access Protection For Hemp Products - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from customers who wish to purchase hemp products. It will guide you through a series of questions to ensure you understand our policies and laws regarding hemp product sales.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Full Name**.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the correct date format.
3. Select the type of government-issued **ID Type** you possess (e.g., Driver's License, State ID, etc.).
4. Enter the **Government ID Number** as appears on your ID.
5. Enter the state or province that issued your ID in **State/Province of ID Issuance**.
6. Provide your current **Address**.
7. Enter the city of your residence in **City**.
8. Enter the state of your residence in **State**.
9. Enter your **ZIP Code**.
10. Provide your **Phone Number**.
11. Enter your **Email Address**.
12. Select **Yes, I am 21 or older** if you are over 21 years old.
13. Confirm that you **Acknowledge that hemp products containing less than 0.3% THC are legal under federal law**.
14. Indicate how you intend to use these hemp products by selecting all relevant options under **Intended Use** (e.g., Personal consumption, Research purposes, etc.).
15. Indicate which hemp products you are interested in by selecting all relevant options under **Product Interests** (e.g., CBD oils, Edibles, etc.).
16. Confirm that you agree to purchase only the amount permitted by state and federal regulations under **Purchase Limit Acknowledgment**.
17. Acknowledge that hemp products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease under **Health Disclaimer Acknowledgment**.
18. Check the box to indicate your **Signature Consent**.
19. Provide the date of your agreement in **Signature Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Government ID Type**: Select the type of government-issued ID you possess (e.g., Driver's License, State ID, etc.).
* **Government ID Number**: Enter the number on your government-issued ID.
* **State/Province of ID Issuance**: Enter the state or province that issued your ID.
* **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State**: Enter the state of your residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Age Verification**: Select **Yes, I am 21 or older** if you are over 21 years old.
* **Legal Compliance Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you are aware of and agree to comply with federal laws regarding hemp product sales.
* **Intended Use**: Select all relevant options under this field to indicate how you plan to use the hemp products.
* **Product Interests**: Select all relevant options under this field to indicate which hemp products you are interested in.
* **Purchase Limit Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you agree to purchase only the amount permitted by state and federal regulations.
* **Health Disclaimer Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you are aware that hemp products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
* **Signature Consent**: Check the box to indicate your **Signature Consent**.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your agreement.
