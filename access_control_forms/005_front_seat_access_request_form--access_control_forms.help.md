# Front Seat Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Front Seat Access Request Form is a tool used to submit requests for front seat access to school events. This form captures essential information for processing and managing such requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Contact Person** field with the name of the person responsible for submitting the request.
2. Enter the email address of the contact person in the **Contact Person Email** field.
3. Provide the contact person's phone number in the **Contact Person Phone** field.
4. Enter the name of the event to which the front seat request pertains to in the **School Event** field.
5. Choose the date and time of the event in the **Event Date/Time** field.
6. Select the type of event (e.g., Tour, School, Event) in the **Event Type** field.
7. Indicate whether you prefer a **Front Seat**, **Rear Seat**, **No Preference**, or **Other** in the **Front Seat Type** field.
8. Optionally, provide additional comments or reasons for the request in the **Front Seat Request Message** field.
9. Fill in the **Submission Comments** field with any additional information or details about the request.
10. Select the tool assigned to manage the front seat access in the **Assigned Tool** field.
11. Choose the date of submission in the **Submission Date** field.
12. Fill in your name as the person submitting the request in the **Submitted By** field.
13. Choose your role or position as the submitter in the **Submitter Role** field.
14. If you're affiliated with a school, enter the school's abbreviation in the **School Abbreviation** field.
15. Enter your email address in the **Submitter Email** field.
16. Provide your phone number in the **Submitter Phone** field.
17. Optionally, provide additional comments about the request in the **Submitter Comments** field.
18. Choose whether you **Agree** or **Disagree** with the request conditions in the **Submitter Agree** field.
19. If you choose **Disagree**, please provide a reason for your disagreement in the **Submitter Agree Reason** field.
