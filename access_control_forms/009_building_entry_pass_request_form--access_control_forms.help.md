# Building Entry Pass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request entry into a secure building. It is intended to be filled out by visitors before arriving at the site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Select the type of identification you will present upon arrival.
5. Enter the name of the host employee you are visiting.
6. Enter the department of the host employee.
7. Enter the email address of the host employee.
8. Specify the purpose of your visit.
9. Enter the date of your visit.
10. Enter the estimated arrival time.
11. Enter the estimated departure time.
12. If you will be arriving by vehicle, enter the make and model and license plate number.
13. Indicate if you will be escorted or entering independently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Full Name (visitor_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (visitor_email)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (visitor_phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Identification Type (visitor_id)**: Select the type of identification you will present upon arrival.
* **Host Employee Name (host_name)**: Enter the name of the host employee you are visiting.
* **Host Department (host_department)**: Enter the department of the host employee.
* **Host Email Address (host_email)**: Enter the email address of the host employee.
* **Purpose of Visit (visit_purpose)**: Specify the reason for your visit.
* **Date of Visit (visit_date)**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Estimated Arrival Time (arrival_time)**: Enter the estimated time of your arrival.
* **Estimated Departure Time (departure_time)**: Enter the estimated time of your departure.
* **Areas Needing Access (areas_access)**: Select the areas you need to visit.
* **Will You Bring a Vehicle (vehicle_information)**: Indicate if you will arrive by vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make and Model (vehicle_make)**: Enter the make and model of your vehicle if you are arriving by vehicle.
* **License Plate Number (license_plate)**: Enter the license plate number of your vehicle if you are arriving by vehicle.
* **Will You Be Escorted (escorted)**: Indicate if you will be escorted or entering independently.
