# Visitor Management Login Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate the login process for visitors to access the visitor management system. It is a crucial step in the visitor management process, allowing authorized visitors to access restricted areas of the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Enter your password in the "Password" field.
3. Click on the "Login" button to submit your credentials.
