# efin_software_access_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to efin software applications for specific purposes within the organization. Review the relevant policies and procedures before submitting a request to ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals in place.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the requestor.
2. Provide the date you are requesting access to the software application.
3. Choose the type of software access you are requesting.
4. Enter a brief reason for your request.
5. Enter any additional comments or details as needed.
6. Select the approver's name, title, and department.
7. Enter the approver's email address and phone number.
8. Choose the approval level for the access request.
9. Enter any approver notes or comments.
10. Select the approval date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the requestor.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting access to the software application.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of software access you are requesting.
* **Software Access Required** (`software_access_required`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the software access required.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for your request.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team approving your request.
* **Approver Title** (`approver_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person or team approving your request.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person or team approving your request.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Approver Note** (`approver_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or team approving your request.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the approval.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the approval.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Approval Level** (`approval_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the approval level.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the requestor.
* **Approver Department** (`approver_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the person or team approving your request.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the requestor.
* **Approval Comment** (`approval_comment`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the approver.
* **Requestor Location** (`requestor_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the requestor.
* **Approver Level** (`approver_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of the person or team approving your request.
* **Approver Notes** (`approver_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the approver.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
