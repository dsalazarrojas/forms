# Parental App Blocking Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental App Blocking Request Form is a tool used by parents or guardians to request blocking access to an app for their child. This form helps to collect information necessary for the evaluation and blocking of the app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Parental App Blocking Request" text field with a brief description of the app you want to block.
2. Provide a reason for the request in the "why" text field.
3. Enter your child's username in the "child username" text field.
4. Choose whether you have given your child permission for the app in the "parental permission" select field.
5. Select the apps to block in the "app to block" select multiple field.
6. Enter any additional comments or information regarding the request in the "comments" text field.
7. Enter the current status of the app (blocked or unblocked) in the "blocked" and "unblocked" text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental App Blocking Request** (`Parental App Blocking Request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the app you want to block.
* **why** (`reason_prophets`, text, required): Provide a reason for your request to block the app.
* **child username** (`child_username`, text, required): Enter your child's username associated with the account that will be affected by the block.
* **parental permission** (`parental_permission`, select one, required): Choose "Yes" if you have given your child permission for the app, or "No" if you have not.
* **app to block** (`app_to_block`, select multiple, required): Select the apps you want to block.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter any additional information or reason for the request.
* **app details** (`app_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the app you are requesting to block.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
* **blocked** (`blocked`, text, required): Enter the current status of the app (blocked or unblocked).
* **unblocked** (`unblocked`, text, required): Enter the current status of the app (blocked or unblocked).

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in to complete the form.
