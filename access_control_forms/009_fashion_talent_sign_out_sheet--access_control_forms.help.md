# fashion_talent_sign_out_sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fashion_talent_sign_out_sheet" form is a document used to record the sign-out of talents from the fashion industry. It is used to maintain accurate records and ensure proper management of the talent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sign-out date in the "Sign-out Date" field.
2. Enter the name of the talent in the "Talent Name" field.
3. Enter the item name in the "Item Name" field.
4. Enter the reason for sign-out in the "Reason" field.
5. Select the status of the item in the "Status" field.
6. Enter the item value in the "Item Value" field.
7. Select the condition of the item in the "Item Condition" field.
8. Enter the location of the sign-out in the "Location" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the sign-out in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter the contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
11. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
12. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
13. Enter the agency name in the "Agency Name" field.
14. Enter the studio name in the "Studio Name" field.
15. Enter the production team in the "Production Team" field.
16. Enter the lost value in the "Lost Value" field.
17. Enter the action taken in the "Action Taken" field.
18. Enter the action taken by in the "Action By" field.
19. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
20. Enter the name of the person who created the record in the "Created By" field.
21. Enter the name of the person who last updated the record in the "Updated By" field.
22. Enter the date and time the record was last updated in the "Date" field and "Time" field.
23. Enter the name of the person the record was assigned to in the "Assigned To" field.
24. Enter the unique identifier of the form in the "Form ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign-out Date (Sign-out Date, date, required)**: Enter the date when the talent was signed out.
* **Talent Name (Talent Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the talent being signed out.
* **Item Name (Item Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the item being signed out.
* **Reason (Reason, text, required)**: Enter a brief reason for the sign-out of the talent.
* **Status (Status, select_one, required)**: Select the status of the item from the available options.
* **Item Value (Item Value, number, required)**: Enter the monetary value of the item being signed out.
* **Item Condition (Item Condition, select_multiple, required)**: Select the condition of the item from the available options.
* **Location (Location, text, required)**: Enter the location where the talent was signed out.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required)**: Add any additional notes about the sign-out.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, required)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Agency Name (Agency Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the agency.
* **Studio Name (Studio Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the studio.
* **Production Team (Production Team, text, required)**: Enter the name of the production team.
* **Lost Value (Lost Value, number, required)**: Enter the monetary value of the lost item.
* **Action Taken (Action Taken, text, required)**: Enter the action taken to address the sign-out.
* **Action By (Action By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who took the action.
* **Comments (Comments, text, required)**: Enter any additional comments about the sign-out.
* **Created By (Created By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who created the record.
* **Updated By (Updated By, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the record.
* **Date (Date, text, required)**: Enter the date the record was last updated.
* **Time (Time, time, required)**: Enter the time the record was last updated.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person the record was assigned to.
* **Form ID (Form ID, text, required)**: Enter the unique identifier of the form.
