# Guest Communication Portal Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Communication Portal Access Request form is used to request access to the guest communication portal. Please fill out this form to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Describe the purpose of your request in the "Purpose" field.
5. Specify the duration of your requested access in the "Access Duration" field (in numerical format).
6. Add any additional notes about your request in the "Contact Notes" field.
7. Indicate whether you need access to the guest communication portal by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Access Type" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_communication_portal_access_request**: This is the form title and is not editable. You are required to enter a value in this field. Please enter your request for access to the guest communication portal.
* **guest_name**: Enter your name in this field. 
* **contact_email**: This field is for your contact email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **purpose**: Describe the reason you need access to the guest communication portal.
* **access_duration**: Enter the duration of your requested access to the guest communication portal in numerical format.
* **contact_notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your request.
* **access_type**: Select "Yes" if you need access to the guest communication portal, or "No" if you do not. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not already submitted a request for access to avoid duplication of work.
* If you have any technical issues with the form, please contact your administrator for assistance.
