# helipad_helicopter_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The helipad_helicopter_reservation_form is a tool for collecting information about helicopter reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required and optional information.
2. Review the form for any mistakes or inconsistencies.
3. Submit the form for further processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to include with your reservation.
* **Booking Info (2)** (`booking_info`, text, optional): Enter details about your reservation, such as dates and times.
* **Arrival Details (3)** (`arrival_details`, text, optional): Enter information about your arrival, including any relevant details.
* **Departure Details (4)** (`departure_details`, text, optional): Enter information about your departure, including any relevant details.
* **Facilities (5)** (`facilities`, text, optional): Enter any facilities or equipment you'll need during your reservation.
* **Venue (6)** (`venue`, text, optional): Enter the location or venue of your reservation.
* **Team Info (7)** (`team_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about your team or group you're reserving for.
* **Email (8)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (9)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Reserve Facilities (10)** (`facilities`, select_multiple, optional): Select any facilities or equipment you'll need during your reservation.
* **Reserve Venue (11)** (`venue`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you'll need to reserve a venue for your helicopter.
* **Reserve Team (12)** (`team`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you'll be working with a team for your reservation.
* **Date (13)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your reservation.
* **Time (14)** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your reservation.
* **Note (15)** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your reservation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Be as specific as possible when providing information.
- If you're unsure about any field, please feel free to ask for clarification.
