# Software Premium Membership Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Premium Membership Access Form is used to onboard new customers or partners and grant them access to premium features. This form should only be completed by authorized personnel with the necessary credentials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address.
2.  Select your team type (Jotform, IT Admin, or Customer Success Team).
3.  Choose the members of the IT Admin team who will have access.
4.  Select the members of the Customer Success Team who will have access.
5.  Enter a valid reason for your upgrade request.
6.  Click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in a format such as `username@domain.com`.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you need to be contacted.
*   **Reason for Upgrade** (`reason_for_upgrade`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the reason for your premium membership request.
*   **SaaS Team** (`saas_team`, `select_one`, required): Choose your team type from the options provided (Jotform, IT Admin, or Customer Success Team).
*   **IT Admin** (`it_admin`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the members of the IT Admin team who will have access.
*   **Customer Success Team** (`customer_success_team`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the members of the Customer Success Team who will have access.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, optional): This is the final step to submit the form, which will be reviewed and validated by our internal teams before access is granted. 

## Tips

*   This form should only be completed once per team member.
*   Only authorized personnel with the necessary credentials should fill out this form.
*   Ensure that you are entering accurate information to avoid delays in processing your access request.
