# Visitor Escort Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Escort Authorization Form is used to gather information and authorization from visitors and their escorts for visits to a facility or institution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **User Information**, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your **Visit Details**, such as the date and purpose of your visit.
3. Specify the **Escort Details**, including the name and contact information of your escort.
4. Enter the **Visit Duration**, in hours.
5. Provide your **Visitor Signature** to confirm that you have read and understand the form.
6. If required, obtain the **Approvals** from the Facility Manager, Security Officer, or Receptionist.
7. Enter any additional **Note** or **Approval Reason** if necessary.
8. Enter your **Email** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Visit Details (2)**: Enter the date and purpose of your visit.
* **Approval Date (3)**: Enter the date on which this form was approved.
* **Escort Details (4)**: Enter the name and contact information of your escort.
* **Visit Duration (5)**: Enter the number of hours you plan to spend on campus.
* **Visitor Signature (6)**: Enter your signature to confirm you have read and understand this form.
* **Escort Signature (7)**: Enter your escort's signature, if available.
* **Approved By (8)**: Select the person who approved this visit.
* **Approval Reason (9)**: Enter a brief reason for the approval.
* **Note (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Information 2 (13)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Visit Details 2 (14)**: Enter the date and purpose of your second visit.
* **Escort Details 2 (15)**: Enter the name and contact information of your second escort.
* **Visit Duration 2 (16)**: Enter the number of hours for your second visit.
* **Visit Duration 3 (17)**: Enter the number of hours for your third visit.
* **Visit Duration 4 (18)**: Enter the number of hours for your fourth visit.
* **Visit Duration 5 (19)**: Enter the number of hours for your fifth visit.
* **Visit Duration 6 (20)**: Enter the number of hours for your sixth visit.
* **Approved By 2 (21)**: Select the person who approved this visit.
* **Approved By 3 (22)**: Select the person who approved this visit.

Note: All fields marked as `required` must be filled out for the form to be valid.
