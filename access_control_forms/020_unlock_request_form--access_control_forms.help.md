# Unlock Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for users to submit a request to gain access to a system, area, or tool.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the provided fields. Make sure to select the relevant options that apply to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input, text, required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Request Type** (Request Type, select_one, required): Select the type of access you are requesting (e.g., "System Access", "Tool Access", etc.).
* **Description** (Description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of why you need the access.
* **Date Needed** (Date Needed, date, required): Enter the date you need the access by.
* **Time Needed** (Time Needed, time, optional): Enter the time you need the access by (leave blank if not applicable).
* **Reason** (Reason, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Area Name** (Area Name, text, required): Enter the area you are requesting access to.
* **System Name** (System Name, text, required): Enter the system you are requesting access to.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your request.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_multiple, optional): Select the tools that are related to your request.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, required): Select the status of your request.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your request.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Email** (User Email, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **User Name** (User Name, text, required): Enter your name.
* **User ID** (User ID, number, required): Enter your user ID.
* **Access Level** (Access Level, select_one, optional): Select the level of access you are requesting.
* **Access Type** (Access Type, select_one, required): Select the type of access you are requesting.
* **Request Status** (Request Status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request.
* **Request Category** (Request Category, select_one, optional): Select the category of your request.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select_one, optional): Select the person who submitted the request.
* **Updated At** (Updated At, time, required): The date and time your request was last updated.
* **Created At** (Created At, time, required): The date and time your request was created.

Please submit your request and a member of our team will review your submission.
