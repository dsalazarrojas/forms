# Educational Library Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users seeking access to an educational library. This form helps the library administrators to understand the user's requirements and provide access to the relevant library resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information in the "User Input" field.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Enter the library details you are requesting access to in the "Library Details" field.
4. Specify your request details in the "Request Details" field.
5. Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter in the "Submitter Details" field.
6. If you have any additional input, please enter it in the "User Input 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the user.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you effectively.
* **Library Details (3)**: Describe the library you are seeking access to, including its location, collection, and any specific resources you are interested in.
* **Request Details (4)**: Specify the reason for your request, including the duration of access you need and any specific resources you require.
* **Submitter Details (5)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you for any further information.
* **User Input 2 (6)**: If you have any additional information or comments, please enter it here.
