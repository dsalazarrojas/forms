# Narcotic Reconciliation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Narcotic Reconciliation Form is used to record the reconciliation of narcotics, ensuring accuracy and accountability in the quantity received, used, and discarded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the received quantity of narcotics and verify that it matches the expected quantity.
2. Check the "Received Quantity" field and enter the actual received quantity.
3. Determine if any items were returned or not, and select the correct option in the "Returned Items" field.
4. Enter the number of items used in the "Items Used" field.
5. Enter the date received in the "Received Date" field.
6. If any items were discarded, enter the number in the "Items Discarded" field.
7. Indicate if the reconciliation was completed successfully in the "Reconciled" field.
8. Add any additional notes regarding the reconciliation process in the "Reconciliation Note" field.
9. Enter your name in the "Pharmacist Name" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Pharmacist Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Received Quantity** (`Received Quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the actual quantity of narcotics received.
* **Returned Items** (`Returned Items`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if any items were returned, and "No" otherwise.
* **Items Used** (`Items Used`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of items used.
* **Received Date** (`Received Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the narcotics were received.
* **Items Discarded** (`Items Discarded`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of items discarded, if any.
* **Reconciled** (`Reconciled`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if the reconciliation was completed successfully, "Yes" or "No".
* **Reconciliation Note** (`Reconciliation Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the reconciliation process.
* **Pharmacist Name** (`Pharmacist Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the person completing the form.
* **Pharmacist Phone** (`Pharmacist Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
