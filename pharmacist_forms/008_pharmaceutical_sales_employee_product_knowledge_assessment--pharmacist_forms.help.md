# Pharmaceutical Sales Employee Product Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the pharmaceutical sales employee's product knowledge, identify areas where they need training, and evaluate their sales performance.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form below to provide detailed information about yourself and your sales performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Rep (Sales Rep)** (`sales_rep`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as a sales representative.
* **Product Knowledge (Product Knowledge)** (`product_knowledge`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you have in-depth knowledge of our products, "No" otherwise.
* **Training Needs (Training Needs)** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the areas where you need additional training. You can select multiple options.
* **Date First Assessed (Date First Assessed)** (`date_first_assessed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your first assessment. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Territory (Territory)** (`territory`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you have sold in our territory, "No" otherwise.
* **Role (Role)** (`role`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you are in a leadership role, "No" otherwise.
* **Training Status (Training Status)** (`training_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer "Active" if you are currently receiving training, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Product Category (Product Category)** (`product_category`, `select_one`, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you have knowledge of our product categories, "No" otherwise.
* **Sales Region (Sales Region)** (`sales_region`, `text`, required: false): Enter your sales region.
* **Sales Manager (Sales Manager)** (`sales_manager`, `text`, required: false): Enter your sales manager's name.

Note: Please answer all the questions honestly to provide accurate information.
