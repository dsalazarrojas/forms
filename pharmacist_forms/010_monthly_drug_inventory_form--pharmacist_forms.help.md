# monthly_drug_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by pharmacists to report their business organization's monthly inventory. The form is crucial for accurate and up-to-date inventory management and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the business organization name and date of the inventory count.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each inventory item to indicate if it is available or not.
3. Enter the name of the pharmacist completing the form.
4. Enter the asset value of the inventory.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the inventory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Organization**: Enter the name of the business organization that the inventory belongs to.
* **Inventory Date**: Select the date of the inventory count using the calendar widget.
* **Inventory Items**: Select "Yes" for each item that is available and "No" for each item that is not available.
* **Pharmacist Name**: Enter the name of the pharmacist completing the form.
* **Asset Value**: Enter the asset value of the inventory, which is likely the cost price of the items.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the inventory, such as any discrepancies or issues with the inventory count.
