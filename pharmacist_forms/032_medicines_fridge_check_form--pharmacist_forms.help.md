This help guide is for the "Medicines Fridge Check Form" designed for pharmacists and healthcare professionals to monitor and maintain accurate temperature readings in refrigerators where medicines are stored. This form serves to gather data on the current temperature, temperature range limits, and notes from the user. It helps pharmacists to track and maintain records of the fridge's performance, ensuring the quality and safety of stored medicines.

### Purpose

This form is intended to facilitate the recording and tracking of refrigerator temperature and performance data to support quality control measures for stored medicines.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the necessary details for each field as prompted.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fridge Name** (`fridge_name`, text, required): Provide the name of the refrigerator unit, e.g., "Fridge A" or "Storage Room 1".
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, required): Record the current temperature inside the fridge.
* **Min Limit** (`min_limit`, number, required): Enter the minimum allowed temperature limit for the stored medicines.
* **Max Limit** (`max_limit`, number, required): Enter the maximum allowed temperature limit for the stored medicines.
* **Current Reading** (`current_reading`, number, required): Record the current temperature reading from the fridge's thermometer.
* **Previous Reading** (`previous_reading`, number, required): Record the temperature reading from the previous check.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the fridge's performance, maintenance, or issues.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the user who recorded the temperature reading and notes.

### Tips

* Make sure to record accurate and up-to-date temperature readings to ensure the quality and safety of stored medicines.
* If the current reading is outside the min and max limits, note the reason for the deviation and take corrective action as necessary.
* Keep the notes field for tracking any recurring issues or maintenance required for the fridge.

This guide is provided to help you complete the Medicines Fridge Check Form efficiently and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to your supervisor or colleague.
