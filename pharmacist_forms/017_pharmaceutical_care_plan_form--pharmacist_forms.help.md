# Pharmaceutical Care Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's pharmaceutical care plan. This includes details such as patient name, date of birth, reason for visit, medical history, medication history, assessment, treatment plan, follow-up, and medication reconciliation. The form is essential for healthcare professionals to provide accurate and safe medication management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form's header section, which is a note field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field. This is where you can add the patient's full name.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient DOB" field. Make sure to use the correct date format.
4. In the "Reason for Visit" field, describe the reason for the patient's visit.
5. In the "Medical History" field, provide any relevant medical history or allergies.
6. In the "Medication History" field, list any current or past medications the patient is taking.
7. In the "Assessment" field, describe the patient's current health status.
8. In the "Treatment Plan" field, outline the recommended treatment or care plan.
9. In the "Follow-up" field, select whether follow-up is required.
10. In the "Medication Reconciliation" field, select any medications the patient is currently taking. This is a required field.
11. In the "Signature Date" field, enter the date of the signature.
12. In the "Signature" field, select who signed the form, such as a pharmacist or another healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, not required): This is the form's header section, where you can add any additional notes or comments about the patient.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, `date`, not required): Enter the patient's date of birth using the correct date format.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, `text`, not required): Describe the reason for the patient's visit.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): Provide any relevant medical history or allergies.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, `text`, not required): List any current or past medications the patient is taking.
* **Assessment** (`assessment`, `text`, not required): Describe the patient's current health status.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, not required): Outline the recommended treatment or care plan.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether follow-up is required.
* **Medication Reconciliation** (`medication_reconciliation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, not required): Select who signed the form, such as a pharmacist or another healthcare professional.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a healthcare professional or the form's creator.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to ensure optimal patient care.
