# Prescription Refill Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from your patients for prescription refill. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure their prescription is refilled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Medication Information** (`medication_info`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the medication you are requesting to be refilled.
* **Doctor Information** (`doctor_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your doctor's name and any other relevant details.
* **Prescription Information** (`prescription_info`, text, optional):
	+ Provide the prescription number or any other relevant prescription details.
* **Refill Request** (`refill_request`, text, optional):
	+ Specify the reason for the refill request.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for your digital signature.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for the patient's digital signature.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for the doctor's digital signature.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional):
	+ Once you have filled out the form, press submit to send it in. 

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely. Ensure your patient's prescription is filled out correctly. If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the pharmacy directly.
