# Pharmacy COVID 19 Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to manage the COVID-19 waitlist for pharmacies, allowing you to add or remove patients from the waitlist, and provide information about their status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "Pharmacy COVID 19 Waitlist Form" field.
2. Select the category of the patient's condition (e.g. "COVID-19" or "Other").
3. Provide a brief description of the patient's condition.
4. Check the "Embed" box if you want to embed the patient's information on your website or platform.
5. If you want to share the patient's information, enter their phone number and email address in the corresponding fields.
6. Describe the device or equipment used to treat the patient's condition.
7. List the features of the treatment or service provided to the patient.
8. Confirm that the patient's information complies with HIPAA regulations.
9. Enter the patient's phone number.
10. Enter the patient's email address.
11. If the patient is on the waitlist, click the "Add to Waitlist" button to add them to the list.
12. If the patient is already on the waitlist, click the "Remove from Waitlist" button to remove them from the list.
