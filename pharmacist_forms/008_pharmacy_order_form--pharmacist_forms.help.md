# Pharmacy Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Order Form is a crucial document for ordering prescriptions. It ensures that pharmacists and medical staff receive all necessary information about patients and prescribers, facilitating accurate and efficient medication dispensing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Name**: Fill in the patient's name, making sure to include any relevant titles (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the prescription or the date the form was completed.
3. **Medication List**: Select whether the patient requires medication or not.
4. **Dosage Form**: Select the correct dosage form of the medication, if applicable.
5. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of medication prescribed.
6. **Instructions**: If necessary, add any specific instructions for the patient.
7. **Prescriber Name**: Fill in the name of the prescriber (the doctor or healthcare professional).
8. **Prescriber Information**: (Optional) Additional information about the prescriber.
9. **Pharmacy Address**: Enter the address of the pharmacy or where the medication will be dispensed.
10. **Patient Phone**: Add the patient's phone number for easy contact.
11. **Prescriber Phone**: Add the prescriber's phone number for verification or follow-up.
12. **Prescription Number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the prescription.
13. **Medication Notes**: (Optional) Additional notes about the medication, such as side effects or allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Date**: The date the prescription is being ordered or when the form is completed.
* **Medication List**: Check 'Yes' if the patient is taking medication, 'No' otherwise.
* **Dosage Form**: Select the correct dosage form if the medication has specific forms (e.g., oral, injectable, or topical).
* **Quantity**: Enter the exact amount of medication prescribed.
* **Instructions**: Add any specific instructions for the patient's medication regimen.
* **Prescriber Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional who prescribed the medication.
* **Prescriber Information**: Additional information about the prescriber (e.g., contact number or specialty).
* **Pharmacy Address**: Enter the address of the pharmacy or where the medication will be dispensed.
* **Patient Phone**: Add the patient's phone number for easy communication.
* **Prescriber Phone**: Add the prescriber's phone number for verification or follow-up.
* **Prescription Number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the prescription.
* **Medication Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the medication, including side effects or allergies.
