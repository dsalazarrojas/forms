# Pharmacy Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Service Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with the pharmacy service. This feedback will help us evaluate our performance and make improvements to enhance the patient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction section to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
2. Answer each question based on your experience with the pharmacy service. Use the provided scale (e.g., 1-5) to rate your satisfaction with each aspect of the service.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
4. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes, select the contact information that you prefer (e.g., email, phone number, or address).
