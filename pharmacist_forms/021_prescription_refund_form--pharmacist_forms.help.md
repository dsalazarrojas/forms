# Prescription Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Refund Form is used by patients to report any issues or concerns they have with their prescription. This form helps us to gather information about any errors or unsatisfactory outcomes with the prescription so that we can take corrective action.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions carefully and fill out each field accurately.
2. Choose the option that best describes the issue or concern you are experiencing with your prescription.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
4. Provide a detailed message explaining the reason for your refund request.
5. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prescription Error** (`prescription_error`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to report any errors or issues with your prescription. Choose 'Yes' if you have received an incorrect or missing prescription, or 'No' if your prescription is correct.
* **Unsatisfactory Outcome** (`unsatisfactory_outcome`, select_one, required/optional): This field helps us understand if your treatment outcome was not satisfactory. Choose 'Yes' if you are not satisfied with the results, or 'No' if you are satisfied.
* **Other Concerns** (`other_concerns`, text, required/optional): If you have any other concerns or issues not listed above, please describe them in this field.
* **Reason for Refund** (`refund_reason`, text, required): Explain the reason why you are requesting a refund in this field. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your issue.
* **Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your name accurately and completely.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your refund request.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your refund request.
* **Message** (`patient_message`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your refund request.
