# Pharmacy Staff Leave Month Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select a month for which a staff member is on leave or not. It is essential to complete this form accurately to prevent leave duplication and ensure proper leave records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the month for which you are on leave from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the year if applicable.
3. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Choose whether you are on leave or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the options for Leave Type.
5. If you are on leave, provide a brief reason for your leave in the Leave Reason field.
6. Enter your Leave Start Date if you are on leave.
7. Enter your Leave End Date if you are on leave.
8. If you are on leave, enter the duration of your leave.
9. Enter the date you will be returning to work.
10. Enter your staff name and ID for identification purposes.
11. Enter your manager's ID for approval and verification purposes.
12. Finally, enter the location and department you are working in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Month** (`1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the month for which you are on leave from the list of options provided.
* **Year** (`2`, `number`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the year for which you are selecting the month.
* **Start Date** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Type** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose whether you are on leave or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the options.
* **Leave Reason** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): If you are on leave, provide a brief reason for your leave.
* **Leave Start Date** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): If you are on leave, enter the start date of your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (`8`, `date`, required/optional): If you are on leave, enter the end date of your leave.
* **Leave Duration** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): If you are on leave, enter the duration of your leave.
* **Return Date** (`10`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you will be returning to work.
* **Staff Name** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your staff name for identification purposes.
* **Staff ID** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your staff ID for identification purposes.
* **Manager ID** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your manager's ID for approval and verification purposes.
* **Location** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where you are working.
* **Department** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department you are working in.
