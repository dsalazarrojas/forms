# Security Bond Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Bond Form is a tool used by lawyers to gather necessary information for creating and managing security bonds. This form helps ensure that all required details are collected in a clear and concise manner, making it easier to process bonds efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Company Name** in the first field. This is the name of the company involved in the bond.
2. Enter the **Employee Name** in the second field. This is the name of the employee who will be receiving or providing the bond.
3. In the **Bond Amount** field, enter the agreed-upon amount for the security bond.
4. Enter the **Contract Start Date** in the fourth field, which is the date the contract begins.
5. Enter the **Contract End Date** in the fifth field, which is the date the contract ends.
6. In the **Payment Terms** field, provide any additional notes about payment terms or conditions for the bond.
7. Enter the **Employer Address** in the seventh field, which is the physical address of the employer.
8. Enter the **Employee Address** in the eighth field, which is the physical address of the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): This is the name of the company involved in the bond.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): This is the name of the employee who will be receiving or providing the bond.
* **Bond Amount** (`bond_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the agreed-upon amount for the security bond.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required/optional): This is the date the contract begins.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required/optional): This is the date the contract ends.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, note, required/optional): This is an optional field for adding any notes about payment terms or conditions for the bond.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required/optional): This is the physical address of the employer.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, required/optional): This is the physical address of the employee.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check for accuracy as the form data will be used for legal purposes.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your legal team or refer to local laws and regulations.
* Use clear and legible handwriting when filling out the form, especially for address fields.
* Save the completed form for future references or for submission to the relevant authorities.
