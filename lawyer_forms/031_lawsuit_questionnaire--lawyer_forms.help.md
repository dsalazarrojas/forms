# Lawsuit Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lawsuit Questionnaire is a form designed to collect and track information about lawsuits. This form is intended for use within an organization or legal firm to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the case.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check the accuracy of the information entered, especially contact details and case dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Potential Plaintiff**: Enter the name of the individual or entity bringing the lawsuit.
* **Plaintiff Address**: Enter the address of the potential plaintiff.
* **Defendant**: Enter the name of the individual or entity being sued.
* **Defendant Address**: Enter the address of the defendant.
* **Case Type**: Select the type of case (Civil, Criminal, or Personal Injury).
* **Plaintiff Contact Information**: Enter any relevant contact information for the plaintiff.
* **Plaintiff Attorney**: Enter the name of the plaintiff's attorney.
* **Defendant Attorney**: Enter the name of the defendant's attorney.
* **Case Status**: Select the current status of the case (Open, Pending, or Closed).
* **Plaintiff Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the defendant.
* **Case Location**: Select the location where the case is being heard (Court, Outside, or Online).
* **Plaintiff Phone**: Enter the phone number of the plaintiff.
* **Plaintiff Email**: Enter the email address of the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the defendant.
* **Defendant Email**: Enter the email address of the defendant.
* **Case Number**: Enter the unique case number.
* **Case Date**: Enter the date the case was filed.
* **Case Time**: Enter the time the case was filed.
* **Case Description**: Enter a brief description of the case.
* **Plaintiff Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the plaintiff and the defendant.
* **Defendant Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the defendant and the plaintiff.
* **Case Outcome**: Enter the expected or actual outcome of the case.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the case.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter the name and title of the person submitting the form.
