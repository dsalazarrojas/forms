# Name Change Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a name change petition, which is a formal request to update an individual's name in public records. The information collected will be used to initiate the name change process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to avoid delays in processing your name change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY or DD MMM YY.
* **Reason for Name Change** (`Reason for Name Change`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for your name change.
* **Current Name** (`Current Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current full name.
* **New Name** (`New Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your new full name you wish to use.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current contact phone number.
* **Reason for Change** (`Reason for Change`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed reason for your name change.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for further assistance.
