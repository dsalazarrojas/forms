# Partial Distribution Of Estate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partial Distribution Of Estate Form is a document used to divide the assets of a deceased person among their beneficiaries. It outlines the percentage of ownership each beneficiary will receive and the distribution percentage of each asset.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
2.  Enter the address of the property being distributed.
3.  Enter the value of the property being distributed.
4.  Select the type of asset being distributed from the options provided.
5.  Provide a brief description of the asset.
6.  List the beneficiaries who will receive the asset and their respective shares.
7.  Enter the percentage of the distribution for each beneficiary.
8.  Enter the effective date of the distribution.
9.  Enter the time of interest for the asset.
10. Enter any additional legal notes or observations.
11. Enter the name of the legal counsel or attorney involved.
12. Enter the name of the law firm handling the case.
13. Enter the address of the law firm.
14. Enter the contact number of the law firm.
15. Enter the email address of the law firm.
16. Enter the property tax ID number.
17. Enter the name of the person who passed away.
18. Enter the date of the will or testament.
19. Enter the date of the second will or testament (if applicable).
20. Select the type of legal documents involved in the case from the options provided.
21. Enter a statement that will be witnessed by an independent third-party witness (if required).
22. Enter the name of the witness.
23. Sign the form by entering your name.
24. Date and sign the form.
25. Enter your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Address** (property_address): The address of the property being distributed.
*   **Property Value** (property_value): The value of the property being distributed.
*   **Asset Type** (asset_type): Select the type of asset being distributed from the options provided.
*   **Asset Description** (asset_description): A brief description of the asset being distributed.
*   **Beneficiaries** (beneficiaries): List the beneficiaries who will receive the asset and their respective shares.
*   **Distribution Percentage** (distribution_percentage): Enter the percentage of the distribution for each beneficiary.
*   **Effective Date** (effective_date): Enter the date the distribution will take effect.
*   **Time of Interest** (time_of_interest): The time of interest for the asset.
*   **Legal Notes** (legal_notes): Additional legal notes or observations.
*   **Legal Counsel** (legal_counsel): The name of the legal counsel or attorney involved.
*   **Law Firm** (legal_firm): The name of the law firm handling the case.
*   **Law Firm Address** (legal_address): The address of the law firm.
*   **Contact Number** (contact_number): The contact number of the law firm.
*   **Email Address** (email_address): The email address of the law firm.
*   **Property Tax ID** (property_tax_id): The property tax ID number.
*   **Prophet's Name** (prophets_name): The name of the person who passed away.
*   **Testament Date 1** (testament_date): The date of the will or testament.
*   **Testament Date 2** (testament_date_2): If applicable, the date of the second will or testament.
*   **Legal Document** (legal_document): Select the type of legal documents involved in the case from the options provided.
*   **Witness Statement** (witness_statement): A statement that will be witnessed by an independent third-party witness (if required).
*   **Witness Name** (witness_name): The name of the witness.
*   **Sign By** (sign_by): Enter your name as the person signing the form.
*   **Date Signed** (date_signed): Date and sign the form.
*   **Signature** (signature): Enter your signature.
*   **Legal Fee** (legal_fee): Enter any legal fee associated with the distribution.
*   **Total Distribution Value** (total_distribution_value): The total value of the distribution.
*   **Total Distribution Value 2** (total_distribution_value_2): If applicable, the total value of the second distribution.
*   **Asset Description 2** (asset_description_2): A brief description of the second asset being distributed.
*   **Legal Document 2** (legal_document_2): Select the type of legal documents involved in the second case from the options provided.

**Tips**

*   Ensure that all fields are accurately and truthfully completed.
*   Review the form carefully before signing.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, consult a legal professional.
