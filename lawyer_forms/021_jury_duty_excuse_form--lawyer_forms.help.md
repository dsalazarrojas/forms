# Jury Duty Excuse Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jury Duty Excuse Form is a structured document designed to help individuals and legal professionals communicate their reason for excusing themselves from jury duty and their potential conflicts of interest. It is intended for use by courts, law firms, and other organizations involved in the legal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your reason for excusing yourself from jury duty in the `Reason` field.
2. Select all applicable conflicts of interest from the list in the `Conflict` field, or describe any other potential conflicts in the `Reason Other` field.
3. Enter specific dates of potential conflicts or unavailability in the `Valid Date`, `Valid Time`, `Conflict Date`, `Conflict Start`, and `Conflict End` fields.
4. Provide additional details in the `Reason Other` field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your reason for excusing yourself from jury duty.
* **Conflict** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable conflicts of interest, such as `Family members`, `Other`.
* **Conflict Other** (text, optional): Describe any other potential conflicts not listed in the `Conflict` field.
* **Valid Date** (date, required): Enter a date when you will be available to fulfill your jury duty.
* **Valid Time** (time, required): Enter a time when you will be available to fulfill your jury duty.
* **Conflict Date** (date, required): Enter the date of a potential conflict.
* **Conflict Time** (time, required): Enter the time of a potential conflict.
* **Conflict Valid Time** (time, required): Enter the time when the conflict will be valid.
* **Conflict Start** (date, required): Enter the start date of a potential conflict.
* **Conflict End** (date, required): Enter the end date of a potential conflict.
* **Reason Other** (text, optional): Provide additional details about your reason for excusing yourself from jury duty.
* **Reason Conflict Other** (text, optional): Describe any other reason for conflict not listed in the `Reason` field.
* **Reason Conflict Family Members** (text, optional): Describe any conflict related to family members not listed in the `Reason` field.
* **Reason Conflict Valid Date** (date, optional): Enter a date when the conflict will be valid.
* **Reason Conflict Valid Time** (time, optional): Enter a time when the conflict will be valid.
* **Reason Conflict Reason** (text, required): Enter a reason for your conflict with another individual or group.
* **Reason Conflict Conflict Other** (text, optional): Describe any other conflict not listed in the `Reason` field.
* **Reason Conflict Reason** (text, optional): Enter a reason for your conflict with another individual or group.
