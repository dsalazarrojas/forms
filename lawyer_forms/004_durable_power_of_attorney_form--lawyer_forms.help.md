# Durable Power Of Attorney Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a legal document that grants one person the authority to act on behalf of another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the information of the grantor (the person granting authority).
2. Complete the contact information of the grantor.
3. Enter the information of the attorney (the person who will be represented).
4. Sign the grantor's signature section.
5. Provide witness information for two witnesses.
6. Enter the grantor's address.
7. Indicate whether the grantor's phone number is up to date or not.
8. Optionally, add a date if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **grantor_info** (`grantor_info`, text, required: false): Please enter the grantor's name and personal information.
* **grantor_contact** (`grantor_contact`, text, required: false): Fill in the grantor's contact information, including any relevant phone numbers and addresses.
* **attorney_info** (`attorney_info`, text, required: false): Enter the attorney's name and information.
* **grantor_signature** (`grantor_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the grantor's name in this section.
* **witness_info_1** (`witness_info_1`, text, required: false): Provide information about the first witness.
* **witness_info_2** (`witness_info_2`, text, required: false): Provide information about the second witness.
* **grantor_address** (`grantor_address`, text, required: false): Enter the grantor's address.
* **grantor_phone** (`grantor_phone`, select_one, required: true): Answer with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the grantor's phone number is up to date.
* **date** (`date`, date, required: false): Optionally, add the date if necessary.
