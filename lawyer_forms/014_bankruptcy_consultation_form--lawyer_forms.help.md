# Bankruptcy Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bankruptcy Consultation Form is used to gather information about your financial situation, marital status, employment, and other relevant details to help our attorneys provide you with personalized advice and guidance throughout the bankruptcy process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to provide your contact information to ensure our team can reach out to you for further discussions.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or guidance from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consultation Date (<page 1>):** <page 1 name>: Scheduled appointment date to discuss your case.
* **First Name (<client_first_name>):** Your first name (please use your full name).
* **Last Name (<client_last_name>):** Your last name (please use your full name).
* **Email Address (<client_email>):** Your primary contact email (we'll use it to get in touch with you).
* **Phone Number (<client_phone>):** Your best contact phone number (we'll call or text you for updates).
* **Street Address (<client_address>):** Your current residential address.
* **City (<client_city>):** City of residence.
* **State (<client_state>):** State of residence.
* **ZIP Code (<client_zip>):** Postal code of your residence.
* **Marital Status (<marital_status>):** Choose your current marital status:
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Divorced
	+ Separated
	+ Widowed
* **Spouse Name (if applicable) (<spouse_name>):** If applicable, provide your spouse's name.
* **Number of Dependents (<dependents>):** Number of children and dependents.
* **Current Occupation (<occupation>):** Your job title and field.
* **Employer Name (<employer_name>):** Name of your current employer.
* **Monthly Gross Income (<monthly_gross_income>):** Your monthly gross income before deductions and taxes.
* **Spouse Monthly Income (<spouse_income>):** If married and working, enter your spouse's monthly gross income before deductions and taxes.
* **Estimated Monthly Expenses (<total_monthly_expenses>):** Total monthly expenses (housing, food, utilities, etc.).
* **Total Unsecured Debt (<total_unsecured_debt>):** Total unsecured debt (credit cards, medical bills, personal loans).
* **Total Secured Debt (<total_secured_debt>):** Total secured debt (mortgage, car loans).
* **Total Assets (<total_assets>):** Total assets (cash, property, vehicles, retirement).
* **Reason for Bankruptcy Consultation (<reason_for_consultation>):** Describe your financial situation and why you're seeking bankruptcy consultation.
* **Prior Bankruptcy Filing (<previous_bankruptcy>):** If you've filed for bankruptcy before, select 'Yes'.
* **Are You Interested in a Specific Chapter? (<chapter_preference>):** If you've researched bankruptcy options, select the chapter that interests you:
	+ Chapter 7
	+ Chapter 11
	+ Chapter 13
	+ Unsure
	+ No Preference
* **Have Creditors or Collectors Contacted You? (<creditor_communication>):** If creditors or collectors have contacted you, select 'Yes'.
* **Do You Currently Have Legal Representation? (<legal_representation>):** If you have an attorney, select 'Yes'.
* **Specific Questions for the Attorney (<attorney_questions>):** List your primary concerns or questions for the attorney.
