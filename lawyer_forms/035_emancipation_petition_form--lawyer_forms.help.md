# Emancipation Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emancipation Petition Form is a legal document used to terminate the parental rights and responsibilities of one parent.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the parent seeking emancipation.
2.  Enter the name of the child.
3.  Choose the relationship between the parent and child (e.g., Parent, Guardian).
4.  Provide a reason for the emancipation (optional).
5.  Enter the date and time of the court hearing.
6.  Enter the location of the court hearing.
7.  Enter the date the signature was made.
8.  Sign the form.
9.  If applicable, enter any additional information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent seeking emancipation.
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child.
* **Relationship to the child**: Select the relationship between the parent and child, such as Parent or Guardian.
* **Reason for emancipation**: Enter a reason for the emancipation, if applicable. This field is not required.
* **Court Date**: Enter the date of the court hearing.
* **Court Time**: Enter the time of the court hearing.
* **Court Location**: Enter the location of the court hearing.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date the signature was made.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your name.
* **Child Signature**: If the child is of legal age and able to sign, enter their signature.
* **Parent Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the parent.
* **Child Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the child.
* **Other Information (if applicable)**: Enter any other information that may be relevant to the emancipation request.
