# Lawful Presence Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lawful Presence Verification Form is a document that helps verify an individual's status as a citizen or national of the United States. It is used to confirm that the individual has a valid document that proves their current lawful presence in the country.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Verify that you are physically present in the United States.
2.  Choose the type of document you are providing as proof of your lawful presence. Options include US Citizen, US National, US Green Card, US Permanent Resident Card, US Work Visa, or Other.
3.  Enter the number of your document.
4.  Choose the date your document was issued.
5.  Choose the date your document will expire.
6.  Enter your phone number.
7.  Enter your email address.
8.  If you selected 'Other' as your document type, describe your document in the 'other_details' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of document that you are providing as proof of your lawful presence in the United States. Options include:
	+ US Citizen: Select this option if you are a citizen of the United States.
	+ US National: Select this option if you are a national of the United States.
	+ US Green Card: Select this option if you have a US Green Card.
	+ US Permanent Resident Card: Select this option if you have a US Permanent Resident Card.
	+ US Work Visa: Select this option if you have a US Work Visa.
	+ Other: Select this option if you have a document of a different type.
* **Document Number** (`document_number`, number): Enter the number of your document.
* **Document Issue Date** (`document_issue_date`, date): Enter the date your document was issued.
* **Document Expiration Date** (`document_expiration_date`, date): Enter the date your document will expire.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text): If you selected 'Other' as your document type, describe your document in this field.
