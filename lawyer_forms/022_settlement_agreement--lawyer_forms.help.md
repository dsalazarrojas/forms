# Settlement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about a settlement agreement between two parties, including the plaintiff and defendant. It gathers details about the reasons for the settlement, income disclosures, and other relevant information. The form also includes fields for signatures from the parties and their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the plaintiff's name in the **Plaintiff Name** field.
2. Enter the defendant's name in the **Defendant Name** field.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reasons for settlement in the **Reasons for Settlement** field.
4. Disclose any income or assets in the **Income Disclosures** field.
5. Describe the disposition and dispute in the **Disposition and Dispute** field.
6. Enter the effective date in the **Effective Date** field.
7. Sign the form with your signature in the **Signature** field.
8. Sign the form as the plaintiff with their signature in the **Plaintiff Signature** field.
9. Sign the form as the defendant with their signature in the **Defendant Signature** field.
10. Enter the name of your lawyer in the **Lawyer Name** field.
11. Sign the form as your lawyer with their signature in the **Lawyer Signature** field.
12. Enter the company name (if applicable) in the **Company Name** field.
13. Enter the date of the agreement in the **Agreement Date** field.
14. Describe the type of dispute in the **Dispute Type** field.
15. Enter a description of the dispute in the **Dispute Description** field.
16. Enter the reason for the dispute in the **Reason for Dispute** field.
17. Enter the amount of the dispute in the **Dispute Amount** field.
18. Enter the status of the dispute in the **Dispute Status** field.
19. Enter the outcome of the dispute in the **Dispute Outcome** field.
20. Enter the date of the dispute outcome in the **Dispute Outcome Date** field.
20. Enter any notes about the dispute outcome in the **Dispute Outcome Note** field.
