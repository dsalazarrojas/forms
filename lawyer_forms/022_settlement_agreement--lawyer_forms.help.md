# Settlement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about a settlement agreement between two parties, including the plaintiff and defendant. It gathers details about the reasons for the settlement, income disclosures, and other relevant information. The form also includes fields for signatures from the parties and their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the plaintiff's name in the **Plaintiff Name** field.
2. Enter the defendant's name in the **Defendant Name** field.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reasons for settlement in the **Reasons for Settlement** field.
4. Disclose any income or assets in the **Income Disclosures** field.
5. Describe the disposition and dispute in the **Disposition and Dispute** field.
6. Enter the effective date in the **Effective Date** field.
7. Sign the form with your signature in the **Signature** field.
8. Sign the form as the plaintiff with their signature in the **Plaintiff Signature** field.
9. Sign the form as the defendant with their signature in the **Defendant Signature** field.
10. Enter the name of your lawyer in the **Lawyer Name** field.
11. Sign the form as your lawyer with their signature in the **Lawyer Signature** field.
12. Enter the company name (if applicable) in the **Company Name** field.
13. Enter the date of the agreement in the **Agreement Date** field.
14. Describe the type of dispute in the **Dispute Type** field.
15. Enter a description of the dispute in the **Dispute Description** field.
16. Enter the reason for the dispute in the **Reason for Dispute** field.
17. Enter the amount of the dispute in the **Dispute Amount** field.
18. Enter the status of the dispute in the **Dispute Status** field.
19. Enter the outcome of the dispute in the **Dispute Outcome** field.
20. Enter the date of the dispute outcome in the **Dispute Outcome Date** field.
20. Enter any notes about the dispute outcome in the **Dispute Outcome Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Settlement Agreement Form**: This is the main form that captures the details of the settlement agreement.
* **Plaintiff Name**: Enter the name of the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Name**: Enter the name of the defendant.
* **Reasons for Settlement**: Explain the reasons for the settlement agreement.
* **Income Disclosures**: Disclose any income or assets.
* **Settlement Property Agreement**: This field is currently empty, but it may be used to capture additional details about the settlement property agreement.
* **Disposition and Dispute**: Describe the disposition and dispute.
* **Effective Date**: Enter the effective date of the agreement.
* **Signature**: Sign this form to indicate your agreement with the terms of the settlement.
* **Plaintiff Signature**: Sign this form as the plaintiff to confirm their agreement with the terms of the settlement.
* **Defendant Signature**: Sign this form as the defendant to confirm their agreement with the terms of the settlement.
* **Lawyer Name**: Enter the name of your lawyer.
* **Lawyer Signature**: Sign this form as your lawyer to confirm their agreement with the terms of the settlement.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company (if applicable).
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Dispute Type**: Describe the type of dispute.
* **Dispute Description**: Enter a description of the dispute.
* **Reason for Dispute**: Enter the reason for the dispute.
* **Dispute Amount**: Enter the amount of the dispute.
* **Dispute Status**: Enter the status of the dispute.
* **Dispute Outcome**: Enter the outcome of the dispute.
* **Dispute Outcome Date**: Enter the date of the dispute outcome.
* **Dispute Outcome Note**: Enter any notes about the dispute outcome.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required information accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before signing it.
* Sign the form in the presence of your lawyer, if possible.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
* Consult with your lawyer before filling out this form, if you have any questions or concerns.
