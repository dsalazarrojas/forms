# Legal Benefits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Legal Benefits Order Form is designed to collect information from users to process their legal benefits requests. This form helps businesses gather the necessary details to provide legal benefits to their employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form completely to ensure accurate processing of your legal benefits request.
2. Please make sure to enter your business name and contact information accurately.
3. Provide your employee's first name for the legal benefits to be processed for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and information for the legal benefits to be processed for.
* **Employee 1 First Name** (`employee_1_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your employee's first name for the legal benefits to be processed for.
