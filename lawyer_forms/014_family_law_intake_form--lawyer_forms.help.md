# Family Law Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Law Intake Form is designed to collect basic information from clients seeking family law services. This form is meant to gather essential details to ensure that our legal experts can provide you with the best possible assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Client Name**, **Client Email**, **Client Phone Number**, **Address**, and **Date of Birth** sections with the required information.
2. Select your **Employment Status** from the dropdown menu.
3. In the **Family Member 1** and **Family Member 2** sections, list the names of all family members in the household, separated by commas.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled in to ensure accurate assessment and efficient service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Name**
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
### 2. **Client Email**
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
### 3. **Client Phone Number**
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
### 4. **Address**
* **Address**: Enter your physical mailing address.
### 5. **Date of Birth**
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
### 6. **Employment Status**
* **Employment Status**: Choose your current employment status from the options provided:
	+ Employed: You are employed full-time or part-time.
	+ Self-Employed: You own your business or work for yourself.
	+ Unemployed: You are not currently employed.
### 7. **Family Member 1**
* **Family Member 1**: List the names of all family members in the household, separated by commas.
### 8. **Family Member 2**
* **Family Member 2**: List the names of all family members in the household, separated by commas.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure efficient service.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us for guidance.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in to avoid any delays in processing your request.
* Please note that this form is intended for the specific purpose of seeking family law services. If unsure, please do not fill out the form for other types of legal matters.
