# Disclaim Inheritance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disclaim Inheritance Form is used for a person (disclaimant) to disown or renounce a claim of inheritance. It collects information from both the disclaimant and the beneficiary to formalize the disclaiming process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:
- Enter the name of the inheritance you are disclaiming.
- Enter the amount claimed and the amount received.
- Provide the reason for disclaiming the inheritance.
- Enter the date of the disclaimer.
- Enter your name and address as the disclaimant.
- Enter the name and address of the beneficiary.
- Describe your relation to the beneficiary.
- Enter your phone number.
- Enter the phone number of the beneficiary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disclaim Inheritance** (`inheritance_disclaim`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the inheritance you are disclaiming.
* **Amount Claimed** (`amount_claimed`, `number`, required): Enter the amount that was claimed for the inheritance.
* **Amount Received** (`amount_received`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you received for the inheritance.
* **Reason Disclaimed** (`reason_disclaimed`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for why you are disclaiming the inheritance.
* **Disclaimer Date** (`disclaimer_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are disclaiming the inheritance.
* **Disclaimant Name** (`disclaimant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the disclaimant.
* **Disclaimant Address** (`disclaimant_address`, `text`, required): Enter your address as the disclaimant.
* **Beneficiary Name** (`beneficiary_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the beneficiary receiving the inheritance.
* **Beneficiary Address** (`beneficiary_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the beneficiary receiving the inheritance.
* **Beneficiary Relations** (`beneficiary_prophets`, `text`, required): Describe your relation to the beneficiary.
* **Disclaimant Phone** (`disclaimant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as the disclaimant.
* **Beneficiary Phone** (`beneficiary_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the beneficiary receiving the inheritance.

## Tips
Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of the disclaiming of inheritance. If you need help or clarification, contact the relevant authorities or authorities handling the disclaiming process. 

This guide is meant to be used as a reference for completing the form, and you should not rely solely on this guide for legal advice. Consult with a lawyer or other relevant authorities for professional advice and guidance. 

Also, make sure to save this form for your future reference and record-keeping.
