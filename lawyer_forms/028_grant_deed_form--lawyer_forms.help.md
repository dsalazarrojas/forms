# Grant Deed Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Deed Form is a document used to transfer ownership of a property from one person to another. It's a legal agreement between the Grantor and Grantee, ensuring that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the Grantor and Grantee.
2. Provide a detailed description of the property being transferred.
3. Ensure that the Grantor and Grantee signs the form in the presence of a Notary Public.
4. Have the Notary Public sign and stamp their seal on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Deed Form** (`grant_deed_form`): This is the main title of the form. Please fill in the title to ensure it's accurate and clear.
* **Grantor Name** (`grantor_name`): Fill in the name of the person or entity transferring the ownership of the property. This is a required field.
* **Grantor Address** (`grantor_address`): Provide the address of the person or entity transferring the ownership. This is an optional field, but recommended for clarity.
* **Grantee Name** (`grantee_name`): Fill in the name of the person or entity receiving the ownership of the property. This is an optional field, but recommended for clarity.
* **Grantee Address** (`grantee_address`): Provide the address of the person or entity receiving the ownership. This is an optional field, but recommended for clarity.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`): Describe the property being transferred, including its location, features, and any relevant details.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Provide the address of the property being transferred. This is a required field.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Fill in the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`): Fill in the time the form was signed.
* **County** (`county`): Mention the county where the property is located.
* **Notary Public Name** (`notary_public_name`): Identify the Notary Public who signed the form.
* **Notary Seal** (`notary_seal`): This field is for the Notary Public's official seal.
* **Notary License Number** (`notary_license_number`): Provide the Notary Public's license number.
* **Grantor Signature** (`grantor_signature`): The Grantor signs the form to confirm their agreement.
* **Grantee Signature** (`grantee_signature`): The Grantee signs the form to confirm their agreement.
* **Notary Signature** (`notary_signature`): The Notary Public signs the form to confirm their role.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely.
* Make sure that both the Grantor and Grantee understand the terms and conditions of the transfer.
* Have the Notary Public verify the identity of both parties before signing the form.
* Store the signed form safely and securely to prevent tampering or loss.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, seek advice from a legal expert.
