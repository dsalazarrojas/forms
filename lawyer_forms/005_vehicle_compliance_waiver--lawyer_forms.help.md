# Vehicle Compliance Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Compliance Waiver form is used to gather information and agreements from customers and companies about a vehicle's compliance. It's a crucial document for ensuring accuracy and preventing miscommunications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions before filling out the form.
2. Fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid any mistakes or omissions.
4. Sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Record the name of the individual associated with the vehicle.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the model number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, required): Describe the type of vehicle, for example, sedan, SUV, etc.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, required): Describe the overall condition of the vehicle.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, select_one, required): Indicate that the customer has read and agreed to the terms. Please sign and date as required.
* **Lawyer Signature** (`lawyer_signature`, time, required): Record the signature of the lawyer or representative, signifying their agreement and verification of the customer's agreement.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Record the name of the company associated with the vehicle.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the company.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the company.
* **Company Signature** (`company_signature`, select_one, required): Indicate that the company has read and agreed to the terms. Please sign and date as required.
* **Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, text, required): Describe any waivers or agreements made for the vehicle.
* **Waiver Note** (`waiver_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the waiver.
* **Lawyer Agreement** (`lawyer_agreement`, text, required): Describe the lawyer or representative's understanding and agreement to the terms.
* **Lawyer Note** (`lawyer_note`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments from the lawyer.
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes from the company.
