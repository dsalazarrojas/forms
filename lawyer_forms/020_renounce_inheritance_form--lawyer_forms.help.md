# Renounce Inheritance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renounce your inheritance, which is the act of giving up your legal right to inherit property or assets from a family member or relative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Select your **Relationship status** from the dropdown options.
4. Fill in your **Address** in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone number" field.
6. Fill in your **Date of birth** in the "Date of birth" field in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
7. Describe your **Reason for renouncing** in the "Reason for renouncing" field.
8. Sign and date the "Signature" field.
9. Sign and date the "Witness signature" field.
10. Fill in the "Witness name" field with the name of the person witnessing your renunciation.
11. Fill in the "Witness address" field with the address of the person witnessing your renunciation.
12. Sign the "Notary public" field, which is notarized by a notary public officer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): The first name of the individual renouncing their inheritance.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): The last name of the individual renouncing their inheritance.
* **Relationship status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required): The status of the relationship between the individual and the person or entity from whom they are renouncing their inheritance (e.g., spouse, parent, sibling).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): The current address of the individual renouncing their inheritance.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): The contact phone number of the individual renouncing their inheritance.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): The date of birth of the individual renouncing their inheritance in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Reason for renouncing** (`reason_for_renouncing`, `text`, required): A brief explanation of the reason for renouncing their inheritance.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): A signature of the individual renouncing their inheritance, which should be in the correct format (e.g., printed name with date).
* **Witness signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, required): A signature of a witness who attests to the individual's identity and intention to renounce their inheritance.
* **Witness name** (`witness_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person witnessing the individual's renunciation.
* **Witness address** (`witness_address`, `text`, required): The address of the person witnessing the individual's renunciation.
* **Notary public** (`notary_public`, `text`, required): A notarized signature of a notary public officer, which confirms the authenticity of the form.
