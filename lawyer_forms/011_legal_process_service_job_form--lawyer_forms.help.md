# Legal Process Service Job Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Legal Process Service Job Form is designed to collect and track information about the legal process service jobs. This form is meant to be used to efficiently and accurately capture the details of each job, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible, taking care to enter accurate information for each field.**
2. **Read each field explanation carefully to ensure you understand the intended use of each field.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Service Information
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter the address where the service is to be performed. Enter the accurate address to ensure that the service can be efficiently located.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of service to be performed, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the service, e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the service, e.g., specific instructions or requirements.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the service is to be performed.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the service.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the service is to be performed.

### Page 2: Client Information
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your responses before submitting the form.
* Use the service address to ensure that the service is performed at the correct location.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
