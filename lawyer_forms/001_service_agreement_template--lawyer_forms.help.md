# Service Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Agreement Template is a standardized document used to capture the essential details and terms of a service provided to a client. It ensures that all necessary information is included and easily accessible, creating a binding agreement between the service provider and client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Provide the client's contact information in the **Contact Information** field (optional).
3. Describe the service provided, including its scope and any specific details, in the **Service Details** field.
4. Select the type of service provided from the **Service Type** dropdown menu.
5. Indicate the payment terms and conditions in the **Payment Terms** field (optional).
6. Enter the terms and conditions of the service in the **Terms and Conditions** field.
7. If applicable, include the service agreement template, which outlines the scope, terms, and conditions of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Agreement Template** (`service_agreement_template`, text, required): Enter a brief template outlining the scope, terms, and conditions of the service. This template is used as a starting point and can be customized as needed.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information, including address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): Describe the service provided, including its scope and any specific details.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service provided from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, optional): Indicate the payment terms and conditions of the service, using numerical values (e.g., monthly, hourly, lump sum).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Enter the terms and conditions of the service, including any specific requirements or restrictions.
* **Service Agreement Template** (`service_agreement_template`, time, optional): If applicable, include the service agreement template, which outlines the scope, terms, and conditions of the service.
