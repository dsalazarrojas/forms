# Affidavit Witness Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affidavit Witness Statement Form is a legal document that is used to collect information from a witness about an incident or event they have witnessed. This form is used to gather details about the witness's identity, their relationship to the parties involved, and their personal knowledge of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the witness information section, which includes your full name, address, and phone number.
2. Provide your email address for communication purposes, but this is optional.
3. Enter your current occupation or profession.
4. Enter the case information, including the case number, name, and the name of the court where the case is being heard.
5. Enter the date of the incident, approximate time, location, and any other relevant details about the incident.
6. List the parties involved and your relationship to them.
7. Provide a detailed account of what you witnessed, including any observations or details that are relevant to the case.
8. Declare that the statements made in this affidavit are true and correct.
9. Confirm that you have personal knowledge of the facts and were in a position to observe the events described.
10. Declare that you have no interest in the outcome of the case.
11. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Witness Information**: This section is where you provide your identification information, including your full name, address, and phone number.
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter your current residential or mailing address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter the city, state, and ZIP code of your address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes, but this is optional.
* **Occupation**: Enter your current occupation or profession.
* **Case Information**: This section is where you provide information about the case, including the case number, name, and the name of the court where the case is being heard.
* **Case Number**: Enter the court case number if known.
* **Case Name**: Enter the name of the legal case.
* **Court Name**: Enter the name of the court where the case is being heard.
* **Attorney Representing**: Enter the name of the attorney representing the case.
* **Incident Details**: This section is where you provide information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date when the event occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the approximate time when the event occurred.
* **Location of Incident**: Enter the specific location where the event occurred.
* **Parties Involved**: List the names of the individuals involved in the incident.
* **Relationship to Parties**: Enter your relationship to any of the parties involved.
* **Witness Statement**: This section is where you provide a detailed account of what you witnessed.
* **Factual Account of Events**: Provide a detailed and accurate account of what you witnessed.
* **Witness Capacity**: Explain how you know the parties involved or why you were present.
* **Truthfulness Declaration**: Declare that the statements made in this affidavit are true and correct.
* **Personal Knowledge Declaration**: Declare that the facts stated in this affidavit are based on your personal knowledge.
* **Ability to Observe**: Declare that you were in a position to observe the events described and that your senses were unimpaired.
* **No Interest Declaration**: Declare that you have no interest in the outcome of this case that would influence your testimony.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign and date the form to authenticate your identity and declaration.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the form to confirm your declaration.
* **Notary Public Section**: This section is for the notary public to complete, including their signature and seal.
* **Notary Seal**: Declare that the notary seal was applied.
* **Notarization Date**: Enter the date when the affidavit was notarized.
* **Attachments or Documents**: List any documents or evidence attached to this affidavit.
* **Additional Relevant Information**: Provide any other information that may be relevant to the case.
* **Oath Administration**: This section is for the notary public to complete.
* **Witness Declaration**: This section is where you declare that you have no interest in the outcome of the case.
* **Notary Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your declaration.
