# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Leave Request Form is designed for employees to submit their parental leave requests. This form is a crucial part of the process for requesting parental leave, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed by the relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name.
2.  Enter your job title.
3.  Select the start and end dates of your leave.
4.  Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Parental, Family, or Other).
5.  Provide a reason for your leave.
6.  Add any comments for your supervisor.
7.  Add any comments for you, the employee.
8.  Select the status of your request (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
9.  Add any comments for the manager.
10.  Choose the duration of your leave.
11.  Select the format of your leave starting and end dates.
12.  Select the frequency of your leave (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Occasional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
*   **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of your leave using the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Leave End Date** (`leave_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your leave using the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (Parental, Family, or Other).
*   **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for your leave request.
*   **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or instructions for your supervisor.
*   **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback for your manager.
*   **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your request (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
*   **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback for your manager.
*   **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your leave in days.
*   **Start Date Format** (`start_date_format`, select_multiple, required): Select the format of your leave starting date (MM/DD/YY, MM/DD/YYYY, or M/D/YY).
*   **End Date Format** (`end_date_format`, select_multiple, required): Select the format of your leave end date (MM/DD/YY, MM/DD/YYYY, or M/D/YY).
*   **Leave Frequency** (`leave_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose the frequency of your leave (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Occasional).
*   **Leave Starting Date** (`leave_starting_date`, date, optional): Select the starting date of your leave using the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YYYY.

Please note that all required fields must be filled in according to the specified format to ensure proper processing of your leave request.
