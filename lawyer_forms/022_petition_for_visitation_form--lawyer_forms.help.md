# Petition For Visitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petition For Visitation Form is a legal document used by parents or guardians to request court permission to visit their child. This form is used to provide the required information to the court, ensuring a smooth and legal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's name in the "child name" field.
2. Enter the parent's name in the "parent name" field.
3. Provide the child's address in the "address" field.
4. Enter the state where the child resides in the "state" field.
5. Enter the case number related to the child in the "case number" field.
6. Enter the child's date of birth in the "date of birth" field.
7. Enter the date of the hearing in the "date of hearing" field.
8. Sign the form in the "signature" field.
9. Enter your court signature in the "court signature" field.
10. Choose your residence status from the select_one options.
11. Provide a reason for the visitation request in the "reason for visitation" field.
12. Enter the court cost related to the case in the "court cost" field.
13. Enter the total cost of the case in the "total cost" field.
14. Enter any additional fees in the "fee" field.
15. Enter any other expenses related to the case in the "other expenses" field.
16. Enter any additional information about the case in the "other information" field.
17. Enter any relevant parent information in the "parent information" field.
18. Enter the date of your signature in the "signature date" field.
19. Select the case manager from the select_one options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Petition for Visitation Form** (`petition_for_visitation_form`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the purpose of the petition.
- **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the child involved in the case.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian requesting visitation.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide the child's current address.
- **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state where the child resides.
- **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required: false): Enter the case number related to the child.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the child's date of birth.
- **Date of Hearing** (`date_of_hearing`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the hearing related to the child.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form to confirm your identity as the parent or guardian.
- **Court Signature** (`court_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form as a representative of the court.
- **Parent Residence** (`parent_residence`, select_one, required: false): Choose your current residence status.
- **Reason for Visitation** (`reason_for_visitation`, text, required: false): Provide a clear explanation for the request of visitation.
- **Court Cost** (`court_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the cost associated with the court case.
- **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost of the case.
- **Fee** (`fee`, number, required: false): Enter any additional fees related to the case.
- **Other Expenses** (`other_expenses`, number, required: false): Enter any other expenses related to the case.
- **Other Information** (`other_info`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about the case.
- **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, note, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the parent or guardian.
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required: false): Sign and date the form.
- **Case Manager** (`case_manager`, select_one, required: false): Choose the case manager assigned to the case.
