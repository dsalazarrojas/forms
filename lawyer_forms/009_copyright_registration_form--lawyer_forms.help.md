# copyright_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The copyright registration form is used to register multiple works with their respective owners' information. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure proper registration of each work and its owner's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the work you are registering.
2. Select the type of work being registered.
3. Enter the date of the work's creation or publication.
4. Enter your name and address to identify yourself as the author of the work.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
7. Sign the form to confirm your authorship.
8. Enter the name of the client who commissioned or paid for the work.
9. Enter the client's address.
10. Enter the client's city.
11. Enter the client's state (if applicable).
12. Enter the client's zip code.
13. Enter the client's email address.
14. Enter the client's phone number.
15. Enter the serial number of the certificate issued for the work.
16. Enter the date the certificate was issued.
17. Enter the amount paid for the work's registration.
18. Enter the second serial number of the certificate (for multi-certificate works).
19. Select the type of certificate issued for the work.
20. Select the type of work being registered (if multiple works are being registered).
21. Select the owner of the copyright for the work.
22. Select the type of work being registered (if multiple works are being registered).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **copyright_title** (`copyright_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the work you are registering.
* **work_title** (`work_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the work being registered.
* **work_type** (`work_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of work being registered (e.g., book, music, art).
* **work_date** (`work_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the work's creation or publication.
* **author_name** (`author_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the author of the work.
* **author_address** (`author_address`, text, required): Enter your address to identify yourself as the author.
* **author_email** (`author_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **author_phone** (`author_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **author_signature** (`author_signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm your authorship.
* **client_name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client who commissioned or paid for the work.
* **client_address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter the client's address.
* **client_city** (`client_city`, text, required): Enter the client's city.
* **client_state** (`client_state`, text, optional): Enter the client's state (if applicable).
* **client_zip** (`client_zip`, text, required): Enter the client's zip code.
* **client_email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **client_phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **certificate_no** (`certificate_no`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the certificate issued for the work.
* **certificate_date** (`certificate_date`, date, required): Enter the date the certificate was issued.
* **fee_amount** (`fee_amount`, text, required): Enter the amount paid for the work's registration.
* **certificate_no2** (`certificate_no2`, text, required): Enter the second serial number of the certificate (for multi-certificate works).
* **certificate_type** (`certificate_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of certificate issued for the work.
* **work_title2** (`work_title2`, select_one, required): Select the type of work being registered (if multiple works are being registered).
* **copyright_owner** (`copyright_owner`, select_one, required): Select the owner of the copyright for the work.
* **work_type2** (`work_type2`, select_one, required): Select the type of work being registered (if multiple works are being registered).
