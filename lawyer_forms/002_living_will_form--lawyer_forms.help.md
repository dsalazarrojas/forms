# Living Will Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Living Will Form is a legal document that outlines the wishes of an individual regarding the distribution of their assets after they pass away. It is essential to complete this form accurately and comprehensively to ensure that your wishes are respected and your assets are distributed as you desire.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by listing all your assets, including properties, investments, insurance policies, etc.
2. Specify how you would like your assets to be distributed among your beneficiaries, including the percentage of assets to be left for each beneficiary.
3. Confirm the names of your beneficiaries.
4. Provide your executor's contact information, including their email address and phone number.
5. State how much you owe to others, including loans or debts.
6. Confirm the assets you would like to leave for your loved ones.
7. Confirm the assets you would like to leave for your loved ones (2).
8. Enter your executor's contact information for your second executor.
9. Enter the remaining assets you have left behind.
10. Enter additional assets you own.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your assets (e.g., properties, investments, insurance policies, etc.)?**
	+ This field is used to list all your assets. Please provide a comprehensive list of your assets, including properties, investments, insurance policies, etc.
	+ **Type:** select_multiple
* **How would you like your assets to be distributed among beneficiaries?**
	+ This field is used to specify how you would like your assets to be distributed among your beneficiaries. You can select 'Yes' to distribute assets, 'No' to not distribute, or choose 'Other' to specify a custom percentage.
	+ **Type:** select_one
* **Please confirm the names of your beneficiaries.**
	+ This field is used to confirm the names of your beneficiaries. Please enter the names of your beneficiaries.
	+ **Type:** text
* **What is your email address for executor contact?**
	+ This field is used to provide the contact information for your executor. Please enter your executor's email address.
	+ **Type:** email
* **What is your phone number for executor contact?**
	+ This field is used to provide the contact information for your executor. Please enter your executor's phone number.
	+ **Type:** text
* **How much do you owe to others (e.g., loans, debts, etc.)?**
	+ This field is used to state how much you owe to others, including loans or debts.
	+ **Type:** number
* **Please confirm your executor's name.**
	+ This field is used to confirm your executor's name.
	+ **Type:** text
* **What is the percentage of assets you would like to leave for others?**
	+ This field is used to specify the percentage of assets you would like to leave for others.
	+ **Type:** number
* **Confirm the assets you would like to leave for your loved ones.**
	+ This field is used to confirm the assets you would like to leave for your loved ones.
	+ **Type:** select_multiple
* **Confirm the assets you would like to leave for your loved ones (2).**
	+ This field is used to confirm the assets you would like to leave for your loved ones (2).
	+ **Type:** select_multiple
* **Please confirm the assets you have left behind.**
	+ This field is used to enter the remaining assets you have left behind.
	+ **Type:** number
* **Confirm the assets you would like to leave for others. (2)**
	+ This field is used to confirm the assets you would like to leave for others (2).
	+ **Type:** number
* **Please enter your executor's contact information for your second executor.**
	+ This field is used to provide the contact information for your second executor. Please enter their email address.
	+ **Type:** email
* **Please enter the phone number of your second executor.**
	+ This field is used to provide the contact information for your second executor. Please enter their phone number.
	+ **Type:** text
* **Assets Owned 2**
	+ This field is used to confirm the assets you would like to leave for others (2).
	+ **Type:** select_multiple
* **Assets Owned 2 Hint**
	+ This field is used to provide a hint for the assets you would like to leave for others (2).
	+ **Type:** text
