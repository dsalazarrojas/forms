# Defamation Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Defamation Reporting Form is a tool for claimants to report incidents of defamation in a structured and organized way. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and reported consistently, facilitating swift and accurate investigation and resolution of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all applicable claimants who are reporting the defamation incident (Claimant 1, Claimant 2, Claimant 3, etc.).
2.  Provide a detailed description of the defamation incident (Description Of Defamation).
3.  Specify the date and time of the defamation incident (Date Of Defamation and Time Of Defamation).
4.  Indicate the location where the defamation incident occurred (Location Of Defamation).
5.  Identify any media involved in the defamation incident (Media Involved).
6.  Add any relevant media details related to the defamation incident (Relevant Media Details).
7.  Provide proof or evidence related to the defamation incident (Proof Of Defamation Media).
8.  Add any relevant details about the claimant (Claimant Details).
9.  Add any details about the defendant (Defendant Details).
10.  If applicable, provide proof of identity for the claimant (Proof Of Identity).
11.  Add any other relevant details about the defamation incident (Other Relevant Details).
12.  Attach any supporting documents related to the defamation incident (Supporting Documents).
13.  Finally, sign the form with the claimant's signature (Claimant Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Defamation Reporting Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Claimant Information** (`defamation_claimant`): Select all applicable claimants who are reporting the defamation incident.
    *   This field allows multiple claimants to be selected.
*   **Description Of Defamation**: Provide a detailed description of the defamation incident.
*   **Date Of Defamation**: Specify the date of the defamation incident.
*   **Time Of Defamation**: Specify the time of the defamation incident.
*   **Location Of Defamation**: Indicate the location where the defamation incident occurred.
*   **Media Involved**: Identify any media involved in the defamation incident.
*   **Relevant Media Details**: Add any relevant media details related to the defamation incident.
*   **Proof Of Defamation Media**: Provide proof or evidence related to the defamation incident.
*   **Claimant Details**: Add any relevant details about the claimant.
*   **Defendant Details**: Add any details about the defendant.
*   **Proof Of Identity**: If applicable, provide proof of identity for the claimant.
*   **Other Relevant Details**: Add any other relevant details about the defamation incident.
*   **Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents related to the defamation incident.
*   **Claimant Signature**: Sign the form with the claimant's signature.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Defamation Reporting Form accurately and efficiently.
