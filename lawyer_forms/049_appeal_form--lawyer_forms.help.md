# Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appeal Form is designed to collect information from appellants seeking to appeal a decision. It captures essential details to facilitate the appeal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your full name in the **Full Name of Appellant** field.
2. Enter the original case number in the **Original Case Number** field.
3. Provide the date of the original decision in the **Date of Original Decision** field.
4. If applicable, specify the **Presiding Judge or Officer** who presided over the original decision.
5. In the **Statement of Reason for Appeal** field, clearly state why you are requesting reconsideration.
6. In the **Specific Legal Grounds for Appeal** field, reference specific laws or procedures if known.
7. If new evidence has been obtained, describe it in the **Description of New Evidence (if any)** field.
8. Specify the desired outcome or relief in the **Desired Outcome or Relief** field.
9. If you have a legal representative, enter their name in the **Name of Legal Representative (if any)** field.
10. Provide the contact email of your legal representative in the **Representative Contact Email** field.
11. Enter the date of filing in the **Date of Filing** field.
12. Finally, certify that the information provided is true and correct by selecting **I certify** in the **I certify that the information provided is true and correct** field, or choose **Other** if the situation is an exception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name of Appellant** (Text)
Full name of the person appealing.

### **Original Case Number** (Text)
The unique identifier number of the original case.

### **Date of Original Decision** (Date)
The date when the original decision was made.

### **Presiding Judge or Officer** (Text)
Name of the judge or officer who presided over the original decision.

### **Statement of Reason for Appeal** (Text)
Clearly state why you are requesting reconsideration.

### **Specific Legal Grounds for Appeal** (Text)
Reference specific laws or procedures if known.

### **Description of New Evidence (if any)** (Text)
Describe any new evidence that may support your appeal.

### **Desired Outcome or Relief** (Text)
Specify the desired outcome or relief you are seeking.

### **Name of Legal Representative (if any)** (Text)
Name of the legal representative (if any).

### **Representative Contact Email** (Email)
Contact email of the legal representative (if any).

### **Date of Filing** (Date)
The date when the appeal is being filed.

### **I certify that the information provided is true and correct** (Select Multiple)
Certify that the information provided is true and correct, or choose **Other** if an exception applies.
