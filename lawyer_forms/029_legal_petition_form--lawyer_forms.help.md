# Legal Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Legal Petition Form is a general-purpose form used to collect information about a legal petition. It is designed to be used as a template for various types of legal petitions and is not specific to a particular type of legal issue or jurisdiction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with your name, address, and contact information.
2. Provide case details, including case number or reference, in the Case Details section.
3. Choose the type of petition being submitted, including the legal basis and petition type.
4. Enter the name of the court and other relevant contact information.
5. Provide information about the legal representative, if applicable.
6. Sign and date the form, including the date served and date received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information)**: Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Petitioner (Petitioner)**: Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Case Details (Case Details)**: Enter the case number or reference, if applicable.
* **Case Number (Case Number)**: Enter the case number or reference.
* **Legal Basis (Legal Basis)**: Select the type of petition being submitted (Civil, Criminal, Other, or Unknown).
* **Petition Type (Petition Type)**: Select the type of petition being submitted (Injunction, Writ of Habeas Corpus, Writ of Mandamus, or Other).
* **Court (Court)**: Enter the name of the court or location.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter name, address, phone, and email of the contact person.
* **Legal Representative (Legal Representative)**: Enter name, firm, or organization of the legal representative.
* **Signature (Signature)**: Date, name, and title of the representative.
* **Date Served (Date Served)**: Enter the date served.
* **Served By (Served By)**: Enter the name of the server or process server.
* **Date Received (Date Received)**: Enter the date received.
* **Received By (Received By)**: Enter the name of the receiver or clerk.
* **Case Manager (Case Manager)**: Enter the name or title of the case manager.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Use a pen or digital pen to sign the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
