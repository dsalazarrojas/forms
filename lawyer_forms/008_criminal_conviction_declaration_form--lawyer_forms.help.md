# criminal_conviction_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The criminal conviction declaration form is designed to collect information about an individual's criminal history, including details about convictions, court cases, and associated dates. This form is intended for use in legal or administrative contexts where accurate and detailed information is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of your Criminal History in the text field.
3. Enter the Date of Conviction in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Select the Nature of Conviction from the available options.
5. Enter the details about the Offense, including Offense Type and Offense Description.
6. If applicable, enter the Court Case Number, Court Name, and Location of the offense.
7. Select the Case Status from the available options.
8. Enter the Date of Sentence and Sentence Length, if applicable.
9. Provide any Additional Info relevant to the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): If you'd like to provide your address for contact purposes, please enter it here.
* **Criminal History** (`criminal_history`, `text`, not required): A clear and concise description of your criminal history.
* **Date of Conviction** (`date_of_conviction`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the conviction in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Nature of Conviction** (`nature_of_conviction`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of conviction from the available options.
* **Offense** (`offense`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the offense.
* **Offense Type** (`offense_type`, `text`, not required): Enter the type of offense (e.g. felony, misdemeanor, etc.).
* **Court Case Number** (`court_case_number`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the court case number associated with the offense.
* **Court Name** (`court_name`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the name of the court where the case was heard.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the location where the offense occurred.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the status of the case from the available options.
* **Date of Sentence** (`date_of_sentence`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the sentence in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Sentence Length** (`sentence_length`, `text`, not required): Enter the length of the sentence, if applicable.
* **Offense Details** (`offense_details`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional details about the offense.
* **Offense Description** (`offense_description`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the offense.
* **Conviction Date** (`conviction_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of conviction, if not already provided.
* **Conviction Docket Number** (`conviction_docket_number`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the docket number associated with the conviction.
* **Sentence Date** (`sentence_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of sentence, if not already provided.
* **Offense Location** (`offense_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location where the offense occurred, if applicable.
* **Court Case Type** (`court_case_type`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the type of court case.
* **Court Level** (`court_level`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the level of the court.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the case number.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about the case or offense.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information before filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or relevant authorities.
- Review and proofread your form before submission to ensure accuracy.
