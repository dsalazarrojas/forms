This form is designed to collect information about an opposition motion, including details about the motion, the response to the motion, any supporting documents, and the date and time of the motion. This form is intended for use in legal settings, where an opposition motion is a formal objection to a previous motion or action taken by another party in a legal proceeding. This guide will walk you through how to complete this form, field by field, to ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.

## Purpose
The Opposition Motion Response Form is a legal document used to respond to an opposition motion. It is a formal document that outlines the details of the motion and the response to it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `motion_details` field with a brief description of the opposition motion.
2. In the `written_response` field, provide a clear and concise written response to the opposition motion.
3. If applicable, select the relevant `supporting_documents` from the list provided.
4. Enter the `motion_date` in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
5. Enter the `motion_time` in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **motion_details** (`motion_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for a brief description of the opposition motion.
* **written_response** (`written_response`, text, required/optional): This field is for a clear and concise written response to the opposition motion.
* **supporting_documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select up to three supporting documents that are relevant to the opposition motion.
	+ Select the relevant supporting documents from the list.
* **motion_date** (`motion_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the motion in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **motion_time** (`motion_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the motion in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

## Tips

* Be sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use clear and concise language in the `written_response` field.
* Select only relevant supporting documents to ensure that they are properly reviewed and considered.
* Use the correct format for dates and times to ensure accuracy.
