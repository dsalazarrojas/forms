# Estate Planning Client Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Estate Planning Client Questionnaire is a comprehensive form used to collect information about a client's estate planning needs and status. This form helps lawyers and other professionals in the estate planning process understand the client's requirements and provide accurate and efficient services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and detailed information for each field.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Double-check your responses for errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Plan ID** (`Estate Plan ID`, number, required: false): A unique identifier for the estate plan.
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required: false): The name of the client.
* **Estate Address** (`Estate Address`, text, required: false): The address of the estate.
* **Estate Value** (`Estate Value`, number, required: false): The value of the estate.
* **Beneficiaries** (`Beneficiaries`, select_multiple, required: false): List all beneficiaries for the estate plan. Options: Yes, No.
* **Legal Representative** (`Legal Representative`, select_one, required: false): List legal representative. Options: Jane Doe, John Smith.
* **Assets** (`Assets`, select_multiple, required: false): List all assets for the estate plan. Options: Yes, No.
* **Liabilities** (`Liabilities`, select_multiple, required: false): List all liabilities for the estate plan. Options: Yes, No.
* **Estate Plan Comments** (`Estate Plan Comments`, note, required: false): Comments about the estate plan.
* **Legal Issues** (`Legal Issues`, time, required: false): Any legal issues related to the estate plan.
* **Estate Plan Completion Date** (`Estate Plan Completion Date`, date, required: false): Date the estate plan was completed.
* **Estate Plan Revision Date** (`Estate Plan Revision Date`, date, required: false): Date the estate plan was revised.
* **Estate Plan Status** (`Estate Plan Status`, select_one, required: false): Status of the estate plan. Options: Active, Inactive.
* **Estate Plan Type** (`Estate Plan Type`, select_one, required: false): Type of the estate plan. Options: Yes, No.
* **Estate Plan Notes** (`Estate Plan Notes`, note, required: false): Notes about the estate plan.
* **Client Relationships** (`Client Relationships`, select_multiple, required: false): Client relationships. Options: Yes, No.
* **Client Documents** (`Client Documents`, select_multiple, required: false): List all documents for the client. Options: Yes, No.
* **Client Phone** (`Client Phone`, text, required: false): Phone number of the client.
* **Client Email** (`Client Email`, email, required: false): Email address of the client.
* **Client Address** (`Client Address`, text, required: false): Address of the client.
* **Legal Issues Notes** (`Legal Issues Notes`, note, required: false): Notes about legal issues.
* **Estate Plan Issues** (`Estate Plan Issues`, select_multiple, required: false): List all issues with the estate plan. Options: Yes, No.
* **Estate Plan Comments** (`Estate Plan Comments`, note, required: false): Additional comments about the estate plan.
* **Review Notes** (`Review Notes`, note, required: false): Notes for review.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form fields and should not be used as a substitute for consulting with a lawyer or other estate planning professional. Please review and confirm the accuracy of the information provided with a qualified professional.
