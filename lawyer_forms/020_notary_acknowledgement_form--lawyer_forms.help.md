# Notary Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for notary public acknowledgment purposes, allowing the notary to verify the identity and authenticity of signers. It collects essential information such as signers' and notary's signatures, names, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Signers Signature**: This section is where the signer will sign their name, verifying their identity.
2. **Notary Signature**: The notary public will sign their name, confirming their involvement in the acknowledgment process.
3. **Notary Name**: Enter the name of the notary public who signed the document.
4. **Signers Name**: Enter the name of the signer who signed the document.
5. **Date Signed**: Enter the date when the document was signed.
6. **Notary Date Signed**: Enter the date when the notary signed the document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Signers Signature** (signers_signature, text, optional): The signers' signature is a verification of their identity. This field is where the signer will sign their name.
* **Notary Signature** (notary_signature, text, optional): The notary's signature confirms their involvement in the acknowledgment process. This field is where the notary will sign their name.
* **Notary Name** (notary_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the notary public who signed the document.
* **Signers Name** (signers_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the signer who signed the document.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, optional): Enter the date when the document was signed.
* **Notary Date Signed** (notary_date_signed, date, optional): Enter the date when the notary signed the document.

## Tips

* Ensure that both the signers and notary sign their names correctly and legibly.
* Double-check the dates entered for accuracy.
* Store or print the completed form for future reference.
