# Women Attorneys Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women Attorneys Survey is a tool used to collect information from female attorneys to create a comprehensive database.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Survey Questions
Please answer the following questions to provide us with your information. You can skip any questions that are not applicable to you.

### Step 2: Enter Your Details
Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you if needed.

### Step 3: Professional Details
Provide your professional details such as your age, practice area, years practiced, firm size, and other relevant information.

### Step 4: Education
Provide your education details such as law school graduation year and attendance type.

### Step 5: Additional Information
Provide any additional information about your professional background such as job title, languages spoken, and areas of interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender (Female, Male, Other).
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Practice Area** (`practice_area`, `select_one`, required): Select your practice area (Civil Litigation, Criminal Law, Family Law, etc.).
* **Years Practiced** (`years_practiced`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have practiced law.
* **Firm Size** (`firm_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your firm size (Small, Medium, Large, Other).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your location.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): Enter your education background.
* **Bar License Number** (`bar_license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your bar license number.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `text`, optional): Enter your certification details.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, `text`, optional): Enter the languages you speak.
* **Areas of Interest** (`areas_of_interest`, `text`, optional): Enter your areas of interest.
* **Practice Areas of Interest** (`practice_areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your areas of interest (Civil Litigation, Criminal Law, etc.).
* **Years as Attorney** (`years_attorney`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been an attorney.
* **Years in Law School** (`years_law_school_attendance`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you spent in law school.
* **Law School Graduation Year** (`law_school_graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter your law school graduation year.
* **Law School Attendance** (`law_school_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Select your law school attendance (Full Time, Part Time, Other).
* **Law School Attendance Type** (`law_school_attendance_type`, `text`, optional): Enter your law school attendance type.
* **Other Law School Attendance** (`other_law_school_attendance`, `text`, optional): Enter any other law school attendance details.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your experience.
