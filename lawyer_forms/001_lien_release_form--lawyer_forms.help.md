## Purpose
The Lien Release Form is a document used to release a lien on an asset. It confirms that the borrower is willing to release their rights and interests in the lien, allowing the asset to be sold or transferred without any outstanding liens.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the lien release in the **Form Title** field.
2. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **Lien Category** field to indicate whether the lien exists and needs to be released.
3. Provide a brief description of the lien and its relevance to the asset, in the **Lien Description** field (optional).
4. Enter the name of the borrower releasing the lien in the **Borrower** field.
5. Enter the name of the attorney or representative signing on behalf of the borrower in the **Lawyer** field (optional).
6. Enter the date the form was signed in the **Date Signed** field.
7. Enter the time the form was signed in the **Time Signed** field (optional).
8. Add a signature of the borrower or representative in the **Signature** field.
9. Finally, enter the name of the lender or party receiving the released lien in the **Lender** field (optional).

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lien Release Form (Form Title)** (`text`, Optional): Enter a brief title for the lien release form.
* **Lien Category (lien_category)** (`select_one`, Required): Select **Yes** to indicate a lien exists, **No** otherwise.
* **Lien Description (lien_description)** (`note`, Optional): Provide a brief description of the lien and its relevance to the asset.
* **Borrower (borrower)** (`text`, Required): Enter the name of the borrower releasing the lien.
* **Lawyer/Lawyer Representative (lawyer)** (`text`, Optional): Enter the name of the attorney or representative signing on behalf of the borrower.
* **Date Signed (form_date_signed)** (`date`, Required): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed (form_date_signed_at)** (`time`, Optional): Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Signature (form_signature)** (`note`, Required): Add a signature of the borrower or representative.
* **Lender (form_lender)** (`text`, Optional): Enter the name of the lender or party receiving the released lien.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the lien category is set to **Yes** if a lien exists.
* A detailed lien description can help clarify the nature of the lien and its relevance to the asset.
* If a lawyer or representative is signing on behalf of the borrower, please ensure they have the necessary authority to do so.
* A clear and legible signature is required in the **Signature** field.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
