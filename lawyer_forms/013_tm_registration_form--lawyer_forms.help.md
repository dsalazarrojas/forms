# TM Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect general information for trademark registration. This form is used to gather details about a trademark, including its name, description, and registration status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "General Info" page, which includes fields for "Name", "Description", and "Class Number".
2. Proceed to the "Trademark Details" page, where you'll find fields for "Class Name", "Goods/Services", "Filing Type", and "Filing Status".
3. Move on to the "Registration Info" page, where you'll find fields for "Priority Date", "Filing Date", "Serial Number", "Registration Date", and "Renewal Date".
4. Finally, fill out the "Contact Info" page, which includes fields for "Email", "Phone", "Address", "City", "State", "Zip", "Country", and "Representative".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you'll provide a general introduction to the trademark. Enter the name and description of the trademark in the designated fields.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trademark.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the trademark.
* **Class Number** (`Class Number`, number, required/optional): Enter the class number of the trademark.
* **Class Name** (`Class Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the class name of the trademark.
* **Goods/Services** (`Goods/Services`, text, required/optional): Enter the goods or services covered by the trademark.
* **Filing Type** (`Filing Type`, select one, required/optional): Select the type of trademark filing (Trademark, Service Mark, or Registration).
* **Filing Status** (`Filing Status`, select one, required/optional): Select the status of the trademark filing (Pending, Registered, or Canceled).
* **Priority Date** (`Priority Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the priority date of the trademark.
* **Filing Date** (`Filing Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the trademark filing.
* **Serial Number** (`Serial Number`, number, required/optional): Enter the serial number of the trademark.
* **Registration Date** (`Registration Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the registration date of the trademark.
* **Renewal Date** (`Renewal Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the renewal date of the trademark.
* **Registration Number** (`Registration Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the registration number of the trademark.
* **Status** (`Status`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the trademark (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or comments about the trademark.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the trademark representative.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the trademark representative.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the trademark representative.
* **City** (`City`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the trademark representative.
* **State** (`State`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of the trademark representative.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the trademark representative.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of the trademark representative.
* **Representative** (`Representative`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trademark representative.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required/optional): Enter a signature for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field or its meaning, please refer to this help guide.
