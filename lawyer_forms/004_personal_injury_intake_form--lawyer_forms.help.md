# personal-injury-intake-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information and details about their personal injury experience. This includes contact information, injury details, witness information, medical information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Ensure that you provide all required information.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if it is not applicable to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): Please fill out your name, address, phone number, and email address to enable us to contact you for further information.
* **Injury Info** (Injury Info, text, optional): Please describe your injury in detail. This is a text field where you can provide as much information as you need to explain your injury.
* **Witness Info** (Witness Info, select_one, optional): Choose the best option from the list that describes the witness's relationship to you.
	+ Option 1: Family member
	+ Option 2: Friend
	+ Option 3: Other
* **Medical Info** (Medical Info, number, optional): Please provide any relevant medical information, such as doctor's visits or treatments.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, optional): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Medical Bills** (Medical Bills, number, optional): Please provide any medical bills or expenses related to your injury.
* **Medical Treatment** (Medical Treatment, text, optional): Please describe any medical treatment you received due to your injury.
* **Witness Statement** (Witness Statement, text, optional): Please provide a statement from the witness regarding your injury.
* **Witness Name** (Witness Name, text, optional): Please provide the name of the witness.
* **Witness Contact** (Witness Contact, text, optional): Please provide the contact information for the witness.
* **Witness Info** (Witness Info, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about the witness.
* **Client Info** (Client Info, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the list:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Witness Count** (Witness Count, number, optional): Please provide the number of witnesses involved.
* **Medical Record** (Medical Record, text, optional): Please provide any relevant medical records related to your injury.
* **Contact Date** (Contact Date, date, optional): Please provide the date you were contacted.
* **Contact Time** (Contact Time, time, optional): Please provide the time you were contacted.
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, note, optional): Please sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional): Please provide your name.
* **Case Status** (Case Status, select_one, optional): Choose the best option from the list:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
