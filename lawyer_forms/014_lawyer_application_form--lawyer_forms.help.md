**# Lawyer Application Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed for lawyers to apply or update their information in the system. It gathers essential details for the lawyer's profile and allows them to provide additional work experience and educational background. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and surname.
2. Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted by the system.
3. If you have any work experience, describe it in the text field provided.
4. If you've completed any formal education, provide a brief description of your educational background in the text field.
5. Once you've filled out all the necessary information, click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name in the field provided.
* **Personal Information** (`surname`, `text`, required): Please enter your surname in the field provided.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): If you have any formal education, please describe it in the text field provided.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): If you have any work experience, please describe it in the text field provided.
* **Email-Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that you can be contacted on.
* **Phone-Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, optional): Please enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, optional): Please enter your address line 2.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Please click this button to submit your application. 

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form. Your email address is required to ensure you can be contacted by the system. If you have any work experience or educational background, please provide a brief description to help us better understand your background.
