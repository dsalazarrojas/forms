# Divorce Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals seeking divorce-related services. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to help us better understand your situation and provide you with relevant information and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you need to fill out each field as accurately as possible. If you're unsure about any field, try to answer based on your best knowledge and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
## 
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, Required/Optional): Please fill in your name as it should appear on any official documents or records. This could be your full name, maiden name, or your name as it's currently reflected on your government identification documents.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, Required/Optional): If you have a case number, please provide it here. This helps us identify and track your case, if applicable.
* **Spouse Names** (`spouse_names`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Indicate if you are currently married and have a spouse, or if you're seeking a divorce. Select 'Yes' if you're married and 'No' if you're not.
* **Divorce Date** (`divorce_date`, date, Required/Optional): If you know the date of your divorce, please provide it. If not, you can skip this field for now.
* **Divorce Reason** (`divorce_reason`, text, Required/Optional): Briefly describe the reason for your divorce. This will help us understand your situation better.
* **Lawyer Info** (`lawyer_info`, text, Required/Optional): If you have an attorney, please provide their contact information or a brief description of your current representation.
* **Case Details** (`case_details`, note, Required/Optional): Share any additional information related to your case. This could be a detailed explanation of your situation or any specific questions you have.
* **Court Location** (`court_location`, text, Required/Optional): If your case is related to a specific court or jurisdiction, please provide the location.
* **Lawyer Phone** (`lawyer_phone`, text, Required/Optional): If you'd like to reach out to a lawyer directly, you can provide their phone number here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required/Optional): If you'd like to receive information or updates from us, provide your email address here.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, Required/Optional): If you have any other relevant information to share with us, please do so here.
