# Receipt Of Inheritance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receipt Of Inheritance Form is a legal document used to record the transfer of assets or properties from a deceased person to the beneficiary. It serves as proof of the inheritance and is essential for legal and financial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review and sign the form to confirm receipt of the inheritance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deceased Name**: Enter the full name of the person who has passed away.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the full name of the person or entity receiving the inheritance.
* **Agent Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the deceased and the person or entity receiving the inheritance (e.g., child, parent, spouse).
* **Inheritance Amount**: Enter the total value of the inheritance received (numeric).
* **Inheritance Description**: Provide a brief description of the assets or properties transferred (text).
* **Receipt Date**: Enter the date the inheritance was received (date).
* **Witness List**: List the names of any witnesses who may have been present during the process (text).
* **Lawyer Fees**: Enter any fees associated with the lawyer's services (numeric).
* **Tax Fees**: Enter any taxes or fees associated with the inheritance (numeric).
* **Other Fees**: Enter any other fees associated with the inheritance (numeric).
* **Total Fees**: Enter the total amount of fees associated with the inheritance (numeric).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before signing to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, consult a lawyer or financial advisor for guidance.
