# ownership_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ownership_declaration_form is a tool to help users declare their ownership of pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required and optional fields as much or as little as you wish.
* Enter the required information in the fields marked as "required".
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Ownership** (`pet_ownership`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pet's ownership.
* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, optional): Enter your pet's name.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pet you own (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, optional): Enter your pet's breed.
* **Pet Color** (`pet_color`, text, optional): Enter your pet's color.
* **Veterinarian Name** (`veterinarian_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your pet's veterinarian.
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased your pet.
* **Time of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, time, optional): Enter the time you purchased your pet.
