# Divorce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a couple's situation in a divorce scenario, helping to guide the conversation between parties involved in the divorce process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and answer every question honestly.
2. Ensure you understand each question and its relevance to your situation.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability, using the options provided.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a space to provide a brief introduction or background information about your situation.
* **Couples History** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the items that apply to your relationship, such as "Item 1", "Item 2", etc.
* **Property Distribution** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the best option for how you would like to distribute property.
* **Financials** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current financial situation.
* **Communication** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the items that apply to your communication situation, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Children** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the best option for how you would like to handle children.
* **Assets** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current asset situation.
* **Other Issues** (`8`, `note`, required: false): This is a space to provide any other relevant information about your situation.
* **Spouse Counseling** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant information about spouse counseling.
* **Court Documents** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the items that apply to your court documents, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Other Expenses** (`11`, `number`, required: false): Enter any other expenses you've incurred.
* **Mediation** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the best option for mediation.
* **Communication Skills** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the items that apply to your communication skills, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", etc.
* **Divorce Process** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the best option for the divorce process.

Note: This form is not a substitute for professional advice. Consult with a qualified attorney or other relevant professional before making any decisions related to divorce.
