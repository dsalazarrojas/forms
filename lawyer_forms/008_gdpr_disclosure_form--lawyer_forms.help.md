# GDPR Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Disclosure Form is a tool designed to inform users about the collection, use, and sharing of their personal data. This form helps users understand how their data will be used and with whom it will be shared.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer according to your personal preferences.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk) to provide the necessary information.
3. You can select multiple options for some fields.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (user_information, text, optional): Enter your name and other contact information. This field is used to identify you as a user and to contact you if needed.
* **Personal Data** (personal_data, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are willing to share your personal data, or 'No' if you are not. This field is used to determine whether you are comfortable sharing your personal data.
* **Use of Information** (use, text, optional): Describe how you would like your personal data to be used. You can type your preferences here, such as for marketing purposes or to improve our services.
* **Shaking Information with third-party partners** (sharing_info, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are willing to share your information with third-party partners or 'No' if not. This field is used to determine with whom you are comfortable sharing your data.
* **Data Collected** (data_collected, text, optional): Describe the type of data you are willing to share. You can be as specific as possible (e.g., email address, phone number, etc.).
* **Data Retention** (data_retention, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you want your data to be retained for a specific period, or 'No' if you want it to be deleted after your account is closed.
* **How we will use Information** (how, text, optional): Describe how you would like your personal data to be used. You can type your preferences here, such as for product development or to improve our services.
* **Sharing Information with third-party partners** (sharing_partners, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are willing to share your information with third-party partners or 'No' if not. This field is used to determine with whom you are comfortable sharing your data.
* **Data Collected** (data_collected, text, optional): Describe the type of data you are willing to share. You can be as specific as possible (e.g., email address, phone number, etc.).
