# GDPR Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to contact the GDPR department with questions, concerns, or requests related to data protection and regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the fields provided: name, contact information (email or phone number), and a brief message describing your query.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, indicated by an asterisk (*) symbol.
3. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us** (Contact Form, required: false): This field is a text box where you can enter any additional comments or details about your query.
* **Name (contact_person, label: Name, required: false)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or business cards.
* **Email (contact_email, label: Email, required: false)**: Enter your email address where we can respond to your query.
* **Phone (contact_phone, label: Phone, required: false)**: If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your contact number.
* **Message (contact_message, label: Message, required: false)**: Briefly describe your query or request in this text box.

## Tips
- Ensure your email address is formatted correctly (e.g., [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com)).
- If your query requires urgent attention, indicate this in your message.
- Please do not include any sensitive or confidential information in your message.
- If you have any trouble filling out the form or have questions, contact the GDPR department directly for assistance.
