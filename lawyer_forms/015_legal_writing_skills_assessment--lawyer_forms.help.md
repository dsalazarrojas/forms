# Legal Writing Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Writing Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's legal writing skills, knowledge, and experience. This form is intended for those seeking to gauge their proficiency in writing legal documents, contracts, and other legal-related content. It's a self-assessment tool to help you evaluate your strengths and weaknesses in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's purpose and instructions to understand what is being asked.
2.  Answer the questions and fields as accurately and completely as possible.
3.  For the "Self-Assessment" field, write a brief, detailed description of your experience and skills in legal writing.
4.  For the "Samples Uploaded" field, select up to three relevant samples that demonstrate your writing skills. You can choose from the provided options or type in your own custom labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant information about yourself that you think is necessary for this assessment. You can write a brief introduction or provide additional details about your background and experience.
*   **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your experience and skills in legal writing. Be as detailed as possible and provide specific examples of your work, such as the types of documents you've written, the frequency of your writing, and any notable successes or challenges you've faced.
*   **Samples Uploaded** (`samples_uploaded`, select multiple, optional): This is a field where you can upload up to three relevant samples of your legal writing work. Choose the options provided or type in your own custom labels to describe your samples. Some examples of relevant samples might include:
	+ A contract or agreement you've drafted
	+ A legal memo or opinion letter
	+ A pleading or complaint filed in court
	+ A client newsletter or article

## Tips
*   Be honest and thorough when completing this form.
*   Use specific examples and details to demonstrate your skills and experience.
*   Proofread your answers for clarity and grammar.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the form, ask for help or clarification.
