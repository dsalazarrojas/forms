# Request For Attorney Representation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured way for individuals to request representation by an attorney for a legal matter. It helps attorneys evaluate your case and determine whether to take on your representation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have included all required information to help the attorney understand your case.
3. Provide any additional information in the "case_details" field to help the attorney understand the specifics of your legal matter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for any additional information or context about your request.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This field is for your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for your contact phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information or questions you have about your legal matter.
* **Attorney Representation** (`attorney_representation`, `select_one`, optional): This field is for you to indicate whether you are seeking attorney representation.
*   - Choose "Yes" if you are seeking to hire an attorney for your legal matter.
*   - Choose "No" if you are not seeking attorney representation at this time.
* **Attorney Fee** (`lawyer_fee`, `number`, optional): This field is for the estimated amount of money you are willing or able to pay for attorney representation.
* **Lawyer Fee** (`legal_fee`, `number`, optional): This field is for the estimated total cost of the case, including any costs or fees not directly associated with attorney representation.
* **Case Details** (`case_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information about your case that you think would be helpful for the attorney to know.

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your responses.
* Ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Do not submit duplicate requests for the same legal matter.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the attorneys directly.
* The attorneys will review your request and may contact you for additional information or clarification before making a decision.
