# Mutual Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mutual Release Form is a document used to formalize the mutual release agreement between two parties. This form helps in providing a clear and concise record of the agreement, making it easier to manage and resolve any future disputes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mutual Release Form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Party Name** of the other party involved in the agreement.
2. Select the **Release Date** when the agreement was reached.
3. Provide the **Our Client Number** and **Your Client Number**, which are unique identifiers for both parties.
4. Enter the **Reason For Release**, which describes the circumstances or events that led to the mutual release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Name** (`party_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the other party involved in the agreement.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): This field is used to select the date when the agreement was reached.
* **Our Client Number** (`our_client_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the unique identifier for your company's clients.
* **Your Client Number** (`your_client_number`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the unique identifier for the other party's clients.
* **Reason For Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the circumstances or events that led to the mutual release.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and clear information for both parties.
- If any field is left blank, it may lead to confusion or disputes in the future.
- Review the form carefully before signing and submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
