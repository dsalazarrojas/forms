# Citizenship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for citizenship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
4. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (1): Provide your full name as it appears on your birth certificate. (Required)
* **Other Names Used** (2): List any other names you have been known by, including maiden names, aliases, etc. (Optional)
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your email address. (Required)
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number. (Required)
* **Current Address** (5): Provide your current address, including street, city, state, and zip code. (Required)
* **Date of Birth** (6): Enter your date of birth in the required format. (Required)
* **Place of Birth** (7): Enter the city and country where you were born. (Required)
* **Gender** (8): Select your gender from the available options. (Required)
* **Marital Status** (9): Select your marital status from the available options. (Required)
* **Spouse's Full Name** (10): If applicable, enter your spouse's full name. (Optional)
* **Spouse's Citizenship** (11): If applicable, enter your spouse's citizenship. (Optional)
* **Do You Have Children** (12): Select whether you have children. (Required)
* **Children's Names and Dates of Birth** (13): If yes, list your children's names and dates of birth. (Optional)
* **Country of Birth** (14): Enter the country where you were born. (Required)
* **Current Nationality** (15): Enter your current nationality. (Required)
* **U.S. Residency Status** (16): Select your current U.S. residency status from the available options. (Required)
* **Green Card Number** (17): If applicable, enter your green card number. (Optional)
* **Date Became Permanent Resident** (18): If applicable, enter the date you became a permanent resident. (Optional)
* **Social Security Number** (19): Enter your Social Security number. (Required)
* **Travel History** (20): List any trips over 30 days outside the U.S. in the past 5 years. (Required)
* **Have You Ever Been Arrested** (21): Select whether you have been arrested. (Required)
* **Please Explain** (22): If yes, provide an explanation. (Optional)
* **Selective Service Registration** (23): Select whether you are registered for selective service. (Optional)
* **Can You Read, Write, and Speak English** (24): Select whether you can read, write, and speak English. (Required)
* **Have You Studied U.S. History and Government** (25): Select whether you have studied U.S. history and government. (Required)
* **Willingness to Take Oath of Allegiance** (26): Select whether you are willing to take the oath of allegiance. (Required)
* **Applicant Signature** (27): Sign your name. (Required)
* **Date** (28): Enter the date you sign the form. (Required)
