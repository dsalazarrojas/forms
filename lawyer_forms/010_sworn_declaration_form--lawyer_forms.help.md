# sworn_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information and obtain a declaration or statement from the user, possibly for official or professional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the corresponding fields: name, address, city, state, zip, phone number, and email.
2. If required, sign the "Signature" field with your name.
3. Select "I, the undersigned, swear that the information provided above is true and accurate." or "I, the undersigned, do not swear that the information provided above is true and accurate." in the "Declaration" field, depending on the accuracy and truthfulness of the information provided.
4. Optionally, enter a date if necessary.
5. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, address, city, state, and zip.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required: false): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: false): Sign with your name if required.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date if required.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the two options, depending on the truth and accuracy of the information provided above.
