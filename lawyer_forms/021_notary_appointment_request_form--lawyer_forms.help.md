# Notary Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notary Appointment Request Form is used to schedule an appointment with a notary for specific document-related tasks. This form captures essential information to ensure a successful and efficient appointment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, contact information, and other required fields.
2. Select the type of document you need notarized.
3. Choose a date and time for the appointment.
4. Select the notary's name and confirm the appointment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of document you need notarized (e.g., Notary Acknowledgment, Affidavit).
* **Document Date** (`document_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the document you need notarized.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, select_multiple, required): Select the time you would like to schedule the appointment from the provided options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the notary.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Available Times** (`available_times`, select_multiple, optional): Select the times you are available for the appointment.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available for the appointment.
* **Notary Name** (`notary_name`, select_one, required): Select the notary you would like to schedule the appointment with.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, select_multiple, required): Select the hours you are available for the appointment.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the appointment will take place.
* **Document Location** (`document_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the document you need notarized.
* **Document Details** (`document_details`, text, required): Describe the document you need notarized, including its purpose and any other relevant details.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on the document.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the notary.
* **Notary Note** (`notary_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for the notary.
* **Confirm Appointment** (`confirm_appointment`, text, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the appointment details.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date, required): Select the date you confirm the appointment details.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, select_multiple, required): Select the time you confirm the appointment details.
* **Confirm Location** (`confirm_location`, text, required): Confirm the location where the appointment will take place.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to facilitate the appointment process.
