**Form purpose:**
The Estate Planning Intake Form is used to collect information necessary for creating an estate plan tailored to the client's specific needs and circumstances.

**How To Complete This Form:**
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill in your name and contact information (client_name, contact_method, contact_phone, contact_email).
3. Provide details about the type of estate (estate_type).
4. Specify the size and value of the estate (estate_size, estate_value).
5. Indicate the location of the estate (estate_location).
6. If applicable, specify the distribution plan for the estate (estate_distribution).
7. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of the attorney handling the estate (attorney_name, attorney_fees).
8. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Client Name (<client_name>):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Estate Type (<estate_type>):** Select the type of estate you are planning for (e.g., single-person estate, joint trust, etc.).
* **Estate Size (<estate_size>):** Enter the approximate value of the estate (e.g., number of acres, square feet, etc.).
* **Estate Location (<estate_location>):** Specify the address or location of the estate (street, city, state, zip, etc.).
* **Contact Method (<contact_method>):** Select the preferred method of contact (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **Contact Phone (<contact_phone>):** Enter your phone number for the attorney to contact you.
* **Contact Email (<contact_email>):** Enter your email address for the attorney to contact you.
* **Attorney Name (<attorney_name>):** If an attorney is handling the estate, enter their name and contact information.
* **Attorney Fees (<attorney_fees>):** Enter any fees associated with the attorney's services.
* **Estate Value (<estate_value>):** Enter the approximate value of the estate.
* **Estate Distribution (<estate_distribution>):** If applicable, specify the plan for distribution of the estate (e.g., to beneficiaries, charities, etc.).

**Tips:**

* Be as accurate as possible when providing information, as this will help us create a more effective plan.
* If you're unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for consulting an attorney, but rather a starting point for the planning process.
