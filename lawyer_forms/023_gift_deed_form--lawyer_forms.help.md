# Gift Deed Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Gift Deed Form is a legal document that facilitates the transfer of ownership of a property from one party (donor) to another (recipient). This form helps to ensure that the process is conducted accurately and in accordance with the legal requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form step-by-step, following the guidelines below.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the label's requirement status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Details** (`donor_details`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of the person gifting the property. This includes their full name, address, and phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property being gifted, such as Residential Property, Commercial Property, or Vacant Land.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, text, optional): Enter the value of the property, if applicable. This could include monetary value or other relevant details.
* **Gift Date** (`gift_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the gift is being made. This is a critical component of the gift deed process.
* **Gift Recipient** (`gift_recipient`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person or entity receiving the gift. This could be an individual, organization, or entity.
* **Gift Details** (`gift_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the gift, such as the reason for the gift or any specific conditions attached to it.
* **Donor Signature** (`donor_signature`, select_one, optional): Indicate how the donor's signature was obtained, either through a written signature or other means. This is an important field to ensure the legitimacy of the gift deed process.

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and attention to detail when filling out this form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to prevent errors or omissions.
* If you have any doubts or questions, consult the relevant authorities or seek legal advice.
