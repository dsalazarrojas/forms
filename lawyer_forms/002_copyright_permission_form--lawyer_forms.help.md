# Copyright Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copyright Permission Form is designed to collect information from users regarding their permission for the use of their work, ideas, or information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Information** field with your details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select the type of permission you are granting from the **Permission Type** dropdown menu.
3. Specify what you will be using this information for from the **Will you be using this information for** dropdown menu.
4. Provide your contact details, including your contact person's name, email, and address in the **Contact Details** field.
5. If applicable, enter the date of permission in the **Date of Permission** field.
6. Include your signature in the **Signature** field.
7. Enter the contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
8. Provide your contact email in the **Contact Email** field.
9. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
10. Enter your contact address in the **Contact Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_input`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, any other relevant contact information, and a brief description of your work or information.
* **Permission Type** (`permission_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Will you be using this information for** (`use_for`, select_one, required/optional): Specify what you will be using this information for, selecting from the available options.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact person's name, any other relevant contact information, and a brief description of your contact.
* **Date of Permission** (`date_of_permission`, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date you are granting permission.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Include your signature.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact address.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you are granting permission for the correct use case.
* Ensure you have entered accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* Understand that this form is not intended for any other use cases not listed on the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant contact person or email address.
