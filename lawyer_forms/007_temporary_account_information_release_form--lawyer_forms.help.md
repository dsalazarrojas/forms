# Temporary Account Information Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Account Information Release Form is designed to collect and release information about customers, including their name, email address, phone number, and address. This form is used to provide a convenient and secure way to manage customer account details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the customer's address in the "Address" field.
5. Review and submit the form to release the customer's account information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address associated with their account.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number associated with their account.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's physical address associated with their account.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the customer's name, email address, phone number, and address correctly to ensure accurate and secure account information release.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields have been completed correctly.
* If any field is incomplete or incorrect, contact the relevant customer service department for assistance.
* Use this form for its intended purpose only and do not use it to disclose sensitive or confidential information.
