# statuary_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a statuary statement. It is a legal or formal declaration form designed to collect information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information on **User Information** page.
2. Provide the date of the statuary declaration on **Statuary Declaration Date** page.
3. Declare your statuary statement on **Declare Statuary Statement** page.
4. Sign the statuary declaration on **Sign Statuary Declaration** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
**User Information** (user_input, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.

### Statuary Declaration Date
**Statuary Declaration Date** (statuary_declaration_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the statuary declaration.

### Declare Statuary Statement
**Declare Statuary Statement** (declare_statutory_statement, text, required: false): Declare this statement as true.

### Sign Statuary Declaration
**Sign Statuary Declaration** (sign_statuary_declaration, text, required: false): Sign the statuary declaration.
