# Law Firm Associate Paternity Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from law firm associates for their paternity leave of absence. It captures essential details about the leave, such as leave type, start and end dates, reason for absence, return date, and contact person details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Employee ID (if provided).
2. Select the correct Leave Type from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the Start Date for your leave.
4. Enter the End Date for your leave.
5. Provide a clear and concise Reason For Absence.
6. Enter the Return Date for your leave.
7. Select the status of your leave request.
8. If necessary, provide any Comments for the leave request.
9. Provide the Contact Person's name and their Contact Details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leave Request Details (field 1)**: This is the main section for the leave request.
* **Employee ID (field 2)** (`number`): This field is used to identify the employee. If you have been provided with an ID, please fill it in accurately.
* **Leave Type (field 3)** (`select_one`): This field allows you to select the type of leave you are requesting. Please choose from the options provided:
	+ Parental
	+ Adoption
	+ Surrogacy
	+ Grandparental
* **Start Date (field 4)** (`date`): Enter the start date of your leave in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date (field 5)** (`date`): Enter the end date of your leave in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Reason For Absence (field 6)** (`text`): Provide a clear and concise reason for your absence.
* **Return Date (field 7)** (`date`): Enter the date you plan to return to work in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Leave Status (field 8)** (`select_one`): This field indicates the status of your leave request. Please select from the options:
	+ Pending
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ In Progress
* **Comments (field 9)** (`note`): If necessary, provide any comments or additional information about your leave request.
* **Contact Person (field 10)** (`text`): Fill in the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Number (field 11)** (`text`): Fill in the contact number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (field 12)** (`email`): Fill in the contact email of the contact person.
* **Leave Request ID (field 13)** (`number`): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
