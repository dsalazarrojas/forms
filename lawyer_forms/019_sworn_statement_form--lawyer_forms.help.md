# sworn_statement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for creating a formal statement, such as a declaration or affirmation. Fill out the form to provide essential information about the declarer, the client, and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1.  Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and content.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information.
3.  Double-check your entries for accuracy.
4.  Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): A brief introduction or explanation about the context and purpose of the statement.
*   **Client Identification** (`client_identification`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or identification of the client or individual involved.
*   **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): Provide more detailed information about the client, such as their name, address, or contact details.
*   **Declaration of Truth** (`declaration_of_truth`, text, optional): Affirm or declare the truth of a specific statement or fact.
*   **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, optional): Enter information about the witness, including their name, contact details, or other relevant information.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the statement with your official signature and name.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the statement.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the statement.
## Tips

*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   Submit the form only when you are confident about the information provided.
