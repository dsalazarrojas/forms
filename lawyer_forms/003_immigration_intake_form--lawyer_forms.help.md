# Immigration Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Intake Form is designed to collect information from applicants regarding their immigration status and travel history. This form is used by immigration lawyers or authorities to gather necessary data for processing immigration applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the appropriate options for each field, based on your personal circumstances.
2. Ensure that you have answered all required fields (indicated by the "required" tag).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **personal_info** (Yes/No, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you are a personal info user. Please select 'Yes' if you are a personal info user and 'No' otherwise.
* **contact_info** (Yes/No, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you have contact info. Please select 'Yes' if you have contact info and 'No' otherwise.
* **immigration_status** (Active/Inactive, required: false)
	+ This field asks for your immigration status. Please select 'Active' if you are currently in an immigration process and 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **immigration_type** (Yes/No, required: false)
	+ This field asks if you are seeking immigration benefits or services. Please select 'Yes' if you are seeking immigration benefits or services and 'No' otherwise.
* **immigration_country** (Yes/No, required: false)
	+ This field asks which country you are seeking immigration benefits or services for. Please select 'Yes' for countries with which you have a connection and 'No' otherwise.
* **reason_for_travel** (Yes/No, required: false)
	+ This field asks why you are seeking immigration benefits or services. Please select 'Yes' for reasons related to travel and 'No' otherwise.
* **travel_history** (Yes/No, required: false)
	+ This field asks about your travel history. Please select 'Yes' if you have a travel history and 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have answered all required fields (indicated by the "required" tag).
* Please select the options that best match your circumstances.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the relevant authority or lawyer for clarification.
