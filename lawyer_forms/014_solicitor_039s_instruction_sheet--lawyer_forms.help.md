# Solicitor's Instruction Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solicitor's Instruction Sheet is a document that outlines the instructions and information related to a legal document or case. It is used to capture relevant details from both the solicitor and client sides.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the solicitor's name in the "Solicitor's name" field.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client's name" field.
3. Select the type of document from the "Document type" field.
4. Enter the date of the document in the "Document date" field.
5. Enter the time of the document in the "Document time" field.
6. Enter any specific instructions or notes in the "Instructions" field.
7. Enter the solicitor's signature in the "Signature" field.
8. Enter the solicitor's address in the "Solicitor's address" field.
9. Enter the client's address in the "Client's address" field.
10. Enter the address where the document is addressed in the "Document address" field.
11. Select the status of the document from the "Document status" field.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Solicitor's name** (`solicitor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a solicitor.
- **Client's name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's name.
- **Document type** (`document_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of document from the available options.
- **Document date** (`document_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the document.
- **Document time** (`document_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the document.
- **Instructions** (`instructions`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific instructions or notes.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature as a solicitor.
- **Solicitor's address** (`solicitor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address as a solicitor.
- **Client's address** (`client_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's address.
- **Document address** (`document_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where the document is addressed.
- **Document status** (`document_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the document from the available options.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
