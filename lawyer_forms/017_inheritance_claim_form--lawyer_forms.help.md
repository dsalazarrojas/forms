# Inheritance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inheritance Claim Form is designed to collect information from the claimant about their claim for an inheritance. This form helps gather essential details about the asset being claimed and the claimant's relationship to the inherited person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Provide the name of the inherited asset.
3. Enter the value of the inherited asset.
4. Specify the name of the person inheriting the asset.
5. Choose the correct relationship to the inherited person from the options provided.
6. Enter your name, address, contact information, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inheritance Claim Form** (`inheritance_claim_form_name`): Enter a brief description of the claim you are making. This field is not required.
* **Name of Inherited Asset** (`name_of_inherited_asset`): Provide the name or description of the asset you are claiming as an inheritance.
* **Value of Inherited Asset** (`value_of_inherited_asset`): Enter the monetary value of the inherited asset.
* **Name of Inherited Person** (`name_of_inherited_person`): Enter the full name of the person who left behind the asset being claimed.
* **Relationship to Inherited Person** (`relationship_to_inherited_person`): Choose the correct relationship you have to the person who left behind the asset. Options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`): Enter your name as the person claiming the inheritance.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`): Enter your contact address.
* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`): Provide your contact information.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`): Enter your phone number.

Remember to review and verify the information provided before submitting the form to ensure the accuracy and legitimacy of the claim.
