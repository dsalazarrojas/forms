# quit_claim_deed_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of ownership of a property from one party (the "grantor") to another (the "borrower").

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the grantor (party relinquishing ownership of the property) on page 1.
2. Enter the name and address of the borrower (party acquiring ownership of the property) on page 1.
3. Enter the address of the grantor (grantor's address) on page 1.
4. Enter the description of the property being conveyed on page 1.
5. Enter the Property ID number on page 1.
6. Enter the date the deed was executed on page 1.
7. Enter the amount of the conveyance (if applicable) on page 1.
8. Enter any consideration given for the conveyance (if applicable) on page 1.
9. Enter the county and state where the deed was granted on page 1.
10. Enter the city where the deed was granted on page 1.
11. Enter the grantor's zip code on page 1.
12. Enter the borrower's zip code on page 1.
13. Select the notary public who notarized the deed on page 2.
14. Enter the date the deed was notarized on page 2.
15. Enter the date the deed was recorded on page 2.
16. Enter the book and page number where the deed was recorded on page 2.
17. Enter the county recording district where the deed was recorded on page 2.
18. Enter the state where the deed was recorded on page 2.
19. Enter the city where the deed was recorded on page 2.
20. Enter the file number of the recorded deed on page 2.
21. Enter the date the deed was recorded on page 2.
22. Enter the seal or stamp of the notary public on page 2.
23. Enter the signature of the grantor on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **grantor** (`quit_claim_deed_form_page_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the party relinquishing ownership of the property.
* **borrower** (`name_of_borrower`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the party acquiring ownership of the property.
* **borrowers_address** (`address_of_borrower`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the party acquiring ownership of the property.
* **grantors_address** (`grantor_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the party relinquishing ownership of the property.
* **description** (`description_of_property`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the property being conveyed.
* **property_id_number** (`property_id_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the Property ID number (if applicable).
* **date_of_conveyance** (`date_of_conveyance`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the deed was executed.
* **amount** (`amount_of_conveyance`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of the conveyance (if applicable).
* **consideration** (`consideration`, `text`, optional): Enter any consideration given for the conveyance (if applicable).
* **county** (`county_where_granted`, `text`, optional): Enter the county where the deed was granted.
* **state** (`state_where_granted`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where the deed was granted.
* **city** (`city_where_granted`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where the deed was granted.
* **grantors_zip** (`zip_code_of_grantor`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip code of the grantor.
* **borrowers_zip** (`zip_code_of_borrower`, `text`, optional): Enter the zip code of the borrower.
* **notarized_by** (`notarized_by`, `select_one`, optional): Select the notary public who notarized the deed.
* **notarized_date** (`notarized_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the deed was notarized.
* **recorded_at** (`recorded_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date the deed was recorded.
* **recorded_book_and_page** (`recorded_book_and_page`, `text`, optional): Enter the book and page number where the deed was recorded.
* **county_recording_district** (`county_recording_district`, `text`, optional): Enter the county recording district where the deed was recorded.
* **recorded_state** (`recorded_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the state where the deed was recorded.
* **recorded_city** (`recorded_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where the deed was recorded.
* **recorded_file_number** (`recorded_file_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the file number of the recorded deed.
* **recorded_date** (`recorded_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the deed was recorded.
* **notarized_seal** (`notarized_seal`, `text`, optional): Enter the seal or stamp of the notary public.
* **grantors_signature** (`grantor_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the grantor.
