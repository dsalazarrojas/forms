# Record Of Court Proceedings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and document all aspects of court proceedings, including depositions, evidence, testimony, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Review each page to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Court Proceedings (Page 1)
* **Court Proceedings** (`court_proceedings`, text, optional): Briefly describe the court proceedings, including any notable events or discussions that took place.
* **Depositions** (`depositions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant depositions that occurred during the proceedings.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, note, optional): Provide any relevant evidence presented during the proceedings.
* **Testimony** (`testimony`, select_one, optional): Choose the most relevant testimony from the options provided.
* **Rulings** (`rulings`, text, optional): Record any relevant rulings or decisions made during the proceedings.
* **Court Reporter** (`court_reporter`, number, optional): Enter the court reporter's identification number.
* **Date Recorded** (`date_recorded`, date, optional): Enter the date the proceedings were recorded.
* **Time Recorded** (`time_recorded`, time, optional): Enter the time the proceedings were recorded.
* **Record of Proceedings** (`record_of_proceedings`, text, optional): Briefly summarize the overall record of the proceedings.

### Depositions (Page 2)
* **Depositions** (`depositions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant depositions that occurred during the proceedings.
* **Assigned Judges** (`assigned_judges`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all relevant judges assigned to the case.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, optional): Enter the case number for reference.
* **Court Location** (`court_location`, text, optional): Record the location of the court where the proceedings took place.
* **Trial Date** (`trial_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the trial took place.
* **Trial Time** (`trial_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the trial took place.
* **Record of Trial** (`record_of_trial`, note, optional): Record any notable events or details from the trial.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the case.
* **Record of Court Actions** (`record_of_court_actions`, text, optional): Summarize any relevant actions taken during the court proceedings.
* **Notes on Record** (`notes_on_record`, note, optional): Record any additional notes or observations from the record of proceedings.
* **Record of Proceedings Status** (`record_of_proceedings_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the record of proceedings.
* **Court Proceedings Notes** (`court_proceedings_notes`, note, optional): Record any additional notes or observations from the court proceedings.
* **Record of Court Proceedings Comments** (`record_of_court_proceedings_comments`, text, optional): Record any additional comments or feedback from the record of court proceedings.
* **Record of Court Proceedings Review** (`record_of_court_proceedings_review`, note, optional): Record any review or updates of the record of court proceedings.
* **Record of Court Proceedings Approval** (`record_of_court_proceedings_approval`, select_one, optional): Choose to approve or decline the record of court proceedings.
* **Record of Court Proceedings Signature** (`record_of_court_proceedings_signature`, number, optional): Enter the signature of the court reporter.

This guide is intended to be a comprehensive resource for filling out the Record of Court Proceedings Form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities.
