## gdpr_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The GDPR Application Form is a tool used to collect essential information from individuals who wish to apply for GDPR rights. This form ensures that the applicant provides accurate and complete contact information, job application details, and personal data, which will be used to assess and process their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the GDPR Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information accurately.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3. Ensure you have provided all necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (1, text, required/optional): Provide your name and address to ensure we can contact you regarding your application.
* **Job App** (2, text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the job you are applying for, including the job title, department, and location.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for updates. This will be used to send you important information related to your application.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number to allow us to contact you quickly and efficiently.
* **Address** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your current address to enable us to send you relevant and timely updates.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth to ensure we can accurately verify your identity.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have provided all necessary details before submitting.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
