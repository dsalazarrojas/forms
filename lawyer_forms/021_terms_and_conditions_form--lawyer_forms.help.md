# Terms And Conditions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to agree to the terms and conditions of an agreement between the customer and a business. It is a standard legal form that requires customer and business information to create a legal contract.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the terms and conditions carefully and ensure you understand them.
2. Fill in your business information (business name, address, etc.).
3. Fill in your customer information (name, address, email, phone number, etc.).
4. Sign the agreement.
5. Ensure that the business has also signed and dated the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Terms And Conditions** (Text): This is the section where you can enter any additional terms and conditions that need to be included in the agreement.
* **Agreement Type** (Select One): Please select 'Yes' to indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Date Of Acceptance** (Date): This is the date you accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Date Of Last Revision** (Date): This is the date the agreement was last revised.
* **Business Address** (Text): This is the address of the business.
* **Contact Person** (Text): This is the name of the person in the business who can be contacted.
* **Agreement Term** (Time): This is the term of the agreement.
* **Customer Name** (Text): This is your name.
* **Customer Address** (Text): This is your address.
* **Customer Email** (Email): This is your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (Text): This is your phone number (optional).
* **Customer Signature** (Text): This is your signature, indicating that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Business Signature** (Text): This is the business signature, indicating that they agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Date** (Date): This is the date you signed the agreement.
* **Time** (Time): This is the time you signed the agreement.
* **Terms And Conditions Agreed** (Select One): Please select 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature Date** (Date): This is the date you signed the agreement.
* **Signed By** (Text): This is your name, indicating that you signed the agreement.
* **Business Name** (Text): This is the name of the business.
* **Customer Copy** (Text): This is a copy of the agreement for you.
* **Business Copy** (Text): This is a copy of the agreement for the business.
* **Customer Acknowledged** (Select Multiple): Please select 'Yes' to indicate that you have received a copy of the agreement.
* **Terms And Conditions Version** (Text): This is the version of the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Version** (Text): This is the version of the agreement.
* **Business Acknowledged** (Select Multiple): Please select 'Yes' to indicate that the business has received a copy of the agreement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions before signing the agreement.
* Ensure that the business has also read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
