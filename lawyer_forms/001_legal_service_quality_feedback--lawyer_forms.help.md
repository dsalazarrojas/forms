# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients regarding the quality of our legal services. Your input is crucial in helping us improve our service delivery. By sharing your experiences and suggestions, you will contribute to our continuous improvement efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. On each page, enter your information in the designated fields.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
3. Use the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is officially known in our records.
* **Email Address** (`title`, text, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up communications. (Note: This field is optional, but it helps us stay in touch if we need more information.)
* **Phone Number** (`title`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any urgent matters. (Note: This field is optional, but it speeds up communication if you have any questions or concerns.)
* **Service Date** (`title`, text, optional): Enter the date when you received our legal services. (Note: This field is optional, but it helps us understand the context of your feedback.)

### Service Experience
* **How would you rate the quality of our services?** (`title`, text, optional): Use numbers 1-5 to rate your overall satisfaction with our service delivery. (1 = Very Poor, 5 = Excellent)
* **What was your favorite part of our service?** (`title`, text, optional): Describe the aspect of our service that you found most satisfactory.
* **What was your least favorite part of our service?** (`title`, text, optional): Describe the aspect of our service that you found least satisfactory.
* **Would you recommend our services to others?** (`title`, text, optional): Yes or No

### Additional Comments
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`title`, text, optional): Share any further thoughts or suggestions on how we can improve our service delivery.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
- We value your input and will use it to improve our services.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
