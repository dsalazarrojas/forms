# Signature Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Signature Attestation Form** is used to collect signatures from clients and notaries, ensuring they acknowledge and validate the document's content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand its purpose and the required fields.
2. On the first page (signature_attestation_form_first_page), please ensure that all required fields are filled out, if any.
3. On the second page (client_signature), please enter your client's signature, which is a required field.
4. On the third page (notary_signature), please enter the notary's signature, which is not required but recommended for validation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Signature (client_signature)**: This is a required field where the client must enter their signature as a form of legal attestation. It is a date field, so please enter the date of signing.
* **Notary Signature (notary_signature)**: This field is not required but is recommended for added validation. If the notary is available, please enter their signature for added legitimacy to the attestation process.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign the form in front of the notary public.
* If you need help, please contact form@gic.mx.
