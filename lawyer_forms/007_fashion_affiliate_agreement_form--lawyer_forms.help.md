# Fashion Affiliate Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Affiliate Agreement Form is used to document and manage affiliate agreements between a fashion brand and their affiliates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Email: enter your email address.
	* Affiliate Program: select the specific affiliate program you are associated with.
	* Start Date: enter the date when the agreement started.
	* End Date: enter the date when the agreement ended (if applicable).
	* Agreement Term: enter the length of the agreement term.
	* Termination Reason: select the reason for termination (if applicable).
	* Termination Date: enter the date of termination (if applicable).
	* Agreement Status: select the current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
2. Review and confirm:
	* Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
	* Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email`, `required`: true): enter your email address to be contacted about your affiliate status and any issues with your agreement.
* **Affiliate Program** (`affiliate_program`, `required`: true): select the specific affiliate program you are associated with (e.g., Affiliate Program 1, 2, or 3).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `required`: true): enter the date when the agreement started.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `required`: true): enter the date when the agreement ended (if applicable).
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, `required`: true): enter the length of the agreement term (e.g., number of months or years).
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, `required`: true): select the reason for termination (e.g., non-payment of commission, non-compliance with agreement terms, change of business).
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `required`: true): enter the date of termination (if applicable).
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, `required`: true): select the current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `required`: true): select whether the form has been submitted or not.
* **Form Approval** (`form_approval`, `required`: true): select whether the form has been approved or not.

Note: The fields not marked as "required" are optional and can be filled out for additional context or information, but are not necessary to complete the agreement.
