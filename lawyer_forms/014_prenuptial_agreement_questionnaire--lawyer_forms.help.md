# Prenuptial Agreement Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a couple's prenuptial agreement, which is a legal agreement between two people who are getting married or are already married. It helps to outline the rights and obligations of each partner in case of a divorce or other separation.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather all required documents and information

Before filling out this form, make sure you have all necessary documents and information ready, such as identification documents, proof of income, and a list of assets and liabilities.

### Step 2: Answer each section carefully

1. **Name**: Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
2. **Spouse Name**: Enter your spouse's full name as it is written on their identification documents.
3. **Address**: Enter your current address as it is written on your identification documents.
4. **City**: Enter the city where you live.
5. **State**: Enter the state where you live.
6. **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
7. **Income**: Enter your current income.
8. **Assets**: Enter the total value of your assets.
9. **Liabilities**: Enter the total value of your liabilities.
10. **Debts**: Enter any outstanding debts.
11. **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
12. **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status from the options provided.
13. **Date of Marriage**: Enter the date of your marriage.
14. **Date of Separation**: Enter the date of your separation.
15. **Time of Separation**: Enter the time of your separation.
16. **Reason for Separation**: Explain the reason for your separation.
17. **Children**: Enter the number of children you have.
18. **Children Names**: Enter the names of your children.
19. **Assets Owned**: Enter the total value of assets you own.
20. **Liabilities Owned**: Enter the total value of liabilities you own.
21. **Employment Income**: Enter your employment income.
22. **Separate Property**: Enter the value of separate property.
23. **Joint Property**: Enter the value of joint property.
24. **Separate Debts**: Enter the value of separate debts.
25. **Joint Debts**: Enter the value of joint debts.

### Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your answers for any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for legal advice. Consult a lawyer or other legal expert for specific advice regarding prenuptial agreements.
