The "Unconditional Release Form" is a legal document that releases a subcontractor, client, or other individual from any obligations or liabilities related to a project or work agreement. This form is used to confirm the mutual agreement to terminate or end a project, release parties from further obligations, and acknowledge the completion of the work.

The form is used in various scenarios where a party wants to terminate a work agreement without any conditions, and both the client and subcontractor are in agreement to release each other from further obligations. The form can be useful in cases where the client or subcontractor has finished the project, but still wants to release each other from any potential liabilities or obligations.

To complete this form, you need to answer the questions accurately and truthfully to reflect the current situation. Ensure that you have a good understanding of the project and its status before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read and understand the form purpose and its implications.
2. Answer the questions carefully and accurately, based on the actual situation.
3. Make sure you have a valid reason for termination, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subcontractor accepts**: Select the correct option to confirm if the subcontractor accepts the termination. This field is not applicable if the subcontractor declines.
* **Subcontractor declines**: Select all the reasons why the subcontractor declines the termination. This field is not applicable if the subcontractor accepts.
* **Company terminates**: Select all the reasons why the company terminates the project. This field is not applicable if the company does not terminate.
* **Work description**: Provide a brief description of the work that was completed or to be terminated. This field is optional.
* **Release date**: Enter the date when the termination is effective. This field is optional.
* **Release time**: Enter the time when the termination is effective. This field is optional.
* **Reason for termination**: Provide a brief description of the reason for termination. This field is optional.
* **Terms agreed**: Select the terms that were agreed upon. This field is not applicable if no terms were agreed upon.
* **Client confirmation**: Select the option to confirm if the client has confirmed the termination. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to have a valid reason for termination, if applicable.
* Ensure that both parties have agreed upon the termination.
* Keep the answers accurate and truthful to reflect the actual situation.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
