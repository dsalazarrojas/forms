# Trust Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trust Amendment Form is used to record changes made to a trust. It requires information from both the grantor and co-trustee (if applicable). This form is used to update the original trust agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the grantor's name and date of birth.
2. Provide the grantor's address, city, state, and zip.
3. Enter the co-trustee's name and address (if applicable).
4. Enter the co-trustee's city, state, and zip (if applicable).
5. Enter the trust name.
6. Select the date of the modification.
7. Provide a brief reason for the modification.
8. Enter a description of the modification.
9. If applicable, enter the co-trustee's phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trust Amendment Form** (`trust_amendment_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the amendment.
* **Grantor Name** (`grantor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the grantor's full name.
* **Grantor Date of Birth** (`grantor_date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the grantor's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Grantor Address** (`grantor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the grantor's address.
* **Grantor City** (`grantor_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city associated with the grantor's address.
* **Grantor State** (`grantor_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state associated with the grantor's address.
* **Grantor Zip** (`grantor_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the grantor's zip code.
* **Co Trustee Name** (`co_trustee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the co-trustee's full name.
* **Co Trustee Address** (`co_trustee_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the co-trustee's address.
* **Co Trustee City** (`co_trustee_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city associated with the co-trustee's address.
* **Co Trustee State** (`co_trustee_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state associated with the co-trustee's address.
* **Co Trustee Zip** (`co_trustee_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the co-trustee's zip code.
* **Trust Name** (`trust_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trust.
* **Date of Modification** (`date_of_modification`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the modification in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Reason for Modification** (`reason_for_modification`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the modification.
* **Modification Description** (`modification_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the modification.
* **Co Trustee Phone** (`co_trustee_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the co-trustee's phone number.
* **Co Trustee Email** (`co_trustee_email`, text, required/optional): Enter the co-trustee's email address.
* **Grantor Phone** (`grantor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the grantor's phone number.
* **Grantor Email** (`grantor_email`, text, required/optional): Enter the grantor's email address.
