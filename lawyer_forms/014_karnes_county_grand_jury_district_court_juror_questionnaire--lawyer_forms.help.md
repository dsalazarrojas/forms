# Karnes County Grand Jury District Court Juror Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a juror questionnaire designed to gather relevant information about potential jurors for the Karnes County District Court. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the court has accurate and complete information about each juror, which is essential for the efficient and fair administration of justice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the correct contact information for you from the options provided on the first page.
2.  Choose the method(s) by which you can be contacted for jury service on the second page.
3.  Answer the questions about your juror experience and qualifications on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the options provided to indicate how you would like to be contacted for jury service. This information will help the court reach you efficiently.
- **Contact Methods** (`Contact Methods`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one or more methods that you would prefer to be contacted by (e.g., Work Phone, Home Phone, Cell Phone, Email, or Other).
- **Juror Information** (`Juror Information`, `select_one`, required: false): Please answer one of the questions about your juror experience and qualifications, selecting the option that best describes your situation.
  * Do you have previous experience as a juror?
  * How many jury trials have you completed in the past year?
  * How many years have you served as a juror?
  * Have you served as a juror in any other courts besides the district court?
  * Do you have been paid any fee for serving as a juror?

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the court for assistance.
