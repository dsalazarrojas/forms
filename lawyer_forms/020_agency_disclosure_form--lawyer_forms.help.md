# Agency Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agency Disclosure Form is a tool for collecting information about agency relationships between lawyers, clients, and other parties involved in a transaction. This form helps ensure transparency and compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the agent's name and the principal's name, as they are represented by the agent.
2.  Select the type of agency relationship (e.g., seller agent, buyer agent, dual agent, transaction coordinator, or other).
3.  Provide a summary of the duties and obligations explained to the client.
4.  Note any potential conflicts of interest.
5.  Enter the details of the compensation terms or fees.
6.  Enter the date when the disclosure was provided to the client.
7.  Select whether the client acknowledged the disclosure.
8.  For internal legal purposes, add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Agent name** (`agent_name`, **Required**, text): Enter the name of the agent representing the client.
*   **Principal name** (`principal_name`, **Required, text): Enter the name of the client represented by the agent.
*   **Type of agency relationship** (`relationship_type`, **Required, select_one): Select the type of agency relationship from the options provided (e.g., seller agent, buyer agent, dual agent, transaction coordinator, or other).
*   **Duties and obligations disclosed** (`duties_disclosed`, **Required, text): Provide a summary of the duties and obligations explained to the client.
*   **Conflicts of interest disclosed** (`conflicts_of_interest`, **Optional, text): Note any potential conflicts of interest.
*   **Compensation details** (`compensation_details`, **Required, text): Enter the details of the compensation terms or fees.
*   **Date of disclosure** (`date_of_disclosure`, **Required, date): Enter the date when the disclosure was provided to the client.
*   **Client acknowledgment** (`client_acknowledgment`, **Required, select_one): Select whether the client acknowledged the disclosure.
*   **Attorney notes** (`attorney_notes`, **Optional, text): For internal legal purposes, add any additional notes.
