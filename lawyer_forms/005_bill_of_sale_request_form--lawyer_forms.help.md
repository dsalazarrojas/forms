# Bill Of Sale Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bill Of Sale Request Form is used to document the details of a bill of sale for a specific item. This form helps to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the terms of the sale and provides a clear record of the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the seller's information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter the buyer's information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Describe the item being sold, including its type, description, and serial number (if applicable).
4. Enter the details of the sale, including the sale price and date.
5. Specify the payment method used for the sale.
6. Indicate whether any warranties are included with the sale.
7. Describe any liens or encumbrances on the item.
8. Enter any additional terms or conditions of the sale.
9. Specify whether notarization is required for the sale.
10. Review and sign the seller and buyer agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Information**: Enter the seller's legal first and last name, contact email, phone number, and address.
* **Seller First Name** (`Seller First Name`): Enter the seller's legal first name.
* **Seller Last Name** (`Seller Last Name`): Enter the seller's legal last name.
* **Seller Email** (`Seller Email`): Enter the seller's contact email.
* **Seller Phone** (`Seller Phone`): Enter the seller's contact phone number.
* **Seller Address** (`Seller Address`): Enter the seller's complete street address.
* **Buyer Information**: Enter the buyer's details, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Buyer First Name** (`Buyer First Name`): Enter the buyer's legal first name.
* **Buyer Last Name** (`Buyer Last Name`): Enter the buyer's legal last name.
* **Buyer Email** (`Buyer Email`): Enter the buyer's contact email.
* **Buyer Phone** (`Buyer Phone`): Enter the buyer's contact phone number.
* **Buyer Address** (`Buyer Address`): Enter the buyer's complete street address.
* **Item Details**: Describe the item being sold, including its type, description, and serial number (if applicable).
* **Item Type** (`Item Type`): Select the category of the item being sold (e.g., vehicle, boat, etc.).
* **Item Description** (`Item Description`): Enter a detailed description of the item, including its make, model, and year (if applicable).
* **Item Serial Number or VIN** (`Item Serial Number or VIN`): Enter the item's serial number or Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) (if applicable).
* **Item Condition** (`Item Condition`): Select the current condition of the item (e.g., new, like new, etc.).
* **Sale Details**: Enter the details of the sale, including the sale price and date.
* **Sale Price** (`Sale Price`): Enter the total amount of the sale.
* **Sale Date** (`Sale Date`): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`): Select the method used for payment (e.g., cash, check, etc.).
* **Warranties Included**: Indicate whether any warranties are included with the sale.
* **Warranty Details** (`Warranty Details`): Describe any warranty terms that apply (if applicable).
* **Liens or Encumbrances**: Indicate whether any liens or encumbrances exist on the item.
* **Additional Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the sale.
* **Notary Required**: Specify whether notarization is required for the sale.
* **Seller Agreement** (`Seller Signature`): The seller must confirm the accuracy of the information entered.
* **Buyer Agreement** (`Buyer Signature`): The buyer must accept the item as described and agree to the terms of purchase.
