# Permanent Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document information about a permanent guardian's relationship, reason for guardianship, and other relevant details. This information is likely used for court purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Select all relevant options for each field as needed.
3. Sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Permanent Guardianship Form Data** (`permanent_guardianship_form_data`, text, required): This section is for capturing additional comments or notes about the permanent guardianship. You can enter any relevant information that does not fit in other fields.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the provided options to describe your relationship with the ward (Guardian 1, Guardian 2).
* **Court Order** (`court_order`, text, required): Enter any court orders or references that relate to the permanent guardianship.
* **Permanent Guardian Name** (`permanent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the permanent guardian's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the permanent guardian's date of birth.
* **Permanent Guardian Address** (`permanent_guardian_address`, text, required): Enter the permanent guardian's address.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter the permanent guardian's mailing address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Permanent Guardian Relationship** (`permanent_guardian_relationship`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to describe your relationship with the ward (Spouse, Parent, Sibling, Other).
* **Permanent Guardianship Type** (`permanent_guardianship_type`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options for the type of permanent guardianship (Full, Limited, Temporary).
* **Permanent Guardianship Reason** (`permanent_guardianship_reason`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant reasons for the permanent guardianship (Court Order, Voluntary, Other).
* **Permanent Guardianship Duration** (`permanent_guardianship_duration`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options for the duration of the permanent guardianship (Permanent, Until Removed, Until Revocation).
* **Permanent Guardianship Termination Date** (`permanent_guardianship_termination_date`, date, required): Enter the date the permanent guardianship will terminate.
* **Permanent Guardian Signature** (`permanent_guardian_signature`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options for how the permanent guardian signed the form (Notary Seal, Signature).

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
