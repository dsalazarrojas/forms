# Copyright Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Copyright Request Form is a tool for clients, authors, and creators to submit their work for copyright registration. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Enter your name" field.
2. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
3. Provide a phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the name of the work you are requesting copyright for in the "Enter the name of the work" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your work in the "Enter a brief description of the work" field.
6. Confirm your contact state in the "State" field.
7. Confirm your contact country in the "Country" field.
8. Enter any additional submitter information in the "Submitter Information" field.
9. Provide your legal name in the "Legal Name" field.
10. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
11. Enter your city in the "City" field.
12. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
13. Confirm your contact state 2 in the "Contact State 2" field.
14. Confirm your contact country 2 in the "Contact Country 2" field.
15. Provide your legal address in the "Legal Address" field.
16. Enter your contact legal information in the "Contact Legal" field.
17. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
18. Provide your phone number in the "Contact Phone2" field.
19. Enter your legal name in the "Submitter Legal Name" field.
20. Enter your address in the "Submitter Address" field.
21. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
22. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
23. Enter your city in the "Submitter City" field.
24. Confirm your state in the "Submitter State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter your name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Email**: The email address associated with your work and contact information. 
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number. 
* **Enter the name of the work**: The title of your work.
* **Enter a brief description of the work**: A 1-2 sentence description of your work. 
* **State**: Confirm the state where you are from. 
* **Country**: Confirm the country where you are from. 
* **Submitter Information**: Any additional information about yourself. 
* **Legal Name**: Your full legal name. 
* **Address**: Your full address. 
* **City**: The city where you are from. 
* **Zip**: The zip code of your address. 
* **Contact State 2**: Confirm your contact state for the work. 
* **Contact Country 2**: Confirm your contact country for the work. 
* **Legal Address**: The address where you can be contacted legally. 
* **Contact Legal**: Your contact information for legal matters. 
* **Email Address**: The email address associated with your work and contact information. 
* **Contact Phone2**: Your contact phone number. 
* **Submitter Legal Name**: Your legal name as a submitter. 
* **Submitter Address**: Your address as a submitter. 
* **Submitter Email**: The email address associated with your work and contact information as a submitter. 
* **Submitter Phone**: Your phone number as a submitter. 
* **Submitter City**: The city where you are from as a submitter. 
* **Submitter State**: Confirm the state where you are from as a submitter. 

Note: All fields are mandatory except for those marked as "optional".
