# Bankruptcy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bankruptcy Intake Form is designed to gather essential information about your financial situation to help your bankruptcy attorney assess your eligibility for bankruptcy and create a comprehensive financial profile.

## How To Complete This Form
This guide walks you through the steps to complete the form:

### Intake Date
* The Intake Date field is a date field where you will enter the date of the current intake interview.
* Make sure to enter the correct date in the MM/DD/YY format.

### Client Information
* The Client Name field requires your full legal name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* Enter your full name correctly to ensure accurate verification.

### Employment and Income
* The Employment Status field is a select-one field where you will choose your current employment status.
* Choose the correct option from the provided list.
* The Monthly Gross Income field requires you to enter your monthly income before taxes and deductions.
* Enter your income accurately to ensure correct calculations.

### Housing and Expenses
* The Housing Status field is a select-one field where you will choose your current housing situation.
* Choose the correct option from the provided list.
* The Monthly Housing Payment field requires you to enter your monthly mortgage or rent amount.
* Enter the correct amount to ensure accurate calculations.

### Spouse and Dependents
* If you are married, the Spouse Name field requires your spouse's full name.
* The Spouse's Monthly Income field is optional, but enter their income if applicable.
* The Number of Dependents field requires you to enter the number of dependents (including children and other dependents).

### Debt and Credit
* The Total Credit Card Debt field requires you to enter your total credit card debt.
* The Medical Debt field is optional, but enter any outstanding medical bills.
* The Student Loans field is optional, but enter any outstanding student loans.
* The Vehicle Loans field is optional, but enter any car loans or vehicle debt.
* The Mortgage Debt field is optional, but enter any outstanding mortgage balance.

### Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your attorney.
* This form is for informational purposes only and may not be used as a substitute for professional advice.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intake Date**: This field is a date field where you will enter the date of the current intake interview.
* **Client Name**: This field requires your full legal name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number for verification purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **Email Address**: Provide your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number, where you can be reached.
* **Marital Status**: Select your current marital status from the options provided.
* **Spouse Name** (if applicable): If you are married, provide your spouse's full name.
* **Is Your Spouse Also Filing?**: Indicate whether your spouse is also filing for bankruptcy.
* **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents (including children and other dependents).
* **Current Street Address**: Enter your full residential address.
* **How Long at Current Address**: Enter the duration of your residence at the current address.
* **Housing Status**: Select your current housing situation from the options provided.
* **Monthly Housing Payment**: Enter the monthly mortgage or rent amount.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Employer Name** (optional): If you are employed, provide your current employer's name.
* **Job Title** (optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Monthly Gross Income**: Enter your monthly income before taxes and deductions.
* **Spouse's Monthly Income** (if applicable): If you have a spouse, enter their monthly income.
* **Other Income Sources** (optional): Enter any additional income sources, such as Social Security or disability payments.
* **Estimated Monthly Expenses**: Enter your total estimated monthly expenses.
* **Total Credit Card Debt**: Enter your total credit card debt.
* **Medical Debt**: Enter any outstanding medical bills.
* **Student Loans** (optional): Enter any outstanding student loans.
* **Vehicle Loans** (optional): Enter any car loans or vehicle debt.
* **Mortgage Debt** (optional): Enter any outstanding mortgage balance.
