# GDPR Erasure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Erasure Request Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to request the erasure of their data. This form should only be used for legitimate requests and will be reviewed internally for validation and accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Date Of Birth**, which is the date of your birth.
3. Provide your **Address** where we can contact you regarding your erasure request.
4. Enter your **Email** address for us to contact you with updates on your request.
5. If you have a **Phone**, please enter it in the corresponding field.
6. Describe the **Reason For Erasure**, the specific reason why you are requesting your data to be erased.
7. Specify the **Data To Erase**, which is the information you would like to have erased.
8. Please sign your request by typing your name in the **Signature** field.
9. Confirm that you **Consent** to the information provided by selecting either "Yes" or "No" from the options.
10. Finally, type a statement in the **Confirm Data Erased** field to confirm that you have reviewed and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please fill in your first name as you would like it to be displayed or used.
* **Last Name**: Fill in your last name as you would like it to be displayed or used.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter the address where you can be contacted regarding your erasure request.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for us to contact you with updates.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it in the corresponding field.
* **Reason For Erasure**: Describe the specific reason for requesting the erasure of your data.
* **Data To Erase**: Specify the exact information you would like to have erased from our systems.
* **Signature**: Please type your name to confirm your request.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you have read and understood the form and information provided by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Confirm Data Erased**: Type a statement confirming that you have reviewed and understood the information provided.

Note: If you have any issues or doubts while filling the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
