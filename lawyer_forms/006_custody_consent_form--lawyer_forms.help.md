# Custody Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custody Consent Form is used to obtain consent from parents or custodians for the medical treatment or care of their child. This form helps ensure that the child's parents or custodians are aware of and agree to the treatment or care being provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand its purpose and content.
2. Fill in your child's name as the "Parent Name" and provide their address as the "Parent Address".
3. If you are not the primary custodian, fill in the "Custodial Parent Name" and "Custodial Parent Address" sections.
4. Provide the name of the medical facility and the staff member(s) involved in the child's care.
5. Indicate your consent by selecting "True" for the "Consent" field.
6. If applicable, fill in the "Date" and "Time" fields to record the date and time of consent.
7. If you are being treated at a medical facility, provide the facility's name in the "Medical Facility" field.
8. If you are being treated by medical staff, provide their name in the "Medical Staff" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: This field is used to capture the name of the parent or custodian providing consent.
* **Parent Address**: This field is used to capture the address of the parent or custodian.
* **Custodial Parent Name**: This field is used to capture the name of the custodian (if different from the parent).
* **Custodial Parent Address**: This field is used to capture the address of the custodian (if different from the parent).
* **Custodian Relation**: This field is used to describe the relationship between the child and the custodian (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **Consent**: This field is used to indicate whether you consent to the medical treatment or care of your child. Select "True" to indicate consent.
* **Date**: This field is used to record the date of the consent.
* **Time**: This field is used to record the time of the consent.
* **Medical Facility**: This field is used to capture the name of the medical facility where the child is receiving treatment.
* **Medical Staff**: This field is used to capture the name(s) of the medical staff member(s) involved in the child's care.

Note: Please review and complete this form carefully to ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information. If you are unsure about any field, please seek clarification from the relevant authorities or personnel.
