# Child Support Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Support Cancellation Form is used to request the cancellation of a child support order. This form should only be used by an interested party in the child support order and the child must be under the age of majority (18 or younger).

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to provide all required information.
3.  Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Cancellation Request Date** (`cancellation_request_date`): Date of cancellation request
    - This is the date you are submitting the cancellation request.
*   **Requester First Name** (`requester_first_name`): Legal first name of person requesting cancellation
    - This is the first name of the person requesting cancellation of the child support order.
*   **Requester Last Name** (`requester_last_name`): Legal last name of person requesting cancellation
    - This is the last name of the person requesting cancellation of the child support order.
*   **Requester Email** (`requester_email`): Primary email address for contact
    - This is the email address that will be used as the primary contact.
*   **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`): Primary phone number for contact
    - This is the phone number that will be used as the primary contact.
*   **Requester Address** (`requester_address`): Full street address
    - This is the full address where you can be contacted.
*   **Other Parent First Name** (`other_parent_first_name`): Legal first name of other parent
    - This is the first name of the other parent involved in the child support order.
*   **Other Parent Last Name** (`other_parent_last_name`): Legal last name of other parent
    - This is the last name of the other parent involved in the child support order.
*   **Other Parent Contact** (`other_parent_contact`): Phone or email for other parent
    - This is the contact information for the other parent.
*   **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`): Legal first name of the child
    - This is the first name of the child involved in the child support order.
*   **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`): Legal last name of the child
    - This is the last name of the child involved in the child support order.
*   **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`): Please provide the child's birth date
    - This is the date of birth of the child involved in the child support order.
*   **Child Age** (`child_age`): Current age of the child in years
    - This is the current age of the child involved in the child support order.
*   **Court Case Number** (`court_case_number`): Court case number for child support order
    - This is the court case number associated with the child support order.
*   **Court Jurisdiction** (`court_jurisdiction`): County or jurisdiction of the court
    - This is the county or jurisdiction where the child support order was issued.
*   **Support Order Date** (`support_order_date`): Date when child support order was issued
    - This is the date when the child support order was issued.
*   **Current Support Amount** (`current_support_amount`): Current monthly child support amount
    - This is the current monthly child support amount.
*   **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`): Primary reason for requesting cancellation
    - Choose the primary reason for requesting cancellation.
*   **Cancellation Details** (`cancellation_details`): Provide detailed explanation for cancellation request
    - This is the detailed explanation for requesting cancellation.
*   **Effective Date Requested** (`effective_date_requested`): When should the cancellation take effect?
    - This is the date when you want the cancellation to take effect.
*   **Other Parent Agreement** (`other_parent_agreement`): Does the other parent agree to this cancellation?
    - Check with the other parent to see if they agree to the cancellation.
*   **Arrears Status** (`arrears_status`): Are there any unpaid child support arrears?
    - Check to see if there are any unpaid child support arrears.
*   **Arrears Amount** (`arrears_amount`): If applicable, total amount of arrears
    - If applicable, enter the total amount of arrears.
*   **Attorney Representing** (`attorney_representing`): Do you have an attorney representing you?
    - Check if you are being represented by an attorney.
*   **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`): Name of attorney if applicable
    - If you are being represented by an attorney, enter their name.
*   **Attorney Contact** (`attorney_contact`): Phone or email for attorney
    - If you are being represented by an attorney, enter their contact information.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): List any documents you are submitting with this request
    - If applicable, list any supporting documents.
*   **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`): Type your full name as your electronic signature
    - Sign your name electronically as the person requesting cancellation.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Date of electronic signature
    - Enter the date of your electronic signature.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information is accurate and complete.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the court or relevant authorities.
*   Make sure to sign and date the form.
