# Non Custodial Parent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Non Custodial Parent Form is used to gather information about non-custodial parents, likely for child support or guardianship purposes. This form is designed to collect specific details about the non-custodial parent, including reasons for non-custody, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out page 1 (non_custodial_parent_form_1) with your general information.
2. On page 2 (non_custodial_parent_form_2), provide any additional general information.
3. For page 3 (non_custodial_parent_form_3), select the reason for non-custody from the provided options.
4. If applicable, fill out page 4 (non_custodial_parent_form_4) with any additional information.
5. On page 5 (non_custodial_parent_form_5), select all relevant reasons for non-custody.
6. Enter a specific date on page 6 (non_custodial_parent_form_6).
7. If you want to add a note, you can do so on page 8 (non_custodial_parent_form_8).
8. Enter your email address on page 9 (non_custodial_parent_form_9).
9. Provide additional information on page 10 (non_custodial_parent_form_10).
10. If you have any additional information to add, you can do so on page 11 (non_custodial_parent_form_11), page 12 (non_custodial_parent_form_12), and page 17 (non_custodial_parent_form_17).
11. Enter a specific quantity or amount on page 15 (non_custodial_parent_form_15).
12. On page 18 (non_custodial_parent_form_18), enter a specific date.
13. Provide a specific information or description on page 19 (non_custodial_parent_form_19).
14. Select a reason for non-custody on page 20 (non_custodial_parent_form_20).
15. If you have multiple reasons or categories, select them all on page 22 (non_custodial_parent_form_22).
16. Finally, enter any additional information on page 24 (non_custodial_parent_form_24).
