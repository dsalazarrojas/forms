# Law Firm Confidentiality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Firm Confidentiality Audit Form is a tool used to monitor and evaluate the confidentiality practices within a law firm. It helps identify areas that need improvement and tracks progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form and ensure you are completing the correct one for your department or firm.
*   Select the department within the firm that is being evaluated (if applicable).
*   Choose the type of document being handled (if applicable).
*   Check the level of confidentiality required for the document(s) being handled.
*   Describe the actions taken to protect sensitive information.
*   Enter the date of the last review (if applicable).
*   Select the next review date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Law Firm Confidentiality Audit Form**:
    *   This is the main title of the form, identifying the purpose and scope of the review.
*   **law_firm_name**:
    *   Enter the name of the law firm being evaluated (if not already completed).
*   **department**:
    *   Choose the department within the firm that is being evaluated (if applicable).
    *   Select one department from the provided options (e.g., Department 1, Department 2, Department 3).
*   **document_type**:
    *   Choose the type of document being handled (if applicable).
    *   Select one option from the list (e.g., Document Type 1, Document Type 2, Document Type 3).
*   **confidentiality_level**:
    *   Indicate the level of confidentiality required for the document(s) being handled.
    *   Select all applicable levels from the options (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
*   **action_taken**:
    *   Describe the specific actions taken to protect sensitive information.
    *   Select one option from the list (e.g., Action 1, Action 2, Action 3).
*   **date_last_review**:
    *   Enter the date of the last completed review (if applicable).
*   **next_review_date**:
    *   Select the next review date for the evaluation (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions and access to complete this form.
*   Be specific and accurate when selecting options and entering information.
*   Complete the form for the correct department or firm to avoid duplication of effort.
*   Review and revise as needed to ensure the form accurately reflects your department's or firm's current practices.
