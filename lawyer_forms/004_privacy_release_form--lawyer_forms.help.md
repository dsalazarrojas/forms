# Privacy Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Privacy Release Form is used to obtain a release from an employee to disclose their personal information to their employer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Please fill out the required fields with your personal information.
* Make sure to provide your correct employee name and address.
* Enter your correct date of birth.
* Sign the form to confirm your understanding of the privacy information being shared with your employer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name**: This is the name of the company or organization you work for.
* **Employee Name**: This is your name as an employee.
* **Employee Address**: This is your address where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth**: This is your date of birth, which is a private information that will be shared with your employer.
* **Signature**: This is where you sign to confirm that you understand what information will be shared with your employer and that you are aware of your privacy rights.
* **Employer Signature**: This is where your employer signs to confirm that they will respect your privacy rights and will only use the information for the purpose stated in this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the information carefully before signing the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask your HR representative for clarification.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
