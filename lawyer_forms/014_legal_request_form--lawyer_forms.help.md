# Legal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Request Form is a tool for users to submit their legal requests to our organization. This form helps us understand the user's needs and provides a structured way to gather relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of user request from the options provided in the "User Request" field.
2. Choose the relevant legal department from the options in the "Legal Department" field. You can select multiple departments if necessary.
3. Enter the date of your user request in the "User Request Date" field. This will help us track the timeline of your request.
4. Provide a detailed description of your request in the "Description" field.
5. If you have a specific legal request, you can enter it in the "Legal Request" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Request** (`User Request 1, User Request 2, User Request 3`, select_one): Choose the type of user request that best describes your needs.
* **Legal Department** (`Legal Department 1, Legal Department 2, Legal Department 3`, select_multiple): Select the legal department(s) that you believe can assist you with your request.
* **User Request Date** (date): Enter the date of your user request. This will help us track the timeline of your request.
* **Description** (text): Provide a detailed description of your request, including any relevant context or information that can help us assist you.
* **Legal Request** (text, optional): If you have a specific legal request, enter it here. This can be a formal request for a legal document, a query about a legal process, or any other legal inquiry.
