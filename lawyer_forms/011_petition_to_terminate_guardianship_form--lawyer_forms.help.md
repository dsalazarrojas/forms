# Petition To Terminate Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for petitioning the termination of guardianship for a minor or incapacitated person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the ward's details in the first field, including their name and any relevant identifying information.
2. Provide information about yourself, the petitioner, in the second field, including your name and contact details.
3. Specify the reason for termination of guardianship in the third field.
4. If applicable, provide the specific grounds supporting the termination in the fourth field.
5. If the termination is based on a court order, attach a copy of the order in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ward Details** (ward_details, text, required/optional): Enter the ward's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Petitioner Info** (petitioner_info, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Termination Reason** (termination_reason, text, required/optional): Specify the reason you are petitioning to terminate the guardianship, e.g., due to the ward's majority age, death, or incapacitation.
* **Grounds for Termination** (grounds_for_termination, text, required/optional): If applicable, provide specific grounds supporting the termination, e.g., abandonment, neglect, or abuse.
* **Order of Court** (order_of_court, text, optional): Attach a copy of the court order, if applicable, to support your petition.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully, as the information provided may be used as evidence.
* If the ward has a legal representative (e.g., lawyer), consult with them before filling out this form.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
