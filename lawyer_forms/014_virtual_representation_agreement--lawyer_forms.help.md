# Virtual Representation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Representation Agreement is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of virtual legal representation between a client and a lawyer. It ensures both parties understand their roles, responsibilities, and expectations during the representation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand all the fields and their purposes.
2. Fill in the client's name, contact information, and representation start and end dates (if applicable).
3. Select the payment method(s) and terms agreed upon between you and your lawyer.
4. Add any other relevant terms or conditions that need to be included in the agreement.
5. Have both you and your lawyer review and sign the agreement to confirm your understanding of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual representation agreement**: A brief description of the representation agreement, including the scope of work and responsibilities.
* **Report**: Choose whether you have agreed to submit reports to the lawyer during the representation (Yes/No).
* **Lawyer fee**: The fee structure and amount agreed upon between you and your lawyer (number).
* **Payment method**: Select the method(s) of payment you have agreed upon (Online, In-person, Other).
* **Client signature**: Your signature confirming your understanding and agreement with the terms (text).
* **Lawyer signature**: The lawyer's signature confirming their understanding and agreement with the terms (text).
* **Lawyer fee terms**: Any additional terms or conditions regarding lawyer fees (note).
* **Payment terms**: Any additional terms or conditions regarding payment (note).
* **Representation agreement**: A summary of the representation agreement, including the scope of work and responsibilities (text).
* **Representation term**: The date the representation term(s) began (date).
* **Client name**: The name of the client (text).
* **Lawyer name**: The name of the lawyer (text).
* **Client contact**: The client's contact information (text).
* **Lawyer contact**: The lawyer's contact information (text).
* **Client email**: The client's email address (email).
* **Lawyer email**: The lawyer's email address (email).
* **Lawyer phone**: The lawyer's phone number (text).
* **Lawyer address**: The lawyer's address (text).
* **Agreement term**: The date the agreement term(s) began (date).
* **Representation start date**: The start date of the representation (date).
* **Representation end date**: The end date of the representation (date).
* **Payment terms 2**: Any additional terms or conditions regarding payment (note).
* **Other terms**: Any additional terms or conditions not covered by other fields (note).
* **Agreement date**: The date the agreement was signed (date).
* **Client acknowledgement**: A note for the client to acknowledge their understanding and agreement with the terms (note).
