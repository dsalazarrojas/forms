# Integrity Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a company's integrity, including declarations about subsidiaries, shareholders, and other sensitive information. The form will help ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User ID**: Enter your unique identifier, usually a number assigned to you for tracking and reference purposes.
2. **Company Name**: Fill in the name of your company as it appears on official documents.
3. **Subsidiaries**: If your company has subsidiaries, list them here. Select 'Yes' if they exist, or 'No' if they do not.
4. **Shareholder Name**: Enter the name(s) of your shareholder(s).
5. **Share Amount**: Enter the percentage or number of shares owned by the shareholder.
6. **Declaration**: Provide a brief statement explaining the company's position or circumstances.
7. **Signature Date**: Select the date of your signature.
8. **Company Declaration**: Choose whether your company has made a declaration. Select 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. **Shareholder Declaration**: Provide a brief statement explaining the circumstances or position of the shareholder.
10. **Declaration Status**: Choose the status of the declaration from 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
11. **Submitted By**: Enter your name or username for reference and tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID**: A unique identifier for the user, used for tracking and reference purposes.
* **Company Name**: The name of the company, typically as it appears on official documents.
* **Subsidiaries**: A list of subsidiary companies associated with the main company.
* **Shareholder Name**: The name or names of the shareholder(s) owning the company's shares.
* **Share Amount**: The percentage or number of shares owned by the shareholder.
* **Declaration**: A brief statement explaining the company's position or circumstances.
* **Signature Date**: The date on which the signature is made.
* **Company Declaration**: A declaration from the company, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Shareholder Declaration**: A statement explaining the circumstances or position of the shareholder.
* **Declaration Status**: The status of the declaration, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Submitted By**: The name or username of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* Verify the legitimacy of the information with the relevant authorities.
* Keep a record of the submission for future reference.
