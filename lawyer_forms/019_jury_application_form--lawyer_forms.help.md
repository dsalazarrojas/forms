# Jury Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jury Application Form is a document used to collect information from individuals who wish to apply for jury service. This form helps us to verify your details and understand your qualifications for jury service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields. If you have a name with multiple parts, you can include them in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Street Address 1 and Street Address 2 (if applicable) in the respective fields. If you have a suite or apartment number, please include it.
3. Provide your City and Zip Code in the respective fields.
4. Enter your Email and Phone Number in the respective fields. Please ensure your email address is valid and your phone number is formatted as a standard phone number (e.g., 555-1234).
5. Choose your Date of Birth from the date picker.
6. Select your Availability Date and Availability Time. Please note that these fields are not required.
7. Enter your Occupation and Work Experience in the respective fields. If you have multiple occupations or work experiences, please separate them with commas.
8. Choose your Jury Service preference from the dropdown menu.
9. Select your language(s) proficiency from the list.
10. Choose your Contact Method preference from the dropdown menu.
11. Enter your Jury Location (if applicable).
12. Select your Juror Status from the dropdown menu.
13. Select your Juror Type from the dropdown menu.
14. Select your Juror Role from the dropdown menu.
15. Enter your Juror Experience (if applicable).
16. Enter any additional notes about your juror experience.
17. Enter your Juror Email and Juror Phone (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Street Address 1** (`address_1`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **Street Address 2** (`address_2`, `text`, required): Enter your apartment or suite number, or any other additional address details.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the standard format (e.g., 555-1234).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Choose your date of birth from the date picker.
* **Availability Date** (`availability_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you are available to serve on a jury.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time you are available to serve on a jury.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant work experience.
* **Jury Service** (`jury_service`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you are interested in serving on a jury.
* **Language** (`language`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your language proficiency(s).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or phone/text).
* **Jury Location** (`jury_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you would like to serve on a jury.
* **Juror Status** (`juror_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your current juror status.
* **Juror Type** (`juror_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of juror you would like to be (civil, criminal, or family).
* **Juror Role** (`juror_role`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the role you would like to serve as (judge, lawyer, or defendant).
* **Juror Experience** (`juror_experience`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant juror experience.
* **Juror Note** (`juror_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your juror experience.
* **Juror Email** (`juror_email`, `email`, optional): Enter an email address for juror correspondence.
* **Juror Phone** (`juror_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a phone number for juror correspondence.
* **Juror Address** (`juror_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for juror correspondence.
