# Proof Of Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proof Of Service Form is designed to collect information about a service provided by an individual or entity. It includes details about the party being served, the service itself, and the service provider's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional fields.
2. Enter the name of the party being served (Form 1: party_name).
3. Enter a brief description of the party (Form 3: party_info).
4. Select the date of service (Form 4: service_date).
5. Select the method of service (Form 6: server_method).
6. Enter any additional details about the service (Form 7: server_details).
7. If the service was provided by someone else, select 'True' (Form 8: server_by).
8. Enter the location where the service was provided (Form 9: server_location).
9. Enter the time of service (Form 10: server_time).
10. Enter the name of the document or record provided (Form 11: server_document).
11. Enter any notes or comments about the service (Form 12: server_note).
12. Enter the address of the party being served (Form 13: party_address).
13. Enter the city of the party being served (Form 14: party_city).
14. Enter the state of the party being served (Form 15: party_state).
15. Enter the zip code of the party being served (Form 16: party_zip).
16. Enter the phone number of the party being served (Form 17: party_phone).
17. Enter the email of the party being served (Form 18: party_email).
18. Enter the name of the service provider (Form 19: attorney_name).
19. Enter the firm or company providing the service (Form 20: attorney_firm).
20. Enter the address of the service provider (Form 21: attorney_address).
21. Enter the city of the service provider (Form 22: attorney_city).
22. Enter the state of the service provider (Form 23: attorney_state).
23. Enter the zip code of the service provider (Form 24: attorney_zip).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proof Of Service Form (Form 1)**: Enter the title of the form, which should be self-explanatory.
* **Party Name (Form 2)**: Enter the name of the party being served.
* **Party Info (Form 3)**: Briefly describe the party being served.
* **Service Date (Form 4)**: Enter the date of the service, in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Server Date (Form 5)**: Enter the date the service was received, if different from the service date.
* **Server Method (Form 6)**: Select the method by which the service was provided, which can be Hand delivery, Certified mail, Email, Fax, or Other.
* **Server Details (Form 7)**: Enter any additional details about the service, such as the location or circumstances.
* **Served By (Form 8)**: If the service was provided by someone else, select 'True'.
* **Server Location (Form 9)**: Enter the location where the service was provided.
* **Server Time (Form 10)**: Enter the time of the service, in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Server Document (Form 11)**: Enter the name of the document or record provided.
* **Server Note (Form 12)**: Enter any notes or comments about the service.
* **Party Address (Form 13)**: Enter the address of the party being served.
* **Party City (Form 14)**: Enter the city of the party being served.
* **Party State (Form 15)**: Enter the state of the party being served.
* **Party Zip (Form 16)**: Enter the zip code of the party being served.
* **Party Phone (Form 17)**: Enter the phone number of the party being served.
* **Party Email (Form 18)**: Enter the email of the party being served.
* **Attorney Name (Form 19)**: Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Attorney Firm (Form 20)**: Enter the firm or company providing the service.
* **Attorney Address (Form 21)**: Enter the address of the service provider.
* **Attorney City (Form 22)**: Enter the city of the service provider.
* **Attorney State (Form 23)**: Enter the state of the service provider.
* **Attorney Zip (Form 24)**: Enter the zip code of the service provider.
