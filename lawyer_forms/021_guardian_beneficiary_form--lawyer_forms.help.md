# Guardian Beneficiary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guardian Beneficiary Form is a legal document used to establish or update information about an individual's relationship with a minor. It is often required for financial, insurance, or other official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure the information is up-to-date and correct.
2. Enter the individual's first name in the first_name field.
3. Enter the individual's middle initial in the middle_initial field.
4. Enter the individual's last name in the last_name field.
5. Enter the individual's date of birth in the date_of_birth field using the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 02/12/1990).
6. Specify your relationship to the minor in the relationship_to_minor field. If you are the minor's parent or guardian, select 'Yes' from the options.
7. Enter your name as the guardian or beneficiary in the guardian_name field.
8. Enter your address in the guardian_address field.
9. Enter your phone number in the phone_number field.
10. Enter your email address in the email field.
11. If necessary, identify an emergency contact person in the emergency_contact field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the individual's first name (e.g., John).
* **Middle Initial** (`middle_initial`, text, optional): Enter the individual's middle initial (e.g., D).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the individual's last name (e.g., Doe).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the individual's date of birth using the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Relationship to Minor** (`relationship_to_minor`, text, optional): Specify your relationship to the minor. If you are the minor's parent or guardian, select 'Yes' from the options.
* **Minor Child** (`minor_child`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable minor children (e.g., John Doe Jr., Jane Doe).
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the guardian or beneficiary.
* **Guardian Address** (`guardian_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the guardian or beneficiary.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If necessary, identify a person to contact in case of an emergency.

**Tips**

* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a relevant authority or expert.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all information is complete and correct.
