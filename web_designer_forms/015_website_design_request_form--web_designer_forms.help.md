# Website Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a custom website design request. It's essential to provide accurate information to ensure that we can deliver a website that meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your details.
2. Provide a brief description of the design requirements in the "Design Description" field.
3. Choose the services you need for your website in the "Services" field.
4. Select the expected launch date for your website.
5. Add any additional comments or services you need in the "Additional Comments" and "Additional Services" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Please fill in your name and company name to help us understand who you are and what you do.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Expected Launch Date**: Choose a date when you expect your website to be launched.
* **Design Description**: Briefly describe what you need from your website design.
* **Page Title**: Enter a title for your website.
* **Services**: Select the services you need for your website (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or requests you have.
* **Additional Services**: Select any additional services you need for your website (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Comments**: Add any comments you have about your website.
* **Services**: Select any additional services you need for your website (this field is duplicated, please ignore it).
