# Website Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and information about website quality, design, and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the URL of the website you are evaluating.
2. Evaluate the overall quality of the website on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
3. Share your thoughts on the user experience, providing any relevant details.
4. Choose the design style that best describes the website's aesthetic (Minimalist, Modern, Classic, or Custom).
5. Indicate how easy or difficult it is to navigate the website.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
7. Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the website.
8. If you are the website owner, manager, or designer, please specify your role.
9. Enter your age to help us understand your demographic information.
10. Enter the time of day you completed the form (optional).
11. If you are the website owner or manager, please specify your role.
12. Enter the URL of the website for further reference (optional).
