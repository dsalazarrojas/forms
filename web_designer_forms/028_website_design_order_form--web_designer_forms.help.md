# Website Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Design Order Form is a tool used to gather information from clients who are interested in ordering a website design service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as *required*).
2. Choose one or more theme(s) for your project from the *theme* field.
3. Select the layout for your website from the *design layout* field.
4. Enter your estimated turnaround time for the project.
5. Describe your project in detail using the *project description* field.
6. Provide your email address and phone number.
7. Set a deadline for your project.
8. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Design Order Form** (text, required): This is the title of your project. Please enter a brief and descriptive title.
* **Budget** (number, required): Enter your budget for the project.
* **Estimated Turnaround Time** (number, required): Estimate the time it will take for the project to be completed.
* **Theme** (select multiple, required): Choose one or more themes for your project:
	+ Modern
	+ Minimalist
	+ E-commerce
* **Design Layout** (select one, required): Select the layout for your website:
	+ layout1
	+ layout2
	+ layout3
* **Color Scheme** (select one, not required): Choose a color scheme for your project:
	+ scheme1
	+ scheme2
	+ scheme3
* **Font Style** (text, required): Enter your preferred font style.
* **Project Description** (text, required): Describe your project in detail.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Deadline** (date, required): Set a deadline for your project.
* **Additional Info** (note, not required): If you have any additional information about your project, you can include it here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

Please make sure to fill in all required fields carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient processing of your project.
