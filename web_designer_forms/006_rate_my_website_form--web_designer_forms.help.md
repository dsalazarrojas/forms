# Rate My Website Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rate My Website Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users to help improve the website's user experience. Fill out this form to provide your honest opinion about the website.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Rate My Website" field and enter your thoughts.
2. Click on "Overall Rating" field to rate the website on a numerical scale.
3. If you'd like to elaborate on your rating, click on "Comment" field and type in your thoughts.
4. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or information, click on "Email" field and enter your email address.
5. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or information, click on "Phone" field and enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate My Website**: This field is where you can enter your thoughts and opinions about the website. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Overall Rating**: This field is where you rate the website on a numerical scale. Please rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comment**: This field is where you can elaborate on your rating and provide more details about your experience on the website.
* **Email**: This field is optional, where you can enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or information.
* **Phone**: This field is optional, where you can enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or information.
