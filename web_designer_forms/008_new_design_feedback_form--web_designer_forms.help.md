# New Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Design Feedback Form is a tool for gathering feedback on new designs. It helps designers and product managers evaluate and improve the design quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide useful feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Review the design carefully and consider your thoughts on its overall quality and areas for improvement.
2. Choose your response to each field based on your evaluation.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Design Feedback Form (1)**: Enter the title of the design you are providing feedback on.
* **design (2)**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ **Yes**: The design meets or exceeds our expectations.
	+ **No**: The design needs improvement or does not meet our expectations.
* **design_file (3)**: Attach a file containing the design (optional, but recommended for more detailed feedback).
* **feedback (4)**: Provide specific comments and suggestions for improvement.
* **email (5)**: Enter your email address for any follow-up questions or comments.
* **phone (6)**: Enter your phone number for any urgent feedback or discussions.
* **product (7)**: Choose all products related to the design (if applicable).
* **comments (8)**: Add any additional comments or explanations for the design.
* **Submit (9)**: Once you have finished providing feedback, click this button to send the form.
* **Cancel (10)**: Discard any unsaved changes and exit the form.
