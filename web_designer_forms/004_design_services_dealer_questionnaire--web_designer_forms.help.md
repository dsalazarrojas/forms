# Design Services Dealer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from service dealers who provide design services. It is used to gather contact information, design preferences, and service requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select your design preferences from the options provided in the "design_preferences" field.
5. Specify your service requirements in the "service_requirements" field.
6. Select the services you offer from the options provided in the "dealer_services" field.
7. If you offer any additional services, provide a description in the "other_services" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`dealer_questionnaire_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed.
* **Email** (`dealer_questionnaire_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`dealer_questionnaire_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Design Preferences** (`design_services_dealer_questionnaire_design_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more design preferences that you have or would like to have. Options include:
	+ Color Scheme
	+ Design Style
	+ Layout
	+ Other
* **Service Requirements** (`design_services_dealer_questionnaire_service_requirements`, text, required): Specify the services you require.
* **Service Preferences** (`design_services_dealer_questionnaire_service_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more services you prefer to offer. Options include:
	+ Color scheme
	+ Design style
	+ Layout
	+ Other
* **Dealer Services** (`design_services_dealer_questionnaire_dealer_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you offer. Options include:
	+ Web development
	+ Web design
	+ Graphic design
	+ Other
* **Other Services** (`design_services_dealer_questionnaire_other_services`, text, optional): If you offer any additional services, provide a description of those services.
* **Other Services Description** (`design_services_dealer_questionnaire_other_services_description`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the "dealer_services" field, provide a description of the additional service.
