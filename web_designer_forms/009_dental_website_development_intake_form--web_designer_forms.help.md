# Dental Website Development Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for web design projects, helping us understand their requirements, timeline, and communication preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** address for us to contact you.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for us to reach out to you.
4. Describe your **Project Description** in a few details.
5. Specify your **Budget** for the project.
6. Set a specific **Deadline** for the project completion.
7. Select your **Priority** level for the project (High, Medium, or Low).
8. Choose your preferred **Contact Method** (Email, Phone, or Chat).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or business name as the client for this project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you regarding the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): A few details about the project will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the project, and we'll do our best to meet or underbid it.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, optional): Set a specific date for the project completion.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, required): Choose your project's priority level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of your preferred communication methods (Email, Phone, or Chat).
