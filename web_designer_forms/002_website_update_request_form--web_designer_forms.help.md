# website-update-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The website-update-request-form is a tool for website owners to submit change requests to their websites.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your website's information.
2.  Select the relevant category for your change request.
3.  Choose the priority level of your request.
4.  Set the due date for your request.
5.  Choose who the request is assigned to.
6.  Add any additional comment about your request.
7.  Select who submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Owner** (`website_owner`): Enter the name of the website owner who is requesting the update.
* **Change Request** (`change_request`): Provide a clear and concise description of the change request.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the change request (optional).
* **Category** (`category`): Select the relevant category for the change request from the options provided.

* **Priority** (`priority`): Choose the priority level of the request from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`): Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format when the change is due.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time in 24-hour format when the change is due.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Choose the person who is assigned to complete the change.

## Tips

*   Before filling out the form, make sure you have all the necessary information ready.
*   Be as specific as possible when describing the change request to ensure it gets the right attention.
*   Choose the correct priority level and due date to help the team or organization prioritize and manage the request efficiently.
*   Double-check the assigned person to ensure the request is correctly directed.
*   If you have any additional comments or information, use the comment field to provide context.
