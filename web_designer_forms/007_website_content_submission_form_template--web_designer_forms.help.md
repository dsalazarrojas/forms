## Website Content Submission Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for website administrators to submit content for review and publication on the website. The goal of this form is to collect relevant information from contributors and track the status of submitted content.

## How To Complete This Form

To submit content for review and publication on the website, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2.  Enter the submission date and time, if available.
3.  Select the type of content being submitted (Blog Post, Article, or Event).
4.  Choose the status of the submission (Pending, In Progress, or Published).
5.  Select the assigned reviewer for the submission.
6.  Select the submission reviewer.
7.  Provide any additional notes or comments.
8.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submission Form** (`submission_form`, `text`, required): Enter the main content of the submission, such as a blog post or article.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the content was submitted.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the content was submitted.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the content.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address of the person submitting the content.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number of the person submitting the content.
*   **Content** (`content`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the content being submitted.
*   **Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of the website where the content is hosted.
*   **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the content was reviewed.
*   **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the content was reviewed.
*   **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the submission (Pending, In Progress, or Published).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission.
*   **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of content being submitted (Blog Post, Article, or Event).
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the person assigned to review the submission.
*   **Submission Reviewer** (`submission_reviewer`, `select_one`, required): Select the person reviewing the submission.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill in all required fields marked as required.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the submission before submitting.
*   Reviewers should ensure that the submitted content meets the website's publishing standards.
*   If you are unsure about the submission type or status, please contact the webmaster for clarification.
*   Please note that submissions are reviewed and published based on website's publishing schedule.
