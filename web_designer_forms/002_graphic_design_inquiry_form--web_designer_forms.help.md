# Graphic Design Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Design Inquiry Form is a simple, one-page form used to gather information from clients or customers about their graphic design requirements. Please complete this form to provide us with details about your project, and we'll be in touch to discuss how we can help you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Graphic Design Inquiry Form" field with a brief description of your project.
2. Select the type of graphic design service you need (e.g., Event Flyer, Business Card, Poster, or Other).
3. Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
4. Enter your contact phone number.
5. Choose the project deadline.
6. Add any additional project notes.
7. Choose the project status (In Progress, Completed, or Pending).
8. Optionally, provide the project budget.
9. Optionally, select the assigned tool.
Note: All fields are optional, but the "Project Status" field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graphic Design Inquiry Form** (`graphic_design_inquiry_form`, text, optional): A brief description of the project you want us to design.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the project. This will help us understand the scope of the work.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of graphic design service you need (e.g., Event Flyer, Business Card, Poster, or Other).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, optional): Choose the project deadline.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional project notes or comments.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select one, required): Choose the project status (In Progress, Completed, or Pending).
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Optionally, enter the project budget.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Optionally, select the assigned tool.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when describing your project.
- Make sure to select the correct project status.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We'll review your submission and be in touch soon to discuss your project further.
