# sidebar_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects contact information from users. It is likely used to store and manage customer contacts or interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter any additional information or details in the "Message" field.
4. Optionally, add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a date for the contact in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time for the contact in the "Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the contact in the "Note" field.
8. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Send" button to submit your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it would be on your contact information.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Message** (3, text, optional): Add any additional information or details about the contact.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for the contact.
* **Date** (5, date, optional): Choose the date of the contact.
* **Time** (6, time, optional): Select the time of the contact.
* **Note** (7, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the contact.
* **Send** (8, text, optional): Click to submit your contact information.
