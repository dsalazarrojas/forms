# Business Logo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your business to create a unique and effective logo that represents your brand identity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label, with your business information.
2. For fields that have options, select the most accurate description that applies to your business.
3. If you're not sure about any field, you can leave it blank or describe it in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, \*): Enter the full legal name of your company.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, \*): Enter your name for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, \*): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, ): Enter your company website URL.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, \*): Select the industry your business operates in. Choose one of the following:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Retail
	+ Food and Beverage
	+ Professional Services
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Education
	+ Entertainment
	+ Other
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, `text`, \*): Briefly describe what your company does.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, \*): Select who your primary customers are. Choose one of the following:
	+ Consumers - general public
	+ Businesses - B2B
	+ Young adults - 18 to 34
	+ Adults - 35 to 54
	+ Seniors - 55 plus
	+ Children and families
	+ Niche market
* **Brand Personality** (`brand_personality`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select words that describe your brand. Choose multiple from the following:
	+ Professional
	+ Friendly
	+ Innovative
	+ Trustworthy
	+ Luxurious
	+ Affordable
	+ Modern
	+ Classic
	+ Bold
	+ Minimalist
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select preferred colors for your logo. Choose multiple from the following:
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Green
	+ Yellow
	+ Orange
	+ Purple
	+ Black
	+ White
	+ Gray
	+ No preference
* **Colors to Avoid** (`colors_to_avoid`, `select_multiple`, ): Select colors you do not want to use in your logo. Choose multiple from the following:
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Green
	+ Yellow
	+ Orange
	+ Purple
	+ Pink
	+ Brown
	+ Black
	+ None
* **Logo Style Preference** (`logo_style`, `select_one`, \*): Select the style of logo you prefer. Choose one of the following:
	+ Wordmark - text only
	+ Lettermark - initials
	+ Icon or symbol
	+ Combination mark
	+ Emblem style
	+ Mascot
	+ No preference
* **Design Inspiration** (`design_inspiration`, `text`, ): Describe any logos or designs you like.
* **Logo Usage Requirements** (`usage_requirements`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select where your logo will be used. Choose multiple from the following:
	+ Website
	+ Business cards
	+ Social media
	+ Print materials
	+ Product packaging
	+ Signage
	+ Merchandise
	+ Mobile app
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, \*): Select when you need the logo. Choose one of the following:
	+ Within 1 week
	+ Within 2 weeks
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 2 months
	+ Flexible
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, ): Select your budget range. Choose one of the following:
	+ Under $500
	+ $500 to $1000
	+ $1000 to $2500
	+ $2500 to $5000
	+ Over $5000
* **Revision Expectations** (`revision_expectations`, `select_one`, \*): Select how many revision rounds you expect. Choose one of the following:
	+ 1 to 2 rounds
	+ 3 to 4 rounds
	+ 5 plus rounds
	+ Until satisfied
* **File Formats Needed** (`file_formats_needed`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select all formats you need. Choose multiple from the following:
	+ PNG
	+ JPEG
	+ SVG
	+ EPS
	+ AI
	+ PDF
	+ PSD
	+ Not sure
* **Existing Brand Assets** (`existing_brand_assets`, `select_one`, ): Select if you have existing brand guidelines. Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes - comprehensive guidelines
	+ Yes - basic guidelines
	+ No - starting fresh
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, ): Enter any other information for the designer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank or describe it in the "Additional Notes" field.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting options.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact info].
