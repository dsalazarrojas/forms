# Eye Tracking Interaction User Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information, eye tracking data, interaction data, and feedback from users to gather insights on user interactions and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the User Information field.
2. Enter your eye tracking data in the Eye Tracking Data field.
3. Input your interaction data in the Interaction Data field.
4. Share your thoughts and suggestions in the User Feedback field.
5. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Information**
	* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your personal information, such as your name, email, or other identifying details.
### 2. **Eye Tracking Data**
	* **Eye Tracking Data** (`Eye Tracking Data`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to input any relevant data related to eye tracking, such as data from eye-tracking software or studies.
### 3. **Interaction Data**
	* **Interaction Data** (`Interaction Data`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to input any relevant data related to user interactions, such as user clicks, scrolls, or other behaviors.
### 4. **User Feedback**
	* **User Feedback** (`User Feedback`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to share your thoughts, suggestions, and experiences with the interaction or eye tracking data.
