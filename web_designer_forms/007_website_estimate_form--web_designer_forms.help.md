# Website Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Estimate Form is used to gather information about potential website projects. This form helps our internal teams understand the project scope, complexity, and requirements. It's a crucial tool to provide accurate estimates for clients and internal teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category of your project by selecting one of the provided options (e-commerce, blog, or portfolio).
2. Enter the URL of your website (or provide a link to a live demo).
3. Give a short description of your project.
4. Estimate the cost of your project.
5. Select the types of files that will be uploaded to your website.
6. Provide your name, email address, and phone number for us to contact you.
7. Add any additional notes about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website Category** (`website_category`): Select the type of website you are planning to create (e.g., e-commerce, blog, or portfolio).
* **Website URL** (`website_url`): Enter the URL of your website, or provide a link to a live demo.
* **Website Name** (`website_name`): Give a short name for your project.
* **Website Description** (`website_description`): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Estimated Cost** (`website_estimated_cost`): Estimate the cost of your project.
* **Website Uploads** (`website_uploads`): Select the types of files that will be uploaded to your website (e.g., images, videos, documents, audio).
* **Designer Name** (`website_designer_name`): Provide your name so we can contact you about your project.
* **Designer Email** (`website_designer_email`): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Designer Phone** (`website_designer_phone`): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`): Add any additional notes about your project.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate information about your project to ensure an accurate estimate.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We'll be in touch with you shortly after submitting your form to discuss further details about your project.
