# Logo Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logo Design Request Form is a tool for companies to submit their logo design requests to our design team. Please use this form to provide us with essential information about your company, your needs, and any additional comments you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the title of the form, which should be a brief and concise description of your company's brand.
2. Enter your company's name and website URL, if applicable.
3. Provide a contact phone number and email address for our designers to get in touch with you.
4. Select the services you require from our design team, if applicable.
5. Attach a file with your company's logo, if available.
6. Add any additional comments or instructions for our designers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): A brief and concise description of your company's brand identity.
* **Company Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required): The name of your company.
* **Company Email** (`form_email`, `email`, required): The contact email address of your company representative.
* **Company Phone** (`form_phone`, `text`, optional): The contact phone number of your company representative.
* **Company Website** (`form_website`, `text`, optional): The URL of your company's website.
* **Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required): A short description of your company, its services, or products.
* **Services** (`form_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the services you require from our design team, e.g. Logo Design, Branding Design, UI/UX Design, Graphic Design, Web Development, or Digital Marketing.
* **File Attachment** (`form_file_attachment`, `text`, required): Attach a file with your company's logo or any other relevant design files (max 10MB).
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, optional): Additional comments or instructions for our designers to better understand your requirements.
* **Additional Services** (`form_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional services you may require from our design team, e.g. Other.
