# Web Design Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Design Service Request Form is designed to gather essential information from customers to help us better understand their needs and preferences for custom web design services. This information will be used to tailor our services to meet their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your project budget from the dropdown menu. If you have a specific budget in mind, select "Yes" to indicate that you have a budget.
2. If you have any special requirements or requests for your project, provide them in the text field.
3. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with any questions or updates about your project.
4. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
5. Finally, indicate your subscription preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select Your Project Budget** (`budget_range`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you have a specific budget in mind for your project.
- **Custom Requests** (`custom_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any specific requirements or requests for your project, please enter them here.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address where you can be reached for project updates or questions.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
- **Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" to subscribe to our newsletter for updates on new services, promotions, and news.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we have enough information to provide you with the best possible service.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your project requirements to help us meet your needs.
* We'll use the information provided to contact you about your project, so make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number if applicable.
