# TTT Team Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TTT Team Registration form is used to collect contact information and volunteer type preferences from individuals or teams interested in participating in the TTT Team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Volunteer Registration Form" page.
2.  Fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Volunteer Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: false): This is the main form section where you can provide your name.
*   **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and opportunities.
*   **TTT Name** (`3`, `text`, required/optional: false): If you're a team, please enter your team name; if you're an individual, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Volunteer Type** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: false): Select all the volunteer types that apply to you or your team: "team" or "individual".

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure we can contact you promptly.
*   If you are a team, please list the names of all team members in the "TTT Name" field.
*   Select all the volunteer types that apply to you or your team to increase your chances of getting involved with the TTT Team.
