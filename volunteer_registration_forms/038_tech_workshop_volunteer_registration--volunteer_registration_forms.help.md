# tech_workshop_volunteer_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for a tech workshop. It will help us gather essential information about each volunteer and assign them to tasks and activities based on their availability and chosen role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, chosen role, and availability.
2. Provide your contact information, including your phone number.
3. Make sure to submit the form accurately to ensure correct assignment of tasks and activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Info** (`main_info`, text, optional): Fill in your name as you would like it to be displayed in the workshop.
* **Volunteer Roles** (`roles`, select_one, optional): Choose one or multiple roles you'd like to take on during the workshop, such as Volunteer, Organizer, or Support.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Select the date you are available to attend the workshop.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your email address or any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or changes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure correct assignment of tasks and activities.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
* This form will only need to be filled out once, so please only submit it for new or updated information.
