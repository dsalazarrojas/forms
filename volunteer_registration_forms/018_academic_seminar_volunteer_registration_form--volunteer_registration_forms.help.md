# Academic Seminar Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for an upcoming academic seminar. By filling out this form, you'll be helping us prepare for the event and ensuring its success.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`volunteer_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (`volunteer_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address** (`volunteer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone number** (`volunteer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **University or organization affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, required): Enter your current university or organization name.
* **Your current role** (`role_at_institution`, select_one, required): Choose the role you currently hold at your university or organization.
* **Name of the seminar you are volunteering for** (`seminar_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the seminar you're interested in volunteering for.
* **Date of the seminar** (`seminar_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the seminar you're volunteering for.
* **Preferred volunteer role (select all that apply)** (`volunteer_role_preference`, select_multiple, required): Choose the volunteer roles you'd be comfortable with, such as registration desk, room setup and teardown, etc.
* **What times are you available on the event day** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Choose the times of the day you're available to volunteer.
* **Have you volunteered at academic events before** (`previous_volunteer_experience`, select_one, required): Select whether this is your first time volunteering or if you have experience with other events.
* **Do you have any special skills relevant to event support** (`special_skills`, text, optional): If you have any special skills or equipment that could be helpful to the event, feel free to mention them here. This could be anything from AV equipment to first aid or languages spoken.
* **T-shirt size (for volunteer shirts)** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): If you'd like a volunteer shirt, please select your T-shirt size.
* **Any dietary restrictions or allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them here so we can provide suitable meals for you.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to fulfill my volunteer responsibilities and follow event guidelines** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to fulfill your volunteer responsibilities and follow event guidelines.

Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
