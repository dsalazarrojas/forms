# Animal Rescue Logistics Network Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Animal Rescue Logistics Network Registration form is designed for volunteers to register their interest and information for the purposes of coordinating animal transport logistics. By filling out this form, you will be providing crucial information to help us understand your availability, skills, and preferences, which will enable us to better organize and dispatch animal transports. This includes providing us with your name, contact details, and vehicle capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Volunteer Full Name in the text field provided.
2.  Enter your Email Address in the format of a standard email (e.g., `username@domain.com`).
3.  Enter your Primary Phone Number, which will be used for communication and verification purposes.
4.  Select the geographic region or county you are willing to cover with your vehicle. 
5.  Choose the type of vehicle you will be using for transportations (e.g. Sedan, SUV, etc.).
6.  Indicate the size and capacity of your vehicle (e.g., Small Crate Only, Medium Crates, Large Crates, etc.).
7.  Choose the types of support you can offer (e.g., ground transport, flight courier, etc.).
8.  Describe your weekly availability in the provided text field, including specific times and days you are available.
9.  Indicate the maximum distance you are willing to travel for a single trip (e.g., Up to 50 miles, Up to 100 miles, etc.).
10.  Select your level of experience with difficult or high-anxiety animals (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
11.  Confirm whether you have a copy of your driver's license or if you will provide one soon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Volunteer Full Name** (`volunteer_name_full`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be known on our system.
*   **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`, email, required): A valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_phone`, text, required): A phone number we can use to contact you.
*   **Region or County Covered** (`geographic_coverage`, text, required): Areas you are willing to drive to.
*   **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type_selection`, select_one, required): Choose from the available vehicle types.
*   **Transport Capacity** (`transport_capacity_desc`, select_one, required): Describe the capacity of your vehicle in terms of crate size.
*   **Support Types Offered** (`support_types_offered`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of support you are willing to provide.
*   **Weekly Availability** (`weekly_availability_desc`, text, required): Describe your availability in specific days and times.
*   **Willingness to Travel Distance** (`travel_willingness`, select_one, required): Indicate how far you are willing to travel for a single trip.
*   **Experience with Difficult or High-Anxiety Animals** (`difficulty_level_exp`, select_one, required): Select your experience level with anxious or aggressive animals.
*   **Drivers License** (`drivers_license_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm your driver's license status.

Note: If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of these fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
