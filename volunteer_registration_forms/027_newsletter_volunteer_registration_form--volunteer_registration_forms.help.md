# Newsletter Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from volunteers who are interested in contributing to the newsletter project. To complete the form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the format `example@example.com` if you wish to be contacted by email.
3. If you have a **Phone**, enter it in the text field provided.
4. Choose your preferred **Role** from the options provided. This is an important part of the registration process as it will determine the tasks you will be assigned to.
5. Select the **Frequency** at which you are available to work on the newsletter. This will help us schedule tasks and meetings accordingly.
6. If you have any **Note** or additional comments, please enter them in the text field provided.
7. If you have a **Description** of your work experience or qualifications, please enter it in the text field provided.
8. Finally, please indicate your **Agreement** by selecting one of the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your name will be used to address you in our communication.
* **Email** (Email): This is your contact information, and you can use it to receive updates and notifications about the project.
* **Phone** (text): This field is optional, but it will help us contact you in case of an emergency or schedule meetings.
* **Select Role** (select_one): Choose one of the three options: Newsletter Editor, Newsletter Writer, or Newsletter Designer. This will determine the tasks you will be assigned to.
* **Select Frequency** (select_multiple): Choose one or more of the options provided: Every Month, Bi-Weekly, or Weekly. This will help us schedule tasks and meetings according to your availability.
* **Date** (Date): This field is optional and will help us keep track of your availability. Please use the standard date format.
* **Time** (Time): This field is optional and will help us schedule meetings or calls. Please use the standard time format.
* **Note** (Note): This is a text field for any additional comments or notes.
* **Description** (text): This field is optional and will help us understand your work experience or qualifications.
* **Agree** (select_one): Please select one of the options: I Agree or I Disagree. This is an important part of the registration process as it indicates your commitment to the project.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is correct to receive updates and notifications.
* Choose your role and frequency carefully as it will affect the tasks and meetings you will be assigned to.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
