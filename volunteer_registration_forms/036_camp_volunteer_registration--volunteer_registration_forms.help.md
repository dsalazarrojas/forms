# Camp Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Volunteer Registration form is designed to collect essential information from volunteers for the camp event. This form helps the organizers to manage volunteer registrations, assign them to their preferred roles, and ensure their safety and satisfaction during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name as required in the "Volunteer Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for communication and updates from the organizers.
3. Enter your primary contact number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Specify your available dates for volunteering in the "Availability Dates" field.
5. Select the volunteer roles you are interested in from the "Roles Interested In" dropdown list.
6. (Optional) Select your T-Shirt size for the camp uniform from the "T Shirt Size" dropdown list.
7. Check the "Consent for Background Check" option if you allow background checks for the volunteer position you're applying for.
8. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name and Phone" field.
9. If applicable, describe your prior volunteer experience in the "Prior Volunteer Experience" field.
10. Write any additional notes you'd like to share with organizers in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Your email address will be used for communication and updates from the organizers.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number for communication and coordination.
* **Availability Dates**: Specify the dates you are available to volunteer. This will help the organizers schedule you for the camp.
* **Roles Interested In**: Select one or more roles you are interested in volunteering for, such as Counselor, Support Staff, Kitchen, or First Aid.
* **T Shirt Size**: Select your T-shirt size to ensure a proper fit for the camp uniform. Note: This is an optional field.
* **Consent for Background Check**: If you are interested in positions that require background checks, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person that can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Prior Volunteer Experience**: If you have prior experience, describe it briefly in this field. This can be helpful for the organizers to assess your qualifications.
* **Additional Notes**: Share any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers.

# Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your availability dates to ensure you are available for the selected dates.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the organizers for assistance.
