# Defense Signal Group Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Defense Signal Group Sign Up form is used to register participants for a secure communication group. Providing accurate and clear information in this form ensures correct group assignment and proper communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Participant name** in the "Participant name" field. This is the full name you want to be known by within the group.
2.  Select your preferred **contact method** in the "Preferred contact method" field. This is the phone number or other contact information you want us to use to reach you.
3.  Specify your **neighborhood or area** in the "Neighborhood or area" field. This helps the group organizers assign you to the most relevant local subgroup.
4.  Confirm your agreement to the group's **code of conduct** in the "Agreement to group code of conduct" field. Please select "I agree to follow the code of conduct" if you understand and will abide by the rules.
5.  (Optional) If you have any **safety and privacy concerns**, please provide details in the "Safety and privacy preferences" field.
6.  Specify your **availability for urgent alerts** in the "Availability for urgent alerts" field. This indicates when you're available to receive important messages from the group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant name** (text, required): Enter your full name for group membership.
*   **Preferred contact method** (text, required): Select the phone number or contact method you'd like us to use.
*   **Neighborhood or area** (text, required): Specify your location to help with subgroup assignment.
*   **Agreement to group code of conduct** (select\_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the group's guidelines.
	+   Select "I agree to follow the code of conduct" if you understand and will abide by the rules.
	+   Select "I do not agree" if you're unable or unwilling to agree to the rules.
*   **Safety and privacy preferences** (text, optional): Share any concerns or requests related to visibility or sharing within the group.
*   **Availability for urgent alerts** (text, optional): Indicate your preferred times to receive important messages from the group.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
