# Social Media Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in volunteering for social media management roles. It gathers information about their personal details, social media presence, interests, skills, and availability for volunteer work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required information in the form, starting with your personal details like First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Create a password and confirm it to ensure your account is secure.
3. Answer questions about your interest in social media, how you found us, and what you can contribute as a volunteer.
4. Indicate how many hours you can dedicate to volunteer work per week.
5. Select your volunteer type and confirm it.
6. If you have any additional information or language skills, please provide them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address we will use to contact you.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Create a secure password for your account, and confirm it in the next field.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, required): Enter the same password to confirm.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-02-12).
* **Interest in Social Media** (`interest_in_social_media`, select_multiple, required): Select all the social media platforms you have an account on (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, Instagram).
* **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you found out about us (e.g., social media, word of mouth, online search).
* **What is your expertise?** (`what_is_your_expertise`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas where you have expertise (e.g., content creation, community management, social media advertising).
* **How many hours can you volunteer per week?** (`how_many_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteer work per week.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of volunteer roles you're interested in (e.g., general, content creator, event coordinator).
* **Confirm Volunteer Type** (`confirm_volunteer_type`, select_multiple, required): Enter the same volunteer type you selected earlier to confirm.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you heard about us (e.g., social media, word of mouth, online search).
* **Time Preferenced** (`time_preferenced`, time, required): Enter your time preference in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 17:00).
* **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the languages you are fluent in (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Language 2** (`language_2`, text, optional): Enter any other language you'd like to share with us.
* **How many people can you volunteer with?** (`people_to_volunteer_with`, number, optional): Enter the number of people you can volunteer with.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Enter your availability in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2024-03-12).
* **Time of Day Preference** (`time_of_day`, time, required): Enter your time preference in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 17:00).
