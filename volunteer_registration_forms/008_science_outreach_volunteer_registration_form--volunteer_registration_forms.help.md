# Science Outreach Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers for the Science Outreach program. It helps us to understand the volunteer's availability, program preference, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Volunteer Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Volunteer Email** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Volunteer Phone** number in the third field.
4. Choose your preferred **Volunteer Program** from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your **Volunteer Shift** preferences from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the **Volunteer Date** you are available to work.
7. Choose your **Volunteer Time** slot for the event.
8. Add any additional **Notes** about your availability or preferences.
9. Review and submit the form.

* **Volunteer Shift**: Select the shifts you are available to work (e.g. Yes, Yes, No).
* **Volunteer Date**: Enter the date you are available to work.
* **Volunteer Time**: Choose your time slot for the event.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your availability or preferences.
