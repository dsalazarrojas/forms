# Disability Advocacy Network Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disability Advocacy Network Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who are interested in joining our network. By completing this form, you will be able to:

* Get access to relevant resources and support tailored to your specific needs
* Connect with others who share similar interests and experiences
* Receive information about upcoming events and opportunities

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User details section with your name, any relevant title or ID number (if applicable).
2. In the Accessibility needs section, select all the options that apply to you, as this will help us tailor our support to your specific requirements.
3. Choose one or more interests that you would like to engage with, as this will help us connect you with others who share similar interests.
4. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about updates and opportunities.
5. Enter your phone number, if you would like us to contact you via phone.
6. Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User details**: This section is where you can provide your name and any relevant title or ID number (if applicable). Please make sure to fill in this information accurately so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Accessibility needs**: This section is a list of options that represent different types of accessibility needs. Please select all the options that apply to you, as this will help us tailor our support to your specific requirements. Some examples include:
	+ Hearing
	+ Speech
	+ Mobility
	+ Visual
	+ Cognitive
	+ Mental health
	+ Physical
* **Interests**: This section is a list of areas that you may be interested in. Please select one or more interests that you would like to engage with, as this will help us connect you with others who share similar interests. Some examples include:
	+ Arts and culture
	+ Education
	+ Employment
	+ Health and wellness
	+ Housing and transportation
	+ Income and financial
	+ Legal and advocacy
	+ Living and social
	+ Personal
* **Email**: This section is where you can enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address so that we can contact you about updates and opportunities.
* **Phone**: This section is where you can enter your phone number. If you would like to be contacted via phone, please make sure to fill in this information accurately.
* **Note**: This section is for any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us. You can use this space to provide any further information that you think would be helpful for us to know about you.
