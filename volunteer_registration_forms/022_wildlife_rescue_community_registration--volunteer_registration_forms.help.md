# wildlife_rescue_community_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in joining the wildlife rescue community. It collects user information, contact details, schedule preferences, volunteer experience, and other relevant data to help the community assign tasks and keep in touch with volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form to provide the community with your details and schedule.
- Be as precise as possible, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you're unsure about any field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information**: Enter your basic details, such as name and title.
- **Contact Details**: Add your contact information, including email and phone number.
- **Schedule**: Select the days and time slots you are available to volunteer.
- **Volunteer Information**: Share your volunteer experience and any relevant information about your skills.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address for the community to contact you.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
- **Volunteer Schedule**: Select the specific dates you are available to volunteer.
- **Activity Dates**: Choose the activity dates you are available for.
- **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with the community.

## Tips
- Review each section carefully before submission.
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to ask the community administrators.
- If you need to make changes or add more information, don't hesitate to let us know.
