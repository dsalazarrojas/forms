# Trampoline Gymnastic State Championships Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers who want to sign up for the Trampoline Gymnastic State Championships. We use this form to collect information about your availability and contact details so we can schedule you for shifts and time blocks during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **Volunteer Name** field.
2. Enter your available **Availability Date** and **Availability Time**.
3. Choose your **Availability Shifts** by selecting one or more sessions from the dropdown menu.
4. Select one day you are available to work from the **Availability Days** dropdown menu.
5. Choose a **Availability Time Blocks** that suits your schedule.
6. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.
7. If you have any **Special Requests**, let us know in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Shift Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work.
* **Availability Date**: Enter the date you are available to work, in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Availability Time**: Enter the time you are available to start, in the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to call you if needed.
* **Availability Shifts**: Choose one or more shifts you are available to work. You can select from Morning Session (8-12pm), Afternoon Session (12-4pm), or Full Day Shift (8am-4pm).
* **Availability Days**: Select one day you are available to work. Choose from Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.
* **Availability Time Blocks**: Select a time block that suits your schedule. Choose from 1 hour block, 2 hour block, or 3 hour block.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any specific requirements or needs, enter them here.
