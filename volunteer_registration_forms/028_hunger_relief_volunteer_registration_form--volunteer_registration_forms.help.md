# Hunger Relief Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from potential volunteers for the hunger relief program. It is not intended for existing volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you are not an existing volunteer.
2. Fill out the form with your information and availability details.
3. Provide a valid email address and phone number to allow us to contact you.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Registration** (`volunteer_registration`, text, not required): This field is the first page of the form. Please fill it in with your name.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, select_one, not required): Choose how many hours you are available to volunteer per week.
	+ 8-10 hours: You are available for 8 to 10 hours per week.
	+ 11-20 hours: You are available for 11 to 20 hours per week.
	+ More than 20 hours: You are available for more than 20 hours per week.
* **Volunteer Days** (`volunteer_days`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
	+ Monday: You are available on Mondays.
	+ Tuesday: You are available on Tuesdays.
	+ Wednesday: You are available on Wednesdays.
	+ Thursday: You are available on Thursdays.
	+ Friday: You are available on Fridays.
* **Volunteer Shifts** (`volunteer_shifts`, select_one, not required): Choose the time of day you are available to volunteer.
	+ Day: You are available during the day (morning or afternoon).
	+ Afternoon: You are available in the afternoon.
	+ Evening: You are available in the evening.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, date, not required): If you have a specific date range when you are available to volunteer, please enter it here.
* **Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, date, not required): If you want to start volunteering on a specific date, please enter it here.
* **End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, date, not required): If you want to end volunteering on a specific date, please enter it here.
* **Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, not required): This is your full name.
* **Email** (`volunteer_email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, text, not required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`volunteer_message`, note, not required): If you have any questions or comments, please write them here.
* **Agree** (`volunteer_agree`, select_one, not required): Please agree to our volunteer terms and conditions.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the "Message" field at the end of the form to leave a note. We will get back to you soon.
