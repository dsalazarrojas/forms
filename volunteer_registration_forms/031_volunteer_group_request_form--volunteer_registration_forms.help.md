# volunteer_group_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for groups within non-profit organizations. It gathers data on volunteer availability, interests, and group details to streamline the volunteer group registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Request" field with a brief message about your group's interests and goals.
2. In the "Group Details" field, provide a short description of the group you're interested in joining.
3. Choose your interests from the options provided in the "Interests" field.
4. Select your availability in the "Availability" field.
5. Provide your schedule preferences in the "Schedule" field.
6. Enter the name of the group's organizer in the "Organizer" field.
7. Enter the name and description of the group you're interested in in the "Group Name" and "Group Description" fields.
8. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
9. Select your non-profit affiliation in the "Nonprofit" field.
10. Enter the location of the group or non-profit organization in the "Location" field.
11. Enter your volunteer time in the "Volunteer Time" field.
12. Indicate your availability for volunteering on certain days in the "Volunteer Days" field.
13. Select the type of volunteer schedule you prefer in the "Volunteer Schedule Type" field.
14. Indicate whether you'll be available to participate in a project in the "Project" field.
15. Choose whether you'll be available to volunteer in shifts in the "Volunteer Shift" field.
16. Enter the make and model of your vehicle in the "Vehicle Make" and "Vehicle Model" fields (if applicable).
17. Finally, provide any additional information about your volunteer experience or qualifications in the "Volunteer Other Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request**: Enter a brief message about your group's interests and goals.
* **Group Details**: Provide a short description of the group you're interested in joining.
* **Interests**: Select the interests that best match your group's goals.
* **Availability**: Select your availability for volunteering.
* **Schedule**: Enter your schedule preferences for volunteering.
* **Organizer**: Enter the name of the group's organizer.
* **Group Name**: Enter the name of the group you're interested in joining.
* **Group Description**: Enter a brief description of the group you're interested in.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and contact details.
* **Nonprofit**: Select your non-profit affiliation.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the group or non-profit organization.
* **Volunteer Time**: Enter your expected volunteer time.
* **Volunteer Days**: Select your availability for volunteering on certain days.
* **Volunteer Schedule Type**: Select the type of volunteer schedule you prefer.
* **Project**: Indicate whether you'll be available to participate in a project.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Choose whether you'll be available to volunteer in shifts.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Other Info**: Provide any additional information about your volunteer experience or qualifications.

## Tips
