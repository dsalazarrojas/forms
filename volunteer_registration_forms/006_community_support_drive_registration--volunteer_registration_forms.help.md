# Community Support Drive Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Support Drive Registration form is designed to collect essential information from potential volunteers. Please fill this form to register as an individual or a group, and it will be used to organize and coordinate volunteers for the community support drives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required information such as your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the volunteer role and drive type you're interested in from the provided options.
3. Choose your weekday and weekend availability by selecting all the days you can volunteer.
4. Provide your available start date and approximate hours per week you can dedicate to volunteering.
5. If you have access to transportation, select the type of vehicle you have.
6. If you're a returning volunteer, please select your prior experience level.
7. If you have any special skills, please provide a brief description in the text box.
8. If you're volunteering with a group, select the type of group and enter the approximate number of people in your group.
9. Select your T-Shirt size (if applicable).
10. Provide the emergency contact name and phone number.
11. Consent to a background check (if required).
12. Agree to the liability waiver.
13. Finally, select how you heard about this drive and the registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to ensure you're 16 or older to volunteer.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Which Support Drive Interests You?**: Select the drive type that interests you the most.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role**: Choose a role that best fits your skills and interests.
* **Weekday Availability**: Select all the weekday days you can volunteer.
* **Weekend Availability**: Select all the weekend days you can volunteer.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date when you can start volunteering.
* **Hours Available Per Week**: Choose the approximate hours you can dedicate to volunteering.
* **Do You Have Access to Transportation?**: Select the type of transportation you have.
* **If Yes, What is Your Vehicle Capacity?**: If you select "Yes" for transportation, choose your vehicle capacity.
* **Do You Have Prior Volunteer Experience?**: Select your prior volunteer experience level.
* **What Skills Can You Contribute?**: Enter any special skills or expertise you can offer.
* **Are You Volunteering as a Group?**: If you're with a group, select "Yes" and enter the number of people.
* **T-Shirt Size (for Volunteer Shirt)**: If shirts are provided, select your T-Shirt size.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's full name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **I Consent to a Background Check if Required**: Select "Yes, I Consent" if a background check is needed.
* **I Agree to the Liability Waiver**: Select "Yes, I Agree" to the standard volunteer waiver.
* **How Did You Hear About This Drive?**: Select how you heard about this community support drive.
* **Registration Date**: The current date will be automatically entered.
