# detailed_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The detailed volunteer registration form is designed to collect information from volunteers about their personal details, volunteer experience, and availability. This form helps us understand your role and how you can be involved in our activities. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your Email in the third field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select your Date of Birth in the fifth field.
6. Select your Date of Registration in the sixth field.
7. Choose your Volunteer Type from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter your Volunteer Shift in the seventh field.
9. Select your Volunteer Frequency from the dropdown menu.
10. Enter your Volunteer Duration in the eighth field.
11. Enter the number of Volunteer Hours you can commit per week in the ninth field.
12. Select your available Volunteer Days from the dropdown menu.
13. Select your Ngo from the dropdown menu.
14. Enter any additional details about your Ngo in the text field.
15. Enter your Volunteer Reason in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address that you use regularly.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Date of Registration** (`Date of Registration`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you registered as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Type** (`Volunteer Type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from Volunteer, Member, Staff, or Other.
* **Volunteer Shift** (`Volunteer Shift`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shift you are available to work (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`Volunteer Frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Volunteer Duration** (`Volunteer Duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of weeks or months you can commit to volunteering.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`Volunteer Hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Days** (`Volunteer Days`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from Monday to Sunday.
* **Ngo** (`Ngo`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from Ngo 1, Ngo 2, Ngo 3, or Ngo 4.
* **Ngo Details** (`Ngo Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about your Ngo.
* **Volunteer Reason** (`Volunteer Reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter why you want to volunteer.
