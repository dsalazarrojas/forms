# Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Volunteer Staff Reference Form is designed to collect information from individuals who have known the volunteer applicant. This information will help assess the applicant's character, skills, and suitability for the volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the volunteer applicant.
2. Provide your relationship to the applicant (e.g., teacher, coach, supervisor).
3. Indicate how long you've known the applicant.
4. Rate the applicant's patience and empathy.
5. Rate the applicant's compassion for youth.
6. Rate the applicant's integrity and honesty.
7. Rate the reliability and follow-through of the applicant.
8. Describe the applicant's primary strengths.
9. (Optional) Provide potential areas for improvement.
10. Indicate whether you consider the applicant suitable for working with children.
11. If necessary, describe any reservations or concerns.
12. Sign your name (as the reference).
13. Enter the date of the reference.
