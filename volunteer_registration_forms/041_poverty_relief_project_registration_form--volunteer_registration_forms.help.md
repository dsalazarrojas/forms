# Poverty Relief Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering with the Poverty Relief Project. Fill it out to express your interest in collaborating with the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Confirm your interest in the project.
3. Provide any additional information requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**: Enter a brief description of your organization or yourself.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number for us to contact you.
* **Organization Info**: Enter your organization's name and any other relevant details.
* **Registration Confirmation**: Confirm that you are interested in the project.
* **Confirm Participant**: Confirm whether you are a participant or not.
* **Volunteer Info**: Enter any additional details about your volunteer role.
* **Volunteer Details**: Enter any additional details about your volunteer role.
* **Confirm Volunteer**: Confirm whether you are a volunteer or not.
* **Confirm Partnership**: Confirm whether you are a partner or not.
* **Partnership Info**: Enter any additional details about your partnership.
* **Partnership Details**: Enter any additional details about your partnership.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state. Options are limited to 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm whether we can contact you via email or not.
* **Volunteer Role**: Confirm whether you have a volunteer role or not.
* **Role Info**: Enter any additional details about your volunteer role.
* **Role Details**: Enter any additional details about your volunteer role.
* **Confirm Role**: Confirm whether you have a volunteer role or not.
