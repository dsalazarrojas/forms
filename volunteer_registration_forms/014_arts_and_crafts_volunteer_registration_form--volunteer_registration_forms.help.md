# Arts And Crafts Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers who want to join our arts and crafts program. We will use the information you provide to match you with suitable activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). Required fields are:
	* Volunteer Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Age Group
	* General Availability
	* Artistic or Craft Skills
	* Preferred Volunteer Roles
	* Consent to use photos of me for organization marketing
	* Date of Registration
	* Emergency Contact Phone Number
2. For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank or fill them out if you feel it's necessary.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, and make sure to select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Full Name** (`volunteer_full_name`):
	+ This is your full name that we will use to address you and match you with activities.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`):
	+ This is the email address we will use to contact you about upcoming events and activities.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`):
	+ Choose your age group:
		+ Under 18
		+ 18 to 25
		+ 26 to 40
		+ 41 to 60
		+ 60 plus
* **General Availability** (`availability`):
	+ Select all the days you are available to volunteer:
		+ Weekday Mornings
		+ Weekday Afternoons
		+ Weekday Evenings
		+ Weekends
* **Artistic or Craft Skills** (`artistic_skills`):
	+ Select all the skills you are comfortable teaching or assisting with:
		+ Painting
		+ Drawing
		+ Ceramics
		+ Knitting/Sewing
		+ Jewelry Making
		+ Paper Crafts
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`previous_experience`):
	+ Briefly describe any previous volunteer experience you have had.
* **Preferred Volunteer Roles** (`preferred_roles`):
	+ Select all the roles you would like to take on as a volunteer:
		+ Teaching Assistant
		+ Event Setup
		+ Registration Desk
		+ Supply Management
		+ Clean-up Crew
* **T-shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`):
	+ Choose your t-shirt size for our volunteer uniform.
* **Health or Allergy Information** (`medical_notes`):
	+ Provide any health or allergy information we should be aware of.
* **Consent to use photos** (`photo_release`):
	+ Choose to consent or not to use photos of you for organization marketing.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`):
	+ This is the date you submit this form.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`):
	+ Provide the phone number of a family member or friend we can contact in case of an emergency.
