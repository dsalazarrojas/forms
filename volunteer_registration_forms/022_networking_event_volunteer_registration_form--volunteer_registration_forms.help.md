# networking_event_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from volunteers for networking events. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly, as it helps us understand your availability and preferences for volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions and review the event details carefully before starting.
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
3. Select the volunteer position you are interested in (e.g., Event Coordinator, Registration Desk, Greeter).
4. Choose the shift(s) you are available for (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, All).
5. If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them in the Note field.
6. Finally, review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Page 1, Event Name): This field is the title of the event you are registering for.
	+ Please enter the name of the event as it appears on the event schedule or promotional materials.
* **Name** (Page 1, Name): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
	+ This will help us identify you and contact you about the event.
* **Email** (Page 1, Email): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication about the event.
	+ This will ensure we can contact you with updates and reminders.
* **Phone** (Page 1, Phone): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication about the event.
	+ This will help us reach you with last-minute updates or changes.
* **Time** (Page 1, Time): Select the shift(s) you are available for.
	+ Choose the time slots that suit your schedule and preferences.
* **Date** (Page 1, Date): Select the date(s) of the event.
	+ Choose the date(s) you will be available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Position** (Page 2, Volunteer Position): Select the volunteer role you are interested in.
	+ Choose from the available options, such as Event Coordinator, Registration Desk, or Greeter.
* **Shift** (Page 2, Shift): Select the shift(s) you are available for in the selected volunteer position.
	+ Choose the time slots that suit your schedule and preferences.
* **Note** (Page 2, Note): Enter any additional comments or requests you have.
	+ This is an optional field for any further information or clarification.
* **Assigned Tool** (Page 2, Assigned Tool): This field is currently not used in the form. Please ignore it for now.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are registering for the correct event and volunteer position.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
