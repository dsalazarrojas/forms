# Volunteer Registration Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Registration Extension Request Form is used to request an extension for a volunteer who has not met their volunteer hours requirements. This form helps the extension request process move forward by providing necessary details about the volunteer's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure you fill all required fields to avoid delays in the process.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Registration Form**: This field is used to track your volunteer registration ID for easy reference.
* **Volunteer**: Enter the ID of the volunteer for whom you are requesting the extension.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the volunteer for whom you are requesting the extension.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the volunteer for whom you are requesting the extension.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the volunteer for whom you are requesting the extension.
* **Date of Extension Request**: Enter the date of your extension request.
* **Reason for Extension**: Provide a brief explanation for the extension request.
* **Hours Requested**: Enter the additional hours requested for the extension.
* **Additional Details**: If there are any other details you'd like to mention, use this field.
* **Volunteer**: Enter the ID of the volunteer who is requesting the extension (same as above).
* **Extension Date**: Enter the proposed extension date.
* **Hours Completed**: Enter the hours already completed by the volunteer.
* **Hours Ext**: Enter the additional hours to be completed under the extension.
* **Total Hours**: Enter the total hours requested after the extension.
* **Hours Left**: Enter the hours left to be completed after the extension.
* **Hours Completed Ext**: Enter the hours completed after the extension.
* **Reason for Completion of Additional Hours**: Provide a brief explanation for the completion of additional hours.
* **Volunteer Registration Form Submission Status**: Select the status of the submission (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or notes, use this field.
* **Review Status**: Select the review status of the submission (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Comment**: Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Review Status**: Select the final review status (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
* **Submitter**: Enter your name to confirm the submission.

Tips:
- Be thorough in filling out the form to avoid any issues in the process.
- Review your submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Keep your form filled out as a reference for any follow-up discussions or communications.
