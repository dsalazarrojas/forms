# donation_logistics_volunteer_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers who are interested in contributing to donation logistics activities. The form will be used to gather basic contact details and availability of the volunteer, as well as their interest in specific roles and agreement to certain guidelines and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate your availability for volunteering, including the days and times you are available.
3. Select the roles you are interested in taking on as a volunteer, such as Logistics Volunteer.
4. Indicate your agreement to the guidelines, code of conduct, and terms of volunteer participation.
5. Add any additional comments or information about your volunteering experience or interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Details**: This field is used to collect your basic contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to collect any additional contact details you would like to provide, such as your address or other contact methods.
* **Availability**: This field is used to indicate when you are available to volunteer, including the days and times of the week you are available.
* **Roles of Interest**: This field is used to select the roles you are interested in taking on as a volunteer, such as Logistics Volunteer.
* **Agreement to Guidelines**: This field is used to indicate your agreement to abide by the guidelines and rules of the volunteer program.
* **Agreement to Volunteer Privacy**: This field is used to indicate your agreement to keep personal information of clients or volunteers confidential.
* **Agreement to Volunteer Code of Conduct**: This field is used to indicate your agreement to uphold the code of conduct for volunteers.
* **Volunteer Details**: This field is used to collect any additional information about your volunteering experience or interests.
* **Agreement to Volunteer Terms**: This field is used to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of volunteer participation.
* **Agreement to Donation Logistics**: This field is used to indicate your agreement to the logistics and processes of the donation program.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is used to add any additional comments or information about your volunteering experience or interests.
