# Youth Volunteering Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students interested in volunteering at the Youth Volunteering Fair. The form will be used to process their volunteer registrations and ensure a smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your basic user information (pages 1-3).
2. Select your interests and choose a program at your school (pages 4-6).
3. Provide contact details for yourself and your parent/guardian (pages 7-11).
4. Select the volunteer fair you'd like to participate in and choose a date and time (pages 12-15).
5. Fill in any additional information you'd like to share (page 16-18).
6. Review and submit your form (page 19).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you'll provide your name, email, and other basic information.
* **Volunteer Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information about yourself as a volunteer.
* **Contact Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your contact details, including your name and email.
* **Interests** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the activities you are interested in volunteering for, such as Arts, Sports, Music, Dance, Theater, or Other.
* **School Info** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Provide some details about your school and why you're interested in volunteering.
* **Program** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a program you'd like to participate in at your school.
* **School Year** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year you are in at school.
* **School Grade** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the grade you are in at school.
* **Parent Contact** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the contact details for your parent or guardian.
* **Student Contact** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact details for the student.
* **Emergency Contact** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Provide contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Student Info** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information about the student.
* **Parent Info** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the parent or guardian.
* **Volunteer Info** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Add any further information about your volunteering experience or availability.
* **Fair Info** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional information about the fair you'd like to volunteer for.
* **Volunteer Fair** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a fair you'd like to participate in.
* **Volunteer Date** (`17`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you'd like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Time** (`18`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time you'd like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Duration** (`19`, `number`, required: false): Select how many hours you can volunteer.
* **Fair Location** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Provide the location of the fair you'll be participating in.
* **Fair Date** (`21`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the fair you'll be volunteering for.
* **Fair Time** (`22`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the fair you'll be volunteering for.
* **Fair Duration** (`23`, `number`, required: false): Choose the duration of the fair you'll be attending.
* **Other Info** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience.
* **Submit** (`25`, `text`, required: false): This is the final step to submit your registration form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* You can select multiple interests and programs in the respective fields.
* You can also add any additional information about your volunteer experience in the `Volunteer Info` field.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the `Other Info` field.
