# Volunteer Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Interest Form is designed to collect information about individuals who are interested in volunteering with our organization. This form helps us understand each volunteer's availability, interests, and commitment levels. Please complete this form to express your interest in becoming a volunteer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields (First name, Last name, and Email)
2. Consider carefully when answering the "What days are you available to volunteer?" and "How much time can you commit to volunteering per week?" questions
3. Select "Yes" if you are available to volunteer on the specified days, and "No" otherwise
4. Choose how many hours you can commit to volunteering per week

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **What days are you available to volunteer?**: Choose all the days you are available to volunteer, from the list of provided options. 
* **How much time can you commit to volunteering per week?**: Choose the amount of time you can dedicate to volunteering per week. This will help us better understand your commitment level and schedule your volunteer opportunities accordingly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes, but this is not required for volunteer registration.
